I agree that there are plenty of people who take joy in hurting 
others, with no sense of remorse or need for any motivation besides
causing pain.  However, I think there is a much larger group of 
people who are just selfish.  It's not that they enjoy hurting 
others, they just want to get the best for themselves and don't care 
how their actions affect others.

The first group will try to hurt others through any tool you give 
them.  The second group will stop if they aren't gaining from it.

> >The only motivation for what you are talking about is pure malice.
> 
> Yup.  It'd probably be masked by greed as well, in the form of ransom: "ok,
> now /consent me and I'll let you keep at least some of your stuff".  But
> malice is the main thing for some people.

That results in an immediate /report and probably a visit from a GM.

People can argue that greed is in character.  Some people will say,
"Of course I kept your belongings, I'm a Rogue!  You should know not
to trust me." or replace Rogue with any evil race or group nominally
opposed to the victim.  Some times the GM can even be persuaded to 
accept this "role playing" argument.

Using game mechanics to screw with someone else is obviously driven
by malice, so there is no "role playing" screen to hide behind.  I 
think that people would not generally risk banning just to be hurtful.

In any case, someone can still only hurt you with this if you consent
them to drag your corpse!  I think it should not be too hard to 
identify the wantonly malicious people and, in an effort per reward
analysis, it won't be worth it for the nasty people to masquerade as
nice for that long just so that they can hide your corpse.

> >I really don't think you would see this kind of problem very often and
> >anyone doing it would get banned pretty fast since it's obviously harassment.
> 
> You can say exactly that of the current system.

Except that Verant was very vague for a long time about whether it was
against the rules to keep someone's possessions after being consented.
Now there are plenty of people out there who think that they have the
right to keep your stuff if you consent them.  This new mechanism 
would fix the situation by making it simply impossible for them to 
take your things.
