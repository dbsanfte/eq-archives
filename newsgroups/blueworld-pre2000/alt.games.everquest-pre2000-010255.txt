I would (and did) switch to electrum malachite after silver jade 
becomes trivial.  Maybe try pearl and topaz, but certainly not
peridot.  You will fail a bit more on the electrum, but you will 
lose a lot less money per failure.

Your chance of success and skill gain is no different with 
enchanted bars than with unenchanted bars.  The finished items
will sell to NPCs for slightly more if they are enchanted.  You
will have to decide whether the time it takes to enchant the 
metal is worth the difference in price to you.

You can also sell enchanted items to players for about 2x cost
of materials.  Stamina earrings (silver bloodstone) and charisma
necklaces (silver cat eye) are fairly easy to make right away.
Wisdom, intelligence and strength rings should fund your practice
for quite a while once you can make them.
