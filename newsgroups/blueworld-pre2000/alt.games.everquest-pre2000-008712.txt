  It depends on what you call "Outdated".

  I used to run a Pentium 233 MMX, and used it quite happily for a long,
long time.  Then, the motherboard fried on it, and because of all the hastle
with an AT case, I went to an ATX case, and a Celeron 400.   That was quite
a while ago, I'm still terribly happy with it.  Sure, I don't get 100 FPS
out of quake3 (Actually, 30-40 fps in 800x600 with *all* options at their
highest, I like the eye-candy), but I'm still terribly pleased.  I suppose
that when Quake 4 (or whatever comes next) comes out, I might have to
upgrade, but until then, I'm perfectly content.

  On the other hand, if you're of the sort that craves the most cutting-edge
technology you can buy, then yes, it will be outdated rather quickly.
