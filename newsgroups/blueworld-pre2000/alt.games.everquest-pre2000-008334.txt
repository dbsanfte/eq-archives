Few facts about rogue class.
they do more melee dmg then anyother class with the possible exception of
monks.
They are not tanks.

them pickpocket is the price you pay. They will not be able to steal
everything, nor will they even take close to the majority.

return. If you get a rogue complaing of lock ups or one who goes LD a lot,
then you know he / she is pickpocketing a lot. If they don't suffer these,
they may be pickpocketing but not really enough to be worried over it.
