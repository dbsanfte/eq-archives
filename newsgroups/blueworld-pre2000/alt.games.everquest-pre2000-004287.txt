I think it's pretty unlikely that the kill is awarded to the person who
gets the most hits.  This problem is well known however, and is probably
just a broken algorithm.  While obviously there's some uncertainty, it's
fairly easy to see this problem occuring in the planes.

First, I'm a level 50 Magician.  I regularly get the credit for kills
when soloing in the planes.  There's a little bit of voodoo involved,
but when fighting in a zone with 40 people (at least 6 full groups), and
me solo..I can usually get the kill about 1 in 6, or 8 times.  The key
seems to be getting the last hit in more than anything.  I rarely get
the kill if I don't get in the last hit.  It should be painfully obvious
that there's no way in hell I'm outdamaging all of those groups.  People
are nuking like crazy, tanks are whacking like crazy.  It's just not
conceivable that I can consistently do this in Fear, and Hate both.

For a while, I thought this was a result of Bards, and Enchanters AE
mezzing, and wiping damage counters.  Naturally if this happens when the
mob is low on HP, and I get in the next hit, killing the mob, it's going
to be mine.  I'm not so sure anymore.  I don't think bard mezzes are all
that effective against Hateplane, or Fearplane mobs.  I also noticed
myself getting kills a lot on solo pulls.  I asked a couple enchanters
if they were mezzing mobs on solo pulls, and they said they had not.
<shrug> It could be clerics Atoning too...

Now, I can't exactly duplicate this at will by saying "Watch, I'll get
the credit for *this* mob."  But what I can do is get credit for far
more mobs than I should(like zero).

This is far more likely a bug in the algorithm, or the result of
damage-wiping spells than something as completely absurd as the largest
number of hits.  It would take a world class screwup in implementation
to let that slip through, and would be easily duplicatable by punching
level 3, or 4 mobs half a dozen times, then having a wizard IC a mob for
the kill.  I seriously doubt Alasdair has done his homework.
