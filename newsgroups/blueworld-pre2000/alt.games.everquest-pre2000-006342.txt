I mastered last summer, but I can't imagine any of this has changed ...

> Now I have some questions about using pie tins, skewers, pots and other
> baking materials...

> 1) Do you place them in the oven/spit with the other components?

Yes.

> 2) Do you lose them if you fail?

No.

> 3) Do you lose them if you succeed?

No.

> 4) What is a reasonable price to pay for these items?

Never gave that much thought ... 1 or 2 pp each, since they're re-usable,
sounds ok to me. [Also a Master Smith and Master Potter, so always made my
own.]

> 5) What is a reasonable price to offer for snake eggs?

1-2pp per 20 eggs. Yes, much more expensive than from a merchant, but at
that level newbs will start going out of their way to collect them (much
like bone chips).

> 6) Is there a place to collect eggs in larger amounts? (1 egg on a moss
> snake every 5th or 6th kill is rather slow)

Not that I know of. However, I've found that Tox Forest merchants and Erudin
merchants seem to have a lot of snake eggs -- newbs start here (just like
Ogguk, Freeport, Qeynos, etc.) but no on hangs around Odus once they're a
decent level.

> 7) Is there anywhere to buy brownie parts or a safe place to hunt them?

No merchant stocks them standard. I keep hearing there's a creature called
"Brownie Scout" in GFay, but I suspect it's a [Girl Scout] joke. Otherwise,
I was soloing Brownie Outcasts pretty easily at 34th. About one in four or
five drops parts. I offer 1pp each for parts ... but here's the thing.
Brownie Parts are also used for high-end caster [practice] Research runes.
I've seen level 40+ Wizzies offering 5-10pp *per* part for them.  =P

> Any other suggestions or hints would be greatly appreciated.

Make Meat Pies to (142), then set aside Baking actively until you collect
40+ Brownie Parts. So far, 18 Brownie Parts -> Winter Chocolate -> Birthday
Cakes has only gained me one point of Baking (to 143).   =P
