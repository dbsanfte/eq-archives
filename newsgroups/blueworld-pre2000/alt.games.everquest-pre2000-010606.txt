The shaman mitigates the need for taking damage.  Two rogues will
usually share the damage between them.  Up until the mid to high
50s a rogue's AC isn't significantly less than a warrior's (though
they do have less hit points).

Taunting is a useful ability however.  IMHO it is more reliable
than using backstab to taunt.  I think Abashi has even stated that
a successful taunt automatically redirects the monster regardless
of hate (though the more hate a monster has for someone the harder
the skill check probably is).  Assuming you can trust Abashi's
statements to be the truth (a questionable proposition at the best
of times).

If the monk's feign pulling abilities weren't needed for what I
was doing I'd swap out the monk for a warrior.  Rogues just work
so well in pairs (if they're roughly the same level with roughly
the same equipment).
