This is a very interesting assertion.  Do you have any hard evidence
for it?  Have any controlled studies been done of a 250 offense/200
defense tank versus a 200/250?

It seems pretty clear to me that the only thing that matters is the
PC-damage-per-second to mob-damage-per-second ratio.  This is why mob
slowdowns are so hugely effective; they dramatically alter that ratio
(and note, by the way, that slowdown is a defensive technique).

If Offense and Defense have precisely the same effect on that ratio
(highly dubious, I grant you) then of course there is no difference
between 250/200 and 1/(200/250).

To illustrate with a very crude and very hypothetical example, there
is obviously no difference between a PC dealing 100 damage per second
and receiving 50 per second and another one dealing 50 per second and
receiving 25.  They will have the same amount of health left at the
end of the combat.

Just wondering how much is actually known on this very interesting (to
me, anyway) issue.
