> it's Y X Z actually .. since North/South is the Y coord, and East/West is
> the X coord, then saying it's X Y Z is incorrect .. when i first started
> using maps, i always got lost because i thought it was X Y and not Y X =)

Unless you like left-handed coordinates, it is X Y Z.  The compass
directions are rotated 90 degrees from what you or I might design.
North is positive X.  All the rest follows from that:

         (+y)
          W
          |
(-x) S ---+--- N (+x)
          |
          E
         (-y)

And Z points upwards out of the screen.
