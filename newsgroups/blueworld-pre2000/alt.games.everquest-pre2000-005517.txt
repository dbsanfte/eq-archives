I actually wasn't considering bow kiting in the mix .  I find it too time
consuming for the rewards, and running out of arrows means your night is at
a standstill until you get more.  Also, it can be unpredictable.  To get
good hits, you have to stay close to the mob (and cycs have a pretty large
attack radius), otherwise you will only be hitting 25% of your shots if
that.  So, if you run out of arrows, or if enough of your shots don't hit
before you run out of mana, it's zone time.  I was talking about going toe
to toe and using spells.  My bad.  At 39, the power of a Ranger goes WAY up
because we get careless lightning, a 99 damage spell which, while being far
insuperior to casters nukes at that level, is a marked improvement over the
37 damage Ignite spell that we had before it.  We also get Healing which
heals a lot better than light healing, and of course, Spirit of Wolf...
