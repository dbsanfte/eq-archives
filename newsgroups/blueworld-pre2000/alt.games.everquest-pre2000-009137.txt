This is *very* wrong. I'm surprised at you =P

Damage output in a six man group is always caster oriented; in this case,
defense matters much more simply because if you're a tank, you're going to
be taking hits (for 100+), and what you do in melee just doesn't add up to
that much.

(Course, this changes to an extent with Togor's, but think about a
shamanless group.)

At level 60, raiding Veeshan's Peak where the mobs hit for 700 a shot (or
even Sebilis, where the bosses are in the 250-300 range, or Sky, where a few
mobs already hit for 400 by Isle 5), you'll be one of 30-40 tanks; your
damage output will vary between 1 and 2% of the mob's health, which means
you can use a mino axe and it won't matter much.

On the other hand, when a mob ripostes, or just decides it likes you for a
round or two (and it will), your extra 150 points of AC will matter.

A lot.
