From reading the website, Neverwinter will have a MMORPG component.  It is a
single player game, but you can register your character with the official
"Vault."  When you go online, you check out your character, and then can go to
any of the servers (which apparently can be run by anyone who owns the game and
has adequate hardware.)  Different servers can be connected together via in-game
portals.  (To connect your world to another via portal, both authors have to
agree to it, apparently.)  If the server running the world is online, the portal
is active.  Otherwise, it will appear closed.  There will supposedly be a few
"official" servers, and then all the player-run servers, so there will pe a
persistant online world, it'll just sort of fluxuate as various realms come
online for the night or shut down.
