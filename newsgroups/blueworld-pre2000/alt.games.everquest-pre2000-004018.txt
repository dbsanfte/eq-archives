This is indeed the practice adopted by most Jewellers, if you check charts
of triviality, the top few gems are mysteriously blank, because no-one ever
bothers going through them, they just skip to the next metal up.  Another
important thing to note is that you will get a similar failure rate on
'near' trivial items (i.e. within a few skill points) than on 'far' trivial
items (20+ skill points), so you might want to skip to a gem that you know
you can sell.  From my personal experience, Malachite doesn't sell, Cats Eye
Agate does.  Try jumping from Silver Emerald to Electrum Cats Eye and
/aucing off your successes.  You will get much better returns than just
selling to a merchant.  This, of course, assumes you are using Enchanted
metals.  If not, always opt for the cheapest metal/gem combo that isn't
trivial and sell back to the merchant (at a loss, normally).
