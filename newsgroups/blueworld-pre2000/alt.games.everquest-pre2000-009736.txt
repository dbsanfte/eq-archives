I have talked to several people who played EQ and they liked it, but also it
takes a big time commitment to develop your character.  Never played it
myself but the cover art is intriguing.  And speaking of UO I saw the latest
version at EB today and they seem to have taken a page from EQ in that
regard.

But everyone I've ever talked to including people with cable modems agree
that Origin never produced anything worth buying since Serpent Isle, and
that UO just downright blows chunks both in terms of game performance and
game play.

If you like action more than RPG you might want to checkout Asheron's Call
which I know several people who own it and they like it a lot, but they have
higher end systems than me.

And of course Diablo 2 is coming soon (again!) but that's stricly a dungeon
hack game dressed up with some quests to appeal to the RPG crowd -- if you
were a Diablo fan it sounds like lots more of the same so you'll probably
love it, and like UO (which I heard about but never played) you'll probably
look forward to spending a lot of time being harassed and killed by other
players, unless that's what you yourself do for kicks I'd give it a miss.
Personally I'm excited to see who cracks the game first and figures out how
to create hacked characters, so I'll be buying it for that reason alone :-)

Great thing about Diablo over both EQ and UO though is that the network is
free.  No strings attached.  They don't put much of anything on the servers
I guess so they can afford to run them with just sales revenues from the
game themself.  As a consumer I like that econmic model :-)
