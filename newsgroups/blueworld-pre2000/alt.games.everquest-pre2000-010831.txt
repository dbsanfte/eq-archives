Actually, there are places like this throughout the game, not just in
Kunark.

There seem to be several styles of special spawns - those on timed cycles
that always spawn at about the same time +/- whether in the same set
location or at a random location within a zone; those triggered by a
specific event such as handing an item to an NPC, killing a particular
spawn, etc.; those that are on a random spawn table with a placeholder
wheter the PH is random or not and whether the spawn location is fixed or
not; and those that are truly random in that they appear at some point
irrespective of time and irrespective of the death of a placeholder.

The biggest factor in hunting rare spawns is to know what pattern, if any,
the spawn follows and where the spawn point is located generally.  If you
get lucky, you can figure out from the patterns observed what the
cycle/trigger is and return repeatedly at appropriate times and in
appropriate locations to get the rare spawns.

As a caviat, if you camp a triggered spawn like Zahal the Vile by sitting on
his spawn point waiting for someone else to kill Regis the Reverant, I think
that you should be reported for KSing the rare spawn as the only way to get
Zahal to spawn is to get Regis and I have seen people sit on the Zahal spot
waiting for him when someone shouts Regis is up.  If someone is hunting the
trigger, they are also hunting what is triggered IMHO.
