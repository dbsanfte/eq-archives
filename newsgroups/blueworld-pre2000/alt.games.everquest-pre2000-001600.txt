	I haven't posted any in a while and figured 'what the heck?'

http://members.home.net/hefferman/Bixie.jpg

	A Bixie Drone, found in the Rivervale and Commonlands areas.  Isn't it
cute?  I killed it.

http://members.home.net/hefferman/Gordaan.jpg

	A pure vanity shot, this is my recently-created Barbarian Warrior just
after his arrival in Rivervale.  He did NOT make the run with all that gear, I
assure you.  It took four tries to get through the Gorge/Runnyeye route; I
would have made it on the first try but JUST as I stepped through the zone
line into Runneye someone's train full of Elite Goblin Guards arrived.  They
killed me in two seconds.  Regrettably I did not get a screenshot of his
funniest death on the journey: after perching on the Gorge wall and waiting
for Muddites and Goblins to leave, Gordaan descended, bolted for Runnyeye,
turned a corner...and hit half a dozen Evil Eyes.  At least they didn't charm
me.

http://members.home.net/hefferman/Griffin.jpg

	A long-distance shot of the East Commonlands' Griffin.  No, I don't have
any close-ups.

http://members.home.net/hefferman/Harpy.jpg

	A Harpy, found in the Steamfont Mountains near the windmills.  Personally
I think these are among the most striking mobs in the game, visually.  For
those interested, they are roughly L12 material, and they breath fire so keep
your Endure Fire spells up.  (I would have thought a Disease attack much more
fitting.)

http://members.home.net/hefferman/HillGiant.jpg

	Taken in the East Commonlands as I watched a young lady named Obrien
recover her corpse -- I had just dispatched something trying to kill her,
looked up, and THERE IT WAS.  To be fair though, I point out that this was
taken with a Glimpse spell so it's not quite as close at it appears to be.

http://members.home.net/hefferman/LeaningOrc.jpg

	Here, we see one of the rare Leaning Orcs found in the Northern Deserts of
Ro.  The Leaning Orc lures its prey into a false sense of security by using a
fighting style featuring precariously-unbalanced stances.

http://members.home.net/hefferman/Nillipuss.jpg

	Nillipuss is a Brownie (or so I'm told) who lives in Rivervale and steals
the rare and magical JumJum plants from the local druids' grove.  One may earn
a reward, I hear, by defeating him, but he's far too tough for Gordaan to even
try.  He is well-known in the area due to his annoying challenge rhyme.

http://members.home.net/hefferman/RatsOnTrees.jpg

	As you can see, in Norrath the rats DO grow on trees.

http://members.home.net/hefferman/SandGiant.jpg

	Taken in the Northern Deserts of Ro.  The bright yellow lights are from a
Glimpse spell.

http://members.home.net/hefferman/Scarecrow.jpg

	Scarecrows are fairly powerful undead creatures found in the Western
Plains of Karanas and (I hear) the Estate of Unrest.  Shakey Scarecrow,
however, is a permanent fixture at the Rivervale druids' grove.  He is not
hostile, seemingly bound to the druids' service, but tends to lose stuffing
from time to time.

	And finally, some UO screenshots.

http://members.home.net/hefferman/vchar.jpg

	The final stats of the UO version of Venture.  I was never much of a
powergamer and as a result, Venture's skill mix stayed well above the maximum
number of viable skills for most of his lifespan, and I tended to wander about
all over the place as well.  (Cartography and Tailoring had only recently been
expunged from his list.)  I neglected to include his attributes, but they ran
in the 80/80/60 range.

http://members.home.net/hefferman/vhouse.jpg

	Venture's house, or most of it anyway.  There's a bit of extra junk strewn
about as I moved almost all of my possessions into the house for Radnor.  I
never did get the place fixed up the way I wanted, though the globe was a nice
touch for the study.  (I'd almost paid someone a fortune for one before they
became a Tinkering item...someone had beaten me to it.)  There were going to
be vendors in the front room (in the SW corner), and a meeting room for the
guild in the NW room, if I ever bothered to recruit members.

http://members.home.net/hefferman/vbye.jpg

	And here's Venture saying goodbye to Radnor, or at least Dividia, one of
Radnor's mul...er, secondary characters.  I refused to slink off in a
deathrobe; Venture rode out in his favorite suit of armor with all the gear he
usually carried.  I also had the deed and keys for my favorite ship (the
Veritas) in the bank; I'd like to think that he sailed off into the sunset,
but there are no sunsets and there's nowhere to sail off to.  (I wasn't about
to litter by sailing into the middle of the ocean and logging out.)  Instead I
popped back to Vesper and logged out at the point I logged in at for the first
time...and then deleted the character (all my others having already been
deleted).  I had some good times in UO, but they were all of my own making,
and it had become obvious that there was no real hope for the game.
