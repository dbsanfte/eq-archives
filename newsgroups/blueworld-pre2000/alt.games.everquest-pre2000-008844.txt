Here are some more reasonable numbers ...

I am a level 43 Enchanter and have been playing this character since
last April.  I have been playing with the same group of friends since
about June when we all met in Unrest at around level 18.  We hit 30
in September and 35 around January.  Most weeks we play three nights
for 3-5 hours each night.  When we are being really efficient we get
about a level a week, two weeks for hell levels.  Lots of weeks have
been lost to things like new jobs, colds and other real life hassles,
but we have kept the characters within one level of each other at all
times and are now spread over about two bubbles of experience at 43.

This group of five only has one character with a really bad experience 
penalty, a Human Paladin.  If we had two or three, our pace would be 
much slower.  If that character were a warrior instead of a paladin,
our whole group would be going up levels about 10% faster just from
removing the experience penalty, not to mention the added effectiveness
of a Warrior over a Paladin.  Of course we adore the Paladin and would 
not want him to change. ;)  I just think that penalty is insane.

I agree that the full group assumption made by Jackie is completely
unreasonable.  In most situations at early levels, you are better off
with a group of two or three rather than six.  And people are wise to
the horrible penalty so are less willing to group with you as a 
Ranger, Paladin or Bard than if you were playing a Druid or Warrior.
