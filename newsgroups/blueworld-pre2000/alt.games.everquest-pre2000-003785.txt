You must first make a sewer to closest coastline.
(GM made explosives and shovels helps alot).
You need permission to make sewer frol the government,
Lord British or Lord Blackthorne depending on your choise to be Order
or Chaos.
Now you can attempt to make a toilet. You need ore, not wood.

Oh, and you need taming, so you can fill your sewers with monsters.
Once you put a monster there, it will start to respawn 7 more of its
kind. The hardest monsters to tame are titans. But they give alot of
gold. To tame lich lords, you need spirit speak as well as taming.

Now you are ready to open your own, private 'dungeon'.
