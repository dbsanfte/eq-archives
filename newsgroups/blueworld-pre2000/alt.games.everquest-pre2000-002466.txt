I just want to point out that it really depends on how you define
"large power differences". 

It boils down to the numerical values of a set of in-game variables. 

Even after a year or so, I still would not expect players, in general,
can kill a red mob consistently in EQ. There are isolated cases of
exploit where you can "trapped" a mob and blast it from safety. But
other than that, I really don't see there is anyway players can kill
mobs 3-4 levels above them (and the level difference clearly depends
on the class).

Now I would not call that "large power difference".
