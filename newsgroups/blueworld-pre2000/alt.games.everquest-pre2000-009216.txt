>"Canary"?

The person whose job it is to call "Run Away" - generally
someone with a good view of what is coming and a good sense of
what the group can do.  This is usually either the cleric or
the puller, but once the canary calls "run away" everyone
goes.  This cuts down on people trying to recover hopeless
situations after half the group has gone.  As for why I call it
that, the Canary in the coal mine is the early warning of
danger, and the group Canary is the one to call for an early
run away.

>>
>>Tempo lags

>>People force the tempo

This is a judgement call, like the George Carlin routine where
he explains that everyone else on the road is either a maniac
or a slowpoke and HE is the only person who drives at just the
right pace.
I do not like groups who sit and wait until at full mana and
full buffs before pulling.  That means that the tempo has
lagged.

At the same token, it is possible for an over-aggressive puller
to bring things back faster than the group can manage.  In a
good group everyone should feel a little pressed for time - if
you get comfortable you are not working fast enough.  I leave
to pull fairly early because I take a little while to make each
pull.  I watched someone last night who "knows the zone cold"
try to pull by running his warrior into the pit and bringing
back a dozen red mobs.  we zoned.

Does this make sense?
