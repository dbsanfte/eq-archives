For a bard do not train ANY weapon.  Knowing that you need
all 3 weapon skills in later life, I bought a dagger and a staff
once my 1 hs first maxxed (level 4.5 or so), and was able to
max all 3 skills by the end of level 8.  After that it is trivial to
keep them maxxed out.  All three of them would max easily before
2 bubbles of experience.

at level 8+ you can start working your (semi useless) H2H skill
up by equipping a lute and weaving CoD,Hymn,Warsong and
punching.  Do this only for the sake of completeness :)

After you get chains you can (pretty easily) work up throwing by
weaving Chains and selos, and throw daggers while backing away
from the mob.  This is best done with a mage friend summoning the
daggers.

I got kind of carried away on maxing the free (no training points
required) skills.
