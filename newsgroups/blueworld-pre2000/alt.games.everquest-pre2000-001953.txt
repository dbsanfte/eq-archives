Ultimately, though, each of us has to stand up for our vision of the
ideal online game because, well, that's what we do. You can't get
going in game design if you don't have courage in your convictions.

be willing to admit which were wrong (and I know, my detractors will
immediately say that I never do...) But you had better be stubborn,
because otherwise your game will never be made. You are trying to
persuade people to spend millions of dollars on your ideas--you had
better be passionate and committed.
