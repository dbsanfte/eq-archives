I really like the Lake of Ill Omen in Kunark.  You can be bound in 
Firiona Vie.  The corpse recovery run up the stream is a bit hairy,
but with Camouflage, you should be able to make it safely every time.
Just con everything and make a wide circle around anything that
scowls at you.

There are generally a lot of Iksars hanging out near the lake, so 
you should be able to find a partner or a group just about any time.
There is also good stuff to solo, the sabertooth tigresses and 
any goblin or sarnak that cons blue to you should be fine.
