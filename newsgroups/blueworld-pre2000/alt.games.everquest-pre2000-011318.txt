Hrielith is seeking two or three creative coders...

We are a small group of literate and serious role-playing
enthusiasts who are seeking one or two fine C coders,
preferably with some linux/unix experience to join our
community.

The ideal candidates will be mature, friendly,
team-oriented, creative, and possibly quietly mad.
Experience with c and the standard GCC libraries is a must.
We are a volunteer project, and members are expected to
commit to following spec, doc'ing code, and working a
minimum of two hours per week. Women are encouraged to
apply.

We have much to offer the right sort of person...

Hrielith is actually something rare. We are building a
detailed, complex, interactive environment that is rich,
rewarding, and decidedly non-magalithic in scope. We aren't
coding java, we aren't writing the very newest in net
technologies either - what we -are- doing is lovingly
hand-crafting a place that will be as fascinating as it is
joyful to play in and experience.

We like to build bright toys...come join us...

We are supportive, mud-activists, with a heartfelt vision of
a role-playing environment that will be truly unique. We are
seeking those of like mind to join our team and assist us as
we grow our codebase and community.

Builders are also welcome.
