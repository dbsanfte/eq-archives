I use

/pet guard here
/pet follow me

/pet attack
/pet back off

Rarely use /pet report health. 

You should have all of these (or the equivalents, ie /pet back off = /pet
as you were) hotkeyed.

Very rarely use /pet get lost (reclaim energy is better)

I never use /pet sit, as /pet guard here is much better. (under /pet sit,
he will chase a runner and when it's dead, promptly sit wherever he is,
even if it's out of command range)

I never use the taunt commands either.
