I do have a totally different opinion, one that may be not as commonly
accepted as the others up there   8)
I have a Ranger 18, an enchanter 7, and a wiz level 3,  and i would
difinitely say BE A RANGER.
An Half Elf one of course; or a wood elf if you prefer. Depending on your
race choice and deity you will either start on Antonica or Faydwer...

Why a Ranger ?
First i have to say that the other posts here are quite right... each of
them in fact.
But some of them miss the "starting character" thing i guess.

So
1/  As a Ranger all wolves (except undead), bears, kodiaks and so on will
con amiable to you. Believe me, when you start your charac it is a real
nuisance to have some of these beasts jump at you when you cross their
path )
2/ As a Half elf or Wood Elf you have infravision... Playing a human
Enchanter now i can say i miss that a lot... at least at the beginning.
Later there are plenty of items which casts light as well as spells which
are way better than infra
3/ You have the track ability, just as a druid. So that you can pick your
targets or know what is in the vicinity that could badly hurt you (Hail Hill
Giant !)
4/ With the gnoll fang quest in qeynos you can level rapidly and once at
level 9 you have Light Heal and Snear... definitely better than sitting
hours to recover from a battle )
5/ Till a certain level you make a very good fighter and have nice casting
possibilities. Many persons thing a Ranger is not a valuable High Level
charac. I Wish to see them wrong as i intend to go as far as i ll can with
that charac. But i know that missing plate armors will be a problem at a
time... And Kunark class only armors as well as Thorny Vine ar merely a
dream )


The only other Hybrid class i know that i would reco to start is bard...
cool fighters and good casters. They have sow (spirit of wolf) and can wear
powerful armors as well as weapons. Druid is fine but is really more a
caster... charm is not that powerful until a certain level. They can Sow and
teleport and bind which makes probably this class, having healing, DD and
DOT possibilites as well as pets the most powerful solo class of the game
(with necros... of course... dont want to wake up tomorrow having someone
casting vampiric embrace on me .Lol)

At last to be perfectly honest, hybrids do have a great flaw... yes they can
fight... yes they can cast. But they level with a "little" more difficulty
due to an xp penalty around 40% i think (that is you have to get 40% more
exp to level thant an other pure class of same level ). Nothing that cant be
dealt with some hunting though, and remember that no matter what class or
race you chose, the last bubble is always the hardest )
