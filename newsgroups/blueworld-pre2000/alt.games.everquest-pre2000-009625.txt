If it is a group that will kick someone out because they are
not at the optimum class or level for powergaming, I don't
think I want to be with them anyhow.

There is no question that hybrids take a larger share of the
experience than a warrior of the same level.  

In theory, the extra abilities of the hybrids should reduce
downtime by a comparable amount.

I think that everyone, including many of the folks at Verant,
agrees that the level-based experience penalties were not
chosen as well as they might have been.

In practice, Bards make a huge difference in group efficiency.
The others add abilities and downtime reduction when compared
to a warrior, but the bonuses are not commesurate with the
penalty.

This is most noticeable with Rangers, because many of our
spells make it easier for us to travel but do not help the
group much once we are fighting.

However, Verant has no intention of changing the experience
structure in the game - imagine the Nerf complaints if everone
logged in after the patch to discover that they were a
different level !  They have stated this several times and are
unlikely to ever change such a basic part of the game mechanic.

Everquest 2 will probably work differently, but there everyone
will be starting from a blank sheet of paper.

Oh, and for the record my primary is a Ranger 26 and I am
sensitive to the experience-point needs of my group members.
