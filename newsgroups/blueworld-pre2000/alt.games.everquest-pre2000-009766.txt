In general, I agree.  I have died 3 times this week with my warrior.
In each case he was bound in a distant location.

>Sometimes people die to things that they had no intention of fighting.

Bingo.  My warrior was scouting Najena looking for Trazdon, a necro,
that drops a dark circlet for crafted greaves.  I was bound in Greater
Faydark.  I was not intending to fight but a group was in the dungeon
that seemed to know the layout and agreed to help me.  I am 30th and
Trazdon is 25th.  The group ranged from 29-34 or so.  The problem
with following people is you lack the time to follow your progress
on your map.  I realized they were taking me to the wrong place,
according to my map, but I did not know exactly where I was located
so getting out on my own after disbanding was improbable.  The
Widowmaker is aptly named.  The monk says she jumped him but a few
in the group think he pulled as we were suggesting going elsewhere.
5 of 6 died.  The one who escaped TP'd me from BB to WC and gave me
SoW for my naked run through Nektulos.  One of the group dragged my
corpse to the zone and I eventually disbanded.

Vowing to get bound closer I mentally checked my options.  As a
dwarf, Neriak was out.  Freeport was the closest city.  That
meant a naked run through Nektulos if I died.  That did not
appeal to me so I decided to see if the Temple of Ro allowed
binding.  It does not.  The friendly druid that tried to bind
me gave me a SoW for the run to Freeport.  After helping her
find SolB, I attempted to find the entry to Nektulos.  I got
lost and met a firescale croc.  It ate me after a close battle.
This time I rode the boat.

Death three happened a day later.  After getting my crafted
greaves a group wanted me in Paw.  They really needed a tank.
I had just been playing my mage and forgot to think about
where my warrior was bound.  Stupid.  The group's wizard
TP'd me back as they still needed a tank.  :)  I disbanded to
get bound in the arena when the TP classes had to leave.  They
had tacitly decided to TP me back in case of death rather than
waiting for me to get bound closer.  

As a player, I virtually never ask for TPs or SoW.  I must say
that in the case of reaching Najena it is a real blessing.

I do not think I will ever start another character that can't
cast Bind Affinity.
