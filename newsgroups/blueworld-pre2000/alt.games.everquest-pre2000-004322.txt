I think there are probably two factors that mess up kill credits
(other than memory blurs).

The first is losing hate.  I don't know if it decays over time
or when the monster gets hit again, but monsters are definitely 
prone to attacking the thing that hurt them most recently.  It
could be that this decay is set at too high a rate, so the 
killing blow gets hate credit all out of proportion to its 
actual damage.

The second is the size of the hate list.  The monster can only
remember so many people before it starts to get confused.  So,
if there are 8 slots on the hate list, a party of six with a 
few pets will overflow it themselves.  And when someone falls 
off the list, they lose all their accumulated hate.  One big
damage dealer only has to outdo the total damage done by the
last few blows rather than all the damage the group has done
over the course of the fight.
