I was just wondering why they would care.  Verant generally only
tries to prevent activities that give a player an unfair advantage,
that ruin other people's game, or that break fiction.

I don't really think that groups of more than six players are 
inherently unbalancing.  They had to pick some number so that they
could do the interface screens, and six is a good number. ;)  Groups
of seven or eight start to get unwieldy.

Another reason they might not want groups of more than six is that 
it could cause memory errors.  They probably do a lot of stuff in
fixed size arrays, and accessing the seventh or eighth item in an
array of length six can be unhealthy for your program.
