I'm pretty sure it has something to do with sitting.  I at lvl 18 am far
beyond the level of a lionness (which is like lvl 7), but every time i sit
down, they'll attack me.  In fact, if you want to test this theory, it's
easy.  Just walk/run up to some weenie green mob.  Stand there for a second
or two (to aleiviate any potential lag problems.)  Chances are the green mob
will not attack you.  Now, sit down.  Once you do, it will probley attack.

that time.  So, I suppose verant did this just to force you to be seated at
you computer the whole time.  To break your resting/medding session.
