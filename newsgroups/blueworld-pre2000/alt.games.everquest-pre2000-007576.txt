Faction HIT for Guardians of the Vale. Hence, if you are JUST good

get it.

when I can't get a new Squad Ring.

Mrubble <sic?> is the Cleric you give the ring to for recharge, but you 
have to have high faction with her, and faction with the Clerics in 
Rivervale is obtained in entirely DIFFERENT ways than getting Faction 
with the Guardians of the Vale.

I just destroy the used ring and get a new one every time I am in 
Rivervale, I haven't found a way to get high enough faction with the
