All of the illusions are shape change spells.  Troll form gives
you regeneration, Air Elemental gives levitation, Water Elemental
gives water breathing, etc.  So that is not a valid argument against 
giving movement speed with the wolf illusion.

Illusion spells have one inherent balancing feature: You can only
use one at a time.

I see two arguments against adding a movement increase to the wolf
illusion.  First, Enchanters don't really need any enhancements.
They are already quite powerful, so there is no real need to add
anything.  Second, since it is an illusion, it would not be erased
by Root.  This would make it superior to ordinary Spirit of Wolf.
