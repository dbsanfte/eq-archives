Parricide wrote:
> 
> Levels  30, 35, 40 and 45 all take 3 times the amount of exp to get through
> them. Levels 31, 36, 41 and 46 (the ones after hell level) are a semi-hell
> because you lose HUGE amounts of exp if you die.

You actually still lose the same percentage of your total xp.  It just 
looks bigger on the experience bar because your total xp is large relative
to the xp needed for the current level.  For the same reason, when you die
during a hell level, you seem to lose less xp because the xp requirement
for that level is large.


Here's a numerical example (with totally fictitious numbers):

Level	XP for level	Total XP after level
1	 100		 100
2	 100		 200
3	 100		 300
4	 100		 400
5	1000		1400
6	 200		1600
7	 200		1800
8	 200		2000
9	 200		2200

Say you lose 2% of your total experience when you die.  

Die during	XP lost		  % of level
1		2% of 50 = 1	  1/100 = 1%
4		2% of 350 = 7	  7/100 = 7% (about 1/3 a bubble)
5		2% of 1500 = 30	  30/1000 = 3% (about 1/5 a bubble)
6		2% of 1700 = 34	  34/200 = 17% (about a bubble)

So, that hell level I placed at level 5 makes you lose an apparently 
large amount of your total experience if you die during level 6 and an 
apparently small amount if you die during level 5, but you are actually 
losing 2% no matter where you die.

I think that the real XP requirement per level goes up each level between
hell levels. If it were flat, as in my example, you would see the penalty 
rising, rather than falling, between level 31 and 34, for example.
