> My main problems with EQ  were:
>
> 1. Although the 3D idea is neat, they won't save the game for you if the
> rest of it is not appealing to you.

Fortunately, the game is vastly more appealing to me than UO. When I first
saw screen shots of the game I was wary. When I loaded it up for the first
time and played it, i was hooked.

> 2. You have ZERO chance to escape a fight if you're outmatched and there
is
> no-one around to help you.  This happened to me sometimes, and is
> frustrating.

Not true...I have escaped several very close calls. It usually involves
zoning, or getting off a luck y gate spell, or having a druid cast SoW on me
before i go hunting.

> 3. You can get blindsided by monsters you didn't know were coming; see
point
> 2.

Surprise! Unlike UO, this is a first person view, and not a magical neeto
radar view. it is possible to be snuck up on. I rather enjoy the uncertain
feeling I get when Im crawling through a dungeon. It adds to the realism...

> 4.  You can't travel the world like UO, due to successively more difficult
> monsters with every zone further away from a city.

Traveling far away from your home town should be dangerous IMHO. Otherwise
every elf in kelethin would be wearing a Polar Bear Cloak and Bear-hide
armor. It keeps the economy diverse. It's nice to spend all the time to go
to a far away land then return home with tales of your battles, and new
'unique' eqipment.

> 5.  The loading & zoning times are annoyingly long (to me...& no it's not
a
> shitty computer system).

I'll give ya that one. That 25 to 30 seconds between zones can be pretty
boring.
>
> 6.  Playing solo is almost impossible....you are vitually forced into

If your preference is to play solo, perhaps you are not playing a class that
is very well suited to solo play. Have you tried a Druid or Necromancer? As
a Necro, I can solo almost any yellow /con'ing mob i see (Unless im getting
close to a new spell circle, in which case i stick to white and high blues)
Check out some of the class quides that are linked from
http://www.gamezone.net/eq . Warning it's a spoiler site.
