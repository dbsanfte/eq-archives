Ratbert wrote:

> Bah!  No level item limits.  What if someone wants a nice item, but is 2 or
> 3 levels to LOW to kill the creature that holds this item.  If they one day
> gave it a shot, and BARELY scraped by to beat the monster and get the item,
> then it is WELL deserved and they can do what they please with it.  Sell it,
> keep it, pick their nose with it, whatever.  They earned it in my book, and
> thus should be able to wield, wear, sell it.

If that was what actually *happened* most of the time, I'd tend to agree...
Instead we see level 50's camping a mob that went green at 35 for an item that
they can't even use--but their new level 3 can.

I'm all for item limits on mobs.  If the guy was able to earn it early, cool for
him; it's that much more of an incentive for him to make those last few levels
to use it.  But when a level 35 weapon winds up in the hands of a level 3, its
value is in no way INcreased--it's DEcreased.

Think of how much more valuable, say, blackened iron would be if it was only
wearable by 20+.  Economics are funny that way.
