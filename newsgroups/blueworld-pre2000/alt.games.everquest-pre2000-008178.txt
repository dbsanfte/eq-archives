I think the PP thing is misunderstood.  As I understand it, if a mob carries 1 plat,
the Rogue can PP up to 2 gold, IIRC.  That's 20%.  But when you loot the critter,
they'll still have 1pp on them.  (Since we never know in advance how much coin a mob
carries, it's impossible to tell for sure.)

Items, though, are another matter; that's when you have to trust your Rogue, or if
they're a very adept PP, work it out so that they don't loot but they can PP in
battle.

Either way, I'd rather have a Rogue in the group that we know will be PPocketing
than a) one who's *not* in our group doing it to our kills, or b) someone who grabs
every loot before anyone else gets a chance, and doesn't share the good stuff (gems,
FS, bronze).
