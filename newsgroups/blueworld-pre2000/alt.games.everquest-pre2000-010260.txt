Trade skills are different from other skills.  You can only
raise them to 21 through training at your guild.  After that
they can only be raised through practice.  There is no level
cap on trade skills.  You could potentially have a 200 skill
in jewelcraft at level 1, although I can't imagine why anyone
would bother.

Research is not considered a trade skill.  When you get it
at level 16, you start with a skill of 16 for that one practice
point.  However, the cap is only 5 at level 16.  The cap goes
up by five points per level, so the first time you will be able
to raise your research skill by any means is at level 19 when 
the cap goes up to 20.  After level 19, you can raise the skill, 
either through practice or training, up to the new cap for each
level: (Level - 15) * 5
