Rogues are a very interesting class because one rogue is almost
a liability (or, at best, a warrior with crappy AC) because they
make themselves out to be a target with their damage output.
Put 2 together and you have the (famous) rogue ping-pong (some
things are greater than the sum of parts).  Put 3-4 together
and they shine.

The rogue ping-pong tactic in its most archetyal form allows
both rogues to get a behind backstab in with virtually every
attack (since that backstab will taunt the monster onto that
rogue allowing the other to get a behind backstab and so on).
If you get two rogues with identical weapons and level this
can be scary to watch (I've seen it).

Compare that to casters who almost have a law of diminishing
returns.  Each caster of the same type adds less than the
previous one.  With druids more than one is wasteful.  With
shamans the limit is about 1-2.  Necros and mages have
much higher tolerances because they have their pets if
nothing else.  Etc.
