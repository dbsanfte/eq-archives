On Sun, 18 Jun 2000 13:41:13 GMT, tzs@halcyon.com <tzs@halcyon.com> wrote:
>Why take iron rations?  I've not found anyone who has seen any type of food
>last longer than any other type, so just go with the cheapest: muffins.

While I don't see any real point to iron rations, I have at least seen
a benefit to using something other than muffins at the early levels.
Muffins, while cheap and light, are sometimes hard to find.  I found
it useful at the early levels to carry rations, as they are very easy
to find.  That way, whenever I passed a shop, I could replenish my
stack of rations.  This kept my food to a single inventory slot at all
times...at the early levels, inventory slots often ran out before my
strength did (what with all the batwings, bone chips, and other light
lowbie quest items).
