At 22 you should just be able to kill Gargoyles (sp?) in OOT at
offpeak time. They range from 18 - 20 and drop 3pp on average per
kill. They are relatively tough, but with a 2 handed weapon and choice
AC gear you should be able to kill the level 20 ones - but with some
downtime as your gona need to use mana on them (run about like a druid
with your new 35pt dd to soften them up before closing in to mele and
flamelicking them). And no bow kitting allowed either. But you will
need a magic kick...... which could be a problem if thats one of the
reasons you are desperate for money :/  (so now might be a good time
to do the watchman boot quest)

I was going to suggest hunting in Cazac in a group for peak time
playing, but as a ranger you wont have enough strength to carry the
fine steel weapons there and the nearest bank is miles off :/  Unrest
isnt so bad for loot dropped at that level - alot better than the poor
loot in nearby Mistmore, but the exp isnt so good at 22 and again, you
need magic kick (though grouping anywhere is always going to give
better exp for a ranger than soloing).
