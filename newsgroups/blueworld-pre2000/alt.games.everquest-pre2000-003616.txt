Another popular loot method when you are hunting in dangerous places like
Guk is one person Master loot.  After fight they loot all corpses and
ditribute coin in Plat.  Weapons like Fine Steel are handed out as they are
dropped.  Lotto for special items with each winner being unable to
participate in next lotto until all have received a "special" item.  This
serves 2 purposes.....the higher level dungeons are usually far away from
banks and players don't get bogged down with crappy coin.  It also allows
your spell casters to med and not worry about loot.  This si very important
with some of the spawn rates. Hehe...it also means there is no alpha
tracking.
