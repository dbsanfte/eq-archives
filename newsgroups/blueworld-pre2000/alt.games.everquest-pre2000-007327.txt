> camp spots? The reaon I ask is that I am wandering Guk at 5th level and
> I constantly come across corpses that have timed out and can be looted.
> It seems that I am unable to get an answer to "May I loot corpses?" in U

If there is nobody around, then it is probably fine to loot.

If there are people nearby who are fighting, don't loot and don't 
ask.  Some fights last longer than two minutes and it is bad form
to loot before the fight is over.

If there are people around who are not fighting, then ask before 
looting.  If you get no answer, wait a couple minutes and then
loot.  Some people are very shy about looting, so the group will 
often let bodies sit around a while in order to try to get them 
to loot something.
