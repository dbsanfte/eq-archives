The amount of time a new server keeps this freshness gets less and less
every time they put one up, as people learn more and more about the
game. Nowadays, for example, when a new server goes up, there's usually
someone selling banded armor withing 3 days of the server opening, while
it took over a month before smiths were selling it when the game was
first released.

Incidently, black pearls are worth less because people have
learned that ac matters more than stats so no one pays huge prices for
black pearl jewellry anymore, not just because they are so common.

All that said, however, new servers are fun to start on if you like the
challenge of not having easily obtainable high level gear for sale at
affordable prices. My druid used a cracked staff up to about L14 when I
started fresh on Tribunal last August, and was still mostly dressed in
patchwork at L20.
