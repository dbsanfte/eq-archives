It's all been laid out before; how quick people forget.

Twinking affects players other than the twinkie because:

1) People then camp for items they don't need, just to pass them to their twinkie
characters.

the item, find there is no market for their old items at a fair price.
3) There are just so many spawns to go around; some spawns take a *long* time to
pop.  Why should someone several levels lower than designed be able to take a MOB
from a legitimate player just because they were twinked to high heaven by a guild
or friends?

Saw a lvl 12 enchanter in East Karana yesterday fighting gorge hounds, bragging
about how twinked he was -- right down to his JBoots.  No way the player would
have even been in the zone if twinking was discouraged by Verant, let alone be
able to survive.

Does twinking affect other players?  It sure does.  Will Verant ever do anything
about it?  Doesn't look like it, does it?
