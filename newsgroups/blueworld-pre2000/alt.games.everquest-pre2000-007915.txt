The way I justified my selling quest items and platinum on EBay was 
this:
1. I don't harm anyone by collecting platinum or quest items.
2. There's nothing I can buy for my platinum in game that I need, most 
of stuff I need is trade only, and I don't want to camp for it or camp 
something else to trade for it, thus depriving other people of the loot 
they need
3. If I sell enough stuff on ebay, I can justify spending same amount of 
money buying items that I wanted.
4. With the way EBay's prices differ from in-game prices, I ended up 
getting a set of Yaks for 5k plat and frog crown. Woo-Hoo!
