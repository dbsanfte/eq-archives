Huh?

Aureal cards are decent, but I wouldn't call them "the best chip for game sound
and music."  I had an MX300 because I bought into all that A3D 2.0 propaganda.
The problem?  Hardly any game ever supports A3D 2.0.  And to be honest, as good
as A3D 2.0 is, it's only useful for certain 3D games.  Most strategy & RPG games
have no use for it.  It's not that great with flightsims either.

A3D 2.0 is a much bigger resource hog than EAX.  The midi for MX300 was
downright crappy, next to SBLive's Soundfont system.  Technical support was
atrocious.  The first drivers they released were nothing more than Beta
drivers... inefficient coding (big resource hog), bugs (stutters), and missing
features they had advertised.  I had to put up with this for the first 6+ months
or so.  It took them forever to release "real" drivers; and a lot of their
promised features were delayed for a looooong time.  I had a lot (and I do mean
a *LOT*) of compatibility problems with MX300, which was always traced to its
vortex roots.  This was last summer, so I don't know how much has changed
since.   Anyway, I finally got fed up with it, and sold it.  I had bought it for
$79, and sold it for $50.  I paid the same $50 for the cheaper & better SBLive
value.

SBLive value, with a cheap adapter from creative, has the ability for a Digital
connection (DIN) to my speakers.  The MX300 was limited to analog.

In short, SBLive has better compatibility, less bugs, better software, better
technical support, much better midi, much better reverb, and has the ability for
digital connection to certain speakers.  It was also cheaper when I bought it
last summer.

I'm not surprised Aureal went down in flames.  I gave them a chance with MX300,
and I was sorely disappointed.
