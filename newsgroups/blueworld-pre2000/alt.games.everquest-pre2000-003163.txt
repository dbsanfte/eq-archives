I'm a new Enchanter on Rallos Zek...

(Yes, this is another rant but not one of those vehement angry spewing ones,
more like a rational suggestive one.)

I believe all exploits should be dealt with in a timely fasion, and I don't
make exceptions for exploits that are specific to my class. In other words,
I am glad Fear and Charm will no longer be seriously unbalancing pvp.

Though I, like most of the other enchanters who are calmly and rationally
disturbed by the state of events,
believe that these spells should have rendered unexploitable instead of
removed.

On the other hand, I can rationally assess that pvp enchanters probably
present only a tiny fraction of the EQ population (though they've been quite
a vocal one lately). I can understand how the higher powers would have
bigger fish to fry than taking care of the tangled mass of code responsible
for the problems with fear and charm.

Yet, the purpose of removing charm and fear was to eliminate an issue which
impacted class balance....

(I wholeheartedly agree that the state of those two spells made enchanters
"cheap" opponents in pvp, and while I would not have exploited those issues,
I would not trust the entire EQ population to do the same)

....And now the balance is uneven on the other side of the scale.

While Enchanters may still be powerful in pvp groups,
(power in a group was the Enchanter's intent after all) One cannot always be
grouped and this leaves the Enchanter defenseless.

I am not asking for us to be given some kind of god status to replace that
which we lost. My only hope is to be able to hold my own or escape at least
half the time against my opponent.

As for resonable suggestions as to how this could be done.... I've yet to
give it much thought, and I hope others will fill in this blank. Or, perhaps
you'll hear from me again later.

I can't expect that fixing this class balance issue will be high on your
list, but I do hope it resides on your list somewhere.

Thank you for your time,
Inkantra
