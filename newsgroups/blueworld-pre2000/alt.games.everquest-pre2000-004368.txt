Here's what you do.  

One:  Make a rogue (half-elf or human, so you don't have to go through
Everfrost).  Log back in with your naked body, /consent your rogue, come
back as rogue.

Hide and sneak like a cat, then crawl through Blackburrow until you find
your body and drag it out.  (Or loot it there and crawl out, then
purposely die and /consent your naked self.)  Viola...

Or...

Once you know where your body is, group up with someone who can invis
*and* cast see invis... Like an Enchanter, say.  Have them cast both on
you (and if you dont know your way out of BB, someone who does).

Hotkey the /corpse command, drop back into the trap, start hitting the
button until you get your corpse, and then book it outta there.  Fast.  

Or...!

If you're a caster-type, get to level 4 and get Gate ASAP.  Repeat the
above, substituting "drag to safe place, loot, and gate" for the last
part.  The drawback of this is that at level three you have very
*little* time before your corpse decays.  (It works fine for upper
levels; it's what I've done.)

If you don't get your body back, though, go get up to level 5 (at least)
wearing nothing you can't afford to lose, and *then* go back to BB.  And
stay away from that tree! :D

(Now you know why you should never twink anything under 6. :X)

Good luck :)
