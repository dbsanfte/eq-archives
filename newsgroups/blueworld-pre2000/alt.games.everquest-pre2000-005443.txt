>Personally, I've been hoping for one or two zones to connect Lavastorm
>mountains to Everfrost peaks.  Its a serious trip to head to
>Permafrost from most anywhere in the world, still a long hike from the
>nearest druid/wizard teleport spots... my vision was of a zone that

The only problem I see with a northerly route is, oddly enough, one of
game balance. Take the following Halas-to-Freeport routes as examples:

Main Route 1 (existing): Halas - Everfrost - Blackburrow - QH - WK - NK
- EK - HH - Kith - WC - EC - WFrep (total of 12 zones)

Main Route 2 (existing): Halas - Everfrost - Blackburrow - QH - WK - NK
- EK - Beholders - RunnyEye - Misty - Rivervale - Kith - WC - EC - WFrep
(total of 15 zones)

Main Route 3 (proposed through Lake Neriuss): Halas - Everfrost -
Blackburrow - QH - WK - NK - EK - Beholders - RunnyEye - Misty - Lake
Neriuss - WC - EC - WFrep (total of 14 zones)

Southerly Route (existing): Halas - Everfrost - Blackburrow - QH - WK -
NK - SK - Lake Rathe - Rathe Mts - Feerrot - Innothule - SRo - Oasis -
NRo - EFrep (total of 15 zones)

Northerly Route (proposed, with two zones between Lavastorm and
Permafrost): Halas - Everfrost - Permafrost - NewZone1 - NewZone2 -
Lavastorm - Nektulos - EC - WFrep (total of 9 zones, 8 if the entrance
to NewZone1 is in Everfrost and you bypass Perma altogether)

Unfortunately, as these examples illustrate, even though the new zones
would be for high-levels, those wishing to become invis-to-everything
can make a very fast run to Freeport from Halas if they avoid getting
atacked. You'd have to add enough zones up there to make the trip have
at least 12 zones, or Verant would likely not consider it on game
balance grounds...and since Verant will likely not put up 6 new zones
just to have a northerly access...catch-22.

There are solutions to this, and even though they'd not likely be
considered, it's fun to discuss them...we might be surprised given that
Gordon reads this ng.

Solution #1: Add another zone for high-levels up there, but don't link
it. Unlikely solution, as many of the other high-level zones are
under-utilized right now, and this would be a zone for the sake of
adding a new zone.

Solution #2: Add the zones and link Lavastorm and Perma together with
them, but since this is designed to be a high-level hunting ground
mostly, make the zone between the two new ones be a teleporter or some
such behind locked doors that are guarded by some nasty mobs who will
drop a key when killed. The key would be LORE/NO-DROP/one-use also (kind
of like dropping a quest item on an NPC, but this time it's a door).
This is more like it for game balance. The t-porter stays open long
enough for a group to pass through, so it's kind of an added game
economy there, and if the mobs guarding are level 35-45 (super stone
guardians, maybe?) then it's like a trainer for plane break-ins.

Just a few thoughts,
