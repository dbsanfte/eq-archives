This is the part where I think Aradune completely misses the boat.
Lots of NPCs hate Trolls, Necromancers, Elves, etc., but not PCs.
Making Rogues a shifty untrustworthy class is fine as long as the
PCs can trust each other.  In a pen and paper game, the thieves
don't steal from the other PCs and the assassins don't kill them.
Everquest is a PvM game, not a PvP game.
