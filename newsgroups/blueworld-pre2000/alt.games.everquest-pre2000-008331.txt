Oasis is a good zone, but it has three problems.

1 - SG & Spectre Hunters.  As long as they don't pull things through
crowded areas, you're fine--safer than fine, since you're not likely to
have an SG rampaging for an hour or two--but now and then there's some dork
who will kite UP the beach.  This is especially deadly with Specs since
they like to come in multiples. :)  Still, though, unless you're on a
server where people are just rude, you shouldn't have much of a problem
with this.

HOWEVER... If you kite, too, you're much more likely to run into someone
kiting a spec or SG away from the beach.  So watch your back.

2 - Over crowding + Bugginess.  Oasis is, in fact, *such* a good zone that
it's overcrowded at every level.  Not only can this make it hard to find
something to kill, but it greatly increases the chance of you being able to
kite--or even pull--a mob without someone else jumping on it.

It's not rudeness, though--it's just that they see a snared croc "ambling"
along and assume it's up for grabs.  Add to this the fact that Oasis is
very buggy (teleporting mobs, mobs embedded in sand dunes), and you have to
be very understanding if you're accidentally KS'ed and very apologetic if
you accidentally do it to someone.

3 - The loot sucks.  You'll never make your 19 spells by hunting gators,
and Orcs and Dervs seem to be perpetually camped.

Given all of that... I'd say that for you, as a Druid, go to North
Karanas.  It's a highly underrated zone with lots of good stuff.  You *do*
have to be careful of the occasional roaming giant or griff, but the guards
at the stone bridge are good, and there's usually some high levels in the
zone (either hunting guards or teleporting in and out) who'll be glad to
take that griff/HG off your hands.

And you've got lots of room to kite if that's your pleasure, merchants,
three places to zone nearby, and for a quick fix of cash, there's the
occasional wisp and a camp of raiders at the shore who sometimes drop
bronze. :)
