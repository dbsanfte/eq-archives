Gordy,

I was rather frustrated last weekend.  Not only was it my first time on EQ,
but after more that two years since I moved out of state, I would be able to
roleplay again with my friends that I had left behind.  We had all decided
to make new characters on Bristlebane and form a party.  What a dead end
that was.  A really disheartening experience.

I do understand, though.  Where I work we have often tested new software,
program changes, new or patched OS's or DB engines.  Often we test these
things for a month or more before they go into production.  After all that
testing and nothing breaking we move them to the production machines just to
watch all hell break loose.  The truth is that it is very difficult, if not
impossible, to duplicate a production environment on a test box.  You just
never know until it goes 'live'.  To quote many people, "The final test is
in production".  That's when you'll know if it works for real.  Some bugs
you just cannot find on a test machine, no matter how much you try to make
it look like the real thing.
