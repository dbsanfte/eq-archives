I liked that post a lot. And it's true that the NPCs don't really make the
game worth playing it's the other people. Just last night a new female
barbarian shaman told me she "idolized me" just because I helped her out
when she was new for no other reason than the fact that I remembered what it
was like getting beat to death by a polar bear cub while people ran by on
their way to here or there, not even asking if I needed help. Then later I
gave her a lot of stuff I didn't need and was just cluttering my locker,
nothing to me but a small fortune to someone just starting out.

Am I bragging about my good deeds? Yep, a little, but only because there
were a few who did the same for me and it wasn't really a "good deed" to do
the same for someone else to me, it was more of a duty. A duty not to ignore
someone in need just because I didn't know them. A duty to give what I
didn't need to someone who really did need it rather than sell it for a
profit or leave it collecting dust in my bank. And seeing her health bar
down to almost nothing, and her running in circles while nobody helped I
couldn't just run by. I knew how awful it was for me when I was in the same
spot. Now she asks me questions about being a shaman, and if I'm not in a
life or death situation I am glad to help her out with any pointers I might
have. I used to try and be this way with people in UO, but usually if I
tried to help them out it ended up getting me and them killed because we
"shouldn't have been outside town". Not the kind of place I want to be a
part of, but EQ sure is turning out to be just what I'm looking for. To each
his own.
