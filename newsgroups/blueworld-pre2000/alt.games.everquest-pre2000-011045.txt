Since my short dip onto RZ, I've used the floating camera view. This works
out very well for me because I have never used the default keyboard lay out,
except when forced to in the tutorial.

Sadly enough, from the beginning I've been playing with the "Quake" layout.
One hand on the keyboard, one hand on the mouse, since the beginning. So to
see around all I have to do is hold the right mouse button and spin the
camera (I love my track ball).

It makes it very nice for seeing things sneaking up on you, etc. The only
draw back to this is that the graphics appear better in the first person
view. And you notice how short your charecter is, which can take a gett'n
used to..
