Yes, he said that, but it is incorrect.  

Hell levels do not help keep groups together.

Say you are starting 29 when your friends are starting 28.  You
all play about the same, so you all hit the next level at roughly
the same time.  
You hit 30 when your friends hit 29.  
Your friends hit 30 when you are halfway through.  
Your friends are halfway through 30 when you hit 31.  
Your friends hit 31 when you hit 32, so you are right back where
you started.

All that hell levels do is hide the fact that people are far apart
in experience totals.  If you are under level 30, you should try 
not to group with people who are just over 30.  Grouping with a 
level 31 when you are 29 is like grouping with a 29 when you are
25, except that the difference in effectiveness is greater 
between 25 and 29 than between 29 and 31.
