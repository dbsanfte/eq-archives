I'm not sure, whether or not this has any relevence to your points but I
thought I'd just spew out something that just came to me. I believe you
were complaining about all the actions in the game having absolutely no
meaning. Creatures spawn, they don't mate and produce offspring so who
cares if you kill that orc right?

Anyway, what if, there was a shard that had some all encompassing goal,
that all the players could work towards and that failure would result in
destruction of the shard. What if say Baja, had a month to equip an army
to fend of the demon hordes swooping down on Baja's sosaria. Perhaps, they
would need to send spies into the camps to see what plans the demons are
making. Send assassins to kill off rival demon leaders. You don't like the
petty humans and their troubles, join up with the enemy and infiltrate
human encampments. Maybe the enemy pays more for your bows, so you can
profit by betraying your kind Perhaps, there must be a quest to find
ancient knowledge to help and discover weaknesses. But in the end, if the
shard is not ready, it will be destroyed, another world lost. BUT the
whole point of ultima online is that it is supposed to be hundreds,  even
thousands of worlds. So, we lose one world, we just have to try and save
the next one.

Anyway, I think I've blabbed on long enough. I don't think something like
this would be difficult to achieve, and it might give people more of a
sense that their actions have meanings.
