Every item has a trivial skill level.  When an item becomes "trivial" you 
can no longer gain skill by making it.

However, that level is not related to the chance of failure.  The chance
of success for making any item never gets above about 95%.  Many items
become "trivial" when you still have a 20-40% chance of failure.
