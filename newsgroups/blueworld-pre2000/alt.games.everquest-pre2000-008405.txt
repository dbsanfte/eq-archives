As I have said before, Biguglie is a borderline name and as
such I would tend to accept it.  It is certainly a better name
than the "Missalot" we talked about a while back.

If your name is changed by a GM you can petition the change -
either asking for your original name back (Missalot succeeded
at that, which I think was a bad decision on his part because I
do not like that name) or asking for a better alternative
name.  Scarie Spyce on E'ci was changed to Scara
somethingorother, which she hated and changed to Scara
Drinkalot, which was a bad name.  Last I heard she was
petitioning to become Scarlotta Rose, (a name that I like a
lot.)

So, the original troll should probably work up a better
alternate name, and then decide if they would rather petition
to get biguglie back or petition to get biugl changed to their
new name.
