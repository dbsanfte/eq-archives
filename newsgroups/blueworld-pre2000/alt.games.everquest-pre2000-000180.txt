Rules of behaviour are set by society and the people who live as a part of
it.  These rules are set by the people and exist without requiring the
presense of a god.

To even sugest that setting rules for behaviour require a god or other
devine being is pure fallicy.  There a many societies that have lived and
survived without the belief in a god.

The belief in god/s or other beings provided for gaps in our knowledge and
understanding of our enviroment.  Religions provided a method of setting
social values in an enviroment that was unpredictable and difficult to
understand.

Belief is an individual idea and an "adaption" to our enviroment, having a
set of rules to live by provides society a static enviroment in which what
happens next is predictable and therefore healthier for the individual.  It
is a "survival characteristic" 
that we have developed to improve our individual survival.
