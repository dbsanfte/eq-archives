The guards do not wander, but the fairies do.  If you take a
troll to Faydark expect to get jumped by Fairy nobles, royal
guards and the like on a regular basis.  These little ladies
range up to the mid teens in levels and they will find you if
you are hunting in the woods.  If you want to be KOS by the
Neriak warriors guild you might try taking your troll to
Crushbone but I would not recommend running a troll in the
newbie garden or on orc hill.


>By the time you are level 12-15 you can visit the tree city.

Of course, it will be a real pain to learn any new skills
before then.

If you really want to try this remember that there is generally
a trainer standing outside and behind the emerald warriors -
trolls can not fit through the single doors in Kelethin so the
bank and many of the stores will not be available to you.

Free lots and lots and lots of wood elf slaves to bring up your
merchant faction.

The whole thing is worth doing if you like a challenge, but I
would not recommend it to anyone.


As for the barbarian, I would suggest making sure that all your
stats are 75 or higher and then allocating them as you see
fit.  Strength helps you with the heavier armor, but better to
pick a combination that fits your notion of the character than
to follow some min-max formula.
