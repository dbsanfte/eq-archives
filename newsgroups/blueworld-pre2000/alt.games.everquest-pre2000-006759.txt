I think I may have had someone attempt to pull this one on me
before...at first I just thought they had ants in their pants, and
gave them the benefit of the doubt.  What happened next made me
reconsider...

I was in the Seafarer's Roost in East Freeport.  I was auctioning a
few pieces of cheap jewelry I picked up from evil eyes (polished bone
hoops, etc).  A druid, about level 14 or so tells me he's interesting
in buying them.  As I'm digging through my backpacks to find them,
suddenly he starts dodging to the left and right every 2 seconds.
I've heard about this scam before, so I decide to give him a piece of
copper to open the trade window, before going any further.  Sale goes
off without a hitch.  

A few minutes later he asks me if I want to buy two greater
lightstones for 14-15pp.  I get ready to put the plat in the trade
window, then think better of it.  I right click on the items...a
greater lightstone and a REGULAR lightstone under it.  I inform him of
his "mistake" and end up buying the GLS for 7pp.  For some reason or
another he ends up giving me the regular lightstone for free...perhaps
he felt guilty for trying to rip me off.
