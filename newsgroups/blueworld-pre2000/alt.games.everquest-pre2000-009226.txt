Here is a brief description of Shadowrun: It is a science-fiction
fantasy, or techno-magical pen and paper RPG created and loved for
many, many years.  The second edition rulebook came out in 1992!    

A better description from the Shadowrun Pub:

"Shadowrun is a role playing game set in the future of earth. The year
is approximately 2060. Magic has returned to the world, along with
elves, dwarfs, orks, and trolls (along with other metavarients). This
all occured after December 24, 2011, when the dawning of the Awakened
world had come to pass.

Mega-Corporations rule the world now, instead of the governments. The
mega-corps rose during the late 1990's, aquireing much more money than
the governments. Civil unrest also added to their ability to take over
the world so easily.

Many long-forgotten creatures returned to the world also. Some being
dragons, basilisks, griffens, and the phoenix.

Technology has made many advances. Cybernetic implant are available
for a price, and the Matrix, the world-wide computer network, is there
for all to use...be it for legal or illegal purposes. "
