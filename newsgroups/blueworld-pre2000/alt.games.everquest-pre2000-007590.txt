I am realy enjoying this card now. You are right, when there are artifacts
or problems, you just tinker it down and find each game's maximum as well as
the system maximum. I know Quake 3 on my system does't like the cpu above

and I am still way over the stock settings. Wth as much heat disappation

with it if it does. That's the risk you have to be willing to take when
doing this and so far it's paying off for me.
