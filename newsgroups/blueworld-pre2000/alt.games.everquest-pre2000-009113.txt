Were you of a proper level to get exp from the croc?  If so, it's a bug.  If
not, however, there's an area beyond which you don't share exp with your party
because they're far enough away that they're not considered to be 
"participating" in the battle when the critter dies, thus no exp.  This is 
probably to prevent a player from sitting in safety at a zone entrance and 
being levelled by buddies out killing things for him.

If _you_ should have gotten exp, too, though, the only other explanation is
that you jumped a wounded beast that someone else had already done 50% or more
of the damage too.  (So near as I can tell, this includes NPCs as well.)

Barring all that, it's a bug that should be reported.
