There are people who are good and friendly.  If you give them consent
to loot or drag, they will bring your body to a safe place.

There are people who are selfish.  If you give them consent to loot,
they'll do it and keep everything.  If you give them consent to drag,
they may drag your body somewhere safe if it does not inconvenience 
them.  They probably would not bother to drag it somewhere dangerous.

There are people who are malicious.  These people will take advantage
of any hook you give them to mess with you.  They'll loot you clean
if you consent them to loot.  They'll drag your body somewhere nasty
if you consent them to drag.

Personally, I think most people (probably 80%) are in the first group.
Of the rest, most are in the second group.  There are a lot more 
people who are out for personal gain than who are out to mess with 
others.  Changing /consent to allow dragging but not looting would 
thwart the selfish ones and limit the malicious ones.
