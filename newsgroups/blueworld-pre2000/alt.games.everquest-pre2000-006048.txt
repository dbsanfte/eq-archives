*sigh* the eternal druid quandary.

I think that most druids over 30 try to solo as much as they can.  They want
cheaper DD spells with less chance of fizzling.

The Evoc druids' main argument while grouping is that we get our last heal
at level 29, and that at high levels that heal is best left to clerics, and
that they don't heal all that often anyway.

Although I really do prefer being support, instead of the star, I chose Evoc
because "everyone else was doing it."   I was not happy.  I ended up going
to the Temple of Ro, giving a 140p gem to the guy next to Genna (I forget
his name) for him to wipe my specialization.  I quickly trained Alteration
up to 51, and I'm much happier now.

At level 35 I am often primary healer.  That will probably change at some
point, but backup healing is a critical job as well.   And when things go
all to hell (even the best groups have problems, such as the occasional
train caused by some other group), SOMEONE has to get their butts outta
there.  Evacuation costs 300 mana.  I BELIEVE a fizzle costs 150 mana
(please correct me if I'm wrong).   I don't know about you, but there are
enough times where I may not HAVE 450 mana to spare (or 600 if I get a
dreaded double fizzle).  I do my damnedest to always make sure I have enough
mana for one Evac...I DON'T WANT IT TO FIZZLE.*


So, if you're basically a soloer, choose evoc.  If you're dedicated to group
support, choose alt.  If you screw up, know that you can reset your
specialty at least once (I've heard you can only do it once, but I'm not
about to test it).


*- The cost of a spell is figured AFTER it has finished casting...you must
have 300 mana free to cast the port, even though it may only end up costing
you 250 once the port is done.
- You always have at least a 5% fizzle rate, no matter what.
