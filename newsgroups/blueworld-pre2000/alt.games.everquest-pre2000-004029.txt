>When trying to advance a skill, do you get more advances by trying to do
>make something that is just above your current skill in triviality or are
>any more challenging items just as easy to advance with?  I know you won't
>be as sucessful, but does the skill advance just as fast?

I  found that for  tailoring it does. 
I was getting basically no raise most of the time
off cured silk masks which trivial at 80 skill I think,
I had 54 or so skill. So I started doing reinforced gorgets
which take higher skill, but I found that on the gorgets
nearly EACH TIME  i  succeeded I gained a point of skill.
On the cured silk masks I'd get 45 swatches = 45  attempts
and I raised skill 3 points  or so (with 84 int on my druid)
On the reinforced,  which is somewhat easier than getting spider
silk since the east commons right outside freeport has unlimted
high qual cat pelts on vendors it seems, I got about 20 or 30 pelts
and making the gorgets rose skill something  like 10.
