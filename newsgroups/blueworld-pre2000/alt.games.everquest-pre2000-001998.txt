PvP in EQ sucks.

Look at something as simple as a monk - Feign Death is worthless.  But
that's ok because I can run out lootless, fight til I die, run RIGHT
BACK into the fight and do it again, and so on.  Compared to Joe
Warrior there who gradually loses all the gear he NEEDS.  Probably
loses it to a naked mage, as far as that goes.  And none of us can
beat the mage anyway because he'll just
nuke-nuke-nuke-invis-med-med-med and then start nuking again.

And that doesn't even begin to address the power difference from low
to high level characters.

EQ needs to be redesigned from the ground-up to accomodate PvP:
including faction hits for murder but not for self defense, and more
of a penalty than "you can't go to town" since, as happened in UO,
they'll just get a mule to do it for them.

All that for one PvP server?  It'll never happen of course.  But if
you like that sort of thing, then UO is a *much* better choice than
EQ.

What EQ's PvP server *is* good for, or was anyway before Verant put in
faction hits, was the mindless, rampant, antisocial playerkilling -
which some people *do* like - that players used to be able to get in
UO and can't anymore.

Except on the Abyss shard anyway.  Which now, thanks to this faction
hit nonsense, is a better system than EQ's for the fans of mindless
carnage (and I don't mean that as a slam - I played on the xmas server
mass death and killing and had a blast, I just don't want to play that
way ALL the time, but some people do).

What EQ is really, really good at - Grouping with old and new friends,
and exploring *dangerous* places and killing *challenging* mobs (which
UO doesn't have, unless you deliberately let the mob have at you,
which is silly).

But if I were into PvP then I'd go back to UO and join SBR's Order
stone or something.  I wouldn't look for it in EQ.
