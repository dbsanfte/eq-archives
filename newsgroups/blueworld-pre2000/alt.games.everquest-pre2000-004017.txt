> This is, in fact, erroneous. I give Gregor Nasin the Stein Quest
> ingredients one at a time, and it has worked every time (except for when
> Gregor was out of his place and not open for business... He would give

Yes, it works that way.  The point is that you can't just hand him the
ingredients as you come up with them and expect the quest to work when
you hand him the last.  All he remembers is which items he has been
given out of the items that he is looking for, not who gave them to him.  
This is a fixed size list.  Once he realizes that he has received one of 
each item, he hands out the reward to whoever gave him the last item and 
then he clears his list.  If there was someone else standing next to you
doing the same quest at the same time, they probably would have messed 
you up.
