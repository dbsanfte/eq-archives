It is more than the trees.

I am running a K6-233 with 64 meg of ram and a Voodoo III 2000
with 16 meg of video ram.

I can run around in empty parts of warsliks woods or the swamp
of no hope with clipping set to a moderate distance and all is
well.

If I end up next to, say, a ruin with 20 goblins in it I need
to turn clipping down.

If I want to look out for a distance I stop, turn clipping all
the way up, then bring it back to move around.

The odd thing about Kunark is that I used to just set my
clipping plane low and forget about it.  With Kunark I am
constantly adjusting it so that it is as far out as possible
without visibly impairing my running speed.

The good thing is that I now know to bring it ALL the way in
before doing my next arrow fletching marathon.
