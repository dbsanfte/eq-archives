Yes, sence heading, Forage, even Feigh Death, while they maybe annoying to
have to practise, but are things you can easily practise during normal
downtimes. I don't understand anyone argueing about how "hard" these things
are to increase. You need sence heading, then while running, fighting,
medding, sitting, chatting, boating, fishing, casting, buying, training, you
tap the Sence Heading box. Eventually you are so good that you get a heading
almost every time. Feign death can easily be practised during normal
downtimes in a group. Forage, again, can and should be practiseed anywhere
and anytime. Combat and casting skills practise during normal play.

Main skills that need to be practised in certain places are things like
baking, brewing, etc. The "trade" skills. And many of them can be practised
during normal downtime through the purchase of sewing kits, fletching kits,
jewlery making kits, spits, etc. Most skills can be practised anywhere you
are using items you can buy. These can be annoying but don;t keep you from
the game.

I can see the annoyance in trying to practise a skill like pick lock. You
have to find a locked door to pick and this can be far from normal play
areas such as dungeons that are very green to you adn far from cities and
places youd normally hunt. To top that off, they are fairly rare. At least
the needed items for trade skills (forges, ovens and the like) are in cities
you have to visit fairly regularily anyway that aso serves to scatter them
throught the lands. This needs to be fixed, and can be with some work by
Verant. Small practise lockboxes or varying difficulty (and price) could be
made available for sale to practise on during normal downtimes thus
eliminating the need to find a door way off the beaten path in an area
boreing to you and stareing at the beautifull wood grain for hours on end
listening to the world go by. Would also be a small money sink for the
Rogues as they trade up to better and better locks at higher prices while
hteir level and skill increases.
