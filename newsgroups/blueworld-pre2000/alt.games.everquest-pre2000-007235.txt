kg wrote:
> 
> Being a level 10 wood elf druid, which would i get more mana from, a +10
> mana earring, or a +3 wis earring? Is there some formula for how much
> mana you get from each wisdom point?

Yes, your total mana is:    (WIS/5 + 2) * Level.

So, if you add one point of wisdom, you get (Level/5) mana for it.
At level 10, you get 6 mana for 3 wisdom.  At level 20, you will
get 12 mana for that 3 wisdom.  I would probably choose the wisdom 
earring over the mana earring because it will be more useful in the 
long run.
