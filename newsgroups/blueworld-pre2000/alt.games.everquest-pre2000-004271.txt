Morgan wrote:
> NBarnes wrote:

> > Enchanters have to choose between alteration
> > (almost everything but mezzes) and conjuration (mezzes).

> Alteration is where your mana is going at critical situations
> and non-critical situations.  I very rarely run out of mana
> doing mezzes, and if I am going to run out it's because we are
> overmatched.  Specialization Conjuration would let us flail
> about for another minute before being forced to retreat.

 What alteration spells do enchanters have that are that critical
in borderline situations?  Charms?  Nukes?  In general, I'd
think that Spec: Conjuration would allow you to keep a higher
pace while pulling and contribute more to group efficiency.
 However, you'd know better than me, Mr. Enchanter.

> > Necros between alteration (lifetaps), evocation (nukes), and
> > conjuration (most DoTs).

> Necros want Alteration.  They get exactly one nuke that affects
> creatures other than undead (and it is a Conjuration).  Although
> their disease and poison DoTs are Conjuration, their heat blood
> and life tap spells are Alteration.

 Ignite Bones is evocation and a not unreasonable nuke.  However,
you're right that most want Alteration for lifetaps and their
best DoTs.  Conjuration is definitely a weak sister, but solo
necros who kite a lot might like it.  Might.

> > Magicians between conjuration (their second-best level
> > nuke, magic based and thus harder to resist) and evocation (their
> > best nuke, fire-based and much easier to resist).

> Magicians generally want Evocation.  Most of their conjuring is
> done offline, with an opportunity to med, while their evoking is
> done at critical moments.

 I'm not going to say the the ability to summon cheap elementals
is at all a good reason to get spec: conjuration for a magician.
However, Shock of Swords, their 44th lvl nuke, is both a shock,
unlike Lava Bolt, and is magic-based, making it potentially
more attractive even if you have Lava Bolt.  If you spend a lot
of time fighting resistant mobs (and who doesn't, on the planes?),
Shock of Swords starts looking better.

> > Clerics, shamen, and wizards are much more cut and dried,
> > though.

> Evocation for Clerics or Conjuration for Shamans are about as
> useful as Conjuration for Necromancers.  There is a choice to
> make, but I think it is pretty cut and dried for most classes.

 Agreed.  Caster's Realm speaks of a wizard who went spec:
alteration for better evacs, but that's certainly a mistake.
