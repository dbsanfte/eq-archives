With SLI you will get either better performance at the lower resolutions
(800x600) or you will get support for higher resolutions (1024x768) at the
cost of performance. There is no free lunch. In 1024x768 mode the graphics
should "look" better but not necessarily run faster. Look at it this way,
Voodoo II in SLI mode is the performance king (for now). You can't get any
better than that!!
