Hear are a list of some of some of the non class specific bugs I can think
of, and im sure others can post more.

1:  The Group bug.  Damn annoying and Im sure everyone has experienced this.
You invite someone into a group but you get the message that they are
already in one, when actually they are not. This results in the person
having to log out and back in.

2:  Invisible Players. This seems to be coming more common. Some players are
Invisible to some players but not others (Nothing to do with having an Invis
spell on them) . The Invisible player or the ones that cannot see them has
to log out and in to get rid of the bug. (or they have to zone, which
sometimes fixes it)

3:  Various Pathing Problems. The NPC pathing problems are ridiculous in
some zones, this can cause nightmares for players with pets and Im sure
everyone remembers being hit at the top of Aviak city by an Aviak that is on
the ground. They must have long arms. It can work to players advantage
sometimes though, like in Upper Zig in Cazic.

4:  Link dead players. Ok, so people go LD, you can expect these problems in
an internet based games but why can other groupmembers not heal or invis
etc. LD players. Why do we have to stand there and watch our comrade die?

5:  NPC Rules vs Player Rules. Perhaps not really a bug but It does need
looking at. Several things I have noticed are :

      A : Kobald Shaman and Froglok Shamen casting SoW indoors. This really
is totally Unfair.
      B:  NPCs hitting for extremely high amount with FS weapons.. e.g. How
come an Ogre Bouncer can hit for 90 + with a FS longsword. A players Ogre
Warrior of equivalent lvl or above could not hit for anywhere near this with
this weapon
      C:  NPC casters hardly ever get interrupted. A player Caster has very
little chance of getting a spell off when hit more than once but NPC casters
can cast without interruption while being hit by 3 or 4 player tanks.
      D: NPCs mana supply. NPC casters seem to have a massive amount of mana
compared to players when of equivalent class / lvl.

6:  Stacked Quest Items and Broken Quests.  I know lots of players that have
been caught out by stacking quest items when giving them to the NPC to
complete the quest. The NPC thinks you have only give them one item and you
therefore loose all items. Very expensive in the case of gems. Also , I see
lots of new quests put into the game before the items needed to do them are
in place, (e.g. the Raincaller Bow, the Gnoll slayer) so players spend lots
of time trying to do quests that cannot be done until a later date.

7:  NO DROP bug. This is where a player loots a backpack off his corpse that
has a no drop item in it. If he has no room in his inventory, the backpack
falls to the ground, but because it has a no drop item in it, it disappears.
Everything in the backpack is lost.



These are most of the main bugs that I can think of. I am sure there are
more.  These are fundamental bugs that should have been fixed months ago but
still have not. Instead, all Verant seem to do is make changes to the
different classes. The bugs in the game that effect everyone are the most
important ones, why are they not fixed first ??
