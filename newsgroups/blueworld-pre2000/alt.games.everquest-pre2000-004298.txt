This would be an example of a guild that I would not join.  In my opinion,
a guild should be a circle of friends, not a hierarchy where some people
are "above" you.  Some poor sucker is going to be the guild leader and get
stuck with all the nasty administrative tasks (sorry Q) and a few people
will be officers so that the leader doesn't have to do all the footwork
when adding new people to the guild and such.  But nobody has any rank 
over the other people in the guild.

I saw many people try to start guilds in UO because they wanted to be at
the top of a large structure.  Others join these guilds for a sense of
belonging.  Inevitably, those higher in the organization get more of a vote
often giving the guild leader dictatorial power over his peers.  I find the 
idea that anyone would take pride in the rank they have achieved in such an 
organization to be frankly repelling.
