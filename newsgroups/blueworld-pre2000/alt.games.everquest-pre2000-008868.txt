You don't mention whether your druid is DoT'ing.  I certainly hope he
is -- at level 24 he has Creeping Crud, which has over a 2:1
damage/mana ratio.  Over 200 damage in a minute is nothing to sneeze
at when you're in the low twenties.

The druid should damage shield the puller BEFORE he goes out, DoT the
main mob as it comes in, Snare everything in sight, then sit and med.
Emergency heals as necessary.  Nuking is to be avoided if possible.
