> dollars US and 500 in game pp.

Because it would diminish the reputation of the game.  People like
to feel as if the game is fair.  E-bay makes the game seem less fair
by putting nice items in the hands of people who have real life money
rather than any in-game merit.

If Verant were selling items for money, first I would think they were
money-grubbing and lose respect for them.  But also, it would cause 
real life concerns to intrude into fiction.  When you are striving for
a sword from some really nasty monster, you would also be thinking 
about it in terms of hourly wages.
