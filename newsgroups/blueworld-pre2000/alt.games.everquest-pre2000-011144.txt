I think it is not particularly useful to raise AC as a mage.
The to-hit curve against AC for monsters near your level looks
something like this:
    ____
___/

That steep part moves up as you get to higher levels.  Warriors
with reasonable gear for their level lie right near the steep 
part of the curve, so adding AC greatly diminishes their chances
of getting hit.  Mages, even with quite good AC gear, lie in the 
first shallow part of the curve, where adding AC does little to 
protect them.

The only time your armor will make a difference is when you are
being pummeled by things that are so deeply green to you that 
the steep part of the curve lies near your current AC value.
