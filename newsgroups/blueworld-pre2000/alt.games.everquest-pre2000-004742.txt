Clarity has not been nerfed.  It is still as effective as it has
ever been.  Enchanters are still welcome merely for Clarity, even
if they are complete idiots with regards to the rest of their spells.
Even manastones are really only abusable by clerics, and they would
still like to have Clarity as well.

I appreciate the message that this protest is trying to send, namely
that whining gets results regardless of the underlying issues.  If
the Necromancers can get all kinds of additions to their class just
because they whine about it, then why can't we?  

However, lets all be honest.  Enchanters are a very powerful and 
useful class.  There are a couple of spells that could be improved
(such as our short term and long term buffs stacking with each other
and Tashania being upgraded), but there is no justification for a 
walkout.
