I have found that there are "sweet spots" for skill advancement... If
you try something that is much too challenging to you, you will still
get your skill up, but more slowly than if you try stuff that is only a
bit above.

I could be wrong, but I think that the reason for this is that you may
have, say, X chance to get a skill advancement upon success, but only
X/2 chance to get an advancement upon failure, and therefore, the more
you fail, the less your skill advances.

This is all from personal experience, and could be completely wrong.
