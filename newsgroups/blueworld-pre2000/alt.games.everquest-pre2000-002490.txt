Shaky <non@none.org> wrote in

> Ever consider letting players transfer their characters ONLY to
> another shard, leaving all material possesions behind? Players thake
> their experience, skills, and knowledge with them, but nothing else,
> essentially arriving naked in the new world. Might soak up some gold
> and items, and free some house space.

I had suggested this once.  My idea was to have a gate open for 30 minutes,
once per real life week.  When you stepped through, (after a warning!) your
character on that shard would be locked from play.  One week later, you
could log on to the shard the gate led to, and your character would be
available where the gate was on the original shard.

All possessions on the original shard would be deleted, including houses,
boats, bank items, etc.  The only thing that would be transferred would be
your gold.  You would lose everything else, including your armor and
weapons.  Just newbie clothing and gold.

I wanted the gold transferred because it would give the players an incentive
to sell their houses instead of giving them to a friend.  This would create
some housing turn over, and the lost items would help reduce hoarding.
Characters going to another shard would know they would arrive naked and
helpless, and so would sell off anything they could so they could use the
gold on the new shard to re-equip.

The once a week was to prevent casual "server surfing" by characters once
they reduced everything to gold.  Also, it would give OSI a week to move the
characters, so they could run the process at some slack time rather than try
to do it on the fly.
