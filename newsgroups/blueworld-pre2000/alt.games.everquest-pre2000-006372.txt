There isn't really a good solution.  If you leave the damage credit,
then the Enchanter can just deal half damage to a mob, memory blur 
it, and let someone else finish it off to get credit.

As it stands now, an Enchanter has to memory blur it and then do at
least half of the remaining damage to get credit.  I have done this
at times when a wounded mob was dragged onto my group and one time
when another group sniped a named mob that my group was waiting for
in Sol B.

If you see an Enchanter making a habit of stealing kills with memory
blur, petition them.  It's a very handy spell and I would hate to
lose it because it was being abused.
