blatantly violating that regulation - they say the software is "for free"
and then as soon as you get inside the site, they say, "well not really,
it's 4.95 for X and Y and Z, but hey, what do you care?!"

5 bucks is entitled to 15 dollars + attorney's fees under the UDAP statutes.

least a 100K fine, along with any other issues. Finally, the civil lawsuit
could probably easily see puni's at 100K or more...just for the fraud. All
in all - this little puppy is probably lookin at 300-400 grand in civil and
criminal liability for it's little deceptive website.... it just irks me the
way they cavilierly said "Free" and then "oh not really..."
