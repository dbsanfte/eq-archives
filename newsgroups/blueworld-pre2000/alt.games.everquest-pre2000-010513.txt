that is either hard to find, too easy for you, or
quite risky for you, and there's always the wandering
druids ready to slice you to ribbons for fighting
back when a wolf aggros on you and you try to
defend yourself.  Tripling your pet's damage certainly
helps a lot, though.

I don't reccomend buying a stack of weapons; you buy
one, hand it to your pet, he uses it until he dies.  Managing
your pet well, he doesn't die unless its saving your life
from (Holly, Sand Giant, whatever) and that's what, a
couple gold down the tubes. Big deal, one run from
Surefall to Qeynos with the mail.  Trick is not to
get cocky, he hits as hard as a level 12 pet but takes
damage like he's still level 4 (he is, after all)

Even after 24th, no buying weapons; summoned daggers
activate the dual weild as well as anything else, and
nothing reasonably priced is going to increase damage over
what your pet does naturally.

I spend no more than a third of my time working on
experience; I travel, I help newbies, I play around...
but then there are the times I'm in the mood for some
fighting, and I like to push the envelope there, take
on tough stuff and beat it.  Show me the edge, I'll
take it; I'm likely to need it at the challenge levels
I tend to take on.
