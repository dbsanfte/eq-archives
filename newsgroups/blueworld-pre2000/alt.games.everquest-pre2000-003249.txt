Well, people recommended a lot of good reads...i really have to say
that the Dragonlance series (chronicles and legends trilogies mostly),
while meant to be really just a Dungeons and Dragons commercial, are
some of the most entertaining books ever.  I've read them many many
times.  They get the spirit of everquest in the manner of a group of
people getting together, all different classes (warrior, ranger, mage,
etc).  But they ARE a good story.

And for those who liked them, the authors (weis and hickman) also wrote
two trilogies that are good as well.  The Darksword trilogy and the
Rose of the Prophet series.  The Darksword series is good, very magic
based, different from the standard AD&D platform standards.  The Rose
of the Prophet i feel is a more mature read, based in the middle
eastern mythologies (djinns and ifrit and such).  Also it's chock full
of good names for characters.  ;)  not that we're supposed to do that.
Heck, the second book is The Paladin of the Night which has a group of
charaters very similar to Shadowknights...hmmmm...

Oh and you should read some Lovecraft too...hehe not really fantasy but
still a good read.  =)
