If every name that ever appeared in any book was banned, and
any homonym of every name that ever appeared in any book was
banned, there would be very very few names available.

The concept behind the naming policy seems to be: No pop
culture references.

The main characters from Lord of the Rings are pop culture
references, as are some of the secondaries. Very little from
the Silmarillion is a pop-culture reference.

My wife's warrior is named Ribayaz.  If he makes 20 (she has
been playing her bard lately) she will ask for the last name
Luz Y Norte.  She might get it.

Why?  a musician named something or other Ribayaz wrote a
treatise on music in the 15th century titled Luz Y Norte.

Now, Ribayaz Luz Y Norte is a very good name - it fits high
fantasy and fits the face (the human with the big moustache).
It is not a modern pop culture reference - it is a medieval pop
culture reference but that is a different story.

Or I play a high elf named Lynet, taken from the tale of Sir
Gareth.  Perfectly reasonable name.

I saw a bard named Taliesin the other day - is that too much of
a famous name or is that a perfectly good fantasy name?  I
don't know.  I only thought that it was a darn shame that a
character with a fairly cool name was begging in /shout for
plats and starter armor.  

The problem with a rule against pop cultural references is that
we all swim in different parts of the pop culture ocean.  I
suspect that if you and I compared bookshelves we might overlap
on about 30% of our fiction - which is a lot.  (maybe more if
you have read the Silmarillion).  And a random guide who shows
up when someone petitions the bard named Dizzie Gillespie may
not have any understanding of jazz.  

But the fact that there is a grey area does not mean that any
and all possible names is perfectly reasonable.  And the fact
that not everyone shares the same combination of pop culture
does not mean that in general pop culture references are not
appropriate.

The tougher call comes from people who want to play low-fantasy
characters.  There is a Snacko Bagodonuts on E'ci.  He carries
around a lot of pastries and hands them out to all around him.
It is a perfectly reasonable low-fantasy character, and is in
many ways more fun than yet another Jabober out camping the
hermit.  But it is NOT a high-fantasy name.  I would not
petition it, but it bothers some people.
