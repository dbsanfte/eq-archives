DEX has very little impact on the number of procs. Procs have very

At low levels you need a fast weapon to do damage, as most weapons are 
capped. My max hit with Gatorsmash was 25, average was about 20, same 
with Staff of Observers. The delay on staff of observers was twice 
shorter, hence, twice the damage

Correct. At higher levels the only use for huge hitpoints is to utilize 
complete heals, making them more effective. Other than that, noone dies

doesn't matter that the warrior has 2-3 times more hitpoints
