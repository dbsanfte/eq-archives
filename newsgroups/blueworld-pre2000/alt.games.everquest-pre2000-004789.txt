There are two different reasons for NO DROP.

For quest items, the item in question is really a token that
represents the fact that you have completed part of a quest.
By bringing it to the correct NPC, you advance the quest.  The
concept of having done the first part of the quest is not really
transferable, so neither is the item.

Powerful magic items are made NO DROP to keep them in the hands
of the person who earned them.
