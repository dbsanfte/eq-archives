A task swapping program for EQ may be a real acid test for the enforcement
of the no 3rd party programs rule. If this program does not already exist,
I suspect it soon will. I know I will be very tempted to use it, I bet
others will be too. And I would not consider this cheating, because in no
way would it interfere with (or even look at) EQ's data nor would it
attempt to access the EQ servers. Yet at the same time, my bet is
that the EQ folks would take a very dim very of the use of any such
program that bypassed one of their 'security measures'. It could be a very
interesting legal fight if it they actually started banning people for
using such a program. What exactly is a third party program... Where
is Bunboy when you really need him?

These are real questions, and there are no simple answers.
