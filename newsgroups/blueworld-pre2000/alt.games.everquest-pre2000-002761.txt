The big difference between UO and EQ is the execution.  In UO, you
gain "levels" (skill levels, but levels nonetheless) very quickly, and
can be wearing a full suit of plate within a week.  However, if you
ever die, you lose everything.  All your equipment will be picked
clean from your body before you finish your death animation, and you
begin losing skill levels at an accelerated rate.  In EQ, on the other
hand, progress comes MUCH more slowly.  You have to work, usually in
teams, for each new level, and the prices of armor and weapons are
nothing short of astronomical.  However, when you die, you get to keep
almost all your weapons, equipment, and experience points.  You only
lose items if you choose to be PvP, and the experience loss is
negligable compared to the skill decreases in UO.  Sure, there's a
chance your body might truly be lost forever if you fail to reach it
in time, but that really doesn't occur too often.

It all depends on what sort of game you favor.  The quick path that
frequently sends you back to the beginning, or the long, hard path
where you get to keep what you earn.
