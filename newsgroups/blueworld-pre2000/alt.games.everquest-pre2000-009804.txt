I have been playing EQ since the Beta days. Many of the above posts I agree with,
but the ones that say you simply hack and slash, sit, med, and repeat just never
got into the spirit of the game. Like UO it is about interaction with other
people. Very simuliar to the open ended-ness of Daggerfall you can explore for a
year and still not have seen it all, yet unlike daggerfall your not likely to
reach level 16 and get bored as your usually doing something different with
different goals as you level. You want to get that special item or complete that
quest. I am on the rathe, I have a necro dark elf, an ogre shaman, a wood-elf
ranger, an iskar monk and an iskar Shadownight. Each race plays very differently
and each requires it's own stratedy. probably the easiest character to solo is a
necro or shadownight but frankly, I'm doing okay with is iskar monk, Ogre Shaman
and Ranger too. At higher levels downtime is a factor, but as you sit and med you
can interact with your party as well, or go get a beer.
try it, your like it
