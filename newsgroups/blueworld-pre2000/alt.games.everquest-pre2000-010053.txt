The problem isn't programming your gamepad or whatever to replicate a
keystroke or command.  It's the repeated, unattended use of macros that
allow you to circumvent the learning process.  This gives you benefits that
Verant had designed to be gotten only when you were actively playing the
game (yes, I, as well as many others, will be watching tv, reading or
whatever as we mindless hit SENSE HEADING over and over, but it's still a
human at the keyboard).

Verant seems to have come to the conclusion that using this type of macro
throws of the balance of the game enough to try to stop people from using
them.  Further, they can NOT snoop your PC to see if you ARE using a macro,
they can only tell from what they see happening or not happening.

The analogy you try to draw with video cards is not really applicable.
Verant designed the game to display the action a certain way.  Better video
cards do not suddenly add more information, they merely make it easier to
show everything as they had designed.

So...use your brain:  feel free to use a programmable gamepad, joystick,
keyboard or mouse.  Just don't use them to run macros over and over, and the
GMs will leave you alone, because they won't be able to tell the difference
between that and your finger.
