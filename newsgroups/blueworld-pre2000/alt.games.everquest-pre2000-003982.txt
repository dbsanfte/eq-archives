I saw this idea brought up somewhere else.  Make the Shadow Knight pets be
the highest level they can be.  For example at level 49 a Shadow Knight can
summon a level 26-29 pet.  Instead of level being randomly decided, make it
always be level 29.  This should improve usefullness of our pet a little
bit.  A level 29 pet can dual wield for example.

Another idea for Shadow Knight pets.  Make out pets have the same amout of
hit points as Necromancer pets but leave the level and skill level the same
as it is now.  For example, at level 49, Shadow Knight pet should have same
amount of hit points (or at least more than they have now) as level 49
Necromancer pet but leave its level at level 29 and its skills at level 29.
This will make Shadow Knight pet capable of tanking a lot more but still not
deal out damage.

I of course do not expect the second idea to go anywhere but I hope it will.
Right now my pet at level 49 can take about 4 hard hits at most by a level
40+ monster.  That just does not make my pet usefull.  I saw a Necromancer
solo a frenzied ghoul the other day.  I can only dream about it.  I know
Necromancer is one of those classes that are supposed to be more soloable
but I think we, Shadow Knights, should be more soloable as well being part
Necromancer.  And we probably could be if the second idea is implemented.

Oh, and while I am on pet subject.  I think a new pet command should be
implemented.  Something like /pet ignore attacks.  This command would make
my pet ignore all attackers.  This could be very usefull when I need to
relocate while fighting.  I would tell my pet to ignore attackers and then
to follow me and move to a different (safer) spot.  Then I would tell the
pet to attack (/pet attack) which would cancel the previous ignore.

Let me know what you think.

Manr, level 50 Shadow Knight on Povar.
