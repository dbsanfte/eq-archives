I was in the same position game time wise as your self.  The main reason I
couldnt get into AC was the fact that once again, I was a Newbie.  I'd
played UO for a while and never even got a house.  (Not just unable to place
a house, but not able to afford one)

Before I lost interest in EQ, my main character (Talorc the Barbarian
Shaman) got up to Level 22.  (heh admitedly I did just wander around a lot.
And a warrior I "power leveled" (eg hung out in Black Burrow for ages) got
to 15 in about a week. WAY faster than Talorc)

But I just dont think I am interested in learning not only the interface of
a new game, but all the Game Play mechanics/ World Names, Areas and
Locations / Hints and Tips / Character Class permutations / and relative
Monster strengths just so I can be  a newbie character in a game world that
offers much of the same experience as before.

Plus I didnt find the character models very appealing.  The variability is
certainly a step up from EQ. (But still not on a par with UO.  The different
clothing options available there is excellent.  Even if all the 12 year olds
dress like what they think medievil pimps would look like). But the
Character models are , well pretty average.  Even though the character
models in EQ get a bit the same after a while, most of them at least look
pretty good.  They certainly had a "wow" factor the first time you saw a
Troll, or a fully equiped Dark Elf or Half Elf in Full Plate.

But in the end its going to be the World interaction that makes or breaks
this game - That is the Player base and how involved the GM's get.  If AC
turns out to be a genuinely changing game world, with truly ongoing plot and
changing World events/politics Ill be there.
