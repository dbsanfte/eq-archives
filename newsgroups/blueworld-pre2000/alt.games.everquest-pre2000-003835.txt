Sort of.  The "Game of the Millenium" poll actually isn't the first one. 
PC.IGN.com has actually been running the things for a few months now.  I believe
that at least three of the Ultima games were in the "Best RPG of the Millenium"
poll.  And if I understand it right, the entrants in each of the "sub-polls"
were selected from a list of write-ins submitted by readers.  The "Game of the
Millenium" list was compiled from the winners of each of the previous
categories, plus the two runner-ups with the most votes.  (That's why there were
two alleged "RPGs" on the list, FFVII and EQ.  One was the winner, the other the
runner up.  I can't remember which was which though.)

The problem is, that there are just more "new" gamers than there are ones who
played the classics.  I'd bet that a huge percentage of the people who currently
and actively buy and play games don't have a clue what Zork and Kroz and Bard's
Tale were, or at least don't care.  Heck, considering how much the gamer's
market allegedly grows each year, I wouldn't be suprised to hear that a majority
of "modern gamers" have never played on a system more primitive than a Super
Nintendo.
