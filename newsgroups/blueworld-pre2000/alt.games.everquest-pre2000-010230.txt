I also agree that it is not a good idea for Verant to wipe Test.
They are within their rights to do anything they want to the 
test server, but deleting all the characters is bad both 
immediately and in the long run.

It will be a long time before anyone reaches level 50 on Test
again, so a lot of high level areas and monsters won't be tested
for a while.  This could be remedied by allowing people to 
bring existing characters from regular servers to test.
(I would suggest that this be a copy operation, not a move,
and that the characters be brought over naked.)

People will not want to start new characters or work on existing
characters on Test if they feel those characters are likely to 
be deleted.  Nobody will put in the effort to build a level 50 
character when it is likely to be deleted next time Verant has 
an unrecoverable bug.

This time, Verant should wipe all items and money and try to
track down and delete all characters that should no longer 
exist.  They can probably do this on the honor system, with 
some stiff penalty for people who don't delete their illegal
characters and are discovered.

In the future, they should keep multiple full backups of Test
and roll back to earlier state whenever they introduce a nasty
unbalancing bug.  Clearly their backup system could stand to 
be tested as much as any other aspect of the game. ;)
