Morgan wrote:

> Harmony is an area effect Lull spell.  The big difference between
> Harmony and Lull is that if creatures see their friend resist a
> Lull spell, they charge you bringing the one who resisted with them.
> 
> The effect of Lull is to narrow the creature's range of aggression.
> They will still react to the pleas of their friends or to your
> proximity, but at a much smaller range.

 It's actually my understanding that Lull does _not_ work like that,
though Harmony does.  If you successfully Lull something, you can
con it and see that it's gone from scowling to indifferent.  Lull
works, I believe, by making a mob non-agressive.  Harmony, on the
other hand, works by reducing the mob's aggro radius (often to zero).
Both methods would work in theory, but because of it's AoE nature
and the fact that it's hard to resist and even if it is resisted,
it doesn't aggro the mob in question, Harmony is clearly the best.
