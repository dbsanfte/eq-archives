I wouldn't go that far, but it does have some drawbacks, definitely.  (Note,
I'm judging FSAA from my GeForce 2, not from a V5 5500.)  The problems I've
noted are:  text blurring, texture banding (REALLY bad in FoB on the night
sky, quite noticeable in Innothule at night on the ground texture, too), and
of course the noticeable speed decrease.  Nevertheless, quite a few things 
look VERY nice with the FSAA (notably the creatures in Kunark, which already
have much nicer textures on them).  I think overall the nasty effects are
more a bad interaction between the brute force FSAA implementation and the
original limitations in the EQ engine.

On a completely seperate note, I can't recommend the GeForce 2 highly enough.
The V5 might be slightly better on FSAA speed, but the GeForce has a much 
nicer lineup of additional features in case you don't like the FSAA afterall.
And I could find a generic GeForce2 32m for cheaper than V5s, to boot.

Of course, YMMV.
