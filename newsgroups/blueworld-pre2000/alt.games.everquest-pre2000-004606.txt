> I am looking for a used CD.  I do not want to pay full price on Everest.

Each CD comes with an account key, so nobody can sell you their CD
without giving up their account.  A person who was selling their
account would have to be very certain they would not want to play again,
because they would have to give you their characters or delete them.
(Most people would probably choose to delete their characters rather
than have someone else play them if there was not some financial 
incentive.)  If a person quits the game but keeps their CD, their 
characters will be saved by Verant for a few months in case they change
their mind.  So you see, it is unlikely that you will find someone who
will sell you a used CD on this newsgroup.

You would probably have better luck on eBay.  (In fact, a search for 
"everquest cd" turns up five auctions.)
