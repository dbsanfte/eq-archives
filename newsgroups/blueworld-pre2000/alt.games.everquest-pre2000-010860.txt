"Chris D." wrote:
> 
> While it does not happen very often, sometimes another group moves in on the
> room or area we are camping, from the orc camps in east commons to graveyard
> area in mistmoore, what should my party and I do? We are getting good
> experiance killing everything, and it is only fun because there is enough
> kills for our group. If another group moves in and wants to share a spawn
> what should we do? We would be getting half the original experiace, probably
> making it not worth our time.

It depends on why they want to share your spawn.  

If they are sharing your orc camp or graveyard spawn because 
overcrowding has forced them there, then you gracefully accept that 
you have to share.  Normally, I try to split up the spawns by group 
sizes, so if two people show up to share a camp with your party of 
six, you allow them 1/4 of the spawns.

If there are equivalent open hunting grounds nearby, but they could
not hold the camp without you there to kill the tough things, then 
you may ask them to leave.  Let them put together a solid group to 
break and hold the other camp.

> And the same thing with a long single camp, if I higher level comes 
> in when he hasnt spawned yet and I have been waiting for a couple
> hours what should I do, if I know this guy can and will killsteal
> me if given the chance?

If you are camping some long single spawn, then you can ask them to
leave.  I normally start by asking their intentions.  If they say
they want to see what spawns or time the spawn, then offer to send
a tell after you have killed your prey.  If they ignore you, buff 
themselves up, summon a pet, and settle in to wait, then petition a 
GM that you think the person plans to steal your kill.  Malicious
intent is often easier to gauge before the fact.
