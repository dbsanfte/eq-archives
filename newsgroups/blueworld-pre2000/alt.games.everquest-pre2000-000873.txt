1. Recent Upgrades to the Service

*Brought to you by Raph Koster, aka Designer Dragon, on behalf of the
UOLive Dev Team

We�ve added colored ore to Ultima Online! Eight new minerals of
varying rarity were added, each with a distinctive color. The
locations of rare veins have become prized secrets traded from miner
to miner. 

Mages are excited about the new skill of Meditation. This skill gives
the expert vastly increased regeneration of mana. The price of using
it is that you cannot wear heavy armor whilst meditating. Already
mages are dividing into two camps, the "pure mage" and the "warrior
mage," each with their own unique play style. 

We�ve added changes to the combat code to help differentiate weapons.
Mace weapons in particular have become deadly indeed, as they now have
the capability to rob the target of stamina, and they do far more
significant damage to armor. Your swing rate is now dependent on how
tired you are, so dexterity has assumed a major new role in melee
combat. In addition, ancillary skills such as anatomy are gaining
importance in combat as well. With these recent changes, weapons are
better balanced than ever before, and warriors are now finding
sub-specialties viable that were never practical before.

We have to mention treasure hunting! Monsters throughout Britannia are
now found to be carrying stained and battered treasure maps that
require a master cartographer to decipher. They lead to treasures
hidden throughout the land, usually guarded by groups of aggressive
monsters. Many groups have perished in the attempt to dig up
long-forgotten loot, and a new profession has been born: the
professional treasure hunter. 

Many of these changes were tested out on the Abyss shard, a temporary
shard that features no city guards and full player-vs-player action in
a game of castle warfare. This shard proved very popular, particularly
once a scoring system was added so that players on different teams
could keep track of achievement.

A lot of new additions have made their way into the game, and they
will keep coming.  If you want to keep an ear to the ground on this
kind of information, be sure to watch the website at:
http://update.owo.com and click on "Comments from the Team" and "Game
Updates."

2. Recent In-Game Events and Activities

*Brought to you by GM BEAST on behalf of the GMs

We premiered the Volunteer Appreciation Celebration last week, held at
two of our Counselor Guild Halls. We intend to have a regular series
of similar events where the players can meet the volunteers in Ultima
Online. This event will take place the first week of every month, as
we take time to honor the best Counselors, and new Counselors and
Elders are recognized. It was also a great way for players to meet the
people who work behind the scenes to make Ultima Online a better
place.

Also last week, we held our first Tour of Britannia. There are many
strange and interesting places to visit in Ultima Online, and many
exotic and colorful people to meet. Sometimes a player can forget how
large this world actually is. The Tour gives even the casual player
the opportunity to visit all of the more popular taverns, cities, and
inns with a tour guide!  In the future, we will be highlighting venues
each month and Elders will escort groups of players to these places.
This will give the players a better opportunity to get to know the
patrons of different taverns or the citizens of the different cities
and maybe even make some new friends along the way!

News about game fiction can be found on the Town Cryer, located at
http://town.owo.com.  Check it out!

3. The Second Age Membership

The vast majority of our UO players have taken advantage of the
specially priced UO:T2A upgrade and have become citizens of the Second
Age.  Currently, over 90% of our customers are members of the Second
Age, enjoying all the new features including the translation software,
the chat client, the new lands and monsters, and more that come with
this addition to Ultima Online.  If you've been holding out on
upgrading, why not join the rest of the players and check out the
Second Age? The price is very inexpensive, just US$6.99 (including
regular shipping and handling) in the US and Canada and US$12.99
(including regular shipping and handling) in all other countries
except Japan.  For more information, please visit this link:
http://old.owo.com/t2a/introduction.html

4. Upcoming Upgrades to the Service

*Brought to you by Raph Koster, aka Designer Dragon, on behalf of the
UOLive Dev team

The team�s major focus right now is to reduce the amount of
anti-social activity in the game. We have posted a proposal for
handling the thieves that fill many locations around the world. Our
plans divide thieves into two types, the "players-vs-player thief" and
the "adventuring rogue". The former will operate under tight
restrictions, while the latter will have an array of new features and
skills to play with, including the ability to disarm the many traps
that we intend to add to dungeons. You can read this plan at the UO
Update Center, http://update.owo.com, under the section entitled
"Comments from the Dev Team."

We are working on a similar plan to tackle the problem of the
so-called "blue PKs." Our goal is to eliminate the loopholes that
these players use to avoid the penalties of the reputation system.
Keep an eye on the update center, http://update.owo.com for details.

Other features planned for the near future include a revamping of the
guild system to include shard-wide guild rankings, the long-awaited
introduction of necromancy, the addition of an 800x600 gameplay window
option, and the permanent return of the Abyss shard. We�re pretty
excited about these additions, and hope you will be too.

5. Upcoming Events

*Brought to you by GM BEAST on behalf of the GMs

Something insidious is stirring in Ultima Online. For a long time, the
pirates of Buc's Den have quietly gone about their business of raiding
and plundering. They were never organized and often fought amongst
themselves, so their threat was never too large to Britannia itself
outside of the occasional caravan attack. That was until today. It
appears as if someone or something has arrived in Buc's Den, and many
of the pirates have chosen to follow.  Keep a keen eye on the Town
Cryer at http://town.owo.com, and on Buc's Den as something strange
has occurred, and, knowing the pirates, it *can't* be good.
