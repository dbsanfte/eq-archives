Fabulous idea. IMO, ogres and trolls are a bit overpowered right now (I
know, I know, it's still beta). If they had to spend a bunch of time
foraging for food, it might even things out a bit. Beyond that, though, as
you point out, this would add a lot to the feeling of roleplaying. Right
now, I notice a lot of people in EQ are choosing the various races not to
roleplay a different race, but because they like the stats they start out
with or because they think Dark Elves or trolls or whatever look "kewl".
It's sad, but design teams, if they want any level of roleplay on a regular
basis, must implement features that *demand* it, i.e. making various classes
and races have different needs, along with lots of other stuff. Of course,
UO never even pretended to require any level of roleplaying. That's just
something you do on your own, if you feel so inclined, and if the idiot next
to you doesn't kill you for being a "lame rper".

of that acronym being "Out Of Control"). Then again, go ahead and do so -
hell, new years is only once a year ;)
