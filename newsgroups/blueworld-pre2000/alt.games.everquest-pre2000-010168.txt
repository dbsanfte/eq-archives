I saw the other answers and think they may have missed your intent. You
asked how to spend the money.  Here's my $.02

Going for light weight, high strength and near-best AC (not best in game
type stuff) I'd recommend the following:

Executioner's Hood  (300pp  maybe?) good STR and AC
Foreman's Tunic    150  good AC wt is zero
Azure Sleeves          300, best AC sleeves before planar
Cured silk shoulders, sandals, cloak, leggings, etc about 50 PP for whole
set (2 PP per AC)
Wu's Fighting Gauntlets 50-75 pp   acts as a GLS too
2 amber earrings (STR +3) about 100 PP
2 Bracers of Erollis about 150-200 for both  (STR +5, good AC (5) for a
bracer)

if you shop well, get the Drakescale leggings instead of cured silk.

and get 2 of the Fire Wedding rings AC5 +55 hp each for the other 1000 PP.
You will need the AC hit points (the equivalent of 5 levels) in the
beginning.


Ask me tomorrow, I'd probably pick different items.
