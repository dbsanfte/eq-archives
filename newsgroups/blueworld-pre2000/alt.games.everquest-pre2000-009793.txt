FWIW, Karnor is about right: the MOBs have about the right number
of hit points, and hit about as hard as they should, but the train
factor and the weird aggro makes Karnor less attractive than a
dungeon on the mainland.

>90% of the trains in there are directly resulting from one person's
>pull getting mixed up with another's.  A bit of coordination or better
>yet, hunting away from the known pull paths will negate majority of
>the train problems.

Wishful thinking. The only way to avoid most of the trains is to
get off the path. Even then you end up getting screwed with them
unless you're very selective about where you pull from.

Aggro in Karnor is pretty nasty - things will aggro but not path.
So someone can do a pull past a lot of MOBs, which will aggro, and
get their pull back, leaving a bunch of MOBs somewhere that are
pissed off and who will train the next person to come close to
them.

The only time I've successfully pulled in Karnor with no trains is
when no one else was there or when I did my best to keep away from
everyone. Karnor pretty much sucks because of this, but it has some
really nice loot.
