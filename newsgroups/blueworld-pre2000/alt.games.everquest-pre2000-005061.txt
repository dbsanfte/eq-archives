I think the simpleminded approach to just look who's in the zone will not 
neccessarily give you an idea if it is a *good* hunting area. It very 
much depends on what hunting you are looking for:

  * solo: this is the hardest because it depends mainly on the 
          possibility to get a monster without training others, e.g.
          snake-ledge in BlackBurrow, Giant Cog in Steamfont, most
          undead circles, many zone boundaries, like NK/SK, Gorge/RE,
          Cazic, Solusek(?)

  * group and roleplay: Well if you just want to bash something and not
          die too often, then the main consideration is a high 
          availability of monsters, BlackBurrow first landing, Befallen, 
          Unrest, Cazic ... depending on your level a great many places 
          come to mind

  * powerlevel: Here the main consideration is exp per time, so you need
          a high respawn and a high exp/danger rating. Oasis (when not 
          camped =P), Everfrost (in the teens), South Karana (in the 
          10-20's) etc.

So even without the multipart layout of most zones you have the problem 
to find your preferrd mode of hunting. Nice data though.
