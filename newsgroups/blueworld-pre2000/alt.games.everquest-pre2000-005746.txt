 While turning on the Roleplay flag certainly doesn't necessarily indicate
that one is Roleplaying, it is what I use it for - and I have generally
found a larger percentage of players with the flag on tend to also be
roleplaying (in my experience, on my server, YMMV).

 I also wouldn't say the anonymity hinders roleplaying - perhaps even the
opposite. I cannot glance at a businessman and say "Hmm, that fellow is a
chartered accountant, of six years experience". I have to ask him. 

 Now, I can, perhaps, glance at a lady, and see a pager, leatherman, and
some cat-5 cable sticking out of her pants pocket, and think "Hmm, she
probably works in MIS, networking computers". In the same way, I can see a
anonymous character holding two steins, with a animation hanging about,
and think "that is probably an Enchanter of some experience". 

 I don't think having the flag off hurts roleplaying, however - it's
largely a convenience issue. I like the anonymous aspect, so people have
to chat with me to find out specifics about my character.
