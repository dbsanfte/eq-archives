Indeed.  Though we are sensitive to some people's religious concerns
regarding playing a character that worships a fictional deity, we still
believe that context, story, and role-play requires this in some cases.
Paladins and Clerics are very good examples of classes that are holy in
nature, and therefore require a deity to serve.  We provide many agnostic
choices for those who wish to abstain from role-playing that aspect of a
character.
