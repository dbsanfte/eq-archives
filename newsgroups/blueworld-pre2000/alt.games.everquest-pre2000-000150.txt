The existence of God is self-evident.

This means that His existence doesn't need to be proven.  However, because
of the closed minds of humans, we think we need proof.

If you hold a painting in your hand, do you need to have someone prove the
existence of the painter to you?  No, of course not.  The painting itself is
proof of the painters existence.  After all, the painting didn't just appear
out of nowhere.

Consider the building you sit in.  Do you need someone to prove to you the
existence of the builder?  Of course not.  The building itself is proof of
the builders exixtence.

For exactly the same reason, we already know of the existence of God.  We
have His creation all around us which proves His existence.  We don't need
proof of a Creator, we have the evidence of His existence, His creation.
