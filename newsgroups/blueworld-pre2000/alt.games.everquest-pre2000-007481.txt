Unfortunately, this has been shown to be wrong. No stat makes a meaningful
difference for a warrior. Highest STA you can get? You get a few % more
hit points. Highest STR you can get? You get about 2% more damage overall.
Highest AGI? Your AC (well, to be exact, your defense, since the displayed
AC is the sum of AC and defense) gets maybe 5-10% higher.

In other words, your stats have such a low impact on your performance
that race-specific abilities ARE the deciding factor. And there's no
more crippling racial ability than any form of night vision.

> moves.  If the stats are well balanced, then these are all valid

They are, since they make almost no difference. Highest STR possible
(once you go over the "cripple threshold" of 75) means you will end up
the fight with one less blow to make. Highest STA means you can absorb
one more blow before dying. Highest AGI means you will receive one less
blow during a fight (I'm oversimplifying).

Any class that casts spells requires maxing out its mana-stat (WIS/INT),
then maxing out STR to carry loot/equipment. The rest is useless.

Any class without mana (Bard/Monk/Rogue/Warrior) requires maxing out STR
(to carry armor, except those poor monks), then typically CHA to get the
best prices possible.
