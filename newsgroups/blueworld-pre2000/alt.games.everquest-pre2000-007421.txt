Oasis was idiot central last night.  I finally hunted "Orc Highway" 
for the first time.  For those who don't know, Orc Highway is a good
hunting ground for about levels 13-18, hunting "an orc warrior"s and
"an orc priest"s and the occasional terrifying "a orc raider", who 
is about 5 levels higher.  Sand giants are a constant threat, since
they tend to walk down the highway picking up succulent young 
adventurers and devouring them in two bites.  The only defense is to
be alert and have Spirit of Wolf.

An idiot (who happened to be a druid in wolf form) decided to kite a
sand giant through Orc Highway.  A sand giant that is being kited 
does not approach in the same leisurely manner as a sand giant that 
is wandering.  Instead it tends to fall from the sky on top of its
hapless prey with no warning at all.  Of course, the sand giant lost 
interest in the druid for a couple of minutes and stopped to kill
everyone who was hunting the orcs.  This made the idiot angry because
all those people were delaying his experience gain by making him come
back for the sand giant.  So, after he was done kiting the sand giant,
(his route was orc highway, gypsy camp, docks) he got even with all
those teen level fighters by training spectres on them a few times.

If I see him again, I will be watching very carefully.  I do hope,
however, that his actions of last night will be enough to get him
banned.
