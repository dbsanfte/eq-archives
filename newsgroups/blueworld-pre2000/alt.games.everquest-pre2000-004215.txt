Gordon,

    I would like to point out that this is not the way exp and looting
rights is currently working in the game.  Many many times my group has had
kills taken by solo necros or warriors.  I will give an example here but
many other similar situations have occured where a full 6 person group of
mine has had kills taken by solo people:

example 1)
    Saturday night in Split Paw on Rodcet Nife

    My group:  49 wizard, 49 warrior, 50 cleric, 50 necro + pet, 44
necro+pet, 41 wizard.
   other group:  50 necro

    My group and the solo necro were deep in Paw fighting the gnolls in the
Ishva area.  We were working together to get to the Ishva, he wanted summon
corpse, we wanted the robe.  We were able to  pull single gnolls.  For each
fight every member of my group threw as much damage as possible at the
gnoll.  The solo necro sat there and had his pet join in the fight.  All 3
necro pets involved had 2 fine steel daggers each.  EVERY SINGLE TIME the
solo necro got the exp and looting rights for that gnoll.  This happened for
about 10 gnolls in a row before we left.

Now there is absolutely no way that a single level 50 necromancers pet could
outdamage my group yet somehow he got looting and exp for everyone of the
gnolls we battled for.

So Gordon please explain to us the real way exp and looting is calculated as
it is most definately not by group damage.

Steve
