Recently Gordon stated that all new servers in the forseeable future
will be split-servers. I think this is a short-sighted decision, and one
that should be rethought. Reasoning follows:

1) There should be at least *one* new post-Kunark server now that
everyone has had a chance to "get the feel" of the new lands/races, and
can make an informed decision about where they want to play. Kunark
presumably brought a number of new people into the game, many of them
are undoubtedly now at the "wow, I wish I'd done (whatever) different
when I created my character" stage.

2) Quoting from Gordon (news post re: test ppl moving to Torv): "People
often start on new non-split servers because they want a fresh
environment. There is often a �race�, so to speak, shortly after the
server comes up to be the first character or group to reach high levels,
get good equipment, and do the dragons and planes. There are many people
who enjoy this fresh environment as it gives them the opportunity to
become a well-known and respected person on their server."  Well said.

3) Quoting from Smed (Computer Gaming World, July 2000) "Verant and Sony
have just launched the game in Europe and expect to add another 100,000
players in the next 3 months. We're adding servers like crazy."

4) If the game really has "no need" for new non-split servers, then I
submit that the game is dying. New players = new servers. No new players
= moving old player around on split servers.

5) New servers help keep the game fresh for long-time players.

Personally, I'd suggest merging the 2 PvP servers, since they're both
grotesquely underutilized... but however you do it, I think Verant
should make the next new server truly new, not merely another split.
