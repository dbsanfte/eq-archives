It's a worthy challenge, but one that is doomed, I think.

The trick is to be simultaneously:
	1) Accepted in Freeport to get the compendium
	2) Amiable with Clurg to get the stein

One is easy enough to attain. The second seems to require a way of
offsetting your faction, and usually, you need at LEAST one faction spell
(base faction spells give you +100 faction points. That's Alliance or
 Cinderella's Carillon).

The bar is hard, since it has different factions inside (not all NPCs are
of the same faction).

The illusions, if you need one, typically cancel the main factor of your
faction, i.e. your race. But if you have the wrong divinity, it still
counts against you, and no illusion offsets that. And divinity counts a
lot (if I'm right, divinity is a -150 to 50 faction factor, depending on
your target). Too much to offset with a spell.

Your ranger will never get it, I'm afraid. Wrong race (cancelled by wolf
form), but more important, wrong deity. An agnostic bard gets to indifferent
by being changed to wolf form, so a single carillon gives him the required
amiable.

The goal of the bard is to get enough faction to come and go in Oggok without
any "band aid", go bank there, and so on.
