   In games like Civilization, a computer-controlled player can build a 
certain team of forces, send them specific missions (destroy that Wonder),
and react to incursions on its domain.

   Imagine having a Civ-type AI in control of the Crushbone orcs in EQ.  
When players are identified, it would send orcs in teams towards them.  As
the players get better, it could upgrade the power and number of its troops,
and respond to player tactics (they're camping Trainer Hill?  Move a dozen 
centurions from the slave pits that way.).  And every once in a while it 
might decide that it is strong enough to stage a raid on Kelethin, providing
an automatically-generated server event.  

   There's no technical limitation preventing this kind of team AI.  The
responses and decisions are pretty simple, compared to strategy AIs today.
(Sample Goal for a Raid:  Kill guards.  Once guards are killed, move as many 
orcs as possible up the elevator pathnodes and into the trees.  Cease after
X number of orcs killed, or Y time passes.)  The big limitation is creative;
it's never been done before.  :)  Right now EQ has Individual AI but not 
Team AI.  But it will be done, someday.
