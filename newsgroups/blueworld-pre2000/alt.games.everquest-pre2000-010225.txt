At low levels, give your pet any high damage rusty weapon you can find.
As a pets delay is fixed, something big and slow will cause more damage
and the pet will still attack at the same rate.

In particular, rusty scythes and rusty two handed swords.  They are only
worth a couple of gold and between levels 4 and 8, can double the damage
output of your pet.
