> 2) How can i get a list of who is on for those just in my guild? there must
> be a "/" command right?

Not exactly.

There is the "/who" command that can give you a fair amount of 
information.  It recognizes a lot of strings: races, classes, levels,
"all" and "count".  The "all" tag makes the query include all zones.
The "count" tag returns the number of characters that match the query
without listing all of them.  Any string that is not one of those is
matched against guild names and character names.  If a person is 
anonymous, they will not match any class, race, level or guild name 
queries, even in count mode.  Guild names are not matched unless the
query is an "all" query, although they will prevent you from seeing
people in your zone when you do a local query.  This is a bug in the
/who command.

/who		- lists all people in the zone
/who war	- lists all warriors in the zone
/who 12		- lists all level 12 characters in the zone
/who 10 12	- lists all characters between level 10 and 12 in the zone
/who 12 war	- lists all level 12 warriors in the zone
/who 12 count	- tells you how many level 12 characters are in the zone
/who all war count - tells you how many warriors are in the world
/who all 34 war	- lists all level 34 warriors anywhere in the world

So, to answer your question, suppose your guild is called "Joiners",
the query:

/who all Joiners

will show all your guildmembers who are currently logged on and are
not anonymous as well as any characters whose names start with
the letters "Joiners".


> 3) Is there anyway to tell my faction status with different factions?  How
> do I know what my status is with a particular faction?  For example, how do
> i know who i have the highest faction with so that i can use their merchants
> for the best prices?

When you consider an NPC, including merchants, the way they look at you 
tells you how much they like you:

	warmly 
	kindly 
	amiably 
	indifferently 	- indifferent or better should get you good prices
	apprehensively 
	dubiously 	- won't buy or sell at all
	threateningly 	- will attack you
	ready to attack - will attack you immediately
 
> 4) is there an url, for a going list of prices for different things like
> enchanted jewelry and putting spells like SOW and Breeze on people?

There are a few sites that try to record player prices on items.  
Maximum EQ:	http://www.maximumeq.com/
Casters Realm:	http://eq.castersrealm.com/

For jewelry, you can get some idea by looking at the material cost:
http://eq.castersrealm.com/misc/jewellery/jewel_table.asp
The prices I quote are roughly what I use.  Others will have 
different rules.

Anything combined with cheap stones (up to amber) is around 2x cost 
because the people working them are usually learning the skill and 
have a success rate around 50%.  Gems between jade and opal are about
3x cost for platinum, 2x cost for the rest.

The high end gems (star ruby and up) have different prices according 
to the metal:
  Silver:	1.25 - 1.5x cost
  Electrum:	1.5 - 1.75x cost
  Gold:		1.75 - 2x cost
  Platinum:	3-4x cost (or more for really rare gems)

When purchasing spells, I think that 1pp is usually a reasonable
tip for simple spells like Spirit of Wolf, Bind Affinity, and
Breeze.  Spells that come at higher levels generally cost more.
Clarity is probably 2-5pp for that reason, teleports are 10-50pp
because they are such an inconvenience for the caster.  Metal
enchantments are 5-10gp, 1-2pp, 2-5pp and 5-25pp.

If you are auctioning to cast a spell for money, state your price
if it is fixed or accept whatever people offer as tips without
complaint.
