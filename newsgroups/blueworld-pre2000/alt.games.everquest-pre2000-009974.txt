He wanted to give me his money. ;)  When I refused, he asked what
he could give me, so I asked that he bind people who need it.  

I think he took it as an immediate request, which is not how I meant
it.  I know what a pain it can be to try to get a bind.  I always
try to bind people who need it, even if it is pretty inconvenient
for me.  My hope was to put the thought in the back of his mind that
he should be nice and bind people because I was nice to him.

I've helped out low level characters a lot (not the beggars of course!)
and they usually feel happy, but guilty, at accepting whatever I want
to give them.  When someone seems to feel that they really have to
give me something, I always ask the same thing.  "Some day, do the
same for someone else."  It makes them feel ok about accepting the
gift and it makes me feel better about everything. ;)
