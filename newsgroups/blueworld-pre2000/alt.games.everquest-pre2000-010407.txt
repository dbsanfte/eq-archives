I agree that Enchanter armor should not be improved.  We have a lot
of power and we pay for it, in part, by being extremely frail.

> You need brains to play an enchanter succesfully and that is what I love
> about them. Well played we are the gods of Norrath. badly played you're a
> nasty stain on the ground.

Even well played Enchanters end up as nasty stains on the ground with
rather annoying regularity.  The mez spells are a huge taunt and you
always cast them before anyone else has had a chance to get the 
monster mad at them.

When three huge nasty things charge your group, what do you do?  Do 
you Tashan or mez first?  If you mez, it might be resisted and then
you are up a creek.  If you Tashan first, you start out up a creek 
and you haven't even tried to mez yet.  

If you can get a stun off, that will buy you enough time to mez if the
stun isn't resisted, but certainly not enough to Tashan and mez.  It 
also might attract the attention of the third monster that nobody is
hitting and then you aren't getting anything off.

Mem blur leaves you still being hit and still trying to mez the thing.
It's a great spell for getting you off the hate list after debuffing
the primary target, but not too useful for the initial mez round.

I have found two things that actually keep me alive for the mezzes.

1) Big cleric buffs.  Having twice as many total hits gives the
   healers enough time to save me.  The healing also taunts the 
   monster off so I can mez it. ;)

2) Having someone run interference for me.  Lately my paladin 
   friend has started rooting, stunning and taunting the thing
   I am trying to mez.  None of those will break the mez if it
   is successful, but all of them give me some breathing room
   to get my spells off.
