>Why should players be penalized for just having a little fun?

My take on it is this: the players weren't penalised for having a
little fun, the players were penalised for having fun at the expense
of another player. I doubt most Guides would care that they were
killed, since it doesn't really inconvienence them, but if one does
take offense to it, then you're in trouble.

The idea behind it is that fun is a good thing, but it's a bad
thing to have fun at the expense of another player. That's why KS
on some servers is warnable, offensive names are warnable, and
player harrasment is warnable.

Having fun at the expense of a Guide is just asking for trouble,
IMHO.  If the Guide takes offense, you will be at least warned,
and most likely a GM will come down and put the smack on you after
one offense. The reason for that is because Guides are volunteers,
there to help people, and if you piss 'em off enough, they'll quit.
Verant doesn't take kindly to people who make Guides even think of
quitting.

Personally, I just feel they should be more thick skinned. I see
no crime in killing a Guide. I think it's kinda funny. But them's
the breaks.
