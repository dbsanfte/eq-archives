It sounds like you are a considerate player.  I don't need to run your 
test because I don't think it is profitable to play selfishly either.  
I loathe the attitude I described above, but there are a lot of people
on the Rogue discussion boards who advocate that kind of behavior.
The person I was responding to said that he thought it was ok to pick 
the pockets of monsters that other people were fighting.  He was also 
defending Rogues who steal from their group by saying that they don't 
take very much.  He claimed to have gotten a gem at some point, but 
didn't mention how he shared it with his group or how the people he
stole it from reacted.

I still maintain that there is no legitimate use for the skill.  Rogues
already taunt plenty with their backstab and high damage output.  Any 
loot that you take either would have been gotten by your group, in which
case pickpocketing is pointless, or it would have been gotten by another
group, in which case you are being a jerk.  Why would anyone want to 
keep the skill?

If the pickpocket skill were removed, I would probably try playing a 
rogue because they are great fighters and I like the hiding/sneaking 
aspect of the class.  I would also include them in groups quite happily 
because they are very effective.
