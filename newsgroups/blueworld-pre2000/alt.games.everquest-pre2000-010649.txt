>
> For mages, before level 16 (although there's only
> a marginal improvement from 12-15) and after 24.
>
> Regardless of what weapon you give a pet, it keeps
> its original delay.
>
> As far as damage goes, a pet has a base unarmed
> damage.  If you give them a weapon and their base
> unarmed dam < 2*(weapon damage) then they'll
> get 2*weapon damage instead.
>
> So, prior to level 12, the best pet hits for 10
> unarmed, give it a rusty 2-hander (dam 9) and
> it starts hitting for 18's.
>
> After 24, mage pets get dual wield, so you give
> them 2 weapons (any weapons will do).  Unless

THe only reason to hand a summoned dagger
to a low level pet is to enable it to hit magic only
creatures, notably the will-o-wisp.  Otherwise,
the damage won't beat the normal damage of
the pet, either get it a harder hitting weapon
or don't bother.

A Staff of tracing is actually of marginal improvement
to a lev 8 to 11 pet, so this summon is a little more
worthwhile, as it conserves the cash you might otherwise
put into rusty...  but I've found the cash to be worth
spending if you are going to be keeping the pet around
for a while.

Do _not_ hand your pet any weapon that has the
same damage but lower delay, this is a complete
waste of cash, in some cases large quantities.  A
rusty two hander may be had for 3 to 5 gold.  Any
weapon that costs more but has no better than a
9 damage is a waste of money for this purpose... the
pet ignores the delay of the weapon at any rate.
