I like the earlier stuff he's written, but most of the Discworld series
lately has been subpar. Kinda like Piers Anthony's early work just before he
completely lost it.

In terms of pure fantasy...well, I love most of Salvatore's books, Wheel of
Time 1-5, and the Assassin series (I think the author's last name is Hobb),
plus 99% of everything Feist ever wrote. Harry Turtledove's work is great;
he's more of an alternate history guy, but he has written a lot of fantasy
stuff as well. And there's also the Recluse saga, by L.E(?) Modesitt.

The best fantasy world setting for me, though, has always been Moorcock's
Elric saga. Elric is *the* tragic hero archetype, more or less, although
it's hard to explain why without spoiling it...

(Oh, and Planescape: Torment. Yeah, it's a computer game- but it deserves to
be a Moorcock saga; it's that good. There are three endings- the second one,
the most involved, is better than anything Squaresoft has ever done, which
is saying a lot.)
