How about using a second mouse?
The Windows functions all assume there's only one mouse -- everything from
mouse drivers hooked to interrupts to the pointer functions to the video
drivers are all just as intractable as apps that assume there's only one
'focus'.  So you can't just add in a second mouse driver on a Windows
machine.  But you can write your own little device driver; the OS doesn't
even need to know that that thing on COM2 is actually a mouse.  If you write
your driver to treat your cheat app's window as the entire screen (meaning
its little pointer can't move out of the cheat app's window), you can create
a scheme that's actually a whole lot simpler than standard mouse drivers.

USB ports also open up the possibility of using a second keyboard in a
similar way.
