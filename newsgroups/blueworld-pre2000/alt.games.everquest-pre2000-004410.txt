It's not a stack overflow, just a ring buffer. Stack overflow would
be bad. :-)

Anyway, the hate list and damage list are seperate, but the problem
still arises because the damage list is also too small for larger
excursions.

And what you suggest would lead to the problem of KSing wizzies
again. Nothing is ever going to outdamage that IC in the length of
time it takes for the hate list to fill up on a plane-like excursion,
so the IC will remain at the top and the wizard will get credit
for the kill.

The only way to really fix it is to bump the size of the hate list.
The other solutions that I've heard just cause other problems, and
never correctly attribute kills to the group that did the most
damage.

It's all kind of moot, really, since when planes get done, or
dragons, it's a co-operative venture, so people don't ninjaloot
and try and keep stuff to themselves.
