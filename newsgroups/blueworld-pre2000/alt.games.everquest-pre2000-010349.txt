Ok here is my 2 cents

First off having played with my wife (31st season cleric) and alpha loot and
FFA and no greed If one person gets greedy kick them out of group. You
should be rolling on loot that is worth selling for good money.
If they refuse to let you loot then simply state I will not heal until I
loot.  There are many groups that will run alpha loot so that EVERY one gets
a chance a looting.
I play prime healer with my druid all the time since there are not enough
clerics where we are fighting. I am glad when we got a cleric and I do
everything to make them happy (keep sow and strength buff and skin like
steel on them at all times if they are lower than me and if they are even or
higher I take their buffs. and keep them in SOW (spirit of wolf). And I ask
when I enter the group first questions are
is there a looting order?
do the healers get to loot?
are we rolling on items that are worth trade or big money

If I don't get the answer I want
yes  either alpha or FFA
yes They do 
and something to know what to do on rolling items you loot it you keep it
(alpha loot) or FFA and we roll on all items.

If I don't get them answers I back out of the group.
There are lots of groups out there that will do it the fair way or make it
fair for the casters.
Today's groups the higher you go NEED a cleric for revive, resurrects, with
partial and all experience. I as a player want to keep every cleric happy I
know I will need there services and I know they will need mine

With alpha loot there should be no questions or guesses on who's loot it is.
The leader (should be healer since everyone checks with you on mana) should
keep the list and let the other players know who's turn it is to loot.
If the healer (cleric, druid, shaman) is not the leader, Then you need to
simply state I want to loot and will not heal until I get one out of every 6
(if there are 6 players in the group) 5 for 5 players and so forth.

So to answer your question what do you do if your not including in the
looting order? 

Leave the group find one that will let you loot
or 
Tell them your going to loot the next few and keep all items or start
including you in the looting order.

Its just that simple.
