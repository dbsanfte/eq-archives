Most higher level dungeons are split up into rooms + corridors between
them, corridors have wandering spawns and rooms have fixed spawns (often
including a named mob with special drops). Each room is usually camped
by a group, and it is considered kill stealing to pull from a room that
is already camped.
So giving the person who pulled the mob a big bonus to looting rights
would make it even easier to steal a mob from a camped room... and
believe me it can be *very* annoying when your group has been camping
for hours in the room with the rare spawn Frenzied Ghoul in it, he has
finally spawned you are just getting ready to pull, and some guy comes
in and pulls it away to his own group camped in a room down the
corridor... I've had it happen to me where the stolen frenzied then
dropped his rare drop the Flowing Black Silk Sash, worth (if it ever
sold for cash) around 5000-8000pp.
Slightly annoying :)
