On Wed, 3 May 2000 18:52:38 -0400, Tinarandil <afdns@gaads.com> wrote:
>I'll just do a pros and cons list -

    Just wanted to comment on a few small points...

>Everquest:

>Cons:
>Inferior graphics

    As opposed to what?  I played EQ for about a week before playing AC to
see
what the differences were.  I'd have to say that the character graphics are
better in EQ, the overall textures are better, the models are better.  
The trees are better in AC and the long-distance graphics in AC are far
better.  There is nothing cooler, environmentally, than leading a person to
a 
hill, pointing to the South and saying, "See, there's the road you need to 
take", point to the East, "to that town over there."  I'd /love/ to be able
to run with render radius at 25 all the time instead of 5 but my Celery
won't take it.

    Point is graphics is a relative term.  I like the graphics in both for
different reasons.  Surely, of nothing else, the cartoonish graphics for 
inventory in AC could be improved upon.

>Bad lag

    Never encountered it.  OTOH AC has been lagging for me lately because of
the updates.  YMMV.

>Really bad handling of PvP (people who go PK are too restricted and will
>rarely find opponents)

    How so?  "You suck?"  "Oh yeah?"  "Yeah!"  "Well, step into this
arena and let's settle this!"  ;)

>Bad landmass (It's broken into "zones" like in MUDS, but each has long load
>times between each.  Travel is boring)

    I dunno.  I rather liked swimming and just the pure coolness of sitting
on
a boat and it taking me to another land across the sea.  I much prefer that
form of travel to the AC portals by any means.  

>So combat oriented it's practically Doom Online

    Uhm, no.  The games I played the most before EQ/AC was the
Counter-Strike
mod for Half-Life and Unreal Tournament.  EQ was quite refreshing after
those
two and is by no means even /close/ to "Doom Online".

>Asherons Call:

>Pros:
>Great graphics

    Again, subjective.

>Great people (lot less assholes, most were filtered out by Everquest and
>Ultima)

    Well, so far people have been more prone to grouping than in EQ even
though
everyone says it isn't "needed".  OTOH I still see people walking around
saying, ICly, "I got my strength up to 85 finally.  Only 3000exp more to
level
10."  "Yeah, I might have something on my mule to help you with that."  
Alliegence talk, Fellowship talk, tells are all fine, IMHO for OOC chatter
like that, but /NOT/ normal talks when people can be walking by and pick
up snippits of the conversation.

>Great land mass

    Lousy water.

>Cons:
>Not enough of a user base

    I dunno, 13k users at any given time is large enough for me.  I just
wish
there were more quality RPers out there.  Maybe when I get up to the higher
levels.  I will say that people are more apt to RP in AC than in EQ.

    My assessment of the two is that they are just different games that
address different segments of the genre.  IMHO both have strengths and 
weaknesses.  I prefer AC.  Even with that, though, I find that I want more.
Maybe in the next generation of RPGs.
