The color of the gem signifies the area of effect of the spell, not
whether it is beneficial.

Yellow = self only, no target
Red = single target
Blue = area effect around caster
Green = area effect around target
Orange = ??? rain spell?
Purple = ??? group?

Some of the icons have a second level of information in the form of a
colored halo around the central picture.

Red = offensive
Blue = buff or helpful spell

So an arm with a blue halo on a red gem is a beneficial spell that can
be cast on others.  Snare should be a boot with a red halo on a red 
gem, which would appear to be a boot with no halo. ;)

It would be nice if they designed a whole slew of new gems.  It can't
overrun your texture memory, because you can only see eight of them
at once!
