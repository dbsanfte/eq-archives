It's not just a cute system to encourage cooperation.  It's a 
structured part of the world and a measure of your character's
advancement.

Xenomorph wrote (in another post):
> some people feel its not fair that some magic items only work if
> you are of a certain allegiance rank

This is what makes it not optional.  Your ability to advance your
character and use nice items is dependent on how many people you
can get under you.  Given the way the pyramid works, only a small
fraction of the population can ever be two or three ranks up
(unless you can have allegiance mules ;).

People complain in EQ about the separation into haves and have-nots.
This happens merely because the game changes and some things get
harder to achieve or acquire.  In AC, this separation is hard-coded
into the game mechanics.  It seems unlikely to me that a newcomer
to the game, or even a casual player, will ever be able to achieve 
the highest ranks, and this is by design!  At least in EQ everyone
can get to level 50 eventually.
