Personally, I've been hoping for one or two zones to connect Lavastorm
mountains to Everfrost peaks.  Its a serious trip to head to
Permafrost from most anywhere in the world, still a long hike from the
nearest druid/wizard teleport spots... my vision was of a zone that
held both ice giants and hill/fire/sand giants who would roam around
and attack each other.  This would 1. add another place for high
levels to fight, 2. give us an alternate yet dangerous route to
permafrost, and 3. allow the Neriak inhabitants another group of
newbies to pick on if they are thus inclined.  There is already a
travel loop from freeport to quenos through the south... how about
adding one in the north as well?

Of course I'd love to see ANY new zones... I'm rather split on
paineel/the hole at the moment but it could be interesting.  I
understand that one of these zones is aimed at level 40+ characters,
and that will hopefully lower the populations of Lguk an SolB... but
being so far out of the way is rather demanding of 'groups' going
there, rather than going solo and picking up a group.  

Also, I've always wanted the mintoaur caves in steamfront to lead to a
tough dungeon... get those damn guard killers away from my guards and
somewhere that wont cause my young bretheren grief when they're
running away from all those spiders that jumped into the fight.
