Sigh. Whatever. From my experience with EQ I found the game to be nothing
more than an endless stream of frustration, the down time, enforced
grouping, absolute need to camp everything from experience to treasure just
break the game to me. Asheron's Call have its own problem but they're not as
profound.

 I wasn't too impressed with the game during the beta, and only played the
retail version because I got it as a gift. Being an EQ addict I found AC to
be rather dull at first. The "town" seems totally barren, there were only 3
people in my outpost, the terrain and looks great but the character's model
are rather crude. I wasn't totally thrilled to say the least and tossed the
game aside...playing it once in a while only releave my frustrations with
EQ. But the more I played AC the better I like it. I made new friends,
discovered more lively towns, got better equipment and basically had more
fun adventuring than I ever did with EQ. My level 25 AC Monk not only looks
much cooler, but I also had more fun and had more adventures with him than I
ever did with my EQ level 41 Necro and level 33 Paladin combined.

Thats what it comes down to folks, fun and adventure, something that
Asheron' Call can offer but Everquest can't.
