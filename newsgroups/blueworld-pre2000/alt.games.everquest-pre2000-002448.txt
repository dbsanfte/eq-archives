I agree with this definition.  However, I don't think spells fit that
definition.  Spells aren't something that takes money out of the game; they
are an investment so that you can make more money.

Milk and muffins are a drain.  You eat them; they are gone.  There are lots
of drains in UO (almost everything has a drain), but the faucets are turned
on too high (one in particular).  Spells are not a drain.  You spend
something, you get something, which turns into more money.
