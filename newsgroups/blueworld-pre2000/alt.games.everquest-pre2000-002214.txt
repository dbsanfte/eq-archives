                                   -=The Swords and Sorcery Forum=-

Greetings.  If you would rather go straight to The Swords and Sorcery Forum,
here is the link - http://tssf.icestorm.com/ .

The Swords and Sorcery Forum is a place that deals in all aspects of
medieval swords and magic.  Currently, the purpose is to research and
provide references to a certain subject.  Many of these are games, such as
Total Annihilation: Kingdoms and Everquest.  As long as it has metal and
wooden weapons and a touch of majick, it might be here.

At present, we have covered big name games such as multi-player smashes,
Everquest and Ultima Online.  We also deal in many types of genres like
Gemstone III in text-based games, but since most sword and sorcery games are
role-playing, the majority of game are RPGs.

We, by no means, cover only games.  We have links and more links to places
that sell metal and magic books, like the famous Lord of the Rings, by J.R.R
Tolkien, a favorite of our visitors.  In addition we inform you of the best
places that sell real weapons, whether its swords, shields, or full-body
plate armor.  Lastly, we cover the important issues like decay of the RPG
from its original form and into its current fighting mindset state in
Zsaru's controversial article, What happened to the RPG?.

We have much in store for the future including covering many more subjects.
Of the like will include Full scale role-playing events and conventions that
can range up to 4, 000 people.  We will also be informing more of Pen and
Paper CRPGamers and the games they do love so much.  That and much more is
in store for the Swords and Sorcery Forum!  :)     HTTP://TSSF.ICESTORM.COM
