Please allow me to tear into your sorry ass.  Thank you.

1) Pulling.
Second only to monks (distant second, but second none the
less).  Lull just doesn't work often enough to be reliable, so
forget about that.  In fact, the ONLY reason monks are
better is because they can pull indoors.  Outdoors, unless
your monk is damn good, Rangers are the way to go.

With a partner or two, my 28 Ranger buddy can break the
Unrest basement at full pop, because of the way harmony
works.  Most Rangers don't seem to know that Harmony
is a damn-near unresistable lull spell which *lasts about
80 seconds*.  Theoretically, you could harmonize half an
outdoor dungeon before the first cast started to wear
off.  Basically, this gives you free run of an area, and lets
you pull whatever suits your fancy.  /em awaits the 'nerf the
rangers' follow-ups

2) Taunting
No, not that stupid little button you melees punch every time
it pops up.  Rangers have some of the most unbelievable
taunts I've ever seen.  When a ranger leads off with a snare,
it actually takes work to make the mob leave him alone.  They
also get damage shields, and NOTHING annoys mobs more.

If I were to get a mob angry at me, and the ranger needed to
get it off me fast... I hope to god he has something from the
"Flame Lick" line memmed.  The taunt value of that miniscule
DOT/debuff spells is enough to get the attention of all but the
most frenzied attacker.  Again, not everyone knows that.  But
they should.

3) Self-heal and snare
Don't even get me started.

Bottom line is that no tank can take control of any situation
like a ranger.  And don't tell me they can't take hits.  They
seem to do just fine when I'm around.

The only Ranger that is useless in groups is the kind with
extensive brain damage.
