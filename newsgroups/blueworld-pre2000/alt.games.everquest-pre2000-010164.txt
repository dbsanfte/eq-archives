Back when, everyone used alpha loot and started with the first letter 
most of the time.  If your name started with "Aa", then you would end
up getting a slightly larger share of the loot most of the time 
because you would always get at least as many loots as any other 
person in the group.

If you do this for nice items, you will often only find two or three 
nice items for a whole group in a night of hunting, so only the first
few people on the list would get them.  In the long run, this is very
unfair.

Now, if someone has a name like "Aahloot", you know that they are a 
selfish and inconsiderate person and you probably would not group with 
them at all.  If they have a reasonable name that starts with Aa, then 
they might be selfish or they might just have not thought about it.
If they object to running the alpha loot backwards, then it is probably 
the former. ;)
