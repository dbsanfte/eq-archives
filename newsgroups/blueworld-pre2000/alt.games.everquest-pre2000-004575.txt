I don't believe that a necro pet is twice as good as a shaman pet, have you
had your pet dual a necro pet since the patch? Perhaps there is some huge
disparity with the 49 pets not seen at 44, I'm not sure.

At level 44, pre-nerf, I had my pet duel a Shaman friends 44 pet three
times. My pet had no daggers (which is why its at all relevant) and they
were both given alacrity by the shaman. The shaman pet won every time, with
20-30% health left. If my pet had been dual-wielding his fists its
reasonable to assume he could have beaten it, bearing in mind that dual
wield is not double the amount of attacks, its more like +25% at best. At
the time it disturbed me to see how much our pets sucked without weapons,
even more so because it seemed that this nerf was pretty much inevitable.

Post-nerf, I would say our pets are marginally better than Shaman pets.

>In addition, Necros retain most of their tricks, Fear,

Fear is definately useful in very specific situations. It's a very important
part of the soloing necros strategy.
But it is
- useless in dungeons
- can be a mana hog (100 mana for effect that could last anywhere from 1
second to around 30 seconds)
- not usable where there are assisting mobs or pathing problems
- certainly not unique to necros- clerics, enchanters and SKs all get the
same fear spell we do.

Personally I'd trade it for the drowsy line in a heartbeat.

> Engulfing Darkness,


The damage on this isnt great, and its not usually worth risking the mana on
the high chance of a resist. I was still using clinging darkness, the level
4 version, at 46 (when i retired my necro). It is good to have a snare-like
spell, but the only real use is to slow down feared mobs.

>great DoTs,

Yes we have reasonable DoTs. It made some degree of sense for us to not have
the best DoTs in the game (despite our class being built on them) when we
had powerful pets. Now we have neither, while Shamans have a pet almost as
good as ours, better DoTs than we do, can use appropriately powerful DD
throughout their lifetimes if they wish (necros get two DDs, ever), and the
most powerful debuff spells in the game.

> *hugely* under-rated DD (given that it is based on Poison resits).


The poison DD you mention may be underrated because it is 160 damage for 100
mana. As someone who is often mentioning mana efficiency and the uselessness
of DD in your posts I'm almost surprised you mention this. Then again, the
necros good points are looking somewhat thin on the ground at the moment.
