The problem comes not from the ability to choose good or evil, this I agree is a
good thing in a game.  The problem is that there is no real social impact to the
evil character  for what he does.  In real life he sees that if he does these
things ie. murder, theft, looting... that there is real punishment this provides
fear and incentive not to do these things.  However in these games there is no
fear of reprisal, the truth is if you are a powerful enough pk you don't really
need to go into town and that social factor is not an issue, also there is the
fact that pk's can use "dummy" characters to hawk their ill gotten goods.  And
since there is no penalty for associating with a pk thier friends if any can also
sell off their goods.  I have seen this from the beginning and not just in uo,
but in games like diablo and which while not massive multiplayer per say, it did
suffer from those same problems. Is there a solution to this, I don't know but I
know that I hope the gaming comunity represents a slanted crossection of today's
society, for if it doesn't there are about 30-40% of us out there who would be
mass murderers but for a few little pesky laws, that I find very disturbing.
Just some food for thought, flame away at it if it will make you feel more
justified.
