A "semi-easy" one is to hunt in the Karanas or the Commonlands, kill a
few black bears until you get a Low Quality Bear Skin and a Medium Quality
Bear Skin. Take a few paltry gold with you (I think 35 is enough) and
head to Chanda Miller, near the entrance to Surefall Glades. Say you are
coming from Baobob, and enquire about "boots" and "cloak". Trade the skins
and the required gold, and get Bear Skin Boots and Cloak, non magical but
a LOT nicer AC (1 above leather) for almost nothing.

But, unless you find something in your price range and with pluses in mana
or int, that's all you should care about.

For a spellcaster, your equipment list should be, in order of importance:

more INT raises.
more Mana raises.
more HP raises.
more save vs X raises.
more STR raises.
more AC raises.
more CHA raises.
the rest you don't care about.

(I rank STR raises before AC because the capacity for carrying more loot will
 usually be more useful than the 2 or 3HP shaved off a melee from AC)
