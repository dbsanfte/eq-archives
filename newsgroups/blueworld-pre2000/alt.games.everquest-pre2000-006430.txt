Can't you see that this is essentially the same complaint the necros had
post dagger nerf? The most powerful aspect of our respective classes lowered
to be inferior to what another class can do.

Whereas necros got a hearty 'Lap it up, bitch' from you, a proposed change
much smaller in effect has you decrying the end of the Shaman class.

The reasons behind the changes were different certainly, necros were nerfed
in the name of balance, where with Shamans it was a pathetic attempt to make
the expansion more attractive.

But still the effect would have been essentially the same, both left without
a useful specialty

But like you said,

>The Shaman would be left with a fair pet, poor DD and good DoTs which do
the
>equivalent of a single DD over 42 seconds or two minutes, depending on
>choice.  Plus Shamans can tank a bit.

Others may do some of those better than you, but no-one else has that
combination of abilities. Spreading some of them out to other classes may
make the Shaman less unique, but it doesnt make him useless any more than a
Ranger is worthless because he can't cast like a druid or melee like a
warrior. There are trade-offs to be made for being a generalist.

Funnily enough the necro has also turned out to be a little bit of a
generalist (mez a little, heal a little, resist debuffs). Personally I'd
rather they get rid of some of the extraneous stuff and make us more focused
on killing.

Anyway the moral of the rant is not to be an asshole when another class gets
nerfed, then start screaming melodramatically when something similar happens
to you.
