Quite frankly, you aren't supposed to be able to "level" solely through
quests.  Many quests have experience as part of the reward, but it's not
meant to be an efficient way to advance.  There is a good reason why the
quests you quote do not have experience as part of their reward, we want to
discourage people from doing quests for the class specific items that they
can't wear.  Many of the items needed are rare spawns, and we feel that
shamans, clerics, etc., shouldn't have to compete with all of the other
classes for their own quests.
