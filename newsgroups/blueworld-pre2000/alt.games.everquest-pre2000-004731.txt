Magic/Lore one or the other and most of the times both. IF it were to say NO DROP
it would be not included. So Magic/Lore/No Drop would be ok. IS this too hard to
comprehend? Could you not figure it out yourselves? So the whole point of having
NO EXP = NO MAGIC/LORE LOOT is to stop the FARMING. Simple as that, if im of the
right level to adventure and gain something, then the last thing I want to see is
some loser with 16+ hours a day waiting to collect loot to sell or twink or what

then they should be equally entitled to said item, if not then they should not be

the player with minimal time for games, not all the time but the majority of the
time this is the case.
