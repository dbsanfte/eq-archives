The /con function is pretty reasonable up to the mid twenties.  
After that it becomes completely ridiculous.  I can divine a
person's level pretty well if they lie in the range 45-47.
Everyone above that is red.  Everyone below that is green or
blue, and the cutoff is at a meaningless place.

You still have to ask people what level they are before you
group, because the range of people who can reasonably hunt the
same thing is much larger than the range of people you would
care to group with.
