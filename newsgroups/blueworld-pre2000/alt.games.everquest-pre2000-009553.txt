An NPC's reaction to you depends on four factors: race, class,
religion and deeds.

(Gordon is quoted as saying this on Casters Realm:
 http://eq.castersrealm.com/viewarticle.asp?Article=1444 )

I think you get one number for each of these which are added
together to produce your reaction.  The enchanter alliance spells 
add to this total.

I believe that illusions merely change your race for the purposes
of faction calculations.  However, I have recently been confronted
with that old rumor that you have separate factions for each form.
Basically, a friend is trying to convince me to hunt in the bad 
Venril Sather dungeons of Kunark using the argument that I could 
escape my deeds by using illusions in Overthere.

I am looking for a nice clear demonstration that shows how your
reputation affects your factions no matter what form you were in
when you took the action and no matter what form you are wearing.
I also want to do this without destroying my own factions. ;)
In particular, I need to show that my deeds follow me even when I
am in a non-humanoid form, which is hard because most groups have
very strong opinions about elementals, werewolves and skeletons.

Here are some of my ideas:

  Since I have already become KOS to the froggies in Guk, I can
  demonstrate that they hate me in all forms, even ones that have
  never killed a frog.  I didn't go overboard there and have tried
  to redeem myself by killing the undead froggies, so my faction is
  only borderline awful.

  I am currently considered kindly by the guards in Kaladim.  I 
  may have enough faction with them to offset the huge negative
  that the elemental forms have.

  I could make an erudite enchanter and raise them to level 4 to get 
  the human and half elf illusions, then go to Qeynos and kill the 
  precious fish as a human.  That should make me sufficiently reviled 
  to be killed repeatedly in my natural form or as a half elf.  This 
  seems like a lot of effort to prove a simple point. ;)
