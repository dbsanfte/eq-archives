You can create a story with your equipment.  I do that
sometimes.

The person who sold me my first bow had a fairy-folk fetish and
did a lot of roleplaying about them.  He made me promise never
to use the bow to shoot fairies or pixies, I did promise that,
and if I ever give the bow he made to someone else (it is in
the bank at the moment) I will ask them to carry on the same
promise.

The bone claymore that I bought cheaply was named the Bone Lady
(or the Big Bad Claymore) and it fights better when saving
someone else from peril.  The friend that I gave it to has also
started calling it the Bone Lady.

My Dragoon Dirk, by contrast, is just a knife that I bought in
EC.  Although I suppose that it reminds me of the time I spent
camping DVinn before I got sick of Crushbone, and of the
backpack of arrows that I fletched in Kelethin and /auctioned
from freeport to Qeynos before selling it in East Freeport for
the money that I then used to buy the dirk.

Just for fun, try walking from East to West Freeport via Qeynos
sometime.  It makes for an interesting evening.
