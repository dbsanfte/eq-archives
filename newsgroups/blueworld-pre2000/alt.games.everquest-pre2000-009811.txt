The effects are similar, but the mechanism is entirely different.
Bard songs are not Enchanter spells.  Most Ranger spells are Druid
spells.  Most Paladin spells are Cleric spells.

And Bards also get some basic abilities that neither Rogues nor 
Enchanters possess:
  Tracking (Ranger)
  wear plate armor (Warrior ...)
  use two-handed weapons (Warrior ...)
  fast movement (Shaman, Druid)
  corpse location (Necromancer)
  Forage (Druid, Ranger)
  healing (Cleric ...)

Bards are a unique class, not a simple blend of a fighter and a
caster.
