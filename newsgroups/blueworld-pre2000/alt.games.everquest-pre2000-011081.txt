That's not quite accurate. At level 34 you will not get exp if you are grouped 
with a 51, or a 50 for that matter.  At level 35 you will get exp if grouped 
with a level 50, and it goes up from there on a level by level basis.. 
meaning at 36 you can group with a 51 etc. This last part is hearsay, my 
highest level character on any server is a level 35.  Though  I would expect 
that by level 39 you can get exp if grouped with someone up to 60, based on
the fact that at 39 the 50+ buffs start to affect you (if I recall correctly).

Another thing that I noticed when I (briefly) grouped with people much higher 
than me (45+)is that you may get exp even if the higher level person does 
not, but the critter has to be at least blue to you.  Actually, that seems to 
hold true with even similar level groups, the high greens that would give exp 
if you solo'd them may not give exp when you are grouped.

My rule of thumb for grouping is as follows.
level 1-5, don't bother.. solo the newbie areas, it doesn't seem worth it.
6-20 no more than 3 levels difference with party members.
21-27 4-5 levels difference.
28-35 up to 6-7 levels difference, though as noted above at level 35 you can
group with someone up to level 50.   

YMMV as this is from personal experience and the advice of higher level people.
