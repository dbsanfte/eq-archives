there is a lot of things that suck about AC, and a lot of things that suck
about EQ..

just because AC doesn't have some of the things EQ already had doesn't mean
its a step backward, just a step in a different direction. the lack of
'secure' trading and banks drives me nuts sometimes.. i have avoided buying
anything from players because of it - but the lack of them only drives me
nuts because i am so use to them in EQ. if they were never in EQ, most
people would of never complained about the lack of them in AC. for every
thief in AC (or EQ) there is still 5 honest people.

but if you can find 10 things bad about AC (compared to EQ), there can also
be 10 things good about it.. even though i may get annoyed I cant bank a
prized sword i may find like I could in EQ(and end up just selling it), it
is a lot more relieving to know I can still run from town to town without
ever having to wait for a zone to load like they do in EQ. even though i may
want to sell a sword to another player for a profit, but decide against it
for fear that i may get ripped off, i still feel good knowing that later in
the game I can run off someplace and solo somewhere without having to search
for a group (and solo without using exploits)...

and yes, everyone will evntually be a tank/archer mage (it can be the best
way to play), but in the same sense  a hybrid is in EQ (like a Ranger, not
as good as a pure Warrior, and certainly not as good as a pure Druid) - its
like an Archer pulling out a Wand and buffing up his coordination and
lowering the target's ability to defend against piercing arrows, or a
Warrior buffing up his swords to make them a little faster or hit a little
harder... My main character is a Warrior. I have "Item Enchantment" trained
(got it at level 16).. that allows me to make my swords better and my armor
stronger. a character that is half Sword and half Nuker will be absolute
crap (he will not be able to wear armor as good as pure warriors or cast the
same spells pure mages cast)..

I'm basically saying the two games (AC and EQ) are very different. And AC
shouldnt be looked at like its supposed to be an EQ clone.. You may not like
AC, but you seem to make posts that show you hate the game with a passion..
I play the game almost daily, and its weird to see someone make posts that
*hates* a game that I like so much.
