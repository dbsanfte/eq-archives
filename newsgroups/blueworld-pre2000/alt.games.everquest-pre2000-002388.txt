Again, to use UO as an example.  No matter how big you make the mobs
currently, a DP'd weapon or an opportune EV will always take them down.  It
may take 10 EV's, or dying 5 times to actually hit with the DP and hide long
enough for it to do its work, but it WILL work some % of the time.  Take out
open-ended mechanics like poison, Blade spirits, and Energy vorteces, and
blatant monster mob weaknesses like the tendency to get caught up on a stone
and forget to go around it, and pretty soon there are simply non-soloable
creatures.

  Sure, people are going to figure out more and more effective ways to fight
them over time, but there reaches a certain point where the math just can't
be stretched any further, without adding more bodies.  So far EQ seems to
have avoided the open-ended lethality of UO... maybe this is (as you seem to
feel) simply because it's new and players haven't come close to maxing the
system, or maybe it's because there just aren't enough open-ended
exploitable mechanics.  Time will tell.  But it IS possible to not leave a
back door to be discovered.

Likewise, UO has it's share of monsters that are fairly easily beatable, but
just not worth it, no matter how high or low your power level, because they
use open-ended mechancs like poison. (Poison Elementals, and pretty much any
snake in the game come to mind).  Even if you beat them 99.9% of the time,
there is a certain fractional chance of dying not because you aren't good
enough, but simply because there is no such thing as "good enough" when it
comes to level 5 poison, which kills everyone equally as quickly.
