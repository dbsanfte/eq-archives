Personally, I'd like to see a skill-based system based on "archetypes"...
kind of like a class-based system with a lot more flexibility. 

Say for example you want to create a magical assassin. You'd choose the
"mage" archetype and have an amount of "points" which you can allocate to
different "spheres" of spells which would help you in your assassinations
as well as skills for the same purpose. 

Of course you'd only have a limited amount of points, and all of the
skills would have to be CAREFULLY balanced (perhaps with disadvantages),
otherwise you'd see everybody identical... but I honestly think that it
_can_ be done properly, it just hasn't been done YET. 

The class-based system is much simpler and leads to much greater
cooperation between players as well as a higher _likelyhood_ for real
roleplaying. (not to say that there's that much RPing going on in
Everquest anyway, but still) 

I definitely don't think it's a _bad_ thing.
