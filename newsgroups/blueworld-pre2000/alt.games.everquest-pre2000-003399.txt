In <3873CD3E.9915A90B@misleading.com> Morgan <morgana@misleading.com> writes:

>I have found that my game enjoyment has only improved as I have
>reached higher levels.  Each spell circle brings me new abilities.
>The fights get more challenging.  I gain access to new areas.
>And I just achieved the magic level to not stare at the spell book
>any more!

I find the same thing. New places to go, new spells to cast, new
strategies to work out with those new spells. But for those who can't
learn to work in a group, for whatever reason, the game probably does
get pretty boring after 20 or so. The places they can go to hunt are
more limited, and more crowded, as they fill up with the soloers who
can't find groups, don't want to find groups or are waiting for their
regular group to get on.

If you like people, know how to play your character effectively, and
spend a bit of time building a list of friends to group with the game
can be a lot of fun all the way to the mid 40's. By then, you're down to
two dungeons where you can get any kind of reasonable experience, and a
few small outdoor areas, and then it gets a bit boring.
