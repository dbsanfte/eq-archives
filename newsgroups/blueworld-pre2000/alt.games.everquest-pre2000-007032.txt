>Specialization has two effects: it reduces mana cost and decreases the
>chance of fizzles.  It does not speed up spell casting or help with
>channeling (interruptions).
>
>When I made my decision, I examined the spells in each college:
>
>	http://www.darkflame.org/data.pl?search=spells
>	http://eq.castersrealm.com/spells/enchanter/
>
>The two favorite specializations for Enchanters are Conjuration and 
>Alteration.
>
>Conjuration spells include the Mesmerize line, Mesmerization, and all 
>pet summoning spells.
>
>Alteration spells include the Charm line, Clarity, speed up and slow
>down spells, the Memory Blur line, and all of the metal enchantments.
>All of our random stat buffs and debuffs are also Alteration.
>
>Not many enchanters choose Divination.  That covers the illusions,
>vision spells, invisibility, and the Color Flux line.  Evocation and
>Abjuration would both be pretty useless as primary specializations
>for an Enchanter.
>
>I chose Alteration, since that was where most of my mana was going.  I 
>could see switching to Conjuration now that I have completed my 
>jewelcraft training, but I think I still spend more mana on Alteration 
>in general.
>
>There is a very nice discussion of the options at the Casters Realm
>web site:
>
>	http://eq.castersrealm.com/misc/pages/special.asp
