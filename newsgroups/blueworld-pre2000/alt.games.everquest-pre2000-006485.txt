Read it again.  Erudites can be Necromancers and Shadowknights.
Thus, Erudites can wear the hoop.

It looks as though Verant wanted all evil characters to be able
to wear the hoop, whether they be evil by class or by race.  However,
because they can't specify class-race pairs, they ended up with a lot
of non-evil characters who can wear it.

What they really wanted was to have an implicit OR instead of an 
implicit AND between races and classes, and include just the evil
ones:

Races: DEF OGR TRL
Classes: NEC SHD

In order to get all of the non-evil classes for the evil races, they
had to add the Classes: WAR ROG CLR SHM WIZ MAG ENC

In order to get all of the non-evil races for the evil classes, they
had to add the Races: HUM ERU GNM

Because of the above limitation, Human Clerics and Erudite Wizards
can wear the hoop, even though they are not evil.  Of course, they 
screwed up and added Rangers and Half Elves to the list and omitted 
Gnomes.  Either that or my assumption is wrong. ;)
