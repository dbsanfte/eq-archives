>> occasionally you can get overflow elsewhere.

>Do you know if that's the ultimate source of the DOT bug?

Yeppers, sure is. If a mob is killed by damage not owned by a PC, the corpse 
poofs and nobody gets experience. That's to stop people from luring hill giants 
to guards and then making a fortune off the loot.

DoTs count as damage too... if the PC has zoned, died, gone linkdead, been 
feared, or been charmed and the DoT gets the kill, the corpse poofs, since the 
damage isn't owned by a PC. The same happens if the DoT has done over 50% 
damage to the mob, which is basically impossible in planes and very rare 
elsewhere, particularly in groups.

They mostly fixed the DoT bug in non-plane situations (I've seen it happen once 
or twice), but it still rarely occurs in planes... not often enough to tell 
people to not use dots, but it happens. 

As I understand it, the fix implemented a few months ago changed DoTs to always 
count as PC damage for the >50% damage/hatelist rule. It didn't fix the killing 
blow bug, however.
