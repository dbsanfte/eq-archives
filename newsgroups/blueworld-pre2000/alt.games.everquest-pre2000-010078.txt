Enchanters can solo at any level.  Any class can solo at any level.
However, they are not very good at it, in the sense that an Enchanter
will level more slowly than a good soloing class.

There are a few odd cases where an Enchanter could solo something and
another class could not, but they are not generally very efficient at
it.  If you want a really efficient machine for turning monsters into
yellow pixels, play a Necromancer.  The difference in raw death-dealing
ability and basic durability is amazing.
