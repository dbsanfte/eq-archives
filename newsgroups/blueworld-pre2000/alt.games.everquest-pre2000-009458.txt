According to the ShowEQ guys, you actually don't.

They worked out that you lose a percentage based on your level of the 
amount of experience a Human Warrior would require to get from the 
beginning of the previous level to the beginning of your current level.
So, everyone actually loses the same number of experience points if
they die in a level, but it is a bigger fraction of the xp bar for
Warriors and Halflings than for Trolls and Shadowknights.

So, don't let your Warriors die!
