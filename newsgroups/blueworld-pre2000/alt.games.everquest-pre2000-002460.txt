I agree with this.  For example, almost all my EQ time these days is
spent with a friend I have made.  If I decide to create a new
character, and there's only one character per server, then I will not
be able to socialize with her, unless she TOO creates a new character.
But suppose she's happy with hers, whereas I have decided that a
different sort of character would go well with the one she's already
got?

I think the one character per server idea only sounds good to the sort
of person who decides right away what sort of character they want to
play and plays it straight up to 50.

To those of use who enjoy having multiple characters and trying new
ones all the time, the idea is not very attractive at all.

As far as I'm concerned, the advantages of multiple characters per
server far outweigh the disadvantages.
