> The point at which items become Trivial...
> ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
> Patchwork 26

> Cured Silk 82
> Backpacks 90
> Reinforced 122
> ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
> Suggestions for Skill building...
> ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
> First, dump 20 practices into the skill (This is the only time you can
> do this, you'll
> probably wish you had done this later if you don't now>
>
> Second, build your skill to trivial with Patchwork, you can do this in
> the field (No need
> for studs or boning). This will bring you to 27.
>
> Third, bring your skill to trivial with Raw Silk. Silk is considerably
> more common than
> pelts and tends to raise you skill faster. Silk can also be done in
> the field. This will
> bring you to 37.
>
> Fourth, IMO it is better to SKIP Studded altogether. Not only do you
> have to use the
>  extremely rare pelts, but it cannot be done in the field (Try
> carrying around patterns,
> pelts, and studs <Not Stackable>, along with quest items, regular
> gear, spell components,
> bandages, food......)... Instead proceed directly to Cured Silk. Heady
> Kiolas ARE stackable
> Silk is more common and is LIGHT Weight and Stackable. I was able to
> move from 37 to 82 in 1
> day by camping the spider rooms in Upper Guk. I also suggest making
> masks, as it uses the
> least silk and the least amount of Kiola.
>
> Once you have hit 82, Backpacks are fairly easy, by the time I had hit
> 82, I had 20 HQ Bear
> pelts saved up... Filled my bank with them, and 4 for my inventory
> (Along with my EE Bag
> and Light Burlap Sack), and plenty to give to the people that helped
> me along the way. Of
> the 20 that I did, only had 2 failures. This will raise you to a skill
> of 90.
>
> Once Backpacks are complete, the only thing left to raise your skill
> is reinforced, maxing
> your skill is very time consuming at this point, making the boning is
> tedious and there are
> a lot of failures.
>
> You could probably sell most pieces for around 10pp at this point
>
>
>
> The size of the tailored item is based on the type of pelt that you
> use. Bear pelts make
> large armour.  Cat pelts make medium armour.  Wolf pelts make small
> armour.
