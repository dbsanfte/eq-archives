You could get around that by making a temp char... having your friend do
the trade... then kill the temp char and consent your REAL new char to
loot the temp char.  Voila you have your new stuff and noone knows your
toy char.  If you are that desperate to give the stuff to your twink
without using a friend, you can decide whether the monetary value of the
stuff is worth the price of death to your "giving" char.  In other words
what would hurt most? The exp loss or losing the stuff.  If the exp loss
is too much for you to bear and you don't want a friend involved at
all... choose a place like Tox or Misty Thicket  GF or LF where there is
lots of untraveled space (and darkness) to drop your stuff.  Or pick
really out of the way places that people really don't use.  But you
ALWAYS run the risk of someone doing the same thing as you and finding
your loot along the way.
