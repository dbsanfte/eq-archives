That doesn't follow at all.  Consider warriors.  First of all, 
there are other abilities and characteristics beyond the stats 
that make the different races valid choices.  You can get night
vision, hide, sneak, forage, or slam depending on what race you
choose.  Different stat distributions then have their effects.
You could go long in strength for melee damage, stamina for hit
points, agility for defense, or dexterity for special combat 
moves.  If the stats are well balanced, then these are all valid
choices for your warrior.  Just because you know the numbers 
does not mean there is one optimal strategy that everyone would
choose.

You are also completely neglecting role playing.  Some people
could not stomach the idea of playing a dark elf, no matter 
how favorable their statistics might be.  Others will pick the
face they find most attractive or the race that looks best in
plate mail.
