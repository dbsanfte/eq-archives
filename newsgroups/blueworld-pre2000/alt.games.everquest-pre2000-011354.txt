I wrote a log file parser.  Bang on it, let me know what you think.

It's quite small compared to the others that are out there (125K).  It also
doesn't splatter DLLs all over the place.  And it's fast.  On my 450mhz
P-III, it ran through 350,000 lines of log file in 67 seconds.  That's over
5,000 lines a second.

It can handle multipe log files of any size.

It tracks faction, skill increases, mobs fought, damage taken, damage dealt,
spells, merchants and their wares, players, zones, and other stuff.

I also wrote a program that will split up the monolithic log file that EQ
creates into multiple files, one per session per character.

Check'em out.

No source code yet, but I'll release that as soon as I polish them up a bit.
