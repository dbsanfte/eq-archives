run back to get arrow components several times and to recover my body two

For me, the trick is to turn the clipping plane up really close so that
my motion is smooth.  I have never had a spider appear from nowhere.
I use a steep follow camera to get a clearer view of the terrain ahead
and I try to run around hills rather than over the crests so that I
don't find myself instantly in line of sight of a hostile spider.
