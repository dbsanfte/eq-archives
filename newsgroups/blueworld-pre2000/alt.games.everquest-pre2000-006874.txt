        I know this is rather long, and I apologize for that, but I would
appreciate it if a Verant rep (Gordon :) ) were to take a few minutes to
read it.  Thank you.

        I'd like to make a few comments on the new policy.  I know this
policy was a sincere attempt by Verant to improve the playing experience
for everyone.  I don't think anyone would disagree with that.  And yes,
for the most part, when we all play nice, the game is much more
enjoyable.  However, being forced to play nice is not fun.  This policy
hugely minimizes the social interaction component of the game.

        A sizable component of the EQ experience is the immersion into a
"world" that is extremely realistic (in a fantasy context of course.)

        In the real world people are nice to other people for three
reasons... 1) They are inherently kind people and being nice to people
makes them feel good, 2) They are subject to guilt, and being unkind to
people makes them feel bad, and/or 3) fear of consequences.

        Now, obviously we are looking at #3 here in this policy, and in a
non-PvP world the consequence are somewhat limited, so obviously Verant is
trying to balance that out.  However, in externalizing that policy you are
removing that level of the game.  What you are, in essence, doing is
dampening the social structure in the game by inserting (on a much closer
level than ever before) a social director.  That changes the whole dynamic
of the "world" which was a very loose structure appropriate to the setting
into a very strict "Big Brother"-ish  structure.

	I'd like to make the suggestion that you make every effort to
internalize the social structure in the game as much as possible.  Remove
"the company" from decisions about what is right and wrong whenever
possible.  Some specific ideas I had are these (some may be terrible, of
course J )

	1) Spend some time and expand the dueling component of the
game.  Specifically, I'm thinking of switches to the /duel command to
allow for pre-agreement of rules.  For example, duels to the death or to
submission.  Allowing or disallowing looting.  Allowing or disallowing
pets (before and/or during the duel.)  And maybe the option to disallow
interferance/help by other players. 
	2) Add the option for group dueling
	3) Add the ability to force a duel in certain situation (maybe by
long term proximity after a challenge.)
	4) Include a bounty hunter of some sort.   Whether PC, NPC, or
whatever.  Of course using a bounty hunter should be expensive and/or
damaging.  (Like both you and your target lose xps for it.)

	Now, I'd like to say, that I have never once dueled.  I've never had such
a conflict with another player, so I am not some sort of duel junkie, but
that is a very effective way (at least in my opinion) of internalizing the
social structure and keeping right and wrong largely within the game.  

	I hope that you consider what I'm saying, even if you don't like
my specific suggestions.  They are attempted as constructive
criticism.  Thank you for your time (I hope.)
