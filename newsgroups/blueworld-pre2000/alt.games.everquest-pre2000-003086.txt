I am an archer in AC, and I love this feature (after swearing like a
sailor about it for days while I learned how to use the bow
effectively). It gives the timing of your shots vs. evading the
approaching critter a nice tactical twist.

Believe me, I watch the body movements of a golem which I just started
to attack from a hundred feet away *very* closely. If he twiches,
instead of starting to run towards me immediately, I know that he is
just about to fire a spell in my direction, and I have to time my
sidestepping very carfully - considering that drawing the bow string
(an animation I cannot interrupt) takes about two seconds, and that the
missile he shot will hit me within three or four seconds.

Without this feature, being an archer would be as dull as hunting
gromnies with a bow - shoot, shoot, shoot, shoot, kill, yawn, next one,
please.
