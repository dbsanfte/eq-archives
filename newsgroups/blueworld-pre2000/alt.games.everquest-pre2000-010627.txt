<rat_thing@my-deja.com> wrote in message news:8k2kje$bj1$1@nnrp1.deja.com...
> When is it useful to give pets weapons?  What do summoned daggers do,
> what do fine steel swords do?
>
>

For mages, before level 16 (although there's only
a marginal improvement from 12-15) and after 24.

Regardless of what weapon you give a pet, it keeps
its original delay.

As far as damage goes, a pet has a base unarmed
damage.  If you give them a weapon and their base
unarmed dam < 2*(weapon damage) then they'll
get 2*weapon damage instead.

So, prior to level 12, the best pet hits for 10
unarmed, give it a rusty 2-hander (dam 9) and
it starts hitting for 18's.

After 24, mage pets get dual wield, so you give
them 2 weapons (any weapons will do).  Unless
you want to waste an expensive weapon, don't give
him anything but summoned or rusties.
