When making that run, I always get Spirit of Wolf.  Since I can't cast it
myself, I am very aware of just how far I can go on one shot. ;)  Spirit
of Wolf lasts 36 minutes, not counting zone times, and I can get from 
Freeport to Qeynos on one casting if I don't stop to kill anything.  So 
that run is about 40 minutes with Spirit of Wolf.  The boat from Freeport
to Butcherblock takes 22 minutes, with a 0-20 minute wait.  The boat from
Qeynos to Toxxulia takes about 10 minutes, with a 0-10 minute wait.  So
Erudin to Butcherblock would take between 1 hr 10 min and 1 hr 40 min.
Running to Ak'Anon adds another 15 minutes.  I don't know about Paineel.

Of course, the trip normally takes all day, since I stop to chat, get
Spirit of Wolf, cast Clarity, sell jewelry, save newbies, etc.

The problem isn't even that there aren't many things to kill.  The problem
is that you run past tons of stuff that is too high or too low.  EQ's steep
progression means that at, any given time, only about 10% of the monsters
out there are worth killing.  You are further restricted by the fact that 
the monsters that are in the right range might be difficult to kill because
your group is not up to the fight or because they are surrounded by friends.

> More or less, yes.  What's more it needs mobs able to reactively adjust to
> the average player level on the server.  Unfortunately, it appears to not be
> in Verant's best interests to let people do that.  In fact, it's not in
> anybody's best interests - once you reach level 50 in EQ, your done.  Kunark
> will expand this out to level 55 or lvl 60 (Yes, I know Aradune was level 60
> on test server, but that doesn't really prove anything).

People will still crowd the few known places where mobs spawn either with
nice loot or undefended for easy killing.  Where would you have the extra
high level mobs spawn for the benefit of the high level players?  Would
you flood the Ro desert with sand giants?  The Kithicor undead experiment
was not terribly popular and didn't even interfere with hunting.
