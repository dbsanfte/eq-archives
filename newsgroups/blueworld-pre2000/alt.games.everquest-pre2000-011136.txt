Actually this is one of the most optimal groups I've seen.  With good people
behind these classes I've had pretty much non stop fighting of multple mobs
at once deep in higher level kunark dungeons(NOT at the zones as some people
always try to claim).  Our tactics involve one tank, or both, pulling a few
mobs(4+ is optimal) and gathering them close to each other.  The two wizards
start chain casting AE spells while the enchanter chain casts AE stuns.  If
chanter gets a run of bad resists the wizards can toss in his own AE stun
spell(level 54+ wizards only).  Cleric heals those who needs it, usually a
CH on the warrior.

One big improvement to this group is to replace one of the warriors with
another wizard.  Same tactics but with 3 wizards casting post 51 AE spells
you can wipe out large numbers of mobs in seconds.
