Now this is hardly true.  Grouping allows members of a party to *share*
experience but, given your other complaints about obsessive leveling and
the lack of roleplay in EQ, I am rather surprised that restrictions on
grouping would be the focus of your complaint.

High level players can certainly help their lower level friends when they
are on a hunt, *exactly as in UO* where there is no in-game grouping
option. The game doesn't banish you to the other side of the world if you
help a lower level friend knock a monster down a few pegs, or if you
intervene should your friend get in over his head.

Of course, if your friends are going to insist on hunting monsters that
are too high of a level for your to approach safely, I'd say that this is
their fault and not the game's... or, if you insist on seeing it as a
fault, a fault that is identical to that found in UO for the exact same
situation.

As for the remainder of your suggestion that you couldn't do anything else
with them, I would remind you that the majority of roleplay is brought
*into* games by the roleplayers.  If your friends were only interested in
hacking and slashing high level critters, I'd suggest finding another set
of friends to satisfy your desires for character interaction.
