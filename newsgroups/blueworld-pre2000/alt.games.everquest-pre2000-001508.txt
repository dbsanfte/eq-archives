	Here's some more EQ screenshots of a few reasonably-cool things I've run
across.  Some of these shots may have yellow sparkles in them from a Glimpse
spell used to get pictures of things you don't want to get close to....

http://members.home.net/hefferman/AkAnon.jpg

	A shot of the Gnome city of Ak'Anon.  It was curious, though, that there
don't seem to be any actual GNOMES in the Gnome city...the clockworks have
taken over!  I was lucky to be able to get in, as many Rangers (the ones who
follow Tunare, the Treehugger Goddess) are KOS by a merchant in the Ak'Anon
entrance.

http://members.home.net/hefferman/AkAnonATM.jpg

	Now THIS is an ATM!

http://members.home.net/hefferman/Equestrielle.jpg

	A close-up of the Unicorn, taken without Glimpse.  It was raining, though,
and that Pixie Trickster popped out from behind her at the last moment (just
like a Pixie....)  As far as I know, she's the only one.  I assume she's part
of a quest somewhere.

http://members.home.net/hefferman/FaeDrakeHatch.jpg

	A pair of Fae Drake Hatchlings.  They're cute.  (Then I killed them.)

http://members.home.net/hefferman/FaeMaiden.jpg

	A Faerie Maiden, again taken without Glimpse.  No, I didn't kill her too;
she wasn't hostile.  You can't get a good idea of her size in this shot, but
she's TINY.  I had to walk right up to her to get a decent picture.  And all
the Faeries make a great musical sound as they float by.

http://members.home.net/hefferman/HPass.jpg

	This is a shot of the winding canyon path in East Karanas that leads to
Highpass Hold.  I thought the face on the canyon wall was worth the shot.

http://members.home.net/hefferman/Kelethin.jpg

	A shot of the Wood Elf town of Kelethin, from below.  And no, I didn't
take this after plummetting to my death, but thanks for asking.

http://members.home.net/hefferman/LFaydarkTotem.jpg

	An ominous totem found in Lesser Faydark.  I've heard some nasty Orcs can
be found around it.

http://members.home.net/hefferman/Minotaur.jpg

	I took this one in the Ak'Anon zoo (which has a whole three exhibits, it
seems; the other two are a Brown Bear and a Tiger).  This is a Lesser
Minotaur, which Venture could defeat in combat.  They come in much, much
stronger varieties as well.

http://members.home.net/hefferman/Treant.jpg

	A close-up of a Treant, taken in Western Karanas after a good deal of
running around.  Since Venture is a Ranger, Treants 'kindly consider him' when
/considered.  And yes, they ARE as big as the trees.
