Astitious Maximus XIII wrote:

> I want to explain my position briefly before people start flaming me

Too late!  Your subject line did it for you:

	BOYCOTT EQ. Reply to this post in order to sign petition

Good grief.  You have to assume that most replies to your post are 
going to be flames, not affirmations.

>   I have played EQ now for about 2 months. I have tried many different
> classes and races, and have enjoyed the experience. However, lately I
> have been exposed more and more to the "darker" side of Verant.
>   I suggest we, the disgruntled, unite our voices. I have made a list
> of complaints that I feel will satisfy most everyone.
> 
> 1. Remove all Nerfs. Undo the damage to each character class. Leave
> the enhancements but remove the handicaps placed on each race and
> class. NO MORE NERFS!!

As long as the game is not balanced, expect tweaks both up and down.
I would not want to hamstring Verant by only allowing them to make
changes in one direction.

> 2. Double amount of servers. Including chat/login servers.

Some simple math showed us that about 5 new servers would bring the
populations to reasonable levels.

> 3. Leave NPC's alone. If someone figures out how to kill it, good for
> us. Spend your time fixing quests,  and adding new elements.

I want them to fix exploits.  If you figure out how to kill a
monster through incredibly cheesy tactics or cheats and you 
repeatedly do this, I want the GMs to find you, warn you and, 
if you don't stop, to ban you.  I want Verant to fix the AI so
that monsters put up a fair fight and behave rationally.  At
the same time, they are fixing quests and adding new stuff.

> 4. Allow users to transfer a character from one server to another.

That would make new servers a whole lot less interesting.

> 5. Make available 3 new PvP servers; Good vs Evil (by alignment). Coin
> loot only, and full loot (including spell book and bags).

The existing PvP servers are woefully underpopulated.  EverQuest
was not designed around PvP and, hence, doesn't support it very 
well.  There are not going to be any new PvP servers.

> 6. Make GM's and guides more accountable.

I don't object to this if it takes the form of passive logging that
can be checked later.  I think that all petitions and reports should
be logged and that the GMs should keep a little database of who's
been naughty and who's been nice.  That way when they get ten complaints
about people exploiting or harassing, they will have some way of deciding 
who to check out first.

> 7. Allow casters to bind others everywhere.

Sure, whatever.  I think this would be nice.  Verant thinks it would
be bad.  Rational debate is the key to resolving the issue, not
protests.

> 8. Individually poll users of each class (lvl 20+) for suggestions on
> class improvements. Implement the most requested suggestions.

I want a game designed by a small team of people with a vision, not 
by a general consensus.

>   I realize there are probably a dozen other things we can list. I am
> sorry I couldn't list everything. But if we can stick together on
> these points, they will have to listen.
>    Everyone who signs this must agree to:
> 
> A. Cancel account if Verant does not heed us.
> B. Tell everyone they know to avoid playing EQ
> C. Get all their friends on EQ to sign this as well.
> 
>   Please don't pick this apart. If we can stick together and win on
> this. Things will improve for everyone.

Sorry, there is nothing in your list worth supporting.

If you put in "Add direct hot keys for sit/stand, spells, and meditate", 
I might wish you luck, but I would still not boycott.
