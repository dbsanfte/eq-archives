Well, here's my take on it.

I've played human paladin to level 16, and human ranger to level 21.
Although I had fun with them both, I found the ranger to be a much more
powerful class than paladins.  Here's why: dual wield.  The amount of
dammage you can dish out with dual weild weapons is huge!  (Oh, rangers get
a kick attack too, extra 11 points per kick.  paladins/shadowknights don't
have that.)

It seems paladins loose their ability to dual wield and kick to gain Lay on
Hands.  I don't really think it's worth it, but that's the way it is.

I found the paladin spells more useful than my ranger spells, but that was
my view.  Yes, camo is nice, but the imporved healing that paladins get over
rangers helps the paladin a lot.  At higher levels, much less downtime.
Much better dammage vs. undead.  Better abjuration spells.  Equal direct
damage ability.

If you want a better tank, choose ranger --  (people like you for your
tracking ability.)  If you want to have better casting abilities (in later
levels), choose paladin.

Shadowknights are just as effective tanks as paladins, but much much less
useful spellcaster than a paladin.  There isn't a Soul Fire equivelent
weapon in the game either for a shadowknight.  The dark reaver doesn't
compare to a Soul Fire.
