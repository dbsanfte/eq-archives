>what does it mean?  level numbers will be skewed.

Averages will be distorted by people being in the zone who aren't
there for any real advancement purpose. Eg: You have a newbie zone
right next to a tough zone. People in the tough zone get beaten up or
are trained and zone into the newbie zone for a break. This pushes the
average level in the newbie zone way up.

The numbers in the table are just raw statistics, a rough guide at
best.
