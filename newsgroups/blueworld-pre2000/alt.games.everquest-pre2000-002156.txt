just changes as you get past the "being a newbie really sucks" levels.

Thing is, I'm really easily amused.  I can kill the same mobs over and
over for hours.  In UO I could wander around chopping trees and
carving heavy xbows over and over for hours.

or such as that.

and walk around solo, or just me and Shade, whether the dungeon was
crowded or empty.

two things and then these two mage/wizard-types asked if they could
join us (and we did join them, and killed some more stuff).  That
never happened in UO, ever.  But it kind of *has* to happen in EQ
because wizard/mage types are soft and easily bruised, and it was sure
as *heck* better for us to group with them, nuke things into dust in 2
seconds flat versus getting all beat up.

But I don't know if they would have asked to join us if we'd all been,
say, level 8 in Blackburrow.
