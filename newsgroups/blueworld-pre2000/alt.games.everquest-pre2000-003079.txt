>Now...  Seriously, I'd be *much* happier to see a group for online
>RPGs.  No game stays popular for very long, there's always followons.

Online games are different, though.  Lots of them tend to stay around
for years, far longer than any regular game.  UO is getting pretty
long in the tooth now, but its still extremely popular, and UO2 has
now been semi-announced.  The UO group will be getting traffic for
years to come, and I'm sure that a EQ group would have the same sort
of long term following.

>The thing is though, that there's always going to be first person
>shoot 'em ups, there's always going to be on-line RPGs.  Half-Life
>(the current successor to Doom/quake) won't be around forever, neither
>will Everquest.

But most likely there will be an EQ2, and a EQ3, and so on.  Just like
the Quake groups now handle discussion about Quake3, the EQ group
could handle any future expansions and rereleases.

>Besides leaving dead newsgroups around, why keep inventing the wheel?
>Pass a generic group once and be done with it.

Yes, there should be a general, online game group, but the online game
market is one that is constantly growing.  With only one group to try
to handle it all, the discussions would be unmanagable.  Its hard
enough to keep track of all the posts in just EQ's alt group.  Tossing
in UO, Asheron's Call, and the dozens of games of this type that are
currently in development would make it impossible.  There should be a
group about general online gaming (rec.games.computer.online.misc,
perhaps), but I think there should also be subgroups dedicated to
games that are showing themselves to have longterm value.
