    OK, then. Let's say the game is the ONLY one out there of its kind, and
you really enjoy it. You play it without the excessive user agreement until
you become used to it and don't like being without it (For the most part,
the case with Everquest).  Little by little you get addicted to the game
(sounds pathetic but it is all too common). Then the company that runs the
game starts to encroach upon your rights in the new EULA. You already paid
$50 to buy the game, and spent considerable time playing. You wouldn't want
to quit, and they'd slowly strip away any and all of your rights.
    In the US, we have a Bill of Rights to prevent things like that from
happening, but in the communist dictatorship that is EQ, there are no rules
protecting us. We can choose not to play, but then there would be a
substantial waste of money and time, not to mention the withdrawal symptoms.
