I don't tailor so I don't know how useful the pattern is.  However, I
find the quiver itself to be quite useful.  At first I thought they
were a waste (.4 weight with only 4 slots).  But when someone told me
that it is linked to the ammo slot (you can't put it in the ammo slot
directly), I found out that it was decent to have if you use a lot of
arrows.  I put arrows in my ammo slot, and more arrows in the quiver
and when I get down to 1 arrow in the ammo slot, it automatically pulls
arrows from the quiver.  So, I can have up to 100 arrows (4 stacks in
quiver plus 1 stack in ammo slot) that I don't have to ever worry about
swapping around or running out of when I need them most (until I've
fired 100 of them, anyway).  Neat item.  Would be neater if you could
actually put it in the ammo slot, but this is still useful.
