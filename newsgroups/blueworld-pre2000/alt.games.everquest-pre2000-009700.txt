>Just a general question as I see a lot of people saying that Wizards are the
>worst class in EQ and I have no experience with them. Are they really that
>bad? Why? Can anyone who has played a Wizard for at least 20 levels give
>examples, personal experiences, etc.?

Wizards are not optimized for experience grinding.

They get very good very fast direct damage, 2 of the 3 travel
spells, and some other little spells.

The problem is that direct damage is not very mana efficient.
Time efficient, yes, but not mana efficient.

If you are gunning for experience you will generally want a
melee -heavy group with a bard or shaman that can grind through
melee mobs.  The wizard adds fairly little to that, especially
because it can be hard to sit on a full tank of mana and NOT
nuke.

On the other hand, when your puller brings back a caster it is
nice to see the wizard stand up and introduce himself.  Of
course, most people do not camp festering hags for experience
just because casters can be so very dangerous.

Wizards are a niche class, they are not nearly as powerful as
their initial image makes them look.  They are also a lot of
fun to play.  Two of my RL friends play wizards, and while I
like grouping with them I have also learned that it is best if
we do not plan an experience grind for that gaming session.

If I ever roll up an intelligence caster it will be a gnomish
wizard.  I do think that wizards, like magicians and warriors,
are easy to play in a medioce but non-dangerous manner and very
hard to play well.

Is there anyone who actually plays a wizard who would like to
expand upon this.
