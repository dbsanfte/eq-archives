When you see someone with a cool sword and you ask where they got it,
you already know they camped for 12 hours or more to get it, nothing
dramatic or exciting about it.

I still think that instead of making the monster drop a single item,
it should be a quest for each item. Let me kill something nasty and
get a hide from it or a body part that when taken to an NPC will tell
me why it was good I killed it, then let him give me the item for
smaller quest.  Since the monster would drop the item each time for
the quest you can cut down on camping for loot. Players would have to
complete the quest in order to get said item. Bigger items would
require more steps.

For larger quest let it be that when I take the part to the NPC he
sends me somewhere else. Attach a story to this and you can stop the
farming, keep the story and put the quest back in everquest.

You can do far more interesting things with this idea. An example
might be that an woodelf that would be KOS around the dark elf guards,
might have to change faction along the way in order to collect an item
he needs from a certain guard.  Add a story to this along the way as
to why he is doing this.


This would give alot more meaning to EQ and rid it of the just hack n
slash game.

The only people so far I have seen object to this idea are those that
are farming and realize that it would be too much work, compared to
sitting in one place, to get items to sell.
