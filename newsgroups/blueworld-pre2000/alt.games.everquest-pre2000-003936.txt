This would be a great idea; it may single-handledly save
EQ's overcrowding!

Back a few years ago, I remember playing a lot of
Total Annihilation.  The tools to making maps were
released and there were always tons and tons of
very high quality maps put out there for free
on places like TAMEC.  These maps were on par or often
better than the original Cavedog maps and contributed
greatly to the long-term playability of the game.

Now granted, there was a lot of crap at TAMEC too,
but the TAMEC team made it a point to review and
rate every single submission they got.  I'll also
admit there's probably much more to designing a dungeon
than a TA-map, but hell, look at all the maps out
there for Doom, Quake, etc. not to mention total
conversions.  There are a lot of people who wouldn't
mind making these dungeons for free, which would
help Verant substantially.
