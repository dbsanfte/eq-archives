The enchanter size problem is a bug that Verant won't acknowledge as a bug.
They know of it, just have no plans for fixing it because they say it's not
a bug.

If an enchanter casts the illusion, anyone currently in the zone with them
will see them as the proper size.  Anyone zoning in after will see them as
their regular size (tiny ogres or huge gnomes, for example).

BTW, if you do a /who <enchanter's name> it'll show the illusion race.  If
you do a /who all <enchanter's name> it'll show their real race.   Same
thing for a druid in wolf form ("Unknown" if doing a local /who).
