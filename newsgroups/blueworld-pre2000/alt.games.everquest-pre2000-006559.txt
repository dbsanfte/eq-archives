Which is better :-

1200 damage in 42 seconds

 800 damage in 60 seconds *and* 800 damage healed

Perhaps some people might go for the 1200 damage.

Now consider you have a spell that turns 32 hps into 16 mana every tick
which means that using your 800 damage spell has an effective mana cost of
*zero*.

Now which is the best DoT in the game?

Also, after Envenomed Bolt (the 1200 in 42 seconds), Necros have *3* DoTs
which are superior to the second Shaman DoT.  The Shamans only have two DoT
lines the Necro has 5 lines.

Stacking all their DoTs, the Necro is doing around 400 damage per tick.  The
shaman is doing around 260.  The Necro is also healing as a side effect and
can trivially turn that into mana, including while on the run.

In addition, if the Shaman cannot be hit by the mob, for whatever reason,
I.e. they choose to kite, the fully stacked Shaman DoTs result in only 170
damage per tick.

The Necro can do the same thing (not be hit) and still have their DoTs do
full damage.

Plus the Necro pet will do up to 160 damage per *second* or 960 per tick
(obviously this will seldom happen) while the Shaman pet is limited to 80
damage per second or 480 per tick.

Depends if you count pets are DoTs or not.
