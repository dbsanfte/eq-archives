I only had one thought on giving wizards a "form" of pet - a familiar.  A
small, innocuos pet, like the size of a bixie/fairie/etc that would be used
by the wizard to get some sort of stat/mana/ability bonus, but that has very
little hit points and should not be used for any direct combat.  The wizard
would be able to turn them invis (but would have to keep doing so) to keep
them from being easily spotted, but if the familiar was killed, it would
severely cripple whatever ability it was providing.  This would give the
wizards a unique (yet not) ability, that could help them in various ways -
the familiars would be of varying power and type, and wouldn't give any kind
of "overwhelming" ability to the wizard - on the other hand, the abilities
bestowed should either help with some sort of magic debuffing/enhnanced
night vision/providing light (summon a will-o-wisp-type pet - not as strong,
but provides a glowing light that follows you around)/agility/ability to see
through the pet's eyes/etc to self-only.  Eventually you might give them a
pet that would potentially offer group benefits, in the 35 level range.
There are a lot of possibilities for this.

No, the pet should not be able to do anything other than maybe 1 SECOND of
combat in a fight, so that it may give the wizard half a step to start
running :)  You could certainly give a familiar that casts root, but it
would have to be visible to do so, and thus it would taunt creatures to it,
causing it to pop with one hit.  Exploitable?  I supposed so - you could
buff the familiar so it could take more than a token pop, but it still would
be unable to deal any form of significant damage - more of  a defensive type
of thing; I suppose you could stick a damage shield on it so when it dies it
at leasts hurts the thing swiping at it.... I am sure this group can think
of many other exploits - not something I actively look for.

It's just an idea, and it may give wizards some added benefit that might
make them more fun to play.
