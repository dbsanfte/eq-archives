>Spam is still spam, even if the advert is for something related to the

Uh... are you confusing Spam with Advertising?  They are not at all the
same.  If it's something related to the groups where it was posted, and
the groups don't disallow advertisements, it's not spam.  Even if the
groups did disallow adverts, it's still not spam, just a charter
violation.  In reference to Usenet, new terms were coined due to the
confusion of "what is spam," ECP (excessive crossposting) and EMP
(excessive multiposting).  Both of these together are usually
considered to encompass the definition of spam (along with a mathematical
index higher than a certain threshold).  None of these are violated
by a carefully tailored advertisement sent to a handful of groups.

I have, on rare occasion, left a very carefully-tailored advertisement
in one of the groups I moderate (which doesn't allow ads), because the
advertiser took such obvious pains to make sure it went to the proper
places and was free from hype.
