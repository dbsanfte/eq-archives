As a Rogue and in all honesty, the only time I found having a Wizard
with me bearable was when he was ripped to the tits on Clarity and we
had a Bard howling manna song non-stop.

They are just too inefficient, especially when it comes to an experience
grind. They're quickly out of manna using their (often resisted) Direct
Damage, and they have absolutely nothing left to offer other than
Evacuate and transport to the Planes. I got tired waiting around for
Wizards to meditate back up at about level 12, and I've been grouping
exclusively since level 8.

On the other hand, as long as your meleers are healthy their damage just
keeps on going, and going, and going. It really adds up. It's never
ending mob after mob evisceration while you are sat down meditating.
Enchanters' haste spells, indirectly through melee probably out damage
Wizards by an order of magnitude. Damage Over Time spells defecate over
your DDs from a great height as well. Not to mention pets. Wizards are
out of it after a couple of poxy nukes.

Also, one or two nukes and the mobs promptly go apeshit at you poor
sods, and my fellow meleers and I have the unenviable task of getting it
off you quickly before you fall over dead, which is far more easily said
than done. Fizzlers crumple, and Wizards generate far too much hate for
too little benefit.

Now, I'm not exactly a powergamer, and definitely not a number-cruncher.
I like being evil and sneaky, and the Rogue was the class for me. I
picked a Dark Elf Rogue and even gave him a religion. Finding that I
could be so lethal was a pleasant surprise. If you want numbers to back
up what I'm saying here, you may want to dig up the posts of some of the
more sagacious number-crunchers like Alasdair.

In my opinion, the only thing Wizards are good for is with their fellow
fizzlers knocking caster mobs down quickly. And they're not even that
good at that, all things considered. Unless Verant goes and does
something drastic, and I do mean drastic, I'm afraid as far as I am
concerned Wizards are a waste of space I'm sorry to say.
