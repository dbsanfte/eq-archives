On Mon, 07 Jun 1999 08:27:52 GMT, matuse73@yahoo.spam.me.not.com (Matt Frisch)
wrote:

|The money is just as created and infinite supply if it comes from mobs, has
|to be earned with a trade skill, falls from the sky, or is teleported
|directly into your bank account.

	Not true.  The money from mobs is limited by the system in terms of how
much gets put there in any given day.  It will vary depending on the inherent
randomness of loot generation and whether or not the PCs actually kill the
critter and sell off any non-coin loot, but the designers have (or at least
should have) an expected outcome for the amount of money that's being added to
the economy.

	The money you get from the NPCs just comes out of thin air, though.  You
can try to limit it in various ways, but they're mostly unsatisfying, and it
will interfere with those not practicing craft skills; at the very least you
will have to either reduce the amount generated by mobs to account of the
amount being introduced by tradesmen, or you will have to raise prices to
compensate (which is basically the same thing as far as the PCs are
concerned).

	It's far better to make the trades horizontal instead of vertical; rather
than have them introduce money into the economy, just have them move what's
already brought in from the mobs around.
