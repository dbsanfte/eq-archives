Well, not really.

As you level, you become a better fighter without any skill increases.
Your chance to hit and average damage goes up through the sole act
of levelling. The same goes for the average damage you take and
the chance for a MOB to hit you. According to a friend of mine
who's run a bunch of tests, through levelling alone, without any
skill increases, you get about a 33% gain in offense effectiveness
on MOBs per level. Skill increases make up the rest of the difference.

Monks improve more per level than any other class, mainly because
we have no items. Brett has hypothesised that your HTH damage
rating, as a monk is level / 2 + 1, then figure in strength bonuses.
What I've seen pretty much backs this claim up. So by 50 I'll have
25 dmg 30 del fists, which can DW and DA. That's a pretty mean
weapon, throw a sash on it and you're doing even better.

It's worth noting that at 47 I've seen my punches get as high as
57 damage. I do have about 130 STR, which figures in, no doubt.

That said, a large factor of my real-life performance is based on
the equipment I carry. I did need to camp for a sash, do the Hero
Bracer quest, camp the Crown of the Froglok King, etc.,. I need my
equipment to be really effective in combat. I do better naked than
any other melee/hybrid, but that doesn't mean an awful lot.

I do out-taunt paladins, hands down, except agains undead, and
excluding Plane Armor, my AC is about the same as theirs, Mend is
a much better skill than LOH, I do a lot more damage, and I taunt
better. So, as tanks go, Monks are a much better tank than pallies.
