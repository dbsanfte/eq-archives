I think Verant did a very good job on EQ.  Some aspects of the game
are better than others.  

- The interface could really use work.  The mouse is required for things
  that could be mapped to keys quite easily and the limitations on hot
  keys are ridiculous.  Verant's policy of secrecy is maddening when combined
  with unintuitive game mechanics.

= The classes are fairly well balanced and the game mechanics are reasonable.
  In UO, game mechanics were a definite minus.

+ The content of the world is wonderful.  The zones look great.  The monsters
  look great.  The characters look great.  There are lots of cute details in
  the quests and conversations.  I have yet to find a direct contradiction in
  any of the quests or story lines.  The huge variety of interesting spells
  is really nice.  I love Verant's general philosophy that people have to be 
  respectful of each other and are not allowed to grossly violate the fantasy 
  theme.

I would say that "Best Game Ever" would have to go to Civ II, but EQ is a
very good game and definitely the "Best MMORPG Ever". ;)
