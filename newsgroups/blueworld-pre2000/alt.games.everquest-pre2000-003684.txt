Xenu wrote:
> 
> When my character was young I saved all words etc in order to sell
> them, but they quickly clogged upp my bank and I grew tired. Now, it
> seems like some words etc are actually worth sparing. Which ones should
> I keep for the friendly casters and for those who pays?

One easy filter is to sell the cheaper ones and keep the more 
expensive ones.  Higher level words/runes are more expensive,
harder to find, and generally worth more.  

Words and runes are harder to track than pages.  Enchanter's
pages come in sets.  First you work through Tasarin's Grimoire,
then Velishoul's Tome, Salil's Writ, and finally Nitilim's 
Grimoire.  So if you are saving pages for an Enchanter, they 
can tell you which pages are still useful.
