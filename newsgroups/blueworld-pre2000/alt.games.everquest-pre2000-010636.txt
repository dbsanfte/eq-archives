Patently untrue, as I've had a good deal of fun with
an all mage group.  Further, people have reported
enjoying all bard groups, all wizard groups, all cleric
groups...

Now you can argue that only the two groups you
mention would be Optimal, that the other group
possibilities are missing something important, one
way or another.  I'd like to see some analysis
rather than a bald faced statement of fact, though.

What, precisely, are Mages missing, for example?
Crowd control is the weak suit, Earth pet root is
a wimpy version of this, and takes a bit of skill
to use well.  TO counter this, though, you have
as many as 6 tanks and six nukers; crowds sufficient
to overwhelm that big a group are not all that
common.  In fact, you have better than 6 tanks; you
have tanks that can be replaced...  normal tanks
have a long run back from their bind points and
do a lot of complaining about their exp loss while
they do it!   I can't think of anything else the
6 mage group is particularly weak on.  It isn't
optimal, five mages and an enchanter would
be better, and four mages, an enchanter, and
a bard better still...  but then we are out of the
same class group zone entirely.
