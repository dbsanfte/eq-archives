Here is how specialization works.  Find your error. ;)

You have to train at least one point in a specialization to advance it.
This will start you with a skill of 20 in that specialization.

No specialization can advance past 50 until you have trained at least
one point in all five specializations.  (This is most likely what you
forgot to do.)

Only one of your five specializations will advance past 50.  Once one
of your specializations has reached 51, that is your primary 
specialization and the other four will be capped at 50.
