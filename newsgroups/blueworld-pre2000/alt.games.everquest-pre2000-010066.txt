Jonas Datum wrote:
> 
> what is a dd spell?

DD = Direct Damage

The spell hurts the target immediately after it is cast and generally
has no lasting effect.

DoT = Damage over Time

The spell does some damage to the target once every 6 seconds until
it wears off or the creature dies.
