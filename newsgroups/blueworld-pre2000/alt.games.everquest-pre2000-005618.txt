Mystarian Phathumber <Mystarian@yahoo.com> wrote:
> I have heard that you can learn languages by training them partially and
> then practicing between two people in a group until they are both excelent.

> Does anyone know how this works?

> Do you need to be atleast a certain mastery level before this will work or
> can you do it with only one training point spent?

You can train any language to one skill point higher than your current skill
level.

You have to zone before any language skill levels take effect.

You learn languages via /groupsay, but you have to be in the same zone.

Keeping this in mind, here's what you do.

Person A and Person B want to get to Excellent in Elder Dragon.  A is awful
in it, B doesn't know it at all.

They group.  A spams B with Elder Dragon until B stops learning.

Both zone.  Then B spams A until A gets two skill increases.  He won't get
any more.

Zone again.  A spams B until B gets two increases.  Zone and repeat until
you're excellent.
