Yes, essentially.  The lack of faction makes it fairly cheesy.  To be

the fact that people like something doesn't necessarily mean that it 
should stay the way it is.

> >It would be easy to make Guk and Permafrost just like South Karana.

The Shin Lord room provides a very nice little base of operations,
away from any train tracks and not overly crowded with spawn points.
Most of Guk is twisty passageways with too many froggie spawns.

> Also I think they should either add a small amount of exp for greens or remove
> the agro, and since you bring up faction why not fix whatever is wrong with the
> faction in guk?  After people spend so much time killing undead frogs and
> raising faction they should be able to waltz around upper guk without agroing
> anything (until they kill their faction again).

I think the problem with faction in Guk is just that the hit you take
for killing one frog is much larger than the increase you get for 
killing one undead frog.  It would be nice if bad faction in Guk were
reparable, but it looks like it isn't.  However, considering that those
people with really bad froggie faction probably spent a goodly amount 
of time repeatedly slaughtering a hero of the realm, it makes sense 
that it would take an astounding effort to make them think well of you
again. ;)

> >High Keep and South Karana are two places I hate to go.  Their basic

> Or you could say that the ratio of experience and risk in SK and HK are

If the whole world were balanced like SK and HK, everyone would be 
level 50 in a month.  The only reason they can't now is that those
two zones fill up with people.

> The point I am trying to make is that nerfing High Keep or South Karana will not

Of course nerfing South Karana won't make the other areas better.  Guk and 
Permafrost have problems that need to be addressed.  The big one (in my 
opinion) is green aggro.  Nasty things that can and will kill you should
give some reward when vanquished.  A lot of places are balanced very well
in terms of reward for risk.  I think Cazic and Unrest are great dungeons.
Crushbone, as I recall it, was also a nice place to hunt.  Mistmoore also
provided good challenge and worthwhile loot.  Solusek A has way too much
green aggro garbage, as well as annoying little bridges and holes over lava
that are just not worth navigating.

> >I would not suggest changing the loot or the experience.  I just think

I imagine that you would never gain as much faction from killing the enemy as
you would lose by killing them, so at best you could pick which of the two
groups would hate you if you were going to start killing either of them.
