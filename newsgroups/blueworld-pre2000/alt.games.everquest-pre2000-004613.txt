Nerfing or buffing doesn't happen as a result of whining.  People whine
when they think something is unfair.  When something really is unfair,
such as the amazingly overpowered Necromancer pets, you hear a lot of 
whining about it.  It getting fixed is a result of Verant figuring out
that it is broken, not a result of whining.  (It's that whole correlation
vs. causation thing.)

In general, I don't think most people are opposed to nerfing.  I think 
they are opposed to being nerfed, but that's a somewhat separate issue. ;)
