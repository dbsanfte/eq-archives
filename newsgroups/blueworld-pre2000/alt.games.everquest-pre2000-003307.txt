I also use the numpad for navigation rather than the mouse.

7 - look up
8 - forwards
4 - left
5 - backwards
6 - right
1 - look down
+ - attack

I use various letter keys on my left hand for auto-target, jump,
consider, and the rest.  Maybe it's just me, but I like to have
the jump key on the other hand from the movement key because I
fail at playing chords while trying to run away. ;)

It's a good idea to use the numpad keys for attack and such because
you can still use them while in talk mode.  (I don't know how many
times I died saying "Greetings~~~~~~" when I had attack on the tilde
key.
