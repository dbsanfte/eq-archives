Targz wrote:
> 
> And can anyone tell me how to change specialization, and can you
> only have one?

You can have some specialization skill in each of the five magic
types, but only one can go past 50.  Your primary specialization
can go to 200 eventually.

None of the five will go past 50 until you have put at least one
point into all five.

If you want to change your primary specialization to another school
of magic, there is a quest in the Temple of Ro.  Talk to Ostorm and
give him a ruby and 50gp.  All your specializations will be reduced
to 49.  Sit down and cast spells from your new choice until the skill
in that school reaches 51 and then go about your business and gain
the skill normally.
