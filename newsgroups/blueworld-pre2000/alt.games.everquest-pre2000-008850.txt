Morgan <morgana@misleading.com> wrote:

Not that I disagree with most of your arguments, nor agree with the (rather 
draconian AND poorly framed, to boot) petition, but:

> Get rid of /anon so that nobody ever has to say what class and
> level they are.  Anonymity hurts role play by making characters
> less distinctive and forcing ooc communication.

If people truly _want_ to roleplay, this is not a particular problem.  You know
the _basic_ type of a character based on what they're holding/wearing (unless
they're truly naked, in which case they may be TRYING to be anonymous).  A 
simple, in character, method of asking for class:

"Excuse me, good sir, but I can tell by the way you hold your weapon that you
must be a warrior of mighty skills indeed.  We might have need of someone with
such skills..."

Now, is that as "direct" as saying "What lvl/class?"  Of course not.  But 
assuming everything _else_ about the RP server assumptions are true, it's a 
perfectly great segue into character explanation which encourages RP.  Similar
arguments hold true for other apparent class distinctions

e.g. "... I see you wear the robes of a magi..." or "... you seem quite at home
in the outdoors.. (for the everpresent female, wood-elf druid look)"

> Enforcement comes from a small panel of judges who decide which
> guilds are up to par and what kind of behavior is acceptable.  Is
> that correct?  The guild-based squealing will get you part of the
> way there, but people are generally not inclined to turn in their
> friends, so you will still need an objective authority.

Any such systems have tremendous amount of leeway for abuse.  Ignoring that
problem, a generic "player voting" system which tied your maximum level 
attainable to a ratio of good/bad RP votes would serve well in such a medium.

Both solutions, of course, completely ignore the abuse of power argument.  
Dealing with this is the _difficult_ part of trying to do RP only servers.

>   Allow each account only one character on the server.

What could this possible have to do with RP?  What if I want to RP a dark elf
AND a wood elf, who know nothing of each other?  This seems more like an 
anti-twink type argument.  Hell, twinking should be perfectly _acceptable_ on 
a RP server, so long as you've got a DAMN good story as to where the equpi came
from.  For that matter, I disagree with you on the PK issue, too.  Mainly 
from the viewpoint that PK as a conflict vehicle is perfectly sound.  It's the
PK d00dz who are the problem.  And as long as we're living in a fantasy world
where RP is enforceable, why not live in one where intelligent and mature PK
is as well? :)
