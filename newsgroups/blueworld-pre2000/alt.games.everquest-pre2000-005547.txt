Sapphires are for sale in the jewelry shop in North Freeport.  It's
a blue building just outside the market square.  The merchants are
named Jade and Amber (and, ironically, sell neither jade nor amber).

In general, the merchants who sell metal bars also sell star rubies,
fire emeralds, and sapphires.  The rest of the gems, including rubies,
are sold at gem merchants.  There is no general gem merchant in 
Freeport, but you can get many of the other gems from the mage shop
since they are used as reagents for spells.

The nearest sources for rubies are Neriak (The Bauble, Third Gate) 
and High Keep (Merchant Edina, above the bank).

They are also for sale in Qeynos, Kelethin, Felwithe, Ak'Anon
and Erudin.  The jewel merchant table at EQCasters also lists
Grobb and Kaladim.

Anything that says "18 Types" on this table should have rubies:
