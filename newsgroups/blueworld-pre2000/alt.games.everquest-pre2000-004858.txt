Below is a table in tab format (sorry, will probably mess up on some
fonts) of the mean median and mode levels in various zones. You could
use this as a rough guide to the question
	"where can I hunt now?"
Note that where there are low Obs (nr of people observed in a zone)
then the stats may be way off.

To give you an idea, the average level in blackburrow is 12.7, but you
can start there around 6 or 7 up the top and stay until about level 18
or 19.

ecommons is distorted by the high level traders there. I would rate it

center)

Max = maximum level

Note that there are all sorts of shortcomings with this data as
discussed in "EQ Population study"  29 Jan 2000 in alt.games.everquest
