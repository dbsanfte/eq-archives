1. Cross the water to zone to Everfrost.

2. Go passed the merchants, left, passed the guards.

3. Follow the path into everfrost, at the 1st "crossroad" go left.

4. Go down that path to the next "crossroad" you will notice that
it goes straight, slightly to the right is another path that looks
to go straight, and off further to the right is a path that looks
to go somewhat back and to the right (so left, center, and right).
Take the path that is in the center (righthand/straight), that leads
out to where the dog snowflake is.
