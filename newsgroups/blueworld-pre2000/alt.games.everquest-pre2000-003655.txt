>One other non-tax related note:  Weapons should get old and break
>and armor should get damaged and become unserviceable after a
>while so they must be replaced.


    Now this one's almost feasible, and we could kill three birds with one
stone this way!

    Have a "many hands" penalty.  The more often an item gets passed from
one PC to another, the more likely it is to break later.  If you loot it or
buy it, it'll always be perfect.  If you give or sell it to another person,
it has, say, a 0.05% chance of degrading somehow every time you use it.  If
it gets traded to a second person, it now has a 0.1% chance of degrading.
And so on.

What does this solve?
- The economy.  Stuff will get WAY cheaper unless you buy it directly from a
merchant.
- Item camping on EBay.  People won't pay outrageous prices for stuff,
therefore there is less incentive to camp items.
- Twinking.  Your first-level character you just gave your third Ykesha to
won't have it forever any more.
