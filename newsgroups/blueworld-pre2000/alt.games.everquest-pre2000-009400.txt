It's definitely higher than you would want to wait for.  Rangers 
don't get Camoflage until level 15.  I left the Faydark to do my 
first quests at about level 5 and moved to Antonica to find new 
hunting grounds at about 10.

I would advise any wanderers under level 5 to put everything in
the bank before setting out.  That way you don't lose anything
other than travel time if you die.

As for the guys in High Pass, I don't know about those three in
particular, but there are a lot of seedy characters around there.
The leader of High Keep, Carson McCabe, is in league with the 
corrupt guards in Qeynos and Freeport.  He's a smuggler and all
around bad guy.  You will probably be able to pass freely when
you have gotten to a higher level and killed some of the gnolls
and orcs around there, but for now be very leery of the people
in High Pass.
