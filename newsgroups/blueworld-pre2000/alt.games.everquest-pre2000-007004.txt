Your solution has two flaws. One it requires changes (I expect big ones) to
EQ and second it doesnt really solve the "you have to camp to get a really
cool item" problem - and the related one which is people EBay camping.

A better solution IMO would be to have more alternatives. You can either go
camp the SMR in LGuk (cos you enjoy camping for 20 hours without the arch
mage ever showing) or you can do a quest which takes 20 hours but can be
split that gives you an alternate item but with slightly differing stats (I
don't know, perhaps Robes of Reflection 25MR 50mana 30hp - something casters
would value as much as the stats you get on an SMR).

And that's the problem I think. Basically there's only one line of
progression for many items.  I'm going to continue with the casters robes
theme, but applies to many items - armour, weapons pretty much everything.
For a caster the robe progression is: newbie robes --> apprentice robes -->
thaumatergist robe --> flowing black robes --> robe of the oracle --> shiny
metal robes. Mages and Necros get to choose something different at the same
level as the oracle robe but that's it. So every caster level 20+ is looking
for FBR then ROTO, every caster 35+ starts looking for SMR. And you get a
log jam. Perhaps if some mean mob in SolA and Kedge and maybe Paw (probably
too low level) dropped a robe of equivalent value to the SMR there would be
less need to camp. And there would be more variety in casters appearance.
Its interesting but twinks aside you can tell the level of a caster by their
robes. Now that is *yawn* boring. Verant half sold the problem (Robe of the
Keeper and enshroudment), but it would have been nice if enchanters and
wizzies also got a quest only robe - which was about as nice as the Oracle
robe.

Anyway you get the idea.
