My vote is for Agility.  At higher levels, you'll be spending most of your
time in a group, with other people helping to do damage.  The Agility will
help you stay alive while you all beat on the mobs.

Also, in case you haven't seen this in your research: when you start your
new monk, train one point into your Dodge skill so it will go up.  All the
other 1st level skills will go up with use, even if you don't train them,
but Dodge won't go up on its own until you've trained in it.
