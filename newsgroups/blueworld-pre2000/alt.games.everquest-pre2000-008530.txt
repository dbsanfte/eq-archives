How many mobs per hour can you kill?  How many mobs per hour can you
pick pocket?  Your argument makes sense if you are picking pockets
of monsters you could kill in the same time it takes you to steal
from them.

If you were fighting, you would be taking a small number of mobs out
of the general pool.  Each one would probably be replaced by a new 
spawn in the time it took you to kill the next.  The net impact of
your activity is to decrease by one the number of mobs that are
available for others to hunt at any given moment.  Other people are
limited by the amount of time it takes to make each kill and recover
afterwards.  They get full experience and full loot for all of the
kills they can make.

If you are picking pockets, you can pick a few times and move on,
as you say.  There is one more creature available in the general
pool of prey for other people to get, but many of the creatures 
have less loot on them.  The net effect on other people is that 
they kill as many creatures as they were before, but they get less
reward for it.

> Not to
> mention the fact that if I could kill it, I probably wouldn't be picking its
> pockets.

That destroys your whole argument.  Saying that it is better to 
pick its pocket than kill it only holds water if you could actually
kill the thing.
