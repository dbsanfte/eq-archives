Yep. My own tests pretty much confirmed this. I'm at 60% hit ratio
no matter what I wear. That said, STR does increase average damage
rather dramatically for some of the high level shaman buffs. 40
points has given me about 4 more points of damage on average. That's
about a 20 to 25% increase for me.

It's worth noting, also, that from tests a friend of mine ran, AC
doesn't affect how often you are hit either. He dropped his AC on
the order of 120 points and there was less than 1% difference on
hit ratios. AC /does/ affect how much damage you take for those
hits, though. That 120 points of AC gave him about 20% damage
absorbtion, or so, IIRC.

What's that mean? Two things, one for melee characters, one for
casters:

Melee:
	When getting buffs, there are only two or three worth
	anything with regards to stats: STR, so you do more damage,
	STA, so you can take more, and maybe DEX for proccing.
	Shaman buffs last an hour, so the downtime on casting the
	STR and STA on your party is negligible, long term, for
	the effectiveness you gain in killing MOBs faster.

Casters:
	Since interruptions are only based off of whether you move
	or not, and how often you are hit (not how much you are
	hit for), AC is only useful for keeping you alive through
	hits, but it /will not/ help you channel better.
