It seems that everyone is interpreting "individual characters" as "I
want to play solo". It's nice to look after yourself, and be
independant and all that, but you really could do that with a non net
game. It is fun to have the human on the other end of trading/chat,
and the inevitable my character/your character comparisons, but think
carefully before you start shelling out $ to your ISP and Verant, for
a game that you're gonna play by yourself, with other people in the
background.

Most people solo to about level 10 anyway, but you should give
grouping a go. Even if you're only partly on the ball then you will
learn all about group dynamics, spot the good player vs greedy bastard
/ overbearing leader / idiot companion / nice guy - poor tactician /
10yr old (ahem 13) / etc. Of course, we are all learning so give
people a chance, and talk through alternatives (deficiencies) in post
mortem.

Then you should relax and not concentrate on levelling but on fun.
Trades, quests, haggling, giving newbies stuff, helping those out of
near death experiences.

As for rogues ... I love 'em. I have played a Druid, Necromancer,
Paladin, Celric and finally a  Rogue, with which I have had the most
fun. Mind you, I think you get better and better at playing characters
as time goes by. I've also played on a couple servers, and I'm not too
sure about this but I get the feeling that the bastard ratio is higher
on newer servers.

Would I recommend EverQuest? No. Turn back now, it isn't too late.
This game will suck all the spare time out of your life and start
eating into time that would have been spent in more profitable
pursuits, like sleep.
