a group, mostly because my giant snake fang and my pet do considerable

bit more, i think. Add to that my 10-12 damage a hit with my gianrt snake
fang, and the 10-12 damage my pet is doing(juli's animation worked for me
from the beginning) and i feel that i do contribute something to a group os
