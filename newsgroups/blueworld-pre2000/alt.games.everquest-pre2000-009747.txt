What you are saying here is that in one situation (exp grind) against
specific mobs (non-casters) Wizards are non-optimal (which is a fair
distance from useless).
No-one can disgree with that. I tried once, but you can't argue with the
figures.
That leaves a myriad of cases where a Wizard can shine, or even be desired.

thread, but as well as taking down casting mobs fast (especially healers),
there are few classes better for helping to break a tough room when there is
no enchanter/necro available to mez stuff.
