Don't confuse skins and pelts. They're relatively different.

Pelts come in three sizes and four qualities. You have wolf (small),
cat (medium) and bear (large), along with ruined, low quality, medium
quality and high quality.

All these are used in tailoring. Ruined makes patchwork armor (intermediate
between cloth and leather), medium quality makes studded (leather, except
for 1 piece), and high quality makes reinforced (intermediate between
leather and mail).

The market for high quality pelts exist, but apart from bear petls, which
can be tailored into 10-slots containers, it's real narrow. It's mainly for
people who try to raise their tailoring skill for fun, as most reinforced
pieces don't sell back. You'll get 1pp, maybe 2 if you are providing
large amounts as a bonus.

Skins are different. Skins are not used in tailoring, they are used in
pottery, mainly to make vials. The Puma Skin is worth as much as a medium
quality cat pelt, but is only used for pottery (and any skin works).
