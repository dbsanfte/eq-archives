It's probably a lag issue.  I noticed that my SOW got interrupted a lot and
eventually I noticed that it was usually interrupted when I was traveling
somewhere and then stopped to cast it.  When you have a 500 or so ping, it
takes the server a half-second to a second to know that you've stopped, so
if you stop and immediately cast a spell, it is interrupted because it
thinks you're still moving.

Try counting to 3 after stopping before casting a spell, this should get rid
of the problem.
