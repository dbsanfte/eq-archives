> where do I go to get these rings?  are they lore?

They are the common drops of the Dwarven Ghost, in Unrest. Level 35 mob.
(The rare are his dwarven workboots, which are what people are after,
mainly).

And no, of course, they are not Lore.

Unless you are below 100 STR, I would not suggest getting two, however.
It cost a bit more, but Fire Opal Wedding Rings have AC5, HP+55 and will
help you more than +3 STR, notably at low levels. My own suggestion would
be:

For a monk: 2 wedding rings, always
For a plate tank: below 100 str, 2 jagged, below 125 str, jagged+fire,
	above 125, forget the jagged, get 2 fire.

Given your characters, you're a looong way before you can afford either,
I think :)
