Chris Divine wrote:
> 
> 1.) Does anyone know where I can find info on skill caps? My
> paladin had 2H Slash stuck at 40 at level 8. It bumped to 41 the
> moment I dinged to level 9, and it is 50 now and it doesn't seem to
> be going higher. Is 50 the max for my level? How can I know? Is
> there a chart posted anywhere for various skills, races and classes?

Normally, the skill cap is (Level x 5 + 5) for skills and
(Level x 5) for Offense and Defense.  There are a number of
exceptions to those rules.  All skills have a maximum value
that varies widely by class.  Trade skills (Jewelcraft,
Tailoring, Smithing, etc.) are not bounded by level, but have 
a cap of 200.  Language skills have a cap of 100, but you
never see the skill values.

The class creation guides at Casters' Realm list all of the 
skill caps.

http://eq.castersrealm.com/

http://eq.castersrealm.com/creationguides/Paladin.asp

> 2.) What is the difference between LORE ITEM, NO DROP,
> NO RENT, etc. for items? What will I lose if I log off? Can
> they be kept in the bank?

LORE = You can only have one of the item in your inventory or 
    bank box.

NO DROP = The item cannot be dropped, traded or sold.  There
    is no way to give a NO DROP item to another person.  These
    items can be handed to NPCs, and often must be in order to
    complete quests.

NO RENT = The item will vanish when you camp or go linkdead.
    If it is a container, such as a Spirit Pouch, all of its
    contents will also vanish.  All summoned items and a few
    quest pieces are NO RENT.  There is no indication on the 
    item that it is NO RENT.

> For instance, I have 3 of the 4 "Raw Short Swords" from the orcs
> in East Commons, and am working on getting the 4th to complete
> a quest. Can I keep them in the bank? (they weigh 10 each, so I
> become encumbered really quickly).

Yes, the bank is equivalent to your inventory.  It is safe
to put the items in the bank.
