Well, speaking as an Enchanter, I can't really comment on how
useful a Shaman is in a group with no Enchanter.  However, I love
having a Shaman in a group.  In theory, I can slow the monsters.
In practice, I can't.  I am normally busy doing crowd control
while the biggest baddest thing we have pulled is beating down
the tanks.  If we only have one foe, then I have to wait until
the thing is down some health before I slow it so that I don't
get beaten on.

With a Shaman in the group, the monsters get slowed right away
and I don't get pounded down nearly as often.  Having the
Enchanter get pounded on is bad for everyone.  The Enchanter gets
smacked for full damage every time, taking up loads of healing
power which slows down the group, or they die and slow down the
group even more.  The Shaman is far more likely to survive the
aggro after slowing because they have real armor and Chloroplast.

We are all just there to help out the group.  I keep the extra
monsters off the tanks while they kill things.  You keep the
monsters from hitting the tanks too hard and take them down
faster with poison and disease.  My abilities pretty well suck
without other people, but that does not mean that I am not needed
for my own abilities.
