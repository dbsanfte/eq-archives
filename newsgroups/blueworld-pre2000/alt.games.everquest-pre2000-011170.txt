Assuming you don't need AE spells, I would say that they are not
at all needed except for Hate raids.

Druids can handle evacs just as well as a wizard, and can do
backup healing/snare/sow as well. They even have decent DD/AE
, just not quite wizard level.

IMO it's not that AE can't work, it's that you need
a) the right group makeup (enchanter, 2+ high level ae casters,
   and a bard)
b) the right place (where your ae nukes won't aggro through
   walls)
c) the right mobs (ones that can't resist your snare/ae effects)
d) a high availability of mobs (you need to fetch a lot of mobs
   at once in order to get the efficiency levels you're bragging
   about)

Since the probability of getting all of those things without
a regular group set up for this is pretty near zero, people
tend to pretty much ignore AE. Anyone disagree?
