Would you, as a member of another class, not also be in the same (as you
so put it) "whining, snivvling [snivelling], newbielooking" train? what
if you were a warrior and all you armor, even the nice enchanted plate,

looked like your plain apprentice robe? What if all the newbie casters'
class robes were faded blue? Have you seen a 50th level halfling druid
who has +WIS armor on instead of the "go for the look" bone armor? They
look like busboys holding a dolly and a book with those big brass
buttons on their tunics!

EQ, by its design and wild success, has transgressed beyond the realm of
a plain game and into the realm of an online social community (one with
its share of posers and whiners, to be sure, but a community
nonetheless). and communities need some flavor and color; they need some
variety.

This is why many of us are calling for non-combat wear that does nothing
but look different, or for armor that actually shows up on the body. My
druid put on some armbands a while back (platinum, I believe) and his
arms went from leather green to naked rusty tunic! I was hoing for some
plate-ish looking arms, at least, if nothing else.
