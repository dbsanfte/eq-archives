I think the idea is to learn the hard way at around level 12 when
death takes only half an hour to recover from.

Some ideas for finding good groups:

When you join a group, expect your group members to identify 
themselves and give you a plan of attack.  At the very least,
they should tell you who to /assist.  If there is an Enchanter or
Bard, they should explain how they work if anyone is confused.

If casters are going OOM after easy fights, you probably don't
want to group with them.  

Only Wizards and Magicians should be nuking regularly.  Other 
casters nuke only to practice Evocation when pulls are slow and 
they are swimming in mana.

Watch for tanks that don't understand or use Taunt or Kick.

Avoid greedy looters.  Even if you don't need the loot, it's an
indication that they are not concerned for other people.

Some people are just ignorant about how to play their class and 
will play better if you give them some pointers.  I was with a 
Cleric who kept nuking until OOM.  After I told her to save her 
mana for heals, I stopped dying and we had a great time.

Never ever group with someone who says, "I guess I can't actually
solo here.  Anyone want to group?"
