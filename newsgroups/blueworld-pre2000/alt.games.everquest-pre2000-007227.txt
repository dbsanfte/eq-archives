I think it is an unhealthy social dynamic.  You have a small number
of people who are on top, using those below them in the structure to
get money and items.  The people on the bottom stick around because
they feel like they have to be in a guild if they ever want access
to the high level areas in the game.

I am not sure what the guildwar command is supposed to do in this
situation.  I doubt the guild is going to accept the declaration of 
war.  If they wanted PvP, they would be playing on a PvP server.
This also requires that for every bad guild there exists another
guild of comparable strength that is willing to police the bad guild.
