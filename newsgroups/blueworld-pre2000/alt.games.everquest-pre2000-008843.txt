I've played all three, so I can answer - DO NOT GET EVERQUEST.  It is a
pathetic piece of crap.  I played it for three days before deciding that it
would have to burn in eternal hellfire.  (As in, I lit the CD on fire)

I'll just do a pros and cons list -

Everquest:

A hack and slash game that happens to have other people on it.

Pros:
Gameplay designed for simpletons (only a pro if you are kind of slow)
Lots of races
Lots of classes
Lots of spells

Cons:
Long load times between tiny zones
Inferior graphics
Crappy updates
Crappy game system
Crappy people
Repetitive "become a better thief by killing trolls" advancement system
Boring quests
Imbalanced classes
Bad lag
Asshole GMs
Really bad handling of PvP (people who go PK are too restricted and will
rarely find opponents)
Exploits abound
Bad landmass (It's broken into "zones" like in MUDS, but each has long load
times between each.  Travel is boring)
Campers heaven
So combat oriented it's practically Doom Online

Ultime Online:

A good game, great user base.  A rough, unfinished feel but it does serve
all types of players.

Pros:
Established, won't be going down any time soon
Huge landmass now that the new patch is out
PvP switch
Craft skills handled well

Cons:
Lots of assholes
Magery unbalanced
Dated graphics
Boring combat
Lots of exploits

Asherons Call:

Picture Daggerfall with no bugs, better graphics, and 3,000 people on at a
time.

Pros:
Great graphics
Great people (lot less assholes, most were filtered out by Everquest and
Ultima)
Possible to adventure alone
Inventive quests every month
Great updates - for an example, last months patch added in a new town, a new
sword, a new magic interface, improved the AI, and fixed a bunch of bugs.
That is typical of one month's patching
Great magic system
Great land mass

Cons:
Archers a little unbalanced
Olthoi not creepy enough
Shadows too creepy (anyone got the sound effect that the Spires play as a
..wav file?)
Not enough of a user base
