that their finances are not unlimited (and it does cost money for them to go
to court, even when lawyers working for it agree to work for free), neither

victory, not when it looses or when it has to turn down helping someone
because it can't spare staff or resources. Poor people get routinely
trampled on in the courts by the rich and by industry because they can't
afford decent legal representation.
