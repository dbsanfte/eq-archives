A little off topic, but my two cents on this -

In the 20's you're probably having a hard luck time, but I think a lot
of classes go through this in the 20's.  As a druid, when you hit
30-35 I will almost insist on having a cleric present.  

Any priest class who is not a cleric - their top healing spell is
Greater Healing. - 270pts.  Even in areas such as Cazic Thule (not
even the Avatar of Fear) melee'ers can get in over their heads, and
easily over the heads a druid.  If there are multiple druids/shamans,
then you might be ok, but a cleric's stun spell is also a nice tidbit
to have against those casters.

Once you get even higher up 36-40ish a 270pt heal spell won't do
budkis for your 1.5k HP tanks.  You'll be OOM before you've healed 4/7
of their HP  :-)
