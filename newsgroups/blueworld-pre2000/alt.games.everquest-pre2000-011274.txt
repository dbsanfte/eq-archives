No, it doesn't fail.  Unless you specifically get the "Your target has
resisted the Harmony spell" message, it took.

What you most likely saw was a misunderstanding of what harmony
actually does.  It doesn't make mobs stupid or "sleep".  What Harmony
does is reduces the aggro range of the mob.  The amount by which it is
reduced is related to the level of the caster and the target.  The
greater the difference in favor of the caster, the smaller the aggro
radius.

When you harmony a bunch and pull and get more than one, odds are the
reduction in the radius wasn't sufficient to get single pulls.  Or you
may have just not targetted the correct one.

Many have reported failure of Harmony on shadowmen.  Usually comments
like "they are very resistent" or "Harmony doesn't work on them",
etc...

Shadowmen are probably the single most annoying mobs to target
properly because all you have are those stupid names over invisible
mobs.  I've even stood right infront of them clicking like crazy
trying to target them--no dice.
