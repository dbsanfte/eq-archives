As Gordon has said there are only a few things that affect how much
damage you do and miss rates: offense skill, weapon skill, and
level. Strength also increases damage caps.

Now, given that, and the fact that monks have a higher cap for
offense and weapon skills than paladins, and have the same level
cap (50), a monk, without strength bonuses, will hit more than a
paladin, and harder, too.

Given that monks typically have higher strength, their damage caps
also tend to be higher.

Dry bones or spite golems, at the same level, excluding procs (and
the vast majority of pally weapons don't have procs), a monk will
outdamage a paladin, rather significantly.

Other classes, most notably the rogue, will outdamage monks, and
with planar gear you can bet that a warrior, ranger, or rogue will
leave a monk in the dust for damage. That's the problem. But pallies
aren't a threat to monk damage, IMHO.

>The bottom line here is , a monk can't tank anywhere near as good as a
>paladin. I played them both to 50 and I stand by what I'm saying.

Outside of planar gear, I disagree. My AC is currently 50 or 60
points higher than a barb warrior friend of mine in full crafted.
It's even higher compared to a paladin or ranger. Moreover, my
Defense skill hasn't capped. Once it caps it'll be a lot higher.

My hitpoints are at least equivalent to a Paladin, if you count in
Mend, which you should. Given that I have significantly better AC
and equivalent HPs, I can take a beating better than a paladin.

The only reason I'm not a better tank than a paladin, ranger, or
SK is that I don't have a Taunt button.

With planar armour, though, there's no comparison. The AC on planar
armour is insane, with the exception of shiverback. Shiverback has
almost nothing in the way of stat perks or AC, and is only marginally
better than the stuff I got off of level 30 MOBs, and in some cases
I don't think I'd swap out my current stuff for shiverback (except
that I really dig the look). So a warrior, pally, ranger, SK, and
maybe even the rogue, in full planar gear, can take a beating better
than a monk, and the rogue, ranger, and warrior can significantly
outdamage the monk as well. And that's the problem at high levels,
that's why monks are getting a built in haste.
