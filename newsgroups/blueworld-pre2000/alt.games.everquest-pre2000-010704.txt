DriftingDust wrote:
> 
> I will be joining two friends of mine for what will most often be 3 person
> groups.
> 
> We have one Druid, and one Necro.
> 
> Seems like an Enchanter or a Mage might be the best way to complete the
> trio.
> 
> Any opinions?

The Magician would be fairly redundant with the Necromancer.  I would 
choose a real fighter over the Magician.  A Warrior, Ranger, Paladin,
Shadowknight or Monk would work well.  Bards are not great tanks, and
pretty redundant with the Druid.  I would not trust the Necro pet to
taunt well enough to make a Rogue viable.  Since you are making a 
permanent group, you can rely on the Druid and Necro to bind you or run 
to town to buy things for you when you need it and for the Druid to 
move the whole group around later on, so the limitations of a tank 
should not be too tough on you.

The Enchanter is a terrific class to play and makes a great partner 
to the Druid and Necromancer later on because of crowd control and 
Clarity.

Druid and Necromancer cover just about every basic ability between
them.  Any class you choose to play should work well with them.  Pick
whatever you think will be the most fun for you.
