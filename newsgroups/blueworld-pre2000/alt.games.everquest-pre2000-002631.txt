What do you guys think about setting up a NGI guild on the new shard
when it goes up (targeted for the next week, I believe)? Given that
the experiences here run the gamut, some of us could be guardsmen and
fighters while others are craftsmen of various sorts. 

In case you don't know what Siege Perilous is, it's the replacement
shard for the Abyss. Among its features are:

1) One character only per account
2) Elimination of the recall spell
3) NPCs do not buy items from PCs and only sell at 3 times current
price (in other words, tailor mules need not apply)
4) Faction - based warfare system

The shard will be fairly pvp intensive, so this is not a panacea for
those looking for freedom from pk attacks. Many of the elements that
make pks so pervasive to some will not be present, however. I wouldn't
be surprised that if it works out well that it became the model for a
non - pvp shard. 

Anyway, if you're interested in setting up a guild let's work on the
details for it. 

This is crossposted to alt.games.everquest because I know some of the
UO veterans there would be very interested in this new kind of shard.
Hey, if Dennis can post his little screenshots here, a little
crossposting back won't hurt.
