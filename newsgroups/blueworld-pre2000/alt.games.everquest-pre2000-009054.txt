> OK, I'm a newbie (if it's not obvious by the topic of this post :)  When I
> created my human character, I could not add a space in the name I wanted to
> use (I also tried using the "_" [underscore] key).  Can you add spaces in
> your character names?  If so, how?  Does it depend on your race?

No, you cannot add a space or any other non-alphabetic character in your
name.  You also can't put a capital letter in the middle.  Your name will
be a single word starting with a capital letter.

It sounds like you are trying to start out with your full name.  Keep in 
mind that you will get the chance to add a last name at level 20.  So, if 
you are thinking about a descriptive name, save it for your last name.  
Your first name is the name your parents gave you, so it probably should 
not be something like "Orcslayer" or "Runeblade" anyway.
