I disagree.  I was in a group comprised of a ranger, paladin, cleric, druid
and an enchanter in the unrest tower a few weeks ago.  We were all mid to
high 20s and everything conned high blue to yellow.  We frequently faced 6
or more mobs and we didn't have a death in 4 hours.  We handled constant
pulls and only ran for zone once when a pull from the second floor picked up
a basement train coming back.

The Palidin and I both had all the cleric buffs and damage shields up.  I
was casting AoE lightning for little or no damage but lots of agro effects.
We would allow all of the mobs to pound on the tanks, with the enchanter
casting mez on hags and getting the cleric to assist in stunning the Hag if
they got a chance to cast.  The Paladin and I could take any mobs one at a
time in a very short time, maybe 30-45 seconds for the first and 15-30
seconds for the next (as they had damage from shields) down to 5-10 seconds
for the last one or two mobs.  The druid and cleric kept us healed and the
Enchanter kept up clarity (She was the high level in the group).

I felt that given the mobs we were fighting, we could easily take 10+ and
win, and at one point did take a full tower and bar spawn.  I think any
group that works well together and uses all of their abilities can choose
good places to hunt and get great experience.  You just need to find places
that play well to your strengths instead of trying to fight things that are
out of your league.  I don't think this group could have fought and killed a
single hill giant, but we were hell on wheels against primarily non-casting
undead.
