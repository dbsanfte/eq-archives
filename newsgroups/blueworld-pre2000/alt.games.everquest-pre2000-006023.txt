You know your right.  They are scanning your ports and who knows what
else.  Don't forget that REAL got caught with there hand in the cookie
jar not too long ago for just this sort of thing.

The thing is, is I don't know what to say about this issue. When
people have problems with dial up ISP's I have one answer.  Change
ISP's.  They are a dime a dozen and all cost you $20.  If more people
changed more frequently it would lead to better ISP's.

However @HOME and at the moment all cable ISP's are in the unique
position of not having competition.  I suppose the idea of a monopoly
that isn't nation wide isn't new, but it seems new to me.

This means that @Home can abuse you in new and fantastic ways that you
have never been abused before.  Granted you are always free to give up
your cable modem.  But let me ask you, now that you have it could you
give it up?

Hell I don't even have a 2 way cable, I have one way and I cant give
it up.  My ISP has recently pissed me off.  They farm out there
newserver and the news server has recently changed there policies on
multipart posts (you know 1/2 posts). Apparently they don't have
access to as much bandwith as they want.  So they randomly drop parts
in mulitpart posts to compensate for bandwith.  I can regularly only
pick up maybe less than 10% of all multipart posts.  It is really
pahetic.  In the past I would have changed ISP's over an issue like
this at the drop of a hat.  But I cant.  Means giving up the closest
thing I have to a big fat pipe.  Hell I have even been tinkering with
the idea of paying for my  own news access, I am that addicted.

And you have the same problem with port scanning.  Sounds to me like
it pisses you off to the point that you would change providers, but
you cant.  I bet you are as addicted to that modem as I am to mine.
All that you are left to do is get abused.

Here is the clincher.  This is the part that really annoys me.  @Home
has a bit of a point.  They are exercising a legitimate concern over
bandwith usage.  The biggest reason they discorage servers is cause it
degrades the service they are offering to your neighbors and other
people that share your hub.  That is why they have that in there
contract.  It makes sense to me and I cant dissagree with it.
They are faced with the dillema that they know people are doing it.
They know if they could stop people it would improve things for
everyone but they cant do it without pissing people off.  
Cant say I envy there situation.

Here I am on a roll lets make this more complicated.

Back to this being a monopoly (which bugs me).  Okay it costs
mulitmillions of dollars to retrofit a small town to digital cable.
Tax dollars pay no part of this.  Your local cable company bears the
brunt of this.  They pay for it by jacking up your prices, then
offering you services at even higher prices.  You pay for it cause
digital cable is just so pretty you cant stand it.
They do something else too.  They either sell off bandwith to a 3rd
party (in my community Blazenet, in yours @Home) or they start a Cable
Modem ISP on there own.  This generates even more money.

Okay anyone have a problem with that?  I don't.  Seems perfectly fair
to me.

Think about it.  A private business has rebuilt the network at a huge
expense to them they need to recoup somehow.  I like the idea of them
owning or going in as partners with a Cable Modem ISP.  Means that
they don't have to jack prices up high enough.  Besides they own that
cable.  They own that line.

So then there are people like you and me. We get upset when the
afformentioned company starts scanning our ports or subscribing to
sucky news providers.  The company starts abusing us and we get pissed
we cant change providers.

Enter Congress.  Congress (actually I think this is State Legislature)
is working on passing laws now that say that cable companies MUST
share lines with mulitple ISP providers.  They must offer allow
competition on there own Cable lines.

That doesnt seem very fair to me.  Can you imagine how much that must
suck as a business person.  You take this huge bet on the market by
investing a huge amount of money, and then they pass a law waving
there finger at you and say you are not entitled to do what you want
with it?

Anyways I am not trying to offer a solution to the dilema.  I am just
showing different viewpoints on it.  I for one havent made up my mind
as to wether I stand with the business or the anti-monopoly crowd.  I
think I will end up somewhere in between.
