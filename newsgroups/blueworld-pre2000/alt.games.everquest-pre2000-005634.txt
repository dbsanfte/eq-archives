None of the good warriors I know would consent to a bind in Oggok
without strong assurances that there would be a cleric available
to res them when they died and that they would be rebound in the 
Arena or Freeport later that day.  Bad things can still happen.

go linkdead, leaving the warrior to fend for himself or herself
when the servers come back.  However, the fact remains that most
good warriors would rather take the boat from Greater Faydark than
run past the Bouncers naked.  I don't know what to assume about 
the warrior, other than that he made a catastrophically bad 
decision and suffered nasty consequences.  I'm not saying that 
binding isn't an issue, just that the particular example didn't
support the argument.

Evil races have a much harder time of things because their options 
are more limited, but the good warrior should have left himself 
bound in Freeport.
