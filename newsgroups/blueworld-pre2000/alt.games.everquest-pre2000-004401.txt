I am pretty sure that the positions of distant mobs are not
actually updated by ShowEQ.  (I read the message board. :) When
you enter a zone, you get a complete list of all of the PCs and
NPCs in the zone.  With each mob, you get the position, health,
and an id that is unique within the zone.  Your client makes a
table of all of this information.  This gives the client
something to hang extra information off of.  You get an update on
the position and status of every NPC and PC within some range of
your character a few times a second.  If anything enters or
leaves the zone (or spawns or is killed), you get an update for
your table.

I had figured out about the bulk download as you enter a zone
when I first got EQ and couldn't play.  There was some bad
interaction between EQ's network protocol and my ISP.  That bulk
download flooded the modem and prevented my computer from acking,
so I always got dumped on entering a zone.  I solved the problem
by getting a DSL line. :)
