You get Sneak at level 10.  You have to put one practice into it before
you can use it.  It is accessed through the abilities screen.

You should probably have enough hot key slots if you put all of the 
combat abilities in the combat slots.

Combat:
	Melee Attack
	Range Attack
	Kick
	Taunt
	Bind Wounds

Abilities:
	Hide
	Sneak
	Forage
	Tracking
	Sense Heading

This leaves one slot for either Fishing or Begging.  If you use Beg for 
an extra taunt, then put that in.  Fishing can be swapped in when you
actually want to fish. ;)
