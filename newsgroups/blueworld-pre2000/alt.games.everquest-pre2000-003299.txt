This silly bug would be catastrophic in PvP.  I would like to see

>   II. More Bugs (NPC problems)
> 
>       Currently, NPCs pay almost no heed to illusions at all. NPCs base
> their reaction to you on
> your non-visual traits, almost exclusively. Don't believe me? Make a
> Dark Elf, Innoruk
> worshiping enchanter and see how much the guards (let alone paladins) in
> Freeport like you. Kill
> a couple hundred Erudin guards and and try to walk back into town.

NPCs base their reactions to you on:

1) Your standing with their faction group.
2) Your race
3) Your religion

Illusions change your race only.  Ogres, trolls and dark elves who
would kill my high elven enchanter react quite positively to her when
she matches their form.  An Enchanter gets Alliance and Benevolence, 
as well as very high Charisma and Charisma enhancing spells, to further 
improve their opinion of you.

Choosing to worship Innoruk will give you a good reputation with other
followers of Innoruk, but tends to make a lot of good people think
very ill of you.  It is mostly going to be a handicap to your character.
I could see making that choice for role play reasons, but you should 
understand the drawbacks.  Personally, I would not choose to make a
religious Enchanter and I am very careful about what faction hits I 
take.

>   I. Tashan / MR reduction
> 
>       I thought that magic resistance reduction was the domain of the
> enchanter. I am apparently
> mistaken in that belief. I recently witnessed a magician reduce the
> magic resistance of a gnome
> guard to "below zero" to quote the mage himself. While I found this hard
> to swallow, the effects
> of this spell were undeniable: Every spell stuck, no resists, and full
> damage. Why give other
> classes this ability if it is intended to be the forte of the enchanter?

Tashan (Tashani, Tashania) is a great spell!  I notice a big difference
between casting on a creature that has been Tashanied and one that has 
not.  By the time a Magician gets Malise at level 24, the Enchanter has
already gotten Tashani at level 20.  Tashani reduces magic resistance 
further than Malise does, but Malise reduces fire, cold and poison as 
well.  Malise costs 60 mana, compared to 20 for Tashani.  The Tashan line
of spells are never resisted and they stack with Malise.  I would not 
complain about Tashan at all.

>   II. Zoning and illusions
> 
>       I must give credit where it is due. Extending the life span of the
> illusion spells was a
> very nice bonus. I did not expect it, and had become resigned to living
> with short duration
> illusions.
> 
>       I cannot speak for every enchanter out there, but I presonally
> would gladly trade in all
> that extra duration to be able to keep our illusions through the zoning
> process.

I like the longer durations a lot!  It lets me live in a different form.
The 5-10 minute durations before were only enough for a novelty, not 
long enough to relax in your new form.

I would like to have illusions last across zone boundaries.  More 
importantly, I would like to see illusions survive logging out and back 
in.  Going linkdead in a hostile city can mean instant death when you 
log back in as yourself.  At least you can prepare in advance for zoning.
