Neil Strotton wrote:
> 
> Just out of interest, what are bone chips used for?  I often get them from
> killing skellys, but usually leave them with the corpse as merchants don't
> pay much for them.

They are the spell component for all of the Necromancer skeleton
summoning spells.  High level Necromancers will often pay around 
2pp for a stack of 20 bone chips to avoid the tedium of whacking
wimpy skeletons.

Some paladin and cleric guilds also have quests where you turn in
bone chips for small rewards, good faction, and some experience.
I assume that people who need to do those quests would also be
interested in purchasing bone chips.
