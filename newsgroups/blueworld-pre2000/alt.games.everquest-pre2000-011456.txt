I've changed my mind.  I've been reading a bunch of threads on 
eqtraders, and there's a lot more to smithing than I'd realized, 
both in the imbued and otherwise.

Ogres can pretty much choose either agnostic, Cazic-Thule or 
Rallos Zek.  Amber imbue results in Cazic-Thule armor, and gives 
stamina and wisdom.  Jade imbue results in Rallos Zek armor, and 
gives strength.  Agnostics will probably be able to get enchanted 
war plate (though so far the recipes appear to be broken), while 
"religious" ogres could in theory be able to add an imbue to the 
enchanted war plate.

At any rate, it looks like even an agnostic ogre won't be lacking 
for sellable recipes, so I retract my concerns with one 
particular line of imbued.  Yeah, I'm eating my own words, now 
that I get what's going on.

At least Ogres are not by far the only race with broken recipes.  
From the way it's looking on eqtraders, there are more recipes 
broken than working.  Fixes are in the works.
