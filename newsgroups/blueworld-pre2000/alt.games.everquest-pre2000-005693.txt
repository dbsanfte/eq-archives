Enchanters have two area spells:

Color Flux/Color Shift - an area stun centered on the caster with 
a very small radius, a fairly short duration (4 sec/8 sec), and a 
long hold time (12 sec).

Mesmerization - area effect Mesmerize (24 seconds, breaks if the mob
takes damage), centered on a target, short hold time.

Both are incredibly handy spells in different situations.  

The standard Enchanter escape is Color Shift/Gate.  It is possible
for an Enchanter to keep a group of mobs stunned by swapping between 
Color Flux and Color Shift.  The spells have a very long recast time, 
so you need both of them.  Color Shift is a nice cheap quick stun, 
helpful for interrupting casters.  The area is pretty small and the 
Enchanter has to be in the middle of the battle to use this spell,
but I always have it memorized.

Mesmerization is great for holding down large numbers of mobs while
your party picks them off.  You have to be careful not to catch
yourself in the radius or you will be inert for 24 seconds along
with all the mobs.  The memory blur effect of Mesmerization is very
unreliable, so you had better hope your party fights cleverly or 
you'll be mobbed.  The other great use for Mesmerization is to put
the whole battle to sleep so that your party can heal up or get away.
Casting AoE spells on a mesmerized batch of monsters is a good way
to get both the wizard and the enchanter killed.  Mesmerization is
not the same as stun.

(All AoE spells are essentially useless on PvP servers, of course.)
