If you want to keep the playing board equal for players, and not have the
people at the top bashing the newbies, there's only one thing to do:

Separate them.

I don't think its impossible to have gates in the game which activate once
a player reaches a higher status.  The gate activates, pulls the
character out of the bunny slope area, and prevents him from returning.
In the next level area he has more difficult monsters to fight, equitable
players to engage in battle with, and greater rewards when victorious.

Towns could still be shared, commerce still occur, but Mr. GM everything
could not go back to the newbie areas and bash hordes of frustrated
newbies who are mining or fishing or logging.

The enticement would be reaching the pinnacle of the server, seeing the
neatest most powerful monsters, fighting the best of the best players,
seeing the most challenging dungeons.  If you wanted to roleplay with your
underling buddies, you go back to town or to home areas.

The forced socialism of UO is kind of a bummer for a guy who gets on and
does not want to belong to a guild of spanky's.  The spanky's are all
training using mass numbers on hard monsters or sparring, and are
overwhelming the popular areas.  The single player, who may just want to
explore, dies over and over while trying to build up enough of a bank
account to purchase and train in the very hard to gain skills.
