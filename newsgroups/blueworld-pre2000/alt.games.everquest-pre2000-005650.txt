People have figured out that most warriors can fight on a half tank of
health.  Unless you have bad connectivity problems, you can start 
affording summoned bandages at about the same time that your health 
downtimes get really long.  At early levels, if you group with a healer 
of any kind, you can enjoy almost non-stop action if you stick to single 
whites and below.

Warriors have gotten some significant buffs since you left and are now
one of the most desirable classes:  easy at low levels, nice at medium
levels and essential at high levels.
