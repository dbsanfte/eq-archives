Personnaly I believe Verant does not tell us about the mechanics because 
they keep changing the mechanics all the time to ... err ... ENSURE 
GAME BALANCE. And if you've ever been around one of those "I have to win 
by the rules" types, who will memorize every rule in the system and then 
intentionally abuse it to their advantage, you can just imagine the flood 
of hate mails saying: 

  "Why could I not jump across that gorge? My STR is this and my AGI is 
   this and I carry only THIS much weight ... by the rules I should not 
   have died!" 

So even tho I disagree on a lot of policies, I still think learning by 
doing and a certain safety margin are a good choice on Verant's side ...

: In my opinion, the information you're talking about is only
: valuable to people who view the game as a challenge to
: overcome no matter the means.  Power levelers and such.
: While I don't really care if people power level it seems to
: me they are missing part of the fun of the game.

... however I think there are some things that are just TOO 
counterintuitive and we should know about. Like say the DEX stat. Now any 
*sane* roleplayer would assume that -- since it is a stat -- it should 
influence you success chance at what you do. Unless you are *insane* like 
Verant and say "no the STAT values are learning skills only, the real 
success values are only governed by the SKILL value". So really my SHM 
does not get to succeed in channeling more often because of the higher 
DEX ... he only gets to improve his channeling SKILL faster because of 
it. 

I summary I am opposed to telling people the exact mechanics (you can 
jump (3xSTR + 5xAGI - WEIGHT/5) feet) but I *am* in favor of giving them 
a basic idea what they need or not. I mean why should I (SHM) buff STR 
below level 20 ... unless I am overloaded and get speed maluses from 
that? 

Just my $.02,
