Some of want more than camping and levelling, levelling and camping,
camping and levelling, levelling and camping, ad nauseum. Verant
created a working pk switch and seemed to think that was enough, that
all people wanted was to bash some critters and get neato magic stuff
and new spells to cast. That's great for a little while especially if
you find a good group to do it with (by no means a guaranteed thing),
but eventually people want more than that. To keep people in the game,
Verant will have to keep expanding the experience limits, which will
make it harder and harder to balance the game for incoming players.
Twinking is already a huge problem, and it's just going to get worse
with time. 

UO lets you make a mark on the world, to do things like setting up a
business complete with regular customers and little competitive wars
for clienteles. There are entire player cities where they can act out
their own vision of roleplaying. EQ offers none of that, and
irrespective of its PvP options it will suffer in the long run for it.
