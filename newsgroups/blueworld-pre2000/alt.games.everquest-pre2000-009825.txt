It's all of a feeling. A level 40 blue will net your group 160k XP.
A level 45 blue would give you 202k XP. That's only 25% more xp.

As long as you can pull and lay down 5 low 40 mobs in the time you'd get
4 mid-40s, you're even. The trick is that you usually have a whole lot
less downtime with low 40s. If you have a steady source of low 40s, you
can outperform a mid-40s hunting group even with steady pulls.

(the trick is: have a steady source, so that you continuously mow them
 down. Which is why you usally do that with a group of 3, maybe 4 people
