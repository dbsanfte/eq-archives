>It seems pretty clear to me that the only thing that matters is the
>PC-damage-per-second to mob-damage-per-second ratio.  This is why mob
>slowdowns are so hugely effective; they dramatically alter that ratio
>(and note, by the way, that slowdown is a defensive technique).

If you are soloing, (or in a 6 vs 6 melee with no crowd control, aka
dead), they are, in principle, equal. If you kill the mob 20% faster,
you take 20% less damage.

If you have 6 people trying to kill a single mob, things are a bit
different.

In this case, only one persons defence gets used, but all six's offence
(loosely speaking, whether that is spells, melee, or harsh language).

If the main tank makes a trade-off that gets them 20% extra damage on
the mob, but causes them to take 20% more damage from the mob, then
that's pretty much a bad thing.

The reason is simple - 20% more damage from 1 of 6 people drops the mob
20/6 = 3% sooner than it would have died anyway. So you just took 20%
extra damage for no reason.
 
Of course, things are not always that simple, but the principle is
sound.
