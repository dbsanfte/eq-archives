I've played both and do not believe they're in the same category. Like all
online games Sierra has put out, the Realm is an enjoyable game that mainly
provides a background for you to socialize with other people. No matter how
many hours I spent playing Realm, there was never a single moment I wasn't
aware I was playing a game, sometimes annoying, usually laggy game dressed
in RPG colors.

EQ is a totally immersive role playing environment. Regardless of hours
invested here, I usually don't think of it as a game as I'm "playing".
Unlike Realm combat where a turnbased screen pops up and takes you out of
the adventuring world, combat in EQ is part of your everyday world as it
should be and totally unpredictable. Will passersby help you if you get in
over your head? Is the near death foe you are chasing down leading you into
a trap? Will I be able to keep ahead of the guy hacking my back to ribbons
long enough to reach the town sentries and safety?

I'm sure there's a healthy market for the simplistic uninvolivng gameplay of
the Realm, but if you're an RPG lover or a gamer looking for an alternate
reality to get involved in, EQ's the only game in town.
