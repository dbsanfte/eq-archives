No pks, no thieves, no looters shall ever bother you again.

You can talk while in autorun.  The beta journals tell of a man setting
autorun into a direction, then typing a warning and apology to the players
in front of him, for leading a gnoll train by.  You need food and drink to
survive, otherwise you slow down.  Stamina, health, and mana do not
recover when you are hungry.

You can almost certainly talk during combat.

Job skills exist also.  Smithing, tailoring, jewel craft, fishing,
fletching, pottery, begging, etc.
