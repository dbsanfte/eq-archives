Any class you play will seem very hard for the first level. You won't know
your way around and you will die.....often. The race you choose will
determine where you start. I created a High Elf Paladin and started him in
Kelethin. It's a nice area if you like trees. I also have a human cleric
running around Freeport somewhere.
Getting to level 4 is pretty easy over all, maybe 1 or 2 nights of serious
play. With 8 slots to choose from on any one server, you can play several
different combinations until you get one that feels right for you. Remember,
at level 1-4, you don't really have all that much to lose so it's no big
deal to delete and start over.

****IMPORTANT***
Do some research on the 'net (you can also ask in-game or here) once you
decide on your main char. Once you choose a *name* and distribute the
*ability points* you can NOT change them.
example: You started a wizard and dumped all your points into strength.
(useless)
example: You started a warrior and dumped all your points into intelligence
(useless)
If you are playing with real-life friends, you MUST be on the same server or
you will never see them.
You will have questions...lots of them. Ask. There is no such thing as a
stupid question.

Newsgroup Tip: Put a shortened version of your question as the subject like
"Warrior guild in Freeport?" It lets us know what's inside :)
