Most spells are found at all cities.  Until you have to start doing 
research at level 16, all your spells can be bought somewhere.  The 
few spells that are found in only one or two cities are listed in
the spell lists at Casters Realm:

http://eq.castersrealm.com/

Mage summoning spells: library in Erudin and Neriak
Enchanter metal enchantments: Neriak
Enchanter animations: High Keep

Each city with a mage guild will have one vendor who sells those 
spells, like Gate and Shielding, that are shared by all the caster
classes.  In Felwithe, that vendor is in the building next to the
big floating building with all the gates in it.
