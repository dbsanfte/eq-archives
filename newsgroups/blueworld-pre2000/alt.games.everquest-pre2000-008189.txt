> 1: The developers are stuck up/

Sounds like the game was too hard for you and you wanted them to make
it easier.  You have to accept that what makes the game more fun for
you might make it less fun for others and Verant's goal is to make the
best game they can for everybody.  There are lots of games out there
that are easier, faster and more action-oriented.

> I'll admit, some things were taken care of, sometimes not
> with the best acceptance from players, but look at some key issues with

> makes over 2 mil a month revune to buy more servers, or how lazy are the

They are putting up servers, but there are too many people buying the 
game for them to keep up.  I think they have been holding back on 
adding new servers because Kunark is going to increase the capacity
of all existing servers by a significant fraction and they don't want
to find themselves with *underpopulated* servers.

> 2: bugs/
> it's pathetic and lame that you can't hide in a building to get away from a
> monster, and they can slice right through it.

This is not a bug.  It is intentional.  Buildings are for scenery,
not protection.

If there were safe spots that you could hop in and out of, it would be
trivial to just jump in and out of the door while casting or shooting
at a monster.  As long as you accept that buildings aren't safe, you
won't have this problem.

> It's pathetic that the
> programmers can't even fix, or don't want to fix pathing bugs, which cause
> creatures to teleport to and away from your character at times most
> opportune to the monster.

There was a fix for this in the last patch.  I haven't heard yet 
whether it was successful.

> 3: slow game progression/
> 4: slow and boring gameplay/
> 5: static storyline/

It's a role playing game.  If you ignore that aspect, you will probably
find it pretty boring.

Game play is only slow and boring if you play it that way.  If you try
to play the whole game by kiting solo, you will be insanely bored.
With no risk, no group tactics, and no company, you will probably have
little fun.  If I thought this game were for you, I would suggest 
finding a nice balanced group and taking on some real challenges.

> 6: "Everbrag"

That is probably true. ;)

Some people put an awful lot of importance on the level their characters
have achieved, the nifty equipment they have acquired, or their status
in big guilds.  The best response to these people is to ignore them.
Most adult players will not fit this profile.
