shrike@alcaudon.com (Shrike) wrote:

>After getting heavily into trading this last few weeks (master Jeweler,
>Smith, Baker, Potter, and soon Tailor and Brewer), I have a couple of
>suggestions.
>
>One would be NOT to make trades easier. Trades right now are exceedingly
>frustrating, but part of the reason they are worthwhile is that not
>EVERYONE feels like becoming a tradesman, and this is the way it should
>be. In fact, the few trades that are vital to certain classes (i.e,
>Rogues and Pottery) are the easiest to master (and no, Jewelcraft is not
>vital to Enchanters, though it's nice). So that works.
>
>The other would be to make more items stackable... That has no bearing
>on how easy a trade is, and a lot on how insanely inconvenient things
>get. For example, it makes no sense that pelts aren't stackable (come
>on, something that's perfectly flat is not stackable?) and something
>like glass bottles are. Right now there isn't really much rhyme or
>reason to why some items stack and some don't, and I don't think it
>would hurt the game to make more items, particularly trade items,
>stackable.
>
>The third suggestion would be to implement portable Forges, Ovens,
>Kilns, Potter Wheels and Brewing Barrels as Tinkering items. This would
>enhance Tinkering, and give us traders some freedom of movement (how fun
>is it to have to wait hours for a free forge?). This is a goal, but if
>you guys think this would completely unbalance the game and bring all
>servers to a screeching halt, at least make the forges and barrels
>usable by more than one person at the same time, just as bankers and
>merchants are.
>
>Any comments?

Yes. I agree on all points except for one minor one. There is a portable 
oven; it's called a spit.

I'd like to add another to the list. This doesn't affect just trades, but 
many purchases. When we sell something that's stacked, we get a slide bar 
to select how many to sell. I'd like one when we buy something. This would 
help trades people and anyone who buys food, water, arrows, ....
