Heartguard has it right.  Paladins are warriors who fight on behalf of
a god.  That god grants them extra powers to assist them in their tasks.
If the holy warrior violates the tenets of his religion, whatever they
might be, then he should be stripped of his powers.  Of course, this
doesn't happen in EverQuest.

There is no Good and Evil in EverQuest.  This is one area where EQ 
deviates from AD&D canon.  You may have noticed that there is no 
Detect Evil/Detect Good/Know Alignment spell in EverQuest.  Some of the 
gods are loosely defined as good.  Their holy warriors are Paladins.  
Some are defined as evil.  Their holy warriors are Shadow Knights.  
Prexus, the Oceanlord, should probably be considered a neutral god ... 
at best.  I imagine it was a toss-up whether he got Paladins or Shadow
Knights.

So, if you are a Paladin or Cleric of Prexus, then killing Trantor 
Everhot is a task that will bring favor with your god, so it is a good
thing to do.  If you don't follow Prexus, but follow Brell Serilis,
then you probably should not go killing dwarves in Kaladim just to
get a spiffy weapon.

Similarly, just because the froggies have a Paladin does not mean that
they are good.  If your religious leaders tell you to go kill him and
take his sword, then it is a good and righteous thing to do.  This is
the nature of religion (in EverQuest ;).
