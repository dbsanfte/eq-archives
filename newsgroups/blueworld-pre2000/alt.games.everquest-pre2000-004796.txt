Damn, that was my line! But really, I have grown up in the 'my computer 
is better than your computer' wars of Atari/Amiga. And I love linux for 
what it's worth: 

  * start box. come back 2 years later. running fine
  * stop demon. recompile demon restart demon. no reboot
  * wow, I can actually see and trace what is running on my comp!

  * MS Office 97 is finally the product that everyone else could have
