Also, watch out for the oversimplification.

I did not think that folks were reporting that "all melee stats
are always worthless" but rather that there is only a trivial
increase in real-world effects when you change a stat from the
low 100s to the medium 100s.

Best to think of it as:

under 75.  This is bad and will reduce your damage (strength),
slow your running (agility), and interfere with some combat
actions (dexterity)

75 - 100:  expect stat changes in this range to be moderately
meaningful for low level characters.  They will be very
important for helping you build the mental picture of your
character (strong but clumsy, or whatever)

over 100:  this is good.  Expect increases over 100 not to make
a big difference in combat damage.  Of course, Bronze plat
still weighs a lot and it sucks to try to run to zone when you
are encumbered ...
