Uh-oh.  Get out the asbestos suits boys.

Ultima Online:  Basically, the role-playing equivalent of Quake.  Kill
or be killed, except you can buy some things and put them in a bank
vault too.  And build houses.  Don't forget to download the cheat
programs from Dr. Twister, you WILL need them, just to stay alive long
enough to kill people back.  Not in 3-d; if you liked Postal, you'll
love this game.

EverQuest:  3-d, but no player killing (unless you want it).  Buggy,
still effectively in beta, but it shore looks purty.  No known cheat
programs, only a few exploits available (will probably change
eventually).  More addicting than crack (voice of experience <grin>).
Don't expect to make level 50 in a day and a half (unless you're a
GM's girlfriend); there's no easy way to become a superhero.  Great
for people who dream of one day becoming pest-control technicians
("Here, rat, rat, rat... Mister two-handed sword wants to say hi...").
