I only liked facets of it.  PvP makes rude conduct rare if you can't
protect yourself.  On Tallon Zek certain zones, by an unwritten code,
were non-PvP.  Unrest, for example.  I enjoyed protecting Steamfont
from large raids of elves.  20 players rallying in an impromptu
fashion and fighting.  Some on each side would invariably be enchanters
using illusions which made choosing targets a challenge.

However, most PvP encounters were different.  For example, an Erudite
enchanter waited while invisible in the minotaur mine.  My cleric
began battling two minotaurs.  The enchanter began sniping on me, too,
as I was halfway done killing the second.  I managed to gate.  If he
had waited I would not have had the mana to gate.  That is when most
attacks occur.  Boring.  Good tactics but boring.

My cleric is pretty much retired at 24th level.  Too few meleers
exist for decent groups.  Not allowing armor looting may have
improved that problem.

Personally, I'd like to see PvP events of wars.  Kaladim and
Ogguk go to war for a week.  After the war faction hits get
expunged to pre-war settings.  It would be interesting to
witness a few Bashers crashing the gates of Kaladim with
players coming along.
