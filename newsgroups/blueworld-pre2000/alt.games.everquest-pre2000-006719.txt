The game plays well over a 56k connection (it plays fine over a 28.8
connection).  The lag and disconnections have increased about 5000% since
1 year ago, which really isn't surprising considering the number of people
connected, but it is still playable (most of the time).

As far as overcrowding goes, other than the lag that results from having
50,000 people connecting through the same wire, if you are just starting out
you won't feel overcrowding until you are up to level 20 or 30 or higher. 
And if you are just starting out that will take you a while to reach,
therefore the expansion will probably be out by then and I expect it to
alleviate some of the high-level crowding that is a problem right now.

On the other hand, South Karanas, Oasis, Lower Guk and Sol B will always be
jam packed.  So you _will_ eventually have to deal with overcrowding.
