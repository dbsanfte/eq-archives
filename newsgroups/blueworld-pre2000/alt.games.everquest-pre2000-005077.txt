Well I play a druid (which has no armor quests) and a bard.  Seeing as
there's really no comparisons with the druid, I'll use the bard as an
example.

Most of the bard components are No Drop, and you have to kill some
pretty evil Mobs to get the items needed.  My level 21 bard has no
real chance of getting most of the pieces.  So I wait until 30, 39 or
so and I go after the Mobs that drop the items.  At those levels I
will still get exp for the item dropped.  Now once I gather all two or
three pieces needed to get one piece of Lambent I should have gained a
good chunk of exp from just killing the Mobs.

When I turn in the items to the person in Ro Temple who makes the bard
armor, I should get a great faction adjustment because I was able to
perform this task.  

However, I should not gain exp - why?  Because why would you gain exp
for turning an item in?  The exp was gained in the gathering of the
components.  The reward for the hard work is obtaining the armor.

Also, think of it this way.

Imagine if there was a quest which gave you exp all the way up to
level 50.  Imagine if that was the Tumpy Tonic quest.  So imagine a
level 50 wizard walking around with all his skills around 65.  This is
a great and fearsome wizard who'll fizzle 80-90% of the time casting
level 30 spells.  Just because he roamed all across the continents
making alcohol doesn't mean he should be ready to learn Ice Comet.

Granted, these are just my opinions.  :)
