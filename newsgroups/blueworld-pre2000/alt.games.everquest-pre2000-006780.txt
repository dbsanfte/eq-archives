I believe that a druid's strategy changes after L49...Before L49 nukes
aren't worth a lot in a group and since they are fire based even less so in
SolB. So I did spec alt until 49 then reset to evoc. The 49 spells are
resisted much less and more often than not do full damage. Plus by 49 my
heal is so pathetic that I avoid being the primary heal if possible. It is
amazing how much Chloro helps...a group with a high level druid goes into
most fights with full hp. But chloro can be cast and refreshed before battle
and med can recover the mana fairly quick.
