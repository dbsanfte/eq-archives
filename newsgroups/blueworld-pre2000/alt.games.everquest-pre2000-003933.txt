Camping items for sale on eBay brings a whole different and unpleasant
dimension to the game.  Those people aren't playing the game for fun 
or to advance their characters and meet in game goals.  They are in the
game world camping items for real world cash.

One big problem with this motivation is that it never ends.  If you are
camping for your guild, your friends, or your own lower level twinked
characters, you fill them up pretty fast and then go on to do something
else.  People won't be done camping for eBay until there is no more
market for the items.

Also when people are motivated by personal real world greed, they are 
far less likely to be polite and reasonable in the game.  Why would they
ever let someone else get a chance to earn an item?  That is just one
less potential customer for them on eBay.

The whole thing is just nasty.  I say make the cool items NO DROP and 
make accounts non-transferable.
