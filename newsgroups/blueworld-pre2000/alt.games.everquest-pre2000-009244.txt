Ok, Kunark is slow because the graphics are more complex than in the 
older zones.  If you currently can get good performance by pulling 
your clipping plane to your nose, then the only way you are going
to get good performance with the clipping plane further out is to buy 
a better graphics card.  You need a decent amount of texture memory 
and a good drawing rate, both in polygon count and pixel fill rate.

RAM, CPU, disk or strange BIOS settings are not going to help you
nearly as much as getting a good video card.
