This is the official description of the Babylon 5 groups:

rec.arts.sf.tv.babylon5	Babylon 5 creators meet Babylon 5 fans.
rec.arts.sf.tv.babylon5.moderated	Babylon 5 creators meet
Babylon 5 fans. (Moderated)

The RFD for the unmoderated group included the following:

  Not to mention the fact that JMS, who does not currently have 
  killfile processing, would have to wade through discussion of other 
  shows to get to discussion of his own show."  [this is with
  regard to use of rec.arts.sf.tv]

  JMS has been a "connected" television producer for some time;
  "connected" meaning he has been active in various computer networks,
  commercial or otherwise.  An internet savvy television producer 
  alone is significant, one who is public and accessible to Joe Fan is
  amazing.  It can be argued that this television program is the first
  to be born out of the networks, and it behooves us (the net-workers)
  to support it.

  J. Michael Straczynski, Babylon 5's creator, (hereafter: JMS) has
  stated flatly that for legal reasons that if any story ideas appear

  on a "B5" newsgroup he will be forced to leave the electronic forum.
  This would be, to our reckoning, a net.tragedy, and is to be avoided
  at all costs.

The RFD for the moderated group included:

  Additionally, posts containing story ideas (as defined below) create
  legal problems and tremendous vulnerability for the show's
  creator/producer, J. Michael Straczynski, when he participates in   
  the newsgroup.  

  Measures attempted in the past have not seemed to alleviate these
  problems.  This has led to the departure of many regular newsgroup
  users including Straczynski.
