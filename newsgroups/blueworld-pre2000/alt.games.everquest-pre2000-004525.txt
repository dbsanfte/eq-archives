The only time I ever camped an item in EQ, was when I helped a friend get 
the Ghoulbane. At the time, we were the only group there, and it still 
took about a week of logging into guk, spending a few hours there, giving 
up, logging off for the night...

But sitting around waiting for a puller sure feels a lot like camping to 
me, too. I always thought of dungeon crawling, as progressively moving 
towards the end of the dungeon, killing and looting all the evil 
creatures found along the way. EverQuest redefined that to just sitting 
in one spot, bring the whole dungeon to the group. That's rediculous, and 
as far as I'm concerned, it's camping. And there seems to be no way 
around it, in EQ.

I will readily admit, the dungeons in EQ are nicer and more interesting 
than the ones in AC. But in AC, there are countless dungeons to explore, 
and dungeon crawl in, with no camping necessary. As far as gameplay 
goes... I don't know, I wish a game could manage to get it right in both 
areas, as I can't honestly say which I prefer, and don't think they have 
to be mutually exclusive...
