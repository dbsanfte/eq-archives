There's more to it than that:  For reasonably skilled archers, summoned 
arrows are the way to go.  I can blow through 200 or more arrows a day, and
while equivalent arrows aren't insanely expensive to make, they're not cheap
either.  I don't know about others, but I'm much less hesitant to pepper an
incoming pull with three or four arrows when they're not costing me a gold or
two per pop.  And with a fast bow that's a not insubstantial amount of damage
for (essentially) free.  As a ranger with my Trueshot, that's usually around
60-70 points of damage before the monster closes to melee, and sometimes 
upwards of 120.  But it's hard on the arrows, and I normally don't carry more
than 100 due to weight restrictions.  (I didn't put the "proper" points into
STR on my ranger, and I'm sure not starting over now.)
