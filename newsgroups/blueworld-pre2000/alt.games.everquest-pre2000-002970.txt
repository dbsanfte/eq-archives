It is not an unreasonable expectation to want a game where cheaters can't
affect you, this is true even for people that like to have some PvP. I will
cite Diablo as a good example of where I liked to get in some PvP action,
but was restricted to private games since every idiot with an ISP was just
going from one public game to another in god mode looking to pk someone that
wasn't cheating.

In that light, all online games need to provide some form of protection for
players. In Diablo it wasn't a switch, it was private games for example.

OSI was lovin the pooch on this one. As soon as they found out there were
problems with massive cheating they should have done something.

EQ has less of a problem in that they have fewer bugs, but they have had
problems with duping and cheating. They have the switch, so it makes it less
of an annoyance.

Personally, I would prefer not to have a switch, the Diablo private game
system was better for me then the EQ switch.

If a software company was to come out with a game that said they were going
to have a switch for 1 year after the last bug was found, then it would be
removed, I would probably buy the game.

If you believe the current philosophy that all non trivial software will
have bugs, it kind of follows all games should have a switch or some kind of
mechanism to keep cheaters from affecting you.
