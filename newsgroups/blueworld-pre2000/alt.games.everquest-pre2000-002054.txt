Well if only 1 in 20 people you meet each night tries to kill you, that's a
pretty low percentage, but considering the sheer number of people on, that's
a lot of murderers you're likely to come across.  If even 1 in 100 people
you came across in real life tried to kill you, you'd not have survived past
the age of 1.

It's true that people tend to notice the jerks the most, but that's because
the jerks affect you the most.  I once had a VERY cool UO vet make me some
GM archer armor after I asked him to make me a ringmail tunic and bottoms
from some ingots I had in my bank box, because I'd just been killed by PK's.
That made a great impression on me.

At the same time, I've had 10 PK's kill me and take not only my armor but
any weapons, gold, regs, etc that I had on me at the time. (considering I've
never been killed outside a dungeon, and never before I'd managed to weaken
myself fighting multiple high level monsters, this stash has been
considerable)  And the number would have been higher except that I generally
either recall out or on a few occasions have fought and won (basically
because the PK was incompetent).

PK's and jerks take far more than other cool players give, and in so doing
make it even harder to help another player out.

The first few times I played the game, the only people I met outisde of town
were people trying to kill me.  I've since met a lot of other cool people,
