This is the case with most AE spells. Cast an AE damage spell and if
you're in the area of effect you will get hurt, but other members of
your party will not. This is used to good effect when targeting many
mobs in a busy fight...simply target your tank and the mobs around him
are affected, but he is not. Just make sure to stand back enough or you
will feel the effect of your own spell.
