I believe the line is that the no pk server would lend itself to any
number of exploits which would require a lot of extra customer service
to deal with. Based on their past and present GM policies, it's
customer service costs that really cut into their bottom line, so OSI
probably accepts this belief. If they billed it like the Abyss,
though, where everyone knows there's no support and so only a few
complaints are heard, it would probably fly. People would bitch about
how OSI supports the "pvp" servers more than they do the no pk zone,
but at the same time it would perhaps finally put this issue to rest
without resorting to a universal switch (which I continue to believe
would be a disaster). 

I also suggested making the T2A zone pk / theft free. The limited
resources available there combined with heavy monster concentrations
would work well with that kind of thing.
