In rec.games.computer.everquest Gordon Wrinn <gwrinn@verant.com> wrote:

>> Verant is just not willing to make radical changes to EverQuest

> Why on Earth would we want to make radical sweeping changes to a vastly
> successful game?

   Replayability, Gordon.

   You (meaning Verant) seem to have the idea that just because EQ is a 
persistent online world, it has infinite replayability.  It doesn't.  People
become bored of killing the same monsters in the same dungeons to make a
level of power that is essentially the same as what they had before.  EQ
has kept people playing for almost a year now, and that's an amazing length
of time for any game...but its replay value is finally wearing too thin to
spark any enjoyment, anymore.

   Making the ultimate parts of the game tougher is a bad option, by the
way.  Yes, I can't get onto the plane of sky without 500 pp, 46th level, and
three dozen friends, and all that will require me to log another 3 months of
played time.  That's sufficiently difficult that I have no intention of even 
trying.  Make enough zones that frustratingly difficult, and people will 
start looking around and realizing that there is nothing they can
realistically do in the game anymore.  Excessively Tough zones decrease the 
replayability of the game.

>> If the time comes that you just don't seem to enjoy it any longer, move on

> Here's a sound piece of advice.  We really don't expect people to play EQ
> forever, but we will continue to make improvements and additions to the game
> to keep the people that are here playing.  Certainly, if it gets to the
> point that playing the game is a chore, perhaps you should put it on the
> shelf.  I really can't fathom why people would keep playing if they hate the
> game.

   Because people have fantasies.  :)  I bet a lot of EQ players fantasize
about a game just like EQ, but with random spawns/more to explore/realtime
generated dungeons/truly interactive quests/what-have-you.  *That's* the
game they want to play.  They stick around in the hope that you (again,
meaning Verant) might make some of these changes so they can have fun
playing this game again.

   Kudos on the most excellent game of 1999.  It's now 2000.  What have you
done for me lately?  :)
