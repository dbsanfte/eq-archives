Well, personally, I decided that I'd want to get a few levels
under my belt before going to PvP, so I banked my tome
and went out to play.  It didn't take long to learn that
A) almost noone was PvP
B) PvP would mean the non PvP players would be
unable to be nice to me, or me to them
C) PvP combat would be no exp, no loot, just a
coup counting deal

So I destroyed the tome and never did switch
over.

I know that almost all of the above points would
be different on the PvP servers;
A) Everyone is PvP
B) Since this is true, everyone is capable of being
nice as well as mean
C) there is even a bit of loot involved, although
it appears now that this is strictly cash on person;
I imagine this is great for the banks, but lousy
for actual loot purposes.

Still, no exp gained or lost, except for the
loss side which appears to be buggy...  and
no grouping up to go take out that evil
necromancer and grab his Savant's cap
for yourself, either, if you want that kind
of action, it will have to be against the
NPCs.

My point, though, is that it is not necessarily
true that you'd expect to see a bunch of
newbies going PvP right off the bat; I'd think
most would do just as I did, and assume the
POD thing was in place to give us the ability
to roam around for a while with the safety on,
and switch to PvP once we had a little
experience with the game and our characters
weren't quite so wimpy.
