That depends on what you mean by "easily available".  What I have
is many sheets of paper with boxes for each metal/gem combination.
In each box I have written the skill levels followed by a sequence 
of +/- for each success or failure and a notation (triv) if I found
that the combination was trivial.  I still maintain the sheets,
although I stopped noting successes and failures for silver and
electrum long ago.  Just typing it all in would be quite a task.

I find it very useful to have a history of my success rate when 
setting a price for an item.  It's handy to be able to say, with 
solid evidence, "No, I would be losing money at that price."
