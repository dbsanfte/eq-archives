They're only tactics if the other side labors under something approaching the
same conditions.  Obviously, there should be no limitations, and when you
zap a critter through a window, he should take cover, sneak out the room, 
sneak around behind you, and pummel you.  That doesn't happen because it's a 
hard programming problem.  (Okay, not just hard.. research level hard).  Same
with characters being able to deal with the z axis.. pathfinding in a real
three dimensional space is MUCH harder than 2-D.  It's a limitation of the 
technology at the time the game was made (and probably still, for that matter).
Part of playing a computer game.  It doesn't signify willful minded desire on 
Verant's part to screw the customer.  More likely, it won't be nearly so bad 
in the next product, but it would require large capital investment to change in
this one (and would likely introduce more bugs.. etc..).  So, we're stuck with 
it the way it is.  If it offends you that much, quit.  That's the only way to
show them that it's _that_ important to you.
