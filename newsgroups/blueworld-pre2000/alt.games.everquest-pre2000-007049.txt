That sounds like a server-specific problem.  I really don't 
think E'Ci has overlords.  I have experienced one attempt, by 
a very nice bunch of guys, to seduce me away from my guild with 
promises of power-leveling, but I really think that would ruin 
the game for me.  Your friends probably dropped out of the game
as much because they found themselves suddenly at level 50 with
nothing to do as because they were surrounded by dorks.

The guilds you are describing sound much like the worst guilds
in UO.  They were set up by people who gain satisfaction from
being placed above others, no matter how trivial the situation.  
They feed on joiners, people who need to be a part of something.
Pyramid schemes are common, where the way a member achieves rank
in the organization is by bringing in new members beneath them.
The more egregious of them required weekly dues in gp or ingots.
(Incidentally, this is why I was so opposed to the AC allegiance
system.  It seemed to codify the very worst aspects of guilds 
from UO.)

EQ's guild system is really great since the social aspect is
emphasized over the structural aspect (chat channel, no visible
guild titles).  It can be a nice way to get groups and meet 
people, but I would beware of any guild that places importance 
on your level or your gear.
