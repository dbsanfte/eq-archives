The easiest way to implement what Gordon describes is to distribute
group experience weighted by total current experience and use an
exponential experience gain curve.  We know that the experience gain
curve is not a straight exponential (because of hell levels), but I 
think it is close.  

The effect that this split algorithm will have on classes with an 
experience bonus or penalty is that the penalty is essentially shared 
with the group.  For example, if your group consisted of two people, 
one with a 25% penalty and one without, all experience earned by those 
two people would be split (100/225) and (125/225).  So the one with 
the penalty would get 5/9 of all experience earned and the other would 
get 4/9.

If you group with someone who is on the other side of a hell level from
you (say you are 29 and they are 31), you will get a tiny fraction of 
the total experience, and they will pull away from you.  Gordon's 
statement that hell levels bring groups closer together in levels is 
not actually true in the long run, although it will appear that way
when the group crosses a hell level.
