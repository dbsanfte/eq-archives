> Try to put yourself in the game world.  If you were going to hire
> a fighter to guard your keep, you would be able to guess something
> of their ability by looking at them.

Walk up to a character. Click them to target. Press "c" for /consider.

If the con message is red, they are more than two levels higher than
you.

Yellow, they are 1 or 2 levels higher.

White or black, they are the same level.

Blue, they are lower level.

Green, they are much lower level.

The only case where /con doesn't provide enough information for
grouping purposes is when the /con response is blue, since that can
cover quite a wide range of lower levels.
