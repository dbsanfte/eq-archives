The "bad" price merchants are not limited to one area.  Take Boomba in
W. Freeport, Sonsa Fromp in Misty (Kevlin Diggs in Rivervale talks of
her), and Rathmana Allin in South Ro.  I think (not certain) that the
Shady Swashbuckler may have the same deal, as well... or it might just
be that the things he sells are more expensive.

Sometimes it's based on the item (A Lightstone, for example, has a base
price of 1pp.  A GLS, though, has its base priced doubled when selling
back (9pp versus 4.5pp or something like that.  Been a while since I
checked.)  This is obvious to anyone who has seen bronze armor / fine
steel weapons at a vendor.

I am not sure what this is based on - it seems only to occur with higher
valued items (fine steel), so that was my initial assumption, but that
argument doesn't hold for things like Rubies and Combine weapons (so
maybe it's based more on whether the item is dropped from a mob or
purchased from a vendor, like steel armor / combine.)  This wouldn't
explain why regular lightstones sell back for the same base, though.

While we are on this subject, let me throw something else out... Morgan,
you have stated before that enchanted jewelry sells for more than
unenchanted jewelry.  I suppose this may be the case for some items,
but I have not experienced this, at least on the easiest JC items.  For
example, the silver turquoise (cost = 525cp Silver + 115 gem = 640cp)
creates a (mostly) worthless +2 vs cold bracer.  I had both the
enchanted and unenchanted flavor sell back for 639cp, 1cp less than the
cost of the components.

This seemed to be the case for at least the first 4 gems - I did not
try anything beyond that as I just created the enchanter to try out JC,
not as a character to play.  Does this depend on the higher quality
gems, or is it different on the server you play on, or what?
