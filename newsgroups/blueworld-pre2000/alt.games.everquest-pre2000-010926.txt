Wizzies make very efficient group members, but are a huge pain to play (and to
have around) if the group does not include a taunter and/or a healer. After I
took my Wiz into Lower Guk, I really began to see what we could do in a good
group.

/assist as a hotkey, and make sure that you (and everybody else) know who you
are gonna be helping. They Taunt the nastiest Mob back off of you, and in the
meantime you are seriously burning the target a new (ahem)...

Biggest problem is that a Wizard in Firebase mode REALLY needs mana support. If
they get it, combats go a LOT faster. If Wizards do not get Taunt support and
some sort of help getting mana back, they are basically stuck watching the
action while getting back enough mana for 4-5 good nukes.

Best group I was ever with in Lower Guk was in the BR loop. I went into full
'Damm the Mana Cost!' DD throwing mode (Enc + Bard with Mana Song) - And every
combat I emptied my mana bar. When the dust settled, the most wounded member of
our party still had over 80% of their hits, and that was without a Cleric.

For all the combats we hit in that loop (including the GIBs), for two straight
hours...

I repeat: A properly supported Wizard is frightening. An improperly supported
Wizard is at best mediocre, and at worst is going to get themselves and their
party killed.
