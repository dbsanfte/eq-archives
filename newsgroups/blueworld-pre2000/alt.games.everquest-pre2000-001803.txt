Raph Koster wrote:
> 
> I still think the PK switch is a mistake in EQ, but oh well. :) We're
> still going to have to wait a few more months to see whether my belief
> turns out to be correct.
 
I think in the case of EQ you may be correct.  The reason being is that
players really cant exist outside of the bounds of society in EQ.  You
always *have* to have some type of social interaction with NPCs to exist
in EQ.

Add factions in such as Dark Elves, Humans, etc.. and you really have
the potential for some negative impacts on someone who just randomly PKs
other players, regardless of their race.  In essence someone who engages
in random PKing in EQ will end up being hated by every NPC, and damn
near all of them can insta-kill you.

In UO, however there have been no *real* repercussions for character
viability if you randomly slay people.  You can have your own "home" and
deal with vendors out in the middle of nowhere that really dont care
about your past actions.

In any event I was glad to see some "newbie" protection going on.  I
think it will help out those people coming from standalone games to
become accomodated to the environment of a world that is not all "good".

For the same reasons as above, I'm going to give the upcoming race wars
server in EQ a try though.  I think PCs *should* have the ability to be
"enemies" - but even the strongest has to be able to go home.

Of course EQ may have solved a perception problem of  being killed by
Dewds, simply because you are not around to see them chant how bad they
"rokked yo azz".  Its alot harder to get upset about something you dont
know is happening - out of sight, out of mind.  Sure you may lose an
item and your money, but at least you are not being berated at the same
time.
