Though he is rarely mentioned around here, Raymond E Feist is, IMO, one of
the best I have read.  His imagination is amazing, and it is very easy to
get to know his characters.  His main series, "The Riftwar Saga," is
probably his best work.  "The Serpentwar Saga," which takes place decades
after the "Riftwar," is also very good.  Also, if you are into political
intrigue, his Empire series, coauthored by Janny Wurts, is an excellent
choice.  The Empire series covers the other half of the "Riftwar."
