My main problems with EQ  were:

1. Although the 3D idea is neat, they won't save the game for you if the
rest of it is not appealing to you.

2. You have ZERO chance to escape a fight if you're outmatched and there is
no-one around to help you.  This happened to me sometimes, and is
frustrating.

3. You can get blindsided by monsters you didn't know were coming; see point
2.

4.  You can't travel the world like UO, due to successively more difficult
monsters with every zone further away from a city.

5.  The loading & zoning times are annoyingly long (to me...& no it's not a
shitty computer system).

6.  Playing solo is almost impossible....you are vitually forced into group
play (not my preference).

7. And last, but most importantly:  if you're an EQ player, don't get too
proud of the fact that there are no assholes/PKs there.  The game is not
wide open, and the vast majority of player interactions that are allowed are
co-operative ones.  People don't f**k with each other because they aren't
allowed to, not because they are good people.  The only way to get people to
help each other is to restrict the hell out of a game.  Face it...there just
ARE lots of pricka around....in real worlds or virtual ones.  Might as well
get used to them (or killing them heheh), they're not going away.
