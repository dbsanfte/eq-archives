Leather armor can't be tailored by PC's.  You could learn to make studs and
eventually steel bonings to make your own studded and reinforced leather
armor, but in all honesty, unless you REALLY want to spend alot of time and
money to learn smithing and tailoring, it'd be much easier to just buy armor
from a PC smith or tailor - you could probably get banded for about the same
price as the better leathers - just because it's so much more common.

I think he means he has a 50 in the Pick Pocket Skill, since he said at the
top of his post that he's 9th level.
To answer your question though, make sure you are picking from things that
actually have stuff to steal.  The goblins in Misty Thicket are your best
bet for practice near your home.  Make sure you aren't attacking them when
you try to pick pocket.  If you get the "Your attempt at stealing is
unsuccessful" - then either the goblin doesn't have anything or you failed
your skill check - just keep trying.
