To actually answer the question, here is what I've seen:

Celeron 300 and Monster 3D card.
    32MB Ram - jumpy, not recommended
    64MB Ram - definitely playable, still jumpy with lot's of activity
nearby
    128MB Ram - Jumpy if running through EC when a bazar is going on.

Celeron 300 (overclocked to 450) with voodoo 3D
    64MB Ram - Better than not being overclocked and a better card than the
Monster
    128MB Ram - Again same

My machine: PII 400 with 256MB Ram and Riva TNT
    Hardly ever jumpy.  Again only noticable jump when EC has a bazar going
on.

I have noticed that zoning is faster with more Ram, but the difference in
speed is much smaller with the jump between 128MB and 256MB.  After 128MB
ram, connect speed is the most noticable factor in lag (screen, movement and
zoning).

I've never seen the problem you described about the lag (sounds like you are
actually describing Packet Loss, which isn't really lag but has the same
effect).  What other differences are there between the machines?  Operating
System?  (95a and 95b are quite different even though they are both 95).
Modem?  (The settings here could make a difference).  Also the MTU/MRU/MSS
settings could affect you, but since things are fine first off, then get
progressively worse, it seems more like a modem/driver problem.  Possibly a
memory leak in the driver eating up resources until there is none left and
you then see the problem.

Hope that helps.
