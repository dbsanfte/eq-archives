> Barbarian warriors are nice to get the hang of the game I find. Easy to level,
> nice newbie zone, and out of the way of hell-holes like Greater Faydark.

I'd have to disagree; for true newbies the Everfrost crevasses are confusing,
and ten to funnel many monsters straight at you.  Worse, there's a plethora of
level 2/3 monsters mixed in with the level ones, and they'll aggro on you 
without any actions on your part (bear cubs, mainly).  I agree with the
dwarf suggestion; the only things that immediately aggro near your starting
point are goblin whelps, which are level 1 monsters.  Once you get past the
first level, or are working on a secondary character, though, Everfrost is
a nice newbie area since you don't have to chase down the first five levels,
but they instead come to you.

Plus, barbs are night blind, which makes life more difficult.
