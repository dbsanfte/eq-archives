One class groups are terrible. There are very few '2 of the same class'
combos that let you double the number of mobs you can kill a minute, and
this only gets worse as you add members, so you can count on massive
downtime after every fight. (This is mostly because spell lines don't stack
and either melee, spell damage, or fight control is missing. Shamans and
necros are really the only classes that can group with themselves with few
to no penalty, and even that gets bad after 3 people or so.)

> I'm also curious what happens when you charm

Not quite. He'll heal you occasionally, but he's far more likely to heal
himself or wrath the other mobs, and he definitely won't cast on any other
party members.

BTW, mobs do have mana, and they do run out; it just takes longer. You can
use this to your advantage occasionally as an enchanter, once you get mana
sieve and Theft of Thought.
