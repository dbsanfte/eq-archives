> 75 strength and 80 weapon skill makes you able to hurt an ettin, but
> anything else is going to outmatch you.

Exactly her point I think.  For normal roaming with friends, being strong
enough to kill an Ettin should see you through.  Others in the party will be
more buff, and you ARE strong enough to do some damage.  As long as you
don't go into a major dungeon, you can do things with your friends without
too much fear.

By yourself is a different matter.  By yourself, you're a prime target for
pk'ers, and a character like this is simply defenseless against them.
Anytime you leave the guard zone, you have to get to where you're going
FAST, or be pk'd.  Kind of removes the joy of exploring.

You know, I've thought about the problem with craftspeople being
defenseless.  It seems to me that the skill system could be tweaked to
provide some protection.  GM Smiths might be able to create custom plate,
specially fit to themselves and themselves only, that provides massive
protection.  Their effective attacking strength could be enhanced when using
hammers because of their lifetime of swinging a heavy sledge.

GM Tailors could make self-only clothing that blends and camouflages, making
them difficult to hit, and very high agility in combat when using thin
piercing weapons.  Maybe even allow them to equip a dagger in their off
hand, and give them a chance to cut the fastening of the opponents armor,
dropping it to the ground.  Who else but a GM Tailor would know enough about
clothing to do such a thing?

Lumberjacks with strength and an Axe, Fishermen with a pole and a net, Hell,
even cooks would have their massive knowledge of anatomy from all that
butchering, and be very handy and VERY fast with a cleaver (Ever think of a
Benihana Chef in a fight?).  It seems to me that rather than separate Combat
and Trade skills, they could easily make high end trades-people very
formidable.  Maybe even make them MORE formidable as compensation for only
being able to use one weapon/fighting style, and in reward for their
mastering a difficult trade rather than just hacking/slashing/pking.

This sounds VERY interesting to me.  If my trades-people were something more
than just lambs for the pk's to slaughter, I could very easily become
interested in the game again.
