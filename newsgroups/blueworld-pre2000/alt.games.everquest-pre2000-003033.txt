Actually I think EQ is a lot better.

Mobs will use spells in appropriate manners. They will summon pets,
heal themselves or friends, buff themselves up, cast DD or DOT spells.


They also know to go after healer/caster just like we do.

Regarding to strategies, EQ mob AI is way ahead of AC.

Pathing is a very difficult problem, particularly in 3D space. In EQ,
mobs are given a number of fixed part to go from point A to point B if
there is no direct path to an enemy. (Go to raider room in High Keep.
If a raider focus on you, he sometimes will go around the room poles
to get to you instead of going the direct route.) This is a good
compromise solution without using a lot of computing power.
