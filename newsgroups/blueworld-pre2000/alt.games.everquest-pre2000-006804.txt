I really can't understand why people don't use common sense a little bit
more. Let's give it a try, shall we?

a) the group that got there may have expended great effort to get the area
in a position where they could reap the benefits, therefore they should get
some reward from it, be it XP or an item.

b) said group cannot get rewarded to the exclusion of others who come along.
everyone is entitled to have fun on a given mob.

so instead of following the letter of the policy, follow the spirit of the
policy.  tell the guy "oh i've been waiting here 5 hours for this spawn" and
give em a chance to say "ok, how about you get this one, and i get the next
one..."

or "we cleared this camp out ourselves" and give em a chance to say "ok, how
about i take every 1 in 4, or 1 in 5."  and move on from there.

the play nice policy should be treated like an evolving policy that can
adapt to the situation at hand. people who look at policies to exploit are
the main reason why we need policies in the first place.
