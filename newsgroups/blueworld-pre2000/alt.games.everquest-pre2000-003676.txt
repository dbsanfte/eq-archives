You can destroy the item.  The only purpose for NO DROP is to 
prevent trading items, not to force you to be burdened down by the 
item for all time.

There are two in game reasons for NO DROP items.  The first is
for quest items.  When you are doing a quest, you are supposed to
actually perform the actions required by the quest.  If you are
told, "Kill the giant and bring me his head!", you should do that,
not go buy the head from someone who is killing the giant over and
over and selling the heads.

The second reason is for special items that are earned through 
quests or special encounters.  The reason it is NO DROP is that you
have the item only if you earned it yourself.  Plane of Fear and 
Plane of Hate items are NO DROP, so you can only have them if you
have personally survived in those places and won the gear in battle.
