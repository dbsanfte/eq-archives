> how is it a massive step back?

Lessee.  Removal of basic required features (secure trade, banking), tacit
support of exploits ("trapping" is permitted), lack of basic support of
roleplay (no naming policy - every little helps), lack of diversity amongst
player races (they have *one*, of which there are three casts), skill based
system (everyone will be a tank/mage - well archer mage - in 6 months if not
sooner).  I've not even mentioned the lack of challenge and poor creature AI
(although this sort of leads from some of these problems).

This genre is new, it needs each new product to propel it forward and add to
the previous.  Ultima was a kind of side-ways step.  It added to the
Massiveness that Meridian59 could never manage but it's playability was a
huge leap backwards, Everquest was a great progression.  Asherons, has no
redeeming features, except, possibly, for the dynamic world events.
