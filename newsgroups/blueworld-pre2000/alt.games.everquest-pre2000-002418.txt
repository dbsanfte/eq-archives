There can be quite a number of other things to do, build and acquire
without so much "building stats to become more powerful."  Although
learning/acquiring a skill or "becoming bettter at x" *is* stat
building, it needn't be something that makes your character more
powerful, directly.

It's mainly the "power building" I would do away with.  As for
acquiring other skills and knowledge and wealth and whatever else...
eh.  Not too much wrong with that.

I think I would have characters focus more on improving their
"standard of living", upgrading their environment, improving their
station or standing in the game, without the character itself slowly
transforming into a lesser deity.

But then, I would also go with a community-oriented sort of game,
which would included community-vs-community competition (and
cooperation), and the manner of improving your standard of living
would be by improving the community in which you live - benefitting
from your success indirectly, in other words.

And yeah, it'd be hell for "I wanna play solo" and "i wanna rp an evil
dude that kills everything" type people.

Although a suppose a mess of rangers scattered throughout some forest
somewhere that hardly ever talked to one another could be considered a
