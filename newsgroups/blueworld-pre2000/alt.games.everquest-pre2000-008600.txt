The spell messages could definitely use some work.  It would be
nice if they found someone with decent spelling and grammar to 
type the things in.  Failing that, they could use a spell checker.
However, I think that short messages are better.  Spell casting
messages should be no more than one line of text or we would be
drowned in spam.  Also, your interpretation adds more detail 
than I would want.  It leaves me no room to interpret the spell
myself and thus diminishes my role playing experience.

I don't think a zero recast time is a good idea for the spell.
First of all, I think there is a minimum recast time of about 2
seconds for everything to keep people from spamming the servers
with spell casts.  But also, the total cast+hold time determines
the delay for setting up your whole group for travel.  That 12 
second cast would be a real pain.

I have traveled under the Scale of Wolf and find it to be a nice
speed improvement.  My companion and I moved so quickly that 
nothing even had a chance to aggro on us.  If you find that the 
speed increase does not justify the added risk, then don't use it.
