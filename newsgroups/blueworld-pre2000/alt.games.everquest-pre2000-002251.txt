I totally oppose making corpses unlootable. That would be even worse
for the game than a switch.

Here's what I would do:

1) Nix the current Order / Chaos system. Implement some variant on the
faction system that's been discussed. Make sure that this has tangible
goals for each side, such as city control flags that provide a bonus
to those factions which control them, etc. Have some kind of public
scoring system, things like that. Membership in a faction is
contingent on having no more than one short term count (one is free,
so that assholes can still be killed). If one is exceeded, that person
is immediately booted out and cannot rejoin for a given period of
time. 

2) When a blue person is killed, his attacker remains flagged grey to
him for one real life week. If the victim was a member of a guild, his
killer is flagged to everyone else in that guild as well for the same
period of time. Death of the attacker to any cause has no effect on
this flag. 

3) If the victim is a "true innocent", meaning no counts of his own,
no combat skills above 70, however you define it, the killer is unable
to recall out for ten minutes and his stats are cut in half for that
same duration. If the attacker is not already red, he receives an
eight hour temporary red equivalent flag. 

4) A stat loss murderer's (meaning five or more short term counts)
other characters are given negative level 5 karma for the duration of
that murderer's stay in stat loss land. That cuts off access to NPCs
and therefore banking outside of Bucc's Den.

5) Looting a blue corpse provides a ten minute flag, not a two minute
one, and it remains a guard kill offense for the whole of that time.
