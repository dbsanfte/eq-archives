I've been playing with the same group since about level 19 when I 
met them in Unrest.  We only get to play as a group for about 8-10 
hours a week and we try not to play at other times because we want 
to stay the same level.  We are all level 40 now.

As a group, we like challenging fights.  Riskless xp farming may 
be popular with others, but we get bored with it really fast.

If you want to fight in an organized and efficient group, you will
probably need to build up a regular party.  You have to be able to
rely on everyone to do their job without instruction and that is 
just not going to happen very often in a pick-up group.
