The real problem with this, as with granting broader and broader
power to the police, is not that they will use it to do what they
state, but that they will *abuse* it.

A lot of people have financial information on their
computers...credit card numbers, checking account numbers and
balances, etc. Imagine the following scenario. AVerantEmployee_01
needs more money for a downpayment on a car. They get the evil idea
to come in to the Verant office at some time when it is
unpopulated...night, lunchtime, whatever. They scan random
harddrives, looking for the Quicken directory, until they find a few.
A month later, people are having problems with their banks and no one
knows why. And some guy in San Diego has a new car.

Given how buggy the game is, do you really trust Verant to make sure
that something like that could not happen?

BTW I am not suggesting that this would happen or that Verant would
offically condone it, just that it is a possibility.

Think very carefully before giving away your rights, people.
