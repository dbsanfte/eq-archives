Dan Harmon wrote:
>

> 1) In which enchanter guilds can I find the Tome of Endless Enchantments?
> The only place I've checked so far is the bottom floor of the WFreeport
> caster guild & didn't see it there.

I think Dulia Jestes, the woman outside by the pool, has it.

> 2)  I've heard (not verified) that a MAGICIAN'S level 16 research skill is
> capped right at 16 (or can't go up, one or the other).  Apparently they
> can't gain skill until level 20.  Is there any problem with me putting in
> Practice Points as an enchanter?

The skill cap for Research is 5 * (Level - 15) for all casters.  When you 
first get it at 16, the cap is 5 but you train it to 16.  The cap raises 
to 10 at 17, 15 at 18, and 20 at 19.  So you have to wait to level 19 
before the skill will rise at all beyond your training.  At that point you 
can use training points or raise it through practice.

> Before I try to create the AE mez spell, I want my skill to be as high as
> possible.  I have LOTS of other pages in my locker, really just gathering
> dust.

Ask a higher level Enchanter to combine the pages for you if you only
have one pair.

> 3) Can I combine ANY two pages - even if they aren't supposed to combine -
> and have a shot of increasing my research skill, or must they be a VIABLE
> combination to gain skill?

No, they have to be a valid combination.  You also can't practice
by making spells for other professions.

> 4) If I have a pair of pages for a level 24 spell (I'm almost 16, as I'm
> sure you can tell), can I attempt to make that now?  Can I gain skill?  Do I
> have a shot of actually being successful?

You have basically no chance of success and (I think) no chance of skill
gain.  Basically, you gain the ability to make level 24 skills when your
skill is maxed for level 23.  The level 20 spells become trivial soon
afterwards.
