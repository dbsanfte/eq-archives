I did. You have to take the package as a whole, not just look at the
skin of it. Druids are better in open and mostly flat outdoor areas.
They are balanced because they are worse in dungeon areas. I have seen
druids die more often than any other class in dungeon areas (bad armor,
lousy melee, many spells don't work, etc.). If you only look at the
outdoor areas, then druids will come out on top of just about any other
class (but not at any level, only at specific target levels). If you
just look at dungeons, then druids lose half their effectiveness and are
worse than just about any other class.

But you originally made a point of comparing the druid's ability to find
*named* spawns...please note what my druid's equipment from named spawns
is and where it came from (originally, not where I actually got some of
it, which was through plat-based purchase or trade, since I'd die trying
to get it otherwise):

Shoulders: Braided Ivy Cords, from an outdoor mob
Chest: Foreman's tunic, from a dungeon
Weapon: CIMS, from a dungeon
Shield: CGS, from a dungeon
Feet: Kobold Hide Boots, from a dungeon

Note that as a druid, I would be forced to go into dungeons (where I am
least effective) and hunt named mobs to get loot that is useful to me. I
still want a runed circlet; it comes from a dungeon. I can probably
count on my fingers the number of outdoor named mobs that I can be most
effective hunting; there just aren't that many and the loot they give is
of questionable value to me.
