The highest level bat is 42, about half are green to me at 54 now.
The lava beetles are the same level as sonic bats.  LDCs can go as
high as 45--making them lower level than the guardians and on par with
the big doggie sentries.  They hit about the same as well.

Imp protectors go as high as 48, and they hit about the same as most
of the skels in Karnor.

Kobolds:  33 to 37.
Sonic bats:  38 to 42.
Lava beetles:  38 to 42.
LDC: 43 to 45.
Imps: 44 to 48.

Compare these to Karnor mobs:

Sentries (little and big ones):  38 to 44
Guardians:  46
Skels:  42 to 50 (from skel guardians to decayed soliders)
Ghosts:  40 to 51 (callers/curates to knights).
Hands:  44 to 52 (hands and constructs).

Karnor is a bit tougher than SolB.  SolB, you don't see the imps until
you get well past the kobolds/bats/beetles area.  In Karnor you don't
get the skels (you can get them sooner since the moat area is always
roaming with them) until a bit past the second bridge.

Karnor has higher mob density, more higher level mobs nearer to the
entrance and have about 12 minute respawn compared to more than twice
that in SolB.
