Well, if you're looking to spend your plats, then try to find cured
silk items (the best armor a monk can have) and maybe see if someone
is selling some "Wu's Fighting Gauntlets" which are magical.

I dont play a monk personally, but my fiance has, and has grouped with
many. Biggest thing, STAY LIGHT. The more you have the slower your
body reacts to fighting. I've been told that more than 15 pounds tends
to encumber a monks movements in battle.

Monks require very little in the way of supplies from the experiences
I've heard of and seen. Even though they're 'fist fighters' train up
in a blunt. I've heard that the monk only staves (i think they're
1handers) are better at dealing damage.
