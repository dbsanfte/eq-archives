To the best of my knowledge, here's why this happens, especially
in the planes:

MOB has a hate list, the hate list is N entries long

N+M people area beating on the MOB constantly, so the hate list
will always have M people not on it, even though they're hurting
the MOB.

What this means is that even though that wizard did 1200 points of
damage with his IC, and that group of warriors only did 300 with
their swords, the wizard will be knocked off the bottom of the hate
list because there are so many people swinging at the beast.

This can lead to the impression that XP/looting is awarded to those
who swing the most, not how much damage they do, since swinging
puts you on the hate list and will knock off those who haven't done
anything in the last few seconds.

This can also lead to soloists getting XP, if they can time their
megadamage to right before the MOB dies, so they can keep their
place in the hate list. If a melee soloist gets lucky, he can also
get solo XP in this way.

The fix is simple: bigger hatelist, but that has it's own set of
problems, depending on resource limitations. Another one would be
to have X swappable slots at the bottom of the hate list, but that's
also not particularly fair, but would mean that wizzies can `KS'
almost every time in the planes versus a group of melee characters.

Anyway, I hope this answers some questions.
