T2A is not different than UO from a game-play stand-point.  OSI expanded the
world (a little) and upgraded some of the graphics; but even the "new"
graphics are not up to par with other 2D games at the time.

The EQ vs UO comparison can be summed up very easily.  EQ offers players
many choices in the style of game-play while UO offers almost no choices in
the style of game-play.  The downside to EQ is that YOU must play the game
in a manner that YOU enjoy, if you match your play-style to that of someone
else; you may become very bored or discouraged with the game.  UO is just
the opposite, if your play-style does not match one of the two offered by
the game; you have no chance to enjoy the world of Britannia.

I have a couple of favorite characters from old pen & paper RPGs that I
wanted to see come to life in an online world.  They both can be very
successful and enjoyable in EQ.  Only one has a chance of being successful
in UO and neither has a chance of being enjoyable in UO.
