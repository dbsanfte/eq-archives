That logic is faulty.

You are paying for a service that you can use any time you choose.
The price for that service is set under the assumption that nobody
actually uses it all the time.  Instead, they make estimates of 
how much time people will actually spend using the service and how 
many people will be using it at once and plan hardware and other
infrastructure accordingly.  If people started macroing on a large 
scale, the percentage of users on at once would climb tremendously.
Either we would experience a lot more lag or Verant would have to
raise rates to cover the additional hardware.
