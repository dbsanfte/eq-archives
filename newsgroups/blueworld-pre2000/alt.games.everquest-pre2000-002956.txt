The guards in each area have different personalities.

In Everfrost, the guards will kill anything, even mobs that may (and do)
have the better of them so they themselves end up dead.  THey seldom stray
far from their post.

The guards in Kelethin are less pious and often seem disinterested in the
plight off their kin.

Meanwhile the guards in Nektulos are overzealous and chase around well away
from their post killing anything around.

I like this different character and it doesn't take long to get used to it.
