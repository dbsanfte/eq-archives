I don't like crowded zones myself (so why is my Ranger in South
Karana?).  Try Oasis if you want - I have never hunted there
and can not advise.

I had a lot of fun in the Gorge - camo and sow the warrior.
Minos have so so loot, muddites have terrible loot.  You are
not yet big enough to start working the evil eyes safely unless
you are in a group, but one polished bone bracer will pay for a
lot of spells.

Unrest is currently very crowded, but it is out doors and the
yard trash would be perfect for you two.  Great experience, so
so loot, and if it gets too crazy you can hunt aqua goblins in
Dagnor's cauldron.

Terrible loot but out-doors would be the basilisks in Rathe
mountains.  Bind the warrior in the arena to hunt here.

You might try Befallen - some folks like it and others do not.
