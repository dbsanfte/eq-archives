The point is that some people will shout for help, not yell.  Other 
people see big red text that says "HELP!" but have no idea if 
they are close enough to help.  If you use /yell instead of /shout
then the people around you will know that you are in trouble and
might be able to come to your assistance.

When I see someone shout for help, I usually just tell them to use
/yell next time or at least give an indication of where they are.

The other issue is that some people will routinely get into fights
that they can't win, get the monster down to half and then call for
help to finish it off.  The second or third time you see the same
person getting in over their head with the same kind of mob, you
tell them that they should group up.
