    Having never played UO, I won't comment on it. However, I do play EQ,
and I can tell you from first hand experience that there are lots of helpful
people out there.

    I started EQ exactly 2 weeks ago, and in my first play session as a
newbie, I had an elf give me 5 gp, a barbarian give me a leather tunic, and
another barbarion made me some patchwork armor, and none asked for anything
in return. Other PCs were very helpful when I asked them questions, some
even going out of their way to lead me to various locations (usually to my
corpse).

    Within these 2 weeks, I've added 18 people to my friends list and I've
joined a guild. I meet with several of my new friends and guild brothers on
a nearly daily basis to adventure as a group. Whenever I'm on EQ, I check to
see if any of my friends or guild brothers are also online, just to say
hello and ask how they're doing. Usually, someone will invite me to join
their group within a few minutes of my arrival online, even though I'm still
a newbie and of relatively low level.

    As far as higher level people not wanting to group with lower levels,
its because the lower level character couldn't survive the monsters the
higher levels need to fight. And if they fought monsters of levels more
appropriate to the lower level character, noone would get any experience.
BTW, I watched as a 10th level character took a 5th level out to hunt, not
as a group, but just as backup in case the 5th level got in over his head
with a monster. She let him get all the kills and experience and just stayed
close by in order to watch his back.
