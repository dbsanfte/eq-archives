Yes, and no, in some places, you would like to have Harmony, sow and
the healing powers of the druid, like for instance when you are trying
to break a spawn. In other cases, you can make the necro's more
efficient, with Clarity , dd's, debuffs and your Animation.

the mob, or deal the mob damage with very little risk to yourself.

Rangers does the same, especially after 39, when they have Sow, and
you can fear kite.

Against undeads in Guk, or specs, you could also have a cleric as a
partner.
