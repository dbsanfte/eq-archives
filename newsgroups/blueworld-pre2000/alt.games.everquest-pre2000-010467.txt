If you want to play PvP, you are much better off doing so on a real
PvP server, not on a regular server, or using duels to work out your
aggression.  Blue named characters can't cast any spells on red named 
characters, so you can't be buffed, healed or bound by 99.9% of the 
players on your server.  Also, you won't be able to find any other 
red named characters to fight with because there are very few of them.

The Tome of Discord concept is a relic of early decisions by Verant
to provide a PvP switch because they had no idea how phenomenally
uninterested the RPG-playing population was in PvP.
