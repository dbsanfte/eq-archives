I just figured that it was the non-linear thing.

If you look at item prices for multiple items in the same line
(check maximumeq for some ballpark numbers), ranking them from
worst to best, you will often see a price change such as the
following example;

15 plat
80 plat
300 plat
2000 plat

Now the 4th item might only be one or two points better than
the third best item, but at some point there will be folks
willing to pay whatever it takes to get the very best.  

A similar pricing pattern appears in salaries for sports
superstars, computer programmers, and business executives.  In
a situation where marginal increases in ability have meaning,
the top performer will get a surprisingly large reward.
