Actually, the duration of Augmentation is fixed at 27 minutes.

All of the haste spells, including Alacrity, have a duration of
(12 + 2 x Level) ticks.  So when you get Alacrity at level 24,
it lasts 60 ticks, or six minutes.

The haste effect of Alacrity is a fair bit better than that given
by Augmentation, but the stamina regeneration and agility bonus
are very nice.  Also, if the fighter has a haste item, all haste 
spells end up giving the maximum possible haste value due to an 
error in the math, so you should use Augmentation.

I never cast Alacrity after getting Augmentation.  I do use 
Celerity on fighters without haste items because the haste value 
is enough better than Augmentation to be noticeable.
