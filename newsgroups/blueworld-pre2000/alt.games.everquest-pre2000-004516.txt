In my opinion, the information you're talking about is only
valuable to people who view the game as a challenge to
overcome no matter the means.  Power levelers and such.
While I don't really care if people power level it seems to
me they are missing part of the fun of the game.

Let me ask you this:  How many professional baseball players
studied the physics involved in throwing, catching, hitting,
and running before they become good at the game?  Would it
help them to go study it now that they are good?  I know the
pros use sports medicine and study mechanics, but would that
be a good thing to use in Little League and municipal adult
softball leagues?  Sometimes the game is best learned and
enjoyed just by playing it and I think that's what Verent
has in mind.

For one, I appreciate their efforts to keep cheat codes and
such out of the environment.  Perhaps keeping some game
mechanics secret is not such a bad thing.
