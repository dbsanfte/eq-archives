Well, there are also the single target versions and the stuns.

Level 
 4: Mesmerize		Single target 24 second stun.  Any damage 
			taken by the target breaks the spell.  This
			spell has a small chance of causing memory
			blur (wiping the hate list).

16: Mesmerization	Area effect version of Mesmerize.

16: Enthrall		Upgrade to Mesmerize.  Lasts 48 seconds and
			has a greater chance of memory blur.

34: Entrance		Upgrade to Mesmerize.  Lasts 72 seconds and 
			has a yet greater chance of memory blur.

49: Dazzle		Upgrade to Mesmerize.  Lasts 108 seconds and
			has an even greater chance of memory blur.

52: Fascination		Upgrade to Mesmerization.  Lasts 30 seconds and 
			has a greater chance of memory blur (possibly
			similar to Enthrall).

54: Glamor of Kintaz	Upgrade to Mesmerize. ??
59: Rapture		Upgrade to Mesmerize. ??

There is also the line of stuns:

 4: Color Flux		Area effect stun around caster, lasts 4 sec.

20: Color Shift		Upgrade to Color Flux, lasts 6 sec.

44: Color Skew		Upgrade to Color Flux, lasts 8 sec.

52: Color Slant		Area effect stun around caster, lasts 8 sec.
			and mana drain to all targets.

All but Color Skew are great in a train situation to give enough 
time to cast Gate or to give everyone a short head start on that 
mad dash to the zone.  Color Skew has a 2 second cast, which makes
it a bit too slow to be really useful.

Memory Blur spells are useful for derailing a train of normally
non-aggressive creatures, but are not particularly useful in 
standard train conditions unless you are interested in sacrificing 
yourself for the greater good.  However, Memory Blur and its kin
are great for getting monsters to stop aggroing on you after you 
have debuffed them.
