Now that is a mistake.  You were right the first time.  AC, AC and more AC
is the primary concern.  After that choose either Wisdom (if you expect to
cast a lot - I don't) or Resists.  HPs next then Strength for carrying
capacity is next (*not* for combat it has no worthwhile effect) then finally
a little Stam can't hurt you and some Dex for procs.  (This assumes all
stats are over 75 to start with.  If *not* then you *must* get stats to 75.

> I hope some wise and sagely advice come my way. Thanks in advance!

Basics :- 

1. All stats must be above 75.  After that they will *not* provide a
worthwhile benefit in combat.

2. Armour Class will help you fight.  It *must* be maximised.

3. Saves against magic resistances are good.  HP are good

I have 727 AC at level 39 with BI arms and legs which when replaced should
give me around 750.  Add to that my Resists are all between 40 and 60 making
me pretty much immune to magic from equivalent level mobs and at 39 my
natural HPs are better than my Shaman is at level 50 *with* his best HP
buff.

I've been able to do that *and* keep the overall "all in black" look, so it
is possible to stick to an appearance and still get the necessary bottom
line AC.
