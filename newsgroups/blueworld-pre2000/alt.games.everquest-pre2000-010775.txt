> I am now reaching level 16, and I was wondering what type of weapon I must

> what?

The animation will use whatever you hand to him.

Useful things to give him are:

  A weapon with a high base damage.  Your pet will use the higher
  damage but keep its natural delay.

  A Sword of Runes (summoned item).  The Ward Summoned proc effect
  will go off on any target, not just summoned things.

Once your pet can dual wield (level 24, I think), you will want to 
hand it a weapon of any sort, even a rusty dagger.  By then, its 
base damage is high enough that you won't be increasing it with 
throwaway weapons any more, but the Sword of Runes is still great 
if you can get one. :)
