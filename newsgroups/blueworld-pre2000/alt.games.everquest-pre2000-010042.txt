The solution to this is simple:  all you need to do is take a break
occasionally, move around and give a /tell or two for a few seconds, and
you're off the hook.  Once you break the pattern, the GMs will have no
pattern to look to to accuse you of using macros.  I'm not saying that a GM
isn't going to falsely accuse someone sometime, but if you're careful, you
shouldn't have a problem.  If they were really going to crack down on this,
there'd be thousands of people ALREADY banned, and I haven't heard of this.
Hell, *I'D* have been banned already, as I repeatedly cast spells every time
I level in order to max out.  No one has said word ONE to me about this.  I
would think that they only take note if they see the same command going on
for <pulls a number out of his ass> an hour or so.

Unfortunately, Verant has made their decision.  I'm doubting that there's
any new justification someone's going to come up for the use of macros that
they haven't already heard and discounted.  I think that this issue has
wandered off the topic.  The problem is not programable controllers, it's
repeated macros.
