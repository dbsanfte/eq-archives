If my "acceptable" you mean that I wouldn't be upset, of course it wasn't
"acceptable".  However it is understandable.  When things break we have to
fix them even when it's really inconvenient.  Something that went in with
the patch was causing severe problems by increasing the amount of data that
was being sent back and forth between the server and the client.  This
wasn't really apparent with the standard loads that we see on the test
server.  As the number of people on increased for the weekend, we realized
that we had a serious problem.  Leaving the bug in would have made the game
unplayable for nearly the entire weekend for 80% of the subscribers.  A few
relatively short outages appeared to be the better course of action.

We apologize to everyone that was inconvenienced by the problem.  We don't
expect you all to "accept" these problems without getting upset, but we do
ask that you understand.  We don't take servers down for the heck of it and
giggle as we see the "DIE VERANT" posts build up in the newsgroups and on
the boards.
