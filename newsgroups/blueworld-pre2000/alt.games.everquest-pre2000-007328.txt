Yes! Same ratio as the ykeshas without the proc, which is just fine for
planes/dragons since the proc gets resisted anyway.

However. In order to get the waks, you have to camp the maid and the butler
for the fangs then hand them in to a mob that is usually dead. Would most
players rather do that or get into the Ghoul Lord group and just kill the
three spawns in there over and over until the yaks drop? I think the answer
is obvious.

If the items dropped off of a mob are used in quest, those items should
ALWAYS drop, and the mobs should spawn OFTEN. That would be the point, I
think, of questing.

So uber powergamer would have 2 yaks because they had the stamina to camp
the ghoul lord for a gazillion hours, or casual player or quest seeker
would get two electrum bladed wakizashis after a couple hours of doing
something that is difficult but not impossible. Naysayers would argue
that that would make high level items too easy to get.. I would disagree.
