	Remember all those warnings about strange mushrooms?  These guys are also
found in Innothule, in various sizes.

http://members.home.net/hefferman/Froglok.jpg

	Frogloks are to Innothule what Gnolls are to the Qeynos region.  Innothule
itself is home to a variety of smaller Frogloks; the adjacent dungeon of Guk
has a wide variety of much larger ones.  Just stand still for five minutes and
someone will train a few into you.

http://members.home.net/hefferman/Werewolf.jpg

	A battle between the West Commonlands Werewolf and Hannah, whom I took to
be a Druid somewhere between L17 and L20.  I was standing quite close during
the fight, Not Being Seen thanks to my newly-acquired Camoflauge spell.  She
won this fight, though she took a beating...and then not long later, she
hunted down the Werewolf and killed it again, this time with only 25 HP's
left.  Regrettably she didn't get the highly-coveted werewolf pelt either
time.
