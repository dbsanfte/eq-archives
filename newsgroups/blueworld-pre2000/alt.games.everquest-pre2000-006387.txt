The thing is, while I felt Togor might be adjusted, I didn't (and still
don't) think it is a good idea.

This nerf *is* different to any other nerf.  This nerf, *does* negate,
completely, the Shaman class.  Every class needs *something* to be on a high
level raid.  Enchanters *already* have crowd control and clarity.  Their
speed slowdown was not as good as the Shamans, so decent groups would add a
couple of Shaman to their 24 for a Plane Raid.

There is no way you would choose a Shaman instead of more Enchanters,
however, if there Slowdown provides no advantage, except, possible for
Malise.  Yet they also plan on giving that away.

The Shaman would be left with a fair pet, poor DD and good DoTs which do the
equivalent of a single DD over 42 seconds or two minutes, depending on
choice.  Plus Shamans can tank a bit.

This is *great* for soloing.  But why oh why would you choose that for a
Plane Raid.  The entire justification for the Shaman was gone.

It was a very bad call by Verant.  Fortunately they now appear to have
recinded.
