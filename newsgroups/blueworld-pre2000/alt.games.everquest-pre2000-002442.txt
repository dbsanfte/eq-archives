high level character is great (regardless of whether it is a level based or
skill based system) PvP tactics mean less.  Obviously in a system where the
difference in stat/skill/weapon/etc. based power between a newbie and a high
level character isn't so great, PvP tactics mean more.  It could be argued
that the latter is better suited for a PvP environment, and the former for
PvE.

I pretty much agree with this, and think it's a very important part of an
evolving game.  Players get better, obtain more knowledge, more items of
power, etc. and can over time accomplish that which their predecessors

accomplishment, and even nostalgia, for long time players.

> >See, the designer can ALWAYS add tougher mobs.
>
> There are massive pitfalls here. To list the two most important ones:
>
> 1) this means (mathematically) that the game as a whole loses a degree
> of accessibility to newbies. Features and things to do will tend to be
> added at the high level in order to satisfy the high level players
> demanding greater challenge. It is a very common thing to see muds
> where 90% of the zones are intended for groups of maxxed out
> characters only. Newbies are reduced to one or two areas per level
> range, and the entire process of levelling up is seen as just "the
> prelude to the real game." This renders the game less friendly. UO
> suffers from this ("build your character to GM, then go play") and so
> does EQ ("the game doesn't really start until level 10"). It is a bad
> thing.
>
> 2) It runs the risk of unbalancing the entire economy and creating a
> Monty Haul system. The addition of higher level areas implies the
> addition of cooler and more powerful items to serve as rewards in
> those areas. Over time, these items filter down into an economy that
> has insufficient drains. The average standard of play rises as a
> result, and the previously difficult areas are rendered less
> challenging. So, the designer can ALWAYS add tougher mobs, which means
> even more powerful items, which...
>

I think #2 balances out #1 in that the lower level player will become more
powerful over time just as the higher level player does, as more items and
areas are added, and existing areas and items are upgraded.  It's merely the
responsibility of the designers and maintainers to remain vigilant and monitor
and react to this normal and natural game evolution.
