Sorry, I was tacitly assuming he would pay a higher level enchanter 
to enchant the metal for him.  If you start out with 21 points of 
training in jewelcraft, you can make +2 STA earrings with about a 
50% success rate.  If you pay to have the silver enchanted, then
you are looking at selling them for about 3pp each to break even.
Jasper rings will be about 4pp each to break even and you can start
doing those at about 24 skill.

I would still advise the young enchanter to wait until level 8.
Not only will you be able to enchant your own metals and so save
some money, but you will also have more money to work with at that
level than at level 2.  I have heard people suggest that you wait
until after level 20 to take up a trade skill, but I thought that
was too long to wait and started in at level 8.
