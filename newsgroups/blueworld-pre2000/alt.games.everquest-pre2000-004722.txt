I tried getting the second computer a desk and some flowers...nothing seemed
to raise my faction...I hear that if you stop by a vendor and buy some
diamonds then take the diamonds to the spouse it raises your faction almost
immediately.

The best fix yet for fixing this seems to be sending the person your trying
to raise faction with on the "Quest of the Jealous and Lonely Spouse"

You'll need the following lore items...a platinum card and an airplane
ticket to a faraway city with a spa. Walk up to the Insecure spouse...hand
them the plane ticket and the platinum card. At this point they say
something like "Oh my God, how wonderful. Would you like to go with me?" you
respond, "No, I have to work" at this point the spouse leaves to prepare for
the trip and you have a few days of raised faction. It doesn't permanently
raise your faction but it does help for a while...

Then again...you could just have your local kiter kill the spouse but this
lowers your faction with local law enforcement and the spouses relatives.
You'd be KOS in quite a few places.

Good luck
