Mallow wrote:
> 
> What is power leveling? From what I have heard in the game and read, it just
> seems like cheating.

It pretty much is just cheating.  Power leveling is using a high level 
character's skills, spells and abilities to get a low level character 
up levels quickly.  People often do this for their fourth or fifth 
character when they feel they have experienced as much as they want
of the lower levels and they have a friend who is willing to help.
It is a very bad idea to power level if you have not played through
the level normally at least once.

The only time power leveling is likely to impact your life is when 
someone is doing it in a place where you would like to hunt.
