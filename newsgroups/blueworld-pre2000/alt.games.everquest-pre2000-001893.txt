I realize you were being sarcastic, but I'll respond anyway.

Towards the end of my UO days, I played a couple of
archer/lumberjack/tracker characters. I spent all of my time hunting
in the woods by myself, so I didn't encounter many PKs. On the few
occasions where I did encounter PKs, I fought back. Not out of pride

game. I never won a fight, but I never lost one either. I ended up
running to town after 10 minutes of playing cat and mouse with the
would-be PKs, but the sheer fact that I made them waste their time
trying to kill me was reward enough. Having the desire to defend
yourself and what you've earned does not make you a sociopath.
