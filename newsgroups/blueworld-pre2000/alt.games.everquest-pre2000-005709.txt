We've been through this before, but I'll briefly argue the other side.
There are two main reasons why anonymity hinders role playing.

Anonymity forces OOC discussion to transmit necessary information
about your character.  When forming a group, people need to know
your level.  If you are anonymous, you have to talk about such 
nasty game mechanics issues instead of just sweeping them under
the carpet with a /who.

The character models in EQ are very limited.  Things are a little 
better now that everyone is color coded ;) but seeing a character 
model in EQ gives one far less information than seeing a real person
would give you.  The additional data of class and level makes your
"appearance" a tiny bit more interesting.

The /anon flag should be independent of the /roleplay flag so that
people who want to role play will actually use the flag for that 
purpose instead of as an alternate method for going anonymous. 
They could also add a /showguild flag for people who like to toggle
that option.  We're talking a whole three bits of data here and a 
very simple interface.
