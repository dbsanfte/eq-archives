Morgan, I agree with you that the optimal strategy is to kill the mob 
and be read for the next as soon as you possibily can. But how can this 
be accomplished if we do as some people suggest and depend on the 
meleers to grind down the hp of the mob. Yes, they will adventually 
kill the mob but not without taking a lot of HP damage. Which is more 
important, a healers near full mana and nukers near empty or nukers near
full and healers empty? If you depend on the meleers to do most of the 
damage all you are doing is shifting the mana drain from one class role
to another. And I personally would rather have the healers near full 
should the shit hit the fan wrong then having the nukers. 

BTW, at no  point was I suggesting that nukers mana dump on any 
one mob (unless it is a key god type mob). Evenly space nukes from a
several caster classes players will greatly out damage as a group
then a group of meleers would. And be able to do this without
really going completely empty at the end of the fight.
