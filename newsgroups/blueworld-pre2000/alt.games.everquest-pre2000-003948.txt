Greater Faydark, and runs towards the blockhouse killing
low=level types until he either passes somebody tough or runns
into the guards at the crossroads.  He usually hits the road
near the 2 cabins and then roughly follows that to his date
with Nyzil Bloddforge.

Oh, and he is in the high single-digit levels, so you might
want help at 5th level.
