Everquest is great.  People get so worked up about the things that are wrong
with it because we have all spent a lot of time and energy on building up
our characters and emmersing (sp?) ourselves in the world of Norrath.  I
personally get upset with downtime that is unexpected because I look forward
to playing EQ at those times.  I beta tested AC and really, after playing EQ
I didn't like it, this is a personal opinion but it has no variety like EQ,
you don't feel like you are a part of the world and really, I didn't get
upset when I couldn't play, it was a computer game, whereas EQ, sadly
perhaps,  has become a part of my life.  That is why I take everything
personally and feel I have the right to complain, wether I do or not,
Norrath is in my blood and unlikely to ever remove itself.

Asherons Call is also new, people haven't invested months and months of
playing time into it yet, they don't feel like the world is theres yet and
so don't complain as much.  I think this will change in the future, after
all, does everyone remember the days when EQ was wonderful and there were
hardly any complaints to be made? I sure do. *sigh*
