You didn't read the rest of my story, I guess. Today that mob is
routinely soloed by level 50s--it's not nearly as awful as you think.
(It was originally defined as a level 150, btw). The expected group
tactics involve leaving one member of the party outside the door so
that when the tank is pushed through, the other guy can unlock the
door and come in with reinforcements, including a cure blind of
whatever type. If you had a tank who was prepared, you could also
shove him in to take the initial hit, then open the door and toss in
massive amounts of area effect damage via explosions of some sort to
take out the helpers. You could lure the vamp out into the sunlight,
which pretty much ended the fight rapidly. All a matter of smart
tactics.

The soloers today have a variety of tactics that involve accomplishing
most of the above with a single character.

tactics to defeat. Eventually, the optimum set of tactics was
