There is no active economy for NPC merchants in EQ.

Every item has a fixed price that is part of its data record. For 
the steel breastplate, that price is 750pp.  When you buy from a 
merchant, you get a multiplier based on your faction with the 
merchant and your charisma.  That multiplier is never less than 1.05.  

The lowest possible buy price for the breastplate is:
750 * 1.05 = 787.5pp.  

The highest possible sell prices is:
750 / 1.05 = 714.286pp

Some items, mostly items found as loot, have an extra factor thrown 
in to allow for players to set prices.  Fine steel weapons, for 
example, have an extra factor of 9 thrown in between the buy price 
and the sell price.

A fine steel 2H sword can be purchased for 66.15pp but you can only 
get 6.667pp when you sell one.  I am guessing that your modifier for 
these items is multiplied by 3 and that sword has a base price of 21pp.

If you want to get the best possible prices, make sure you are at 
least indifferent to the merchant and get your charisma as high as
you can (up to about 105).  You will know when you are getting the
very best prices, because a flask of water will buy and sell for 1sp.
