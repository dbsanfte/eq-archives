Good original post, and your reply brings up a good point as well. The
only downside I see to this is that in order to complete said quest and
burn the required amount of time doing it they'll have to have the
player turn in upwards of a dozen items to satisfy quest requirements
(especially if the items are common drops off of relatively easy mobs).
Unless they change the UI (they apparently won't, not in this version of
EQ), NPC's will continue to only accept four items from you at any one
time.

This is easily remedied, though. Simply have the player complete the
quest in parts. Let's say the item is a sword: hand in three items to
the first NPC, who gives you a blade; bank it. Hand in four items to the
second NPC, who gives you a hilt; bank it. Hand in three items to a
third NPC who gives you a precious stone for the pommel. Now take the
three items plus some reagent (maybe viscous mana) to a fourth NPC (or
maybe to an enchanter PC) and have them enchant the components and give
you back the magical blade. End of quest. Total items: 11; total time to
complete: 12 /played hours; total camping time: nil or close to it.

The original proposed solution is not only simple, but elegant as well.

I would propose one more thing, even though I'd be shooting myself in
the foot by proposing this, but Verant should -- in concert with making
quest item drops much more common -- consider cutting out the shortcuts
from quests. Let me use the oft-shortcut Langseax quest as an example...

Okay...I'll keep this as short as possible...

In the long version of the quest, (1) you talk to Renth, find out about
the murders, are (2) sent off to talk to Dok, who sends you to WK to (3)
talk to Ulrich. Ulrich tells you about the sweaty shirt and you (4) run
back to Halas to ask Dok about it. Dok tells you to (5) find Tundra Jack
in Everfrost, who will ask you to give some lion meat delight to Iceberg
for the shirt. (6) Go back to Halas and talk to Teria, who will ask you
to go to WK again and get the lion meat shipment. (7) Back at WK, pick
up the lion meat shipment and (8) return it to Teria in Halas, who will
give you the lion meat delight that you can (9) trade in to Iceberg for
the sweaty shirt. (10) Go back to WK and give the shirt to Frostbite,
(11) Frostbite leads you to the killer; you kill him and loot his head.
(12) Return head to Renth in Halas for your reward.

Twelve steps and a lot of zone hopping involved...but we all know you
can do this a LOT quicker...

(1) In WK ask for the lion meat shipment and (2) take it to Teria in
Halas; she gives you the lion meat delight. (3) Find Iceberg in the
tundra and trade him the lion meat delight for the sweaty shirt. (4)
Take the shirt to Frostbite in WK, follow him to the killer and exact
justice upon him. (5) Return the killer's head for your reward.

Five steps and only half the zones hopped. This is a great quest, BTW,
but if the initial steps were required, it would become a three hour
quest (as originally intended, I'm sure) instead of a quickie one hour
jaunt (if that). This can be easily solved by having the initial NPC's
(Renth, Dok, Ulrich, Teria) give you lore notes or somesuch that you
must give to the next one down the line. It would also make things a
little easier for the casual player, believe it or not...if all you have
to do is take a note (which you can read and it has the clues you need),
then you don't have to remember what exact phrase to say to the next NPC
down the line if the next time you'll talk to that NPC is the following
weekend when you can play again.

Reaction from casual gamer (and most eveyone else): "Cool! I can do this
at my own pace and complete the parts as I make my way around Halas,
Everfrost, and WK!"

Reaction from farmer: "What?!?!? I'm not going to spend three hours on a
sword that sells for 25pp!"

Just a few thoughts,
