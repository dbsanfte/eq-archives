I generally use OOC when asking for buffs and auction when 
offering to pay.  Shout seems rude and demanding.

/ooc Looking to trade Clarity for Spirit of Wolf near XXX.

/auction Paying 1pp for Spirit of Wolf.  I will come to you!

/auction Paying 5pp if someone will come to XXX and cast 
         Spirit of Wolf on me.

My shaman and druid respond much better to requests that are
proper sentences with whole words, including a location and an 
offer of payment or a reason why you need the spell.  I am also 
more inclined to help people who are not anonymous and who have 
reasonable names, but that's just me. ;)

"Come to me and cast SoW" is about a 5pp request, while "I will 
come to you if you will cast SoW" is about a 1pp request.

I don't need to hear your race, class and level in your request.
Obviously, if you are asking for SoW, you can't cast it yourself.
If I am curious, I will use /who to get that information.
