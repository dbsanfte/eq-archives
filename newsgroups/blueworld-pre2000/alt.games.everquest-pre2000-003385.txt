Gladimir wrote:
> 
> Well, I'm not too sure about this one.  I like the idea of titles, but I
> think they should be based completely on faction or quests; not on how much
> time you spend camping items.  I say this because you and I both know that
> only people who camp items for sale can come up with that kind of money at
> those levels.  Now, I'm not against rewarding those who can play EQ in large
> blocks of time; but I think the casual gamer who plays in 3 or 4 hour blocks
> of time should have the opportunity to participate in the high-end economy.

This is not a new idea.  This identical scheme was proposed for UO a 
couple of years ago, and I think it is drawn directly from MUDs.

However, I still think it's a good idea in general.  Achieving a title
is another very nice goal to set for your character and a means of
customizing the character.  I think it should require a difficult quest, 
good faction with the person bestowing the title, and possibly a lot of 
cash (not as a direct expense, but to purchase gems or other items).  I 
would not tie tangible rewards, such as access to superior equipment, to 
the titles.  The title itself would be the reward.
