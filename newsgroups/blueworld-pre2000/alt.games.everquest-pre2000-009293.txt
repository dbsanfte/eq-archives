Well I have purchased the G400, TNT2 Ultra, GeForce, Geforce DDR, now the
Elsa. I was having problems with some games on the DDR, these were not as
apparent on the SDR. Which prompted me to overclock and fry my DDR (I had
3.1 volts going to agp instead of the required 3.3 which caused problems in
some games with small issues yet playable). The new GTS models are .18
micron, so they are using alot less voltage. Anyways to cut to the chase, I
have installed this card, played the games I had problems with, and I must
say not only does this card run faster than anything I have owned, I have
yet to find a problem. Elsa has a good website, and the latest driver set is
up already 5-8-00. I hear the 64meg versions are due in June. I will post
here if I find any problems. So far I like this card alot.
