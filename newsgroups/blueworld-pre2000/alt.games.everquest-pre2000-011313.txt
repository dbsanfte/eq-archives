New spells have a much higher fizzle rate at the _first_ level that you can
use them, since you've just learned how to use spells of that complexity.
Then the fizzle rate drops down as you advance to higher levels.

For example, at level 8 the new level 8 spells will fizzle more than lower
level spells; once you reach level 9 the fizzle rate for level 8 spells will
drop down closer to that of your lower level spells.


Also, keeping your spell skills (Alteration, Abjuration, etc.) maxed out at
each new level helps cut back on fizzles.
