A text-only game where you can (occasionally) pick a race and class, then go
around typing

"Kill XXXX. Kill XXXX. W. N. Kill XXXX Kill XXXX." and perhaps at later
levels "C YYYY XXXX." Use a MUSH, MUX, or MUCK. (decending in use of code)
They're the same except you roleplay and there are no MOBs.

(In the example above XXXXX is a MOB and YYYYY is a spell. The C is for cast
and the W + N are for West and North.)
