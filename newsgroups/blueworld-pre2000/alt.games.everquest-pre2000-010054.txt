> No, you used your finger, not a macro.

But how is a GM going to tell the difference?  Real-life example:  When my
chars are low level, I practice sense heading on that annoying boat ride b/t
BB and FP.  Now, once I get on the boat, it takes about a minute before I've
got the cycle time on sense heading down.  Moreover, I don't need to look at
the screen to tell when it's time to get off in FP.  The game luckily gives
me audible clues as to when I've arrived in a destination zone (in the form of
a jaunting seafaring ditty).  So, in the meantime I press sense heading at a
pretty constant rate, and watch television.  It would be _VERY_ easy to 
completely miss any communications.  In fact, the only reason I catch things
from friends is because I know they're on and occasionally scroll back.  If I
had nobody on that I expected to talk with me, I could miss all sorts of GM
inquiries.  Obviously, then, just *asking* if I'm macroing is insufficient 
proof, as I've *never* macrod, but can easily *appear* to be.

More to the point, though, is that, if the game is so tediously easy to 
manipulate in such a way, what POSSIBLE difference can it make if this is a 
macro or not?  I await a logical argument where the validity of unattended 
button pressing is somehow lessened when done with my finger rather than a 
gamepad.  If the unattended part is the problem, I'm JUST as guilty for 
watching television and (mindlessly) using my finger to press buttons.  
Assumably I should closely watch the screen and feel the joy and tragedy of
each successful/failed sense heading skill?  Then it would be _valid_?

Face it, Verant screwed up on these skills.  Not all skills are like this.
But some are.  And they should be treated in some reasonably manner.  The
random skill increase on success approach is woefully inadequate for things
which don't involve other players and/or components.  It's just mindless 
tedium.  Does this make macroing okay?  No.  But there's no fundamental 
difference between macroing these skills and mindlessly  pressing a button.
