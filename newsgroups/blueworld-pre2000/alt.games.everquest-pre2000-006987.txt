Randomness is not the answer but I think quests could be. The idea of having
every major item a quest would solve many problems. Especially for gamers
like me who do not have the time or inclination to camp monsters.  I think
camping is very boring and quests are much more interesting. Quests on the
other hand provide more interest in the game. Much better than go kill that
mob because he will eventually drop this sword. After all it is supposed to
be a game filled with quests surely we could just expand the quest lists a
bit further.

Also if these items where lore then the players would have to get rid of one
before they could get another. Making things lore has proved quite
beneficial for non camping players, for when the camping group already have
their fill of an item they must leave it on the body because they can have
only one. This enables other players to pick it up.
