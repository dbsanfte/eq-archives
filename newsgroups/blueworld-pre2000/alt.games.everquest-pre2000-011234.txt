>For what it's worth, I've been unable to establish a correlation between 
>the target getting sparklies and being successfully harmonized or not.  It 
>seems the only thing the sparklies indicate is that you're _definitely_ in
>range.  (I've seen successful harmonies with no sparklies, and unsuccessful 
>with sparklies.)  I'm not sure which is more dangerous:  trying to get in close
>enough to get sparklies without aggro, or harmonying from a distance and being
>too far away.

In my experience, if there were sparklies then it worked, no sparklies and
it didn't work.  Sometimes the extras would still come even though they had
sparklies, but I generally attribute this to pathing taking them too close
to each other because it was repeatable and thus not a resist or a fluke.

There is an excellent example of how this works at the front of the Sarnak
fortress in Lake of Ill Omen.  The two door guards, sometimes joined by a
third wanderer who stands between the two guards for a little while and then
goes back down the tunnel.  Stand just a little too far away, no sparklies,
get both every time.  Stand close enough to get sparklies, get one every
time.  With the third one there they all three get sparklies, pull the
middle one and whichever one he has to pass to get to you, or pull one
of the guards and the middle one will always come.

Of course now I have to have particle effects turned on to be able to see if
this spell works.  And I _don't_ turn particle effects on because I hate the
clutter and in big fights, especially with certain classes, your screen just
becomes a psychedelic screensaver until the end of the fight.

So, Verant needs to put the sparklies back in for harmony at the "particle
effects off" setting, or needs to put an out of range message in.  I can't
think of any other spell that _requires_ seeing the sparklies to know
whether the spell is doing anything or not.
