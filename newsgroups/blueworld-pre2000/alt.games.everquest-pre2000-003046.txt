One thing I really like about AC is that the land is huge - way bigger than
EQ. I've run across about half of the direlands and there are tons of
interesting little nooks and crannies. During my runs across the deep Dires,
I have yet to run across another human being. The feeling of exploration is
very intense.

One bad thing about AC is, as others have pointed out, the monster AI is
absolutely brain dead. There are monsters that can cast high level spells,
but have yet to figure out how to walk through a door or go up some steps.
