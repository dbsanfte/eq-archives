Well, the one BIG outright lie I can think of, being a player since day one,
was the one about lag being a client and internet phenomenon and not a
server side issue.  They were telling this to those of us fortunate to be
playing on VERY high speed connects and awesome routes to the UO servers
while we sat scratching our heads wondering how it could be.  You see, it
was interesting how alot of us could get lightning fast ping responses and 0
packet loss from all hops on the routes to the UO servers, and even from the
UO servers themselves, but then in-game go apparently link dead, sometimes
with several *minutes* of freeze before moving again.

The real "mother" of all patches, IMHO, happened shortly after jan or feb of
98 (anyone help me out here?) where that lag was eliminated.  Period, end of
story.  You can tell it is gone because I wouldn't be posting here now, had
that lag still existed.  Plus back then, it was hard to hold OSI totally at
fault for server-side lag since in the face of the stackables duping frenzy,
people I'm sure were causing the servers much more grief than they had been
designed to deal with.
