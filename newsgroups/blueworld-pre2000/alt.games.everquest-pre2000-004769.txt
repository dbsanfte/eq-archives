I get tired of the endless shouts for SoW's and Heals and Clarity and
teleports.  I am more than happy to heal, SoW, bind and so on, but I
usually don't respond to shouts but rather if someone sends me a tell or
talks to me in person.  And when I'm in need of such a service then I
use /who druid 14 50 and see who is in my zone or standing nearby.

And sometimes rather than asking directly for a heal or whatever, I do
emotes that reflect my current state in a humorous manner and, if
usually someone responds.

"Caora wants a bottle of iodine, some bandages and a bottle of gin"
usually gets a grin and sometimes a heal or nice buff while I'm waiting
to recuperate.

And, since I have played a monk and utterly hated all the downtime
waiting for my broken body to heal, whenever I play my cleric and I see
someone sitting around with low hit points, and I have mana to spare, I
heal them up!  They're usually really grateful.  And when I'm playing my
druid, then sometimes I go give SoW on everyone who cons blue or green
to me (She's only level 14).

I absolutely love it when people are randomly kind to me in this game. 
And so I try to spread the good feelings around by sharing whenever I
can.
