epicac wrote:
> 
> I am a cleric. Do I understand correctly that whichever casting skill
> (alteration, evocation, etc.) I have the highest score in when I reach level
> 30 will be my specialty? Also, can casting skills go up by casting lots of
> low level spells that fall under the appropriate skill heading or do I have
> to cast skills at my highest level to learn. What I want to know is if I can
> just cast minor healing until my skill goes up or do I have to cast gheal,
> my highest level healing spell to gain skill- therefore burning lots of mana
> and needing to med. I am worried that there may be some rule similar to the
> rule with melee skills that limits us from learning on anything that does
> not give exp. I cast about 10 minor healings in a row and no improvement. I
> don't think I am at my skill cap, but am not sure 'cause I can't remember
> what my last improvement was.

Only one of your specializations can get past 50, the other four
will cap at 50.  You have to have put at least one training point
into all five skills before any one will pass 50.

Specialization goes up just like your regular spell skill goes up, 
from casting spells of that type.  You get skill from cheap easy 
spells as fast as from hard expensive spells.

If you are going to specialize in the kind of magic that you cast
all the time, then don't worry too much about the specialization.
Just put one point into each of the five and try to keep an eye
on it.  My Specialize:Alteration went up so much faster than the
other four that I didn't need to do anything special.  It hit 51
before any of the others were at 30.
