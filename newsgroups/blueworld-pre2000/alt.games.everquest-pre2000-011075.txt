Actually, this isn't true.  When I was level 34 I was in a group camping the
maid and butler in MM.  We got run over about as bad as I'd ever seen it in
my entire playing days and my corpse was stuck up in the maid room.  Sadly,
a number of my group members were having connection problems, so I found
myself sitting by the lake in MM, naked and alone on CR.  There was no way a
level 34 without invis or any other reliable way of eliminating aggro would
be able to get the corpse out of the maid room solo.  So I called up 3 of my
level 51 friends and had them come and join me.  By that time my level 36
hunting partner had shown up.  We all grouped up for convenience's sake.  So
the group was level 51 warrior, shaman and druid, 36 ranger, and me.  We
killed everything from the pond in MM to the entrance to the castle, before
one of the 51s ran in to pull my corpse out of the room.  What shocked me
was not only did I get experience from the kills, but after the 9 or so
kills we made to get up to where I could loot my corpse, I had gained a
noticeable amount of experience.
