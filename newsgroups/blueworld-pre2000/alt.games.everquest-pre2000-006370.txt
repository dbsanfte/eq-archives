	Okay, we can understand that.  Wizards really need a good
group to be effective.  None of us are really seriously expecting our
class to pick up Clarity, pets, damage shields, or magic debuffs.
We're a narrow class, and I think most of us can live with that fact.
	But there's a flaw in the program here.. in order for a wizard
to work at full potential, he requires:
*someone to debuff magic resists
*someone to increase mana output
*someone to tank for him
*and probably someone to heal him, because he's gonna get smacked no
matter how careful you try to be.

	Now, the fact of the matter is, any group that can actually do
all that probably doesn't really need (or want) the wizard around.
You need a teleporter?  Hire a druid, they get better TP spots anyway,
and their DD is about as mana efficient.  Plus, they bring a whole
great big bag of tricks to the table the Wizard simply cannot compete
with.
	Let's face facts, the only thing the wizard does better than
anyone else in the game is DD burn.  But Wizard DD as it stands now is
a *terrible* way to get the job done.  You have to understand how good
those DoT spells that every other casting class has .
	Ignore the problem with magic reisists.  Ignore the problem
with mana regen.  Hell, ignore the totally screwed up matter of hard
resist caps.  When it comes down to it, the one glaring fault with the
class is what it relies on the most.  And it can be solved simply by
increasing the damage/mana ratio of our DD spells.
	C'mon, Gordon.  Even those dozen or so playtesters that you
guys keep around have to admit that DD burn as it stands now is a
detriment to our class
