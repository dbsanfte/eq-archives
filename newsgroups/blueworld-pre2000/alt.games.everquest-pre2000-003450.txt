There are hundreds of Merchants in Norrath tha we interact with, and
presumable thousands that we don't. Many live in nice, clean, well
guarded cities and go out of their way to fleece us every time we sell
loot to them. They usually make a 100% profit off of us, often times
more. Let them pay the taxes. We are itinerant adventurers, going
where the latest quest or promise of loot takes us. Why should I pay
taxes to Freeport when I spend a lot of time out in the Everfrost
tundra, for example? Are the Freeport guards going to help me if
someone trains the Perma Ice Giant? I don't think so...

On the topic of Heraldic Titles: Neat idea, but I prefer to have to
quest for it, prove my worthiness to carry that coveted title rather
than purchase it ("oh, yes...I _am_ the Earl of Schweimizer...but the
title came with the house"...kind of reeks of Lifestyles of the Rich
and Famous). With regards to the overhead names being longer than
characters are tall: Would it require a lot of programming to break it
up into two or three lines? Line one would be the character name and
Surname (e.g. Snorkeel Watchhander); line two would be the heraldic
title, if they have one (e.g. Earl of Schweimizer); line three (or two
if no heraldic title) would be their guild affiliation (e.g. Rich and
Famous Bunch). So, a name would look like...

               Snorkeel Watchhander
                Earl of Scheimizer
              (Rich and Famous Bunch)

...instead of...

  Snorkeel Watchhander, Earl of Scheimizer (Rich and Famous Bunch)

Just a few thoughts on the subject...
