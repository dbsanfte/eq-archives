Here's a bit of friendly time saving advice.  If a certain thread bothers
you, just filter it out so you don't have to look at it or even know it's
there.  I see you are using Outlook Express.  Filtering threads with certain
key words such as "Florida" is a piece of cake.  The following 11 steps take
about 30 seconds to complete.

1.  Click on "Tools" in the top menu bar
2.  Highlight "Message Rules".
3.  Click on "News".
4.  Click on "New" button and a multi-paned window will appear.
5.  In window 1 check the box beside "Where message is on specified
newsgroup" and check the box by "Where subject contains specific words".
6.  In window 2 check the box by "Delete it".
7.  In window 3 click on the highlighted word "specified" and a window with
your newsgroups will appear.  Click on the desired newsgroup and click "ok".
8.  Click on the highlighted words "contains specific words" and another
small window will appear.
9.  Type in a subject line word you wish to filter out then click the "Add"
button.  Repeat to add more words.
10. When you are done adding words to the list, click the "ok" button.
11. Now read the sentence in window 3.  If it's to your liking click "OK".
Done!
