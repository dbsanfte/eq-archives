Ever Quest is about crowd control.  Damage is nice but
Rangers provide enough damage and the ability of rangers to
root and park is highly under used and very few players
understand the power of their own root ability.

You think Rangers suck because you haven't grouped with a
good group of Rangers?  Rather than do the classic blah,
blah, blah here.  An over reliance on the enchanter and
shaman to control the number of mobs attacking is
inefficient.  A good team / group can survive almost any
train or pull it's the ability of a group to control the
train or bad pull quickly and then sort and kill the most
dangerous mobs first that counts.

In a group consisting of only three players that can control
the train or bad pull rather than five is markedly less
efficient.  While Rangers lack AC and HPs (not when compared
to a Rogue btw.) when compared to a warrior their ability to
help bring the number of mobs currently engaged down to one
more than makes up for the lack of HPs and AC.

A two Rogue full group simply lacks the crowd control
ability of a two ranger full group.  In the two rogue group
if the shaman is under attack and the enchanter is under
attack and the cleric is under attack and two more mobs
appear then what?  In a two-ranger group, the ability to
control a pull or train is much stronger.

Crowd control, not damage, is the most important thing any
group should be concerned with.  It's too bad most players
fail to utilize this ability (root line) but far to many
don't even realize they have one of the best abilities in
the game.  This is why Warriors are so irreplaceable, they
are a really shitty form of crowd control, but a form most
players can understand and use.  A Ranger can do the same
job with the root line and bring the number of mobs hitting
down to one, much quicker than a warrior, monk or rogue can.

I'll bet you agree that being able to engage mobs one at a
time and controlling the situation is far more important
than the ability to deal damage quickly?  The perfect group
you've selected has an emphasis on crowd control just to a
lesser degree than mine.

I'm biased, I get to group with my dream group less the
cleric and add a shaman.  *Shrug*
