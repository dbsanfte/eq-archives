Well, in virtually everything I've ever read, elves (with the
exception of dark elves) are generally considered to be the embodiment
of good.  Considering how much EQ has stolen from D&D, I'd bet that's
supposed to be true here, too.

Either way, though, even neutral races should look _very_ unfavorably
on individuals that murder other races they are not currently at war
with.  There seems to be some sort of alliance between all the races
in Faydwer, so really killing any of those races should make you take
faction hits with all 3.  The Faydwer races also seem to get along
with humans and halflings so there should probably be faction hits
with Freeport, Qeynos, and Rivervale.  Erudin does seem to be sort of
antagonistic towards other races, I guess, so I suppose they might not
have too much of a problem, although I suppose they'd consider it
rather uncivilized (although, as you point out, their justice system
is rather harsh.)

>> Druids and rangers should _not_ be slaughtering Aviaks.

Well, personally I view them as extremely advanced animals.  They
don't have much in the way of merchants (one who barters for shiny
baubles) and not much of an organized government.  Most of them don't

intellegence, however, they'd be exactly what druids and rangers
should revere:  A race that's learned to live in complete harmony with
nature.
