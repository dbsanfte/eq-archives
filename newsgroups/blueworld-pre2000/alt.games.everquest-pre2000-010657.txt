I find quite often that when people talk about the way things "are"
they really mean at some general level, but fail to specify.  I'm
as guilty of this as the next guy; I tend to lump up all my experience
from 1st to 26th and assume it more or less will hold true for all
levels.  Thing is, I already have evidence that things change, from
1st thru 3rd, for example, mages have no pets at all.  For several
more levels, the mage is serving as tank for his pet, but then
it becomes silly to do so at all (at least for a while, who knows what
change will take place at higher levels than I've experienced.)

At any rate, I am quite used to not bothering doing the five recast
dance; time and money at low levels will often see me casting
my pet and not killing him unless he's _really_ low.  From what
I've heard about mana regen rates at higher levels, although the
money will become meaningless, the _time_ will again reduce
this to a special tactic for when I need the very best.
