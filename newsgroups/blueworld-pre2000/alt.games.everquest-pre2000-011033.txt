> I play a Ranger and as such I get an exp penalty. I believe it's 40%, is
> that correct? Secondly, in a group, does this penalty apply to the
> experience from the whole MOB or just the bit I recieve? i.e. If I pair with
> someone and get 60% of the experience from the kill, do I then only get 40%
> of that? Or is the whole experience of the MOB lowered by 40% then I get 60%
> of that?
> 
> The main reason I ask is that I usually play paired with a Paladin so we go
> up at the same time and I'd like to know how this is going to affect pure
> classes if I pair with them.

Yes, you have a 40% experience penalty.  This means that you need 40% 
more xp to get to the same place as someone with no penalty.  

For example:

When a human cleric hits level 11, they will have 1,000,000 xp.
When a human ranger hits level 11, they will have 1,400,000 xp.

If those two hunt together, they will get shares based on their total xp.
Your ranger will get 1,400,000 / (1,400,000 + 1,000,000) = 14/24 = 58.33%
and the cleric will get 10/24 = 41.67% of the experience for each kill.

The experience that the monster gives will not change based on the 
classes that kill it.  The only effect of the penalty is to cause you to
have more experience and thus take a larger share.  For example, if the 
monster gave 10,000 xp to your group of two, the ranger would get 5833 xp 
and the cleric would get 4167 xp, still totaling 10,000 xp.

A group of two rangers of the same level would get 5000 xp each.  If you 
were different levels, the higher level ranger would get a larger share 
of the xp because he would have more xp.
