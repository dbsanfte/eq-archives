That is an option, but there are, in my opinion, better and more 
interesting things a level 10 ranger can do besides camp Dervs.

>2, Change to my corpse kit, grab my 3rd best weapon, and run to
>Rivervale so I can explore Runnyeye.  Would give me an outside
>chance at some blackened iron, and I like dungeon adventuring.

Despite the low level Goblins on the surface (all of which will either /con 
green or very low blue to you at level 10), Runnyeye is NOT a low level 
dungeon. There is really nothing for you to do in Runnyeye at level 10, 
besides get killed. 

However, Misty Thicket is an excellent zone at your level. It is rarely 
overcrowded and there are plenty of things for you to hunt here. Plus, 
there is a ranger camp in Kithicor Forest where you can train, etc. You 
could realistically spend levels 10-14 here and enjoy yourself, for the 
most part. Just be warned that Halflings can be very annoying at times!

>3, Run all the way to West Antonicia and explore around there,
>which is less interesting because my wife's character is based
>in Queynos and so I have seen that part of the world when
>watching her play.

There is quite a bit for you around Qeynos. You said that you like to 
adventure in dungeons, and BlackBurrow is, to me, the best low level 
dungeon in EQ. You could also hunt on the Everfrost Tundra (Rangers are 
great here, due to their tracking), camp bandits in W. Karana, and when you 
are a little stronger and have a good magic weapon, the island in Erud's 
Crossing is great for hunting wisps, which are great loot for characters in 
the low-to-mid teens.
