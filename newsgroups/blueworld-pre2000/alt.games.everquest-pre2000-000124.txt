Many people prefer the reply on top and the quote below. Usually the
header is enough to remind a person what the message is about (not this
time though), then the reply text also usually indicates the subject.

If the quote is on the bottom you don't have to scroll by something
you've already read to see the reply. Many times messages contain quotes
from a whole thread, reply, counter reply, etc., etc., etc. then finally
the sender's response. Really aggravating to have to scroll past all the
old stuff to get to the new content.

Unfortunately, a standard on this has never been established and, in
fact, Communicator 4.5 has provision to automagically do it either way.
So it comes down to a matter of preference. <sigh> <shrug>
