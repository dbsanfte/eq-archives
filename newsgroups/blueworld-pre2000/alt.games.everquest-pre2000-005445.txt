If you are looking for quests that result in gear for you, then you
might try the Darkflame Items database.  It's a nice searchable database.
You can filter by race, class, stats and slots.  This will at least
give you an idea of the names of the things you want.  Some have 
information added about the source of the item.  Then you can either
try to find it in game or totally cheese out and get the walkthrough
from Allakhazam.
