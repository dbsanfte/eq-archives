One sign of a good group is that people have to remind each
other to loot.

That said, I will loot fairly quickly after a battle just to
get the critters out of the way so I can target during the next
fight.

But if I get anything more than coin I will usually ask if
anyone wants it or suggest that we roll to see who gets it.

Hearing that question is another sign that you are in a good
group.
