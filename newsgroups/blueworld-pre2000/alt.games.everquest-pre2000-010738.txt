at level 52, there is literally never a situation where
mages rarely get hit, this won't disprove my thesis, because
I am dead certain that there are at least 25 levels of play

>
> Healing is a priority when you are injured.  :)

Not in the least.  Healing is a priority when you are injured and
expect to get injured some more.  If you are injured a bit thru
some fluke or controllable situation, and can reasonably expect
to go the rest of the fight without getting hit again, then healing
is a back burner issue.  One that you probably aren't going to
address in combat, in fact.  Combat healing is needed when
you are tanking something and it is beating you down faster
than your party is beating it down.  For a mage (at least in
my experience) "get it off me" is what is desired in most
situations, healing be damned.  Now healing the _pet_ can
be a priority, but if several are up and the mobs are double
teamed or rooted, even pet death is not as critical as a PC
death.   After all, the pet doesn't lose exp for dying, doesn't have
to make the run from his bind point....  just resummon, all
you need is a little time, a little mana, and an inexpensive rock.

Not only have I pretty much shrugged off fairly massive injuries
in mid fight (once "get it off me" came thru) but I've gone into
the next fight down to 50% hp, without a qualm; bandaged
up to that point as a matter of form, but relied on the low
probability of lighting striking twice in that short an order, and
a little extra caution in the bargain...  by the time a couple more
fights went by, I'm usually back to full heath never having
taken a hit or spent any extra downtime waiting for it.  Sitting
on my can medding mana does not count, that's a given, and
more of that is done in combat than between combats in
the kind of groups I prefer.
