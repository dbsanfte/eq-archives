Do you have your correct monitor chosen in your Display Properties?  If not,
it could be that you're running your monitor over its specifications.  For
awhile I was running my NEC 4FG at 1280x1024 @ 75Hz -- which it apparently
can do, but is most likely not supposed to.  (I haven't looked up the actual
specs, but what leads me to believe that it shouldn't be able to is 1) when
my monitor is set to nec 4fg in the display properties, windows doesn't let
me set the res over 1024x768, and 2) what 15" monitor should be running
1280x1024?)  Anyway, the monitor is now on its last leg -- you have to leave
it on otherwise you get a nasty yellow picture for 2 hours when you turn it
on, and also it flashes bright then dark sometimes... (it's a spare now).

So maybe if you set the refresh or res too high when you installed the card,
you'd inadvertently think it was the card and not the fact that you may be
exceeding the monitor specs.

Just a possibility.
