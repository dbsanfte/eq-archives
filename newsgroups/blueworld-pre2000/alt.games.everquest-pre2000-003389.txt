Alex Mars wrote:

> Most people don't want silver magic jewelry because it is mostly worthless.
> The only things my enchanter can sell are WIS and  INT rings, and those rarely.

When I was first able to make it, I sold tons of WIS, INT and STR rings.
They are cheap enough that people will buy them on a whim just to fill
out their jewelry slots until they can get something better.  The AGI
rings are also pretty good all the way along.  I also sold a fair number 
of STA earrings, since that is pretty much the cheapest magical earring 
around, and CHA necklaces that people bought to get better prices at 
merchants.

The resistance jewelry is basically garbage all the way along, never worth 
the cost for the tiny amount of resistance you get.

The silver jewelry with high end stones, (ruby veil 4str/4wis, sapphire 
necklace 4str/4int, star ruby veil 5dex/5cha and fire emerald ring 
3str/3dex) still sell about as well as they ever did.  They are nice 
pieces and tons cheaper than the platinum versions.
