I am in australia, and one of our telco's, Optus is starting up cable
access, in partnership with @home.
With many ISP's, and with Optus@home, you are required somewhere during the
signup to read and accept the AUP and the T&C of that ISP.. otherwise you do
not sign up..
Same with software.. the EULA is there.. it's up to you to read it.. if you
click "Accept" or "Continue" etc, then you have agreed to it whether you
read it or not..

in the contract allowing them to scan common ports to check for active
servers.. it it also usually written in somewhere that they may take
"precautions" to protect their service and equipment.. scanning for open
proxies and the like is one thing that this entails..
I don't think that @home is trying to hack your PC.. they are simply doing
what many ISP's do..
