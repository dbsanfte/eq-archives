I don't know what it is about Cazic, especially the Throne Room groups
I've been in, that brings out the most incredible greediness in people. 
I've got a level 30 cleric and quite often the tanks are pulling like
mad, not bothering to check with the casters first, and I'm doing
everything I can to keep them alive.  Any moment I'm not casting a
spell, I'm meditating to keep their greedy butts alive.

At the end of the battle, they're looting all the corpses they're
standing over, or sometimes even DURING the battle, and I get up to see
if there's anything to loot, and it's been picked clean.  

Last night I made a casual reminder that the folks medding might like to
loot from time to time, and a tank said to me, "So get off your lazy
butt and loot".  This particular tank (actually, was a rogue) was the
same one that I was sinking the most mana into trying to keep alive.
Strangely enough, she was the one always screaming "heal me, heal me". 
It only took another comment or two from her before I decided that there
was no way I was going to spend another drop of mana keeping her alive,
and I gated out of their and disbanded.

So, the moral of the story is, and I'm probably preaching to the choir
on this one is, if someone is sitting down medding it doesn't mean they
don't want loot.  If you're doing free-for-all looting, loot 1 out of N
corpses, where N is the number of people in your party.  Don't hog all
the fine steel or just loot the best monsters.  If people are nice and
look out for me, I always go the extra distance to make sure they're
alive at the end of the battle.  If they're being rude jerks, well, I'll
still do my part in the group, unless I really can't tolerate then I'll
leave, but I'll probably not take anywhere near the personal risks to
keep them alive that I would if they were nice.

My take on FFA looting is that it's never fair.  There's always some
greedy jerk taking the best stuff and looting every corpse he's standing
over.
