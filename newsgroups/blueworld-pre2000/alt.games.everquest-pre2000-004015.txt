I can explain why they are no drop, but I'm not sure you would call
it a "justification". ;)  

Before burned out lightstones were added, people could tell the 
difference between wisps that had lightstones and wisps that did
not have lightstones.  (It is still possible to distinguish between
lightstones and greater lightstones by how brightly the wisp glows.)
Burned out lightstones were added to the game as a kludge to make 
wisps that did not have lightstones glow in order to prevent the
selective wisp harvesting that was going on.  They were made no drop 
to reinforce the fact that they have no real value.
