Actually, I don't use spells on hot keys because of my Quake/Unreal
training. Right hand on mouse for directional/look movement and fire
(left click). Left hand on number pad to control forward/back/strafe
operations.  I then need to reach all the way across the keyboard to hit
any hotkey.  This also requires me to take my eyes off the screen.

I would probably use the hotkeys far more if I could map them to the
nicely arranged set of keys immediately to the left of the number pad.
But some bright nit at VI decided that Home/Page Up/Page Dn must always
be used by the Screen and can not be bound to anything else.

I would also love to have the wheel on the mouse allow you to scroll
thru the macro tables. Then I would very seldom have to actually look at
the keyboard.  (What you don't have a wheel mouse?  That's like using a
MAC mouse with only one button!)
