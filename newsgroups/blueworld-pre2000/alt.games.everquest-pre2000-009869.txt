Scyther592 wrote:
> 
>    People keep talking about having their skills maxed oout for their level.  I
> was wondering how can you find out if your skill is maxed out for your level?

Pay attention to the messages as you go up levels or use a log parser 
to track them.  ( http://www.gralin.com/everlogs/ )  Most skills max 
out at 5 + Level * 5, with few exceptions.  Check out Casters Realm 
under creation guides ( http://eq.castersrealm.com/ ) or some other 
web page for particulars.

If you are fairly certain that you are maxed out for a level but you 
want to be sure, try to train the skill with your guildmaster.  If 
you are right, you will get a "You are at the limit of your abilities" 
message.  If you are wrong, you are out the training cost and one of 
your practice points, but you have one more point in the skill.
