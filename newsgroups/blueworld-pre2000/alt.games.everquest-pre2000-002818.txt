I have played both and both have their good/bad points. The one thing on EQ
that rocks is the graphics (you would require a 3D card to play) and UO had
a "bird's eye view" of your surroundings, I believe that character
interaction on EQ is better and PK'ing is frowned upon (on UO I could not go
10 min without being attacked by a PK'er). UO on the otherhand there was
more room for "character development" and you could purchase housing and the
like. I could go on for hours and hours but you asked for my
opinion......and the winner is.... (drum roll)...........EQ!   Seriously
though both are good games I just happen to like EQ better..... the one
thing I have not had a problem on EQ is lag and on UO that like at times was
utterly intolerable..... that's my opinion and I am welcome to it!
