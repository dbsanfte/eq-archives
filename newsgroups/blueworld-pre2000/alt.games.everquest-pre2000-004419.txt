> 1) INT, INT, INT. Yes I know that high intellegence is critical but has
> anyone noticed of skills that sem to help? DEX? WIS?

These may be urban legend, but:

INT - Affects how quickly you learn the skill
WIS - High wisdom gives you a second chance when you fail, and so
      increases your success rate.
CHA - Gets you better buy prices for materials and sell prices for
      finished products.

High Elves do very well on all these stats.

There are rumors that having any physical stat (STR, STA, DEX, AGI) 
under 75 lowers your success rate.  There are also rumors that the 
critical value is 100.  Another popular belief is that you should
never practice trades while overloaded.

> 2) I have no interest in leveling the character - just smithing. Can a level
> one character obtain a high blacksmithing skill? If not - Why?

There is no skill cap for trade skills associated with your level.
Earning any trade skill takes money.  It is much easier to earn
money at level 20 than at level 1, so most people wait until higher
levels before they start practicing trade skills.

Honestly, if I were you I would just learn smithing with a character
who can use it.  Your Ranger is probably smart enough.  Just get some
nice cheap cat eye jewelry for the charisma.  It is much more 
satisfying to have a fighter who can make their own armor than a mule 
to make armor for your characters.
