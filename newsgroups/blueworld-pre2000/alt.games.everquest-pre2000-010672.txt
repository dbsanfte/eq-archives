"rmturk@netcom.com" wrote:

> I think he means the rest would be far from optimal
> on a sustained basis.

Possibly so, but then I'd have to disagree about the "far from"
part.

>
> Mages miss the ability to regenerate mana and heal
> themselves.  You can't bind wound and meditate
> simultaneously.  Bind wound only works below 50
> percent health.  If the mages nuke little the
> pets take a beating.  Necros can use snaring spells
> and fear.  6 pets won't let a critter running get
> too far and will take little damage.
>

Mages rarely get hit, healing isn't a priority.  Rather,
their pets get hit, and they heal themselves very well,
and never ever seem to need to meditate despite
being journeyman spell casters as well as tanks.

The pet taking a beating is indeed the point; they heal
up quite rapidly, part of the game is to not waste mana
nuking when the pets can soak up damage... just like
in a regular group where the tanks should be allowed
to handle things more than instinct might suggest, if they
have a healer handy to eliminate the recovery time.  Think
of the pets as having a bards healing song running continuously;
they get better, in a hurry, unless they get dead.

>
> Mages die while necros feign.  :)
>

Now I'll grant you that mages are weak on evac, other
than the "let the pets hold them off while we gate" plan,
death does occur when things go truely fubar.  But this
isn't a feature of mages grouped together, particularly,
rather it is a mage flaw in all circumstances...  getting
away from a really bad situation when it gets to every
man for himself is not something mages do well.  In fact,
its easier in an all mage group, where we can see that the
pets are not hacking it, and give the "run" order knowing
that we all have gate ability and are only abandoning
pet rocks, in final analysis.  Running away from normal
groups is more problematic, ethics often demand that we
stay and try to pull things out of the fire until too late for
our one really good escape plan to pay off.

> Both groups would be fun.  Mages would need a much
> safer environment because of down time.

Depending on the situation, down time may not be
nearly as much as you think...  Hunting black to red
gargoyles in the OOT, the three man group I was in
just took turns, two medding while the third pulled,
admittedly we never all went FOM at the same time,
but since it wasn't constant reds, this wasn't a real handicap
often.  Biggest problem we had was when the spectres
got aggroed accidentally, and we'd all bound at the
sister dock...  not all that far a spec flight from the island!
Luckily the boat happened by in time for me to get out,
the other guys had decided to run for some high level
folks they knew instead, but didn't make it...

As a mage, I am happy to have people out hunting
when I'm at 75% or more, and generally don't run
down past 50% unless things are FUBAR.  Since
I'm medding during combat, I'm usually back up to
that 75% plus mark at the end, perfectly ready to
see more incoming.  Pet heal rate is similarly good
enough that dropping to 50% will still likely see him
in fine fighting form by the time the next Mob shows
up.
