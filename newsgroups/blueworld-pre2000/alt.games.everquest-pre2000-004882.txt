Mesmerize, is similar to a stun or a root.  It freezes the mob in place and
holds for a while (longer than stun or root) when the cast is successful.
It will hold until it times out or you cast a damage spell at the target.
So Mes, then tashan until it isnt resisted, then weaken or enfeeble until it
isnt resisted, then damage (suffocating sphere or choke)...that way you get
the target debuffed before he starts running at you.
