I never did either, the first time I encountered that problem. It is most common
with GeForce + AMD Athlon combinations, or so I hear. To check the power rating of
your power supply, first inspect it from the ouside without opening your case. I've
never seen a Gateway up close and personal, but some PS's come with a stamp or
label on the back, whre the fan and sockets are. If not, you will have to open the
computer's case and scan the outer surface of the power suply (with the computer
off, and *never* open the power supply box, as that will void the warranty on the
power supply, if any is left, and most important is *very* dangerous).
