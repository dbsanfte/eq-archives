Indeed Bards are NOT tanks.

This is kind of a side discussion on bards but I thought someone
might find it interesting.

A good bard knows how to avoid tanking.  We can't FD but we have
lots of other skills.

If something is whacking on me I can charm it.
Or my pet can taunt it off me (I can keep 2-3 mobs busy for a long
time without taking too much damage given enough room for me to work
my mojo).  Or I can chain and it simple hate kite it (let the rogue
run behind it BS'ing it while I keep it slowed and just out of range).
Or I can charm one and hate kite the other with my pet.  Or I can
debuff it's attack speed so even though it's still hitting me I'm
taking a lot less damage.

Or I can keep it stunned until we can deal with it.  I can also charm
mobs that are aggro'ed on the healer/etc.  While not a tank I can still
tank a lot better than an enchanter -- it's very dangerous
for enchanters to charm but because I can charm over and over and tank
a little bit in between (my songs are almost never interrupted) I do
it quiet often (pretty much every fight that's more than a single
unless we have an enchanter, then I just stand around and look pretty).
But when grouped with a bard the enchanter can be the primary charmer
and the bard can take over when the enchanter's charm breaks.
And outdoors (and in select dungeon locations) I can fear kite
with charmed pet just like a necro :) [ok ok maybe not JUST like
a necro]

I even trained one enchanter to time my charm (12 seconds) and then
recharm right as my charm would break.  Was an awesome combo.

Another part is that mearly attacking a mob increases your aggro.
Good bards know that their melee output is pathetic at best compared
with the damage output from a good group (in 10 seconds I can do about
150-200 damage, while the rest of a good group can do 3000-5000 in that
time).  Fast hitting/low damage isn't a good thing -- ask a Ranger.
I'm much more effective with my songs when using instruments
so I do *NOT* find it necessary to be melee'ing with my weapons unless
I'm bored and there is no danger.  Less melee = much less taunt = happy
bard = happy group.

And finally -- position matters for taunt.  There is a certain distance
away from a mob you can stand and it will aggro on someone else unless
your WAY up on the hate list.  A good bard knows to stand back a little
bit when he's aggro'ed.

So, you have a bard in your group and they are melee'ing and taking
lots of damage, what can you do?  Sort of depends on the level
of the bard but ask them to just try using their lute and twisting:
Largo's and Hymn and whatever else they want (probably either magic
resist or a battle buff depending on the situation).  If they have
Niv's it's a good choice too (string based with healing component).

Largo's is AE so use with appropriate caution (Largo's does NOT break
mez so you can safely use it in combonation with an enchanter).
Substitute Chains in tight situations.  Largo's = Heaven if you
don't have an enchanter or shaman around to debuff attack speed.
It also lowers AC a bit which makes the melee'ers more effective.
Final Caution: resists suck -- and buffs are never resisted -- switch
tactics if something isn't working on that mob.

Anyway -- Not exactly on topic but hopefully you will find it useful.
