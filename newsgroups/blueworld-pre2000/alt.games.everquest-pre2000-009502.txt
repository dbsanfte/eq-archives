Quite true. Customers in the computer industry put up with things that no
other customers would put up with. I never said that couldn't be changed;
personally (and professionally) I'd like to see the shift. However, that is
THE CURRENT REALITY OF THE SITUATION. If you buy a leading edge product, you
should expect bleeding edge performance. Don't like it? Don't buy bleeding
edge products. If enough people do the same, you'll see more robust products
at ship. You'll see them later than you would have, but you'll still see
them. And they'll work.

Unfortunately, the current trend is to ship earlier and earlier... and patch
like hell. Wanna know a secret? The current crop of computer hobbyists just
eat it up. "Hey man, did you hear that UT v12.3.4.5.10a is out?" "No way
man! Did they fix the tri-lateral buffer overflow from 12.3.4.5.09d?"

I think this will all change as PCs and dedicated devices converge. Just
Plain Folk won't deal with patches -- if it doesn't work first time, it'll
go back to the store. Nothing like big old AFR numbers to force a change.

Me? I'll miss my PC, but only because I dig the bleeding edge (and I
secretly like patches too ;-)
