As I understand it there are four elements to your faction:

1) Race based, Illusion changes this.
2) Religion based, most NPCs ignore this. For those that do care this seems
to override the other elements.
3) Earned
4) Magic (eg Benevolence)

Add them all up and you get you reaction. This is how it seems to work for
me (Dark Elf, Enchanter, Worships Innoruk, good factions in "local" form in
most cities).

Your "earned" faction is personal to you and is certainly NOT separately
recorded for each of your illusions.
