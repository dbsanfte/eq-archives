> The thought of making an orc bashing me with a mace seem more realistic than

> Wouldn't you have to blur the image for the 3-D effect to work though?

You don't need to blur, but you do need anti-aliasing to keep the image 
from looking too crawly.  

All you need for stereo viewing is to render the world twice, once for 
each eye, and to send the two views to your stereo display.  The purpose 
of stereo hardware, like glasses, is to send the proper image to each eye.

EverQuest would probably look pretty bad with 3D glasses because they
get so much from textures.   It is not immediately and painfully obvious 
that the world is built from painted blocks most of the time.  A stereo
view would bring that fact right out.
