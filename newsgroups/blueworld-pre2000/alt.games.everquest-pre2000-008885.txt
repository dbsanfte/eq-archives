Someone in this thread asked to have "what's wrong with the Prima EQ Kunark 
Guide" detailed.  I'm not entirely sure this is the same book, but here you 
go:

http://boards.station.sony.com/everquest/Forum4/HTML/037376.html

This is advice from the "official" strategy guide for EQ: 

Bards - "Don't be a packrat. One kit (or bag even) full of stuff is plenty."
So bards are supposed to get less loot than everybody else? 

Mend - "It can't be used more than once a day or so at lower levels."
Really? Man, my monk must be really special - she can mend every 6 minutes
of real-time. 

"The only real drawback to Mend is that using it unsuccessfully causes
damage instead of healing it." When unsuccessful, there's a chance that it
will cause damage, more often than not it doesn't. 

Monks - "Don't even try to learn a weapon." Ummmm...Verant has basically
forced Monks to start working 1hb and 2hb, with all the awesome weapons
coming out of Kunark. 

"A monk can easily outpace his enemies." Last I heard, Monks run no faster
than all other classes, which means at the same speed as most mobs. 

Monks - "Regular attacks (hand attacks) don't become magical in and of
themselves no matter how high the Monk's level..." Every 30+ Monk out there
can refute that one. 

"Iksar Monks immediately start off with Dual Wield ability, so once you can
come up with a second weapon you're ready to rock." And Human Monks don't
start with Dual Wield? And what's this about a 2nd weapon? Didn't it just
say (about 4 paragraphs ago) not to try to learn a weapon? 

Monks - "And never forget the ever-present tail attack." Tail Rake is a
skill learned at lvl 25 in place of Dragon Punch. 

Monks and Strength - "Furthermore, it affects how much you can carry, which
is of major concern for a Monk." The 14 lb limit is NOT affected by
Strength. 

"Older Erudite Necromancers will often give you a teleport of off Odus, if
you can find any." Good luck finding a Necro that'll teleport you (and by
the way, that typo "of off" is in the book, not my typo). 

Rangers - "Go for max damage. Given the choice between 2-Handed Slash and
1-Handed Slash, remember that 2-Handed does more damage. 1-Handed is great
with a shield, but then, shields are heavy, and there's the encumbrance
issue ... and Rangers don't get Bash, anyway. So, unless, there's a good
reason not to, go for the 1-Handed Slash." Am I wrong, or was that whole
paragraph saying that 2hs is better? And then it says to go with 1hs? Huh? 

Shadowknight - "Vs. red NPCs. Best to party with strong characters and
Clerics." Shouldn't that be in a general advice section? Or is it only for
Shadowknights? 

Wizard - "INT suggestion. Wizards should put all but 5 of their starting
points into Intelligence..." "...The other five points? The Wizard should
keep putting as much as she can into Intelligence, even raising it to 200,
before worrying about just increasing mana. Increasing the attribute
increases more than just mana." Huh? What about the other 5 points? 

(This is probably my favorite. It speaks for itself.) "Only the Wizard can
take people to the Planes of Sky and Hate, but she shouldn't." 

Dwarf - "Warrior. Because of their size, Dwarves are harder to hit in
combat." Size means nothing. 

"As a Dwarven Warrior, you can wear a full suit of bronze armor - no other
race can do so." Oh really? 

"Short legs. For obvious reasons, Dwarves have a hard time running away from
their opponents." Size means nothing. 

Gnome - "Like a viper. Gnomes can strike fast in combat and move out of the
way even faster." Uh, no. 

"Half Elves can Track." Sure, if they're Rangers, Druids, or Bards. 

Half Elf - "The only other race that can yield Bards is Humans" Wood Elves
are REALLY good at imitating Bards then. 

Halfling - "One drawback Halflings have is that they earn experience more
slowly than most other races" And I could have sworn they gained exp the
FASTEST. 

Iksar - "they have Dual Wield from the outset." Umm, if you're a Monk, sure.
Otherwise, nope. 

"Iksar have regenerative powers. Like Ogres, they're hard to keep down."
Shouldn't that be "Like Trolls"? 

Iksar - "They've got an extra attack (the tail)" Iksar Monks get Tail Rake
at 25 in place of Dragon Punch. No other classes get a tail attack. 

Wood Elf - "Practice your 1H and 2H skills in equal increments..." I'll make
sure to tell all the Wood Elven Druids and Bards this. 

"Necromancers are the kings of kiting." A little later on..."Druids are the
masters of kiting." Consistency please. 

Wizard - "Level 20-24 are hell levels" Really? I'm sure that they mean those
levels suck, but using the term hell level is really stupid. 

Shaman "Keep...everyone moving quickly with Spirit of Wolf (haste) spells."
Again, I know what they mean, but calling it a haste spell is really stupid.
Use the terminology that the real players do. Haste spells are ones that
increase your attack speed/decrease the delay on your weapons.
