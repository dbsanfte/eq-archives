>As for creating large scale ongoing player-driven plot lines,

All they need is a general, vague outline of a story.  The corrupt
guards in Qeynos have be plotting revolt for 11 months now, probably
about 1 EQ decade.  Come on, let take that _somewhere._

>If Verant started the same story on every server, it would take a
>different twist and they would end up having to write a unique
>story line for each server.  There are hundreds of NPCs out there
>who would have to change their conversations based on the ongoing 
>plot.  Your $10 a month just does not buy that kind of service.

My $10 a month _will_ buy that someday, when there's enough different
games out there that the companies actually have to compete for
customers.  You don't need to have different stories on every server -
its easy enough to force the story to the direction that you need it
to go.  Say the corrupt guards are revolting and you want the good
guards to flee to Erudin.  Wow, I never knew there were that many
Bloodsabers hidden down there in the sewers.  Players basically have
the choice of fleeing or dying - Voila!  The remaining good guards
have been forced to flee to Erudin.  Move 50 or so NPCs, give another
100 or so new things to say and you're done.  Maybe 2 days work for 1
person unless Verant has the worst development tools imaginable.
That's the story for the month of March.  Guess the story coordinator
can take the next 3 weeks off.

>Verant has chosen rich detail over flexibility in most situations.
