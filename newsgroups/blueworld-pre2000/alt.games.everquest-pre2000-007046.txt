I think you are missing the point about Play Nice and why it has been
implemented.  Its not a solution to over-crowding (there really isn't much),
it's a solution to *camping*.  Verant has a problem - people camp spawns. 
They've tried Lore Items, upping difficulties, introducing Placeholders, and
eventually they simply nerfed spawns so drops that happened every 3 to 4
hours (FBSS) became every 3 to 4 days!

AND STILL THEY CAMPED!

I would contend that, as FooBar suggest this will end camping.

Why?  Well, lets examine.  At the moment camping *is* rewarded.  You spend
72 hours at the Frenzied Ghoul and you *will* generally get an FBSS.  Under
Play Nice, this *guarantee* of Time Invested=Reward is gone.  No longer is
staying in a place forever guaranteeing you the item.

They have finally managed to remove the reward for camping.  That is a good
thing.

Now, players, like you, can and probably will institute "agreements" to
avoid this.  But it only takes one person/group to end that and the GMs will
back that person/group 100%.  You and the rest of you campers out there have
lost.  Spending ridiculous amounts of time on a spawn is no longer going to
be rewarded.

And who knows, it might even let Verant turn the drop rate back up...
