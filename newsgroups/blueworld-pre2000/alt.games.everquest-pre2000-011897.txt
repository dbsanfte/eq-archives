Why does everyone have problems understanding the need for insanely
high frame rates?  If you have 40 frames per second, you need
something faster if you play shooters.  Why?  Because frame rate drops
of 70-80% are the norm for intense battle areas.  So, if your frame
rate is 40 FPS, a 70% drop leaves you at 12 FPS.  If I'm on a system
pinging 200 with a worst case frame rate of 35 FPS, and you are on a
system pinging 60 with a worst case frame rate of 12, I'd be willing
to bet I win more duels than you do.  Any hard core gamer is best
server with a frame rate of 100+ on average.  And because no video
card out there yet does true motion blurring (3dfx claims
notwithstanding), you need 48 FPS to have a smooth, consistent
illusion of motion.  Go to voodooextreme and read some of Hook's old
columns about this.

If you aren't doing this for first person shooters, sure you only need
18-20 FPS, but I'm a shooter fan, and I want everything as smooth as I
can get it.
