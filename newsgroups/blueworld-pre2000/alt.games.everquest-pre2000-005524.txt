More musings about the MX400...

   I am frustrated by the lack of control over the sound card whatsoever
outside the multimedia and mixer in Win 98. This card did not come with any
Sensaura control tabs that I can find. If anyone can point me where they
are, if they do exist (which I don't think they do with all the looking I
did), I would appreciate it. One problem that is rather bad in a game like
Unreal Tournament and how the card handles the sound, is that you have to
keep your reciever volume low becuase the variance between low (far away
sound sources) and high volume (near sound sources), is so vast that you
will be unable to keep the volume on your reciever/amp/powered speakers
high. The end result is that everything far is low and hardly audible, and
when let's say, you encounter someone nearby shooting an autocannon, the
sound is loud and above what you would normally want. So there is need for a
whole lot of tweaking, control, and maybe even more fitting software support
from the game to make this all fly right. Yet in Quake 3 this was completely
not the case at all. Obviously this entire issue falls squarely on the
shoulders of Diamond failing to deliver as I have seen other cards have this
option to configure and control. Which leads to the next important
conclusion...

   What is also a problem is how much things vary from game to game overall
outside performace issues. The style, laws of sound, variables in the sound,
and general tone change from game to game with automated chaos. You in
effect, get whatever the card decides to play according to how the game
itself handles sound. With the Aureal system, you have a solid uniformity
and whether this comes from direct support or just how well the system has
been refined (since it's had a huge head start), it is an important thing to
note none the less.

   One of the things I will be looking dirtectly at when I get the Aureal
2500 V2, and use the new eax emulator, is how the games/driver allows you to
opt whether you want to use eax emulation or a3d for each game. Not how it's
suppose to work, but how and if it actually works or not.
