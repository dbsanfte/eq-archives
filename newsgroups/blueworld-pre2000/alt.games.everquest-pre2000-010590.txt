A Ranger will probably be doing more damage with a 2-hander
from level 20 to somewhere in the mid 30s, after which the dual
wield takes over again.  On the other hand, dual wield will
taunt and interrupt better.  Rangers should keep both skill
sets maxed.

Cazic and hunting in the "bowl" might be an option at 22.  The
good news here is that if you have camo and sneak you can
easily sell to merchant Fugla in the huts just north of the
stone bridge.  Lizards are fairly rich, and it is good
experience too.

22 is a little low for the yard trash in mistmoore, but many of
those dark elves have a plat or two on them.  Unrest is also
possible at those levels, but I don't know if you will have the
oomph to carry your weight in groups that are hunting the more
lucrative spots in the house.

The icy orcs in Everfrost also load fine steel fairly
regularly, and now that you have harmony you should be able to
handle these with a partner.  You will want a group of 4 at
your level before you try Redwind, but he does drop a rune for
you ivy bracers.

Level 22 is great for a ranger - time to work on archery.
