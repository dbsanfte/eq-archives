Verant has stated that is the way higher level mobs work.  Pets are
considered NPC's and NPC cons are different in terms of their proportional
power at the higher levels.  For example, the equivalent power of a yellow
con at level 6 would be a blue con at level 30, while an equivalent powered
yellow con at 30 would be blood red at 6.

Conning does not tell you how POWERFUL a mob is, it only tells you what
LEVEL they are, which makes it difficult to rely on other than to tell if
you will get exp or not.  Your new pet is probably level 10 or 11, which
would be the equivalent in power as your level 5 pet was when you were level
4.
