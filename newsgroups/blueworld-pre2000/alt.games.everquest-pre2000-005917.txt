Levels 1-4:

Kelethin newbie area:  absolutely terrible
Felwithe gates:  adequate - lots of bats and lots of people
buying bat wings.

Butcherblock:  great
Steamfront - never used
Ak'Anon mines: never used

levels 5-8:
orc hill: fast, furious, and crazy.  no zoning.
butcherblock:  good

levels 9-14:
crushbone; crazy but fun.
mino caves - a good time but watch out for the hero
go to blackburrow


Good points of Faydwer:

you can bind anywhere in GFay and be close to anywhere on the
island.

good market in Kelethin for other players - sell your bat wings
and buy cheap bronze

dungeons nearby support levels 7-18 and 14-25

bad points of Faydwer:

lots of rude players
lots of junk in /shout and /ooc in GFay and Butcherblock
a fair number of beggars

So I would not say it "blows chunks" - I like it better than
greater freeport myself.  My wife started in Queynos and found
that the quests were great, the local color was great, and the
absense of a player market made her very envious of me.

Also good newbie areas are Everfrost and the new Paineel.
