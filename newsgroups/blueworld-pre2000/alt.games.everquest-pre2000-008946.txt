1, I understand that there is a sweet spot for strength at
about 100.  I will not be raising it much above that but I do
give up a little bit of AC to get there.

2, Let me tell you about encumbered.  I generally carry between
1 and 3 extra bows with me for re-sale.  I am regularly hunting
near the limits of my encumberance, especially if I sell a bow
while in a dungeon.

That said, I am aware that I am giving up about 9 points of AC
over items that I have in the bank or have given to my
secondary character:

spiked collar over banded collar.  I lose 2 points of AC for 3
points of str.  As soon as I can get 3 points of STR elsewhere
I will replace this with a collar of undead protection.

dwarven ringmail tunic over banded tunic.  I decided that for
general purpose wear 12 ac and 24 points of resists beats 15 ac
and lighter weight.  If I hunt regularly in Cazic Thule I might
chase up a banded tunic because so far most of my deaths have
come from melee damage.  I want to upgrade to ivy as soon as I
can.  

Mammoth hide leggings (ac 6, cold resistance) over banded
leggings (ac 8).  I play a female wood elf.  I do not like the
look of the chain/banded pants on a female wood elf.  I hope
that there are some leather-look ranger leggings in Kunark.

Grotesque mask over banded mask.  ac 3, +dex, - cha vs ac 4.
The dex makes up for the ac, especially if I am using a bow or
a procing weapon.  Given that I was fighting melee only
recently and using a BIBS I should probably have swapped these.

I already have opalline ear rings and jagged bands, so the next
set of armor upgrades are going to be Ivy and Crested, and at
level 23 I need to sell some bows before I can buy these.

>Roughly, 3 AGI points are worth about 1 AC, so if you find an item
>with 1 less AC than your objective, and +3 agi, you may pick it.

My rule of thumb for general purpose gear is:
1 point of ac is the same as
2 points of resists or
3 points in a useful stat

And I will only break this rule for a specific purpose, either
if I will be fighting melee heavy, magic heavy, or the like.
