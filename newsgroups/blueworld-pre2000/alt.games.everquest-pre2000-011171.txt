Ok, rephrased for the people that don't read what I say;

Wizards are the most efficient damage dealing caster class when using AE
PROPERLY.  Here is the raw TRUTH, read it and understand;

Wizard - Pillar of Flame (950 damage PER mob, for 364 mana).  Our
standard pull setup is Enchanter pulls as normal (fight as normal until
Wiz hits fom), when Wiz is ready Ench lines up mobs (new pulls) under
mez within AE radius of each other.  Wizard unloads AE's.  57th wiz with
good equipment gets around 7 AE's.  7*4*950 = roughly 27k (call it 25
after resists).  oom to fom is roughly 8 mins with Clarity2.

Necro - To quote you, I am the most efficient caster class damage wise.
 I disagree given the above example.  If I DoTed EVERY single mob we

resists call it 14k).  IF I GET FULL DURATION.  Which I almost never do.
 Argue that Lich gives me a couple more DoTs in the same timeframe sure,
argue that my pet will do a good amount of damage, but at the end of the
day the wizard is still causing more damage, more reliable damage, and
more direct controllable damage.

There is NO question that Wizards rule the caster classes.  This is the
truth.  It is not possible for another caster class (however well
played) to outdamage a  professionally played Wizard using AE's.  Fact.
If you disagree, you disagree because you haven't done it, or you
