The naming policy is a rule for Verant's game. Because they made the rule, that
gives it all the merit it needs. Players are allowed to bring the GMs attention
to what they believe are improper names. The GMs are the ones that "determine"
whether or not the names are in violation.
While there are some subjective grey areas, most name violations fall directly
against specific points in the naming policy.

It's not a matter of imposing subjective preferences on others, the GM is the
one with the final word on the matter. All a player can do is bring names to
their attention.

I find it refreshing to not have to deal with the ridiculous names that people
had in games like UO. Although at the time I was playing that game there were so
many other things that were wrong with it that  the names were way down on the
list. But it was the whole attitude of the company that ran the game, that
anything goes including cheating and profane names that made it an undesirable
place to spend my entertainment time.

Verant has taken a stand against such an environment in their game. The rules
have developed a more mature atmosphere and I believe will keep more adults in
the population because of it. To those that can't deal with the rules, there's
plenty of games out there that will allow you to behave as obnoxiously as you
wish, go find them.
