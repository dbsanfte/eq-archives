    Here's my question on dubious names...
As a general rule, I will not petition anyone's name.  Regardless of what I
think
about it.  I saw a guy named 'Texaslonghorn' the other day.  Being an Oklahoma
Sooner, I gave him a tell saying (joking) how it offended me and we chatted for
a couple minutes then went our own ways, but no petition.
    A couple weeks ago, however, I saw three people fighting together in a
group.
Their names where Enus, Vulgina, and Ulva.  This to me seemed to be in violation

of rule #1 of the naming policy.  Especially considering some of the lewd emotes

from Enus.  One of these names might survive on it's own, but together, the
connotations are to obvious.  I did petition them, but a couple days later still

saw Enus running around.
    Does anyone else agree that they were violating naming policy?

    And my quick opinion on names like 'missalot' (previous thread).  Many
people
say that 'No parent would name a child that, ergo that name is not RP and is a
violation'.  In many games, and many RL tribes/cultures, a person's adult name
is
not the same as that given by their parents.  After acheiving a certain age, or
completing
(or failing) a rite of passage, a new name is chosen or given to the person.
So,
a person should be able to name themself 'missalot', 'slowfoot', or 'smellyfeet'
and
not have someone complain that it's not RP.
