The normal chain of events is this :-

Everyone starts a character, powergamers examine the spell and ability lists
and try and work out which is toughest, others go for who they like the
sound of or fancy roleplaying.

Time Passes.

Powergamers see combination X dominating.  They scrub their initial
character and begin to play the Uber char.

Time Passes.

Regular players see that all the good stuff and all the fun is going to the
players of uber chars, they also see tuning changes that make it tougher and
tougher to progress if you are not an uber char yourself.  Some leave,
others begin again with Uber chars.

So after a few cycles, a single type comes to dominate in a skill based
system.
