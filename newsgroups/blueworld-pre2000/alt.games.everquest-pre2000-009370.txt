> Zonealarm?  Eq related? or something else entirely?

Zonealarm is a personal security program.  It prevents unauthorized
programs from accessing the internet on your computer.  

The only problem I have had with Zonealarm in EQ is when some program
spontaneously decides it needs internet access while I am playing and
Zonealarm pops up a dialog.  EQ loses focus, which crashes it.

The easiest fix to this is to tell Zonealarm to reject all such 
requests without bringing up a dialog.  If you do this, you will get
some bad behavior from other programs.  

The other fix is to uninstall RealPlayer.  This is the only program
I have found that thinks it needs to talk to the net at random times.
I am much happier giving up RealPlayer than Zonealarm. ;)

Also, check for any scheduled processes, like antivirus updates, that
might pop up while you are playing.  The people who set up defaults
for these things are suffering under the delusion that 2am is a good
time to perform such tasks.  There's nothing like having your computer
decide to defrag its disks while you are engaged in a fight for your
life.
