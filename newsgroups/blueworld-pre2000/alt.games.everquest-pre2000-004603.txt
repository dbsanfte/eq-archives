Actually, the success rate for high end platinum items is under 50%.
It is very easy to try something three times and fail three times,
destroying all materials.  I would never sell anything above platinum
black pearl for less than three times cost, because I can't afford to
do it.  Currently I am 0 for 3 on platinum black pearl rings, so I 
haven't had to worry about a price for those. ;)

Platinum bars cost 105pp.  Black Pearls cost about 200pp.  With
an assumed success rate of about 33%, you can expect to spend
915pp making one ring.  It is easy to fail three or four times,
spending 900-1200 pp and not end up with a ring to sell, so 
making these things is not for the faint of heart or the thin
of wallet.

Selling enchanted jewelry back to vendors is not a money making 
endeavor.  You get about 5%, at best, profit above the material
cost.  Since the failure rate is never less than 5%, you can't
actually make money doing this.  At best it slows the drain of
cash from your purse while you practice the trade.  The only way
to earn money with jewelcraft is to sell to other players.
