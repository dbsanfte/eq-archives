I took a little bit of Latin a long, long time ago, but I still have
my little Collins Gem Latin Dictionary. ;)

Puer = Child,    Plural Pueri = Children
Odium = Hatred,  Genitive Odi = of Hate

Children of Hate would be "Pueri Odi".

If Sholeas means hate (which I doubt, since Latin doesn't have "sh")
then Sholeas Puerorum would be "Hate of the children".  Puerorum is
plural genitive.
