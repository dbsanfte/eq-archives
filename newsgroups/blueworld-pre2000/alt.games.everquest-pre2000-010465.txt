Z-buffer error is not a likely culprit for invisible monsters that
are right in front of you.  The z-buffer is what makes it ok for the
computer to draw the terrain before the monsters.  The only time
things vanish due to the z-buffer is when they are far away and very
close to something big.  In that case, the computer might decide that
they are actually at the same distance and not draw the monster.  You
can tell if this is happening by the big jagged edges showing up at
the edges of things and the monster popping in and out of a wall or
tree as you move your head.

The most common reason for monsters appearing and vanishing is that
their motion prediction brought them into your visual range when they
were actually quite far away.  When you finally get an update on the
monster, the thing vanishes.  You get very infrequent updates on
monsters that are not close to you.

I am pretty sure that tracking is local, so you will track the monster
at the same position that your client would draw it.  If you really
have tracked the monster to be directly in front of you, been attacked
by it, and still can't see it, then there is some new bug here.  If
you didn't manage to catch it, then you were probably just running
around tracking wild vectors because you never got close enough to get
frequent position updates.

Ghosts and spectres that are partially transparent will actually
vanish if the transparency is not handled properly.  Transparent faces
should be drawn back to front after the rest of the world is drawn.  I
have noticed the spectres in Trakanon's Teeth partially vanishing when
they pass in front of trees.  However, even when their shapes
disappear entirely, the names still show so I don't think that is the
answer here either.
