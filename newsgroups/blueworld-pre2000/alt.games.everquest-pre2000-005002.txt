Thanks for the clarification about the Tumpy Tonic quest exploit. ;)

I only had one experience with the vile beverage, and it was so
obnoxious that I have avoided them ever since.

[long story follows]

With my first character on my first quest, a Ranger at level 5,
I was trying to get parts for a Trueshot Longbow to please my
guildmaster.  I had figured out where to get Treant Wood and
Spiderling Silk, but Trantor wouldn't talk to me.  Eventually,
I managed to extract from him the fact that he would give me
Dwarven Wire in exchange for a couple of Tumpy Tonics.  Tumpy
said he needed Kiola Nuts from the Ocean of Tears, so I threw
everything in the bank and hopped on the boat.

When I found the island where the Kiola Nuts were sold, I found
that they cost over a gold each, so I sold some of my armor to
the nice lady and bought two nuts.  Then I continued to Freeport,
got the other parts for the bow and headed back to Kaladim.
It took me an age to find water flasks there, but a kind local
directed me to the proper vendor and I was able to get the tonics
from Tumpy.  

I handed the two tonics to Trantor, and he said, "I wanted two
tonics!" and "Ah, thank you, here is your wire."  But he didn't
give me any wire.  I turned all my bags inside out ... no wire.
I petitioned and waited for about fifteen minutes.  Then it
occurred to me that I could probably get more tonics before a
GM would answer, so I got back on the boat, sold more armor,
bought more nuts, got more tonics, handed them to Trantor
carefully one at a time, and got my wire.  About then, a GM
answered and said there was nothing they could do.

Getting the last part of the quest took days of hunting clockworks
in Steamfont before I got the right part.  Actually, someone took
pity on me and gave me one.  

I gave all the parts to my guild master.  He said thanks and gave 
me a couple silver ... not even enough to cover the Kiola Nuts.
I was rather hoping for a bow.  I didn't bother doing another 
quest for quite some time.
