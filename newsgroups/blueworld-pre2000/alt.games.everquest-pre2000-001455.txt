> Is it true that you Americans can leave your machines on-line the whole
> time in the States?

Most places they can, as they pay a larger fixed fee for having a phone
line in the first place, and get local calls for free.  Before modems
became commonplace, this was a way for the phone companies to make MORE
money, as very few people would use the phone all that much, but
everyone liked the idea of "free" local calls.  Nowadays, the phone
companies regret that decision very much, as people tend to hog the
lines for long periods at a time.

Even though you usually can be connected for as long as you like
without paying extra for it, many ISP's cut the line after long periods
of inactivity, or after N hours of being connected.

As for the UK, I think BT too can offer fixed charges, where you can
use the line as much as you like for local calls without paying extra.
Don't expect it to be cheap, though.  A fixed 33k6 line or 64kbps
frame relay might be cheaper.  :-)
