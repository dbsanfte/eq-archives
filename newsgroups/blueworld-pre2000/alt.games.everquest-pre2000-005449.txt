How about this, when you kill someone you can loot their money and their head.
That head can then be turned in to your guildmaster (the person you gave your
starting note to) for a small amount of experience.  In order to avoid a exploit
where you can level a friend by letting them kill you repeatedly, the person
killed looses the same amount of experience gained by turning in the head.
Because the experience loss and gain are balanced, those that kill only for
role-playing can allow the person killed to regain the lost experience simply by
not looting the head (which the player can then turn in to regain their lost
experience); to avoid people just looting heads of corpses they did not kill,
the head never becomes lootable to the general public, when time runs out no one
else can loot the corpse (as opposed to a mob which can be looted by all when
time runs out).  I dont play PvP, but hought I would put this idea out there to
be picked over.
