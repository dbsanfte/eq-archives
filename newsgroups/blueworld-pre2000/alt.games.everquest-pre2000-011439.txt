Play a dwarven cleric.  You have good starting stats and all spell types
available to you at low levels.  You don't get to learn about pets, but you
have
tried a necro so you probably have some idea how pets work.

The dwarf town is easy to find your way around in, and Butcherblock is a
great starter zone with newbie areas in front and mobs growing steadily
harder as you move away from town.  The roads are easy to follow and lots
of high levels hunt dwarf NPCs in the zone so if you have a question about
how something works, you can ask in OOC.

Play to level 9 to get a feel then read class guides to see what sounds good
to you.  Don't read guides first as they frequently don't offer much insight
if
you don't have a feel for the basic mechanics.  Don't group until you are at
least 4th level - and probably not until level 5.  You can easily solo to
4/5 when
you get your first spells, and the level 5 spells are very good.
