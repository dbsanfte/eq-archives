At first, I tought that Alasdair meant the data transfer rate between
your card and your main memory, which is used to partially to transfer the
polygon (vertex, to be exact), and partially to transfer textures.

I do agree with you. I have been able to compare, with the same system,
using a modem, cable modem and 256K leased line. And the game plays about
the same on each. Thank god, EQ does not stream individual polygon
information from the servers, and tree swaying is purely an individual
and local animation.

What trashes the game is the overuse in Kunark of transparent multitextures.
Which pushes your fillrate (how many individual pixels are set per second)
way up, since many pixels are calculated, found transparent, and then remain
unset for the next calculations.

And yes, the trees are the main culprits. Not because they sway, but because
they are more complex now.
