Zack wrote:
> 
> Relative newbie to the game. I was wondering if the autosplite feature
> divides the coin in an even manner among all party members, or if it
> determines if the actual looter gets a set amount first; then divides the
> rest equally among the remaining party members.
> 
> The reason I ask is that when I was grouping and I looted a corpse, the
> majority of the time I would get between 3-5 silver. When I was receiving my
> split while someone else loots, the majority of the time I would get between
> 2-4 copper and only occasionally 1-3 silver. So this doesn't seem at all
> even. Was just wondering if documentation exists?
> 
> Zack

No, it is not even.  Each coin type is split between the 
group, with any remainder going to the looter.

Example:  Group of three, Monster has 2gp, 5sp, 6cp
The looter would get 2gp, 3sp, 2cp and the others would 
get 1sp, 2cp.

The easiest way to make the money come out fairly even is
to either rotate loots or have one person loot all the
coins and split them up at the end.  Rotation is easier 
in pickup groups.
