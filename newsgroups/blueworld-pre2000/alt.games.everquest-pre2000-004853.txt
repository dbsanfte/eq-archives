Just a little story about what a good reputation can do for you:

I was camping the AllyMcBealasaur in OOT with a few people I'd never met
before in my life.

After one fight, I'm at 100% health 0% mana.  A couple of the folks start
passing around the only manastone in the group like a bong.

I did an emote:
/e druels on Wiztar's manastone.

I considered asking if I could have a hit, but thought, I wouldn't want
someone I'd never met before asking me to take that kind of risk with a now
impossible to get item.


Then I see the trade window pop up and there's the manastone.  Before I
clicked trade I /tell'd "Wow.  That's a lot of trust you're displaying
there."

His reply:
"Well, you have a good reputation, and so does your guild."



'Twas quite nice to be able to at least use this item that was removed from
the game right as I got to a level to get one.  But even more, it was nice
to think that word of my good deeds had earned that kind of trust.


And yes, I did give it back.
