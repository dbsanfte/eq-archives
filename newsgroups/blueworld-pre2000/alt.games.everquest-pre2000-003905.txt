When I hit level 8, I had to go to High Keep for my new pet spell.  So I
put all my gear in the bank, ran to Butcherblock, got on the boat, and 
carefully made my way to High Keep.  I asked someone what route to take
and got directions in each zone about how to find the next.  I was 
invisible the whole time but kept a wary eye out for griffins and other 
nasty things that might see through my invisibility.  I reached High Keep
safely, bought the spell, memorized it, got my gear, and then wandered 
the area.  I figured I should check out the city before gating home.  I
was promptly killed by a prisoner (don't they lock those cells?) and got
to repeat the trip. ;)
