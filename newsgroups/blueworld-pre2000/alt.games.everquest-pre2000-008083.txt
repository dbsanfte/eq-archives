No offense, but these quotes have nothing to do with whether a shrink
wrap license is binding (a discreet legal question that raises many
special issues not addressed in your citations).

The citations simply discuss whether Microsoft's practice of not
contractually allowing OEMs to unbundle Explorer was permitted under
the guise of "protecting its Windows copyright" or constituted an
anti-competitive act under the antitrust laws.  The issues were
statutory, not contractual.

The conclusion that properly formed contracts in general are binding
unless they violate some law and not valid (in whole or in part
depending on severability of the offending provision) to the extent
that they do violate some law is black letter law not much open to
debate.
