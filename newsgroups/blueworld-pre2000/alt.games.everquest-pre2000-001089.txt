Yes, there were no non-dispairagement, non-disclosure sections in the
settlement. They amount of the settlement donated to charity was
$15,000. Both sides paid for thier own court costs. We waived claims
to damages and further pursuit through governmental and business
oriented agencies (like the BBB) as well as other minor issues. The
most the plaintiff's alone might recieve (unless you factor in
punitive damages which is something that is always very hard to
actually be granted) without class action would have been around $200
a head give or take here and there. Maybe some minor expenses as well.
All in all I think it was a broad stroke of luck on behalf of EA/OSI
to have the appeals court rule this way on another case. None the
less, EQ is enough to make anyone forget UO imho.
