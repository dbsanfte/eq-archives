I generally nuke 2 or less times a giant.  However, in less ideal groups
and overpull situations nuking fast is quite necessary.  For example,
when there is no group healer and the group is small.  Or when the 'tanks'
are a paladin and bard.  Or when something is red to the fighters and
blue to me.  They hit less often and for less damage and take a beating
while I hit for full damage.  Guess who taunts more?

It is not uncommon to be fighting two or three things at once.  Throw in
an enchanter and bard charming foes and charms/beguile breaking and often
you need to retarget the pet as events dictate.  You better believe I am
going to burn taunt the enchanter free so he can recast beguile or chase
the moon if the warriors are busy.  

Normally, I am not within command range of my pet unless I am using my
bolt series.  Those I do point blank to avoid blocked shots.  Those are
mostly used when magic is resisted heavily.  By the time I decide this
is needed the warriors have built up enough hate that my 156dmg hits
bring no reaction.

What this boils down to is that when I am needed to overburn or be in
command range it is not a good time.  :)  Getting hit is expected.
Getting hit is routine under these circumstances.  However, assuming
that simply hitting assist then /pet attack, not casting and moving back
means I'll be safe from hits is farfetched at best.
