True, I should have been more precise.  Both "/who" and "/who all" 
match anons.  (I did say that anonymous people will not be matched
by class, race, level or guild name queries.)

I use those "/who all count" and "/who all 1 60 count" to figure out 
how many people in the world are anonymous.  On E'Ci, around 25% of 
the population is anonymous most of the time.


One thing to watch out for when you are searching is that the search
will match prefixes for any of the words it knows, except for "all"
and "count".  This includes guild names.

"sha" matches shadowknight, not shaman, because shadowknight comes
before shaman alphabetically.  If you are looking for people in a
guild named "Friends of the Forest" and there is another guild named 
"Friends of Innoruk", you will have to type in:
	/who all "Friends of the"
to find them.  If you just typed "/who all Fri", you would only find 
the people in "Friends of Innoruk", even if none of them are playing 
at the moment.  Similarly, if you were looking for your friend, Friday
in the zone with you, "/who Fri" would return nothing because it would
match "Friends of Innoruk" and would punt because you can't search for
guild members in a single zone.  It's a very quirky command.
