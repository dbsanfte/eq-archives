On Tue, 7 Mar 2000 11:10:35 -0800, "Gordon Wrinn" <gwrinn@verant.com> wrote:

|The ease or difficulty of implementation here is really moot.  How often
|must we take steps to save people from their own bad choices?

	It is not a question of bad choices.  A player can bind himself someplace
that he does not know is not safe until too late.  During the Kithicor debacle
we were solemnly told that there are NO safe places anyway.

	That you can be attacked before your character is completely in the world
and under your control is a BUG.  It's wrong.  The last thing that should
happen when putting a PC into the environment is clearing it for manipulation
by other characters.  The next to last thing that should happen is getting
acknowledgement from the client that the player is in control and clear to
move.  You guys did it the other way around.  Likewise, when a player is
zoning out he loses control of his PC before other characters lose the ability
to affect it, and that's wrong too.

	I don't see why a confirmation box should be necessary, but if it's the
only way to fix it, then we need one.
