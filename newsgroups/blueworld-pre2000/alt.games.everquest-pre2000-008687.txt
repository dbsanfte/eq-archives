> 1. Selecting targets:

> I'm not sure what on earth is happening when it comes to this. Clicking
> on a mob to target/consider it, I often end up selecting some corpse
> that's in the way, or end up inspecting a group member's equipment. It's
> very frustrating sometimes. In melee, I find judicious use of Target
> Nearest NPC a quick and easy way of selecting a straggler, or some mob
> that's taken to me, but there must be an easier way. This rings
> especially true as my Enchanter, desperately trying to target mobs from
> a distance.

The targeting system in this game is abysmal.  My guess is they somehow project
(reasonably sloppy, it seems) bounding boxes from the monsters to your screen
and then check for bounds in screen space.  This would explain, among other
things, why you'll pick a giant spider corpse in the foreground by trying to
target through it's legs, and why it's so damn hard to pick the farther away 
of multiple critters in a mob.  (I.E. trying to pick the snow orc shaman behind
the shoulder of the snow orc trooper).  I'd love a "cycle through nearest NPC
commands" in addition to the select nearest NPC key.  Of course, this wouldn't 
be AS bad if...

> 2. Use of /assist:

> How exactly does this work? I've set it up to a hot key and learned to
> use it after a fashion to concentrate on taking down mobs from a group,
> but often the results go awry. I'll select the 'puller' or someone else
> who looks like they need an assist, and hit my hot key. Oftentimes,
> nothing will happen or it'll target some other group member, or some
> bizarre thing off in the distance. Does it work by auto-attacking your
> target's current target? Am I using it right?

This has been discussed elsewhere on here recently.  The consensus was that
/assist was broken.  After playing today, I'd agree.  /assist now seems to
choose a target based on some esoteric criteria  (It's not last critter to
aggro on a char, as was previously discussed, unless aggro is overridden after
the fact by a critter being too low to give chase.  I kept targeting random
giant spiders in everfrost all day when assisting our tank who was currently
_fighting_ orcs).  I've had problems with /assist for at least a couple of 
weeks now.  I learned about the command, decided it was a great design 
foresight (e.g. it worked long enough for me to think "They did a good job of
handling this type of situation") then it broke.

> I'm sure you all have your own style which reflects your personality,

I can't add much here, except to say that since it's real people on the other
end, you'll find some who work better or worse with you.  I've slowly begun to
get an idea as to who I do/don't like to work with (Ex:  Let me KNOW you've 
wandered off to pull.  Don't wander out to pull if someone else is currently
not there without seeing if they're pulling first, at the very least.  Don't 
say "I need to sell." then immediately gate without seeing if everyone's okay
with that first.)
