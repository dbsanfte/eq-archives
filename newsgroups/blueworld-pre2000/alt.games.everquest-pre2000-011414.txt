Don't play a Barbarian/Troll/Orge yet.  Stick with Wood Elf, High Elf, Half
Elf, or Human.  Freeport, Qeynos and Kelethin are good places for a warrior
to work in and the newbie zones don't kill to easily with lots of anti-agro
critters.  For instance...  Freeport, the only major agro critters are
Deathfist Pawns, the occasional wolf, and beetles and skeletons if and only
if you hit a beetle/skeleton near another one.  For Qeynos, it is some Gnoll
pups, Flippy the Gnoll, Loathsome individuals, and the klincks/skeletons,
again for the same reason above.  For Kelethin, Orc Pawns, black wolves on
occasion, then Skels/Widow Hatchlings/Wasps if you hit one of them while
another one of them is nearby.  For the Freeport zone, Bats and Snakes are
pretty good, snakes are poisonless and their stackable skins are good for
loading up for money.  Then you can work on large rats for other things.
Get the bats at night, since their wings sell at around 1sp (give or take a
copper depending on your cha) and the Bat Fur is worth a bit more.  Beetles
will give you the later experience along with the Orc Pawns.  A big plus for
you if you are a human, is Beetles will give you a fire beetle eye you can
use as a light source which is much better than the piddly candle or having
to pay for a good lantern or torch to see better.  For Qeynos, snakes again,
hunt the bats at night, then work up on skels and gnoll pups, then when you
are able to con loathsomes, take one that is blue if you can or work with a
small group to camp them.  Kelethin is one place I have worked fairly fast
in XP, and it has bats up the wazoo.  Given Kelethin is in GFay, and all of
Kelethin is in Gfay, you will find a person who might be willing to make you
patchwork armor if you bring them a ruined wolf's pelt and a little money,
this alone will help later on to quickly level you up since it does reduce
the amount of damage and number of hits compared to not having it at all.
