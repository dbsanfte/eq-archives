Actually i would put wizard at the top of a single class group and there is
a very good reason for this.  It seems to be little know but i shall
explain.  A wizzie duo needs no tank whatsoever all they have to do is stand
on opposite sides of a mob and keep away from it.  They then ping pong the
nukes.  Each time one of the wizzies nukes it aggroes the mob, then the next
nukes and aggroes again.  Therefore the mob is just running backwards and
forward never actually managing to get close enough to hit one if the
wizzies.  This obviously is not as effective in a larger group but in a duo
it works a treat me and my partner have taken down reds (at the lower
levels) in a matter of seconds with this method.  it takes a bit of practice
but the gains are worth it.
