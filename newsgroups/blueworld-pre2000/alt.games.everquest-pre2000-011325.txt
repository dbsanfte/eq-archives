Just thought I'd list the comic strips I know of so far that feature EQ 
themes, to see if anybody else has found some good ones as well:

Ding!
<http://eq.castersrealm.com/ding/>
A weekly single-panel strip.  This is by the author of:

<http://www.pvponline.com/>
A daily four-panel strip which is most excellent.  Only does actual EQ 
themes once in a long while, but covers all manner of pc gaming subjects 
and is thoroughly enjoyable.  I would recommend reading through the 
several years of archives - there are some VERY amusing storylines 
throughout.

Just today, thanks to pvponline, I've found two more:

<http://www.gucomics.com/>
A daily one-panel strip devoted to EQ.

<http://www.evercrest.com/>
A daily four-panel strip devoted to EQ.

All four of the above are excellent reading.  pvponline is so good it's 
my web browser's home page now.

There is one other EQ humor page I've found, but it not only lacks the 
quality or wit of the other pages, it includes despicable and extremely 
out-dated racist humor.  What isn't racist is just dumb.  I'm sorry I 
ever found it.
<http://www.everfeast.com/>
