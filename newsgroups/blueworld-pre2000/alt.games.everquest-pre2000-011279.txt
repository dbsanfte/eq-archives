Wizards apply large amounts of damage _in a short time_. In any
properly balanced group fighting opponents of appropriate power, the
monks and warriors and DOT-casters will deal more damage total. But it
could take a warrior 2-3 minutes to inflict the same damage that a
wizard can inflict in 3 casts.

The wizard is there to flatten runners, ensnare groups of monsters, do
some stunning, and above all melt dangerous casters into a steaming
puddle before they kill a party member. Evacs are just a convenience,
IMO.
