ZoneAlarm comes up in Locked mode.  You'll know when that happens because
nothing can connect to the internet.  Just hit the big red button to unlock
it, or right click the tray icon and click on "Engage Internet Lock".  You
will also have to add the two EQ programs to the list of authorized programs
every time they are patched.

It's a great tool.  Anyone who has a permanent net connection, like cable or
DSL, should run it or something like it.
