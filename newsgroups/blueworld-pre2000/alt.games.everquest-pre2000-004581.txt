In AC, there are so many dungeons, there are quite a few that I only go 
through once or twice. And generally, a dungeon is aimed at a certain 
level range, not low level stuff at the top, high level stuff at the 
bottom. That way, a player in the right level range can explore the whole 
place, thier first time there. And also, know there are many more new 
dungeons ahead.

It would be nice if quests worked the way they did in Daggerfall, in AC. 
In Daggerfall, I often went back to the same dungeon, but with a 
different goal each time. Its not perfect (quests have to be very canned) 
but it added short and long term direction to the game that both EQ and 
AC really lack.

Like, I was killing creatures that were stopping me from saving the 
kidnapped girl, or killing the vampire that was terrorizing the nearby 
town, not just killing for no apperant reason, but to gain the almighty 
XP. And when I accomplished the task, I would gain faction with whoever I 
was doing the quest for, and maybe the local populace, government, etc.

EQ already has the faction system, but doesnt have the huge number of 
dungeons to make it work. AC has lots of dungeons, but no faction 
system... oh well, maybe UO 2 will get it right LOL...
