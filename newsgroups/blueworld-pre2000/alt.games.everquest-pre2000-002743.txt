These numbers are Deja approximations. 580,000/30 would equal 19300
posts per day, which you can see is unreasonable. You must either tab
through all the results to get an exact number or set the offset in the
URL to a high number to force the exact number out of Deja.
      offsets=db99p5x%02100  to offsets=db99p5x%0223800

That tells me "Messages 23801-23852 of exactly 23852 matches for search
*"  23852/32  is your average from July 1 to Aug 1 or about 790 posts
per day.
    
 
You don't need a separate group for every sub topic. It will split into
confusion. Take a couple of well-defined topics and leave the rest in
the general group. Forsale or marketplace is fairly easy to separate if
there are a lot of those posts. Some topics might be combined. Wouldn't
'hints' cover where to find rare objects and spoilers?  However this is
an area to work out with the group who understands the game, not
alt.config.
