Greetings Abashi!
I am the owner of Fulcrum's Tinkering Compendium, a site that seems to
recently have become an official voice for all Tinkers of Norrath.
Many Gnomes have written in asking a series of questions that we don't
have answers for yet. We are hoping that you may be able to track down
the correct source of Tinkering information and pose these questions
to them:

1. Thermal Cloak
The building of a Thermal Cloak has puzzled Gnomes since the
beginning. Gimblestan's recipe for the cloak was not as clear as his
others. The Gnome community would like to know if the Tattered Gnomish
Cloak does indeed exist in Norrath, and if it is called just that. It
would also be good to know if it can be purchased, obtained from a
quest, constructed by a tailor, or loot from a kill.

In addition, could you confirm that it is indeed possible to build a
functional Thermal Cloak? As you may view from the Thermal Cloak
section of my compendium, Gnomes have tried a number of combinations
without any luck.

2. Diamond Dust
With the recent discovery of Diamond Dust in Norrath, Gnomes have
begun to create Gemcutters (see the following section). Question: Why
is Diamond Dust a Lore Item? This prevents a Tinker from carry more
than one at a time, and greatly hinders a Tinker's ability to create
Gemcutters.

3. Gemcutter
Now that a handful of Gemcutters have been created, they have been
promptly lost in attempts to make use of them. Could you please
confirm that Gemcutters are indeed functional as intended?
Unfortunately, their use is a little vague (Gimblestan was not as
clear as he could have been).

4. Gnomish Fireworks
With the recent update to Gnomish Fireworks, they are a hit! Would it
be possible for the firework launcher to disappear after it's use? It
is a pain to delete a launcher after it has used up it's single
charge.

5. Tinkering Components
Would it be possible to make Tinkering components stack? As there is
only one location in the world where most components may be found, it
is quite cumbersome to have an entire bag filled with a single metal
rod in each slot, etc. As other trades have stackable items (being a
Jeweler too, I know that gems and metal bars may be stacked), could
you please investigate the possibility of Tinkering following suit?
This change would make many Gnomes happy.

Thanks for all your help.
