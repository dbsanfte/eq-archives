I am in no way, shape, or form a hard core roleplayer.  I think the
/roleplay switch is stupid.  I have no problem with the idea of taking
about a weapons stats, how many hit points I have, the damage a spell
does, or the fact that I'm experiencing bad lag.  I think people who
say things like "I am a ranger in my 37th season" are idiots.

That said, I _do_ have minimum standards for roleplaying.  Good races
should _not_ be killing dwarven guards.  Paladins should _not_ be
murdering other paladins in cold blood for kewl loot.  Druids and
rangers should _not_ be slaughtering Aviaks.  No one should be
chatting about last night's football game in /shout.

I consider these the minimum things to do to even give the appearance
of roleplayinging, yet they're constantly being violated and most of
the people who violate them don't even give them a thought.  95% of
the people playing EQ have no clue what a roleplaying game is.  As I
said, the limit of their definition of a RPG is any game with stats,
HPs, and elves.  These are the same people who show up in the CRPG
group and try to defend Diablo as a roleplaying game.  Its a fun
games, yes - A fun _action_ game, not a RPG.  Stats do not an RPG
make.
