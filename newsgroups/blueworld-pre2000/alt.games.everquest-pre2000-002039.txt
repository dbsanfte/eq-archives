A virtual world and not a game?!  I think not.  It is a GAME which is
supposed to be fun.  This is not Sim-Britannia.  It has just as many
made-up and arbitrary rules as any other computer game out there, and is
equally lacking in realism.  All computer games must sacrifice realism
for fun, becuase it most case, reality is not fun. Challenge is good,
occasional setback are fine, but when any computer game is no longer
fun, I drop it and move on to one that is fun.  Especially if it
involves paying a monthly fee.  Deal with hardship in their
entertainment?  Entertainment is not supposed to involve hardship beyond
the cost & time associated with it; if it did it wouldn't be
entertainment.

> EQ's interface is horrid.  I don't even like talking to my friends
> on it.  Hell, I still continue to use ICQ while playing EQ, since all
> the friends I play EQ with but 2 can also use ICQ while playing EQ.

EQ interface is different.  Personal opinion as to whether or not you
like it.

> One time every other night is *not* reason enough to run away from
> *everyone*,
> IMO.  In two typical night play sessions I could encounter upwards of
> a few
> hundred people in a few scores of encounters.  For *one* of those to
> turn sour
> is *no reason* to bugger off and hide.

Why not?  If those one time every other night encounter ruin your
evening's entertainment is it really all hard to justify trying to avoid
it? Every other night would mean half of the evenings you play.  This is
not a victim mentality so much as it is common sense.  If, in your
opinion, the potential COST (be it emotional [anger, irritation or
disgust], virtual [your chars stuff], or even just time lost [need to be
rezzed]) of being killed by another player outweighs the potential
BENEFIT of interacting with other, non-PK strangers (most of which would
probably be nuetral in terms of overall effect on your night
entertainment), then you don't do it.
