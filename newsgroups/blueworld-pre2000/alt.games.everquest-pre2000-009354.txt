The trip is not bad from Freeport, just tedious.  Get on the boat
in East Freeport and get off at the docks in Butcherblock.  Turn
right and go to the far dock.  Get on one of the barges there.  The
barge will take you to a large ship which will take you to Firiona
Vie.  The shop that sells Breeze is all the way at the south (safe)
end of the elven settlement in Firiona Vie, so you do not have to 
risk anything to get there.

Be a little cautious around the Butcherblock docks.  There are 
occasionally aqua goblins there.
