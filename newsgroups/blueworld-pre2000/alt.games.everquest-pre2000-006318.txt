This is the whole correlation-causation confusion.

(1) New servers are opening up.
(2) The populations on all EverQuest servers are growing.

The fact that these things are both happening does not mean that
(1) implies (2).  If the new servers did not open up, do you think
that the population of existing servers would not have increased?
My guess is that they would have increased even more than they did
and that if more new servers opened up, the population on existing
servers might actually decrease.  

Right now, Verant is not opening enough new servers to make it 
worthwhile to switch.  If there were actually enough new servers 
opened that some of the new servers would remain at a low 
population, then people would switch from older more crowded 
servers.  As it stands now, the numbers don't encourage switching 
because the new servers fill up to normal levels within a couple of 
weeks.  Hence, Verant needs to open up more servers to resolve the
overcrowding issue.
