Comparing with your results, 3 skill advances in 40 seems close to
what I got, but 10 successes out of 20 seems a little low.  That 
could easily be a result of the two lower skill, however.

I think that every time you make something non-trivial, you have a chance
of gaining a skill point.  There is no history, so if you practice 20 
times, you have exactly the same chance of advancing on the last one as 
you did on the first one.  

I currently have a skill of 197.  My last few points have taken around
100 trials to advance each point.  

> 2. Is there a better way of advancing? Should I be making Gold Jasper
> Rings which I can sell at a profit instead? There is probably a higher
> fizzle rate but maybe the profit makes up for it.

You might consider sticking with electrum a bit longer.  Your success
rate will be much better, so the points will end up costing you less
in the long run.

Use what you know about the bad random number generator.  If you get 
a failure, twiddle your thumbs for a minute before trying again.  If
you get a skill increase, make jewelry like mad for a short while, 
because that means there is a really good number in the pool.

> 3. How did you make 2000pp to get your skill up? I would have to sell
> 160 pairs of Jaded Electrum Bracelets with no fizzles to make 2000pp. I
> can't even sell the 6 pairs I have on me. A typical hunting day nets me
> 15pp - 25pp. That means at least 80 hunting days to get my next 46
> skill advances.

I always carried around silver bars and amber, jasper, jade and rose
quartz, as well as a silver ruby veil and a silver fire emerald ring.
Periodically I auctioned them.  I stuck by my prices.  Now I will not
make silver jewelry, because I want to leave that market open for 
people who need it.

> 4. Do you ever make enough profit in this skill to cover the 5000pp or
> so it will take to get to 200 skill level?

At that skill, the best profit you can get is from high end silver
jewelry.  Once you can make gold amber and gold jasper earrings with
a reasonable success rate, those become better money generators.
