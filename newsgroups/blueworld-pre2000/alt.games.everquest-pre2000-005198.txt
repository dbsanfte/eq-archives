If we are talking about the same experiment done by that one guy,
I believe he did his experiment with multiple other characters who
were all green to him and did not get experience.  However, his error
was in assuming that because they were green to him, they don't 
decrease the amount of experience he gets.

Say he groups with someone who has 3% as much total experience as he
does.  This person is very low green, so will not receive any experience
from kills.  They kill something.  Because they are a group of two, they
get 105% of the normal experience.  High level guy gets 105*100/103 = 102%
of normal experience.  Low level guy would get 105*3/100 = 3% of the
experience, but because he is too low level he gets nothing.  

High level guy makes the erroneous assumption that there is a 2% bonus for 
a group of two and that the lower level guy is getting nothing.  So he 
grabs three more people to check his results.  They have 5%, 12% and 30% as 
much total experience as he does.  They kill something.  As a group of five,
they get a 20% bonus to experience.  High level guy gets 120*100/150 = 80%
of the normal experience for the kill and starts ranting that groups get
less experience than solo players on the same kills.

When a group of two or three who are all using ShowEQ to check their
experience and who are all getting experience from the kills run the
same experiment, then I will believe it.  The lone ShowEQ player could
also make pretty good measurements if he grouped with two or three 
level 1 characters who have not made any experience at all.
