Gravity defines our world, but knowing that I am accelerated
downwards at a constant 32ft/sec2 (or 9.8m/sec2) somehow does not
diminish my joie de vivre.  It's a handy number to know when deciding
just how fast I can take a corner though.

Yes, when I play Bridge, I do have a very good idea of just how 
likely it is that my partner has three cards in my suit.  When
I play Hearts or Cribbage, I also have very solid knowledge of
the probability that certain horrible things can be in my
opponent's hand (don't you hate when someone pulls the Queen out
of your five card spades suit?).  I played a lot of Cribbage with 
a friend who had earned enough money counting cards at blackjack 
to buy real estate.  I know he knew the distributions.  It was a 
lot of fun.
