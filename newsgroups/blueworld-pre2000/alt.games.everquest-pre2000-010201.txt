Raven, I just have to give you a word of warning.  Mage is unplayable
past 51.

It is a blast through 50 though.

If you have any more questions, I'll try to answer them.

These are the pet commands I used:
pet attack
pet back off
pet health
pet guard here
pet guard me

Those are the only ones I can think of that I actually used, hope I
didn't forget one.

You can never get a weapon back from a pet, to my knowledge, although
there used to be an exploit with this which I think was fixed.  But it
would have been too much trouble to recover a weapon, it was used as a
way to drop "no drop" items.

Use summoned weapons or, if you need more damage early on, rusty ones.
