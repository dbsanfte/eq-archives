You have a bunch of slots that don't have stat-boosting items.  At
this point, it may be more effective for you to buy several cheaper
pieces instead of one very nice item.

Dusty Bloodstained Gloves, Blackened Iron Medallion, Lizardscale Belt,
and a Bracelet of Woven Grass should be high on your list.   You can
also look for good deals on Gatorscale Sleeves, Braided Cinch Cord,
and a Sheer Bone Mask, but don't expect to have much money left over
for anything else.

For the long term, think about better jewelry.  Upgrade the rings to
platinum, and check into golden sapphire earrings and a platinum
sapphire necklace.

I highly recommend the Black Tome.  It will cost a bit of money, but
you'll probably keep if forever.
