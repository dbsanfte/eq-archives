yeah sure, I got hp regen as troll illusion but it's no big deal, I only
ever use it in an emergency where I have spare mana (isn't that a
contradiciton? hmm), don't know about spirit wolf illusion (not level 39
yet) but I don't believe it does give SoW effect (it is not the same as the
druid wolf spell btw, which you might have confused it with, that does give
SoW). Of course we get clarity, apparently that's a class defining
characterstic of the enchanter (according to Abashi) personally I find that
insulting and disagree anyway, if anything defines enchanters its mezz and
charm (and maybe stun). As to pets, heh, enchanter pet's are completely and
utterly worthless above level 20. If you need a pet, charm one and burn it
up.

Wizards are about dealing damage - fast. Which they do, they get the fastest
casting, fastest recycling DD spells in the game. No-body said speed was
efficient, you trade off speed against efficency. The only argument for how
"worthless" wizards are is that their spells are resisted too much. Well
weird that, I never had that trouble, (except with that horrid level 20
force DD - but that's supposed to be resisted a lot - trade off of high
damage (for the level) against high chance of resist). And wizards I group
with (as my enchanter) still manage to deal an awful lot of damage - in some
groups I just about manage to get off Tashani before the mob is blown to
smithereens by our wizzies. If I tried to use my DD - which does a
respectable amount of damage but is S-L-O-W to cast, I wouldn't even have
got half way through casting before the mob was dead. Maybe they're managing
to match the type of DD (fire, magic, cold etc) more efficiently to the mob
?

Wizards are about FAST damage.
