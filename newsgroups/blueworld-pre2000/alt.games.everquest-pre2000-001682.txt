I think what people refer to, speaking from a UO perspective,
naturally, is freedom of action of all sorts, which is generally
greater in UO. People tend to focus on PvP in this regard, but it
applies equally as much to other things. Especially if these other
things add additional advancement mechanics to the game.

So for example, ability to found and govern towns, run police forces,
hold sporting events, create quests yourself, run a Mafia, form a
business cartel to corner the market on specific goods, operate a
roleplaying tavern, etc, are all things that add depth. UO supports
all of them to some degree, some more than others.

More specifically in terms of ladders of advancement, it supports them
via different advancement mechanics, rather than all of them being
tied to one ladder, like experience points or level. This creates the
feeling of more depth for those who are interested in these other
activities.
