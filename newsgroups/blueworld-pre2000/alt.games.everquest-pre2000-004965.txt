of largely uninterrupted time to the game.  People who can put this
time in while most other players are at work are especially priviliged.

who can put in a four hour session each and every day will never be
able to obtain, even though they put in more play time.  There are

let alone actually obtain.  Certain armor quests require camping spawns
that appear less than once every six hours or more.

That's the main reasons the only characters I've played much past 20

competitive, casters can get by without any items at all quite well.  I
simply can't take vacation time to have a reasonable shot at obtaining
