> This was posted by a player in the AC newsgroup quoting Turbine
> ---------------------------------------------------------------------

> Complaint: Monsters are sticking to me like glue!

> In the March 7 update, a fix was made which we call "sticky melee". The
> intention was to fix a long-standing bug that made it nearly impossible
> for melee fighters to hit monsters which were running, which in turn made
> it nearly impossible to rescue friends who were being chased by monsters.
> The fix works well for this purpose. However, testing also revealed that
> this works both ways: monsters can now hit players who are running from
> them. Though monsters will still break off the chase as they get further
> from their home spot, they now are more likely to do damage to you as
> they chase you.

> We posted the update anyway because we felt the good outweighed the bad.
> It fixes an aspect of the game regarded as broken. It also makes it
> genuinely dangerous to antagonize monsters which can catch and pursue you
> for long. This danger was the original intent of the game designers. We
> have heard many positive comments from players about the fix -- not all
> of them high-level, PK, or melee fighters, either.

> However, since posting the update, we have found that players are being
> chased and heavily damaged by monsters which ordinarily would be a mere
> nuisance to stop and fight. This seems to be more irritating to many
> more players than we anticipated. We are investigating a change to 
> mitigate this.

> We intend to address these problems in a way that preserves the better
> aspects of the update, while making it less harsh for players who should
> be able to safely outrun nuisance monsters. We do not yet have an ETA for
> the changes. We will need at least a few days of testing. We likely will
> post the changes without much advance notice, and at that time explain
> exactly what we did. However, it is possible that we will not change
> anything until the major update planned for the first week of April. We
> are also looking into longer-term solutions to further improve the
> situation.

> Do not be surprised if, after the changes, Dereth is still a bit more
> dangerous than it was before March 7. It will still be a bad idea to
> venture too close to monsters that can catch you and do much damage to
> you.

> Another consequence of the March 7 update is the way that monsters appear
> to be "skating" behind you while in their standing pose. To improve this
> cosmetic problem involves a new game client, which we are unlikely to
> want to thrust upon you before our next major update. If we do the
> above-described changes before our next major update, it likely will not
> fix this cosmetic problem.

> We appreciate your patience as we continue to improve AC and maintain the
> tricky balance between fun and challenge.
