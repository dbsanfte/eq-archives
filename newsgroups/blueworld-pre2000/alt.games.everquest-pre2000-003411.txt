I don't think that twinking is the primary reason why you can't sell silver
or electrum jewelry any more.  The basic reason is that nothing ever leaves
the world.  Enough silver rose rings have been made to equip every mage who
could use them.  The price has dropped nearly to what a vendor would pay,
which is well below what it costs a beginning jeweler to make the things.
This is true of lots of items that are found as loot, such as minotaur axes
and dervish rings.  But this was all true as soon as these items started 
flooding the market, well before anyone was ready to start twinking them to
their own lower level characters.

Yes, the economy is hosed.  But it it the fault of a gross imbalance between
supply and demand, not of twinking.
