I would say south karana most definately.  There are always pickup groups
for gnolls and aviaks, you can solo rooks or elephants (and bull elephants)
if no groups are available.  In my experience groups are easy enough to find
here.  If you dont mind your druid faction going to shit there is always the
treants, though there may be a waiting list, and last but definately not least
is the ever popular hermit.  He hits hard for a lvl 20 mob (42) but casters love
this guy.  He is on a 3 minute spawn, drops up to 2 plat in coin and the
occasional fine steel weapon.  He is almost always camped but if you give the
camper a /tell and are nice they may let you know when they plan on leaving.
Some will also share the spawn it is so fast.  Pet casters are better here, but
my druid could solo the guy at 20.  I made 24 off him in a few hours /played.

He is that easy.  I just melee'd him with all my buffs and a dmg shield, every
once in a while I would back off and careless lightning his ass but he is a wuss.
Melee classes might have a harder time do to his pretty high dmg output but
casters should do fine, just root and blast till oom and finish him in melee with
rage, you should be fine, if not there are a million other things in the zone.
