I had that happen once in Guk with the Shin Lord.  My group walked in just as
an anonymous naked high elf charged out.  The Shin Lord, at full health, was 
on her tail.  The fight was not too tough and he had a Ghoulbane.

Of course we all expected to get a very sheepish tell from some young paladin
explaining how this had come to pass and asking nicely if we would yield the
sword.  And we were prepared to do so.  Instead we got a hail of imprecations
from some anonymous person (I would never trust a paladin who would not admit
to being a paladin) about what vile people we were and that we should hand
over the sword with all haste to redeem ourselves.  So we gave the sword to 
the paladin in our group who had spent some time trying to get one.

Then we headed down to Lower Guk where we were planning to try to hunt for the
night.  After getting completely lost in Upper Guk and wandering around for 
about half an hour, we finally found the zone.  Apparently this guy had been
shouting about us while we were lost upstairs.  By the time we got down there, 
everyone knew we were nasty people who had barged in on this guy's camp and 
stolen the Ghoulbane from under his nose.  We were being shouted at and
insulted for a while.  The rest of my group had turned off OOC and shout and
were putting people on ignore one by one.  I finally got sick of all the 
ranting and explained the true nature of the "kill steal".  It took me one 
sentence in OOC.  The shouting stopped.  The insults stopped.  The guy realized
he was not going to get his way through slander and started trying to appeal 
to reason.  It was way too late for that.  He even offered to trade a Bag of
the Tinkerer's for the sword, but we could not accept the trade honorably.
