I agree with a lot of what you say, but there's one fundamental flaw
that made up my mind to cancel: I cannot tab in and out and cannot get
into or out of the program quickly. I work at home a lot, and I'm
often doing many things at once. I need to monitor my email pretty
well all the time, for example. EQ cannot save a game in progress (of
course, wouldn't expect it to) - you have to log out, and while only
30 seconds have to pass it's still a tedious process to sit down,
camp, go back to the login server, exit there and finally from the
game. I can keep UO minimized or in a window while checking mail or
reading this newsgroup, and if push comes to shove I can recall to an
insta-log point and be out of the game in a few seconds if an
emergency comes up. With EQ, I found myself playing rarely because I
just couldn't dedicate too much time at once to the game. Combine that
with the monthly fee and my reservations about the gameplay, and I
decided to cut my losses.
