When you notice the overcrowding the most is when you get up higher in
levels and all the convienent places to hunt are taken.  You can try to
share with folks, but a lot of times they won't share, and the spawn
rate is so slow you'll find experience (thus gains) come very slowly. 
And if you're intent on getting a specific item that requires you kill a
specific monster, the overcrowding becomes a huge issue because there's
lots of other people competing for a very limited resource (that one
particular monster that only spawns once ever 8 hours). So, as a newbie,
it's usually not a problem, but when you get into your high teens you'll
probably start getting frustrated with the crowding issues.

You might want to go ahead anyway.  The expansion should, I hope, take
care of much of the overcrowding and it takes a while to get your first
character going.
