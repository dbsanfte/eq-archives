Low level spells are pretty expensive when you get them.  Cure Disease
and Cure Poison both require a fairly serious mana expenditure from a 
level 5 Cleric who does not yet have meditation.  Spirit of Wolf eats
a full bubble of mana for a level 9 Shaman.  Fighters who expect that
cleric to heal them so that they can get back to fighting should just
group with a healer and share the experience.  

Binding is a separate matter.  It is a necessity, not a luxury.  I 
would only accept a donation for a bind from someone who is clearly 
higher level and wealthier than I am.
