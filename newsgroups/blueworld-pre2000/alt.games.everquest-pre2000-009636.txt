One of the things that affects your armor class is your defence
skill.

This skill is capped, with different caps for the different
classes.  I know that melee types are capped at 5*level, and
think that clerics are as well.  I do not know about the caps
for other classes.

You raise this skill by being hit.  If you don't squint to read
the little blue messages as they scroll past you may not see
yourself improving, although you are.
