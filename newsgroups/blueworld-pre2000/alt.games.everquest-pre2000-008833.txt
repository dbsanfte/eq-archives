> /assist playername This will switch your target to the target of
> playername. This could be the groups tank.

As far as I know, this has never been true. Assist always targets the
enemy who last struck the named player -- via arrow or melee, as far
as I know. I don't believe that spell attacks enter the assist
register. Assist has always meant "attack what is attacking them".

We do need another macro (/target or somesuch) which does the same
thing, although I suspect that target confusion was intentional
design. If a dozen nearly identical enemies are attacking your friend,
it should be pretty hard to isolate the one that he has targetted.
