You would want a Druid to help you break a spawn?  Don't forget,
this is an Enchanter we are talking about.  Spawn breaking is 
not a problem.  (It does help that we are in the same room so I 
can say, "Going to Enthrall ... say pet back off ... now".)
The lack of healing was a big problem for my Necro/Enchanter pair 
in the late teens, but the share life spells work fairly well now.

The Enchanter's spell set meshes better with the Necromancer than
with the Druid.  All you can really do for a kiting Druid is cast 
Clarity and Tashan.  Any other buffs and debuffs are irrelevant 
because they aren't actually going to fight the thing.  If the 
Druid is kiting, they are really better off with a second Druid 
than an Enchanter, although it is more fun with the Enchanter. ;)

The Necromancer is very happy to get Tashan and Tepid Deeds, as 
well as a backup Fear caster and crowd control/spawn breaking 
ability.  The pair is very efficient outdoors and in dungeons
and does quite well against single nasty targets or groups of
weaker targets.
