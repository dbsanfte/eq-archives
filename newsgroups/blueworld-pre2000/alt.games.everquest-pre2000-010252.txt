Aha, but better is defined oddly.  The pet's damage will never
be worse than his unarmed damage, this is true.  However,
the pet will always use the fastest weapon he is given, although
weapon speed does no affect him in any way.  Thus, your pet
will use a rusty dagger (and hit for his natural damage) instead
of a Halberd ( and hit for perhaps substantially more than his
natural damage, if he is low level) regardless of the order in
which the two are given to him.

It is possible that better is not strictly a function of delay, but
utilizes the magic number formula...  but since the delay is
going to be ignored, this still produces silly results, just occaisionally
one that would seem to disprove my thesis; a high damage
weapon with a delay marginally longer than that of a much
lower damage weapon might still be selected over the latter.

SInce handing the pet only one weapon ensures that he will
wield the correct one, this is the procedure I follow... or
at least did up till 24th, when I began handing him one for
each hand!
