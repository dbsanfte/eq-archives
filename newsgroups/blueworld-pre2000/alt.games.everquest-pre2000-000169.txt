Brian Bennett wrote:
> 
> The existence of God is self-evident.
> 
> This means that His existence doesn't need to be proven.  However, because
> of the closed minds of humans, we think we need proof.

So let me start with an open mind, and read on...

> If you hold a painting in your hand, do you need to have someone prove the
> existence of the painter to you?  No, of course not.  The painting itself is
> proof of the painters existence.  After all, the painting didn't just appear
> out of nowhere.

Now, let's examine this carefully.  Yes, from what we know and assume about the
universe, we would tend to make the inference that a painting must have been
painted by someone.  Makes good sense.  But I certainly wouldn't stake my life on
the assumption that it is *impossible* for a painting to simply appear out of
nowhere...  

> Consider the building you sit in.  Do you need someone to prove to you the
> existence of the builder?  Of course not.  The building itself is proof of
> the builders exixtence.

...And here I certainly wouldn't stake my life on my assumption that a building
couldn't *possibly* spontaneously erect...

> For exactly the same reason, we already know of the existence of God.  We
> have His creation all around us which proves His existence.  We don't need
> proof of a Creator, we have the evidence of His existence, His creation.

...and for the same reason, I wouldn't stake my life on the assumption that the
universe hasn't *always* existed.  So it all boils down to certainties: I can say,
in everyday talk, that I am CERTAIN that a painting I see must have been painted by
someone, but in in reality I am not CERTAIN of anything (other than _possibly_ my
own existence).  How could I be?  "This" (meaning my life) could, as many people
have pointed out, be nothing more than a dream.  Thus the existence or
non-existence of *anyone* (besides, possibly, "me" (or "you," etc.)) cannot be
absolutely proven or disproven to or by "me."  That naturally includes any concept
of "God."  

That is why I don't believe in "God," and why I also don't say that "God" couldn't
possible exist.
