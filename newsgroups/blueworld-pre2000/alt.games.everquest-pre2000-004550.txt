I don't think there is any other way around it.

It is always a better tactic to sit at some place safe and bring the
mobs to you.

The only thing they can do is to have the mobs sit tight and NOT
follow the puller .. but that probably will open up huge exploitation
potential.

The single player dungeon crawl assumes that fact that there is only
one player party and they need to kill each mob once. Once you have
players at different parts of the dungeon, you need to have spawn
points and so the camping thing becomes a nature strategy to follow.
