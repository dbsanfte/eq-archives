I think you start losing experience on death at around level 5,
but it is only a tiny amount and you can make it back up by killing
one or two things.  As you get higher in level, it gets worse.  If
I die now, at level 45, I am hunting for an hour or two to get it
back.

It would be possible to die enough to actually lose multiple levels, 
but it is extremely unlikely unless you are playing with very little 
regard for your own safety.  You could do it if you mindlessly
attacked your own guards over and over or if you got bound right where
something hostile spawned (at a guard post in the Ogre city, for 
example).  If something like that happens to you, unplug your net
cable or type /quit and log in as another character to seek help.

At very high levels, when attempting difficult tasks like killing 
dragons or gods, people do take insane risks and expect to die a lot.
Sometimes they lose a level or two doing this.  Then they normally
go off and do something safer to regain the lost ground so they can
try it again. ;)
