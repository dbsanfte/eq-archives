>The GeForce2 is hampered by the fact that its memory cannot keep

That's pretty much the case with all high-end video cards anymore.

I'm running a 64Meg GeForce2 card, and with FSAA turned off for D3D,
it's flawless running EQ at 800x600 (limited tests at 1024x768 seemed
good, as well, but the text in this game is already hard enough to
read). Even in heavily wooded Kunark zones, with the clip plane pushed
out to its extreme limit and all special effect (sky, detail) at max,
it's smooth.
