It sure has.

My wife got bored with her warrior and decided to start a
bard.  The notion of a bard has her very excited.

So there she is, level 1, dying to bats in Greater Faydark,
when someone /shouts that they have goods for a true newbie.
She answered and the giver asked her if she had another
character, and what level it was.  She told the truth: level 9
human warrior in Qeynos.

The passing person then said here: take this.  And handed her a
pair of silver plated bracers, bronze helm, breastplate and
boots.  And she then ran around for the next two levels as a
bare-legged wood elf in bronze.

She sold bat wings for the money to buy her own leather pants
and to buy a FS rapier at a newbie discount.  A week later and
she is level 4 and alternates being terrified with being
exhilerated - she is having a lot of fun with her bard.

The down side to this sort of thing happened to me last night.

My primary is a Ranger 20.  I finally made it to Kelethin, only
to have Julie refuse all offers of aid to her newbie.  

I was fletching and /auctioning, and someone gives me a /tell
saying that they are level 1 and they really need a bow and can
I give them the elm/silk bow that I had just /auctioned for 8
plat.  I check and sure enough it is a level 1 ranger.  I
explain that I will not give anything away to beggars, but that
I will gladly give a cut price - I said that I would take 60
bat wings or bone chips, in any combination, and would have
accepted anything over 20 if offered in good faith.

Instead I got some whining, I told him that if he could find me
using track I would give it for the 4 plat he offered to pay
(where did that come from?).  When he finally made it to the
fletching hut it was a newbie all right - in full metal armor.
Rather than inspecting however, I quickly hit "hide", waited
for them to turn their back, dashed out and then camoed on the
next platform.  I have sold bows for cheap to half-naked
newbies who pay me in pocket change and trade goods, I will not
charge anything other than full price to a visible twink or
beggar.

It is a tough call, do you give to random newbies or not, do
you accept gifts or not.

I know how to kill bats and will not send my newbies off to
find the folks who promise goodies to the first newbie to find
them.  But many folks will, and I have accepted random gifts
(like the person who inspected my high-elf cleric level 5 clad
in full leather and handed her a ringmail skirt and gloves)
