Mystarian Phathumber <Mystarian@yahoo.com> wrote:
> Thank you for your help.  I am sorry to have created some misunderstanding
> in my question.  I was looking for a way to learn non-race languages.  i.e.
> Dragon, Giant,... etc.  With a minimum of skill points used.

You do it the same way.  SOMEBODY has to put that initial point into the
language at their guild.  Once you have that initial point, you can spam
each other and zone back and forth and get it up to excellent.

You can also get the language from someone that already has it to any skill
level, and train up to excellent.

My enchanter got to excellent in all languages this way.  By all, I mean
ALL, as in Dragon, Gnoll, Giant, Froglock, etc.

It takes about an hour to learn one language.  I recommend getting all the
languages to at least awful, and bring them all up at the same time.

Obviously, if you have the chance to learn the language from someone that is
already excellent in it, do it that way.  It's much less painful.
