I think it is the most cost effective upgrade you can buy. I had a 450Mhz P3
on an Asus P2B-F motherboard for a little over one year. Yesterday I
purchased and installed a boxed 600e that I purchased as a local computer
fair for $265. Nothing else had to be changed, and I know I'll appreciate
the additional speed.

Keep in mind that in order to upgrade, you may have to change some jumper
settings depending in the type of motherboard you have. I had to change
three jumpers. Also, a BIOS upgrade may be required. I was running 1009.A
BIOS on my machine and upgraded without flashing my BIOS. After installing
the 600E, my system reported 598MHz and also reported power and CPU ID
errors. However, it still seemed to work OK. Since I didn't like the errors,
I immediately flashed my BIOS to revision 1012 and now I get no errors and
everything works with one exception. In the past, I was able to turn on my
system by tapping on the keyboard. Now it won't start that way (it still
starts the regular way). I have to check out the BIOS options, as something
has obviously changed in the revision.

The long and the short of it is, go for it.  Goodl luck.
