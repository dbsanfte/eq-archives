Want some complaints?  hehe, here's a chat I had with my friend last night
who is an AC addict (i'm still kicking myself for sending him the free
account key during the beta-invite-a-friend stage...):

<me> Why be an archer/item-mage?
<him> you buff your arrows up and down and kill everything
<him> there is even a bug that allows archers to use xbow bolts
<him> arrows usally do 7-9, bolts do 12-15
<him> xbows are very slow, thats the reason for the bigger damage

<him> the only thing i really love about item magic is a spell called portal
<him> you can tie to one point anywhere in the world
<him> there is a point i go to all the time, but it takes me 10 min run to
get there. item mages can pop back and forth np
<him> the spot is a fort in deep dires(area) that buys equipment for 20-30%
above value, most places only offer 60-70% of value
<him> so I loaded a friend up with old equipment, he poped back and forth
and in 5 min had my money, it would have taken me a hour at least to do that

<him> the servers are getting pretty busy, there are about 7 of them, each
one having about 2200 people each, except darktide(pk server) which has
about 800

<him> being a melee fighter, i would get pissed off, everytime i would get a
magic user (85% of people are) almost dead , they would just cast portal
recall and book

<me> How is the trading system going anyway?  In everquest, a trade window
comes up with stuff and each party has to hit TRADE before the stuff is
swapped.  But in AC, you give something to someone, and they ahve it and you
have to pray they give you something back...
<him> yep, thats how it is
<him> but, i am at a point where my level helps me
<him> i say give me the item first, if they ask me if i can be trusted, i
say i wouldnt risk the name of my level 31 character to  a bad trade, but to
them most of the time they are a mule, so it doesnt matter if they are a bad
trader

<him> you can really tell the melee fighters from the magic users in ac
<him> its almost a hatred at this point
<him> melee fighters get sick to death of wimpy mages casting life drain
thru walls to kill stuff
<him> for example, i was in a olothi cave getting my butt whooped, and this
stupid level 16 mage is just sitting there, hiding behind a wall
powerleveling
<him> i hope they fix it on ac, it so so unfair
<him> thats why so many swordsman quit and go with the cheese
<him> its hard to find decent melee fighters
