The limiting factor on the price seemed to be the requirement of an
ice gaint drop - though as they are often still hunted this may not be
such a huge deal to aquire for any level character - though you may
have to pay singnificantly for it.

All the armour seems to be on par with totemic, or better in some
cases. 9AC +2 wis cloak, for example. However I would refuse to wear
any visible pieces as they still look the same as banded. Wearing
Jarseth scale bracers and legs one day is one thing, but this armour
isnt really any better than Totemic.

It is a shame, though, as I was looking forward to wearing matching
dyed chest and legs :(

The possible pieces worth looking in to are the mask, sholders, belt
and obviously the cloak (arms should also be 9AC +2 wis). I would
guess the mask is 5AC, +2 wis - no better than plat ruby. Sholders
maybe 7/8AC +2 wis - possibly worth it if you are having trouble
sourcing a tattered mantle like me. Belt maybe 7/8AC +2wis - worth it
if you are tired with hauling a strawspun about. Cloak - worth it to
save you 4k/camp on alchemist.

I'm likely to only look into purchasing a cloak. But I'm in no rush,
as I have my totemic and expect it costly to aquire - assuming I can
even find a highly skilled Barb smith on Torvon. The only person so
far seeming to be doing high quantities of new armour there is a well
known high level Ogre warrior opperating at a loss :/
