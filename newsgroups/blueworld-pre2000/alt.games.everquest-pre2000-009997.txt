> Sometimes the spells get interrupted without any reason.

> during casting. It just gets interrupted. In the message

> 
> "Your casting begins"

> I didn't move. To test whether it is my own attack that

> BUT IT JUST GOES ON BEING INTERRUPTED from time to time.

If you mean that in a test condition, just you standing still
for 20 seconds with no monsters around, your spells are interrupted,
then I think there is something wrong.  Perhaps you have a really
flakey network connection or ... something.  No good idea.

If you mean that in combat you get those two messages in sequence
with no other messages in between, then you are seeing other effects.
The actual event may have been in order, but was displayed out of
order on your screen.  I have noticed that if a monster hits me
just before I begin casting, it often interrupts the cast and if a 
monster hits me just before I end a cast, I don't even get a "regain
concentration" message.  It seems like the casting time is shifted
forward when interruptions are calculated.  Maybe the server is 
"correcting" for lag that isn't really there.
