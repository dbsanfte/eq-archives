>Good god, you really hate mages, don't you? 

No, just magery. I think it's a gross imbalance that a nekkid guy with
a fistfull of reagents can kill a Sir HasALottaLoot with virtually no
risk to himself at all (because if you die, you lose nothing).  And if
the magery doesn't kill Sir HasALottaLoot, the nekkid mage is probably
a GM fencer with 100 str, too (well, cept in the old days it was GM
ArcherMage, but whatever works).

>Making spellbooks lootable
>would make the cost of being a mage go through the roof, 

Right now the cost of *being* a mage is almost zero.  The cost of
making a mage isn't too terribly relevant - it's an investment not a
"cost", per se.  Invest another 80 points or so in inscription and
keep a backup spellbook in your bank (for those 5 or 6 spells that
you'll actually I use - I mean let's be honest, no one is going to
have a completely full spellbook, except in the bank, and the cost of
replacing it is a *lot* less than the cost of making a full
spellbook), and it's really not a huge increase in cost there.

It would make it more difficult to become a mage, but why not?  There
are too many mages in UO anyway.

Other people should be able to take the scrolls back out of your
spellbook though, that way they can use the spellbook (makes it
attractive to loot), but not *as* a spellbook.

>inscription to magery would make it unbelievably difficult to advance
>in. Creating a GM scribe is harder than making a GM smith, and it took
>me months to make a GM smith. 

You'd only need about 80 points of inscription.

As far as that goes, they could make it easier to scribe a scroll into
a spellbook than it is to make the scroll.  So maybe a GM Mage only
needs 50 or 60 inscription.  Has the added benefit of keeping
Inscription as a viable trade skill.

>I agree that archery desperately needs tweaking, though.

With arrow-less sparring gone, you'd think they could kick it up a
notch.  I'd rather see it improved in a way that benefited bowyers
too, though.  Better bows, arrows, whatever crafted by better bowyers,
much in demand.  Leave em "can't be repaired" so they can be sold more
than once, or put in a chance for bows to break when you go toe-to-toe
(not a HUGE chance).

I also with armor would be just about completely redone so that there
weren't so much a "best item syndrome" with armor and weapons.

Parrying needs to be made useful or just deleted or something.  What a
waste.  A shield is like carrying around a sign that says "please kill
me, I have no magery".

I think I would tie a bow's chance to hit to what armor the archer
were wearing, and kick up the damage a bit overall (in addition to
adding more/better types of player-made bows).

I'd crank up the value of wearing plate by a LOT.  Lose the
"plate/chain combo" nonsense.  Plate would be the best armor in the
game, period... as far as armor went.  Downside being that there are a
mess of skills you can't use while wearing it (hide, stealth,
snooping, stealing, meditation and I'd add archery as well).  I'd also
make it so that no matter what your weapon speed is, while wearing
plate your weaponspeed would never be better than about the speed of a
longsword.  Same thing for chain, but the threshhold would be a
slightly faster weapon.  Heck, you might see people wearing *gasp*
different types of armor.  And weilding different types of weapons!
