Gordon, there is a bug in the code, no "possible" about it. I've seen it with 
my own eyes. A perfect example is the situation in the Planes, where a solo 
player will regularly get the kill on mobs on which four or five full groups 
are fighting. Of course, in this circumstance, it's not really a problem, 
since on my server, at least, plane runs are done as a collaborative effort, 
so it's not a life-and-death contest a la the old J-boots situation, but it's 
clear to me that there is a problem with the method used to calculate the GAD. 

Interestingly, it's not just soloists who can take advantage of the bug; 
the damage contributed by smaller groups also seems to be weighed more 
heavily. I was in a group of three - a shaman, a cleric and a magician (me) - 
that was managing to get a good portion of the kills in Hate one night. When 
we added another magician, the proportion of kills dropped.
