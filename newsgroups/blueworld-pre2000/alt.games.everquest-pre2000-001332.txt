Is this a hero?
I was thinking about recall one day and imagining a world that was
huge and with no recall but with a ruin in the middle of the center of
the oldest city that was supposedly the hub of a magical transport
system.
There would be rumors of an ancient magic lost long ago, and
eventually someone might actually discover the magic. The problem
would be that to create a node of the magic transport system X number
of high level mages would have to preform a ritual. This ritual would
be the imbuing of the sight with a substantial amount of raw magical
power. Power which is permanently lost from the imbuing mages. 
Thus first off you have to get people playing high level mages who
would be willing to give of themselves. 

I'd consider that to be somewhat heroic.
But there's a catch. While preforming this ritual, any of the mages
can do a certain act, and doing so makes them the focus of the
transfer instead of the artifact.  The first to do so gains the extra
magical power of all the others that would have been transferred to
the artifact. Only the first to do so gains anything.
Sort of a prisoners dilemma come to life. If everyone can trust
everyone else then a permanent addition to the game world comes into
being, one which will benefit every other player in the game. On the
other hand there is a perfect opportunity for treachery on a vast
scale as some would be power hungry mage steals the magic from all the
other mages. But he wouldn't get enough to be a superpower and he
would still have X-1 other pissed of mages after him. Interesting
scenario to me, one that has the promise of real interaction between
the players through their virtual avatars.
