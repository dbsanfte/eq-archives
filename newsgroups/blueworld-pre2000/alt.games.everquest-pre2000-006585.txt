The underlying flaws of the new policy are this:


1) I am a wizard, so I root my target (It is now aggressive on me), another
player sees the mob standing still (ie, just spawned) and attacks it.  Now,
since the mob is aggressive on me, it is "my kill" and I can have the other
player Warned(tm).

2) I am a necromancer using fear/dots to kill my target.  I lay on my DoTs
and fear the target as per the strategy.  The mob is still aggro on me.
Another player comes up and attacks said mob.  Again, I can report this
player.

The above cases are easily made and has happened to me several times (More
so the first, than the last).


As others have pointed out, the "Play Nice" rules are also flawed.  What if
I was camping Hadden, or Pyzjin -- both are several hour spawns -- and
someone comes up and DEMANDS that I share.  Of course, as per the rule I
have to (or be warned for disruption).  So, 5 hours and 30 minutes into the
respawn time (out of 6 hours, for hadden) a level 50 <class that can KS me
here> shows up and demands that he have the next spawn.  Since I've been
sitting here for at least 5.5 hours, I say no to him and he
reports/petitions me.  Now a guide is involved.  The guide will try to make
me share and will sit there and watch to make sure that I do.  In mean time
til spawn, he can't leave (because the spawn may be soon ~ how does he know
when the spawn is?) and answer petitions that can be YOUR corpse stuck in a
wall, or YOU stuck in a wall and need to be summoned.  Great idea, folks.  I
can't wait to see how/if this is enforced.
