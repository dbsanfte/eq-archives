a lot of people that only play AC sometimes complain about the allegiance
system.. some people feel its not fair that some magic items only work if
you are of a certain allegiance rank ('cause they will never be of any rank,
since they never want to be part of an allegiance)... the allegiance system
has actually helped me alot in the game. i play a warrior, and a lot of
magic items have a certain requirement that only mages can achieve. well i
recently found an item that didnt have this 'mage requirement', but instead
had an allegiance requirement that i happened to meet. having people sworn
to me allowed me to use it.

in a guild I was in EQ, it didnt really do much for me. we were all of
around mid level (mid teens to mid 20s), but never went on any adventures
together... we did fight in Crushbone together a lot, but it was just by
chance some of us were all there at the same time... a guild in EQ was just
a new chat room for us.. we just talked. that was it. my character had a
guild name after her character name, but nothing else came out of it. i dont
even know who the guild leader was... another guild i was in was even less
fun. i knew who the leader was, but he was never on. i just never had good
experiences with guilds in EQ.

in AC, someone asked me to swear to them, with promise that they would help
me out in the game (i was still new), and to give me some better armor (i
was wearing store bought studded leather).. i swore to him, and he
adventured with me, and gave me a lot of stuff.. he had played in beta, and
knew a lot of the good hunting places. he helped me advance far.. as he got
to the very high levels, he started to give me some of the best armor
available (stuff no shop sold).. he gave me money and items, and over time
he also got a little exp generated from what i made.. after so many
weeks/months of being under him, i have since gotten my own vassals that i
give money and items to, and my patron has gotten more vassals under him..
there is now 50 of us in our "little group".. we have gone on many trips to
dangerous places, and have fought hard battles together and gotten rare
items... we commonicate a lot with each other, and help each other on
difficult corpse runs..

an allegiance is like an EQ guild, but makes you "closer" with the people 1
rank above and below you. you can click on a button to see if your
patron/vassals are signed on, how much exp has been generated, and your
current titles.. you can communicate with the people above you or below
you.. the way exp is generated makes people want to go out and get vassals.

its true though that some allegiances are made for the wrong reasons (greedy
patrons wanting exp from vassals, or greedy vassals wanting loot from their
patron)

a good allegiance is just like a good guild in EQ. usually its just a bunch
of friends that have fought together or have a common interest (role playing
for example).. the "guild leader" is at the top, everyone swears to him. the
"guild" is now made..

basically, an allegiance in AC is like a guild in EQ but with perks...

i swore to my patron in hopes he would show me around. i got my first
dedicated vassal with promises that i would do the same for him that was
done for me.. (its a good way to help newbies.)
