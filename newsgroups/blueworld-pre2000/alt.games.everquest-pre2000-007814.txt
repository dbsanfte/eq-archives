"Jeff Lanham" <jeffl@etcscan.com> wrote:

>When do you need the large sewing kit?

You need it as soon as you try to make something that has more
components than will fit in the small kit.

So long as you're making stuff that consists of a pattern and a skin,
or a pattern and a silk swatch, the small kit is fine. However, if you
start making cured silk, for example, which involves a pattern and 1
or more silk swatches and 1 or more heady kiolas, then you need a
large kit which has enough slots to hold them.

Personally, I recommend that unless you're a flat broke newbie
character making your own patchwork armor, you just cut to the chase
and start by buying the large kit. Heck, you can even use it as a
low-weight backpack when you're not tailoring.

Kit size, by the way, has nothing to do with the size of the armor
created - only with the number of components you have to assemble.

>Can you buy the larger kit?  I heard that it had to be made by a blacksmith.

You do buy the larger kit. No doubt there are vendors all over who
sell them, but the only ones I remember offhand are in Misty Thicket
and the Inns of North Ro.

>Can you stack things in your kit?   i.e. 2 swatches in one square or 1
>swatch per square?

The game doesn't like stacked items. If you stack two silk swatches or
two heady kiolas, the tailoring will not work. Each single item mut be
in its own slot.
