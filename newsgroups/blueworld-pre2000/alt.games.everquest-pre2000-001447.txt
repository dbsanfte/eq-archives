Doesn't the game world change somewhat based on your "faction" with various
NPC groups? I would think that if you kill friends that have characters that
have "positive" faction with a group it would lower your faction with said
group. While this may not chage the underlying basis of the game it should
have a negative impact on your interaction with NPCs who have positive
faction with that group. In fact there is no reason that it shouldn't also
effect your relationship to other PCs as well.

I also agree that you should be able to "change" the world of EQ. For
example, if a group could get together and kill all the corrupt gaurds in
Freeport we could rid the city of them. Until that is another group of
corrupt guards rise up and try to take back the city (if they can).

As for heros, I agree with the previous poster on item 1. Help your friends.
But I disagree with him on point 2. Don't help strangers. A hero helps
anyone in need.  If a hero saw a person unconscious and bleeding to death
he/she would help that person becaue they wouldn't be able to determine that
the person was good or bad.
