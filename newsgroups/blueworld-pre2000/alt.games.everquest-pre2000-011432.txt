Create a pure warrior and play it up to 9 or 10 to get a feel for the
game. Stay in the newbie areas (where you start) and consider your
targets. target (clicl on the creature so that it's name appears in a
red bar) and hit 'c'. you will get a response. The color of the
response is most important. dolors from easiest to hardest based on
your level are light green, dark green, blue, white/black, yellow, red.

Attack blues and whites now, after 5 or so, try blues only. Light
greens give no experience, dark greens negligible experience. As a
warrior, you have kick, and as a barbarian/troll/ogre warrior you will
have smash. Make a button for these skills, and use them in combat for
more damage. Group with casters who seem to know what they are doing,
and watch them. you can learn alot about being a casting class while
still enjoying your game.

For initial ease, try a troll warrior. They get kick,bash,smash, dual
weild, and they regenerate. downside, you are Kill on sight in any zone
but your home zone until you work up your factions, which is very hard.
read the newbie and race/class guides at www.everlore.com
