On Sun, 19 Mar 2000 04:56:53 GMT, Debby <debbyhall@optushome.com.au> wrote:
>Randomness is not the answer but I think quests could be. The idea of having
>every major item a quest would solve many problems. Especially for gamers
>like me who do not have the time or inclination to camp monsters.  I think
>camping is very boring and quests are much more interesting. Quests on the
>other hand provide more interest in the game. Much better than go kill that
>mob because he will eventually drop this sword. After all it is supposed to
>be a game filled with quests surely we could just expand the quest lists a
>bit further.

On the other hand, there are people who *like* to camp.  They have a
large community of friends within EQ, and to them EQ is basically a chat
room where they occasionally have to get up and fight something.

It seems to me a way to give everyone a good shot at any item, while
accomodating all types of players, would be:

(1) Some items are available through both camping and questing.  Note
that the items needed for the quest should not require camping! :-)

(2) Because the quests should not involved camping, and Verant probably
does not have the resources to come up with good quests for everything,
some items remain camp-only.

(3) Camp-only items that are not no-drop get distributed by the player
economy, so nothing further needs be done there.  To allow the
non-campers to get the no-drop items, there are two changes:

(3a) Some no-drop items are changed to one-drop.

(3b) Some no-drop items are changed so they can be traded if the player
receiving the item in the trade has some special quest item, e.g., "The
Orb of the Merchant", which is consumed during the trade.  So, if a
non-camper wants one of these no-drop camping items, he quests for the
Orb of the Merchant, then finds a camper who has the item and buys it.

Perhaps that should be several quest items that allow trading no-drop
items.  The easy ones would only allow trading for the less powerful
no-drop items.  The idea here is that given that Verant does not have
the resources to come up with a good quest for each item, instead they
can come up with a few good quests for the trade-enabling items.
