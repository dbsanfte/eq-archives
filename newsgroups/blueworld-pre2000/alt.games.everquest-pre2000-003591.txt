>My Ranger is in the same boat.
>She has saved up enough for a vendor long sword, but for much
>less than that she can get a Mino Axe in kelethin from another
>player.  They have been auctioned for 4 or 5 pp on E'ci lately.

Buy a 1H Dwarven Axe (6 Damage, 26 Delay). Usually costs between 7-12pp, 
and has better stats than a store bought long sword. They are auctioned in 
Greater Faydark all of the time, since they come from Crushbone and are 
fairly common. 

>
>I want to get her a forged bastard sword instead, because I
>like the look, but I am not sure what that costs a blacksmith
>to make and if I will be able to find one.

Don't buy a Forged Bastard Sword! Both Mino Axes and Dwarven Axes have 
better stats and are more common. The only forged weapon even remotely 
worth buying is a Forged Two Handed Sword, and even then you are better off 
with a Fine Steel Two Handed Sword.
