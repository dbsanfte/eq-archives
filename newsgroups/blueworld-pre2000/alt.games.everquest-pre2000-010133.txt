> I was wonering do any of you EQ player that have Broadband
> always on connections stay on the everquest world?

No, real life intrudes from time to time.  For one thing, 
I have to sleep eventually.  Food can normally be procured 
while playing.

> Lets say I am researching and I want to increase my stats.
> Could I just stay in the world (aka logged on to EQ) and
> research over and over again increasing my stats? Is there
> any danger?

Researching does not increase your stats.  Nothing increases
your stats except items that add to your stats while worn.

You can't repeat research over and over.  Each time you do
research, it consumes components that you found while hunting.
Before you do research again, you will have to go out and
kill monsters to get more components.

If you pick a nice safe town where everyone likes you, you
should be safe to practice your skills.  I like to fish 
while I practice my spell casting, just to keep a bit more
occupied and to make some cash to buy more spells. ;)

Using an external device or macro program to repeat any 
activity while you are not at your computer is against the 
rules of the game.  If a GM catches you at it, they can ban
your account.
