> You took this COMPLETELY out of context.  The original point was that
> OSI was supposedly catering to the needs of PK's over the needs of
> people who were totally opposed to pvp.  I was merely pointing out that
> nearly all of the changes in the past months have made PKing harder to

> Ultima 7 and not Ultima Online.

What they have been doing is taking half-steps, steps which have hampered our
own ability to fight the jerks, in order to avoid taking the whole step of
eliminating pk.  It's like building a bridge.  If you want to span 1000 feet,
and build a 500 foot bridge, you have accomplished nothing.  750 feet, still
nothing.  987 feet, you still do not have a bridge.  We still do not have the
game we want.

For instance, while thieving is way down, and no longer rules the game, I
still have to do Everything I did during the worst of times to avoid being
stollen from because of the possibility of one thief getting to me if I do
not.  And doing all this is costly, time-comsuming, and oppressive.  By the
same token, I still have to act as if Every place I go is going to have an
ambush of pk waiting for me, because just once in a while that is exactly what
there is.  While some of my characters are at the top of the game in character
abilities, and as well as I can handle certain dungeons, I still do not dare
go to them much, because they have become so much worse with all the pk.  On a
private shard, not having to do all this relieves me of a great burden, and
makes starting over, even with the world populated with such killer monsters,
much less of a drudgery.

In short, these half-steps have not really made the game better, they have
just shifted it around.  The pk may not be happy, but they are to me as much a
problem as they ever were.
