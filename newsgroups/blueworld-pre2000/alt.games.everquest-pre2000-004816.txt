I think the main problem here is that with any of the changes you
described, a lot of 'powergamers' (which, admit it, is most of us to
some extent) are going to be adversely affected in some way, and will
(naturally) complain... several examples spring to mind - 

for example, you are nearing the end of the Soulfire quest, all you have
to do is kill Sir Lucan, you have a guild behind you to help you do it,
you organize a time (probably several weeks in advance) when everyone
can be there to help... the day before, Mithaniel Marr comes down from
heaven and smites the militia - Sir Lucan is no more, you can't do your
quest..

If dwarves declared war on the elves, there would be uncountable people
killed and needing GM assistance to recover their corpses. Elves would
suddenly have to go through the problems that evil races have getting
through butcherblok. Many hunting areas would become depopulated because
of the reduced number of players who can go there, increasing the strain
on other areas.

If druids declared war on the barbarians, the barbs would join the evil
races in being unable to use druids for teleportation, and would have a
lot of trouble getting out of their homeland..

the list goes on...



maybe have one server where all this happened, a real Role-playing
server?
I think Verant have better things to do, however...
