Electoral votes ARE based on population, however, a state gets a minimum number
of electoral votes regardless of population (three).  In an interview with a
college
professor on one of the news programs, it was shown that one elector in
California
represents more people than live in the entire state of Montana (which gets
three
electoral votes I believe).  Therefore, it takes less people to equal one
elector in
Montana than in California ( one Montana citizen = three+ California citizens).

So in essence, one person's vote is not necessarily equal to another person's
vote
in this electoral system.
