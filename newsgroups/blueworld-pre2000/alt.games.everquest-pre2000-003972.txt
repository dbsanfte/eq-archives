Gee, I wonder who was unfamiliar with EQ game mechanics.

For quite some time, the kill credit went to the single person who did
the most damage.  Then if that person was in a group, their group got
the credit.  It is not too hard for a Wizard a couple of levels over a
group to swoop in and out damage any member of the group.  And if the
monster is already wounded, it goes down without killing the Wizard too.

This was fixed a while ago (to the sound of great rejoicing) and there
has been much less complaining about kill stealing since then.  Or as
you would probably describe it, they nerfed a Wizard's ability to steal
kills.  (Damn Verant and their nerfing!)

So, if Wizards are such gods on earth, why would they be forced to 
resort to such vile tactics?  If they are so amazingly powerful, why
