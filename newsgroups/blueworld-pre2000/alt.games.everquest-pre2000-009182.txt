Here are a couple of things that I look for: other folks can
and should add to the list.

No one talks about intended tactics or roles, i.e. who is the
primary tank, who is primary healer, who is canary, who is the
enchanter's bodyguard, and so on.

People take forever to buff up

People run in to fight without any buffs.

People say negative things in group talk

People do NOT say positive things in group talk

People run around and try to explore while others are resting

People harass one another without first clearing it in a
/tell.  (My cleric is a snob with a talent for verbal abuse.  I
warn people before she starts to insult their characters.  When
people play back it is a LOT of fun, if they do not it could
be trouble.)

This is a big one:  people do not talk.

Puller leaves without checking to see if group is ready
(personally I call "looking" when I head out, "mob description"
when I get ready to pull, "pulling" when I aggro it, and "inc"
when it is almost on the group.)

Tempo lags

People force the tempo

--

There are more, but most of the things I list here involve:
communication, and working as fast as you can without becoming
unsafe.

More thoughts anyone?
