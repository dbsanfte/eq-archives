	Computers are not luggage.  Luggage contains only those items a traveller

	Computers are far too useful and interesting to permit them to be
handicapped by Big Brother.  Simple fact is, corporate rights are secondary to
personal rights, because corporations are artificial entities.  The
individual's right to privacy is more important than any corporation's right
to protect its interests.
