I think that the pets should taunt extremely well, just not deal all that 
much damage.  That is IF the necro is truely suppose to be a solo class.  
The "necro" is the one who is suppose to be dealing the damage, not the 
pet.  The pet is there so that the necro can have time to inflict his 
damage.  Just like the magician pet, he's there to keep the mob busy while 
the magician nukes.  Of course the pet has to deal some damage, because you 
can't root and nuke like a wizard does.

But the argument with necros is that they're pet is more powerful than a 
warrior of it's same level when it comes to stats and hps.  Not smarts of 
course.  The player controlled warrior will always be smarter than the pet, 
and know what to attack and what not to attack.  But the main complaint for 
a lot of melee characters is that the necro has a warriors fighting equal 
(or better) on top of the fact that their spells are already more powerful 
than melee.  Spells are SUPPOSE to be more powerful, there's no problem 
there.

The best analogy I can think of is that a PC necro with pet NPC is about 
the equivelant of a PC warrior having a petless magician NPC at his side.  
The necro is not as powerful spell-wise as a wizard, but he gets a warrior 
to help him.  The warrior is just as powerful as the necros warrior, so he 
gets a magician (which is not as powerful spell-wise as a wizard).

It's just silly that they complain about not being "playable" now, because 
they're STILL about the equivalent of 2 PC characters in one.  The closest 
EQ has to a tank mage (through use of the pet).
