If you have been playing a Pally long enough to get some good

As I summarize them:

Wizards: fast smackdown, teleports, some utility spells
Magicians:  good pets, good blast, great damage shields,
summoned toys.
Enchanters:  crowd control, buffs, debuffs
Necros:  dots, pet, everyone hates them.
