	This whole play nice policy is a REALLY bad idea.  Yeah, there are
some problems with folks perma-camping spots, but if you want everyone to
play nice, then don't setup a gaming senario where there are moral
ambiguities and opposing factions.  

	It's my impression that for the most part people do play nice,
with some variation on what nice is (for example first come first serve or
rotation.)  All that creating a RULE that about "playing nice" is going to
do is bring out the bastards who like manipulating the system.  And we
will see a lot of those.  And you folks who do the GMing are going to see
a lot more whiners.  Hell the *ONLY* time I've ever petitioned a GM
regarding a conflict with another player involved a game interface flaw
and the other player was sitting on top of my kill to prevent me from
looting.  If it doesn't involve a game design problem or loophole that
gets someone killed repeatedly then the GMs should stay the heck out of
it.  They let the rest of the world evolve socially, starting to impose
outside rules on it now is a bad road to go down.
