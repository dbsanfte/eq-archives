Leaving your rogue automatically practicing lockpicking on the door
in Befallen while you are not at the keyboard is bad for two reasons.

First, you are botting the game.  (I'll just leave the discussion
about why this is bad for those other threads.)

Second, there is often an open-and-close door part in the macro.
I have heard more than one story of people getting killed in 
Befallen because some macroing rogue slammed the door on them and
trapped them.

In any case, I thought the reason they did not want people picking
the lock in Mist was because the difficulty of the key quest is
supposed to serve as a warning for what you will find behind the 
door.  Rogues were picking the lock and then losing their corpses
when they found out what was behind the door.  GMs were doing
corpse recovery for them, because they were not supposed to be able
to do that.
