The more I play my warrior in groups and soloing, the more and more I
disagree with this.

Going from 117 STR to 141 STR from items and buffs, I average over the
course of a fight (level 22-26 mobs) up to 75 points more of damage, and
that is not something to complain about. It's not so significant in a group,
but when I have to solo (and the hours I play means I have to do that a
lot), it's not something I'm going to say is insignifcant. Thats a result of
an increase in the damage done for maximum hits, but more significantly, a
big increase in the damage done by critical hits. My average crits (I use 2
langseaxs) at 117 Str are 29. My average crits with 141 STR are 34. My
average on Crippling Blows (and you see these a lot when you solo,
unfortunately) has increased by 15.

The effect is obviously more noticeable the longer a fight lasts, and when
you're soloing they can last altogether too long. In a group, you're just
not going to see that much of an impact except with the Criticals/Crippling
Blows.

The additional Strength is also nice when you've got a ton of loot to carry,
you're not in a group, and you have to run 2 zones over just to sell
something.

I still can't see the relation between Dexterity/Agility and how often you
land Crippling Blows/Criticals, which is ticking me off. I increase
Dexterity, my percentage chance of a Critical definitely goes up. I increase
my Agility, and while it goes up, it's not as noticeable as with Dex. Right
now, sans Dex and Agi buff (and these again are with mobs level 22-26,
myself being 23), I land 2% Criticals. With my Dex buffed up by 25, I land
5.5%. With my Agi  buffed up 25, I land 4%. With both buffed up, it's just
shy of 6%.

One thing about Double Attack/Dual Wield (which is nothing to do with the
above, but I just thought of it) is the very rare, but always nice Double
Attack Critical/Crippling Blow.

I am assuming the formulae decides first whether or not a specific hit will
be a Critical, and THEN processes whether or not Double Attack procs. I've
only seen it twice, but it is SO nice to see (forgive lack of exact text,
but you get the idea)

Hassan has landed a Critical Hit! (38)
Hassan has landed a Critical Hit! (38)

Or even better, being at 10 hit points and seeing:

Hassan has scored a Crippling Blow! (81)
Hassan has scored a Crippling Blow! (81)

And watching the Graymalkin that was about to have you for dinner drop like
a sack of hammers.
