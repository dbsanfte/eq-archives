Bandit camps.  Without a doubt, the best money available at your
level.  There's at least four or five out in West Karana, one each in
East, North, and South Karana, two in Lake Rathe, and one in the Rathe
Mountians.  Not only do they drop bronze weapons, but you can turn
their sashes into the Pally guild in South Qeynos, for more coin,
bronze weapons, faction, and a remarkably good amount of exp well up
into your mid 20's.
