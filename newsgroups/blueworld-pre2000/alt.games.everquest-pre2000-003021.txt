    Bringing up the sacred Zelda 64 for reference.

    I miss the oddball characters from the Japanese RPGs.
    You'd go up to an old lady and she might say, "I used to be beautiful, now
I'm just sexy."
    Or a small child might exclaim, "Someday I shall be a knight, just like my
dad."
    Or a teenager would run around chasing his dog.
    Or a dog just starts following you for no reason and not to attack.
    Or you'd attack the chickens and the flock would come down on you.
    Or a kid would say, "I don't want to play with you.  Go away."
    People would be sawing logs or cooking food or reading.

    EverQuest always lacked a human ambiance.  The simple things people do to
keep busy or the goofy things people say.
    If someone buys out Verant that gives a damn, I'd be more inclined to
venture back on the expansion pack.
