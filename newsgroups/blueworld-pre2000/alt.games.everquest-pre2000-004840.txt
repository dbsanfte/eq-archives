> looting, possibly out of order.  If he's not in your group, then he
> can only loot after the minute-and-a-half goes by, and at that point

Basically, ninja-looting is looting something when it isn't your 
turn to loot, whether or not you are in the group.

In general, bodies that are more than two minutes old are not first 
come, first served.  Always ask, even if nobody appears to be 
standing up to loot.  After a big fight, it can take a minute or two 
to sort out whose turn it is, especially if the body contains a 
valuable NO DROP item.

There are times when the body can't be looted within two minutes.
Enemies killed early in a big fight may well be lying there because
nobody can be spared to loot.

The sword in question is NO DROP, so it's possible that the Paladin 
who was supposed to loot it died and was waiting for a res when 
Bobes stole it.  I don't know the particulars of the situation.

If you value your reputation, ask before looting someone else's kill,
no matter how long they have left it there.
