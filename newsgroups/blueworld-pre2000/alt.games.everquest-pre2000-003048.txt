I posted a message on this subject higher up, but I'll restate here. The AC
world is huge and it is a definite plus.

I have explored most of the southern half of the Dires. I have yet to see
another human being in the deeper areas. I have found many interesting
towers and forts...only a few of which I bothered to stop and look at since
the place is also crowded with monsters.

I really enjoy the sense of exploration. I just wish they would tone down
the monster density a bit so a person could stop and enjoy the scenery a
little or just wander around a little. Most of the time I'm running full
blast...225 for me and probably still a bit slow.

EQ is tiny. Worse, there is a certain monotony to the landscape. AC can also
be monotonous, but because of its size perhaps some of the secrets of the
Dires will remain unknown for a longer period of time. In EQ, secrets
lasted...oh...one or two mintues...
