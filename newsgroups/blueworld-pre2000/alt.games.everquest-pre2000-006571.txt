>    I am only a lvl 18 Wiz and this is what I think about having a Wiz in
>the group.  If the group understands mana management and doesn't expect him
>to be "chain casting" thru the whole fight, then you are using your wiz
>member effectively.  The problem I have seen when joining some groups, is
>they expect me to be able to nuke all of the time.  There is no way that I
>can.  The game would become completely imbalanced if I was given "mana on
>tap".  The best groups for me to fight with, and the ones I've had the most
>xp success with, are the ones that want me to med as much as possible and
>only use 1 or 2 nukes per mob, unless our situation starts deteriorating.
>They like having the piece of mind that their Wiz can nuke a threat into
>oblivion.  So the groups that I like to play with realize this and we all
>keep track of everyone's mana and health levels.  We also chide overcasters.
>If a wiz overcasts, the tanks don't get the time to increase their melee
>abilities.  The caster is OOM (out of mana) and useless, especially if an
>emergency develops.
>    Now, for soloing.  I believe that the wiz needs a class specific
>spell/ability that will help him flee/cast.  When a mob starts attacking a
>Wiz, we being fragile, find it nearly impossible to cast a spell to flee.  I
>would choose an ability, not a spell that takes up one of the 8 slots, that
>can help a wiz to get in a quik cast to flee/cast(root, etc.) would be in
>order.  A mage has his pet, even if it gets killed, because he usually has
>had time to see things start going bad.  An enchanter has the ability to
>charm, along with druids.  A cleric has heal spells.  Not sure about
>shamans.  If a wiz gets in trouble, there is usually no getting out of it.
>He is done and dead, unless he has one foot thru the zone.
>    I heard an idea about a familiar.  That would be just as good an
>ability, with the familiar being able to taunt/distract the mob for just a
>second.  The familiar being very weak, but with one or two very limited and
>weak abilities (taunt, distract, blind, poison, slow, etc..).  The wiz would
>have to go to lengths to purchase, catch, raise, cast, meditate in specific
>areas, or a combination of to obtain a familiar.  In this way the Wiz would
>have to be worried about it's well being.  Not just a freebie.
