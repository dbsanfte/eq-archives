I have done this - the various empty rooms and guard towers in
the cities are good places for it.

If you are moving items off of a character and are willing to
take an experience loss (taking them off a low level or simply
retiring on old character - or protecting the investment in
twinking items for that matter) load up one character with the
stuff you want to transfer, find a safe open place, and suicide.

then /consent your other character.

I did this when re-starting my clerics - on my third attempt at
a cleric now.

But for exchanging studs and leather armour, just find higher
level character who is standing by the bank and selling things,
and ask them if they can hold something for you.  A few plat is
not worth the loss of their good name and they will probably be
a very safe helper.

Or, next time you are in a group that breaks up, ask one of
those folks to hold an item for you.  I did this for a friend
who was giving dwarven ringmail to his cleric (a level 18
twinking a level 5)
