Yup.


> 2) Do you lose them if you fail?

As long as you fail because of skill...  Nope. :)  Thank God!  If you fail
because your ingredients were wrong, though, you lose everything--including
hte tin, etc.


> 3) Do you lose them if you succeed?

Nope. :D  After you put the pies (or whatever) into your inventory, the tin
(or whatnot) will reappear on your cursor.

They cost around 1pp to make, so I'd expect to pay a bit more than that.
But since you can only make htem after sinking a lotta money into pottery
or smithing, if you can afford it, be nice...Offer 5pp or something.  It's
cheaper than teaching yourself how to make them

> 5) What is a reasonable price to offer for snake eggs?

are over; I tend to offer twice that--1gp per.
