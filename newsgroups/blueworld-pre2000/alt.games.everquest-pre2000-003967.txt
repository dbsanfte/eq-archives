This is where you take a giant leap into disaster.  Frankly I
consider the mere existence of no drop items to be a HUGE problem
(rather than solving one).

Why?  Because with transferable items you have two choices in how
you get them:

1) Camp for them

2) Trade for them

Making them no drop takes away (2).  This has a number of effects:

1) It forces you to camp for them and compete with everyone else

2) It usually takes the items out of the reach of casual gamers

If you want to see how well no drop items work (or don't work as
the case is) just look at the old Drelzna camp for Jboots.  You
won't find many people espousing the joys of doing that (with all
the competition, KSing, etc).

The existence of trading allows market forces to sway the
distribution of items.

Oh and no drop has another quite nasty effect:

3) The create a huge power looting problem in the planes

In the planes, all the class specific armour (and most of the
other items) are no drop.  This means that when one drops you
have to decide who gets it before it rots.  That person is
often not the one in the group who killed it.  As such they have
to wait until anyone can loot it.  When they do, often someone
does loot it.

Now compare that to dragon raids.  On dragon raids (in my
experience) power looting is either never a problem or its
extremely rare.  Why?  Because usually one person loots all
the items and then hands them out when its been decided who
gets what.

This is orders of magnitude less aggravating and more preferable.

Frankly I don't see a reason for 95% of the no drop items in the
game being no drop.  You should need a really good justification
for making it so and I've yet to see one.
