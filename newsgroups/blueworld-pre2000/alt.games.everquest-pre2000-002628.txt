>How would it help the economy?  I don't see much of a problem, save for the
>sources of reagents.  What's wrong with the economy?

What's wrong is that there IS essentially no economy among established
players.  Most players with established accounts can make most of what they
need, and those very few things that they can't, they can easily afford
(usually from NPC vendors)  While it's NICE to be a GM smith, or whatever,
it's far from unique or special.  because most every veteran player has one.
Same goes for Gm mages, scribes, etc.  If you've played for more than 6-8
months you're probably well on your way to at least one if not more such
skills.

One character per shard means that you can't make a seperate "mule"
character to support your "adventurer"... Either the adventurer supports
himself or you must find someone else willing to help you out.  If you can't
provide everything for yourself, you must deal with others, through trade.
THIS is a dynamic economy.
