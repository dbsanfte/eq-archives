I'm trying this right now, simply due to the fact I am trying to determine
WHAT impacts Critical Hit frequency, Dexterity or Agility, because whatever
it is, I want to max out.

At first I thought it was Agility due to the effects buffing had on me in
Highpass while I was fighting orcs. I was averaging 1.5 (on the nose)
critical hits per fight against Orc Medics, and I had NEVER had a frequency
like that before. This was over 4 hours as well (about 25 medics total I
believe, I'll have to check again).

I posted here about it, and the flamethrower brigade decided I was an idiot
because it's clearly Dexterity that impacts criticals.

So I tried boosting and buffing dexterity to get the same results.

I didn't.

So I tried Agility again, and I got almost identical results as before.
Massive increase in the frequency of Critical Hits.

Lately, though I decided to try Dexterity again, if only because I've come
into possession of a LOT of Dexterity boosting items, and I'm thinking my
initial Dex test may have been a fluke (and that Agility, while it may play
a role, does not play as much of a role as Dexterity).

I've been laying down a huge percentage of criticals lately with the
Dexterity increase, far more than I thought I could ever do, and enough that
I can actually rely on them to an extent in the harder fights.

Still, most of this is anecdotal, and one day when I'm bored I'll put it to
a real test and see what happens.
