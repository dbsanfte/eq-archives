Necros rule solo. Their pet are weaker, but they have enough DoT, and
are reverse kiters (i.e. they slow and fear the mob, who crawls away

if not more exp when grouped, however. But the advantage is that, if you
haven't a group, you simply play by yourself instead of camping out in
disgust.
