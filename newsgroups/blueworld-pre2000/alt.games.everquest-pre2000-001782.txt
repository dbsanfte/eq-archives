I'll have to agree with that one.  When UO came out, I and several of
my friends signed up and started to play.  We were getting really into
the game.  My brother (who lived in another state) and I spent the
most time together that we had spent in years due to UO.

At the end of the free trial period, we had accumulated 2 houses, 3
boats, horses, strong bank accounts and very respectable characters.
We enjoyed adventuring together as a group and it was a blast except
for one thing: PK.

A group of PKers decided that they liked our spread and proceeded to
camp out at our houses.  I kid you not.  In 2 days, we had been
severely set back in experience(skills/stats) terms and had lost keys
to every posession that we own.

It was a total rip off and we all cancelled our accounts that week.
It showed a total lack of understanding of what gamers want.  Because
a vocal few want to kill other people and turn the game into
deathmatch, many had to suffer as we did.

Now I can only hope that UO has improved.  I refuse to even stray near
the newsgroup, so bad is the taste in my mouth.  For the sake of the
current and future players I hope it was made better.

When EQ hit the scene, we saw it as a chance to get into a game that
corrected this major flaw.  And it does.

In truth, we loved both games for various reasons.  I wish it could
have been different.
