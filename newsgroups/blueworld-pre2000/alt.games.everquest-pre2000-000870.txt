Okay, it's like comparing Real-Time-Strategy (RTS) to Turn-based strategy
games.   It's fun to play a turn-based game, but it'll NEVER get my blood
flowing the way a RTS will.  You can't ponder your next move, you have to
act NOW before your opponent pops you again.  If you don't time your spell
correctly, you'll get hit and the spell will be interrupted.  Geez, you're
that much closer to death!  Anyway, there's a major difference between the 2
games, as similar as they may seem.  Some people will just prefer turn-based
over RTS, but RTS is far more popular nowadays. (We're talking computer
games, not your classic board games)  The same will hold true for EQ.
