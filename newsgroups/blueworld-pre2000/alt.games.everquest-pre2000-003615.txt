What the game really needs are a couple more 35+ level areas.  That was
the primary motivation for the Splitpaw and Kithicor changes.  The game
doesn't need another low-level dungeon with good loot.  That describes
the old Splitpaw perfectly, and it was always empty.

Low level Erudites ALREADY have another excellent low level dungeon
available to them.  It's called Blackburrow.  They could already be
using  Kerra Isle, but its empty.  Its not empty because of the lack of
loot.  The loot in Blackburrow sucks rocks, too.  Its empty because
it's isolated, unlike Blackburrow, and has even worse trains.

Adding items of marginal worth MIGHT make it appealing to low-level
Erudites, but probably not.  Few tanks are going to make the trek to
Kerra Isle to help a L12 Erudite caster get an Erudite-only Feathered
Headband.  Erudite Paladins as tanks?  The only rarer class/race combo
has to be the Gnome cleric.

I hope that both the Hole and Kerra Isle wind up as 35+ zones.  Odus
would become very popular very fast. It would even help out newbie
Erudites.  They could make a killing gathering spell-reagents for busy
high-level casters.  Toxx might even attract Qeynos newbies.
