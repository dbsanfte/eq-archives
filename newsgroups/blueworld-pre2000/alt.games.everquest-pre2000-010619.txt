Necro and Enchanter? Why on earth would you do that? Clarity doesn't stack with

and a shaman will do *alot* more than 1 warrior, 1 shaman and 1 enchanter. But
if you're doing a dungeon crawl enchanters become almost essential. Every time I
hit Sebilis I want an enchanter, because those extra frogs can put a world on
hurt into you.
