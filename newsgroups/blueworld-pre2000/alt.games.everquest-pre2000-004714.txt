EQ is a Diku MUD at the core. (If you don't know what that is, ignore this-
but you should know why that's important if you've ever played one.)

It's a game that has a hundred glaring flaws. Every day there's at least one
thing that just makes my teeth clench for a few minutes.

And despite that, it's the best online RPG out today, by far. It's
addictive, immersive, and has a sense of 'this COULD be a real world' about
it, however slight.

AC isn't a terrible game, really. It's solo-friendly, it's got a decent
magic system, and the staff is 100% honest and open with their players.

And it's also the most boring, bland piece of crap I have yet to come
across. The reason there's no bitching in their newsgroup is because it's so
bland, there isn't much to improve- it's just a sterile world where you can
spend 2 hours a day running around whacking the same drudge shreth over and
over and never see another person.

I'll take EQ- even with the camping, kill stealing, and ninja looting- any
day, thanks.
