McKlusky would have been yellow to the guy at 33.  Also, the 
"Even" con level should have been level 35, a hell level.  This

> One interesting thing from this is that killing 1.5 risky blues is the

You are forgetting something in that analysis.  What you can draw
from this experiment (assuming that the numbers are close to real)
is that killing a mob when it is red brings you 1.5 times closer 
to the next level than killing that same mob when it is blue.

Two factors can make your deduction incorrect:

1) You need more experience for each level.

   The difference in the number of McKlusky kills could be due to
   the increased experience requirements for subsequent levels,
   rather than any adjustment to experience awards based on your 
   relative levels.  I imagine that quest rewards are constant, so
   you could calibrate levels by repeating a quest.

2) Higher level monsters give more experience.

   At any level, any red con creature is at least four levels above
   any blue con creature, so it may give lots more experience.
