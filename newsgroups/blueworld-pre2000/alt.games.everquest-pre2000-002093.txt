As far as your question is concerned we were largely fed up with the 
prevalence of Pk'n in the game. I've never been a fan of non-
consentual Pk'n. I see other peoples point that it adds an "edge" of 
sorts (assuming you're of that mind set), but I've always preferredd 
to engage in PvP combat, not Pk'n. I find the prior much more 
rewarding and a lot more "sporting" as it were. That's a personal 
preference issue obviously, but as it turns out most in my guild 
viewed things in a very similar light.

Moreover, there were a lot of related problems that stemmed from 
this concentration on an open Pk environment. Many of which you 
mention above. With an open Pk environment the jerk-factor is 
increased by a substantial magnitude and you had no way of shutting 
them out. They could attack, verbally or physically and there was 
nothing you could do but fight back or run. The minute you logged 
in, you were on the defensive in every aspect of your game play. 
That was unacceptable imo.

By appealing to this crowd you also, by default, bring in the type 
of people that will do everything possible to get the upper hand on 
you. Heck, the game was such that you needed to have that mind set 
to survive the next Pk assault. OSI propagated the problem largely 
by paying it only passing lip service. Cheats thus became the norm 
and the arms (read macro) race was on.

So there were a lot of related aspects that drove us away, but the 
prevalence of Pk'n was certainly the underlying factor.
