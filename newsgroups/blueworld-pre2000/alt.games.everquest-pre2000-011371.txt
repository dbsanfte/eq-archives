As Bruce said, all the servers are in California, U.S.A., so the geography
doesn't really matter.

Main thing to look at is how crowded the servers are: try to pick one that
has a low population.  The 'recommended' servers, shown in green on the
server selection screen, are the ones that Verant feels have the most room
for new players.

Note: if you plan on playing with friends, make sure you all start your
characters on the same servers.  Once you start a character, you normally
won't be able to move it to a different server.
