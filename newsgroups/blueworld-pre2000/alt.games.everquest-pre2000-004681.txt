Playing a druid and a ranger, I have a bunch of experience with this
skill. Being a game developper IRL, I maybe can "feel" how things
work.

There are bugs that affect tracking making them appear as if the skill
was bugged, here is a little list of the bugs I have noticed and I can
explain by observation (this is theory, I may be wrong).

* Well, a general bug that is more like a network limitation is that
the client gets info on the position of a limited amount of creatures,
specifically creatures that are within a certain radius of the PC. Not
doing that would drastically increase the internet traffic.
Consequence on traking info is that you get directions to the
simulated position of the creature which may be different of the
actual position server side. Usually going there makes you get info on
the real position as the creature is in the "info update" radius
server side and info is sent.

*West karana: there is a specific bug which make creatures appear and
disappear, and it seems like tracking is bugged. The creature is
straight ahead and the second after looks like being behind you, then
straight ahead again. You cannot track efficiently in this zone unless
you have seen the creature at first.

*West commons: ever heard of the "ghost" griffin or hill giant? hehe
only trackers know they exist and can kill them in west commons. Track
the ghost griffin on track (it looks like a regular griffin), then it
appears as there is nothing where that bird SHOULD be. He is there,
you simply dont see it because he is high in the sky, maybe under the
world, or maybe it is the same bug that in SolA where one PC is
absolutely invisible for another PC. And he does not attack (though
maybe he was responsible of my death once when I was medding and did
not even see what killed me). But you can check that he is there by
using another bug, the fact that a 2 dimensionnal check is used to see
which creatures are affected by an area damage spell: cast Tremor or
Earthquacke right where he should be, and he will attack, and hunt you
until you kill him or zone. You can even cast spells on him, and he
stays invisible.

Well, these are minor bugs.
