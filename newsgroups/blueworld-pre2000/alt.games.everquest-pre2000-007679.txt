as "beetch" or "biotch" for emphasis.  Personally, I would object 
to Bitch, Bich, Beetch, Beatch, Beech, or Biyotch on similar 
grounds.  I somehow doubt that the guy chose to name his wood elf 
Beach because he likes to surf.  Not only is it a loose homonym for 
Bitch, but it's also a pretty stupid name.
