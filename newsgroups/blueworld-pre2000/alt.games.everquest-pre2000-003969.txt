Hm ...

It's funny that the two biggest problems with EQ are caused by its
phenomenal success.

Pretty much all of the other problems you could think of are caused
by overcrowding.  People claim spawn points, steal kills, and are 
often just annoyed with each other because they are all competing
for limited resources.

My other complaints are minor.  Although I think the game is great 
and I enjoy it a lot, it can always be improved. :)

There are a few minor balance issues left, but the classes and 
monsters seem pretty balanced in general.

There are a couple of rendering issues, like fog being too thick
and light sources not illuminating the world very well.

The UI is a bit annoying to use.  It requires the mouse at times
for no good reason.  Customization is very limited.

There are not many options for customizing your character's 
appearance or abilities.
