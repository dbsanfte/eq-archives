I never really saw the downside to the PK switch myself. I think it
would remove the aggrivation factor for quite a few players and greatly
reduce the rampant anti-social FPK/NPK.

The "thrill" of walking around in a dangerous world is a moot point. My
personal "bad" expieriences of Pk activity out number the "good" about 6
to 1. Most of the time you fight someone because they are attacking you
because your grey, or they greatly outnumber you, or they irritated you
to the point that you don't care and you just want them to die and leave
you alone. Very, very few instances do I say "Damn, I died, but that was
one hell of a fight!" Oddly enough I think this more common in the
Dreads vs Lords days.

The only people pking hurts are those who are not even vaguely
interested in PvP. Having 6 GM Mage Pks spending all night, every night
wiping out miners gets rather old. What it does is reward those who play
24/7 who have nothing better to do. The pk who want to stay blue simply
use the "piss somone off til they attack you" tactic. Kill stealing,
looting, vulgarities, etc... This gets pretty damn old too. Escpecailly
because they have nothing to loose (they either get loot/fame, kill
you, or get to put a muder count on you).

The bottom line is, PK activity only benifits the PKs. It contributes
next to nothing for the many, many other players, they piss off,
aggrivate, loot and kill.

Its self-destructive for the game. Ask some who quit UO why they left?
Go to comp.sys.imb.games.rpg and ask around. I'd say 1/2 of the people
who left UO did so because of PK activity of some kind. The other half
left because they got tired of pking...

I seriously doubt UO will ever have a Pk switch, so all of you NPKs out
there can loot and steal until you vomit. The option of a Non-PK shard
is a long shot as well. It would be too easy to abuse the current system
in the horrid format it is in right now, not to mention the bug that it
would induce. So, I guess it really doesn't matter. Hell, why did I even
write this post?
