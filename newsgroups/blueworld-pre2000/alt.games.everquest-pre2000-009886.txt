Jerry wrote:
> 
> Oh hell...let them beg.  Some people are more charitable than others. 
> I don't have a problem with them.

It's when you say no and they berate you for it that it becomes a problem.
When you say no and they say:
	"Well, I guess you are just an asshole"
	"Sorry, I thought you were a nice person"
	"You're level 30 and don't even have 5pp to spare?  You suck!"
it makes you feel bad, even though you know you are right.

Also, there are a lot of people who are dishonest in their begging - 
starting new characters for the sole purpose of begging cash or taking 
off their armor and claiming to have lost their corpse.  If you help a 
person who is not asking for it, you are far more likely to find 
someone who is honest.  People like to help others because it makes
them feel good.  When you find you have been duped into assisting
someone who doesn't really need it, you don't feel so good.


Charity and giving to beggars are not the same thing.  I like to give 
things to people who don't ask.  I always say no to beggars.

I've given out lots of small pieces of jewelry to people around level
10-15 who looked wholly untwinked.  I know that jewelry is a bottom 
level priority after spells, weapons and armor, but it is still fun
to have some useful rings and earrings.

A couple days ago, someone was asking for a bind in Kelethin.  I 
said I would come bind him and then saw that he was a level 12 
Magician.  I said, "Sorry, I must be confused.  You can bind yourself."
No, he couldn't, because he couldn't afford the spell.  So I bought
the spell and came to meet him at the orc lift.  The payment I 
requested was that he bind others who need it.  If he had been asking
for money for spells, I would have ignored him entirely.
