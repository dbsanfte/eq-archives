>option of a PvP-free server, you have limited the game options and also
>limited your pool of potential customers.  Neither seems wise to me.

The pattern I would predict for a a nonPvP world would be huge initial
demand, forcing opening of a bunch of worlds to cater to it (actually,
more likely, conversion of existing worlds, which means that the
current style of play vanishes), followed by lower retention rates
overall and an inability to go back to the previous model.

Remember, the money is in retention, not in demand.

Which is why I think the right answer is to support both within a
single world (and note that this does NOT mean a switch, as far as I
am concerned), so that players who tire of the safe gameplay can try
the current gameplay without starting over.
