I believe the expression is "If it aint broke, don't fix it".  People seem to
enjoy hunting in South Karana and High Keep, why mess those zones up?

Instead see why people like them and make some of the other zones more like
them.  The way to
spread people around is to make them want to hunt the under utilized zones; Not
to make the popular zones suck so much that the other zones look good by
comparison.

I haven't spent to much time in South Karana but I did spend some time in High
Keep, it was fun to play.  Convenient binding for a melee character, convenient
merchant and banking, and if you get in to a balanced group that pays attention
to details you can get decent experience/loot for the level you can hunt there.
When you die (and you will) you get back up run to your corpse and get back to
playing, instead of running naked through half an hour to get your corpse.

One would hope that Verant would look at the zones people enjoy playing in (most
of the people playing, play for enjoyment (the rest are earning money farming
for ebay)), and make more of them.  But unfortunately the tuning team has
probably all ready decided to solve the problem by nerfing the fun zones.  I am
probably being overly cynical, but that is the way I read Gordon Wrinn's
statement "and/or decrease the draw of the favorite camping spots"; if that
doesn't sound like a nerf in progress I don't know what does.

Good luck and good hunting.
