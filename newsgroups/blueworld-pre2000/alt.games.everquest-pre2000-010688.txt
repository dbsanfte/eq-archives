Nope.  Its a not a bug but a design problem.  The code is done exactly as
designed.  A bug is defined as an error in the program, and not working as
design.  It has nothing to do with predictable results at all.

In fact, given the design, the hell level should be predicted.  Whether the
designer has predicted it beforehand is not the issue.

And there is little to gain for taking away the hell levels.  They could just
spread out the xp required to all levels and make it seem like the hell level
is gone.  But you would still need the same amount of xp to get up to the next
hell level (eg from 30-35)
