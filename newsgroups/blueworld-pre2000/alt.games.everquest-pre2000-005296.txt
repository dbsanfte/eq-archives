Cool!  That is interesting. :)

It's almost enough to get me to run ShowEQ to try to figure 
it out. ;)  

Yesterday, my level 30 Necromancer friend was grouped with a 
level 30 Troll Warrior.  They both started out one bubble away
from level 30.  The Necro got about half a bubble while the 
Warrior got a full bubble.  Our theory for that situation was
that he was not out-damaging his pet and so was only getting
half the experience.

The only other explanation I can think of for grossly disparate
advancement is that some of the kills are made by the tank out
of experience range of the caster, but that was not the case for
the Warrior/Necromancer team yesterday.

It does sound like there is a bug in the code.  Either that or
the experience tables for different classes are not related by
a simple multiplicative factor.  If the tanks' experience tables
flatten out more than casters' at high levels, then you would
also see this effect.
