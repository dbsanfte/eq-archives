I don't think it' s fair to say that UO sucks. It has an enourmous amount of
history to it that had come before it in all the various Ultima games...Give
Lord British come credit. The guy made his universe the way he wanted it to
be. Respect atleast that.

Personally, i think EQ is the most captivating online rpg there is for
having such atmosphere, whereas Asheron's Call is the most unique. The
alliance system has a lot of potential once the bugs are ironed out and the
story and plot is worked on. Heavily.
Now given, UO does have an issue with player killing, which, if you wish to
not deal with such things then i would heartily reccommend EverQuest or
Asheron's Call (once it is released)
UO is an interesting world nonetheless. I actually like the world of
Britannia, I think it is highly captivating, as is Norrath (EQ)
I would not say the same of Dereth (Asheron's Call) which although it
contains a very well thought out combat system, the world itself is
somewhat...boring.
In the end the decision is yours...we can only tell you about it.
If you are a true RPG/CRPG fan then you'll know that what matters is not the
graphics but rather the gameplay. Gameplay can always save the day no matter
waht the game is.
