Cassiopeia wrote:
> 
> Wolf form is not an illusion.

It is as much an illusion as any of the Enchanter illusions.  They also
give extra properties: strength, infravision, ultravision, regeneration,
levitation, water breathing, and resistances.  They should more properly
be called polymorphs.  The only major difference between wolf form and
the Enchanter illusions is that you can cast wolf form on others.

John is wrong.  Wolf form and all enchanter illusions do the same thing 
as far as faction goes.  They reset the base faction for your race.  
Your earned reputation and religion adjustments are added to it.  If you 
kill a lot of Ogres and go visit Oggok under illusion, you will get 
exactly the same reaction that a real Ogre would get if he had been 
killing Ogres.  The bug was that base racial faction for wolves was very 
high with lots of monsters that were supposed to be hostile to everyone.
