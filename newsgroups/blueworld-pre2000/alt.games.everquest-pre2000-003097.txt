OK, here's why rec.* groups are better than alt.* groups:

1) Alt groups aren't carried by as many ISP's. They tend (in general) to have 
more spam, off-topic postings, random binaries, etc. 

News administrators are *much* more likely to add an officially recognized 
rec.* group than an alt.* group... including possible future groups within the 
hierarchy like rec.games.everquest.classes.paladins. 

So, more people will have access to a rec.* group than an alt.* group, and it 
gives us more flexibility in dividing them up later.

Also, since more ISP's carry rec.* groups than alt.* groups, each individual 
post will get peered (sent around) to more news servers, which means that posts 
will generally show up quicker.

2) Alt.* groups generally have a lower retention time, for much the same 
reason... some ISP's even group ALL alt.* groups in with alt.binaries.* and 
drop retention down to a day or two.

Is that all clear enough? Basically, more people will be able to read the 
group, posts will show up in less time, and if they're away for a couple of 
days, they'll be able to catch up on what they missed. It's a good thing all 
around.
