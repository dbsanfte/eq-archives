Personally, I think that's a horrible idea.  Look, I'm not much for
role-playing, but the idea of some magical eBay that exists in the game is
stupid.  I don't deny this would be a tool for making buy/selling faster and
easier for the player.  But for me, it would ruin the whole point of a
medieval fantasy genre game -- the bartering system.

There's a concept known as "Price Skimming."  Basically, its the technical
phrase for the old concept of "sell for what the market will bare."  If
someone's willing to pay 300pp for my rusty longsword, then by god I'm gonna
sell to that twink for 300pp.  Is it worth 300p?  even 3pp?  No!  I know
that.  But if someone will buy it...who am I to argue!  THAT is the attitude
of most people who play EQ.  And I think it's good.

There's nothing greedy about this at all.  A person looking to buy items
will look for the best deal.  Likewise, a person selling items will look for
the best deal possible.  It's just as easy to say, "You only want to buy my
BBC at 10pp instead of 90pp because you're selfish and greedy."  If someone
wants to buy an item at a very low price, aren't they greedy too?  That's
the basis of Wall Street..buy low sell high.

And for what it's worth, this is the reason eqtrader.com has come about.
There are people who wanted to establish fair and standard prices for
high-use items, to slow "twink-inflation."  Lot's of little EQ-Alan
Greenspans running around.
