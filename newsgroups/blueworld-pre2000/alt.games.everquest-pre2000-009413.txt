I find the anonymous flag annoying.  It is why many people do not 
use the /roleplay flag, despite the fact that they do roleplay.
Being anonymous hurts roleplaying because it hides some of the few
externally identifiable aspects of your character.  Also, many 
people use the /anon and /roleplay flags to hide.  If I /who a 
character and they come up anon, I take that as a "Do not disturb" 
sign and leave them alone.

I do answer bind requests and other reasonable requests from anon
players, just as I answer requests from people with silly, but not
truly objectionable, names.  I don't let any request for a bind go
for very long before I offer to stop what I am doing and come bind
the person, unless they have a catastrophically bad name.  (Sorry,
but if your name is Phallus, you can just get a bind from someone
else.)

However, I don't cast Clarity or other random buffs on anonymous 
people and I can't really judge whether they need help in a fight 
unless they call for it.  If I see a level 15 troll warrior getting 
hit by a hill giant, I will try to save him by charming or fearing 
the giant.  If I see a level 40 troll warrior getting hit by a hill 
giant, I figure he is probably ok and I will just slow the giant and
haste the troll.  If I see an anonymous troll fighting a giant, I 
give up in disgust and just hope he doesn't die, because the stupid
giant will probably kill me if I make the wrong judgment call.
