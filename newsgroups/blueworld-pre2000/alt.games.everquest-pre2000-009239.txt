> My Bard (Human) is currently LV 4. Some of the skills have gone up as
> I am using them, like Singing (21), Offense (20), 1H-Slashing (24),
> Defense (15) etc.
> 
> My question is: which skill should I train with the practise points I
> get when going up a level?
> 
> It seems to be a wast of resources to train the skills I regularly use
> as they go up anyway(see above), but what other skills do you
> recommend?

My suggestions are the same for any class:

I would recommend that you train five or ten points in any new weapon
skills you pick up after about level 8, or more if you take them up
later.

If you haven't been working on your Sense Heading, put five points
there to get it started and then start hitting it as you run around.

Training 20 points in a trade skill can save you some time and effort
when you take up a trade.

I would never bother putting points into Offense, Defense, or your 
primary weapon type.
