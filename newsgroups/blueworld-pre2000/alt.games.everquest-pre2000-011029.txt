My primary is a 26 ranger.  Most of the points are valid, but a couple things
to consider:

1) If you're a wood elf ranger, the gnoll fang quest requires going a LONG
way through some very dangerous places.  This is imfeasible for a first char
without a guide.  There is no happy replacement for a wood elf ranger on 
Faydwer for this quest.

2) Rangers level slower.  Quite a bit slower.

3) Rangers are more difficult to play.  For the first few levels, not
significantly; but at level 6 a warrior has bash.. he gets dodge around there
somewhere, too.. by 12 he has dual wield.. and he never really worries about
more than whether he should be bashing and one hand wielding, wielding 2hs, or
dual wielding.  At some point to be more useful than a warrior, a ranger must
develop some tactical sense about spell use vs. fighting, etc..

Still, on the assumption that the char might not make it beyond 10 since it's
a first char, the suggestions are reasonably sound.  Of course, under this
assumption a ranger will also have little to offer over a warrior 
(Except not being mauled by random wandering animals, which is DEFINITELY a 
plus)
