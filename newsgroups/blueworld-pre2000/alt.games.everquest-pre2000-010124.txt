Soloing with an Enchanter is easy; let's define what IS soloing:
if you mean smashing mobs to gain XPs, well, en Enchanter is bad in this;
if you mean discovering new zones, visiting many places, speak with other
races everywhere, well, then an Enchanter can do it starting lvl 4.

Let me explain why:

at lvl 4 an Enchanter gets invis, so he can travel to everywhere he likes to
( I was in Oasis at lvl 4 and other dangerous zones);
with 'illusion human' even a dark elf can visit every town in Norrath;
with 'Gate' and/or 'Color Flux' you can save his butt from nearly every
mistake.

In order  to travel far away, I use to leave my equipment in the bank, just
taking iron rations and milk... if my corpse get lost, I don't worry about
it.

This way I saw locations every other class could dream of at lvl 4.

Well, the drawback is that I gained absolutely no XPs for about 10 hours of
game play. Hum.
