Reductio ad absurdum. :) What I was describing was achieving a broader
perspective on systemic relationships as they affect play. As a
player, a fireball, a thrown grenade, and a crossbow bolt are merely
visual differences to me--they are all missile attacks. In terms of
thinking strategically, if I like staying out of monster's attack
ranges, being a mage, a grenadier, or an archer is a largely
superficial difference to me. I decide on the basis of the other
mechanics available.

Over time, players tend to abstract out the descriptions of the
various play mechanics down to a very few archetypes. Even in
class-based systems, even in AD&D, you see people saying, "well, I'm a
tank type" or "I'm a shoot from behind type" as they discuss
formations and order of battle. The greater the experience with the
systems, the less the details matter.
