>Your second saying here leads to your first.. So which is it? Can you or
>cant you zone to avoid death? 

Actually it doesn't. Examples:

Case 1 : I'm fighting a blue mob. Then some of its friends come to help and 
I know I can't take all of them so I zone. Not an exploit.

Case 2 : I see a yellow mob I *might* be able to solo. I pull the mob to 
the zone and fight there. If I can't win I zone. Exploit.

If I intentionally pull the mob to the zone so I can run if I need to is an 
exploit, but just running away so I don't die isn't.
So, to anwer your question... Yes, you can zone to avoid death, but if you 
pull the mob to the zone so you *can* zone is an exploit.
