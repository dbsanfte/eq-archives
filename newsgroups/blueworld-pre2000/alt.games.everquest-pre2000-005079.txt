I agree with what the original poster said... maybe his exact changes that
he mentioned wouldn't be the best things in the world for the game, but his
point was right on the money.   Everquest is supposed to be a role playing
game... but there is almost no role playing.  For one thing, EQ panders to
the power gamers.   Power gamers make the most noise and complain the most
and basically whine so much that Verant has to listen to them at least a
litte bit.  The other reason there is so little role playing though, is that
there is nothing to RP with... I mean if I want to create my own little RP I
can do that, but hell I can do that in a chat room.   The truth is that
nothing ever changes in EQ.  It is just as the original poster said... you
go out and kill stuff until you don't get xp any more then you go kill
something different... and so on and so forth.  Even the quests (which there
aren't nearly enough of) are stupid and static... they never change.  I can
do a quest 100 times and it just keeps being the same thing.   There need to
be a lot more quests... a lot more DYNAMIC quests, and although it is more
work on Verant's part, there need to be more GM run quests... and I mean GM
run quests that we can all take part in.  Just taking a zillion weenie
skeletons and having them over-run a city does not constitute a GM quest
IMHO.    We need story lines and activities to make this game real.
Honestly, if it weren't for the great friends I've made in my travels
through Norrath I probably would have left out of sheer boredom before I
ever got to level 20...

Eachann Bronzeleaf
Cleric of Tunare, Tarew Marr
