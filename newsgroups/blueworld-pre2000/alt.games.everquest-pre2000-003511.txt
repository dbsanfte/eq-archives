There are several different methods of looting out there, but the most common method
and the most generally accepted is the following:

You alpha loot to some money limit or item equivalent (e.g. alpha with 4pp or Fine
Steel) is reaced or exceeded. If old group members leave and new group members join,
they are placed in the list as their name is located. If they are next in the list
(luck of the draw), they get to go. If the list has passed them up (unluck of the
draw), they will have to wait until the list returns to them. All major and rare
spawn items are randomed (/random 100) with the winner removed from the random
should a group get lucky with another major or rare spawn until everyone in the
group has received an item.

Now there are some disadvantages to this method such as the people at the end of the
alpha list could be left out should the group completely dissolve. These
disadvantages are defeated or are unimportant in the long run. If the group is not
together for any length of time, then it is not only the X, Y, and Z that are not
getting to loot but just about everyone but the first one or two in the list. To
correct this, stay in a group longer. If the length being in a group is not short
and people are still not getting to loot, reduce the money limit. If you are in a
dungeon that the mobs give you usually only gold and silver, you should not be
having a 4pp or 5pp limit. The front of Mistmoore or the upper floors of Unrest are
like this. They cannot support a 4pp or 5pp limit. The basement of Unrest or the
castle of Mistmoore, on the otherhand, can easily handle 4pp to 8pp loot limits. I
really depends on the area that you are hunting. Another example is, Kedge Keep is a
free for all on everything but the major items which are random. The reason for this
is that the general loot of Kedge Keep is useless (mostly worthless cheep spears,
fresh fish, and scales).

Just my 2cp
