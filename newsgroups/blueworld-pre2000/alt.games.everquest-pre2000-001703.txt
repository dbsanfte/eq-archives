Well, I have an account on UO as well as EQ. Personally, I think that these
two games cannot even be compared to each other as they are completely
different. The only thing they have in common is that they paly over the
net.

I have to admit that at first, EQ did not excite me. It seemed that there
was much less to the game than I would expect for my $50. It has a cheasy
paper map and cheap looking maps in the book. UO has a cloth map (If you
bought that version) and well written book.

After, giving EQ it's fair chance (about 10-12 hours of game play), I have
to admit that it is rather addicting. it is very difficult to keep a new
character alive and rather frustrating having to go find your body each and
every time you die so you can get your stuff back. However, once you get
into the groove of it and get the chat stuff down, it is rather fun. I find
the users on EQ are much friendlier that on UO and I had a lot of fun
because of it.

However I will not get rid of either account. When I am playing one, my
daughter is playing the other. It works for me.

UO, however has a very good interface, but the world was so obviously huge I
was intimidated at first. Then when I got the hang of things, I liked the
game better. UO already had an edge with me as I have played and solved
every single Ultima game ever written (Including Acalabeth). But still, it
is hard to get from point A to point B without getting PK'ed (which is one
thing I loved about EQ - I can turn off Pk'ing).

Anyway, for those of you who are fighting about how EQ is so much better
that UO (or visa versa). Don't bother, it's like saying "I like my dog so
much better than my chair". Give me a break!!! Two different creatures
altogether!!!

Well, my two cents worth.
