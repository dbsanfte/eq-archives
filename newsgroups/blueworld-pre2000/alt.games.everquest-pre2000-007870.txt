I agree.  Realize that Verant's "vision" based decisions to reduce the

Agreed on the Verant sales.  OTOH, some people already think of getting
items in terms of  "hourly wages".  After all you can figure approximately
how long it takes you to get an item (bypassing the issue of whether you
really "need" it here).  For example, it is possible to trade a GBS for two
SSOYs, maybe not common, but possible.  Do I spend X hours camping for it
with my warrior or do I fight the crowds and lines in LGuk for the dubious
pleasure of lottoing for the items _if_ I happen to get lucky enough for
them to drop when I happen to be there and in the correct group.
    If I have limited playing time and sufficient disposable income, the
case can be made for working on experience and picking up the items from an
auction site.  Players have suggested and begged Verant for questable
_equivalent_ items for those who cannot devote large chunks of time to
camping, Verant disclaims interest.  Therefore, the cash auctions continue
and will continue whether any of us like it or not.
