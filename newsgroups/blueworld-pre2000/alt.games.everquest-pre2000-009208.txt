What you're forgetting is that all magical damage require mana--that
means you have to factor in the time to regen the used mana.  Even in
melee damage, you're using mana (healing/buffs/debuffs).  But the
difference here is that the damage generated per point of mana for
melee damage is FAR greater than damage generated per point of mana
for magical damage.

This is why melee damage is always more efficient than magical damage
over time.
