Jason Dozier wrote:
> 
> How is this done.  I've seen people running along with a corpse sliding
> along the ground after them, and heard people mention it on this ng, but I
> have never heard the command to do so.

The command is /corpse.

If you have a body in the zone that is close to you and in line of sight,
it will be transported to directly under your feet.  People put /corpse 
on a social and put the social on a hot key so that they can run through
a zone, dragging their corpse behind them.

If someone else has consented you to loot their corpse (via the /consent
command) you can target their corpse and drag it in a similar fashion.
