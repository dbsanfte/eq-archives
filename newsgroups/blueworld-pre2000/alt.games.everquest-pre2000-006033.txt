Why flame? You just didn't know...
Twinking means providing a character with equipment that he would not be 
able to get at his level in any sane amount of time. It can be done by a 
higher level character of the same person, guild, friend, stranger, 
whatever.
Different people accept it to a different extent. I can't classify it as 
twinking if a stranger gave you something nice when you helped him. My 
bard got her bronze pants and boots for helping some level 40ish guy to 
find his corpse in Kithkor for example. Some people manage to twink 
themselves via complicated trades/begging/scheming (whether good or bad 
is beyond the point). A level 5 rogue borrowed 50 pp from me once. A 
week later he found me and gave me my money back (I was amazed), he 
traded it up to be a full suit of banded, some jewelry, and a dragood 
dirk
