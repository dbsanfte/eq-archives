As your primary role is tank, AC is first, HP just behind (better
not be hit at all rather than be able to survive more hits and drain
more mana out of your friends later). STR is distant, unless you need
STR to carry armor and loot.

Roughly, 3 AGI points are worth about 1 AC, so if you find an item
with 1 less AC than your objective, and +3 agi, you may pick it.

(agility causes your ennemi to miss - that's the first half of the "AC"
 value displayed, along with Defense skill - while AC causes you to suffer
 less damage when hit - that's the second half of the AC value)
