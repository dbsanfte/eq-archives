People seem to confuse melee and tank.

The purpose of a "melee" character is to deal damage for the group. To
achieve that, you need STR. Lots of. And the best dmg/delay ratio. And
DEX if it is a procing weapon.

That's a valid play style at low-to-mid levels.

The purpose of a "tank" character is to absorb damage for the group. To
achieve that, you get the most AC you can, then the most HP you can (to
survive) *and* the highest possible taunt skill you can. Why taunt?
Because it's your purpose to be hit, not the caster behind you. It's as
defining of the class as the rest.
