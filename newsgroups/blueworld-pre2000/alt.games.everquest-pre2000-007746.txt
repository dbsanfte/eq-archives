The missing factor is that you can accumulate hate without accumulating
kill credit.  The hate list is the kill credit list, but it holds two
numbers.  A damage shield adds hate without adding kill credit, in the
same way that healing or buffing a combatant adds hate without adding 
kill credit.

I think the hate list has eight entries.  If someone produces aggro
in the mob, they are added at the end of the list.  After eight people
(or mobs) are on the list, it starts overwriting from the top.  Since
mobs are constantly getting angry with people merely for being nearby,
the list is continually being flushed.  The killing blow will always 
be on the hate list when the final kill credit is computed, and it 
will often be the only entry with any kill credit, so killing blows 
will often get credit in crowded situations.  If the killing blow was 
from a damage shield, then there may be no player with non-zero kill 
credit on the hate list and the corpse will poof.
