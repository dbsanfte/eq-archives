Well, if you're looking into the "spirit" of EQ, I guess you really
couldn't do better than to read some TSR books based on the AD&D game.
The Drizzt books by Salvatore are actually surprisingly good.  Be
warned, tho', a great many of the books relating to AD&D are great big
steaming piles of crap.
Some will tell you to try the "Wheel of Time" series by Jordan.. not
really sure if you should start in on something that freakin' huge to
start off with.  The first few books were great, but after a while,
you just begin to wonder if Jordan couldn't have made everyoine happy
and just ended the damned series 4 or 5 books in.  Almost a chore to
read the books at times.
The "Sword of Truth" series by Goodkind is good.  Kinda like the
"Wheel of Time" books, but it doesn't meander all over the place, and
the action is kept at a pretty good clip.  Still, any book that's
around 1000 pages is about 500 pages too damned long, IMHO.
If you're looking for something different, (and not so damned
long-winded) try out the Chronicles of Thomas Covenant by Stephen
Donaldson.  Although the series really isn't what you'd call "Classic"
fantasy, The Land is an amazing fantasy world.  And while the "hero"
of the series isnt' very likeable at first, but seeing him transform
his character through the series is really worth reading.
Of course, I'd reccomend Heinlein's Glory Road  to anyone who likes
fantasy material.  Very different, very good, and an under rated
classic.
