I have used hail to indicate the incoming target, I will start
using it more when assisting.

I find that in laggy situations, assist will often not take.
This is more true if there are a lot of targets around.  It is
less useful in blackburrow for this reason.

Also, sometimes I will /assist before the lead tank gets their
autoattack going.

My cleric has a hot-key that just reads /assist.  when grouped
she will use the F1 F2 F3 keys to target group members, then F2
to get to the lead thank and numeric 2 to assist them again.
This combination is more robust than typing the name of the
lead puller every time a pick-up group changes.

Of course, you can also type /assist once and then use shift-up
to call up the command a second time in a group situation.
