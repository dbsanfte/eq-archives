   After spending most the day trying out games with the Monster 400mx with
the Sensaura EAX emulation/Direct3d sound, and spending the time since
posting this thread until now, trying to figure out what made Drakan so
noise prone, I found out the source. Apparently when CD music is played it
causes a static popping problem. Other games I do not really notice this. I
do however still notice a low level of popping between sound start/finishes
which are most likely driver issues (for example in Quake 3, hit esc, go to
the main menu and just run the mouse cursor up and down the selection of
choices, you will hear alot of popping as many sounds are started and
stopped over and over, this is not an issue in the mx300 at all). So, with
this card so far, you cannot play CD music with Drakan, and high saturation
of sound samples (not sure if all on same channel or not, depends on how the
game is designed, and not sure how this card handles those specifically)
under the current driver, have a little popping and static.
