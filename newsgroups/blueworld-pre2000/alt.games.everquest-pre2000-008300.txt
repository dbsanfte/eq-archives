My cleric secondary was grouped with a rogue last night.  My
first question was whether he could backstab yet (the answer
was no, so I did not stun the non-casters).  His first question
was whether we were OK with pickpocketing.  We said sure,
please share the cash from time to time.  Apparantly he had
been having trouble getting folks to agree to the pickpocket
because his skill was very low.  He got about 4 coppers.  This
was on Orcs in the East Commons and I at least was hunting for
experience.

But he got very excited when I told him about the person who
pickpocketed D'Vinn's heart and said that any GOOD rogue could
get away with it.
