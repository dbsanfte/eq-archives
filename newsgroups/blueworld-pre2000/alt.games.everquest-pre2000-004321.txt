>I saw many people try to start guilds in UO because they wanted to be at

>belonging.  Inevitably, those higher in the organization get more of a vote
>often giving the guild leader dictatorial power over his peers.  I find the

Didn't you ever play D&D or some other over-the-table rpg?  If so,
didn't any of your characters ever aspire to rule their own kingdoms?
Empire building is one of many valid goals in fantasy gaming, and it
certainly has its precedents in fantasy literature.  I think the
biggest limitation of the current crop of multiplayer rpgs is that the
support for goals other than monster bashing is weak. In that sense
they haven't advanced much beyond the multiplayer rpgs popular on
timesharing systems in the early to mid '80s.  The phenomenon of
tinpot guild dictators could just be players trying to kluge the
system to allow them to break out of the "kill monsters to get
exp. and items so you can kill bigger monsters to get more exp and
items..." cycle.  The next logical step would be to allow a band of
heroes to "liberate" a region that has come under the sway of some
such miscreant.
