I have spent 2 evenings in Unrest so far, and I intend to go
back.

Things for the original poster to remember:

get in a group - there are pick-up groups getting together all
the time.  Any fight in the yard will pick up 2 or 3 death
beetles and low-level undead.  A group likes this, a soloist
dies to it.

Do NOT spend any time anywhere near the train tracks.  Run in,
follow the walls, get to the back of the house.  I like the
left side and back wall better than the pagoda just because
something about the pagoda area really kills the framerate on
my computer.  Stay very close to the walls unless fighting.

The front of the zone is a death trap because all the
over-pulls go out the door and to the zone.  How many ghouls
can you solo?
