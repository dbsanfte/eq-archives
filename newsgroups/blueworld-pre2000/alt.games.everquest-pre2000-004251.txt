For Elven names, you can't beat Tolkien.  This web site has a great
summary of Tolkien's elven languages and some useful vocabulary:

http://www.uib.no/People/hnohf/sindarin.htm
http://www.uib.no/People/hnohf/vocab.htm

I spent a while picking a name for Xymarra.  I wanted a Sindarin name
that described her occupation as a jeweler.  I chose Elen-gariel, which
means "star holder".

When picking a name, I think it's important to decide what really defines
your character.  Then choose a name that reflects your feelings.
