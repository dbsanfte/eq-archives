But with a good enchanter you would only be fighting 1 mob at a time,
and a cleric and druid are usually enough to keep a ranger healthy
against any single enemy.

Do you use Harmony? Cazic is a great place to learn how to use it, since
nothing there has a special resistance to it, nothing can see camo so
you can scout, etc.
Also, with an enchanter you can pull 2 mobs at once without losing much
efficiency, so there is room for error.


>enchanter mezzes, speeds ranger, debuffs mobs

>we need more practice at it)
Against tough single targets when you have a few stunners it can often
be better mana efficiency to chain-stun rather than heal (esp with the 2
wizards), so that is worth practicing.

>problems I have noted:
>not enough beat-down for efficiency (we NEED a rogue)
There are quite a few places where there are animals around (they don't
even have to be your level, 5 or 10 below is still useful) so you can
add a tank there, enchanter pet will start doing more significant damage
soon (completely outdamage the poor little ranger).
Also, with Breeze/Clarity/etc in most situations the limiting factor
will be what you can pull, not waiting for casters to get mana back :)


>when the chanter gets resisted, life gets brutal and short
>cleric, stun-wizard and chanter NEED good timing (we are
>getting better at it) for all 3 to survive the fight
Have the ranger concentrate more on taunting off the casters than trying
to do damage, in the bowl it's fairly easy to use the outside view so
you can keep an eye on what's happening.
Just remember when getting mobs off the enchanter to only use taunt,
don't hit the mob :)
Also remember to taunt a few times before breaking a mob out of mez,
then have a taunt ready to land right after mez breaks.



>Any tactical thoughts that I could pass on to the others?
>
>ps - it can get surprisingly hard to take a lizard that broke
>charm and taunt it off the enchanter.
This one has many solutions, but here are the ones probably best suited
to your group

Best is before the charm breaks, taunt the mob a few times so that at
least one will succeed, now even tho the mob will still go for the
enchanter as soon as charm breaks, one successful taunt will bring it
back onto you. Enchanter can now re-charm, mez, whatever.

If the enchanter isn't urgently needed elsewhere he can just Color Skew
and root/mez/memwipe or some combination of the 3.
