It will depend on the situation.  I rarely solo so
usually I am doing inferno shield on the puller,
nuking once or twice and or using malise so rangers
can stick snare.  You do need to practice how and
when to do full burns without making an overpull
worse.  You need to know how to nuke without pulling
the critter off the pet and fighters because they
are already being hammered and can't run to taunt
you clear.  Group in Frontier Mountains where the
whole zone is blue to red cons and you will learn
what I mean.  Conserving mana for emergencies is
necessary.  Breeze, clarity and mana songs help
mitigate use.  Today my group was taking down
giants with only a ranger for our healer.  We did
not let fights drag out but kept a fast pace.
I am worried you may not be balancing your powers
enough because you foresee some pre-ordained role.
Roles change as groups change or you solo by
choice or circumstance.
