No, its not just the Criticals that are affected by the increased Strength,
*all* your hits are.  Its simple that the number of hits for the higher
amount is too rare in the non-normal distribution of damage to make more
than a fractional percentage difference (think +150 Str = 2% more damage).

AIUI, Crits are a doubling of damage whenever they hit.  So all you are
doing is double a hit which is *still* along the non-normal distribution, it
could be 1 it could be 16, doubling to 2 or 32 respectively, alternatively
it will still usually be ((2*basedamage)+1) which doubles.

Therefore unless there is a *linear* addition to the Critical value, its not
worth maximising Strength for better Crits any more than it is worth
maximining Strength for better normal damage.
