On Thu, 15 Apr 1999 Drasten@L.S wrote:
> On Thu, 15 Apr 1999 14:23:49 GMT, Dundee@SPAMSPAMSPAM.COM (Dundee)
> wrote:
> >From what I've seen, darn few people want to be heroes.
> 
> Well, I'm sure you'll agree that despite visions of player policing in
> UO, there isn't really an opportunity to do things on a grand scale.
> Maybe it's just me, but I get the impression that if people are given
> a chance to either sit on the sidelines or play a role in an epic
> stuggle, they will take the struggle.  Yes, this differs from RL,
> where it seems many are content just to sit back and watch, but that's
> because there is nothing to truly lose in a game.

     I see just the opposite.  It's when there's nothing truly to lose
that you see people in a game jump into the fray.  When there's something
important at risk (the holly doozy-whatsit of power they're carrying back
home, or the loss of a level, or whatever) is when they're reluctant to
become part of a struggle.

> But in the world we have a bit more control over (UO of course), it's
> the players that cause many of the problems.

     If you design your game such that if the players lose "something
truly awful happens," you have to prepare for the eventuality that
"something truly awful" will happen.  You can't predict the behavior or
the capabilities of large numbers of players.  What if the nobody fights
the skeletons and they spill out of the graveyard into the streets and
kill all the shopkeepers (other than players complain they can't buy
anything)?

> Can you imagine if you were offline during one of the grand struggles
> between good and evil?  You log back on to find your town enslaved by
> orcs, or some such beast... yep, an epic struggle between your town
> and the orcs and you missed it!  Shame on you!

     It would need to be possible to retake the town.  People who would
have logged back in there should be able to log back into an "allied
town" and have an opportunity to retake the town.  Of course, if the
monsters keep respawning how do you do that?
