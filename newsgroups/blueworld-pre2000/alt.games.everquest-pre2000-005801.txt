Are you sure about that?  I think the memory blur component is still 
there.  Your post is the first I have heard of the memory blur 
component being removed and the spell data file parser still lists
the spell as having a "Remove Standings" component.

The spell works just like an Enchanter's Mesmerize.  If the spell is 
not resisted, the subject will sleep for the duration of the spell or 
until the subject takes damage.  There is a chance that the subject 
will be memory blurred at the beginning of that time, but they will 
remember any debuffs that are cast on them while they are sleeping.  
I find that the memory blur effect of the Mesmerize line of spells is 
very unreliable against tough opponents, so I imagine the same is true 
of Screaming Terror.
