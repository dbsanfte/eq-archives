yeah, i dont know what some are talking about its better to buy a
manufactured pc... bleh.  i have ALWAYS been able to do a lil smart shopping
at the various online shops through pricewatch, online auctions, and local
mom and pop shops to find the cheapest prices for the items i Wanted, then
go into any retail computer store, look at their specs and price and know i
came out way ahead....  EVERY time.

so anyways, to get back to the point, i would personally head towards the
athlon route unless you are willing to stick with a 100 mhz fsb intel system
(can still do that with a 600 or maybe even 700, then you will want to get
an asus p2b board)  but the intel chipsets are heading towards rdram, and
believe me you wanna avoid that like the plague, intel fucked up big time it
seems, at least with the current state with the quality/ technology rdram is
at *might* get better in the future, but not any time soon, so just stay
away.  you can try the via chipset boards for the intel cpus, but i dont
know anything about the quality of those boards so i wont personally
advocate them.

but i would go for the athlon system.  my asus k7v board with athlon 650,
256mb pc 133 , leadtek geforce screams along like a true champ.  and i got
great prices on all of em.  you just have to take your time over a few weeks
to make sure you can get the lowest price for the same component that you
want. its not hard,just need to be patient and look around.

1) get GOOD ram.  like mushkin, kingston, corsair, etc. yes, its expensive,
but you will hate yourself when your system is crashing all the time when
you buy cheap ram. and it will happen, because most of the fastest systems,
esp the athlons, but not limited to, dont like cheap ram at all.

2)get a geforce.
3)get a decent hard drive. take a high rpm/buffer drive over a bigger drive.
i doubt you truly need a 20 gig hd.  esp if you are smart and get a cdrw
drive.

4) get a good case, that allows for extra cooling, cause todays systems get
DAMN hot.  dont get some micro tower or some shit, cause it will over heat
so fast you wont be able to play winamp without a crash.

and make sure you have a good powersupply with the case.
