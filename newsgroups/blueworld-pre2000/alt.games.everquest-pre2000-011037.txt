I would recommend delaying rather than picking a name as soon as you
hit level 20, unless you are very certain what you want.  Once you
pick a last name, you are stuck with it.  GMs will change the name 
only if it violates the name policy or if you get married in game.

Check a dictionary if you are not absolutely sure about the meaning
and correct spelling of your name.  You really don't want to end up
with a name like Revengeance or Incarcerator.  (Both of those are on
E'Ci.  Good server for dumb names.  We have a guild on E'Ci called 
Harbingers of Ire.)

And type carefully!  There is a story of a guy running around with 
the last name Dargonslayer.


If your character is an elf, you might consider choosing an elvish 
surname.  Since all fantasy elves are basically derived from Tolkien's 
world, it's still an appropriate language to use.  Here is one good 
reference for Tolkien's elvish languages:

http://www.uib.no/People/hnohf/sindarin.htm
