I spent a little time examining this behavior with a young Ranger. 
Based on my experience and the behavior I observed second hand in many
gaming sessions involving a friend's Ranger character it is pretty clear
that there is a range based on your tracking skill within which you get
accurate directional information.  If you are tracking a target that is
(or moves) outside this range you begin to get bogus direction
information.  This can make it appear that your tracking is broken or
eing interfered with should you start to track a group member when s/he
is already outside of your range limit.  Note that we have not seen this
effect with my friend's ranger in some time (he is now in his 20s with a
tracking skill over 100).
