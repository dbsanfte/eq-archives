I don't know exactly how the engine works, but I think the issue is
a texture budget, rather than a restriction of certain models to
certain zones.

The game assumes a certain amount of texture memory on the client.
Some percentage is allocated to player textures.  This should hold
all of the textures for every race/gender/class/armor combination 
and all of the pets, because it is possible for all of these to be
present in a zone at once.  Verant may have allotted less than this
on the assumption that you will never see every possible combination
at the same time, or that if you do you'll just have to suffer with
thrashing texture memory.

Another chunk is allocated to zone-specific textures.  This has to 
hold all of the monster and terrain textures.

Some of the budget is left unused.  Some will be consumed by future 
expansions, such as the addition of the Iksar race.  This budget 
can also be used for special events, like placing fire giants in
Oasis.
