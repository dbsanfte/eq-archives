You don't need to group. There are different ways, here are a few I can 
think of:
1. enchanter beats something up, mem blurs it, you finish it off and get 
experience
2. if you are a caster -- enchanter casts clarity on you, casts Rune on 
you (damage absorbing shield), and keeps the mob stunned while you nuke 
the piss out of it
3. if you are a melee type -- a higher level character somehow ensures 
that you don't get hit while you swing at mobs (taunt, stun, etc)
4. shaman buffs you. Regeneration and haste will do amazing things at 
low levels
5. druid casts some hp buffs and damage shields on you. If the druid 
that is babysitting you is level 49+, all mobs that touch you will take 
25 damage back, and effectively beat themselves to death on you. Druids 
also have regeneration. They can find 6-7 mobs that are yellow to you, 
have them attack you, and then heal you while the mobs kill themselves 
beating you up
6. the simplest, and least effective -- a higher level character could 
pull single mobs for you and protect you, making sure you don't die. 
Long as you did more than half the damage, you will get experience

Most of these presume you are fighting white to low red mobs
