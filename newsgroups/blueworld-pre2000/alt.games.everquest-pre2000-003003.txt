IMO PvP only truly exists where there are options in the world or game
to _not_ be involved in PvP.   Ie a mechanism exists where it is
possible to play the game with a great amount of freedom without
people being able to attack you. 

In UO, PVP has come to mean forms of play where a higher level of
consent has been given to be involved in combat - prearranged rules or
guild war, where people signal in some way they are interested in
combat.  For me consent cant really occur unless there is an
alternative _within_ the game.

Some people of course take leaving town or playing the game to mean
consent - even when players make it quite clear here that many people
are _not_ consenting by these actions - I have not seen this problem
occur in regard to guild wars, presumably becase there is no ambiguity
about consent.  

Pking IMO is where consent is ambiguous, or clearly not given,
implied or otherwise.  In practise in UO this has usually meant
attacking blues, given that this is the most one can currently do in
UO to signal 'non-consent' (apart from never leaving town).   And this
of course gets complicated by 'blue meanies'.
