I haven't asked this for a week or two, so it's time again.

I think it would be tremendously handy to have direct key bindings 
for Sit/Stand, Spells, and Ranged Attack, so that I don't have to 
clutter up my hot key panels with these commands.  Currently I have
Sit/Stand and Spells on virtually every panel of every character
because I use them so often.  I have a direct binding for Duck, 
which I don't use nearly as often as I use Sit/Stand.  How hard 
could it be to add in these key bindings?

It would also be nice to get direct key bindings for the eight spell 
slots, but I can understand how people might feel like they were 
running out of keys. ;)

One last bit, it would be nice if the Meditation button were pressed
automatically when you open your spell book.  Is there any good 
reason why you would ever sit down to your spell book and *not* 
meditate?  It's just a needless mouse click.
