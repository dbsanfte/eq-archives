Here is a good tactic.  The ranger pulls a creature.  One wizard roots

ranger.  Rangers take a lot of damage and as you get to the upper 20's
your one cleric is going to be hard pressed to keep him alive.  I can
see your group doing great in non dungeon situations, but I can't see
it working well inside with larger pulls.
