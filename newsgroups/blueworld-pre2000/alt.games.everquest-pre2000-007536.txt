And they are, as you surmised, over-confident. They actually THINK that
their uber-equipment gives them a huge edge, whereas it only gives a
slight one.

Most twinks think that, because they have uber-equipment, they can tackle
mobs two or three level highers than they should. They are usually wrong.
What the twink equipment gives you is a lessening of the downtime you get
for fighting the same mobs.

Trying to tackle ubermobs is counterproductive:

- they don't give *that* much more experience compared to the downtime you
  get
- you level with less combats, meaning your fighting skills don't get as
  many occasion to increase
- the occasional bad streak of rolls gets you in more trouble

My experience (as a warrior, as a shaman, as a bard) is that a steady
stream of medium blues gives you more experience and faster skills than
tackling a yellow, healing, finding the next yellow, and so on.

(The right way of twinking, is, of course, to have a high level spellcaster
 standing next to you, casting 49-level buffs, runes, absorbing skins,
 and/or healing you. This doesn't let you fight higher mobs, just
 reduces your downtime to zero, and enable you to level "normally", but
 faster overall)

> Oddly, role-played twinks tend to have a very low scrub index,
> comparable to that of female melee types.

Role-played characters are usually played by mature-minded people, who
tend to pay attention to everything they are doing. It pays.

The younger d00ds still have to learn that one.
