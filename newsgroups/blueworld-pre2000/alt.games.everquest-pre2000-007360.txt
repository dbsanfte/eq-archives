You do if you learn physics or statistics. ;)

Suppose you were playing poker and instead of being dealt cards from a
deck, someone would give each player a hand of cards.  You don't know
how likely they are to choose any particular hand, perhaps they give 
out royal flushes 5% of the time.  Even if they were perfectly fair and
trustworthy, this would not be satisfying.  You may not know exactly 
how likely it is that someone at the table can beat your three of a kind,
but you generally would have an intuition.

> I sincerely believe that to
> "know" too much about the game detracts from it.I also feel fairly certain
> that whichever race /classes come worst off in the gained exp stakes would

Verant erred in creating different experience curves for the races and classes
in the first place.  When they combined that with an experience split formula
that gives more experience to the characters with penalties, they created a
real mess.

If they actually wanted to fix the problem, all they would have to do is 
create a single experience curve (probably a nice exponential) and renormalize
everyone's experience to that curve.  They could get rid of the hell levels
while they were at it as well.
