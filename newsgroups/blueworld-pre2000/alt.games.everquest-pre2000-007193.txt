 How the heck did that happen?

 I mean, I know that detect traps is mostly useless, due to 
implementation problems with traps. I know pick locks is rarely useful.

 I gather pickepocket used to be different, I don't recall reading when 
it changed.

 It just seems weird now - I mean, when would you use it?

 If you are soloing a MOB, it's pointless - you are going to get the 
loot anyway. 

 If you are grouped, you are ninjalooting or essentially kill stealing, 
as far as loot goes. If you want to be fair with your group (as I 
would), and you would have to report it or not do it. If you don't want 
to be fair, I can't see a group that would want you, or your class.

 If you are doing it to MOBs that others are fighting, you are 
nijalooting/ loot KSing from them. I guess in a game condoned sense?!?

 If you do it to MOBs that aren't fighting anyone, and aren't the type 
who automatically aggro on you, they soon will. Then it turns into you 
soloing them (pointless) or you fleeing to the zone from them. 

 Perhaps, when you get a high enough skill, you can steal without 
getting aggro'd on by a MOB, but I haven't got there yet.

 So, for me, it seems that the only non-pointless way to use my skill 
without robbing my party, or others, is in a way that encourages either 
trains to the zone, or training the MOB on to others (steal, train to 
zone or someone else, repeat). This seems both highly annoying and 
inefficient.
