You are correct, Joel, but missed my point a bit.  The issue here is not "Where
can the Halfling newbies hunt?" - the issue is how they can improve Kithicor
since they've done a piss-poor job of it so far.

First they turn it into a deathtrap for the vast majority of players -
especially the poor Halflings.  After they finally got the message that they
screwed up, they set it up to clear the uber-mobs during daylight hours - not
like there's much difference between day and night in Kithicor.  The problem now
is half the time the high level players are sitting around waiting for night,
and the other half of the time the majority of players who just want to pass
through are waiting for daylight.  Not that Verant cares at all about that
problem... sitting around doing nothing is, after all, part of their elusive
'Vision'.

A new outdoor zone for either the high level players (perhaps south of Kithicor)
or the low/mid level players (conveniently bypassing Kithicor) was one proposed
solution back then, and I hope it's still being considered.  That way Kithicor
can go back to either high or low level and waiting around for day or night is
reduced significantly.  If nothing else, they could at least consider looking
into the pathing issues in the zone to fix the problem - which would probably
require re-arranging landmarks in the zone to compensate.
