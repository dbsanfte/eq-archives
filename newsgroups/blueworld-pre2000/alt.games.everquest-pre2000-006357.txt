> 2.   I've heard of people trading items from one of their characters to
> another of their characters. How is this done without having a friend's
> character act a a go-between?  (I'm making a series of trade-skill
> characters and wanted them to do things like the smith[lvl-2] supplying
> the tailor[lvl-32] with studs in exchange for money or studded armor.
> While I've made a few friends, I don't want to have to depend on them

Simple, just drop the  items on the ground. Log off and log into
the other character and pick up the items. Do it in an out of the
way place so no other people 'accidently' pick up your 'abandoned'
items and HOPE and PRAY you do not have any connect problems.
