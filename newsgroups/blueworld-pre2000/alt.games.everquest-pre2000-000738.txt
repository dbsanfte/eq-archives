I'm not in the beta but I've been devouring all the info released
about EQ for quite a while now...it looks like the EQ developers are
requiring it to some extent as you suggest.  The simple fact that
there are race-specific cities and areas that are hostile to certain
other races sets up a good environment for the cultivation of at least
a little RPing.

If a  PvP+ d00d selects a troll character because of certain racial
attributes he will be encouraged to play the role to some degree by
the fact that he can kill humans and elves and the like with impunity
and be welcomed back to his troll city as a hero, whereas if he
molests other trolls he will be acosted (butchered) by the guards in a
troll city.  He may still become a raving psyco and murder his own
people if he chooses, but it'll be a lot harder on his character if he
can't deal within a city of his own race and is relegated to dealing
soley with other criminals or with other races who may dislike him
strongly even if he's done them no wrong.

If the EQ team does a decent job of balancing the races so not every
powergamer or d00d chooses the same race, this system should
facilitate behavior consistant with racial roles while hampering
behavior that violates that aspect of the game.
