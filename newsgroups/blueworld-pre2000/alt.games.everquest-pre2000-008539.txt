In alt.games.everquest Morgan <morgana@misleading.com> wrote:

: Reread the post.  What he said was that it is no more antisocial to pick
: the monsters in an area clean than it would be to kill them.  My response
: was that he was making things less pleasant for other players by depriving
: them of loot.  If he killed the monsters instead, new ones would spawn 
: with new loot to be gotten.

Actually, if I *were* to pick the monsters "clean" it wouldn't be /really/
clean.  Chances are VERY good that the items will be left on the mob, with
coin being reduced to 1 of each that it initially had, if I am lucky
(meaning that this is best case scenario... if I am not lucky I didn't
get to start by picking the pp or gp, I started with sp, and all the pp
and gp are left on the mob).

Now, lets look at the two possibilities very carefully:

I. I pick pocket but don't kill

	A. Mob will still have some loot
	B. Next group will still get exp off the mob
	C. The next group kills it AND gets the respawn with exp and full
	loot

II. I kill the mob I pick pocket.

	A. Mob is gone completely, no one else gets anything but me.
	B. The next group to come on this spawn only gets the next spawn


The way I see it, it's better for the next group if i don't kill it.  Not to
mention the fact that if I could kill it, I probably wouldn't be picking its
pockets.  I'd like to reiterate than I am taking a HUGE risk in doing this.


: Consider it this way.  If you are in a place with enough monsters that you
: can be constantly fighting, then you could be killing monsters with loot or 
: monsters that had been pickpocketed.  Which would you rather be doing?

Consider this:

A. If there are that many monsters I'll never make it out alive. ;)
B. If I killed them, the next group would have to wait for everything I just
killed to respawn.  Would you rather be waiting on the next spawn to get
ANYTHING or would you rather kill what the thief left to at least get the
experience?

Keep in mind when I pick pocket something I usually only take one or two
succesful pick pockets before I leave.  If I continue, I run a continually
increasing risk of the mob detecting what I am doing and aggroing on me. 
Better to settle with what little I got on the first round or two.
