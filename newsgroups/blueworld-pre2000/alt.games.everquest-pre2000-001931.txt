> That is a blanket statement, and so, false.  I found some incredibly
> wonderful social interaction in UO... For quite a while, I was a
> regular in Kinship, a player-run town on Catskills.  I belonged to a
> few different roleplaying groups.  I spent long hours at the tavern,
> or hunting with friends.  Heck, I even "sung" a song at a Skara Brae
> wedding for two of my online companions, and toasted their good
> fortune at the reception afterward.  Is that all, or is there some
> "rich social interaction" that the above doesn't cover?
> 
> The problem? It was generally ruined.  Kinship itself was a magnet for
> d00dz who wanted to kill all the "lame azz rpers".  For the longest
> time, you couldn't tell whether it was safe to walk amongst its
> buildings.  The furniture, nothing more than a few chairs and tables,
> was continually stolen.  Thieves would hide in the common room, hoping
> to steal someone's keys, scrolls, or reagents.  Not to far away were
> homes and towers belonging to rampant PKs, PKs who used to hide along
> the route to Kinship, in the hopes of killing people for - what? -
> their costumes? The few bits of leather armor I carried? And there
> were groups that had it even worse, like the Shadowclan Orcs.  There,
> they had d00dz attacking them nightly, slaughtering them for their orc
> helms and ringmail armor.  Or just gating in monsters by the dozen so
> that they couldn't use the fort for roleplaying.
> 
> And for so long, I was telling myself, "It will get better, it will
> get better... The rep patch will help.  We won't see all those
> murderers then."  And then the rep patch came out, and for awhile
> things got better...I could use UO Assist to pick out red names before
> people appeared on my screen (better to get the head start).  But then
> some d00dz discovered the exploits...die and get a friend to hand your
> head in, and collect your own bounty.  Ok, so we'll make it so that
> you get penalties from dying if you're a murderer! Great.  Except,
> they made it so those penalties accrued on res, not on death.  Thus
> the red ghosts macroing off their kill counts.
> 
> One fine day in UO I decided to take one of my favorite characters
> over to Kinship.  I recalled not far away from the tavern, and walked
> up to it to see if anyone was around.  I got a few steps from the door
> when it suddenly bust open, and "PiMpMaSsAfLaSh" came out (dressed in
> his mandatory robe, bone armor, and heavy crossbow) and said, "im
> gonna kill you, biotch!"  Of course, I took off running... A
> roleplayed character is rarely any match for a powergamed, experienced
> PK; roleplayers generally put some self-imposed limitations of
> weapons, armor, or spells to be in-character, and I was no exception.
> I ran most of the way from Kinship to Trinsic... It seemed that
> whenever I'd stop, he'd catch up and start firing crossbow bolts; I
> couldn't shake him, no matter where I ran.  (Of couse I couldn't; he
> put auto-follow on me when I was walking up to the tavern.  He knew
> all along he was going to kill me.  I didn't.)  I saw a group of
> players ahead, and started yelling, "Help!"  A moment later, I saw
> them whip out heavy crossbows, and was forced to resist a Paralyze

> that I could stop and get out a recall scroll.  I recalled back to
