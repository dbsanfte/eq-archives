Quick calculation: a rogue wielding the 8/17 piercer from Kunark
in primary hand and a bone razor (10/22) in the other hand at
level 50 swapping in a crystalline spear (13/30) to backstab.

Assuming magic number of 8, 41.6% chance of offhand attack and
a 50% chance of a double attack you arrive at (unhasted) a
damage output of 26.8MN/s (magic number/second).  Now against
blues you're doing roughly 80% of that as your average damage
output or 21.5damage/second.

Now a backstab cycles every 8 (7?) seconds.  At 50 with a 
crystalline spear several rogues told me their mean damage
is about 150 per backstab from behind.  Thats averaged over
all their backstabs including misses.  That is another
18.75damage/second.

So *unhasted* we have a rogue doing (given backstabs from
behind) 40damage/second.  Haste them up to SLTW speed (through
SLTW or lower hastes + items) and that goes up to 80+ per
*second*.

Thats from *one* rogue.

Now multiply it by 3-5.
