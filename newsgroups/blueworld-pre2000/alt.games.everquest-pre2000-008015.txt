>agility isnt worth getting over 75?

No, all stats will help you the more you raise them.

Agility is not worth sacrificing other attributes for above 75.

If you have 2 items, same ac, one with an agi buff and one
without, then wear the buff.  No brainer.

If you have a choice between stats and AC, well, AC is
generally better.

My rule of thumb is that for general purpose wear 1 point of AC
is worth 2 points of resists or 3 points of stats.

For special purpose, of course, you do things differently.
