Great.  Now, if you would personally guarantee that your cleric will be 
available any time, on any server, in any zone, I have no arguments.  I'm not
trying to say that casters should be stripped of binding.  I'm simply saying 
that binding is a _game_ mechanic, not a _play_ mechanic, and as such should
be possible (within some reasonable limits) with or without the aid of other
players.  It's hard to illustrate this to casters, apparently, because they
see the balancing factor being a warriors viability in combat.  Sure, you NEED
tanks.  And tanks NEED casters, regardless of binding.  But tanks also need 
binding, which has NOTHING to do with a symbiotic group relationship.  
Comparing an attribute warriors provide within that symbiotic relationship 
with one outside it is spurious.  The closest analogy I can think of would be:
What if YOU could _only_ bind so long as you were in a full group?  Would that
be fair?  The current lack of any type of solo binding for melee classes is 
similar, in that it forces some type of dependancy when the dependant party may
or may not be available/amenable to their end of the bargain.  There is NO 
comparable dependency in the other direction.  (Or, more accurately, there are
two dependencies in the melee-> caster direction:  group viability and binding.
There is one in the caster->melee direction:  group viability.  This leaves the
issue of binding decidedly unbalanced.)
