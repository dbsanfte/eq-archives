GuildWorld      www.guildworld.com
Is a community-based website which focuses on providing management tools
and resources to online gaming guilds. Guild World offers a variety of 
time saving features to help cope with the "growing pains" associated 
with managing your on-line family.

Features
Chats -   You can setup a wide variety of chats for you guild. Including
          private chats for guild business. Public rooms allow visitors
          and potential members to converse with existing members.
Forums -  Setup up your own discussion group to talk about the topics 
	   that are important to your guild. Security levels allow Public,
	   Private and Administrative forums.
Voting -   Now you can easily create polls to keep your finger on the  
	    pulse of your guild.
Profiles - All members of your guild can build their on-line profiles,
           including past history and portraits.
Screen shots - Allow members to share their deeds of valor or tales of 
		 woe in a fast and fun way.
User Directories --    To help locate members of your guild or players 
			  from other guilds.
Event Scheduling --   Allow the members to plan and schedule guild 
			 events.

Group Management Made Easy
Managing a large group of people is not a easy thing to do. GuildWorld
provides you with the tools to do it as quick and painless as 
possible.Save time and make your On-line life easier Websites are 
designed to be configured quickly and easily, and allow custom tailoring 
without requiring any programming skills. Administrators who do have 
programming skills can further modify their site with advanced HTML, 
graphics, and even sound.

Quick Setup
Registration is fast, simple, and automatic. Guild Leaders register once,
and are immediately walked through the basic setup of their site on first
login. When completed, they can email all prospective members of their 
guild with a special invitation tool, then sit back and relax.

So check it out!
