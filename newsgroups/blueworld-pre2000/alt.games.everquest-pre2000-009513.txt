I think it depends on a lot of factors.  I am level 45 now.  Most of the 
time I am playing in a single group of five or six or I am playing with 
2-3 full groups.  I am nearly always the only enchanter present.

I always keep Clarity on everyone with mana and Augmentation or Celerity
on the fighters.  When mana permits, I also cast Brilliance and Insight.

This buffing eats a lot of mana and I am rarely back to full mana before
the next pull.  I know that in the long run, most of my mana is spent on
Alteration spells.  I can make my mana last longer by attacking the cost
of the most expensive spells (Amdahl's Law).  If specialization helped
channeling, then I would agree that Conjuration was a better choice, but 
if all it does is save mana, then I am better off reducing the cost of
the Alteration spells so that I can enter combat with more mana.

When my mana runs out before the mobs do, I am usually just shouting 
"Twitch me!" across the living room rather than typing OOM. ;)
