No, that's like eating yourself sick at an all-you-can-eat 
restaurant.  Stop before you hate it.  You paid to have a 
pleasant experience, not to put yourself in pain.

I play all the time because I like to play.  The nagging feeling
in the back of my mind is that if I ever want to see my ranger
and druid get last names, I had better start playing them. ;)

I think that what draws me to EverQuest (and UO, when I played
that) is the persistent world.  When you finish playing some
huge game of Civ II or AoE, it was fun, but you haven't done
anything other than pass some time.  When you camp out from EQ,
you have often earned some gear or money, advanced a few yellow 
pixels along your experience bar, or practiced some skills up
a few points.  There is a potential for long term advancement 
that is not present in games without a persistent state.



My advice to a person who is quitting would be to look at why
you want to quit.  If it just isn't fun any more and you want
to stop paying the $10, then cancel your account and set it 
aside.  In a couple months, you may find yourself wanting to
come back and you will be glad you didn't do anything rash.

However, if you feel that you are playing compulsively and
ruining your life, then by all means, delete your characters,
cancel your account, and don't look back.  Don't give away 
your gear, just trash it with the character.  Don't leave any 
ties or debts in the virtual world.
