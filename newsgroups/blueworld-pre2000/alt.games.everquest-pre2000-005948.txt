I hope Verant DOES do this and it won't affect the player economy at
all. Keep the following in mind: we're talking about Bind only, not SoW,
Rezzes, or buffs (all of which would be impractical at best...by the
time you're done running from Freeport to Guk, they'll be mostly gone
anyway). And even Bind itself would still be a practical spell for the
self-caster. Remember, we're talking about having a binding NPC in the
cities only, where melees can be bound, not out in the countryside
(melees *should* be bindable in at least one place in every outdoor
zone, but that's a different argument). The caster will still be able to
bind him or herself to any square yard of Norrathian property, as they
can now.

We're all *hoping* that if Verant does it, the NPC's charge 5, 10 or at
most 20pp; they might charge a LOT more. Even at 10pp, the player
economy isn't hurt unless you're in the habit of overpaying for binds.
Most players will bind you for 0 to 5pp, so setting a 10pp NPC price
might even push the bind price up to 9pp or somesuch, which may be a
hardship to lower-levels needing a bind, but has just added to the
player economy, not hurt it.
