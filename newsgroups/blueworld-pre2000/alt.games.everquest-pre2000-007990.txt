There is no way to make money buy buying and selling from merchants.

All merchants use the same algorithm for pricing.  Every item has a 
base price.  For example, the base price of a ruby is 125pp.  You 
get a price factor with the merchant depending on your charisma and
your faction.  This factor is never less than 1.05, even if you have
200 charisma and the merchant adores you.

B = Base price
F = Price factor

You pay B*F to buy the item and you receive B/F when you sell.
Since the base price is constant for all merchants, you can never
make money from buying and selling the same object.

You can tell when a merchant is giving you ideal prices because they
will buy a flask of water for 1sp.  If they offer you any less, your
prices could be improved.

For example, the ruby with maximum charisma and faction,
you would buy at 125*1.05 = 131.25pp
and you would sell at 125/1.05 = 119.048pp

Those are the exact prices that I get when I buy and sell rubies.
