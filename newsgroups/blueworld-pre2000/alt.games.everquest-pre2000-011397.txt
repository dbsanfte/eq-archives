Have you tried contacting Verant's Customer Support or checking their web
site for information?

Phone and E-mail addresses for Customer Support should be in the
documentation you got with the game; you can also find contact info on their
web site, <http://www.station.sony.com/everquest/>.

The account and billing FAQs in the 'Support' area of their web site cover
some of the of common problems.  I don't remember if the FAQs cover your
specific issues, but they're certainly worth checking.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------

As far as the message about the account key being already in use: I will
make a _guess_ that it's referring to the account you tried to create the
first time (six months ago).

What you probably need to do is to just log in, using the same user name and
password you selected the first time, and then go ahead and update your
billing information after you've logged into your account.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------

I don't have any specific ideas about why EQ wouldn't take your credit card
the first time, but here's a couple of general tips for entering credit card
info online:

1.  Make sure the name, address, etc. you enter matches what's on your
credit card account info.

2.  Some online forms are very picky about the format they'll accept for
account numbers: some will reject an entry if you separate the groups of
digits in the account number with spaces or dashes; others will reject the
account number if it doesn't include separators.

So some forms might accept account numbers formatted as
0000-0000-00000-00000, but others will only accept the 000000000000000000
format.
