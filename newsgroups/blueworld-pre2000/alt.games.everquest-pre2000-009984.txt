> If you mean that in a test condition, just you standing still
> for 20 seconds with no monsters around, your spells are interrupted,
> then I think there is something wrong.  Perhaps you have a really
> flakey network connection or ... something.  No good idea.

> If you mean that in combat you get those two messages in sequence
> with no other messages in between, then you are seeing other effects.
> The actual event may have been in order, but was displayed out of
> order on your screen.  I have noticed that if a monster hits me
> just before I begin casting, it often interrupts the cast and if a 
> monster hits me just before I end a cast, I don't even get a "regain
> concentration" message.  It seems like the casting time is shifted
> forward when interruptions are calculated.  Maybe the server is 
> "correcting" for lag that isn't really there.

I think it's something different.  Specifically, I think it's related to this
"spell recycle time" bug that's been a problem recently.  In reference to 
that, somewhere, somebody mentioned that spell handshaking had been 
implemented. (I read this to mean that both client and server have to agree
that all the conditions for spellcasting must be met.)  My guess is that lag
is causing the client side to believe it is stopped and start casting (which
ought to be successful), while the server side believes you were casting 
while moving.  Obviously, this is a no-no, so your spell is interrupted.
That, at least, seems to be the case to me, as I have a HELL of a time 
chasing after a monster, stopping briefly to cast snare, and then continuing.
It's highly annoying and sometimes life-threatening, but I assume it's the 
price we pay for being protected from the anti-social jerks out there who 
find it perfectly acceptable to hack the packet stream.  /sigh
