I don't usually respond to threads like this since I'm no where near the level
to go to the Planes.  However, it's the underlying issue that really bugs me.

How long did they spend testing Plane of Sky?  How many people - in-house and
out, were involved?  Did anyone at all ever bring up the possible issues of
Levitate during testing, if that is in fact the reason it was nerfed (it made
the trip easier)?  It doesn't make any sense that they would never have imagined
the fact that players would use the Levitate spell in Plane of Sky.  This makes
it glaringly obvious that their testing methods are very limited and not very
controlled nor very well organized;  maybe it's simply a significant lack of
imagination and/or tunnel vision syndrome on their part.  And this is only the
most recent incident of - IMHO - a rather obvious issue that should have been
caught prior to release.

With all the problems that seem to be slipping through the cracks, I would think
they'd start looking at their testing methodology and trying to make
improvements in that area.  I admit that's a generalization, and if anyone at
Verant responds they're most likely going to question what 'problems' I'm
referring to;  imagine the lists that will start showing up on this thread and
in their e-mail if they do that.  I'm almost dreading the release of Kunark for
that very reason...  How many bugs are we going to have to deal with after
installing the expansion?

I do intend to purchase the expansion, I still enjoy the game despite it's
annoying little shortcomings.  I'd just like to see a little better performance
and attention out of their development and testing labs.  Heck, if they need
help perhaps they should consider recruiting some current high level players to
test major changes before releasing them.  How many people actually play on the
Test Server on a regular basis?  Maybe they could allow players to temporarily
copy (not move) their characters to Test in order to encourage more thorough
testing of new patches - I think a reasonable number of people would jump at the
chance, if their favorite Character were involved at no risk (on their usual
server).

I apologize if my comments are incorrect in any way.  It's just starting to get
on my nerves that a great game with such wonderful potential is slowly going
downhill due to poor testing methodology, internal misinformation, and lack of
attention to detail.
