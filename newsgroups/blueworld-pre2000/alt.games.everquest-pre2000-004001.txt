An interesting idea, but there are a great many variables that would be hard
to overcome.  There could be times when the person who won the auto-roll
wouldn't want or need the item that was rolled for.  This could present a
problem when the item is nodrop.  In addition, I can see cases as well where
someone already has the item.  There's also cases where several groups are
involved taking down the same critter, and roll among 12 or 15 people.

I think that printing the loot message to the group will give everyone the
information they need.
