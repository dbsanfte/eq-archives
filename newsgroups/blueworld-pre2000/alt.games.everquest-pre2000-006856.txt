Ok, here's the way it usually works in Guk on E'Ci from my experience.

99% of the time, Magi and Frenzy are list camps, most of the time the
Lord is too. This is because there's a group in there 99.9% of the time,
filing the space as one leaves. Sage often comes under the same rules.
In these rooms, the primary item is on a group join order list. Every
other item would be rolled for, whether the common drop on the mob, or
anything pulled from outside the room.

King is usually broken by one group at a time, so randomed among that
group. Sav, Cav, exe, Ass etc. are usually random (except, somtimes,  if
someone in Ass is specifically there for TBB)

Play nice rules may come in, but Guk has a good balance of tried and
tested 'player rules'. Calling a GM to interrupt the generally accepted
etiquette would get you blacklisted in the same way as ninja looting
would. NOTHING in Guk is worth losing your reputation for
