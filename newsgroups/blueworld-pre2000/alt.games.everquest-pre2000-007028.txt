Perma - hunt in the preacher room.  I fought there for 3 levels - from 22 to
mid 25 - with a group of 3 people, Ranger, Druid, Enchanter.  I tanked
everything one at a time while the enchanter kept all of the mobs mezzed and
the druid healed and cast damage shield.  In about 3 days, all of us got at
least 2 runed circlets (400pp each) and 2 of us got archeologist packs when
we had time to clear both rooms.  The preacher seemed to spawn every hour or
so on average and always drops a good item.  And the experience was great -
constant fighting of blues.  I would suggest getting an enchanter and a tank
or 2 and head to Perma.  Even if the Preacher is camped by another group,
the archeologist room is also good hunting and was never camped in the 3
days we stayed in Perma, and the lag was very low because the zone was often
empty except for us.
