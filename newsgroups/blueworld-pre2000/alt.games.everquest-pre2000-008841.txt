I disagree that the inabilty to bind is a huge disadvantage.  Well... it
is a disadvantage.  But I could argue that my caster's inabilty to wear
plate is a disadvantage too.  BUT,  I really think that melee characters
(while not being able to cast bind themselves) SHOULD be bindable
ANYWHERE that a caster can be bound.  The city only thing has never made
one lick of sense.  I believe the argument, originally, was that mage
types were more fragile and more likely to die more often, thus
"needing" closer bind points.  That never sat well with me.  I have
caster characters and melee characters.   While death leaves both types
vulnerable, my casters are in much better shape to do a corpse recovery
than my melees.  When a melee dies they spawn pretty much helpless
(except for monks perhaps).  When a caster dies, we have no armor (like
we really had any to begin with) and no weapon (like we use those... and
clerics / mages can summon one up), but we generally spawn with 70-90
percent of our functionality (depending on our level and class).
Rememorize our spells and its usually a cake walk back to our corpse.
My casters don't even bind outside of cities unless the trek is
exceptionally long or dangerous.  The only way I could see casters
having a special need to be able to bind anywhere is if we couldn't
rememorize our spells till we looted our corpse.
