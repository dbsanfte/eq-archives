I think we went through this a week or two ago in another thread. You
simply can't "write yourself free" like that. You always has a certain
responsability of any product you sell and what harm it may do. There
are of coure limits and depends a lot on what product and what can be
expected of it and so on. But writing "we take no responsability
whatsoever" does not work and such parts of agreements are typically
void by law just to prevent someone from taking responsability. I
can't commenty on this specific case but it is always good to take
whetever you find in such software licences with a grain of salt.
There are many things in them that are just bogus and not valid but
apperantly software companies hope enough people will believe in them.
Another larg part of them are things allready covered by various laws,
copyright issues are a good example, meaning there is usually only a
small portion of them tat are actually something "new" and applies. Of
course, you also have to in some way actually had agreed to it,
usually they don't apply at all but that is another story.
