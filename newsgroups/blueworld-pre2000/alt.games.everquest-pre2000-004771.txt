As someone pointed out, the Erudite can start out with the same Charisma
as a High Elf and 5 more INT as well.  It just requires that the player
have the foresight to put at least 10 or 15 points into CHA instead of
dumping them all into INT.

The High Elf's 95 base WIS is definitely nice to have over the Erudites 
base 83 if you are going to do jewelcraft, but DEX is the same at 70.
As far as we can tell, the extra 15 points of AGI on the High Elf does 
absolutely nothing, so I wouldn't worry about it.  The extra 5 points
of STR and STA for the Erudite might let them win an arm wrestling 
competition, but nobody is really lining up to see Erudite and High Elf
Enchanters compete in tests of strength.

In the end, play what you want to play.  I'm an elf-loving girl, so I was
going to play an elf however it worked out.  I didn't actually do the
comparison when I started out either. ;)
