In many cultures where there were multiple gods the gods are often known 
as being simply Immortal. Ie. Not Mortal. They don't follow the same 
rules or laws that simple mortal beings do. That usually never meant they 
were invincible or unkillable. There is also many stories in Norse 
mythology (not real familiar with roman mythology, so can't say about 
them) of gods and demi-gods being slain by very powerful mortals, large 
groups of lesser mortals, the half-immortal offspring of matings between 
god and mortal, beings wielding god forged or enchanted weapons, and 
especially other gods or outworldly beings.
True there is no "God" that we know of (where is The Nameless? Hmm...) 
but there is at least 2 gods. Beings of incredible power and strength, 
but still very killable.
