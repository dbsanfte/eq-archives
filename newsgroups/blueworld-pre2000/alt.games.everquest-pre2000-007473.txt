Some thoughts from the lower end of the level range.

1, it is rather fun to plink an aqua-goblin with an arrow and
then have the critter get into an archery duel with me.  They
only carry 5 arrows though, and so it quickly becomes melee.

2, I like seeing some randomness in what is out there.

an interesting experiment on patch might be to add throwing
boulders to the giant loot tables, and give them throwing
skill.  Not every giant would have them, and they would not
have a lot of them, but the chance would exist for the snared
critter to shoot back while chasing its tormenter.

Heck, give some of the guards a bow and arrows as well.

without changing the effectiveness of missile weapons, this
would add some randomness to the game.

If you want to make a big change (comparable to a kiting nerf)
as some folks here suggest, it would mean a LOT of work on test
making sure that the new balance of skills, abilities, and mobs
would be interesting to the players.  I don't see anything like
that happening for a while.
