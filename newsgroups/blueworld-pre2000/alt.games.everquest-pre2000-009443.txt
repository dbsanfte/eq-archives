I always assume that when people are casting buffs for "donations"
they will buff for free and don't require money from anyone.  
Certainly that is what I have meant whenever I used the term.
It never occurred to me that it was a euphemism.  ;)

There are three different kinds of offers for such things:
"Binding at the West Gate" - probably has a fee in mind
"Binding at the West Gate for free" - free, will probably refuse a tip
"Binding at the West Gate for donations" - free, but welcomes a tip

Some people will specify what they want: one plat, a smile, or a 
properly worded request.
