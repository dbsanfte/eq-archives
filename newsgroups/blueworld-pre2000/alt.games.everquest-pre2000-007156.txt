This is the very reason why EverQuest is in such a pathetic state.
People cry 'nerf' when they perceive an imbalance in a certain class
that they don't play. Verant nerfs said class. Those who play said
class get upset, while everyone else goes about their business. Then
the people who cried 'nerf' and the people who turned a blind eye to
previous nerfs to other classes eventually get nerfed and find out
what it's like to have something taken away. A little foresight from
the EverQuest community as a whole might have prevented a lot of the
problems and in-fighting that persist today, but most people are too
worried about their chosen class to give a shit about anyone else.
EverQuest mirrors real-life greed in a frightening way.

If anything, the skill should be returned to how it used to be.  I
always thought that the point of the skill was to allow rogues to take
a little extra cash from a MOB that they're fighting, on top of what
the MOB drops when it dies. Making the pick-pocket skill take from the
main loot pool is like pissing in the wind. Stupid design decisions
like this are why more and more people are finding Verant to be an
incompetent development house.
