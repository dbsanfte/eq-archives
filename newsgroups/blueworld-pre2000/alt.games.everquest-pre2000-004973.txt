You are right on track for the most part, but I did want to comment on this.
We *did* factor in the "12 hour a day people".

At some point in the game design process you have to decide what percentage
of people is the game going to be tuned for.  There will always be those
people in the top 10% that play the game 12+ hours per day, in the same
fashion as there will be those who play the game 1 hour per week.  Trying to
make people advance at the same rate based upon the calendar wouldn't be
fair to those who do put in 12+ hours per day, and trying to make people
advance at the same rate based upon hours/day wouldn't be fair to those who
play 1 hour per week.

The only fair way to tune the advancement rate in the game is by number of
hours total.  Now certainly some people will find ways to advance a bit
faster than the design, and some will level slower, but it all evens out to
the rate of the average player.
