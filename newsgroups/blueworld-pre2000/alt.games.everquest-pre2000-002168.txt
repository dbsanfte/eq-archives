PvP is a self-refreshing ladder, which is why it is so key. It's also
a TOOL and not just a ladder--in other words, it lends itself to use
in additional ladders. So for example, you'll generally find your best
roleplayers demanding that PvP be an option for them. It adds new
dimensions to economic conflict. And so on...

Games which have done a safe world and non-safe worlds have all failed
at the experiment. In the commercial arena alone, Meridian 59 and Dark
Sun Online attempted it and it failed.

Games which allow for both styles of play in the same environment via
a PK switch have generally had very high admin costs, and have found
that most of their players migrate to +pvp over time (generally after
3+ months of play).

The term "acquisition cost" is a standard term for service industries.
Basically, you think of it in terms of how much money it costs you as
a business to obtain one new customer, and how much money that
customer is likely to pay you over the lifetime of their subscription.

All of the above methods are in UO in one form or another, and are
fairly easily accessible. Methods 1, 4, and 5 are generally dismissed
by the pacifists as "being forced to play the game their way." Methods
2 and 3 are generally considered to be too hard to organize.

>Protecting marian doesn;t not have to be an absolute.  The trick is
>decided what is reasonable for her protection. After all, some
>expressions of conflict SHOULD be eliminated (i.e. Boffo kills Marian in
>game because in real life she is a woman/black/jew).

Agreed there.

So in UO you can (and many have) formed police forces, militias,
guards, etc. You can indeed get recall methods, hiding, etc. You can
choose to stick to safe areas and pay a premium for goods brought in
from outside. You can stay behind your locked door, or in your house
which has an extremely high degree of safety. All that stuff is there.
Yet the pacifists claim it is insufficient for one reason or another.

Our hope was that by providing some of this stuff we'd see
interdependence promoted. And that we'd see interesting social
formations occur. And to some extent this occurred. But generally
speaking, players found it too difficult to deal with the above, and
preferred to request that we assume the burden of the above methods
instead.
