It's easy to say that because for people like Bernd and myself -it's
completely true!  To begin with - "Fun" is entirely subjective to begin
with - and GrimBlade is clearly asking for people's opinions.

I for one was a beta-tester for UOL and quit around the time T2A came out.
I actually purchased T2A - but UPS screwed up the order and I never got it..
I did read the specs on it - and it seemed to me - that while adding new GUI
features - new "zones" and "mobs" (pardon my use of EQ terminology) I saw no
fundamental change in the gameplay itself.

So further based on Grimblade's situation - as someone who played UOL and
didn't like it - I doubt that he would like the current state of UO.

Now McCannon has a good point.  Simply saying EQ is more fun without going
into details is doing a dis-service to someone asking for specific info:

EQ is more of a "traditional" RPG (especially Computer RPG).  You have
levels, you have classes, you have races.  To advance you kill things - get
experience get more powerful.  UO is more open-ended.  The weakness I saw in
UO (and perhaps it has been changed - it's been a while) - there was no
advantage - in fact there was a huge disadvantage - to try to specialize as
a class - if that's your desire as a RP'r - for example a pure Fighter.  If
you didn't have "Corp Por" you would be dead so fast it isn't funny...

But for most people - the absolute worst thing about UOL is that the game
encouraged the absolute worst types of behaviors.  Get a gang of adolescent
players twinked and using cheats and exploits and pick off people one at a
time and take all their stuff.  Again - maybe it's gotten better since I was
in the game - but I completely gave up on it.

I think that the designers of UO are too hung up on their "theories" of a
virtual world at the expense of making a game that's fun.  I recall a huge
argument on the UOVault board where players suggested that they create
non-PVP servers (sound familiar?)  And Designer Dragon was against it with
religious fervor saying NO ONE would ever play on the PVP servers.  He also
complained of a problem with there being no recourse of players policing
anti-social players.

He is almost completely incorrect.  So far - on EQ the vast majority of
players have chosen the non-PvP server.  On the other hand - people don't
like the anarchic nature of anyone attacking anyone (if we want to play
Quake - we will!) and the response to the structured PvP world of the Race
War has been overwhelming.  As far as the policing issue - while it has put
greater pressure on Verant's GM's who have had varying degree of success of
responsiveness - My experience on the Xegony server has been that the
problem has been pretty rare.  At lower levels you get training of tough
monsters - and kill stealing.

I rarely sees these.  In the first case - there is no reward - and it's
risky..  These people get bored pretty quick - it's not like they are
looting all your items.  In the second case - there are ways around it. Go
to another area - or if there are spawns fast enough - you'll get some while
they are re-genning (and there are other tricks - esp. at higher levels)

So in summary - I think EQ is many many times more fun than UO.  If you are
a hard-core RPG'er who wants an open-ended world.  If you have a great
desire to spend lots of time crafting things and selling to other players.
If you want to be a k3Wld00d with packs of same picking off new and
mid-level characters and taking there stuff - you'll want UO.

If you LIKE spending most of your time hunting monsters and getting there
loot - and don't mind a weak economic system - like me - you'll find EQ to
be the fun game that UOL never was.

In closing - please don't mean this as a put-down to the many people who
find UO a rich fun environment.  It's just not my cup-of-tea..
