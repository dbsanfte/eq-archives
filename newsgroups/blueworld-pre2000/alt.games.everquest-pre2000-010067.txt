Hi all,

I think we are talking around the subject:
Verant tries to create some kind of 'cyberspace', a copy of a *real* world.

If Verant banns activities like those mentionned in the topic, they have an
aim. I would compare this to the AI in Matrix, which hunts every disturbing
element (here Verant is hunting players that do not respect their point of
view).
If you have ever played a Role Playing Game with a human as game master (and
not a processor), you would easilly see that you are NOT allowed to do what
ever you would like! Verant (or human game masters) have an idea of the worl
you live in because they created it, they translated thougths and ideas into
this artificial world, and they do not want this to be perverted by some
players.

EQ is not just like PacMan where you try to be the best at all costs (using
macros, slow downs or what ever): EQ is more like 'no one can tell you what
the EverQuest is'.

...but I thing a ban should be reserved for players doing REAL harm. If you
are told to stop, I think most people will do it - if they see the message
on the screen ;-)
