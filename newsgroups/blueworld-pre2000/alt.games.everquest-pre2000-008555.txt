Last night, while hunting in The Overthere, I got lost.  (I wasn't that
far from the city, but I have to keep my clipping at a minimum.)  I did
what I always do then--I gated back to the city and eventually my pet
caught up with me.

Today, the same thing happened, but...  The zone reloaded, as if I'd
gone to an entirely new zone.  For a moment, I wondered if I'd hit the
wrong key!  But sure enough, I arrived at my bind spot, still within the
Overthere.

I tried this with and without a pet, far away from my bind spot and
right on top of it, to the same results each and every time.  When you
gate within a zone, you lose your pet, your agro list vanishes, all of
that.  HOWEVER...  I could NOT repeat this on Antonica.  Over there,
when I gated within a zone, it was just as it always was--I simply
popped back to my bind spot with no zone loading.

Question is... Is this a bug, a change, or a nerf?

I'm thinking it's an intentional change, made to avoid exploitation.
The zones in Kunark are HUGE, and it'd be very easy I imagine for
someone who gets into trouble to simply gate back to their bind point
across the zone (think WK x4) to rest up and meditate while they wait
for whatever they were fighting to come find them.

Either way, it's a steal change/nerf...  But after stewing for about ten
minutes, I realize it makes a *lot* of sense.  Even if it's aggravating
to have to lose your pet just 'cause you got lost.  :>
