I mean the quest armors.  
  Barbarian in light blue = Warrior.  
  Barbarian in khaki = Shaman.

  Wood Elf in 000 black armor = Rogue.  
  Wood Elf in 010 green armor = Ranger.
  Anyone in 000 black robe = Necromancer.  
There are a few almost matches between class armor and plane armor, 
but it's still a handy to identify a person's profession at a glance.
