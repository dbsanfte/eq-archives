More mobs, and removing the silly cap that says you don't get xp for killing
green mobs.  Who the heck cares - the green mob xp should be statistically
insignificant in the grand scheme of things - why shouldn't we get xp when
the stupid lion comes up and claws us while we are medding?  Oh right - as a
high level character (let's say a necro) I could potentially level by
sleeping all night and letting my pet guard me, let's say in a goblin camp
in BB or something :)

Look at the Karanas - what a huge place.....and for the size, fairly
sparsely populated - oh sure, plenty of lower level mobs, but only a few
that cater to the above 20 crowd (aviaks, centaurs, etc)....

Add into this mix a better randomness for dropping items and we could all be
happy.  The main limitations I can see to this is how mobiles figure into
how well a zone can support players (ie a boatload of mobiles might
drastically reduce the number of players a zone can have operating at one
time).  Certainly expansions of existing zones would probably not hurt, like
adding a few additional zones to move around various hotspots on the map
(for instance, getting around Kithicor), or adding additional zones off the
water sharing zones (Oasis could have a small land bridge at the southern
end that extends out to a series of islands or something with lots of water
all around).

Just my 2cents, and prolly not worth that either :)
