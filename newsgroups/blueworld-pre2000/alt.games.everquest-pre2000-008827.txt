> Polygon Count

If you mean scene complexity, then yes, but that is a simplistic
term for it.  Polygon size affects fill rate.  Too many big textures
can overflow your video card's memory and cause texture thrash.

The video card has to do a certain amount of work to set up each
polygon and a certain amount of work per pixel to fill the polygon.
Lots of polygons means lots of setup.  Big polygons means lots of
fill.  Kunark has a lot of overlapping large polygons from bumpy
terrain and big trees and that makes for slow renders.

>

pushing my spell buttons, but the framerate is fine.
