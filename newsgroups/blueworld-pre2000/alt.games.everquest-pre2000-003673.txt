> If the EQ economy needs a cash sink, and I think everyone agrees
> it does, here it is -- taxes.  Pay for those roads and guards and
> such.

Why does it need a cash sink?  The EQ economy is not suffering from
inflation, but from deflation.  Prices in PP on all items, at every
level, appear to be in gentle decline.  Providing a money sink woiuld
probably drive down prices even lower.  Why would that be a GOOD thing?

It is true that many high levels have really fat bank accounts, and
some complain they have nothing to spend their money on.  But that's
because each character can use only a limited number of items anyway.
Once you have "the best" items, what's left?  And the best items are no-
drop, making them essentially worthless in PP terms.

> One other non-tax related note:  Weapons should get old and break
> and armor should get damaged and become unserviceable after a
> while so they must be replaced.

Including rare-drop magic items?  Given how much time, effort and
politics goes into acquiring these things, that would lead to a VERY
unhappy player base pretty quickly at the higher levels, especially if
item decay was random.
