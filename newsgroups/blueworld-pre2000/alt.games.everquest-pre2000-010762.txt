> Try the Lake of Ill Omen.  There are some great hunting spots around 
> the perimeter of the lake.  There is almost always at least one other
> person in the zone who is in your level range.  You have to pull from

I would second this.  You are a pulling machine in the lake with a decent 
bow and SoW.  Of course, my solution to this problem was to cultivate a 
strong group of friends who'll play with me regardless of the fact that a
ranger might take more exp than a warrior.  Also, you'll find yourself
QUITE useful at 22, as you finally get harmony and can pull from more
complicated spawns.  At the lake specifically, if you take your time and
are careful, you can pull all those goblins in the ruins.  Since very few
venture in there due to mob potential, this makes you valuable in ensuring
the presence of nice quality blue (or higher) level critters for your group
to grind through.  And it's something nobody else can do because a)warriors
can't harmony and b)druids can't handle getting hit a few times by a few
critters should the pull get screwed up.

Til you're there, I suggest the random wandering critters right outside..
just don't pull gobbos too near the ruins as you go by.  But, really, by
all means, get off the mainlands.  I've found picking up people is, in 
general, easier on Kunark.  So long as you don't mind reptiles in your 
group. :)
