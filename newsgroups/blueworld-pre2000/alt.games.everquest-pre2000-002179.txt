> The downside, which I'll point out before my detractors do,
> is that if you hadn't made it, boy, would life suck for poor
> Mairi the tailor.

Not really.  What's most important to Mairi?

1)  Her tailoring skill
2)  Her shop staying the way she wants it (no looters)
3)  Her time
4)  Her stuff

She doesn't lose any tailoring skill for being killed.  That's good.
Everything is locked down in the shop.  That's good, too.  It's fairly easy
to find a wandering healer (or a PC resurrecter).  That's good.  Mairi
doesn't carry 'phat lewt,' so she doesn't lose much when she dies.  Part of
that is being a smart shopkeeper - keep extra sewing kits, etc, in the
chests.  Part of that is - what PK would want sewing kits and colored
cloth? - so most of her stuff would very likely be left on her corpse.

However - I will point out something that you did not...

This only happened once.  If it had happened on a daily basis - or even a
weekly or bi-weekly basis... it would get wearisome.  Quickly.  Mairi wants
to work on her tailoring skill and dress people up, not run around looking
for the SBR to come save her.

Once, it is exciting, and maybe even charming.  Twice or three times, even.
After that (depending on the frequency), the experience becomes a lot less
exciting; aggrivation takes its place.  The time factor, again.


This is where the Game's responsibility is key.  The game needs to do two
major things:

1)  Make it so that Boffo doesn't want to kill Mairi.
2)  Make sure that Mairi has self-preservation options (maybe even
expensive ones), even though she does not have combat skills.


Oh... and I'd like to point out *another* thing...

Tailors have it easy.  We stand there in our little shops
click-click-clicking away, and can say 'I ban thee' as we please.  But
there are no mountains in my shop to mine; there are no trees to chop.
Come to think of it, there are no hides to harvest, either - and now I am
forced to find those outside of my shop, too... if I want to do #1 on my
list above.

Miners have it the worst.  They are relegated to the very few spots they
can forge ore into ingots somewhere close by... and their Loot is the most
coveted in the game.

I stand by my statement that public forges need to be installed along the
mountains.  I'll even make a story about the Minoc Mining Company branching
out, if you want.  :)  Fact into fiction, again.

Then there are people like me... who don't necessarily care about 'winning'

use them for what they're there for... that type of thing.
