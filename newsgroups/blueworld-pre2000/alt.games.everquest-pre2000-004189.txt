Actually, the AC patch had more noticable nerfs than EQ (keep in mind i am
pro AC).. in AC, they nerfed the price of Arrows. Before, a fletcher could
make lots of money buying parts, making the arrows, and then selling back
the finished product. You now get less money than before. That was a nerf.
They also changed it so you could not jump up from a lying down position. If
you were lying down, and a whole mess of crap was running towards you, you
use to be able to just jump up and run, now you have to go through the
sitting up animation, then the kneeling animation, and then the standing
animation - this has caused me to take extra hits before I could even draw
my weapon. This was a nerf. there was also several Landscape changes that me
and a former vassal used all the time.. they were both exploits, but still
nerfs :).. the EQ patch had a lot of character changes... the AC patch added
a lot of stuff (monster, dungeons, etc) but it still changed some stuff i
didnt like, while leaving existing bugs in the game (you can still load
crossbow quarrels in a bow, and exiting and re-entering the game quickly
will still give you an error message) - Turbine is acting very much like
Verant here, where they change things for the worse that were working fine,
while overlooking existing bugs..

In EverQuest, I bitch for months that monsters in Qeynos Hills teleport when
they run. Verant doesnt fix that, instead they change a monk's skill that
worked fine, or a Necro's spell that worked fine..

In Asheron's Call, I complain that when I exit the game and try to enter
back in with another character, I get an error message saying the character
is already in the game. Turbine doesnt fix that, instead they make it so I
can no longer jump up quickly from lying down. Players complain that people
are doing more damage on the PK server because they can put in higher damage
quarrels in their bows instead of arrows, instead of fixing that, Turbine
makes it so fletchers make less money now.

They are almost the same. They are changing/nerfing the wrong things.
