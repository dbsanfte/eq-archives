Virtually anyone. The druid at this point can solo so well alone
on anything outside that almost anyone else works  great. 
But as far as the best complimentary partner goes i'd say a shaman. 
Druid and shamans spells go well together, different lines of dots,
both can heal, etc. A necro with a pet is also great fun, there is not
much that the two of you and the pet can't take out. Even melee types
are good as long as when you are attacking a giant they know what to
do; they are NOT supposed to take hits in a two person group on a
giant. They are supposed to wait until you have the  giant very mad
with a dot and a dd then chase it beating on it with possibly a couple
smaller damage weapons instead of some big giant one. I say 2  smaller
weapons cause you don't want the warrior or ranger to be doing enough
big fast damage to get  hits.

My druid did this before with a ranger, i used very little mana as we
tackled 2 hill giants in rapid succession and the ranger took no hits
at all while continually beating on the hg from behind.
