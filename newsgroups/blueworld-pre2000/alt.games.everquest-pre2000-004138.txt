2) is a bug.  I can Flame Bolt my pet, and it doesn't care.  If I cast Cancel
Magic on it, it kicks my ass.  On a non-pvp server, this may actually be
considred an PvP exploit, eventually.  A level 50 Magician or Necromancer could
go into, say, BlackBurrow, summon a pet at the zone, Cancel Magic it, and turn
the zone into a death trap.  From what I've heard, it takes at least a pair of
level 50 warriors to safely kill an Earth pet, and since pets don't give exp,
you're going to have to hope some high level guild members aren't too busy
camping to come kill the thing for you.

They added the mob aggro code to correct enemy mob behaivior.  I don't think it
was supposed to modify pet behaivior.  Also, this change prevents you from
removing harmful effects from your pet.  I can no longer cancel Burnout to
return my pet's regeneration to normal, and I can't cancel any of the
magic-based Dots that enemies like to stick to my pet.  Since the level 8 pet
heal was apparently not itemized on the vendors correctly, this means that
Burnout is even less useful than it was before.  Below level 20, it is mostly
just a really mana expensive way to practice Alteration.  (And since Magicians
get almost no Alteration based spells before level 20, just Gate, Bind, Burnout,
your skill at level 20 will probably still be too low for the 20th level pet
heal to be reliable in combat.)

On a non-PvP server, your pet should not care if you cancel buffs on it.  It
currently does not care if another player hits it with Ice Comet, so I fail to
see why it should kick my ass for trying to remove a harmful DoT.  (And at the
moment, my Alteration skill is only 60, at level 22.  I can cast the 20th level
pet heal about once every four tries.  And I've wasted a lot of time fishing and
casting Burnout on my pet to get clear to 60.)
