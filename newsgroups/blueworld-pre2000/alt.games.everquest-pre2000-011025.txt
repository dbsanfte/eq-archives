There is also one buff series, fire shield; many have said it is worthless,
but up thru 27th, I did not find that to be the case; in any protracted

was never resisted.  I have literally been in groups where the only
things I could do were support my pet, bind wounds on the tank,
and flame shield every party member; a bunch of guys three or
for levels my senior who were quite appreciative of an extra ten points
damage going into their mobs every time one connected with them.

 Of course there should be little difficulty determining
who is casting this; the mage with the highest damage output from
it should do the job unless there are sound mana based reasons otherwise;
in a mass melee situation everyone not running a fire pet should likely be
tasked to put this on their own pet.  Tactics aside, I just thought it
appropo to mention the series since it is an instance of an unstackable
mage spell line.   (Worse, though not relevant to the Single Class
discussion per se, it doesn't stack with the Thorn series, although
