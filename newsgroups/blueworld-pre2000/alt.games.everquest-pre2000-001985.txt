Jeff,

As in real life today, or in real life in the old west 125 years ago, it pays
to be cautious when encountering the unknown.  As you have said, fear should
not rule our lives. One should however be aware that there are forces of good
balanced by forces of evil.  Without one, how are we to know the other.

I have three accounts on UO, one for me and one each for my two sons.
Sometimes we play as a team, sometimes we venture out solo. We play the Baja
shard.

Scenario #1:
When we go to the Orc Fort in the New Lands, we always get killed by
murderers. They always wait till we are weak from fighting and then are
cowards and attack from the rear. How do we handle this? We know their names
and run when we see them.  We have now decided it is not worth going there
anymore.  Should UO ban these players?  We all say NO!

We will eventually make a deal with a guild to go to the fort and put up such
a presence that the offending murderers will not attack us.

Scenario #2:
Outside of Moonglow there is a murders house. The offenders are Toxic Mustard
and Bean Bag, On the weekends there are at least 10 "Good Guys" trying
unseccfuly trying to kill these murders. The odds are 10 to one and these two
keep on fighting.  I have the deepest respect for these two.  Firstly they are
very good. Secondly they are almost impossible to kill. Their tactics are very
good and provide the three of us excellent sport (along with everyone else).
When we meet them in town, we treat them with the high respect they deserve
(unless they or we are gray at which point one of us dies).

Scenario #3
I have a miner that always gets killed and looted by a lone
murderer/ore-theif.  I took time out and formed a guild. I spared fencing with
my other members (my sons) and have 70 fencing now. Last night while mining I
was attacked from behind by a PKr. I killed her. Twice more I was attacked and
killed the same PK.  The final score was miner=3, PKr=0.  I feel better about
myself.  If you are in Baja and find a miner wearing a red scarf it is a
member of our guild. If you attack, we will fight back.  If there are three of
us, we will dog pile on you. We don't care if we die, we will sting you on the
way down.

About dressing:
If you go into the ghetto, do not drive a high visibility BMW and wear a Rolex
and a gold chain and medallion. You will be car jacked
_______________________________________________________

So. . . I give three votes for balance. Without contrast the gaming scenarios
are bland. Fighting NPCs and Monsters is for computer games. Fighting bad
humans is life. Ultima Online is more artificial life than a game. Keep Ultima
Online the way it is. If you have a problem with "Bad Guys" look to the days
of the old west in the US and solve the problem of nasty gunfighter/murderers
the way they did.
1. Hide till a hero comes along
2. Form or join vigilante groups (Guilds)
3. Head them off at the pass!

Inga (Baja)
