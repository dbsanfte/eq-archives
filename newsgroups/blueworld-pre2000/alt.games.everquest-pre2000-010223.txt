> 2) If you give your pet a weapon, can you get it back? Or should you only
> give him summoned weapons?

  You can't get the weapons back, so, yes, you should only give them
cheap or summoned weapons.
  Pets attack with weapons differently than players.  Pets disregard the
delay of a weapon, and always attack with a delay of 30.  The maximum
damage a pet can do with a weapon is two times the weapon's damage
stat.  If the weapon does better max damage unarmed than it does with
the weapon that it is equip with, then it will do unarmed damage.
  Basically, it is good to arm your pet with rusty scythes, twohanded
swords and halberds (halberds being the best) in the beginning.  Most of
the summoned weapons don't seem to be an improvement for the pets of the
same level, but a staff of tracing might improve your level 8 pet's
damage a bit.
