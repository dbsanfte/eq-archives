South Karana - close by, lots of blue stuff.
Go for the aviaks: Stick to darters if you're soloing, the rooks might
cut you a little too close for comfort.  Getting in groups is
relatively easy - but if there's more then 60ppl in the zone note that

portion of Splitpaw.  Get a group near one of the spires.  South
Karana by itself can level a person from 20-35.
