wrote:

>Sorry, I meant to compare damage shields with other ways of dealing
>damage for mana.  Assume that you have slowed your foes.  You end up 
>with your tank getting whacked on for maybe three minutes out of the

3 minutes?
Most mobs, even the 15,000hps plane mobs don't live that long.  In the
vast, VAST majority of cases it's right around 1 minute.  I think
you're underestimating how much damage a high level meleer is capable
of when properly equipped and hasted.  Toss in a pet class and a
couple of dots and those mobs just don't live that long.

>The damage per mana is quite good, even for a slowed mob that 
>is only getting hit by the shield once every 6 seconds (after 
>factoring in misses and double attacks).  You end up with something 
>like a 5:1 ratio.

No, I'm sorry, it doesn't come close to 5:1.  Infact, after Turgur,
you're hard pressed to break 2:1 ratio.  Mobs just don't hit often
enough before going toes up to make it all that good.  The mana
efficiency of damage shields borders that of a nuke when the mobs are
slowed.  And nukes are absolutely the worst mana efficient way to kill
things.

And remember damage shield works if the mobs in question if focused on
only one tank--have multiple tanks and damage shields start sucking
more.

>Damage per mana is only one simplified way of looking at what you
>really want to measure, which is kills per time, including recovery
>time between fights.

With Turgur, there's very little recovery needed.  Regrowth does 15hps
per tick, toss in SLN and it's 17hps per tick.  If the tank is a
troll, it's 23hps per tick not including the normal regen that high
level tanks get.  Against a turgurred mob, usually one greater or
superior heal at the end of the fight is sufficient before you pull
the next--this is with or without damage shield.

Without Turgur (and therefore making damage shields far more
efficient), you burn a lot of mana for combat time healing--and I mean
a lot.  I'm always burning close to 30 to 40% of my mana per fight
when there is no slowdown class in the group.  That means after about
2 fights, I have to have about 1 minute of downtime.

Damage shields when there's a slowdown class in the group does not
affect the downtime in any significant way--this is from my own
experiences and my own logs.  Infact, I've "forgotten" several times
to damage shield tanks when we had shamans in the group and no one
noticed any change (other than less battle spam).
