    Yes, he has a point.   According to 3Dfx, the Voodoo5 will not work
correctly if it is sharing an IRQ with anything other than PCI IRQ Sharing
or something of a similar nature.  If your Voodoo5 and your sound card are
on the same IRQ, one of them needs to change.

    To find this out, go to Device Manager under System Properties and
double-click on Computer.  That will give you the IRQ list.  You can then go
to the individual properties of each card to try and change their IRQs.
Some of them won't let you, so you might have to fiddle around to find one
that you can move.
