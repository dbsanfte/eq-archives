Charisma is much easier to get from items than intelligence is.

When you can afford it, get yourself a pair of electrum star ruby 
rings (7cha 5dex) for about 100pp each and a silver star ruby veil 
(5cha 5dex) for about 90pp.  

You will get spells to enhance your charisma, starting with 
Sympathetic Aura (16cha) at level 20.

If you still feel that your charisma is too low, you can also get 
gold cat eye bracelets (7cha) for about 18pp each.  If you are doing 
jewelcraft, then get the items as you gain the ability to make them.
