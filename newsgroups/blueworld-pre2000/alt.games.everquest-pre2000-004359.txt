It seems that there needs to be a better, and larger group of testers for 
these patches. I think that something like the following should happen 
(mind you this is just a suggestion and I don't know how they do things 
to the test server so.. grain of salt anyone?)
There should be a new test server. Right now the test server is a 
hodgepodge mix of things that will and might be. There needs to be a 
seperate, nearly regular server (preferably right off the main server 
screen), that will get the official patches a week or so in advance. Yes, 
people will be able to prepare for things before they go live (this time 
there would have been massive runs on the plains while mana stones worked 
and guards would have been slaugtered for steel) but a larger group of 
people would be testing it (because frankly, playing on the test server 
is a pain. Most people on it aren't there to help test. They're [mostly] 
there for reasons like slow machines that lag too much on servers with a 
normal population). Suppose only 1000 people played on the early patch 
server; The soundblaster problem would have likely been found before it 
went live.

Or, if that is too much at least put out a notice that you would like 
more people on the test server in the news box. Put instructions on how 
to allow people to play both (with dual EQ instalations), and just let 
people know it's there and why. And what they can expect when playing 
there. There are a huge percentage of people who don't know the server 
exists. And a huge percentage of those who do know it's there think it's 
an employee only thing or otherwise undesirable.
