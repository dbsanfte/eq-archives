Advice:  don't ask which game is best in a newsgroup devoted to one of the
choices.

As to your other questions, your computer is fast enough for the older
continents...barely.  If you double the memory you'll be fine.  If you want
to go to Kunark, you can also survive there, but with bad graphics (since
you'll have to turn off your clipping plane...which means you won't be able
to see very far).

Rogues are a grouping class.  If you want a decent shot at soloing much of
your career, your best choices are necromancer, magician, or druid.  Verant
(and others) will say that it's possible to never group, but that's
unrealistic at best.

Do not buy EQ.  They don't call it EverCrack for nothing.  It's terribly
addicting, and unless you already have a nymphomaniac or extremely
understanding significant other, you'll never get sex (for free).
