I played it for 6 months and got so bored that

>I have no interest in AC, haven't tried it,

I tried AC and like it lots more than EQ.


>It's a classic high fantasy RPG.

Yup, with player names like 'Kizmyass', 'Tritium
Isotope', 'Corpsefucker' (done in German, so noone
would recognize it)...


>The character and monster graphics are great.  
>The world has many interesting places to explore.

Agreed, nice graphics EQ has. One could call it
eye candy, but... ;-) EQ has zones, meaning when
you want to go from area1 to area2, you have to
wait for EQ to load all the relevant info for
area2 before you can proceed (this means you stare
at you screen saying 'loading please wait' for
about a minute before you can continue).


>Between the large number of races and the choice 
>of faces for each race, you have a fair degree of
>control over what your character looks like.

Yes, a Troll with one big green eye/one small red
eye/two big yellow eyes... It really does not go
much f(a|u)rther. The rest of your looks cannot be
changed at all. Equipment shows up on your body,
though some does not and some looks ridiculous.


>At higher levels, the monsters you have to kill are much tougher than
>you are, so it is necessary to cooperate in order to advance.  This is
>done by grouping.

Grouping is pretty much mandatory after a certain
point in your career. And good luck finding a group, 
unless you have some friends you regularly play with. 
Finding a group can take you from 1 minute to 1+ hour, 
depending on where you are, what lvl you are, what 
time you are on. YMMV, of course.


>Good gear is also important, but there is a lot of competition for the
>best items in the game.  This tends to bring out the worst in people, so
>I don't bother with it myself.

With nearly every server well-crowded nowadays,
there is competition for pretty much everything,
from monsters carrying great items to monsters in
general to guards (who should protect newbies) to
merchants... In other words, you can hardly turn
around without meeting a thoroughly annoying
player or two.


>Verant makes a huge effort to keep the game fair.

'Fair' being highly subjective.


>This means that if there is some ability or tactic that is 
>hugely powerful relative to the rest of the game, they are 
>likely to tone it down.

Which leads one to wonder why such tactics/abilities
were allowed to exist in the first place.


>People who have been enjoying that power tend to get 
>upset when it is diminished, so they come here whining 
>about nerfing.  They also whine about back door nerfing
>if someone else is made more powerful, so you really can't win. ;)

I won't get into this can of worms. Search for 'nerf'
at deja.com and read the 100s of posts if you want.
'Stealth nerf' would be another popular term.


>Also, you are not allowed to take advantage of weaknesses in monster AI.  
>For example, if you are on top of a building shooting arrows at a monster, 
>the monster can't figure out how to get to you and isn't going to run 
>away.

OTOH, monsters are free to attack you from wherever
they please. Like to be attacked through the floor
by monsters you do not even see? Happens at some 
places.


Some final notes:

EQ has quite a few bugs, some long-standing, some 
introduced with the patches.

The performance of GMs and guides is highly variable
and it is not exactly easy to appeal any of their
decisions. I count myself lucky that I never had a
problem that required guide/GM intervention.

Verant has a nice 'We are right, you are wrong'
attitude, unless presented with irrefutable evidence
to the contrary over and over again (and even then, they 
might just deny that there is anything wrong and refuse
to act - alchemy, anyone?)

Verant tends to shoot themselves in the foot. 'There is
nothing wrong with X. Any body who says so is wrong, wrong,
wrong.' Some time later: 'There was something wrong with X.
We changed it. Be happy that we provide such a great
service.' Both quotes paraphrased, of course.
