Not sure what you mean by that.  EQ's models are geometrically simple
but use very nice textures.  The anti-aliasing you get with the Voodoo 5
cleans up the geometry and the textures.  If you get enough people or
monsters in a small space, you will have a lot of geometry to render,
so you will really want the rendering power of the more modern cards.

In any case, I don't really care what a V5 does for Quake or Asheron's
Call because I don't play either of them.  I do want to know how my EQ
experience will change when I get a V5 ... and it looks great! ;)
