Greater Faydark has an annoying quantity of jerks and beggars.
I find that I trim my ignore list during downtime, and then
fill it up again when I go home to Kelethin.

That said, there are some generous people there as well: my
wife's wood-elf bard appears to be a heavy twink.  In fact most
of her goodies came from drive-by gifts or from my characters'
friends selling her goods at deep discount (dragoon dirk for 20
plat, etc).  She does not beg, but does tend to wander over to
find people who announce that they are giving things away.

When I started my elven cleric I did not bother going over to
find the shouters, I just smacked away at bats and such.  Of
course she was twinked with 50 plat and a suit of leather
armor, which was enough.

But there are kind folks everywhere, and there are folks
everywhere who will head back home and help out new characters
who share their race/class solidarity.
