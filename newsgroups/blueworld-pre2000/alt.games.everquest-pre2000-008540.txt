Any necro faces more or less this problem. Everyone ranges from scowling
to dubious (except Dark Elves, who are amiable with their entire city,
and I assume Erudites now, if one assumes Paineel to be their "true"
city).

There are outdoors merchants you can sell to. Many of them, unless you are
a rabid powerleveler that kills everything that cons blue to yellow. What
hurts you is access to the banks. On Antonica, you can bank in Neriak.

As a DE, I was scowled at first by the paladins in Freeport, so banking
was a no-no. Now, after a few hundred orcs, I'm "glares threateningly",
so it is possible that I will finally manage to get into Freeport up to
the bank (I don't trust the merchants yet). A gnome should be able to do
that too.

Once you are level 8 (and you shouldn't travel much till that anyway),
you can use "remote con" to check your faction. Visit an area under
Shadows, click on your "target", then go away in a secluded and empty
location, drop shadows, and /con. You can assess safely your faction
without running into danger.
