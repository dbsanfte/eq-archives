Let's look at the facts:

	o People skipped from the faerie island directly to the Gorgalosk
	  island.

	o Verant claims that this was a much easier way to complete the
	  islands.

	o By virtue of the fact that /everybody/ did this, we can assume
	  that Verant was right, and it is an easier way to complete the
	  islands.

Here's my problem with this: what sense does it make that the
islands get /less/ difficult? The faerie island is easy, the gorg
island is harder, but still quite doable, the pegasus island is a
lot tougher, about on par with the Azrak island.

Why do we now have to get to one of the easier islands by way of
one of the harder ones? It just doesn't make any sense. Sure, as
singles, the Azracks are much simpler than the stuff on the gorg
island, but unlike the gorg island, it's damn hard, if not impossible,
to get them as anything fewer than 5 at a time. With no mes, that
means people, a lot of people, are going to die.

Then throw in the (what I can only hope is a) bug where invis will
sometimes poof as soon as you use a teleporter and you spell instant
death upon porting (woo! Verant's favourite). With our party of
18, we had three with a broken invis instantly upon porting, with
a party of 30 (probably about what it takes to at least start
breaking that place), that's 5 down, instantly, or damn close, so
now we have to bring in a whole other group? 30 is bad enough.
We've cleaned out the whole gorg island with 20, why should we need
36 for one of the earlier islands?

Yeah, I'm a little peeved at this, since POS was their best zone,
to date, and they're fucking it up, starting to make it as intolerable
as POF or, now with super aggro range, POH.

Which brings me to another point: POH used to be a pretty good
zone, you could zone in, prepare, and start killing stuff. The MOBs
were tough, and it was easy to train unless you were a skilled
puller. You had to have a good team effort to make good of the
zone, but if you had it, it was a lot of fun. Okay, so using the
non-KOS forms was cheesy, and I can see why that's been removed,
but tell me, why, /why/ did you up the aggro range? Now, if you
fight at the zone in, you pull a train of at /least/ four MOBs,
with the broken pets, you can get many many more. Does Verant
actually /like/ the idea that zoning is deadly? Did they actually
think POF's zone-death was /good/ design?

Bah.

Maybe I'm just bitter.
