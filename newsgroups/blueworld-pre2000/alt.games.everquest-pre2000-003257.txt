i really like dennis mckiernan's stuff.  some of his early works (the
dark tide, shadows of doom, etc.) seem quite derivative of tolkien,
but it's still good.  and his later stuff is quite a bit more original
(voyage of the fox rider, eye of the hunter.)

on a more humorous note, check out lawrence watt-evans.  the
misenchanted sword, with a single spell, the unwilling warlord, and
more.  this series is related, but you can read them in almost any
order.

and of course terry brooks, katherine kerr, raymond feist, david
eddings, robert jordan, katherine kurtz are all good too.
