Picking your auspice and clan in Werewolf have much less of an effect 
than picking your starting parameters in Shadowrun.  In Shadowrun (at
least when I played it) you order four categories: resources, stats,
money and magic.  You get really good values in one, then mediocre in the 
next and so on.  If you want to be wired, you have to pick resources first.
If you want to be a mage, you pick magic first.  These choices are not
alterable later in the game and they heavily affect the skills that your
character can learn and use effectively.
