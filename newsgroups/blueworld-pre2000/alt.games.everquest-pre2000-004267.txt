I do a lot of buffing, so that preparation stuff (Clarity and 
Augmentation) can eat a lot of mana.  I think that anyone who has 
mana needs Clarity and anyone who swings a weapon needs
Augmentation (unless they want Alacrity instead).

In combat, I have short term buffs (Alacrity, Berserker Strength).  
My DoT spells are Alteration (and are much more useful than my DD 
spells).  Most of my debuffs are Alteration (Tepid Deeds, Listless 
Power and Weakness spells).  And, as you mentioned, Charm spells 
are Alteration, but I don't cast those often enough to care about 
the mana cost.

In fights against masses of foes, all I do is Mesmerization and 
Entrance.  In fights against two or three tough opponents, I 
Entrance the extras and debuff all around.  In fights against a 
single tough opponent, I am a debuffing machine.  When things are 
slow and the fights are too easy, I actually get to swing my
weapon. :)

My personal selfish reason for wanting Alteration is that it makes
enchanting metal for jewelcraft more tolerable. ;)
