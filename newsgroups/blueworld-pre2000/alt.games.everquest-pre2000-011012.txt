That /loc is a good idea :)

Mine is:

/yell
/panic
/say Help!  I am beset!
/gsay Help!! Help!!

It's a good idea to make up some kind of hot key for each of the 
basic messages: Help, Run, Train, and Corpse.  They seem to get 
used in that order too. ;)

I don't have a plain /loc macro.  It's fast enough to type /l
when I am about to die.

For pet classes who group a lot, it can be useful to add a line of
text in your commands to let the rest of the group know what you
are doing

/pet back off
/e tells her pet to back off and stop attacking

/pet attack
/em tells her pet to charge in to battle!

and if you are getting pets by charming, add "/pet who is leader"
to the attack macro so everyone will know exactly what is going on.
