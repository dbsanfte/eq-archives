I have an AMD K6-233 machine.  With 32 meg of Ram the game had
a LOT of difficulty, especially in Kelethin.  We bumped it up
to 64 meg and that made a HUGE difference in gameplay.

Then I spent $100 for a Voodoo 3 2000 card, and now things run
smoother and I can use a higher display resolution. 

I still drop sound regularly, especially in busy zones, and
have to keep my clipping plane very close, but it is better
than it was.

Still, consider their "recommended" setup to be the minimum
practical setup to play Everquest.

I resist the urge to buy a $1,600 computer to play a $35 game.
