An interesting observation, but then what evidence is there
that they accept the contract in the form that they first
presented it?

Since a contract is legally an agreement between two equal
parties, they are both required to sign in order to make it
binding - as we both seem to agree.

Therefore, if they don't sign their offered contract,
then it was never binding on either of us.

But i accept your argument about voiding a contract by
altering it after the other party had signed it.  So the
question arises: how many contracts offered by a company
do _not_ have a signature of an authorised officer
representing the company?  Any?

> Lastly, changing the contract with the intent of "tricking" the other

Good point, but i _do_ expect professionals to process contracts
