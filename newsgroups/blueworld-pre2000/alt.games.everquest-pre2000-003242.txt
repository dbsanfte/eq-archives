 The Otherland series by Tad Williams (3 of 4 books are available) is
not traditional fantasy, although a strong traditional fantasy element
is present throughout the plot. This element is interjected via the
exploits of two main characters who are addicted to an online fantasy
RPG game.

 One of the characters Thargor (a Barbarian Warrior) is the highest
level character in the game. His faithful companion is Pithlit, a
Thief. 

 I would surmise that Otherland could strike the fancy of some folks
who play EQ. Although the setting of Otherland is around 2050... and
the online fantasy RPG named Middle Country, is a wee more immersive
than EQ... Brad will not be surprised to learn that Barbarian Warriors
are still complaining about not being able to bind. ; - ) Just
kidding.

 At its very core, Otherland is LOTR... though many would not
recognize this fact. Unlike say, *cough* the earlier efforts of Terry
Brooks and a slew of others... No one can accuse Williams of
bastardized Tolk.
