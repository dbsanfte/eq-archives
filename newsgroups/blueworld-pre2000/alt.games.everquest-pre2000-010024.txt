The bandwidth requirements are more tied to the number of players, and
less to how many zones there are (their clusters are 100Mhz Ethernet
linked, probably. And maybe even on a separate net than the incoming 'net
trunks).

One of the most annoying feature is that, with Kunark zones being immensely
larger, and the bandwidth being specifically throttled back to the same
average amount as "old" zones, I get a lot of "phantom mobs".

By that I mean a mob that appears to be sitting at a specific spot, but
is in fact somewhere else entirely. It is very characteristic: you see
the mob, you can even walk up to the mob (looking in the white of the
gob's eye) and trying to cast starts up casting sequence, and it
aborts (after a ping round trip) with a message saying: "Your target is
too far away".

Then, when the update finally comes, the mob suddendly disappears. It
happens a lot, and for someone roaming the zone rather than camping the
sarnak fortress or the goblin mines, it is a pain in the a..e.

The "important updates" algorithm seem to be, let's say... a little
deficient :)
