UO *was* the first MMORPG, but that didn't make it appeal to MUD'ders; most
of us hated the game, if only because when it came out, it was the laggiest,
most antisocial place we'd ever seen.

EQ is a DikuMUD with a graphical shell, something most of us were looking
for years ago. It has glaring, stupid problems, and the dev team...leaves
much to be desired...but until competition arrives, it's the best out there.
As with all MUD's, word of mouth was key, and it was overwhelmingly positive
simply because *everyone* who'd ever played a MUD was playing EQ within a
month of retail.

AC is another type of MUD, where your character gets to choose his skills at
creation (which I totally forget the name of...Rom? Oh well), but the
graphics suck, and the 'evolution' of the landscape is nothing new- in fact,
in text based games, new zones are a once-every-few-weeks occurrence. It
doesn't appeal to this market because it's too bland and there's really
nothing there to engage the imagination in the way EQ does. Being six months
behind EQ didn't help in that department, either...but it would have gone
the same way in the long run.
