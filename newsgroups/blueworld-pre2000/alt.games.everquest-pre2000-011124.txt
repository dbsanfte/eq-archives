AC in EQ seems to be very similar to AD&D, the mobs need to roll a certain
number to be able to hit you (or have a % chance - works either way). The
higher the AC, the less chance they have to hit you (although as far as I am
aware, a mob will *always* have a chance to hit you). If the mob scores a
hit, they can then roll damage, and you get to roll your AC score against
this to soak some of the damage (this may not be 100% accurate, if the mob
gets a high enough roll then you may not get an AC check to it).
In simple terms you make 2 checks on AC Vs. a mob, the first is to see if it
hits, the second is to see if you reduce some of the damage.

If you want a "does this armour make any difference at this level ?" answer,
then it's yes, it does make a difference for survivability. The real
question should be "how much difference does it make ?" and unfortunately I
*still* haven't found any good sites that cover this 8(.
