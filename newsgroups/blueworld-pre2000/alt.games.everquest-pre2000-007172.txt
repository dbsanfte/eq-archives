The issue is not that picking pockets makes the Rogue class too 
powerful.  It's not about being unbalanced.  The issue is that the 
rogue is essentially stealing plat from the other players.

There is no valid use for the skill.  There is no reason to pick the
pockets of a monster you are going to kill and loot, since you would
get the stuff anyhow.  If you pick the pockets of a monster that 
someone else is going to kill and loot, then you are stealing their
reward.  The only potentially reasonable use for the skill would be 
to get loot off of a monster that is unkillable by anyone.

> If anything, the skill should be returned to how it used to be.  I
> always thought that the point of the skill was to allow rogues to take
> a little extra cash from a MOB that they're fighting, on top of what
> the MOB drops when it dies. Making the pick-pocket skill take from the
> main loot pool is like pissing in the wind. Stupid design decisions
> like this are why more and more people are finding Verant to be an
> incompetent development house.

I don't actually believe that the loot ever did come from a separate
pool.  I think that either it was generated completely at random (so
a rogue could pick the pockets of a single monster for eternity) or
it came from the monsters' pockets all along and Verant just said it
was separate.
