Hmmm ... while I take a more negative point of view on religion I would 
say there IS, even in EQ a penalty for opposing your god's will. It is 
miniscule I admit and probably couild be extended quite nicely to 
actually strip cleric and pally/SK of their powers: faction. After all if 
you do what you should your faction with your 'guild', i.e. your god will 
go up, otherwise it should go down. A very simple mechanism, in my eyes,
to enforce 'lawful' behavior would be to make the merchants who sell 
religious spells more faction conscious: they'll only sell to you if you 
have 'amiable' faction or something like that. Voila, now if you 
misbehave you still get to keep the powers you have but won't get any NEW 
powers.
