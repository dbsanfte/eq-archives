There are work arounds: I frequently group with non groups. That is a
difference of levels from 4-17 or so.

What happens is kind of a tit for tat type relationship. In practice, while
the lower levels are mopping up on the dregs of the zone, the higher levels
just kind of cool their heels waiting for loot and experience giving
monsters.

When one of the uber NPCs spawns, it would kill 90% of the players in the
area: This is when the higher level chr comes in off the bench.

Occasionally, the two different groups interact with heals, buffs, whatever
it takes.

At the end of the evening, the high levels have lost nothing since they let
the lower chrs kill most of the worthless crap for great experience.

They both benefit from not dieing as often.

This frequently degrades when a kill stealer or black hole of emotional need
shows up and has "Heal me!" macroed; he attacks everything that spawns, but
in the mean time it is good.

Trick is not to group but cooperate.

Think about it a minute, not sure what level or class you play, but say for
instance a cleric 5 levels below you is in the same area with you and
cooperating. Would you want them to leave? Would you be willing to bail them
out if they got in trouble? Seems like a reasonable conclusion would be to
form a non team.
