I've noticed a similarity between the stories of human's existance in Greek and Bible 
stories...

In Greek mythology, humans were once no different from animals, but a God called 
"Prometheus, the fire bringer" showed people how to build fires, and hence, civilization 
sprang forth. In vengeance, "the fire bringer" was cast down from "Mount Olympus" in 
shame.

In the bible, the devil (a fire bringer?) tempted Adam and Eve to eat of the fruit of 
Gods and partake in his wisdom. Before that, God had blinded them to the world... Once 
they had eaten of the fruit, they changed... God -cast down- the devil in shame.

Devil worshippers think of him as a tragic hero, who was cast down by God, who wanted to 
keep people blind to the world, like a pet. They believe that the devil is the only one 
who will answer their cries for help.

Weird similarities, eh? In both, humans start out as pathetic creatures who were no 
different from animals, who were changed by the -rebellion- of Gods.
