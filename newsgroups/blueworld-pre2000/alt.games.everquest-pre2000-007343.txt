A few ideas...  (keep in mind, these are all Fennin Ro prices, which
I'd imagine are kinda low across the board)

Moonstone rings (finger slot):  +30 mana each, that's better or equal
to any mana than you'll get from any peice of jewerly until you can
afford those Black Sapphire rings.  Raw mana is better than
intelligence anyway, and it gives a good (I think +3 or so)STR bonus.
At a 100 plat a pop, they're a great deal.

Petrified Erudite Heart(neck slot):  Pricey at around 500 plat, but
for +5 intelligence and +15 mana, you're simply not going to find
anything better.

Black Tome with Silver Runes(weapon slot):  With the raw mana bonus,
It's *probably* better than that stien until you get higher level.
Another good way to spend 400-500 plat.  

Dusty Bloodstained Gloves are about the best option on the Hand slot
that you're gonna find until you can save up the plat to pick up the
Impskin Gloves.
