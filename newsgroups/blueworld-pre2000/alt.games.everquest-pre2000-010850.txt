Hmmm.. better in what way?  I picked up the GeForce 2 mainly because it seemed
to be a great overall package:  More affordable, HW T&L, per pixel shading, 
and I really think in the long term NVidia has it together and is going in
a much better direction than 3DFX.  Whether the V5 antaliasing is better or
not, the GeForce still does a great job in my eyes;  with anti-aliasing on
all the jaggies disappear and the game runs reasonably well (considering what
I'm asking of it.. effectively rendering at 1600x1200) on my Celeron 300@450.

Of course, my old 3D card was a S3 Savage 4, so I may not be as "particular"
as others.  :)  As a note:  I remember reading about the GeForce not being 
able to anti-alias D3D apps in all cases; Everquest, I believe, was one of
these cases.  Since I can force FSAA and have no issues, I assume these 
reports were driver problems.
