We've investigated this and really don't feel that there's a problem after
the patch that included the innate resistance debuff for wizards 35 and
above.  As usual, I'll suggest that you hang out with a shaman, enchanter,
magician, or even a Necro.  They all have the ability to debuff resistances.
You might consider a bard as well.

Much like the Warrior, the wizard isn't meant to be a class that goes around
without another class to support it.  Warriors need healers.  Wizards need
resistance debuffers.
