> What then is to prevent a company from "deeming" that

Well, some people do try to do that, but they need to be able to prove
that such a contract was entered into.  Without your signature, this can
be very difficult.  They would also have to show that there was
consideration given (to receive some benefit under the contract, you
have to have given consideration (money or whatever) to the other party.

So simply having all these agreements that they state were accepted by
you isn't going to help a lot.

Back to the original point - if you change a contract and don't have the
other party clearly acknowledge and accept that change then I'd say you
ain't got a leg to stand on trying to enforce it.
