Pets have a max unarmed damage based on their level,
the ones I know off the top of my head

10th    12 dam
11th    14 dam
12th    16 dam
17th    19 dam  (may be higher, the only yellow I've
                          summoned at 16th, and I'm not sure that's
                          the best he had in him)

If you arm the pet, they keep their regular delay, but
get 2*Weapon Damage for their max IF it's greater
than their unarmed damage.

Since the hardest hitting cheap weapon is a rusty
2handed sword (dam 9) the breaking point is
between a 12th level summons and the one you
get from the 16th level summon spell.

At 24th, of course, you arm them with two weaps
to get dual wield.
