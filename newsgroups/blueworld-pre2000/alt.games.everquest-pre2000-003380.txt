>     I grouped with a guy in Unrest (level 27 bard) a few weeks back with my

A lot of people seem to have ego issues with level.  Of course, if 
they don't have these issues, then you will probably never know that 
they have the higher level character.

> Numerous times I have asked for help from a level 50 for a quest or
> something similar and, although they are under no obligation and perhaps I
> am too low to attempt this on my own, I get a quick turndown.  

I would expect you to get a quick turndown.  If you are too low level
to complete a quest, why not just wait until you are powerful enough
to do it with a group of friends who are your level?  I think it is
very cheesy to get higher level characters to babysit you on quests
that are too tough for you.

> I can
> understand being busy and I can understand having other friends to help
> instead, but how can every level 50 be that arrogant?  I'm sorry, and I know
> I'm not there yet, but I can NEVER see myself not helping a lower level at
> 50 if I'm not busy (and I'm 100% certain some of the 50s I've asked for help
> were not busy).

If you were level 50 and some lower level character asked you for money,
would you give it to them?  Almost certainly you will have the money to
give.  Would you consider it rude or arrogant to refuse the person?

Similarly, you may have buffs at level 50 that will make a huge difference
to a level 5 character.  It is fun to help out newbies, but it it not
terribly satisfying to hand out buffs on demand.  If someone shouts for
help, it is easy and feels good to get them out of a fix, but if they keep
bringing their fights next to you so that you will be handy when they get
into trouble (over and over), it can become pretty annoying.

A lot of people will appear to be not very busy when they are deeply
engaged in conversation.  Often when I am sitting in town, apparently
doing nothing, I am actually talking to my guild, discussing where to hunt
with my regular companions, describing jewelry effects and prices to five
different people, meditating mana to enchant metal, and trying to ignore
whatever the NPCs are saying around me.  I can only carry on about three 
different conversations without being completely flummoxed.  If someone 
walks up to me and starts talking, I may or may not even notice.
