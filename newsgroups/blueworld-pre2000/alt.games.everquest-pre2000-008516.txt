I too like to compare Norrath to a 'real' world.  When I venture out of my
home town and travel to distant cities I use a map.  After living in a new
town for a while, I no longer need a map.

At first I used maps of Freeport, but no longer need them.  I still refer to
maps of E Commons, but less frequently as my experience grows.

So, IMHO maps are not cheating and almost a necessity.

My main complaint is how Norrath has East and West reversed from the
coordinate system I'm used to in the 'real' world.  I'm constantly reminding
myself that, 'it's getting more negative so I'm going E'  which really goes
against the grain.

the magickal mystical WWW.

even extract a useful tidbit of information from this newsgroup!  ;)
