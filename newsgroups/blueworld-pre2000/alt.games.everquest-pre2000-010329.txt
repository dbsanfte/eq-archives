Problem solved - kudos to Michael at Verant's tech support.

To use a Geforce video card and an Athlon processor to run
Everquest, go to your bios control panel and turn down the agp
from 4x to 2x.  

It took a long time to try all the common solutions, but this
fixed it.  I hope that the add a comment to this effect to
their video-card troubleshooter as it appears that I am not the
only person with this problem and this solution.

Thanks to all here who offered suggestions.
