Act 1 Sc2 -After the Battle------

The weary companions sat down to rest panting after their long
battle,corpses lay strewn all over the windy Frontier Mountain hillside.The
Druid looked at his two friends;the Wizard grinning scratched the furry ear
of his wolf form;despite this the druid saw the glaze in his eyes and knew
he was mentally exhausted,there would be no more brightly burning fireballs
to save the day until this one had rested long.The Warrior licked the deep
gouges in his side,they were already festering.The druid could see there was
poison in the wound and the warrior was weakening.Being unsure wether the
warrior would live or die,the Druid pulled his spell book from his pack and
began to memorise the appropriate spell that would stem the tide of the
poison.Drawing on his mental faculties he came to the conclusion that with a
few more moments of concentration he would be good for perhaps one more
spell.The Warrior suddenly slumped back, his breathing faster but shallower
now,swivelled his yellow eyes to the Druid,"I don't think Im going to make
it,I cant hang on anymore".The Wizard stood up in alarm,his meditations
broken"Poison?" he said,"Dammit".The Druid stood,gathering the last of his
strength he began to speak the words-At first a trickle then a torrent of
cleansing blue fire poured from his fingertips,coalesced into a burning ring
of blue which momentarily seemed to ignite the Warrior.The warrior spasmed
then relaxed his breathing easier-the poison had passed.The druid's knees
buckled then gave,the Wizard half relieved half concerned  for his comrades
sat down to meditate and take the first watch looking at the two prone forms
nearby.
Just as he began to relax a little he was startled by approaching footsteps
and hissed a warning to his friends.The Warrior near dead stumbled to his
feet and bared his teeth the druid barely getting off his knees did
likewise.The Wizard remained seated, frantically trying to find the power
for one more spell.
Horrified the weakened trio saw a huge Troll, his green skin gleaming in the
sunlight,his one eye glaring balefully at them-baring down on them with
increasing speed,too weak to run the group realised judgement day had come.
The wizard stood beginning to cast his last spell closing his eyes as he did
so in anticipation of the crushing blow his instincts told him would find
his head,a blow which as it turned out never came.
The Troll's galloping charge had come to a halt mere inches from the Druids
face,the Troll grinned and casually scratched himself under the loin cloth.A
deep rumbling began from the Trolls chest and with horror the companions
realised the monster was about to speak to them.
It said"Hey Dude can I get SoW plz".
The three companions looked at one another-the druid rolled his eyes and
said"Err actually I dont have any mana and I dont have it memorised
anyway".The Troll grinned and said "Hey no probs dude i can wait a
little,mind if i loot this shit?",he said as he walked over to one of the
freshly slain corpses on the hillside.  Kneeling alongside it momentarily he
grinned and said,"WooHoo Phat Lewt:P.Can I get that SoW now?"
The Druid sighed noting with relief the Wizard taking the valuables from the
remaining corpses,he said "I told you I have no mana".
The Troll stopped, a scowl crossing his face"Look dude your what lvl 40?SoW
aint shit mana 2 u anyways and i know u got 40mana at least,you will med
that crap back in no time"
The druid felt a stab of frustration ,fighting down the rising urge to rant
at this strange being he said"Look I dont even know you,my group and i are
wounded-when i get some mana I will heal them,then I will get some more mana
and if you are around I will  cast the spell on you".
"Yeah fricken right ass-wipe,like I'm going to wait that long.If you didnt
want to sow people u shouldnt have chosen a SoWing class should u?"
Just at that moment the druid felt the familiar feathery,tenuous telepathic
touch of a person far away and began to receive a message----Hi,would u port
me and my buddies from BB to NK,we will pay u 10p-----The druid concentrated
his will and sent back his thought               ---Sorry friend im
busy-----
-----12p then?-------
-----Im in a group-----
-----How much would u do it for-----
-----I dont know 50p maybe?I dont want to do it------
-----50p????hahahahahaha yeah right look i tell u what i'll give you 20p----
The Troll snarled"U gonna sow me or just prove to everyone what a selfish
asshole you are?-------No sorry I dont want to teleport you---
"Ok ok i'll memorise it"
-----Man you are greedy-------
The Troll said "Look ill give u 400s for it to make up for the trouble"
"I dont want 400s thankyou"
The druid began to meditatate and rememorise the spirit of Wolf incantation
as he did so he realised that he had not detected the footfalls of the
cheery young wood elf who now stood beside him.
-----My buddy says u wont port us-look man I really need to get to NK it
wont take 5 minutes,will you do it?----
The druid looked up into the eyes of the Elf whose grin widened"Hey man!Can
I get a SoW?"
The Druid began to giggle a little at first,then laugh and then began to run
around in circles
------Look plz man......-----
"Geez I only want one fricken SoW"
"Is he sowing or what?"
------20p???-----
The Druid's mad circular dance spiralled around the small group-even as he
spiralled,so did his sanity spiral away from him.Finally reaching the centre
of his spiral he stopped howling out his anguish to the skies his true
identity revealed to him at last"I AM A SOWING MACHINE".
