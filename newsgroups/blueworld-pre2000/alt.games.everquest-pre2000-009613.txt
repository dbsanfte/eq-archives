I realized this myself awhile back, and stopped worrying about ever making a
profit at jewelry.  I got into it to be able to make stuff for myself and my
friends, and I do it for cost of materials.

As for tinkering (the subject), that skill is so totally befuckt that I
stopped trying to raise it once I hit 101.  At 102, the only thing that will
raise it is aqualungs, and that's 250pp per attempt.  Aqualungs sell for
750pp, for 50 charges of enduring breath.  That's 7pp per charge.

When aqualungs trivial (at 148, I think), the only thing that will raise the
skill is rebreathers.  950pp per attempt, they sell for 2000pp.

Since you can easily get a backpack full of summoned waterstones for 10pp or
less, the market for aqualungs and rebreathers is somewhat limited.

Sure, there are some cool items you can tinker.  The spyglass (which doesn't
really show you anything you can't see anyway) and fireworks (which are a
pain in the ass because they don't stack, and don't disappear when
discharged) come to mind.  Gordon has said that tinkering will be looked at
sometime after they fix rogue poisoning.  I'm not holding my breath.
