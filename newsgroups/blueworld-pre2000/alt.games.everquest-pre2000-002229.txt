>What we need in UO is a way to punish murderers akin to the way it

Stat loss (which btw pkers really really hate) is one way we are doing
this. Yes, it is possible to avoid the stat loss, but it's very
difficult (one of the most popular GM calls is "can you tell me how
much longer until I am blue?"). There's also loss of access to towns,
increased cost of vendors, etc. What other things can you think of as
in-game penalties that might work?
