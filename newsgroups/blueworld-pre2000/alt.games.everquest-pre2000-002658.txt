Judging from your response, I doubt you'll allow yourself to see any
problem, but I suppose I'll make the attempt.

My situation is this.  I work online... nothing fancy, I just moderate a
couple message boards.  So long as the boards remain trouble free, my time
is my own, to do with as I wish.  However, if someone decides to act like a
total jerk and annoy everyone there I have to be there.   Other people can
look in on it, and see how things are going, but I'm the only person with
the access to delete the messages and put a stop to it.

I use ICQ to accomplish this.  If there is a problem, someone tells me, I
take care of it.  In a game that disallows ICQ, I have to constanly exit to
find out myself if something needs to be taken care of or not.

Quite aside from this, entirely too much time in EQ is spent sitting on your
ass.   Either you're sitting for a half hour waiting for hit points to come
back, or you're sitting there for 10 minutes staring at your spellbook while
you meditate... or you're sitting there for 10 minutes staring at your group
member who is staring at thier spellbook.  These times wouldn't be nearly as
annoying if there was anything to do, such as talk to your friends.

Not a lot of my friends play Everquest, as I've met them over several years,
playing many differnet games....  and of the few that do, not all of them
are on my server.  One in particular I almost never see anymore, because she
plays on The Rathe while my characters are all on E'ci.   We still want to
talk, even though she doesn't want to leave all the friends she has built up
there any more than I want to leave all the friends I have found on my
server.  All of my friends however have ICQ.   But, since ICQ is disallowed,
I quite often end up sitting there doing nothing, talking to nobody as I
wait, when I could have easily had a great conversation with whoever was on.
If I exit to talk to the person (if they are even out of the game as well)
I still have to do the sitting on my ass waiting time when the conversation
is over.
