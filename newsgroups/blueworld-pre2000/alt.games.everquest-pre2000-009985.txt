It's quite believable to me.

When my enchanter hit 20, I got on a little early the following evening.
I decided to spend the couple hours before my normal group gathered in
setting my specialization.  So, I went and trained a point in each spec,
and went off to a spot away from most people (to avoid spamming them with
spellcasts) and cast strengthen on myself until Alteration was past 50.

I can't speak for whether lock picking gives success/failure messages, but
spellcasting sure does.  I was watching TV as I pressed the cast button,
checking back at the screen every minute or so to try and spot some of
the skill increases.  I caught enough to let me know how my progress was
going, but missed a lot.  If a GM had been sending me a series of tells,
say a minute apart, I likely would have missed them unless I was lucky.
When casting a low-mana, fast-casting level 1 spell repeatedly for
long periods of time, you generate a lot of spam, and stuff scrolls off
pretty fast.

Granted, I could have been more aware of my surroundings in-game by
turning off all of the zone-wide channels and all spellcasting messages,
but that shouldn't be necessary to avoid a banning.  Hopefully the story
is nothing more than a troll.
