Well, I'm going off the UO model. A year after intial release, they had
about 100,000 active players, and released UO:The Second Age. 

Since people already playing got it for a significant discount, that
wasn't a factor for counting new players.

75,000 NEW accounts appeared on the roles.

As it currently stands, EverQuest is bigger than UO (more units sold,
more active subscribers, same amount of time) and the expansion is
slated for release in March. Even assuming it sells LESS than UO:The
Second Age, you're still looking at the potential of up to 50,000 new
accounts over the course of 3 months (thats when sales figures are
usually going for). That's fully a third of the current active accounts,
and at LEAST half the players in the game now are still under 30th level
(the mid level and above range).

That's what I'm basing my assumptions on =).
