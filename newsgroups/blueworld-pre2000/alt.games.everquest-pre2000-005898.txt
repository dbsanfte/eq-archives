Off the top of my head:

Illegible scrolls from orc oracles, give to Generaly Jyleel in
Felwith paladin guild

Hunt the orc runner in Butcherblock (near the 2 huts) - get
pouch and give to either Dill Firestone in Kelethin Ranger
guild or to General Jyleel

Hunt for corflunk and zarchoomi in Butcherblock, give heads to
_NAME_ bloodforge in basement of Kaladim warrior guild.  You
need to give him both heads.

In Crushbone, collect the warlord's bracer and Crush's tunic
and then do the screaming mace quest with the slaves in the
cabin.  (I decided to wear the tunic instead)

In Crushbone, kill the slavers collect keys and free slaves. If
you give a slave a copper they will tell you what key they need.

In Butcherblock, kill Krag Elders and give the talons to the
dwarven paladin in North Kaladim.  These might start out
indifferent to you, and so you might decide not to do this
quest.

There are more, but if you go into every guild hall and hail
everyone in the hall you will find most of them.
