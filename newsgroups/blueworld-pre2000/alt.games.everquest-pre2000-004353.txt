I think there's a misunderstanding about what a "Critical Hit" is. When you
score a critical hit, you *always* do more damage for that hit than you
would have done had you not rolled a critical hit.  In other words, instead
of hitting for *2* damage, you hit for *32*, despite the fact that your max
damage is *54*.  Basically, you can still get a bad roll.
