I think you have sample size problems.  I don't think I've met
even one of the people you describe.  Of course, given the time
of day and the age of the server on which I play, the stereotypes 
are all different.

High Elf Female - Usually going to be an Asian player.
	Hard for me to say much beyond that since I don't
	speak Japanese.

High Elf Male - I only know one and he is, well, not a 
	powergamer, he hates guilds, and he's rollicking good
	fun to have in a group (along with his Barbarian
	Warrior wife).

Dwarven Male - Some min-maxer who picked the dwarf because
	of the high total for primary stats (before people
	figured out that STR and STA are useless to Clerics).

Dwarven Female - Do these exist?

Dark Elf - Subject to the Dark Elven tendency towards being
	total dorks, but not as bad as Necromancers and
	Shadowknights.

Human, Halfling or Gnome - Probably a role player. ;)

Erudite - Did you know they could be Clerics?

Of course, despite Alasdair's ranting and my snide comments,
there are role players of every race and class.
