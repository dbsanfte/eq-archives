   One mud I played on had a very nifty global auction system. People would
place an item up on the block, and it was then broadcast on an independent
auction channel mud wide. People would then bid ebay style with the server
handling the auctioneer task automaticly (It even counts down with "Going
once! (3 second pause), Going twice!, (3secs), SOLD! to player name for X
pieces"). It would get the item from the player, auction it, then instantly
deliver the item to the buyer while automaticly depositing the cash in the
sellers bank account. This caused a true player valued economy for an items
worth. What we have in EQ now is a suckers/traders economy, people are
mostly looking to sucker someone or only do things in trade due to the
devalued platinum piece on many servers. This can also be reserved for
premium items and not as a conduit to pass spell components. Systems like
this can make the game alot of fun and give a real competative bidding
opportunity while giving every player a shot at an item on the block to bid
upon.
