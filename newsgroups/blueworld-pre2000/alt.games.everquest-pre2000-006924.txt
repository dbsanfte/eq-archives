For a friend or a guildmate, I will combine materials at cost.
They agree to live with the success or failure.  I call it the 
jewelry lottery. ;)

For a stranger, I would do it if it were a non-trivial item
that I might get skill from.  People generally give me tips when I 
succeed, usually in the 10-50pp range.

On E'Ci, the choker sells for about 500pp.  The black pearl
costs about 200pp.  I think that any jeweler would probably agree
to make the choker for you for about 100pp.
