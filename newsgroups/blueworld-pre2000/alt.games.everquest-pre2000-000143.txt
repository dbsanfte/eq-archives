    Hi if I can add my "layman's" two cents to this stimulating but
off-topic subject. I agree with the statement "The key word is THEORY". The
scientific method works with first a theory with predicted results. Then
secondly with experiments that produce the results. If the experiment can be
repeated by some one else with the same results, then the theory is accepted
as a scientific fact. The one problem is, and this has plagued almost all
scientist's, is that the results may in fact be produced through another
mechanism then the one the theory predicted. So not even Einstein's oft
quoted but poorly understood work, is infallible. And in fact if I'm not
mistaken, Quantum mechanics came about in attempt to deal with some problems
within Einstein's work. Within Einstein's universe "faster then light" is
impossible, due to our understanding of how light and time interact. That is
not to say in our universe it IS impossible, thou all evidence point's to
that conclusion. Making it extremely IMPROBABLE that "faster then light"
exists. Today's theories allow for "black holes" "worm holes" and "super
strings" that are all considered to work, to one extent or another, outside
of our universe/dimension. And some theories use these to show how "faster
then light" and/or "time travel" could exist. In fact one theory, the
overused "Star Trek/Super Man" concept is that going "faster then light"
would in fact, send you back in time. But because of the very problem of us
being part of the equation, and there by unable to step out of the picture
to see the bigger one. And our vast, though very limited, knowledge of the
universe's workings, ANY theory, no matter how half-baked, has the
possibility of being true. Though probability is another question all
together.
    As for other dimensions. First of all, some theory's state that "time"
IS the fourth dimension, as for the truth to this, it's a matter of
interpretation. Beyond that there are two schools of thought. One where due
to the "uncertainty principal" when a photon/particle has a choice of states
and or paths it chooses both, and where we decide to look determines which
one we perceive to exist. So it is extrapolated that every possible outcome
to every action occurs, i.e. when you walk across the road you trip, fall,
and get run over by a bus, and another where you don't trip, etc. Making for
a infinite amount of dimensions. The other being a mathematically concept
which is merely a tool used with "knot" problems, and really has very little
connection with anything in the "perceived" real world, not saying the first
one does either. And this one is where the concept of there being a numbered
amount of other dimensions comes from. So one is quantum in nature the other
spacial, and both at this point are merely theory's. So in conclusion any
thing is "possible" and in fact according to one dimensional theory every
thing is a certainty. But the concept of "proof" is at best a slippery one,
where perspective as much as evidence, is a factor. And personally I take
everything with a "grain of salt" but with an understanding that I don't
know everything and could be surprised. Pleasantly so I hope.  Later
all.....  :o)
