From an enchanters perspective:

Our ability to dish out a decent amount of damage is almost solely
dependent on our animation, wich seems to be about 5-9 levels below us
in abilities (in the best case where we get a blue conning pet.) Or we
have to try to solo in extremely dangerous circumstances where we are
certain to have several mobs, so we can charm one of them to wack the
other(s) with. As for DD and Dot's everyone of the wis and int
casterclasses dish out harder damage than we do  after a certain level
(8-12)


At upper medium levels 30-38 I say our stun line of spells get
resisted too frequently. :(  Color flux/shift wich is the enchanters
only means of TRYING to stay alive in an often hopeless sitution,
where our Area Mez gets resisted, or we have several or one very aggro
mob, like our former pet, beating on us.

Reason is probably that the stun line of spells (Color series) is a
different line (Divination) than our one maxed specialization skill
wich for most enchanters are specialization alteration. 

I can't really understand why the first color series (Stun) were made
Divination (the same as invisibility and illusion) 

Color Skew was made Alteration however, and that seems to help
somewhat for the high level survivability of our class, but if you
check the played time for enchanters just getting level 44 versus
other classes something obviously should be done about the class for
lower levels than 44.

And the other line of spells wich we are so valued for is neither
within our specialization namely the Mezmerize series wich is
Conjuration, all in all this sort of makes it very very difficult to
play an enchanter, especially at mid-higher levels.

At about level 30+ you notice a huge difference in casting success,
and resist rate between the spell lines you have max specialization
for, and other lines.... And this does not bode well for an enchanter.

Both The mezmerize series and the Flux series should have been
Alteration, for us enchanters to be able to be effective at what we're
supposed to be good at.....

Being the traffic cop in the group.

As it is 29-43 we're just clarity chumps, and PC merchants.
