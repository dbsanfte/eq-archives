SolA is a washout if you refuse to hunt blues.   You CAN solo there, though.
SolA is a washout if you plan on grouping.

SolB is a much more appropriate zone for a 34 cleric, and you shouldn't have
too hard of a time finding a group, if you have a lot of grouping experience
(if you suck in groups, your reputation will be built as that quickly).

Upper Guk is worse that SolA for xp at 34.  Lower Guk is much more
appropriate.

Cazic can be good experience if you get into the Avatar of Fear group or the
Archon Temple (the temple floor is ok, but the temple itself is where you'll
get more consistent xp).

I can't really think of any decent place for a 34 cleric to be soloing
(clerics aren't supposed to solo).
