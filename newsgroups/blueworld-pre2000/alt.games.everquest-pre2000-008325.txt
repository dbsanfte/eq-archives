 - April 6:  The Kunark store opens, and I gleefully order my copy
within an hour.  I feel good, having beat the rush!  Since the opening
was still weeks away, I didn't bother with overnight shipping.

 - April 14:  I am finally billed.  No email is sent to me.

 - April 19:  Still no email.  I track down the number for shipping
information and call.  My order is STILL SITTING IN THEIR WAREHOUSE.
The operator tells me it should be going out this afternoon via Priority
Mail.  I am already regretting not asking for overnight shipping.

While sitting on the phone waiting for my shipping information
(blissfully short wait, I have to admit), there was a recording about
how they had been overwhelmed with orders, etc., and--direct
quote--"Please be assured that you will receive your software and be
ready to explore the Ruins of Kunark on the release date of April 24."

I wish I could have faith in that implied guarantee, but I don't.  I
have no doubt that even if I don't get my software until June, I'll be
the one who's SOL, with no compensation of any kind, or even an
apology.  I just know that I love EQ and one of my favorite things is
seeing new places, so I'm dying to explore Kunark PRE-spoilers, and I'm
going to be disappointed if that doesn't happen.

I don't Verant bash much, nor Sony bash, but I have to say...  This was
poorly planned.  It took *eight days* to bill me on a credit card,
another five to even get my order ready to go.  (Heck, even checks go
through in three.)

Even accounting for non-work days, that's still just too long,
especially considering I was one of the first people to put in an
order.  (Sony apparently doesn't hold to the first-come, first-serve
idea.)  Sony/Verant *knew* that Kunark was going to be big and that
people were chomping at the bit for it, yet it looks like they did not
do their homework enough to ensure they'd have the staffing to handle
things.

I don't know when they'll do a second expansion or EQ2, but I sure hope
that the online ordering then goes better than this has.

For the first (and hopefully only) time, I can honestly say I am not a
satisfied Verant/Sony customer.
