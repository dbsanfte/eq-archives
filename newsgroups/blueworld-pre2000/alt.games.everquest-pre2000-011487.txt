I would first point my finger at the Linksys and the Creative board, as I
have run Everquest for over a year and a half on a P2B system, using the
Voodoo3 and the Voodoo5, my current card.  I have never had any of the types
of problems you're describing (or any problems, for that matter).  I have
run it under Win98, Win98SE and Win2k since RC1 with no problems.  I have
used 56k dial up, then moved to DSL, and now to cable.  I am running a 3com
3C905B for my current NIC and an SBLive.  In short, I don't think it's EQ
not liking your mobo.

For starters, I would try loading the "failsafe" settings in the BIOS and
see if that helps.  Make sure, of course, you have the latest drivers for
the linksys (if there are any.  I don't keep up on Linksys since I have
never seen anything but trouble with those cards) and the SB PCI.  If that
doesn't work, consider springing $100 for a 3com 3C905 and an SBLive Value.
You should be able to find each of those cards for ~$45 on
www.pricewatch.com

Good luck!
