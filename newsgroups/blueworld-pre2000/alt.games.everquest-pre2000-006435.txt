Most of those design mistakes were just that. Things worked as they
intended, but created results they didn't want.

Take machine gun fireballs. They worked as intended. The problem was
simply people who created macroes to fire off an impossible number of
fireballs at the same time, creating imbalance.

One hit weapons did exactly as intended. They added all the appropriate
numbers and did exactly as they were designed in combat. Certain
combinations of spells and effects simply created a weapon that could
kill in one shot.

Paralyze worked exactly as intended. Being unbreakable until duration
went off, originally, simply meant you were rooted in spot and unable to
do anything while PKs mocked you, then killed you.

This is 'design mistake' as opposed to a bug.
