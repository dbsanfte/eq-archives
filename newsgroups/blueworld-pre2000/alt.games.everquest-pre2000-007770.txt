I will also enchant bars for quests for free.

I don't have any objection to enchanting bars for non-enchanter jewelers
in principle, but I rarely do it because it takes so much time.  I once
enchanted 100 bars of electrum in exchange for an FBR.  It took me about
three hours.  I will enchant a few bars of metal for nice people for free.  
I won't enchant metal for jerks at any price.

It is possible to practice the skill on unenchanted metal.  The resulting
items sell to a merchant for a little less than the cost of materials, but
the difference in cost from a merchant between enchanted and unenchanted
items is very similar to the price an enchanter would charge for enchanting
the metal.  So, most non-enchanters (and many enchanters) practice on 
unenchanted metal.

This is the range of prices I have seen for enchantments:

Silver:   2-5gp
Electrum: 5gp-1pp
Gold:     1-3pp
Platinum: 5-15pp
