Ok, I'll be honest...I usually don't group because of the loot question.  It
become way to much of a hassle to follow the alphabetical, especially if
someone gets dropped (like THAT happens in EQ <g>)  In the end it depends on
how much you trust your group mates.  What we have done in the past is one
person loots and sells.  When they become encumbered, they pass the loot
sacks onto the next player and continue looting.  At the end of the hunt, we
do a /split on the take with ONE person selling all of the loot.  That
eliminates the cries of he got item A which is worth more than item B.  If
someone is specifically looking for an item, i.e. sbs, that person usually
will get the item if it is found.
