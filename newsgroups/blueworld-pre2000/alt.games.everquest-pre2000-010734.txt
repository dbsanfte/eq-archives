I have been happy playing a human monk for a while now and have found the
ability to dodge is much more important than the ability to hit hard, if you
can avoid damage you can stick around longer eventually finish the creature
off, sure the ability of hitting three times and killing all is certainly a
fun thing, but the longer you are fighting, I have found the quicker my
points in things such as dual wield and round kick go up, then those skills
can increase the damage I do. Without a doubt put at least 1 point into
dodge, I made the mistake of only learning it at lvl 5 and at lvl 9 i can
dodge a fair amount of things but I know that from lvl 1 this skill would
help more than anything else.
    In comparison to other monks about, I probably would not do as much
damage as them, 12-13 points for a punch and about 15 for a round kick, but
I can hopefully get in a couple of more hits in higher levels, who knows..
this is the first monk I have played and I personally think they are a lot
more fun than people give them credit for.
