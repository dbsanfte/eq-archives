The Problem:
My wife (god love her) is a games-playing fiend, especially RPGs. Over
the last several years she's probably bought and played most of the
popular RPGs. of the dozens she's bought (and I've installed), only ONE
has loaded and played flawlessly without all kinds of system tweaking
(and that was Myst). All the rest have been gigantic pains, requiring
many hours of fiddling with system settings, bios settings, windows
settings, and so on and so forth. Everquest is, far and away, absolutely
the worst I've encountered. I've tried all manner of settings,
tweakings, and general fiddling about in order to get this GD thing to
play consistently without any luck whatever.
When trying to start the game, she d'clicks on the icon, the screen goes
to black, and then:
1. most usually there is a single "click" from the display, and then the
Black Screen of Death and a complete system lockup; or
2. sometimes there are multiple "clicks" from the display, and then the
BlkSoD and complete lockup; or
3. about one time in twenty (and it's getting worse), the game will
start and run.
Oddly enough, TestEverquest usually runs without difficulty. However,
she can then disconnect from the test server, attempt EverQuest, and get
the system lockup.

In the past, the clicking followed by lockup has meant that the
display-monitor combination was being overdriven, that I was trying a
screen resolution that was beyond the capability of either the adapter
or the monitor or the combination. However, that simply cannot be the
case here, as we're trying to run EQ in 960x720 @ 16bpp on the adapter
default.

Outside EQ, I can load the complete MS-Office suite, the entire Corel
WP8 Suite, run MSIE and Netscape, and only get a moderate performance
hit... and no lockup, even at 1152x864. and so it does not seem the
adapter-monitor combo is the problem.

The System:
ASUS P2B mainboard (with latest BIOS)
224M RAM (PC100)
3dfx Voodoo5 5500 (latest drivers, properly installed)
Creative Soundblaster PCI128 (slot 4, IRQ 5, legacy emulation disabled,
joystick disabled)
LinkSys LNE100TX Ethernet Adapter (NIC), PCI slot 2, IRQ10.


Solutions Attempted:
For a while it helped to re-install EQ every time she wanted to play,
but that no longer works.
Norton Diagnostics regularly reported corrupt files and executables.
Without exception these turned out to be EQ files or executables, so
I've set NDD to run on a daily basis during slack time.
I've swapped out memory, sound card, NICs, and HDDs. No diff.
I've changed BIOS settings (current ones available if you need to see
them).
I've changed settings in the 3dfx toolkit... no dice.


Personally, I think EQ is a shabby bit of software, but there could be
something wrong here that I'm missing. Any hints, advice, suggestions,
or help would be appreciated. This business is driving me nuts!

Thanks in advance.
