"Vincent Dupuy" <sales@go2vortex.com> wrote:

>If I understand correctly most of the reading I've done, Reinforced Leather
>is the best non-magical armor for druids. But they required high Tailoring
>and High Quality pelts. Where can I get these as a not-so-rare drop?

If you're looking to make Reinforced Leather for medium sizes, then
you're in luck. The lions in the Commons and the Karanas (and
presumably cats elsewhere) drop the HQ cat pelts reasonably often.
Plus, since these pelts are not generally useful except for tailoring
reinforced armor, players generally sell them to vendors. This means
you've got a good chance of just purchasing these in zones with lots
of cats.

If you're looking to make large size armor (unlikely if your target
market is druids :), then you have a serious problem, as this requires
HQ bear skins. These are in very high demand already, commanding as
much as 15 plat per skin on the player market. 

Finally, for the small druids, you'll need an HQ wolf skin. Bad news.
I've only ever seen *one* of these. While they're not incredibly rare,
apparently they are even rarer than HQ bear skins.
