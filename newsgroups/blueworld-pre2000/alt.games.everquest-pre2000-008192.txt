The problem comes when macroing becomes widespread.  Of course if
there are only 5 people macroing and 400 playing the impact is minimal.

However, if there are 1500 people macroing and 400 people playing or
1000 people macroing and 2000 people playing you will notice the effect 
on lag.  Even if they go into an empty zone to do it, those zones are
managed by the same processors that manage busy zones (assuming Verant
has done their load balancing properly).

If lots of people were to start macroing, it would impact performance.
Verant would have to take measures to combat it, eating still more 
server resources and taking more of their programming time.

Now do you understand why macroing is a bad thing?
