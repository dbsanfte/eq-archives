Azmogeddon wrote:
> 
> SK's don't completely negate their exp penalty, but the penalty is
> spread around the group.

The shadowknight still has the penalty.  If the shadowknight were 
soloing, he would have to kill more things to get to the same level 
as a warrior.  In a group, the whole group has to kill more things 
for the same benefit if they include a shadowknight instead of a 
warrior.  The effect is averaged over all the party members, so you
probably won't notice a difference unless you switch from grouping
with all warriors to grouping with all hybrids.
