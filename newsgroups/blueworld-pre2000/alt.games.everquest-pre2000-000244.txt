Arthur C. Clark has been quoted as saying that "to any primitive culture a
very sophisticated technology will appear to be magic". And you could quite
possibly replace "magic" with "miracles" with it making the same amount of
sense. How did Christ walked on water? How could Moses part the sea? How
could any one, with our rather limited understanding of how everything
works answer that? We can't, but that doesn't say it couldn't and/or didn't
happen, just that we have no way of explaining how it could of happened. Why
do all most all the non-believers think if it can't be explained that it
can't be real? I'm sure many could come up with plausible explanations, but
it doesn't really matter. It's the same as saying "dark matter" explains the
extra mass in the universe, it's pure speculation nothing more, until
proven. So until any of us become omnipotent, we'll never really know the
true answers.  Later all  :o)
