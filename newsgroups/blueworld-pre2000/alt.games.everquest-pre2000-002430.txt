You have to do away with character advancement.  Make the character
you want to play, play it.

> Perhaps, it all comes down to computer size? 

No, the problem isn't world size, the problem is you devote resources
to that minority of players that are "at the top".  Anything you add
to keep them entertained will never be *seen* by new players, because
they are too weak.  Most of them won't even play long enough to go to
the new areas.  And next month, you'll have to add new areas that are
even tougher, that even fewer people will ever see.  It's a waste of
resources (not just hardware and bandwidth, but manpower).

Eliminating "Character Building" solves that.  You add new areas, and
they are new areas that *everyone* can go to (given enough time, which
is WAY easy considering the size of worlds in UO and EQ).

Character building is just one of those "this is how you do it in
CRPGs, so this is what we have to do" sorts of things.  Not only is
that a bad reason to do it, but it  doesn't even make good business
sense for MMORPGS.  It's not as stupid as static encounters, but it's
close.

>EQ bashing? I didn't read any negative comments.

Typical EQ Zone:  weak mobs surrounded by tough mobs with an
occassional UberMob thrown in there so that no matter WHAT level you
are, you die.  Then you die again retrieving your corpse.  This is so
you can complete a "quest" that consists of killing the same NPC three
or four times in a row, sitting on your butt (or getting instakilled
by the UberNPC) waiting for it to respawn.  If it's a dungeon, add
trains.  If the "quest" is for a mediocre or better item, add
kill-stealers.

And I don't mean for that to be taken as a negative comment... it's
just an observation.
