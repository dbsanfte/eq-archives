Sang K. Choe <sang@choenet.com.remove.this.com> wrote:
>Ok, care to explain to me how decompiling an executable is going to
>tell you what language said executable was written in?  Unless the
>executable is linked with all the debugging and symbols directly in it
>(which no one in their right minds ever do for a released product), I
>have yet to see anyone figure out what language actually generated the
>machine codes.

You should still have function call names in the executable,
depending on how it's compiled. From those you can tell the language,
based upon name munging, or lack there-of. Most compilers munge
names in different ways, but they're normally similar enough that
you can guess the language, and even the compiler used, from the
brand of munging.

You can also tell based upon the call dispatching. Sure, it could
be written in C and have dispatch graphs that look a hell of a lot
like C++, but if they're that close, it's probably C++.
