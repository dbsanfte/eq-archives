Fire becomes much more interesting at higher levels.  I've found them to be very
group friendly, since they don't have to bear the brunt of the attack.  Their
Elemental Aura does absolutely hideous damage, slightly more than the
Elemental's max melee damage, and it's totally unresistable.  Against creatures
with high magic resist, I've found it to be more efficient to just Renew the pet
while the mob beats itself to death at 25-something points per blow.  They're
especially handy for dealing with psycho lawn or hall trash (Death Beetles and
Frogloks scream to mind) since half of what's pounding on them is so green it
mostly hits for 1 or 2 points, when it hits at all.  At level 24, I decimated
the entire population of BlackBurrow, with a weak Fire pet and the 20th level
Pet heal, and didn't have to med until I'd killed everything at least once.

Of course, if you use them as a primary tank when you solo, you'd better be
willing to either play Cleric or share the pain, because they really don't have
the health to be a tank.  At level 27, the heal will restore a full half of the
Elemental's life.  I'm not sure how much the heal restores at that point, but I
know it starts at 100 points, and maxes at 200 per cast.  That means that a
level 24 Fire pet has somewhere between 200 and 400 hitpoints, approximately. 
My Magician has more health than that, if only just.
