I think there is a basic error here.  The life as lead designer has infected
your artistic side, and you are starting to depend heavily on what is easily
quantifiable, because when doing business numbers speak louder than
emotions.

This is a game however.  I can tell you that I was there so long because you
had no competition that could come close to you.  When EQ came out though, I
tried it out, and going back to UO seemed like going back to a dark period
of my life.  Did this ever happen to you?  Did you ever get reminded of a
time in your life when you didn't even know you were unhappy, but in looking
back you realized how worried/bothered/miserable you had been?  That's the
feeling I got when I started up UO after a few weeks of EQ.

Maybe I'll discover differently.  Maybe after a few months I'll be begging
to come back.  When I first read about EQ, my thoughts were "Good!  The
kewlios will fly to flashy graphics like a moth to a flame, and leave us in
peace in UO."  From what I'm seeing though, It appears the opposite is
happening.  The switch makes EQ undesirable to the kewlio pk since he can't
easily screw over his fellow player.  Every PK (I'm a GM Smith remember, I
have many pk's that know me) I've talked to has tried EQ, and they ALL say
they don't like it "cuz u cant do anythg to the othur playerz".

So, you bleed off some of your nicer players, and the concentration of
kewlios increases.  Which causes more to leave, which increases the
concentration.  You may end up with more players than you had before, but I
hope you've put some thought into what kind of playerbase you will have.
