I think that's the underlying beauty of the game. +PvP players have 
the ability to interact on a social level (which was what I was 
referring to), but not on a combat level. For that they need to look 
for those of similar ilk. This really is the only solution to the 
unending +PvP/-PvP coexistence question...they don't coexist in the 
strictest sense. They can interact, but not in any combat oriented 
way. You compromise that rule and you compromise the effectiveness 
of the switch in its entirety.

The solution of providing specific +PvP servers is a functional and 
effective one. Those wanting +PvP play have free reign in their 
realm. Those who don't care for that style of play just don't log 
in. Yes, one dose not have the ability to interact "fully" at that 
point, but one also dose not have the ability to force that "full" 
interaction upon someone else should they not want to participate. 
In my mind that's a definite step...nay, leap...in the right 
direction wrt online MMRPG's.
