My problem with the mid-weekend patches wasn't that they happened.  I can
fully understand the need for patching serious bugs ASAP.  However, I
can see no reason why there couldn't be more warning given.  I'm certain
there was more than a couple minutes between when the patch was finished
and when the servers were taken offline.

That being the case, the following situation (at least on Prexus)
shouldn't have occurred.  The announcements were given with under
2 minutes time that the server was going down.  If you're deep in a
dungeon, or swimming somewhere, or on a Plane, that just does *not* give
you time to get to someplace safe to camp.  So, when I woke up after
the restart, the rest of the evening was spent in retreiving corpses,
trying to re-group, and the like.

It didn't help that the GMs announced that there would be no compensation
given to those who died, since there was a warning.  Please, next
time, give a decent warning.  A half hour at least.
