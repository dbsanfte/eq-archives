I have no problem with a pk - free server and I have said that. I DO
oppose a pk switch because it would completely change the character of
the game, and I happen to like the character of the game. THAT is the
threat...the destruction of a game that I love and its replacement by
a version of Everquest, a game which I find little more than mediocre.
I happen to agree with a lot of what Raph has said about the nature of
conflict and how it adds to the game. I find the psycho - babble talk
about different types of players a bit much, but I've seen empirical
proof of some of the other stuff in how things have developed on the
Ches shard. 

In my time playing UO I've run the gamut of experiences. I've done
pretty much everything. Currently active are a GM smith who mined most
of his own ingots, a kewlio killer (goes to places like Deceit and
interferes with kewlio duels, robs people gone grey looting, etc.), a
bard and my original character Damocles, a red who sits on his porch
and talks to his friends and does not fight if not attacked. Damo was
never a pk and currently has no short term counts at all, but the long
term count is still far too high (3 dead blue idiots, 120 hours) for a
character seldom played to lose anytime soon.
