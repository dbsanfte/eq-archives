My wife started a human warrior in Qeynos and a wood-elf bard
in Kelethin.  Both newbie areas have thier problems.

Qeynos does not have much of a market - you can not sell bat
wings and bone chips the way you can on Faydwer and there are
not as many people going there to sell low-mid end items.  Her
human arrior is relatively poor and was happy to get my
cast-off leather armor at level 9.  He had been wearing mostly
cloth before that.

Kelethin is a zoo, but it is a rich zoo.  Stay clear of the
ruins under the city and ignore the shouting idiots and it can
be rewarding.  I prefer to hunt bats and decaying skeleton
outside of Felwithe - which is a short run from Kelethin - and
in generall Faydwer has a lot of opportunites for low-level
characters and a strong market for them to buy and sell.  Did I
mention that it is full of drive-by twinkers - and of the
beggars who flock to them.
