Just to add to this:  I was in a guild group huntin trash near the zone border
of Trak's teeth.  I'm a ranger, so would rank significantly above the wizzy in
damage output.  Yet when the spectral keepers began gating, nobody really cared
if I was hitting or not (I wasn't; not well at least).  It was solely the fact

but HOW, WHEN, and HOW QUICKLY it's applied is every bit as important.

bad that you begin to appreciate their ability to heal a LOT right NOW.  The
same is true for a Wizzy and their damage.
