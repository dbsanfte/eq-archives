>Charm is extremely unreliable.  Except in certain degenerate situations
>(such as fighting spectres - slow creatures that live outdoors) it is
>probably more trouble than help.  The duration is up to about 10-15 minutes
>(12 + 3*Level ticks) but it can easily drop earlier.  
>
><MATH>
>The end time is essentially a Poisson process.  The creature gets periodic 
>saves and is your pet until it makes one of them.  This is why creatures 
>tend to be your pet for either a few seconds or the entire duration more 
>often than intermediate durations.
></MATH>
>
