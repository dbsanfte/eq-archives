Winning the lottery is a statistical improbability.  You have a greater
chance of being bitten by a crocodile than you have of winning the lottery.
And, yet, people do win.  IF you won the lottery AND were unaware that
anyone else was playing, you might believe in a god too.  Given your limited
understanding of your very fortunate circumstances, who could blame you for
looking to the supernatural for answers?

The point is that ours is not the only universe, there are an infinite
number of them, playing right along with us.  We just happened to win and
"get" the one in which life as we know it began and evolved.
