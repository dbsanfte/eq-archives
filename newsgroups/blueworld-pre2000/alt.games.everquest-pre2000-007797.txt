You have to do at least 1 point of damage--as long as you do that, it
can die on the damage shield and you still get the credit (as long as
no one else does any damage to the mob).  I've powerleveled folks and
this has been the case every single time.

Imagine it this way:  If a mob has 100hit points and the damage shield
does 99hit points of damage, you can think of that mob as being a mob
with just 1 hit point of damage.  And whoever does that 1 hit point of
damage, gets the kill.
