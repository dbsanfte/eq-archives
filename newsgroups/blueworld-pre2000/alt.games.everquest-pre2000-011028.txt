Actually I can attest to this, in the mid 20's I was with a group of melee's 
in Cazic.  It wasn't a straight 5 of them one of me, it was 2 warriors, a 
paladin, a SK and a monk.  We ranged from 24 (me) to about 29, most were 27.
All I did was twist the Hymn of Restoration (with a mandolin) and the Anthem
de Arms (combat buff). Starting with a full spawn, we were clearing the temple 
(and the grounds) from top to bottom faster than we could get the darn lizards
to spawn. When I had to leave (after only about 3 hours of this) the group was
looking for a replacement bard. 8)

I can't say how well this works now that I am in the mid 30's, the response I
get from most groups is "we can't do this without a cleric", completely
ignoring the fact that if you can drop a critter in a REALLY short period of
time that it doesn't hurt you much.  The other common response is "ooooo, 
manasong, please play that at all times." To which I respond no. 

It seems far too common for me to meet people who don't know what a bard can
do (other than heal or sing manasong), and that during a fight we are better
