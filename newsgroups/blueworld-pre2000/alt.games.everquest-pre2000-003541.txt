sang@choenet.com.remove.this.com (Sang K. Choe) writes:

> Not "any given time", please read my post a bit more
> carefully.  The 26% is for *peak time* with the following
> assumptions:

> 1.  Most people play during peak hours.
> 2.  Most people who own the game live between the PST
> and EST time zones.

You are also making another assumption: 

3. Everybody plays every day.

Assumptions (1) and (2) are unknown, but (3) is clearly
wrong.

You see, if you _don't_ assume that everybody plays
every day, the "25% during peak hours" figure isn't
odd at all. If the average player plays only 2 or 3
days a week, only 36% of the player base would log
on on any given day -- this would mean that 70% of
the people who log in on a given day log in during
PST-EST prime time -- which is in keeping with your
first two assumptions.

To sum up briefly: if the bulk of EQ players are
casual players who only play a few days a week (or
only for a short time, or both), there is nothing
even remotely odd about only 25% of the player base
being logged on during prime time. That figure would
only be odd if the bulk of EQ players were the 7-days-
a-week types that populate much of this group. :)

On a side note -- "prime time" stretches over a long
period of time, especially when you consider the
3-hour difference between the PST and EST players.
