If your group is fighting one creature at a time, then stuns are
great.  You can reduce damage taken substantially if you keep the
critter stunned all the time.  Don't try this against very magic
resistant critters.  You'll only make them mad.

If there is more than one critter, mez before you even think about
stunning.  Once you start stunning, you are basically stuck doing
that until everything is dead.  Creatures hate being stunned and
will often turn on you if you stop stunning them.

If there are tons of critters, you probably should not think about 
stunning at all, except for the purpose of buying time, since you 
will need all your mana for mezzes.

Stunning chews through mana.  If I stun during fights, I have to 
get some med time between fights.  If I don't stun, I can usually
keep up with buffs and crowd control without ever having to ask
the group to stop pulling while I med.

Enchanter nuking is nearly always useless.  It takes a ridiculous
amount of mana and makes creatures really mad at you.  I use it
when soloing or when I have a ton of mana and nothing else to do
with it.
