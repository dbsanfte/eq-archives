OK, let's use our brains.  First, let's take our brains down to CompUSA
and stroll down the input devices aisle.  What do we see?  Numerous
programmable input devices from major manufacturers.  Now let's take
our brains over to microsoft.com.  We see that Microsoft sells such
devices, designed to work spiffily with DirectX, which is what EQ
uses for input.

Now let's take a look at our other games.  Golly...most of them work
with these programmable input devices.  The game companies *expect*
many of their users to have them, and take that into account in their
game design.

Given that, if we have a normally functioning brain, it would not even
occur to us that using a programmable gamepad with EQ would be something
Verant would frown upon.

Yes, a programmable gamepad might give someone an advantage.  So what?
A better video card also gives an advantage...should people be banned
for getting their frame rate too high?  It is generally accepted in the
gaming world, among both players and manufacturers, that those who can
afford more money for hardware get an advantage.  Why pick on gamepads,
which are at the cheap end of the "hardware that gives an advantage
category"?
