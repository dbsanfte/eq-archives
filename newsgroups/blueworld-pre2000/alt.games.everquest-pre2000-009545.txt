Florian Bodenseher wrote:
> 
> I consider myself still a newbie (I have a LV9 bard and an LV9
> enchanter).
> 
> I am still quite unsure about the concept of kill-steals. As far as I
> have learned,
> a kill steal is when someone else has hurt a monster and you deliver
> the final
> lethal blow, getting all the xp.

It's not the final blow.  You have to do more damage than any other
player or group.

> The situation is as follows:
> 
> I see another player in battle. I check his health and the monster's
> health.
> Looks to me as if he is loosing the fight.
> I try to help him and I cast a damage spell that kills the monster.
> The player is really pissed off and accused me of kill-stealing, which
> was
> not my intention.
> 
> I apologize and our ways part.
> 
> So here comes my question: should I help at all? or only on request?

If someone calls for help, feel free to attack, heal, or do
anything else you can to keep them from dying.

If the fight is close and the person is not asking for help, then
you can help by buffing the person or weakening the monster.
If you were a cleric instead of an enchanter, then you could heal
the person.

If the person is clearly losing, under a bubble of health while 
the monster is still over half, then you might help even if they
don't ask.  Even this might be stealing if the guy is buffed up
with high level cleric spells and is really only down to 60 hits 
out of 600.  (I have "saved" people like that. ;)

If the monster is well below half health, you can probably help
out by doing damage.  You should still ask if they would like 
assistance.  Avoid doing large chunks of damage, since you can 
steal the kill even if the monster is below half health by doing 
more damage than the other guy has done so far.  Damage shields 
don't count for kill credit, so it is very easy to steal a kill 
accidentally from someone who is using a damage shield.
