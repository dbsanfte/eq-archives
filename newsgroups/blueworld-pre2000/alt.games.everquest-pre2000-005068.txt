LoW sell for 500pp on Eci, last I saw.  Probably cheaper by now.

And 1000 pp before level 20 is EASY.  Here's a list of ways I or my
partner did/do  it :

1)  Quests (made 6000+ pp before level 10 making steins)  Not always
possible for all race/class combos.

2)  Hunting bandits in W Karana.  They drop bronze weapons and bandit
sashes that turn into money - Making about 200 pp a level at 14.

3)  Orcs in the Oasis - Was making about 100-150 pp a level off the
cash and rawhide armor they drop.  Easy at 14.

4)  Running errands for higher level characters - Sell stuff for the
evil characters in good parts of the world and make 25-50% off the
top.  Made 5-600 pp off of our dealing with one necromancer from
levels 10-12. (Hi, Mordin)

5)  Hunt skeletons at the reoccuring GM event in Qeynos Hills.  Was
making 50 pp an hour at levels 8-10.

6)  Hunt wisps anywhere they spawn frequently - W commons, wisp
island, N karana are where I've hunted.  8 pp per Greater lightstone.

7)  Hunt Cubert for +3 int earrings in Qeynos sewers - 30 pp per
earring, doable at 6th level.

There are a ton more ways to do it - those are just the good ones from
personal experience.
