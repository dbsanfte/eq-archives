The modulating rod is a Magician summon item, level 44.  It's Lore, so if you
can't summon your own, you can only carry one.  And they cost as much mana as a
pet, and almost as much as a group teleport.  Think of them as a somewhat
inefficient mana battery.  The Magician summons some (one per group member, or
enough to equip the decaying skeletons in the zone, or whatever) at 200 mana
each, and then meds.  Later, they can supposedly be used for an emergency, or to
power a mass-res, or whatever.  You click on it and *poof* 225 of your health
becomes 175 of your mana, and the Rod vanishes.  Of course, if you have less
than 225 hitpoints...  Let's just say that *bits* of you will be coming down for
a while.

Of course, Magicians are the masters of evil equipment scams.  I happened to be
in a zone once, when some high level Magician had equipped *all* the local
Decaying Skeletons with Sword of Runes.  This is a fairly decent sword, that
happens to have a 40-something point Ward Summoned proc.  It also happened that
at the time, any procing weapon would *always* proc in the hands of an NPC,
regardless of level, or wether the spell would normally work on that target.  I
imagine the newbies were quite suprised when an inoffensive Decaying Skeleton
proc'ed them for 40 points and struck them dead with a single blow.  Especially
since in the hands of a PC, the weapon only proced at a high level, and against
Summoned targets.  Alas, I believe Verant has "fixed" the sword so that it works
the same for NPCs as for PCs now.
