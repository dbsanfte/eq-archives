   Yes, after the success of Everquest we are being SWAMPED with new
MMORPGs.  It's going to be difficult to keep track of them...and because
they take so much effort and time to play, only a few will survive while 
the others will fade away.  I expect a lot of them won't even get out of
the development stage.

   If you want to try and keep track of the MMORPGs coming out, a good site
to read regularly is GA-RPG (Gamer's Aliance RPGs) at http://www.ga-rpg.com/.
I find they have good interviews and news about upcoming games.  Although
I suspect most of them will turn out to be vaporware.
