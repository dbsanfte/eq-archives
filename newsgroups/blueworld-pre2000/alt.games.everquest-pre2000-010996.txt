That's normal and how you normally figure out you have targetted
someone else's mob.  When you enter a zone or when the monster 
spawns, you get a health reading for it.  That reading will not
be updated until you get quite close to the monster.

So, most of the time when you target a monster that someone else
is fighting, it will appear to be at full health.  You have to 
get very close to see how healthy it really is.

Also, be very suspicious of a health bar that appears low when
you see the monster from a distance.  Odds are the monster has 
healed up since you entered the zone and you will find it is at
full health when you actually close to melee. ;)
