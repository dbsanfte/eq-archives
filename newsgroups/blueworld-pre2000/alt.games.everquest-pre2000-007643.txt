	Harmony is a L5 Druid spell; anything your Ranger buddy can do for you, a
Druid can do for you -- better, because the Druid gets wolf form earlier and
thus can pull without aggroing lots of stuff.  The Druid is more powerful,
more versatile, and eats less of your EP, too.

	Incidentally, most Rangers *DO* know how Harmony works.  Rangers as a
group tend to be very well-informed about their class, because if they're not,
they'll die all the time and quit.  You have to work a lot harder when you
don't have the game handed to you on a silver plate.

	Oh, and FYI, Harmony is useless in a lot of situations.  It doesn't
totally remove the affected mobs' aggro distance, it just reduces it -- so if
the mobs are close together, you still can't pull them apart.  If you pull a
mob that has a pet, you will get the pet as well whether or not it was
Harmonied, and this usually sets off the rest of the group (as the pet runs
over them).  And if there's a mob in the AOE that you don't have LOS to, the
_entire spell_ is voided, not just the effect on that mob.  (There are posts
to EQRangers detailing the _precise spots_ on which to stand for breaking
certain spawns with Harmony to avoid this problem.)

	Rangers have Rogue armor and Rogue hit points (actually, Barbarian and
Dwarf Rogues have MORE hit points than Rangers), and less damage-dealing
ability than Warriors (their Offense caps higher).  If you want a fighter to
deal damage, you get a Rogue or a Monk; if you want one to take damage, you
get a Warrior, or failing that a Paladin or Shadow Knight.  Rangers are a
