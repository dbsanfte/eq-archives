See, that's the thing...no, they don't.

Prior to 35-40, and the 'wonder' (using the term loosely) that is Lower Guk
in all it's glory, 4-6 hour spawns aren't the rule, they're the exception.
In general, rares spawn fairly often (compared to the lategame) and always
have the item you're looking for.

In today's Lower Guk (as opposed to the version that was around six months
ago, when every third spawn would have a rare of some kind somewhere),
things like the TBB or the SMR drop at the rate of one every 24-36 *hours*.

Any given group of six individuals camping the room with a list will get, if
they are very lucky, one rare in an eight to ten hour period. Needless to
say, if someone wants to get a rare item from LG themselves, they're in for
a 36 hour marathon to have a decent chance...and figures of 72 hours for
*one* rare-carrying spawn are not uncommon (witness that 42'nd level wizard
in the original post.)

The list system as we have it today rewards 'no life' campers...but it's the
only fair way, because there is NO WAY someone who is there for 10 minutes
should be allowed to roll for a rare on an equal footing with someone who's
there that long. No way.

Now, under Play Nice, you take those six people, and you turn them into 18,
each group of six with their own list (if, that is, the original group
doesn't quit in disgust), and even the 36 hour campers now having only a 1
in 3 chance (if you think this will make people switch to random rolls,
you're mistaken; that won't happen, not in Lower Guk.)

Someone who is there for six hours might have a chance now, if everyone
ahead of him leaves early; I've been first in line inside of three hours at
times (not that it did me any good.)

Under the new policy, you'd need to be there for 18 or more, just for a
fighting shot.
