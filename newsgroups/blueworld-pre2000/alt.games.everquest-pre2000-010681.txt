Billy Shields wrote:

>
> : Good pets are always worth the effort to save.
>
> For shamans they are (because of the high casting cost) but not
> for mages or necros.  The advantage of having multiple pet
> handlers in a group is because it takes the pressure of
> keeping the pet alive off.
>

For mages, a pet which has been summoned half a dozen times
to get the best one up, then buffed (even if its just burnout and damage
shield... even more important in a mixed gourp when half
the party may have been induced to buff the pet) and armed,
is certainly worth keeping rather than let die and recast.  Chain
summoning pets is a tactic all its own, and it _can_ be the best
way to take down a mob, but you can't make a blanket
statement that at all levels its always easier to let the pet die.

Even at lowly 7th level, I found that keeping my pet alive
meant having a 6th level pet weilding a two handed
sword, while recasting in mid combat meant sending in
one that was on average 4th level, and unarmed, so
doing about a third the damage.  Lesson; /pet back off,
let the tank take some damage, or even (shudder) tank
yourself if it comes to that.  If I'd had renew, I'd have
been using that, because of the weaponry, I'd be netting
more damage that way over DD.  Unfortunately, there's
no 4th level pet heal to worry about...
