I would place Magicians within this same category, under most situations. In
fact, I would go so far as to say that the Magician can actually clean up
most mobs at a greater multiplier factor than the number of Magicians in the
group.

Almost everything a Magician casts can be stacked with other Magicians'
attacks. There is only one debuff in our arsenal (the Malise series) so you
just get one Magician set to deal with it. The key factor though is the
Magician pet.

Solo, the Magician is completely and utterly reliant on the pet. It dies,
and Mage dies, end of story. In a group of 4+ Magicians, if a pet dies its
no biggie. The mob will have to chew through 3 others. indeed, the Magician
whose pet has died can without much pressure simply recast a pet.

I have grouped in a full group of Mages inside Paw, and it was godly. We
controlled the entire zone before the iron doors when mobs were yellow/red
to us. DDs were almost secondary, the pets carried 70% of the workload.

The only drawback I can see is that against really really tough and
hard-hitting mobs (say HGs against 29th Spell level elementals) the pets are
still going to get worked. In this case a /pet taunt, using earth pets, and
"relay" of magicians works well. What do I mean? The Magicians stand in a
wide circle about the HG evenly spaced apart. Magicians on either side of
the HG blast it with heavy nukes and draw it across the circle to each
other.

This is still dangerous though. The penultimate HG killing group I would
have to concede would be a druid and/or necro (snare/darkness, SoW and Fear)
and fill rest with pure casters of any description. Key to killing HGs is to
just not be hit =)
