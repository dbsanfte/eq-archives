None of this describes an agnostic.

Agnostics do NOT scoff at religion or failt to respect people who worship or
believe in gods, they simply do not accept the existence of such WITHOUT
proof. Agnostics are best described as those who would believe if they were
provided with proof.

In the context of a world like EQ, where God's and their agendas are clearly
made manifest each and every day (through the existence of Paladins,
Clerics, and Planes upon which Gods dwell) an Agnostic would be one who does
not recognize the DIVINITY of any of the recognized gods...not hard to
fathom since it's been proven (in the context of the world) that the Gods
are mortal, and can be slain.

I actually role play in the game, and my Warrior is agnostic. His view is
pretty much as above. He recognizes that the 'gods' worshipped by all the
races are mortal with a finite existence, and limited in their impact on day
to day lives, and therefore does not believe them to be divine, simply
powerful beings.
