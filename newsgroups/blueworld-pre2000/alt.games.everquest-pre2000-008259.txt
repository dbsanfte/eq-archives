Back in the good ol' days, Verant used to make a note on the patch
server screen to keep folks informed about upcoming downtime.  I haven't
seen them do in quite a while, and certainly not more than once or
twice--tops--this year.

Now, I know that there's a website to list scheduled downtime, but
y'know, this goes back to making it easy on the customer.  If it takes
them 15 minutes to update the patch message, so be it...  It might save
10,000 customers from having to go to their site and look.

Then again, those 10,000 customers would give them 10,000 adspot hits,
so hey, maybe I see the logic in it after all. ;D

Anyway, I'd like to see Verant post reminders about the downtime on the
patch server screen, even if it just said, "There will be downtime on
Tuesday 2/13.  Please visit www.everquest.com/blahblah for times and
further information."

Call me lazy or call me a spoiled consumer, just don't call me late to
the ball. :)
