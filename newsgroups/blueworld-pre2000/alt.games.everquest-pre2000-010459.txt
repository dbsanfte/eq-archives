> Mr Foo Bar wrote:

Yes indeedy.  A well-played enchanter puts his ass on the line more
than any other player.

A great cleric is the best friend an enchanter will ever have (and vice
versa -- more on that later).

My cleric's #1 priority is keeping the enchanter alive, and I tell that
to the group right up front if the occasion arises.  He gets symbol,
and I pay for it.  When he starts to throw a mez, I watch for resists,
and if it is resisted, stunning mobs becomes my focus.  The enchanter
at 4 bubbles of mana might get a minor heal before the warrior at one-
and-a-half gets a big one.

I am overstating a little -- obviously when a monk comes in with the
pull at 2 bubbs, he is going to get a heal in a hurry.  But any cleric
knows that good enchanters die early and often, and put them on the top
of the list.

There are three players who can commonly aggro an entire pull of
multiple mobs:  Puller, enchanter, cleric.  A puller or cleric can
generally survive this for a while without help.  An enchanter cannot.

A good cleric (and a good group in general) is the key to enchanter
survival.

A good enchanter is a cleric's best friend too, even more so than a
warrior.  With the crowd of mobs mezzed, he can just heal and med.  It
is so easy.  The cleric's biggest nightmare is being hit by four mobs,
HP's almost gone, and having to hit Divine Aura while a tank is sinking.
