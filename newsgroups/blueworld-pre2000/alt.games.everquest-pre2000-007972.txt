There are two ways to implement this.  Either they could attach a list of
people who have completed the quest to every quest or they could attach a
list of completed quests to each character.

Attaching the quests to each character makes the records huge, no matter how
you do it.  This is a problem because those records get shipped between the 
servers in the cluster a lot, so making them bigger would add lag.

Attaching the names to the quests would be less of a problem for lag, but it
would create an ever growing list of names with each quest.  Also, if the 
rewards were good enough and not NO DROP, people would create a string of
new characters to perform the quest over and over, making the whole solution
meaningless and bloating the database.
