One of the nicer things put into the game was the random
number generator.  My characters name starts with an X and
before there was a fair random way it was common for me to
not get a chance at looting AT ALL.  The group would usually
do alpha loot (naturally starting at A, and I was the last of
the group) and then the dungeon had defacto standard rules of
looting that including (loot upto 2plat or items worth same).
In a poorer dungeon like Unrest (or sometimes mistmoore),this
might take a long time when you are killing carrion ghouls and
what not.  Usually I would have to leave (I can only play 90 mins
or so a night) before it got around to my turn.

I frequently would get into a group and have to leave for the
night without looting at all.  And suggestions to change the
loot order or rules were not kindly considered at all.  (By *any*
of the groups i was in).  And there were some idiots who claim
that this alpha looting was fair.  Of course if the group broke
up and reformed then the looting would start at A again.

There were some idiots who actually thought that Alpha looting
was completely fair.  It is fair in the long run of course.
But the long run never seems to happen in EQ.  Groups break up,
people have to leave.

Nowadays it is not so bad, we roll on the high ticket items
fair) and it is informal looting on the rest.  But I don't think
I had 40pp to my name (including items) all the way upto the mid
20's because I wasn't able to loot at all.
