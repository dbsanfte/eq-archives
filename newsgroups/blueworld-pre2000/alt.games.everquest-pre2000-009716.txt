You can not use the same account on two computers at once.  You
will need to purchase a separate copy of EQ and pay the extra
monthly fee to play together.  You will also have to start up
a character from scratch on the new account.  I would suggest
that the person on the old account start up a new character as
well and just play the new characters together until they catch
up with the old ones.

I play with my fiance and it's great!  All OOC chatter is done
in the living room, so we can role play in the chat window. :)
Grouping with someone who is sitting in the same room allows you
to time stuff really closely as well. ;)
