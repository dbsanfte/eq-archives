The game is worth playing if you fall into one of two categories:

1) You are a typical casual gamer who plays simply to have fun, and
doesn't really care how l33t his character gets.

2) You are into character advancement and have a very, very high
patience threshold.

If spending eight hours online and seeing no discernable change in
your character's abilities would bother you, don't play EQ.  That is
pretty much the litmus test for whether you will love or hate this
game.
