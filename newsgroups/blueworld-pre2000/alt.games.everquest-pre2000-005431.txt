Venting helps, especially when we can't help but agree with you.

If someone yells for help and I'm available then I'll go all out on the critter
attacking them.  If they're going to go down I won't back off unless they
insist... but I won't let them die unless they're utterly rude about it.  I
don't expect or go after xp when I'm doing this, but if I get it so be it...
they yelled for help and got it.

If I yell for help then I fully expect I'm going to go down hard without it, and
xp is the last thing I'm concerned about at that point.  If I do get help I'll
try to back off until the critter leaves me alone, but I will not turn off
auto-attack until they do.  If the 'helper' can't get them off me then to heck
with the xp either way... whoever gets it, gets it.

You did nothing wrong, and the guy helping you was being a jerk about it.  I'm
surprised he helped to begin with, but I suspect he was having a bad night and
took it out on you.  If he really wanted to steal the xp he would have stood
back and let you go down, then finished them off... I'm glad he wasn't that
awful, I know it's happened to me.
