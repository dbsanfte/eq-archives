You are correct.  I saw 50,000 people online again on Monday.  If 
Verant wants to see an average peak population of, say, 1500 people, 
they would need about 33 servers, which is 6 new ones (or 7 if they
aren't going to merge Tallon and Vallon).  Five would be a nice 
conservative start.

However, they are probably assuming that they are at a local maximum 
for total population.  A lot of people started playing just recently.
Many students have been on vacation, but as school starts up the number
of people online will start to drop.  Most likely, the total number of 
accounts will go down through normal attrition.  However, when the 
expansion comes out, there will probably be another surge in new 
accounts and people who stopped playing at level 50 will come back to 
get the next levels promised in the expansion.

Another consideration when starting servers is that people like to start 
out on new servers.  If Verant put up a bunch of servers right now, there 
would be no new servers for a while.

I think they should open about three servers very soon, with one of
those being half of the split Fennin Ro, and plan to open two new
servers within the next month.
