AC doesn't have level limits per say.  It does have
skill-level limits.  For example, "Sword of Flaming Kick-Ass"
might say "Arcane Lore 85", "Sword 120", or "Helm
of Falling Rock Protection" might say "Arcane Lore 55".
Anyone can pick these items up in AC and use them.  It's
just that you have to have your skills at a certain
level before the *magic* in them will work.

The answer to your question is no. 

Very powerful items are cherished and considered 
very valuable prizes (either for oneself
or as a recruiting gift for a vassal).  Even
if the person doesn't have the stats to make
the item work "magically", he can still use it
as a normal item (or at worse, hold onto it)
and build his stats up until he can use it.
