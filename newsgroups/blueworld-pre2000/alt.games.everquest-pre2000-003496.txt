This is not a very good analogy.  When you take a job, you enter
into an agreement with your employer about what kind of pay and 
what kind of retirement plan you will be getting.  If they decide
to change it, you can sue them and make them follow the original
agreement.  

When you play EverQuest, you are playing the game as Verant designs 
it.  They let you know up front that things will change.  Items and 
abilities can be changed, added or taken away as they see fit.

>    What we who *piss and moan* are complaining about is that we
> already have less time (we are casual because of the amount of time we
> put in, ie. i spend 10-20 hours a week tops, while some people spend
> that in a day), and then its 2, 3, or 5 times harder to get there?
> Even worse, the items that we were told to get to help us are no
> longer available!

On the other hand, there are new items and quests and even new game
functionality.  The cheeseball items and tactics are removed and 
are replaced with more balanced ones.  Holes are filled with new
items, such as the Solusek Ro quests.

I think you are right that the game is getting harder as time goes
on, but it's not because Verant is nerfing players.  It's because
they are fixing cheesy exploits and hacks that allowed people to
advance easily.
