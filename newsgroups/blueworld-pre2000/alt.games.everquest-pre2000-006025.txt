On Mon, 28 Feb 2000 15:58:25 -0600, "Ratbert" <ratbert@texas.net> wrote:

>Bah!  No level item limits.  What if someone wants a nice item, but is 2 or
>3 levels to LOW to kill the creature that holds this item.  If they one day
>gave it a shot, and BARELY scraped by to beat the monster and get the item,
>then it is WELL deserved and they can do what they please with it.  Sell it,
>keep it, pick their nose with it, whatever.  They earned it in my book, and
>thus should be able to wield, wear, sell it.

I only wish this were the case, and this is easily addressed by saying
that a Level 40 mob's item can be used by a level 30 player, or that
level limits only go up to 20, with all characters over 20 (the point at
which the genuinely-earned plat-based player economy really starts to
take off) being able to use all items, regardless of level. In other
words, give a reasonable range for a character based on the mob's level.

The whole point here isn't to stop a level 16 character from using a
level 20 item, but to stop a level 3 twink from wearing level 35
equivalent crafted or totemic armor. I guess you could say they can wear
it but the magical benefits don't actually work for them until some
level (much like proc effects on weapons), but that won't stop a level 3
character from looking like a level 35 character, and it's that first
impression you want to set right.

Regardless of what Brad says about hard levels and the fair and square
obtaining of items, the fact remains that the game already has many
level limits imposed; this would not be anything new. For example: most
weapons don't proc unless you're of a particular level; You can't use a
level 19 spell if you're level 12 caster; planar items are no-drop and
thus technically limited to a level of 46+, since you can't even visit
to loot unless you're that level; you can't use many skills unless
you're of a particular level (try to dual wield as a 4th level warrior);
and so on.

Level limits already exist. This would just be a logical extension that
has the potential to solve a sticky issue with many players.
