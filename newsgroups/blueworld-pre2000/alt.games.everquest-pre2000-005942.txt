Heh, you can also roll a same-race group of 3, and make it barbarians. 
That would be quite a functional party -- warrior, rogue and shaman. 
Barbarians make great fighters, and would be able to complement each 
other quite well. 
With shaman and warrior slamming constantly, the rogue would be able to 
hit off most of his/her backstabs. The two things this group would lack 
is snare and crowd control. 
All three wouldn't have problems with SoW, shrink, and infravision.
Besides that, if you make one of the characters (I'd suggest rogue or 
shaman) female, you would get awesome possibilities for roleplaying as 
well. When the rogue gets her inkie mask at later levels, it would be 
even more fun -- the warrior and shaman have failed to convert the 
smelly rogue to ways of Tribunal, and look what happened to her now -- 
she turned slutty, and started modelling the Victoria's Secret armor
