Relatively recently (several months at most), there was an alteration to the
reputation system that made it vitrually impossible to both PK and stay blue
via macroing or any other means. Now, if you're in any danger of being PKed
by a stranger, you'll know because they WILL be red. Now the extremely
skittish needn't run from everybody (or even avoid), but simply from
everybody who's red. They'll still at times be overreacting, but at least
now one has a reasonable guide to recognize who's potentially dangerous.

Between the rep patch patch, and the thief patch, it's a lot more pleasant
of a world. There are still PKs, but at least they're forced to go red
(which has cut down the number of people willing to PK). And there are still
thieves, but there are no longer thief/pks, and they're much less the tool
of "game bastards."
