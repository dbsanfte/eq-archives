I think it was the nature of the quest that was disturbing, rather
than the fact that people were getting experience from doing a quest.

The quest is:  Buy Kiola Nuts.  Buy water.  Give them to Tumpy.
Get tonics.  Give the tonics to Trantor Everhot for Dwarven Wire.
I'm not sure whether it was Tumpy or Trantor who was handing out
so much experience.  I sure didn't notice any experience when I
did the Trueshot Longbow quest as a level 5 Ranger back in May.

I think you can see that this quest should not give a significant
experience reward.  It just doesn't merit a big reward.

I would hope that a Paladin gets a huge chunk of experience on
completing a part of their quest armor.  That represents a lot of
effort.  But handing Kiola Nuts to Tumpy?
