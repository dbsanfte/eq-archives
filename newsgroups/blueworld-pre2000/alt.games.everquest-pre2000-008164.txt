Actually, I believe the distinction the poster is grasping for is
between civil and criminal penalties.  IOW, if this backdoor caused
some FP user somewhere some actual damage -- say, some hacker
somewhere exploited it to do damage to the user's company webpage --
then the aggrieved company could conceivably sue Microsoft for a
product defect, much as injured Pinto owners sued Ford.

OTOH, there does not seem to be anything *criminal* about the back
door -- that is, there is no criminal statute that I am aware of that
the backdoor would violate.  Thus, MS probably could not be
*prosecuted* for anything.
