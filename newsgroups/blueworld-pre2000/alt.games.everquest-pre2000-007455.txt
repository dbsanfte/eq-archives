To do these comparisons, you can divide damage by delay for a
ballpark figure.

Before level 20 or so this should rank weapons in the same
relative order that you get from the more complicated
calculations.

So, by eye, the mino axe does 1/3 more damage, and has less
than 1/3 more delay (it is 8/37 by the way).
