Everquest is poorly conceived.  The world has almost no
backstory, so there is no way to get immersed in the story.

Everything you kill pops back into existence fairly soon
afterward.  So the world is static and unchanging.

The quests are often pointless.  The NPC's don't even
remember you completed the quest and
send you out to do the exact same thing seconds later.

It is difficult and tedious for the lower levels.  The fun
factor is absent excluding the presence of other players.
Leveling is only an excuse for more pointless frustration
and tedium.

If you play the game, and have an IQ in the triple digits,
you'll conclude that playing Everquest is pointless.
There is the eternal promise of fun "later on".  Which never
arrives.  Avoid this game until they refine it, if ever.

And you can't build homes or color your clothes.

It has many great points.  First person 3D.  An excellent
in-game chat concept, good grouping, and some
fun items and skills.  But you feel like you're an unwelcome
visitor in someone else's house, not allowed to touch
their stuff, and mostly ignored when your host isn't
present.

To call Everquest a "Questing" game is an outright lie.
To call Everquest a "Role-Playing" game is an insult to
every RPG out there.

            *** Don't be seduced by the box or web sites.
***

It is a game to be avoided.  Tell your friends, family, and
anyone who cares.
