That's not quite right actually.  Nice items don't always have allegiance
ranks on them.  In fact it's fairly rare..   I have only found one item that
I wanted to use that had an Allegiance rank.  However, I do occasionally
find items that I'd like to use that are race specific, so that can be
rather frustrating at times.

Also, everyone in a guild can get to the same character level.  The
Allegiance system in AC is just an incentive to help other players out.
However, the xp you get from having vassals, although a nice little extra,
really isn't worth the hassle of having vassals.  *at least if that's all
your looking for*.   The way it works anyway.. the monarch can have a lower
rank level than one of his vassals.  Since to achieve a rank 1, you have to
have 2 vassals, and to achieve a rank 2, each of those vassals have to have
2 vassals.  That's not to say your limited to 2 vassals.  However, it's
entirely possible that a monarch will have an Allegiance rank of only 3 and
maybe one of his vassals in the guild constructed his vassal tree well and
has a rank of 7.  When I talk about Rank Level that's the level in an
Allegiance, not the Level of the character.

and you never have to join a guild or allegiance.  You can make your way
through the world just find without any help or assistance.  Having friends
in an Allegiance just adds that much more to it..
