There are a few things I really like better in EQ and a few 
I liked better in UO.  I stopped playing UO entirely after I
picked up EQ.

EQ advantages:

  No PKs.  No thieves.  Other players can't directly mess with
  you in any way.  Names are unique and you can't have an
  offensive one.

  Character advancement is very satisfying.  It takes a long
  time to get your character up levels and there is no macroing
  so you don't find yourself basically maxed out within three
  days wondering what else there is to do.  Also, as you gain
  levels, your power and abilities increase by lots.

  Very nice mechanisms for cooperation.  You can put together
  groups to take on challenges that would be beyond any single
  character.  Everyone in the group shares the experience 
  awards, so support roles are rewarded.

UO advantages:

  It was much easier to make your mark on the world in UO.  You
  could have a house, make goods with your name on them, run a
  shop, and put together interesting outfits.  There was a lot
  of room for individuality in UO.

  The interface is a lot better and more customizable than EQ's.  
  You can bind basically any key to any action.  EQ has a lot
  of bizarre modes of operation and very limited key binding
  capability.
