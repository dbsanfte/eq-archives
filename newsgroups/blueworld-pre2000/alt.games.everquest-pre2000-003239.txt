A couple of my favorites are David Gemmell (Waylander, Legend, Knights of
Dark Reknown), and Michael Moorcock (Elric, Swords Triology).

Neither one are the traditional elves, dwarves, etc Tolkien like fantasy,
but both have a lot of interesting characters and ideas for medieval sword
and sorcery genres.

They also both are a bit on the dark side, Moorcock moreso the Gemmell, but
I guess thats why I like them because you never know for sure whats going to
happen and who is going to live and die.  Good and evil are not clear cut
black and white and the good guys don't always win.
