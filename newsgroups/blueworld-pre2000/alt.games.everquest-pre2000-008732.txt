 Overclocking doesn't shorten the life of the processor unless you run
it outside its recommended voltage settings. My p3-600 E runs at 800
with no extra cooling or voltage tweaking. The extra heat generating
by upping the voltage (which you have to do to get some chips to OC,
like my old Celeron 300 @ 450) is what shortens the processor's
lifespan.

 So yeah, he should definitely OC whatever he gets. Anyone that
doesn't nowadays is just throwing money away, when the P3-E models can
run at 133 Mhz FSB with no extra voltage.
