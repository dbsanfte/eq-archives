Well, I am interested, little things like magic numbers keep popping out
of this game, as often unintended by  the designers or in direct contradiction
to what the rule book or statments issued by the design team claim
as not.  For all I know, I'll run faster carrying 20/65 than I do carrying
50/65!
It is quite possible that the amount of armor a mage can wear falls so far
behind that of even blue mobs at middle or higher levels that wearing
it is not merely a case of "why, I'm not planning on getting hit" but "heheh,
did you think that would make a difference if you did?"

>
> The underlying reasoning for the original question seems to be this:  do I

Although that's certainly my principle angle, it isn't my only one; I prefer
casters, but experiement across the board; right now I'm playing a
SK on and off, he's at 7th level so far...  at any rate, here's an off the
wall thought; we spend most of the game (thru the mid 20s, at least,
perhaps things change as we go up) fighting stuff that is light red
or yellow.  Simple tests against stuff that is blue to a low single digits
character show a marked effect from decent (for the level) armor,
but we seem to still get hit fairly consistently and hard by stuff that is
yellow.  If a character isn't twinked, does his armor really count for
anything at all against something red to him?

>

I think it is interesting to see the focus change over time, and it makes
a contrast to the claims that leveling in the game consists of getting
more powerful versions of the same old spells and facing more powerful
versions of the same old monsters.

If this is so, then why wouldn't sauce for the goose be sauce for the
gander?
