My own .02...

 - Certain weapons are deemed unholy for devotees of a particular
religion, and to touch them is taboo, to try and weild them, anethema.  An
example of this is already in the game--Druids may use no bladed weapon
(save for the Scimitar, for some reason), since it's offensive to their
class.  This would not apply to Agnostics who, for the sake of gameplay,
can be those who have chosen not to devote to any one god as well as those
who don't hold belief in the power of gods.

 - Other weapons are blessed.  Say Tunare created  a holy sword--do you
really think she'd want followers of Bertox handling it?  Nope, she'd want
her own followers using it.  Just like only the future King of England
could pul the sword from the stone, only a follower of Tunare could bear
it.  Agnostics couldn't handle them, as they don't have the faith needed.

 - Still other weapons are cursed.  Bertoxx creates, say, an uholy staff.
For a follower of any other god would burn their flesh, and thus, it means
it's unweildable by them.  Again, this would rule Agnostics out, too.

I can't say that my Agnostic warrior chick would be happy about not being
able to use the Sacred Pig Poker of Marr, but at least there'd be an IC
explanation for it.
