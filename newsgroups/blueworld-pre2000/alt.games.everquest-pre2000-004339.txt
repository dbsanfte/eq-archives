Try "/ 1 50" to get a count of the non-anonymous people in the zone.
In general on E'Ci, roughly 25% of the population goes anonymous.
I think that the high hassle factor classes are slightly more prone 
to going anonymous than the rest, but I see anonymous people of all 
classes and levels.  Almost all rogues are anonymous, both because
of the stigma of pickpocketing and because the people who choose to
play rogues are more likely to want to hide their identities. ;)

Also, if you actually want significant numbers, try:

/who all count			- total server population
/who all count 1 50		- count of all non-anonymous people
/who all count Paladin		- count of all Paladins
/who all count Paladin 20 50	- count of all Paladins between 20 and 50

(repeat for each class)

I would really like it if count included anonymous people.
