I can tell you one thing thats entirely testable: damage shield
damage does not count towards who gets the kill.  I was waiting
in Lavastorm at the entrance to Najena for someone to turn up
to give me something and this level 10 troll was fighting things.
I had nothing else better to do so I cast a damage shield on
him.  At that level a 16 point damage shield tears things to
pieces.

That prompted the troll to take on bigger things (con reds) and
he actually got into trouble once so I hit it a few times (with
my scimitar) to finish it off.  He didn't get credit for the
kill.

This got me thinking and I tested it out with two melee types
of the same level.  I'd cast damage shield on one.  That one
would use a weapon he had no skill in.  They'd both attack
the same creature.  The damage shielded one would also taunt.
The other got the kill every time (due to the fact that he
could hit because he was using a weapon he had skill in).

As for the "limited size shitlist"--that is an interesting
theory.  I'd be interested to know how the testing results go.

I don't buy anything to do with killing blows being special.
As far as I've seen every one of these theories has been debunked
when scrutinized with any sort of credible testing.

One thing I'm not sure of though is if a creature has 50 hit points
