As a long time player of UO I feel the need to step in here.  First off the
comments made against Mr. Koster
were very rude to say the least.  He is a human being.  He did a job, whether it
was good or bad is no
reason to attack him so savagely.  I am sure he is trying his best.

   That being said, I fail to see how anyone can call UO a "great" product.  I
was not an official beta tester, but I sure felt like one.  I have played UO
since the original charter edition.  For the first long while there were a lot
of things that made me feel like I was paying to Beta test.  Now this too I do
not believe to be Mr. Koster's fault.  It seems like many things the suit and
ties demand a product be released when the programmers no damn well it isn't
ready, but they have little choice.  I don't know for certain this is what
happened, but form other stories I know to be true that were very similar I am
guessing this is close to the truth. If you look at it that way you can see
these guys as designers and GM's were making the best of a bad situation.

   I feel the one great thing EQ has over UO, and where UO severely dropped the
ball, is in class separation.  Everyone in UO can do it all.  My character was a
GM fighter with high stats in magery related skills and hiding.  Not to mention
all the secondary skills I was pretty damn good at.  Nobody really needed
anybody except to have greater numbers.  I could do it all.  Need a spell? I had
it.  Need a heal? Got that too.  What that? Need a lock picked? Hold a sec, let
me get my picks.  By definition you didn't ever need anyone to accomplish any
tasks.

   The main reason I quit though, and I will try to make this brief as I know
the subject has been beaten to death, is pkers.  I had to play a certain way
just because of pkers.  What weapons I used were affected by pkers.  I would
have loved to go with a katana shield combo but if pkers would show up I needed
spells to fight in a hurry and trying to click on the slim katana and a shield
wasted precious seconds needed to combat the pkers.  I HATED having the way I
played dictated to me by those scum, and no matter how many you killed there
were always more.  On top of it all they always tried a cheap trick like waiting
till someone was low on health fighting a creature to attack.  Or, my favorite,
was how someone could get three levels down un my pack to steal my pearl as we
were fighting.  You would think one could tie his pack in such a way that
someone couldn't get that far down but I guess not.  The point here is UO never
did anything about any of this.  Pks rule their world, and seemed like more and
more were using hacks to do it.

   Add this to never delivered promises about quests.  I know they eventually
came up with some but these were very lame.  I know UO is working on fixing
things but it seems to be too little too late.  It was especially hard for me
because I was such a die hard fan of the Ultima series, and I kept talking up
UO  to my friends long after it's deficiencies became apparent.  I can but hope
they will fix some of these issues and I may someday return.  As it stands I
have lost total interest, even in the single payer game.

     All this being said, Everquest will have it's flaws.  We will have bumps
along the road.  But I will give EQ the same unquestionable faith I gave UO at
the start.  I will let a year go by as the improve.  My hope is that they will
not let me down as UO did.

   I really do wish to reiterate that attacks on Mr. Koster just aren't fair.
Many of us have been disappointed by the game.   Let us not forget he is not so
different from us.  He goes to work and he tries his best.  I don't work at
Origin systems and I don't think many of you do either.  Let's not be to harsh
then as we don't really know what went on behind the scenes.

-Me
