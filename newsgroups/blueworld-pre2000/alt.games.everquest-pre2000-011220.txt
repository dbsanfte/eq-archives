Well, actually I think I proved to myself, at a very early stage in my EQ
'career', that harmony is a great spell when it works as one would like it
to (and, as I mentioned, much better than spells used to do similar things)
but .. I have never found it to be trustworthy based on what you are saying.
Just because the 'sparklies' appear around a particular target has never
been a reliable indicator for me that it has stuck on that target.   Which
is why I play with caution.

One other suggestion (which I learned the hard way on shadowmen, and have
read about a fair bit as well) is to never pull anything but outside MOBs
even if you think they are all harmonied, especially when they are tightly
grouped .. and always pull from an extreme side angle to there is no chance
the pulled mob will at all 'walk through' another's aggro range.
Perspectives can be distorted.
