In almost every state, each party designates a list of electors that will
vote should their party win.

Florida has 25 electoral votes.  What we're waiting on is final word on
WHICH 25 electors will be voting.  The 25 that the Republicans picked or the
25 that the Democrats picked.

In at least one state (CT I believe) there are two "at large" electors who
are not chosen by any party so aren't under much political pressure to vote
with the popular vote.

In MOST states the electors aren't bound by law to vote with the popular
vote, but there's never been an election where a rogue elector has made any
difference.  The last case of an elector who voted different than the rest
of their party was when Dukakis (sp) ran against Bush (daddy).  One elector
voted for BENSTON (Dukakis' running mate) as pres, and Dukakis as VP.  It
was just a minor political statement that probably an extremely few people
ever heard about it (bet you didn't!).

There's no way in hell that the electors in Florida will vote against their
party.  I'm in Florida and BELIEVE me, we want this done as badly as the
rest of you.  Fires from the political camps are burning brightly and the
state is caught in the middle.  The electors will vote for the right
person...they want this damned thing over just as much as you do.
