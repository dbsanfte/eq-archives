A game provides you with NOTHING apart from leisure. You may enjoy the
nuances of a highly thought provoking game, enjoy the time you spend with
friends sitting around trying to puzzle out some weird text adventure, but
ultimately the presence or absence of a single game in your life deletes
nothing from your life experience.

Free press allows for the spreading of alternate views which may or may not
be palatable to those who make decisions that affect your daily life.
Free speech provides the same.
The ability to move beyond your home town is something required if you wish
to pursue certain lines of work in your field, and therefore its absence can
have a direct and deleterious effect on your life.

The presence or absence of EverQuest in your life would mean sweet shit all
unless you had no other life. Thus, a decision to play or not to play can
easily be made.
