    Just allow me to get everything out of the way before this thread goes
any further.  In science "theory" refers to an explanation or description of
a physical process. When talking about scientific theories it's best to
forget about the word meaning anything about how much data backs it up,
because it isn't really used that way.
    Evolution is change in a gene pool, and it has been observed. Natural
selection is the non-random selection of various randomly varying
replicators, and it has been observed. Theory of Descent is the theory that
all living things evolved from an ancestor or set of ancestors, it can be
observed in the fossil record. Nobody knows if a change from one "kind" to
another has ever been observed because creationists refuse to define it.
