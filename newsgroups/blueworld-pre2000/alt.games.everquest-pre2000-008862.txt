And this is why EQ attracts more players than AC. Because EQ drives you,
gives you well-defined goals, pushes you heaps of disadvantages, and
challenges you a lot more than AC, because they make things DIFFICULT.

Yes, frustration is an intrinsic parameter in EQ, because the rush when
you overcome is incredible. You hate it, but feel rewarded by it. That's
the secret of EQ success.

all soft edges, smooth progression, hundreds of little choices.

a game, even if it lacks character (but it's all a tradeoff between time
required to design an area and area distinctiveness). EQ is more addictive,
and has been aptly named EverCrack. You hate all its myriad faults, the
push... ahem company that sells it. But you keep coming.
