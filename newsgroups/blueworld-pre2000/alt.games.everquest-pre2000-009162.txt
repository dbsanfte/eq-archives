Doing damage is a very secondary concern. In a 1 group situation, the
priority for a tank is taunting; in a 24+ person raid, the priority is
staying alive, minimizing cleric mana lost, and, incidentally, trying to
poke the dragon with a stick. Being the hero on a raid doesn't work; the
mobs hit for so much so fast that doing too much damage is simply a way to
make your cleric spend 10 minutes at the end of the fight ressing you
anyway.

I'm not saying there's no difference between a ykesha and an Oggok Cleaver-
but in a raid, the Cleaver will, at most, end a mob's life 15 seconds
faster.

Having 850 AC instead of 1000 will cost you a lot more than 15 seconds.
