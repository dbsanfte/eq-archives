I've only seen one set of data that showed how experience is split
and that data seemed to be interpreted incorrectly.  The person
doing the test was making an assumption that if a group member gets
no experience, they take no experience.  I don't think this is true.

Total experience is something like (X * (1 + Nx5%)) where X is the 
base experience award and N is the number of other people in your
group.  Each person gets a share of that experience based on how
much total experience they have.  If a person does not get experience
either because they are too far below the highest level group member
or because they were too far above the level of the monster, they
still take their share from the award but the experience is lost.

It would be nice if that same guy would try the experiment grouped
with level 1 characters who have earned no experience at all.
