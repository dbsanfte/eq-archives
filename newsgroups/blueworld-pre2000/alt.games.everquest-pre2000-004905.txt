Indeed.  We have 12 races, 14 classes, well over 1000 spells and magical
effects, and tens of thousands of items.  The public will *always* find
things that developers of these games overlook or fail to consider.  It's
the greatest strength and weakness of Massively Multiplayer Online Games.
Even with over 185,000 active accounts with over 120,000 playing the game
each and every day, there are *still* things that haven't been encountered
by this group.  Nearly 50 full man-years are spent in this game *every day*,
and the players are *still* coming up with new ways to do things.  We are
*still* adding content, and every piece of content we add has the potential
to interact in unforeseen ways with the millions of aspects of the game.

It's fairly daunting, and my hat goes off to everyone here at Verant and
everyone at any other company that produces MMOG's.  The scale is immense.
