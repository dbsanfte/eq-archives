Yes.  It's called "latency", and some people confuse it with bandwidth
issues.  Consider a server in orbit around Mars; you might be able
to spray many megabits per second at it, given enough energy, but
it will take many seconds (depending on orbital positions) before
the server can see the first bits arrive, and more before the reply
can be seen.

The inability to get around real network latency was said to be the
reason that Star Wars Episode One Racer is not playable over the
Internet; a delay of a fraction of a second can cause a major
difference in position at simulated 1000KPH speeds, and this would make
play pretty much impossible to synchronize without ruining game play.

Games like "Myth" are somewhat less susceptible, since player
activities are time-tagged, but substantial delay in getting data from
here to there leads to laggy, jerky multiplay.  Playable, but one
prefers getting into games with better locality.

Of course, that's assuming that Bungie can figure out why some
of us can't connect to bungie.net at all...
