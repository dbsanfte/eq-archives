Power leveling is having a high level character help a low level 
character gain levels quickly, either through healing and buffing
or helping them damage the monster.

Normally, power leveling is done by people who want to bring
low level friends up to high level quickly so that hey can hunt
together, or by people who have two accounts and two computers
and want to bring their own new characters to high level quickly.

Twinking is giving gifts of extraordinary value to low level 
characters.  Often these "gifts" are actually transfers from a
high level character to a low level character on the same account.

Both twinking and power leveling are scorned by many players as
compromising the game and removing the fun and challenge.
However, both are endorsed by other players who feel that they 
earned the experience or gear once and should not have to earn it
slowly a second time.

The best thing to do with your monks is to group with other people
and slay things near your level as fast as you can.  Try to get
at least one healer and at least one other fighter in your group.
The level 4 probably has a couple more levels of solo play in him 
before grouping is easier and faster.  Try hunting in North Ro near 
Freeport.
