I am not a big trader, so I generally sell in one of two modes: fixed 
price or auction.

When I sell jewelry, I know what the price is.  I ask the price I intend
to get and I don't bargain much.  I found that asking a higher price led
to silence, not bargaining.  Small items with very set prices operate in
the same fashion.  Know the price and ask it.  Most people hate bargaining.

When I sell a desirable item that I didn't make and do not know the price
for, I will auction it.  I set a duration, like 5 minutes and hold to it.  
I auction whenever someone sends a higher price and repeat my auction with 
time remaining about once a minute.  Do not try to do this during a bazaar.
Greater Faydark is a great place to auction things (at least on E'Ci).

I generally assume that the buy price is about 50% higher than the sell 
price for all but the most common items.  For example, if you really want
a flowing black robe, you will probably not get one unless you offer 
around 300pp.  If you are trying to sell one, you probably won't get any
offers if you ask over 200pp.  This is because the pools of buyers and 
sellers are both quite small, so it is unlikely you will find a real 
buyer and seller in the same zone.  The gap between these prices is where
the wheelers and dealers come in. ;)

Similarly, if I take an item in trade for jewelry, that item had better 
be worth at least 50% more than the trade value because I am going to have 
to haul it around until I find a buyer for it.

If you type like that in the game, it could just be you. ;)

Try using capital letters.  When I get tells that look like your typing
here, I am generally not inclined to talk with the person further.  It's
too much work to read.

Other options are that you set your initial price too high or that you 
word your statements as if the price is fixed.  If someone sends a tell
saying "How much for the XXXX?" and you respond "350pp", they will 
probably assume that the price is fixed.  If you are leaving room to 
bargain, make it clear in your response.  Say, "I am asking 350pp" or
"I would like to get 350pp".  That makes it clear that you are open to
negotiations.
