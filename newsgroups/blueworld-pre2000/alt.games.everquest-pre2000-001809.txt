One common mistake in such argument is that assuming if the rule is
changed, the other guy will still do the same thing and then you will
have a way to deal with it.

The other guy is not stupid and he will change his behavior too. If
there is no switch, he will PK you and then run away.

At least with a switch, 1) you don't get killed and looted and 2) you
can call your friends to help drive him away. He won't be staying
there waiting for you friends if he PK you.

And you do have recourse, namely get some friends and kill-steal him
too.
