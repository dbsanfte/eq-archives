Technically, there are not any "camping rights" or "access rights" for mobs.
Any mob is free to whomever can kill it.  No matter who happens to be
"camping" it at the time.  This is direct from Verant, and just about every
GM you try to petition about "killstealing"  a camped mob will tell you that
it is not against policy to do so.  Rules for this have been created by the
community and are enforced by popular opinion, not by Verant.  The only
thing stopping you from taking any mob you want is the fact that if you do
so you will be ostracized by the major part of the server community (not
that this stops some people out there).  In fact, this is one of those
little "rules" that Verant cannot change.
