In my humble opinion KSing is impossible to prevent if you want to 
maintain any semblance of reality because someone will find a way to do 
it. 

It is my opinion that there are certain kinds of behavior that cannot be 
changed. Not by common sense, because if you have enough people, 
someone will see it differently and not by rules, because then there will be 
NO freedom and thus no fun (go read a book if that's your game). So to 
you all who are bitching I would suggest to improve on your roleplaying:

 (a) if a big bully is KSing you, call your big guild brothers, train a 
     SG on HIM (what ... I shouldn't do that? ;) etc.

 (b) go away and come back later, as you would IRL

 (c) Go and kill something else ... who sais you need to get the PBB 
     *now*, you can just wait another level when you are the strong man 
     on the block.

The idea to remove loot from things you get no exp for has its merits, as 
most KS disagreements ensue over those items ... on the other hand I have 
met enough plain idiots in my 20 circles to believe that then some high 
levels would sit there and just kill the monsters to keep the loot rare 
... that wouldbe a neat exploit wouldn't it: "give me 10 pp or I'll kill 
that MOB". Also a great many essential items (like Bat wings) are needed 
by people who are way beyond the level where they get exp from the 
critters so that would mean changing tons of loot lists ... not good 
either. 

So my suggestion to Verant: If there is anything that needs to be changed 
it is design attitude, and I'll keep saying that:

 * give us more SPACE. It is nice that we can have 2000+ people playing 
   on a server now (at least for your income) ... but the zones are 
   getting crowded ... like 3 good spawn points in a zone and 40 people 
   sitting around them waiting for kills ... that's too crowded unless you
   are a good sharing communist.

 * give us more spawn points. There should be more stuff than people can 
   reasonably kill ... have you ever thought about what the plains of 
   Karanas should REALLY look like with a population of lions, griff*s
   and beetles whose average lifetime must be less than a minute? I would
   say a barren desert. And that's MOBs that aren't even *special*.

 * Even if you want people to group ... the way it is done is SILLY, 
   pardon my french. I have a semblance of a life. I have about 2 hours 
   per day that I can spend on line. I can not usually pick the same 
   times as all of my friends, so I can't always group with them. But in 
   most of the places where you need to group it will take you about an
   hour to find a decent group. BAH, what sort of freak do you think I 
   am? Now I have only about 50 minutes of playtime left because I have 
   to get so safety an med up at the end. In that same amount of time I 
   could have played Tombraider (or whatever) for 110 minutes AND chatted 
   with my friends on the phone for 10. I like grouping ... but not like 
   THIS .

Just my $.02,

  Rudolf
