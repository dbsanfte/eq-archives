> This is the crux of the argument. In mud design circles it is
> phrased as "Marion's Tailor Dilemma" after Marion Griffith,
> who runes the !Overlord Project to foment roleplaying in muds.
> The formal statement of it is:
>
> ---
> Suppose Marian is (role)playing a tailor and in the game that is
> a feasible profession. She learns the requisite skills and enjoys
> her work, designing clothing for other players, and the opportunity
> it provides to talk with many other players.
>
> Along comes Boffo who doesn't like Marian, Tailors in general or
> is just in a bad mood. He attacks, and kills, Marian, loots her shop
> and leaves her to pick up the pieces.
>
> The question now is: who should protect Marian from this? Marian
> herself, being a tailor, has neither the skill nor the interest
> in learning to fight and should arguably not be bothered with it.


This happened to me, once, actually.  Except Mairi wasn't killed, and Best
Dressed Britannian wasn't looted.  But the experience is always an example
of why I am glad for not having the pvp switch in UO.

What she did, was run (after saying "I ban thee!" to all of them).  She ran
up to the Ranger Hall, while Talyn stuck around to fight the pks (she
died)... told SBR what was happening at BDB, SBR got a squad together and
dispatched the aggressors.

So - both the game and the players protected the tailor.  And, that's how
it should be, i think.  The game should give players some tools ("I ban
thee!" being a good one for shopkeepers)... and the players should step in
and do the rest.

But I LOVE that they were allowed to be my heroes.  I LOVE that I made it
to the SBR station without dying.  I LOVE that I won, and I didn't have to
fight or kill anybody to do it!
