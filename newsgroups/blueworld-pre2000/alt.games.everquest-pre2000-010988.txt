Mages in general...

If you are being aggroed fairly regularly and your pet can't taunt it off
you, you're doing something wrong.  Mages have this in common with wizards.
Kinda sounds like you're over-nuking.  And being out of command range is
easily fixed.   STAY out of command range until you actually have to talk to
your pet.  It's not all THAT common that you do, but you should be able to
either be ignored those few times, but if not, you'll still be taking a lot
less damage than what you seem to prefer to do (stay within command range).

Earth (root) or air elementals (stun) should be able to give you enough time
to step back and remind the critter that the elemental is the biggest
threat, not you.   If the critter STILL comes after you - again - you've
screwed up and over nuked.  This can usually be fixed by doing the root/stun
(by elemental) and you getting out of the way again.  By THEN hopefully
you're back down in the hate list where you belong.

Playing primarily a healer it bothers me whenever I have to heal a magician
or wizard any more than once out of 5 fights.  It tells me the nuker doesn't
know his job.  Same thing goes for my enchanter whenever he has to stun
and/or mez critters off the nukers.
