Do you know anything about how pickpocket works?  It's easy to tell if the
rogue in your group is picking the mob clean, ends up with one coin of each
kind.  If this happens label them as a bad group member and boot them.  If
they aren't picking the mobs clean they certainly aren't taking much money
from the group.  Magic items can't be pickpocketed.  *Very rarely* is a
non-magic item picked unless you count things like gnoll pup scalps.  
If you are high level you won't care because level 45+ mobs aren't pickable 
anyway.  Most speaking mobs when finally detected will yell "Stop Thief! 
<Player-Name>"

The most I've ever picked off a mob is 4 gold.  Usually it's silver or less. 
*One* time I picked a gem.

It's pretty bad form to not allow someone in a group because of what they
*might* do, especially when the results will be so small or will be obvious.
