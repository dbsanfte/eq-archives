A note on the two games in comparison... it's just too difficult to match
them both.  They both got strong points, and they both got weaknesses...
depends on who likes which the most.  Me?  I went back to UO for now... not
here to flame, I just like to keep up with EQ.

soloed.  My necro made it to 20th pretty smoothly I thought, but my fiance
had a helluva time with making 19th with his rogue.  Seeing him get his ass

strong point there, allowing someone to mind his own business if that's what
he wants to do *shrug*.
They'll both have players... and they'll both have flamers... fact of life,
choose which you wanna play and go have fun.
