A good rule of thumb is, if a random name generator might have
turned up that combination of letters, then don't let it bother
you.  Maudib could easily have been random.  Mua'Dib or however
Frank Herbert spelled it would not be random.

Likewise I see a lot of Leggolass's and Gandalffs out there.  I
figure, why petition it when, for the rest of the time that
they play that character, they will be punishing themselves by
running around with a sign over their head that reads: "Yes, I
have no imagination."

And so, for folks like the elf named Drixoral, I just figure
that the pharmaceutical company got the name for the drug our
of a random name generator as well, so let it be.
