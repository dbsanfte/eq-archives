Davian wrote:
> 
> Feel free to consider this all my opinion, but to me, the game seems based
> around the idea of inducing as much downtime as absolutely possible.
>
> If I added up all the time I spent meditating or healing on my characters,
> and then compared it to the amount of time I spent in combat.... I bet it
> would be over twice the amount.

Hm, I don't really think this is the case at all.  It seems to me that
a lot of the game balance centers on resource management.  You have so
many hits and so much mana and you can choose to expend it in different
ways to try to advance your goals.  If your mana and health came back 
instantly, then there would be no reason to conserve them.  A long time
spent meditating is the price you pay for blowing all your mana.

Also, unless you are soloing, I bet that you actually spend a lot more 
time doing stuff than you spend sitting around.  The sitting around just 
feels longer.
