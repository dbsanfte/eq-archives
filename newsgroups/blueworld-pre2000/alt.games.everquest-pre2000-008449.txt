Fix what?

Bards cannot cast Clarity.  It is an Enchanter only spell.  Bard clarity 
(Cassindra's Chorus of Clarity) works the same way that bard healing (Hymn 
of Restoration) does.  They are not persistent effects.  Each one adds a 
burst of mana or healing on each tick.  They are not enchantments like 
Clarity (or Regeneration), so they don't overwrite the enchantments.  This 
is also why neither can be productively twisted with other songs and why
multiple bards can speed up the effect.

I should not have to be explaining this to a Bard.
