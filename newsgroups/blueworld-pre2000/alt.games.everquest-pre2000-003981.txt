I am guessing that it is a problem with recording information.  When you
/consent someone to drag your corpse, their name gets added to a list
somewhere (PeopleWhoCanLootYourBody) attached to you or perhaps to your
corpse or to the person you consented.  If they add a completely separate
command, /drag, then they will need to make another list and track both
of them.

There are lots of places in the game where things work in a strange way 
because making them work "the right way" would involve tracking a bunch 
of information.  For example, you can repeat quests, because tracking
who had done what quest would be too much bookkeeping.  You have to give
all items to a quest giver at once, because the quest giver can't keep
track of who has given them what.  Memory blur spells wipe kill credit
because the hate list and the kill credit list are the same.
