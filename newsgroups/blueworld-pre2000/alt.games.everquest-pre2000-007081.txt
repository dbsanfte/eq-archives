I have looked thru the spell lines anew, and you DO get some very good
spells in the alteration line in the later levels. As for now, I see
no reason to change from Alteration, mez being Conjuration and Color
Divination...

The spells you really don't want to fizzle would be your Color series.
You are going to be cycling them thru fights a lot... But then again
they don't use too much mana. I think Mez also... doesn't use enough
mana/fizzles enough to warrant it being specialized when your skill is
up there in the 200's.

The main point should probably be to save mana, and as the new spells
you get at higher levels use proportionately more mana, you will find
that Alteration might not be so bad a choice afterall.
