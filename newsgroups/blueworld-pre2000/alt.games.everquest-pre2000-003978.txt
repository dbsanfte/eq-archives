>When trying to advance a skill, do you get more advances by trying to do
>make something that is just above your current skill in triviality or are
>any more challenging items just as easy to advance with?  I know you won't
>be as sucessful, but does the skill advance just as fast?
>
>For example using Jewellry Craft Skill of 46:
>
>Silver Emerald trivals at 48, with a cost of approximately 14 pp per
>attempt.
>Electrum Malachite trivials at 74, with a cost of approx 3pp per attempt.

>Wouldn't it be wise to just skip Silver Emerald and go right to Elec
>Malachite, foregoing the sales of successes in an effort to save cost?  Even

This was exactly my approach to baking and tailoring.  Baking I did fish
cakes from (1) to (81) (I think 81) and they finally went trivial at that
level.  Yes, I failed a lot at first, but my skill went up on failures too,
so no big deal.  With tailoring I did (and still do) swatches just cause
they stack and sell for quite a bit more and the only other thing I tailor
is backpacks.  I don't buy anything but the pattern, so it costs next to
nothing for failures, and my skill still raises.  If I get a success then
_cool_ cause the backpacks sell for a decent amount.
