When I first read your message I assumed you arrived second, figuring if you had
been there first you certainly would have mentioned it. While it's highly
unlikely that you both arrived at exactly the same time, I'll take your word for
it that you did.

Gynok Moltar is not a FFA type spawn. He's the only spawn on the top floor of
Befallen that will give you exp at that level so it's an incredibly boring
spawn. To have 2 people sitting there camping him is just plain silly. You
couldn't figure out a way to cooperate? There was a thread recently about
camping Mitty and how they handled sharing her. Since she (and Gynok) drops more
than one kind of ring, they grouped together and made a deal about which person
would get which ring that dropped. After the first camper received their ring,
they stayed and helped the other camper with theirs, etc. etc. as other people
joined the group with the same deal made. Another way to handle it would be to
decide with person would stay and then tell the other person when they were done
with the camp. The reason people "others would show up and then leave" is that
it's generally understood that if someone is camping Gynok, it's rude to plop
down and camp him too.

Rare quest spawns like Gynok, Regis, Mitty are not considered FFA spawns. Even
Dyllin is not considered one even though he's in that horrible place called
Highhold Pass. You can treat them as FFA spawns but you'll eventually earn quite
the reputation and subsequently find grouping difficult. Good thing you're a
druid.
