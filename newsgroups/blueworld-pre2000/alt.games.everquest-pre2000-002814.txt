This is a new guild that is being formed on the Povar server

The guild name is "The Order of Truth"

 The Order of Truth
The Order of truth believes in order, discipline, and the pursuit of truth.
The Order  will not recruit ogres or trolls for theses races have no order
and are filthy animals. The order respects the Gnomes and Halflings and will
recruit them. However a Gnome or Halfling will never rise in rank in the
order because the order believes that one of such short stature is not fit
to order troops with the exception of short stature ones who go above and
beyond the call of duty. Dwarves  may become officers a bit easier then the
Gnomes and Haflings because the order has noticed that all though dwarves
are small, they pack a mean punch and they have at times shown the skills
needed to command troops. The Order will recruit all classes and all other
races then the ones stated above. The Order believes in the pursuit of truth
and in that members of the order will journey to the end of  Norrath to find
out about Norraths history and no foe or friend will hold this Order back
from finding the truth.

See you in Norrath
