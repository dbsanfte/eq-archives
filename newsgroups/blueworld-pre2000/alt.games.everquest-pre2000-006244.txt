As I understand how tracking works, it parses all mobs in range
at the moment that you hit the button, and then shows the
newest mob at the top of the list.

If there is a situation where a number of low mobs have spawned
recently in the regions that the ranger can see and the druid
can not, then these will crowd out the top of the list.

At 17th level I am beginning to wonder how to keep my skill
from increasing any farther.

I wish that there were some way to filter tracking, but I
suspect that as long as there are a good number of rangers out
there and a good number of folks rolling up new rangers the
skill will not be changed.
