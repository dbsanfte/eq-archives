Like the sentiment goes, if you wait to get a computer because the latest
technology is just around the corner, you'll NEVER get one.  Buy it now.

If you are primarily going to be playing games with your system, a 1ghz chip
is probably overkill and way too expensive.  Get the 800 mhz and a GOOD
graphics card, which will make the most difference in gaming speed.  I also
recommend a large capacity harddrive.  They are very cheap and games take up
a lot of space nowadays.  I installed Soldier of Fortune and it doesn't even
give you an option before installing 700 megs!
