A little tip for a new mage, If you give your pet a rusty 2 hander, he will
hit for 18. That is worth the cost of a 2 hander, so I would buy them or
loot them whenever possible. Just cast your best pet, give him a 2 hander,
and tank for him so he doesn't die, you will be amazed how many yellows you
can kill.
