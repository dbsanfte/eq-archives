It sounds like he was not recognizing the fire emeralds for 
some reason.  I am pretty sure that there is only one item
in the game called a "Fire Emerald".  Those are gems that 
you buy from the metal/high end gem merchants for roughly 
90pp each.  They look like a mottled light green candle with
a dark red base.

The other possibility would be that you handed them to the 
wrong centaur.  Bad targeting makes that easy to do.  (I
once handed a completed Shadow Silk to my friend's pet.)  You
can make sure that you have the right one by looking at the
name on the top of the trade window or by hailing him after
you have put the first item in the window.  Initiating a 
trade always sets your target to the person you are trading
with.
