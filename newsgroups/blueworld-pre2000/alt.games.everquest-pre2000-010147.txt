In and of itself it does not. I don't think anyone can find fault with them
haveing a problem with people going off to school or work allowing their
character to "play" for them all day practising some skills. I have a
problem with allowing that kind of playing, or should I say, lack thereof.
Does it directly effect my play? No. But it does effect the atmosphere of
the game when everyone is standing silent, not even at their machines, while
their characters mindlessly perform the same task over and over again. Like
weaving through a room of Woolworth manequins.

I do think the advancement of some skills needs to be looked at. Pick Locks
is certainly one. I think one fix would be to make available for purchase
locks of varrying difficulty that one could use to practise on during other
downtimes, like when healing from a fight. Isn't that how most of us would
practise such skills in real life rather than hopeing to find a handy door
to play with? That would reduce the draw of macroing to advance these skills
and also make a nice money sink. More advanced locks could cost more and
even be from quests or drops.
