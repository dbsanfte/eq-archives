> The ease or difficulty of implementation here is really moot.  How often

> We've gotten tons of suggestions for various confirmation boxes in game.  If

> having to click YES.  Among those suggested: Box for Attacking. Box for
> Dropping items.  Box for handing items (above and beyond that which exists).
> Box for Hailing NPCs.  Box for Zoning.  Box for Combining Items in a Kit.
> Box for Forgetting Spells.  Box for Disembarking a Ship.  In addition, for

> "Are you *absolutely* sure?" boxes for most of the things listed above.

In general, confirmation boxes that double check whether you really 
wanted to do something are a lousy fix for a weakness in an interface.

I can see having one for zoning (or some other mechanism for confirmation)
since the invisible line of pet death is a terrible nuisance, but you're
right that the rest are ridiculous.  A visible indication or warning that
you are about to pass through a zone would be handy and would be far less 
intrusive than a confirmation box.

As for combining items in a kit, I would not let the materials be 
destroyed on a "these items do not combine".  That solves the problem
without a confirmation box.

Returning to life at an unsafe bind point is the result of bad planning
on the part of the player.
