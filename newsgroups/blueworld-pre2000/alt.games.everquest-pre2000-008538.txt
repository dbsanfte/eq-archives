pearls.*  I've seen avos spawn with rusty weapons and all of 3 silver in coin
on them.  Lots of avos spawn with that kind of loot.  If I engage an avo with

who engaged it first.  Sure, they are getting the seconds, but this is
really no different than a group that killed it for exp, and left the loot. 
I'm taking some loot, and leaving the exp for someone else who wants it.

Note, that as a solo pick pocketting the avos I am taking significantly more
risk than a group who engages for experience then leaves the loot.
