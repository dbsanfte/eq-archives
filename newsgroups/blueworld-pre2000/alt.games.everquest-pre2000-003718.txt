Perhaps you misunderstand me.

Pulling to a safe place to fight, away from any possible interference,
allowing the casters to meditate undisturbed, is good tactics.  
Pulling to the zone boundary so that you can hop out if things don't 
go well is cheesy, because you are taking advantage of a very artificial
property of the world, namely the fact that monsters can't cross zone
boundaries.  It is also bad tactics in most dungeons because all trains
go to the zone.

When you first enter a dungeon with your party, you will have to deal 
with the gate guards somehow.  Either you will fight them where they 
are, or you will cast invisibility and sneak past them to get to where
you actually want to fight.  If the only thing in the dungeon that you
can take is the gate guards, then you will end up fighting right next
to the zone and fleeing from other people's trains fairly often.

In Unrest, the best place to camp out is the yard.  Too many trains 
come through the courtyard.  Similarly with Mistmoore, the best place
for a young group is up behind the lake where you can watch the
vampirish ancilles run to the zone and walk back to the castle.  In 
Paw, you probably want to be in one of the little alcoves before the 
bridge.  I am not as familiar with Lower Guk and have never been to 
Kerra.  Yes, I have seen people playing the way you suggest in every
dungeon and every zone.  I think those people are using a very 
cheesy tactic and not even an effective one.
