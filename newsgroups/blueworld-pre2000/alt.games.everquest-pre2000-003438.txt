Losing equipment would suck, and would hurt classes unequally.  Better
incentives could be come up with than losing items for not paying taxes.

If it were determined that taxation is a good idea (not sold on it myself)
to improve the EQ economy, the following might work:

Taxes based on level, paid in person to NPCs in various towns.  Failure to
pay results in loss of faction, payment in addition to required levels
results in improved faction.

Taxes based on bank account balance, charged either based on realtime
passed (semi-realistic) or on online time passed (more fair).

Taxes assessed by various NPCs on an ad-hoc basis - this would be amusing
but probably too hard to code.  Various NPCs in towns and travel lanes
would pick passerby at random, evaluate their equipment, and demand a fee.
Pay them, hide from them, run from them, or kill them, just like real-world
revenuers.

Another option might be to introduce much more expensive food and beverages
and give some incentive to use them (like stat penalties after X online
hours (time based on level) eating only muffins and drinking only milk).
