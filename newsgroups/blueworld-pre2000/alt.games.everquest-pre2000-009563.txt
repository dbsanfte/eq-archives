That person was mislead.  The material cost for one attempt is 160pp.
They are harder to make than plat ruby.  I can usually make plat ruby
veils about 1/3 of the time, making the cost around 700pp, and I sell
them for 750pp.  The fire opal rings succeed less than 1/4 of the time, 
for a similar cost of 700pp.  If I ever had one for sale, I would ask
about 750pp also.

Costs for platinum jewelry are pretty stable now on E'Ci:

Plat jade/jasper/star rose rings: 225pp
Plat ruby veil: 750pp
Plat sapphire necklace: 700pp

... unless, of course, you can find a clueless nimrod or very kind 
person who is willing to give you a gift of several hundred plat 
and ask less than those prices.
