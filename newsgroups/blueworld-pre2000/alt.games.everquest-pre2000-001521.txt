I think the big problem with 3D rendered graphics on PCs is that the objects
have to be rather simple in order to keep the frame rates up and the
textures are less detailed in order to accomodate the various 3D cards out
there which have varying amounts of memory for textures. If graphics
accelerator technology continues to advance at the same rate that it has
over the last 3 years we will have some smokin' fast cards in the coming
year or so and that will translate to better graphics quality all around.

One of the differences between UO and EQ is that most of the objects in EQ
are not pre-rendered so when you see a skelly with a sword, it will actually
have that sword when you kill it. In UO, all monsters of a particular type
always look the same. Or perhaps there are only a couple of variations (with
weapon, without weapon).

I also enjoy going into a 3rd person view on EQ and pulling the camera back
for a panoramic view. It's very cinematic. OTOH I prefer games where the
graphics look, well..., graphical (for lack of a better word) not
photo-realistic. For me it's a tossup between EQ and UO in the graphics
department. It's the gameplay that will make the difference in the long run.
