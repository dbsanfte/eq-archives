As far as I could figure out, the only rats in Kaladim are the
rat that spawns in the mines.  It comes in pretty quickly, and
will hang out in the mine for a few minutes and then run out
and wander over towards the forge.  My cleric was killed by the
rat at level 3, so be careful with it.

Wander out of the city - go to South Kaladim then walk through
the big gate with the guards and into the Butcherbock
mountains.  Right in front of the gates is a good lil hunting
zone for newbies.  Kill lots and lots of bats, decaying
skeletons, and other things.

Remember to save all the bone chips (from skeletons) and bat
wings you can.  Other players will buy these for a gold piece
each in stacks of 20.  That is good money for a newbie.

And be careful not to fall into the fountain in front of the
warrior's guild.  I think that fountain has killed more people
than many of the monsters.
