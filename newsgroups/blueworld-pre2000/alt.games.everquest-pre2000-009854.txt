The best way to earn money around level 10-15 is to kill wisps.
Every one of my characters has funded their new spells by 
killing wisps. :)

Get yourself a magic weapon (runed totem staff is about 10pp) 
and head to any of the zones where wisps abound.  At level 9, 
you will probably need a partner.  Kill and kill and collect 
up all the lightstones and greater lightstones you can find.

Take the greater lightstones to the gypsy camp in North Karana.
Give them to the gypsy there who talks about research.  She
will give you a book that sells for 7-9pp, depending on your
faction and charisma.  Make sure you have a top level inventory
spot open, since the book is a container.

You can also give her regular lightstones and get a different 
book that sells for a few silver, but if you are after money
you might as well just sell the lightstones (about 1pp).

Both of these quests also give a significant amount of experience.

If you don't want to go to North Karana, then auction off the 
lightstones.  Players will usually pay 7pp for greater lightstones
and 1pp for regular lightstones.
