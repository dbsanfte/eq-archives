He's talking about place holders.  Each zone has a number of spawn points.
Most of the spawn points will spit out one or more creatures some period
of time after the last thing generated by that spawn point was killed.
The period of time is generally fixed.  Spawns will slow down when zones 
get too crowded with people and monsters.  Also, Abashi said that a random 
component has been added recently to certain long spawns, like gods and 
dragons.

Most of these spawn points can potentially generate a number of different
creatures.  So, if you want a particular thing to spawn, you have to kill
all the other things generated by its spawn point.  If the creatures stand
still this is relatively easy, but if they wander around you have no real
way of knowing which you have to kill.  By killing everything in the zone,
you may be clearing out the spawns from the spawn point you need and 
increasing the chance of the rare spawn.
