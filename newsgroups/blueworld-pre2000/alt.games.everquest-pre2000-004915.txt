At some point it becomes necessary.  My fiance quit the game for a 
while.  When he returned, I was nearly ten levels ahead.  He wants
to play again and I want to play with him.  Through some mild
power-leveling, we managed to narrow the gap to about 3 levels, but 
then he stopped playing again and we are back to 5.  

Unless you can manage to let the higher level player die a lot, 
there is no way to narrow the gap while playing as a group.  I wish 
Verant would tweak the experience code so that the gap narrowed a 
bit over time, closing one level difference over several levels 
played, but they seem uninclined to do so.  In face, from something 
Gordon said, it seems that they don't even understand the
implications of their current scheme.

In any case, what I would suggest is having your wife help you kill 
things while not grouped.  This will close the gap very quickly.
