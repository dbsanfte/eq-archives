This is EXACTLY the problem with PKs and it's why the virtual world
arguments fall apart.  The same solutions that work in the real world
don't always work here.  If you kill someone, that character doesn't
die.  Reputation problems are avoided with mules, twinking to 'clean'
characters, etc.  Even if someone gets a bad enough reputation, they
can always make a new character and go right back to what they were
doing.

The consequences aren't there.  If a murderer gets killed in the real
world, he's dead.  He doesn't come back to pick up where he left off,
perhaps with a grudge against the people who killed him.  There just
isn't any way for the people who dislike their behavior to get rid of
them.

To add insult to injury, most PKs aren't even playing the game as it
was meant to be played.  These are 'Role Playing Games'.  These people
may occasionally try to excuse their actions by claiming to be role
playing 'evil' or something.  Of course, they are really just around to
be an asshole and ruin other people's game.  Some people in this thread
have commented that some of these people specifically target role
players.

That's why people keep trying to get the company to do something to
protect them in the game and it's why many people are very skiddish
about others.  The criminals aren't playing the same game and they can
run rampant without fear of consequences.

Reading the message above gave me an interesting idea for a deterrent
to PKs.  Don't kill them.  Arrest them, put them on trial, and throw
their character's ass in Jail for X amount of time.  That takes them
out of circulation for a period of time and it means the PK can't get
his jollies with that character unless he's PKing other inmates (and
that's more appropriate in jail anyway).

Sure, he can still make other characters, but that's more trouble (and
couple this with a game where you actually have to work your character
up instead of this macroing I've heard of with UO).  If you are limited
to X many characters per server like in EQ, that's going to limit that
approach too.

Make it so characters can arrest someone and take them in for trial (by
computer so there can be no tossing someone in jail when they are
innocent and to prevent exploits.)  That'll catch the ones that do
things out of town.  It's unrealistic, but allow arrests only if guilty
so this can't be a harrassment tactic (arrest innocent person and haul
them to jail just to annoy them).  Of course, you would have to subdue
them somehow.  After all, that gives people a chance to play the
bandit, murderer or whatever and perhaps keep getting away with it for
a while.

Add some property confiscation there too.  Confiscate everything while
in jail and don't give it all back.  After all, axe murderers don't get
their axe back when they get out of jail, do they?

To curb any complaints about paying to play the game, make the jail a
big complex where they can run around and do things inside.  Perhaps
make it a prison-mine kind of thing with lots of tunnels and nasties
running around inside so they can fight (with tools since no real
weapons are allowed to criminals).  Perhaps part of the jail is an
arena where they can fight for time off their sentence and non-prisoner
spectators can watch the fun and place bets.  (Fights would, of course,
be to unconsiousness to prevent suicide as a way to get out of jail.)
Put rumors in of a secret way to escape, but make it very difficult to
find and random (to prevent the first PK to find it from posting it for
everyone).  Escapees, of course, would be subject to rearrest and
wouldn't get any of their confiscated goods back until they had been
released for time served.  Put wanted posters out so PC bounty hunters
can make a reward bringing in PC criminals to serve out their time.

I don't think the Non-PKs should be the only ones with consequences.
Give some to the PKs too.
