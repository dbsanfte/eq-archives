No, you cannot patent an idea.  You may patent a particular
implementation of
an idea.  (This is somewhat analogous to copyright applying to a
particular
expression of an idea.)  This is why companies will often attempt to
protect
technologies with a whole slew of patents, especially technologies they
wish
to license (once a patent is granted the patent becomes part of the
public
record).
It is also worth noting that it is the patent holder's responsibility to
enforce the patent, by asserting it publicly and taking violators to
court.
Why is it that every attempt to restrain jerks in a civilized fashion
winds up
generating fees for more lawyers?  There has got to be a better way!
