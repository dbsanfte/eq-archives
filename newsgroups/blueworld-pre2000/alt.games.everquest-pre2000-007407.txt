>why turn off /ooc and /shout?

The Butcherblock is home to a lot of people who camp dorfs.

Many of these folks are very nice folks.

Many of these folks (and these two categories overlap) like to
conduct extended conversations using the /ooc and /shout
channels.

I don't mind it when they use /ooc to hold across zone
conversations about the best fast food for playing everquest,
but this sort of conversation can get annoying and so I turn it
off.

When these conversations move into /shout I will ask them to
take it to /ooc

Usually I am just passing through butcher and find it easier to
ignore the discussions, but if I am going to be there for a
while I will be prepared to turn on my chat filters.

That is all - I don't say turn them on, just be ready to turn
them on if you feel the need.
