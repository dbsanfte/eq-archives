The way I used to explain this when I was about 14 was this :-

"Imagine a piece of paper.  Its plane is two dimensional (plus a third
dimension - time).  Now imagine two points on the page and measure the
distance between those points.

Now fold the piece of paper along a line anywhere between the two points to
a perpendicular (right angle).  You have just moved the points from three
dimensional sapce-time into four dimensional space-time.

Our perception is of four dimensions, however, if it were possible to
identify multi-dimensional space-time above four dimensions, the reduction
of distance between points continues."

So basically, beyond 4 dimensions, the limit of C is still there but the
distance is reduced and in the perceptionof 4 dimensions movement at close
to C takes place faster than C when you measure the points between them.

So faster than (4 dimensional) light travel is possible in
multi-dimensional space-time.  To date, a number of scientists believe they
have located points in space-time with (last time I checked about 8 years
ago) 15 dimensions.  They are an important part of Superstring theory.

You can probably read up on this on the Web.
