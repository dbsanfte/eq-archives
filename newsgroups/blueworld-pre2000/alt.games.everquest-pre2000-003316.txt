I'm surprised that nobody's mentioned C.S. Lewis or George MacDonald.
Everyone seems to like Tolkien, but he was also the member of a group
of writers called the Inklings during that time - from what I gather,
they sat around at a pub drinking beer and smoking pipes and fueling
each others' literary fires. Lewis has written the Chronicles of Narnia
series - for children, but hardly something any adult should pass up
because of that. MacDonald's best "fantasy" work would be The Princess
and the Goblins and the subsequent items in that series.
