It depends on the group and the situation.  If you are fighting
single easy targets and your only concern is taking them down
fast, then you probably want an Enchanter in a group of four.
Less than that, and their buffing/debuffing abilities are not
too exciting.  Of course, if things are that easy, then you can
use the Enchanter as a weak Wizard in addition to their buffs. ;)

If you are getting pulls of two or three then you want an
Enchanter right after you have basic healing and damage dealing.
If you are a Necromancer, then the next person you want is an 
Enchanter.  After fighter, healer, the next one you want is an 
Enchanter.  So you would want an Enchanter in a group of 2 or 3.

> :> The best soloing classes are necros and druids.
> 
> At 40+ magicians, shamans and enchanter all outsolo druids.

Enchanters are not better soloers than Druids.  Enchanters are
considered good soloers because, with enough luck, they can take 
down bigger stuff solo than anyone else.  However, the fact that 
they have no healing at all makes solo play slow.  Eventually you 
take damage and then you are sitting around in Troll form waiting 
to get back to full health at a rate of 40hp/minute.  Perhaps you 
can work out an arrangement with a Druid to keep you healed and 
SoWed in exchange for Clarity, but then you aren't really soloing. ;)

If what you want is fast reliable solo advancement, then 
Necromancer, Druid or Magician is a far better choice.
