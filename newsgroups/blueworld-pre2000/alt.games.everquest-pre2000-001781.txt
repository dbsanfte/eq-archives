Interesting statement.  It becomes even more involving when you dont know if
the person on the other end is using some new hac, knows a new exploit to
one hit kill your char and the permanently ruin your char(remember poisoning
ghosts?)

That is why non-consensual PvP will NEVER work in an massive multi game and
why i think that so many people scream CHEATER when they are killed.   You
just never know if you wre beaten by a better player or if you were beaten
by the latest 3rd party hack or some new exploit.

The switch works very well in EQ, the only people complaining about PK'ing
are the PK's and thats the way it should be.

I do not think a switch would work in UO, because IMO, UO centers around
PvP, the game was designed from the ground with PvP in mind.  It's just a
shame that they didnt work a little bit more on the UI or it may have been
more accessable to people.

For the record i was a UO player for over 1 year, it was one of the best(if
not the best) games(experiences)I have ever in the PC world, as it is for
most I assume.  I left UO when i started EQ beta4.  To many cheats, to many
exploits and far to many ways for others to ruin my gaming experience.
