Take my roommate, who is playing a wood elf druid with 100 cha and 95
wis.  He is assuming that charisma affects Befriend Animal, and plans on
being the only druid to use Feral Spirit.  :)  He has stated that he
does not plan on being the "most powerful" druid out there, but that he
will have a lot of fun playing this character.

This game should be played to be enjoyable.  Perhaps a lot of people
would prefer to compute what the best-suited race / class combo might
be.  To each his own;  but it would be nice to _know_ what the points do
(such as whether or not CHA makes Befriend Animal more effective).

: You are also completely neglecting role playing.  Some people
: could not stomach the idea of playing a dark elf, no matter 
: how favorable their statistics might be.  Others will pick the
: face they find most attractive or the race that looks best in
: plate mail.

Personally, I find myself shying away from wood elves and dark elves.
This may be because I find the decision in EverQuest to be more one of
class than of race, so I pick a class and then evaluate the various
choices available to that class.  (In my opinion, the way character
creation is handled is silly - you should be able to pick a class and
select among the races just like you can currently select a race and see
which classes it can be.)

On the other hand, some choices I find myself making for reasons that as
far as I can tell appeal only to myself.  I prefer half-eves to humans
because of a) night vision and b) they are slightly shorter, making some
things easier (such as walking over merchant's counters - they do not
have to duck.)  Also, take an enchanter to freeport and try to walk up
the trees.  You will find that gnomes, humans, and high elves have a
hard time going up this incline, and that it is downright impossible as
an Ogre.  But a half-elf, wood elf, or halfling can waltz up it with
ease.

(Use the tree out in the newbie area if you are going to try it as an
Ogre, or benevolence the guards first, of course.)
