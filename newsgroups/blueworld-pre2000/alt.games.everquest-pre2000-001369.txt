I played a text based MUD that was a fairly major one called GemStone III
for at least 6 years. While it was never as graphically intense as.... well,
anything (looked like DOS for cryin out loud), it was still a great world
that I fell in love with. While I couldn't SEE the Massive Troll King, I
could surely imagine how huge and horrid it must be... especially after my
first encounter with one which ended in it actually sitting down and making
a meal of my corpse. The healers decided it would take a team of experts to
patch me together after that fiasco! And there were a LOT of invasions in
that game. Everyone loved invasions because it really gave the Lords in the
game a chance to shine and, in the classical sense.... be a hero. I remember
one invasion where a level 90 something wizard who had been around forever
and was truely a legend in the game got grabbed by an ancient blackened
wraith and sucked into a void ala. Gandalf and the Balrog in the Mines of
Moria.... was incredible to see, and even though everyone there knew it was
a show put on by the GMs - that no monster could actually DO something like
that - it was very exciting as most of us were dead on the ground, and only
he and the wraith and a few powerful others were still alive in that room.
He came back victorious, and after all were healed and rezzed we celebrated
his victory.

Then there was the time that I was dead on the field, being one of the first
to an invasion of the main town in the game.... (notice, I spent a lot of
time dead... I was the one TRYING to be a hero and not quite making it <g>,
but all the clerics and healers new me by name!). A powerful rogue/assassin
who was a friend of my "in game" fiance came swooping down and slew
everything in the room.... quite easily I might add, yet another legend in
that land.... it was incredible. My point is, it's the people who make the
game, and the powerful people who make the heros... not just by slaying this
or that monster but by helping people for no other reason than they need
help. The GMs can create spectacular situations and invasions (like the last
day of Beta) and give this or that "hero" a chance to shine, but it's up to
the players themselves to be heros to each other, just by being kind. It's
the simplest thing in the world, but we often miss it in the rush to be the
"highest level thisorthat". Take the time to interact with other people, and
the game becomes what it should be... an online world, not just a
multiplayer game where we're all trying to get to level 50 before the other
guy.

<End rant>
