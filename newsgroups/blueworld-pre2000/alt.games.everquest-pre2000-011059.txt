For bows you add the bow range and arrow range together, and
then you add the bow damage and arrow damage together.

So with my range 150 compound darkwood bow (19/41) and 2/150
arrows I have a range of 300 and a modal damage of 21.

Summoned arrows would give me a range of 200, which is still
pretty good.

I have only begged magic arrows from a mage once, and that was
when I was expecting to solo the ghoul messenger and thought I
would be kiting.  (hint for those at home, ghouls cast root)
