Honestly, UO has its advantages.  If not for the jerks and the mouse-intensive
interface (tendonitis), I would probably still play it.  Any posts comparing
it to EQ at this late date are quite rare and probably trolls.

I would agree with nearly every change Verant has made.  They have nerfed when 
it was needed and buffed several classes that were underpowered.  I was sad to 
lose Whirl til you Hurl (my most effective spell at the time) to nerfing, but 
I do not feel that the Enchanter class was reduced below average by the change.

I'm not sure what lower levels you are talking about who have nothing.  I don't 
think anyone would complain about the variety of hunting grounds available to 
characters under level 20.  The set of possible hunting grounds starts out huge 
and dwindles as you get to higher levels.  Now at level 35, I choose between 
about 5 different zones in which to hunt.  There are more than that available, 
but several options are precluded by my character's beliefs, the importance of 
her faction standings with various groups, and my personal taste for the type
of hunting I like to do.

I agree that it would be better if the world were larger, but I would much 
rather see Verant turn out a slow trickle of well designed zones than have 
them spew tons of unbalanced boring garbage.
