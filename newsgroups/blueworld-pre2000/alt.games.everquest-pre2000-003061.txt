Yeah, but I think that you're missing a point here.  I remember when
people were having a blast exploring Norrath - the size didn't even
matter at the time.  Then slowly but surely as the place began to get
more and more explored, the gripes concerning how long it took to get
from point A to point B started increasing.

The AC world is titantic.. it's still at the stage where it's fun to
explore, etc.  But as the secrets of the world get revealed to the rest
of the gaming population, the same thing will start to happen.  People
will start bitching like old women how long it takes to travel the
world.  Also, as stated in a previous post, a large majority of people
ARE going to congregate to areas that offer relatively better
"experience" and "loot" (just like in EQ) - people will gribe about how
long it takes to get from hot-spot 'A' to hot-spot 'B'.
