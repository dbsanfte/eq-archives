There appear to be sweet spots for stats at 75 and again at
100.  So it makes sense to raise stats to that point.

Above that the effects are relatively minor and many people
will focus on AC.

If you do want to increase your stats more, then it will depend
on how you equip and how you fight:

str for encumberance and some extra attack
dex for archery and some extra weapon procs
agility for a little extra armour class
