No, the hate list is only so long.  In addition, as people do
damage, heal and so on they are constantly moving up on the
hate list.  The damage list seems to be very close to the hate
list.  When the mob dies, the person or group at the top
of the damage list gets the credit. (so no, a well-timed taunt
should not let you kill-steal)

In a situation where many people are hitting the same thing you
just have to be at the top of the recent damage list to get
credit.  

Of course, if the critter is killed by a DoT cast by someone
who has since been pushed off the hate list, expect the corpse
to vanish as if a guard had killed it.

In normal combat with one group of six and a passing stranger
or two, everyone should remain on the hate list and the normal
rules of damage-dealing apply.  Plane mobs, Lockjaw, and some
other situations are about the only place where the short hate
list comes into play.
