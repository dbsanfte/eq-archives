The main concern is the storage of tracking everyone who has ever
killed you. We'd have to have cleanup either with timestamps or when
the character in question is deleted; we'd have to consider whether
there is a maximum size to the list (would someone try to crash the
server by making mules to kill a buddy over and over until the memory
load got silly?); how slow would searches through the list be once the
dataset got large; etc... you get the idea. It's feasible, but it may
be too expensive.

I noted when I replied to your post, Shaky, that we kinda do an
abstraction of this already. Instead of tracking it per person, we
just say, "at 5 incidents, you're permaflagged to everybody."
