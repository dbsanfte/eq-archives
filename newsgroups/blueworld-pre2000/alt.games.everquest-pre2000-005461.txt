SOW and Selo's are better than Journeyman Boots, and shaman/druid/bard
regen spells are better than the rubicite breastplate, but the point
is that any single class in the game used to be able to obtain these
two items before level 25 (the teens if they were in a guild). There
are all sorts of spells that reduce downtime (like the ones you
mentioned in the previous paragraph), but they are class-specific or
shared between a few classes.

Also, don't forget that rubicite regen stacks with other forms of
regen, such as troll and spell-based regen.. Rather than nerf the
breastplate and cause widespread anger, they took it out of the game
and replaced it with class-specific armor that looks better on the
surface (stat buffs, higher AC), but is missing the one thing that
made rubicite truly useful -- regen. When people complained about the
regen thing, Verant used the extra stat bonuses as a crutch to fall
back on.
