I pulled this off EQVault

Kill Stealing Policy:

Kill Stealing is now officially defined as Killing an NPC or Mob for any reason,
that is already attacking another player. Any player that is caught
intentionally kill stealing by a guide or GM may now be warned. These warnings
when accumulated, can lead to the player being suspended or banned from
EverQuest by a GM.

Play Nice Policy:

Spawns in the game can no longer be claimed or controlled by a single player or
group. Wether it is a single or multiple spawn, for an item or for XP, low or
high level, all groups wishing to camp a spawn must work out some type of
rotation or means to share the spawn.

- Zackron
