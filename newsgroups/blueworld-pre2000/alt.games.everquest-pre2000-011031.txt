It's true that most of your spells are more useful to lower level
characters than higher level characters, but there are a few things
that people really appreciate from Mages:

Summoned Bandages - quite a boon for any non-healer up to about 
	level 20 when people start being able to afford real ones
Summoned Arrows - great because they hit magic creatures
Sword of Runes - for fighters at low levels or for any pet class
Coldstone - ultravision for any poor night blind non-casters
Damage shields - great to throw on lower level fighters when you
	find them in combat

I have often found myself trying to trade Clarity/Brilliance for
a couple Swords of Runes for my Necro partner. ;)
