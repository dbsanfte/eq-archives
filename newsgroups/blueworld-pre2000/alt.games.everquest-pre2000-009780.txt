Complete set of CHA jewelry from a jeweler:

	Gold Cats Eye Bracer: 7 CHA		30pp a pair
	Electrum Star Ruby Ring: 7 CHA (5 DEX)	220pp a pair

	Silver Cats Eye Necklace: 3 CHA		2pp
	Silver Star Ruby Veil: 5 CHA (5 DEX)	90pp

or, for the wealthy ...

	Platinum Cats Eye Necklace: 9 CHA	225pp
	Platinum Star Ruby Veil: 9 CHA (9 DEX)	550pp

Total is 36 CHA for about 340pp or 46 CHA for about 1000pp.

If you are very good friends with the jeweler and they have 
around 200 skill, then you might be able to get the first set 
for cost at about 270pp.

You can pick up just the silver and gold cats eye stuff to
get 17 CHA for 32pp, which is definitely worth it if you are
going to be making some big purchases.  The star ruby items
are really only useful to bards and enchanters.

Other items to consider are:

	Opalline Earrings (5 CHA, 2 AC)
	Crude Stein (15 CHA, 10 STA)
	Split Paw Tooth Necklace (5 CHA, 2 AC, 5 vs Poison)
	Silk Evening Tunic (5 CHA, 1 AC) 

I don't think any of those are terribly expensive.  You might 
try Maximum EQ for prices ( http://www.maximumeq.com/ ) but
take everything with a grain of salt.  Those prices are often
very far off.
