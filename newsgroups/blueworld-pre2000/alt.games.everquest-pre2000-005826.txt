of a parallel. Personally I think you should ignore any issue to do with

cleric and any other theist character in the game), good = what my god wants
me to do, or alternatively "how my god would act in a similar situation" -
hence the importance of myths.

And that's actually the problem - there's not much real reward/penalty for
acting for/against your god's dictates. (And given the setup in EQ real
