miniLinux wrote:
> 
> I am wondering what is the best view for playing EQ?
> I love the 1st person view but it limits all the spell visual and
> side/behind view?
> 
> What view do you use?

I use first person view most of the time in combat since that is 
the only view where the auto-targets work well.

When traveling, I normally use the follow camera, pulled fairly 
far out and all the way up, but centered on me.  This allows me
to see things that approach me from behind or monsters that may
be hiding just over the crest of a hill.

When camping an area, I normally set up the two fixed camera 
positions to look back at the group from the two main directions
of travel.  That way I can target monsters that are approaching
the group and mez the extras before they get to us.  Targeting
through the melee is nearly impossible, either with mouse or 
with auto-target commands.

I never use the top down view.


I would really like to see a couple changes to the camera system:

First, I would like my camera settings to be saved when I log out
so that I would not have to set them up again every time.

Second, I would like to be able to turn off some of the views so
that I could just switch between the views I actually want instead
of rotating through all five views.
