Mason Barge wrote:
> 
> >As a starting character, keep it simple.  Dont play a human... their night
> >blindness really hinders the gameplay until you have a good grasp of it.
> >Choose a high elf,
> 
> High elves are supposed to have infravision and don't.  I don't really see this
> as such a huge problem, though, and would consider it very little in picking a
> starting character.

Yes they do. :)

My High Elf can assume Human form to verify the difference.
The only races that do not get night vision are the humans:
Human, Barbarian and Erudite.

> >For classes, I would recommend a warror, paladin, ranger, magician, or
> >druid.
> 
> I would not recommend warrior or monk for a first PC, because they are so
> one-dimensional you will find it difficult learning group battle tactics.

If you expect to make a character just to try out the first few levels
and then start a new character once you have a feel for the game, any
kind of fighter is a good choice.  I would absolutely recommend making
a character that you expect not to play after a week or two.  Wait 
until you have a better idea of the world of Norrath and the roles of
the various classes before you pick the one that will occupy you for
months. :)

> I would also recommend an enchanter very highly, although you must realize that
> it will be quite difficult in the early levels and somewhat difficult in the
> 20's.  But:
> 
> 1)  It is fun, you get to charm animals and turn into objects.
> 
> 2)  At Level 29, the enchanter becomes the most valuable and desirable player
> in the game.
> 
> 3) You really get to learn a lot about group tactics
> 
> If you want to be an enchanter, ask someone else to recommend race.  This is
> the one place I would even consider being a dark elf, since many spells cannot
> be bought outside Neriak.  I would guess though, again, high elf might be the
> easiest to play, as humans have too little intelligence and erudites have
> balance problems.

For stat reasons, I would pick any of Dark Elf, High Elf or Erudite,
although Gnomes also make decent Enchanters.  Humans are a distant last
choice.  You will get a Dark Elf illusion spell at level 12, so buying 
spells in Neriak is not a concern.  Enchanters get a Human illusion at 
level 1, so a Dark Elf Enchanter can safely travel within the human and 
elven cities from very early on.

In the end, play what you like.  Enchanters are very powerful later in
life, but they don't appeal to everybody.  If you want to be able to 
hurt things, any other class is better.
