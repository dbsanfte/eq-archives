Find a nice druid and hit Lavastorm!  Avoid the elementals because they
have nasty damage shields and the imps because they cause too much fiery
grief to be worth the effort.  Rock Dervishes and Lava Crawlers are great.
You want the druid to cast Spirit of Wolf and Endure Fire and to heal you
during fights.  More tanks means more fun. :)

The dervish camp in West Commonlands provides decent hunting, and you can
kill wisps for cash and kodiaks or young kodiaks for experience if you 
can't get a good dervish group.

I liked Crushbone a lot when I was there.  I think you have to fight in
or near the castle at your level though.

You probably don't want to hit High Pass for another level or three.
Either the gnolls or the orcs are really good for an 18-20 group.
