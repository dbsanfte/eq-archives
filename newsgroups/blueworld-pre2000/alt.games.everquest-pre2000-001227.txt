Yeah, imagine people picking a race for roleplay reasons rather than for what
goes best for the class.  Also note that you can arrange the stat points
anyway that you like.  Giving you much more variability than the limited
system that EQ uses which only allows like 5% of total stat points to
allocate.

It is also true that you are not limited by the developers idea of what
classes are available in the game, but rather you can design your own.	This
is not the idiotic system that UO uses though where you can skill in anything
at anytime. You cannot make a character good at everything but are free to a
point to design your class.  And it will have some spell casting fighters,
just as EQ does with the Shadowkight, Ranger, and Paladin.  But you wont see
any full fledged Mages with tremendous fighting abilities running around.

So you can go with very limited options such as EQ, or much more varied
options as exist in AC.
