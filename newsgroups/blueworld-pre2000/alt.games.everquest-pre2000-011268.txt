Harmony cant fail in the *conventional* sense that it is resisted.
However things can go wrong with a harmony pull***.

For instance, try pulling the two spawns separately at the bottom left
of the Mistmore Entrance ramp......... You cant.

Either one of them's pathing will take it over the top of the other
when you try to pull one separately (and more mobs from behind the
lake too) which could give the impression that the spell has 'failed'
when in fact it has not.

Harmony can also run into trouble when you get a 'You cant see your
target' message, meaning that another(s) mob will be in the spell
radius of the mob you are trying to cast on. If you now go ahead and
pull, that out of sight mob will come as well, possibly running over
and waking up other harmonied mobs.

So basically, you must be pysically within casting distance+sight of
all mobs inside your target radius.

Harmony also has an effect duration. Some Druids I've seen try to use
harmony have been *excruciatingly* slow about harmonying a set of mobs
- often insisting on casting on all mobs and then having the spell
ware off on their fist cast.**

I also remeber having one Druid blame spell failure on an *auful*
harmony attempt in the Mistmore Grave Yard. Harmony worked on all the
ones by the tree ok - but what he failed to take into account, was
that by running directly back to the group, the mobs would go round
the other side of the crypt (agroing the extra mobs) rather than
straight down the left hand side.

He hadent a clue what went wrong - and neither did the others - they
gave up, refusing to have another go even when I tried to explain, and
the group disbanded. The Druid explained to the group enchanter who
had died, that he was sorry about the death - but there was nothing he
could do about harmony being 'resisted'.

** the worst case of this I ever saw, was a druid who insisted on
being the puller at the Icy Orcs in Everfrost. He refused to pull
until all 5 spawns were up, and then proceded to cast harmony 5 times
before pulling. When there were only 1 or two left, he would wait for
a full spawn of 5 before pulling again - scared of one poping mid
pull. 

I disbanded. FYI you only ever need cast harmony once on Redwind
before pulling any one of the other 4 at an angle. The pathing there
is fine and they will always run directly towards you.
