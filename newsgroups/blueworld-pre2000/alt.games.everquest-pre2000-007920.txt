I am one of those that couldn't find anything *actionable* about
Verant's intention to amend the EULA and scan.  It is a private,
contractual matter and does not implicate civil rights or any criminal
laws, IMO.

However, nothing is to stop consumers from complaining to legislative
and regulatory authorities or lobbying groups for either a legislative
ban/regulation of this practice or, at least, a system of voluntary
corporate compliance with a ban or a  set of standards.  That is the
purpose of these entities.  While I personally doubt the ACLU would be
interested (this does not involve fundamental constitutional rights
IMO), the EFF is a perfect body to push for a formal or informal ban
or at least a set of standards.  That is part of their mission.

Lobbying to change a corporate practice with which you do not agree
(but which is not currently prohibited) makes perfect sense.  That's
how laws are made in this country.  Nothing laughable about it.
