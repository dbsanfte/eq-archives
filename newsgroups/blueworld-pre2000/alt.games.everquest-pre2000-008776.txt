Sorry disagree Tim... as someone who has played a Rogue since BETA,
lemme tell you how pick pocket really worked:

Back in the "good ole days", Rogues used to Pick Pocket ridiculous
amounts of low end money off of MOBS and not affect their loot pool in
the slightest... in fact, I could tell you exactly what a MOB would
drop based uppon my pick pockets during combat... it was roughly
1/10of my initial PP!  If a MOB had 2 silver, 5 copper, my PPs during
combat would go something like this (and I am not exaggerating!) = 20
silver, 10 silver, 5 silver, 2 silver, 50 copper, 25 copper, 10
copper, 5 copper.  This is no joke!  and then I would loot the 2
silver 5 copper off at the end. I used to crawl out of Crushbone at a
snails pace, grossly encumbered by a couple thousand copper and 100s
of silver. I used to keep the MOBs alive as longas possible to sipon
off themaximum amountof coinage.  I loved it... I felt is was a good
justification for having fewer hps and worse armor than a warrior.  A
warrior can brute his way through a combat, but I got to fight the
critter and make a ton of cash (literally, do you know how much 2500
copper weighs)?

But now it is majorly different.  When I finish working over a MOB
during combat, he drops 1 coin of each type... period.  I no longer
get the huge sums I used to... my first pick pocket is roughly 2 times
the amount of coinage... I've tested it on numerous critters I've
soloed (believe it or not, I don't PP when I am in a group - there is
some honor among us thieves), and  a MOB that drops about 2 gold, I'll
get messages like = 4 gold, 2 gold, 1 gold, then on to silver,
 etc.  Just thought you'd like to know...

Now, as to the original issue of grouping with Rogues, I have this to
say:  If you don't like Rogues, too bad... I can find groups to be in,
and there are advantages to us:

1) Corpse retrieval:  I can drag a corpse out of anyplace and bring it
right to the front of the zone, no problem... I have saved numerous
party members from having to risk a naked foray into a dungeon to get
their bodies back... that's gotta be better than a sharp stick in the
eye anyday...

2) Ashroot Poison:  If my fellow rogues are not taking advantage of
this wondrous substance, shame on them!  When I poke a MOB with
Ashroot toxin, all the casters in my group get to blast the MOB who
suddenly has -30 to all his resistences.  Tell me that ain't a help...

3) Poison (in general):  If I am planning a big foray with my guild
buddies, I take poison along... I love doing an exta 100-200 points of
damage... it may cost a little, but the MOBs sure die faster.

4) Damage in general:  Rogues are not primary tanks... DON'T ASK ME TO
BE ONE!  But if I got a Warrior, SK, or Pally in my group, I get to
backstab MOBs like crazy... at 28th level I do 85-100 points per
backstab, and it's not hard to get a MOB to turn his back with the new
Evade skill (providing I am notthe only tank)... Plus I do a
continuous stream of damage from two fast weapons, duel wielded,
double attacking, and occasionally procing.  Yeah, I may not be the
primary tank, but I do so much damage in a combat, that by mid-point
the primary tank is slamming his Taunt button to keep the MOB off me
(and half the time getting ignored for all his work)... tell me again
that I can't dish it out.

All you people that don't want Rogues in your parties, have a grand
old time... I don't wanna be hanging with you anyway... there are
those that know our capabilities, and will invite a Rogue everytime...
Pickpocket issues aside... and we seem to do very well...
