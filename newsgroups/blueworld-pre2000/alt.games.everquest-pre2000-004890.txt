At least one tremendous enhancement to the Enchanter class has been
overlooked by many.  The casting times of the higher level mesmerization
spells (Entrance and Dazzle) were reduced enough that the spells became
viable.  Everyone forgets these things too quickly. ;)

This was a much needed fix because Mesmerization is considered better
than Enthrall, making it very difficult for Enchanters to transition
from the first to the second.  (When you get down to two or three 
opponents, you want to start using Enthrall, but you have to wait for
them all to wake up from Mesmerization first.)  Of course, it would be 
nice if that were fixed so that Enchanters could use their Mesmerize 
line more effectively between 20 and 34. ;)
