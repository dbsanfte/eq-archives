Yes, it DOES involve setting up a router if you use the Linux Box to 
IPMasq your game machine (As I assumed most people would want to do) - it 
serves as a NAT type router.  You have to configure the net card (eth0 or 
eth1, depending), the routing device (either ppp0 for modems or eth0 if 
you have cablemodem or ISDN), IPMasq using IPChains, and then set the 
proper routes in the routing table.  And you also have to know to turn on 
the IPV$ forwarding in the kernel as well.

Now if you already have a lan setup, and something that routes your IP to 
the internet, this step is not needed, if you dont mind running your 
Linux box with the ethernet card in PROMISC mode.  But for completely 
undetectable SHowEQ, you *must* do the routing stuff.

For you to not know the above shows that you really are the ignorant one 
here.
