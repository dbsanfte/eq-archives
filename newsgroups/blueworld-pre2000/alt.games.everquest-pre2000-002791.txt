Well said. To be honest, I don't like either UO or EQ. I've tried both for
months at a time. Perhaps it's the limitation of the technology, but I've
yet to see a truly balanced and enjoyable online RPG experience. I don't
believe any massively online RPG can provide enough depth for a player who
wishes to follow complex storylines. And as for balance, there will always
be assholes and assholes will always try to beat the system.

Origin's decision to go online only may make good business sense, but I
seriously doubt that many hardcore gamers who like a good, involving story
will purchase such games.

Now, online games that limit the number of players to the single digits ( a
la Baldur's Gate ) would be different altogether and I would certainly play
those.
