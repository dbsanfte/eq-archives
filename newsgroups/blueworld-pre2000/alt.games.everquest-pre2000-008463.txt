I think you are possibly right to nuke when fighting hill giants 
with a Necromancer and a Shaman as long as you don't get the
giant to attack you.

After everyone is buffed and the fight starts, I would normally
Tashan and slow the monster, then hit it with my DoT, then wait
and see how the fight progresses.  If the monster hits too hard,
I use color spells to keep it stunned so that the tanks take
less damage.  Alternately, I could cast a damage absorbing shield
(Rune/Rampage/Berserker Str) to take some of the healing work.
If I have a ton of mana and am feeling frivolous, I nuke.

The Shaman has better slow spells than you, so you don't have to
cast slow.

The Necromancer has better DoTs than you and is already down to 
three lines because of the Shaman, so you don't have to cast a DoT.

Giants can't be stunned or mesmerized, so no sense casting those.

Everyone has regeneration from the shaman, so heals are probably
not a problem.  If someone starts getting whacked on, back up and
let the pet(s) take some heat while you get healed.

Basically you can either nuke or Rune and you're better off nuking
than wasting resources keeping the pets up.
