Indeed bp's are either more common or the demand has dropped or both.
Being a master jeweller I've made bp chokers and rings for 8+ months.  First I
used to have a hard time finding (buying) bp's but sold the choker for 800 and the
ring for 1000pp while paying 150-175 per each bp.
Then people found out about the prices of the jewelry and bp started going for
around 200pp each.
Then lately, as people were of the impression that dexterity and agility stats
didn't matter, they changed to ac/hp and str/dex rings instead.  Now I buy the
bp's for 80-100pp and sell chokers for 250 and rings for 500pp.
Maybe with Aradune/brads posting of stats effects maybe there will be a resurgence
of these items.
