According to eq.castersrealm.com, any place that has critters from 16-22, or
so, is a good place to hunt.  I got mine from auctioning in Oasis, and
checking every merchant I came across.  That only yielded one side, and I
think it cost around 3 or 4p.  Since I was offering up to 10p for EITHER
side (I wanted a couple of each before finding a high level enchanter to do
the research for me), it was a good deal.

I DO recommend that you find a high level enchanter to do the research for
you.  Your Research skill at 16 SUCKS, and that danged page is HARD ENOUGH
to find, I didn't want to blow it.  After my level 16 enchanter got his
made, he came across another set and got that made for a guild member.

Both times, the high level enchanter was found in N Freeport.  I made it a
point to word my /ooc very similar to this:

/o Might there be a high level enchanter available to combine a couple pages
for me, please? I'll be glad to come to you!

I find it's important to NOT say "is there a high level enchanter in the
zone?"  They'll probably assume you're a clarity beggar.

If you can't find one there, always carry the tome with you on your travels.
Continue to try to find a high level enchanter.  You need the tome yourself
because the chances of the enchanter carrying theirs is unlikely.  BTW, both
times the enchanters were thrilled to help a lowbie enchanter with their
most important spell.
