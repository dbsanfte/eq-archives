This is great advice. My first character was a human warrior (something
i had no desire to continue:). This let me not obsess about his stats,
not worry about advancement or faction, and just settle in and learn how
to whack thinks and hit "run" and talk to npc's without dying. FYI, be
sure to die with this character(it's not that hard to do). That way you
can practivce the most importand skill in EQ: CORPSE RECOVERY :)

EQ has a good interface, but it takes some learning, don't make all the
newbie mistakes on the ONE character you plan to take to 50 :) Also, be
sure to  stop playing this character befor 5th or so, so you don't feel
such an attachment to it. Then you can start the character you WANT to
play (a Troll Shadow Knight for me) and feel good about you knowledge of
EQ
