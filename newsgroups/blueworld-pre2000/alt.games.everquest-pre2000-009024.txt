>There is an item(Sceptre of Sorrow, I believe) needed for one of the Necro

>know which NPC in Mistmoore drops it and its general location.

In the graveyard behind the crypt is a named Shadowman.  When you talk to him,
he tells you he cant enter the crypt, and asks you to get a flower from on top
of the coffin and bring it to him (the flower looks like a bag on the coffin and
respawns reasonably fast).  After bringing him the flower he gives you the
scepter.

Warning:

Make sure and walk straight to the flower because there are pit traps to the
left and right of the coffin.
