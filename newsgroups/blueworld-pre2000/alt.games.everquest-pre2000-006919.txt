Well I completely and utterly disagree and its this kind of warped
"fairness" that encourages camping.

The advantage you get over being a sad no-life and spending 36 hours camping
that its more likely the mob you want with the item will appear in that time
that in 1 hour.

If your in a group *everyone* has an equal claim on the items - and no I
don't accept arguments about it only going to people who can use it -
everyone can use a rare drop - to trade for something else so they dont have
to camp that. (The sole exception of course are no-drop items).

Randoming is the only fair way, and certainly the only way I would accept
without prior agreement (so I can choose not to stay in that group). Camping
in Najena for the JBoots convinced me absolutely of this.
