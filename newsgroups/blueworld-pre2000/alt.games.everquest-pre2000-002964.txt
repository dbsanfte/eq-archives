Well, I myself deal with the 'need to occasionally re-install and take a 
vacation whilst waiting for patches to download' in a rather simple manner.

Every couple of months, or just after a large patch, as soon as things on
my end seem to be working right, I just burn a CD-R of my UO directory.

Could do the same with a tape backup as well, or keep a copy of the most
recent UO directory somewhere else on your HD.

UO patches NEVER put any information in any other directory on the HD.

In the event of problems on my end, I do the following:
	A) Reinstall DirectX (Usually cures the problem, as these are system
	   files that oft get trashed by other programs)
	If A doesn't work, then:
	B) Delete the UO directory, and copy my backup there.
