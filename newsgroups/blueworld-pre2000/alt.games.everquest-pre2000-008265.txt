Mike  <friend@lightspeed.net> wrote:
>I have played a high-elf cleric to level 3 and he only has a strength of 
>65.  Some are telling me what a poor choice I made because dwarf clerics 
>have a strength of 95.  I prefer the height and appearance of the high 
>elf but want to know if the strength issue is going to be manageable and 
>how hard it is to gain strength raising items later in the game.  I 
>imagine strength is the easiest thing to raise.

Strength is easy to raise, but you will still be giving up
otherthings to raise it.

I started with a dwarf cleric, then decided that the
personality was boring and that there were TOO MANY other
dwarven clerics.  So I switched to a ranger.

I recently re-started a cleric as a secondary.  This time I
went with a high elf because there was a personality I wanted
to play and because I liked the look of the armor.

I went with a 70 strength, everything else into Wisdom, and I
do expect to have trouble looting after I start wearing heavier
armor. 

But if you like the notion of a high-elf cleric, there are a
lot of good high elf clerics out there.  heck, there are level
50 gnome clerics.  The important thing is not the stats, it is
whether you enjoy the character.  If it is fun to be a high
elf, then be a high elf.
