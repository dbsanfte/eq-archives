Yes, we get the same shit on Tribunal, it really sucks, but it isnt as bad as
the normal lag characterized by either high pings or crazy packetloss.

What I experience goes like this, there is a 15-30 second time frame where time
seems to stop, if you are in the middle of casting, the casting time window just
stays there, if anyone is meleeing, or being wailed on, that stops too.  After
this dead period, there is a perhaps 1-2 second period where the game seems to
cram about 5secs of activity into, a fast forward if you will.  Then everything
goes back to normal.  This is annoying (and damning evidence that their shit is
_way_ overworked) but it isnt very deadly, because unlike real lag, you arent
taking damage during the time freeze.
