Exactly, and some of the most rewarding moments in the game come when
you are helping someone through a rough spot.  Earlier tonight, I (a
level 7 ranger) and my friend ( a level 7 shaman) were cruising around
Blackburrow, when we saw this half-elf *walking* away from 2 gnolls
that were pounding on him.  Everyone in EQ knows this is a major
danger sign, otherwise he'd be running his butt off.  :)  So I and my
partner jumped in and started wailing on the beasts... Managed to get
them off of him, and finished them off while he healed  himself back
up.  He thanked us, and I in my best honorable ranger style,
responded, "Our pleasure, sir.  No one should have to fall under the
foul paws of those beasts."  Unfortunately our conversation was cut
short, as we noticed someone else in trouble, and ran to assist.
