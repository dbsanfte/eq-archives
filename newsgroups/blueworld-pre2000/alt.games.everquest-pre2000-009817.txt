> I understand the formula and the reasons for going to + mana after you reach

> how much damage they do. example do max damage 3 out of 10 times vs 9 out of
> 10 times.
> 
> I think i will sacrifice the additional mana if have a higher wis will make
> my spells hit for max damage, for often.
> I am looking for information on how this pertains to all caster classes

No, your wisdom will not affect spell damage or resists.  I don't think
it affects fizzles or channeling either.

In an article that was quoted on Casters Realm, Brad described the 
effects of the various stats:

   http://eq.castersrealm.com/viewarticle.asp?Article=1227

   INT: directly affects how quickly you can learn most skills; affects
   the amount of mana for bards and all mages, as well as shadow knights

   WIS: affects the amount of mana the priest classes have, also rangers;
   also affects how quickly you can learn many skills if your wisdom is 
   higher than INT.

We were later told that DEX affects how fast you learn physical skills,
although I don't have the reference for that.

There is also an ancient rumor that WIS affects success rates at trade
skills.  If you fail, you get a (WIS/500) chance at a second roll.
