Really when you break camping for profit down it works like this:

Player "A" gets to a high enough level where he can kill MOB "B".
MOB "B" has an item/items/loot that in some context of the game are
valuable enough to Player "A" to motivate them to camp MOB "B" and thuse
beat out any other lower level player.

The solution proposed by me:

In an extension of the PK switch - When a player attempts to attack a
MOB, any MOB so far below the level of a player will be considered as
"not worth your time" to kill.

For example - I play a Grandmaster Warrior / 20th level Paladin and spot
an obviously lowly Orc.  I target the Orc and run up to attack it, but
get the message "Attacking such a lowly creature would be a waste of
your time and talent".

This leaves a problem - Player "A" can no longer save Player "B" because
he cannot attack a lower level MOB.  The solution to this problem is
easy and fun.  Any high level PC will exude a level of fear in any
nearby potential *threat* causing it to drop what it's doing and flee in
fear.

For example - Player B is being mauled by an Orc Shaman.  Player A runs
up to the site of the battle.  Since player A is much higher than the
orc shaman the orc turns tail and flees, because it understands it will
die very quickly.

In this manner higher level players can "escort" lower level players to
safety, while keeping them from camping MOB/Spawns that they obviously
shouldnt be  messing with.
