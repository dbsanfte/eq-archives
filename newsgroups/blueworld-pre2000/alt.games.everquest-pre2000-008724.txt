I built a 600E @ 800, GA-6VX-4X, PC133ram system a month back and have had
no trouble with it. It runs very stable. Plan on putting in a 800E when they
get cheap enough. Some are clocking them over 1100.
http://www.asetek.com/html/p3_bench.htm   The whole thing for long term use
is the correct motherboard.
http://www.tomshardware.com/mainboard/00q2/000410/index.html   Spend the
extra $30-$50 on the mobo you know will be upgradeable. Be sure to get pc133
memory. If your only gonna spend $1k I imagine you plan on using parts from
your old machine. In that case be sure the new mobo has ISA slots if your
old stuff is ISA. Some of the newer boards have only 1 or no slots. The new
Celeron chips will get you 900(about the same as 800 from a 600E) with pc100
on a sub $100 board.  http://www.firingsquad.com/hardware/p3vc2/default.asp
But the savings between that and the 600E and $130 mobo would just about get
you a new board when you'd be ready to upgrade again. Don't buy new pc100
memory. Get 133 and run it at 100 if you have to. If I hada do it over again
I may go with the celeron and the cheaper board. Just so I could play with a
new system in 6 months or a year instead of just putting in a new cpu.  Hope
this helps
