Ok, I went to eq.castersrealm.com and checked all of GZ's and Gordon's
pronouncements on the matter:
 
   Dexterity only affects Channelling in relation to raising your skill in
   it.  How quickly you raise your Channelling ability is determined by a
   combination of Dexterity and Intelligence.

   Dexterity does not affect your chance to actually recover casting.

   Dexterity does not figure into Dodge, Riposte, Block or Parry.

   Dexterity determines:
	How often a weapon will fire a random process (proc).
	Figures into critical hits for warriors.
	Figures into some of the rogue skills.

In summary, Dex does jack for casters but seems reasonably worthwhile 
for melee characters.
