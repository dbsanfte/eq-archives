Mallow wrote:
> 
> I am new to the jewelry crafting trade and have a fairly low skill in it
> (26) right now, and I spend all money and training credits working on it. My
> question is: Is it possible for a PC to make magical jewelry, like INT +2,
> or WIS +4, or anything like that? If so would I have to have a higher skill
> rating, or be at a certain level? Any help is appreciated.

You can make magical jewelry by using enchanted metal bars.  The
spells that turn ordinary metal bars into enchanted ones belongs
to the Enchanter class and can be purchased in Neriak:

Enchant Silver		level 8
Enchant Electrum	level 16
Enchant Gold		level 24
Enchant Platinum	level 34

If you are not an Enchanter, then you might be able to hire one to
enchant the metal bars for you.  However, the spells take rather a
lot of mana, so the cost might be high, and some Enchanters won't 
do it at all because they want to maintain a monopoly on the trade.

Approximate costs to enchant:

Silver		3-5 gp
Electrum	5-10 gp
Gold		1-3 pp
Platinum	5-25 pp

If you are interested in a chart of all the magical jewelry that 
can be made by players, try the jewelry chart at Casters' Realm.
They can't spell the word "jewelry", but apart from spelling the
site really is very good:  

Casters' Realm:    http://eq.castersrealm.com/
Jewelry Chart:     http://eq.castersrealm.com/misc/jewellery/jewel_ow.asp
