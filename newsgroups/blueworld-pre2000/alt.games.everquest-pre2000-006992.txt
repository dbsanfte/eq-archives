> > I have just made an Enchanter, and I was wondering if, at low levels,
> > jewelcraft could help get me a little extra cash...does it usually turn
> > a profit early on? or will I need to wait until I can enchant the metals
> > to make money?

If you always sell to players, track your success rate and always charge 
over your costs, you should be able to come out ahead.  However, your 
advancement in jewelcraft will be insanely slow.

> Well, platinum ruby veils, which cost the enchanter 250p in parts seems to
> sell for an outlandish 800p  (I won't pay more than double the material cost
> for anything made with a trade skill).

Platinum ruby veils top out at around a 35% success rate.  The material
cost is 246pp.  So the average cost to the jeweler to make one is around
700pp.  It is very easy for it to take 4 or 5 tries (or more).  If you 
find one for sale for less than 800pp, you are getting a pretty good deal.

The easier platinum items get up to around a 50% success rate, so I would
expect to pay at least twice cost (a minimum of 210pp) for any platinum
item.  The going rate for such things as platinum jasper rings is about 
225pp each on E'Ci.
