I played a mage up over 50 and I cannot think of a time when I was not
looking for a group.

Being able to solo is a convenience and a poor second choice to being
in a class that finds it easy to get a group.  The only really major
exception I can think of is camping the AC, where there is a real
benefit to solo play.

For most purposes, although I agree that mage and necro are strong
classes, they have a real hard time getting groups and are constantly
listening to ignorant bullshit and incorrect "instructions" on how to
use their pets (or being forbidden to use their pets) by people who do
not know who to play the classes.  That is why they solo.  Not because
they can't be bothered, and not because they aren't good in groups.
However, they are not as attractive to groups as some other classes
such as cleric, enchanter, warrior, and shaman, even if the group
understands the class.

I think the same is true for most druids.  Necros generally represent
the class that plays intentionally solo.

Mage has also been so severely nerfed post 50 that I would not advise
anyone to start one.  Start a necro instead.
