I saw ordered loots done all the time in Unrest (right up to
dwarven boots).  Generally it'd work where each person would
take a turn looting and if you got lucky you got lucky.  New
group members would be inserted into the order at the end of
the current rotation (so each existing member got another
loot before th enew guy).  If you got the boots then you
generally bowed out of future loots.

In Lower Guk I tend to find it adds a level of complication.
Generally you have two lists: commons and rares.  Whenever
an item drops each person who hasn't got an item (common list
if its a common item and rare list if its a rare item) can
put their hand up for it.  Those who want it generally /random
for it.  Once you get a common item you can't get another one
until everyone else does.  Same for rare items.

Its a bit more convoluted but imho its pretty fair.
