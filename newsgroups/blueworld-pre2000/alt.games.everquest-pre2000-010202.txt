I agree, this is the principle set, but I use report health
quite often.  Target the same mob as your pet, and
tap report health occaisionally to keep track of how
your pet is doing compared to the target.  If he has
lots of hits and the target few, let him deal with it.
If his %hp is going down faster than the Mob, then
you are going to have to help out.

Much handier than switching back to him as your
target, then back to his target.

>
> You should have all of these (or the equivalents, ie /pet back off = /pet
> as you were) hotkeyed.
>
> Very rarely use /pet get lost (reclaim energy is better)
>

I disagree, reclaim takes up a slot, or you have to
mem it, and then get rid of it afterwards.  The mana
you get back is minimal.

There are times when your pet will not be able to hear you,
but at those times, reclaim will be out of range almost
certianly.  Here's a "stupid pet trick" to use when that
happens.  Cast invisibility on yourself.  Your pet will
kill itself immediately.

>

Yep, while he will have an uncanny ability to get back to his guard
spot, even if he chases a runner.  I've had him go up three flights in
High Keep, and return more smoothly than I could.  Try to get him
down the stairs there using /pet follow me some time.

> I never use the taunt commands either.

/pet no taunt is useful if you have a damage shielded tank or otherwise
don't want your pet getting beat on.  Rare circumstance, I agree, and
surely no need to put it on a social key.

> > 2) If you give your pet a weapon, can you get it back? Or should you only
> > give him summoned weapons?
>
> nope, yep (except at lower levels where rusty halberds and such might be

At lower levels, he will not be able to hit certain creatures (notably
Will o Wisps) without a magic weapon, use the one you can summon
with the best damage.  If you can afford it (and it isn't all that
expensive)
weapons with high damages will increase damage at lower levels too.

Between 12th and 24th, giving pets weapons is strictly for looks.
At 24th, a pet will weild two weapons.  Rumor has it this will make
him dual weild, doing, I suppose, twice as much damage, by hitting twice
as often.  I can't say that I've noticed a marked improvement, but
what the heck, it does look cool.  In particular, I handed him a short
sword and a mace (both rusty loots that we were destroying as too
heavy for the value) and got to see him do both crushing and peircing
(or whatever the two disperate sorts of damage are) proving that he
was using both.
