I think the list system is pretty awful.  Yes, it is fair in 
some sense, but it is also grossly unfair in others.  Basically
it locks out prime time players from choice hunting locations.

The idea of hunters taking numbers to get a turn at killing an
enemy also violates the game fiction.  I don't think Verant would
want to encourage a system like that in any way.  Instead, they
work to make the system unnecessary, by putting up new servers 
and by creating more nice items to acquire.

For now, the old technology of pencil and paper should suffice 
for most list taking needs.
