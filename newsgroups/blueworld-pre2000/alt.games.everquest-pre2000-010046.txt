My take on it is, that if you beg me for money or items, I'll give you some
(relatively) minor task for the reward you want.  For example, someone asking me
for money is usually told that I buy stacks of items from some common newbie
critter (bat wings, bone chips, wasp wings, etc) for 2 or 3 pp each, but *only*
full stacks of 20.  Or I'll Scavenger Hunt them, asking for several odd, but
fairly common items from the same newbie critters.  (Four Beetle eyes, six rat
wiskers, and a rusty sword.)  The idea is, if they aren't just beggar scum, what
I'm paying for these odd trinkets is still *way* more than they could earn from
just killing the same critter.  Plus, sometimes I actually do want the items,
especially batwings and bone chips.  (My Magician feels it is their duty to act
as the party supply manager.  You need it?  I've got it in my backpack
somewhere.  :)  Also, since my Magician is a Dark Elf, I'll occaisionally reward
someone who will either buy some item for me, or sell my random garbage.

Of course, I've very rarely actually had to worry about paying someone.  It's
amazing how many of the beggars just disappear when there may be actual work
involved.
