>Instead of using the mana to make ranger's job (actual damage dealing)
>easier, use the mana to burn the mobs down fast (ie let the casters do
>the damage).
>
>Everyone casts his/her best nuke ONCE and that can pratically bring the
>mob down to 30% hp's.
>2 wizards, 1 druid, 1 enchatner, 1 cleric.
>The wizard can do around 175 dmg each (I'm a wizard), and assuming the
>druid/chanter/cleric can nuke for 90 dmg on average each, that's 620 dmg
>total. (I remembered a low 20 druid can do 100ish)
>The ranger can do nothing much more than taking the beating.
>
>This tactic will work as long as there aren't too many mobs in a pull (1
>or 2) because then you can safe mana by not mezzing, rooting, stunning.
>Let both mobs beat on the ranger, but you can burn them down fast, so
>ideally you won't have to tank too long. This also save mana on healing
>since the mob's dieing fast. (actually single target mez is good to use
>on the 2nd mob, but if everyone including chanter and cleric nukes at the
>same time you will be amazed how fast you can bring the mob down.)
>
>But if unfutunately there are more than 3 mobs, you will have to switch
>back to AEmez because 3 mobs can beat down the ranger too fast even if
>the casters are doing full mana burn.
>
>btw being a wizard I can tell you at lvl 25, stunning costs way too much
>mana for its use. It is around 75mana/(30dmg+ ranger's dmg) and the lvl
>24 nuke is about 130mana/175dmg. I realize this when I fought at outside
>paw. I stun the mob, but the tanks (yes, several of them) fail to bring
>the mob down at a satisfying rate, while my biggest nuke can take away
>30% of the mob's hp's. Thus I gave up stunning during that session.
