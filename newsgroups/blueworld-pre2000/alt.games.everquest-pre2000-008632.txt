It's 30%, and 40%.

You can't actually solo anything from 30% (past level 35, at least; I forget
if it's easier before then, but probably not), but you can do very well from
half or 60%. (Another tip: get a bunch of +sta gear, and take all your HP
gear off when you bandage. You'll be at or over 3 bubs with the right kit.)

Once you bandage, take on low-med blues near a zone line so you can run if
needed. You're a warrior, so you don't start the death march until you're
under 100 HP; make use of that.

(For soloing, there are also certain weapons that are a must have at the
start of a fight. Look for a Kisch Der Pavz; it's no longer no drop. There
aren't many around, but they're probably cheap, and the proc is *perfect*
for someone who can't stop a runner.)
