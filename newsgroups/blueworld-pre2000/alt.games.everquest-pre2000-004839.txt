I have my doubts on all this: I have somewhere read an article on the 
effect of stats and it seems that Verant has taken a totally braindamaged 
attitude to STATS. Beside the minor direct effects stats have (STR for 
carrying, AGI for running speed, STA for the yellow bar) the stat values 
are primaruly used to determine increase in SKILL values. So buffing DEX 
for a single fight might have no effect whatsoever. Buffing it for ALL 
fights it will be added to all 'does my weapon skill increase?' rolls and 
make you hit your cap faster.

: >
: >Short answer - how much weight you can carry before encumberance.

: >Long answer - Strength below 75 hurts your damage.  Strength over 75
: >increases your average damage by a tiny amount.  A very tiny amount.  As an
: >example, the level 49 Shaman buff "Strength" adds 67 Str - much more than
: >you could realistically get from items - it will only offer an increase in
: >*average* damage of around 0.25%.
: >
: >A lot of people see the increase in *maximum* damage from strength and
: >wrongly presume that they are that much better.  But the distribution of
: >damage is non-normal - there is no Bell Curve, just a single peak at the
: >"Magic Number" (2 times weapon base damage plus (level-25)/3) and an even
: >distribution at all other points.
: >
: >Offload the Str, Dex and Agil items.  Stam will give you some hitpoints
: >(but only really at 50 is it noticeable).  There is also some evidence to
: >support maximising Armour Class (through AC on items).
