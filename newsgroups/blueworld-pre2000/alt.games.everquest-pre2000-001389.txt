But given this world we are talking about to preserve the NPC balance
we would have to assume they are reborn when they die as are we. Thus
it makes sense that Blackburrow would never be emptied because when
you kill a gnoll he is resurrected somewhere in its depths.  Imagine
the effect if the resurrect sight for Qeynos was locked in say
Kithicor forrest or even the Northern Karanas. In that case the gnolls
could theoretically win and take over qeynos because of the defenders
logistical problems.  

What I would like to see is some sort of artifact that controls
respawn in say Blackburrow. If you can get to this artifact and
destroy it then it would be possible to kill every gnoll in
blackburrow while the artifact regenerates (which would take at least
an hour depending on how efficient the mop up teams were) Thus players
could actually invade and take over areas, perhaps with the goal of
looting the treasury for a little while at least. This should be a
fairly rare and costly occurrence though, and there should be the
potential for the same to occur to any player run city as well.
