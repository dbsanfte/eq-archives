Oh, I really gotta disagree about the game working better.  Of course, it's
true that the rep system would work better, but the whole game?
 With only one slot/shard, players would be extremely frustrated with
the skill/stat caps.  Most players can get a decent conection to only 2 or
3 shards, so they would be limited to playing only 2-3 characters.  Now,
I don't know about you, but I like to be able to go beat on a mountain for
a while, then run out and thrash a elemental or two, then a day or two
later, I get an itch to wander the countryside searching for wild reagents
to mix some potions with, then maybe slip on some armor and beat on
some ettins, orcs, and gargoyles (oh my!)

Now, with only 3 shards to chose from -- and the associated 3 characters --
this would be  . . . . impractical at best.


I assume you mean that the game would work better if everybody didn't have
a smith/tailor/alchemist 'mule'.  The solution for this is:
(warning, smiths won't like this)
in The Real World(tm) it takes a blacksmith at least a day to make a single
sword.  It also takes a tailor at least a day to make a formal dress.  Now,
the
best thing you could do to improve the game (in my not-so-humble opinion)
is to make NPC-supplied armor, weapons, clothing, etc.. cost an amount more
in-line with the items true cost *and* drastically increase the time it takes
to
craft armor, weapons and clothing.  Make it so that a craftsman continues
'working' on an item even while logged-off.  Of course, he wouldn't really
'continue working', he would simply be 'out of the game' untill he finishes
the task or the player logs back in.
Now, what about failure at the smithing (for example)?  Yes, it would suck
massive twinkies to start smithing the item, only to find that 4 hours later,
the
skill check failed.  No problem, since the only thing that failure really
does
is to increase the number of ingots it takes to make the item, have the
character
continue attempting to make the item untill he succeeds.  The player could
specify the maximum number of ingots to waste in the crafting, and if that
maximum number is reached, then the character stops trying.
