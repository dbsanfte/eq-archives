I understand that many folks on the pvp servers use a 1-hour
rule for gear: they wear nothing that they can not replace with
about an hour's work.

I would look for a thick leather apron (ac 8), 20 plat on E'ci
but I was given one and turned down an offer of another.

Banded generally has better ac than black iron, and weighs
less.  I prefer it.  The only reason to wear black iron is the
look, or to punch/slam/kick magic critters.

You need some magical boots, BI are the cheapest.

If you think you can afford to replace them, Ivy bracers are 90
plat for the gem, plus a ring from Dagnors and a rune from
Everfrost.  A group of 3 low-20s can take Redwind (one should
be a healer) and 2 people low 20s can take a tidal lord.

You should be working on your 2-handed skills as well.  Bone
claymore is a good ranger weapon because the kitty feet cancel
out the agility penalty.

Also consider trading in the strength ear rings for ac ear
rings: if you can do this and still keep str over 100 then do
it. 

Good luck with it
