I agree with all of the above except in part for the last paragraph
which I have found to be true only if one is thoughtless and uses
repeated AE nukes. The way I handle such is to put one AE nuke in to the
fight at the point the puller stops to fight which at my level does
fifty or so damage to all of them. Then I send my pet in a fire pet in a
group heavy on tanks because it does more damage or an earth pet in a
group light on tanks because it can take more of a hammering and will
root. I then switch to single target nukes and change targets whenever
the mob hits about a quarter health. I might finish off a group of badly
wounded mobs with a final AE nuke if I have the Manna and the tanks and
my pet need the help or I any help or I may just sit and meditate and
let them finish up. I however always monitor my pets health and am ready
to heal it as necessary.

I would worn people against no range AE nukes what Jason says about AE
nukes is always true of these, and I died a couple of times or made
inglorious exits via gate before I learned that the hard way.
