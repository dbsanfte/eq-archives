I disagree.  I play a DE Cleric that worships Innoruk.  I speak only in DE
or
Dark Speech and I only converse with other DEs or our slaves (Trolls and
Ogres).  I dream of one day invading Rivervale and hunting halfling children
because I think this would appropriately spread the message of "hate" to
an all too happy race.  I do not buff or heal people, I do not offer aid of
any
sort and frequently insult people who ask (of course most dont understand
because I speak in DE).  However, I am not a "jerk" in game.  I dont kill
steal, I dont train people, and I never ask for anything.  I die frequently
as
I would rather die than be aided by one of the feeble races of Norath, and
have stopped fighting and turned to berate someone who healed me for
their interference while the Mob pounded on my back.  Still, I think if you
are
going to play a DE cleric, you should go all the way.  If anyone seems
insulted or actually upset (and this is rare) I send them a tell and explain
that
I am roleplaying.  Because I never break character, this never seems to
offend.  Generally, I group with the same friends who all started evild to
roleplay and we have great fun.  Often we add a player or 2 to our group who
does not generally roleplay, and within minutes they start roleplaying with
us and start enjoying the taste of playing an evil race.  I think there is
room
in EQ to roleplay and have fun with it, you just need to stay focused on why
you are playing that character and why you are doing what you do.
