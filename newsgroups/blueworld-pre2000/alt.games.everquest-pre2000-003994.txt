Well player-run UO quests vary greatly in scope and quality, but all
of them are limited by the tools which players have to them, and the
willingness of participants to suspend disbelief, knowing that it's
"just players" who are running things.

Other than that, there are no real quests in UO, other than
self-imposed ones, such as character development. A notable exception
are the "escort quests," which aren't very interesting, but are very
profitable.

EQ has many quests, but almost all of them suck, being simple
gopher/"kill this monster" type quests that either

- give an awful reward, and/or
- take far too long to do

I mean, any check for complaints about quests in the EQ newsgroup will
reveal this in spades.

From what I understand, AC's quests are quite similar to EQ's - with
the same complaints applying. I hope I'm wrong, though.

I don't expect any of this to change soon, for any game. It's *hard*
to make game-coded quests that are:

- Fun/interesting
- Properly rewarding
- Properly challenging
- NOT one-shot deals
- NOT just for one (type of) person
