How does being grouped with a Necro limit choices?  To me, it actually makes a
few areas stand out like beacons.  There is an undead camp in the north eastern
corner of West Commonlands, which occaisionally has Ghouls.  At level 14, they'd
be a really tough fight, but good experience.  There's also Befallen, which is
mostly Undead, with a few Necromancers thrown in.  The two problems here, are
nasty undead trains (not as huge as in Blackburrow, but much more lethal. 
Gnolls can't give you rabies.) and a few "live" Necromancers who tend to run
away well before they're at risk of actually dieing.  I don't know if Clerics
get a normal version of Root, and I know Necros don't for a long time.  However,
Ward Summoned goes a long way in Befallen, and Clerics are still appreciated in
groups here for their ability to remove disease.  (Also, Druids seem to hate the
place, since it is both underground, and swarming with creatures they have no
advantage over.)

Also, once you get to level 20, you might consider the estate of Unrest, on
Faydwer.  It's also crawling with undead, and has a few decent items that you
could trade for other things if you happen to get them.  The main problem with
this, is that I don't think clerics get any normal form of Invisibility.  You
can just jump off the boat and avoid the guards at the docks (I'd suggest
jumping after the boat passes port, rather then as it's comming in.  That side
has a large strip of uninhabited beach you could Bind on, and there are a lot
fewer guards between you and Dagnor's Cauldron.)  However, it'll be kind of a
problem when you need to leave again, since there is a hostile Dwarf merchant at
the base of the docks.  While you can swim in and avoid the other guards and
merchants, that Dwarf will get you if you're not invisible.

Lavastorm is also a good place to hunt, with decent treasure.  I believe Clerics
get Ward Summoned, so you can do a lot of damage if you want to (a lot of the
critters here are summoned beings.)  The nice thing about Lavastorm is the
relatively huge amount of wandering spawn.  As long as you are willing to just
pick a spot along the zone edge, rather than cowering near the gates of Najena
or the valley to Nektulos, you won't have to compete with other groups for
spawn.  Just be aware, the terrain is *extremely* hazardous.  Auto-running in
here will kill you, as will SoW if you aren't careful.  If you don't fall into a
lava pit, you'll go flying off into a ravine or end up jumping off one of the
many spires of broken rock.  However, if you aren't a speed freak, and slow down
when you cross the top of a hill, you shouldn't have too many problems.  I've
broght two characters from level 14 to almost level 20 there, and I've never
gotten killed by the zone itself.  Just be careful where you rest, because the
Imps and Elementals are very aggressive, and will group up as well.  Imps,
Elementals, and Lava Crawlers are all summoned beings, but the Crawlers are
non-aggresive.  They look like snakes made out of greenish-gray stone, and they
"proc" a fire-based DD.  Imps and Fire Elementals have a built in damage shield,
but you can remove it with Cancel Magic, if you have it.  The Imps also have a
fire-DD attack, so they're probably the most dangerous thing in the zone if you
don't have the magic to handle them properly.  Also, all the summoned beings are
magic only to-hit, so if you don't have magic weapons, you may want to go
elsewhere.
