Err, the fact is, you *DON'T* have the "right" to play the game. A
game belongs to the company that puts it out. Even the copy of the
software you own still belongs to them. You are buying permission to
use it. Under the terms of the real owners of the software. Under
copyright laws, they have the right to change those terms as they see
fit when they see fit (within reason, of course.). The Bill of Rights
does not now and will not ever apply to your imagined "right" to play
EQ. Just like many other things in life, it is a privilege, not a
right.
