I can agree with that. But, there is nothing about seeking advancement
or ownership within the game that is inherently better as PvP than non.
The reason I think so many players turn to PvP in UO as a ladder of
advancement is b/c it has been relatively easy to advance up the other
ladders within the game.  By making advancement through other ladders
more challenging, thereby taking longer, you could push back the
envitable drive towards what should be the most difficult ladder, that
of PvP.  By the same token, adding new ladders of advancement (i.e. new
skills, trades, spells, etc etc) will further extend the retention rates
of these acheivers.  (I know, I know, easier said than done.)

Also, since the social types are not interested in advancement per se,
allowing for more ways they can extert ownership should extend their
viability as well, irregardless of the PvP nature..  Rentention rates
seem more directly linked to the continuing development of the gameworld
with new ladders and ownership options than it does PvP.

IMHO, the reason it is not a resolved dilemma is because you cannot
address it without knowing a lot more about the situation that is
stated.  Did the two players know each other?  What was the reason
behind the attack?  What effect does death have on a player?  All we
know from this is that a pacifist player was attacked and killed by an
aggressive player, and that the pacifist player will live again.  You
can't resolve such a dilema in such a vacuum of ambiguity.

But a couple of quick ideas (bearing in mind that we no nothing about
the capabilities of said gameworld).

1.  Marian could depend upon the local guards/miltia to find an punish
her attacker, possibly with recompense to her.
2.  Marian herself could hire a player guard, who also acts as an
assistant, or an assasin to kill her attacker, or a thief to steal back
her goods..
3.  Marian may join the local tailor guild, who looks out for the safety
of its members - perhaps through a safe working environment.
4.  Marian may institue some environmental safeguards- locked doors,
peepholes, passwords, disguises, stay in remote areas, etc etc
5.  Marian could purchase some form of magical safeguard - rune of
recall, gas trap, invisibility ring, etc etc
6.  If there is no reasonable way for Marian to be protected, she may
elect to stay dead (i.e. leave the gameworld).

Protecting marian doesn;t not have to be an absolute.  The trick is
decided what is reasonable for her protection. After all, some
expressions of conflict SHOULD be eliminated (i.e. Boffo kills Marian in
game because in real life she is a woman/black/jew).
