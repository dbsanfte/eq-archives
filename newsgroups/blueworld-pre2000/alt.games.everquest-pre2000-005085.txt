I generally give for free, both bind and SoW (and I usually enhance the 
SoW with Spirit of Cat, too). I am indifferent to what medium people use 
(/ooc, /shout, /tell), although I have to admit it depends on what screen 
I am on because on one the red and on the other the green is easier to 
read =P

However I react very strongly to phrasing. After all that is the only 
thing distinguishing one being from another in EQ (unless you want to 
play out Race hate etc.). Thus I will not react kindly to "Need sow", 
which to me reads "Come on you fucking lazy bastards who can sow, I 
am here, come and find me, sow me ... and I won't even bother to say 
thanks), whereas a "Could someone please bind me at South Gate" or "Could 
I donate some pp to a kind soul to bind me at South Gate" will ususally 
make me go out of my way to get to them (usually I charge for THAT) and 
bind/sow/buff for free.

However everyone is different and I think you can't be out of line any 
way you do it. I have always thought it ridiculous how some players won't 
buff without payment ... but hey it's their life/enjoyment so let them 
play the way they like nad play the way you do. If someone bothers you 
(yes Neolis I am still thinking of you), well, start ignoring them. 

Just my $.02

  Rudolf
