Rubbish, Everquest has a depth that UO can only dream about.  here is an
example which is also a big spoiler :-

My Shaman was exploring Highhold Pass, an orc and gnoll infested town in the
mountains mid-way between Qeynos and Freeport.  I had noticed an underwater
entrance and decided to equip Enduring Breath so my Shaman could explore it.

The entrance I'd seen only linked the two obvious water areas in the pass
but while down there I noticed another smaller hole, going through I came
out in a hidden area filled with Smugglers.

Not wanting to go visible I went back and met up with a Rogue friend of mine
and we both returned.  The smugglers were dubious to me but didn't attack
and as we looked round we eventually discovered a High Keep guard amongst
the smugglers.

By rights, he should be fighting the scourge but seemed to be there as a
leadership figure.  Suddenly things made sense.  When fighting in High Keep,
returning goblin ears to the Captain raised faction with the Keep guards but
also the Corrupt Qeynos Guards and Freeport Militia (who are also rumoured
to be corrupt).

My Rogue friend also said he had heard in his guild that the Freeport
Militia's corruption involved bootleg Blackburrow Stout.  Now this means,
that the Corrupt Qeynos guards and the Freeport Militia are involved in
smuggling Stout via High Keep.

But it also means that all three human guards could be in league with the
gnolls!

All wonderful underlying plot which I intend to try to find out more about,
as a Barbarian we aren't involved in the Humie punies schemes (I hope!) so
it will be great fun to shed more light on what is going on.

Thats depth, thats sub-plot.  Absolutely brilliant.
