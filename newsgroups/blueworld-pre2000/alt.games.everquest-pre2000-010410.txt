Personally I think Qeynos blows.  In all respects.  But lets just
address the newbie zone issue.

North Qeynos blows.  And I mean blows with a capital B.  Hard.
Have you ever seen a server with 2000+ people with 70+ people in
that small newbie area?  I have.  It just can't cope with it.

Same goes for QHills.  For its level range it simply can't support
the population.

Then its onto BB which is train central and is ultra crowded
as well.

Hell even if you trek over to Everfrost it can get pretty crowded.

Now compare this to Freeport.

Freeport has two newbie zones (West and East Freeport).  On top
of those you can hunt at the edges of North Ro and East Commonlands.
I have never seen the sum of these areas crowded (East Freeport
in particular).

East Commons ain't that great for 5-10 but its OK and fairly large.
It does get crowded but usually only at the very peak times.

If you're somewhat game you can journey to South Ro and have the
best level 6-15 zone in the game.  Plenty to kill.  No competition
for it.  Clear delineation of monster levels (like theres nothing
really tough in the grass area unless someone trains it on you).

And nowadays with everyone camping the AC, SGs aren't really a
big issue.  At 10+ you can go and hunt at the 2 derv camps when
the Oasis gets really crowded.  I recommend going to the Oasis
if its not too packed because it really is a good zone (people
don't go there for no reason).  Hunting dervs in SRo is fine
but dry bone skeles can ruin your day until you're in a level
15 or so group.

You should be able to get to level 12 easily on the orcs though
(there are *seven* camps of them) and you can find multiple
spots with 1, 2 or 3 spawns so if you want to solo or go in
a small group you will find things to kill.  No problem.

I can't say enough good things about South Ro really.  Combined
with Innothule Swamp it makes the troll starting area the
best starting area for evil races (excluding iksar).

By the mid to late teens the Karanas start becoming really
good (and at 20-30 South Karana is excellent) but prior to
that the western side of Antonica really has nothing to offer
(imho).  At least you can say that North Qeynos and Qeynos
Hills is better than Steamfont (before level 10) but thats
about it.
