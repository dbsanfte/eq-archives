At the risk of steping into something messy here...

I have experienced a restore myself. I accidentally deleted my character
(DOH!) and recreated the character and then did absolutly nothing but
politely petition about this. Poof!  I was kicked out and my character
reappeared as it was approx 24 hours previously. I lost some items I had
gained (in the middle of a quest) but it was far better than starting
from scratch.

I must point out that because I recreated a character with the same
race/class/name and did absolutely nothing with the character (didn't
even move), I received no "xxxx saved" messages. Also it was the next
day before I recreated the character and petitioned. Thus the "24 hours"
is very subjective and may actually been from some number of "saves"
previously.

I can understand Verant's reluctance to perform this on a regular basis
tho. What if you give all your plane equipment to another character (a
twink) and then petition for a restore. Bang, a quick way to duplicate
all of your equipment!  Very prone to abuse.
