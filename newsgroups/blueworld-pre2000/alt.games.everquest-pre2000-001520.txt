	Another Butcherblock resident, the Krag Elder, which is a species of
Aviak.  I had heard that killing these things was not good for faction

of them at this point; this was the only one that was armed.  It's a shame
these are an enemy race, as I think they're quite majestic.

http://members.home.net/hefferman/EbDrake.jpg

fought without a chance to rest in-between.  I did win...and after healing
about one bubble's worth I got jumped by an L10 Skeleton AND an L11 Large
Plague Rat, and still somehow managed to survive with a sliver of hit points
left.
