Bok's don't come close to level 50 (they top out at level 46).  Even
the guks top out at level 49.  Imps top out at level 48.  And those
LGuk froggies are horribly under powered for their cons.  The only
non-fear/hate mob that's level 50 without resorting to insane critters
like trainers and such, are the Efreeti lord (a caster so will have
lower hit points but still about 10khps), Dino in OOT (a warrior type
and have about 15khps), Dark Assassin (rogue type, have about 14khps),
various fire/ice giants (all over 12khps--except for the casters who
have about 10khps).

>Unforunately, there's a rather limited data set for stuff of that
>level pre-Kunark.

Right.  Which is why many claim that Kunark stuff are harder.
However, if you compare Kunark mobs of similar levels to Norrath mobs,
they are within spitting distance.  The problem is that in Kunark,
there are plenty of mobs that are beyond what's comparable to Norrath
mobs.

Toughest insta-kill mob in Norrath outdoors is probably a toss up
between giants, griffons and spectres (excluding the NPC guards).

Compare to mountain giant craigs--exactly the same level as hill
giants, exactly the same hit points and the same melee damage per hit.
Infact, other than graphics (and the tendency to run when they get
hurt), identical to hill giants.

Of course, in Kunark the toughest insta-kills are considerably tougher
than hill giants.  In dreadlands, the toughest instakill not including
the partriarch or the dreaded widow (both considered named and
relatively rare spawns) is the ravishing drolvarg--level 44 mob.  Has
about 4500hps and can drop kick a hill giant's butt anytime of the day
and twice on weekends.

Toughest insta-kill in burning woods--wurms (again not including the
relatively rare named mobs), range up to level 47 with upto 8900hps,
pops you for over 140 each and completely immune to fire based spells.
