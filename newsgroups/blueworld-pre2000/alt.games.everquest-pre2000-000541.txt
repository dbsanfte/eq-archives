Yes, but I'm not dealing with specifics, but rather a class argument. 
I'm not saying that "GOD'A' IS ALL POWERFULL", but rather that any god
that is represented AS all-powerfull and flawless must posess (or
rather, not possess) certain qualities.

To be a supriem being, to be perfect, one must be without flaws.  God as
represented by most mainstream religions has a number of flaws ranging
from petty jealosy ('Worship me or go to hell!') to malicious inent
('Kill all the 1st born sons in this town!') both of which imply
emotions, and therefore imperfection.
