	Make sure you save the Greater Lightstones and run them to the Gypsies in
North Karanas.  Hail them and find the once that gives out the Concordance of
Research in exchange for Greater Lightstones.  They're containers, so you'll
need a top-level slot to hold them, and I believe they're Lore as well so
you'll have to do this one at a time, but get one, then go sell it to the
Gypsy merchants.  With good CHA it's worth about 9pp, over double what you'll
get for a GLS from a merchant.
