Add story telling NPC's in some areas... for example:

Young ogre walks up to the floating skull altar (Skull of Murdunk?) in Oggok
and double clicks on it.

"Hear me my children.... I was in-life Murdunk, last High Priest of the
Empire, and am the Servant of Zek, even in death..."

"Listen to the Word of Zek..."

"And in those days there were two youths who followed after the Host. The
wise youth listened to all that was said of the Way of Zek, and the spirit
of Zek was in him. The foolish youth looked upon the glory of the Host, the
fine raiment upon them and the fell and terrible weapons in their hands. He
saw the fear and wealth that victory had brought to the Host and was filled
with greed, and Zek looked not upon him."

"The wise youth came unto Grimhand and falling to his knee cried out unto
him saying, 'I am not worthy that I should touch the hem of thy cloak, but
show unto me the Way of Zek, that I might serve him in His Holy War!'"

"Grimhand looked upon the youth, and seeing his ernest and devout desire to
walk the Way, said 'Rise, go forth into the Host and be one with them, for
Zek has seen your heart, and you shall not be found wanting.' And he gave
unto the youth a Sword of finest steel, and Armour of the leather of bears,
and into his hand put a purse with fourty pieces of gold. And the youth went
among the Host singing and praiseing The Warlord."

"Then the foolish youth came unto Grimhand and said, 'Give unto me a Sword
of steel and armour for my back and a purse of gold to my hand, as you have
done to the one who came before me. You are rich and have enough, and to
spare.'"

"Hearing this Grimhand flew into a rage and cried out, "Have you no hands to
crush, no feet to kick, no teeth to rend? The beasts of the field are fit
meat for the likes of thee!" And grasping a scourge in his hand he smote the
foolish youth and drove him from out of the host into the wild places."

"And the General proclamed unto the host, 'Let it be know in all the lands,
that he who seeks to know the Way shall find it, and comrads and brethren
besides. The Beggar shall find only scorn, and the whip, for the Worlord
shall turn his face from him.'"

"Heed the Word of Zek..."

Now THAT would be a vehicle for some interesting storytelling and background
info.  You could periodically rotate through different tales, or even give
out dynamic quest information.

Just a thought.
