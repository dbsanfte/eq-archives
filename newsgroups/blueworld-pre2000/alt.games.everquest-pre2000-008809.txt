   Rogue type?  Okay...

   You *cannot* solo in EQ.  Oh, you'll get away with soloing for a few 
levels, but the game is designed so that eventually you *must* group and
be a party player.  Not for the lone wolf personality.  Also the 'Rogue'
class in EQ is woefully inadequate, being more of a weak melee fighter with
a few extra tricks than anything else.  There are essentially no traps,
locks, or anything else for a rogue/thief.  The graphics in EQ are lovely;
the gameplay and game design is inferior, especially for someone determined
to be self-sufficient.

   You can solo well in AC.  The 'thief' type character has things to do in
AC, with several areas requiring lockpicking and loot often appearing in
locked chests.  The lone wolf can be self-sufficient here, learning as much
as he needs to in whatever fields he wishes to study.  The drawback is that
you pretty much *have* to be self-sufficient, as the grouping interface is
vestigal at best, and there is often neither reason nor benefit to join
other adventurers in a fellowship.  The other big drawback to AC is poor
graphics, but I find the gameplay more than makes up for that.

   Never played UO.  I hear it's similar to AC in spirit.
