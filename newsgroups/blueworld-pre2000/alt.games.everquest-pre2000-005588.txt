> Does this setup still give you rear panning?  I guess with the
> receiver setup you have, you only really have one viable input to it.

Yes, with the settings above I do get rear panning surprisingly enough. You
can stand at any stationary sound source, spin around slowly and it will pan
to the rear when the source is directly behind you. This happens on other
sounds too, just easier to test out with a stationary source. Another trick
to get this emphasized is to crank up the volume on the rear speakers a bit.
It's like having a quad set up with the additional center speaker helping
carry the sound as well and my speakers are positioned in a way that the
center is straight ahead, the towers are actually to my forward right and
left, and the rears are to my rear left and right. Most surround setups
position the speakers with the center and towers straight ahead and then the
rears as I have them. So in essence it's a quad with extras, the ability to
use surround when the game/mood strikes, and all the features of a reciever
as well. I have an ac-3 so no sub yet but that is going to be added soon as
well.
