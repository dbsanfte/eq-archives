If you can hold off until fall, I would. I built my two main desktops less
than a year ago -- at a time when uTNT2 and P3/600 were the tops. And I can
afford to upgrade one of them now, but I'm not yet. Here's why ...

Processors -- P3/850 (100) and P3/866 (133) are both quite nice and fast;
Athlon 1Ghz rocks about the same (better than 866 in some places, worse in
others). I prefer Intel only 'cause I've been with them since 8088 days, and
most game code, if it leverages special processor instuctions, usually does
Intel *first*.

Chipsets -- I'm not up on the Athlon side, but Intel's 820 is ok, but not
incredible -- a small step up from BX. The Apollo chipset looks different
but equivalent (like processors -- better in some areas, worse in others).

Currently, it seems AGP 4x Fast Writes aren't being used well -- many
reports seem to indicate that turning Fast Writes off IMPROVES system
performance ... meaning somewhere between chipset, graphics and software,
there needs to be improvement.

Graphics -- I still run Ultra TNT2. GeForce 256 DDR and GeForce2 are both
small steps up, but I don't play as much Unreal Tournament (or other 3D
shooters) as I used to, and that's where the real payoff is in upgrading
(like maybe 20-40% over my uTNT2). VooDoo5 looks average, and not a huge
breakthru. Any of the three (G, G2, V5) would probably increase my EQ
framerate 5-10%. That's not worth $250 - $500 to me.

Come fall, I expect the wide release of the p3/1000 to drive the 800-866
prices down (1GHz only shipping in OEM pre-built machines currently) and the
graphics card wars to heat up more -- also driving prices down. And I'm
hoping the faster RAM required for the really fast processors [to see peak
performance] will fall. 128MB PC800 memory is still what, $750 or so?   =P
My 256MB of PC100 cost me less than half that. Then there's DDR coming too
... <grin>.

These are my opinions; your mileage may vary.
