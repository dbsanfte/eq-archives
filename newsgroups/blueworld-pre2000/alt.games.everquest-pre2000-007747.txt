Medium ringmail drops in high-hold pass.  I would suggest
either killing orcs yourself or heading up there and
/auctioning that you are buying ringmail.  it is heavy, and as
long as you offer more than the merchants you should get some
sellers.

Banded is lighter than ringmail and has better ac.  Again, make
an /auction hotkey and just press it every 10 minutes
(especially in Qeynos proper and the Qeynos hills) It usually
sells for 1 plat / ac (more for cloak, less for other parts)
and you can get a discount if you buy a full suit at once (I
paid 85 or 90 plat for mine - forget the exact price - for 104
ac)

You will have an easier time buying banded in the Freeport
area, but might find a seller in Qeynos.  I have sold some
random bits and pieces of Banded there.
