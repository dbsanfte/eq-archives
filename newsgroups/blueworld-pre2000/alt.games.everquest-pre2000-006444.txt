Here is a random thought:

Wizards do damage very quickly, teleport and are fragile.  That
is their schtick.

They should not be buffing, de-buffing, summoning, DoTing or
entering melee.

But resists are not much fun (and Brad or Gordon has already
stated that Wizards are expected to depend on group members for
debuff as well as for clarity.)

Here is a random thought for a wizard spell line:
1-shot procs.

Wizard holds an weapon in his hand (i.e. on his cursor) and
casts the spell.  The item now gets 1 charge of a DD proc -
used when the weapon hits.  

It is not a buff, not a summons, but does fit in with the
notion of wizards being connected to the elements and using
their brains to re-shape the world around them.

and it gives them something group-friendly to do, and makes
them feel less guilty about standing around through most fights
waiting for runners, suprise casters, or other situations that
call for a rapid smackdown.

Of course, it would also make Wizards a new favorite for
powerleveling people (especially if it were possible to lay
this on an arrow or throwing dagger).
