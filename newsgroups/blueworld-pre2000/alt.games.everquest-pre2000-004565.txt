Actually you can explore in a more "classic" sense in EQ. But, it
requires a very good group as well as a good puller. (and an uncrowded
dungeon) Basically, the puller clears up ahead, observing the mob's
patterns and paths and then determines the next "safe" spot. Once he has
cleared the wandering and static mobs to that spot the entire group
moves up and you repeat the process. My first char explored lower guk
that way with a really good group long before their were maps and
spoilers on all the web sites and it still stands as the best time I've
had in the game.

It's still possible to do this in the less used dungeons like Sol A,
Permafrost, and possibly the new Paw, though from what I've seen, the
lousy design of that one makes it not much fun to try.

Where this completely breaks down is the planes, where the mob agro
radius, path bugs, pet bugs (revenants in hate particularly) and the
through the wall agro make it stupidly difficult unless you get lucky on
zone in. Our weekend's trip to Hate turned into an 8 hour CR because
there were 4 spite golems that agro'd on the room if more than 2 people
zoned in at once. As spite golems are completely immune to magic there's
just about no way in hell you can deal with that on zone in. I'm not
saying how we managed to recover the corpses because Verant seems quite
happy that these bugs and design flaws add to the difficulty, so we'll
just keep the little flaws that allowed us to get our corpses back
quiet.
