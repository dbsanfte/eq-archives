> Teh server that I'm on is the E'ci Server, I'm a wizard. I"m not looking for
> charity, but some simple help, how I can get more spells and better wepons.

You can buy all your wizard spells from the merchants at your guild
or from the general mage merchants in a nearby building.  Hail your
guildmaster and the other people in your guild.  There is often a 
quest you can complete at your guild that will result in a new spell.

Your first new weapon will probably be a cracked staff and you will 
probably get it from a decaying skeleton.  Try to kill decaying 
skeletons with weapons.  Even if you can't use them, each weapon
sells to the merchants for a fair amount of money so you will be
able to buy those new spells. ;)

For lots of information about spells and basic strategies, go look
at Casters' Realm:

http://eq.castersrealm.com/
