I started out with EQ and played it for a while it got really boring. I
would play one character and when it started to get really really boring I
would start a new one. Different Race, Class, Everything, etc. Then that
would get boring. Also there was never a feeling to want to talk to people.
You just went out and hunted and if you were out numbered you train your
enemies to the guard. All of this and the problems got really old In my
opinion.


Then I found AC in beta!! I was addicted from the first day. Everyday
something new is happening. Everyone is allot more social. Also the world is
so much bigger and you don't play in a box!! That's just a few thoughts on
it. I also have to add that the staff is so so so much better. I don't think
I ever talked to an EQ staff member. On here I have had the chance many
times. I really feel that Dereth is a part of my life and I never had that
feeling in EQ. Plus I personally hated zones.

No bashes here just my personal opinion!! =)
