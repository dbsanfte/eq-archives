Having worked in fulfillment (that's what they call the process of filling
your order) I knew that only those orders placed for overnight delivery
would be shipped first...that's just the way fulfillment houses work...I
ordered for overnight service and got my confirmation on the Friday 14th and
received my order on Monday the 17th (I also knew that overnight meant Next
Business day too) I have installed and updated everything and am ready to go
on Monday barring any unforeseen problems with the new zones....I give the
fulfillment house high marks for their handling of RoK

Stuff happens to shipments...demand exceeds supply...software shipped was
buggy, please ship it back...
I've seen em all....

If you can get Overnight service the day of release, always go that
route....overnight is almost always shipped first no matter when it was
ordered.
