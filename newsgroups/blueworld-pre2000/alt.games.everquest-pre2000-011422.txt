Here's how you slow down your dying rate:  learn the game.  You die, you

knows the game extremely well, die occasionally.  It's a part of playing.

game.

Anyway, easiest classes to play until you learn your way
around...hmm...magician is probably the easiest caster and monk the easiest
melee.

Make note that I said easiest to PLAY.  They ARE NOT the easiest to LEVEL
unless you have an affinity for that class (translation: if you really like
the class and learn it, you'll level easily enough).

Stay away from evil races (dark elves, trolls, ogres) except Iksar...Ikkies
have great newbie zones so if you want to learn melee, an ikkie monk is your
easiest option.
