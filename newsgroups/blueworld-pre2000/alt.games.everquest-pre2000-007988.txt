That's because, IIRC, the only reason those songs even use mana is as a
timer for when you can play the song again. Verant had no built-in way
to set a lengthy timer for Bard songs and decided to use mana (and the
regenration of said) as the timer. Since it isn't really mana (but a
timer) it is not affected by mana-regenerating spells or items. I
believe this was originally mentioned by GZ in one of his Archmage Q&A
columns.
