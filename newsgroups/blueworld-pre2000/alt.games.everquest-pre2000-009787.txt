I would second this recommendation.  It's a dungeon area, so you get the dung.
exp. modifier, and it's a reasonably good area to fight.  The only real problem
is that this is a known fact, so it can sometimes be hard to find consistent
critters to beat up.  Also, since it's the next logical step to a newbie area,
there are some problems with youngsters going in WAY too early, getting in over
their heads, and causing problems for you.

Nevertheless, if you can get into a group and hang out in the throne room, 
the action is pretty constant (unless there's more than two groups in there), 
the experience is good, and the danger is reasonably low.  Just be aware of the
occasional tower train which will lead Ambassador DVinn on a rampage, with him
taking out everyone he meets.  (He's one of those underconned to us melee types
and can hurt you badly quickly.)  The pluses are that both the Shiny Brass 
Shield and Dragoon Dirk drop in this zone (although you'll have to wait quite
a while to earn the Dirk through killing DVinn), and the crushbone belts and
legionnaire shoulder pads are reasonably lucrative to sell off to people 
wanting to level twinks or lower friends (currently seem to be about 1/2 plat
per belt/pad on Tunare).
