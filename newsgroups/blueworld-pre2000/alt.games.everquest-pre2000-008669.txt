Argument by appeal to higher authority?

Binding is the _only_ thing in the game that is essentially NECESSARY to play
the game.  You can live without tanks/casters in general.  (life may become
decidedly harder, but you can do it).  The game becomes unplayable if you're
a wood elf adventuring in erudin with no binding.  You're talking a TREMENDOUS
amount of time to recover your corpse.  As such, it does seem patherically 
lopsided that, should a melee type want to be bound to a new CITY, they _must_
find a caster to do it.  I know it probably is hard to conceive, but there are
times when the entire server population is low, the caster population is 
also low, and therefore the caster population in your zone is next to nil.  At
such times, if that small caster population is indifferently or antiosocially
inclined towards the melee type, the melee type is effectively precluded from
playing the game.  Please justify how that is an _advantage_, rather than being
blatantly unfair (or, alternatively, blatantly poor game dessign).  Casters
being able to bind anywhere vs just cities for melee types is an advantage.
Melee types simply not being able to be bound without a caster can end up as a
method of denying game service.

Having said all that, 95% of the time, it's not a problem.  But sometimes it 
_is_.  And when it _is_ a problem, it has nothing to do with class balance, 
being a warrior, or anything else.  It has to do with fundamental assumptions 
made by Verant about the dynamics of social interaction which are no longer 
true.  Logically, when the predicate of your statement is false, nothing is 
known about the validity of further inferrals.
