Depends what you want out of a game. UO is NOT for folks who want a
'traditional' RPG, in that there are no elves, dwarves, halflings, so on
and so forth, most of the characters you run into will be named
'Bitchslappa' or 'Pimpinator', monster AI is ATROCIOUS, and there are no
quests at all. However, it is a good game if you want to hang out with
your friends and socialize, or if you want to kill off your fellow
players.

EQ is more of the traditional fantasy mold, although it is a HUGE time
investment and it's very similar to a lot of MODS. Graphics are 3D as
opposed to 2D Isometric as in UO, and while EQ is definitely the more
popular right now (somewhere in the neighborhood of 170,000 active
accounts, whereas UO at its peak had 150,000), it's got challenges of
its own (overcrowding on some servers being a big issue). 

Best thing to do is NOT ask in the newsgroups. Both games have rabid
fans who can't see past the flaws in both games and will bash the other
for no other reason than they don't play it. Asheron's Call is another
online RPG you may want to check out as well. It's far more like EQ than
it is like UO, but it's lacking in the graphics department and has a
skill based system as opposed to a stat based system (ala EQ).
