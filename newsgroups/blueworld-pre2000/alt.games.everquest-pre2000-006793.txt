SakredDenial wrote:
> 
> can someone please explain the concept of binding to me.. what it is.. what
> it's for.. who can do it.. and why it is so in demand.. thanks.....

You have a home point.  Initially it is just outside the city of your birth.
Every time you die, you reappear naked at your home point.  The Gate spell
teleports you to your home point.  The Bind Affinity spell resets your home
point to be your current location.  

It is handy to be bound near to where you are fighting so that, when you die, 
you don't have to run very far to get your body.  However, the Bind Affinity 
spell can only be cast on others inside of city zones, so you may not be able
to be bound right where you are fighting.  Greater Faydark is considered a
city zone because it contains Kelethin.

Pure casters (Wizard, Magician, Enchanter or Necromancer) get Bind Affinity 
at level 12.  Healers (Cleric, Shaman or Druid) get Bind Affinity at level 14.
You should probably expect to pay 5gp-1pp for a bind, although many will do it
for free, particularly if you are very low level.
