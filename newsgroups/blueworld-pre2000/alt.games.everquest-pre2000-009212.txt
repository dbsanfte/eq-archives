To stay in context we were talking about high level parties but
to look at your specific example:

1. You are either on a new server or your warrior is underequipped
for your level.  A BBC is not a very good weapon when you can buy
a BIBS for 200 plat and a GZ for not much more.

2. You are talking about damage in a few seconds.  I am talking
about damage output over an extedned period (10 minutes is fine).
A 20th level tank with a BIBS has a damage output of about
8.7MN/s plus slam or kick as appropriate to class and race.
Taking double attack into account this is roughly 7-9damage
per second for an extended period.

Now compare that to the wizard who is doing 171 per force shock
per 120 mana.  At level 20 with 150 int a wizard would have
about 600 mana--roughly enough for 5 of those (asssuming no
fizzles).  Assuming ZERO resists (which isn't going to happen;
force shock is highly resistable) it comes out at 855 damage and
then the wizard has to med to full which takes about 5-6 minutes.
Lets say its 5.  So for 300 seconds medding and about 20 seconds
spent casting you come out with a damage output of... 2.67/second.
Thats with NO resists.

So even at 20th level a *paladin* (whos damage output is lower
than most melee classes) outdamages a wizard by a factor of
2-3 over an extended period if they're allowed to fight
constantly (or close to it).

The problem here is:

1. Players see the big numbers of the big nukes without looking
at the big picture.

2. They become accustomed to crappy groups who have large
amounts of downtime so the factor the tank needs (time)
works against them (since they're not hitting anything).

Despite anything Verant says the wizard is a crappy class (at
*every* level) by a *huge* margin but thats getting sidetracked.

Melee damage is king.  My advice is get in the habit of avoiding
wizards (and clerics at low levels) and getting in tank heavy
groups with a shaman or druid.  Pull constantly.

For yourself get the best weapon you can afford and (next) get
the best armour you can afford (probably banded).  Ignore stat
items and go for straight AC.

You'll start to see how much better things can be than the
wizard and cleric heavy groups.
