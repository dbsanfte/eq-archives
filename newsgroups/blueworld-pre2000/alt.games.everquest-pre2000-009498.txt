The official Network Status page is a good place to check first
when you are having trouble connecting:

http://everquest.station.sony.com/network_fs.html

The EQ Monitor in EverTools is also reasonable, although I would
not rely on it.  I have had it tell me the servers were down when
they are not.  Sometimes you can use it to distinguish between 
the servers being completely dead, EQ being cut off from the world,
and the chat server being overloaded.

http://www.hydracomp.com/eq/
