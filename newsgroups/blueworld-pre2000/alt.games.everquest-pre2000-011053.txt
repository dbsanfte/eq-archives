    i play an enchanter as my primary, and I hate to group.  So I do a lot 
of soloing and I LOVE the charm spell.  It is fantastic *IF* you are very 
very careful about picking your fights.

I am only lvl 22... but here is my strategy.

Spell list
Choke
DD
Color Shift (or flux)
Tashani (or tashan)
Mezz
Root
Charm
Languid Pace

    	My favourite spot for this is Neriak C and Ratraz.  Ratraz is a level 
19 troll.  Right next to him is a level 15 Rogue.  Nothing Aggros when you 
attack Ratraz.  

    	Strategy is this.  Mezz-Tash-Charm Molon (the rogue) /pet attack 
Ratraz.  Wait for Charm to break at which point Molon goes for the troll.  
I Tash-Root-Dot-Nuke-Languid Pace the Troll.  Please Note that i want the 
troll to kill my "pet" it makes life much easier.

    	I will generally have to re-root the troll a few times and I have to 
be careful not to let him wander off... because a guard will kill him if he 
is actively fighting molon.  (which is annoying, but not dangerous.) 

    	If The troll kill Molon I keep him rooted and nuke til he dies... not 
usually a problem.  If Molon kill sthe troll (very frequently) I do a pet 
guard here... run like hell to the end of a long alley forget LP and mem 
Mem Blur, and then break the charm (DE Enchanter can Hide... breaking 
charm.)  Casting Mem Blur on molon as he runs down the alley at me is cake.  

    	I could Just kill Molon, but I don't want the faction hit.  All 
faction hits with the troll are recoverable easily and cheaply.

    	Now... for what could go wrong!!!!  Charm breaks... damn they suck.  
You have 2 options.  Color Shit (flux) and recharm, or run the hell away.  
I love Neriak C because I can easily outrun Mobs as long as my health isn't 
too low.  Then I can zone, zone back in and try again.

    	However, charm needs to be used judiciously.  It is great for when 
too many guys get pulled (or you can just mezz them.)  I have also found it 
to be a great last resort spell in certain situations.  But in general, 
don't use it without planning it out well before hand.  I used that Ratraz 
deal when I was 16th level and got up to 20 that way... That is soloing 
reds... not easy for an enchanter... impossible without charm.  Just REALLY 
dangerous with it :)
