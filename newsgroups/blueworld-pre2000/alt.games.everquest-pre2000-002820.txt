When I initially tried Ultima Online, I was skeptical since a friend had
mentioned that he thought Everquest to be better. However, I was pleasantly
surprised when I got started. It was nothing like I had imagined. As to
comparisons, it will be hard to find someone who has played both, but my
recommendation is UO based on the descriptions my friend has given me of
Everquest. UO offers more opportunities to role-play (right down to the
mundane), and you are not restricted in any way in your development. Only
experience differentiates the good from the bad.

Now I am raving, don't believe me, buy a copy and try it out.. free with the
software is one month's game time. So, spend a little and find out.
