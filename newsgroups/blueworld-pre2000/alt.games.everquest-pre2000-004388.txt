I can sympathize.  I did this twice.  Once at 17 with piercing and a Dragoon
Dirk.  I think it took me 2 levels to max.  The one I remember more is
2hslash though.

I began raising 2hslash at lvl 22 as soon as I realized how ineffective
dual-wield was at dealing damage.  It was like 2 when I started, then I sunk
all my practice points into it and got it up around 30.  I then began, with
a BIBS, to use it no matter what in my regular group.  Mostly we were
fighting aviaks.  I always got quickness when I could (annoying my enchanter
pal).  With 75int, it took me until lvl25 to max it.  You might try grouping
with a shaman and an enchanter wherever possible to get speed buffs and dex
buffs.

I never had to resort to swinging at others mobs, although SK is the place
to do it at if you are going that route.

Good luck.
