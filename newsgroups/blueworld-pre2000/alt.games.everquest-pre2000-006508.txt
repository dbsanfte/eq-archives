I believe Color Shift lasts 6 seconds.  With the 2.5 second hold
time on all spells, you can cast exactly one spell before everything
unstuns.  So, I guess the algorithm is:

Cast Mesmerization
Count seconds and run into the crowd of monsters
At 23 seconds, cast Color Shift
Cast Entrance on one of the monsters
Cast Color Shift when it recycles (long wait)
Back up to Mesmerization range
Cast Mesmerization
repeat

Or if you are really brave:

Stand in the middle of the horde
Cast Mesmerization
Wait for the mobs to wake up
Cast Color Shift
Cast Entrance
Cast Mesmerization
repeat

Letting them all wake up at once is probably disastrous.  Once
you have the whole crowd mesmerized, I think it's better to just
repeat that spell every 24 seconds until the group is down to 
about three enemies.  If you are absolutely determined to get the
crowd entranced instead of mesmerized, then you could probably
use your weapon to wake them up.

Cast Mesmerization
Run into melee range
Whack a mob
Cast Entrance
Back up to Mesmerization range
Cast Mesmerization
repeat

You might be able to get more than one Entrance in there, but I 
wouldn't count on it.

Honestly, with Entrance not overwriting Mesmerization, I really 
don't think there is a good way to transition from Mesmerization
to Entrance until the group is small enough that you could have
Entranced them all in the first place.
