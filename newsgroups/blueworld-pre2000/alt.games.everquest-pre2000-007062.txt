Rallos just to get a feel for it.  Random PKing is hindered by the
PvP level limit.  Everyone is anonymous, so there is no way to tell
whether you can even affect another player most of the time.

spells on you, except for other PvP players.  You can't even get a 
bind from a non-PvP player.  If you want PvP, play on a red server.
