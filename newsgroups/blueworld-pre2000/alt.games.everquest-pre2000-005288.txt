Yep, you are deluding yourself.

What you are noticing is the change in *maximum* damage.  Sadly, all extra
strength does is increase the top end of the range over which damage hits
can be spread.

Sadly this makes little impact on *average* damage because the distribution
of hits is non-normal - i.e. not a regular Bell Curve.  If you analyse the
hits over time, the most common hit (a little under half) will be your
"magic number" (normally two times base weapon damage but there is a linear
addition - see other posts).

This means that Strength (in effect increasing your potential maximum) makes
no real difference in combat.  100 extra strength will give less than half a
percent increase in damage over time.
