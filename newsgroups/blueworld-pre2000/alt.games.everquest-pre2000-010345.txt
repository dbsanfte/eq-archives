Actually, the fire opals cost considerably more.  For high levels it's
rather trivial cost, but for low/mid levels, the jagged bands are

used to dump the jagged bands for 50pp for a pair.

The fire opals on the other hand has a material cost of about 165pp
(if memory serves) and are non-trivial even at 200 skill.
