Why is it a problem that the buildings are solid to you.  Assumably, players 
know immediately to go for the doors (or portal type windows.  I've SEEN that
advice for Crushbone.)  The problem with doing as you say is the following:

Buildings would never have portals of any type.  You could open a door, pop in, nuke a character, pop out.. the character is NEVER going to be able to figure 
out that it should go out the door around the wall and back down the wall to
get to you if, for instance, you're even with it.  This is the basic problem.
So, the solution is to let the monsters deal through the building.  Given, it
would be nice if the monsters would check to see if there was a dividing 
surface before they aggro on you, but once they do, consider it their version
of your "tactics".  It's no more cheap than exploiting an obviously 
insufficient AI.  (Which is what happens despite these pains that Verant has
taken.)  I'm not saying I don't wish it were different, I'm just saying the 
reason it isn't so is a fundamentally technological one, not some desire to 
screw the paying player base.
