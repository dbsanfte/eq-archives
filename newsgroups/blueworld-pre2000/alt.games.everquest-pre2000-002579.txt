skill curve", whatever that means. Things will progress as though you were
on one of the old shards. I think I read that they lifted the figures from
Sonoma.

But, to respond to your rant about what's still wrong with UO-
I think it's good that they only fixed a few things this time around. I
mean, ok, archery needs to be fixed, and there are other issues that are
minor enough, but the big changes, like Recall and shopkeepers... I'm glad
they are trying them and I'm glad that they're trying not _too_ many of
them. Because there are going to be new problems, and they are going to
need to be able to identify the sources. If they changed _everything_ at
once, it would be worse than useless. Now they have a control, they have
an experiment with a few changes, and once they see the effects from
these, I expect to someday see Siege Perilous II with even more changes,
and possibly some things undone. 
Upon reflection, I'm going to be interested to hear about it, but as I
said, I don't have time to participate, and if I did find the time, ya
know, I have other projects. :P But I'm thrilled they're trying this just
because it will mean a better UO overall, once they see how these ideas
and changes work.

This isn't so much a response to Dundee's rant, actually, than to the
general issue of What's Wrong With UO. <g>
