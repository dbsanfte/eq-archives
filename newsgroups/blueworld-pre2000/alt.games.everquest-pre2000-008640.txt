Actually, total XP is not proportional to XP needed to level.  It would
be if they had used an exponential curve, but they used a cubic curve.
Until ShowEQ proved me wrong, I had been assuming that Verant was using
the most logical choice, an exponential curve.

XP to get to level N is C * N^3 for some constant C.
XP to get from level N to level N+1 is 
	C*((N+1)^3 - N^3) = C *(3N^2 + 3N + 1)
	or roughly 3C * N^2

So the fraction of your total experience that you need to get up one
level gets smaller as you go up in levels (3CN^2/CN^3 = 3/N).  This 
means that if you get separated in levels, the gap will slowly widen 
and you will fall further behind.

Hell levels make the math at each level somewhat messier, but for
the most part, they just change the curve from cubic to quartic and
the same property holds.
