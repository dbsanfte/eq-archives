I love these arguments by the paranoid and willfully ignorant. There is 
a world of difference between an institution funded by the public and a 
private corporation.

For starters, if in a country the police are ALLOWED to do the above, 
you have two choices: Move (emigrate) or break the law and resist. You 
still have choices, they just aren't appealing ones.

In a country where they are NOT allowed to do this they are held 
accountable and can be arrested themselves.

With a private corporation, you ALWAYS have the choice to stop using 
their product, depriving them of their lifeblood, revenue. Few, if any, 
private corporations produce a product which is REQUIRED as a necessity 
of life, therefore the choice exists to simply not use it and not be 
affected by their decision making.

This is not an invasion of privacy issue, as much as the black 
helicopter crowd wishes it were. EQ is NOT an Operating System. It is 
NOT required to run anything on your computer OTHER than EverQuest. You 
can choose NOT to play it or own it, and unless you are completely, 
utterly devoid of a life, it will not impact your standard of living. It 
is not required.

So unless Verant clearly violates laws in what they do, they can demand 
that customers name themselves 'Cob Chomping Muff Diver' before they can 
play, because you can ALWAYS choose not to play.
