No. alt.* groups cannot realistically be rmgrouped. Further, many
people will choose not to move, for technical logistical or
cyber-political reasons. Many people won't be able to move. You'll end
up with lots of crossposting back and forth. It will be a mess.

There's no point to duplicating a successful alt.* group inside a
big-eight hierarchy. The problems outweigh the advantages, especially
in this day-and-age.
