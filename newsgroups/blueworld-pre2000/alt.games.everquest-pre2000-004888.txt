Lets bring this into the "real" world.

Is any L49 necromancer ever turned away from a group because Shaman get
envenomed bolt and necromancers do not?  Of course not.

Shaman don't seriously compete with necromancers as DOT champion until
L49.  At that point, Shaman get access to one really, really good DOT
that is better than any other single DOT in the game for general use.

OTOH, Shamans have to share their entire disease line with necromancers.
The necromancers get a different disease line all their own(now anyway),
a lifetap DOT line, a heat based line and a darkness based line that
acts as a snare.  All of these are potentially useful in circumstances
poison won't work, and some give benefits that poison does not.

The necromancer not only gets 4 additional Dot lines, all of which
stack with the lines he shares with shaman, he also normally has more
mana to spend when he's full, and gets back that mana faster.

Is anyone turning away necromancers because their DOTs, of all things,
are deficient?

If a group plans to kill every single MOB off within 7 ticks, I suppose
a shaman would always be a better choice.  But how often is that going
to happen if the only damage spell an L40+ mob is hit with is Envenomed
Bolt?
