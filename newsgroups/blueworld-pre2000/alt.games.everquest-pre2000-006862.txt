Aye.

A while back my Ranger grabbed a guild-mate and a random bored
higher-level druid and went to camp DVinn for a Dragoon Dirk.

Folks kept wandering by.

If they were my level, I would invite them to group with me,
telling them that I was camping for a dirk and that they were
welcome to whatever other loot they wanted.  If I had gotten
one before it was time to go I would then have helped them get
one.

Higher level folks, I told the same thing, and then invited
them to share in the other critters that popped up there.  They
either accepted or wandered off, and the folks who did accept
and my group ended up cross buffine one another and pulling
extra pops off each others back.

It all worked very well until the Druid left and then then
entire tower popped at the same time.  Luckily a high level
Paladin had wandered by and only one of our group died.  No
Dirk, and soon I got bored of Crushbone, went to Antonicia and
ended up fletching a dragoon dirk.

There are a LOT of ways to split something up.  The only thing
that matters is that the folks there agree with them.
