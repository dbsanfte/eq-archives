There's probably some bug with experience distribution or something.
When they split up experience, they add up all the group member's 
total experience, and then each person gets:
(player's total experience)/(sum of all group members' experience)
of the award.  If the group has more than six members, that sum
only includes the first six.  So, find six low levels to be the 
official group and you get some insanely low divisor.  Heck, each
of the extra high level people could end up getting more than the 
total experience for the kill. ;)  Of course, this would take six
people who sit around getting nothing for their actions all day.
