A true sequence of random numbers will, from time to time, have
long runs of similar values.  Flip a dime 100 times and you
should see this.

The Verant psuedo-random number generator also throws off
long runs of similar values.  

I am not sure if this means that they are doing it right, or if
they are trying too hard to mimic true randomness and in the
process have introduced a bias towards runs.
