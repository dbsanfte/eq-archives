It still costs its mana, so it is always a question of casting that or
casting something else with that mana.

I can cast:

Celerity on the two fighters in my group,
Shiftless Deeds on the monster they are fighting,
Feedback (my damage shield) on the warrior who always takes the hits,
Suffocate, Incapacitate, and Weakness on the monster they are fighting,
and of course I am keeping any extras mezzed.

Any mana I spend during the fight is mana I don't have at the end, so 
it is not really ever "free" to cast the damage shield.  A good damage
shield is better than any nuke, but probably not as good as most DoTs
unless you are in an uncontrolled many-on-one situation.

In most fights against blues, I don't cast any debuffs at all.  When 
fighting a yellow, I normally slow and sometimes incapacitate.  I try
to end up the fight at a similar mana level to the cleric so that we 
will both be ready to proceed on to the next fight at the same time.
