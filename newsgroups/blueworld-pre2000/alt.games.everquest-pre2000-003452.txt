There is one broken link in the EQ economy that could be easily
fixed.  Here's the question:  Who pays for all the guards, roads,
bridges, public areas, and such?  Apparently nobody.  Why?
Because no one is paying their TAXES!  Yes, that's right, taxes.
What we need is some sort of tax that must be played by each
player.  Perhaps a sales tax on every trade, or an income tax, or
perhaps just an inventory tax.  Or maybe a fixed tax based on
level and time played -- for example, 1sp per game day per level.
Maybe this should be progressive so that lower levels don't have
as great a burden as higher levels do.  Maybe a progressive tax
rate such as:

Levels         Tax per day
1-3             None
4-6             1sp per level per 24 game hours on line
7-10           3sp per level per 24 game hours on line
11-20         1gp per level per ....
21-30         3gp per level per ....
31-40         1pp per level per ....
41-50         5pp per level per 24 game hours on line.

This would end up with a 250pp tax on a level 50 every 24 game
hours played.  Better get out there an hustle for that loot.  A
level 15 would have to cough up 15 gp every game day online.
Again, better get out and hustle.   These numbers are just
examples off the top of my head, but, I am sure that, with a
little thought, better numbers could be suggested.   Penalties
for not paying?  Confiscation of possessions such as armor or
weapons?  You tell me.

If the EQ economy needs a cash sink, and I think everyone agrees
it does, here it is -- taxes.  Pay for those roads and guards and
such.

One other non-tax related note:  Weapons should get old and break
and armor should get damaged and become unserviceable after a
while so they must be replaced.

Comments?  Discussion?
