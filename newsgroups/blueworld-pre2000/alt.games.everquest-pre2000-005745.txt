I still haven't received an email about this from the guild involved, but I
did hear about it from another source and I've reviewed the logs of the
issue.  There are a few things I'd like to share with those reading this
thread.  First, at no time was Lokkison less than polite.  He in fact
discussed the issue actively for over 40 minutes, which is quite a bit
longer than he should have.  It would have been far easier just to give her
a Paw.

That said, it is true that it is not our policy to reimburse items unless we
have a record of it in the backups, regardless how many witnesses there are.
We work off of hard fact, and it's too easy for someone to get 10 friends
together and support each other with a false story.  That is not to say that
we think everyone is lying, as then we would suspend/ban them from the game,
it just means that we have insufficient evidence to support the claim.

Certainly, these rules are strict, but it is the only method we have to
reduce the impact of dishonest people upon the game.
