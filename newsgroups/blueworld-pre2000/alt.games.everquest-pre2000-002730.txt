Your numbers are quite exaggerated, I'd be surprised to find out I'd killed
100 goblin whelps or gnoll pups in 4 levels.  However, it did take me two
weeks to get to level 5.  I hunted with a warrior friend and we never
visited the same place twice in those two weeks.  It was about the 5th level
when I started to feel like we had explored the "newbie" area, then I found
the ridge in Everfrost Peaks while wandering around lost.  I excitedly told
my friend about it and retraced my steps to lead him there.  The first thing
we noticed, GROUPING!  There are 6 people in these groups, and there are
ALOT of groups all sitting on this ridge!  By the way, Klan (my friend) and
I had grouped but we didn't even know about 'groupsay' mode.  Well, a 5th
level shaman and warrior are the underdogs when it comes to Polar Bears,
Snow Leopards, and Snow Orc Troopers.  So, I suggested we look around and
observe some of the group tactics.  Of course, Klan being the way he is,
interpreted that to mean attack a Wooly Mammoth standing nearby.  *WHACK*
*Mammoth Noises* *WHACK* He's dead.  I am ROFL!  I tell you the truth, he is
a level 25 warrior and to this day he does not con anything he attacks.  He
recently decided to show those new Deepwater Goblins who's boss.
Unfortuntely, he was still bound in High Pass, so it was not one of the more
fun nights but we still had a good laugh.  Klan has quite a history of
attacking "ice goblin whelps" that kick his ass (he did the same thing in
Dagnor's Cauldron at level 12)...  Blah blah blah...

Look, I don't think you have the imagination to come to the Cazic-Thule
dungeon, look around and say to yourself "Everyone is camping in the
courtyard, I think I'll explore the maze".  I think you'd find it more
enjoyable to just bitch about over-crowding in the UO newsgroup.  So, I am
not going to share my experiences with you because you have no desire to
enjoy this game, but I will tell you that my friends and I get a pot-load
more xps exploring the maze than the camping the courtyard.  It is a matter
of choice and that choice is totally up to you.

- Gladimir, Barbarian Shaman of Clan Blackwatch, Cazic-Thule
