My primary is a wood elf ranger, only level 19 at the moment so
remember that I can not speak from experience about the higher
levels.

I find that Rangers give up some combat ability in order to get
useful spells.  Think of how the druid is the utility infielder
of the caster classes, now consider that the Ranger's spell
list is driven from the druid spells.  We can hit well, do not
take hits well, and have lower downtime than other casting
classes.

Little things, like being able to camo up for a corpse run,
make life a lot easier for Rangers.

Paladins are clerics lite - they get a lot of neat little
weapon quests at the lower levels, they can heal some, they can
wear plate.  I like grouping with paladins.  Lay on Hands is a
darn neat ability, and the decent healing spells make paladins
a very social class.

Again, both have large experience penalties, both have several
ways to reduce their down time as compared to a warrior.

I wouls play the more compelling personality.
