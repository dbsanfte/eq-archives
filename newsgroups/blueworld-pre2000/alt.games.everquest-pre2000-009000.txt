I think there is something wrong with your counting technique.
Shadowknights are roughly as popular as Rangers and Paladins on
other servers.  You are probably typing in the wrong thing when
you count them.  

Try: "/w all war count", "/w all shad count" etc.   Note that
"/w all sha" returns Shadowknights, not Shamans.
