Huh.  My ranger spent quite a bit of time in OOT on Seafuries, when he
was in his low 40's.  The group most commonly consisted of two rangers
for primary tanks (and usually a druid and an enchanter, plus possibly
2 random pick-ups).  We had no problems handling the tanking role at
all.  We did have an enchanter for debuffing the mobs, though.
Perhaps that had something to do with it.

I found Seafuries much easier to handle than Ice Giants.  We used
snare/scare against Ice Giants, but just did stand-up tanking (with
damage shield) against Seafuries.  Seafuries were a lot easier.

My Ranger's AC at that time, buffed, was probably just under 800.

Not that you should take this comment as disagreement about Rangers
getting hosed post-50, though....  If they got rid of the XP penalty
it would be almost reasonable, but as-is it is quite bogus.
