Well, that's why we created towns.  It wasn't a free-for-all housing system
like the one in UO (you can tell I'm a fan of that aspect) and required just
a wee bit of planning.  We were allowing a limited number of houses per IP
address (I know this wouldn't work with EQ, but it's an example) and it was
sort of an avatar-assisted set-up.  We had specific avatars assigned to
housing and they helped people access their new abodes.  The house existed
before the player acquired it because, as you mentioned, in this type of
environment (MUD, EQ 3D) locations must be preexisting.  As for lag, I would
really have to hope that EQ is using more than one server for the entire
world and that player cities could have their own servers... if not, well,
more power to them I guess...
