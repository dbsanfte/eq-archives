In addition there is an element of reverse snobbery in not
having a last name.

I do not go anon, but I never found a surname for my Ranger
that I liked better than having no name.  So I have a 1-named
Ranger in leather pants and partial ivy running around.

I still get people asking me if I can SoW (not for 9 more
levels) but I also get people worrying when they see me
meleeing a griffene or werewolf, root it, and step back to cast.

My new characters all have names that were chosen with a
surname in mind, and I do strongly recommend to people that
they think ahead at character creation.
