Yes .. this is a real problem .. one which I, and I would say, MANY others
share.  Yes, it seems to have started after the last large patch (the one
followed by several emergency patch jobs).

The tendency to go LD is also compounded by the emergency fix implemented
last Saturday to stop the FD/LD dupe problem.  That fix resulted in the
'synchronization' message you are getting .. you cannot get back on as long
as you have an 'active' character in the game.  In my case, the wait does
not seem to be long .. since I consistently go LD on every fight .. in
mid-fight .. and the mob kills me fast since I am a caster.

Your assessment is in line with what everyone else has been posting to many
boards.  It is not acknowledged by Verant as far as I know yet.

I understand your frustrations .. I am thinking seriously for the first time
of cancelling my account .. which is sad because I still love this game.

The only responses I have seen from Verant on this issue are along the lines
of 'this is a development issue' (and then the thread was closed), and 'give
us a loc and all your system specs and all the details or we can't even
begin to look at it'.  Unfortunately for me it happens on every server, in
every zone, with every character and every mob, every time I am on a few
minutes, and definitely the first time I attempt to fight a mob.  This is
not sufficient for them to isolate the problem I am told.

I do suspect that it is related to the old SBLive problem.  Numerous
suggestions have been put forth by players to re-install DirectX latest
version, etc.  I have checked all these things, and followed other
directions to set my acceleration back to standard, and delete a number of
files from the Creative package and reboot.  These have not, for me, solved
the unrecognized problem. I think that somehow some code changes were made
during that already problematic patch which caused more of us to begin to
have 'compatibility' issues with the sound cards.  I don't use my sound in
EQ .. it is disconnected.  But these symptoms sound very suspiciously like
the ones we have been hearing about from some players for months.  It is not
related either to whether you dialup or use a TI or a cable modem or DSL.
No one is sure of the common denominator.

Good luck, my friend.  I hope, for the sake of all of us who love EQ, that
someone can find a solution soon.
