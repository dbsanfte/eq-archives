The faction will go up, but slowly...it's very easy to lose faction when
you kill things, and a lot harder to bring it up...during beta, for
example, we could see exactly how much faction you gained or lost,
although it was numbered, not named, so unless you spent a lot of time
calculating things, it was hard to tell (I worked a lot on figuring out
which numbers went with what factions). 

Killing something often lost you 5 points of faction, for example, while
recovering faction with said group meant doing something specific 5
times over...

Best way I have found to bring up your rep with the Freeport Militia is
to turn in Gnolls Scalps and Orc Scalps in Highpass Hold. Tonight, for
example, I grouped with folks at the Orc Side of the Pass, and we were
the only ones there for 2 hours. I managed to get 18 scalps in that
time, which translated into a nice big faction gain =).
