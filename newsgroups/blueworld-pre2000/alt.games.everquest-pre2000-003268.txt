Lag will be more noticable than in EverQuest. There is no more lag in AC
than EQ though. About 9 out of 10 times i get lag is because of the same
reasons I get lagged in EQ. Meaning they are basically the same in the Lag
department. Just in EQ, your "Lag O Meter" will drop to zero, and in AC
you'll just run in place, or experience "rubber banding" where you and the
monster slide towards and away each other. I really wish AC handled lag like
EQ, but Turbine has stated all movement code is checked server side for
hacks to prevent some of the movement hacks that have already been made in
EQ (like the memory hacks where you can set your run speed a lot higher).
90% of the time i notice no lag in AC though. just when it hits, you notice
it more than you would in EQ.
As for portal storms, i've only been in one since release. (actually
portaled out of town like 6 times in a  row during it though).. that was
because of an event. towns are always crowded, but few have portal storms (i
hear about them all the time from the town called Arwic)..
Whenever i was portaled out of town, it was into an empty field. ive never
heard of anyone being portaled anywhere near monsters or anything like that.


> 2.  Are the graphics still as bad, with memory running out and buildings
> becoming just shapes with white surfaces? (this, I believe, was basically
> due to it overpowering my card(s)...two voodoo2's, but the display options
> wouldn't help it either...)

there are issues with 3dfx cards in general (Voodoo 1,2,3,Rush, Banshee)
that cause some yucky graphic errors (black lines around textures, running
out of vid memory and getting white textures)... On a Voodoo 3/Banshee, you
have to turn the settings down to that of about a 8 or 12 Meg card, and with
a Vooodoo 2, youd have to turn the settings down to that of an old 4 Meg
card... (yuck!) ..a new video card would give you better graphics (something
like a TNT/TNT2/GeForce).. I use a Banshee and have my settings turned down.
My friend has a realatively new "killer" system he built (500MHz+, 128Megs+
RAM). He put a Voodoo 3 3000 in it. When he loaded up AC, he was like "WTF?"
.. he got white textures, messed up rain, black lines on people's faces, and
other stuff.. even with all the new stuff, just having a 3dfx card made the
game look bad. my web site has screen shots of 3dfx cards and AC..
(xenomorph.net)

> 3.  Is it nearly as annoying as EQ, where it's extremely hard to get items

Items are 100 times more available to people than in EQ... instead of just
having 1 super item, there will be like 5 items with similar stats to it.

around. Ive gotten excelent weapons for my characters.. So no, it is not
hard at all to get great items for your character..
