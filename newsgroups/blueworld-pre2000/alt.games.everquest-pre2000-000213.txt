>The existence of God is self-evident.

>If you hold a painting in your hand, do you need to have someone prove the

>For exactly the same reason, we already know of the existence of God.  We
>have His creation all around us which proves His existence.  We don't need
>proof of a Creator, we have the evidence of His existence, His creation.

This makes absolutely no sense; your painting/building/God analogy is
hopelessly flawed.

When you look at a painting, you know that *somebody* painted it, but
you don't know who.  When you look at a building, you know that
*somebody* built it, but just by looking at it, you can't tell who.

Likewise, when you look at "His" creations, you know that someone or
something must be responsible for their existence, but you have no way
of knowing who or what created them.  You may believe that a God caused
these creations to be; or that there's some perfectly reasonable
explanation for them that has nothing to do with a God.  Either way, you
don't know.  You just *believe*.
