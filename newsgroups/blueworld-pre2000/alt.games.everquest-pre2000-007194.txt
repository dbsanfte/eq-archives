This is the rationale for why my party's Rogue stopped playing that
character and started up a Warrior... He always liked the dynamics of
pickpocket in the past. My favorite thing was hearing him shout "WTF!
80 copper??" But now they changed things... I don't know if our server
is different from most, but I have come to find that in most zones, if
a rogue pops his head in and says he is looking for a group, he will
be laughed at. Now, the opinion of this message board has always been
that "Those people are morons if they don't want a rogue in their
party", as I've had people tell me on the most part... But, on the
servers themselves, it is something totally different. Basically, a
rogue has to make friends with every person roughly his level, and
network to make connections to group. There is one great way to
partially solve this problem, though... Make a text line that says to
the party when the rogue pickpockets... Now, the rogue could still
casually steal from other players, just by walking up to a mob they
were fighting, but this would promote rogues to find groups even more
quickly... Think about it, you want them in groups, at least that way
you know they aren't sneak/hiding and stealing your loot... I just
wish the new message would actually be implemented...
