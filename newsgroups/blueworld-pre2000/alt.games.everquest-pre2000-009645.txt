>Uhm, I think I was not clear in what I was asking.  You say trade skills

>But what about lower level people?  For a non-twinked level 10 or 20, is
>it still best to go for items to sell to raise money for equipment or
>spells, or are trade skills worthwhile?  

Sam's correct across the board.
To raise your skill to a level where you can even think about making
any money, it takes a TON of time.  In that same time, you can easily
hunt things to make more money--even if you do not camp rare stuff.

Even if you manage to raise your trade skill in certain specific
trades to make money, the time wasted actively pursuing this can be
better spent with hunting as far as time invest vs. profit is
concerned.

As someone who has mastered many trade skills in the game (with the
notable exception of jewelery), I strongly discourage anyone picking
up a trade skill to make money.  Do it because it's fun, do it because
you want to provide a service to your friends, do it for pretty much
any reason but not to make money.

>I can see that someone at your
>level is not excited by making backpacks, but for my level 19 wizard,
>one backpack would pay for *all* the level 20 spells I want.

How long do you think it will take for you to raise your skill past
the point where backpacks are relatively reliable to make?  Studded
armor is an ungodly pain in the ass to make--and medium pelts aren't
too common.  You can get medium cat pelts, but I have yet to see a
single medium wolfskin and the medium bear skins are just a hair more
common than the HQ bear skins.  Of course, even if you luck out and
find a bunch of medium quality skins, there's absolute *NO* market for
studded armor.  Cured silk, if you don't do studded first will result
in you taking a very big loss because you will fail practically every
single attempt (each swatch can be sold to the vendor for close to
4gp--you can go through a backpack full of swatches in a real hurry).
And reinforced (the only way to raise your skill beyond when backpacks
become trivial) is another nightmare (hauling around small bricks of
ore is ridiculously annoying).  And NOTHING for reinforced armor stack
(except for the patterns).

Remember, just because backpacks become trivial to make doesn't mean
you will succeed all that often--infact, at trivial (88 if I recall),
you usually fail about 33% of the time (possibly higher since I never
bothered to waste HQ bear skins until I've capped my skill).

>Also, I'm asking how much money people at lower levels should expect to
>have if they are playing reasonably and not twinked.

Go hunt, trust me that's a FAR better way to make money than any trade
skill.  At level 20, you can kill avaik darters and still get exp for
them.  Darters can drop upto 2 or 3pp per kill.  In a couple of hours
of killing, you can not only make exp but also enough money for all
your 19th level spells.
