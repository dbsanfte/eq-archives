The problem with this no mana comment is that folks always seem to
forget that there is a mana cost.  The effects take twice as long as
the spell to cast.  Sup camo is a 5 second cast, the helm is a 10 sec
cast with a 1 second hold.  To camo 6 people, it takes 65 seconds of
constant casting.

Using sup camo in the form of a spell, that's 35 seconds instead.  So
you burned 240 mana (40 * 6), but in 30 seconds sitting and medding at
level 56 with maxxed out meditate skill, I can regen about
280mana/min, so it's 140 mana in that time.

So the helm in the *BEST* case senerio saves you a whopping 100 mana
or about 20 seconds of medding.  This is why ES helm is largely a toy
and something that will no doubt rot in my bank inventory.

The tuning folks for these kunark armor did something right--by giving
it the high casting time, the benefit of the effects are much lower
than they seem.
