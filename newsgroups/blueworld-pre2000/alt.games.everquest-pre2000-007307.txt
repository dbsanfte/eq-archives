Alright, I got myself a pair of Crafted Gloves tonight (+5 Strength, 12
AC), and they replaced my Snakeskin Gloves (+3 Dex). I wasn't sure I
wanted to go for this, given all the hoo ha over the uselessness of
Strength and the wonders of Dexterity, but I wanted to see for myself.

So far, the only difference I can discern is an increase in the amount
of damage my criticals do. My average critical damage pre gloves was
about 22-24 (depending which hand landed. I have a Langseax and a
Combine Scimitar). With the gloves, I'm getting 28-32. One even landed
for 35, which I have never landed for since Criticals were introduced.

Now, I'm level 23 right now, so I don't know what about that could
factor into things. I played a lot tonight so I got together a nice set
of numbers (I landed about 30 criticals over the course of the evening,
and before tonight I'd landed hundreds since they were introduced).

Other than the gloves, my character is pretty much identical. I'd also
aquired a Blood Stained Mantle (+3 Strength, +10 Save vs Magic) but I
hadn't seen an real benefit, strength wise, with that other than an
increase in carrying capacity (which is always nice considering I do a
lot of soloing and like to carry things around as long as possible).
Before gloves I was 112 strength, after 115.

Now, my average DAMAGE hasn't increased any, I'm still hitting for an
average of around 13, so I know the Strength Boost hasn't had an effect
there. However, I'm thinking that the Critical Formulae may factor in
Strength in some way, shape or form. 

Anyone else have more experience with Critical resolution that could
shed some light on this, I'm curious...
