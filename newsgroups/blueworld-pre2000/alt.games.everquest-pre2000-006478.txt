Back to the original topic at hand (scarab armor). My [male] halfling
druid quested for this, and it was a sight to see him in it, even though
he looked like a green water heater wearing the thing (I don't like
face-covering helmets).

Anyway...the green suit was retired to the bank long ago in favor of
magical leathers and I had a chance to pass it on to my wife's little
female halfling druid character last night. Damn, but she looks hot in
it! A nicely shaped helmet that doesn't cover the face, a full
breastplate (as opposed to just about all the other female halfling
chest armor, that looks like a bosom-enhancing bodice), and boots that
show! I compared her to a friend of hers she groups with (female
halfling warrior). In banded, the warrior's boots don't show, the
sleeves don't show, and the leggings look like a crinkled-up, flimsy
bluish mylar apron.

Someone did the scarab armor on female halflings right, and the banded
plain wrong. Even leathers look better than the banded...
