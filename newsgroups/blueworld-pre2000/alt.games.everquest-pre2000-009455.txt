I noticed something very strange while fletching in Kelethin
the other day.

There are four fletching merchants in the city: two are in
little huts and two are outside on the same platforms. The
merchants in the huts have the same faction as other Kelethin
merchants - they love me.

The merchants outdoors, Lanin on the platform near the PoD lift
and Sylnis who sells bow staves, are both apprehensive.

Does anyone know what faction group they belong to, and how to
raise faction with these two?

Even with sneak and 118 charisma I could not quite get the best
prices from them - and it is odd to think that I get better
deals from Ogres in the Feerott than from wood elves in
Kelethin.

It is not that their faction is bugged, but rather that I was
wondering if anyone knows what their faction group actually IS?
