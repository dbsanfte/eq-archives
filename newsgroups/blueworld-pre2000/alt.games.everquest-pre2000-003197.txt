spend doing something were destroyed on purpose by someone else.

In EQ, if you die in a lava pit in a dungeon you had no business being
in, carrying a weapon it took you months to get, you're going to be
pissed, because you're watching those hours of effort melt away and
there's nothing you can do about.

These games aren't like traditional games where you can reload to pick
up where you left off.
