Your task is hopeless.  Unless you are going to put all your players
through an application process to exclude those people who just like
to ruin other people's fun, you are going to be stuck with the same
nasty PK problem that UO had.  What are you going to do about whole
PK guilds?  UO had plenty of those.

EQ fixes the problem by not letting people screw around with other 
players.  Before playing EQ, I believed Raph Koster when he said that
you need human enemies to give the game depth.  He was wrong.  It 
took me only one day of play to realize that the ability to interact
with other players without any concern for my welfare was a huge 
improvement.
