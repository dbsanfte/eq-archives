A game based on the Shadowrun world could be fun, but they will have
to create a game system from scratch because the existing one is
terrible.

If you think EQ has bad math, you've never looked at Shadowrun.
Those guys can't do statistics at all.  They came up with elaborate
dice roll systems to prevent people from over-analyzing everything,
but then they couldn't analyze it themselves.  So they screwed it
up mightily.  Adding points might double your chance of succeeding
at something or might have no effect at all.

They tried again to make a dice system in Earthdawn, but screwed up 
so badly there that you could actually get a worse chance of 
succeeding at a task by raising your skill at it.  Somehow they 
managed to get all the way to publishing without discovering this
basic flaw in the system.

And then you want Microsoft to write this thing?  Remember, 95% of
writing a game is content creation.  That's creative work, not brute
hacking.
