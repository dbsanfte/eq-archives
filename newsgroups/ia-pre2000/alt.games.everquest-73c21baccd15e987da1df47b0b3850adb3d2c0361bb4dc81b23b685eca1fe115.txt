I just spent a night getting over 11. Wheee! And then I lost it all
back to an EQ bug. Or maybe it wasn't a bug. Maybe it was crappy
design. Either way, I am steamed again.

We're in Beholder, right next to the Runnyeye entrance, luring in
Muddites, etc. We're in a fight, I'm down to half health, low mana.
But we're OK. An eye comes along, so we zone back into Runnyeye. OK so
far.

But I notice that one of my party members is missing, so I zone back
to find him. Maybe a dim idea. But hey, I won't get there.

"BLAM" I get a "zone down" message and am magically transported into
the middle of a tunnel some where. And a whole lot of goblins. And I
die. Wham, back to 10. Several hours wasted.

Now, I could accept death in beholder. And I could accept death on the
other side of the zone border in runnyeye. But I'm not happy about
dieing in some unknown tunnel somewhere. So I petition.

And, after lots of discussion, I get the answer "you were transported
to a SAFE place. (Don't worry, be happy.) Just your tough luck there
were goblins and goblin guards there, etc."

But if I'd known where I was I could have escaped. Is there some
goddamn reason they couldn't just bounce me back to the other side of
the zone where I'd started? Then I'd have known where I was.

"No, no, that's not a bug. Nothing we can do. =)"

OK, it's not a bug, it's a dim design decision. Nothing like dieing
because of a (cough) design decision.

On the whole, I can't see why I bother with this game. The experience,
taken as a whole, sucks. The game design sucks. (Admittedly, the 3D
and the networking are great.) I feel like I'm hitting myself on the
head with a hammer.

Sure, there are times when I'm healing folks and enjoying myself. But
nowhere near enough to justify the aggravation. Endless futile
struggle is what it feels like. (And it's not that I don't level. I
just don't enjoy the goddamn process.)

I wish reviewers would go back and review the whole game. Most of the
reviews seem to review the first 5-7 levels. Not very representative.
=(

Anyhow, my review: NOT RECOMMENDED. Unless you like continual
suffering and frustration.

Addictive, yes. Good, no.
