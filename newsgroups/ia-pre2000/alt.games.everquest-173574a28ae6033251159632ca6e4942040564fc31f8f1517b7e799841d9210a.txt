Sorry to hear your experience has frustrated you. One thing I must say though
if you pin all your enjoyment on this game from levelling, gaining stuff and
winning, you will get frustrated. This game is horrible for MedievalQuake. It
is a poor sword and sorcery combat siumulator and it should not be treated as
such. I've heard over and over again how "now I'm 20th level and bored".
Really there is only one answer...

Role-play.

We have been presented with a really well thought out world with an
interesting back story and the best role playing AI ever invented: other
people. If all you do is the mighty pursuit of the level, well franky I can't
imagine the game being fun for very long.

Final point, you may hate this answer but roleplay the solution of your
dilemma. Innoruuk has cursed you for your goodlyness. From an adventuring
party to regain you junk. Demand Innoruk's head be put on a pike. Get into a
religious 'debate' with a Tier'dal. ANYTHING has got to be better than killing
another minotaur.
