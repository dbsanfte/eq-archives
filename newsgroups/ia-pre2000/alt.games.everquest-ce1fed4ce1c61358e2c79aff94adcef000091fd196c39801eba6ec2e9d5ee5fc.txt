When you use a skill it will go up in level. Combat skills seem to go
up fairly quickly and when skills do increase you will get a message
saying something like " 1h/s has increased to 15 ". The messages are
sometimes hard to notice, but you will get them. Some skills seem to
take forever to go up - I've been using the sense heading skill
consistantly for the last 2 levels (I'm now a 4th lvl ranger) and its
only gone up to 6 (ie: my sense heading skill blows!).

I have also not spent more than 1 point on any skill, relying more on
use for it to increase. This has worked well for the combat and
tracking skill but less well for some others (forage comes to mind). I
believe the increases are also tied into your intellegence and have a
per level cap.

Hope that helps,
