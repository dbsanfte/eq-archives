>FYI - routers, firewalls, proxies servers, switches can be configured to
>accept or reject tracert, and ping or any other service that has been
>defined in a RFC for the internet, they can also be configured to look for
>the packet identifier for these services on a "nonstandard" port. Therefore
>if you tracert, ping to a location that has moved or removed this ability
>you will always get destination host/node unreachable. If you want more
>information go to www.internic.net and look up all of the rfc's that
>reference these functions.
