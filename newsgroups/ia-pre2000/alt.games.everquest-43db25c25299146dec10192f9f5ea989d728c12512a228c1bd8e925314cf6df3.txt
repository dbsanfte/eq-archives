> - How do you eat things?  I recall it being mentioned in the tutorial,
>   but I really don't want to go back through the whole thing just for
>   that.

Right click on items to quaff them. Some items are broke like mead
found in a cognac style decanter (still trying to figure this one out
hehe) on goblins, etc.. Some of them you may not even be able to drink
becuase they are broke. Eating food/water fr sustaining your character
is an automatic function, unlike UO. You just have to have them on
your character.

> - What is the "effects" button in the upper right?

This enables/disables 9toggle) the display of spell or other effects
your character is presently under the influence of. Like if someone
casts courage on you, you will have a big yellow shield in the upper
right corner of the view screen if effects is toggled "on".
