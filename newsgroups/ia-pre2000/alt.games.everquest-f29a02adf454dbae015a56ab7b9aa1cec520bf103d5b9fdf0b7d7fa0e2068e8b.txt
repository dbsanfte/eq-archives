>You can look at some of my earlier posts... I was really down on this game.
>I think it is important that I am just as vocal when things are working.  I
>now have had 3 uninterupted sessions in which I got on easily and played as
>long as I wanted.  I had no dumps to desktop or hangs.  I know others may
>still be having problems but hopefully there will be further improvements.
>The network problems are very minimal for me now.  Some occasional lag and
>I see some warping also with other characters.
>My crash problem was caused by a modification I made a week ago.  If I had
>been playing other games, I probably would have seen crashes there also.
>The problem was that I was running a Pentium II 300 with 100 Mhz bus speed.
>Some of my cards or memory just weren't up to snuff with the higher bus
>speed.  Once I lowered the bus speed back to 66 Mhz I haven't had a single
>crash of EverQuest.
>
>I have 3 characters in the game.  My most developed is a Monk character.
>I've done a couple of NPC quests, one of which earned me a magic sash (like
>a training belt).  The combat is sufficient for me... about as good as I
>would expect for real-time.  I can't see an effective way to use movement in
>combat yet.  I need to do some experimentation and research on some of the
>fan sites.  The way combat works is that you press and autocombat button and
>then move near a targeted creature.  Your swings happen automatically unless
>you have some kind of special attack.  In my case, I punch with both hands
>on auto and I have a kick button which I have to press with the mouse.  You
>can see your own health bar and your enemies health bar.  If you start to
>lose you can run away but many enemies can keep up and still attack.  Wolves
>are expecially good at this as my two or three corpses outside of Freeport
>will attest.  If you can make it to the city gates, the guards will dispatch
>your enemy (you get no experience or loot for this).  Sitting replenishes
>your strength.  You can also get healing.  If you form a group you share
>experience points.  I know everyone is raving about the weather... I'll add
>my kudos to theirs.  I got in a thunderstorm last night (game night) and it
>was very cool.
>
>All in all I find the game very addictive and full of potential.  I haven't
>been talking a lot to others or been in any groups but I look forward to
>doing this or maybe talking some friends into getting the game once I'm sure
>things are fixed.
