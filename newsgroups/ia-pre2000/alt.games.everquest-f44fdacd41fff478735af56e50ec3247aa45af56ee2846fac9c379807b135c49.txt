> Okay, I've been playing a couple of days, and have some questions I
> haven't been able to find the answers to:
>
>  - How do you eat things?  I recall it being mentioned in the tutorial,
>    but I really don't want to go back through the whole thing just for
>    that.

Eating (and drinking) is automatic.  If you have food and drink in your
inventory, even in containers, when you need to eat or drink, you just do
it, 'in background'.  Booze, on the other hand, you must actually drink, and
like all objects that are usable, you single-right-click on them to 'use'
the mead, or whatever.
>
>  - What is the "effects" button in the upper right?
>

This turns on or off the little 'boxes' showing what
spells/poisons/diseases/etc are currently affecting your character, up in
the upper middle right part of the screen.

>  - How do you get a numeric representation of your skill level(s)?  I've
>    seen several references on usenet and the web to skill level as a
>    number (15, 17, 44, etc), while all I see are the vague descriptions
>    (awful, below average, etc).
>
You only see numeric value of your skills when you gain in them, by use.
You have to make a note of it, to know what it currently is.  There are,
however, known ranges for the text 'values', so you can use them to know a
rough level.  Once a skill is in the range that costs to practice, you could
also make up a list of what cost equates to what value, I guess, though it'd
be tedious to do.
