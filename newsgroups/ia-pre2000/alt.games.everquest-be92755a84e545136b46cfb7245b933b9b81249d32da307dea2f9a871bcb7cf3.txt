The NPC's aren't generally talkative unless they are quest givers. You
are doing the /hail thing right? I would If I were you go into the
options chat section and turn on /ooc and /shout.  This will allow you
to hear people yakking in the background which may help you get
oriented. Also remap your autoattack key to something less obtrusive
then "a" and just start talking to people. The lower levels are a free
fora ll because at level 1-5 grouping is counterproductive. Once you
get to level 6 or so you will be better off grouping and will find
that shouting for groups works. Also play around with the /who command
it can really help you get to know who is in the zone etc. Also allows
you to see the regulers etc. once you start getting to know people you
can add them to your friends list as well. You don't  really lose
anything dieing before level 3 so playa round with the interface until
you get it down pat.
