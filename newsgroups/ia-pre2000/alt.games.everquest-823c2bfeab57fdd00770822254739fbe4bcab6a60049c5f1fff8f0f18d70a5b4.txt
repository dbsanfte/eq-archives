I thought Star Wars I - The Phantom Menace was little more than a 2 hour
commercial created to sell toys to 10 year olds. The acting was stiff and
putrid, and the story (if you could call it that) was designed for 10 year
olds as was the incredibly shallow and juvenile attempts at humor (including
that shameful JarJar character).

Having said all that Phantom Menace was visually interesting at times, but
certainly would rate no more that a 5 on a scale of 1 to 10 with 10 being
the highest. For comparative purposes I'd give the original Star Wars and
the Empire Strikes back an 8.5. I'd give Return of the Jedi (otherwise known
as the force meets the muppets) a 6.5. So you can see that in my opinion all
three original movies were better than this one, and the first two were by
far the best of all.
