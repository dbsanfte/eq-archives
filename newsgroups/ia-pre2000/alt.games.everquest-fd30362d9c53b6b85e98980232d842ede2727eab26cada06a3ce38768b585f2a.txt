> > Reroll stats when dying??? now whats that... then you can go die a million

You can "reroll stats" in games that have RANDOM character generation system,
but in skill/point based chargen systems, this will not be the case. I don't
like the idea of random stats anyway so wouldn't put in in my MMRPG. If you
reincarnate over and over without going out and playing the char, in my
theoretical MMRPG it's not going to help you (in fact the time you waste in
trying to do it ends up hurting you), because you are going to start the game
over with the same exp. you had when you died. I guess if I gave a bonus for
"dying is an experience" then it could happen, but there could be limits
placed on this too.... I mean, if you're dying every few minutes, just how
much more of an experience is it to die another time? So as you die more often
the benefits of gaining experience of death lessen. Also, you could make it
where you only gain experience if the WAY in which you died was different
from previous deaths. "Ok, last time I got hung. Boy, that wasn't any fun.
Now this time it's death by fireball. Ack!"
