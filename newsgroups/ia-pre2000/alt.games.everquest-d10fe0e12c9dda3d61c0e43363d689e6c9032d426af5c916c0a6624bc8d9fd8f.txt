On Tue, 1 Jun 1999, John Galt wrote:

) I thought Star Wars I - The Phantom Menace was little more than a 2 hour
) commercial created to sell toys to 10 year olds. The acting was stiff and
) putrid, and the story (if you could call it that) was designed for 10 year
) olds as was the incredibly shallow and juvenile attempts at humor (including
) that shameful JarJar character).

I finally saw it this weekend, and I was a little disappointed.  Without
spoiling the plot, it really just seemed like a disjointed sequence of
subplots with no overwhelming story (well, almost no overwhelming story,
though if you hadn't seen the other three movies you'd probably miss it).
Taken on it's own as the first movie in a series, there wasn't enough
background to really give a sense of what is going on.  It also appears
the audience they are shooting for is younger than ANH or ESB - more on
par with RotJ and all those obnoxious ewoks.

Don't get me wrong, it had its moments and by applying my knowledge of
whats going to happen later I was certainly able to fill in all the holes.
Overall though, for a two+ hour movie, I really felt like I only saw about
half of a movie.

Also, Jar Jar wasn't nearly as annoying as I'd heard, though it certainly
was distracting trying to figure out WTF he was saying sometimes.  Oh, and
frankly, these climactic light-saber battles are getting just a little
stale.  How about a good thumb-wrestling match?
