On another, related note... Has anyone who has read the novels (I haven't)
heard of a female Jedi? That's sort of one thing that's burning my ass a
little... Yeah, it's traditional for "knights" to be male, but, hey... there

matrilinial considering that it's sometimes difficult to ascertain
paternity. Much more stability in a matrilinial society... but I digress.)

females than males. Admittedly, in the higher species that trend reverses.

female-dominated. I am having a very difficult time justifying the omission
of female Jedis. (Lucas chose not to feature women in power very frequently,
but did give them a heck of a slice of the pie considering Leia and Amidala
are two exceptionally powerful monarchs.)

What's up wi' dat?

(I much prefer the gender balance of Star Trek and B5... especially Star
