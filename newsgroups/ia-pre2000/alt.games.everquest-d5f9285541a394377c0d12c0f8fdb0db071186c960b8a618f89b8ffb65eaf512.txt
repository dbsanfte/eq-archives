Come join us on Rellos Zek.

Granted the same-race faction hit isn't -quite- in yet, but posts from Brad have
made it very clear that this something that they are currently working on adding
in the near future.  Looting is limited to coins on your body and one single
item, so getting whacked does NOT mean that you lose everything and have to
start over.  On the other hand, some morons like to corpse-camp, so running back
to reclaim your "stuff" should not be your #1 priority after dying.  Let it sit
for a half hour or so, tip a few ales in the local pub, and wander back after
they get tired of waiting for you.

There is a +/- 4 level limit on PvP, so those worries about a super powerful
player killing you are almost completely unfounded.

There were three MAJOR problems with Rallos Zek when the game went final, and so
far one has been solved completely (looting other player's corpses), one is
slated to be fixed shortly (same-race newbie killing), and the last (corpse
camping) is easily solved by utilizing a little common sense.
