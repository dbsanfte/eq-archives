"Reb" Ruster wrote in message <376d8614...@news.earthlink.net>...

>I suspect than Lucas knew exactly where the story was heading.

I don't believe that at all. I believe he knew the overall story, but I
don't think he knew the details of it.

>1. Who was Luke Skywalker's and Princess Leiha's mother?

Queen Amidala. The relationship between Amidala and Anakin is clearly
foreshadowed in Episode I.

>2. If C3PO was built by Anakin Skywalker, why did the 'droid not
>mention this fact in the original film?

Because:
a) Lucas didn't know that he was going to make C3P0 be built (in the past)
by Anakin
and/or
b) C3P0 might be utterly  unaware that Darth Vader and Anakin Skywalker are
the same person. Nobody but Obi Wan and Luke's aunt and uncle seem to know
this. Leia obviously didn't know it, and she was in posession of the droids.
If the droids knew it, then Leia would have known. Since Leia did NOT know
that Darth was Anakin, we can assume that neither C3P0 nor R2D2 knew it.

>3. If the Jedi and the Force were so powerful in Ep 1, why did
>everyone disbelieve this in Ep VI?

My guess: Not everybody knew a Jedi knight, even in Episode I. How many of
them do you think are running around the galaxy? Even in the time of the
Jedi's, they are somewhat legendary. Just my take on it, though.

>4. What cause Anakin to turn toword the Dark Side? He seemed like a
>nice, unassuming kid.

This is also clearly foreshadowed in Episode 1. The Jedi council observed
that Anakin was filled with fear. They easily sensed this fear in him and
therefore turned down Qui-Gon Jinn's application for Anakin's training. Fear
leads to hate, hate leads to the dark side.

>5. R2D2. Where the heck does he fit into all this?


I always felt that R2D2 knew MUCH MUCH more than he let on. If, in the first
three movies, you pay close attention to the reactions that R2D2 gets,
you'll see this. C3P0 is especially frustrated by R2D2 because R2 usually
just takes off, based on information that he has but won't share with C3P0.
Sorta gets' C3P0's metal panties bunched in a wad.

My guess? R2D2 knows about Darth but won't tell. Pure speculation on my
part. R2D2 may beep and whirr, but he has a fully-developed personality in
there somewhere. He's not just a scrubber.
