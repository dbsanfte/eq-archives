I'd guess there are a few reasons why the game seems "unfriendly" at the
moment.  The main one being that Graphical MUDs differ from the text based
in the demographics they support - a text MUD is *very* community focused
and this is a common link amongst most players - in graphical MUDs there is
a much higher representation of gamers who aren't that interested in long
social interaction.

Added to that basis, Everquest has a shitty communications interface and
even many of the more social people may be wanting a quick flier gainign a
few levels before settling into a more community based role.
