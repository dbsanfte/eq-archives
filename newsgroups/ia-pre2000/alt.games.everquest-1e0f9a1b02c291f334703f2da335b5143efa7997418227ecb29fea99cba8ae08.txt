Well I went to work and read both cases, long time sense I read crimainal
cases.  To say either of those cases say the constitution supports jury
nullification is ridiculous.  They are death penalty cases that say that it
is ok for states to pass laws that let juries decide on life or death for
murderers.  So what. I personally think that is great that juries are given
that discretion by "law".  Jury nullification is where a juror decides that
he doesn't agree with the law and fails to follow the evidence
and the law provided to him.  An example might come to mind that someone
might not agree with the current state of drug laws and not be willing to
convict someone of  pot possesion even though the guy was caught dead
smoking , buying, or selling it.  Those people get kicked of juries when
they state not only do they not agree with the law invovled in the case but
would and could not convict even if the law and the eveidence supported
that.
      IN death penalty cases people can be on a jury if they disagree with
the death penalty but agree that if the law and facts support it to vote for
the death penealty, those that cannot are excluded and to my mind rightly
so.  You can't have individual citizens making the law the way they think it
is.  That's a nice start towards anarchy.  No I am sorry those cases don't
even slightly approve of jury nullification and as I said and you have not
pointed out anywhere the constitutuion supports it.  The constituion it is
pretty damn clear that it is the legislature(Congress) that makes the law
not juries.  As a trial lawyer who really tries lots of cases have I
actually seen jury nullifcation,no.  I have seen some pretty convaluted
discussions amongst jurors to stretch the facts to what the conclusion they
wanted, but not nullification.  The few nullification examples I have read
about have been over turned on apppeal.

believer in the jury system but not to the level to let them make law.   If
the law allows for abortions I don't want some nutt job anti abortion person
on a jury causeing problems for someone trying to get an abortion.   I hate

never sit on a jury invovliving drugs because I wouldn't be able to put my

drug case and then vote not to convict because I didn't believe in it would
