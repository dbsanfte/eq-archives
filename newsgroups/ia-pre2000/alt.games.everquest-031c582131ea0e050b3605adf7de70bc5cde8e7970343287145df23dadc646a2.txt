This simply isn't true.  Your modem speed is only important for your
connection to your ISP.  After that your modem makes not one single whit of
difference where the required throughput is lower than that the modem to
ISP link is capable of.

If the game needs a steady throughput of 1k per second, then it is playable
on a 14k4 modem.  It is *always* the ISP that is responsible for keeping
the modem saturated with data.

Assuming no problems at the game end.  Any trouble you find when your modem
speed is more than required for the throughput then either your ISP is
failing or the Internet bandwidth *purchased by your ISP* is not enough.

So 28k8 is enough for Everquest.  In fact a good ISP at 28k8 will provide a
better gaming environment than a cablemodem or ADSL link without enough
Intenret bandwidth purchased by the cablemodem or ADSL provider.
