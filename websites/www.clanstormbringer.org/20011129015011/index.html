Sangreal
The word Sangreal, pronounced 'san-'grA is derived from the Middle English Sangrayll, from the Middle French Saint Graal and although the literal translation is Saint Grail, the word means Holy Grail. Within the Guild Sangreal you have found the perfect friendship. We hold honor, truth, and friendship high and above all else. Our relationships forged in Sangreal are like finding the Holy Grail!
