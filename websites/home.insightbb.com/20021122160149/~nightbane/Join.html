** The Application Process **

To start your application process you need to receive a sponsor within Nightbane. This sponsor will be your mentor as well as a source of information for the officers about you. They will help you adapt into Nightbane and explain any questions you might have.

One of the officers will then interview you. This is for us to get a general idea as to your character, your reasons for playing Everquest, and why you are looking to join Nightbane. This also gives us a chance to explain how Nightbane works in more detail, so you will be fully prepared for everything that goes on. We move very fast and do not explain many of the intuitive day to day things that go on in the guild.

After you are interviewed, you will be invited along with us to raid. At your first couple raids you will be temp tagged so you can follow along without having to get instructions secondhand. Based on your performance at these raids, you may be asked to become an applicant.

At this point you will be allowed to leave your guild tag on and should post for board access. No applicant will receive loot over a full member, but you will be allowed to accumulate points toward our looting system. This process will last anywhere from 2 - 4 weeks. At the end of this period you will either be cut from Nightbane, or asked to join as a full member.
