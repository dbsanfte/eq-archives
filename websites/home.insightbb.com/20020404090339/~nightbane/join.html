** The Application Process **

To Start your application process you need to receive a sponser within Nightbane. This Sponsor will contact the Guild Leader. An officer will be assigned to interview you and then your app process can begin.

After your interviewed an App thread will be posted on our boards and you will be invited along with us to events. At your first events you will be temp tagged so you can easily follow the guild event. Based on your performance at these raids you may be asked to become a full applicant.

At this point you will be allowed to leave your guild tag on and you will be assigned a mentor. This mentor will make sure you know what your doing at raids, and will also make sure you're up-to-date on how your application process it going. No Applicant will receive loot over a full member, but you will be allowed to accumlate points toward our looting system during your time as an App. This process will last anywhere from 2 - 4 weeks. At the end of this period you will either be cut from Nightbane, or asked to join as a Full Member.
