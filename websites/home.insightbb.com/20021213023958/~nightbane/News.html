News: 11-08-2002 - Berginyon Got Update?

A new expansion came out, and I have not updated the page yet, what's the deal? Well with every new expansion comes a far increased workload, and I just had not gotten to this yet. I was caught up in the flurry of leveling with the rest of the guild. I think we proved nicely we are far from a guild of slackers. We had the first level 65 on the server, Gaash. I wouldn't normally be so shameless, but these people worked hard, with many others not named here, to get the job done we asked of them. Our current raid average is 64, we will keep working on that though. 8p

So we have the levels, what have we been doing with them? Well, a lot. We have been exploring PoP almost exclusively, getting flags, trying new things. The loot has been subpar, compared to say what we were getting in VT, but everyone is having a lot of fun trying these new encounters. To me that is the most important part. I have no problems leaving Luclin behind.
