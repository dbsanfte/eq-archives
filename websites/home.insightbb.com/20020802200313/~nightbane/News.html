News: 7-18-2002 - Berginyon Today ended up being an unexpected patch day. We started out in the Deep, and holy god, attendance was staggering. We musta done something to get people excited. Burrower, well was changed for like the 300th time. One change is kinda funny, which I will leave you to figure out on your own. The other changes, well they keep gimping Burrower and his ring every other patch. It's frustrating when VI made a perfectly balanced, hard, but fun encounter, and then gimps it to hell over and over. Make more mobs spawn at once, and make the spawn time between waves shorter like it used to be. Bring it back to it's former glory when it was FUN!

We then headed to Ssra to do our usual clearings. We started out with High Priest. I am not sure what to say about this fight exactly. It was one of the most amazing High Priest fights Nightbane has ever been apart of. How should I put it? From the Jail, you have to kill around 10 or 12 mobs before you can even get to the High Priest room. Lets just say, High Priest was dead and looted, with everyone back in the Jail before the FIRST Golem had even repopped. The fight was something like 8 minutes total. Considering we have had 36+ minute High Priest fights, that is some DPS.

Finally, it was time to try Emperor again. In many ways the second kill is more important than the first. We had to prove to ourselves and everyone else, this was no lucky win. We had to repeat our performance, and did we ever. Tonight we had 60 people. The fight was 21 minutes, with only a single death. Well let me put it another way, we lost a ranger. No healing in the world is gonna keep rangers alive, usually it's a GOOD sign when one drops. We tried to kill Sollador too, but he is getting better at evading me. Next run Sollador, I will have you. (Sollador managed to get himself caught in a bug and somehow lived, shrug.) So those two offset, and I cannot really call it a perfect fight.

New loot, did someone say new loot? Yeah we got two new loots, well two of the same item. Monks, prepare to drool. The winners of these two bad boys were Munkeyfeet and Jonnyman.



Emperor also dropped another Acrylia Handled Broadsword, pictured from last night. Vann picked that item up. Wizards were not so lucky. No Garrison's Superior Sundering on either kill. Fix this please VI, for as hard as he is, and being he is the ONLY mob that drops this spell, he should drop it every time, or nearly every time.

High Priest loot was also amazingly great tonight, so I figured I would post it too.





Marzin's Mark also dropped. We have been killing High Priest for months, and up until this week, a shield had never dropped on this server. Tonight we had the second shield in a row drop. Neat stuff, I am hoping it keeps up. Asilum scored the belt, I snagged the nifty Bloodbath, and Gaash got the second Shield to ever drop on the server. (Allis won the first, but was never posted, so grats to her as well) Sinlainu grabbed the spell.

To those that were asking about last night. Kreiger won the Acrylia Broadsword, Vann won the Envenomed Moccasins, and Daebola got the amazing Shawl.
