News: 04-01-2002
Today was another busy day. We started off by killing Dain Frostreaver IV. Then we went over to our friend the A Burrower Beast Parasite in the Deep. At first he did not want to come out and play. Mysteriously, the ring stopped spawning mobs on us, twice. Aprils Fools joke on us by Verant? Conspiracy theories started spawning rampantly, and many people told the guild "They knew why the ring was bugging." In truth I am not sure why, other than maybe it just being another example of Verant's lack of playtesting and / or coding ability? Either way, after much bitching, the officers herded NightBane around, got the ring started for the third time, and the beast decided to come out. The highlight of the run for me was hearing our MT say he had gotten a 6800 Complete Heal. Maybe we are taking this efficiency thing too far? We still managed to take down the beast with zero deaths. He dropped a few nice items, here are a couple new ones to Karana.





The Tunic went to Andelia. After she was hit on, leered at, and thoroughly taken advantage of by our male and female members, I was able to take a fast pic.



After that, it was time for revenge. Shei Vinitras has been bugging me, especially after our last shot. He had no visible life left when we wiped out. So we heard he was up, and headed to Akheva for some payback. NightBane was angry, we came into that fight guns blazing, there was no talk of losing. We knew he was going to die. Now this is a DT'ing mob, so it isn't exactly possible to say we had zero deaths, but the fight was nearly flawless. Other than the few DT's he got off, no one died. So we bring you yet another Karana first.

Shei Vinitras is Dead.



For as hard as he was however, his loot was rather craptacular. I believe Wimbus was happy with the robe, but come on, Rhag 1 drops better items. Here are the pics if you are curious.





After that Rhag 1 popped, so we took him and Rhag 2 out. Nothing special, nice easy finish to a full hard day. On a side note, something I have not seen anyone mention. Moonfire is dropping, and even more amazing, it is dropping off more than one mob! So druids rejoice or do whatever you do. We have gotten 5 copies over the past week or so, so it does not seem to be rare either.

Finally, a late addition as to what we did over the weekend. It was fairly eventful as well, we got in a Sontalak, Klandicar, Velketor, Ikatiar, and Eashan kill, as well a reunion with our favorite goddess.

Tunare is Dead.



So what is left for us to do now? We haven't tried that new Arch Lich everyone is complaining about, sounds like a challenge. The High Priest has been calling our names as well, saying he is going to tie up our halflings and shave off there foot hair. We just can't have that, over in NightBane country we like a challenge, and it seems those two mobs are on deck.
