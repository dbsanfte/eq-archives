The Aredhel
Guild Charter

 

Greetings fellow explorers. We are the Childrn of Norrath gathered from near and far. We come together to explore our beautiful world and along the way we strive to correct all injustices we come across in able to preserve our great lands. Home is where we are at any given moment, as we are true explorers at heart. And as such, it is our goal to visit all lands, known and unknown, north to south and east to west.

Whether you be Elf or Ogre, or one of the many other wonderful races that inhabit this great world, you are welcome among us as long as you revere peace and have the heart of an explorer.....
