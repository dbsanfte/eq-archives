Books Online


  • Gutenberg Project
    The Gutenberg project is a huge attempt to put all public domain literature in plain ASCII text and distribute it freely in an electronic form. This page is an index to Gutenberg documents already available. There are also links here to other Etext sources.
  • The Gibson Digital Library
    A large and varied collection of online books and literary links.
  • Bookport
    They said it best themselves: "Bookport is a World Wide Web site dedicated to offering readers Internet access to books. Bookport is sponsored by Fortuity Consulting, a consultant to publishers with an interest in using the Internet as a market channel. Bookport provides immediate access to online editions of books; online ordering of bound books; and information about books in the form of catalog descriptions, excerpts, and publishers' marketing materials."
  • Internet Book Information Center
    "The granddaddy of book-related sites on the Internet"--Publishers' Weekly, June 1995
