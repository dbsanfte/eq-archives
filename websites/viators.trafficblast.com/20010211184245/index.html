In Memory of the Guild of Viators

July 24, 1999 to August 27, 2000

The Great Merger

On August 27th, 2000 8pm Central Time Guild of Viators threw down their travel packs and closed down their caravans, by order of the new Grand Master Gozaro Sehalfling, to Join forces with the 
Lords of the Ters'Tai. May the memories of our travels live on and our fight against the evils of Innorruk ring true.

Honor, Fidelity and Courtesy.
