Hail and well met...

    As the struggle between good and evil rages across the realm of Norrath, a group of honorable adventurers has gathered together to maintain order and goodness, lest all of Norrath be consumed by the tempest of chaos and greed.  Pure evil will never be driven from the realms, but it can be kept from harming the innocent and, in this way,  the greater good will prevail. We promote the causes of good and answer the call for heroes.  We ensure that evil is not allowed to upset the fragile balance.

      If ever you feel the need to become a Harper, contact us in the realm of Brell Serilis or through the mystical messaging spell (e-mail).  Also, we seek to form alliances with guilds that have common interests.
