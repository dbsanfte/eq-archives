Welcome to the Tome of the Skyfire!

You have opened the Tome of the SkyFire, a Multi-Guild Storyline on Everquest. The purpose of this site is to chronicle the events, stories and lives of the participants in an epic roleplaying storyline taking place on the Karana Everquest server. The characters involved represent multiple factions from a number of guilds and individuals on the server.

The Forum is the place where the can participants discuss the goings-on in an out-of-character fashion. Stories and Lore is the fabric of the story, the background and events that have occured so far in the Skyfire Saga. There are Guidelines that all of the participants must follow. The Characters and Factions will give the newcomer a small introduction to all the various personalities and guilds who make up this epic. Anyone on the server can Participate by filling out the form, or Contact this site's administrator.
