Death and Betrayal
by Haemish MacLennan, Fudil and Cassia Spell Singer, of the Silver Daggers

From the Journals of Haemish MacLennan:

"The message came as a surprise to me.... I'd not heard from my father's friend Maccus in years, since soon after my father's untimely death. It was Maccus who had given me over to the care of the shaman Queeg, before I'd chosen the path of the sword. Maccus who had warned me of the danger of the world as I left Halas to find my glory as a mercenary and sword for hire. I looked again at the yellowed parchment in my hands with Maccus's seal on it. I thanked the young Dagger Temek for bringing this message to me, from the frozen tundra of Halas all the way across the continent to the Seafarer's Roost in Freeport. He could have dispatched it by bard, but felt it important enough to bring it to me directly. I looked at Temek, with his thin moustache and malformed ears that marked his heritage as partially elven, and handed him a few platinum coins for his trouble. I saluted him, but couldn't help but notice his rahter mechanical reactions to me, and his shifty eyes poring over the message in my hands. I would have to watch Temek. Closely.

The message chilled me like no wind of Everfrost could... Maccus had something to give me, something from my father, an amulet. My father had given him the amulet days before his death, with dire warnings, and now Maccus must give it to me. He had forseen his own death, felt it lurking near him, so must discharge his duty to my father before it was too late. He spoke only vaguely of the amulet, but I had heard the stirrings in the underground of late. The Guardians of Innoruuk had been searching for an amulet, for artifacts of great power. This could not be a coincidence. I summoned a bard to me, and sent him to gather the Daggers who I could trust to this mission. Fudil and Dougglas came to me then, offering their lives to accomplish this mission. I remembered that Maccus had a daughter, yet I knew nothing of her... I must make sure she is cared for if the worst comes to pass.

But I must also know about this amulet, and why our enemies could be looking for this amulet. I dispatched Cassia to research artifacts of power which may be connected to some form of amulet. Though it was a thin thread, it was all that we had."

From the research journals of Cassia Spellsinger:

The news of the theft...of Temek's theft of the amulet was foul indeed. That he had not only murdered, but betrayed his guild for the amulet only lent more credence to the importance of the amulet. Whether he had been hired to commit that atrocity, or was simply planning on selling it to the highest bidder was unknown to her. And perhaps Gamlaen was correct in that he had been forced by great evil into those acts. Ultimately, it was unimportant. What was important was the that amulet was recovered. She trusted her brethren to hunt him down and retrieve it. When they did, she was certain she could determine not only the origin, but the powers and purpose of the amulet. Though there were many amulets of power throughout the dusty pages she had been searching, they were all distinct. It seemed that when someone chose to hide such a thing with a disguise, they hid it as a normal necklace, rather than disguising is as another item of power. This made a certain amount of sense to her, but more importantly, it meant that she could identify why it was so important.

It had become abundantly clear to her that she was not the first person who had come to Qeynos to research the amulet. As in Freeport, those making inquiries had not been on the side of good and light. They were servants of evils better left unnamed. She could tell that they had made some discoveries, but not what they were, nor how they had made them. The trail continuously led to Neriak, and she had finally decided that her search must continue there. She had written a few letters, made arrangement for her quarters to be kept and sealed, and for messages to be retained here for her. She left much of what she valued behind. This time, when she purchased a teleport, she was careful to not do so in her true form. Her dwarven was exceptional, and she rather liked being short.

She wandered merrily into Freeport, then vanished, and made her way back to Nektulos. In the darkening shadows of the forbidding forest, she shifted form again, this time becoming the dark skinned mockery of the high elf. She worked her tongue around a few Ter Dail phrases, warming up, making sure she hadn't developed an accent, and then strode into the forest. The tall shadows greedily swallowed her slender form."
