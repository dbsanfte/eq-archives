Tome of the Skyfire Stories and Lore

These guidelines were created to provide a more indepth and fair standard for roleplaying within the Tome of the Skyfire Saga. Although they may take a little getting used to at first, we feel that they clarify the roleplaying requirements needed to participate.

Prologue

An Unprovoked Attack - The Silver Daggers leader, Haemish, is ambushed along the road in Butcher's Block by the Guardians of Innoruuk.

Dogedme's Disappearance - An ally and friend of the Silver Daggers disappears and is captured by Katrya.

The Rogue's Rumour - A wood elf rogue tells a Silver Dagger of evil machinations.

The Messenger - Haemish dispatches a guild brother to take a message to an underground investigator, and another attack is made.

Two Old Enemies Meet Again - Novant and Haemish, men with a mysterious past, meet again, and a battle is joined anew.

Dogedme's Return - The missing Seeker of Knowledge, Dogedme is rescued, but how much of him is left is still unknown.

Death and Betrayal - Haemish receives a message from an old friend, and dispatches two Daggers to help him, only to find that the Daggers have been betrayed.

The Hunt for Temek - In which a group of Daggers seek out the traitor Temek, and seek to recover the amulet, only to be opposed by a group of Dark Elves seeking the same thing.

The Daggers Strike Back - Haemish and the Daggers set a trap for the enemies who have been plauging them, and consults his Oracle.

A Rogue's Exit - The Daggers come to Temek's rescue, and a new enigma arrives on the scene.
