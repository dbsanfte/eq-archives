The Hunt for Temek
by Fudil, Shaman, Rbecka, Warrior, Cassia Spellsinger, Enchanter, and Haemish MacLennan, Clan Chieftan of the Silver Daggers

From Fudil's journal:

"We started the night out by meeting at the tower in Qeynos Hills. By way of we, I mean myself, Rbecka, Cacheclipe and Oakdale. We met with Haemish to find out the rules and the starting points. We then traveled to qeynos, and Rbecka found our first contact in the wizards area. He was an apprentice to a powerful wizard. The Apprentice didnt give us much but he did point out a few important items.

First off, his master wouldn't even look at the item, or speak to us. Secondly he pointed us in the direction of Freeport. We left for Freeport without delay. On our journey we went across the plains of Karana without incident, then traveled through the Gorge, and into Runnyeye, where I got lost for a minute but luckily nothing happened. We journeyed on into the Mistey Thicket and into Rivervale which is where we lost our first member. Oakdale was interested in the area and asked to leave. We, of course, could not fault our young companion for not wanting to continue on, so we parted ways at that time.

We traveled on through the Kithocor Woods, though how we escaped with no incidents is beyond me. Traveling across the Commonlands was as uneventful as traveling the Plains of Karana. As we enter within sight of freeport, Rbecka was attacked by a lioness. We outran the dumb animal so as not to disturb mother nature.

Entering Freeport we saw very few people compared to how many were normally there. We went staight to where our last contact recommended. We met this new wizard at the Wizards spire. He was a rude person who didn't know much other then what the other apprentice had known. We had traveled across the continent to find that where we really need to go was Erudin. At this point we despaired then myself and rbecka remebered we had left part of our selves in Qeynos, so we could cut our journey in half. Bidding Cacheclipe goodday we traveled by way of mystical forces back to Qeynos. In Qeynos, Rbecka got extremely tired from all the travel and because of the mystical way in which she had just journeyed, so i bid her farewell.

I had to complete the journey; I made a vow to have the amulet in my hand this day and i would. I traveled over thru Erud's Crossing with no incident. As I got to Erudin, something felt wrong to me the Devil was there! I traveled to the libarry seaching for anwsers. Unfortunately there were no anwers to the questions I was asking, but i did catch sight of the rouge Temek running into Toxxula Forest, so I chased after him.

I demanded that he give back the amulet or face the consequnces. During the chase I was attacked by a Stupid sorceror named Randdall who I dispacthed without too much pain. As soon as I had knocked Ranndall out, Temek attacked. Though it was a long, drawn out battle, I was the Victor. I recieved the Amulet and spared Temek's life, so he could be brought to justice. During our fight, he kept mumbling about the amulet.

He said it was named Asherok. He also said in a different voice "DEATH TO THE SKYFIRE BORN, FOR THEY ARE THE WAY TO LIFE AND DEATH." I am not a scholar, so I will leave that to them to explain the meaning of those words, but when he said them they chilled me to the bone. Unfortunatley, before Ii could totally recover, I was attcked yet again by another caster. Althazar, I believe he said was his name. He said that the amulet belonged to him and that he wanted it back, I had no choice but to honor his challenge. I fought long and hard with him, but he had too much for my weakened state. As i lay between death and life, Temek came and challenged this dark elf bastard to save my life. He was vanquished to save me. As I limped away, I realized Al had saved Ranndal from justice and Temek had saved me from bondage but I screamed to them to watch themselves for I would come to them to get the amulet back and my brother back. "

From Rbecka's Private Diary:

"The events of that journey were retold to our chieftan by Fudil quite well, yet, there is something that comes to mind after reviewing them. Nearing the end of our journey, it was as if some magic was cast upon me, draining me of my strength and causing my mind to go into a dull, downward spiral resulting in a sleep-like trance. After a bit of research, and a discussion with a magical friend of mine that lives very close to where I was born, it has occured to me that it may not have been sleep that I sought after.

More than likely, I was placed under a curse of some sort that had a very similar effect. May this have been caused by the powers that lurk deep within the mystical secrets of the ever powerful, yet illusive amulet? Or were those same magical wonderments somehow twisted by those who were in possesion of it at that time? It is highly possible.

Shortly after I became stricken with what I would call "drowsy", I attempted to contact my dear friend by using a means of communication he developed. It was a means of communication I do not fully understand, but it has always worked in the past, so I tried it again. Odd.. no reply, no "feeling" that he was even there. Could this mean he was dead? Or was he to under the spell that effected me."

From the Annals of the Silver Daggers, response of Haemish MacLennan:

"I am greatly distressed indeed by this news. Fudil came to me, still bearing the scars of his fight with the vile Athalzar. Console yourself in this brother, ye did well. Taking on two opponents with such vile magics in one eve, ye are lucky to have survived.

It sounds to me as if Temek was not in control of himself at the time. And what he said has me disturbed as well. On a few instances during the attacks on myself, I have heard this "Skyfire Born" before. And in Cassia's research on the amulet, she also heard the "Skyfire" referenced, though she was not sure in conjunction to what. If confirms my fears that all the attacks upon us have been related, and though our enemies may not be working together, they do seem to be after some of the same things.

We must rescue our fallen brother Temek from the clucthes of his captors. Even if it means going into hell itself. Only he has the answers to this riddle of the Skyfire born. I may also need to consult the gypsy oracle about this reference, as it seems she may know more than I originally thought. "
