Dogedme's Disappearance

        "Who leads the Silver Daggers?" She grabbed his chin and tilted his head so that he was looking at her. She'd lost track of how long he'd been hanging from his arms like this. Torture was hard work, and the days and nights seemed to all blend together. The subject could never be allowed to sleep more than half an hour at a time, and that meant his tormentor couldn't sleep either. She did know that it had been more than two days since she'd let him eat, and nearly that since he'd had anything to drink.

        He was slow to wake, and she rewarded him for his impudence with a backfist across his face. It struck, as she had intended, his broken cheekbone, waking him instantly. She kept a tight rein on her clerics, allowing them to only heal as much as was necessary to keep him alive. It was best, of course, to err on the side of healing too much. Inflicting more damage is not only easy, but pleasurable to do, and resurrecting the dead was not nearly as rewarding.

        "Who leads the Silver Daggers?" She asked again, letting her irritation at having to repeat herself show plainly on her face. He knew that the more irritated she got, the more pain she inflicted.

        "Please, Mistress, anything but that...you ask too much of me. I'll crawl behind you again, on the ground, eat the dog's food...anything but that. Please." She loved it when he begged, especially for her to humiliate him. Humiliation was part and parcel of torture and conditioning. It destroyed the sense of self, just as lack of sleep, and constant fear stripped away the less important things in life like loyalty. She would never permit him any shred of dignity, which is why he had been hanging here naked and in agony for most of the time he had been here. She only let him down to hurt or humiliate him.

        "I ask too much of you?" She demanded, her eyes flashing angrily. She moved close to him, pausing a beat. He knew pain was coming, but not from where, or how much. She slid her hands up his arms, to his hands, and his eyes widened with fear. In a fit of rage earlier this day (he'd called upon that life nuturing witch Tunare to save him), she had smashed both of his hands onto beds of nails shattering bone and tearing flesh. The clerics had wanted to heal him, but she had forbidden them, taunting the goddess of love to do it herself. Hate would always be stronger than love. She straightened his shattered fingers with her own, jarring bone against nerve. She forced her fingers through his, and began to squeeze.

        He screamed so nicely. She just squeezed harder, until his throat was raw from screaming, and his head lolled back. She let go, and stepped back, studying him for a moment. He was on the verge of passing out, and as pale as she'd ever seen a woodelf. Wiping her hands on her robe, she walked to the fire, and pulled from it a red hot poker.

        "Wake him with water. He stinks." She couldn't quite keep the smile off of her face. Though it displeased her that he had refused to answer such a simple question, she never could get enough of his screams.

        They threw bucket after bucket of freezing water on him, until he was begged her to make them stop, and she waved them off. She approached again, holding the poker near his face. He was shivering uncontrollably, probably from both the chill night air, and his fear of her. She had threatened to maim him permanently with the poker before...and would probably do so now, if his answers weren't more forth coming.

        "You tell me you will do anything I ask, if I will just stop the pain. And yet, I stop the pain, and you won't answer a simple question half the world knows the answer to. Haemish leads the Silver Daggers." She brought the poker to his face, close enough that the heat began to burn his skin. He started to move his head away. "Don't move unless I tell you you can move!" She hissed, pulling the poker away, and struck him hard in the ribs with it. She was rewarded with the delightful sound of bones snapping, and drew the poker along his skin. He screamed. She kept it there, waiting. She'd been conditioning him for over a week now, and she didn't feel he deserved the prompt to end the pain. He knew what she wanted to hear.

        "No..Mistress...please..I beg..forgiveness. Forgive my foolishness, please Mistress Katrya. Yes, Haemish, yes..he leads, of course...I won't move my head, please..." She pulled the poker away, and brought it to his face again, just beneath his eye. This time he didn't shy away, and she smiled.

        "Very good. Answer the rest of my questions, and I might let you eat." She stepped close again, not letting the poker waver.

        "Yes, please, and water...I need water, Mistress. Please. I'll tell you what you want to know." He did need water. He looked like he might die if she made him go another day without it. She might give him some, if he answered her.

        "Who are the officers of the Silver Daggers?"

        He hesitated for only a second. "They..they are..Davish..yes him, he's one...and..and Dadan, I think....I don't....and Cassia..yes of course Cassia is one..I..think... that's them..I think...I don't know, Mistress..I think....."

        She'd really wanted to use the poker. She handed it off, and it was replaced in the fire. It didn't do to reward obediance with pain, not if you were trying to condition them to obey. She grabbed her water flask, and took a drink, her eyes never leaving him. He licked his lips as he watched her drink, and she smiled.

        "Do you know any of them well?"

        "No..I..yes, Mistress..I know..Cassia, Mistress...Cassia Spellsinger....and Belce. I've hunted with Pike and Oakmon...but I don't know them well." He tried to wet his lips again, they were dry and cracked. "Please, may I have some water, Mistress?"
       
        "Am I done questioning you?" She asked, letting a frown darken her attractive features.

        "No, Mistress! And I'll keep answering you, it's just my throat hurts so badly..." He caught the warning look in her eyes too late. "I'm sorry, Mistress. No, you're not done, I shouldn't have asked.." his voice was weak, begging now. "Forgive me?"

        "You are nothing but a disappointment to me, my pet." She drove her knee up hard into his groin, then stepped back and waited. He couldn't curl, because his legs, like his arms, were tied apart, and gave him no slack at all.

        "I'm sorry, Mistress. I won't do it again, please, ask me what you want to know? Please.." He could barely speak, but he knew she needed to hear him beg her, or she would hurt him again.

        "Who are they allied with?"

        "Us...the Seekers.." he said quickly, and she began to regret setting her poker back into the fire. "and the Dark Oracles, I think. Yes, the Dark Oracles, led by Eleysian."

        She let the smile return to her face, and poured just a touch of water between his parched lips. "And do you know any of them well?" She asked, circling slowly around him.

        "No, Mistress. I'm sorry, I do not." She completed her circle, and studied him again. Her smiled faded, and he knew that answer hadn't pleased her. "I don't know any of them well, but I know Cassia and Belce well. I hunt with them often...what do you want to know? Please don't hurt me, please..."

        "I want to know what their weaknesses are. What are the Dagger's weaknesses? Cassia's. Belce's."

        "Weaknesses, Mistress?" She could taste the fear in his voice and smiled.

        "Do I have to repeat myself?" It was such a simple question too...they had the weaknesses that all "good" people did. They thought love was the answer, and they let kindness blind them to the dangers around them. Which is how she had caught Dogedme.

        "I don't know, Mistress, I swear I don't. If they have weaknesses, I don't know them....please don't hurt me again..." He knew what was coming, and she let him wait. Anticipation of pain, could almost be more powerful that the pain itself. And fear? Well, fear could destroy a mind...and she was going to break him.

        "That's not good enough, pet....and you know it." She drew close, her smile growing wider with each moment that passed. His screams would fill the forest tonight, it would be a glorious sound.
