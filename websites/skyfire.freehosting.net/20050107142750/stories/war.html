The Daggers Strike Back
by Haemish MacLennan, Clan Chieftan of the Silver Daggers

From the journals of Haemish MacLennan:

"The time had come to strike back at the enemies who had hounded the Silver Daggers. My mind was made up... we would lure our enemies out into the open and fight them, in the light where we were at our strongest. I booked passage for myself in Freeport to visit the Gypsy Oracle in the Steamfont Mountains, and made no secret of it. Using the underground contacts I had built up, I let it be known that I would be there, and only bring the clan bannerman with me. Gamlaen and I made our way there early, and the rest of our ambush party made their way by teleport to the Steamfont Mountains, thanks to brother Oakmon Bigtree.

Setting up the ambush outside of the Minotaur Caves, I placed the ambush party in hiding behind a gigantic clockwork gear, some relic of a past time before the Combine empire when the giants ruled the lands by the size of it. Meanwhile I made sure myself and Gamlaen Lifegiver, the Clan Bannerman, had magical means of protection and vision which would allow us to spot enemies who might sneak up on us with invisibility. Minutes went by, and a fog settled over the land as we walked towards the caves. Then, out of the fog, a figure formed... a dark elf, who's dusky skin was certainly out of place in this land of high elves and gnomes. Then another and another, trolls as well, and it seemed they kept coming, until Gamlaen and I were outnumbered more than four to one. They formed into battle line, brandishing evil grins and drawing weapons. They begin to shout insults at us, cursing the Daggers and all the gods of good there were. I waited, then finally drew my bow and fired, shouting a signal to our ambush party to ATTACK! Let the battle begin!

My blows fell hard, but the number of opponents overwhelmed me, and soon I found myself separated from my sword by the quick hands of one of their number. Bashing with my fists, I tried to fight them off, but lost the battle and soon lost consciuosness. When I regained my senses, I looked about for friends and enemies, and was greeted with a pile of bodies, most of them our enemies. A lull had occured in the fighting, as our enemies regrouped, and my companions dragged me to a safe place and applied what bandages they could. Next to me, they brought Gamlaen and Fudil, and I despaired... had the battle gone so badly? But I repositioned myself such that I had a vantage point on the field of battle and my heart soared to see the fallen forms of my enemies! A full 6 of them were down, and our strongest members remained bloodied and battered, but alive and in good fighting trim. Manslayer circled the field in a blood frenzy, covered in gore with eyes of fire, searching for more enemies to slay. Kordail stood unmoving, his skin glowing with the magics he wielded, his pet wolf panting happily. Cassia sat mediatating over her spellbook, as did Oakmon, and pious Shoji, panting with the exertion he'd obviously been through, looked about wild-eyed at his first battle. Soon, the attack began anew, but weaker and piecemeal. Three more of the dark servants attacked, with mystical pets in tow, only to be overwhelmed by our unity.

Finally, no more of the evil minions of Innoruuk presented themselves for battle, and we gathered ourselves together, healing the scars of the battle and surveying our wounds. A few spells from Kordail, and I was walking again, the wounds barely visible where moments before they'd been bloody. I told the Daggers that I must see the Oracle, as there was still work to be done, and answers to be had. Steeling myself, I walked wearily into the cave, and made my way past the minotaurs who lived in the cave.

But as soon as I reached the Oracle's chamber, I knew something was amiss. Her fire, always so eerily warm and inviting, was cold, extinguished. No one had been here for days at least. The Oracle was nowhere to be found, and nothing that showed a sign that someone lived there presented itself. As my eyes adjusted to the darkness, I began to see words, scrawled in chalk on the wall of the cave. Bringing my lightstone closer to examine the writing, I read the message that chilled my soul...

"Evil walks in sacred places, seek out the heart of tears."

I returned to the area outside the cave, and relayed this message to the Daggers. We must find the Oracle, and our only clue is to "seek the heart of tears." What this cryptic message means, I know not, but I will discover it."
