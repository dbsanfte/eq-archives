Tome of the Skyfire Roleplaying Guidelines

These guidelines were created to provide a more indepth and fair standard for roleplaying within the Tome of the Skyfire Saga. Although they may take a little getting used to at first, we feel that they clarify the roleplaying requirements needed to participate.

Participation in the Tome of the Skyfire Saga

  1. Individuals may join on their own.

  2. Guilds applying to join will need to present these guidelines to all of their members. Each member that agrees to adhere to the guidelines should notify their Guild Leader. In turn, the Leaders will send the Skyfire Site Administrator a Roster of Participation. This roster should be updated as new members join or leave the story.

  3. Although you may play more than one character in the story, this should not be used to unfairly benefit one or more of your characters in ways that they might not be able to be benefitted without your other characters. If you have a question about a specific situation and whether or not it would violate this policy, check with the Skyfire Site Administrator.

  4. Any use of non-participating parties, allies, guilds, or individuals to obtain information or perform story-related activities is not allowed.

Roleplaying / IC & OOC

Roleplaying is the main purpose of the story; to provide a story line (or plot) for the Players of EverQuest on the Karana server to participate in, make accomplishments, and have an outcome all in the spirit of a true RPG.

  1. For the purpose of continuity, all participants are considered IC (In Character) at any time that an event is taking place, unless they state otherwise. Being OOC (Out of Character) should be declared as participants encounter one another. A Player may opt to go OOC during an encounter, but only to perform real life actions, or resolve a necessary conflict that requires the involved Players to all step OOC to discuss it. Any unresolved conflicts should be brought to the Skyfire Site Administrator for resolution. Players may hunt or play together even when their characters would not normally be allowed to in the context of the storyline, but only if all participants agree (such as Council of Guilds Mixer Hunts, etc.)

  2. Character-crossing is not allowed. Character-crossing is when one character is created by a Player to obtain information (either IC or OOC) and uses that information to benefit another character.

  3. Any communications between guilds (messages, stories, threats, treaties, etc...) should be posted on the Skyfire Forum. However, the majority of roleplaying interactions should be arranged to be done in game and not on the message boards.

Quests

  1. The Skyfire Story Masters will arrange several quests for the players to participate in. These are usually by contact from one of our NPCs while in game.

  2. Interference from opposing factions during a quest is permissable (even encouraged!).

  3. More on quests will be written on a per quest basis.

Dueling

  1. Level Gaps - In order to deter Player Killers from actively hunting lower level characters one of two things can occur;

    • If the challenger is 5 levels higher then the character they are challenging, the lower level character may legally decline to duel without recourse.

    • Lower level characters are free to challenge higher level characters at their own risk, without penalty.

  2. Duel challenges should be accepted within a reasonable length of time, and “stalking” is not permitted. Stalking is waiting to accept a duel until the challenger is low on health.

  3. Dueling participants may have one Assistant each. The Assistant for each side should be declared before the duel begins. Healing, buffing, or any other effects by anyone other then each sides Assistant can render the results of the duel null and void (unless both sides agree that the interference was not significant enough to disrupt the duel). It is recommended that a Mediator standby OOC to tell anyone passing by or watching to not assist in the duel.

  4. When a character is challenged to a duel several things need to be determined before the duel commences:

    • The levels of the two participants are verified. If there is question to an anonymous characters level, that person should prove it by temporarily turning off /anon, or /roleplay.

    • The two participants declare their individual Outcome Option, in the event that they are the Loser of the duel. (** See Outcome Options below). Or they agree to not use the Outcome Options.

    • Assistants are chosen by each participant, if any.

    • A neutral Mediator is agreed upon, if any.

    • A proper location for the duel to occur, within reason, is determined. It is recommended to be near a zone point and away from KoS or aggro NPC’s and Mobs.

    Outcome of a Duel (Options):

    • A. The Loser willingly hands over all coins that they are carrying to the Victor. If no coins are carried, this is not an viable option.

    • B. The Loser willingly hands over 50% of their coins and any Skyfire-related item to the Victor.

    • C. The Victor rolls a random number between 1-21. This is the number of inventory slots of worn items; starting from the top left (Left Ear), going down to the bottom right (Ammo). This does not including the 8 slots for baggage. After the random roll, the Loser hands over whatever item is in that slot. Empty slots means the Victor receives nothing. If this option is decided between the two duelists, it is recommended that each side take a screenshot and review each others worn inventory before dueling. Any items that you are not willing to lose should be taken off and put into one of the 8 baggage slots before the duel and before the viewing by your adversary.

  5. All duels begin with both participants at full health and mana, and after the OOC details of the duel have been determined.

  6. The intervention of a mob or NPC during the duel is a valid risk, so chose your dueling locations carefully.

  7. Running away for an entire duel in order to “out last” the other character and wait for them to log is very unethical. It defeats the purpose of accepting the duel and wastes a lot of time. So, don’t do it. Admit defeat or surrender instead.

  8. Going LD or being disconnected is an uncontrolled event during a duel. Any duels that end due to this are null and void, or should be restarted upon the characters return.

  9. Gating away or being teleported are the only means that a character can escape a duel without being captured or slain. Non-casters may be teleported away by non-Skyfire participating characters.

  10. The Loser of the duel admits their own death or capture. This admission should be posted upon the Skyfire War and Death Board. The Skyfire Story Masters will then keep track of the current status of the characters (dead, ressed, captured, etc..).

Battle Events

  1. Any group (regardless of guild affiliation) may declare a Battle Event.

  2. Only participants of the Skyfire Story may be involved in a Battle Event.

  3. Time and place will be announced ahead of time on the Forum so that any and all participants have the option to attend. Additionally, the Skyfire Site Administrator will post a page detailing the event beforehand, provided the information is given to him at least 4 days in advance of the event. Provided the Site Administrator is notified, an additional press release will be sent to 6 top EQ sites to announce the event for any spectators to see.

  4. At least one of the Skyfire Story Masters should be present to mediate the battle. If one if not available, one of the Guild Officers of a participating guild will do.

  5. Characters that die during the Battle Event cannot return to battle without being revived or ressurected. This revival/ressurection does not require the normal time restriction of other revival/ressurections and may be done immediately.

  6. Characters that leave the designated battle area are considered to have fled the battle. They cannot return to the battle if they flee. If a spell knocks them out of the designated battle area, they can return, but must do so immediately and not wait to heal or meditate.

  7. The Battle Event is over when all members of one side either flee the designated battle area or are killed.

Surrender & Conditions of Imprisonment

  1. If the Loser chooses to flee or surrender from a duel or battle, the Victor may opt to capture him. The Loser will then go directly to the Arena in Lake Rathetear. An escort may be required to help the captured character to get to the Arena, but the traveling time is considered OOC.

  2. Duels that result in capture are made official by the prisoner admitting their own capture. This admission should be posted on the Skyfire Forum, under the Prisoners Forum.

  3. The captured character must remain in the Arena until they are released by a Trial Event, Rescue Event, or Escape Event.

  4. Leveling, traveling, gaining XP, attending other events, or any other questionable activities for a prisoner are not allowed during the term of imprisonment. You may not go OOC to perform any of these actions, regardless of guild requirements, commitments, etc... You are in prison!

  5. Should a prisoner of war be detained for a lengthy period, food and water should be provided by the captors.

Rescue Events

  1. Any non-prisoner may declare a Rescue Event.

  2. Only participants of the Skyfire Story may be involved in a Rescue Event.

  3. Time and place will be announced ahead of time on the Forum so that any and all participants have the option to attend.

  4. Rescue Events should take place in the area where the prisoner is detained. This is to be determined at the time of capture.

  5. At least one of the Skyfire Story Master should be present to mediate the rescue. If none are available, a Guild Officer of one of the participating guilds will do.

  6. The prisoner being rescued must be present for the Rescue Event. However, they cannot assist their rescuers in any way during it.

  7. The Rescue Event will begin with the captors within the capture area and the prisoner, the rescuing side waiting just outside.

  8. Characters that die during the Rescue Event cannot return to battle without being revived or ressurected. This revival/ressurection does not require the normal time restriction of other revival/ressurections and may be done immediately.

  9. Characters that leave the prison area are considered to have fled the battle. They cannot return to the battle if they flee. If a spell knocks them out of the prison area, they can return, but must do so immediately and not wait to heal or meditate.

  10. At this time, no other prisoners will be detained from a Rescue Event.

  11. The Rescue Event is over when all members of one side either flee the prison area or are killed. Depending on the winner determines whether or not the Rescue attempt was successful or not.

Death

  1. Duels that result in death are made official by the deceased admitting their own death. This admission should be posted on the Skyfire Forum Deaths Board.

  2. The deceased character will remain dead until a Resurrection or Revival Event occurs. Until one of these two Events occur, the character is considered a ghost.

  3. No contact is to be made with anyone, no experience gained, and travel is limited to Speak to Dead Events..

Speak to Dead Events

  1. Only Necromancers of (19th level) or Clerics of (19th level) may speak to the dead.

  2. The person performing the Speak to Dead must be at the location of the ghost.

  3. All Speak to Dead Events should be transcribed and sent to the Skyfire Story Staff.

Ressurection & Revival Events

  1. The ghost must be present for a Resurrection or Revival Event.

  2. Resurrections and Revivals must be transcribed and sent in to the Skyfire Story Staff.

  3. Moving a corpse for Ressurection or Revial requires the transporting of the “body”. This must be transcribed and the ghost cannot participate in any way in battles or help the group that is transporting the body.

  4. Revivals must occur within 24hrs of the death and should last at least 5 minutes.

  5. Resurrections can occur anytime and should last approximately 20 minutes.

  6. The Skyfire Staff may be contacted to create an NPC for a Ress or Revival, however these will usually cost the people hiring for the Ress/Revival (to help fund the saga).

  7. Each character may be Resurrected/Revived any number of times, but should try to keep their deaths to a minimum. Keep in mind that the Coordinators of a Duels and Battle Events must specify if a game death in these situations requires a Revival, ressurection, or something else.
