A Rogue's Rumour

Last night, Monday June 5th, i was in Lfay when a LVL 1 wood elf rogue named Rufiel /tell me, this is the just of what he told me:

Rufiel was in a bar in freeport and he over heard 2 guys talking that looked like humans at first but werent, one said that Haemish didn't have "it" on him, and the other said "well we will just have to roust him again, and his flunkies" Rufiel assumed the flunkies were the rest of the Daggers. Rufiel also said that he picked up on that the "it" was some kind of artifact. I asked Rufiel if he remembered thier names or anything like that and he said that they were the Guardians of ___ he had forgotten the rest. He said he had come back to Gfay because he heard that one of the Silver Daggers might be in the area and he had the feeling that he was being followed.

I can't say i understand any of it, i've only been with the Daggers for a very short while so i hope some one else can get more out of this than me

~Eliana Ravenstar
