An Unprovoked Attack

From the journals of Haemish MacLennan:

"Seeing the destruction wrought upon the strange lands of Kunark, I felt the loss these people must have felt as their homes were destroyed. The future of the Daggers felt so uncertain, that I was moved to consult my Oracle. Fighting my way back from the cliffs of the Field of Bone, all the way back to the Elvish outpost at Fironia Vie, I boarded the enchanted boat The Maiden Voyage, and drifted back to Faydwer. Once there, I wearily made my way to the Steamfont Mountains, past the bizarre yet beautiful gnomish windmills to the minotaur caves. There, I drew my sword and cut through the minotaur slavers guarding the entrance and disappeared into the side tunnel I'd found so many years earlier.

There sat the wizened old gypsy, the oracle who had read my future many years earlier. Blind and scarred from centuries of living, the old gypsy crouched before a fire, as I'd found her years before, almost as if she'd not moved since that day. She'd known I was coming, and greeted me. I sat in front of her and heard her tale, and it chilled me to my bones. A warning, of great danger coming to the Silver Daggers and myself, and of the hand of a great evil stretching out to cover the land of Norrath.

I ran from the cave and to the weirdling hole of Ak'Anon. Not even noticing the cold, mechanical hum of the gnomish city, I dispatched the bards there with messages to the four corners of the world, gathering the Daggers to meet me in Butcher's Block, at the docks. And then:"
