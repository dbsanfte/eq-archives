Guild Structure

 For ease of administration and to avoid getting too top heavy. We have decided to group the races and to have 3 Marshall for all the races.

All positions with exception of Guild secretary are elected positions on a 3 month cycle.  Guild secretary is appointed position by the Guild master.

Malice Marshall (Trolls, Ogres, Dark Elves) 

Middle Marshall (Humans, Elves, Erudites, Barbarians)

Mini Marshall (Dwarves, Gnomes, Halflings)

Then we have divided the classes into counsel groupings. Each class will have two officers for ease of recruiting. Those are as follows: 

Religious Counsel – Clerics, Paladins and Shamans. 
Arcane Counsel – Wizards, Enchanters, and Necromancers.
Military Counsel – Warriors, Monks, Shadow Knights. 
Information Counsel – Rangers, Rogues, and Bards.
Elemental Counsel – Druids and Mages.

Lastly, we have a Guild Mistress
