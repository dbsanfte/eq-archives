Guild DO's and DON'Ts

Recently, it has been brought to our attention that a number of our members and not quite aware of our general stance for a number of our guild guidelines and rules of the Road. So in effort to curb any confusion, we will address those here. 

  • 1. ABSOLUTELY NO KSing -- this is guild remove situation and will NOT be tolerated. 

  • 2. NO SHOUTing of profanity or at other players in anger, if you are shouting it should be presenting a good light of the Daggers in a roleplaying mode. 

  • 3. Respect thy officer. You will abide by the our chosen officers, if you have an issue, please email me or Gandorian. The current officers have been chosen by myself and Gandorian for their loyalty and willingness to help others and make our guild strong. If you disrespect them you are also disrespecting the guild. 

  • 4. Email - All members must have an email posted with myself (Jedzea) and your officers. If this is abided you will no longer be a member of the Airbe Druad. I apologize for the tone but we have a number of newer members that I have NO way to contact besides in game. We  need to contact ALL Players.

  • 5. Power leveling is allowed, but you are not to request this of a higher member, it is a Gift of their and energy, DON'T abuse it. 

  • 6. Twinking is allowed, but again if you receive a Gift, please show your gratitude and appreciation, and understand that this is a kindly act of your guild mate.

  • 7. Begging -- is NOT tolerated, although we have a "Need before Greed" policy this does not mean that you are entitled to request or ask for $$ or items off of our other players.

  • 8. If you have problems with a player, please refer that to their officer, if the issues, please contact myself, directly via email or in game.

Thank you for being a loyal member of the Airbe Druad.

Sincerely,

Yelwin Bladesprite and Jedzea Briarwitch
