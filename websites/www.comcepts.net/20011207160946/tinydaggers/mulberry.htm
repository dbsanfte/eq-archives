Scales of Pain & Tiny Dagger History
 

When the Iksar awoke from their slumber, they saw that their empire was I chaos. The lands of the once so mighty Iksar, sons and daughters of Rile, where ridden with filthy Giants, Goblins, Froglocks and their Broodlings. Even some of the Smoothskins had built outposts on Kunark, trying to get a foothold on the lands that the Iksar held so dear.

          The Iksar ranks where sent out by their Cast leaders to regain the land of the Iksar, and to train for the coming war. The lands around Cabilis where soon filled with Iksar, and the Froglocks, Sarnaks, Giants and their like had to fight for their life. The lands of the Iksar where no longer for the taking. The Broodlings of Cabilis where growing in might, and all was well.

          As  the Broodlings grew in might and numbers, guilds where created and leaders where chosen, one of these guilds were The Warriors of Kunark. The guild worked for the sake of the Iksar and believed in furthering the goals of the race. All was well, until one day when an alliance with dark elves appeared to be the wish of the current leader, this Issue was controversial. Would they bring Smoothskins into their Brotherhood? A few Iksar, who believed that Iksar shall and can remain pure only if they leave smoothskins out of their deepest and most sacred plans for conquest, left the Warriors and created a new guild.  

The Scales of Pain was created, Iksar only, built on the principle that all Iksar where just scales on the body of an Iksar, all equally important. The Scales where a democratic guild, focused on Spreading the Word and Pain of whom they considered the greatest of Lords - Cazic Thule.

          The Iksar were hated everywhere and by everyone, this soon came to light, traveling of Kunark was a dangerous task indeed, and the Iksar had to sneak and evade all Smoothskin authorities, unless they where seeking death.

          This was a problem for the Scales of Pain, needing help from other races was a delicate matter, staying pure and not granting membership to Smoothskins was a must, but still they needed help.

          Then it came to pass, that the Scales who traveled elsewhere in Norrath met smoothskins who sympathized with their cause. This was a good thing, and had to be taken into use. The Tiny Daggers where created to support and spy for the Scales of Pain in the rest of Norrath. Serving as Insiders and helpers, they acted as spies for the Iksar. The reasons for helping the Iksar where many and up to every individual. Either way, they served the Iksar cause, and by not being part of the Scales of Pains, they would never get to know the sacred plans and battle tactics of the Iksar empire. The Scales of Pain had found a powerful ally, and the Tiny Daggers where amongst the few smoothskins not regarded as slaves or prey by the Iksar.

          As time passed, The Scales of Pain grew in size and power, and the Tiny Daggers expanded and grew. It was then decided that the Daggers needed guidance from Iksar who knew the proper plans. A few dedicated Scales left their brethren for short or long periods of time, to perform the task of Oberservers and advisors.

          And so it was that the Scales of Pain and the Tiny Daggers organized themselves to further the Iskar empire so that one day the Iksar could regain their former glory.

 

          -Ghankha Painscale, Knight of Greenmist, Bringer of Pain
