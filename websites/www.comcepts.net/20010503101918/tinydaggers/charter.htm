Scales of Pain & Tiny Dagger History
 

     

Behold, the world of Norrath is world of races living in harmony together. Our primary mission in the world of Norrath is to keep the peace. Those of us of smaller stature have decided to form a guild called “Tiny Daggers” explicitly for those of us of a diminutive size.

            What do we mean by small stature? Well, in some barbarian or trolls families that means you were just smaller then the normal, so in turn you were probably the brunt of many not so nice jokes. For the smaller races, it means trying to find a barstool they can actually reach the bar from.  For the other races, this means that you maybe got the hand me downs and actually had to roll up sleeves and roll up pants legs otherwise, you were tripping over them constantly.       

 Requirements of Recruiting

            We are open to all recruiting of personnel of sound mind and small body, those of role-playing persona and of kindly nature. Being kindly nature also means that you have to “bigger” then others. In the case of kill stealing, profanity, zero ability to role-play, we have to express our disappointment and annoyance in a different fashion. Many times challenging the other player to a duel or calling upon reinforcements is a better option then name calling or “stooping” to their level. Remember, you are a member of “Tiny Daggers” the patron caretakers of Norrath, act like it!

Guild Structure

 For ease of administration and to avoid getting too top heavy. We have decided to group the races and to have 3 Marshall for all the races.

All positions with exception of Guild secretary are elected positions on a 3 month cycle.  Guild secretary is appointed position by the Guild master.

Malice Marshall (Trolls, Ogres, Dark Elves) - 

Middle Marshall (Humans, Elves, Erudites, Barbarians)

Mini Marshall (Dwarves, Gnomes, Halflings)

Then we have divided the classes into counsel groupings. Those are as follows:

            Religious Counsel – Clerics, Paladins and Shamans. 

            Arcane Counsel – Wizards, Enchanters, and Necromancers.

            Military Counsel – Warriors, Monks, Shadow Knights. 

            Elemental Counsel – Druids and Mages.

            Information Counsel – Rangers, Rogues, and Bards.

Lastly, we have a Guild Mistress

 Guild Benefits

            A group of fun role-players to adventure with and the satisfaction of a barstool or clothing made to fit. A support network for character growth, a break from the hack and slash monotony and a shoulder to cry on. As the guild grows awards will be given to member who have performed outstanding services to their guild brothers and sister. Quests shall also be forthcoming. Remember you are in our world now – Watch your head! (Duck). “Walk with your head held high with no fear of knocking yourself out."
