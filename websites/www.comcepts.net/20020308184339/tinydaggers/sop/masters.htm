Masters and Mistresses

As a Master or Mistress to the Scale of Pain you are an active participating member. What does this mean?

Duties and Responsibilities:

1. Vote and Participate:
Without your input on a daily, weekly and/or monthly basis we have no idea of what you are looking for in your guild. One way we know the guild is healthy and alive is by your active participation. Joining us, if possible at guild events, making your presence know and helping others. If you participate in this manner, we know that your and your guild members are active. Do your part to make this the best guild you can! Give us your feedback, please! 

2. To lead by example
Visit areas to support our guild if you are without current plans. Backing lowers or just getting to know them better. Show generosity and manners so that they see how we treat others and that we are a mature guild. (Guild Raids are great for these)

3. Support
If you know a tradesman is in town working on his craft. Give him aid. This could be in the form of donations to shouts to others who may need his craft. In time our new members or even existing members should be well equiped from our tradefolk.
