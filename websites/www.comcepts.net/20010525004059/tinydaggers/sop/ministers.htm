Minister/Ministeress

Ministers/Ministeresses will hold the term of office for 3 months at one posting. Ministers/Ministeresses will oversee his/her class of Iksar. 

Best job description: You are guildmaster for you class within the guild. 

Duties and Responsibilities:


1. Recruitment - not as easy as /guildinvite xxxxx
To be recruited you need to have 3 sponsors. 1st would be the Officer who believes the IKSAR is worthy of being in the Scales of Pain. Then that person needs two more individuals of Master or above  to approve of them. This may require spending time with them if you haven't already done so. Other ways of doing this is by trusting the people below you. However, you will be responsible for the recruit if you are one of the three. Merits will be awarded for recruiting. If you wish to see your standings for the month. Click here. 

2. Responsible? Yes you should see to it they they contact you or your chain of command for information. You should see that they come to our website and fill out the application. You should answer the question "Who makes banded, where is a good place to hunt?... In general be a leader and mentor. You may not have the answers but direct them to where they can get them. Whether that be other players or websites as needed.

3. Voting on guild issues. We are a democracy please be as active as you can. The more you participate the closer the guild will be to what you envisioned.

4. Organize. There is too much to over look for one person. Many duties can be split up or added. Minister of languages, Minister of War, Minister of Strategy (Dungeon raids), Minister of PR (Non combative guild events, Adopt a newbie for a day), etc. These are ideas not official.

5. To lead by example
Visit areas to support our guild if you are without current plans. Backing lowers or just getting to know them better. Show generosity and manners so that they see how we treat others and that we are a mature guild. (Guild Raids are great for these)

6. Support
If you know a tradesman is in town working on his craft. Give him aid. This could be in the form of donations to shouts to others who may need his craft. In time our new members or even existing members should be well equiped from our tradefolk.
