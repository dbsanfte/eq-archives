Tiny Daggers  & Scales of Pain

 

The world of Norrath can be a treacherous and wondrous place. Many folks such as the Iksars, Trolls, Ogres and Dark Elves find hostility all around them and are killed on site in many a town. The other folks also have similar problems in just those towns. The Tiny Daggers are a non bias group of folk that believe in the harmonious co-existence for all, with the exception of those who wish to destroy the world of Norrath.

    Hence forth the Tiny Daggers will work in side by side with the Scales of Pain as a sister guild and rebuild and rid the land of Kunark of the remainder of the race of the enslavers, known as the Sarnaks.
