Airbe Druad

 

Long Ago during the reign of Arthur, Albion and Hibernia worked side by side to defend our realms against the scourge of Midgard, but since the death of their great King Arthur they have strayed from the council of the last druid of Albion, Merlin. The people of Albion have lost sight of the dragon and are no longer respect the land and no longer worship the mother Goddess, which we, as Hibernias still hold dear and true. The Old Gods and Heros we still pay homage and give thanks to daily. It is in these Dark Times we have bonded together to form and come together as the Airbe Druad. In our old tongue the "Airbe Druad" is a Magical impassable barrier. Each rock in our walls is strong and will defend our homeland with our lives. Our wall has been enchanted and blessed by our Mother Goddess and defended by the Gods and our Heros, such as the great Master, Cuchulainn.
 

 
Cuchulainn guarded the House of Ulster and defended his lands with his magical powers and we, as the Airbe Druad will defend and guard our home of Hibernia against the traitors of the land, the Albions and the evil invaders of Midgard.

So our battle cry of "Blood and Sky" to defend our homelands and our ways rings in the ears of the betrayers and the Norse invaders. We shall defend, we shall protect and we shall always stand as the Airbe Druad.
