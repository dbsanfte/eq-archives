"All the scales of an Iksar make up the body of an Iksar. No one scale is more important then another, so is the Body of the Scales of Pain".

The Guild known as the Scales of Pain have been formed on this premise. We are a body of Iksar formed to benefit the superior race of Iksar and to uphold the fear instilled by our God Cazic-Thule.

As Cazic-Thule has demanded we shall remain pure. Our organization is ruled by our Lord Guildmaster. If you wish to understand how the organization is governed, please go to here.

Guild Guidelines

We are all here in EQ to play and have FUN! Belonging to a guild can be just that and more. What we ask of you is that since your are becoming a member of Scales of Pain you roleplay your character to all of the outside world. When you are in guildsay, tells, or group (it is not necessary) but is preferred for the flavor (if you select to). Being a part of the Scales of Pain means that YOU KNOW you are the superior race and that others will bow or fall to your will or be it the will of Cazic-Thule. 

How should you act -- off color shouts are not acceptable. If you are going to roleplay with  softskins make sure you have informed them and have their consent in tells. (Many times you are playing with under age people), we don't want to actually hurt anyone or their feelings. We still regard ourselves as a noble race. And we act as such. 

What are your obligations to the guild:

            1. Participate

             2. Roleplay

             3. Give of yourself and it shall be returned in kind. 

              4. Have FUN!  and may Cazic-Thule bring you Pain!
