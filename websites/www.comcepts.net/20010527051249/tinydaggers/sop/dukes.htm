Dukes and Duchess

These individuals are appointed to their positions during the 3 month term of a Minister. If a Minister find his/herself in need of additional resources person, they may appoint a Duke/Duchess  underneath themselves. The Duke or Duchess must be of the same class and must be in good standings with the guild. 

After the Minister has served his term and has not been reelected to the office a duke/duchess may retain their standing as a Duke/Duchess if they have a minimum of 500 merits to their name. Otherwise they will return to the rank of Master/Mistress unless another Minister appoints them as their Duke or Duchess. 

Duties and Responsibilities:

1. You can view yourself as a Minister in training, while your primary function is not recruiting you are still to help our Minister with his tasks, which this is one. 

2. You are to lend aid to your Minister for the other guild members under his charge. This can be directing a new recruit to our website and fill out the application. You should answer the question "Who makes banded, where is a good place to hunt?... In general you are his first line in the chain of command. . You may not have the answers but direct them to where they can get them. Whether that be other players or websites as needed.

3. To lead by example
Visit areas to support our guild if you are without current plans. Backing lowers or just getting to know them better. Show generosity and manners so that they see how we treat others and that we are a mature guild. (Guild Raids are great for these)

4. Support
If you know a tradesman is in town working on his craft. Give him aid. This could be in the form of donations to shouts to others who may need his craft. In time our new members or even existing members should be well equiped from our tradefolk.

5. Vote and Participate:
Without your input on a daily, weekly and/or monthly basis we have no idea of what you are looking for in your guild. One way we know the guild is healthy and alive is by your active participation. Joining us, if possible at guild events, making your presence know and helping others. If you participate in this manner, we know that your and your guild members are active. Do your part to make this the best guild you can! Give us your feedback, please!
