Guild DO's and DON'Ts

Recently, it has been brought to both myself and Gandorian's attention that a number of our Daggers and not quite aware of our general stance for a number of our guild guidelines and rules of the Road. So in effort to curb any confusion, we will address those here. 

  • 1. ABSOLUTELY NO KSing -- this is guild remove situation and will NOT be tolerated. 

  • 2. NO SHOUTing of profanity or at other players in anger, if you are shouting it should be presenting a good light of the Daggers in a roleplaying mode. 

  • 3. Respect thy officer. You will abide by the our chosen officers, if you have an issue, please email me or Gandorian. The current officers have been chosen by myself and Gandorian for their loyalty and willingness to help others and make our guild strong. If you disrespect them you are also disrespecting the guild. 

  • 4. Email - All members must have an email posted with myself and your officers. If this is abided you will no longer be a Dagger. I apologize for the tone but we have a number of newer daggers that I have NO way to contact besides in game. We ready to hold general elections on the Daggers side and need to contact ALL Players.

  • 5. Power leveling is allowed, but you are not to request this of a higher member, it is a Gift of their and energy, DON'T abuse it. 

  • 6. Twinking is allowed, but again if you receive a Gift, please show your gratitude and appreciation, and understand that this is a kindly act of your guild mate.

  • 7. Begging -- is NOT tolerated, although we have a "Need before Greed" policy this does not mean that you are entitled to request or ask for $$ or items off of our other players.

  • 8. Training  and Q & A - As of the first of 2001, all officers in their class will be required to have monthly contact with the players in their trade. Please post a monthly training letter to your class directly on the board and send an email training letter to all members of your class. The purpose for this are two fold. First,  we have a number of officers that are MUCH higher then the newer players and don't get a chance to game with the young ones. I would like to see tight and friendly bonds between you, as a player and your elected or appointed officers. Secondly, this will give you training tips, items necessary for your professions and quest tips too.

  • 9. If you have problems with a player, please refer that to their officer, if the issues, please contact myself, directly via email or in game. On the Scales side, I am known as Duchess Brakslayer and here amongst the Daggers, I am known as Mistress Ardathrae Kuchenfrau.

Thank you for being a loyal member of the Tiny Daggers.

Sincerely,

Ardathrae Kuchenfrau and Gandorian
