Lord/Highness 

This position is held by the Guild Leader. Election term is for 3 months. 

Minister - Ministress  

(Elected position after 50 members)

These are the governing Ministers, they have the ability to bring in new members and appoint Dukes, and or Counts to uphold the guild's objectives in their sted. Election term is for 3 months. 

Dukes - Duchess

(Appointed positions)

These members are appointed by the Ministers, they have proven their loyalty and fealty to the guild many times over.

Master - Mistress

An active guild member that participates in events and has been loyal to the guild. 

Novices

(Entry Level)

A new guild member  and just beginning their training. Under the guidance of some of our higher ranking officials.
