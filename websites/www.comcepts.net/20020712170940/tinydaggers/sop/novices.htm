Novices

1. Vote and Participate:
Without your input on a daily, weekly and/or monthly basis we have no idea of what you are looking for in your guild. One way we know the guild is healthy and alive is by your active participation. Joining us, if possible at guild events, making your presence know and helping others. If you participate in this manner, we know that you and your guild members are active. Do your part to make this the best guild you can! Give us your feedback, please! 

2. Is to yourself and your development. We are here to support you. Please don't be shy -- ASK. We have brought you into the guild, because we felt that you would be a good addition to a very happy family. Let us know how we can help with your development. 

3. Know your chain of command. You should seek out your sponsor or your co-sponsor advice first. They are your mentors. If you have any other concerns, please feel free to bring it to a Minister/Ministresses of your class. That's what they are there for to help you in your development.
