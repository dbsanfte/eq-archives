Headlines

Guild Challenge - On Saturday Mar 3, the honor of our females paladins was challenged by Ryaen of the Knights of the Round. The Tiny Daggers and the Scales of Pain turn our in mass numbers at the Arena in Lake Rath. The showing by the SK division of the Scales deserve an honorable mention. Ryaen's apology to our ladies was our primary objective. This was acquired and our ladies honor has been restored. 

This proved to be a outstanding event for all who attended and even though the Daggers/Scales were under level of the Knights that did partake, we very much were a power to be reckoned with in numbers, organization and sheer morale. 

For the Knight of the Round, I would like to mention Meeky, (6th level wizard ) was outstanding. Gorgor and a number of the Knights are VERY good folks. We salute you!

A Great Night in Permafrost - Tsunan

(exurbs from the message board)

Last night we got 3 White Wolf Hide cloaks 1 off of each King that popped, this is the rare drop, with the symbol being the common. Also we didn't get any of the usual 1 handed silvery war ax, but we did get a 2 hander. And only 2 ice totems...

So I round up people and go in looking for guild toys and end up getting something for everyone in the group. Zyr got a totem so he can get his boots, Att got the ax as it was better than what he was swinging, Sad, me and Can (other monk) each got a white wolf hide cloak (one of the best cloaks a monk can have), I got the second totem so that I can get a second pair of boots for a guildy, and Ven ended up weighed completely down with loot and a pair of mammoth hide leggings to show for it (the Alche made 1 appearance).
