·Some Basic Information

What is Brewing?

     Brewing is the art of making alcoholic beverages in the land of Norrath.  Like all trade skills, your skill will reflect your chances of successfully making items depending on it's difficulty.  By gathering the necessary components a brewer can make various alcoholic beverages that can be consumed by players or sold to merchants.  The types of beverages are determined by recipes and success is determined on your skill.
 

Trade Skills?

     Brewing is a Trade Skill that all players, regardless of class or race can attempt.  All characters start of with a rating of 0.  Through practice and by spending practice points, one can raise their skill.  Unlike skills like 1 Handed Slash or Evocation there is no level cap.  In other words, regardless of your level, you can have a skill of 0 to 200 (200 being maximum) depending on how much time and effort you put into the skill.
 

Difficulty?

     Like all trade skills, some items are more difficult than other to make.  The easiest way to track this it the trivial level.  Most items become "trivial" at a certain level.  This is the trade skill level when you no longer get skill increase when trying to make this item.  Though the wording "trivial" might make one think that it would be very easy to succeed in making a item once you get to the trivial level, do not be mislead.  To get consistent successes with little to no danger if failure, you usually must work your skill 10, 20 sometimes every 30+ the trivial level mark.
 

Making Money?

     The term trade skill can be misleading to many that start playing this game.  Though one could make money by making items and selling to other players, this rarely ever happens.  A number of problems exist on most servers that make trades skills not a viable means of making money.
     1. NPCs for the most part buy items from you for lower than what it cost you to make the items.  In some rare instances you can make a profit but usually its extremely minimal and can barely off set the cost of failures.
     2. Many items, mostly the lower level items, are sold by NPC merchants for far lower than what it would cost for players to make.  Only the really high level items require a brewer to make.
     3. Supply and demand.  This applies more so to older, more establish servers.  As the novelty of brewing starts to wear off, supply starts to dwindle.  Offset by the increased number of players taking up brewing, the supply becomes larger and larger.  Soon, there are lots of booze but no one wants to buy.
 

Why Brew?

     You must be asking yourself, why should I take up brewing?  Well, there are a number of reasons -
     1.  Brewing "can" be profitable in some circumstances.  See #2.
     2. Some brews that can only be made via player made Brewing skill are required for certain quests and other trade skills.
     3. Brew for fun!  It can be tedious or it can be a way to spend some time to gain a useful skill.  Its up to you to decide!
 

How to Brew (Step by Step)

     So now you are saying to yourself, how do I brew?  The first thing you need to do is learn the recipes on different drinks to brew.  These recipes can be found in books you can purchase in the game from merchants, or by going to various resource on the internet that list brewing recipes.  Below is one such listing of known recipes in the game.  It's up to you if you wish to use this type of "spoiler" information or if you wish to learn everything in game.
     Once you have learned a recipe, its time to brew.  Gather all the ingrediants that the recipe calls for.  Some can be bought from merchants, other can only be gained from killing monsters and looting them off their corpses.  You can also try to ask fellow players if they know where to get these items.  However you get the ingrediants, prepare to gather a bunch.  For the most part, all ingrediants are consumed when you attempt to make a drink - regardless of failure or success.  So if you wish to make more than one drink, you'll have to gather multiple ingrediants.  In regards to some drinks, you will get more than 1 "shot" or "drink".  In other cases, when you fail on a recipe you will be returned one of the components.  This usually happens with the container - whether it be a bottle or a cask.
     In any case, once you have gathered all the ingrediants it's time to brew.  Find a brew barrel in town.  Some cities have multiple brew barrels scattered throughout.  Many towns have brew barrels inside inns.  Wherever you go, find a unused brew barrel.  Once you have found one, left click on the brew barrel to open it up.  You will get a 10 slot container showing on your screen labeled brew barrel.  This is where you want to place all your ingrediants to brew a drink.  Notice at the bottom of the container that there are 2 buttons.
     One is the done button that will close the container.  All this will do is clear close the brew barrel window and will allow any other player from clicking on the brew barrel to open the window and use it.  Therefore, if you place anything in the brew barrel window and then close it, anyone can open up the brew barrel window and see the items you place there.  The second button is the combine button.  Be very careful clicking this button.  What this button will do is essentially take all the items in the brew barrel and combine them to attempt to make an item.  Anything you put in the brew barrel and hit combine, will immediatly destroy the item, making it impossible to get back.  Make sure that you place all the correct ingrediants in the brew barrel before hitting the combine button.
     IMPORTANT NOTE : When putting the ingrediants in the brew barrel, do NOT stack items.  In other words, if a recipe requires 2 or more of the same item, make sure you place only 1 in each slot.  Do NOT place all 2 or more of the same item in one slot in the brew barrel.  The recipe will NOT work and you will lose all the ingrediants.

     Once you have placed (not stacked)  all the necessary ingrediants in the brew barrel the moment of truth awaits.  Click on the combine button and cross your fingers...
 

Success vs. Failure

    ...You will get 1 of 3 messages when brewing.

  "You have made something new!" - Which means that you have succeed in turning your ingredients into a drink!  You will notice something new on your mouse pointer.  This is the new drink you have just brewed!  Enjoy!
  "You have failed" - Self explanatory.  Don't give up hope.  Try again.  Check the trivial chart - it could be that you are trying to make something that is far to hard for your skill.  If this is the case, try making something easier and build up your skill.
   "Those items did not combine..."  - This means that the ingredients you put into the brew barrel were incorrect and you have failed.  You will lose ALL ingredients in the brew barrel when you do this.  Check to make sure the ingredients were correct and also make sure you do not stack ingredients.

Skill Increase?

     Like all trade skills, the end goal is to increase your skill so that not only can you make more difficult items but also to have a lower failure rate when making items.  How does one increase their skill in brewing?  Why brew, brew and brew - try, try and try.  The key is to attempt to make an item that is not trivial and also is not too difficult.  Every time you brew a item there is the potenial of a skill increase, regardless if you fail or succeed.  But remember, failing to make an item and trying a recipe that is not valid are 2 different things.
     In other words, as long as you attempt to make a actual drink that's a valid recipe, then regardless of if you succeed or fail, you have a chance for a skill increase.  If you try a invalid recipe, then no matter how many times you try, youwill never get a skill increase.

     The key to skill increasing is persistance.  As long as you got a valid recipe, keep trying.  Sooner or later your skill will increase if you try enough times.  If you attempt to brew a drink that is not too difficult for your skill level, then you might even suceed a fair amount as you try to build your skill.  For the most part, it's suggested that you do not try a drink that is way to difficult for your skill level.  Don't try to brew Ginesh when your skill is only 20.  You will never succeed in making it.  But if you try fish wine then you will still fail a lot but every so often you will actually succeed.
     Whether you wish to actually succeed in making an item or not, skill increasing is important, as later on, you will see your success rate with lower difficulty drinks rise and your abilitity to brew exotic drinks become a reality.

Drinking and Brewing

     Brewing, like all skills are based on your character's intelligence.  In other words, the more intelligent you are, the faster you will go up in skill and the more successful you will be.  Though you might be inclined to drink your wares, be careful.  As you drink alcohol, your intelligence will momentarily go down.   Like in the real world, your character will be negatively impacted when he/she drinks too much and gets drunk and this in turn will negatively affect your brewing skill.  In other words, do not drink while working on trade skills.

Fast Track to Brewing

     Here is a step by step guide on the fastest way to get from 0 to 168.  I will state that this is not the most efficient or cost effective way but it requires the least amount of time and headaches - IMHO.

Step 1 - Visit your guild master and put 20 points into brewing.  This will allow you go skip bog juice.

     When brewing, I attempted to try recipes that required ingrediants that can be purchased from merchants.  Because of the sheer number of attempts one has to make, its easier to work on recipes which require ingrediants that can be easily purchased from a merchant that has a infinite supply.  This guide will reflect this.

Step 2 - Short Beer.  Work Short Beer up to 31.  The only problem with Short Beer is that it requires a cask, which does not stack.  This will require you to run back and forth between the merchant that sells it and the brew barrel.  Luckily, its only for 10 skill points.  You might want to save the short beer you make for later - especially if you are brewing somewhere that does not have a merchant that sells it.

Step 3 - Fish Wine.  This is the first hurdle you will have to get over.  Now, some people will do Ale, but Ale is far to costly.  Fish Wine can be done cheaply, even without fishing.  Most towns have a merchant (usually near the docks or near water) that sells unlimited amounts of fresh fish.  Grapes and a Bottle are both sold by all standard brew merchants and water is available off of most merchants, if not the merchant that sells the fresh fish.  Try, try and try until Fish Wine becomes trivial.

Step 4 - A little bit more increase.  Now that you are at 62, do a little Ale or Gypsy Wine to get to 68.  You'll see why in step 5.

Step 5 - Foraging.  Here is where you will start to require foraging goods.  If you don't have the foraging skill be prepared to start begging, pleading and buying what you need from friends and strangers.  Starting at 68, start working on White Wine and Red Wine.  Try to atleast get up to 80.  When you can't stand any more, move on to Step 6.  The higher you get before giving up on Step 5 the better.

Step 6 - Ol'Tujim's Fierce Brew.  Get ready to spend some money, but this drink will save you the most amount of time.  You can get up to 135 with Ol'Tujims Fierce Brew and all the ingrediants are purchasable.  No foraging and no monster looting.  In my experience, the BEST place for this is in High Pass Hold.  There are essentially, 2 merchants, within crawling distance from each other, and a brew barrel right next to one, that sell ALL the ingrediants for this drink.  Go seek it out and get brewing!  You will now have  skill above 100.  It's offical, welcome Master brewer!

Step 7 - Agony once again.  Now that you are at skill 135 you will now be forced to work with foraging again.  You MUST have foraged vegatables.  Either you will need to make Vodka for Hurlgarsh and then Bleeding Brains OR go straight to Ginesh.  I suggest Ginesh, even though you will have far more skill failures not doing Hurlgarsh first.  It only requires vegetables, not Vodka, and will in the end take less time.

Congratulations!  Your skill is now 168.  You should be able to make pratically all the drinks in the above table fairly easily.

Quests, quests, and more quest!

     Currently, there are a few quests that involve the brewing skill ~

   ·Brewing Books - If you go to Halas there is a NPC named Thadres.  She will give you a quest regarding her brother Delius.  You want to go out to Everfrost and hunt 3 skeletons that are called the vengeful soloist, the vengeful lyricist and the vengeful composer.  When you kill each of these skeletons you will get pages 74, 75, and 76 of Delius Thymes' Diary.  These pages are lore, no drop and no rent.  Once you return all three of the pages to Thandres she will give you four tomes listing brewing recipes.

    ·Tumpy Tonics - Many might remember the tumpy tonic fiasco.  Before Verant fixed the amount of xp given by this quest tumpy tonics were worth their weight in platinum!  But now that the quest has been "fixed" many have forgotten about this very good quest.

    ·Burning Rapier Quest - The Burning Rapier quest is too long to list here, but its reward is a great Rogue weapon.  This quest requires the gathering of a large number of drinks, and at one point requires the skill of a Brewer.
