Guild Charter

 

We are a group of People all met and formed friendships while fighting together on the Rathe. The basis of our guild is to help each other to grow and prosper. We are also avowed to being good citizens of Norrath.

 

 

Code of Conduct

As a member of Circle Of Wonder there are expectations of how you play the game. While we have No RULES we do expect that all members will act accordingly. 

1.Be respectful in playing the game.
(even while playing an "evil" character in role play
mode, there's a certain type of respect you must have
towards the game and players)

2.Play by Verants "guidelines" however ridiculous they may seem.
(that way it keeps our "ends" covered so unnecessary
problems will not arise)

3.Have fun :P
