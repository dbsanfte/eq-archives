Smithing:

I'm not exactly sure of the trivia lvls i put up but its within a few skill points.

The first thing u need to do when smithing is find a forge,water supplier (never use summoned water btw) and a orce supplier. After you have found all that you can begin.

In my opinion the best place to start is FP cause it has everything you needinside the city.

Skill 0-21: For the first bit of skill i would suggest putting in the 21 pratice points as it will save time and money if you're on a limited budget. If not and you wanna save your skill points just make metal bits untill trivia. These can be made with 2 small pieces of ore and a flask of water. Make these until trivial.

Skill 21-46: For these next lvls make large lanterns. Large lanterns can be made with a metalbit, a lantern (or large lantern) mold, bottle and a flask of water. Make these until trivial.

Skill 46-105: Now it's time to make skewers. To make these just combine a metal bit, skewermold and a flask of water in the forge. At first you will fail alot but there cheap so it won't cost that much. Just keep making them until trivial.

Skill 105-135: GRATS!! You're now a master smith and can start making banded, although i wouldn't try and make anything besides bracers, boots, gorgets, helms, sleeves and maybe the occasional belt or mask. Do not try gloves or mantles till at least 125 skill cause they're not trivial till 175 when the skill is maxed. Unless you have alot of extra cash on hand I wouldn't even make banded at all till 135 when small sewing kits are trivial. To make these you need a metal bit, a needle mold, a thimble mold and a flask of water. Once these are trivial, make banded and start raking in the cash!

Skill 135-175: As far as I know, there is nothing that will raise you past 140 besides gloves, mantles and cloaks. At least not till they finish smithing patch.
