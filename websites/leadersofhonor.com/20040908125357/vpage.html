Who are we?
We, here at the Leaders of Honor, strive for harmony and peace throughout all of Norrath.  "Excellence" and "pride" are words that ring within our hallowed halls.  We take comfort in the fact that we lend a helping hand to those in need and try to assist others in any way that we can.  In our quest for truth and justice, we have made many friends throughout the years and we are always looking out for more in the future.  However, due to our ideals, we are not as large as some other guilds...  Looking for the best of the best to strengthen our still growing numbers ensures not only our survival in a realm of infinite possibilities, but our ultimate goal of peace and prosperity.

How do I become a member?
To become a member of LoH (Leaders of Honor), we ask that all possible applicants be at least level 20 or higher.  There are some exceptions to this rule however.  Those being outstanding preformance above and beyond the call of ones duties or a standing member, past their trial period, sponsering a lower level into the guild.  After that, then there is a matter of being interviewed by two guild officers and them possibly grouping with you to help determine an invite to the guild...  We apologize for this long process, however we have deemed it necessary to avoid a conflict of interests within the guild later.

After I become a member, how long is my trial period?
As there is nothing every really "written in stone," neither is one's trial period.  Usually, it lasts for only one or two grouping sessions with an officer so that they can determine one's  personal goals and compatability with the guild.  We try to keep this period as short as possible however, since we do make our interview process so rigorous.

What can Leaders of Honor offer me as a guild?
We would like to say that we offer everything one could desire in Norrath, however, that is not always the case.  We offer a leadership that works for the good of the members of the guild.  The members of LoH treat each other with respect and kindness.  We enjoy a family environment where the higher levels players try to help out and guide the lower levels as a parent would their children.  After all, we in LoH consider the guild as our family and would, as most all families do, like to see our family grow and become stronger with the passage of time.  We have recently started a guild bank to help in the developement of new characters and future raids we plan on going doing.  

Is there a set age limit on the guild?
No...  The is no set age limit on the guild.  And since there is no age limit on the guild, we ask that all language be maintained in a suitable environment for children to play in.  We ask that any sexual comments and suggestions be kept out of our guild chat and also ask that cursing and any other foul language also be kept from it.  We want LoH to be enjoyable for all ages...  And do not want to offend anyone.
