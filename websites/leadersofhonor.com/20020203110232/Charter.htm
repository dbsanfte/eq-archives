Leaders of Honor


The goal of Leaders of Honor (LoH) is to provide a fun environment for its members. Each member of the guild will be a friend to all, a shining light against those that wish to bring darkness to the land of Norrath. Leaders of Honor guildmates will demonstrate their worthiness by adhering to the code. As most of us know, not everyone in the world of Norrath has good intentions. Therefore we must all abide by the code at all times.


The Code


Guild Conduct

1. This code must be read upon joining the guild.
2. The minimum level requirement to join is 20. All races/classes are welcome. Exceptions are made by officers as needed
3. LoH is looking for mature players who will treat others the way they want to be treated.  As always with respect.
4. Any derogatory comments in guild chat regarding real life race, sex, or religion are grounds for immediate dismissal without warning. The end will be swift for you if you engage in these types of conversations. Role-playing comments regarding the various religions and races among Norrath are acceptable. In fact, who among us hasn’t made fun of a dwarf on one occasion or another?
5. Any comments about situations of a sexual nature are to be kept out of guild chat. If you want to talk dirty to each other, take it to /tell.
6. Keep arguing out of guild chat.  If any guild member has issues or complaints regarding another member then you are to send an tell to an officer and they will handle the problem ASAP. 
7. If any guild member has a problem with people outside the guild, remember, as a Leader of Honor....act like it.  Problems will happen, it's the way you (and everyone) handle it, that makes the difference.  Try to handle it in the appropriate manor. 
8. Begging will not be tolerated. Do not ask to be twinked or powerleveled. Do not even joke about it. Twinking a character for your own personal use, however, is fine.
9. Sow/Clarity/Teleports/Rez are a luxury, not a necessity. Excessively begging any other members for any of these is grounds for dismissal. If another member is nearby and agrees to provide it for you then so be it. Members are well within their right to ask for a donation. Spells are costly and downtime is tedious at higher levels. Members who have the ability to group teleport are asked to announce when/where the taxi is leaving over guild chat, before they make it available to the general population. I would expect each member to exercise his own judgement in regards to this rule.

And of course, the obvious....Don't KS, Ninja Loot, etc. Stuff happens, I have unintentionally trained people in my year of EQ. However, if you develop a habit of doing it and we get a lot of complaints then you'll need to find a new home.


Officer Conduct


1. An officers division will be set-up and maintained. The number of officers will fluctuate depending on guild size.
2. You are the leaders of the guild. You have the power to remove anyone at anytime.   P
3. Do not subject yourselves to pointless complaints or arguments from guild members or other players.
4. Officers will be in constant communication with other members. This will not turn into a ‘secret society’ where we separate ourselves from the rest of the members. We are all one guild.
5. All officers need to have an e-mail address so that officers and guildleader can maintain, game related, communcations, even when not on.
6. You are responsible for recruiting. Inevitably, we will get our share of bad apples. Use your discretion when selecting members and instruct them to read the charter after you invite. If any officer develops a history of recruiting bad members then the gods will be angered. More importantly, the guild leader will be angered and he may need to evaluate that officer's recruiting skills.
7.  Tell new recruits to visit the webpage at www.leadersofhonor.com

Guild Events, meeting, raids, trade skills, etc. will be discussed on the message board and in-game. The purpose of this charter is to outline the basic guild rules. Forums will be opened to allow discussion of these types of events.

It is my intent for this to be a casual type of guild with strict enforcement of the rules. I hope that everyone has fun and treats other with respect.
