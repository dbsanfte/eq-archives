Friday, December 7, 2001

Hello again all. I've finally regained my internet connection since the ugly Excite@Home Chapter 11 bankrupcy incident. You would think that now that I have my connection back, I'd have alot of new web material to upload, since I know doubt had plenty of time to work on it. You'ld be wrong though, since the lack of internet caused me to realize that there is life outside the computer! Yes, there is! I couldn't get to it though, since my apartment was in quite a state of ruin. Thus, you see where I am going with this. I cleaned. 4 days of it in fact. Not much progress, but I feel better about at least trying!

Anyway, here's what's been going on in RPG land. Everquest released its much anticipated SoV expansion, and I've only heard mixed opinions. The largest complaints so far are that the NPCs look like they are now cut and pasted against the background, and the fact that EQ was down for most of the last few days. Oh well, I'll be canceling that account I guess. Maybe I'll ebay it. lol

On a more somber note, alot of people have been on vacations or away from the game recently, and I've heard some rumors that there has been a few people who have left the guild shortly after joining. If everyone can, please drop me an email at kherog@pawns-of-chaos.com so I can be certain of who is still with us. That's it! More when I get it.

-Kher
