Pickpocket
"...thievery will not be commonplace. I'll personally see to that, or else I give myself a bad name. Perhaps pickpocket is something we won't teach to very many people, or whatever. I'm not sure... but I will deal with it." -Shanna

"Thieving is allowed, however, it is not easy to pickpocket another player. A backpack is the easiest to steal from... its behind and loosely bound, a small sack is much harder usually knotted and tied to the belt. Each bag carried has different levels of detection by a player and any item equiped is almost impossible to steal without detection. NPC's will adhere to the same rules... so if you try to pickpocket there is a high chance of getting caught." -Shanna

Lockpick
"Lockpicking will be a skill, but it will require lockpicks (which can break while attempting to pick a lock, etc.)

Think of it as a graph, with skill on one side and difficulty of lock on the other, with time being the variable portion. A thief with 10 skill at lockpick might be able to pick a difficulty 1 lock in 30 seconds. If he attempts to pick a difficulty 3 lock, he might fail, might break his lockpicks, and even if he succeeds, it will take him a lot longer. But if a thief with 100 skill attempt to pick that difficulty 3 lock, it might only take him 10 seconds and his chance of failure is close to none.

You can purchase basic locks, or more advanced (difficulty level) ones, depending on how secure you intend to make something. But keep in mind that "brass deadbolt" and "security systems", etc. only tend to make thieves more curious... and want to find out what's behind that door or in that chest.

If he's picking a lock that isn't in a dungeon or whatever, meaning that he SHOULDN'T be picking it in the first place (door to someone's house, the chest of the royal treasury, etc.) he will be flagged as an agressor and therefore subject to attack or capture during the time he's involved in picking the lock.

So, if he picks the lock successfully and gets what he's after, he escapes without penalty. But if you witness him doing it, you can take appropriate action because HE'S in the wrong. " -Shanna
