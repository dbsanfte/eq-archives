  Lockpicking, by Shanna
Lock picking will be a skill, but it will require lockpicks (which can break while attempting to pick a lock, etc.)

Think of it as a graph, with skill on one side and difficulty of lock on the other, with time being the variable portion. A thief with 10 skill at lockpick might be able to pick a difficulty 1 lock in 30 seconds. If he attempts to pick a difficulty 3 lock, he might fail, might break his lockpicks, and even if he succeeds, it will take him a lot longer. But if a thief with 100 skill attempt to pick that difficulty 3 lock, it might only take him 10 seconds and his chance of failure is close to none.

You can purchase basic locks, or more advanced (difficulty level) ones, depending on how secure you intend to make something. But keep in mind that "brass deadbolt" and "security systems", etc. only tend to make thieves more curious... and want to find out what's behind that door or in that chest.

If he's picking a lock that isn't in a dungeon or whatever, meaning that he SHOULDN'T be picking it in the first place (door to someone's house, the chest of the royal treasury, etc.) he will be flagged as an agressor and therefore subject to attack or capture during the time he's involved in picking the lock.

So, if he picks the lock successfully and gets what he's after, he escapes without penalty. But if you witness him doing it, you can take appropriate action because HE'S in the wrong.

  Thieves and Dirty deeds, by Shanna
.."Will it be possible to artifically change faction? Can we bribe or intimidate people?"..

I see bribery as an intricate part of dealing with NPCs of various factions. In EQ, all a rogue had to do to deal with a foreign merchant was "sneak" behind them in order to deal with them on "indifferent" terms... I see HZ taking that to a point where you'd have to bribe them in order to talk to you.

I don't know to what degree we'll take it, however. If I guard wanted to search you, then he might just have the power to do that, with or without a "warrant"... but at the same time, if you can coerce him into taking a bribe, you might be able to pass through unnoticed.

But that "unnoticed" only goes so far... if you get through the gate by using a bribe, that means that you're still suspect. If a crime happens and alerts the guards, you'll be one of the top ones on the list, because you had to bribe your way in.

Coding the AI for situations like this will take quite a lot of thought. I'm not prepared (at this time) to detail what we can and cannot do... but I will say that we're thinking about all of it.

Thanks for the good idea! Keep em comin!
