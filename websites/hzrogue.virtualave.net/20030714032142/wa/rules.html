Guild Vault:
What items and cash that is achieved during raids and can´t / won´t be used by anyone paticipating in the raid (items) or guildmates is put in the guildvault. The items / gems will be used to finance future raids and events. Items and spells will be handed out to members if anyone will use them or be up for trade for items one of our members can use.
The guildvault characters are named Wavault and Wamule and are placed on Caeryn´s account.

Guild Run Events:
Hosting events is highly encouraged. Post in the raids section and notify Lamandar (who will be maintaining the calendar) so he can put it up on the schedule. Also indicate if it is a public or guildonly raid. If it is a public raid, also post on Brellserilis.net under the proper raid section.
