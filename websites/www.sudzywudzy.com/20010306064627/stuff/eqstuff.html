Dilbeck Longfelow: Druid of Veeshan, Worshiper of Karana, currently 35th ranking Norathian.

The Story of Dilbeck Longfelow.

Dilbeck like most Halflings was born in the quiet
little woodland area known as Surefall Glade. There
he grew to be quite the stout young Halfling with a
natural affinity with the wildlife that surrounded the Glade.
With his discovery and toning of his wilderness skills
he soon took to the road not knowing what compelled
him.

Although he has had many encounters in the wilds of
Norath he has yet to find his purpose, seeking his purpose
like that of Vecks life long search for the Eye of Jamola, or
Sloraxe's search for the Foe Slammer Axe of Ispep.

Check to see the advancement of Dilbeck while he
continues his journey. To the right are some images
