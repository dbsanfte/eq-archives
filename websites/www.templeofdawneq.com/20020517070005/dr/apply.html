So How Do I Join?

DR is about achieving, accomplishing, stretching and actually reaching challenging goals.

The leaders will do their best to meet the common needs of our organization and put into action what ever it takes to conquer the toughest challenges. But it means a high level of commitment and dedication from you. Part of this commitment is reflected in our membership process. Here’s how you can become a Dawn Raider.

First off, you must meet the following minimum requirements:

Minimum Requirements
  1. Level 57+. In addition, anyone not level 60 should be able to commit to spending time leveling when not raiding with DR.
  2. Unbuffed FR/CR of 100+ and MR of 80+.

You must also agree to the following commitments:

Minimum Commitments (Read more here)

  1. Must be able to commit to ALL of at least one DR raid per week.
  2. If online during a DR raid, you MUST attend the raid.
  3. Raids are fun-work. No yelling at each other but no screwing around either. We're going to be a lean, mean, fighting machine.

You must also agree to live by and accept DR policies which you can find here. There won’t be many because we’re not interested in running a bureaucracy here - we want to MAKE STUFF DEAD!

If you meet and agree with the requirements listed above you need to apply using the application form provided here.

Applications will be considered by the Dawn Raiders Council who will review your application for completeness and vote. A unanimous vote of the Council is required for acceptance. If you fail the first vote you must wait a month to reapply.

That’s it. There’s really not that much to it but for doing your part, Dawn Raiders will commit to making your in game adventure the most memorable to date. We look forward to seeing you soon.
