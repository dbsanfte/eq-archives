Dawn Raiders is an organization/project designed to funnel the highly focused energy of hard-core raiders into an organized exploration of the high-end game.   We are not a separate guild.  We remain committed members of the Temple of Dawn.  But we have banded together to take our game experience to new level and attempt things that can only be done by dedicated players.

We hold to these basic precepts:
Raiding is our goal. Exploring the high-end game is this group's main function. We will push the limits, operate in a far more professional fashion, explore a variety of loot systems and policies, and change and adapt to the environment of a growing and changing Everquest.

Dawn Raider's is about getting the job done and doing it well.
