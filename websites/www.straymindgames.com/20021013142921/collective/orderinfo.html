Ordering Information

If you are interested in purchasing any card we have listed here you should do the following:

  1. Write down the cards that you want.
  2. Email us with your order listing our current prices for each card (to lock the price).
  3. Wait for a response from us to ensure that we have set your cards aside. (We will hold your card for 10 business days. If we have not received your payment, the cards will come off reserve and be returned to the lists.)
  4. For prompt delivery, send a Cashier's Check or Money Order made payable to David Whitaker to PO Box 20440, Bloomington MN USA 55420.
    • All Prices are in American Dollars.
    • You may also send a personal check if a US resident, but please allow up to an additional week on delivery.
    • If you would like us to send your cards via insured mail, include an additional $1, and say so in your initial email.
    • For any orders less than $10, please include $1 for postage.
    • All international orders add $2 for postage.
  5. Wait for your cards to arrive in about two weeks

It's just that easy!
