EverQuest Spell Database
The EverQuest Spell Database (EQS for short) contains information on all spells currently used in the game. (Yes, even GM-only spells, imagine that...). All data was extracted from the game files by the wonderful EQCaster program.

KNOWN BUGS
  • Because there are still some fields in the spells database that haven't got a known use, some of the information listed here might not be accurate.
  • There are some spell duplicates in the database. Why, I don't know - but some of the information for duplicates is different, so they've been added anyway.
  • Certain abilities and disciplines (notably AAXP abilities) are listed in the spell database with a level of 1. Don't let it fool you :)
  • The spell database also contains formulas for calculating damage or other effects at certain levels, or what kind of effect stats have on a spell. These formulas were all pieced together by the EQCaster programmer and might not be accurate, hence, take the information that is displayed as a result of these formulas as a guideline instead of the gospel. They are used on every spell that has a valid formula.


Below you will find various ways to search the spell database - so enjoy!

Browse By Class
Select a class in the dropdown box to the right. You will be shown all spells usable by that class, sorted by level.
 

Browse By Skill
Always wanted to know what you can do with Divination or Evocation? Select a skill to your right and find out!
 

Browse By Category
Browse spells by the category of damage they do - categories are a bit of a strange point since some spells end up in utterly strange categories. This is just here for your enjoyment and doesn't serve any real useful purpose that I can figure out just yet...
 

View Spells Using Focus Items
Always wondered what spells benefit from your focus items? Check it out here...

View Spells Requiring Reagents
This will give you a list of all spells requiring some form of reagent.
