:: Policies and Procedures ::
   
    All policies are living documents and are subject to change upon any amendments or changes being voted in by full guild members.

  » Invitation Policy «

   1. Aim for about 30 members total in the guild. Subject to change with guild approval.
   2. It is everyone's responsibility to post those that are interested in joining OE on the
       message boards in the Member Concerns section.
   3. Only Full OE Members may motion for an invite.
   4. The thread for a new member invite will be open for 7 calendar days.
   5. It is the responsibility of ALL OE Members to meet and talk and get an opinion on
       said person before proper voting.
   6. At the end of 7 calendar days, any Nay will automatically cancel the invite.
   7. At the end of 7 calendar days, if less than 3 votes are counted, the invite is not
       extended.
   8. At the end of 7 calendar days, if at least 3 votes for Yay are accounted for and no
       Nays are counted, ALL officers must make it a duty to /guildinvite said person
       and follow determined duties under being an Officer policy.
   9. 30 days after a new member is invited, said person will become a Full OE Member
       unless removed from the guild beforehand.

Note: There will be no discrimination of what type of person we are looking for in terms of who to guild. Sure, we are short on certain classes like tanks, but I would rather invite people who uphold OE family values rather than fill a spot on the "how to properly group" chart. Besides, OE has always broken the mold on who we need to do what in the world of Norrath.

  » Guild Removal Policy «

   1. Only a Full OE Member will be able to motion for a removal of another guildmate.
   2. Probationary OE members, those that have not passed the 30 day trial period,
       are automatically removed.
   3. An impartial Full OE Member will be assigned to arbitrate between for all other
       cases.
   4. To minimize risk of tearing the guild apart in civil dispute, the OE member who
       initiates the motion will state his/her reason. The Defendant will also give his/her
       side of the story.
   5. The Arbitrator will gather evidence and try to settle this without a removal.
   6. If the issue cannot be resolved, the Arbitrator will host a thread for all the guild
       to vote Yay or Nay on banishment.
   7. Majority wins in 3 days time.
   8. If Yay, said person is to be removed immediately with the reasons of banishment
       restated. Message board priviledges are further banned from said person.
   9. If Nay, said person will remain in guild, but it is imperative that all OE members
       FORGIVE. There will be NO internal banishment. If said person is voted to stay,
       holding a grudge will make things very tough on the guild.

  » Member Status and Duties «

        » OE Probationary Member «

   1. May not motion or vote on any issues...only state opinions.
   2. Will be removed from guild at a single motion from a Full Member.
   3. Represents OE and its ideals...ALWAYS.

        » OE Full Member «

   1. Passed the 30 day probationary period.
   2. Can only be banned from OE with guild approval.
   3. May motion for the invitation of any new members.
   4. May motion for the removal of any members.
   5. May vote on all guild policies and motions.
   6. May request for Officer status after 6 months time with automatic approval.
   7. Represents OE and its ideals...ALWAYS.

        » OE Officer «

   1. Same as Full OE Member, except has the responsibility to invite guild alts and new
       members as requested by the guild.
   2. An Officer that invites a new member MUST make a post in the Members General
       Chat within 24 hours of the invite stating name of new member, class, and level.
   3. An Officer MUST frequent the boards regularly to make sure that there are no invites
       pending.
   4. An Officer will upon invite release message board URL and password information to
       the new member and communicate to him/her that he/she should respond on the
       boards for introduction.

  » Membership Agreement «

       
(To be signed/accepted by all members of Outward Eye)


   1. That as a member of Outward Eye, you will not retain any other close affiliation with another guild on the Quellious Server. Your first loyalty should be to the Outward Eye Guild.

   2. That you keep your word of honor sacred. Always honor your deals/trades and maintain the guild’s good name in all of your dealings with others.

   3. Practice good sportsmanship. Never abuse the Everquest system by taking advantage of a bug or glitch in the system. Never abuse another player by tricking them into dueling, or trading with intent to defraud.

   4. “Read with best intentions.” Always read /guildsay and guild board messages with the idea that the writer had the best intentions at heart. If you read something that sounds like a slam/attack against you or another person, try and read it out loud. See if you can say it in a way that makes it not an attack or that makes it humorous. Try and remember that 80% of communication is non-verbal, and text message can easily be misunderstood.

On the other hand, whenever you write a message in /guildsay or on the guild message board always go back and re-read it before you submit it. Make sure that you are not writing something that could be taken the wrong way.

Also realize that the Outward Eye has a few younger members. Please refrain from using profanity in /guildsay and on the guild message board.

SLURS AGAINST RACE, SEXUAL ORIENTATION, OR NATIONALITY ARE STRICTLY PROHIBITED.

   5. As a member of Outward Eye you are expected to check in with the guild at least once per month. If you are without a computer, you can still attempt to check in with the guild via this message board from a friend's computer, a public library computer, or a work computer. In addition to your normal online time, at least some time should be spent helping out the guild or guild members. This can take a variety of forms. Some examples might include working on the guild’s web page, hunting for items for other guild members, helping level-up your guild mates, or doing research on different zones and reporting back to the guild. Ask your guildleader or other officers if you need help in finding something to do for the guild.

   6. If a guildmate asks for assistance on /guildsay it is the duty of every guildmate online at that time to see that they get assistance.

NOTE: If a guildmate abuses this power (s)he will not be a member for very long. However, it is right to think that a member of Outward Eye should always have friends that they can call on at any time.

   7. “Guildmate Priority” It is suggested for all guild members to :
* Keep the guild informed if you are in a good group/spot. If there are less then 6 members in that group you should try your best to fill the group with additional people from Outward Eye.

* Upon entering the game and do not have prior plans your first attempts to group should be with existing members of Outward Eye. In addition, if possible you should try and group with other guildmates unless there is a specific reason not to.

   8. “Courtesy and Charity.” Although it is not required, it is in good taste to always be courteous when dealing with other players. In problem situations always try and resolve things amiably and never resort to name calling, bullying, or profanity. Whenever you are dealing with a GM or a GUIDE always treat them with respect. In any situation where you are being subjected with profanity or training/harassment feel free to /report and /petition them to the GMs.

To be charitable is to give without recompensation. It is suggested that you be charatible to those that deserve it in order to help the image of Outward Eye. This does not mean that you *have to* always be giving, and non-selfish. However, occasionally you can give free teleports, free buffs, low-level items (backpacks, leather armor), or simply take the time to explain the system to new users and help them with their problems. All of these things give the Outward Eye and its members a great reputation.
