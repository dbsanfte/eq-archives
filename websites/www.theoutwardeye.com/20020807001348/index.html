Rage, rage against the dying light! Posted on Aug. 04 2002,10:22
Another day...another Ragefire dead.....

So, I manage to log on EQ late Saturday, but when I did the response from guild was overwhelming. No, they weren't shouting their love and admiration for me - they were shouting "RAGE", "RAGE UP", and "RAGEFIRE", "GET TO SKYFIRE", "DOES ANYONE HAVE EXTRA MUFFINS?".

Our favorite high elven cleric, Salubre of The Violet Hour, was tonight's lucky cleric. My timing was impeccable as always. Taluzan and Karahd rushed to FV to pick me up and we ran to the staging area in Skyfire.

Outward Eye was in full effect along a gathering of Quellious's finest. About the time I finally reduced my resolution so I could actually move, was the time Rage was pulled. He was beatdown and beatdown fast.

The drops this time around were a Blazing BP, 100% weight reduction bag, and red dragon scales. Outward Eye proved once again that all Ragefire lewt are belong to us. Karahd came away with the bag and yours truly won the red dragon scales.

All in all, a great time. We've got another friend to pester for rezzes! Karahd has something to hold all her foraged roots, and I am one step closer in what is a very long journey.

OE curse was broken!! Posted on Aug. 02 2002,00:27
Tachia and Brazzo break the OE rolling curse!

What started out as just another night of cabbage picking, turned into a night of killing dragons and wurms.  Our friend Salubre of The Violet Hour was helping camp Ragefire for the cleric in front of her on the Rage List.  Salubre called out to OE for assistance and we quickly assembled with minimal banking.   13 OE members showed up to help take down Ragefire.  Ragefire when down, the camping cleric got the cleric drop, and we waited for the rolls on the red scale and the 100 percent weight reduction bag.  The camping cleric announced that everyone will roll on the red scale /random 1000.  After about 40 rolls the winner was announce.... GRATZ TACHIA!!!  The next set of rolls are for the bag.  Everyone rolls and the lucky halfling wins.... GRATZ BRAZZO!!!

Could the curse of the OE rolls be broken forever???  We will find out next time.

Good luck to Salubre of TVH on camping Ragefire.  OE members be ready for the call, cause our friends from TVH are next up.  Don't forget to send her a tell or two to keep her awake, while she waits for that evil vendor to spawn.

Part 2:

Since we were in Skyfire and 2 Guardian Wurms were up, we decided to test our luck on obtaining a few the elusive scrolls that these two wurms have been know to read.

The OE crew pulled in these 2 big level 60 wurms and quickly dispatched the 530 quad hitting wurms with ease.  OE's luck didn't roll over to the drops, as there were no Torpor or Malo drops tonight.  Thanks to the east coasters that stayed up late to help kill these two.  We'll get them next time.

If I Ever See a Reaver Again, I'm Gonna... Posted on July 29 2002,01:25
Well, I'll probably run and hide behind a big barbarian tank.  Anyhoo, we made our way to City of Mist today to take another shot at getting the Reaver camp (what is it with this camp these days?).  Finally caught a chance at the camp, rushed in there, got Harfa smooshed, and then we really got down to business.

Any fans who followed the tale of our last trip to CoM will know that we didn't have a single reaver respawn on death in a full 4 rounds of spawns up and downstairs.  Not one.  It was amazing.  That's normally a good thing if you're just clearing them to get the Shaman epic quest done, but when you are there explicitly to get Jade Reavers, the more spawns in the least time the better.  Well, we outdid ourselves this time.  I don't think anyone has an official count, but the concensus was that in one cycle we had a reaver respawn itself 10 extra times.  That is a good way to burn through a mana bar.  If you think the healing was bad, try casting Cure Blindness three times per tank every 30 seconds.  Next time we get them seeing eye dogs and I'll save my mana for more important things...like drawing pictures on the ground using summon bread.

In the end, we got enough MoSS to supply an army of twink ShadowKnights, a random 50ish spell, some sellable junk, and 0 Jade Reavers in about 4 hours of work.  Oh well, maybe next time.
