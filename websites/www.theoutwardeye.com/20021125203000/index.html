Whatcha gonna do when OEmania runs wild on you? Posted on Sep. 12 2002,06:39
Last night, the Eyes and their distinguished guests headed to Sirens Grotto. Not since the Real World Seattle have more fishes been thrown, ugly girls slapped, or sexualities questioned.

As MC Hammer was once known for commenting, we truly "turned this mutha out". Hell, Taluzan was a walking Exxon Valdez as he stormed his way through the place making sure to club every baby seal in his path. First we took out Mistress Latazura in what was a very cool battle full of bloody death but perseverance on the part of the OE crew. Our next target was some named Priestess. Yeah, she got smacked down too. Then after a long and thrilling night we prepared to camp...until Shimmering Sea Spirit came up on track. That's when I turned into a 4 year old in a grocery store that won't leave until you buy him something. So we worked our way back to the spawn and delivered swift justice to the disgruntled elemental. What did we get? My beloved wavecrasher or the uber Drums of the Beast? Hell no, all we got was an  average knight shield.

That said, the night was a blash. Walrus skin drums were falling from the sky, sure to keep our legions of bard twinks happy. A couple of Immaculate Shell Horns were rumored to have dropped as well. Plus a slew of other stuff I forgot to write down. And our velious quest gem coffers have been well replenished.

And don't worry, we didn't forget about you Behemoth and Fellspine, we'll be back.
