:: The Oath - Guild Charter ::
   

The Outward Eye Guild is a collection of minds and swords, brought together by a common set of principals and beliefs; beliefs which transcend race, gender, profession, and at times even religion. These basic tenets act as the glue that joins each individual member to the rest, in effect, forming a single unified entity. And just as single stones, weak and useless on their own, may be joined to form a mighty fortress, so shall we be. United in our goals, assertive in our pursuits, and stalwart in our beliefs, no matter the opposition. The basic precepts that guide our attitudes and action are as follows:

  » Honor «

Each member shall conduct him or herself in an ethical manner, and as a result, take responsibility for all her or her own actions, and be eager to face the resultant consequences, wether glorious or dreadful.

  » Justice «

Each member shall strive to be at all times fair, and ever mindful of the rights and interests of others over those of the self.

  » Humility «

Each member is constantly aware of their weakness when separate from their kindred. This mind set however, should never lead to an attitude of downtroddenness or pessimism. Rather, each member realizes his or her infinite value when untied with others, as well as the heightened strength and wisdom inherent in such glorious a collection of hands and minds.

  » Loyalty «

There is a bond between members. Unspoken yet known, intangible yet undeniable. Our ardor and devotion to one another is our greatest strength. Our goal as a guild is to uphold these sacred concepts, and to act as champions for their cause. Each member considers it a personal duty to stamp out dishonor, selfishness, injustice, vain gloriousness, deceit and disloyalty where ever it occurs.
