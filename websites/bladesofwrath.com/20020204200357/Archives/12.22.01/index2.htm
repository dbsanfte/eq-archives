All I Want For Christmas is My Two Front Teeth...

...and a shot of Tandaman dead on the guild web site, the guild web site, the guild web site. All I want for Christmas is Tandaman dead on...You get the point.

Sadly, I have to be honest. Tandaman didn't die at all this week. That's an old picture. I know you're all very disappointed. As am I. But take this to heart, dear BoW fans - he didn't die this week because we didn't really raid. So we'll have to make sure he dies more than usual after the holidays.

We did, however, gather a few people who were ignoring their families for EQ (and significant others, as last week's poll shows) to go whack this guy named Thought Horror Overfiend in the Deep. To my "delight", Arakias led this raid instead of taking me to dinner and a movie.
