Honorary Members was an Idea brought about by on of our Officers. I think it is a great idea. There are times in our Travels when we meet and hunt with people & Guilds that we think are great but for what ever reason they can not join our guild. For people & Guilds like this, we will add them as Honorary Members. Any person that is an Honorary Member will be given all the same benefits as any full guild member and is always welcome on our web site, message board, and all guild events. If anyone knows of someone they think should be a honorary member please e-mail me.

Honorary Members list:

All members of Obsidian Fear (Allies) Guild will be treated as Honorary Members.

All members of Wrath of Vaer (Allies) Guild will be treated as Honorary Members.

All members of DarkBlood (Friendship Treaty) Guild will be treated as Honorary Members.
