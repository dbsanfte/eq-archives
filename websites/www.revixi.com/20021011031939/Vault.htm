Welcome to the Revixi bank. Where you can get a Loan or Equipment.

To get a loan you must meet all these qualifications. All these qualifications are easy to do if you are a good and active member of Revixi.

Be a Level 10 or Higher member for 2 months or more.

Post on the message boards at least once a week regularly.

Visit our web site weekly.

Be Registered and setup under the Revixi guild at Guild Magic.

Make at least one guild event a month.

Be a main character.

Agree to pay back the cash loan with in a month with interest or longer if agreed on by banker.

Agree to return a piece of equipment when you get a better one.

Loan Limits: All loans are for 30 days and must be paid back by then unless arrangements have been made. Larger Loans may be made but only by arrangements by the guild council. Also all loans are subject to funds being available.  There will be a 1 loan per month limit or until the previous loan has been repaid. The below listed amounts are the Max amounts to be loaned so some loans may be made for less. To apply for a loan post in the apply for a loan area of our message board. One of us will get back to you asap.
Lev 10       200pp Max      Pay back 210pp
Lev 11-19 300pp Max      Pay back 320pp
Lev 20-29 400pp Max      Pay back 430pp
Lev 30-39 500pp Max      Pay back 550pp
Lev 40-49 600pp Max      Pay back 700pp
Lev 50-60 1k pp  Max      Pay back 1200pp

Deposits: If a member wants to deposit 200pp or more it will earn 5% interest per month but must be left in the whole month to collect plat. Deposits may be withdrawn on the 1st of the month only. Interest will be paid on the 1st of the month also.

Donations: This idea will only work if we get donations from all our guild members. It will pay off for you in the long run. To make a donation please send a tell to any Officer.
