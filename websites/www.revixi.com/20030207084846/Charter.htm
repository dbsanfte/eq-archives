Revixi Charter

Hail and well met, adventurer! Welcome to our home!

This charter has been set forth to acquaint you with our ways, and to protect you and all others within this guild.

Our Name

We are Revixi, derived from Latin meaning "to return to life". In choosing this name we kept in our hearts the history of our beginnings, which you can read below, and made a commitment to never give in to adversity. We will ever strive to live life to its fullest and help each other achieve the goals that we desire.

Our Goal

We wish to provide a fun, safe, and supportive atmosphere for all adventurers to enjoy their days in the world of Norrath. We will continue to form a guild that is honest in wanting to help one another and thinks of each other as family. We also hope that through our actions maybe somewhere along the way we can bring a smile to the faces of our fellow adventurers.

Membership Requirements

All adventurers seeking entrance to Revixi will be of level 5 or higher unless the Guild Leader makes an exception.
Pledges are required to go to our Message Board and post an introduction or reason for wishing to join in the Applicants Forum to be considered for an invitation.
After Officers have read your introduction and contacted you, an Invitation will be given. Within three days of accepting an Invitation, the new member is expected to go to GuildMagic and register their character with our Guild and to post in the New Member's forum on our Message Board. New members will be considered on probation for their first few weeks for observation.
The reason we expect these things is to ensure the adventurer is making an educated and serious decision to join our Guild, and so that the other Guild members may get to know them.

Expectations

Using the Roleplaying tag is allowed, however, using the Anonymous tag is NOT allowed. This hides your guild tag from view and has lead to problems in the past.
Always remember that your actions reflect the guild as a whole. While it is not possible to be polite in all situations, please do your best. If you have a question regarding conduct, please consult our members, or an Officer privately. Many of our members have been in this game a long time, and some have been, or may be EQ Guides.
It is unacceptable to repeatedly ask other Guild members for ports, easily accessible corpse retrievals, equipment, and other things you may desire. Many will help you in such situations, but it is at the discretion of the individual. Keep in mind that your Guildmates will not always be available to help you, but we will make an effort to do so.
Don't Kill Steal. We all hate it when people do it to us, and we find it unacceptable to do to others. Accidental kill stealing does happen, but this can be a basis for removal from the guild if the behavior is found to be a habit.
Please make an effort to say hello and good-bye in guild chat to announce your presence and departure. As a family we like to know when our siblings are around.
If you are having difficulties with any Guild member or one of our Allies, please contact an officer and the problem will be investigated and dealt with.
You may have one alternate adventurer for each 10 levels of your main adventurer in the Guild. Therefore, if you are level 10 you may have one alternate in the guild and once you reach 20 you may have 2 alternates in the Guild, and so forth. This encourages members to experience new professions without a tremendous number of seldom played alternates counted as members of the Guild.

Benefits

All Guild members will have access to the Guild's tradesmen to help with armor, weapons, and other items. The Foreman oversees these duties. Any item produced through tradeskills will be provided at cost to guild members, or the combining performed for them on their own components free of charge.
Magic users will receive assistance getting their spells up to the fourth circle.
Participation in Guild raids and events. These are always fun and are a great way to make friendships as well as having the possibility to win the random roll for equipment and collecting pieces needed for quests. We adhere to the NBG ("Need before Greed") policy unless instructed otherwise.
A Message Board that is used as an information tool as well as a place just to post what's on your mind. This is the primary place for postings on upcoming events and information.
Help with any questions you might have. We have Officers dedicated to helping new members, to help with class information, one specifically for Epics as well as many helpful members that will answer your questions.
A Guild Bank and Vault. Loans of platinum and equipment can be made to Guild members through this system.
A Guild of friendly people to group with, laugh with, and talk to.
Allied Guilds that we can do group events and quests with.

Events

We strive to keep an ongoing flow of events. These events include hunting events, weddings, educational activities and many more.
Check the Message Board for upcoming events and quests. All Guild members have the opportunity to organize and run events if they wish to do so. Non-Guild adventurers may also participate in our events and quests upon the approval of the event leader.

What not to expect from the Guild

We do not encourage higher-level adventurers to power level younger adventurers. If members wish to volunteer to do this, that is fine, but asking for it is not.
We do not provide equipment to twink our members. Some members may have extra equipment that they have outgrown that they wish to give or sell to Guildmates, but do not expect free equipment simply because you are in the Guild and continued begging after one warning is grounds for immediate removal from the Guild.
If these are things that you seek in a guild, then this is probably not the home you are looking for.

Conclusion

Please understand that having read this Charter and the expectations set forth within it, if you post seeking an Invitation you are agreeing to live up to the expectations of Revixi that have been outlined here or accept the possibility of being removed. The expectations can and will be changed by the Guild Officers and/or the full vote of the Guild. Any concerns voiced about our expectations by members will be discussed and a decision made. These words are binding in the Lands of Norrath in that removal from the guild by force can be enacted if they are not heeded. If you find these expectation unacceptable, peace be with you and you have our well wishes that you do find a family that is more suited to your needs. If, on the other hand, you desire to be amongst us, we are waiting to hear from you.
