After hunting cougars in Iceclad Ocean from 34 to 39 this is what I think. Hunting cougars here can be very dangerous. But if done right, it can be the fastest place to go from 25 to 40 I have found. First, make sure to stay in a group. There are groups here all the time. Next, kill all the time. You can kill non stop every hour you want to play. This is where the fast leveling comes from. The Cougar Claw ear ring comes from here. They sell for about 400 plat most of the time. This is a very nice place to level and zip from 25 to 40. There does not seem to be much loss of exp when grouping with higher level people. I grouped with Lolili when she was 25 and I was 36 and she leveled just as fast as I did. Good luck.

Snow Cougars are 28 to 35. The best and safest way we have found is to park your group just a bit from the Igloos and have your puller bring them to you. Have them pull one or two at a time. If your puller pulls 4 and your group is not battle experienced or of high level you will most likely die. There is no zone to run to so death by a over pull is a real possibility. There is a black cat here called Midnight he(she) is a good fight so try to take him on by himself. If you are by the Igloos you may see Shadow Guardian level 27 to 31 and a Dire Wolf level 28 to 36 at times. Some people hunt next to a tower here there are snow cougars here also and many more Shadow Guardians and Dire Wolves. It is much safer to hunt by the Igloos in my opinion. If you are a caster try not to agro the cats on you as they hit hard :)
