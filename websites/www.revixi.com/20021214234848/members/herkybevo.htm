Barbarian Shaman
Assistant Guild Leader



Herkybevo is a Shamaness born in the northern town of Halas. Her Father was one of the Shaman elders, while her Mother was a well respected and liked Rogue with the Legion of the White Rose. Generations before, Herky's father's family, the ancient Sowalots, was one of several families that were entrusted by the mighty Veeshan and the Ring of Scale to keep watch over a treacherous white dragon entombed in the Permafrost lair. This dragon's name was Lady Vox.

One day, while her father and others were on a routine patrol of Vox's prison, they were ambushed by Goblins and Ice Giants. Somehow Vox had gained control of these two races and charmed them to do her bidding. No one survived the ambush... All of Halas could hear what sounded like a maniacal laugh, but many passed it off as just the wind. The entrusted families knew better. Herky swore vengeance on all dragons (not associated with Veeshan) and Giants of any faction.

Herky's mission in life was to see these monsters die by her hands. She knew that she needed more than just her powerful magic, so she went to her uncle who was a Master trainer in the Warrior guild. But since she was not a Warrior, it was forbidden to show her the lethal ways of Barbarian combat. Still the old Master felt his niece's pain and anguish and thus, agreed to train her in secret. She quickly mastered the practice of blunt and piercing weapons over the passing months, with revenge being her primary motivation and the oath to never let another wee Barbarian child feel the pain she had endured. But before she could be trained with swords and other slashing weapons, her uncle was sent on a secret mission to the Nektulos Forest. He has not been found or heard from since...

A short time later while on a routine patrol in Everfrost Peaks, Herky ran into a female High Elf. Herky had never seen an Elf of any sort before, and was amazed at her mystical powers. She asked the Elf to join her. She later found out the High Elf's name was Brierley and that she was a Magician. They became instant friends. She took Brierley friend back to Halas, but to her dismay Brierley was looked down upon with cold shoulders from many other residents of Halas. She was told by the Council members of Halas that Brierley had to earn their trust, and that it would be a long process. Upon this it was agreed, but Herkybevo refused to shun Brierley, thus then and there she decided to leave Everfrost Peaks and explore the world together with new found friend...

As the Assistant Guild Leader of Revixi, Herkybevo uses her responsibilities to teach, help and lead the Members in new and adventurous directions. She has a reputation known far and wide as being kind-hearted to those who need it, but also as a brutal "Battle Shammy" to those who deserve it. Herkybevo's political savvy also serves Revixi well as she has many contacts with our Allies and serves as an esteemed Council Member on the Ring of Honor.
