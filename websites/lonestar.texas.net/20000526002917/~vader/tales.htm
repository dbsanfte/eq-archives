Off the western coast of the great Celtic nations and just south of the Isle of Skye lies a mystical island forever shrouded in the thick fog of the northern seas. The island of Inziladun, perhaps the last refuge of the Queya Elves, remains to this day all but lost to the human races. This is where I, Anarion Karnevalinar, known to the human Celts as simply Keegan, was born. The Quenya Elves stayed constantly in battle against the evil Istari and Khazad Dwarves, so I was forced to learn the ways of steel early in life. It was in one such battle against the Istari that I was to become aware of my destiny....

Everything was dark. I could still hear the sounds of war, but they sounded faint and distant. The nauseating stench of my own burnt flesh filled my nostrils, and the taste of blood and bile filled my throat. I had been struck by the evil magiks of the Istari and knew that soon I would be standing at the gates of Elvar. But just as my last breath filled my lungs, so did a soft light fill my eyes. I felt a gentle touch on my forehead and heard a faint whisper saying to me, "It is not yet your time, Anarion. Your destiny lies beyond the seas." As I struggled to open my eyes, I heard the soft whisper again, saying, "Evermist..." As I looked around, I saw that I was no longer where I had been. My pain was gone, and my flesh once again whole. As I climbed to my feet, a shaft of light shone down and lit the path before me, shining on a golden sword suspended above an altar atop seven marble steps. I was awash with awe and compelled toward the golden blade. As my fingers entwined the haft, a word burned into my thoughts. The sword had spoken to me. It had told me its name. BANE... I awoke as if from a dream, lying on a grassy hillside. The smell of heather was strong in the breeze, and the sun shone warmly on my face. I sat up slowly, my head still spinning from my strange dream. I felt for the reassuring familiarity of my sword, but my hand fell upon a pommel unknown to me. I looked down and saw the golden sword of my dream. Looking around, I frantically searched for the sight of something familiar, yet nothing was. I raced to the top of the hill, and upon reaching its crest, the sight of a sprawling human city lay before me and the whisper of "Evermist" brushed my ears as if a breeze. I had not a dream.

Many questions remain unanswered to me and I know not how I got here. But I continue to fight evil, weilding BANE, the divine sword gift and fulfilling my destiny............
