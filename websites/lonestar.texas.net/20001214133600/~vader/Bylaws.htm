ByLaws of the

Right and Noble Order of EXCALIBUR

The Right and Noble Order of Excalibur is an Order of Knighthood dedicated to the high conduct required of members set forth in the Code of Chivalry. It exists to encourage and foster Honor and Chivalry in real life and online, as well as provide a forum for those members who wish to roleplay in a medieval atmosphere.

We are dedicated to helping all our members with adventuring and tutoring them in all quests they persue as well as the fine art of combat and Quests within the games we are playing in. We have many ceremonies, including those of promotions from Squire to Knight, Paladin and perhaps even KnightMarshall. We have an ArchCleric to perform weddings as well as bless the knights as they go into battle, and an GrandMage to oversee the mystic requirements of the Guild.

Monarchs - The Queen, LadyDiana and King, SirGawain, shall preside over all ceremonies, guild meetings, and Curia meetings. In their absence, the Regent shall perform these functions. The Monarchs may also present items to the Curia for discussion.

Regent - (Sir Morgon de Oro) The Regent manages the day to day affairs of the Guild, tournaments, and games. He is entitled to one vote of the Curia. Additionally, he shall be responsible for matters that arise not covered by the Bylaws. The Regent also keeps orders of business of the Curia.

ArchCleric - (Accoma) The ArchCleric shall manage all ceremonies, cast one vote in the Curia, and governs the clerical body of EXCALIBUR. The ArchCleric may also present items to the Curia.

GrandMage - (Mintar) The Grand mage shall lead the Mystic Order of Old, set forth policy regarding all mages, assign apprentices to experienced Mages for training, and cast one vote in Curia meetings.

Curia - Consists of permanent members. These include the King, Queen, Regent Morgon de Oro, Accoma, the KnightMarshalls. Additionally, the Crown Champion and the King and Queen's Counsel may sit in the Curia meetings. The Curia discusses and votes on new members, and all other issues brought forth.

Crown Champion - (Changes monthly) The Crown Champion shall be chosen by trial by combat upon a decided day of the month. In the event that a permanent member of the Curia wins the tournament, the highest placing non-Curia member shall serve in this position. Currently, in Illusia, we do not yet have challenge combat. Once it is available, we will reinstitute the monthly Crown Champion tournament.

Ranking within the Guild

Page - all members entering EXCALIBUR-!- will not be inducted into the Guild until that Page has adventured with several current members. There is no time factor involved. The members who have adventured with said Page shall either verbally or written, send their recommendations to the Curia. The Curia will then vote on admitting said Page and assign him to an Order at that time. He shall then be inducted and wear the EXCALIBUR-!- Guild tag.

Squire - A squire is a knight in training. Promotion will require knowledge of questing as well as, most importantly, the standards EXCALIBUR-!- expects of its members. They are assigned to a Knight for guidance in what we stand for as well as the Knight determining the intentions of this player. It takes more than just joining a guild to be an Excal member, it takes a show of honor and loyalty. Once an oath is taken - we only want people who intend to honor that pledge during the good times as well as the bad. Requests to join EXCALIBUR-!- by any player must be accompanied by a letter of recommendation from a member of no less than Knight rank.

A squire advances to knight, upon the recommendation of the knight to whom he is squired. The proper protocol for this is: First - the Knight, with the approval of the KnightMarshall of the Order, shall assign a quest to the Squire - as an example - gathering some specific items and returning them to the knight, showing he as performed the quest as asked. This quest may not be given until the Squire has been a member for no less than 30 days. Also requesting of the KnightMarshall of the Order you are in to consider promotion for this Squire. A test should be given to this Squire on the Code of Chivalry or the Knights Oath - this just insure they have read them and understand them. At this point, with the approval of the said KnightMarshall, the squire shall then be known as Knight-Errant. The Knightmarshall then requests of LadyDiana to be added to the agenda for a meeting to announce his request for promotion for his squire to the Curia members present. The squire must have shown dedication to his brethren, humility, basic knowledge of our homeland, RPing ability, and a chivalrous manner at all times.

Knight - The finest and most noble and chivalrous characters shall attain this rank. A proven reputation for honor, valor, and chivalry are neccessary for this rank.. Advancing to Paladin protocol - the Paladin or KnightMarshall of the Order may recommend a knight to the Curia for promotion by following the above-mentioned steps. The knight must have proven his ability to teach squires, learn the quests of the land, all aspects of battle, and a wish to aid all new players, and prove his willingness to do more than just play a game online.

Paladins - They are responsible for leading battles and assisting other members in honing their combat skills, as well as aiding others in their respective quests. They need to have good skills with handling problems if the KnightMarshalls are not available and the dedication to work outside the game to keep their Orders strong within EXCALIBUR-!-

Promotion to KnightMarshall may only be attained when one of the Curia recommends the Paladin for promotion or the Crown has taken notice of exceptional dedication of a member. Requirements for KnightMarshall are a willingness to dedicate time to their Order - keep justice and compassion in mind at all times, and teach by example the values of Honor and Chivalry.

KnightMarshall - These are the most highly decorated Paladins that have proven their leadership abilities. They assist all other KnightMarshalls with any advice they may have to offer on certain situations. Dedication to the Order in every aspect, including suggestions to the Crown for bettering the Order, consideration and understanding for all members, organizational skills, and above all, responsibility are of great import for this title.

Rules of Conduct -

Under no circumstances shall a member assault, harass, or attempt to disgrace another player while wearing the EXCALIBUR epithet. Dishonorable actions (abusive behavior to any player, rudeness, foul language, or a generally unchivalrous attitude) shall not be tolerated by the Crown and is grounds for immediate dismissal.

It is also encouraged to be of aid to those who do not know the game as well. Role-playing is encouraged as long as it is done in a fine and noble manner. EXCALIBUR-!- portrays a middle ages gaming world and is based in a Celtic background.

Dismissal -

If it should become necessary to implement dismissal procedures against a member the following shall occur:

1. The accusation will be made to the Curia, including all facts related to the matter, such as cheating or violating the Code of Chivalry, to which the knight has given his oath.

2. A meeting of the Curia shall hear evidence and testimony in the matter.

3. The member may contest the dismissal and may request audience with the Curia to present his case. This member may ask to bring witnesses to testify on his behalf.

4.. The Curia shall vote on dismissal and inform the member of the results.

HONOR Glory Majesty Unity --- EXCALIBUR`
