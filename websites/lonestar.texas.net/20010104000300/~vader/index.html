The Right and Noble Order of Excalibur is a role playing guild that originated in the now defunct Imagination Network, and has expanded its presence to other online role-playing games. We meld the courage and perserverance of the Celts, the honor and chivalry of Arthurian Knights, and the loyalty and bravery of the German Knights into one being - a member of EXCALIBUR-!-   We currently have online homes in EverQuest, Illusia, Faldon, and 4th Coming.  We will also be expanding Asherons Call and any other interesting places we may find.

Guild Excalibur is dedicated to the preservation of chivalry in all aspects, and is based on the divine providences of:

Excalibur was created and is ruled by Queen LadyDiana and her liege, SirGawain. The above four cornerstones represent our core ethics, and all members are equally judged by these standards. Our primary purpose is enjoyment of online role playing games via expanding the experience thru re-creation of renaissance nobility structure and medieval customs. We employ these values in our questing, honorable challenge combat, and, of course, the pleasure of sharing these experiences with our members and friends.

Our ByLaws detail the organization and standards of its membership. Please review them if you are considering petitioning for membership in Excalibur.

If you wish to petition for membership in Excalibur, please contact LadyDiana.   Please include your inet email address in the text of your letter.
