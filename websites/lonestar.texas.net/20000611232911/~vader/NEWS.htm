    Erudin Civil War Report The Erudin Civil War took place today. The following is an account of it: The Civil War that took place in Erudin today had a great turnout. At its peak over ninety people participated. The zone was filled to the brim of eager spectators and combatants. Due to the large nature of this event, the event became unorganized and the attack was delayed for a half-hour. People just stood around outside the city. The war finally began with the crowd of spectators and warriors charging into the city and invading the Erudin City Library. The guards put up no resistance at all and the library quickly found itself under siege. People literally poured into the small door flooding all three stories of this massive building. Spectators and warriors scrambled everywhere. The Library had three stories, each completely occupied. It became so crowded on the second and third floors that people began falling to their death to the ground below. The guardians and keepers of the Library were quickly slain, as if lightning had struck them dead. Only one woman gave any resistance. Her name was Onissa. This fearless librarian killed more than any other NPC or PC. A few brave individuals gathered the strength up to try and best her in combat, but all were quickly slain. Eventually two fearless Barbarians engaged her in battle and defeated her. (These two fearless Barbarians were Sir Pythos and Sir Galan) Moments later the zone crashed, ending the war. The building had been completely conquered. The only NPCs left standing were the pets of Necromancers and other spellcasters

The Crown and Curia of EXCALIBUR-!- would like to congratulate all the new squires and apprentices that were formally accepted into the Order at the meeting. We look forward to adventuring with you.

As EXCALIBUR-!- is growing to great proportions in many various lands, the Curia has determined a new policy for prospective members.

    Should any squire or apprentice find a prospective member, please have them read our main page and print a copy of the Code of Chivalry. Then seek out any KnightMarshall, Knight, or Mage to request that the Curia to review a prospective member. They shall spend time with that person to determine if the prospective member truly understands and adheres to the Tenets of EXCALIBUR-!-, before requesting they be accepted as a member. That person, should they be accepted, shall be Squire to the member that recommended them. Shouldst a Squire discover a person requesting membership, he shouldst then consult with a KnightMarshall, Knight or Mage, and inform them of the request, so that the above requirements shall be met. There will be a test on the code of Chivalry given to each prospective member!
