Code Of Conduct
You will treat all other members of the guild with respect and dignity.
No stealing.
Be courteous about all actions you do.
Think about certain actions before performing them.
Candidate should demonstrate (Fully In Character) the full understanding and agreement of our Guild Charter.
If there is a problem, deal with it responsibly. Any complications, see an Officer.
When dealing with other Permanemus members, you must be courteous, respect other members.
Members will not insult, slander or demean a fellow guild member personally or publicly. Members must not cause conflict within the guild, ripping on others or cutting others down will not be tolerated, UNLESS in jest. If a personal issue arises between you and another member you will immediately bring it to the attention of an officer online.
You will uphold the same level of conduct as stated above when grouping with other guilds and/or nonguilded players. There are players in the game who do not uphold the same standards as we do. Get over it. If someone joins your group and ninja loots the item you have been camping for 10 hours then promptly gates, you are still required to uphold this code. Simply report the situation to an officer or me and then let it go. We will not tolerate any member who breaks the code in these situations, regardless of rank.
Guildchat will not be abused.
Guildchat is used for communication. You can talk about anything as long as you do not offend others.
Members must not bring the guild into disrepute, acts that will diminish the name of Permanemus will NOT be tolerated, resulting in up to 3 warnings and ultimately may result in withdrawel from the guild.
We do not condone anti-social behaviour (eg. Kill Stealing)
Excessive begging over guild chat will not be tolerated. Everyone gets taken care of.
Existing members may have their other characters join the guild without going through the approval process.
Any member who has left the guild and wishes to rejoin must apply for a full vote of the council before they may be readmitted into the guild
All Members must submit a valid e-mail address. To be used in correspondence for guild events and such.
HAVE FUN. Enjoy the game.
Permanemus Tactics
Soloing Tactics:
Always try to find a place to camp that is next to a zone.
Do not encumber yourself, always sell at the closest merchant.
If you are a non-warrior, try to stay far away as possible from the enemy as you can.
Stay buffed. If you are a low level person, try to get higher level friends to buff you.
Always consider your enemy. By doing this, it makes the difference if you live or die.
Grouping Tactics:
Have a designated healer.
Make one person pull to the whole entire group. (Make sure that this person has spirit of wolf).
Always assist on the puller. (It is very useful to just make a hotkey).
For the puller, always consider your enemies. Target your hardest enemy first.
Always buff everyone to the fullest.
Always go into battle with as much mana as possible or until the healers and nukers say they are ready.
Let the tanks tank, nukers and healers should stay at a distance from the enemies.
For looting, decide on an acceptable method.
Always try to form a group with tanks, nukers (aka mages, enchanters, etc), and healers.
Some good combination of groups are:
Tank, Tank, Mage, Enchanter, Druid, and Cleric.
Tank, Tank, Tank, Mage, Bard, and Cleric.
Tank, Shaman, Tank, Necromancer, Druid, and Cleric.
Tank, Shaman, Tank, Enchanter, Bard, and Cleric.
Tank, Tank, Tank, Tank, Druid, and Cleric.
PERMANEMUS: Duties/Roles of an Officer
At all times, officers must show leadership.
Officers will get the privilege of recruiting members.
Officers will also get the privilege of removing others from the guild with their own discretion.
When members need help, you must go out of you way and respect their plead.
To join Permanemus a potential recruit must be a minimum of level 5 and be recommended to the guild by at least one guild member who has held membership for at least one month.
A recommendation is not to be taken lightly as it reflects on the person providing the recommendation.
No existing members should recommend a potential recruit unless they have spent time "partying" with that person.
The Recruit initiation involves a one month period, which begins the first day they join, in which members of the guild have an opportunity to comment and evaluate the potenetial guildmember.
