CooperBeast and CheckBeast are BMW Mini Coopers, PugBeast is a 206cc






 
 
 
 
CooperBeast  and CheckBeast are BMW minis and PugBeast is a Peugeot 206cc owned and loved by Adrian and Chrissie - Beastmaster and Peanut to you.CooperBeast is an October 2002 build Mini Cooper in Chili red with a white roof, which we got from new at the start of November 2002. CheckBeast is an October 2005 build Mini Cooper S special edition called Checkmate, which we got from new at the start of November 2005. It is metallic space blue with a silver roof and bonnet stripes and chequered graphics on each front side panel. PugBeast is an October 2001 Peugeot 206cc se 2.0 litre in cayman green with matching green and black cloth interior.
 
This is a small website designed to enable us to share some of the adventures and fun we have had with the minis and the Pug. The emphasis though is on our mini adventures. The website has plenty of photos and write ups from mini meets and runs that we have enjoyed and we have an ever increasing selection of mini videos on the mini movies page too. View at your peril because they will make you want a mini!             

The mini is not just a fun car but a whole social life too, thanks to the mini community, which for us is centred around MINI2.com and newminiseverns.com where we have met hundreds of mini enthusiasts and participated in lots of mini days and mini runs. For Pug enthusiasts www.peugeot206cc.co.uk has all the information you could want.


Anyway, have a look around our site, we hope you enjoy it.
