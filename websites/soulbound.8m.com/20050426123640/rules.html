Guild Rules

1.  Respect all players, including non-guild members. 
2.  Do not pester other guild members for equipment or money, it's a good way to ensure you do NOT get any.  This is not to say that the guild won't help you but we will not tolerate beggars!
3.  There will be no cursing or lewd comments in guildsay.  Everyone gets frustrated at times and this is understood, but please do not type anything a parent wouldn't want their child to see on the screen.  No one should need to have a guild member on /ignore. 
4. No kill stealing from any player in Norrath.
5. When hunting in a party respect the loot order.  Almost every one has experienced a player that loots in the middle of combat or before the corpse even hits the ground.
6. EVERY guild member MUST read the guild message board at least once a week. A LOT of important information is said on the boards; that people miss if they do not read it.
7. We will strive to hold a guild raid for ALL levels once a week. These raids aren't about loot or exp; they are about the camaraderie between guild mates. When we get level 20's in our guild, they will probably get exp. But us 40+ wont, and that is fine. I have no problem doing a guild raid in places like Befallen, Split Paw, Najena, etc etc. Like I said before; it's about the camaraderie.
8. We will attempt to hold weekly guild meetings either at a designated area, EQ chat, or through guild chat.
9. Level 20 is the lowest level allowed in the guild for a primary character.  Twinks must have reached at least level 5 before being invited and must remain on /roleplay until such time as they have reached the 20th level.
10. All races and classes are allowed in the guild. A person's race or class should not affect their true character. As long as the person is honorable, loyal, and kind to others; they are eligible to join the guild.
11. The Squire / Questor program is still in effect. This is the best way to see if someone is right for our guild. General Trinto is the Quest General, and he will assign all the questors their quests. The quests he assigns must pass the council's approval first before they can be final.
12. Whenever anything is brought in front of the Council, there must be at least 3 Officers present. The guild leader has the ability to veto, if he does not agree with the Council's decision. But he CAN be overruled if EVERYONE in the Council disagrees with his decision. The leader is  not a dictator.
13. NO ONE in the guild may be /anon. We need to know who is on in our guild incase something comes up. If you want to be anon, you may use /roleplay.
14. Power Leveling Primaries is a no no. Power leveling twinks is allowed though. If your twink out levels your primary, then your twink should become your primary.  In general, however, power leveling is harmful to the character and should be considered heavily.

In Addition

* Repeated violations of the rules can and will result in being removed from the guild.
* No problem can be solved if you do not tell your officer about it. Even if you feel bad about reporting another player you must inform someone of poor behavior by a member. The enjoyment of the game and Guild depends on it.
* You represent the Guild and its laws.
* Loyalty and honor bring rewards.
