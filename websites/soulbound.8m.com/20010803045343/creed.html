Soulbound is a loyal and honorable guild on the Prexus Server. 

 

We fight for truth, justice, and to help those who are in need.  If someone is in trouble and they are noble of heart, we will come to their aid and help them in any way possible.  Together we can help the good people of Norrath heal the scars of our land that have been caused by centuries of strife and disease.  We are always loyal to each other and hold our heads high, for we are Soulbound.
