Name Leata Amberthorn

Leata is a young Halfling woman, the only daughter of Marshall Tarbin Amberthorn. She is a Guardian of the Vale, and is fiercely proud of her people. She is also very proud of her profession. Her quick temper and general lack of social refinement make her appear to be brash, arrogant, and un-approachable. Once someone gets to know her, however, they will find her to be a fun-loving and warm person with a strong sense of loyalty and fairness. She is well known for her incredible appetite for and tolerance for alcoholic drinks, and spends many nights out drinking with her good friend Dagtier Jaggedstone.

She is a member of this guild to the end that she seeks to broaden her knowledge of the world to better defend her home. Many of her friends are found in this guild, as well the man she loves, Litten Litefinger, a Halfling Rogue.
