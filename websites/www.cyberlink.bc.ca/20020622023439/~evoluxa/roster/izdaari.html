Name Izdaari Eristikon
Rank Erudite
Class Enchanter
Level 43
 

Izdaari was born in Neriak, where she spent her early childhood. Her father was a Teir'Dal court noble, her mother a powerful Erudite necromancer who was serving as the Ambassador from Paineel. Needless to say, the social positions of her parents required her mixed-race heritage be kept a secret, and since she took after her mother, that was possible without undue difficulty.

When her mother's term as Ambassador was over they returned to Paineel. As Izdaari grew up, she took her mother's brand of strict logic and self-reliance a step further and refused to worship any god, much less an evil one, instead sneaking off to Erudin to become a student of philosophy, and eventually one of the brightest stars of the Erudin academia. Not content with this, she resolved to travel the world and see what other races had learned, and so became an enchanter, as only this profession would allow her complete freedom to explore.

She traveled the world for a time, eventually settling in Freeport as a visiting scholar at the Academy of Arcane Sciences. There she became heavily involved in the debates about the struggle between good and evil, almost always finding herself aligned with a renegade Teir'Dal cleric who championed a third way, Lokus of Evoluxa. Intrigued, she investigated his doctrine and unique guild, and so found her true home and family.
