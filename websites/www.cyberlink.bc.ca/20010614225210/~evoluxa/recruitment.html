So you want to join Evoluxa?

This is a close-knit guild of role-players of varying degrees, from hardcore to casual. We're a family, which means all the benefits and challenges of any family are included here. We have fun, tease each other in /guildsay, roleplay our butts off then go hit a dungeon for several hours, bicker and argue about things, and in general do our best to enjoy the world known as Norrath. We're all misfits in one way or another, mostly due to our stance of neutrality that makes Necromancers and Paladins alike call each other friend. Well, at least, not try and kill each other. We're not as concerned with leveling or doing high-level raids, but there's always a dungeon somewhere some of our members are rampaging through. We don't twink our members like crazy, but if we've got an item you need then you're usually welcome to it.

If you find yourself standing apart from the crowd, and have a hunger for some good fun and good role-playing in a neutral-aligned guild, then Evoluxa may have what you seek. Contact our Dia Armpala (the Council Member responsible for recruitment...didn't you read the charter?) or look for announcements on this website as to guild meetings.

Since Evoluxa is a family, we prefer to get to know a person before adding them. The best way to do this is to seek out our members. Talk with them in character, hunt with them, and in general try to show us what kind of person you are. We have no restrictions as to level, race, class, or anything. Evoluxa has members all the way from the teens to our 51st level Warrior, and a good mix of casters, fighters, and hybrids. See you in Norrath!
