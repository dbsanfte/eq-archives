Tyshalae, like all members of House W'Rath, is something of an enigma.

No one, save her immediate family and some trusted servants, know of her existence for the first five years of her life for fear that she would be the target of an assassin. Her mother's pregnancy was kept secret for the same reason. Tysh's early childhood was an anomaly in Neriak, for she was raised with love and indulgence. Her small stature, speed and agility earned her the nickname "Mouse."

Though Tysh showed magical potential from a very early age, she did not have the attention span to learn even the simplest of spells. Instead, at about age five, she discovered what she was most talented at. The same traits that earned her the name Mouse also made her a natural born thief. Tysh could steal the rings from the hands of sleepers, hid from her parents at bath time and worked locked cabinets of sweets open. Her family recognized these natural skills and started nurturing them.

Tysh's family taught her at home, not trusting the academies of Neriak to keep her safe from harm. Morrigu the Redhand taught her the arts of rogues, Dantrazz instructed her in the arts of combat. The Necromancer Solaufein taught her academic matters as well as how to recognize magic use and how to protect herself from spells. Her training still continues.

At the age of eight she snuck to an Evoluxa meeting and gained membership in the guild. While Tysh is somewhat unsure of the exact intricacies of the guild, she knows that it fights against ignorance, which she abhors, and that Evoluxa's members treat her with respect, not as a freak.

Tyshalae is the youngest member of Evoluxa, House W'Rath and the Ebon Mask. She is both a cheerful little child, forever discovering new things and asking questions, and a young noble of Neriak, born into the deadly intrigue of Neriak and fully capable of surviving there.
