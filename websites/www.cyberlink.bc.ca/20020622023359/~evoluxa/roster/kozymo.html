Name Kozymo Dogman
Rank il-Khan Bakkus
Race Erudite
Class Magician
Level 38
Growing up in Erudin, under the tutelage of his mother, a member of the High Council of Erudin, he was pampered and protected from the outside world.  At 9 years of age, he was finally allowed, with a guardian of course, to start on his first tier of training, not in fighting in Tox forest, but only in taking the mail from Erudin to Qeynos, for the Bards.  When he reached his fourth season, he had enough money to by his first stave, and some spells.  He was finally allowed to go train his magiks in Tox, only because he could summon a guardian, but the Sentinels, were always close.

Gandolph, as he was known in the Palace, trained in Tox forest till his 8th season, when his mother gave him a special ring of invisibility.  At this point, he started to travel the world of Norrath, and learned to master many skills.  On reaching his 20th tier of learning, he chose the surname of Thegrey, in remembrance of a mighty Magi of lore.  Soon after, he received news of his mother's death, and returned to Erudin.  After the funeral, he found his mother's diary.  In it, he found out his mother was abducted by the evil ones of The Hole, and that he was a product of magiks, and was intended to fight for evil, not against, but he and his mother were rescued.  Reading through the diary, he found he had no real father, but was a product of  magiks, mixing Erudite and Kobold.  Soon after, the gods changed his name to Kozymo Dogman, in mockery of his Kobold blood.

Just recently, he had learned the language of the Dark Elves, which sounds a little like the old tongue of the many Kobolds he had killed, which his mother called Elder Teir'Dal, making him wonder if he might be part elf also..........
