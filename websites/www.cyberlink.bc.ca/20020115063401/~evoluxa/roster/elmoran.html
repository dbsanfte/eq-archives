Class Ranger
Level 34

You notice the difference the moment you look closely. The darkness of his skin, the white streaks in his hair and beard, and the sharply chiselled and angled facial features tell all who see him "I am not your average half-elf". Born the son of a Tier’Dal shadow knight and an Erudite wizard-turned-druid, his ancestry, and the prejudice which has accompanied it all his life, has given him an insight to bigotry and hate that few will ever know.

When his parents were killed by the Darkpaw Gnolls of Blackburrow while defending Surefall Glade, he was taken in by the rangers of the glade more as an unwanted obligation than a potential candidate for the group. For this reason he quietly and secretly left Surefall Glade before his training was complete and has wandered the face of Norrath in search of a home he has not known since his eighth winter. On that day, so vivid in his memory, he stood at the funeral pyre of his parents and vowed to bathe in the blood of Trannix Darkpaw and all his kin. The mouldering corpses that litter the Qeynos Hills bear grim witness that he has not forgotten or forgiven.
