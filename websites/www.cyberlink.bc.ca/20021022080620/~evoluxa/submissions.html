Submission Guidelines

Hiya everyone.... I'm Litten Litefinger, Rogue Extraordinaire and  keeper of the Evoluxa Website. I'd just love to hear from you. Please send me your screenshots, stories, events, links suggestions and personal bio's. But when you do, could you please oh please try to follow the guidelines I've set out below? It will make it oh so much easier for me to get your information onto the website quickly and efficiently.

Thanks in advance

Events
Please be sure to provide all of the following information when submitting Events for the Evoluxa Events Calendar.
  • Date of the Event
  • Time of the Event
  • Description of the Event including URL's to more information available on the Internet
  • Your character just incase people need to get a hold of you in-game.

Guild Roster & Biographies
Guild Members are encouraged to submit the character information to be entered in the Guild Roster.  Please note that only primary characters will be listed in the Roster with alternate characters being listed on the Biography page of your primary character. If you have a bio for your alternates please feel free to submit them as well.

Please provide, at a minimum, the following information with your submission.

  • Character Name
  • Character Class
  • Character Race
  • Level
  • Guild Rank

Please feel free to email me at any time with up to date information.

Screenshots of your character should be taken against a contrasting background. So if your character wears black armor don't have a picture taken against the night sky. Likewise if you wear golden armor don't have your picture taken against the background of a sandstone wall or such.

Do NOT crop your image before sending it to me. I would like to be consistent with the size of images being place in the biographies this is difficult if people are cropping their screen shots at different resolutions. You can convert the image to a 100% Quality JPEG if you wish otherwise the BMP file is just fine.

Screenshots
Guild Members, Allies and friends of the Clan Evoluxa are encouraged to submit screenshots of Evoluxa members in action. Please take the time to critique your screenshots and only send those worth putting on the site.

Please provide, at a minimum, the following information with your submission.

  • Character Name of the person who took the screenshot
  • Location where the picture was taken
  • Character Names for those individuals in the shot (sometimes it can be hard to read all the names)
  • Description of the picture.

Do NOT crop your image before sending it to me. I would like to be consistent with the size of images being place in the biographies this is difficult if people are cropping their screen shots at different resolutions. You can convert the image to a 100% Quality JPEG if you wish otherwise the BMP file is just fine.
