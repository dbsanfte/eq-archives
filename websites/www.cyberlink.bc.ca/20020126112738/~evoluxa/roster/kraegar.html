Kraegar is a rather secretive member of Evoluxa.  While he's always about talking, fighting, and having fun with his guildmates, he rarely discusses his past.  He was born and raised in Qeynos, and has talked of having a younger brother.  Exactly why he chose to become a rogue and begin his travels is known to few, if any.

Currently, Kraegar's primary ambition is fun - fun in the form of exploring, and being with friends.  He's always looking for new places to explore, and considers the more dangerous realms in Norrath some of the best places to adventure.

Kraegar has also made a name for himself by hitting on near every woman he sees - though he's never actually pursued a relationship with any of them...
