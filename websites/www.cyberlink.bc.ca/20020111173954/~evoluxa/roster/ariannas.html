Ariannas Heartbinder was born into a warm and loving, if not highly protective family.  She was taught at a very early age the power of kindness as well as the power of magic.  Her mother schooled her in the art of magic.  To say that she was close to her mother would be an understatement.  One of her life goals, and still is to this day, is to live up to what her mother was, a woman of outstanding character, a woman who denied no one of her kindness and generosity no matter what the stakes, and a woman who believed that ignorance was an evil seed that needed to be destroyed no matter what the cost.
                                                                                         Shortly after her mother's death, Ariannas decided to leave the protection of her home and, against the wishes of her father, sought out to discover the world of Norrath and the many adventures it held for her.  Having been sheltered for a big part of her young life, she soon learned that not all beings or lands for that matter, were going to be kind or understanding towards her.  For awhile, she lingered on the idea of returning to the safety of her home and family, but knew that she would never obtain her life goals this way.  So, acquiring all the courage and knowledge her mother had given her, she fought back against the unkind beings and found out happily that she could make it on her own.
 
One of her proudest moments came when she was given the chance to join Evoluxa and it's vast number of enlightened members, some of whom she is happy to call close and endearing friends.  She will always stand proud with any of her guild mates and will always lend a hand or heart to any member in need.
