Name Lokus Z'Asrithe

Born the youngest of twins to an influential family in Neriak, Lokus began his interest of politics and diplomacy at a young age. Traveling with his mother on errands of the Queen, he learned much of Norrath before her death and his acceptance into the Clergy the following year. After serving on the courts of Cristianos Thex as a High Priest of Innoruuk, Lokus resigned honorably upon her abandonment, using the confusion it sowed to slip out and begin his own plans. Within months, the doctrine of Evoluxa, based on Lokus' own 'self-divination' ideals, was a breathing and growing institution.
