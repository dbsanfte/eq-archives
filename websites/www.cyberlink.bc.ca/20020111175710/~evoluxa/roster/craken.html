 		 Craken Danel grew up a mercenary of the Freeport Militia.  Trained in the art of slaying others through many years of Arena battle.  He used his fake past of a vengeful farm boy to gain trust in others, and to once again attain the sword of his desires.  His acceptance into the Blade of Enric taunt him the honour that he now fights for in each battle.  His lost mentor Gruggoth was a brave and honourable warrior.  Craken then found his new family, that of Evoluxa.  He was now an ex-mercenary, one whom desired honour more than blood.  He strives to keep others alive in battle and uses his will to do so.

Though his past was well know, the rumors of dark riders slaying his parents were all false.  Instead, Craken was so disgusted that his father was in fact the leader of the Freeport Militia, that he had to slay his own father to gain the trust in himself.  So it was done, Sir Lucan Danel was slain upon the Freeport grounds, and Craken, could never go home again.  He now searches out his brothers, fighting all the evils bestowed upon the land, in hopes that one day, he could be freed of his father's blood.
