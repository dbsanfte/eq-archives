Name Litten Litefinger
Rank
Race Halfling
Class Rogue "Extrordonaire"
Level 39
Growing up an orphan in Rivervale Litten survived by "borrowing" what he needed to get by. At an early age his new found skills brought him to the attention of the local thieves guild where he quickly climbed the ranks.

A short stint as a member of the legendary Leatherfoot Raiders, Rivervale's Elite protectors, honed his combat skills.

After setting off in the world to make a name for himself and his ever elusive "fortune", Litten met and fell in love with Leata Amberthorn a legendary warrior halfling from the Clan Evoluxa.

And the rest as they say in the story books...... is history.
