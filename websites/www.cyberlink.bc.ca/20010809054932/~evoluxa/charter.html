Evoluxa Charter

A new age has dawned upon this land. The young races have proclaimed glory, establishing empires to challenge the might of Dragon-kind built over thousands of years. The elder races, in turn, have retrieved their thrones from the ashes, with their kingdoms spreading across the face of the continents. But, the Gods have shrouded themselves from the face of Norrath, and now bicker with each other by pulling the strings of their mortal creations like marionettes. The Gods, retreating into secrecy, now use their creations to fight their battles vicariously with faith as their tool.

The formation of this guild founds a brotherhood of all races that believes that in order for the Gods, and the followers of those Gods, to achieve their goals, a unity of sorts must be maintained. This is our defining characteristic, our crowning achievement of intellectual progress. How better to aid the Gods in their motives, and how better for the Gods' mortal creations to teach them that endless conflict of equally powerful beings will never find a victor? The Gods, as we know, advocate the extermination of opposing races not out of malice, but as a way of removing an obstacle from the path of their will. We wish to remove these obstacles for each other, hopefully allowing an open circulation of progress and alleviating the mass, unnecessary loss of life that is the result.

There is one enemy: ignorance. Unity can only be attained by unanimous belief. The ignorant stand in the path of the enlightened. Enemies of the enlightened, those who oppose progress and intellectual evolution...must be eradicated. Mercy will be freely given to the indifferent and doubtful, however, those that may take arms against our teachings and outlooks will not be pardoned. In our aims of the formation of this brotherhood, we look to establish pseudo-utopian culture within ourselves. All of our personal goals and aspirations are those of the guild's as well. Members of the guild will have access to their allotment of the guild's monetary and collateral resources, in exchange for donations back to the guild as a whole. In this way, each member will benefit monetarily from all others. In addition, each member of the guild will be given responsibility for an amount of guild money to purchase needed items for the guild as they are found.

There will be ranking systems and responsibilities attributed to those ranks. Your rank will define what it is that the guild requires of you, and your role in the overall bureaucracy. The Council of Five, the Dia Pentala, will govern over the entire guild, with each of the Council members governing its respective branches. The branches shall be the Dia Chixom, which will perform the task of acquisition of items for the guild, the Dia Armpala, which will perform the task of recruitment, the Dia Sepia, which shall have the task of communications between members and Council, the Dia Bakkus, which would act as the trade-master, and the Dia Luxa, in which all members of the guild will belong in addition to their other responsibilities. This branch is our Diplomacy and War branch, which will be presided over by the guild-master himself. Each council member will have an officer-ranking assistant, in order to help with their normal duties, and also to take over in case of absence or retirement. It is this ten that will form the core governing body.

Initiate This is a person seeking entrance into Evoluxa or having been guilded for under one week. This ranks constitutes a time when the other members can get to know the newcomer, and vice versa. In this way, the new member can decide if the guild is what they seek, and the guild can become acquainted with and ascertain the character of the Initiate.
Privatus This rank requires the member to have been with the guild longer than one week, and have demonstrated good character and compatible ideals with the guild as a whole.
Legatus This rank requires that the member have been guilded a minimum of two weeks, and proved themselves in training and character. To earn this promotion, the member must complete a quest issued from the Dia Pentala, and overseen by his or her Imperator.
Magister This is an honorary rank for a member who had performed above and beyond the call of duty for the guild. A Magister is required to have served a minimum of 1 month with the guild and must be recommended by at least 3 of their fellow guild-members. There would be no real duties by this person beyond a mentor and minor performance of functions needed.
Imperator This is a rank bestowed upon one person of each class represented in Evoluxa. The Imperator for each class is responsible for helping members of that class train, stay upgraded on equipment, spells, and supplies. The Imperator acts as a representative for the members of that class to the Council at meetings, usually as a silent party unless there is particular business to report. The Imperator is also responsible for evaluating new members of that class during their first week, dictating whether the Initiate is promoted to Privatus. This rank is obtained from a vote of members from each class.
il-Khan This is the officer rank that designates the assistant to a Khan in the Dia Pentala. They provide support for their Khan, and represent them at meetings in their absence.
Khan The five Khans make up the Dia Pentala and form the core governing body. They are responsible for their particular section and the duties subscribed to that section. Khans are responsible for the policy of Evoluxa, and the enforcement and execution of this policy.

The members of Evoluxa would see her aims met, but never would we wish this at the expense of the lives of the young. The young are not to be held responsible for the ignorance of their race or cities, either directly or indirectly. In this thought, it is forbidden for any member of Evoluxa to maliciously hunt the guards of areas and townships who serve to protect the youth. This is only for those whom serve protection; those of militias inside the cities are not deemed worthy of mercy by purpose. Also, this does not include self defense. Any member of Evoluxa who is attacked has the Council's backing to react accordingly.

New members are always welcome in the guild, yet we do wish to remain rather small in our younger years. We also wish to make sure that all new members are of like thought and motives. Prospective recruits must be brought before the Dia Pentala during one of the scheduled meetings. Upon arrival, they will be interviewed (more of meeting the rest of the guild) and may be subject to performing a quest. Despite the conditions, the Council will vote on whether the prospect is admitted, with only a unanimous vote granting membership. This is not to take power or duty away from the Dia Armpala, for it is their duty to find these recruits and bring them before the Council.

This charter will be updated as the members see fit. It is also to be said that the structure will of course smooth out, and may not be word for word of this doctrine. This is fine, as long as the core remains. As long as we all benefit, and destroy our ignorance, then all of the goals will be obtained.
