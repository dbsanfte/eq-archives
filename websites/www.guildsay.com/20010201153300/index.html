Welcome to Guildsay.com
Hello! Guildsay.com is the way to manage character, guild, and server interaction for EverQuest. We offer a lot to keep you busy, providing guild news, message boards, and event calendars, amongst other things. Feel free to browse around! I won't stop you!

An update?! Like hell you say!
Yes, kids... I updated the site. It's kind of a partial update, but it has a few new things you may enjoy.

Guild Information: You can now enter guild information such as your guild's official web page (yah... like you need anything other than Guildsay.com. FEH!), a guild charter, guild creation date, a guild history, and a guild picture.

Character Auto-Validation: The last option available in the guild information editor is something dealing with how characters are validated in guilds. If this box is checked, every character you attempt to create in your guild will need to be validated by a guild officer or leader. If this box is unchecked, the character will automatically become validated, saving some hassle and time.

Proper Image Scaling: Before the update, all the images on the site were scaled to a finite size no matter what size you uploaded them as. This is pretty much a pain, especially if you don't have nice image software to manipulate the size. Now, the page will try to set the image to the proper size, but if it can't, it will scale it down accordingly and won't stretch or skew it in any way. You can also click on images now if they have been scaled down.

News Archive: There is a news archive for the main site. I figured this would be a good idea, so I quickly added it in. I'll have guild news archives and editing/deleting capabilities up Real Soon Now(tm).

By: Heffling at 6:17 PM PST - December 5th, 2000

Problems?
It occurred to me that the site, in its current state, may not be too intuitive. I'll try to guide you through a few of the steps to get you on your way to making full use of the site.

First, you're going to need a user account. This is just so we know you're logged in and can bring up your character/guild information. Go here to create a user.

After creating your user, you hit a fork in the road. If you have a guild, you can create it here. Just be aware that guilds need to be validated by our admins, so your guild may already be in the queue, created by a fellow guildmate of yours, waiting to be verified. This process should take no longer than a day.

If your guild is already created, then you can create your character(s) under your guild page. The option should be in the upper right-hand corner of the screen. Any guild officer or guild leader can validate your character once it's created.

On the other hand, if you don't have a guild and you want to create your character, just navigate to your server and click on Create Character from there. Make sure to leave the guild as "[ None ]" and don't enter a guild password and you're all set! Your character will be validated by the site admins and should be ready within the day.

If you have any other questions, please feel free to e-mail me and I'd be glad to answer them.
