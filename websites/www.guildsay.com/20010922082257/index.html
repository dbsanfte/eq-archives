By: Heffling at 9:49 AM PDT - July 26th, 2001

Do a little dance...
Make a little love, and thank god I finally updated the site.

I know you hate me, everyone does. I do, too. Truth is, I was motivated to post some updates because I'm leaving in the morning for New York and couldn't stand to have this site as out-of-date as it was for the two weeks I'm going to be gone. So here is the list of additions:

Guild News Editing/Deleting: Yes, you can do this now. Why did I wait so long to do it? I don't know... it was never about difficulty, just time.

Primary/Alternate Characters: You can now assign ONE character as your primary character. All your other characters will automatically then be set to alternates, and all your characters are set to alternates right now, so I implore you to go update them to reflect your primary character's status.

Character Privacy: I think the Edit Character page says it best, so I'll just rip the quote out of there directly... If you're ashamed to say you broke down and created the 15th High Elven cleric of the guild, check this box and only you and your guild officers and leaders will know your dirty little secret. Please note: this function is disabled when editing your primary character. Only alternates may be private.

Message Post Editing: You can now edit your message board posts, provided you have adequate access in your guild, or you posted the message. I've also added the ability to delete posts, but it's about 95% stable (I know what's missing, but I don't have the time right now as I need to get to sleep for the trip) so I figured I'd just disable it until I get back and finish up that last bit of code.

Polls: You probably notice the poll link on the left. This, as well as the message post deleting functionality, is disabled for now. It will be enabled when I get back (oh, and this one is about 90% complete).

As for some small things, I've updated text to read better, changed things which don't make sense anymore (COPPA is no longer a "recent" government regulation). I've also upped the amount of guild leaders your guild may have from three to four (if you have more than four, feel free to tell me about it, but jeez... think about making one or two of them officers! =).

I hope you like the new changes and I'll be back around the middle of this month. Take care!
