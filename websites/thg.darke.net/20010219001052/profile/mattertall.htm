Biography:

There are three paths to follow in life. The one they call Good, is considered the high moral path. Filled with the laughable idea of rightiousness, those who travel this path hide their weakness behind the self-imposed shame of their laws. Laws designed to protect the weak from the strong, and allow a man to be judged by the idea of virtue, rather his ability and strength. These people I dismiss as weak, protecting those unfit to survive behind the veil of being 'civilized'.

The second path is followed by those withut the spine to follow their inner convictions, regardless of cost or personal effort. They follow whims, inconsistent from sunrise to sunset. Hiding behind their so called Neutrality, they feel free to make no stands, to leave their lives devoid of meaning. These people I dismiss as untrustworthy, their lack of commitment to a moral principle an appalling indication of their lack of loyalty.

The third path, called by the ignorant Evil, is in truth the path of strength. Without self-imposed laws to hide behind, those that follow this path follow the laws of nature. The weak fall prey to the strong, and the strongest are unfettered but by the will of the masses to achieve the heights of greatness. Survival is based solely on strength and ability. This is the path I choose. I am Mattertall, true first to Myself, my Guild second, and the few I find worthy to call Friends third.

See me and know fear, for I am unfettered by anything save my own sense of honor. "So what are you?" I ask *evil grin*
