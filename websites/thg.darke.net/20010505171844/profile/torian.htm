Biography:

I was born in the Slums of Qeynos, my mother being a human, and my father a no-good Wood Elf who had a tryst with my mother and disappeared (presumably back to Kelethin). Being a Half Elf, I really wasnt accepted by the populace of Qeynos, so I took to spending time in the hills outside the city. I found peace and happiness in those hills, watching the animals and romping in the woods.

One day I saw a stranger who was chasing a man, who in turn was being chased by a bear. They all run out of my sight, so Isat by a tree to ponder this strange occurance. After a short time, the stranger (the one chasing the man, not the man being chased by the bear) came wandering back by where I was doing my pondering. He approached me and asked me what I was doing in his hills. I responded that I enjoyed the peace and quiet of the hills, and like observing the wildlife.

So on that fateful day, I was recruited by Cros Treewind to become a ranger, and begin my training in Surefall Glade. I spent many years in that glade training with the use of weapons and the bow, and learning to track the beings of Norrath. When i finally reached my coming of age, I was sent out into the world to ply my skills in the defense of all cute fluffy animals everywhere.

While wandering, I have met many people, both good and bad, and finally came apon a group of people with the same ideals as my own, and am now a member of thier fold.
