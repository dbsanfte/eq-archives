Biography:

I am Perdition. Here is my life.

My life has not been one I'm proud of; however i'm trying to change that. You see, my mother died while birthing me. My father blamed me for it and beat me because of it. I vowed to get back at him any way I could so I defied him and became a member of the spurned instead of the dead. After I had trained in my art and grew to sufficient power, I killed him. I was exiled from Neriak soon after and my hate for Tier'dal grew stronger everyday but sadly so did my disgust for myself.

I spent many years in the area surrounding my old home all the while murdering all who dared to venture out. That all changed one day. I was out in the commonlands where I saw a group of people all laughing and drinking together. I tried to ambush them but I failed - they had heard my approach and turned to meet me. As I started to summon my fiend from its plane someone came up behind me and knocked me out. I woke up to find myself in a bed being tended to. I soon found myself friends in those i was going to kill. I realized the err of my ways and vowed to help, and not to destroy those who walked our land. Tragedy struck years later when i came back to town after a journey to Befallen. I found my comrades dead in our hall; the Tier'dal had tracked me down and as a message killed those I cared about. So to this day I wander the lands helping those who need.
