Biography:

Kroxiss was born in the communal egg beds of Cabilis like all Iksar. He was raised not knowing who his parents were and decided Krogoxx was his brother, because the two grew up together.

He joined the Swiftails when he was young and believes that through pain and suffering comes strength, which includes the pain and suffering of others. He wears his shackles with pride knowing he is above the petty political squabbles of the other castes

Kroxiss has an extreme hatred for all who have ravaged his homeland, giants, goblins, and the traitorous Sarnaks and Frogloks. The dragons are the only ones he has respect for, because of there immense power and awe they inspire, but that will not stop him form killing them when he gets the chance.
