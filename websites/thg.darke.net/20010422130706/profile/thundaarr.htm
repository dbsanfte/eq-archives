Biography:

Living on the streets was tough for young Thundaarr, made even worse by having to take care of his little brother, Kieran. Dar and Kie, spent years on the streets of Freeport until some bandits came and kidnapped Kie when he was six years old and brought him to Qeynos. Dar searched for his brother and trained as a Paladin so that he might finally find his brother. Getting his fine steel blade he calls Ragnorok he searches for the mystical weapons and armor of the Paladin so that he might be able to find his brother.

Of note, he has a tattoo of an angel spreading the length of his back, signifiying the guardian angel he believes protects him and has brother.
