Biography:

Zetranifon being a young lad, was a street rat in the trees, and always sensing someow when the guards were near, made an excellent bread stealer. One day, he realized that his plight was a long one, and after much conversation with a Kellithen drunkard, he decided to make an honest wage. He found a note for a certain Maisaen Trueshot, and took it to him. Armed with a sword and dirty green tunic, Zet was then off to kill bats.

Much time later, fighting in Crushbone, Zet was scared for his life by a certain legionnaire. Vowing revenge, Zet went off to Qeynos to visit the mystical surefall glade. Being a ranger, Zet will always be in the woods, shooting his bow, looking for a friend. He believes in the honor of his guild, and the strength and trueness of his bow, and one day, will be a hunter infamous throughout Fay.
