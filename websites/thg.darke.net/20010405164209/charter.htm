Guild Charter  
 


Honor, Friendships, Loyalty, these are the keys too the heart & soul of this guild.  Honor to one's self, Honor to the guild and Honor to the opposition.  In all things we must conduct ourselves with Honor.

Friendship is a necessity to maintain our strength within this guild.  Without friends the higher goals and accomplishments of Norrath are unattainable.

Loyalty is a cornerstone which supports Honor and Friendship.  To be an honorable friend you must be loyal.

We reflect these values in our guild motto "Need Before Greed".  For a Loyal & Honorable friend gives all they can before taking for themselves.

     Helam Honormaster

 

In joining our guild I would like you to ask yourself what do you want out of Everquest?  A high level character?  To acquire things?  Explore?  Conquer? 

More than anything else I want this to be a guild of people and friendships.  The friendships that I have established in EQ are every bit as real as my real life friends.  I want, in this guild, to be a place where people can find friendships and people to adventure with.  Without these friends this is just be a computer game.

If you want to join a guild for greedy reasons, if you can't act in a (somewhat) mature manor or make a habit out of using people then this won't be the guild for you. 

That's about all I have to say.

     Oognog Tew  -   Raziah
