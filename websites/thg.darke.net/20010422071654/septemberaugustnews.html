Third on my list! You people better know your officers, cuz the THG just got a new weapon! Don't upset your officers!

Finally, this will be wordy, but it must be done. Many of our members are getting to be dragon/plane age, and it's time to know the unwritten rules. These are things that are done by all participants of dragon raids and are generally accepted. With this being said, also be aware that the leader of the raid can change the rules. It's their raid. If they want all the monks to loot the Vox White Dragon scale, well... he can. Anyway, here's the general rules:

  1. Listen to the raid leader. These raids usually take 5-8 full groups of six, and there can be a lot of spam. The raid leader, as long with every group leader, will talk in shout channel, whereas everyone else uses OOC. So when your group leader says (classic example) "Stay on the wall". Don't move. Don't wander, don't twirl around. Stay there and med, stay there and sit. But under no circumstances are you to move. There are always reasons why the leaders ask you to do something, so abide by them, or you're gonna have 35 high level characters charging you for the price of the coffins =P
  2. Only group leaders loot. If your group is lucky enough to get experience and lootability on a kill, only the person who has been designated leader will loot. Ninjalooting will get you kicked out of a raid and future raids so fast it will make your head spin. Here's a famous story. Naggy raid, all the bad guys go down. JOE, a wizard, loots a NO DROP caster item that about 17 people could use. Claims he had no idea it was no drop. No excuse, cuz he was instructed only leaders were to loot. Joe is now blacklisted from every future raid, and in fact, is rarely ever seen on the server again. (of course, i changed Joe's name, but it's easily found if you ask any member of Inner Fire.)
  3. Loot rules will be determined before the raids. For example, usually monks and enchanters only roll for the King Tranix crown, and this is usually announced at the beginning of the Naggy raid. So, when Tranix drops and the crown is there, the raid leader will announce "Xian 1 Laean 2" and then /random 1 2, the die turns up on the number 1, Xian wins. (Trust me, I always win =D ).
  4. You only roll for what you NEED. There is absolutely, positively, no rolling for greed. If you have cobolt greaves (you warriors should know that cobolt is better than the planar indicolite), and the indicolite drops, even if you are a warrior and can use it, you don't get in on the roll. Only warriors who will use these items get to roll. Most of the items dropped by the gods/dragons are all/all, so it's important to remember the annoucned looting rules (see point 3), and remember that for some classes, the item represents the best item for that slot, while for some classes, it's only number 4 or 5 on the list. Therefore, it's not who can use, it's who needs.
        • If you are seen trading or selling a dragon/god item, you will upset EVERY person who was on that raid with you, and you will be blacklisted. I promise. You cannot explain why it's OK to 17 very upset casters why you are trading away your White Dragonscale Cloak. You cannot justify it to the raid leaders who vow to never let you raid anything again.
  5. Do not go to a raid expecting to gain experience or an item. You are there for fun and the chance at a good drop, you are not there to level. If you are always saying 'oh, we're never getting the experience', then you will have the reputation for whining, and you don't want that reputation. Most likely, YOU WILL LOSE EXPERIENCE. Always go there knowing this is a possibility. Go there expecting it, that way any experience you get is seen as a bonus.
  6. Pet classes, do as your raid leader says. On Naggy runs, it's generally accepted that there are no pets until the final battle with the red dragon himself. If you pop a pet up under a no pet rule, you'll be booted. Guaranteed. Everyone realizes that you're not as powerful without your pet, but everyone also realizes that if you lose control of your pet on a mezmerized Fire Giant, or he goes for a little stroll up to Nagafen's lair, you will kill the entire raid group.
  7. Tanks, in the planes, DIE. If you are attacked/summoned/feared and are some place with 3-4 planar bad guys on you, don't ask for a heal, don't run back to the group. Just get a /loc and die. If you can feign, feign and tell the group you were agroed. They'll tell you what to do. Trust me, a corpse retrieval for one lost person is much better than corpse retrieval for 35 people who were wiped out by your train.
  8. Find out who is main assist and always use your assist hotkey. Never use your mouse to target, never use your F8 key to target, never hit the Autoattack key. Only press your assist hotkey, and you will only attack whatever the main assist is attacking. You will not unmezmerize a monster, you will not kill the entire raid group.
  9. Be prepared to make a time commitment. Naggy takes as long as 5 hours, if there's a big med up time, or if there's a big corpse retrieval. Vox is shorter, but can be long. The planes can take damned near a whole day. In emergency situations, you can get a gate out or gate yourself, but get some caffeine, get some food, catheterize your bladder, and be prepared for the long haul. (I'm a med student, you shoud see the I.V. setup I have =D )
  10. These raids are a lot of fun, and are very rewarding, even if you don't get a drop. Wizards, go to Hate. Clerics, go to Fear. Enchanters, go everywhere. Even if your class does not have armor in that plane. Why? Because you will get your name known, you will be asked on future raids in your desired plane, you will meet 30+ members of high level characters. It's fun.

I know, it sounds like a lot of no's, but those rules are there for a reason, they keep everyone alive, and keep everyone happy. A well run raid (see Inner Fire's recent raids, Sagara Alliance, Soul Assassins, our Future Raids) is a thing of beauty. It's an amazing collaboration of teamwork and skill, and you will feel very satisfied doing it. A messy, unorganized, unplanned raid (see ummmm... well ask Eyez, Laean, Bolavin, or myself for good examples of bad raiders) will frustrate everyone, and bring out the worst in everyone.

Other than that, expect more frequent updates now that I CAN update the site!

Be safe and well my guildmates, you make your monkie proud in so many ways.

Your friendly neighborhood monkie,
Xian

But your high levels are trying to level too and buy their own equipment, so please be considerate in this matter.
