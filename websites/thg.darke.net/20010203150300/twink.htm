Twink and Power Level Policy  
 


This is a very difficult subject.  Every person in the game probably has a different idea of how much twinking and power leveling is acceptable.  Our policy is going to be that we, as a guild, do not twink or power level our members.  Asking to be twinked or power leveled on the guild chat will NOT be allowed.  There are three reasons we won't twink our members.

  1. We don't want newbies joining our guild to get items or to be leveled quickly.   Pestering our high levels will not be tolerated.  This does not mean you can't ask for help.
  2. If you power level you miss a lot of the game which is the reason why we play. 
  3. If we power level  in-experienced players to high level we will have high level guild members representing our guild that don't know how to play their characters.  This is the last thing we want.

We are against excessive twinkage, this does not mean that you can't give someone a item or help them out in some way.  Level 4 warriors in full crafted with a exe axe is a example of excessive.

If EXPERIENCED players want to twink a second character this will be allowed.  Rolling against guild members for items for your twink will be frowned upon.  Rember our guild moto, Need Before Greed. 

If you have a EXPERIENCED friend that wants to switch servers to play with you than twinking will be allowed.  I don't see anything wrong with leveling someone that leaves a high level character to be with us.
