Trakanon Schmakanon
 - a poem by Xianmonkie

The goal of every man:
To slay the beast Trakanon.
I was asked to lend a hand,
So I left my derv2 spawn.

I ran fast in my dress,
To meet the noble forty.
My hair was dishevelled,
But, hey, it looks sporty.

My staff swung away
Ton Po's fury invoked.
I wanted them to play
But the Froggies only froaked.

We went to the bridge
But we medded too late.
Trakkie came over the ridge,
LOADING ... PLEASE WAIT.

Rezzed and armor-wearing,
We were told to get ready
I was psyched, swearing
"Trak's gonna be my teddy."

The charge was then called
The first wave attacked
I checked out my robe,
Damn girl, you're stacked.

With my pride running high
I went in to attack.
I saw many members die
to that big badass Trak.

It went as I knew,
The fight was outstanding.
The mighty beast flew,
We helped with the landing..

Inner Fire in the lead,
With a raid party so great
Forced Trakanon to read:
LOADING ... PLEASE WAIT.

I want it to be shown
Over Lanys and afar
Why Inner Fire is known
To be the sweeties they are.

They really didn't need me
To give Trakkie a whap.
But they took me to see
If Sebilis had a GAP

-xianmonkie
