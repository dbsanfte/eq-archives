Biography:

From as far back as I remember, I had always wanted to be a wizard. When I started my wizard career, I remember fighting alongside my best friend in the forest of Nektulos, Brooder. We had a lot of fun taking on decaying skeletons, fire beetles, spiderlings, and the occasional black wolf. I fell in love with my spells as i noticed the considerable damage they did compared to Brooder's Necromancer spells. At first, I was jealous of Brooder because I did not have a pet, but I soon learned that I did not need one to do my job.

I do not remember ever having a family. All I remember is running around in the Neriak Commons training with my master, Gath N'Mare. Later on while traveling, I began to realize that many people did not like me, which I learned was because I was an evil race. After that I took pride in being evil and soon found a guild that thought the same, Da Grobb Sqwadd. I enjoyed the company of big Trolls and Ogres who talked differently.

When I found out that my guild would be merging with a good race guild, I did not like the idea. But soon I realized it would only result in good things. Right away I made new friends and saw how high levels of other classes and races helped other guildies. Since then I have been happy being a member of the T'Vyl Honor Guard.
