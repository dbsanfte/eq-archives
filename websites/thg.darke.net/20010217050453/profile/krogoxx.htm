Biography:

On the day Krogoxx was born the sun was eclipsed and the city fell into complete darkness for a day. He spent most of his youth not realizing what this omen meant. He was trained how to use a sword and then one day was shown that he could wield dark magics. He was to become a shadow knight, an honored position in the Iksar city.

Krogoxx spent many of his days training with his brother Kroxiss in the swamp of no hope, until one day he saw an odd creature walking through the swamp. This creature did not have scales and had soft pink skin and no tail, he had met his first mammal. Krogoxx being a bold Iksar walked up to the mammal and told him to get off of his ancient homeland. The mammal, which he later found out was called an elf, was much more powerful than he was and challenged him to a duel. Krogoxx knowing that the elf would surely kill him if given the chance refused the duel because he did not want to throw away his life foolishly. The elf went and found a traitorous Iksar to challenge Krogoxx to a duel, one who was more around Krogoxx's rank. Krogoxx accepted the duel and was killing the traitor when the elf started using his magic on his pet, as he called it, and Krogoxx was quickly defeated.

After his defeat, he went to the temple of Cazic-Thule, and prayed to his god to show him away to defeat this elf. He was rewarded after many days of fasting and praying with a vision of a great sword called Green-mist. If he found this sword and grew proficient with it, one day he would be able to defeat the elf who had cheated him of his victory against the traitor. He later learned the elves name was Greywillow and has vowed one day to defeat him and show him the power of the Iksar.
