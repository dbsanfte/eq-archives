Gharritt Venomblade has returned from Mistmoore with an urgent request from our liege, Lanys T'Vyl, the daughter of the Evil god Innoruuk.

     
 

T'Vyl Honor Guard,

These are dire times for myself, hence no other reason I would approach thee. I once released you from the magical shackles my father placed on your souls to protect me from harm, and now I plead with your reason and compassion. It is not clear with what intentions I was taken, but I am smuggling this letter out through a trusted rogue who managed to find me in the castle and I pray escapes with this letter or all may be lost. Be wary of all, I fear a traitor is near or I would not have been found in such seclusion in lesser Faydark.

Strength In Honor,
Lanys T`Vyl

  
     

As such, we will be organizing a rescue effort in Mistmoore. All Guildmembers who are available are encouraged to attend to help free our benefactor, Lanys T`Vyl. This rescue effort will commence in approximately one weeks time. Please prepare to fight valiantly, and even die for your liege.
