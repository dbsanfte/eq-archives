Biography:

Miggen was born a young halfling some 19 and a half years ago. Unfortunately, he doesn't know quite who his parents are. A basket was found on the doorstep of the Cleric guild in Rivervale. Inside was a baby, wrapped in cloth. There was also a small necklace with the name "Miggen" inscribed upon it, around the baby's neck. The Clerics assumed that this was his name, and thus Miggen was born! Well, not literally, it's a figurative thing.......never mind.

Anyway, the clerics tried their best to keep young Miggen on the right path in life, but soon he fell in with the "wrong crowd". He spent most of his time in the Fool's Gold, drinking and joking with the more shady inhabitants of Rivervale. Soon, with a little instruction from Lendel Deeppockets, leader of the Rogue guild in Rivervale, Miggen found that he could easily steal from drunken visitors in the Fools Gold. This was the beginning of Miggen's life as a Rogue.

After a few years, Miggen got into a little hot water with the deputies in Rivervale, so he had to hide out in Highpass for a little while. However, Miggen didn't exactly know about the high concentration of Orcs in Highpass. Screaming for help, Miggen was about to smashed to a goopy, puddle-like oblivion, when he was saved by a hulking Ogre named Ogagog. Soon, they became the best of friends (touching, isn't it?)

Since Miggen needed a place to hide out, Ogagog managed to get Miggen into Da Grobb Sqwadd, the infamous gang of Ogres, Trolls, and Dark Elves. There he met Gharritt, a Dark Elf who was also into the trade of stealing and backstabbing. Gharritt became like a father to Miggen, training him and helping him survive the dangerous world of Norrath. Since then, Miggen has always looked up to Gharritt (literally and figuratively) Miggen is now an active part of the new T`Vyl Honor Guard. Though he crawls a little slower through the levels, he's still an active participant in the ever-growing world of Norrath!
