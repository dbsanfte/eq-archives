Biography:

Raised in a poor but decent family in the slums of eastern Freeport, Nilzef's early life is of little consequence. He was brought up a kind and honorable boy in worship of Erollisi Marr, queen of love. His parents harbored none of the evil or deceit found in many dwelling near their home. However at the age of 4 his parents' lives were cut short. while traveling to the northern edge of Freeport, Fezlin and his wife Lamiir were wrongly arrrested for the murder of a shop keep. The paladins of Freeport tried and convicted Nilzef's parents, sentencing them to death. To this day Nilzef holds a deep hatred for the "noble" paladins of the Hall of Truth.

Lost and grief stricken, with no one to turn to, Nilzef wondered the city for many months. One day while exploring around the docks, Nilzef discovered a secret passage below the Seafarer's Roost. Exploring the many hallways and tunnels of this hidden guild, he stumbled upon Elisi Nasin, the guild master for the Coalition of Tradefolks. Intrigued with his nack for sneaking and discovering, the Coalition took him in and showed him the ways of the Rogue.

It soon became clear that Nilzef possessed a great talent for many of the skills required. With years of hard training he grew to become a strong and powerful rogue, one of the greated to be seen by those in Freeport. As age 6, he ventured beyond the commonlands surrounding Freeport, hopping a boat to the continent of Faydwer. However, the teaching of his parents never left him and he did not slip into the ways of an evil rogue, and he continued his worship of Erollisi Marr. Nilzef was driven by the hatred and grief of the loss of his parents and grew stronger every day, seeking justice in his mind for the event that occurred so long ago.

Several years later, at the age of ten, Nilzef returned from Faydwer, having been shunned by those who distrusted him. once again he laid waste to the inhabitants of the commonlands, attaining great skilll in the arts of backstabbing and duel wield. As he continued to search for the answers hi smind yearned for, he began to reaalize the true power of the rogue.

Years passed as Nilzef gained power and prestige, as well as the honor bear his surname, Mortalpesadilla. Upon venturing through the southern plains of Karana, at the age of 25, slaying many a Torn Ear gnoll, he came to meet with sever valiant fighters bearing the mark of the T`Vyl Honor Guard. These good people helped him on his journeys and conquests, offering help in the name of honor and goodness. Nilzef decided to join this group of adventurers, hoping to find, at long last, what he was looking for, and to help in the ways the mighty rogue can, to spread honor throughout the land of Norrath.
