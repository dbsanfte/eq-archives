Biography:

The only child of Liang Hsiao, one of the Ashen Order's greatest champions, Hsian-li was immersed in the world of martial arts since birth. She was raised under the careful guidance of her father, developing skills of both the body and mind. By the age of 15, Hsian-li had won nearly every junior martial arts tournament in Norrath, and many in the Order believed her potential to be higher than even Liang's.

On the eve of her 16th birthday, Liang left for the Ocean of Tears to prepare a celebration for Hsian-li's induction into the Ashen Order. One week later, his ship was discovered, completely destroyed upon the shores of Butcherblock Mountains. Decimated by the loss of her father, Hsian-li fell into a deep depression, and despite her mother's best attempts, was on no path to recover.

Two years after her father's disappearance, Hsian-li went to the docks in East Freeport. In a fury of tears, she called upon Quellious, the god that she had abandoned, to come take her life. A storm came upon Freeport the likes of which had not been seen in years, and Hsian-li was sure she would die. However, in the fog and saltwater mist, she saw her father. He smiled at her for a brief moment, then disappeared in the crash of the waves. Hsian-li's transformation was instantaneous; filled with the love and memory of her father, she rededicated herself to the Order.

Inducted into the Ashen Order after years of vigorous training, Hsian-li continued the path her father had set for her. She left Freeport at the age of 22, to assist those in need throughout Norrath, and to re-establish the name Hsiao as one of respect and honor.
