Biography:

Since his birth, Aeaen's best friends were always his parents. His mother, Raine was a Wood Eleven Druid, and his father, Martine, was a Human Warrior. He was born and lived in Kelethin until his 16th birthday, the most tragic day of his life. His mother and father had been defending the Greater Faydark from Emperor Crush's vile minions. While healing themselves inside the Crushbone Citadel, a family enemy and rival, and at the time mayor of Kelethin, Gavin Deling, led Crush's Emissary, Warlord, the cursed follower of Innoruuk D'Vinn, and Crush himself to where Raine and Martine were hiding. They were massacred.

After their funeral, a week after the incident, Aeaen was talking to his friend Raldo of how his parents truly died, he had been told that they were somehow found in their hidden state by the Orcs. Later that night, Aeaen charged into Deling's home, sword drawn, in an attempt to kill the traitor and murderer. Guards arrived soon, and captured Aeaen before he could invoke his revenge. The next day, he was cast out of Kelethin, his now orphaned brother Cylian was left alone. He was able to make his way to Kaladim and across the ocean to Freeport, where his aunt Aena and uncle Andreas lived. They welcomed him into their home.

Aeaen spent the next five years with his aunt and uncle, until a summons from Kelethin arrived. It read:
"To: Aeaen Sabriel, Freeport
From: Lezandr Aremhic, Ministry of Justice, Kelethin
Re: The Murder of Raine and Martine Sabriel; Your Exile

Mr. Sabriel, New evidence has been presented by a witness that was in the Crushbone Citadel on the date of your parents murder. He says that he saw Gavin Deling facilitate the murder of your parents. This account has been verified by others in the area on the same date. An investigation by the Ministry of Justice has verified these accounts, and Gavin Deling was brought to trial on June 2nd of this year. He was convicted on June 26th on two counts of murder, and was exiled from Kelethin.

As a result, the new Mayor of Kelethin, Mr. Raldo Lightbearer, has pardoned you of the atempted murder charge against Gavin Deling. You may return to Kelethin at any time.

Lezandr Aremhic
Ministry of Justice
Kelethin"

And he did. That same day, Aeaen Sabriel returned to Kelethin on the first boat from Freeport. On his return, he was welcomed by an invitation to join the Emerald Warriors guild as a new Warrior for the city of Kelethin. His younger brother, Cylian, was welcomed into the guild as well, and Aeaen helps his brother train in the ways of the Warrior. He now trains himself to the highest level he can, in hopes that he will soon vanquish his enimies, the Crushbone Orcs.
