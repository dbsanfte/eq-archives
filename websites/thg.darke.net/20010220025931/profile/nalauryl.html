Biography:

A very long time ago, a rogue named Pyrithione was born on Povar, and soon after came Nalaureth, the other half of Pyrithione's soul. Very early in her life, Nalaureth met a very handsome and engaging High Elf enchanter named Laean (sometimes known as Llaean), and together they roamed Povar's Norrath, slaying many orcs and goblins in the service of Mother Tunare. Nalaureth lacked a certain caution, so many were the times that Laean followed her and kept her safe as she retrieved her body. In turn, Nalaureth devoted her sword to Laean. Perhaps, even, a piece of her heart, though that still belonged to Pyrithione. But the day came when Laean felt the pressure of a growing population, felt that Norrath had become too crowded, and so he left Povar for the fairer lands of Lanys. Nalaureth remained, for she must bide with Pyrithione, and there were many friends and guild family on Povar ... yet she missed Laean deeply.

And so, when I, Nalauryl, was born, Nalaureth sent me forth to join Laean on Lanys, so that she herself might always be with Laean through me. Like Nalaureth, I have devoted my sword to Laean, in service of Mother Tunare, and like Nalaureth, I have given him that part of my heart that is mine to give. At heart, I am an explorer. I revel in new vistas, new adversaries, new lands, new friends. I seek to know all there is about Norrath, to hold my friends and guild family dear, to slay all who come against us, and to see every place and everything there is to see in Norrath. I am restless, constantly travelling from place to place, seeking the newest of the new. For Mother Tunare, I seek to rid this world of every evil that exists, no matter how small, no matter how large. I have, and will continue, to sacrifice myself whenever necessary to meet this end. I live for honor, and will not ever endanger my good name for mere gain. There is always another day, another chance, at whatever is disputed ... the wiser soul learns to walk away from petty conflicts.

I count every single member of the HHG as my family, not merely friends, and strive to recall every kindness done me, so that I may return it someday with interest. I count no enemies, for none have done me sufficient ill to warrant a death sentence. None of us know what the future will bring. My sister set me on this path thinking I would follow in her footsteps and be much like her. But I am not.

(And yes, Laean gave me my first kiss, indeed, all my kisses. No one else may claim that privilege. I may flirt outrageously, but in the end, I live for him alone. Never doubt this.)
