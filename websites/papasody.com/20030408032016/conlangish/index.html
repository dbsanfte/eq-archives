Mia's Magical Conlang Page

Out here on the 'Net, constructed languages (aka invented languages, made-up languages, model languages, etc) are sometimes called "conlangs", especially by denizens of the CONLANG e-mail list. It's a name that's stuck for our hobby. (And I like that name for it, myself.)

Many people enjoy this hobby. To some other people, it seems crazy. Let me tell you, I think collecting stamps, watching NASCAR races, and shopping when you don't actually need something are incomprehensible hobbies that other people seem to enjoy, and I don't question it. Making up languages is my thing.
