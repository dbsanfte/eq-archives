One Thing A Day
I registered for school yesterday. I got offered a scholarship based on my placement test scores and the program I am going into. I have to fill out the paperwork for that today. Hopefully that will go through without a hitch.

I am also in the mood to go do some exploring. I saw a shop in Dover yesterday that looked interesting to me, so I may go down there if I am going to go take care of the scholarship application too. I also want to get to a park and take a good long walk. If I go to Dover, I may go to Silver Lake and walk up to Legislative Hall and back.

Walking helps me think. It's a meditation in motion. I've got a lot of real life, mundane stuff going on right now to think about. I also have other, less mundane things I've been thinking about recently.

I want to change my world. I figure if I do one thing a day, it puts me ahead of all the people who are just thinking about doing something, and miles ahead of people who aren't even thinking.

New Look, New Title, New Me
This blog was formerly titled "Bright New Day. " The template I was using before (ironically?) was a sort of dreary, steel blue and grey. Mostly, there wasn't anything there. I think it put too much pressure on me to try to make my days "new" and "bright" during a time when very little was really improving in my life. So, I have gone back to a different title ("Arradiary"), which matches the screen name I use here, and doesn't really say anything at all about what sort of days I might be having. And I picked out a new template that suits my mood these days.

We went and talked to the financial aid office at the local tech'n'community college. I think things are looking up on that front. Tonight I am supposed to go to a meeting about the homeschool association I am considering for my son. I don't know if I can afford to shell out their tuition right this moment, but I will go check out the meeting anyway. Tomorrow I have to go talk to my academic counselor at the college and find out what classes I should be signing up for. This week, I have to register the other kids in whatever schools they will be going to. It's going to be a busy week, but a lot of this stuff is just stuff that needs to be gotten out of the way. Things will calm down between now and October as we all get into school time routines.

I still have a handful of things to do today. I need to turn in a job application, mail our mortgage, and maybe go by my mom's for a little bit. I am almost certain that there was something else, but I don't remember what it was.
