          PC GAMES!
          EverQuest: Trilogy -- This is the original EverQuest online roleplaying game, plus 2 expansions (Ruins of Kunark and Scars of Velious). This, a computer, an Internet connection and a credit card or a Everquest 90 day Pre-Paid Game Card is all you need to get started adventuring with thousands of like-minded geeks.
          Everquest: Shadows Of Luclin -- Another EverQuest expansion, including new zones, a new race of cat people and a new class. I liked this expansion a lot more than I wanted to.
          Everquest: Planes of Power -- This is the latest thing to expand your EQ experience.
          OR... Just buy Everquest Gold, while supplies last, and get EverQuest plus all the expansions including Planes of Power. (Pre-order, supposed to ship Nov. 4.)

          The Elder Scrolls 3: Morrowind -- This is a single player RPG. It's an interesting world to run around, and it is open-ended, so you decide what you want to do and when. My son and I have both been playing it in very different ways-- his character is a sneaky thief, and my character has spent most of her existence performing tasks for the Imperial Cult. It's nice to play at your own pace, sometimes. My main complaint would be about my character's slow running speed, but that's minor (and you can cheat your way around it).

          Tropico -- This is also a single player game of the Sim sort. Run your own island! Build up your island's economy, try to keep your people happy, and even build resorts to lure in tourists.

          The Sims Deluxe Edition This is The Sims plus Living Large and some other stuff. This is what to buy if you are just getting started with this people simulator.
          The Sims House Party Expansion Pack -- This adds more content for your Sims, around the party theme.

          The Sims Hot Date Expansion Pack -- This adds more content for your Sims, and lets you take them downtown (one at a time, according to the reviews-- I don't own Hot Date yet, myself.
          The Sims Vacation Expansion Pack -- Another expansion that we haven't gotten yet. Take your Sims on vacation. (duh.)
          The Sims Unleashed Expansion Pack -- This is the newest expansion. Your Sims can get pets.
          The Sims Online (Pre-Order). This will be an on-line version of The Sims. Considering the popularity of The Sims and online games, this should be pretty big.
