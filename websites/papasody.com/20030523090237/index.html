19 March 2003
The big news for the day is that we are rearranging our webspace to cover news and previews of selected MMORPGs in development. There will be a complete redesign of this site's style and content before I am done, but I figured I might as well get started.

The games we are looking at right now are:

  • EverQuest II, sequel to the immensely popular EverQuest,
  • World of Warcraft, Blizzard's entry into the MMORPG ring based on their Warcraft series,
  • Mythica, a Norse mythology game by Microsoft that looks to be quite innovative,
  • The Saga of Ryzom, an European game with an interesting world/story concept , and
  • Dark and Light, another game that caught my eye; the English translations on the site are rough in spots, but it looks interesting.

    Hopefully once we get it all up and running, we'll have links to other fan sites, guild web sites, and other helpful resources.
