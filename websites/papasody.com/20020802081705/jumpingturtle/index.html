Jumping Turtle Homeschool Association

Welcome to the Jumping Turtle homepage!
We are a homeschool association (also known as an "umbrella school") based in Smyrna, Delaware. We accept all families who embrace concept of The Golden Rule, regardless of faith.
You might be wondering why we chose the name "Jumping Turtle". The reason is this: noone thinks of turtles as jumpers. Noone expected the tortoise to beat the hare. We believe in stretching one's self, reaching for a dream, achieving unexpected things, and rising to one's greater potential, even if it is a long journey to reach that destiny. We can all be jumping turtles!
