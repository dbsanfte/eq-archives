Oak Meadow (the curriculum we are using this year)

10/25/2002: Our whole school is one student this year. Until that changes, we'll be dedicating some of this webspace to what our student is doing and how he's coming along. It's been a good year so far. Although he is technically an 8th grader, We are using the Oak Meadow 9th grade courses as the backbone of his program of study. In addition to academic progress, he's made new friends in the neighborhood and tried out some new activities. We've got some interesting projects planned, so stay tuned to this webpage for updates.
