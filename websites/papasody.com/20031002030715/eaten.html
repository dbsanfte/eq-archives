MOVIES IN WHICH PEOPLE GET EATEN:

Pitch Black (Unrated version, DVD);Pitch Black (Rated Version, DVD): I have to admit that I expected it to be totally cheese-o-rrific, and it was maybe only 75% cheesy. It is a bit like the Aliens movies, but not as well done. Still, it really is worth getting if (like me) you like to curl up on the couch late at night to munch popcorn and see people get eaten by creatures that live in the dark. -- Mama.
Vin Diesel hasn't done anything any better than this since then. Case in point, we rented xXx; part way through, I got up and went to check my e-mail. Sometime later, I turned around and our entire family was in the basement watching Octopus on Sci-Fi while the other movie was still playing in the VCR upstairs. I liked Pitch Black actually. -- Papa.

Lake Placid (DVD)/(VHS): This movie was more comedy than horror, though there were a few "gross-out" bits. It's about giant crocodiles eating people in Maine. Enough said. It's a completely silly movie, which is a good thing. My favorite character is a foul-mouthed old lady (played by Betty White) who treats the giant croc as a pet. Oliver Platt is in this as a rich eccentric who sees divinitiy in crocodiles. I just thought I would mention him because I think he's cute. --Mama
Best line of the whole movie: "I'm rooting for the crocodile. I hope he swallows your friends whole." --Papa.

Deep Blue Sea (DVD)/(VHS): Deep Blue Sea is a shark movie. The very best part of it is that everyone that typically survives in this sort of movie gets eaten. The hero guy doesn't get the girl in the end-- the shark does. That alone is enough to make me recommend this movie. Consider it the shark movie that makes fun of shark movies. --Mama
Jaws... On steroids... Nuff said. --Papa

Ravenous (DVD)/(VHS): This is a movie about cannibalism. I suspect that half the budget for this film was spent on fake blood; this is not a movie for people who get queasy easily. In any case, it's a quirky little movie with moments of humor. --Mama
Soup's On... Just don't ask what kind of meat this is... It might be someone you know. --Papa

Dragonslayer (DVD)/(VHS): You have to like a movie in which the only people the dragon will eat are virgins. This dragon has a lot to smile about. But you would smile too, if you'd just eaten a maiden. -- Papa

Reign of Fire (DVD)/(VHS) -- Dragons eat people and devastate the world. Shut your brain off before you put this movie on if you want to get the most mileage from it. --Mama
Wonderfully done special effects. Superb acting. Dragons and characters done so well, you can actually believe in them. The down side? Next to zero plot. However, as long as you don't think about the plot too much, you'll like this movie. --Papa
