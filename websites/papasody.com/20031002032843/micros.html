MICRONATIONS:

    Microfreedom.com: The Micronation and Sovereignty Website Index -- This is a good place to begin to explore the world of micronations. It has a very short explanation of what micronations are, and links to various micronations sorted by category.
    micro-nations.org -- This site has a directory of micronations in a convenient table format. The "Categories explained" link at the top of the table gives the key to the symbols used on the table.
