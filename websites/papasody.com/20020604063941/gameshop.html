Massively Multiplayer Online Role-Playing Games (MMORPG or MMOG)
This is, hands down, our favorite category of PC games. In these games, you play the role of a character in a fantasy (or sci-fi) world, but you aren't just playing alone or with computer-controlled companions. You are, rather, playing in a world populated with hundreds, if not thousands, of other player characters, just like yours! You can chat while you play, form alliances, buy and sell equipment, and gather together in groups to go hunting monsters. There is usually a monthly fee charged for these games ($10-15) which may seem like a lot, but is cheap compared to going to the movies or having a smoking habit.

Everquest Trilogy: This contains the original EverQuest game, plus the first 2 expansion packs. This game is set in a 3-D fantasy world called Norrath. You can choose a number of races (for example, human, troll, ogre, 3 different kinds of elf, and more), and then you can choose from the professions available to your race (for example, warrior, druid, shadow knight, or any of several others). From there, you can hunt down monsters, look for equipment and make friends with some of the thousands who play this game together. This is Papa's gaming addiction choice. The Official Site

Everquest: Shadows of Luclin: Do you already play EverQuest? Well, then you probably already know that a new expansion is being released! Here it is, for those of you who can't get enough EQ. You need the full game to use the expansion.

Ultima Online: Renaissance: This game is also available as "Ultima Online: Third Dawn", which has updated graphics, but I haven't tried that out. I have played UO:Renaissance. It has somewhat dated 2-D graphics, but it is still a good game. While the only character type available is human, there is great flexibility to make your character whatever you want. Nicro swears by it. The Official Site

Asheron's Call: Dark Majesty: While Asheron's Call hasn't taken the massively multiplayer world by storm the way EverQuest did (and the way that Dark Age of Camelot appears to be doing), this is still a game worth mentioning. It is set in a fantasy world called Dereth, but there are not a lot of cliché fantasy creatures like elves and dragons. The thing that is most interesting about Asheron's Call is that it has an evolving story line and monthly global events. The title of this game on Amazon.com includes the words "Expansion Pack", but it comes with the full, original Asheron's Call as well, so you don't need to buy both. The new expansion includes things like new places to explore and housing. The Official Site

Anarchy Online: This sci-fi based game got off to a really bad start, which has given the game a bad reputation. You can see this in the reviews on Amazon.com. This is a real shame! It has been patched up, and now it isn't bad at all. There are still some lag issues in crowded city areas, but these can be gotten around by shortening the distance that you can see ahead of you. It has, in my [Mama's] opinion, the best inter-player communication system of all the games I have played. I have no problem finding people who want to team up, and the part of me that loves first-person shooter games also really enjoys the single player missions. I don't normally play MMORPGs with the game music, but I make an exception for this game; it's actually pretty good. Finally, I must mention that Anarchy Online has a story line based around the conflict between the mega-corporation, Omni-Tek, and rebel Clans. You get to choose which side to join, or you can take no side and be Neutral. This is Mama's MMOG pick of the day. The Official Site

Dark Age of Camelot: We have not played this game, but it is the latest thing and it has gotten rave reviews. As soon as I know more, I'll certainly be back with the news! The Official Site
