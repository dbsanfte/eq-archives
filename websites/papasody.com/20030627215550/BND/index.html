:: Thursday, June 05, 2003 ::

I started this some time ago, but then never used it. My bright new days didn't turn out to be so bright nor was there anything especially new about them. Now I have reached a point where things have to change for me. It's not a matter of luxury or choice. New things are going to be coming down the pike whether I like it or not, so I might as well try to find the bright part while I am at it.

I am not good at letting go of the past or adjusting to changes. I always want the good thing that I used to have, especially when I feel like I am being dragged off in a direction that I don't want to go.

But where is it that I don't want to go? Or is the right question where I do want to go? Both questions are hard. They require the kind of introspection that I have been avoiding for a long time. But my time is up now, and I'd better get to answering those questions for myself pretty quickly before I really do get dragged off to somewhere I don't want to be.

But for now, it is 2am, and I am going to go to sleep.
