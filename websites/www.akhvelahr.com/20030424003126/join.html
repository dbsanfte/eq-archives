Welcome! Akh'Velahr Charter


We the Akh'Velahr being of sound mind and body, do hereby pledge ourselves to the banishment of evil, to the ongoing support of our fellow guild members, and to our fellow Norrathians in life and death.


Akh'Velahr Guild Rules of Conduct


1. Valor - The strength of mind and spirit.
Above all you are Akh'Velahr. Be you a caster, cleric, warrior or the like, you are elite . Your cause is your duty to your fellow members. Fight for your cause, and fight bravely. Never show fear, for fear leads to doubt, doubt leads to hate, and that leads to USWest.

2. Chivalry - Being gallant and distinguished.
Be polite and courteous at all times. Think before you speak, especially before harsh words. You are a direct reflection upon your guild and your cause.

3. Honor - We are ethical in conduct.
Have honor by behaving in a way that reflects well upon yourself and your guild. Be worthy of respect and esteem. Recognize those that are honorable.

4. Respect - Having high and special regard.
Treat everyone you meet with the same respect you would like them to show you. Have respect for yourself and the respect of others will follow.

5. Justice - We merit rewards and punishments.
Do not tolerate the bad behaviors of others. Report to the guild those of bad conduct. We will not tolerate corrupt persons. Recognize the good acts of others, we want to know those that do well.

6. Compassion - Alleviating the distress of others.
Help others whenever you can. Your good reputation will grow, and will reflect directly on your guild.

7. Pride - Having proper self-respect.
Be proud of who you are, and the guild you are in. Show pride in your class, race and membership in Akh'Velahr.

8. Duty - Our moral obligations.
Have duty to your guild. If you can help a fellow member, do so, even if it is at your own expense.

9. Comradeship - We are friends and family.
Respect each other in the highest esteem. Don't cheat, lie to or mislead a guild member. Disagreements between guild members should be dealt with privately, and not aired on guild or public channels. If a solution can not be reached, consult a guild officer who will be happy to help.
