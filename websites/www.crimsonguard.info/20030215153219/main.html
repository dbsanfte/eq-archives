Marathon Raiding.- Update by Tribulation ~ 1/14/03

Well after Friday's marathon of raids, one would hardly be surprised by the low number of people on over the weekend.
I am going to break down Friday's marathon for all of you to see what can be done when people put their minds to raiding. We didn't have a single wipe out on Zlandicar, Keeper, Yelinak, Gozzrem or Telkenor so it was a nice smooth day.
The first thing we did (not sure on time), since nothing else was up, was kill Velketor. We took hi with down with around 24 people, probably the easiest I have seen him go down.
Grats to Vilisto on Boots of Bladecalling

We then headed to Kael and took down Vindi about 3pm
Grats to Relann on Chestplate of Vindication.

On the way to WToV we saw that Zlandicar was up. We jumped down to DN and ganked him with very little effort.
Grats to Seddie on First Brood Talisman.
Grats to Jiraia on Zlandicar's Heart.
Grats to Deorul on Frakadar's Talisman.

By this time it was about 6 PM and we still only a hand full of people on, very odd for a Friday and it being a patch day. Went still went after keeper and took him out while only being 29 people Strong.
Grats to Talcon on Truesight Helm.
Grats to Zumarry on Silver Girdle of Stability.

8 PM we headed to Sky Shrine and made our way to Yelinak's layer. We ganked him with about 40 people.
Grats to Daladen on Yelinak's Head.
Grats to Talcon and Teri on Dragons Blood Earring.
Grats to Toren on Dragon's Eye Sage Ring.
Grats to Iadara on Celestial Essence Robe.

We decided to head back to Kael but found that King Tormax and TheStatue / AoW were either killed or being engaged, so we went back to WtoV and killed Telkenor and Gozzrem.
Grats to Polyx and Orcdestroyer on Lance of Thunder.
Grats to Jiraia on Transparent Eyepatch.
Grats to Korthur on Shovel of the Harvest.

By this time is was 3am, so we moved over to EToV to take out DoZ. I think fatigue set in because we didn't take him down.

Even though the day ended on a sour note, you would all have to agree that 14 hours worth of raiding is not something that very many guilds can say they have done. Well done to all those that hung in there.
