An expanding area of information security management is the concept of "Proactive Security Management." Vigilant's Proactive Command Center (PSCC) service harnesses this concept to provide our clients with proactive approach to security management. Whereas historically security management has been reactive, focused on detecting attacks as they happen, PSCC concentrates on aggressively preventing the loopholes and vulnerabilities that allow attacks to take place, thereby beating beat potential hackers to the punch.
PSCC's base methodology is to use a continuous series of penetration tests to identify vulnerabilities. By integrating the data gathered from the PSCC service into the Integrated Command Center (ICC), our clients can maintain both a real-time and forward-looking view of their current security posture.

The objective of PSCC is to:

  • Provide our clients with insight into risks in their network infrastructure that could lead to its compromise.
  • Prioritize identified vulnerabilities according to risk level to ensure that our client's resources are spent on the highest-risk issues.
  • Establish industry-recommended approaches to remediating all discovered vulnerabilities in the form of a security action plan that encompasses both the technological and the business objectives of the client.
  • Integrate ongoing penetration testing activities into the overall day-to-day operations of the ICC, allowing the client to have ultimate ownership of all aspects of their security management.
    The sections below provide a comprehensive methodology for penetration testing as well as a high-level description of the types of tests that are included in the PSCC offering.

Penetration Testing Methodology
Penetration testing is designed to simulate the conditions under which actual malicious penetration takes place, pinpointing a client's susceptibility to application, network, and operating system breaches. By finding the weaknesses in our client's system before the hackers do, PSCC can help implement defensive strategies that will protect business-critical systems and information. The PSCC service furnishes experience-based mitigation strategies for each discovered vulnerability that take into account the potential business impact of both the vulnerability itself and its recommended fix.

PSCC provides a graphic picture of a client's security controls' effectiveness. As part of this service, a penetration team tries to aggressively breach client networks or internet applications, and then exploit vulnerabilities. The idea is not to damage a client's system, but demonstrate the consequences of not remediating security loopholes. Our assessment teams can perform a variety of agreed-upon exploitations, using client-specified markers or tokens to indicate penetration levels. Alternatively, if our client does not want us to actually exploit vulnerabilities, Vigilant will just verify and document the existence of security loopholes. PSCC pinpoints threats; verifies a client's ability to identify and react to threats, and provides a baseline to measure improvement over a specific time interval.

Vigilant's consultants have established leading-edge penetration assessment procedures and regularly update them with the newest techniques and vulnerabilities. A typical assessment begins with passive measures designed to establish the parameters of the target network, and then escalates in progressive steps as the penetration team exploits weak configurations and improperly secured systems to gain unauthorized or privileged system access.

Throughout the testing, Vigilant provides the client with constant progress updates, identifying appropriate target systems and high-risk findings for which immediate remediation is required. The end product of a penetration test is a technically-accessible management-level report that includes detailed technical findings on identified problems, their potential consequences, Vigilant's recommended solutions, and potential business impacts.

The PSCC penetration testing consultants are experts in the latest methods and techniques used to attack IT infrastructure. Every PSCC consultant has in-depth experience in assessing firewalls, operating systems, Web servers, e-commerce applications, PBXs, and remote enterprise dial-in access systems. All consultants' backgrounds have been rigorously verified to ensure that they have the kind of ethical profile that penetration testing demands; members of the hacker community and persons with criminal backgrounds are specifically excluded from employment in our company.

The Vigilant PSCC service is ultimately intended to provide our clients with the ability to undertake Proactive Security Management themselves rather than being dependent on a third-party to ensure their security. The PSCC service includes a comprehensive knowledge transfer component: client security personnel participate in penetration testing activities and undergo hands-on training under real-world conditions. PSCC is offered as a standalone service, but can also be integrated into the operations of an ICC to enable strategic planning around threat remediation issues.

Types of Penetration Tests
Although Vigilant can tailor our PSCC service to conform to just about any parameters, we provide a standard suite of tests to meet client requirements for confidentiality and accuracy. The following are the assessments most commonly performed in the context of PSCC:

Web Application Penetration Assessment: This test is designed to assess the development effort of our client's Web application, identify design flaws, and recommend methodologies for improving the security of the Web application at the development level. We also can assess transactional Web applications to verify the security of client-developed software components.

External Penetration Assessment: In this test, Vigilant consultants attempt to access or electronically transgress external firewalls, routers, and any network perimeter devices protecting the client's network.

Internal Penetration Assessment: This test encompasses an internal vulnerability assessment against selected systems within the client's Internet infrastructure to verify that the security controls implemented by the servers and supporting systems provide an adequate level of protection against external and internal network attacks.

Wireless Penetration Assessment: This test is designed to assess the security profile of client 802.11(b) wireless networks by mapping and identifying active client wireless networks; assessing wireless implementation for vulnerabilities; and exploit vulnerabilities to access other network segments.
