Dark Vigilance Membership

*Dark Vigilance is currently only accepting petitions for members of the 35th season and above*

Before submitting your petition for membership, please make sure you have read the Code of Conduct. Once you have completed the form, you will receive a response within a day or so, via email, letting you know that your petition has been received. Please include on your petition for membership if you have a recommendation from a current Dark Vigilance member. Once your petition has finalized, you will receive a response, in game. Please feel free to contact any guild Officer in regards to your petition, however, know that only a select few are involved in the recruitment process. So the status of your petition will not be known to all Officers, but they can answer any general questions you have about the guild.
