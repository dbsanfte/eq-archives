** Customer Service Limited Holiday Staffing **

This Monday, September 3rd, Sony Online Entertainment Customer Service will have limited staffing due to the Labor Day holiday. Our telephone and technical support will be shut down on Monday as well. Normal Game Master hours, phone and technical support will resume on Tuesday

*** New Free Zone - Stonebrunt Mountains ***

That's what this patch is all about. We're adding the Stonebrunt Mountains. You will need to choose the Optional Patch to download the files you will need to be able to enter the zone.

From the press release:

"Located on the small continent of Odus, the Stonebrunt Mountains are rich with vegetation and distinctive hills that give way to the towering Mount Klaw; the largest mountain in Odus and home to the Kerran village of Kejeka. The zone of Stonebrunt is designed to be a mid-level, outdoor zone similar in scope and scale to the Lake of Ill Omen on Kunark, which has proven to be a very popular zone in EverQuest."

You can see the press release in its entirety on our web site www.everquest.com

- The EverQuest Team
