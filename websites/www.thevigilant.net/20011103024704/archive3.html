An Epic Sunday
July 29, 2001

Sunday, Sunday, Sunday!- Today, two of Dark Vigilance's crew completed their epic quest and have received their epic weapons. Send /tells of congratulations to Enuch, who obtained his Staff of the Shissar earlier on Sunday morning. Then after that, send /tells of WOOTS to Finnister for being our first shaman to complete his epic quest, and getting himself a bright new shiny Spear of Fate! Way to go you guys! You both deserve em.

Sunday treats!- On our way to slay Lord Rak Ashiir for Finnister, we were able to nab a few good weapons. Look to the Latest Spoils section to find out what dropped, and who has what. Also, I added Finnister and Enuch to our Epics section. Other sections have been updated, from our Archives, to to our Roster, and to or General links sections. Take a peek!

Its not a Sunday, but Tuesday can suffice... maybe- Tuesday and Sunday have alot in common. They both have the word day in them. Yup yup, many similarities between the two. Or not... Anyways, on July 31, 5 pm PDT (8 pm EDT), Kellinar and the gang will be heading down to Kaesora to obtain. Sign up to go and get your Xalgoz fang so you can start playing in Howling Stones.

Sunday by any other name- Is not Sunday. Its just another day. But not a Sunday. Like Thursday. Or more specifically Thursday, August 30th. On the day that is not Sunday, beginning at 4 pm PDT (7 pm EDT), we will be raiding Plane of Fear. To sign up for some Planar butt kicking, sign up here and fill out the info.

No more Sundays, but Fridays are good! On Friday, August 3rd, we'll be heading up to the Plane of Air and do some exploring while we'll help Archaeus obtain his one of his epic pieces, the Fiery Avenger, and soon the Fiery Defender, his epic. He'll be our second paladin to complete the quest. Maybe he won't get massacred by Orc Pawns this time around... For more info for this event, read it here!

Ok, maybe not all these events are on Sunday. Well, actually, none of them are, but thats besides the point! Sunday is a good day, and the site is updated. Hope y'all enjoy!

Yours Holy,
--- Themis
