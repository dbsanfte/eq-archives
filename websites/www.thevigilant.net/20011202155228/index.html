/GUILDDISBAND- The Guild Disband command happened at noon, 1 pm pst . Elizabetta will get the guild reformed on Saturday, November 17, approximately around 3 pm PST by GM Deyvon. As stated by our new Guild Mistress,

"And I think this would be the perfect time for those choosing to move on to do so. No guilt, no pressure... when your tag is off, if you're not wanting to come back, then move on with my blessing. Don't feel that you owe me to "stay" if you're not happy or comfortable.

If you're coming back, then let's meet on Saturday in S Ro to reform. Or anytime after Saturday once guild is reformed send me a tell.. or whatever Officer you can find online."
-Liz

This was in order to get rid of some twinks of people who left the guild, and to start anew.
