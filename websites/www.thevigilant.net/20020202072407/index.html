DV 2.1
Dec 2, 2001




Merger- And here we thought it would only be DV 2.0! We are proud to announce the release of DV 2.1. Not only do you get the great folks of the remaining Dark Vigilance members, but you also get not one, not two, but the entire Justice of the Silent guild. Whoa! What a deal?! How can this be you say? Is this too good to be true you say? Nay! Our two guilds have merged for the greater good of each. Our classes and skills are proving to compliment each other very well in the early going! So if you are looking for a friend that was in JotS, look no further than the new and improved DV 2.1!!

Other News-The Dark Vigilance home page (yes the one you are reading right now) should be coming down in a day or so for a short time for a server move. We will be moving from an NT box to a Unix server with a few more features. Hopefully it will lead to the death of Guild Magic for DV (and there was much rejoicing.../cheer). This will in no way affect the access to our message boards which can always be reached at http://pub88.ezboard.com/bdarkvigilance

To the moon Alice!
