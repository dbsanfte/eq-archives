Rules and Loot Policies on Planar Events
As of August 05, 2001



The Planes- Behavior, Loot and Epics

Copied straight from the Policies in our Forums and modified as of 08/05/01


Expectations for All Planes Raids:

1. You will read any emails/message boards regarding the raid, raiding plans, groups, event scheduling
2. You will post any questions/clarifications that you need, either via email to Officers, or under DV Only section entitled Guild Events, prior to the raid. (Do not OOC/SHOUT out questions during the raid) All issues should be cleared up prior to entering the Plane.
3. You will sign up on the Message Board, at least 2 days prior to scheduled event, under the GUILD EVENT section.
4. You will verify attendance the day of event
5. You will have yourself to the designated meeting location at least 25 minutes PRIOR to the raid’s scheduled start time (unless the Guild Leader/Raid Organizer has previous notification, and approves an alternative suggestion. Example: You have to work and cannot attend raid till after an hour after start of raid. This must be pre-approved to avoid confusion and DEATH to those already in the Plane)
6. You will listen to the Guild Leader/Raid Organizer, Puller, Group Leader, in that order.
7. You will ASSIST the main assist, creating a HOTKEY to assist the designated person. (Not assisting as instructed can lead to your death, the death of guild mates, and most importantly the death of the ENCHANTER!)
8. Evacuation location will be pre-set, before entering the plane. Make sure that you evacuate to the designated location.
9. Most importantly, remember to follow all orders to the word.

Let’s have some FUN!!!!

Words, Runes, and Pages:

1. Will be distributed to their respective classes
2. If the rune/word/page is not going to be used by recipient, then it is to be passed on to a guild “mule” to vault for guild use, of those that need that item

Sellable Items (including Gems/Rare Gems):

Go to the Guild Vault, as we self fund our own raids.

Class Specific Armor:

On GUILD only raids, with guests in attendance that does not require any Class Specific Armor:

1. The Breastplate piece, and only the Breastplat piece, will be awarded based on Guild Seniority. (Realizing that as each senior member receives the breastplate, you will move up the seniority list. And/or a senior guild mate to oneself will not be in attendance for each plane raid) **
2. On all other pieces, randoms will be conducted for any person in need of that item.
3. Once an item is awarded, until every other person in attendance in that class wins an item, one is out of the randoms, unless the Breastplate drops. ** The Breastplate will then be awarded to the Senior Class guild mate in attendance. (Yes, on a very rare occasion, a person could win 2 drops on a single raid!! However, the odds are extremely against this.)
4. If the item you currently possess is not a no drop item, and you receive a better item on a guild sponsored raid, it anticipated you would be willing to pass it down to another guildmate in more need of the item, before seeking profit outside the guild for it.

**(In the case of a linkdeath, it will go to the 2nd in seniority, if there is a tie for seniority, a random will ensue)


On GUILD raids with guests in attendance that require Class Specific Armor:

1. BP/Robe Seniority rule applies
2. All other pieces will be randomed
3. Once an item is awarded, until every other person in attendance in that class wins an item, one is out of the randoms.


Class Specific Weapons:

You have the right to pass on any item, opting out of a random. Choose wisely.
1. Every piece will be randomed.
2. Shields count against Class Specific Armor (if it is a shield with an AC greater than 14).
3. Once an item is awarded, until every other person in attendance in that class wins an item, one is out of the randoms.

Epic Item Drops:

Method One will be used when guests are in attendance, based on Guild Leader’s discretion:
Will be pre-randomed, before entering Plane. If an Epic Item Piece drops, the winner of the Pre-Random will loot. (In the case of linkdeath, the 2nd runner up will loot)

Method Two will be used for all GUILD only raids:
Based on NEED, if an epic quest is in progress, with the Guild Leader’s approval, the item will go to the guild mate in the most NEED of an item to finish the quest, based on percentage of quest completed. One can default the drop to another guild mate of the same class.
