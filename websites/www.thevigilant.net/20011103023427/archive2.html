Update, Zergling rush style!
July 9/10, 2001


Sorry for the long delay on the updates. Here goes:

Guild Meeting!- Thursday, July 12th, 6:30 pm PDT (9:30 pm EDT), Dark Vigilance will have its 2nd gathering over at the wizard spires in Toxx Forest. If you're online, you need to be there.

Events!
July 3, 2001

I'd like to remind everybody to periodically look at our Events Calendar underneath our Guild section. You can find upcoming events there, ranging from guild activities, to personal events, as long as there is a date and time created for them.

There will be 3 upcoming events. Before attending these events, please read over our Planar and Nonplanar policies,

Chardok Raid- Dark Vigilance will attempt to take down the Royals on Friday, July 6, 2001. Raid will begin approximately 2-3pm EDT (11am-12pmPDT). We're looking for epic drops, as well as some nice toys along the way.
Plane of Growth- We'll be getting our feet wet here, prepping up for our soon to be upcoming week long raid here for those that don't mind ruining their Tunarian faction. It will begin at 4pm EDT (1pm PDT) on Saturday, July 7th.
Plane of Hate- Our second Hate raid! This will begin at 7pm EDT (4pm PDT) on Monday, July 9th. Make sure you are there as soon as possible, at the least, unless you stated somewhere else on the sign up post, as we will begin forming up the groups and passing out the Portal regeants.

--- Themis
