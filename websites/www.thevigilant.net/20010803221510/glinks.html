Here are some general links to some of the most used EverQuest related sites. These sights include very useful maps, equipment ranging from armor to weapons to hand held items, faction lists, and walk throughs of zones and quests. If you'd like to add more, or something is wrong with one of the links, please e-mail me at Themis_Silverlight@hotmail.com.



The Links

Allkhazam General Site with great info on quest, zones, equipment, emotes, pet commands, and other general EQ stuff.

Caster's Realm Constant up to date messages from the Sony EQ Boards. This also has a wonderful spell database, as well as class specifics, forums, and mana calculators.

EQ'Lizer Site that specializes in spoilers for quests, has info on equipment and zones. Also has an astounding Bestiary. Their new address is here.

EQVault Another spoiler site! Not only does this site include game info, its also a cross roads for the servers of Norrath. You can find all the servers VN boards here, and you can keep yourself up to date with the server status!

Everlore Basic EQ Knowledge, as well as forums to quests. Nice spoiler site!

EQtraders Great place to learn trade skills! Learn how to bake, jewel craft, and other wonderful tradeskills!

EQ Trade Skills A compendium of EQ trade skills, including minumal success charts, ingredients, etc.

EQ Atlas Awesome site that has many maps to zones. Ever get lost? Well, print some of these maps and you'll be ahead of the game a step or two!

EQmaps Sadly, this has turned into a pay site. But it also has many maps that are still for free, and that you can print up. If you are able to pay, this site provides detailed drop tables and zone guides.

EQ Client information Nice little link directly to the EQClient section of Caster's Realm dealing with your EQini file.

Mana Calculator- Stolen straight off of Caster's Realm and into your guild site. Figure out your mana total here, be you hybrid, priest, or caster.

Time zone Converter Just as it states! If you are having trouble converting the times due to you/us living in different time zone, look it up here to find out what time the event is happening!
