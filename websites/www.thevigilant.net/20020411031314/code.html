Code of Honor

Before you lies the Code of Honor that our family strives to uphold on the lands of Norrath. We welcome all within our fold, requiring only a stout heart, a loyalty to family, and the desire to pursue the adventures that appear before us.

We hold sacred these Truths:
~ Treat all guild mates as brothers and sisters, for they form your extended family on Norrath.
~ Honor diversity
~ Treat all those you encounter in your adventures, as you would your guild family, with respect and honor in all negotiations and actions.
~ Respect the wishes and ideals of all your brothers and sisters, even if you do not agree.
~ Hatred, intolerance, sloth, and greed are not vices tolerated within the guild.
~ All are equal in the eyes of the guild, be they Guild Leader, Officers, or brothers and sisters in arms.
~ All are allowed the freedom to role play, and those that choose not to, shall respect and not ridicule those that follow this path.

We seek adventurers, of the 35th season, that will follow the laws set forth by our Leader and Guild Council, and seek a family to grow and adventure with!

Bylaws of the Guild:
~ Open disrespect for any guild mate will not be tolerated, and can lead to guild removal
~ All requests/commands of the Guild Leader or Officers is to be obeyed, even if they are on a secondary character, granted they notify you that they are an Officer, and who their primary Officer character is.
~ Attempting to defraud another player, will be automatic grounds for guild removal, if found guilty of the deed.
~ Members should not accept a gift they will not use. Allow another to benefit from the generosity of the giver.
~ Seek to gift others in need, with items you no longer need, before seeking profit outside the guild.
~ Ask only for assistance with a quest from the guild, that you can complete with a group of your peers, being a helpful hand, not a hindrance. Hence, the word assistance.
~ Begging in guild is not permitted, be it for items or powerleveling.
~ Requests for resurrections and/or ports, should be addressed in tells to the appropriate class. Seek services in the zone, before seeking guild assistance, unless a guild brother or sister is in the same zone. Response from the cleric/druid/wizard is to be taken as the final word. If they can assist, they will.
Do not pester your brothers and sisters for services.
~ Seek to aid and assistance your guild brothers and sisters, sometimes, before they ask.
~ Foul language in guild chat is not permitted.
- Any usage of the F* word will be punishable by a warning, on first offense
- Second Offense : A Planar Sanction will be imposed of <-125> on randoms (Random: 200 to 700, you roll 300 to 575)
- Third Offense : Removal from the guild
- At any time, if a problem or attitude, in regards to the Foul Language Policy, is found offensive by the Guild Leader or Officer, you can be removed for profanity usage, without any counseling sessions, fines or sanctions imposed.
~ If you approach the Application Review Committee, or Officers, regarding initiation to the guild of real life friends or family, they may be added to the guild with no level requirement. However, you are personally vouching for them, and they will be treated as your "twink." Choose wisely.

Word of the Officers:
The word of the Guild Leader and Officers is the law of the guild and shall be followed accordingly. Problems amongst guild members will be settled privately. Should guild members not be able to resolve their differences in an adult, non-combative, non-abusive manner, they can request Guild Officer mediation. The decision of the Guild Officers will be final and binding. By agreeing to Guild Officer arbitration, guild members thereby indicate acceptance of the Word of the Officer.

Promise of the Officers:
~ The Guild Leader and Officers promise to treat all with respect and dignity.
~ Should a guild member have a problem or issue with an Officer, they may redress their concern to the other Guild Officers, without fear of reprisal from the Officer in question.
~ The Guild Officers will follow the guidelines set forth by meetings of the Officers. We seek to keep all actions and decisions uniform and fair.
~ We shall treat each other as we wish to be treated, and in return we shall receive the fairness and respect deserved for our actions.

Counseling:
If there is a problem with a guild brother or sister, the Officers will appoint an single Officer to address the concern to the guild member. A "counseling" session will ensue, with clear guidelines for resolution of the issue. Should an agreement on the outcome not be able to be reached, the guild member in question has the right to Appeal to the other Guild Officers or Guild Leader, and a majority vote will uphold the final decision. If no agreement can be reached, the guild member can/may be removed from the guild, with no further actions taken. Depending upon the seriousness of the issue/problem, instantaneous guild removal can occur, and is not subject to review.

The laws, ideals and promises set forth in this document are a guideline for the guild. They are subject to revision, interpretation of the Guild Officers, or override of the Guild Leader.
