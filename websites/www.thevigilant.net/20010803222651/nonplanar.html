Rules and Loot Policies on Non Planar Events
As of July 1, 2001

NEED BEFORE GREED

Copied straight from the Policies in our Forums

Expectations for All Non Planar Raids:


1. You will read any emails sent regarding the raid, raiding plans, groups, event scheduling.
2. You will post any questions/clarifications that you need, either via email to Officers, or under DV Only section entitled Planes, prior to the raid.
(Do not OOC/SHOUT out questions during the raid) All issues should be cleared up prior to entering the Plane.
3. You will listen to the Guild Leader/Raid Organizer, Puller, Group Leader, in that order.
4. You will ASSIST the main assist, creating a HOTKEY to assist the designated person. (Not assisting as instructed can lead to your death, the death of guild mates, and most importantly the death of the ENCHANTER!)
5. Evacuation location will be pre-set, before entering the plane. Make sure that you evacuate to the designated location.
6. Most importantly, remember to follow all orders to the word.


Words, Runes, and Pages

1. Will be distributed to their respective classes
2. If the rune/word/page is not going to be used by recipient, then it is to be passed on to a guild mule to the vault for guild use, of those that need that item.


Sellable Items (Including Gems/Rare Gems)

1. Sellables will be given to Enchanters and Clerics to pay for costs of peridots and runes
2. Rares will go to Guild Vault for Jewelry for Guild
3. Questing Gems will be randomed to the class who can use and WILL use them for questing, if not, please do not random


Class Specific Weapons/Armor/Items

You have the right to pass on any item, opting out of a random. Choose wisely.
1. Every piece will be randomed.
2. Shields count against Class Specific Armor (if it is a shield with an AC greater than 14).
3. Once an item is awarded, until every other person in attendance in that class wins an item, one is out of the randoms.

Epic Drops

Based on NEED, if an epic quest is in progress, with the Guild Leaders approval, the item will go to the guild mate in the most NEED of an item to finish the quest, based on percentage of quest completed. One can default the
drop to another guild mate of the same class.

Only classes that can use a certain item will roll for it, if it is clearly a caster item, only casters,and melees for melees in the case of an all/all!
You roll and win, you are out till everyone else of your class wins.
You cannot roll to win an item to sell it(see top)

If you win a very nice item it is encouraged you pass down your old item to the Guild or someone there at the raid to build and strengthen within the guild, will make lots of friends this way and live a long and health life.

If no one there can use, most likely will be put in the Guild Vault,and given to someone who needs and deserves the item. Those who get these will for sure pay their old item forward to another guild member

1. Be on time.
2. Be Patient.
3. Do not talk about fight club
4. Have fun, everything is going to be ok

If late possible - will be put on random, example:
guildparty members on time will random 200 700
late member 200 600
really late 200 500

Most of all lets kick some butt and stuff like that!

Fine print

Guild leader and Officers may or may not make adjustments to this page, it is encouraged you read the rules often.
If a problem arises during a raid, they will hammer it out quickly as possible.
