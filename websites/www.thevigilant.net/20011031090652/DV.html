Dark Vigilance: Who we are

This is a summary of who we are, what we do, where we came from,
and where we are heading, using DV's own guildmember's words.
How Dark Vigilance was born- Dark Vigilance was created on March 7th, 2001. Dark Vigilance, otherwise known as DV, was a guild resulted from a merger between two smaller guilds, The Vigilant and Dark Knights of the Black Rose.

This occurred when Skybye, from The Vigilant, was a guest to a few of the Dark Knights' raids. With words of encouragement from Skybye, the two guilds joined together in hopes to tackle new obstacles, with a larger resource of people and knowledge from these people.

Who we are- We are not a guild, but a family. We are here for each other and willing to die, and go out of our ways to help each other. While doing so, we enjoy, laugh, smile, cry, and share tough and joyful times together...

We are a guild of intelligent players, who thrive on knowlege rather than loot, adventures over the monotony of Sebilis. Each and every one of us are capable and worthy of being trusted in the most trying of situation. We place trust in our fellow guildies, knowing that each one of us prides ourselves on playing our class to the fullest extent of reserch, experience, and knowlege. Questioning is accepted, where as bickering is not. All members place the utmost of trust in the guildleader's decisions, knowing they are made with clarity and in fairness. All future members are inducted with the same, if not greater, expectations.
