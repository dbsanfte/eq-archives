Fear Wrap Up
June 30, 2001

Congratulations and great job DVers! Fear was a great raid for us! Congrats to Archaeus for his Valorium Chestplate, Ssath on his Greenmist BP, bracer, and mask. Congrats are also in order to three of our members who got pants: Reidas, Taamdar for their Thorny Vine legs (sounds painful), and Talmar for his Vermiculated! Also, Reidas got TV gaunts, and a Fluxbladed Axe. Sweet stuff for that old school ranger!

Speaking of gaunts, congrats to Chansellor on receiving his. Also, three cheers for wizards -- Nick on getting some nice Carmine sleeves and Reyina on her pants! Wait, that makes four members with pants...what the heck?! So many people running without pants. Its scary.

Last, but no way in the least, a nice warm WTG to Enuch on killing the Shissar! We'll soon have two chanters with their epics. By the way, send /tells to Enuch for C2!

Great work, Dark Vigilance! Keep it up guys and gals!
Patches and Dragons
June 26, 2001

All servers will be taken down on June 27, 6 am EDT (3 am PDT). The patch will take approximately 10 hours. As soon as the servers are up, Dark Vigilance will attempt to take on Lord Nagafen if we slay the first Fire Giant. If we are able to call the dragon, we will need everyone that can help to head that way to assist with buffing, killing FGs, and finally, slaying Naggy once again.

After this, we will head to Permafrost and will see what the situation is between Storm and Vox, and will go from there.
Vox Slain
June 25, 2001

Lady Vox fell under the might of Dark Vigilance as we, and guests, went on through her lair slaughtering her minions, and finally, the Young Dragon herself. Her loot included two Runed Bolster Belts, two Staff of Forbidden Rites, a Tobrin's Mystical Eyepatch, and a Kavruul's Mystic Pouch. Congratulations to Marilyn, Kuroc, and Mileena for winning some of Vox's bounty! Good job Dark Vigilance!
