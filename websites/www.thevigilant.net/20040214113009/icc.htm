The Integrated Security Command Center (ISCC) service encompasses the design and implementation of a comprehensive information security management solution for clients who either have no current solution or who are dissatisfied with their existing system. The ISCC service has two main objectives: To optimize a client's ability to monitor and manage the security of mission-critical networks and systems, and to do so as inexpensively as possible through the use of existing information technology infrastructure.

The typical ISCC engagement involves the following phases:

  • Security Design and Requirements Review
  • Implementation
  • Production Cutover and Knowledge Transfer
  • Continuing Support
Security Design and Requirements Review
An ISCC engagement begins with an assessment of a client's resident infrastructure, particularly focusing on security and network management tools. During this phase, Vigilant works with our client to establish goals for the information security management system, as well as outlining event priorities and establishing escalation procedures. With the consolidated data serving as a baseline, Vigilant maps client requirements to a leading-edge security management design, whose salient points include the following:
  1. A comprehensive process for data collection, correlation, event handling and archiving.
  2. Customized software to automate data correlation and normalization.
  3. A solution to eliminate false positives and filter out other unimportant event data.
  4. A user-friendly, web-enabled dashboard highlighting important security-related events in real time.

Given the emphasis in recent years on reduced IT capital spending, a primary underlying focus to our design efforts is to use existing resources, whether security personnel currently in place or already deployed hardware and software. Where necessary and appropriate, Vigilant will leverage our in-depth relationships with leading security vendors to suggest products which will serve as bridging elements to optimize the functioning and integration of system elements already in place. Any products proposed for integration into the Security Management System will be tightly focused on client business goals and drivers.

A key aspect of any Design Phase is ensuring that the subsequent implementation and cutover phases are streamlined and do not result in any system downtime. As part of this preparatory process, Vigilant will perform the following tasks:

  • Review all relevant Information Security policies.
  • Review network diagrams or other necessary topological schematics or documents.
  • Interview key personnel.
  • Draft an implementation and cutover schedule.

Implementation
With the completion of the Design Phase, Vigilant begins implementation of the client-approved system. Physical implementation is typically carried out by a small group of Vigilant consultants who work in collaboration with project teams from the client's security and network organizations.
Depending on client requirements, this phase will typically include the following action items:

  • Configure existing services for integration into the ISCC.
  • Install and configure any additional security software/hardware components.
  • Perform any necessary system hardening in order to eliminate unnecessary services.
  • Implement a customized "dashboard" or GUI that will provide the client with complete, real-time visibility into security system functioning.

The goal of the Implementation Phase will be to put in place a fully operating system with the following key capabilities:

  1. The ability to process all IDS and firewall log files to identify known attack profiles
  2. The ability to correlate IDS and firewall logs to interpolate malicious behavior
  3. The ability to analyze security events
  4. The ability to perform notification of and respond to harmful security events
  5. The ability to store all logs off-site in a secure manner
  6. The ability to maintain current code revisions on various IDS devices
  7. The ability to backup configuration files of IDS devices
  8. The ability to provide security incident reports

Ultimately, the system's functionality will be directly driven by the client's requirements as defined by its security policies and needs. As a final step to the Implementation Phase, Vigilant will rigorously test the system to ensure it meets client specifications and best-of-breed industry standards.

Production Cutover and Knowledge Transfer
In this phase, Vigilant will migrate the system into active production. The Vigilant consultant team will retest the system to ensure that it is functionally operable and conforms to client specifications and security policies. The consultants will also perform knowledge transfer to appropriate client personnel who will take ownership of the system for the purposes of maintaining and administering its components.

Training will take the form of hands-on, site-specific operator training. It will be offered to the ISCC operators to ensure that all personnel are capable of performing their job functions as operators of the system. As part of the knowledge transfer process, Vigilant will develop comprehensive documentation describing all aspects of the ISCC system.

It is important to note that knowledge transfer is ongoing throughout all phases of the project. Vigilant's methodology calls for close collaboration between our consultant team and client project stakeholders and personnel. A key target for our team will be to perform minimal knowledge transfer to our client when the ISCC system comes online. Our ultimate objective is to ensure client personnel to have strong insight and understanding of the ISCC system beginning from the early stages of the project.

Continuing Support
To ensure success of ISCC implementation, Vigilant will provide continuing operational support of the ISCC and act as a trusted advisor for our client. Our consultant team will work under the direction of the client ISCC Program Manager and will be responsible for refining and maintaining the efficient operation of the ISCC system in the production environment.
