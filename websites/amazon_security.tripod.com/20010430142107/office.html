Duty of the Security Officer and Guards

Protect the Queen The main duty of the security officer and the Royal guards is to protect the Queen. If she requests our assistance then we are obligated to get to her as soon as we can. (RP duty)

Organize Rescues We also aide the tactics leader in the organization and the rescue operation of sisters or belongings from dungeons or the outside world.

Assist in Training As royal guards we are obligated to help the tactics leader train sisters in the art of combat.

Functions and Diplomacy We provide security for functions and encounters in the form of keeping roaming animals from interrupting the occasion. We also keep unwanted visitors away from meetings such as guild functions.
