The Rules of the Guild
 


I. Rules of Recruitment and Membership

1. Ritual Misery shall preserve its evil purity by only allowing completely evil
beings into its ranks. By birthright, Dark Elves, Iksar, Ogres, and Trolls are
acceptable characters to recruit, providing that they meet the remaining
standards of the guild. By trade, Necromancers and Shadowknights are also
acceptable characters to recruit, again, assuming the remaining standards
of the guild are met.

2. Half Elves, High Elves, and Wood Elves shall never be allowed to attain
membership to Ritual Misery. These races are not only disgusting pieces of
vermin, scattered about Norrath; they are also incapable of being completely
evil, no matter how many evil deeds they perform. Light Elves shall always
remain at the end of our blades and under the wrath of our magic.

3. Almost as disgusting as light elves are vile little dwarves. Dwarves shall also
never be allowed into the guild.

4. Humans are not totally incapable of being consumed by the darkness.
However, because of our past experiences with these weak minded, weak bodied,
and all around weak beings; humans will be required to achieve a special level
of evil. First, and foremost, only human Necromancers and Shadowknights
(and possibly Bards with Dark Elf illusion)will be allowed into the guild. Second,
any human Shadowknight or Necromancer seeking membership, shall first attain
the scorn of ALL non-evil cities in Norrath, including their own city. This is to ensure
loyalty to the guild and the darkness. Third, humans must increase faction with
the nobles of Neriak (murder a countless amount of Halflings), increase faction
with the...eh...nobles of Oggok (murder thousands of Lizardmen), and increase
faction with the......nobles of Grobb (murder tons of Froglok's and Fungi). Fourth,
Humans must bring the meat/parts of dead high elves, wood elves, half elves to
one of the RM officers or leaders. Fifth, any human who wants to join
Ritual Misery, must also have an existing guild member sponsor them. Beyond
the initial sponsorship, humans must fight alongside an officer of the guild to
prove their skills and loyalty to the guild, and to evil itself. Only after all these
requirements are met, will a human be allowed to reap the benefits of joining our
guild. We have yet to meet a human worthy of joining our guild.

5. Though prototypical Gnomes are quite putrid, Gnomish necromancers have
earned the respect of the elders of Ritual Misery and are acceptable characters
to recruit, assuming the remaining standards of the guild are met. Furthermore,
Gnomish necromancers with superior fighting skills, can attempt to join the
Special Task Force of the guild, known as: The Gnomish Death Squad. Final
Approval must be given by the Leader of the GDS.

6. Erudites are not ideal allies of Ritual Misery, however, they are not quite as
repulsive as humans. Erudites have secluded themselves from the light
skinned races, and with their high intelligence and general casting abilities,
the Eruds have earned a modest (very modest) amount of respect from the
elders of RM. Of course this respect only applies to the Heretics and Reavers
of Paineel. Residents of Paineel interested in joining this guild will be required to
do the following deeds before joining. First, Necromancers and Shadowknights
hailing from Paineel must attain the scorn of Erudin by murdering the paladins
in Toxxullia Forest. Second, Erudites must become killed on sight by the guards
and citizens of Freeport and Rivervale before joining. Third, Erudites must work
on their faction in either Neriak, Grobb, or Oggok. Ritual Misery has yet to meet
an Erudite completely worthy of joining the guild.

7. Barbarians, at first glance, may just seem to be large humans. However, the
elders of Ritual Misery have fought countless battles, side by side, with these
less than smart beings. Their simplistic, uncivilized ways seems to have
sheltered them from the arrogant ways that Humans, Halflings, and other Light
races have adopted. We have witnessed many Barbarians murdering the
Dwarves and Wood Elves (their mortal enemies) in the Ocean of Tears, simply
for the fun and profit of it. There have also been numerous times that Barbarians
have aided members of Ritual Misery in killing Druids, Rangers, and Treants.
We have also discovered that Barbarians have a natural bloodlust and are
inclined to be filled with hatred. All things considered, Barbarians we have
encountered have been marginally evil by nature. BUT, this is not quite evil
enough to join Ritual Misery. Barbarians that intend to join our guild must achieve
the following tasks, before joining: First, Barbarians must become Killed on
Sight in 2 of the 4 "good" cities in Antonica. One of these cities MUST be
Rivervale. Second, Barbarians must work on their faction in Neriak, Oggok,
and Grobb. Third, Barbarians must bring the meat/parts from a Wood Elf,
a High Elf, and a Half Elf to one of the elders of RM. Fourth, Barbarian Rogues
must obtain a Mask of Deception and keep the Dark Elf illusion active at
all times. In the history of Ritual Misery, only 2 Barbarians have proven
themselves evil enough to join our cause.

8. Halflings, under normal circumstances, are not allowed into the guild.
However, Halfling rogues murder their own kind in cold blood. By this aspect,
we share a common interest. There are a few deeds that must be done before
a halfling rogue will be allowed in the guild. First, Halflings must prove their
loyalty to us by becoming Killed on Sight in all the non-evil cities in Norrath.
Second Halfling Rogues must obtain a Mask of Deception, and keep the
Dark Elf Illusion active at all times.

9. All non-Dark Elf rogues in the guild must obtain a Mask of Deception and
keep the Dark Elf illusion up at all times. They must also become Killed
On Sight in all non-evil cities.

*10. All Bards in the guild must obtain a Mask of Deception and keep the
Dark Elf illusion up at all times. They must also become Killed On Sight in
all non-evil cities. Furthermore, all Bards must learn every Metallica,
Ozzy Osbourne/Black Sabbath, and Type O Negative song before being
invited (this will include playing multiple instruments at once). No bard will
be allowed to play country music at any time....that would be grounds
for instant /guildremove.

II. Rules of General Gameplay

1. A member of Ritual Misery must always help his or her guild mates.
This does not mean you have to give your last copper piece to another
member of the guild so they can get a new item. Nor does it mean you
have to take a 4 hour trip across Norrath to help somebody camp an item.
The meaning of this rule is as simple as it sounds. If somebody needs
your help, and you have the time and means to help them...do it. If a
younger member asks for your advice, take a few seconds to answer
their questions. This also means that if you are in battle with a fellow
guild member, and you can give them a tip or two on fighting
tactics, please do so.

2. A member of Ritual Misery must never turn their backs on, or criticize
another member for anything. For example, if a member you are fighting
with, fears a mob in a dungeon....instead of saying something like
"YOU IDIOT, WHERE DID YOU LEARN TO FIGHT?", explain why its
probably not a good idea to fear a mob in a dungeon. Then promptly run for
the nearest zone, gate, or camp, because you are about to die. Remember,
nobody starts up their first character and automatically knows everything...
.they must learn by trial and error and other people's advice. Criticizing
others does nothing but weaken the guild...conversely, helping others and
offering advice strengthens the guild.

3. If a member of Ritual Misery has a problem that can not be solved by
themselves, they are to talk with an officer or leader to try and come up
with a solution. For example, if a member has a problem with another player
in the guild that can not be solved between them, they should send a tell to
any of the officers and explain the situation.

4. No member of Ritual Misery will Kill Steal from other players, purposefully
train other characters with the intent of killing them, beg from people in or
outside of the guild, or ninja loot. Also spamming stupid things in a zone
and just being a general piece of crap will not be tolerated.. These acts
are inexcusable! They give the guild a bad name and will be rectified by
guild removal! Remember, members of the guild represent themselves
as well as the rest of the guild.

5. Members of Ritual Misery are encouraged (although not required) to group
with other players from the guild in their level range (whenever possible).
This is to promote closeness and friendship within the guild, and to make
guild groups for guild raids, item camps, and planes raids, the best
that they can be.

6. All members must kill Travis Two Tone at least once it their EQ life.
This is mostly because I like watching him bleed.

7. All members must understand that BLOOD MAKES THE GRASS GROW...
punishment for not understanding this is suffocation by Uhgly.
