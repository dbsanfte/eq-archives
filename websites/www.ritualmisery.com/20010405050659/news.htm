Friday, March  30th-

Client Patch - Assorted Video Glitch Fix 

March 29th, 3:00 am
------------------------------

*Client Patch*

Tonight we patched out a new EverQuest client. This client will address issues reported by those using video cards that require square textures, and who are crashing or experiencing garbled textures. 

We are working on the other problems reported. As always, please be sure you have installed DirectX 7 or higher, and have also installed the latest drivers for your video card. It is very important that the video drivers are installed AFTER DirectX.

- The EverQuest Team

 Wednesday's (3/28) Arena Guild Event 
After-Action Report (abbreviated, 2 v 2 tourney results)

Shortly after I arrived we started the 2 on 2 dueling tournament. It was awesome. 

We had 9 teams:

1. Platypie/Various 4. Razell/Vandylar 7. Ralor/Onyxe
2. Aaeil/Nofo 5. Mordris/Dafe 8. Uhgly/Ravenhooks
3. Bloodi/Myanu 6. Lotll/Visaeb 9. Mackius/Corvet

It was filled with tough battles that were very close to going either way!

Round 1

Razell/Vandylar vs. Mordris/Dafe   Winner: Razell/Vandylar

Ralor/Onyxe vs. Lotll/Visaeb  Winner: Ralor/Onyxe

Platypie/Various vs. Aaeil/Nofo  Winner: Platypie/Various

Uhgly/Ravenhooks vs. Mackius/Corvet  Winner: Uhgly/Ravenhooks

Bloodi/Myanu (bye)

Round 2

Bloodi/Myanu vs. Platypie/Various  Winner: Bloodi/Myanu

Ralor/Dafe vs. Uhgly/Ravenhooks  Winner: Ralor/Dafe

Razell/Vandylar (bye)

Round 3

Razell/Vandylar vs. Bloodi/Myanu  Winner: Razell/Vandylar

Round 4

Razell/Vandylar def. Ralor/Dafe  Winner: Razell/Vandylar

Overall Winner: Razell/Vandylar

NOTE: Dafe took the place of Onyxe (since Onyxe  had to log), starting in the second round. 

Razell and Vandylar will be defending their title at the next tournament, which will be held on the last Wednesday of the month (April)  in the Arena. Hopefully it will be bigger and better with more prizes and a bigger duel tourney with level ranges. 

Rumors of 25-44 mixes and 45+, with  the 25-44 having [a large stack of coin] for a prize. Details will develop throughout the month of April!
