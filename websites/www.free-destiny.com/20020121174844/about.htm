As a guild on the E'ci server, Free Destiny strives to simply make the EQ gaming experience more enjoyable. We are all about leveling, raids, and teamwork. We strive to help out the younger and lower level guild members, while maintaining the fun of the game. Part of the fun of Ever Quest is finding and earning items and experience. We will help you level and get items, but we will not completely twink you out and have a level 60 Cleric get you from level 1 to 30 in a week.

This simply spoken, makes the game not near as fun. Camping an item for a guildmate is very commonplace, for as each individual guild member gets stronger, the guild gets stronger. We do not discourage or encourage roleplaying, as either way of playing can be equaly enjoyable to different types of players, but we do not tollorate immature or annoying players. If you are looking to join a guild that has no reputation or no precidents, look elsewhere, because FD has a reputation of kind and good-hearted members.
