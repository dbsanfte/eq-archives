What you need to know about Plane raids

The how to guide for planar raiding

By Sunyang

 

Before I start I’d like to make one thing clear: This article will not help you lead any plane raids. Such experience can only be obtained by reading up on the multitude of sites that contain info on each plane, and by participating in many raids. This will explain on how/where to sign up, on showing up and how to behave on raids. Also you need to be at least level 46 to be able to zone into any plane.


Which planes are for you

Now we know there are 6 Planes : Air, Fear, Hate, Sky, Growth and Mischief. But which one do you need to go to?

The answer is not quite that simple. This depends on a lot of factors. For instance, if you want to join a Plane of Growth and your religion happens to be Tunare, then it would be best if you do not go. Tunare rules that plane, so you could zone in and be non KOS, but don’t attack anything or your dead and your faction gets destroyed.
Generally though you can go to any one of them and hunt (Provided you don’t fall under the Growth example).

Just for a quick reference, certain planes drop epic pieces for most classes, For more specific info on mobs, gear and quests for each plane are found here.

 

Signing up for Plane raids and possible other raids

A big question is where do you go to sign up for these things. Since I have been doing raids almost everyday for the last 3 months, I have collected a few links on where to go for signing up.

Eqvault forums

This is the only forum on the net hosted by a major site to have a raid planning board (Forget that it’s unofficial, there will never be a official one), and the most popular place for post on raids. In this board you will find post for sign ups to Plane Raids and lots of other raids (Chardok, The hole, etc). You will also find a sort of held together schedule for each plane, so you know who is running which raid on what day of the week.

Guardians of Order raid schedule page

I found this out from a friend of GoO. This is a well-designed page that works like a calendar. You need to register your character first, and then you can sign up to any raids posted on it and even post a raid yourself. Kudos to the designer!

PS: If you experience any problems trying to sign up on this page, let me know. I know how to get past the problem.

Na koa Forums

This is Na koa’s forum. They usually hold plane of growth and plane of hate raids weekly. They are one of the first guilds that where created on Eci and they have a good
reputation.

Make sure you read the first post in the thread to know exactly what the raid leader wants for info from you, and where he wants to meet and at what time.

 

Now that you signed up….

Now that you have signed up, all you can do is wait until the raid leader posts the draft groups for the raid. If you didn’t see your name on that list, don’t worry.

Note I said draft groups. The reason for that is because there will be people in those groups that will not show up! (Always happens). So if your still determined to go, just go to the meeting place for the raid at least 30 minutes before the meeting time, turn LFG on, and wait. You will most likely end up in a group. The only exception is when there are not enough clerics for the raid.

By my observation, Clerics, Enchanters, Necromancers and Wizards will always be needed. Warriors are the priority in melee with monks as pullers, but there is always a good number of each class that is needed.

A note to warriors: There is always a lot of warriors on any plane raid. We warriors run the biggest risk of not getting on. This is very normal and don’t get mad (Although never pay attention to me when I get mad about it hehe, it’s just the way I am). So just keep signing up for raids and trust me, raid leaders remember the people that always try to come and participate. You will get your chance.

In general, the higher level you are the greater the chance you will get on. Do not hold me on this, where as there are lots of exceptions to this. This doesn’t mean not to try when your 46. The more raids you go to the more seniority you get (This applies to the so called “EQ politics”, which I will not get into at all).

 

 

Grats! Your got on the raid!

So you got yourself on. Nice job!

Before you get going into the raid, if this is your first time, inquire about the entering procedures. Each zone has it’s own unique way of entering. For the most part, invis/see invis will be called for. Not much else to know other than what the raid leader will
announce.

This is when the loot order for each class is determined. Now the usual way this is done is by how many pieces each person has. The more pieces you have already, the higher in the list you are. If more than one person has the same number or no pieces at all, they will roll for position. Arguing about this will not help you if you don’t like it. This has been the system used for a long time.

If a epic piece drops in the plane you are going and you need it, It will be decided by one of 3 ways: By rolling for it, by how far you are into your epic, or if one person needs as there last piece to complete there epic, they will get first dibs.


So your raid party successfully gets in and sets up camp. The raid begins. Certain things you should know while you raid:

-Always listen to what the raid leader says. With chaos will come lots of wipeouts and lots of wasted time.

-Never say anything in /shout unless you’re the raid leader, puller, Main tank, Secondary tank, cleric for the tanks, and chanters. This is to keep communications sorted out. Just use /ooc or /say unless stated otherwise by raid leader.

-If your expecting to make lots of exp… DON’T. The main reason for going on any raid is for loot. If a group seems to get most of the kills, it’s just the way the group was formed.

-If your group gets the kill, only your group leader is allowed to loot the corpse and call loot, unless he/she died during the fight.

-Plane raids can last up to 8 hours! Be ready for the long haul.

-Most important: Have fun!

The smoke clears …

Once the raid is over any gems or blue diamonds that have dropped during the raid will be given to the raid leader who will distribute them. Casters that normally use components in the raid will get paid back in gems, Blue diamonds should normally be rolled for and If there are enough gems left, everyone that has not received any gear on the raid will get one. So everyone goes away with something.


I hope this has helped answered most of your questions and concerns about plane raids. Just please hurry up and level so you can join me! hehe
