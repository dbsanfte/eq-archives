Handy Websites
GuildMagic
Free and easy guild and character management system (it's the one we use to keep track of our roster)

Traders Corner
A great site for all your tradeskill needs. It includes recipies, trivials, ingredient locations, costs of ingredients and recipies, etc. It's kept very up to date (more so than any other we've seen for tradeskills)

Everlore
Great general purpose site.

CastersRealm
Best site around for spell lists. They've also got a few other helpful tools such as character profiles and such that you may want to use to keep track of your characters items.

Allakhazam's
Another great all purpose website.

EQ Maps EQ Atlas
Both of these sites have very nice maps that will definately come in handy.

EQ Fashion
The name says it all! If you wanna check out what that new robe or piece of armour is going to look like before you purchase it for yourself this is the place to go!
