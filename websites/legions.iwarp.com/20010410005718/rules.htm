Code of Honor

Imperial Legions
Conduct

You are expected to honor the guild:

  1. You are expected to help members in need if you are available to do so.
  2. You are expected to not attempt deliberate kill stealing of any kind.
  3. You are expected to follow orders and honor the Council and the officials of the guild.
  4. You are not to display abusive comments, pointless arguments, in the guild chat. You must resolve issues between people in tells as well as contacting a member of the Council to help resolve the issue.
  5. The use of guildsay, message boards and web page is strongly encouraged, to find out events, news and current happenings in the guild.

  6. Continued infringements of the conduct will result in a guild remove.

  7. If you have any questions or concerns please contact the members of the Council or any other officer present:
    Council members: Revenx, Auricus, Pyen, Andruim, Gamadan, Berkana.
    Officers: Kaihne, Galeon, Palanthanas, Takeda, Towser

What should you do?

*Attend all events*  Attendance is mandatory, please try to attend all events which had been scheduled on the message board or on this web site. Please inform us if you cannot make scheduled events due to RL issues.

*Inform the council of any disruption in the guild internal operations

*Just be yourself, and have fun!

Loot system

The loot system will be pre-determined depending on the type of event. ALL PLANAR events will be randomed between classes who can use the item and people who have not yet acquired an item in a particular event.

Loot for none planar events will be determined by the Council and the vote from the members. Upon an established loot system everyone is expected to follow it!

if any arguments arise from an established loot system the Council will make the final decision based on the facts.

Recruiting and Electing


First, all officers must vote on that person (all officers must have agreed) then we will give that peson one week trail. If he/she fails then a guild remove will be issued. The person must be of level 40+ rank.


ALL APPLICATIONS TO JOIN MUST BE POSTED ON THE MESSAGE BOARD!

Electing new officers and Council members will be done by votes from officers and selected members.


***Most importantly try to have fun and play a good game!***
