Spell drops in different zones
 

NPCs can have individually set regen rates, for both a peace time, and when they are mad at someone. For instance, it's not unusual to see an NPC that will regen at 50 points per tick while idle, but only 1 point per tick while in combat. Another NPC might regen 200 per tick regardless of the state of the NPC.

It's one of the things that we have the ability to set in order to adjust the difficulty of an encounter.

In other words, I'd recommend that you try another NPC for your test.

"Abashi, archery unlike melee skills does
*not* have a 2* weapon damage bonus."

Though the formula that you are using may or may not be correct, there certainly is a bonus that applies to ranged weapons, but it's not as large as that for melee weapons. This was part of what was needed to balance archery.
