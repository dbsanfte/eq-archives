Reality Checksum is the weekly column of Mike Talon. Currently it is syndicated to several websites, and available to the general public via a Yahoo! Group.

The name is derrived from a networking term - checksum - where a mathematical formula is applied to packets of data before they are sent across the network. At the other side, another formula is applied that should yield the same results as the original. If both formulas provide the same result, the data is considered to have been sent and recieved uncorrupted.

In much the same way Reality Checksum looks at situations where technology collieds with reality, and attempts to determine if the technology in question made it across uncorrupted, or if it simply doesn't make sense in the real world.
