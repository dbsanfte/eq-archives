The Officers of the Norrathian Federation of Honor:

Guild Leaders:

Talonheart of Clan Darkfyre
Talonheart is the leader of Clan Darkfyre, which currently consists of himself, Talonhymn and Talonbane, though others may be brought within the clan at any time. Though generally to be found in the Greater Faydark region, he often travels with the guild to places throughout Norrath and her moon.


Vashtoril of Clan Wynemaker
Vashtoril is the leader of Clan Wynemaker which currently consists of herself, Vashtor, Sonjata and Vashtoral, though others me be brought within the clan at any time. This clan has homes far and wide throughout Norrath, and it's exploits are spoken of in all the lands of the realm.
