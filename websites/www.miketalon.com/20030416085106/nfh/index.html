Welcome to the home page of the Norrathian Federation of Honor (FoH).

We are a group of Everquest Players who play on the Zeboxuruk Server and have identified ourselves as "Good Aligned" in the characters we create.

If you were directed here to learn more about the Guild, please take a look at the info and charter pages, which contain the necessary information you will need to become a member of the FoH. You may also wish to take a look at our Officers and Members pages so you can find out who you will be playing with online.

You will also want to head over to our Message Board at http://pub77.ezboard.com/bfederationofhonor77214 to find out about joining.
