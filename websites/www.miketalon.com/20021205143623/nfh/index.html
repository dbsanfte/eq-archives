In memory of Real Life Heroes. . .

The Federation of Honor would like to take a moment to recognize and remember those lost on 9-11-2001 and those who so bravely battled the fires and falling debris. We will never forget your sacrifice. To all the families and friends of the lost, our deepest condolences. To all the volunteers, Firefighters, Police Officers and others who helped put the country back together again, thank you, from the bottom of our hearts.

Welcome to the home page of the Norrathian Federation of Honor (FoH).

We are a group of Everquest Players who play on the Zeboxuruk Server and have identified ourselves as "Good Aligned" in the characters we create.

If you were directed here to learn more about the Guild, please take a look at the info and charter pages, which contain the necessary information you will need to become a member of the FoH. You may also wish to take a look at our Officers and Members pages so you can find out who you will be playing with online.

You will also want to head over to our Message Board at http://pub48.ezboard.com/bfederationofhonor to find out about joining.

Current Members will also want to check out http://groups.yahoo.com/groups/nfhguild for the Guild Calendar and other information.
