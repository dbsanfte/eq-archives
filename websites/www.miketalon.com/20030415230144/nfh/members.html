The Members of the Federation of Honor

Note: this page will change OFTEN! We're a growing guild =)



Ahluria
Allie is one of the Founders and Officers of the FoH. She can often be found trying (REALLY trying) to quad kite something.


Clan Raith
The members of Clan Raith include Ritah, Rihat, Hatir and Tahir, among others. Spirit Brothers of Clan Raith include Hierog and friends. And of course we can't forget Heirog's constant companion, Mayli Jade. Rihat is also an officer, and one of the Founders of the FoH.


Nize
Nize helped to start the FoH, and brings a certain musical flair to the group as our most advanced Bard. His sister Rhoe also makes an appearance from time to time.
