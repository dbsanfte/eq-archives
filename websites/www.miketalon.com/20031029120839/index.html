4-1-9 Scam Alert
Just a heads-up, the scammers are at it again. Yet another wave of 4-1-9 (or "Nigerian Money Laundering" scam) e-mails have been making their way across the net. Don't be taken in, NEVER give money to anyone promising anything too good to be true!
