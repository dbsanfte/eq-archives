Norrathian Federation of Honor (NFH)

Norrathian Federation of Honor (NFH)

Guild Charter

 

Section I: Statement of definition and document purpose

 

The Norrathian Federation of Honor (hereafter NFH) is a player guild in the game of Everquest (a property of Sony Online Entertainment) and is comprised of players of Everquest on the Zebuxoruk server.  This document will serve as the founding guidelines and rules and regulations for the NFH which all members of the guild agree to be bound to.  In the event of disputes between members and/or between the NFH and other guilds, this document will serve as the foundation for mediation for said disputes where said disputes are over operations and/or procedures of the guild.

 

This document will contain information on selection criteria for new guild members, policies for guild operations, and procedures for nominating and electing guild officials – as well as definitions of the roles of said officials.

 

 

Section II: Statement of Guild Purpose

 

As Everquest is a game based on player community and interaction, the founders of the NFH have created a player-run guild to foster this community spirit and cooperation among its members.  The NFH is a guild on the Zebuxoruk server consisting of good-aligned characters who prefer to group together in the furtherance of their characters.  Membership is open to all characters level 12 and higher after said character has grouped with other NFH members on several occasions.  Good-aligned characters can be of any race/class combination provided their characters seek justice, honor and valor on the field of battle.  In this way a “dark” character (e.g. Necromancer) can indeed be good-aligned if said character is devoted to the assistance of others, the pursuit of justice and the furtherance of the guild.  All members must agree to work together with other guild members in a constructive manner, abide by all “fair play” rules of the game (as described in detail below) and generally assist all players if possible.

 

The NFH will not participate, instigate, accept or otherwise participate in guild wars.

 

The Guild is designed for adults – not for reasons of graphic language or inappropriate behavior (see Etiquette section below) but so that Real Life players will have more in common with each other.  We suggest that the minimum age to join the guild be set at 18 years old (RL).

 

Section III: Definition of Guild Roles

 

 Leaders:  There will be at all times two Guild Leaders (GL’s) who will be responsible for the administration of the guild and the maintenance of this document.  GL’s will be elected on an annual basis by such means as defined in the attachment to this document labeled Appendix A: Procedures for Guild Elections.

 

GL’s responsibilities include, but are not limited to the following:

 

1 – GL’s will be part of a Tribunal called as an appeal of last resort for a guild dispute (see section on guild disputes below).

 

2 – GL’s will keep and maintain this document, including all amendments and changes that will be applied. 

 

3 – GL’s will assume all roles of the Guild Officer (see below) in addition to the above duties.

 

 

Officers: The guild will at all times hold a minimum of six (6) and a maximum of ten (10) Guild Officers (GO’s) who will be responsible for the administration and day-to-day operations of the guild.  GL’s will be elected on an annual basis by such means as defined in the attachment to this document labeled Appendix A: Procedures for Guild Elections.

 

GO’s responsibilities include, but are not limited to the following:

 

1 – GO’s are arbiters of guild disputes that cannot be immediately resolved by other methods (see sections below on guild disputes).  The decision of a GO can only be overruled by the decision of a Tribunal (see section below on guild disputes).

 

2 – GO’s are responsible for evaluation of prospective new guild members, and for inviting those prospects who are to be invited to join.

 

3 – GO’s will distribute Messages of the Day (MOTD) as needed to alert members to upcoming events and vital information.

 

4 – GO’s are responsible for organizing NFH functions.

 

5 – GO’s are responsible for issuing warnings and, if necessary, removing people from the Guild.

 

Ambassadors: The guild will at all times hold a minimum of one (1) and a maximum of three (3) Guild Ambassadors.  Ambassadors will be appointed by GL’s on an annual basis.  An Ambassador must be either a GO or GL.   The sole role of this position (in addition to whatever duties they hold as GO or GL) is to establish and maintain relations with other guilds and with the Verant/Sony staff and their appointees in the game.

 

 

Section IV: Membership

 

Subsection IV.I – requirements for prospective members

 

In order to become a member of the NFH, a player must meet several requirements:

 

1 – The character in question must have reached the 12th level (9th level for alternate characters of existing members).  This is to ensure the character is well created and that the player has the intention of continuing to play this character.  Alternate characters of existing members must meet only this requirement for membership.

 

2 – The player must be willing to play a minimum of 4 hours a week as part of guild activities and groups with any combination of their guild-member characters.

 

3 – The player must group with current guild members a minimum of four times in order for NFH members to get to know the prospect in a game-world environment.

 

4 – After grouping the required number of times, the prospect can be nominated for membership by any NFH member he/she has grouped with in the past.  Nominations will be accepted by Officers of the guild and put to a vote (as per Appendix A: Procedures for Guild Elections).

 

5 – Once the vote is closed the prospect will be notified as to if they have or have not been selected for invitation into the group.

 

Subsection IV.II: Responsibilities of Members

 

All guild members must spend a minimum of 4 hours per week in guild-related activities.  This includes but is not limited to:

 

-          Meetings of the Guild

-          Grouping with other NFH members

-          Assisting NFH members in creation of new characters

-          Other online activities with NFH members

 

If a member fails to perform this responsibility for a significant period of time without reason they will be warned by an Officer. If they continue to refuse to participate they will be removed from the guild.

 

All guild members must follow the policies and procedures in this document and agree to be bound by the rules and regulations herein.  Failure to comply will result in a warning by an Officer.  Continual or repetitive breaking of the rules or refusal to follow procedure will result in the member being removed from the guild.

 

Section V: Fair Play

 

As Verant and Sony Online have been very liberal and vague in their definitions of Fair Play Rules, the NFH has decided to outline policies that our members will follow.  The general policies are as follows:

 

Loot:

 

If a member of a party or group requires a certain type or piece of loot, they should state so at the beginning of the group.  Other members of the group will honor this request and either not loot the item (if it is no-drop) or give the item looted to the requestor.  No member of the NFH shall use this specific request policy to obtain items they do not require.  This is intended to allow for a friendly looting policy the all can abide by. 

 

If two or more members of a party all require the same loot (e.g. spell components) then each will receive said loot in turn, based on alphabetical order.   If the item in question is a rare drop, a “roll” will be held from 1 to 100 with the highest number winning.  A tie will result in a re-roll of only the tied participants.

 

Coin is always autosplit.  If a party member does not wish to receive coin, they may distribute their share of the autosplit as they see fit.  The single exception will be if a member of the party cannot carry coin (i.e. member is a Monk) and therefore some other methodology should be used to loot coin – suggested methodology is alphabetical looting in turn.

 

As a rule, if another member of your party can use an item you should offer it to them before selling it.  This just makes the game more enjoyable for all.

 

Camping (holding a spawn site):

 

If you are currently holding a camp at or above your level you should feel entitled to keep that camp as long as you wish.  You may, at your discretion, give up the camp to another player or group who asks for it, however it is your camp to do with as you please.

 

If you are holding a camp that is below your level (for coin/loot generation for example) then  you should give up that camp to the first party that asks for it and is at the appropriate level for that camp.  You can – and should – refuse to give up the camp if another party attempts to “steal” the camp simply because you are “too high” to hold it.  The NFH frowns on camp stealers, as evidenced below.

 

You may not, under any circumstances, steal a camp.  Camp stealing is defined as taking MOB’s that spawn from a room or clearly defined area (such as a bandit/dervish camp or tent area) when another group or party is currently attacking creatures in that area.

 

You may ask to share the camp, ask if they campers require help, or even ask to join their group.  If you are refused, you may NOT, under ANY circumstances, take MOB’s from that area while you are a member of the NFH.

 

In the same vein, you may not attack any creature that another player is fighting (when  you are not grouping with that person, that is) unless they have asked for help.  You may ask if they need assistance, and this is encouraged.  You may heal/buff the person fighting the MOB, but you may not attack the MOB for any reason unless the original attacker requests help.  The sole exception to this rule is if you are aggroed while not attacking the creature, you may take measures to protect your own life.

 

Etiquette:

 

Though Everquest is Role Playing game, each member of the NFH realizes that there is a real-life person sitting behind the toon.  Therefore Guild members will be respectful of other players in the game, and act accordingly.  You may feel free to place a personality around your toons, but this does not give you free rein to ruin the playing experience of other players.

 

In addition the Guild will be open to all who meet the above requirements for membership.  We will not discriminate based on sex, creed, nationality, religion, sexual orientation, ethnicity or any other factor.  We do suggest a minimum age requirement, however if a prospective member is well liked by those Guild members he or she groups with, this requirement is flexible. 

 

In this spirit the following behaviors are prohibited while playing as a NFH member:

 

-          Inappropriate language of a graphic and/or sexual nature in open channels or in private messages when the recipient has expressed their desire not to be exposed to said language.

-          Excessive use of foul language on open channels

-          Racist, stereotypical, biased or otherwise hurtful comments, slurs and speech in any chat channel

-          Any and all activities and actions described by Verant or Sony Online as inappropriate

 

Violation of these Fair Play rules will result in a warning, any additional violation will result in immediate removal from the Guild.

 

Twinking:

 

Twinking, or the giving of items and coin to lower level toons by higher level toons is both acceptable and encouraged in the Guild within reason.  The following two suggestions are strongly encouraged among Guild members:

 

1 – Twink inside the Guild first.  Try to give loot either to your own lower level toons or to other toons of Guild members before twinking  strangers.

 

2 – Each toon should reach the 14th level untwinked for the most part.  This is not to slow down leveling, but instead to ensure that each toon fully increases their skill sets so that they can be effective as part of a group.  A level 25 toon who’s offense skill is still at level 10 is not very good to anyone.

 

Power Leveling:

 

Also accepted and encouraged within the Guild is Power Leveling, or a very high level toon protecting a lower level toon so they can level up faster.  Again we strongly suggest you only Power Level other Guild members.  As a matter of Guild Policy, you may not accept nor require any payment for Power Leveling another Guild member.  You are not required to assist them in this manner, and should only do so out of a spirit of altruism, not financial gain.  You may accept payment from non-Guild members for this service, but it is polite to offer the service to other members free of charge first.  This is especially true if a member is attempting to Power Level one of their alts for acceptance into the Guild.

 

Profiteering:

 

No member of the Guild shall charge any other member of the Guild for services.  The sole exception to this rule is where a service requires an expendable component (i.e. stones for spells) in which case the person providing the service should be compensated (if requested) for whatever component is used.  You may not profit from this exchange but you can be reimbursed for whatever the component in question will cost to replace.

 

This means that you may not charge or accept fees for Power Leveling, porting, healing/buffing, summoning items, etc. from or for other Guild members.

 

You may not turn down a reasonable request from another guild member for these services. Use your best judgment, but for example if your group is in LOIO and you are asked for a port to Butcher, you can reasonably turn down the request since leaving a group one evacuator short in LOIO could have deadly results. If, however, you are in Freeport – give the guy a port.

 

As to non-Guild members, feel free to charge whatever you like and/or the market will sustain.  We all have marketable skills and should use them to make plat, just not at the expense of our fellow Guild members.

 

The reverse holds true as well. If you see that the player in question is in the middle of something profitable, be polite and wait until they are finished with paying customers before you ask for your freebie.

 

Section VI – Guild-wide Operations:

 

Certain procedures will need to be established in order to allow the guild to run smoothly.  These include, but are not limited to, the following:

 

Mules:

 

Mules are lower level toons that serve a single purpose.  They exist in towns and near banks to hold and/or exchange items for guild members.  Mules are used – for example – to allow for players to move items from one toon to another.  They can also be useful as ATM’s to swap out small coin for plat.  Guild Officers will be creating Mules in most cities, however all players are encouraged to ask around and see if any city is lacking and create their own Mules as well.  A suggestion is to create Mules that have spells like Invisible and/or Camouflage whenever possible so the Mule doesn’t get destroyed just outside the city gates.

 

Anonymity:

 

We realize that as an RPG, Everquest allows people to be completely anonymous in the game world.  However a Guild requires that some information be disseminated outside the game world as well.  Therefore each NFH member must provide an e-mail address for Guild communications.  This can be a free service like Yahoo.com mail if the member in question does not wish to use their real e-mail address.  No other personal information will be required from a guild member at any time beyond the names of those toons they wish to have listed in the Guild.

 

Section VII – Dispute Mitigation:

 

We realize that disputes may arise during normal play that cannot be settled between the parties in question. In those cases the following procedures shall be called into play:

 

1 – Any Guild Officer may be called upon via a tell or e-mail to make a judgment call.  We ask that participants in the dispute be ready to accept the decision of the Officer.  If for some reason this cannot be accomplished, then:

 

2 – A Tribunal of three Officers (one of which must be a Guild Leader, and none of whom may be the original Officer who made a decision in this matter) will be called to handle the dispute.  The decision of the Tribunal is final, and refusal to abide by this decision will result in removal from the Guild.  The Tribunal will be called via e-mail and each participant will have their chance to state their case before a judgment is made.  Tribunal Judgments are made public, and a two-thirds majority will determine the winner of the dispute.  Please keep in mind that a Tribunal should not be called except in the most extreme of circumstances.  Tribunals called simply because you don’t like the Officer’s decision will almost definitely result in that Officer’s decision being made final unless you have conclusive proof of impropriety. 

 

In the event that a member has violated any part of the spirit or letter of this document, a warning will be issued by a Guild Officer.  If continued infractions take place a Tribunal will be called to determine if that member should be removed from the Guild.  The member in question will have the opportunity to state their side of the story, and the decision of the Tribunal will be considered final.

 

 

Section VIII – Changes to the Charter:

 

The Guild Charter is a living document and will undergo revision and changes over time.  These changes may only be made by a Guild Leader after a vote has been called (see Appendix A) and the change has been approved by a simple majority of the membership.

 

Any member can call for a revision.  Such a call for revision must be made via e-mail to a Guild Officer, who will convene with other Officers to determine if a vote should be held.  The decision of the Officers is final, and therefore no less than three Officers shall evaluate the potential revision before it is decided if it should be voted upon.  The potential revision shall be made public prior to the Officer’s decision, so that the general membership may discuss the potential revision. 

 

 

 

 

Appendix A:

Guild Election/Voting Procedures:

 

Whenever a vote is required by the membership at large, the NFH Guild Yahoo Group’s polling facility will be engaged to handle the vote.  This system allows for time limitations on voting, anonymous voting and handles all of the minutia of the polling process.

 

All voting will be by open vote (meaning no one is required to vote) and will be by secret ballot.  The ongoing results (percentages) and final outcome would be publicly available to all Guild members.
