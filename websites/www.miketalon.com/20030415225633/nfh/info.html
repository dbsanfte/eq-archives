The FoH is a "Good Aligned" guild that plays on the Zeboxuruk server in the mythical world of EverQuest.

About our characters
The members of the FoH have main toons generally in the 30-45 level range, with alts anywhere from level 1 to level 25 and higher. In real life, we span all professions and education levels, and for the most part are adults in the working world.

While not specifically a role-playing guild, many of our members are avid role-players. To role-play or not is your choice, but be aware that we do require you to have a real-life e-mail address (hotmail at the least) so that you can keep in touch with the group.

We are not a raiding group. Our members play for fun and entertainment. We do participate in raids, but generally do not arrange them. This may change as our group grows.

Joining the FoH
In order to join the FoH, your toon must be at least level 14 and have fought with our members before. After that, and Officer can give you a probationary guild tag for 30 days. At the end of the 30 days the Officers will vote on your permanent entrance into the guild. SO, go find someone with a guild tag and group with them, and we'll take it from there.
