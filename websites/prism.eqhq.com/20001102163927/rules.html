Membership into the Order of the Prism

All races and professions are welcome to join the ranks of the Prism. As long as you are of one mind with the Order and wishing to aid and help protect all the people of the great lands of Norrath, then you are one step closer to being recruited.

No person that is wishing to join shall be shunned due to race, sex, or profession. Yes, even necromancers, and rogues have a place here in the Order. Our belief is simple...

"All citizens have to right to choose their own path and follow it"

We must all work together to keep our world safe. And sometimes even the Goods and Evils must work together to stop a greater threat to us all. There will be times where one side will protest the other, such is the way of things. But that is where the Council of the Prism will be called upon to hear these disputes and then make their ruling.

To be recruited into the Prism one only need contact one of the Profession Commanders on the Council and petition them for membership into the Order. Please send with it your Profession, your current Level and a brief story of yourself. Also include a time when it will be best for the Master to meet with you in person to discuss your entrance into the Order.

Your level in your profession makes no difference in the Order. Even a citizen of 1st Level may be recruited and work his way through the ranks to become one of the Orders finest members. Your Commander will then submit your name at the next recruitment meeting, which usually occur ever 30 days and he will then give them a brief summation of your meeting. You will then be brought before the Council and asked some questions as to your intentions while a member of the Order. You will then be voted upon by the Council members as to your recruitment.

Upon acceptance into the Order, you will be given the rank of 3rd Order member and your Commander will be your sponsor from this point on. Remember, your actions will be a reflection upon your sponsor as well as yourself, so do your Commander proud and be loyal to him or her, as well as the Order itself. You will also be a representative of the Order while you are adventuring abroad, and we expect you to live up to the standards of our Charter
