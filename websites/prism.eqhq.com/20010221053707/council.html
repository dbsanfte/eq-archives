The Council of the Prism

The Council of the Prism is made up of a member of each Profession of Norrath. In this way each class will have a representative to voice their concerns and or ideas that will be benefical to the Order and to the citizen of Norrath in general.

The Guild Leader is the elected Leader of the Order of the Prism, his or hers purpose is to remain neutral in any discussion or dispute and to keep order during such events. The Leaders powers within the Order are the direct result of the ruling of the Council. Each Profession Commander is considered the Officers of the Guild.

Any persons wishing to contact the Class Commander for any questions, please touch their name and a scroll will be sent to them.
