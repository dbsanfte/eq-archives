The Charter of the Order of the Prism

Overview

The Order of the Prism is an adventuring/fellowship Guild, made up of all the various races of the great lands of Norrath. No person shall be deemed unfit to be a member of the Prism because of race, sex, or profession.

The Orders main goals are as follows:

The protection of the citizens of Norrath, from enemies both foreign and domestic.

To provide aid and information to the adventuring citizens of Norrath.

To provide a place of Friendship and Harmony for all the races of Norrath.

"All different colors focusing into one powerful light. All members working together to be one greater power"

Tazort Trozat

The Charter of the Order of the Prism

Article 1: General Laws of the Prism

Sec 1:All members will make every possible attempt to aid any citizen whom is in need of defense due to attack by any creature or person. But no member may interfere with any citizen whom has accepted any duel, or like style situation.

Sec 2:No member will interfere in personal combat between individuals who are not a part of the Order. Members may attempt to quell such disturbances, by acting as a mediator, but if combat begins the Member must refrain from involving themselves in the conflict unless they are personally attacked and must defend themselves. Any Member found guilty of such crimes will be subject to Fines and/or Retribution Quest.

Sec 3:No member of the Order shall be aparty to or aid any conflict that is racial or religious in nature. The Order is about bringing harmony to ‘all’ the races and classes, not tearing them usunder. The Prism will act as a mediator/peacekeepers in such conflicts. Members may only attack in defense of themselves or other inncocent parties. Any member found guilty of such a crime will be expelled from the Order.

Sec 4:No member may initiate an attack upon another citizen of Norrath because of insults or any manner derogatory to a Member’s beliefs. All citizen’s have a right to their opinions, and the Order respects that belief. A Member may only attack to defend themselves from harm. Members found guilty of such crimes will be subject to Retribution Quest.

Sec 5:No Member is allowed to involve themselves in Ksing (Kill Stealing) for personal gain. If a member is involved in aiding a group of citizens who are being harassed by a more powerful group and/or person that is Ksing, then is shall be deemed acceptable. Any Member found guilty of such crimes will be subject to severe Fines.

KSing defined: KSing is defined as a person who is willfully and with malice killing any creature that another group or person is currently attacking and without the consent of the group or person.

Sec 6:No Member will take any item from a kill that is not theirs, or is not an agreed upon loot by members of their party. Loot Stealing is a most serious crime within this guild, much like KSing. Any member that is found guilty of such a crime will face harsh punishment and or expulsion from the guild.

Sec 7:All Members will try to the best of their abilities and knowledge to provide any information that a citizen may require, if asked. But the Member may not divulge any information that may be of a sensitive nature, such as Quest information or any information that may go against their racial or professional beliefs. It is the belief of the Order to help any person that asks, as long as it does not infringe upon their own personal belifs or their profession.

Sec 8:Members may have a maximum of (2)two characters within the guild. But only one will be listed on the roster. This is so that our numbers will not be inflated by members with multiple characters.

Sec 9:Members with multiple character may have a character in another guild. But, if the Prism should happen to war with the guild that a member has a character in, it is warned. Should it be found out that the prism member with a member in the opposing guild was giving information to the other guild. 'Both' Prism members characters will be removed from the guild.

Sec 10:Members may not recieve aid from higher lvl members to camp valuable items, if the member would not be able to gain the item with a group of people of the same level as the member. (i.e A 10th lvl character wishing to get JBoots).

If a members is of the proper lvl to camp these items and wishes aid from other members, then this is acceptable. But the member 'must' be present and aiding in the combat for such items.

Sec 11:The Prism will not tolerate the twinking of its members. Twinking is defined as giving items of High Levels(Oracle Robe, Mith Armor, etc...) to members of a level that could not aquire such an item even if grouped with like leveled members. And/or completely outfitting such a member with like items.

The guild has no problem with presenting a member with a 'gift' of one or two items, but to completely outfit a member is forbidden.

If a member cannot be an active and useful addition to a party to gain such an item, then other members will not be used to gain those members such an item.

It 'is' permissible to completely outfit a members second character (The guild allows members to have a max of 2 characters in the guild). This is due to the fact that the members other, higher lvl character, will have paid for or earned these items in good faith.
