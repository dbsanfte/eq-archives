Welcome to the Order of the Prism

An official Guild of Hosted by

Welcome to the Order of the Prism, we serve the people of the Innoruk Server, we are honored by your interested in our guild. We are one of the few guilds that seeks to bring all the beings of Norrath together for the worthy cause of bringing harmony to the various races and professions of the land. As well as assisting our fellow citizens with the paths that they have chosen, and protecting the lands from all forms of enemies that seek to destroy us.

Our guild will help you in many ways. By aiding you in your adventuring by grouping you with like leveled members, so that you can advance through your profession. To sending you on various quests to help promote teamwork.

The Council of the Prism is also on hand to help you in disputes that you may have with other members, or even those not of the Order. The council will do their best to help you with your questions or problems. Though some questions cannot be answered due to some guild guidelines about revealing professional secrets, they can send you in the right direction to aid you in your quest. Besides..One cannot advance if everything is given to you on a silver platter.

Please visit our guild as often as you wish, you and all your friends are always welcome.

    Nov 19th 1999

    Guild Meeting and Changes

    As everyone knows for the last 2 weeks, we had scheduled a open guild meeting. I sent out emails to all that I had current records off, if you did not recieve one, that is due to you not informing me that you had one, or it had changed. This is your responsibility. Out of the 71 members I had on record...only 13 showed. As you might expect, we were all clearly disappointed by this. Many members on my list I found out at the meeting were simply gone or had moved on to other guilds. This is unacceptable.

    With this revelation, the time has come to make drastic changes to the guild. The first being that the members that showed up will now be known as the Core Group of the guild. Also, I will be making changes to the ranking system, plus the Council itself.

    During the meeting, I read this current roster of members to the core group, and we all voted on keeping or staying the members on it. From the 71 I had, we have whittled it down to 28 members. This will be the new beginning of our guild. I will be removing the members that were voted to remove. If you are one that has been removed and you wish to stay with the guild. You must send me an email stating why you were not able to attend the guild meeting. I will pass this on to the new council and then we will schedule a meeting to discuss your remaining with us. If you do not wish to stay in the guild, simply target yourself and then type /guildremove this will remove you from the guild. I and rest of us truly hope you understand that this must be done, and that there is no hard feeling.
