Welcome to the Order of the Prism

An official Guild of Hosted by

Welcome to the Order of the Prism, we serve the people of the Innoruk Server, we are honored by your interested in our guild. We are one of the few guilds that seeks to bring all the beings of Norrath together for the worthy cause of bringing harmony to the various races and professions of the land. As well as assisting our fellow citizens with the paths that they have chosen, and protecting the lands from all forms of enemies that seek to destroy us.

Our guild will help you in many ways. By aiding you in your adventuring by grouping you with like leveled members, so that you can advance through your profession. To sending you on various quests to help promote teamwork.

The Council of the Prism is also on hand to help you in disputes that you may have with other members, or even those not of the Order. The council will do their best to help you with your questions or problems. Though some questions cannot be answered due to some guild guidelines about revealing professional secrets, they can send you in the right direction to aid you in your quest. Besides..One cannot advance if everything is given to you on a silver platter.

Please visit our guild as often as you wish, you and all your friends are always welcome.
