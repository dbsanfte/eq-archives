Oct 30th 1999

Invasion of Neriak

I am truly pleased by the turn out of our members in an impromtu invasion of Neriak and equally pleased at the outcome of the invasion. While we did not venture any further than the 1st Gate, I was impressed by how well we held that position.

After the initial battles with the guards, which we all quickly dispatched, we held the gate with a minimal loss to our troops.

I was proud to watch our guildmates holding their own along with several of our ex-guildmembers that joined along for the adventure.

Special commendations go out to Darkomen whom at a great threat to his own life, ventured deep into the city to retieve the body of our fallen guildmate Silph whom had been charmed and lured deep within. Darkomen was successful in finding and dragging her body from the city and was rezzed by our Grand Cleric Ainu. May we all learn from Darkomen's example of bravery and honor.

Oct 29th 1999

Meeting and Announcements

Last night a General Meeting was held. Though this meeting was held on short notice, we had approx. 16 members attend. While I was a little disappointed by this, it is understandable due to the short notice. But much was accomplished and I feel this was a positive meeting.

As everyone may or may not know, we have lost several members in the Guild. Trickyone, Sanarie and Buffe quit due to 'personal reasons'. They will be sorely missed but the guild will go on. We still have a strong core group of members and we will grow stronger over the coming days.

The problems have been sited as lack of fellowship and harmony within the guild. Plus a lack of organization. There is some truth to this and it was discussed at the meeting. The following announcements are in effect immediatily:

  • Dilvish has been promoted to the position of Prism Ambassador, he has the same authority as myself and Tazort should we not be on. It was agreed that we must have a person of authority on at all times and since myself and Tazort are usually only on during the night, we needed a person to fill in during the day and early evening. Dilvish is the perfect person for the job and I know he will serve the guild with honor.
  • Rainor has been promoted to High Ranger. Congratulations to him, he will be the perfect member for this.

    I will be looking over the Class Leaders in the guild and making some changes to them as well. I will be looking for Class leaders who have a strong presense during the game and can take charge of their various classes.
