Code of Conduct
The following describes the Code of Conduct of the Triune Alliance.

The number one rule is to have fun and enjoy this great land we call, Norrath, in the fellowship of our family. Members of Triune Alliance will be ranked in the highest regard when in battle and times of rest. Triune Members will achieve this goal by having systems in place to protect our good name and reputation. These Codes are subject to change as the High Council and Officers of Triune Alliance see a need to make a change.

General Rules

  1. At no time shall the Code of Conduct be used against the Guild Leader or the Triune Alliance in attempt to undermine the guild as a whole by a single individual or group of individuals.

  2. All members regardless of rank will not verbally degrade, abuse, or intimidate any other member of the guild.

  3. All members regardless of rank will not ridicule or belittle any other member's ideas. Disagreements will always occur, but lack of respect for another's ideas and attacks in an attempt to "win" an argument is not acceptable and will not be tolerated.

  4. No member will be overtly crude or sexual. If a person is feeling discomfort it is their responsibility to calmly tell the offender. If it continues, the person feeling discomfort should /tell an officer to resolve this issue.

  5. All members regardless of rank will refrain from use of overtly foul or crude language even in jest (Hell, Damn, OMG, Shit do not fall into this category of being overly crude).

  6. All members will always uphold the highest standard of integrity. A person will never intentionally rip-off, cheat, or steal a person's possessions through game bugs, lies, misdirection, or to loot a corpse without intent of returning the items. Intentional Kill Stealing (KSing) and Training for personal gain are strictly forbidden.

  7. Members shall be respectful of each other's time. Never shall one member place pressure upon another to do them a service (i.e. ports, resurrections, etc...). No profession should be made to feel as though they cater to another profession's needs. All members are expected to make a reasonable attempt to pull themselves out of a problem before calling upon the guild. Members shall not inconvenience their peers to selfishly save themselves time. (i.e. expecting a port from a fellow member that is involved in any sort of activity). Requests for ports should be made via /tell to the one who can perform this action, rather than spamming guild chat. At no time shall the requestor pressure or intimidate the porter if the request is refused.

Decency

  1. All members will refrain from Spamming on any forum whether it is the guild, group, OOC, auction, tells, shout, message boards or normal talk.

  2. All members will refrain from "guilt trip" tactics in any guild medium in an effort to acquire group, quests, personal gain, etc.

  3. All members will refrain from having arguments with another on any forum.

  4. All members will refrain from overly talking about OOC issues in guild chat. Real life issues occur, but one should not monopolize guild mediums to overly discuss them.

  5. All members are expected to support another's ideals within reason.

  6. At no time shall any guild medium (guild chat, group chat, OOC, tells, shouts, message boards or normal talk) be used for personal attacks on another member.

Hunting Parties & Looting

  1. All members shall adhere to the looting rules established by the group. No Triune Alliance member shall ever display open greed while grouped with other family members or non-guild members.

  2. A hunting party consists of two or more groups.

  3. When rolling for loot, while in a hunting party, all members shall consider the needs of those present before rolling for loot.

  4. All group members will consider the time other group members have put into a camp before rolling for loot. (I.E. If you join a group that has been camping an item for two hours, you should give rolling preference to the veteran group member)

  5. Items won while in a hunting party become the personal property of the individual who won the roll.

  6. All members should refrain from looting while grouped in combat. Triune Alliance shall not display open greed while group members are in danger, or at any other time.

  7. Triune Alliance does not condone the abuse of game bugs to hunt safely. Exploits of this nature can not only get an individual banned, but also a guild if it is universally executed. (i.e. killing mobs where you can not be harmed or any manner of personal gain that extends past game design).

  8. Triune Alliance should refrain from the obvious looting of other people's kills (We will not be known as scavengers).

  9. Triune Alliance members shall never intentionally Kill Steal another character's mobs or camp.

  10. Members shall refrain from activities and open verbal fights that result in tainting the guild's image. At no time shall one member place their individual pride over the pride of the guild. Members are expected to avoid involving the guild in petty or personal issues.

  11. Triune Alliance members will not intentionally train another individual or group of individuals, whether it be for revenge or personal gain.

General Trade & Wealth

  1. Triune Alliance has no problem with personal wealth, but being that we are a family, we ask all members to help out within reason by donating unneeded items to the guild for distribution to other members in need. This transfer of wealth makes us overall stronger as a unit. It is not mandantory, but is appreciated.

  2. Members that choose to donate to the guild and/or Triune Alliance have the right to 1. Give items directly to members of their choosing or 2. Place items up for lotto on the boards or 3. Set up a lotto on the guild net or 4. Ask a ranking member if there is a person in particular that needs such an item or 5. Simply give to a ranking member to distribute. When a member determines that they shall donate an item to the guild and has chosen a method, they must follow through with their choice.

  3. Items passed down to members from other members or from the guild bank belong to the guild inasmuch that when said member no longer has a use for the item that said item will be returned to the guild bank for redistribution. Items received as gifts or withdrawn from the guild bank may not, at any time, be sold for personal profit.

  4. Triune Alliance funds are in place to help the overall guild. Members should refrain from putting a strain on the guild's wealth by constant or extreme requests. The guild's funds are not to be used for personal loans.

  5. Members shall not solicit help from the guild in attempt to gain general loot items over other members i.e. begging for help in gaining items without allowing other family members to roll.

  6. Members that choose to sell quest gems to people outside of the guild give up the right to draw gems from the guild's bank. There are few exceptions in such cases where people donate excessive amounts of gems then occasionally sell off acquired gems for personal funds.

Looting & Rolling on Raids

  1. Triune Alliance operates on a "need before greed" policy. Only those who can use an item shall roll for an item during raids, unless it has been announced as an open roll.

  2. The needs of main characters shall be considered before alternate characters on all rolling for loot during raids. However, if it is the alternate character who is participating in the raid, he/she may roll for useable items. (It is encouraged to consider the needs of those main characters present before doing so)

  3. Members shall at no time persuade other members to not roll on loot items regardless of intent.

  4. Members that roll on loot may always step down and not claim the item. At no time shall a member ever win a lotto then pass such item down to anyone but the next highest roll.

  5. Members that win an item on a raid are expected to not conceal the fact when multiple items drop to be lotto'd. It is everyone's responsibility to let the guild know if they have previously won an item before rolling or looting a second. On a raid, once an item has been won on a roll, one may not roll for another item unless it has been placed up for open roll.

  6. Items won on raids are the joint property of both the individual and the guild inasmuch that those items shall never be sold outside of the guild except for the obtaining of guild funds or an item that strengthens the guild. Said trades shall only occur with the prior knowledge and consent of the guild leader and banker.

  7. Never shall there be tolerated general greed where members openly claim to deserve items over other family members whether on the boards or guild chat regardless of profession and intent.

  8. Though it is acceptable to recruit help to acquire epic pieces, the epic list shall be followed when at all possible.

  9. On guild raids, loot will be distributed based on the overall needs of the guild. The guild's officers and guild leader shall come upon the best choice on whether select items shall be awarded directly to individuals or generally lotto'd. Membership within the Triune Alliance is an acceptance of this method of loot distribution and trust with the leadership.

Triune Alliance Policy on Alternate Characters

Triune Alliance understands the enjoyment of sometimes playing an alternate character. Alternate characters are as much a part of our family as main characters are. However, we do have a few policies regarding alternate characters.

For those members who are still within their 30 day probationary time, alternate characters who meet the level 40 minimum will be eligible for an invite, as well as one (1) alternate character under level 40. To have this type of alternate character invited, you simply need to post on the forum "New Members" board. Your post should clearly state who your main character is, the name, level and class of your eligible alt and that you are seeking membership for an alternate character. (Alternate characters are considered an extension of your main character and are not subject to the 30 day probationary period)

After the initial probationary period (30 days) is completed, you may request that other alts under level 40 receive an invite into the guild. To do this, simply post on the New Members forum stating who your main character is and then listing the alternate characters you would like invited. Please list their name, class and level.

It needs to be stated that main characters will always remain our number one concern. Triune Alliance will always see to the needs of our main characters before alternate characters. However, we also very much enjoy the diversity that alternate characters bring to the guild. Just know that we do not plan raids and go out of our way to equip alternate characters. We have to think of the overall best interests of the guild by making sure our main characters are equipped and ready to face any challenges we may encounter.
