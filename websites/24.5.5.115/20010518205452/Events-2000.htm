Our Events in the year 2000

On Saturday, December 30th, we began gathering our forces and making our way towards the City of Mist. Cilia was the first to arrive and she claimed the city in the name of the UNC. As the other groups arrived we noticed an eerie silence within the city. No other adventurers were present besides our raiding party. The silence was soon broken by the sound of fighting as a small group moved forward to battle the few monsters in the vicinity. Once everyone was properly prepared, we moved out, carefully avoiding a nasty fall off the main bridge. Fighting was constant but smooth as one after another the Plaguebone Skeletons, Fog Golems, Haze Golems, Shadows, Phantoms, and Gyrating Goos all succumbed to our attack. Valksis pulled a steady stream of monsters to keep us busy and then led us forward after each battle. After one such pull, the city erupted with more creatures than any of us had ever seen before! Things were coming down the ramps and across the paths at us on all sides. It seemed almost impossible to keep track of and small skirmishes sprang up as each of our groups was assaulted. Once the dust had settled, we saw that some of our comrades had fallen and we mourned their loss briefly until they could be resurrected. After tending to our injured, and regaining our breath, we moved ahead once more. Again, the fighting went very well and there were no casualties to speak of. Amazingly we arrived at our destination, right in front of the room which held the Black Reaver. (Take a look at our party!) As we determined our next course of action, Anavrin and Valksis moved forward to check the room for inhabitants. Altho we thought we had the key from the Skeleton Captain that would unlock the door, it did not work on the lock after all so Anavrin picked the lock instead, allowing Valksis to see into the room. Once the main group was ready, Anavrin picked the lock and opened the door, keeping it open while Valksis cast Harmony on the creatures inside. Keeping our fingers crossed Valksis then shot an arrow at the Black Reaver while the other creatures stood silently by! A successful Harmony! While Taluil chain cast Rune on the tanks, Cilia kept their health levels up as Chienvoxx passed his mana off to both of them. One Black Reaver fell and from its lifeless corpse another sprang up in its place. We redoubled our efforts and finally that Black Reaver too fell beneath our onslaught. This time, from the body rose Lord Ghiosk, an undead ghost of an Iksar. The battle seemed excruciatingly long but in the end we triumphed and Jalanea was awarded, from the body of the slain Lord, several books for her Shaman epic quest. After such a triumph, clean up of the rest of the rooms creatures was easy and we moved forward to set up camp in the room. After a brief rest we moved around the corner of the room into a narrow hallway where we encountered a small elevator of sorts. Wondering if the elevator could be lockpicked, Anavrin tried out her skill and successfully started its ascension. Unfortunately, Valksis and Bridgecrusher were standing on the elevator at the time and they were in for an eyeful once the elevator reached the top! Goos and reavers of all sorts awaited them. The elevator began to descend but it was too late, the monsters were angry and they set upon Valksis and Bridgecrusher while below another group of monsters began attacking the main group. Pandemonium would be the best way to describe the next few moments as evacs were called for and many members ran for their lives. Anavrin rushed to the front to open the locked door to let as many people as possible out and they all fled for the exit. Mindert was left inside the locked room pretending to be dead while Faedor and Talann were in separate places and most definitely dead. anavrin went back in for corpse retrieval and was able to let Mindert out and recover the bodies of her lost comrades. Thus ended the City of Mist raid!


Saturday morning, December 16th, present and future members of UNC gathered together to raid Velious. Noffin and Etha came in from the Coldain city of Thurgadin, Nolina ran in from Firiona Vie, Faedor and Bridgecrusher traveled from the deserts of Ro, and Chienvoxx left the Spirocs of Timorous Deep to join us. Torth stopped by to wish us well; he was too busy and short on time to stay, but it was nice to see him again. Talann sent his regards and hinted at a rare treasure he'd found that might interest a certain rogue; but the gods have barred him from Velious until such time as he buys the expansion. We were going to explore Velious today, but the other people we were expecting didn't show up, so we instead decided to explore just one place, the Tower of Frozen Shadow. Nolina, Noffin and I traveled with bard song through the hazards of the Iceclad dragon circle. Upon porting in, we were set upon by two snow dervishes and a frost giant scout. We did unto them before they did unto us, and headed to the gnome pirate camp to buy some new songs for our bard. Noffin gave me a spell he'd 'found' in the spell vendor's pack, and once memorized we were off! The Tower of Frozen Shadow thrummed a funereal beat as we came to the spires and spikes of the black monolith. The shadow guardians rattled menacingly as we neared, but Nolina's lilting voice kept them calm and unaware. We came to the portal and were swept inside. The trollish warrior Bridgecrusher greeted us as we left the mirror. Chienvoxx, his sunken blue cheeks and red-rimmed eyes marking him as a master of the dead, nodded at us as we arrived. The wizard Faedor, resplendent in his deep crimson robe, formally invited us to join him. Healer, warrior, and thief; wizard, necromancer and bard. A perfectly balanced group at the beginning of an adventure that took us all back to our roots, back to the reason we play in the first place. We set off to the echoes of rolling dice to the mirror that would transport us to the second floor. The brightness of the bottom floor gave way to the still and quiet of the second floor library. Around us, the souls of students and scholars studied, unaware of the demise of their bodies eons ago. A ghostly librarian slowly turned the pages of a moth-eaten book. She saw us and screamed; we saw her, and that was the last of her. We worked our way down the shelves until we came to the mirror portal that would lead us to the third floor. Faedor held the key to the mirror, and magic swept us into the servant's quarters for this towering tomb. Undead servitors of every description plagued us as we searched for the mirror that would bring us further up. Bridgecrusher finally found it, and we gathered before it in anticipation and dread. None of us had ever been where we were about to go. Faedor held the key to the next floor to the mirror...We were dropped into a labyrinth of tile and stone. We'd found the crypt, where the sleeping dead are protected by creatures of shadow and puddles of ooze. Noffin went ahead to search for traps and to open locked doors, while Bridgecrusher mapped our path through this maze. We found several dead who had not yet forgotten the names they'd had in life. One of them wore a shield of shadow, which Noffin relieved him of, another had a shadow necklace that now adorns Chienvoxx's neck. Noffin found an illusory floor (by stepping into it),while showing us where it was, he fell in again! We followed him in to see what we could see; monsters prowled the pits. Another necromancer prowled the crypt, and let us have his spare key to the fifth floor. We thanked him, and then Faedor again presented us to the mirror; and we were at the end of a long hall. A wedding ceremony was attempting to be held at the other end, but a mage was doing his best to prevent an unholy union from ever taking place. Undead ushers came by to show us to eternal seats, but we begged off and that was the end of them. We had to leave, but we vowed to come again. Many floors remain! We had a blast! A balanced group in a dungeon that encourages the classic dungeon crawl. Good loot (besides the two rare magical items, we got many fading shadow weapons and some gnomeskin armor, as well as the usual level 35-40 monster loot. Fading shadow weapons are better than both fine steel and velium weapons, though they don't have a proc). High adventure. A developing storyline told in bits and pieces by disembodied voices. We all agreed; this was the most fun we'd had in far too long!

On Saturday, October 21st, members from the allied guilds of the UNC, LoF, and RNA met at the ramp leading into the Barbarian city of Halas and began their preparations to meet and kill the blue dragon known as Lady Vox. Once most people arrived, the group moved to the Permafrost gates, casually pulling Ice Giants, Mammoths and wandering Orcs to keep things interesting. The groups were already determined ahead of time and after a few last minute changes by Toran everyone split up to find their designated partners. There were seven groups of people on hand for this exciting battle. Toran elected Sweetspot and Valksis to be the pullers and so began the march into Permafrost. The groups easily slaughtered the Ice Goblins, then started in on the Ice Giants and the Giant Wooly Spiders. Time and again the Giants could be heard summoning different members of the groups but the Allies were too strong and the Giants fell almost as fast as they were pulled. The last few monsters to be pulled before the Allies made it to Vox were a few Ice Giants, a couple Icy Terrors, which are a blue variety of Tentacle Terrors, and the Priest of Najena, an evil looking Fire Giant! Again however, these creatures were truly no match for the invaders. Toran began instructing everyone to begin debuffing themselves and to have a few "junk buffs" cast on themselves first, then the more important buffs like Resist Magic and Resist Cold were cast because, as was explained to everyone, Lady Vox will dispell magical buffs if given a chance. While waiting for the groups to get ready, Sweetspot accidentally stepped too close to Vox's lair and was banished from the dungeon because she was too experienced for this type of battle. And then, the time was at hand. Wave 1, consisting of groups 1-3 were called in to charge the dragon, then, moments later, Waves 2 and 3 took up the fight. Immediately Lady Vox began letting off her Dragon Roar which sent fear into some of the clerics who promptly fled for their lives. Without the clerics, the tanks were having a rough time. Vox was slashing at them with her huge claws and blasting everyone with her Freezing Winds. The fighters one by one began to fall but Vox was down to 25% health when she began to cast a spell. Lady Vox suddenly was feeling much better and boy was she angry! With the warriors out of the way she turned her icy glare upon the casters who stood no chance against her. The dragon raid was over in less than a minute. The next 5 hours was spent in corpse retrievals and for a lucky few who remembered to purchase coffins, their corpses were summoned right out from underneath Vox's nose. The rest held their breath as Valksis, Legalis and Ramsar ran in under an invisibility spell to pull the dead bodies out of Vox's lair. Several times they were not so lucky and Vox was seen chewing on their corpses. Twice the lady dragon came right out of her lair and began chomping on the unsuspecting adventurer's, making them scatter for safety. Jalanea, Cilia, and Soulblaze experienced some very odd problems with link deaths and required a GM to come stop their incessant naked running back and forth thru the icy tunnels of Permafrost, but in the end, everyone was finally retrieved. The group has however already decided to try a rematch with Lady Vox in the very near future!

On Monday, April 10th, in the year 2000 (Earthtime) we raided Infected Paw!! This underground dungeon is located in South Karana near the aviak city and has been over run by a band of Split Ear gnolls. We began, as we usually do, slightly disorganized but in great spirits! Gathering outside of Paw we welcomed in a new member, Seethax, and broke up into groups. Mustering our courage and casting probably every buff in the book, we proceeded into the depths of Paw. The first group easily took out the gnolls near the entrance and everyone moved into the alcove, settling in for a long night of fighting. After several rounds of easy pulls there came a train of gnolls so huge that legends should have been written about it! At least twenty gnolls, many of them casters, converged upon our Guild and quickly dispatched several of our members, including Taluil and Etha. One of our groups evacuated to North Karana, the others ran for their lives towards the entrance, where the gnolls decended upon not only us but another hapless band of adventurers, killing more people. Regrouping back at the entrance of Paw we learned that we were not responsible for the train and so, we pushed forward again into the darkness of the dungeon. This time, we carefully made our way to the alcove and began to slowly clear a path towards a deeper and more dangerous spot in Paw. Two groups of us, which included Guild and non-Guild members alike, finally made our way to the 'Paw Stones' meeting up with Gnoll Judges and Gnoll Executioners who have an odd habit of killing one of their own who refuses to give up his eye. Close by we encountered a Tesch Val gnoll who reluctantly gave up his weapon, the Kicsh Der Pavz, which Anavrin won in a random roll. Etha gives us this first hand account of the rest of the raid...

"We'd set up base in front of the massive iron gates of the Splitpaw prison

and were hunting the wicked creatures imprisoned therein, when the

remaining members of the raid (me, Grokx, Stratis, Gallorn, Faedor,

Quimbius and Bridgecrusher) were attacked by two high level Rosch Mas

Gnolls (gnoll necros) and their pets.

An evac was almost immediately called, but the gnolls ignored the warriors

to go straight for the casters. Stratis died nearly at once, while I was

overcome with fear many times. In the end, Quimbius gated out while Faedor

managed to evac himself and Grokx to safety. Bridgecrusher, Gallorn and I

died helplessly far beneath the verdant plains of South Karana. The

After that, Grokx and I went to Beholder to find what we could find while

the others retired to brightly lit homes with no dogs nearby to jangle

their nerves"

Altho the raid began slowly at first, and some of the rare items we had hoped to find were unobtainable, the Guild did manage to bring out valuable items  such as the Kicsh Der Pavz, Split Paw Hide Belts, Split Paw Hide Gloves, Split Paw Hide Masks, Split Paw Tooth Necklaces, a Gnoll Hide Lariat and more Preserved Split Paw Eyes and Split Paws then we know what to do with! One day we will go back and finish the job!

Altho no prizes were awarded and our attempts to round up the Guild to have our own Guild battle fell through, the batles held in the Arena were certainly some of our finest, if not funniest moments.

On Monday February 7 in the year 2000 (Earthtime) an impromptu raid on the fortress known as Najena, located close to the Dark Elf city of Neriak, was attempted. Two groups made their way into the depths of this well guarded dungeon, encountering elementals of every sort, necromancers, magicians, an enormous ogre guard named Moosh, and even Bonecracker, the notorious undead froglok. Rushing farther into Najena, we fought our way towards our goal! Upon breaking into the room, we were mobbed by casters and their pets on all sides! Suddenly, an undead knight spawned, nearly bringing several of us to our knees! After much healing on our part, we were finally able to defeat this first round of creatures. Needing a much deserved rest, Etha sat down to meditate, when out of nowhere Drelzna, a powerful necromancer attacked and brutally killed her! We avenged Etha's death several times that night, but were unable to obtain the treasures we were looking for. Needless to say our second attempt at a Guild raid was not quite as successful as our first!

Our first event as a Guild was held on Sunday February 6 in the year 2000 (Earthtime). Several of us stormed the Goblin citadel known as Runny Eye located near the Halfling city of Rivervale. For several hours, we battled fiercely against The Goblin King, The Goblin Captain, The Goblin Warlord and what seemed like hundreds of Goblin elite guards, Goblin guards, Goblin shamans and Goblin workers. Victorious right to the very end, we lost not one of our members to the evil Goblin King or his men. Several times the powerful Evil Eye named Borxx attacked, however even it was not strong enough to defeat us. We brought out from the depths of the citadel blackened iron armor, blackened iron medallions, a grotesque mask, a sleek black cape and great handfuls of money. Plus, the most important treasures were the Evil Eye lenses taken right from Borxx, which enabled some of our members to complete a quest in which they were awarded with the powerful Staff of the Observer. All in all it was a very very prosperous night and one that will not soon be forgotten!
