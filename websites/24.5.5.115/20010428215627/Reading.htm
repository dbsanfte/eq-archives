Interesting Reading...

Upon this page you will find various diaries, journals, scrolls and other writings that have been retrieved from the wilds of Norrath.

This first book entitled 'Venril Sathir of the Kunzar' was found in the Emerald Jungle in Kunark. Thank you Jalanea for giving us these pictures! (We later learned this book disappears over night!)

Here is a note dropped by Yiz Pon in West Karana. He's not seen very often and is always on the run, trying to escape a Gnoll Assassin!

Within Infected Paw, several scrolls are dropped explaining the History of the Verishe'Mal. Here are three of those scrolls plus a tanned split paw hide scroll.

Each of us is given a choice...Order or Discord? Not all of us paid attention when we were given this choice but here we can read about it!

An Orc Chief in Lesser Faydark drops this book entitled 'The Chronicles of Wu Volume 3'.

These next few books can be bought in East Cabilis from the Court Chronicler. They are all Lore items and cannot be resold to a merchant. They also disappear over night.

Genesis of Fist and Tail

Before Green

Talon Southpaw

Captain of the Sea

The Crusade of 1312 A.G.

Drelzna in Najena used to have Journeyman's Boots on her. Word reached a sneaky Rogue named Hasten Bootstrutter and he came and took them from her. This is the note he left in their place.

In the Skyfire Mountains, you can occasionally find this no drop book, called the Book of the Immortals, off a Faerie of Dismay. It is apparently for a Monk quest, perhaps their epic one?

All of us at one time or another have picked up Velishoul Tome pages or Tasarin Grimoire pages, and this book titled, The End of an Age, explains why the pages were scattered to the winds.

In the City of Mist, several different notes are dropped. Bits and pieces of clues about where all the people may have gone.
