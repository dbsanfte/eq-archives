Loathsomes and more...

(by Etha Tanglewood)


The officials of Qeynos finally cleared out the corruption outside Qeynos (no
more loathsome individuals, no more Defiled Grizzly) and captured the priest of
Bertoxxulous responsible.

Various NPCs got up and testified against him in the trial (including fan fave
Holly Windstalker), but toward the end, an army of zombies invaded and in the
confusion managed to free their master who gated away...


Front bottom: Antonius Bayle, the King of Qeynos and the Plains of Karana.
Sat as judge. During the trial, comments in the crowd ranged from "what will
the King do?" to "what would Judge Judy do?"

From left:

Mordavin Telase, the evil priest of Bertoxxulous. Twirled his moustaches a lot,
and tore off his fingernails as he listened to the testimony against him.

Guard Montgomery, the guard. He chose the jury and controlled
troublemakers in the crowd. When he pointed at a heckler, the heckler
vanished. Don't anger the guards of Qeynos.

Vegalys Keldrane, Chief Magistrate of Qeynos. The person who finally realized
that Qeynos was being invaded by stealth and disease. He played the attorney
for the state.

Rykonis Sw'Vaye, a local smuggler and arms runner, it was he who was
contracted by Mordavin to bring supplies to the temple beneath the city from
across Norrath. He was responsible for the bandit runners who dropped the
accursed paint needed to decorate the new shrine with which to call up the
Avatar of Bertoxxulous. He turned state's evidence in this matter and was
cursed by Mordavin.

Tristani the Terpsichore, Bard and court reporter. He's the one dressed up like
Spiderman.

Al'Kabor, legendary wizard and expert witness, testified about the Tomes of
Greater Magics and the runes that could be made in them. Haughty and
arrogant.

Protector Holly Windstalker, tireless killer of newbies, gave testimony about
the poisoning of Surefall and the killing of Cros Treewind. Most wanted to see
her dead; comments like "Why Cros and not her?" were common. She rushed
Mordavin a couple of times and was just barely restrained by the others. At one
point, for some reason, she wondered aloud if her outfit made her look fat...

Earron Huntlan, owner of the Lion's Mane Inn, much discussed in the Harpy's
Head Tavern BBS. After he gave his testimony, he became very drunk and
occasionally stumbled into the proceedings or shouted nonsense in slurred
words. Apparently his wife won't sleep in the same room with him and his
daughter avoids him. Someone called out for an intervention.

Velarte Selire, priest of Rodcet Nife (can just barely see his name). I don't
remember what he was there for... testifying about someone stealing scrolls.
