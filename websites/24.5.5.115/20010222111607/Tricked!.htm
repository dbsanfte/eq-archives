As Valksis and I were saying our farewells and beginning to prepare to retire for the evening, a cold wind began to blow through the treetops of Kelethin. Feeling the sudden chill in the air, I moved beneath a merchant's awning just as the rain began to pour down from above. From somewhere in the distance, a sorrowful voice began calling out to anyone who would listen. Tallena Kieras was seeking assistance and her pleas did not fall on deaf ears. Valksis and I split up in order to cover a larger expanse. He ran to Felwithe to search the area and I looked below Kelethin to see if Tallena could be found there. Valksis soon found Tallena walking a path from Felwithe to Butcherblock Mountains and I ran to intercept them and lend a helping hand. 

     Their progress along the path was slow but this enabled her to recount her sad tale. By the time she reached the Mountain area, a small crowd had gathered to listen and to give aid. She explained how, not too many months before, she had been wed to a wonderful man by the name of Falthin Vess. Soon after their wedding however Falthin's attitude towards her changed. She had seen the contempt in his eyes when he looked at her and his cruelty began surfacing until finally he had walked away from her and their marriage forbidding her to follow. Tallena was beside herself with grief. What, if anything, had she done to provoke this reaction in her beloved husband? She begged us to go find him and learn what secret he held in his heart. She asked only that we not mention her name, nor provoke him in any way. She knew he had gone into the Mountains and into the Cauldron beyond so our little group went in after him.

    We found Falthin not far along the path in Dagnor's Cauldon. He seemed slightly annoyed at our intrusion of, as he put it, "..our meditation.." altho he was obviously alone on the path. We tried speaking of trivial things yet nothing we said seemed to interest him. He glared icily at Valksis a few times and try as we might we could not figure out what secret he had nor why he acted so oddly. I took a seat nearby and wondered to myself about the large sword he was carrying and also spoke to Valksis about whether or not Falthin could be possessed and in need of a good dispelling. After several minutes of getting no where with this strange High Elf, Tallena arrived much to our dismay and began to plead with her husband for his return. 

    Falthin looked at Tallena coldly and warned her to stay away from him. "But I love you!" she cried. As I began to stand up and get closer to the quarreling couple, Falthin suddenly lashed out at Tallena and within a few swift blows had slain the poor woman. The crowd gasped, then started attacking Falthin. I was too stunned to move but Valksis had waded in and already done a considerable amount of damage...it was too late to turn back now. Falthin was attacking people at random and within a few more swings had killed one of the group members. I jumped in and began attacking, completely unsure as to whether this was the best thing to do. Moments later, Falthin lay dead at our feet and I had struck the killing blow. We mourned the loss of Tallena and Falthin and still wondered how this had ended so tragically. As if from thin air, Vaelian Vess appeared before us and asked us what had transpired. As the others began to retell the events, I leaned over and retrieved a Dark Reaver and an Essence Emerald  from Falthin's lifeless corpse.

        Vaelian listened intently and I moved back...way back and noticed that these two men were...brothers? After hearing out the story, Vaelian asked if Falthin had anything on his body. I cringed and wondered if I could hide. Valksis told me to fess up and so in a meek voice I said "Yes, he did." Vaelain replied, "We would like it back. It was very dear to us." There was that 'US' again. Vaelian turned and looked at me, then bowed. With an unsteady hand I gave over the Essence Emerald in the hopes that he would revive both Falthin and Tallena and explain the bizarre behavior. In return he handed me a silver black sapphire necklace.

    Suddenly before us stood Falthin...but not the Falthin we knew! Falthin  grinned evilly at Vaelian. Vaelian turned to him and said, "Welcome brother. We are glad to see you well again." I had a strong feeling this wasn't going to turn out as I had hoped. Falthin replied, "Thank you brother." Vaelian continued, "And our plan? It went well?" Falthin grinned and nodded, "Quite well." This was not good! Vaelian then turned to us and said, "Thank you for helping us to forever destroy the soul of the innocent Tallena." as Falthin laughed cruelly at his 'wife's' corpse. Falthin then turned to us and laughed in our faces saying "So nice to have the help of the fools of light. Goodbye 'friends'!" With that, they were gone...they simply vanished. We were tricked!! Tricked by the powers of Darkness! But to what end? What good did it do these two men to hurt and destroy such an innocent soul? Why did they pick Tallena? Where had they gone?? The questions piled up yet there were no answers available nor any real clues. The few who remained wandered heavy hearted back to our homes and I vowed not to be taken in by seemingly kind Dark Elves again!

~Anavrin Stormfyre
