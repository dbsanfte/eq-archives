Soulblaze and Synew, the closest of friends, had traveled far and wide thru the land of Norrath looking for a Guild they could one day consider home. Disillusioned by some, they resigned themselves to fighting side by side with good friends, but apart from any Guild.

Far across the land, the Stormfyre family was growing by leaps and bounds. They also dreamed of a Guild worthy enough to join...one that would be their second family.

When these two groups of people met and combined their forces, they became

The United Norrath Coalition

And so begins the story of one Guilds adventures thru Norrath...

Our motto:

Debeo amicitia; nos an unus!

Bound by friendship; we are one!

Our Charter:

UNC is a Guild banded together by friendship. The members of the Guild are dedicated to assisting each other thru the difficult times that can plague our lives in Norrath. We have united together under one name to do battle against the villainous minions that roam the continents and, best of all, we have vowed to have fun together while doing it!
