-Definition of a raid / raid rules-

  1. 12 people must attend a minor raid.  18 people must attend a major raid. No less. If less than the minimum required people are there, no points will be awarded or paid.
  2. A raid MUST be posted on the board. Otherwise, you have people picking favorites. Everyone must be given a chance to sign up
  3. Raids must be coordinated through the Raid Coordinator.
  4. Any number of Raids will be allowed per week.
  5. All raids must be approved by the Raid Coordinator.
    1.  Major raids- ALL Alter Planes, Dragons, Zone Boss's (Levels 45-60)
    2.  Minor raids- (Levels 25-44)
    3. Sub Raids - (Levels 15-24) Do not earn points. Loot from these raids will be handed out at the discretion of the Raid Coordinator/Raid Captain.
  6. Number of people per raid - 
    1. Minor raids - Min 12, Max is TBD per raid       
    2. Major Raids - Min 18, Max is TBD per raid

-Earning Points-

  1. Minor raids - can earn raiders a possible 5 points (4 hours will automatically grant full raid points or until 3/4 of the UNC Raiders have left the raid). 1 point if you are there on time and staying at least one hour, 2 point if you stay after the break or join after the break and stay for one hour, and 2 point if you stay for raid duration....from break to finish.
  2. Major raids - can earn a possible 10 points (6 hours will automatically grant full raid points or until 3/4 of the UNC Raiders have left the raid). This is because of the length they take to complete. 2 pt for being on time and staying at least two hours, 4 point to stay after break or if you join after break and stay for 2 hours, and 4 pts if you stay from break to closure. If you cannot stay from break in to closure, you still may earn a bonus by at least being there 50% of the time or greater. This bonus will give you 2 points, rather than the 4 points you could earn if you stay from break to closure.
  3. NOTES - On raids that are 10 plus hours and such, you are going to have people going LD, or people who have to grab a quick lunch. It is silly to say that if you have to run to the store for 20 minutes, yet you are there the other 10 plus hours, that you wont get the full 10 points. This being so, here are the buffers that were decided on:
            a. On Time point buffer- There is none. Either you are on time or you are not. If you are late and we still have a spot for you, you can receive other points during the raid.
            b. Attending the raid late buffer- You of course may join us after we break the zone if we have a spot, BUT, must be with us a minimum of an hour to get your points.
            c. Bonus point buffer from being there start to finish- You MUST be in on the break to start. You may leave the raid no MORE than 30 min before it is completed, and DURING the raid, you can NOT be gone for more than 1 hour total time.
    These times will be documented. We compare all of our information before we post the final point totals    .

* Points can only be earned if you participate in raids suitable for your level.

** Points can NOT be transferred between members or between characters.


These rules must be adhered too. People being late, going LD for extended periods of time, leaving for extended periods of time disrupts the tempo of a raid and the overall length of a raid.

-How points are spent-

Points are spent in two ways:

 Class armors / Non-god items

  1. Can be claimed with 15 or 10 points. If no users wish to claim with 15 points, then it is offered at 10 points. If no users wish to claim a no-drop item at this point, the item is randomed between the users for no points. If the item is droppable, non-users are now allowed to claim for 15 points, then 10 points. If no one claims at this point, the item is randomed between everyone in the zone. Highest point totals get priority on items they wish to claim.
  2.  Example, Cleric Legs drop in hate. Cleric A (who has 50 points) passes on the item for 15 points. Cleric B (Who has 49 points) also passes on the item at 15 points. Cleric C, D, and E already have legs. Now Cleric A can either 1) pass on the item again at 10 points, or 2) Claim the item for 10 points. Cleric A passes on claiming the item for 10 points. At this point Cleric B now has the same two options. If he too passes, Cleric A and B random the item.
  3. Each person gets one claim per raid per tier (assuming you have the appropriate number of points) until all other users claim an item per tier OR pass on the particular item in question. Example: Cleric A has claimed a 15 point item and a 10 point item. Cleric Boots drop, and Cleric B, C, D, E all pass on the items at both 15 points and 10 points. At this point, Cleric A can claim for 10 points or allow it to go to random.

God / Dragon / Zone Boss Loot

  1. Can be claimed with either 20 or 15 points. If no users wish to claim with 20 points, then it is offered at 15 points. If no users wish to claim a no-drop item at this point, the item is randomed between the users for no points. If the item is droppable, non-users are now allowed to claim the item for 20 points, then 15 points. If no one claims it at this point, the droppable item will be taken for our Guild Treasury. If this is an unwanted non-droppable item it will be offered up to the zone. Highest point totals get priority to claim on items they wish to claim.
  2. Example -- Cazic Thule drops a soulleach, SK A does not wish to claim for 20 points (he has 80 points total), SK B does not wish to claim for 20 points either (He has 60 points). Now, SK A has a chance to claim for 15 points. IF he passes, SK B has the same opportunity. If they both pass, since it is NO DROP, they random. This is not counted as their one GOD LOOT.
  3. Each person will get one god loot CLAIM until all the other classes that can reasonably use that item have a god/dragon loot.
  4. God loot claims will not count as against your 1 claim per raid.
  5. People who already have their God Claim may claim another god item assuming that all of the other players wishing to claim have <20 points. Example: Cleric A, who has 60 points picks up Donal's Breastplate for 20 points.  Later in the same raid Donal's Legs are dropped.  Clerics B, C and D have 15 points each.  Cleric A can now claim the leggings for 20 points.  If Cleric A passes on the leggings then Clerics B, C and D have priority in claiming the leggings for 15 points, because Cleric A already has a god loot item. If Clerics B, C and D pass on the leggings then Cleric A may claim them for 15 points. *(yes we know it is impossible to get both the breastplate and the leggings in one raid)

Open issues/Q & A:

  1. Q: What if I don't have enough points to claim an item?     A: If you do not have enough points to claim an item, you cannot keep someone from claiming their xth item of the raid. For example, Wizard A has 60 points, Carmine Sleeves drop and he wishes to claim. Normally, this is Wizard A's one claim on the raid until any other wizards on the raid get one claim as well. In this case a Carmine Robe drops after the sleeves, the only other Wizard on the raid only has 7 points. Wizard B cannot stop Wizard A from claiming at 15 points, but could stop him from claiming if it went to 10 points.
  2. Q: When do I get my points?     A: You will not be given points for the current raid until after the raid is completed. Therefore, if it is your first raid with us, you will not have the opportunity to CLAIM anything. You will however be allowed to random on anything that you can use that goes to random.
  3. Q: What if the item is droppable but I don't wish to claim it?     A: If it goes to random, and no one wishes to claim the item, it will be taken for our Guild Treasury.
  4. Q: Is this loot policy perfect?     A: Probably not. Please provide us with feedback on our message board or speak to us in game with your suggestions. We are always looking to improve the process.
  5. Q: What do I do with the Jewel Craft and money items?     A: The Raid Coordinator, Raid Captain or some other officer will be collecting these items. Jewel Craft Gems (Jancith, Diamond, Black Sapphire) will be given to people that have received nothing at the end of the raid. There may or may not be enough for all those who have received nothing.
  6. Q: What happens with Plane of Sky items?     A: Plane of sky quest items will go through the same process as regular items with the exception that a 1 point claim option will be implemented. If all players that need the item for a quest pass at 10 points and 5 points, the player will then follow the same process with a 1 point claim. Each classes player will be allowed to claim one item at 1 point AND one item at 5 or 10 points per raid.
  7. Q: What if I don't know the stats on an item?     A: Read up on any items that have a chance to drop that you can use and/or PoS quest items used in your quests. We will not waste time reading the information off over and over again during raids.
  8. Q: What if I signed up for a raid and when I get there I don't know who my group leader is?    A: We will not spend time announcing the groups unless major changes occur.  Before the raid begins you must look at the message board.  Once at the raid it is your responsibility to locate your group leader.  The Raid Coordinator or Raid Captain will have already spent a considerable amount of time planning and organizing the raid.  We feel the least a raider can do is look at the message boards.
  9. Q: What if the entire raid only take an hour?    A: In the event that the raid goes by quickly, full points will be issued for those who attend the entire raid.  Points will also be issued at the discretion of the Raid Coordinator/Raid Captain for members who were not present for the entire raid.
