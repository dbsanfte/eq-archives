Guild Events

Saturday morning on the 20th of January we met at the South Ro Wizard spires to tempt our fate in the Plane of Hate! The first group to port up was Lorika, Valksis, Cilia, Jalanea, Bridgecrusher, and Stratis. while Lorika ported back down to gather more troops, the rest made it safely to our spot at the Northwest corner. Altho Fellowship of Honor had taken the Southwest corner area and was working hard at battling the monsters in their vicinity, the rest of our troops to port up were not as successful as the first group. FoH kindly resurrected Mayasarena but she was sadly killed again in a freak train incident. Anavrin and Isanene immediately began retrieving corpses and then called Cilia in from the chat room to resurrect Mayasarena and Mindert after which she promptly camped out again. Within a few minutes a small group of adventurers were up and ready for action. As more troops were being prepared, a sneaky Spite Golem popped up and began beating on the group. Bridgecrusher bravely led the Golem away from the rest, sacrificing himself so that some of them may live. Another round of resurrections got underway while the corpse retrievers finished locating everyone but Taluil. Try as they might, Taluil's body remained hidden so Chienvoxx was called upon to summon Taluil's corpse from some hidden crevice of Hate. Borrowing a coffin from Azul, Tal's body was brought to us safe and sound. Once everyone was up and ready, Sweetspot went East to locate a monster or two. Seeing only one Forsaken Revenant, she brought it back to camp but somehow the North patrol caught site of her and it all came moments later. We were truly unprepared for the slaughter that occurred next. soon all had died except a handful of people who had either feigned, hidden or were able to camp out. Anavrin called everyone back once the creatures had returned to their patrol and while people were springing back to life, FoH graciously called her over to loot a pair of Woven Shadow gloves. Once again we were ready and we fought thru a few battles on the Northwest wall. We wanted very much to fight monsters to our South but because FoH was near there and working their way towards Innoruuk, we decided to move to the Northeast wall instead. anavrin began scouting out a few of the buildings and reporting back to the monks, Sweetspot and Mindert, who successfully chain pulled several things before Mindert fell under the hand of one too many creatures. The buildings near us rapidly became cleared of monsters and we discussed our best course of action. during this time Anavrin snuck over to the Maestro's bedroom and retrieved the Book of Souls, a piece of her epic quest. As soon as FoH defeated Innoruuk, Etha, our raid coordinator, gave the word and we moved back towards the Southwest corner, where the monks were able to chain pull several more monsters to the group. At one point however, there were less than 16 creatures in the zone! As the day wore on our troops began to get weary. As some of them took their leave, Lorika gathered fresh troops and brought a new wave of energy to the raid. This was a very successful first Hate raid and we want to congratulate and thank everyone who made it so.
