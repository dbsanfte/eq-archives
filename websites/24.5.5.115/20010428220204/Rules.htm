Rules to Live By!

(Also known as: 'Tips to remember in Norrath')

 

1. No Dying!

2. Refer to Rule #1

3. See Rule #2

4. Do not forget to meditate.

5. Do not sit next to angry Mobs in battle!

6. Con your target and not yourself when casting snare. *

7. To save time and energy, thoroughly investigate your trade skill. Otherwise you may make a roundtrip boat ride thru the Ocean of Tears, only to to learn the trade supply you seek is back in Kelethin where you started.

8. Stock up on food and drink! This way you won't be tempted to snack on your edible Quest items.
