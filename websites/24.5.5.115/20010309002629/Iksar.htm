On Sunday March 23 (Earthtime) an Iksar Prisoner was drug thru Greater Faydark into the High Elf city of Felwithe by Sir Jevik Isqual and Sir Galeth Veredeth. He was brought before the High Elf King, Tearis Thex and this is what unfolded...

A large crowd had gathered inside the Paladins Guild by the time the prisoner was led in front of the King. The crowd was torn between being outraged at the High Elves treatment of the prisoner and the obvious threat this Iksar presented.

Sir Galeth Veredeth began to tell the story of how this Iksar came to be his prisoner and the crowd listened attentively. He explained how Princess Firiona Vie, himself, and several others, had embarked on a trip to the Forgotten Lands when suddenly a portal opened and the Iksar came thru. Firiona was grabbed and pulled into the portal by an Iksar. Charging forward Galeth tried to follow the Princess but the portal closed leaving behind one of the attackers.

Upon hearing this tale the King became so enraged that with one swift blow he killed the Iksar prisoner and left the body upon the Guilds floor. The crowd was in an uproar and silence was called so that the King could speak. The King furiously told Galeth that from this day forward his Knighthood was stripped from him because he had failed to protect the Princess and then he struck Galeth across the face and turned away.

Stepping back to his place upon platform the King issued one last order before turning and walking away. He gave Galeth two years to bring the Princess back to Felwithe and with a bow Galeth departed the Guild...his destination, perhaps to Kunark to recover the lost Princess.
