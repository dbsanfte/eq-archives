March 25, 2001

 

          Hail and welcome all to the Ordains web page.  I would like to start off by thankin’ Spartican and the rest of the guild for makin’ our first night a blast and I wish to welcome all those I did not yet met.  My hopes are that this guild will grow to a large and powerful guild while keepin’ the family atmosphere of the Norrathians.  I understand that many of ye have never been in the Norrathians but ye have more than likely grouped with one or two of them.  I will have the web page up and runnin’ a little bit better in the weeks to come, as it is there is not much here.

          I know this guild has made some enemies and there might be a reason behind it but I shall not go into that I just wish that we shall build ourselves a reputation that will span the land of Norrath and others will follow in our wake to join us.
