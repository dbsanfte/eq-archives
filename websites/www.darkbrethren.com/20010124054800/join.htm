Joining Dark Brethren :

1. Goal :

We are looking for individuals who will fit in with our Guild, want to have Fun, combat new challenges, and are able to play their character a cut above the norm.

2. Level restriction :

52 : Cleric, Wizard.
53 : Monk, Shaman, Warrior.
54 : Enchanter.
55 : Bard, Druid, Magician, Paladin, Ranger, Rogue, Shadow Knight.
57 : Necromancer.

3. Equipment needed :

. Sebilis Key

. Charasis Key

. Resist Gear (Cold, Disease, Fire, Magic & Poison in a good amount).

4. Sponsoring :

You will need a sponsor who is a member of Dark Brethren. Once done, you will be know as applicant.

You will have to join as many raids as possible, and try to join single hunts, to know everyone and let them get a feel for you.

Once it is felt you are well known enough for a vote the 1st will be held.

5. Voting & Joining :

You will have to pass two votes in the guild, you cannot have more than one "NO" or too many "DON'T KNOW" to pass them.

If you pass the first vote, the second one will be held a short time after and if you pass it, you will be able to enter the Guild.

Failing a vote will not necessary eliminate any chance of joining but it would say you will have to work more with Guild Members to be known better.

 

Good luck to you.
