Sunday, January 30, 2000
     Submitted by: Fyndolyn Faefyre
Another Ghoulbane retrieved by the Order for Rohaden Spiritblade!! Shin Lord is defeated!!!
The Order of Ascension once again descended into the bowels of Upper Guk to confront the latest Shin Lord, with the intent of capturing another of the famed sword, Ghoulbane for a paladin of the Order, one Rohaden Spiritblade. After rallying our forces in the entryway of Upper Guk early Sunday morning, we made our way into the slimy warrens of the Frogloks. The invasion force consisted of regular Order members Rohaden Spiritblade, Fyndolyn Faefyre, Razk Stargazer, Dyce Steelslinger (OOC due to the Prophecy event), and Ramesys Brettonian. Also entering the dungeon to aid in this cause were Mistical & Elega Ta`Veren and Rizma Battlebunnie, friends of the dwarf Rohaden.
The group was forced to wait while the current Shin hunters held the room, so they kept the surrounding areas clear of frogs. Joining them in short order were other regular members of the Order, Rogarth Rotharian, Zalan Wolfstrider, Kilana Koldsteel, Bargarar Stormchaser and Talamar. Santhini Shadowheart made an appearance to say hello to her old friends and stayed awhile to fight by our sides again!! Finally, the disappointed hin hunters left the room and we were headed in!! After several hours of waiting for the Shin Lord to spawn, he did!! Wielding the mighty blade Ghoulbane, his futile efforts of trying to stay alive long enough to swing the blade were for naught. The Order members completely annihilated the frog, slicing him to ribbons and blasting him with powerful magics. Soon he lay dead at our feet and Rohaden was gifted the blade by Razk, who had looted the corpse and presented the blade to him. We were all very thrilled with having acquired another of the famous swords, our last jaunt with Ketharyl Spiritshield proved successful as well. Congrats and huzzahs to you dwarf friend!!!

Thursday, January 13, 2000
     Submitted by: Fyndolyn Faefyre
Order of Ascension descends into the depths of Upper Guk to retrieve the famed sword Ghoulbane and are successful!!
Early Sunday afternoon, members of the Order gathered in Innothule Swamp, to set upon retrieving the famed paladin sword Ghoulbane, for Ketharyl Spiritshield. He had called the Order members to assist him in this endeavour. Mustering outside the froglok warrens were Fyndolyn Faefyre, Elarien Silverfield, Terrek Clouddancer and his friend Kirrek the monk, Razk Stargazer, Rohaden Spiritblade and Nueilea Naz'gul. Later in the day during the assault, the members would be joined by Dyce Steelslinger, Rogarth Rotharian and Llyrenne Rosehaven. Entering the dungeon, the Order divided into two groups; one lower level group for the entrance and the immediate surroundings and one upper level group for the further inward areas, including the lair of the Shin Lord himself. Joining the upper level group were one Kenyen Solowolf, and friend of the Order Thain Bjarki. Both were wonderful additions to the foray party. Not being able to find the proper location of the Shin Lord's chambers for some time, due to incorrect maps, Thain Bjarki led us to the correct room and we proceeded to engage in lengthy and tiring battles with the Tal, Tsu, Ton, and Shin frogs, including the infamous Shin Lord himself! After a few hours, the Shin Lord made an appearance and was slain. In his frozen death grip was the famed sword Ghoulbane, the object of our quest, and Ketharyl was presented with the sword he sought!!!! The lesser group in the warrens above fought valiantly in their efforts to cleanse the area of frogs. A few deaths occured throughout the day, but the overall objective had been met with resounding success!!! Thanks to all of those that participated in this event!! Huzzah!!!
