Bylaws of the Order of Ascension

  1. Article I: Goals
    1. To encourage the citizens of Norrath upon the worlds created by Erollisi Marr, and Brell Serilis to play out the roles set before them at the time of their birth, be it for the enlightenment or destruction of all.
      1. Members of the Order are strongly encouraged to notify others of our intent to play out our roles via whatever means we have at our disposal (use of the roleplaying flag and active roleplaying).
      2. All members must play out their roles (within reasonable limits) in manner befitting the profession they have choosen and the race to which they were born.
    2. To help shape the world of Norrath into a better place for all who follow the Path of Light, regardless of ethnicity or profession.
    3. To roam the lands spreading mirth and joy, if only unto ourselves.

  2. Article II: Rules of conduct and membership
    1. Membership:
      1. Eligibility:
        1. All spellcasters who follow the Path of Light may walk the Path of Initiation.
          1. Paladins and Rangers come into their magical power later in life. Until they are granted these magics, however, they are not eligible for membership in the Order. A young knight or woodsman may, however, begin their walk upon the Path of Initiation as they near the time of their magical training.
          2. Necromancers and Shadow Knights are practicioners of black arts and possess evil and malicious hearts and as such may not walk the Path of Initiation.
          3. Bards are musicians and although their music possesses qualities magical, they are not spellcasters and as such may not walk the Path of Initiation.
        2. Defenders of the Order
          1. For every ten who have walked the Path of Initiation there shall be made available a position for those who lack magical talent
          2. Only bards, monks, and warriors are eligible candidates for Defenders.
      2. The Path of Initiation:
        1. The Path of Initiation, in actuallity, is first set upon when the would be initiate makes first contact with the guild leadership via private messenger.
        2. Once an event has been held to officially set foot upon the Path there are but a few requirements:
          1. All initiates must travel the land with several established members as they walk the Path of Initiation. During this time the initiate is to chronical his or her travels and exploits. After such a time as the member sees fit, the initiate will be told one of three things: the the member thinks the initiate will prove a good addition to the Order, that the member thinks the initiate would be a poor addition to the Order, or that the member was unable to form an opinion. Was it such that the member was unable to form an opinion of the initiate, the initiate has two options: Make arrangements to travel with the member on another occassion or to seek out another member. Should the member have judged that the initiate would make a poor addition to the Order, the member is required to draft a missive to the Order explaining his judgement. Should this judgement bear true, the initiate will be scrutinized as completely as possible.
          2. The next step upon the path requires the initiate to tell the tale of his travels. In as detailed a manner possible, the initiate shall compose a message to the guild describing the adventures of he or she and his or her sponsoring member. The sponsoring member will then, if necessary, make any corrections or additions that need be made and back and forth until the story is complete. When completed the tale shall be drawn into the scrolls of the Order and placed within our files pertaining to the initiate, that is to say, upon the scrolls which define our membership and more specifically upon the scroll defining the initiate, a magical rune shall be transfixxed entitled "Tales of Initiation" which shall lead all who inquire to these tales.
          3. Three such tales are required before the walk down the Path of Initiation is completed, more if there is a valid argument from any member as to why the initiate should not be made one of the the Order. Please note, that should the argument prove strong no amount of reconciliation will aid the initiate along the Path.
      3. The Path of the Defender:
        1. The Path of the Defender is unlike the Path of Initiation, it is the Path that one walks as due course. It is where all Paths meet. It is where the warrior finds that their armour never dents or rusts, where the bard finds the perfect note, where the monk discovers perfect harmony between body and spirit, and where the rogue is like a gentle breeze. Few make it upon the Path of the Defender and even fewer are happened upon by those who walk the Path of Light. Not all Defenders were meant to walk beside those who walk the Path of Light and as such it is sometimes difficult to determine which Path they need to defend. For those that were meant to walk beside the Order, it is a matter of observation.
          1. Of those who are eligible as Defenders we seek only those who, over the course of a great many moons, have proven themselves good of heart and honor to a member of the Order (the acquaintance may indeed extend to days before the member walked the Path of Initiation.)
          2. Those who would defend the Order need to first be nominated by those they call friend.
          3. Defenders will undergo a period of observation, during which they will travel with a great many of the Order, weave many a tale, and drink many an ale.
          4. Members should report frequently upon their travels with candidates.
          5. Candidates for Defender should chronical their travels with the Order as well, though as the extent of these travels will be far broader than those who are upon the Path of Initiation, a tale is not necessary from each journey.
      4. Conduct:
        1. All members are expected to behave in a manner befitting an order of intellectuals as well as a manner befitting their race and profession.
          1. Abusive behavior towards or harrasment of others will not be tolerated.
        2. Honor and goodness in all things.
          1. As spellcasters we often are accused of dishonorable combat, slaying a monster for our own glory when another was engaged in battle with it. Each of us walk the land with a different purpose, to some it is a distraction from other worlds, to others it is a chance to fight that which is evil, and to others it is to make war and gather as much glory and fame as possible. None know which it is when one happens upon a stranger and as such it is best to avoid conflicts of interest.
            1. Intentional kill stealing, as it is called, will not be tolerated.
            2. If you are the cause of an incident, be diplomatic and apologetic.
            3. If you are the recipient of an incident, be diplomatic.
          2. Buying, selling, and trading goods and services:
            1. Members of the Order shall not charge for magical services.
            2. Members of the Order shall not deal with those who follow the Path of Darkness.
            3. Members of the Order shall use their discretion when setting the value of an item, bearing in mind its strengths, rarity, and difficulty in obtaining. All prices should be fair and just and within the means for those who are meant to have the item.
      5. Voting:
        1. As a general rule all voting will be done informally via our messenger network.
        2. As a general rule all issues require that two-thirds of those who vote be in agreement to pass.
        3. Should an issue arise for which a formal and secretive vote is required, two members will be named to independently tally the votes. All votes must arrive to the appropriate officers by the deadline given with the item to be voted on.
