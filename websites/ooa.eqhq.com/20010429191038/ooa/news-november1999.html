A real life hero.
     Submitted by: Bargarar
While we do not usually include Earthly stuff in our news, it is good to know that we can make a difference every once in a while. Awoken from his sleep Sunday morning by the ringing phone, Bargarar (Brian Rose) found himself on the recieving end of an elderly woman's random call for help. Thinking it a possible prank, but not wishing to contemplate the consequences that it was not, Brian phoned 911 and quite possibly saved the womans life.
A hearty, "HUZZAH!" to Bargarar, a true to life, real hero.
To read the full story, check out the Asbury Park Press.

Guild Gathering & LooOooOOooOOooottery!!!
     Submitted by: Fyndolyn Faefyre
The Order gathered recently at the Tiger's Roar Inn & Tavern in Highhold Pass to celebrate our recent victories in battle and to share tales of wonderous battles over many fine alchoholic beverages!! During the night, Fyndolyn held order over the crowd as some of our Initiates were welcomed into the Order as "permanent" members of the Order of Ascension! Ketharyl Spiritshield, Eskaia Ashtoreth, Bargarar Stormchaser and Daestro Bowcaster were all bid welcome and to display their guild tags proudly as members of the Order. After them came Kilana Koldsteel, Llyrenne Rosehaven, and Santhini Shadowheart, who having completed their walk upon the Path of the Defender and completing their 3 Tales of Ascension as those had before them, were also welcomed into the Order as "permanent" members!! It was a joyous occasion for all, and the Order is richer and stronger by their presence!! HUZZAH!!
After the furor died down a bit, we held our second ever guild LooOOooOoottery! We sold an amazing 96 tickets! Everyone gathered around Akhenaton as he stood upon a table to draw the winning ticket. Tension mounted as a few misrolls on the Random Roll were called out, but after a few moments, Akhenaton had announced the winner of the 2nd Order of Ascension LooOooOoottery as Fyndolyn Faefyre!! Everyone cast magics at the same time, blinding the room in the process, with magical motes of energy everywhere!! Fyn decided to go for the item instead of the coin, which means we deposited a total of 188 platinum pieces into the coffers that night!! HUZZAH!! Fyndolyn is still contemplating which item he would like to go after.
Akhenaton Amun'Ra Missing!! Order members search for him in Cazic-Thule and rescue him!!
     Submitted by: Fyndolyn Faefyre
Akhenaton Amun'Ra, resident Enchanter and borderline nut, recently went missing during his excursion in the lands of Cazic-Thule! A message was posted by the Magistrate of Freeport offering a reward for our brother's return, offered by his wife, Thebes Amun'Ra. Various Order members gathered in the hopes of setting out to Cazic-Thule to find our missing brother. Included in the group were Fyndolyn Faefyre, Daestro Bowcaster, Santhini Shadowheart, Thebes Amun'Ra, and Talamar. Making their way through the dark, dreary lands of the Feerott, the group entered the lands named for the Dark God that resides within, Cazic-Thule. What was Akhen thinking when he entered these dire lands alone? Mayhaps he was fresh out of marbles, or left them at home completely!? Regardless, the group of friends forged on and into the lizard infested grounds in search of Akhen. Wading through the scaly beasts, the group located the Enchanter locked in a cell guarded by two lizardman defenders, dispatching them and saving Akhenaton!! Akhen wanted to reward the group with the Polished Bone Bracer, but being his friends, the group did not want to take it. He insisted and a random roll was called out, Fyndolyn once again being the lucky individual, and thus the PBB was given to him. HUZZAH! For the safe return of our friend!!
