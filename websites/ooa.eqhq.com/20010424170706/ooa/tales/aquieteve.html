A Quiet Eve


It was quiet on the plains. The sun was setting and the moonlight began to shimmer off the dew on the grass. Nueilea the High Elven Wizard sat next to Aleigha the Wood Elf Druid. They where sent there because local farmers where complaining that there was a nasty ghoul running around at night and killing the innocent wildlife and people.
"So is this where you they told you it should be?" said Nueilea.
"Yes there have been alot of killings around here. I shall slay any beasts that harm my woodland friends." said Aleigha.
As they sat there looking into a open medow below them a large stone monolith rose into the skyline. As the last rays of light disapeared off in the distance. They heard a low grinding noise. With there keen sight they saw a boulder at the base of the monolith begin to roll away. Out came a hideous ghoul with a foaming mouth and large yellow eyes.
"Here this will help us." said Aleigha as she began to cast a spell.
"Ready to do this." said Nueilea
"Yeah lets slay this beast before it causes more harm." saind Aleigha.
With that Nueiliea and Aleigha both began to cast. All of the sudden the ground near the ghouls feet began rumble and roots shot up and wrapped around its legs. Then a ball of fire came hurrling at the creature and knocked it down to the ground with its feet still entwined with roots. With that out of the ran another larger ghoul. Before he knew it his feet where also entwined in roots.
"This may be a problem." said Nueilea
"It seems there were two of them. Just focus on the first one for now." said Aleigha.
The ghouls began furiosly slashing at the roots holding them down. Nueilea and Aleigha began to continue there casting.
With her eyes shut Nueilea began to concentrate and started to feel the power centering in her fists. Before she was able to discharge this energy a sharp blow was hit to her side knocking her down for a second. Looking up the ghoul was standing there frothing at the mouth. It raised its arm and showed hideous claws. Before it could swing Aleigha crushed it in the back of her head with her mace. The ghoul hit the ground dead. Looking over the larger ghould was tearing away the last of the roots holding it to the ground. "Time to go." said Aleigha as they both bolted off for the horizon. The ghoul freeing himself just then ran off after them. Aleigha turning around began to cast and so did Nueilea. Another ball of fire hit the ghoul knocking him back. Then up from the ground came a swarm of bees stinging the creature everwhere. It shrieked in pain trying to swat the bees. Then in anger began chasing after the two heroes again.
"I can't run anymore, keep him busy." said Nueilea
"What?" said Aleigha turning, but Nueilea was no where in site. A bit bepuzzled she began running again the ghoul gaining on her. Quick on her heels the ghoul began trying to strike. Slashing her leg Aleigha hit the ground and they ghoul pounced at her. As this happened the sky opened up from above and a bolt of lightning came down right through the ghoul stopping him in mid air. By the time he hit the ground he was fried and dead. Looking over Aleigha saw a blurred image appear and become more clear. It was Nueilea walking towards her. "Some trick my sister." said Aleigha steam still rising off the corpse.
"Come, lets go and see what the guild has for us next." said Nueilea. And with that they headed west.
