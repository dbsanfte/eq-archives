We, the spellcasters of Norrath, in an attempt to enhance roleplaying on the Erollisi Marr server, have formed a new guild. As it has been noted on more than one occasion by us and by others, Erollisi Marr lacks an active roleplaying population. Our primary goal is to change this, or to at least get the server headed in the proper direction. All members of the Order of Ascension will be required to use the roleplaying flag (and more appropriately, to roleplay) and to contribute towards this goal. Secondarily, it is our goal to help all of the good aligned races in Norrath to thrive! We will accomplish this by inserting into the economy an element designed to aid the people, to make items of value obtainable to those for whom such items were just beyond their reach as well as making magics available to all, free of charge. Our main role in the societies of Norrath is to provide guidance to all casters who are good of spirit. Any being of honor and goodness that can sling a spell will be welcome into our fold without prejudice. As our members shall number druids, rangers, and shaman as well, we shall endeavor to help heal Norrath of the ills brought about by the coming of the peoples to Norrath, to educate all the races on the goodness of the land. We shall set right the record, we were put here by the good mother to serve Norrath, that only as a whole might we destroy those who revel in carnage and destruction of the natural and spiritual world. And as our numbers grow we shall make war on the beings and beasts that terrorize all that is good, for has it not already been written thusly? Together we shall combine the magical might of Norrath to learn all there is to learn and to pass our knowledge and goodness unto the next generation of the good mother's children.
