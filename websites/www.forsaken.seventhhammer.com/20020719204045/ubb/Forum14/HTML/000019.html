Greetings all prospective members...

Here are a few Rules so you know what Kind of Guild you are applying to join.

Eastern Faith sees itself as more of a family than a guild.. we are all willing to help each other whenever possible.


  • 1. Our loot system is Merit Loot. Basically this means all loot is Awarded by the officers attending the raid to Players, based on Raids attended, Level and Need.
  • 2. We NEVER ever discuss Loot Problems in Guild Chat, tell an officer if you have concerns.
  • 3. ALL new members will be on 2 Weeks Trial and in that time they won’t be awarded loot unless it’s going to Rott.
  • 4. On raids you must Listen to the raid leader, try to keep spam to a minimum and do what is asked of you Without question. If you are unsure of what your role is on a Guild Raid then you can ask a group member don’t send unnecessary tells to the raid leader.
  • 5. Those members seen to be Loot Fiends, Disrupting play, annoying other members, using Guild Chat to Sell items or any other actions that cause distress within the guild will be removed.
  • 6. When asked to join a raid we expect FULL guild support those that don’t support the guild will be removed
  • 7. Any Items awarded on a GUILD raid will stay with the Char that was awarded the item, if you wish to trade or sell the Item YOU must get permission from an Officer to do so. You may be asked to pass down awarded loot to other members of the guild if you do not need the item.
  • 8. Officer’s decisions are FINAL.
  • 9. Level limit for Joining EF is level 55

To aid any request to join we require that you have all resists close to 100 unbuffed with Blue diamond equipment on.

The following keys are required:
Sebilis, Cobalt Scar, Skyshrine and Howling Stones.

Our ideal play times are from
1600hrs GMT - 0300 GMT Weekdays and 1200hrs GMT until 0400 hrs GMT weekends.

We raid many different places and have been doing some joint raids with Sanctuary to achieve server 1st Kills of King Tormax and Cazic Thule.
