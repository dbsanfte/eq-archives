Of Khiranus Myrhin
(Part the First)
By Tikhen Myrhin

A harsh wind blew through his hair as he stood knee deep in the chilling snow. He gazed far into the distance with elven eyes, eyes not hindered by the coming darkness. The look of rage and of passion on his face would almost make one think that he could summon his poor beloved wife and young son to him with sheer will power alone. Khiranus had been in the harsh snows of Halas for weeks, weeks unfed that would have killed a mortal man or even an elf of less strength than he. He was driven by an unstoppable force, a force that no wind, no cold, and no amount of hunger could stop. He would find his wife, and he would find his son, even if it killed him and anyone that stood in his way.

As he searched the horizen for any signs of life, horrible memories filled his mind. It had happened almost two fortnights ago, but the images still invaded his thoughts, filling him with sorrow and with anger. He had returned to his home, high in the trees of Kelethin, after a day of hunting only to come upon a sight of horror. The fine oaken door he had built by hand was torn off its hinges, hanging crookedly from a thong of leather. Inside, furniture was overturned and signs of a struggle were evident. Of more terribleness than all the damage was the blood. Smears of it, up and down the walls of his once peaceful home. This blood, this pure elven blood, had obviously been intentionly wiped on the walls, the blood of his wife. It was to Khiranus, a mockery of his home and of all Feir'dal. Who had done this? It did not take him long to decide it was Teir'dal, only they were capable of such madness. But how? how could Teir'dal enter the heart of Kelethin undetected in the middle of the day? He would not stay to find out. He was on the trail almost as soon as he had opened the door.

For days he used his superior tracking skill, honed from years of service to Kelethin and Felwithe, to track the criminals. Through Greater Faydark, on to ButcherBlock, and finally to the seaside village then known as Anik'amyen. Here he could see that they were many and thankfully that his wife and son still lived. At least ten bandits had met up with the group here and no doubt caught a ship to the mainland. The odds were impossible, Khiranus knew it, but he was a brash and bold man. He would not tarry on Faydwer rallying support. He left immediately, alone...
