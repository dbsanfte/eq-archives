Elven Common Laws         

The Common Law is made up of 6 bills. Each of these 6 bills the Elves of the Guard agree to perform once they become a member. Failure to uphold the Common Law will result in a court hearing among your peers. Any act against or undermines the Guard will be asked to leave.

Passing Common Law

The 6 bills can be modified or have append to the clause or become a new bill. The process of passing law should go as follows. The bill will be passed to the officers on a meeting platform for open debate. A majority vote of 70% is required inorder to make it a common law.

To be part of the Eleven Guard you must have a committed character at level 5 or higher. Subject to change at a later date.

The Bill of Rights

  1. As a member of the Elven Guard; you agree to the following terms and conditions as a member. Failure, you will ask to leave the Elven Guard.
  2. Killing of an Elven Guard member is a immediate remove from the guild; Elven Court will not be held.
  3. Killing of your brethren in Psychotic Killing (PK) purpose is not allowed. However killing of Psychotic Killers (PKers) of any races is allowed.
  4. Protection of newbies and any other true blood elves.
  5. Defense of the senseless killings of newbies and other brethren in Norrath. All PKers are either to be forced to leave the area or Killed On Sight (KOS).
  6. All Guards must participate in Guard events, and maintain the guild's good name.
