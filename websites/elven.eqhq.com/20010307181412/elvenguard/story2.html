The Journey of a Friend

Part one

As told by Machinoxen

(the father of the recently deceased Machinoix)

One stocky Dwarf was leaving the Tapped Barrel Tavern as morning broke the sky and the moon faded into the rays of the morning. A tall fellow one of Half-Elven heratige came close to the Dwarf, picked him up and set him back down, "Cervanties!" the young half-elf said with a bit of excitement in his voice, "Thou hast been seventeen seasons sence we last saw each other has it not?" the dwarf replied with a hard but foolish slug to the arm and said, "Aye it has been seventeen seasons, Machinoix, thou has aged well for one of dark elven and barbarian heratige, Thou hast show me thyn wife of yours you so speaketh about last season we spoke." The elf replied "Oh *he laughs* he is doing well but she can not stand my will to hunt stronger creatures then I." "That beeth true. Thou hast hunted those damn Ice Bones sence we first meet, Give it a rest Mac...." They both laugh as Machinoix leads them back to the docks and they wait for the boat. Both share stories and telling jokes as to pass the time.

A month passes as the stocky little dwarf makes his temporary home on the boat for another few months. "Thou hast been thinking again Mac...." Machinoix replies short of breath from running laps to keep his mind of something, "You have a good eye, friend, my wife is to give birth a month from now and I fear I am not the best father and I shalt be here when it happens...." the dwarf ponders then laughs "I have a friend that can get you there faster then you can spit off this boat he is on the next stop but that is 2 weeks away and I do not know he may do this for free because he owes me but I am not sure about this." the dwarf pauses as he laughs, "I know!" he pulls out a shiney stone that is black in color and is no larger then a copper piece, circular wise. "He gave this to me to use next time I was in trouble and he said only use it once or it will make the gods mad" he softly presses it into his palm and says a few words. Just then a portal opens and a tall shadowed figure steps out.
