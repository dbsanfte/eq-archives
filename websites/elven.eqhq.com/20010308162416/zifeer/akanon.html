Wanderings in…Ak’non

Passing between the bubble-tanks on the surface and into the yawning tunnel mouth, one is immediately struck by the heat and the noise. The tunnel winds its way down into the bowels of the earth, amid a cacophony of ticking, banging and hissing of steam, which parts to reveal a great metal wall, and a sign; "Ak’non"

The home city of the Gnomes is, for me, something of an uncomfortable place, yet also a place of wonders. Buried deep below the Steamfont Mountains, the entire place is one big workshop, as Gnomes caper on high gantries and adjust this or tweak that. The place seems awfully confused, yet nonetheless, seems to move with one purpose. I only hope it is a benevolent one.

Everywhere one looks, there are panels of metal and whirling gears and cogs. It takes some getting used to, particularly if like me, you are claustrophobic. I rarely spend long in here; sooner or later I panic and Gate to somewhere where there aren’t several million tones of rock hanging above me. Also, there is the incessant ticking. All the time, everywhere, tick, tick, tick…(Snap out of it, Zif!) I once asked a passing Gnome if it was strictly necessary, or just a tourist thing, and he simply replied "What ticking?"

My own psychosis aside, Ak’non is truly a marvelous place. It has all the facilities, shops and guilds you’d expect from a major city, and many breath-taking sights to boot. In one part of the city, is a giant water-wheel, kept turning day and night to power some process which escaped me. I asked the Gnome Mechanic who told me to "Stop running on it, it’s not a toy!" what it was for. I’m afraid I lost track when he began talking about "Alternative Currants" but nodded and smiled anyway, trying to fight that glassy-eyed look.

Another place of note, which I found in quite poor taste, was the Ak’non Zoo. Here proud creatures of the wild are held in captivity in tiny metal cells, far below ground for the amusement of visitors. I was shaking with rage when I left, but, not wanting to cause trouble, I held my tongue.

One of the more important features to discuss, is Gnomish Spirits. Now I don’t really drink much, so the invitation by one of the locals to come sit with him in a bar and exchange tales of distant lands seemed innocuous enough. I think I remember unstopping a jug of the fierce stuff and proposing a toast, but not much after that. I think there was singing involved, and magic. Next thing I can recall clearly, was waking up, high in a tree near Kelethin. The moral, if any, is don’t drink and gate. (Took me most of the morning to get down safely)

Ak’non also contains a bank and is home to one of the post-bards, she’s easy to spot, the only Wood Elf in the place. I forget her name exactly, my mind was slightly…pickled…that day.

(*groan*…I didn’t did I!?…oh no…I’ll never be able to collect the Ak’non post again!)

And on Rallos Zek…

General city etiquette seems to apply here, and murders are rare. Gnomes, of course, will have no problems here, but other races are more uncertain.

For instance, I am always stepping over Wood Elf corpses on the way in here, yet I am regarded indifferently by most here. I am inclined to believe the Gnomes care less about race and more about creed and profession. If in doubt, see how the guards outside feel about you and use that as a guide.

(Note: Druids beware, this city is NOT an outdoors location, and as such will rob you of many of your powers, including Camouflage. It’s also very claustrophobic!)
