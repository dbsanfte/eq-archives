August 28, 1999

Written by Lunar

Vampires take over Mistmoore for a short time.

I got word from a friend in Mistmoore that three mysterious vampires appeared.  These vampires would shout out names of nearby warriors and began slaying them left and right. The leader of the vampires was exceptional strong, as was the two with him.  Daring adventurers attempted to fight back but were quickly slain. The corpses were in piles.  Now it was time to take action.

Shortly after telling some of my friends to come down, word spread to their guild and soon we had around 30 adventurers coming to assist us in this invasion. The first vampire to die was the head vampire, Grayfed Soulreap. We focused all our firepower on him, thinking that the other vampires would scatter without their leader. This was almost true. I got very low on health and had to flee. When I came back another vampire was just about slain. Then one by one, the vampires dropped to the floor. They carried many valuable items, I have not heard exactly what was dropped, but I looted a Saffire, and a Potion of Undead Recourse. There was a very powerful sword on one of them, but it could not be looted because it was in the vampires weapon slot. Now it was time for revenge.

With all the people there we decided to raid the castle, and that is what we did. We were killing everything with ease, we cleared out the castle up to the princess. Afterwards, I had to leave and get some rest.  My bones were weary indeed!

- Lunar
