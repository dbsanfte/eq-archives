I have a feeling that if Narthain were still with us on this day he would have wanted me to speak on his behalf and keep the new generation of guardsmen informed of your valiant history.

Let me start from the beginning. Narthain served as the guild ambassador for many months and did a fine job representing all of you to the high courts of Freeport, Neriak, and even Qeynos. Back then most guardsmen stayed within close proximity of the Faydark and Crushbone so Narthain was essential to keeping ties open with the mainland Antonica. Narthain was well fit to lead diplomatic actions for the guard, he was well acquainted with ancient Koada'Dal and Feir'dal traditions and customs. He acted in a very upright and reserved manner. (until you got to know him!) He strongly supported the guard acting as a strong military organization and was often known to be in conflict with the rowdier members.

Machinoix Darkpaw was perhaps the rowdiest of them all. He and Narthain had been in conflict over some of his policies as drill seargent. He would force new recruits to run laps and act in a very boastful manner that eventually forced a member to leave the guard and flee to the mainland for safety! When Narthain confronted him about it he stated that he was friends with powerful people within the guild who wouldn't believe him or punish Mach for his actions. Narthain posted this text for all of the guard to see and Machinoix conveniently vanished for a substantial period of time. When he returned he challenged Narthain to a duel and during that battle allowed him to be slain by a random murderer in the area. It was uncertain whether or not this was intentional on Mach's part but did not seem entirely unlikely.

Supposedly during that period of time Machinoix was possessed by the ancient lizardman god of pain and suffering, Thepa. Thepa had a strong following led by the possessed monk, Amano Jyaku. It is well documented that those under his control (including myself) were not difficult to recognize. A typical Thepan can be spotted by a strong blue glow in his eyes, an unquenchable hunger for pain (pain makes him stronger!), and the tendency to speak in lizardman speech. To learn more about Thepa please check your local library for the Official Thepamicon. Those who observed Machinoix during his supposed time of possession reported no signs of glowing blue eyes, a particular hunger for pain, or any knowledge of lizardman speak.

More arguing persisted between the supposed Thepan (who was now cured) and Narthain. He was then asked to leave the guard, and did so without much fuss. Shortly after his departure he returned with his supposed new guild "Assassins of Darkpaw" and began threatening people left and right. Within a couple of days he was gone again, supposedly off to the mainland in hopes of a better life.

For some time he had persisted to lurk on guard boards sometimes cursing us and at other times pretending as though nothing bad had happened. In one instance Aaele said "He messaged me today, and tried to lure me into the arena.". Also, when Larial decided to leave the guard he congradulated her on "seeing the light". What his intentions were are anybody's guess. He would return time and time again, apologizing and then threatening us all over again!

So here we are again, with Mach cured once and for all, ready to rejoin the ranks of society once more. I can forgive him for costing us that member so long ago, I can forgive him for being a jerk while "possessed" by Thepa, I can forgive him for the countless threats he has waved at the guards in hopes of drawing more attention to himself, but dragging Narthain's name through the mud after his tragic death is simply too much for this bard to handle. If Narthain's corpse wasn't so badly mutilated it would be rolling around in it's grave. He gave so much to the guild and was a superb example of what every elf on the face of Norrath should strive to be that it pains me to see him remembered as a lowly dog.

-Zaikir "Narthain would have wanted it this way" Akengro
