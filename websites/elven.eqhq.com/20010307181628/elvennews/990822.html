August 22, 1999

Written by Anduril Valar, Major of the Elven Guard

Finally, the gods have heard our cries and bestowed upon us our Guildname!  Last eve, Sentinel Michaela did what was above and beyond the duty of any Guardsman;  she helped us obtain our name!  Something I have been painfully trying to accomplish for days.  In this picture, you can see three of the first six members to have the title given to them:  Amaroqq, Anduril, Fellun, Grifterr, Michaela, and Sinbad!  I hope to have many more names within these ranks in the next couple of days!

August 20, 1999

Written by Anduril Valar, Major of the Elven Guard

It appears that the Teir'Dal were not content with the beating we had shown them a few days before.  A small force again invaded the Faydark and were quickly repelled.  The Dark Elves ran into Crushbone and small skirmishs concluded within there.

Although we could not slay all the Teir'Dal fore they hid in the Castle walls of Crushbone and our force was not quite large enough to overtake it, we did have a hand at slaying Emperor Crush's Prophet and a few members of the Royal Guard.

Justice and Vengeance were served.

August 18, 1999

Written by Anduril Valar, Major of the Elven Guard

Well, it was an intense night, to say the least.  I was in Freeport doing a little fact finding when Grifterr shouted to me through the special telepathic bond we have.  He told me that a force of Teir'Dal had invaded the Faydark at the Wizard Spires.  I quickly contacted as many Elven Guard members as I could and our allies.  I knew I could not make the trip fast enough to be of any help so I talked Grifter through as much as I could.

Quickly, the Feir'Dal and some of our neighbors the gnomes and dwarves rallied a considerable force to face this threat.  Many Guardsmen and members of the Vengeance of Elddar were spotted in the heat of the battle.

Here you can see Amaroqq, Sentry of the Elven Guard, taking on a Teir'Dal by the name of Volrox.  In the end, we were victorious, but it appears as if a heavy price was paid.  Ordieth Lightblade, member of the Elven Council and Guildmaster of Tunare's Justice was slain.  Ordieth is a great Elf and has lead many a battles against our enemies.  Although our views do not always meet eye to eye, I respect him highly.  Many of those that fought by his side that day gathered around his body and prayed to their god for help in this time of need.  Even members of the Vengeance were seen, such as Boaz and their Guildmaster Aeranis Stormwalker.  But, in the end, I believe Tunare heard our prayers for Ordieth was returned to life and given another chance.

Although I was not present, nor was Grifterr, of the celebrations that happened afterwards at the Tapped Barrel Tavern, I heard fun was had by all and a small brawl broke out!  I guess it was a good thing Grifterr did not show, he would of started another game of Acromage!
