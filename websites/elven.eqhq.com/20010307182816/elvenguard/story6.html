Adventure of a Lost Soul
as told by Machinoix

Everyone must knoweth me by know....I am Machinoix,...Paladin.....,Father......,Adventurer..
I currently was remembering my Tale of when i was kicked out of the guard...

It was the 1st of May....I was enjoying myself with a game of darts in the local tavern. When something evil swept over me..I could not contain myself, I begain attacking others, the bar keep threw me out but I attacked the first person I saw..., Narthain, he was standing there looking at me as if I was a fool, he always hated me I saw it in his eyes.., but i had no control over my body, I could hear, feel, see, and even touch, but I could not control myself! I kept hearing,..."Thepa's will is your will, You will do what Thepa does, Your Thepa...Thepa will kill all.." Over and over and over....I could not stand it but I could not do anything about it..

Narthain was running towards the top of the City. I gave chase untill I had killed him once he was dead I dropped my axe and stepped back. I had killed my own guardsmen...correction Thepa had killed him. I knew what I had done so I ran and got scared... I heard a decree that I was to be hung by the Guard from several of there memmbers..I did not know so I ran out to an Unknown land..a Place that had never been touched by the hands of men...I later found out from one of the local, "Lizard Men" that i was in there home of Kunark...I later left to join the Judicial Enforcers they had not know of Thepa's deeds with my body and I was safe till about 9 days after...

It was May 10th and it was my birthday and everything was heading fine till I met Narthain again, back in Kelethin. He left me no choice but to kill him...and this time he was under his own hate for me, but not under Thepa's will that was gone..

This is my story and I hope you realize what mistake you made in kicking me out of the guild...I only Hope Tunare's bidding with you all is quick and painless....
