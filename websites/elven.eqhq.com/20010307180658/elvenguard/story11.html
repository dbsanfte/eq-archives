It was early in the morning. Daylight had just started to peak through the forest trees. A young watchman, new to the Guard, was making his rounds healing and buffing young elves as they fought bats and such around the lifts at Kelthin. The Guardman eagerly took a break around noon to buy some bread and a couple of short beers, then took off to complete his rounds. Venturing tward the orc camps, the warriors there had rumors of a pesky Gnome who had slain a couple of thier companions. After hunting for this Gnome for a couple of hours and slaying a couple of orc centurion the Gnome was finally spotted close to the entrance of Crush Bone Castle. The watchman quickly recognized the Gnome as Bonken, a known murdered and member of 'The Gnomish Empire' (for the two had done battle before). Bonken quickly attacked as soon as he was confronted of his crimes. Being a couple of seasons under the Gnome, the watchman knew he was outmatched but fought anyways for the principles of good and honor. Pulling a couple of tricks and stratigizing a bit, the battle looked as if it could be won for the side of good. A few minutes of fighting had past and the watchman was growing tired and low on mana though. Bruised and scraped from battle the he gritted his teath and clinched his morning star tightly. Charging at Bonken to battle it out to the finish, the morning star hit hard. The Gnome stood strong and delivered an equal relpy. Bonken slashes again to score a hit that would prove to be fatal for the watchman! Droping his weapon and falling to his knees, the young Guard member looks up at the evil killer with a stern look on his face and mumbles, "Your time shall come some day." Falling to the ground, his lasts thoughts were that he had no regret... for he was fighting for what he believed in. The evil Gnome laughs and praises, "Ah, you fool! Yet another victory against good." Was this battle a lost cause? Some may say so... but I think not!
