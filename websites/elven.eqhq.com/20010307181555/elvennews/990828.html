August 28, 1999

Written by Anduril Valar, Major of the Elven Guard

The Elven Guard has been in much turmoil of late.   Internal conflicts and confusion have made for difficult times.  And no worse of a time could this happen.  Word of an invasion by the Teir'Dal army upon Freeport has reached us.  The Elven Council made the determination that the Elven Guard should set sail for Freeport.  The reason why is that an innocent Teir'Dal girl by the name of Sazami holds a tome of immense knowledge... a book written by the ancient Feir'Dals.   Our mission was to protect the book and return it to Felwithe.

Many of the Guardsmen packed up a few belongings and made their way for the docks near Kaladim.  These individuals knew rough times were ahead of us.  Word that the Blood Coven would lead this assualt against Freeport brought shivers down many warrior's spines.

Once they had arrived in Freeport, the Guard met up with other defenders of the Book; Amano Jyaka, Lunema, Aurore Akengro, and our own Council Member Gildurath to name a few.

Combing the tunnels for the Teir'Dal forces, the Elven Guard is always on alert.  This conflict would make for strange alliances.  As you see, a Teir'Dal by the name of Valarea was here... but she was not part of the Teir'Dal force.

The time came.  All the warriors met at the Temple of Marr and led Sazami into the tunnels, hoping to keep her from harms way.  We hoped the Teir'Dal would not think of this...

Unfortunately, the Teir'Dal did not only think of it, they counted on it.  The Blood Coven and various allies assaulted the tunnels as their brethren kept up the guise of a frontal assault in the East Commons.

In the heat of battle, a lone Paladin heard the clashes of steel and sounds of energy as he rushed down to defend the Book.  Unfortunately, this brave soul would not see the end of the battle.

The battle raged on for many hours.  Our plan was to keep Sazami safe at the end of the tunnel, surrounded by clerics and casters while the melee forces met the forces of evil head on.  Many deaths occurred that day.

In the end, though, the Teir'Dal succeeded.  We tried our best but their forces were too prepared and their numbers too overwhelming.  Came towards the very end of two hours when Sazami was charmed and ran from her protective barrier of warriors.  Out in the open, she was no match for the Teir'Dal force.   Quickly, the Blood Coven took her and the Book and left, their mission complete.
