To join the Elven Guard, you must:

  1. Be in good standings with the people of Norrath
  2. Of the Elven race
  3. You must also be at a training level of 5 or above
  4. Be on the Rallos Zek server
  5. Roleplay

Also, you must understand these rules that ALL Guardsmen follow:

1. The Elven Guard is a military unit. It will run as such.

2. All commands of superior officers will be followed without hesitation. The time for questioning is later. If you feel an order is unjust, contact General Amaroqq.

- The Chain of Command that the Elven Guard follows is:

  1. General
  2. Major
  3. Captain
  4. Sergeant
  5. Corporal
  6. Guardian
  7. Guard
  8. Sentinel
  9. Sentry
  10. Watch

3. Guardsmen will always attempt to judge others by their actions and morals, not by race or nationality. Furthermore, you SHOULD NOT attack anyone (whether he/she is a member we are at war with) if he/she is unarmed (i.e. Just died and have nothing on them). If this person is retrieving their body, warn them to move their body to an area away before hostile actions are taken (i.e., This guild will NOT tolerate zone camping, bind camping, or killing people while they loot their corpses).

4. Read the sections called Common Law, About Us, Receiving Promotions.

If you meet these requirements, you need to send a pigeon with the following questions answered, preferably not 1-line answers.

  1. What race are you?
  2. What class are you?
  3. What is your level of training?
  4. Do you practice any trades?
  5. Why do you want to join the Guard?
  6. Can you tell me a little about yourself?
  7. Why did you choose this profession (class)?
  8. Do you attack murderers on sight?
  9. What do you do if you see an elf attacking an elf?
  10. We have a military-esque structure with ranks. Would you listen to all superiors' orders and strive to attain promotions?
  11. Would you visit our guildhall (board) at least once a week ?
  12. ooc: Upon joining, will you post your character's background story at our guildhall (board)?
  13. Would you help all good beings who need it, and not tarnish the Guard's good name?
  14. ooc: We are a roleplaying guild, and expect all of our members to rp in public, and in the guildhall unless they say 'ooc:' first (we're a little more lenient when in a group hunting mobs). Would you accept this?
  15. Where did you hear about us?
  16. How do you feel about "dark" races?
  17. Have you ever been in a guild before?
  18. What do you expect from the guild?
  19. What can you offer the guild?
  20. Have you ever been to anything of interest (wars, adventures, player events, gm events, etc)?
  21. Which god, if any, do you pledge loyalty to?
  22. ooc: Have you ever played pen and paper RPGs (like D&D) before?

By the way, all answers should be in-character.
