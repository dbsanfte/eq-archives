Of Khiranus Myrhin
(Part the Second)

By Tikhen Myrhin

The boat ride would be long and tedious, this he knew.

"Damn" he said out loud, "they've gained days on me by now."

He stood staring over the edge of the great ship, contemplating the impossibility of his task.

"What makes you so impatient?"

Khiranus whirled around, startled that another was so near him without his knowing. Upon the deck sat a squat dwarf with a scar closing off his right eye. He stared with his one eye not at Khiranus, but out at the waves of the Ocean of Tears. Khiranus knew it was he who had spoken though.

"I would advise you to mind to your own affairs dwarf."

The dwarf replied " I make anything that happens on this boat my affairs, whether an elf likes it or not."

"Why you little..." Khiranus stopped short, "whatever happens on the boat?".

"Yes", the dwarf answered "and sorry, seems we've gotten off on the wrong foot here."

He extended a stubby grizzled hand. "I'm Daerloff, only seafaring dwarf you'll ever meet. And this is my daughter Havam."

At this point a timid dwarven youth stepped forward and looked nervously up at the stern figure of Khiranus. He eyed the two for a moment, and then bowed low.

"I am Khiranus Mahryn, keeper of the first hope. Well met earth folk."

Daerloff looked pleased at the gesture of respect from an elf. "Now friend you said all comings and goings of this boat are your business yes?"

"Aye, nothing passes my eye, without my knowing it."

"Well," Khiranus continued "what sort of passengers have you had in the last day?"

At this, Daerloff briefly made eye contact with Khiranus, then looked away with a shudder. "Aah..just uh...the normal type. Adventurers, seeking fortune on the mainland, families, the like, you know.." he quickly wiped his nose with his sleeve. Khiranus was in no mood for avoidance of his questions. He moved quickly towards the dwarf and laid a firm hand under his arm, lifting him easily to his feet.

"Now listen closely dwarf, whatever threats they made to you, nothing will compare to the pain I'd put you through if you withhold information from me." Khiranus' gaze pierced a hole in the shocked dwarfs will.

"I..I. they...fif...fifteen of them." He finally gasped. "Fifteen of the sort I would never like to meet again. A few of Halasian stock. The rest, slight fellows,all robed and covered despite the sweltering heat. That..That's all I know." The dwarf struggled slightly under Khiranus' powerful grip.

"Don't make me refresh your memory son of Brellis!" The dwarf could not keep it to himself, his will was weak compared to the hawkish eyes of Khiranus. The child began to weep softly as it watched the proceedings.

"Go below Havam" Daerloff sputtered "They..they said if I told anyone of their...I..I can't"

Khiranus tightened his grip until the sweat poured off the dwarfs head like a wellspring.

"They'd kill me...I suppose you'll do so if I don't... To Everfrost they were going, they had two downtrodden looking elves with them, a woman, and a boy, dirty as sin..." Khiranus shook him stiffly, scowled, and released him, walking to the riggings where he began to weep gently. Daerloff slumped back to the deck and rubbed his shoulder.

"Damn fickle elves....one minute they scare the beard off ya, the next, cryin'..."

Khiranus ignored the remark and stood, perhaps for the first time truly feeling the immensity of his loss.

"Those two", he began, "those two are my family, the greatest gift Tunare has ever given me." Daerloff looked shocked and ashamed at the same moment.

"I'm, I'm sorry to hear that." It was a weak phrase, he knew this, but Daerloff had a daughter of his own. He could empathize with the pain. Now that day, a strange thing happened to that dwarf, a thing that he would at times regret, and at times would proclaim it as the proudest moment of his life; for at that moment he showed something he had never had. Courage.

"I will help you find them, Khiranus, Keeper of the first hope....."
