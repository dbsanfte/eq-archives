Veras' Songs

By the minotuar caves, my party stood.
We had fought quite well, our cause was just and good.
All around lie the corpses of slavers,
The gods had surely shown us their favor.

So confident were we that the warrior stood,
And said,"I think we should kill the Minotaur Lord."

We thought we were strong, and we thought we were brave.
We pulled him out of the cave and opened the path to our grave.

The barbarian was felled in two mighty blows,
The paladin bellowed in pain and then crumpled low.
The cleric could not withstand the attack.
The rogue looked at me and yelled,"EVAC."

I pounded my drums to the tune of a speedy song,
The rogue screamed in pain and then he was gone.

Alone,
I ran over the hills, I ran through the trees
I ran like a child with the Lord chasing me.
I made it to the gates, not a moment too soon,
The watchmen slayed the Lord, under the full moon.

The song records the memory of my friends,
And remember: the Lord cannot be killed by a group of Level 10s.

Watchman Veras
Bard of 10 Songs
