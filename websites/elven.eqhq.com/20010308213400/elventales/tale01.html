A Ranger's Battle
By Ordieth Lightblade

Well as I have walked the world of Norrath I have heard many stories of murderer's and thieves, and I have hunted many of them . . . I love the chase, becoming the hunter, its a ranger thing. Anyways I have heard enough about Rosealia that I would most definitely kill her on sight. I was strolling through Kithicor on my way to High Hold when I ran across her, I turned . . . she turned . . both our fists were red with fire at the same time . . . the fight began . . .

As she started to cast on me I rushed her with my bow and then bagged that and started hacking at the Halfling with my bladed thulian claws, she had magical barbs covering her body and I was constantly getting stuck in them as I fought to slash at her . . . she seemed to have magical shielding and healing properties around here, I would slash at her and cut her skin, but the wounds would disappear, I was starting to get worried as bolts of lightning started falling like rain, I quickly side stepped around them going at her with all my strength. I finally resorted to using a magic orb I had bought from a wizard friend of mine . . . he told me that if I were to use it upon an opponent that I would be assured victory, as I pulled it out she laughed at me and asked, "now what is that supposed to do? You gonna hurt me little boy . . . " I didn't even take notice to her taunts after that, I held the orb in my hand pointed at her chest . . . my hands started to glow and the orb began to shake, a flash of light blinded us both. when I could see again she was standing in front of me so I began to hack at her.

Apparently the orb had removed her magic shielding for now I was ripping at her flesh and she was so dazed she had no idea what was happening, she tried to cast spells but I would just rip new holes in her skin as she tried. Then she ran screaming, "NO how could this be!?! I could not have been beaten by the likes of you!" She thought she could just run from me but no, I snared her and followed behind pumping her full of arrows, she struggled to move as arrow after arrow pierced her arms and chest, finally I heard the sound of her skull crack as the last arrow drove its way into her head, leaving her pummeled body at my feet.

I had one, but I knew she would be back someday, druids of that power always come back, but I will be ready for her return and shall beat her again if that's what she wants. So once again I had hunted down one of Norrath's infamous criminals, but I had other pressing matters at hand, I must reach the caverns of Permafrost to find my friend Nwene, she sent a letter of distress to me asking me to join her in the Halas region immediately, I have just received word that she disappeared . . .

-Ordieth Lightblade
