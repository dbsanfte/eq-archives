Welcome, friend. You have entered the guildhall of the Elven Guard.

We are an anti-pk and roleplaying guild on the Rallos Zek server of the massively multiplayer online game Everquest.

We have a strong family aspect, and love hunting together and helping each other. If we ever see anyone in need, regardless of race, we will stop and try to help them. We do not kill on sight the "dark" races, as we believe Tunare loves everyone, and we must lead them in the right direction. They can never change, and embrace the Mother's love if we kill them. They cannot help what they were taught when they were young.

All members must speak in character except when fighting mobs. If they wish to speak out of character, they must first say ooc:. We encourage all of our members to rp differently, and make up their own character background stories; not to just copy the elves from Tolkien and such (although if you're not familiar with rping, his books are good to read).

Most of all, we encourage participation. All members must visit the board at least once a week, because we discuss many important matters there, as well as create polls.

The Guard does not simply hunt monsters all day. Sometimes, we enjoy just sitting down and talking to our friends, or sharing a drink in one of Kelethin's bars. We also enjoy helping younger elves (newbies), by healing and buffing them, or even giving them directions, or tips on how to play their class better.

We have members from all over the world, with timezones ranging from CST, MST, PAC, EST, to Europe times.

Our level range is currently 5-50. We only have one 50 though, as we do not encourage powergaming. We'd rather have a great 10th level rper than a 50th powergamer who doesn't know what rping is.

We frequent the ralloszek.net rp board, as we love planning many events and gatherings, and try to attend those of others as well.

We are also a member of NATO (Norrathian Alliance and Trade Organization), and attend all of their events.

Questions can be answered on our board, in-game, by ICQing me at 4912305, or by e-mailing me at Amaroqq@hotmail.com.

Be sure to visit all of our pages, listed on the nav bar.
