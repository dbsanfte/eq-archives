Structure

Amaroqq is our General and should be treated as such. He strives to make our Guild a powerful force in Norrath, thus everyone should respect him at all times. From time to time a difference of opinion may occur. When this happens take it to him personally. He is a reasonable Elf and will work with you as best he can. Everyone has the right to express his or her opinion so if you still disagree then I urge you to post it on the Private Forum. There is no need to publicize our private disagreements on the public board. Just remember that he has finial say on all maters and as devote Guardsmen we must follow his call.

Ranked Officers

Let me Start of by saying that Ranked Officers do not receive their rank because of their level. Instead, it is based on previous and current service, devoutness, honor, and leadership qualities. Despite the fact that some may hold a lower level than you is no reason to disrespect them. We work hard to do what we can to better this guild and make it better for everyone. Please give us your support at all times. As with General Amaroqq, if there is a disagreement then please take it to the individual first then to Amaroqq then post on the Private Board. A ranked Officer has the authority to give orders and they must be followed. An officer will never give an order unless absolutely necessary. Don’t worry about us trying to get you to “run laps” or “Hail them as Ambassador Narthain,” that simply isn’t our desire or purpose. We, like the General are here to help you, the Guild, and Norrath!

Guardsmen

New Guardsmen are important to the Guild and should be invited in when necessary. However, it is important that we get high quality people so as not to have to try and weed them out later. To do this it is important to ask several questions as well as observe them for a while. These questions should include.

1) Do you kos (kill on sight) murderers?

2) What do you do if you see an elf attacking an elf?

3) We have a military-esque structure with ranks. Would you listen to all superiors' orders and strive to attain promotions?

4) Would you visit our guildhall (website: http://elven.eqhq.com) at least once a week (and post on the board, where we discuss many important matters)?

5) ooc: Upon joining, will you post your character's background story at our guildhall (board)?

6) Would you help all good beings who need it, and not tarnish the Guard's good name?

7) ooc: We are a roleplaying guild, and expect all of our members to rp in public, and in the guildhall unless they say 'ooc:' first (we're a little more lenient when in a group hunting mobs). Would you accept this?

8) Where did you hear about us?

9) How do you feel about "dark" races?

If you know of someone that would be a fit guardsman please report them to Amaroqq, myself or the other officers for further questioning and observing. Also give us your opinion on them.

No one should ever be invited into the Guild if you have just met him or her for the first time. It is important to get a good view of the person before allowing them to join our ranks.

Guardsmen should spend much of their time protecting the people of Norrath, and roleplaying. It is also important to gain levels as well. (read high level post). A guardsmen is expected act as such. You must not do anything to gain us a bad view in the general public. We are here to protect them. Doing anything to give them a bad opinion of us is the last thing we want to do. Helping newbies is a great way to boost p.o of the Guard. Try to help anyone that asks. If you can’t then try to find someone who can. (try asking in /guildsay). I’m always willing to give free food or a bind to those who need it. It is my duty as a Guardsmen and it should be the same with everyone.
