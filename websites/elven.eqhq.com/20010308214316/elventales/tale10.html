Shrobuk's Tale
By Shrobuk

Hullo. If you libs in dem Grater Faydwur, den you problie nose me alreddie.

Mie nayme is Shrobuck, an dis is da stoarey abowt how ebryfing happened tew me, frum goingk on a bote, tew visitin a tree sitee, an going inside!

It all startid wen me wus a liddul Oger. I was so yung bak den. An I trustid a lot uv peepul dat I shoodint hab, I fot dey wus gude, but dey wus jus bad. Dese is Ogers like Zulort, Clurg, all da bownsurs, an all da Ogers. All ub dems. Well, sumday, dey is gunna lern dere lessin. Me tells dis stoarey lots, an ebrytime, it's a liddul bit diffrunt. Dis bekus Shrobuck habs bad mesmerie. Dis is alsoe wie Shrobuck mebbe wood furget name ub frends, or mebbe forget if him go huntin wid you.

Dis how dem stoarey starts. Wun day, Shrobuck wus huntin dem spydurs in da Fearot. Me just gottid dem lebel fife, so me fot me wude selumbrate. So, ta dew dis, me fot me wud eksploar a bit. I hab herd lots abowt dis spektur kayve, ware dere is evul, skaree monsturs. Me neber kared so mutch if me ded, me fownd me wood die lots, so I fot I wude eksploar. Wow, it wus skariest playse me hab eber seed, an I didunt ebin sees it, it jus FEELD skaree. But, me gottid kloase. Klose enuf tew see sumfin reelie stranje, I seed hoomins. At dis time, me not knowed wot a hoomin wos. Me onlie sees dat dey is smoll, but dey must hab beend tuff, kuz dey wus shoor gibbin out sum bashins tew da spekturs. Sum ub dem sayed: "Hullo, Shrobuck" an me wud say Hullo bak. Den, me buffs dem, kuz me lern dat is job. Me buff udders fer wen dey fites. Aftur me buffs dem, me gottid most fings I hab eber habbed in me life. I gottid sithes, an fiftean plattermums. Dis wos most me eber habbed. Aftur dat, me go bak tew Oggok, an bie me new speels. I gottid ebry spell dere wus dat me kud yoos. Den, I gottid armirs, an sum gude fude tew eets. But Zuloart, me gildmastur, he gabed me berie unyoshual luks wen me go ta git tranin. Den, he ses tew Shrobuck: "Shrobuk, ware you get all dese munies? Spydurs not gibs enuf munies fer dis."

So me say tew him "Zoolort, me gottid munies frum new frends. Dey wus smoll, but dey wus smashin big spekters."

He not liked dis at all. Him tells me, dat dese liddul wuns wus kalld hoomins, an dat dey wus emenies. So, he send me owt huntin agen, an says fer me tew not kome bak fer a wile, till me fink abowt it. So me finks abowt it. Me fink abowt fings, like: How kome hoomins is emenies? An how hoomins gibs me munies. So, wen me go bak, dere is lot ub Ogers watin fer me. Zoolort aks me tew kome owt, an tells ebrywun wot I finks ub hoomins now, dat me fot ub it fer a wile.

Well, me go up, an I em standin rite nekst tew Zoolort. An Zuloart ses tew me: "Okay Shrobuck, tells us wie we is goingk tew Freaporte tewnite ta bash hoomins."

I heers dis, but me not baleevs it.. all me life Zulort wus frend. But now, he wonts tew bsh hoomins.. kills dem. Even if dey dew nuffin rong. Well, me koodint lets dis happin, so me wus kwik tew finks up plan on how ta stop invayshun. I ses owt lowd: "No no no no no. You gots it all rong" I ses. "Hoomins not emenies, Hoomins is frends!! We shude be frends wif hoomins!"

I fot me wus so smart. I thot dat dat wood stops invayshun fer gude. But, me sune lern dat it not werk so well aftur all...

Zollort showts owt "WOT? ME TELLS YOU NOT TO LIKE HOOMINS! NOW LUKE AT YOU! YOU IS HOOMIN LUBBER!!!"

All da Ogers startid tew laffs at me, makin fun ub me, kuz ub wot Zoolort sayd. Dis make me feels nervus.. it is reel skaree bein laffd at bie a lot ub Ogers gettin reddie fer war, who gots dem klubs, an gots dem shinee armirs on.. dis skare me fer shoor...

Den da Ogers startid tew pulls me off ware me wus standin. Dey pull me down, an starts ta poosh me arownd, hittin me, an kallin me naymes.. dis wus wurstest time. Make me so skared.. me startid tew cry, but dey keeps hittin me, an pushin me. Dey chaysis me owt ub Oggok, an owt ub Fearot. Dey takes me munies, an says "Shrobuck! You is hoomin lubber! Not kome bak! We not wonts you heer no moar! GO bak tew Freaporte, das ware you balong!"

Eber sinse den, me wus loanley. I habbed tew sleeps in desurt. I not new ware Freaporte wus. I koddint foller da uther Ogers, kuz dey wood bash me.. I not ebin reelie new wot dis Freaporte wos. But, I figerd it wus only playse tew go.

Desurt is skaree playse, ebin in day times. Big spydurs, skellumtins, gools, madmen, pirats. Ebryfing hated Shrobuck, and ebryfing wus tuffir. But, me makes it froo. It wus tuff jurney, but me kood makes wadur wif me mojos. Wen me gottid tew Freaporte, it wos not wot me ekspekted. It wus jyant sitee, wif lots ub hoomins.. an lots ub ded Ogers layin arownd.. at furst me habbed tew hide, kus hoomins fot me wus like udder Ogers, but sune dey lern dat it bestust tew be frend wid Shrobuck. Me heels disees, an koor poisins, an heels, an buffs, an make ebrywun hapee. Sune ebin da gards wood lets me go intu da sitee. But most hoomins, it seemd like dey not like me tew mutch. Dey jus like me kuz ub me mojos. Dis wood makes me loanlee, I wontid frend, but not new ware tew gets gude frends.

Den, me heeld dis wun hoomin. But dere wus sumfin diffrunt. Dem eers. Dey wus pointee.I aks why da eers wos like dat, an she ses it bekus she not hoomn, but she is Ulf. Den she startid tew tells me abowt da tree sitee. She sed it wus as big as Freaporte, but dere wus elebaters, an brijis, an stoars, all up in trees! I koodint baleevs dis. So, me dasided tew go tew tree sitee, an sees it fer me sulf.

I gottid on bote, an wus on me way. Wen me furst git dere, I koodint see no tree sitee, ebin do me lookd reel hard, me not finds wun. So, eedur way, me not wonted to go bak ta Freaporte wen me ded, so me dasided to aks fer a binds, so dat dis Eye-land wood be hoam. Shoor enuf, sumwun kamed to hulps me. I aksed him ware dis tree sitee wus, an sed me wood wont tew git bowndid dere. He bowndid me dere.

After dat, me woks arownd. It feeld gude tew be dere. I heeld mutch Ulfs. Ulfs wus sune me frends. But den me notiss sumfin. Norf ub da tree sitee, dere wus dis playse. A kave, kalld Krushboans. I wentid dare, an seed orks atakin an Ulf. Da Ulf wus runnin, tryingk tew ekskape. But she falld down. Ork bash hur hard. Me wus kwik ta yoos me healin mojos. I heeld hur gude, she gottid up, an kept runnin. Orks fot she wus ded, but I fink she wus purtendin. Wen she wus runnin, me keepd heelin hur, an smashed orks. Aftur orks wus ded, she say how she neber for Oger wood hulps hur. Den me say dat me is gude Oger. An me wood hulps frends, an not let frends git hurt. Aftur me hulps mennie Ulfs, an dies tryin tew hulps, sumwun aks me if me wontid tew be afishul Ulfin Gard. Dis was me bestust momint, bekus me seed Ulfin gards bafore, an how ebrywun likes dem. So, me joins Ulfin gards. But dis, was jus ware ebryfing gottid so komplikatud, but, das anudder stoarey.

So, heer me is. I libs neer ork lift, an ebry mornin wen me wakes up, me say hullo tew all da Ulfs arownd. An Ulfs says hullo bak. So Shrobuck happy. Him habs frends agen. FOr da furst time sinse me wus aloan in desurt, me gots frends hoo says hullo tew me. So me happie agen. Sumday, wen me is a bit tuffer, I em goingk bak tew Oggok. But dis time, dey not gonna kiks me owt. Kuz I em gunna brings me frends wif me. An we gunna show dem how me feeld.
