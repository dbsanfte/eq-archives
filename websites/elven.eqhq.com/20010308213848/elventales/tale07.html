A GentleElf's Duel
By Gildurath

Today as I walked through Greater Faydark. I heard the shout of a Dark Elf named Zargath, seeking to be bound to the area, that we call our home. I yelled for our brethren not to bind this soul as we did not know if he brought us harm. I shout that he should prove himself before he asks for such kindness from our people. Being as we had just been invaded by his dark brethren. So he throws out a duel to me. He demands one on one combat for the right to hunt in our forest. He also states that he does not mean anyone here harm, he only wishes to hunt bandits. The conditions for the duel where as follows:

He wins: I will always seek him a bind when he visits Faydwer.
I win: He will never again hunt on Faydwer.
We also agree to loot each others leggings.

As the battle commenced we did exchange many blows. And all faired well for me until I felt his harming touch. I did feel the life leak from me to the very earth I fought to defend from his kind. I was left very weak after this attack. So, I brought forth the forest magic, that I have learned, to bring roots from the ground to hold him. Once they had secured him I let lose a few arrows, sinking them deep into his armor. He broke the roots and rushed me once again. We slashed and dodged and dealt each other blow after blow. Until finally he had no more life to spare. He slumped to the ground, in a heap. The battle was over, my life was also very nearly over, but I manage to maintain a grip on life.

After being healed by some passerby's who caught the end of the duel, I struggled back to Greater Faydark. Where I announce my victory and the conditions that would be meet.

Shortly after I received word from Zargath that he would respect the condition. He displayed very honorable traits for a Dark Elf and it saddened me to see him banished but he insisted that he leave, as the agreement had been, for surely he will be killed by his own race for being honorable.

I must sadly inform everyone that due to my grave injuries, during this duel, I will be in bed healing for about a week. I hope to be back to full health in time to help in the matter of the book. Good luck to all!!

Gildurath
