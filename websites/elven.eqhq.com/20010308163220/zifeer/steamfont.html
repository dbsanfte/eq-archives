Zifeer's Travels        

Wanderings in…Steamfont

(After a brief spell of Thepa-induced madness, I’m glad to be getting back to what I do best…wandering. Watch out for the Glowing-Blue-Eyed fiend though, he’s a tricky one!)

As the wanderer leaves the gloom of Lesser Faydark, the air clears and takes on a sulphurous whiff. Soon the valley opens up to a high, blasted volcanic plateau, broken by pillars of steam, from where this land gets its name.

Perhaps it is these vents of raw earthen power that attracted the Gnomes to this place, but what ever the reason, this land is now firmly under the yoke of Gnomish technology. This is mountainous terrain, and navigating through the twisted valleys can be tricky at best. Fortunately for the wanderer, this land is crossed by several well-maintained and easy to follow trails, leading to most of the important places of interest.

The area provides hunting for many levels of ability, ranging from the diseased rats, to the strange malfunctioning mechanical spiders, and there are several convenient shops and rest stops around. Kobolds operate in this area, striking at wanderers from numerous camps dotted around the mountains, beware! Also watch out for the steam vents that are scattered about the land. A Gnome once explained to me how these "Geo-thermos-vents" worked, but just as I was starting to go glassy-eyed, she distractedly fell down one, wedging herself in it. I tugged and tugged at her, but fifteen seconds later there was a shriek, a whistle and a pop. I waited for half an hour, but when she still hadn’t landed, I gave her up as lost. Now there was a true wanderer…

The Ring of Steamfont: To the south of the land, on the southern edge of a massive dormant crater, lies the Ring of Steamfont, the destination of the Druid spell of the same name. There is a helpful young druid here who will buy and sell most nik-naks, but most importantly, for me at least, high level Druid spells can be found here. Do not be surprised to see druids appearing out of thin air here. Also, this is a good place to hunt Rogue Clockworks, mechanical helpers gone "off the rails" which can be pried open to reveal Microservos, a vital part of the Trueshot Bow.

Ak’anon Gate: The entrance to the underground city of the Gnomes is difficult to miss, the chimneys and strange cogs are easily visible from a long way off. As all wanderers know, it is important to check how well the guards of a town will receive you before approaching, and this is no exception. I have heard tales of Wood Elven Rangers, who are slain on sight here, yet I find I am allowed to pass without hindrance. Check from a distance.

(Also, try not to get too mesmerized by the bubble-tanks outside. I was once stood there staring at the bubbles going up and down, up and down, for twenty minutes before a helpful Gnome guard gently pulled me away. To this day, I find entering Ak’anon is a struggle.)

The Windmills: Incredible feats of Gnomish engineering which serve no apparent purpose (Gnomish Fizzics tends to send me Glassy-Eyed very quickly), these large towers sport massive whirling blades and are a useful landmark. Roughly occupying the center of the plateau, they are a good place to rest and restock, assuming you are welcomed here. I am also told that this is perhaps one of the only places on Norrath where Tinkering supplies may be obtained.

(The ingenuity of the Gnomes is at once a source of awe and worry for me. A while back, I heard that they had successfully constructed a tower capable of putting Gnomes on the moon! As a wanderer, I can’t wait until they turn their attention to the inordinate wait for boats at the docks of Butcherblock. One fellow once showed me a plan for something he called a "Hover-craft", but it made little sense to me. However, long before the evil magics of the Teir Dal can bring the world to it’s knees, the Gnomes will have invented a bomb big enough to destroy it all anyway!)

The Minotaur Caves: To the north of the land are a series of caves, similar in design to Ak’anon. They are easy to spot, just look for the huge cogs lying around outside. This popular hunting location is one of the sources of the much-vaunted Minotaur Axe, so expect to queue here, and watch out for the skeletons around this area, they can be quite fierce.

(Personally, I think you can’t do better than a good stout staff)

Possibly the abandoned ruins of an evil power-mad Gnome, this area is, to my mind, the number one candidate for the "You’ve ruined your own lands, you’ll not ruin mine!" award for bad land management. It’s a mess.

The Dragonbones: To the Northeast lies an immense skeleton, half buried in the volcanic mud. This is, I guess, the remains of a Dragon. (This is also the closest I want to get to one) The evil magics of the beast linger on after death and many skeletons now attend the long fallen creature.

(And people go looking for live ones!? On purpose!?!)

Onward travel is limited, either into the underground city of Ak’anon, or back, to Lesser Faydark’s gloom and fairies.

And on Rallos Zek…

As with any training area for the young, this land has its fair share of murders and retribution. The Gnomes are typically less numerous than many other races, (Which accounts for the large numbers of clockwork servants around the place I guess), so this place is rarely as busy as Kelethin. The Gnomes, as a whole, are a more neutral race than most, neither overtly evil, or good, and some Gnomes conduct the art of Necromancy, an art which takes a supreme effort of will to turn into a tool for good. Having said that, each Gnome can, and will, pick out his own path.

For the foreign visitor, things can be a little hit and miss. Not so long ago, a full-scale state of war existed between the Gnomes and the Elves, and although now history, the occasional raiding party from one or the other serves to hamper the peace. The Elven wanderer must be careful here. Disease is a major problem here though, transmitted by the various corrupted creatures of the region, and a Druid can do much to dispel hostility by offering his services in healing and curing.

Wanderers of other races are a rare enough sight this far out to mean that there is no real generalization in place, (Or at least none I have seen), and typically they will be ignored unless they cause trouble, although shouted comments about lawn ornaments are generally A Bad Idea.

Those wishing to grab a haul of Minotaur Axes should be careful, and go with a party they trust, since it is far, far easier to liberate an axe from a battered and weary adventurer, than a Minotaur!

Nearest Wizard Gate – Greater Faydark

Nearest Druid Ring – Steamfont

Nearest Bind – Ak’anon

I tend to hurry through here, but if you catch the dreaded Rat-itis, give me a shout!

Zifeer the Wanderer
