Casualty in the Family
A Letter to Gildurath
By Grifterr

Tis a horrid letter I must write you Gildurath. And the only way I know how to tell you is by starting at the beginning.

It was early in the eve and I was training in the Butcherblock, slaying goblins. Although these beasts were not trouble for a seasoned warrior, they did cause problems among some of those just beginning their training. I was doing my share to clear the path from Kaladim to the Greater Faydark for them and, at the same time, learn more parrys with my blade.

After slaying a goblin, I cast camouflage upon myself and walked back to the guard shack. As I approached, I heard a low voice asking around for a Wood Elf named Grifterr! I rounded the corner and in front of me I saw a large barbarian. Judging by the size of his axe and the way he held it, I knew him to be a seasoned warrior and his skill to be greater than mine.

One of the guards eyed the barbarian and told him that he saw me heading towards Kaladim not long ago. He also told the barbarian that if any harm befell me, he would have the barbarians head. Behind my mask of camouflage, I grinned. I had only been in the Butcherblock for a few days but in that time, aside from training, I had aided many warriors, most with more skill than myself, with heals and buffs and almost even gave my life for a poor dwarf that happened upon a goblin wizard. I attacked the beast, knowing it would slay me but it gave the dwarf a chance to escape. Tunare (or lady luck) must of been smiling upon me that day, though, for I made it back to the guard shack when the goblin wizard knocked me unconscious. The guards slayed the beast and I soon regained consciousness.

The barbarian told the guard he wished me no harm... but the way he clutched his axe made me think differently. He thanked the guard and began to walk towards Kaladim. On his way there, he saw a large scarab and began to do battle with it. When the dust had settled, the barbarian had won, but his life was low. Now was my chance.

Mana crackled at my fingertips as I uttered the ancient words and my fingers made the correct symbols. The barbarian turned to face me just as large roots reached up from the ground and held him in place. I began to chant again as my hands burst with flame... the spell was at my lips... all I had to do was to make the correct symbols and the barbarian would burst into flames.

"What do you wish of me?" I shouted to the barbarian. I knew in a fair fight, he could take me easily. But, after battling that beast and rooted far away where I could cast spells upon him at will...

"Ahhh, you must be Grifterr," the barbarian said to me. "I am Kromme and I have word for you from your cousin Anduril. He bid that I take the trip to you myself."

I knew something was amiss. Anduril would either send a scroll to me or reach me with that infernal skill of telepathy he is always bombarding me with (and usually during a good game of Acromage!). I let the mana seep from my hands and the flames died away.

"What is wrong? Is he... dead?"

"Nay, but he is close to it, I fear."

"What happened?"

"Let us walk back to the guard shack where there is light inside. I fear I do not have the ability to see well at night as do you elves and I fear I would get lost easily here... I can hardly see my own feet as it is," he says with a smirk.

Leading the way, I bring him back to the guard shack. The guards look me over, making sure no harm has come to me as Kromme and I enter the shack. We both take a seat in the back portion of it.

"Let me begin...

I was walking back from the SplitPaw Lair and had made it to the end of the West Commons when I felt my feet being grabbed. I looked to them to see they were rooted, much like what you had done. I withdrew my axe and looked around, trying to see who had done such a thing to me. Just a few moments later, four dark elves were upon me.

"Prepare to face your god, barbarian," one of them said, who I would later know to be Ghenocide, a necromancer.

Just as mana began to form at two of the dark elves hands, I grabbed my pumice stone from my pack and used it to release my feet from bondage. As soon as they were free, my axe slashed into the one known as Ghenocide, halting his spell. I quickly turned to the other one and did the same. By this time, though, the two warriors (or perhaps Shadow Knights, I am unsure as they cast no spells) joined the fray. All appeared to have less skill than me, but with their combined strengths, I felt my life begin to slip from me. Just as I thought I was to die, a flash of white appeared from behind the dark elves, cutting its way through them to me.

I felt the darkness begin to soar over me just as I heard the words "Lightbringer, grant me the strength". No sooner were those words uttered than my life was partially restored. I looked to see a High Elf hovering over me, obviously a Paladin. I stood up but as I did, the High Elf pushed me back.

"Run now," he told me. "This is not a fight we can both win."

Just as I was about to argue, the dark elves launched their assault again. The Paladin, who I would come to know as Anduril Valar, pushed me back again as he rushed into them. Stunned, I just stood there for a moment and then hid behind a nearby tree. Watching him fight was almost like a dance. His blade slashed at those with magic as his shield saved him from the blows from the melee fighters.

Using precious mana, he cast the flash of light spell upon the two warriors and continued upon the casters, slicing them down. He slaughtered the first, but before he could finish killing Ghenocide, the two warriors recovered.

Ghenocide ran to the side, fearing for his life and the other warrior stayed near him, protecting him. This left Anduril and the other warrior standing at one another. The Dark Elf charged him and as he did, he said "You are no match for Neriak's finest warrior! I have been trained and developed to be the finest of my species... to excel in combat!"

Just as the Dark Elf brought down his blade, Anduril parried it with his own and bashed the Dark Elf in the face, sending him to the ground.

"Your strength is considerable 'dark cousin', and while your appearance is not familiar to me," Anduril said, bringing down his scimitar that the Dark Elf was barely blocking, "your defeat at my hands will become all to familiar to you!"

The other warrior then rushed to join the fight. The first one still talking as Anduril and him exchanged blows. "That you are still standing at all marks you as a worthy opponent. But it is the matter of your imminent defeat that should concern you now, High Elf!"

The other Dark Elf ran behind Anduril, bringing up his blade to strike him in the back, but without losing a move, Anduril swung his shield behind him, bashing this Dark Elf in the face, too, sending him to the ground. By the effects of it, I could tell that this Dark Elf was no where near as strong as his companion who faced Anduril. My life was nearly whole again. I could hide in the shadows no longer. Grabbing my axe again, I rushed to the battle and began my own battle with the Dark Elf who tried to stab Anduril in the back.

As I continued my fight, I could still hear Anduril and the Dark Elf exchanging words as they fought. Just by that alone I could tell that Anduril was a Paladin and his combatant a Shadow Knight. Only these two types of warriors have I ever known to be as deadly with the tongue as a blade during battle.

"Nothing would give me more pleasure than to continue this challenge," Anduril boasted as he brought down his scimitar hard and fast, "but there is no challenge where your defeat is concerned, Drow!"

Anduril then bashed his shield into the Dark Elf's stomach and brought his blade down again, the words coming from his mouth as he did so. "The beginning of my existence means an end to yours, evil one!"

But the battle was taking too long. As Anduril moved in to make the death blow, mana surged around him, stealing some of his life... Ghenocide had rejoined the battle. Turning his attention back to the fallen Shadow Knight, Anduril administered the deathblow and turned back to Ghenocide just as he cast another spell upon him.

Anduril sat there for a moment, gaining strength but never ceasing to talk. "Mithaniel Marr warned me that you would be formidable foes. Great in power and in numbers..."

"You sure are full of yourself, Paladin," Ghenocide snickered.

"... But let there be no doubt! You will fall like those before you did! I will not rest until you have been defeated!" And with that, Anduril grabbed a throwing axe from his bag and stuck it in Ghenocide's chest... not killing the necromancer but injuring him deeply. Anduril went to administer the final blow upon Ghenocide when he noticed that the warrior I faced had gotten the better of me.

Anduril came to my side and saw my life deathly low again. He called upon the Lightbringer again and a flash of bright light surrounded me. Unlike last time, my life came to full health. As he had done so, the Dark Elf warrior stuck his sword through Anduril's gut. Anduril dropped his shield, grabbing the blade that was protruding from his stomach as his sword swiftly moved, removing the Dark Elf's head from his body.

Anduril fell to his knees as he reached the sword from behind him, pulling it out as he held in screams of pain. Just as he started to stand up, evil mana attacked his body again. I looked up to see Ghenocide not far from us. Grabbing my axe, I attacked the fiend. It only took a few blows until Ghenocide decided to show me the better side of valor and flee with his life.

I returned to Anduril's side as I saw the life slipping from him. I bound his wound as well as I could but my skill in such things is not very good. I could keep him from death, but little more. I lifted him over my shoulder and quickly made my way to Qeynos.

Once I reached the city, I brought him to the Paladin's guild, who's care he is under now. His injuries are extensive and, truth be told, no one is sure if he will make it. He comes in and out of a deep sleep and during one of those times, he spoke to me. He told me of you and how he wished you to have his blade and armor and to use it well until he returns to you.

"He said that you must continue the fight... to fight for the just and the innocent."

With the words spoken, I was dumbfound. I was no where near the warrior as Anduril. And I had just begun my training as a druid. How could he do this to me? How?

But as those words crossed my mind, I felt very ashamed. Anduril had always fought for the betterment of everyone else, not for himself. And he may have given his life for what he deemed the most honorable way... his life for another.

I stood up as did Kromme. From his back he withdrew a shiny scimitar and handed it to me. I have heard barbarians are the coldest of the human races, even more so than the Erudites, but I saw tears form in this barbarian's eyes. He knew it too, Anduril possibly gave his life for him and it was not a debt that could be repaid.

"Use his blade well and with honor," Kromme said to me. I took the blade and felt a surge of pride fill me. I was not the warrior Anduril was... I was not as honorable as him... I do not even have the way of words as he did... and I may never be... but I would do him proud. I would protect all those who are innocent or treated unjustly. I would not attack anyone just deemed on their skin or race alone. Anduril said it best... Vengeance must be served.

With that done, I walked Kromme back to the docks for he had to leave back for home. I would join him on his trip and visit Anduril but I know that is not what Anduril would want. My place for now is on the Faydwer, protecting my homelands.

So, Gildurath, that is why I wrote this letter to you. Anduril may never be back, although I pray to Tunare nightly that he will return to us. In the meantime, he cannot run for office in the Elven Council of Faydark nor serve in the Elven Guard. I am not a politician and never will be but, for all other purposes, I will serve in his place. If you ever have need of me, you have but to utter my name and I shall be there. I have stepped up my training and will assist you by whatever means necessary for us to regain this "book".

May Tunare help us all.

Grifterr
