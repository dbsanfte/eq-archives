Zifeer's Travels        

Wanderings in…Kelethin

Ahead, through the mist come the shouts of laughter and merriment, and the occasional fight. As we approach, we can see glowing lanterns, casting warm colors through the thinning fog, illuminating it with their cheery glow. Suddenly, above us is revealed a marvel of natural engineering, for above, nestling in the mighty trees of Greater Faydark, is Kelethin, the Tree-City.

Kelethin is my home, and that of all Wood Elves, and though my travels often keep me away, sooner or later this is the one place where I am guaranteed to turn up. Whilst part of the land of Greater Faydark, it really does deserve it’s own, more detailed mention. To reach it, follow the trail from the Butcherblock Mountains, all the way around, continuing past Felwithe, to it’s end.

The first thing that confronts the visitor, are the lifts. Intricate pieces of clockwork jiggery-pokery, as a child, I would play on them all day, until The Accident that is. (Which I still don’t like to talk about, thank-you.) I suspect their design, at least is in some part Gnomish, but to those who are unused to them, their operation is simple. Press the button on the top of the post, wait, climb aboard the platform, press the button again, wait and disembark. Seems simple, but I have seen no-end of troubles while on Healing Duty at the Orc Lift, including, bizarrely enough, people actually falling through the lift platform itself, sometimes fatally.

(Oh, and please give your fellow passengers adequate time to get off, I was once on the "Pod" lift for three hours, and felt quite ill toward the end.)

The first and most obvious thing the visitor will notice about Kelethin, is its altitude. One wrong drunken step can kill. Naturally, Wood Elf children grow up agile, or not at all! In particular, watch your step on the "down" ramps, since they are not easy to spot until you are on them, and forgetting which edge of a platform is which, is a Bad Thing.

(Having said that, one of the more popular entertainments in Kelethin, particularly during the Festival of Rising Sap, is to watch visiting casters apparently "leap" from the highest platform of the city, only to cast Levitate halfway down, much to the wonder of the watching crowd. The current record for this is about three feet from impact. Well, technically, it’s two feet after impact, but anyone can do that! My advice: Don’t volunteer to jump on behalf of another caster in the hope they will save you.)

The city has a somewhat confusing layout, designed not in the two dimensional way familiar to most city-dwellers, but in three, and some places can appear to be in the same place on some maps, when in fact, they are above and below. The best way to remember the way to a particular destination, I find, is by turnings. For example:

Left, Straight Across: The way from the "Pod", or Priest of Discord Lift to the Main or "Newbie" Lift.

(The Priest of Discord really is a most depressing chap. Occasionally I talk to him, just to cheer him up, but its hard work. He keeps banging on about how nobody needs him, and how that, on other worlds, his kind holds positions of extreme importance. I just nod and smile, trying not to look too bored. If you are young and still have your Tome of Discord, be a sport and hand it over to the poor chap, it’ll make no difference to you, but might just cheer him up a bit.)

Once you get a few landmarks down, the rest is easy, just don’t run until you are used to it. Kelethin has many of the facilities you might find in other great cities, with a few exceptions.

Wood Elf magic is based around the nurturing power of Tunare, so the visiting Enchanter, Wizard, Magician and Necromancer will find no guild or supplies here. (In fact the visiting Necromancer will have a hard time getting up the lift with his head still where it should be!)

Also, all Wood Elven Clerics, are in fact Druids, and our Paladins are Rangers. Our temple is the forest itself, so visiting Clerics and Paladins will need to make do with what our own guilds can offer.

Kelethin has many points of note, (All directions are from the top of the "Pod" Lift) including:

The Orc Lift (Right, Across, Right, Left)

This is where the "action" is, most nights. This lift leads down to a small trail over a low hillock in to the valley of the Orc Camps. I sometimes plant my roots here and help wounded passers by with healing and enhancements. (This area is more fully discussed in "Greater Faydark")

The Bank (Straight Across, Up Left):

A vital service to all wanderers, it is unwise to lug huge bags of platinum around with you, bandits are everywhere these days. The bank is also used as a meeting ground for trades and as a general hub of the city, since everyone knows where it is.

(I talked to the Bank Manager about considering larger premises, since often I will enter this tiny building and find myself having to queue for hours. I suggested one of the large empty buildings toward the north of the city, but he seemed reluctant to move. Indeed, he only moves when physically attacked, which then causes no end of disruption.)

A lot of people add to the crowding problem by sleeping here as well. I prefer somewhere a little quieter to be honest.

The Soldiers of Tunare (Across, Up Left, Down Right, Down Across)

This is my guild, the Druids. Here the young druid can find everything he or she needs to get started. The merchants here stock all Druid spells up to fourth rank (level 14) but after that we are required to hunt down our own spells from across the world.

(Anyone having trouble with these, let me know. I’ve already wandered through most of the Druid Circles, despite only being in the third circle myself. It pays to think ahead.)

Also, this is where the Heartwood Master presides, our guildmaster. He is the only person in Kelethin, who can train young druids in new skills, and it is to him, novice druids must first report.

(I think he’s not too impressed with me, on the whole. We got off to a bad start from day one, when, upon being asked to fetch him various rusty bladed weapons, I just said, "But…" and pointed out of the window to the smithy next door. Since then I have only been back to collect spells as necessary, and have carried out far more work for the Bard’s Guild than my own.

I think he almost disapproves as much of my aimless wandering, as he does of the alarming gambling habit of another druid of my acquaintance. I’m an excellent wanderer, but I fear, a mediocre druid.)

Faydark’s Champions (Across, Up Left, Down Right)

Not, as the name might suggest, a band of mercenaries, but in fact, the Rangers Guild. It is here that the young Ranger begins his career. Also, their guildmaster is the sole maker of the fabled Trueshot Bow Stave, and part of a young Rangers initiation is to assemble the components for this stave.

(I had a go at this, since the job description implied a great deal of travelling. I definitely recommend it for the novice wanderer, it gets you out and about, but on triumphantly returning from my epic journey, was somewhat miffed to just get a stick out of the deal. I assume a Master Fletcher would know what to do with it, but mine was too heavy, so I slung it out. I’ll note the locations in my other travels, keep your eyes open, Rangers!)

Emerald Warriors: (Right)

The Warriors guild of Kelethin, it is to this guild that the Guards of Kelethin also technically belong, so don’t upset these people. To be honest, I’ve never seen the attraction of being a warrior, to the Wood Elf, when either the path of the Druid or Ranger is available, but for the visiting warrior; this is where you can find training, supplies and safety. (Assuming of course your standing with them is good. Actually, thinking about it, you wouldn’t have made it up the lift if it weren’t!)

These people are also charged with keeping Kelethin’s food stocks up, and I’ve often hunted for shark meat for them. (Like fishing, but only YOU are the bait!)

(Note: Don’t confuse this with Emerld’s Warriors (a.k.a. The Blood Coven), which is something altogether different! That is not a mistake you’re likely to be able to make twice!)

The Scouts of Tunare: (Right, Across, Left, Right Up, Across Up, Right, Across)

Nestling high in a quiet area of the city, are the so-called "Scouts of Tunare" What they ‘scout’ I have no idea, but as far as I can tell, this is little more than a Rogues Guild a den of pickpockets and thieves. They style themselves as lovable bandits and dashing heroes, and on the whole, I suppose their intentions are good, but they are not above common crime and even murder if necessary. Watch your pouch when in this part of the city.

Songweavers (Left)

This is the Bard’s Guild, another popular profession among the Wood Elves. It is here instruments and songs may be found. Unfortunately it is also here a great number of very inexperienced bards can be found too, and some nights, with favorable wind, the cacophony generated by so many bad bards practicing can be heard from Ak’non!

(This is my own private theory as to how the recent Gnome-Elf Wars came about, the Gnomes simply came around and asked if the Bards could keep the racket down, which the Bards ignored. Hostilities escalated from there. Funnily enough, the nearer High Elves never complain, although I have seen them on occasion, gathered thoughtfully, stroking their chins, as they contemplate the hidden meaning within what they call "The Strange Ethereal Music From The Outer Spheres")

This building also acts as the Antonican League of Bards, Faydwer Regional Depot, receiving mail from all around our continent, sorting it and dispatching onward letters to Freeport. Whenever I travel to Antonica, I always carry a bag for them, the money is good and I’m going that way anyway. Talk to Jakum for inbound letters and Idia for Freeport bags.

Taverns: (Hell, try just about any old platform!)

Kelethin has possibly the largest number of taverns and inns of any city on Norrath, (Although I shall be attempting to prove this statement with fact, as soon as possible. It’s a tough job, but…), so finding a place to relax and unwind is not difficult. Despite this, Kelethin is strangely short of any kind of brewing supplies. Imports however, continue to get through, although my advice would be to not get too drunk, bearing in mind you are fifty feet above the ground with no handrails in sight. If you can find it open, try the Tapped Barrel, to where there are links elsewhere on these pages.

Elsewhere: Onward travel from Kelethin consisted of descending from the Tree City, in a controlled manner if possible, and picking up one of the trails in Greater Faydark, below.

And on Rallos Zek…

The city of Kelethin is perhaps, after Freeport, one of the busiest in the world and sometimes over a hundred adventurers are present here, or below in Greater Faydark. The air is alive with shouting and barter, and this sometimes makes it difficult to be heard. If it’s important, make sure you repeat yourself.

City Etiquette seems somewhat more lax here than elsewhere, and although rare, one can sometimes see battles atop the trees. The frequent Guard patrols ensure they never go on for long though. In spite of this, Kelethin is on the whole safe from murderers, most of whom are too preoccupied scurrying to the bank with today’s loot to bother anyone.

The presence of so many souls sometimes plays havoc with the magic of the lift mechanisms, and occasionally people end up falling through these devices, to their deaths. (OOC: Watch out, bad lag can drop you through them, wait until it’s quieter!)

Pretty anyone with any skill can be found for hire here, most times, so don’t be afraid to ask.

Foreign wanderers are generally welcome, but, as ever, check the guards at the base of the lifts before attempting to ascend. Dark wanderers will have a harder time of it, since the Kelethin Guards are only marginally more forgiving than those of Felwithe, and usually agree on the matter of "undesirables", approach with care. Consider perhaps Crushbone to the North instead.

Nearest Wizard Gate – Greater Faydark

Nearest Druid Ring – Steamfont or Butcherblock

Nearest Bind – Greater Faydark

If you need to find somewhere in particular up here, give me a shout, but watch your step, it’s a long way down!

Zifeer the Wanderer
