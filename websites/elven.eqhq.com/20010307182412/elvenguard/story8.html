Last night, I decided to travel to Odus and try and view the famed hidden city of the Heretics. After finding the entrance to their city, I was able to sneak around their guards and examine their young citizens hunting grounds. I was also able to get some drawings of myself fishing in their strange pond off to the side of the grounds. Soon I got bored, so I decided to try and enter the city proper. After clicking on a strange rock, I realized that it required a key, and I wasn't holding it. Digging into my pack for some Teir'dal Jerky, I found my seldom used lockpicks. "What the hell", I thought. "The don't do me any good anyhow." So, I placed the picks in the lock, and lo and behold, the tumblers tumbled, and I had access to the city. I immeadiatly hid myself again, and entered the lift to their city. After sneaking past several guards, I was able to make it to an interesting pub that had several dancing and singing skeletons. There were also ghosts, skeleton guards, and a very interesting shop named "Sinfully Handsome" with some of the finest clothes I've ever seen.

After exploring the city further and making several drawings, I once again picked the lock and left the city. I needed a place to rest, so I stealthily made my way to an apartment overlooking the hunting grounds to camp. However, I should have just left Paineel all together. I dropped my guard, and was immeadiatly hit for a great deal of damage (ooc: 137 per hit, what's up with that???) several times by a skeleton guard at least 2 levels below me. Through 4 walls no less. Truly magical guards, I must say. SO, although Paineel was interesting, I must now reclaim my corpse.
