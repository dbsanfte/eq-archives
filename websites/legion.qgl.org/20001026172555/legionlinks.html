EverQuest Australia: An Australian EverQuest news site.

EverQuest NZ: A New Zealand EverQuest news site.

EverQuest: The Official EverQuest site.

EQ Atlas: A great page for maps of many of the zones in EQ.

AlaKazam's Magical Realm: A great site for quest and item info.

EQ'Lizer: Another great site for quest and item info. Also has class guides and spell lists.

EQVault: A large EQ news site. Also has lots of EQ specific information, editorials and guides.

EverLore: A Great info site where you can add your own info. EverLore has one of the best bestiarys out there.

EverQuest Stratics: Another large news site, similar to EQVault and just as good.

EQ Castersrealm: Brilliant site for both Int. and Wis. Casters.

Questor of Norrath: This is definitely the best map site out there. Unfortunately, it is mostly in Japanese.

Bilge Hunter: A site that attempts to track down and discover the elusive rare spawns of Norrath.

Mythiran Tower: The definitive resource for EQ research information. If you are a magic user and need to know how and when to research your new spells, this is the place to go. It has lists of information for each class and several well used message boards where you can discuss your problems and concerns.

Illia's Beastiary: A compilation of all of the Creatures and NPC's in the game, with information on where they spawn, what they drop, what level they are and more, collected into a fast and easy to use database.

Crossroads of Norrath: Another news and info site.

The Dojo: Monk site.

Paladins of Norrath: Paladin site.

EQ Clerics: Cleric site.

EQ Necro's: Necromancer site.

EQ Druids: Druid site.

Rangers Glade: Ranger site.
