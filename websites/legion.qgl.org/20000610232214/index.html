Hail Adventurer! Rest your weary feet and open your ears and eyes to what we have to say. We are the Southern Legion, dedicated to adventuring and travelling for the benefit of the mind and body throught the realm of Norrath. Friends, fear us not as we are dedicated to help wherever help is needed. But I warn foes, may the gods help you if you haplessly turn on one's fellow adventurer or traveller. And may the greater good protect you if you attack a guild member! The Lands that our spirits hail from are from another dimension, a place during the Elder Age where the Ogres of Rallos Zek invaded. Our spirits hail from the Plane of Earth, and from the Earth we once battled the Ogre Hordes untill we were forced to flee. We turned our voices to the heavens to give us aid in our darkest hour. It was Tarew Marr who answered our call, who carried our spirits to the place of Norrath to start a new life and to continue our fight with those bound to evil. We are especially driven by the rage of our homelands being taken apart by the lesser bipedals. Thus our souls may not rest in peace in these lands. Thus our commitment to each aid other, and to the demise of the evils that plague Norrath.

I would just like to add this, we are here to enjoy our new lives. And though we are made up of all races, we have travelled from the same lands. Please remember this, or all that we do means nothing.

Lanladan
High Elf Paladin
