I
To explore the unknown. To face the dangers with strength of steel and power of spell and good companions at one's side. To never let one's companions die a useless death, and to never die a useless death yourself.

II
To give aid to fellow guild members without expectation of reward. To never let a guild member stand in confusion or frustration with the world. To give aid to those that ask, without putting fellow members in danger.

III
To enjoy oneself, for without this all the above means nothing.
