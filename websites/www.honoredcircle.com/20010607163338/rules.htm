Guild Rules
1.  We are NOT a guild of kill-stealers and we will not allow members to give us a reputation as such!  Your actions, believe it or not, reflect upon the guild.  If you are having problems, there's other places to hunt, try to work the problem out with the other party or you can just ask the guild to help you.

2.  If you need the assistance of a higher end guild member (teleports, resurrections, etc) and they say they are busy, DO NOT harass them!  We are all here to help our fellow guild mates, as well as have fun playing the game!  It's very frustrating when you wait 3 hours to get your turn in a group somewhere to then have someone ask you to drop everything and help them.  Don't get me wrong, as guild members we will help everyone as much as we can, but if they are busy please be understanding.  Sometimes you just have to loot that corpse or ride that boat.

3.  No one is going to be expected to contribute to the guild.  No guild fees (why anyone would want to be in a guild like that is beyond me) or and contributions of any kind will be necessary.  If you wish to donate things to the guild, go right ahead.  On the same token, if you are going to sell something to a guild member, please keep in mind that it's a fellow guild mate that you're selling to.  You will not be required to see below fair market value, but you will be asked to not ask for an outrageous price either.  If you wish to give money to the guild for plane raids or cleric peridot or other components please give them to Mahari or Clara as they spends huge amounts of money on the gems.  Or who ever is leading the current raid.  Remember hate and sky stones cost 230ppish, peridot costs 10ppish.

4.  If you would like a Guild member to make you something you will be required, at a minimum, to provide the materials needed to make the item.  Unless that guild member is willing to provide the items for you, there will be NO exceptions to this rule.

5.  Consideration to others is our biggest rule.  Remember, everyone is here to play a game and to have fun.  Arguing and fighting with guild mates has torn many guilds apart.  There is NOTHING in this game that cannot be worked out in a civil manner.  The Honored Circle Officers are there with you and there to resolve any and all disputes.  Remember, your guild is your family and we all care about each other.  Fights may happen and if they do please talk it over.

6.  Loots isn't our number one reason to do something. Number one is FUN.  Loot is secondary. Remember that on your next raid.  Also remember that even though you can use something doesn't mean you need it.  If you have a better item already let one of your fellow guild mates get the first item.  

7.  Women are always right. Not sure I really need to say this but though Id put it in here anyway.  Don't argue you'll only lose.

8.  For a raid.  BE ON TIME.  Don't show up an hour late and expect everyone to just drop everything and come get you.  You were late they weren't.  If this means you can go so be it.  If possibly try and show up 30mins or so early to a raid start time, that is if you can make the start time because of the earlier/later raids in the day.
