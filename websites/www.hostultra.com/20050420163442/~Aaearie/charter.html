Guilds Charter

Our insignia has a symbol in the center signifying the four alliance guilds that build the Common Wealth Alliance guild. With the merging of Brimstone, the alliance guilds Shadows of Veeshan, Druid Empire, Femmes Fatale and Legions of Damnation make the foundation that is Common Wealth Alliance. All though our members no longer belong to the alliance guilds we still are committed to them. We keep open communications at all times and assisting our alliance members to reaching the level necessary to enter CWA.

Above all else we are a family brought together to strive in the Everquest world and defeat its foes. The current members have formed a long lasting friendship in a fun atmosphere. We are here to stay. We are here to conquer.
