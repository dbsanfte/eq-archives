Alliance Guilds Invitation Proceedures
The following are proceedures on how to submit your guild to be part of the Alliance.

Applilcation
Applications are taken ONLINE ONLY. If this is something you think you are interested in and you are well enough acquainted with CWA and its rules the guild leader should fill out the form in our Alliance Guild Application section of our Forum.

Evaluation
Any prospective guilds must first wait for a certain evaluation period to pass. This is a participation based alliance and if a guild wants to join it must participate, play and help in practice not jut theory. Come out on raids quests or groups with Alliance guild members, get to know us and let us meet you and your members. If we can get along well and work together we can talk about joining.

Forum Posting

  • Guild leadership or representative officers must post in the Forums.
  • Your guild web site and message board (if any exist) must be provided in the application form and provide an approximate assesment of membership level profile so that their charter, principles and conduct may be asessed.
Interview
  • Once you have completed these two steps the CWAG recruitment team will set up an online interview with you and some of your officers.
  • A second interview will be set up after the evaluation period has passed to let you know about guild membership status and progress of your application.
