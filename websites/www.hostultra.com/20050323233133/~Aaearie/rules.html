Guild Rules

1.)Guild Tags
You are required to have your guild tags on at all times.

2.)Events
One for the lower levels alliance guilds and one for the higher levels in which you are expected to attend, if you are online. Unless you have a real life issue and cannot attend please let an officer or guild leader know. Please check the calendar in the forum section for any scheduled events.

3.)Guild meetings
Meetings will be held at the end of every month which you are expected to attend, if online. In these meetings you can voice any problems or concerns and also give any ideas or suggestions. If you feel the need to arrange a meeting to voice a concern or suggestions, contact an officer or guild leader to arrange one.

4.)Raids
These will happen every 2 weeks. We will have a meeting point to take attendance and gather for ports if need be. You are expected to be there at least five minutes before raid time.

5.)Player Killers (PK)
There will be NO BUFFING, or GROUPING with ANY Pk's for any reason. If someone asked you for buffs please /guildstat command to see if they are guilded by a pk guild and have tags down and ask in CWA chat channel and guild chat to see if anyone knows of the person. We have a good reputation and will not tolerate our guild being smeared by buffing or assisting any pks. Violation to this rule will be an immediate probation and can lead to dismissal.

6.)Chat Channel
The chat channel was created for the communication of the alliance guilds. It gives the alliance guilds a way to find grouping partners and ask for assistance. You are required to always be logged in the cwa alliance chat and you are to give NO ONE the Chat channel name or password unless they have an alliance guild tag on.

7.)Invitation
Invitation to our guild is very exclusive. To see our invite policy please go to Invitation Proceedure page.

8.)Conduct
We think of CWA as an extended family. We are all here to have fun and help each other out. We just ask that you obey Everquest Rules of Conduct, and use good judgement. If by any chance you have a problem with any of the guild members or another Everquest player please contact the judicial officer or Guild leader for assistance.

9.)Loot
In raids, loot is given out by raid leaders, officers and guild leader on a need basis. We try to keep spells, weapons and armor items in the guild bank to give to members. In xp groups, loot is usually rolled for. Raids and xp groups can be strategically made if you need help camping an item. Just ask an officer to arrange it for you or you can try putting a group together yourself.

Violation Procedure

  • The judicial officer will review your case and pass a warning. If member does not comply, the judicial officer will bring the case up to the council and a vote will determine member removal from the guild.
  • During this time, you will have the opportunity to appeal the decision to the judicial officer. Depending on evidence and further research of the case, the member may be allowed a probation period.
