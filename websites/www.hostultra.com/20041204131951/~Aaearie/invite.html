CWA Invitation Proceedures
The following are proceedures if you wish to become a member of CWA.

The Common Wealth Alliance Guild recruitment requirements are:

Basic character Requirements

  • Must be Level 55 (Outside of CWA member guilds) Level 50 for CWA member guilds
  • Registered at our forum to fill application
  • Membership to Alliance Guild
  • Raiding/Team play/Questing Evaluation
  • Interview with our Recruiting Officer
  • Character background check
Application
Application to CWAG are processed only online through the Recruitment section of our forums. If you have been with the Common Wealth allies long enough to know if this is the right guild for you and wish to apply please hit the REPLY button and answer the questions and provide the information requested. Enter your Name and level in the Subject Title.

NOTE: Application is OPTIONAL, you may wish to remain with your current guild and check us out as a guest on raids just contact an officer to set up a sponsorship for you from our guild.

Alliance Guild
You will given membership to one of the Common Wealth alliance guilds. This process is MANDATORY for a period of at least 3 weeks.

Raiding/Team play/Questing Evaluation
ALL applicants must meet basic guild time and raid requirements. Satisfactory evaluation in Common Wealth Alliance raids from 3 Officers is necessary(*). Your time in your current guild and the number of raids and your conduct are taken into consideration.

Evaluation General Guideline for New Members:

  • Alliance guilded for 3 weeks and attend 6 raids
    or Alliance guilded for 6 weeks and attend 3 raids
    (This allows you to see if we are right for you and vise versa)
  • An Interview with recruiting officer
  • Character background check

Interview
You will be interviewed by our Recruiting Officer online.

Background Check
Our Recruiting officer will ask around about you. He may ask during your interview to provide some references.

Current Alliance Members
(*)Current Alliance guild officers or members in good standing may be approved immediately given the proper references from CWA guild officers.
