Here is a little reminder about what people have to know about raids.

A raid always has an aim. usually people do not go only to a raid to "destroy everything" with 20 or more people. There are plenty of different aims, to get a particular item for someone (like epic raid), to get some armor for everyone, to increase his faction and so on ... It is important for everyone to know the aim of the raid.

A raid usually has different people to lead it :

- The raid leader, is the person who defines the aime of the raid, and the strategies which are to be used during the raid. It s quite often somene who knows the zone well an who has some experience. You have to obei him without asking questions, a bit like in the army. He usually does not have the time to explain why he is doing this or that so you have to trust him/her. If possible try to avoid to send direct tells to him/her he is usually very busy.

- The Main Assist (aka MA) is the person in charge to target the mob for everyone. It is very important to have the same target as him, it allows enchanters to mez the others mobs and remove them from the fights. Everyone in the raid MUSt have a hotkey /assist {name of main assist} and on the second line /g assisting {name of main assist} on Traget (%t)

- The secondary assist is not as common. Sometimes we will meet mobs that cannot be mezzed, either cause they are immune to it (like giants) or because we have no enchanter with us. the tactic here is that if we pull of these mobs the second mob will be tanked by the secondary assist to avoid letting him roam everywhere and hit the casters clerics and others.. you will also need an /assist key on the secondary assist to target the second mob when the first one is dead.

- The Puller is in charge of bringing the mobs to the camp, it can be a monk, bard, ranger, warrior , enchanter, necromancer ...it really depends on the raid, usually he is in the same group as the main assist and the enchanter.

- The Master Looter, is in charge to loot all the items that drop and lotto them at the end of the raid.Since it is possible that his group does not have the kill and thus cannot loot, each group leader is in charge to loot and /ooc the loots on the mobs if there group has the kill. they loot the item to give it to the master looter.

- The group leaders : there function is important in order to increase the speed of the raid and to filter communication. If you have a question, a doubt, or a good idea, submit in in group chat first, because maybe someone has the answer and if it s not the cse the group leader leader will transmit it to the raid leader, this system allows to avoid that the raid leader gets too many tells. The Group leader is also in charge of the mana reports, when the raid leader asks for a mana report everyone answers in group, and the group leader makes an average value and annouces it in OOC. if there is a druid at 10, a cleric at 30, a wizard at 50, the group leader will answer /ooc group X 30% mana.

Those are the main persons acting in the raid. Now there are some simple rules to follow in raid :

The SHOUT channel is only used by the raid leader, main assist, main puller and master looter, it is important to avoir jokes in shouts in raid.
The OOC channel is usually free of use, but is mainly for group leaders, and don t feel forced to send jokes on it since i say it s free of use :)
