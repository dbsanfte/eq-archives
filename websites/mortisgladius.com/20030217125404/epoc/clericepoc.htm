Cleric Epic

Information regarding the Cleric Epic quest was last updated 2003-01-22 22:44:00. There are 14 steps to complete, and completion of this quest results in Water Sprinkler of Nem Ankh.

Steps to complete quest Cleric Epic

Step 1: Shmedrik Lavawalker Mark Step Complete Go to pos3600, 0 in Lake Rathe. Schmedrik is on a three hour (real-time) timer, and is level 30, with about 1500hp. Kill him. Upon his death, a spirit of flame will spawn. The spirit is level 40, with about 3300 hp. Neither are particularly magic resistant. Kill the spirit of flame, and loot a Damaged Goblin Crown.
Members who have completed: Aeani

Step 2: Lord Begurgle Mark Step Complete AKA Long Boring Camp 1. Lord Begurgle is in Lake Rathe, at the top of the aqua goblin tower under the lake. The entrance to the underwater cave is at pos2831, neg912, neg221, and the tower itself is at pos2800, pos 150. Enduring breath is required, as well as enduring patience. Begurgle spawns in the middle of three spawns at the top of the tower. He is on a 28 minute timer, and his placeholder is a deepwater goblin. The outside two spawns are both deepwater aqua goblins, and do not need to be killed. The ledge above the spawnpoint is very safe, and a good place to med. One viable strategy is to pull the placeholder back to where the cave opens up, to prevent multiple agros. Begurgle himself should be pulled all the way to the top of the island -- he will happy follow you, stopping only to attempt to nuke you. Begurgle is level 40, with about 6000hp. He can and will complete heal himself, so you must keep him stunned after he gets below 30% health. From his corpse, loot Lord Bergurgle's Crown.
Members who have completed: Aeani

Step 3: Shmedrik Lavawalker, Part II Mark Step Complete Once you have Lord Begurgle's Crown, grab the Damaged Goblin Crown you looted in step 1, and go visit Schmedrik again. Give him Lord B's crown, and Natasha Whitewater will spawn. BE PATIENT HERE. Natasha is initially not visible to you as you do the hand-in - she spawns out of LOS. After 20-30 seconds or so, she will agro Shmedrik. You need not do anything except make sure that Shmedrik is rooted next to Natasha. Once Shmedrik does, the spirit of flame will spawn, and, if you did the first part right, will agro Natasha. If this doesn't happen, stand next to Natasha and nuke the spirit. Once Natasha is aggroed, she will kill the spirit of flame as well. Once the spirit is dead, give Natasha the Damaged Goblin Crown, and you will receive An Ornate Sea Shell, and her thanks. Important note: do not skip step 1 - you must have the Damaged Crown to do it this way!! If Natasha gets the killing blow on the spirit of flame, the corpse will poof, and you will have no Damaged Goblin Crown to give to Natasha! This means you'd have to camp Begurgle again.
Members who have completed: Aeani

Step 4: Omar Vastsea, Part I Mark Step Complete This fine fellow is in Timourous Deep, loc (neg 11567, neg 2227). To reach him, take the cave entrance at loc ( neg 11530, neg 3728), then the entrance at (neg 11336, neg 1307). Give him the Ornate Sea Shell, and he will give you A Coral Statue of Tarew.
Members who have completed: Aeani

Step 5: Lord Gimblox Mark Step Complete AKA Long Boring Camp II. Gimblox is in Solusek's Eye (SolA), at exactly loc neg796, neg366. This is in the room adjacent to the King Room. Gimblox and his placeholder (a large fire goblin) are on a nineteen minute timer. There are two other static spawns in the room, plus a wanderer. All of these are easily handled by anyone seriously contemplating an epic. Gimblox himself is level 30, with about 1800 hp -- the placeholders and other spawns are weaker. This is an excellent opportunity for powerleveling, as you can keep this room, the king room, and the mage/priest spawn near there cleared fairly easily. Gimblox drops Lord Gimblox's Signet Ring
Members who have completed: Aeani

Step 6: Plasmatic Priest Mark Step Complete You will need at least one group of 50+ to succeed here. The priest can be found in the Temple of Solusek Ro, at loc (pos 326, pos 38, pos 29). This is up on the second floor. The priest is level 55, about 3500 hit points, and summons. He likes to nuke and heal, and is unstunnable. He spawns at 9am and despawns at 9pm each game day. The easiest way to kill the priest is to have a tank pull the priest to the zoneline and either FD (if monk) or zone. The rest of the party is waiting in the hallway, including the cleric. The cleric gives the priest the Coral Statue of Tarew, and the priest then agros the rest of the party. Loot the Blood-Soaked Plasmatic Priest Robe.
Members who have completed: Aeani

Step 7: Omar Vastsea, Part II Mark Step Complete Go see Omar again. Give him the blood-soaked robe from the Plasmatic Priest, and you get the Orb of Frozen Water, and Natasha Whitewater spawns (inside the inn -- go in and turn left). Give Gimblox's Ring to Natasha, and you get an Ornate Sea Shell.
Members who have completed: Aeani

Step 8: Ixiblat Fer Mark Step Complete This, and all subsequent encounters, require at least three balanced groups of 50+ adventurers. The more, the better. Overkill is a good thing here. Find Naxot Deepwater in Burning Woods, approx. loc (pos 3400, neg 2150). Give her the Ornate Sea Shell, and you'll get a Message to Natasha, and Ixiblat Fer will spawn. Ixi is a fire elemental lord, level 62, about 32000hp, with a resistible 300hp DD AOE fire attack. The most common practice is to have a bard or ranger pull Ixi to the Skyfire zoneline, an open area free from adds. He can and should be slowed, and will run when low on health. Loot the Sceptre of Ixiblat Fer from his corpse.
Members who have completed: Aeani

Step 9: Overking Bathezid Di'Zok Mark Step Complete This encounter also requires at least three groups of 50+ adventurers. The Overking is one of the Royals in Chardok, get a map for his exact spawn point. The Overking is at least level 63, and has an AOE Fiery Death (approx 500 hp dmg, cancels 1 buff). He also likes to complete heal, and cast Gravity Flux and Gate. He spawns every two hours. Loot the Singed Scroll from his corpse.


Step 10: Omar Vastsea, Part III Mark Step Complete Go see Omar again, and give him the sceptre and scroll. He will give you the Orb of Clear Water, and Natasha will spawn again. Give Natasha the Message to Natasha you got from Naxot, and Natasha will give you a Shimmering Pearl.


Step 11: Zordak Ragefire, Part I Mark Step Complete Go to Nagafen's Lair (SolB), to approximately neg 1300, neg 300. Look for a banner there, Zordak will spawn there. Zordak will only spawn once every 24 hours. Give him the Shimmering Pearl, and you'll get a Swirling Pearl, plus a Box of Binding. The Box of Binding is a container, be sure you have room for it.


Step 12: Pearlescent Fragments Mark Step Complete Now go to Skyfire and collect four Pearlescent Fragments. Apparently they can and do drop rarely off nearly every mob in the zone. Once you have four, put them in the Box of Binding and hit combine. You will now have an Assembled Pearlescent Shard.


Step 13: Zordak Ragefire, Part II Mark Step Complete Find Warder Cecilia in Skyfire, at pos 3200 neg 3200, in front of the big dragon statue. Give Cecilia the Assembled Pearlescent Shard, and the human form of Ragefire will spawn. The human form is level 55. When the human form of Zordak is killed, Zordakalicus Ragefire (the dragon form) will spawn. The dragon is level 60, and is similar in strength to Lord Nagafen. When the dragon form is killed, loot the Impure Heart of Ragefire.


Step 14: Quest ending Mark Step Complete Place the swirling pearl and the Pearlescent Seal (that you got from Cecilia when you turned in the shard) inside the impure heart, and hit combine. The container becomes "Heart of Ragefire". Take the Heart back to Omar Vastsea, and he will give you the Orb of Vapor, and Jhassad Oceanson will spawn. Give Jhassad the three orbs, and he will return the Orb of the Triumvirate, and the Avatar of Water will spawn. The Avatar will wander for quite a while, then settle next to Jhassad. Give the Avatar the Orb of the Triumvirate, and you will receive the Water Sprinkler of Nem Ankh. Followed very closely by your first guildie asking for a click-stick rez...
