Magician Epic

Information regarding the Magician Epic quest was last updated 2002-12-08 15:58:00. There are 10 steps to complete, and completion of this quest results in Orb of Mastery.

Steps to complete quest Magician Epic

Step 1: 1: Token of Mastery Mark Step Complete Go talk to Rykas in Lake Rathetear. Say 'I wish to learn the tale of Magi'kot'. Receive the Token of Mastery.


Step 2: 2: Words of Magi'kot Mark Step Complete Turn in: Token of Mastery to Jahsohn Aksot in West Commonlands for experience and storyline. Then turn in: Torn Page of Magi'kot 1 (Dread Wolves in Kithicor Forest), Torn Page of Magi'kot 2 (Tentacle Terrors, Unrest), and Torn Page of Magi'kot 3 (Bloodthirsty Ghoul, Lower Guk) to the same person. Receive Words of Magi'kot


Step 3: 3: Power of the Elements Mark Step Complete Turn in: Power of Wind (Gypsy Dancers, Mistmoore), Power of Earth (Fairy Guards, Lesser Faydark), Power of Fire (Blazing and Lava Elementals, Solusek's Eye), and Power of Water (Famished and Enraged Raveners, Kaesora) to Walnan in Butcherblock Mtns. Walnan isn't a common spawn, so be patient. Receive Power of the Elements


Step 4: 4: Words of Mastery Mark Step Complete Turn in: Torn Page of Mastery: Water (Magicians,Najena; Alligators, Cazic Thule), Torn Page of Mastery: Earth (Magicians,Najena; Alligators, Cazic Thule), Torn Page of Mastery: Air (Magicians,Najena; Alligators, Cazic Thule), and Torn Page of Mastery: Fire (Magicians,Najena; Alligators, Cazic Thule), to Akksstaff, in Najena. He is a wandering NPC, so he could be anywhere. Receive Words of Mastery
Members who have completed: Ezzudzen Runefire

Step 5: 5: Power of the Orb Mark Step Complete Turn in: Words of Magi'kot, Power of the Elements, and Words of Mastery in to Rykas in Lake Rathe. Receive Power of the Orb.


Step 6: 6: Element of Fire Mark Step Complete Turn in: Torch of the Elements (Neh'Asshirr, City of Mist), Burning Embers (Named Wurms, Burning Woods), Blazing Wand (Undertow, Kedge Keep), and Power of the Orb to Jennus Lyklobar on the western wall of Skyfire Mtns. Not always spawned, be patient. Receive Element of Fire.


Step 7: 7: Element of Earth Mark Step Complete Turn in: Staff of Elemental Mastery Earth (Magi P'tasa, Plane of Hate), Dirt of underfoot (Slixin Klex, Burning Woods), Broom of Trilon (quest item from mage focus item quests), and Shovel of Ponz (quest item from mage focus item quests) to Tiblner Milnik in Firiona Vie. Receive Element of Earth


Step 8: 8: Element of Water Mark Step Complete Turn in: Staff of Elemental Mastery: Water (Phinigel Autropos, Kedge Keep), Tears of Erollisi (Captain Rottgrime, Overthere), and Rain of Karana (Trabul Earthstrider, East Karana) in to Jinalis Andir in Dagnor's Cauldron. Receive Element of Water


Step 9: 10: Master of Elements Mark Step Complete Turn in: Element of Water, Element of Earth, Element of Fire, Element of Air to Master of Elements, who spawns after you turn in your items for the Element of Air to Kihun Solstin. Master of Elements spawns in the middle of the quest room in the Plane of Air. Receive Orb of Mastery. Congratulations!


Step 10: 9: Element of Air Mark Step Complete Turn in: Crown of Elemental Mastery (Random Drop, Plane of Sky, 7th Island), Elemental Binder (Elemental Warriors, The Hole), and Pegasus Feather Cloak (Quillmane, South Karana) to Kihun Solstin, Plane of Air, I'm assuming the first island, (the quest island). Receive Element of Air
