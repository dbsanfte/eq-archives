Druid Epic

Information regarding the Druid Epic quest was last updated 2002-11-09 10:24:00. There are 24 steps to complete, and completion of this quest results in Nature Walker's Scimitar.

Steps to complete quest Druid Epic

Step 1: Shiny Tin Bowl 1 Mark Step Complete Burning Woods - Hail Telin Darkforest and say "What action should I take?" - Receive a Worn Note


Step 2: Shiny Tin Bowl 2 Mark Step Complete Greater Faydark - Give the Worn Note to Faelin Bloodbriar - Receive Faelin's Ring (PH is an orc pawn around +1500, +500)


Step 3: Shiny Tin Bowl 3 Mark Step Complete Kithicor Forest - Give Faelin's Ring to Giz X'Tin - A Dark Reaver will spawn, just run to the zone. Receive a Dark Metal Coin.


Step 4: Shiny Tin Bowl 4 Mark Step Complete Burning Woods - Give the Dark Metal Coin to Telin Darkforest- Receive the Worn Dark Metal Coin.


Step 5: Shiny Tin Bowl 5 Mark Step Complete East Karana - All steps must be done to Completion - Give the Worn Dark Metal Coin to Althele - Receive Braided Grass Amulet. Give this amulet to Sionae - Receive Frayed Braided Grass Amulet. Give this amulet to Nuien - Receive the amulet back. Give this amulet to Teloa. This will cause a Dark Elf Corruptor and two Dark Reavers to spawn. Kill DE Corruptor while trying to protect the druids - Loot Fleshbound Tomb.


Step 6: Shiny Tin Bowl 6 Mark Step Complete East Karana - Give Fleshbound Tomb to Althele to recive Earth Stained Note. Althele will despawn after receiving tomb. *NOTE: If druids die you will have to wait 30 min. for her to respawn - this is true if you need to complete the cycle more than once.*


Step 7: Shiny Tin Bowl 7 Mark Step Complete Misty Thicket - Give the Earth Stained Note to Ella Foodcrafter - Receive Shiny Tin Bowl.


Step 8: Create the Hardened Mixture 1 Mark Step Complete Everfrost - Forage Chilled Tundra Root


Step 9: Create the Hardened Mixture 2 Mark Step Complete Misty Thicket - Forage Sweetened Mudroot


Step 10: Create the Hardened Mixture 3 Mark Step Complete Innothule Swamp - Forage Speckled Mushroom


Step 11: Create the Hardened Mixture 4 Mark Step Complete Greater Faydark - Forage Ripened Heartfruit


Step 12: Create the Hardened Mixture 5 Mark Step Complete Combine the chilled tundra root, sweetened mudroot, speckled mushroom and ripened heartfruit in the Shiny Tin Bowl to create the Hardened Mixture.


Step 13: Ancient Bowl Pattern 1 Mark Step Complete Timorous Deep - Hail Alrik Farsight on Chessboard Island and say "I will take the artifact" - Receive a Crushed Pot


Step 14: Ancient Bowl Pattern 2 Mark Step Complete Felwithe South - Give Crushed Pot to Farios Elianos - Receive a grocery list


Step 15: Ancient Bowl Pattern 3 Mark Step Complete Felwithe North - Give Grocery List to Merchant Nora - Receive a Bag of Provisions.


Step 16: Ancient Bowl Pattern 4 Mark Step Complete Felwith South/North - Give Bag of Provision to Farios Elianos - Receive Receipt for the Ancient Bowl


Step 17: Ancient Bowl Pattern 5 Mark Step Complete Timorous Deep - Give the receipt to Alrik Farsight - Receive an Ancient Pattern


Step 18: Platinum Speckled Powder 1 Mark Step Complete Firiona Vie - Forage a Rose of Firiona


Step 19: Platinum Speckled Powder 2 Mark Step Complete Surefall Glade - Give the rose to Merdan Fleetfoot - Receive a Wood Painting


Step 20: Platinum Speckled Powder 3 Mark Step Complete Frontier Mountains - Give the wood Painting to a Human Skeleton (Spawns around -2100, +2000) Receive a Silver Chained Locket.


Step 21: Platinum Speckled Powder 4 Mark Step Complete Surefall Glade - Give the Silver Chained Locket to Niera Farbreeze - Receive the Platinum Speckled Powder.
Members who have completed: Lilanye Nosoforu

Step 22: Create the Rune Crested Bowl 1 Mark Step Complete City of Mist - Kill a Black Reaver and loot a Jade Reaver (this item is tradeable)


Step 23: Create the Rune Crested Bowl 2 Mark Step Complete North Kaladim - Give the Jade Reaver to Kinlo Strongarm in Everhot Forge - Receive Enchanted Clay


Step 24: Create the Rune Crested Bowl 3 Mark Step Complete Combine the Ancient Bowl Pattern, Enchanted Clay and Platinum Speckled Powder in any pottery wheel to make the Rune Crested Bowl (no pottery skill needed.)
