White Dragon Scale & 53+ Bards
** Last updated 25 July 2001

The White Dragon Scale is without a doubt the most difficult item to obtain in the bard epic quest. A bard with it, is virtually assured of an epic someday, a bard without, is still very far away from completion.  Bards are stuck in that the only two sources of the scale are Lady Vox (for 52- bards) and Gorenaire. Gore is exceedingly difficult to kill, and to do so requires an army of almost 50 level 58-60 players. Needless to say, she rarely dies.  Therefore, 53+ bards without a white scale are in a horrible position.   To help address this problem, I have opened some options for 53+ bards to obtain the scales without having to pay 100k+. The last offer I saw 2 weeks ago on Cazic-Thule was 140k...

The White Dragon Scale cloak is a nice item, but there are definitely better cloaks and back items in the game. The Heirophant cloak (rare drop in Seblis) is extremely close, if not better, in many areas. The bard epic, is without a doubt, the most uber item a bard could ever get and has no equal.

FOR STARTERS, HOW LOOT IS DONE:
No loot items on my raids is guaranteed or reserved, including for myself (and I'm the host). Because of my open loot rules three other bards won a WDS prior to myself, open loots can be aggravating at times, but the ability for anyone to win anything, including first time attendees, makes my raids very popular. Every time someone attends one of my dragon raids, they are given a raid point, which helps their odds on nice items (RBB, CoF, books, scales, & eyepatch).

With that in mind, nothing is for certain, but with enough persistance, you can build odds in your favor and win. This works for regular attendees and for 53+ bards hoping to get a WDS.

HOW WDS IS DONE:
When people report in for the raid, I enter all the names into a spreadsheet that tracks everyone's attendance, does groups, and calculates loot numbers. Everyone's name is listed alphabetically. For some items (e.g. Pouch), everyone gets one number, I do a "\random 1 N" look up the correponding name, and that person wins the item. Good items, like the WDS, are based on 1 number + raid points (from prior attendance).   The WDS is open to bards and casters on a random roll when loot is divided out. Bards (52-) on the raid get (1 + raidpoints)x5 numbers and casters get (1+raidpoints) for numbers. Just for information, the rolls used to be bard-only, until 5 bards had won scales, and then was opened to casters.

In the event that a caster wins the WDS and if a 53+ bard is present with a White Dragonscale Cloak, Heirophant Cloak or better, I force a trade. The cloak is given to me, I will give the WDS, and then give the cloak to the caster.

I'M NOT AT RAID, DO I STILL GET A CHANCE?
Yes, send me a tell sometime prior to raid and I'll put you on my list of bards wishing to make a trade. If you are on-line during the buff stage of the raid (when I actually have free time), I will send you a tell seeing if you are still interested.   Everyone who is on-line and interested, is given a chance in the roll. You must be prepared to come to west commons on a moment's notice if you win.  A large percentage of my raid attendees are not from my guild and I want to make a quick exchange.

WHAT CAN I DO TO INCREASE MY ODDS?
When doing the random between the bards wanting to make the trade, I will include raid points when determining the numbers. You can increase your odds by:

1. Come to raid and sing mana song to help things move along. I will award you with 1 raid point each time.
2. Come to raid with Shaman, Enchanter, or Cleric that can help with buffs (52+). I will award you with 1 raid point each time.
3. Trakanon assistance (to finish Brewall's epic). Once Spiritsong wins a WDS, the guild that assists Brewall to kill the spawned Trakanon will get rights to trade for WDS in the event that a caster wins. He is currently the only 53+ bard with multiple attendances and I will wait until after he wins to do this. In all likelyhood, this will be from a powerful guild on the server, and I would like to make this a win-win for everyone. I would like that guild to include 1-2 additional items (that are extra's to them) on top of the Heiro cloak. The items will be randomed out that raid as well (e.g. 1 additional heiro cloak, 1 extra trak bp, 1 extra VS legging, etc). This way, multiple people on the raid win nice items, bigger guild gets rid of some extra stuff (versus just giving to twinks), and 2 bards wind up with epics. The trade only happens, however, if a caster wins.

(2001.07.25) Update: I currently have an offer on this scenario. Once Spiritsong wins a WDS, I will guarantee the next trade rights for the WDS when a caster wins the roll. With an epic, I will not have pressure to level and kill Trak myself, meaning more WDS for everyone.

Example: 4 bards (BardA, BardB, BardC, BardD) send me tells that they are interested in making trade a few days prior to raid. All are entered into spreadsheet and kept indefinitely on the list. On the night of the raid, BardA shows up to sing mana song and BardB shows up with level 55 cleric alt to help with buffs. During buff stage, BardC is on-line, but hunting in seblis and BardD is not on.  If this was the first raid for all these bards AND a caster won the roll for the WDS, the random numbers would be (done alphabetically): BardA 1-2, BardB 3-4, BardC 5. I would then do a "\random 1 5" to determine who gets the rights to make the trade.

Depending on how many bards are on the list, I may add a factor to the calculation to reward those that directly help with the raids.

53 BARDS
If you just dinged into 53, have no cloak to trade, you can still win a WDS, by attending the raid, helping to clear, and singing mana song. If your resists are needed, you may be asked to delevel in order to keep yourself in on the main open roll.  If there are plenty of people, you may get to keep level and still be in the main open roll. This is done on a case-by-case basis and only offered to bards that have come up through the raids with more than 5 prior attendances. I stongly urge these bards not to get far into 53.

WORDS OF WARNING
Please do not try to bribe 53- bards from my raids that won scales. If I hear of them selling the scale, they will never receive another potentially sellable item from one of my raids again - ever. There are a lot of snitches out there, and if I hear of a sale, I will check with that person to ensure that the scale was not sold, if it was sold, they will go on my blacklist. I realize everyone wants an epic as quickly as possible, but I've been at 52 for over 5 months now working on white scales while many others decided to pass up on Vox and to level instead.

ANY IDEA HOW LONG THIS COULD TAKE? 
Hard to say. Vox has been stingy of late with scales. 0 in the past 4 raids. On 10 raids, she has dropped 7 so far, all of which have gotten into bard hands. Seems to be multiples or none most of the time. I am currently the only one raiding her, and have killed her within a day of all of her spawns.
