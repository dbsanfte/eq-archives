Crimson Talon News
AUG 03, 2001
Lady Vox was catching up on some late-night reading when the dragon slaying team swarmed into her lair last night. She finally graced us with a few books to make up for the absolute lack of drops recently. No enchanters were present at the raid to seive, so made due with bards singing mana drain.  The plan worked perfectly, and Vox went down with not so much as a whisper.  Loots were: 3 Frost books [Ixnus, Tularane, Rizzhogg] and Ice Comet [Infernostar]. 41 book points gone in one kill and thins out the numbers considerably for the other long-time paladin attendees.

I appoligize to anyone that was turned away. It is difficult to get everyone into each raid with so much demand for dragon killing out there.

Brewall led dragon raids now 17-0 (11-0 on Vox, 5-0 on Nagafen, and 1-0 on Faydedar).

AUG 02, 2001
Lord Nagafen falls for a fifth time to a Brewall led dragon raid. Interest in dragon raids remains high and at least 15 people were turned away. All those that did not make raid are given an attendance point to make it easier to get into future raids.

EL cleared out the giants prior to the raid, dramatically shortening the total raid time.   Lot of new buffers this evening and took a little bit to get all the kinks out. Excellent job pushing Nag to back of lair and grats to Tularane for the kill shot.   Loots were: Cloak of Flames [Lumper], Gauntlets of Firey Might [Chiun], Treasure Hunters Satchel [Kirby], Orb of Tishan [Niewlut], Orb of Tishan [Brewall], Prayers of Life [Lisner], Fire Emerald Ring [Alianora], and a Diamond [Azumar].

Brewall has also posted his Guide to Fear in the "Raid Information" section of the site. Please read it over and give feedback prior to our upcoming raid.

JUL 31, 2001
Two words, absolutely bizarre. Raided CoM this evening to get a few epic items. We believed someone ninja looted a jade reaver during our fight with the golems (petitioned a GM to investigate).  Then, during the assault on Lord Rak'Ashiir with 4-5 fire pets and at 50% health, the pets pushed him into the water somehow. Rak'Ashiir vanishes along with a couple of the pets and continued to fight somewhere out of sight within the zone. Pets eventually die and Rak'Ashiir summons a couple people and just when are beginning to evac, he pops right in the middle of the raiding pary and kills a bunch of us with his 2000AE dmg spell (petition again regarding the pathing and lost epic piece). Perform corpse recovery, but a couple summoned bodies are hopelessly stuck in walls (petition yet again). GM comes by after a while, gets corpses, ignores questions on ninja looter and Rak'Ashiir pathing. Instead, he decides he has nothing better to do than to change the name of a 57 wizard in our guild from "Gooveit Allnight" to "Groovie"!?! He would not respond as to what naming rule was violated. Grooveit has 7 days to get his name fixed, and hopefully, he can get his original name back. Absolutely bizarre, no resolution on ninja looter or the pathing problem and loss of epic piece. We have very rarely ever petitioned anything and this was a truely horrible experience.

Numerous updates to all three dragon raiding guides.

JUL 30, 2001
Our rampage on the aristocracy of Kunark continues. Tonight's victim was Korocust, the prince of Chardok. Was our first large-scale guild raid on the dungeon and went off very well. Will definitely have to return and do another crawl of this often-empty dungeon.

JUL 28-29, 2001
Exp hunting time. Groups in Velk, Karnors, and few other spots. Numerous dings around the guild including Tomwin (56) and Greymask (54).

JUL 27, 2001
Killed Venril Sathir's remains early in the evening [Laurax]. Decided to move on to his undead wife and teach her a lesson in manners. Demolished her private guards one-by-one leaving Venril's wife to ponder her fate for a moment, until we dispatched her as well. Dropped Rod of Obulations (1hb 6/27 mana60 fr10 cr10) [Skylynn].

JUL 26, 2001
Raid on Howling Stones numerous spell drops and another haste belt. Tomwin claims to have overheard ds trash talking CT, going to have to show her a lesson.

JUL 25, 2001
Lady Vox met an untimely demise this evening to a determined group of dragon killers. Interest from melee/hybrids is higher than ever, but the lack of frost book drops (just 1 in 10 raids), has clogged a lot of spots. There are currently 16 50+ paladins on the raids with 5+ prior raids. 12 of them are 52 and have been stuck at that level hoping for their epic book. On most raids, this basically fills 3 full tank groups (out of 5). I appologize to those that did not make the raid, but all were given an attendance point for coming.

Vox was indeed in the hallway again, but the first two tank groups did an excellent job dislodging her. She did manage to get off 1 CH, but it did her little good.  Was the first raid with 3 wizards, and the quick damage at the end was very impressive. Also thanks go out to those that stuck around to help recovery corpses from the pits. My advice: never fall in the one near buffing ramps, it leads into a room with 6 mobs. Read my Vox raiding guide, I clearly show all the pits...

Drops were: McVaxious Horn of War [Ferdinand], Dragon Bone Bracelet [Sintosa], Ice Comet [Grimstaff], Kayrul's Mystical Pouch [Irrenne], and 2 Staff of Forbidden Rites [Sardaukar, Saltz].

JUL 24, 2001
Lord Nagafen's blood cools the lava streaming within his lair again tonight. He perished at the hands of an eager dragon hunting group. Unfortunately, many people had to be turned away again from the raid. I was able to work in all 4+ attendees and all 50+ pal/war/brd with epic items at stake.  Raid was off to slow start with SL clearing the FG. Originally I was told that they were just after FG's, and since they had beaten us to them, I honored their attack. At the end, however, I was told they were really after Ragefire. Cost both us and them time waiting for respective mobs in the end. Instead of Nag being dead around the usual 9:30 cst, it wasn't until 11:30 cst that he was killed. If this happens again on a patch day, may either just push through or simply switch to Vox that night and hit Nag the next day without having to wait - this won't be popular for clerics wanting RF to spawn though. If this sounds like a flame, it is not intended to be one, I just have to have raids that move in and kill dragons as quickly as possible. Keeping a raid of 40+ attendees from 20+ guilds under control is no easy task, the longer it goes on, the more difficult it becomes. I lost almost 1 full group during the wait and was crushed with tells for an extra 2 hours because of this delay, making this one of my more physically exhausting raids ever. The inane comments from some of the people in SolB didn't help either, and insulting tells didn't endear me to some individuals who are now on my ignore list - hope you never want in on one of my future raids...

Loots were: Orb of Tishan 2 [ Greymask, Kathandros], Blight Hammer [Sabore], Prayers of Life [Morgx], and 2 gems [Aalthur, Ardath].

I suggest that Greymask go out and buy a lotto ticket today, use numbers 1, 3, and 13. On 1st item roll, Greymask won Orb with roll of 3. Then, when rolling for gems, 13 came up - Greymask - can't win again. Roll over: 3 -Ardath wins. Roll again for next gem - 13 - Greymask again! Yet another rollover:  1 - Aalthur wins. The numbers 1 and 3 were strangely common - Believe it or not!

Brewall dragon raids now 9-0 vs Vox, 4-0 vs Nag, and 1-0 vs Fay.

JUL 23, 2001 (report from Khallin / Brewall)
Great night all around. The evening started out with Greymask getting another enchanter epic item off shaman mob, and then moved on to Howling Stones, where the drops were incredible. Cleared several wings again tonight and among the drops were: 2! Sash of the Dragonborn (ac2 ~26% haste / FBSS is ~21%) [Madmat & Dodja], Golden Bracer (ac5 str7 int7) [Mysyn], Blood Point (prc 10/20 str5 cha-5 pr10 dr10) [Sventhal], Kylong War Helm (ac12 hp20 mana20 mr7 pr4) [Cerritus], Enshrouded Veil (ac7 wis2 int2 hp30 mana30 fr10) [Riverwake] and numerous spell drops. Tenara also gave the Mask of Obtenebration to Brewall from the prior night's HS raid (a 'fun' illusion item, but stats actually inferior to DE illusion mask most brd/rog get in their 20s). Tried out in WFP and was inspected no less than 6x in 5 minutes. Never seen an erudite bard :)

Moved up Vox raid to Wed night. Might as well maximize kills and hope that VI has to patch again. Also frees up Fri-Sun for non-dragon raiding.

JUL 22, 2001
Lady Vox perishes in the frosty wastelands of Permafrost this evening. Unbelievable number of people present wanting to kill her, but only so many spots. Tried to accomodate 1 extra group over the 42 I normally do, but that wound up being a huge problem. At least 8 people went LD during the assault (including myself). Everyone has now seen what happens with too many people, so please be understanding if you don't make raid. I prioritize based on minimum classes needed: shm(2), brd(5-6), clr(4-5), enc(4), dru(2); and then raid+attendance points. Anyone not making list gets an attendance point which helps get into future raids.

To date, 325 different people have attended my 12 dragon raids! These raids are truely the most open on the server (or any other server for that matter). With 20+ different guilds on any given raid, I am looking at the people, not the 'tags'. If you are mad you didn't get on list, just think of time wasted trying to get a group for sometimes hours on end. I try to determine final list of raid people within 1 hour of start.

For 45's - 50's melees, your odds of getting on raid is rather low right now. There is a backlog of people with 5+ raids still hoping to get items (some people have 10+ raids). As they win, they tend to float off (loot is still and always will be random). My advice: Show up to 1 raid, get an attendance point, and watch raid from zone-line to familiarize yourself with process. Then use time to get some good resistance gear and get a couple levels. If you come to a later raid saying you are 49 with unbuffed cold/fire resistance at 130+, witnessed a couple raids in past, and have at least 1 attendance point, you are in pretty good shape. This is better than just coming to each raid getting attendance points.

Drops were: RBB [Ciladin], Eyepatch [Milennia], Hammer [Irrenne], and DBB [Cowabunga won, but donated to Punchen]. Grats to all.

Going to send a note to VI asking if 1 book in 9 kills is an acceptable drop rate for an epic item.

Trojar, don't come to any future dragon raids I host or any other CT raid for that matter. This was your first dragon raid and when you didn't make the list, spamming me & the zone with cursing was unacceptable. I included anyone on this raid with 1+ prior attendance, an extremely open raid.

Now 13-0 against dragons. Vox 9-0, Nag 3-0, Faydedar 1-0.

JUL 21, 2001
Raid on Howling Stones. Did a complete crawl of North and West wings of the dungeon. Among the drops were: Dragon Sash [Tenara, did random of FBSS and Sventhal won], numerous spell drops, and a few other items. Working toward killing the boss mob in East wing who was overheard trash-talking us...

JUL 19, 2001
Lord Nagafen falls again to a determined group of adventurers seeking to slay the foul fire wyrm. Attendance and interest was very high and many people were turned away. Excellent job by all listening to instructions on the fire giants and during the dragon battle. Drops were: Red Dragon Scale (2) [Brewall, Ralio], Selo's Drum (2) [Ferdinand, Tresten], Bladestopper [Tularane], Saphire Necklace [Barelil]. Giant drops were:   Polished Mithril Torque [Tico], Mithril Greaves [Tularane], and Rokyl Channeling Crystal [Tadyan]. Paladins need to clease their karma and do good deeds, book drops have been exceptionally light.

With the red dragon scale, Brewall is now just a spawned Trakanon guts away from his bard epic! And to answer everyone's question, yes, Brewall plans to lead more dragon raids on both Vox and Nagafen.
