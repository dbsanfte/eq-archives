    Five Guilds in Alliance
    Posted by Atlantiss on Tuesday, June 12 @ 19:09:20 EDT
    Guild Alliance - Please read! -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Hello everyone, Please pass this message to your perspective guild boards.

    If you get bored, PLEASE atleast read #4, but I really encourage everyone to read my blabber. First of all, I am Alrien from Eternal Vengeance.

    Now that we have guild alliance talks underway, lets decide upon a few things. From my understanding, the following guilds are involved:

    Eternal Vengeance

    Dark Templars

    Exiles

    Order of Justice

    One Hundred Swords

    (As a side note, I hope all guilds, as seperate entities, are internally spreading information about this alliance.)

    I'm also understanding that right now there are no talks of an actual MERGE, but we are setting up a psuedo-Xegony Alliance. I hope I'm correct in my thinking - I like that choice best right now. Following are few points of which I think are of critical importance, and need addressing. 1.) Global Meeting Place If we are to do something similar to xegony alliance, the first step would be to establish a forum, recognized by all 5 guilds, as the main meeting point for cooperative guild raids. It will simply be too difficult, for me atleast, to visit 5 different message boards and to find out raid info.

    2.) Representatives Lets have each guild pick out a few represenatives, or atleast have anyone who posts state which guild they are from. It is GREAT to see all activity from visitors to this board, but sometimes I get lost as to who is from which guild.

    3.) Regarding Xegony Alliance What we have here is a cooperative agreement between 5 guilds. In this sense, we are very different from XA. Honestly, my biggest gripe with XA has been the lack of strategy on raids. Basically, the XA tactic is to overcome with sheer man power. While we may have the option of doing that now, let's try to keep things somewhat tactical. I'm not saying we need to restrain the amount of people we send on a raid - not at all, but lets all be smart about things. I would hope we could all make a GOOD effort to work well together. This means lets try to focus on more than just "killing mobs." Let's take into consideration things like efficiency. Since we all now have access to a wonderful pool of resources from the alliance, lets all learn to work well together and not be easily satisfied.

    4.) My Vision Here is what I am seeing we are trying to set up. Tell me what you all think. I'm going to annoying type the important words in all caps In an effort to retain INDIVIDUALITY everyone is staying with their respective guilds.

    In a place, not yet decided, we are going to setup a forum for the five guilds to INTERACT. At this forum we discuss COOPERATIVE events which involve the guilds.

    At this forum we establish a RESPECTED raid schedule. Before we can expect any other entity on Xegony to respect what we want to do, we have to hold to our own. Hopefully these are four valid points on everyone's mind.

    -- I apologize for the stern-faced attitude of this post, but I think in everything there needs to be some level of seriousness. ~Alrien / Lerene

    What the...
    Posted by Hemrik on Thursday, May 31 @ 11:32:08 EDT
    I wake up this morning, check the weather website (gonna be 105F today), grab a drink and procede to log into Everquest.

    Error:2004 You have been temporarily suspended from Everquest due to inappropriate behaviour.

    Imagine my surprise. I figured maybe the servers were down or something, so I go check my email. In comes this email from GM Vedan:

    Greetings Hemrik

    Your everquest account has been suspended for 7 to 10 for aiding in stealing items from a hacked account. If you have any questions about your account please email eqaccountstatus@station.sony.com. Best of luck to you.

    Sincerely, Vedan

    I hurt my chin cuz it hit the floor so hard. I just replied to the email asking what specifically I did. I asked politely and am hoping for a response.

    Realistically I know this probably won't get my account unsuspended because we all know GM's are never wrong. In fact my reply for information will most likely be filed in the Trash (I'm pesimistic so I'll be pleasantly surprised if I do get a reply).

    What I find most fascinating is that no GM ever mentioned anything like this to me in game. I would have thought that a GM would at least confront someone with this kind of accusation before summarily suspending their access. Wait... What was I thinking.. *Smacks forehead*.. This is Verant. I keep forgetting this is a totalitarian dictatorship that requires nothing so mundane as evidence or facts. It only requires a rumour or bad word from someone in good favour with the one holding the reigns of power. We've all heard the stories of people having their accounts suspended because someone they wouldn't kowtow to was friends with the GM

    Just never imagined it would happen to me.

    I'll keep you all posted on what's going on, if I find out.

    PS. If this issue isn't resolved to my satisfaction, I will be leaving the game of Everquest permanently.


    May 30th Patch Info
    Posted by Atlantiss on Wednesday, May 30 @ 06:31:42 EDT
    May 30th, 1:00 am ------------------------------

    *** Fan Faire ***

    The new EverQuest expansion, Shadows of Luclin, will be displayed and demonstrated at the Minneapolis Fan Faire! Don't miss this chance to see the latest and greatest upcoming features in the world of Norrath!

    Registration for the event closes on June 4! Hurry and reserve your spot! Visit http://everquest.station.sony.com/fanfaire/ to register or for more information!

    *** Message about our Guide Program ***

    Hail citizens of Norrath!

    Hear my words, for I bring news of great importance.

    Today I present to you an adventure, perhaps the greatest the Lands of Norrath have ever offered. It is a quest of great proportions, and a journey fraught with danger. Fear not though my friends, for the spiritual rewards of this journey far outweigh any hardship involved.

    As of now, we are once again gathering an adventuring party to protect and serve the lands of Norrath. Scribes are lining up even now so that they may take your application to become a Guide of Norrath.

    We are seeking those strong in character, bright in wit, soft with compassion, and filled with joy.

    If you've ever felt good helping a fellow adventurer retrieve his corpse, or with helping someone when they felt all was lost, then this is truly the adventure for you. Or perhaps you feel your sense of humor could bring smiles to the faces of Norrathian citizens. Maybe your renowned acting skills might be of help. If you are kind, compassionate, and helpful, then look no further for the rewarding journey you've sought.

    If you are accepted, I will not mislead you, the journey will be difficult at times. You can be assured that you will be tried... and hopefully you will be found true. In the end though, the citizens of Norrath will benefit from your thoughtful participation, and the lands of Norrath will continue to become a better place.

    If you feel this is something you must be a part of, then I invite you to point your browser to: http://guideapp.everquest.com

    We ask so little of your time, but the benefits you can bring to Norrath are grand.

    A few things to remember: 1) You must have an active EverQuest account in good standing. 2) You must be 18 years of age, or older. 3) Your account cannot be shared with any other individual. 4) You will need good communication skills. 5) Positive attitudes lead to positive results. 6) Ability to participate as a part of team is a must.

    Come one, come all... help us to make the lands of Norrath better than they already are... come be a part of the EverQuest Guide Program!

    - The EverQuest Guide Program

    *** Important Reminder ***

    Due to some of the things we are going to be doing, there is a small chance that some corpses might disappear during the patch. GMs will have the ability to verify any corpses that are lost. That means that they can help you get them back if you do indeed lose one. It is also important to understand that attempting to scam a GM about a lost corpse could mean banning from EverQuest.

    ** IT IS IMPORTANT THAT YOU PETITION TO HAVE A MISSING CORPSE PROBLEM ADDRESSED WITHIN 5 DAYS OF THE PATCH! **

    We will not be recovering corpses that might be lost after that time. In fact, it would be best for you to petition immediately if you have a problem. Don't wait. If you are worried about a corpse that you were unable to deal with before the patch, then please log in and check on it right away after the patch.

    So please make certain that you have as many of your corpses cleaned up as possible before the next patch. We're not expecting a lot of corpses to disappear, but the fewer lying around, the less chance of them disappearing.

    Thanks.

    - The EverQuest Team

    *** Patch Message ***

    ** Zone Changes **

    - Runnyeye has been changed. Something is up in Goblin land...

    ** Spell and Song Changes and Bug Fixes **

    - We've been fixing holes in the spell stacking code. For the most part the changes fix bugs that allowed tricks to get spells to stack that were never intended to stack.

    - Increased duration on Bard Charm songs to 18 seconds from 12 seconds.

    - Fixed the Spell Scroll for Imbue Amber so that Erudite Clerics of Cazic Thule can scribe it.

    - The casting messages for Necromancer pet summoning spells have been changed to be more appropriate.

    ** Features and Interface Changes **

    - The variable %t will now work with /assist. So you can use /assist %t if you wish. Though it should have exactly the same effect as /assist without the %t.

    - %r returns "corpse" for corpses and "NPC" for targeted NPCs.

    - Sense traps failure message text color has been changed to user color 15 (same as other skill messages). The success message was already set to user color 15.

    - Ignore Lists now work the same way that the Friends List does. It has been expanded to 30 names per character per server. The new ignore list is stored in the same ini file as the friends list (_<#>.ini). The first time a character is created or loaded after the patch their new ignore list will be a copy of the old ignore list, but will from then on be unique to the character.

    - The Alt key now works in several new situations. It will now show item names when in the trade window. Because the trade items of the local player and the trading partner are so close together, the item names of the trading partner will always show up unless the player positions their mouse pointer over the local player part of the trade window, which will cause the game to only show the local player's trade item names.

    - The Alt key will show item names for items in your bank.

    - The Alt key will also pop up names for merchant items and corpse items when you hold it down in merchant mode or loot mode.

    - The Alt key will even pop up names for items in open containers when held down and the mouse cursor is over the open container window (they don't show up otherwise).

    - The client will now automatically reject a /duel request from any player that is on your /ignore list. Some players were using this to annoy players that had ignored them.

    - You also will no longer see /consent messages from people that are on your /ignore list (this was also something people were doing to annoy people that had ignored them).

    - /who GUILD (and the GUILD must be in caps) will now show you the members of your guild in your zone. You can also specify a guild name, such as /who "Irontoe Brigade".

    - /safelock command added. /safelock will lock the EverQuest interface (mostly just mouse clicks, keyboard commands/movement, and most slash commands) with the password that you supply to the command. You must use this command a second time, supplying the same password, to unlock the interface. Passwords are NOT case-sensitive. So to lock your interface, you would type /safelock password. You would do the same to unlock it. REMEMBER: If you forget what password you typed, you will need to reboot your machine.

    ** Item Changes **

    - All the shields in the game that have the Ribcage graphic should now also have the ribcage icon when they are in your inventory.

    - Tinkered Watchman helm should show up now.

    - Ring of Pureblood is now equipable only on your fingers instead of your waist.

    - Grand Master's Leg Wraps and Fists of the Grand Master are now No-Drop. They were intended to be No-Drop when they were created.

    - Peacekeeper Staff and the Bug Collection box are no longer equipable. There are problems that can arise when containers can be equipped, so we had to disable that for these items.

    - Sewing Kits: No existing sewing kits are being changed with this patch. But from now on, kits sold on vendors will be somewhat different. Large Sewing Kits will have 8 slots. Deluxe Sewing Kits will have 10 slots and weight slightly more than the Large Sewing Kit.

    - Changed the color of the Kael SK quest armor from a very pretty blue-green to a more appropriate reddish-gray.

    ** Other Stuff **

    - Changed the spelling of Troubador to Troubadour for the 55th level bard title. :)

    - King Tormax's spawn rate has been adjusted to be more in line with that of his counterparts in Velious. This is not the sort of thing that we will usually announce, as spawn times and things of that nature are part of the dynamic content of EverQuest. We're announcing this, however, to help avoid some confusion.

    - The EverQuest Team
