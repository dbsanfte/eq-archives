    Announcement of next patch
    Posted by Hemrik on Tuesday, May 15 @ 10:37:08 EDT
    Here's the scoop:

    Our next patch is planned for May 30th, probably some time in the wee morning hours here on the West Coast. The important part of the message is not that, though. It's this:

    Due to some of the things we are going to be doing, there is a small chance that some corpses might disappear during the patch. GMs will have the ability to verify any corpses that are lost. That means that they can help you get them back if you do indeed lose one. It also means that I should remind everyone that attempting to scam a GM about a lost corpse could mean banning.

    So please make certain that you have as many of your corpses cleaned up as possible before the next patch. We're not expecting a lot of corpses to disappear, but the fewer lying around, the less chance of them disappearing.

    Thanks.

    - The EverQuest Team

    Wow
    Posted by Hemrik on Friday, May 11 @ 20:42:43 EDT
    Ok you guys are looking at the website quite a bit lately. Don't worry, that's a good thing hehe.

    I was just going over the daily generated statistics and as of last night, the hit count for May (only 10 days) has surpassed that of April (all 30 days!). We had 41921 hits in April and so far we have 42383 hits in May. It's good to know you guys are using the website.

    Because if the increased usage, I've been seeing a few new bugs pop up here and there. Some of you probably noticed the Database Error in the forums section. Well never fear because there's a newer better version of the website in the works. I'm waiting on a finalized version of the backend part of the software I want to use (PHPNuke) before I really start building it. Some of the new features will include:

  • MUCH better forums area with support for multiple moderators.
  • Calendar of events for scheduling those guild events and raids.
  • Revised guildmember info page, more info like tradeskills, gear list, etc
  • Picture Gallery! Someplace to put snapshots of guild events and the like.
  • Live chat area. For those server patch days we'll be able to log onto the website and chat with each other in real time.
  • Real Email support. Not sure if I want to do this one or not but if I do then each member will have a real email address like Hemrik@exiles.dhs.org or something similar.

    If you've got any other ideas, I'd love to hear them. Better yet start a thread in the forums area with your idea and we can all discuss it.

    Cya in game!
