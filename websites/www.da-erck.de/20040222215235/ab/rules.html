Antonius Bayle Community Centre - Board Rules
Last revised 8th February 2004

 

Where to post
 

Keep rants, flames and raves and everything like that in the proper forum. Keep your tests in the proper forum, keep trade posts in the proper forum.. and so on. Failure in doing this may lead to your post being deleted instead of moved to the proper forum.
 

Being anonymous
 

Always ensure you sign your post with your ingame name, either it being (in) your ezboard username, or in the end of your post. Whilst some try to come up with a bright idea to sign with their twink(s) name(s), it's not a very bright idea from my point of view - and failing to follow this rule may lead to you being immediately and permanently banned. Conclusion? Use your main characters name. Flaming anonymous is very weak - and it's not allowed on this board.
 
 
Main Rules
 
 
Language
 

Being a European based server, the people who use this board will speak a variety of different languages. Although the most widely spoken language is probably English, and so will produce the most responses, posters are welcome to use any language they wish.
 
 
Language continued; (flaming)
 

Whatever language a post is started in, it should retain that language all the way through for communicative purposes, it is unfair to cut people out of a conversation they were following. Changing languages during a flame is not accepted.
 

Moved posts
 

Posts that either start out as, or become, rants will be moved to the NAG forum or in rare cases deleted. The moderators can also move posts to a different section of the board if they believe it to be in the wrong section.
 

Moderation and Understanding
 

While the administrators and moderators of this forum will attempt to remove or edit any generally objectionable material as quickly as possible, it is impossible to review every message. Therefore you acknowledge that all posts made to these forums express the views and opinions of the author and not the administrators and moderators (except for posts by these people) and hence will not be held liable.
 

Board Violation
 

If you find out a rule violation which havent been adressed, I urge you to contact Venven at Venven@Telia.com for the fastest assistance in the matter.
 

What you can and cannot post
 

You agree not to use this board for illegal purposes or for the transmission of material that is unlawful, harassing, libelous (untrue and damaging to others), invasive of another's privacy, abusive, threatening, or obscene, or that infringes the copyrights (rights of an owner of written material) or other intellectual property of others. Posts that contain racial, sexual, pornographic or other unsuitable wording will be removed.
 
 
Breaking the Board Rules
 
 
Guidelines and Actions
 

Guidelines on board behaviour should be followed when given by a board admin/moderator, in extreme cases failure to do so, may lead to warnings and ultimatly banning from the board.
Breaking the Board Rules may lead to you being immediately and permanently banned (and your service provider being informed). The IP address of all posts is recorded to aid in enforcing these conditions. You agree that the administrators and moderators of this forum have the right to remove, edit, move or close any topic at any time should they see fit.
 
 
Advertisement Rules
 

Do not use the Antonius Bayle Community Centre forums for advertising purposes that dosen't have to do with EverQuest. Any url's may be removed at the admins discretions, if the links advertise to non EQ related matters.
Do not advertise anything that would go against the EQ Eula.
 
 
Marketplace Rules
 

This forum is for EQ related sales and trades. This is not a place to trade your BMW Z3 for an CoF. All trades here must comply with EQ's EULA or will be removed from the boards. Management accepts no responsibility for 3rd party trades conducted on this forum.


Signature Rules
 

Not everyone is lucky enough to have a fast connection. All signatures should be kept small in size to minimise post loading times. While we do not have a limit, you shall do your best effort to keep them as small as possible. Signatures that disrupt the layout of the forum (for example, large sized personal pictures) will be removed. Signatures may be used as many times you like per thread. If your signature is too big, admin and mod's will have the right to disable your signature if it have been posted more then once per thread.


Misc
 

Posts used just to test signatures will be deleted. We have a Test Forum for those kind of posts.
 
It's obvious that the Rave forum will be used for raves only, but since some may not understand it, It's stated here so there will be no questions or doubts whatsoever.
