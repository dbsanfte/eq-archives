MINISTER OF RECRUITING

Position Open

The Minister of Recruiting is in charge of the Public Relations section of the guild, and all recruiting activities. He and his Captains are in charge the following things:

1. Handle all parties interested in guild membership or who are seeking information about the guild.

2. Maintain accurate records of the guild's rosters.

3. Maintain the guild's public appearance, and represent us at multi-guild gatherings if a High Council member is not available.

He can assign his available Captains to any of the above tasks to assist him in performing his duty. All Recruiting Captains report to him.
