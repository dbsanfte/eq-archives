FAQ

Q: What is the goal of this guild?

A: The Brotherhood of Power is dedicated to gaining power in all forms. We strive to have our members advance in levels and equipment, while at the same time seeking to gain the respect of the community.

Q: Don't you think 'power' is a somewhat undefinable goal?

A: Not at all. Power is a feeling inside of you. You know when you have it and when you don't. We seek to give our members that feeling, while remaining fair and balanced role-players.

Q: Right, so what types of benefits do members get?

A: Members are a part of the whole, therefore they can expect the full support of other members. The group is stronger than the sum of its parts. The guild also serves as a network for information.

Q: Who can join the Brotherhood of Power?

A: The Brotherhood of Power does not discriminate against any race, class, or gender. We readily accept people of all types based on their actions, not on a generalization of their occupation or appearance. The Brotherhood of Power refers to the universal bond between all people, and is not meant to exclude women from our ranks. We readily accept both genders with equal enthusiasm and benefits.

Q: How do I go about applying for membership?

A: Check out the Join Us link on the bottom of the guild page, that takes you to our form. Also, you can email Keldonus, the Guildmaster.


If you have any further questions, contact either the Guildmaster or the Warchief.
