MINISTER OF INFORMATION

Hadorram Bloodhilt

The Minister of Information in charge of the Information Services section of the guild. He and his captains are in charge of the following:

1. Have up-to-date knowledge of news regarding quests, i.e. new quests, broken or fixed quests, etc...

2. Maintain knowledge of game mechanics, such as modifications to gameplay resulting from patches, new rules, and the like.

3. Have rough ideas of the values of major items, so as to assist members who are interested in buying or selling.

He can assign his available Captains to any of the above tasks to assist him in performing his duty. All Information Services Captains report to him.
