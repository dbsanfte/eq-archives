THE FOLLOWING IS A BREAKDOWN OF THE HIERARCHY OF THE BROTHERHOOD OF POWER

THE HIGH COUNCIL

At the head of the guild is the Guildmaster and the Warchief

The GUILDMASTER is the one who sees that business is carried out appropriatly. It is the guildmaster that deals with conduct and heads all guilds activities.

The WARCHIEF is of equal rank to the guildmaster and is in charge of all military operations guild interactons of he Brotherhood.

THE THREE MINISTERS

MINISTER OF WAR is in charge of military action in the Warchief's absence, and reports directly to the High Council. This Minister takes command should the Warchief fall. He heads the Strategy section of the guild, and has a staff of Captains to aid him.

MINISTER OF RECRUITING is the head of recruiting, and makes it his business to contact all interested parties with the appropriate paperwork and contacts. He heads the Public Relations section, and all inqueries can be directed to him or one of his captains.

MINISTER OF INFORMATION is in charge of the Information Services section of the guild, which gathers knowledge of the latest happenings within the world, prices, quests, and other such things. His captains assist in gathering knowledge in areas he is unfamiliar with.

OTHER POSITIONS

CAPTAINS are members who have shown leadership abilities and initiative beyond that of a normal player. They enter into one of the three sections, under a Minister, and they specialize in that area. They are officers, and as such are entrusted to make decisions that can affect the whole guild.

AGENTS are the backbone of the guild's operations. All new members of the guild enter as agents, and work up from there. Agents are the all important members who make up our events and are in the eyes of the world.
