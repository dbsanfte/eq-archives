MINISTER OF WAR

Position Open

The Minister of War is the top ranking field commander of the guild. Should the Warchief fall in battle, or be unavailable, the Minister of War immediatly assumes command. He is in charge of the Strategy section of the guild, which is tasked with the following:

1. Know the layout and terrain of an area in which we are holding an event or raid. (We endorse eq-atlas maps)

2. Know the MoB's that are likely to spawn in that area, and any special strategies that must be used when dealing with them

3. Lead others on the field of battle in an efficient and competent manner, so as to minimize casualties and wasted time.

He can assign his available Captains to any of the above tasks to assist him in performing his duty. All Strategy Captains report their findings to him.
