MEDALS AND COMMENDATIONS

The following are the medals members of the guild can earn through combat, service, and loyalty.

Combat Service Medals(CSM's)

Level 1 Combat Service Medal: Awarded for acts of valor in the field resulting in the accomplishment of a guild goal that would otherwise be difficult or unattainable.

Level 2 Combat Service Medal: Awarded for demonstration in combat of skills above and beyond the call of duty and which save the life of a comrade or which present the guild with a previously unforseen major opportunity to accomplish a charter goal.

Level 3 Combat Service Medal: Awarded for extreme acts of bravery, courage, and skill, which save the lives of multiple comrades at incredible or mortal risk to self, or which advances the goals of the guild in such a way that it permanantly changes our position in the world for the better.

Social Service Medal's (SSM's)

Civic Service Star: Awarded to those members who show exemplary care for their community and who work willfully to make life better for those around them.

Cross of Honor: Awarded to those who have made such great additions to the community, that their positive influence can be felt throughout the whole of the continent, if not the whole of Norrath. Reserved for those who strive to bring order and understanding to the world no matter the cost to self.

Rank Medals

Ministers' Medal: The medal exclusive to the Ministers, symbolizing their high rank and power base.

Captains' Medal: The symbol of rank for those who have attained captain.
