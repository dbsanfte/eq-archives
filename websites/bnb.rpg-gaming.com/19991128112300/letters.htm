A Rangers Voice

I am a Ranger from Mithanial Marr, usually found in or somewhere around Qeynos. I go by the name Daphne, but people usually call me Daph.

Have you ever noticed that Rangers are quickly asked to join groups? I'll tell you why. It's not for the healing, that can be accomplished by those weak little mages. It's not for brute strength, those more-brawn-less-brains warriors can handle that. It's not for the pleasant company we make, although I am sure that's an added bonus. No no, it is for our ability to track pretty much anything.

What irks me, and I am sure a few of you have noticed this, is that others have limited understanding of our innate tracking ability. I have been in some groups where someone will ask me "whats on radar". Hello? Do I look like a bat? Gee, I don't know, the smell from the sweaty leather armor on Tank is keeping me from being able to sniff anything out. Sheesh. Others will never understand that to TRACK something is not simply to have some kind of clairvoyant grasp of things around us. Oh no. It is a skill that we as Rangers must hone. It is a special ability we are blessed with. We can take the tiniest bit of information dropped by things around us and gather evidence of their wherabouts. We are the Sherlock Holmes of Norrath. As such, we can not only track people, animals, and monsters, but occasionally we can find stuff others can't see that may be good to eat or drink. Although I am getting sick of roots, I do appreciate being able to gather fruits and berries. Some scoff at my gathering pods of water lying there in the fields, but they have forgotten my Ranger sense. If some nasty Ogre had been using the pod, I would of course be able to tell and would then toss it away.

Some folk take us for granted and ask us to forage or track something for them. To this I reply "puh-leez". It isn't some kind of magic spell, although I am handy with a few of those myself, it is a skill that takes practice. I can't simply will a bunch of rabbit meat into existance. Give me a little bit and I might find some, and then, if I am in the mood and/or I have too much food on me already, I may let you have some. I also cannot simply produce a Gnoll Captain or a Skeleton to appear out of nowhere. If one is around, I will pick up his trail, but silly folk who ask me to find them one over and over again are going to see me roll my eyes. If I am really fed up with that particular behavior, I might say, "Oh yes, over there...a nice skeleton laden with plat and holding a pretty sword. And he's weak. And tired. Go get him," all the while pointing towards a particularly large body of water, or worse, a Hill Giant.

Oh don't get me wrong, I am not so cruel. I just get a little sick of all those of all those who take for granted a Ranger's abilities. We are strong enough to hold our own toe to toe in a fight, unlike weak mages. We are smart enough to find food and drink, and maybe a few nice kills for our groups. We know how to use a bow like nobody's business and on top of all that great stuff, we have enough magical sense to heal others, snare foe, buff ourselves and our friends armor, and even cast the occasional flame lick. We are the most well-rounded class in all of Norrath, which goes especially double if you are a half elf and can see in the dark. But we are also the most taken for granted class as well. It's a tough life....well....no, not really. But it sure is fun!

Letter submitted by Daphne of Mithanial Marr

A Tale Of Treachery

I am here to tell of a treacherous act that occured on the Bristlebane server. A fellow guild member, an honorable, niave monk that goes by the name of Adrum, was the victim of a treacherous, heinous act by a witch named Crysania.
The tale begins nicely enough. Adrum and Crysania have many a time joined forces to rid the land of Norrath of the vermin that plaques its citizens. One day, they happened upon an unknown dark person. This darth person insulted the honor of Crysania, and being a just monk, Adrum challenged him to a duel. Unfortunately, Adrum was under the common misconception, one that was reinforced by Crysania, that your corpse will be looted and you will be relieved of one item of armour if you die in a duel. While Adrum's experience was not great, he valued what little armour a monk of his standing had. Crysania offered to hold his armour for him, suggesting this will assure that he will keep his armour. Believing that Crysania had any honor to defend, and recognizing the fact that as a monk, he can more than adequately defend himself without armour, he gave her all his armour. Once the evil witch Crysania had his armour, she and the Dark person, who was in cohoots with the evil witch, dissapeared.....gating, and taking with them all the hard earned armour that Adrum had. This tale has spread all across Norrath in the Bristlebane server. The tale of Crysania's treachery has reached to all cities and being that value justice and honor. The loss of armour is not the great crime here, but the act by the witch Crysania and her dark conspirator. I plead with all honorable members of Norrath to hunt down this witch!! Make her pay for her crimes.
And to Crysania, I will find you!! I will not rest till your blood becomes my water, until your heart becomes my sword's sheath, and until your head is mounted on my spear for all that value justice to see!!! You will pay for your crimes!!

Fletching, Archery N' Bows

Greetings Fellow Rangers, I have a few thoughts to share about fletching, archery, bows, and arrows. About fletching, I am currently at a skill level of 140 in this skill. Because of the new batch of 5 patch, it cost me around 350pp to get to 140 from 132. This includes the money I started with and the money I aquired reselling assembled arrows back to the vendor. Isn't this a bit much? Does any other trade skill cost nearly this much? I realize that jewelry and smithing costs alot to increase, but at least the products made in practice can be sold at this high level of training. No one will pay 1pp per arrow even if they can do 6 damage. The cost would be tolerable if the batch of 5 patch wasn't in effect. I would have had 5 times the number of chances to improve my skill that way. Now for archery. This skill, from what I have see, is used for 2 reasons- pulling a mob and finishing one off. Does anyone actually stay out of melee range and shoot instead of fighting? Unless they are hurt badly or not even in the group they engage in melee because it does so much more damage. Bows and arrows are barely utility items in EQ. They do not rank as the feared weapons from history.

The following is a paraphrase of the email I recently sent to eqmail@verant.com in the hopes that these ideas would be looked over and given proper consideration. If anyone else sees the reasoning behind these ideas, please join me in asking the lords and ladies at Verant to make a few changes if possible.

  • -Bow Specialization for Rangers. Let Rangers do damage with bows like normal weapons. Damage=weapon rating x2+1.
  • -Let Ranger made arrows be Damage=arrow rating x2+1. This might actually get someone to BUY some arrows (I'm a Ranger and I'm biased) No one pays 1pp per arrow even if they can do 6damage. Maybe if they could do 13damage they might consider it. This and the Bow Specialization might actually make bows use full for real combat and not just pulling. Besides, history shows the kind of damage a bow can do. It rivals any other handheld weapon save for firearms :).

    Now a slight review of the patch the made arrows "easier" to make. Yes, making arrows in batches of 5 is MUCH faster. But, it has raised the expense to train fletching exponentially. Maybe I'm the only one that feels this way. Maybe I just suck at my trade skill for some reason. All I can say is I recently spent over 350pp making the arrows that currently challenge me and I only pulled off 8 points in fletching., from 132 to 140. I realize this is a high trade skill and it should be expensive but I don't think any other trade skill touches that kind of cost. Also, their is a monetary return on skills like Jewelry Craft and Smithing. No one will pay 20pp for 20 arrows. Could the arrows be coded so they can be bought individually again and made either individually or in groups of 5? This would allow both speed in times of making lesser arrows to use or sell and efficiency in making HIGH cost arrows to train. If arrow parts could still be bought singularly I would have had 5 times as many chances to raise my skill with that 350pp. If I am wrong and this is NOT a ridiculous amount of money please tell me. Is Smithing this EXPENSIVE to raise at that level of mastery? I don't think so. Can people sell Smithing products at that level of mastery to pay for their training...yes. Do arrows sell...no.

    Thank you for listening to my thoughts, and once again, if you like the ideas and think they should be in the game, please write to eqmail@verant.com and let them know. I would also be happy to hear any ideas that anyone else has and help push those ideas if I believe in them.

    Letter submitted by Draxorian Stoneforge Ranger of Tunare - Mithanial Marr Server

    Why I Carry A Bow

    When I started playing Everquest one of the things I noticed was the difficulty I was having in chasing down my prey. So I thought if I had a bow I could shoot them as they were running away and decrease my TTKL (total time to kill and loot). Having a lower TTKL will average out to more experience quicker.

    So I did some research in the game and found out I needed to devote 1 skill point to Archery and 8 to Fletching to get started plus a Fletching Guide and a Fletching Kit. I bought the Fletching Kit from the fletcher by the clock towering South Queynos and Fletching Guide and the parts to make the arrows in Surefall Glade.

    After getting 20 arrow's made I equipped my bow loaded my arrows and went out hunting snakes at first. The first issue I encountered was I could not get my bow to fire in Auto Attack mode. After turning Auto Attack off I select my combat abilities and saw the Range Attack button had a bow on it. I rolled up on my first MOB and low and behold I had another problem this time I was to close. So I backed off but now I was to far. So I crept closer and finally I saw my hands and bow appear in front of me and loose and arrow.

    To my surprise the arrow hit the snake and the snake came right over to me I didn't have to chase it. I then kicked it went to Auto Attack by selecting my Melee weapon and boom it was over.

    What I love about this is that the arrow basically delivers my prey to me, I get an arrow in the target for damage, and a free kick in before I ever swing my sword! The if the MOB runs away I just stay close enough to shoot it in the back.

    The other thing that is cool is arrows are just 1 Silver Piece if you don't want to make them. Which I find well worth the price to get home delivery of kills.

    Other things you should be aware of is that once fired arrows are gone, if your firing into a crowd they can block you shot.

    Bears, Wolves & Philosophy - Revisited

    Greetings Dalvarian & All Rangers

    On this issue of hunting bears and wolves outside Surefall, I am torn between 2 basic issues. First the need for experience and money; Second the health of the region. While no self-respecting Ranger would state explicitly that we 'need' money, facts are facts. Some (most) things in this game require purchasing power. Even to fletch ones own arrows requires that one buy the parts to fletch. Now where does this money come from? Most likely it was taken as loot from some creature killed or sold as an item found on a conquered foe. Moreover, how many of us have had to pay for a bind or a heal or whatever one may need to continue our adventure without overwhelmingly foolhardy risk? (Read, "Hunting Emperor Crush in Crushbone while bound in Qeynos Hills"). Now we have the question of, "What is appropriate to hunt?" My personal philosophy regards common sense. If one is hunting for money, hunt those creatures which will attack another Good Alignment PC's (Skeletons, Gnoll pups, etc). If one is hunting for experience, attempt to do the same as many times money and experience go hand-in-hand. In all my playing time (played = 14 days, 10 hours, 19 minutes), I have never been attacked by a bear or wolf. I Have, however, been attacked by both rabid bears and rabid wolves; which leads directly to my second point.

    In my view, Rangers are the protectors of the outdoors and those who roam there. As such, if a rabid wolf poses a health risk to travelers, it should be exterminated for the good of the community. Do I actively seek out rabid wolves and bears? No, I do not. But I have no qualms about a Ranger hunting these diseased NPC's. I agree with both Kel'anth and Treena that bears and wolves should not be hunted for hunting's sake. The point regarding Hagar Sureshot rebuffing a "killer of bears and wolves" and the attacks by Holly Windstalker and Cros Treewind on hunters of bears and wolves is well taken. These are Rangers killing PC's for their actions. What more clear example may one have for what is and is not appropriate game for rangers to hunt? I cannot think of one. I hunt those creatures which will attack either me or another Good Alignment PC. In 16 levels of advancement, I have never been attacked by a non-rabid bear or wolf; therefore, I will not hunt them.

    Are these sacred creatures? I do not believe so. I do believe that they are part of the mold from wence we came and should not be hunted for hunting's sake.

    May Tunare Be Served

    To Bow Or Not To Bow

    The article written by Draxorian Stoneforge lays out the problems clearly. I agree with everything he has to say and I have another idea. I would like to see all users except for Rangers to get damage as follows. Arrow x2+1 (Fletcher made arrows only) + Bow x2 (Fletcher made bows only) + 1/2 skill level of the archer (when using a Fletcher made bow only). This would give players a real incentive to buy a bow and arrows made by a Fletcher. As it is now I have seen Rangers! that have never used a bow. One of them a 27th level Ranger. A bow in the hands of a Ranger is a dangerous thing so I think Rangers should get 2/3 skill level added. With this method the same bow will do different amounts of damage based on who is shooting it. Some would say this is unbalanced but if you look at the history of the bow it is realistic. A man 100 yds from an archer with a long bow is facing suicide. A bow against a lvl 20+ MOB is used as a draw and nothing else. The most damaging bow in the game would be a Rough Darkwood Bow. 25 + 7 (bladed, steel shaft). This may be alot of damage for a 4-5 level MOB but it is not even a very small dent in a 20+ MOB. It just makes it mad. It should not be that way. Rangers should also get a shorter delay time as they increase in skill. I would also like to see the "Too high/below target" restriction removed. This will allow some instances of sniping but that is also a strong suit of the bow. Rangers have their melee skills reduced. They are not as good as warriors in melee. This is the way it is supposed to be. Put a bow in a Rangers hand though and he should be one of the most powerful of classes. A Ranger with a bow should be able to hold his own against anything that a Warrior of comparable level could. As the situation stands now the bow is a weak and pitiful weapon that is close to being deadweight. It should be one of the most powerful non-magical weapons available.
