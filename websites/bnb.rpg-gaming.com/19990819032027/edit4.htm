Editorial



Greetings all. Well gee, I haven't had any connection problems to date, (knock on wood). It looks like they have gotten things straighten out. Well, they have lessened the exp ponts when you die and had changed the respawn rate of creatures. I don't know if this is a good thing or bad. It seems like they are making the game easier when they said in the beginning you would have to work and earn in this game. I guess some like it easy. Me, I get a better sense of accomplishment when the game is tougher. I hope this game isn't turning into the Realm. That would be a shame. On the plus side, it is a little nicer to have more monster activity, especially when there are a lot of people playing. Although I hope in the future they have a little more NPC activity, especially in the towns. They keep saying they are adding more quests, I can't seem to find any new ones in the Qeynos area anyways but the place is pretty big so maybe I am not looking in the right areas. I haven't sent Verant any of my ideas that I discussed in the last issue. They seem to be concentrating on fixing the things that are broke, which is a good thing. I hope that soon they will start to focus on making the game more "alive" by having player homes and villages, armor and weapons wearing out. Maybe it can't be done....but if it could.....it would be a game worth getting red eye over.....
