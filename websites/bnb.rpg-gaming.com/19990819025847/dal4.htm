The Creation of A Ranger, Dalvarians Chronicle



Well I have finally made it to level 13 at the time of this writing. And its been slow going so far soloing. A few things to discuss.

First, Magic. All my magic skills are really up there now and the meditate skill is proving invaluable. Now I dont have to wait forever to get my mana points back up once depleted. I just hit meditate and it replenishes much faster. However when you meditate, you are oblivious to your surroundings, so pick a safe spot before you do this. Or if your in a group, have someone watch your back. Monsters LOVE to attack when your not paying any attention.

My Tracking Skill, Forage Skill, Fishing Skill are in the 70's now. I have foraged more food than I know what to do with. I wish they would let you sell your foraged food to the shopkeepers. I am still hunting in the T2 area in the Karan Plains, I have been to both bridges, but since they have made the bandit activity more aggressive, I have been having fun with them. I have only crossed the bridges to see what was over there. It's not the best place to be when your solo. I'll wait till 15 before I go. There is still plenty of things out here to kill, so I am not that rushed to get to the other side of the bridge. Not much to report this time, I have been doing the remodeling thing on my home with the weather being great out here in Ohio. So I havent played EQ much, so anyone willing to write a few articles or find out about more quests to take some of the load off until I get my house finished send them in. It would be greatly appreciated. I'll get on EQ and play the heck out of it shortly!

As always if you have any information yourself that would help others no matter how small, or worthless you think it is, send it in. It could make the difference between life and death to a fellow Ranger.
