Fletching Guides



Well, here is what I have learned about Fletching and bow making as a Level 10 Ranger with a skill of 55 in the craft.
  1. I suggest you invest very few points in Fletching to get it started. I would recommend between 4 and 8. I personally used 5 pts at level 5 as a ranger to get going. The skill improves rather rapidly.
  2. Be warned that fletching is NOT fun. It is very tedious. You have to put 1 of each of the 4 arrow components in to the kit 1 at a time. If you put in a stack you will get back 1 arrow if successful. So, before you start get comfortable and be ready to excercise your wrist often.
  3. Begin by buying up 20 of each component, and get started. Buy the cheapest of each of the 4 components, so each attempt costs you 4 copper. You will use these arrows to archer with as they are the basic arrow, providing a range of 50 and damage of 1. You usually rise 2-4 pts per grouping of 20 attempts. So depending how many training points you invest, you will reach the cap of triviality on the basic Wood Arrow in 3 or 4 batches of 20 arrows. Not too bad.
  4. The next type of arrow is the Class 1 Wood Arrow. This requires the 3 cp notch, which brings your cost per attempt to 6 cp. You will fail a lot at this point in making arrows. Failures can increase your skill, however, and the rate of increase is comparable to the previous arrows. I would use these arrows as well to archer with, as they provide a range of 75 and damage of 1. The cap of triviality for these is a rating of 36. Making a rough guess at this point you have probably spent 1 to 1.3 platinum on increasing your skill to 35. You also have a nice number of arrows in your inventory. I would now go and get a bow....
  5. DO NOT BUY A BOW! The best part of being a fletcher is that you get to make bows. The store bought bows are very expensive and not good. With a skill of 35 you can easily make a bow that is better than the store bought ones. I would recommend buying the bowyering book, a Hickory staff, and some Linen string. This is rather cheap, and your success rate should be very high at 35 skill. This lets you make a Rough Hickory Recurve Bow with stats of Damage: 9 Delay: 46 and Range:50.
  6. Now go out and increase your archery skill. The best way by far to do this is beat on animals that tend to run from you. When they run do not chase. Instead you pull out the bow and you get easy target practice. All the players I've seen w/ high Archery skill do this. The second way to get in free shots is to group w/ a warrior or other melee type and fire at the monster's back while they are engaged.
  7. Once you cap out at a skill of 36 I'd make basic arrows to practice archery with. Otherwise your expense may increase. At some point however you wanna go above 36 to make better bows. Making Class 2 Wood Arrows will do this. They are composed of the better notch, and the better fletch. The cost per attempt is now 1 sp 1 cp. At this point things move up much more slowly. I only went up 1-2 pts per batch of 20 attempted. The skill cap for this type of arrow is a fletching skill of 46.
  8. To go beyond 46 I suggest moving to the basic Bone Arrow, which includes a Bone Shaft and all the other basic 1 cp components. The cost per attempt now is almost 2 sp. I haven't reached the skill cap for this item yet. I sell back the made arrows, because in my opinion they are way to expensive to use. I take a loss on the sale, but I think it's the right move.
  9. At this point you can make an Elm Bow, which provides an important range bonus. Using a linen string and elm staff you can make a Rough Elm Recurve Bow, with stats of Damage: 12 Delay: 47 and Range: 75. Getting better. I now use Class 1 arrows as my basic archery arrows as they both have ranges of 75 and I can now hit for 13 pts per shot. The cost of the components for this bow are around 2.5 Platinum.
    Submitted by Charron of Kanara


    A Bit More About Fletching


    Fletching, the art of making arrows from component pieces, is one of the oldest weapon crafts on Norrath. This tome details the construction of arrows, as well as providing information on the sister craft of the Bowyer. Bowyers and Fletchers both require a Fletching Kit to practice their trade. Fletching kits can be purchased from archery shops.

    Arrows

    There are four parts to every arrow. Generally, the cheaper the part, the easier it is to assemble. Expensive componenets produce superior arrows.

    Nock

    There are three types of Nocks, differenciated by the size of their grooves. The larger to groove, the easier the arrow is to make. The smaller the groove, the farther the arrow travels.

    Shaft

    The shaft of the arrow can be made from a variety of different materials. The most common material used for shafts is wood. Bone shafts are stronger than wood, and Porcelain stronger than Bone. Steel is the strongest material used for arrow shaft.

    Tip

    There are many designs for arrow tips. The most common is the point tipped arrow. Slightly more damaging is the hooked tip - it's small blades cause tearing and bleeding. Actual bladed arrows do more damage than the hook tipped arrows, though they are easier to remove.

    Fletch

    The fletch, or feathering, of the arrow influences the distance the arrow travels, as well as the impact delivered upon a succesful hit. The easiest pattern to fletch is a round cut. Slightly more difficult is the parabolic cut, whos spin gives the arrow additional flight range. The shield cut fletch pattern has a shorter range than either the round of the parabolic cut, but produces a spin providing superior armor penetration. There are a few parts to Bow crafting, not fully explained in the manual. I will attempt to clarify this wonderful craft to they Laymen.

    Rough Wood Staffs

    First you must decide on the strength of the bow you wish. Damage done buy the bow is dependant on the strength of wood and the string used. The more costly the wood the stronger it is, and subsequently the harder to craft. Starting from weakest the selections are: Hickory, Elm, Ash, Oak, Darkwood. You may also fashion a bow from Steel and Ceramic, these being stronger still than wood.

    String

    The strength of string also determines the damage done by the bow. The better quality of string the better the damage. There are 3 types of string, they are: Hemp, Linen, and Silk. Only the best bows are strung with silk.

    Tools

    Next we come to increasing the quality of the bow, wich usualy means decreasing the dealy or rate at wich the bow is fired. Unfortunately damage is also decreased by this process. There are 2 tools to date: Whittling Knife, and the Plane. Once again each is an increase in quality and price. Each again is more difficult to use.

    Pulleys

    To pack a whallop, adding pulleys will cause damage to increase dramaticaly. There is only one class of pulley but you may add one or two, they are unfortnately very expensive! There is only one kit to buy for crafting both Bows and Arrows. To use it for bows, it is fairly simple. At beginning levels you need only buy the staff and string, place both in the kit and press combine. As with any craft you will wind up making a bow, or not. Using the tools is both expensive and risky as they are expended when added to the kit. A tool can be used with Elm or better wood, it is simply placed in the kit with the staff and string. Pulleys, I have not used as of yet but I will have to assume you place them, either 1 or 2 into the kit with the staff and string. I will update as soon as I can aford to find out.

    Fletching Guide

    Ok, I'm a lvl 8 Ranger Half Elf and here's what I learned so far. I think I have found easier less time consuming way to make arrows.

    1. Fill all your inventory slots and put the fletching kit in slot 1.
    2. Right click on the next guy next to Hager Sureshot (he's the only one I know of that sells all you need to make arrows). So now you have the buy window opened on the left side of the screen.
    3. Open your inventory so you can see your ammo slot for arrows.
    4. Open your fletching kit and position it so you can see your ammo slot behind it.
    5. Left click the Field Arrow Point, purchase, and notice that the arrow is now in the fletching kit.
    6. Click on the Wood Shaft, purchase.
    7. Click on the Round Cut, purchase.
    8. Click on the down arrows 2 times to see the grooves.
    9. Click on the Large Groove Cut, purchase.
    10. Now you have all the items to make a Class 1 arrow in the fletching kit.
    11. Click Combine and you will see a red or blue message. If blue you have succeeded in making an arrow and it is being held by your cursor even though it does not show up on the screen. Now click on your portrait and watch the ammo slot, it will show one arrow. If the message is red, you have failed. I can make about 20 arrows in 5 minutes.
    10. After you make 20 arrows and your ammo slot is full, ESC from buying and move the arrows to some inventory/bag slot to make room for more in the ammo slot. I found out that if you put one arrow in another slot or bag and have your ammo slot full, and then make the same type arrow, it will go to that slot/bag just by clicking on your portrait. You could open that bag to watch the arrows go into also. Please becareful what items you buy! I don't want to tell you how much coin that I have personally wasted by accidentally buying the wrong stuff and then combining them only to see that I have failed, or it was the wrong stuff to try and combine. 10g's shot just like that.

    Now for some comments and notes.

    I noticed that when I was more skilled in making the basic arrow, I got a message saying something like "You are far more skilled then this" or "This item is trivial for someone of your skill level". Anyways at that time I started to make a better arrow buying the next cheaper item (of the four), which was the med. groove nock and made only those arrows until that message came up again, then I went up to the next level Class 2 arrows - Field Arrowhead, Wood Shaft, Parabolic Fletch and Med. Groove Nock. My fletching skill is currently 48. I thought it could only go to 45 (5 times my level plus 5 is the formula I heard). I have never been able to make more than 1 arrow at a time, I think EQ maybe working on this? I don't know how to use a whittling blade or plainer. Maybe just having them in your inventory uses them? I noticed that if I have arrows of the same type in my bag (or probably slots too) that they automatically were being used. It showed 1 arrow in my ammo slot but my bag of arrows was being depleted. YEEPEE! Auto loading. But only the same class that was in your ammo to start with. And a final note on quest's in general. Do them if they are easy but don't waste time. Hunt and get those levels. A lot of quests have more than one part and the second part seems to be much harder. So level up first. This has been (is being) learned the hard way. That's all I have for now. I hope this helps out other fellow Rangers.

    Cut Down Your Time

    Hail all of Norrath,
    For those of us (the Rangers), who strive to become jack of all trades and fighting styles. I have yet another way to cut down the time it takes to make arrows. If one can afford it, I suggest buying 2 fletching kits. Open up inventory and both fletching kits. Put 20 of each component into one of the kits. Select one of the components and subtract one. You will now have 19 in you cursor. Drop this bundle into the empty kit. Do the same thing till there are one of each in the starting kit. Hit combine. When an arrow is made drag over to inventory icon. Repeat until all components are used. This here speeds up the process and keeps you from buying the wrong pieces and combining them. We all know time is money in the land of Norrath. Although the extra kit raises the cost on the spot. But trust me I have not missed it. I make arrows a lot faster now. Another tip get a quiver. you can fill it up with the same type class of arrow. I have not verified this, but I also noticed that my weight doesn't go up when all my arrows are in the quiver. Plus that gives you 100 arrows to hold; 80 arrows in the quiver and 20 in your ammo slot. Use this to the best of your ability. LIVE ON MOTHER OF ALL, TUNARE!!!!!! Your children will watch over you.
