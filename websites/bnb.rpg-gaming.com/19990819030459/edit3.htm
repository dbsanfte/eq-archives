Editorial



Greetings all. Well what can you say about the past month concerning connectivity? Pretty muched sucked. Servers still going up and down, UUnet and CERFnet to blame, packet loss, cats and dogs living together, total anarchy. Recently the bumps have been smoothed out though. It would be nice to be able to play for a month without experiencing any problems. With every fix, they say things are much more stable now, and there shouldn't be anymore problems. And a week later it's back to the ole drawing board for another router solution. But I do have to give them credit, the system is slowing becoming more stable than before. I don't know why they can't setup servers on the east side to lighten the load of the routers on the west side. Maybe nobody wants to be shipped out to the east because they won't be able to "hang ten" during their lunch breaks. Whatever the reason is I hope they get it straightened out once and for all, so they can concentrate on improving the game. It needs it in a lot of areas.

One area I would like to see improved on are the Inns. This is the first "RPG" game that I have played where the Inns are about as empty as my wallet after a Friday night of drinkin. The Inns are supposed to be the central focal point of any RPG town where rumors abound and all of the towns folk gather at night giving the player a "flavor" of the town. And the bartender's are full of info. You can't even get the bartender's to say jack squat to you as it is now. At least make the bartender's a little more interactive. The Inns are nothing more than a store for buying overpriced ale and wine when water is cheaper over at Voleens or Sneeds.

The second is to make skills have some value in the game. Nothing in EQ, item wise, gets destroyed. This is a bad thing. That creates a glut. A glut will drive prices down. A glut of any one thing could make your skills useless. Why? Everybody has it. Once x amount of any item is made in the game, it's in the game forever. However, if they were to impliment your armor wearing out and your weapon becoming dull, (as in Might And Magic 6), then there would not be a glut. Demand would be high, skills would come into play. Also the level of your skill would determine the AC rating of armor or damage of weapon. If my skill in Tailoring is a level 22, and Joe Blow level is a 45, then the tunic that he makes would have a higher AC than the tunic that I make. After all, he is more experienced. This would create unique and sought after items in the game. This would apply to all the skills.

The third is being able to have the ability to have a home in this game. A place to hang my hat. Right now we are all wandering nomads. No real place to call home. It would be nice if they could set aside in each zone or certain zones a village where one could buy a house. You would enter your house through a password entered by you. Similar to Sierra's The Realm. And yes Rogues could break into your home through a back door with the use of lockpicks. If he was successful in breaking in, then his PvP flag is turned on for good. And after a certain number of unsuccessful attempts, the lockpicks would break. And the Rogue would have to go buy another expensive set. Now if your home was broken into, you would be upset. Even want a bounty on the guys head that broke into your home and stole that banded armor you worked so hard for or got from a "mule". Hmmm, bounty hunter as a profession...could be fun and adds a whole new deminsion to the game. Also I would think one would be more inclined to protect something that is "his" in a game. Attack a city in the game and it still stands. There is nothing in the city that is mine that I have to worry about. The people and shops will be there again as if it never happened. However, attack my village where I have a home, where I have my personal belongings that I have aquired, thats another story. Now I have something to protect. This could lead to village attacks and village wars. A whole new ball game. Maybe a player could even volunteer to be the village idiot. I've seen a few players that would fit the bill perfectly.

I have sent my ideas in to Verant, they liked the idea about armor that degrads over a period of time. They wanted to know if I had any others. I was going to send in the idea of a player having a home, and about villages but at this point in time I would much rather be able to sit down at my computer, fire up EQ and not have to worry about seeing that familiar "Could not connect to login server, please try again shortly" message, or the dreaded "Server Not Responding". Get EQ running like it should, worry about the icing later....
