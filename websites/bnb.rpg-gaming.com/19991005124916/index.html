Who Are The True Masters Of Archery?
Dalvarian, I would like to make something known that seriously bothers me, Rangers are not the masters of archery, Warriors are. Here's the facts...

Ranger archery skill level can reach 200. Warrior archery skill level can reach 200. Warriors are able to make critical hits with the bow and arrow.

Each class has a crest, a logo of what that class is all about... a Warrior's crest is that of a sword and shield... a Ranger's crest is that of a bow and quiver...

Other than the fact that Rangers are rather limited on equipment for being a fighting class, we get our very own bow.. the Trueshot longbow.

-Trueshot longbow - 20 damage, 45 delay, 100 range... ELF/HLF - RNG A very nice bow indeed, however... there is a more powerful bow that can be used by any of the archery learned classes...

-Runed Oak Bow - 21 damage, 43 delay, 100 range... ALL - PAL WAR RNG ROG SHD

Once again... if the Trueshot bow is meant to set the Rangers above the other classes as the masters of archery, it has failed.

Finally, in discussing this matter with Verant officials, it seems that critical hits are an all or nothing deal, if a class can hit critically with their melee weapons, they can with a bow, a thrown object as well. This is bad news for all the Ranger's who believed that they would be the most powerful archers as historical Rangers are known for. For one to know exactly where to aim their trusted arrows to do as much damage as possible would take years of training and practice, teachings from the finest marksmen in all the land. A Warrior is a master of combat, yes... but a Warrior would be considered the masters of armed combat, getting in the face of danger and teaching said foe a lesson in the fine art of dying, by the blade...

When speaking to Maesyn Trueshot, Guild master high of the Champions of Faydark, he expresses great pride in the Ranger's abilities with the bow. "...we are the finest marksman in all of Norrath, with our trueshot bows we never miss a target no matter what the conditions or distance...". Another observation, every Ranger guild master in Kelethin displays their bows proudly, other weapons shrugged aside. In surefall glade there is an archery range, where Rangers from all across the land come to finely tune their skills as a sharpshooter...

I have yet to see a warrior guild master running around with his bow... or speaking so proudly of his skill in wielding it. The bow and arrow are very much emphasized by the Ranger guilds, the guild merchants selling bows and arrows for the young Ranger to get familiar with as soon as possible. From the warrior guilds I have visited, I have yet to find a friendly merchant who will replenish my supply of arrows.. or to sell me a new quiver because the old one has seen too much use and needs to be retired, this is simply because archery is just not as important to the proud Warrior.

Finally, I close with this... Warriors deserve to have their critical hits, with any weapon they pick up.. they are masters of physical punishment.. they know where to strike to make every blow count. Rangers are archers, they train their entire lives with a bow.. a Ranger should not be able to do critical hits with their close combat weapons, we just aren't made for it.. not as strong as the mighty warriors, but very clever.. and we should know where to fire an arrow to find it's mark.

I play both a Warrior and Ranger, this is an unbiased account of what I have observed, and what should be well known. Many know of this, and have expressed their concerns with Verant, the guides, the developers... but I am sure that many proud Rangers are unaware of this inconsistancy... help make it known, help change it.

A Warrior of 30th rank, a Ranger of 40th rank

Bears, Wolves & Philosophy - Revisited

Greetings Dalvarian & All Rangers

On this issue of hunting bears and wolves outside Surefall, I am torn between 2 basic issues. First the need for experience and money; Second the health of the region. While no self-respecting Ranger would state explicitly that we 'need' money, facts are facts. Some (most) things in this game require purchasing power. Even to fletch ones own arrows requires that one buy the parts to fletch. Now where does this money come from? Most likely it was taken as loot from some creature killed or sold as an item found on a conquered foe. Moreover, how many of us have had to pay for a bind or a heal or whatever one may need to continue our adventure without overwhelmingly foolhardy risk? (Read, "Hunting Emperor Crush in Crushbone while bound in Qeynos Hills"). Now we have the question of, "What is appropriate to hunt?" My personal philosophy regards common sense. If one is hunting for money, hunt those creatures which will attack another Good Alignment PC's (Skeletons, Gnoll pups, etc). If one is hunting for experience, attempt to do the same as many times money and experience go hand-in-hand. In all my playing time (played = 14 days, 10 hours, 19 minutes), I have never been attacked by a bear or wolf. I Have, however, been attacked by both rabid bears and rabid wolves; which leads directly to my second point.

In my view, Rangers are the protectors of the outdoors and those who roam there. As such, if a rabid wolf poses a health risk to travelers, it should be exterminated for the good of the community. Do I actively seek out rabid wolves and bears? No, I do not. But I have no qualms about a Ranger hunting these diseased NPC's. I agree with both Kel'anth and Treena that bears and wolves should not be hunted for hunting's sake. The point regarding Hagar Sureshot rebuffing a "killer of bears and wolves" and the attacks by Holly Windstalker and Cros Treewind on hunters of bears and wolves is well taken. These are Rangers killing PC's for their actions. What more clear example may one have for what is and is not appropriate game for rangers to hunt? I cannot think of one. I hunt those creatures which will attack either me or another Good Alignment PC. In 16 levels of advancement, I have never been attacked by a non-rabid bear or wolf; therefore, I will not hunt them.

Are these sacred creatures? I do not believe so. I do believe that they are part of the mold from wence we came and should not be hunted for hunting's sake.

May Tunare Be Served

To Bow Or Not To Bow

The article written by Draxorian Stoneforge lays out the problems clearly. I agree with everything he has to say and I have another idea. I would like to see all users except for Rangers to get damage as follows. Arrow x2+1 (Fletcher made arrows only) + Bow x2 (Fletcher made bows only) + 1/2 skill level of the archer (when using a Fletcher made bow only). This would give players a real incentive to buy a bow and arrows made by a Fletcher. As it is now I have seen Rangers! that have never used a bow. One of them a 27th level Ranger. A bow in the hands of a Ranger is a dangerous thing so I think Rangers should get 2/3 skill level added. With this method the same bow will do different amounts of damage based on who is shooting it. Some would say this is unbalanced but if you look at the history of the bow it is realistic. A man 100 yds from an archer with a long bow is facing suicide. A bow against a lvl 20+ MOB is used as a draw and nothing else. The most damaging bow in the game would be a Rough Darkwood Bow. 25 + 7 (bladed, steel shaft). This may be alot of damage for a 4-5 level MOB but it is not even a very small dent in a 20+ MOB. It just makes it mad. It should not be that way. Rangers should also get a shorter delay time as they increase in skill. I would also like to see the "Too high/below target" restriction removed. This will allow some instances of sniping but that is also a strong suit of the bow. Rangers have their melee skills reduced. They are not as good as warriors in melee. This is the way it is supposed to be. Put a bow in a Rangers hand though and he should be one of the most powerful of classes. A Ranger with a bow should be able to hold his own against anything that a Warrior of comparable level could. As the situation stands now the bow is a weak and pitiful weapon that is close to being deadweight. It should be one of the most powerful non-magical weapons available.

Bamas, Ranger of Surefall Glade,Rodcet Nife


More On The Killing Of Wolves & Bears

As a ranger of the 7th season, I've been spending much of my time lately in the North Qeynos hills, hunting gnolls. It was a recent experience that spurred me to respond to the ongoing debate about the killing of wolves and bears outside of Surefell Glade. While adventuring in the hills with a small party, the leader would continously halt us, warning of danger. After the third or fourth time, I asked what (or who) he and the others were so afraid of, when all appeared to me to be completely safe. "Holly Windstalker," they all replied. "She'll kill you on sight!" Holly Windstalker, I thought to myself...don't I know her as a friendly NPC?

The next day, I tracked and found Holly, and a quick glance told me she viewed me favorably, so I struck up a conversation. In an instance, all became clear. As a defender of the forest (and remember, this is in the Qeynos Hills outside of Surefell Glade), she has pledged her life to attacking anyone who preys on bears and wolves. I can only assume that my fellow party members from the previous day had attacked wolves and bears indiscriminately. But then, that brings up another issue -- why wouldn't they attack bears and wolves? We're rangers, my friends, and have been taught to love and respect these particular woodland creatures and those same creatures show their appreciation and mutual respect by not attacking us in turn. I have never been attacked by any species of wolf or bear (and that includes the rabid variety) anywhere in Norrath, but see others attacked without provocation all the time.

So, it is pretty obvious to me that the ranger code forbids the hunting of wolves and bears ANYWHERE, and not just in Surefell Glade. Hey, don't take my word for it, just ask Holly Windstalker -- because you can.

Angrivar, Ranger of the 7th Season, Karana
