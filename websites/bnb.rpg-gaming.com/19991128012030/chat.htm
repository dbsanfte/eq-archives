Welcome to Blade N' Bow real time net chat. You MUST have JAVA enabled for this to work. Please wait till everything loads. It does take a few moments depending on server traffic. If you would like to change your name when you are in the chat room from Guest to your EQ name, just type "/nick EQname" in the box at the bottom where you type your text. Just hit enter after typing your text and you will see it appear in the chat window. This is real time chat and not a browser chat where you have to hit reload after each sentence. Please be kind to others in the room, if I get e-mail from people saying that they always get flamed, then I will remove it from my site. The room will not be moderated. Users of ICQ can also join without being on my site. This is not a private chat room. I take no responsibility for anything said. Use it at your own risk. If you would like to private chat with someone in the room, then double click on their name, and a separate chat window will open for you to chat privately with that person.
