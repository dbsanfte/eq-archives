Hints And Tips To Blackburrow


Blackburrow, a popular hangout in EverQuest for newbies and mid-range characters. It is also the way to the city of Halas, Permafrost and Everfrost Peaks. Your starting level should be around 5-6 depending on your equipment. At level 12 and higher you can pretty much walk around without to much fear. If you only hunt in parties, then keep it small. It can get awfully cramped inside at times. My personal opinion would be a party of no more than three. Blackburrow is a progessive dungeon. By that I mean you will find low level monsters at the entrance and upper levels. As you go deeper in, the monsters become tougher opponents. It has a good distribution of monsters, good low/mid-level experience, plenty of goodies to aquire and close to Surefall Glades for when you need a safe haven. Gnolls who are part human and part wolf, enemy of Surefall Glades, are the main attraction. Not to bright in the intelligence department, but can be a dangerous foe in battle, especially in tight corners such as Blackburrow as they fight in packs, like wolfs. If you get one alone, it will turn tail and run when he realizes he's losing the battle, (here is where the Snare spell comes in handy). However, attack one and don't be surprised if four or more gnolls join in the battle. Its very easy to get a train started inside Blackburrow. I have seen many gnolls of all types trailing a fleeing victim. So if your going solo, becareful. Things can get out of hand REAL quick due to the gnoll "pack mentality".

Don't forget about the trap door's in the hut's, as they drop you right smack in the middle of a elite gnoll guard room, (take it from someone who knows first hand). The long ramp down takes you to the main rest area of the zone which is a good place to hang around, get help and hunt the random gnolls nearby. Once you hit about a 9 in level, most of the zone will be open for hunting as long as you're hunting in a party. If your going solo through it all, you might want to hit a 13 in level depending on your armor and weapon before going deep into Blackburrow. Remember, gnolls attack in packs which is what makes Blackburrow dangerous for soloist's. It only takes one extra gnoll guardsman, or train, to join the two that are already attacking you to tip the scale of battle against you.

Lone guardsmen and giant snakes will be your main targets. There are three different giant snake areas, the first being at the bottom of the ramp overlooking the water, where four appear. The next is the snake pit (with the spikes), where there are two more. Three more hang out at the top of a ramp leading to the deeper part of Blackburrow. Once you hit about a 12-13, you can go hit the three gnoll commander rooms. Also the two elite gnoll ledge across the first wooden bridge. The room across the snake pit is the best as this is a 4-5 respawn spot. Just sit back and let them come to you.

Monsters Of Blackburrow
Scrawny gnolls, razorgills, gnolls, patrolling gnolls, giant snakes, burly gnolls, gnoll guardsmen, gnoll shamen, elite gnoll guards, gnoll commanders, giant plague rat, Tranixx Darkpaw.

Bounty Of Blackburrow

  1. Rusty Spiked Shoulderpads (3 ac, +5 str, -10 resist magic)
  2. Onyx Earrings (+2 agi)
  3. Spiked Collar (3 ac,+3 str)
  4. Studded Leather Collar (3 ac, +1 str, +1 sta)
  5. Giant Snake Fangs (1 hs, 5 dam, 29 delay)
  6. Gnoll Fangs (worthless)
    If you wondering what to do with all those worthless gnoll fangs that no shopkeeper will buy from you, take them to Capt. Tillin in Qeynos, he can be found on the upper balcony in the Grounds Of Fate. He will give you a moonstone for every gnoll fang, which shopkeepers will buy from you which translates into $$.
