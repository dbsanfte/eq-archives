List Of Commands


COMMANDS

  1. /anon or /a (ON or OFF) - Makes you Anonymous in /who and /who all, preventing other players from seeing your class, level, and current location.

  2. /afk - When activated, all private tells sent to the user will auto-respond with the following text, "You told , 'Sorry, I am A.F.K. (Away from keyboard) right now.'".

  3. /assist - if you target a player who is engaged in combat, the /assist command will automatically target for you whoever that player is fighting at the moment -- this can be very useful in heavy combat. To be most effective, it is best to set this as a Social (see IV.F above)

  4. /auction - Allows users to send auction messages throughout the current zone.

  5. /autosplit - automatically splits any loot you get with the rest of your part. Each member must do this to split all loot.

  6. /bug or /b - takes you to a bug-reporting screen

  7. /consent (player name) - gives player permission to loot your corpse

  8. /consider or /con - gives vital statistics of a targeted PC or NPC

  9. /corpse – will summon the character's corpse if it's within a 50' radius of the player.

  10. /decline - Allows the user to decline a duel.

  11. /duel or /d - challenges targeted PC to a duel - to accept, they type /d with you targeted as well. Duel is in effect until one "dies" (is knocked unconscious) or flees the zone

GUILD COMMANDS

  1. /gsay or /g - text is seen by all in your group

  2. /guildsay - Sends a text message to fellow guild members currently on-line.

  3. /guildinvite - Guild Leaders and Officers may use this command to invite new guild members.

  4. /guildremove - Guild Leaders and Officers may use this command to remove guild members.

  5. /guilddelete - Guild Leaders may use this command to disband a their guild.

  6. /guildstatus - Lists all current members of a guild.

  7. /guildleader - Transfers guild leadership status to a new member.

  8. /guildwar - Allows the guild leader to challenge or accept another guild in guild war.

  9. /guildpeace - Allows the guild leader to decline the challenge of a guild war.

PET COMMANDS

  1. /pet (command) - gives orders to a charmed or summoned pet. Sample pet commands:

  2. /pet guard here - tells the pet to guard its current location.

  3. /pet guard me - tells the pet to attack anyone that attacks it's master, or that it's master attacks.

  4. /pet follow me - tells the pet to follow you, but ends when you cross a zone line.

  5. /pet attack {target} - tells the pet to attack the designated target.

  6. /pet back off - tells the pet to stop its attack.

  7. /pet as you were - returns the pet to neutral, belaying all prior orders.

GAME COMMANDS

  1. /ignore (player name) - turns off all text from that player.

  2. /location - Displays the user's current coordinates.

  3. /loot - Allows the user to loot a corpse from a short distance. The corpse must be targeted when performing this command.

  4. /log - Logs all text locally to the user's machine. The log.txt file which is generated can be found in the EverQuest directory.

  5. /ooc - allows you to say something as yourself, not as your character, heard throughout the zone.

  6. /petition {msg} - Used to request assistance for a GM. The more detailed is, the quicker the assistance can be rendered.

  7. /played - gives you the birthdate & hours played w/ your character.

  8. /random (x) – Generates a random number between 1 and x. This can be used to decide things randomly, for instance, who gets a recently acquired treasure. Simply have the players who want the item each guess a number between 1 and x, and then use the command to randomly generate a number. The player who guessed the number closest to the randomly generated number wins the treasure.

  9. /reply {text} - If a "/tell" is received, the user may respond by using this command.

  10. /roleplay - This command activates the "/anonymous" flag along with changing the user's display name to purple.

  11. /shout - text is seen across entire zone.

  12. /shownames (ON or OFF) - turns on or off visible names above PC's and NPC's heads. Red names are PvP, Blue names are –PvP.

  13. /split # # # # - splits a defined amount of money with your group: plat, gold, silver, & copper respectively...i.e. /split 4 3 2 0 splits 4 plat, 3 gold, 2 silver, and 0 copper with the rest of the group. All numbers must be present, use 0 for coins to not be split.

  14. /surname {last name} - Players over level 20 may use this command to assign a last name to their character.

  15. /tell (player name) - text is seen by named player anywhere in the game

  16. /time - gives you the time of day in Norrath.

  17. /who - lists all player characters in your zone

  18. /who corpse - lists all the corpses a player has in the current zone

  19. /who all - lists all player characters in the world

    Note: /who and /who all are also usable with masks to look for certain players or classes online. For example:

  20. /who wiz all - generates a list of all Wizards online in all zones
  21. /who 1 5 all – generates a list of all players in online who are between levels 1 and 5.
  22. /who ae all - generates a list of all characters whose names begin with Ae
  23. /who gm all - generates a list of all the GM's that are online
  24. /who all friend – generates a list of the players in your friends list who are online (see the /friend command)

Please also note that the GM's are grouped into differing types.

GM-Admins are Sony Administrators that can answer your questions and resolve issues.

GM-Coders and GM-Areas are Sony developers and artists that are resolving game issues and are not available for questions.

GUIDES are fellow testers that are there to answer gameplay questions, and assist in problem resolution due to bugs.
