The Sylvan Fellowship


Are you a ranger or druid, tired of a dull life, looking for something to spice it up with? Are you looking for some fellow tree-huggers who share your ideals? Do you enjoy roleplaying in a neutral style, not the great god of goodness or one evil bastard who toments little children in his (or her) spare time? Where am I going with this you ask? Good question. I just thought some of my fellow rangers and druids might like to hear about a guild that is causing some commotion around Norrath right now. We are the Sylvan Fellowship. Some of you might say, Druids and a guild, they dont mix, Druids are supposed to be loners. Welp, not anymore. We have noticed that many rangers and druids who roleplay as loners have alot of fun, until they die. And die. And die. Day in and day out, they die. What the Sylvan Fellowship offers you is a chance. A chance to say NO to the nasty troll who thinks you would make a nice rug in his dank, moldy (not good for the rugs, errr, you) subterranian dwelling (no offense to any "civilized" trolls out there). No, we arent some trigger happy band of cutthroats, out to get revenge on the world for killing us all the time. We are explorers, we are guides, we are friends. This is a guild where no one needs to feel shy about asking another member for money, or for help. Sure we may be small, but being small is what brings us all closer togeather. And one small group of good friends who work togeather, in unison, is more powerful than a legion of nasty-looking, drooling, fetid smelling ogres. Really though, give us an honest chance. Friends help each other, they learn from each other, and they dont cross each other. Some multi class guilds out there may be strong, they may be good, they may be evil, and good for them! We dont want to be the great champions of the people, we just want to be ourselves, doing what we love to do. Who cares about the money? Any true (roleplaying) ranger/druid has little need for money! We dont live in the cities, we rarely even visit them save when we need the items and equipment that only a large group of people with different skills can provide. Think about it.

(OOC: Did that make you think? Hope so. It sure took me awhile to write =]. the point im trying to make is that we arent one of those awe inspiring guilds with their penny pincing, power-hungry, trigger-happy war lords. This is less a guild and more a family, sure there is a leader, yeah, it is me, but I know that a body cant function with just a head, it needs arms and legs, and all that blood and guts and stuff. What im trying to say is im a leader by skill only, I dont give orders, if I ever did I would hope you would laugh at me and punch me for being such a snotty bastard. I just hold everyone togeather and organize. If I ever tell you to do something, like if we are fighting some big nasty bad dude who has been terrorizing the locals and I tell you to go up on that ridge and use your bow, You look at me say, based on your opnion, eaither "Aridain, you are a dumbass" or "Ok" I really dont care, unless it ends up getting us all killed. And if there's a few goblins up on that ridge, and they kill you, well, you can just smack me upside the head, I wont mind.)

Thanks for taking the time to read this!

--Aridain The Sylvan Fellowship
