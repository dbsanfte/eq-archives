The Law

  

  • 1. No ks'ing, training, flaming, ninja looting, or any of the other shit that gives us a bad rep.

  • 2. 3 strikes and you're out. You break the rules and you will be given 2 chances to change, after that, you're out.

  • 3. Disputes within the guild must be handled in tells, not in guild chat or shouts. If no resolution can be found, it will be brought before one of the officers. Disputes outside the guild will be handled the same way. If no answer can be found that way, go to one of their officers. If that doesn't help, come to one of us and we will do what we can to resolve it.

  • 4. No one comes in under the level of 20, I said 15 before but 20 would be better and 30 for classes we don't need. NO ONE under these levels will be allowed in!

  • 5. Guild events shouldn't be a MUST attend but we should put in some kind of prize to make people want to come.

  • 6. Everyone in this guild is a representative of this guild. Actions that make you look bad make us look bad and will not be tolerated. When in disputes, members will behave in a polite and courteous manor!!!
