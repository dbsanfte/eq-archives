Originally, I was gonna do a huge rant on how to be a good beta tester and how there are too many people in AC2 who don't really know what a beta entails and that they weren't helping anyone by flaming Turbine with no thought behind it. But instead I just decided to get into what I like, dislike, and am on the fence about so far in AC2.... Lets start with the bad stuff cause I wanna leave on a good note=P

Stuff I don't Like
       1) Not enough of the critters we knew from AC1. Now I know I said not to worry cause the critters in AC2 are still very unique, and they are. I do like the new critters, but it would have been nice if they also included more of the older variety. I've only gotten up to about 12 so far so I know I haven't seen everything, but the only critters I have seen so far are Drudges, Dillos, Shreths, and Wasps. I know there are also Virindi in the game, not sure if they are around as critters or just summoned, but they are there in some form. I really miss Olthoi, Bandies, and Kna'ths (yeah you heard me, I like Jello!), maybe they are still around and I haven't seen them yet or maybe they will be part of the early plot lines, I still miss em.
       2) Slow Combat. I think this will get fixed properly by launch but as it stands right now combat goes awfully slow. And vulnerabilities don't happen often, at least early in game, so there is very little reason to put xp in the vuln related skills early.
