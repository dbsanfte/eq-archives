Stuff I'm on the fence about
       1) Trade Skills. My main problem with trade skills right now is that it seems at some point, everyone will be able to make the best of everything. And I realize one of the reasons they wanted to do it the way it is now is because they wanted to eliminate the trading mule, but man I miss the days of UO where you could be an armor smith as a profession and have that be a viable way to play the game. As it stands there won't be any player economy in this game cause everyone can make it. Maybe they could institute a trade skill credit system that somehow rewards true craftsman....
       2) Skills. The main reason I'm on the fence about skills is that they just changed it so that higher level skills cost more to train. This is a good thing and it may add diversity in that everyone won't be able to train everything, you will actually have to pick and choose what to take and this adds a bit of strategy to the game. However if there are inherently imbalanced like in AC1, everyone will just go after certain skills and that'll bring everyone back to the same. Hopefully it all gets balanced, but it could go either way.

Stuff I like
       1) Batman effects. I haven't heard too many people talk about this, but this has got to be one of the more fun things that Turbine has added. Basically, you can change the Gore level down to Fun. This way you get batman balloons  over the critters and characters when they are hit. I can't see people using them all the time, but if you've been hunting for awhile and need a change of pace, it is absolutely hilarious to hit something and get a "BANG
       2) Graphics. The graphics are sweet. And true to their word there is stuff everywhere to look at. Interesting trees, bridges, windmills, cliffs, mountains, etc litter the game world. And I especially like the night sky, the moon is as near to a real moon as I think I've seen in a game and the sky at night is just so beautiful.
