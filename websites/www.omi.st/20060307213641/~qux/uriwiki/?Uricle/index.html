7th Shawl 進捗メモ。すぐ忘れるので。 †

  1. Runed Sea Shell + Small brick of velium = Enchanted Velium Powder
  2. Enchanted Velium Powder + Water + High Quality Firing Sheet = Blank Rune
  3. Swordfish Tooth + Molkor Hide + Ulthork Tusk = Burin (Fletching Kit)
  4. Blank Rune + Burin + Etched Rune pattern = Etched Rune (Fletching Kit)
  5. Iceclad Cutlass Fish * 3 = Cutlassfish Oil
  6. Rubbing Alcohol + small piece of Velium = Liquid Velium (Brewing Barrel)
  7. Cutlassfish Oil + Liquid Velium + Royal Kromrif Blood = Sacred Velium Ink (Brewing Barrel)
  8. Sacred Velium Ink + Etched Rune = Velium Etched Rune (Fletching Kit)
  9. Runed Casing Pattern + Small piece of Velium + Platinum Bar * 2 = Casing (Jewelry Kit)
  10. Casing + Velium Etched Rune = Encased Velium etched Rune (Jewelry Kit)
  11. Encased Velium etched Rune + Runed Prayer Shawl Pattern + 6th shawl = Runed Coldain Prayer Shawl (Sewing Kit)
