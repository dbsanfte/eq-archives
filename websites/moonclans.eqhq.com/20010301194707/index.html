The black marble path to the Moon Clans home is easy to find for those who know what they are looking for...impossible for those who do not. Located in a dark, misty woodland, the walkway curves between the thick copses of trees before opening up, abruptly, in a small clearing. Just ahead, lit by lightstones mounted into ornate gothic pillars, sits Castrum Luna (Fortress of the Moon). Cut into the rocks of a large natural outcrop, a majority of the fortress rests securely underground. But here and there, sticking out of the stone, are thick towers, with arched windows dotting their surface.

Welcome to the Moon Clans...

Monday, May 8th, 2000
  Late in the year 1999, the Moon Clans were voluntarily disbanded by the Sovereign Mikall Ravenmoon. The specific reasons are best left to those that were involved, but it was best for the long-term survival of the ideals of the Moon Clans and it's history. It is rumored that ancient enemies may have had a hand in the breakup, but that is unproven and unconfirmed.
  Many former members still adventure in the lands of Norrath. Some joined other guilds, some continue to work independently, and some went on to form their own families (the most notable being EON Forever). Most are still in frequent contact with the remaining members of the Moon Clans (Mikall, and his brother Cambron - the founding members of the Moon Clans), and still adventure with them as much as possible.
  Currently, the Moon Clans are not accepting new members. They still exist, in the shadows and the darkness, traveling with the raven and the wolf. They are awaiting their time when the moon ends it's waning cycle and begins it's path once again to full.
