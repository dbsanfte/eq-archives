PERFECTION WILL NOT BE TOLERATED!!!
 Posted : 07/08/2003 by Reila
  
After a 2 month hiatus from working on Xegony, Queen Bitch of Air, we returned to pay our respects. To be honest, on day 1 of the return, the only respect doled out was us respecting the handy ass-whoopin that we received. The only good thing was that is was was pretty obvious where the problem areas were. Well, we regrouped, came back tonite, and after a bit more strugging, we plugged the leaks and proceeded to dole out 50 minutes of the cleanest raiding I've seen in a long time. I personally went into (what turned out to be) the winning attempt just hoping we could fix some of our start-of-the-fight problems. Not long after it was pretty obvious that not only had we had fixed those issues, we were well on the way to a big "W".

Of course, as the title suggests, perfection is for amateurs. In our worry (clerics running on fumes whole fight) and excitement (Xegony whooping our ass more times than we can count, seeing her even at 20% was almost too much to handle) we blew our proverbial load a bit early, and just zerged the shit outa her without really thinking about the "what if's" after her death. She died, but her minions didn't take too nicely to us violating her corpse, so they violated ours =/. The guild mourned the loss of 2 extremely phat lewts, please join me in a moment of silence... ... ... [Bitch, we're gonna be back to claim them lewts!]
