Hell Yeah - Rathe Dead; PoTime Breached
 Posted : 10/15/2003 by Reila
  
Let me start by shedding a tear over the parting with the Elemental Planes... sorrow strikes my heart like a dagger at the thought of leaving behind beloved friends such as Reparm, Groihin, Offassa, Blazzax. Err, fuck that. Good riddance to farming that shit... we ran out of beastlord alts to dump the rotting leather pieces on anyway. Truth be told we've still got work to do in Elementals, but it feels good to see the light at the end of the tunnel. Anyway...

Tonight, IvM gathered in PoEb. Similar to last night... and last Saturday, and last Friday, and last Thursday, and last Wednesday, and last Tuesday, and ... err. Maybe it would simplify things if I just said we've been working on the Rathe... a LOT. I could go into all the ups and downs of this encounter, but I'll just sum it up as "tough". The guild really stuck in there though; everyone continued to show up, night in and night out, with the knowledge that if we kept plugging away we'd come out on top. Well unless it was a weekend in which case 50 of us tossed pebbles at the wall in PoE. Sad when 50 people is a "low" turnout and you have to call a raid. Tonight though, things just kinda clicked. The random acts of god that had plagued us on previous attempts were nowhere to be seen... everyone did their jobs to perfection, and as a result, the Avatar of Earth finally showed his face.



Promptly after he appeared, we gave him a nice warm welcome. In return for day after day of staring at a bleak brown landscape, AE's falling like chinese water torture, death, death, death... well we kicked the living shit outa him. And cheered as we danced on his lifeless corpse.



Obviously I'm glossing over what everyone else sees as the true driving force behind the win, "Team Pink". You know when you log in and the first thing you see is Seratonin trying to give you armor dye, it's time to run and hide. Label me a hater I guess :o



Last but not least, I want to thank all the guildmates who helped get us here. Those that have long since retired, but were part of the IvM oldschool crew that set the tone, the many newer faces that have come in with fresh enthusiasm and carried the torch, and of course the lifeless sociopaths who have been around since the beginning. Most of all, the officers (past and present) who put up with all the drama and bullshit day in and day out in hopes of days like today.
