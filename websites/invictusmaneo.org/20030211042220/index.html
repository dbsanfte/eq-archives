Another news update?! This must mean...
 Posted : 01/13/2003 by Reila
  
... that we just had another guild first. In this case, two firsts.

On Sunday, amidst a slight bit of moaning, we had another Rydda`Dar fight scheduled. What this had come to mean was that Rydda`Dar was going to get PL'd and ding about 15 AA's. But by some miracle (otherwise known as us learning from the blood we spilled over the past two weeks to Rydda) we were the ones standing at the end of the fight.



Killing Rydda opened up the portal to Mithaniel Marr's lair, and we spent Sunday night testing the waters there. We cleared up to Marr, took out his fearful guardian (AKA giant froglok, *oooo*), but the night ended with him playing a lethal game of grabass with us... we got bent over and ended up back at the graveyard (which really sucks BTW... nice little deathtrap they got set up there).

  


Minor setbacks behind us, we returned to the Halls of Honor tonight to finish the job. End result - Mithaniel Marr owned, us one step closer to the next planar tier. It's been a hell of a last two weeks for us, with a lot of hard work paying off with some big guild firsts - Saryrn, Agnarr, Bertoxxulous, and Mithaniel Marr. Viva la pwn~





 
Bertoxxulous dead!
 Posted : 01/12/2003 by Reila
  
All the hard work getting keyed/flagged up is starting to pay off... tonight we finally bagged the Plaguebringer. Our trip through the ring was real clean this time, finishing with plenty of time to spare. Our first shot at this encounter saw us getting Bertox to ~45%... the next two we didn't quite do as well. This time, however, we put together everything we learned and pretty much owned him (ok well a "few" people died, those screenshots somehow didn't make it into this update - go figure).
