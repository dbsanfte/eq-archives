Risen Phoenix Rules of Conduct

As a member of Risen Phoenix, you must agree to these Rules of Conduct. Most of them are common sense and anyone with honor should have no trouble following these rules. If a member is accused of violating any of these rules, that member will be innocent until proven guilty. The incident will be looked into by the guild officers and they will investigate the issue. Should the accused be found guilty of violating the rules, the guild officers will decide a punishment based on the severity of the crime. Please take note that sometimes the only course of action will be to remove the accused from the guild.

  1. All Of Verant's Standard Rules Apply:
    In addition to our own rules, Risen Phoenix also follows all of the standard rules that Verant has posted. They can be read here and must be followed.

  2. Visiting the Guild Web Site is Required:
    This rule cannot be stressed enough. All members must visit and participate on the guild web site and message boards. All of the latest guild news is posted on the message boards. This includes such things as the Guild Treasury, Raids, requesting help with a camp or general information, discussion of guild news, and more. If you do not visit the guild message boards, you have no right to complain about being left out of the guild activities. Failure to appear on the site will result in your not being permitted to join guild events. If you are having trouble posting or getting to the site, request help from an officer.

  3. Keep Your Character Info Up To Date:
    To help remove some of the stress to our webmaster (who is also the guild leader) in keeping the roster up to date, you are responsible for keeping your character info correct on the web site. The way this is done is by entering your character into Guild Magic. Your main character MUST be in Guild Magic. Your alts are optional, but recommended. Keep your character's level current. It makes planning raids much easier when we know what we have. This is required of all Risen Phoenix members.

  4. Respect The Words And Wishes Of A Risen Phoenix Officer:
    All officers of Risen Phoenix were given that title because of how they conduct themselves inside and outside the guild, their contributions, and many other aspects. Guild officers are to be fair and just and not biased towards guild members. If you have a problem, take the matter to a guild officer. If they give you instructions on how to handle the matter, you are to obey those instructions. There will be no challenging the authority of a Risen Phoenix Officer. If you feel the way the officer handled the matter was unjust or unfair, take the matter to the guild leader at a later point in time. Do not argue with the officer.

  5. Disputes Between Another Player Or Guild Will Be Handled By The Guild Officers:
    If you have a problem with a member of Risen Phoenix or another guild, please contact one of our officers, and we will handle the problem. Disputes are to remain off the public (/ooc, /shout, /auction) channels and taken to guildchat until an officer is involved. Once an officer is involved, keep the discussion in tells and do not take it back into guildchat.

  6. Be Polite To Your Fellow Risen Phoenix Members:
    Treat your fellow members as you wish to be treated. Remember, we are a family guild, and although disputes will happen, it is to remain a friendly atmosphere.

  7. Be Mature In Your Actions And Words:
    Do not resort to childish tactics or words. Be mature in how you handle yourself both to our guild, and to the public. Be a shining example of our guild.

  8. Tolerate All Beliefs, Races, and Practices:
    As a member of Risen Phoenix, are you not to make jokes, comments, or attacks in either public or private, against race, religion, sexual orientation, or economic status of another player. This goes for any player, whether they are a Risen Phoenix member or not. You are not required to agree with that person on their beliefs, but you are not to bash them in any way. This would include a things said that aren't directed at anyone. For instance, calling Greater Feydark (almost always known as FeyMart) GayMart is unacceptable. Using the term "gay" in that context is a derogatory comment against the sexual orientation of a group of individuals and we will not tolerate such comments. Failure to adhere to this policy will result in your dismissal from our family. Note that such comments when used in a roleplay manner about in-game things only is acceptable as long as it doesn't go too far. For instance, dark elves are typically called "inkies" in the EverQuest world.

  9. Guild Tags Must Be Shown At All Times:
    In Risen Phoenix, you should be proud to show that you are a member of our family. The use of anonymous (/anon) is prohibited, as this hides your guild tag from public view as well as hides your on-line status from anyone seeing who is on-line in our guild. If you wish to hide your level, race, class, and location from view, please use roleplay mode (/role) instead. This will turn your name a purple in color but still show your guild tag. Remember, since you are always showing your guild tag, make sure your actions reflect the kindness and honor we strive for.

  10. Do Not Beg For Handouts:
    Begging from other guild members via /guildchat or /tells looking for free donations will not be tolerated. This applies both inside and outside the guild, and includes but is not limited to, begging for items, money, spirit of the wolf, resurrections, teleports, powerleveling, or camping of an item. A guild member, and especially officers, should never feel pressured to drop what they are doing to help you, give you items, free money, or other services just because you both are in the same guild. You can ask for help in getting an item if done in a polite, non-invasive manner.

    Unacceptable: "Hey, can a level 50 head out to Mistmoore and get me a werewolf talon I need for a quest? I really, really want it!"

    Acceptable: "I'm about to go to Mistmoore to camp the werewolf talon for the crafted armor quests. Is there anyone who's free who could lend me a hand?"

    If someone is not available to help you at that time, do not take offense that the guild refuses to help you. Also, do not ask for help in getting an item that you normally could not get with a group of people your level, even if they weren't in Risen Phoenix. For example, a level 20 does not stand a chance at getting the werewolf talon listed above and that person should not be asking. If someone is asking to get an item at a level that is not possible to get that item and they are told so, the discussion is over. People saying that a level 20 could not camp the werewolf talon because they will die are not being rude, they are being honest.

  11. Alternate Characters Cannot Join Other Guilds:
    We know that many of you play more then one character and you usually have a favorite that you play more often then not. This is typically referred to as your Main character and any other character that you have are considered your Alternate (alts) characters. As a member of Risen Phoenix, we ask that you devote your time to our guild and not split your loyalties between guilds. Typically, a person in more then one guild will spend a large amount of their time in one particular guild. Rather then hope that one guild is ours, we require that it be ours. If you have a character in Risen Phoenix, all of your alts must either be members of Risen Phoenix or remain guildless. In addition to not having your alts in other guilds, if you choose to keep your alts guildless, those character are expected to continue to follow the Risen Phoenix Rules of Conduct. We will not risk an unguilded alt doing dishonorable actions and having it traced back to a guild member, and any such action could result in the removal of all your characters from Risen Phoenix, whether your alt was guilded or not. This is a rule that extends beyond the realm of Norrath and affects the person on the other end of the computer.

    Grandfather Clause: All member of Risen Phoenix as of April 18th, when these rules were posted are protected from this rule to a certain extent. It would not be fair of us to force everyone who was already a member to make this choice. Therefore, you will not have to leave RP or pull all your other alts out of other guilds. However, all new members MUST follow this rule, no exceptions. Also, if you are protected from the clause, we don't want to hear about your alts in other guilds please. Also, if you fall under this clause, any new characters you make we would like to come to RP, and not go to other guilds. That is all we can ask of you.

  12. Do Not Kill Steal:
    If another player or party is camping a spawn, it is unacceptable to jump in with the express intent of stealing the kill, loot, and/or exp from that monster. If it looks like they are in trouble, it is best to ask before jumping on in, unless if course it looks like that indeed will not survive without your help. An example of this is if someone is using the Yell (/y) command to yell for help. If you come across a group or player that is monopolizing an area or spawn and will not allow you to join, share, or be on a waiting list, you are to take the matter to a GM via /petition. You are to adhere to the "Play Nice Policy" and try and work out a compromise before taking the matter to a GM. Always remain polite, even if the other party is rude. Do not, under any circumstances, log in a higher alt of yours and go to that area and steal the camp away from them. This could very well result in your immediate dismissal as it is kill stealing and dishonorable.

  13. Do Not Deliberately Train Other Players:
    Trains are a way of life in Everquest, and the smaller and tighter an area is, the more common they are. Some trains are unavoidable and are accidental and they will indeed happen to even the best player. It is sad when innocents get run over by a train and we understand sometimes these things will happen. But sometimes people will take it upon themselves to deliberately cause a train with the express purpose of driving out a group of players or attempt to get them killed. This is a bannable offense by Verant and look upon this in the same manner. If you are caught causing an intentional train and are found guilty, you will be removed from Risen Phoenix.

  14. Do Not Bash Other Guilds:
    There will be times where you will encounter a member or group of members from another guild that cause problems in one form or another. Do not take it upon yourself to badger, harass, annoy, or insult this guild in any way. This includes all public channels (/ooc, /shout, /auction, etc). Immediately contact a Risen Phoenix officer and explain the situation and let them handle it. They will in return take over the matter and work on a resolution. Try and remember, that it is a player that makes a guild bad, not the entire guild. We would hate to have Risen Phoenix looked down upon due to the actions of a single member, and we ask that you do the same when dealing with other guilds.

  15. Do Not Ninja Loot:
    Ninja looting is the looting of a corpse of a monster that you did not participate in killing. This is typically done by sneaking into an area in the middle of a mass combat and looting corpses while the party that is there is busy fighting and usually happens with high level monsters. This is a bannable offense by Verant and look upon this in the same manner. If you are caught ninja looting and found guilty, you will be removed from Risen Phoenix.

  16. Twinking Is Allowed As Long As You Aren't Taking Items Someone Else's Main Needs:
    Twinking is the act of giving money or items to a character that does not have the ability to get those items themselves due to their level. For instance, a level 1 warrior wearing a full suit of Crafted armor is considered a twink. We do allow you to twink your own characters in Risen Phoenix. However, you cannot get a group of members together to camp an item for your twink if a member present needs that item. This will be covered in more detail concerning the gathering of twink gear in the Loot Rules.

  17. Be Respectful And Courteous To Verant Staff Members:
    Though you rarely have to deal with GM's in the game, when you do encounter them, you are to treat them with kindness and respect. If they ask you for information, do not being deceiving or mislead them. If a GM gives you instructions, you are to follow them, even if they are not the answers you were looking for. During a GM event, you are to conduct yourself in a polite manner. Either you roleplay the event and join the fun or you quietly watch. Disrupting a GM event in any way is unacceptable.
