Vision

The Purpose of our Guild is to increase our members' enjoyment of the game, EverQuest. We believe that the key factors in game enjoyment are grouping together, accomplishing great deeds, and improving our characters. Our goals, then, are to facilitate guild groups, share knowledge and work together so that we can accomplish great deeds, and support and contribute to one anothers' character improvement. While in pursuit of our goals, we shall always act in accordance with the following seven virtues in order of importance:


Honor
having and deserving a good reputation and good name, being true to our word

Loyalty
being faithful and reliable

Honesty
being truthful and sincere

Charity
providing help or relief to those less well off

Diligence
pursuing actions with attentive care, following through

Courage
facing danger with self-possession, confidence, and resolution

Humility
being without hubris, being willing to learn


Membership Process

Deadly Virtues is about having fun. We believe a key element of having fun is grouping together with our guildmates and friends. To this end, we plan to decide on a locale to focus upon for some period and encourage our members to stay near to it during this period of focus.

We plan to change locales as suits our desires. These locales shall include all the major dungeons of Kunark and Velious as well as the Planes. Secondary characters are strongly encouraged so as to reduce travel time and maximize play time. We do not want people to log on for 10 minutes, be unable to find a group for their primary, and log off for the night.

We are in the process of balancing the classes so as to easily form groups from within the guild. This means that we will not be inviting more people from classes in which we already have substantial strength, namely druids, magicians, monks, necromancers, paladins, rangers, and shadow knights. We are seeking bards, clerics, enchanters, rogues, shamans, warriors, and wizards. Our target guild size is approximately 65 people.

We are not a power guild for power alone. We strive for excellence merely to avoid frustration and CR's, to maximize our fun in the time we have online. Because of these beliefs, we feel we need to ensure that we have a good fit with each person in Deadly Virtues. We believe that complete participation with the full membership over a substantial period is the best way to determine if a person fits into our guild. Candidacy will be by invitation only. Persons interested in Deadly Virtues are welcome to seek out our members who can then pass along the interest to the guild leadership.

We do not have a policy against accepting members who have previously been in other guilds, as we believe that needs change and guilds change. At the same time, we do not actively recruit from other guilds. We expect that a person who becomes interested in a guild change would leave his or her current guild with all due respect and consideration, and volunteer this information when seeking a place with Deadly Virtues.

The guild leadership will conduct one or more interviews with the candidate and possibly with other players and the candidate's previous guild. If the interviews are satisfactory, the candidate will be invited into the guild for a trial period.

The trial period will last 30 days during which time the candidate will have full access to the guild. The membership of the guild, especially the leadership, is responsible for spending time with the candidate and for supporting their efforts to fulfill the minimum equipment requirements. The candidate is responsible for seeking out members and for fulfilling the minimum equipment requirements.

The guild leadership must unanimously approve any candidate for full membership.


Membership Requirements

These requirements are formulated to support our goals as a guild. In any case where a requirement as stated conflicts with the vision, it will be revised. Please bring any such conflicts to the attention of the guild leadership.

People and Characters

First, we require that members enjoy the game of Everquest in general and their chosen class in particular and plan to play and enjoy the game with their guild mates on a regular basis. We want to get together with others who are having fun and, in doing so, have more fun for all.

We require each member to have a primary character of level 47+. This requirement may be waived in certain circumstances, at the discretion of the guild leadership. There currently are recruiting class restrictions.

We further strongly recommend that each player have in the guild one or more secondary characters which he or she enjoys playing. This is an experiment in work designed to help maximize our fun before, between, and during downtime of outings with our primaries.

Equipment and Preparation

We require the following equipment and knowledge for full members. We do not expect our candidates to fulfill this requirement before joining for the trial period, but we do require them to work throughout the trial period, with the support of the guild, to acquire these items and knowledge. This requirement means checking the required camps to see if they are camped, and inviting guild members to come along and help. It also means working with guild jewelers to acquire resist items. Quick and thorough preparation lets us spend more time together enjoying ourselves, and with increased safety, and less time waiting for folks to get ready.

We recommend the following equipment and preparation for full members. Again, we will support members who seek these items, and we encourage members to organize outings to acquire them.

Journeyman boots preferred, or bankable potion or the like.

Water breathing item (e.g. fishbone earring, rebreather, item proc) preferred, or bankable potion or the like.

Gate potion (Cazic Thule preferred, but any will do), for non-gating classes

Sebilis and Charasis keys and Cobalt Scar Tooth (must be obtained before the end of the 30 day trial period.)

Geography

We require you to have a working knowledge of all outdoor areas of Norrath. Know where the primary dungeons are and how to get to them quickly, whether by previous experience or use of maps. Use of maps is encouraged, especially when learning a new area.

Resistances

Unbuffed Fire, Cold, Poison, and Magic resistances of near or over 100.

We require fire and cold resists of near or over 100 and are looking for effort and improvement in magic and poison during the 30 day trial period. The resist levels for magic and poison are ultimate goals, but are not required for membership. Check the guild resources for a list of resist equipment.

What's Expected

We believe that understanding expectations is the surest way to avoid disappointment. To that end, we set forth these expectations for every member.

First and foremost, believe in and follow the vision. Think of the virtues when faced with any situation, and let them guide you.

Respect for the Guild and its Members is required, not requested.

Read the web boards every day. We plan outings and provide all kinds of information on the boards.

Participate in guild activities. We want to group together and have fun. This means we want active people who respond to calls for raids. Try to set the expectation when you join experience-farming groups that you may be called for a guild outing, with 15 minutes notice.

Help guild members above an experience camp or the like.

Be good citizens. Stop and offer assistance, buffs, or a friendly wave when time allows.

Respect others’ time in guild chat. This means very limited requests for gates, resses, items, and the like.

Contribute your thoughts and ideas to the guild on the boards, and by talking with the leaders. Your input on all matters is welcome, and is vital to the health of the guild.

Show patience, understanding, and support for the officers. Especially when they are trying to resolve a situation. Please keep in mind that they are seeking information for a swift, fair resolution to a problem and are not trying to accuse or hassle anyone, or take anyone’s side.

Always strive to maintain calm, respectful communication in game and on the boards. Give everyone the benefit of the doubt, and seek support to resolve conflicts.

What To Expect

Clear communication of short-term and long-term goals and expectations

Organized guild activities

Support for your goals for advancement and equipment for your characters

Friendly atmosphere

Swift response to problems and concerns

Discipline

Every membership is subject to a review at any time, including officers. Reviews will be as open and constructive as possible, with full participation of the member.

In the event of a reported incident involving one or more members, the officers will conduct an investigation and hold a review. Expectations will be emphasized; the officers may impose probations, with follow up over an agreed-to period.

In all but exceptionally severe cases, members will be allowed two incidents as long as corrective action is taken. Upon confirmation of a third incident the member will be expelled.

Leaving The Guild

We understand that at some point a person’s interests and fun may lie elsewhere, alone or in another guild. In that case, we wish them well.
