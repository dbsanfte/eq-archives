Welcome to the home of Deadly Virtues. Please feel free to look around, browse our site, and join in conversation via our message boards. This site is primarily for the use and support of the Mithaniel Marr guild of Deadly Virtues in the game of Everquest.

If you have any questions, suggestions, or the like, please direct them to the DV Officers (email link to the left) and we will do our best to answer you in a prompt manner.

Vision

The Purpose of our Guild is to increase our members' enjoyment of the game, EverQuest. We believe that the key factors in game enjoyment are grouping together, accomplishing great deeds, and improving our characters. Our goals, then, are to facilitate guild groups, share knowledge and work together so that we can accomplish great deeds, and support and contribute to one anothers' character improvement. While in pursuit of our goals, we shall always act in accordance with the following seven virtues in order of importance:


Honor
having and deserving a good reputation and good name; being true to our word

Loyalty
being faithful and reliable

Honesty
being truthful and sincere

Charity
providing help or relief to those less well off

Diligence
pursuing actions with attentive care, following through

Courage
facing danger with self-possession, confidence, and resolution

Humility
being without hubris; being willing to learn
