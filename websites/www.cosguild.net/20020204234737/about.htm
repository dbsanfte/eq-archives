Joining our Guild:

We are constantly looking for mid to high level players that are willing to group with others to find items needed for quests as well as help to power level our lower level players. This does not mean that the lower levels should expect to be power leveled all the time. Remember that the high level characters were created by someone's hard work and many many hours. Always consider your guild before selling any items. A general rule would be to sell items to guild members at 20%-40% lower than what you might sell outside of the guild. This might sound ridiculous but remember that other guild members will be selling to you as well.

Raid Rules

  • Any Character in guild can participate in Guild raid as long as he or she fits into level range.

  • Have character there 30 minutes before raid starts. (This will allow me time to set groups.)

  • All drops will need before greed.   (If there is a raid set to loot a specific item for a Guild member it will be stated in the raid news at the bottom of page.)
