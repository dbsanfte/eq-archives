I am going to start with the lower level dungeons breifly, and as you will notice, the higher level dungeon will have a much higher degree of explanation due to the fact the low level dungeons are very simple.

 

Black Burrow

 

Black Burrow is the lowest level dungeon in the game – sitting right between Qeynos Hills and Everfrost.  A great place to start at level 5 (but beware of crowding).  There are really only a few items here, and all of them are located in the same area – [Spiked Collar, Rusty Spiked Shoulders, Onyx Earring and Studded Collar]  As you make your way through BB, go through the door that leads you down to the water area – follow that water area to the right until you come to another door on a small bridge (door is opposite to a doorway of water) – go through that door and up until you see a snake pit (3 giant snakes) – make your way around and up a platform and go left – you will see a door, go through that door and at your right, you will see a hallway (Elites and Commanders both spawn in those little rooms). Commanders drop Rusty Spiked Shoulders (rare) and Onyx Earrings (common) while Elites drop Spiked Collars (rare) and Studded Collars (common).  Coming from that same hallway where you found the commanders and elites, take a right and walk around to the other side – you will see a narrow hall passage – leading to another area with Commanders and Elites – room has about 5 total if I remember correctly. That is all you need to know about BB J - rest is xp.

 

 

PAW

 

 

PAW is located in South Karana – due South of the White Bridge. It’s been a long time since I have been to PAW (I skipped it my last time) – but if I remember right, there is really only one important area within PAW – and it’s near the back (the area with a balcony – commander at the top).  That commander carries Snakeskin Jerkin, Snakeskin Mask and Snakeskin Gloves. There are 2 doors – Through the first (straight ahead), should be 2 in the room – magi carries Runed Totem Staff. I will head over to PAW before my first update and make sure this is all correct J - otherwise, great xp in this dungeon once your done with BB.

 

 

Befallen

 

 

To get to Befallen, take Freeport to East Commons – follow the path to West Commons and then follow the desert border until you see a doorway – welcome to Befallen. Befallen is a fun dungeon – first undead  dungeon there is (bring a cleric).  You are going to need some keys to get around this dungeon – and the first will be the locked door (2nd door on left) – you need to go into the 2nd door on the right to kill and find your first key (Don’t jump down the well, unless your at least mid-20’s or have a group – that brings you right to the 3rd floor). Go through the locked door and follow that until you leads you down the cliff – at the bottom, there is a door off to the right (first door you see) – inside is a female human SK – kill her for the next key.  Follow through the next door until you get to a room with a hall (large plank diagnally placed in the middle you need to duck under) – up ahead is the next locked door (to the right) – go through, and use the next door – then the last door – you will be in the room with the Orge SK – he drops the Barbed Arms and the final key.  The door across from the last locked door will take you directly to the 3rd floor (hole right inside the door) – or you can go around the way you came from the cliff are (just go right instead of left) and you will come to a door, leading to stairs and the final locked door.  Both ways bring you to a hallway – in the center of this hall, there is a doorway and a hidden wall (directly opposite of the doorway – walk along the wall to find it).  Once your through the wall, we have 2 droppers in this section – the Thaumutigist and the Troll SK – walk straight past the chair until you come to a large boulder (middle room) – from that room there are 3 ways to go. To your left is the Thaumutigist (she spawns at the very back of that small section – not behind rocks however – she drops Thaumutigist Robe, Gossimer Robe, Chipped Bone Rod and Antiqued Silver Ring). If you go straight, that is where the well would have dropped you. And off to the right, is the area of the Troll SK – he spawns right in the middle of that mess of rats and skels you see down there (Troll SK carries the Bone Bladed Claymore). Go back the way you came (towards that chair) and walk back into the hallway and into the doorway – once in that room, you will see a cracked wall – within that room beyond the wall is the final dropper – a Dark Elf SK (drops the Barbed Legs).  The rest is xp….enjoy the dungeon and be careful – the 3rd floor has been buggy since day 1 and mobs like to gather in masses.

 

Crushbone

 

 

Crushbone is located in Greater Faydark (follow zone line from Buthcher Block to Falwithe).  Once inside this blue orc land, the first dropper you will see is straight up that mountain you see – called the Trainer (drops Brass Shield – need this to make Shiny Brass Shield of course). Moving off to the right, you will see the castle – there are 2 droppers inside – both residing within the tower – Emporor Crush himself (drops Dwarven Tunic) and the Ambassidor D’vinn (drops Dragoon Dirk – which can also be obtained from Dragoon in Neriak forest btw – he is just much higher level). There is the Prophet (doesn’t drop) along with these 2 mobs…so prepare for a fight.  Right outside the castle (in the middle section) is the Warlord spawn (drops Bracers of Battle – this is used along with Dwarven Tunic for Screaming Mace btw – take it to  the little house with the 2 dwarves and high elf). That is pretty much all there is in CB – so enjoy yourself (and prepare for crowds in this zone).

 

 

Unrest

 

Unrest is located in Dagnors Cauldron (follow path from Kelethin to the left – at the T-intersection, make a right and go into Dagnors from there. Once in Dagnors, follow water around to the right until you see a large opening (doorway almost).  Once inside, you will see the Castle – and this is the place to be if you’re a cleric (happy leveling) – Paladins as well no less.  This dungeon along with all its droppers, is where you find small bronze plate – which is dropped from numerous mobs within the castle.  Lets talk about the good stuff now J.  If you go inside the castle from the front door,  make your way around the back to the stairs – go to the second floor and go to the room in the far left corner of the house – inside the room, you will see a balcony with stairs leading to it – this is where the ghoul magi spawns (on the balcony) (he drops Bloodstained Gloves and Savants cap). Onto the 3rd floor – you have the room just above the front door – in fact, there is a door leading to the balcony above the front door – this is where the Reanimated Hand spawns (right on the carpet) (drops Ivory Bracelets and research books).  If you go around to the tower section – there are 3 spawns – the center spawn is also the Undead Knight (drops Bloodstained Tunic and Bloodstained Mantle).  You can bring him down alone if you watch for him to spawn from outside (where the small waterfall is) – and use a spell or bard song to attract him – do not sit in the tower waiting for him to spawn (he wont while your there). The bartender is directly below him in that same tower section (drops Thick Leather Apron). The very last mob to fight inside Unrest is the Dwarf Ghost named Garanel Rucksif – to get to him, start at the front door of the castle, follow hall to right, go into room with bookcase, open bookcase (door), follow down hall – go down then back up stairs (hag room is right there btw) – go directly up those stairs and head straight (through fake wall) – head down that hallway until you see a small pool of lava – walk on lava (its fake lava, there are traps on both sides of lava pool). Kill all mobs on your way in, and you will then be to the end of that hall – off to the right, there is a hallway – kill that hag in the hall and that will lead you into a room with a tenticle on your left and Garanel (or tentical in his spawn) in the right corner (Garanel drops Dwarven Work Boots and  Jagged Bands). That is all of Unrest! Great xp here, so make sure to stay a while if it isn’t already overcrowded.

 

 

Najena

 

Najena is located in Lavastorm – from zone, follow zone area to the right until you see a very large door built into the mountain side – cant miss it.  Once inside, follow all the way around until you see an orge roasting weenies with a couple skels.  In the room just past that, sits the first tenticles (drop tenticle whips). Take a left, and follow that until you see a doorway to your right – walk inside and then go through the fake wall. Before going down the hall to your right, make sure to pull the 2 darkbone skels from the hall straight ahead. Leave your group in that room, while someone heads down to pull the droppers. Go down the hall til you get to the end – once there, you will see 3 doors – use the door on the left – inside you will see Bonecracker and 2 necros. You should be able to pull bonecracker alone, but always be prepared for those little DE’s to follow him (he drops Barbed Leather Whips and Flesh Bands). The next two doors both lead to the same area – the Orge Captains room (he drops Orge War Maul). Make sure to bring up only a few at a time – that last room is the Captain, Lutenient and Guard. Lutenient  also drops Large Bronze btw. Go back out the way you came, and continue to go right – into a very large room – follow that to the end (expect a tent on the way) and fight the magician (she drops some demask leather items) – go down that hall (fight other magicain) and you will come to a hall where you can turn right or go straight and through a doorway on the left. We will go straight for now – go through that doorway, and make a left (go right and you will fall into a trap). Head down the stairs and you will see 3 doorways here. Doorway to your right is Drelzna’s room (she drops Journyman Boots, Ashenwood Spear, Stilleto of Bloodclaw and key to Najena). That door is locked, and to get the key, you need to use the door straight ahead from the stairs – head down, take a right, follow hall, take another right when you get to a room with a picture on wall (looks like a painting) and that will lead you to the jail area (same jail you fall into that trap I warned you about earlier – the key to get out of that jail resides on Bonecracker). Go past the jails and to your left, you will see 2 rooms – room on that left wall is Rathes room (drops Travellers Backpack, Travellers Pouch and Drelzna room key). Head back up to Drelznas room, and prepare to camp here for a while I am sure J - she has 2 other necros with here btw – get prepared for a good battle. After your done, there is only one section left – the master Necro – Najena herself. Head back up the stairs, to that hallway you passed before – head downwards towards the water – there are goblins in this area (magi drops Stone Bracelets). Jump into the water (there is a frog there to fight as well) and beyond that, lies the door to Najena (she drops Flowing Black Robe and Black Tome with Silver Runes) – enjoy! J

Runnyeye

 

Runnyeye is located between Beholder and Misty Thicket – a dungeon with green goblins galore. As you make your way down the corridor, head off down the hall until you come to a door – take a right and  fight the 3 goblins in the first room you come upon. Go through that door and the door just to the right (on same wall) will have the Warlord (drops Blackened Iron Crown and Black Iron Medallion) along with 2 Elites (who drop blackened iron chain btw). Head past that small room and follow the hall (go down either side) to the next doorway – enter that hall and the next door you see will be the Goblin Kings room (drops Black Iron Girdle, Grotesque Mask, Blackened Iron Crown and Black Iron Medallion). He also has a few elites in the room with him, so be careful.  The rest of Runnyeye has not been added to this list yet, but will shortly – we have a team finishing that area now – it’s very large (even a bank down there) – as well as a few Evil Eye droppers, etc….

 

 

Cazic Thule

 

 

Cazic Thule is located along the edge of the zone in Feerrott (from Innothule zone line, follow zone wall to the right until you see a large concrete platform leading to a doorway).  CT is a busy place and popular due to all the “red” stuff everyone wants J  As you enter this lovely place (outdoor too btw) – you come to the courtyard – go straight across and take a left and enter that door – walk through the hall, and take door to right (door to left will lead to Avatar) – once inside, the droppers are in the back of that long room up the stairs (both are Tai – something lizards – I always forget the exact names) (they drop Lizardskin Tribal Mask, Rubicite Greaves, Lizardscale Cloak, and Rubicite Bracer). One spawns right between the 2 gorillas and the other just to the right along the wall. The other dropper in this area is just before you get to the gorillas, through a door off to your right – go through and fight your way up the stairs (be careful, lizards run and bring friends back if you lose em) – once up top, in the uppermost room – to the right in the corner, is the Golem spawn (he drops the Rubicite Helm).  Now back in the courtyard again, go up one of the ladders, and through the doors and follow the hall all the way down to a small water hole – swim down the hole until your in a pool you can get out of – take that hallway to the other side where you will find yet another large pool of water. In that pool, in the very back, underwater is the Steel Golem spawn (if he isnt there, it’s a lizard in his spot) (he drops Golem Metal Wand and Rubicte Gloves).  Starting back at that door I mentioned that lead to the Avatar – head down that hallway – all the way to a door that opens and puts you into water – you will see a large green dome in the center – that is where the Avatar Spawns from. You can swim into the green dome, and end up outside of the ball by swimming out – be careful if the Avatar is home – he is a tough bastard.  Under the dome area, you will see a few lizards, including one that roams in the back area (cant think of his name, but its different then the rest – he drops Lizard Blood Potion and Rubicite Boots) – The Avatar drops Rubicite Breast Plate and Demon Claw.  The Avatar is not only a slow spawn, but a rare spawn – so prepare yourself for a long ass wait if you want the chest piece from him. If you can still get xp from lizards at that point, there is a room off to the right that you can pull from.

 

 

Upper Guk

 

 

Upper Guk is located in Innothule Swamp (take the wall to the left from the South Ro zone).  Before I begin to tell you all the secrets to Upper and Lower Guk – you should know about how the factions work (except for evil classes – this wont affect you).  Frogs already like you, and until you go killing here, you can travel all through UG without any problems for  the most part. The only way to increase those factions again is to kill the undead frogs (Ghoul Frogluks) in Lower Guk. Now also keep this in mind, you may want to stick to the important items only in UG until you can work your way into Lower Guk to work with factions – because there is a live frog side of Lower Guk that will be a pain in the ARSE if you screw up your factions too badly in Upper – Just FYI – Now, as you enter LG, walk down the hallway until your at an intersection (go left for this first trip) – cross the circle bridge – and then go straight and to the right through that door way and down the platform. This puts you in what I like to call the mushroom room. If you look straight ahead (and a little left) there is a staircase – walk up that staircase to the top until it ends and turn around – you are now facing 3 frogs – the one closest to you on your left is the spawn for the Frogluk Idealist (drops the Reed Ring). Starting back from the platform again, instead of looking left to the stairs, look to the right a bit and go up that other platform – follow that around until you see a room to your left with a ladder – go up the ladder and follow to the right – you will see a room stright ahead – go into room and you will see 3 frogs in that room – the frog on the right is the spawn for the Frogluk Realist (drops something – just forgot – Ill update this shortly).  Back to our starting point again, go towards the staircase again, but head down that hallway until it ends at a doorway to the right – there should be a frog as you enter that doorway (the platform room) – and that is the Frogluk Scribe spawn (drops Runed Bone Fork – great in throwing area for those that can throw).  Continue on past the platform boards and go into a room with a ladder going down in the middle of the floor – go down and take a right coming out of the room. You will be on a very large stone bridge at this point – follow that to the other side and you will see a small hallway to your right – follow that down to the prison. If you see a frog as you come into the room, off to your right, that is the Warden spawn (he drops Troll Hide Belt and Elf Hide Gloves) – If you don’t see a frog, you might want to try “see invis” – because the Warden goes invisible as soon as he spawns J  Now go through the other hallway and head down until you can take your first right – cross the platform into the castle looking building, walk around either way (leads to same spot) – go past the frogs behind counter to the right, and you are in a room with 4 halls (2 on each side) – only one is important here – the one to the left, furthest away from you – that is the might Frogluk Lord spawn (drops Silver Plated Bracers and Ghoulbane) – his spawn is the frog standing directly under the lamp. To pull the lord alone, you will need someone with Jboots activiated or Spirt of Wolf. You will also need your group waiting by the Lower Guk Dead side zone – from the Lord to the Dead zone is as follows – go all the way back up to the large bridge, take a right this time, go straight until it ends, take a left, follow that hall until you get to the spider/platform/bridge room, cross the platform towards the heart spiders, and then head to the left to that hallway – go straight until you see another hall directly to the right (will have a boulder and a fire pit) – that is the Lower Guk Dead side zone – once you familiarize yourself with that path, tap the Lord, run the Lord and his friends to the Dead zone, zone through, and the rest of your group will be waiting there – here is the catch – the Lord is always the last to leave the zone area – so as the rest of the mobs leaves, your group can kill the Lord alone (keep in mind he is a Paladin, so he will heal himself full once).  Leaving from the Dead zone hall, take a right and follow that hall – turn left in that next room (only way you can go) until you get to the next intersection that allows you to make a quick right – make that right and follow that into a room with a fire pit and stuff in the middle of the room – there is a hall off to the left, use that and follow it to the water, cross the planks  and use the second plank to the right (not left). Once your across, continue down the hall until your at water again – stop there – there are 3 ways you can go – if you head off to the left, you will hit another area of water that leads you right to the Lower Guk Live zone – if you jump in the water and go right, you will Zone in the water into Lower Guk again (I never use that zone) and if you jump in the water and go straight, you will hit the croc area. Kill the crocs that are there, and you will eventually see Ancient Crocs spawn (drop Gator Skin legs and Gator Skin Sleeves).  I want you to now head back to the beginning of Upper Guk again, and instead of taking that half circle bridge, I want you to take a right and jump into the water, follow the water around until you can get out again – you will then see off to your right – heart spiders – front spider in that room to right is large heart spider, that spawn is for the Giant Heart Spider (drops Bone Shield). Continue past the spiders, go down the stairs and you will have entered a room with 2 waterfalls on each side of the room. Up on the platform, to the very right (while facing it) is the Scryer spawn spot (drops Bracer of Woven Grass and Shimmering Orb). That should give you a nice huge jump on Upper Guk J

 

 

Lower Guk

 

 

Lower Guk is a fantastic dungeon for getting some fast levels and great items you keep even at level 50.  At level 32, you and a small group can sit in the early parts of LG and gain a very quick 10 levels – and start moving into the Tower (where all the droppers are).  As you enter LG from the Dead side zone, go down the path – take a right, head down to the water – go under water, take a right – under the wall and come up on the other side. This will put you into the Bedroom (as most people know it).  Walk down the hall to the left, and then take a left down the hall – you will pass 5 Greater Icebone skels (they drop Large Bronze Plate btw) – and you will then be at the beginning of the tower. You see 2 guards standing on the stairs – just past them, off to the right are 3 wizards on the wall (the middle one is a spawn for the Frogluk Ghoul R(cant think of name off hand) – he drops Black Embroidered Sleeves and Black Embroidered Cape. As you enter the actual tower you will go straight, turn to the left and to your right is a staircase down, and straight ahead, a ladder going up – on top of that ladder are 3 frogs – 1 is the spawn for the Frogluk Ghoul Supplier (drops Light Burlap Sack and Thick Banded Belt), and another is a spawn for the Frogluk Ghoul Assassin (drops Guise of the Deceiver and Serrated Bone Dirk). The other frog with them is usually a FG Wan. Going down the stairs, you can drop off to the right if you want to meet the FG Cavalier (drops Adementite Epolets and Dark Reaver).  Going to the left, you will go across the way (past the spikes room) and go off to the left, then left again to the end of the hall – stop when you see a wall with hallways on both sides – that is the Executioners room – if you slowly go down and stun the Executioner (or his spawn by using  the right hall and hitting the frog closest to you), you can pull him alone usually (he drops Executioners Axe and Executioners Hood). Going back the way you came and then going straight, you will be in a room with a bed – the frog near the stairs (heading down) is the GF Savant spawn (he drops Enamelled Black Mace and Enamelled Black Chest). Heading downstairs puts you on the 3rd floor of Lower Guk – go around wall until your in a hallway with large posts on each side – safe area to rest btw. Just before this hallway, if you look up is where those 3 wizards are on the wall, so if you see people jump there using invisible to undead, now you know why. As you get to the end of this hall, you will see some jail cells and a lava pit and 2 other doorways – take a right this time and walk around to the right, go into the little room (either side, both are the same) with the GF Zols, and walk through the curtain – then walk around to the other side of that hallway, take a right, walk around that wall to  your left, take the very first left and you are now in the hall way just before the Bloodthirsty Room.  The only roamer you have to worry about is a Zol that does come into the hall  – other then that (you may want to clear out the larger room in case one gets away from you ).  Inside the Blood room, you have 3 frogs – any one of them at any time can be Frienzied (they drop Moonstone Rings and Flowing Black Silk Sash) – and let me tell you, if they are wearing that haste sash, you will KNOW it. Bloosthirstys also pop in that room , they don’t drop but they are a very tough melee as well (other spawns range from Wan’s to Dar’s).  Stay in the hallway until you find at least One Flowing Black Silk Sash (remember the first time you might have to kill all 3 – hard to do with certain mobs, but once you do it, stay until you get what you want) make sure to kill at least a couple more frienzies before you leave – Every single time I have seen a Sash drop, the next Frenz had one as well! Otherwise, It can take up to 12 hours before you see one.  On the other side of the hallway, down the stairs, through the fake wall sits another spawn spot FG ? (forget his name now, drops Brigadine Tunic and Dark Mail Gauntlets). Now head back to the lava and cells room, and instead of going right from the hall, go straight from the hallway – follow that around until you cross the lava (don’t go through it or on its sides – traps – walk on edge of it). Stop in that section, because you should always walk along the wall at this point since there are traps in the floors from here on up. If you go to your left (along the wall) your first opening hall to your left is the Reanimated Hand room – You should only see a FG in the room – the Hand is actually above there (you jump up into the water, and swim out above to get him – he is also with a Frog on top) – the Hand drops Serpentine Bracers and Adamantinum Rings.  Go back the way you came now, and go on the opposite side of the hallway, and enter the next room to the right (stay along wall until your in next hall as well) – follow that around to the right and jump over gap – get ready for a good battle here – around the corner is usually a FG Dar and behind him is the FG Archmagi (drops Shining Metallic Robe and Silver Silk Leggings). You will have to fight both of these together, and if your waiting for that robe – expect to wait up to a week of camping – it takes FOREVER to drop, not to mention the Archmagi alone doesn’t like to come out and play too often – making it even worse to wait. As far as the Undead side is concerned, there is only one more spawn to hit, at the end of the main hallway, we have the FG Lord (along with 2 Boks (up to lvl 45)) Keep in mind that the Lord is a level 48 SK, so whoever pulls gets nailed with 720hp right off the bat (but a pet can also take that hit too). The lord drops Short Swords of the Yekesha (you can see it plain as day if he has one) and Skull Shaped Barbute. You are now done with the Undead side, and its time to kill some Live frogs again.
