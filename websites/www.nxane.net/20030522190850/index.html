If you wish to re-register a domain name, please complete our credit card information form by clicking on the link below:

Credit Card Info Form

Please note that we cannot guarantee that an expired domain name can be re-registered. If that is the case, you will not be billed. Either way we will let you know via the email address you provide by clicking on the form above.

If a domain name is re-registered, a one year registration fee of $30.00 will be charged to your credit card. Accordingly one year will be added to the domain name's expiration date. You may add additional years to a domain name's expiration date only after the domain name has been successfully re-registered. To do this, simply click on the link from our menu entitled "Renew Your Domain".
