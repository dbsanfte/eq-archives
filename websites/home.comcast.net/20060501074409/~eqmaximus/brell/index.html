  Announcements and Previous Topics
   

Sunday, April 17, 2005: Nexus Being Updated...

What is the "Nexus"? this site is a 'hub' of links to relevant information for our community. It is currently being revamped, and updated. So please stay tuned. Please note that the links on the left (a lot of them) are broken. These will be fixed, and will reflect resources for CT.

Saturday, April 16, 2005: Board Move...

As many of you already know, the Brell Serilis forum that was run by Relbeek can no longer be maintained by him, due to time constraints and other projects he's working on. So we're returning 'home' to our original stomping grounds on ezBoard, founded back in 1999.

Looking around at the old posts, you can see there is a lot of history left behind 2 years ago. We lost some community regulars when we made the move away to the php forums. But we're hoping returning to our original messageboard, run by the old staff, will be a move in the right direction for the community.

Threads will be moved over as best as they can, to maintain continuity. I believe that the Admin Staff is looking to grow, with people passionate and willing to help create forum community centric activities.

I'm just the 'artist' for this site, but I'm committed to helping this board grow and thrive again. Please use the Admin Contact to send suggestions or ideas to Vumil. Or post them here.

-T.King
