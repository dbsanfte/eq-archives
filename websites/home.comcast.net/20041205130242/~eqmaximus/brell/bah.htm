The Brell Museum
1.) The Original, created by Delsonora.

Upon opening the dust covered Tome, you see runes that translate as "Brell Serilis: Tome of Knowledge", the first page reads "Chapter 1: Brell Serilis is born", in this chapter a story is told of a doorway in the fabric of time opening.
2.) The very first t.king Brell Banner, depicting a community desk "place of study".

A comfortable place to sit down and relax, next to the soft and warm flicker of candlelight, here Brellians would learn about Arcane Magic in Norrath, the second chapter in the Brell Serilis: Tome of Knowledge.
3.) The Discovery of Kunark, a descendant of the fallen reptilian empire emerges.

Firona is taken hostage by the Iksar, magic emanating from her staff is symbolic of Firona's connection to Brell Serilis, in this chapter there are many maps of ancient ruins, and stories about the aftermath of bloody wars fought between the Iksars.
4.) The Discovery of Velious, in the Shadows of Giants. *note* this banner is missing a light-blue + gold detailed frame, text, and misc. objects.
.
Firona still in bondage by her Iksar captors, a new chapter in the History of Norrath begins to unfold, stories of battles between Dwarves and Giants from a frozen wasteland are told, rumors of deep caverns, and vast Dragon metropolis's emerge.
5.) Celebrating the new BS.net website, which was developed by Veranon. (no longer in existence)


The community desk is moved from the cellar, to a more comfortable room in one of Brells castle towers, stars from the midnight sky cast a soft blue glow from the window left of the desk.

6.) The Millworks, first of the "animated banner series", also known as "Druidic Age of the Millworks". The idea behind it's design was to reflect a "living community", it was created during a time of turmoil, to help facilitate a warmer sense of community.

An image emerges from a distant memory, of a time when Druids lived peacefully in the forests, and when it would all change, their homes were sacked by traveling Barbarian hordes from the North, Druids would be faced with the dilemma of protecting themselves from a brutal menace, knowing that they were no longer safe under the cloak of the forest, they began to lay bricks, and the first Druidic Castle was born.
7.) Dark Age of the Nexus, marking the discovery of Luclin.

All that remained in our memories of the "Great Battle of Erudin", were tales told in children books, all had been nearly forgotten...until one day "The Scions" suddenly appeared at spires all across Norrath, the Scions told us of the spires they built in a place called "the Nexus", so they could reach their beloved motherlands in Norrath, they came to us as representatives of a distant moon, curious about this place they referred to as "Luclin", you accepted a portal crystal and began your journey into the stars above, upon arrival you notice that you're standing in the center of several different paths, it is clear that Luclin is divided, a sanctuary of varying beliefs, you proceed cautiously.
8.) Transitional Phase, Legends.

In the most recent chapter of the Brell Serilis: Tome of Knowledge, a slightly faded page reveals a story enclosed within a draconic frame; corners harnessing opalescent gems denoting it's chapter number, within...a tale is told about a community divided by different factions, yet they stood together for a greater cause, that of a magical realm where legends are born, Brell Serilis.
9.) Prophecy of Time.

A new portal is dicovered deep within a cavern near the peaks of Dawnshroud, this place was once home to Al'Kabor a great Wizard of the Runes, the incriptions on the spires indicate that it was used to travel through time.
