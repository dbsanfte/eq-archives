How to Join <Maximus> - Questions & Answers

Want to join the ranks of Maximus? Here is all the information you will need.

- As of September 18th, 2002 we are accepting applications for ALL classes.

- IMPORTANT: Maximus is currently a Kael faction aligned guild, however, factions can and will change to suit our purpose, for this reason I strongly advise all applicants to be willing to switch factions at a moments notice, if we need some things for our members, and mobs are in the way we kill them, simple as that. Be sure to read the guild charter and rules on our website at home.attbi.com/~eqmaximus, note: these need to be updated.

- About levels, the minimum level to join our ranks is 60, with a strong emphasis on the players with AA points/titles "Baron, Venerable etc.", our main requirement with AA points is that you at least have Run 3.

- About resists, we expect you to have gear which brings your resists all to 100+ each.

- About armor, we expect you to already have decent gear (ready to raid).

- About Weapons, we expect you to already have your Epic or better.

When posting an application, please include the following information:
- Name
- Class
- Level
- Hours played per week
- Time zone
- Addt'l info about yourself
- Known Maximus members

Becoming a Recruit:

1) You MUST post your intentions to join in this forum, after which you will wait for your sponsor if you have not done so already. If you do not have a sponsor, you can still apply, but you will not be eligible for Recruit status until you have done so. Members are only allowed to sponsor one recruit at a time, so don't be surprised if it takes awhile for a member to sponsor you.

2) Once you have your sponsor you will then enter the Recruit status, at this point it is your responsibility to group with members as much as possible and attend all of the raids that you can. We have a weekly schedule under our Raid forum, check there often. Another way to keep up with current events is to speak with a member of the guild in-game. The more of an effort you give and the more you participate, the greater your chances are of being guilded.

Becoming a Member:

3) After you have attended at least 3 raids with us, along with grouping with most of our members, you will enter the guild for a 2 week trial period and you will be put up to a vote in our Recruiting section. After the trial period has expired, the votes will be counted and an officer will make a decision to welcome you to the guild or remove you until you achieve the sufficient amount of votes. You cannot receive more than 2 member NO votes and 1 officer NO vote.

Final Words:

* You should know that <Maximus> is a family guild, we're very close and enjoy a comfortable playing environment together.

* Good luck and we hope to see you applying to Maximus.
