The Rules, Policies, and Guidelines of <Maximus> - revision III.
DIES SATURNI A.D. XV KAL. APR. MMDCCLIV A.U.C.

This section is subject to revision without prior notice, to suit the purpose of this guild, it is not a complete listing of our rules, policies and guidelines, nor is it guaranteed to be up-to-date, if you disagree with anything said here, feel free to bring the matter into debate with us, we are open to change, so long as it is viable to all.

  
  • I. Honor the Guild Tag <Maximus>. When you are seen with our guild tag on, you represent everyone in the guild, and the long dedicated work that led to the evolution of this guild. We expect you to compose yourself in a manner that is respectable to all. Negative activities not tolerated are;
    1. Intentional Kill Stealing.
    2. False Slander of Character or Guild.
    3. Intentional Training of others.
    4. Blatant Camp Stealing.
    5. Out-of-Character messages, or shouts containing extreme Vulgarity or Profanity.
    6. Racial Slur's or prejudice (role-playing or not).
    7. Sexual Harassment.
    8. Stalking of other players.
    9. Disclosing private matters of the our guild to the public, especially those involving sensitive issues.
    10. Constant impeding and disruptive behavior to other members or players.
    11. Constant negating, or false advertisement of our guild, it's activities, events, or members in or out of guild chat is considered "mutany", a very disrespectful and ungrateful behavior.
    12. Abuse, or misuse of the guild vault, resources, or it's members, for immediate personal gain, rather than practicality or earnest use of.
    13. /q (quiting) out, going LD intentionally, or intentionally camping out to keep items attained from master-looting for a group, or raid.
    14. Vindictive, or malicious forms of antagonization, with the intent to cause insecurity within our guild.
    15. Constant badgering (harassment) of members, and nonmembers alike for personal gain.
  • II. Respect. Though we don't expect all of you to "get along" with one another 100% of the time, we however ask that you show some common courtesy in the presence of other members, and nonmembers alike. The conditions we have for conflicts are;
    1. Personal disputes are to be conducted via. /tells or e-mail, not to be publicly disclosed in guildchat or public forums.
    2. Disputes brought into public forums by nonmembers, will be "cautiously responded to" then derailed to a private matter, as it should be.
  • III. Honesty. This is a virtue that is mandatory for enlistment into our guild, we expect you know what the definition of this is, but here are some things you should consider;
    1. If you are invited into a "pickup" group, and an item drops that you already have, but another does not, we expect you to be honest and inform the group that you will not be rolling on the item.
  • IV. CaWU (Can and Will Use). This falls within the "honesty catagory", there are many considerations with this policy, which was formed upon the discovery of Kunark. The CaWU Policy is always in effect for either groups you form, or "pickup" groups, but here is our guilds exact look on this policy;
    1. Person (A) and person (B) both want the item, this said item is better-than what both persons have currently in the slot said item would occupy, but Person(A) has far superior equipment in a general sense, the said item defaults to Person(B). There are exceptions to this, but that is purely circumstantial.
    2. Your in a "pickup" group where the rules of loot have not be stated to the entire group, it is then your duty to request what the group-leaders intentions are regarding the loot distribution system. If they do not support the CaWU Policy, then it is at your own discretion of whether or not to continue to group with them. We only request adherence to this policy "if you are the leader" of the formed group, or group that consists mainly of members of our guild.
    3. Abuse of CaWU will not be tolerated, if it is discovered that you "falsely" claimed to have needed said item, you will be disbanded immediately.
    4. If you win an item in CaWU, if you are offered an item of greater benefit "not value" to your class, you are authorized to do so.
    5. A melee-class specific item drops, and you are a caster-class, you are not to roll on the item even if offered to, with the exception being that nobody in the group needs the item, having better already etc. The provision for this is, if there is only 1 melee class, and that person already received an item by default, they are allowed to trade the item up for the newly dropped item, and/or offer it to be rolled to the rest of the group. A single person should not wholly benefit, continuously through the efforts of others, that is selfish, greedy and self-serving.
    6. FFA is now widely accepted for experience, and pickup groups in places like Sebilis, we consider this completely acceptable, CaWU has been abused by many people, and as a result it has lost it's viability, CaWU mainly exists now for raids.
  • V. Member Price Considerations for Items. You are not to charge the maximum "market value" of any given item back to a member of our guild, if you do not wish to sell an item for less than market value, then do not offer it back to the guild. Any "loans" taken by fellow members are expected to be paid back in full on an agreed date and time. If you are disbanded, it does not relinquish you from debt with the guild if one is owed. Here is a list of things you need to consider under this provision;
    1. Do not take money or items from guild members under loan consideration without a formal agreement, that said items/money will be returned at such and such date and time, please log the entire event, including screenshots. If a member "cheats" another member or nonmember out of money or items, our guild officers will work feverishly with Verant Interactive to see justice is served, this has been done in the past "in other guilds", and can/will be done if needed again.
    2. If you are caught "price-hiking", meaning charging more than the market value back to a current member, and you are aware of this, you will be immediately removed from the guild.
    3. Spells that are rare-drops are often up for "trade" for members. You are not required to "give" spells away to members, "though it is a nice and highly commendable gesture to do so". We ask that if you sell any given spell, common Kunark spells for instance, to charge a fellow member no more than 300pp.
  • VI. Vault Keeper of Guild Inventories. Currently Thomus/Greybur are the Vault keepers for the guild, if you wish for items to be "held for the guilds internal use", you may give the item to Thomus, and you can be sure that "it will be safely held until you request it to be withdrawn". We would rather not hold items in all honesty, but the option is there if you so choose.
  • VII. Raids. If you are invited to a raid by another guild, or individual, we ask that you represent yourself by playing your class at maximum efficiency, this means no repeated AFK's during key staging points. The loot policy of the raid holder "should" include <Maximus> members, but is not a requirement, raiding should be for fun, not about loot. Our guild raid loot policy is as follows;
    1. Nonmembers of <Maximus> that participate in groups or raids with our guild, are given 100% consideration on all loot specific to your class along side our members, if you were there, you are eligible, and we ask that you give our members this same consideration during your raids, and within your groups.
    2. We expect you not to attend raids only for the acquisition of items. We do however believe that if your presence in any way, shape or form contributed to the success of any given event, you should be entitled to it, and if you are not, we simply ask that you do not dispute the matter, simply do not attend future raids where you are being "used repeatedly" for the betterment of others at the expense of your time "repeatedly" that is. Always ask what the loot distribution policy is if in question.
  • VIII. Raid Participation. We "ask" that you attend raids whenever possible, and as often as possible with other guilds, to help us establish good relations.
  • IX. Blacklisting. We do not currently hold a blacklist for bad players, however, if you have your own personal blacklist, we will respect that. Do know that we generally do not support blacklisting, with "extreme circumstances" being a consideration.
  • X. Gratification. Our accomplishments, or failures for that matter are our own, we will not participate in self-gratification by gloating on public message boards, nor shall we act or compose ourselves in manner that suggests we are better than anyone else, nor shall we use the name <Maximus> or it's members as a tool for leverage in any matter or under any circumstances. Exceptions to this are:
    1. Posting a "rave" for those that helped you attain Quest items.
    2. Posting "raves" regarding a positive experience with a group, individual, or guild.
  • XI. Summary of Loot. Basically this is a "bonus" on raids and in high-level only encounters, we ask that you not cheat yourself out of equal opportunity in the course of non-guilded events, but that you also not "monger" over items. Our views on loot is that there are people who play to attain items and gloat about it, play to be the highest level and gloat about it, or play just for fun. We are here to have fun, and do so with dignity and honor.
  • XII. Leave of Absence. If you are going to be gone from EverQuest for approximately (30) days, we ask that you give us formal notice in our Message Forum. If you are going to be gone for more than (30) days, we ask that you give formal notice on our Message Forum, and disband yourself from the guild. If an officer is disbanded, or disbands, that position is immediately available to the next person in consideration.
  • XIII. Summary: I-III. If you're honest and dignified, you don't really need to read (above), these things will come into play automatically for you, so consider it a reference.
  • XIIII. Officers of <Maximus>. As an officer of <Maximus>, you must follow specific guidelines, so that we are fair and just to all members of our guild:
    1. Guild Invitations. You must receive the approval of (2) other officers, or (2) of the guild leaders/founders for invitations, and they must meet our established requirements for joining, if (2) or more officers veto a recruitment submission, then the membership will be void, guild leaders/founders reserve the right to veto membership without officers approval.
    2. Disbanding Members. You must receive approval by (2) other officers prior to disbanding a member, if other officers are not present you must post your reason(s) for intent towards disbanding, exceptions to immediate disbanding are stated clearly (above).
    3. Disbanding Officers. Since only the guild leader is capable of this, the proceedings for disbanding an officer, or stripping them of their title is a matter for discussion in The Senate forum only, and not for the public disclosure, the details of this are subject to the matter on a per incident basis.
    4. Disputes. If an officer and member enter a dispute in the context of differences, we will treat it no differently than (2) members, or (2) officers in a dispute, we require that you take the matter to private via /tells or e-mail, if you believe that another member or officer are being extremely disruptive to the guild via /guildchat such as in personal attacks including profanity, and you are not party to the disruption in any way, you may disband the member with (1) other guild officers approval.
    5. Guild members that are removed, or remove themselves from the guild. We do not allow members to rejoin our guild again, ever. There may be mitigating circumstances, or stipulations, which will be reviewed on a "per case" basis.
    6. Voting. As officers, you may initiate votes regarding "any matter you so choose".
  • XV. Members of <Maximus>. As an honored member of <Maximus>, you should know that you have been entrusted by those of us who have worked long and very hard to make <Maximus> a good guild, we ask that you be fair and just to one another, many of the following (below) apply to officers as well, you may:
    1. Lead and Organize Raids/Events if you so choose, the process for this is that you must post within our "Raid Intentions" forum, and our guilds "official" Events Scheduler will follow-up in Raids + Scheduled Events with an official reservation post. Posts for a raid with an "official date" not previously posted in Raid Intentions, will automatically be moved to Raid Intentions.
    2. Request to become an Officer, or serve a specific purpose to the guild.
    3. Expect the aid of fellow guildmates involving corpse recovery, assuming it does not conflict with real-life schedules.
