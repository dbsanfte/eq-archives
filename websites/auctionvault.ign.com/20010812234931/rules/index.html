Rules of the Road

AuctionVault is a service of IGN.com and is provided for the convenience of the gaming community. IGN.com makes no guarantees, either express or implied, about the accuracy and validity of the listings found in the AuctionVault. Use this service at your own risk. IGN.com cannot guarantee that a trade will be made successfully. This service is only intended to match potential buyers with potential sellers. The successful completion of the transaction is left entirely up to the parties involved.

A seller has the right to accept any offer he deems reasonable, even if it isn't the top bid. Conversely, someone bidding to purchase an item should be aware that just because they had the top bid does not obligate the seller to accept that offer.

Be careful when trading with other people. Always take proper "in-game" precautions to make sure that your items are traded safely and that you fairly receive payment in return. Be aware of any security holes with the client or the server program that could allow other people to cheat in some manner during the trade. Consult other people for the best way to carry out a trade fairly.

AuctionVault does not use real money. When bidding on an item, all bids are in the currency of the game where the item exists. For example, all EverQuest auctions are in platinum pieces.

There are several policies that will be actively enforced on AuctionVault:

1. No cheating. If a player falsely bids up his own items, or cheats other users or this system in any manner, he will be banned from AuctionVault.

2. Abusive behavior of any kind is grounds for banning.

3. IGN.com reserves the right to close down your auctions and account at any time, for any reason.

Please also be aware of the IGN.com User Agreement (http://www.ign.com/useragreement.html) you agree to when you register with AuctionVault, as well as our online Privacy Statement (http://www.ign.com/privacy.html).
