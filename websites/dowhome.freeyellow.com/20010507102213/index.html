GuilGUILD CHARTER
The Dogs of War does not hold to the ideology that you have to be of a certain race or class to be a member, therefore we accept our new members from every aspect accessible in the game.  We promote camaraderie and fellowship within our corps; we are constantly challenging each other to use our assets to their fullest potential.


RULES OF MEMBERSHIP


1. NO KILL STEALING

2. YOU WILL NOT beg, borrow, or in any other way attempt to obtain items, money or personal time from another guild member.  If someone offers these to you, then consider yourself lucky, and thank them profusely

3. NO cussing or vulgarity during your EQ game play.  We do not want to see it in /ooc, /shout/ /tell or /guildsay

4. USE your "Chain Of  Command" as shown in the ROSTER section of our website.

5.  YOU WILL ATTEMPT to attend at least one scheduled guild event every two weeks, as long as it is appropriate to your class, race and level.

6.  ANY  Inner Guild conflict should be taken to /tell's or the message board.

7. ANY guild war has to have a unanimous vote from every guild member.
