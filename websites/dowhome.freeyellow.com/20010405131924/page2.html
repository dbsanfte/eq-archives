WELCOME HOME DOGS!!!!!!


Hello! I am Feanara Fyresky, the guild leader of The Dogs of War, and would like to welcome you all to our home page and message board.
Tamena has done a wonderful job of setting these up for us (Thanks again Tam!) and I hope you enjoy your visit.
The Dogs of War is one of the oldest guilds on the Tarew Marr server, founded in the early days of Everquest.
We also have one of the best reputations of any guild on the server.
This wasn’t always the case. In the early days of Everquest, The Dogs of War was a guild full of people who didn’t have any concept of the effect negative actions can have on every member of the guild.
When I first became a member of The Dogs of War, I was confronted with the problem of not being able to find a group because I was a Dog. I left the guild, and did not rejoin until Docter Stormfist took control of the guild. Once Docter had taken control of the guild, he reinvited those of us who wanted to be in an honorable guild and we began the considerable task of repairing the reputation earned by those we evicted from the guild.
Many months later Docter grew tired of the responsibilities associated with being guild leader and asked if I would take the job. I have been the guild leader since June of last year, and I like to think I have done a good job.
We currently have many members ranging in level from level 10 to level 52. It has been a long road to get to where we are now, but with the hard work and dedication of our members we are beginning to reach the planes and other high level encounters for the first time.
I am proud of each and every member of my guild for their willingness to help one another, as well as others who are not members of the guild.
Many have commented on the friendly banter that constantly occurs in guild chat, and I love the family environment that we have built.
I am so proud to be the leader of such a wonderful guild with members who exhibit so much honor and loyalty!
I would like to share with you all a poem I wrote about The Dogs of War:
Cry Havoc!
By: Feanara Fyresky a.k.a. Alicia Goss
When we enter the land you will hear us shout
“Cry Havoc! And let slip, The Dogs of War!”
May our foes be warned that we are about,
For if we find them, they will wander Norrath nevermore.
Our valiant warriors, paladins, and shadowknights
Will rush in with their shining blades drawn.
They will steadfastly stand and fight
Until every last enemy is gone.
Our casters will stand in the second regime,
Evoking incantations with every breath.
The foe will break and begin to scream
With premonitions of their impending death.
Our noble healers will come behind,
Blessing our army, and healing our wounds.
The Dogs of War will be victorious for all time,
As the enemy meets his inescapable doom.
As we attain victory all will hear our yell,
“Cry Havoc! And let slip, The Dogs of War!”
“This day havoc was wrought upon our foes ere they fell”
and The Dogs of War will wander Norrath forevermore.
