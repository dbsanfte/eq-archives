You are at the homepage of The Heretics of the Apocalypse, a guild in the game of EverQuest found on the Torvonnilous server.

 

Guild Background

The guild was founded in the early days of the server by Scurge Darkterror, an Erudite ShadowKnight. He was under the impression that since the residents of Erudin has forced the followers of Cazic-Thule out of their city that they would be KOS. So the guild was formed for the purpose of one day taking revenge on the residents of Erudin. Imagine Scurge's quandry when he first ventures near Erudin and finds out he isn't KOS, only "Glowered at Dubiously." Well, you can't go around killing folks that only glower at you dubiously now can you? Soooo, a new purpose for the guild had to be created... and that purpose was soon discovered as Scurge made his way to Antonica. There he found plenty of people only too willing to attack him just because of his religion or chosen profession. And thus the new purpose of the guild was formed... to take vengeance on any NPC that is KOS to you. The guild grew and prospered, a lot of good friendships were formed. Many NPC's fell to the wrath of the Heretics. After awhile our mission of vengeance started taking a back seat to other ventures. Namely leveling up and adventuring into the higher level areas of the game.

 

So that's where we are now. A group of friends that have been through a lot together, that want to stay together and see the "end-game" of EQ. We will welcome new blood into the guild, as long as you understand our philosophy and agree with it. See our philosophy explained on our mission page, or contact one of our officers in game.

- Betel Geuse (aka Scurge Darkterror) -
