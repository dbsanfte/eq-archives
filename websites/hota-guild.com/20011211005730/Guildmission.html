This page will explain the guild's philosophy and rules regarding how we play the game.
 
A brief history of the guild was given on the "home" page. The guild has existed for over a year now. We have grown large, had problems and lost most of our early members, re-grew, merged with another guild, and come back together as Heretics when the other guild imploded. Most of the current members have been with us since the re-growth days. In that time we have formed close in-game friendships with the guildmates. Our primary goal is to stay close as friends as we approach the "end-game" of EQ.

We all want good armor, epic weapons, and all the other material things the game offers. But our friendships are more important than loot. We don't exist solely to get stuff for our members. By all means we will work towards getting the good stuff, but not to the point that it becomes the driving force of the guild. We will loot, when only guildmates are involved, on a NBG basis. If we are in mixed company then a /random system will be used. Point systems and other such systems give the membership the impression that loot is our main focus.

And yes, we realize that the NBG isn't perfect either, but it does most closely follow the philosophy of the guild. IF YOU AREN'T CHOSEN FOR AN ITEM IT DOESN'T MEAN WE DON'T LIKE YOU AS MUCH AS THE OTHER PERSON WHO GOT THE ITEM. It just means that, all things considered, the guild would be made stronger at this time if the person we chose to loot the item had it. You'll get your turn.

IF LOOT IS SO IMPORTANT TO YOU THAT YOU'D /GUILDREMOVE YOURSELF OVER A LOOT ISSUE THEN WE AREN'T THE RIGHT GUILD FOR YOU.

Potential members should visit our message board and post in the "recruit" thread that you are interested in joining and why. You should also be aware of our policy that allows any current member to veto any potential member's application.

Once you become a member we request you visit magelo.com and create a profile for your character.

Members are strongly encouraged to create an EZ Board account and check our message board on a regular basis.

Alternate characters are welcome to join us provided your main is guilded, exceptions can be made in the case where your alt (that you want to guild) is played regularly and is sufficient level to participate in guild events.

If your primary leaves the guild we request you unguild your alts as well. Currently guilded alts that fall into this category are exempt.

So there you have it. Who we are and what we believe in. If you agree with us then look us up in game and talk to us, we're always open for new members who share our goals.

- Betel Geuse
