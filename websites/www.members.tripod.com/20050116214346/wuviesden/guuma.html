Orignally I intended for this page to be written by Guuma about Guuma. However, after months of nagging it's still blank. Recent events in our real lives have led me to the conclusion that the only proper way to deal with this blank space is to fill it with my thoughts and feelings about that silly troll in Norrath everyone calls Guuma and the Man who controls him.

Guuma the troll is often carefree, ready to charge into battle at the drop of a hat, and willing to stand and die while others make it to safety. He's also arrogant to the umpteenth degree, outspoken, brash, greedy, often unkind, seemingly uncaring, yet honest as the day is long. Guuma does everything on the grand scale; he laughs hard and loud, plays like there's no tomorrow, argues as though his life depends on it and loves with every ounce of his being.

Guuma the Man is much like that character he plays on Everquest. For three years I've been learning everything about this Man, every admirable quality He has and every character flaw. I've had the priviledge to get to know them all personally because He opened His heart and let me peek inside. What a trip we've had, He and I. Together we have soared to the highest plane and sunk to the depths of despair, both in game and real life. With much effort on His part He has learned everything there is to know about me, not just the good things, but all the dark deep secrets. Not once has He ever judged me harshly because He knows that I am all of these things; all the good and bad make me who I am. Just as all of His wonderful traits combine with every character flaw to make Him the Man He has become.

Guuma, You brighten my darkest days, enhance every happy moment with Your presence in my life, give me strength when I'm at my weakest, make me smile when I thought there were none left, and love me like I've never been loved before; thank You for being part of my life. Together we can conquer the world!
