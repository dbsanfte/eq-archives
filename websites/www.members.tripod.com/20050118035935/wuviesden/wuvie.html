Wuvie Wanderingsoul

Born: January 4, 2001
Home: Kelethin
Race: Woodelf
Class: Druid
Guild:

Wuvie Wanderingsoul was born on a blustery winter morning in the tree city of Kelethin. Her parents, rather ordinary folk, noticed from the beginning that there was something special about their small Woodelf daughter. Not long after she was born, Wuvie exhibited an ability to conjure spells. Her parents knew she had been blessed with magical powers by the great Goddess, Tunare.

At this time the world of Norrath was undergoing vast and disturbing changes. Evil had reared it's ugly head once again threatening to spread its vileness far and wide. Knowing their daughter's powers would grow in strength with each passing day, her parents sought out the assistance of a well known druid, Trissleena Travlesome. They placed their daughter in her care with the hopes that someday Wuvie would be strong enough to help rid their homeland of evil.

Under Trissleena's tutelage Wuvie soon learned the ways of the druids. Her mentor taught her how to channel the powers Tunare had given her. Soon Wuvie developed a desire to join others in stamping out evil in Norrath. In her seventh season Wuvie kissed her parents goodbye and left her home in Kelethin to venture forth in the world of Norrath.

Early in her travels Wuvie met and partnered with a Troll warrior by the name of Guuma Mightyfist. In the beginning Wuvie was leary of her newfound partner because it was well known thorughout the land that Trolls were evil in nature. However, Guuma showed his devotion to the small Woodelf by dashing into the fray of battle each time she was threatened with harm. Together, in their own little clumsy way, Wuvie and Guuma set out on their journey.

Not long after they joined forces Wuvie and Guuma came across a band of strangers united together calling themselves Loyal Defenders. The two adventurers proved themselves worthy of joining the Loyal Defenders by fighting evil beings side by side with them. To this day Wuvie, Guuma, and their Loyal Defender friends can be found in Norrath struggling to smite all who oppose goodness. They share the common goals to rid the land of all evil and have a fabulous time in the process.
