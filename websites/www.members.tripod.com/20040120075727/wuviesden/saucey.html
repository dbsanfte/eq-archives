DECEASED
Home: Kaladim
Race: Dwarf
Class: Warrior
Guild: Loyal Defenders

Well now, hello thar! Come on in, come on in, make yerself ta home. Mind ya be wipin yer feet, though, Wuvie's mighty particlar bout her floors and, bein as she just got done scrubbin'em, t'wouldn't be doin us no good to be markin'em up with our dirty boots. Let me be gittin ya a mug'a some fine dwarven ale an we kin sit ourselves down and have a nice chat. Here let me move that cushion a'fore ya be sittin yer arse upon it, Wuvie's might particlar 'bout them cushions too. Wuvie's mighty particlar 'bout a lot'a stuff. Personally, this place is a bit fanicified fer me, but Wuvie's a dear soul so's I don't be wantin' ta upset her none.

I's wonderin when ya'd be making yer way over. Ya be wantin ta know this ol' gal's tale, eh? T'ain't much ta be tellin, really, but since ya've asked, why I guess I should oblige ya. Hmmmm, where ta begin, eh? Why, at the beginnin I s'pose. I's born down yonder in Kaladim. That thar's a wonderful place ta be if ever there was one. Kinda tugs at my heartstrings thinkin 'bout it, guess I ain't a tough old bird after all. Anyways, Pappa an Mamma, why, they're just plain simple folk. By that I mean they's hard workin dwarves that don't go about puttin on airs like some people does. Raised us young'uns up good, though, we never did want fer nuthin; I don't think any other folk could'a loved their lil ones any more than Mamma an Pappa did, an that's the truth.

Mind ya, t'wasn't easy on poor ol' Mamma tryin ta raise up one such as myself. Why, she tried hard ta git me ta do all them thar girly things, but, me, I was a stubborn one an wouldn't have nuthin ta do with that. From the time I's 'bout knee high ta a rat I's out gettin inta one kinda scrape or nother. Why, I member the time I took a tumble off'n the roof a one a the guard shacks cuz I had ta prove ta that scrawny Timpy Sloan that I wasn't afeard a no heights. Boy howdy did Mamma git her knickers in'a uproar that day, she bout wailed me half ta death. By golly, I did have a habit of keepin Mamma on her toes, yes I did.

The only one who seemed ta understand me was me dear sweet Gramps, Frumpy Irontoes, he's me Pappa's Pappa. He's alus tryin ta tell Mamma that bein a fighter was just in me blood an I cain't help the way I am. Mamma alus did say I got my hellion ways from Pappa's side a the family. Anyways, when I was a lil tyke, much ta Mamma's dismay, Gramps gave me his ol rusty sword, a bit a dusty patchwork armour an sent me ta be trained in the ways of war. T'was a sad day when I left me home an family behind an set out ta explore Norrath. Let me open up the door an let some air in; seems ta be gittin a bit smoky in here an me eyes are all waterin up.

Where were we? Oh, yea, I member. I left Kaladim an set out for that faraway city, Freeport. That thar's a evil place if ever thar was one. Why, they let all types wander the streets in that town! Don't be gitten me wrong, I be likin jus bout everbody, but I cain't be sayin I trust them humans an blueberries much. Ack, an them trolls an ogres, why, I never did smell anything quite so bad 'til I met up with some a them. Ah well, I've learned ta git along wit'em just fine. Drink enough ale and ya kin be friends wit just bout anyone, ha ha.

T'was whilst I was in Freeport that I come across this here bunch a do gooders that calls themselves the Loyal Defenders. Genrally I's a loner, but they seemed ta be some good folk an we soon discovered we all had a common goal so's I joined right up wit'em. Wuvie gal and that thar troll, Guuma, that she seems ta be smitten on took me right in an gived me a nice room here so's I could lay me head down between battles.

As ya kin see, I's a purty simple gal. I's honest as the day is long, willin ta work harder than most men I be knowin, ain't afeard a gittin me hands dirty, an, well, I'll be fightin right thar next ta the rest a the Loyal Defenders an thar friends when somethin treatens'em. I ain't got no grandiose idears like Wuvie bout savin the world or nuthin, but I'll be right thar doin me part ta keep the heathens down.

Now, if'n ya don't be mindin, I's got ta go git me armour repaired for the next expedition. Feel free ta look around all ya be wantin, but like I said, mind ya don't be makin a mess. Wuvie gal'd have me head on a platter if'n she comes home ta disorder. Ya might be wantin ta stop in an talk ta my cousin, Temeritie, if'n she's around. You'll find her down yonder in the holler by the big oak tree prolly moonin over some fella ... now that thar's a girly girl if'n there ever was one, ha ha.
