Heroes Lair
Dec 09, 2002 - posted by Ghesta

Well, Sunday December 8th was a memorable day in Heroes history, our first trip into the mighty Kerafyrm's Lair.
Its been a long time coming, can you beleive it was over five months ago that Heroes defeated Sontalak for our first ST key ? Well, the time had come, with 42 strong members geared up and ready to step forth into this new experience we gathered in eastern wastes.


Of course, Sjoc couldn't resist playing with the locals before we zoned in.

Once inside we formed up groups, buffed up and began the daunting task of exploring a zone new to all of us. With excelent pulling from our monks we began to move in. The one thing I was astounded by about this zone is the pure SIZE of the zone. Everything was just HUGE.


someone tell whirl that shes facing the wrong way please.

Sadly, our first big target, the Master of the Guard was not up. We of course did not let this discourage us, we had plenty else in this zone to explore.

We continued the battle deeper and deeper into Kerafyrm's Lair. The enemies got tougher and tougher, and BIGGER and BIGGER the further in we went.


Now THAT is a big axe.


Ohhh a statue. uh... yup.


This guy seemed Fanatical about not letting us past... Too bad for him. =P


When we got to this point, things started to get REALLY hard, we knew we were getting somewhere.
At the end of this hallway was a pit, Badoo, who was leading things decided he was going to jump in first. After taking a measly three hundred points of damage, he tells the rest of the raid to jump in.


XD - SPPPPPPPPPPPPLAT !
So we rez up, rebuff and move to the end of the tunnell at the bottom of the pit to see this...


FINAL ARBITAR !
What ? Doesn't look scary to you ? Pfft, its all a matter of perspective.


Just to give you an idea of perspective, yes, that is Hanibull, our Ogre warlord tanking the FA. (ok, so hes shrunk a bit. heheh) FA seem a bit scarier now tho?

He did fall however to the might of Heroes dropping not one, but TWO prismatic pericers for our guild rogues.

Imagine our suprise to learn that ALL four of the Ancients were up. These are the guys who used to be warders a while back, but due to being really bad at their job got demoted to just ancients once LoS woke Kerafyrm.


ohhh dragon !


ohhh, dead dragon. =P

Heroes took a victory lap around the sleepers bubble and then gathered ontop of it for a group shot before porting out. It was a fantastic raid, loot was plentiful, and it was challenging and satisfying to finally attempt this zone.

I have only one question... When can we go back ?
