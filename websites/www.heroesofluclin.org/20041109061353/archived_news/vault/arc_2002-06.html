Sontalak Owned! Another Heroes First!
June 24, 2002 - posted by Valorenn

Sontalak stood before the Temple of Veeshan in defiance of Heroes! Never before had we taken down this mighty beast.

Heroes gathered our forces and prepared for the coming battle!

Sadly, our first attempt at Sontalak met with defeat.

But Heroes rallied and battle was joined once again! This time we emerged victorious!

Sontalak dropped a Sleeper's tomb key (congratulations Dorrian!) and several spells! Congratulations to those who won new spells!

After taking down Sontalak Heroes paid Derakor the Vindicator a visit. After a flawless job pulling by our always incredible monks (I think Vyil in particular this time ;) ) we did battle with Vindi.

Easily won, we rejoiced over our victory!

Loot from Vindi:

Congratulations to Bakkal on his new BP!

Congratulations to Tililea on the head of Vindi for uber druid gloves!

Once again, huge thanks go to our wonderful raid leaders for an amazing day of raiding!
