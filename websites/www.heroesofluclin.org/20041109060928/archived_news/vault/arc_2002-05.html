Telkorenar Owned! Another Heroes First!
May 26, 2002 - posted by Valorenn

Heroes were on our way to ToV to do some HoT farming for armor when we found out Telk was up so we decided to pay him a visit!

We fought the beast valiantly and were doing very well! Our hopes were high for victory!

But alas, we were stopped just short of victory...
He was actually a bit lower than this when the last of us fell.

We then mustered our courage, regrouped, rebuffed and were ready to take on the dragon again! With vengeance and determination in our hearts we faced the monster once more! Our mettle was once again tested but this time the gods smiled upon us and we were VICTORIOUS!

The battle was fought by 29 Heroes (including one app) and lasted just over ten minutes with ZERO deaths!

Here's what Telk dropped:

Congratulations Lufan on your new Black Rock Maul of Crushing!

Congratulations Pattonius on your new Transparent Eyepatch!

Thank you Erasmus for an awesome job leading us to victory once again!

Who says Patches are a bad thing ?
May 10, 2002 - posted by Ghesta

Well the call went out, "Zlandicar is up!" Nervously Heroes made their way to Dragon Necropolis to attempt this mob we have never done before. Redphoenix did a great job leading the raid, and we managed to get him down to 50% life before we wiped. Thanks to our friends in Five Rings for waiting patiently and helping with the rezzes.

To release our rage at the wipe out we decided to pay a visit to Velketor. Heroes had tried him once before unsuccessfully, and was determined to take him down. Well, Erasmus lead us to a victory over the spell slinging giant, grats to our casters who got loot !

Owned: Talendor, Phinny, Kelorek'Dar, Lodizal
May 9, 2002 - posted by Valorenn

What an amazing night for Heroes! We began by gathering forces to take down Phinny in hopes of aquiring Toxy's final piece for his epic. But the call went out that Talendor had been spotted! So Heroes quickly mobilized to Skyfire and slew the firey beast!

Next we went back for Phinny. He was still up and waiting for us so we promptly zerged his scaily arse into oblivion! Congratulations to Toxy on completing his epic! Woot! (sorry, no pics of Phinny)

After Phinny we had a choice, wait for a possible shot at Gorenaire or go after Kelorek'Dar. We chose to pay the sea monster a visit... much to Kelorek's annoyance. Before too long we were having fish for dinner!

And what else would go so well with fish than turtle soup? Lodizal was found up and about and so we felt obliged to pay a cordial visit to our favorite turtle. Heroes finished up our whirlwind night by killing the great turtle in short order!

Congratulations to all who won loot and to all Heroes and friends of Heroes for a wonderful night of comraderie and fun!
