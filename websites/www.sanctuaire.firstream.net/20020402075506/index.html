News: EVERQUEST Sanctuaire Guild Approved
Transmis par Pouik le Tuesday, January 29 @ 23:27:02 CET
(15 lectures)
Congratulations!
Your Guild, Sanctuaire, has been approved. We look forward to seeing your guild throughout the lands of Norrath.
To actually have your guild created, you will need to have 10 players (that includes yourself) online and then petition that you are ready to have your guild created. Please be aware that the 10 member minimum is now required to keep your active status as a guild, and that if you fall below this number, you could be subject to deletion of your guild.
If you have any problems in scheduling the creation time for your guild, feel free to email and we will work with you to get your guild up and running soon.
Good Luck!
The EverQuest Guild Team
