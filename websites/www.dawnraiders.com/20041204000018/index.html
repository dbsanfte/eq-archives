Dawnraiders Community
posted by Rundark @ 29-9-2004
As new games are being released we see many guilds disband or move to new games as a whole guild. People who do not want to follow and stay in the game they like most are being left behind. To avoid this from happening with dawnraiders we have changed our structure from an EQ Guild to a MMORPG Community. We will be making some more modifications to the website soon enough to reflect this change. If all goes as planned you will see Dawnraiders in EQL, EQII and WoW. Recruitment for these new games will probably open as soon as the new game is released. Keep an eye on the forums if you want to know more.

Regards,
Rundark Level 1 Community Person
