Thursday - June 1, 2000 - Arramar
As I'm sure you all can see, the site has had a major overhaul. I figured with all the changes (for the better) that the guid has been going through, the site should get a change (hopefully for the better). I'm sure you all have noticed that our little guild is growing, I think it has almost doubled in the past few months. I must say that I am very excited about all this! There have been old friends returning and new friends being made.

We have also been doing more as a guild. I have seen more groups composed of guild members than ever before in the past. There have also been dungeon raids and other events that have been guild oriented. I have been having a blast on these events and I only hope that more of these events occur in the future. I would like to see more events where we can get the whole guild together, as I'm sure there are people that still need to meet one another.

As always, keep your eyes open for new members to join our family!

Be sure to let me know what you think of the site. I think that I have gotten all the bugs out, but if you do find one, be sure to email me about it.
