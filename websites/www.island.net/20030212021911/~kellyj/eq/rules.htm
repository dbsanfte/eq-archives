HOME RULES MEMBERS APPLICANTS MESSAGE BOARD


Leadership - The Infinity Watch is led and governed by a small group of Officers, and 2 Co-Guild Leaders. Every decision is decided on by majority vote among the Officers. There are no plans at this time to add officers, but we will if our numbers increase. 

Kill Stealing - Will not be tolerated. 

Twinking - We have nothing against twinking, but, do not join this guild expecting it. It is not uncommon for gifts to be given out, but, it is not a mandatory practice. Any gifts/twinks are dependant on the higher level players discretion. 

Begging - Unacceptable. Wish lists are fine on the message board. There is nothing wrong with saying what you would like. Saying it every day and asking people to get them for you is wrong. Warnings will be issued. 

Powerleveling - If someone wishes to help you do this, we are not against it. We are here to have fun and enjoy each others company. 

Inner guild fights - Keep it out of /gu. If there is a problem or disagreement, try to solve it through /tells. If it cannot be worked out that way, let an officer mediate. We'll try to get into a group or maybe a chat room and talk it out and find a reasonable solution. 

All members must recognize that everything they do reflects either positively or negatively on the guild and should take pains to insure the guild name retains a good reputation. Follow game rules. Exploitation of bugs or cheats will not be tolerated.

If you choose to leave the guild that is your choice. If you wish to return to the guild, you must re-post on the new member board to be reconsidered back into the guild.

Maturity is important to the success of a guild. You will be responsible and accountable for your actions.

Profanity in /gu will not be tolerated.

Loot Guidelines are as follows: 

For organized guild outings and raids (this means Fire Giants, Kedge, etc.), the loot will be distributed as follows: If the group is there specifically to get an item for one or more members, obviously that/those member/s gets it. (example: Lucan Testament or Mermaid Hair) 

If an item drops that multiple members want/need, the item is randomed among the classes present that can best use it. If there are no members who can and will use it, it is randomed generally among the rest of the group. 

If a member has already received an item on that outing, other members who have not will have precedence until all have received an item. 

If an item is randomed for specific classes only, you MAY NOT roll on it with the intention of selling or trading it. Infinity Watch will follow the "Need before Greed" policy. If you receive an item which is an upgrade to an item you currently equip, we'd like to encourage trading the item to another guildmember or offering it for random among the members of the raid.

***For guild groups or in pick-up groups with non IW, the above is a guideline only, and it is up to the individual group to decide how to handle item drops. The only thing we ask is that the loot distribution be worked out in advance, so that no disputes arise once the item drops. 

***For large guild outings, I will make an effort to get a possible loot table together before we head in, so that in cases of no-drop or very 'kewl' items, we can decide quickly what classes can use it best.

 ***Keep in mind that all of this is subject to discussion before the raid.
