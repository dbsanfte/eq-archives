Driven by a desire for knowledge, a love of travel, and a need for adventure, Infinity Watch is governed by the principals of racial equality and the tolerance fostered by mutual respect and loyalty.

As knowledge knows no bounds and limits itself only to the level of courage evidenced in its pursuit, it is understood that the people and means used to collect it, not be bound to a single ideal or practice, but free to act in their best capacity for the greater good of all.

Ultimately, it is the goal of the Infinity Watch to lift the dark veil of ignorance clouding the eyes of all Norrath's populace. To help lead the lost out of darkness, to help promote greater understanding between all classes and races, and raise the level of understanding of all people, of all things.

~ From the quill of Stylist Firstsage.
