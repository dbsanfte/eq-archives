HOME RULES MEMBERS APPLICANTS MESSAGE BOARD


Currently there is a limit of Level 50 for IW Recruits.


1) Candidate expresses the desire to join Infinity Watch to a Sponsor.

2) Sponsor posts to New Application private forum. Post should contain the Sponsors relationship to the Candidate and a brief reason why we as a guild should consider this applicant.

3) Sponsor will be the initial interface between IW and the Candidate. This includes helping Candidate to learn further about IW, assisting in getting the Candidate to attend raids and events, and introducing the Candidate to other guild members.

4) Membership Election begins with members posting factual information relating to this Candidate to the New Application thread already created by the Sponsor.

5) Leadership Election and Review will vote upon the Candidates suitability for IW based on the character as a whole. This vote will take into account member presented facts and first hand officer experience.

6) Assuming a positive vote of confidence by the Leadership Council, the Candidate will be granted a Probationary Guild Membership. This includes receiving the IW tag.

7) Having no incident during the Probationary Guild Membership period, the member will then be granted Permanent Guild Membership status.


Sponsor - an active member or officer of Infinity Watch who has interest in bringing a non-IW player into our guild as a Permanent Member. The Sponsor is responsible for submitting an Application on the Candidates behalf and assisting the Candidate during the Qualification Stage.

Candidate - a non-IW player who has an interest in becoming a Permanent Guild Member of IW. The Candidate is responsible for proactively seeking participation in IW events, raids, and casual encounters.

Qualification Stage - occurs when a sponsor presents a candidate to the IW. This is the time where the Candidate gets to know IW, and IW gets to know the Candidate. Candidates are in no way guaranteed Permanent Guild Membership with IW. It is at this stage where a Candidate must truly demonstrate to the IW community at large that they are a proper fit for membership. During the qualification stage a Candidate will be exempt from normal guild loot rules. Exceptions would and can be made on an individual basis by a raid leader, but should by no means be expected.

Membership Election - show of support or rejection will be conducted on the IW private New Applicant forum. This is not a popularity vote. Posts should contain factual information of an exceptional nature, negative or positive. These posts will be taken into consideration during the Officer Election and Review process.

Leadership Election and Review - at a time chosen by the leadership council, a candidate will be considered for election through a vote. This vote must be unanimous. This will occur at a point when a sufficient number of leadership council members have first hand experience with a given candidate. A positive vote will result in Probationary Guild Membership. A negative vote will result in denial of Candidates application.

Probationary Guild Membership - having been approved through the Leadership Election and Review process, a Candidate will now be considered a member of Infinity Watch. Permanent guild tag will now be given the new member. For a period of 2 weeks this new member will be considered probationary. During this time, the leadership council will reserve its right to remove this member based on its discretion.

Permanent Guild Membership - having completed the probationary period , the member will now be considered a permanent member of the Infinity Watch guild. This is the highest honor, and holds with it all rights of the general membership community.

Review Period - defines the period of time between a Sponsor application submission and a final vote by the Leadership Election and Review. This time period will vary by applicant depending on a number of factors including number of raids attended, exposure to members and officers, and a clear demonstration of the Candidates "fit" with IW.
