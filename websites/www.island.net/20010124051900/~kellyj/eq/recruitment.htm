Please post to the New Recruits Forum with your request to join.

Also, read over the charter and rules section of this board to make sure that your ideals match what is currently upheld in our guild.

The process from there involves 2 IW members posting under your introduction to vouch for you. These members must know you, or have grouped with you recently. If no one in IW has grouped with you recently, you will be asked to join an IW hunt so we can get an idea of your character.

After 2 members have posted under the intro, Beni will post a general comment about being invited for a 2 week probationary period and ask you to contact an officer in game. The 2 week probationary period is informal, but gives us a chance to see if you fit in with our current membership, and also allows you the chance to remove yourself with no hard feelings on either side.

We currently have a level limit for your main character of 10, unless someone in IW knows you well. Twinks of main characters are allowed in at any level.
