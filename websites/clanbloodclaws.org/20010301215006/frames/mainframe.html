Thank you for visiting Clan Bloodclaws.

What is a clan, but a close knit group of individuals, close and unique to what one would call a family.   This is the concept behind Clan Bloodclaws.  We are but a group of tightly knit individuals who's sole goal is to take the most enjoyment and satisfaction out of this game that is possible.

We enjoy having fun, and we hope you do to.  Please feel free to take a look around our website and send us any comments that you may have.
