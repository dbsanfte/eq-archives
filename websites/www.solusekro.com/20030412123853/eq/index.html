Tuesday, February 18, 2003


City of Mist ... REAVERS!!!

Just what to extend a quick congrats to everyone who was involved in what turned out to be quite an unexpected event last night ...

After taking down Nah'Ashiir for the shaman epic piece, and receiving the mage epic piece as a bonus, the Spirits of Sanctuary (and a couple BT members) went back down to the SS to pull more black reavers for ranger/druid epic drops.

We were joined by members of Scarlet Horizon, who offered to assist with the reavers so they could attempt Rak'Ashiir once we finished.

For those who don't know, each time a black reaver dies, it has a chance of spawning a new one from its corpse ... and the final pull of the night did just that.

70 TIMES!

Just to confirm ... the legendary 70-reaver chain rumours are true.

Tally: 70 reavers, zero deaths, and 8 jade reavers (druid/ranger epic) drops.

Kudos to all who were involved!

- -Kizal (Spirits Of Sanctuary)
