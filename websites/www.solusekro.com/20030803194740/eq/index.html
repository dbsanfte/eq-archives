Blood Tide Recruiting Changes

So you want to be a member of the Tide?
We're looking for hard working, dedicated, and committed individuals that want to expand outside the midlevel zones.
Blood Tide is a close knit guild focussing on getting to the PoP expansion zones, and beyond.
Requirements:
1. Minimum level of 58+ no exceptions. Hard zones require high levels. You will be required to be level 60 with 1 month.
2. Minimum of 20 hrs online, in game raiding or xp'ing per week. No exceptions. We provide the best, and expect nothing but the best from our members.
3. We require loyalty. We do not consider people who become known as 'guild hoppers'. You are either 100% Blood Tide or you are 0% Blood Tide.
4. No whiners, loot whores, or self centered players. If you're not doing it for the team, we don't want you. We don't care how good you are. Blood Tide must provide guildmates who enjoy each others company.
5. Message Board Participation. You are required to read and participate on the message boards on a daily basis. This is our primary point of contact outside the game and will keep you informed as to what the guild is doing.
6. We are an adult guild. We consider EQ an adult game. We require adult responsabilities of our members. If you cannot take on those responsabilities do not apply.
7. Game "smarts". Knowledge is power. You need to know everything there is about your class and what you can do, as well as the zones of the game. You must demonstrate your ability to quickly OTM to a raid zone and/or mob.
8. Epics. We will provide assistance on you obtaining your epic, but realise that epics are not a priority for the guild. Epics are done on free time only, unless it is deemed critical for the progress of the guild.
Raid knowledge is not important, we will provide that as we are unique in how we raid.
Intimate mob knowledge is an asset.
If you want to be part of the team then please apply online at: http://www.bloodtide.com

- Zalbar
