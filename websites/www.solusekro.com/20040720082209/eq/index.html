The Madness of WORMS!

Ok Gang, I got me a fix for at least 2000 Windows or better. Obviously if you are seeing this you have not gotten infected.. but just in case you have not protected your system as of yet.. GO TO Microsoft's website and get it protected ASAP!

We were having some major problems getting back online after our move and the silly worm kept getting hold of the connection. Microsoft held my hand and patiently aided me in protecting the system so I could get our Sat system operational. Here is what he had me download, mind you at 21.6 connection it took about an hour. Also keep in mind that we are running XP Pro. Pay attention to the download for your particular OS :)

Here is the steps:
Go Here:
http://www.microsoft.com/downloads/search.aspx?displaylang=en
Then Click:
Sasser (A-F) Worm Removal Tool (KB841720
http://www.microsoft.com/downloads/details.aspx?FamilyID=76c6de7e-1b6b-4fc3-90d4-9fa42d14cc17&displaylang=en
Download. This is the detection tool for the worm.
I suggest saving all these files to your desktop for ease in finding and using.
Now Download This:
Windows-KB841720-ENU-V4.exe
http://search.microsoft.com/search/results.aspx?st=b&na=88&View=en-us&qu=KB841720
Now Download the Security Patch for it:
WindowsXP-KB823980-x86-ENU.exe
http://search.microsoft.com/search/results.aspx?st=b&na=88&View=en-us&qu=KB823980

Now execute the last one downloaded, then the first one and then the second one. Now go MAKE SURE you have all the Critical Updates. Also make sure if you are infected that you reboot and DO NOT CONNECT to the internet and tunr on the Windows Firewall. Then connect to the internet and go to Microsoft and follow their directions for disinfection and protection against this happening again.

I hope this helps a bit and if you have anymore suggestions please please comment!

Special Thank You to Andrew at Microsoft Tech Support.. The FIRST actual HELPFUL person I have encountered there.

Knights of Nobility Is Accepting New Members

As the winds of chaos died, only a few remained to stand strong against the darkness. They were deemed the Knights of Nobility by Mithaniel Marr himself.

Knights of Nobility seeks to shed light upon the inhabitants of Norrath and its heavens by banding together to facilitate hunting parties, protect each other from evil forces, and share our wealth of knowledge with each other.
