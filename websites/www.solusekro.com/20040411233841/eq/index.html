Saturday, April 10, 2004


199 Black Reavers in CoM

Last night Sacred Honor took a trek to CoM. Only reason for going was to finish Stogard's Shaman Epic. I have finally found the trick to getting those Jade Reavers out of the Black Reavers! Don't go with any intention of getting one, go for a different item!

Anyway, Rak was sitting up in the room off the lift. Somehow, while scouting, we ended up with 3 reavers at once. Being only eight of us, it was a hard pull and on that final reaver came the one after another spawn... 2 more guildies showed up midway through the count for the Rak kill and helped dispense with these beasts.

When all was said and done, 199 reavers dead, 16 Jade Reavers dropped, 4 deaths in the group were incurred and Stoggie finished his epic!

Sun Clan move over, we took our record back! hehehehe *teases*

- Adrastea
