Friday, January 10, 2003


BUY/SELL Section

BUY and SELL at your own risk.

There are alot of scammers out there. The Buy and Sell section was originally intended for in game trading only, but has been over run by plat and account selling for quite some time. The Buy and Sell section is just a message board that anyone can post on. There is no way for us to verify what people are selling or whether or not it's a scam. Most likely we'll have to remove this section from our web site. Randomly I will be deleting all account and plat for cash sales in the list. I'd like to encourge people to continue using this for in game trading if this can not be done I will remove this section.

If you have any feelings about how the Buy/Sell section should be handled please post a comment to this news article.

- Ganza

Monday, December 09, 2002


Black Reavers & CoM!


Last evening a few of us took a little journey to CoM. You know, that place that drops the JADE REAVER'S? Well, we ran the circuit of reavers. And then ventured down to start over. Only had one JR drop and need like a trillion more for needy Druids and Rangers in our guilds. (We had 5 out of 13 people rolling for JR's, each representing a different guild. Our one druid received his JR on the first circuit. GRATS Leaves!) We zip back down and in to the first room, clear out the trash and pull the last
reaver.


As a side note: Dryzun and a some guildies of his at Darkblood compadres had come down to get Rak for the completion of his Spear of Fate Shammy Epic... Grats Dry!


Anyway, we get the reaver and another spawns, and then another, and another... Those of you that have experienced this are shaking your heads going "Uh huh... and at what reaver did you all bite it
at or evac at?"


Bah, we thrive on death and destruction!


Sometime after the 12th spawn I petitioned. Ok, call me chicken... but there was no way I was going to get popped for exploiting some bug.


As this was happening DB jumped in and aided in keeping our MA Sunnybeach, healed. Their Mages kept us supplied with rods and our chanter saw more work in that 1 hour than probably in the past few weeks! Yes, 1 hour.. I went through two full rounds of C2!


Somewhere around the 30th spawn a Guide popped in. She left after watching for a few to get a GM. The GM came somewhere towards the end or after the spawning stopped finally. I am unsure, but I think we hit a new Kunark record for Reaver pops...
87 A BLACK REAVERS DEAD!


And NOT ONE DEATH suffered!


The GM never told us it was a bug as Sunny was
talking to him, so we will assume..., ahh love that word ASSUME!, that it was NOT a bug. Just a heads up gang... It CAN be done and you CAN live through that eternal endless black reaver spawn!


My hat goes off to Darkblood members present and all our friends that gathered to get those bad boys. Oh and only 2 JR's dropped off those 87.. BAH HUMBUG!


Ecclesiastique has carpal tunnel now!


Yesterday in CoM
