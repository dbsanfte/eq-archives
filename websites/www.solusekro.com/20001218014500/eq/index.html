Thursday, November 30, 2000


RPGwear new design - Clarity

Battle survival Pill new target against slow mana regeneration

Norrath, Nov 29 (www.rpgwear.com) - Speeding the action of a gene that helps mana regenerate on less oxygen could provide a new strategy for fighting baddies, researchers report. 

Dr. RPGwear and colleagues at the RPG Inc Institute in Freeport, Norrath have designed a pill that speeds up the pathway by which this gene, CLARITY, initiates a series of events that allow mana cells to regenerate in low-oxygen conditions faster. This pill speeds up the growth of mana in various casters throughout the land, according to the test reports done on plague rats in Befallen. 

"These findings raise the exciting possibility of a new approach to faster mana regeneration with a pill, though a number of issues will require careful consideration in developing such a strategy," Dr. Feelgood and colleagues from the Fay Trust Centre for Enchanter Genetics in Felwithe, Faydwer. 

Prescriptions are available under the counter at your local EQ Pharmacy located at www.RPGwear.com.


- Xar
