The Fade of Glory

The Warriors' Caste, a guild known for a profound sense of honour and judgement, has retired from the servers as it was formally known. Tubzinsac leader of the Caste and a friend of mine has disbanded the guild due to the fact that it has been too difficult for him to maintain the iron grip he ruled us with as he has lost a large percent of his closest friends and 3 of his four elite officers to their players quiting EQ. Whether Tubz himself will stay is unknown as he has made several comments on how he has grown tired of fighting the battle to remain honorable and just on a server that seems to edify the corrupt and selfish. We of the Warriors' Caste Wish you all luck in your endevors and may better times come soon to the face of Norrath though I must admit I see not the light at the end of the tunnel.
