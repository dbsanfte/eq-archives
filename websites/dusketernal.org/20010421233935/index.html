Dusk Eternal is a guild founded around group events.  It's primary founders, a group know as House of Bastards (HoB), used to live on the Cazic Thule server and belonged to a guild named Oracle of Virtue.  This small group gathered every week to band together and attack some new location.  As we grew in levels we grew together as a team.   Dusk Eternal is now a home for HoB and provides a home to many other new friends.  The guild actively supports the formation of regular groups and scheduled events.  Many of these new members are gathering together to form their own regular raiding parties and will soon have their own names as well.

Want to know more?  Check out the HoB home page.  It gives a detailed list of the HoB history, including all of our raids, many screen shots, some write ups and some other fun information.

Think you might want to join us?  Check out our charter and our recruiting policy.  If those are agreeable to you, head to the message board and let us know who you are.  We will let you know when the next event is happening that you want to join in.

In the meantime, always remember the first rule of EverQuest as taught to us by the Esteemed guild leader of Oracle of Virtue.....Have fun!
