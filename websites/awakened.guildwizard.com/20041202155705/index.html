Well i figured it was time for an update, yeah so what its early, i was bored and i got some screenshots of guild stuff i want up hehe.

Guilds are starting to alliance and merge. Numbers are down with a reported estimate of 800 people per server. Thats ok however..... We still get to Phase 4 in PoTime regularly, we still take out boss mobs in OOW and as of last night we had people zone into KT for the first time. Not to mention some 10 dragons in various zones fell to a group of awakened - just for fun, for me it was payback.

Wanted to take a minute on guilds, i got a tell the other day.... "stop sending tells to people in my guild recruiting" I thought this was pretty funny. One, cuz i don't recruit and two - if someone asks for information what do you say? none the less we found out whos recruiting and why - its all good. Just speaks to how freaked out guilds are to lose the few that still log in to see this game we call home.

So the direction this month should be heading to Ikk and uqua, the numbers are there, the flags are finishing up - if not completed - and we even have some strong PM's that im looking forward to seeing in action. Yay us.... /rude sony, and lets get moving forward. With some luck we and some good battles we might be doing our next report from ikk.
