Site use. Posted Tuesday, July 31, 2001 by dangalak
I'l have to write up a little instruction booklet for the website, but here's a little heads-up for now.

The member database is pretty straight foward, just make sure you are logged in to the forums and go to the DB and you should see 4 buttons at the bottom. Hit add and fill in your characters info. Don't forget to click the 'this is my main' checkbox for your main character.

You can see the details of a member by clicking thier name.

You can sort by class or level by clicking the respective column headers.

If you want you can upload a pic of your character. Click add pic and choose which character you wish to add a pic for and you'll get a browse file button. Choose the file on your system and hit upload. Notice that there is a size requirement (shown on upload page). Also only upload GIF or JPG pictures. Click upload and it should return back the stats of your image and what it looks like. You may change your pic at anytime using the same procedure.

Links is self explainitory, just don't forget the HTTP:// for the link. You may modify and delete any links that YOU have entered.

Post any bugs you find in the General announcements section of the boards.
