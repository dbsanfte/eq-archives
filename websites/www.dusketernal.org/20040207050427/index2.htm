Welcome! Welcome my friend!
Welcome to the halls of Dusk Eternal!


Grab a chair, we have much to chat about. Oh, and grab yourself a stout beverage as well. Might as well enjoy one of Sylander's fine brews while you're here. Skip the fish wine by the way, even if Fibben tries to push it on you. Trust me on this one.
Oh, and hey. While you up, grab me another scotch will ya? Thanks.

All right then. My name is Ziff Harbinger. I'm sort of Dusk Eternal's lore master of sorts. I have a lot of odd tales to tell you if you want to hear, but I'll try to keep it refined to just what you want to know. In fact, a tale you might want to start off with is our HISTORY. Gives a good sense for how we got here and what we are about.

In any event, let me tell you a little about Dusk Eternal. First off, and I am sure you can see by looking around, we are not an uber guild. If you want to join a guild with 500 of your closest friends and go raid the plane of Time next week, well you wont be very happy hanging your shield in our halls. But let's be clear, we also are not an exclusive guild. When we do stuff we invite all our friends along, no matter what guild they chose to name home. I guess it's best to look at us like a big family. Just because you live in a different house doesn't mean we wont invite you to the harvest festival. Even if ya did marry a troll.

So, your sure to be askin' yourself. What makes Dusk Eternal so special then?
Well, I'll tell ya. As I said, we are a sort of close knit family type guild. When you come into our halls you feel at home, not like you just got dumped into some giant, cold gnomish machine. We also raid. I'll tell ya a bit more about that in a bit, but the basics are, we run 2+ raids a week. Some raids are for those in the 38 - 48 range. Others are for 48 - 65. But at least one a week for each category. You also aughta know that pride ourselves on TACTICS. If we succeed on a given night, as we often do, it wont be to mass numbers or shiny spanky equipment. It is mostly due to teamwork and TACTICS. Actually, we have been raiding together so long that we have adopted a lot of other interesting ideas. One big thing is our regular RAID GROUPS have names. I won't sit here and brag and try to tell you that the guild is home to the best Enchanter, Cleric or Bard in Norrath. That's foolishness and bragging is not really our style. Still, our players are top notch and enjoy adapting, thinking and finding new ways to achieve more with less. You'll find a grandmaster of every trade skill in Dusk Eternal and a group who's teamwork probably can't be beat.

This kind of leads us off into why a lot of people call me crazy. See, a lot of our raids are dungeon crawls. I been leadin' them for over 3 years now with a raid group known as House of Bastards. So yeah, we might be after some bad meanie when we do a raid, but mostly we pick some deserted, nasty, hole and crawl our way through it killing everything in site. We live for this. We are just as likely to take an "off" night and do a short crawl rather than park our butts on some spot, start a camp fire and eat marshmallows waiting for some ugly monster to return.

Don't be scowling at me now! There is nothin' wrong with camping! Dusk Eternal folks do it too. That's how we often meet folks like you. But it's not what we live for. Heck, what fun is it sitting in a puddle listening to your amour rust? It's far more fun to take the fight to them. Run up to the nasties! Spit in their eye and then kill em in their homes. Propping your back against your friends, ankle deep in blood, outnumbered by your enemy, fingers burning with mana and blades gleaming in the torchlight. Now that's adventure!

Hehehe. Sorry. Got a little carried away.

A few other things about Dusk Eternal that I thought you might like to know. Although we raid frequently we fully support the casual adventurer too. A lot of our members have other things to do and so can't spend 7 nights a week ripping the lungs out of monsters. Heck, I'm one of those casual folks. I am rarely found on for more than 2-3 nights a week, and even then only for a few hours. We also don't have attendance requirements. We don't have equipment requirements. We better not! Look at this tattered old robe! It has served me well though!

I guess in the end we basically ask that you be level appropriate, show up on time and have fun. About as basic as this here scotch in my hand if you ask me.

Ok, so that's the basics about us. I can tell you a lot more details if you want. Although a pretty loose guild, we do have same basic RULES AND GUILDINES. We also have a RECRUITING process that is a little different than most. To join us, you'll not only need to be voted in, you will need to complete a QUEST. The QUESTS are meant to be fun and give everyone a chance to get to know you a little better. I usually find something that will take working with quite a few other guild members but not take too long. Heck, what fun is it to collect 10,000,000 cougar skin pelts? The land of Norrath is ripe with problems and I'm sure I can find one appropriate for your level to challenge you.

Oh, one other thing. Over here on this wall is the MESSAGE BOARD. With our folks out on the trail so much, we use this a lot. Lot's of important information goes up here and you will find information posted here daily. It's also a good thing to check out.

Anyway, I wont bore you any more with tales of our guild you may not want to hear. Look around. Ask some questions. I'll gladly answer them. If your interested in joining us, make sure you take a glance through our THINGS TO KNOW and check out our RECRUITING process and CHARTER. Then toss a post on the MESSAGE BOARD asking for access and we'll get the ball rolling. In the meantime, enjoy! Feel free to have another of Sylander's brews. On the counter is one of Tulini's pies as well. Help yourself. Quite tasty. I am going to have another scotch and get back to my research.
