Dusk Eternal is a guild founded around group events.  It is a small guild where we are all friends and thus it has a more "family" orientation than the large "uber" guilds. The guild forms a structure for us to gather together and have fun under. The gatherings we have are slightly different than most groups however. We are not content with camping a spawn, outdoor zone pulls or most other activities other EverQuest players do. We are the mad players who crawl dungeons, scour open wildernesses and take on challenges most fear to consider. We can be found buried at the bottom of Kaesora, Crawling the corridors of the Tower of Frozen shadow or deep in the Bowels of the Warrens (lately). We succeed in these things through teamwork, communication and knowing our characters. Sure, you can find our members scattered across Norrath camping spawns with other groups just like anyone else. We love to make new friends and that is where we find them. But deep in our hearts we are all lusting for the dungeon delving we love so much.

Want to know more?  Wander around the site. You will notice we are also organized a little differently. We have sub groups that are regular raiding parties. These sometimes have names and their own web sites to hold their history, so don't get confused. New groups are always forming and members of the appropriate level are always welcome to come raiding with a sub group.

Think you might want to join us?  Check out our charter and our recruiting policy.  If those are agreeable to you, head to the message board and let us know who you are.  We will let you know when the next event is happening that you want to join in.

In the meantime, always remember the first rule of EverQuest ... Have fun!
