Who is who?!? [3:03 AM EDT] Kerrec
I have finally gotten off my behind and started working on the websites database again. There is a major update as of tonight.

Any MEMBER of Sacred Fyre will now be able to add, edit and delete up to 7 alternates into the databases. I ask that members limit their alternates to characters on Fennin Ro. There is also a new By Roster page called By Roster w/Alts, which will display all alternates and who they belong to.

To access the ability to add/remove and edit your alternates, you will click on the Roster link, top left of the website. From here, you will have the options to go to byGroup, byRoster, byRoster w/Alts, byStatistic and Member Login. To view the roster listing the alternates, simply click on the byRoster w/Alts from this page. If you want to add an alternate, you will have to click on Member Login.

This brings you to another webpage. Here you will sign in to obtain access to the restricted part of the website. High Council and Officers already have passwords to access this section, however, members who access this section for the first time will have to click on the HERE at the bottom where it says, "For first time users OR if you have forgotten your password, click HERE". This brings you to a webpage with a long winded description of how your password will be sent to you. At the bottom, it asks for your Username. This will always be and MUST be the first name of your MAIN character (in other words, the first name of the character listed in the byRoster page).

By inserting your Username and clicking on the button, you will be brought to another page. The email address stored in the database will be shown to you. IF you do NOT have an email stored in the database, you will be redirected to the login screen and a message will be posted at the top. IF the email address posted on the screen is INCORRECT, you cannot go any further until you contact a HIGH COUNCIL member who can insert/edit a new email address.

IF the email address indicated is correct, just click on the SEND button and it will send you your password. Once you have your password, you can login. From the login screen, you will have various options, including Change your password. With this link you can change your password to one of your choosing. If you ever forget it, just restart the whole process over again.

The other options include Add, Edit and Delete an Alternate. I hope these links are fairly easy to use and self-explanatory. You the member will be responsible for maintaining and updating the information regarding your alternates.

Also, keep an eye out for new options and new webpages as I manage to make them. Enjoy!

Kerrec Snowmane
