Guild History(EverQuest) - by Takashi Ikkitousen


In the days before the Time of Adventure in Norrath, there was a band of 6 dear friends who could be found together at all times. They came from all over Norrath, a band of three brothers and their friends. These men decided to dedicate their skills to each other and not the traditional houses and factions of Norrath. The names of these men were as follows: Bephsi McDorne the Human Paladin, Valgamon McDorne the Human Monk, Zeyk Wyndsdale the Half-Elven Ranger, Rogir the Dwarven Warrior, Krovius Neoturi the Erudite Magician, and Seethus McDorne the Half-Elven Rogue. They decided to try their luck as a mercenary band, seeking work from the houses of Norrath.


The small band found gaining acceptance (and adventure) hard in the early days. Mercenaries were not sought out the way they are now. They where looked down upon, scorned for their choice and tolerance in travelling partners. As the young guild grew, it began attracting members that longed for the earning of a quick coin, and the excitement of battle. The focus of the order was to earn a reputation of excellence in battle and honesty. This way those who purchased the services of the Mercenaries, would know their money would had been well spent. The order became known as the Mercenaries of Doom from the Orcish cry during the Mercenaries first job for the Knights of Truth in Freeport: "run for your lives, our doom comes this way!"


The guild has always followed a military style of operation, but a very flexible form of it. Each member’s individual styles of fighting were allowed to flourish but never in a way that would be harmful to the greater whole. Many different races and beliefs can be found following the path of the mercenary, but they understand that it is their job to serve each other before all other concerns they may have. They are always ready to do the impossible when the price is right, the Mercenaries can be found in every corner of Norrath.


On the wall in the guildhall, the mantra of the mercenary can be found:
"Champions of the weak, destroyers of the cold hearted. The Mercenaries of Doom are always available to serve your needs, for a price of course."
