Ranking System


Commandant - Guildleader, All permissions

General - Officer, Emblem, permissions to be determined.

Colonel - Officer, Emblem, permissions to be determined.

Major - Officer, Emblem, permissions up to adding/removing, promoting/demoting members below their rank.

Captain - Officer, Emblem, permissions up to adding members, can speak in Officer chat
2nd Liuetenant - Officer, Emblem, can hear Officer chat

Master Guns - Member, Emblem, Highest member status.

Sargeant - Member, position after 3 months of Service, standard member ranking

Corporal - Member, will remain in this positions until 3 months of service has been completed, can hear/speak in guildchat

Private First Class - Initiate Member, can hear but nor speak in guildchat, initiates will remain PFC status for no less than 2 weeks
