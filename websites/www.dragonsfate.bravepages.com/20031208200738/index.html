Welcome to Dragon's Fate

Since the founding of Dragon's Fate, it has become one of the fastest growing guilds on E'ci server. DF strives for excellence within it's members and prides itself on loyalty among them. The word family is used most often when describing DF because of the closely knit relationships that are formed and supported by teamwork. A solid infrastructure of strong leadership from caring yet disciplined officers with overwhelming support from loyal and dedicated members is the foundation of this family. Dragon's Fate welcomes any and all who may wish to join a flourishing and motivated guild to help make a difference on E'ci.
