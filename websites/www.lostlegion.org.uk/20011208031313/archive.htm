Software

EQ Parser

A great log analysis program, used for picking out data from any logfiles you make - link to the authors website is here.

EverMon

The definitive EverQuest Monitoring program.  Getting booted offline and wondering if it's just you, your server or the whole damned thing?  Look no further than this program.  Link to the author's website is here.

EverQuest Zone Explorer

Nifty little program that lets you view most of the game's zones via the tutorial. Link to the author's website is here.
