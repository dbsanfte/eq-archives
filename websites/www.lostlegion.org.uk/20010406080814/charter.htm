Article I : Name
 The name of this Guild shall be the Lost Legion.

Article II : Purpose
 The purpose of this Guild shall be to cultivate and maintain
friendship, education, and exploring upon which Norrath was founded.

Article III : Membership
 The members of this Guild shall be those females/males, all races,
and all classes who have been duly initiated into the Guild.

Article IV : Organization
 The Guildleader shall be only the initial creator of the Guild.
The Guild shall be a democracy.
The Guildmembers shall nominate appropriate members to fulfill the
duties that will optimize the performance of the Guild.
The Guildmembers, each of whom shall have one vote, shall select the
following duties to the elected:

Events Coordinator –
 His/her duty shall be to organize Guild events.
The Events co-ordinator shall have assistants as necessary.
War Officer –
 His/her duty shall be to preside during the act of war
with another Guild(s) of Norrath.

Treasurer –
 His/her duty shall be to organize all the Guild items and money.

Recruit Coordinator –
 His/her duty shall be to inform the Guild of all new applicants,
and in due course inform the applicant if He/she is granted
admission to the Guild.
The recruit co-ordinator shall have assistants as necessary to cover
timezones for the purpose of inGuilding applicants.

Article V : War
 The War Officer shall be preside in pre-war and war
preparations, including personal combat training.
The Guild must have a 100 percent approval to commit to war
with another Guild(s) of Norrath.
The war strategy may be vetoed with a 50 percent
Guildmember disapproval.

Article VI : Recruitment
 The Recruit Coordinator shall administrate all applications to the Guild.
All applicants must have the recommendation of at least 2 Guildmembers.
Each applicants membership form shall be posted to all
Guildmembers and comments noted.
The Applicant must have at least 20 "yes" votes
to be initiated into the Guild.

Article VII : Treasury
 The Treasurer shall organize all Guild items and monies.
The Treasurer shall set Guild dues.
The Treasurer shall maintain a current stock list of the treasury and
forward the updated list monthly for inclusion on the Guild website.

The Guild shall use the treasury if:
The Guildmember needs to purchase an item for his/her needs.
The Guild needs to provide a token to a person(s) and/or Guild(s)
of Norrath as a form of compensation.
Any Guild member wishes to donate an item to the Guild, for re-distribution
to other members. (e.g. Armour, Weapons, Spell components)

Article VIII : Events
 The Events Coordinator shall inform all members about upcoming events.
All events must have 75 percent attendance.(on-line)

Failure of attendance / lack of response to official Guild messages shall
be brought up during Guild meetings.
Consistent lack of attendance/response, without prior warning, will
instigate a Guild meeting that will question the acceptance of his/her
membership to the Guild.

Article IX : Grouping
 Guild members shall take precedence when choosing group members.

Article X : Guild Meetings
 The Guild shall meet once per RL month to discuss matters arising.
All Guild members shall be informed in advance of the date,
time and location for each meeting.
All Guild Officers shall be in attendance. Other members
attendance is not manditory, but appreciated.
Each Guild meeting shall be 'logged' and made available for
viewing on the Guild website.

Article XI : Items
 It is frowned upon to 'furnish your twink' with items better
used by other Guild members.
All excess items should be passed onto the Treasurer for
re-distribution in the Guild.
A short list of Guild members wishing to use an item will be
made on a case-by-case basis.
The Treasurer/GM will alpha random to decide who
is awarded the item.




Penned this 21st of December, in the year 3185, with full
consent and agreement from the members of Lost Legion.
