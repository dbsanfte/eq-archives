Current News

01/01/2001:  Happy New Year!

I still haven't become any better at getting regular updates done :)  No surprise there then.  

We have had a fair old time of things over the last few months, though I have to say that the guild feels to be taking shape.  The Officers are highly active, and the new guild policies are being finalized as I write this.  We stand, in all likelihood, to lose some members when the new guidelines are published, but I feel that the Guild as a whole will become much stronger than it stands now when the new guidelines are in place and enforced.

Congrats to Popplo, the first from amongst our number to gain his Epic Weapon - hasn't done bad for a funny looking monk really :)  The first of many more to come in the next few months :)

Seeing the /who all lost constantly full during peak times has been nice, allowing us to assault zones we would normally have had to partner with other guilds to stand a chance to enter.  With the membership's level increasing, and our shift to accepting new members of 39th lvl or over, this can only continue.  In the words of Popplo, OWNAGE!

My thanks to the Officers, and to the Guild Members, who have bashed away at the Guild, and at EQ over the past months, and helped to bring Lost Legion a little closer to what it should be.  It's been a hard journey so far, it probably won't get that much easier, but amongst the blood, sweat and tears, I've had some fun - I hope the same can be said for the rest of you 'losties' :)

My best wishes to everyone for 2001 - Githnaur

27/10/2000:  Much has happened since the last update!

After long discussions with Tallace, Lost Legion has merged with Dragonlords of Justice - this has brought about a 30%+ increase in the membership of the guild overall, and has made those 'harder to get places' much more accessible for everyone in guild-only groups.  I would like to personally thank Tallace and his entire membership for taking the plunge and getting lost with the rest of us :)

For long service, and way too many good deeds to mention, Danwen has been promoted to Guild Officer.  Congrats old bean, you deserve it :D

Tallace & Feather are the newly designated event co-ordinators - thanks to both of you for the hard work you have put in thus far, and for the slogging you will be doing in the future.

On a non-EQ related note, please click HERE and press the 'donate free food' button - add the site to your bookmarks and do it once a day to help alleviate hunger in the world. (Don't panic, it wont cost you anything! - just 60 seconds (max) of your time).

Hail Lost Legion! - Githnaur

18/08/2000:  Unfortunately our old GM Xastlin will not be returning to these fair shores in the near future - I wish him luck in amongst his current guild, Dawn Brigade.  On the bright side however,  we have continued to see a large increase in mid/high level membership, which can never be a bad thing.  Seeing the /who all lost list cut short due to too many guild members online makes a very refreshing change :)

I am still awaiting email addresses, and more character updates on the message board to include amongst other things, members actual timezone and a screenshot of yourself for inclusion on the message board.  Please spare the ridiculously low amount of time required to supply these details chaps(esses).

Finally, on a personal note, Grimrot (live) finally spawned for me about a week ago, so no more horrific South Karana camps for yours truly.  /cheer!  - Githnaur

03/08/2000:  Sorry for the lack of updates, a few new character piccies prompted me back into action again :)  Many new members are filling our ranks, although someone seems to have lost their details - hopefully that will be rectified in the very near future.  The application form should be slightly more stable, although it *still* is bugged on the current server so I am forced to link it externally - a brush up of page flinging skill is required from yours truly.  All in all, an interesting few months.  Planes raids are becoming the norm activity on a weekend which gives the guild's elders some much needed fun and I am seeing the new members mixing and grouping with the old guard - it's a great time to be in the legion :)  - Githnaur

Again, I want you all to send in screenshot of your ugly mugs for the members page - go there and have a look - if you have a picture up there, you're forgiven, but a newer piccy wouldn't hurt.  If you don't have a piccy up there, for shame! - send one in.  If you're not even on the member's page, kick my backside by sending an email in with you characters name, race, sex, class and email address (along with, yes you guessed it, a PICTURE!).

Back to camping that substantially annoying Lord Grimrot for yours truly :) - Githnaur
