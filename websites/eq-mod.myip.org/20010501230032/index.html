Welcome to GASD Tech Support.. The purpose of this web is to enhance the support services we provide to the District. We've provided a number of resources here to help you resolve problems, report new ones, and suggest improvements.

    The FAQ section will (eventually) have answers to the common problems that come up in the district. It will be updated with new information as it becomes available, along with links and instructions if necessary.

    The Download section is a listing of helpful software and updates that you might need to upgrade or repair a problem.

    The Discussion section is for posting questions and answers to discuss problems.

    If you really can't find what you need -- Look for it under the Search section.
