Welcome!

The Tome of Knowledge is the web site for the Roleplaying guild Nihilist Dynasty of the Prexus Server of the game Everquest™.

Feel free to browse the site and learn more about our guild. The goals of our guild is to roleplay on Everquest™ and have fun with other like minded inviduals. This site is dedicated to explaining and promoting the guild.

The site is divided into several sections:

  • Home- This welcome page.
  • Announcements- News and Announcements about/for our guild.
  • Charter- The EQ™ gaming charter for the guild.
  • Rules- The rules that members of the guild must follow.
  • Structure & Members- Explains the organization of the guild, the officers and lists the current members.
  • Membership Application- An online form so you can email us your wish to join.
  • Contact Information- How to contact the guild or the site webmaster.
  • Guild Forums- Come and read and post on the Dynasty's message boards.
  • Coming soon- We intend to place informative guides to trade skills or links to such information up soon. Also, we will be adding links to the home pages of those who are our allies and friends. Several Other projects are in the future for the page as well. As a reminder, PLEASE contact either Fergussen or Thyneswelf with any ideas/concerns you might have for the page.
