Nihilist Dynasty Charter
The realm of Norrath has grown into a vast world of trade, prosperity, and unity since it's violent beginning. All races have made attempts at peace, and communication with few exceptions. Norrath has seen no greater era of such mass cooperation.
However with this time of bliss, it also has spawned a new era, an era of politics. As we look across this great world, observing the cooperation and compassion being demonstrated, we grow disgusted with what we see. Races depending on one another, each unwilling or unable to help themselves, worried about what faction they offend. Every trade, alliance, and kill closely reviewed by an effected political faction. Any transaction or death could cause a huge celebration, or a devastating war. You are no longer judged or considered by what you are able to do, or what you know, only by who you know. Heroic deeds are only applauded if done by the right person for the right affiliation.

We can no longer tolerate this injustice. We seek to forge a new beginning, a new era. An era of evolution, not run by politics, but by your beliefs, courage, and spiritual growth. Freedom from the chains of political faction and the opportunity to adventure and discover your true self without reverberations.

We demand nothing, we fear nothing, our honor and pride are second to no one. We live by only a few decrees: If it is not earned, it does not belong to you, If there is a tragic wrong doing, we will be the first to help, If there is a call for bloodshed, it will be our voices you hear, If it is your plead for mercy, it will be our laughter that is given.
Without destruction, there is no rebuilding, without villains, there are no heroes, without chaos, there is no bliss, and most importantly without us, there is no evolution.
