Nihilist Dynasty Rules
1) Guild members must uphold and maintain the guild reputation at all times

2) No kill stealing

3) No use of offensive or sexually explicit conversation in guild chat.

4) No verbal attacks towards the guild or its members in guild chat.

5) No excessive or intrusive demands for ports, items, or powerleveling.

6) Refrain from giving high end items to those who would other wise not be able to obtain them,
instead issue a quest to them for the item or offer it at a reduced price.

7) Any guild business requiring a vote shall be voted on at a guild event.

8) Awards of higher titles will be given by the House leader, in which part the members, valor,
reputation, and over all guild particapation will be reviewed.

9) Ideas or suggestions for guild improvement should be referred to your house leader.

10) All members are to maintain a valid email address.

11) Raids will be posted on the message board in advance. If you wish to attend, you must sign up
there. Those who did not sign up for the raid will only end up in groups on an on need basis.

12) If you wish to set up a raid for an item or quest, you must be within 6 levels of the creature that must be killed if you are underl level 40. After level 40, you may attempt to set up item/quest raids as seen fit by your house leader and the Raid coordinator.

Recruiting

All new recruits must be sponsored by a current member, in which they would be responsible
for the new recruits, behavior within the guild during their probationary period. This also includes
informing the new recruit about our website, rules, and getting them introduced into the guild.
When you have someone you would like to sponsor, direct them to our web sight and have them visit our message board and fill out an application there.
