The Great Gnoll Hunt
(or Hunted by Great Gnolls)
07/15/2000

Journal entry - July 15

I have spent many a day hunting those vile gnolls. Through the caverns of Black Burrow, I have hunted alone and within groups, taking down as many as we could handle, taking as few casualties as possible. Our constant raids must have reached the powers that be in the land of Gnoll, for out of no where, my group was ambushed by Gnolls of greater strength and ability, than any of us had ever encountered.

Lead by a caster who was healing the warriors as fast as we could strike, the Gnoll warriors, very quickly ravaged our group, with seemingly little effort.

With our group bleeding to death and no fight left within, these hateful Gnolls headed for the more open areas of Black Burrow and once again went on a rampage. Taking down any would be hero who tried to stop them.

After much damage and much carnage, the superior Gnoll attack ceased, as the caster teleported them away, leaving a faint echo of a lesson being taught to all those who oppose them.

My desire to hunt down the race of Gnoll has increased ten fold due to such an attack, but alas I must prepare... my training must be advanced, my skills and my strength increased.. and a master plan of attack devised with just the right hunting group put together....

Your day will come you vile gnolls.. mark my words!

Ryand,
15th Season Ranger
