Guidelines - the "Do's" and "Don't" of Ruis Saille

"Do's" | "Don't" | Responsibilities

Do's

1. Adventure with Ruis Saille brothers and sisters whenever you can. We are a family and we should stick with each other whenever we can. That does not mean you are in Steamfont and the only member that is of your level is in Kerra, that they should be obligated to rush out to where you are.

2. Be courteous to your brothers and sisters and treat them as equals. This goes the same for our Extended family, the Atani. Treat all with respect and show that courtesy to others that are not of either Guild. To acquire true unity we must be kind to those that do not share our beliefs.

3. Be generous when you have plenty and be willing to accept when you are not. In other words, when you are bank is full and you have an item that you know a Guild member can use, I encourage you to hand down items/cash to the family. This does not mean just scrapping by on cash and giving all your "best" items away that you can't use, but use common sense. Your generosity will be remembered when an extra item of your race/class combo comes up.

4. Play an active role in Ruis Saille. Do something out of the ordinaries. Surprise the guild with something. Get with an officer and plan an event. Officers and myself cannot always plan things for everyday of the week. To be honest, we have trouble thinking things up on the weekends as it is. It is not to say that we will not plan things but you are encouraged to "make" your own fun. Plan a language fair, hold a raffle. Just adventure with family and Role-play. We are a guild of seeking knowledge, live that philosophy and the fun will come. I want Norrath to see and hear Ruis Saille, because the magic of EQ comes from YOU!

Don'ts

1. Kill Steal. Even the Gods themselves admonish this. If you are found Ks'ing you will be immediately disbanded. I know there are times when you do not mean to or are trying to help someone, but if it is found you have intentionally taken a kill, you are out. No questions.

2. Unfairly taking an Item from a mob that is not yours. We are not about having the "best stuff" at the expense of others. If you are found taking an time out-of-turn in a group or even not in a group, you will be immediately disbanded. We are an honorable family and this is unacceptable.

3. Overuse the Guild channel. Please keep only guild business on the channel and if you must speak to someone take it to tells. Please do not swear or slander on the channel. We are mature and swearing is not needed. Swearing/slandering could lead to discipline/disbandment if excessive.

4. Ask a Guildmember to powerlevel you. All of us have agenda's and some of us do not have time to spend leveling a player even though they are a brother or sister. You are encouraged to find members to adventure with and level with others. We have allies in Atani that are of a diverse levels that would love to adventure with you if you cannot find a Ruis Saille member. Now if someone offers you a powerleveling session, that is a totally different story.

5. Beg for items from others. As with above, we all have worked hard for all our items. If you wish an item you are HIGHLY encouraged to create a group to go get said item or speak with an officer or myself on arranging a time to get enough people to "get" said item. And as with above, you may just be offered items as we are a very generous guild, but please do not expect them.
