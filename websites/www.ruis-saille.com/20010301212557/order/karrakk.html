Karrakk Bouldershoulder
___________________________________________


Karrakk Bouldershoulder is a pure and true example of the heart of the Order of Ruis.  Time and time again Karrakk has dedicated himself to brightening the hearts and minds of Norrath.  Proving himself devoted to Ruis Saille's cause by serving myself and my Champion in the battlefield and in our quest for knowledge and wisdom.

Karrakk had befriended my family and our hearts from our first adventures together.  He has taken time out of his schedule and away from his Guild to come with our family on many adventures and gladly giving Ressurects, heals and binds.  His gruf personallity covers a heart of gold.  And Ruis Saille would be lost if we never had the chance to know Karrakk Bouldershoulder.  But he has come into our lives and he has blessed us.  And the Grudge Bearers are blessed to have such a fine example of a Dwarf in there Guild.  Thank you Grudge Bearers and most of all, thank you Karrakk.  You truely have the heart of Ruis.

All Hail Karrakk! 
Order of Ruis!
