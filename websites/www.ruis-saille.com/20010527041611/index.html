Ruis Saille (def: Druidic for Elder Willow Tree or Wise Willow Tree)

As the wind blows so does our guild,
Through the branches of a Wise Willow
Tree.
'Till a Willow seed is broke loose,
And it's Wisdom is set free.

Our wind will blow in the rain of Truth,
When our seed is laid to rest.
We blow in seeds from near and far,
From the east and from the west.

And from these seeds a forest grows,
From mountain high and ocean sand.
A forest grown of living knowledge,
Too share with all the land.

-Sunray Grimwillow
