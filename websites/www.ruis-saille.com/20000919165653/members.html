  Trueye Sbenancalhar
 
Upon the hills Of the Northlands lived the mighty Barbarian Clan of The Red hand. so noted because of the red marks they made on the walls of their cave homes with the blood of there foes, This mighty band lived beyond the safety and shelter of Halas, In lands even more unforgiving then Everfrost peaks. Into this Clan was born a baby boy named Kilon. Life was good for the child there in the caves and cold planes of the north, his mother and father loved him and the clan was Very strong slaying all that challenged them.

This fact sadly was not missed by the Ice Queen of the North Lady Vox and with the help of the Vile Lich Mirgul they sought to clear the northland of its only mighty clan and thus safely build a ice goblin army there to make war on the rest of Northern norrath. for at this time the vast frigid plane was already in the grasp of the ice queen. So with much Vile Necromancy Mirgul conjured a vast Army of Undead Goblins from the corpses of all the goblin ever slain in combat in the Northlands, Mirgul alone was not up the task he had not the power or Will to hold sway over so many undead soulless husks at once. Lady Vox herself had to fly from her dark cold lair and use much of her power over the Ice goblin race to help the lich control their new army. It was with this new undead army and all the live Ice goblins Lady Vox could muster that they marched Upon the home caves of The Red Hand.

The battle raged for 9 days and 9 nights all who could hold the most simple of weapons fought the wave after of wave of both live and dead ice goblins. Thanks to powerful magics held by the barbarians the lich was unable to raise any slain Barbarian for so tightly were there souls tied to the Love of family and Tribunal. But this was little comfort, for all the living Ice goblin that were killed were reborn and must be killed again. Many died that day but the ice goblin army won and as a clan the Red Hand were no more. a few old women and small children escaped and fled south into Halas telling the tale of the Fall of the Red Hand, within a few years all had forgotten the tale, all but Kilon for during the battle he was Gravely wounded loosing the use of an eye, not a uncommon thing for barbarians what made this wound special was it was given by a Very power ull ice Goblin prince wielding a Vaporal blade forged in the breath of Lady Vox herself, the cut this blade left was deep, So deep that Kilon lost a part of his soul onto the blade and part of the blade was left in Kilon's soul thought this was not clear at the time, but over time it was very clear that Kilon was no more and someone else was forming in his body this man child called himself Trueye, and spoke no more of his life as Kilon Mcfalos. At the age of 13 he left the warm huts and shops of his adopted home of Halas and Searched for the caves of his Youth. Needing for some mystical reason to face the carnage and chaos of the cave battle For months he searched The barren wastes now strewn with Ice Goblin skeletons searching for anything living to kill. But at last he found the Caves he was born in and though it took him weeks he burred all the bones of every barbarian he could find. 

Returning to Halas he changed his family name to Sbenancalhar meaning "The last Red Hand " and left the frozen north. To this day he prefers Out doors to any underground or even a building. These places fill Trueye with dread for the time he saw an entire clan die protecting a few children in the last cave. And over time his strange broken soul has granted him Wondrous powers as a Shaman unto the Tribunal. Only Time will tell what his Fate is.
