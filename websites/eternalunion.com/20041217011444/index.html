The Eternal Union: Bringing The Light of the Alliance to the Defiling Legion of the Horde!

The Eternal Union has built it's foundations on the integrity of its Members. It is build on a basis of pure meritocraty, which
views it's dwarves, gnomes, night-elves and humans as heroic beings, with their own happiness as the moral purpose of their lives, with productive achievement in their Unions as their noblest activity, and reason with honor as their only absolute path.

The light of the Eternal Union shall emerge from the darkness of the World bringing protection to it's brotherhood, progress and production to it's members and a collective power unseen by the scrambling mass of the rest of Azeroth.

The Guild Structure of the Eternal Union is as follows:

Malakim (tier one) – represent the initiates of the eternal union. Malakim are those people who have shown interest in joining the guild but have not as of yet demonstrated themselves to be guild material. Through interaction and chivalry Malakim can be promoted to the level of Sentinel

Sentinel (tier two) – compose those people that have proven themselves valuable to the guild and have demonstrated promise and determination. Typically lower level, sentinels will often gather in groups to tackle tough quests and indigenous denizens of the land of Azeroth

Archons (tier three) – As sentinels progress through their development they can be promoted to the rank of Archon. As archons they are expected to lend a certain amount of assistance to their lower level peers. In doing so they gain the favor of the higher tier individuals (including the Seraphim) and when they feel they are ready they may challenge the “Nerin Vedda” (eternal ascension) as prescribed to them by either the Seraphim or a Praetor or Solaris.

Shadow Stalker, Champion of the Union, Battle Maiden/Battle Master (tier four) – This tier has the unique distinction of possessing three separate factions within the guild. Upon completion of the Eternal Ascension, the successful Archon candidate will be promoted to one of the three positions of their choosing within the fourth tier.

<Shadow Stalkers> are those guild members who have a special affinity when it comes to hunting down/stalking vile horde members who have demonstrated a lack of honor or respect for guild members. Shadow Stalkers will often be called upon by lower tier members to exact (justified) retribution on those who have wronged them. This division shall also act as guardians of the Cherubim and Seraphim when in times of war. This position is of great honor and service to the Eternal Union.

 <Champions of the Union> are guild members who have chosen a position of assistance and aid. Where Shadow Stalkers are called for after the member has been wronged, the Champions of the Union will travel with those that may need assistance in an area that may prove too dangerous or fatal to weaker members. While it is expected of members to understand the concept of risk vs. reward, should a quest or area prove too much to handle, they may request the assistance of a Champion.

<Battle Maidens/Battle Masters> Leadership and warfare are the calling card of the members of the battle division. These guild members thrive on mass combat and thus are responsible for coordinating and executing raids composed of not only guild members but allied guilds as well. In combat the members of the Battle Division are the generals in charge of the field of battle. Their word is law during a war. The Battle Division will also be responsible for maintaining dialogue and communications with allied guilds during times of peace should we ever be called upon to defend those who need it.

Praetor, Solaris (tier five) – these guild members have demonstrated the highest level of honor and dedication to the Eternal Union. Upon promotion by the Seraphim to the fifth tier the candidate chooses one of the two possible divisions. Praetors are responsible for the implementation and execution of labor concerned with the gathering of resources and material. Solaris members are responsible for the execution of labor concerned with the manufacture of goods. Tier five members also have the distinction of being the only members (aside from the Solaris) to be able to promote lower members, demote wrong doers, assign the “Nerin Vedda” challenge to archons, and maintain guild order in times of internal dispute. While praetors and solaris members are no longer responsible for fourth tier activities, they will be recognized as experts of the positions they vacated and will often be asked for either assistance or advice by tier four members.

Cherubim (Tier Six) – The Cherubim is second only to the Seraphim in their knowledge and dedication to the honor and integrity of the Eternal Union. This position is one of honor and respect and can only be appointed by the Seraphim to individuals whom have shown intense levels of leadership, equality and fairness in all situations. The function of the Cherubim is to act as the right hand of the Seraphim, execute policy and choice made by the Praetor and Solaris, act as Arbiter in times of confusion and establish a body capable of creating stability and order.


 Seraphim (high tier) – The Seraphim is the guilds leader. As founder the Seraphim promotes/demotes and delegates responsibilities to lower members. While the Seraphim may intervene in the functioning of the guild (be it war or labor) with the confidence vote of at least 4 tier five members, it is more likely that he will lead through divine suggestion rather than force. The Eternal Union is a guild based on honor and responsibility and even the Seraphim is bound by our internal laws.
