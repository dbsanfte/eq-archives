Anyone who has ever come face to face with this beast knows what's involved in getting there. The endless timesinks and mindless farming are enough to break up any guild. But all of that was forgotten in the blink of an eye the moment we realized that it was finally Go Time.

Fennin Ro being the server that it is, we had more to deal with than keys and banes: we had to face the mindless forces of Team Grief. But today, the Griefers got their due: an AE Translocate. Buh bai.

I want to thank all Gorilla Warfare members for their dedication and hard work. It was all so worth it.
