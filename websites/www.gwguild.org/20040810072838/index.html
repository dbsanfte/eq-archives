It's about at this point that my cable modem went on hiatus, but waking up this morning and seeing "Quarm - Guild First" in the DKP system reminded me of why I'm so pleased to be a member of this guild. I knew we could do it, every other member knew we could do it, and we got it done.

Planes of Power - done. *bleedeep*

We are pleased to be the third guild on the server to accomplish this feat. When we started this guild, I knew we had the drive to get here - it's very rewarding to see the goal realized. No one knows what the future holds as we look to Gates, but for now, we'll just keep that warm fuzzy feeling and enjoy the moment.

Initial foray in Time = bunch of dead crap
Bi-Triny - June, 2004
Saturday night, we went in to Time for the first... time. We weren't really sure what to expect as far as difficulty goes, and we were kinda confused by conflicting reports as to how the instancing works. But we usually dont worry about crap like that anyway, so why start now.

We started by beating tiers 1 through 3 on the first try, and we quickly found ourselves in tier 4, in the presence of the lesser gods. After plotting and scheming about how to beat Terris Thule for, oh, about an hour, we finally just decided to beat her down. That worked - dead TT, first try. Then we looked to Saryrn. The "beat her down" strat seemed to apply once again, and lo, it worked - Saryrn down, first try.

Feeling pretty good about ourselves, we looked to Tallon Zek. Knowing from research that he is no joke, we schemed a bit, and made a run or two at him. We ended up calling it a night at that point, and came back the next to resume. We got him down to 17%, but got jacked by the "mob in wall / cant cast or hit him" bug, so that was lame. He's cooked tho next time.

Fun zone! Lots of loots. Well worth the effort. Come back next week for TZ and (fingers crossed) VZ down!
