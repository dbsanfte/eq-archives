Adventures in Monster Land - By Sandy Willow

Once upon a time there was a tiny guild called the Fun Folks. They were a hearty little group, full of wonder and excitement. The Fun Folks embarked on many wonderful adventures, seeking treasure and glory, until one day while preparing for battle they encountered the dreaded forces of...

Once upon a time there was a small guild called the Awesome Adventurers. They were a hearty group, full of excitement and wonder. The Awesome Adventurers embarked on many dangerous adventures, seeking treasure and glory, until one day while preparing for battle they encountered the dreaded forces of...

Once upon a time there was a large guild called the Rough Raiders. They were a hearty crew, full of experienced players with a drive to succeed. The Rough Raiders embarked on many awesome adventures, seeking treasure and fame, until one day while preparing for battle they encountered the dreaded forces of...

Once upon a time there was an uber guild called the Leet Legion. They were the top force in their realm, conquering all enemies and taking all treasure for themselves. Being at the top of the food chain, they often had to go back and vanquish old, long-conquered enemies to keep their battle hungry troops seasoned. In doing so, they sometimes ran in to the forces of the Rough Raiders. The Rough Raiders always seemed to be upset by the presence of the Leet Legion, accusing them of taking everything for themselves. Confused, the Leet Legion just went about their business and continued to smite their enemies.

The Rough Raiders were so mad at the Leet Legion. Why did they have to come here? Why couldn't they leave our monsters alone? Why! In the end, since the Leet Legion took all the monsters in this area, the Rough Raiders had to look elsewhere, and eventually they met another band of warriors...

The Awesome Adventurers were so mad at the Rough Raiders. Why did they have to come here? Why couldn't they leave our monsters alone? Why! In the end, since the Rough Raiders took all the monsters in this area, the Awesome Adventurers had to look elsewhere, and eventually they met another band of warriors...

The Fun Folks were so mad at the Awesome Adventurers. Why did they have to come here? Why couldn't they leave our monsters alone? Why! In the end, since the Awesome Adventurers took all the monsters in this area, the Fun Folks had to look elsewhere, and eventually they met another band of warriors...

The Ol' Man sure does like that story, confusin' as it may be. But just remember, at any time o' the day you gots fish that yer a'chasin, and fish thats a'chasin you! It's all the dad gum same.

The Big W
Labiara - October 6, 2003
 
And so, after months of hard work, we hit paydirt.

Anyone who has ever come face to face with this beast knows what's involved in getting there. The endless timesinks and mindless farming are enough to break up any guild. But all of that was forgotten in the blink of an eye the moment we realized that it was finally Go Time.

Fennin Ro being the server that it is, we had more to deal with than keys and banes: we had to face the mindless forces of Team Grief. But today, the Griefers got their due: an AE Translocate. Buh bai.

I want to thank all Gorilla Warfare members for their dedication and hard work. It was all so worth it.

Of course the Rifts were what we were after, but there were some other goodies that dropped:

The Sword of Ssraeshza - Larenae
Envenomed Moccasins - Angalus

Mace of Confusion - Windiigo

In closing, I want to again thank all the members for making it through. No alliances, no handouts: we stood alone and won.
