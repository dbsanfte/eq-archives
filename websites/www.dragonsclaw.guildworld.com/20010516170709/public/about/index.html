A B O U T  
You work hard. You play hard. Why not take some of the work out of playing? Guild World has been designed to do just that.

Ever find yourself playing a great new game with a few of your friends and before you know it your small circle has grown into a small circus? Where you used to spend hours enjoying the game, you now spend hours keeping the circus going.

Guild World gives you a means of keeping your group organized without the hassles of "doing it on your own". From member data, to screen shots, to event scheduling Guild World has the tools you need to get the job done without spending all your time doing it.
