The Scourge | CHARTER

The scourge, founded May 2000, was formed on the principle that there shall be evil in the land of Norrath. Our guild takes pride in its members, and adorns them with all the riches and wealth that can be obtained in the foul outreaches of the lands. The basic principle that makes up The Scourge, is that all members play their characters how they see fit. We do not shun the rogue who's lifelong goal is to make the world a more difficult place by fattening his purse at the expense of another persons kill. We do not look harshly towards the wreckless mage who finds joy in creating chaos by poking magic at a camp filled with giants. We laugh, and continue to press onward.

We are the daerlics of Norrath, the vagrants. We are the ones who will befriend you, and turn on you in the blink of an eye. We're a shadey bunch, with an apetite for disorder. Our only loyalty is to ourselves. To our guild and its members.

Our aspirations as a guild, is to grow strong. To spread over Norrath like the virus we are.

A plague.

The Scourge!
