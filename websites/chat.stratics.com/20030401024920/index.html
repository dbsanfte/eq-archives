Saturday - March 29 - 2003
  New Features on Stratics IRC!
  IRC News - 12:57 AM EST - Posted by: Gilthas

We’ll be updating our network’s IRCd (IRC daemon) and services (ChanServ, NickServ, MemoServ, etc…) package with some new features tonight:

  • Added 500 slots for channel access lists, from 2000 – you can now have up to 2500 people on your channel’s access list!
  • Channels now have 80 AKICK slots!
  • Upped the channel limit – you can now be in 15 channels simultaneously!
  • You’re now allowed four connections to the network from the same host/IP!

    These enhancements will be active on Stratics IRC in a couple of hours!

    Never been on IRC before? It’s easy! Just download an IRC client such as mIRC (http://www.mirc.com) and connect to one of these servers on port 6667:

    irc.stratics.com – redirects to the least busy server out of the ones listed below

    irc.frws.com – USA – Colorado
    irc.expaxsys.net – USA – Colorado
    irc.glowfish.net – EU – Germany

    Hope to see you around!

    Tuesday - March 25 - 2003
      Upcomming Ballerium HoC! April 3rd
      Event News - 4:33 PM EST - Posted by: Wolfgang

    Majorem Ltd. (www.majorem.com) And The Stratics Massively Multiplayer Gaming Network (www.stratics.com) are pleased to announce a dev-chat with the developers of Ballerium, the Massive multiplayer strategy game.

    The Chat will be held on Thursday April 3rd at 8PM GMT in #BalleriumHoC on the Stratics IRC servers. Our topic this evening will be "Ballerium Beta". We will also be discussing the game's current development status as well as the upcoming E3 showup so make sure to join us that evening!

    To connect to one of the Stratics IRC servers you need to download an IRC client (mIRC, pirch, Ircle, or one of your favourite linux clients) and have it connect to irc.stratics.com port 6667 or, alternatively, to one of the following servers, preferably the one closest to you:

  • irc.frws.com port 6667 USA - Colorado
  • irc.glowfish.de port 6667 EU - Germany
  • irc.epaxsys.net port 6667 USA - Colorado

    Once you are connected, you can join the #BalleriumHoC channel by typing /join #BalleriumHoC

    For more information on how to get connected using mIRC, please see http://chat.stratics.com/content/irchelp/mirc/mirc.shtml.

    About Majorem Ltd:

    Based in Bney-Brak, Israel, Majorem Ltd. approaches the games market from its technological aspect, developing technology for massively multiplayer persistent games. Majorem's server technology will support MMP games for the Internet, and later cellular technology as well, and is being used in the development of their upcoming online strategy game ‘Ballerium’. For more information with regards to Majorem Ltd. or Ballerium, please see the respective websites at www.majorem.com and www.ballerium.com, or simply mail info@majorem.com.

    Friday - March 21 - 2003
      A little more about Stratics Plus...
      Network Announcement - 7:42 PM EST - Posted by: Gilthas

    For over 5 years Stratics has stood as a comprehensive, free service to the massively multiplayer community.

    This will not change.

    We have debated long and hard as to the extent of the Stratics Plus
    service; whether to put our existing content behind subscription, as so many other websites have done. We came to the conclusion, rightly, that our effectiveness as a community resource and leader would be far too compromised by such a move.

    Therefore we will not, nor will we ever, make you pay for the Stratics content and services that you currently enjoy. This applies not only to our present websites, but to our coverage of future titles as well. Stratics is and always will be a free service to the massively multiplayer community.

    So what we're doing is offering you an option - an alternative - that will prevent all pop-ups, flash ads, and obtrusive advertising from disrupting your experience at Stratics. In addition, we will be making available comprehensive databases that we never before had the resources to put together, as well as enhanced forum features, site skinning and other new services that go above and beyond that which we currently provide.

    By becoming a member of Stratics, you'll also have the future option of cooperating directly with developers as a valuable focus group of experienced online gamers. We're working diligently to bring you closer to those who design and build the worlds we play in, so that the Stratics group of members will be able to directly influence and shape those worlds.

    Our hope is that the Stratics Plus service will allow us to push our network further than ever before possible. If at any point you've had the urge to help Stratics in some way, you now have your chance to do so:

    Every new subscriber to the Stratics Plus service will lead directly to more bandwidth, increased coverage, and continued, comprehensive service to the entire massively multiplayer community.

    What we do is not free, yet the service we provide is. For those of you who may be offended by our offering a subscription, or are distressed financially, please feel welcome to continue your use of the Stratics sites, chat network and forums. For those of you who truly appreciate what we're trying to do and are willing to help out, please allow us to provide you with a service that we hope will be of even further value to you.

    Either way, we appreciate your patronage and thank you for being a part of this thriving online community.

    More details will be available next week, so stay tuned...
