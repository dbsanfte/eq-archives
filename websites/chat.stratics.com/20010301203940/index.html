Tuesday - February 27 - 2001
Entropia House of Commons - 2:16 PM EST
 

Entropia Stratics is pleased to host the first ever Entropia House of Commons chat. The conference will occur this Wednesday (tomorrow), February 28th, at 6pm CST (1am Central European) in #entropiahoc on the Stratics IRC Network. MindArk will be onhand to answer your questions about their upcoming release, Project Entropia.

Never been to a House of Commons before? Basically it is a developer chat, where the gamers and the designers sit down. Players get to ask the game's developers their questions via a live moderator. The designers then answer the question in the chat. It's a tried and true format for finding out what is in store for your favorite Massively Multiplayer games.

Project Entropia is an MMOG set in the future in which you are a colonist on the planet of Calypso, attempted to survive in an untamed land swarming with mutants and a mysterious robotic threat. And make money while you do it! In Project Entropia, the in-game credits have a real-world dollar value. Need that rifle? 5 bucks will get it for you! Need some cash for rent? Go kill a few mutants and cash out your credits! It's that simple. Be sure to check out the chat to find out all about this exciting new title!

To join the conference, connect to one of the following Stratics IRC servers, then type /join #entropiahoc. Or, use our Stratics Java IRC Client.

  • irc.stratics.com

  • compu.stratics.com

  • dhp.stratics.com

  • frws.stratics.com

  • intworks.stratics.com

Sunday - February 25 - 2001
Dark Age of Camelot HoC log online - 9:37 AM EST
 

Last thursday's House of Commons with the Dark Age of Camelot Developers was a great success.
Over 130 people showed up to ask questions, and even after the HoC the Camelot Devs stayed in the #Camelot channel to chat with the players.

Here is a sample from the chat:

<Mag> *Ogmuk* How does the current DAoC loot system work? Is the loot off MOBs randomly generated upon drop (ala Diablo), meaning unique loot. Or is all loot off MOBs written in stone (ala EQ), meaning all loot -no matter how rare or uncommon- is identical?

<Travin_Mythic> The loot system is a bit more robust than that of EQ. Each mob has a standard value setting, yet we assign a unique list of treasure to each monster type. As the mob dies, it pulls from this list at random, yet maintains the correct value of loot the PC should receive. The loot system is also able to vary the amount of reward against your level. This feature is only part of the many tools we have to control camping issues.

The whole chatlog can be found here.


- Mag
