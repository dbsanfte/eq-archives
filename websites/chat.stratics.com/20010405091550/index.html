Wednesday - April 4 - 2001
Neverwinter Nights House of Commons - 8:34 PM EDT
 

Neverwinter Stratics is proud to host another Neverwinter House of Commons developer chat this Wednesday, April 11th, at 8pm CST. The conference will occur in #nwnhoc. Bioware, the developers of Neverwinter Nights, will be onhand to answer your questions about this terrific new game.

Set in the popular Forgotten Realms campaign and based on 3rd Edition Advanced Dungeons & Dragons rules, Neverwinter Nights takes players to the windswept plains of the Sword Coast, where they can choose to play the game either in single-player mode, or as it is truly meant to be played, in the multi-player mode. Neverwinter Nights is the first game to fully bring the table-top RPG experience to the computer, enabling players to create their own campaigns, and host servers where others can participate in their adventure. Endless varieties of customization allow players to tailor their server to their tastes, whether it be PvP exclusive, no PvP at all, a mage's paradise, or for chaotic evil halflings only. For more information on this truly one-of-a-kind title, visit Neverwinter Stratics.

Never been to a House of Commons before? Basically it is a developer chat, where the gamers and the designers sit down. Players get to ask the game's developers their questions via a live moderator. The designers then answer the question in the chat. It's a tried and true format for finding out what is in store for your favorite Massively Multiplayer games.

To join the chat, connect to one of the following Stratics IRC servers, and type /join #nwnhoc. Or, use our Stratics Java IRC Client. The topic will be announced prior to the discussion.

  • irc.stratics.com

  • compu.stratics.com

  • dhp.stratics.com

  • frws.stratics.com

  • intworks.stratics.com

#Ultima-Online Scramble Bot Quiz! - 12:20 AM EDT
 

#Ultima-Online will be holding a Scramble Bot Quiz at 7:00PM, CST April 11th, the winner of the Scramble Bot Quiz will recieve a prize on the shard of their choice. Prizes are random, and can be gold, holiday bells, or even Ranger Armor! The Scramble Bot Quiz is quite simple, Help-Bot will tell you a word, such as 'Flamestrike', only it will be scrambled. It's your job to type 'sb' and your guess at the word after it before anyone else does, if you do, the bot will record it and and the end of the round the person with the most correct guesses will win.

Never been on Stratics-IRC before? It's simple! Download an IRC client, such as mIRC and connect to one of the following servers:

  • frws.stratics.com

    Set in the distant future on a war-torn planet Earth, Fallen Age deals with the remnants of the human civilization, who emerge from a sheltered existence deep beneath an earth that was long ago conquered by the Dark One. Not knowing what to expect, a land finally free from the Dark One's influence, or a world that has been utterly corrupted, it becomes your place to make the world into what you would have it. A blend between science-fiction and fantasy, Fallen Age promises to appeal to the roleplayer and the PvP enthusiast alike. Fallen Age will begin its beta next month.
