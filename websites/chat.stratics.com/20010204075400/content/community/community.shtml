STRATICS IRC COMMUNITY

Why Stratics?

The Stratics IRC Network is designed to serve as a forum for members of the MMORPG community to converge and discuss their favorite games, interact with developers, and host chatrooms for their guilds. Unlike other IRC networks, Stratics markets around a small, niche community, so you can easily navigate our channel list without surfing through hundreds of spam and porn channels. And rest assured, virtually anyone you run into shares the same interest in gaming that you do. The latest in Dalnet based technology not only allows you to reserve your nicknames, but to protect your channel from takeover and make it as public or private as you desire!

With a trained staff that is on round the clock and a user-friendly Public Policy geared to create a safe, non-hostile environment, Stratics IRC is dedicated to not only providing public chatrooms where you can go for the latest, news, hints, and general discussion on your favorite online games, but to enable you to set up your own private guild chats.

Stratics IRC offers the following benefits:

  • Hostmask mirroring protects you from hostile users out there, making it virtually impossible for them to gain your IP address via IRC.
  • 24 Hour Technical Support from our experienced IRC staff in #irc-help. Help is always just a channel away!
  • Public Gaming Channels based on all MMORPG's on the market, with staff and visitors always available to provide assistance.
  • Public Policy enforced in our public channels, providing a safe, user-friendly environment.
  • Developer Presence in many of our public channels...game designers are constantly popping in to talk to the users and solicite feedback on their games.
  • Nickserv/Chanserv allows you to reserve your own nickname and reserve your own channels for personal use.
  • House of Commons Program offers regular conferences between the players and the developers, providing the latest on the most popular games.

Come in and see for yourself why Stratics IRC is the only IRC network for the online gamer!
