The heart of Massively Multiplayer gaming is the online community. And what drives these communities is the idea of guilds. Groups of players linked by ideas of playstyle, roleplaying values, ties of friendship, and more, belonging to a guild and playing a game together is one of the most rewarding aspects of the Massively Multiplayer genre. So what makes a good guild? Communication. No matter what game(s) you play, what keeps a guild going is communication between its members. And this is the value of IRC.

What is IRC? Internet Relay Chat. IRC allows you to create a channel (or chatroom) and have real-time conversations with your fellow guildmembers from around the globe. And Stratics IRC offers the benefit of being on the same network with other online guilds. Many game developers frequent our chatrooms and regularly talk with gamers. You can even join public discussions on your favorite games and offer your own opinions, receive assistance, or hear interesting rumors. As a network devoted exclusively to online gaming, only on Stratics will everyone you run into know and be familiar with the same games that you are. Why waste time surfing through endless porn and trash channels on other networks? Head to Stratics IRC and find out why Stratics is your online source for online gaming.

Stratics IRC offers the following benefits to those guilds which would like to host their chatroom on our servers:

  • Hostmask Reflection keeps others from gaining your IP address via IRC for malicious purposes.
  • Chanserv/Nickserv Services allows you to register your favorite nickname and chatroom so that no one can steal it from you and take control...you even have the ability to ban unwanted users from your channel.
  • Secret and Password Protected Modes help you to make your chatroom as private or as public as you like...even allowing you to require players to have the correct password to join your channel!
  • 24 Hour IRCop Coverage means that IRCops are always available to assist you should you require it.
