#HEROSJOURNEY

Hero's Journey is the long awaited MMORPG from Simutronics, better know for their GemStone III and DragonRealms games. Merging top of the line 3d-accelerated graphics, with state of the art design innovations, Hero's Journey brings to the table the experience of a company used to creating online games. Constantly seeking out player input to further improve their design, the creators of Hero's Journey are highly active both on the boards and in channels.

#Herosjourney is one such venue for players to come together and give their thoughts, opinions, and ideas for this eagerly awaited game. The web staff for Hero's Journey Stratics are constantly online providing support and guiding discussion. Drop in and find out more about this much anticipated title.

To join #herosjourney, simply type /join #herosjourney after connecting to the Stratics Network.
