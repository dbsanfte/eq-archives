    Wednesday - July 4 - 2001
    UO House of Commons Chat - 10:33 AM EDT
     

    Hello everyone,

    Stratics is happy to announce its weekly Ultima Online House of Commons Chat.

    It will be held on this Thursday, 7/5. The usual time at 7pm CST (8pm EST,5pm PST).

    The Room will be #uohoc as usual

    To connect to Stratics IRC, first boot up your favorite IRC client. Two of the most popular clients are mIRC and pIRCH.

    Once your client has been started, access one of the follow Stratics
    servers by typing /server *.stratics.com:

    irc.stratics.com
    dhp.stratics.com
    compu.stratics.com
    frws.stratics.com
    intworks.stratics.com
    Or, feel free to use our own Java client via your web browser which can be found at:

    http://chat.stratics.com/content/java/800_600chat.shtml

    This java client connects directly to our servers and even allows you to select which channel you wish to access upon startup.


    Thanks and have a good time

    Stonegate
    Stratics Lead Event Coordinator


    - Stonegate
     

    Monday - July 2 - 2001
    Neverwinternights House of Commons - 2:44 AM EDT
     

    Neverwinter Nights Stratics will host the next House of Commons chat with BioWare on July 5th at 6:00 PM PST/9:00 PM EST. (If you are in other time zones, check out World Time Zones to find out what time that is where you are).

    The chat will hosted in the moderated channel #nwnhoc on the Stratics IRC servers. For more information about House of Commons chats and connecting to our IRC servers or our Java IRC interface, please go to chat.stratics.com. If you wish to join us before the chat, you may join us in the #neverwinter channel.

    As the BioWare staff has limited time to respond to questions, we ask that you take the time to read the Frequently Asked Questions documents so that we can focus on questions that have not been answered in other media. You can find the official Neverwinter Nights FAQ at Neverwinter Facts
    Page     or Official Neverwinter Nights FAQ . The href="http://webboard.interplay.com:8080/~neverwinter">official Neverwinter Nights forums and the Neverwinter Nights Stratics forums offer other sources of information about the game.

    Check Neverwinter Nights Stratics for more information about the chat and how the question/answer process will be managed.

    We look forward to seeing you there.

    Marizelle
    Project Manager
