  Project Entropia House of Commons Chat!
   - 12:20 PM EST - Posted by: Wolfgang

Stratics is pleased to host yet another Entropia House of Commons chat. The conference will occur this Thursday, November 6th at 3PM CET in #entropiahoc on the Stratics IRC Servers.

Never been to a House of Commons before? Basically it is a developer chat, where the gamers and the designers sit down. Players get to ask the game's developers their questions via a live moderator. The designers then answer the question in the chat. It's a tried and true format for finding out what is in store for your favorite Massively Multiplayer games.

Project Entropia is an MMOG set in the future in which you are a colonist on the planet of Calypso, attempted to survive in an untamed land swarming with mutants and a mysterious robotic threat. And make money while you do it! In Project Entropia, the in-game credits have a real-world dollar value. Need that rifle? 5 bucks will get it for you! Need some cash for rent? Go kill a few mutants and cash out your credits! It's that simple. Be sure to check out the chat to find out all about this exciting new title!

You can connect to our servers by using either mIRC, pirch, Ircle, or one of your favorite linux chat clients and entering one of the following servers:

  • irc.frws.com (USA, Colorado)
  • irc.glowfish.de (EU, Germany)
  • irc2.frws.com (USA, Colorado)
  • irc.stratics.com (re-routing to one of the above servers)

    Once connected, just type /join #entropiahoc

    We look forward to seeing you there!
