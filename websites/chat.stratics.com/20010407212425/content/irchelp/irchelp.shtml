1: What is #irc-help

    #irc-help is the official help channel for Stratics IRC. #irc-help is staffed mainly by our Customer Service Coordinators and other IRCop's.

    IRCop's on our network are highly skilled, professional mannered, volunteers dedicated to helping those are our network who require assistance.

    #Irc-help IRCop's and other volunteers will assist you with any questions relating to the Stratics IRC network including using commands, dealing with issues of harassment, and general assistance starting your own channels or registering your nickname. They can also assist you with questions regarding your IRC client software like MIRC and Pirch, as well as a variety of other topics relating to IRC.
Return to the top of the page

2: How to get assistance
    2a: Who are the official help people
      The official help in #irc-help will be designated with an @ symbol before their name.
    2b: Who are the other people in the channel
      The other people in the channel are people volunteering time to help the official #irc-help team out or other people answering questions. We encourage people to join our channel if they are capable of providing assistance with IRC, its a great place to also learn some things you may not know yourself.

    2c: I tried to get help but no one is responding
      If you are unsuccessful in getting help please try the following:

        Find someone who has an @ symbol before their name and /MSG <That nickname> your question here.

        Trying saying his or her full nickname as commonly this is a method to page someone who is away from keyboard (AFK).

        Then if no one answers after few minutes try !help <your question here> in the #irc-help channel.

        If you still cannot get someone to respond please address an e-mail to irchelp@stratics.com with your nickname, the time you tried to reach us, and a full description of the problem you were experiencing and we will try and respond ASAP

    2d: What does !help <your question here> do?
      !Help <your question here> sends a message to a channel where all online IRCop's can see your help request. This is a way to get help with your problem in a prompt timely manner, however we do suggest trying your question in the #irc-help channel first.
Return to the top of the page

3: How can I reach you for help outside of IRC

    We can be emailed at irchelp@stratics.com. Please include your nickname and a full description of the problem you were experiencing and we will try to respond ASAP.

Return to the top of the page

4: What are the rules for #irc-help

    Public channel etiquette should be adhered to. Spaming, constant nick changes, flooding or any type, and any type of rude behavior will not be tolerated. Failure to abide by any of these rules could result in banning from the channel.

    The #irc-help rules are as follows:

      1: Profanity is not allowed in #irc-help as it is a public channel

      2: Please cease all chatting when a help request is placed or is currently being resolved.

      3: If an IRCop or someone with an @ before their name in the channel is helping, please do not interfere

      4: Do not contradict help being given, /MSG the person giving help if you feel their assistance is not correct
Return to the top of the page

5: Can I help in there?
    Yes, we welcome any experienced IRC users to join us and take part in helping those who need assistance. We ask that if you do provide your help that you make certain your answers are accurate and clear. We ask that if an official IRCop or person with an @ before their name, takes over the help request, you allow them to do so.
