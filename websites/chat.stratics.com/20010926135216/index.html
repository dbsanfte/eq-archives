Sunday - September 9 - 2001
Destination Games Chat - TONIGHT! - 7:05 PM EDT
 

The time has come!

TONIGHT, Monday September 10th, at 7:00PM CDT Stratics IRC will be hosting a special Celebrity Chat with Richard “Lord British” Garriott, Starr “Darkstarr” Long, Dwight “Melantus” Spaulding, and Susan “Leilo” Kath of Destination Games as well as Jake Song of NCSoft.

Never been on Stratics IRC before? It’s easy. Download an IRC client such as mIRC and connect to one of the following servers:

  • irc.stratics.com
  • irc.stratics.dhp.com
  • stratics.frws.com
  • riot.tac.net
  • stratics.truefear.net

    As an alternative, you can connect with our Java IRC client located here. Once you're online, type /join #Celebrity-Chat

    We hope all of you can join us on Stratics IRC! Who knows who else will show up?


    - Gilthas
