The Legal Stuff - [Top]

You agree upon connecting that you, the user, are responsible for your own actions while on this network. Please, protect yourself from other users through the use of firewalls and other products that discourage viruses and trojans. Do not accept files or scripts from any users unless you are absolutely sure they are safe. Stratics IRC is not responsible for the actions of ANY users on this network. If you have questions, please contact irchelp@stratics.com or ask in #IRC-Help for clarification.

The Stratics IRC Network provides this service "as-is" with no warranties expressed or implied. We reserve the right to change system policies at anytime and also reserve the right to allow or disallow anyone from the IRC network for any reason, at any time.

Stratics is a privately-owned network, and we reserve the right to refuse service to anyone at any time for any reason. Usage of our network indicates agreement with the rules of the Stratics IRC Network. Our policy is subject to change at any time without prior notice.

Stratics IRC is a 100% Volunteer IRC network.

Policies and Expectations - [Top]

This IRC Network was created to support the MMORPG gaming crowd. It has since expanded to include most all forms of online gaming. With this in mind, we ask you to observe the following:

  1. Harassment of network users or staff is not tolerated. If you have a problem with another user or a member of our staff, please take it to #IRC-Help and ask for assistance. This includes but is not limited to: verbal attacks and/or threats, and sexual harassment.

  2. Channels on the network:

    1. Channels must not compete in name or content with our sponsored channels.
    2. Channels must be kept appropriate in both channel name and topic. Channel topics cannot contain profanity or be disgusting/rude if they are publicly viewable using the /list command. If you feel the need to express yourself in creative ways with your channel's topic, please make your channel secret (+s). Channels that are inappropriate or have profanity in their name are not allowed and will be closed.
    3. Channels created for the purpose of belittling or slandering others are not allowed and will be closed.
    4. Channels created using special characters in their names to place them at the top of the channel list are not allowed.
    5. Channels that deal with religion or politics, either in channel name or channel purpose/content are not allowed and will be closed.
    6. Channels that deal in pornography, warez, mp3s, game cheats/hacks/exploits, or are sponsored by, or related to, websites that condone or distribute cheats/hacks/exploits/warez/mp3s/porn are not allowed and will be closed. This also applies to channel topics. (Topics will be removed, but the channel will not be closed unless there are repeat offenses.)
    7. Please try to keep channels as related to gaming as possible.
      1. Guild channels are welcomed and encouraged on Stratics IRC.
      2. General discussion channels that do not compete with Stratics-sponsored channels are OK.

  3. Nicknames on the network:

    1. Please keep your nickname(s) appropriate.
      1. No racist nicknames.
      2. No sexually-explicit nicknames.
      3. No nicknames that belittle or harass others.

  4. Sponsored Channels:

    1. Bots are not allowed in our sponsored channels without permission from network staff.
    2. Profanity and suggestive language are not allowed in our public channels.

  5. Respect the other users and the staff on this network, and everyone will get along fine!

  6. In General:

    1. Channel/server/user floods, takeover attempts or any action that may disrupt the network will not be tolerated.
    2. The use of this network to transfer illegal software (viruses, zombies, trojans, pirated software) will not be tolerated.
      1. File server bots are not allowed.

Stratics IRC has a policy of non-interference in channels not run or moderated by Stratics. However, we have a low tolerance for people that abuse the use of the Stratics IRC Network. Causing trouble in public or private channels can result in a ban from the entire network - both for you and potentially for your entire ISP.

If you wish to use this IRC Network, you agree to the above rules and policies, and any others as may be posted on the website.

If you do not agree with any policy or rule - please disconnect yourself and find another network that better suits your needs!

General Information - [Top]

Servers

Our current servers are as follows:

  • irc.frws.com - USA - Colorado
  • irc.epaxsys.net - USA - Colorado
  • irc.goldfish.de - EU - Germany

Please choose a server from the list above if you have trouble connecting to irc.stratics.com. Choosing a server in your general geographical area reduces lag, connection issues and helps to even out server loads.

Connections

Stratics IRC limits user connections from any given host. If you have a legitimate need that requires more than the amount of connections allowed from one host, ask in #IRC-Help for assistance. The staff there will tell you the procedure involved in getting a connection exemption granted.

Linking Servers

Stratics does not allow the linking of servers to its network. If you wish to be a part of the Stratics IRC Network, ask in #IRC-Help and they can direct you to the appropriate staff member. Stratics will not distribute its IRC code in any way, shape, or form so please do not ask for it.

IRC Assistance

If you require assistance with IRC, including but not limited to: network problems, client problems or user problems; you can do one of the following:

  • Join #IRC-Help (/join #IRC-Help) and ask for help.
  • E-mail irchelp@stratics.com
  • /MSG ChanServ HELP for channel-related help.
  • /MSG NickServ HELP for nickname related help.
  • /MSG MemoServ HELP for help with sending memos.
For information regarding k-lines (link here to IRC terms page, k-line reference), please e-mail kline@stratics.com - PLEASE NOTE: Only the party that is k-lined will receive information regarding their k-line. If you e-mail us for your buddy who was removed from the network, we will not discuss it with you.

Network Staff

The Network Staff is here to assist you with your channel and customer service needs, and can be found in #IRC-Help. Please be respectful to these folks. They have volunteered their time and in some cases, resources, to make your gaming and IRC experience more enjoyable. If you do not respect the Network and Channel staff - do not be surprised if you find them reluctant to assist you when you need it!

To see a current list of:

  • Sponsored Channel Staff, click here.
  • Network Staff (IRCOps, etc.), click here.

Events

On a monthly basis (for each event, usually - there are multiple events in a month) Stratics hosts events on the network. Events not hosted by Stratics and that compete with sponsored events are not allowed. Please contact events@irc.stratics.com with any questions you may have or if you have an event you would like assistance with, or that may compete.
