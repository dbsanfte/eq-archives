Tuesday - November 19 - 2002
  Dragon Empires DevChat - November 25th!
  Event News - 6:29 AM EST - Posted by: Antarion

Stratics IRC and Codemasters are proud to present another Developer Chat with the makers of Dragon Empires, an upcoming massive multi-player online game.

It will be held on Monday, November 25th 2002 at both 7PM GMT -and- 8 PM PST (If you are in other time zones, check out World Time Zones to find out what time that is where you are) in the #DE-DevChat channel on our IRC Servers. You connect to them by using either mIRC, pirch, Ircle, or one of your favorite linux chat clients and entering one of the following servers:

  • irc.frws.com (USA, Colorado)
  • irc.glowfish.de (EU, Germany)
  • irc2.frws.com (USA, Colorado)
  • irc.stratics.com (re-routing to one of the above servers)

    Once connected, just type /join #DE-DevChat

    We look forward to seeing you there!

    Monday - November 11 - 2002
      Anarchy Online House of Commons, Nov. 17th
      Event News - 9:11 AM EST - Posted by: Antarion

    Stratics is proud to host another Anarchy Online House of Commons chat on November 17th at 8pm GMT.

    The conference will occur in #aohoc. Funcom, the designers of Anarchy Online will be onhand to answer your questions about the upcomming booster pack "The Notum Wars".

    We expect the following Funcom guests:

  • Gaute Godager (gaute) - Game Director
  • Johan Karlsson (Belith) - Lead Designer
  • Enno Rehling (enno) - Lead Coder on Notum Wars
  • Morten Byom (Znore) - Lead World Designer
  • April Jones (FuncomApril) - PR Manager
  • Darren Scott (Dai-Galean) - Designer
  • Thomas Johnsen (Cz) - Community

    Anarchy Online is set in the distant future on the world of Rubi-Ka, where mankind has affected the course of evolution via genetic splicing. Controlled with an ironfist by the Omni-Tek corporation, Rubi-Ka is made up of a large assortment of peoples with differing values, beliefs, abilities, and weaknesses. With political intrigue in the air, it is inevitable that the conflicts between these groups will soon simmer over and Anarchy will be born!

    You can connect to the irc servers by using mIRC, pirch, Ircle, or one of your favorite linux chat clients and connect to irc.stratics.com port 6667 or to a server closest to you:

  • irc.frws.com port 6667 USA - Colorado
  • irc.glowfish.de port 6667 EU - Germany
  • irc2.frws.com port 6667 USA - Colorado

    We hope to see you all there!
