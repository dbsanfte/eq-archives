#Anarchy-Online - Anarchy Online

#Anarchy-Online

In the world of Rubi-Ka, the company known as Omni-Tek has tampered with the human geno code, resulting in a variety of new breeds of humanity. And as the varius factions of life on this desert world begin to clash, the world degenerates into...Anarchy.

Such is the premise behind this eagerly anticipated title from the Norwegian company Funcom. Anarchy-Online offers the choice of playing a variety of character types, from the Opifex assassins to the mage-like Homo Nano, able to wield nano-bots to dire effect. And #anarchy-online is the place to go to discuss the intricacies of these characters and to form alliances.

Featuring round the clock chat, #anarchy-online offers the latest in news, information, and general discussion regarding this futuristic landscape.

To join #anarchy-online, simply type /join #anarchy-online after connecting to the Stratics network.
