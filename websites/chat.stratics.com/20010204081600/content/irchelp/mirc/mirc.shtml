mIRC Help

Setting up and using one's IRC client can be confusing to do for someone new to community. There are a variety of different IRC clients to choose from, and it is not always obvious what one needs to do first when they have their IRC client. Stratics understands the confusion and frustration that can result from this experience. But fear not...once one is used to IRC, it becomes a breeze. It is this innate simplicity which has made IRC chatting such a successful part of the internet community for years.

mIRC is one of the most popular IRC clients available. Simple, yet possessing a wide range of commands and features, mIRC allows you to take command of your IRC chat experience. Kehleyr, our Lead Training Coordinator for Stratics IRC, has prepared the following help files. Complete with screenshots of what your program will look like, these easy-to-read pages should assist you in starting off your IRC chat experience right.

  • Setting up a Server - How to set up your mIRC client to connect to one of the Stratics servers.
  • Selective Acceptance of DCC Files and Chats - DCC is a method by which users can send files back and forth to one another. And as anyone who is aware of the internet knows, not all files are benign. This section teaches you how to set up your client so you can choose which files to receive.
  • Ignoring DCC File Sends & Chats - And this is how you can shut off DCC altogether.

For more information on how to receive assistance in using mIRC, contact our Customer Service staff via email, or visit them online in #irc-help.
