What is the House of Commons program?

What is the House of Commons program?

The House of Commons program first began back in 1997 with Ultima Online. Since then, the House of Commons program has spread to cover most MMORPGs in development. Today, many of our Stratics sites have an HOC event occur on a monthly basis. For a full listing of House of Commons events coming up, please refer to our Events section.

What is the House of Commons? Quite simply, it is a conference set up between the players and the game designers. Held in a moderated channel, questions are submitted to a designated question taker to be posted, one by one, for the developers to answer. The chat is moderated by players of the game, most often those who update fan sites and are familiar with the community.

How can I participate in the House of Commons program?

First, join the channel where the House of Commons is occuring. This channel is always announced in all publicity surrounding the event prior to its occurrence. Or you can check the Events section.

To submit a question, merely type /msg NICK -Type your question here-, where the NICK is the nickname of the question taker (these names are always announced prior to the chat). Please be aware that due to the sheer number of questions submitted, not all can be answered.

Where can I view logs of previous HOCs?

All logs are kept on the Stratics sites based on that game. Currently, we have HOC programs set up for the following games:
