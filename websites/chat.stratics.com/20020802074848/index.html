Motor City Online House of Commons, August 8th - 7:36 PM EDT
 

Stratics IRC and Motor City Online Stratics are proud to present another House of Commons with the event staff of Motor City Online. The topic of this months HoC will be: Motor City Online Events and First Gear Club

It will be held on August 8, 2002 at 6 PM PST (If you are in other time zones, check out World Time Zones to find out what time that is where you are) in the #MCOhoc channel on our IRC Servers. You connect to them by using either mIRC, pirch, Ircle, or one of your favorite linux chat clients and entering one of the following servers:



  • irc.stratics.dhp.com
  • stratics.frws.com
  • coldfront.gamers-irc.org
  • stratics.truefear.net
  • irc.stratics.com / irc.gamers-irc.org (re-routing to one of the above servers)

    We look forward to seeing you there!



    - Kiir

    Thursday - July 4 - 2002
    Adellion House of Commons, July 14th - 11:26 AM EDT
     

    There is going to be an Adellion House of Commons Chat on July 14th at 10pm BST, 5pm EST, 2pm PST in #adhoc. If you are in other time zones, check out World Time Zones to find out what time that is where you are.

    Adellion is a Massive Multiplayer Online Role Playing Game created by Honour Bound and Adellion enthusiasts all around the world. Adellion will be a diverse and vast world with many possibilities and options for the player. Anyone with enough dedication will be able to directly affect Adellion’s future. Founding a town, leading an army and farming land are just few of the things possible for players. For more information about Adellion, check out their homepage.

    To join us, either use the Java client located on chat.stratics.com or get yourself an IRC client for your operating system and join #adhoc on one of the following servers:

    • coldfront.gamers-irc.org - USA, Oklahoma
    • stratics.frws.com - USA, Colorado
    • stratics.truefear.net - USA, Texas
    • irc.stratics.dhp.com - USA, Pennsylvania
    • irc.stratics.com / irc.gamers-irc.org (re-routing to one of the above servers)
    John "Crydee" Bates will of course be there along with his team discussing the current status of Adellion and what it's future holds - we hope to see you all there!


    - Antarion
