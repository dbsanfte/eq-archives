PUBLIC POLICY

Legal Stuff -

The Stratics IRC Network provides this service "As-Is" with no warranties expressed or implied.
We reserve the right to change system policies at anytime and also reserve the right to allow or disallow anyone from the IRC network for any reason, at any time. Stratics IRC is a 100% Volunteer IRC network.
You agree upon connecting that you, the user, are responsible for your own actions while on this network. Please, protect yourself from other users through the use of firewalls and other products that discourage viruses and trojans. Do not accept files or scripts from any users unless you are absolutely sure they are safe. Stratics IRC is not responsible for the actions of ANY users on this network. If you have questions, please contact irchelp@stratics.com or ask in #irc-help for clarification.

This IRC Network was created to support the MMORPG gaming crowd. It has since expanded to include most all forms of online gaming. With this in mind, we ask you to observe the following :

  • Keep server and channel content to subjects as related to gaming as possible.

    • a) Guild channels are Welcomed on Stratics IRC.
    b) General discussion channels that deal with gaming issues and that do not compete with Stratics sponsored channels, either in name or content are OK.
  • Enjoy yourself! Have FUN! Always! (Really! That is why we are here!)
  • Do not do anything to disrupt or detract from the enjoyment of others using the network, to include (but not limited to):

    • a) No channel/server/user floods, takeover attempts or any actions that may disrupt the network.
    b) No verbally attacking or harassing other channels or users, or Network Staff.
    c) No channels dealing with sexual related topics, warez or MP3 content will be allowed.
    d) No channels dealing with religious or political subjects/matters.
    e) No channels created that slander or make fun of other users or staff will be allowed.
    f) Do not place bots of any kind in the Stratics sponsored channels without explicit permission.
  • The use of this network as a means to transfer illegal software (viruses, zombies, pirated software, etc) is strictly prohibited. NO file server bots, please!
  • Please - do not harass the Staff! If you have an issue with a channel, staff members or the rules - take it to #irc-help and ask for assistance.
  • Profanity is NOT allowed in public or private channel names or in public channel topics! (If you feel the need to express yourself in such a manner in your channel topic - then please, make the channel +s channel) No profanity in channel names is allowed under any circumstances.
  • Keep the peace, have fun! Be respectful of others and the Network Staff!
    Respect others and they will respect you...

Stratics is a private network, and we reserve the right to refuse service to anyone at any time for any reason. Usage of our network indicates agreement with the rules of the Stratics IRC Network. Our policy is subject to change at any time without prior notice.

Channels -

  • Channels that are similar in name or topic to any of the Stratics sponsored Channels are not allowed (See the channel list below).
  • Channels created using special characters to place them at the top of the Channel list and that serve no other obvious purpose are discouraged.

    • Stratics has a policy of non-interference in channels not run or moderated by Stratics. However, we have a low tolerance for people that abuse the use of the Stratics IRC Network. Causing trouble in public or private channels can result in a ban from the entire network - both for you and potentially your entire ISP.

    Take your complaints to #irc-help or email irchelp@stratics.com.

    So act nicely towards each other and the Network Staff and we all get along just fine!

Network Staff -

    Network Staff are here to assist you with your channel and customer service needs, and can be found in #irc-help - please be respectful of these folks. They have volunteered their time and in some cases, resources, to make your gaming and IRC experience more enjoyable. If you do not respect the Network and Channel staff - do not be surprised if you find them reluctant to assist you when you need it!! :>)

Events -

    From time to time, Stratics hosts events on the network. Events not hosted by Stratics and that compete with sponsored events are not allowed. Please contact events@stratics.com with any questions you may have or if you have an event you would like to have, or that may compete.

Server Specific Help -

    ChanServ help can be found using /chanserv help
    NickServ help can be found using /nickserv help
    MemoServ help can be found using /memoserv help
    or check in the Navigation Bar to the left at "IRC Assistance".

Connection Limitations -

    Stratics IRC limits user connections from any given host. If you have a legitimate need that requires more than the connections allowed from the same host, ask in #irc-help for assistance. The staff there will tell you the procedure involved in getting a connection exemption granted.

Linking a Server -

    Stratics does not allow the linking of servers onto its network. If you wish to be a part of the Stratics IRC Network, ask in #irc-help and they can direct you to the appropriate Staff member. (NOTE: We are not accepting server links at this time).
