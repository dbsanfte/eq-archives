  Stratics needs your help - a letter from Den
  Stratics Plus - 6:01 PM EDT - Posted by: Gilthas

As you may or may not be aware, we have been adding more and more advertisements to our pages over the last couple years. This was an effort to offset the declining ad revenue we have been receiving during this period. Over the last two years we have seen our overall number of daily impressions go up and our ad revenue go down. This is very important to note because this means our expenses have gone up, but our income has gone down. We worked very hard to overcome this by adding more pop-up ads, more intrusive forms of advertising like takeover ads, flash ads and so forth.

Even with these additional advertisemets, our ad revenue is no longer enough to cover our hosting fees. Let me reiterate the importance of that statement. Advertising alone no longer covers our monthly hosting expenses which cost us thousands of dollars a month. The irony of growing expenses and declining revenue should not be lost here. I’d laugh if it were not so serious for this network. We have done everything in our power, and I do mean everything – we’ve sacrificed quite a bit - to keep this network online.

We have worked hard to provide a valuable service to the MMOG community by providing as much information and services as we can for each title we cover. By offering community forums, news and information, guides and reviews, developer chats and interviews and so much more we’ve strived to become a complete online resource. We hope that you’ve come to count on Stratics as a source for information about your favorite MMOG title.

We tried hard to avoid exactly what I am doing here with this message to you; asking you for help. But really, it has come to just that. We need our visitors to help us stay online. Yes, it is that serious. We have done the best that we could to keep the Stratics Network online without any cost to our visitors, but we just cannot achieve that end through advertising alone anymore. The trend we’ve seen over the last year or so has been sites like ours asking for donations or offering membership services. It seems we now must move in that direction as well.

In order to try and help offset this declining advertising market, we’ve launched a new service called Stratics Plus. In partnership with Flashlink, we can now offer an ad-free Stratics, as well as promising future enhancements for our Plus (and, as always, non-plus) customers. Beyond that, a Stratics Plus membership helps to pay for our hosting support, and the Flashlink membership provides you with access to a number of other sites that have had to move in this direction.

We believe we offer a valuable service to the MMOG community and we hope you feel the same way. Help us continue to provide these services by joining the Stratics Plus program. I must admit, it is pretty cool to browse the network completely ad free! We do understand that many people have their own pop-up and ad blockers (which ironically might have helped the decline in ad revenue), but Flashlink gives you much more. You also gain some great new perks in the forums. Like the ability to edit your own title, the ability to post polls, special search functions, access to a special smiley icon library of over 55 icons and much, much more. We’ve also added a new EverQuest Database that includes over 11,600+ detailed items and three new modules are in development. They will be added over the next few weeks (one module a week). These include a reference material on spells, quests and factions. A new DAoC database is also going to be released in the very near future! We really want to show our appreciation to those who help us by providing as many tools, features and services as we can. Be sure to watch for announcements as each new item is released.

Your help is greatly appreciated and we hope that with this new service we can not only continue to provide great content and services to you, but we hope it grows enough to allow for us to expand our services even further. So please, consider helping Stratics by becoming a Stratics Plus member today. We appreciate the contributions that so many have made in the past and we really did not want to have to ever ask for a financial contribution from anyone. We have done our best to find other ways to keep the network financially viable; we're just running low on options at this time. For now any help, even the smallest commitment you feel able to make really can make a difference. Please help us so that we can continue offering you the services you’ve come to expect from Stratics.

Warm regards,

Ryan "Den" Findley

Founder

  Two new Stratics Plus forum features added!
  Stratics Plus - 1:22 PM EDT - Posted by: Gilthas

We have added two more features to our forums that are exclusive to Stratics Plus members! Right after the launch of this new service, requests came in for the option to toggle the Plus icon and membership text from individual posts. We listened and you now have that option! We have also added the ability for Stratics Plus members to edit their own title whenever they like. This should be a fun new option for everyone! Here's a more detailed break down of the new features:

Plus Icon Toggle

You may now toggle whether or not other forum visitors see the "Plus" icon next to your name and also whether or not the "Member since." text appears to others as well. This option basically allows for you to be a Plus member, but not have the icon and text shown to others in the forums. You have two options here, you can remove these items from being viewed in general (i.e. posts, who's online, etc.) and also in your profile which others view when they click on your username. To manage this feature, go to your Control Panel and, in the Main Configuration section, click on the Display Preferences option. At the bottom of this page you'll find the new options.

Edit Title

The second feature which we feel will be a very popular one is the option to edit your own title! You can now edit your title any time you like. This will allow for you to match your title to your title of your character in the game. You can be silly, whatever you like. Please note however that you will not be permitted to impersonate staff or anyone else for that matter. So use proper judgment when editing titles, we will enforce the RoC for our forums. To edit your title, visit the Control Panel and, in the Main Configuration section, click on the Personal Information option. There you will find the new option.

Please be sure to let us know what you think of these enhancements. Over time we will continue to add more and more tools and features to both our forums and our web sites.

Wednesday - April 16 - 2003
  Announcing The Official Launch of Stratics Plus!
  Network Announcement - 3:02 PM EDT - Posted by: Gilthas



[ Stratics Plus Registration ]

Announcing The Official Launch of Stratics Plus!


For over 5 years Stratics has stood as a comprehensive, free service to the Massively Multiplayer community.

This will not change.

Therefore we will not, nor will we ever, make you pay for the Stratics content and services that you currently enjoy. This applies not only to our present websites, but to our coverage of future titles as well. Stratics is and always will be a free service to the Massively Multiplayer community.

So what we're doing is offering you an option - an alternative - that will prevent all pop-ups, flash ads, and obtrusive advertising from disrupting your experience at Stratics. In addition, we will be making available to our Members comprehensive databases that we've never before had the resources to put together, as well as enhanced forum features, site skinning and other new services that go above and beyond that which we currently provide... [ Our Pledge ]

Advertising vs. Subscription

Why does Stratics now offer a Subscription service if our stated goal is to remain "free"? Well, the reality of the situation is that Stratics is no longer able to support itself with Advertising revenue alone. We've been forced to seek out other options in order to stay alive. Due to this, we have launched our new Subscription service, presented as an option to those of you who would like to assist us with our growth toward a more complete, database-driven network.

The decision to move towards this type of service was not an easy one. Since our launch in 1997, we've done our best to remain 100% free. Unfortunately, the Advertising market has not recovered from the downturn it's taken over the past few years--in fact, it has gotten progressively and consistently worse.

In the past, money earned from a single banner ~ such as the one located at the top of this page ~ was more than sufficient to keep Stratics online. Over time, however, the Advertising market has steadily declined and we've been forced to run more and more intrusive forms of Advertising ~ such as the dreaded Pop-up ~ merely in order to survive.

We continue to deliver more impressions, but earn less and less.

Advertising no longer covers our hosting costs.

An alternative has become necessary.

The Stratics Plus Service

Finally, we're now able to provide you with your chance to banish into the Nether Realms every single one of those annoying Pop-up, Pop-under, JavaScript, Flash and Take-over ads that appear on Stratics. By signing up and becoming a Member of Stratics Plus, you will not only help support our network by providing us an alternative to the failing Advertising market; you'll benefit from exclusive access to a number of new features and databases available only to Members:


  • No More Invasive Advertising!

    The very first thing that you'll notice about becomnig a Stratics Plus member is the removal of all advertisements from our network!


    - The removal of all intrusive and third party agency advertisements (i.e. UGO) from our pages. This means no more pop-ups, pop-unders, takeover ads, flash ads, etc.


    - We’ll also be removing the standard 468x60, 120x600 and similar ads that are currently deliver by third party ad agencies.


    - The removal of ads from the forum headers as well.


    - Stratics Easter Banner Hunt! If you find any banners that have escaped our notice, just send us the offending page address and you'll be entered into a drawing to win signed memorabilia from OSI!



  • Comprehensive Database Development, Exclusive to our Members

    We are just beginning the rollout of many new comprehensive online relational databases for the wide variety of MMOG titles we cover. These databases will provide in-depth information on items, quests, spells, factions, and much, much more. This will be an ongoing development process where we continue to release new databases and expanded database features over time. So as this work progresses, the value of these databases will increase tenfold.


    To begin with we will be rolling out our EQ Items Database that has thousands of EQ Items covered. You can utilize standard or advanced search features to obtain detailed information about each item. In the coming weeks, we’ll be releasing a new add-on to this database that further expands the feature set considerably. Please refer to our Stratics Plus development calendar for details on what is on the schedule. But just to give you an idea, the plan is to begin with EverQuest Databases and Dark Age of Camelot Databases. On the calendar you’ll see information relating to each component that will be released over the scheduled development period.


    This is an extremely exciting new expansion on the content we provide for our web sites and so we hope you’ll find them to be as useful as we expect you to. This is an open-ended series of development projects and our Plus members will find them to be quite useful.



  • Free Stratics Plus e-mail aliases for every Member! (To be added in the coming months)


  • Members-Only Status and Perks

    Becoming a Stratics Plus member not only provides access to special databases and the removal of advertisements, you also gain access to an expanding number of new features within our forums! I say expanding because the list below is only the tip of the iceberg with regard to what features we offer to our Plus members.


    By joining as a Stratics Plus member, you will have the following unique features added to your account in our forums:


    - A special icon indicating your premium status within the forums.


    - Access to post polls in your forum posts. Currently reserved for moderators and administrators only.


    - Access to special premium smiley icons to use in your posts within the forums.


    - A new search option has been added to the main forum display to allow for direct searches for posts by specific forum members. This option is still available to standard users, but the location of the search form adds a level of convenience for Stratics Plus members.


    We have many new features in development as well! Be sure to watch for more announcements as they are rolled out.


    As a Stratics Plus member you will also obtain access to some exclusive areas that tend to require file transfers. These include exclusive access to online movies, demos, and more.


  • Access to the Flashlink Network of Premium Sites

    By joining the Stratics Plus membership program you also will be joining Flashlink, a network of gaming web sites that all offer exclusive content to Flashlink members. This means you’ll not only benefit form obtaining special access across the Stratics Network, but a great many other web sites as well.


    To list a few:


    EQMaps
    EQ Maps is your complete EverQuest resource for items, NPC's, landmarks, quests, and maps in the vast world of Norrath. Built on a dynamic, intuitive, and easy to use navigation system, EQ Maps has easily become a fan among the elite EverQuest players.


    EQSpoilers
    EQ Spoilers is the most advanced EverQuest database in existence. Quests, Equipment, Bestiary, Zones, Spells and Faction are all 100% integrated into one comprehensive database. This unique set up allows for a level of detail not available on other websites.


    DAoCSpoilers
    DAoC Spoilers features the most advanced Dark Age of Camelot database on the internet. Quests, bestiary, equipment, class and skill guides, atlas, and maps are all integrated in to one gigantic database.


    EQ’Lizer
    EQ'Lizer is proud to be the original and most complete EverQuest spoiler site on the internet. Boasting a completely searchable database with nearly 8,000 items, over 2,000 spells, hundreds of quests, and so much more, it is easy to see why hundreds of thousands of players visit this site every month to get "the edge" on the game.


    As more and more sites join the Flashlink network you’ll also have access to those sites as well. So with each new Flashlink site added, the value of your membership increases that much more! [ Stratics Plus FAQ ]

In addition, every subscriber to the Stratics Plus service will receive one month's free subscription to Time of Defiance, a great new downloadable MMORTS from Nicelycrafted Entertainment. As if that weren't enough, the first 1,000 subscribers will be entered into a drawing to win a brand new GeForce Video Card!

All of the Stratics Plus features are ongoing premium services that will grow and evolve over time. You have my pledge that we will do our very best to provide you, as a Member, with customized, on-demand features and options that will consistently expand upon our current list of membership benefits.

As a result of your subscription to our new service, the network will not only stay online, but will continue to flourish and launch new features according to what you, as a Member of Stratics Plus, would like to see added to our sites.

Now is only the beginning. With your assistance, Stratics will enter a much more stable phase and, as a result, roll out bigger and better content n' features well into the future.

I sincerely thank you for your patronage,

Ryan ~Den Dragon~ Findley

Founder
