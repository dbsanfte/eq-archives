Friday - November 23 - 2001
AC Radio: Sunday night - 4:06 PM EST
 

Join us this sunday night at 10pm EST for another installement of AC Radio, this week I hope to get a Dev call-in to give us some insight to what has been changed in AC, as well as what will be changed! If you need info on listening read below.

A side note, the two radio shows, Richard Garriott(Lord British), and Mark Jacobs need mirrors, I have a temp mirror setup for both shows, Mark Jacobs and RichardGarriott. If you can mirror please email me at dariuas@stratics.com Thanks!!

Ok back to AC Radio:
To listen you'll need a media player, such as Winamp, or Windows Media player. In your player, find where 'Open Location' is, in Winamp, you hit ctrl-L and in Windows Media player, goto file, then Open Location, put in the address 66.28.48.200:18692 and hit connect!

Wednesday - November 14 - 2001
Live Phone interview with Richard Garriott. - 4:25 PM EST
 

Come one, come all! Tuesday November 20th, at the stroke of 8 o'clock PM on the American Eastern time. For a live phone interview with Richard 'Lord British' Garriott, creator of the Ultima series, including the smash hit Ultima Online. Now his workings take him to his new company Destination Games, where there is no telling what wonders will be created!!! The show will be run from Stratics IRC, in the channel #radio, and hosted by yours truly, Me, Dariuas!


To listen you'll need a media player, such as Winamp, or Windows Media player. In your player, find where 'Open Location' is, in Winamp, you hit ctrl-L and in Windows Media player, goto file, then Open Location, put in the address 66.28.48.200:18692 and hit connect!

I hope you enjoy the show, bug me if you have questions!


- Dariuas
 

Sunday - November 11 - 2001
Ultima Online Celebrity Chat – November 12th! - 2:30 PM EST
 

Join us in #Ultima-Online, on November 12th at 7:00PM CST for another Ultima Online Celebrity Chat. Our Guests this chat are Prophet and Evocare, the two developers behind the Guaranteed Gain System (GGS) – they’ll be here to answer your questions about the system!

Never been to a UOCC before? The UOCC is a forum wherein we bring to you celebrity guests from the UO community, ranging from OSI personnel to people from your very own shard! To join us, simply download an IRC client such as mIRC and connect to one of the following IRC servers:

irc.stratics.dhp.com - USA - Pennsylvania
stratics.frws.com - USA - Colorado
riot.tac.net - CAN - Toronto
stratics.truefear.net - USA – Texas

As an alternative, you can use our Java IRC client located here.

We hope to see you there!


- Gilthas
