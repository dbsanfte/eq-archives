#ATRIARCH

Atriana, a living, breathing world, where drastically different species interact. Atriarch is the long awaited game from World Fusion which offers players the opportunity to select from such varied characters as the plantlike Cavalon and the desert dwelling Lokai.

In #atriarch, players meet to discuss the latest news and host general discussion on this eagerly anticipated title. Featuring frequent guest appearances from Atriarch's lead designer, Serefina, #atriarch is the place for the Atriana enthusiast to be.

To join #atriarch, type /join #atriarch once connected to the Stratics Network.
