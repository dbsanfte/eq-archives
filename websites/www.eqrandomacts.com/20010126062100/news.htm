May 18, 2000: The idea is shared between two of the founders of this guild, Random Acts, based on the thoughts expressed in the book "Random Acts of Kindness and Senseless Beauty". It is a radical idea for a RPG game that it is possible to have an guild that has as its basic tenet being kind to others as the first goal of its members.
