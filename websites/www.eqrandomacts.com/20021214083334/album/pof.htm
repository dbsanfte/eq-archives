The kind folks at Wind of Fates were nice enough to invite us on their raid.  We had a relatively small force of about 20. 
Break-in was a breeze with no mobs at the portal.  We had a couple wipes, but our well-disciplined force did very well overall. 
From Moaneek's pulling, to the crowd control of our enchanters, to the excellent healing from our clerics, to the amazing firepower
of our tanks (led by Rasor and his light-sabers), everyone did a fine job and made for a most enjoyable raid.
