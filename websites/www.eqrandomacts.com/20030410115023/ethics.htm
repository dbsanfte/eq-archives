The Guild Ethical Guidelines of Random Acts

Remember that your personal actions in any situation reflect on everyone who wears the Random Acts guild tag. Please make your decisions accordingly.

Remember the guild charter at all times:
  • Practicing kindness begins at home
  • Kindness is infectious
  • The individual is important
  • We all work together
Treat others with respect:
  • Share camps if a dispute arises
  • Give others the benefit of the doubt
  • Use zonewide communication for positive purposes
Community is important:
  • Make an effort to attend events
  • Greet other members and get to know them
  • Speak up -- share your suggestions and constructive criticism
Help is important:
  • Give help when you can
  • Ask for help when you need it
  • Accept help when it's offered
  • Trust others to do what they can
Friendship is important:
  • If you lose your temper, patch things up, in direct tells if possible
  • If you're upset, find someone to tell about it
  • If you're just too angry, take a break
Suggestions, comments, questions? Send them here.
