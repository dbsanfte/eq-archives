Jedi Praxium - Dark Praxium Affiliation Agreement

 

This agreement will govern the affiliation between the Jedi Praxium and Dark Praxium. Any questions as to the meaning or intent of this of this agreement will be interpreted under the terms of and in accordance with the Code of the Jedi Praxium.

Section 1. The Jedi Praxium and Dark Praxium shall be separate but affiliated entities. Affiliated shall mean "a group that is closely connected with a larger group".

Section 2. The Dark Praxium shall share the "Praxium" guild tag with the EverQuest Legion of the Jedi Praxium.

Section 3. The Jedi Praxium shall assume the general administration functions of the Dark Praxium. In addition, Dark Praxium members shall be allowed to utilize and access all Jedi Praxium propriety materials, information and services.

Section 4. The members of the Dark Praxium shall conduct themselves in a responsible and mature manner and in keeping with generally accepted customs of good gaming. Inappropriate conduct by a member of the Dark Praxium will be dealt with in keeping with Jedi Codes and customs.

Section 5. The Dark Praxium may recruit members that aren'tcurrently members of the Jedi Praxium. However, any such recruits must go through the normal Jedi Praxium application process. Such recruits must first join the Jedi Praxium as an individual. Only after the individual has been accepted into the Jedi Praxium may that individual have a character tagged by the Dark Praxium. An individual need not be a member of the EverQuest Legion of the Jedi Praxium to be a member of the Dark Praxium.

Section 6. The members and recruits of the Dark Praxium shall be restricted to those races and/or classes generally not allowed in the EverQuest Legion of the Jedi Praxium.

Section 7. The members of the Jedi Praxium may make recommendations about a Dark Praxium applicant based on the actions of the individual, regardless of his or her character's class or race.

Section 8. The Dark Praxium shall operate in accordance with basic democractic principles, just as the Jedi Praxium.

Section 9. The Dark Praxium and its members shall be subject to all applicable Jedi Praxium rules, regulations, and policies.

This agreement was passed by the Assembly of Jedi in March 2001 and amended to allow for shared tagging in September of 2001.
