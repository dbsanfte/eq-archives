EverQuest Epic Application

The EverQuest Epic Application is the important first step in the process. From this application, the Senate gains a great deal of information to aid Senators in determining your spot in the Epic queue. The Senate takes in a multitude of factors so every bit of information is helpful. The Senate meets monthly to update the Epic queue. The Senate is made up of all elected officers of the Jedi Praxium. They are:

Guild Master: Warderick
Guild Senator: Samany
Guild Senator: Grimwood
EQ Senator: Kenobe
EQ Legion Commander: Ailyei
