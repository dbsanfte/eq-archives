T'was a blustery morn as Warderick made his way to the lil shack up ahead. As he stepped into the shack, Wranglir the new Knigths of Neverwinter champion greeted him. Wranglir had just last week snatched the championship belt away from Llewdoc who had held it for months. Wranglir had even defeated Warderick only weeks ago in his rise to the top. But, today things would be different, the old Jedi thought to himself. For today, Warderick would not only have the Force in his corner, but a new cloak to serve him as well.

     Quickly, the wolves and hounds were unleashed and the dual began. Warderick entered the frey first and quickly globed in a corner. Wranglir then entered and immediately began casting on the Jedi. Most of the pack surrounded Wranglir, and as they did, a lil smile broke across the old Jedi's face. After a heated exchange of spells, Warderick slipped a fumble through Wranglir's defenses, and the champion became clumsy. As a big smile broke across Warderick's face, he remembered a fight he had lost to Llewdoc under similar circumstances. So, Warderick kept his mind clear and pressed his advantage. In the end an old champion fell and a new champion was crowned.

     After Wranglir fell, Warderick dispatched what was left of the wolf pack and made his way to Wranglir's side. Warderick healed the fallen knight and entrusted him to the care of his squire. The squire handed over the Knights of Neverwinter championship belt to the old Jedi. And with that Warderick exclaimed, "I claim this belt not only for myself, but for the honor and glory of the Jedi Praxium."
