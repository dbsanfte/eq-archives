Procedure for Special Exceptions

 

Section 1.  The special exception process for members and non-members alike starts with with turning in the normal application.  The applicant must be explicit in the application about the race, class, and god of the character in question.

Section 2.  Once the Guild Master recieves the application, the Guild Master will instruct the applicant and sponsor a petition must be filed on behalf of the applicant. The petition requires 10 Jedi signatures.  The Guild Master will email the Guild and post on the JP Public Board about the application.  The Guild Master will also post a petition on the JP Public Board.

Section 3.  The Guild Master will inform the Legion Commander when the petition requirement is completed. The Legion Commander will give the the applicant a questionaire, which must be filled out and returned. The questionaire asks questions that will aid in the evaluation and decision making process.

Section 4.  Once the application, petition and questionaire are recieved, the Legion Commander will review the applicant by personal interview and the information. The Legion Commander may either reject the applicant or accept the applicant. If the Legion Commander accepts the applicant, then he/she shall forward the matter to the Senate.

Section 5. The Senate shall consider applicants on a case by case base. The applicant and their sponsor must attend the hearing when the application is considered.

Section 6. The Senate shall review each applicant with a presumption against granting the exception unless the applicant meets certain criteria. Such criteria include:

    a) Does the character profess to be good?

    b) Does the character profess to honor Jedi principles?

    c) Does the character have an appropriate God? (NOTE:  Some race and class combinations        could never be a Jedi due to their God.  A Dark Elf cleric must worship the God of Hate so that combination could never be a Jedi.  And a Troll Shaman can only worship the God of Fear so that combination could never be a Jedi.  I believe these are the only two, which are unacceptable.)

    d) Has this person sufficiently RP'ed their characters background and history?

    e) How well is this person and their reputation known among the Jedi? (NOTE: This favors actual members above those who are trying to join initially.  The reason is because it's an undeniable fact that the better we know someone then the more able we will be to make the appropriate decision.)

    f) Will this be the persons main character? (NOTE: A Jedi shouldn't play a "dark race" just because they want to be a baddie race but hang out with their friends. A person should be seriously committed. Remember not every DE is a Drizzt nor should we consider every DE a Drizzt. We only want Drizzts.)

    g) Would allowing the character negatively affect the Guild? (NOTE: The idea is a couple of exceptions won't negatively affect our image and will bring in diversity. However, allowing a whole mess of exceptions will eventually negatively affect our image and at that point the line must be drawn. IN ADDITION, if too many exceptions are granted, then the exception swallows up the rule.)

Section 7. The Senate shall accept an applicant on a majority vote of the entire Senate. If an applicant is rejected, the applicant can re-apply for a special exception after two months.

Section 8. All matters may always be RECONSIDERED by the Guild Assembly or Legion Council. This means if the Senate comes to one decsion then the majority of the Guild or Legion can overturn that decision. (NOTE: RECONSIDERATION is a special JP democractic feature. Actually, no decision has ever been brought up for reconsideration. Regardless, it stands for the proposition that if the majority doesn't like a decision, they can by a vote make the outcome right.
