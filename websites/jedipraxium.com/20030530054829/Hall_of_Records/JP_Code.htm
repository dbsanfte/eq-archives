The Code of the Jedi Praxium

 

Article I.

Authority of the Code

Section 1. The Code of the Jedi Praxium shall govern all affairs of the Jedi Praxium, including but not limited to the affairs of: the Assembly of Jedi; the Jedi Senate; the Council of Jedi Grandmasters; the Legions of the Jedi Praxium; all Legion Commanders; the Guild Master; Officers; and/or the conduct of all Jedi and Jedi Apprentices.

Section 2. Before acceptance into the Jedi Praxium, an individual must declare in public and without reservation that he or she will honor and obey the dictates of this Code and the Charter.

 

Article II.

Organization and Structure of the Jedi Praxium

Section 1. Assembly of Jedi: The Assembly of Jedi (hereinafter "Assembly") shall be the supreme ruling body of the Jedi Praxium (hereinafter "Guild"). The Assembly shall have the authority to take whatever actions it deems necessary for the betterment of the Guild and nothing in this Code shall be deemed to limit the absolute authority of the Assembly.

Section 2. Jedi Senate: In order to foster communications and ensure harmony between the Guild and the various Legions, the Jedi Senate (hereafter "Senate") is established to address those issues of special concern to both the Guild and the Legions. The Senate shall have enumerated powers described below, and in addition, those additional administrative powers necessary to conduct the affairs of the Senate.

Section 3. Guild Master: The Guild Master shall be the chief executive officer of the Guild. The Guild Master shall have enumerated powers described below, and in addition, those additional administrative powers necessary to conduct the affairs of the Guild.

Section 4. Legion of the Jedi Praxium: Where a group of Jedi wish to establish a chapter of the Guild in a distinct gaming forum, a Legion of the Jedi Praxium may be formed. The ratification process and other rules and regulations governing Legions are set out below.

Section 5. Council of Jedi Grandmaster: The Council of Jedi Grandmasters (hereinafter "Council") is the oldest and original executive body of the Guild. Election to the Council is the highest Honor a Jedi can receive. The Council is an advisory body of the Assembly.

 

Article III

Assembly of Jedi

Section 1. All Jedi shall have a seat in the Assembly. Each active Jedi shall have one vote, and inactive Jedi shall have no voting rights.

Section 2. An active Jedi may vote on a matter in absentia or by proxy. In either event, the Guild Master must be informed in writing by the Jedi in advance.

Section 3. Assembly meetings shall be open to all Jedi.

Section 4. The Assembly shall meet at least the first and third Sunday of every month and notice shall be given at least 24 hours in advance.

Section 5. The Assembly shall be conducted in keeping with parliamentary procedures.

Section 6. The Guild Master shall preside over the Assembly. In his or her absence the senior Assistant Guild Master shall preside over the Assembly.

Section 7. The Assembly shall take official action on a majority vote of the entire active membership. In the case of a tie vote, the Guild Master shall have the tie-breaking vote.

Section 8. All official actions shall be published.

Section 9. The Assembly may take unofficial action on a majority vote of the Jedi present at a meeting. Unofficial actions shall have no binding effect unless it is made official at latter time by an official action of the Assembly except as provided in Subsection A & B below.

Subsection A. The Assembly by an unofficial action may accept new members.

Subsection B. The Assembly by unofficial action may adopt treaties if the same treaty is later adopted by an official action of the Senate.

Section 12. The Assembly shall elect two persons from its membership to serve on the Jedi Senate. The Assembly may only remove a Senator from office upon a two-thirds vote of the entire Assembly. Senatorial elections will be held every 6 months.

Section 13. The Assembly shall elect a Guild Master by a majority vote of the entire Assembly. The Guild Master may only be removed from office upon a two-thirds vote of the entire Assembly. Guild Master elections will be held every 6 months.

Section 14. The Assembly may reconsider any action taken by the Guild Master, Senate, any Legion, any Legion Commander, or any Officer. For an action to be reconsidered, five Jedi must inform the Guild Master in writing that they want the matter reconsidered. The Guild Master shall then forward the request for reconsideration to the entire Guild. If a majority of all active Jedi vote in favor of reconsideration, the matter will be shall be reconsidered and the action in question shall be stayed.

 

Article IV

Jedi Senate

Section 1. The Jedi Senate shall be comprised of the following persons: the Guild Master; the Legion Commanders for the various Legions; the Legion Senator of each Ratified Legion; and the two Senators elected from the Guild as a whole.

Section 2. Each member of the Senate shall have one vote except a Legion Commander of a Chartered Legion shall have a seat but no voting rights.

Section 3. The Senate shall be convened only when a majority of its members are present.

Section 4. The chairmanship of the Senate shall rotate among its voting members.

Section 5. Prior to a meeting, the Senate shall give at least 24 hours notice to the membership of the Guild by email or post.

Section 6. The Senate shall be conducted in keeping with parliamentary procedures.

Section 7. Senate meetings shall be open to all unless otherwise specified and only if the circumstances so dictate a closed meeting.

Section 8. The Senate shall take official action upon a majority vote of the entire membership. The Senate may only take official actions and may take such action only in an open meeting.

Section 9. The Senate shall oversee Legions and the Legion ratification process.

Section 10. The Senate shall oversee the awarding of ranks to ensure the integrity of the ranking system.

Section 11. The Senate shall review all disciplinary matters forwarded to it.  The Senate may formally hear and adjudicate the matter or the Senate may forward the matter to the Assembly.

Section 12. The Senate by official action may adopt a treaty only if the Assembly has previously voted to adopt the same treaty by an unofficial action. Such an action by the Senate shall make the treaty binding.

Section 13. Any member of the Senate missing three consecutive meetings or three of the last five meetings shall lose his or her voting rights. Voting rights will only be reinstated when the member again attends meetings regularly. The Guild Master shall keep track of all absences and report it to the Guild. The Guild Master shall inform the Guild when a member of the Senate loses or regains his or her voting rights.

 

Article V

Guild Master

Section 1. The Guild Master shall maintain a membership roster. This roster shall at least list the active and inactive Jedi; Jedi Grandmasters; Jedi Masters; Officers of the Guild; and Legion Officers.

Section 2. The Guild Master shall oversee the Jedi Praxium Web Site.

Section 3. The Guild Master shall oversee the publication of the Praxium Post.

Section 4. The Guild Master shall ensure that the Code, Charter, any rules or regulations, and all practices of the Jedi Praxium are recognized and respect in the various Legions.

Section 5. The Guild Master may appoint such Officers, with the advice and consent of the Senate, as he or she deems to be necessary and desirable. These Officers serve at the will of the Guild Master.

Section 6. The Guild Master may establish such committees as deemed to be necessary and desirable. The purposes and terms of such committees and their members shall be specified at the time of their establishment.

Section 7. The Guild Master shall informally investigate all disciplinary matters. In doing so, he or she shall act in a fair and equitable manner. If for some reason the matter cannot informally be resolved, Guild Master shall forward the matter to the Senate.

Section 8. Upon a showing of just cause, the Guild Master may temporarily suspend a Jedi until the matter can be considered by the Assembly at the next regular meeting. However, two Officers of the Guild must witness, either in person or in writing, the Guild Master's execution of power.

Section 9. In cases of emergencies, the Guild Master may take any necessary actions for the good of the Guild. However, the matter must be immediately reported to the Guild and if necessary voted on by the Assembly.

Section 10. Where the Guild Master is temporarily unable to perform his or her duties, the Assistant Guild Master(s) shall carry out those duties.

Section 11. Where the Guild Master has resigned or is permanently unable to perform his or her duties, the Assistant Guild Master(s) will temporarily discharge those duties while the process to nominate and elect a new Guild Master is under taken.

 

Article VI

Legions of the Jedi Praxium

Section 1. All Legions shall honor the supremacy of this Code, the Charter, and the Assembly.

Section 2. A Legion may be established in any distinct gaming forum where Jedi are active.

Section 3. Each Legion must go through the three step ratification process to ensure the stability and integrity of each Legion of the Jedi Praxium.

Section 4. A group of five or more Jedi may petition the Senate to Charter a Legion.

Subsection A. The petition must include: the name and description of the gaming forum; a Legion Charter; a list of the active Jedi wanting to form the Legion; a basic Legion web site, and the name of a person to contact person for more information about the Legion.

Subsection B. The petition must be emailed to the Guild Master who will then forward it to the members of the Senate and inform the Guild where a copy of the petition can be seen.

Section 5. Before a Legion is granted a Charter by the Senate, it must hold a Caucus where the Legion Commander is chosen. The Guild shall receive advance notice of the time and day of the Caucus.

Subsection A. Only those Jedi who are active or are certain they will soon be active in the distinct gaming forum where the Legion is to be formed shall be permitted to vote in the Caucus. A Jedi may appoint a proxy or vote in absentia if he or she cannot attend a Caucus.

Subsection B. The Guild Master or Assistant Guild Master shall chair the Caucus to ensure the fairness and integrity of the proceeding. The chair shall have no voting rights and should conduct the proceedings in an unbiased and neutral manner.

Section 6. The Senate will grant a Charter to a Legion upon a showing that: (1) the Legion will be viable in a distinct gaming forum; (2) a Legion Commander was properly chosen; and (3) the petition is satisfactory.

Section 7. A Chartered Legion may petition the Senate for Certification when it has (1) ten or more active Jedi and (2) a Charter at least two weeks.

Subsection A. The petition must include: the name and description of the gaming forum; the Legion Charter; a list of the active Jedi in the Legion; an enhanced and updated Legion web site; names and positions of the Legion's Officers; a brief description on how the Legion awards ranks and membership; a brief description of the Legion's recruiting practices; information on the regularity of meetings; and an outline of proposed bylaws.

Subsection B. The petition must be emailed to the Guild Master who will then forward it to the members of the Senate and inform the Guild where a copy of the petition can be seen.

Section 8. The Senate will Certify a Legion upon a showing that: (1) the Legion has a strong and stable membership core; (2) the Legion is developing an organizational framework; and (3) the petition is satisfactory.

Section 9. A Certified Legion may petition the Senate for Ratification when the Legion has (1) twenty or more 20 active Jedi and (2) a Charter at least 6 weeks old.

Subsection A. The petition must at least include: the name and description of the gaming forum; the Legion Charter; a list of the active Jedi in the Legion; the Legion web site; names and positions of the Legion's Officers; a brief description on how the Legion awards ranks and membership; a brief description of the Legion's recruiting practices; information on the regularity of meetings; a draft of the bylaws the Legion intends to adopt; and a brief explanation of the voting process

Subsection B. The petition must be emailed to the Guild Master who will then forward it to the members of the Senate and inform the Guild.

Subsection C. Legions seeking ratification will be monitored for two weeks during this period of development.

Section 10. The Senate will Ratify a Legion up a showing that: (1) the Legion has a developed a stable and reliable membership; (2) the organizational structure of the Legion is significantly developed; (3) the Legion adopted bylaws; (4) the Legion elected a Legion Commander; (5) the Legion elected a representative to the Senate; and (5) the petition is satisfactory.

Section 11. Any Jedi active in a gaming forum where a Legion exists shall be promptly granted membership if he or she has requested it from the Legion Commander.

Section 12. An Officer in one Legion may not be an Officer in another Legion or the Guild. However, where efficiency dictates, Legions and/or the Guild may share Officers.

Section 13. If a Legion Commander appoints an Officer, the Legion Commander must seek the advice and consent of the Senate.

Section 14. The Legion Commander must supply a Legion membership roster to the Guild Master on the 1st and 15th of each month in the format requested.

 

Article VII

Council of Jedi Grandmasters

Section 1. The Assembly shall elect persons to sit on the Council.

Section 2. Each member of the Council shall have the title of Jedi Grandmaster. And those who became Grandmasters under a previous system shall retain their title.

Section 3. Any Jedi Grandmaster missing two consecutive meetings or three of the last five meetings shall be considered inactive and lose his or her voting rights in Council matters. The Guild Master shall reinstate the Grandmaster's voting rights when the Grandmaster again attends meetings regularly.

Section 4. Once the title of "Jedi Grandmaster" has been granted, it shall remain as a Jedi's Surname in honor of his or her service to the Guild.

Section 5. Each active Jedi Grandmaster shall have one vote. Inactive Jedi Grandmaster shall have a seat on the Council but no voting rights.

Section 6. Prior to a meeting, the Council shall give at least 24 hours notice to the membership of the Guild by email or post.

Section 7. The Council shall be convened only when a majority of its active members are present.

Section 8. The Council shall be conducted in keeping with parliamentary procedures.

Section 9. The Council shall take official action upon a majority vote of the entire active membership of the Council. The Council may only take official action.

Section 10. Any official actions of the Council shall be advisory in nature unless later adopted by an official action of the Assembly.

 

Article VIII

Duties of Jedi

Section 1. All Jedi shall uphold this Code, the Charter, and all generally accepted practices and customs of the Guild.

Section 2. All Jedi shall act in a manner befitting the honor and dignity of this Guild and the Jedi tradition. A Jedi shall not be rude, boastful, mean spirited, or bad tempered.

Section 3. All Jedi shall fill out and update their Jedi Membership Form.

Section 4. A person who has a Jedi character may have characters in other guilds.

Section 5. A person shall never use his or her Jedi or non-Jedi characters to cause SERIOUS HARM to another Jedi or the Guild.

Section 6. Each Jedi shall be allowed one Jedi character screen name that doesn't use the "JP" prefix. However, all other Jedi character screen names for a person must use the "JP" prefix.

Subsection A. The Senate may waive this requirement where it would act as a hardship on an individual.

 

Article IX

Ranks & Titles

Section 1. The Senate shall oversee the awarding of ranks to ensure the integrity of the ranking system.

Section 2. The Jedi Praxium shall have the following ranking system:

Jedi Initiate

Jedi Warrior

Jedi Knight

Jedi Grand Knight

Jedi Master

Section 3. A Legion may opt not to use the ranking system; however, a Legion may not use another ranking system in its place except as described in Subsection A below.

Subsection A. The Senate may grant a waiver to a Legion to vary the ranking system, however, in no case shall the ranks of Jedi Knight or higher be varied.

Section 4. The Senate may veto the awarding of any rank to ensure the integrity of the ranking system.

Section 5. Jedi Grandmaster is both a rank and a title, and it is bestowed upon a Jedi as previously set forth in this Code.

Section 6. The Guild Master and Legion Commanders may bestow titles upon their Officers to reflect their positions.

Section 7. The Assembly, Senate or Guild Master may bestow special titles upon Jedi for great deeds or service to the guild.

 

Article X

Jedi Apprentices

Section 1. A Jedi may sponsor up to three persons at one time for membership. An Officer may sponsor more than three persons temporarily until a permanent sponsor can be found.

Section 2. All persons who have turned in an application shall be known as a Jedi Apprentice.

Section 3. Before a Jedi Apprentice is considered, he or she needs a sponsor and three recommendations. One of the recommendations must be from an Officer.

Section 4. A Jedi Apprentice's application will be considered and voted on by Guild or a Legion which will be determined by how the Jedi Apprentice applies for membership. A Jedi Apprentice can apply for membership directly to the Guild or through one of the Legions.

Section 5. A Jedi Apprentice who is granted membership by one of the Legions also becomes a member of the Guild.

Section 6. The Senate may waive portions of this Article where it would create a hardship for a Legion due to its small membership or lack of Officers.

 

Article XI

Amendments

Section 1. The Assembly may amend the Code, the Charter or other governing rules or regulations by a majority vote of the Assembly.

Section 2. Any active Jedi may sponsor an amendment. Amendments shall be offered initially in a rough draft form, and the final draft must be in keeping with the intent of the sponsor(s).

Section 3. During scheduled Guild elections, five Jedi must petition the Guild Master in order for an amendment to be placed on the ballot.

Section 4. At any time other than scheduled Guild elections, the Senate shall determine the number of petitioners necessary to bring about a Guild vote on an amendment. In making its determination as to the number of required petitioners, the Senate shall consider the imperative, necessity, timeliness, and administrative burden of both the amendment and vote. The Senate may also consider other criteria if needed.

Subsection A. At any time other than scheduled Guild elections where a recall vote is sought for on an amendment that had previously been voted upon, the Senate shall determine the number of petitioners necessary to bring about a Guild vote on the amendment. In making its determination as to the number of required petitioners, the Senate's overriding consideration shall be how many petitioners are necessary to demonstrate a high probability the petition will pass, if a recall vote occurs.

 

Article XII

Referenda

Section 1. Referenda are issues put to the electorate that are not amendments to the Code, the Charter or other governing rules or regulations. The Assembly shall adopt a referendum by a majority vote.

Section 2. Any active Jedi may sponsor a referendum. Referenda shall be offered initially in a rough draft form, and the final draft must be in keeping with the intent of the sponsor(s).

Section 3. During scheduled Guild elections, five Jedi must petition the Guild Master in order for a referendum to be placed on the ballot.

Section 4. At any time other than scheduled Guild elections, the Senate shall determine the number of petitioners necessary to bring about a Guild vote on a referendum. In making its determination as to the number of required petitioners, the Senate shall consider the imperative, necessity, timeliness, and administrative burden of both the referendum and vote. The Senate may also consider other criteria if needed.

Subsection A. At any time other than scheduled Guild elections where a recall vote is sought for on a referendum that had previously been voted upon, the Senate shall determine the number of petitioners necessary to bring about a Guild vote on the referendum. In making its determination as to the number of required petitioners, the Senate's overriding consideration shall be how many petitioners are necessary to demonstrate a high probability the petition will pass, if a recall vote occurs.
