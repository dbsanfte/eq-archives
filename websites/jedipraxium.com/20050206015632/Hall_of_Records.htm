Hall of Records
Entering the Hall of Records, you are astounded by the limitless number of library stacks filled with scrolls, parchments, books, and writings of all kinds.   However, the stacks area is roped off and two Iron Golems stand watch.  One Golem has a sign around its neck stating, "Those without a valid library card will not be admitted to the Main Library by order of Ryym, the Jedi Librarian."  To your right is a large oaken door, and the plaque on the door says, "Office of the Jedi Scribe - Ryym.  No Admittance!!!" However, to your left you find there is a small comfortable study which must be the Public Reading Room that Warderick mentioned. There is a fire going and a table and chair to read at.  Above the fireplace is a painting of the puritanical library autocrat, Ryym. Which guild document are you interested in reading?
