JP Litning finds himself once again teleported out of Neverwinter. A moment of desperation finds him stomping his feet and swearing, before he catches himself and calms down. "One must never let himself do anything in anger," he thinks to himself, "for it can only lead to the dark side." Continuing this silent contemplation, "I absolutely must find out why I can not stay within Neverwinter and my Guild hall. There must be an answer somewhere." Thinking that perhaps the library might contain an answer, Litning decides he should start there. After all, Ryym has been doing a good job of gathering tomes on all sorts of interesting things, and not all of them having to do with the Jedi. OK, first to teleport back to the guild hall... A flash of light, followed momentarily later by the roll of thunder and he is gone.

     Litning materializes with a :::CRACK::: outside of the library, where he is confronted by a large Iron Golem standing by the door with hand outstretched, the sign around his massive neck reading "CARD PLEASE."

     "CARD?!?" he says, "I DON'T HAVE A CARD!!!!" Remembering that Ryym has proclaimed the library off-limits to all without their library card, Litning moves to knock on the door and take care of the matter when the large hand of the golem reaches out for him. Just as the construct is about to grab Litning, there is a crackle, and the golem is slowed, giving Litning time enough to roll away from the creature. The words to a spell come hastily to his lips, and time moves twice as fast for the Jedi. Then the golem starts moving after Litning, while Litning starts moving away from the golem. A slightly ludicrous chase ensues, with the both of them circling the room, golem chasing Jedi. Litning thinks to himself "This is Ridiculous!" "RYYM!!!! RYYM!!!!!" he cries, "GET OUT HERE AND CALL OFF YOUR TOY!!!!! AND BRING ME A LIBRARY CARD!!!!! I HAVE TO DO SOME RESEARCH!!!!! IF YOU DON'T GET OUT HERE, I'M GONNA DEMOLISH THIS GOLEM!!!!!!"

     Hearing this, Ryym decides to answer this one call from outside of the library. He moves across the hall, passing rows and rows of articulately placed tomes, maps, and scrolls to get to the door. He swings open the door to see what is the matter just as Litning is passing by it, and another loud CRACK resounds across both rooms as JP Litning is stopped dead in his tracks, and slowly topples backwards to the floor, his haste spell expiring. Litning is out cold.

     Sometime later, Litning awakes to find himself sitting propped up in a chair with a glass of ale set in front of him. Momentarily disoriented, and feeling like someone or something has been pounding on his head with a war-hammer, the Master Jedi looks around, as memory awakens. I'M IN THE LIBRARY he thinks to himself. Then he mutters, "Where's Ryym?" At that moment said librarian comes around the corner bearing a largish tome and hands it to Litning. "Ward brought this to me," he says, "and he said it may be of use to you." "Thank you Ryym. I hope this is just what I'm looking for in my search to find the reason why I am unable to stay here within these realms. I have spoken to Ward about this before, so maybe the answer lies here." With that, Ryym returns to his studies, and his own ale... :)

     Litning looks over the large book beside him, and does not recognize the language it is in. Ah, well, just a little spell to help me, he thinks. So he then casts a Comprehend Languages spell, and the writing flows into legible script for him. A surprised murmur escapes his lips, "What a Strange title!!", as he realizes what it says... America On-Line Technical Hints and Problem Solvers. After many hours of reading, and becoming totally confused with the strange terms within, such as Files, Keywords, Mailboxes, and such, Litning finally comes to an area that he recognizes. It reads, "When entering into the Neverwinter Nights game", and Litning thinks "GAME?!?", "you must not have your Buddy box open. If running Windows 95 click on the X in the upper right hand corner of the Buddy window to close..." and so on. "If you do not close your Buddy window, you will be forced out of the game by packet reflection, and surely it will happen in a timely and often manner."

     JP Litning reflects upon what he has learned. All of these things such as Windows, Buddy boxes, packet reflection, and so on and so on... so many strange things. He realizes finally that these words are written in Ward's strange handwriting, so he must understand what these are, so... Litning reaches out with the force intent on "Closing his Buddy box" whatever that is, and feels a small rounded item fit into the palm of his hand with two buttons on it that click. Thinking to himself that this is truly a strange way to use the force, he tries the first button, and hears a click, and a voice says "You have learned something new here today, Jedi. Always trust in yourself, and trust in me, my son." "FATHER!!!!!" cries the Jedi, realizing that his father, The Force itself, has spoken to him directly this time, and not just with the ways of The Force. "Return to the Realms my son, for there are disturbances beyond your imagining, and they must needs be stopped. The other Jedi know of these already, and are taking measures to bring justice to the realms. Remember this, and do not lose Faith. Your mother, Nature, and I are watching over you and the other Jedi, Son. I must go now."

      With that the presence recedes. Litning is struck by the implications of what his father has said, so he quickly returns this book to Ryym, and asks about the current dealings of the Jedi since he has been away. Ryym thrusts several scrolls, messages, maps, and small tomes at Litning, and says "Here ye go. That's all of it. Oh, and take, this, ye need it" and thrusts a small tablet into his hands. "To enter the library from now on." With that, Litning retires to a corner of the room to read all of what has been happening of late, to determine how best to help out....."
