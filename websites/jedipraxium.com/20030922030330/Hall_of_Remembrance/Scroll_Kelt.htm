From thru the snow-covered trees emerges a warrior. His deep-set ice-blue eyes proclaim him a Northman. The catlike grace with which he moves despite his exceptional size, coupled with the large well used sword resting familiarly at his side lend him an air of the silent, cold, deadliness one might feel when gazing into the eyes of a reptile.

     Passing silently through the trees, like some extra-planar predator, he cuts quite the imposing figure. As he passes by you, grunting in your direction (what you take to be more of a sign of annoyance at your presence than a greeting), you notice writings fashioned into the dark hand-tooled leather that adorns his scabbard, "JEDI PRAXIUM" they read. It is then that you notice, your eyes growing wide in realization, the carvings in a fine stylus that adorn the cross-piece of his sword: the square-cut letters that so boldly read "KELT".

     You fall over yourself, taking rushed backward steps, "It is true!" , you exclaim to yourself in astonishment. "The legends are true… he really does exist!" Suddenly you remember a few of the campfire tales that you've heard concerning your dark visitor over many a cold, black evening. Tales, they were, that bespoke his solitary nature, and his heavy-handed intolerance for its violators. Fearing his wrath, you quickly look toward him, barely hearing his steps breaking thru the thick, snowy crust covering the earth from behind one of the surrounding trees up ahead. As you vaguely make out the outline of his giant form disappearing into the snow-covered trees ahead, you realize that you've been holding your breath all the while. You exhale slowly, unconsciously trying to make as little sound as possible.

     You sheepishly catch yourself in what you consider to be a moment of foolishness, and start to move away. -SNAP- You freeze in place at the sound of a broken twig. You feel an icy finger slowly trace its way up your spine as you imagine your grim visitor returning to repay you for some perceived intrusion. Your gorge rises, your heart races. Not able to resist the cowardly urge screaming within you, you bolt head-over-heels into the surrounding wood, screaming like a babe. Later, as you mop the sweat off of your brow, chest heaving from exertion, you gather your children close to you. Holding them tight against your breast, you mutter a prayer, thankful to be alive. Off in the distant snow-filled twilight, a lone wolf howls.
