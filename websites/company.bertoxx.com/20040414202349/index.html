2/13/2004 7:16:56 AM Rallos Zek The Warlord Jarrington Avendaar

    Rallos Zek the Warlord

    Standing on the battleground of the arena, the stench of death and decay filling the room, The Company waited. The journey to this place and the difficult task ahead weighed heavily on each person there. At length, the rally was called and the stench of old battles faded as the adrenaline took over. Out of the looming darkness rose a figure so forbidding as to give us pause and wonder just what insanity had overcome us to believe that we could actually pit ourselves against a god such as Rallos Zek!
    The Warlord, master of the Plane of Tactics called forth his minions even as The Company's battle cries rang out amidst the clash of steel on steel. The battle was pitched, and before long, through the dull red glaring eyes of ultimate evil, we saw desperation.
    Rallos Zek called forth even more to his aid, but even as his cries grew louder, we grew stronger. Finally, in a swirl of magical elements and the wrenching sounds of steel upon bone, The Warlord succumbed to his wounds at the hands of The Company.

    We have entered the Elemental Planes.

  • Black Collar of Boundless Might - Blyndem

1/14/2004 6:11:59 PM Changes Jarrington Avendaar
On January 23rd, 2004 The Company will be changing its recruiting and sponsorship guidelines. As a family guild, it is important to maintain a balance between new and old players in order to promote the healthy, friendly atmosphere we all enjoy. We are firmly opposed to imposing level, class and flag restrictions on new recruits, but the new guidelines are designed to stem the flow nonetheless and will be detailed in the Recruiting Policy on the 23rd. Potential recruits should be forewarned that The Company is in the process of backflagging members to enter the Tower of Solusek Ro this month and possibly into the next. Once this period has passed, we will be making no planned efforts to continue backflagging those entry requirements, which may make it increasingly difficult to raid with us. This is not to say that we will never be revisiting these backflag targets in the future, merely that our focus will be changing and these targets will become ones of secondary or tertiary importance.

As The Company nears its 5-year anniversary on the Bertoxxulous server, we look forward to continued successes in the game, under the guild tag in which we proudly represent.
