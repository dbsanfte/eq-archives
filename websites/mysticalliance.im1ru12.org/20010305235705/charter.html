    Guild Charter

    The Mystic Alliance is a multi-server guild founded by Lesbian, Gay, Bisexual, Transgendered, and our Non-Gay friends and allies to provide an atmosphere where our members can enjoy the world of Norrath free from anti-gay harassment. Other benefits of our guild include: stronger bonds with other players, using our members' trade skills and quests to help equip members of our guild, participating in guild or multi-guild events, and most importantly, having fun. The mystical force that brought us together, and which gives our guild its name is stronger than the traditional race and class values that often separate the peoples of Norrath. Therefore, we welcome all the races and classes of Norrath into our fold. While our guild has a presence across multiple servers our strongest force is on the Rodcet Nife server.
