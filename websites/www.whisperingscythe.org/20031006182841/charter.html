Mission/Purpose
The Whispering Scythe is a role-playing guild on the Morell Thule server. The Whispering Scythe was formed to provide its members a means to give aid to fellow guildmates and all good aligned races on Norrath. We emphasize fair play, loyalty and having fun.

Summary
We are not a guild of "power gamers". While we perhaps envy those that have enough time to make 2-3 levels per day, our real life families and jobs take precedence. A number of members play very sparingly while others play more frequently. We generally plan guild raids for Friday nights starting at approximately 9 PM EST. We are a member guild of the Gathering of Banners, which is a roleplaying guild alliance created to form a healthy role-playing atmosphere for those travelling the lands of Norrath. It is comprised of multiple RP Guilds who strive to make Norrath a better place to travel and live in. It shall facilitate the organizations of larger scale raids, acceptance on the Rotation as well as become community for all guild members to meet and offer aid to all. Should you need aid in obtaining an item or finishing a quest, simply call upon those of the GoB for aid, and we shall strive to be there. At the heart of our fellowship are the core virtues of honesty, integrity, courage and companionship.

We want to emphasize that we believe in fair play and having fun! This guild was started by a bunch of friends that work together. We enjoy the excitement of the game and the few hours of mindless distraction it provides. If this sounds like the type of group you want to belong to, then contact us in game on the Morell-Thule server. We would be happy to discuss our charter with you further.

Guild Structure
The Whispering Scythe Guild Structure is simple:

  • Guild Leader - This is the position held or delegated by the founder of the guild. There can be only one Guild Leader. Responsible for the overall leadership of the guild. Given Veto power in the Guild Council.
  • Officer - Guild officers will be appointed by the Guild Leader and will hold a position of power, leadership, and responsibility over others in the guild. Allowed one vote in the Guild Council. There can be many Officers. Please note: This charter mentions nothing about "respect" for officers!
  • Member - All accepted members that are in good standing with the guild are considered Members. The majority of people in the guild will be Members.
Note: This is just a general outline of the Guild Structure. Within this structure there can also be special titles given to anyone at any level of the guild structure. For example, there can be someone named (at any tier) as Raid Coordinator (responsible for the coordination and execution of a raid), Chief Brew Master (responsible for ensuring in-game beverages are provided), or Master of Confusion and Corpse Recovery - essentially everyone.

Guild Council
The Guild Council is made up of the following:

  • Council Leader - This is usually the Guild Leader but can also be someone appointed by the Guild Leader. Council Leader has the power to approve or veto any resolution made by Council Members. For any issue to be enacted it must be approved by the Council Leader.
  • Council Members - Every Officer and Class Leader in the guild are considered Council Members and are given one vote.
The Guild Council, aside from voting on issues, shall set the direction of the guild itself; e.g., planning out strategies for further growth and making decisions on issues at hand. There are no mandatory meetings. On occasion we will ask for everyone to be present for a dungeon raid, but we have neither the time nor energy to coordinate monthly guild meetings.

Article 1: New Members
For any new member to be considered by the guild he/she must have a Sponsor. A Sponsor can be anyone in the guild that is in good standing. It is the responsibility of the Sponsor to acclimate the new member on the ways of the guild.

The following rules apply to all new members:

  1. Anyone seeking membership must be at least Level 12. Note: The level 12 restrictions are here for two reasons. First, it ensures that a person has invested some time in character growth and development. Second, at level 12 every character should have managed to attain basic armor, spells, and weaponry. The guild maintains an armory and treasury to help others out but it is not capable of fully "twinking" multiple individuals.
  2. They must have at least one good reference from a Member in good standing with the guild (this person will usually be the Sponsor) and one character reference from someone outside the guild.
  3. They must also receive a copy of the guild charter and agree to all the rules and code of conduct contained herein before receiving an invite from an Officer.
Essentially, all players, regardless of race or class, are welcome to join the Whispering Scythe. Please remember it is our intent to have a guild that promotes fun, fair play, and camaraderie. If a member of the guild knowingly acts in a way that reflects poorly on the guild (and the charge can be proved) they will be dismissed. Remember, this is only a game - but be nice, help others, and have fun!

Article 2: Guild Rules - Code of Conduct
The following are a list of rules and Code of Conduct that should be followed by all Members of the Whispering Scythe.

  • All Members are expected to treat their fellow guild members with respect and dignity at all times. No harassing, belittling, or other derogatory behavior will be allowed.
  • All Members should also have the same civility toward other non-guild members.
  • Kill Stealing is not permitted.
  • Cooperation between everyone in the guild is expected. We should all try and help our fellow guild members whenever and wherever we can. This includes obtaining needed items or help in finishing a quest. We are all only as strong as our weakest link. We want every member in the guild to be properly outfitted in the best gear possible.
  • If a Guild Raid has been planned and is scheduled and you have a character of the appropriate level, we hope you can participate (barring any Real Life obligations, of course).
  • Grouping with fellow Members is always encouraged (Soloing is fine from time to time but an effort should be made to team up with fellow guild members). If a group you are in needs a replacement you should always ask someone in the guild if they care to join your group as that replacement.
  • Begging for free items or platinum is frowned upon (but if it works, let us know which idiot gave it to you so we can ask as well!). If you need assistance in obtaining class armor or a class item that you desire put in a request for a possible raid to help you obtain these items.
  • Anyone can plan a raid - if you need help in the execution of a raid, please ask.

Article 3: Guild Raids

  • Loot is lottoed by Class.
  • If an item is dropped and any representative of the class(es) that can use the item has better items in their possession then they shall not participate in that lotto.
  • If an item drops and ALL of the representatives of the class(es) the item is usable by have better items, then the dropped item will be placed into the Guild Armory.
  • Lottos will take place immediately if the item is NO DROP; otherwise it will take place:
    1. During a downtime during the raid.
    2. After the raid has been completed.
