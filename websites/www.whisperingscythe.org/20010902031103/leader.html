Thanks for stopping by the Whispering Scythe web site! I am Menludarja, leader of the Whispering Scythe. To give you a little background on the Scythe, our guild promotes the following principles:

  • Fun - We realize EverQuest is a game and played for the enjoyment of all. We always seek to help each guild member have the most fun possible while playing. To increase the fun of playing we always help everyone recover his or her corpse quickly and efficiently after getting everyone killed.
  • Friendship - Besides being guild mates, we are also all good friends. We seek to aid each other at all times. Although we have a more formal raid on Fridays, we often get together during the week to go on quests or just group and spread a little chaotic joy around the countryside.
  • Goodwill - We look to help others on Norrath whenever feasible. We are always interested in helping all the good citizens of Norrath, as well as our fellow guild mates.
  • Bizarre Guild Events - We like to get together and go on corpse recoveries, as opposed to dungeon raids. In truth, we have a standard raid every Friday night. We certainly welcome any people who want to attend. The raid site and time will be posted on our public message board. Simply drop us a note to let us know you would like to come.

    • If these principles make sense to you, send me an e-mail! You're probably strange enough to fit in with the guild.
