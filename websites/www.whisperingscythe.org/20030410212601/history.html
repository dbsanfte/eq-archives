In the beginning, there was the Whispering Scythe. Well, actually, that's not true. The Scythe wasn't around for several more weeks after that. The Scythe has come a long way from those early days, when it was formed to provide a hearty group of adventurers with a means to band together -- actually, that's not true either. It was formed to provide its founding members with an easy means to chat across zones without having to group up. A humble beginning aside, the guild has grown rapidly since.

The core value of the Scythe remains today the same as in the beginning: We are all friends who enjoy playing the game with friends. As the Scythe has grown, we have made friends from all over the country. Currently the Whispering Scythe has members from California to Montreal and many cities in between. To help us get together regularly, we have a standing raid time of Friday nights from 9:00 Eastern time to whenever the last Scythe member finishes his corpse recovery, falls asleep on the keyboard, or gets booted off by his wife (or her husband as the case may be). If the idea of playing EQ with a group of friends on a regular basis appeals to you, then you should drop us a line on our message boards and show up for one of our raids.

Our Friday night raids range from simple hunting parties to quest completing events. Each week, usually no later than Wednesday, the Guild Message of the Day will let everyone know where we are raiding and where to meet. During the week we open up a discussion on the message boards to determine the next objective. Often members will ask for assistance on completing specific quests. Always, the Scythe members have responded by enthusiastically helping their guild mates.

Personally, I look forward to our Friday night raids as the highlight of my EQ week. The dungeons we enter provide excitement, risk, and rewards beyond what any of us could accomplish on our own.

So take a look at our Best of the Scythe page to see pictures of our recent raids, and give us a tell online or drop us a note on our public message board. We look forward to hearing from you and always look to have new friends join us in the Whispering Scythe.
