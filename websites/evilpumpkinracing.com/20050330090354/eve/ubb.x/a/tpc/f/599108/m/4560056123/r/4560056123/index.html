Saturday morning, I leave the house and its raining... nice way to start the first race of the season. I have rain tires, but I'd prefer to race in the dry if given the choice. Friday, was a track day and about a third of the guys were there... it was sunny and dry all day long, so they're all ready to go.

A dry line starts to develop around 10:30, but I decide to sit it out until it looks better. No need to throw it down the track during practice. By 1:00 its fully dry and I go out for the first practice session and do 7 laps then pull in. I always get arm pump on the first session. Its really just to try to get my head back in the game and try to loosen up.

The second session comes and I got out, feeling pretty good, the only person that passes me in the 20 minutes is Kane Lasky and he seems to be on fire. Its only my second session and I'm not going to push it yet.

I pull in and put on new tires, expecting to get one more session to scrub them in for tomorrow. No dice, they call the day early to allow everyone time for the awards banquet that night. No problem, we have one practice on Sunday to scrub the tires as long as it doesn't rain...

It pours as I'm on the way to the banquet and I have that nasty empty feeling in my stomach for tomorrow.

Sunday comes and its there's a steady light rain all the way to the track... ugh, this is gonna suck donkey d*ck.

First and only practice session comes and they CCS declares it a wet race, almost everyone goes out on full rains... I've got my brand new DOT's on and decide to wait it out. My first race isn't until about noon, so I'll wait and swap to rains if it keeps up.

By 10 am, the sun is out, but the storm clouds are teasing us and it seems to be swirling. I can hear the race gods laughing at me in my head... whatcha gonna do now ? huh ?

I wait some more, the track is drying faster, but there are still some puddles. They call my first race, middleweight superbike... oh well we'll give it a go on the DOTs but if it starts to rain, I'm just going to pull in. Michelin DOT's are like skates in the wet. Hmmmm now I get to scrub in my brandy new tires during the race... I can hear the voices laughing at me again...

The first race is a huge grid. We haven't seen grids this big in years. There are 30 experts and about 25 amateurs and alot of new faces. The flag drops and I get off the line nice and easy like a volkswagon rabbit.... diesel (the non-turbo version). I hit the brakes for the first time and the front starts to slide as I'm dipping it in... ahhhhhhhhh, not here, not now... the laughter in my head is getting louder... I stand it up and run a little wide and get out of T2 in about 9th... the first year experts and coming around me, and it takes everything in me not to wick it up, when I know the tires aren't ready yet... coming out of T3, a guy shows me a wheel, I get on the gas and go sideways... nope not yet, and he goes by.... down to 10th or so...

I hold this position for one full lap then slowly start picking up the pace... I pick off one guy on the brakes into T1, then another going into T3... one more in the fast left on the outside T4... back up to 7th now... we start to get into heavy traffic around lap 5 as I catch the guy in 6th. There is a fairly quick left/right transition and a very shitty place to make a pass, but at this point I need to do something fairly stupid if I'm going to catch back up... I catch 6th going into the transition, he's held up by a lapper and looks like he's going to pass him on the inside of the left to get the drive coming out of the corner on the right... I screw up his plan by taking to the gator strip on the inside of the left and passing them both... inside my helmet the voices are going "Jesus, wtf was that?"... I'm wondering myself...

Last lap and I'm gaining pretty fast on the guys in 4th and 5th who are going back and forth pretty hard.... T3, the guy in 5th loses the front and slides right thru the corner... I have to get off the gas to keep from running him over. Its not real close but enough to screw up my drive to catch 4th... over the line in 5th... oh well, I stayed up.

2nd race is Formula 40. Rick Stratton gets a monster start and I tuck in behind him in to T1, right on his rear tire by T3, then I pass him on the outside of the fast left, screwing up my line, but I'm hoping to make myself wide enough for him not to come back around towards the left/right transition... remember the shitty place to put in a pass... he catches me side by side as we enter it, and decides to let me have it, and I put immediately put some distance on him in the next 2 turns. I'm thinking its gonna be him on me, when 3 laps later, a new Formula 40 guy Joe, shows me a wheel on the brakes into the last turn... I saw off his front end to stop his drive and look back to see him all over me like stink on sh*t going into T1.... ahhhh, somebody help !!!!

Into T3 and he outbrakes me and I pull up underneath him and we race nose to tail for another lap, then the lappers come... he beats a guy into T1 and I get held up... I'm yelling go, go, go, and he seems to be laughing at me, mocking me... he dares to mock me ? Does he know who I am ? Apparently he does... and I pass him coming out of T2... I hold the distance to Joe, but can't make up any time, I'm hoping more lappers will get in his way, but he gets thru them as well as I do and he wins... 2nd for me.

Third race is heavyweight superbike. The flag drops and I get the start of my life...I'm on the outside going into T1, a right and a guy from the Star school pulls up even with me on a GSXR 750... I lay off the brakes just a hair and slide just enough in front to have the line and he has to back off... I can't believe I'm leading the damn race... I come out of T2 ahead of him and Chad, my real fast buddy was in 3rd, said I immediately gained about 6 bike lengths there, then my full on Rossi-sideways into T3 on the brakes... ahhhh shit, am I gonna make it.... I trail the brakes all the way in and it sticks, then come out of T3 like a cat with turpentine on his ass, I hit the fast left hander expecting to see a front wheel of either Chad or Kane somewhere, but nothing... At this point, the race god woke up, spit the beef jerky out of his mouth and said, "whoa, something is wrong in the universe that requires some divine intervention."

I hit T5 still not believing I'm out front, when the red flag comes out... I sit up, put up my hand and look back to make sure I wasn't gonna get plowed, and I realize that I had about 10 bike lengths on them... nice, except I know I can't do it again...

We pull into the pits while they clean up the mess, one rider hit the back end of another one and he went down going into T1. Chad pulls up beside me and looks at me with wide eyes... Nice start... I tell him I couldn't believe it myself... Kane pulls up and goes, "wow, nice f*cking start"... and the Star school guy says the same... Its a nice moment, but I just know the restart is going to be much different...

Race restarts and I get off in 3rd behind the Star school guy. Chad is leading... Kane passes me going into T3, and pulls right up on Chad. I pass the Star school guy in the fast left hander and it seems like I'm able to keep the same distance on Chad, but Kane is walking away. 6th lap, going into the last turn onto the front straight, the GSXR sticks his nose in and I saw him off, he has to get on the brakes pretty hard and it screws up my drive as well... and because of that I don't get into 5th as I usually do, but don't realize it... going into T1, I hit the brakes, go in deep, down 3 gears, let out the clutch (at this point the laughter in my head is reaching a peak), I find I'm actually in 1st, instead of 2nd, and have the sweetest slide I've ever had... full sideways, very cool... but I'm certainly not going to get this thing turned in time... right off the track into the dirt... I look to see how far back that GSXR is, and he's just rounding T2... I stay in the gas back onto the pavement at about 100 and keep the position... I'm sure the sh*t coming out of my leathers is distracting him a bit at this point...

Every lap I look back and I can't shake him, he's about 6 bike lengths back... ok, last lap, I can hold him off for 3rd, white flag comes out... no problem, just keep your head and you've got it... into T1, down 2 gears, slow, down 1 more... nope right between gears, no engine braking.... ahhhhhhhhh, f*ck, in the damn dirt again, this time it takes more than a moment to get it back in gear, and get my sh*t together, as he passes me, then another guy comes around... I'll pause at this moment to reflect on the moment of crack whore level cussing coming from my helmet... I lose the momentum and can't catch them in time... 5th. I can't believe the level of stupidity that sometimes wells up inside me that has to be let out at the most inopportune time on the race track.

Last race of the day is middleweight supersport. There are 36 experts and 24 amatuers I believe... we haven't had a grid this big in a very long time. The top 4 guys on this grid are all AMA regulars including Nicky Moore. The start is one of my worst...I get pinched off. Almost tag one guy that leaned into me, then almost take out another guy as I have to react... I come out of T2 in about 12th place... I'm sucking wind at this point...

As I hit T5, I can see a pileup that happened to be Nicky Moore making contact with Mike Queen in that shitty place I mentioned before to pass... Mike hits the dirt on his face and Nicky stays up but has to come back across the track... just as I get there... I jam on the brakes and I can see his eyes are pretty big.... I nod and he comes across right in front of me and manages to keep it on the track...

He's off like a shot again, and I run around picking off a few guys... end up in 7th or 8th, I didn't even bother to check...

Well, all things considered it wasn't a bad start to the season. I stayed up, it stayed dry, and I went home tired and in one piece.

Picking up the trophy's as I was leaving, one of the girls looks up and says, "hey, looks like you got alot of wood there," to which I reply, "why thank you for noticing..."

They laugh, and I shuffle on home...


Its a sick world and I'm a happy guy !!
