Mark's Racing Experience

June 25th - 27th, 1999

Well, the first time out on the track (BIR) this year in June was excellent! I Missed the first weekend do to work. This is only the second weekend I've been able to race at (BIR) since new riders school last year. I Bought a brand new GS500E to race and was immediately told by the rest of the EVIL PUMPKIN RACING TEAM that if I came in last in a race or let the girl racers beat me that my bike would be used for spare parts via five finger discount in the middle of the night.

Well sorry PUMPKINS, but I still have my bike, still in one piece and I haven't crashed on this bike so far. June's race weekend was lots of fun,I had new rear sets to get use to,a new pipe, a new jet kit that didn't work out all that well,& rain which was very interesting especially on the new patch in turn ten. YES, there were great tips and help from the EVIL PUMPKINS TEAM and PIT CREW as always! My lap speed is about the same as last year, hopefully to improve this coming race weekend, my third weekend of fun in the sun at BIR see ya there!

 

1998

This is Mark's first year with the Evil Pumpkin's.  He bought a shiney new GS-500E and through his connections,  managed to procure parts at cost.  Using our advice,  he's running with a stock exhaust for the first few races and has only put money into suspension & setup enhancements.  The team helped him get the bike all safety wired the weekend before the May event,  but as luck would have it...he broke his hand 3 days before his CRA new rider school & race weekend.  He came up to the track anyways,  went thru all the training ( minus the on bike stuff ) and the school instructors are going to let him do the on bike stuff  for his license at the next CRA event.

June 28th-30th:

Mark's first official race weekend,  with a still recovering hand,  was a success!  He got his license and registered for 3 races,  two on Saturday and one on Sunday.   He didn't crash, managed to turn a 2:25 as a best lap time, and he didn't finish last in any of his races.   Mark is looking forward to the next race weekend.
