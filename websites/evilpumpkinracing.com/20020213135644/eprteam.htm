Team History

The team was formed by a patch of CRA (Central Roadracing Association) wanna-bees in August 1995. The term "pumpkin" refers to a new racer. A new racer is required to wear an orange jersey for their first race weekend to help the more experienced racers identify them as a potential hazard. Our team decided that if we where going to be called "pumpkins", we might as well have some fun with the label and call ourselves "Evil Pumpkins".

Now in our 4th year as a "team" we have decided to keep the Evil Pumpkin name even though most of our team has experienced a year of racing. We are adding a few new teammates though, so there will still be actual pumpkins among us!

Derek, Rich, Steve Guynes, Ned, Derrick & Mark
