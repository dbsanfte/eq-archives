Collar of Neshika - AC 4, +9 Sta, weight 1.5

[Starting Info] In Kithicor, after entering from the west commons zone, if you follow the path, the first house on your left will have a few people spawn in it. One of the spawns will cycle between a ranger, a gnome, and a monk. The monk's name is Grim Oakfist, and he's looking for help from a willing monk to kill some tigers. The tigers run at the speed of SoW. A ranger will be usefull in tracking down the tigers, but don't have him assist you in killing them or he will lose faction with rangers. The tigers con around 25, but hit pretty hard.
Ask Grim 'what mission within the woods', he tells you to kill the tigers Eenot, Reggit and Kobb. Gather their skinds and return them to Grim for the Collar.
