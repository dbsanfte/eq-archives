The Darker side of Monk-dom

From a thread on The Dojo Message board, original author Leewei - View entire thread

I've come up with a few scattered historical facts of the history of the three known orders of monks. Here goes:

The Lost Circle (aka Whistling Fists) date back to the Combine Empire. The founder was Zan Fi, a bard who mastered the monastic discipline.

The Ashen House is historically fairly recent, having been a nomadic order of monks in the Ro Desert until only a generation or two ago.

Solusek Ro demolished the Ro Desert making it what it is today. The only remnants of the Elven city which once stood there appears to be the spectre-haunted tower in the Oasis of Marr.

Neither monks nor druids have quests offered at the Temple of Ro.

Additionally, desert madmen seem to come in two flavors: monk and druid.

Ro Desert is surrounded by outposts of Shadowed Men. In general, several of these dot Eastern Antonica. Innothule, Nektulos, Kithicor, and the Western Commonlands specifically contain outposts.

The priests of Solusek Ro are well-known to hate the Shadowed Men.

Now to the conclusions. Solusek Ro likely demolished the city and created his desert either in retribution, or in an attempt to stop something nasty from happening in that city. My guess is that a permanent gate to the realm of the Shadowed Men was being forged there. So, how do monks fit into this? Well, consider Zan Fi, who brought his understanding of bardic ways to the monastaries. Is it possible that he had some help along the way? The Ring of Scale, adherents to Veeshan, are allied with (possibly inseparable from) the order of Antonican Bards. Maybe ol' Zan Fi had a draconic mentor? Anyway, the one artifact we know of from this order is the Robe of the Lost Circle. Said robe uses a scroll of Jonathan's Whistling Warsong in its creation. It also uses a swatch of shadow silk, a unique robe pattern, and a needle of the void.

It seems our forebears used or were acquainted with dark lores to some degree. Solusek Ro doesn't seem opposed to this -- his temple creates stuff for necromancers to wear from different varieties of shadowsilk. Even so, I find the lack of direct correlation between the Lost Order and the Temple of Ro to be somewhat disturbing. I wonder what kind of role monks played in the fall of Combine and in the creation of the Ro Desert.

Castle Mistmoore has a special inhabitant known to some as the Ward of Ro. He hangs around behind the crypt and has a couple of quests he gives out to PCs. Said ward looks very much like a Shadowed Man. Mayong Mistmoore apparently hates the Ring of Scale.

Certain of the monk quests are for 'evil' items, ostensibly to destroy them. The Skull of Jhen`Tra, the Coronet of Buried Blood, the Sapphire of Souls, the Dagger and Wand of Marnek. I have heard that the Sapphire of Souls and the Wand of Marnek are used in a shadowknight quest as well as the red sash quest.

So, are our masters destroying these? Is it possible that they are being used in some way in the crafting of our sashes and headbands? What if they are being traded away to someone else?

One final thought: Fear and Feign Death abilities are both found in three classes: necromancers, shadow knights, and monks. The first two are obviously linked. Is it possible monks share some common roots with these?
