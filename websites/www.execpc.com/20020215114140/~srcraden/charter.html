Charter

Greetings fellow vessels of hatred,

We, the Nation of Eridine are the chosen of our great Lord Innoruuk. We are his up and coming army of hate. We Teir`Dal Have for too long been forced back into the shadows of Norrath. We will stand for the oppression of the Light Ones no more. Soon we will sweep out and chisel the gravestone of this age, the age of light. We shall be the foundation of the new age, the age of darkness. Much needs to be done until this time, my brothers. We must recruit our young brethren and train them and ourselves in the arts of war. We must water the seed of Hatred with the blood of our enemies if we wish to see it grow into a forest.

 

The Rules of Eridine

  1. Loyalty to the guild above all. We are brothers.
  2. Maintain a standing of honor among the Dark Ones
  3. Build a reputation of infamy among the Light Ones. Remind them of their inferiority.
  4. Show respect to all of our Nation. Those who are small now shall become great Hate Bringers
  5. If a traitor to our race is revealed, let the forest Nektulos ring with his traitorous name.
  6. Never debt yourself to a Feir`Dal.
