1. Talk to every NPC you run into, try different languages when you do. Try asking about everything you can possibly think of. Brainstorm the NPC in question. Repeat portions of their responses back. Download copies of known quests and use them to come up with more questions. Ask NPCs about each NPC monk exhaustively and possibly generate some leads. Another step would be using the identify spell to find out true item names and abilities (remember that Zahorn's Coronet identifies as the Coronet of Buried Blood).
2. If you're really bored, see if you can figure out what the point is to the "Who is Puab Closk" and "Who is LuSun" responses you get from the appropriate guild. It seems that [Puab Closk] and [LuSun] are the keywords, because I was getting that response for a lot of questions where that was the only word in common. Oh, and I tried asking the Freeport guild a few things about the Whistling Fist monks, and didn't get anything from them. Next time I see either of those two, I'm going to see if they know about Khenur.
3. If you are very serious about seeking out undetected quests, Set up one of your socials with good 'quest like' phrases.... Such as... I am a monk of the Ashen Order - I will assist you - What items do you need? - Tell armor? - What weapon? ...make a couple of these keys...(so you have 10-15 generic quest activators for NPCs)...and go to every NPC you see and hit all 3 keys...
4. From Alakazamn's - "Monk Blue Sash: The sixth monk sash. It is not known how to get this yet (at least without killing one of the guild masters). If you ask Velan Torresk about the blue sash, he says 'Brother Klom is the Ashen Order's second Master. He is in charge if Master Puab is absent or falls ill. " This interesting led was brought up by Mintern on the Dojo Quest Message Board
5. Monk Black Headband: This is the seventh and most powerful monk headband. It is not known yet how you obtain it (short of killing a guildmaster). If you ask Togahn Sorast about the black headband, he will tell you that these are given out by Lu'Sun. However, Lu'Sun, the guildmaster, will not respond to any questions about the headband.
