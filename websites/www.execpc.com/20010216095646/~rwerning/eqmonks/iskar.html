Monk Quest in Cabilis
Whistling Fists from Zadkiel's messageboard post
Give Master Raska the 1st and 2nd half of torn note (The notes are dropped by Iksar bandits) and he says 'An old entry from the journal of [Gandan Tailfist]!! You have done well, unfortunately I have given up my search for the legendary [Whistling Fists]. Seasons and seasons and all my patrols have ever found was this worthless flute!! This journal listing is obviously false. There is no such thing as the Lost Valley.'
Turning in the notes Your faction standing with Swifttails and LegionOfCabilis got better. He then gives you an Ancient Thin Flute, which is no drop lore. There is obviously more to this. (Submitted by Bayawak)
Ask Master Raska What about gandan tailfist Master Raska says 'GandanTailfist was the commander of a small band of Swifttails which I sent in search of the [Whistling Fists]. That was one season ago. I have heard the Iksar Bandits have information regarding him. If you have a clue to his whereabouts then hand it over, unless it is merely half of a note. That would do me no good. I would require the full note.
You say, 'what whistling fists?' Master Raska says 'The legendary Whistling Fists are said to work only on the hands of a monk. It is said to bestow great power onto the wielder. It is written that they were crafted by the gods who devised the disciplines of this caste.'
Monk's Shackle of Clay: The first Monk Shackle. Master Raska in the Court of Pain hands you the First Rite Pack and has you seek out Goblin Scout Beads, Large Scorpion Pincer, Froglok Skipping Stone, and Sabertooth Cub Canine. Combine and return to him and he will give you a Shackle of Clay. Note that this quest can be done a second time after completing the Shackle of Stone to have two shackles again. This improves faction with Swifttails and LegionOfCabilis.
Monk's Shackle of Stone: The second Monk Shackle. After completing Monk First Rite, ask Master Raska in the Court of Pain about the Shackle of Stone. He commands you to seek out Iksar Bandits and acquire two Bandit Masks and return them along with your Shackle of Dust and Shackle of Clay to acquire the Shackle of Stone.
Monk Shackle of Rock: The third Monk Shackle. After completing the Shackle of Stone quest, Master Raksa will mention that you need to see Master Niska. Ask her about the Shackle of Rock, and she will tell you she needs the Fists of Talon, a legendary monk. When asked for more information, she refers you to the Court Chronicler. The Court Chronicler has a book about Talon, which describes his whole life, and at the end states that his fists were removed after his death and placed in a jar inside of Kurn's Tower. However, despite a lot of searching, nobody has found them in Kurn's Tower. In addition, when you tell the bartender in the pub near the bank in east cabalis "Who is Tallon Southpaw" he replies with a small story about how fast talon was, and tells you that he believes one of the hands of southpaw was in the keeping of Master Rinmark. Master Bain in the Court of Pain says that Master Rinmark has left the Court of Pain to mediate in The Overthere on the wind and thunder. Return them to her to receive the Shackle of Rock.
Monk Sparring Armor : Master Bain has a few menial tasks for you. He gives you a Leech Husk Pouch and fill it with leech husks. Once done, you are asked to combine the skins and take the Full Leech Husk Pouch to a local tailor by the name of Klok Mugruk. He will then hand you a ready made Training Bag Husk and tell you that you need to get three Piles of Granite Pebbles and then you can tailor a Monk Training Bag for your caste. When you tailor these together, you get a Monk Training Bag. Hand this back to Master Bain and he will reward you with a random piece of Sparring Armor.
