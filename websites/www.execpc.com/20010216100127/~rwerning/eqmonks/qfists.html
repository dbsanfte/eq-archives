Treant Fists - AC 8, +3 dex, +5 hps, weight 0.7. Red color

[Starting Info] - Grim Oakfist still spawns in Kithicor Forest, asking monks to avenge the death of his wife/family at the claws and teeth of those named tigers. When the quest is completed, he mentions something about treant fists. He then despawns. Following up this topic when he respawns will lead to him saying that he gave a few of these to Paub Closk in Freeport. Gauntlets carved from the woodlike flesh of a treant, killing holy tigers for a collar, wearing a robe made from in part from a shadow wolf skin altered by the magic of a necromancer scroll.
Step #1. Freeport, guild house of the Ashen Order, talk to Puab Closk. Ask him "what are treant fist" and he will respond that he has these items, and will gift them to any "monk of great skill". Tell him that "I am a monk of great skill" and he will give you Puabs Token and tell you to travel to the Isle of Kerra to assist an old student of his, a Kerran Cat-man named Clawfist who has been banished by his people.
Step #2: Travel to Kerra and find Clawfist ("banished kerran"), he is under the queen throne in one of the two rooms. Tell him "puab sent me", and he will ask for proof. Give him Puabs Token and he will recognise you as a monk sent by Puab, and will give you a "box with paw prints" a 4 slot combine box and will ask you to find the remains of his fellow student who fell fighting goblins somewhere.
Item One: Ribcage (AC 6, +2 vs save, Wt 1) Goblin Alechemist in Misty Thicket in goblin camp near Runnyeye
Item Two: Humurous-handled Mace (1hb, Dmg 5, Dly 29, +2 vs cold, Wt 1.2.), on Goblin Alchemist in Beholder
Item Three: Cat Skull Cap (Head AC4.+2 vs magi, Wt 0.5) Runnyeye gelatinous cube in passage behind king
Item Four: Fractured Femur (Pierce, Dmg 4, Dly 25, +2 vs dis, Wt 0.5.) Runnyeye on Goblin Captain near king room
Step #3: Put all the items in the box, hit "combine," and you'll now have a Box of Cat Bones. Take this back to Clawfist (the "Banished Kerran") and turn it in for a note, which you then bring back to Master Puab in Freeport. Turn in the note to Puab to get the Fists.
