Brother Balatin is green to me at 42, and scowls. When killed, you lose faction with BrotherhoodOfTheLostCircle, CombineEmpire, and gain faction with ScaledWarriors(?). Have an Enchanter Charm him, which will get him to 'scowling', but he will then talk to you and accept quest items.
"Hail Brother Balatin"
Brother Balatin turns to you. A transparent human face materializes upon the skull. As he speaks the smell of rotted rat corpses rushes into your face. You must fight the urge to vomit. What discipline do you [follow]?
"I follow the brotherhood of the lost circle"
Brother Balatin says 'Then we are one. The rebirth of our circle shall start with you. I have waited decades for your arrival. You should seek the living brothers within Tunaria. When you have earned your garb then you will be [ready for the true drape].
"I am ready for the true drape"
Brother Balatin says 'Seek the broken flute of our master. Taken from us by the Iksar twins of Vistrei. To me they will come and to me your robe and to you will come the dawning of the new brotherhood.
Balatin wants the pipe of Zan, pipe of Fi, and your Robe of the Lost Circle - for which he'll give you the Robe of the Whistling Fist.
PawBuster drops the pipe (Zan). He is in Karnors, and spawns in the same room as the Drolvarg Warlord & his bodyguards. A decent group of low 50's can hold the hand room, pull the jail and the warlord room for good xp and decent chance at item drops. Trying to pull Pawduster back to the hand room seems to cause problems - he usually ends up despawning - so you should fight him in the warlord room.
The Iksar betrayer drops the pipe (Fi). When you zone into chardok, you'll follow a hallway down. At the bottom of the ramp you'll see what looks like a castle. Directly across from you you will see an opening in the far wall. It's a tunnel, and its a couple feet off the ground. You need lev to get into it. Go into that tunnel, travel down the ramp a bit. We camped at the area just before the first intersection. There are 3 spawns that wander the top half of the hallway, plus a bunch more lower down. When the betrayer spawned for me he was just wandering up the hallway.
Getting Brother Batlan to indifferent was really tricky. I ended up using a necro to do it. First, he mez'd him (not sure what the spell name is called), then he cast Numb the Bones (it's spell message is "soandso looks amicable(sp?)" Then he charmed him, then chain casted Numb the Bones over and over again. WARNING: If you hail him once he's indifferent, he instantly becomes KoS again. I believe he had to be indifferent in order to hand in the 3 items. I didn't wish to risk it on a threatening con. So.. we spent about an hour to figure out how to make him indifferent, handed the stuff in and... well, my sig's picture should show you the result.
