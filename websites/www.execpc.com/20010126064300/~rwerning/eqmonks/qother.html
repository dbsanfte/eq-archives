Other Quests

Note for Konem (Qeynos)
Talk to Phin Esrinap of the monk's guild in North Qeynos and say 'Where is Konem?'. He will say he left somewhere and hasn't returned yet and he gives you a note to take to him. Konem Matse is in Qeynos Hills. He is always very close to one of the two paths and he is usually under a tree. Give him the note and he gives you a new note to take back to Phin. Take the note back to Phin for faction, exp, and a water flask. Silent Fist Clan faction goes up.
Tomer's Rescue (Qeynos)
You probably need high faction with the monks to do this quest. Tomer Instogle is a Monk hurt near the western walls outside of Qeynos city. He will tell you that he has been hurt and needs to get back to his guild in Qeynos. Leave him and go to the Silent Fist Guild in N. Qeynos City. Talk to Seta Bakindo in the back room. Ask her about Tomer and she will ask you to escort him back. Go back to Tomer and tell him "I will escort you back". He will ask you to hold his backpack for him and will follow you. Take him back to Seta and tell her "I rescued Tomer". Give Tomer back his backpack for Faction and Exp. This raises faction with Silent Fist Clan and Ashen Order. (Submitted by Jambong)
