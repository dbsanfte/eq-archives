Ok, so you need help on being able to see the fonts on this extraordinary web site.

There is one thing you will need before you can do anything: winzip

If you don't already have it, click and go download it. its about 1.5 Megabytes

1) The first thing you must do is download the fonts specified at the bottom of the home page. (the page with all the news) Make sure you know which file you are saving the fonts to.

2) Once all fonts are downloaded open the folder you saved them in. you will need to have winzip or something to the like.

3) Unzip the files and open the "My Computer" icon. From there open the "Control Panel" and find your "Fonts" folder.

4) Now go back to your unzipped fonts you just downloaded. drag the .ttf file into your "Fonts" folder. There, you got 1 font added to your font list! Congradulations! you will now be able to see these fonts on web sites and use them in your word proccessor. Repeat these steps for each font you downloaded and wish to see.
