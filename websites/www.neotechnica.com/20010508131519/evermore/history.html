Our Guild's Charter Story

This story begins during the Lost Age during the fall and decline of the Elven Empire. Mother Tunare watched as the differing Gods interfered with the races. While keeping her distance, Mother Tunare had gained wisdom and knowledge of the future of Norrath. The time period is largely unknown, as very few bits and pieces of history were to be discovered throughout this era. As a mere scribe, I can only speculate as to what could have been the cause of the lesser Gods to have abandoned their created races and depart from Norrath. I shall assume that the Nameless One intervened and banned the lesser gods from this world. But before Mother Tunare left, she wanted to be sure that her beloved land would be protected. As a last gift to the world of Norrath, she created a group of sisters , only 3 of whom are known , the rest may remain a mystery. They are known as the Sisters Of Tunare. Hence, begins the story of Evermore.

The three sisters known remaining as the gift upon Norrath , are of elven blood. Two are high elfs, while one is a wood elf. One gift was Princess Shahara, a wood elf trained in the ways of the ancient Druids. The other being Iadiene, a High Elf trained in the ways of the ancient Magician. Finally, Sinare, a High Elf, trained in the ways of the ancient Wizards, is the remaining known sister of the trio. Shahara remains on Norrath to protect the lands and the animals that dwell in harmony. Iadiene remains on Norrath to teach of the power of magic and generosity. Sinare is on Norrath to patrol the skies and keep the peace among the races with her wizardry. As a last deed to her great land, the great Mother Tunare bestowed a blessing of enduring strength unto a great agnostic Warrior, Chas St. Vitus, known as the Protector of these three Sisters of Tunare and to understand the motives of all other deities. The Great Tunare's deed was almost fulfilled; however, it somehow lacked a certain balance. So the wondrous Mother of our lands sought out a great Wizard named Quex to be as a mentor to Chas.

One day, news came to the great city of Felwithe that the orc legions had launched an assault on the Wood Elf City of Kelethin. Being of royal blood, Sinare and Iadiene would never be able to be involved in any type of warfare. Therefore, Sinare sneaked from the Felwithe castle and, while running to the aid of her elvin cousins, came upon her sister Iadiene, who had the same intent. While engaged in battle of mayhem and magic, there in the center of the orc camp, they came back-to-back with the Wood elf Princess named Shahara. The fighting was fierce and the three sisters were on the brink of exhaustion when another wave of orcs came over the hill from Clan Crushbone. Their fate was certain death. There, the sisters stood, shoulder to shoulder, awaiting their deaths, when all of the sudden from out of nowhere came a glorious young warrior nomad whose name was Chas St.Vitus. Chas hacked through the orcs as if they were not even there and he finally came to stand in front of the sisters shouting certain orders for their retreat. Of course, being of royal blood, the sisters did nothing of the sort. The battle lasted well into the night but in the end, the Orcs were turned back into the depths of Clan Crushbone.

Now that the group has matured and traveled the vast lands of Norrath, they realize that in order to obtain the goals set for by their Mother, they must seek out other children of Tunare. They reach out to the other deities to promote wisdom and knowledge. Hence, the guild was formed. In their Everquest adventure, they now seek all those with a true heart and a shared common goal to make the lands of which the Great Mother Tunare had intended them to be.

Through their Norranthian life experiences, they built a strong guild, and also forging many strong friendships with others across the land.

This is how the guild was formed and united as one, to be known as EVERMORE. Over the Norranthian years, they started expanding their adventures in dealing with the more challenging Norrathian mayhem, each step of the way.

Other groups shared the same spirit as EVERMORE and The 'Order of the Hand' and The 'Thundercloud Renegades' have since merged into the guild. Wizard Mentor Quex maintains the guild as the leader, with the beautiful Wildiyenya at his side and with the help of Mother Tunare and his very loyal officers and friends, for now and EVERMORE.
