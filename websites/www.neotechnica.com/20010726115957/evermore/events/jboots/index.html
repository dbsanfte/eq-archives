December 1, 1999

Journeyman Boots Quest

The Quest for the Journeyman's Boots actually began on Friday, 11/19/99. We finally finished the complete quest on 12/1/99. The initial meeting place was Erollisi Island, which became known as our "Safe Shore" while waiting for a clops spawn from across the bay. This guild event included several members and friends including, Haldolmer, Tanila, Tarube, Quex, Lana, Qnyil, Yanglo, Sinare, Shahara, Alail, Tavia, Zaelicor, Heylel, Traz, Iadiene, Tremalkin, and Chas. (Did I leave anyone out? I hope not!). This was a very LONG quest that required patience, teamwork, dedication and risk. First, we had to kill the Ancient Cyclops in the Ocean of Tears (OOT). The Ancient Cyclops alone took a few days to catch his spawn. It has been discovered that the Ancient Clops has a 12 hour spawn cycle. Other spawn takers include a Pirate, Aqua Goblin and a variety of level 35 to 40 cyclops. Be prepared for many tough battles before killing the Ancient Cyclops; not to mention several deaths of party members. I died twice at level 34.

TIP: Keep "levitate" and strength and armor buffs up. Keep EVAC up. As usual, Spirit of Wolf is necessary. The island can also be invaded by other seafury cyclops from the neighboring island. DO NOT try to swim as an escape. The clops will swim after you and kill you in the ocean. If you know you are doomed, die along the shore (I died at the very deepest section of the OOT!). This is why levitate is so handy as you can run across the water to the main island; however, the clops will also eventually swim across to you to Erollisi Island and possibly reek havoc on the innocent.

We finally killed the Ancient Cyclops and Iadiene Trueheart was present to take the Ring of the Ancient Cyclops. Iadiene is one of our highest level guild members and officer. The Ring of the Ancient is a NO DROP with +5 int. A very nice item in itself. The ring remains upon logging from the game; so you need not worry about losing it as it will stay in your inventory.

The Next part of the quest required killing and looting a Shadowman for a Shadow Rapier sword. This too required many risks as often times the shadowmen spawn as a "stack" and their spawn numbers can be misleading. You'll need a classic party mix of two mid 30 level casters, tanks, and healers (Druids, Clerics) to deal with a full spawn of Shadowmen. Shadowmen are extremely fast and powerful that can include pets, casters, healers, and warriors. A shadowman CAN NOT be outran! Spirit of Wolf will not save you if you try to escape by running once you have interrupted their aggrosphere. Druids and Enchanters can pull individually; however, it is not always the case. There were deaths among party members.

TIP: The shadow Rapier WILL disappear upon logging from the game. This Rapier quest took several days as each day we were required to loot for another rapier. Our groups had concentrated on the Shadowmen located in the West Commonlands. The level of risk is high as I died twice at level 35, not to mention the entire group dying at one point plus two other guild members in their own group. Group member character levels included level 35 through 28.

Finally, Iadiene had to carry 3250 gold pieces to Rathe Mountain with a shadow rapier and the Ring of the Ancient. Our group traveled to Rathe Mountain for several consecutive nights with no sighting of the little gnome, Hasten Bootstrutter.

TIP: This zone is HUGE and can be extremely dangerous due to the Hill Giants, Cyclops, and giant skeletons. Look for the "Spin Rock" gorge area and know where the Feerott Zone is as a escape.  

Every night, preceding the Journey to Lake Rathe, we also had to loot a shadow rapier as well. Our persistence finally paid off as our exploration of the Rathe Mountains lead to our general idea of Hasten Bootstrutter's spawn and travel route. On 12/1/99, Our friends of the honorable Guild, FLEAS (Fyrelake Elven Association) had finally found Hasten and "Hailed" him long enough for our group to loot another shadow rapier and allow Iadiene to travel to Rathe Mountain in an encumbered condition with the 3250 gold pieces required. Officer Shahara teleported from the West Commonlands into Rathe Mountain to deliver the new shadow rapier to Officer Iadiene. Iadiene finally complete the new Journeyman's Boots quest.

 

The quest exists! Thanks to everyone involved!
