Policies

Evermore was established to be a group of friends adventuring together to the mutual benefit of everyone involved. We always want to be seen as a positive force in Norrath. We do not wish to be overburdened with policies, rules, and procedures. However, with this being said, we do need to have some policies on admissions to assure that we maintain the culture of family that we currently enjoy.

(Additional information is found on our Guild's EZBoard Message board.)

Invitations

1 - As a general rule invitations will be given to:

a) characters that have reached at least level 30 and are recommended for invitation by another guild member.
b) or who have posted to our Guild's EZBoard Message board. These characters must be recommended for invitation by a guild member. Those who post an invitation request on our message board, please note the Evermore member's character name for reference. You must be a good person willing to uphold the values of our guild. (If you are below level 30, we suggest you spend some time with Evermore guild members.)
NOTE: The invitation will be extended after a discussion among the guild officers.

2 - Exceptions will be:

a) If you have one character in the guild your other characters can be automatically invited even if they are below level 30.
b) If you become engaged or married to a guild member you are automatically eligible for membership [based on their recommendation]
c) Characters that are personal real life friends of guild members of good standing will be  considered for invitation and review by the officers.
d) Other characters that are below level 30 can only be invited with the approval of the guild officers and following an online posting asking for those opposed.

Etiquette

If someone is causing a disturbance, we must try to help that member improve their behavior. The goal of our guild is to facilitate our enjoyment of Norrath. The best solution is to correct the behavior and not to disband the individual.  However, if someone has repeatedly caused problems  for other members, we can not allow this to detract  from the harmony of the guild. As such appropriate actions will be taken to correct the issue. (For more see the EZBoard Message Board)
