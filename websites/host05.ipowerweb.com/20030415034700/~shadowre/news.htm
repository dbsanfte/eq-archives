September 28, 2002

Ok stop the tells, I updated the site. As you can see by the featured SS I found myself bored and in search of the quickest way to rack up those AA before PoP release. Well it seems the search continues. Im sure many are wondering after the huge ex patch what kinda of crazy ex killing 4 IBs solo yields ???? Well I wont keep the secret anymore......16%. Yup thats right 4% AA per kill. 45 minutes of work living on the edge for all of em since I couldnt snare em and verant refuses to fix the freeze bug when someone zones in with new textures on. Well at least for my troubles I got ..... hmmm what did I get again .. O YEA not a damn thing but a few silks for my troubles, not even pp loot.

We finished up reaperrezzers epic to make 3 cleric epics in just a couple days... well 2 I guess more on that in a few. ToV continues to equip our alts at a rate we can barely keep up with. Its too bad we still havent seen our 1st plate BP from there yet.

Well the news is out with PoP skill caps can finally be raised over 252, with most it appears going to 305 and possibly 355 for int/wis. Thus a few questions arise such as .. will they finally raise the caps for melee and caster skills ??? this would finally give them the chance to right alot of wrongs with balancing. Give the monks the chance to get a 350 H2H and other classes the chance to finally raise there skills above others. As part of that the chance for them to break the old +125 cap on items for stats/hp/mana also becomes a question (+126 really cause items have to go from -126 to pos 126 thus = 252) and if any of these suddenly bugged items like this Cold Iron Bracer are proof then things are lookin good.
