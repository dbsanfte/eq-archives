Goblin Raiders

Reward: Minor Item, and BattleMaster Quest
Starting Zone: Fironia Vie

 

Captain Keatar tells you that the goblin raiders have been quite a nuisance to his trackers. Bring him proof that you have taken at least four of these beast down and you will earn the respect of this outpost and insure your well being. The raiders sometimes drop headbands. Bring him 4 and he will reward you with a minor item and the battlemaster quest.
