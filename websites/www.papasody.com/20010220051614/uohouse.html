My house.

Almost everything you see here can be crafted by players. By finding a skilled craftsman, you can turn a plain old storage shack into a cozy little home for you and yours. Some of the items (namely, the paintings and pillows), are found by fisherman who fish too close to a shipwreck area. The spinning wheel and loom are quite useful to most any home. Decorative, and functional. Stigmata often uses the standing harp to calm his nerves as he looks out over the bay.

The cozy bedroom of my wife and I. The bearskin rugs help keep it cozy. Who wants to walk on cold marble first thing in the morning? Oh, damn. I thought I told her to put that whip away. Tsk Tsk.

This is my dirty little secret. It turns out, there are always people (NPC's) looking for an escort to various places around the world. They expect you to take them to where ever it is they wanna go, for a small gift of gold. Budding mages use this as a source of income as they practice their magery skills by casting gates for these people. I discovered one could gate to ones own home, and slaughter them, taking ALL their riches off the still warm corpse. And they would blindly follow me each and every time I did so. It was this source of income that allowed me to upgrade to the larger home. Salute!
