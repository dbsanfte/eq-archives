The Cash Box

Here are a few opportunities I thought I would pass along. They are all things I have signed up for. I won't steer you toward anything I am not willing to try.

PayPal is easily one of the coolest things on the Net. I use it all the time to pay by credit/debit card when I buy things off of e-bay because it is far faster than mailing a check or money order and it is secure. It also allows you to accept credit card payments, no matter who you are or what you are getting paid for. You can do direct transfers with your bank account, too. All-in-all, it is a really cool thing. All this and it is free to sign up! In the interest of full disclosure, I will get $5 if you click on the logo to the left and sign up.
This is one of those pay-you-to-surf-and-refer-everyone-you-know programs. You download their software which puts a bar on your screen with banner ads on it. I have tried a few of these programs and this one had the least annoying view-bar, and didn't make my browser or system crash. Some people might find the view bar too annoying-- it does take up an inch of screen space. I haven't been doing this long enough to get paid, so we will see how it works out. If you sign up through my link, I get credit for the referral.
When you open a Compubank account, you can earn money by getting your friends to open accounts too. This is a legit bank-- I have a checking account and savings account there, and I haven't had any problems getting to my money or having my checks accepted.
