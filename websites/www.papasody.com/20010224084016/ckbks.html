Cookbooks

Buying cookbooks can be a little risky-- I have a number of cookbooks that are beautiful, but really quite useless for everything except the most special occasions. The recipes call for expensive or hard-to-find ingredients and the preparation is time-consuming or difficult. Those aren't the kind of cookbooks you will find here. You can buy any book suggested from amazon.com by clicking on the picture.

If you have a cookbook that you think is spectacular and you can't live without, feel free to suggest it for this page. Drop me an e-mail!
