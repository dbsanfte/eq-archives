Food-- You can't live without it, and that's what this page is all about. Give me some time and I will have a lot more of my own content on this page. In the meantime, let me give you a few of my favorite links.

Weight Loss:
Cyberdiet -- They have everything. They even have free customizable menu plans with recipes and shopping lists. I actually lost some weight on their program before I got off track. Now that I have put this link here, I think I will go back.
Dietwatch-- This site has a great community, but the greatest feature (in my opinion) is the food diary. It is amazing! It calculates all sorts of nutritional values for the day so you can really see how you are doing.

Vegetarian:
Vegsource-- It's a little heavy on the propaganda, but there is a lot of good information there.
