Entertainment For The Easily Amused

We watch a lot of movies. We read a lot of books. Here's some of what we think about these amusements:

MOVIES IN WHICH PEOPLE GET EATEN:
Pitch Black (Unrated version, DVD);Pitch Black (Rated Version, DVD): We recently rented this movie. I have to admit that I expected it to be totally cheese-o-rrific, and it was maybe only 75% cheesy. It is a bit like the Aliens movies, but not as well done. Still, it really is worth getting if (like me) you like to curl up on the couch late at night to munch popcorn and see people get eaten by creatures that live in the dark. -- Mama. VHS still rental market priced at this time.

Lake Placid (DVD)/(VHS) : This movie was more comedy than horror, though there were a few "gross-out" bits. It's about giant crocodiles eating people in Maine. Enough said. It's a completely silly movie, which is a good thing. My favorite character is a foul-mouthed old lady (played by Betty White) who treats the giant croc as a pet. Oliver Platt is in this as a rich eccentric who sees divinitiy in crocodiles. I just thought I would mention him because I think he's cute. --Mama

Deep Blue Sea (DVD)/(VHS): Deep Blue Sea is a shark movie. The very best part of it is that everyone that typically survives in this sort of movie gets eaten. The hero guy doesn't get the girl in the end-- the shark does. That alone is enough to make me recommend this movie. Consider it the shark movie that makes fun of shark movies. --Mama
