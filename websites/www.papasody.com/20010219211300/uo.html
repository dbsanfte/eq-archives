Ultima Online

Ultima Online can be purchased here for $19.99. It has a monthly charge (less than $10) and requires an active internet connection to play.

UO Resources

Origin's Official UO site. Fairly vague info on skills and such, but ALOT of useful services. Keep track of your guildmembers, show off your skills, check for events. Good site to bookmark.
Stratics.com has information on every MMORPG. From skill explainations to weapons studies to message boards, this is the place to look for answers to all your questions.
The good people at Tugsoft.com have come up with a rather interesting and extremely useful tool. UOAssist. For a minimal cost of $15, you can register it forever. There is a free 15 day trial period, so check it out.

Here are a few screenshots of my various adventures in the world of Sosaria. The house shown is one of the nicer houses you can own in this game. But even the smallest of houses provides something you wont find in any other game. A sense of belonging. You may log out and go to sleep, but your house is there for all to see.
