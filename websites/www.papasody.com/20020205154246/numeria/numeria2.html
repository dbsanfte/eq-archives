Gens Numeria in Nova Roma

A Word From The Materfamilias

I have been giving a lot of thought lately to the character and "mission" of Gens Numeria. While we have, at the time of this writing, only 2 members, I would like to begin developing a distinct character and public presence for the gens. I want to see this gens grow and bloom!

So, what are the defining characteristics of Gens Numeria?

  • We are family-oriented. Personally, I can't help but be home and family oriented, as I am a mother of five. I would like to have a gens that is comfortable for both singles and families, of course, but exploring ways to integrate the Via Romana into modern family life can be seen as one of our goals. I'd also like for our members to consider this a real family.
  • We practice the Religio Romana. I do perform Lararium rites on behalf of the gens, my family, and myself, based on the information at the Nova Roma site. As we grow, I'd like to see members of this gens actively and publically involved in the Religio.
  • We support the use of Latin. I'd like to find a way for us to support the study and use of Latin without getting too tangled up in the official issues of language policy.
  • We are involved in the community. This could mean a lot of things, from getting together for face-to-face meetings, to running for office, to merely voting. No amount of involvement is too large, or too small.
The Structure of Gens Numeria
In order to promote the face-to-face, real world aspects of Nova Roma and Gens Numeria, members are encouraged to form or join local/regional familiae within the gens. Those will be listed here (with links as appropriate).
