GRACE  may be called by a Knight (Officer) of the Guild for minor violations
               of The Testimony, should the guild administer maximum punishments
               for acts against the Testimony that place a members points below
               zero.


MERCY may be called by a Knight (Officer) of the Guild for a Banishable
              Offence if the Knight can prove the act was committed without malice
              or premeditation and the accused was acting due to duress of
              understandable preportions. 


FAITH   may be called by any member of the guild to allow time for an accused
              to gather evidence in their defense, but only under the circumstance
              that the caller believes the accused is innocent.
