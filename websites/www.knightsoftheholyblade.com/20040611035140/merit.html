Order of Merit

The Order of Merit list was created to establish a priority for the 4 Primary Quested Paladin Weapons.  The guild may seek to Prioritize its goals towards a Paladin not of this guild, because their actions were deserving and their intentions honorable and they are more deserving in the eyes of the members, than the members themselves.  Their name shall be added to this list as well.

All other loot for guild members is based on points, however the order of Merit List shall reflect these points properly.

This page is subject to change often.  Please check it regularly.  Any discrepancies are to be brought up in "Grievances".
