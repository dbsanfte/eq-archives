Corrupt Gaurds

Corrupt Guards (Paladin version): The guards in Freeport are corrupt and the Paladin's Guild wants you to do something about it. Kill the guards and return their helmets to Theron Rolius one at a time for some decent money, experience and a faction adjustment. Beware that this quest will make you very unpopular among the guards as your faction will go up with the Knights of Truth and down with the Freeport Militia.
