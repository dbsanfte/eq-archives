The Testimony
I, as a Knight of Truth, Defender of the Innocent, Warrior of Righteousness, Keeper of Honor, Slayer of Evil, Enemy of the Corrupt shall...



o    Seek the respect of my peers, and not the loot of their kills.                                    

o    Find my self-worth in the eyes of my brother and my enemy.                                   

o    Aid the needs of others before the needs of my own.                                                

o    Give my Healing Touch to the greater good of all, before I give it to thine self.     

o    Expect to receive less than the bounty that I give.                                                    

o    Speak the Truth without fear.                                                                                       

o    Liberate the trapped Souls of the Undead.                                                                  

o    Damned the Darkness for I Wield the Light...                                                         

o    Take not, ask not and it shall be given unto me.                                                     


For I am a Paladin, a Holy Warrior of the Righteous God!  Proud of my commitment, My Sword serves all that is good and holy.
