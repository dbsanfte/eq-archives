Holy Blade's Accrued
Merit Points

Updated June 5, 2002


The point system is used for the dispersal of equipment on guild raids and equipment from the guild bank.  Disciplinary Actions may reduce the amount of points you have.  Squire points are determined by the Knights and the Crusader.

A maximum of 500 points is required before a Squire can become a Knight and a vote cast by the Crusader is required before this happens. 

A maximum of 100 points can be awarded for one act and the award of points is based on a majority decision.

All new members members are awarded 25 points to start. 

All members are awarded 25 points for recruiting new members. 

All members are awarded 25 points for Acts of Valor witnessed by an officer or two squires.
Completion of Ghoulbane = 50 Points
Completion of Soulfire = 100 Points
Completion of Fiery Avenger = 250 Points
Completion of Fiery Defender = 500 Points
Grandmaster Skills = 500 Points
Master at Languages = 25 Points Per
Public Raid Leader X5 Raids = 250 Points
Raid Participation = 25 Points per
AGE Raid Participation = 50 Points per
Message Post on Guild Site = 1 Point Per
Designated Experts = 100 Points (Applies to Quests Only)
