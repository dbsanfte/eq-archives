How to Become a Knight of the Holy Blade
If you weren't invited to become a guild member, here are some tips for joining the guild...
o    You must be a Paladin.

o    Level does not matter in becoming a member.

o    Un-Paladin like or Vulgar names are not allowed., ie  "Darkone", "Palcrap", etc...

o    Conduct yourself with honor, nobility, and valor in the presence of Guild 
      Members and you will be noticed and possibly asked to join.

o    Request to join the guild to any member and display one of the following:

       1.   Earn the Band of Rodcet Nife and display it to a member.

       2.   Present the H.H. Key 122 or note for Brother Hayle to a Guild Officer.

       3.   Ask a member to be present when you complete the Knight of Truth Quest.

       4.   Present the Falchion of Koada'Vie.

NOTE:  Do not ask a guild member how to complete the above quests if you are considering
            joining the guild.   Take the time to learn them on your own.  It  demonstrates
            dedication to the class. 

o    We are more impressed with Bronze and Fine Steel than Deepwater and Sword of Mourning.

o    Convince a member you are a person who likes to play a Paladin for the right reasons.  I'll
      let you figure out what "right" means...

o    Enter our guild chat room and present your character and alternates to us.   Explain why you
      want to join the guild.
As of this moment, we do not have any initiation requirements. 
                            This is suject to change.
