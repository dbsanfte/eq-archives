Hail Friends
 
When was the last time Eci had a BoB Competition?  Let me answer that question....
 
Its been a long time.
 
Anyone remember Whyldkeel?  He may have been a SoTS member, but he sure knew how to fight.  ;)   Course, he also had the most uber equipment available at the time, but so did the other server champs he fought against.
 
Eci (Whyldkeel) took the BoB Championship for all EQ Paladins on all Servers!
 
The KoHB Officers have been kicking around an idea for a Paladin Best of the Best Competition sponsored by the KoHB.   We can front a Black Acrylia Halberd as a Prize and a Walk Thru Free Soulfire, but I am hoping for something of a more grandious scale. 
 
Tentatively we are looking at the Saturday of Labor Day Weekend or quite Possibly, Labor Day itself.
 
Some problems arise from this idea.
 
#1  Level difference and fairness.   With only one prize to offer, we cant fairly open this up to all Paladins 40-60 because the higher level Paladins would win my hard earned money quite easily  :)  (Points at ----->Pazen)
     
Suggestion:  4 separate groups, 40-45, 46-50, 51-54, 55-60 and better prizes.
 
#2  Respectable Prizes.  We would need some of the better weapons on the market to get some serious interest from the server Paladins.   Weapons that the specific levels would desire and accomodate their hunting levels.
 
Suggestion:  Guild or individually donated prizes.   The better the prize, the better the turn out.  I am attempting to toss the idea around to a few guild members and guild officers to see if there is a positive response to the idea.
 
I'm not donating my Jeldorin  :)  Sorry...    Its all I got  :P
 
#3  Rules, Standards and Judges.  This is not as much an issue.  I was thinking, debuf them in the arena, allow them to buff and med to full, no damage shield potions, once outside the pvp zone, you forfeit.   Lay Hands?  We do have more than one life to give  ;)  Too the death or too conceit?  I say both.  If the loosing player does not conceit, then battle is to the death.   If after the conceit is given, and the victor continues and slays the conceitor, the victor looses.  We are Paladins.   5 Judges inside the arena with all spell effects on and watching, and 5 volunteers amongst the spectators.  Cheaters are punted from BoB Channel and disqualified.  (and put on ignore as far as I am concerned)   
 
One on one or Free For All? 
 
One on One is time consuming but you get the best out of people that way.  I don't see more than 5-10 people per level group coming to this (unless someone donates an 80+k weapon) and we can get all four level groups in the arena at the same time, each with a separate judge.   The 2 competitors are grouped together or we have 4 competitors at once for each level group and they are all grouped.
 
What do you think?  Either that, or we can all just meet in Arena in Bazaar and bash each others brains out.    Winner gets a Rusty Broadsword!!
 
Just asking for your opinions atm.  We will do all the coordinating.

We are petitioning Verant for Paladin only weapons. I have sent them numerous Emails regarding the unfair disadvantage Shadow Knights have with the accessibility of the Mace of the Shadowed Soul and Nocturnal Blade and how many of the better 1hs weapons out there are unfairly used by warriors and rangers. In the past I have written Verant about Black Armor for Rangers and Druids and as you can see, there is now Black Armor for these two classes. Not, that I directly had anything to do with it, but I am sure I am not the only one. 
The point is, Verant (aka Sony) listens. And we are at an unfair disadvantage as there is no comparable weapons to the Mace of Shadowed Souls and Nocturnal Blade that is as easy to attain as these two. They are flooded in the market right now. And since we ARE primarily limited to 1hs and 2hs and CANNOT dual wield, we need a 1hs weapon that rivals those of warriors and rangers and bards. Hence my suggestion for the following: (Might as well go for the gold and expect copper). 

Swift Justice: 17/20 1hs 3str 3wis 3agi procs 80dmg stun against undead at Level 45 or +3dmg bonus against undead

Gavel of Justice: 19/29 1hb 5wis 5stam procs 80 dmg stun against undead at Level 45

Blade of Nife: 20/27 1hs 5ac 30hp 7str 7wis procs 162 dmg stun effect against undead at Level 45

This weapon would aid us in maintaining agro and give us a special gift against our sworn Foe, the Undead.  Stand with me on this, and we might actually see it happened. I recommended Vindication last year and now you see a weapon in game called Vindication. My stats were different, but the blade exists. Unfortunately, SK's can use it too.

We are here to make a difference in Norrath Paladins. Stand with me on this...
