Charm of the Brotherhood

Starts In: Neriak Commons

Firon Dernkal asks you to collect four different medallions of service found in Dulak's Harbor. The Silver Medallion, Gold Medallion, Platinum Medallion, Bronze Medallion. They fall off various mobs in the zones ranging from the trash yard mobs to the lieutenants and officers. Firon cons indifferent to non-dark elves, so anyone should be able to do this.

Location for Firon:

He is approx neg 90 neg 1100 pos 5 As you look at the outside of the warrior guild, with steps going up the middle, between 2 glowing crystals; there are doors on each side.  Inside those side doors are stairs that lead you to the top of the front part of the warrior guild building.
