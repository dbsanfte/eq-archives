About the Knights of Holy Balde

We are new and very lenient currently, but below is our direction. 

We created the guild with a valiant purpose and burning desire to influence the Paladin community on E'ci to hold fast to the true values of a holy warrior, the Paladin. To create a new generation of Paladins that will someday be seniors on this server, and therefore influence the generations to come.

Our goal is to train low level and mid level Paladins to respect and adhere to the religious honorable role of the Paladin, as well as teach them the Paladin Class Role in the community, groups, and raids. 

We will guide young Paladins thru the numerous quests and assist the Knights in acquiring their questable armor and weapons, sharing a vast amount of information and years of EQ experience. However, don't join this guild seeking equipment or we will reply with our number one Oath of The Testimony:

" Seek the respect of your peers, not the loot of their kills "

The guild is much like a Military Organization, as it consists of Holy Soldiers.

Eventually, we would like to become the center of information for the Paladin on Eci and seek to grow into a server renowned guild and play a larger, grander part in the Paladin's life.


OUR GUILD HOLDS NO SECRETS:

  • Joining this guild means that you present your Primary and Alternate Characters names for every member to see. Though you will not be required to log on your guild character to assist the guild or its members, you will be available to answer questions or provide advice when you are on-line.

  • Being a Paladin is a constant learning experience for some. Even Officers and Guild Leaders make mistakes. There is NO FAVORITISM amongst Paladins and any member can call a Violation if they feel they are right. A hearing will be held openly on ALL disagreements and ALL members are asked to participate.

  • This is not a happy-go-lucky, jolly, lets get drunk and run amuck kind of guild. There is order and discipline here and punishment is given when there is not..

  • There is no punishment for lack of attendance. Failure to keep your word is punishable.

  • The Members work on a Point Based System. Each time a good deed is witnessed, a member receives a point(s). Each time a bad deed is witnessed or unveiled, points are taken depending on the severity of the offense.

  • 10% of your guild characters wages/profit are donated to the guild bank. This is an honesty based donation, but if it is found you have lied to the guild, you will be punished and possibly disbanded. No one said it was easy being a True Paladin. Guild purchases are spoken about freely in guild chat, but final decision lies with the Guild Leader and the Officers and are made openly. Disagreement by unbiased members are welcome, but present your case.

  • Purposely Kill Stealing, Training, Illegal Looting or Despicable Acts are banishable offenses. There is no forgiveness for these crimes. If you are concerned about what is considered a despicable act even after reading the definition in a Dictionary, maybe you shouldn't join this guild.

  • Politicking, brown nosing, cheezing the Officers and Guild Leader gets you no where in this guild. You may receive a "warning" for it.

  • Members/NonMembers do not ask for equipment. However, if you feel your needs have been overlooked by the guild, then by all means, bring it to our attention.
