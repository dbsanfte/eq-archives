Journeyman's Boots

Starts in: Rathe Mountains

 

Hasten Bootstrutter in the Rathe Mountains wants several items in exchange for his extra pair of Journeyman Boots.

First, he asks for a shadowed rapier, from a Shadowed Man, which will poof when you camp, so get this last. Second, he asks for a Ring of the Ancients, which is found on the Ancient Cyclops.

The Ancient Cyclops spawns in a number of zones. South Ro, Ocean of Tears, South Karana and East Karana. In the Ocean of Tears, the AC spawns on the island between the sisters island and the seafury cyclopses. It is the only spawn point on the island other than the magician in the tower that sells spells. It usually has a Cyclops, although sometimes it will be a pirate, aqua goblin, or Boog Mudtoe. This may be the fastest spawn (relatively), but he is a higher level in this zone.

Finally, he asks you for 3250 Gold Coins. He talks like Yoda, and runs really fast, and doesn't spawn that often.

Note: You can't get the boots by killing him.
