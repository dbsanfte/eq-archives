Guild Etiquette
In a guild designed for Paladins, there is a need to outline the required Etiquette within the Guild.  Please ensure you read about the etiquette as you can be penalized for failure to comply.  (See Commandments) 

BEGGING:  Will not be tolerated. 

VULGAR LANGUAGE:  Is not permitted at Guild events, or in any public Everquest Channel where you will disgrace the guild piblicly. The exceptions of "Crap" "Damn" "Shit" are permitted in GUILD CHAT ONLY and ONLY under extreme circumstances! Abuse of this privaledge can lead to penalties below. NO VULGAR LANGUAGE IS AUTHORIZED IN PUBLIC EQ CHANNELS. Use of the word "Smeg" (For you old White Cross Members) is punishable by death! It IS acceptable to use "Cheez an Rice" "Dag Blamit" etc.

RESPECT: Respect will be shown to the Knights and the Crusader at all times, even in Guild COURTESY: Chat. It is wise to memorize the names of the Officers and the Crusader. All Knights and the Crusader will be addressed as "Sire" for Males and "Ma'am" for Females when addressing the personably. When adressing in guild chat

SALUTING:  All Squires will salute Knights/Crusader other upon the first meeting of the Norrathian day. Squires are not required to salute squires, but as a gesture of honor, it is allowed. The lower ranking will initiate the salute and the higher ranking is required to return the salute, regardless of level. When the meeting is finished and the parties are to part different paths, another salute will be rendered as described above. If a Knight is 24 and a Squire is 35, the Squire is required to initiate the salute. This custom is based on Rank, not level. When approaching a group of members, always salute the highest ranking first, wait for their return salute, then target the next highest if one exist. If you are a squire, and an officer is present amongst squires, you are not required to salute other squires. It is recommended that you do not. However, a Squire of sufficient level (above 31) deserves the salute of a squire at level 7. Use common sense.

GUILD EVENTS:  To be decided... Tardiness at guild events can cost you 1/2 a point with 1 point max. If you give your word you will be penalized in accordance with the Commandments and Testimony for failure to honor your word. For specific rules for raids please see the Guild Raid Rules

DISAGREE:  Use tact when disagreeing with the decision of a Knight or Crusader. Be smart before speaking out against a decision and be prepared to provide a just reason for your disagreement. Just saying "Coz I dont think its right" will get you penalized for failure to use tact. And after you have disagreed with a decision and you have been heard, and the decision was not turned, continuing to disagree can get you banned. We don't have the time to waste on someone who can't even see they are wrong and arguing is unproductive. If you fail to turn a decision against you, then you are given the chance to ask "Do any of my brethren see an injustice in this decision?" If no one speaks up for you, then suck it up and drive on holy soldier. This guild is all about Justice, Fairness, Honor and the like. Chances are that if you cant turn a decision with your first /disagree, then perhaps you were wrong to begin with... Be a adult about the matter and lets move on towards more productive things.

TRAVELING: When Traveling with a Knight or the Crusader, the lower ranking member should always stay behind a step or two and to the left. The Highest ranking member is always on the right. Remember this when approaching a pair of guild members. It is an easy way to remember who is the ranking person. When traveling with more than two persons, all members will auto-follow the ranking person, unless the ranking person is unfamiliar with route to your destination. If this is the case, the person who is familiar will lead and the ranking person is second in line, with subordinate members following. No more than 6 members per train when traveling. If more than 6 members are traveling together, break the groups up in as even numbers as possible and proceed as described above. If more than one Rank of Members is traveling together, then the two ranks will travel in unison, side by side, requardless of sow, with the lead members maintaining proper intervals on the right and left of each other with enough space between them to fit another member.

FORMATIONS:  When gathering at Guild Meetings or guild events where the leader of the meeting must speak to the group, the leader will give the Command of "Fall In!" At this time, the members will create a row of members standing side by side, facing the leader of the meeting with no more than 6 members per row.  The first person to 'fall in' will establish themselves so the rest of the row is centered on the leader who is running the meeting. If more than 6 members are present, the members will form as described above in two separate rows, one directly behind the other, formed on the leader just as the first squad. Oops, did I say Squad? Welcome to the Army Soldier!

SECRETS: Secrets are for Rogue guilds. There are no secrets here. If you know a member       has committed an offense and you fail to inform the guild of it, then you are as guilty as the one who committed the offense. Secrets and Lies go hand in hand. Always be honest.
