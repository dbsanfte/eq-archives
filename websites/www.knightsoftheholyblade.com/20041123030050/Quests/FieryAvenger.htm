The Fiery Avenger

Reward: The Fiery Avenger

 

Based upon the log with Inte Akera, these are the items you need for the fiery avenger.

Note. To get the blessings, you need to talk to Inte Akera. He is located on the Fourth Island in the Plane of Sky. You first need to talk to Dirkog Steelhand in the quest cave and give him 500 pp to cause Akera to spawn. Although technically this is the start of the quest, I would suggest you get the head and robe of Miragul, Ghoulbane and Soulfire first before doing this step, as Akera is very difficult to reach. Also, I have been told that Akera will despawn pretty quickly, so you might want to be at the island and have a friend hand Dirkog the money so that you don't lose the spawn.

1) The Blessing of Nobility: You need a high faction with Akera, who is a Deepwater Knight. This blessing is given to you by Akera in return for giving him Soulfire. The details on getting Soulfire are here.

2) The Blessing of Sacrafice: You need a high faction with Akera, who is a Deepwater This blessing is given for giving him Ghoulbane.

3) and 4) The Head and Robe of Miragul: First, you want a Burnt Book and a Frost Book. You can get the Burnt Book off either Lord Nagafen or Talendor. The Frost Book can be looted off either Lady Vox or Gorenaire.

Take the Frost Book and Burnt Book to the bartender in the Blind Fish Tavern in Neriak along with 1000pp. She will combine them into the Book of Scale.

Give the Book of Scale to the Oracle in the Ocean of Tears and he will give you back an artifact called Miragul's Philactery. You must then head to the Everfrost caves, which you get to by going through a hole in the ice at the river (enduring breath is very useful for this trip). Get an enchanter to charm the Lich and then hand him the Philactery. This will spawn the true Miragul, a 53+ necromancer who is as hard to kill as a dragon, so make sure you have lots of friends with you. When you kill him, he drops the robe and his head.

Turn in these four items for the Fiery Avenger.
