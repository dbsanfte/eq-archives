Holy Blade's Accrued
Merit Points

Updated June 5, 2002


The point system is used for the dispersal of equipment on guild raids and equipment from the guild bank.  Disciplinary Actions may reduce the amount of points you have.  Squire points are determined by the Knights and the Crusader.

A maximum of 100 points is required before a Squire can become a Knight.  A maximum of 10 points can be awarded for one act and the award of points is based on a majority decision.  All members are awarded 1 point for recruiting new members.

New members are awarded 5 points upon joining.
