Orc Runner

 

Talk to the Paladin Lord, General Jyleel, in the basement of the Paladin guild in Felwithe.

Occasionally an Orc Runner can be seen running from the entrance to Greater Faydark along the path towards the crossroads. He is relatively tough (be at least level 8 if you want to solo him), but usually will yield a sealed letter and a pouch upon his death.

Take the pouch to the Paladin Lord. Give it to him to receive a faction bonus and the quest for the falchion.
