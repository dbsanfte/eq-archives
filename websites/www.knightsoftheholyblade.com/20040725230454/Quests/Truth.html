Knights of Truth Quest

I am told this is now given to people after level 9. Because it is an important Paladin quest, I have left in the various shortcuts people have sent in so you can try various ways to get it done. This quest is given by Merko, in the Hall of Truth. The first thing Merko asks you for is to help Altunic Jartin in the E. Commons. Take him the letter, and he will ask you to help him with a thief named Squire Narl. See the Squire Narl quest in E Commonlands. (basically you need to kill squire Narl and get his head, plus Jartin a cloth shirt - you can do this after level 9 even if he doesn't ask for it and still get the token).

This will get you a token of generosity. After you inform him that you have earned the token of generosity, Merko will ask you to get him a spider venom sack. Give him one and you will get a token of bravery along with some experience anf faction (again, even if he won't talk to you after level 9, if you give him the venom sack he should still give you the token). He will then tell you to give him both if you want to go further on. Give him both tokens and he will ask you to take the test of truth, which is killing the guard that appears in the sewers (again after level 9 he might not ask this, but the guard will still spawn). The guard is actually a female militia spy. Kill her quickly or she will run to the zone and be gone. If you kill her and take the token she has to Merko, you will recive the Testimony of Truth Scroll, proclaiming you are a Knight of Truth. This scroll is needed for the Soulfire quest, and is a no drop item.
