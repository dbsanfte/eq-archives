So, you're interested in joining Triad Continnum.

First of all, for clarity's sake, ANYONE can join our forums. They're not forums just for members of TC. We have a GENERAL section that is open to the public. So if your only interest is in joining the forums, please feel free to do so at any time.

However, if your interest is in joining our fine Guild, before you make that final decision, you should have a very clear understanding about what it means when you join TC. Please ensure that you have read the links "About Traid Continuum" and "Guild Charter & History". This way you'll be fully aware of what it is that will be asked of you, at least in part. You will also want to familiarize yourself with our LOOT FAQ. This gives you a clear understanding of how loot is awarded during our events.

Assuming you have already completed all of the necessary reading, here then is a more detailed list of what you can expect by joining TC.

First of all, we consider ourselves as an adult, family oriented, casual raiding guild.

We are an adult guild in the sense that everyone must act like an adult when playing. We do not allow doodspeak and we expect each of our members to behave in a generally adult fashion. Sometimes conversations may get a bit ribald. While we discourage the use of excessive profanity, it's not uncommon to hear (read) topics of an adult nature, nor is it uncommon to see occasional profanity used in guild chat. If these things offend you easily, you might consider the use of the word filter in game. If adult jokes and conversation is something that makes you uncomfortable regardless of it being filtered, perhaps you may not find TC to be a good fit for your needs. That said, continued, excessive use of profanity won't be tolerated for long. Everyone has bad days or bad encounters in the game. It's normal to be upset and to sometimes vent to the guild. However, if you are persistent in using profanity, you may be asked to take it to tells (in the case where you are speaking to one specific person) or you may be asked to check the language altogether. You are expected to obey a warning immediately. Arguing, threatening or being abusive to an Officer, will most definately get you booted from the Guild.

We are family oriented in that we do consider each of our members a member of a close-knit group of people.. our online family if you will. We also have related family members playing together in the Guild. Fathers, Sons, Daughters, Husbands, Wives, Boyfriends and Girlfriends. We encourage players who are involved in RL (Real Life) relationships to join our Guild. Selfish reasons aside, it encourages them to attend raids and events together, it bolsters our numbers and it usually makes our members happy.

By describing ourselves as a "Casual Raiding Guild" we mean that we do not require mandatory attendance at guild events / raids UNLESS SPECIFICALLY ANNOUNCED. We do raid frequently, typically Monday, Wednesday and Friday nights and often on the weekends and on other days of the week. However as an applicant, it will be your responsibility to acquire a sponsor from the guild. The Sponsor will be responsible for introducing you to the rest of the guild and act as liason between you and the guild. Additionally, the Sponsor will answer any questions you may have and also coach you along in the Guild Policies as well as some of the tactics that Triad utilizes during raids. By attending raids and events, you provide the members an opportunity to meet you and play with you in various situations. Beginning in April, 2006, you will be required to attend a minimu of 3 Raids within a 2 week period of time before you are fully sponsored and voting will be permitted on your membership.

Yes, voting. In TC, all new applicants are voted upon by the members of the Guild. Your application is posted in a message thread where others may leave feedback (good and bad) about their experiences with your play character, their assessments of your abilities, your willingness to learn and your capability in your chosen class. In order to be accepted to TC, you must receive 90% in YES votes. Two NO votes will immediately disqualify your application. If the NO votes cannot be resolved with a reasonalbe period of time, your application will be rejected. Initial rejected applicants may reapply after 30 days, providing they commit to making an effort to improve.

You are expected to treat your Guild Mates, Officers and Leadership, with respect at all times. To be honorable in your interaction with other players. You may not cheat, steal, "ninja loot", train or behave in any manner that would be considered inappropriate conduct to the Guild Leader and Officers of Triad.

You are expected to partake in guild chat, to the extent of saying hellos and goodbyes to your comrades as they log in and out.

You are expected to carry your Guild Tag proudly. You may not use the /Anon feature with any of your characters that carry TCs tag. (Anon hides your location, your Guild Affiliation as well as your level, class and race. If you wish to hide your class for various reasons, please use the /Roleplay command. This will hide your personal details but will permit your Guild Tag to be displayed properly.
