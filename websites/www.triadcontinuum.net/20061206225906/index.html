9/2/06 - ENDLESS SUMMER

Summer is finally over and as we look back over the past few weeks, we're proud of the accomplishments we've made. We've completed PoP Progression for most, with only two major fights left for many. We've begun moving into GoD and Planar Zones. We've begun Ikkinz and Tipt Trials, completed countless DoN Missions, DoD Missions, and PoR missions. We've gotten many of our members their 1.5 Epics and have moved many more closer to achieving theirs. Visit this LINK for some highlights of the last six weeks.

Traditionally, this time of year sees Guild Numbers wane as people return to the grind and get a jump on school or work. This is typical and lasts only a few weeks. On September 19th, the new expansion, "The Serpent's Spine" will launch. Immediately following launch, TC will discontinue raiding for two weeks only, and we encourage our members to use that time to level up to the new Level 75 Cap. After that two-week sebattical, we'll move back to our regular raiding schedule.

PROMOTIONS

With TC's growth this summer, we also saw some much deserved promotions. Congratulations are in order for CHAWN, ELANAINA / ALASIA and VIOLETTE and thank you for your continuing contributions to the Guild. I think it's also important to recognize existing Officers for their recent contributions to the Guild. Many don't really know what goes on behind the scenes or how much work it really is trying to keep a guild moving in the right direction, plan and lead a raid, and deal with the minutae that always creeps up. So thanks to EINILE , CAELLUS , MALIFON , DANAR / ZEESPEEDE , JALCEA , TNESY (who is just rejoining us after his recent deployment) and EXOTIC (who is leveling up to replace his old character). Why do we have so many Officers? Real Life being what it is, Officers aren't always available. Some are in the military, some are hard working on their careers and others just have committments outside of the game. With the current mix however, chances are you'll find at least one online at any given time ready and available to assist you with any administrative issues.

RECRUITMENT CHANGES

Triad also changed our recruitment process during the summer and we have eliminated the role of "Sponsor" for new applicants. Instead, we have appointed LORDWINTER as Applicant Liason. In Lord's new role, he will help Applicant's become acclimated to TC, evaluate their needs and their potential and report back the the voting committee. Congrats to LORDWINTER and thank you for taking on such a demanding role.

On the subject of recruitment, TC will soon be re-evaluating new applicant's requirements for membership. If you have been holding off in applying to TC, you may have more stringent requirements to meet in the near future. Apply now HERE if you wish to get in before requirements change.

That's our update for now. Please remember to visit our FORUMS to stay informed!

Aaikan

4/6/06 - WELCOME TO TRIAD'S NEW HOME

Welcome to Triad Continuum's new website! It wasn't easy to part with our old home at Guild Portal however the desire for greater flexibility, particularly in the forums area, required us to consider another option. This new site will be updated frequently, at least as often as our old site was.

If you haven't already done so, please take a moment to create a new User I.D. for our forums. Unfortunately, it will be necessary for anyone who wishes to use the forums to create an ID. Anonymous posting won't be allowed. I promise to validate new users within 24 hours, and during these times when everyone will be re-creating new login information, I'll be validating as often as possible until we have all our old members registered.

Eventually, the old Triad site will be cancelled however I will give everyone a heads up when that will occur. In the mean time, we'll continue to move all posts of a relevant nature to our new forums. This would include strats, tips, special links, expansion progression etc. plus some of the more whimsical threads such as our "Show Yourself" thread.

I hope you''ll enjoy the new site. There's still a few things that must be completed but feel free to enjoy. And please let me know if you have any problems.

- Aaikan
