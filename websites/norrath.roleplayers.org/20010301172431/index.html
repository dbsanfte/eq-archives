Impromptu Raid On The Oasis This Past Tuesday - Quickening on Fri February 16th, 2001 12:51am
    While I myself can't speak for Ajack's group that was hunting in the Oasis, I can speak for the group I was in by saying that we rocked the sand dunes to their very cores. The group consisted of Myself, Eldaronmyth, Gluhwein, Putup, and Kenthas. While we originally were out hunting for Sand Giants (to no avail) we, after much coaxing, proding, and ordering (j/k eld) from Eldaronmyth, we took on three spectres, and won. This was a big step for our guild, as this was our first (as far as I know) successful raid on any zone, and we took out some of the hardest critters that Oasis has to offer. I won't, however, say a word about us turning tail and running from them when 3 of them came after us at the same time towards the end of the raid...thats another story for another time. :) Look forward to the Najena and Beholder raids this weekend! If you need info on them, send a tell to myself (as I got the times written down on my notepad on my desk) or Eldaronmyth, as he kinda posted when and where everythings happening. =P Safe travels, and good hunting. ~ Quick
