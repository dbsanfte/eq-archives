    • What follows is a detailed walkthru of how to attend the Cleric Quest Armor - images will follow later.

    Bracer of the Reverent

    • Rathe Mountains: Find a Gypsy named Lianna Ferasa (Loc 3982.16, 1464.10). Ask her "What has become of Regis" She wants you to go to North Karana and kill Zahal (Who is found by the Treant in NK) and free the soul of Regis and return her Flute to her.
    • After you kill Zahal, go To the Northern Tree line to the East and look for Regis (Loc 1499.59, -1511.01) who is a level 28 Zombie. Kill him and get the Icon and the Flute.
    • Return the flute to Lianna and she should give you the Ingot.
    • Buy 2 Enchanted plat Bars and give the Icon, Ingot and 2 Plat bars to Gavel for the Bracer of the Reverent.
    Breastplate of the Constant

    • Head to Runnyeye and find the star-shaped fountain near the bank and jump in. Kill Sludge to get the Ingot of the Constant
    • Go to Dagnors Cauldron and look for Dwigus Lowater and kill him and get the Icon of the Constant. He can be found by the zone to Steamfront and the Goblin camp by unrest
    • Buy 2 Enchanted Plat bars and give them to Genni in return she will give you 2 Galvanized bars
    • Give the Icon, Ingot and 2 Galvanized bars to Gavel For the Breastplate of the Constant.
    Shield of the Devout

    • Go to the mines of Meldrath in Steamfront and kill the Minotaur Sentry and get the Ingot.
    • Go to Everfrost to the Ice River there is a hole in the Ice at LOC 1171.24, -5026.08 you will need enduring breath to do this, swim north when the river forks take the left side you will come to a cave where you can breath once again. In the cave go to LOC 5095.11, -4460.11 and face southwest looking at an intersection.
    • Kill the Glacial Bear and get the Icon of the Devout.
    • Buy 2 Enchanted Plat bars and give them to Vilissia in return she will give you 2 Vulcanized bars
    • Give the Icon, Ingot and 2 Vulcanized bars to Gavel for the Shield of the Devout.
    Boots of the Reliant

    • Go to Kithicor Forest and search by the burned down cabin (Loc: +1725, +1125) there you will pick Up a diary
    • Go to a tree stump at (Loc -90, 1100) jump on to the top of the stump.
    • Take the diary to Turgan in Lake Rathe (Loc 3641.08, 149.10) and give him the Diary for the Icon Of the Reliant.
    • Buy 1 Enchanted Plat bar
    • Go to Blaze, give her the Icon, Ingot and 1 Enchanted plat bar for the Boots of Reliant
    Gauntlets of the Ardent

    • Kill Babbinsbort in Befallen (LOC -800, -184).
    • Go to Everfrost and look Under the Ice (Loc 2996, -5771) for the icon
    • Buy 1 Enchanted Plat bar and give it to Genni and in return she will give you 1 Galvanized bar
    • Go to Blaze and give her the Icon, Ingot and 1 Galvanized bar for the Gauntlets of the Ardent
    Vambraces of Fervent

    • Go to Aviak Island in Ocean of Tears (LOC 2000,4000) and kill an Aviak named Gull for the Ignot of Fervent.
    • Go to Highpass and Find Jovan you can find him in the sawmill upstairs by the still, he wants 1 Tumpy Tonix (1 Kola Nut 1 flask Of water)
    • Buy 1 Enchanted Plat bar and give it to Vilissia and in return she will give you 1 Vulcanized bar
    • Go to Blaze give her the Icon, Ingot and 1 Vulcanized bar for the Vambracers of the Fervent
    Greaves of the Penitent
    • Go to Upper Guk and go to the Scryer room where you will see two pillars of water when you enter (LOC -209, 458). Inside the water you will find the Ingot on the floor.
    • Go to Kerra Ridge and swim out to the small Island on the other side of town there you will find a Erudite heretic Named Maugarim and kill her for the Icon of Penitent. (Kill the 3 roamers to make her pop.)
    • Buy 1 Enchanted Plat bar and give them to Ostorm and in return he will give you 1 Magnetized bar
    • Go to Blaze and give her the Icon, Ingot and 1 Magnetized bar for the Greaves of the Penitent
    Caduceus of Sacrament
    • Hail Gavel, and ask him about the Icon of Sacrament
    • Head to Castle Mistmoore and at the back of the tomb in the Graveyard there's a secret wall. Go through that secret wall you will enter into a small room with 4 doors. Open the door that you see as you enter the room, that will trigger the other doors open as well. Then turn to your left, there's 2 rooms. Go into the left hand side room, the icon is on the floor, which looks just like a voodoo doll.
    • Head to Rathe Mountains and kill a Darkened Giant Skeleton (Loc -620, -1600) to get the Ingot
    • Get 2 enchanted platinum bars and give the 2 Enchanted Plat bars to Ostrom in Temple of Solusek Ro; he will then magnetized them for you.
    • Go to Gavel and give the icon, ingot and 2 Magnetized platinum bars to him and he will then make you a Caduceus of Sacrament.
    You will need a total of 14 Enchanted Plat bars for all the armor and weapons

    3 Galvanized
    3 Magnetized
    3 Vulcanized
    5 Enchanted
