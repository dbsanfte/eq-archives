    Foraging For Forge (03/03/2000)

    I popped online this morning and went to increase my Blacksmithing talents in E. Freeport, and outside the armor shop, a fellow halfling was using the forge. 1/2 an hour later I said screw it and went up to N. Freeport. 1/2 an hour after that, I came back down, and the forge was free. So I began turning the ore into metal bits - then I changed a backpack, and all of a sudden the jerk hogging the forge was there again! He snuck in back of me and was continually clicking on the forge hoping I would do what I did. A couple of clicks later the forge was in my possession again, so I opened it, and kept it open for about 45 minutes while he was still standing in back of me waiting. Of course, I wasn't actually doing anything - but he must pay for stealing it from me when I was switching backpacks. Anyway, it was quite fun thinking of him just waiting there like I did before. Muahahaaaaa.
