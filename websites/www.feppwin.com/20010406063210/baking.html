    • Thanks to Loucyfer over on Bertoxxulous, I was able to become a Master Chef in no time! Below you will find a guide for success, as well as different recipes to try at various levels. Enjoy!

    Becoming a baker is probably one of the easiest and most profitable at the lower levels. It is not even necessary to put anything into your baking skills to start. Many beginners also think to themselves, "Man, then I need to go find those damn ovens every time!" Wrong! While ovens are necessary sometimes, you can carry your own oven - a spit - around with you and bake on the spot! You can find a spit in several of the local shops, one being the Trader's Holiday in East Freeport. It looks like a chunk of meat on a rotating spike with a handle, as seen below.

    The first thing to get your skills going is to make batwing crunchies. These morsels are easy to make and will get your experience going in no time. Find a local merchant who sells bat-wings (or kill a ton of bats, but it's easier to buy them) and then you will also need to find a merchant who sells a bag of frosting. Both merchants can be found in the E. Commonlands adjacent to each other along the dirt path. Place 1 bat-wing and 1 frosting in your spit or oven, and hit combine. DO NOT STACK THE INGREDIENTS! ake sure you put 1 and 1 and hit combine - 20 and 20 will NOT work.

    Your skill will increase dramatically and soon you will be able to make another favorite dish - fish fillets!

    Fish Fillets

    This is also a GREAT money maker. If you have the cash, go buy a ton of fish from the Innkeeper at the Port in E. Freeport, or your local merchants. Then, you will need to find someone who sells spices. The same merchant in EC you bought the frosting from also sells the spices. Combine 1 jug of spices and 1 fish and, if successful, you will receive 2 fish fillets! The fillets sell for 4 silver 1 copper a piece - so if you catch your fish with a fishing pole - you are making quite a profit! Fish fillets get trivial at skill 82.

    Even though you have a high skill, you will fail a lot. To increase your skill faster, try making Fish Rolls.

    +=

    Fish Rolls

    Take 1 bat-wing and 1 fish, and hit combine. The result looks like 4 egg McMuffins. These don't sell as high as the fillets - but they do help your experience. This becomes trivial at 135. Then I would suggest going back to fish fillets to start racking up the platinum.
