Class Talks A Bust?
As you know, Absor initiated threads on Whineplay to discuss class balance. The threads became quite large and spilled over into many new threads before the issue was finally concluded. He posted his summaries of the discussions and as expected, many people are unhappy with what was summarized. The most common complaint was the lack of detail in the summaries (ie not every issue of importance was included). As these threads were meant to bring concerns, EVERY point brought up (that was reasonable of course) should be included in the summary. Even if the Live Team rolls on the floor laughing, the concerns should be included, no matter the content. Now the next question is, what will happen with this information? Will the "broken" classes be fixed and the over-powered classes be toned down? Might be an interesting spring in Norrath.

Pending Lawsuit Is Now Pending Existence
I just saw an update over at Lum The Mad's site about the pending lawsuit against VI & SOE and their attempt to eliminate auctions of their intellectual property. After a little research, it seems that the lawyers that were all hot to trot have hit a roadbloack.

They have suspended taking on any more clients at this time. Looks like the legal arm of Sony bitch-slapped them or they woke up and realized they didn't have a case. Their complaint involved people selling time and that Vi & SOE had no right to restrict the time being auctioned... I was going to offer a little tirade over this, but I think I will hold off. Although I heard that our colleagues at The Cage Of Rage are putting together an article regarding this and will be releasing it soon.

It appears that Player Auctions is allowing auctions of EQ intellectual property for the time being. They are most likely hoping that VI will miss them over and/or they can make enough money not to go belly up.

What will happen now? Who knows... The next question is who else will test the waters of stealing intellectual property and giving a lame ass reason why they can do it?
