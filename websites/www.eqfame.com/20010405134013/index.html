Is Multiquesting dead?
A recent comment by Absor sent the EQ community on a rampage not seen since Abashi left his post. Absor made an offhanded comment regarding how he felt about multiquesting. While many agreed with him that allowing more than the benefactor to hand in quest items broke roleplay, many others felt that a change of this nature would be disastrous for the game. Too many parts of the game are time intensive or for that matter, too freaking hard, often resulting in many players being left out of enjoying various aspects of the game.

There was fear that today's patch would nerf multiquesting. As of my reading the patch message it does not appear to have been in this patch. I am sure there is "discussion" on whineplay regarding this and hopefully VI will opt to not shut down this feature. Many of the changes in the patch seem interesting, minor stuff at best in the grand scheme of the game.

The server side filters were introduced, which may be the only real change of importance to many.
