March 18, 2001

Many News!

This was a really exciting Weekend so far. Thats also why these news gonna be very long :-)
First of all, we got a new member: Lailea joined us yesterday. We are very proud to have a new member and she for sure is a great choice.
Then, Vibes is back. The Guild didn't even get used of the fact, that he was gone and there he is again. He has been part of us forever and we are happy to have him back. Welcome home ~V~!

Now about this Weekends Events so far in chronological order:
Friday night, Lucan had to die for Taino's Soulfire Quest.
After long discussions how to pull him the best way we suddenly had him completely seperated and ready to die

After this we went to the Mistmoore Castle, where we failed to kill Xicotl since he again didn't show up.

Saturday Afternoon we gathered again some friends together and camped Xicotl until we had him. Hurry Hurry we moved back to Antonica and in the deepest corners of Paw to turn in all the items Brother Hayle asked from Taino. After a few very stressy days and much efforts from many friends, Taino finally has obtained his first Holy Sword.

This was a great effort and I would like to thank the following persons for all their help:
Celanya, Nusaja, Noemi, Bakual, Sivass, Gleam, Camolron, Aindaar, Danadiele, Boniel, Rainstar, Polenna, Pinay, Lifeson, Animax, Grevenil and Ndinie.
Those friends spent up to 10 hours or more just to help me with my Quest. Thank you all very much and I will do my best to give this back whenever I can.

After having obtained the Soulfire, we rushed over to Permafrost where the Forgotten Spirits together with the Jokers were already killing the Mobs.

As if this wouldn't be enough, the Jokers gathered everyone in Steamfont Mountains and decided to rush Castle Mistmoore as far as possbile.
With all in all 13 people, we cleaned the way to the Castle and celebrated a very nice small raid.
Everything worked fine until we got trained by a well dozen of Mistmoore Critters which seemed very hungry.

The first total wipeout in the existance of the Jokers. After this the Guild showed very good teamwork again during the Corpse Recovery which was fully finished after around half an hour.
This was very good work for this young guild. We learned much about our Guildplay and we had a blast during this time also.

Today we will go kill Lucan for Goroth of the Forgotten Spirits, may he wield his Holy Sword soon!

A very exciting Weekend so far and very successful. Thanks for everyone that participated in it in any way!
