Saturday/Sunday. February 23/24. 2002

Oh my guys!
This weekend is the most successful weekend of this guild so far! Easily.
I have written the same after Hate already, but this here really beats it.
In Hate we mostly cleared 1st Floor and managed to clear half of 2nd Floor.
This weekend we cleared the entire Plane of Fear twice!

Saturday we had the best turnout ever. We had the best break-in ever. I think we didn't have more then 4 deaths during the whole night.
Sunday we had some bad luck at break-in, since our guest didn't get invisi in advance and some people also lost invisi during zone in.
It was close, but at the end we had to accept a wipeout. No problem, after 20 minutes everyone was ready to go back.
Invisi up, run in, loot the weapon and fight. 10 minutes later break-in was considered a success.

Sunday worked mostly like saturday. Shosa up, Shosa down, Shosa up, Shosa down.
Thanks to Shosa's and Kungs GREAT Pulling work (as always), the only downtime there was, was Shosa's deaths when FD failed.
Saturday it took us 6 hours to clear it, Sunday it took us 4 hours (not counting the wipe at break-in).

Best thing to happen was after we cleared the temple, as suddenly Fright spawned.
Within 3 minutes I got 8 tells about people asking for em. But we wanted to get him on our own.
After an aborted attack, we succeeded without any problems.
Waiting 15 more minutes we saw Terror pop, and again 10 minutes later even Dread showed up.

20 minutes later they showed up and we went for it.
Dread was a cake.
Terror managed to get rid of around 8 of us, but kissed our feet fast enough too.
(at this time I got around 40 tells from half the server about the golems...)

Therefore we declare Fear for OWNED and move on to harder targets

Special thanks to those that volunteered as DT victims: Snootch twice, Csirke and a few that didn't really volunteer but managed to still grab one.
Also great thanks to Shosa for his, as always, insane pulling work and his, as always, at least 15 deaths which sure cost MUCH exp.
