Thursday. May 30. 2002

Welcome back faithful readers! Well it’s been a few weeks since our last big update, but its certainly not due to lack of things to report! There have been many glorious battles, many dramatic victories, and many happy moments that I am pleased to finally have the chance to share with you.
As our interim update mentioned Yamael and Shosa recently completed their epic quests adding themselves to the growing list of Jokers who have done so. To quote our leader Taino, “…we no longer have to watch closely to find one member with an epic.” The signs of our maturation into a formidable force are also being illustrated by the increased pace that our members are gaining levels. Most notably we have three new level 60s: Virtuoso Meggan, High Priest Jarann, and Warder Lanilya. All are to be congratulated for these great accomplishments, not only because it benefits them, but also because it benefits the guild as a whole. Many new doors are open for us now, and we have started to move into the more difficult high end game.
The last update ended with me telling you about our impending trip to the Plane of Fear. I am pleased to report to you that after a flawless break in, courtesy of our Enchanters befuddling the would-be attackers, we cleared the zone of all opposition with relative ease. Drops were plentiful for everyone most notably for Gaediianiel who came away with some great new gear. All bards in attendance were rewarded with the “Army Whip” component for their epic quest, and Shamax came away with the next piece of epic as well.
The day concluded on a more civilized note, as we all put our weapons aside to attend the wedding of our dear friends Barinz and Wolk, in the deserted home of the god of hate himself. It was a beautiful ceremony preformed by Milken, and catered by Nikademuss. Barinz, and Wolk we wish you all the best for your union, and pray that it will be filled with many happy times.
The guild has found a new friend in Lord Bob of all people…giant…whatever. Whenever we show up these days he always gives us nice items with little resistance. We have visited him twice since the last post, and Maxisum and Anterak have new axes. Iranicus and Shosa also came away with gifts that he “donated.” Now that we have perfected some of our new tactics we plan to visit him a lot more often.
We have also been spending a great deal of time as unwelcome guests deep in the heart of Chardok. We recently had our first attempt at the royals. The Prince fell dead by our hands twice, and we even had a chance to wash our feet in the blood of the Overking Himself. Iranicus recently lead a strike force deep into the slave tunnels and after much patience came away an herb pulled from the cold dead hands of Grand Herbalist Makha.
SG has also become a new hunting ground for Joker groups. Recently an elite team penetrated deep into the belly of this zone and came away with some great drops, including a walrus skin drum that they passed on to a very grateful young bard J. Future plans will have single groups returning there in the near future.
As we have grown as a guild, rolls have recently changed for some people. I would like to extend our gratitude to Wolk, Shosa, Jarann, and Aaldin for their long periods of dedicated service as officers, and representatives of our family. Also I want to congratulate our new officers on their recent appointments. I have gotten to know all of you over many long hours of raids and guild chat and I am proud and honored to be serving under you.
Wow we sure have been busy! No time for an afk though, as we are planning our biggest strike yet for the upcoming weekend. Keeping our exact target quiet for now but be sure to check back Monday to hear about the outcome.
I do wish reiterate that we are looking for a few more players to complete our team. So if you are a cleric, chanter, wizard, mage, or BL that wants to become part of the Legend, please read our charter, and requirements and post in the applications forum. One of our recruitment officers will respond and make time to meet you in game. Look forward to meeting and writing about you!

Best wishes as always,
Hugs.

Antonin

Monday. April 22. 2002

What a great weekend in Joker land! We added an extra raid day to get the most out of the Hate Plane. Drops were plentiful, everyone played their parts well, and even our one wipeout was quite memorable (insert movie link here).
But before we talk about Hate, I am pleased to report that the big Joker auction has come to a successful close and item pick up has started. Thanks to Barinz for organizing the auction and doing such a great job keeping it under control. Thanks also to all who participated, the money will come in very handy for organizing new raids in the future. Enjoy your items, and remember the store is now in the Bazaar not Freeport. As for Hate:
After a bumpy start on day one we managed to come back strong, slicing and dicing our way through all the mobs that resisted our advance. Even the Maestro himself proved no competition to pure Joker might. Antonin picked up a six note blade, which although not uber, was a memorable first piece of loot from the planes. Cstrike picked up "Essence of a Vampire" a very rare Epic piece, and Dorina, our new main assist, got the "Maestros Hand," the next piece for her epic swords. Many other Jokers also got new upgrades from the mobs we took on.
Day two was equally entertaining. We had quite an epic battle with a Champion of Innoruk who just would not die. In the end, superior tactics gave us the edge and we came away victorious. Notable pick ups included the staff for Nikademuss' epic, and a shattered emerald, the final piece for Ewing's second ranger epic sword. Diamonds and blue diamonds were also plentiful.
The next target we set our sights on was the chanter house in Kael, and finally the Arena. Grats to Wolk, Blessid, Dorina, Dholyan, Jarann, Maxsium, Meggan, and Kalthor on their new items.
All in all it was a most enjoyable and profitable few days. We are really starting to get our flow down. Old friends and new friends are starting to get along very well. Next Saturday we will be continuing on our roll up on the Fear Plane. More news to come soon!
