Royal Guardians a Dark Age of Camelot guild on Morgan Le Fay.

 

 

Who are we?

 

Royal Guardians has been founded as a guild that arranges Realm vs Realm events for the realm Midgard.

Yes there are already some alliances within Midgard guilds present, but Royal Guardians is trying to take these alliances and create 1 big force out of all of them.

We do not do this by trying to lead these alliances/guilds in general, we just try to get everyone together so the entire realm can be aware of events going on and they can choose if they want to participate in these events.

 

Where are we going?

 

As soon as we have finished our first weeks of testing (these tests are implemented to ensure cooperation and communication will be as effective as possible) we will weekly set up events for the entire realm. We will organize them, plan them and assist the guilds/alliances with executing them. For this we will always need the assistance of everyone involved and this makes every guild/person equal to the other.

 

Who are our members?

 

To be a Royal Guardian you must follow certain rules and must have a certain amount of knowledge about each realm. You will also be tested on 4 different disciplines to see if you have the skills, knowledge and dedication to become a full member.

To players in our realm must also always be able to call upon you. With this we mean to say that as soon as a Midgardian is in trouble he/she can depend on a Royal Guardian to assist him/her.

We are here for our realm and dedicate ourselfes to protecting its borders and the people within.

 

How do we test our members?

 

Each member will get 4 different tests he/she has to take.

These tests are class based. With this we mean to say that each class gets different kinds of tests to ensure that we use the persons full ability and don’t have him/her take a test that would never be used in a real time situation.

For instance:

If you are a ShadowBlade, we will ask you to penetrate an enemy fort and take screenshots inside a keep. A simple way of testing your climbing + stealth abilities.

There are far more difficult tests to perform but those are not open to the public and are adjusted to suit each different person as well.

 

How can you join us?

 

To join us you must be able to speak the English language, must be online at least 5 times a week (to stay up to date) and you must commit yourself to the task we as a guild have set for ourselfes.

You must agree to our rules of conduct and must accept to take the 4 tests we will give you.

Failing 1 test does not mean we will not accept you as a member but we might.

Orders given by your superiors (Guild Leader, High Priest and or Officers (in that order)) must at all time be followed. If you are somewhere out getting exp and you are being called upon you must follow that request. Not following a request made by an Officer may result in removal (either instant or after confirmation).

 

Final note.

 

We are here to assist Midgard in every possible way. If you need us you can always count on us!
