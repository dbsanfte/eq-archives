~ Raid Rules ~

 
Those Raid Rules are not actually RULES. Its just tips and suggestions concerning Raids.
We do encourage you to take those following lines for serious and act with them, however, you do not act against the Guildrules if you don't, you may *just* spoil a raid....


  • RAIDs will be announced in the specific Forum (MUST) - On the Agenda (MUST) and eventually in the GuildMotd soon enough. Announce either your presence or absence soon enough on the specific Forum.

  • buy enough Components/Reagents in advance and get your Resist Gear from the bank.

  • Be in time (MUST) - if you are late you don't just waist your time but also the time of EVERY other person at the raid. Nothing is more annoying than the endless waiting for a Raid to start cause people are late. If you are not in time, you will not join. If we have good reason to still bring someone to the raid that is late, they will be summoned to the Raid with Call of Hero.

  • At all times check your settings pre raid. Turn off particle effects, 3D Sky off, Clip Plane... so you don't go LD all the time.

  • Perpare all Hotkeys in Advance. (/assist Main Tank or special Keys like *Target has been snared* and so on)

  • Turn autoattack off in any way by typing /assist off.
    This will make you NOT attack a mob when you hit your assist button, so you will not accidently break mezzes. If the main tank has broken the mezz, hit your assist button and then hit attack.

  • Don't waste time when you are OTM to a raid or at a raid.
    Go to the next Druid or Wizard Ring or to the meeting point ASAP. NEVER meet in Gfay, Karnors or FV. Be patient!

  • If you arrived at the Meeting point, the Group leader in your group will tell all classes and lvls to the Raid leader to make definitve Groups.

  • The Group leader is responsible for the Buffs in his/her Group - NOT the Raid leader. Also the Gruop leader is responsible to gather his people at a seperate point so we see easily where which group is.

  • NEVER ask questions to the Raid Leader! Ask questions in /say or ask your Group. Never hesitate asking questions, always ask. But DO NOT bugg your RAID-Leader. He/She willl be busy like hell anyways.

  • The only persons to bug the RAID Leader are the Group leaders. THey ask questions and at all times report their groups Mana/afk status and such.

  • Take care and read EVERY WORD that the Raid Leader shouts or says. Never bug the RAID-Leader with tells. Do follow his orders in ANY WAY at all times IMMEDIATELY!

  • Try to NEVER go AFK but on places where we rest and buff and medd.

  • Groups have 5 Members. The 6th Spot is available for the Haste/Clarity/Cleris Buffers.

  • Don't ask for useless buffs like Wis/int/Mana. THey just slow down the RAID.

  • Try not to pull or fight if the group is not ready. Be Patient.

  • ALWAYS and at ALL TIMES use /assist. If the Main tank switches targets, he will try to aanounce it, however, push your /assist Hotkey several times a fight to make sure you DO have same target! This is the very most important thing during a RAID!

  • Most damage is done by TANKS! Heals your Tanks and keep them alife. Classes like Paladins are perfect to defend the Enchanters Life - if they are not used as main tanks.

  • The ONLY player to get agro is the Main tank! To have all agro on one person makes heals easier and so buffers and healers can focus on less HP Bars. The Main tank should be supported by several Healers in any way. Once the Tank needs a CH, one healer heals a certain amount of HP to keep the Tank alife during the Cleric casts the CH.

  • Just the Main tank breaks a Mezz! In NO WAY break a mezz if you are not main tank. Period!

  • Root and Stun mobs that have not been mezzed. If a mob is unmezzable or the mezz just failed, you need to prevent the mob from killing your Enchanter. But never make damage in such cases on other mobs than the main mob.

  • NEVER use DOT's in Dungeons!

  • Just Mages and Necros should use Pets. Make your pet attack the mob when its at 75% HP, not earlier!
    Controll your pet at any time. Nothing kills as much people as pets breaking mezzes!

  • Always pull the Mobs off the walls. Rogues need to backstab and walls tend to be buggy.

  • Just Wizards (sometimes Mages) nuke a mob. The others keep your mana for heals. And never nuke a Mob before its HPs went down to at least 70%. Else you get agro too fast and die...

  • At all times defend your Enchanter. Then defend your CLeric and then defend your Main Tank. Everyone else but those 3 is replaceable. Face it, if the Paladin dies and the Chanter lives, he made his job well. Fact.

  • Do not sit when a mob is being pulled, mobs love to hit sitting people and they usually hit you for the max dmg.

  • At all times stay with your group. period.

  • Do not ask for special Mobs or loots.

  • If you get agro or your invisi fades, stand still and die. Do NEVER train your Raid, even if you think they can take the mobs. Yuo kill your Raid. If you die, we rezz you. If we all die, we have a CR of many hours...

  • Try to have a second equipment and weapon at your bank. If we need to make a CR, we have to fight our way in sometimes.

  • EVERYONE needs to have one or several Coffins in the Bank to summon corpses. Also buy several Port Stones.
    Coffins you get in Neriak - Port Stones in Erudin. Ask Guildmembers to buy you some if you are KOS in those cities.

  • Have a CR Plan BEFORE the Raid stars (the Raid Leader).
    Have the Clerics camp if you see that you are gonna be wiped out. Act FAST. Maybe have a COH Mage logged too.

  • If anyone makes a fault or error, do not get pissed or even shout at that person. We all make faults and mistakes and we have to learn out of it.

  • Always be thankful to the people that joined a raid.

  • Always inform your group leader about any important things. If you see additional Mobs, shout ADD, if spells resist, shout it, if you have slowed a mob,shout it, if you have lost speed, shout it and so on...


    Have fun.
    If we all remember those rules and act within those rules, Raids will be fun and a success.


Print out this Guideline and read it carefully every time before you join a raid!
