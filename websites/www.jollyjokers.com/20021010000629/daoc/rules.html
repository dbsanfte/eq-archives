Rules for Royal Guardian Members.

1. You will always try to assist players in your realm.

2. You will act respectfully towards the players, your guild members and the representatives of Mythic (CSRs). Also act respectfully towards the players of both enemy realms. No corpse-jumping, laughing or any disrespectful behavior.

3. You will not set up direct communication links with players from the enemy realm during raids/events (crossteaming).

4. You will not discuss any information received from your guild leader/officer unless you have received the permission to do so by your guild leader/officer.

5. You will not give any internal information to members in- or outside the guild.

6. You are a free person (human being) and can at all times express your feelings or your idea's as long as this is done in a respectfull way.

7. Outside of this game (guild) you are free to do as you please (forums are considered to be outside of this game).

8. You will know your realm back and forth, you are representing a guild that is RvR based and acts as advisors to the entire realm. We expect you to know your own realm and also expect you to have good knowledge of the enemy realms.

9. You are not property of the guild, with this we mean to say you are free to go as you please. However we as a guild have rules and will enforce them as we see fit. Breaking any of the rules as stated in this rules section may result in immediate removal from the guild.

Rules created on February 06, 2002 by Ytrok ChantingWoods.
