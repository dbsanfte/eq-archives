~ The Rules of Conduct of the Legend of the Jokers ~

Introduction

The Legend of the Jokers is a community that is here for eachother, without cutting out any members individuality. We try to share a home for each member, to protect them and to be with them in case of emergency. Most important will always be the fun and joy every member and the guild as a whole, gets out of the game. Every member of the Guild is asked to treat everyone with respect and friendship.
We have two main goals. Be friends and achieve something out there in Norrath.

This Guild would like to reach something. We have goals and work hard to reach our goals. Therefore it is critical that the Guild works together at all times and that everyone helps eachother out. At all times. The needs of the Guild as a whole are the very highest priority and at all times come before the needs of the individual.


Rules of Legend

- You will accept these rules unconditionally when you are a member of this Guild.
- You will follow orders given to you by your Leader and Officers - at all times.
- You do not have to join all Guildevents when online, but we highly recommend and trust your motivation to do so if you are available.
- You will inform your Leader or Officers about your absence if you cannot attend a Guild Event.
- You will treat everyone, even the ones you do not like, with respect.
- You will never use /anon.
- Alts under lvl50 must be on /role.
- You will never intentionally kill-steal, train other players or mess up other players efforts or work.
- You will not be greedy.
- At all times you will act responsible in- and outside Norrath.
- You are not allowed to sell items to Guild members; you either give them for free or sell them to a non-Guild member.
- You will be present at the Guild-Forum at least twice a week and be informed about the most important issues of the Guild.

*Violations of these rules will end in either a warning or an immediate removal from the Guild, depending from the amount of violations and the seriousity of it.

Detailed rules and explenations

Being a Joker:
Your voice counts in any guild decision. This is your guild, treat it as such. Don't be afraid to speak up in support or in contest of anything the guild may do. Your willingness to speak up is your vote. Take advantage of it. If you do not speak up, you do agree in the things as they are. If you have an issues, speak up - if you do not speak up, there is no issue.

Being a Joker requires loyality and presence. Actually being a Joker is a matter of heart. Joker by heart or not.
We will NOT accept you being in another Guild with an alt or even your main char. Being a Joker is a full time job, a matter of attitude, even if ou are not logged in EverQuest, you are still a Joker.

If we find out that you also have a char in another Guild, expect to be taken from the Guild immediately or not to be invited in case you are an applicant.


Applicants:

Requirements for new applicants.

The requirements for applicants inthe Legend of the Jokers have increased drastically over the last time. Reason for it are the fact that we are very full. We have reached a critical size. We want to stay a family and close friends. Therefore it is a need to not become too big. On the other hand we have grown up and are no longer a mid lvl guild.
Generally, our lvl requirement is lvl55.
In special cases (means for classes weare actively looking for), we also may accept new members at lvl50+.

If you want to join the Legend you have to fulfil the following requirements:

- You must be a respected person in the World of Norrath. We will look for feedback from people that had to do with you in the past.

- You must have a certain amount of playing experience. We will not accept members that have powerleveled their chars up to lvl50.

- You must know about Norrath. If you have spent your entire life in exp camps and just know Oasis, Dreadlands, Karnors and Sebilis, then you are not what we are looking for. You need to know about tactics, raids and you must spend a lot of time on information websites to increase your overall knowledge.

- You need to be in EST, GMT or CET timezone. This does not mean you need to live there, but you should play during the primetimes of those zones.

- You need to be online frequently. Several times a week and you should be able to join most weekend raids. So if you work on weekends all the time, we may not be the right guild for you.

For further information, read up the Application requirements topic.

Second Characters:
Second Characters of existing members can always be invited without further notice of the Guild. Please make sure you identify yourself in the case you play on a second character that isn't known in the guild yet.


Raid Rules:
A Raid requires more discipline, more concentration and more Teamwork to be a success, to even work out a little.

We have a Raid Team which splits up all needed tasks to have a successful raid.
This team is made out of several officers. We will announce each officers task during th epreparation of a raid. You will at all times listen and follow to the orders given byone of the members of this raid team.
This raidteam is the tactical officers of the guild, Nimbo, Armain, Iranicus and Taino, as well as other members / officers depending on needs on the single raids.

The Raid Team will give the orders and the Members will follow those.

So there will be a few rules to be added for Raid Situations.

- You HAVE to be at the meeting point (usually the zone in Point) at the time that has been communicated.
- If you are not able to make this, be at a common TP Druid Ring (like WC / NK / BB...) 30-60 minutes in advance so you get a lift to the Meeting point.
- During the raid do in no way act different to what you are asked to do by the Raiding Team. If you are asked to stay in a Room, stay. If you are asked to jump, jump.

If you are late for a raid you can generally still join. But you have to reach the zone on your own and you will not recieve any help other then a CotH link if possible. So check whether we have a CotH link available in advance. If you die on the way, you have to CR yourself. If you join late, expect to not get a group or loot.

If you act against the will of the Raid Leader during a raid, you will be removed from the Guild Event since you just represent a danger for the whole raid. Expect to be removed from the next raid too, in case you repeatly risk lifes of others by your actions.

Look up the details on Raid rules in the Special Raid Rules Section.

Loot Rules
Loot at Guild Raids:

Loot will be assigned!
Taino has the exclusive right to assign loot or to exclude members from lottoing. If Taino assigns loot to someone in the guild, the loot is assigned, no further discussion. Same goes for excluding or enabeling someone from lotto.

If an item will not be assigned to a specific person, the members that can and will use the item will lotto for it.
We will lotto need before greed.
This means that the persons that CAN use an item and that WILL use an item, will lotto for the dropped item. Period.
We trust your honesty that you will step back from a lotto in case an item is not really an update for you and let those members roll that really need it.

Loot within pickup Raids or Groups:
We still random need before greed if possible. However in normal pick up groups we encourage simple rolling - or find a suitable solution within the group. This is not guild business and is up to everyones feeling. Still, do not show greedyness during such situations since everytime you group with people ooutside the guild, you represent the Jokers and we want to stress a good reputation within the people of Norrath.

Hand downs:
We strongly encourage you to hand down your loot from Guildraids. This is not a rule, so you are not forced to, but we strongly want to encourage you to remember who helped you to get some loot - the Guild.
If you aquire an update to a former item, looted at a Guildraid, then you should pass down your item that you do not need anymore, to a Guildmember that can use it.

Guildstore:
There is a Guildstore for the Members of the Guild to hand out.
Equipment to members in need. Read up the Store funciton and rules at the Joker Store Forum.
