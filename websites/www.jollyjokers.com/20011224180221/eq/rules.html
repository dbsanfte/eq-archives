~ Guild Rules ~
~ of the Legend of the Jokers ~


The Legend of the Jokers is a community that is here for eachother, without cutting any members individuality. We try to share a home for each member, to protect them and to be with them in case of emergency. Most important will always be the fun and joy every member and the guild as a whole, gets out of the game. Every member of the Guild is asked to treat everyone with respect and friendship.
We have two main goals. Be friends and achieve something out there in Norrath.

This Guild would like to reach something. We have goals and work hard to reach our goals. Therefore it is critical that the Guild works together at all times and that everyone helps eachother out. At all times. The needs of the Guild as a whole are the very highest priority and at all times come before the needs of the individual. Our explicit goals are to reach a lvl55-60 averaged Guild. We want to be able to hit most Mobs in Norrath, but not all. We will never be a so called Uberguild, but we will be able keep up with the very most.


Rules of Legend

You will accept these rules unconditionally when you are a member of this Guild.

You will follow orders given to you by your Leader and Officers - at all times.

You will post your absence if you cannot attend a Guild Event.

You will treat everyone, even the ones you do not like, with respect.

You will never intentionally kill-steal, train other players or mess up other players efforts or work.

You will not be greedy.

At all times you will act responsible in- and outside Norrath.

You are not allowed to sell items to Guild members; you either give them for free or sell them to a non-Guild member.

You will read the forum as much as possible (at least 3 times a week) and be informed about the issues of the Guild.

You will register at the Guildroster at eq.guildmagic.com.

You will not be in /anon mode ever. If you really wish to have your peace, use /role.

*Violations of these rules will end in either a warning or an immediate removal from the Guild, depending from the amount of violations and the seriousity of it.


Detailed rules and explenations

Being a Joker:

Your voice counts in any guild decision. This is your guild, treat it as such. Don't be afraid to speak up in support or in contest of anything the guild may do. Your willingness to speak up is your vote. Take advantage of it. If you do not speak up, you do agree in the things as they are. If you have an issues, speak up - if you do not speak up, there is no issue.

Being a Joker requires loyality and presence. Actually being a Joker is a matter of heart. Joker by heart or not.
We will NOT accept you being in another Guild with an alt or even your main char. Being a Joker is a full time job, a matter of attitude, even if ou are not logged in EverQuest, you are still a Joker.
If we find out that you also have a char in another Guild, expect to be taken from the Guild immediately or not to be invited in case you are an applicant.


Applicants:

The Legend of the Jokers have a lvlcap of lvl40 for all Applicants. We focus on applicants on our average lvlrange and higher, means lvl50+ at this moment. However, every player at lvl40+ is allowed to apply.
Wolk is the recruiting officer.
A new applicant has to talk to him.
The applicant has to post about the application on the forum after having read and agreeing to live by these rules.
The applicant has to name his/her sponsor, if there is any, so the recruiting officer can talk to the sponsor and get feedback.
The ONLY persons to decide about a new applicant is Wolk. There are times other Officers can make that decision. No other votes are needed. However the whole guild has a right and is asked to give feedback and comments about a Temp Tagged recruit if they want. Those comments will have a strong influence in the final decision of making a Temp Tag a Full Member. Taino and all other Officers got a Veto Right on every new applicant - just in case.


Once you are invited as a Temp Tagged recruit:

You will gain access to the Forums, to the Jolly~JoKeRs Newsgroup and will be a member of the Guild as much as a full member of the Jolly~JoKeRs Foundation except you will not qualify for getting items from the Joker store or receive Phat Loot from raids. A rare exception may be made based on the decision of an Officer. As a temp tagged member you are asked to show presence as a normal guild member in any way. Presence on the guildforum, register at the guildroster and be present at raids and events.

Temp tagged Guildmembers are on a 30 days trial. During those 30 days they will be tested and be observed to find out whether they fit in the guild. This is simply done to make sure we can aquire the most motivated and the most suitable persons in the Guild. There is no use in having people in the guild that do not meet our requirements, it would even do bad for both sides. Once you made it through the trial time, you will become a full member, a Legend.

Second Characters:

Second Characters of existing members can always be invited without further notice of the Guild. Please make sure you identify yourself in the case you play on a second character that isn't known in the guild yet.

We strongly encourage the Guildmembers themselfes to bring up ideas about raids and participate in organisation of raids. The more efforts the Guildmembers show themselfes, the more good raids we will have.


Raid Rules:

A Raid requires more discipline, more concentration and more Teamwork to be a success, to even work out a little.
So there will be a few rules to be added for Raid Situations.

You have to be at the meeting point (usually the zone in point) at the time that has been communicated.

If you are not able to make this, be at a common TP Druid Ring / Wizard Spire (like WC / NK / BB...) 1 hour in advance so you get a lift to the Meeting point. If you are NOT present at the Meeting point at the time given, you will not participate in the Raid!

During the raid do in no way act different to what you are asked to do by the Raid Leader. If you are asked to stay in a Room, stay. If you are asked to jump, jump. If you act different, if you bring your raid in danger, if you mouth, are greedy, are simply being a pain in the ass - you will immediately be removed from the raid. Either you get a TL out, or in case this is not possible, you will simply be excluded and you can fight your way out on yourself. People that are not able to support raids and act responsible do not have a place in our realms.

Do not send your Raid Leader / Guild Leader any tells. They are busy! Any comments other then Raid related issues are not allowed since they stress the Raid Leader / Guild Leader. After the raid they turn back in human beings and are available for Chit Chat.

If you act against the will of the Raid Leader during a raid, you will be removed from the Guild Event since you just represent a danger for the whole raid. Expect to be removed from the next raid too, in case you repeatly risk lifes of others by your actions.
Look up the details on Raid rules in the Raid Rules Section. Those raid rules are part of the Guild Rules!

Loot Rules
Loot at Guild Raids:

Loot will be assigned!
Taino has the exclusive right to assign loot or to exclude members from lottoing. If Taino assigns loot to someone in the guild, the loot is assigned, no further discussion. Same goes for excluding or enabeling someone from lotto.

If an item will not be assigned to a specific person, the members that can and will use the item will lotto for it.
We will lotto need before greed.
This means that the persons that CAN use an item and that WILL use an item, will lotto for the dropped item. Period.
We trust your honesty that you will step back from a lotto in case an item is not really an update for you and let those members roll that really need it.

Comments like: "Whoa cool item, I could use this" "Nice! I have been trying to get this item for months" "This is definately a Ranger/Monk/Wizard (enter your own class) Item!" will not just result in you specifically NOT get the item, those comments are also considered being selfish and greedy, trying to convince Taino about you needing this loot, being sneaky and trying to bring up a reason why YOU deserve it.
This will not do any good to you. It will even put you back in considerations for loot. You sure are allowed to be happy about loot that drops and you should be happy for other people to get loot, but trying to make "remarks" or even worse, send tells about how good you could use loot, will result in Taino sure offering loot to another person.

This system requires trust. Taino is aware of the sensitivity of his position. He tries very hard to assign loot the most appropriate way, to assign loot to the persons that can use an item the most and that deserve it the most.
There is no sense at all in giving loot to someone that has not earned it. If you do not get loot that you expected to get, ask yourself whether you truly deserve the loot, whether you have done enough for the guild, whether you helpeed enough, were present enough and are an old enough member to recieve a specific item.
If you still think you (or someone else) deserved the loot more then the person that actually recieved it - please contact Nimbo and Taino about it. Taino is not perfect and with all efforts and good will, he makes mistakes. If you believe that there has been made a mistake, please conact those persons and we will talk it out, either explain why the assignements have been done that way, or then agree in the mistake and fix it.
At all times be aware that mistakes to not happen out of evilness or personal preferences. They happen because of misinformation or by accident.

Loot within pickup Raids or Groups:

We still random need before greed if possible. However in normal pick up groups we encourage simple rolling - or find a suitable solution within the group. This is not guild business and is up to everyones feeling. Still, do not show greedyness during such situations since everytime you group with people ooutside the guild, you represent the Jokers and we want to stress a good reputation within the people of Norrath.

Quest Gems:

As full members, you are expected to GIVE your Velious Quest Gems to the Guild. THis is done to be able to offer Quest gems at all times needed. It is an organisational issue. Hardly anyone just got the right Gems available when recieving a certain Quest piece. By collecting them at one point, we will have most Gems available at all times.
At the moment, Taino is holding all gems for the guild. please give your Quest gems to Taino.

Hand downs:

We strongly encourage you to hand down your loot from Guildraids. This is not a rule, so you are not forced to, but we strongly want to encourage you to remember who helped you to get some loot - the Guild.

If you aquire an update to a former item, looted at a Guildraid, then you should pass down your item that you do not need anymore, to a Guildmember that can use it.

Guildstore:

There is a Guildstore for the Members of the Guild to hand out Equipment to members in need. Read up the Store funciton and rules at the Joker Store Forum.
