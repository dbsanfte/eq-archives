Introduction

ntroduction The Legend of the Jokers is a community that is here for eachother, without cutting cut any members individuality. We try to share a home for each member, to protect them and to be with them in case of emergency. Most important will always be the fun and joy every member and the guild as a whole, gets out of the game. Every member of the Guild is asked to treat everyone with respect and friendship. We have two main goals. Be friends and achieve something out there in Norrath.

This Guild would like to reach something. We have goals and work hard to reach our goals. Therefore it is critical that the Guild works together at all times and that everyone helps eachother out. At all times. The needs of the Guild as a whole are the very highest priority and at all times come before the needs of the individual.

Basic Rules

- You will accept these rules unconditionally when you are a member of this Guild.
- You will follow orders given to you by your Leader and Officers - at all times.
- You will join all Guild Events if you are online - as long as you are of a high enough lvl.
- You will inform your Leader or Officers about your absence if you cannot attend a Guild Event.
- You will treat everyone, even the ones you do not like, with respect.
- You will not use anon unless requested by your Officers or Leader, if you wish to be anon you will use /role-play.
- You will never intentionally kill-steal, train other players or mess up other players efforts or work.
- You will not be greedy.
- At all times you will act responsible in- and outside Norrath.
- You are not allowed to sell items to Guild members; you either give them for free or sell them to a non-Guild member.
- You will be present at the Guild-Forum at least twice a week and be informed about the most important issues of the Guild.

*Violations of these rules will end in either a warning or an immediate removal from the Guild, depending from the amount of violations and the seriousity of it.

Detailed rules and explenations.

Being a Joker:


Your voice counts in any guild decision. This is your guild, treat it as such. Don't be afraid to speak up in support or in contest of anything the guild may do. Your willingness to speak up is your vote. Take advantage of it. If you do not speak up, you do agree in the things as they are. If you have an issues, speak up - if you do not speak up, there is no issue.

Being a Joker requires loyality and presence. Actually being a Joker is a matter of heart. Joker by heart or not.
We not accept you being in another Guild with an alt or even your main char. Being a Joker is a full time job, a matter of attitude, even if ou are not logged in EverQuest, you are still a Joker.
If we find out that you also have a char in another Guild, expect to be taken from the Guild immediately or not to be invited in case you are an applicant.


Applicants:

The Legend of the Jokers have a lvlcap of lvl30 for all Applicants. We focus on applicants on our average lvlrange and higher, means lvl40-45+ at this moment. However, every player at lvl30+ is allowed to apply.

The applicant has to post about the application on the forum.
The applicant has to name his/her sponsor, if there is any, so the recruiting officers can talk to the sponsor and get an idea.
The ONLY persons to decide about a new applicant are Nadera and Shamax. NO other votes are needed. However the whole guild has a right and is asked to give feedback and comments about an applicant if they want. Those comments will have a strong influence in the Vote.

Taino and all other Officers got a Veto Right on every new applicant - just in case.
Once you are invited:
- You will gain access to the Forums, to the Jolly~JoKeRs Newsgroup and will be a member of the Guild as much as a full member of the Jolly~JoKeRs Foundation.

 

Second Characters:

Second Characters of existing members can always be invited without further notice of the Guild.
Please make sure you identify yourself in the case you play on a second character that isn't known in the Guild yet.

Events:

We will try to have one Event (at least) per weekend.
Raid leader is Najina from now on with support from Andarial. Shamax is responsible for the raids within the Leaadership, but he has more of a controll and advice function, than an executive function.
We strongly encourage the Guildmembers themselfes to bring up ideas about raids and participate in organisation of raids. The more efforts the Guildmembers show themselfes, the more good raids we will have. But Najina is in the lead and no official raid will be hold without her knowledge. Sure everyone can at every time make raids on their own, they will then just not be official. Also if you want to make raids on your own, we strongly suggest to team up with Najina for help and advice.
Please be aware that if you want a raid for your personal profit, you will have to work for it and organise things (together with Najina), if you don't, your raid may not take place for a long time since the Event organizers make raids for the good of the whole guild.


Raid Rules:


A Raid requires more discipline, more concentration and more Teamwork to be a success, to even work out a little.
So there will be a few rules to be added for Raid Situations.
- You will confirm your presence at a raid. Every member is asked to join a raid if its within his/her level range. If you cannot make it to such a raid, inform the leaders about your absence.
- You HAVE to be at the meeting point (usually the zone in Point) at the time that has been communicated
- If you are not able to make this, be at a common TP Druid Ring (like WC / NK / BB...) 1 hour in advance so you get a lift to the Meeting point. If you are NOT present at the Meeting point at the time given, you will NOT participate in the Raid!
- During the raid do in no way act different to what you are asked to do by the Raid Leader. If you are asked to stay in a Room, stay. If you are asked to jump, jump.
- If you join a Raid and are not within the appropriate level range, expect to not get experience, loot or expect to die very fast and not get a rezz or a CR before the event ends. This does not have to be the case, but expect it.

If you are not in time, you will not join the raid. If you confirm your presence and do not show up, you will be removed from the next raid (excludes very good excuses of course).
Exceptions may be made in case your presence will be critical for the raid itself. Then you stil may get asked to join the raid.
If you act against the will of the Raid Leader during a raid, you will be removed from the Guild Event since you just represent a danger for the whole raid. Expect to be removed from the next raid too, in case you repeatly risk lifes of others by your actions.

Look up the details on Raid rules in the Special Raid Rules Section.


Loot Rules


Loot at Guild Raids:

Loot will be assigned!
Taino has the exclusive right to assign loot or to exclude members from lottoing. If Taino assigns loot to someone in the guild, the loot is assigned, no further discussion. Same goes for excluding ôr enabeling someone from lotto.

If an item will not be assigned to a specific person, the members that can and will use the item will lotto for it.
We will lotto need before greed.
This means that the persons that CAN use an item and that WILL use an item, will lotto for the dropped item. Period.
We trust your honesty that you will step back from a lotto in case an item is not really an update for you and let those members roll that really need it.


Loot within pickup Raids or Groups:
We still random need before greed if possible. However in normal pick up groups we encourage simple rolling - or find a suitable solution within the group. This is not guild business and is up to everyones feeling. Still, do not show greedyness during such situations since everytime you group with people outside the guild, you represent the Jokers and we want to stress a good reputation within the people of Norrath.

Hand downs:
We strongly encourage you to hand down your loot from Guildraids. This is not a rule, so you are not forced to, but we strongly want to encourage you to remember who helped you to get some loot - the Guild.
If you aquire an update to a former item, looted at a Guildraid, then you should pass down your item that you do not need anymore, to a Guildmember that can use it.
