8/11/00

For those who were not able to attend, we held a guild meeting in game on Sunday night, and plan to have future meetings the first Sunday of each month. We covered a lot of ground in this weeks meeting, but to recap the high points:
 
  • Recruiting: Everyone should be on the lookout for new members. If you meet someone you like, talk to them about joining. If they are interested, contact an officer to have them invited into the guild. New Initiates should be encouraged to visit the site asap in order to be added to the roster. We particularly need wizards and druids at the moment.
  • Apprenticeships: We will continue the Mentor/Apprentice plan, but only for those interested. If a member wishes to take an Initiate as an apprentice, the initiate will be promoted to Bloodpawn upon completion of the quest(s) given by the mentor. New Initiates who do not elect to take an initiation quest will only reach Bloodpawn with time, as they become known to the members of the guild.
  • Guildwar: Kreate (and others) will be looking for a suitable opponent.
  • We will be having a guild event Sunday, August 13. We will be raiding N Freeport, slaughtering paladins as long as it amuses us.
  • The Roster has been updated. Please check and make sure your information is correct, and post any changes on the Roster message board.
  • Arachnis has added an equipment message board. Everyone is encouraged to post the items you are looking for, and the items you have to give away. If you have something you are planning to sell (something really nice that you cant afford to give to another member) go ahead and put it here too, with the discounted guild price, so brothers get the opportunity to buy it before you sell it to someone else.
  • Mordus is working on a big facelift for the site... no timetable now, but expect to see changes in the future.
  • We need someone to master the major trade skills, so that we can better outfit our members with banded, jewelry, potions, etc. I will help finance anyone who is interested, hopefully other high levels will make contributions to this effort as well. If anyone is willing to put in the time to work on trade skills, please post on the message board.
  • We are also looking for someone to fill the role of Loremaster. The Loremaster will be responsible for seacrhing the spoiler sites for game related information, particularly quest info. Anyone interested, please post.
Thats all for this meeting, stay tuned for future events. Good Hunting!

In the Master's Name,

Charoth Bloodoath
Patriarch of Oblivion
