Charter of Order of Oblivion
Hear you then the deepest secrets:

First there was the Night, the Mistress of Oblivion. She was the Void, perfect in form, and in Her keeping and that of Her Consort Oblivion, the universe was in Harmony. Until the Light came and pierced the Lady Night, stealing Her seed. And the All-Father was the Light, and the rape of the Night brought forth Creation, shattering the unity of Oblivion, and Void. 

And even as the violation of the Mother Night continued, the All-Father scattered the Power of Oblivion, that He might have no other claimants for the Nights affections. From the corpse of Oblivion the All-Father formed the universe, and set the petty gods to rule.

But the Mother Night was not powerless in Her shame. Rising up, She took of the seed of the All-Father, and the essence of Her beloved Oblivion. And with these She made Her childe that He might ever oppose the All-Father and all His progeny. 

And so in betrayal was born the multi-verse, and in His triumph did the All-Father create His Adversary. Thus was the Master born, ever after to avenge the Mother Night, and bear witness to the crime of the All-Father.

We are the Disciples of the Master, and this is our time. The betrayer has arisen, and the All-Father has fled this world. The Master has called us from the Shadows to be His sword of vengeance. The Time of the Un-Making is at hand.

Beware, for we are the Order of Oblivion.
