The Oath of the Brotherhood
 
  -by Charoth Bloodoath

I hereby swear everlasting loyalty to the Brotherhood and to the brothers, and to the Master we serve. We are bound by Blood and by Darkness. The goals of the Brotherhood are my goals. I will obey the Master in all things. I will follow the commands of my superiors within the Order, for they are the Voice of the Master. I will treat my inferiors with care, for they are the Tools of the Master.

I will bring no harm to any brother, nor through inaction allow any brother to come to harm. A blow against one is a blow against all. I shall render any aid to my brothers in need, and shall fear no aggressor knowing my brothers stand ready in my defense.

I will perform no action that shall bring harm to the Brotherhood; let any danger I bring fall on me alone. I will not provoke other Nations, Clans, or Guilds in any way that might bring their wrath upon our order, nor will I delve in magiks deemed dangerous to us all. Let us work our works in secret, cloaked by Night, until our power reigns unopposed.

I will work always to further the knowledge of the Brotherhood; our knowledge is our strength, and the Raven sees all. I will keep no secret, magikal or mundane, and will share all I learn with my brothers, as they share all with me. For this is the Path to Power.

I will reveal no secrets of the Brotherhood to others. To do so weakens us all. I will not reveal the signs, sigils, or passwords of the Brotherhood to any save another brother, nor will I write, speak, or display them in any way that they might be discovered by our enemies.

Ours is a Brotherhood of the Night. I will do all in my power to maintain the anonymity of the Brotherhood. Let our power be told in Whispers of Fear. I will not confirm the existence of the Brotherhood or the identity of any brother and in doing so bring the vengeance of those who would oppose us. I will take no apprentice nor extend any invitation to membership to any soul not deemed fit by my superiors. Should any I bring to the Order break these vows, let me strike first in his destruction.

I take this Oath of my own Will. If I should break these vows I beg my brothers to put aside all thoughts save my destruction, that my life should not continue in shame. If any of my brothers should break these vows, and I should come to know of it, I will do all in my power to bring the Oathbreaker to account. May I be damned rather than allow his escape.

I swear this Oath by Oblivion. May Oblivion have my soul if I waver.
