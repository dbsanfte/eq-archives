    Passwords

    Posted by Arachnis on Thursday, July 12 @ 17:39:21 EDT (4 reads)

    Please note that about 70% of the guild has access to all portions of the site now. If you do NOT have your passwords needed for access, before mailing me, please (as your main character) ask the password over guild chat. Most people know it.

    If you click on the link, Roster of Members, under the Community tab in the floating menu and do not see yourself, please note you are not signed up on the roster. As the officers will be using it for points and loot allocation, this will mean that you will be passed over when they are coordinating their efforts. There are quite a few members to keep track of, please assist your officers in this endeavor by providing them the tools they need to do their jobs.. Your information.

    Signing up for the roster should be very straight forward, simply enter your name, and your email.Note: you are NOT done yet. Next go check your email box and retrieve the password. Write this down in a safe place! Use this password to log in. Next under the Member Control Center click on Edit your Info. From there type in you Class and your Level. That is all that is required, you can fill in more, or not as you please. It only takes a minute or two, so please be sure you take care of this ASAP to be considered for loot or points.
