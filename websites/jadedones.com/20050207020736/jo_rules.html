Guild Rules

Our rules are simple:

  • Be a nice person
  • Be honorable and have a sense of personal integrity
  • Enjoy the game and all that it offers, not just the gear and loot part
  • Don't be a loot pig
  • No whining
  • Have a helpful nature
  • Do not engage in verbal debates in public chat channels.  Take it to tells.

If the officers feel that you are acting in a manner that is contrary to these rules, you will be confronted about it.  They are here to ensure a pleasant environment for all of our members.

Remember that what you do reflects on all of us.  People may not remember the name of someone who made them angry, but they almost always remember the name of the guild.  Please help keep us as one of those guilds people seek first when looking for people to fill a group or help on a raid. 

We value our reputation as nice people.  Please do your part to uphold the honor and integrity of the guild.
