Rules

General Rules

  • No whining!
  • Trade skills are encouraged. 
  • Leaders are not here to hold your hand or tell you what to do every day when you logon. We will use the MOTD to indicate if we have any special plans happening. 
  • If you need help with something, please ask. Don't expect members to be mind readers. Members will attempt to help to the best of their abilities given the circumstances at the time. 
  • Help is a two way street. 

Regarding Loot

  • Need before greed. 
  • Awarded to participants (the character you're actively playing) first before alts are considered.

Rules are subject to change or modification as issues arise and are put forth to the leadership of the guild.
