Joining The Guild

  • If you are interested in joining our guild please approach one of our members and let them know. They will forward your name to the Guild Leaders who will then make an effort to group with you and evaluate your play and determine if you would be a good fit with our Guild.
  • Subject to unanimous approval of leaders. 
  • All members can be subject to guild remove if the circumstances warrant it. 
  • Public flaming of members or of others is not tolerated. You are expected to handle any problems with dignity. 
  • Membership will never be restricted by level, class or race. It will be dependent on the person playing the character
