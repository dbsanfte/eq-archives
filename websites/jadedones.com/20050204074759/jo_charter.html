Guild Charter

Jaded Ones is dedicated to providing a place for people who are fed up with the petty politics and silly bickering often found in a lot of guilds today to gather under a common banner and help each other with our individual in-game goals.  We try to keep the rules and processes simple and straight forward.  Our members are dedicated to the principle that we are here to have fun!  We understand and place high value on the fact that different people have different goals.

  • We don't expect you to play a minimum number of hours per week.
  • We don't require that you have any minimum level or gear to join.
  • We don't require you to attend every raid (or even any raid if you don't want to).
  • We don't have complicated loot rules (we use "Need Before Greed" and /random as appropriate).

All we require is that you be a nice person and have a strong desire to simply have fun!

The White Rose

The White Rose has been used by a number of organizations as their symbol to represent their struggle against tyranny and oppression.  For us it is a symbol of our steadfast determination to not succumb to the pressures that this game brings to bear on a person's sense of what is right and good.  We do not tolerate lame behavior in ourselves and it disgusts us when we see it in others.  There are those who, for some reason, seem to believe that because the game allows a particular behavior, regardless of how reprehensibly immoral and dishonorable it might be, that it is somehow acceptable; that if it is truly wrong, the game would prevent it in some way.  We do not subscribe to this philosophy.

We believe that a person should listen to that inner voice when it chimes the chorus of a hundred mothers' voices guiding us to do the right thing.  The words of our grandparents and respected aunts and uncles should be a clarion call to our spirit, helping us to stay the course and bolstering our confidence that we can and will let our feet fall upon the path of virtue.

The White Rose symbolizes our resolve to the ideal that it is our sense of personal integrity and honor that defines the true nature of who we are.
