Guilty Pleasures is a family type guild. As friends we strive to help others
as well as our family. Loyalty and having fun are the main goals of our guild.
Our family is well recognized amongst other guilds as our alliances will show.
We may be small but we are close as friends, and will come to the aid of anyone
in need. No race or class will be denied the chance to join us.

Membership Raid Rules

Rules
AttitudeTreat others as if you wish to be treated. No cursing or trying to cause fights in a public channel or putting someone down in /ooc. If you need to fight with someone please keep it to tells. Do not give the guild a bad name or upset others in the guild.
ArgumentsArguments between members should be brought to the Attention of an officer, arguing in guild chat is not recommended. If you have an argument with another player outside of the guild, try to resolve the issue in a peaceful manner. If things cannot be resolved, contact an officer for assistance.
Kill Stealing / Ninja LootingIf any member is caught, they will be given a warning. If a member has been reported of KS'ing or Ninja Looting several times, a vote will be taken of all Officers on course of action.
Begging There is no begging for items. There is a Vault of items donated to the guild as well, any available member will gladly help camp items. During Guild Events items will be given out also.
Twinking There is nothing against twinking. Don't brag about being a twink.
Anonymous Command You are not to be /anon. If you want to keep your information hidden use the /roleplay command instead. That command will keep all information unavailable, except your name and guild.
Power Leveling We will try to help you power level. If someone has the time and isn't doing anything else then he/she will help level your character. Constant begging to be power leveled will result in a warning. The next action will be a vote of all Officers on course of action.
Level Requirements There is no level requirerments to join the guild, everyone is treated equaly. There may be times when we are recruiting a certain level to even the guild's average level. Friends of existing members are welcome to talk to the Leader or Officers about joining.
Other Characters All existing members of the guild are welcome to get an invite for any of their other characters if they so choose. They also will be recorded for the members page.
Guild Events All members must attend all guild events that are posted in the Events Forum. There will be arrangements made to aid you to get to the location of the event. Acceptable reasons not to be at an event:
1. You are not in the level range of the event.
2. You are not online.
3. Transportation was unavailabe.
4. Prior Engagement.
