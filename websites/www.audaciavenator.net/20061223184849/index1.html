October 28th, 2006 - AV > GoD

So another expansion falls to AV as we downed GoD's final head honcho to cap off the most impressive display of pure and total zone-and-everything-in-it ownage AV has ever done. I think most GL/RL's will agree, GoD is the most satisfying one to put under your belt.

Last night we went in to Tacvi with one goal, Tunat's head on a platter. I fully expected a wipe or two because I had always heard this event was pretty crazy, but in the back of my mind I was thinking how nice it would be to first shot this bitch. Perhaps it was the hype surrounding the entire run so far with us steamrolling the zone, or maybe it was the extra time we took setting up or maybe it's what I think it was.. we just kick that much ass.

So here we are, staring down the head cheese.

We engaged, commenced the beatdown and within minutes he was dead! There was much rejoicing.. until...

WTF Mate?!?! This bitch is still alive, it was a fakeout! Just like the entire expansion the final encounter was no different, it was a spinetwisting drama of deception and lies. Would it ever end?? Fuckin A it would. We had enough of this bitches bullshit. Ikkinz 3 class kill golems! Ikkinz 4 instance turn in NPC location! Uqua! Stonemites! Noquipunkass! Zun`Muram PITA! With every reason we beat his punk ass a little more. Tunat'Muram Cuu Vauax was dominated in ways that will be spoken about in hushed tones for eons to come.

October 25th, 2006 - Tacvi Day 2/3

Day 2 in Tacvi resulted in 2 more first shot kills, Pixtt Riel Tavas and Zun`Muram Shaldn Boc. We faced off against Pirik and did very well with a 32% run on our first go. Imo it's the most difficult one of all the fights before Tunat because so much can change very quickly in it.

So last night we headed back in because:

a) we still had shit left to kill in zone
and
b) our timers for Pixtt Xxeric Kext and Pixtt Kretv Krakxt were gone. So the gangs all there minus Tavas and Boc, god these names are going to kill me, and we let loose the beast.

Xxeric went down easy.. again, more loot obtained. Next up was a Pirik rematch. Excellent execution and precise DPS control netted us a big win. Very clean fight.

Next up was Yhist Vor, killed first shot. Followed by another Kret V. Krak which was alot cleaner this time and finally, we meet Zun`Muram Mordl Delt. Very interesting event and alot of fun imo but more importantly ...Killed first shot.

Overall I am very happy with our first visit to Tacvi since we have cleared everything but Tunat. Of the 7 named pre Tunat we first shot killed all but 1, damn you Pirik! I look forward to getting into it with Tunat, I hear he is not pleased with AV /grin, get in line fucker.

I have no screenshots for the rest because for some reason my screenshot files are corrupting. Just imagine a dead snake and a couple dead Zun models.

More to come.

October 21st, 2006 - AV + Tacvi = $

Ahhh yes, it's that time again, so here is a fun filled update detailing the pimptastic adventures of the guild you all love to hate.. hate to love... whatever. And while I am talking about pimptastic, I would like to congratulate Empire of the Forgotten Noble Blade on their recent victories. It's always nice to see a couple old school guilds merge.

As always we have been busy doing what we do best, gearing up and moving forward. Refining our farms to the point we bulldoze them like a tidal wave. Stand strong all you want bitches, but when we hit you just close your eyes. All but OMM in Anguish killed just about flawlessly in under 3 hours, Yar`Lir, Kess and Rikki are killed in an hour. Backflagging is a joke with Uqua in 1:47, Ikkinz 4 in 45 mins.. takes us longer to get there then it does to run through it. Inktuta in 2 hours and thats not really pushing. Noq in 9mins. DPS is scary and execution is brutal. After 2 and half years of playing together, low turn around and being able to retain lots of raid experience in our crew we just get tighter and tighter. With 1,700+ raids under our belt anticipating what eachother will do is second nature to us. I couldn't be happier with this guild.

Every major key/flag based expansion has a cockblock mob, Luclin has Emp, PoP has Fishy, OoW has Foreskin, DoN has... uhm.. ok DoN doesn't count.. DoDH has Sendai etc etc. Which brings me to GoD and Zun`Muram Tkarish Zyk a.k.a Zun`Muram Pita the Cockblock God of GoD. This event sucks for one reason and one reason only, something I like to call the luck factor. Any event that makes you rely on a RNG component for victory is re-atarded with a capitol R.

Our previous run a couple weeks ago went perfect, we had it under control faster then 40 pimps in a room with 3 hookers, however we ended up losing. Why? Because the RNG would not give us what we needed. We held out a long time but due to the nature of the event you can only last so long before you run out of juice and that is exactly what happened we just ran out of everything. It's frustrating because you can lose regardless of how well you are doing if you don't get lucky.

Despite this event favoring healer/tank heavy guilds which AV has never been we stomped a mudhole in Zun's ass. We went in this week and dominated Zun and his two bitches, Lady Luck smiled upon us and we got ourselves an all access pass to Tacvi.

Since it didn't take us long to get to this point in the night we decided to head into Tacvi to play around.

We buffed up and zoned in to Time 2.0 and hit up the first trash mob. All I can say is lol. Imagine being the first guild in the zone, pre run 8 at lvl 65 and pulling this monster to the raid. Wonder how many pullers died before they just said fuck it and sent in a defensive tank to get it to camp. Needless to say shit hits hard and has a fair amount of HP, it's like killing reparm in fire as a trash mob. So far, it's pretty fun. The encounters are exciting, despite having fucked up names, since they have so much going on and the first fight is no exception. Meet Pixtt Xxeric Kext, hi!

Killed first shot. Next up Pixtt Kretv Krakxt, besides having a personality disorder she is an alright snake and also dead first shot.

So, our first adventure into Tacvi results in 2 dead minis and 0 wipes, not bad. I look forward to coming back next week. Till next update.
