August 30th, 2004 - Vyzh`Dra the Cursed
Tonight, with the server going down, we got another shot at Cursed. Redemption was the only thing on our minds. Cursed slipped away from us the night before due to a small timing error. The thought of waiting nearly 16 weeks for us to get another shot tore at me all day. We contacted the appropriate people, made our request and within minutes the "Goin Green" signal was called.

We gathered in the basement and started the TM slaughter. Within 12 minutes, all TM's and Warden were dead. At 18 mins the Rhoz's were down. At 20 mins, we pulled the lootless Glyphed and beat him down flawless. 0 Deaths made it possible to pull Exiled within 3 minutes of his Spawn. Exiled was pulled, gogo Laeadern for taking the first DT! Exiled was then suckered into our fight spot and the beatdown began. Exiled lived just over 4 mins, another flawless win. 28 Mins in to the cycle Cursed was popped.

Within 5 minutes of his spawn Cursed was pulled and this time, the fight was very different. We were playing by OUR rules. Everything was clicking, it was just a matter of time.

The fight lasted just about 30 minutes. Not once did we get even close to losing the MT. Cursed was controlled perfectly, he was in OUR house tonight. Who's house???


OUR HOUSE! Cursed owned flawlessly. Gratz AV on another rotation!
