Audacia Venator's mission is simple, kill shit. And kill lots of it. Anything that moves, slithers or flys with funny animations is a target. We want end game. Ele's, Time, GoD, nothing is sacred. If it has something we want it's on our hitlist. We will not be a zerg guild or a guild of asses. We are one. Our members come first.

We are dedicated to reaching the end goal which is to be a top end raiding guild. We have the knowledge and experience to kill every event to get to plane of time. All we need are the people to make it happen. If you would like to be a part of a guild that has goals and a raid leader capable of leading a guild to time, then AV is where you should be. If you like to kill mobs with less people than typical guilds, again I say, AV is where you should be.

Where some guilds will use 35 to kill a mob, we will use 20. We use tight strats and perfect execution as our weapons for success. We don't give up when the going gets tough. We tighten up and stick with it until we win. We need dedicated raiders who want to be part of an elite raiding force who will not whine or complain when we wipe, people who will take the defeat as a lesson and use it to assure a victory on the next attempt.

When I say "elite" I mean it in the sense we will not be elite in attitude, we will be elite in action. We strive to be the best at our classes so we can kill mobs with less numbers than anyone. If these things hold true for you and what you want out of EQ then you know what to do, contact a recruit officer or leader and let them know you want to be a part of Audacia Venator.

 

 

Behavior Inside the Guild:

Remember at all times the Golden Rule. Your guildmates are your friends, your brothers and sisters, your heroes, your mentors, the people that fight at your side. A slander against your guildmate is a slander unto yourself.

We are a guild of adults with full-time jobs, families, and personal lives. Try to be considerate of your guildmate's play time. If you need something done, let people know and something can easily be worked out. However, sometimes people are going to have their own things to do and need to be allowed time time to do so.

While we do not support nor discourage power-leveling, please do so with discretion. DO NOT bother your guildmates for such assistance.

We, the leaders, maintain an "Open door" policy. This means if you have any comments or suggestions send a tell to Revicus or Ailish and we will do our best to work out whatever issue you are having. If we can't be reached contact an officer and they will do the same.

Raiding:

During raids you are EXPECTED to do as you are told. If this means that you have to cut down on chatter in order to read what the raid leader has to say, then so be it. Please use your common sense. Complaining publicly will not be tolerated. Please save your comments and suggestions until after the raid is over. DO NOT send tells to those in charge of a raid unless you are asked to do so. Please look over the Raid Guidelines for more details on normal raid behavior.

Upon Leaving Audacia Venator

If you leave Audacia Venator, please inform a leader or officer and do so quietly. Posts to the message board made in anger will be deleted and harassing current members will be dealt with in short order.

If you feel that you want to go, please do. No one will think any less of you. Contact Revicus or Ailish, or any of the Officers, and we will be willing to steer you in the direction of a few friends that are recruiting officers of many different levels of guilds.
