June 20th, 2004 - Overlord Ngrub Strikes Back.... sorta
It was a nice peaceful day in Norrath, and I was making my rounds checking on the locals when whom do I spot? Yes, Grubby, and the PH for the Wretched event. So we assemble and stroll over to do the Wretched event and once we down the first wave the event stalls. Yay SoE for buggy content!

Well we waited for a short while, traded jokes back and forth, until I decided we would leave it be, head over and smack Grubby around, then come back and see if it decided to work again. We head over and make short work of his minions then engage The Grubster. Well as fate would have it, Grubby had lady luck on his side this day and haste faded from the raid 10 seconds into the fight.

Well he AE'd, we fizzled, He AE'd some more and we healed, eventually he ran us oom and we fell while he stood at 1%. This didn't sit well with us since we can't remember the last time he got one up on us. So we spanked the chanter (She liked it by some accounts), rezzed, buffed and went forth with a vengence. This time Grubby had as much chance as a snowball in hell.

He dropped this for us:
