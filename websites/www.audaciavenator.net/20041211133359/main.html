December 2nd, 2004 - Knock, knock, knocking on elementals door..
So what has AV been up to the last week or so? Well, we killed the 5 gimps in Sol Ro, welcome to Sol Ro Minis rotation, so Mr. Flaming Head is gonna die soon. We killed Velitorkin again and picked up a leather tunic this time, we got chain last time. We took advantage of the Thanksgiving holiday FFA and went on a killing spree in Kael and Vindi, Statue, Idol, AoW and King Tormax all got thoroughly reemed. We saved the Virgins again in HoH for backflags and some nice loot. We smacked Grummus around for some backflags as well. The biggest news is we made a run at Rallos Zek the Warlord, and here is the story.

With a force of 41 people, we formed up and entered Tactics with fire in our eyes. We made our way to the triggers and gave em a beatdown which popped TZ, who gave us a lil scare when he took out a few people while we re-arranged positions. We beat him down and started rezzing, but unfortunately we didn't get to VZ in time before the triggers reset. So, we did what any hungry guild would do, we popped the triggers again to give TZ a "proper" beatdown, which we did. This time with only one death we were able to get VZ in camp withing minutes. VZ gave his best mighty hoowha! but it was not enough to defeat us. In the end he lay dead, right next to his brother. We prepped for RZ and pulled him to camp, where we layed the smackdown on him AV style! He poofed, we clicked our heels and set up at zone in for the big man himself.

Since we had to kill TZ twice it slowed us down a bit so we didn't have enough time to setup for RZtW like I wanted but we went for it anyways. With only 5 minutes left on the clock we staged for RZtW and pulled him. We got him to 62% before the adds ate the healers and inevitably wiped us, but what a hell of a run on our first shot with only 41 people. Our next run will go much smoother and with proper setup time for RZtW I expect him to fall to us on our next attempt. I am very proud of our attempt, RZtW is as good as dead, and we will be elemental. Gratz AV, We are knock, knock, knockin on elementals door...
