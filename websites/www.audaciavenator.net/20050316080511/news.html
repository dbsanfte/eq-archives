News - March 2005

March 11th, 2005 - Welcome to Elementals AV.

I am almost at a loss for words.. almost. /grin However, so much has happened with AV in the last week I almost don't know where to start. I guess I will start with the best part which can be summed up by one picture.

In what was our most amazing effort to date we not only beat this encounter, we beat it with roughly 80% of our raid force still standing at the end. After weeks and weeks of dissecting this encounter down to the smallest detail we finally worked out the last detail which allowed us to stomp RZ into the ground. We also managed to flag every single person at the raid. Everyone in attendance did an amazing job and made it look easy.

And then finally this...

Gratz AV on Earth, Air and Water.

Gratz Hichy on the coveted:

Gratz Quickfingers

Now one would think that a victory of this nature would satisfy the hunger for a night, however this is AV we are talking about. We weren't about to log out missing Fire with Sol Ro alive feeling all safe in his tower.

So after our massive MGB celebration to the denizens of PoK and a few trips up the elevator of the PoK library we gathered up and headed into Mr. Flaming Head's place for a little chat about access to the plane of fire. Negotiations didn't go well.. for him anyways as you can see by this picture.

In the end, he managed to take out 2 of our people so we didn't get our flawless first kill, but 2 on first shot ain't half bad :)

Welcome AV to Fire, and full elemental status. All those months of hard work have paid off and I can't even begin to say how proud I am of everyone. This was a great day for AV.

Gratz Swill

Gratz Himeo

Now on to other news that has happened recently. AV has been removed from the RTA, yes I know many RTA diehards will make it sound like we "left" however that is simply not the case, we offered them a chance to work out a compromise, they chose not too. We have no ill will towards the RTA or the guilds that support it, we are not out to destroy what they have built, however we now have the freedom to pursue our progression on our own terms. So far, I'd say we are doing well under this new freedom and it will only get better. AV is still recruiting players who would like to experience the elementals with us so check out our forums for more information on how to apply.

Now on to some older news and screenshots that were not posted previously.

Gratz Reeni and Blendar on the 7th Hammer portion of their epics. Here is a couple shots of the fight and a dead body. Just imagine it x2 as we did them back to back.

We also took out Worils Epic fight in Lake Rathe, the Ancient Megladon or as we so affectionately refer to him "Moby Prick" as pre-fix he was a true bastard. Now he is actually inline with his intented purpose. To be challenging, but not so much so that you need 40 mgb's to beat him. That dot was way overkill with his dps output and insane hp's. March 3rd, 2005 - Updates..

I appologize for the lack of updates, but AV has been busy busy busy. We recently changed hosts, since our old host was teh suck. The new host is much better than the "3 guys in a garage" host we had before. We are still however waiting for the DNS server to point our domain name to the correct server. Hopefully that get's updated soon. Now on to the kills and such.

Heyda, Mithanial Marr, Velitorkin, Slavedriver, School Leader, Trak, HoH Trials, Rydda Dar, VZ, 7th Hammer(x2), G/E/C among others all fell to AV recently. We even found time to single group Klandi (FFA night kill, yay patches!)

We have been refining our strategy on RZ and just about have it perfected, doing him with 40ish people is no easy task but we are so close we can smell the elementals. Once that asshat is dead it's going to be pure elemental ownage because Sol Ro is a gimp. We are also getting close to completing a few epic 1.5's and have more people getting closer each week.

It won't be long now... /grin
