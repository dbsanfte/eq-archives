Welcome to the home of Audacia Venator

An Everquest Guild on The Rathe Server

Audacia Venator formed on June 5th 2004 and have rapidly progressed through PoP content with less numbers than most guilds using highly developed strategies and unmatched tenacity. Our tight knit guild structure has allowed our members to flourish while having a great time in a competitive yet friendly atmosphere. We pride ourselves on our accomplishments and our unwillingness to give up or quit when faced with a tremendous challenge. We are always looking for like-minded individuals who seek a guild with goals and the ability to accomplish them.

If this sounds good to you, check out our Recruiting Rules and Requirements and fill out an application. If you have any questions regarding AV please feel free to visit our forums or contact a Recruiting Team member in game.
