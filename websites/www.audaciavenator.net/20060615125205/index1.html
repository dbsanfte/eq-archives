Latest News

June 14th, 2006 - First romp through Texvu

AV has been busy since our last update. We have been working on the last 3 trials for Anguish access and have them all very close, Specialization 7%, Foresight 2 @ >60% left, Endurance @ 14mins. Unfortunately we have had alot of folks buying houses, moving, on vacation or on business trips so we haven't really had an optimal raid setup on our recent attempts. Despite that we have still made some good progress and once everyone gets back we will hopefully get all 3 trials dead within a short timeframe.

In the meantime we finished up another run to round out our flags so we could start working on Texvu. Ikkinz 4 went down in less than an hour, Uqua went down in 2hrs 15 mins (our fastest run to date) and we did it pretty much flawless up until Barxt and even then we only lost a few folks. Inktuta went smooth despite us doing it before they fixed Stonemites in yesterdays patch.

The best news related to Intuta is we perfected the "Night Train" maneuver! We also took a second run at Adaptation and downed it very smooth as well as farmed some loot mobs.

We had a smallish raid force last night, about 40, so we went and poked around in Texvu to get a feel for the zone after we smoked Rikki. We had no intentions of "progressing" we just wanted to see what the zone was like and maybe score some loot. Mission accomplished.

We cleared a bunch of trash and made our way to Aneuk Nyrt Ymosa.

Pretty straight forward fight, killed first shot.

His twin brother wasn't up so we went after Ixt Quall Nalnn next and killed her first shot.

We'll be back for a progression run at Texvu soon.
