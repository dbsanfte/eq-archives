Fiasco in Progress


Fiasco in Progress is an EverQuest guild on the Bertoxxulous server. Someday we'll have a real webpage. That day is not today.

CHARTER:
--------

It's all about the name.

We have no intention of setting up complex rules or rigid leadership or conducting successful, large scale raid planning. We are not here to power game or support beggars or become the largest guild on the server. We are not about power trips for the leadership, as it should be noted for posterity that the *loser* of the roll between Daazinthael and Gristlebone was forced to become leader.

We are forming this guild to collect a close knit group of like minded people who enjoy going on loosely planned "adventures". Should we fail on these adventures, we strive to do it in some spectacular way that we can at least be nostalgic about later. Those without a sense of humor or an appreciation for irony need not apply.

Should we succeed, we will blink our eyes in amazement and wait for the other shoe to drop.

This is not to say that we will set out to fail on our adventures, we just seem to have a strong aptitude for it and the sense of humor to appreciate it. We are perpetually on a "Fiasco in Progress".

RULES AND GUIDELINES:
---------------------

1) The guild will operate as a benign dictatorship. If you find you don't like the way it's being run, the problem should be self regulating.

2) Read the second paragraph of the charter. If at some point you wish you were in a guild that did some of those things, please go join that guild. We can still be friends.

3) We have an important niche to fill in EverQuest. We are the guild your alt joins until they find a good one. If for some reason you are silly enough to still be in the guild at 40th level, your punishment will be automatic promotion to officer. If you don't like it, too bad... I didn't ask to be leader either but here I am.

4) Whining is encouraged, begging is not. Partucularly creative whining will at least score you the admiration of your guildmates.

5) If you are going to criticize somebody in a forum that would affect the reputation of the guild, please first make certain that your target is a bigger jerk than you are.

6) It is ok (and in fact encouraged) to be moody, judgemental, beligerent and/or critical of others if you *at least* have a sense of humor about it and know where to draw the line before hitting the enter key. If you can work whining in, all the better.

7) Always treat the GMs with kindness and respect, even if they are wrong. Please vent your frustration on your household pets. If you only have a goldfish, that was just your poor planning. -=\*1*/=-

8) This guild is founded first and foremost on a sense of humor. If you find yourself a member and don't have one, it's probably because you laugh at the other members' jokes. This is a good thing. :)

9) We enjoy a good laugh at somebody else's expense. If you find yourself adventuring outside the guild and come to the realization that you are on a Fiasco in Progress, we fully expect you to tell us about it in guildchat and stick with it. If you really want our admiration, take screenshots and write it up afterward for the web page.

10) Basically we don't like other people. This makes it hard to choose who to invite, so for the most part we just avoid it. If you are truly determined to get a person into the guild, you mostly just have to talk an officer into it. Our standards will lower considerably if we fall below 10 members, however.

Failure to comply with these rules and guidelines will carry stiff penalties. After a while we will stop responding to your hellos in guildchat. Continued offenses will result in us saying rude things to you and ultimately ostrasizing you completely. An officer deliberately violating these rules in order to get demoted will be immediately promoted to leader. Go ahead, try me.
