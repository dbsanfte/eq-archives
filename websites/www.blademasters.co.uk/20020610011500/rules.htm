Guild Rules

1) NEVER KILL STEAL - Stealing a kill from another player to gain experience or loot is strongly frowned upon, not only will you be removed from the guild but based on the scale of what you did other guilds may be made aware of your foul play. Noone will be mad at you for casting an AOE in a newbie zone and stealing someones decaying skeleton as that is highly trivial. Noone will exploit us by stealing our camps, if someone has attempted to take a MoB that you have engaged in combat then you must try your best to ourdamage them and /petition /report the incident if you can.

2) HELPING THOSE IN GENUINE NEED - When someone is in trouble the guild is expected to help them and support them, if someone has lost their corpse at the bottom of some dungeon then they require YOUR help. Noone is expected to drop everything to help someone though, if they only need a port or only need a rez, these can be easily attained from the general player base of EQ and using your guild for them to save money is not honourable. I would reccomend though, if a guild member needs a port and you are in the zone with nothing to do, then go and port them! :)

3) NO RUDE OR ABUSIVE BEHAVIOUR ESPECIALLY TO OTHER MEMBERS - This is pretty self explainitory, although punsishment will only come in the form of a verbal warning, anyone found spamming a zone, guildchat, or anyother player with abusive or excessive language will be removed.

4) USE OF THE /ANON TAG - The command /anon hides your level and class but it also hides your guild tag, if you are not happy to show your Blademasters tag this can be remedied with the /guildremove command. If you do need to hide your class and level please use the /roleplay command as this makes your guildtag visible to all.

5) HAVE FUN - Remember, Everquest is a game, it isn't supposed to be work. Unless of course you enjoy work that is ;) If you find yourself becomming bored with leveling and exp feel free to organise a wacky event or raid for the guild to come on.

6) YOUR CHARACTER - We are a good alligned guild as far as roleplaying goes if anyone does it. You should try to learn how to play your class as best as possible so you leave a great impression on everyone you group with.

7) BEGGING - Begging will be frowned upon, one warning will be given before removal. Begging includes asking for items, money and powerlevels in guildchat constantly. This is an example of begging: "soandso tells the guild, 'can any1 giv me some plats plz?!!1'". This is an example of asking for assistance without begging "soandso tells the guild, 'Anyone have a weapon better than 6 / 22 they would sell or give me?'" and that wouldn't be repeated every 2 minutes.. see the difference?

8) BEHAVIOUR ON RAIDS WITH OTHERS - Over use of the /shout channel, complaining, moaning about not getting exp, hassling the raid leader and running around like a noob will seriously degrade our chances of ever being invited on pick-up raids again.. Respect the raid leader, loot master and main assist and everything will be fine. Acting like this on an all Blademaster raid will still be frowned upon but we can guide you and teach you about how raids work to stop you making a fool of yourself ehhe..

9) GUILD MESSAGEBOARD - Everyone should be looking at this frequently, it will be where events are organised and where discussion will take place. When you are bored in college or work head over there and make some comments.. when you log on or off by habit just skim over the page, this way we can get to know eachother and become more organised better.
