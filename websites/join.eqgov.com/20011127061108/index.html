The members of this clan banned together under the common thread of Veeshan, the Great Crystalline Dragon who claimed the newly formed planet of Norrath thousands of years ago. Some members support Veeshan's claim to the planet, while others support Veeshan or its clan members, and even others claim to be ancestors of the Dragon Veeshan herself. Who knows much about this mysterious clan, other than they will live in Norrath's history as one of the greatest clans that ever walked the surface.
