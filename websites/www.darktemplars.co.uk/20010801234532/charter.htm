The Dark Templars are a roleplaying guild based on the Xegony server, who's motives are to promote justice and fairness for all inhabitants of Norrath.
pre-requisites
Dark Templars was originally a closed guild, open only to those who knew each other through mutual friendship of one of it's members. However the guild will soon be opening it's doors to external members, preferentially those from Europe and the United Kingdom. Essentially organising raids etc on a GMT (Greenwich Mean Time) scale.

Requisites for joining Dark Templars will be posted shortly, once a council of officers has been formed, and the requisites debated.
