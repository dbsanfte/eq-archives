Requirements for joining Dark Templars.

As it stands currently, we are taking any applicants for membership. The method of joining is simple, use the Applicants form to submit your application to us. If there are any initial objections by existing members of the guild you won't be invited to join. You will be informed of this by  a guild officer. If your application is accepted, you will be guilded for a period of 1 month in which time you are expected to group with other guild members wherever possible. After this month period and with the general consensus of other guild members you may or may not be asked to stay as a permanent member of the guild. If for some reason you are not asked to stay as a full member, a guild officer will disband you and give you the reasons (where possible) why you were not invited to stay. (as of 2nd May 2001)
