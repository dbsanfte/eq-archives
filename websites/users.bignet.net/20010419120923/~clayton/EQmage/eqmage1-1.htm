Duncanett's Diary
By Duncanett Bint-Erud (Cazic-Thule)

I was born in Erudin on 05/20/1999 I am a direct descendant of Erud himself Founder of Erudin and Arch-Magi. On mothers side there is the stories of  "Duncan the First" a Priest and Warrior. From the great city of Tos in a land far away. Known as Meridian. prefix 'Bint-' means 'Daughter of'  Duncanett Bint-Erud Mage Level 54 and Adventuring.

 I graduate today, I am a Sr. Apprentice.  It is time I use magic in the real world  I cant seem to find any odd jobs here.  I will explore the forest today. The forest  is overrun with snakes, rats and undead  I work hard defending Erudin from   vermin and I am better with a dagger.  Poachers always attack and I hate them.  It is unclear where the skeletons come   from. I have found and killed many of  the kobold camps in the area. There is    a Very underrated Troll by the bridge.

I Learned how to summon a Elemental!! they seem very protective. This makes  killing Skellies and Kobolds just to easy. An I am not sure the cat people are Evil even though they really hate Us!! I have heard of wars being fought in Antonica against Trolls and Orcs. They seem to be much more evil and bares investigating.

I found some old maps & scrolls in the library from before Erud. I will go to find these places. I arrived in Qeynos today its a quaint city.  They are at war with  Gnolls. I am lost today in the gnoll cave.  If don't find my way out soon I will be  killed. I found a exit but its cold here.  Their are Orcs  everywhere and casters  too. I was captured by Barbarians  today. I like them if they don't kill me.   And So it Begins.
