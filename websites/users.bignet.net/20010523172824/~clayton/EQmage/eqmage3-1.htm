The Basics EQ
By Duncanett Bint-Erud (Cazic-Thule)
 

Ever Quest - Is a challenging, frustrating,  addictive, and most of all fun virtual society.  If you want a easy game play something else, but if you can handle the deaths and frustration it's a great game.  Like all societies it has rules of etiquette and behavior.  Here are a few pointers to a successful EQ life.

1. READ THE MANUAL.... it will help u pick a char. and class you will like. 
2. Try a few different classes before you get to attached to your main character
3. Make friends, get in group , build a group, find out when people you like play. 
4. Don't Kill steal , Always Loot fairly , Don't beg or Whine.  
5. Ask all the questions you can think of  and more we love to help  nice newbies
6. No one can do it for you so learn to earn you way.
7. Learn your class read up on spells ETC. on the web or wherever.
8. Don't choose a guild to fast wait till know the game, so you know who you like
