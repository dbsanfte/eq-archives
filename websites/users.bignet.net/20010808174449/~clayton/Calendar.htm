Things to do before attending a Event:

  1. Read the web page for the event, so you will know what will be required.

  2. If you you have any questions after reading the web page contact the Event Leader.

  3. Be there early, Get a teleport, Log off at the entrance. Plan to fight at the start time.

  4. Expect to events to last for 6 hrs or more. 

  5. If you are a Cleric please make sure one of you will be there Or inform us you won't so we can reschedule.

  6. If your spells require a component bring lots. 

  7. Invite a friend to join us.
