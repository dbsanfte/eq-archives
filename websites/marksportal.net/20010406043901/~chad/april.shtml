April Fool's 2000

for pictures click here


Randy and Rene' got married one week before April 1st.  As a personal tradition I enjoy greeting friends and family with a nice welcome home gag.  My best man did it to me, and I have done it to several people.  It's always important to have a good team with you for these types of things.

The list of victims includes:
Scott and Patti
Dave and Daphne
Chris and Julie
Randy and Rene
and myself

however these are simply the wedding pranks.

For Randy and Rene's house, we thought long and hard.  Ideas proposed were to post signs around the neighborhood saying "Pokemon and Beany Babies for sale @ (their address) @ (the time they'd be home)".  Or putting a condemned sign up, or a FOR SALE sign in their yard.  A motion triggered fan aimed at the doorway with confetti in place.  Toothbrush bristles in the sheets. We had several ideas, but we were desiring something that said "welcome home" and was playful without being too messy for clean up.

Chris Izor's quote was "Messy? I had to repaint my walls!"  Randy helped me with the prank on Julie n Chris. (another web page altogether).

Our goal was to fill a room with balloons, and to make their bathtub into an aquarium.
Ingredients needed:
Balloons
Helium Tanks
Fish
Aquarium accessories/filters
One fish net "scooper"
a Blue tarp for EFFECT
Duct Tape
Balloon Clips (so we wouldn't hurt our fingers)
a nice bottle of Champagne
Welcome Home signs
------------------------
we replaced the helium tanks with hand pumps, and we neglected the champagne.

Assistants:
Cori
Julie and Chris
Mark
Sydney

Preparation:
Kendal was gone for the day at a meeting so I needed Julie to watch Sydney (my daughter) while Mark and I went to get the ingredients.
Chris (Julie's husband) stayed with Julie until we all met up again.  As I left the driveway I noticed a swelling bottle of orange juice (a week old in the sun), in my king cab.. I tossed it into the bed of my truck.  We went to Party America and gathered all supplies but the fish. Then we went to get the fish at Dolphin Pet Village in Campbell.  They of course didn't even ask what all those fish were for.  50 small feeders and 5 large Feeder goldfish. A funny thing happened, the clerk helping me had to scoop so many fish, he forgot that he had put a hose in a nearby tank and by the time he was done, it was overflowing and spilling fish and water EVERYWHERE on the floor.   It was time to go pick up the rest of the team and head over to Randy's and Rene's.  While we were turning left out of a lot, I heard a pop and my rear window was SPRAYED with white stuff.  I thought we were hit by an egg or a water balloon. It turns out that bottle of orange juice exploded and sprayed not only us, but a lady with her elbow out the window in a nearby car.  She was surprised and then disgusted at the smell.  Unfortunately we didn't have a chance to pull over and apologize because of the turning left.  I hope she's not too mad.  It was pretty funny. Fortunately that the orange juice was found when it was.

Implementation:
Chris, Cori, Julie and Mark began to inflate balloons.  Cori became dizzy quickly, and Mark got a headache.  Apparently they were using more of their own hot air than the pumps and began to feel a bit woozy.  I found the plug to the main bathtub and put it in place insuring ZERO leakage with duct tape.  Then I duct taped the blue tarp into place so that it would appear "ocean like" and also hold the necessary tub full of water.  I then threw in a few shells and a pirate skull, we decided against putting gravel in, as it would be too messy.
We really lucked out because Rene's decor was that of an OCEAN theme anyway, the reason we had gone with the  FISH was because their choice of honeymoon was that of a cruise.  I then placed a stuffed lobster toy (which fit the color scheme perfectly) on the sink closed the door, and helped inflating balloons.  We filled their little hallway between all the rooms about 2/3 full with large balloons.  Total 216 balloons.

Finishing touches:
We placed two signs in the living room to welcome them. "Welcome home" and "congratulations".  WE then took a polaroid of the pranksters with me holding the fish scooper, and taped it to the inside of the "balloon hall" door.  I then placed the fish scooper on the ground some where.

Results:
Randy called me when he got home and told me that when they opened the door they were avalanched with balloons.  It sounded like they enjoyed the prank.  They had wondered what the scooper was for, and apparently hadn't yet discovered the bathroom.  I instructed Randy to the bathroom where he found the stuffed animal and like it, but it took a moment for him to see the tub.  "you dog.. that's great!"  or something to that affect.  He then let me hear Rene's discovering it.  IT was awesome! Everything went well.  They said the balloons were all over the place.  It was good fun.  I'm glad they received it well and we're all happy for them.

Congratulations Randy n Rene'
