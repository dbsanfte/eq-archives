Hi there.

I put this web page up in the event of two things:

1. A drastic world-wide tragedy has jsut occurred where people have disappeared and you came here seeking answers.
or
2. It hasn't happened yet but you still seek answers.
 

I won't be too wordy here or make a lot of opinionated statements.  What I will say on this page is simple, to the point, and true.  In this world truth is so hard to determine, it's all a mixture of ideas that we are told to accept..  People believe there is no absolute truth.  If a bunch of people and all the children in the world recently disappeared, then perhaps that adds credibility to what I'm writing here.  You've probably even lost someone because of it.

That event was foretold by God in His scriptures.  And the people who were taken away were believers and followers of His Son, or too young to have been given the chance to choose.

I am speaking of Christ.  The only Son of God.  He died and was miraculously raised from the dead.  He died as a sacrifice for every person's sin.  That death has allowed for us to be cleansed of the sin in our lives.  If we believe this, God will save us.  We will be with Him in heaven.

John 3:
16 "For God so loved the world that he gave his one and only Son,  that whoever believes in him shall not perish but have eternal life.
17 For God did not send his Son into the world to condemn the world, but to save the world through him.
18 Whoever believes in him is not condemned, but whoever does not believe stands condemned already because he has not believed in the name of God's one and only Son.

So there are a few questions.  Why did He have to die?  Why did He have to come back to life?  What sins do we have in our lives?

Does it even make sense that ASSUMING we do have sin in our lives, that in order to clear us of it, there has to be a blood sacrifice?

Why would a loving God demand a blood sacrifice?

Why would a loving God allow so much tragedy to go on in the world?

So many questions, and there are answers.  I would highly recommend you read the Gospel of John from the Bible (NIV translation might be good to start with).

I'm going to let you down now, because I'm not going to answer those questions for you.  Remember when I said I wasn't going to go into opinionated stuff?

If you truly seek answers then begin reading John.

Let me assure you though, that once you read and understand and believe on Christ as the only Son of God, and accept that He died to save you, it'll begin to make sense to you.

Ask yourself two questions.
1. When you ask yourself the above tough questions, and can't figure out why God would do this or that, are you willing to let yourself believe that because you don't know WHY He would do something, that He doesn't exist?

  -People tell me they don't believe in God because they can't understand how He could let so much suffering occurr.  It doesn't make sense to them and so they can't believe in God, or they simply don't want to.

My good friend is constantly doing things I don't understand.  But if I spoke to him and asked him about it, perhaps read something he wrote on the subject, then maybe it would make sense WHY.  And even if it didn't, He still exists, regardless of how well I understand what he does.  He's sitting right next to me as I type this.  There I poked him.  He exists.  It is the same with God.  Because you can't understand something about Him is not a good enough reason to believe He doesn't exist.  Perhaps it's a good enough reason to not want to follow Him or love Him, but not enough of a reason to believe He isn't.  He does love you. Admitting he exists is the first step in finding out why He does what He does.  Finding that out will solve the problem of whether or not you want to follow Him. Allow Him the opportunity to show you some truth in His word.  Read John.  Do not simply write Him off because you can't understand something you haven't given Him the chance to tell you about.

2. Who makes the rules about how to get to heaven?

 - I know it's tough to believe that there is a heaven, because then you have to believe there's a hell.  How many times have you been wrong about something you thought?  What about something you thought was true when you were much younger?  Say Santa Claus.  You thought he existed, and he didn't.  As humans we have a very limited knowledge of things we can't see.  Are there Aliens? Is there a God? Is there a heaven? What happens when you die?  A person could go batty thinking about all these unknowns!  So I ask you to simply think about one thing.  If you are willing to admit that you haven't yet seen what happens when you die, then you should also be willing to admit that you don't know for sure what will happen.  That anything you currently think, is only "your best guess".  So if you don't know then you can also agree that you're not positive whether or not heaven exists.  And that means, that there is the possibility that heaven exists.
Follow me now I'm trying not to be too puzzling.

Can you know something for sure and then be completely wrong about it?  Maybe, maybe not.  The only way you find out is when it's over and then you've known it for sure.  This "knowing" something without actually KNOWING is called faith.  Athiests "know" there is no God, but if you don't know, how can they know?  They're only guessing, but they are so convinced of it, that it's a sort of faith. (by the way, they're wrong).

So now we can all agree that even if we KNOW, or THINK there is no heaven, that there still is a chance that it exists.  What makes me so sure?  That faith we were speaking about.  There is much I haven't seen, and things I don't know, but I have a peace that the things I believe make sense and are true, so says God.

Ok so now back to the question.  "How do you get to heaven?  Who made the rules?"

Who makes the rules in your house?  You own the house, so you set the rules.
At my house we have a rule, at the door you have to wipe your feet.
Now if you came and said to yourself  "I think the way to get into Chad's house is to ring the doorbell two times then give a secret knock.", but refused to wipe your feet, you'd never get in.

Several people believe in heaven.  But they believe that they have to "do good things" or "never steal or kill anybody" to get in.  This is simply not so.  The Bible clearly states that:

Eph 2:8-9 "
    "For it is by grace you have been saved, through faith - and this not from yourselves,
    it is the gift of God - not by works, so that no one can boast."

In other words, you can't get to heaven by doing good things or avoiding doing bad things your whole life.

So, if your rules on how to get to heaven are wrong.
And if the owner of the house gets to set the rules.

Then heaven, being created by God, is attainable only through the rules which God has set up.

Read the book of John to learn the rules.

You can only get to Heaven through a faith in Jesus Christ, you can't earn your way there.
 
 

So think about those two questions for me.
1. Is there a God?
2. How do I get to heaven?

and then read the following stuff, it's very helpful.  I can't urge you enough to make the right decision.... all it takes is a prayer, confessing your past sin and accepting the gift of salvation.

Please if these words touch you, find someone you trust as a believer and ask them for guidance.  Read the Gospel of John.
