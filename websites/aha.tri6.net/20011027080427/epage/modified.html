About Our Modified[KAI] CTF


Modified[Kai] CTF

Ok, First things First. What does Kai mean? Well, simply put, Kai is the Japanese word for "modified".

Since this is a Modified Version of CTF running on a server based in Japan, we added the word KAI, just to bring a little culture to the game. :)

A Word or Two about Hit Points (HP)

At the beginning, or every time you die and respawn you are giving 100 Hit Points. You can Increase your hit points up to 150 by getting regular med boxes!

(e.g A hitpoint average of 150 in [kai]CTF is equal to 100 hit points in a regular version of CTF.)
