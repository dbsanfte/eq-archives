Privacy Policy

Providing Visitors with Anonymous Access

You can access our Web site home page and browse our corporate site without disclosing your personal data.

Automatic Collection of Information

We do not automatically log personal data but we link information automatically logged by other means (e.g. IP address and unique generated numbers) with personal data about specific individuals.

We do make use of cookies to store personal data. Essentially, a cookie is a text file placed on the client machine by our Web Server allowing us to provide a more efficient service by identifying you each time you access our Web site and not requesting that you log in to the Support Zone on each visit.

Data Collection and Purpose Specification

We collect the personal data that you may volunteer while using our services in the Support Zone.

We do not collect information about our visitors from other sources, such as public records or bodies, or private organisations.

Syspro may send out periodic e-mails informing you of technical service issues related to our product or service. You will not be able to choose to unsubscribe to these mailings, as they are considered an essential part of the service you have chosen.

In addition, Syspro send out a fortnightly Support Zone newsletter to which users may select to unsubscribe.

Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal data from children, as working professionals are the only people that can register and therefore volunteer personal data in our Support Zone.

Disclosure and Visitor Choice

We disclose our visitors' personal data to our subsidiaries in other countries but only for registrations pertaining to that specific country.

By using this Web site, you consent to the collection and use of the information as described here. If we decide to make changes to this privacy policy, we will post the changes on this page so that you always will know what information we collect, how we use it, and when and how we will disclose it.

Confidentiality / Security

We do not give visitors to our Web site the option of using a secure transmission method to send us their personal data because no financial transactions are done on our Web site.

We have implemented security policies, rules and technical measures to protect to the best of our ability the personal data that we have under our control from:

  • Unauthorised access
  • Improper use or disclosure
  • Unauthorised modification
  • Unlawful destruction or accidental loss

We trust that you appreciate such security measures, although currently proficient, are not infallible to unscrupulous sources and cannot be warranted to prevent every incident of interference which may occur.

All our employees and data processors, who have access to, and are associated with the processing of personal data, are obliged to respect the confidentiality of our visitors' personal data. Should any one or more of our employees or data processors breach their obligations in this regard, we will take the necessary legal action against the offenders but cannot be held responsible for any loss occasioned to you in consequence thereof.

We will not intentionally disclose any of your personal data to State institutions and authorities except if required by law or other regulation.

Access to the personal data we may hold about you

Your personal profile is available for viewing and editing by you within the Support Zone. At any time, you may view the personal data that we have stored about you.

We allow you to challenge the data that we hold about you and, where appropriate, you may have the data

  • Rectified or amended
  • Completed
  • Removed

We reserve the right to refuse to provide our visitors with a copy of automatically collected non-personal data, but will give reasons for our refusal and will allow you to challenge our decision to refuse to provide you with a copy of such non-personal data.
