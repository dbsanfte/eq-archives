Policy Posted by Grymm - On November 3, 2001
Drakkon’s Blood Clan.

A Gaming clan for Everquest on Druzzil Ro server.

We strive for a dynamic group of Internet friends. A community of people who put honor, fair play and the love of fun before all else.

Our policy will grow and change as our guild grows and changes. This is no more then a living document to grow on and enhance the guild. A means to keep the guild moving and playing to the enjoyment of the many and not the few.

Officers are the representatives of the guild as a whole. They should keep the guild in mind first. Being an officer is a privilege and an honor, not a right. Officers are limited to 15 active members of the guild. Officers will make policy only with the approval of the majority of Officers.

Communication is paramount in all things. Be impartial as best as possible and work things through.

Our rules are simple and constant.

Play fair and honest.
Be courteous to all players. Grant all people in the game common respect.
If a problem can’t be solved be the bigger person and walk away.
Communicate. If you can not talk in game or on the forums use an email to an officer.
Guildmate's first in all things.
Use the officers for problem solving. That’s why they are officers.
Officers will have final word on raids and events. This is to keep it well rounded and fair. Any concerns or problems should to be addressed with any officer. Your voice will only be heard if it is used.
Members make the guild what it is. You are Drakkon’s Blood in all things you do. If you want change bring it about.
Golden rule: Do unto others as you would have done unto you.

But This was a Begining. Posted by Grymm - On October 22, 2001
We have been in a state of flux lately. A few members have left for personal reasons or for a diffrent run at things in game. We say farewell to them as guildmates but hopefully not as friends.
We have also gained a few new members. Some new to the game, some old hands and high in level. As always Drakkon's welcomes many people of all levels but it has finally came time to close the doors temporarly while we adjust and pay much needed attention to old and new members alike.
A few Guilds have approched me with alliances but hesitate to firm up the deal due to some of our members who are more outspoken and extream then most prefer to deal with. We are working on our image as we speak. I ask all members to remember you represent Drakkon's in all you do. The server impact our reputation has can make our lives easier or much harder. So please keep this in mind at the least.
Epics Epics Epics. Its on everyones minds. Mine yours, all of us.
Let me just say do what you can and get the minor stuff out of the way. If you need a small group to do a part we'll work on it.
But big point here, Epic=levels.
In other words the higher you are, the higher I am, the easier it is for all of us in the end. We can't have 8 grps of forty something's we need 3 groups of 55+ usually. So lets all work on our levels so we can do all of our epics, Mine yours, his hers.
I will be working on a few things for epics and becoming more familiar with whats needed so i can help get the minor things out of the way for all classes. That way melli can prepare to plan the hardcore raids for the end results. The leaders I think should decided on who is feasible and easiest to accomplish and go from there. I know the mage and cleric epics are extreamly hard so we will be paying close attention to these and working on resources to complete them.
No sense going to die and not leaving with what we came for. But also we will not make you wait to be last either.
The guild is coming together but we are far from the end result. All I can say is it's going to get a lot harder but it's also going to get more fun.
I am glad to be here and doing it with you guys and wouldn't be with any other guild but this one.
Grymm
