[10-11-02] - Aselus
 Well, it's that time again- more epics to announce. Ordinarily I wouldn't be too excited except both of these epics are MV firsts. Jordynede at long last has her Holy Water Sprinkler of ResGoesToPage50 and Senci got her Shissar staff so I don't have to listen to her scream about overwriting VoG with HoS. A lot of hard work went into getting these epics by the entire guild and we're already reaping the benefits. Thanks a lot to all of the MV members who took time to help these two lovely ladies (well Jordy anyway, Senci smells (like flowers)). The MV epic list now stands at (in no particular order): Rogue, Ranger, Shaman, Cleric, Wizard, Druid, Enchanter, and Monk. l33t.

Amazingly, both epics were pretty much completed in the same day. After getting the turn-in in SolB, everyone was itching to get Jordynede her epic, so we massed in Skyfire and slapped him silly. The gang then went to Iceclad for another 8th Shawl war, ending with Tollenn getting his 8th shawl.

One of the epics that MV doesn't have yet is the Warrior epic, but we took a huge step toward getting one when we caught Severilious with his pants down almost immediately following the 8th shawl, and MV gave him something to think about until he spawned again. Sev was the first true solo Kunark Dragon kill by Menacing Visage! Sev was kind with loot: a Green Dragon Scale for the aforementioned Warrior epic dropped in addition to an RBB (Grewd) and a Shield of the Green Dragon (Cubpaws), plus other miscellaneous loot not really worth mentioning. Then, much to everyone's surprise, we caught the Wraith Of Shissir up in Fear and dropped him like a bad habit for the most highly contested spawn of Senci's epic, all but completing it.

Oh, and we raided some other place. Temple of Somethingorother... Veeshan maybe?
