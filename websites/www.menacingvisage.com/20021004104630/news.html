[9-18-02] - Aselus
 It's been a very busy month. Tonight's news is the happiest news I've ever had the pleasure of reporting- MV tonight took down the Prince and the Overking in Chardok, and Senci and Jordynede got their respective epic pieces. This raid has elduded us for several months, and it's a wonderful feeling to have accomplished it at long last. In addition to the primary loot, we netted an Argent Protector for Hagrok, a Nathsar Breastplate for Mutsumi, a Di`Zok Imperial Katana for Kynia, and a Golem Tear Ring for Linabec. Jordy seemed somewhat excited about nearing her epic at long last.

Many congratulations to our beloved Jordynede on this difficult step of her epic quest. We love you Jordy! 8)

Hate has been popular lately. Senci is getting very close to her epic, especially with the downing of the Prince, and we've been trying to get her Vampire Essence from Hate, but the revs are being stingy. One such attempt last week had a surprising but welcome drop-- a Shattered Stone of Corruption, which coincidentally happened to be the last piece Shyran needed for her Earthcaller. MV now has two Rangers with dual epics! But that isn't all by any means -- a week ago Rogan completed his 4th and 5th Coldain Rings Phlome completed his 8th Coldain Shawl (woot extended duration DMF) and we picked up some neat loot from that; an Ice Forged Helm went to Rogan, Xeretic got a Tracking Wolf Collar, and Uggzy finally got a new club in the form of a Spiked Velium Mallet. Not content with just that for ganking, Tawro went down again to just 13 late-nighters MVers and Beodin got the highly coveted (and l33t) Edge of Honor and Slarck got one of the 15 stamina bracers. An excellent night.

More fun loots and raids that are starting to become old hand: the wench Treah in the Plane of Growth dropped a Mage robe that rotted the first time we killed her, which pissed us off enough to kill her again so she actually dropped something useful: a Wizard robe for Yaeadwen. Other miscellaneous loots were of course netted in these efforts, notably Shaman and Necro pants to Beurlom and Xeretic.
Khuul and Phlome both acquired their 9th Coldain Ring, and though the 10th ring may be some distance off, the ring has excellent stats and is a great accomplishment.
Hate and Fear have both been done several times since the last update, including two near-complete clearings of Fear (minus only Cazic-Thule himself) netting lots more armor.
The most ghetto Tangrin kill of all time: Cleric, Druid, Necromancer, Shaman, Shadowknight, Wizard, Enchanter. The Cleric tanked.

Welcome back to Tollenn and Arhra, both of whom had been gone for some time. They always come back. :) More humor shots (of course), and don't forget the informational page is there. I've been slacking on updating that, but if you have something you'd like to add, feel free to post as such in the forums and I'll try and get to it.

And now, a nostalgia shot from the first clearing of Draco and the golems!
[8-28-02] - Aselus
 I'm baaack...

So I decided I missed the folks in MV a lot. I log back on after a couple months of hiatus, and in addition to half a yellow of XP in 54, I find that MV now knows what the hell is going on when they raid. Case in point: the target of my first raid in 3 months was the Spirit of Tawro, a resident deep in the ice caves of Umbral Plains. After a run-in with the Netherbian Swarmlord and his goons (who wiped us handily on the final wave.. but he will get his soon enough) and a most impressive display of raid discipline, MV netted a Flaming Dirk for Khuul and a Bracelet of Fortitude for Beurlom. Four months ago when I retired, pulling to the Umbral zone-in was a nightmare. You've come a long way, baby.

Anyway! The night wasn't over yet, so after some mucking about Strumph discovered the Fallen Sergeant in Umbral Plains was up (Strumph says he got a tell from the Sergeant begging him to put him out of his misery but he can't prove it). Being good MV people, we dropped him like a sack of potatoes off a skyscraper and Nilus got a cool new Vah Shir Skin robe, which he promptly gave up to Qalinar a couple days later when he scored a Fear robe. Go figure. Of course, Nilus wasn't the only one to snag some Fear gear this week- unfortunately nobody really recorded what dropped, or if they did, I don't know about it.

It's damn good to be back, guys.
