Recruitment

Menacing Visage is not a large guild. We are a bunch of people who play a game to relax from real life, and many of us have jobs or attend school. We don't raid NToV, and we can't go drop a dragon on a whim. If this is what you are looking for, you won't find it here. If you are looking for a terrific group of people to talk to and play the game with, go no further.
Our membership is starting to swell, and though we still need more bodies to take on the obstacles that face us, we also want to make sure we are inviting the right kind of people to Menacing Visage. Thus a few membership guidelines have been set forth:

1) There are level limits in effect; the level required is dependent on the class and our need for that class. For the current level limits, please read the sticky topic in the Applicants forum.

2) When applying, a post on the forums in the Applicants section is mandatory. This is so all members have a chance to see who is currently up for application and voice any concerns or praise they might have.

3) After posting in the Applicants section of the forums, a minimum of two weeks hiatus time is also required so that current members can, as mentioned above, discuss the applicant. Note that this is not a maxiumum time; it is a minimum. MV reserves the right to extend the time an applicant is up for membership.

4) Direct approval from three members and one officer is required before an invitation to the guild will be extended. Obviously it is in your best interests as an applicant to get out and group with us, so we might better know you.

5) Raiding with MV is optional, but very reccommended, for the application process. Check the Raid Info forum for more information about upcoming raids.

Recruiting directly from other guilds is bad karma, and a practice that will be avoided by all members Menacing Visage. If you wish to say that we would be glad to have them apply, that is certainly another story, but we will avoid pulling members directly from other guilds.

Membership

Members of Menacing Visage will conduct themselves with honor and dignity at all times. We are not a guild of assholes. If you want to be an asshole to other players, go play on a Zek server where they can PK you if you lip off. Complaints of verbal abuse, including but not limited to: swearing in public channels, inappropriate conversation in any channel, and arguments in public channels, will all be taken very seriously.
In addition, all guild members are expected to not only uphold the Rules of Conduct set forth on the Everquest website, but to encourage others to follow them as well. No ninja looting, kill stealing, fighting over spawns, etc. Use your head. Failure to observe the rules of conduct will result in dismissal from Menacing Visage.
In guild situations, Need Before Greed is the rule. There are no exceptions, ever. Looting as such is grounds for immediate dismissal.

Raids

If you are able to attend a raid, please do so, even if you don't need the loot or won't get any experience. Attending raids shows your dedication to the guild and your friends, and will increase your odds of recieving loot when you do need it. In addition, the more people we have on a raid, the more smoothly it will go and the less our clerics will be ressing.
In general, loot will be given to those who need it the most. The raid leader ALWAYS has the final say in loot distribution.
The Order of Battle and Rules of Engagement forum topic areas contain more information.

Equipment

Any loot that drops on guild-sponsored raids will be distributed in one of two ways: handed out based on past attendance and effort, or at random to those that need it. If there are no members present who need the item, it may be added to the guild fund. Any item in the guild fund may be used by any member's main character for an indefinite period of time. After two weeks in the guild fund, any item will be open for distribution to twinks that are also members of Menacing Visage, or to be sold. If the item goes to a twink and the need arises for it on another player's main character, the item is expected to be promptly returned. If the item is sold, the money is expected to return to the guild fund.
Any members that replace their exising equipment may opt to add their old equipment to the guild fund. In this case the item may not be sold, it may only be used by another member. The original owner will have the right to recall their item at any time.
Cash in the guild fund will used primarily to reimburse casters for reagents used on raids, or as deemed neccesary by the guild council.

Remember above all else: it is a game. If you're not having fun, then you're doing the wrong thing.
