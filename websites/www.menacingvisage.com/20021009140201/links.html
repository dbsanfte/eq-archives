Everquest Links

Everquest - Sony/Verant's official Everquest Website.

Allakhazam - If you play Everquest and have never been to this site, you're nuts. The absolute best overall resource for everything in the game.

EQ Atlas - 99% of all maps utilized by Menacing Visage will come from this site. Bar none the best maps anywhere.

Caster's Realm - An excellent news site, and quite possibly the best spell layout and info of all Everquest sites that aren't class-specific.

EQ Prices - An excellent price guide, with a detailed search engine that makes it easy to find an upgrade to that armor or weapon that just isn't cutting it anymore.

EQ Traders - Every last detail known to the player population about tradeskills.

Mobhunter - News that you might not hear about elsewhere, and some fairly insightful comments on things you may not have considered.
