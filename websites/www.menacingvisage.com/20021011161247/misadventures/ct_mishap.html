I refer, of course, not to the guild's adventures in Cazic-Thule as a whole, but to those of myself and my good buddy Hagrok. MISadventures would be closer to the truth of what happened, but those of you that know us know that we do tend to get in over our heads periodically. Part of our charm, I'm sure.

After having worked diligently with the rest of the guild in the 4c area in CT, battling our way to the top of the maze-like pyramid, we were sitting around resting, waiting for something to pop, hoping it would be the semi-legendary crusaders that drop the coveted decaying armor we were there for.

Hag and I decided to explore, Hag in skeleton form, myself but one-third of my normal stature, having been shrunken to miniscule size by our resident shaman, Aselus. I believe this moment could be defined as the point at which our adventure became a MISadventure. We weren't even together, really, we were both being bone-headed on our own.

I walked out onto the bridge in the little room at the top of the pyramid. Of course it occured to me that I had no idea what was beneathe me. Panning my eyes downward, and carefully stepped up to the edge of the bridge to look down. I viewed the panorama of multiple square pools of water, seeming to flow into each other like a long series of waterfalls. Neat, I thought to myself, that looks cool. Better be careful though, and not fall down there, I'd have no idea how to get back... wait... why are all the pools getting closer? SPLASH!

Well. Now I'm swimming around in a little square pool. I attempt to climb to the edge of the little square pool, and promptly plummet into the NEXT little square pool. This happens several times, until I'm at the lowest little square pool, and climbing to the edge of it merely knocks me back into it. Oh dear, I think. This can't be good. Suddenly, I'm being attacked by three alligators. This is worse, I think. Three blue monsters attacking me, and no healer nearby.

I've been too busy to notice guild chat until this moment, but I send out a message now, saying "Uh-oh. I may have done something stupid. I don't know HOW we're gonna get my body out of this one." You see, I'd just assumed that I was going to die at this point. I fail to notice that Hagrok has also been guild chatting that he's in some sort of trouble, due to my own peril. My declining hit points suddenly begin to rise. Apparently, our cleric can reach me with heal spells from the bridge! I'm ecstatic. I attack the gators with renewed vigor. The problem of my finding my way back is still there, but at least for the moment I might not die.

Suddenly, a skeleton pops up from a previously hidden (to me, due to my small stature) hole in the middle of my little square pond. Before I can think about whether or not this is a good thing, the skeleton begins to attack the gators at my side. Damned if my old buddy Hagrok didn't go and do the same bone-headed thing I did. Well, misery loves company. Hag and I go to work, and soon the gators are a memory.

I swim into the hole in the floor of my pond, through a short tunnel, and up in another small square pond. "Maybe there's an exit from here..." I say to the guild. "Nope." responds Hagrok. "This is where _I_ fell in." I can't help but chuckle at that point. We swim back into the hole, and find that in the middle of the short tunnel, there's a crossroads of sorts. I arbitrarily turn left, Hagrok right behind me. A long tunnel later, and we finally come up into what looks like a small room with some bridges across it. We realized that a) we've picked up three more gators, and b) we're officially out of the range of the healers now. We go to work, and soon the gators are all dead, but we're starting to run low on hit points.

We cautiously begin to thread our way through what we will later find out is referred to as "the ponds". Little did we know that the ponds are a direct path link between the courtyard in CT and the room containing the Avatar of Fear. All we wanted at this point was to find our way back to the rest of the guild, and the healers. I'm at about 60percent health, and Hag is down to a measly 40percent. There are nasty lizards about every ten paces, and the constant fighting begins to wear down both our hit points and our hope of ever reaching the surface alive.

Suddenly, what appears to be a female rogue appears in front of us, out of a doorway leading to the next pond. I'm terribly sorry, but I can't remember her name at this point. This wonderful person was kind enough to lead us through the ponds, and out into another room, a cliff overlooking a large cavern with a large underground lake at the bottom. Apparently our rogue-saviour decided we were safe at this point, and disappeared. She gained my enough of a breather, however, that I was able to print out a map of the CT sewers while Hag managed to med up a bit and regain a few precious hit points. I consulted the map.

"Where to now?" says Hag. Over the edge, I cry, as I plummet off the cliff into the icy water below. (Editor's note: how I swim as fast as I do with 200 pounds of crafted armor and an arsenal of weapons on me is really more than my feeble mind can understand.) With Hag behind me, and me turning around every now and then to make sure he's keeping up, we swim through the murky water, into a crack in the underground wall, up into a water tunnel that seems to lead up for miles and miles, and suddenly, we pop out into the courtyard!!! Where we were promptly trained with about twelve green wimpy lizards, but Fargham met us there at that point, and saved us the embarassment of being killed by wimpy critters due to lack of available hit points.

So, all's well that ends well, and our crew rejoined us in the courtyard in preparation for continuing the raid. I'll leave out the point where I later accidently attempted to solo the Avatar of Fear and became a blue greasy streak on the floor.
