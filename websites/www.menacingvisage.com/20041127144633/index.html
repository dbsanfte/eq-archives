Undead RogueDamage is what the rogue does best. Walking in shadows, their attacks are swift and deadly. Sneaking behind a target, a rogue can deliver a massive amount of damage before most adversaries can respond. Rogues have the ability to do minor crowd control, pick locks, and scout areas before the group enters. A rogue relies on evasion versus armor, and can not take substantial damage. Luckily, his agility and prowess can get him out of most sticky situations. If you have the patience to engage mobs with stealth and precision, instead of strength and force, the rogue is your class. ~Jaycin, level 10 Undead Rogue

Brief: Art in MMOs Grapics are the first thing people notice about any computer game. Without a doubt the graphics of WoW stand out from the generic MMOs of our time due to the superb and vibrant style of Samwise Didier. His colorful and exaggerate artform is prevalent in the WoW universe. Induvidual races are truely unique, especially thier habitations; cities are a spectaclular show of the cultures that live in WoW. This is a sharp contrast to the sombre and almost stark world that Sony has opted to go with in the new EQ2, whose art has been under Thomus King's excellent direction. ~ Alias
