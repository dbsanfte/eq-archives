Yesterday, we lived, breathed, and died in Dragon Necropolis.

We had a very successful raid and there were bunch of nice items and quest pieces dropped. The most prominent item being the Willsapper; gratz Marken.

After picking up the Willsapper we headed over to check out the waterfall dragon. It was decided we needed reinforcements and sent out for more troops.

Once the troops arrived we zoned out of Necropolis and to my surprise... went after Klandicar instead!

The fight against Klandicar was intense. I spent the majority of it kiting around trash with Kalo. When I would swing by and check Klandicar's health, we were s-h-r-e-d-d-i-n-g him.

I feel we did extremely well vs Klandicar and soon we will be feasting on first brood dragon meat.

We spent alot of time organizing and gathering, but I think the end fight was well worth the wait and effort. I hope those who came and helped enjoyed the hunt.
