The Codex

With regard to the controversy over guard killing, I believe the possible scenarios are too many and too varied for any specific rule or guideline to cover all potential situations. Most of us seem to be in agreement that there are times when it would be appropriate and acceptible to slay guards and other NPCs and times when it would not (not for a Truthseeker anyway). Instead of trying to anticipate every contigency and create some all-encompasssing legislation (which may not even be possible), I suggest that we simply rely on the rules and guidelines already in place and allow our members to make their decisions accordingly. Whenever this controversy arises, as a Truthseeker you must first ask yourself "Is this combat really necessary?" Everyone has the right to defend themselves against attack, so if you have no choice (can't zone, can't gate, etc.) then it seems only fair that your right of self preservation should outweigh other considerations, but if you do have a choice, then you must also ask yourself "Is it possible that my actions here could cause harm or distress to others?" If the answer is "Yes" (or if you aren't sure) then don't do it. If killing a guard or any NPC will result in nothing more than you gaining exp, loot or satisfaction (revenge, for example), with no repercussions to others, then go for it! Just don't ask me to get involved (help in combat, buffing, etc.), because I personally oppose killing non-combatants and those assigned to protect them. If you disagree with my opinion on this matter (as many members obviously do), then I submit that each of us is free to go our own way, so long as our actions do not relect badly on the guild.
