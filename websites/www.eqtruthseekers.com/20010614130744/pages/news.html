March 8th 2000

 

Wounded and trapped deep within Befallen, Ishiro waited, hoping that his message had gotten through to the other Truthseekers. Just when all hope seemed to be lost the voice of Ebonsahara rang out "Ishiro! We have come to rescue you!" The Truthseekers gathered at the entrance to this home of necromancers, more than a dozen of them had left their quests to aide a fallen brother. Ishiro could hear their battles echo through the halls and caverns as the denizens on Befallen tried to keep the Truthseekers out. Before long Ishiro himself was discovered and attacked. Wounded, but full of renewed hope, he battled his foes. The Truthseekers burst into the fray and rescued Ishiro, and along the way had assisted a dwarf, Porthoes, to find his own items long lost to the depths. With Ishiro healed and safe, the Truthseekers returned to the surface victorious!

February 29th 2000

 

Greetings Truthseekers

(The following is a guild hunt, meant for those among us from the 10 to 25 range of level)

A young man in tattered clothing crashes through the door of the Truthseekers Guild Hall, and with his last breath gasps, "Ishiro." As his soul escapes the physical world, his body slumps lifeless, and his hand rolls open, a note sliding to the floor. The guild is quiet, as no one is here. All the Truthseekers are out in the world, completing quests, seeking knowledge, slaying dragons, conquering kingdoms, or rescuing young maidens from their virginity with the assistance of alcohol.

The note is left until morning when the servants of the guild hall return, one of them takes it and reads aloud: "In the lower levels of Befallen. I have become trapped. If you have read this, my friend Dravil has done his job and reached you safely, treat him well. Please, came and remove me from this place. -Ishiro" The servant rushes to a desk and quickly sets ink to paper, hurriedly jotting several copies of a note. He turns to the others, "Quickly, to the four winds, take this message." He hands a copy of his note to each servant who promptly readies his travel gear and sets out on foot to find the Truthseekers.

Each servant travels the land, each in a different direction, each carrying this note: "Truthseekers, Ishiro has become trapped in Befallen, meet outside it's entrance in the West Commonlands as soon as you can. You must hurry."

Dungeon caution for newer members of Norrath.

Creatures initially will attack the weakest (lowest level) member. Once a creature is hit by Melee or spell it attacks the person causing the most potential damage. The clarification to this is that if a Cleric is healing a few Warriors in the fight it is the Cleric that is doing the most potential damage. If I cast Ice comet and it is fully resisted it is still me that is doing the most potential damage. Don't start a fight unless you are at full health and full mana be patient the dungeon isn't going anywhere. a lot of spells do not work in dungeons or are much harder to use. example: shadowstep is suppose to teleport you away from a mob 50-100 feet (estimate) in a dungeon you will go nowhere. also line of sight DD's and others do not work around tight corners (which dungeons are full of).

Good luck
