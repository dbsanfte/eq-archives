If The Big Fish Is Up

  1. We meet at the entrance to unrest until all are accounted for.
  2. When all are there and everyone can breathe underwater and see invis and invis are cast the direction will be given to head to Kedge and zone in, when you find the zone, zone in, no need to wait for any instruction.
  3. There are a series of pauses we will do to insure invis holds and to handle any problems with creatures we encounter along the way. Everyone meets at "Start of raid #1".
  4. As a group we proceed to "Pause 2", Pause 3", etc.. until we reach the final staging area.
  5. At the final staging area buff and make pets. (keep your pets close).
  6. Monk will pull the four seahorse individually to the final staging area until they are dead.
  7. One by one the Main tank will target a mob and when targeted tell everyone to assist. You have to use your assist hot key. Do not manually target the mob and do not use F8!
  8. When you get the command to assist, finish off the mob with your individual talents :) move on to the next mob as directed by the main tank and use your assist key only. From the time we kill the 4 sea horses we have about 5 minutes to med before Phinigel
  9. Phinigel and guardains will be pulled to the staging area. When he is pulled do NOT wait for the command to attack just attack. Phinigel is the target, Guardains are mezzed. When Phinigel is dead we kill the mezzed guardains. Pulling Monks be careful not to get stuck under the floor while pulling.
  10. We then enter the lair until 3 Paladins get thier bone. If there are 3.
  11. On command everyone goes back to the final staging area, with invis up we then go back to the south room at Pause 2.
  12. We then Proceed to take the northern route and take out any named up. Estrella,Tainted,Undertow, etc...

Phinnie only has 16000 hit points approximately. Gregory and I, by ourselves, can do that much damage if we didn't have to cast snares/roots and resists. So with the number, level, and caliber of people we have there shouldn't be too much trouble if everyone plays smart and watches their breathing. Remember we are there to have fun. Don't plan on any loot or experience. Those will be a by-product of our trip but not a reason for going.
