Guild Structure

Guild Officers are selected out of the membership by the Council when either a position opens due to the demise or retirement of an existing Officer or from necessity due to growth of the Guild.  The structure consists of The Guild Leader, Assistant GL and Class Representatives, The Council decides on appointing the Class Representatives. These are all members of the Council. A majority vote of the Council will be enough to demote an officer of the Guild who the Council believes is unresponsive to the needs of the Guild. An Officer of Truthseekers may not be a member of another Guild.

Guild Leader:

There will be one Guild Leader. The Guild Leader is the ultimate decision-maker and will declare, plan and coordinate all Guild Policies. The Guild Leader receives input from the Council through a variety of communications channels. The Representatives will gather information from all Members on issues, suggestions, complaints, and ideas. The Guild Leader formats this information for Council discussion and review. Once a decision is reached by the Council that document is amended and passed on to the membership for review.  Representatives will then pass on comments to the Guild Leader for final actions. If the action or document is approved it is immediately acted upon or incorporated.

First Advisor:

There will be one First Advisor. The First Advisor has the same responsibilities as the GL in the GL's absence. The First Advisor will serve as advisor to the GL. The First Advisor will coordinate and Manage the recruiting effort, although all Guild members can and should recruit. All members will direct new members to the web page. The First Advisor will follow through on the application process. The First Advisor will assume the GL position should the GL be unable to continue in the position or if absent without notice for 7 days.
See Recruiting section of the Charter and Rules for more information

Representatives

There will be a Representative for each class with over 5 members in it and a Representative for multiple classes with less than 5 members to help with the communications with the membership. The Representatives will aid in the dissemination of information from the Council. The Representative will hold Class meetings for the discussion of concerns of the membership. Members will go to their Representatives when you feel you need help with hunting parties, What to hunt, Where to hunt, and other Class specific areas.

 IMPORTANT: Members will address all guild concerns or issues to their representatives for Council discussion and resolution. All members have a voice in all that happens in the guild, use it! Without these Officers the Guild would be impossible to manage. The Representatives are full voting members of the Council. If you feel that an issue has not been addressed or are having trouble contacting your Representative, feel free to talk to the First Advisor or the GL. A second Representative will be added to the class when the class has 20 members.

Guild Scribe:

The Scribe handles gathering information for the website. Taking the minutes at Council and other meetings as necessary. If you have guild news or if you are planning or scheduling an event please pass this information onto the scribe for action. The Scribe should try to keep track of our levels and keep the information on the website up to date. After an event involving any Guild Member (we are sometimes invited to other events) The scribe collects information and sends a follow up report to the Guild Leader and a Report will be posted in the News section of the web page. The Scribe can have up to 2 Assistant Scribes.
