Guild Charter

We, the members of the Truthseekers, promise to uphold all that is right and true in our quests in Norrath.We will conduct ourselves with honor and dignity in all of our transactions. We will help those in need to the best of our powers. We will at all times conduct ourselves in the most honest and respectful of ways. We will not scam, steal, or act dishonorable at any time. We are a good law abiding guild and we will conduct ourselves in a manner befitting that.

Guild Purpose

The Truthseekers is a Good Aligned Guild whose purpose includes the following points:

  • Helping to uphold the Honor of the Guild.

  • The Guild is bound to help citizens in need of assistance.
  • The Guild will look after there own above all else.
  • Giving due respect to all people of Norrath regardless of experience.
  • Sharing our knowledge of the adventures and quests of Norrath, with all the Good people of the lands.

THROUGH GOOD WORKS THE GUILD SHOULD PROMOTE A REPUTATION OF HONOR

Guild Guidelines

Guidelines for conduct and performance for the membership of Truthseekers are as follows:

  • All Officers are expected to attend Officer meetings. Members are expected to attend meetings scheduled by their Class Representatives. This will keep us from trying to schedule a meeting and force a hundred members to meet at unrealistic times and places.

  • Foul, abusive, or vulgar language will not be used by a member of the our Guild at any time.

  • Rude behavior by a Guild member will not be tolerated.

  • No arguing or harsh words will be tolerated on the Guild Channel, any disagreement is to be taken to /T immediately. Officers will NEVER disagree on the Channel

  • Multiple Characters are allowed a Signifying mark will be made on the members page to link the Character.

  • Members of the Guild are not allowed to beg for anything.

  • Members making a profit on an item obtained from the Guild or another Guild member at below Auction Channel cost, then sold on the AC for a profit, must share that profit with the individual or the Guild.

  • Stealing from any player in the game will not be tolerated and will be subject to immediate expulsion from the Guild. There are a number of ways to steal ALL are included. i.e. Kill Stealing

  • No player Killing allowed

  • No involvement in Guild War.

  • The decisions of the Council are final.

Truthseekers being an honorable guild will not engage in the act of killing guards that help us protect the citizens of Norrath.

This rule should not restrict the members of Truthseekers from accomplishing personnal goals in Norrath as long as the actions are done with honor. There are numerous quests and faction adjustments that can be made by the killing of certain guards. For example: An Ogre can kill corrupt guards in East Freeport and by turning in the spoils of the battle they can increase faction with the good guards of North Freeport allowing them to do banking there. Truthseekers also reserve the right to protect themselves from all attacks.

The world of Norrath is filled with all types of guards. The corrupt guards of Qeynos, The corrupt Freeport Militia, The Honorable guards of Felwithe, and the Watchmen of Steamfont to name a few. This guideline does not address the killing of all the guards in Norrath. The question of Honor in this regard will be left up to the individual. The purpose of this clarification to the Charter is clearly stated as pertaining only to those guards that help us protect the citizens of Norrath. There are guards throughout the land that aid us in the protection of citizens. These guards are our allies in the assistance of citizens and we should aid them not kill them.

The Use of Guild Chat

All members will be encouraged to move all sales and item requests to the message board and leave the guild chat channel free for other discussion.

Dueling

Dueling is a matter of personal choice, however dueling out of anger at another player should be avoided. We are here to make friends not to make enemies. If you end up in a situation like this it has probably gone too far and you should be apologizing for your behavior.

Borrowing

Begging is already against the Charter and is grounds for immediate removal from the Guild. Asking for money should be frowned upon. We all should make do with what we have or schedule a hunt with friends to get sought after items. We are now going to places that offer great loot for any level. The credit system is an illusion that can very quickly get out of control with situations such as someone owing 10 people money that they could never pay back. We are not banning borrowing but will restrict it. A member may ask another member for 1 loan and should only have 1 loan until it is paid. A member should never ask a non member for anything. All loans should be no more than a person can pay back in a reasonable amount of time. The Maximum loan will be 100pp. Some of these requirements will have to be self managed and each member should police their actions and act with Honor (it is what are Guild is all about). Any abuse will result in a reprimand and maybe cause for dismissal from the guild. note: Dow (another guild) had a problem with lower members constantly asking for items and money from the higher level players which drove some higher level players out and the guild is no more. We will not follow that route. Our higher level members worked very hard to get there, we need to do the same. If you think you will have a problem with this pass on your loan information to Gregory to help you keep track of it.

Gaining Membership in the Truthseekers

  • The Guild will be exclusively located on the E'ci  server for EverQuest.

  • After agreeing with the Charter and the rules,  any citizen who is level 10 or above and who would like to become a member of the Guild should fill out the Application Form, including character level, race, and class. We will respond within 24 hours. If your not level 10 fill out the Application anyway and we will follow your path to 10.

  • A Citizen will then be contacted by 2 Officers as  directed by the Recruiting Director.

  • The 2 Officers will schedule a 2 hour hunt (each) to get to know the prospective member. The Citizen will be required to make the time available to hunt with the 2 Officers.

  • The 2 Officers will select a hunting ground in-line  with the applicants level. The hunts will take place within one week of the receipt of the Application.

  • After the week is through the 2 Officers will  make a mutual decision on weather or not the Citizen meets the requirements and has the characteristics that fit the Guild.

  • If the two Officers agree the Citizen will  receive an invite. If the Officers disagree or the Officers agree that the applicant will not make an acceptable member then the Citizen will be informed that he has not been accepted with a reason why.

  • At this time there is no mechanism in place to  allow a Citizen to resubmit an Application after rejection by the 2 Officers. A persons Character takes years and years to develop. It is only wishful thinking to believe someone can turn their character around in our short time in Norrath.

  •  

    Opinions from ALL members are welcome and  sought after.  Direct you approval or concerns to the Recruiting Director to be passed on to the 2 Officers doing the Process.

  • Once accepted as a member of the Guild, a member  is responsible for knowing and following all the information contained in the Guild Charter, and Rules.

Advancement

  • To become a Protector, a Guild member must complete  assigned tasks and have their performance evaluated (not completed).
  • Officers will be selected from the membership by the  Council as needed to run the Guild.
  • A Guild member that recruits 10 Quality members has earned  a Representatives position.

 Final Notes

Truthseekers is not here to help you get stuff for your character, or to help you become a powergamer, nor do we want people that are just looking to have a name above their heads. Remember the reason you are apart of or wish to be apart of the team is so YOU can help others, yes I do understand that we all are on the need for help side from time to time, and the team will be more than glad to assist you when your luck is down, but please keep in mind that we are all trying to advance our characters, we all have been in the same boat as you and have paved our own path. Does this mean you can't ask, No not at all, if you do need assistance please feel free to ask. But keep in mind, we all want better armor, better weapons, more money, etc. Remember if you are willing to help your self, then others will be more willing to help you.
