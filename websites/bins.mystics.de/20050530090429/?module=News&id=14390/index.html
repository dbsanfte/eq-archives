Alpha FAQ Part 1: Registration Questions


Where can I sign up for the DDO Alpha?
The url is http://beta.turbine.com/signup. You need to enter a valid email address on this page, and pass the image verification challenge (this is put in place to prevent automated registrations). Once these are submitted, an email will be sent to the entered address with a link that lets you continue registration.


Why can’t I see the verification image?
Make sure that your browser is set to display images. If this doesn’t work, try using a different browser or computer.


I entered my email address and passed the image verification but I never got the response email. Please help!
Well first of all, email sometimes gets delayed, and can in some cases take over an hour to reach your inbox. If it’s been over an hour since you requested the email, try checking your spam folder to see if the email got flagged as spam. If you still can’t find the email, try disabling your spam filter (if you have one), and then try entering your email address on the signup form again. If this still doesn’t work, try using a different email address.


Please note that if you have a spam filter that requires manual confirmation of emails sent from unknown addresses, we will be unable to confirm these emails for you. If you have this type of spam filter in place, please use a different email address.


When I click on the link in the verification email, I get an “Invalid Registration” message.
If you get this message, your verification email has probably already been used to create an account. If this is the case, please login at http://beta.turbine.com/login. If you’ve forgotten your password, you can retrieve it by going to http://beta.turbine.com/password. If you’re positive that you did not create an account, you can start the process over at http://beta.turbine.com/signup.


I started the signup process, but didn’t get a chance to finish it. Now when I try to use the link in the verification email to get back to where I started, I’m getting the “Invalid Registration” message.
As long as you made it past the screen where you enter a username and password, your account was created. You should be able to login at http://beta.turbine.com/login in order to complete your registration. If you’ve forgotten your password, you can retrieve it by going to http://beta.turbine.com/password.


Okay, I finished registration and got to the “Congratulations” screen, but never got any corresponding email. Did I definitely finish? Is there anything else I need to do?
Once you get to the “Congratulations” screen, your registration is complete. There is no confirmation email. If you’re interested in learning more about the game, you should register at the DDO Community Forums if you haven’t already done so.


I recently upgraded my computer and would like to edit my profile, but I can’t figure out where to do it.
At this time, there is no way to go back and edit your profile.


I can’t remember my username. Can you help me find it?
At this time, no. If you get picked for alpha or beta, your selection email will include your username in case you’ve forgotten it. You will also be able to request your password at that time as well if you need to.




Alpha FAQ Part 2: Alpha Status


Did you let anyone into Alpha yet?

We have successfully tested the Alpha signup and game client download process. Currently Turbine employees are playing the game in its Alpha state. In the near future, we plan on adding the first wave of external players to Alpha, and we will continue adding additional players on a regular basis.


When does Alpha start? When does Beta start?
There are two slated Alpha phases, Alpha 0 and Alpha 1. After those are completed, we will start with Beta 1, then Beta 2, then Beta 3, etc.


What’s the difference between Alpha and Beta?
The Beta phases will have significantly more players in them than the Alpha phases.


Will there ever be an open Beta?
This has not yet been decided.


If I pre-order the game, will I be guaranteed to get into Beta?
No pre-order details have been announced at this time.


If I register for the forums, do I have a better chance of getting into Alpha or Beta?
Yes. Just make sure you use the same email address for both signups. You can register for the forums at forums/register.php.


What’s the Turbine Beta Rewards program?
Current subscribers to Asheron’s Call (1 or 2) may be eligible for the Turbine Beta Rewards program, which gives members preferred status for the DDO Beta and other future Turbine Betas. For more information on the Beta Rewards program, please visit http://www.fuzeqna.com/asheronscall/consumer/kbdetail.asp?kbid=919.


Is there anything else I can do to increase my chances of getting into Alpha or Beta?

Other than making sure to sign up for the forums, not really. We may be hand-selecting some players based on the quality (not necessarily quantity) of their posts however, so we do encourage you to participate in the forums. We’ll be inviting people based on a number of factors – our goal is to build a large, diverse beta community, so we’ll be looking for players with a wide range of experience. Of course, there will also be a large random element involved as well. The best advice we can give is signup, answer the questions as accurately (and truthfully) as possible, register on the forums, and keep your fingers crossed!
