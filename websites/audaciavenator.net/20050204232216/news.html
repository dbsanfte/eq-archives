News - February 2005

February 4th, 2005 - What's AV been up to?

AV has been cranking out kills and loots since our last update and have made some amazing progress. We adventured into the depths of Splitpaw to reveal the truth about this new revamp so you don't have to. What did AV find in our adventures in Splitpaw? We came to the conclusion this zone sucks ass, and has no value whatsoever, at least none that we found. If there is anything worthwhile in this zone, besides that lame 60hp aug which requires entirely too much effort for the reward, Sony has done well in hiding it so you will never find it, since you will leave the zone with hate in your eyes before you ever discover it.

What else have we done? We killed Heyda a few times, XTC, Bertox, worked on a new mob (which shall remain nameless at this time) and most importantly...

We actually got him to 17% but I was a bit too busy by that point to be off snapping pictures. Sony was truly against us on this run as we had more client crashes and ld's on this raid then we have had in ages. Hopefully todays patch is addressing that. We had 2 charm classes and a ranger go LD on pull, and RZ stopped and whacked 2 clerics on his way to the corner, I'd say 17% is what he gained in that exchange. Regardless of his tricks on this attempt, we are gonna chump him as soon as humanly possible. 17% ain't shit Rallos, you got lucky this time, next time you won't be so lucky.
