Serenity Within


Serenity Within is dedicated to providing a home for it's members within the wilds of Norrath. We are a family, and each of us would gladly give of themselves to help any of their brothers and sisters in Serenity Within. We are not motivated by the desire for wealth, or advancement, or items, but instead by the desire to enjoy the richness that Norrath has to offer in the company of our friends. We adventure together, go on quests together, and fight monsters together, and are always willing to help each other out in any endeavour. Our members will always have someone to talk to, a shoulder to cry on when things go bad, and someone to celebrate with them when things go well. Each member of our family contributes a part to the whole, and no one member carries greater or less worth than another.

Serenity Within's members are not mercenary in nature, nor motivated by the lust for power or greed. This sets us apart from many other guilds. We strive to set an example to others, and our strength comes from our trust and respect for each other, and our ability to work together to overcome any obstacle. Our members will never attempt to profit from another's misfortune, insult, or cheat others. They will be steadfast in combat, and conduct themselves with honor and skill wherever they go. Most importantly, members of our family are mature and will resolve conflicts without resorting to childish conduct and arguments. We provide an example for new adventurers in Norrath to follow, and we uphold the qualities that have kept us together for so long. We are Serenity Within.
