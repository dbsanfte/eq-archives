Infantry players, play ALL of the Skirmish Zones!

Effective Wednesday, December 11, Infantry Online games will change in the way that Exp, Rank, and Stats in all [SK] Skirmish zones are handled. This means that players who have all of their Exp on one map (and this pertains to most players) will be able to play on ALL maps and keep their exp/rank/privileges.

For more specific information on these game changes, go to the Infantry news page.
