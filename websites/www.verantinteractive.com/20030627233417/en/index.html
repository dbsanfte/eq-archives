The EQ Fan Faire Goes To Chicago!
On September 26-28, 2003, EverQuest fans will blow into the Windy City! It's been announced that the Fall Fan Faire will be held at the Sheraton Chicago Hotel and Towers. There will be discounted room rates and airfare rates for selected airlines. For more information and for pre-registration, go to the newly launched Chicago Fan Faire site!
New EQ Zippo Lighter Series On Sale!
Because our first official EverQuest Zippo lighter series has been a smashing success, the Station.com Store is proud to present our latest series of EQ Zippo collectible lighters. These newest custom crafted lighters pay tribute to eleven EverQuest character classes from Bard to Warrior.
Introducing The SOE All Access Pass!
"SOE All Access" means that subscribers to this new service will have access to all current Station premium games for one low fee of $21.99 per month! This will include EverQuest, PlanetSide, EverQuest Online Adventures, the Station Pass games, and EverQuest II (scheduled to launch this Fall). The SOE All Access service will be offered on May 19th, just in time for the launch of PlanetSide, SOE's newest multiplayer game. For additional information, see the official press release.
Upcoming EQII Incentives Announced!
SOE has announced that special incentives will be offered to subscribers of both EQII and original EverQuest. These rewards will include crossover quest zones, allowing characters in EverQuest II and EverQuest exclusive access to hidden treasures; and a special EverQuest II in-game reward. Over the coming months, even more incentives will be disclosed. For more details, see the official press release.
