The Charter of Primal Brood

Primal Brood is an Everquest Test Server guild for serious high-level players interested in exploring some of the most dangerous places in Norrath. Members of Primal Brood endeavor to play skillfully and work together as a team to accomplish progressively greater challenges. Every member of Primal Brood is important, respected and appreciated.

We have a system of Rules and Guidelines that is designed to prevent arguing or disruption of game play. All Rules and Guidelines are clearly posted and are expected to be upheld. The guild leader is entrusted to protect the interests and vision of the guild and has final say on any proposed rules or guild lines, which would threaten to damage the vision.

Several things to know about Primal Brood:

  • Our primary mission is to raid and seek to play together regularly.
  • All members have a "level focus" and are actively seeking to reach level 60 and further enhance their character with raid friendly AA abilities.
  • We play skillfully and look for others that do the same.
  • We have a low overhead, politics reduction, guild management system.
  • Every member's opinion is important and should be treated with respect.
  • Members of Primal Brood endeavor to be honorable, kind, and respected by the Test Server community.

Attitude:

We expect every member of Primal Brood to be respectful, polite, and empathetic to other players on the Test Server. Our members are mature, responsible, and caring and it is expected that they will act that way. Any inexplicable reports of bad behavior (i.e. disrespect, rudeness, disregard for other players safety) will result in warnings and possible expulsion from the guild.

Attitude is not limited to:

  1. Being nice to people and being easy to be around.
  2. Your willingness and desire to raid. We are primarily a raiding guild.
  3. Not being loot centered/oriented. If someone in Primal Brood needs the item for raiding more than you, chances are you will not get that item before they do. Primal Brood awards loot. There is no point system, no random rolling.

Buffs:

Primal Brood sends tells for buffs. Primal Brood announces buff assignments at the start of each raid. Write down who has what you want because we do not use /ooc, /shout, /gu or /raidchannel for buff requests. With certain exceptions, i.e. haste, only group leaders will send tells for buffs. If you are not the group leader, do not send tells for group buffs. In emergency situations, it is acceptable for the MT or pullers to use the raid channel to request buffs or anyone to request heals. Be polite to those buffing you, they are not your servants. Be understanding and patient if a limited number of a buffing class are available, or if they are out of mana. Don't suggest methods for them to get additional mana, be assured they already know they can cannabalize, use mod rods, ask the necros, or whatever methods are currently available.

Class Balance/Characters:

While Primal Brood membership is not based specifically upon what class a person plays, it is a factor. If a person is undecided upon which "main" class or character they wish to play in the end game, we suggest you take the time to determine that prior to applying to Primal Brood. The character with which you apply to Primal Brood should be the one you have settled upon as your "main". We need to maintain class balance as much as possible. This may affect your membership into the guild.

Communication:

Pledges and members of the guild should NEVER argue over /guildchat and/or /channel, it is unacceptable in all situations. If you have a disagreement with someone, we expect you to be mature and use tells to civilly resolve your differences.

Primal Brood forbids arguing or negative comments that reduce the enjoyment of our members and pledges in /guildchat, /channel or the message boards. This includes, but is not limited to, discussion of politics, religion and any other "hot" topics. Discussion of current events that are important to Americans may offend our Danish members or visa versa. People should feel free to communicate openly about anything which doesn't cause emotional frustration or negative attitudes, provided the communication is either related to the game or is being appreciated by the majority of the players (example, a humorous conversation).

People are free to use whatever language they see fit, so long as the purpose is not to attack another person. If you do not like to see "curse" words, we suggest you turn your filter back on. Please try to limit lewdness to something that wouldn't offend the average sailor.

Do not use /guildchat and/or /channel to carry on a conversation with another person (or several people) in which the majority of the guild is not involved or interested. An example of inappropriate /guildchat and/or /channel conversation would be three members discussing their feelings on bowling, where as no one else cares or wants to hear it. Use tells or create a raid channel for your conversation so as not to spam the main communication channels. Some people consider this kind of conversation as /guildchat and/or /channel spam.

When raiding, do not assume authority to communicate things which you are not responsible for, this would include instructions to zone or camp in bad situations, and giving instructions. The raid leader assigns people these responsiblities, any additional instruction just confuses people. Be sensative to "over-using" guild chat. People that are excessively chatty tend to make people that are quiet wish to turn off guildchat. It is not acceptable for members to turn off guildchat, it is our primary source of cross server communication. Greeting, goodbyes, game discussion, travel arrangements, distress calls, humor are all appropriate for guildchat. Anything that is generating a lot of chat can be easily moved to a raid channel to reduce the amount of guild spam those not interested are subjected too.

Connectivity:

Going LD every 5 minutes is counterproductive to everyone. A stable internet connection is highly recommended and we encourage you to contact your ISP to determine any alterations you can make to enhance it. Additionally, it is recommended that you look into purchasing the Shadows of Luclin if you do not have it already.

Focus:

Primal Brood follows it's Charter closely and takes it very seriously. If you still feel you would like to be considered for membership, you can fill out the application for review.

Primal Brood does not consider "guild shoppers" to be serious or focused. So if you've applied to other guilds, it would be to our mutual benefit if you concentrated your efforts on them. This will increase your chances of gaining acceptance into the other guild(s).

    • Loot:

    Primal Brood guildleader, Foofee, determines who will be awarded loot based upon the raiding needs of the guild as a whole and in certain situations, raid leaders authorized by Foofee will award loot. This method is not open to debate or discussion. We do not equip characters that have not contributed to the guild in any way. Requesting loot for other characters is severely looked down upon and will seriously affect your membership consideration. If this system of rules is not comfortable for you, then you should not consider applying for membership.

      Loot is decided based on:
    • Raid participation. Contribution = raiding. There are some other things you can do to help, offer ports, help people with quests, collect quest gems, donate things, etc. but from the prospective of earning items, the biggest contributing factor is raiding. Show up for the raids.
    • Who needs an item the most and how it benefits the guild the most. Show up consistently. If you appear for a week, then disappear for 3 or more. Don't expect to be rewarded items. Items do no one in the guild any service if you're not going to play with us somewhat consistently. This means, if you can only show up for one day a week, don't miss that day. If you cannot play this consistently, please don't discuss how you feel you are being shafted on loot. Foofee keeps track of how often you go to all events, but he doesn't sort them by individual encounter. If you want Item X, the best way to get it is to show up for every run you can. If you only show up for Item X runs and do not contribute to the guild in any other way, you may be asked to leave.
    • How far along you are on a quest/faction. (such as epics) or how your faction is with the quest giver.)
    • Level. If you are not level 60, get levels - that is part of your contribution. Foofee will not reward a non-level 60 items if he doesn't see a members level moving at a reasonable pace.
    • Attitude. Are you constantly asking for loot that is obviously not appropriate for you? Do you feel you can debate a loot decision with the guild leader? Are you constantly negative about everything? Primal Brood is NOT the guild for you then. Think about your own actions and how they are perceived by other because others certainly will remember and vote appropriately. It wears on people and then they don't want to be around you.

    All of the above are taken into consideration when loot is decided with how it helps the guild to accomplish goals taking priority over other factors.

    Raiding:

    We raid almost every day. We kill many boss mobs and sometimes die figuring out how to defeat them. Be prepared to learn and die. In order for you to get to know us, and for us to get to know you, you need to spend a significant amount of time raiding and grouping with Primal Brood members. This is the only way to see if you "mix" well with us. If you apply and disappear without communication for more than two weeks, you will be removed from consideration and required to reapply when you have more time to dedicate to getting to know us.

      Reasons for denied membership or guild-removal:
    1. Constant negativity (especially concerning loot).
    2. Attempting to lead Primal Brood anywhere for a "raid" without prior authorization from guildleader.
    3. Sharing your account information with non-Primal Brood players from any server.
    4. Sharing our strategies and tactics with people outside guild.
    5. Sharing things said in /guildchat or /raidchannel with people outside guild.
    6. Only participating in raids that can further your character(s).
    7. Using the name "Primal Brood" to intimidate others.
    8. Defaming Primal Brood with rude and insulting conduct to others; or by badmouthing Primal Brood.
    9. Ninjalooting, Killstealing & Training.
    10. Selling items on ebay.

    Skillful Playing:

    We will look at the following to determine if you are playing skillfully or are willing to learn to play skillfully.
    1. Waiting for proper instructions before engaging.
    2. Listening to the raid leader and paying attention.
    3. Not interfering with the announced main tank assignment. (Trying to steal agro, etc.)
    4. Aggro control and management.
    5. Refraining from speaking, emoting, spam eating food/drink and similar activities while instructions are being given.
    6. Following directions closely.
    7. Asking questions of your group leader or a class-peer if you don't understand something.
    8. Being able to determine when it is appropriate to take initiative (doing helpful things without being told).
    9. Refraining from taking initiative when it is not appropriate (taking responsibility for things not assigned to you over the appropriate person)

    Transportation:

    Primal Brood understands that we might take you to zones that you have never been to before but everyone should know how to get to SG zoneline. Movement is a critical element of getting dozens of groups from one event to the next. Knowing how to get across basic zones on your own if left behind is critical. See eqatlas for maps.
