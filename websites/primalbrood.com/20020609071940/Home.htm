Ssra Temple - Rhag defeated

Following our defeat of Zlandicar, we decided to visit Ssra Temple and see our friend Rhag'Zhezum. It was good we arrived when we did to say our last goodbyes before he collapsed. The reading of the will immediately followed and several of us inherited his treasured belonging. Don't feel bad though, the Shissar believe in reincarnation and I am sure he will be back soon.
