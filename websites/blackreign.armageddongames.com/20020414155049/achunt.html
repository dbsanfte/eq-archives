THE SRO ANCIENT CYCLOPS HUNT

Hunted by BLACK REIGN on OCTOBER 19 & 20, 2001


Eleven of us blanketed South Ro for 2 Consecutive Nights and a total of 14 hours. At times there were very high level groups, rangers, druids, and morons. WE PREVAILED! It was a Grade-A Success. Here's how it went down . . . .

Friday. 7 Hours. No pops. Rman, Momur, Mastlia, Giesk, Vven and Bumf provide the man-power. A few Sand Giants provide entertainment during the night. Ah well, there is always tomorrow. We are undaunted.

At 6:40 Saturday Night Momur spots the AC! Master Tracker that she is, she loses it in the desert! No matter, Vven out-nuked Giesk and ripped the first AC a "new one". There were some cries and rants about KS'ing, but I laid the first spell on it and Black Reign took it down! Rolls Giesk 66, Tynazel 84, Verlorenamour 94, Momur 0 (Yes Zero. Stop Laughing). Verlorenamour takes the ring! Hehehe, from now on Tynazel is going to have trouble keeping up with her. Verlorenamour remained to help the rest of us hunt. Thanks!

The night is winding down. Vven, Staven & Mastlia have trouble with a certain Named Orc and have to do some zoning and other shenanigans. Only 4 of us left hunting in zone, Mastlia, Momur, Rman and Israiel. We are about to call it a night at about 11pm. Then Pop! Pop! Pop! Terrorantula! Sand Giant! Sand Giant! Momur was down to a HAIR of hp when Rman and Izzy saved her sorry butt. Med up. Something is gonna happen. Mo spots another AC! Charms it and calls Mastlia, Rman and Israiel to partake in the slaughter. It took only four of us to drop the second one! We are undisputed. Rman takes the random roll and the ring!

So, my Familiars, after long camping and detailed analysis, I think I know what made the Ancient Cyclops Spawn and I know where it will spawn. That will remain a Black Reign Secret.

ps Everyone should know that Mastlia already has JBoots and put in 10 hours on the hunt. It is cool that you took so much time to help your Brothers & Sisters. Thanks Mastlia!
