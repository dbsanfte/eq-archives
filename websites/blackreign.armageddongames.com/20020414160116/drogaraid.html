WRAP-UP REPORT for TEMPLE OF DROGA RAID

Raided by BLACK REIGN on September 2, 2001


Twelve of us Raided Droga. It went pretty damn well. Here's the tale . . . .

We initially formed up with Davth the sole healer, Rezilaturb Chief Tank, Giesk Nuking, Blacknite and Delicti our Black Knights, and Momur Buffing and DeBuffing. Kadath provided critical extra power and our Dreadlord Talan handled pulls.

It was childs play locating the poorly concealed Temple entrance in the North-West Corner of Frontier Mountains. Overpowering the half-dozen or so guards was a mere prelude to the carnage that followed. Our roles and responsibilities now understood, we rolled into the tunnels and dropped through the concealed entrance. Awaiting us were a trio of unfortunate goblins. Their heads were promptly separated from their shoulders. There was no turning back.

In a manner reminiscent of ingenious Gnome machinery, Black Reign cleared room after room en-route to the Inner Sanctum. Our Tactic? Talan carefully pulled 1 or 2 pitiful creatures into the waiting blades and powers of the rest of us. Davth diligently kept us all heathly.

At Last! The Gate Room. The Inner Sanctum is Breached! At this time Brother Xerzzel began periodic unholy sacrifices imploring the Father to aid us in our effort. Brothers Manadae, Staven, Vven and Sabrar were incoming. We decide to hold the Gate Room until they arrive. Here was nearly our undoing. A series of rapid Harm Touches from Goblin Mosstroopers killed Kadath. Vven died trying to reach the Gate Room. And what must have been a Dozen Goblins took our Dreadlord down! Still we held the Gate Room. Some tense moments ensued, but, having regrouped, 11 of us proceeded . . . . without Talan.

Manadae assumed the role of puller. The new infusion of strength became essential to our advance as Momur, peeking around a corner to get his bearings, brought four goblins sweeping into our camp. These were killed and we proceeded.

Outside of the Table Room, assaulted by a rapid series of Harm Touches, Davth was briefly reunited with Cazic-Thule! We cannot proceed without our healer. Momentarily, Talan and Davth returned to us.

For the first time we are a Full Dozen Strong! Room after room was assaulted and cleared until our ultimate goal was achieved . . . . Chief RukGus' Ledge Room!

Two incarnations of RukGus and a Maddened Burynai fall to Black Reign. RukGus' Sticker and Essence of Dol drop. Hundreds of plat in coin and mundane items are gained. And at Midnight we disband, port, and gate across Norrath.


TECHNICAL SUMMARY

As far as Raids go, I'd give this one a solid "B" grade.

  • (+) We worked well as two interdependent groups. An extremely difficult, stressful task made easier by a very strong Group 2.

  • (-) We started 1 hour late and wasted 1 hour in the Gate Room. Had we been on-time, we could have camped RukGus much, much longer and gotten substantially more loot.

  • (+) The overall turnout was Strong - 12 people.

  • (-) If we are to take better targets, we MUST HAVE more healers!

  • (+) Everyone was well disciplined and patient. Only 2 or 3 "poorly conceived" moves. (One of which was me, Momur, pulling 4 goblins)

  • (-) 4 of 12 Died. 33%. One-third. Maybe could have been zero to two max.

  • (+) Didn't get lost. Didn't get trained. didn't bicker, bitch or moan.

  • (-) Bind Affinity. Know it. Love it. Use it.

  • (+) Although probably too low level to be useful, we did get loot with almost 1,000pp street value.
