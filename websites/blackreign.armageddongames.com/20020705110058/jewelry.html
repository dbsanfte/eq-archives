Jewelry 101

The Dark Elf Enchanter's Guide to Becoming a Jeweler

Before We Start

Most people say that Jewelcraft is practical for Enchanters and others shouldn't bother with it. I tend to agree. It can be a pain to find an enchanter to enchant a stack of bars for you. Even if the enchanter doesn't charge you for enchanting the bars (a donation is usually due), you have a lot of cash tied up in enchanted bar stock. You are holding onto stock which is cash you can't spend elsewhere. It's expensive, no doubt. But, of course, anyone can be a jeweler, since you don't have to enchant metals to increase your skill.

There's a lot of info to be found at Everquest Realm and this is My Chart. I suggest you print out the chart. Write down "your cost" under each gem and bar heading, and write down the Trivial Value under each piece of jewelry. As you gain experience selling, you can also write down the "sell price" or "going rate". I've found this is a very good way of keeping track of things.

The Basics

No need to start until at least 8th Level when you get the Enchant Silver Spell. Save your Practice Points. Put 20 Practice Points into Jewelcraft. Why all the practice points? Because you avoid losing cash due to failures all the way up to 20 and you get right into the midst of the skill level needed to make useful jewelry. You might consider not starting until level 16 when you get Enchant Electrum and have some cash saved up.

Now look at the "Trivial Values" on My Chart. . This is the Skill Level needed to make that Jewelry without failures! So at 20 Skill the Silver Hematite Ring is Trivial (but useless), your next goal is to make Silver Cat's Eye Necklaces which is Trivial at 22.

Go to Shiney Tings in the Neriak Foreign Quarter and buy a Jewelry Making Bag and a Stack of Silver Bars. There is no need to enchant the silver bars. You are practicing. You'll be increasing your skill level and selling the stuff back to the vendor. This way you skill up and conserve cash. The vendor pays the same for magic or mundane Jewelry.

Run to the Bauble in Neriak Third Gate, buy 4 Cat's Eye Agate. Combine one silver bar and one Cat's Eye to "TRY" to make a necklace. As you practice and make jewelry, It's a good idea to keep track of your exact skill level, so you know when to move on to the next Gem and don't waste time and money. If you succeed in making necklaces, sell them back to the vendor. If you have not achieved Skill 22, buy a few more Cat's Eyes. If you have achieved skill 22, buy 4 of the next Gem - Bloodstone. Repeat.

When I set up for a serious practice session, I'll bind at Shiney Tings, I'll buy 40 bars at a time and head over to the gem dealer. When I need more bars, I gate back to Shiney Tings.

Skilling Up

Continue with Cat's Eye, Bloodstone, Onyx . . . stay in silver until you can make the Jade Silver Ring - Skill 40. Then switch to Electrum Malechite Bracelet which requires skill level 74. Why? Because after Jade is Pearl, where each failure will cost you 5pp to 6pp, while Electrum Malechite only costs 3pp per failure. Oh, about failures, keep your INT high to reduce them.

Stay in Electrum until skill level 114, the Electrum Peridot Bracelet. Then switch to Gold and Malachite.

Now once you start on Gold Malachite, you could start enchanting the gold. You can recover a little more plat if you have your gold enchanted, but it's gonna take longer to skill up. Once you start making Golden Sapphire Earrings, you actually earn some cash, on a per item basis, if the gold is enchanted (but it will never offset the failures.)

You will take gold all the way through the toughest, storebought gems - Ruby. The reason that you shouldn't jump to Plat-Malachite after Fire Emerald is that your failure rate with gold is very low at this point, so stick with the gold. Let me explain - there's some good news / bad news. The good news is that at about skill level 180, Pearl, your failure rate drops enormously. So you are not losing a whole lot of cash to failures. The bad news is that you will skill up very, very, very slowly. I'm talking about 1 point per 40 attempts. I have gone nearly 100 attempts without getting a skill point.

Once you've reached Skill 207, proceed with Platinum. Blast up to 250. And don't forget to tell your customers that there is always a chance of failure when you make jewelry.

Some Tips

Tip 1 - Once you reach skill 35 you can make some cash. Three items are in demand by this time: +2 INT rings, +2 STR rings and +2 WIS rings. Make a pair of each (wear the INT rings) and sell them at the Newbie Log or in EC. You can occasionally find a Monk to buy the AGI rings, but you might be carrying them around for a while, so I don't deal with those anymore. Sell the INT and WIS rings for 3pp each or 5pp a pair. STR rings are 4pp each or 8pp a pair. Be patient, always carry some rings, and have an auction hotkey. Depending on how much time you spend, you could sell about 100 of these over a course of a week. That's some nice plat profit.

Tip 2 - The next target is skill level 62. At this level three items are desirable. In order, they are: the Silver ruby Veil (+4 STR +4 WIS) for Druids and Clerics, the Silver Sapphire Necklace (+4 STR + 4 INT) for Casters and SK's, and the Star Ruby Veil (+5 DEX +5 CHA) for Enchanters mostly. You should wear the star Ruby Veil and the Silver Sapphire Necklace, but always offer to sell them! These cost from 70 to 130pp to make and sell for 150 to maybe 200pp each. I once traded a Ruby Veil (130pp) for a Flowing Black Robe which I later sold for 200pp. But be careful making jewelry on speculation, if you aren't wearing it, you might be carrying these pieces for a while since they are expensive and fewer people can afford them. There is pretty good demand for the Ruby Veil.

Tip 3 - The next plateau is skill level 106. At this level you need to make enchanted electrum stuff. In order of popularity (IMHO) are: +2 WIS Earrings (Electrum Jasper), +1 STR Earrings (Electrum Amber) and the 10mana/10hp/2AC Jade Electrum Bracers. You can sell A LOT of the WIS earrings. And even though they are only +1 STR, you can sell a few of STR earrings, don't ask me why. Wear a pair of STR earrings for extra carrying capacity. Wear a pair of bracers. Always sell the jewelry right off your back. Makes sense, doesn't it?

Tip 4 - Skill level 135 is another important goal. At this stage you can make Electrum Star Ruby Ring (+5 DEX, +7 CHA), Electrum Sapphire Earrings, (+4 STR +2 INT) and the Electrum Ruby Ring (+4 STR +2 WIS).

Tip 5 - Skill Gold up to 172 for Charisma Bracers, Wisdom Earrings and Strength Rings.

Tip 6 - Once you get your skill into the 180-190 range, your failures drop substantially and you can try to "overskill". When I was skill 188, I was making Gold Sapphire Earrings, which are trivial at 204. But platinum jewelry was still out of the question.

Tip 7 - As of this writing, I am skill 204. I can make Platinum and Velium, but my failure rate is about 3 of 8 on items requiring skill of 250.

Tip 8 - Important! Velium and Platinum are on the same trivial table. Whatever you can make in plat is similarly easy/difficult to do with velium. So you might as well make velium.

Tip 9 - Don't mistakenly cast Charm instead of Alliance on the Troll Vendor in Shiney Tings. She will KICK YOUR BUTT!!
