Welcome my Dark Kin, at last you have found the true home of Hate. May your stay be long and profitable.

We are the true solders of Innoruuk and we have forged this guild on the anvil of suffering to serve the divine goals of our lord. Know then thy will of our all mighty master and suffer not the weakness of kindness or pity on the lesser races. Instead look down upon them with all the contempt and apathy that they so richly deserve. For they exist solely to fuel the furnaces of hate with their weak and reprehensibly misguided lives. The wise know that even the wheat of the fields needs the shaft to grow and there should be no sadness when it comes time to cull the two. Thus is it with us and them. So, cut them from this life like the lecherous cancer that they are and take joy in the spilling of their blood. For they were born to die so that we may prosper and grow. Never forget that you alone are the chosen children of Innoruuk, the noble born rulers of Norrath. May he bless you with the deaths and domination of those made weaker than you.
