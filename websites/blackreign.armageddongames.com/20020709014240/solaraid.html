WRAP-UP REPORT for SOLUSEK'S EYE RAID

Raided by BLACK REIGN on OCTOBER 12, 2001


Nine of us Raided Sol A. As expected, the Dungeon was not worthy of our skills. Here's how it went down . . . .

We were all on-time. Momur delayed the raid with a last minute, run to Neriak for Coffins that we ended up not needing. Vven, Talox, Mastlia, & Ohuglugok formed one group. Momur, Veranna, Rman, Tynazel & Verlorenamour were the second group. Veranna provided levitation rings to anyone who wanted one. It seemed that Momur was the only one who knew the area, so we had to abandon our plan to split up and cover more camps. That was unfortunate because we were certainly strong enough to hold all the camps in the plan.

We easily pounded our way into the window room. No one fell into any lava pits. And we proceeded towards Singe's room. Singe was not home at the time. We were presented with 3 doors and some of our more eager raiders proceeded to Naggy's Lair. It took a moment to regroup and we moved rapidly to claim the Kindle Spawn and Goblin Captain Spawn. Regrettably, we were not quick enough to lay claim to the Reckless Efreet Spawn.

So the camps were set. Vven's crew held the Captain and environs. Momur's team held Kindle. The fighter types got restless (thankfully) and began wandering around to learn the lay of the dungeon. Soon Tynazel and Verlorenamour were taking turns camping Singe's room and Mastlia was running to assist wherever she was needed.

Suddenly Talox shouts,"Goblin Captain!" Seconds later,"Dropped. Charred Boots." Clearly, that group was in control. Then Tynazel hears Singe flapping his wings! Momur's group frantically runs to find Tynazel and gets split up. Momur and Verlorenamour are briefly . . . mislocated. Singe falls, but, fearing that we were too low level to loot the corpse for rare loot, we delay looting. When we are all regrouped and return - Singe's Corpse is Gone! Momur sees someone gating away. Ninja Bastard! We are not 100% certain but it appears that Ganallron of Magic Reign of Norrath has Ninja looted our corpse. Momur enters into discussion with Gonad-allron and is promplty put on "Ignore". Ah well, the Obsidian Bead Hoop that Singe usually drops is merely OK loot anyway. We shall try to contact their guild.

"Goblin Captain up!" "Dropped Charred Gauntlets." "Can we get a Rez?" Uh oh. Huggy (Uhuglugok) is down. We eventually reunite him with his corpse. In the meantime, Kindle Pops! Impskin Gloves? No, Obsidian Ring. Crap, ah well maybe next time.

It's getting late, the Efreet spawn is free, so we camp it for 3 cycles of Blazing Elemental placeholders, but the Reckless Efreet does not pop. We slash and nuke our way out and it's good night.


TECHNICAL SUMMARY

This raid gets an "A-" grade. Because we couldn't split and camp all the targetted spawns and we didn't get the Rare Loot.

  • (+) We worked well as two semi-dependent groups, supporting each other as needed. Great!

  • (+) We were pretty much on-time.

  • (+) The overall turnout was Strong - 9 people.

  • (-) If we are to take better targets, we MUST HAVE a Rezzing Cleric!

  • (+) Everyone was well disciplined and patient. With minimal spazzing and getting lost transitting to camps.

  • (+) 1 of 9 Died. Awesome.

  • (+) A few of us got some exp!

  • (+) Didn't get lost. Didn't get trained. didn't bicker, bitch or moan.
