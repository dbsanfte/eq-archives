June 6, 2002

CoM Epic Run, Mistmoore Owned, Rings in TT, Item Updates, Spells & Quest Gems

    City of Mist Epic Run
    Enough fooling around! Trezark is gonna get his Torch of the Elements or DIE TRYING! (again) Planning is underway. Lend your support and provide some input on the Message Board.

    Mistmoore Ravaged
    At the last moment, the Ry'Gorr Raid was changed to Mistmoore. A small contingent had the run of the zone. There were a few mishaps caused by incompetent magi.
    Otherwise, a pair of 3 man teams was formed - one low group with a high level chaperone at the entrance for good exp and one high group rampaging throughout Mistmoore.
    At one point Talan and Vven had pretty much cleared the zone, because they determined that was the simplest way to get Ragan his epic piece. All-in-all an excellent time, an easy place to get to and not too hard to manage. Thanks to Vven for arranging this outing.

    Rings in Trakanon's Teeth
    The Band of the Arisen Cultist is an "ok" ring that drops off of skeletons at or near the Cultist Geralissan Camp, but maybe not off of the Cultist himself. If you have any information, provide it in the Member Discussion under Help TT. We should check this out asap.

    Focus Effects Added to Existing Items
    Focus effects have been added to many existing items! Effects such as Spell Haste, Improved Damage, and Spell Component Preservation can make casters and hybrids more formidable. For example, the SMR imparts Spell Haste II, GEBS gives Enhancement Haste II,and Roklys Channeling Crystal has Extended Buff III. Check your stuff, maybe you've gone from puny runt to powerhouse overnight! Some Items and Effect Descriptions

    Grandmaster Crafted Items
    If you are a Craftsperson, you might want to check out Eq Traders Corner for the new Grandmaster Tradeskill Trophy Items. Most Crafts can now create an item that has +10 INT +10 WIS +50 CHA and can be put in Primary, Secondary, Ranged or Ammo slot (although the Ammos slot will not impart the bonuses . . . don't know why). The recipes are costly and revolve around a Potters creation of an Artisans Seal that is a combination of pricy jewelcrafted gems and purified mana.

    Spare Spells and Quest Gems
    Vven has been kind enough to hold and list his collection of spare researched and dropped spells that are available to those in need. He has also agreed to hold and distribute quest gems. So if you have any spare spells or quest gems, transfer them to him.
