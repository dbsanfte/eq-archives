Journeyman's Boots Walkthrough

Your Step-by-Step Guide to Getting those JBoots

(based on information generated by Virosoft)

There are a few ways to do this quest. I've camped the South Ro Ancient Cyclops over 30 Hours and saw only one spawn that was KS'ed from me. It only took 2 hours for a spawn at OOT.

This Quest is given by a gnome NPC called Hasten Bootstrutter in Rathe Mountains. Hasten Bootstrutter says "I call for three things and Journeyman Boots are yours. I call for a [Shadowed Rapier], call for the [Ring of the Ancients] and call for Gold, 3250 Gold Coins! This the trade that I call."

First thing you should do is get the Ring of the Ancients Which is the hard part of the quest. It drops from an Ancient Cyclops. There is one at a static camp in Ocean of Tears. You could also camp the AC in South Ro and on RARE occasions it will spawn in East Karana. The OOT Cyclops is stronger, about level 40-45. The SRO and EK AC's are supposed to be low 30's.

- Ring of the Ancients -
There is an island in OOT where the Ancient Cyclops spawns. When arriving from Freeport, the boat will bring you to the Sister Island Docks. From there, if you face the water, go to your right. Here's a picture of what the island looks like.

The Placeholders and the Ancient Cyclops all spawn at the same EXACT spot - location: +1002, -800
Just back away a bit and sit down watching the spot for a spawn. The MOB that spawns will move if idle for a few minutes, but it always spawns at the same spot. A Pirate spawned immediately before the Ancient Cyclops who dropped the Ring of the Ancients.



- Shadowed Rapier-
The Shadowed Rapier is a common drop from Shadowed Men. There are plenty of zones with Shadowed Men. I camped them in West Commonlands. The Shawdowed Men are in the SouthWest corner of WC. You can actually see him holding the rapier. Very easy, no need to explain more, I think they're lvl 30. Make sure you don't log out after getting this item, it is NO RENT, which means it will disapear when you log out.

- 3250 Gold Coins -
Of course this will weigh you down, depending on your Strength you might get encumbered. My weight allowence after removing a few items and putting in the bank was 49/73 and after putting 3250 gold on me I was encumbered with 127/73. Remember that you CANNOT use Platinum as a substitute. It has to be Gold.


- Hasten Bootstrutter -

Hasten is a static spawn in the Rathe Mountains. He is not always in the zone. When he is in the zone he is on the trail between where the Hill Giants roam and the lvl 40 Griffin Zahasmouth(whatever) spawns. There are some Place Holders for him including a 50+ monk and a drake which is around lvl 20. He sometimes runs somewhere else, but most of the time he is running back and forth (short range), he can de-spawn pretty quick, like 1 - 10 minutes. Hail him, if you continue Hail'ing him he won't de-spawn. You could also get a friend to spam Hail's on him until you get the gold.
Give him the 3 Items he asked for:
Ring of the Ancients
Shadowed Rapier
3250 Gold Coins

You will Receive the Journeyman's Boots
