Spyhunter Web Design and Graphics will register and set up your domain name for FREE, however InterNIC registration fees will apply for new domain names.

The InterNIC is a cooperative activity between the U.S. Government and Network Solutions, Inc.

InterNIC service controls all custom domain name registries and the location they are addressed to. All domain name must be registered with InterNIC before they will function.

The registration fee is billed to you by InterNIC and generally runs $70.00 for a two year registration period.
