Welcome to Fortress of Honor

Chardok Raid Results: The entire night was plagued with problems from linkdeaths every fight, to an actual total server drop partway through the raid. We pressed on and found our first target, the Prince. During what seemed to be a bad pull the Prince came with 3 or 4 guards, but amazingly enough the guards *and* the Prince were dispatched in quick order. Unfortunately, yet another problem out of our control, due to the massive lag spikes from the AEs, the Prince took a walk and seemed to path right through a wall, which ended up agroing the back room, causing a massive train and a massive wipeout. After a long CR we regrouped, pressed on and found the Prince again, this time dispatching him with but 1 death, and held his room captive. We then proceeded to move to the Herbalist room (who decided not to show) where we set up camp to knock off the Overking's guards. Unfortunately, during one of the guard pulls, the king came along, which itself wouldn't have been a problem, however he managed to squeeze off an AE which agroed the pets, causing our room to flood with about 8 guards plus the king. After the CR we decided not to go back that night, but rest assured friends, we'll try again soon, and we will be looting his corpse. It was a success however, as we managed to get 2 epic pieces for our enchanters.
