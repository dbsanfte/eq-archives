November 22, 2002

     Essence of Darkness continues it's regular slaughter of named mobs across Norrath. Below is a screenshot of one of the raids against Trakanon, who was killed very fast, 2 days in a row. Trakanon was nice enough to drop some breastplates (no, we do not let them rot anymore)..

     A few members have been exploring the Crypt of Decay lately. Here are 2 of the mobs from the zone:

Foul Pusling   Mob in Halls of Honor
 

     Recently, a large number of members from the server, got together to attempt a community raid on Aerin'dar, a level 70 Dragon in the Plane of Valor. While Kupo Flux said such a task was impossible, the raiders proved them wrong, and slayed the dragon. A number of EoD members were on the raid, as well as at least 1 DCoE member! Slaying the dragon, gives players access to the Halls of Honor (\ooc 51 Cleric is LFG).
