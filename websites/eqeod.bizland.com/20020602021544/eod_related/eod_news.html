May 30, 2002

     For those of you who may have missed it. It was recently announced in guild chat, that Maikd and Talb will be getting married in real life.

 

  

 
 

May 28, 2002

     Zuras got his epic yesterday. This is the first Shadow Knight Epic for EoD. Kestrel also completed his epic (Rogue). That makes 4 epics over the extended weekend, with a number of people, who need 1 more mob, to complete their epics.

     The schedule for raids in June, is now up. Go check it out (the link takes you to the current month). Some changes from the previous months.

     Blade is on Rage watch, and we are next.

 

  

 

May 27, 2002

     EoD was busy on the Plane of Air over the weekend, and with a quick jaunt into the Plane of Fear. EoD made it to the top of PoA. Please note, that the keys are no longer norent, and stay with you forever. If you have some keys, don't destroy them. All 3 days were successful, with a minor annoyance on day 2 (for some reason, the islands did not pop like they should). Due to the lack of mobs on day 2, EoD cleared what was around, and then stopped into PoF for a while. EoD returned to PoA for day 3.

     Both Makid and Burbridge completed their epics, with more epic pieces being obtained by Xingtau (1 piece to go), Tiffa, Kubaan and Kanom.

     A number of DCoE members attended the raids, and made out with a number of upgrades to their gear.

 

May 23, 2002

     EoD has a number of raids coming up. We will be in the Plane of Air, starting Friday at 8:00pm (EST). The initial push into POA will be conducted by a limited number of EoD members (using the keys). On Saturday, we will continue moving into POA, and the rest of the guild will show up (1pm EST), and will use coffins to join up with the main force. We will be in POA on Sunday also (1pm EST). DCoE members who are high enough level, may attend (send a tell to the current raid leader).

     On Wednesday the 29th, we will be heading into the Plane of Hate (7-12pm EST), and Plane of Fear on Friday the 31st (8-12pm EST). Once again, DCoE members of sufficient level are welcome to attend.

     Note, we will be back on Rage watch very soon. Bellatoris is up now, followed by Blade, then Essence of Darkness.

 

May 17, 2002

     The web site has been re-designed, yet again. The news sections are back, as is the un-official guild roster (the official roster is on guild magic). This new site should be easier to read, and navigate.

     Take a look at the Guild Calendar, to see when raids are scheduled this month.

 

May 15, 2002

     News reports have not been done for a short time. They are now back, and we will try to get something out, at least once a week.

     EOD recently completed our first Bard Epic. Congratulations to Tayloor. This was the 2nd time we took down Trakanon.

     Va'Dyn was also taken down once again. He dropped a Skybreaker, and a level 60 Bard song (last week he dropped a level 60 Druid spell).
