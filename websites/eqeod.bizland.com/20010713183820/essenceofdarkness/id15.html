Plane of Fear


INTRODUCTION:

The Plane of Fear is the home of the god Cazic Thule and his immediate family. It is a difficult zone to clear, but can be quite rewarding in terms of equipment dropped and feeling of accomplishment. The following is the plan that EoD will follow to ensure we have the best chance for success. It is designed to allow those experienced with the PoF as well as first timers to the plane to be able to understand what the other is doing. It is in no way designed to insult anyone's intelligence, but will give details from the basics to the advanced. First you should understand that you will die in Fear! Accept this fact from the beginning. Youu WILL die. However, there will be level 49+ cleric rezes so it should not hurt to bad. Do not rush. Sometimes things take longer than it seems they should, but do not try and rush the raid leaders or the puller. It will only cause more death and slow things down in the long run. Pay attention to instructions and listen to them. If you have an idea or something to put out send a tell to the raid leader. Do not give suggestions in ooc or shout. It will only confuse people as to what they should be doing or how they should be doing it.

PREPARATION:

SETTINGS - There are normally a minimum of 6 full groups of peeps on the raid, which makes lag and going LD is a major concern. To help minimize this go to OPTIONS/GRAPHICS and turn 3D sky OFF and particle effects OFF. Then go to OPTIONS/CHAT and turn other misses, my misses, others hit, my hits, mobs hits, etc OFF. You don?eed to worry about if the mob is getting hit for 5hp or 105hp. You need to be concerned about lag and going LD.

HOW TO COMMUNICTE - Default your text to OOC. The ONLY ones who will use shout are the raid leaders and puller. Minimize the use of tells because it causes others to scroll back through text to see what was said to them and they will not be paying attention to the fight at hand. NEVER give the puller a direct tell. He/she is trying to concentrate on making a good pull and should not be interrupted. If you need to tell the puller something send one of the raid leaders the tell and he will forward the message if he cannot answer it and it is essential that the puller get the message.

Make the following hot key:
/loc
Been agged! Noted my location. Trying to camp.
You will probably use this key during the break in and if you get feared or summoned to an unknown location.

WHAT TO BRING - Each Shadow Knight and Necromancer needs to bring a coffin in case there are corpses which cannot be recovered. Clerics should have a generous supply of stones to cast symbols and enchanters should bring some stones in case they need to cast runes in emergencies. An attempt to reimburse these peeps with sellable loot items will have these priorities Necro/SK who use coffins, Clerics, Enchanters.

WHERE TO MEET - All should meet in Ferrott at the fear portal, which is located at neg1450, pos2700. It is an invisible entrance in the hillside. Go in and stay to the right all the way till you see it open up to a large area with the fear portal. You will encounter several specters on the way. You can train these to the camp and they will be easily killed by the group. Just say in OOC that specters are inc and you need a hand.

BREAK IN

If possible we will wait til another raid party/guild has cleared from the North safe spot to the portal. Then one of our clerics, enchanters and druids will zone in and safely run to the NSS, bind and TP out. That way prior to the raid we know that we have a cleric safely there. Other classes that can bind are welcomed to go bind at this time. Then, there will be less corpses to retrieve. Others will just zone in on raid day and die on the path if they must die. If that is not possible then we will go to plan B. There will be 2 sets of groups. One for the break in and one for the actual fighting. The first group will be made up of FD classes and the raid leader. Their job will enter Fear and FD along path from the portal to the North Safe Spot (NSS), which is located just north of the fire ring. When you zone into fear you will be facing the direction you need to run. This group should be inv and sowed to speed up the process. If you get agged FD. If you get to much agro it may be necessary to /q to clear the agro and come back.

The next group will be made up of Clerics and Taunting Tanks. This group should be buffed as much as possible, have sow and be inv. When the raid leader (who should still be in Ferrott) gets the all clear from the FD group he will tell the next group to enter.
The important part about this insertion method is GETTING A CLERIC TO THE NSS. So, they will zone in and head toward the NSS following the path outlined on the map. The goal is to get at least one cleric to the NSS and camped out to chat. The tanks job is to protect the clerics at all costs. If a cleric gets agged a tank should taunt the mob off and the cleric should SIT and CAMP immediately where he is at. DO NOT go to camp if you have been agged or you risk training any clerics that made it there and are trying to camp. Also, MAKE SURE YOU DIE AT LEAST NEAR THE FIRE WALL ALONG THE PATH if you have to die. If you die on the path you can be easily CRed by a decent monk. If you make it to the fire wall nearly any one can get you, the main problem with this path is mobs see though invis, but having a monk look out for them and giving the cleric time to get bound at the SS assures you will be off to a good start. Once all clerics are dead or camped then the tanks should attempt to go to the NSS and camp out.

If you made it to the NSS, camp out and goto/make the chat channel TP Fear (no password). It is important to use chat for this.

The remaining groups will be numbered and should all buff up, sow and inv. The raid leader will give commands similar to this

Group 1 enter
Group 2 to portal and inv
Group 3 top off buffs.

Enter only when the raid leader says to do so and remember your group number. He/she has direct communication with all the Fd'ers still and will tell you when it is all clear. Enter and when you zone in you will be facing north. Run forward following the path on the map until you see the last Fd'er at the NSS. If you get agged STOP! DO NOT train camp. Just stop and die along the path. Try to camp if possible and use the hot key you made to let others know you are agged and write down your location. Do not assist someone who is agged though. Just continue to the NSS and try to camp out. When you are west (left) of the fire ring pause to see if you have been agged before taking the chance of training the camp. Once at the NSS camp out and go to the chat channel TP Fear.

Once all groups are in the raid leader will enter and all Fd'ers will attempt to get to the NSS and FD. They will be at camp to let others know when it is safe to log in. Do not log in until someone tells you to. We will have someone with 2 computers monitor both the chat room and those FD and dead in the PoF.

CORPSE RECOVERY
CR can take awhile. DO NOT complain or rush things. The raid leaders and monks want to get started with the raid ASAP to and are doing their best. 3 monks at a minimum should be getting corpses. That way if one gets Death Touched (DT is an instant 20k of dmg to the target, is nearly impossible to resist and recycles every 30 seconds) the other 2 can camp immediately. One will recieve another DT normally, but one will get camped out and be able to drag the first 2. When the CR party has 5 corpses to the NSS they will stay there and communicate to the rezzing party to rez 5. At this time a Cleric, Chanter, Druid and Necro (if all are at the NSS and logged safely) to log on. Chanter will clarity everyone and log back off. Cleric will rez all 5 at soon as possible. Druid will heal the rezzed and the necro will feed them mana. Once those 5 are rezed they will immediately sit and camp to chat. When all 5 are rezzed the rezzing party will sit and camp to chat again. The monks will then repeat this process. The monks will NOT be dragging corpses during the rezzes or we risk the chance of agging the rezzing party. Once all corpses are rezzed the raid leader will inform those in the chat room to log on. Raid leaders should form their groups. Use /target <name> to locate group members. Group leaders should then report to raid leader if all members are present. If any peeps are missing we will determine if we can clear our way to them or if coffins are needed. If everyone dies along the path CR should be easy and no one should be unaccounted for. If you paniced and ran in an unknown location when you got agged then you will wait til we can clear to you and probably miss good portion of the raid. So, it is important that you die along the path.

PULLING
When everyone gets logged back on stay at NSS. Do not leave for any reason. Do not attempt to retrieve any corpses. The monks will start retrieving corpses and dragging them to NSS for rezzes. Once you log back on or are rezed make sure RUN and SoW are OFF!!!!!!! Never have SoW or wolf form or run on. The only time you should be running is on the break in and when we are changing camps. Corpses that cannot be easily reached will NOT be CR'ed at the start. After the rezzing is all done we will re organize groups, buff up and proceed to clear the zone in a counter-clockwise motion, starting with the scarelings, shiverbacks, and spinechillers in the NW corner. We will attempt to clear the northwest corner as fast as possible so as to move out of the SS before mobs path into it - cause they will as the plane is cleared, the camp will move several times to the following spots - SS, NW corner, West wall (this one may not be always used), FF house, Bowl (south of portal) WW/Sam Tower (Gray stone tower east of Bowl), Frightfinger house, and then back to the Yellow Tower to pull the temple.

Never leave the camp for any reason and never train the camp. If you see more than 4 mobs come on a pull Camp and goto/make the chat channel again. If the RAID LEADER says camp then do the same thing immediately. Do not make pets until told to do so. Be careful while using dots. Make sure you are dotting the mob that is being taken currently or else your chanter is dead! Do not have gate or evacs loaded, because if you do gate the raid will be over for you, even if it was hit by accident

Some general duties will be
Junior enchanter Tashania
Senior magician Malise
Senior enchanter Mezzing
2nd senior enchanter/shaman Slow
3rd senior enchanter/shaman Incapaciatate/debuff
Senior druid Snare

Depending upon how many people and what classes they are pulls may be fast or slow. Please do not complain if you feel the pulls are going an inappropriate speed - even a modest CR takes longer than 3 pulls. Usually a pull will work as follows - the monk goes out and gets hit by 1-10 mobs. He will then run out to a predetermined spot a good ways out of camp and FD. The picker will be standing about halfway between the FD'er and the camp and will pick (shoot straggling mobs) off the FD'er. This is used to separate large packs of mobs. If the pull contains a scareling, 99% of the time the mobs will ALL come, because scarelings can agro on the camp all the way from the pick spot. The picker will run back to camp with the pull, at which point everyone should /assist the main assister. Once all the mobs are dead, the next pull will be made as mana permits. Sometimes the monk will get too much agro on him and be forced to /q to clear the agro lists.

Sometimes the FDer will be busy or LD and the picker will be able to make a pull. DO NOT TRY TO DO THIS YOURSELF. DO NOT LEAVE CAMP FOR ANY REASON. If you are not he puller you should NEVER EVER leave the camp. The reason why is that agro ranges in fear are HUGE - 90% of mobs can agro from VISUAL range. For a non-FDer to make a successful pick, he must be able to isolate pixel-sized mobs in his view and judge pathing of possible invisible wanderers. If the picker gets more mobs than the camp can handle, he should DIE outside of camp. It may be possible for someone else to pick off his corpse, or the pull can be aborted and the picker rezzed to try again.

If Cazic Thule is up, the zone will become more difficult (note if you see CT and target him for a pic or something, then make sure you untargeted him before you try to cast a spell on a mob.)
. The reason why is this - Cazic's agro range is monumental - he can DT you from the NE corner while you are sitting at the SS. DT is a 'Death touch', an instant death to the PC of his choice.
No damage, no nothing - DT is an instant 20k of dmg to the target and is nearly impossible to resist. It recycles every 30 seconds. When Cazic has DT'd someone, many of the mobs in the plane will converge on that person's last known location - usually their corpse. Not all the mobs will do this - mostly only the ones that can speak, IE thought bleeders, glare lords, Amys, etc. The most visible side effect of this is the 15 or so Amy Knights in the temple running out and HT'ing the unlucky person's corpse several times. This means you do NOT want to be near the area. If the person who got the DT got it in camp, everyone should camp out to CHAT and wait for the FD'er's all clear signal. If not, everyone should stay far, far away from the spot of his/her death until all the mobs have gone home.

DT can happen for any one of several reasons -

A - Cazic Thule sees you.
B - Cazic Thule is informed of agro (IE a mob that is agro on you runs to the NE area of the zone. This is very easy to let happen. Also, if a pet is near the camp and its master is not, or vice versa, there's a good chance one of those two is running back to CT to tattle on you. Also, if you touch the firewall or any mobs near the firewall while Cazic is anywhere near any other part of the firewall, there's a good chance you'll become very famous.)
C - You hit one of Cazic Thule's immediate family. This consists of 4 mobs - Fright, Dread, Terror, and Dracoliche. The instant anyone hits any of these mobs CT will Zap them. The mob will then try to run to CT to tattle on the rest of the camp. It's important to kill these mobs instantly - do NOT let them get away or you will have to pull them again, which means still more DT'ing. Once one of these mobs are killed you must camp out to clear the argo that was sent to Cazic Thule. Even if we have one of the mobs plus 2 other mobs, once its dead CAMP to CHAT!!!

DT's are sometimes isolated, sometimes not - IE sometimes Cazic will chill out after DT'ing one person, and other times he will DT until everyone has camped. The best way to be sure whether you need to camp is to wait 30 seconds after the first DT - if he zaps a second person, it's time to camp. How he chooses who he DT's has to do with chain agro - in other words, it's bad to be a bard in fear plane.

Now, the specific areas of pulling-

First, here's a description of each of the types of mobs. Just about every mob in fear has about 8,000 to 11,000 hit points, varying MR, and hits for about 120-140 points of damage at 10 delay. This means an unslowed fear mob will do 300 damage per second. Do not underestimate their melee.

Scarelings (level 48-52)- The single most hated mob in fear, with the possible exception of glare lords. These look like little fire imps and can backstab for 400ish. They fly at SoW speed, and can agro from a quarter of the zone away. During the fight, they have the ability to randomly teleport you anywhere from 5 feet to 100 feet away in a random direction - this is very bad. If you get teleported while fighting, INSTANTLY type /loc and look around to get your visual bearings. Wait a moment or two to see if anything agros on you. If not, run back to the camp to continue the fight. If something does hit you, DIE. We will CR you after the pull. They are also difficult to mez, so they should be a priority target in a multi-mob pull.

Fright (level 50), Terror (level 52), and Dread - These are all the same mob, with different names and textures. They look like gigantic Mud, Rock, and Steel golems, cast EarthElementalAttack (root) while chasing you, and cast Ghoulroot when they hit you. Otherwise, easy mob, easy to mez. Be forewarned, the first person to hit, mez, or cast on them will INSTANTLY be DT'd if Cazic is up, so it's usually a good idea to have some ranger or something hit them when they first come into camp to take the DT, then have the enchanter mez. The enchanter will be almost guaranteed to get the second DT, but hey, they live 30 seconds longer to be useful. Each Golem is guarded by 2 Amy Knights. The puller should run within agro range of the Knights, get HT'd, then proceed to run all three mobs around in the SE corner of the zone. The picker will attempt to pick the 2 Amy's off the puller as he runs around kiting. At NO TIME should EITHER the picker or puller hit the golem - they will get zapped, and the golem will run off to tattle on CT. The picker should shoot the amys with an arrow or spell. If the golem agros on the picker, he should trade places with the puller and kite the mobs around the SE corner, while the puller attempts to pick the amys off the picker. They will trade off until one of them has the amys and one has the golem. You may have to take the amys back to camp one at a time. The person with the amys should run back to camp with them and let camp kill them, then once the amys are dead, the person with the golem should run him into camp. Be careful not to lose the mobs - they will run to Cazic and tattle. Once the golem is dead, everyone should camp and come back. If CT is not up, these mobs can be pulled normally.

Dracoliche - A big bone dragon. He complicates the zone a lot, mainly by being in the way for a lot of your pulls. This especially makes pulling the GY difficult. What will usually happen is the puller will attempt to pull around Draco as much as possible until most of the West and South areas of the zone are clear, then they will pull him to the SW area. It is important that the party get Draco solo - you don't want him to bring friends. He is basically an undead Dragon that hits for 205-220, has a very nasty AE fear, and an AE DoT that is VERY deadly and is poison/disease based. DMF helps with this fight. He drops The Bone Clasped Girdle, The Warrior/Ranger Spined Dragon Claw, the Rogue-Only Bone Razor Dagger, and the Warrior-Only Monsoon sword. The Warriors on the Raid should roll BEFORE draco has been pulled, because once he is killed everyone should loot and camp IMMEDIATELY - Cazic will be DT'ing like mad and you will have lots of mobs bearing down on you. A major undertaking.

Lastly, Cazic Thule himself, the God of Fear. He looks like a Giant Avatar of Fear, hits for 355-400, and has both Deathtouch (recycles every thirty seconds) and Harm Touch (only the first person to charge him takes this). He also casts Gravity Flux. To fight him, kill everything in the zone First. Then buff EVERYONE up with 4 junk buffs, followed by full buffs and Lev/DMF (VERY IMPORTANT). Cazic's AE Gravity Flux will do negligible damage with Lev on, but will dispel your first buff every time he casts it. If you have Jboots, hit them after every Gflux. Cazic's main vulnerabilty is Stuns - he stands there and does not attack while stunned. However, to cast on Cazic, you MUST stand DIRECTLY underneath him, which is very dangerous since he can HIT you there He also has about 6-9 irritating little skeleton pets, which can be AE'd to death or kited by a druid. These can be a major impediment as they interrupt your casting. Now, for Areas of pulling and mobs that are there:

The first area you pull will be NW corner - these mobs are pulled to the SS. The mobs here are Shivers, scares, and spinechillers. Once this area is clear, everyone should move to NW corner and pull the west area, which will be more of the same mobs, plus some FFs from the FF house. Once THAT is clear, you can begin to pull the tents from the bridge near GY and the mobs from SW corner, which consist of FFs, toads, amy warriors, shivers, scares, and sams. Once SW and the tent bridge are clear, camp should move to the FF house, and pullers should pull the 2 towers and the boogeymen house, as well as any accessible parts of the GY that can be easily reached. After 2 towers, boog house, and southern part of GY are clear, party should move to the bowl south of the portal (little dell indention in the ground kind of thing) and pull the SE corner of the zone and the rest of the GY (boogeymen, FFs, sams, ww's, amy wars, and toads mostly), as well as the gorgons and the Frightfinger house, and then Draco if he is up. Once those areas are clear, everyone should move to the Gray tower (ww/sam tower) to pull the golems and the glare lord bridge, then everyone should move to the FF house to pull the Mares and Phobos. If Cazic is up, this part will be tricky because the mares and phobos will have to be pulled while he isn't looking, which is very difficult. The same problem applies to pulling the glare lord bridge (as if that wasn't hard enough). Lastly, everyone should move to the yellow tower near the portal to pull the temple.

Pulling the Temple is difficult and requires very precise coordination. Here's how it works. Ideally, everyone will have jboots - important, because a glare lord is often inside the temple and can and will dispel everyone's SoW. First, if Cazic is up, someone needs to be watching him - you do not want him to be near the firewall when the cleric runs in, or he will get a fat DT.

A brave cleric will DA and run thru the firewall, run along the western wall of the temple (NOT over the top - that's a good way to get a DT) and get HT'd by many, many amys. The DA will absorb the HTs, and he/she will run out of the firewall towards the tent bridge, looking back to see how many are on him/her. If there are more than six, cleric will run under the tent bridge, which should get some of the amys stuck in a pathing bug which will cause them to disappear back into the temple for a short time. The cleric should run around and around the hill on which the portal is seated, and the picker should attempt to pick a few amys off him/her. If picker gets more than 4 amys, he/she should run them under the bridge to split them up. If he/she STILL has too many, he/she should kite them around the NW area while the puller attempts to pick off him/her or the cleric. Be careful while kiting, because the Amy knights can cast clinging darkness, which will slow you up and get you wacked. Eventually, SOMEONE will get 1 or 2 amys on them. These can be brought back to camp to be slain, then the process will repeat. Once all the amys being kited have been slain, cleric should DA again and run up to the firewall, then around to the north and out by the west side of the SS. When they do this, some of the mobs that got peeled off on the bridge will teleport on top of him/her. He/she will kite these around the portal and picker and puller will repeat the earlier process until all the mobs in the temple have been slain. Once this is done, the entire zone will be clear, unless CT is up. If CT is up, everyone should will then run to the SS and buff up for him, then send the sacrificial druid in when he wanders to the NW area of the NE corner of the zone. After the druid gets the finger, charge CT and bring him down. Congratulations! You have cleared the Plane of Fear.
