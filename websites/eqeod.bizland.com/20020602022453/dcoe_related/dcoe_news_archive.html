April 17th, 2002

DCoE was busy this past weekend. On Saturday, a groups began a week-long raid on the Ry'Gorr orcs, to equip members with some nice armor. Sunday, was DCoE's 1 year birthday. To celebrate in style, the majority of DCoE raided Kael, with the help of EOD. A great time was had by everyone, and a lot of equipment was passed along. DCoE members in need of spells, contact Gilraam, he is not in charge of the DCoE spell mule.

 

April 08th, 2002

DCoE officers have decided to start working on their Coldain faction, to begin doing Thurgudin related Quests. With EOD planning on changing their faction within a few months, DCoE will stay with the Coldain. Dranak has also decided to start equipping members in Ry'Gorr armor this week. For this to happen, he needs people to help slay the vile Ry'Gorr. Sign up in the DCoE Raid forum (get loot, XP and faction all at the same time) Here are some shots from DCoE slaying in the great divide. DCoE will be increasing the number of officers to 9 members very soon, and begin to actively recruit new members.

 

April 04th, 2002

Last Sunday afternoon Gilraam led a raid into the Tower of Frozen Shadows (ToFS). Keys to the third floor and a Student's Homework for Thest were the objectives for the raid. Unfortunately we didn't get any homework to drop. However we did have 2 nice drops during the raid and keys to the 3rd and 4th floors were gained for the guild. We had a Teacher's Syllabus drop off the Angry Librarian and a nice melee ring drop up on the fourth floor. Both Gilraam and Jasonn were quite happy with their prizes from the tower. Everyone that was there on the Sunday raid had a good time and experience and some loot was had by all. A GM quest started right as we left the Tower and came back into Iceclad Ocean (IC). I know Skor, Tendiian, Thest, and Tuvic jumped right in on the quest as soon as they could.

Gilraam is looking to lead another expedition into the tower this weekend. Look in the DCoE raid forums (EOD is also looking for ideas, to assist DCoE on a raid).
