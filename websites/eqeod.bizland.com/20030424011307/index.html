March 31, 2003

Well, Time for a little Freehand Experimenting!

 

Well in the weeks that have followed our AoW kill we went back and smacked him down again. We have been running

amok in PoP and checking out new content. Many have been grinding to get up to 60 or 65 and knock out some AAs.

 

There has been much farming of the Spectrals, Runes  and other goodies in BoT. Getting flags and marks still happens

from time to time but we have refocused.

 

Finally we came to our senses and decided that the only way for us to progress is to to start trudging through the wonderful

content they call Time sink... Oops I mean Luclin.  Many have been farming shards and Ssra temple non stop to get their

emperor key and all the shards for the VT key.   We still break for a quick Greig kill or to take a quick wack at some

velious thorns that just beg to be farmed but our focus is on the prize that is farming VT.

 

Completed Keys are now bringing DKPs so get in there and start grinding out a damn key. We can't kill the Emperor

without our whole force and we can't get our whole force in without them all having the keys.  lets get them keys!
