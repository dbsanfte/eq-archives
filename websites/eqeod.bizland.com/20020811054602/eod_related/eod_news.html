July 15, 2002

     Essence of Darkness and Dark Conscripts of Essence, have recently changed their recruiting policy. Essence of Darkness has raised the minimum level to 55 for all classes. Those that have already applied, and are under level 55, will still be considered for EoD. The current members of DCoE will be grandfathered, and still may enter EoD at level 52 if they choose. However, they are encouraged to wait until level 55.

     There are several reasons for the change:
1) People entering EoD will be more focused on raiding, rather than leveling.
2) More experienced applicants applying to EoD.
3) Enabling DCoE to successfully conduct Planer / Kael raids without outside assistance
4) Allowing EoD to be more attractive to the high end players on Tholuxe Paells.

     In addition, Dark Conscripts of Essence's new minimum level will be raised to 30. The reason for this is that having members between level 20 to 52, was too big of a level difference, to enable guild raiding and guild groups. DCoE will also become a more experienced guild, with level range from 30 to 54, to give them all the tools they need to become a respectable mid level guild on the server.

     Current EoD members, who are under level 55, may stay in EoD. However, if individuals under level 55 choose, they may enter DCoE and take advantage of a more EXP oriented guild. You will also be able to play a more active role on raids, than you would in EoD, due to your current level ( i.e. level 54 warrior as MA, level 50 monk as main puller, level 53 druid on snare). The same applies to DCoE members under level 30. DCoE members under level 30 will be allowed to stay in DCoE as long as they continue to respect the rules of the guild in all other ways.

     These changes should be smooth and almost transparent and will should only affect new members applying to DCoE/EoD.
