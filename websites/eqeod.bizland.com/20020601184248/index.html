     Welcome to the homepage of Essence of Darkness and our junior guild, Dark Conscripts of Essence. Use the navigation bar on the left to navigate the site. Visit the guild forums, for information on joining EoD or DCoE. This site is best viewed, if your screen resolution is at 800x600 or greater.

     Essence of Darkness was established in West Commons on October 12th, 2000 near the Derv Camp by Waldoo Mezmen and the late Roland Bain. We were the 5th guild established on Tholuxe Paelles, and are now the eldest surviving one on the server. EoD was born from the mother guild of Essence of Courage on the Karanas Server. On April 12th, 2001 Essence of Darkness merged with Chapions of Olympus guild at the stone bridge in North Karana.

     On April 14th, 2001 Dark Conscripts of Essence was formed at the Toll Booth in West Commons by Expoc Petmann for our mid level apprentices to learn the ways of Essence of Darkness. On July 30th, 2001 Sanctuary of Salvation guild merged with EoD at the West Commons druid ring.
