Epic Quest Plan

Epic quests can be very time consumming and dangerous. All will need help in getting their Epic and this plan should assist. Small parts of the quest should be achieved by individuals. If you need a small group or the help of a tracker you can post a time and date you would like to gt this help and have peeps sign up. Once a week we will get together as a guild for the parts that require power. We will start with the highest guild members first. Once they recieve and item we will move to the next senior member. If you do not recieve and item one day you will be next on the list on the next Epic Day. This way those that are regularly attended will be rewarded before those that come on an occasion. I will post results and next to recieve and item here. You MUST do your own research. Have a couple of MOBS that you need that we can take with a few groups just in case a senior member kills the mob you needed, you will still have an alternate mob rdy. If we can not get your mob currently then you will go to the top of the list. If you need a mob that requires a full out raid then plan and conduct the raid. GOOD LUCK TO ALL!
