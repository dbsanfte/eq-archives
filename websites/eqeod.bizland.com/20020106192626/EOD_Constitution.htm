CONSTITUTION

ARTICLE I (ELIGIBILITY) -
Essence of Darkness will not be race, class or religion specific. Members should be at least level 46 and preferably a master in some trade skill, but at all times acceptance will be based the views of the guild about the individual. A guild member must sponsor a person into the guild, and then current members online at the time will give their views of the person. If one EoD member disapproves of the person then the person will be denied membership. Real life friends, family and alternate characters of current guild members will be invited into the guild as long as they are level 46+. Those real life friends under level 46 will be referred to Dark Conscripts of Essence guild. Dark Conscripts of Essence member who reach level 46 will automatically be invited into EoD without prior approval of current EoD members. Members who have left the guild will not be invited back into EoD unless extenuating circumstances exist. All new members will be on a probation period for 2 weeks and may be removed by any officer if the values and goals of the new member are not consistent with those of EoD.

ARTICLE II (OFFICERS) -
Any member of EoD who successfully plans and conducts 3 raids will be promoted to officer. This is to encourage guild members to assume leadership and gain knowledge of planning and conducting events. In the event of a guild merger, the leader of the merging guild will retain officer status. For every 10 members above level 46 of the merging guild the merging Guild Leader may appoint an officer. If the merging guild has 25+ members level 46+ they will be given rights as Co-Guild Leader.

ARTICLE III (REMOVAL) -
It is inevitable that with so many members from different cultures and countries around the world that personality conflicts will result. At no time should a member be removed from the guild simply because an officer "doesn't like the member." In most situations prior to guild removal the member will be warned that some action he/she has done may be grounds for removal. This warning may be done by any officer having knowledge of an offense using a direct tell to the member. 3 officers must be in agreement before a member will be removed from the guild and the guild leader and 2 officers must be in agreement before and officer is removed from the guild. Offenses that are grounds for removal are, but not limited to:
Arguing with a guild mate in GUILD TALK
Attempting to organize a mutiny
KSing of a guild mate
Repeated begging of a guild mate or non-guild mate
Harassment of a guild mate to include harassment based on sex, rl race, rl religion or sexual orientation.
Repeated harassment of a non-guild mate (except during times of war)
Fraud to a guild mate
Excessive use of bad language
Our name implies that we have a dark nature to us and good role-play is always encouraged, but at no time should a member violate the norms of the Norrath community. Members that have been removed from the guild under circumstance outlined in this article will not be invited back into the guild.

ARTICLE IV (ANONYMOUS AND ROLEPLAY)
Members above level 40 should not use anon. If it is essential for a members race, class or level to be unknown then role-play will be used. Exceptions would be during times of war or special missions, which will be coordinated through an EoD officer. Members under level 40 should always play utilizing role-play until they reach or exceed level 40.

ARTICLE V (RAIDS)
Raid ideas will be proposed on the raid page and voted on by guild members. All members who are on during the times of the raid and meet minimum level requirements for the raid are strongly encouraged to attend. This is the one day of the week when we can all get together for a common cause. Many times there will not be experience to be had by all members, but senior members of the guild should still attend and assist our younger members. The raids date and time will be posted and announced preferably 2 weeks prior to the day of the raid.

ARICLE VI (INACTIVE MEMBERS)
A member will be considered inactive if he/she has not been seen for 2 weeks unless prior knowledge has been stated. If the member is an officer his position will be considered null and if that creates a vacancy in that tier a new officer will be voted on. Inactive members and officers will retain their current position if they return at a later date.

ARTICLE VII (BEGGING)
In all cases begging is discouraged. However master tradesman should offer services to guild members at a reduced rate. Also, if a member has an item for sale they should offer to sell it to the guild prior to auctioning it to the general public. Members should not expect a rare item to be sold to them at a reduced cost and should be thankful if a member does choose to reduce the normal cost of the item or give it to them. However, if the guild is responsible for you getting an upgrade to an item then the old item should be given to a guild member having a use for it. This will make us stronger as a guild and promote cohesion among guild members. A mule will be maintained by the Guild Leader to hold items for the guild. If the item is not of use to EoD it will be filtered down to DcoE for use.

ARTICLE VIII (VOTING)
As the land of Norrath changes and EoD continues to evolve there may arise a need to change various articles of this constitution or the norms of EoD. Changes will be proposed at announced officer meetings. A majority of the officers present at the officer meeting will decide if the change will proceed.
