Plane of Hate

UNDER CONSTRUCTION


The Plane of Hate is the God of Hate Innoruk?ome. Innoruk and his minions are in complete control of this zone which has the look of a cursed town.
This zone is of extremely dangerous nature and can only be accessed through the help of a wizard of level 46 and over who has the correct teleportation spell. Upon entering the zone you are immediately under threat by Innoruk and his minions as they welcome no visitors to their plane. There are no safe areas in this zone and the only way to approach it is with a large group of adventurers and fight your way through.

Monsters in this zone are all very strong but the rewards are well worth the risk. Many of Innoruk?inions drop weapons and armor which is considered very good quality. It is not uncommon to see many Kireukei, Clerics of Innoruk, Ire Ghasts, male and female Forsaken Revenants, Treasure Chests that have taklen a life of their own and Ashenbone Drakes wandering the backways of this cursed town. Leading these abominations is one of the strongest of Innoruk?inions, The Maestro of Rancor who is a bard and is extremely powerful having an area of effect attack and hitting extremely hard and fast.

SETTINGS - There are normally a minimum of 4 to 6 full groups of peeps on the raid, which makes lag and going LD is a major concern. To help minimize this go to OPTIONS/GRAPHICS and turn 3D sky OFF and particle effects OFF. Then go to OPTIONS/CHAT and turn other misses, my misses, others hit, my hits, mobs hits, etc OFF. You don?eed to worry about if the mob is getting hit for 5hp or 105hp. You need to be concerned about lag and going LD. Put serverfilter on and show spell effects off.

HOW TO COMMUNICTE - Default your text to OOC. The ONLY ones who will use shout are the raid leaders and puller. Minimize the use of tells because it causes others to scroll back through text to see what was said to them and they will not be paying attention to the fight at hand. NEVER give the puller a direct tell. He/she is trying to concentrate on making a good pull and should not be interrupted. If you need to tell the puller something send one of the raid leaders the tell and he will forward the message if he cannot answer it and it is essential that the puller get the message.

Make the following hot key:
/loc
Got summoned! Noted my location.
You will probably use this if you get feared or summoned to an unknown location.

WHAT TO BRING - Everyone needs to bring 50pp to pay for the Fulligrans Soulstone. It take one of these stones to TP up to hate. The gems can be purchases in the Neriak, Ak'anon or Erudin libraries. Someone will be designated to go buy the stones after the pp is collected. Each Shadow Knight and Necromancer needs to bring a coffin in case there are corpses which cannot be recovered. Clerics should have a generous supply of stones to cast symbols and enchanters should bring some stones in case they need to cast runes in emergencies. An attempt to reimburse these peeps with sellable loot items will have these priorities Necro/SK who use coffins, Clerics, Enchanters.

WHERE TO MEET - All should meet in SRO at the wizzy spires. Be on time. I would suggest looking for a TP to SRO about an hour early unless you are already in the area.

BREAK IN
It takes a wizard to TP you to hate so groups will be made up of 5 people. First group up will have a rogue or monk. Remember your group number and who is in it. You will invite a wizard and when told he/she will TP you up. The monk or rogue will hide/FD as soon as they zone in to watch for wanderers into the area for future groups. Everyone else move a few feet from the zone in spot so we are not all on top of each other and sit and camp to chat. The first person to chat make the room TPHate with Password EoD. DON NOT TOUCH THE WALLS IN THIS ROOM. If you touch the walls you will agro alot of stuff so never touch the walls. The wizard will immediatly zone back down to SRO and get an invite from another groups. He/she will coordinate with the Hidden/FD person in hate as to when it is clear to take next group up. We will do this until all or raid is in hate. If you get agged just sit die. Make sure your cleric can camp out before you do. Then log into chat. Once in chat you may add another person to your group. Do not have any type of gate memed. It cost to much to get you back if you gate out. If you do gate the raid is over for you....even if by accident so dont mem it!
