     On October 12th, 2000: Essence of Darkness was established in West Commons,  near the Derv Camp, by Waldoo Mezmen and the late Roland Bain. Essence of Darkness was the Fifth guild to be established on Tholuxe Paelles. Essence of Darkness is now the eldest surviving guild on the server. EoD was born from the mother guild, Essence of Courage from the Karanas Server (they no longer exist).

     On April 12th, 2001 Essence of Darkness merged with the Chapions of Olympus guild at the stone bridge in North Karana.

     On April 14th, 2001 Dark Conscripts of Essence was formed at the Toll Booth in West Commons by Expoc Petmann for our mid level apprentices to learn the ways of Essence of Darkness.

     On July 30th, 2001 Sanctuary of Salvation guild merged with EoD at the West Commons druid ring.
