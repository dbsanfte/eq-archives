ARTICLE I (ELIGIBILITY)
Essence of Darkness will not be race, class or religion specific. Members should be at least level 55 and preferably a master in some trade skill, but at all times acceptance will be based the views of the guild about the individual. A guild member must sponsor a person into the guild, and then current members online at the time will give their views of the person. If one EoD member disapproves of the person then the person will be denied membership. Real life friends, family and alternate characters of current guild members will be invited into the guild as long as they are level 55+. Those real life friends under level 55 will be referred to Dark Conscripts of Essence guild. Dark Conscripts of Essence member who reach level 55 will automatically be invited into EoD without prior approval of current EoD members. Members who have left the guild will not be invited back into EoD unless extenuating circumstances exist. All new members will be on a probation period for 2 weeks and may be removed by any officer if the values and goals of the new member are not consistent with those of EoD.

ARTICLE II (OFFICERS)
Any member of EoD who is nominated to be an officer by a current officer will be discussed in privacy among the existing officers. If any current officer disapproves the promotion then the nominated member will not be promoted. A reasonable amount of time for discussion will be given, but normally will not exceed 1 week. In the event of a guild merger, the leader of the merging guild will retain officer status. For every 10 members above level 55 of the merging guild the merging Guild Leader may appoint an officer.

ARTICLE III (REMOVAL)
It is inevitable that with so many members from different cultures and countries around the world that personality conflicts will result. At no time should a member be removed from the guild simply because an officer "doesn't like the member." In most situations prior to guild removal the member will be warned that some action he/she has done may be grounds for removal. This warning may be done by any officer having knowledge of an offense using a direct tell to the member. 3 officers must be in agreement before a member will be removed from the guild and the guild leader and 2 officers must be in agreement before and officer is removed from the guild. Offenses that are grounds for removal are, but not limited to: Taking NBG items while on a guild raid solely for the purpose of later selling Arguing with a guild mate in GUILD TALK Attempting to organize a mutiny KS'ing of a guild mate Repeated begging of a guild mate or non-guild mate Harassment of a guild mate to include harassment based on sex, rl race, rl religion or sexual orientation. Repeated harassment of a non-guild mate (except during times of war) Fraud to a guild mate Excessive use of bad language Our name implies that we have a dark nature to us and good role-play is always encouraged, but at no time should a member violate the norms of the Norrath community. Members that have been removed from the guild under circumstance outlined in this article will not be invited back into the guild.

ARTICLE IV (ANONYMOUS AND ROLE-PLAY)
No member should use anon unless specifically instructed by an officer. If it is essential for a members race, class or level to be unknown then role-play will be used. Exceptions would be during times of war or special missions, which will be coordinated through an EoD officer.

ARTICLE V (RAIDS)
Raids will be based on a priority system.  Possible targets will be given a priority level based on risk vs reward and on the current needs of the guild.  Each day will be given a designated priority level and if mobs of a equal or greater priority level are discovered up then officers will evaluate the current guild strength online and determine if a mobilization (raid) is to be initiated.  If an officer initiates a raid it will be mandatory for all EoD members online unless otherwise stated.  If a member is online and cannot attend the raid he/she will coordinate with an officer. 

ARTICLE VI (INACTIVE MEMBERS)
A member will be considered inactive if he/she has not been seen for 2 weeks unless prior knowledge has been stated. If the member is an officer his/her officer status may or may not be retained. Inactive members and officers will retain their current position if they return at a later date.

ARTICLE VII (BEGGING)
In all cases begging is discouraged. However master tradesman should offer services to guild members at a reduced rate. Also, if a member has an item for sale they should offer to sell it to the guild prior to auctioning it to the general public. Members should not expect a rare item to be sold to them at a reduced cost and should be thankful if a member does choose to reduce the normal cost of the item or give it to them. However, if the guild is responsible for you getting an upgrade to an item then the old item should be given to a guild member having a use for it. This will make us stronger as a guild and promote cohesion among guild members. A mule will be maintained by the Guild Leader to hold items for the guild. If the item is not of use to EoD it will be filtered down to DCoE for use.

ARTICLE VIII (VOTING)
As the land of Norrath changes and EoD continues to evolve there may arise a need to change various articles of this constitution or the norms of EoD. Changes will be proposed at announced officer meetings. A majority of the officers present at the officer meeting will decide if the change will proceed.

ARTICLE X (TARDINESS)
To encourage members to be on time for raids any member that is late for the start of a raid for any reason will go to the bottom of the loot list for that specific raid regardless of their current loot points. However those that are late, but come and stay a reasonable amount of time, usually 4 hours, will receive credit for attending the raid. 15 minutes prior no more port request will be honored so that our wizards/druids will have time to prepare themselves for the raid. Members that request ports after the "15 minutes to raid" is announced will need to arrange their own transportation to the raid, outside of the guild.

ADDENDUM (Alternate Characters)
Official guild policy on Alt characters under the new DKP loot distribution system:

The guild understands that some of its members may feel the need or desire to have and play alts to high levels and is willing to take them in. However, considering the guild's limited resources, the desire to progress as fast as possible and the time left till EQ2, the guild encourages all members to focus on one character mainly, if possible.

1) alts are allowed but not encouraged.

2) Each member should designate one character as main before level 55 (i.e. the current minimum entry level into EoD). Changes in main character are generally not allowed past that level unless there are attenuating circumstances and the member must request permission to do so, explaining all the reasons behind it. Officers will then approve or disapprove such request.

3) officers may at times ask someone to play an alt character for a raid but that should be regarded as a favor and there is NO OBLIGATION to do so nor will the person be penalized in any way should they decide against it. However, if any member wants to bring an alt instead of their designated main to a raid they need permission from the officers and there is no guarantee that they will agree with it. Just to put it simple: if an officer asks, you don't have to, if you want to, you must ask.

4) unless authorized to do otherwise, you MUST bring your main character to every guild mandatory event. If you are online, you have to participate. Each particular guild event will be classified by officers as mandatory or not but, in principle, all pre planned guild raids are mandatory. If for some particular reason you can not participate despite being online you should send a tell to an officer and explain the situation (this is important to avoid any ill feelings and misunderstandings)

5) alt characters can be allocated guild resources (loot, epics, etc) but priority will be given to mains, even if they raid less. The alts may have to wait longer than normal to get their chance at help on quests (epic included) but eventually should be considered for it. This is an example of the guild letting you have alts but in a way discouraging them. on the one hand, freedom of choice, on the other hand, the guild's own agenda and priorities.

6) double boxing may be allowed only after explicit permission and you still need to have one character designated as main. The guild considers double boxed characters generally less effective than single ones. Officers may ask a member the favor of double boxing sometimes but members are under NO OBLIGATION to do so and no penalty will be applied should they refuse. However, members must request permission to double box if they so wish.

7) DKP points will be assigned to members, not to individual characters. Using Kirak/Syric as an example: Kirak is designated as main, Syric as alt. Any point earned or spent by any of these characters is added or deducted from the single "Kirak/Syric" account. When Kirak is the one raiding he can obviouly auction for something for himself but needs permission to auction for an item for Syric (just like he would need permission to bring him to the raid in the 1st place). If its Syric at the raid he can always auction for an item for himself but needs permission to auction for an item for Kirak unless it was the officers who asked Syric to come along in which case he can always auction for one or the other.
