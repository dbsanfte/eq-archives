Feb 02, 2003

    With all the patching this week has been crazy. Standing at the entrance to ToV about to raid because its outs, all ours finally abd Poof! they re-pop all the zones.. Nothings left. We beat feat to Sleepers Tomb and found we had it all to ourselves. Much death and lootz ensued. We tested our metal against the Idol unslowed and made it through alive. We felt good about the AoW that ensued but he proved to be just a little too tough for us this day. Dain gave up a cloak to me finally. And then finally, a full night of sleep was given up by 50 EoD ers as we started with Roy and cleared all the standard named from the main NToV loop. Woot EoD. Again Much Loot was shared by all.
