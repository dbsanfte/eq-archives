April 17, 2002

     Rage has fallen, with a well executed battle. The battle lasted seconds, with only 2 casualties. Rage took almost the full 6 hours to spawn, and we had multiple people watching him non-stop during that time. He finally appeared at 10:30 Monday morning (while everyone was at work). Calls were made, people left work, and now Qualien has his epic piece. This is the second time EOD took rage down, using only guild members. Also, at the end of last week, Maynaard got his epic. This was the first Beastlord epic for the server. A couple of members are now 1 item away from their epics right now.

March 25th, 2002

     EOD was busy this last weekend. The guild spent a lot of time in the Temple of Veeshan, equipping a number of people. Piccalo got a group to assist him in taking down Eejag, putting himself another step closer to his epic. While on the way to ToV on Saturday, we noticed Kelorek'Dar was up, and we quickly mobilized a group to take him down. Karakas got the "Cloak of the Crystaline Waters", and "Xingtau got a Bioluminescent Orb". Saturday got off to a start in Akheva Ruins. We quickly mobilized to POF, to bring down an epic mob, but due to confusion with the server's reservation calendar, we backed off. We did not want to jump another guild, so we ended up slaying giants in Kael Drakkal in the end. While I don't have a list of everything people got this weekend, a lot of guildies got upgrades to their gear.

March 20th, 2002

     All previous news have been moved to the News Archive. Information that is over a month old will be moved to the archive from now on. There have been a number of changes to the web page over the last week, but most are not things you will notice (like all the spelling corrections, or the fact that the forums button now opens in a new window, to get around the auto-logon problem). However, some will become more apparent over the next month, as we try to make the web site more informative.  If you want to see something posted here, let the web masters know, by posting in the forums, or emailing us. We do not attend every event, so we need more info from you.

Some recent events since the last update:

  • Kuysat, Scaythe, Valkie and Ulaenia have completed their Epic quests.
  • Hirishi is hosting an open POH raid on Wednesday March 27th. Sign up here: Open Raid sign-up
  • Tric is now 60.
  • Venril Sathir's Remains was taken down again, on a fast-called raid, with help from some of the higher leveled DCoE members (looked like another guild was trying to mobilize also, but we were very fast).
  • ToV raiding is going very well. EoD is dropping more mobs in the same amount of time, and the loot is dropping for a number of our guild mates.
  • EoD is now on Rage rotation. Our turn is coming up soon, to get a rezz stick for the guild. We did this once, and we will be successful again.
