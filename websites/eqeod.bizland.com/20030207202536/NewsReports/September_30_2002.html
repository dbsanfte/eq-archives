DCoE Raid Guide


 		 

September 30, 2002

     Essence of Darkness has been busy these last few days. On Wednesday evening, we went to Icewell Keep to trigger Dain Frostreaver IV. It ended up being (Dain) version 425, but we made the attempt anyways. Wile we did loose the battle, We've got the pull down to a science. We have gained some valuable insight on tactics and we expect to beat his ass, next time around.

     On Thursday, we managed to catch both Vaniki and Zlandicar up in Dragon Necropolis. Mithrill now owns a Willsapper, and Qalien has recieved a Key to Sleeper's Tomb.

On Friday, VI was nice enough to do another server reboot. This time around, we took down Klandicar (gratz Solsara on a key to Sleeper's Tomb), then headed over to the Temple of Veeshan, for our first attempt on Aaryonar. After some spinning of wheels, we were able to get into position and single pull him. We did pretty well until we got swamped by hatchling repops. We had not planned well enough for them. We did learn some valuable things, including the fact that we're not all that far from being able to kill him. After Aaryonar, we found Dozekar the Crused up. We had some problems keeping him positioned, and mustering the appropriate DPS (but due to a weakening raid force), so we were unable to kill him.

     Saturday: so many mobs were up, amd so little time. EoD Killed The Va'Dyn and Talendor early in the day, then came back later for revenge on Dozekar the Crused (black and white tears), Telkorenar (gratz Raisty on loot), and "Gozzrem" (gratz Karakas and Shabonkerz on shovels). We ended the day with an attempt on Lendiniara the Keeper. Did a good job clearing around him, but had some problems with the healers being out of range during the combat, so we only got him to about 50%.

     Essence of Darkness snacked on Lcea Katta early in the day (too bad she dropped junk), then came back later for another attempt on Lendiniara the Keeper. We cleared around him quickly, and had much better positioning this time, but we ran out of gas and wiped when he was literally at 1% health. Lost our mages after the CR and were unable to try him again. He should feel very luck to still be alive. After the attempt on the Keeper we zoned out to Western Wastes and let off some steam in a slug fest with Sontalek. EoD got him down to about 40% before he stomped all over us. With a full squad of nukers along we might actually be able to kill that bad boy soon.
