October 30, 2002

     EoD is now using a new system for Raid Loot. Read the rules, and everything, at the EoD Raid site ( you can find the link in the EoD Raid forums - guildies only).

     EoD and DCoE have spent some time exploring the new Planes of Power. While not all guildies have purchased PoP yet (it is recommended), many players have found the new expansion to be a lot of fun. Groups have been gaining tons of experience, and some nice loot. The Plane of Knowledge is a great zone to bind in, with quick access around the world. On Saturday, EoD hunted down some mobs that were not on the Planes of Power, and managed to take down Telkorenar, The Va'Dyn and Lcea Katta (unfortunately, Gozzrem did not fall this time around). Meanwhile, DCoE had some members grinding out experience on the Plane of Innovation. The group decided to move over to the Plane of Nightmare. After a few deaths, the group decided to invite more guildies, and tried out the raid system, to make a large XP group (we were very short on healers). We did a little old-school exploration of PoN (got to love the new graveyard system). While no good loot dropped, players had fun checking out a new zone, new mobs, raiding system and the graveyard. On Sunday, both guilds got together, to explore the Plane of Disease. The first big mob to fall, was Gryme the Crypt Guardian, followed by Aramin the Spider Guardian.
