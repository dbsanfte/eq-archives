September 22, 2002

     On Tuesday, September 17th, Kanom became the first Dark Conscripts of Essence guild member, to complete his epic. Special thanks to those EOD members who helped DCoE with some of the battles, as well as the advice passed down. The screen shot has been slightly edited by Chebby, to look much nicer.

     There has been a lack of updates to the web site for a while now. This is not due to a lack of DCoE events, but due to a busy web administrator. Updates will be on a regular basis once again.

     DCoE has a new logo, thanks to the effort of Chebby.

     DCoE has been busy in the last month, with raids to Kael, Plane of Hate, Epic quests, The Hole, City of Mist, Plane of Growth, as well as other smaller events. The guild has been very active. DCoE will be going to Kedge Keep this week.

     DCoE would like to say thanks to members of "The Guard", who shared the Arena in  Kael with DCoE. After "The Guard" left, DCoE continued the camp for a few more hours. To top this off, "The Guard" allowed Xylar to loot the Scepter of Mastery. Thank you. Some of the loot from the raids (or quest pieces):
