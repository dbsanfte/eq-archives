General Raid Instructions

This guide will be designed to specify the norms and procedures EoD and DCoE will follow on raids in general. Raid leaders my give other guidance for a specific raid, but these rules should be followed in most situations.

1. Lag
Lag will always be a problem when we put 30+ peeps into a small area. However, there are some ways to minimize this problem.
A. go to OPTIONS/GRAPHICS and
(1) turn clip plane way down
(2) turn 3d sky off
(3) turn pc names off
B. go to OPTIONS/GRAPHICS and turn sound realism down
C. in the text box put
(1) /showspelleffects off
(2) /serverfilter on

2. chat and battle spam also seems to be a contant issue.
A. go to OPTIONS/CHAT and
(1) turn others hits off
(2) turn my hits off
(3) turn other misses off
(4) turn misses off
(5) turn spells off (certain situations will require mobs spells to be seen)
B. only certain people will use shout
(1) raid leader(s) to give instructions that need attention
(2) puller to give the next incomming mob(s)
(3) the main assist (MA) to announce the mob he will be taking first or switches.
C. only certain people will use OOC
(1) group leaders to announce their groups readiness level (ie mana, LDs, AFKs, other issues)
(2) mezzes
(3) snares
(4) slows
(5) malise and tash
(6) cripple line of spells
(7) dispells
D. all others will default to group talk. Group leaders will be informed of each members status on a regular basis so he can OOC it to the rest of raid.
E. never give the pullers direct tells
F. aviod tells to the raid leader when at all possible. Inform your group leader and let him relay information to the raid leader.
G. never make suggestions in OOC or shout. It confuses people on what they should be doing.
H. do not OOC or shout for buffs. If you need buffs give a tell to the person you want to get it from and be patient. That person may have several tells for buffs.

3. do not leave the camp unless you are directed to do so

4. be patient at all times

5. never be disappointed if you die or we have a TPW (total party wipeout). On raids it is likely you will die or that the raid will be wiped out. Part of the chance everyone takes. Accept that you will die now.

6. all are encouraged to research a zone we are raiding. It is a must, though, for certain key people on a raid to make this a priority. The following people must research a zone
(A) monks (should know where mobs are located, how to pull them, what will DT and what will cause it and any pulling strategies
(B) Tanks (warrior, paladin and shadow knights) and enchanters need to know the MR and mez capabilities of the mobs in the zone. Tanks should have a list of priority targets handy incase you are called upon to be MA.

7. only group leaders will loot. they will also call off what loot is on mobs by item and class (ie chanter robe, pally boots, etc). They also need to call off if mobs have not loot (ie scarling empty). Pages, ruins, and words are not considered loot and will in most cases be FFA.

8. Respect loot rules. If it is a guild raid then we are EoD not individuals. If one of us get a piece of equipment then we become stronger as a guild. Be happy for the guild and not mad that you did not get the item. We will all get it in the long run.

9. do not complain about CR, pulls, loot, etc. It only lowers morale and makes others doubt the success or chance of success of the raid. Always stay positive!

10. if you must be late for a raid or know you will have to leave early it is normally not a problem if you let the raid leader know so he/she doesn't assign you a significant role or have a plan of who will assume that role in your absense.
 
11. make a hot key to assist the MA and SA.  Do not assist until the MA calls it.  He/she needs to build aggro so the mob does not ping pong and healers can concentrate on one person.  It may seem like a long time before he calls assist.  Remain patient.  If he/she forgets to call it then they will figure it out real soon and correct there mistake or they will die.
      /assist Warrior_01

12. MOST IMPORTANT is have fun! Get together with friends and have a good time.
