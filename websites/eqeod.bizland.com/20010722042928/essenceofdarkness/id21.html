Member to Officers

Any member of EoD who successfully plans and conducts 5 raids will be promoted to officer. This is to encourage guild members to assume leadership and gain knowledge of planning and conducting events. 1 of these raids can be a key getting sesion for the guild as long as it has enough participation to qualify as a major guild event. To plan a raid first reserve it on the Community Calander, then post it on EoD's calander for quick refernce, then put a sign up sheet on the raid page in forums and posts.
