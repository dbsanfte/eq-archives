- Welcome to EoD

Essence of Darkness was established in West Commons on October 12th, 2000 near the Derv Camp by Waldoo (Mezmen) and the late Roland (Bain). We were the 5th guild established on Tholuxe Paeles sever. EoD was born from the mother guild of Essence of Courage on the Karanas Server. On April 12th, 2001 Essence of Darkness merged with Chapions of Olympus guild at the stone bridge in North Karana. On April 14th, 2001 Dark Conscripts of Essence was formed at the Toll Booth in West Commons by Expoc (Petmann) for our level 20 to 45 apprentices to learn the ways of Essence of Darkness. On July 30th, 2001 Sanctuary of Salvation guild merged with EoD at the West Commons druid ring.
 

 

- Waldoo Mezmen
