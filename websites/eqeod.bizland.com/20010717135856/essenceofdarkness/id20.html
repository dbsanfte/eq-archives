Raid loot order and instructions

Anyone can plan and conduct a raid. Just reserve it on the Community Calender. Then post it on the EoD Calender. After that post on the raids page with instrucions on the raid (ie. where we meeting, what time, what is the goal). Everyone is encouraged to plan and conduct raids.

EoD will give loot items to the member that participates on the most raids. This does not apply to open raids. We will keep track of who attends a raid and what they looted. For instance if someone has been to 3 raids and got 2 itmes they are one behind. If another person has been to 5 raids and looted 3 itmes they are 2 behind. The second person would get to loot item. In situations of ties we will random 100 then. This wont hurt new members or members that havnt been able to attend alot of raids, because those that are always there, probably have many of the items.
