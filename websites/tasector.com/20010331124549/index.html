New ReCorder Version! Posted Saturday, March 24, 2001 by ElmerElite1

Its a bit late for a news submission but here it is anyway!

A new TA Demo Recorder has been released.
This was made by the Swedish YankSpankers

Here are the new features.

New commands includes

.cmdwarp (host only battleroom) pause game at starts and enables everyone to teleport their cmd until unpaused

.sharelos somewhat like what +sharelos should have been (shares radar also)

.sharemappos shows where you are looking on the minimap to your allies

shift-f2 tahook config

.income/.total (replay only) shows income/accumelated income for players

.lockon name (replay only) locks your screen to someone that was using .sharemappos during game

ctrl-b centers on a unemployed con unit

ctrl+shift add 100 units to factory build ques

whiteboardkey+mouse1 draw lines

whiteboard+double click add named marker

whiteboard+ mouse3 add unnamed marker

whiteboard+mouse2 remove drawings

dtline+shift+hold mouse1 create dt line

dtline+shift+mouse2+mouse1 create dt ring


You can download the recorder Here

http://www.tauniverse.com/SY/download/tademo/tademo98.exe
