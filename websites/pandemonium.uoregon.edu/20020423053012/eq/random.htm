all the activated alt skills are done by a text command... just hit shift+up next time you hit can5/mgb or whatever... they command is:
/alt activate #

where # is the skill number (you can just scroll through numbers to see what they are, or scroll up after you use the skill)
mgb = 35
can5 = 47

so.. my macro:

/g tearing off a random limb...
/alt activate 47
