So, you want to join the ranks of The Valiant Crusaders?  Great!  But there are some requirements that must first be met.

1)  An officer of the appropriate Order or an approved standing member of The Valiant Crusaders must sponsor you during your evaluation period.   If you are unsure which Order you fall into, use the chart below.

2)  Your character must be of an accepted class and must be at least level ten.  Yes, 'dark' races are allowed only if they are role-played in accordance with the guild charter.  You must email your character's name, race, class, level, brief character background, where your character is currently located, and three suggested dates/times for your sponsor to meet you in-game (see the next point) to Kadath Dreamfire, Lord of Vision.   Your character's history will be posted on our message forum so that standing members will know who is petitioning to join us and give them a chance to get to know you during your evaluation period.  A final decision will not be made regarding your character's admittance into the Valiant Crusaders until a character history is received.

3)  During your evaluation period, your sponsor will observe you for a minimum of three hours of real time over any number of game sessions.  You and your sponsor will arrange where and when these sessions occur.  Your sponsor may choose to group with you during this time.  In most occasions a sponsor will be chosen that should be able to group with the initiate.  If the levels of the sponsor and the person being evaluated are too widespread, the sponsor will only stay near the evaluated individual instead of being grouped with him/her.

During this evaluation period, your sponsor will be making observations on several aspects.  These include, but are not limited to: fairness, role-playing ability, and self-conduct.

4)  After this evaluation period, the sponsor will submit his/her findings to the High Council so that a final decision may be reached.

5)  The High Council will then pass a final judgment on the character being evaluated.  You will be contacted with results.

Alternate Character Policy

1)  Your character must be of an accepted class and must be at least level five.  You must email your character's name, race, class, level, and brief character background Kadath Dreamfire, Lord of Vision.   Your character's history will be posted on our message forum to let other guild members know this new alternate character is linked to the same soul (player) as an existing charatcer.  Your alternate character will not receive a guild tag until a character history is received.
