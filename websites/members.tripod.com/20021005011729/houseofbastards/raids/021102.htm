RECOUNT
by Ziff

Well, once again we KICKED ASS!

Evening got off to a strange start. Grimling Forest, normally empty, was occupied by 30+ 60th levels crom CB. The result was laaaaaaaaaag! Unberable, unplayable lag. So, after some talk with CB and the crew, we decided to reloacte to Castle Mistmoore. The castle of the undead was owed some major payback.

We ported to Stemfont and popped directly into the beginning of a GM event. We decided to make a short stop and wintness/participate.

Seems a gnomeish inventor had a new creation he wanted to show off. He had a few Dark Elf engineers along who helped him build his new invention.

When he revealed it there was great awe. A giant mechanical spider! He spoke at length about it's wonderful power source and how great it would be and....

It went crazy! It attacked everyone! It was VERY red to HoB but we charged in anyway. There were about 30 50+ levels there who unleashed great force upon the mechanical monster. Ziff ran around mezzing small mechanical adds trying to save the gnome as he was attacked by them. In the end, the spider fell and the small ones were destroyed as well.

THe GM then handed out rewards of various stuff to folks who helped. Some got small parts from the destroyed machine, including Nic (Congrats Nic) who also got a special mention for his assistance.

I have about 8 screen shots. Faan, if you have space can I send em to you to post on the HoB sight?

So, then we were off to Mistmoore. In Mistmoore, Mulak, who had never been inside the castle before, was set to lead us crawling through the place. Almost everyuone had no clue where we were and no one knew where we were going. Crawl, HoB style! We lost Bria in an early big battle due to some communication breakdown and cramped quarters. A while later Mulak decided to pull a huge number of VERY angry residents. 12 to be exact. Ziff stood in the center of the fray chain stunnning and, near the back 1/2 of the battle, was cut down again due to some communication issues. But, HoB prevailed. (By the way, seeing 12 things effected by an AE stun is pretty scary. Especially when about 1/2 of them are red RESIST. Fills your entire chat window with text and an enchanters heart with dread.)

After a revival we returned to the crawl. We finished off in the princess's chambers/throne room. Mulak again pulled 6-10 angry residents at a time and the fighting was nearly non stop. Had some close calls, but never lost another soul.

When it cam time to leave Wildride got caught climbing above the throne canopy. (darn Paladins) so, we stood on the throne and began ourt escape. Unfortunatley, while Ziff was attempting to invis those who needed it the room began respawning. Cleric out of mana, Wild not in the group... trouble.

Ziff ran around and enthralled everything he could but the spawn rate was getting ahead of him. The mobs were pounding on him hard and doom was creeping in. Suddenly Wild was back in the group and the room went blurry. We gated out with Ziff at less than 20% health and the room occupied by 6+ angry residents. Closest escape of HoB's history.

Was a good night over all even if it started rough.
