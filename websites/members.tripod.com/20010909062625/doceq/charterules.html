Disciples of Chaos Charter

We, the Disciples of Chaos, believe it is our lot in life to spread disorder and chaos across the face of Norrath. We value the individual's ability to gain power and use it as he or she deems fit. Most importantly, one is to take deep pleasure out of the spreading of chaos and the use of one's power.

All decisions reguarding the guild will be made by the guild council. For more information please see the page devoted to the guild organization .

Guild Commandments

  1. Thou shall never betray a fellow guild member for any reason.
  2. Thou shall strive to become more powerful.
  3. Thou shall learn skills that shall benefit the guild and yourself.
  4. Thou shall render aid to a guild member whenever needed.
  5. Thou shall only work with the good aligned peoples when there is a profit invovled or a similar goal to be achieved.
  6. If thou choose you may betray allies as long as they are not guild members or friends.
  7. Thou shall not judge one by race. One shall be judged by actions and religion. In this way we shall ensure that future gain to oneself and the guild is not hampered by prejudice.
  8. Thou shall go to war for the guild when the need arises.
  9. If thou art given a job in the guild then thou shall strive to perform as best ye can.
  10. Thou must be agnostic or whorship an evil god.
