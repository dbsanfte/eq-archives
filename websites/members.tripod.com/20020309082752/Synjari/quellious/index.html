Hail and well met, dear friends. I am known on the Quellious server as Synjari Na`Myraen, however I have many faces and identities. Synjari, though, is best known to you all.

I felt that many of you would like to have your real life photos posted. In saying that, I now present to you all, your very own personal, Quellious Player Photo's site! I will try to maintain this site as best as I can.

Please feel free to submit to me your photographs (limit 2), your online name (surnames where applicable), and your race/class. If you do not wish your email address to be posted, please specify that in your email. Please remember that it makes my job lots easier if you submit the photographs in either gif or jpeg format, preferably jpeg format. New posts will be denoted with an asterik and will show up as new posts. And now onto the Photographs!
