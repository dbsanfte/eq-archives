Tower of Haetyr T'nak

Hallway to the Guest Chambers

You walk down a long corridor which seems like it has no end. Every few strides down the hall, brings you to a doorway. Some are closed, some aren't. These are the guest chambers of the Tower. Many of these rooms which have closed doors, usually mean that they are the frequent guests to the Tower as well as being allies to the Haetyr T'nak. Several different rooms have nameplates upon their doors. These are the most honored guests within the Tower and therefore the room is only used by these people.

Misery Deireadh an Tuath oversees those who enter the Tower and sees that they are escorted to their rooms. She has set these rooms apart from the others by using a strange type of wood with which the door is made. These doors have a strange glow upon them which makes most guests hesitant to explore the rooms. Maybe she will tell you of these doors someday.
