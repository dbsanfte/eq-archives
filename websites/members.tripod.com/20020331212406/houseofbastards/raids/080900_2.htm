DAGNOR'S CAULDRON TIDAL LORDS
Aug. 9, 2000


DAGNOR'S CAULDRON RAIDING PARTY
Faan - cleric (19), Krug - monk (19), Purgatori - ranger (17), Wildride - paladin (19), Ziff - enchanter (20)


After getting a teleport from Kiah, binding in Kalladim and running to Dagnor's a smaller group assembled for part 3 of the quest. Zoritsa and Luifang had stayed behind, planning something horrible for the Goblin's of Runnyeye. With Blladesinger having to leave on personal business, it was down to Purgatori, Krug, Wildride, Faan and Ziff to hunt the Tidal Lord.

HoB assembled at a camp of friendly warriors. After organizing and buffing themselves they set out to find a Tidal Lord. Within Minutes Purgatori spotted their giant clawed tracks. She gave chase and soon returned with one in tow. Battle lust still flowing in their veins, the small band of HoB members fell upon him with a vengeance. Less than 2 minutes later they stood over a hacked and smoldering corpse of a defeated Tidal Lord.

They bent to loot and indeed it carried the ring they sought. At this point Purgatori had to leave and so the remaining members of HoB spent another 30 minutes hunting Goblin's and even finding and killing another Tidal Lord.

It was a glorious evening and the remaining members made camp at their newfound friendly Warrior's camp. They ate and drank into the night, trading tales of valor and slept well into the next dawn.
