NAJENA
July 5, 2000


RAIDING PARTY
Blladesinger - ranger, Faan - cleric (16), Fibben - druid, Krug - monk, Lorsua - druid (18), Luifang - monk, Purgatori - ranger, Triton - cleric (21), Wildride - paladin (18), Ziff - enchanter (17), Zoritsa - enchanter


It took us a bit to get organized, but, with excitement in our hearts, we gathered at the edge of Najena's lair.

We had a brief meeting with Ratskeller, a friend of Ziff's and member of OoV. Ratskeller was kind enough to give away a few items to some members of HoB, including a big huge Ogre War Maul and a Flowing Black Robe. He then proceeded into the depths of Najena and waited patiently in case he might be called upon for assistance.

HoB began diving through the tunnels of Najena. We quickly and ruthlessly wiped out the opening rooms, taking down ogre guards, skeletons and elementals with ease. Being 9 strong, very little lived for more than a few minutes. We then proceeded into a hallway and went through a secret wall. We met several more skeletons and made short work of them too. HoB was getting into a rhythm. Luifang was sent deeper into the pits of Najena to retrieve more victims and she soon brought several waves of Ogre guards, dark boned skeletons and elementals. All met with a swift and furious death.

Luifang then came back with a scouting report. Bonecracker had been found. Bonecracker had 2 necromancers and their pets keeping him company. Even worse, they all conned red to her accept the pets. (meaning all 22+ level) She was scared and stated such. HoB, being fearless and foolish as a group, cheered her on and sent her down to retrieve the evil frog and his friends. She was encouraged by the shouts of "kill the frog!" emanating from several members.

Soon Luifang returned with Bonecracker and his friends and they were made short and ruthless work of. The enemies were handled with the efficiency that HoB has become known for. When the dust settled the group was at full health and no one was below 50% mana. A cry was heard "Bonecracker is dead!".

As a group we then proceeded top his lair and began luring ogres into it to meet their fate. We continued this until Bonecracker and his friends reappeared and then it was decided that, after another merciless victory, HoB should press on, deeper into Najena.

HoB organized and began moving towards the ogre captain. Ogres were dying at our feet and we pressed on. Soon we had cleared everything up to him and we waited in his entry hall for Luifang to summon him out.

With skill and precision Luifang managed to bring only the Captain out to meet his doom. HoB decended on him and although he was hitting hard and seeming to take forever to weaken, he was dying. He soon realized his fate and decided to flee. This is the first point where things got ugly. Being more than 4 levels above our best casters, his running could not be stopped. HoB pursued him, trying to chop him down. But his bellows brought help and Ziff, in a classic communication blunder, said something which some people interpreted as run. Chaos ensued. Ziff tried to recall the running party members but as the y returned, more monsters were in tow. HoB regathered and fought on in the small entryway, and barely survived.

We gathered our breath, rested for the next fight and discussed briefly the coming strategies when we discovered that we had waited too long. Ogre guards began appearing in the room, seeking revenge. Soon we were overwhelmed, and just as it was getting bleak, Officer Grush appeared. A level 25 shaman who hits for 40 - 50. Pandemonium broke out. A cry for help to OoV was made. Soon, blood and magic mingled as Raskeller, Kalladin and Faans friend, Rekinom, assisted HoB in slaughtering the ogres. When all was said and done, we had lost Zorista and Blladesinger. Rekinom was kind enough to resurrect the dead members and thanks were made all around.

We began to regroup and reorganize. We again slayed the Captain as he returned. Lorsua had arrived to help making the HoB 10 strong. We were discussing moving to another part of Najena when Grush returned for revenge, bringing a room full of guards. Somehow, this also brought out several officers. HoB was again descending quickly into trouble. A shout for help was made again. Ratskeller and Kalladin arrived as Krug fell to the mighty ogres. They immediately helped us regain control of the room, but sadly, Krugs corpse lay crumpled on the floor.

As we again said our thanks and regrouped, Purgatori arrived at the entrance to Najena. So, we began fighting our way out to retrieve her. In the meantime Fibben looted Krug's body and gated back to Krug to return his stuff. HoB fought to Purgatori and warmly accepted her tired a bleeding body into the group. We then began a short decent, fighting and killing two tentacle terrors in the process.

As it was getting close to 11, we decided to call it a night. Also, since Ratskeller said he had more gifts, we wanted to take some time to spend with him.

Ratskeller, in his infamous generosity, gave items to everyone still present. Of high note was a Jacinth gem! Luifang won the roll for the item and will have chance to turn this planer only loot into a fantastic piece of jewelry.

So, I sold all our loot but the stuff Blladesinger looted, and made 57 platinum. (yes, I owe money to almost everyone from the last few raids. I'll try to remeber to give it out next time.) Not bad for a night with no special drops. With Blladesinger loot, we could easily be in the low 70's, depending on the amount of Bronze and Ringmail he received. Experience seemed to be pretty good, between 1 and 2 bubbles. Not bad for a first night in a new place. Overall I would call it a success. We learned a bit, we are beginning to get better at working with 2 teams and we have a new place to hunt that people seemed to enjoy. We did, unfortunately, suffer 3 deaths, but fortunately two of those were resurrected.

I think we learned that we need to keep moving in Najena. Sitting in a room seemed to cause us a lot of trouble. I think next time if we clear and move, we will do much better.
