June 28th

    We have made a new alliance.  The Dragon Knights of Freeport have joined our cause, alongside the Brethren of Wolves.  We've tested it out (at the high cost of Danar's life, the guildmaster of DKF), and there is no noticeable exp loss at lev 18, and there is no looting in a guild war.  We are well on our way to face the vile DeVir's and their kin.  To celebrate, I've "modernized" the Alliance page.  Keep the preparations for battle!

    Another idea I've been thinking about, before the battle, it might be fun to go with an earlier idea we had, to invade Permafrost Keep, a usually 100% empty dungeon which holds medium sized bronze (I bet Okame would like that:) as well as untold treasures.  We might want to vote on this at an upcoming meeting.

Your Founder,

Lexaien

 

June 26th

    Your leader has been reborn.  Along with a new body, comes a new outlook.  With the abolishion of the dues system and lessening of the guild admissions, we have well become a bustling powerful guild, with my potential.  Modal is back (even if busy with other matters), and we've voted for a guild battle against the infamous DeVir, a very well roleplayed guild of dark elves and other scum.  Although no date has been set for this, as I haven't managed to reach the leader, Dinin, who apparently is overworking, according to his RL friend.  The important thing is that we will have a battle, and for that we must plan for it.  The problem is... when?

    The meeting was originally set for tomorrow, but something has come up in the real world, so I will be unable to attend.  If someone would like to organize this meeting further, please post so on the msg board.  Otherwise, we'll try to organize something during the week, next weekend at the latest.  The good thing about organizing the meeting we had earlier this week to get everyone to know each other, is that now that we have a good idea of who each of us are, we can handle the rest of the meetings over guildsay.

    I'll be updating the guildmembers list, along with the new Alias subject, for those of us with two "bodies", such as myself.  That's it for now, I suppose.  Quest on, noble Faydwerians, and never lose hope.

Your Founder,

Lexaien
