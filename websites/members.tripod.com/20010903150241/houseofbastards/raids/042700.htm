NORTH RO
April 27, 2000


RAIDING PARTY
Faan - cleric (12/13), Fibben - druid, Krug - monk (13/14)


In a somewhat impromptu gathering, Faan, Krug and Fibben (yes, our favorite little druid made an appearance!!) returned to the scene of the Wednesday bloodletting for another attack on dervs.

After Faan and Krug had an early-on, brief, ugly run-in with Dorn at Derv 1 we abandoned that post, found Fibben, re-grouped and moved over to Derv 2.

Joining forces with a paladin and a warrior, the 5 of us were able to clear the camp and keep it cleared for over an hour. Fibben tried out his as-yet-unused "harmony" spell and it worked like a champ. He was able to harmonize the whole camp and allowed us to fight only one or two things at a time. The carnage was everywhere. We looked quite busy .. and we were. It was challenging, and I believe there was only one casualty, that being due to my video refresh acting awfully strange all night. Fibben came close (way too close, sorry Fib), but the derv was eliminated and I wasable to revive him back up from unconsciousness.

The happy news is that Krug made level 14 and Faan 13. Congrats!! The loot I sold for us garnered 1pp each and I think Fibben has even more to sell.
