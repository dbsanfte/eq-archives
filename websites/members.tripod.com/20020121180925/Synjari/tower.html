You are now within the tower and walking along blue and white-veined marble halls that seem to stretch over vast distances. Misery walks in front of you and her catlike grace makes it seem like she is almost floating across that cold marble floor. Finally an intersection of many directions branch off from the main hall. Misery stops and turns around to you, smiling.

"This is our home, of all Haetyr who are left in this world. We extend warm welcomes as well as the freedom to walk through our halls as you wish or just sit down in one of our rooms and possibly chat with one of the elders about our history and purpose. If you wish to speak with me again, you need only call and I shall answer."

With that last sentence Misery fades away with a giggle that almost sound like tinkling of small bells. You now have the choice to walk into any hall or room you wish and discover the secrets of Misery and this mysterious Order she mentions...
