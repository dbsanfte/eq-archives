GUK SQUIRE
June 21, 2000


RAIDING PARTY
Blladesinger - ranger, Faan - cleric (16), Fibben - druid, Lorsua - druid, Luifang - monk (17), Wildride - paladin (17), Ziff - enchanter (16), Zoritsa - enchanter (16)


This raid was a raging success!

HoB began assembling around 7:00. With three members on we decided to try and move in to make sure no one took the Squire room. There was a small communication error (Ziff's fault, sorry) which resulted in a lot of us dying quickly.

Not to be daunted, we assembled the available crew, including a new friend who may get an invite to join, a ranger named Blladesinger. So, with Lui, Bllad, Faan, Fibben, Zor and Ziff we began to move into the Squire room.

In a matter of minutes we cleared it and had solid control. Lui began pulling frogs to us. They came fast and furious and died even faster. Lui was practicing her feign skills and the damage was being dealt so quickly, Faan barely ever had to heal anyone. Ziff had a chance to experiment with some new group Mesmerizing techniques. All in all, it was off to a roaring start. Only problem was, no visit by the squire.

About 1 hour into the blood bath Wildride arrived. When this occurred, Bllad announced he needed to leave anyway. We were sorry to see Bllad go. He seemed to fit in well.

After the change of the guard the slaughter began anew. Deeper and deeper into Guk Lui went and continued to lead the hapless frogs to a horrible death. Occasionally pulling Shaman as high as 21st level or more and still, they died quickly and horribly.

Soon, Fibben received messages from Lorsua and Purgatori telling him that they were alive and well and able to join us if need be. This must have been heard by the Squire because within minutes he appeared. The party turned on him and quickly dispatched him, being only 17th level. Fibben, the looter for the night bent over his corpse to discover he wielded, The Runed Bone Fork! Rare spawn, rare drop. HoB began a short celebration, passing the fork around for everyone to see and sharing Elven wine.

Soon after Lorsua arrived and we secretly made a party of 7 to begin the slaughter again. Purgatori was too far away and was encouraged to stay in East Commons for the night with a promise we would join him soon.

After 30 minutes of death and dismemberment of Frogs a large train of them came charging into the room, returning from chasing a few high level adventurers. We backed against the wall and they kept charging off into their holes. Yet, the last knight, weakened and angry, decided to seek vengeance on HoB for our ruthless slaughter of our kin.

In his weakened state he was quickly reduced to near death and decided flight was a better option. Unfortunately, this happened so fast, Fibben did not have the time to snare him. As he tore down the hallway screaming for help Luifang gave chance. Seeing the frog leap into a pit that leads to some very large, very destructive frogs she announced "ZONE!" repeatedly. HoB began a systematic retreat from Guk. Unfortunately, Lorsua and Zorista failed to escape. However, everyone else made it out, some with nary a scratch on them. (Even Faan! Miracle of Miracles!)

HoB reassembled itself and charged back into the Frog holes to retrieve their corpses and reclaim the Squire's home. After doing so, and waiting for one more chance at the Squire we left the smoking ruins of Guk. The Squire, in his fear, appeared no more.

Over all it was an excellent night. 2+ bubbles of experience for most everyone who played all night. 3.6 plat for each party member and the Runed Bone Fork to sell and divide the cash for.

Guk waits in fear for the return of HoB.
