PERMAFROST PREACHER
Aug. 2, 2000


RAIDING PARTY
Faan - cleric (19), Lorsua - druid (23), Luifang - monk (20), Purgatori - ranger (17), Wildride - paladin (19), Ziff - enchanter (20), Zoritsa - enchanter (20)


HoB assembled outside the Permafrost Keep. Many of us were very early and were enjoying discussions amongst each other and friends of Norrath. It was a jovial time with a disastrous sudden interruption. Someone who was headed on the Vox run brought an ice giant in tow. After he safely zoned into Permafrost the giant turned upon Ziff and, deciding he was the frailest of the possible adversaries, crushed him flat. The rest of HoB escaped without a scratch.

Soon all members had arrived and we began assembling for the assault. We shouted threats and warnings into the caverns and rattled our weapons.

As HoB began to dive into the Goblin stronghold some conflicting information was accidentally provided. This, coupled with the disappearance of Purgatori and Luifang wandering deep within the keep caused mass confusion to the group. Unsettled and off balance, we took on the edge of the flag room. This proved a fatal mistake. Fighting in the twisting tunnels, numerous errors were made by everyone. Party members were fighting different goblins, Ziff and Zorista had trouble controlling the fight in the winding passageways. The druids and rangers couldn't see everything they needed to snare. As time went on a fight that started out with 3 goblins grew into 5, then 7. The tide was turning against HoB. We attempted to rally, but too late. Ziff was destroyed by an evoker. Soon many others fell as well and a hasty retreat was called for. Many escaped, but many lives were lost.

At the keep entrance we reassembled again. It was already late in the night and we were frustrated by our failures. Purgatori had now returned with Lorsua and so we broke into 2 groups. We reorganized and began the assault again. This time it went much smoother. The flag room fell easily and we turned the corner heading for the sanctuary. There we met and easily killed the Priest and many of his younger studies. We continued to carve into the goblin worshipers and we arrived at the stage. We soon cleared it and, after a discussion with a more seasoned barbarian who appeared to want to help, but was causing us great trouble as a result, set up camp. We began shouting taunts to the priest and slaughtering his minions. A kind druid who also was looking for a conversation with the hated priest agreed to allow us his audience on the grounds that if he was wearing a special headpiece we sell it to him. We agreed.

After a while Luifang began drawing the goblins pets, dire wolves, from their lairs. This proved interesting as several were 24th level and one was 27th. These mean dogs were taking huge chunks out of our party, hitting double attacks for 68 at times. But, like the goblins, they and their puppies died fast and viscous deaths.

Then, as we were slaughtering more of the preachers clan the Preacher himself showed. He was furious and immediately began assaulting the HoB members. In the midst of battle HoB turned as a single fighting unit from the Goblins minions at hand to the Preacher. The Preacher did not stand a chance. The wrath of HoB was so great that before he could even begin to run he was slaughtered. His blood polling on the frozen floor, we turned back upon our former prey and cut them down as well.

Once the hall was clear the Preacher's body was searched for the cold iron morning star he was rumored to carry. It was not to be found. Upon his head was a circlet of magic and carved with runes. The circlet that the druid friend was seeking. We gladly made a trade for the Preachers headpiece and all parties were satisfied. Then, HoB carved "Kiban Felewithe" in the preachers stage, filled the letters with the Preachers cooling blood and allowed it to freeze solid. Leaving a permanent reminder of our vengeance for the remaining goblin hoards to stare at in every religious ceremony.

While waiting for it to freeze we continued to assault the goblins and wolves. For another hour we drew them from the depths of the keep and slaughtered them till their bodies lay knee deep in the temple.

This so enraged the goblin hordes that they sent the head of their religion, the Prelete to kill HoB. This deplorable beast met with a quick and horrible fate as well. HoB would exact their revenge, in the blood of the hated goblins.

The night wore on and HoB decided it was time to retire. We needed to exit the stronghold and so we organized into a strike team and splayed goblin after goblin as we marched out the doors. Proclaiming loud and long HoB's name and imploring that the surviving Goblins remember that name, next to the name Kibin Felewithe.

Around the campfire that night a discussion was had. The members were confused as to why the Preacher did not carry the morning star he was so proud of. Why did he carry this runed circlet instead? Pondering this the friends all retired to their beds and slept the sleep of the just. Except Ziff. Who could not get the idea out of his head. It bothered him so. So, being extremely proficient in the goblin language, he formulated a plan. Under cover of invisibility he snuck back into the keep. He ventured deep within it's walls until he came upon and adept, studying alone in the Preachers darkened chambers. Ziff revealed himself and charmed the monster. With his language ability he questioned the beast. He asked it where the morning star was, why the preacher was not carrying it. Since goblin's are a cowardly race at heart, the adept immediately told the story. It seems that the HoB scouts had frightened the goblins. They knew what the HoB was there for and they began to be afraid since they so easily reached the preachers chambers with only a scouting mission. so, to protect the beloved morning star, they hid it.

Ziff forced the adept to show him the hidden location of the morning star. The adept gave in after only a small amount of torture and so he procured it from it's hidden cache below the stage. Ziff then murdered the adept on the spot and wrote with the Goblin blood across the floor. "You can not hide from HoB. We have the morning star. We will return for the rest of your lives later."

Ziff then snuck back out of the keep under cover of invisibility, morning star in hand.

It was a frustrating night in many ways. Disorganization, bad communication (especially on Ziff's part) and general confusion caused late starts, sloppy fighting (at times) and imbalanced experience gain. Still, we achieved the first objective. We profited by more than 50 platinum each and many of the group got a decent amount of experience. In the end it was a good night and we were all very pleased with the outcome.
