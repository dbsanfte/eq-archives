GUK SQUIRE
June 28, 2000


RAIDING PARTY
Blladesinger - ranger, Faan - cleric (17), Krug - monk, Lorsua - druid, Purgatori - ranger (14), Thana - monk, Triton - cleric (21), Wildride - paladin (17), Ziff - enchanter (17), Zoritsa - enchanter


Well, someone sent advanced notice to the frogs of our upcoming raid. Either that, or they had figured out the pattern. (Every Wednesday hundreds of us are slaughtered...) We also ran into a bit of bad luck. The end result was something akin to a Froglock raid on HoB.

Unfortunately, Fibben let it be known that he was too drunk to attend the raid and we found out that Krug, Purgatori and Lorsua would be there soon, but were going to be about 30 minutes late. So, HoB assembled into a group of seven and, wasting no time, began the assault on Guk.

They stormed the Squire's hiding place to discover that Ratskeller, an old friend of Ziff's from his guild was sitting causally on some of the fallen beams, surveying the room. Pleasantry's were exhanged, Ratskeller, in his infinite generousity, distributed gifts to a few members of HoB and proceeded to tell us that his pet was lost in Guk somewhere. It apparently was slaughtering frogs and could not be controlled. (Seems to be an OoV trait) HoB promised to look out for his pet Gober and Ratskeller decided to follow us on our raid in case we found his pet.

HoB began clearing rooms and soon came upon a ladder that dropped deep into the heart of the foul swamp. After a bit of discussion, we decended.

In a small room with a cramped ceiling we met our first batch of dangerous frogs. A knight and several shaman soon fell to the hands og HoB and we prepared to move deeper, heading for the Fungus room. HoB decended a greasy slope to the edge of a vast cavern. There, they could see 3 heart spiders crouched upon a ledge.

The party gathered itself together and Ziff gave the order, "slay the spiders!" Thana, who had some arcane knowledge of the place screamed "NOOOOOO!", but too late. Wildride's arrows had flown and the injured heart spiders were charging him in anger.

We soon discovered why Thana was so upset. On a narrow ledge, in a small hallway riddled with broken stone HoB made a stand, fighting wave upon wave of Frogs. We slaughtered dozens of them and they kept coming. During this slaughter the rest of HoB arrived at the entrance of Guk and were promised an escort to our current location as soon as we had control of the situation. But, it was a madhouse. We would gain control of the situation and then more frogs would arrive. We fought long and hard and had finally stood, bleeding, nearly out of mana, facing two small scared frogs. HoB swarmed the frogs and as they began their decent into death a giant Troll came rumbling by. This troll spelled disaster for HoB, as he was being chased by 6 Froglock knights. HoB was too weakened to be able to control this new onslaught of frogs and the call was given to run. We could almost see the entrance to lower Guk from where we stood, and now we had to flee.

There was no escape.

HoB was cut down to the last man.

So, at the entrance we reformed into two groups. Not to be daunted by the frogs we began to work our way back to the place of our demise. Group 1 entered the Squires waiting room and began clearing it. Purgatori's eyes were wide with fear and excitement since this was her first time entering the depths of Guk. Then something strange happened, we were swarmed by dozens of small, week frogs. We could barley move and team 1 was alone. This had never happened before, but we were in trouble. Another retreat was called for and, although most made it out, Faan died.

After a bit of discussion as to what went wrong we began the assault again, taking the waiting room with very little problems.

The two groups then entered the Squire's room and began clearing it of the waiting frogs. Once cleared we began pulling frogs from down the hallways. Both groups were in the middle of a pretty serious firefight when another Troll came lumbering past. This one was being chased my Fungus Drone's, a Shin Shaman and his minions. HoB stood it's ground. We all attacked the Shin Shaman. (conned red to the 21st level cleric) The enchanters were low on mana and the other 6 enemies were getting harder and harder to control. The Shaman was standing tough to the combat. It was time for HoB to flee yet again. And again, HoB was cut down by the frogs. More than half of the group was slaughtered as a controlled retreat became impossible.

Frustrated, bleeding and tired, we reassembled again. We sent Thana in to retrieve the corpses of the fallen HoB members. Those that died in the Squire room were given invisibility to retrieve their corpses. We gathered our belongings and reassembled for one more attempt. Fully armored and fully prepared.

Hob cleared the waiting room with ease. HoB cleared the squire room with ease. At this point several members had to leave for the night so we reformed as a group of 7. We began pulling frogs from deeper in guk and somewhere, somehow we were yet again knee deep in enemies. We were swarmed. It was getting dangerously out of hand. In the confusion Faan was cut down again. HoB struggled on. This time we would not surrender.

We were down to our last frog and everyone was breathing a sigh of relief when Faan cam charging back into the room trailing 6 more frogs. A quick retreat was called for and, yet again, several members were cut down on our way out the door.

At this point it was late. Our armor was dented and badly stained. Our magic wielders fingers burned. We decided to call it a night.

Profits from the evening have yet to be calculated. It is expected they will not be extremely large. On the whole members were lucky to have broken even on experience. Many ended up losing experience for the evening.

It was a frustrating and difficult evening for the group. Next week, with new tactics, a new dungeon and a new approach, we hope for a more enjoyable evening.
