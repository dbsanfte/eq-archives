ERUDINE ISLAND WISPS
March 21, 2000


RAIDING PARTY
Faan - cleric, Phoole - bard, Raaj - monk, Ziff - enchanter


This week was met with mid level of success. 4 party members stormed the island one more time and came away with a small profit and a minor amount of experience. It seems the island is no longer a big challenge. (Fibben who was supposed to show never appeared. We suspect he was drunk and passed out on the docks from fish wine again.)

So, it was decided that we should move to new hunting grounds and Raaj suggested we start heading for Freeport so we can participate in the Saturday auctions.

With that in mind, Ziff did some scouting and discovered a nasty patch of bandits in West Karanas. Seems there are tons of the evil things and it would be good to rid the area of them. Additionally, it is on our way to Freeport. The plan is to move towards Freeport, hunting as we go. The bandits were seen to be in groups of 2-8 and levels 8-12 with on occasionally high level one, around 15-18 thrown in. This seems to be a perfect challenge for the House of Bastards. However, it will probably mean certain death without a full party. It even comes with the bonus of an occasional wisp and an often spotted lion or lioness.
