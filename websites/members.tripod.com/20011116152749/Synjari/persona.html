Hall of the White Tigress

Hello there. Well you wanted to know who was the woman behind Misery Deireadh an Tuath. Here she is.. I introduce myself to you as Monica Nicole Yrrobali. I was born in El Paso, TX, on August 22, 1977. If you are too lazy to do the math, I'll do it for you, I'm 22. I suppose that makes me a Leo, eh? A while back, my friend Chastity's mom helped me discover who my animal totem spirits were. All of them are important in different areas of your life and they are associated with different directions and such. Some of those are: north, south, east, west, above, below and within. There are two others that walk beside you. Those two spirits signify a mother and father and they are the animals that you relate to and feel closest to. Mine turned out to be the White Tigress and the North American Wolf. Basically this explains the reason for these two pictures within this page. I want to thank these spirits for guiding me, I see them as guardian angels. Call me weird but I feel God's angels can take any form. My guardian angels just happen to be in the form of two majestic animals. My life hasn't exactly been what you would call eventful. *grin* I was raised in this same smallish city which lies on the border of the U.S. and Mexico. A Geography lesson for you all, El Paso lies at the westernmost part of the state of Texas. Right on the Rio Grande river and across the ways from Ciudad Juarez, Chihuahua Mexico. In plain english we are the sister city to Juarez, Mexico.

I graduated from Eastwood High School in 1995. (which at that time *was* the best high school in the city *grin*) I was in the Trooper Marching Band for all four years and my senior year I was the Flag Guard Commander. (All that hard work sorta paid off) I was also in Choir and Latin Club. These past few years have kept me quite busy as far as traveling goes. I was back and forth to San Diego, I spent the summer in Rochester, N.Y. and spent part of fall/winter in Detroit. I was also attending El Paso Community College where I was studying for a major in God-knows-what. I have, as of last year, gotten engaged and since then moved to New Jersey. And right now, I am in need of a job so iffen ya gots one in the Woodbridge area.. GIMME! Also I am QUITE addicted to a wee game called EverQuest. Not for the game itself, but for the dear friends I have managed to make on there. Love you all!

To my family and friends, I love you dearly. Alrighty, enough of the mushy stuff. I will add more to this section of my little tower as I see fit. Ciao!
