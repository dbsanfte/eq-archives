Tower of Haetyr T'nak

Teardrop's Room


Through the doorway, you step into a different world than what you ever imagined. Trees surround you as a misty dew kisses your face. The ground is slightly damp and moss covers the trees and stones alike. A faint giggling is heard throught this enchanted wood. Suddenly, a beautiful fae floats to your cheek and kisses it lightly. You have entered the path to a well-hidden faerie circle. Teardrop uses this magical path to travel easily between her world and the Tower.

Beside the door, a beautiful bed of white satin pillows sits on a floor of marble. Other than that, the faerie woods spread out as far as the eye can see. Looking closer to the door, you notice that there is nothing beside it or around it and you realize it's a portal to the Tower.

There is much to see and all the time in the world to see it, for in the world of the Fae, time does not exist. If the Fae allow it, you may stay as long as you like and return to the Tower to find that no time has passed and if they feel mischievious, a few moments in here will be years passed by when you re-enter the Tower.

A path runs directly east, and ends up winding its way to a secret wishing well. The magic of the Fae is laced throughout the entire well. A soft faint glow, comes from this well but is masked by the mist. From the well, all sorts of paths can be seen. Even through the mist, there is one path that is wide and seems to pull your soul to it...
