PERMAFROST
Aug. 1, 2000

RAIDING PARTY
Awzz, Faan - cleric (19), Purgatori - ranger (17), Wildride - paladin (19), Ziff - enchanter (20)


Tuesday night, a preliminary and impromptu raid was held. Five brave and foolish HoB members assembled in the barren cold of Everfrost peaks and struck out across the plains in search of the caves of Permafrost Keep. Ziff could be heard from mountain to plain shouting curses and threats at the Goblin hoards. After some time we arrived on the home of the vile ice Goblins and stood at the mouth of their caves. Purgatori, Awzz, Faan, Wildride and Ziff decided to exact a small revenge, to make a scouting run.

We began moving into the caves, shouting additional threats and warnings to the Goblin's. We made no effort to be stealthy, we intended to slaughter them and they should hear our names before they die. Those that survived should know to pass on our name. We even had a little assistance in threats to the Goblins from a large group of adventurers deep in the keep, assembling for an assault upon the dragon Vox.

As we moved into the caverns we made fast and clean work of our enemies. Goblins fell fast. Their screams and cries echoed down the icy corridors. Wildride would assault a room and run away. The Goblins would foolishly begin pursuit and quickly find death. We would move up to the next room and continue the process. Soon we had cleared the room in which flew their banner. We had now entered the keep proper.

We veered left and readied ourselves to begin an assault on their sanctuary. Wildride ran down the hall, spotted a goblin priest and stuck and arrow in it's side. He returned, Priest, Acolyte and several diviners in tow. A battle ensued that nearly cost Wildride his life. We found it difficult to organize in the narrow hallway and as a result, the fight was dangerous and slow. But, in the end every Goblin lay in a heap on the frozen ground. All HoB members were alive, if a bit harried and bruised and the sanctuary was cleared. We moved in and took refuge for a few minutes, preparing to assault the temple itself and see if we can get the head of the preacher on a pike one day early.

After a bit of rest we stormed the temple, sending many goblins to an early death. In minutes the preachers stage was cleared and all the was left was smoking corpses. The preacher was no where to bee seen. He was hiding in fear. HoB waited for about a half an hour. The preacher sent a new batch of minions to assault us but this proved little challenge. These Goblins also died quickly and horribly.

HoB decided we needed to leave. We would return the next night to exact our revenge. We rested up, cast see invisible on the party, cast invisibility on everyone and snuck out the front door, shouting insults to the confused Goblins. We had struck the first crippling blow to them and then left without a trace. Whispers could already be heard amongst the Goblin clan. Whispers of fear and concern and the only word in common tongue was HoB.

We will be back.
