SOLUSEK'S EYE GOBLIN FOREMAN'S ROOM
Aug. 16, 2000


RAIDING PARTY
Blladesinger - ranger (20), Faan - cleric (19), Krug - monk (19), Lorsua - druid (24), Luifang - monk (20), Wildride - paladin (20), Ziff - enchanter (20), Zoritsa, enchanter (21)


Early in the evening Luifang and Ziff met at the mouth to Solusek Eye. They intended to scout the caverns and get a better feeling for the depths of what they were about to undertake.

They entered the caves and began killing off Goblins one by one, progressing slowly down the treacherous stairway. After 30 minutes or more they had only managed to get 100 yards into the enclave. They were already fighting goblins of equal strength. They were certain they were not going to be able to perform a scouting mission this night. It would be too dangerous. so, they took a stand on a small ledge and proceeded to kill the Goblin menace one at a time. Soon they were joined by Blladesinger and they fought a valiant and subtle war near the entrance to Solusek Eye.

As they fought on a cry was soon heard. A goblin shout of war. Ziff turned and on the platform were not 1 but 4 Goblins bearing claw and fang. A hasty retreat was attempted. In the efforts to escape, Ziff was swarmed by the Goblins and killed.

Soon, the entire HoB group was collecting outside the caves and so the teams were set up for the coming raid. Luifang, Faan and Zoritsa would gather as one group. Ziff, Wildride, Blladesinger and Krug would gather as another. Lorsua was to oversee the groups and provide emergency healing and buffing. Each member had a special prayer said over them and, primed with the lust for vengeance, they dove into the caves shouting threats and cries of vengeance.

The parties carefully wove their way along the twisting paths. They slaughtered every Goblin in their wake. The heat and oppression was becoming nerve wracking, but HoB held strong and continued upon they way.

After some time HoB had established a camp in the intersection of three hallways. A pitched battle ensued as a large number of Goblins rushed the camp form every direction. HoB had the fight under control when a monk of high and unknown level decided to entertain himself by attacking some of the Goblins. This quickly lead to a deterioration in battle tactics and for a short while HoB was pressed to control the fight. But, bravery and teamwork prevailed and soon the Floor was slick with blood and every HoB member stood breathing, if a little harried.

HoB soon began moving through the final halls and entered the lair of the Kobold Predator. The Predator was not at home, but his Goblin wizards were. HoB fell on the Goblins with a roar and soon slaughtered each of them.

Luifang then proceeded to extract the goblins from the Foreman's room. The Foreman was hiding and not to be found. But his minions were chopped down in brutal fashion and so HoB moved into his work area and established a encampment.

For the next 2 1/2 hours Luifang charged through the hallways taunting Goblin masses and bringing them back to meet their doom. She demonstrated skill closer to art, carefully extracting Goblins of 18 - 26 level and bringing them in virtually always alone. The scene was repeated endlessly, the Goblin would pursue her to the door. she would fling it open and charge in, wounded and tired. The Goblin would charge in thinking Luifang was surely seeking water from the Foreman's well. He would scream in terror as the might of a well organized HoB force fell upon him, cutting him to shreds.

As the night grew deep, the bodies were piled high in the room. The Foreman's minions continued to hairy his work area, but he never showed. Teamwork and artful skill kept HoB members from any significant danger, even though we were fighting Goblins exceeding our levels by sometimes 6 or more. HoB could be heard shouting obscenities and threats to the Goblin hoard. HoB would be victorious.

Then a horrible screeching noise could be heard. The noise sent chills through the spines of every member. Luifang charged out to see what the noise was and could soon be heard yelling, "The Predator!". Panting and wounded she burst through the door, pursued by the Kobold predator and several Goblins. HoB, fell upon them with brutal efficiency, demonstrating why they were an elite force to be feared by the Goblins. In moments, the predator and his Goblin friends were no more than bleeding smoldering husks. Ziff bent to inspect the Predator and found he was wearing boots made from the hide of his species. These boots were discovered to be wearable by many HoB members and after a bit of discussion it was decided to award them to Zoritsa for her outstanding bravery and long dedication to the band of vengeance seekers.

After a bit of celebration Luifang charged back out into the Goblin caves and began harrying the foe again.

Soon a loud boom could be heard and the walls shook with the noise. Luifang was laying gasping in hall, smoke rising from her tunic. A Goblin Wizard stood over her wounded body casting incantations. Luifang, realizing her attempts to fool it by faking her death were not working leaped to her feat and charged into the room. Faan immediately began attempting to heal her wounds but the Wizard was powerful, being of 26 levels, and Faan could not match it's spells. HoB attempted to distract the wizard and to kill it as fast as possible, but in it's rage it exacted revenge upon Luifang was killed.

HoB mourned her loss and, knowing she would attempt to return soon, sent Krug out to lead the Goblin hoards to their death.

After a few small battles Luifang returned to us and HoB was relieved that she was OK.

For the reminder of the night HoB held the Foreman's work area and killed Goblin after Goblin. The floor was greased with their blood and their bodies were thrown down the well in the hopes of spoiling their water system. But, the Foreman failed to show. HoB had scared him away and he remained cowering somewhere deeper in the Goblin caves.

So, the time had come for HoB to perform a controlled exit. As a team we worked our way through the halls, fighting and killing the Goblins in our path. Ziff wrote Kiban's name in blood across the walls as they left. The fighting was going well when a particularly young Goblin entered the Fray, and seconds after falling under the brutal assault of HoB, turned and fled. All this happened so fast, Blladesinger could not control him. He ran screaming in Goblin down the halls and masses of Goblins came to his aid.

HoB stood it's ground for 30 seconds. HoB fought valiantly and attempted to control the situation. After a short time, it was admitted that there were too many. HoB was forced to run. Ziff made a last ditch effort to control as many of the Goblins as possible and HoB charged down the hallway, chased by a half dozen angry Goblins. Ziff was also the last of the members to run and as he ran through the halls they clawed and stabbed at his back. He was growing week and as he turned a corner, he slipped and fell into the lava pits below. In a panic he attempted to gate out of the lava, but in he already weakened state, he could not cast the spell before he was consumed by the burning rock.

The rest of HoB survived the flight and as they rejuvenated themselves and said goodnight, Ziff reentered the caves with his friends Thana and Kiah to retrieve his corpse from the fire pits. With the help of Thana he was able to do so and was soon restored to full health.

Satisfied with a night of phenomenal teamwork HoB made camp. They vowed to return the next week and slay the Foreman. They vowed to root him from the depths of the eye. They renewed their vows to avenge Kiban's death.

They would be back.
