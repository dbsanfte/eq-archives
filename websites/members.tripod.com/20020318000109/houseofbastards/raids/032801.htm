Mar. 28, 2001


RAIDING PARTIES
Faan - cleric (29), Galadrial - druid (34), Garall - paladin (29), Guadzilla - shaman (30), Lanorolen - bard (27), Luifang - monk (28), Nekko - shadowknight (32), Shumirek - bard (27), Tasslehof - druid (30), Tinea - necromancer (26), Wildride - paladin (30), Ziff - enchanter (29)

OTHERS: Thana - necromancer (54), Kodman - paladin (35)

RECOUNT
by Ziff

After sitting for more than an hour on the shoreline being forced to listen to the viscous and arrogant comments of a most vile necromancer, I was glad to see that we were finally off to find Luifang. We were understandably a bit disorganized and confused at first. Thana supplied no information as to where we were going, what we should do or how we should go about it. In a short while we arrived at the entryway to Dalnir.

We were told to "cast magic's upon ourselves as she was" and so we attempted to do this. But, we made a fatal error in this regard. We failed to notice she had cast a spell to make her levitate and so we, began to follow her, invisible, cautious and very sneaky.

She quickly led us into the depths of Delnir and then proceeded to jump down a hole telling us to "follow". Blindly, we did just that. We had forgotten that Thana was the epitome of evil. More than 1/2 of the group that gathered there fell to their death down that hole. I few were cut up by the beasts below and a remaining few were killed by the beasts awaiting above.

The result was an utter loss of life. Three members survived. Two by evacuating to East Karanas and one by having levitate cast upon him and surviving the beasts below. The rest lay in a heap on the floor.

So, HoB, angry and frustrated by the vile trick, regathered. This took significant time and, since several members left before we were fully rejoined, our friends Galadriel and Kodman joined us in their place.

Once we had regathered we proceeded even deeper into the crypt of Dalnir. Thana took us into a deep room that was clearly a morgue of some sort and there told us a tale.

She told us that Luifang had been partially converted by the Kly. She told us that in order to save Luifang we must collect these items:

burnai legion gi
choker of madj
earring of essence
goblin parrying dagger
4 leather whips

Faan was to use them performing some sort of exorcism. She did not explain how they were to be used, but instead, told that first "we must save ourselves" and vanished.

With no idea how to get out of this place we were now alone.

And then Luifang appeared. But Luifang was clearly not herself. She shouted words of hatred, attacked us and then stared blankly for long periods. Then she would act as if nothing happened and great us all with great enthusiasm.

Wildride began leading us on adventure to escape this place. As we worked out way out, killing Kly enemies as we went, Luifang would change from the normal friend we have come to know to a stranger who professed love of the Kly, and then back again. She was clearly distraught and confused. Eventually in our wanderings and fighting we came upon a room where they performed the evil experiments and caught sight of the head of the Kly himself, the overseer. Knowing that fighting him meant certain death we cast invisibility upon ourselves and ran past him to the magical escape pad.

I have begun searching through Kunark for how the items Thana listed will help us, but to date I have no knowledge. I have not heard from Thana and frankly do not wish to ever see her again. Her trickery nearly cost HoB it's collective existence. Wildride has agreed to begin searching Antoonica and it's resources. When we have more information, we will plan the next attack.
