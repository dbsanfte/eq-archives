The Tower of Haetyr T'nak

You stand before two large, silvery gates that extend up at least 20 feet high. They slowly open and a slender woman appears before you dressed in a dark blue gown and a matching cloak. She extends her hand out to you and smiles warmly.

"Welcome to the Tower, weary traveler. My name is Misery Deireadh an Tuath and I am Grand High Council of the Order of Haetyr T'nak. Please come in and rest a bit. The Haetyr are very well known for their hospitality.. you can say it is quite legendary," says Misery as she motions you to follow her.

You have been officially invited within the Tower and may enter through the enchanted gateway.
