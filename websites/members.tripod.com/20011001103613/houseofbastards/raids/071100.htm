NAJENA
July 11, 2000


RAIDING PARTY
Blladesinger - ranger, Faan - cleric (17), Lorsua - druid (20), Luifang - monk, Wildride - paladin (17), Ziff - enchanter (18), Zoritsa - enchanter


A much sparser group met the minions of Najena this week. We were expecting a crowd of 10 or more. Due to some personal, computer and unknown problems, we were a group of 7 instead. We gathered as 6 to start with, at the entrance to the catacombs. Several members from OoV met up with us to talk and observe. Seems HoB has become a bit of a spectator sport!

So, we gathered our resources and began carving our way into the caverns. As we cleared room after room we began forming some new strategies. First, tonight we would not chase runners. Second, we would attempt to move through Najena.

We delved deeper into Najena and compliments were being offered regularly from the OoV crowd. We were preparing to pull Bonecracker from his stinking pit when a challenge was offered, in secret. The challenge was, extract Bonecracker from his home, alone, leaving his necromancer friends behind. Luifang was up to the challenge as she had done this several times before, but also wanted to try something new. So, at her request we moved down into the corridors of Najena and waited outside Bonecrackers home for him to appear.

Luifang snuck into his dwelling and he and his guests quickly turned on her, rooting her to the floor and abusing her with disease. She laid down and played dead, but they did not believe. After several attempts she finally succeeded and lay bleeding and sickened on the stone. Again she rose to draw out Bonecracker and again she was rooted and assaulted. She dropped to the floor and waited. Feeling week and slow she tried once more, but this time the plan failed entirely. As she backed into the hall the necromancers spotted the rest of HoB as it lay in wait. The jig was up and a pitched battle ensued.

In an open room HoB would have stood a chance. In the twisted narrow intersection and the steep staircase HoB was in dire trouble. Crowd control of the 7 creatures quickly became impossible as they could not all be seen for area effect spells to take effect. The warriors were focused on brining down Bonecracker, but the necromancers and their pets were interfering. Faan could not heal fast enough and, once he fell under attack, was quickly slaughtered.

Spotting Faan's corpse, Luifang gave the cry of "Run!" and HoB began fleeing for their lives. Only half of them made it out alive.
