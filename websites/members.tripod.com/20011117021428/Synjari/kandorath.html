You step through the doorway and into a beautiful forest covered in a blanket of snow. The sounds of nature surround you and a sense of peace and tranquility falls over the entire place. In the distance, wolf-brothers call out to you to join in the hunt. But it is not you who they seek. It is Kandorath D'Archanus, the lord of this forest realm and "He who Runs with the Wolf-Brothers and Hunts with the Striped-ones." Most wolves refer to him as Tigerwolf. This is a man whose soul is bared to few and is an enigma to most. Because his deep understanding of nature's ways and especially of the wolves and tigers.. He is linked closely to Misery, who grew up in the forest of elves and grew to live in the Way of the Forest. Kandorath's passion is to run free with the Wolf-Brothers as well as joining in the occassional hunt with the tigers. The forest is full of life and suprisingly enough, the cold doesnt bother you too much. Several paths lead from here.. One heads far west to an enormous colony of warriors and is the widest path. The others lead to various places both deeper in the forest and out of the forest. North of you, there seems to be a cave nearby. The doorway that led from the Tower is behind you, in the trunk of an enormous tree.
