Vampyre's Room

You step from a corridor of light into a dimly-lit room. The room has a certain air about it, dangerous yet inviting at the same time. Deep, red satin runs through the room, from chair cushions to the drapes. Except for the few candles and an occasional torch, the room is quite dark. Looking around, a light can be seen to the far end of the room. A box made of an unknown substance fills that corner with light. Walking toward the box, pictures on the side of this box can be seen. It appears to be connected up to a smaller box, somewhat, as well as several other things, by some sort of cord. This room leads to the lair of Vampyre. There is a window here and possibly, a way of seeing where this place lies.

Moving away from the window, a doorway can be seen to another end of the room. This door leads to the rest of Vampyre's Lair.
