You have entered a large, drafty room, surrounded by dim lighting from an unseen source. The rest of the room is pitch black, with one exception, there are portals all around you, above and below. You have entered a black void, the very heart of the Haetyr's ability to Travel. This is the Corridor of the Waygates, in it.. anything is possible. You can travel to any world with a single step into a portal. New waygates are discovered frequently, so one may want to check back now and again to see what treasures may be discovered within other realms.
