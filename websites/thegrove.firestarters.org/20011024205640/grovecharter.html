We, The Grove, are dedicated to a quest of knowledge and learning

all about our world of Norrath.

We are not biased towards any one faction, race, class, or religion.

 

We, The Grove, will give our own life in defense of

another Grover, and if another guild or faction seeks to abuse one of

our members,

the whole of the guild will rise up in defense against the aggressor.

 

These words, as written, shall be our charter, and all who come

willingly unto The Grove shall treat them as Law.
