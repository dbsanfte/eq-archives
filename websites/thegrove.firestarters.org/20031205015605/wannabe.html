So, You Wanna Be a Grover eh?

You sure? Do you know what it means to be a Grover. It means you must be Trustworthy, Loyal, Helpful, Friendly, Courteous, Kind,   ...   no wait!   ...   Thats a boyscout!    Silly me!    But hey! It works for us too!    The Grove is not a 'I', 'Me', 'Mine' group of people.    Actually, pretty much the opposite is true.    We tend to help others more often then we help ourselves.    We don't item farm.    We tend to give hand-me down equipment away to other guildmates or newbies rather then sell them back for cash.    We spend whole evenings acting as guardian angles for lower levels.    But most of all... we have fun doing it!

Still want to be a Grover?

If you answered yes then here is what you need to do:

1 You must have a sponsor. A Sponsor is a current member of The Grove in good standing who is willing to do some work on your behalf to help you gain acceptance into the guild.

2 Your Sponsor will announce you on a special Forum we have (Called: 'Member Applications'). This will normally be how most Grovers first hear about you. The announcement should contain some information about you (not personal) that will give the grovers a clue as to what you are like. (Examples: SoAndSo is a RL friend of mine. Has level XX char on Prod Server called Xxxxx. Has played other RPGs such as Xxx and Yyyy. etc.)

3 Your sponsor will let you know that they have posted your name in the Membeship Applications section. Once this has been done, the Grove will know who you are, and will be able meet you from a 'prospective member' point of view.

4 At this point it is up to you. You should try to get to know guild members via Grouping, Questing, Socializing, Posting on the public parts of The Groves forum, etc. You should seek out Grovers (they will NOT seek you out) to try and group with (within level range of course). Keep in mind that even if not grouped you will be watched. your actions during this week are very important. It is recommended that you simply be yourself. it's how The Grove got created anyway!

5 After some time, as members have gotten to know you, the guild will vote (via /GuildSay). A Minimum of 7 people are required in guildsay for vote. You must receive two-thirds of the votes in your favor to be accepted

6a If approved you will be informed by either your sponsor or a guild officer. If an officer informs you then you'll be invited on the spot. If the sponsor informs you, then you need to give a /tell to an officer when you are in the same zone as them.

6b If Declined, You will be notified by your sponsor and hopefully given a reason as to why you were not accepted. It might simply mean that not enough grovers know you well enough to feel comfortable voting positive. Any applicant can re-apply again after 1 month since last vote. Take this time to get to know us. It's your best way in!
