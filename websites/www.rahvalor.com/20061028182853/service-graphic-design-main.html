The many faces of graphic design

When you hear the words "graphic design" you might think of a web site or an animated ad banner. Either way, you get pictures in your head, some more memorable than others. With our graphical design services we aim to provide lasting, quality images that live in the minds of your customers. Design and animation have so many applications that it's only a matter of how creative you want to get, and how much your CFO put in the marketing budget this year.

Electronic Designs and Animations

The applications of electronic designs are many and varied. Here are a few possibilities:

  • Web site graphics including Flash elements
  • Ad banner advertising or HTML e-mail advertising
  • Logo animations for web or CD-ROM
  • Interactive training or educational program interface design
  • Interactive sales or speaker support presentation interface design
  • Digital promotional "give-away" pieces or trade show presentations
  • Electronic product portfolios

Whatever your design requirements, we will be happy to discuss them with you. All of our designs are 100% unique and custom-tailored for your organization - no boilerplate solutions, no standard templates. Your company is unique - the marketing materials that represent it should be also.

Graphics do not stand alone

Graphics add value to any marketing campaign. Pictures live in our memories and create impressions that can be vital to a company's branding program. Would a web site be as friendly if it were pure blocks of uninterrupted text? Would your employees really pay attention to your fantastic training program if it did not capture their aesthetic eye? Chances are: no.

The problem arises when graphics overwhelm the message. You've probably been to a web site that looks great but does little to explain its purpose. Or seen an ad that is so visually stimulating that your eye is drawn from one image to the next until you're too dizzy to read the offer. Graphics are meant to enhance, not to obscure.

When we design, everything from your home page to your CD-based identity piece, we do so with the primary focus on getting your message across. We design graphics around your goals - not the other way around.

The bottom line: why pay more when you can buy a template for a few dollars?

Any design project is an investment in your business and the future of your success. Sometimes an inexpensive templated solution will fit your needs but other times, you may need something tailored to meet specific design and customer requirements. Should the latter be true, we can help define your goals and develop a product that fits your budget.

Contact Us to explore the options for graphics that can be used as part of your marketing strategy, or for new customers or existing customers with a new project, take advantage of a free initial consultation and no-obligation project planning.
