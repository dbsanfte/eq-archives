web site and e-mail hosting

Exclusively for Rahvalor Interactive customers

When we design and develop your web site, we also offer you the opportunity to host your site with us. We are not a "hosting company" with hundreds or thousands of sites to manage, nor do we accept "off the street" requests for hosting. Our hosting services are an added value that we offer only to our customers so that we can focus on customized development and provide "one-stop-shopping" for all of your organization's web needs.

Our servers reside at secure, top-level, national hosting facilities so that you don't have to worry about down-time or technology problems. Nor will you have to worry about tech support or dealing with a third party. We handle all of your technology issues directly, so that if you ever do experience a problem, simply call or e-mail us and we'll address the issue immediately.

If you're interested in our site and e-mail hosting services, or if you'd like to find out about these services as part of an upcoming development project, contact us for more information about the marketing-focused hosting packages we offer.

Hosting packages and options

We can provide you with a number of services including:

  • Basic Windows-based hosting for small to medium sized sites
  • Advanced Windows-based hosting for large or eCommerce sites with database requirements
  • Dedicated hosting for sites with specialized or intensive bandwidth needs
  • Security certificate purchase and/or installation and hosting
  • Web-based e-mail hosting including forwarders, aliases and full client-side administration, plus spam filtering and virus protection
  • Standard or advanced web site traffic logs and statistics
  • Tech support for web site or e-mail questions related to technology issues
  • Off-site web data and e-mail backups
  • Search-engine-friendly redirects

Other web services include:

  • Domain name registration, renewal or transfer
  • Web site transfer from an existing ISP to our hosting facility

The bottom line: hosting and e-mail fees

Depending on your web site and e-mail needs, we have a number of packages that may suit your business. As a Rahvalor Interactive customer, please Contact Us for a hosting recommendation and price list.
