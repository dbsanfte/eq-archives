What is "Rahvalor"?

If we had a nickel for each time we were asked that question...

Pronounced {ra vuh lore}, we admit the name has raised a few eyebrows, caused numerous spelling mishaps and occasionally made us wish we had called ourselves "Jane". But our company by any other name would certainly not be as sweet. "Rahvalor" is important to us and as all good names, it has its own history.

The story begins with our company president and founder, who was - and still is - a tech-loving aficionado, also known as a nerd. Since his early days as a tot he had a penchant for gadgets, gizmos, comic books, alternative film and nerd games. Back in those days, when a can opener was considered high tech and online gaming was unheard of (hey, remember the “Adventure” game that steered you through a vivid imaginary world through use of a DOS prompt and text commands?) our founder spent many a summer (and fall and winter and spring) afternoon playing Dungeons and Dragons with the rest of his nerd friends.

During one Dungeons and Dragons campaign, his sister developed a character for him named “Rahvalor”. Legend has it that she decided he needed a valorous character, a “Rah! Rah! Go get ‘em!” type of character. Hence… “Rah” and “Valor”. As nerds are wont to do, they got very excited by their clever thinking and thirty-odd years later the name is still alive and well, representing a not-so-nerdy marketing company.

We must also admit that the nerdiness paid off. Rahvalor Interactive has been gaining and maintaining new clients for over ten years. Tech savvy, dedicated to customer service and committed to superior products and services, Rahvalor Interactive is proud of its humble – and unique – origins.
