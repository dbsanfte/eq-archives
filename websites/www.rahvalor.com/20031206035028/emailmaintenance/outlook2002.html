Step 1:

From the Outlook 2002/XP Tools menu select E-Mail Accounts.

Select View or change existing e-mail accounts then click Next.
Step 2:

Select the mail account you want to modify and click the Change button.
Step 3:

Type mail.rahvalor.com in both the Incoming and Outgoing boxes.

Confirm all your setting and make sure that the User Name box has your full e-mail address in all lower case.

When your settings are confirmed, click on the More Settings button.
Step 4:

Confirm your settnigs, then click the Outgoing Server tab on the top.
Step 5:

Check the My outgoing server... box.

If you do not check this box, you will not be able to send mail.You will receive a 550 Mail Relay Not Allowed error.
