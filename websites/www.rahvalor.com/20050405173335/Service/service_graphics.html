The many faces of graphic design

When you hear the words “graphic design” you might think of a web site, or a full color tri-fold brochure. Either way, you get pictures in your head, some more memorable than others. With our graphical design services we aim to provide lasting, quality images that live in the minds of your customers. Design and animation have so many applications that it’s only a matter of how creative you want to get, and how much your CFO put in the marketing budget this year.

Logos, letterheads and other static designs

If we’re repeating ourselves… good. Your logo, letterhead, newsletter header, business card… in fact any printed or digital representations of your company… should be fully integrated with each other. As a marketing company, we can’t repeat it enough. And therein lies the key to a successful marketing campaign: repetition. No matter what the design of your marketing pieces, they should retain a similar look and feel so that one single image of your organization is branded into the memories of your customers.

At Rahvalor Interactive our designers can work with your existing marketing materials or create new ones to develop an image for your company. Through a process of discovery, we work to understand your market, your preferences and your message to create a unified look for your organization.

How many choices do you get?

At Rahvalor Interactive we don’t believe in a rigid take-it-or-leave-it methodology. We will present you with a number of designs based on your preferences and your budget and once you choose the design you would like to continue with, there is still room for revisions. Since all of our projects are custom, we establish goals, cost and scope based on your individual needs.

Graphics do not stand alone

Graphics add value to any marketing campaign. Pictures live in our memories and create impressions that can be vital to a company’s branding program. Would a web site be as friendly if it were pure blocks of uninterrupted text? Would a brochure get as much attention if it didn’t use those eye-catching reds and blues? Would your employees really pay attention to your fantastic training program if it did not capture their aesthetic eye? Chances are: no.

The problem arises when graphics overwhelm the message. You’ve probably been to a web site that looks great but does little to explain its purpose. Or seen an ad that is so visually stimulating that your eye is drawn from one image to the next until you’re too dizzy to read the offer. Graphics are meant to enhance, not to obscure.

When we design, everything from your home page to your CD-ROM identity presentation, we do so with the primary focus on getting your message across. We design graphics around your goals – not the other way around.
