Why do you need a Search Engine marketing program?

Did you know that studies have shown that over 75% of visitors to your site will reach it through a search engine? With a statistic like that, who can afford not to have a search engine marketing program?

No matter how terrific your site is, if nobody can find it reasonably quickly on a search engine, you are losing a major potential customer base.

You need to spend time, and sometimes money, to make sure your site can be found. It’s not enough to put it out there on the web where every day the big engines are bragging about the millions of new documents they’ve added to their indices.

At Rahvalor Interactive, we build web sites to be search engine friendly from the get-go. From good code to good structure, we make sure that your web site works for visitors - and search engines. jargon alert!

> deciphering technobable

Search Engine Optimization: "Optimization" refers to the process of making changes to a web site in order to improve its rank in search listings.

The goal of optimization is to make a site "search-engine-friendly", which simply means creating a "good site" that also follows guidelines set forth by the search industry.
