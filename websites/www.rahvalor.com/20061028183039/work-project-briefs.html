our work / project briefs

Here you will find a sampling of some of our other projects and a brief description of each. This assortment spans the past decade and a variety of industries with unique marketing and communications needs. For more detailed projects, please visit our case studies.

Lucent Technologies.

Over the years we have produced a number of self-running and speaker support presentations and animations for Lucent Technologies. Below is a sampling of some of these projects. Top left: A still-shot of an animation depicting how a communications system can break down during an intrusion event. Top right: A still-shot of an introductory animation for a security presentation. Bottom left: A still-shot of an introductory animation for a training program. Bottom right: A still-shot of a historical background presentation used by Lucent during training sessions and trade shows.

Trump Casinos

We developed various 3D backgrounds and animated characters that were used to enhance a series of human resource training programs for Trump Casinos. The training programs consisted of movie spoofs strung together throughout the progam. The first frame (pictured left) shows an animatic that we developed to spoof "Star Wars", in which a spaceship lands in a river. The second frame (pictured right), also a "Star Wars" spoof, shows the character "DooDa" engaged in a dialog with a live actor.

New Jersey Devils

We were asked to develop a series of marketing materials for a cross promotion between the NHL's New Jersey Devils and a local Internet Service Provider. The first image (pictured left) shows one frame of an animation that was played on the JumbotronT at selected NJ Devils games. The second image (pictured right) is a still-shot from the CD-ROM that we produced containing software distributed by the ISP.

The College of Mount Saint Vincent, New York City

As part of a student recruitment program, we developed several kiosks for the College of Mount Saint Vincent that contained both self running and interactive presentations. The kiosks were used by the Admissions Department during Open House events to showcase the college's benefits and programs. Prospective students could view program offerings, student testimonials, admission requirements and take a photo tour of the campus. While idle, the kiosk cycled through a set of predetermined scenes.

Multi Image Services

"Otto" is a digital puppet who enhances trade shows by interacting with live talent. Otto has been used by national and international companies including public relations firm Burson-Marsteller. We designed the character and developed the programming required to control the puppet by a live performer. The puppet is flexible and customizable. He has a large selection of clothing and backgrounds, and custom elements can be added on the fly as needed.

RSIG

Pictured below are four frames from a video introduction produced in conjunction with a logo (lower left) and collateral print materials (lower right) including folders, letterhead, business cards and CD labels.
