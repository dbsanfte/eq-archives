why choose us?

Last we checked, there were 15,100,000 listings in Google and 39,900,000 listings in Yahoo! for the term “marketing company”. With all these options, why would you choose us? Good question, but first we have a few of our own.

Do you share our values?

At Rahvalor Interactive we value relationships. We believe in building a shared vision and fostering cooperation between our team and yours. Our goal is not to provide you with a single solution and then drop from sight, but rather to develop an ongoing relationship based on mutual respect and support. We do not work in a void. We believe that you are the expert in your area, as we are in ours, and that we can benefit from shared knowledge and insights. We also believe that your relationships with your customers are just as important, and our goal is to provide you with the tools to develop, enhance and manage those relationships.

We also value service. At Rahvalor Interactive we answer our e-mails and phone messages. We chat with you online and ask about your kids and your dog. We even work past five. We aim not only to give you the highest quality product, but superior customer service. Why? See value number one: because our relationship with you is important.

Do you want personalized service?

We are not a multimillion-dollar corporation with dozens of marketing executives at our disposal. We are a modest team of like-minded professionals with similar goals and vision. Everyone on our team knows what everyone else is doing and we know each other by name. Our size allows us to interact with each other and with you on a personal, individual level.

The services we provide are custom tailored specifically to meet your organization’s needs. There are no cookie-cutter solutions, no boilerplate products. Everything we develop is built after a process of exploration and discovery so that we can learn about your organization and formulate a plan that will help you meet your goals. Your newsletter will not look like a dozen others. Your web site is not developed based on a handful of prepackaged templates. We believe that your business is unique and your marketing should reflect that.

And for those who are tired of pressing “one” for sales and “two” for tech support, there is no phone queue here for you to get lost in.

Are you looking for a company you can trust?

Naturally, trust is something we will earn as our relationship with you develops, but before that happens, it would be nice to have a reasonable expectation that we will deliver what we promise. For that, we will allow our work and our customers to speak for themselves. Take a look at our portfolio and our case studies to see how we have been serving our customers for over a decade and to see how we have continued to help them grow over the years. The best confirmation of our merit is witnessed in the number of our customers who have remained loyal and satisfied over the years.

Do you want to save money?

Ok, that was a trick question. If you’re looking for a $300 web site, or, after learning about us, still think your nephew in college can do just as good a job as we can, then we invite you to explore other options. Our services are highly customized and specialized. We have the experience and the expertise necessary to design, develop and implement either a part or whole marketing strategy. We invest a great deal of time, skill and human resources in custom-tailoring a solution for your organization’s needs.

We also understand budget. We will never try to oversell or advise you to undertake a strategy for the sole purpose of advancing our bottom line. In fact, we don’t have a problem telling you “no” if we simply don’t feel that a certain solution will benefit you. We would love to build your web site – but if we don’t believe it has value, we will tell you so. Ultimately the decision is yours, but we will always advise you in your best interests.

When you begin a project with us, we spend time outlining goals, expectations and costs. When we present you with a proposal, you will know exactly what you are getting for your money. There are no hidden costs, no unexpected fees. Sometimes our clients find, midway through a project, that they would like to add or modify the plan. Should that be your case, we will redefine goals and costs so that you can evaluate and understand exactly what is happening.

Do you know what a “Mouse” is?

We’ll admit, it makes life easier for us if you do – but our true goal is to take the “technology” out of technology and make it as simple for you as making toast in the morning. We make every effort to eliminate jargon, tech-talk and industry lingo from our products and services. We also offer training and support for those times when you may need it.

Do we fit?

Now that you have answered our questions, it’s only fair that we answer one for you. Why, after reading all of this, should you work with us?

The answer, simply, is because we “fit”. If you feel comfortable and confident that our company can mesh with yours in a mutually beneficial way, then give us a call, drop us an e-mail, and we will gladly answer questions, listen to problems and discuss solutions. You should work with us because you feel that you can, and because our knowledge and work speak of our competency. You should work with us because as you read this, you found yourself nodding your head, felt a connection or a spark of interest and wondered if perhaps Rahvalor Interactive is the missing piece of the puzzle that means success for your business.

If you’re confident that we can offer you a positive marketing experience, or if you’re still not convinced and would like to learn more about us, Contact Us and we will be glad to answer questions and discuss options. The Rahvalor Interactive team is here to guide and assist you.

Explore it!
