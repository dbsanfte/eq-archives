CoV Goes Retro
By Reny
We convinced ourselves it would be fun to hit our old stomping ground NToV to pay a visit to the dragon formerly known as Vulak. After a quick clearing of all the trash, err ikatar, vyemm, koi, cekanar, Sevelak, Zlexak, Jorleag, Nevederia and Mirenilla and alot of "remember when..." stories, we started the ring of Vulak. Armed with our super secret strategy (lots of coffee) we downed the former uber dragon collecting Sal'Varae's Robe of Darkness for Yadyin, Camii's Bracer of Vigor for Lyldread, Akkirus' Mask of Warfare for Elendyn and Jaelen's Katana for Treyon
