Ranks
 

Upper Leaders

How to become: This rank is currently taken.

Abilities:
Votes on all topics.
Can initiate rules and ranks.
Can recruit members.
Is targeted at members above 30.

Limitations:
There may only be 2 at a time.

Lower Leaders

How to become: One spot is currently open and will be determined by the current leaders.

Abilities:
Votes on all topics.
Can initiate rules and ranks.
Can recruit members.
Is targeted to members below 30.

Limitations:
There may only be 2 at a time.

Advisor

How to become: This rank is currently taken.

Abilities:
Votes on all topics.
Can initiate rules and ranks.
Can recruit members.
Makes and updates the website.
Is targeted to all members.

Limitations:
This rank will be maintained by me until the guild dies.

Officers

How to become: Must have been in the guild for 2 months, and must be a trusted member.

Abilities:
Can recruit members with a leaders permission.
Votes on all topics.
Enforces rules

Limitations:
There may only be 15 at a time.
