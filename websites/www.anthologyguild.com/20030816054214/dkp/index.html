Public access turned off. (submitted by Eelyen at 02:26pm CDT)

Public viewing access to the dkp scores have been turned off. An account must be created and Zenoflare can upgrade your privs if you are a member.

Most that have already registered to the dkp site should have access, you may need to clear your cache and reboot but you must log in to view the site. Hitting refresh on your browser after logging out should work, did for me. Also click the remember me (cookie) checkbox and make sure you have your internet browser's cookies turned on!

If you have not made an account it is very easy to do so and you will need one to view the dkp site, contact Zeno if you just made an account and need access or have tried doing all of this and still can't access it.
