Are you tired of doing the same old content over again? Do you feel that your guild isn't achieving what you wish they would? Are you tired of having a out of whack loot system? Are you tired of having to fight over level 65 spells? Do you want to see the true challenges Everquest has to offer? Do you want to just be the best you can be at your class?

Now's your chance to step up and see more of the game and see it how you wanted to see it. Fires of Time is accepting applications until July 28th, 2003. We're looking for the best players out there. Players who are motivated to play 5-7 days a week for 4-7 hours a day.

Our applicants start earning DKP from their first raid. Applicants also do pick up loot more often then some think. Melee like to upgrade those weapons? We are getting weapon drops like the Ornate Greatblade, Crow Footed Lance, and others that are starting to rott. Want your 65 spells? We've been getting so many runes we've almost completely filled up every member's level 65 spell list. Hell, out of our cleric brigade, the only thing they really don't have is Yaulp 6.

Do you want to enjoy in the joy and success of being a part of overcoming new encounters? Fires of Time is progressing very well in the Elementals, but we still got more ahead of us. And you can be a part of that now, and not just come in and be a number later.

We are highly looking for players who can log on prior to 5:00pm EST (2:00pm PST). We aren't recruiting a whole lot of people, we have a good array of members that fit alot of our needs. Having a good attitude, don't get discouraged easily, more then willing to die, and play fairly daily. Then Fires of Time might be the guild for you. If you only play a couple days a week and hate dying, you probably should go elsewhere.

We had started the day with the knowing that it was going to be the day we killed him. We had believed we would of killed him the day prior, but after a almost flawless Tallon Zek (ring version) the zone crashed. Joy. But we came back today and put this ring through the paces. On our first Rallos Zek the Warlord engagement, we got him to 2% health. We were very sure he was going to die after that considering the lowest before that had been 42%.

People were hyped and refused to log off discouraged. We got regrouped and restarted this ass of a ring event again. With another near flawless Tallon Zek, scoring a incredible 2 kills on us, followed by a quick death for Vallon. The fake Rallos Zek went down fast and easy. We moved to the pit and recovered our strategy. Then we pulled the gimp and the result is displayed above. It was a very satisfying kill.

While this wasn't the cleanest kill I will admit, I can't say we annihilated him. But it is a kill we're proud off. And you can believe we'll be going after him plenty more.
