LOOT GUIDELINES

Synthalia pulled together the following guidelines for distributing guild loot. Send all comments to Synthalia.

Common Knowledge Law:
Loot Definition: any item of value of 200pp or more (street value of course)

Guild Hunt Definition: more than one group of predominantly AoW *NOTE* one full group of AoW alone, getting to, breaking, and holding a camp does NOT constitute a guild hunt

All NON "Guild Hunt" loot will be determined by members of the group as would a single AoW inside a normal camp... loot dropped on all non-guild hunts is decided like any other non-AoW camp loot and not expected to be given to the guild but always appreciated when it is.

Members during a "Guild Hunt" will have preference on lootover all AoW not present

Should an item not be "needed" (an actual improvement to thier main character) then it will be "up for grabs" by the rest of the guild not present... We don't want items sitting in the bank or sold when they can be used.

If it is not "needed" by anyone in the guild then it will be placed in the guild vault and either saved for later, sold for guild funds, or traded for a needed item

Characters WILL NOT be logged on to loot an item that is needed by a member present at the time of drop. The only time a character will be logged on to loot is if no member present has a true NEED of a no drop item or as per the planes class armor clause it is pre-determined by that class that a specific person is to be logged on to loot it.

Planes Armor drops are to be decided collectively by each class with only officer intervention in cases of disputes among the class or multi-guild organization (ie one for us one for them or similiar style).

Gems/trinkets will be collectively decided upon by the guild as to be sold for funds, used for jewelry, or reimbursal/reward to members present.

All guild loot awarded to a person is theirs to use but is expected to be returned when replaced and never sold, traded, or used by a tweak without approval of an officer. Many items replaced can be put to good use by others in the guild. This does not include any items replaced that were earned outside of a guild hunt but again all donations are appreciated.

Everyone is to have a declared main character regardless of how many they have on the server. Having more than one 50th level does not entitle a person to any special consideration over another in the guild. Examples: Selronde/Synthalia and Saeppin/Zhao... all 4 are 50+ and have been in hunts but only Synthalia and Zhao are considered mains. Thus Selronde and Saeppin will NOT be part of any loot decisions other than in the case of poof prone items with no one else able to be logged on to loot. Secondary characters are just that and secondary to ALL the guild's mains in loot. People switching main status between characters must submit their intent to an officer and wait 30 days for the status to be changed (this in order to keep people from switching based on planned hunts).

Loot will be awarded to full members in the guild primarily with some special exceptions to probationaries. In terms of no drop items to full members, then probationaries, then prospects/friends, other.
