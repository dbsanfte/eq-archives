RECRUITING GUIDELINES

  • A trial member needs one sponsor if he or she is of a class and level which would benefit AoW. If he or she is of a class that would continue to unbalance AoW's roster than one sponsor and 2 subsponors are required. Decision as to which classes would be beneficial to AoW at the time will be done by myself with console of random guild members.
  • A sponsor may only sponsor someone for a 3 week trial once a month. A subsponsor may also only subsponsor once a month.
  • The trial period will continue to be 3 weeks. At the end of the trial period voting begins. The voting thread will be on the board for 1 week, allowing all a chance to vote. At the end of the week the votes will be reviewed. All negative votes will need to be addressed, if there are 3 negative votes than the applicant is not suited for AoW and will be deguilded.
  • If a trial member is not AoW material he will be deguilded swiftly. This means even before voting takes place in extreme circumstances.
  • The sponsor is responsible for making sure his/her invite is aware of the boards/officers/loot rules. If it is found that an invite is not aware of these things then his/her sponsor will need to meet with the officers until he/she may sponsor someone again.
  • Please encourage all applicants to post an application on the public board stating their class, level, normal playing time, and approximate playing time a week. The sponsor will then post to the applicant board about this person, as well as the 2 subsponsors if necessary.
  • Either Chasm, Veldor, Durak, Synthalia, Veldor, Klubfoot, Aarkane or Faen may invite a trial member with my "ok". I play late nights so I know I wont always be around to get to everyone.
  • In most circumstances there will be no more than 5 trial members at a time.
  • The officers reserve the right to ammend the rules as necessary.
