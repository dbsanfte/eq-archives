Rallos Reloaded.... again and again and again
Posted by Moltenfist on October 17, 2003

Rev's been busy over the last month farming the elementals, doing a round of backflagging (it was much more fun this time I swear) and our 3rd annual drunken gnome punting competition! Along with all the normal farming, we've got a warm spot in our vault for Rallos and his Blade of War, so we've been hunting him down like Esquire pics of Britney on google! Unfortunately, our luck on BoW's is about as good as the Yankees last few ballgames and we're 0-10 on drops..... yes that's ZERO BoW's from 10 kills >..< To change our bad luck we've tried everything in the book. Lucky LDoN charms, horseshoes from caster mounts, and rabbits feet from EW and nothings worked! We even had our monks float lucky number 7's all over the place, but still no Blade of War. To keep'em busy our warriors have been put on coffee detail while waiting to be balanced.


In desperation for a BoW, Janson has called upon Giwin to turn the drops in our favor! In return for a BoW or 5, we've promised Giwin an hour alone with Fourier (the apps needed a break anyway) and first dibs on all rotting loot from now on. Mort and Gage aren't gonna be happy bout this. The little gnome better deliver the goods though or he'll be MA the next time we kill Reparm. Oh and btw if anyone has an Illusion Modrod item for sale please send me a tell in game. I'm only good for about 3k though cause I spent the rest on ebay buying one of those "Elemental Gods for Dummies" books.


Loot..... well it's been dropping but since I lost the list when I got my new hard drive you'll just have to make do with Allakhazams this month :o
