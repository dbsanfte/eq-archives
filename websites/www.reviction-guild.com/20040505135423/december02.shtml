Reviction and Kevin Garnett Take Down High Priest/PoS Lords
Posted by Cobbyl on December 23, 2002
You might say to yourself,"Self...those guys at Reviction guys are pretty gimpy. I mean, look at em - half their guild is made of hobbits. How the hell do they do it?" Well, I'll tell ya. It turns out that the real MA for Reviction isn't really Janson "I Like to Get Stuck Behind Boxes" the Hobbit, but none other than NBA superstar Kevin Garnett! Earlier this week, Reviction ventured into the deeps of Ssra to take on the deadly, dastardly, d...evil High Priest of Sshraeshaka-kahn. There, Kevin Garnett joined in, bravely soloing the ssssssssnake.

When the smoke cleared the High Priest lay dead. Kevin Garnett left the Reviction team to enjoy the fruits of his labor, promising to get his colleague Shaquille O'neal to aid the Revictionettes in their next battle.

But though what they found waiting for them was pretty huge…and ripped…and…blue, it wasn’t Shaq, but a giant lord with an unpronounceable name!

Tier 2! Show me! Show you!
Posted by Zilben on December 08, 2002

You might say, Tier 2? You guys should have been there already. Yes, we should have. But the previous extremely bad luck with this encounter in the past just made this that much sweeter to crush. We entered the Plane of Disease with high spirits and revenge in our heads, ready to fight Captain Stinky, tooth and nail! This time bringing gas masks and lots of Lysol, the crypt of decay flag was ours, at the cost of our sense of smell.

As in a final act of disgustingness, Grummus let loose a loud one, releasing the gas cloud inside him. Unfortunately also, we had to speak with this stink to move into the Crypt of Decay. Most are still washing themselves in tomato juice to get rid of the smell.

Not known for sitting around when there's loot to be had, Reviction kept busy with all the usual suspects. But we started finding some odd behaviour from the round-up. It all started with Grummus, and then when we rallied to kick the Avatar of War's butt, he muttered this odd line:

The king of crap, Cazic Thule, was on our itinerary recently, when some members started blurting out nonsensical lines out randomly also. Something was seriously affecting people.

While taking a stroll through Kael Drakkel recently, we were told by a local giant they were holding a party for us in King Tormax's castle. We were shocked because..well..Senior Tormax was on our "list of people to wedgie" on a regular basis. When we got there though, the second the last member entered the great hall, the lights went out. And lo and behold, a team of giant super-ninjas jumped us. Luckily we had our team of monks, who had put all their AA's in the "flipping out and killing everyone" monk class skill. After cleaning up these apparently sub-par ninjas, we turned on the lights to find Tormax playing with his dolls (KT claiming them to be action figures). We decided to give him his wedgie early.

While sitting around seeing who could eat the most Jum-Jum, we got a strip-o-gram (we were confused until the dark elf got going, then we shut up and listened). Apparently the Burrower Parasite was pissed at us for not sending him a christmas card already, especially since he sent us those nice robes and rings in the past. The goon squad decided to pay him a visit and "correct his attitude". He gave us some more swag then because we reminded him just how nice a guy he really is. We were very perplexed finding an odd marking tattoo'd to his tongue during the visit. And immediately upon seeing this, people started spouting odd lines again. This "Kikkoman" must be behind all this.

While in the deep, word got round that this guy, the Thought Horror Overfiend, made the best cockroach frosties this side of luclin. When we got there though, he denied all claims and said he would not sell anything to our kind. The goons, as persuasive as always, helped make THO to decide we were alright. While feasting upon the horde of frosties, more of this "Kikkoman" influence was overheard all over the place. It was decided that the entire guild would be sent to see the best barbarian therapists the guild vault could buy. Hopefully they can knock some sense into people, and un-do the influence this "Kikkoman" has done.

With some excellent detective work, this Kikkoman has been found out! Behold: Original English Subtitled
