This Week In Sports!
Posted by Illya on August 10, 2002

There was lots going on over the last several days... let's hit the highlights!




Grieg


Ahh... sweet revenge! After tossing around the casters, and testing the mettle
of our melee force, the big purple blob falls to the hands of Rev... only
to find out he's just a human mage! He did happen to leave behind a few choice
tidbits for a lucky few:

Bonecurse - congrats Zintin!

Boots of Stability - congrats Ambnesia!

Rare Spell Drops - congrats Aetreea and Rathamon!




Statue of Rallos Zek


Hobbit's Rule! Here our vertically challenged fearless leader goes head to..
um.. toe(!) with SoRZ himself. Once again... Rev prevails, proving that skill,
strategy, and discipline outdo size every time!



Cloak of the Falling Stars - congrats Rubrik!

Breastplate of Eradication - congrats Cenena!

Iron Scroll of War - congrats Zintin and Illya!





Plane of Fear - Cazic-Thule




Poor old Cazic-Thule. He's getting tired of Rev kicking his backside. Rumor
has it he's thinking about changing his title from 'the God of Fear' to 'the
God in Fear of Reviction!'.



Brain of Cazic-Thule - congrats Kellim!

Bile Etched Obsidian Choker - congrats Rubrik!

Cloak of the Fearsome - congrats Sebkorle!

Pauldrons of Ferocity - congrats Horammer!





Temple of Veeshan and Velious at Large




As a guild, we spend a lot of time in Velious....We had a new picture of
Klandicar grinning about his death at the hands of Rev... (he wants us to
have his talisman as much as we want to have it) ... but we've visited a
bunch of his relatives this week too: Zlandicar, Lendiniara the Keeper, Telkorenar,
Ikatiar the Venom, Eashen of the Sky, and more! Here's a sample of their
gifts to us:

Breastplate of Superiority - congrats Ghangus, Gravee, and Acidica!

Mask of Superiority - congrats Aramelus!

Lance of Thunder - congrats Tingling and Trapps!

Cloak of Venom - congrats Zintin!

Ancient Wyvern Hide Tunic - congrats Sebkorle!

Mask of Venom - congrats Meakkon!

Onyx Chain Sleeves - congrats Skykam!

Twisted Steel Gaunts - congrats Razart and Banntus!




Sleeper's Tomb...

And Primal Avatar - Isazo and Conaan

Congrats everyone!
