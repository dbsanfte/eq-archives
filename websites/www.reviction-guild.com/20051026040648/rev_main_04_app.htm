Applying to Reviction

So, you've made it this far...

Before you submit your application, there are a few things that you should realize and come to understand about both the application process and the guild itself. Let's try to clear up those things now, rather than later.

Reviction has been around for over 3 years now, and the guild was built on the premise of being a family atomosphere. Though, in that time, we have also matured into a high-end raiding guild, we have not forgotten our family guild roots. We place high value on friendship and teamwork amongst the guild members. After all, none of us would have gotten as far as we have in the game without the aid of our fellows. If you're not able to get along with people, and like to fight... save both us and yourself some time, look elsewhere. If you are a team player and a likeable individual, please read on...

First, a lot of people want to know how long the whole process takes? Well, the application process takes anywhere from 3 weeks to a month.

Secondly, what does Reviction expect from you, as an applicant? Well, from the start, you should completely fill out the application form, and do so in a thoughtful manner. Answer the questions meaningfully, and don't be shy. Write a few sentences -- long applications tell us a lot more, and are received better, than a few sentence fragments and one word answers.

The application is designed to give us a high-level understanding of what you and your character are all about. While we ask for flag and magelo information... please don't think that you would be rejected purely based on your flags or gear. You are expected to work towards catching up on flags as part of your application process. Some flags are pertinent, others are not. The flags that allow you to raid where we raid are the important ones.

Once you've submitted your application, a guild officer will retrieve it and post it for viewing amongst the other officers. The officers then decide whether to accept or deny the application.

If your application is accepted, you will be brought on-board as an applicant, and given additional information by a recruiting officer. This information will include channels to join, access to the applicant forum on our message boards, and perhaps other information as he or she sees fit.

The recruiting officers are your conduit to the guild. They are in charge of administering all applications and able to answer any questions that you might have during your application period. You should feel free to talk with other members and officers as well, but the recruit officers are the people running the show as pertains to you.

The application period is a subjective thing. There are no formal tests, no quizzes, and no cut-and-dry right or wrong answers.

Your attendance should reflect what your attendance as a member would be like. If you make 80% of raids as an applicant, get voted in, and your attendance drops to 5%... you'll simply be removed. Attendance is important. You should be able to make at least 3 raids a week, ideally more.

You may be asked to fill key roles during a raid, such as tanking, organizing a rescue effort, scouting an area, or whatever else the raid leadership decides. Applicants are expected to perform these tasks to the best of their abilities. In this way, you may be put on the spot... nobody said it would be easy!

Throughout this time, your progress will be tracked within the member boards. All members have access to view your application and discuss it. At the end of your application period, a vote will be called that will allow members 72 hours to vote on your application. A 75% approval rating or higher (that's 3 yes votes to 1 no vote) is needed to pass and become a member.

Being a part of this guild is a lot of fun. Our members are good people and our raids are generally quite challenging. We hope you find it as fulfilling as we do.
