    Recruitment

Reviction is always seeking talented individuals to add to our great team. If you're an active EQ player on the Morell-Thule server and interested in joining a high-end raid guild that retains the charm and family atomosphere of many smaller guilds, read on, Reviction may be the guild for you.

We will accept applications from any class, however, at any given time we may be looking for applicants from certain classes more than others (see below).

Requirements for admission into Reviction are as follows:

  • Minimum level 65
  • Submission of application form found on this website.
  • Attendance at a minimum of twelve (12) raids within a 30 day period after application. Raid attendance will be counted only if the applicant remains at the raid for at least 3/4 of the duration of the entire raid.
  • Demonstration of a good attitude, willingness to help others, willingness to learn, and selflessness.

    The qualified applicant must have decent playtime, and be online frequently during our normal raid hours (generally we raid evenings on Eastern Standard Time).

    It is the sole responsibility of the applicant to find out what Reviction is raiding and when, and to show up on time for raids. If the applicant has not attended a minimum of twelve raids at the end of 30 days from the date of application, the application will be discarded. The applicant may choose to reapply at a later date. At the end of 30 days, the Recruiting Coordinator will decide whether to call a vote or to require further raids from the applicant.

    Feb 23rd, 2004

    Currently, we are especially seeking the following:

    Cleric
    Ranger
    Enchanter
    Wizard
    Magician

    However, as stated above, we will consider applications from any class.

    No minimum requirement with regard to level, flags, or AA is required, however the officers will use common sense in determining whether you are ready to raid aggressively at Rev's level of content and contribute to the guild as an applicant/member. Being level 65 with your basic flags out of the way is a bonus, as is high-end raid experience.

    If you would like to apply, please click the "Apply" link and fill out the form there.
