    Charter

Reviction was created by a group of veteran players who banded together in an effort to conquer the greatest of foes while having the maximum possible fun. We are a guild of players who view EverQuest as a fun, challenging and social game, but not a chore or a job. Reviction is governed by a leader, who makes all final decisions, and a council of officers, each of whom plays a leadership role in different areas such as raid coordination, epic coordination, recruiting and loot/points systems. But the guild itself is run by the members, whose opinions and desires dictate the direction of the leadership.
