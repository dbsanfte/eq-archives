Happy Holidays from Reviction!

News Update : 10 December 2005
by Fafner Wabbitslayer
with additional pics from Appox

Reviction celebrated the holidays with a Christmas Party on Saturday, Dec 10. Members and their guests want back in Time - literally, with a return to Plane of Time. The gods were good to us - dropping the second chanter slow stick ever for Rev (after probably more than 30 killings of Tallon Zek). Afterwards, the regular members did an augment run in Citadel of Anguish.

We've put together something very special to show off all that we, as a guild, have accomplished in 2005. Looking back, it's been a heck of a time, and its amazing what we've accomplished by working together. Click on the link or the picture below to see our animated Christmas Card - be sure to crank the sound up before clicking

Reviction Christmas Card - 2005 In Review - click me!

But Rev hasn't been taking the holidays off. We've been working our way from DoDH, and revisiting Vish, CoA, and all the looting favorites. Stay tuned for a lot more excitement as our push continues.
