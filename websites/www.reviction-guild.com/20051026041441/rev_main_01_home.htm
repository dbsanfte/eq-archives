Hurricane Reviction Makes Landfall in Darkhollow

News Update : 30 September 2005
by Fafner Wabbitslayer
with additional pics from Appox, Janaa & Maddey


Mobs of Darkhollow have begun to flee in panic as Hurricane Reviction makes landfall.

This is a Category 5 guild, and no mob can be considered safe. Already, Bloodeye, Matriarch Shyra and Emperor Draygun the Lich King have been laid waste, and this fast moving storm will doubtless claim many others in the very near future.

Reviction is not limiting its damage to Darkhollow. Past targets such as Citadel of Anguish's Archmage & Jelvan, Vishimtar the Fallen, Yar'Lir, and many others have found themselves hit again and again, as Reviction doubles back to deal further blows on past victims.

All Darkhollow residents are urged to evacuate. Emergancy shelters are being set up in Crushbone. Do not attempt to take your possessions. Rather, leave them behind for Reviction to loot, in hopes that they become too encumbered to follow.
