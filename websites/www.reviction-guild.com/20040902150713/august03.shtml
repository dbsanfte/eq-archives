Fennin Fired, Blazzax Blitzed, Fire Cleared!
Posted by Moltenfist on August 20, 2003

As a guild we like to focus on multiple objectives when we raid. Reviction doesn't just raid to kill stuff, we raid to have fun, see how many times we can kill the MA, find out how fast we can buff our dead monks, and to pull down some loot doing it all. While taking the 50 cent hobbit tour through Plane of Fire one day we ran into old Blazzax the Omnifiend. Having met Blazzax before in an encounter of the close kind, we knew just what to do. Our magicians immediately began summoning massive amounts of gnomish ale, and while the MA kept him distracted we got Blazzax completely blitzed!


With a blitzed Blazzax on our hands we did the only respectable thing we could do.... we snagged his loot and ran outta there! Not being satisfied with a blitzed demon, we decided to have a chat with his boss Fennin Ro to let him know his employee was drunk on the job. Fennin Ro it turns out is fairly well protected, like the real email address of that spammer who keeps sending you those 'Get Rich Quick' schemes.


Reviction, never one to back down from a challenge, continued in our efforts to drop off an employee questionnaire form about Blazzax. When we finally met Fennin Ro we were astonished that he refused to believe us or accept the form! In his anger, Janson swore that he would change corporate policy and demanded the immediate removal of Fennin Ro as CEO of Fire. As you might imagine, Fennin immediately charged Reviction who promptly kicked his butt outta office. One down, three to go....

Reviction Sees Green!
Posted by Moltenfist on August 06, 2003

With all the rain we've been getting lately mobs have been respawning like weeds! To counter this onslought of enemies, Reviction has developed a green thumb and gone on a massive farming spree! We've been so busy we can't even take the time to pull one mob at a time.... it's just too slow! Our pulling corps(e) having now perfected the 'Multi-Zone Pull' (tm) has gone wild seeing just who we can pull where and the results have been outstanding!!


With such a powerful weapon as the 'Multi-Zone Pull' (tm) at our disposal, our tacticians hit upon a devious plan to trap mobs indefinently for easy access! Setting our mighty arsenal of tradeskillers to work they have created an indestructable magical barrier from which there is no escape!! After passing out straw hats and painting our weapons green, Reviction went to work cutting down the weeds making the world a safer place for all! When our work was done, we looked down upon the planes and saw that it was clear. And on the 7th day we rested and went exping.
