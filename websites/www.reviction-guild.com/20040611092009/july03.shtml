Posted by Moltenfist on July 22, 2003

We've been having fun exploring the Elemental Planes and learning our way around. Croc Hunter Meets Reviction!
Posted by Moltenfist on July 08, 2003

It was a warm muggy evening and most members were lounging around exping when word came out that Samoht had seen Steve Irwin the Crocodile Hunter on track in PoEarth. Curious about what he was up to, we called in the troops to PoEarth to investigate. It seems that Steve was off on one of his safari's and whatever he was after its footprints were HUGE!



Eventually he found what he was after and it turned out to be a GIGANTIC Croc! Steve figured that this fellah was just a little too big to handle, so Reviction moved into action! The fight went as smooth as mechanical gnome punter with just one casualty. Apparently, Fizband accidentally hit Bengali's warder with a bolt spell which didn't go over too great with the huge beast and well... it wasn't pretty. In accordance with the Book of Armaments chapter 12, verse 7, Bengali's warder had to be put down.



We buried Bengali's warder beneath these strange doors we found, runes on the door roughly translated as "Pet Cemetary" so who knows maybe he'll be back some day. In order to replace the dps of Ben's pet, we would like to welcome our two newest wizards into the fold, welcome to Reviction Iluvatar and Aandyil.

Rallos Zek the Warlord Slain!!
Posted by Moltenfist on July 02, 2003

Between the time when the oceans drank Atlantis, and the rise of the sons of Aryas, there was an age undreamed of. And onto this, Janson, destined to wear the jeweled crown of Aquilonia upon a troubled brow. It is I, his chronicler, who alone can tell thee of his saga. Let me tell you of the days of high adventure!

Seeking revenge for past days, Reviction rolled into Drunder, home of Rallos Zek Incorporated. With shouts of "Do you want to live forever?" Reviction goaded the gigantic god into showing himself and a huge battle ensued! The forces of Zek struggled bravely against the might of Reviction but fell back before the unflagged fury unleashed upon them!


With a roar that cracked stone, the gargantuan god fell to his doom! Some said Rallos muttered a prayer to Crom on his death, though who Crom is we have yet to discover... perhaps we will find out next expansion. What lies ahead for Reviction now that the future is wide open? That is for another story.
