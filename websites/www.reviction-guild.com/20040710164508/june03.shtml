Tower of Solusek Ro Invaded !!
Posted by Moltenfist on June 27, 2003

It was a warm summers eve in Knowledge when orders came down from 'The Hobbit' that our target was the home of a spoiled hothead prince. Apparently, in a fit of rage one day he disobeyed his dad and tried to burn down Rivervale! What Solusek Ro didn't realize though, was that a good quarter of Reviction was born in Rivervale or I'm sure he never would have made such a dumb mistake (he'd have gotten Freeport instead, no one cares about them)!

We strolled into the Tower Sol Ro and proceeded to drop an extremely large Can of Whoop-O-Ass on Solusek Ro's body guards! The spoiled prince we left alone for now.... his time will come soon enough! Rumor has it Janson sent him a calender with a date circled along with a note saying he'd be back for the calender, though that is still unconfirmed at this time.

As a guild we kill a lot of mobs and go through our fair share of loot every week, but sometimes we run into a situation where no one wants an item. In the past we would call in Mort or Gage our trusty backup looters to clear up the problem.... until now! Reviction is pleased to announce Giwin Mirakon's addition to our 'Lootwhore Corps' :-)

Saryn's Sentence Served
Posted by Moltenfist on June 04, 2003

In our relentless quest for flags, we took a stroll back into the Plane of Torment. You see, our elite scouting crew had gotten word that the 3 fingered man that Tiralle has been hunting for these last 20 years was hiding up in Torment. Since Torment has no extradition policy we decided to exact justice Reviction style!


After taking out the trash we were confronted by the Keeper of Sorrows. After a fight so short I barely got a picture off, the Keeper of Sorrows was sorry he had 3 fingers on his right hand... err hoof!


Before he died, the Keeper told us that Saryn had ordered Papa Tiralle's death for reasons unknown. So we strolled upstairs and threw her a goodbye party. Apparently Saryn doesn't like surprise parties... but we were most insistent and she died just like everything else that stands in our way! RIP Papa Tiralle (Mar 7 3122 - Sept 23, 3151).
