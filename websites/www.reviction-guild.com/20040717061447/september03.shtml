Lord of the Planes: The Two Powers
Posted by Moltenfist on September 05, 2003

It began with the forging of the great guilds. Three were given to the Elementals, immortal, wisest and fairest of all beings. Seven to the middle planes, great miners and craftsmen of the planar halls. And nine, nine guilds were gifted to the lesser planes, who above all else, desire power. For within these guilds was bound the strength and will to govern Morell Thule. But they were all of them deceived, for another guild was made. In the land of Doomfire, in the fires of Mount Doom, the Dark Lord Fennin forged in secret a master guild, to control all others. And into this guild, he poured his cruelty, his malice and his will to dominate all life.


One by one, the free planes of Morell Thule fell to the power of the guild. But there were some who resisted. A last alliance of Hobbits and Elves marched against the armies of Doomfire and on the slopes of Mount Doom they fought for the freedom of Morell Thule. It was in this moment when all hope had faded, that Aragorn, son of the King, took up his father's sword and Fennin, the enemy of the free-peoples of Morell Thule was defeated. The leadership passed to Aragorn, who had this one chance to make greatness forever. For the time will soon come when Hobbits will shape the fortunes of all!

After the battle for Doomfire Keep, we sent Tingling back to school. It would seem he was mentally scarred when he learned that his skill in train-creation had been surpassed! We sent him to the best school the guild vault could afford.... Bastion of Thunder :p

Time for the spoils of war. The battle was won but the war is far from over. To be continued.....
