Ikkiniz Kompleted, Keeper Krushed!
Posted by Moltenfist on June 7, 2004

It's summer, and the warm weather has brought out more bugs than SoE's code. But nothing stands in the way of Revs progress, so we've been plowing our way through the Gates of Discord. This weeks target was the complete destruction of Ikkinz and so we took our merry band into the heart of the enemy what we found astonished us! Who'd have thought this is where they filmed all those old Wrigley's commercials....

With the twins outta the way, we stopped in to check on their Keeper. He wasn't too happy to learn we'd killed them and he challenged us to a fight. Well... at first we were a little hesitant, that is until Spara gave us a motivational speech that send the whole raid head first into action :p

A few minutes later the Keeper lay dead, and Spara was found bound and gagged in a laundry basket. What do I see on the horizon?!? Why it's Uqua, last stop before Qvic! All aboard!!

The RNG has been good to us lately and we've been pulling in the good stuff. Our only complaint is that every single ranger, pet, etc now has an Elf Shoe. We don't know what to do with the things anymore, even Giwin won't loot them anymore. Maybe you can help?

The Fifth Phase....OWNED
Posted by Moltenfist on May 14, 2004

It's been a while since the last update but there's been a lot goin on here at Rev Central. We've changed our web address, lost a ton of older news graphics (/cry), and fubared the news script so I have to do the coding manually. But enough with the website, let's see what we've been up to lately!

After rampaging our way through P1-4 we found ourselves face to face with the god of Fear! Apparently we caught him completely off guard since he was kicked back watching a movie. We taught him the real meaning of fear with our new tinkered toys our gnomes whipped up for us! The fight wasn't even close.... even Tingling lived and we only had the rangers healing him since the clerics were too busy meleeing!

After waxing Cazic, we sent our spies to check on Bertoxxulous and see if we could ambush him. Unfortunately he'd heard the ruckus and was ready so we sent in Ruby to distract him while we snuck in behind. With his back turned, Rev went into action and before long the only thing left was his feet sticking outta the wall (hence no corpse shot).

Now for Innoruuk we decided to call in some backup because this guy makes Vallon look like a pally! Janson quickly called up the supreme being Leeloo to give us a hand. A little while later Inny was dead, Leeloo had gone through the autowash, the guys had drooled all over themselves, and Quarm had been spawned!!

Cya soon Quarm! Hopefully in a few days I'll have an update on our speedy GoD progression but for now you'll just have to make-do with PoTime info :P. Your regularly scheduled program will return after a brief message from our sponsors.

Coming Soon - Rev Home Movies
Posted by Moltenfist on April 02, 2004

Today we'll be taking a look inside the secret inner workings of Reviction thanks to your intrepid reporter and his trusty Revcam (tm). Yours truly risked life and rostership to bring you back these top secret clips of Rev in action in the Plane of Time! Enjoy!

It is the year 3174, the treacherous gods have conquered the home plane of Reviction. But from secret staging grounds in Tranquility, the valiant Revbots are preparing to retake their homeplane! After AoEing our way through 3 phases of trash, we set our sights on PoTimes most wanted.....VALLON!


The battle with Vallon was progressing well until we received an email from the Revbot LAN coordinator that we would be getting sensor upgrades. Moments after the upgrade, we suddenly realized that the laws of physics had changed! Solid objects, space, and the time continuum no longer had any meaning and Vallon started warping around like the Starship Enterprise. The battle was fierce..... but there can be only one. REVenge is ours!!

With phase 5 now open we took the 10cp tour and stumbled across our old friend Rallos. Thinking the god of war was as good a place to start as any, we rolled into action and single tanked him on our first attempt. Unfortunately it was getting late and Sanadeb needed his beauty rest so we called it a night. Cya next week Cazic, Bertoxx and Inny :-)


The gods have been good to us..... not that they had much of a choice in the matter. We're also sacking our Revbot LAN coordinator for a severe lack of pre-testing.

It's Time For A Roadtrip!
Posted by Moltenfist on February 27, 2004

Well we finally took the plunge and dove headfirst into the Plane of Time, and I've gotta say this is the most fun I've had in a long time! It reminds me of the old days back in NToV. After massacring the Phase 1-3 minions we strolled into Phase 4 where the real fun began!

All that Phase 1-3 clearing had made us hungry so we stopped by the kitchen for a midnight snack. Terris Thule didn't wanna share but Rev can be very persuasive when it comes to the grub (it's our high percentage of dwarves and hobbits I think)! Oh, and did I mention she's leashed to the kitchen? =P


While we were eating at Terris's house we made a grim discovery on the back of a milk carton. I was sure the police would've found him by now, I mean the guys been missing since last March!

Our stomaches full, we decided to check out the night life and see what Time has to offer. You won't believe what Saryn will do for five Jumjum Spiced Beers and $80! Soon to be appearing in "Girls Gone Wild - EQ Break" coming soon to video store near you!

Gates exploration is coming along nicely, though we haven't really hit it full force yet. I guess the Time's not right yet.
