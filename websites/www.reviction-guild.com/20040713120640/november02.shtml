EMPEROR AND SHEI DOWN!
Posted by Zilben on November 26, 2002
Coming out of nowhere, Reviction prepped itself for quite possibly one of the most thrilling fights in its history. It was decided we had enough members ready to take down the Emperor himself, so we headed out to the darkest of dark corners of Norrath. After dealing with a couple oracle and bodyguard adds while storming his chamber, we almost wiped when Ambassador Dvinn jumped us from behind. But in the end, victory was had! Grats Aramelus on his Dwarven Ringmail Tunic! To wear or use for the screaming mace quest, it's a huge upgrade for him!



If this wasn't enough, we continued to work our way to akheva. We took a minute to tip over a fairly angry rock pile on our way to TIV, who's mood didn't improve after the fact. TIV being his regular self, big...ominous...and constantly jabbering about his favorite Star Trek episodes. We couldn't take it for long so we sent him to the big sci fi convention in the sky. Coming as a shock, the Reviction goon squad was told their job in Akheva wasn't done yet. As we all stood around scratching our heads, we were led back down the dark, grimy hallways of the ruins to stop at a peculiar fellow. After we talked, consulted, and threw some rocks thoroughly pissing this "Shei Vinitras" off we decided it needed a wedgie. On what is a very uncommon kill on our server, we were able to chalk up another personal first for us. Congratulations all around to the healers, support crew, and damage dealers on effectively dealing with one of the odder/trickier encounters pre-PoP. As well as some props to casters who had to interim act as Rampage-Tanks. ;)

Let the bodies hit the floor!
Posted by Zilben on November 22, 2002
Well, what has Reviction been up to lately? We <3 Project Illusion.



Other than having fun with the new gnome-illusion craze, we decided it was time for another Reviction first. First stop, Arch Lich in Ssra! After the blades were cleaned, boots were strapped, and gnomes were hugged, we charged ze Arch Lich who promptly panicked and died of fright. After much discussion on what caused this, we decided Tingling will now lead the charge naked every fight.



The villainous Avatar of War was spotted again recently, doing very despicable things such as, stomping on dorfs, wearing othmir-skin moccasins, and watching episodes of Survivor. We would not stand for this, so we sent our monks to bring him to us for some "chiropractic care". After stealing an object apparently very dear to Mr. War, his Hello Kitty underpants, he pursued our monks until he rounded a corner facing the Reviction goon squad. Needless to say, the underpants were the least of his worries.



On a recent spelunking expedition, one of our hobbits was unfortunate enough to slip down a mysterious dark hole. Exploration of the hole in question revealed that our lost hobbit was eaten by a very large and very scarey looking worm. After a heated debate on whether to revenge the halfling in question or to guildinvite the worm (tagging the worm having a very strong showing), it was decided to do the moral thing and beat the worm into a bloody pulp. A moment of silence was held for the worm promptly after.



Last and pretty much least, we ended off our tour de force with a laid-back TIV kill. Wanting to get some rest from the long day, we engaged with haste and zeal. And of course, TIV not wearing his zeal-proof robe this night, died more brutally than usual.

Avatar of War has fallen...and we made damn sure he didn't get up!
Posted by Zilben on November 11, 2002
PHONE CONVERSATION TAKEN FROM BEFORE THE INCIDENT:

*phone rings*

Avatar of War: Ahoy hoy.

Statue of Rallos Zek: Dude! I think something's goin down soon man.

Avatar of War: Whatchu talkin 'bout willis?

Statue of Rallos Zek: It's a madhouse here avvy! I was takin my evening constitutional and I turn this corner, and whatta I see, this BIG FREAKING mass of goons lookin ready to do some spine "adjustment" if you know what I mean!

Avatar of War: HOLY NOODLE! Did you figure out where they were headed?

Statue of Rallos Zek: I dunno man, I heard some of them talkin about all the crap they had to take to get to you...and some talkin about mr avatar fancy pants needin a new pair of cement shoes! I think they might be heading your way!

Avatar of War: CRAP! Did you get any names or who might be doing this?!

Statue of Rallos Zek: I know they had this main dude, he was small but man, his temper ALONE could level Tormax's castle! A stumpy guy named Janson spouting curses and orders! The rest, they all had this tag above their heads saying Revic..

*KNOCK KNOCK*

Statue of Rallos Zek: ...one sec avvy, I think someone's at the door. BRB.

*SoRZ opens door*

Statue of Rallos Zek: .....UWWAAAAAAAAAAAGHHHHHHHHHHHHHH!

Avatar of War: SORZY! SORZY! What happened! Come back!

Statue of Rallos Zek: *kicking and skull cracking noises*.......*silence*....WOOT!..*more silence*



Rallying spirits and numbers to the occasion. Reviction decided to put itself to a challenge which would test itself to finally show what we're made of. Swords were polished, armor was cleaned, and the ale was distributed. Stumbling into a shocking battle to start off, unprepared and with a lvl 60 add and 2 misc others, SoRZ was engaged. After what was a brutal battle and some amazing effort, victory was shouted amongst the destruction even against significant odds. The wounded were then nurtured and weapons were cleaned to prep for the real target. With an amazing effort put forth by the entire healing and support crew, The Avatar of War, in a SINGLE-TANKED effort, was brought to his knees by a warrior not even the size of the avatar's foot. The only death being a single paladin, who was promptly brushed aside for the screenshot.


After the following partying down and rounds of naked twister, the deity claiming the plane of fear to be his bachelor pad, Cazic Thule, was spotted doing really nasty things in general. It was decided this must be stopped immediately. With speed and much begging by a specific gnome, the plane was AoE cleared of its few remaining inhabitants and Cazic was brought to answer for his deeds. Cazic pleaded insanity, but we would hear none of it, so we sentenced him to give us his swag.


Last but not least, deciding that El Dorfo Numero Uno was seen as being a threat to...well...he's real ugly and that's reason enough to sqoosh him.


After what was quite possibly one of the most thrilling and satisfying evenings of Reviction history, ultimately what made it all worth it, was for our grumpy but dedicated leader to give us these words of wisdom.


Congratulations everyone on an AMAZING night.

Tinkered Dragon Targets Guild Vault!
Posted by Illya on November 03, 2002
Don Zilben Gnomercy, leader of the Boldrunt Mafia, and famous for his clockwork contraptions and fiendish schemes to pillage Reviction's vaults, has once again leapt towards the life of crime. After several long hours in the lab, he and his diabolical clan came up with a new plan to impoverish Reviction.... Xana....er.... Xanamech...er... the Clockwork Dragon!


Reviction, always alert for attempts from the misbegotten brain of Gnomercy, was quick on the defensive... and defeated the Clockwork Dragon like a well-oiled machine!

Unbeknownst to Gnomercy, the guild vault had previously been secreted away deep within the... er.. Deep, lorded over by a gigantic worm, the Burrower Parasite, and several waves of his Brotherhood of Worms. After the threat of the Clockwork Dragon was dismissed, the Vaultmistress kindly asked for the return of the loot... but the Parasite had other plans! Skillfully (for luck had nothing to do with it), Reviction made quick work of the big bug, and in a single-tanked effort, took him and his cronies down smoothly and efficiently.


Sources close to Reviction revealed that the guild, in thanks to their members for the defense and recovery of the guild vault, awarded the following items:

Cord Hilted Spike Driver (Meakkon), Woven Shim Choker (Tiralle), Burrower Flesh Cap (Roda), Bile Drenched Flesh Robes (Rathamon and Chrissepwoz)... and the biggest reward of all...



Now that's something you don't see every day!


Congrats to Reviction, and special thanks to our Clerics, Druids, and Shaman for a job well done!

Snakes!
Posted by Zilben on November 02, 2002
Well, we got ready to have a little ritual halfling burning ceremony when we got word that our recon team found a bunch of snakes up to no good!

Needless to say, we wouldn't hear of such reptilian fun-having and we went off to teach them a lesson. We knocked off their Rhags1 & 2, Glyphed, and Exiled before the Cursed stopped us in our tracks. Since cursed was wearing his rather sturdy pajamas of raid-repellant, we snuck around him and took out the next best thing, Creator.

Quite possibly some of the most fun encounters to date. They'll think twice before having their snake tom-foolery again!
