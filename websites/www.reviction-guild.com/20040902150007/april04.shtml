Coming Soon - Rev Home Movies
Posted by Moltenfist on April 02, 2004

Today we'll be taking a look inside the secret inner workings of Reviction thanks to your intrepid reporter and his trusty Revcam (tm). Yours truly risked life and rostership to bring you back these top secret clips of Rev in action in the Plane of Time! Enjoy!

It is the year 3174, the treacherous gods have conquered the home plane of Reviction. But from secret staging grounds in Tranquility, the valiant Revbots are preparing to retake their homeplane! After AoEing our way through 3 phases of trash, we set our sights on PoTimes most wanted.....VALLON!


The battle with Vallon was progressing well until we received an email from the Revbot LAN coordinator that we would be getting sensor upgrades. Moments after the upgrade, we suddenly realized that the laws of physics had changed! Solid objects, space, and the time continuum no longer had any meaning and Vallon started warping around like the Starship Enterprise. The battle was fierce..... but there can be only one. REVenge is ours!!

With phase 5 now open we took the 10cp tour and stumbled across our old friend Rallos. Thinking the god of war was as good a place to start as any, we rolled into action and single tanked him on our first attempt. Unfortunately it was getting late and Sanadeb needed his beauty rest so we called it a night. Cya next week Cazic, Bertoxx and Inny :-)


The gods have been good to us..... not that they had much of a choice in the matter. We're also sacking our Revbot LAN coordinator for a severe lack of pre-testing.
