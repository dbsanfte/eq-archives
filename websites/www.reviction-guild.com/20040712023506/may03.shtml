Posted by Moltenfist on May 22, 2003

The Reviction crew decided to pay a little visit to the god of gimp paladins and pink hearts tonight to see just what kind've a man he was. After plowing our way through his guards (I'm allergic to cats btw SoE) we got to meet the pink man himself! To our surprise, many of our members felt that they had seen him somewhere before but they couldn't quite figure out where.....


until he hit the floor and his helmet fell off! It seems that the Macho Man Randy Savage retired from the WWF and became a God! Savage refused to answer questions on camera about becoming a god and pointed us to his agent Zebuxoruk. Reviction here by formally apologizes to anyone who was hoping the Macho Man would make a repeat appearance in Spiderman 2, sorry bout that.


We got gimped on loot this time, but we'll be back again for our piercers and haste belts!

Bertoxxulous and Aten Ha Ra Wed!!
Posted by Moltenfist on May 13, 2003

It was a spring afternoon when the friends of Bertoxxulous and Aten Ha Ra gathered in the CoD to witness their long awaited wedding. The guest list included The Emperor Ssraeshza, The Vyzh'dra triplets, Rydda'Dar and Lord Inquisitor Seru among others.

The wedding was proceding as planned until Minister Diablo Xi Xin Thall asked if anyone objected to the marriage and that's when a hobbit entered and yelled "I DO!". Janson, upset that he hadn't received a wedding invitation (after spending all those late nights with AHR) had brought Reviction to crash the party! A huge battle ensued and in the end the plague bringer and friends caught a disease that even they weren't immune to! SARS or Severe Acute Reviction Syndrome has entered Norrath, GODS BEWARE!!!


A while later Reviction walked out alone after divvying up the wedding presents! Grats us on no blenders or toasters!

Crimson Permanant Assurance Sails Again!
Posted by Moltenfist on May 07, 2003

One afternoon when Reviction was lounging around Tranquility, Karana appeared in a thunderstorm and begged us to save his gimp self from the evil Agnarr! Never one to back down from a fight (and phat loot) Reviction boarded their ship the Crimson Permanent Assurance and set sail for the Bastion of Thunder!



The battle was long and hard, especially when someone agrod the Black Beast of Aaauugh, but Reviction prevailed and Agnarr was slain! Special thanks goes out to Brother Maynard for excellent timing with the Holy Hand Grenade of Antioch.


When the Crimson Permanent Assurance sailed out of BoT sometime later, there wasn't much left standing! Karana worshippers will be happy to know that BoT has been declared a federal disaster zone and will be receiving federal reconstruction aid.
