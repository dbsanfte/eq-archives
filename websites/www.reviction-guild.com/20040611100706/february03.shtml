Silly Seru and the Salubrious Sandwich Contraption
Posted by Zilben on February 25, 2003

While doing our normal exercise routine of drinking 20 bottles of booze each on the cliffside, and then racing each other across Western Wastes to Burned Woods (it's in WW this week), Ting asked us to come along with him. Apparently he had received a letter that day asking to come to Sanctus Seru for some very important information, and he got nervous and wanted his guildmates along. So we packed all our slobberingly drunken selves to the nearest taxi and zoomed off to see what it was all about. We arrived at Seru's place and were escorted into his personal quarters where he greeted us, and then spoke to Tingling.



We waited while this long, overdrawn, emotional conversation was being conducted between the newly reunited pair. But we decided that we weren't gonna come all this way and leave empty-handed. So we did what we're best at. Attacking the closest and meanest-looking thing in the vicinity with total disregard for circumstance and safety.(That and we were all still piss drunk)



The fight ended victoriously! The truly evil and overly emotional Mr Seru (AKA: Daddy Ting) was defeated and we took all his neat shiney things.



Congratulations all around on the fight. Condolences to ting. The partying was resumed shortly after and Ting was sent for years of counselling with the best erudite psychiatrist the guild vault could buy.

Robotic Revenge
Posted by Zilben on February 08, 2003

What would seem to have been an exhausting week for the whole crew, as well as the website staff. Reviction chalks up another personal first after tempting its luck yet again against the frustratingly unforgivable Manaetic Behemoth and its horde of spider minions. But disaster struck in the most sinister of ways! There were no screenshots for the update! Alas, being somewhat resourceful, the prolific poster decided he would create a virtual depiction of what the fight really looked like. These images are 99% correct of the fight in every single detail of course. Our raid force engaged the Behemoth full-on, in a blinding cloud of swordplay and spellcraft, sending wave after wave of goons at the beast!



Even throwing everything we had at the beast turned out futile! The behemoth mocked us for our pitiful efforts, but we kept on persisting! The whole fight seemed to be a disaster until one of our warriors came up with a brilliant plan. Wasting no time, the plan was initiated without the rampaging robot's knowledge!



After the plan was enacted, the behemoth became a bumbling shell who was easily tipped over, unfortunately crushing a poor cleric upon impact. A moment of silence was held, followed immediately by extreme partying-down.



Congratulations to all the crew and recruits who helped us advance to the next level. Drunder, Fortress of Zek...here we come.

Hot Date for a Hot-Headed Hobbit
Posted by Zilben on February 07, 2003

Day in day out of leading a guild is tiresome work. I mean, planning, scheduling, organizing, talking to people, shouting at people, being harrassed every single day has a way of putting a cramp in yer style, leaving no time to work the clubs, bars, and other locales. It's damn near impossible to meet a girl in-game, so what can a guild leader do? Well, we took it upon ourselves to set up Janson with a date he'll never forget. Move over Joe Millionaire, there's a new bachelor in town. We went off and set the "king of cranky" up with Terris-Thule, a fetching, if not ill-tempered gal. What started off well, soon became a challenge for the young hobbit to win her over with his poetic words.



Unexpectingly, with some mis-chosen words by Romeo, Terris unleased wave after wave of other-wordly spectres and monsters. With the goon squad backing him up, the fighting got intense. The demented visions even seemed to be too much for some of the most hardened members fending them off.



After what seemed to be an eternity of fighting the vexed maiden, the fighting suddenly came to an immediate halt, leaving all involved quite confused at the quiet. It would seem our fearless leader finally won over the testy Terris. One look at those "Buns of Steel" and it was over.



We decided to leave the pair alone for a while. When the night was over and we came to pick him up, Terris had quite a satisfied look on her face from what we could tell. Our lil bundle of fury was more than enough to sweet talk this seemingly un-pleasable wench.



Congratulations to Janny having quite the unforgettable night, and to the whole crew for backing up our formerly-frustrated leader in his time of need. Good work all. And here's some swag we nabbed along the way.

How...did....ugh...
Posted by Zilben on February 02, 2003

When you first started EQ, the path was simple. You picked up your guild weapon/spells and tunic, you marched right up to your guild master and started your claim to fame. Then you ran straight out and smacked something with the largest stick you could find. Which usually ended up getting you beaten senseless by a gang of orcs, a large snake, a rat, or even the occasional griffon. Whatever the case, you dusted yourself off, patched your wounds, and ran headlong into danger again. And that's what EQ is all about. Feeling the itch to stupidly run face first into danger, Reviction decided it was time to finally see if it could answer that primal EQ urge and come out on top against it's biggest challenge to date.



The call to arms was announced and the temple we love to hate was entered once again. Battling our way into the deepest, darkest, and creepiest areas of Ssraeshza Temple, we brought the fight and we definitely intended to finish it.



From the first assist call to the last swing of the sword 18 minutes later, the entire fight was a rush. And yes, we're all still waiting for our hobbit lubbins. =P



I'd like to take this moment to make a BIG HUGE THANK YOU to every person who helped this happen. To every person who spent a minute in ssra helping to make sure as many people as possible had their keys. To every person who sat in the mines for hours on end until they went braindead. And especially to those who ignored exp/anything until everyone was ready. And now to the loot!!!
