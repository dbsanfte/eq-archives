Halls of Honor
Posted by Zilben on January 14, 2003



Being thoroughly sick of snakes in general, it was decided a new target was in order from the previous weeks of many XTC, HP, etc killings in ssra. What was a late accomplishment, but nothing to scoff at, Reviction ultimately kills Aerin'Dar to gain access to the Halls of Honor, without outside help. Grats all around on what was a surprisingly clean first kill.



There were a bunch of other kills along the route, which weren't Reviction firsts, but did offer us some decent swag. And to make up for the lack of posts I will give away another Reviction secret! Following is a shot of how we get Tunare so angry, she is completely hypnotized while we chop her to bits.



And also on a recent Shei Vinitras kill, we had 2 of the /drool-worthy Bard Cloaks drop. But to our surprise, only 1 was claimed.
