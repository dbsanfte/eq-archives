Qvic, You're In Our World Now!
Posted by Moltenfist on August 6th, 2004

It's been a while since our ace reporter (that would be me) updated the world on our electronic exploits, so sit back and enjoy the ride! Wait... better yet I'll take you with me inside the game so you can see it for yourself! Don't move, this may hurt a little! ZAP!! Welcome program to the game of Everquest, you're in our world now!

Today we're playing Uqua, now for those of you who are new to Uqua I'll give you a brief rundown. You have to defeat the evil MPC (Master Progression Controller) in order to go to the next level. Our first stop is the Furiously Enraged Wondertwins who after a brief scuffle end up in an anger management class!

After clearing our way through piles of programs, we reach the MPC's room. There's just one problem, in order to defeat the MPC you have to kill his top program, the Guardian of Destruction! Lets call him Sark for short.

With his guardian dead, we launched a barrage of gnomes at the MPC to distract him while we tore through his defenses with magical frisbies and stuff like that! With the MPC (aka Barxt) slain, control of Qvic was returned to Reviction! Oh and did I mention we killed im on our first attempt?!

It's incredible what you can find on someones computer, here take a look!

Big 4-Headed Dragon Dies Hard!
Posted by Moltenfist on June 15, 2004

The Time had come for Reviction to finish the Planes of Power! Fighting our way through twinky wielding cops, SWAT teams, FBI agents, and APC's full of gimpy gods/goddesses, we made our way to Quarms lair! There in his lair lay our target, 740 million plat in negotiable paypalbonds!

It was now that all our careful planning came into play! Distracting Quarm with a barrage of gnome bait, we sent in our blackhat hacker Bengali to crack the vault code-key while we engaged Quarm to clear a path! The fight was incredible..... blades flashed, magic flared, and Quarms body lit up light a christmas tree! I'm pretty sure the zones gonna need a paint job and a crap load of screen doors!


When the smoke cleared, we opened the vault and to our surprise we found Zebuxoruk inside with a limo! It seems he'd been chauffering Druzzil on one of her Vegas vacations and took a wrong turn at the PoI windmill and she missed her flight. She locked him in the vault for eternity as punishment (kinda harsh imo).

He was nice enough to offer us a ride out which was nice after a long day looting and pillaging. Btw have you ever wondered what you could buy if you were filthy stinking rich?! Take a look!!

Ikkiniz Kompleted, Keeper Krushed!
Posted by Moltenfist on June 7, 2004

It's summer, and the warm weather has brought out more bugs than SoE's code. But nothing stands in the way of Revs progress, so we've been plowing our way through the Gates of Discord. This weeks target was the complete destruction of Ikkinz and so we took our merry band into the heart of the enemy what we found astonished us! Who'd have thought this is where they filmed all those old Wrigley's commercials....

With the twins outta the way, we stopped in to check on their Keeper. He wasn't too happy to learn we'd killed them and he challenged us to a fight. Well... at first we were a little hesitant, that is until Spara gave us a motivational speech that send the whole raid head first into action :p

A few minutes later the Keeper lay dead, and Spara was found bound and gagged in a laundry basket. What do I see on the horizon?!? Why it's Uqua, last stop before Qvic! All aboard!!

The RNG has been good to us lately and we've been pulling in the good stuff. Our only complaint is that every single ranger, pet, etc now has an Elf Shoe. We don't know what to do with the things anymore, even Giwin won't loot them anymore. Maybe you can help?
