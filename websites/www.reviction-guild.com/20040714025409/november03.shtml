Sushi Anyone?
Posted by Moltenfist on November 24, 2003

We've been having a good run for a while now so Janson decided to treat us to a seafood dinner at Chez Cornaiv. Well we found the service to be somewhat lacking. The drinks were watered down, the appetizers were soggy and the main course was cold. Since Janson was incoherent after 16 Gruppa's Poppin Ale we sent in Twompy to talk to the manager.... it seems Twompy may have lost his temper!


We didn't know this before we sent him in, but Chez Cornaiv doesn't serve gnomes (except on a platter) and tried to abduct our short friend! Not one to let a guildy be eaten (the gnomish boldrunt mafia is based out of Rev) we ran in to rescue our friend and a short battle ensued! Wave after wave of evil waiters, chefs, and middle tier management were defeated before we faced and slew monsieur Cornaiv himself! The officers decided not to be rude so we left them a tip..... don't mess with Reviction!


On our way out the door we snagged some loot from the cash register and a poster of a hot chick with butterfly wings we found in the mens room. Congrats also go out to MiM, Wudan, and Ascending Dawn on Rallos for killing the unkillable!

Spaceballs the Video Game
Posted by Moltenfist on November 07, 2003

In a galaxy very very very far away there lived a ruthless guild known as Autistica. The leaders of this guild, having foolishly squandered their raid targets have devised a secret plan to steal raid mobs from other guilds! Today is Ladines wedding day. Unbeknownst to Ladine but knownst to us, danger lurks in the planes above.....


The shwartz has been with us the last few weeks and we've been racking up the kills at a ludicrous speed. We've had a couple Blades of Shwartz drop from Rallos, Cornaiv and Xegony are works in progress, and Kellim's been waxing poetic on the GMOTD. On Halloween we even had a run in with Dark Helmet, we showed him what to do with his shwartz:p


We took the weekend off to relax and get a little xp and what we found on Mr Rental shocked us all. No... no don't look at it, go past this part.... in fact never look at this part again!

We were combing the desert when we found this stuff in some matched luggage.
