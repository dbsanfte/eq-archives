    Happy Halloween Reviction!

    News Update : 27 October 2005
    by Fafner Wabbitslayer
    with additional pics from Appox, Hamafa, Knutplate & Soreel

    It's Halloween time - time to dress up in bizarre and possibly scary costumes, and run around the neighborhood getting candy. And for Reviction, it's been a bountiful candy time. Epic 2.0s for all, and more CoA, Vish, MPG, and Tacvi loot than our bags can hold.

    We've also had a few runs at trick-or-treating in DarkHollows. Although the loot was initially less than spectacular, it's getting better.

    The first time we took down Emperor Draygun, the Lich King, mean ole SOE decided it was a bit too easy, so they cranked up the difficulty. But Reviction came back and showed them that even un-nerfed, the mighty Lich King was no match for us. We've plowed over the Matriarch and Bloodeye a few times as well.

    We've also gone exploring - checking out Antraygus, the Sporali King. He was a fun fight, but relatively easily dispatched, and his candy was about as appetizing as a mold-covered mushroom.


    We've also been finishing up about the last of the epic 2.0s - at least for those who have been with us for awhile. The epic fights have been a lot of fun, with some interesting places to revisit and new mobs to kill, but it's nice to be finishing them off and moving onward.

    Reviction is looking for a few more good folks who like to raid regularly. If you're interested, check out our application info and talk to an officer.

    Hurricane Reviction Makes Landfall in Darkhollow

    News Update : 30 September 2005
    by Fafner Wabbitslayer
    with additional pics from Appox, Janaa & Maddey


    Mobs of Darkhollow have begun to flee in panic as Hurricane Reviction makes landfall.

    This is a Category 5 guild, and no mob can be considered safe. Already, Bloodeye, Matriarch Shyra and Emperor Draygun the Lich King have been laid waste, and this fast moving storm will doubtless claim many others in the very near future.

    Reviction is not limiting its damage to Darkhollow. Past targets such as Citadel of Anguish's Archmage & Jelvan, Vishimtar the Fallen, Yar'Lir, and many others have found themselves hit again and again, as Reviction doubles back to deal further blows on past victims.

    All Darkhollow residents are urged to evacuate. Emergancy shelters are being set up in Crushbone. Do not attempt to take your possessions. Rather, leave them behind for Reviction to loot, in hopes that they become too encumbered to follow.
