About Reviction

Welcome to Reviction

Reviction is a focused raiding guild. We do not necessarily desire to be the top guild on the server, but neither do we desire to limit ourselves to anything. Personal respect for one another is a cornerstone in Reviction, no matter what is occurring.

A typical raid schedule for the week would be as follows:

  • Sun - Free day
  • Mon - Raid evening EST
  • Tue - Raid evening EST
  • Wed - Raid evening EST
  • Thur - Raid evening EST
  • Friday - Raid evening EST
  • Sat - Raid afternoon EST

Note: This is only a guideline; these dates are not set in stone. They WILL be changed from week to week, depending on what named mobs are up and/or due.


Mandatory Raids:

If you are online, in any form, you are expected to be on your main character and at the raid.

Mandatory Raids are those raids which we would need full guild support for.

Possible penalty for failure to comply with the mandatory raid policy is removal from the guild. This rather bold statement requires further clarification. We are not looking for ways to guildremove people and, as a matter of fact, such action would be a last resort. We will consider those who are online and missing mandatory raids on a case-by-case basis. Some of our players are European and will be unable to stay online for the duration of some of our mandatory raids. These players and others like them will not be penalized. However, we will seriously look at penalties for the player who consistently misses raids because he/she is playing an alt or hiding behind an /anon tag.


Optional Raids:

These occur on scheduled raid nights in which no named mob is up and full guild support is not needed.

Once an Optional Raid night is called, you may do what you wish. If a named spawn pops, those not at the raid are not obligated to attend for the named spawn.


Exp Nights:

These are designated free nights, to do with as you wish. Go experience, work on a quest, or sit in the Bazaar; whatever you want to do, so long as that does NOT include raiding. On these nights, nobody should even be out looking for a named spawn to pop.

Exp Nights may change each week, depending on when we think the named mobs might spawn.


Inactive Status:

In order for you to be able to loot, you need to be considered active. Your active status is kept if you raid at least twice a week. If a member fails to raid twice a week over the course of a two-week period (meaning 4 raids within 2 weeks), even if due to real life (travel, illness, etc), you will be declared inactive.

Inactive means you are not guildremoved, but simply cannot loot. Once you raid two times a week where you are not eligible for loot, you will return to active status with full looting rights. This is not meant to punish anyone, but rather, to reward people who are newer members actively raiding. People with a lot of latency points who cannot raid, even if they have a legit reason, need to come participate in order to spend points. This is a fairness issue. Even while on inactive status, you will continue to earn points for raids attended as a regular member.


Scouting for Raids:

We need scouters who are willing to scout certain mobs. Officers will be coordinating with various scouters to fulfill this job. Scouters are responsible for seeing what targets are up on Raid Nights an hour or so before raid time, if possible, then reporting to Officer in charge. Officers will then determine a target. Additional scouting will be required throughout the duration of the raid at the officer discretion.


Raid Leaders:

All active officers may act as a raid leader if there is a reason to. At a raid any instruction from an Officer is to be done immediately. The officers will work together to decide on a target, work groups, and find a pointkeeper for the evening.
