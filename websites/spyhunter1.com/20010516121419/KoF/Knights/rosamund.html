Distinguished
Cross
  

Awarded on 02-19-01, for assisting and sacrificing herself, in Mistmoore, batteling the Enynti, so Rafa could complete a quest.

I would like to sing the praises of a fine and distinguished knight in your order. Her name is Rosamund Pureheart. Here is my story:

I was trying to complete an enchanter quest for the Incandescent Mask (AC 3, INT +5, CHR +7) from the Temple of Solusek Ro. To complete it I need to retrieve the Rune of Fortune from Enynti in MM and a glowing mask from UGuk.

When I first entered MM, I was almost immediately invited into a group. When I told them that I was wanting to kill Enynti, the group leader said that she didn't want to go back there, that it was to dangerous. However she was willing to go to the Graveyard and hunt there. At first I decided to stick around a bit until I could find someone to help me kill Enynti. However that changed when I saw how disorganize this group was. They suffered three deaths before even leaving the zone in point. I told them that I would love to stick around but I need to complete the quest.

So after I left, I went to the back of the canyon to eyeball things. What I found was Enynti herself (blue to me), a Jeering Gargoyle(Blue), and a Leering Gargoyle(Yellow). There were also those four Bloodwolves. After sizing everything up I decided to try and solo her myself. I went to the back of the canyon and buffed myself up, invis and got full mana. I then targeted Enynti who was the center of the three targets. I casted AOE mez and hooked Enynti and the Jeering, with the Leering resisting. I nailed it with Enthrall (single target mez). I then targeted Enynti and casted a couple of debuffs on her. I then started to nuke her. It looked like things were going to be easy when both gargoyles broke mez at the same time. It didn't last long after that.

I went back and pulled my corpse to the zone. About that time, Rosamund entered the zone. After talking to her a bit, she agreed to help me on the second try. We went to the back of the zone, and I buffed us both to the hilt, and then waited for full mana. Again I did the AOE mez and got all three. We started to take Enynti down fast. Then both mezzes broke again, and again I was killed. I memed my attack spells and ran back into the zone. Rosamund was still fighting. However by the time I could get to the canyon she died too. But not before she killed Enynti and the Jeering gargoyle, and had the Leering to half. I looted Enynti and found the Rune of Fortune on the first attempt. I dragged both corpses to the zone line. I attempted to get an Exp Rez for both of us (mainly for Rosamund) but could not find a cleric that was willing to come to MM, even if I could get them a port to and from.

To Rosamund I would like to offer my deepest thanks. After I left MM I was able to complete the quest in short order. I am now the proud owner of the incandescent mask. If you or any member of your Order ever need anything that I am able to provide please ask.

(sub/wit: Rafa Delphi )

On my way back to them I fell in to a moat and couldn't get out for quiet some time. I finally got out of the moat with only half a bub of health left and run to them for assistance. As soon as I got to them, I yelled for help, so they knew I wasn't kidding. Without thinking, Rosamund gives up her Lay hands to me, which saved my life, and then the frogs started to pile in. As they did, I turned to attack along with Taita and Rosamund. Herpally without thinking didn't attack but instead took the place as a healer and healed me and the others as we fought. Need less to say I lived only because of the fast thinking of Herpally, Taita, and Rosamund. I feel that these 3 noble KoF members deserve a medal of some sort for putting them selves at risk for another. (sub/wit: Trayz)

Awarded on 12-18-00, at lvl 13, for victoriously engaging several Gnolls in Black Burrow while performing a CR for a girl named Yablo.

Today a very young human died after falling into the tree trap in Black Burrow, and came looking for help. She could tell us only that she had run up several levels, crossed a bridge and died in a room full of gnolls. Cetra agreed to help her and began the difficult task of finding her body, only to find it guarded by many gnolls, including a commander and elites. Cetra thought our odds of winning were small, but we were both willing to give it a try. He showed great leadership, gave me instructions on which to take and in what order. After a long battle, using 2 LH's and virtually all our health, 7 gnolls lay dead. Cetra dragged the corpse to the zone. It was his calm in the face of chaos that gave me the courage to fight on.
