CODE OF CONDUCT

The Code is divided into three paths:

  • The Path of Nobility
  • The Path of Dishonor
  • The Path of Evil

As Knights of Freedom, we walk the Path of Nobility, Punish the Path of Dishonor and banish the Path of Evil. It is our intent to uphold Freedom, assist the oppressed and guide the lost. We require no reward and ask for nothing in return.

All Knights are responsible to read the Code of Conduct in its entirety and follow the Path of Nobility.

PATH OF NOBILITY

Those who choose the path of Nobility are true Knights of Freedom. Travel this path for its long journey and Valor will follow. Those of Valor will be rewarded with leadership.

1. General Nobility:

  • Let no undead walk among the Living.
    Undead are the highest abomination to man. They are the incarnation of all evil and should be vanquished frivolously.
  • Gladly help the oppressed.
    We as Knights are frequently called upon to help recover the corpses of those oppressed by by evil. With no thought of reward, our responsibility as Knights is to go out of our way to bring them to freedom.
  • Give unselfishly.
    Both in your time and riches. Spend time teaching and assisting the lesser ones. Help others without concern for payment or reward. Give to those in need and reward those who have achieved. Beggars should be examined and evaluated for there true intent and worthiness. Not all beggars should be given to. For it is far more useful to teach a man how to fish than to just give him a fish.
  • Treat all races with a respect for life.
    Although the evil races are relentless in their evil deeds, our mercy and forgiveness shall prevail. Saving the life of your enemy is far more Noble than saving the life of your friend, for even the Devil himself will assist his friends.
  • Take no revenge and show no anger.
    If someone wrongs you, do not respond with strong words or anger, for you have stooped to the ways of the offender. Do not lower yourself because of him but instead heap blessings and assistance upon him. This will uproot even the rudest of offenders and makes them look foolish. It guaranties you an overwhelming victory in the eyes of all who happen to be watching you. It is a far greater achievement to win the friendship of your enemies then of those who want to befriend you.
  • Be Strong and Courageous in the face of Death.
    It is a rare and fleeting moment when you as a Knight can step in and spare the life of an unsuspecting person from the wrath of death. A split second of hesitation and the moment is lost and your left standing over a corpse you could have saved. Do not fear death, however do not die foolishly. Your sacrifice will bring Honor and Valor to your name.
2. Group Combat Nobility:
  • Heal others before you heal thyself.
    Your mana is better spent healing others rather than yourself. This act demonstrates a general selflessness that will bring Honor to your name.
  • Do not loot until invited to loot.
    This will avoid any perception of greed or selfishness by others and severs to Honor your patience in life.
  • Do not loot while others are fighting.
    Although it may be your turn to loot, as a general rule it is disrespectful to loot while others in your group are fighting. This code should not discourage combat looting where tactics and common sense calls for it.
  • Looting should be of the "Honor" method and roll dice for items of value.
    The Group Leader determines the loot method and should recover the items to be rolled for. Once you have won a certain item in a roll, then you shall pass on all other rolls for that item until all group members have received that item.
  • Assist your main "Tank" and Protect the Clerics and Casters.
    All group members should assist on the main tank in the group, however when a healer/caster is being disrupted or endangered, one melee type should disengage and taunt, root or fear the disruption and then return to the primary assist. When your healers are endangered the group is weakened.
  • Group Leadership.
    This is the responsibility of the highest ranking member or the organizing member of the group. Leaders should seek the advice of those in the group who have more experience in a certain area or dungeon. A group leader may delegate leadership if tactics call for it. Obey the group leader and remember, mistakes can be seen as valuable lessons.

3. Procedure and Policies.

  • Show respect for Rank and Achievements.
    Obey the orders of your Superior Officers. As a minimum, Knights should bow or salute when greeting all guild Officers and Noble persons of higher level. All Knights Shall Kneel before all Guides, GMs and Gods. Congratulate all leveling announcements. When your there, you will want respect shown too.
  • Awards.
    All Knights (including Squire Knights) are eligible to receive awards. All Knights (including Squires) may submit, at any time, to the Guild Leader for consideration of awards to be presented to themselves or other members as outlined on the Awards Page.
  • Power Leveling.
    Power leveling for experience is allowed at any level. Knights are encouraged to perform this service for those under lvl 30, however Squire Knights should not ask or beg to be power leveled for the sake of of self respect and humility.
  • Crusades and Quests.
    All Knights are encouraged to Crusade for any item or quest they feel they can accomplish. This includes as a group or solo. However, a high level Knight shall not give direct assistance to a younger Knight in accomplishing a high level quests until the Knight you are assisting has reached a matured lvl of 30 +. Once a Knight has reached a level of 30 + they shall be considered qualified to receive any assistance needed for any quests or crusades. The term "high level quest" can be construed to mean any quest that involved the defeat of an opponent level 36 or higher.
  • Meetings and Events.
    Members should only show up for raids in which they meet the minimum requested level as posted for that raid. At hosted events, the hosting or oirganizing member is in charge. Officers of higher rank than the Host should follow the direction of the Host to facilitate the event. Events (such as trade fairs) may be conducted by any Knight and should be submitted to the Guild Leader in advance for posting. Meetings for the entire guild are held as scheduled on the News Section. Your attendance is crucial, however you do not need to physically at a location only available for guild chat.
  • Disputes.
    If a reasonable dispute arises concerning an item(s) or spawn(s), do the Honorable thing and surrender the item(s) or spawn(s) in question as a show of Nobility. This does not imply that you should allow yourself to be taken advantage of when the item(s) or a spawn is rightfully yours.

    Disagreements or anger with another person’s actions are expected to happen from time to time. It should not be left unresolved. Anger is understandable, however it is not acceptable to lash out with your anger in the moments that follow. In KoF, Nobility demands a polite, calm and tactful attitude when communicating your disagreement or anger. Nobility runs both ways. If you fall victim to a person’s anger, KoF Nobility demands understanding a polite, calm and tactful reply. Remain calm and continue with the event, leaving it undisturbed, and then address the issue with the member at an appropriate time using direct tells. Guild chat shall not be used as a dispute or discipline forum.

    For the benefit of the guild, the Round Table shall not be used to work out a disagreement or complaints you are having. Knights must keep in mind that the Round Table is a public forum, viewed by many, and reflects substantially on our guild and the members. Topics in violation may be removed by the Guild Leader at any time.
  • Grievances.
    Knights should attempt to resolve disputes with non-members or allied guild members on their own, in a polite and noble manner. If the matter cannot be resolved, then it should be presented to a guild Officer. The Guild Officer should attempt to resolve or mediate the issue. As a last resort the Guild Leader should be notified.
  • Duels.
    Duels are the discretion of the individual.. Duels between guild members for fun should not result in any deaths, unless agreed upon. Duels to defend your Honor should be a last resort, for ours is not the path of revenge or anger. Duels should not take place during Guild events which are not of a dueling nature.
  • Discipline.
    All KoF Members are responsible for advising all Knights concerning deviations or violations of the Path of Nobility, Code of Conduct or any posted KoF Policy. See the "Policy" pages for further clarification concerning discipline.

PATH OF DISHONOR

Those who choose to walk the Path of Dishonor may be punished. Warnings, reprimands and tasks of punishment may be assigned to those who stumble along this path. As always, natural consequences follow those without Honor.

  • The use of profanity, sexually explicit or derogatory words or statements, Lying, Deceiving , Misleading or harassing other players.
    Basically, if its not Honorable, don't be doing it.
  • Disrespecting.
    Although a true Knight would never even consider this path, the issues must be outlined. This includes but is not limited to: Disobeying a reasonable order from a Superior Officer. Failing to maintain a reasonable respect and care for the life of another. Insubordination, rudeness or ridicule displayed in any form towards another player (such as shouting). Failing to show proper respect when called for. Failing to follow the Path of Nobility. Failing to follow published KoF Policy or guide lines.
  • Intentionally causing another player or groups death by training or other means.
    You can judge the size of a Knight by the size of the event that angers him. Revenge is not our method.
  • Kill Stealing.
    Whoever hits it first has privilege to it. Don't take others pulls. If you want to assist or skim from a pull then ask first. Immediately disengage from any spawn in question. This does not apply to cases where it's obvious the puller needs assistance.
  • Camp Stealing.
    If they are there first they have control over the spawns in that area or room. Don't pull from their control. See the Disputes policy above for futher information.
  • Ninja Looting.
    This is the act of looting another player's or group's kills when they are busily fighting the remaining Mobs they have engaged.
  • Associating with the Banished:
    At no time shall any KoF member Group, Chat or Contact in any way a Banished player. KoF members shall by all means avoid contact with a Banished. Limited contact may be excused for the sole purpose of saving the Banished persons life.

PATH OF EVIL

Those who choose to walk the Path of EVIL may be BANISHED. Punishment or banishment are the Fruits of Evil. As always, natural consequences follow those who walk with evil.

  • Stealing from your group.
    Leaving the area or camping with an item you did not legitimately win in a roll or under an agreement is FORBIDDEN! Items of value are occasionally recovered from your kills. These items should be awarded to the winner of the "roll" (/random 100) unless other arraignments have been agreed upon prior to the drop in question. For disputes over items, or spawns, refer to the Procedure and Policies section,
  • Stealing from a person.
    Failing to make a reasonable attempt to return any item accidentally dropped by another player is FORBIDDEN! Most accidents occur while trading. A reasonable attempt involves: directly asking the person (if known) or persons near the area if they want it back. You shall surrender the item to anyone whom requests its return, providing the claim is of a reasonable nature, and shall not charge for returning the item. Borrowing or using another players item(s) and then leaving the area or camping without making a reasonable attempt to return the item(s) is considered stealing. It is recommended you compensate the person(s) offended with at least 20 PP for their inconvenience if you go LD with and item and cant immediately return with it.
  • Exploiting what you know to be a game "bug" for personal gain.
    All "Bugs" should be reported via the /bug command.
  • Fraudulent petitions, E-mails or postings.
    Reporting or inflating acts of misconduct which never occurred with the intent to dishonor or seek revenge on a player is FORBIDDEN and shall result in punishment or banishment. Honor and Valor are destroyed in the face of lies.
  • Refusing to submit to a punishment.
    After the appeal has been reviewed and a punishment sustained, The Knight offender is obligated to carry out the Punishment in a timely manner. Failure to do will result in Banishment.
  • Repeated acts of following the Path of Dishonor.
    As Knights we can't maintain our Nobility and Valor in the face of ones who choose to dishonor us. When several courses of action have been exhausted, banishment may be imposed.
