My History: The path of a Paladin is a long and thankless art form, however, it's with great conviction that I embrace this harsh, cold life of dedication knowing that the force of light grows stronger daily. The evil resistance I encounter throughout my life continues to craft me into a survivalist capable of fighting under all conditions with the sharp mind of a methodical holy executioner. I've released foul minions from the bowels of Unrest, earned my Knighthood within the Hall of Truth resulting in a wanted poster among the corrupt walls of Freeport, defeated countless black-hearted murderers & thieves while leaving Marr's mark along the way as an omen to darkness. I am a protector of humanity and will always stand my ground when matched along side a war party. I've been confronted by materialistic guilds with promises of riches and fame... I seek neither. My calling is to join a band of knights that are dedicated to each other first and foremost. Without loyalty, dedication, courage and justice we might as well crawl back into the primordial ooze from which we came. Fame and riches are becoming catalysts of evil upon this land causing men to steal, backstab their fellow brother and mindlessly allow their decedent egos to command their actions versus relying upon their divine intuition. The Knights of Freedom follow my virtues and I'd like to commend Dami on such a well-respected organization. I announce with pride, I am a Knight of Freedom. We will not rest until our land has been cleansed.
