Awards of Service.
Multiple awards of the same type may be received.

     
Star Icon
Awarded to Knights who have donated time, effort and resources to the guild worthy of recognition and praise. (Example: A Knight spends several days in a dark dreary dungeon to retrieve a Crusade Reward item for a guild member.)
*May be self reported.
     
Shield of Nobility
Awarded to Knights who have performed an act of Nobility or Selflessness worthy of recognition and praise. (Example: A Knight retrieves a strangers corpse despite risk to himself in doing so.) *May be self reported.
     

Sword of
the Valiant
Awarded to Knights who have displayed an act of Courage or Bravery in combat worthy of recognition and praise (Example: A Knight courageously engages an opponent of a great risk and is victorious.)*May be self reported.
     
Silver Medal of Freedom
Awarded to Knights who have performed a selfless act which directly or indirectly saved the life of another, however did not put the knight in serious risk.
* Must be confirmed by a witness.
     
Distinguished
Cross
Awarded to Knights who have performed a selfless act of Nobility or Courage worthy of recognition and praise which involved serious risk to the Knight.
*Must be confirmed by a witness.
     
Medal of Valor
Awarded to Knights who have performed a selfless act of extreme Nobility or Courage, which directly or indirectly saved the life of another or others and knowingly involved serious risk or death to the Knight. *Must be confirmed by a witness.
     

Crusade Achievments.

   
Gauntlets of Divine Might
Awarded to Paladins only, who have Complete the Quest for Divine Might and have obtained the Holy spell. Evidence must be provided to support your award. (Screen shot)
     
Crest of Wisdom
Awarded to Clerics or Druids, who have Obtained a consistent Wisdom of 200 or better through items which they themselves now own and use. Not borrowed items. Evidence must be provided to support your award. (Screen shot)
     
Shield of the Planes Walker
Awarded to Members of KoF who have walked on all the Planes know to be in existence and have participated in the death of at least 3 Planier Gods. Evidence must be provided to support your award. (Screen shots or exact event dates)
     
Shield of the Dragon Slayer
Awarded to Knights who have actively participated in the defeat of at least 3 named dragons. Evidence must be provided to support your award. (Screen shots or exact event dates)
