Shield of Nobility
  

Awarded on 9-16-00, at lvl 19, for recovering the corpse of a lost Paladin in the waters of TD.
The other day Awyrun and I were out killing ogres in Tim. Deep. All of the sudden I got a tell from a 32 paladin, who fell of the boat and zoned into our zone. He asked me for the location of a dock. I ran to the dock, got the location and told him. In a little bit I recieved another tell, saying that he died kinda by the location I gave him. I told Awyrun and we ran to the dock to see if we could help. We swam around some but couldnt find him. All of the sudden Awyrun told me that she found it and that I should send him a tell giving her consent to drag his body. I did, but it said that he was no longer online. She waited out by his body and he finaly came back on. He consented her and she drug his body all the way back to shore and down to the beach that the boat from Oasis lands on. I explained to the paladin how to get to the boat in Oasis, and he managed to get there and loot his stuff. He was very gratefulf. While Awyrun was never in any real risk, if she wouldnt have found his body, there was a very good chance that he would have lost all of his gear. (Sub / Wit :Galis Battlebliss)
