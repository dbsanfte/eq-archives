Invite Policy:

- Absolutely NO "Necromancers" or
"Shadow Knights" will be considered for invite. Sorry!
- Paladins and Clerics do not need a member referral to be invited.
- All other classes must have a *referral from a KoF member to be invited.
- Minimum Levels: Clerics and Paladins lvl 10, all others lvl 20.

*To receive a referral, simply find one of our officers in Norrath.
Give him or her a tell and ask about getting a referral to join.
You may have to spend some time chatting or fighting with the
member. You may have to complete a quest or two. We do this
to preserved the quality of our members and protect the reputation
of our Noble Knights of Freedom. When your trust is earned, then you
will be allowed to use the members name on your application.
