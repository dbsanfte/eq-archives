My History: During his infancy, Queamin was spared by the Orcs of the Crushbone Clan to spend the rest of his life as a slave working in the mines located near the Crushbone Clan Stronghold in Felwithe, only to be saved one day by a raiding party consisting of elite paladins of Felwithe under the command of Tynkale.

Tynkale saw potential in the innocent young elven boy and decided to train him in the ways of nobility, honor and freedom. When he was 17, Tynkale ordered him to venture into the fortress of the infamous dark elven leader, Mayong Mistmoore, there he encountered a group of people fighting against all that is evil – calling themselves "The Knights of Freedom". At the age of 45, Queamin left the Knights of Freedom for a short period to investigate the chain of command of the Crushbone Clan that destroyed his village and mercilessly killed his family. Finding out that the clan is in league with his greatest and most dangerous enemy, the dark elves, and that one of its ambassadors currently planning a visit to Crushbone Stronghold. Knowing this, Queamin reported back to Tynkale and an offence to launch a dangerous mission to raid Crushbone the day of the ambassador's arrival began. Their objective was simple – stop the alliance between the dark elves and the Crushbone Orcs. The raid went grim, most of the elite paladins was killed in the attack but during all the chaos and fighting Queamin and a small group manage to infiltrate the tower section of the castle where ambassador Dvinn was making final preparation to ally with the Orcs. A furious fight insued with more of Felwithe’s noble knights and orcs fell. With the help of a fellow Knights of Freedom member, Prazarael, Queamin manage to pierce his sword into Dvinn’s black heart. With the fall of the Orc King and Dvinn, the remaining orcs knew that they have lost the day and decided to flee.

To this day Queamin has rejoin the ‘restructured’ Knights of Freedom who is under command of the valiant Dami Valatron fighting along them in their quest to banish evil forever.
