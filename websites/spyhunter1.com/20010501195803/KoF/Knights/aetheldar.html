My History: Born the son of a little known Knight of Truth, Sir Gerhard and the Lady Amanda, Aetheldar's early childhood was an easy one. When Sir Lucan D'Iere betrayed the Knights of Truth and occupied two thirds of Freeport, Aetheldar's father Gerhard was slaughtered while fighting in defense of the city, his lands seized and Aetheldar and his mother cast out.

Slipping from the easy life of petty nobility in the country to the slums of East Freeport was not an easy thing for anyone but an abiding sense of goodness and the pity of the Knights of Truth saved Aetheldar from what could have been a sad tale. While the Lady Amanda did what little work there was for a woman ill used to the hardships of labor, Aetheldar began to associate more and more with the Knights of Truth. First as an errand boy and then slowly advancing in importance and popularity with the Order of Paladins.

Aetheldar's resolution to walk the path of the Paladin was tempered by tragedy. On the day he received his invitation to join the Knights of Truth he returned home to find his Mother dead of her own hand, two notes nestled in her lifeless palms. One, Aetheldar's invitation to membership in the Order of Truth. The second, a suicide note in tear stained script stating that she could not bear the thought of her son dying in the name of a God that had so obviously forsaken them all.

Aetheldar's Faith took him in another direction than sullen mourning... Instead he saw the Shadows of the Dark Gods-Innoruuk, Cazic Thule and Bertoxxulous fallen across the bright soul that had been His Mother. If the influence of these Dark Gods was so prevalent as to be able to overwhelm his Mother, the threat must be more dire than the darkest of prophecies espoused by the Priests in the Temple of Marr...

Aetheldar turned his back on the past, and now stands as his Father before him for all that is good and just.

"Let those who worship Evil's might beware my power, Mithaniel's Light!"
