Shaken, on the verge of hysteria, I asked my friend Demasus to ask this guild for any volunteers to help me and my husband, Tryyall, get my bodies so I could be rezzed. Wow, what a response! Demasus, Valvarent, Kalerilae, and Rastana all volunteered! We made haste to Sol A, where Rastana SOLOed his way to this room deep in the dungeon to drag BOTH my bodies out! He was amazing, I was awed by his courage. I was rezzed, successfully, and prepared to leave. But then...they all offered to help me finish my quest!! Not only did they come to my aid, but they took the time and had the courage to stand up to the foul goblins and slay them all time after time! After some time had passed, the wizard I was waiting for spawned, he was quickly brought down, and I had my quest component.
