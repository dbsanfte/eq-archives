Last Updated : June 2, 2001

The KoF Vault is accessed only in North Freeport in the Bank. Knights may donate items. Once an item is donated, then it becomes KoF property. Minor level items (Bronze/FS or lesser) will not be stored in the Vault. Max of 3 items per Member at one time.

Any non-expendable item removed from the Vault shall not be given away, sold, traded, dropped, destroyed, used to Twink alts or left on an inactive character. KoF members may use the Vault items until they get a better item, or no longer need the Vault item, then they must deposit it back into the Vault for others to use.

Ro quest items are, of course, expendable. When removing a Ro quest items, Knights are asked to purchase, trade up (i.e.. Take out a melatite and deposit two sands) or replenish the item as soon as practical. Money received will go into the Vault Platinum Reserve.

Items not distributed after 60 days will be auctioned off and the Plat deposited into the Reserve.

 

Platinum is distributed by special request and may only be borrowed:

Reserve Platinum Pieces: 420pp
