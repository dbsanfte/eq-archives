Testimony of Truth
Ghoulbane

My History: I am a Knight of Freedom. I was born in Freeport. It is a city corrupt with an influence of evil, under the hand of Sir Lucan D'Lere. I have made it my goal since I was a young boy wandering the Commons area to one day vanquish the hold that he has on this beautiful city. As I was fishing one day Sir Valeron Dushire of the Knights of Truth approached me. He asked me to perform a task for him. Of course, being completely in awe at the Knights of Truth and what they stand for, I accepted his task. Upon completion of the matter, Sir Dushire asked if I was interested in apprentice training at the Hall of Truth to become a Knight. I of course accepted and left my parents to live at the Hall of Truth to fulfill my training.

Within a few months I had honed my skills and earned my Testimony of truth. My father was the only Fletcher in Freeport and had many customers. In seeing that my father was so successful selling bows and arrows to the many travelers, he was approached by the Freeport Militia and told that he had to pay a protection tax. My father being a very proud and strong willed man told the Militia that he had no need for their protection. This of course angered the Militia and late one night my father and mother were paid a visit by some strangers that were not from the area. An altercation ensued and there that night my mother and father were killed. I received word the next morning of what had happened. In anguish I left the training hall and never returned. I wandered many of the lands of the surrounding area of Freeport.

One day, the Lady Dasi Valorton approached me. She told me that her husband was interested in helping young Paladins face the onslaught of evil forces that were making themselves ever present in the local area as of late. I told her of my own struggles with many of the undead that had wandered into the area of West Freeport and the East Commons area and would be honored to meet her husband. She told me that she would arrange a meeting. I waited by the first Orc camp in East commons developing a plan of attack when I was approached by a noble figure in silver and black armor. He introduced himself to me as Dami Valorton, the leader of the Knights of Freedom. He invited me to join him in his travels and he would show me places that I had never seen before. I accepted his invitation with much anticipation and excitement. After a couple of weeks of fighting the undead minions with Dami, he asked if I would join his crusade against the undead. I was completely speechless. I tried not to rush the decision to join as my father had always taught me but I could not think of one possible reason to not join this small and wonderful group. I accepted Dami's offer with much enthusiasm.

I have traveled with Dami for many years now and faced many foes. All the while the thing that I appreciated the most was the way he took me in when I was down and has guided me to be what I am today. My father would be proud of us both. I have since taken up my family trade and have become a master fletcher. I have also taken an interest in blacksmithing and you can bet that I will use my skills to help train and equip all of the Knights of Freedom.

Cross
  

Awarded on 10 -17-00, at lvl 35, for recovering Dami's corpse from the Hungerd Ravener room deep in Kaesora.

Aerren, Aetheldar, Dami and Dasi were grouped in Kaesora. At the Hungered Ravener they were jumped by several undead minions of Xalgos and Warders and Spectral Guardians. A long and fierce battle raged. Dasi fell in the middle of the fight; her mana was nearly gone. After beating down the undead to only 2 left, Dami could not hold on any longer and fell also. Aerren and Aetheldar continued the battle to within pixels of death and were Victorious! They retreated to a dead end hallway and attempted to recover, and were attacked by another Warder but defeated it. They knew they could not survive there just the two of them.

The group devised a plan of escape and corpse recovery for the members. Aetheldar and Aerren would Invis to the undead. Aerren would grab Dami's corps and carry it. Knowing that the Spiders near the surface would see through UI and attack, Aetheldar would run ahead and clear a path for Aerren by pulling the spiders along with him as he attempted to make it to the zone. Aerren would then have a clear path to put Dami's corpse in a safe spot on the second lvl were Dasi could drop down for a resuscitation on it.

The Plan worked beautifully, however Aetheldar got stunned up top and fell into a pit putting him back on the bottom lvl. Aerren made it untouched to the safe area at the left drop from the zone. Dasi resuscitated Dami whom then recovered. Aerren, Dasi and Dami cleaned out the spiders and Dami used UI to recover Dasi's corpse from the Hungered Ravener area. The three then attempted to locate Aetheldar who was bound in the Arena zone.

Things seemed hopeless as Aetheldar's corps could not be found. Dami jumped down a pit in an attempt to pull up Aeth's corpse however it could not be located. Dami died near the zone while trying to escape as a result. Several hours later Aethaldar arrived back at Kaesora and Dami was able to use a chipped bone rod to get a direction on Aeth's corpse. Mortigan, Ulvick and Rohwin also arrived to help. Dami gathered the group in a safe spot and Jumped into the pit. Dami found aeth's corpse and managed to drag it out from behind the library, while being hit by Warders and several spectral. Dami go the corpse up to the spider room where he dropped it off and fell to his death a short way farther. The undead and spiders left the area and Aetheldar was able to recover his belongings.
