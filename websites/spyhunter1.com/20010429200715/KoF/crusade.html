Crusading for Paladin Armor of Ro: (by: Dami Valorton) Under Construction

A Paladin's Armor of Ro is smithed "Paladin Only" armor made up of seven pieces as listed below. To make each piece requires the mold for that armor piece and a bar of Ronium. Simply combine the Ronium with the armor mold inside any forge and the results will produce the Armor of Ro piece corresponding to the mold you used. At this time, no smithing skill is required to make the armor pieces.

Armor of Ro molds are given out by Paladin NPC's who live in a camp on a plateau in the Rathe Mountains. Click on each piece of Armor of Ro to learn about obtaining the mold for that piece.

Sol Cauldrons (No Drop, Lore) are obtained by speaking with Lord Searfire, a Dwarvin Smith in the Temple of Solusek Ro. This temple is located in a cave on the North side of a huge lava pit in the Lavastorm Mountains. The pit is at the far North end of the land. Once inside the Temple, make your way to the large chamber with the glowing lava river and several NPC characters, in armor, standing around. Lord Searfire can be found by going up the stone bridge over the lava river, though the iron doors and making all left turns through several more doors. Hail him and then say "I need a Sol Cauldron". Your faction must be high "Amiable" before he will give or speak to you about the cauldron. The best way to get better faction with Lord Searfire and the Temple is to go to the South Desert Ro and find an Orc named Ortallius in a crevice on the sand at North East corner of the zone. By giving him 4 Derv Cutthroat Insignia rings at a time, your faction will raise. Hail Ortallius for details. To get the rings, there are two Derv camp in this zone located on the sand near the east and west zone walls. There are also two more camps in the Oasis of Marr located in the south east area by the lake. Kill the Dervish at the camps and collect their rings then give them to Ortallius and the con him to see your faction. Do this until he cons amiable, then give him approx. 20 more rings to get high amiable. ( approx. 40 - 50 rings in all to get the faction you need ). One can also give 30 gold pieces at a time to the vendor Rathmana Allin in the tent behind Ortallius to increase your Temple faction, however I am not sure if you can go above Amiable in this way.

Falchion of Koada'Vie: (by: Rohwin)

The falchion is a Paladin only quest one hand slashing sword. The stats are 6 damage, 24 delay, magical, lore item, no-drop, and Paladin only. It is a decent weapon for Paladins in their late teens to mid-20's. It is good for developing your one hand slashing skill since it is fairly fast. It also has in interesting graphic; the blade gets wider as it goes away from the hilt, then comes to a point near the end. I recommend doing this quest at around level 20, perhaps a little earlier if you can get some help.

Test of Truth: (by: Dami)

This quest is given by Merko, behind the Hall of Truth. Merko will talk about the tokens of generosity and of bravery. Merko then asks you to help Altunic Jartin in the East Commons. Take Altunic a letter that Merko will give you. Altunic can be found at his house across from inn 3 or walking near the orc camps. Altunic will ask you to help him with a thief named Squire Narl. You need to kill squire wretched Narl and get his head, and give it to Altunic. Narl will spawn outside the house across from Inn 3 when you give Altunic the letter. Then give Altunic a cloth shirt and 5 gp. This will get you a token of generosity. Return to Merko and inform him that you have earned the token of generosity. Don't give it to him yet.

Merko will ask you to get him a spider venom sack from Nro. Get him by one killing spiders and give it to him and you will get a token of bravery along with some experience and faction (again, even if he won't talk to you after level 9, if you give him the venom sack he should still give you the token).

He will then tell you to give him both tokens if you want to go further on. Give him both tokens and he will ask you to take the test of truth, which is killing the guard that appears in the sewers (again after level 9 he might not ask this, but the guard will still spawn). The guard is actually a female militia spy. Kill her quickly or she will run to the zone and be gone. Immediately after giving Merko the tokens, run to the sewer entrance by the water next to Merko. Don't stop to read what Merko says because you don't have much time. Kill the guard in the sewer (it wont be hard, but she will run so be ready, she is fast, she will swim to the front of the Hall of truth and run out then front.) Take the token she has to Merko, you will receive your Testimony of Truth Scroll, proclaiming you are a Knight of Truth.

This scroll is needed for the Soulfire quest, and is a no drop item. Keep it in your bank.
