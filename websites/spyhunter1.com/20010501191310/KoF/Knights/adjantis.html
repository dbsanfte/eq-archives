Silver Medal of Freedom
  

Awarded on 12-16-00, at lvl 36, for saving the life of Mortigan in the East Commons while under attack from Sgt. Slate.

Adjantis and I were on the verge of engaging in a friendly duel (down to one bubble- he wanted dueling pointers from a master =) near the first torch in the Commons/Ro tunnel. I had previously dispatched several corrupt Freeport guards on the way to the tunnel and must have left one for dead when he was actually only unconscious. That guard awoke, ran to Slate and reported where I was last sighted. The Sarg then started his trek towards my location in the Commons/Ro tunnel system. As Adjantis and I bowed out of respect prior to the spar session, I heard a loud roar followed by the cold tinge of metal in my back. I spun around and low and behold, the infamous Sargeant Slate stood before me with my blood staining his shortsword. I began to attack Slate and shouted for any brave noble souls to help with his onslaught. Alas, Adjantis and I were surrounded by mere merchants who feared such a challenge. Adjantis and I began the trek towards the Ro zone once things looked grim. Slate was hot on my heels and systematically thrusting his shortsword into my back. I was encumbered and did not have sow so things were looking very grim. Just as I was about to hit LoH, Adjantis graciously used his on me so I could reach the zone. Slate then immediately attacked Adjantis out of hatred and began beating on him as we both zoned to safety. I would like to thank Adjantis for his selfless act in the heat of battle. Its actions such as these that make the KoF shine. (sub / wit: Mortigan)
