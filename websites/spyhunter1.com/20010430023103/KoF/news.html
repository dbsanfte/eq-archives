March 27th, 2001

 

Member Invite Policy Change:

As you know, KoF has only allowed the invite of Paladins and Clerics. After much discussion with the officers (marshal and above), I have come to agree with the general opinion that KoF has reached a point where our lack of alternate classes is seriously inhibiting our ability to progress into higher level raids as a Guild. The Alliance with MoF is a pleasure and great benefit, however it is not providing enough alternate class support for the ventures KoF is striving to accomplish. The option to create further alliances has its down side - communication, organization and looting issues. Therefor, as Guild Leader, I have decided to open up the guild to all classes for conditional invite. This will include alternate charicters of current members. The written policy for this amendment needs to be reviewed by the Counsel before full implementation is made.

The new invite policy will most likely exclude Dark Elves, Trolls and Orges. It will also have a minimum level of possibly 20 (or higher) for classes other than Paladins and Clerics. It will require a referral from a current member 30 +. The final written details will be worked out by the Counsel and posted within the week.

If you know a player who is of "other classes", who have had an interest in joining ranks with KoF, go ahead and give them a tell and inform them of the coming policy changes. Have then submit an application and mention you as a reference. As always, the "quality" of the invited player is of high consideration.
