History: Born to a wealthy couple in felwithe Rastana was raised to know a life of pleasure and comfort. Early in his life he fancied himself as a future master of the wonderful arts of magic. He applied to the school of wizardy in Euridin and to his delight he was accepted. After speaking to his parents and gaining their approval preperations were made to make the long trip to Odus, and to Euridin.

Rastana and his parents traveled to the Kaladim Docks, and where headed to the far city of Qeynos when tragedy struck. Upon entering Kithicor Forest the family was approached by an old Knight, covered in his own blood. He warned the family that a curse lied upon the forrest greater than any he had seen during his travels. He advised that the forrest would not be safe for travel until around 8am. Rastana's family ignored the warning. Being masters in both the magicians, and wizards guilds they ventured on, believing in their own vanity to protect and guide them. As they went forth the Knight again called out his warning to heed the call of Tunare and await the dawn. Rastanas parents ventured on.

Now deep into the forrest Rastana's father noticed that his sentry had been tripped. They were being followed. Soon it became apparent that these undead were not simply wandering, that something was commanding them, leading them. They were an organized army. Without warning the caravan was hit. Rastana looked on as his personal tutors were shred asunder from a blast of frozen shards. His father immediatly responded sending a huge comet of ice into the frey of undead, blowing skeletons apart, and leaving the rotting corpses of zombies scattered upon the ground. Rastana's mother was fast in her arts also as a guardian of the earth was summoned to help in the frey. Looking on in awe of his parents mastery Rastana sit back and relaxed to enjoy the show.

The battle seem to be ending until a menace that evades Rastana still was upon them. With cruel accuracy the Dark Reaver engaged his mothers guardian. Within a few swings the Reaver had dispatched the guardian and headed towards her. Looking towards his Father he knew he would not be able to assit his mother as he pursued the undead away from the caravan to dispatch those remaining from the first wave. Rastana scrambled to memorize a spell, any spell to distract the Reaver long enough for his mother to call another guardian to her side. Opening his book of spells he scanned desperatly for one that therin would lie his mother redemption. Rastana's concentration was broken however as he heard the wailing of his mother. Looking upon the scene he watched as the Reaver bathed in the blood of his loved one, and dispatched the corpse thereof to the ground.

Hearing the cry of his wife, Rastana's father returned to the caravan. He was now upon the Reaver. Comets of ice, pillars of lightning, over and over they slammed into the Reaver felling him to the ground time after time. As the Reaver lay upon the ground, his strength failing him he began a chilling prayer. Within moments Rastana's father was struck with pain and suffering. Although not defeated he was felled to the ground unconcious. Rastana, cowering inside the provisions wagon of the caravan watched in horror as the Reaver regenerated part of its strength. As it arose to stand tall once more it began another incantation. With tear filled eyes Rastana watched as his mothers corpse was reanimated and ordered to kill his father. Paralized with fear Rastana watched helplessly as the abomination that was his mother bludgend his father to death. Her eyes then turned upon him. Rastana scrambled to jump from the wagon. Triping and falling to the ground Rastana landed at the feet of the Reaver. The Reaver raised him up and commanded his mother to slay her child. Rastana closed his eyes and rememberd the old knights warning. He began to mutter a prayer to Tunare, and in that instance light broke through the tops of the trees. Morning had come early. The undead, including his mother receeded into the ground as the Reaver, smiling evily at him released his hold on Rastana. Then all was silent.

Hours past until a loan Paladin named Melchisedec came upon the remains of the caravan. Having pity on Rastana he took him to North Freeport and the Hall of Truth. The Knights arranged a proper funeral for his father, but were unsucessful in finding the remains of his mother. Seeing their kindness Rastana stayed with them and learned the path of nobility and honor. He began to worship Tunare and found that more than his study of the arts of magic he wanted the head of the Reaver, destruction of all undead, and rest for his mother.

Over the years Rastana has had victories as well as defeats in his life. He was grieved at the loss of his longest friend Melchisedec. A few months after this event Rastana was blessed by Tunare to meet a young high elf named Atyana. Within a few weeks they were engaged to be married. As the time of the marriage approached though Atyana was slain by dark elfs lead by Kixdin Gix in the western commonlands. For awhile Rastana lost his faith in the gods, for how could one so loving allow such cruelties. Rastana receeded from the population of norrath, giving up all his wordly
possesions, and lived the life of a hermit for some time.

Many years passed living in solitude for Rastana. One night while he slept he was visited by Tunare and was told that he was blessed and favored of her. She expressed her need of his blade once more in the fight against the undead. New lands had been discovered, and the gnomes were currently working on reaching these lands. A host of undead evils would be released upon the world and that balance had to be restored. His blade was needed once more. With his hatred of the undead renewed Rastana returned to the eastern commonlands to seek information on these new lands. During his time here Rastana met a young knight by the name Demasus. Demasus realized the hurt and pain Rastana had seen in his life and showed kindness to him. Unbeknownst to Rastana was the guild that young Demasus belonged to. Bent on the destruction of all undead, Rastana had found a guild of Knights united in brotherhood against the undead evils that plauge norrath. Rastana soon joined the ranks of The Knights of Freedom and his real journey towards the destruction of all undead began.

During his first weeks of release from his unwalled prison Rastana met a young woman named Magdelynn. His heart came alive again with feelings he thought could never again be felt. The relationship was short lived though as Magdelynn recognized that there was someone Rastana was holding onto within his heart. Saying goodby to him Magdelynn retreated to the clerics of Tunare in North Freeport to live a life of service to Tunare in prayer and fasting for the love she knew she could not have. She remains with them to this day, praying that one day her love will return to her, but loving him enough to let him follow his own heart.

After many months with his new family Rastana learned that Tunare had showed mercy upon him once again. His lost love, Atyana had been reincarnated as a wood elf. Upon their first encounter Atyana remembered Rastana and was pleased to see him. Their relationship is now growing once more. Finally Rastana realized that with the love of Atyana, as well as the support and strength of the Knights of Freedom he would one day lay his mother to rest and destroy the evil that is the Reaver.
