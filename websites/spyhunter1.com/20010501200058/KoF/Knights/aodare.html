AWARDS RECEIVED (sub = submitted by / Wit = Witnessed)
       
Medal of Valor
  

Awarded on 02-19-01, at lvl 19, for dying and undoubtedly saveing the life of Conny in W Karana while they were attempting to help Aetheldar get back to W Karana for a CR.

The other day when Aodare was heading to Highpass Hold, Aetheldar asked for help from anyone who was close to the W Karana area. Apparently he had found Froon and Choon in W karana when he was oom and had no lay hands left. He died near where they usually hang out. He was bound in GFay of all places. So he needed to know if anyone could go there to try and get him a res. Aodare volunteered to go and pull his corpse to a safe res spot and ask for one for him. Conny and Herpally also sprung to action in helping out our fallen guildmate. Aodare, Herpally, and Conny all grouped together and went to find Aetheldar's corpse.

After a bit of searching we finally found it at the Camp of Froon and Choon. Aetheldar gave Aodare consent to pull his corpse and just as she was targeting it, Conny was attacked by Froon after her invis failed. She was being attacked and severly damaged. Herpally had lost us when we all split up to find Aetheldar's corpse. Knowing that she was Aetheldar's most likely way of getting back to his body, Aodare decided to try and mezmerize Froon. She knew full well that he was red to her and that her invis would fall as soon as she cast it on him. A message appeared stating that Froon was too high in level to have that spell used on him and he promptly turned around and started attacking Aodare. Aodare decided to try and pull Froon away from the area that Conny was in. She was able to get him far enough away for Conny to escape. Aodare died shortly after and Conny was able to get far enough away from Froon and forget about her. She was then able to Revive Aetheldar. If it was not for this selfless act there might have been a great deal more problems in trying to get Aetheldar back to his corpse in a timely manner.
