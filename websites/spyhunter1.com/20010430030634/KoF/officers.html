Officer Guides are provided as a tool for all KoF Officers. The intent here is to inform both Officers and Knights of the standards and practices involved in the KoF guild leadership. These guides are fairly detailed, however, they are not "all encompassing" and in no way should they supersede common sense, or sound judgment.

All Policy here is mandatory and shall be followed. Any deviation from Policy shall be reported to the Guild Leader for review (and approval, if time allows).

ADVANCEMENT POLICY

Levels 6 - 29 (Squire) May not hold an officer position, are undergoing a probationary period.
Levels 30 - 45 (Knight) Eligible to advance to any rank up to and including Captain or Bishop.
Levels 46+ (Elite Knight) Eligible to advance to any rank as appointed by the Guild Leader.

Squires are recommended for KoF Knight status by their Training Officer upon reaching lvl 30. They may obtain KoF Knighthood status only when approved by the Guild Leader. Squires will have a charicter picture on their Personal Page when Knighthood status is obtained.

All positions below Commander, in the Chain of Command, are class specific.

Officer Positions of Captain, Bishop or below are filled by application and review. The positions will be announced on the "News" page for eligible members to apply for via e-mail. All applications will be reviewed by the Elite Counsel and a vote will be conducted. The positions will be filled based on the vote of the Elite Counsel. Any voting deadlocks will be decided on by the Guild Leader.

All Positions, in the Chain of Command, above Captain or Bishop are filled only by appointment from the Guild Leader. Elite Counsel positions are appointed by the Guild Leader and approved by a vote of the Elite Counsel members.

OFFICER DUTY POLICY

 

General Duties for all KoF Officers:

  1. Maintain a strict walk on the Path of Nobility as an example to all.

  2. Recruit Noble and Trustworthy Paladins and Clerics for KoF membership.

  3. Observe and report to the Guild Leader all acts worthy of award performed by KoF members.

  4. Administer to Squires advice, warnings, reprimands or punishment as needed.

  5. Report to the Guild Leader any acts of Dishonor or Evil committed by any KoF members.

Duties assigned to all KoF Training Officers:

  1. Primary Duty: Recruit Noble and Trustworthy Paladins and Cleric Squire as outlined in the Recruiting Policy.
  2. Train your Squire as outlined in the Guidelines for Squire Training.

Captain/Bishop Duties :

  1. Primary Duty: Schedule and Host guild raids for members below lvl 30 once a week or as practical.
  2. Monitor, the performance of your section with regards to the Code of Conduct and Posted KoF guidelines.
  3. Report any deviations of KoF guidelines to the Commander, Elite Council or Guild Leader.

Marshall/Cardinal Duties:

  1. Primary Duty: Schedule and Host guild raids for members lvl 30 - 40 once a week or as practical.
  2. Monitor your subordinate Officers to ensure the they are following the Code and all KoF Guide lines.
  3. Report any deviations of KoF guidelines to the Commander, Elite Council or Guild Leader.

Commander Duties:

  1. Primary Duty: Schedule and Host guild raids for members lvl 40+ once a week or as practical.
  2. Warn or administer punishment to Knights or Squires who have deviated from the Path of Nobility or committed acts of Dishonor or Evil.
  3. Monitor all subordinate Knights to ensure the they are following the Code of Conduct and KoF Guide lines.
  4. Assist with KoF raids as needed.

Knight Elite Counsel:

  1. Primary Duty: Vote on revisions or additions to the KoF Code of Conduct, Policy, Guides and Procedures.
  2. Primary Duty: Appoint Guild Training Officers as needed.
  3. Review and sustain or reduce, as appropriate, all KoF member appeals concerning discipline.
  4. Evaluate and and suggest revisions or additions to any and all KoF Code, Policy or Guides, when needed.
  5. Organize Special Raids for Knights who request crusade items or NPC killings.
  6. Assist in all Events and Raids as needed.

Officer inactivity for more than one month, without prior approval from the Guild Leader, may result in your position being turned over to another active and qualified Knight.

GUILD INVITE POLICY

 

  1. Absolutely NO "Necromancers" or "Shadowknights" will be considered for invite.
    All other classes and races are welcome to join KoF provided they meet the minimum levels for invite as outlined below.

  2. All invites must be accompanied by a "Join Application" via the Web Site. (This could be approved or un-approved depending on the situation as outlined below.)

  3. The inviting Officer shall inspect the prospective member prior to activating the /guildinvite command to ensure that the person to be invited is not wearing any disguises to hide his true race / class and that the player meets the required level needed for invite. Players heavily "Twinked" for their level shall be referred to the Elite Counsel or Guild Leader for invite. (Example: if he's at level 25 and wearing better stuff than Dami.)

  4. New members will be assigned to a Guild Training Officer or Branch as appropriate for their class.

  5. Inviting a player into the Guild is done by typing "/guildinvite" followed by the players name. The invited player is given a box on his screen that asks him to join. They can select "Yes" or "No". The player to be invited must be in the same zone as you are in order to invite them.

  6. E-mail the Guild Leader informing him of any newly invited members. The E-mail shall report the name, lvl, class of the player you activated the /guildinvite command for.

Minimum level for invite:

  • Any and all Paladins or Clerics: Level 10
  • Any and all classes other than Paladin or Clerics: Level 20
  • Memeber's Alternate charicters: level 20 (This is to avoid and influx of alts that never get played.)
  • Real Life immediate family of a member: Level 6 (This is to promote family unity.)

Paladins and Clerics:

  1. All Officers may activate the /guildinvite command for Paladins and Clerics, who are under lvl 26, without an APPROVED "Join Application" from Dami. The application do not need to be APPROVED, however it must be submitted before the /guildinvite command is activated.

    Prior to invite the KoF Officer shall insure that the player to be invited has:

    • Submitted a Join Application via the web site.
    • Reviewed and agrees to the Code of Conduct.
    • Reached the minimum level for invite.
  2. All Paladins and Clerics level 26 or higher must have a PRE-APPROVED application from Dami.

    If necessary, have the prospective member camp and visit the web site to review the Code of Conduct before activating the invite command. Give out the KoF site address in this fashion: "www dot spyhunter 1 dot com slash KoF". Remember that other persons will not see dots or slash symbols in the game chat window.

All Other Classes:

  1. All players of classes other than Paladins or Clerics must be recommended by a of KoF or MoF Officer in order to join. This recommendation should be noted in the player's "Join Application". When referring a player for invite, have them specifically state your name and that they were recommended to join by you in the Join Application.

    Officers should use discretion when giving out a referral for a member. Take some time to get to know the prospective invite. Chat with them a while or assign an appropriate quest. Take note of their attitude and demeanor. Remember, our members reflect on us all as a guild. Look for quality in the person, not just the character.

  2. All classes other than Paladins or Clerics must be PRE-APPROVED by the Guild Leader before the /guildinvite command is activated. This approval is done via the "Join Application" on the web site. Once an application is received, if it is approved, it will be forwarded to all Officers. All Officers receiving an APPROVED "Join Application" forwarded from Dami Valorton may activate the /guildinvite command for that player regardless of the class or level. On occasion, certain conditions may be specified in the forwarded E-mail.

GUIDE FOR SQUIRE TRAINING

Guidelines for Squire Training:

  1. Training Officers should have no more than 4 Squires under their training command at one time. As a Training Officer, the players you recruit will be assigned to you until you have 4 total active Squires. Any Squires you recruit while you already have 4 in training shall be assigned to another available Training Officer who has less than 4 Squires. If none are available than another Training Officer will be commissioned from the rank of Knights.

  2. Do not "Twink" your Squires. ("Twinking" is regarded as giving a player items which are far above his lvl of achievement.)

  3. Teach your Squires how to walk the Path of Nobility.

  4. Power leveling is permitted, however take care to give equal time to each of your Squires.

  5. Report the the Guild Leader all acts performed which deserve recognition or award.

  6. Administer and report disciplinary actions as needed.

  7. Monitor, track and report to the Guild Leader all advancements in your Squire's leveling progress.

  8. When your Squire reaches lvl 30, submit a report to the Guild Leader recommending his approval or denial for full KoF Knight status. (The Guild Leader has final say concerning all denials.)

  9. After Completion of an Squire Knight's training, the Training Officer should take sufficient time off (Vacation) to continue his own personal leveling progress. Generally 2-3 levels of vacation is advised. Requests for longer Vacations should be made to the Guild Leader.

GUIDE FOR AWARDS

Guidelines for Awards:

  1. All awards shall be reviewed and approved by the Guild Leader prior to being presented.

  2. All Officers Shall record and forward to the Guild Leader any and all details concerning reports from players of other guilds, or players of no guild, which concern a KoF member involved in an act which the other player feels to be worthy of recognition, praise or award.

  3. All Knights (including Squire Knights) are eligible to receive awards.

  4. All Knights (including Squires) may submit, at any time, to the Guild Leader for consideration of awards to be presented to themselves or other members as outlined on the Awards Page.

  5. Multiple awards of the same type may be received by a member.

  6. It is the responsibility of the reporting member to provide all the necessary information to the Guild Leader. This includes providing the name of the recipient, witness (if required), Date, location, details of the event or act and the name of the award you feel is deserved.

DISCIPLINE AND PUNISHMENT POLICY

Discipline:

All Guild Officers are responsible for monitoring and advising all Knights concerning violations of Nobility, the Code of Conduct or any posted KoF Policy. Officers should first politely and respectfully advise the offending Knight(s) accordingly and then, if needed, advise the Guild Leader concerning the incident. All acts of Dishonor or Evil shall be reported to the Elite Council or Guild Leader.

Officers shall not administer or conduct conversation regarding discipline or punishment via Guild Chat - Direct Tells shall be utilized.

Only KoF Officers may assign punishment to Squires. A Squire has the right to appeal any punishment assigned. All appeals from Squires concerning discipline or punishment should be addressed to an Elite Council member who will bring up the appeal to the entire counsel The Counsel will decide to sustain or reduce the punishment as they see fit. Their decision is final.

Only the Commander, Elite Counsel or Guild Leader may administers punishment to Knights level 30 or higher. All appeals for a punishment administered to Knight level 30 or higher will be reviewed by the Elite Counsel for final say. The Guild Leader has final say concerning all maters involving Elite Counsel Members. It is the responsibility of the Knight being punished to initiate his or her appeal via E-mail or in-game tell.

Banishment is administered only by the Guild leader.

If an officers receives a report of Misconduct, that officer should immediately attempt to clarify the incident via witnesses. Ask the reporting person for the names of his group members (if grouped) or of any witnesses. Write down their names and send tells to each witness asking them politely if they can elaborate on the incident. If non-concurring evidence is found concerning the report, then inform the reported violator about the accusations and attempt to resolve the issue by making apologies for the misunderstanding regardless of the facts. If concurring evidence is found to establish misconduct then proceed to ask the reported violator to explain his actions. If you are satisfied that the incident was a misunderstanding then attempt to resolve the issue by having the reported person make an outright apology regardless of the facts. If it appears an actual violation or act of dishonor occurred, then take actions/punishment necessary and report the incident to the Guild Leader. Include all actions and witnesses interviewed.

Disputes, Continued:

Disagreements or anger with another person’s actions is expected to happen from time to time. How you choose to handle the incident is a big reflection on your commitment to the Path of Nobility. As a Knight you should already have thought out a plan of action on how you will react in a manner which will reflect the Path of Nobility you have chosen to walk. Anger is understandable, however it is not acceptable to lash out with your anger in the moments that follow. In KoF, Nobility demands a polite, calm and tactful attitude when communicating your disagreement or anger. Nobility runs both ways. If you fall victim to a person’s anger, KoF Nobility demands understanding a polite, calm and tactful reply.

It is the responsibility of all Officers to squash any and all disputes that are being conducted in Guild Chat. Have the members take it to tells and offer to hear both sides and suggest a remedy or offer advice. Be carful not to get emotionally involved. One can only be truly helpful if one remains objective.

For the benefit of the guild, the Round Table shall not be used to work out a disagreement or administer discipline. Knights must keep in mind that the Round Table is a public forum, viewed by many, and reflects substantially on our guild and the members.

Punishments:

For a violation of, or deviation from, the Path of Nobility, these forms of punishments are recommended and approved:

1. (levels: 10 - 19) Gathering 4 stacks of 20 bone chips to be deposited in the KoF Vault.

2. (levels: 20 - 30) Gathering 24 Derv rings (3 back packs) to be deposited in the KoF Vault.

3. (levels 30 +) Gathering 20 Fairie Dusts or other quest items to be deposited in the KoF Vault.

For a violation of, or deviation into, the Path of Dishonor, these forms of punishments are recommended and approved:

1. (levels: 10 - 19) A gathering of bone chips and a fine imposed of 100 - 500 pp to be deposited into the KoF Vault ASAP.

2. (levels: 20 - 30) A gathering of Derv rings and a fine if 500 - 1200 PP , or equivalent item value, to be deposited into the KoF Vault ASAP.

3. (levels 30 +) A gathering 20 Fairie Dusts or other quest items and a fine of 1200 - 3000 PP, or equivalent item value, to be deposited into the KoF Vault ASAP.

For a violation of, or deviation into, the Path of Evil, Officers should contact the Commander, Elite Council or the Guild Leader for advice.
