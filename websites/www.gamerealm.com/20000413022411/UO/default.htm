03-7-00
Because of the seemingly slow process of moving into our new domain, I have opened a temporary message board. You can find the link here: TLH Discussion Board  or at the top and left sides of the page.

Now for a LH update:
-The realm of Hannah heats up as a few new features are added to the server. Keep your eyes peeled! 
-A new boat fix was posted, and it seems that almost all boats are working now. The boats that don't work at this time include the dragon boats, and the large boat. All other boats should have full functionality by now, and not fall apart. Big thanks to the TUS dev team and Natha for getting that fix up!
-Are you ready for Necromancy? WE ARE! Keep your eyes peeled, cause it's coming!
-In the "Bring as many permanent players to TLH" Contest, Magus currently leads the way with 16! Keep sending Dupre your signups on ICQ at 56072218.
-The staff application has also been fixed, Sorry to those who filled out that huge form and couldn't send it.

03-2-00
This is 2 days late, but I want to congratulate everyone on the shard for a successful month! In our first full month, we have successfully conquered all of our major bugs, put together many new scripts, and best of all, acquired hundreds of unique signups. Expect the second month to be exciting, as we can now begin to focus on adding some new features.

Currently, we are waiting on the highly anticipated TUS server version .48g. This version will allow us to add many features that Menace has helped us out personally with. We have some of these features all ready to go, just awaiting the new server.

Now that we are beginning our feature expansion, lets push for user expansion as well! The contest is continuing through this month: Bring the most unique users and you will win a special prize from Admin Dupre. Simply ICQ him at 56072218 and let him know of your new user, and he will keep a running count.

Finally, we are currently in phase 2 of the implementation of our races. We are tweaking the abilities of the races and we want your input! We have already gotten some great feedback, and this is what we need! Send us your requests, nags, or whatever to nick@kscable.com and we'll look it over.

Thank you for your support,
-Nicholas

03-2-00
A few wanderers in the lands claim they too, have spotted the Successor. Many claims have been made as to what the Successor looks like. He has now been spotted in Deceit, and much of the Northern Territory. Be on the look out for his presence, as he may be anywhere.

02-29-00
The Successor shows itself for the first time. In an attempt to scare the populous, he returned to Deceit, where his wrath was unparallel. His ability to deceive, much like Guardian, showed no mercy. The chosen 4, Magus, Blackbeard, Vahn, and Seeker, came upon the sudden call of duty to defeat the Successor's army, and were rewarded for their quest. Great Job! 

Join us for the next quest in the Lost Heavens!
-Nicholas

02-22-00
We ran a few updates last night that have fixed a couple of minor bugs. First, boats are now fixed. And for the most part, they work pretty well. Second, we fixed a mapping bug that was being exploited and caused some mishaps on the outer sides of town.

-Nicholas

02-21-00
A new form has been put up in the UO menu to the left. It is the Staff Application. If you feel that you would be an asset to the team, then apply! We are looking for trustworthy, hard workers! 

-Nicholas

Secondly, pre-registration is now open! But why PRE-register?? This way, it gives us an estimate of how many people will be joining us on the launch date tomorrow. In the unfortunate event that we have a flood of people, we may have to put a restriction on accounts until the flood dies. 

What does this mean to you? Register NOW! Ensure that your account is going to be up and running when the shard goes public!

On another note, we will be hosting player resources that deal with race, guild and social aspects of the game on this site. These include player documented tutorials, guild requirements, special tips, etc. Once you write your article, send it to nick@gamerealm.com for review.

We'll be looking forward to seeing you on TLH,

Nicholas
