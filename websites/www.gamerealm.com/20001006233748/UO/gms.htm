Game Masters

Priorities
GMs answer time-sensitive calls first, they prioritize their calls accordingly. Harassment and exploitation calls require an immediate response and GMs will attempt to respond to those calls as they are made. The GMs will assist with other serious, time-sensitive circumstances as they arise. High priority calls include the following situations.

1. Hate Mongering, sexual harassment or racial slurs. 

2. Your character is physically stuck and unable to move

3. Players are abusing a loophole in the game

For clarification, we define this as a player utilizing an unintended loophole in the game system to gain an unfair advantage over another player or players. These techniques fall outside the range of normal game play. In the case of loophole calls, the GMs must witness the use of the loophole before they can intervene. They will investigate the call and if they witness the loophole they will take appropriate action against the exploiting player. A few examples of past and present loopholes include:

a. Town Killing
b. Duplication of Items
c. Utilization of weapons with inflated damage modifiers
d. Illegal house break-ins


Examples of help that can be provided directly
GMs are also able to help in certain instances because they can immediately assess and verify the problem. For instance, a GM can also be of assistance in the following situations.
a. Your spell book crashes
b. You have built a house on top of an existing house or have miss placed the building


Unverifiable Complaints
Unfortunately, GMs cannot verify every complaint. For example, GMs cannot track character's stats or ownership histories, so they cannot help with those kind of losses. Therefore, a GM will not respond to non-verifiable issues such as:

1. You wish to change your character name
2. You have lost your keys
3. You want to be resurrected
4. You want to be teleported from one location to another
5. You have lost items
6. Your pack horse has vanished
7. You have lost points in a skill or stat
8. Items in your house are missing or have decayed
9. You were accidentally resurrected with penalties and want to be restored to your previous state
10. You lost items or time due to server downtime


GM or Counselor Complaints
It is our goal to maintain the game and help those who truly need help from a GM. Due to the amount of players vs GMs not all pages will be answered, only those that need a response. Be assured that all pages are being looked at and if it is a problem that truly needs attention, it will be given. However pages that are a waste of time will be removed and you will get no answer. If you have any complaints or concerns about a GM please contact Samson. samson@gamerealm.com
