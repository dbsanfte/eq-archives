Karma

Karma has confused us from the time we first found out it had been introduced into the game.  We've muddled through, listened to what Vircom had to say, and figured out a lot on our own.  We still do not have the full story, but we will try and get you "Karma Smart" in the following paragraphs.  First, will print what Vircom released about Karma.  After that, you will find out what we have learned and some tips.

Vircom's Words on Karma

Newswire for September 1, 1999
(14:00 EDT) There has been a great deal of noise around karma in the last few weeks. We feel it's time we clear up the air about that "invisible" attribute.

       Karma was introduced in the server last fall, but was not used until a while later (late November). Because of a server-side bug, player characters created during that time were given a karma of -100 instead of the expected default value of 0. The problem was fixed last winter, but a retrofit to update the -100 karma of all characters already in the database to 0 was not run. (And we apologize for this blunder.)

       Since negative karma values represent evilness and positive values represent goodness, the characters created during that period started evil. Now that karma is fully implemented and in use with two exclusive sets of good and evil quests, it has made it difficult for these characters to follow the "good" storyline.

       There are "alignment" levels in the game: evil, unaligned and good. Unaligned is defined as values still relatively close to 0. But -100 is to far from 0 and is considered evil.

       There are some in-game ways for evil characters to redeem themselves, although some actions are permanently recorded (which will block a character from completing both evil and good quests). There are numerous "small karmic" quests that are simply too irrelevant to absolve an evil character though; these quests only give unaligned character a little push toward the path of light. (Hint: Artherk will favor those who financially help his church, regardless of his current karma.)

       Lastly, karma is and will remain an "invisible" attribute. It is meant as a tool for NPCs to know at a glance what kind of person you are, or are reputed to be. Still, while you cannot see your karma value, there are NPCs who can tell you what it is (roughly).  (Hint: he's big, he's red and he theoretically breathes fire.)

Player's Discoveries

Ok...is our turn to talk about what we have learned to get you "Karma Smart".

How do I know if I have Good or Bad Karma?  Since we cannot see our numbers, the only way we can guess at it is by which NPCs will talk to you and which ones won't.  Many NPCs will tell you which way your karma needs to go for them to talk with you.  I know, that isn't an answer, but is the best we can do right now.  Odds are that you are on the good path.  It seems the bad path takes more work, so you probably have a positive number.  (Note:  GMs can see our Karma number.)

Do I have to worry about Karma?  For normal gameplay, no.  For doing the quests in the game, for the most part, yes.  Some quests, like newbie ones, are karma-free.

What actions affect my Karma?  Killing certain monsters or NPCs will increase or decrease your Karma.  Jarko raises Karma, Mhorgwloth and the Orc Renegade Leader both lower karma.  The 3 of these will give you an onscreen message when they do raise or lower your karma.  There may be a 4th...another that raises karma, but haven't found him yet (possibly Balork).  All normal monsters in the game are considered neutral...thus, your karma is not affected at all.  Killing a horse or antelope will not affect your karma as many have thought.  However, killing a Forest Guardian does "flag" you.  Does not change karma, but if you are doing certain quests, you will have to "atone" for your sins.  Many of the "deeds" you do can raise or lower karma.  We have no clue what all of these are...but, Vircom did say giving money to the church is a good thing.  Also, appears returning Sunrock's diamond to him and Jarko's spellbook is a good thing.  Just take a moment and think about what you are doing...if it is good or bad.  If you go to Thieve's town and do some gambling, you might find you have a lower karma number.

Stored Karma?  We now know that some actions you do that affect karma are "stored".  They are not reflected on your karma number until they are "released".  To release this stored karma, go kill Jarko to release the good karma.  To release bad karma, our guess is kill either the Orc Renegade Leader or Mhorgwloth.  This is fairly new info, so we are still trying to figure out even more on this.  But we do now know there is stored karma that must be released to affect your number.
