Character Races 

Your race will determine many factors in the world. How you act around and with other races, who you socialize with, and your place in the grand scheme of things.

Your race lends a great deal towards your characters personality.  What will you be?  A giantman who looks down on other races because they are smaller and weaker? or a dwarf who feels that anyone who doesn't live underground has some kind of mental problem?

Each race has it's own abilities and shortfalls.  It is a difficult choice, a choice that will shape the very core of your characters being.

What will you choose?

Dark Elf

Elves, though similar to mortal men, differ in a number of important ways. They are lithe and agile, with little body hair, fine features and unmarred skin. Elves are much more resistant than men to the raves of time and disease. They are able to withstand a greater range of temperature extremes, and their senses are extremely keen. Because of their extended lifespan, they suffer from a natural lack of discipline.

Studier and darker than their fair cousins, the Dark Elves are great builders and craftspeople. Skilled in fine metalwork and weaponsmithing, they are also possessed with a great love of knowledge and learning.

As with each race, Dark Elves have bonuses and penalties attributed to their statistics. Look at the bottom of the page for a comparison chart of the races.

Dwarf

Little is known about the dwarven cultures in Hannah, as few dwarves leave their homes for a life of adventure. Dwarves have a natural resistance to certain forms of Elemental magic, but they are less adept at using it.

As with each race, Dwarves have bonuses and penalties attributed to their statistics. Look at the bottom of the page for a comparison chart of the races.

Elf

Elves, though similar to mortal men, differ in a number of important ways. They are lithe and agile, with little body hair, fine features and unmarred skin. Elves are much more resistant than men to the raves of time and disease. They are able to withstand a greater range of temperature extremes, and their senses are extremely keen. Because of their extended lifespan, they suffer from a natural lack of discipline.

More numerous and rustic than their two higher kin, elves have ruddy complexions with sandy hair and light eyes. They are slight and lithe, moving about the woodlands and hills virtually undetected, garbed in forest green or brown clothes

Giantmen

Giantmen are huge, averaging 7' tall. They are also extremely strong and hardy. A variety of giantman cultures exist on Hannah, and many are quite civilized and noble. Others are more barbaric. Giantmen are not, however, unintelligent. Their physical appearance also varies, though they tend to have dark hair and eyes. They are great warriors because of their physical prowess.

Half-Elves

Elves, though similar to mortal men, differ in a number of important ways. They are lithe and agile, with little body hair, fine features and unmarred skin. Elves are much more resistant than men to the raves of time and disease. They are able to withstand a greater range of temperature extremes, and their senses are extremely keen. Because of their extended lifespan, they suffer from a natural lack of discipline.

The progeny of human and elf parents, they are sometimes looked down upon by both races. Similar in most atributes to Giantmen, they also share the elves' quickness, agility, and low discipline.

Halfling

Their culture tends to be simple and rustic, and they value good drink, companions and freshly-baked tarts. Halflings have little physical strength, but no race can match their dexterity. They also have remarkable resistance to elemental magic.

Human

Humans on Hannah, like those on Earth, vary in their appearance. Human cultures on Hannah also vary wildly, though they tend to be urban folk. They have no remarkable strengths or weaknesses in their stats, and they are adept at any profession.

Sylvankind

Elves, though similar to mortal men, differ in a number of important ways. They are lithe and agile, with little body hair, fine features and unmarred skin. Elves are much more resistant than men to the raves of time and disease. They are able to withstand a greater range of temperature extremes, and their senses are extremely keen. Because of their extended lifespan, they suffer from a natural lack of discipline.

Related to elves, Sylvankind are extremely rare. Females are the most numerous, though males are not unheard of. They have pale complexions with sandy hair and light eyes. They are slight and light, moving about the woodlands and hills virtually undetected, garbed in forest green or brown.
