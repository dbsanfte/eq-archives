The Lost Heavens Storyline:

In the depths of the night, Guardian’s death cries shrieked out. His death, reluctant and sudden. As your Blade Slash's through what life he had left the World trembled. You, the Avatar, have defeated the vile creature known as the Guardian, stopping him in his quest to rule Britannia. As the Guardian drew upon his last breath and then faded to the depths of the Abyss, another has stepped up to take his place, this so-called Successor.

He took on the Guardians identity while you left to return to your home known as Earth. If you only knew of the terror that he would bring forth you would have never left, even if you had known of his existence.

A new light shines upon Britain, where peace is now in abundance. You, the Avatar, have since returned to Earth, having completed your quest, or so you thought. And the "GUARDIAN" now tries to conquer what his predecessor failed. And no one has a clue. YET...

This "new" Guardian is using the powers of Necromancy and the powers of the Levels of hell to conquer this land known as Britain. And with your most of your companions long gone there is no one left to protect it. Even our dear Lord British has no clue on the peril that secretly roams our fair land.

You must now find a way back! You must also use purity against evil. Henceforth you must learn to harness the powers of the 7 rings of Heaven. Since the rings also exist in the same place at the same time anything you do to one affects them all. Britain is located in the 3rd ring known as Hannah.

…The 7 rings of heaven, from which the after life are born, shine over Britannia in alignment. These heavens, exact mirrors of Britannia, are now under threat of corruption. The Successor, in his attempt to corrupt Britannia, has set his eyes on Hannah, the 3rd realm in alignment…

…This is where your quest begins.

You are born free into the heavens, where you must join forces with those around you. In a land that strives on peace, honesty, and loyalty, you must strive….

…With a new light shines a new darkness, the heavens depend upon you!
