Welcome HackersQuest
We will be hosting the new test server for the Everquest Emulator being built by HackersQuest and we here at gamerealm wanted to give the HackersQuest staff and players a big welcome, I know we all look forward to the completion of the Everquest Emulator. For those HackerQuest watchers who are curious the new Test Server will be on a P III class machine with its own dedicated T3 connection, we hope to have the server up and running for Ashran by tonight or tomorrow at the very latest.
