MAD'S PAD

 

Hell-o and welcome to my cool new abode.   Gamerealm graciously offered me this spot on your cyberdial, and I intend to take advantage of them.. err it. *grin*

My plans for this page include indepth tips for newbies to The 4th Coming, as well as some advanced concepts for vereterans. I find with every RPG I have encountered, it always seems that after a few days/weeks of play, a person then begins to understand and appreciate where the optimum path for perfection lies. Only after learning first hand where experience points are best distributed, what weapons cost and require, etc, can one then return to the character creation process, and make the wisest choices during those crucial first few levels.

That would be a good point to return here, for I am doing some of the work for you =). In the Advanced Tips section, you will find concepts that would confuse a newbie, but be valuableto someone looking to improve their character before they killed a single rat. Of course, if you really want to be prepared before you even try t4c, then a bit of study could yield positive results.

If you are just going to jump right in, I would encourage you to at least check out the manual. It is short and valuable beyond its bytes. My Newbie Tips section is designed to familiarize you with the kinds of situations that exists in The 4th Coming. Forehand knowledge can vastly improve your enjoyment of the game. If you can avoid some of the more common early  mistakes, you will have an easier time leveling, and possessing better and better weapons as you do. Nothing is more frustrating than falling short one attribute point, which holds you back from adventuring further.

I will be updating this page as time goes along, adding more and new relevant material. Keep in mind that as of today, t4c is still a beta, and therefore, come final release, some (let's hope not all) of this information will become irrelevant. So stay in touch!

Good adventuring to you!
