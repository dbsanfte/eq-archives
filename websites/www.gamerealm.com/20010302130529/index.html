State of GameRealm and T4C
posted by shane on Friday February 16, @08:35PM
from the T4C dept.
I don't have the best news right now and I will try to explain this as best I can. T4C is not making money and there is a lot of overheard expense. I have fought to keep the servers up and running but we can no longer justify the cost. Today as we were putting the finishing touches on Chaos II we ran into some more problems and the whole cost issue once again came up.

The main server is currently costing us close to four grand a month (salary, bandwidth, maintenance, etc) and we are not seeing any incoming cash flow and the operating costs can no longer be justified. I have been told to scrap Chaos and we have sixty days to find a solution or we will be scraping the Main server as well. As I doubt we can get enough paying users to subscribe to a monthly fee GameRealm is currently seeking to sell both the Main server and the Chaos server and in the worse case scenario we will sell both servers back to Vircom.

It is my goal to make sure the Main server is sold to someone who will be able to pickup the Main server license and to keep the server running so users are not affected by a change over beyond the time to do the switch. If this goal can not be met then we will do our best to work with other servers and Vircom to get our users moved as I don't want to see any characters lost.

The GameRealm Main T4C server is one of the busiest North American servers with peak times averaging about 150 to 200 players online at once as well as having over three thousand subscribed users. This is the more valued server and a selling price would have to be negotiated.

The Chaos T4C server is completely empty and GameRealm will sell it for the listing price of $10,000.

GameRealm will continue to run portal sites for different games and will be in search of new game servers for the future with a business plan that can sustain the day to day operating costs. If you would like to enquire about buying either our Main server license or our Chaos Server license please direct your emails to sbenson@gamerealm.com.

Shadowbane beta starts today!
posted by shane on Monday February 05, @08:51PM
from the All dept.
Source:Shadowbane WolfPack Studios, Inc. Announces Phase One Beta Test of Shadowbane

AUSTIN, TX - February 5th, 2001 - Online game developer Wolfpack Studios, Inc. announced today that the company has entered the first phase of beta testing for their flagship title, Shadowbane. Shadowbane is the culmination of two years development effort and is designed to allow tens of thousands of gamers to play concurrently in a single game session. Hailed as one of the most anticipated titles in the burgeoning Massively-Multiuser Online Gaming marketplace, Shadowbane offers players the unique opportunity to cooperatively and competitively vie for political and economic control of a virtual world.

"This is a huge leap forward for our team," said Josef Hall, founder and president of WolfPack Studios, Inc. "Today marks a unique turning point on our journey from 'development house' to 'entertainment provider'."

In Shadowbane, players are challenged to carve out their destiny in a world blanketed by political unrest and torn apart by storms, disease, starvation and an endless war. Players can assume the roles of warlords, kings and powerful wizards and interact with each other in a dynamic, fully interactive 3D environment.

Similar in many ways to a large-scale 3D Chat system, Shadowbane will allow players to connect via the Internet in order to unite, form armies, build kingdoms and conquer virtual territories. "It's not like a typical computer game," explains J. Todd Coleman, Wolfpack's Vice President of Sales Marketing, "Most games top out at a few dozen concurrent players. With potentially hundreds of thousands of subscribers, this will end up looking more like a living simulation of feudal Europe."

This beta test follows an arduous three month period of internal alpha testing of both the client and the server codebases. "We'll be executing the beta test in multiple stages," explains Keith Brewer, Director of Production Rollout for the Shadowbane title. "The first phase will consist of around fifty users, testing the character creation and advancement systems. From there we'll continue adding more testers as necessary to achieve the specific goals we have for each phase."

Shadowbane will be distributed throughout North America by Gathering of Developers and throughout Western Europe by Swing! Entert@inment Media AG.
