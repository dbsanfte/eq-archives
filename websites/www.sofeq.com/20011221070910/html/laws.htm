Before you decide to apply to join us, read over this page carefully and be certain you both understand and can comply with our requirements. 

If you have any questions, feel free to email me, Duerana.

When you are finished, please follow the link at the bottom and submit an application through our forums.

 

Requirements:

 

  1. As mobility and the capacity to rapidly deploy is a key factor in the raid oriented world of Everquest, Spirits of Fire has certain standards to which all guild members must adhere.

    a - Wakening Lands key at a MINIMUM, prefer Cobalt Scar key.
    b - Sebelis Key.
    c - 100+ skill in sense heading.
    d - 150+ skill in SWIMMING.
    e - Ability to Jboot or SOW oneself (potions etc.)
    f - Maps printed of all major zones along with a knowledge of how to get between them
    g - A note on being considerate: These standards are in effect to allow the guild to raid zones quickly and efficiently. If a raid is slowed by you and someone loses a rare/epic spawn etc. then the guild as a whole has paid a price for a member’s lack of preparedness. Please ensure that you meet these guidelines, otherwise you WILL be left behind.

  2. Faction
    SoF is on Claws of Veeshan / Thurgadin faction (we kill giants). If you are currently on another faction path, you must be willing to change to CoV faction. Faction requirement is for main characters only, alts can be whatever you choose.

  3. Level requirements: The current level minimum for SoF is 40.

    We have chosen to set the level at this for several reasons:

    a - It ensures that the people applying to the guild have demonstrated a devotion to the game and will likely continue playing for a period of time.
    b - It prevents people from gaining a bad reputation and then just starting over with a ‘twink’ and joining a new guild.
    c - It helps to ensure that those that have reached this level have a knowledge of the game, it’s zones, common tactics etc.
    d - It allows all members to attend the majority of joint raids – thereby making sure we can all group with each other and have fun.

  4. Member requirements:
    a - Profile on EQ.CASTERSREALM.COM detailing your current equipment etc. This is REQUIRED so the guild leader can allocate loot properly.
    b - Participation in the guild message forums IS REQUIRED. This is where we discuss raid strategies and times as well as topics that affect the guild as a whole. Failure to login and participate WILL RESULT IN REMOVAL FROM THE GUILD.
    c - As above, but with regards to the members email database. Please check your private mail.
    d - We do NOT recruit from other guilds. If you wish to join SoF you are REQUIRED to be guildless for a minimum of two weeks before application. If you have applied to SoF and are found to be in another guild you will be declined membership. We are not headhunters and do not scalp other guilds. Period.
    e - A level of participation is expected, if you join and do not assist in guild events etc. you will be declined membership. Level of participation is at the discretion of the guild leader.

  5. Equipment requirements. New members must be in possession of at least a moderate resist kit (50+ avg. on saves overall). Equipment must be on par with what is considered ‘average’ for the level of the character.

  6. Loot Rules
    a - Loot from guild raids is allocated by the guild leader and those he chooses to seek counsel from. It is allocated in a manner which he feels will benefit the guild the the most.
    b - To avoid possible exploitation of guild resources, new initiates are not eligible for loot drops until their probation period is completed.
    c -  New initiates are also not eligible for Epic raid help until their probation period is up.

  7. Process for full membership:
    a - Apply for membership. Detail the following in your post:
        i. Class and level.
        ii. Play time available (timezone, hours etc).
        iii. Anyone you know from the guild, recommendations etc.
        iv. Previous guild affiliations.
        v. Time /played.
        vi. Large achievements: IE High trade skills, epic completion, etc.

    b. Contact an initiation council member for an interview. The responsibility for finding an IC member is YOURS. Your interview must be completed within 2 weeks or your application will be filed as inactive.  

    Who to seek out for an interview 

    McKenna (Mistress of Initiates)

    Charisi 

    Euenyil

    Falelinax 

    Jazinar

    Lucane 

    Moonwolf 


    c. The class leader will contact you in game for an interview. This process will test your overall knowledge of your class and ensure that you are an asset to SoF. Possible questions could include common strategies of the class (Fear Kiting, FD pulling, Cannidancing etc.), common class loot questions (what armor are you wearing and why), or game mechanics questions (agro control, class responsibilities during raids etc.).

    d. Fulfill the membership requirements of the guild (castersrealm profiles etc, message base useage etc.).

    e. Attend no less than 5 guild functions and play consistently so you can learn about SoF and we can learn about you.

  8. note: The probation period is a minimum of 30 days to a maximum of 60 days at which time you will either become a full member, if all requirements have been met, or you will be declined membership if all requirements have not been met. The probation period may be waived or shortened at the discretion of the council and guild leader. It is up to you to become a member.

 

 

General   Council    Initiates   Loot Policy

General

  • Be aware at all times that you bear the name of your guild wherever you go!

    • remember that your actions reflect upon other guild members

    • refrain from excessive swearing, using belligerent or disrespectful language in an open channel (talk to your friends however you see fit, just be certain to respect others).  ***Disrespecting a fellow guild member WILL lead to removal***

    • anyone caught purposefully kill stealing will be removed from the guild

    • anyone caught purposefully training others will be removed from the guild

    • dueling for fun or honour is permitted; however challenging someone to a duel without just cause is grounds for a review and possible removal

  • Do not use the anonymous function, use the roleplay function instead.

    • Usually the anonymous function is used by people who wish to commit acts that they do not want to get back to their guild....This is grounds for guild removal.

    • The roleplay function also will show your presence when someone does a "who all Spirit"

  • This guild is NOT a guild for those who wish to be given items & cash or power leveled.  We frown upon those that would pester other members to do this for them.  Constantly requesting either of these WILL lead to your removal.

  • DO NOT spam the guild channel. Friendly conversation is ok, but mindless constant chatter WILL lead to your removal.

  • We expect our members to act in a mature and respectful way at all times.  We are here to have fun and relax.  We do not appreciate anyone who interferes with our ability to do that.

  • A member may remove themselves from the guild at any time they wish, but they may not return to the guild without full Council approval.  

    • So be VERY certain you wish to leave before you do, as chances are that you will not be allowed back unless there are extenuating circumstances!!!

    • If the member quits the guild a second time, they will not be permitted reentry into the guild.

    • The removal of a guild member after this period of time must first be passed in High Council.

  • Alternate characters 

    • There are no restrictions on the number of alts you are allowed to have in the guild; however, alternates MUST be a minimum of 20th level before they can be permitted entry.  This minimum is in place to keep too many "throwaway" characters from joining the guild.

  • Members/Initiates missing in action 

    • Anyone away from playing for more than two weeks should post and let the guild know before they take the time out. 

    • This is to help prevent having people simply disappearing from the guild without letting us know why or that they did, then return belonging to someone else or even turn up doing nasty things while still running the SoF tag.

    • As a result, if anyone goes MIA for more than 2 weeks without letting us know why we will be removing them from the roster, having their access to the forums revoked,  and Council will be watching for them to be online at which point a /guildremove will be done. No questions asked.

    • If the /guildremoved person has a valid reason, they have only to get in touch with Sig or I and they can be reinstated.  Sorry to be having to do this, but we value our name far too much to risk having loose cannons possibly wrecking it for us all.

  • PVP is not allowed within the guild, we have a presence on a pvp server for those that wish to participate

Council 

 

The guild is not run by any one individual, rather it is headed by a Council which is lead by the Guildleader.  Each council member is also an officer and has an equal say on the council.  Any issues brought before the Council will be brought to a vote.  In most cases this will be a majority rules.

  1. Keep up on what is happening in the guild

    1. check the page and forums as often as possible;

    2. try to "listen" to what is being said in guild chat whenever possible

  2. Vote on and give your opinion on any and all issues in the Council Voting booth forum

  3. Try to be online as much as you are able

  4. Try at all times to set an example for guild members

  5. Help to guide and aid your Guild mates

    1. Obviously twinking and power leveling is not required,

    2. nor is dropping everything because a guild member needs help finding something or killing something, etc.

Initiates

 

The Initiates are those newly inducted into the guild.  They remain initiates for at LEAST thirty days before they are inducted as full members.  This "trial period" is for both the initiate and the guild.  

If either party does not feel that the relationship is what they expected during this time, the Initiate may leave or be removed from the guild without anything having to go through Council.  Also, they may rejoin the guild at a later date should they change their minds and had left the guild of their own volition only one time previously.

After the trial period, a member my remove themselves but they may not return to the guild without full Council approval.  The removal of a guild member after this period of time must first be passed in Council.

  1. Initiates must supply all information required BEFORE their initiation into the guild

  2. All other laws must be adhered to.

  3. There is an initiation period during which both you and the guild decide if you are right together

  4. Initiates are responsible to and the responsibility of the guild as a whole

    1. remember that you carry the Spirit of Fire name with you at all times, please be respectful of your fellow guildees

  5. Fellow Guild members are NOT required to drop everything and come running to  your aid for minor problems, such as being lost, etc.  It is up to you to know how to play EQ, we are not here to train you.

Loot Policies

  1. Loot, once given, will be OWNED by the person who receives it.  Although this may not be for the best of the guild folks do need the certainty of ownership of their equipment.  And it just feels better to say 'this is mine' vs. 'this is on loan'.

  2. The guild leader (or raid leader if I cannot attend) will be handing out items gained by the guild on guild raids.  We are all family here and the loot distributor has only the best interests of the guild at heart.  If an item is not given to you be happy for the person that received the item cause next time it may be you. The best way to insure that the loot distributor knows what armor / jewelry / weapons you have is to sign up to Casters realm.  Add yourself to the player database and keep your info updated.  This allows the loot distributor to be aware of all members of the guild and what they have.

  3. Trade down of old items is encouraged but not required.  We would like to see items that are given to guildees stay in the guild. I will not require you to hand down your stuff but if a recurring trend of selling guild given items happens that person may be dropped way down the list of people to receive items.  I have absolutely no problems helping people to get items but if they are just going to sell that wurmslayer or fbss or whatever then I will just give the item to someone else next time. This is pretty much a moot point as everyone in the guild has the best interests of the guild in mind but it must be stated for the record.

  4. Loot priority is always to a players PRIMARY character. This is not a guild for twinking alts. If no one's primary requires it, then it can be considered for participating alts or for sale by the guild. Every member must post in the primary members roster which is their primary toon. They are then encouraged to make sure that toon is listed on EQ.Castersrealm with all equipment listed. If you decide you just don't like your main then talk to either me or one of the council members about it and we will discuss changing your listed main. This is not to discourage playing alts rather it is to discourage people getting equipment for one toon and then changing to another toon to "twink" them.  Again this would never happen here but it must be stated for the record.

  5. Some items will be retained by the guild for sale.  This is done to give the guild operating funds to defray coffins, hate TP stones etc.  Just as a FYI: a guild I was speaking with had to outlay almost 5000pp to get their members out of a Chardok TPW (total party wipeout).  SoF cannot fund that right now so we had better make plans to do so in the future.  Each HATE TP costs 1k for the gems.  We NEED operating capital.  And if we ever want to go to the plane of Sky it will cost even more.

  6. All coin & gems on any guild raid will become the property of the guild.  Gems will be sold to guildees first and foremost (at 50% of the market value) so that if you are working on jewelry making you have an easier time, or smithing the racial armors, etc.  Gems will be sold for cash only.  It occurs more and more that guilds must have operating capital in order to succeed.

  7. This policy is in effect for posted guild raids only.  Be advised however the guild intends to post a LOT of raids to zones etc. We want to gear our members as quickly as possible.

  8. To be eligible for raid loot - must participate in raids in general.  If you are not able to attend a given raid because of RL commitments or such that will not be held against you but if you are online during a guild raid you are expected to attend if you are of the applicable level.

    NOW - some explanations of raid structures. The guild will be have TWO kinds of raids. We will be raiding a LOT and the differences are this:

    EXP : if a raid is listed as an EXP raid the theory is we are going to get xp for guild members. If loot drops, it is up to the group that got it to decide what to do with it. Specifically: Guild loot policy is NOT in effect.

    LOOT: If a raid is listed as a LOOT raid we are going to achieve a goal for the guild. IE: acquire GEBS etc. Guild loot policy IS in effect help someone with their epic, get someone some boots, etc. These will be posted on the calendar. Keep your eyes peeled on the boards for further info and for dates and times.

    We are looking at having at least one LOOT and several EXP raids per week. Guild council would like to see SoF grouping with itself more.

    There will be raids set up for all level ranges. This is not a way to outfit just the high level players. This is for the benefit of all in the guild.
