Awards

Any full member can make the motion for another to receive any of these awards.  

Simply post your nominations in the member's area and they will be reviewed.

Commendation    True Spirit  Chivalry    Friendship    Charity    Valour  Trades Master    Research Master  Accomplishment (Epic Quest)

Commendation

 

This award is for those who go above and beyond in order to make the guild better.  They give of themselves and their time regularly.  Without them, Sof would not be what it is.

 

 

 

True Spirit

 

These are the members that are in every way True Spirits, they embody most closely what people think of when they think of the Spirits of Fyre.

 

 

 

Charity

 

Members who show uncommon Charity on multiple occasions are given this award

 

Chivalry

 

Members who show uncommon Chivalry on multiple occasions are given this award.

 

 

Friendship

 

Members who have been tried and proven friends to several are given this award.

 

 

Valour

 

Members who show uncommon Valour  on multiple occasions are given this award.

 

Dolza Mortenera * January 2001
