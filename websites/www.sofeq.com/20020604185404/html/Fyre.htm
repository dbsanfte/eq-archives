The Story Behind the Spirits

The history to this guild begins shortly after the world of Norrath was first discovered by the Gods.  The people started warring against one another, the "good" against the "evil" races, light against light and dark against dark.  Unruly and chaotic.  Among these people was a small clan of little note that fell during the warring and chaos. Only one of the Children of this clan survived the brutal warring.  She was simply called Fire.  She was now left alone to fend for herself, all because of the wars, disputes, and chaos.  

Fire ventured out into Norrath and found that there were others like her: people of the same heart and soul, people who wanted to live with Honour and Valour, despite what their race or culture might be.  As she matured and discovered more and more people of this nature, she decided that her purpose in life was to recreate what she had lost, her family. 

With this in mind Fire began to gather these people she had met, those with uncommon Valour, honesty and integrity.........................
