Current Guidelines for Recruitment
We are currently recruiting very active players and are in need of high level Bards, Wizards, and Clerics. In order to be considered for admission, please meet the following requirements:

1) Resist Gear at 100+ for all Resists. This is a MINIMUM.
The reason for this is our guild spends a lot of time fighting dragons and other monsters. These monsters can
AOE fear and AOE damage and without very high resists ( 150+ prefered ) you will be quickly become a casualty.
2) Sebilis Key, Charasis Key, Cobalt Scar Tooth
In addition to having these keys, we hope that you are able to traverse the entirety of Norrath unguided.
You need to be familiar with how to walk to any zone in the game and know the location of all high level dungeons.
3) Dubious or better faction with Giants in Kael
Our guild works within Temple of Veeshan collecting armor for the Kael quests. That being said, however, a player with
a CoH capable mage on CoV faction or a backup res capable cleric on CoV faction as well are a bonus.
4) We also ask that you are at minimum level 57.
Many classes achieve critical raid capabilities at or near this level. We need druids and wizards who can zone
evac across Sirens Grotto. Mages need CoH. Clerics need to be able to cast reviviscence.
We also are a raid organization, Raid participation is mandatory and there will be little free time to spend exping.
The closer to 60 you are the better we like you.
5) We ask that you are able to raid frequently.
Standard raid times are weeknights approximately 5-11pm CST, as well as most weekends, sometimes all day.
We understand people have real lives, but we need dedicated flexible players who can devote their time to the guild.
6) We ask that applicants please have a stable computer and internet connection. Our raids may have up to 40 people or more in one
area, sometimes in zones with many other large guilds at the same time. Constant 'link deaths' are a hinderence to the guild
and raid progress.
7) We seek friendly, intelligent people. You must be able to work well with other people, not prone to arguments,
or causing disturbances with other members or the guild or with other players on the server. If you come with a shady past,
or have a history of conflict with other players, particularly ones in our guild, please do not apply.
8) We expect that you know your class well. We are not fond of 'E-bayers'. We expect and hope that you are
an above average player and will complement us well on raids. However, please be open to constructive criticisms,
as our methods may be different than you have seen in the past.

9) Additonial Requirements:
* Melee: Must have some sort of HASTE item and at least some PLANAR equipment or equivalent.
* Casters/Priests: Should have 180+ Int/Wis with resist gear on. You should as many of the spells for your level as possible.
* All applicants: Should have already completed at least some portion of their EPIC as EPICS will be a mandatory part of our guild.
* All Applicants: Must be willing to die horribly multiple times and willing to attend at least 3 large guild events per week.
* Applicants must understand and be willing to accept LOOT awards based on leadership decision. Whining about loots is not tolerated.

This may sound a bit demanding. However, if you want to be part of a guild full of talented and experienced players you need to demonstrate your willingness to meet these standards as close as possible. Exceptions will be made on a case by case basis depending on what other special talents or abilities you can bring to Pravus Dei. In return, you will have the opportunity to see places no single group can visit, conquer the greatest monsters Norrath has to throw at you, bond closely with a team of people that care about one another, and win fabulous treasures. Remember, as an applicant, you will help be part of what is making Pravus Dei the best guild on Veeshan. A guild dedicated, principled, and in one word "OWNAGE".

If you are interested in joining Pravus Dei and feel you meet the above requirements, please visit our Forum and submit an application. Our recruitment officer will work with you. If we feel we have a need for you we will tag you as a trial member. During your trial membership, you must be on your best behavior and show that you are a skilled player. Trial membership is one month. Trial members do not receive assistance with their Epic weapon, and they will only receive loot awards under rare circumstances. It is during this time you are trying to impress us and at the end of the trial period your permanent membership will be voted on.
