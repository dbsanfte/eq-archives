Welcome to the Guardians of Valour website! We hope you have learned enough about our guild to be excited about joining us. We are currently recruiting active, main, 50+ characters from all classes.

If you are interested in joining GoV, please read through our Charter and our Raiding Guidelines. All Guardians are required to register their character information on the Member List within 2 weeks of joining or will default their membership. All potential Guardians are required to have a GoV sponsor to be tagged into the guild. Once tagged, a new recruit will have a 30 day probation period. The Mentor for the newly tagged Guardian will help him/her adjust to the family and educate him/her on the inner workings of the guild.

If you would like to continue with the membership process, you need to post in the Applications for Membership area of the forums indicating your character name, level, class, race, your GoV sponsor, previous guild membership and your acceptance of the Charter policy to be considered for membership into GoV. Our officers will then be actively looking for you in game.

If you have any questions regarding the guild or guild membership please post in our General Forums.
