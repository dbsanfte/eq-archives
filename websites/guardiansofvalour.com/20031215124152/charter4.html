Recruiting Policy

Welcome to our recruiting section. We hope to give you all the information you will need to join our happy family.

First off, we are a 50+ family guild. We are not a hardcore raiding guild. We raid once a week with smaller group or multigroup functions organized on other nights by members. We strive to keep the low stress family environment at all times. Raids are not required, but we like to see everyone attend when they can.

The 50+ requirement is there for one reason only. We like to make sure that no matter what we do, everyone in the guild can be involved if they choose to. This level requirement ensures that this is the case. Exceptions are made on a case by case basis, but your experience the the guild will be much better if you have already attained this level before joining.

We require you to have a sponsor in order to be tagged into the guild. This is easy enough to do just by grouping with members and letting us get to know you. Your sponsor will serve as your "Mentor" during your 30 day probation period. They will help make sure that your questions get answered and that you become familiar with the inner workings of the guild.

You will be tagged once you have obtained a sponsor. You will then go through your 30 day probation. This is simply a time for everyone to get to know you and you to get to know us. Once you have passed the 30 day mark, full membership is granted. We find this system to be more effective that the voting system.

So where do you go from here? Make sure you read the charter and the other guild guidelines. If you think that Guardians of Valour is the home for you, then put up a post in the application section of our message boards. From there I will be looking for you in game, but we also ask that you actively seek out members to group up with and get to know us.

Thank you for visiting here and we hope that you decide that GoV is the place for you. If you have any questions you can contact me ingame on Bludpoint or Goliyath. My email address is bludpoint@yahoo.com

  1. No discrimination will be made regarding a character's race or gender in EQ or RL. Guild recruiting restrictions based on class are based on numbers of current players in each class.
