Loot Distribution

  1. Naming the System
    The first thing is to do is change the name from DKP to GoV Raid points (RP)
  2. Administering the System
    This system will only be as good as the people that administer it. First and foremost, only officers will administer this system. All officers will be required at some point and time to track attendance, assign points, handle a dispute or update the website. This will be a team effort and will succeed only if we work as a team. This does not mean that there can not be a primary for a particular task.
  3. How do I get RPs?
    All guildmembers on November 19, 2002 will start with 10 RP.
    You will receive Raid Points (RP) for attending raids that fall under the guide lines listed below.
  4. How are RP awarded?
    One (1) Raid Point each are awarded for the beginning, middle, and the end of the raid giving a total possible of 3 points per scheduled raid. FFA mobs award 2 points per encounter since attendance is tracked slightly different. Points are awarded to the person not the character. Your points will be tracked under your main's name. If you have an alt that is guilded and attends the raid, then the points are awarded to your main's account. If you dual box, you only receive points for one (1) character not both. See the section on alts for details on how they can spend points.
  5. What constitutes a Raid?
    There are two types of raids: Scheduled and FFA. In order for a raid to qualify for the GoV RP system it must meet the following criteria:
    1. Have a post in the raid section that schedules it for a future day and time. FFA mobs obviously can not follow this but must meet all of the following criteria.
    2. Must have an officer present. If only 1 officer is present then that officer will handle all the aspects of this system (Attendance tracking, loot distribution and posting afterwards) and appoint someone else to be Raid Leader. NOTE: An officer should never be both the Raid Leader and Loot Officer during the same raid.
    3. Must consist of at least 15 GoV members.
  6. Who can lead a raid?
    Any one can lead a raid but only officers can handle loot and administration within this system.
  7. How is attendance tracked?
    The Loot Officer will take attendance at the beginning, middle, and end of scheduled raids. Both a start time and end time will be posted for scheduled raids. This will help determine when to take attendance for the middle of the raid. It will also help people schedule late arrivals or early departures. Please keep in mind that raids can go longer than the posted end time if the raid leader chooses. This will not change when final attendance is taken. For FFA raid mobs, attendance will be taken twice: once at 20 mins after mobilization is called; and once after mob is killed. You earn points for only those attendance lists you appear on, so if you leave before the end or arrive late, you only receive partial points. The loot officer will do their best to make sure no one is link dead or dead before taking attendance.
    Attendance is taken by taking a log of the zone.
  8. What loot is covered under the RP system?
    All raid loot is covered under this system except for Epic drops.
  9. How do I spend my Points?
    Points are used in an auction format. A loot channel will be created for each raid. When an item you want drops respond in the loot channel with your bid. You bid must be 1 higher than the previous bid. First bid to show in channel is considered the current bid. If you are awarded the item then the points for that item will be deducted from your total. You may only bid the points currently showing on your balance. Points in dispute or from a current FFA raid will NOT be considering in your bid.
  10. What happens if no one wants an item?
    If the item is a droppable item then it goes to the guild bank for two (2) weeks. The first week it can be purchased by any MAIN character not at the raid. If the item is not purchased in the first week, then anyone can purchase it for an alt character. If not purchased after the second week, it is sold and the money goes to the guild bank. If it is a no drop quest item (HOT armor) it will follow the same rules above to be Multi-Quested. Other no drop, non MQ items will rot. If no one wants the No Drop item, you can purchase it for an alt if doing so will not disrupt the raid. No Drop items that no one wants can be awarded to guest at the loot officer’s discretion.
  11. Can I have a negative balance?
    No.
  12. How are Alts handled in this system?
    Alts earn points under your main's name. Alts can only bid if no one’s main wants the item. Alts can only bid on items for their class, they cannot bid on items for their main.
  13. How is item value determined?
    Auction style with the bidding starting at 1 RP. Bids can only be placed in whole number increments.
  14. How do Epics work in this system?
    Epic rules still follow the epic charter as far as epic drops are concerned. The raid to get the epic drop can use this system for all other loot as long as the guidelines for what determines a raid are met. If those requirements are met the points will be awarded for the raid. If not, then it’s up to the Raid Leader or Loot Officer to decide between random or assigned.
  15. What do I do if I have an issue?
    Please note we are human and will make mistakes. Our goal at the end of the day is to do what is right for the guild not the individual. If you have an issue with points or anything please address it like an adult in either a tell or email with an officer. Posts for point disputes may be placed in the Raid Point System forum.
  16. What happens to my points if I guildremove?
    Your entry for the raid point system as well as the Members' Database is deleted. If you are re-invited to the guild, your entry will be added to the list with a ZERO (0) value.

The Loot Policy is subject to change as deemed necessary by the guild and its officers.
Please continue to read this page to keep current on GoV's loot policy.
