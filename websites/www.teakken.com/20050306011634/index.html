Welcome to Teakken.com

Teakken.com is the virtual home for me, Matthew Jones. The website is named after Teakken Oakwind, a druid character I started for EverQuest in November of '99. I used to chronicle his adventures for my own amusement so I could have a diary of this "wonderful new game" I had started playing. I don't have much about the original EQ on this site anymore. Nowadays I use this site for testing HTML ideas (I like doing HTML, PHP, MySQL for hobby), but visitors will usually never see my HTML experimentation.

Since creating the "Teakken" persona, I have taken it to other games, MMORPG as well as FPS and other types. My gamer friends now just refer to me as "Teak".

I intend to begin recording my adventures in EQII and World of Warcraft here soon. Check in the forums for updated "diaries". And drop me a note in there to say hello.

Teak, ratonga fury, Freeport
Wednesday, February 16 @ 02:59:28 EST (8 reads)
Teak is my ratonga fury (druid). He has aspirations of one day ruling the entire world, but for now he conceals those ambitions behind a false show of worshipping Overlord D'Lere. In truth, he can't stand the idea of being ruled over, and wants nothing more than to hang D'Lere from the bottom of his own floating castle... after Teak takes it over of course.

Here we see Teak outside of his apartment in Temple Street, with floating castle of D'lere visible in the background.

Teak is an active carpenter. He loves building furniture for his own home, as well as building strongboxes and furniture for his friends and selling on the market.
