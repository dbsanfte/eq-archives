There is only the Blood

I welcome you to the last known gathering of the Band of the Crimson Hand. The Band has a long and illustrious history of pride, honor, and ferocity the likes of which is often unassociated with such a mixed-matched group. The last living Redarm, as an individual within the Band is called, lived and died over a thousand years ago. So what is the Band of the Crimson Hand?

During the ages of strife that brought many of the empires of Norrath to their knees, and amongst the chaos of the times arose a name that all came to know: Shen an Calhar, the Band of the Crimson Hand. Originally formed as an elite regiment of the Alliance forged out of the ancient wars between light and darkness, it became something more. The soldiers who fought with the Band were veterans all, and was one of the first military organizations to include specialist positions. As time wore on, the Band began to accept all races of all walks of life within their ranks, provided they were willing to leave their past behind them in the name of the Blood. In an archaic ritual which required absolute devotion from those involved, all Redarms were bound to each other by magics older than the gods themselves. Honor, duty and family were the driving goals of the Band, who sought to uphold those values through whatever obstacles were put in their paths. As the wars began to subside, there was no longer a need for the Band's services, and they began to seperate to continue on with their lives.

The Shen an Calhar did not end there, however, as the Blood passed on the rites of the Band through oral traditions, eventually culminating in the reformation you see here today. Whether you seek to answer the call of your ancestors to continue the honored traditions they began, or you simply believe in the values of the Blood, the Band of the Crimson Hand is open to all willing to serve. Your past is your own, and your future is with your new family. Come, let us hunt and join together in the Blood of old.
