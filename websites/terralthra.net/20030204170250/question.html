Every Question but Why

What more is there to me?
What else is there besides this body?
Is there a soul?
No god touches this mind,
can there be spirit?
What long-forgotten creed stirs this heart?
For when all is gone,
will anything remain
of me?
Will there be a person,
a single
solitary
soul
Who will tell them of what I was?
What was I?
What am I?
I do nothing, I am nothing
Thoughts are all that I was
When I am gone, my thoughts
go with
me.
Where will they go?
Will they be remembered?
I hope.
I would pray.
But there is no one to hear my prayer.
The night descends upon my eyes.
coldness rushes in like shadows,
when light has fled.
always an actor, playing a part.
Exit me. Stage center.
Fade to Black.
Curtain.
