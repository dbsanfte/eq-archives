Carpe Noctem

jump off
fall in
cover up
break the skin
slide down
break my fall
forget the past
now is all
let it begin
so soon it ends
call us nothing
only friends
we are the same
you and I
we both live
we wished to die
we come together
just this time
no more will we
make sweet rhyme
you give me a little
of all that you are
it doesn't mean much
but shines as a star
this night
this place
your beauty
your face
what more
is
