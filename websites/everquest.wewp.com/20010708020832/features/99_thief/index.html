Looking Glass Studio's Thief : The Dark Project
Jason Beaumont
Warren Liu

Our visit to Looking Glass's Office in Cambridge gave us a first hand look at the making of Thief : The Dark Project. Looking Glass Studios is one of the pioneers in the computer gaming industry with a track record of creating genre re-defining games. Games like System Shock, Ultima Underworld and Flight Unlimited are all well-loved by gamers all over the world. Thief : The Dark Project stands to again re-define the FPS genre with it's intricate plotline and immersive gameplay.
