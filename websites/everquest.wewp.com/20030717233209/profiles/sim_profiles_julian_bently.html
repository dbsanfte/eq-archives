After graduating from the University of Exeter, Julian worked initially as a tour manager, overseeing the live performances of musicians in the UK and Europe, and then in management, guiding the careers of recording artists, actors and comedians.

After further studies to become a lawyer, Julian joined The Simkins Partnership in 1991. He completed his training in 1993 and became a partner in 1997.

Julian holds a Post-Graduate Diploma in UK and European Law of Copyright and Related Rights from King's College London.
