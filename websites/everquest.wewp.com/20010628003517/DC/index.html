Mars Matrix Review
April 30, 2001

AllDreamcast.com recently completed their review of Mars Matrix. Here is a sample of what they found:

"Graphically, Mars Matrix is one of the better looking 2D shooters, although it doesn't even come close to matching the visual splendor of Capcom's own Naomi-based Giga Wing 2. However, the game does feature an impressive opening movie sequence, and the graphics are large and detailed, with some cool effects and only an occasional problem with slowdown. The best part of Mars Matrix though is its replay value. Most games like this are over in less than an hour and leave you without any reason to play them again. With Mars Matrix, very little of the game is available to you right from the start, including some basic features like the amount of lives and continues you can use, as well as different ship colors, power settings, and various gameplay modes. You'll have to purchase and unlock all the extras in the game's shop mode, and unless you're the world's most skilled gamer, you'll find it nearly impossible to beat this game using only the default options. If you want to see all that the game has to offer, you'll have to put in the time and effort, giving Mars Matrix considerably more replay value than most games of this type.

Regardless of how you feel about 2D sprite-based games on systems capable of pushing millions of polygons per second, Mars Matrix is still an excellent effort that no shooter fan should overlook. The gameplay is solid even if it is a little on the difficult side, the graphics are decent, it has excellent replay value, and the overall presentation is top notch. Considering you read through this entire review, it's doubtful that you've already brushed off 2D games entirely, so if you're a fan of shooters and have been searching for one that's a little more interesting that what we've seen in the past, Mars Matrix is definitely worth a look."

Overall: 7.5
