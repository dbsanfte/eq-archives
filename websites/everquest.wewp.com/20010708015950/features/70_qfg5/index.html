Wewp! Entertainment Presents
SierraFX's Quest For Glory V : Dragon Fire
By Warren Liu & Benjamin Stein

One of the longest and most innovative games in the history of computer gaming is about to make a fifth and final appearance. The Quest for Glory series has successfully blended elements from the Adventure and RPG gaming into one single product without one element over-shadowing the other. Dragon Fire may be the game that could revitalize interest in adventure games again bringing the genre to the forefront of computer gaming.
