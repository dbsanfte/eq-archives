Proclamation to all who will hear

Let me tell you a little about our guild.  We are a guild of many different minds and different characteristics.  We are a guild of many races, High Elf, Erudin, Half-elf, but we hold many things in common. 

We are a guild of brotherhood which stands up for one another.

We are a guild that does not keep secrets from one another. 

Our quests is supreme knowledge and understanding of the world we know of called, Norrath.  Through exploration and fellowship, we will see that goal reached. 

We of Ruis Saille see knowledge as power and want nothing but to learn and grow together and to explore. 

We are a generous guild, one that willingly helps the helpless.  One who gives when we are abundant. 

We wish to share our knowledge to others in hopes that this world can benefit from our studies and journeys. 

We are a guild of unity, and judge people by there actions not by what society has pinned on them. 

We are not a weak guild, just because we seek knowledge does not mean that we cannot be fierce in battle.  To overcome the many obstacles on our journey, we are aware we will have to fight and we will not back down if provoked by creature or sentient. We will fight fiercely until the end. 

In short, we want nothing more than to see the world, learn its secrets and bless this world with this knowledge.

 

IN DRUIDIC OGHMA (real Druidic characters)
RUIS (Elder) SAILLE (Willow)

(def: Druidic for Elder Willow Tree or Wise Willow Tree)

 

I have never really thought of the Guild Ruis Saille as a "Guild".  I believe that it is more of a "family"and try to treat each member with great respect as Brothers and Sisters in this vast world.  You will hear me frequently say Brother and Sister to even the most greenest of initiates because I want you all to feel at home when you join this family.

I believe it is all about my years of growing up near Surefall Glade and how I was raised.  My Father and Mother were very close and always taught me to respect them and my sister and anyone else who I happened by in the Glade.  My Druidic heritage taught me that we are all one in the grand universe and the Earth and Karana and all it's people should be treated equally.  My Guild is just an extension of the passion to share and grow with others in the world we know as Norrath.

Please, if you see one of my brothers or sisters say hello and bid them a good day and I know they will do the same.  We are a guild of giving and sharing and wish to hear your tales.

-Sunray
