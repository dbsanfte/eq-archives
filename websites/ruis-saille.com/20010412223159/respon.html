Here is a summary of the responsibilities in Ruis Saille

Guild Leader | Officers | Members

Guild Leader

1. Schedule regular meetings to keep open communication

2. To regulate/delegate responsibility to officers and members

3. Initiated/Disbanded officers and members

4. Submitting vote for approval of new initiates

5. Discipline officers or members who violate code/ethics

6. Approving/Disapproving player run "guild" events

7. Coordinating with members to establish/start "guild" events

8. Leader has final say on all decisions

<top>

Guild Officers

1. Coordinating information from members to other officers and Leader and visa-versa.

2. Inviting members into the family

3. Disciplining/Disbanding members who violate code/ethics

4. Attending scheduled meetings whenever possible

5. Submitting vote for approval of new members

6. Coordinating with members/Leader to start "guild" events

7. Loyally play this character/alternate character on Fennin Ro

8. Understanding codes and ethics of Ruis Saille and being an example to members.

<top>

Guild Members

1. Showing mutual respect to all brothers and sisters in the Guild. And showing this same respect for others around you that deserved and earned your respect.

2. Following orders/guidelines given by Leader/Officers to the best of you ability

3. Attending Guild meetings whenever possible

4. Role-play (Trust me, it is fun *grin*)

5. Be loyal to your family, this includes Atani, and stand behind them at all costs. It will come back to you tenfold.

6. HAVE FUN!!!!
