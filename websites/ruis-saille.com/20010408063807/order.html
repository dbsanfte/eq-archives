The Order of Ruis

And so the day had come to pass,
When blessing came to Ruis at last.
Her forest grew, her children blessed,
We had conquered odds and passed all tests.

But still a wind ran through and through,
A wind too strong for Ruis to subdue.
Yet blew in seeds from across the land,
And was not brought in by Ruis Saille's hand.

This wind blew in with many-a-face,
Dwarf, Elf, Gnome, all kinds of race.
It spoke of Brell and Fizzlethorpe,
And plucked and dined to Errolsi's harp.

And too this wind was given pause,
Ruis bowed down to it's mighty cause.
For though they were not sister or brother,
Was given respect, as if another.

And so the Order of Ruis began,
To spread great seeds across the land.
To take the Wisdom of the Mother,
And spread good word with sister and brother.

                 -Sunray Stormfury

Order of Ruis members are honored here on this page.  People who have proven themselves to be honorable and courageous.  They are kind and generous and have shown there devotion to Ruis Saille and all it stands for.  Order of Ruis is given to friends of Norrath who we consider brothers but do not fly the Ruis Saille tag.
