Rules of the Tears of Veeshan
Expections of Guild Members by the Guild

Above All Else, Be Nice
Treat all guild mates and other players with respect. Do unto others as you would have them do unto you.
Kill-stealing or loot-stealing, be it within the guild or without is strictly forbidden
Act with Honour, and never bring shame upon your Guild.
Work together to Accomplish the Four-fold Purpose of the Guild Described in the Charter.
Help others in need whenever able, not only when convenient.
Role-play your interactions with others, and wear your Guild Tag with Pride
Please don't use /anon, use /roleplay instead, unless the roleplay situation demands anonymity.
Expectations of the Guild by Guild Members

Tears of Veeshan will organize raids or events for all levels of its membership at least once a month.
Teears of Veeshan will make a commitment to every one of its members, and demand the same from them.
Every Elder must plan and hold at least one event per month with the guild, be it individual coaching or hunting parties.

Many ideas shamelessly adopted from Vivix Protectorate :)
