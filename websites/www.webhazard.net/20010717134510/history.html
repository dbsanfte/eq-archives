History of the Tears of Veeshan
In an age long past, when the gods walked the face of Norrath, Veeshan spawned her Children upon the world.

Above all others, her favor rested with her First Child, the Queen of all dragons. The Queen was cruel beyond description and Norrath was a playground for her brood.

The details of the First Battle are few, much of the information having been lost to the relentless march of time, but this we do know from Legend:

In the Elder age, a great champion was born, his fate foretold by the prophets. This First Warrior looked across Norrath and saw the terror that Veeshan had unleashed with her Spawn. Resolute, he did equip himself in the finest magical armour, and girded himself with the brightest steel for battle.

This Warrior sought out the First Child of Veeshan, and challenged her within her lair.

Metal clashed against fang and claw as the Warrior pitted his muscle against that of the Dragon, in this First Battle to free Norrath from the Tyrrany of Veeshan.

The battle did rage for a day and a night, and the blood of the Wyrm and of the Warrior flowed together, running in a river down the side of the mountain the Child had claimed as her own.

As the hours wore on, each combatant became more and more exhausted, sweat pouring over the brow of the Warrior, his every sword thrust bringing heated agony to his muscles, which begged for mercy.

Finally, in a last act of despiration, the Warrior threw himself at the Child, plunging his sword deep through the Wyrm's eye, planting it firmly within the Dragon's brain, killing it instantly.

An instant after the sword had found its home within the Dragon, a scream which tore the heavens asunder shook the mountain. Lightning arced across the sky in a thousand bolts. Veeshan had felt the passing of her First Child and awoken.

As Veeshan looked down upon Norrath, and in anguish saw what damage this Warrior had wrought upon her Child, tears came to her eyes.

The Tears of Veeshan fell through time to mark at birth each upon Norrath who would stand against her Children. Her Children would henceforth be forewarned against those who would do them harm, a dragon shaped mark placed upon their bodies serving as a beacon of danger.

It is the destiny of those marked by the Tears of Veeshan to band together to fight side by side against Veeshan's Children, and the kind of tyrrany they would stand for.

Veeshan has marked others upon the face of Norrath, at the behest of their individual gods, to band together in a war against Evil.

The Mark upon those brave souls, known as the Mark of Draconians, and the Mark upon this band of intrepid warriors, known as the Tears of Veeshan, are often difficult to distinguish by appearance alone.

It was only later in life that some of those so marked realized their Mark to be derived from the Tears of Veeshan. All those marked are truly noble and their causes just.

Blessings will fall upon any who would stand and battle against Veeshan, her hoarde, and any that would bring Norrath under one rule.

In the end there will be a Final Battle, and The Tears of Veeshan may be the front line, but they will need the support of all those that stand against the larger Power to prevail.
