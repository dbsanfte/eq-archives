Charter of the Tears of Veeshan
The Tears of Veeshan have marked us as a warning of Danger to her Children. Why should we pose a threat to them?

For some, the simple fact of the Tears's existance is proof that it is their destiny to take up arms against the Dragons. Others have looked across the face of Norrath and decided that no race, least of all Dragons, will enslave them, and decided to follow the Path of the Tear.

It is for this second reason that, above all else, the Tears of Veeshan stand against any that would enslave the inhabitants of Norrath. Tyrrany in any form is not to be tolerated, and we must rail against it at all times. In particular, the Children of Veeshan desire domination over all others and thus must be stopped before any other.

As Dragonkind is a force outside of Good and Evil, those who walk both the Dark Path, or the Light Path may fight with us. The Destiny of those Warriors born to defend Norrath against Veeshan was pre-ordained. The Paths that they follow, be the Light or Dark, must eventually unite, the Yin and Yang of Good and Evil banding together against a common foe.

However, as evil also tends toward domination, the Tears of Veeshan are primarily comprised of those that walk in the Light, and is thus a good aligned guild. In the end, however, any who stand against Veeshan's Children are our allies, and thus there are those of darker talents that walk with us. Though this may cause some disagreement in the ranks, we stand together first and foremost against opression, and the greater Evils of Norrath.

The goals of the Guild are four-fold (in order of importance):

Roleplay
Teaching
Training
Questing

First, this is a roleplay guild. However, we of Tears of Veeshan believe that there is much opportunity in Everquest to roleplay in a way that makes the rollplayer stronger and more powerful as a character.

As with childhood, in adulthood there are rites of passage. These include equipping oneself better, and teaching those of lesser rank how to be better prepared for the Evils of Norrath.

Quests and Monsters are scattered throughout Norrath as fodder for us to practice our skills against, in preparation for the Final Battle against Veeshan. Additionally, there are great items of magic to be found to help empower us in the battle against tyrrany.

After all -- Veeshan's Children gain power every day, and we will surely perish if we remain Weak.
