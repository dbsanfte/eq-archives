Bylaws of the Tears of Veeshan
The Bylaws of the Tears of Veeshan describe the method by which the governing body of the Tears operates.

Definitions and Voting Process

In the following, a Guild Quarum is defined as two-thirds of the Guild members. An Elder Quarum is defined as two-thirds of the Elder Members, when there are more than 5 Elders. Less than or equal to 5 elders, an Elder Quarum is defined as all the Elders.

In order to achieve a Guild Quarum, due to timezone differences and other real life issues, voting may take place over a designated period of time (three days) via. Email to the Guild Leader, or Tell to the Guild Leader. Voting will take place via email if a Guild Quarum is not present at a Guild Meeting.

The Council of Elders


The Council of Elders consists of the Guild Leader and all elders. At the time of the Guild's Creation, there are two members to this governing body: The Guild Leader, and the First Elder.

As the guild grows, one Elder is elected by popular guild vote for every ten people in the guild, e.g. when the guild grows to twenty people, there will be one Leader, plus two Elders.

A simple majority of the Guild Quarum is required for this election. The Guild Leader has the right to veto the selection of an elder, but the Guild Quarum has the right to overrule that veto with a two-thirds majority.

This power is given to help avoid any problems with the smooth running of the Guild that may occur due to personality conflicts between the Guild Leader and any Elders. (Not that this will happen, though, because the Guild Leader is so lovable )

The Elder election process thus removes any relation of Elder to Level, and relates it instead to willingness of the elder to do things for the guild, niceness, charity, etc. Some folk may consider Level important in the decision, but a particular Level is never mandated.

Succession of Leadership

Should the First Elder leave the Guild, a new First Elder should be promoted from the Council of Elders by popular vote of the guild. A simple majority of a Guild Quarum is enough to win.

As in the selection of any Elder, The Guild Leader has the right to veto the decision, and the Guild has the right to overrule the veto with a two-thirds majority. A new Elder member is then elected using the process described above.

Should the Guild Leader leave the Guild, the First Elder becomes the Guild Leader, and a new First Elder is selected in the manner described above.

Any Elder except the Guild Leader and First Elder may be removed from office after election by a two-thirds majority vote of the Guild Quarum.

Voting

The council of elders shall be called to vote on any issue affecting the guild as a whole. A simple majority of the Elder Quarum is required to decide the issue.

The Guild Leader votes with the Council of Elders. In the case when there are an odd number on the Council, the First Elder will abstain from voting. In the case when there are an even number on the Council, the First Elder may vote. In all cases the First Elder's voice will be heard with Equal Weight.

A no point should the council let any discussion become an argumnet -- there should always be a calm and fair way to solve any disagreement.

Voting will generally take place over a several-day period, to allow all members a chance to voice their opinion on a matter. This works around real life obligations which may prevent members from attending guild meetings.

Guild War

Guild War Should it become necessary to declare a guild war, for roleplay reasons, or otherwise, a two thirds vote of the entire guild is required. Should the proposal to declare a guild war pass, the war will be announced via Guild message-of-the-day, email, and the Guild Homepage, for three days prior to it becoming active. Rules regarding the war will be posted at the same time, to avoid any confusion.

Ammendments to the Bylaws

Ammendments to the Bylaws These bylaws will be voted into an active state by a 2/3 majority of all members. Voting will take place over a one week period, via in-game tell to the Guild Leader, or email to the Guild Leader. No response from a member during the one-week voting period will be considered an acceptance of the bylaws. Once accepted, the bylaws may be altered only by a two-thirds vote of the Guild Quarum.
