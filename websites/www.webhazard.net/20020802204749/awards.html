Guild Awards and Honors of the Tears of Veeshan
One of the foundations of the guild is questing. For particular quests and special events, awards are given by The Tears of Veeshan to its members and those that support them. This is where honors are explained and remembered.

Quest awards are based on the difficulty of a quest, as well as the relevance of a quest. The Titles for quests are listed below:

Title Rite of Passage Level Approximate difficulty
Novice of the Hunt No quests completed 1-5th season
Child of the Hunt 1st 5-10th season
Brother/Sister of the Hunt 2nd 10-15th season
Follower of the Hunt 3rd 15-25th season
Assigner of the Hunt 4th 25-35th season
Master of the Hunt 5th 35-45th season
Hunter Elite 6th 45-55th season
Wurm Hunter last Epic completion
Dragonraid Awards
In addition to Quests, The Tears of Veeshan also recognize its members that participate in the Slaying of a 'high' dragon, or Wurm. These currently include The Lady Vox in Permafrost, and Nagafen in Solusek B. There are 2 awards, one for assisting the raid, and the other for directly aiding in the slaying of the dragon. The only requirement other than participation is that the dragon is slain.

The title WurmBane is granted to those that assist in the raid.

The title WurmSlayer is granted to those that assist in the killing of the dragon.
Non-Guild Member Awards
Also, Awards are given to those non-members who offer aid and assistance to the members of the members of The Tears of Veeshan. More detail will follow on this topic in the days to come.
