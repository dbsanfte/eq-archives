Join the Tears of Veeshan!
To join the Tears of Veeshan, simply post a message in the Application section of our message boards, with the following information:

Name
Race
Class
Level

To be seriously considered, please provide some history for your character (this does not have to be Epic in length. Just give us an idea of who your character is, so we can get to know you).

After reading your application message, one of the officers will find you in-game and converse with you.
