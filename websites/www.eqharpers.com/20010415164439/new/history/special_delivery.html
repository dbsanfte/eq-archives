Special Delivery - Performed by the Harpers of Brell, chronicled by Korie Desmodromic

The night was clear, although a crisp breeze blew across the prairie lands of the Western Karanas. Such things were far from the minds of Pashunn and Qilwenith however, they had been charged to deliver a special package with instructions to get it there.... or else. They were unfamiliar with the land, and only had a roughly sketched map to get them to the house where a cranky old dwarf by the name of Clangadin lived.

And to make matters worse, the package was as fragile as they come, a very rare and very expensive egg of the infamous Goatsucker bird. They clearly needed assistance to complete this delivery.

So they put out the call around the local area by hiring a monk known as Carelen. He rounded up likely adventurers from the gates of Qeynos and met Qil and Pashunn in the middle of the night. Deciding to play it safe, more guards were recruited to ensure the safety of the egg. Forelor, Harriot, Vekn, Malveril, and Dareos all volunteered their services. And so the journey began.

They stuck to the path, amidst the relative safety of the guard towers until they came to a crossroads. Out jumped a kender to see what excitement a large travelling group could have. As we all know, curiosity and kender hands meant the egg was soon out of Qil's possesion and into Thwack's. Luckily, level heads prevailed and the hired retainers were able to convince Thwack to return the precious egg to Pashunn. Of course it was decided by Thwack that a prize this great could only be trusted to the security of a kender guard. And so the slightly larger party set forth again through the tall grass of the plains.

The ferocious wildlife of the area were easily dispatched by the party's guard retinue, and progress continued to the house where Clangadin lived. A knock on the door garnered no reponse though, so Pashunn peaked her head into the cottage. Of course Thwack had already crawled in the window by this time and invited everyone in with open arms.

Then a voice rose up from the shadows and a dark elf, dressed in full plate and wielding razor sharp claws on each hand, came into view. She demanded the egg, on orders of the barbarian counsel, and threatened to end the life of anyone who would stop her.

Thwack, happy to see his presence was in fact needed, jumped onto the assassin's shoulders from the top shelf of a nearby bookcase and the fight was on. The blows rang out across the fields, and soon both combatants were bleeding from various wounds. Things looked grim for the kender until the magic and strength of the adventurers healed his wounds and made his jabs strike true.

Soon the dark elf lay dead on the floor, and a very proud kender showed off his style used to deliver the killing blow to all who would watch, and watch again, and again from a different angle, and again in various languages, and.....

Then Qilwenith spoke up, "Where is Clangadin? Didn't the assassin say he was tied up nearby?" The ranger Malveril quickly found the trail and the group located Clang a short ways away, tied up in a neighboring farm house. He was very thankful for the rescue, and brought everyone back to his home so the egg could be delivered to it's carefully prepared nest.

Clangadin then turned his attention to the corpse of Korie, the assassin. "All is not as it appears here," he told the assembled group, "Let me try something..." And with that he cast a powerful spell which brought Korie back from the dead, but in her true form as a wood elf.

It seems the shaman counsel had cursed Korie and forced her to act as a thief and assassin to steal the egg away. Now that all the loose ends were tied up, the hired mercenaries were rewarded for their efforts, and some even choose to take up the Harper title and lead their life in pride.
