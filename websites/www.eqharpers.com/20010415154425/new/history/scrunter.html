Scrunter's quest for the Visceral Dagger

     After hours of searching in the depths of Dalnir, a haggard group of Harpers finally found the creature that had the final piece of the puzzle of the Visceral dagger... a dagger that had been broken apart years ago, its abilities forgotten in time.  The Kly Imprecator knew that the adventurers were hunting for him.  He hid himself for several hours, but the keen ears of  Clangadin found him.  After a flurry of spells and swords, the vile Imprecator lay dead, and Scrunter found the final piece that he needed.  (The picture is one of the Kly Followers receiving final justice at the hands of the bold heroes.)
