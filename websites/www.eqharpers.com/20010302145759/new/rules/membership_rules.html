Membership Rules

The intent of these rules is to create a guild in which every member feels welcome and no internal stresses are present.

1) Minimum level cap is 10. It encourages new players yet is high enough to keep out the "I want to try this class for a week" players.

2) Candidates must read the Harpers webpage and the Charter, and agree to abide by our rules of play. Use of the forums located here is also highly recommended.

3) Candidates must be sponsored by one guild member. The sponsor will start a thread in the Review Board section of the forums, or ask an officer to start one for the candidate. Guild members will then have the opportunity to comment positively or negatively for the candidate. Once the grouping period is finished (see below) the officers will vote for the new member. Once the officers vote the thread will be locked down to prevent access and maintain the confidentiality of the posts within.

4) Candidates must group or interact with member(s) of the guild at least three occasions so we can meet them and determine if they will fit in with the rest of the guild. This puts responsibility on the candidate and sponsor to make sure that the candidate meets as many members of the guild as possible and also talks with the officers. A current list of members is located here and the officer list is here.

The officers will quite possibly vote for a candidate they never group with so its imperative that the sponsor and guild members make comments on the thread and also have the candidate contact and meet the officers in game and/or here in the Lion's Den. If no one posts comments to the thread and the candidates fail to meet/talk to the officers then it is unlikely they will receive the unanimous vote for guilding.

5) Candidates will be invited to the guild only at the bi-weekly meetings. If this schedule doesn't work for the candidate, other arrangements can be made.

6) Alternate characters of current members are welcome to join the guild, and are not required to follow the above rules (with the exception that they must be above level 10). We are a guild made up of real people, not individual characters.
