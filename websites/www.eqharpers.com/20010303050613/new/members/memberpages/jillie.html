Name Jillie Beene

Character Biography:

Jillie was born in the town of Rivervale to a couple who works the land.  They sent her to explore the surrounding area and see what she would like to do with her life.  

She wandered around Misty Thicket for awhile, enjoying the scenery and avoiding the nasty goblins.  One day, she wandered out the other end of Rivervale and ended up in a forest that she had never seen. She went to explore it and promptly lost her way.  It was near sundown when she came upon a group of friendly rangers that offered to bring her home and told her how close she had come to the very real and nasty danger of being out in Kithicor woods at night.

It was then that Jillie realized her major fault... she was prone to getting lost.  She called upon the deity of her people (Karana) to guide her in her purpose.  A sudden wrenching and falling sensation surrounded her, and she opened her eyes to a beautiful wood elf named Ellona standing in front of her and a ring of stones surrounding her.  She realized that this is what Karana wanted her to do with her life.
