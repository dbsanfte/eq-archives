Rules for Harpers

1. Be courteous and respectful of everyone you come in contact with, regardless of race, level, or class.

2. Be trustworthy and honorable...Harpers should be trusted and respected.

3. Kill Stealing, camp stealing, ninja looting and other bad behavior is strictly forbidden.

4. Guards, treants, druids (and other good-aligned NPCs) are not to be hunted.  The exceptions to this rule are: quests and self defense.
