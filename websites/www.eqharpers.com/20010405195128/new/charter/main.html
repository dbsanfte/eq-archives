Harpers Charter
Updated 10-27-00


By way of this charter of the Harpers of Norrath, being formed this nineteen hundred and ninety-ninth year of Verant (tm) on the twenty-seventh day of the month of October, I, Alvimar, do hereby seek petition to form the Harpers of Norrath in the interest of all citizens, to help guide and assist in whatever means available to us, as a guild or individual, on the server known as Brell Serilis, to be a force for good always available to the citizens at large. 

Harpers should be trusted wherever they go.  To that end: be honest, honorable, trustworthy, and think of others while traveling the land.  Verifiable kill stealing with be dealt with severely, up to and including expulsion from the guild.  Ninja looting, camp stealing, and other dishonorable behavior will be dealt with as severely.  Greed is not a Harper trait.  To that end, item farming, especially to the detriment of other players trying to hunt in an area for experience is frowned upon. 

Harpers care for their own, as well as others.  Item requests should be made to the Treasurer, who will fulfill them as best he/she can.  Begging for items from other players is strictly forbidden.  

Primarily we are out here to have fun. If at any time this is more labor than fun for any member, a vacation from the guild will be considered an option, with no ramifications from the guild to be expected, and easy access back into the guild will be available to all vacationers.  The limit on this is: if you are taking a vacation from Harpers to visit another guild, you should let the officers know.  If you do not let someone know, and are seen in another guild, the assumption will be that you have left Harpers for the other guild. 

Guild leadership and guild meetings:

The guild leader, high counselor, 4 officers, and several committee heads lead the guild.  The officers are responsible for inviting and voting in new members, overseeing the committees, and reviewing and enforcing the rules of Harpers.  Committee heads are responsible for various jobs within Harpers.  They are also responsible for finding others in the guild to help them if they desire help. In most cases, the committee head does not need to be an officer.

The positions are:

Secretary (posting the meeting agenda and posting the meeting minutes)
Chancellor (organizes and runs the guild meetings)
Membership (keeping the membership list up to date and keep track of who is up for promotion etc.)
Guild Historian (writing stories about members, raids, events, etc.)
Treasurer (holding guild items and money)
Event Planner (organize and lead non-raid events)
Emissary (Keeping tabs on other guilds that we may like to ally with and courting guilds that we would like to notice us and invite us to raids.)
Enforcement (Enacting a set of guidelines and punishments - to be approved by the guild.  Note: this should probably be an officer)
PR/Recruiter (To actively seek new and worthy members)
Raid Planner (To organize and lead/assign someone to lead raids)
Webmaster (updates the website)

 These are volunteer/appointed positions.  If the appointed person is abusing their position, not fulfilling the job, or is otherwise unsatisfactory, please let the guild master or the high counselor know as soon as possible that it may be dealt with promptly.   Each committee will be assigned to an officer and that committee head is responsible for sending a report to the officer weekly via e-mail (or other agreed-upon media) for the guild meeting.

Guild meetings are held weekly in Butcher Block at 3pm (pacific) for approximately an hour.  Officers are expected to attend.  If an officer cannot make it, a proxy should be sent to another officer.  Proxies will allow one officer to speak and vote for another officer.  A proxy should include: any and all reports to be given by the absent officer and views/votes of the absent officer.  If no views and votes are given, the officer holding the proxy should use his/her best judgment to vote as the missing officer would have voted.

To get to know everyone, it would be good to have a face to match a name.  So, all members (especially new members) are encouraged to send in a picture or screenshot of their character to jillie@eqharpers.com or webmaster@eqharpers.com
