Name Alvimar Beene

Character Biography:

Not much about the rascal in blue is let into the public eye, but this should shed some light.

Life for the half-elven is interesting at best and such is my tale, one of a happy wood elf bard and human rogue in another plane known as Shadowdale.  Life is seldom boring in this realm though and the early demise of my bardic father Alishan left our family wandering when I was but a lad barely recalling the strains of my father.

During this wandering the natural attraction and fascination with all things musical lead me to meet a wondrous group of people known as Those Who Harp, or more simply the Harpers.  The more time spent in their company, the closer to their ideals I was drawn.  Then I met one of the Chosen and life took on new meaning.  Magic and the desire to keep it from driving fear in the harts of the common man became a passion well worth spending sweat and tears in the songs of the Silver-haired bard of Shadowdale, Storm Silverhand.

Through the Chosen the realm of Norrath was found and an emissary sought to scout the realm and further the cause of maintaining the wonder of magic more than it's inherent power.  I became that emissary and continue to seek those of like mind now.
