City of Mist Falls to the Harpers

     Urgent! For distribution to all Kunark Outposts immediately.

Early reports from the scouts in the Antonican Bard Courier Service of Kunark report that a raiding party led by the Harpers entered the legendary City of Mist and laid waste to all evil they found there. A force of approximately 20 people entered the old stone walls in the early afternoon Saturday, December 2, 2000. Losses within the walls were reported to be very minimal, despite the heavy resistance put up by the spiritual guardians of the lost City.

The pulling efforts of Passarinho resulted in orderly fights that continually brought the force closer to their goal. The troops were led by the gruff orders of Clangadin, renowned dwarven paladin. Magical duties and crowd control were handled by Loranis, and main healing duties fell to Avoreen. But they could not do the job themselves, and the triumph of the day was shared by all the participants present.

Shortly after entering, the apparition of Lord Ghiosk was found residing in one of the black metal animations known as reavers. He fell swiftly to the blades of the Harpers. The victory celebration was short, as the raiders began the siege on the castle, and on to the magical walkways above.

The narrow confines of the translucent walkways presented unique challenges, but quick minds and sure feet prevailed to keep most everyone alive hundreds of feet above the fractured walkways below. After the grueling fight up into the skies, the group entered the first of the floating buildings.

The guardian behemoths and other monstrosities were cleared until only the black reaver of Neh’Ashiir remained. The shell of the black iron golem was soon cracked, and the wispy essence of the Lady of the City floated above the heads of the adventurers. Blades crashed and magiks flew, until the only remains left in this world of the apparition were an Ornately sculpted Shield of Runes.

The Lord of the city was no where to be found, and so the raid was deemed a success and the brave members went their own ways, vowing to gather and exact their revenge on Rak’Ashiir soon.
