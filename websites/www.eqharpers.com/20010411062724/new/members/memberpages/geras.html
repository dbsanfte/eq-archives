Name Geras Darkbow

Character Biography:

    Geras was born in the great tree city of Kelethin, his mother was a Druid known as Lindra and his father, a Warrior known as Velon.  Both were adventurers following the ways of Tunare and well known among the local population.  At the age of 17, Geras's parents perished by the hand of the Crushbone Legions.  To honor both his mother and father, Geras sought to become part druid and part warrior.  He immediately requested to Hartwood Master that he join the Faydarks Champions to avenge his parents lost in combat. 

    Not long after he joined the Faydarks Champions he had met other adventurers that wished to battle the Orcs.  Geras and his friends  joined together to slay the Evil Emperor Crush, leader of the Crushbone Orcs.  Once in Crushbone the party had no trouble in reaching the castle.  Once they entered the Crushbone Castle, the small party made it past the throne room to the tower where the Evil King awaited.  After a glorious battle the party, battered and bruised had defeated the evil king and his followers.  Geras had avenged his parents, and resumed his training as a Ranger of Tunare under the guidance of Heartwood Master. 

  Having found his place in life, Geras continued to fight across the face of Norrath, meeting new friends and enemies along the way.  To this day he remains a powerful Ranger and will combat evil wherever it can be found.  If any evil threatens his friends or hometown Geras will be there to fight those beings.
