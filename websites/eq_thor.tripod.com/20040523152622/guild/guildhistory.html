GUILD HISTORY TIMELINE
May 16, 2001 Charter Approved - The House of Rahl Created

On this day, the House of Rahl passed it's first trials and became a recognized guild at the gates of the western city of Qeynos.
May 29, 2001 The House of Rahl is taken into a war against the Sacred Realm of Norrath.

These were hard times for us and we lost a lot of good men and women in that battle, but from the ashes we rose anew.
June 6, 2001 We declared war on the Gnolls of Blackburrow and thrust our swords deep into the heart of the dungeons below that evil mountain.
June 24, 2001 Naedian, our first junior officer, was promoted to the status of Officer, once again proving that it takes determination to reach goals hard sought after.
  And our history continues....
