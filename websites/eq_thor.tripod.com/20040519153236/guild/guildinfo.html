THE HOUSE OF RAHL - GUILD CHARTER
We are at war. Not a battle of countries or religions, but one of good against evil. As it appears in the ancient texts, magic is the source of all life. But there are those who would seek to destroy it's life giving essence. We are those who would choose to stand up and fight against those who seek to destroy life. We lift the weak, we teach the ignorant and we protect the innocent. We are the defenders of Norrath and all that stands for good. Join our cause and fight for freedom.
