GUILD ROSTER - HOW TO JOIN THE HOUSE OF RAHL
To join the House of Rahl, you need to first have a character between levels 1 and 5 on the Vazaelle Server. Then you need to log onto that server and find one of the officers listed below.

If you are above level 5, you need to find me, Guild Master Richarrd Rahl, I'm on most nights. Once you are a member of the guild, it is your responsibility to email me so that I can update your guild information here. This will not always be the case as I'm writing scripts so that each member can update their own information as needed.

Here are the rules of the guild once you are a member.

1) First and foremost, we are a good aligned guild, absolutely no EVIL allowed. The following races/classes are not allowed in our guild. -
(races) Iksar, Ogre, Troll, Dark Elf. (classes) Shadow Knight, Necromancers and Rogues (unless the rogue is being played in a 'Treasure Hunter' aspect.

2) The officers in this guild were chosen by me for specific reasons, do not badger them with requests for money or promotions to officer. If they feel it is in their power to help you, they will. If you step out of line and violate the guild bylaws and charter, at the discretion of the officer, you may be expunged.

3) If you desire to become an officer, take it up with me. I have specific qualifications for promoting members to officer, the first of which is - You must be level 15 to request promotion. If you do, then you will be put on probation as a Junior Officer, this comes with no real power, but you will attend meetings and have a say in goings on.
Once you reach level 20, I may grant your wish to become an officer, after you complete a quest designated by me. (Nothing too fancy) and then you will be charged with the responsibility of upholding our guilds honor with the rest of the officers. 4) You are not allowed to use the /anonymous and /roleplay functions. This is so that
   a) I can keep an eye on everyone online.
   b) EVERYONE knows you belong to the House of Rahl. There is no better advertising than a newbie who gets help from a member of our guild, then asks him/her if he can join.
That is all.
