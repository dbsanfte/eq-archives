Dragonheart is a land in which eight Realms rule. At birth denizenss start by choosing first a native realm. These orientations include, fire, water, air, earth, death, life, chaos, and order. Its denizens then must choose a profession from which to serve their realm. Each realm has different classes and races from which to choose. The followers of death may becomes vampires or liches, while the members of earth take the form of druids and rangers. Each realm is unique in both source of magic, and philosophy in which they live. The world of Dragonheart is a harsh one in which there is very little protection outside the realms they call home. Those who are ranked the most powerful of each element recieve certain additional powers, the top #2-#5 players have "hero" status which grants certain additional powers based on realm, the #1 player has a special power which grants the ability to polymorph into a dragon that supports the realm. The ability to transform into a flame dragon, or a dracolich is something to be feared.
