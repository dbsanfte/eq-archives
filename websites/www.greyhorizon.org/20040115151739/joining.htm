Do you want to be a part of Grey Horizon?  Then read on...

 

Requirements:

1)  A good attitude!

2)  Know how to play your character.

3)  Level 62

 

If you meet these requirements then post on our potential members messageboard.  Make sure to create an ezboard account with the same name as your in game primary character name.  Post at least your character name, level, class, and what keys or flags you have.  Once this is done, you can either contact an officer (officers are listed on the members page) or wait for officer to contact you.  You can also contact officers Sarkus or Albertt in game.
