You have seen the ads with the three sexy women kicking butt and taking names in the new movie, "Charlie's Angels".
Reviews of Leldara's performance could not be more glowing. Everyone agrees it's a major hit and she's a major score, er....star.  
All of you want to hear from the star herself so recently, Leldara   took time out of her busy schedule to med with a reporter from Rolling Boulder. Here are some excerpts from that interview.

Q:  Ms. Leldara, you were one of the first stars chosen for this movie. Why do you think the producers wanted you so badly?
L:  Honey, EVERYONE wants me. I am Leldara .
Q:  The on-screen chemistry between you and the other two costars is electrifying. Can you comment on that?
L:  Honey, EVERYONE wants me. I am Leldara .

Q:  At the West Coast premiere party, you seemed to get along very well with the other two angels. When you joined the cast, did you know that would happen?
L:  Drew and I had known each other prior to the shoot.When Lucy came on board, I knew right away we would be best friends.

Q:  Speaking of Lucy, there have been rumors of Lucy fighting with co-star Bill Murray. Can you comment on that?
L:  Oh, it was just silly. Lucy and Bill had a creative issue because Lucy felt Bill had a nicer robe on in that one scene they did together. I'll admit Lucy was a bit underdressed and my fashion alarms were in the warning stage, but there was no fight. It's funny how the press exaggerates these things.

Q:  Is it true that you did all your own stunts?
L:   Yes. The other girls trained for months prior to the shoot but I was a 25 Mage at the time. There was nothing I couldn't handle. I didn't even need my pets!

Q:  So what's next for Leldara ?
L:  Oh, a fashion shoot in Milan, a new episode of  FASHION EMERGENCY!, and a promotional tour for Maybellenium Cosmetics.

Q:  Thank you for taking time to speak with us but before we go, may I have your autograph?
L:  Certainly. Now remove your hand from my knee before you draw away a bloody stump.

Leldara's  new ad campaign certainly struck a cord with her fans.
Vogue met up with Leldara   at a recent guild event to discuss her new ad.

V:  Ms. Leldara, you have never been one to shy away from the camera and this new ad is no exception.
L:  We really wanted to get people's attention.
V:  It worked! Where did you ever come up with the idea?
L:  Oh, it's an original, of course. I felt that it was time to make a bold statement.

Q:  Well, it is an intensely alluring pose.
L:  Thank you but I was just being myself.

V:  Were you concerned that this ad would suggest you were into S & M?
L:  Oh, there's always going to be someone who can't get past the whip and the leather.

Q: And someone who doesn't want to!
L:   I'm not going to argue with good taste.

Q:  So will we be seeing more of this new look in Norrath?
L:   I think I have a responsibility to do all I can for all the fashion needy, so I would say, yes.

Q:  Oh, one more thing. There's a rumor that you used a body double for this photograph. Is it true?
L:   Of course not. Check all my photographs. I came equipped with those curves.
