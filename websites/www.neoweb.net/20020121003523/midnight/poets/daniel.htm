Poetry By: Daniel Collyer

MyLove

My Love is like a Bluebird
Soaring high bove the sky
It's seeking out your company
If only you would try
We could fly so free together
And maybe everyone could see
That what we are together
We are surely meant to be


My Love is like a candle
It wavers in the wind
If you push the flame too hard
It's almost certain to give in
It's purpose is to show you
The way you want to go
Without it life has no meaning
you might as well just go
