     The members of Divine Alliance are a close group of people with one common goal. That goal is to form a family of long lasting friendships with one another while having fun playing Everquest. It is this close nit family attitude that we base all other actions upon.
     Divine Alliance members attempt to help one another out in anyway, shape, or form. Be it that a fellow member needs help with a quest, camping an item, or other situations that may arise. Whenever grouped with one another we watch one anothers backs and never leave family behind.
     Every member of DA has their own individual wants, needs, and even expectations of their charcters that they play. It is the intentions of DA members to help each other achieve these goals as much as possible, while still striving to achieve their own goals.
     The greatest attribute of any member is Loyalty. We value this beyond all other characteristics because this alone guides all other actions. Once attained, everything else falls into place, and the guild is that much stronger because of it.
     The family of Divine Alliance treats each person with dignity and respect. No one person is more important than another. Basically "Do Unto Others as You Would Have Them do Unto You."
     To become a member of the family, a person must be a team player, have a good heart, and hold themselves to the highest standards of conduct. If at anytime it is found that any member of DA is no longer upholding these values, then a guild vote shall be taken to decide this person's outcome.
