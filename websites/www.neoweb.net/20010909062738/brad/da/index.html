The Divine Alliance is an honorable role-playing guild that adventures in the vast and wondrous lands of Norrath. We are a guild that seeks to promote justice and purge our world of evil anywhere it rears its ugly presence.To accomplish this task we enlist all good races and classes as we thrive off our diversity. Not only does our diversity allow us to seek out evil in the farthest and most remote reaches of Norrath, but it also allows us to maximize the potential of each different class. Our only other defined mission in Norrath is to have a great time playing online with one another and playing the game! If you aren't out to have fun, then why play at all?
