Poetry By: Nena

My Black Angel

In a wooden char beside my window.
I watch the pouring rain.
I talk to shadows from a lonley candle.
I can´t explain .... this pain.
the walls will tell the story of my pain.
Waiting for my black angel to ease my pain.
He is the one that can ease my sorrow
my love for him will help me find my way.
my Black angel,
always in the dark when light is what you need.
