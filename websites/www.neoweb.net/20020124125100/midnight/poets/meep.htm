Poetry By: Josh

Kingdom Of Fire



May I ask thee squire
Is there another Sire
To replace I…
Now the truth does come
I have no home
Within the kingdom.
Now in the darkness of night
I set outta sight
I’ve made up my mind
Now its time
For my last and only desire
To see them all perish including the sire
Should I use Ice or Fire
I choose fire
Now they all burn bright
Much like a fire at night
I see the hot embers in flight
Fire, Fire
Burning higher
Destroy them all
As the kingdom does fall
I can hear the screams as they call
Help us, Help us
And in spite of it all
They admitted their sins
That’s all it took for me to save them
My guardians are now descending
To the fire below
Before it burns them below
The guardians are now ascending
With the ones who did confess their sins
Still there are many sinners
That is burning in the fire of everything
Because they do not believe in only one King.
Are you still Burning?



JDS 10/08/00
