Poetry By: Lioness

HIM

I think about him.
I dream about him.
I even write about him.
In my mind,
The light is dim,
And I see
No one but him.
I have a small problem.
Because it seems
That my dreams
Will probably be ripped
Up at the seams.
Even though my thoughts were caught,
I cowered away
While my shallow confidence fought.
"Tell him the truth!", it said,
"He won't bite!"
But I looked in his eyes,
And it just didn't feel right.
So, now I'm forced
To secrecy once again.
And it drives me to
Think, dream, and write about him.
Hopefully, one day soon,
My confidence will have
A victorious win.
