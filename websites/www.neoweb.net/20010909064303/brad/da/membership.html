The Divine Alliance's Joining Rules:

Long ago, The Divine Alliance did away with its formal recruitment policy. We were comfortable in the size we obtained (approx 35 members) and did not wish to grow more. We never set out to be the biggest guild on the server, or the most powerful. Overall, we are not a powergaming guild. We do have members that play much more than others, but as a whole, it is not the way to define us. If that is what you are looking for in a guild, there is no need to read further.

We reached our desired size, but have never prevented further guild invites. When excellent people are found, we will not force them away due to a membership cap.

The Divine Alliance is not a highly structured guild. We have a gm, and several officers, but are not militaristic in nature. We could better be defined as a group of friends that enjoy gaming together. We have no race, class, or level prerequisites for membership. We offer invites to those we feel would add something to the guild, making it a better place for all, through participation, both in action and conversation. These people are found from spending time with them online...hunting, talking, etc.

If you are interested in becoming part of our family, what you need to do is spend time with us. Actively seek us out and group with us--as many members as you can. Since we are comfortable in our size, this means we take little to no active part in finding groups for you. You must take the initiative and show us you are interested and that you would make a great addition to our guild.

Obviously, this can take time. Groups can be difficult as it's often a matter of "right place at the right time" to get included in them. Do not be impatient, some people wait months before getting enough time in with members. If some members take particular liking to you, they will probably try to help you meet others, thereby accelerating the process.

The key thing to remember, is you must come to us, we will not come to you. This does not mean we do not want to group with people outside the guild or meet new people, it just means we don't actively seek out people for membership, as we are comfortable with what we are already. Show us our family would be better with you in it. While we prefer people that have been playing awhile, and know how to play their class exceptionally well, we also love people that are talkative, friendly, and good natured. A good sense of humor is mandatory however =)

Rane Westwind
Officer
