Regrets

gives me enough drive to sign up now.
If i get shot and die
I dont think anyone will really care anyway
Im expendable to everyone
If i would happen to kill some people

Maybe i do need to join the army and get shot
Help me end the pain
that is in my mind constantly

Lack of Interest

The World is dark
Like it always has been
Never have i seen light
I probably never will

Hating people makes me feel alive
I dont like it
But it does make me feel alive

Understanding
Something that no one bothers to waste on me
Possibly they will fear more by understanding
Who the hell knows or cares?

Ive tried so hard to love this planet
To love my life
To love others
It's only brought more pain
No good has come from it
It saddens me to know now that there is indeed no hope for me
I guess i will take it in stride
And be a bitter, dark person.

I hate my heart
It's so black
It's so incapable of being human
Yet i am human
That i do not understand
I wish i could just be locked up
I probably will one day

I hate not needing anyone
it would be nice to need someone by your side
but frankly it's not worth the trouble
You always get screwed over in the end anyway

Either the person you need doesnt need you back
they may not be faithful to you
they may just play games with your heart
once again
who the hell knows and
who the hell cares

I just wish that whichever entity
contains my soul
Would come and claim me as well
So that i no longer have to be on this world
God or Satan
It hardly matters which comes to get me
I dont care
I just hope that if it's Satan that claims me
He lets me bestow all my pain and suffering onto the world
Instead of being tortured.
He can torture me later
Just let me have my fun
If it's god that comes to claim me first
i hope that he brainwashes me so that i forget all that which has hurt me

-Scream
