Poetry By: Misti

Microcosm

I no longer believe in loneliness
(hesitating little lies we tell
to pretend we aren’t afraid.)
And I no longer believe in fear
(excuses made to keep us from our laziness
at trying something new.)

No, I no longer believe in
your mantras (slowly worked
a chasm
into my passionate belief in life)

Now

as I claw out.
bleeding, dirt caked fingernails
hang onto the earth-
and I believe in that earth
which chokes
burns
tastes like salty bitter death
on my lips…
…and pain in every limb
as I creep ever steadily forward
….upward……….
I believe in that pain
a sweet burning death
once dulled by every kiss you gave.

I am not lonely or afraid
without you.
I am not strong
Or brave.

I am a microcosm
A little universe of possibility.
A woman fully aware of desirability
and grass and sky and cool lapping water
around ankles when I’m not conscious
of falling in love….

But it’s there in the ripples of the tide
As I drown and resurface….
……………….I drown and resurface…..
……………………………….drown and resurface………..
…………………………………………………………….resurface…………
because I choose to suffer
rather than bear your numb cross
of safe and secluded dreams.







Weeping Willow

Quivering
I watch the vast flowing tendrils
of the weeping willow
race the wind toward the lake.
My wavering hands follow
the patterns of her dark and bony roots
which lie deep and strong.

Bound to our earth,
she may not drown into the shadows
of the cool muddy water;
but must witness shards of lightning
burn wicked nightmares in this storm.
As I huddle beneath her,
she shall dance
against the pelting drops of rain
her strands twisted in their shivering;
fear inaudible within this tempest.

Sly Park; August 1998

the sunlight was slowly fading
drowning into our shadow recesses
where we hid
among the silence.

we punctured the air
with charged mutterings and whispers
surrounded by the momentary flickers of possibility
existing once or twice a lifetime.

our passions spread through the cool lake;
from your smile,
to my muddy toes
we were full of nervous expectations.

Finally, stripping away the chill
I misplaced myself,
raising the camera between us:
I nearly captured your soul.
(there you always remain; a framed, perfect long ago.)

Tangled

You are tangled indecision
your desire
your displeasure
splinter me
into a mass of shattered pieces.

Hang your knots
upon twisted bony roots
raw and exposed
by those little whispering echoes
of your hesitancy
blowing
through
my hair.

Photographs


The light is my lover.
She is my depth,
my focus.
Even
in the deepest shadows,
the corners of dim hallways,
or the sprawling shade of an oak,
her shifting patterns emerge
to taunt me.
I linger
to witness her explosion
against the horizon,
struggling to capture her reflections
upon the cool marble headstone
hoping to become wise to her changing temperaments.
But she is eternal
wiser than my amateur eyes.
