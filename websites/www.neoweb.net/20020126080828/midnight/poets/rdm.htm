Poetry By: RDM

The Prisoner

the cell is dark and damp. cries of pain and fear
fill the air, coming from the other prisoners.
I am alone
in a cell meant for two. the extra space
invades me, haunting me. lonliness has consumed me.
time has only intensified my sentence.
I have forgotten my crime.
a click. her voice.
she puts the key in the lock and twists.
she enters the cell. she has a candle.
the candle drives back the darkness and brightens the cell.
we talk for hours. no, it's only a few minutes.
her visits always end too soon.
a click.
the key turns, locking the cell. she pauses for one
last look, then leaves.
I whisper to her but she's
gone.

-rdm 8/23/95
