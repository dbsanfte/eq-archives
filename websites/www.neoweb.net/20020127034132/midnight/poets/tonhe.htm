Poetry By: tonhe

darkest day


the days are short
and filled with rain
my heart is broke
and filled with pain

am I alive
i do not know
my mind is lost
i crawl down low

my face hidden
hands clutched
alone on the floor
my soul untouched

and here i am
lost in doubt
drenched in fear
alone... without

and now i go
i cannot stay
my soul escapes
forever this day


I'm Free


Don't grieve for me, for now I'm free.
I'm following the path God has laid, you see.
I took his hand when I heard Him call,
I turned my back and left it all.
I could not stay another day,
to laugh, to love, to work, or play.

Tasks left undone must stay that way.
I found my place at the close of the day.
If my parting has left a void,
then fill it with remembered joys.
A friendship shared, a laugh , a kiss.
Oh, yes, these things, I too shall miss.

Be not burdened with times of sorrow,
I wish you the sunshine of tommorow.
My life's been full, I savored much.
Good friends, good times, a loved ones touch.
Perhaps my time seemed all to brief,
don't lengthen it now with undue grief.

Lift up your hearts and peace to thee,
God wanted me now - He set me FREE!

untitled


Memories afloat,
he watches it all pass by.
With no control over his emotions,
all he can do is cry.

Built up,
is all of this hate.
Punches fly,
with no plotted rate.

Releasing,
all of the past deceit.
No stopping him,
he will not meet defeat.

Living,
with eternal underestimation.
The boy wanders,
thus not knowing his destination.

Been shot down,
every other time.
Its like his life,
isnt worth a dime.

Wondering how he has lived,
with such regret.
No one has found him,
first one he's met.

He screams out,
with an ear-piercing tone.
How,
could this one ever known?

Trying,
deeply to behave.
He feels all that,
feeling of a slave.

Living now,
but dying to cope.
No one to find me,
in my mind there is no hope.
