Poetry By: torzan

Butterfly

Butterfly
Such dark
and beautiful colors

Dancing
upon a grey
and boring sky

Sparkling
You shine
reflecting unemitted light

You stepped here
You stayed
You had to fly away

I knew that
Yet I was joyous
You appeared joyous too

But those
were only your colors
And that
only my sky.

And there you were
dancing
With such dark
and beautiful colors

Dancing
upon my grey
and boring sky

My Secret

I am not crazy.
I am not sane.
Just travel on a journey,
Climb inside my brain.
And you will see the beauty of
The dark and cold wet rain,
That is pouring heavily outside
My silent window pane.
Only then will you ever see,
The pain that can't be slain.
