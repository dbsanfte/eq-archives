News: Undectable Hacking on the Horizon?
Posted by Blayloc on Saturday, July 20 @ 23:02:34 EDT (7 reads)

(taken from the Vetgamer.com boards, posted by (VET)Codsack)


Apparently, in an attempt to boost their sales, the motherboard manufacturer and now videocard manufacturer ECS was testing the waters at putting a feature in their videocard that basically is the equivalent of wallhacking. They were calling this feature transparency and it has raised holy hell within the honest gaming community.

Look at the two pics under the transparency header:
http://www.vr-zone.com/reviews/ECS/AG400/page3.htm

Notice the second PIC under transparency is a shot of some doubledoors in de_aztec where you can see right thru them.

After an assload of hellraising and petitions, it appears that ECS has issued a statement that their *final* drivers will be released without this feature. Well, thats great for us but the thing that scares me it anyone who screws around with computers knows that sooner or later the drivers will either be hacked to enable transparency or the beta version of the driver with the feature will be leaked.

This is extremely unfortunate as this type of blatent cheating will be virtually undetectable.
