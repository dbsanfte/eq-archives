Bringin' Back The Gods

 








 

 

 
Levinson Antilles

Human Monk

I suppose it is finally time to tell my tale, or at least a beginning. I
still think my past is something of a secret I care not to tell or reveal. So
instead, I will speak to you now, of how I first found some good in my life.

As I sat in the Seafarer's Roost of East Freeport drinking ale after ale I
became relaxed and for once was able to forget my life thus far. Pretty soon,
I was down to my last silver. In a drunken state I stumbled up and slammed
the silver coin upon the table and stammered, " 'Nother ale.pease". The
bartender, with a grin on his face, told me promptly, "You've had 'nuff
already you bum, and 'sides that, one silver is only likely to get you a
swift kick in the arse." Angry and bewildered I lunged at the bartender but
all I got was a fistful of air and a whack on the skull. The last thing I
heard before the darkness was some strange giggling. I awoke later in a bed,
less of a bed and more of a simple blanket made out of wolf's fur and draped
over some stiff boards. I strained to open my eye, but the immense pain from
my head kept me from doing so. Instead I heard two voices become louder,
voices engaged in a conversation. ."But, Master Puab you have taught us in
the ways of Quellios, all life is sacred yes?" "True," responded Master Puab,
"But he is really nothing more than a drunkard, a fool who has wasted his
life away, you can not honestly tell me that you hope to teach him the ways
of the monk. "You may mock me now Master Puab, but let me share with you a
dream I had only a couple of nights past..In this dream I saw the gathering
of sixteen armies, upon a endless plain of grass. As these armies clamored
for war, the sky grew dark and the clouds hid the sunlight. Rain poured down
and battle cries were shouted. The earth tremble ferociously as the armies
ran across the field to meet each other.and just as the battle was ready to
begin, a wonderful thing happened. Across the distance I saw thirteen lights,
and as they grew closer I realized they were no lights but beings..beings
that were all the races of Norrath. One race was blackened, but I could make
out a shape.These beings walked together and as they passed each army fell to
its knees as if praising them. The center being held something in its hands,
but the light blinded me from making it out. The beings then made a circle
and began chanting something in a strange language, They began to shift
together and the light,  the light it was so beautiful. Blackness swept the
world for but a moment and in the beings place was a Dragon.at least that is
what it appeared to be. At first many seemed to be afraid of the creature,
but their fear soon turned to shouts of praise.  And in this dream I was
told, told who would begin this change and possibly finish it as well. A
gathering is to take place, a gathering soon will be held in Freeport.and
this drunkard as you call him, must be there. A voice in my dream told me, I
would find a man consumed by his past but instrumental for the future. And
that his present was to be shaped, for this future. I believe in peace
between all races, I hold this belief in the highest regard. If this dream is
to hold any truth at all I must have your blessing to instruct in this man
all that I know.and if my mind has been wandering then I have at least saved
someone from certain death." " Very well, You have been a most stubborn
student but at the same time, one of my greatest. Although I displease of you
using this man for what you have seen in a dream, I grant your request and he
shall train under you. Be well, and may the light of Quellios guide you
always..Brother Antilles."
