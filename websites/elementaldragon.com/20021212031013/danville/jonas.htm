Press Release from Danville Ballet

Danville Ballet's Winter Gala to Feature
Diablo's Lauren Jonas

ALAMO, CA—Last minute preparations are taking place, costume
fittings, and dress rehearsals as the pre-professional Danville Ballet
Company gets ready to present their Winter Gala 2002, December 6 - 8 at the
Del Valle Theatre in Walnut Creek. The dynamic production has something for
everyone in the family including The Nutcracker, and Pointes of Light, a
seasonal celebration of music and dance. Due to injury, guest artist Keturah
Shelton will be unable to perform the role of the Sugar Plum Fairy in The
Nutcracker. The Danville Ballet Company is very fortunate that accomplished
dancer and choreographer, Lauren Jonas will replace her. Tickets are
available by calling (925) 943-SHOW.

Lauren Jonas, the artistic founder of the DIABLO BALLET, received her
training at the Marin Ballet. Ms. Jonas has danced professionally with the
Milwaukee Ballet, the Oakland Ballet, and the Southwest Ballet. She has
toured the United States, with the Moscow Ballet, represented the United
States at Expo '86 in Vancouver, Canada, and danced in a PBS documentary
film about Isadora Duncan. Dancing the role of the Nutcracker Prince will be
guest artist, Miroslav Pejic, also with the DIABLO BALLET and formerly with
Ballet West.

Our cast comprises sixty energetic dancers ranging in age from eight to
eighteen from throughout the Tri-Valley area. Other guest artists include:
Lewis Smith, Bay Area Actor as the Mouse King and Mother Ginger; David
Henry, performing the new role of the Gingerbread Man Doll; and Katarina
Wester-O'Rourke, formerly with the Royal Swedish Ballet, as the Soldier Doll
and Russian Soloist. The role of Dr. Drosselmeyer will be portrayed by Bay Area Actor Bruce Purrington.
