Bringin' Back The Gods

 








 

 

 
Amelya

Wood Elf - Bard

Keeper of the Giggle

 

Amelya peers through a window into the well lit tavern, seeing a few familiar faces in the crowd. With everyone paying attention to Nocturne's tale of the dwarf paladin, the beautiful sixteen year old wood elf enters unnoticed. She spots Kibray and Sumelin sitting beside one another, and Kiko, sitting backwards on his chair, all facing the stage away from her. She looks around the room at all the others present and is amazed at the diversity of the races represented.

As Nocturne's tale concludes, Amelya waves to Nocturne, still unnoticed by the rest of the crowd who are busy applauding Nocturne's tale and wiping a few tears from their faces. Giggling to herself, she brushes by Kibray and Sumelin with a wink and whispered 'hello' and positions herself behind Kiko. As the unsuspecting half-elf turns around, Amelya wraps her arms around his neck and locks him in a passionate kiss! Kiko stumbles and crashes back over his chair, quickly camouflaging himself and vanishing.

Amelya runs the tip of her tongue over her lips and giggles, as everyone turns towards the sounds of Kiko's crash to the floor and Amelya's giggling.

"Silly Kiko! Quit being so shy! I was only saying hello."

Amelya giggles and sets her banded helm on Kibray's table, along with all her weapons; a fine steel rapier, matching morning star and long sword, and a bandolier of throwing daggers. With a smile at Kibray and Sumelin, she glides through the tables and takes the stage, hugging Nocturne with an accompanying giggle as she passes her.

Hi everybody! I'm Amelya and I want to join The Elemental Dragon. But Kibray won't invite me and won't tell me why," she says, pouting prettily. "Sumelin wants me in the guild, don't you handsome?" Her voice drops to a conspiratorial whisper that still carries through the bar. "But we all know why he wants me in the guild don't we?" Amelya bubbles into laughter as the room full of people snickers at the blushing barbarian.

"But Kiko told me I should tell you all about me, and maybe if you like my story, you'll let me join." Amelya smiles as people settle down to here her story.

"Well, it all begins with my papa Tyrian. He's a ranger who was trained in Surefall glade. He's really good with his bow. But he gave up his giant hunting and bar hopping when he met my mama in Kelethin. They got married and had me!

"Papa and mama raised me real good, but I never was good at being a ranger like papa and mama, but I have a good singing voice and they hired the best music teachers in all of Kelethin to help me get better. I lived with mama and papa my whole life, but I got bored. The other elves are so strict about their music. They don't create, they just repeat what's been done. And they are so stingy and stuck up too.

"But a friend of papa's came by a little while ago and when he left, I followed him and stowed away on a big ship that took me to the human city on the east of Antonica. Oh, it was wonderful! The humans were so friendly and this big barbarian cast some magic that bound me to the city, all for just a kiss!

"And I think..."

Amelya locks stares with Saedara and giggles, running down off the stage and giving her a big hug.

"You offered to bind me there too! Oh, that means you were the first dragon I talked to!

Amelya lets Saedara go and bounds back onto the stage.

"I was real tired and went to sleep in a bar and had a scary dream. This pretty elf woman was walking along and got attacked by nasty, smelly orcs. She fought really well, but got hit real hard and fell down. Then a big shadow came out of her and hovered over the orcs and it was shaped like a dragon and had real big wings and green eyes and when it touched the orcs, they vanished. Then it vanished too and she sat up, looking none the worse for wear except for a bleeding nose. Then I woke up.

"I remembered the dream so well that I told it as a stroy when I first performed in the bar that night. Everyone liked it and asked to hear more about the pretty elf woman, but I didn't know anything else so suggested they come back tomorrow to hear more of the story. I sat at a table for hours trying to think of another story to go with it, but everytime I had a thought, it vanished like the orcs in my dream. I must have fallen asleep because then I had another dream.

"I saw a handsome hero in shining armor facing a nasty troll woman. He was yelling at her to give him some rings and she was laughing at him and at some others I couldn't see. I could see her leaving, without giving the handsome warrior the rings, but then i saw a great shadow I recognized. The shadowy dragon hovered above both warrior and troll and it gestured slightly at the troll. She immediately attacked the warrior with a big black sword. But he fought her off valiantly with a pair of gleaming black axes. The troll cried out to Innoruk, and I think the dragon smiled when nothing happened and the heroic fighter struck her down. Then I woke up.

"I told this story the next night, to an even bigger crowd, and while they were unhappy that the elf woman wasn't in it, they soon became entranced with the hero of the tale and my pockets overflowed with platinum by the end of the night. And the dreams came to me the next night again.

"I saw another troll, this time a man troll, hiding in a dark dungeon. He had the pretty elf lady tied up and was running a hot poker over her back, making her cry out. He must have punched her because her nose was bleeding again. My dream changed focus, switching from stranger to stranger, all who looked up and started running towards Freeport when I saw them, running to save the elf lady. But when they got near Freeport..."

Amelya looks around the room, and gasps slightly as she focuses on each person, a look of recognition in her eyes. Her eyes linger a bit longer on one half-elf and she mumbles something about a hero before gathering her wits and continuing on in a shaky voice.

"But when they got near Freeport, my dream stopped and it was all dark except for two green lights. Eventually, I came to see shapes and found myself face to face with the shadowy dragon. I reached out to touch him, but my hand passed through his snout as if he wasn't quite real. Then his gaze stole my breath and I saw the city of Halas. And I saw a half dozen thieves chasing a barbarian woman and striking her down in the bank, killing her. Then the dragon spoke.

"He said 'Go there and make this final thing you have seen happen. Go to the desert and follow the darkness.'

"Then I woke up."

Amelya giggles to break the silence in the room and to calm her own nerves, then continues in a bubbly voice.

"Then I went out to the desert. It's a scary place, but real pretty if you can ignore the mean nasties that try to kill you. But I met Kibray and Zastrum there and we talked and I mentioned Halas and Kibray said she was going there and I just felt like I should go with her so I asked if I could go and she said sure so we went together and she took me through the desert and swamp and ogre place and I saw the this ogre and it was big and..."

Amelya gasps, completely out of breath and giggles at her own excitement.

"But she wouldn't let me kiss it, you can tell alot about a person by kissing them you know, and you can get lots for a kiss! Look at this," she says, running her hands over the curves of her armor. "I kissed Zastrum a few times and he got Sumelin to make me this armor! He kept saying it's a gift from Sumelin, but I know better because Sumelin gave me this pretty necklace and that shiny morning star too, and all for a few kisses!"

Amelya smiles at the deeply blushing barbarian and wizard and then continues.

"And we got to this big lake and I hate swimming, but I swam it anyway and saw these orcs and big eagles that walked on the ground! Then we went to the Karana's and I showed Kibray an ancient teleporter that papa told me about. It was real neat and made us feel a little sick and tingly too! And then Kibray showed me through the gnoll house, and oh, what a smell! But we finally got to Everfrost. I had lots of maps and we found Halas no problem. But we were tired and then Zastrum showed up and he showed us his house. He's got such a big bed! Big enough for all three of us! And that's where he gave me this armor!"

Amelya laughs at the extremely red faced wizard as he tries to hide behind an nearby ogre.

"Look at him blush! He was so silly, he slept on the cold floor. What a ninny.

"The next day Kibray and I hunted polar bear cubs and collected a whole lot of white skins that she was using to bribe the guards into not killing her. And then I met Nocturne and Sumelin and Kiko and they all helped lots! Then we finally got her safe with the guards and a whole bunch of thieves jumped her in the bank, even though she was wearing an illusion of..a..barbarian.."

Amelya's voice trails off and she looks at Kibray, wide-eyed and with amazement in her face. Then she claps happily!

"You're the darkness the shadow thing told me to follow, aren't you? And you're the barbarian that I saw get killed in my dream! You were testing me the whole time, weren't you! No wonder you wouldn't tell me how to join your guild. You wanted me to figure it out on my own and to figure out I already had been asked!"

Amelya jumps off the stage and starts towards Kibray's table, but stops in front of the ogre the wizard is hiding behind.

"Can you move? Zastrum looks like he needs a kiss," she says to the ogre. When Zastrum continues to hide, she shrugs, then jumps up and wraps her arms around the ogre's neck and kisses HIM deeply for a good thirty seconds. She lets go finally and fishes a white finger bone out from her mouth. Dropping it on the table in front of the ogre, she grins up at him and says, "Here you go, that was stuck in your teeth!"

Amelya giggles, ruffles the ogres hair and runs over to Kibray, giving her a big hug.

"Thank you Kibray, for everything! Can I join your guild now? Can I please?"
