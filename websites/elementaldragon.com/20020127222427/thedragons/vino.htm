Bringin' Back The Gods

 








 

 

 
Vino

Gnome - Magician
Well I come from a long line of Ak'Anon tinkers. A common profession I know but my very own grandpa is the inventor of the walkulator! Oh I know. You're all thinking of the ankle massacre back a few years ago but they were all most definitely not following the instructions!

Still, after that I knew that I would have to have something other than tinkering to fall back on. So I packed up the burb knife and my beloved dweeblewhacker and followed the nearest guide halfway to the Ak'Anon spellcaster guild until it fell apart halfway there. I fortunately stumbled upon the building only three days later. Not bad without the guide eh?

After they tended to my parched lips I took the placement tests. Due to my manual laziness and thirst for knowledge I was pegged almost immediately as a Mage. Little did I know their myriad uses at that point. Kabantik, my fire elemental is roasting me a fine kodiak loin as we speak but I digress.

I was not long for that place either however though there was a few heady months. The Burst of Warmth drills. The hours of Summon Lump. There was great comraderie during our after hour battles with each others' pinkymentals. Still it was all not enough. One could practically see the grooze of previous footsteps on the path I was walking.

One night then I had a dream where I hard the words of the great tinkers all speaking as one. I hesitate to tell it to non-gnome ears as much of the language doesn't translate very well but here goes.

"Left to your works, the boat won't leave unless the pin has been pulled. Oh and turn that screw clockwise before it blows!"

Obviously it was time to leave. Time to make my own way.

So off I went from the busy underground to the bright overland and have yet to return. In the search for knowledge I've begun as per the wishes of the great tinkers I've come to the conclusion that most mortals have only so much to teach. The only ones whom I can learn from and never stop learning from are the gods themselves. So until I have them in front of me, I will continue to walk Norrath picking knowledge from the branches.

And turning the screw clockwise.
