Burog

Troll - Shadow Knight
Never before has a Troll so enthralled me. Slowly but surely, Burog has learned the art of the 'sparklies', guided by the dubious Kibray, and guided by his own sadness. A kind Troll, dreams of revenge to the Gods, and of learning to Foxtrot

Me maw cook for da big barshin man, me paw-paw work for da big barshin man. Big barshin man not gud. Him mean to me paw-paw and maw. Me paw-paw tell me not work for big barshin man. Go work for strange barshin man. Me go to strange barshin man and ask join. Him say yes, but me need know of Cazic-Thule and all bad things him do to me if me not make everybody fear Cazic-Thule. Me
say ok.

Me was barshin froggies in da swamp when me heard thunder and saw sparklies movin at me. Me scared. Scared Cazic-Thule mad at me and was goin barsh me gud. But it was brudda troll makin sparklies at another froggie. But me so scared me get barshed dead by froggie. When me wake, me paw-paw ask what happen. Me tell him. Him go to strange barshin man to barsh him. Me never see paw-paw again.

Strange barshin man, now me boss barshin man, say him kill me paw-paw for me paw-paw not fear right. Him say Cazic-Thule kill me paw-paw and kill me too if me not be scared right. Me know better. Me smarter dan him tink. Me know him kill him. Me know Cazic-Thule made him kill me paw-paw. Me WILL kill strange barshin man. Den me WILL kill Cazic-Thule.

Me no scared no more. Me know what me do. Strange voice say Freeport. Me go there so me can get me barshin done on bad god.

Do no be afraid. Fear tool of bad gods.
Me make them pay for making me fear for too long.
Burog - Troll formerly in service to Cazic-Thule
