Alliance of guilds on Seventh Hammer Server
Purpose; To increase communication between guilds on the server to further enhance the game play experience.
To provide the means for guilds (small or Large) to accomplish goals and Have fun through events and Raids.

All guilds that exsist on Seventh Hammer Server are welcome to join this alliance regardless of status, evil or not, roleplay based or not. There is no application to join. Simply E-mail Azula with a rquest to be placed on Guild Listing.

Each guild will have 1 to 2 Advocates to represent their guilds. Each guild will be responsible for their advocates behavior on the council.

Each guild will have 1 vote in regards to Alliance decisions and issues. Every guild has equal say on matters. One guild is not more important than another regardless of size.

The alliance will announce to the public through press releases, any and all events raids etc. that it holds.

All Alliance Events are open to members, non-members, Guilded or Non-Guilded alike.

The Alliance Members will respect all guilds/chars wheither or not they are a part of this alliance or not.

The Alliance is not responsible for each individual guilds conduct, behavior, etc.

The Alliance will not dictate how each guild is ran/organized individually.

The Alliance will not resolve conflicting guilds problems or dictate how they resolve those problems.

All members of the Alliance will benefit from it depending on their input into it, in regards to, planning and participation.

Individual Alliances formed on the server will be respected and recognized as brought forward to the Guild's Alliance Council.

The Alliance is not a Governing body.

The Alliance is about RESPECT.....Respect for members, non-members, guilded, and unguilded alike.

Each Guild will be encouraged to Host Raids & place on public boards all details of such raids.

Each guild will have their set of Raid Rules and the hosting guild Raid rules will be followed if you plan to attend that raid.

The purpose of the alliance is to encourage friendships, groupings and communication of guilds on the 7th Hammer server.

This site is still under construction.
