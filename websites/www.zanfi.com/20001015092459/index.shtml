Patch Day
Sensei Jagriel - September 26, 2000 @ 7:27 PM (WST)

A few interesting notes in this patch that affect us:

1. Weight reduction at 55 and 60 implemented; the exact amount is as yet unclear, but almost certainly will be 4 pounds at each benchmark. Nice.

2. Max Haste fixed. There is a lot of whine happening on the monk boards over this one, but frankly I don't see an issue with it. We rode the bug for a long time, and now there really is a difference between a Cof or RBB and a FBSS, as it should be.

3. Damage bonus minimized for weapons with a delay lower than 28. This burns those few with a RFS, at least slightly...we'll have to see what the exact change is to be sure if it really is noticeable. Weapons with a delay of 28-39 are the same, and any weapons with a delay 40+ get a bonus increase. Again, let's see how this plays out.

A reminder that we meet in two days usual place and time to continue our work on the FoF...please come prepared with your own contributions to the event, both material and physical for the day itself.

Master...you look...stronger...
Sensei Jagriel - September 19, 2000 @ 4:08 PM (WST)

Yes, in case you hadn't heard, Verant has posted their intent to increase our class weight/AC penalty at the higher levels...most likely 55 and 60, and tentatively by 4 pounds for each of those levels. This would cap us at 22 pounds instead of 14 at 60, and 18 at 55, allowing the use of better armor, or perhaps using a tranquil staff or other weighty weapon without penalty.

Monk response has been mixed, surprisingly, with many stating that it should happen at lower levels (40-50-60, or 50-55-60 for example), probably in response to largely ignored sore spots with monks that are more important, such as Shiverback AC. Still, this is a nice bonus to look forward to.

In other news, major congratulations to Kuanshankein for acquiring his Robe of the Whistling Fists! /cheer

A reminder to all Zan Fi to post inventory and areas of interest for the upcoming FoF, and to attend tomorrow night's meeting for preliminary assignments.

Festival of Fists General Information
Sensei Jagriel - September 12, 2000 @ 7:50 PM (WST)

Hail!

The Zan Fi welcome you, and invite you to participate in our second server-wide event, the Festival of Fists!

Tailored to assist monks, or players interested in monks, the FoF is a weekend (exact date soon to be announced) offering:

1. Seminars in Feign Death, Beginning Feign Death Pulling, Advanced Feign Death Pulling, Trade Skills, Languages, Panel on a Monk's Role, Equipment - Armor, Equipment - Weapons, Equipment - Jewelry, with room for more.

2. A series of raffles (free registration) with donated items being awarded to winners in each level class. Current breakdown is 1-20, 21-35, 36-50, 51-60, again with specialty items being awarded to the winners in each category.

3. A pair of tournaments, both one-on-one Kumite, open-hand, as well as three-on-three Kumite, with choice of weaponry. Prizes awarded to winners in each class, with special awards granted by the tournament panel.

4. VJ's Brewhouse, including periodic demonstrations of his famous "drunken monkey" form. Often accompanied by lewd remarks and his trademarked, "Bah!," this promises to be both the funniest and scariest entertainment we have to offer.

More in the planning stages, look here for details.

Volunteers are encouraged to assist in areas they can, as simple as a donation and as magnanimous as what I hope to bribe Veranon to do, which is run an alchemy "booth" for a time convenient for him, offering critical monk potions (sow, gates, etc.) at a discounted cost, offset of course with funds and components the Zan Fi will provide him in advance. Thanks V! :p

We hope to have the direct support of our allied guilds, Order of Eternity and Evoluxa, who have been and remain to be outstanding friends and comrades.

Thanks for your consideration, and we hope you will join us in any way you can. Visit our message board for more detailed information.

Jag
