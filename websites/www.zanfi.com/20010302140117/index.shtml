Next Meeting
Sensei Jagriel - March 1, 2001 @ 2:06 PM (WST)

The Zan Fi monks will meet on Monday, March 5th, at 6 pm PST at the North Ro Totem. Officers are asked to arrive one hour prior to conduct guild business before the general meeting.

I ask for all of you to attend this meeting: we will be saying goodbye to one of our most beloved brothers, Rakol, who leaves Everquest to pursue real life opportunities. Rakol, as much as any of us, truly embodies the spirit of the Zan Fi and the True Path, and was a source of inspiration for many many monks, both in and out of our guild. Please join me in wishing him only the best of luck, and thanking him for his long service.

Fist and Faith

Jag
