Parting Wishes
Sensei Jagriel - March 25, 2001 @ 10:19 PM (WST)

It has been 20 months since the seed of the Zan Fi Monks was born, out of the voluntary work I had started in the commons, back when the Brell Server was in its infancy. Like many of you who came later, I was attracted to the idea of a Dojo, of the monk class being a true brotherhood/sisterhood, and wanted to have a hand in creating that atmosphere. When I look back, I am proud of what such small beginnings have become. This is easy to communicate and the simplest part of this message, my final message, to you.

What is difficult to make you all understand is the frustrations I have felt that have been accumulating for over a year. I want to stress first off that it is due to no fault of any existing members, so please don't look internally at yourself or at the guild for an answer. The center of my reasons for leaving revolve around making Everquest an enjoyable place to be again, and the Zan Fi - regardless of the pride I felt for the guild - wasn't able to do that for me any longer. In a summary that does the growth of my angst no justice whatsoever: I was simply let down too many times along the way, both by individuals and by the nature and evolution of the game, and regardless of how I justified the reasons with my intellect, I felt betrayed. I watched our class become the top twink choice in the game. I saw a revolving door in and out of our guild. There are many more reasons, but I feel that stating all the specifics serves noone.
I wish to immediately follow this by stating that I have always had and still have the utmost respect for the current officers and my choice for successor, Sister Aaia, and have great faith in their ability to continue leading the longest standing class-only guild on any Everquest server. It is not their doing that has led me to this decision..as I said it is the cumulative effect of 20 months of leadership, and a vision that was never fully realized. Were my expectations too high? Perhaps. But we are driven by what we hope for, by what we are passionate about, and when things happen to douse those fires...well, there are only so many matches in the matchbox. I can only hope you can understand that it was long past time for me to move on, and my presence - carrying a building resentment noone in a game should have to carry - would only have become harmful. You deserve better, and as the guild's creator, I deserve better.

I had told you at my last meeting that I wished to walk alone for a time. I sincerely meant that. Many of you were upset by seeing me guilded (and an officer at that) in Elysian only days after the meeting. I can understand how you must feel, as this was the very thing - repeated over and over by some of my most trusted officers - that sparked my slow decline in fueling the spirit of the Zan Fi. I told Maya, after rejecting her request to join twice, that my joining Elysian would be on a trial basis until I worked out what I needed to do. The first night I received the invite I eventually accepted because those I had hunted with (daily for over a year) wished to talk with me about my feelings and how difficult things were. And honestly, I needed to talk with them. Why was I invited immediately not as a member but directly as an officer? Ask Maya. I didn't care what it was, as long as I had people that cared about me giving me the support I needed and still need. Elysian has been nothing but understanding, even when I told them 5 months ago that I would not join as a member because of my responsibilities to and desires for Zan Fi. They couldn't care less what guild tag was over my head: they saw me for who I am and what I do, as do some of you. Others seem quick to condemn: probably an outgrowth of feeling hurt, which I understand all too well, even though some of the tells I received in the past 24 hours, especially just prior to Sunshu and Shoshannah's wedding ceremony, have hurt me. I will add that rejoining Zan Fi was still a possiblity in my mind, up until the tells I received today. I thought I could possibly work out what I needed to work out, and possibly contribute more to the Zan Fi in the future. I realize now that this will never happen, and have accepted it. I don't know what else to say about it.

Some of you have expressed your support and friendship to me privately. Thank you. To those that feel otherwise I wish you the best regardless. I hope this final post has helped some of you to answer the "why?" questions you may have. For those new to the guild, it is a time of excitement and new beginnings, and your leaders are some of the best hearts in Norrath. I wish you well, Zan Fi.


Fist and Faith,

Jagriel Jubei
