Iksar or Icksar, weapons and shoulders galore, and meeting highlights.
Sensei Jagriel - May 16, 2000 @ 6:10 PM (WST)

Well, the feedback has been interesting over the past 5 days, since we decided against the Iksar joining us, and I suppose this comes from over 80% of you wanting to allow them into the guild if they complete the sash and headband quest. Thing is, we are really discriminating against them with both choices; I find the morals of my RL personality interfering with this decision. Basically, we chose to roleplay racism...that bothers me.

I have no solution, ironically, because allowing the Iksar in with Novice Quest adjustments (the most open-arms approach) doesn't sit well any of us, being as close to Quellious, and the principles of honor, respect and selflessness that go along with Quellious, as we are. So which way do we go?

I told all of you at the meeting that the road was open for the Iksar in the future...that maybe they can on an individual basis prove themselves and join us. Then I thought, later, that we do exactly the same thing with human monks..we make them prove themselves, in a way that WE determine represents the traits that are Zan Fi. With this in mind, I would like all of us to move toward investigating the Iksar life, beyond the obvious negative elements (evil god, faction, etc.) for a potential way they could prove themselves within the realm of reason for their class. This doesn't mean we are opening up, but I think with that knowledge I can rest easier about any decision we make as a guild. What we had last Sunday just isn't enough, in my opinion.

In more positive news, at our meeting we also decided that the two best ways we could improve our service in the guild was to:
1. Accelerate our aid to new monks in all areas (meetings, advice, basic equipment, etc.). Nagasakee heads this department, but all of us should consciously work to bring knowledge to our younger brothers and sisters. please keep this in mind as you travel and play.
2. Continue to work on server-wide events. The Festival of Fists is in the planning stages. Rakol again took the helm to draft an outline of ideas for the event /cheer! Preliminary discussions yielded a tournament, skill demonstrations (FD pulling - beginning and advanced), a skill point usage forum, trade skill forums, and even one act comedy performed by Zan Fi and friends, poking fun at our EQ lives and events happening in Norrath. Watch threads and contribute ideas and polishing as you can.

On the note of equipment, some nice monk drops in Kunark, primarily blunt weaponry, have raised some eyebrows in the monk community. The Master Wu Trance Stick is the most notable 1h blunt, at 7:18 proc smite (83dd), although many maces and hammers are popping up that are monk useable. As our 1h blunt caps 7 points higher than our fists with the expansion, it is clear that from an early age we should encourage the use of weapons. Shoulder pieces are abundant at all weights, the most popular being the Tattered Mantle (ac12 hp20 disease10 wt4.5 - heavy but awesome) the Drolvarg Mantle (ac7 agi6 fire5 wt1.5 - for hero bracer lovers), and the newly discovered Hand Woven Shoulder Guards (ac5 cold5 wt1.9 - a good vox raid shoulder piece). The Kunzar Mantle (ac7 dex3 agi3 wis3 wt4.5) is simply too heavy to compare to the other options above. The real shocker though was the Meditative Blanket (ac10 wis3 int3 str2 agi2 wt0.5 all/all magic item no drop lore), certainly one of our best options of all, the low weight giving us a legit shot at rolling against casters and healers. All of these are, of course, found on Kunark: more details can be sought here: http://everquest.allakhazam.com/

Congrats to Kuan on hitting 51, and many others who have been leveling lately...seems the expansion has brought new life to many players across Norrath. It is, so far, a wonderful piece of work Verant added to the game.

Our next meeting will be a midweek evening pst, Thursday the 11th of May, at 6 pm.

In heart and spirit,

Jag
