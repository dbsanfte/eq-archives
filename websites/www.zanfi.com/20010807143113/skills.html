One Handed Blunt
Level: 1
Max Per Level: (Level + 1) * 5; Capped at 200
Description: The ability to use one handed blunt weapons in combat. Although it may be wise to practice this skill for possible expansion weapons, it is doubtful that any weapon will surpass our bare hands for pure damage potential.

Two Handed Blunt
Level: 1
Max Per Level: (Level + 1) * 5; Capped at 200
Description: The ability to use two handed blunt weapons in combat. Again, doubtful that any staff will be a better choice than our hands.

Bind Wounds
Level: 1
Max Per Level: (Level + 1) * 5; Capped at 200
Description: Using this skill and bandages you can heal your chosen target, including self, up to 50% of their hit points. Very good skill for soloing, but somewhat expensive for a lower-level character to practice (non-summoned bandages cost approximately 5 silver each from vendors). Generally starts to be useful and affordable around level 15-20. It will heal for your skill/4 per bandage used. (Example: Skill of 52. 52 / 4 = 13 healed per bandage). Monks are one (if not the only) class that can achieve a very high skill in bind wounds; warriors and casters cap at 100 for example.

Defense
Level: 1
Max Per Level: * 5; Capped at 230
Description: The ability to defend against attacks. Used in calculating your Armor Class, with each skill raise increasing it a bit. Can not be trained at a Guildmaster.

Dodge
Level: 1
Max Per Level: (Level + 1) * 5; Capped at 200
Description: This is a skill that is checked automatically when you are attacked. If you are successful you dodge that attack and take no damage. You must train this skill at a guild master before it can be used. One point is usually sufficient, as the skill raises on its own during combat.

Dual Wield
Level: 1
Max Per Level: (Level + 1) * 7; Capped at 252
Description: This is one of the skills that define what a monk is. It is checked automatically when you attack something, and when successful you will attack with your second hand. At later levels it stacks with Double Attack, so that you have the possibility of getting up to four normal attacks during one round of combat. Monks are the first class to get this skill, automatically included at level one, and we also have the highest max per level and the highest possible max. Making monks the masters of Dual Wield.

Hand To Hand
Level: 1
Max Per Level: (Level + 1) * 5; Capped at 230
Description: The ability to fight with ones fists in combat. This is the best combat skill for a monk, because eventually a monk's fists arguably become the best weapon in the game. However, to attack some creatures you will need to acquire some magical gloves, until you reach level 30 when your hands become naturally magical weapons. Examples of magical gloves: netted, woven and mesh, and if you can afford them, Wu's Fighting Gauntlets (approx 50pp server average).

Kick
Level: 1
Max Per Level: (Level + 1) * 5; Capped at 200
Description: The first special attack that a monk gets and the only one that they share with other classes. It basically is an extra attack every few turns in combat.

Mend
Level: 1
Max Per Level: (Level + 1) * 5; Capped at 200
Description: This is a unique ability of the monk class. When successful it heals 25% of your life. However, it can fail and take 25% of you total life. It recycles every six minutes. Once you reach a skill of 100 you very, very rarely ever fail, but between 75 and 90 in the skill it tends to fail and worsen your wounds a great deal. You must train this skill at a guild master before it can be used. Strategies concerning the use of mend in combat vary between monks. Some prefer to use it immediately at 75% health to minimize the recycle time and save group mana; others prefer to save it for an emergency that may occur later in battle (unexpected mob spawns/wanders and attacks, for example). The choice is individual and is influenced greatly by your party strength and where you're hunting.

Offense
Level: 1
Max Per Level: [Level * 5] till level 15 then [(Level +1) * 5] till level 30 then [(Level + 2) * 5]; Capped at 230
Description: The ability to attack in melee combat. Used to generate your Attack Score, so that each skill raise also increases your ATK rating. Can not be trained at a Guildmaster.

Sense Heading
Level: 1
Max Per Level: (Level + 1) * 5; Capped at 200
Description: When successful this skill will tell you what direction you are facing based upon an eight point compass. It raises very slowly early on and the best way to increase your skill in it is to just use it all the time. In combat, when resting, when running, all the time. Once you have reached 50 skill or so it becomes much easier to raise and at later levels tends to be one of the first skills that caps when you reach a new level.

Swimming
Level: 1
Max Per Level: (Level + 1) * 5; Capped at 200
Description: The ability to swim through water. The higher your skill the faster you swim.

Throwing
Level: 1
Max Per Level: (Level + 1) * 5; Capped at 200
Description: The ability to use throwing weapons in combat. Generally used to "pull" a creature, that is to make the creature mad at you and enable you to then take him elsewhere (group base). Using a throwing weapon you get a slight head run. The shuriken is the favored throwing weapon of most monks for its range and the fact that it is relatively light. When you equip a throwing weapon it will sometimes appear that you have a dagger in your left hand, this is merely a graphical glitch equipping throwing weapons does not interfere with you dual wielding your fists.

Safe Fall
Level: 3
Max Per Level: (Level + 1) * 5; Capped at 200
Description: This skill is checked automatically anytime you fall. It reduces the damage that you could have taken from that fall. It can be very useful in very hilly terrain and if you need to run. The best way to raise your skill in this is to find a steep hill and run up and down it, this can cause several skill checks per "fall." For the skill to rise you must fall far enough that you would have taken damage if you did not have the skill. You must train this skill at a guild master before it can be used.

Round Kick
Level: 5
Max Per Level: (Level + 1) * 5; Capped at 200
Description: This is the second special attack that a monk gets and is the first monk only attack. It is an upgrade from Kick and will deal more damage when its skill has risen far enough. It also has a pretty cool animation. You must train this skill at a guild master before it can be used.

Sneak
Level: 8
Max Per Level: (Level + 1) * 5; Capped at 113
Description: When successful this skill allows you to make no noise when you move, however you move much slower using it. It can be used to sneak around creatures that are not facing you. It can also be used to buy/sell from some vendors that will not allow you to buy/sell with them but do not kill you on site (engage sneak, walk to rear of vendor, buy/sell).

Tiger Claw
Level: 10
Max Per Level: (Level + 1) * 5; Capped at 200
Description: One of the monks special attacks. It is an upgrade to the Round Kick. Generally the skill will do less damage per hit than the Round Kick at first, but Tiger Claw with skill raising soon becomes equitable, and recycles faster making it a clear choice in the long run. You end up doing more damage over time with Tiger Claw than Round Kick. You must train this skill at a guild master before it can be used.

Block
Level: 12
Max Per Level: (Level + 1) * 5; Capped at 200
Description: When successful this skill will block the attack of something attacking you. You can only block the attacks of something that is in front of you. This skill is checked automatically when you are attacked, if you have trained in the skill. You must train this skill at a guild master before it can be used.

Double Attack
Level: 15
Max Per Level: (Level + 1) * 5; Capped at 210
Description: This is a skill that is checked automatically when you attack. When it is successful you get an extra attack during that round of combat. It will also stack with Dual Wield, such that you can get in two attacks with both hands in one combat round. You must train this skill at a guild master before it can be used.

Feign Death
Level: 17
Max Per Level: (Level + 1) * 5; Capped at 200
Description: Arguably the most important skill that a monk ever gets. When this skill is successful anything that was attacking you will now think that you are dead and will stop attacking you. It is a major life-saver. It is also used to accomplish a move called the Feign Pull, which allows a monk to pull a single enemy from a larger group of enemies. Some notes about the use of Feign Death: there are various ways that Feign Death can fail. The first is the most obvious skill fail, you will know this happens when you receive a message saying "XXX has fallen to the ground." where XXX is your name. If this occurs, hit your Sit/Stand button and either continue fighting or running, until it recycles (10 seconds) and you can try again. Other ways that the skill can fail are in the way that it is used. For the best results you should always turn off auto-combat a few seconds before attempting to Feign Death, because if you attack between the time you hit the button and you fall down, then the creature will not believe that you are dead and will continue to attack you. There are things that can also disturb a successful Feigned Death. If an enemy caster begins to cast a spell, then you Feign Death, the spell will continue and when it hits you anything else around will also know that you are just faking being dead. So it is best to wait until a spell hits you and then Feign Death afterward. Area effect spells can also disturb a successful Feigned Death and cause things to attack you. Damage-over-time spells also seem to sometimes disturb Feign Death but not always. Some creatures, it seems, will sometimes just see through the Feign and continue attacking you. Some creatures will be initally fooled by the feign, and leave, but when you rise will come charging right back at you. Many monks when Feign Pulling, will fight a mob for a few seconds to "check aggro" on the ones that have left, to see if they will return: this avoids training a mob on your party. You must train this skill at a guild master before it can be used, and many monks recommend dumping a good amount of training points into it, as it raises slowly on its own.

Instill Doubt
Level: 18
Max Per Level: (Level + 1) * 5; Capped at 200
Description: This skill when successful will cause your target to become intimidated causing it to run away, much like a Fear spell. However, the skill is very hard to increase and is not successful very often. It can also be resisted like a spell, and can not be used on creatures that require a magical attack. The effects of the intimidation are very similar to a Fear spell, and like a Fear spell its effects do not last long. Yet, while the creature is fleeing you can freely attack its backside. You must train this skill at a guild master before it can be used.

Eagle Strike
Level: 20
Max Per Level: (Level + 1) * 5; Capped at 200
Description: One of the monk only special attacks. It is an upgrade to Tiger Claw and will deal more damage once its skill has risen. You must train this skill at a guild master before it can be used.

Haste
Level: High Twenties
Max Per Level: N/A
Description: "Monks now receive a built in haste effect beginning in the high 20s. The haste effect increases with level, and stacks with haste-items and haste-spells. At 50th monks will find they do substantially more damage per minute. At level 50, the delay change from a monk's normal, non-buffed delay moves from 36 to roughly 30."

Dragon Punch
Level: 25
Max Per Level: (Level + 1) * 5; Capped at 200
Description: The last of the strike series of special attacks. It is an upgrade from Eagle Strike and will do more damage than Eagle Strike once it has reached a high enough skill. You must train this skill at a guild master before it can be used.

Disarm
Level: 27
Max Per Level: (Level + 1) * 5; Capped at 200
Description: When successful this skill will disarm your target of their weapon and it will fall to the ground. This skill is useful when fighting mobs using weapons that give them lower attack delay; when they are disarmed, they attack less frequently and often for less damage. You can not, however, disarm NPCs of their magical weapons. In PvP combat if you successfully disarm another player of their magical weapon it is either placed into their inventory, or if that is full onto their cursor.

Flying Kick
Level: 30
Max Per Level: (Level + 1) * 5; Capped at 200
Description: This is the last of the monk special attacks. It is an upgrade to Dragon Punch and will do much more damage when its skill is high enough, however it has a slower recycle time. Many monks also save skill points to jump start Flying Kick, and begin to see high amounts of damage immediately. This skill is the turning point in the damage dealing career of a monk, as it is far and above any other attack (some monks kick for well over 100 with the right buffs active). It also has the coolest animation of all the special attacks. You must train this skill at a guild master before it can be used.

Riposte
Level: 35
Max Per Level: (Level + 1) * 5; Capped at 200
Description: This is the last skill that a monk gets. It is checked automatically when you are attacked and when it is successful it not only makes it so you take no damage from that attack but you also get a free attack, causing you to get an extra attack that round. You must train this skill at a guild master before it can be used.
