Festival Of Fists

The Festival of Fists is a server-wide event geared primarily at monk education and celebration scheduled for Saturday, October 14th, in Qeynos, Freeport, and the Arena zone adjacent to Lake Rathe.  Consisting of three full combat tournaments, over 15 informational seminars, a variety of raffles and games, and the wedding of Steejans Shadowangel and Reoni of Order of Eternity, the FoF promises to be a glorious day for the Brell Serilis server.  The event is hosted by the Zan Fi Monks.

All times indicated are in Pacific Standard Time (United States West Coast).

Tournaments

Hear ye!  Hear ye!  The Monks of the Zan Fi invite all other monks to test their martial arts skills in our annual Festival of Fists.  There are many martial arts, and it is time to test your style against others!  The Zan Fi is sponsoring open style Kumite events in the Arena Zone.  To accommodate those of you that choose the path of enhancements, a 3 person team event will be held for your benefit.  Our closing event will feature a mass attack on our Sensei, Jagriel, and 5 partners of his choosing.  The monk that delivers the killing blow to our Sensei will receive a special prize.  This is a free event to all participants; significant prizes offered to the victors in all categories (next sash or headband, platinum, and/or weapons/armor/jewelry), with other special awards granted by the panel of judges.

Registration takes place on our site (www.zanfi.com) no later than Thursday, October 12th, and a full bracket of all fighters in each category will be posted on our site the following night.  Both tournaments are single elimination.  Any general questions about the tournaments should be directed to our Event coordinator, Brother Rakol, here.

1. Kumite – One-on-One, Open Hand

All fighters must check in no later than 15 minutes prior to their scheduled match, and all gear must be either banked or held by a friend prior to the match.  Please take care to store no drop gear appropriately in advance.  Any monk headbands or sashes are permitted, and robes are also permitted as long as both contestants have said robe.  All contestants will be heavily dispelled to prevent a buffed advantage, and periodic mid-fight gear inspections by judges will ensure a fair contest.  All fights are to the death, or until one fighter declares matte (surrender).  Contestants will be matched to opponents of appropriate level, and in a level discrepancy, judges and tournament directors will instruct buffers to ensure a fair fight by granting buffs to the disadvantaged fighter at the judges discretion prior to the match. 

 Register for this event here.

 2. Kumite – Three-on-Three, Weapons

The team must consist of three monks.  Any combination of weapons, buffs, armor and jewelry is permitted.  The fight continues until all team members on one team have been killed at least once, or until one team member shouts matte (surrender).  If a team member is killed, he or she may reenter the fight until its conclusion.  Teams will be bracketed according to mean level.

Register for this event here.

3. Kumite – Mixed Class

Open to all classes. This four member team event may consist of at least one monk and up to three other classes of equal or lesser level. Any combination of classes is allowed, however the monk must be the ranking member. All contestants enter the ring and will be given 2 start times. The first start time is a "preparation" start. This lasts for 30 seconds and allows the casters to buff or bring up pets. No offensive or de-buffing spells are allowed during this time. The second start time will be the start of the fight. Combatants fight until all on one team have died. Groups will be placed according to level of the monk(s) in the groups. No prizes are awarded for this event.

Register for this event here.

 

Seminars I – Monk Skills and Philosophy

Post your intention of attending one or more of these seminars here, and please include which location you plan to visit.

1. Feign Death Skill. 

Presenters: Brother Nagasakee, Brother Sunshu, Brother Shinmu, and Brother Pumasan.

Recommended Levels: 1-17

Location: Freeport and Qeynos

Length: 45 Minutes

Start Times: Qeynos: 9 am, Freeport: 1 pm

 

Description: Learn the basics of Feign Death, the skill that singularly defines a monk, and makes the monk one of the hardest classes to kill as well as one of the most useful group classes in all of Norrath.  Get tips on when, where, and how to use Feign Death, its characteristics, and methods of increasing the skill.

Agenda topics: training the skill at 17, how many skill points to put into it, accounting for fails, using it in conjunction with mend, mob aggro and mem wiping, caster mobs-timing-line of sight, directional tips and/or using F9 camera, and more.

 

 

2. Basic Feign Death Pulling

Presenters: Brother Nagasakee, Brother Sunshu, Brother Shinmu, Brother Pumasan, Brother Wing

Recommended Levels: 17-25

Location: Freeport and Qeynos

Length: 45 Minutes

Start Times: Qeynos: 10 am, Freeport: 2 pm

 

Description: See why this is one of the most important techniques you will master in your monk career.  Learn how to pull singles or doubles at will, chain pull, and most importantly knowing your mobs, spawn points, spawn times, and group composition.  Witness demonstrations that illustrate these methods.

Agenda Topics: Knowing your group (as basic as mana checks and as complex as working with chanter mez, ranger harmony (outdoor) and various root and snare spells), Knowing your dungeon and mobs (spawn times, mob types, pathing, wanderers vs. stationary, named and ph’s), FD splitting, aggro, exponential aggro (previously unaggro mobs becoming aggro after mobs return to spawn points and get reaggro’d when the monk puller rises), sneak pulling, chain pulling (with other monks, bow classes and casters), and most importantly a scripted demonstration of splitting with the above situations illustrated, and more. 

 

 

3. Advanced Feign Death Pulling

Presenters: Brother Jagriel, Brother Goralot, Brother Feian, Brother Kambing, Brother Kaladiel, Brother Shiki, Brother Wing and more

Recommended Levels: 25+

Location: The Arena

Length: 45 Minutes

Start Times: 7:30 pm

 

Description: Designed for the mid to high level monk, this seminar includes a panel of highly experienced monk pullers discussing and demonstrating the finest techniques for breaking dungeon mobs.  An absolute must for those monks planning on high level pull work, especially level 35 or higher mobs.  Set up in a presentation format, followed by an informative discussion.

Agenda Topics: Knowing your group (chanter theft of thought and mob order, etc), knowing mobs (pathing, spawn times, types), multiple caster pulls (line of sight, timing techniques, using dungeon architecture), using aggro to your advantage, F9 and camera use.   Giant splitting may get covered if time permits (Vox and Nag prep), as well as trickier Kunark work (deeper Sebilis, Howling Stones, Chardok, etc.).

 

 

4. The Life of an Iksar Monk

Presenters: Brother Kaiche, Brother Verge

Recommended Levels: All

Location: Cabilis Monk Guild

Length: 45 minutes

Start Times: 12:00 pm

 

Description: From faction to hunting grounds to gear choices, this seminar provides the Iksar monk with the knowledge necessary for success across Norrath.  Come listen and speak with the most experienced Iksar monks on our server.

Agenda Topics:  Open for the team of Iksar brothers and sisters.

 

 

5. Dialogue on a Monk’s Role

Presenters: Brother Jagriel, Brother Nagasakee, Brother Goralot, Sister Aaia, Brother Rakol, Brother Wing, Brother Feian, Brother Kambing, Brother Kaladiel, and any Zan Fi that wish to participate.

Recommended Levels: All

Location: The Arena

Length: 45 Minutes

Start Times: 8:15 pm

 

Description: An open forum discussion lead by Zan Fi Sensei Jagriel on the purpose and philosophy of a monk, both as the wandering traveler and as a member in a variety of group class compositions.  This seminar focuses on the way of the monk and knowing one’s role when grouped with various classes.

Agenda Topics: The philosophy of the True Path, roles of monks at various levels in their career, knowing your group (positioning for rogue backstab, chanter ToT, root/snare techniques, ranger harmo outdoors, etc.), mob selection/switching/natural taunting, assist, using bind wounds to supplement healers, using mend and communicating with healers, summoned bandages, supplying peridot and jasper to clerics and chanters, light buff preferences, damage shields, community service and more.

 

 

6. Monk Weaponry

Presenters: Sister Aaia, Brother Goralot, Brother Nagasakee

Recommended Levels: All

Location: Freeport and Qeynos

Length: 30 Minutes

Start Times: Qeynos: 11 am, Freeport: 3 pm

 

Description: Fists or weapons?  When should I start training 1h and 2h blunt, and with what weapons?  What are my ultimate weapon goals?  At what level to my fists become better than various 1 handers.  Why should I never use the Adamantine Bo Stick when grouped with an enchanter?  What is the primary hand damage bonus?  What is weapon ratio?  How does haste influence my damage output exactly?  What’s the going price on a Wu Trance Stick?  Where do these drop?  All of these questions and more will be addressed at this seminar.

Agenda Topics: Primary hand damage bonus, fist ratios and our natural delay bonuses throughout levels, primary and secondary hand choices, dangers of proc weapons breaking chanter mez, natural progression of weapons from trainers to high end, 2 hander delay-based damage bonus changes in recent patch (1-27, 28-39, 40+), the shift in monk focus from fists to weapons (morale), and more.

 

 

7. Monk Armor and Weight Reduction

Presenters: Brother Goralot, Brother Nagasakee, Sister Aaia.

Location: Freeport and Qeynos

Length: 30 Minutes
