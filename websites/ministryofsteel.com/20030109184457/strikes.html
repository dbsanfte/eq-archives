Three Strikes Your Out.
The three stikes your out policy of MoS states as such:
    If your are warned about conduct unbecoming of any guild member you will be given a MAXIMUM of three warnings. After the third warning is issued, by any MoS officer, your membership will go under review and be discussed among the officers. At the time your membership is dicussed the possibilities of corrective actions to be taken, having your membership put on probation or having your membership revoked will be discussed. Any of these are a possiblity and all will be carried out if necessary .

Examples of conduct unbecoming are:
    Incessant begging in guild
    Slander in public forums of your famliy, other guilds or GM's
    Disgracing your guild tag
    Excessive use of obsenities, inappropriate language,or racial slurs in guild chat
    Getting caught KSing other characters

(NOTE: All the officers are informed when a warning has been issued to any particular player character in the guild, this should help you from thinking that just because one officer gave you a warning doesn't mean that you still have 3 strikes with another officer!)
