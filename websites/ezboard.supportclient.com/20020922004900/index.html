Welcome to the ezboard Support Client

What is the Support Client?

The Support Client is the online question and answer area of ezboard for people who are in need of assistance. We call your questions "Support Tickets". In order to help us answer important questions in a speedy fashion, please refer to the help pages first before you post in the Support Client.

How the Support Client Works:

Posting here goes straight through to Support. You will be notified by email that the post is received and you will be emailed a Support Ticket Number. When your ticket has been answered, you will receive another email with the answer to your question. At any time you can enter a ticket number to see the discussion.

The Knowledge Base

Before you post a new ticket, please search our Knowledge Base for the answer to your question. We have everything categorized for your convenience. Most of the answers to the most common questions are here. So please take a moment and enter your search term, by clicking here.
