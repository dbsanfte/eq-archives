About:
So you'd like to know a little more about Sturmbot, and moreso what it is?

Sturmbot is a bot for the Half life mod called Day of Defeat. It provides users an opportunaty to play the popular team-based multiplayer game offline and in private.
It is not an aimbot nor a cheat system like some people think when they here the word bot, it is however an excellent resource for playing against and fine tuning your own gaming skills.

The bots act as a team, providing support for human players and co-operating with team orders.
Furthermore, Sturmbot is reknowned world-wide as the most realistic bot available for Day of Defeat, often encouraging people away from Internet matches and into a LAN match with the bots.
