The Redoubtable Guild Charter

The Redoubtable Mission

We are a neutrally aligned Norrathian guild that promotes fellowship, community, and justice while travelling and experiencing all the lands of Norrath. We value the feeling that comes from sharing each other’s adventures and take pleasure in each other’s successes. We form a family of our members by sharing our rewards and giving aid and comfort when needed.

The Redoubtable Objectives

  1. The Redoubtable will establish and maintain a positive reputation by always treating other adventurers fairly and never engaging in practices that will hurt the guild.
  2. The Redoubtable will recruit and accept only those members we feel will add to our name and status.
  3. The Redoubtable will strive to promote a feeling of community across the lands.
  4. The Redoubtable will increase its influence and power by assisting its members achieve their personal goals and acquire items and wealth.
  5. The members of the Redoubtable will work to make their guild the most respected and powerful guild known.

Ranks

The guild will be comprised of the following three ranks: guild leader, guild officer, and guild member. All ranks have the same voting rights. There is additional responsibility for officers and the leader to promote the guild and maintain a positive reputation.

Guild Leader

The guild leader is responsible for keeping members informed and maintaining a current list of members and officers. The guild leader is also responsible for inter-guild relations. Other members and officers can establish relationships with other guilds, but the guild leader needs to stay informed to avoid entangling alliances. The guild leader is responsible for keeping the charter up to date and providing copies to members upon request.

The position of guild leader can be opened for election to another member by a 60% vote of the guild. Once a vote for a new guild leader has been affirmed, nominations will take place. A nomination must be seconded. At the next guild meeting an election will take place. The nominee with the most votes will win. In the event of a tie, a second vote will be held with candidates being only those members involved in the tie.

Guild Officers

Members can be nominated for officer status during any regular guild meeting. Nominations must be seconded. A debate may be held to determine the suitability of the member for officer status. When concluded a vote will be held. The person will become an officer if they receive a majority of votes present. The guild leader may also use his/her own discretion and promote a member to officer if he/she feels it is necessary for the good running and order of the guild. This must be made known to the members of the guild at the next meeting and if 50% of the members present do not support the officer status for the member it must be removed.

A member may lose his/her officer status if move to remove said status is made and seconded. A debate will ensue and a vote held. If 60% of members present vote to remove officer status, it shall be removed.

Guild Members

A person shall be accepted into the Redoubtable if they have support of at least three members and no one objects. Once accepted, at the second meeting following membership, all members present will evaluate the candidate. If no one objects, then the person will be made a permanent member of the Redoubtable. In the event of objections, they will be aired. If they cannot be resolved a vote will be held. A person must receive 60% affirmative votes from all members to overcome an objection.

A member may be removed from the guild by a two-thirds affirmative vote of all the members of the Redoubtable. This should be reserved for only the most egregious of offences and where no suitable resolution can be achieved.

Guild Meetings

Guild meetings should occur once every two weeks. An agenda should be provided by the guild leader to all members at least twenty four hours prior. Members not able to attend should send comments or concerns to all members prior to the meeting.

Guild meetings should be followed by some guild event. This should be decided by general discussions throughout the previous week. These events can include dungeon crawls, camping for items, aiding members with quests, server community building events, or other event. In addition, these types of events should occur on off weeks when no meeting is held. These events may be less formal, spur of the moment events.

 

The Guild Charter

The final draft of the guild charter should be approved by a 60% vote of all Redoubtable members. Changes to the charter shall be approved with a two-thirds vote of all members of the Redoubtable.

Code of Conduct

  1. Players should make every effort to avoid kill stealing. As defined by Verant, kill stealing is attacking and receiving credit for a kill after it is already engaged with another player character. Often because of miscommunication, this can occur accidentally. Proper apologies should be made in this event and proper compensation met. There are also circumstances where players need to kill steal in order to protect their rights. Players involved in such activity should be able to defend their actions with screen shots, logs, and/or witnesses.
  2. Loot on guild events shall go to a member present who can use the item. In the case where more than one member present may use the item, a random roll among those who have yet to win a random roll item at that event will determine ownership. If no member present can use the item, it will be offered for random determination among all members present who have yet to will a random roll item at that event. In the event a non-member is participating in a guild event, they shall have the same looting rights as members and should be informed of policy beforehand.
  3. Loot at non-guild events should be what the group or groups involved decide. A guild member is not expected to give up an item they have won in a party to another guild member just because the winning member can’t use it.
  4. Guild members should always treat each other with the utmost respect. Differences among members should be handled in a mature manner. If a disagreement cannot be resolved among the parties involved, the matter should be brought to the guild leader to assess the situation. If the guild leader cannot mediate a resolution and believes a meeting of the guild would help, the issue can be brought before a guild meeting to resolve the dispute. The guild leader and non-involved officers will set the rules for the debate. A final resolution may be voted on by entire guild if necessary.
