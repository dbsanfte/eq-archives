How do you like that title?! Well now that I got your attention, I am about to enter into an area of some sensitivity among our members, but I believe that it needs to be done. As a team, we have shown that we are not quite up to par for doing raids. Everyone I talked to has found the fault to be the fact that we are not familiar with one another's way of playing. So, I am suggesting that we start forming guild teams. This would be groups of people around the same level who, when we go to do raids, would be grouped together. These teams would also tend to group a lot when not on a raid. This is where you would become familiar with each other. For instance,

I like to Tashania monster at the same time the puller is casting. This is exactly the type of things I have seen during the raids and the exact type of things that can be avoided with becoming familiar with each other.

So, I am bringing this up here so we can ALL talk about it. I have already talked to several people about this and I am going to list some points and concerns that need addressed.

1. In our raid to fear, there was one main team that recieved most of the experience and who had first pick at the gear (since they killed the mobs). Many of the other members spent hours just helping to beat mobs down. Because of this, EVERYONE I have spoken to has stated that we should spread the power out or come up with a way for every group to have equal access to at least loot if not exp.

2. I talked to one person and they said that there needed to be one Pull Group. The purpose of this group would be to pull. I have talked to other guilds and friends and it seems to be a general consensus that every raid has one group that is the Pull Group and it consists of the highest level players. I would think this group would only need a high level puller, healer and buffer. The other tanks could be lower level. This is because, once the puller gets the mob back to the groups we all would beat on it.

3. Everyone I have talked to has stated that we need to form are groups so that they are fully functional groups with a healer, tanks, buffers, nukers, etc. Just as if you were going into a dungeon alone.


General Observations:

1. IMO, we should pick people who play generally the same amount. For instance someone who plays everyday will have problems working with someone who plays once a week.

2. We don't really have to concern ourselves with levels, because at maximum we are looking at the difference between lvl 46 and 60. I don't believe we have ANY lvl 60 people in the guild. The one exception to this would be pulling and support people in the pull group.

Well, here it is. I am going to duck out of here before the dishes start to fly. Seriously though. I hope we can use this to formulate some kind of plan so that we can become more efficient at raiding.
