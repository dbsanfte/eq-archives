Author Topic:   A Storm of Sorrow
Damane posted 04-04-2001 07:56 PM ET (US)        
A loud clap of thunder jolts a beautiful dark elf out of the arms of her beloved. Light eyes fall on her fiance sound asleep next to her. The slow rise and fall of his chest seems to comfort her dispite their suroundings. A lone cabin in the middle of a cursed forest. Kithicor has been well known to take the bodies of the dead and force them to walk once again. In fact, the night before had been extremely stressful for the couple due to an unruly mummy and his friends. Damane fingered the long scar on her neck, now just a memory. Good fortune was the only thing that led the cleric to find her there. Her beloved was off looking for a meal of some sort to feed her weak form. The cleric gave his name as Aahh. A silly name for a dwarf, but the sound of it brought only joy to the young drows' heart. She looked around again sadly...

"Why are you crying my love?" A familiar voice wispered to her in the breaking light. Damane smiled and turned to hold her fiance tight.

"I want to go home." She sobbed into his chest. His arm instintively held her close, as if to protect her from the unseen horrors of the forest.

"Do not worry my love, after this storm passes, we will be back in Neriak with the rest of our kind..." Spaerkle looked down on her with sad eyes. It had only been a few days since the terrible storm forced them into this shelter, but it seemed as if they would be trapped forever... And she new her condition would only get worse as the storm progressed...
