Welcome, and be of good heart ye who wish to join with the Clan MacLear!

But:
    Be ye warned hereby that if ye be of those who do not wish to join with us in the spirit and times of Azeroth - including, but not limited to, having a name that is appropriate for role playing and behaving more or less in character - or if ye bring with ye lies, deceit, division, selfishness or any other foul spirit, go no further! We are of those who call themselves honorable and whose characters remain in the spirit of Azeroth. Should ye be one of those who call of themselves eL1T3 d00dz or any other of the followers of The Twisted one, we will turn upon you and you shall be from us outcast forever for we shall appeal to the gods and ask them to remove you from lands of Argent Dawn and from the memory of all those who "play the role."

    Come amongst us with a positive attitude and generally positive comments or come not at all. There is much in the real world that is negative and there is much about our lives imperfect, but we come not here rant on such things but to enjoy our lives together in Azeroth, to defeat our enemies and help our friends.

The process of joining is as follows:

- First, you must fill out every question in the form below.  Some of the questions have answers that are found on this site. When you have completed the form, hit the "Submit" button.

- Once your application is submitted, we will review it for completeness and appropriateness. If it passes that minimum muster, then it is posted in the members-only forums for comment. Also, you will be guilded as a lad (or lass), which is a provisional rank.

- Once you gain two full members of the Clan who will sponsor you, you will be given permission to perform the quest that will earn you full kinship in the Clan.

- Once you have a primary character of full Kin rank in the Clan MacLear, your other family members (i.e. alternate characters, not real-life family members) will be admitted automatically.

 

Finally, we have a few rules:

  1. Do not speak ill of your brother or sister except to them privately or in private complaint to an officer
  2. Conduct yourself in a manner that will bring honor to the Clan in everything you do or say. Please take note of this. When you have the Clan MacLear tag above your head, you represent something. If you are using a public channel to speak, please keep it in character. Anything you say reflects on us all
  3. Obey our Chief and the officers appointed by her.
  4. Role play to the extent you are able. The following channels are to be kept in character at all times: /say, /yell. The guild channel is to be kept in character as much as is possible. No one is here to force people to speak with ye's, thou's, and so on. Keeping in character doesn't mean you have to use the accents of the past, but it does mean stop using abbreviations for 'please', 'thank you', 'you', etc... It also means to stop talking about real world stuff and loot stats. Most of this should be common knowledge, as it's even in the ToS for this server. Let's bring back what was meant to be here from the beginning. Limited /ooc talk will be tolerated on /guild channel, but please use the (ooc) to start the sentence off
  5. This is the most important rule: HAVE FUN, and do all that you can to HELP OTHERS HAVE FUN!
  6. Participate in guild events. Plain and simple. If you don't enjoy guild events, and don't help your guildmates, then there is no reason to be in the guild to begin with. When someone takes the time to plan an event, please show your support and attend. Participation includes keeping up with the message board. Please read it at least once a week

    Once you have submitted the application, if you have not heard from an officer in five days, contact an officer in-game.  Please make sure that your email address is accurate and that you include your character name in the application!
