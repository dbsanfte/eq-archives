Beginner's Guide to Baking

Neehobbin's Notes!
The delightful smells surround you while your mouth waters at all the goodies laid out on the counters of your private kitchen. Some of your creations will be for fun, but many will be used to sustain you during your travels throughout the world of Norrath. You prepare your recipes diligently as you take great pride in your baking abilities. You cannot wait to share the exotic and flavorful concoctions you have made with others. This you do for the simple pleasure of knowing, that you are a master baker.

Baking is one of the fun trade skills. It costs very little to take up, usually less than 20pp to master. Baking is not really a profitable trade skill and therefore no information will be provided as such. In my experience baking is used to create fun treats for yourself and others and also to complete certain quests. This will be one of the simpler guides as there is really not much to it. Anyone can be a baker but as with all trade skills, it helps to have a high intelligence and wisdom. Reaching master level at baking takes very little time, usually less than an hour. And with this guide hopefully you will be on your way with the greatest of ease.

Lets Begin!
A few notes before you start:
While not necessary, it helps to put 20 training points into the baking skill before you begin. I personally only put one training point into it.

Many items you will combine are stackable. Place them UN-STACKED in whatever you are using to combine.

Ovens and kilns look almost exactly alike. Make sure you are using an oven and not a kiln.
Pie tins, skewers, muffin tins, pots, cake rounds and cookie cutters are player-made (pottery and/or blacksmithing).

If the recipe calls for a flask of water, make sure it is purchased from a merchant and not summoned.

Spits are like portable ovens and can be purchased from a merchant. However, some recipes will not allow the spit to be used and require you to find an oven instead.

1.) To start, go to East Freeport. Purchase a Spit from Tislan at the Trader's Holiday.

2.) Go to West Freeport, the Academe of Arcane Sciences, 2nd floor and buy stacks of Batwings from Lysa. Batwings can also be purchased from a vendor in East Commonland at the inn nearest the West Commonlands zone.

3.) Now head out to East Commonlands. At the first inns out from Freeport, buys stacks of Frosting from Parthar. Buy as many stacks of Frosting as you bought of Batwings.

4.) Combine in the Spit, 1 Batwing and 1 Frosting to make Batwing Crunchies. Keep doing this until you can no longer increase your skill level (skill level 46). Sell your successes back to vendor, eat them, or give them away. Keep extra Batwings for later use. Sell any extra Frostings back to vendor.

5.) Go back to Parthar in East Commonlands. Buy several stacks of Jug of Sauces (half a dozen stacks may be overkill but prevents you from making a return trip).

6.) Return to East Freeport. At the Port Authority near the docks, buy several stacks of Fresh Fish from Iseba or Tarkar.

7.) Combine in the Spit, 1 Jug of Sauces and 1 Fresh Fish to make Fish Fillets. Keep doing this until you can no longer increase your skill level (skill level 82). Sell your successes back to vendor, eat them, or give them away. Sell extra Jug of Sauces back to merchant. Stock up on Fresh Fish if necessary.

8.) If necessary, go back to Lysa in the Academe of Arcane Sciences and buy more Batwings.

9.) Combine in the Spit, 1 Batwing and 1 Fresh Fish to make Fish Rolls. This will advance your skill to 135.

Congratulations!! You are now a master baker!!
