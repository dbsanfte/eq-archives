STORY:

 

Sydna was raised by a loving mother and father in a land far away from Norrath.  She had no reason to question her lineage until the day arrived, when her elf friends invited her along on their adventures.  Much to her surprise and delight she found rudimentary enchanting abilities!! Loving being part of a roving group she has vowed serious study of enchantment and promises gratitude to Arwa and Parnicail for leadership and patience in nurturing this development.  Together with dear friends Arwa and Syena, Sydna looks forward to the adventures of ridding Norrath of "baddies" while caring and supporting those with like ambition.
