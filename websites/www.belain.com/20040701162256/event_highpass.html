Belain Raid on The Tigers Roar
Chronicled by Cuprin, friend of the guild

The other night, the brave band of adventurers belonging to the Guild of Belain made the treacherous and dangerous journey to High Pass, with one thought on their minds....
PARTY!

Parnicail was there early, to begin the music and entertain the local patrons.

Sal, of course, arrived soon after to defend his area around the brew barrel. None would best him at securing the brews this night!

Soon the party was in full swing.
Wyrmrender and Ivyy called everone to attention, to make a big announcement

They wanted everyone there to know, they had decided to be married!

The crowd went wild with congratulations! Wyrmrender decided to take advantage of the festive atmosphere by treating the crowd to one of his famous table dances, as his bride to be looked on...

Calamus, seeing the attention Wyrm was getting, tried his own dance. But couldn't get their attention. Perhaps another table, cal?


No luck there either? Sorry bud.

Some other misc shots from the party: Had enough to drink?

Parnicail tried his best to get everyone into the party spirit, but some people (cough, Rok, cough) don't seem to be able to cut loose. Better luck next time Parn!

Unfortunatly, my recollection from there is a bit hazy... as I had had a bit to drink. Ok, a LOT to drink!

But in all, it was a wonderful party. As I'm off to Kunark, it will likely be the last time I see all these people at once, which is cause for great sadness for me. I'm sure we'll all find time again sometime to adventure once again. Until then raise those glasses high!

Best to you all,
Cuprin
