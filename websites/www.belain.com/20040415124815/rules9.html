Member Requirements:


1. Show respect to other members and treat them with courtesy. Above all this guild is an assemblance of friendship and to infringe upon this gift shows a lack of maturity. Never speak a slanderous word to another guild member.

2. As always… Have fun. If you are not having fun then you aren't trying hard enough. There are lots of traps and pitfalls in this game, however unfair they might seem. Always keep your head up and look to the brighter side of things. If you are not having fun then you need to separate yourself from the guild members to "cool off" so as to not adversely affect the attitude of others.
