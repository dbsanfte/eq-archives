As the members gathered in anticipation of the event Parnicail was running around tying up some last minute strings. Taluien Mellyrn had not seen action in Norrath for some time, yet he was able to participate this eve. Everyone had emotions flooding thier systems... nervousness, fear, fearlessness, anxiety... all the emotions that tug at you when entering a new place. Soon found out, Arwa and company were able to handle the denizens with slight effort, and with the exception of one Trained Spectre incident... the group succeeded in their quest for survival.

The Members packed themselved into the tight quarters at Dead End #3... Arwa and Calamus were eager to get things started and filleted this Lizzard with fervor.

Wyrmrender, growing bored of the situation, assumed his ritualistic Link Death pose as he sits quietly in the corner... awaiting his inevitable fate.

Arwa, Calamus, Sydna, Syena, and Taluien were holding their own at the Dead End and so the others left in search of Adventure. This they found at the Temple Bottom in the form of Pages, Fanatics, Zealots, Judicators, Crusaders, and Sentinals

Salelar tries to assume the "Wyrmrender Link Death" position but instead is successful at logging out for the night. Goodnight my friend... Adventures await thee.

One quick tell to Taluien and he races to fill the void that Salelar left. Love his enthusiasm. <smile> At this time Arwa and the others had wrapped up their adventure at the Dead End. A long run back to Freeport for them and pleasant memories of a successful event in Cazic Thule.

As a side note: This was the last night for Calamus as a Belainite. Although we were all saddened by his news of departure we were understanding of his reasons and everyone wished him well with pleasant travels. One final word... goodluck to you my friend, and remember that our arms are always open to a friend. If in need don't hesitate a hollar.
