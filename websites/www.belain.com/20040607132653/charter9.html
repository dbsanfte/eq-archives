Version 3:

We are Belain, the power to shape the world. We were founded many years ago on the premise of fellowship and camaraderie. We are a guild of family and friends that seeks fun and adventure, comfortably nestled within the bounds of friendship and caring. We are a small guild and happily so. We add new members cautiously and do not wish to ever grow so large so as to lose our intimate atmosphere.

We've been here a very long time, and endure simply because it is our relationships with each other that are paramount. We also realize that it is the journey that is special, not the destination. We treasure the moment, spent with friends, doing challenging and courageous things.

We are a guild of real people with real lives. As such we are not likely to ever be to the first to kill a certain dragon, or enter some exotic dungeon, but we are exceedingly skilled players. In time, as our real life schedules permit, we will rise to those challenges and we will go to these places. Our guild is a place where you can build long-term friendships along with the knowledge that the people you are playing alongside are both skilled and talented, and where you have a consistent set of friends to talk to, play with, and together can take on challenges.

Belain has a diverse range of people from all walks of life. We strive for an environment that every member can be comfortable in, regardless of your age, sex or background. Of paramount importance is respect - we place respect for one another above "winning" the game, getting loot, or being first at anything.

We are giving and generous people. While we are never obligated to help those in need, we do so because it is right, and warms our heart to alleviate the distress of others. As friends do, we often go out of our way to bring a smile to our guildmate's faces, donate a needed item, or pass on an item because your friend needs it more. We do this all in the name of fellowship, and it is THIS power that lets us shape the world.
