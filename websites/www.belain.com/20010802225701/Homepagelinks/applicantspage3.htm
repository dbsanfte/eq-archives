In order for a player to apply for membership with the guild Belain, their character must be a minimum of 20 levels. This minimum does not, however, apply to existing member's alternates.il@hotmail.com

For the applicant to be accepted into Belain they must pass all of the three phases of admission

 

Application Phases
Phase 1 ~ Profile
Phase 2 ~ Sponsorship
Phase 3 ~ Trial Period

For the first phase, the applicant must submit the required information located in the fields below. The status of the application at this point is set to "Phase 1 ~ Profile". The officers will read through the applicant's story searching for a thoughtful, mature attempt at creating a history for their character. Stories which lack thoughtful, creative content and/or are inappropriate will be declined and the application discarded. If more than two officers veto the story then an officer will approach the applicant in game to relay the announcement.

If the applicant passes through the first phase, their application status is upgraded to "Phase 2 ~ Sponsorship" and the second phase of the process begins. Sponsorship requires that 5 current members group with the applicant and that one of the five be an officer. The Guild Leader will appoint an officer to lead the sponsorship, and that officer will assemble the remaining 4 members to fill the group. During the sponsorship of an applicant the officer and members will be watching the applicant closely to determine that they carry themselves in a polite and respectful manner and that they show respect towards others, whether those be members of Belain or not. If the applicant receives a positive vote from all 5 group members then their application status will be upgraded to the third and final phase.

"Phase 3 ~ Trial Period" is termed the probationary period. The applicant is guilded for the duration of one month, during which time he or she is encouraged to group with guild members and attend guild functions as often as possible. At the end of the trial period the officers will review your conduct during the probationary membership period and if we find that you fit in well with our family your application status will be upgraded to Full Member.

If we find that you aren't a good fit for the guild, then we will thank you for applying and wish you luck in the future.

Since our application process is quite involved, and we carefully scrutinize everyone that we allow into our family, the amount of time that passes during the process can vary depending upon our knowledge of whoever is applying. Please be patient during this time and if you have any questions about the status of your application, please ask an officer in game or view the Applicants section on the message board located at http://pub32.ezboard.com/bthetigersroar for updates.
