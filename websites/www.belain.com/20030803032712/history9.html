The officers of the guild are compiling information on a daily basis which will eventually reside in this page. This information will give the member as well as the prospective member some insight as to who we are based on key events in the History of Belain. Please check back here frequently as our history is constantly being written. <smile>

The inception of Belain started August of 1999, the first summer after Everquest was released. A group of five guys who lived in Portland Oregon, and worked at a start-up financial trust company (Copper Mountain) held a meeting in the NW corner office on the 32nd floor where a decision was reached: their relationship with this game was about to get much more involved... Belain was to be created (although initially this was not the desired name of the guild). These five instigators were Signa, Salelar, Cuprin, Taluien, and Calamus.

As we all know, guilds are formed with a meeting of ten members (with 10 separate accounts) but it was not until February of 2000 that we were able to fit our original ten members together at the same time. The guild was created with Signa as our leader with the other 9 members being: Salelar, Cuprin, Taluien, Calamus, Parnicail, Wyrmrender, Arwa, Sydna, and Krovicia.

The organization of the guild was set up by the officers at that time, Wyrmrender, Salelar, Taluien, and Signa. They decreed that the 14 possible classes would be grouped into Realms of Magic, Healing, and Might and that each Realm would be represented by an officer. Salelar, being a wizard, was the officer for the Realm of Magic. Taluien being a druid, was the officer for the Realm of Healing. Wyrmrender, being a ranger, was the officer for the Realm of Might and Signa, being the guild leader, oversaw all the interworking dynamics of guild life.

The early days in Belain were a time of focus with emphasis placed on the charter and the mission. Members had a common purpose, a goal if you will, of eradicating evil from the face of the world. Everyone strove to achieve this both in groups and on their own. But as we have witnessed through our time here, role-playing in Everquest is not easily accomplished without a strict system in place that allows for it. Signa felt that the direction the guild was steering away from his creation, and that this was not part of the grand vision he had for Belain. He left the lands of Norrath in May of 2000 and passed the role of Guild Leader on to Wyrmrender who gladly accepted the task. At this point I was promoted to the rank of Officer of the Realm of Might to fill the void which Wyrmrender had left. The overall focus of the guild had changed with Signa's passing. The eradicating of evil in the world was no longer the primary goal, and in fact had been totally obliterated from the new charter under Wyrmrender’s leadership. The new course which Wyrmrender steered us towards was one where acceptance and friendship governed our everyday lives. Belain became a guild of close friends and family members... the beating heart of the collective as mentioned in the revised charter. Members felt a stronger bond between each other as time went by and the feeling of family ties grew.

Towards the latter part of 2000, Belain experience a small growth spurt. Several new members were introduced and after much discussion, the officers felt that a new officer position should be created. Qnix became the next officer in the guild and he was in charge of organizing and planning Guild Events. With more members, we felt that having organized events would help maintain everyone’s interest while drawing members closer to each other. Our guild kept chugging along at our usual, slow pace.

In May of 2001 Wymrender announced that his wife (in real life) was pregnant with their second child and that he would be stepping down from his leadership role, and also reducing his playing time in preparation of this joyous occasion. The members unanimously nominated myself (Parnicail) for the position. So I even though I had a hand in the decision making process from early on… I was not officially the leader of this guild until almost one year ago.

The ties of friendship between members of Belain were very strong at this point. I determined that there was not much left to accomplish in focusing on the relationship between members… time had taken care of that. My main goal as leader of this guild was to strengthen relations with other friendly guilds in hopes of joining or creating alliances (fortunately I have accomplished this goal here in NSOG). During this time under my leadership, Belain has experienced a second influx of membership growth, and I have been dealing with the issues that arise whenever this occurs.

I am currently evaluating the status of our guild: where we stand amongst each other, where we stand amongst the guilds within the alliance NSOG, and where I stand amongst our members. The next change that I foresee will be within the guild officer structure and the systems that are currently in place. I have started reworking some of these old systems which initially worked well for us, that have fallen aside, outdated and unusable. I hope to relieve myself of some of the daily responsibilities by delegating them amongst the officers, with the purpose of freeing up some of my time to dedicate to NSOG and the events and programs which are in place here.

The future? Who can say. Personally I will remain within the guild Belain until the end. Belain is comprised of family, friends of family, and a few friends we have picked up along our journeys through Norrath. I feel that all of our members are fiercely dedicated to the guild and to myself as well. When DAOC came out, not one member did we lose! When Luclin came out, for some members it looked like their playing days were over because of the SOL minimum requirements. However, changes were made and playability was increased, and those members have returned to fight amongst us! There is a lot of the game I have yet to see, yet the point I am at now … I am pretty content with what I have accomplished. For me, the joy of the game is the people who play and the relationships engendered. There are a lot of seriously screwed up people who play this game, but for each of them, there are 10 wonderful ones to counter the off balanced scale.

Our old guild leader Signa said it best:


"May the hearts of all members combine to beat as the collective heart of Belain for it is the power of Belain that can change the world and the power of friendship that defines Belain."
