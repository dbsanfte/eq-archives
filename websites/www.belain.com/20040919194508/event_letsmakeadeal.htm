Let's Make a Deal
Chronicled by Krovicia - Official Events Officer

Belain had a special guest appearance on Monday for Embiggen Night. Monty Hall had a one-time appearance in the Tiger's Roar Tavern in High Pass. He invited everyone to a night of fun and prizes. Monty treated Belain to a Norathian version of Let's Make a Deal. Everyone competed in pricing games and intense wheeling and dealing for prizes. Some people did fall into temptation and ended up with a Zonk!. Just ask Hefner and Cathedrus, who managed to walk away with an Orc Bracer and a Bixie Stinger. Overall, the night was a success with Monty giving away more than 8,000p in cash and prizes.
