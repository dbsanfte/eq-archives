Time had come for yet another official guild event, and as Krovicia poured over tomes of knowledge and information, she finally struck upon a new plot. The idea was to create level 1 halflings of any class (except for rogues) and to meet in the Plane of Knowledge at the portal to the Nexus. As folks arrived it was evident there was some type of theme to the naming of each halfling, although no one could put their finger on what it was.

Word spread like wildfire throughout the zone that something was afoot, something was brewing the likes of which none had seen before. A grand army of naked hobbits were mobilizing with the intent of invading the evil city of Neriak, home of the dark elves. Justice would be found today!

Soon, folks were arriving from distant lands to see what was going on. Even our old friend Sandois stopped by to pay us a visit and wish us luck on our venture.

But tonight, victory would be ours! We gathered at the zone in Neriak and were given instructions for this great event. The goal was to see how far into the city each participant could get before the inevitable slaughter from the guards.

The event began with a whoosh as 5 or so halflings fell to the ground bleeding. But the others pushed on, intent upon making it to the second zone of the dark, underground city. After several attempts, the bodies began to pile up, and the hair from the hobbit feet created a nice thick and lush carpet trail down into the city and beyond (look close and you can see that most decided to turn left once inside the gates).

Krovicia's right hand man Wizeird (Ivyy) was setup in the second zone of the city to keep an eye on event participants and make sure all ran smoothly.

Soon after we started, word spread of our great battle and folks traveled to the dark city to witness our achievements. This screenshot was provided by Reide Tenma showing our progress of invasion into the Neriak Commons (the second zone).

Our participants made it as far as the main gate just above the heart of the city. From this picture, it appears our winners were a dead tie between Wyrmrender and Salelar, however what you cannot see is the courage and bravery of young Garacaius who beat the odds and went far beyond that which any halfling dared.

About this time, we were paid a visit by the friendly guide Sqook, who swiftly put an end to our bold adventure. Although it was his duty to see order restored in Neriak, he wished me to extend a congratulations to all who participated for carrying out such a clever event.

As the event drew to a close and we were all summoned out to Rivervale (thanks Sqook) awards were handed out to the winners: Minigar, Wyrmhobbin, and Ninnen for coming in as the top 3 finalists. The event was a HUGE success and stories will be retold of the day when Neriak was overrun by the band of brave Halflings. Hearty thanks to our beautiful and talented Events Officer, Krovicia, proving yet again that sometimes the best events are the ones where the adventures unfold before our eyes.
