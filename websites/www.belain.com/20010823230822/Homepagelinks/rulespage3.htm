Member Requirements:

1. Show respect to other members and treat them with courtesy. Above all this guild is an assemblance of friendship and to infringe upon this gift shows a lack of maturity. Never speak a slanderous word to another guild member.

2. As always… Have fun. If you are not having fun then you aren't trying hard enough. There are lots of traps and pitfalls in this game, however unfair they might seem. Always keep your head up and look to the brighter side of things. If you are not having fun then you need to separate yourself from the guild members to "cool off" so as to not adversely affect the attitude of others.

 

Resurrections and Teleports:

This outline provides guidelines to intra-guild rezzes and teleports. This is not meant to be a set of rules persay. Every player within Belain has the right to share his/her unique abilities as they see fit.

The reason these guidelines are necessary at all is to prevent hurt feelings
 and/or causing another player to feel guilty.

1. If you die, you are responsible for arranging your own rezzes and teleports.

2. Should you require a rez or port, first attempt to contact the appropriate character in your current zone, and secondly, by all means use the /guildchat channel and ask, “Is anyone available for a rez/port?” If no one responds, then it will be up to you to loot your corpse and continue on.

3. Feel free to give the required member(s) a private tell asking for their services, but live with the answer they give you. If they are grouped and reply with something like “I can’t leave my group right now”, then once again, loot your corpse or seek a rez/port from a non-guild member, or wait until that
person becomes available.

4. Be patient. An emergency on your part is not an emergency for all. If you die, your corpse has 3 hours before it becomes un-rezzable. Loot everything except for one item and go play. Expect the person who is going to rez you to
arrive when he/she gets there. Accept that the person you need for a rez/port may be grouped or otherwise involved and wishes to remain so for the time being. Rezzers and porters should provide an ETA that is acceptable to them, not necessarily to the player in need.

5. Do not expect a player to always come to you. Do everything you can to get your corpse to a safe, easy to find place if you need a rez, or offer to meet them at the druid rings or wizzie spires in case of needing a port. In the
case of needing a rez, don’t expect a fellow guild member to travel through ten zones to get to you. Do your best to find transport for them. If no transport is available, expect that member to reply with “Sorry, that is too far and too
dangerous for me to travel right now”.

6. Guild members with the ability to rez/port should try everything within their immediate powers to help those guild mates in need. If you are standing around picking your nose and another member needs your services, you should
answer the call. However, the choice is ultimately up to the individual and should be respected by everyone as such.

7. Be wise when requesting resurrection. You will most likely be laughed at if you are level 15 and ask for an experience rez, especially if travel is involved. Experience is gained much faster at lower levels and can probably be made up in the time it would take the rez effects to wear off. However, if you
are level 15 and die far away from your bind point with no means to get back to your corpse, that is a different matter.

8. Remember that Belain was created in part to be fun. We all take pride in helping one another and maintaining our family-like atmosphere. These guidelines are in place to insure that everyone understands where each other is coming from and no-one's feelings are hurt through this process.

 

Realm Officer Role:

Officers of each realm are required to have a working knowledge of each of the classes that are included in their realm so as to be able to answer member's general questions that arise through the course of the game.

1. These officers are part of the applicant acceptance process.

2. These officers are required to assist the Guild Events Officer with the organization of Official Guild Events.

 

Guild Events Officer Role:

The Guild Events Officer is required to organize, announce, and lead Official Guild Events.

1. This officer must be able to post announcements on the Tiger's Roar messageboard as well as furnish the information to the webmaster for announcements in the Guild Events Section.

2. This officer is also part of the applicant acceptance process.

 

Lore Officer Role:

The Lore Officer is required to have a working knowledge of all of the tradeskills in place throughout Norrath. He or she is expected to be able to answer questions that may arise on this topic from guild members.

1. This officer will be responsible for information submitted to the webmaster for posting in the Lore Library section.

2. This officer is also part of the applicant acceptance process.

3. This officer is required to assist the Guild Events Officer with the organization of Official Guild Events.
