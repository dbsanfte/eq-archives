The Surefall Glade was throbbing with excitement of its own when the guests began to arrive. It was nightfall and everyone was buzzing about the events which were to follow, the wedding of Wyrmrender and Ivyy Treefellow. Everyone was milling about on the lush greeneries of the zone, chatting away about adventures and long lost friends discovered once again.

Wyrmrender finally arrived looking his finest in his green tuxedo and the wedding party began to assemble to take their places.

Even Corun Finsic stopped by to wish the couple well and to remind everyone to please leave the bears of Surefall Glade alone during the ceremony

The betrothed exchanged their gifts as Syena and Arwa commandeered the ceremony.

The ceremony is concluded and the festivities begin with Gnomish fireworks compliments of the bridesmaid Krovicia. Quite a spectacular show for the newlyweds.

And when all is said and done the wedding party poses for Neehobbin in hopes of capturing this special moment for everyone to remember.

The members and friends of Belain wish to congratulate Wyrmrender and Ivyy and to wish them many happy years together. Way to go guys!!!
