In order for a player to apply for membership with the guild Belain, their character must be a minimum of 20 levels. This minimum does not, however, apply to existing member's alternates.

Why Join Belain?

People that join Belain usually do so for reasons related to why they started playing Everquest to begin with - to have some good times with some good people. This is the foundation that Belain is built on.

One side effect of the constant quest for the next level, the next AA, and the next big mob, is that we often forget that this is a game and we're all here to have fun (or as most Belains put it, phun!). The quest for the next flag, next loot, the DKP point starts to make this game become work. If you're in one of those "other guilds" that actually require raid attendance or such things, it starts actually becoming a job!

For Belains, it is all about the phun. We like "phat lewtz". We like downing mobs. We like seeing new things (so, yeah, a flag or two wouldn't break our heart). But those things are just something to do while having phun with each other. We are friends. Many in the guild have met others in the guild in real life, and many of them even "hang out" together when we're not playing EQ (is there ever a time we're not doing that?).

So, to get to the root of the "Why join Belain?" question, it's pretty simple - common reasons:

  • You're here to play a game, not seek employment

  • You like "being there" for other people, and knowing with certainty that they will be there for you as well

  • You may want to raid and progress in the game (or not - matter of taste), but don't want to hear that you're fired for not showing up, and don't want to spend your entire life doing it.

  • You want to be around others that you respect, and that you feel respect you

Some of our members have done the "Uber Guild" merry-go-round at some point in their EQ lives, and they've moved beyond that. Some just never wanted to go there in the first place. For Belain, being "Uber" is about occasionally pushing the limits, but always living up to what you can be as a person.

Sound like you? Then you may want to consider hanging out with us and even - gasp! - applying. We shall warn you up front that we have one of the more tedious application processes in the game - its takes a bit more time to judge someone on character than their Magelo. That said, it provides us with some of the most consistent "of fine character" players in the game, and thus has survived and prospered one of the oldest guilds on Povar.

The application process is as follows:

Application Phases:

Phase 1 ~ Profile
The applicant must submit the necessary information in a formal post on the Tigers Roar message board in the New Member Application thread. Please take your application as serious as if you were applying for a job. We are searching for specific details when we review your profile. Inappropriate or incomplete applications will be ignored and the post will be locked by the applications officer.

Posting Instructions:
Once you are at the Tigers Roar messageboard, please create a post by clicking on the “new topic” button located at the upper right corner of the page. To do this you must have a global (or local) ezboard account. Within your post you will need to complete the following requirements:

Phase 2 ~ Sponsorship
During this period, the applicant is responsible for obtaining a sponsor within Belain. The sponsor must be of Full Membership status, and will be required to help the applicant group and interact with current members and officers. If you have posted your application below, you will have 30 days to obtain a sponsor. If you are unable to find anyone willing to sponsor you, your application will be placed on hold, removed from the New Member application thread, and you may reapply after another 30 days. During Phase 2, the applicant must participate in five guild only events, and or raids. Once a sponsor has been obtained by the applicant, the applicant may take as much time as needed to complete their five events. At the end of the fifth, the applications officer will post in the Application Committee section and all officers will then review the information and vote at that time. For an applicant to pass this phase a unanimous decision must be met, and no votes against can be present.

The criteria which needs to be met for admission into the guild are:

- Does this player show respect towards others?
- Does this player show a willingness to help others?
- Does this player refrain from using vulgar and offensive language?
- Does this player have a light hearted attitude?
- Is this player fun to be around?


Phase 3 ~ Trial Membership
Once the sponsorship phase is complete and the applicant has received the necessary votes for invitation, the applicant is now considered a Trial Member and will proudly bear the Belain guild tag. During this period, the applicant will have access to members area of our forums. This final phase in the application process is considered a probationary period where the applicant will remain under close scrutiny by the officers. During this period, (and all periods subsequent) the applicant is expected to behave in a manner appropriate to the morals and ideals of Belain. Any hint of misconduct during this period, and the situation will be addressed by the officers and the application may be terminated by removing the applicant from the guild. This trial membership period will typically last one month, beginning from the date he or she is guildinvited - the trial period may, however, be extended somewhat if the trial period is found to be insufficient in terms of "getting to know" the applicant (for example, if the applicant was rarely seen during the trial period).

At the end of the one month probationary (trial) period, and been approved by the application committee, the applicant will be required to create an account though Magelo and also register their Magelo profile with eqrankings.com. Once that is accomplished, the applicant will receive access to the member’s section of the message board and thus, be allowed to sponsor new applicants.
