STORY:

 

Syena was born in the city of Felwithe to a learned couple, Quazzle and Aeriel Silverhue, respected citizens in the land of High Elves.  They were kind and nurturing parents who provided a loving home and endeavored to instill values of decency and respect for learning in their only child.  Syena enjoyed a happy childhood and wanted for nothing. 

While still a young one, Syena and her parents were boating on a relaxing afternoon in the Elizerain Lake.  The waters grew dark and a storm arose, capsizing the vessel.  All aboard, save Syena, perished. 

Syena somehow made it to shore by hanging on to parts of the wreckage.  She was found by a member of a cloistered order of holy women.  Since she had no other family she was raised within the confines of this order.  She had little contact with the outside world for many years, spending most of her hours in school or working in the infirmary, a place where those with little resources could come to regain their health without regard to status or ability to pay. 

Syena discovered she had a gift for healing.  She studied long and hard, and soon was more than competent in the skill of ministering to others.  She continued to perfect her craft, and saving others from disease and pain became the source of her satisfaction.

One day, while venturing out into the wilderness seeking to commune with nature, she had the great misfortune to slip and fall into what was left of an ancient fountain.  She had not approached a body of water since the accident and remained terrified at the thought of being submerged.  Luckily, her screams for help were heard by Arwa and Sydna, loyal members of the Belain Guild.  They saved her life and she was eternally grateful. 

Arwa, Synda and Syena soon became close friends.  They taught her of the world Norrath, she learned of the factions, the potential dangers, and the need for honorable beings to fight evil and protect what is good.  Syena hoped to join the Belain guild in order that she could help people, become a noble fighter, and above all, a protector of the earth.
