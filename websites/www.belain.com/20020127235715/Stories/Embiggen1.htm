Embiggen's Lucky Day

by Embiggen Thunderfist
 

Wow, what a fun event! (And I'm not just saying that 'cause I got some of the loot!) :-) That was about the most fun I've had on EQ, and it was great to have all of us together again! Much humble bowing, waving, and cheering for our guild leaders! I was a bit worried when they said "a contest", 'cause I'm just a little guy, but hey, even little guys can kill Skele's and Froglok Tad's. :-)

Our strategy was to split up and forego the teleporting, hoping that being in the different parts of the map would be advantageous and maybe quicker than TPing. So while Cuprin headed toward Blackburrow for the Gnoll Eye, Arwa stayed in the Karanas and I headed toward Oasis. We guessed well... After the Gnoll Eye, Arwa was perfectly positioned to gather muddite clay, and let me say that I'm glad our roles weren't reversed, with him in Oasis and me in Beholder ... :-)

Next was the Froglok Tadpole Eye, and lucky us, I'm in Oasis, so I just headed south a bit and found one. The final object was a snake egg, and as luck would have it, on the way back through South Ro, I happened upon a snake or two, and one of them was about to lay an egg. So we had all of our goodies, and were told to head toward Lavastorm and the Temple of Ro.

Just a sidenote: As I was running through the tunnel to EC, some small dwarf kept following me and saying "stop". I was in a hurry, but after I figured out he was talking to me (I didn't know his name), I did stop. He said "Here, take these", and handed me two Jagged Steel Bands (AC5, STR+3). Nice rings! He went on, "I can't play EQ anymore for a year and a half. My dad says he won't pay anymore until I get my own credit card when I'm 17. So good luck, enjoy." And he ran off to others, giving away who knows what! "Thanks!" I yelled, and astonished at just how far Embiggen's Lucky Day was going, I resumed my run North.

When we finally all arrived in Lavastorm (after worrying a bit about Arwa going LinkDead), we handed over our loot to our leader Cuprin, who was going to present it all to Wyrm for the big prize. We all gave him our stuff, and then Cuprin looked at me and asked "Didn't you also have a snake egg, Embiggen?" Oh yeah, where did I put that?

Hey, it's not in my backpack.

Panicked, I checked my sewing kit! No! I checked my four-slot food storage pack! NOO! WHAT'S GOING ON HERE?! I looked in all the above mentioned places again! And then, for some reason, again. It was gone! I thought back real quick to the snake. Did I actually pick up the egg, or only see it? What happened? I really wasn't looking forward to my next words, but had to say them anyway: "Hey guys, I really did have it, but it's gone now. " I felt smaller than Qnix's younger sister.

"What!" Cuprin and Arwa both asked at once, and I felt like I'd been caught cheating, but I knew I really had got it. There was a moment of silence, and at about the same time, they figured it out. "I'll bet it ended up in your first slot, Em," one of them said. I still didn't get it. "eh?"

"It's food. You ate it!"

"AAARRRRGGGGHHHH!!!" NO... I ate it?! "I ate the friggin' egg!??" But... but... this is supposed to be my *lucky* day! I ATE THE EGG! Sheesh!

/em groans and kicks a rock.

/em curses.

/em bangs his head on the cliff wall.

"That was a pretty expensive lunch, Em", Cuprin said, as he headed toward the border back to Nek to find some snake or other and save my ... uh, hide. "333 PP each." "Wait a sec, Cuprin" Arwa suggested. "I'm going to ask for a ruling." And thankfully, the Judges were gracious enough to accept the word of a humble Monk who had just had a very expensive lunch. Whew! Luck was indeed with me that day! After that, the trip through Lavastorm (my first time) seemed tame, corpse-eating-lava-pits notwithstanding. We made it through to Ro with no serious incidents (only a couple of nuisances), and then were led to ... actually, I don't know where to, but it was a somewhat largish room with an appropriate mound at the other end, and atop the mound, flanked by the denizens of the dungeon, stately like some Lord of Days Gone Past, was our Guild Leader and Fabulous Party Planner, Wyrmrender Treefellow.

Boy, was it good to see him! Parn set off fireworks, and somehow even made it seem like we were each doing it ourselves. Some trick! Congratulations were passed all around, and also... the loot! Wow! 333 PP each, and to think that before this event, all my lifelong savings had amounted to about 70 PP.

(Arwa, being rich, made a generous donation of his share to the poor Monk with expensive tastes, and for that, I will name my first Fighting Baton, which I hope to buy with the proceeds, after him. Fighting Baton "Arwa", maybe the first to have a name...) :-) All in all, wow, what an event! Well done, Planners, Judges, and Airlines! As a final word of praise: I think this is what EverQuest was meant to be.

Embiggen
