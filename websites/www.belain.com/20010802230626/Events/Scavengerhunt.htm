Antonican Scavenger Hunt
Chronicled by Parnicail
 
Hosted/Organized by the Officers of Belain: Wyrmrender, Salelar, and Parnicail
 

Participants (Teams):
1. Embiggen
2. Cuprin
3. Arwa
4. Neehobbin
5. Toone
6. Corrin
7. Qnix
8. Ajah
9. Sydna

10.. Syena

11. Niloc
12. Ravapalic

Once again the members of Belain packed themselves into the famous Tigers Roar Tavern in Highpass. But tonight they arrive without knowledge of what is to come. The Antonican Scavenger Hunt was about to get underway and as all the participants recieve their instructions I could see the feelings of anxiousness and surprise sweep over them like waves on a beach.

The adventure called them to find several items which were located in different locations all over the middle continent Antonica, with a prize of 1000 platinum pieces to the first team to bring back all of the items. With a whisk of his hand, and smile and a wave of good luck, Wyrmrender delivered the clue for the first item...

 

... and they were off to seek fame and fortune.

Syena's group was one of the first to arrive in Befallen in search of the first item, a stack of 20 bone chips

 

Embiggen found himself alone in this hallway as I watched on with eager anticipation. Soon his nervousness wore off as he fell into his comfortable fighting stance making short work of this skeleton.

 

 

I watched on chuckling to myself when Ravapalic, Niloc, and Syena ran through an open door that was trapped. Rav was able to whip out his trusty lockpicks and spring them all free but not after precious time was lost.

This section of the event took the most time for the fact that a stack of 20 bonechips were needed to fulfill the scavenger quest. After this section every group seemed to spread out to different corners of the continent upon which I was unable to document every detail of the events each team encountered. For a recount of the events of Embiggen's group follow the link to the stories section called ...

"Scavenger Hunt"

I found Neehobbin and company in the deserts of Northern Ro gathering skeletal remains. Trying to escape the overcrowded dungeon of befallen was a smart idea.

 

 

 

Soon Sydna and her group showed up in the desert. I tailed her as long as I could without giving away my presence. But duties called me away to the west and I felt I had left them in safe hands.

And then came the need for the teleports. Salelar's Wyrmburner Airways became a busy airline after the first clue was solved and gathered.

The second item the groups needed to find was a gnoll tooth located in the Blackburrow den just east of Qeynos hills. Travel to this section of the continent took the longest time and thus the need for using Salelar's teleportation services. The third item needed was a brick of clay dropped by muddite elders in the Gorge of King Xorbb. The fourth item was a Froglok eye located in the southern swamps, namely Innothule and the underground dungeon of Guk. And finally the fifth and last item was a snake egg dropped by.... yes you guessed it, a snake.

Once all the riddles were solved and the items were looted, the teams had to make their way through the trecherous Nektulos forest, and wind their way through the rocky Lavastorm Mountains to arrive at the Temple of Solusek Ro. I was able to secure the pathway shown below after a roaming guard took out a harmless passerby probably new to the zone himself. Poor little blighter...

The first group had finally arrived in the Temple of Solusek Ro... met by myself at the door and then escorted to the main hall where Wyrmrender Treefellow stood at the bridge waiting to recieve the quest items as well as congratulate the team on a job well done. Embiggen, Cuprin, and Arwa were the recipients of 333 platinum pieces each this night.

I sat back and watched the group celebrate their victory, knowing just what they had to go through to get to the spot they were standing now. The evening was drawing to a close as a few more groups solved the riddles and gathered the components. But everyone was quick to congratulate the winners this evening. All I could do was beam with pride at the success of our guild event.

The final picture shows the two monks Cuprin and Embiggen with their honorary monk Arwa who had taken a liking to their class and marveled at their prowess throughout the evening. Arwa generously donated his winning share to Embiggen who was overjoyed beyond words

(unless of course you read his recount of the adventures that he and his team went through).

All in all I would have to say that the event was a huge success and I am very proud to have been a part of planning such grand adventures. But what made this work beyond anything that myself or the other officers could organize was the contribution and the enthusiasm of the members. My Lambent helm goes off to you all and thanks for making this such a memorable night.

 

Parnicail Lomelindi

Officer of the Realm of Might
