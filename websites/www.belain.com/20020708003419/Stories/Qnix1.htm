Firiona Vie Boatride

A trip to Kunark (subtitled: how to avoid being shark bait)

The following is a true story; the names have been changed to protect the inoccent (er, foolish)by Qnix
 

 

Last night this hobbit I know (let's call him Rnix) was happily on his way to Kunark, embarked on one of the shuttles that move you to the BIG ship. While on his way accross the oceans, R was having fun, playing around with the other passengers and just being a goof.. well some ways into the journey, while practing his gymnastic skills, R accidently dove off the shuttle.

... Well R begins to panic, thinking he is going to be left behind in the middle of the sea. R begins to frantically claw his way through the water to catch the shuttle. After several moments, he begins to realize he will never catch it, R looks and notices he has the levitation spell memm'd - so he thinks.. hey, I'll just cast Lev, it'll maybe raise me high enough, then I can board the ship (which had since come into full view).

Little did R realize that the only thing that results from trying to cast Levitate while underwater, is a rapid sinking and a loss of mana. So, R, being the quick thinker he is, spies the back side of the HUGE ship (did I mention this ship was BIG?) and sees a large rudder angeling into the water. Well thinks R, if I approach the angled rudder just right, I *should* be able to grab enough purchase to hoist myself up on it.. However; just as R was approaching the rudder, the ship begins to move away.. R struggles a couple of times to raise himself up but it seems to be quite slipery. Finally after the third or fourth attempt, and with the ship gaining speed, R was able to hoist himself up on the rear of the rudder of the MASSIVE ship and there he rode, like a wharf rat to the docks at Firiona Vie.

(he told me he even took some photo's of him standing atop the rudder that he will try and post for everyone to see)

So R wants everyone to know this... unless you are very good swimmer and can withstand the thought of being lunch for a plague shark, do not, repeat, do not engage in tomfoolery while aboard a flat topped vessel in the middle of the oceans. (they really need to put railings on those things)

your friend Q... er R
