Version 2:

The founders of Belain were well traveled adventurers that experienced first hand the pain and sorrow that a life on the road brings. They found that they did not achieve inner peace defending the local communities from the various evils that plagued their lands. With each head that rolled after a quick swipe of the sword or each body that imploded under the intense barrage of a magical onslaught, the weary adventurers became more distant, secluded, and lonely. Their world, the world they truly loved, began to retreat deep inside their minds and they began to quickly lose hold of whatever reality they held on to so tightly. They realized their motivation was not properly aligned and that setting out to right the wrongs of Norrath under false pretenses was driving them insane. They longed for days gone past where they could sit in the guild halls or the taverns telling stories and sharing ale. It was companionship that they were truly seeking and in denying themselves this they were denying themselves all that once made them the great adventurers they had boasted of being. Upon realization of this they pledged to let others travel the lonely road hunting down evil but that they would band together and get their strength in numbers. Belain's strength comes not from the destruction of evil, as it was once believed, but from the bonds of friendship it's members have forged. So let it be known that Belain is not a guild of individual members. Belain is the heart and soul of the collective. Belain does not stand high amongst men but towers above them on the shoulders of it's members. Friendship, teamwork, loyalty, and sacrifice are the pillars that hold this lofty banner high and as long as the banner of Belain goes forth so shall it's members, in unison, standing side by side as friends and comrades.

May the hearts of all members combine to beat as the collective heart of Belain for it is the power of Belain that can change the world and the power of friendship that defines Belain.
