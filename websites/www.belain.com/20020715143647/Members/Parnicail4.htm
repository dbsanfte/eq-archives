STORY:

 

As the Sun is to Day, and Moon is to the Night, and even as the stars light the heavens in their ever-twinking presence <wink>, Parnicail was born the singer of songs and the teller of tales.  Like the celestial bodies before him, he comes first and follows after.  He is everywhere… and yet nowhere is he to be found.  The perfect compliment to any group, yet he is often misunderstood or misrepresented.  His true passion has always been deeply rooted in music and song.

Parnicail Lomelindi was born to Parnigar and Anaine Lomelindi, the famous performing duo known to all reaches of Faydwer as the Traveling Lomelindi Minstrels.  For many years the Lomelindi’s had traveled, entertaining audiences at every stop.  Indeed, they had built quite a rapport with the locals and were hailed as the premier show in any city.  At this time it was understood by all that Parnicail would perform in the shows with his parents once he attained his “coming of age” status (the defining moment in a young bards career when he or she is considered an adult and becomes accepting of his or her role in society).

In his youth, Parnicail showed signs of musical brilliance.  An accomplished performer on the lute and mandolin, Parnicail (or Parn as his friends coined him) turned his focus onto music composition and theory.  He traveled to the far off city of Freeport to study with such famous composers the like of which could not be equaled in skill anywhere else.  Years he spent in Freeport in total isolation, studying and practicing.  It was here that he discovered the importance of the aria and the sonata and of course the bards best friend, the polyrythm.  Then he packed his belongings and readied for the return trip to his homeland.  His parents were overjoyed to see him return and of course offered him a position in the famous Traveling Lomelindi Minstrels.  But at the total shock and disbelief of Parnigar and Anaine, Parnicail politely rejected their offer.  He told them of far off lands and intriguing peoples that were unknown to him prior to his pilgrimage.  He greatly desired to travel and see these wondrous sites.  And in one instant his angry parents dropped all guard and in a climactic moment said “ok”.  Finally Parn was free.  What to do he thought… where should I go?  His question was soon answered as the friendly dwarf Signa approached and offered an escort to Freeport and beyond if necessary.  Parn, not realizing the importance of this first meeting between two great friends was leery of Signa.  His clothes, his unkempt hair, Signa looked just like the drunken bar flies that would hover around his parents during their performances.  Once underway, Parn learned to love this kindly dwarf dearly.  Signa had an answer for everything.

About this time, Signa introduced Parn to Taluien Mellyrn and the two of them opened up a whole new world to the eyes of Parn.  These two took him to the unholy estate of Unrest.  Feeling a little green in such an evil place, Parn’s fears were eased by the presence of his new friends.  Next, Parn and Sig were off to Highpass to help control the gnoll population there.  It was important to Parn to have some sense of purpose in the senseless killing of such beasts.

Here in Highpass Parnicail’s life was about to take another turn, because it was here that he met the beautiful enchantress Krovicia.  Right away Krovicia accepted Parn into her group and the two started down a path that lead to a great friendship.  Being adept at illusion and trickery, Krovicia would change her form, often confusing Parnicail to no end.  Yet there were other strange nuances and encounters between Kro and the general populous that lead to questions.  “Who was this woman?  How can she have such an effect on me?”  Emotions flooded Parnicail, clouding his thinking.  Something had to be done about this.  When asked, Krovicia told Parn her whole story.  Krovicia had been trying to seduce him to the evil forces that roam Norrath… for she herself worshipped Innoruk the God of Hate.  But Parnicail was not the only one here affected by another influence.  He and Krovicia spent long nights talking and Parn was overjoyed to hear that Kro would renounce her God and work on serving the causes of good.    From this point on Parn and Kro were inseparable and when Signa approached Parn about the creation of a guild, you could bet that Krovicia would be right there with support.

Parnicail has since joined the guild Belain and has sworn to eradicate evil from the world of Norrath.  He can be found in the forefront of most groups, but more often in a small corner of the local Inn, warming by the fire and strumming a soothing tune… because above all else, the passion in his music is his driving force in life.
