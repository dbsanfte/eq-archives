Another Epic Bard (10/6/03)

Well, not to make any other guild envious, but bards abound in Belain, and it was time to epic bard #3.

This was a TOUGH fight. With 18 averaging around 61, we thought we had it well in hand, but the nuke team went down early and Trak did that touch thing of his far more than average and it got dicey. In the end, all that was standing was one mage and two bards - go figure. Problem though: Our epic bard to be wasn't in the list of bards that were standing, and neither were any rezzers. Fortunately, Parn came through with some quick thinking about about 20 minutes later we had most of the force summoned (from shrooms) and/or rezzed plus one very happy Jubleblu looting some Dragon Gut Strings.

First BoT Run (10/5/03)

Well, I doubt it seems like much to the rest of the world, but last night we did our first "Just Belains" group in Bastion of Thunder. The team of Jumped, Neehobbin, Wyrmrender, Sydna, Salelear, and Singsang held lightning for a few hours with great results. This was actually a pretty "young" team for BoT considering an average level of just 63 and not all that many AA's to go around, but we did very well  - with the only trouble coming off of a couple of adds we got when mobs repopped right as we were pulling. This was many of the team's first run into BoT and, as usual, everyone handled themselves very well and we did very well. Now, if we could just get the named to pop and drop us a ornate chain glove pattern....

Bottom line: We'll be back - and often.
