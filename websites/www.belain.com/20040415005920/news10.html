A Sad Day (02/16/04)

It is often said that we all have our day when it becomes time to move on. Guilds are no different - members come and go. We celebrate new friends, and deal with the sadness when others depart. Normally, we don't make that big of a deal of departures, but when you lose truly special contributors, they deserve a special note of thanks for their time with us and their contribution to the guild.

This week we will be spending some extra time remembering times spent with Parnical (a long time Guild Leader of Belain), Sydna, and Arwa. They have served as backbones to Belain since the very foundation of the guild, and they shall be missed in so many ways.

Even as the guild moves on with new friends and new places, we will always remember the places we've been and the special friends left behind. We wish them the best in finding what they seek, and remind them that they always have friends here at home.
