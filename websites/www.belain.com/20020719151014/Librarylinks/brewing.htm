Beginner's Guide to Brewing


Neehobbin's Notes!

Chug, chug, chug.....ahhhhhh! Nothing like the taste of homemade brew to wet your whistle. While others are drinking that over-the-counter stuff, your pounding down Skunk Breath Ale or taking shots of Bleeding Brain. Beware, becoming a master brewer can lead to intoxication and strange visions. Drinks are on the house today!

Brewing is a fun trade skill to master. For the most part it's pretty useless but is a great compliment to other skills like tailoring, which requires at least a partial knowledge of brewing. Most of your concoctions will be made strictly for pleasure, while some will be used to complete a quest you have undertaken. Brewing is not a profitable skill but is easy to learn and master. Many of your ingredients can be found in taverns or merchants requiring little travel to get supplies. Brewing takes little time and a small amount of platinum so let's roll out the barrel and get started.

Let's Begin!
A few notes before you start:

While not necessary, it helps to put 20 training points into the brewing skill before you begin. I personally didn't put any training points into it.

As with all trade skills, high intelligence and wisdom is a plus for faster skill increases and successes but is not absolutely necessary. Anyone can take up brewing.

Many items you will combine are stackable. Place them UN-STACKED in whatever you are using to combine.

You will be combining your ingredients in a Brew Barrel. These can be found in almost every town. Most look like an old still used for making moonshine.
If the recipe calls for a flask of water, make sure it is purchased from a merchant and not summoned.

It is possible to skip certain steps. However, your skill increases will be slower and it may cost you more in the long run in terms of money. I personally like starting with the easy stuff and working my way up. This guide is exactly how I did it:

1.) You can pretty much master brewing without ever leaving Freeport. Make sure you have at least a couple of empty backpacks with you (10-slot works best) and some coin. There is a merchant named Krystin Charcoal you need to find first. If you enter Freeport from the gate, take your first left and follow until you see her in the corner.

2.) Start by making Short Beer. You will purchase from Krystin 2-4 stacks of barley, hops, and malt. Proceed to the Hogcaller's Inn on the way to East Freeport and inside purchase casks from any of the bartender merchants. Right outside is a brew barrel. Combine 1 barley, 1 hops, 1 malt, and a cask. If you succeed you will have made your short beer. If not, the cask will be returned to be re-used and you start over. Keep making short beers until they become trivial at skill level 31. Sell your successes back to a merchant or give them away. Do not drink them if you plan on continuing. Drinking will lower your stats and that is the last thing you want when practicing a trade skill.

3.) Next make Short Ale. This time you will only need 1 barley, 1 hops, 1 water, and a cask. Combine in the brew barrel until you can no longer increase skill at level 51.

4.) Your next brew is Ale. The ingredients you will need are 1 barley, 1 malt, 1 water, and a cask. Combine in the brew barrel until you can no longer increase skill at level 68.

Note: If you are working on tailoring, you can now make Heady Kiola's (2 kiola sap, 1 water, 1 bottle) with almost a 100% success rate.

5.) Last for now is Ol' Tujim's Fierce Brew. For this you will need 1 barley, 1 hops, 1 yeast, 1 malt, and a cask. Combine in the brew barrel until skill level 135.

 

Congratulations!! You are now a Master Brewer. Time to take a break and sip on a cold one.
