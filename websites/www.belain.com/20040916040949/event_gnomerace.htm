The Great Gnome Race
Chronicled by Krovicia - Official Events Officer
Photo's by Parnicail

The members of Belain headed again to the Tiger's Roar Tavern in High Pass expecting a night of fun and adventure. Little did they realize that they were not the participants in this journey. They were to be transported to a different world and be given a different body: A world of Gnomes on Rallos Zek.



It took a moment for everyone to get used to their new gnome bodies, but soon enough they were greeted by council member of the city. The unsuspecting members of Belain were sent on a mission across the world to help a dying Elder gnome. Everyone took off with youthful exuberance to deliver an antidote to the Priests of Life in Qeynos. Several roadblocks and peril stood in the way. Too many dangers may have been in the poor gnomes paths, because none were able to complete the journey before the graces of Gods returned the Belainities back to their original bodies. Wyrmrender, Taladorn, and Reide were the farthest along in the trek, but alas, the rift of time closed before they were to find out the fates of that strange land and people.
