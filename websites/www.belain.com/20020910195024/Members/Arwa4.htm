STORY:

 

If the story is true, it seems I was found alone at, what is now called, The Ruins.  No one knew how I came to be there, but many a kind Wood Elf helped and nurtured me in my growing years.  In order to earn my keep, I had the fortunate opportunity, to help build the city of Kelethin, the city in the trees.  Many a long year, and countless injuries and deaths  to my elven brethren resulted in the most beautiful and wonderful city in all of Norath.

I spent many an evening, after constructing additional bracing to the platform that supports the Bank, listening to stories and adventures from those travelers who were resting in our fair city.  I would dream of far off lands, battling creatures that I never knew existed.  Wondering where these lands were that I was hearing about.  Thus it happened that one of these kind travelers offered to help me start a new and fascinating life. 

He offered training, advice and some small aids in preparing myself for what lay ahead.  I am determined not to disappoint him, and to someday return the favor to those that may need my help.  So it is at this rather elder age, that Arwa, will begin his life, reborn as a young Druid, and adventure to these mysterious and foreboding lands he has only heard tell of in stories and fables.
