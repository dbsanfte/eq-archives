A Royal Killing Spree and More Dingage (12/21/03)

Seems we've gotten a bit of a backlog in Overking needs for the bot army, and, even more importantly, had a few mains with other royal needs. So, off to Chardok we went....

The evening started right off with a bang - or should we say a "Ding!" - as Hefner and Neehobbin duoed their way down to the castle long before the rest of the raiding party had even got there. Right as we were finishing some buffing, the word came in: Ding 65! for Hefner. GRATZ. Probably a good time for the rest of use to join you.

From there, we made our first AoE run into the Royals. We had a bit of an overpull at the entrance and almost had a wipe, but we finished it out ok, cleaned up a bit of a mess, and continued on in. Prince went down easy (as he always does), but we got a bit of excitement when the Overking room inexplicably aggroed  us as we exited the library. No problem though - AoE's still up, we took down the entire king room plus the hall mobs (Nine mobs including Overking) without a death - well, except for 9 Di'Zok's.

Gratz to Valathia (Prince head), Dakamina (Singed Scroll), and Khaba (Ancient Blade).
