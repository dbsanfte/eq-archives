STORY:

 

At a young age, Cuprin possesed an incredible amount of agility and stamina. Instructors marveled at his timeless ability to overcome exhaustion and were quick to push him beyond a level which normal humans could endure. His training as a monk was long and arduous and many times he would come home at night and ponder his great gift and its implications on his life. However, his differences from others made him an easy target for others to prod, being the brunt of many jokes he was forced into a life of travel on his own. He wandered the lands of Norrath knowing that trust could only be placed in himself. How could he trust another with his life? For him, life was too fragile and precious to entrust to others.

Cuprin came across the members of Belain who immediately opened their arms and embraced his presence. This act of kindness startled him since no one had ever openly shown such affection towards him before. Lured and entrigued by them, Cuprin decided to band together with this group of friendly adventurers and vowed to lend his abilities and gifts to aid them in their struggles. In Belain Cuprin has found a peace that he has not previously known. He has found trust both in himself as well as in others and he sees this as the greatest gift of all.
