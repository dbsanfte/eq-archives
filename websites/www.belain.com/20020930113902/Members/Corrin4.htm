STORY:

 

Corrin Flipperfeet is my name, aye- ya wondering where I got such a name. I will have to start when I was a wee little druid just learning to run about and play with the animals in the woods. I knew to respect all nature so my mum didn't worry to much about me. As I ran and played with some wolf cubs I wandered further and further from my village. I was having fun and the cubs could run so fast, ( they were older than I was, you see ). I didn't take time to see where I was going . When it got dark I realized I was lost. I was scared and ask the cubs to show me the way home. Well, they informed me they had to get home themselves as their mother would be mad. ( I thought about my mum too, and knew she would be upset ) so I ran along home with them hoping I could get someone to help me. We ran so fast I fell into a pond and discovered I could swim if I moved my feet and legs real fast. The cubs laughed at me but later told me they wish they had big feet too. I thought this was odd because my sister teases me about my feet all the time. ( She Is a wood elf, ok she is my half sister) anyway, as we got closer to the den I heard a loud growl and the cubs started whining. She came out of the woods and quickly started Cleaning the cubs, when all of a sudden she stopped and looked at me with sadness. "Ah, I see you have gotten lost little one, no worry I will take you home at daylight." We all went into the den to sleep. The next day we traveled a long way and I wondered how I got so far from home, not paying attention I fell into the same pond, again I swim as hard as I could and got out of the water. I was quickly licked clean and dry so off we went. The cubs were singing, "Corrin is a flipperfoot." Hum- Closer to nightfall I saw my little house and my mum sitting on the steps crying with worry. I ran to her, telling her about my adventure and was going to introduce her to the wolves but they were gone. I didn't get to say thank you. Then next several years went along uneventful. I learned my lessons but found I really enjoyed swimming. On the night of my fourteenth birthday an Alpha female wolf came to me in a dream and told me I was one with the wolves and could run fast as any wolf. The next day I awoke and forgot my dream and went out to play, and all of a sudden I was out running all the children close to my age, each day I ran faster and faster. Then on my twentieth birthday as I was taking a nap, the same alpha wolf came to me and said " Well little flipperfeet, you make us proud, soon you will be able to take our form but for now on you will always be known as Corrin Flipperfeet.
