Saturday, June 30th 2001

The members of Belain gathered in Butcherblock Mountains this fine day with eager anticipation for the upcoming event. Qnix had been scurrying around feverishly in days past putting the finishing touches on what would be the most intensive event to date. The Mountaineering Obstacle Course event would test both brawn and brains, and the participants were geared up and eager to begin.

Members were split into teams and then had to manuever through the zone using location points. At each "checkpoint" they would find a member who would reward each team with a small token and coordinates for the next checkpoint along with special instructions that would make each leg of the course most interesting. On your marks, get set, GO! ...and off they went to test their skills.

At first glance Piin and Ravapalic appeared lost and confused, but time would be on their side tonight.

The first checkpoint was located north of the zone to Greater Faydark at the chessboard, a favorite spot for the Faydwer crew in younger years past. Arwa manned the first checkpoint, and true to druid form was disguised as one of the giant trees that inhabit the area. Very tricky indeed, wonder who's idea that was <eyes Qnix>

The second checkpoint was located far across the zone at the docks to Freeport. Parnicail (as his enchanter Gaebril) was just underneath the edge of the dock at the bottom of the bay. Ironically, he was also in minor illusion with the form of the fantastic underwater burning torch! Panam and Neehobbin were the first to discover this clever disguise.

And off to Checkpoint three went the adventurers, and here is where the lovely and talented Sydna aided us most for she manned the third location which was located in the center of a goblin camp just outside the great gate of Kaladim. Yet again the tricky enchanters foiled the participants as Sydna took the form of a burning fire at the center of the goblin ring. But it wasn't long before our brilliant teams were able to pinpoint her location and recieve her special token, with information to the final checkpoint.

Salelar sat all alone at the south end of the zone near Dagnor's Cauldron. In fact he was alone 90% of the event. Poor fellow. Ever wonder what wizards do when no one is looking? Nestled behind one of the giant pillars which are scattered around the zone, Salelar waited patiently for teams to arrive. And as time had been kind to one of the groups, Piin and Ravapalic were the first to arrive at the final checkpoint. But here is where things got super exciting! When Salelar told the team they must race backwards back to the starting point (Gfay zone) their jaws dropped. In fact, it was quite some time before Piin even moved he was so shocked <grin>. But soon the two were racing off at the prescribed location, on the final leg of their adventure.

Piin and Ravapalic were the first team to cross the finish line and were awarded the grand prize. The other teams followed shortly after, and everyone posed for team photographs:
