Greig's End (10/24/03)

It was a long week - and a busy one. Indeed, it was so busy that we never got any updates up. Well, a week that saw time in Veksar, HoT and some other things culminated in our first crack at Grieg Veneficus.

This has to be the most meticulously researched raid we've ever done. We knew it was going to be a push for us, and a few people cancelling last minute had us concerned. Even with all the research, Grieg had lots of surprises for us - but that's where we shined. We adapted well.

In the end, it was us that fell, and yet we came out big winners. We'll be back for him, and there's little doubt that we'll get him - it was a strong enough showing, and with some tweaks and a few more in turn out.... down goes Grieg!

Stay tuned....
