ASSAULT ON BEFALLEN
Chronicled by Cuprin, Friend of the Guild
 
It was an overcast day in the commonlands outside of Freeport, when the Guild of Belain gathered to assault the forces of evil residing in the abandoned fortress known to the local inhabitants only as Befallen.  While not a Guild member, I am well known to many in the Guild, and was invited to lend my fists to the cause.
 

The group first met in the deserts just outside of the fortress.  The time was spent casting magics and glamours designed to protect us from the evils within.  Once fully prepared, we made our assault.  I had thought that with this level of powers, we would be thumbing our noses at Lrodd as we dived down the well.  Instead, it was an assault on every creature in the depths of the dungeon

 

The lowest levels of this den of evil is guarded by locked doors.  I believe that the guardian of the key for the lowest level must have heard rumor of the assault, because his presence, ever watchful for young adventurers that stray too far from the surface, was not to be seen that night.  Most of us being veterans of this foul place, knew of a secret passage that allowed us to venture to the lowest level, to a little used room that we used to stage our forces for the final assault.

 

Hard pitched was the battle, but in the end, we not only defeated all of the evils of the dungeon, we felt confident enough in the surroundings that many of us were able to relax.  Unafraid we rested and joked of  the grand assault, and the vanquishing of the evil.

 

The latest battle was won.  But Signa stressed that the forces of evil were never stopped for long, and that Belain must remain ever vigilant for the next battle. 

Well, I'm sure he would have said that if I had asked.
