The officers of the guild are compiling information on a daily basis which will eventually reside in this page. This information will give the member as well as the prospective member some insight as to who we are based on key events in the History of Belain. Please check back here frequently as our history is constantly being written. <smile>

 

Belain was founded by ten original members as required by the Unseen forces in Norrath. Some of these ten members remain current in the guild today, others have passed on to other guilds, or no guilds, or other planes of existence. Belain was created on February 10th in the year 2000 with Signa as its guild leader and the other nine members as follows: Wyrmrender Treefellow, Salelar Wyrmburner, Parnicail Lomelindi, Arwa Eldarion, Sydna, Calamus Blackguard, Cuprin, Embiggen, and Krovicia. For quite some time the numbers of the guild remained constant. We were a very tight band of adventurers who were wary of the character wishing to join with a purpose astray from our own. As stated in our original charter, our mission was to eliminate evil from the face of Norrath and to strive for the betterment of lives for all around us of pure heart and soul.

The organization of the guild was set up by the officers at that time, Wyrmrender, Salelar, Taluien, and Signa. They decreed that the 14 possible classes would be grouped into Realms of Magic, Healing, and Might and that each Realm would be represented by an officer. Salelar, being a wizard, was the officer for the Realm of Magic. Taluien being a druid, was the officer for the Realm of Healing. Wyrmrender, being a ranger, was the officer for the Realm of Might and Signa, being the guild leader, oversaw all the interworking dynamics of guild life.

The early days in Belain were a time of focus with emphasis placed on the charter and the mission. Members had a common purpose, a goal if you will, of eradicating evil from the face of the world. Everyone strove to achieve this both in groups and on their own. A very dear friend of Arwa and Sydna's was the next to join Belain. The first outside of the original ten, Syena Silverhue, a cleric from a good family of high elves was eager to lend her wisdom to our growing family. After Syena came a kind druid named Ivyy who was introduced to the guild by Krovicia. For some time after there were a few invites mostly of alternates of existing characters: Nimrais, Riane, Laliani, Hafar, Yilia, and Duran were invited in turn and helped strengthen the numbers of Belainites with their experience.

The first event organized for Belain was orchestrated by none other than the Officer for the Realm of Healing, Taluien Mellyrn. Not knowing what to expect of an event as such, the participants were leery of venturing into this new zone Lavastorm. Watching over everyone, Signa, Taluien, Krovicia, Wyrmrender and Salelar beamed with pride as the younger members made drakescale pie of the mobs in the zone. This was the first time everyone was banded together in support of each other. Following on the heels of this successful outing, Wyrmrender organized a raid on Befallen. Again we found our companionship to be our greatest asset as we fought side by side to eradicate the evil found in this dungeon. At the end of the evening we were all battle weary but emerged from the entrance with smiles of pride for ourselves and our guildmates.

Time marched on as it does and the guild began to grow slowly, adding the quality member rather than following the course of most guilds which search for quantity. All the while our guild leader was feeling the pull of age on his old bones. It was time, he felt, to part ways and pass on to that next great adventure. His decision to leave was a hard one. He had made many friends here in Belain and he didn't want to dissapoint them by leaving, but such is the way of things and his presence was requested elsewhere. Signa left Belain in May of 1999 and passed the role of the Guild Leader on to Wyrmrender Treefellow who gladly accepted the task. At this point I was promoted to the rank of Officer of the Realm of Might to fill the void which Wyrmrender had left.

The overall focus of the guild had changed with Signa's passing. The eradicating of evil in the world was no longer the primary goal, and in fact had been totally obliterated from the new charter under Wyrmrender Treefellow's leadership. The new course which Wyrmrender steered us towards was one where acceptance and friendship governed our everyday lives. Belain became a guild of close friends and family members... the beating heart of the collective as mentioned in the revised charter. Members felt a stronger bond between each other as time went by and this feeling of family ties grew. Quite some time passed before the next member was invited into our ranks. Ravapalic, the witful rogue was deemed worthy of membership and contributed to the guild with his compassion and sensability with the occasional backstab here and there <smile>.

Next came a time where the new charter was tested. The guild's first Dark Elf was accepted and invited to join... Rokuzachi the rogue. Rok was eager to fit in and did many good deeds to prove his devotion to our cause and family, but in the end it was just more than he could bear, his true soul yearning to join his brethren in the deep caverns of Neriak. So Rokuzachi departed Belain in search of himself. About this time Wyrmrender and Ivyy gathered everyone together in Highpass at the Tiger's Roar Tavern to make a special announcement. They had decided to exchange vows and were to be married as soon as it could be arranged. All members were overjoyed at the news as we partied late into the night in celebration for the two.

It was now my turn to organize an event, and I chose to introduce my favorite zone to some of the younger members who at the time were of age to hunt there. Cazic Thule event was fun for everyone and although it was the last night for Calamus as a Belainite, the members once again pulled together and fought with fervor and purpose. Belain had lost a member that night, but we had gained much more through the commin goal of the evening... a new experience.

Two more characters were then invited. Shacast SlayerofGixx, the high elf paladin, and Tyguss, the shaman were these two. The guildmembers were forging stong bonds of friendship when two more exciteable little fellows arrived on the scene. The guild's first halfling brothers were introduced as Qnix Beolach and Neehobbin Runmust. The inseparable duo climbed fast to reach the higher level members and by the time the New Year had come around, they had for the most part caught up. September of 2000 marked the time for the wedding between Wyrmrender Treefellow and Ivyy came due and the ceremony was held in Surefall Glade. The master and mistress of the ceremony were Arwa and Syena while Krovicia was the maid of honor and I was honored by becoming Wyrmrender's best man. The guild's first married couple were on their way to catalyzing a long and lasting friendship. Not long after, Toone Feistman was found roaming the fields of South Karana where Arwa and Sydna were quick to help one in need. Quite taken by the two, Toone vowed to join the family and the number of guildmembers began to grow and grow.

Back in the wooded forest of Faydark, a paladin acquaintance of Syena's was cutting his teeth on the orcs of the area. Impressed with his prowess, Belain welcomed Niloc to the family and he was recieved with hoots and hollars and the occasional WOOT as well. Then came Corrin Flipperfeet, the druidic counterpart to Toone who although shy and quiet at first, was overjoyed at being accepted and fit in very well with the guild. For some time after the only growth Belain saw was through the invitation of more alternates in Angmar, the guilds first necromancer, Groggin the dwarven warrior, and Taladorn the barbarian warrior.

Taluien Mellyrn decided to relinquish his duties as the officer for the Realm of Healing, turning them over to the next able member as he opted for a role with less responsibility. Ivyy then became that next able member as she gratefully accepted the position. Tal remained a member of the guild and still currently remains on the roster but his action is quite limited these days.

The next big stepping stone in the history of Belain was the 5th official guild event. Organized by all officers, this event was far removed from anything that had been done before and was accepted widely with great remarks. The Great Antonican Scavenger Hunt pitted teams of members against each other with the purpose of solving riddles, locating items, and turning them in to Wyrmrender in a quest style format. All members raved about this event and called for More More More!!! Sadly enough, after this Ivyy departed from her position as officer and then also departed from the guild to join forces with her friends in Valor of Virtopia.

Another member was called in to replace the void Ivyy had left and when Wyrmrender speaks... you had better listen. Arwa Eldarion became the next officer for the Realm of Healing and he brought with him 44 seasons of experience, more than any other member at the time. Just after this, Syena was aiding in the struggles of controlling Forest Giant population in the Frontier Mountains when she met a friendly enchanter named Subtle Force. Soon after, Subtle submitted his application and was admitted to the guild, the first new member in quite some time. Then after Subtle came a new young mage named Lightendoom. This Erudite was overjoyed at being accepted and with his warm and friendly demeanor, was welcomed with open arms. The guild's second Dark Elf Necromancer requested admittance and after some scrutiny by the officers, he was accepted. Nedurblud Inavein became the most recent member bringing 34 dark season with him to our table.

Soon after, a happy-go-lucky gnome wizard applied for membership. Piin Draggoon, a dear friend of Lightendoom's was overjoyed to learn that his application had been accepted and that he was now a full fledged member, and not long after both Lightendoom and Piin brought on board their alternates: Panam Truesoul the ranger, and Stoneshield the paladin.

More time passed and more adventures were being had by all. The New Year had come and the membership of Belain had remained strong in spirit and in mind. The family had grown together and learned together through tough times and many hardships. But always in their hearts remained a feeling of lasting friendship among each other.

Early February saw yet another spectacular event in "Operation Fireman's Carry" where members were teamed up with an escortee (level 1 barbarian shaman) and were required to escort him from the druid ring in SRO to his/her home in Halas. Certain rules were in place to make things a little more exciting... and exciting things certainly did happen here. Everyone was once again overjoyed with the organization and applauded the officers for their efforts. The winning team consisted of Qnix the druid, Subtle the enchanter, and Wolverene the bard.

"Hail Airagone and Camster" everyone exclaimed as the dynamic ranger duo came into the scene. Both new members were eager to join our cause and brought with them 15 seasons of experience to add to our numbers. Soon after this a kindly druid named Quesnelle sought entrance into our establishment and was recieved with open arms. One evening, not long after Quesnelle had joined, a friend of the guild: Krosham the bard was invited on a guild raid of the dungeon Kaesora. The evening was quite a success and everyone loved having Krosham there with us. Soon after Krosham decided to become a Belainite, having found a new home where she was loved by all.

The warrior-ess Zhazta Zmilez became the newest member after the introduction of Air and Cam. She was ready and willing to lend her services to our cause. Strangely enough, after Zhazta came a slew of alternate characters into the guild. She was such a fun loving person, everyone wanted to play with her so.... in came the alts. Not long after, the wonderful, marvelous Nitinya Vonlosch submitted her application and was accepted into the family. This crafty wizard was very eager to be a part of what we had become and offered her services to aid in any way she could.

A few months passed and the bonds of friendship grew stronger amongst everyone. Small raiding parties began to form with the introduction of more and more members and the increase in level with the current members. Parnicail organized a raid on Kedge Keep, coupled with the efforts of Aquilo Phoenix's Sulien Mer'Adin, wizard extraordinaire, who led the raid and showed our members just what it takes to be successful in this zone. Many friendship fires were kindled that day between the two guilds and everyone looked forward to future adventures with AP.

Quests had become the "buzz" word of the times and after the raid everyone embarked on solving these. Each class had specific quests in mind and everyone aided each other in solving them. One evening, while questing in the "Burned Woods", several members came across two Dark Elves wandering helpless. Briseis and Derris were cautious to approach the good-aligned Belain members but soon they all became friends and the two, along with a third friend Aerain, were welcomed to the family. Not long after, however, the three of them parted ways as they left the guild in search of solitary adventures.

Noitide Whiteshield, druid of Aquilo Phoenix, needed one final piece to obtain her epic sword, and several Belain members offered their assistance to her. Arwa, Cuprin, Neehobbin, Taladorn, and Parnicail followed Aquilo to the hidden Oasis in Timorous Deep where the great Dragon Faydedar resides. The spawn was forced and the raid on her was successful. Noitide expressed her gratitude to us and wished to thank each of us in turn.

Soon after, when we all were finishing up a ranger quest for Wyrmrender Treefellow, a call came to Parnicail... whispered through the wind, that Aquilo Phoenix was mustering troops to take down the infamous Lady Vox of Permafrost. Parnicail assembled a team of 6 Belain members and they all embarked on the journey that would take them to the Lady's Lair. The dragon raid was unsuccessful, but everyone was in good spirits for the simple reason that once again we had the opportunity to assist our good friends in AP. All in all it was a fun night!

Hail Hail Hail... was echoed throughout the lands! Qnix Beolach had been promoted to "Guild Events Officer" and in late June organized his first official event, "The Belain Navigation and Mountaineering Course". This event was located in the Butcherblock zone due west of Greater Faydark. It teamed members into groups of two where the leader would navigate from one location point to the next. After reaching each checkpoint the lead would then rotate to the alternate team member and off they would go until they reached the finish line. Each team was timed and the first place team of Neehobbin and Panam came in with a blazing record time of 43 min 31 seconds. When the event was all over, Wyrmrender Treefellow made the announcement that he was stepping down from his position as Guild Leader and that Parnicail Lomelindi would take his place. Many members were sad with his departure from leadership as Wyrm had successfully steered the guild through many seasons. But the members were excited to learn that Parn would be taking his place and Parn recieved a warm welcome into his new office.
