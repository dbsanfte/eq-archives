Extended: The Big Picture
[By: Zvi Mowshowitz]
Recently Wizards of the Coast announced a huge overhaul of Extended. The format lost many of its signature cards and sets, as a rotation took down the Ice Age and Mirage blocks along with Fifth Edition. In addition, they even took down the ten duallands. More than anything else, the loss of the ten duallands turns Extended into a completely different format. To understand what's going on, we need to go back in time to the origins of Extended..

The Evolution of Finkula in Type II
[By: Mark Fritter]   $50 Giveaway
This deck takes two principles of modern deck building and puts them to the test. The deck has to be flexible, able to beat both aggressive and control strategies. The best example of that in this deck is Dromar's Charm, which can act as a hard counter, creature removal, or life gain. In the main deck there is only one card that can ever be considered dead. Chainer's Edict, with Mono-black creature-less control and the near creature-less Trenches in the Meta game there are times where it will be your best discard fodder. The other theory put to the test in this deck is, "Card advantage wins games." With Shadowmage Infiltrator, Phyrexian Rager, Chainer's Edict, and Fact or Fiction. Giving us pure card advantage capabilities, as well as Mesmeric Fiend and Meddling Mage helping it along with some pseudo-card advantage. This deck should have few problems out distancing it self from many decks...
