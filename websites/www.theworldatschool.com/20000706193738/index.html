I am amazed and shocked at what I heard today on our local radio station.  It makes me wonder why we even bother striving to give our children an education. 

Our local radio station, WOKV 690, Jacksonville, Florida, has a host, Gregg Knapp who I've come to respect and enjoy listening to on my way home.  Today, he brought up something which really distrubs me.  It is something I haven't heard of until now, and am starting to understand more about it.  I am disturbed that this is going on in the public school systems.  What is it you ask?

Focus on the Family has referred to it as the "Dumbing down of America".  I am willing to agree with that summarization. 

Let me start out by saying, I don't know much about it.  I just found out about it and I plan on trying to research it more.  But from my initial impression, it lead me to at least attempt to do something about it.  Thus this webpage.  I have had this site on and off for a little over two years and have never really been able to make it into something worth while.  Maybe this will move it towards that goal, or maybe not.  Most importantly, as I told my wife tonight, I hope it at least helps others know what is happening in their schools.  It's just another thing that has helped us feel better about choosing to homeschool.

Ok here is what I heard this evening, and what has gotten me all uptight.  Like I said before, my understanding is sketchy and I hope to find out more as we go along, so bear with me.

Mr. Knapp referenced a few new curriculms being introduced into our school systems.   They are commonly referred to as New Math.  These math programs are designed to help those who fail at conventional math programs, namely women and minorities.  He mentioned two programs, Everyday math and another one which I cannot remember off the top of my head.  These programs are based on a simple principle.  One principle is that as long as they, the student, understands the basic idea of math, then they are fine.   For instance, 13-7=5 or 6, the answer doesn't matter as long as they understand the idea.  Um, hello? Is there something wrong with this or am I just crazy?   Another program was more geared towards a psycological look into math.  He cited a worksheet for 5th grade students.  Question A stated: If Math was a color, what color would it be and why?  Question B: If Math was a food, what would it taste like and why?  Question C: If math were weather, what would it be and why?  He said there were no numbers on the sheet.  Again, am I the only one who says, Huh?   

Our local homeschool group here had something on the front page of their monthly newsletter.  My wife and I thought it was just humor, until I heard this today on the radio.  Here is what it said.

A reason why we homeschool

Teaching math in 1950: A logger sells a truckload of lumber for $100.  His cost is 4/5 of the price.  What is the profit?

Teaching math in 1960: A logger sells a truckload of lumber for $100.  His cost of production is 4/5 of the price, or $80.  What is his profit?

Teaching math in 1970: A logger sells a truckload of lumber for $100.  His cost of production is 4/5 of the price, or $80.  Subtract $80 from $100 to figure out his profit.

Teaching math in 1980: A logger sells a truckload of lumber for $100.  HER cost of production is $80 and HER profit is $20.  Your assignment.   Underline the number 20.

Teaching math in the 1990's: By cutting down beautiful forest trees, the logger makes $20.  What do you think of this way of making a living:  How do the forest birds and squirrels feel as the logger cuts down the trees? There are no wrong answers.

We didn't see the light of this humorous article until we heard this story today.  What frustrates me the most is that who are these people who decide what curriculum to place in the school?  Who are these people designing these curriculums?   What are they smoking?  What are they thinking?  Why are we listening and letting them do this? 

What was even more moritifying was that our local school system has just placed one of these new math programs into place this year!  I couldn't believe it!  

Let me just say here, that we don't live in the same county as the one school district I'm talking about, but if that county is doing it, what is ours doing?  We have chosen to homeschool our two girls and EVERYDAY there is something like this going on that just confirms that we have done the right thing.  Not to mention the school shootings as well. 

I am going to research this more and try to have more information for you about this Dumbing down of America, but for now, if you know anymore or have some websites that you can refer me to, to help understand this more I would be greatly appreciative.  I will also post here any relevent links I can find, concerning this issue.
