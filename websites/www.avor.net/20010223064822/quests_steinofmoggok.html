Quests

Stein of Moggok Quest
- Avor, 2/12/01

This is a rather simple quest for a great reward.  If you aren't a Dark Elf, don't attempt this quest until you're level 12 (you'll need the Dark Elf illusion).  This quest took me about three hours without ports.  If you decide to walk, make sure you don't die!  The Stein of Moggok sells for 250-300 pp, and a great fundraiser for your spells.

With Ports

1.

 Bind yourself in East Freeport

2.

 Get a port to Tox Forest and go to Erudin

3.

 Go to the Blue Hawk inn at Eruding and buy Innoruks Kiss of Death

4.

 Give the Kiss of Death to Sinnkin Highbrow.  She can be found at the top of the Erudin Library. You'll get Erud's tonic from her.

5.

 Take the boat to Qeynos and head to the Temple of Life

6.

 Kill the Koadli fish in the pond near the Temple of Life and get the Fish Corpse Item. You may have to kill several fish to find the item.  WARNING: You'll get a bad faction hit for doing this.  The guards that patrol the pool will help the fish in battle, so be sure they aren't around when you attack them.

7.

 Gate back to Freeport and head to Riverdale.  You can also get a port to East Commons from a Wizard or a port to Misty Thicket from a Druid to get yourself there faster (Misty Thicket is right by Riverdale, East Commons is two zones away).

8.

 At Riverdale, buy 2 honeycombs from newbies or go to Misty Thicket and kll Bixies.  Take these and 30 gold to a girl near -200, 180, I forgot the exact location and name of the person. (She's in a building built into a mountain behind a counter alone.) Be sure to hail her to make sure you give the items to her. If she isn't Indifferent or more, change form into a gnome/dwarf and cast Alliance on her.  Give her the honeycombs and the 30 gold.

9.

 Gate back to Freeport (again!).  Take the boat to OOT and get off on the first stop. Buy a kiola nut from a merchant there and gate back to Freeport.

10.

 Go to the Seafarers Roost in Freeport (close to docks) and hail the man behind the counter (forgot his name too ...). If he talks about a recipe book, this is the guy to give the items to. Give him the Honeyjum, Fish Corpse, Erud's Tonic, and the Kiola Nut and he'll give you a recipe book.

11.

 Get a port to South Ro or Feerot and head to Oggok. Be sure to cast Illusion: Dark Elf before going anywhere, and cast Invisibility before you zone (since illusions wear off when you zone).  After zoning, recast I:DE and find Clurg near 200, -180. Cast Alliance on him (he should con Indifferent+ then, if not don't give him the book and wait till you get Benevolence). Give him the Recipe book and you'll get the Stein!
