Buying from Brownies
- Avor, 4/1/01

The brownies in Lesser Faydark are notorious for killing anyone they see. They have a camp in the northwestern(?) area of the zone, and hold some of our spells (the 'thicken mana' type), yet buying the spells is tricky. It's difficult to figure out, but it's easy to pull off.

At the brownie camp, cast Alliance or Benevolence on the guards. This should be enough to get them not to attack you (con them, if they are ready to attack or threatening, don't go in). Head into the camp, find a merchant, and cast 'Minor Illusion'. The merchant should now sell to you. The brownies are also not KOS to Elementals. You can con amiable to them in any elemental form.
