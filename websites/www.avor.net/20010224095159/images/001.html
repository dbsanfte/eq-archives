This is a shot of the windmills in the Steamfont Mountains outside the gnome city of Ak'Anon.  Probably one of the coolest buildings in Everquest.  These windmills are located pretty close in the center of Ak'Anon (0, -500).  How'd we get a high shot?  Ask your local spellcaster for Levitate and clime the hill, from there you can float onto the top of the mills!  Just make sure it doesn't wear off up there ... you'll have a fun time getting down alive :).
