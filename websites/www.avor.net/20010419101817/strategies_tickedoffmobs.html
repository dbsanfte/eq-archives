Tips and Strategies

Ticked off Creatures
- Avor, 2/14/01

In groups and soloing, Enchanters have a tough time with creatures. Their debuffs and choke line of spells increases the creatures hate toward you, making them chase you around - and your group and pet chasing it around. How can one avoid this? This is a problem you will face many times past the mid-teen levels, and it is this point in the game when creatures show how little hit points you have.

Root It!:
Rather than running, just root it! It's a great way to stop it from chasing you, and it lets your group, pet, or both still attack it (unlike mesmerizing it). It can also be rooted while one of your DOTs are on.

Memery Blur It!:
This is great if your in a group, it clears the hate list of the creature. The creature will most likely aggro onto someone else and leave you in the clear. There are times when the creature will still aggro onto you, but the hate toward you will be less than it was before, letting your group have a better chance of pulling it off you. If your soloing and want to memory blur a creature, make sure you don't have a DOT on it. The next time it deals damage, it will come back.

Root and Memory Blur Combo:
Root the creature, run away a little, then memory blur it. An excellent way to get a creature off your back without running to the zone. Use this if a "ready to attack" mob is chasing you. If you memory blur it while your next to it, the creature will just aggro back onto you.

Mesmerize it!
Mesmerizing should be a last resort. While mesmerizing can be successful, it doesn't go very well with groups (they might attack it and wake it up), pets (ditto), and DOTs (the next time damage is dealt, it will wake up), it can act the same way as a root almost.

That's basicly it. I'll update it as I find new ways or if I find these ways have loopholes. Until then, good hunting and good running :)
