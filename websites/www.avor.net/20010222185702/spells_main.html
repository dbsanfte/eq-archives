Spell List
Here's a list of all the spells for the Enchanter class. The one's that are not complete i will soon complete. They are arranged by level and alphabetical order.

The Location column shows where/how you can get the spell. If the location is blank, you can buy it just about anywhere Enchanter spells are sold. Howerver, if you can only find them in a specific city, or by reasearch, it is clarified there.

Location Key
LFaydark Lesser Faydark in the brownie camps. Spells such as Thicken Mana can be found here.
Research Found through research recipes
Neriak Neriak library. Enchant spells such as Enchant Gold can be found here.
Quest Found through a quest.
Erudin Found in the Erudin Library, high level illusion spells can be found here.
HighHK High Hold Keep. Animation spells past level eight can be found here.
Drop Many 50+ level spells are dropped off MOBs in Kunark, and these are them.
