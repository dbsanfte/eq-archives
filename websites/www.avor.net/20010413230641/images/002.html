The boat to Kunark, or the boat to Butcherblock Mountains (depends on wich way your going i guess ... :) ). This is a huge boat, and VERY fast when compared to other boats. I barely got the shuttle in on the right side of the picture. The boat doesn't dock (only in FV), so you have to ride these to the boat.  Be sure not to fall off the shuttles, you won't be able to make it on the deck then, but you can ride the rutter of the ship all the way!
