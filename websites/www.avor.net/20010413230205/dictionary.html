Enchanter's Dictionary

Term

Definition
Damage over Time (DoT)
Damage that is dealt over a period of time.  Example: 12HP/6 Seconds, deals 12 damage to the target every six seconds.
Direct Damage (DD)
Damage dealt instantaneously to target by a spell, not by damage over time.
Mesmerize
Puts a creature into a sleeplike state for a period of time.  You can run by and pull other creatures by it without it attacking.  This effect wears off when it is attacked.
Pet
A creature under your control, whether it is permanent or for a fixed amount of time.  If your attacked, the pet will fight.
