Forum User Groups

To request access to the members forums (you must be a MoS Guild member)

locate the Usergroups option at the top of the forum menu.

 

 

 

Once you click that option you'll get the Usergroup index page, locate the pull down menu and select Guild Members

 

Click View Information after you've selected Guild Members

 

 

You will see this at the top of the Guild Members listing

Click Join Group, and an email requesting acess will be sent to the site moderators.

Once we get a chance to verify your access, you will be added.
