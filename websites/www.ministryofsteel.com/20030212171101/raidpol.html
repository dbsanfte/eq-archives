Raid Information will be posted on the website, the Anvil as well as a message posted as part of the Guild MOTD when you login into EQ.

    BELOW ARE THE POLICIES/RULES OF THE GUILD RAIDS:

  1. SHOW UP ON TIME. It will be your responcibility to be at raid site, or at a transport spot BEFORE the raid starts if you want to attend.  Do not show up 30 minutes after raid has started and expect someone in raid to leave to get you.

  2. After the raid is over transportation away will be provided.  Please do not expect to be ported out right in the middle of the raid, when the porters services are needed in the fighting.

  3. Loot will always be NEED before greed.

  4. No one will be left behind, and all corpses will be recovered, and rezed if at all possible.

  5. Do not expect to always get the good loot, or even exp on every raid.  That isn't possible every time, but please join the raid to help out the guild.

  6. Listen to what you are told, pay attention to the raid leader, and follow instuctions.

  7. Feel free to invite any non guild people to the raid you want,the more we can get the better.  But, they will be expected to go by these rules.

  8. All tells that relate to the raid such as pulls, adds, need for healing (if no cleric/healer in your group) will be called out in chat channel made by raid leader.

  9. Only Group Leaders may give private tells to the Raid Leader during the Raid. Being a raid leader can be stressful and it turns out that the raid leader is usually the main puller of the raid as well.  With this going on the Raid Leader does not need the added confusion of trying to sort through alot of different private tells to see which ones are important and pertain to the raid and which ones don't.
