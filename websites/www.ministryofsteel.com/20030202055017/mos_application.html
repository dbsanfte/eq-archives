Ministry of Steel Codes of Conduct

I will treat all my guild mates like family members because that is what they are.

I will play by the Verant Rules of Conduct including the "Play Nice Policy".

Slandering of GM's, other guilds or members of my own guild will not be tolerated in ANY public forum, including Say, Guild, or Group (when grouped with Non-MoS family members). If I have a problem with anyone I will contact a Guild Officer in private tells for resolution.

I will not Ninja loot under ANY circumstances.

I will not knowingly Kill Steal another character in EQ.

I will try to have my Guild Tag visible at all times. MoS is a family oriented guild and I will be proud to show that I am apart of the family.

I will adhere to the "3 strikes and your out" criteria of the guild.

I will help a family member in need whenever I can.

I will do nothing that will tarnish the MoS Guild Tag. The MoS family is in good standing on the Povar server and we strive to keep it that way. An act that will disgrace the MoS Guild Tag will be taken very seriously and will be reviewed by the officers for corrective action, probation or if the offence is bad enough I can have my membership revoked.

Please do not sell in guild chat. It only makes those of us with really crappy equipment and no cash feel unworthy and somewhat depressed.

Please do not repeatedly ask to be power-leveled, for ports, or for equipment. Everyone is trying to level as best they can, and most are more than happy to help when they have the time. Patience is a virtue, and while you may not have any, try to fake it if I hum a few notes.

I have read and understand the guild raiding policies and everything it encompases.

Please do not beg in guild chat. It's like being stuck at the tunnel in East Commons with no legs to run away with. And I understand that if I continue begging it will lead to the "3 strikes and your out" criteria.

Hug your guild druids and/or wizards... even when you don't need a port. It makes them feel better about themselves, and there's nothing worse than a druid and/or wizard with low self-esteem. I know you love them, you know you love them, THEY even know you love them, but a bit of re-enforcement never hurt anyone.

Please do not ignore others due to level differences. It is rude, and completely unnecessary, as it takes perhaps four whole seconds to type in the word 'hello'. Granted, that four seconds could mean the difference between life and death at times, but if you can, try and greet people as they come on. It's the little things in this life that make our days.

Please do not repeatedly ask to be power-leveled, for ports, or for equipment. Everyone is trying to level as best they can, and most are more than happy to help when they have the time. Patience is a virtue, and while you may not have any, try to fake it if I hum a few notes.
