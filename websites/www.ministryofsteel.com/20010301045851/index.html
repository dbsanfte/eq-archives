Organization rules

  1. Teleports to Misty Thicket will be available 1 hour before the raid, and NONE during
  2. If you need a teleport please go to a druid ring BEFORE 5pm.
  3. Dida (or Feda) will be camped in Rivervale for Binds.

  5. No whining.

  1. groups will be created by lvl.
  2. toons should be at least lvl 12 for this raid.

Once we as a guild (+friends) can pull off a successful raid on runnyeye, I will consider planning a raid elsewhere.
