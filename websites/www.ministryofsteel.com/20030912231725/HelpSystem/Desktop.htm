This will show you how to add a "5 most recent posts" utility to your desktop (windowsXP)

this can also be done with Windows98,ME,2000 and of course XP.
 

To start of, right click on your desktop, it will pop up this menu

and click on Properties..

You will see this menu pop up next

Click on the "Desktop" tab as show above

Then click the Customize Desktop button..

You will get this menu

Click on the "Web" Tab.

Click new and type in http://quantum.sytes.net/desktop.php , in the input field.

That's all, you are set, every 20min you will be updated with the most recent 5 posts.
