Timorous Deep Fire Pot Room

There is a portal room to most of the major cities in Norrath found in Timorous Deep. The /loc of this room can be found at -12260, 4665. The spires of this portal room stick up just a little above the water, get in the middle of the spires and swim straight down and you will find an entrance to this room. Once in the room you are no long considered underwather, so no breathing device is needed. Just as a side note, you use to be able to bind here in this room, not sure if you are able to anymore, from what I hear, you cannot anymore, but have not checked for myself.


  • Face North, Left Hand side: There is a log fire on the floor, click on the fire will take you to the entrance of Grobb. The home city for Trolls.
  • Face North, Middle of wall: There is a six sided gray basin, click on the basin and it will take you to the entrance of Oggok. The home city for Ogres.
  • Face North, Right Hand side: There is marble white medium tall six sided fire pot with a logo on the front. Clicking on this fire pot will take you to the docks in Erudin. The home city for Erudites.
  • Face East, Left Hand side: There is a six sided charcoal basin with a logo on the front. Going to this basin will bring you to entrance of the Neriak Commons. The home city for Dark Elves.
  • Face East, Middle of wall: There is a four sided medium sized wood fire pot. It has what looks like to be the logo of a golden eagle. Clicking on this fire pot will bring you to the underside of Kelethin. The home city for Wood Elves.
  • Face East, Rigt Hand side: There is a six sided rusty brown basin with a logo on the front. Going to this basin will bring you to the entrance of Northern Felwithe. The home city for High Elves.
  • Face South, Left Hand side: There can be found a brown box with a knife resting next to the fire portion on top of the box. This fire box will bring you to the docks of Halas. The home city for Barbarians.
  • Face South, Center of wall: There Will be a dark brown basin with a blue logo on it, that if you read closely will read "Freeport". Clicking on this basin will bring you to East Freeport at the docks. The home city for Humans.
  • Face South, Right Hand side: There will be a tall golden like vase. This vase brings you to the entrance of West Cabilis. The home city for Iskar.
  • Face West, Left Hand side: There is what looks to be like an off white stone basin. This basin brings you to the entrance of South Kaladim. The home city for Dwarves.
  • Face West, Center of wall: You will find what looks to be a lava lamp of sorts. This is the only portal structure in this room that does not actully have fire in it. This tall structure will bring you to the entrance of Akannon. The home city for Gnomes.
  • Face West, Right Hand side: At this location you will find what seems to be a small torch stuck into the ground. Clicking on this small torch will bring you to the entrance of Rivervale. The home city for Halflings.
