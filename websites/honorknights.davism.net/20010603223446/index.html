Mojynx --- 14 May 2001 --- Guild Meeting
Guild Meeting
Our last guild meeting was 14 May 2001. There were a few topics covered and some announcements made. A summary follows:

1) We are holding, and are invited to several raids. Some of which have had to be canceled, post-poned or delayed. This is not good. We need to increase participation in these raids. Please see the Raid website and the message boards for details. Please participate in guild activities.

2) Nuliane is edging towards completion of her Epic, and Misslila has started on hers. A few other members are also doing theirs. Any help you can provide any of these members towards their Epic will be remembered by them when it is time to do yours. Please assist as you can.

3) A wedding has been announced between Dukest and Sardonyx. The ceremony will be Monday 4 June 2001 at 9pm EDT (2am GMT) in the West Commonlands at the East Commons zone line. All are invited to attend.

4) A lowbie raid has been scheduled for next Monday starting at 8pm EDT (1am GMT) for the Warrens. A few porters will likely be needed to assist in transporting the lower level guild members to Toxxula Forrest and escorting to Paineel. Please see the message boards and Raid site for details.

Mojynx --- 7 May 2001 --- GM Event on Faydwer
Princess Vie returns to Felwithe, gets heckled
Fairly large GM event Sunday evening on Faydwer. It seems a certain high elf princess of some type wandered back to Felwithe and thus ensued an event. Being one of the few Dark Elves on Faydwer at the time, I took it upon myself to (lightly, in character) heckle the good princess. Those darn lighties were all threatening to have my head. In all seriousness, though, it was quite a bit of fun and provided some insight into coming conflicts. Parts of the event were in Greater Faydark, Lesser Faydark and Northern Felwithe. I learned a few things in all these locations (had to use my secret stealth dwarven warrior for the Felwithe stuff):

1) Firiona considers Cazic Thule a larger and more immediate threat than Bertoxxulous, despite the goings on in the Karanas.

2) Because of the increased threat of Cazic Thule on Faydwer, Firiona was unable to summon her mother, Tunare to Norrath.

3) A bit of the mystery of the Realm of Heros was unveiled as 8 new members were inducted by King Thex for assisting his daughter through her weary day

4) I missed most of this, because I thought it was over before it was, but the governor of Cabilis, Danak Dhorentath made a visit to Faydwer as well.

If you ask me, I think Kelethin is in for some trouble, and perhaps Felwithe too.
