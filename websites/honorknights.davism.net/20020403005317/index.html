Drjynx --- 31 March 2002 --- Grung says Guud-bye
Grung leaves last tribute to his guild before he retired.

Grung, a beloved Ogre warrior and long time member of the Honor Knights recently hung up his helm, stored his trusty axe, donated several of his most precious belongings to charity, and took all his cooking supplies back to his mother's rock hut in Ogguk. Before he settled down in his new life of retired warrior, he gave our guild a very special gift. He chose to sacrifice himself for the betterment of others, as he has done so many times in the past. He provided the guild with 25 Soulstones, and in doing so wrote out the letters "HK" in the Eastern Commonlands with his corpses. So, Grung, the Honor Knights sincerely thank you for your final gift of selflessness to our order, and salute you for the time and effort you put into this guild.

You will always be a Knight of Honor!

May Tunare bless each of you in your journeys,
Drjynx
Theologian

Zujynx --- 12 March 2002 --- Epic of the Mystic
Pyrrus converses with spirit; receives a spear

A most excellent piece of news has reached my ears! A local mystic knight of honor, Pyrrhus, has recently coordinated bringing justice to Rak`Ashiir! With that done, he was able to convince his guide, the sentinal spirit, to share with him the Spear of Fate. Not only is this a magnificent weapon visually, but it is very powerful as well. Only the most powerful of spirit callers ever achieve this feat. Much congratulations to Pyrrhus on his achievement, and thank you to all Honor Knights that assisted and participated!

Zujynx
Veterinarian
