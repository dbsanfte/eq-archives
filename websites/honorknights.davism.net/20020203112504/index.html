Mojynx --- 29 January 2002 --- Fan Faire, Reminders, etc.
Mojynx and Natakux attend Fan Faire

This past weekend, an Everquest Fan Faire was held in Dallas, Texas. Mojynx and Natakux were able to attend and represent the Honor Knights and the E`ci server. A fun and good time was had by all. Based on discussions in the Q&A forums that we attended, it seems there will be some good and interesting changes in the land of Norrath in the near future. Keep you eyes on Casters' Realm who taped all the Q&A sessions and have promised to provide transcripts on their site. A few photos are available on the screen shots page. Also, look for Natakux to return to playing in the near future as the needed components arrived just before the Fan Faire.

Raid Reminders

Honor Knights is running a raid on Fear on January 30th and a raid on Hate on January 31st. Please visit the message boards for all other details and sign-ups. If you are 45+ you are really needed on these two raids. Please make every effort to attend.

Server GM a true Honorable Knight

The E`ci server Game Master, Grog, recently but briefly became a member of our guild. In working to fix an invite bug, he became a guild member for a few moments. A few screen shots are on the screen shots page. Thanks to Grog for being such an excellent GM, a good sport and a fine Barbarian hunk. Yum!
