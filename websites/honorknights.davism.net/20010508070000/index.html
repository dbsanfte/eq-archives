Mojynx --- 20 April 2001 --- Guild Meeting Synopsis and Raids
Guild Meeting in NK
Our last guild meeting was Thursday, 19 April 2001 at the Wizard spires in North Karana. Thanks to Bertoxxulous, all were treated to a myriad of symtomatic illnesses. There was a lot of information presented at the guild meeting.

Nuliane reminded us that Misslila was to be treated and respected in the appropriate manner for one who is pro tempore of the guild. Please be mindful of this if you see fit to challenge her opinons.

The White Cross and Honor Knights have formed an alliance. Wyvernwill Shadowbreaker from The White Cross attended our meeting and participated in the announcement. Their guild web site is linked to ours in the links section. Check them out, and give them a Hail! when you see them.

Nuliane had a very important announcement; her upcoming nuptuals to Roane Taisse! Much congratulations to the both of them. The ceremony is planned for 2 May 2001 at 7pm Eastern Time (12am GMT 3 May 2001). Location details will be forthcoming soon.

Welcomes are in order for three new guild members who joined up last night! Hails for Lody, Pochacco, and Xdatinelia!

Guild RAIDS coming up soon
Our guild will be participating in some raids coming up. The raids are TODAY (Friday) to Kael and 30 and 31 May 2001 (Wednesday and Thursday) to the planes of fear and hate. Be sure to visit the message boards and the Honor Knights raiding website for all the details.
