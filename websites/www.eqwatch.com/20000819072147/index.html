WAKE OF TRANQUILITY

From the Everquest Gameplay Forums

Wake of tranquility seems to have an inappropriate level assigned to it for clerics. Enchanters currently get it at 51 and clerics at 55.

When GZ was designing the spell layouts, he made a list of all of the spells that he wanted to implement, and then set it up so that a certain number of spells were implemented in a given level. Though it may appear to make sense for a particular spell to be given a few levels earlier or later, it would upset the way that the spells are allocated.

We actually made a mistake at one point, giving group resolution after group heroism. I'm not sure how that happened, but rather than shift the spells back to where they should be, we're actually going to replace one of the spells. I'm mentioning this to illustrate how important it is that we provide spells at the level which they are provided.

- Gordon

VERMICULATED ARMOR

From the Everquest Gameplay Forums

Why does Vermiculated, the planar, chain based armor for druids (a class that CANNOT be large races, btw), weigh almost 10 stones MORE than every set of planar plate? This extra weight is bogging some of us down. Every set of planar plate weighs 37.2 stones. Vermiculated weighs 36.

I've spoken at length with the design team about some various item-issues that appear to be out of whack at first glance. The druid planar armor was among them. My understanding is that the weight is intentional in order to provide downsides for wearing the armor. Melee characters aren't typically penalized for wearing armor as it is needed by them to perform their duties, whereas with casters, it is a luxury.

That luxury has some downsides in this case.

- Gordon

MONK COMPLAINTS

From the Everquest Gameplay Forums

Before Kunark came out, monks were indeed the best at dishing out damage. Here's a table of the skill caps of various classes:

Warriors - Rogues - Monks
1hb: 200 - 200 - 240
2hb: 200 - 200 - 240
Defense: 210 - 200 - 230
Double Attack: 205 - 200 - 210
Dual Wield: 200 - 210 - 252
Offense: 210 - 210 - 230

Here we can clearly see monk skills beating out the other classes, with 40 points of weapon skill, 5 points of Double Attack, 42 points of Dual Wield, and 20 points of Offense better than any other class. The extra 20 points in Defense is nice too, but the purpose of this post is Offensive skills. Now let's take a look at the POST 50 skill caps.

Warriors - Rogues - Monks
1hb: 250 - 250 - 252
2hb: 250 - 250 - 252
Defense: 252 - 252 - 252
Double Attack: 245 - 240 - 250
Dual Wield: 240 - 245 - 252
Offense: 252 - 252 - 252

The gap, apparently, closes GREATLY. Monks are a mere 2 points better with weapons. An even bigger loss is the huge difference in dual wield, as warriors and rogues moved up 40 points, monks were stuck at 252, which closes the gap to a mere 7 points. And the 20 point advantage we had with Offense becomes zero. Double Attack remains the same ratio, and we lose our nice Defense bonus.

Yes, the gap closes. Monks go up. Warriors and Rogues go up faster. No one "loses" relative power.

Backstab is not a "toe-to-toe" situation. Yes, monks still do more damage in a toe-to-toe situation over a given timeframe than the other melee classes. Not as much as they used to, but they still do more than the others given appropriate equipment.

- Gordon

JULY 20  2000

/CONSENT WILL BE CHANGED

From the Everquest Gameplay Forums

Hello all,

Many people have suggested over the past few months that we should implement a version of the /consent command to give people the option to drag a corpse (via /corpse) rather than loot it, due to the amount of item theft that takes place. As you know, we previously ruled out such an option for various reasons, not the least of which were technical limitations.

After considering issue for some time, and taking into account the customer-service issues created by those who loot corpses and steal items under the guise of /consent, we've decided that a change to its operation is warranted. In the next patch, /consent will be changed to give permission to drag the corpse only, not to loot it. Other people will not be able to loot your corpse.

Though we realize that some will miss the ability to /consent in the previous fashion, the proliferation of NODROP items in game has made asking someone else to loot your corpse a very infrequent event. Given the ability to do one or the other, consent for loot, or consent for drag, we believe that most people would prefer the option to /consent for drag.

We hope that this change will address everyone's concerns by eliminating the amount of antisocial conduct that surrounds corpse looting.

- Gordon
