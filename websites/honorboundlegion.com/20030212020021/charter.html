GENERAL RULES:

#1. There is no set leader. All dicisions are made by the High Counsel. There will be seven High Counsel members, these members will be voted in by the general membership of the guild.

#2. If you have ideas or questions concerning the guild tell a counsel member and it will be discussed at the next counsel meeting.

#3. The counsel will meet no less than 2 times per month. If you do not attend you forfeit your right to vote on the subject discussed.

#4. If you cannot attend a meeting or event please notify one of the Counsel members. We want to accommodate everyone so we will set these up so most can attend.

#5. We will try to have guild raids at least once per month. We would like to have them two times per month if possible.

RECRUITMENT:

#1. In order for someone to join they must be aleast level 30 and one of the existing members must sponsor them. When you sponsor someone they are your responsibility. You must make sure they know about the guild and the rules of the guild.
