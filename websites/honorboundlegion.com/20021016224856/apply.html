Raid Calendar



Minimum Requirements
Prospective candidates must achieve a minimum level of 30 for entry into guild and have a member sponsor them.

Trial Period
New candidates have a 2 week trial period in which the candidate groups with members and demonstrates skill and honor. After the 2 week period, all council members must approve entry.

Character Entry
A maximum 3 characters may be guilded: 1 main and 2 alts.

Class/Race Requirements
Entry for evil races/classes is allowed only if roleplaying is not followed. Candidates must play with honor.

The Final Rule
No /anonymous without officer approval and a good reason. /roleplay is allowed. First infraction incurs a warning, second incurs a warning in front of the whole council. Third infraction results in removal from the guild.
