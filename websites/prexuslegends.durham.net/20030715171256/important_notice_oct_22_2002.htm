Important Notice Oct 22 2002

There are a couple of items I would like to deal with, none of which is difficult nor directed at individuals but at the whole as a mention rather then a chastise.

First a quote that I try and live by as a general rule of thumb --

"Good leadership involves responsibility to the welfare of the group, which means that some people will get angry at your actions and decisions. It's inevitable, if you're honorable. Trying to get everyone to like you is a sign of mediocrity: You'll avoid the tough decisions, you'll avoid confronting the people who need to be confronted, and you'll avoid offering differential rewards based on differential performance because some people might get upset.

Ironically, by procrastinating on the difficult choices, by trying not to get anyone mad, and by treating everyone equally "nicely" regardless of their contributions, you'll simply ensure that the only people you'll wind up angering are the most creative and productive people in the organization."

—General Colin Powell

With that said, I do not intend to break up Prexus Legends nor turn it into any type of Military style organization. We may lose members and will be a shame to see them go. I do not believe what I am asking is going to be hard or al that involved in comparison to what is asked of many other Guilds members and is currently being asked of our Officers and Myself.

1) People have asked "When is the next RAID?? " and I continued to answer with soon need to plan it as to the current member base of Prexus as to be sure and have not only a successful raid where the goal is achieved, minimal to no deaths and that AL are happy they attended. I do have many such plans for events and Raids that are possible. The problem is that thee are many Characters that are not on the Roster at the moment.

Why is the Roster important? Well I and other Officers use the Roster to plan a raid based on level, class, number of available characters etc etc. that is our first stopping point to plan who gets what for loot as well.

It has been decided that all Guilded toons are to be on the roster I'm am no longer going to hand hold and beg people to register their characters. I will post notices in the /guildmotd al this week to read this page and as of Monday Oct 28th I will begin /guildremoving anyone not on the roster that I see online.

On the event that I see a non rostered toon online I will send a tell and expect that person to either find a safe spot to camp and and comply with this or be /guildremoved. I run multiple PC's at home when I play and will be able to see said toons as they are registered on the roster.

2) The next item I would like to address is the message boards. I realize there are a lot of popup ads that are annoying, these are there because we are currently using the "free version" and this is how EZboards pays their bills for their servers etc that host the said boards. Have an idea for a raid and or a quest you would like help with post it here and we'll see what we can do to answer it and or help with raid /quest.

As the increase n activity on the message board dictates I have two options in consideration at the moment one is that I will forward the money personally to rid the popups the other is that I will build and host a new server for our message boards also out of pocket for the hardware and software required. I work very close with ISP related companies and have the ability to "co-locate" a UNIX box running MYSQL and a Yabb message board if that is the decided route.
