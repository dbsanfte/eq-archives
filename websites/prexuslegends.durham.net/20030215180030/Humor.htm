PATCH MESSAGE, JUNE 27, 2002

- Servers will be comming down tommorrow for emergancy maintence. Estimated downtime is 3 hours. (kidding!!! its really 10)
- We at Verant will admit, we don't have a clue how Everquest really works.

** General **

- Fixed a problem with the Female Elve graphics and animation. New armor graphics can be viewed here: High Elf, Dark Elf, Wood Elf, Half Elf. That is plate armor shown there. Troll and Ogre females in bathing suits will be available in the next patch.
- Fixed a bug that was causing some people to crash when running really fast into zone walls.
- Fixed stacking issues with dwarfs, halfings, and gnomes. They can now reach reach the hight of an ogre when standing on each other's shoulders.
- Va Shir will no longer have a swimming skill and can "Forage" hair balls as throwing weapons.
- Iksar will have the racial ability of two illusions. Alligator Hide Handbag or Crocodile Skin Boots.

** Class **
- Beastlords Epic effect has been changed. Just hail your guild master and say "Gimm3 tH3 pH@t l3wT!!". Combine in the bag you receive the two claws and four pieces of rabbit meet. Don't be surpised when the effect is just a small snow bunnie pet that keeps multiplying.
- Beastlords will have all skills increased to surpass that of rogues, monks, and warriors.
- Necromancerss will now have the option of summoning Bevis or Butthead pets.
- Changed the names the of post 50 rogue titles. 51-54 Spade 55-59 Rake 60 Shovel

** Spell **

- Removed Spirit of Wolf from Va Shir Beastlords and shaman spell line. Cats are known to be afraid of dogs. Instead, all Va Shir will now be given an innate running skill that is just as fast as Speed of Cheetah.
- Va Shir are immune to the spells with "wolf" aspects. Instead they will become cute colored kittens: Tigger Form, Simba the Lion King, and Fat Tabby.
- All of the wizards imps have been removed in game. They have been replaced insted with monkeys in a fez hat and small vest that wields a Stien of Moggok. Spell adds +10 int.

** Discipline Related **

- Added the following /disciplines for Beastlords:
Level 10: The Discipline of Hissing Kitten. Beastlords will have increased stats for 2 minutes similar to a berserk warrior and their hair will "stand out" causing a 5 point electrical damage shield. Stacks with all known damage shields (we think).
- Added the following /disciplines to Troll and Ogre Warriors:
Level 50: Big Sweatty Guy. BSG is AOE stun and mez with knockback effect.

** Zone Related **

- Sleepers Tomb will be revamped . The four warders are going to be replaced with the Beastlord class pets of the Iksar, Troll, Va Shir, and Ogre races which will also have level 60 ranger pets(they got to have warders too!).

Thank you,

The EverQuest Team
