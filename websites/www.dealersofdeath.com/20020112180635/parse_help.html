This script parses your log file from Everquest. It will parse the whole file that it is given, so if you have logged multiple play sessions on different characters, you will want to edit the log file before parsing. The lists at the top allow you to control what information is displayed. Select those things you wish to see (Ctrl-click to select multiple ones). The sidebar allows you to go directly to the information you are interested in. The parse button does the actual work (this can take awhile, both for the file to be uploaded and a few seconds to parse it) and the Colorize button will display your file in a color coded fashion. The best way to tell if the program is parsing all the information available or if there are things that need to be added is to colorize with only the Unparsed color option selected - any line displayed was not parsed for information.

The drop down list box will be populated with all available monsters/players after parsing for the first time. You can then choose to reparse (including uploading the file again) from that person's point of view. This does not work for all things - some things are only displayed to the person doing them. This would be useful for specially cut logs for major fights, or for simply finding out what the detailed damage done by a monster is. In general the melee information is accurate but all other information is probably for you.

General (non-combat) Information

  • Kills - Displays a list of all the things you killed, solo or grouped.
  • Money - Displays how much money you earned (from corpses, quests, selling, stealing).
  • Zones - Displays the zones you visited.
  • Monsters - Displays all the monsters you conned - how they regarded you and the level indicator.
  • Track - Lists any monsters you tracked (and how many times).
  • Forage - Counts how many times you foraged and what you foraged.
  • Sense Heading - Displays how well and how much you used Sense Heading.
  • Drinks - Lists any alcohol you drank.
  • Inspections - Lists who inspected you.
  • Skills - Lists any skill increases you got.
  • Faction - Displays and faction adjustments you had.
  • Looted - Lists all items looted by your group (I wish they logged your own too, oh well)
  • Linkdead - Lists who went LD in your group.
  • Yelling - Lists who yelled for help in your hearing.
  • Die Rolls - Lists all dice rolls you witnessed (sorted by number range).
  • Die Rolls2 - Lists all dice rolls you witnessed (sorted by player).
  • Duels - Displays any duels you heard about.
  • Random - Lists some random stuff that happened to people/monsters around you (might get sorted out eventually).
  • Costs - Shows how much things cost from various vendors.
  • Purchased - Shows how what you purchased and for how much.
  • Buys For - Shows what various vendors will buy things for.
  • Sold - Shows what you sold and for how much.
  • Pickpocket - Lists any thievery you did.
  • Bandage - Lists any bandaging you did.
  • Spells Resisted - Lists any spells you resisted.
  • Guild - People who chatted on Guild-line.
  • Falling - Damage taken from falling, and count of times other people fell.

Melee related Information

  • Combat By Type - Shows your melee breakdown by damage type (kick, slash, etc.).
  • Combat By Monster - Shows your melee breakdown by monster.
  • Combat Taken by Type - Shows the damage you took by type.
  • Combat Taken by Monster - Shows the damage you took by monster.
  • Stuns - Lists how many times you were stunned.
  • Taunt - Shows how much and who you taunted.
  • Disarm - Shows how much and who you disarmed.
  • Dodge - Shows how much and who you dodged.
  • Parry - Shows how much and who you parried.
  • Riposte - Shows how much and who you riposted.
  • Combat Avoidance - Shows how much melee damage you avoided taking.
  • Evade - Shows how much you evaded.
  • Instill Doubt - Shows how much and who you attempted to invoke fear in.
  • Frenzy - Lists who you saw go into a berserker frenzy.
  • Backstab - Shows the breakdown of what backstab damages you did.
  • Criticals - Shows the breakdown of what critical hits you did.
  • Max Combat - Shows the max you did for each damage type.
  • Combat Range - Shows the total number of times you hit for each damage amount.
  • Combat Done by Type and Monster - Shows your melee breakdown by type done to each monster.
  • Combat Done by Monster and Type - Shows your melee breakdown for each monster by type of damage done.
  • Combat Taken by Type and Monster - Shows your melee taken breakdown by type done to each monster.
  • Combat Taken by Monster and Type - Shows your melee taken breakdown for each monster by type of damage done.
  • Other Combat Done - Shows the combat damage done by every monster and player you witnessed.
  • Other Combat Taken - Shows the combat damage taken by every monster and player you witnessed.

Spell related Information

  • Casting Stats - Shows some general information about your spell casting.
  • Spell Damage - Shows how much damage each spell did total. (they log the spells really annoyingly - this doesn't work real well)
  • Damage Shields - Shows how much damage shields did.

Colorization related Information

  • Chat - Shows and colors the chat messages.
  • Emotes - Shows and colors the Emotes (not all, just some).
  • Unparsed - Shows and colors the Unparsed lines in the file.
  • Other Stuff - Shows and colors some random 'ignored' lines.
  • Stuff - Shows and colors the majority of lines (combat, spells, etc.).
