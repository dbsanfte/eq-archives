These are the base rules that are in effect under normal circumstances.


Rules about rules:

  • Classes have the right to modify these rules or to use other rules.
  • Any rules used besides these MUST be publicly stated in a clear and understanable manner.
  • An officer must hear the rules and understand them.
  • These rules must be presented to EVERY member of the class in the plane.
  • EVERY member must agree to accept them.
  • NO one is allowed to sit silent, if someone doesn't say explictly that they agree, the rules can not be adopted.
  • Rules may be posted to the private boards, and agreement reached there. The same rule of everyone affected must agree still applies.
  • Agreeing to rules changes, or anything else, means that you AGREE. If you do NOT agree, or think that you will regret agreeing tomorrow, then you should NOT say you agree.
  • In the case where something happens that is NOT covered by the rules, the people affected may attempt to come to a unanimous agreement on how to proceed. If they can not, it is an officers right and responsibility to handle the situation.
  • A raid organized may post special rules for the raid on the board at the time of the announcement. These rules do not require the acceptance of the people affected.

General rules:

  • Invites to a raid MUST be cleared with an officer or the raid organizer. The officer or organizer has the right to not allow the person on the raid, or to allow them with restrictions on looting.
  • Anyone INVITED to a raid, or any guild member ALLOWED on a raid is elligble for loot consideration. The only way to change this is for either an officer to STATE that a raid is for a certain level or above for loot, or that invited people may not roll for loot, or for an agreement among ALL people affected. If the raid is a posted raid on the boards, any such modifications to loot elegibilty must be posted there. If it is posted, whoever is running the raid has the right to modify the qualifications, of course, everyone else has the right to not go if the penalties are too harsh.
  • An officer has the right to declare that loot shall go to some individual no matter what the class or anyone else wants. This must be stated in public by the officer as a declaration from that officer. It must be done BEFORE the raid or during the very start of the raid and before the loot in question could possbily drop.
  • In the case where a class is voting (or other group) on wether a person should be allowed to roll for an item, that person's vote does not count. This is for the obvious reason that a person voting about themselves is not real good.
  • Any raid won equipment that is replaced by new equipment MUST be handed back to the guild. The old item is to be lottoed among the loosers of the roll.
  • Raid won equipment only has to be turned back over if you are going to USE the new item. If you get the new item because no one else there wants it, or because a general lotto was held because everyone doesn't want it you are free to keep any old equipment as the new piece is not REPLACING anything.
  • Any personally owned and gotten equipment is yours, you do NOT have to hand it over to the guild when you win something new.
  • No longer used raid gotten loot that is handed over to the guild and will NOT be used by anyone there who is qualified should still be handed over. It should go to an officer who then can decide what to do with it. Such loot should not be rolled for to give to twinks or alts (unless the alt is there and will use).
  • All item counters are kept for the duration of any single given raid.
  • Items that you would not USE but get by default in some manner do not count as won items.
  • All item counters carry over to the NEXT raid, but not to the raid after that. If you win arms this raid, and at the end of the raid there are people who have nothing, you will be counted as having an item at the next raid. If during THAT raid there are people who still have nothing, you will still be allowed to roll during the raid after it.
  • No one is logged in to loot something unless it will rot or unless a previous agreement among everyone affected says they should be logged in. This means that people under 55 will loot BPs if there are no 55+ in the zone to loot them.
  • A primary character is defined as the character (or character collection for those who play multiple accounts at the same time) that is played the most frequently and constantly. A primary is the character you hunt with in XP groups, it is the character that you raid with more than 70% of the time, it is the character that you are, at this time, know to be. If two or more characters are played more or less the same amount, ie. which is played when is pretty much on the whim of the player, then one character must be designated as the primary for the purpose of raids. That designation may not be changed unless playing habits change (takes time, you notice you play one over another, you declare one to be the primary and then demonstrate it over weeks of playtime) or until after the current primay has, in some way, signifigantly contributed in places where the alt would stand to gain the most. This rule, then, forces you to help the guild as a whole before you can help yourself.
  • A primary character always takes precedence over an alt. An alt may not roll for anything unless all other rollers are also alts, the lotto is open, or unless the people affected have all agreed to allow it.
  • If an alt is being because it is needed, and the primary is camped out in or close to the zone, then the alt rolls for the primary as though the primary is being played. The alt may not, no matter what, roll for itself as a primary.

Rules about items:

  • All items may be rolled for by anyone qualified to do so by these rules.
  • BPs are level 55+ only, with the exception of Stonewood Mail. (PoG/Kunark only)
  • Legs are level 53+ only. (PoG/Kunark only)
  • If you have won more items from the plane than someone else in THIS trip, you may not roll.
  • BPs are rolled for with no limits on them, you MAY win Boots, Legs and BP, or Helm, BP, and Legs all back to back.
  • Legs (Pog/Kunark only) are rolled for limited only by BPs. You may roll for Legs no matter what, as long as you have not won a BP. A BP counts as a won item when rolling for legs.
  • BPs and Legs DO count as won items when rolling for normal items. In fact, BPs and Legs count as TWO items for normal item rolls. If you win Legs, it is as though you had won gloves and arms. (PoG/Kunark only for Legs, BP is for all)
  • If you are not going to USE an item, ie. put it on and pretty much not take it off except perhaps for resist gear, you should NOT be rolling for it.
  • A class may agree beforehand that certain pieces are 'trash' pieces and are NOT to be counted for rolling on normal pieces. This must be publicly declared and agreed to by everyone involved.
  • If a person wins a PoG BP of Legs and owns Kunark BP or Legs (whichever they won) they must relotto the BP or Legs. This is because BPs and Legs are raid only pieces and can not be fully and truely owned by the wearer. All other pieces of Kunark armour are fully owned and may be dealt with as the winner desires.
  • A person owning PoG armour may not roll for that piece of Kunark armour. Since the PoG armour is no drop you would have to keep both pieces. This rule exists for the same reason as the one going the other way.
  • Quest pieces for epic items that have not already been assigned during the organization of the raid are lotto'd among guild members. Invites may NOT lotto for epic item quest pieces unless no members of the class are there and an officer says they may, or unless agreement was reached prior to the raid.
  • Spells do not count against armour, nor armour against spells.
  • Spells are lotto'd among every there that can scribe the spell. If no one there can scribe it, it is lotto'd among those people within 1 level of being able to scribe the spell (ie. in the level before the spell's level). If no one there is within 1 level, the spell is held by an officer to be given to someone in the guild that can scribe the spell.

Detailed rules about rolling:

  • All rolls will be from 1 to 1000.
  • You may not sit out of any roll. If an item drops you are eligible to roll for, you must roll. Unless you have previously stated that you do NOT want that item, as in would not USE the item.
  • Declaring you would not use an item to avoid rolling for it is a set declaration. You will not be allowed to change your mind later, nor will you be allowed to give your roll for that item to someone else


Examples:

There are 5 clerics in PoG. They are operating under the default rules. A BP drops. 3 clerics are 55+. Cleric A, B and C. Cleric A has won legs today, B got a helm, C hasn't gotten anything. Cleric B has a Hate BP. Cleric A has been to hate once, C 4 times.

  • The BP is announced.
  • Cleric A speaks up and declares that he is rolling for cleric C.
  • All clerics roll from 1 to 1000.
  • Cleric A rolls 654, B rolls 798 and C rolls 345.
  • Cleric B loots the BP.


There are 3 rogues in PoG. At the start of the raid they stated and all publicly agreed in the presence of an officer, that rogue Y would loot the first BP, and that anyone could roll on legs.

  • A rogue BP is announced.
  • Rogue Y loots.
  • A rouge legs is announced.
  • Rogue Y can not roll because he won the BP.
  • All rogues roll 1 to 1000.
  • Rogue X rolls 5.
  • Rogue Z rolls 987.
  • Rogue Z loots the legs.

a BP is announced.

  • All warriors except C (too small) roll.

  • E loots.

  • E may not roll, he has won 3 items (BP for 2 and boots).
