Requirements for entry into the guild:

Entrance to DOD takes four weeks, minimum, with the final officer decision, and family being the only exceptions to the
below policy. Potential candidates must find a sponsor before the first of the month. DoD will only review 5 pledges at a time,
so you may have to delay a month.

To be considered to be a pledge you must be:
1) At least level 30.
2) Not a member of any guild.
3) Must be known as a respectable person by members of DoD.
4) Needs to be fair and equitable in dealings with others.
5) Must be willing to put guild interests in front of their own to some extent.
6) The membership officer and New Dealer of Death Board must receive notification of a potential pledge from the sponsor via
private board message no earlier than noon PST on the 20th of the month.

The pledge with the MOST positive responses on the board by the 1st will be pledge-tagged. Pledging the month before does
not help anyone's chances, but they should have an edge if they played with us a lot. It pays for this pledge to meet and
greet as many members as possible prior to an official pledging. The idea is to get people in the guild that is known by more
than just a clump of friends. On the 27th the pledge board will be cleared except for those 5 people. NO new pledging
requests are to be posted until the 20th

The membership officer will not keep a wait list, if we already have 5 pledges then anyone else will have to wait until the next
month. The pledge will participate with the guild in large and small group activities over three weeks, during the fourth week
the voting will take place. It is the sponsor's responsibility to notify them of events, as they will not have access to the
boards. During the first three weeks the pledge should write a biography detailing their play style, what they enjoy in the
game, short and long term goals for their character and other information and post it to the public DoD board so that we can
all get to know them. The membership officer will schedule one large guild event each month where level does not matter
that much so that we as a guild can have some fun, and get to know the pledges (i.e. the arena.)

A pledge needs to meet four out of the five criteria listed below to be accepted to the guild. The only exception is immediate
family (spouses, parents, and children) and they should preferably live in your household, who get automatic acceptance if
agreed upon by the officers.

1) They must be at least level 30.
2) They must have played with DoD members a minimum of 5 times over the three weeks. (It is the pledge's job to keep
track of this information.)
3) They must have grouped with at least five different DoD members (again, the pledges responsibility to track.)
4) They must participate in one large guild event, i.e. an arena event, large raid, or event where 12 or more members
continually participate.
5) If a pledge does not receive 100% yes votes, then the officers will make the final decision.

It is the sponsor's responsibility to make pledges aware of our guild dynamics. That they may get a little frustrated when the
guild does things with higher level characters when on big raids that don't include them; but also that they are welcome to
communicate with older members to learn about playing their class, or strategies of playing in certain areas of Norrath.
There will be a "form letter" with the basic expectations drafted by Samita - they should be directed to read it

The officers have the right to final Veto of all invites - this should happen rarely and must be agreed upon by the majority of
the officers (discussion on the officer board or in game)

The Pledge must create an account on solusekro.org and create and maintain a character on www.dealersofdeath.com

Finally - The officers do maintain the right to speed specific people past the process in the case of obvious recruits. This will
no longer be a single officer ninja-invite system. When an officer considers someone an obvious choice for membership they
will post and discuss this on the officer board - or in game with no less than half the officers at which point they are eligible
to be tagged if there is agreement - NOT just acceptance, abstention is not allowed
