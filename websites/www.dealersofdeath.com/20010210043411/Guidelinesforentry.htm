Requirements for entry into the guild:

Candidates must be at least level 20 or greater. The level limit does not apply to alternates of the primary character. Candidates must group with current members (not less than 3) for at least 6-12hours before being considered for entry. (For information on who is in your level range please consult the Guild Roster page.) The sponsor then 'sponsors' them first, and then the guild will vote on them through different channels. If the candidate passes the 'Vote' then the candidate can then be officially 'Tested' as tradition dictates and added to the guild. At which time the Candidate becomes an 'Initiate'. The Vote will work on a point system. After the candidate passes the Vote he/she/it will be tested for entry. Sponsors will assume responsibility for the actions of their Candidate and risk same punishment/reward.

Initiate Period:

The Candidate will have their info added to www.dealersofdeath.com and all of their info upon penalty of pain. They will also be given access to the Solusek Ro private board (requires Solro login info to be given to Kanis.) At this time they would be required to introduce themselves, or the perhaps the Recruiting Officer would post a short introduction they can reply to. This will let the guild know that the new person was added and who they are and maybe what guilds they've been in before. Lots of comments that we're growing, not lots of comments that people know whom the new guild members are.

The Initiate will also be added to the guild. This would be a probationary period during which they can see if they like the guild, and vice versa. The understanding being that either side can and will terminate the relationship if things aren't working out. At any time during the Initiate period (2 weeks) The Recruiting Officer can take private comments about the person. If they receive a certain number of negative comments, at the end of the two weeks, that person is booted. (Or sooner.) General comments will count only once per person, but more than 1 specific problem can count multiple times.


The Vote:

People who are Pledging get their names posted to the private board for open comments, the post to be deleted when they are made full members. The Recruiting Officer also will take Private Comments from people who don't wish to be known.

The Point System:

Each positive and negative vote is taken into consideration. Positive votes count as 1, Negative Votes Count as -2 for a candidate to become guilded his end score needs to be positive 5. The exception to this rule is this, if more than 3 officers give a negative vote regardless of final score said candidate would not be guilded.

The Test:

Requires the current Guild Recruiting Officer to administer. (See Guild Roster for further information on who this is). The candidate will be given 3 questions chosen by the Recruiting officer, these questions can be solely from the RO or queried upon by the current online members. In the case of 1 answer not being acceptable a 4th question may be asked. Questions can and will be of serious/game/guild/silly/goofy nature.


Requirements for becoming an Officer:

Must be level 35+ and have the recommendation of the Majority of officers and the current guild leader. Must be on relatively often and regularly. Must be willing to travel for guild matters. Must be willing to organize/assist on camps, CR's, raids, etc. Should be knowledgeable in both guild and game matters. Must monitor posts on the solro.org board and the private board (not just DoD but other guild/event boards as well, for the purposes of planning and avoiding conflicts with other guilds or to join in with other guilds for events.) Other responsibilities will include the gathering of web data for new members they add to the guild and the update of their current web information.
