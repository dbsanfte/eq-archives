Favorite Sites
TNZ (The Newbie Zone) This is the Newbie zone created by Ashara on EZBoards. As listed in the Newbie FAQ in the Shadows of Luclin Strategy guide!
Allakhazam's The best all purpose Everquest web site out there. I use this almost exclusively to check item stats, and to research quests.
EQ Traders EQ Traders is the best Trade Skills web site on the planet! Be the tradeskills, Know the tradeskills, Live the tradeskills.
EQ Prices Great site for item searching, and general prices. Keep in mind, many people will post bogus prices here trying to raise the value of items. Take any price you see with a grain of salt.

Seibwen Dekan A wonderful guild based on the Luclin server. It was created, and dedicated to one of the greatest posters TNZ ever knew. As a founding member, and still maintain a small presence there.
EQ Atlas Now where would you be in Everquest, if you didn't have a great map!
Everquest Official Message Boards These are Sony's official boards, and the home of the official newbie zone. Unfortunately, it's not what it used to be, but you can still glean some good information out of it, if you have the patience to wade through the 50,000 or so posts there...
