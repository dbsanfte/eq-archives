Balkin Ironfist
Balkin was born in Kaladim on Feb 8th, 2000.

He started as others of his race and class start. With a training sword, and a note to his guildmaster, stating he was ready to go out into the world.

So, he met the master, and handed him the note. The grizzled old veteran read it over, then took a thick cloth tunic from a chest behind him.

"Here, wear this. Tis nae but a guild tunic, but twill offer ye some small protection. Now, ye've received yer training. Get out there and server with honor!"

Well, everyone else seems to be doing it!
