FREEPORT FACTIONS
Alright, the purpose of this page is to help people build up their freeport factions. Be it for trade skills (IE merchant faction) or to get NON-KOS with the Freeportmilitia, I leave it up to you.

Both of these quests have the added benefit of raising your CorruptGuardsofQeynos faction as well. Check em out!


The Inert Potion Quest
Freeport Militia Faction repair via THE INERT POTION QUEST

(((EDITORS NOTE!!! I cannibalized three different write up's, cut and pasted some from Allakhazam's, and consolidated them all to produce this. Most of the research and words here are NOT mine. I want to give credit to the proper authors, Gallux Damazar, 55 Human Knight, Midnight Sojourn, Allakhazam's, and a few others whom I do not know. I verified each step of this personally; (01/28/02-01/29/02) so I can guarantee it works.)))

Let me start by saying that to repair Freeport Militia faction is extremely difficult. Be prepared to spend 6 to 12 hours of mindless clicking to get NON-KOS (similar to any trade skill). Now, odds are good, unless you're an Erudite Enchanter, your faction is far to low with Toresian (Enchanters guild leader in Erudin) to receive the Inert Potion quest. We must start you at the beginning.

NOTE: You MUST have at LEAST Amiable faction with Toresian to get this quest.

1) Raise faction with Toresian Fhabel the Enchanter Guild master in Erudin.

a) Kill Elial Brook

Elial is the Heretic at the dock in Tox Forest. He's on a six minute timer, is an approximate level 7 Necromancer w/pet and has less than 110 Hit points when buffed. Since you're killing him anyway, save the "Bones" he drops to complete the Heretic Battle Quest.

NOTE: Pull him from the NORTH side of the dock, as there is a Shadow Knight who was RED to my 46 Warrior on the South side of the dock. I don't think they're on the same faction, but why take chances that he will assist Elial right?

Faction hits for killing Elial Brook
+ Craft Keepers
+ Crimson Hands
+ Deepwater Knights
+ Gate Callers
- Heretics

b) The Heretic Battle quest

Markus Jaevins, the Magician Guild master in Erudin, will ask you to get the heretic's remains from Jail master Lius. Lius will tell you that the heretic escaped. The heretics are the various Necromancers wandering around Tox Forest. It may be that any of the heretics will sometimes drop the bones that he is looking for, but Elial Brook at the dock in Toxxulia Forest always does. Kill him and return his remains for experience, faction and some coin.

Faction Hits for the Heretic Battle Quest
+ Gate Callers
+ High Counsel of Erudin
+ High Guard of Erudin
- Heretics

Repeat these steps, until Toresian Fhabel responds to the Quest phrase "I wish to assist Toresian" by giving you a potion. (This will be Amiable faction or higher)

NOTE: If Heretic faction is important for your character, you can wait till you're done with all this Freeport and Erudin factioning, and go to the Warrens for a night or two to kill Kobolds. Up to you.

2) The Inert Potion quest

a) On the third floor of the castle, Toresian Fhabel is waiting for someone to take an inert potion down to Vials of vitality, on the first floor of the castle, and give it to Slansin. There are two "Slansin's" you can give the potion to. Some times the "Real" one who looks like a standard erudite merchant 'spawns' in the shop. If you hand the bottle to him, you get some faction adjustments, then he de-spawns. However, usually if you run this quest, a Leather armor wearing Erudite, also named Slansin will spawn instead. If you hand the bottle to the leather wearing Slansin you get the Freeport faction you're looking for and he does NOT de-spawn.

Faction Hits for the REAL Slansin (Looks like a standard merchant)
+ Craft Keepers
+ Heretics
- High Counsel of Erudin
- High Guard of Erudin

Faction Hits for the Leather wearing Slansin (WOOT!) This is the one you want
+ CoalitionofTradeFolkIll
+ CoalitionofTradefolk
+ CarsonMcCabe
+ CorruptQeynosGuards
+ TheFreeportMilitia

Killing Sir Lucan in Freeport is at LEAST a -1000 hit to TheFreeportMilitia faction. IE one faction hit for the live version, and again for the dead will bottom most people out to "Your faction with the Freeport militia cannot possibly get any worse".

You really are going to have to do this quest a LOT of times. We're talking 1500 times, or more. If you try to run from the third floor to the ground floor and back to complete this quest it's going to take you at best 40 seconds. I did time it, and that's just too much.

STOP HERE FOR A MINUTE! I strongly recommend you make this hotkey BEFORE you do the following steps. You may be able to do reduce your pause, depending on your lag.

/target Toresian
/Pause 10
/say I wish to assist Toresian

If you are next to Toresian when you hit this key, it should result in the quest text from Toresian, and a potion on your cursor.

Editors note: To speed up this quest, move the quest NPC's right next to each other. Considering Toresian shows he's red. Leave him alone. Slansin however, is green (Level 10 or so). If you are a class that can root, give Slansin a light smack and then run upstairs past Toresian, and into the Gate caller guild. Make sure Slansin is following, then run all the way to the top of the Gate caller guild. Root him, run down a couple floors out of spell range and camp to clear agro. Alternately, you can have a monk friend pull him and FD next to Toresian and quit out to avoid the camping routine. If a monk pulls for you, when he FD's next to Toresian, hail Slansin so he doesn't wander off. If you root at the top of the gate callers like I did, then when you log back in, you can wait by Toresian for Slansin to come back down. He will path RIGHT next to Toresian, where you can hail him and start questing in earnest. Either way, when all is said and done Slansin will be standing next to Toresian. We've suddenly reduced this quest from 40 seconds down to around 4 seconds!

Now, here's where carpal tunnel takes over. Stand right between Toresian and Slansin, facing Slansin. Click your hotkey. You should now have a potion on your curser. If not, your quest text probably popped up before the target request did. Just click again. That's why the pause is in the hotkey, to combat this. When you get a potion, drop it on Slansin and trade. (WOOT! Faction!) Since Slansin is targeted now, take this opportunity to "Hail" him as well so he doesn't wander away. Rinse, lather, repeat ad nausium.

It's up to you how long you do this step. It depends on whether your faction is bottomed out with the Militia, or not. I started just barely amiable to the FreeportMilitia, and after killing just the live version of Sir Lucan I was scowls, but I was NOT at the very bottom. I recommend a minimum of 1500 if you are scowls, possibly going for a full 2000 if you are the very bottom. When you're done, its time to take a run to Freeport and consider a guard. With luck you should be dubious, and can safely travel Freeport again.

The overall side effects of this questing faction wise are:

+ CoalitionofTradeFolkIll (Freeport)
+ CoalitionofTradefolk (Freeport)
+ Carson McCabe (Freeport)
+ CorruptQeynosGuards (Qeynos)
+ TheFreeportMilitia (Freeport)
+ Craft Keepers (Erudin)
+ Crimson Hands (Erudin)
+ Deepwater Knights (Erudin)
+ Gate Callers (Erudin)
+ High Counsel of Erudin (Erudin)
+ High Guard of Erudin (Erudin)
- Heretics (Paineel) ((+ Heretics, if you repair it with Kobolds in the Warrens))

These are normally factions that are difficult to raise, which is the best part if you ask me. From a roleplay perspective there is nothing wrong with being non KOS with Freeport Militia. You might hate them but they don't mind you. I can live with that. And every Merchant in Freeport should be at Maximum faction Ally now!

I take no responsibility for any arm or hand injuries! This quest is really going to require lots of clicking and mouse moving. I recommend you take a break every hour or so.

The Janam and Rebby Quest
Freeport Merchant Faction via THE JANAM AND REBBY QUEST

Alright, you'll notice this raises the same factions that the Inert potion quest does, HOWEVER, the faction hits for this quest are larger than the Inert potion quest. The MAJOR difference between the two, is that this one takes significantly longer to complete. The plus side is, you don't have to run all the way to Erudin to do it.

If you are KOS in Freeport after a Lucan raid, well, this quest is impossible for you without casting a LOT of invisibility of some sort. SO!!! I'd say, for the most part, this quest is for those who are NON KOS, wishing to raise their freeport merchant faction.

I DID NOT WRITE THIS!!! It was taken from the safehouse a LONG time ago, and I don't remember the author. However, I've personally verified the walk though, so have fun!

*******************************************************************

I'll do this from a first person point-of-view, so I'll tell you which ways to turn, and things like that. Make sure you write this down, or at least print it, because you may have to read the directions a few times. Freeport can be a very confusing place to find your way through.

------------------------

There are many ways you can enter East Freeport from West Freeport. One of the ways you enter it is from the Theatre of Tranquil. If you entered that from the Theatre of Tranquil, this is how you get to the Rogues' guild:

- Turn right after you zone, and then you'll see a very large "room", with a ramp directly ahead of you. Head down that ramp and then you'll have to turn a little bit more to your right. When you come out of that "room", you will see the Priest of Discord, and many many other guards. From that point, go to the Port. You can see a sign next to the wall by the Priest of Discord that says, "Port <---" - So, go and turn into that port.

- When you get into that spot, you'll see a big building to your left, and then ahead of you are some docks. Turn to your left and you'll see a door. Enter that door. On the other side of that building you'll have to exit that door to get onto the docks. From there (where you can see a bar straight ahead of you), approach the bar, but do not go inside the bar. From looking at the bar door, turn left and you'll see a staircase. Go down that staircase, and you'll see a fisherman. Turn to your right and go straight ahead until you hit the rocks, then turn right again. You'll see a door. Go inside that door.

((PERSONAL NOTE!!! You can just jump off the dock instead of going through the building, and swim directly over to the staircase mentioned above. Gets your swimming skill up, and ultimately saves time.))

- Inside here, you'll just see a room with a bunch of cargo. Jump off the side, or go down the stairs, it's your choice. When you get to the bottom floor, underneath the porch, you'll see a bunch of cargo stuff against the wall. You should be able to go BEHIND that wall where there is large stacks of cargo. (It's an illusionary wall). When you do that, you'll see another staircase to your right. Go down that staircase and open the door.

- Inside that door you'll see a room with a ramp to your left, and another door to your right. Open the door to your right, and you'll find a Dark Elf, "Nestral T`Gaza" (Should be at least Dubious to everyone) - Ask Nestral "Where is Janam and Rebby?". She will hand you a note. Hold on now, you're not finished yet.

- Go out of Nestral's "bedroom" and then down the ramp ahead of you and a little to the right. When you go down there, you'll see a Dwarf named "Harkin Duskfoot" - Ask Harkin, "What message?" and he'll give you another note. (Again, everyone should be at least Dubious)

- Go back up the ramp, and then back up the door to that cargo-room, and back up the staircase, and back out the door onto the docks. Go back up the staircase that you came down to get to the docks, and back through that windy building on the other side of the docks.

- When you exit that building, turn left and go straight. You will then again, see the Priest of Discord. Turn to your right, and back up "the ramp" - Go all the way to the end of that "room" and then turn left, and then you will zone.
LOADING, PLEASE WAIT...

- Now, go straight ahead, and you'll see the Theatre of Tranquil. Inside that little area, you'll see a tent, with two peculiar looking fellows named Janam Reshka, and Rebby Withend (I think that's their last names).

((BIG PERSONAL NOTE!!! If you are a class able to GATE, bind right in front of Janam and Rebby, and then after going to the rogues guild, you can just gate back. BIG time saver))

-Give the note that is called "Note for Janam" to Janam, and "Note to Rebby" to Rebby. In return, Janam will give you another note to give back to Harkin. In return, Rebby will give you "Rebby's Rat Whiskars".

- Now, the long trek again. Turn around, and go back the exact way that you got here.

LOADING, PLEASE WAIT...

Proceed back to the first step under the first section, until you get to Nestral T`Gaza.

- Give the Rat Whiskars to Nestral. In return, she'll give you these factions:

+CorruptQeynosGuard
+CarsonMcCabe
+FreeportMilitia
+ColitionOfTradefolk (Merchants)
+ColitionOfTradefolkIII (Rogues' Guild and "Ill"egal Merchants).

You can then ask Nestral "where are Janam and Rebby?" again, and she'll give you the note again.

-Go out of her room, and go back down the ramp. Give the "Note to Harkin" to Harkin Duskfoot (the Dwarf) and he'll in return, give you some money (like 4 copper), and these faction raises:

+CorruptQeynosGuard
+CarsonMcCabe
+FreeportMilitia
+ColitionOfTradefolk (Merchants)
+ColitionOfTradefolkIII (Rogues' Guild and "Ill"egal Merchants)

Don't forget to ask Harkin "What message?" again, so you have another note to deliver.

-----------------------------

Just do this quest over and over and over and over again if you wish to. In about 12 times (not each person, but twelve total (Nestral+Harkin), you should have an apprehensive con with the merchants. You only need apprehensive and above to purchase from the merchants.


North Freeport Guards
North Freeport Guard faction via ORC GENOCIDE

Alright, this one is the easiest of ALL freeport factions to raise.

Got to East and West Commons, and Kill Orcs.

That's it.

Seriously!

Ok, Ok.

It is that easy, but if you're higher level, do the youngsters that are hunting there a favor. Don't take over the camp, that's lame. Stand by, punch, or kick the orcs that spawn. Taunt them so the youngsters take less damage, or heal the youngsters during their fights, or buff them during fights, whatever it takes, just don't KS them. Stealing kills from under 10th level characters is pathetic.

HOWEVER!!! You're really not there to power level lowbies. They accept what aid you provide, or they can shove off. If you find a camp unoccupied by youngsters, then by all means, commit orc genocide. *laughs* but don't prevent youngsters from hunting there if they are able.

You get some good North Freeport guard faction, and can turn in the deathfist slashed belts for faction too.

NOTE: IF you are concerned about your Freeport Militia faction (as most who read this write up are) then you don't want to turn in the belts. I'd suggest you auction them for 1-2PP each. Odds are good you'll find buyers.

The GOOD version of the Deathfist slashed belts is as follows.

Cain Darkmoor, who is located in the banquet room on the 2nd floor of the warrior guildhall. He wants two belts at a time. When you give him two belts, you gain faction with the warriors and paladins, and you lose it with the militia. You also get 2 - 10 gp and some decent xp. Also, when you give Cain belts, he'll thank and ask you for help with his "bigger problem." Asking about this sends you to the paladin guild where you learn about the Corrupt Guards quest.

Faction adjustments for Deathfist belts turnin's are

+ QeynosGuards
+ KnightsofTruth
+ SteelWarriors
- CorruptQeynosGuards
- FreeportMilitia

Be carefull! if your Freeport Militia faction is in bad shape, this can push you over the edge. It would suck to find yourself KOS when you come out of the warriors guild eh?
