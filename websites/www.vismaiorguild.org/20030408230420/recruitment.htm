So you want to be a member if Vis Maior? Fill out this application and our Recruiting officers will be in touch with you. Following is a list of MINIMUM requirements to be a Vis Maior recruit. Please read them carefully before submitting an application.
  • A STABLE Internet connection (Cable/DSL prefered)
  • A STABLE computer capable of handling 50+ people in cramped areas.
  • Playtime of 4 out of 7 days a week with playtime being equal to 4+ hours during peak times.
  • You should have the ability to raid during EST raid times (approximate ontime is normally 6pm EST).
  • The understanding that we are a RAIDING guild ... if you are online (as a recruit OR     member) you are REQUIRED to attend all raids.
  • Must have Emporer Key for Ssra.
  • Must have all shards for VT key. (Orb is a huge bonus)
  • Must be PoP flagged up to the majority of Tier 3 bosses (i.e. Agnarr, TZ/VZ, MM).
