  6th March 2001 - by Carlos  
 

Southern Armada ventured up to Hate on Monday thanks to the fact that Phoneix Guardians kindly let us do an inbetween raid. SA performed well up there with 4 groups and managed to have only 3 deaths and cleared 80-90% of Hate in under 4 hours. /Clap for SA

We were fairly lucky in the drops that happened with the following people getting items. Ryokosama Halo, boots, bracer, legs. Carlos a second apothic Robe, wand. Trundle Arms and Legs. Darkpitone Boots. Healerman Arms and shield. Alltear Helm. Vaniir Axe. Ellyrion BP epic piece. Servalan Rapier. Arradien Legs. and Creac finally got a whip.

We will be looking at these opportunites in the future as well so we can try and get this happening more often. It appears that we have more chance of getting a full spawn of Hate on these raids then in our scheduled Calendar Raids due to timezone differences :)
