Silkesse

Silkesse of the Tier'Dal nobles is a crafty little devil, never will you meet a more graceful yet deadly opponent.

Her family is one of the most notable families from Tier'Dal History. A family was built on blood and ill gotten gains. As the only daughter of the family the running of the family business fell to her at a young age after her parents passed away.

To survive in Neriak you need to be two things, quick and deadly. Never let the opponent know where you are or where you were. After joining the Thieves guild these skills became second nature to Silk, not only could she take out her opponents quickly but she also did it quietly and gracefully.

At some time in her life Silk became bored of life in the city. As a child she had heard much about far away countries and long lost treasures, so at this time she left for the great unknown to make her own mark on the world and to help redistribute its loot to her own coffers.

You may see her in your travels, she has a quick sword and a strong character...... just make sure your valuables are safe before adventuring with her.
