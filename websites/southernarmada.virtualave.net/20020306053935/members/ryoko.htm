Mistress Ryokosama Devylcaller

Ryokosama was born soon after Velious was discovered, she is a full blood highelf and followed the calling of the Enchantress.

Ryoko can normally be found with her comrades, Trundle, Pooby, Zyxx, Servalan and Raila. Through this group she has become a very seasoned adventurer. No job too big or too dangerous as long as some fun is involved and a pot gold at the end of the rainbow!

She is a proud member of the influential guild called Southern Armada and also a member of their high council.
