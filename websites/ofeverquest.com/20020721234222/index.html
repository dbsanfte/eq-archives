 Site Announcements


June 6, 2002 -- Site status

 There has not been any new site news the last month but I wanted to assure everyone that we continue to add new items and content almost every day. We have received a lot of great feedback from many of you as well as item updates. I cannot stress this enough .. We really need your help getting the word out about our site! We have no plans to add subscriptions to ofEverQuest so be sure to tell everyone you know about us so we can continue to add more content and features!
June 6, 2002 -- Focus effects on items

 As most of you know already, Verant added focus effects to many existing items in the last patch. However, they have already removed some of them and altered the effect levels on other items. Due to this, we are going to hold off at least for a week or two before we begin updating our databases with the item focus effects. A lot of players lost a lot of platinum over the ongoing adjustments so we fell it is better to wait until Verant gets through with most of the adjustments.
May 6, 2002 -- Status of our spell database

 Our spell database will go online within a day or two. We are working on a web-based SPDAT.EFF (EverQuest spell database file) parser that will allow us to keep our spell database always up-to-date with the latest EverQuest spell changes.
May 6, 2002 -- We need your help!

 Since we are relaunching our site, we need your help to spread the word to everyone you know. We have no plans to add subscriptions so all EverQuest users will have access to all areas of our site and databases. Our goal is to simply become the largest and fastest EverQuest database on the Internet.
