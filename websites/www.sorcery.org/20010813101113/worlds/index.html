NeverWinter Nights
Type: Distributed Online RPG
Release Date: Summer 2001
Developer: Bioware (www.neverwinternights.com)
Forums: Developer Board, Planet Neverwinter, Neverwinter Vault

Description: NWN is different from the recent wave of online games released lately. Instead of a central commercial server, NWN will be supported by a network of free player run servers using software packaged with the game. NWN is based on AD&D 3rd Edition and set in the Forgotten Realms.

Anarchy Online
Type: Online Persistant World RPG
Beta Date: Summer 2000
Developer: Funcom (www.anarchy-online.com)
Forums: Game Board, Crossroads of Rubi-Ka, Dr Twister's AO site

Description: AO is a new online world rumored to enter beta some time in March. It is set in the distant future. (the very distant future) Unlike previous games of this genre AO boasts of a dynamic wolrd with an evolving plot centered around the struggle between a small band of rebels and the evil corporation (cough: Galatic Empire). Players may choose their team and change later in the game. They plan to run the plot for 2 years with a final resolution.
