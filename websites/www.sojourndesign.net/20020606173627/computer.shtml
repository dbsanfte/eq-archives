Is your computer so old, that it's not worth upgrading and you want to purchase a new one? Let Sojourn Design build it for you. Most major PC manufacturers use proprietary software and hardware that forces you to buy the upgrade parts from them only. Other independent computer builders use cheap (both price and quality) components in order to make a profit. Sojourn Design believes that a non-proprietary computer is the best way to go, and using only reliable components will insure your computer for years to come.

Contrary to popular belief, a decent computer system will last one and a half to two years before being completely obsolete. With a non-proprietary system, you can add components as you need them, without spending too much money. Rest assured, Sojourn Design can build you a reliable computer system that fits within your budget.

The only disadvantage to having a computer custom built is the lack of bundled software. The customer has to purchase the operating system and any programs they wish to run on the machine.

A standard computer system includes CPU, tower case, motherboard, videocard, soundcard, modem, CD-ROM drive, floppy drive, hard drive, mouse, keyboard, speakers and monitor. Prices start at just under $1,000.00. Any additional hardware such as DVD player, scanner, printer, joystick, zip drive and re/writable CD-ROM drives can be installed. Just request one of our technicians to give you a call, and they'll gladly discuss your new system with you.
