We are a close knit group of friends who banded together to hopefully bring up a guild with all the good qualities of our past guilds. USV strives to keep a balance that is hard to obtain. The balance of good vs evil. Friends vs loot. With friendship above all others, we strive to become not the best, not the largest, but the most rewarding and fun guild to be apart of yet. If you wish to join merely for the loot or to better yourself, this is not the guild for you. If you wish to create strong friendships within a guild and venture to new lands to explore uncharted territory, please feel free to get to know us. USV is our last hope, as we are the Doomed...
