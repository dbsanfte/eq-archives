Thursday, December 6th ~ 2:44am

Adreael and Asherea's Adventuring, Inc. brings you amazing new photos of the beautiful moon of Luclin. We started off in Nexus and worked our way through the zones. Sometimes picking up a random person or two for a bit and retrieving our corpses. We laid the smack down on many greenies, things that just looked at us funny and everything that seemed to be spiffy on track.

Click on the thumbnails to see the full picture!

Now, news from Adreael:

If you notice me not on the old world, Velious, or Kunark for the next few weeks, Iam exploring as much of Luclin as I can to figure some new places for us to go as a guild...at least before everything gets set up as a FCFS or Rotation...heh, so far:

Dawnbreak Peaks: OT type mobs, shrooms there can be casters, some HT, and NPCs will assist them, so if you want to pull them stay far away from NPCs. Decent xp up to about 45 or so, then it starts to get kinda weak. Off this zone is Maidens Eye, Sanctus Seru, Griegs End, and the Lair. Note: it looks like there is an in zone dungeon of shrooms.

Maidens Eye: Think planes and there you go. Lots of nasties, had a 4 hour CR here, nuff said.

Sanctus Seru: Light city, and a huge one at that. Tons of NPCs, all light guilds here. shops, etcetera. This zone connects to the Lair, DP, and a desert Ican't recall the name of..I think it's Mons Seru.

Mons Seru: Desert zone, bandit type npcs in camps, haven't killed them yet so no idea what they drop. Looks like the zone goes from the high 20s to the mid thirties. This zone connects to Sanctus Seru and Mons Letalis as well as the Val Shar city Ibelieve. NOTE: named bug spawns in the tunnel to Mons Letalis...she is indifferent and green at 55, she dropped a 25/44 ac5 2 hand pierce for me.

Mons Letalis: Nice zone. Looks like a high 40s low 50s paradise. There is a dungeon in the zone, an old decrepit wizard spire that is H-U-G-E, Undead caster mobs in here that con green to mid blue at 55. We fought a named mob on top of the tower called The Spirit Lord. I am guessing he is 50, not too many hps, but he hits hard and casts, he drops the cloak you see in the news section. There is also a cave of bugs that we didn't explore too much here. Stonegrabbers or whatever will agro you and assist. This zone connects to Mons Seru and The grey and possibly one other.

The Grey: Kiting HEAVEN. Mobs here con blue to a 60. You MUST HAVE an EB item or EB to fight here as there is no air to breath. Tip: Stick to the timeless golems, great exp, low hps, low MR. Shissar npcs here outrun sow, hit hard (130-150) and proc a 50pt DD when they hit. All mobs in this zone assist each other (of the same type). STAY AWAY FROM RUINS. You can't see them right away, but there are about 15 or so undead iksars that will jump you if you get too close.

Griegs End: A 60 necro was sitting at zone and told me, "It's nasty in there, I wouldn't go in if i were you." I listened to him, nuff said.

The Lair: Not the actual name of the zone, but it connects the Nexus to DP and SS as well as one other zone i think. Fungi mobs are in here and drop some armor, not very good, step up from damask it looks like.

~Adreael
