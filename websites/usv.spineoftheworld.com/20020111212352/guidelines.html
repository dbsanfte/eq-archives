Hello from USV. If you are interesting in applying for membership to our guild, please make a post in this forum and give us some information about you as well as answer these questions:

What are your characters names?

What level are your characters?

What is your current guild status? (Are you still in your last guild? Have you applied to any other guilds?)

Did someone refer you to this guild and/or do you have friends in USV?

Why do you wish to be a part of the USV family?

 

After you post your thread, I will move it to the Member's Only forum and put it up for conversation. For now we are recruiting pretty loosely and it shouldn't take more than a week to get you invited unless we have objections to you joining our guild.

If someone objects your application, one of two things can happen. We will put your application status on hold and try to get to know you better by grouping with you. Or someone may use their Mortal Enemy Clause and you will be denied membership.

A Mortal Enemy Clause works like this: If noob_01 wishes to join the guild and member_01 hates this person with such a passion that they would say, "It's me or them... if they get invited, I leave." Member_01 will whip out his handy dandy Mortal Enemy Clause and slap it down on the table. Noob_01 will be denied membership to the guild forever unless member_01 leaves the guild. Guild members get to use this clause once per calendar year, and no, clauses do not build up over the years.

If all goes good with your application, you will be notified and invited into the guild and given status to the Members Only forums. Your application will be deleted from the Members Only forum.

If you have any questions, please contact me at naharet@hotmail.com.

Good luck and hope to see you in USV soon!

Default raid settings:

Raid leaders will use the /shout channel.

Loot will be announced and taken care of in /auc channel.

All other banter and whatnot will be done in /ooc.

 

Raid leaders: The actual raid leader, Loot master, Puller, Main assist, Secondary assist, Main enchanter, Secondary enchanter, Buffers, group leaders. These are the only people that are to use the /shout channel. The /shout channel is reserved for PERTINENT information. Use shout for incoming messages, mez messaged, moving directions, calling assist, group changes, group status, battle orders.

Loot master: The loot master will be using /auction for all loot matters, I would ask that you make sure your auction channel is a color you will notice right away. The only other person or persons to use the /auc channel are group leaders when announcing drops. DO NOT spam in ooc, shout, say, emote, group, or auc if you want to be in on a random for an item, or merit for that matter. We will be using tells, it's a very simple process.

Puller: Simply stated, the puller will use /shout to announce incoming messages, and that is all. Other info will be given to the raid leader Via tells, and the raid leader will keep the raid informed. Anyone sending the puller tells that are not necessary, such as requests to pull certain mobs, or asking if certain mobs are up will be given one warning, if they persist they will be removed from the raid.

Main Assist/Secondary Assist: Main assist will call the assists in shout. MA will also call switched targets in shout. Other communications, such as requests for buffs will be made in ooc or in tells.

Main Enchanter/Secondary Enchanter: Enchanters will use shout to announce mez, charm, mez breaks, and charm breaks.

Buffers/Debuffers: All buffing and debuffing types can use shout to announce for people to send tells for buffs. Shout can be used to announce debuffs as well.

Group leaders: Group leaders will use shout to announce group status. Such as group mana, group members LD, and group members dead. Battle orders will be given in shout, this is pretty cut and dry, pay attention to it and we'll all get by.

Make NO MISTAKES about this, anyone not following procedure will be given one warning, if the problem persists they WILL be removed from the raid. Everything is spelled out here, follow procedure and we'll all do fine.

Merit:

Firstly: GUILD MEMBERS RECEIVE PRECEDENCE ON MERIT ITEMS

Merit for this guild will be determined on 3 factors; Level, Need, Participation. Pretty simple really, the higher your level, the more your need for particular items increase. However it would not be fair to spend all your time leveling to 60 without raiding and expect to get drops based solely on level/need, so participation will be factored in. The number of raids you attend and where you attend raids to will be kept track of. You will also be made aware of your standing merit wise, so there will be no confusion.

Epic pieces will be merited based on level and how close to completion you are to your epic. Participation will be factored in to this as well, but not as heavily. Again, this is fairly simple, raiding helps your merit, so if you want to improve your merit, raid.

Finally, make no mistakes on this: Mains will -ALWAYS- have first crack over twinks. Merit will -not- be carried over from main characters to twinks if said twink is being switched to your main character. Merit is based on the character, not the person playing it.

In the event that a merit item will rot, it -is- acceptable to have a character that is parked close to be logged in to recieve looting rights, and it will not affect your merit on the raid. Again, this is only if an item will rot due to no one in attendance on the raid is wanting/needing the loot.

We also have a "final piece" clause... if a piece of armor drops and it is the last piece you need to complete a set, it will be merited to you. For final quest/armor/epic pieces, you need to make it known BEFORE the raid where your standing is so there is no confusion Merit items will generally be announced when a raid is scheduled.

Typically, BPs and legs are merit items, however, speaking from experience, some leg items are not always worth meriting over other pieces of armor. When a raid is posted on the events board, merit items such as planar armor will be announced. Anything not listed as a merit item, save god, demi god, and epic loot will be done on a full random basis.

Spells will ALWAYS be meritted. If more than one caster is in need of a spell, then a random will be made for it. Spells will also ALWAYS stay in guild unless here is someone out of guild on the raid that needs a spell.

Epic loot will always be done by merit as explained above. Demi god loot will be done on a merit basis. God loot will be done on a merit basis. Quest pieces will be done on the same system epic pieces are done.

We will also be doing a rotating loot system. Once you receive loot due to random -or- merit, you are out on loot until all other raiding players of your class recieves an item. This will carry over on raids as well. This eliminates the "top dog" syndrome. If a merit item drops and you are up for it, you will have the option to pass it off to the next person in line in order to hold out for something else. If this happens, the next person in line is then put at the back of the line again. If a merit item is passed by all members of the raid in the merit rotation, it will go to random. Once an item goes to random, it is available to out of guild as well as in guild.

Winning an item due to random will -not- affect your merit standing overall, however, loot will be done on rotation for random as well. However, it is more a process of elimination. To clarify: on a hate raid there are 4 warriors, a piece of randomed warrior armor drops, the winner of the random is then out on loot for the raid until the other 3 warriors receive loot. The warrior will keep his standing for merit items, but will not be able to random on any more items. IF a player wins a random, AND recieves a merit item, they are out on all loot for the remainder of the raid, unless an item is won by default. Default of course being the item will rot unless looted.

Merit rotation will be carried over from raid to raid. If you are up for merit, and do not attend a raid, you will simply be up for merit on the next raid.

 

Non Merit Items:

Pretty simple really, non merit items will be up for everyone of the appropriate class on the raid to be randomed. The rotating loot policy will affect this as well. If you win, your out until everyone else gets something. The random process is very simple. When an item drops, it's stats will be called as fast as possible and repeated 2 times. The raid leader in charge of loot will then open up for tells, random order will be based on the order of tells received, on no drop items we will be moving fast, loot will be announced in /auc, so I would suggest changing your user color for auc if you are raiding with us. Calls for tells will be made a few times, then the random will be done. It is important that everyone understands this before the raids happen so that loot will be handled quickly and efficiently.

PP items, gems, spells, non no drop pieces that are not needed will go to Geddak, period.
