A Hobbit who's primary goal in life was simply to eat and dream. Until....

One fair sunny morning in the Shire (as is usual for that part of the world)

Young Dim, while dreaming of a way to make coffee more palatable to his tea drinking friends, finds himself put sharply upon his backside.

Looking up, for the cause of his new found distress, he stares upon a big old friendly oak tree. "Not so friendly" thinks Dim to himself, "if only these trees were more careful about where I was going.!!"

As a very young lad Dim remembers the old tales of a Hobbit named Bilbo Baggins.

Bilbo told of a great many adventures keeping Dim and his friends on the edge of their tiny seats. One tale in particular sprang to mind. A tale of trees that could not only talk but move too. "I wonder..." thought Dim, then a new realisation hit Dim square in the back of the head (only the trees heard the echo), and he very quickly and very politely apologised to the oak for walking into him (you never know who that tree might have been) and so decided some adventuring was long over due….
