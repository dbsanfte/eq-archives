Join Mythrilmoon Mystics
Mythrilmoon Mystics, known oftentimes as MM, is a rising guild on 7th Hammer. We hold a strong reputation and are well-regarded in general by members of the server. We are a 50+ guild with a majority of the guild in the 55+ range. We raid regularly on high level targets, are capable of completeing epics, and our members look out for each other. We act with kindness and give our respect to all those that offer the same in return.

If you feel that you would like to become a member of our guild then you have made an excellent choice. Below is a guide to the recruitment process in Mythrilmoon Mystics.

  

A Step-by-Step Walkthrough

I) First of all, check the recruitment status directly below. If you do not meet the requirements listed below, then it is not recommended to apply to Mythrilmoon Mystics. There are some cases in which exceptions will be made but for the most part the listed requirements are firm. If you have any questions regarding recruitment feel free to contact the class leader for your class, as listed on the Roster.

Mythrilmoon Mystics is currently seeking to recruit active weekend players who can consistently attend raids.

II) If you feel you meet the requirements to join our guild, read the charter and see if you agree with the rules that are put forth for members of Mythrilmoon Mystics. If you do not agree with the rules, then do not apply to Mythrilmoon Mystics.

III) Once you have determined that you meet the requirements to join our guild and you have reviewed the guild charter, use the guild message boards to post an application on the recruitment forum. Your application may take as short as one day or as long as a week to be read, processed, and discussed by guild officers, so please remain patient.

VI) You will be contacted once your application is processed, and interviewed. If you feel your application has been missed, it is recommended you contact Gembadar the guild leader. It is also recommended that at this stage in the application process you seek out members of Mythrilmoon Mystics and endeavor to group with some of them, as it will give you a feel for how our guild operates and what kind of individuals are in our guild. You may also sign up for some of our open posted raids in the Raid Schedule forum.

V) If you are found to possess the traits and requirements asked of you then you will be admitted to the guild on a probationary basis. During this probationary period, which is five weeks in length, you will receive reduced consideration for raid drops as compared to a full member, and you will not be given access to the guild spell, gem, or item banks. During this period you will be evaluated and at any point during this probation period you can be /guildremove'd.

VI) When you are finished with the five-week trial, congratulations! You are a full member and have joined the ranks of Mythrilmoon Mystics, a strong proud guild that will carry you far on the 7th Hammer server.
