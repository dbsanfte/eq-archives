Mythrilmoon Mystics Links
Links with utility to members of Mythrilmoon Mystics and other members of the 7th Hammer server.

If you wish to submit a link, please send an email to Eitsekur along with any information you would like to be supplied about the link in question. Also please email me if a link becomes out of date or no longer works.


Links

    Resources
  • Allakhazam's Magical Realm - an excellent resource for quests, item statistics, and featuring regular updates with new items.
  • Lucy - an excellent resource for spell effects, stacking issues, spell changes, and other information
  • Magelo - use this website to create a personal profile of your characters and their equipment, which other can then view.
  • EQAtlas.com - an excellent array of maps created by a very dedicated player. Missing some high end maps but has good quality.
  • EQMaps.com - has a wide selection of player-created maps for a large variety of maps. Use the 'Map Lists by Zone' link to view maps.
  • EQTraders.com - the best site to visit for tradeskill information. At last visit the 'Learn a Tradeskill' links were out of date in respect to the updated tradeskill trivials, so beware of this.
  • Fires of Heaven Guild Website - although this is a guild website (of one of the best guilds in the game if not the best), the forums are an excellent resource for learning about the high end game.
  • Noremac's Jewelcraft Policies - need resist gear? Some fancy earrings? Just want to look glamorous for your next planes raid? Noremac the friendly guild chanter has your back, and this page will be your guide.
    Server Links
  • Seventh Hammer Server Boards - the message boards for the 7th Hammer server, an excellent post to buy and sell items, engage in discussion with fellow servermates, and flame or praise a peer. Not a very good place to post raids (posted raids seem more likely to get jumped)
    Class Links
  • EQ Diva - Bards come here and do bard things.
  • Concert Hall - sing your heart out! Err, that's EQ Karaoke...
  • EQ Beastlord - bestiality at it's finest...err...scratch that!
  • EQ Clerics - if you like puppies, love, and healing people, consider clerical fields of study! If you eat puppies, like to hate, and want to heal monsters, you may still have what it takes!
  • Druids' Grove - Druid forums. Converse with other druids! How fun.
  • EQ Druids - Druid class website. I've never visited but I'm sure that being druids they're a lively bunch...*cough*
  • Enchanters @ Caster's Realm - Caster's Realm forums for enchanters. Closest thing available to an enchanter website I could find.
  • Mage Compendium - aka Modrod Studies 101... j/k
  • Monkly Business - an excellent site for monks and non-monks alike
  • Necro.eqclasses.com - like dead things? Check out the necromancers' website.
  • Paladins of Norrath - website for paladins. Piety, faith, and hitting stuff.
  • Ranger's Glade - treehugging and competitive-class archery at its finest.
  • The Safehouse - The site itself is nothing exceptional, but the messageboards have some value (if you can get past the idiots and lamers that make far too many posts).
  • Shadowknight.org - Rawr! Scary!
  • Shaman's Crucible - I'm out of things to say. If you're a shaman go here.
  • The Steel Warrior - warriors go here and learn to get take bludgeonings to the head.
  • Graffe's Wizard Compilation - excellent resource for wizards.
