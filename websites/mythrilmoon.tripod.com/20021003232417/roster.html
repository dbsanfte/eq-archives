Mythrilmoon Mystics Roster
This roster is a comprehensive listing of the members of the guild, organized by class and name. Included is name, current level, class, race, status in guild, email (if provided), and profile links (if any). Names are arranged alphabetically.

If your name is missing from this list and you would like it to be included, please email me and I will add it. If your information is inaccurate make a post on the 'Roster Information' thread in the 'Mythrilmoon Guild Hall' guild board with the corrections to be made. Please note that you have the option to withhold your email address if you do not want that information made public.

    Position Key
  • CL = class leader - moderates class forum and leads fellow members of his/her class
  • Officer - officer in Mythrilmoon Mystics
  • GL = guild leader - leader of Mythrilmoon Mystics
  • SEC (name) = secondary account main (name of main) - declared and approved main character on secondary account
