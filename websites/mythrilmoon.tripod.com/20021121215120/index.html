The Usual Suspects- 11/17/02
submitted by rukes skyrawker

Another weekend netted another kill of Vindi, another Trak kill, and miscellaneous epic happenings (congratulations to Shokruul for completing his epic, the Claws of the Savage Lord). Vindi went down smoothly (one death, and this time NOT Gembadar as has been the scheme of things in the past, but instead the hapless beastlord Glif who became accidental rampage tank :P ). Kkrasher, dorf goodness-enthusiast (paladin), was the recipient of a Chestplate of Vindication. Gratz on the radical bp, my man.

After Vindi the raid divided into two parts, one of which was dispatched to Grieg's End to get a key for the Servitor of Luclin, while the other fought down to juggs for CoHing raid force to trak. Disguised as a monk named Kessia I went with the juggs group and was witness to carnage as MM tore through Sebilis. Almost non-stop movement, fast kills, and reacting well to some...err...interesting pulls on my part (go go four juggs and change!), mostly because I'm a terrible puller (as many people will attest to). Fortunately I don't usually play a monk or other pulling class so that's not much of a problem. So we got down there, and after a short time (Prast Ianitor was busy scrubbing floors in Grieg's Beachhouse so no Servitor key and thus no kill) the rest of the raid showed up, CoHed down, buffed up, got down, boogied, grooved, shuffled, and finally rushed and killed trak. Ludaa was the recipient of a Donal's Breastplate of Mourning and Daernak received an Elder Spiritist's Chestguard thingy.

After trak there was a short raid in HoT for some more armor pieces, and it was there that DISASTER STRUCK!

Rescue officials were contacted immediately and rescue efforts began to extricate the gnome from the dragon's jaws. As the gnome shrieked and flailed about from inside the beasts jaws a frantic effort began to prize the jaws open and save the gnome. After a 15-hour rescue mission, the gnome was finally set free by the dragon, whose attention was diverted to several halfling rescue workers. Though the diversion worked, four halflings gave their lives to save that brave gnome, who is in stable condition at Ak'anon General Hospital. Zambenz, 7th Hammer resident, gnome lover, and gnome rights activists, has agreed to head up the class action suit against the Temple of Veeshan on behalf of the Royal Brotherhood of Gnomes for discrimination against gnomekind and is reported to be seeking 600k plat and 'phat lewtz' as reparations for damages. More on this story as it develops.

Finally, congratulations to two of our members, Isiiss Qual'valsharess and Nrewfin Givinglife on tying the knot in the Plane of Tranquility on November 15th. : )
