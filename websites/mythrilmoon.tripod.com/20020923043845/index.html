Hate: go fetch a switch, it's time fer a spankin'! - 9/8/02
submitted by Eitsekur

Hate was royally whupped by Mythrilmoon Mystics this weekend. This date marks the first ever god kill from MM as well, which is a huge stepping stone. Starting out with a somewhat rough break-in that was assisted by Canthus the rogue (thanks bro!), MM set up for the 1st floor and cleared it at near-blinding speed. The Hand of Maestro was pulled, resulting in an epic piece for Kodrai. Gratz!

Upstairs there was a slight altercation involving a 20-mob pileup, a summoning drake, and a couple deaths, but MM pressed on and swept through the second floor, quickly and effectively. One of the highlights of the drops that day was a Silverleaf Girdle, which went to Daernak. Huge gratz on this excellent piece of waist-wear. Camdog also received some sort of cool-looking sword, which could be any number of things, but based on my knowledge, I'd think it was a Silver Blade of Rot, part of a quest for a nice sword in Jaggedpine Forest for followers of Karana. Also, I've gathered that Beetlebee was the lucky recipient, at this raid or a previous one, of a very nice robe, the Rainment of the Chosen. An excellent robe! (and it looks pretty dern cool if you ask me)

As the guild approached Innoruuk's place, things slowed down a bit because of problems with positioning and entry, and things got a bit ugly when Innoruuk aggroed, but finally everything clicked. Slow landed, Gembadar tanked like nobody's business, and everyone was stayin' alive. Innoruuk died and left behind sub-par loot and a great battle to remember.

Not a pretty fellow by any means. Here's a selection of screenshots from the Hate raid in question:
Innoruuk being plundered of his ph@t l3wtz Music to my ears (eyes?). Gembadar pulls an ashenbone drake Innoruuk in throes of death

After Innoruuk most logged, but a brave few stayed behind to take on the Maestro of Rancor. Evidentally Sanon had a lot of fun here... ^ _ ^ The Maestro made his last performance before bowing to MM.

As if that weren't enough, a few members of MM headed to Nurga to wipe out Qinn's second-to-last epic encounter. All that's left now is for Qinn to head to LOIO and he'll have his Celestial Fists.

More notable news: I would like to welcome Kkrasher, Ludaa, and Slivertoes as the newest members of our guild. I'd also like to congratulate Noremac on creating a Grandmaster Jeweler's Eyeglass. One of the tradeskill trophies, it represents a massive and expensive undertaking, both in getting his jewelcraft skill to 250 and spending thousands and thousands of plat to make it. The result is well worth it though: 10str 10int 10wis 50cha and usable in primary, secondary, or range slot, plus it summons a 10 slot 100 percent weight reducing pack. Gratz Noremac!

Thanks to Gundail foremost for his help, both with a pair of excellent screenshots, and with a detailed synopsis of the raid. Thanks to both the lovely lady druid Chaniqua and the most masterful enchanter Noremac for some screenshots as well.

However, for everyone that reads this far down: if you're going to send me screenshots, try and send them as soon as you can. I don't especially like having to go back and update the main page three times to add pictures! That said, I do appreciate the submissions and encourage everyone to follow suit. Just do it in a somewhat timely fashion...
