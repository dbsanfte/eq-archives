Bylaws and Regulations of the Concordat Angelis The following are the rules, which we of the Concordat Angelis have chosen to live by.

Role-playing The Concordat Angelis is, first, foremost, and always, a role-playing guild. We have come together to provide a group of like-minded players in the sometimes-hostile Everquest universe. If you are here, you are here to role-play. Though we seek advancement and improvement as all players do, role-playing comes first. Though this is a broad statement, there are several specific items, which deserve mention:

Theme-appropriate character names are required. If your character name violates Verant's naming policy, the character will not be admitted. In this case, /petition a GM, and they will happily change your name once you inform them that you wish to correct the naming violation.

Use of the /roleplay flag is MANDATORY for all members of the Concordat - Role-playing should be practiced at all times face-to-face. Out Of Character comments should be accordingly prefaced whenever possible. Guild chat will be primarily IC, but will not be enforced as such. Since Guild chat is a fairly OOC mechanism, it will be our OOC vent. Our intent behind using this tag is to indicate that we are role-players, not to hide level or location.

As appropriate for a role-playing guild, membership will be on a character basis, not a player basis. If a player wishes to have an alternate character in another guild, we have no issue with that. Likewise, if a player wishes to add an alternate character to the guild they will undergo the same trial period as any other new arrival.

We will not use information gained from occurrence of events on other servers. If it's something that we could have heard within Druzzil Ro, fine. "Let's all go to the druid rings in Lesser Faydark because Firiona Vie just appeared there on Solusek Ro and we're next!" is not acceptable.

In-character conflict between Guild members is allowed, but keep it in character. If someone begins to feel personally offended, they should say so. We're here to role-play, but not at the cost of other people's feelings.

We cannot, and will not, judge role-playing quality. This is not a contest. We can, however, judge the ATTEMPT at role-playing. This WILL be enforced. If a member is shown repeatedly to care little foreven attempting to roleplay, they should move on.

Self-Sufficience The Concordat Angelis exists to improve the peoples of Norrath, both within our ranks and without. This means the following:

Begging will not be tolerated within the Concordat. This is a ZERO TOLERANCE ISSUE. We come together to assist each other, but use restraint. If the assistance you are requesting is for a situation or environment where you could not possibly survive, it is inappropriate. Basic rule of thumb: If you cannot contribute meaningfully to whatever quest or opponent you wish to face, don't ask for help on it.

Equipment and monies stored in the Concordat's treasury shall be distributed based on need first, and in all cases. The same policies, which apply to begging, apply to stored items. They are, however, their to be used, and do little good gathering dust in our vaults. We will attempt to make use of all equipment.

At the same time, begging from outside the Concordat should be frowned upon. Handing someone money or equipment only breeds dependence. Assist them in becoming self-sufficient, and send them on their way.

Admittance Policy All adventurers wishing admittance to the Concordat Angelis will undergo a trial period of observation during which time they will wear the badge of the Concordat, but will not be granted full membership. During this trial period, they will be given missions by members of the Concordat, their behavior and performance judged. When an Officer of the Concordat feels that the prospective member has shown themselves worthy, they will be admitted fully, their name added to our ranks. If the prospective member shows themselves not to have the soul of the Concordat in their heart, we will part ways with no ill will.

Character Admittance The Concordat Angelis stands for the Light in Norrath, in all things. To this end, we welcome all adventurers from the goodly races and professions. Rogues should take care that their thieving skills are left well in their past before joining us, however. The dark races and professions are more problematic, however. Though we acknowledge that one is not limited by their birth, we must also acknowledge nature. Persons of the dark races who wish to apply may do so, and will enter a trial period just as any other. Their testing, however, will be much longer and more arduous. Only after they have well and truly shown themselves turned to the light will they be fully admitted into our ranks. The professions of Necromancer and Shadow Knight are most troubling. It is the judgement of the leadership of the Concordat that the practices associated with these professions are anathema to the Light, and have no place within our ranks. If a Necromancer or Shadow Knight wishes to join us they may… However, they must swear to never again use those of their abilities which are considered unholy - specifically, the raising of corpses into the undead. If they can abide by these rules, then they may join us. If they are ever to be found practicing these arts while within our ranks, however, they will be immediately expelled with extreme prejudice.

Conduct of the Members of the Concordat Angelis The Concordat Angelis stands for the Light and the betterment of all Norrath. To this end, we will strike at evil, assist travelers in need, and spread the Light across the land. We will conduct ourselves in accordance with the laws put down by the goodly kingdoms of the land, be they human, elf, dwarf, or halfling. Though we hope to lead by example, we must never forget that we are servants first - we must conduct ourselves with courtesy and respect for all goodly beings.

Structure The structure and ranks within the Concordat Angelis is simple and straightforward. The Concordat is divided into four Guards, and all members will be assigned to one of these Guards. The rank structure is as follows:

Prelate - Commander of the Concordat Angelis

Legatos - Officer of the Concordat, Commander of a Guard Each Legatos has a specific title depending on the Guard they command:

Veris Legatos - Commander of the Spring Guard

Aestas Legatos - Commander of the Summer Guard

Autumnal Legatos - Commander of the Autumn Guard

Brumalis Legatos - Commander of the Winter Guard

Optio - Second in command of a Guard, and/or a member with additional duties, but not a full Officer

Advocate - Member of the Concordat Angelis

Tyro - Probationary member of the Concordat Angelis

By swearing oath to the Concordat Angelis you agree to be bound by these laws so long as you stand among our ranks. Welcome, warriors of the Light.
