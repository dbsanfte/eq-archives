Joining

All prospective members should review the regulations and history of the Concordat Angelis on this site before applying. This ensures they understand why we are here, and join with full knowledge of the duty we accept.

After review, a character wishing to join should contact an officer in-game to request admission. This should be done In Character, even if it must be through /tell. The Officer will spend some time talking with the potential recruit, and make every attempt to arrange a face-to-face meeting, if possible. At this point there will be further discussion and, if the prospective member still wishes to join, they will be granted the rank of Tyro and the coat of arms of the Concordat. At this time they will also be added to our roster, and assigned to a Guard.

As soon as possible after admission, a Tyro should post a character background to The Source, our message board for such topics. The background should give as much detail as possible about your character, where they come from, and so forth.

The rank of Tyro is a limited one within the Concordat. A Tyro will not have access to funds or equipment stored within the treasury, and will be provided with only limited assistance in any quest or mission they wish to undertake. Though this is flexible, and we will not deny a Tyro our assistance, we will not accept someone joining simply to get extra help with a quest, and then departing once it is complete.

For an indeterminate time, the Tyro will travel with other members of the Concordat, facing challenges and completing missions. During this time all members of the Concordat should be evaluating the Tyro, to see if their goals are the same as ours - OOC this means attention to role-playing, IC it means following the Light and providing service to others. Once it has been satisfied that we share the same purpose, the Legatos of the appropriate Guard will declare the Tyro ready for the rights and duties of the rank of Advocate. This decision can only be made by the Legatos, though they will request and require all possible input from any who journey with the Tyro.

If it should be determined that the Tyro's goals are not the same as the Concordat, then they will be politely informed of such and removed from the guild without prejudice.

At the next scheduled initiation ceremony, the new Advocate shall be formally inducted as a full member of the Concordat Angelis, hailed by all and welcomed to our ranks.
