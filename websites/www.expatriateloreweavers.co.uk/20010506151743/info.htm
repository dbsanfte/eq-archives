About Us

History

EL is a family type guild, at present it is very large, but the family atmosphere remains. EL in it's present form is a result of the merger of EL and Scions of Light, about the time the famed CTC Calendar was breaking up. We had been hunting together alot over the previous months and a merger seemed best for both of us.

Before the merger with Scions, EL was formed from two guilds The Expatriate Brotherhood and Loreweavers. It was a good move for both of us, TEB was mainly euro players with a small amount of US players and Loreweavers had mainly US players. This meant we could both increase our player base but not crowd ourselves.

Today EL members are a wide bunch of players, some casual players, some not, and spread across timezones with a large portion on Euro time and also a good amount on EST, and some on PST who we like to get out of bed at 5 in the morning to raid a plane ;)

As a Guild we have overcome some of the biggest challenges in the game, and we suprise ourselves sometimes with what we can do, even if it did take enough coffins to build a house with ;p

Recruiting

Recruiment Is Closed At This Time
However, here is our recruiment guidelines from the recruitment board

"Thank you for your interest in our guild. Currently we are not recruiting members below level 25 unless they are RL friends or relatives of current members. All recruits must have a current member for a sponsor. Once you have a sponsor, they will arrange for you to attend at least 3 guild raids so we can get to know you better and see how you play your class. Your sponsor will post a note on the message board so that the guild can discuss your application.
If no objections are heard, you will become a member on probation for one month. Your sponsor is responsible for helping you acclimatize during the probation period. Members may sponsor only one recruit per month and new recruits may not sponsor anyone while on probation.

To help us get to know you, please tell us about yourself here. Perhaps these questions can help:
How long have you been playing EQ? What classes have you played?
What is your main character ( and favorite alts, if applicable )? Please include name, level, class, and race.
What timezone are you in? What times do you usually play?
Who are some guild members that you have grouped with in the past?
What skills and/or special areas of knowledge do you have?
What other guild(s) have you been in? Why did you leave?
What are you looking for in a guild?
Thanks and good luck!"
