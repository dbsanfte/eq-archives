Charter for The Sovereign Guard
This document constitutes the charter for the Sovereign Guard, a guild of like-minded people who are found on the Prexus server of the game EverQuest. The Sovereign Guard is dedicated to fair and fun gameplay but mostly to the concept that we will help others whenever we can. Those in need, a newbie who cannot locate his/her corpse, a not so newbie who is having trouble recovering their corpse due to where they lost it, these challenges are taken on gladly and at the expense of our own lives if need be. For surely there is no quest, no gathering of "Exp.", no business we can be involved in that would take precedence over helping someone in his or her time of need. The guardsman/guardswoman knows this and drops everything when they hear a shout for help, finds out what kind of help is needed and then helps if it is appropriate.
Requirements for entrance into the Sovereign Guard:
1. Attain the rank of at least level 15. The reason we require this is that it shows commitment to the character. It also gives you time, if new to the game, to become accustomed to the unwritten etiquette that exists in the everquest world.
2. Sponsorship by at least 5 members and one officer of the Sovereign Guard in good standing. To be sponsored by a guild member that member must have first hand knowledge of you as a group member and the way you conduct yourself in Everquest. Real world knowledge of a person (i.e., real world friends) is also acceptable. As a sponsor they are vouching for your character as a guild member. Please note some guild members may also require you to complete a small quest to obtain their sponsorship.
3. You should not have any outstanding issues with a current member of the guard. These issues should be resolved first by either the person directly working out the problem with the member or an officer mediating between the two.
We do not believe in online forms or email contact for guild entrance. We are a close knit guild so try to get to know us in game by grouping with us or talking with us. If you're having trouble finding us there, post an introduction of yourself on our message board including your level, class, times you play, and character background along with any other things you would like (we enjoy creative backstories). I'm sure many of our members would be more than happy to meet with you for sponsorship.
If you meet all entrance requirements then you are inducted into the guild as a trial member. Your trial period lasts for one month and you are expected to make efforts to group with other guild members around your level and be an active part of the organization. Being a trial member allows us to see your efforts and more of your play style by you being an integral part of the group and having access to guild chat. During your trial period your sponsors will be responsible for observing your actions. At any time during this one-month period your sponsors have the option (should you display unethical behavior or act in a manner they don't see appropriate) to remove their sponsorship of you at which time you will be guild removed by an officer. This is not a personal thing. Remember during this month you are on trial and you also have an opportunity to observe us. Removal by yourself or us isn't a personal thing as this is a time for you to make sure the guard is right for you and you are right for the guard.
Upon completion of the one-month trial period you become an official member of the guard. You are required at this time to register on our message board and post one introduction message if you haven't done so already. Your membership is permanent. Removal from the guard requires a 2/3rd vote of all officers or removal by you.
Removal by you after the trial period is not something to be taken lightly. Self removal is most likely permanent removal. If you should decide to self remove yourself, and it was done intentionally, should you decide to come back please submit a reason for your actions on the guild only board. At this time the officers will evaluate your reasons and it requires a 2/3rd vote of all officers to be readmitted. Again, let me emphasize, guild removal isn't to be taken likely. It isn't something you should do just because you have an issue with the guard or an individual. If you have an issue please tell an officer and post it to the message board. The officers are required to take action and look into the situation. Most likely these situations or issues with another can be corrected and mended.
Thank you for taking an interest in the guard.
