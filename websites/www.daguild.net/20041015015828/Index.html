OMFG, a GoD Update~                           July 02, 2004
Its been a couple months since a content update, and yes we've been working on GoD like everyone else, well at least as much as we can stomach.  To be totally honest, the design and implementation of the expansion certainly has made progress difficult. Rather than try to explain it, let's listen in on some DA guildchat ...........
As much as I absolutely hated GoD when it came out, I'll admit its gotten better, 5 months of free beta testing on Live servers works wonders I suppose.  One thing I hope Sony someday figures out is there's a difference between making content "challenging" versus "pain in the ass".  Let me give a quick example.

In the Ikkinz 1 Raid, there's a stone mob that you kill in a pit (don't ask me for names i can hardly remember the zone names) during which a bunch of his buddies randomly add to the fight.  We love that fight, its exciting, challenging in a fair way, and has a bit of a twist.  Fun content.

In contrast, in the Ikkinz 3 Raid, there comes a point where you have to kill a handful of, you guessed it, the same stone mobs.  This time however, the twist is the kill shot on each mob must be made by a member of a particular class or the mob instantly respawns at full health.  Its totally random and you can't tell what class needs to get the kill shot until it emotes.  Dear Sony Developers, did it ever occur to you that one might have a single say mage in your raid, and that mage might just go LD and not be able to return making that mob unkillable?  Or, the one scenario that makes me laugh/cry the most is when your one necro needs to log for the night but forgets to leave the Expedition ........ whoops.  Gay, silly, pain-in-the-ass content.

Anyway, enough of my bitching.  Basically progression-wise we're KT flagged as a guild of course, and have defeated the first 3 Ikkinz raids.
Yea, I know I'm missing the Ikkinz 2 win message screenshot ...............


So anyway, I'm happy to mention that Operation Recruit has been a tremendous success (of course).  We've been actually having fun backflagging, highlights have been doing Vallon in Tactics and engaging at least 12 splits in the center of the room (go go Shaman offtanks!), to AE-ing the Bert script in well under an hour.  Been some fun nostalgia for the old timers for sure.

We cleared Time with 59 the other week.  With those numbers and about 75% of the clerics boxed, its pretty damn exciting let me tell ya.  Time is definately some very fun content with the exception of Phase 3 of the OMG-wru-loot.  I've never seen this posted on the web before so I thought i'd show folks who haven't beaten Quarm what happens when you free Zeb from his "egg" .............
Basically Zeb walks around some and then chat's with Druzzil Ro a bit then she boots you to the top of the PoK Library.  At this point I try not to have a bad-acid-trip like response when I see Aid Grimel.  Then we all watch Miskal 20k off the elevator for the tenth time .......... good times.   =)

Anyway, that's what's going on in DA these days.  We're 2 events from Qvic access, Ikkinz-4 and the dreaded Uqua raid (ugh) and in about a month we'll be focusing a lot more on GoD so we'll have some more progression updates later this Summer.

Cheers,

Teneran
