Recruitment is ........ OPEN!                    June 16, 2004
Dragon Army is Recruiting (and Flagging) All Classes
Do you like raiding?  Do you take pride in playing your class well?  Have you always wanted to see the Plane of Time and high-end GoD but have lacked flags or support?  If your answering yes to these questions a rare opportunity is available to you.  Dragon Army has opened recruiting in all classes and will shortly commence a complete backflagging run for the Elemental Planes and the Plane of Time.  That's right, we're going to kill everything from Grummus to the Rathe Council.

First of all why are we doing this?  While we're still able to clear Time and work on GoD content we've lost a number of members since GoD came out (as everyone has) and rather than continue to add folks peicemeal, we're wanting to fill some spots at one time, getting new folks flagged, geared, and up to speed in an efficient way.


What kind of guild is DA?

Well, first and foremost we're a high-end raiding guild.  Our goal as a group is to progress through the high-end raid content in the game.  Since our inception in January 2003, we're proud to say we defeated the Velious, Luclin, and Planes of Power Expansions, and were the 4th guild on Tribunal to enter the Plane of Time and defeat Quarm.  We take a lot of pride in playing well, defeating content with skill and minimal forces.

We're passionate people as a rule, we take the game seriously when we play it but definately have a lot of fun along the way.  Although we're passionate we're not into drama.  Drama queens and whiners will quickly find themselves looking for a new home.

We typically raid from 3 to 5 days a week.  Raids last anywhere from few hours to 4+ hours and typically start at 7:30 pm EST during the week.  As much as possible we schedule raids in advance weekly.  Raiding is mandatory if you are logged into the game but of course we don't expect someone to be available all the time.  Many of our players start late or leave early based on RL schedules, and most of us miss some raid days completely.  On the other hand most of us voluntarily cut into our sleep time on occasion to accomplish something in game.  In other words, while we're serious about EQ, we also are serious about our lives outside the game as well.


So what should you as a recruit bring to the table?

First, you should know how to play your class.  We'll teach you the high-end encounters but if you're not up to speed on playing your class, you won't be much help to us.  Additionally we expect our players to give their 100% attention at raids, people who constantly AFK or watch television while playing etc. aren't the type of player we're looking for.  Second, you should have "high" playtime.  No matter how good you are if you only log in once a week for 2 hours, you're not much help to us.  While we provide significant support to our members you shouldn't be the type to want everything handed to you.  Lastly, you should be easy to get along with, low drama, and enjoy working with your guildmates on challenging content.


So ...... if you like what you've seen so far and think you're what we're looking for, head to our Recruiting Section for more information about requirements, the backflagging run, and post your application.

Teneran
OMFG, a GoD Update~                           July 02, 2004
Its been a couple months since a content update, and yes we've been working on GoD like everyone else, well at least as much as we can stomach.  To be totally honest, the design and implementation of the expansion certainly has made progress difficult. Rather than try to explain it, let's listen in on some DA guildchat ...........
As much as I absolutely hated GoD when it came out, I'll admit its gotten better, 5 months of free beta testing on Live servers works wonders I suppose.  One thing I hope Sony someday figures out is there's a difference between making content "challenging" versus "pain in the ass".  Let me give a quick example.

In the Ikkinz 1 Raid, there's a stone mob that you kill in a pit (don't ask me for names i can hardly remember the zone names) during which a bunch of his buddies randomly add to the fight.  We love that fight, its exciting, challenging in a fair way, and has a bit of a twist.  Fun content.

In contrast, in the Ikkinz 3 Raid, there comes a point where you have to kill a handful of, you guessed it, the same stone mobs.  This time however, the twist is the kill shot on each mob must be made by a member of a particular class or the mob instantly respawns at full health.  Its totally random and you can't tell what class needs to get the kill shot until it emotes.  Dear Sony Developers, did it ever occur to you that one might have a single say mage in your raid, and that mage might just go LD and not be able to return making that mob unkillable?  Or, the one scenario that makes me laugh/cry the most is when your one necro needs to log for the night but forgets to leave the Expedition ........ whoops.  Gay, silly, pain-in-the-ass content.

Anyway, enough of my bitching.  Basically progression-wise we're KT flagged as a guild of course, and have defeated the first 3 Ikkinz raids.
Yea, I know I'm missing the Ikkinz 2 win message screenshot ...............


So anyway, I'm happy to mention that Operation Recruit has been a tremendous success (of course).  We've been actually having fun backflagging, highlights have been doing Vallon in Tactics and engaging at least 12 splits in the center of the room (go go Shaman offtanks!), to AE-ing the Bert script in well under an hour.  Been some fun nostalgia for the old timers for sure.

We cleared Time with 59 the other week.  With those numbers and about 75% of the clerics boxed, its pretty damn exciting let me tell ya.  Time is definately some very fun content with the exception of Phase 3 of the OMG-wru-loot.  I've never seen this posted on the web before so I thought i'd show folks who haven't beaten Quarm what happens when you free Zeb from his "egg" .............
Basically Zeb walks around some and then chat's with Druzzil Ro a bit then she boots you to the top of the PoK Library.  At this point I try not to have a bad-acid-trip like response when I see Aid Grimel.  Then we all watch Miskal 20k off the elevator for the tenth time .......... good times.   =)

Anyway, that's what's going on in DA these days.  We're 2 events from Qvic access, Ikkinz-4 and the dreaded Uqua raid (ugh) and in about a month we'll be focusing a lot more on GoD so we'll have some more progression updates later this Summer.
