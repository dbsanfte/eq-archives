Rallos Zek ....... in the dirt!                         9-01-03
Welcome Dragon Army to the Elemental Planes
Fight Stats:  RZ killed on 5th Dragon Army attempt (total) with 70 people
Back in March 2003 we set out some long-term goals for DA, this is back when Arch Lich was a hard mob for us.  We stated our goal was a dead Rallos Zek by September 1.  On September 1, DA took down RZ like we'd been farming him for months.  Every single person played to their best, and words don't do it justice, so I'll keep it brief.  But I have to say I couldn't be more proud of this group, you earned and deserved this win.     /salute

Thanks Kacaroto for this interesting screen shot ........
Arterial picked up a Blade of War (OMG!) while Cendil got a badass War Charm of Crossed Blades

The rest of us ran around and hailed Librarians, fell off elevators, and said OMG about a hundred times.  Grats to Cylli on our first guild death in the EP ..... PoA see invis > Cylli.


So, what's in store for Dragon Army this Fall?  First, please welcome Rallos Zek to farm status now that we figured the script out.  I also know Solusek Ro just soiled himself after he got a tell about our kill, so look for the Burning Prince in the dirt very, very soon.  After that, lots & lots of fun stuff ahead.

See you in the Elementals ..........

Teneran~

Fun Times in the Planes ......                      9-26-03
Despite the dreaded "Backflagging Run" that occurred after we broke into the 4 Elemental Planes, we still managed to finish off the month with some new and fun kills in the EP.


We visited Grioihin the Wise in the Plane of Water a few times ........
Water is a fun zone (well, the melee hate it but ......), its got a very 3-D quality to it and takes real skill in balancing mobs and watching your range.  Its nostalgic to those who ever had to do much in Kedge Keep way back when.  Grioihin drops some nice stuff too .........
We also tried a ring in Earth on top of a Mayan-looking Temple, was pretty wild and we did well considering we had zero clue about the encounter.  After we killed a crapload of rocks of various sizes and difficulty, this dude got up from his nap ...........
Since he lives in the Plane of Earth we obliged him and put him back in the dirt, to thank us, he decided to give us a few presents.  The second loot is a very nice belt (no haste on it though), but I messed up and forgot to SS it.
We also messed around in Air late one night, and killed some pretty weak named that dropped some pretty nice stuff though.


All in all, we've been having a blast exploring new content.  We've re-visited some old friends too, bascially killing every single mob required for Planes access the last 11 days.  Most notable was our Rallos Zek kill on 9-25, which I'm going to make a brief comment about.

After our first 2 Rallos Zek kills, we had some folks on Tribunal blatantly, and more subtlely, state that the only reason we beat Rallos was because the event was bugged or made easier than it previously was.  Well, to those people I'll say this.  Go beat the current, re-beefed up (RZTW with more HP & more adds) Rallos Script with 72 total people AND kill all the adds ........... because that's what we did tonight.


Teneran
Seafood Anyone?                                        10-9-03
Come break bread with Dragon Army.  Our first course will be Calamari, lightly breaded with a delightful cocktail sauce.  It will be served with a shot of tequila to take away the pain of trying to fight a mob that blinds underwater.  Nothing is too good for our guests!
Lots of hungry guests waiting to dig in!  P.S.  thanks for the molds Hydro!


Still hungry?  No worries, second course is Lobster, boiled with clarified butter, classic.  Oh ya, lot's more tequila where that came from, you'll need it.
Mmmmmmm, that was tasty, and another 2 molds went over real well too.  Thanks Krziik!



Also of note, we killed Mystical Aribitor of Earth (MAoE) the other day on our first try.  For those unfamiliar with the EP, this is the guy you need to spawn and kill to get Earth B access.

Talk about bar fights, this was the best knock-down-drag-out I've ever been in.  We had 62 people to start and were left with maybe 10 to 12 standing in the end.  You know you are having fun when you are chain-nuking a mob from 5% with a MT still up but no healers left alive.  Was a real nice "W" for sure.


Overall we're having a lot of fun exploring the EP, look for more DA first kills soon.

Teneran
