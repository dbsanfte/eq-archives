Beruthielle SilverLeaf
A mixed breed whose appearance favors her father's side of the family, while her heart is more what might be expected of her mother's race.

Her friend and mentor, Kolya, stands behind her.
Legolia Greenleaf,
Ranger
Discovered by Rangers of Kelethin, this elven maid's history was lost.

Adopted as a "sister" by Kolya, she learned the ways of the forests. She mourns him now, since he has gone missing from Norrath. Perhaps when he is able to return, though crippled by loss of memories, she will again be seen about Norrath also.
Ldari McLaird,
Warrior
From a noble clan fallen upon hard times, Ldari nearly always traveled in company with her foster brother Meerc.

Since he has gone missing from Norrath, she has withdrawn in mourning. If a day comes when he returns, she will doubtless accompany her dear brother again.
Barbarienne Beastmistress
This shy young relative of Meerc & Ldari McLaird is seldom seen. She admires both, and has some of the skills of each.
Pouncibelle Starwhisper,
Bardess
This young Bard was seldom seen without her littermate, Scre.

Since his disappearance from Norrath, she has been in mourning. Her sorrow is now too deep for words. One day, when he returns, she will once again be seen singing in Norrath and keeping company with her dearly loved brother.
Keleen,
