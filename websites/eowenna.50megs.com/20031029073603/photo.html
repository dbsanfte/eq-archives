Except for the bases and the EQPA, these are all my own work ...

EQPA is a special project by highly talented and skilled artists, a "Drag & Drop" machine for assembling EverQuest character portraits. A link to the main EQPA site is on my links page (see left margin) and embedded within the first EQPA portrait (below).

My other drawings were made on bases from All About Dollz "Free Bases" or from Mystic Dolls. A link to "All About Dollz" is in their banner (below). The "Mystic Dolls" site closed.

Both "Free Bases" and "Mystic Dolls" permit editing AND matching with others. If you are new to these drawings, please understand that this is highly unusual. If I could draw my own bases, I would ... these are the next best thing. ;)
