THE RULES


The Disclaimer

Realize that this is not a sanctioned Sony tournament. That means that we do not have any legal or moral control over the actions of other players in the arenas where our events will be held. We are hoping and expecting to find the other players to be cooperative, but we can’t force them to do anything. This may mean that duels will need to be stopped or moved if any "interference" is encountered. I’m certain this will be a bigger problem for the team events than for the individual events. Please be patient with this aspect of the tournament.


The Rules - subject to change for a little while as we iron out the details.

SINGLES EVENT RULES:

We will be using a tree style elimination. Each player in the Tournament will be randomly selected to do combat against another player. They will duel until one of them defeats the other twice (best 2 out of 3). The winner will continue to the next round, the loser is out.

At the predetermined time the players will meet in the chat room above dead end. The referee will meet them there. The referee will then find an arena where they can duel. If all goes well, the arena will be set up before hand. Players will go into that arena, choosing the colors given them by the referee.

Once in the arena the combat area will be set between 2 recons, one friendly to each players color. The depth of the arena will extend from the back of one recon structure (the "building" in which it is housed) to the back of the other. It’s width will be about 3 grid sections wide (the exact coordinates will be given to the players by the judge when they enter). Players will remain inside this area at all times during combat. Leaving the area will get you a warning. For every warning after the first (for any reason) a player gets he will lose one duel.

Upon the players arrival in the arena they are to choose their tanks and equipment before they leave their base. All tanks and all configurations are allowed. They are to then go to the recon that they have been assigned. Once both players have arrived they will be given the signal to start by the referee. No mines or other devices may be used before this time. If any mines are on the battlefield before each duel they will be removed, either by the judge or the players.

During the duel, players may use the recons if they wish. But they may not return to base until the duel is over. At the end of each duel the players may return to their bases if they wish. There they may reconfigure or change tanks as they wish. But once they leave the base they must remain in that configuration until the next duel ends. Players will have no more than 5 minutes between duel for these reconfigurations.

Unfortunately, due to the nature of these things, if you log out for any reason during a duel, you lose that duel. I know this may not be fair to players that have some sort of technical difficulty, but it must be done. If the player returns to the duel within 15 minutes he will be allowed to continue the contest (if, for example, player 1 accidentally powers off his PC with his foot during the first duel, he loses that duel. He can, however, turn the PC back on and jump back into Tanarus and start duel #2, knowing that he is good enough to defeat the other player twice in a row). This rule can be over ruled if both players and the referee agree. We would like to encourage you all to offer this option to your opponent if he gets disconnected. We really don’t expect people to "LOG" during these duels. Too many people would hear about it later. :)

After the first round we will begin duels for the second round. How many rounds we have will be entirely dependent on the number of players.


TEAM EVENT RULES:

A team for purpose of these events is any 5 players that you can get together. Some of the larger teams out there may wish to field several teams for this tournament. Great, please do so. We will accept one player as an alternate, in case one team member can’t make it. Alternates may not be members of any other team. A player may not be on 2 teams, though we will allow a player to be an alternate for two teams at most.

Team event scheduling will be a little more flexible than the singles events. It will be much more difficult to find a time where both teams can all be there AND where an arena will be free to use. So we will gather the list of teams and randomly determine the parings. We will then ask the two team leaders to find a time within the scheduled times to play. Once they have found an acceptable time, both team leaders will e-mail us and tell us when. The match will be held in whatever arena has room. We will have an order of preference for arenas. This means that we will look at our #1 preference, if it is open, we go there. If not we go to #2. This will help prevent any preference to cloud the choice of arena. We will post this preference list on the Official Unofficial Tournament web page as soon as we can so that all teams will know them and have a chance to practice in them if they wish.

The team event rules are pretty simple. The first team to capture the others flag wins and will advance to the next round. We will use one half of the arena, and we will ask the other players to stay out of the half we are using. Any Recons on the borders (there is often a recon in the dead center of the arena) will be off limits to the teams.

Again, interference is not something we can really control, and it may require us to end the match and move it to another arena or reschedule it.

If a player logs out for any reason, play will not be stopped. The team will have to continue with the reduced number. Again, we would encourage the opposing team to delay combat until the player returns or until a reasonable time has passed.
