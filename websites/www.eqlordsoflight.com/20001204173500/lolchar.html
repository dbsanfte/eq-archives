Being a body concerned with the acquisition and correct dispersal of lore, history, artifacts, and other such lost knowledges, the group known as the Lords of Light shall make it their affair to insure that those knowledges that may be lost be instead brought to the attention of the world, that they may fulfill their proper purpose.

The overriding goal of all members must be the acquisition of said knowledge, and the insurance that it shall be used as each piece most warrants, whether that be to hand the most infernal device over to the gods to do with as they see fit, or to leave a piece of lore to rot. All knowledge must be made to realize its destiny in the grand scheme that is the world.
