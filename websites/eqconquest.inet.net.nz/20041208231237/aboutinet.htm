About inet

So who is inet?

 Inet is Christchurchs largest locally owned internet service provider (ISP), less than two years old and with thousands of satisfied customers we are here for one reason and one reason only. To provide the best internet price, performance, service combination possible. If you are an existing customer reading this and you are unhappy with any aspect of our service, we want to know. Please email The Manager and tell us.

Why is inet?

Sick of having the choice of paying high prices for quality internet access or paying lower prices for poor internet performance and regular busy signals when trying to connect we decided to stop complaining about it and do something about it and so inet began.

Customer service

Despite our size we are determined to retain the personal touch. Unlike most other ISPs where the staff are hidden in some inaccessable office in another town where every time you ring with a problem you get a different person, if you are having problems with your connection and don't want to deal with someone over a phone or just want someone to fix it for you, we are happy for you to bring your computer into the office and we will look at it for you free of charge (note as we are not a computer repair company, this offer can only apply to internet difficulties not computer repairs)
