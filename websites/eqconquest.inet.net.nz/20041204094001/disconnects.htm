Unfortunately due to the fact that modern modems are trying to push telephone lines to their absolute maximum limits sometimes people will have problems with connections if their lines are a little noisy. When you are connected to us our modem sends a constant signal to your modem called a carrier signal. Often if a modem stops hearing that signal for a short period due to a burst of line noise the modem will disconnect even though the call wasn't actually terminated. Here is a list of the most common solutions to disconnection problems.

If you have call waiting and someone calls you will often get disconnected. Add *52 to the front of our dialup number by double clicking on the My Computer icon then Dialup Networking then click with the right mouse button on the inet icon and select properties and just add *52 so the number now looks like *52 086722222 Do not add *52 if you don't have call waiting as you will no longer be able to connect.



If you are using Outlook Express you may have it set to disconnect after sending and receiving email. To check this open Outlook Express and click on Tools then Options then Dialup and untick Hang up after sending and receiving.



You may have your computer set to disconnect if idle for more than a few minutes. Sometimes Windows will decide you are not doing anything even when you are. Click on Start then Settings then Control Panel and double click on Internet then select Connection then Settings and change disconnect if idle to 59 minutes.

If you have noisy lines (you can have lines which are noisy to your modem even though they sound fine to you) try double clicking on the My Computer icon then Dialup Networking then click with the right mouse button on the inet icon and select properties and click on Configure then Connection then Advanced and add S10=70 to the Extra Settings box and just click on OK out of it.

Other common causes of disconnection problems are telephone extension cords and double adapter plugs for telephone points. We highly advise against using these wherever possible.

If you are still having connection problems try unplugging all other telephones, faxes, answering machines etc from other telephone lines in the house when connecting to the internet as they have also been known to cause problems.

It is also well worthwhile ensuring you have both the latest drivers for your modem and the latest firmware revision for your modem. Both can normally be downloaded free of charge from your modem manufacturers homepage.
