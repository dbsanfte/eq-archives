iNET Price Plans:

Note that all plans are based on a 28 day billing period and include 5 Mb of webspace

iNET Flatrate Account ($20)
This account provides up to 280 hours of internet access during a billing period, with no additional charges for extra hours, which makes this account perfect for home users and gamers alike.

iNET Standard Account ($15)
This account provides up to 30 hours of internet access during a billing period. Additional hours used are billed at $1.30 per hour.

iNET Email ($5)

5 hours of internet access during a billing period, perfect for emailing friends and family Additional hours used are billed at $1.30 per hour. (Note: people wishing to use this service must pay by Credit Card, Automatic Payment or 6 months in advance by cash)


iNET Pre-pay (from $30)
Only a light user but worried about overusing the account and getting a large bill? Just select our prepay option. Pay just $30 for up to 20 hours, or $75 for up to 50 hours to be used anytime over the next 6 months. Once you have used up the time, access will be automatically blocked until you top the account back up. Note that the minimum amount of time that can be purchased on this account is 20 hours ($30)
