Conquest Wakes The Sleeper – All of EverQuest is Shaken

My screenies, and a disclaimer.

 

Disclaimer:  I will not enter into a debate as to the tactics we used to against the fourth warder, since that is probably best left up to our guildleader, Itzlegend.  The bottom line is that we believe we did nothing wrong.  We used the world geometry to shield us from the effects of the dragon’s AOE.  This has been common practice on all servers since before Kunark was released to avoid the AOEs of the old world dragons Nagafen and Vox.  I will remind you all… we failed in our attempt to kill the last warder.  The dragon walked away at around 60-65% health.  You can see the last warder’s death message here, clearly after we had all been shocked by the appearance of Kerafyrm

 

http://www.flare.de/images/Sleeper_02.jpg

 

Well, we had just lost to the 4th warder and were ressing our dead when I saw Ralanan get death touched, so I instantly panned to the bubble and snapped my first shot.

 

http://eqconquest.inet.net.nz/sleepers/sleeper01.jpg

 

Camdar was next on Kerafyrm’s hit list.  Kerafyrm warped to him, and dispensed maximum ownage.

 

http://eqconquest.inet.net.nz/sleepers/sleeper02.jpg

 

After owning Camdar, I think he stepped on Furion as he went by, headed for someone else.  Furion didn’t survive.  I realized that I didn’t yet have a screenie with Kerafyrm targeted, so here he is in my target window, clear as can be.

 

http://eqconquest.inet.net.nz/sleepers/sleeper03.jpg

 

After he finished killing the rest of the people looting their corpses, he decided I was next on the list to die.  At this point, I stopped taking pictures because I was frantically trying to hit Divine Aura.  As you can see by my text box, he MISSED me, and then landed a shot for 1614 points of damage.  Ow.  Because of my uberness he decided to charm me, as you can see by the text box as well.  Then I suppose he was afraid of our corpses because it was time to flurry on them.  L337.  I guess he wanted to make sure we were good and dead.

 

http://eqconquest.inet.net.nz/sleepers/sleeper05.jpg

 

After owning everyone else, he decided that it was time to take another poke at me.  Bam, thx 1444 point ownage.  At this point, it’s looking to me like the charm is an effect of his melee.  I guess I do like it rough…

 

http://eqconquest.inet.net.nz/sleepers/sleeper06.jpg

 

Ok, so he had me charmed, and I was looking up to him lovingly as all good pets do, and he realized that if charm broke, that I would pop open a 30 gallon drum of whoopass on him, so he decided to finish me off.  Thx 1954 points of damage.  You’ll notice that after he kills me off, he whacks two more of my compadres before I can even get the LOADING, PLEASE WAIT message.

Instead we are all going to be suspended or banned, and our guild is going to be disbanded.  DOH!
