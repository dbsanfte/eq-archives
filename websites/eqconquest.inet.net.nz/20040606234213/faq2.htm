Frequently Asked Questions
Do you support V90?
Yes. We support both V90 and Kflex 56k.
What is V90?
V90 is the new international standard for high speed (56k) modem communications. This replaces the two older competing standards of Kflex and X2.
Do you have a news server?
Yes. It is located at news.inet.net.nz
Can I have a homepage?
Yes, with all of our plans you are entitled to 5MB of web space if you desire. Please contact help@inet.net.nz if you require a homepage.
I hear you have dedicated games servers is this true?
Yes, currently we have dedicated Counter Strike servers, a Quake III server and a Kingpin server. We are happy to setup servers for other games if enough of our customers are interested. Feel free to email us if you are one of our customers and would like to see a specific server setup.
I'm already with another isp and I don't want to pay another setup fee just to find you are no better than my current provider. What can I do?
We at Inet welcome comparison and are confident that when you try our service you will be so happy you will not want to leave so we do not charge any connection fee.
