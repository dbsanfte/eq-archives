15/10/99

You may be aware that Telecom is introducing a call charge for anybody using a modem to access the internet. This charge can be avoided by following the instructions below.

From November 1st Telecom will be charging 2 cents per minute for calls to the internet for anybody using more than ten hours per month and not using the new 0867 numbers. For this reason we highly recommend all customers regardless of usage switch to the new telephone number. As long as you dial our new number you will avoid Telecoms new internet call charges.

The new number is 0867 22222

If you are using Windows 95 or Windows 98 and are calling from the Christchurch calling area follow these instructions to change the number you are dialing.

1: Start your computer and double click on the "My Computer" icon on the desktop.
2: Double click on the "Dialup Networking" icon.
3: Click once with the right mouse button on the "Inet" icon and select Properties from the menu list which appears.
4: Change the phone number to 0867 22222
5: Click on the "Ok" button

You have now completed all the necessary changes. If you are unsure how to make the change or have any questions or problems please either contact us at Inet Helpdesk or by telephone at 3774103

If you live outside the Christchurch calling area or use a non Windows95/98 system and are unsure how to make the change, we will be emailing a fuller set of instructions to you all individually in a couple of days time.
