KotR Goal

     Above all else we are a GOOD guild and are sworn to uphold and protect all that is good.



KotR Members & Recruits

     All classes/races are to be accepted into the family. In the event of a supposed evil race/class the person must prove to of a good nature. No good person shall be turned away due to class or race.



Family Etiquette

Treat your brothers and sisters with love and respect. Any problems will be handled privately, NOT publicly, by a member of the Round Table.



Guild Representation

All persons wearing the tag represent the guild in all forms of chat. /ooc, /shout, /auc will be viewed as a direct representation of your family.



Honor

All Knights are expected to act honorably at ALL times. Dishonest trades, disrespect, and ninja looting will not be tolerated. Any dishonorable acts will be dealt with by the Round Table, and if seen fit the person may be forcibly removed from the guild.
