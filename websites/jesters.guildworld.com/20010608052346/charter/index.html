Jesters Membership Rules and Procedures

The next phase will be to get all existing members registered. I recommend the following: 1) We first announce on the bulleting board and ask for primary characters to be registered first 2) Every character must have sponsor on the council in this phase. This is done by at least one council member leaving a comment on the applicant saying "xxx Sponsors this yyy". 3) We need at least 5 positive votes to have a player allowed into the new directory [and no negative votes]. If we get a negative vote we need to discuss it with emails. 4) In this phase only council members will be able to vote on new "applicants" 5) Once we have got all the normal readers of the board registered. We start announcing over guild chat. I will raise all the members who frequently read and participate on the bulletin board to the level of Junior Council member [temporary only]. 6) In this phase we are again only asking for primary characters to be registered. Each applicant must have a sponsors [can be a council member or junior counil member]. Both junior and full council members can vote in this phase. We need at least 5 positive votes and no negative votes. 7) Once we have the bulk of the existing primary characters registered, I will demote the temporary junior council members to normal members [maybe a few exceptions]. From this point on any member can sponsor someone and vote on a new applicant. Memebrs should start registering alternate characters. They must put a comment on the character from their primary character saying this is their alternate [stops idiots tricking us]. Alternates are automatically allowed into the club, but have no voting rights and are flagged as an alias character. Does this make sense? SAvin
