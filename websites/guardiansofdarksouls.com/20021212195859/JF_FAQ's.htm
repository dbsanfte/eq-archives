Q. What are the system requirements to run the products?

A. Operating Systems - Windows 95/98/NT/2000, Linux, Sun Solaris, HP/UX, IBM AIX, AS/400

Windows platforms require Pentium 200, 32MB memory with total disk space required at 5MB.

Output is to any HP compatible laser printer utilizing either PCL or Postscript. Label printers are also supported. Limited support for Xerox metacode. Limited support is now available for dot matrix printers. Adobe PDF format output is also available.

Q. What environment does the product run under?

A. Design is Windows GUI-based tools. Central runs as a service under NT and as a system process under UNIX and AS/400.

Q. How will the products fit into what my users are accustomed to?

A. JetForm captures the current output print stream, so integration is seamless for your users.

Q. How easy are the products to use? Do I need programming skills to work with these products?

A. This is a very subjective question. The learning curve varies by the individual. Most people can learn to make simple design changes on any document. This would include an address or telephone number changes. Individuals who are accustomed to working with graphics design tools should easily adapt to working with JetForm Design. For advanced functionality, most people require some training. Many of our clients use our services for the more complicated projects.

Q. How long will it take to implement a solution?

A. With proper planning, a solution can be implemented in a matter of days.

Q. What documents are best used with this technology?

A. Any document can be used with this technology. Some examples of documents that are most frequently requested are: check, invoice, purchase order, statement and bill of lading.

Q. Can I use a dot matrix printer? How?

A. JetForm now provides text and basic graphic support for dot matrix printers on all platforms. Dot matrix printers are also supported utilizing Windows print drivers under Win 95/98/NT/2000.

Q. Can I use a color printer?

A. Yes, both PCL and Postscript color printers are supported.

Q. Can I convert our previously used documents into JetForm documents?

A. Yes – Output Solutions provides a document transfer service.
