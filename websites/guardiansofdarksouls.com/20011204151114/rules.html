Welcome traveler! No doubt if you've come this far you're either curious about joining the Guardians of Dark Souls or you want to know more about how we conduct ourselves. Well before I get into the meat of our rule system, the key underlying factor that all must be aware of within GoDS is that our guild is founded upon the principle, need before greed. If you have no interest in sharing, or obtaining loot only for the sole purpose of selling it, you need not read any further. This guild is not for you.

Our rules are very simple. I will outline them briefly and then go into more detail later on.

  1. Our guild is Need Before Greed. (NBG)
  2. You may not be anonomous while in the guild.
  3. New members are on a probation period for 1 month.
  4. At no time will a member of GoDS participate in Kill Stealing.
  5. We like to have free raids for all. Gems and Jewelery are donated to the guild.
  6. If you have a dispute, take it to tells. No fighting on guild channel.
  7. Watch the swearing on guild channel.
  8. Epic loot is always merited to our guild on our raids.
  9. On joint raids, all loot aside from Epic Loot will be /random'd.
  10. On Guild Only events, loot is distributed through Dragon Points.
  11. You are expected to attend a reasonable amount of raids and events.
  12. Do not sign up for a raid if you are not sure of attending.
  13. You are expected to follow the rules.

Now these rules are very simple. They are in place to make the guild a safe, friendly, and respectable organization. Some of these rules may not be for everyone, and to those who do not agree with them, I do not blame you. No exceptions will be made however, so do not expect any. Regardless of level, class, or ability, all members are expected to agree and abide by these rules. We are very sorry to anyone who can not follow these rules, and we wish you luck in whatever guild you find that does suit you.

Now as promised, I will give in depth details about the rules:

1) Our guild is Need Before Greed - This means that when grouping together or raiding together or doing anything together, you are expected to put loot in the hands of people who can use it. You are expected not to take loot that you do not need or do not want. Never should you take loot with the sole purpose of selling it if there is someone who can use it. Even outside your group, if you know someone in the guild needs an item, you are expected to give it to them if no one in your group can use it. If we all follow this rule, what goes around will come around. You may feel like you're losing out when you give up a FBSS to a guild member who needs it but when you receive a SMR a week later you will understand why this system is built the way it is. Loot is fleeting. What is hot today may not be hot tomorrow and the game is always changing and new loot is always being discovered. Our goal is to equip the guild as best we can so that we can go out and get the better loot later. Even if you don't receive an item back from a guildie, your donation will no doubt make it possible for you to receive even better loot later on.

2) You may not be anonomous in the guild - This rule is fairly straight forward but many people feel it is unfair or needless. Many people ask for an explaination, so here it is. We are a guild. We are a team. In many aspects, we are a family. We are proud of all our members, and we expect all our members to be proud of us. If you feel the need to hide your guild tag, then this isn't the guild for you. Your guild tag decorates you as one of the Guardians of Dark Souls. It is our uniform. Our badge of honor. If you need to hide your level and class, then use /role. Any violation of this rule wil lbe seen as a major violation of the rules and actions will be taken agaisnt you for repeated abuse. This may seem harsh, but showing your tag allows your guild to find you and recognize you and allows for a sense of unity.

3) New Members are on probation for one month - This is not as bad as it may sound at first. All new members have a one month period where they are free to feel out the guild and the guild is free to feel them out. If the guild doesn't feel right for you, then you are free to leave. Now during this period, there are certain privlidges you are not allowed. You are not permited to ask for a loan from the guild, or from any members of the guild especially over guild chat. Even after your probation is over, it is still not considered polite to beg from the guild. You may not spend Dragon Points on high end loot items during your probation. Obviously, we want to benefit our dedicated players and reward them for their continued participation in raids. Our Dragon Point system eliminates the posibility of someone joining the guild for one raid and then leaving once they complete a goal. We state here however this exception so it is universally understood. Also, while on probation, you are prohibited from receiving Epic loot. While this last guideline may be bent, you should not expect it to be. Needless to say, after a month probation is lifted and you are awarded all the rights and privildges of every other member.

4) At no time will a member of GoDS participate in Kill Stealing - Play nice guys. I don't think this rule needs to be explained. We're all here to have fun. If you get so worked up you feel the need to kill steal or act like an ass, take a step back and re-evaluate the situation. If you can't control your temper or play nice, then this guild -really- isn't the place for you.

5) We like ot have free raids for all. Gems and Jewelery are donated to the guild. - We don't want to charge people for Hate Stones, Coffins, reagents, or Sky stones. Raids are expensive to run and we also don't want our officers or raid leaders to have to pay out of pocket. To eliminate this, we have made it policy for gems and jewelery to be donated to the guild to run future raids and events and to repay our clerics who spend money out of pocket on peridots. We do discourage the use of personal reagents, as it can become difficult to repay everyone. On high end encounters, reagents will normally be provided. Also, we do recognize that some gems can be highly sought after. These we can offer for sale to the guild for a reduced rate based on the current fair market value. In certain cases they may even be awarded for epic completion or the creation of resist gear.

6) No fighting on guild channel - We want a harmonious environment for everyone to play in. Disputes will always arise and people will always bicker. This rule is more of a guideline than a adamant policy. If you have a problem with someone please address it personally in tells or through an officer or through the guild leader. Above all remain civil.

7) Watch the swearing on guild channel - Not everyone has a potty mouth. I happen to, but I don't expect anyone else to be forced to listen to it. If I can control my swearing on guild channel, then so can everyone else. Just watch the language ok?

8) Epic Loot is always merited to the guild - Every raid we host Epic Loot is merited to the guild. This is the only loot we will ever merit, and with good cause. Our guild is dedicated to obtaining epics and completing quests. Not only will we merit epic loot to one person at a time, we will make sure we do everything we can to get that person their epic. No one in GoDS will sell their epic loot or use it for another purpose. If you want epic loot, you are expected to get your epic. If you are caught selling or trading it, you will be penalized.

9) On joint raids all loot aside from Epic Loot will be /random - We do not believe in merit. One person works just as hard on a raid as another. If we are not using Dragon Poinrs to determine where loot goes, we use /random, with the exception of Epic Loot. If you are a member or if you are invited to a GoDS raid, you are expected to follow our founding principle Need Before Greed. While we can't force ou to adhere to our rules, we can refuse to invite you back on another raid.

10) On guild only events, loot is distributed through Dragon Points - Dragon Points are gone over in detail under their own section. For more information about DPs, follow the link. Here I will only tell you that the more raids you go on, the more DPs you earn, and the better chance you have of getting specific loot. This system is not to be abused, please don't look for loop holes. You will be punished for doing so. (ie we play by the spirit of Dragon Points, not the letter)

11) You are expected to attend a reasonable amount of raids - I can not stress this one enough. If you attend only raids where you have a good chance of getting something, it will be noticed. If you never attend raids, it will be noticed. Please keep in mind the spirit in which the guild is founded. We are here to help each other, and the guild. Not just ourselves. Fight for your guild, fight along side of your guild, and most importantly help your guild.

12) Do not sign up for a raid if you can not attend it - This is very important. We try to set up our raids well in advance in order to assure success. Therefore, we need to know who will be there and who will not. We want to get all our members into the raid, so advanced sign up is mandatory to assure yourself a spot. Signing up and not being sure you will attend is unacceptable. While it has been more of a loose policy, recent events have lead to the enforcement of the rule that if you sign up for a raid and do not give 24 hours notice that you will not attend, you will be penalized 5 Dragon Points. No exceptions.

13) You are expected to follow the rules - This goes for all members, officers, and especially me. No matter your station in the guild, you are expected to follow the rules and conduct yourself in a benevolent and kindly manner. Role-playing aside, we all want to have fun. DOn't make the game less fun for others by ignoring our rules or the play nice policy.
