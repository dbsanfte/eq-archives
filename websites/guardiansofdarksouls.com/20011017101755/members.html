New Recruits: If you wish to become a member of GoDS you need to do FOUR things.:

  • Read and comprehend the rules on our Rules Page.
  • Reand and comrehend the Dragon Point system.
  • Post an introduction on the New Recruits board.
  • Contact an officer or Zius and announce your intentions.

Once you've done these things, someone will contact you and you will become a probationary member. Keep in mind you must be level 50 to be considered for the guild.

Currently we are looking for 2 new officers. These positions should not be applied for lightly. IF you feel you meet the following requirements, submit an application with Zius by emailing zius@hotmail.com. The requirements are as follows:

  • You must be a full member of GoDS in good standing.
  • You must be willing to lead, organize, and manage raids.
  • You must be willing to follow all rules and guidlines set by the guild leader.
  • You must have patience.
  • You must put yourself to the side and be willing to help the guild before yourself.
