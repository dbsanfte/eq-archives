I am Torment, and I live to do just that.  For to torment is to spread the seeds of hate, and that is one of my masters biddings.  The humans, mere animals really, already hate me, but I will one day make them hate me more.  There are, of course, other fair races as well, and they will one day also know Torment.  One day I will meet and slay my own kin, the elves.  On that day I shall truly know satisfaction.

I was orphaned as a child.  I was taken to the council.  There, instead of being taught the ways of my true father,  I was taught not to discriminate against the lesser races.  I was taught to abide the laws of the lesser races and to be tolerant.  It was beginning to seem as if the council was planning on opening up even more free trade with the lesser races.  Later, this was confirmed when my foster-father told me of the council's wish to someday soon establish a trade route between Neriak and Freeport. 

My foster-father, a member of the council, began schooling me in the ways of the diplomat.  Once, I was even allowed to sit in on a secret meeting between him and a high-elf in a secret cavern on the outskirts of Neriak in the Nektulos forest.  The high elf wore lightly colored, flowing robes.  He seemed disgusted at our appearance and mannerisms.  He was an ambassador, but for an ambassador, he was not well schooled in the art of diplomacy when it came to the likes of us.  I later learned from my foster-father that he was a magician.  The meeting dragged on for several hours longer than it should have and we became hungry and thirsty.  Then, this mage/ambassador did something that astounded me.  He muttered an incantation and before our very eyes appeared food and drink of all sorts.  With another incantation,  utensils appeared.  Such self-sufficiency!!  He could conjure up anything he needed with mere words!  I was very impressed!

The very next day, after stealing a few gold coins from my father's money belt, I walked in haste to the Tower of the Spurned.  Once there, I petitioned, and was granted, an audience with the leader of the magician sect.  I offered him the coins.  In return, he tutored me in the ways of the arcane.  For months, thereafter, I found myself studying tomes of magick for hours on end.  I was also allowed to study books on our culture, the culture of the Tier'Dal.  Of course, I learned the ways of the magi, but I also studied the ways of Innoruuk, and of his blessed servants, The Priests, The Necromancers, and the Shadow Knights.  I learned a deep respect for these ways and these sects, but my interest in the arcane ways of the Magi had not lessened.  I would use this arcane science to further the cause of Innoruuk throughout Norrath.  At the same time, my own needs would always be quenched. 

My studies are now at an end.  Knowledge that I still require can only be gained through venturing forth and experiencing what this world we call Norrath has to offer.  I have recently met with others who share my philosophies.  They are called the Dalharan. I have been learning much from their leader, Kyllnarushi.  They are somewhat of a resistance organization. Our views are generally thought of as old-fashioned by the populace of Neriak.  The council considers us outlaws. Rumor has it that Kyllnarushi murdered a high-ranking council member.  If he did, then I respect him even more for it.  In the future, as I grow in the likeness of my father, Innoruuk. I will update this bio. For now, there is not much more to say about me.
