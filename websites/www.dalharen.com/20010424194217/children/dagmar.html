Eveningstar Chimerabane, a Winged elf and a Paladin from the secret lands of Norrath, was on a Quest this day. She was well versed in the enchantments of mages, as well as the holy prayers of her goddess. She was hunting far and wide in this, her last day of freedom. Though she knew not that these days would soon come to an end. She’d sent quite more then a few demons and demodands to their final dooms, though she knew not what final hell awaited them beyond The Planes of Hate.

With such kills to her credit, surely no Shadow Knight could prove over much a challenge no matter what plague he might be to the good people of Antonica. But plague he was. To both the goodly people, and to Eveningstar as well, as she would soon discover!

Once found, she stalked the Shadow Knight who called himself Stavros d’Morte, the Black Plague, taking both her time and his measure. Casting a spell of silence on herself, she flew in low and struck him without warning, for she expected no less from him were their positions reversed. Though she struck him well, he reacted quickly and buried a throwing axe between her wings as she flew away for another pass. With her wings wounded and useless, she was grounded. He charged.

She knew that her great spear, that was over twice her height, would be useless in such close quarters, so she drew her great-sword, who she called by name, Crusader. He must have been wielding a Dark Reaver however. For with her first parry, her sword shattered, and only magic so foul as this could corrupt so holy weapon as she had wielded just moments before. Then… a feint… followed by a plated fist that broke her jaw. And only darkness followed in its wake.
How much later she awoke, she knew not, but the city she was in could only be Neriak. For though well crafted, the city was shaped in designs honoring all that was dark in the world. She soon realized she could feel a darkness coursing throughout her veins as well. Albeit kindness, or cruelty, her captors provided her a mirror to watch her transformation. Already her wings were blackening, and her skin was slowly taking on a darkling hue. With luck, she would retain her mind, but her flesh and bones would soon forever be like that of the Teir-Dal. But luck was not with her. For in a desperate bid to retain what she could of herself, she embraced the darkness. And resisted not when Stavros d’Morte told her she would become his wife, for what greater trophy could a Shadow Knight claim than to so twist a Paladin of Tunare, and make her his own.

Though their marriage possessed far less love than one she had one day hoped to have, it held far more, for her at least, than she would have expected of a union among the Teir-Dal. And so, Dagmar was born a few, short years later.

* * *

Though not of any less blood than the other children of Neriak, due to the dark magics wrought upon her mother, Dagmar made few friends in Neriak in her earlier days, due to her semblance of a mother of lesser blood than the Teir-Dal. Though a willful child with a bold and careless tongue in her youth, and as insolent as she often was in times of anger, she exhibits far more restraint than you might believe. Had you known her in her earliest days, the fights she had, and the noses she bloodied on the playgrounds of Neriak, you would see for yourself that each and every scuffle foretold of a bright future to come in the service of Innoruuk.

But this “bright” future was “worried” upon by her father Stavros. Was she sincere in her hatred, or was she only striking back at a world that sought to strike her down for being different? She had the potential for greatness. She was even now living in a “crucible of fire” that could forge a hatred for all else even stronger than his own. If only she could survive the “flames.” But to show her his love and support would surely ruin the cudgel she might one day become to the Teir-Dal’s in the service of Innoruuk. He could do naught but pray, and hope, cursing himself all the while for such weak emotions.

And then one day, her hatred of all else was proven when she began casting the dark prayers of Innoruuk, she was now proven among her people. And outcast no more as she roamed where she would, inflicting pain and suffering upon all the lesser beings and creatures of the world of Norrath.

She spent her days and nights as an Initiate of Innoruuk, making the dark woods of Nektulos a land in service to the precepts of hate and suffering, as hailed by all who worship our lord Innoruuk. To this day, roaming far and wide as time goes by, she can still be found delivering pain and suffering to those who would presume to oppose Innoruuk’s vision of a perfect world of naught but hate. A hate so strong, it will bring all those of lesser blood under the heel of Innoruuk and his faithful, and leave no room whatsoever for those beings and creatures, whose very presence on our world does offend  eyes, our hearts, our souls, and our minds.

* * *

As a scribe of my people, I include this quote in closing, heard of Dagmar in her younger days as an Initiate in the church of Innoruuk. It makes me proud to hear such wisdom in one so young among our people.

“Love is of little use and far too fleeting to be tolerated or endured. Only hate can prevail and see us though the times ahead. Since time began, we knew we would one day rule this world. And only through hatred and vigilance, will we bring this day to fruition.”

A rather bold statement for one so young, but her words ring with the truth of darkness that Innoruuk grants all of us so worthy of his enlightenment.

Dagmar Stavroscion
