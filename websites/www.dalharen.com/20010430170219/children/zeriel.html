Zeriel was born into a large, proud family in Neriak. They were well known and often feared. As a child he was sent to the Spurned to learn the ways of magicians. He did not particuarly like it, but it was what his father asked him and he dared not defy his parents. 

He lived at the Spurned so he was not there when the tragedyy struck the Family. His parents where murderd by a gang of humans from the mixed market. After his parents murder he dropped out of magician school and began searching for the killers, he searched for many years growing further and further away from his family. 

He slowly began to change. He started to become like the people that he was around. He no longer wore the proud insignia of his family. As he changed the only person that stayed close to him was his elder brother Leitbur. Zeriel started to visit more and more lowly places. He would live on the streets for weeks at time. Before he knew it he had completly changed -- he was no longer the middle child of a rich and proud family destined to become another fire flinging mage; he was a fearless master of weapons and stealth. He had become the very shadows themselves... He had become an assassin......
 

Zeriel De'Jinn
