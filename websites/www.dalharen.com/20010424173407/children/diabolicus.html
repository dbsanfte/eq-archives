Diabolicus casually saunters into the room, then gently lays his hands on the table. "Greetings. It seems you wish to hear the history of my life, so I shall tell."

I was born into House Nephandi in Neriak. My house is one made up mostly of Necromancers, although there are some Shadow Knights and Warriors in it as well. My father is a warrior, my mother a necromancer. As a young child, I always wished to be a warrior like my father, a follower of Rallos Zek. He and my mother sometimes had interesting theological discussions of their beliefs, I learnt much from those. Although I do follow our Great Father, I do believe there is some wisdom in the words of Rallos Zek. At the age of 10, I was tested by the Nephandic sect leaders. This was to see which one I was best suited to. I miserably failed the physical requirements to be a warrior or shadowknight, although, I did very well in the aptitude tests for a Necromancer, the area that held the least interest for me..

I was initiated into the Nephandic Order of Necromancers and began my studies. I did very well, but never truely enjoyed myself. I learned from our Blessed Father, I hate everything about him and myself. Such feelings please him though, so I do not bother facing the issues I have with myself.

Let me just say though, that my master was a complete and utter bumbler. I hated him for his stupidity and his ability to fail at everything he tried. Being his apprentice was one of the most humiliating things I had to go through. The other Dark Elves tended to leave me alone, fearful that I might be just like my master.

For my rite of initiation to become a true Necromancer, I was to kill my parents, and had only one chance on each parent. Only later was to find out the commonplace of such a request. I immediatly set out and tried to kill my parents without a thought of doubt, nor a moment of hesitation. I first went to kill my father. My puny dagger never even came close to his platemail, which I probably would never have pierced in the first place. In my charge at him he merely sidestepped then tripped me onto the ground and demanded to know what in the name of all that is dark was I try do?! I explained myself and he just laughed. He wished me luck on my mother, then left the house. My plan of matricide failed miserably as well. My mother realized what simple cantrips I was trying to use for her demise and merely rooted me to the ground before I could do anything. In my frozen state she realized what I was trying to do. She released me and was pleased that I hated her enough to try and kill her. (Though, she did mention this practice was getting a little old.)

In passing my rite of initiation, the Masters of the Third Gate recruited me to work for them. It was quite an honor for me and my family was pleased.

I reported back to my stupid master, who was very pleased. I passed. He was not as stupid as I suspected. In collaboration with a wizard, a watching orb spell was developed to monitor the progress of apprentices without their knowing. So sadly, a hated rival killed him the next night, destroying his lab and his grimoire. The wizard? Who knows? I suspect he may not have been a proper Teir'dal! My master deserved what he got for working with the lesser races!

With passing my gauntlet I set out to Nektulos to test my arts. I learned much, and found there was demand for the various things one could find on the bodies of dead animals and skeletons. I also met a powerful Shadow Knight by the name of Brenain. He recruited me into his guild, the "Dominion of Evil." It was interesting at first, but after a while I merely stayed there for the status of belonging to a guild.

Eventually, the challenge I at first found here dissapated after a few weeks, so I soon travelled down south to the East Commons. There I had my first encounters with the lesser races, who truely are inferior. I sent a report to the Masters of the Third Gate that the area be cleansed. I learned much from the conflict and death I caused there, and it was a great challenge.

However, as all things do, this soon became boring. The Masters of the Third Gate wished for me to investigate the outpost of the Erollisi on a puny island in the Ocean of Tears. I was to elimante any followers of Erollisi I found, and report my findings to them. It seemed my Superiors were wary of possible incursions into Nektulos by the wood elves. There, I again learnt much of the conflict I engaged in and the death I caused. It was fascinating! Killing my distant cousins filled a vast emptiness in me, and increased my hate for them immensly. I reported my findings to my superiors. One was quite alarming, the area is constantly being sent reinforcements! I recomended we send more followers to this isle to combat the problem; as I was ordered to investigate the military strength of the inferior races in Oasis.

However, before that while on Sister Island, I met an interesting Mage by the name of Torment. He was of the Dalharen d'Oloth, a mysterious and powerful guild in Neriak that worked in the best interests of the Teir'Dal. They were far more influentual then my current guild, as well as being far more organized. I soon became friends with Torment and learned a bit of the Dalharen, and they're enigmatic leader, Kyllnarushi. I displayed interest in joining with them, as my current guild was falling apart. Torment took me in as his apprentice to teach me the proper ways. I happily relinquished any ties I had with the crumbling Dominion.

Once my report was finished, I headed to the great Oasis of Marr in the Desert of Ro. I realized my hate for the intense heat there, as well, it was difficult to properly hunt there. The area was rife with the presence of inferior races, I sent a report to the Masters of the Third Gate recomending the area be immediatly cleansed. I also noted my extreme distaste for the area, and got a transfer to investigate the activities of the inferior races in East Karana. I have not yet explored it a great amount yet, and there is clearly much to be learnt from conflicting with the local wildlife and especailly their death!

It also seemed that my new found friend Torment was sent to the East Karana by his own guild, so we have on many occasion hunted together. He is for sure ahead of me in unlocking the spheres of mystery and a very competant mage. He has my utmost respect.

After fully investigating the Karana's, I was called upon by the Dalharen in aiding them on a quest to find out about the elder Valshear. Some information of his where abouts was discerned from a half slave trader... The important part is that I killed him! Ah, my first half elf kill! The glory of it! He was by the name of Thamastalias or something like that... However, half elf names are not important. Lord Kyllnarushi inducted me into the Dalharen, which was an extreme honor.

After that, I recieved orders to go investigate the Lake Rathe for the presence of lesser races and the strength of the Aviaks there... The first order I found most confusing, of course there would be lesser races there! I have found the aviaks to be a excellent challenge, and there is truelly much to be learnt in conflicting with them!

With my most recent orders, I am beginning to suspect that the Master's of the Third Gate wish me out of their way... Although, I have no clues as of yet why. I believe I may detain sending a report in as long as I can to see if there's a reaction.

In my travels, I have gathered a decent sum of money. When I first arrived back to Neriak proper, with money from things I had sold in the foreign quarter; I discovered the joys of drink. While under the influence of this wonderful liquid, the hate of myself and my path dissapates, it is the perfect escape from this world.

Diabolicus
