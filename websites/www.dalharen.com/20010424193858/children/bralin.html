My future looked bright.  I was to be taking over my father's lucrative textile business, marry a pretty, young woman and settle into a life of comfort and luxury.  All this changed when my parents were slaughtered by some elvish raiding party.

I guess the events that led up to my current situation started when a local magistrate by the name of T'simbek summoned me to his manor.   I was honoured by the invite since he holds a position in the royal courts and I knew if I served him well, he would be a valuable ally for my future business ventures.

Upon arriving to the gates of the manor, I was surprised to see my sister Morwenn. I would have completely missed her had I not caught sight of her telltale whitened robe that she usually wears.  She seemed a little distressed when she saw me, but her face softened instantly and gave me a reasurring smile that all would be well.  She has always cared for me and is very concerned for my safety.  When I finally was granted audience with T'simbek, he greeted me as though I were a long lost son.  I felt a bit uneasy, but his commanding presence and demeanour quickly dissipated my fears.  He handed me a document, sealed in wax and stamped with his signet ring.  I was to carry this missive to Ambassador D'Vinn in Crushbone.  I've never been one to tangle myself in political matters, but I knew that if I pleased him, he could garner me more business.  Also, he is not a man used to being declined, so I made preparations to sail to Faydwer.

My trip was uneventful, due mainly to the small but lethally trained guards that came with me.  I handed the note to D'Vinn without consequence and made arrangements to leave that same hour, but the Ambassador asked me to stay a while longer.  Ten days I was held there, with nary a reason as to my being there, but I obeyed dutifully.  When finally I was given permission to return to Neriak my homesickness had nearly overcome me.  I had never been away from my dear parents and I knew that my father needed me back in the shop, even though he almost radiated with pride when he heard that I was making contacts with the royal courts.

Triumphantly I returned, only to be greeted by my sister Morwenn, who wore a dour look.  When I asked what was wrong, she burst into tears and fell into my arms.  I tried to comfort her but she just cried.  When she finally composed herself, she told me that our parents had been killed by a group of elves on one of their walks out in Nektulous Forest.  It was as though a knife had seperated my soul from my body.  I had never known anguish before, nay, it was hate, pure and unadulterated hate.  It consumed me. 

Being the eldest, I inherited the family business.  I had no desire to follow that career since it would be a constant reminder of my murdered parents.  Under Morwenn's advisement, I signed it over to T'Simbek, his for the taking.  I then began to plot my revenge.  I devoted my time to training in Neriak's most lethal profession, the hallowed Shadowknights.  I wanted to inflict pain upon the world and in doing so perhaps regain some of my sanity.  Morwenn had just attained the First Circle within the Necromancer guild and I urged her to join me to travel Norrath and join me in my vendetta against the cursed followers of Tunare.  I hope that Innoruuk grants me my one wish someday, to drink the blood of the one who killed my father and mother.

Bralin D'Mort
