The Dalharen d'Oloth have been active throughout the history of the Teir'Dal. Their lives have been enmeshed with the Will of Innoruuk as dedicated tools for his plans. They have been shadowy emmisaries, swift daggers, and avengers who performed their Tasks at the behest of the Father. As long as the Teir'Dal have populated the world, the Dalharen d'Oloth have waited, watched, and when necessary, acted.

Though they existed, their members have lived their lives in secrecy. The name, uttered in whispers, was not a battle cry of the Father, merely a mesh of words that were known as a secret Order dedicated to His Will and Purpose. However, recent events have forced them into a new and active role in the world. They no longer move in the secrecy of Neriak, unknown. They now make a name for themselves in the minds of their enemies.

The Foreign Quarter in Neriak was the event that pushed them into the open. A direct and open attack on Innoruuk, flying in the face of all that Teir'Dal society was based upon. The marketplace: filled with unbelievers, forced the hand of Dalharen d'Oloth. Action: swift, brutal and unmerciful was the only option.

For many years, the beliefs and creeds of the order have been passed on in secrecy. Little is known of the inner workings, but it has been rumored that elite and shadowy Teir'Dal Elders still control the action and inner movements of the guild. Teir'Dal who are the guardians of ancient knowledge, and privvy to the innermost secrets of the Queen, and the Church. However, members of Dalharen d'Oloth will speak no word on the existance of these Elders. The truth of such rumor is impossible to verify.

Now, their purpose is Justice, in the name of Innoruuk. They are not weakened by the hateful curse of the Goddess Tunare, free will is not an option. They are devout pupils of the strictures of Innoruuk who will stop at nothing to see that their Purpose is fulfilled by ejecting the unbelievers from their home in Neriak, and from the rest of the world.

You do not choose Dalharen d'Oloth. It chooses you.
