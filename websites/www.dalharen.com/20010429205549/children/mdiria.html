Collapsed in misery and self-pity upon the cold stone floor, Mdiria L’Soduire contemplated the events of the past fortnight. 

What will I do now? My name is shamed for eternity. There is nothing left for me. There is only the sweet release in the death awaiting me beyond those iron bars. My entire existence had been for this night, the one moment in time when every desire would be mine. The forced tears and laughter, the extensive hours of precious time spent cajoling and manipulating those around me... all wasted. The tiresome research and most especially the distasteful intermingling in order to meet those key to my success... all for nothing. What went wrong? Where did it fall apart? I had planned so carefully, double and triple checking even to the few ticks before the moment occurred, and yet as it did and I stepped forward to claim my prize... what strand of the web broke? Who wasn’t I watching? I was watching everyone; every thing was perfect. I did not miss any detail. Every key player had been bent to my will. How? Why? WHY? 

And slowly a rage grew within Mdiria, becoming fiercer with each passing tick. Each passing thought sparking explosions within the growing inferno, until she threw herself against the bars confining her, cursing all that had ever touched her existence. Her curse raining most loudly upon those who had had any part in the events of the last fortnight; however, nothing was spared her curse, including those whose graves were long since cold or those who had passed her once when she was but a child on the streets of Neriak. Her voice gaining in power from the hatred finally pouring out, traveling beyond the thick dungeon walls to hover over the city chilling all within its walls. 

"My child, you have awakened," whispers through her mind, abruptly distracting her. 

"Who is this? Who dares enter my mind?" she demands. 

A small chuckle floats through her mind. "I can do much more than that. How do you like your cell? I have had it waiting for you..." 

As the chuckle becomes smug laughter, the inferno of hate washes over her, its entirety directed towards the invasion in her mind. In mid curse the world suddenly stands still and The Only True God, The Prince of Hate, Innoruuk appears before her. "Ah, My child, it is time. You are finally ready to serve Me well, your hatred has come alive within you. Feel the power of it. Do not vent it in an uncontrolled rage. Conserve it. Use it. I want you to impart your hatred, My Hatred, upon the heathens that walk this land. If they will not follow My Will, then give them the death I will give you if you fail. Succeed, or die trying." 

The Prince of Hate held his hand out, palm up. Slowly his hand clenched into a fist capturing something intangible to Mdiria. 

"What I hold in my hand is memory, every memory of you. You are reborn by My Will. In the minds of all, there has never been one called Mdiria. You shall make your name known with My Hatred. The tools I will give you are those that I took from you today, that and more. I will teach you powers that make your natural talents in manipulating things around you a trifling. If you serve Me well, you will become a master in the ancient Chaldaic arts. And you will hate even more, as your strength will be what you give or take from others. Never forget how much you depend upon others for your success." 

Thoughts begin to twist through Mdiria’s mind leaving a wake of information behind, enlightenment dawning upon her face. "Prove yourself to Me and I shall teach you more. Fail Me and the wretched future that I have taken from you will be given back." With those last words The Prince of Hate vanished and movement began again. 

Mdiria dropped to her knees and bowed her head to the ground. "My Lord!" 

And so life began... 

*    *    *

"Mistress, may I ask how you found the Dalharen?"  the young dark elf queried.  Mdiria swiftly glanced at the brightest of her apprentices.   "I was curious and you've never spoken of it and ..." he bowed his head and quietly finished,  "Please pardon my impertinence for asking, Mistress." 

Mdiria smiled to herself.  This one will serve Innoruuk well when his time comes she thought.  "You do not deserve to be excused for your impertinence, Child.  But I will answer your question.   It feels like it was so long ago...  It was shortly after a vision I had had." 

Mdiria gradually began to recount the tale.

"I had fallen upon the floor.  I remember, I slowly stood, my mind aglow with new insights.  Bars that had confined me, now hung open.  The path leading from the opening was one into life rather than the mindless death it once promised.  A shaky step forward brought me to my knees again as a wave of weakness washed over me.  I fought to stand up but was betrayed by my body, the effort taxing the little strength remaining within me.  Something was telling me I needed to leave the area quickly.   I fought the panic building within me, my mind madly searching for a solution.   Words, vaguely familiar yet unknown, began to fall from my lips.  With the last of them dying upon my breath, renewed strength consumed me giving me the power to stand and continue towards life.  In a daze, I walked through the bars, lost in the new knowledge that sparkled within me."

The apprentice watched silently as the raw emotion flickered across Mdiria's face.  Her words died down for a moment, her eyes gazing at a time long past.  The ticks passed by in the stillness.  Slowly becoming aware of her surroundings once again, Mdiria shook her head slightly and continued her story.

"I wandered for some time.  My feet taking control of my destination while my mind traveled the maze of mystic words glowing within my head.   I do not know how long I walked before sharp words caused me to focus on my surroundings, the maze dimming slightly.   The words had come from a tall dark elf berating a servant for some slight blunder.   The servant ran off quickly upon being dismissed and the dark elf's eyes turned toward me.   Our eyes met for a brief moment.  I could see in his eyes the same hatred that had recently been awoken within me, its light sending me back to the maze in my mind.

I walked away, lost in my thoughts once again.  In the days that followed everything surrounding me was a haze against the brightness of the maze.   I drifted from place to place with no destination beyond where my feet chose
to go.    Most of my time was spent in Nektulos, occasionally killing but more often reflecting within myself.  There were a few times when I was brought out of my haze briefly.  Each time involved the dark elf I had seen in Third Gate.   I had never seen him before the incident I already mentioned yet I managed to see him several times within the days following my vision.   Once or twice the snap of a twig as he passed through the forest made me return to reality for a few ticks.  Another time, the chant of a spell broke my haze long enough to see him gate away with a troll and another dark elf.

There were more occurrences and they just became more and more frequent. Then the day came when the haze encompassing me lifted completely.  The coldness of the stone I was sitting on registered in my mind.  I stood and
breathed in deeply of the forest air.   I realized that my Prince had decided that I had absorbed enough information and it was time to do His Work.  Turning to head back to Neriak in order to stock the supplies I would need, I noticed that same dark elf sitting a little ways away staring at me. I paused for a moment and stared back, but he did not turn his gaze. Shrugging I was going to continue my task, but something within me made me turn back and walk slowly up to the elf.    I know now that Innoruuk guided me.   The elf was none other than Kyllnarushi."

Smiling at the memory, Mdiria looked her pupil.  "And thus began my journey with Dalharen d'Oloth.  And now it is time for you to return to your lessons," she said, motioning for the child to leave.   Once she was alone, she began to laugh softly, "Ah yes, Kyllnarushi... another beginning, little did we realize then what would follow."
