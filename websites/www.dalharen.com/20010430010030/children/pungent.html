The slightly renowned, but not famous Teir'Dal adventurer Valxx T'Olx is maybe best known, not for his daring adventures but more so for his assorted pet collection... or make that WAS known....

On a trip into the depths of Guk, home of the Froglocks. Valxx, while looking for a new swamp alligator for his collection found a prison deep with in the belly of Upper Guk, where many trolls had been imprisoned. Being of exotic tastes Valxx instantly fell in hate (simalar to 'fell in love', but more dark elfish) with the concept of owning his very own "Pet" troll. Well his companions began to chatter among themselves:

"It can't be done!" and "He's gone mad!" echoed through the room. All of which only made Valxx "hate" the idea even more. So Valxx had one of the trolls bound, gagged and tied to a sti and dragged the small beast all the way to Neriak, where he began to train it.

Despite constant baths Valxx could neither rub, scrub nor scour the stench from it. Finally, in disgust, named it Pungent, a name that Vlaxx found incredibly fitting.

Pungent was soon placed into Shadow Knight training, he excelled at the physical training but failed repeatedly at even the simplest cantrips, which only furthered his masters disgust in him. Pungent, away from the brutish training of a true Troll Shadow Knight, learned the finesse of a dark elf rather than the power and stamina of a Troll which made him a most dangerous creature indeed. What his trainer failed to realize (or neglected in their own arrogance) was to even bother teaching the beast Teir'Dal! It was no wonder he failed his spell training, since he never understood the language.

Valxx soon became a joke to his peers, "How is your... child... heh i mean pet?" they would say. Valxx filled with anger and hate and soon began to beat the troll daily. This in retrospect was surely regretted.... Pungent on the other hand ate well.

The fellow Shadow Knight's welcomed Pungent into their fold, for this act of hatred was beyond even their twisted minds. "Rex'al hrucas vlo'tox!" they cried (which of course meant little to Pungent). Despite their welcome to him, Pungent soon grew bored of the chants and casting in the life of a Shadow Knight (or was confused who can really say?) and bid his fellows "Gud Bye!" and set out to find his true path.
