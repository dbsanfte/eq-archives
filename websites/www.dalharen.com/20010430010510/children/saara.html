From my earliest memories, I have been in the service of Innoruuk. Whoever my parents were, they gave me into the service of the Church, and the Church raised me. There was never a time when I did not know my true Father, as he raised me as surely as the priests and priestesses of the Spires. I can remember countless hours of my youth performing tasks for the priesthood, and countless more in prayer or meditation in the vestibule of the Spires, as I learned more and more of the Father and the task he had set upon the Teir'Dal. My faith is a part of my body as surely as my heart. Even when I was very young, I could feel His hatred, His rage and His Will. 

I was marked for the priesthood myself, and when I reached a suitable age, my studies began. Now, the litany's are
engraved in my soul, and I question whether I was born with all of this knowledge, or whether I really did learn it along the way, as it seems a part of me. 

"My mouth is the Mouth of Innoruuk, The Children shall not stray, the body shall not fail in His service." These words of
the Litany of Purpose still echo in my mind when I think of my task in life, and I imagine they always will. I studied the
arcane kowledge available only to initiates in training, and began to hone skills that would aid me in my Task. Each
new piece of knowledge spurred me forward as I learned to heal, to bless others, and how to call forth the Hatred of
the Father upon my enemies. 

But there were intrigues afoot in Neriak. Forces far above me were in a struggle that I was only beginning to understand. As a young Acolyte, I was cloistered in the church for my training, but even there the rumors spread, as
the priests and priestesses whispered about it in corridors. I began to fit together pieces. There was much controversy
over the Foreign Quarter of the city. A "safe haven" for any race to come and trade, and set up shops. The faithless
had a place in our city, and I soon found that the Hatred bloomed in my mind when I dwelled upon this. Members of
the priesthood were scandalized by it, as it went against everything that Innoruuk himself stood for. Was not Purity one
of the Commandments which we adhere to? 

Finally, when I had reached my third rank of learning, I was instructed by the High Priestess to leave the Spires, and to
travel out into the world. 

"The world will be a good teacher for you. You will encounter those who you must face on their own territory. However,
you will also meet those brothers and sisters who are turning from the faith, and that is always your first duty. Turn
them back into the fold, and truly you will be blessed by Innoruuk. May He use you well." 

So I set forth, wending my way through the dark forest of Nektulos, through the pass, and into the Commonlands. The
lands bordered the city of Freeport, a city of crime inhabited primarily by apeish humans. The Commonlands are
where I was, for the first time, reviled for my worship of the Father. Shopkeepers would not deal with me, and my faith
was spit upon by these lesser beings. This spurred in me a righteous anger and at last I felt the pure weight of the
Task set upon me at my birth. It went beyond prayer. It was the True Will which the High Priestess had known I would
see. I felt the hatred for the other races which had usurped the Father's lands. I felt rage whenever I turned my head
and saw an ape, or one of the puppeted children of Tunare smile or hunt. 

I knew that I would never again sit idly in the Spires of Innoruuk, but would roam the world, converting the Lost, fighting
our enemies and beating them back from Our Father's lands. And, the more power I gain, the better able I am to
serve his Will. 

I discovered early that my Task was not a lone burden, but one that I shared with others. I soon found others in my
travels who felt strongly the pull of the Father, and formed an Order: Dalharen d'Oloth. 

With these other's at my side, I feel confidence that we will defeat the jealous rivals of our Father, destroy their unholy
children and hand the world into the hands of it's rightful owner, Innoruuk. 

May the last embrace of the Father 
welcome you Home. 


Saara Zha'linth
High Priestess of Dalharen d'Oloth
