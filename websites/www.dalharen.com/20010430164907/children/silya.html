You have asked me to reveal my past and my motivations. Even though I feel a certain hesitation to do so, I understand the necessity behind your request. 

Memories of my earliest childhood are clouded in darkness for me, and the little I haven been able to find out required careful research. I was born into an, at that time, well renowned and somewhat influential family. My cursed parents, however, had fallen victim to the false beliefs of one of the lesser species and were secretly operating against their fellow brethren, thus not only betraying their own blood and heritage, but our father Innoruuk as well. Even though their judgment was obviously blinded, they realized that they would eventually have to share the fate of all traitors. 

Facing their inevitable doom, they took precautions so that at least their child, me, would be able to survive the extinction of our family. I was thus given away and raised as an orphan, under the protectorate of a priest whose connection to my family was loose at best. My parents and the other members of their family eventually met their fate, and my survival is the result of the pathetic compassion of my parents, otherwise commonly found among the weakest of the infidels. 

But I grew up as a true child of Innoruuk, having been fostered by his priests from my earliest days on. I joined their ranks as soon as my age permitted it, devoting my entire existence to our Father, the Prince of Hate. Having found out about my past, I am the more determined to bring forth the hatred of our Father to the inferior races as well to the traitors amongst our own race, thus turning my cursed parents act of compassion into one of hate and destruction for their ilk. 

I have spent the last years in the service of the Spires of Innoruuk, traveling to far lands and spreading his teachings among those who are worthy and summoning his wrath upon those who are not. But the example of my cursed family made me turn my attention towards our own lands as well, and I feel the urgent necessity to fight certain recent developments. Humans and other infidels are allowed to dwell freely within our city instead of being forced into our service or slain. Their presence alone is an affront to Innoruuk Himself, but it also seems to spoil the weaker of His children. I have met many of our younger brothers and sisters who seem to lack a even the most basic understanding for our ways, straying from the path of Hate by allying with or even befriending infidels. 

We have to act now, and this is why I have decided to join the ranks of the Dalharen d’Oloth, willing to contribute whatever is necessary to reach our common goals.
 

Silya
