"Blessed Be the children of Innoruuk, for through them shall the welcoming darkness blot out the accursed light. Blessed Be Our Lord and Father Innoruuk, who gave us skin the color
of shadows, minds sharp as the razor's edge, and superiority over all other races, though they be too ignorant to see it."

Thus with prayer does the cleric Desyre greet you as you seat yourself beside her in The Blind Fish. She gazes out the windows which affords a view beneath the surface of the river that flows in Neriak. Light through water plays upon her face, causing her blue eyes to glow like the great ice floes of the northern territories, so quiet and still in the ocean, yet so deadly to passing ships. You may be quick to dismiss her as too meek, silent and shy, but when she shifts her gaze back to look in your eyes, you freeze, knowing then she is like the iceberg, beautiful, but with much hidden beneath the surface.

"So," she begins softly, narrowing her eyes. "What do you wish to know of me? Shall I give you the basics, or shall I blather on about every last detail like a halfling hung over sharks?"

"Perhaps you wish to know why am I a cleric. It has been said that the weak do not deserve to live, thus the healing art is a mockery of Our Lord of Hate and Death."

"What is weakness, then? It is inexperience. Inexperience denotes a lack of teaching. Through my healing and protection of my dark brothers and sisters, Innoruuk teaches His children they live by His power and for His will alone- or they will die for His glory. Mind you, if I deign to...heal one of the light dwelling animals, it is to show Our Father's power over their useless 'gods'. "

She swiftly turns around and seizes the hand of a young man who had nearly liberated her purse strings from her belt. She breaks his left ring finger with a snarled "Go with Innoruuk's blessing, child, and learn your skill elsewhere."

She calmly takes a sip of her ale and stares at you. "We are all His children, are we not?  We all must learn pain and hate from Our Lord Father." A sudden tinkle draws your attention to a small silver ring set with a rosy stone that has just fallen onto the table. You move to catch it as it rolls about, but your hand is slapped away as Desyre lunges for it.
"NEVER EVER TOUCH THIS!!!" she hisses at you, her eyes flashing. She pulls a broken silver chain from her neck and flings it to the floor.

"This," she spits to the ring clenched in her hand, "this is Innoruuk's lesson of hate and pain to me. It is betrayal, scorn, derision, all gifts given to me so I may carry out Our Father's Will against all, but especially the Fier`Dal!!" Desyre trembles with such seething hatred and anger, even you are taken aback with the raw force of it. When she glares at you with fire and rage, you know she will be a Holy Force to be reckoned with later in her training. You begin to ask why she has such a deep, obviously personal loathing of the Fier`Dal, but the words die in your throat.

"If you dare ask about it, I don't care who you are, I will kill you." she purrs so seductively you almost yearn for that death, but the velvet in her voice covers steel, and you feel it crush your soul. You ask instead of her name, it not being a family name that you recognize. She closes her eyes, and when she opens them again none of the previous emotions are there to be seen, but yet again are hidden in the frozen depths.

"What is desire? A wish, a will, it's the longing and ambition for a thing...or a person..." She leans forward and whispers in your ear "It is the hunger and passion that drives you to do whatever it takes in order to gain that which you secretly crave." She sits back in her chair and finishes her ale in one draught. "I am a vessel to carry out the Will of Innoruuk. Desire is not who I am, it is simply what I am. My real name is not important enough to mention to you."

"It grows late. You, no doubt are needed elsewhere, as am I.  May Innoruuk's shadow hide you from those unworthy to gaze upon your blessed face. Fare you well, friend. "

Desyre
