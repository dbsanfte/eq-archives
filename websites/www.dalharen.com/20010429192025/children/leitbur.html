Leitbur doesn't remember much of the early days as a young Tier'dal.  He remembers slight details of the intensive training his father had put him through in order to help him to surpass the skills of his father.  Leitbur has many brothers that are of different classes.  That is what his father wanted so that his house could rule atop the council in Neriak so that no one could deter his efforts.  

Leitbur was almost inseperable from one of his siblings, Zeriel, and they would travel everywhere together.  Leitbur, the stronger of the two in their youth, took the position of following his father in the arts of a Warrior.  He excelled in all weaponry that he equipped and had the highest agility aside from a rogue that his father had ever seen.
 
As the years passed and Leitbur grew stronger and stronger he was given more leadership in the house.  The day finally came when his father stepped down in the house and Leitbur took full leadership of House De'Jinn.  He and his brothers were among the most feared groups seen in all Norrath.  

They ruled amongst the fair city of Neriak until that fateful day;  When the high council of Neriak decided to let humans and other light skins take up shop in the foreign quarter.  Soon after that there were missing persons and their own parents came up missing.  Days later they were found slaughtered just outside the gates of Neriak.  From that day the group of brothers, infuriated, took a blood pact against light skins.  Doing all in their power to disassociate with the lights and to kill all that was a part of the lights.  

The brothers grew apart over the next few years in their search for revenge.  Leitbur is only in touch with a few of the remaining brothers.  His faithful rogue brother Zeriel still by his side.  Enraged with hate Innoruuk guides them when confronting any foe.  This is but the beginning of a long journey for Leitbur.  He searches still for companions in his quest for blood.

Leitbur De'Jinn
