"What would you know of me, my brothers and sister?" Kyllnarushi began to speak to his companions as they started their meal one evening. "I was born in Neriak, into a namesake that was not worthy of me." He looked around the table, as the eyes of his friends began to meet his gaze.

"What do you mean by.." Started one of the others. Kyllnarushi quickly interrupted, "My parents were pathetic weak creatures, who allowed the curse of Tunare to fall upon them. Their dealings caused the School of Necromancy to reject me into their ranks, which is why I fell upon the School of Wizardry. Though some think I am further from Innoruuk for this, it has given me different abilities, that my necromancer brothers do not enjoy." He paused for a moment, as he stared into open space. "Though, I would have liked to learn the proper arts, these will suffice my needs."

"You all ask yourselves, as you sit here. `Why are we to follow you?' This is a good question. The answer being, you are not to follow me. You, as I, will follow our Father's Will. We will be the eyes. We will be the mouths. We will be the arms. We will be all that Innoruuk wishes, for he is all that there is." Kyllnarushi smiled upon his companions, as his voice grew more confident. "We shall find children that have fallen from the path, and have been infected by the curse of Tunare. We will give them the chance to seek council with the church. If they refuse, we will drop them like dogs where they stand. None shall oppose the Will of the Father." He slowly looked around the room, gazing into the eyes of all the participants.

"What have you done, to show your faith? You may be asking." Kyllnarushi snickered as a mumble came from some areas of the room. "My mother and father, fell upon the curse of Tunare. The pathetic Teir'Dal worked with humans, helping them into our beautiful city of Neriak. This would not be allowed." Kyllnarushi said, as a smile came to his face, as wide as the Ocean of Tears is deep, "They refused to seek forgiveness by the church." He paused, as he looked around. "I gave them their escape from this curse. They laid at my feet, as I drove my dagger into their hearts, their last breath not allowed to utter any words, as I reminded them of their loyalty to Innoruuk." A half cocked smile came to his face, "This is the fate of the fallen."

"Now follow me, Dalharen d'Oloth, as we will have the blessing of the church, and the touch of the Father, as we go forth upon the land and spread his word. None shall oppose us, his will shall be heard!" He raised his glass of wine, "For the Father, For the Dalharen d'Oloth!"

Soon they will know pain.
Soon they will know fear.
Soon they will know the Dalharen d'Oloth.
