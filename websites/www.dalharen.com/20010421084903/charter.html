"In His Will, each of Us is born to a purpose."- The Lesser Rite of Surrender , Book of the Firstborn

We were born into darkness, and for that, we are blessed. Our father breathed life into us, and now, we do his bidding. His words are our life, our life, his. Neriak and the Teir'Dal have come upon a plague. The infestation of the surface races into our beloved home. We shall not question the elders directly, for the Foreign Quarter is there doing, but we shall not sit idly by and watch these abominations infect our people, the Children of Innoruuk. 
 

You are charged with the following, Child of Darkness

  1. Adhere to the commandments of Innoruuk at all times Purpose, Unity, Purity.
  2. You should always strive to fight beside your evil bretheren if possible. If none can be found, the lesser races will do, if you can stand their vile stench.
  3. The weakness of our enemies may be the key to their destruction. You may choose to aid as necessary, so that you may witness to them the power of Hatred and of our Father, or you may stand aside and watch them fall beneath their attacker.
  4. You shall treat the lesser races with the contempt they so richly deserve. A knife in the heart serves the will of Innoruuk.
  5. You shall follow the path of wisdom that your Leader has given to you. There are many things you do not know. One day, Innoruuk may judge you worthy. Do not dishonor the Father, by dishonoring His Chosen.
  6. Matters of importance shall be discussed by the high council. If it is an open meeting, all children are asked to attend and voice their opinion.
  7. Never forget thy heritage. Do not dishonor your fellow Children with behaviour that demeans our cause. (Roleplay, but treat people with respect. Avoid confrontation, and speak with your Sect Leader for guidance, when needed.)
  8. Innoruuk is pleased to watch his children grow in power. He has given us his blessing of eternal hate and pain. Do not lessen this by not working for the things you have. Acquire your goods from battle or sale. As you grow, Innoruuk's blessing will be bestowed.
  9. Speak in the tongue of the Teir'Dal, or one of the other, dark speeches only when in the presence of your brothers and sisters. Do not allow the lesser beings to learn our tongue, the pride of our heritage.
  10. Remember that you are a representative of the Dalharen at all times. When appropriate, make use of the roleplay tag. If you find yourself in situations where roleplaying is not possible, remove the roleplay flag.
  11. All children wishing to join the Dalharen must first go through a time of sponsorship. You can see details of sponsor informations here.
