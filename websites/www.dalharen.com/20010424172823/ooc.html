OOC Translation:

  • As a Child of Darkness you will not group with any non evil race.
  • Do not ask for help from a non evil race. If they help, and you wish to thank them, do it out of character, and through a tell.
  • If a non evil race approaches you for discussion, show him/her your dislike for their particular race.
  • If you need help with anything, refer to the proper chain of command. If no one is available, seek out another Sect leader. The word of your Sect leader is not to be questioned. This is, of course, in game terms. If you feel you are being treated unfairly, contact the Guild master and speak to him about it.
  • We will hold meetings about large events, and guild wars, etc. Some will be closed to non-council members because they deal with quests that are being set up for you all. The others, we encourage you to show up and voice your opinion. This will be done orderly, or proper action will be taken.
  • Always play in character. We are a guild put together for the advancement of role playing. This covers everything from vulgarity to harassment and rudeness. We are an evil guild, yes, but we are an intelligent group of people, and wish for people to know us by our role playing, and dedication to it, not by kill stealing or being rude. This will not be tolerated, and will be dealt with by the appropriate Sect Leader.
  • We are a Guild created to advance role playing. We are not here to help lower level players gain equipment, by having higher level players give them items they have found. We as a guild feel that the enjoyment of the game comes from the discovery and sense of accomplishment of getting items for yourself. This does not apply for paying fair value for items, from higher level members. Twinking will not be tolerated.
  • Speak in one of the evil tongues at all times. This, of course, does not apply to private messages to other individuals. Please let them know, that these comments are Out of Character.
  • Remember, first and foremost we are here to have fun. Everquest is a vast world with many different people. The most important rule of the guild is to enjoy yourself in this game. Non-enjoyment will not be tolerated!
