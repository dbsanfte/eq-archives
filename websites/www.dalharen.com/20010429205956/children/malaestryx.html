“So, you wish to know more about me do you?”  Malaestryx gives a mirthless smile, as she reclines back in her skeletal chair.  At a height of five feet, she is short even for her kind the Teir’dal or Dark Elves, but with an athletic build that shows that she has not completely shunned physical work.  Her white hair falls across her shoulders, and her violet eyes stare off across the room completely oblivious to those around her.  Yes she is beautiful by any definition of the word you choose to use, but her features have been hardened by over a century of hate.  Then she looks up as if remembering something important, and stares directly at you.  Her gaze is filled with an intense scrutiny and you feel as if she is looking into your very soul.  Then she begins again… 
 
“I am Malaestryx of The House Durathdal, I was born here in Neriak and have pledged my existence to Our Father Innoruuk and The Dead.  Yes, I am a Necromancer as were my parents, and their parents before them.  Since time immemorial, The House of Durathdal has been Necromancers.  Necromancy is my blood, my birthright!”  As she says this, her eyes take on a malevolent glow, the temperature of the air seems to drop a few degrees.  “But this was not always so, as a young girl I thought to break this chain and studied albeit secretly at the Spires to serve Our Father in a more spiritual manner, I would sneak out of our home after my Necromantic studies were complete to bask in his glory.”  The mask seems to drop for a brief moment as she reminisces, and the change is shocking as the hate melts away given in to thoughts of a life long gone.  The speed of her recovery makes one wonder if what they saw was real as once again the mask of hate takes hold as if nothing occurred.  She looks at you and smiles warmly.

“Yes you saw it my child, Do you think that Hate is all that exists?  I hope not for your sake.  Hate and Love co exist, for without one, what is the other?  During my studies in the Spires I  ‘learned’ this.  As with all things Hate, must be focused, unfocused Hate is as detrimental to you as is Love, and yes my child we do Love, for are not we the descendants of the children of Tunare as well as Our Father Innoruuk?  To think otherwise would be foolish as most of our kind are…They walk around preaching the superiority of our kind without the least bit of understanding of where we come from or our place in the world.”  

As she speaks her voice takes on a distinct air of contempt.  “Yes you have a question, What is our place in the world?  I will tell you.  We were created out of Hate, perfected upon that which Tunare created by Innoruuk, Given purpose.  We Hate those around us for their greed and ignorance.  For not knowing where they belong in the world, for their self righteousness.”  This last word escapes Malaestryx’ lips as little more than a hiss. “It is up to us to show them the power of Hate focused, and this will shall I swear to it.”

“What, you want to know more about my past?  Why? It’s passed isn’t it?  One would do well to focus on the here and now and not worry about the past.  It is better to judge others on how they act rather then acted or else you’ll find yourself with too few friends, and too many enemies, besides who I am is determined from how I feel and think, not what I do.”  Malaestryx stretches her movements akin to that of a cat after basking in the sun all day, she looks at you and her eyes are filled with a cheerful sparkle, she leans forward and with her right hand reaches out to caress your face.  A warmth passes from her hand to your face,  and proceeds to  spread throughout your body. The stresses of the days, weeks, months fall away, scars from battles long fought disappear and strength returns to your body.  Her expression so kind so…angelic and she leans forward and kisses your forehead, then puts her cheek onto yours.

“One more thing.”  She says  “With this hand I give life…” Malaestryx pulls back her face a twisted nightmare of Hate, disgust, and contempt, her eyes shining demonically. Her left hand shoots forward with index finger extended an icy bone numbing chill hits you as her finger touches your forehead where once her lips rested.  Frozen with terror you can do nothing but sit and feel the very essence of your soul being torn from you.  “And with this one, I take it away.”

Malaestryx beams a smile at you.
Malaestryx says “Ahh I feel much better now.”

Demonic laughter fills the halls of House Durathdal and spreads out into the streets of Neriak. Malaestryx reaches out for a small bell on the table beside her and rings it.  The doors to her chamber open, and in walks a young Teir’dal dressed in apprentice robes, “Yes my Mistress?”  He says eyes downcast staring at his feet.  “I’m going out.” says Malaestryx, “Have this room cleaned, it seems to have gotten a bit dusty.”
