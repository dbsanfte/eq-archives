The drunks sit around the fire in the bar and whine about their lives
and their miseries. A few cry, many more mutter angrily. Suddenly, the shadows part to reveal a Teir' Dal woman in black robes, and the patrons fall back from her with a cry. 

"So, you would whine of your miseries and your lives? Pitiful dogs!" her voice drips with scorn. "Let me tell you a story of glorious pain and exquisite suffering before I end yours. My name is Derasa, and I am a member of the Dark School in Neriak. I especially excel at spells which torture my enemies to death, and I use these to give my victims a chance to feel the power of Innoruuk before they are claimed by merciful death. Unless I choose to take their bones and call them up again as undead servants..."

"It was not always so for me. I was a member of a large family in Neriak. What was my family name, you say? Well, I am not allowed to speak it, as it was wiped from the books of Neriak. You see, my family destroyed itself, which is more of a dishonor than being destroyed by another family. My father and mother were both slain by poisoning, while I was taking care of my baby brother. As it turned out, my oldest brother, Krykus, was slave trading, and my father did not approve. He wished my brother to take over the alchemical business he had built so strong. So, Krykus murdered him. But that isn't the worst."

"In order to cover his deed, Krykus sent several of his lieutenants to capture me and Sekura, my youngest brother. Ah... but I had heard the commotion in the house and managed to flee to the Inn, where I took a secret tunnel which ran under the river in Neriak. For months, I only came out during times of the day where I know my brothers men would be drunk or unconscious. I fished and stole to secure food for little Sekura. And all the while, Krykus told everyone that my family had left Neriak to seek business elsewhere, and he had taken over the business himself. All of this continued unchanged until the day I got caught picking the pocket of an instructor of the Lodge of the Dead."

"He at first was going to kill me, but he realized who I was. 'Ahhh... Innoruuk has blessed me with the opportunity to destroy Krykus and his arrogance once and for all' he cackled. He offered my admittance into the Lodge, as long as I promised to help destroy my brother. I was not allowed to bring Sekura, as his presence would present a weakness in my training. So I gave him over to the magicians to learn their trade, and went to learn Necromancy."

"So... this is where my story ends," Derasa says with a sneer. "If any of you survive this night, find me in Neriak, and perhaps I will entertain you with the rest of my story. Just know this, Innoruuk blessed me with the hatred of my own brother to show me that His Hatred is stronger even than family. And now, I take my leave." 

As the screams begin from the undead creatures crawling in the windows and breaking through the doors, Derasa melts into the shadows and is gone.
