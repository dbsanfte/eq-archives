Pricing/Statistic List
    
      I will credit the total cost of the gems and/or metals that you bring me for combining towards your final purchase total.  The base cost prices listed below are what I can purchase with my CHR and faction - not yours. Example: You bring me a gem which cost you 75pp and it is listed below as 60pp.  You will only recieve a credit of 60pp towards your final purchase 
total.

     For orders of three (3) or more, a discount will be offered.  Discounts vary, so you will need to contact me with the items you wish to have made and the quantities.  I will respond with the discount I am able to provide you. 

     Prices listed under the base components are what I will credit towards your purchase if you provide them.  Note: Prices are subject to change.
