FAQs
What is the difference between:
*Imbued Northman Ringmail
*Northman Ringmail 		     Imbued Northman Ringmail can only be donned by devout worshippers of The Tribunal.

     Northman Ringmail has not been specially blessed by a Shaman follower of The Tribunal and is therefore capable of being worn by Agnostic or one whom follows a different god or goddess.

Who can wear the Imbued Northman Ringmail and Northman Ringmail?     Imbued Northman Ringmail can be worn by Barbarians, Humans and Half Elves whom are in the following professions: Bard, Ranger, Warrior, Rogue, Shaman.  Yet remember, they must pledge allegiance to The Tribunal.

     Northman Ringmail can be worn by Barbarians, Humans and Half Elves whom are in the following professions: Bard, Ranger, Warrior, Rogue, Shaman.  And with this set of armor, each of the available races can worship whom they wish.

Can you make Fine Plate Armor?      Yes, I am capable of making all fine plate armour, but do not currently maintain a stock of leather padding to do so.  My pricing structure for this armour is identical to that of Lord Crombattleaxe and Salenie Mithrilweaver as seen here.
Can this armor be blessed to another diety?      This armor can *not* be blessed to any other diety, only to The Tribunal.
Is there a significant delay in placing my order and recieving the armor?      No.  I currently maintain enough stock readily available to make Imbued Northman Ringmail immediately after an order has been placed.  This is subject to change based on orders placed week to week.  There may be a slight delay of a few hours based on raid activities, but most orders should be able to be completed within 24 hours of making the request.
