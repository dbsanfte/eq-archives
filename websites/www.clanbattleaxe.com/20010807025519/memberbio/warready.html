Warready's Biography

Greetings to all who wish to see my life. My life is not extremely exciting, but it is also not boring. My life is plagued with tragedies and triumphs.

Within the depths of Kaladim, a young dwarf was born into the loving family of the Thunderhawks. This dwarf was nurtured under the loving wings of his parents. Tragedy soon fell on this family. On a raid in the Plane of Fear, Warready’s father was killed by an ambush of fiends. Upon hearing of his death, Warready’s mother mysteriously vanished within days. He was orphaned. Soon strong enough to venture out of the great walls of Kaladim, Warready made friends with a wandering woodelf bard named Jesa. Jesa and Warready were the best of friends. They fought bravely alongside one another everyday. But Warready got wanderlust. He ventured outside the area of Butcher Block and in to the realm of Greater Fay. Here Jesa taught him the area and where to hunt. On an adventure into Crushbone, Jesa was killed by that loathsome Dark Elf, Ambassador D’vinn. Upon that battle, Warready fought fiercly and strong to keep Jesa from falling to the brute, but he was to much. D’vinn, after striking down Jesa, turned on Warready. He lashed out at Warr with great tenacity. Slashing Warr’s face, he caused the gruesome scar that plauges him. The gods obviously had humor, for they allowed Warready to live through the battle. Feeling the power of the gods run through every vein and every limb, he was able to avenge Jesa’s death. Warready took Jesa’s body to the city of Kelethin and buried her there under the shade of the flowers that grew alongside the PoD lift. He still visits her grave to this day to mourn her loss.

All was not lost though. He soon met a friend Lynxy. He traveled far and wide with Lynxy, helping her and developing with her. The day came when Warready though was called into action elsewhere. Communication was lost between the two for a great period of time. Warready returned to Greater Fay because something was calling him. He then traveled to the evil city of Mistmoore. There he honed his skills bashing the evil residents there and healing the newcomers. He felt that was not enough though so he traveled to the lost city of Cazic Thule. Upon arrival Warready was met with great hostility but after time, the groups there grew to know Warready. They were pleased to see that he had reached his full potential there and were saddened when he left. He then traveled to Lower Guk and then to the Island of Velious. He soon returned to the Mainland and met up with Lynxy again. Warready had become immensely more powerful than the last time Lynxy had seen him. He was then asked to join Clan Battle Axe.

He was soon accepted into the guild. Today Warready fights bravely for the guild and for himself. But he mainly fight for the memory of Jesa. Jesa maybe dead in the world but she will live on forever in his mind.

Warready
