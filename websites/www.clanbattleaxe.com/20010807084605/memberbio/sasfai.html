Sasfai Cinderhand's Biography
 

           

	Hail friend ::smile::..would you like to hear about my past? I 
assure you 'tis not very exciting, but I am glad to speak of it.  My father, 
Geoffrey, named me..he gave me this name with pride. He believed he 
could create a spell that made time travel possible. (Thus my name..I am 
still trying to discover where my surname originated..tis not Elven, I am 
sure of that) His only distraction from his research was a young maiden 
by the name of Melody, a Cleric of Tunare. He courted the beautiful young 
elf and after a time, I was born. Both of my parents were very involved in 
research into their respective professions..they truely wanted to help all 
of Norrath. 
	So...I was left alone much of my young life. I spent a lot of time 
with my Uncle Ricard; he was a warrior. He would drill me, day after day the 
use of the sword, mace, dagger,axe..but, alas, I proved only a fair student. 
One day, when Ricard and myself were taking a break, he fell asleep (I also 
noticed he carried a flask..I did not know what was in it nor did I ever ask). 
I was still young so did not venture far. I heard a loud growl from the forest so 
I stayed where I was..a full-grown grizzley bear came sauntering out from 
behind a tree.  All I could do was stand there and look at it for a moment.  
Then I walked up and pet it.......it allowed me to ::I could almost hear it 
thinking::. My uncle woke up and, of course, startled the poor bear (there 
were no more outings after that).  He took me back to my parent's house 
later that day. My parents were very gratious about the whole thing but I
saw them staring at the flask in my Uncle's pocket..... 
	I knew the connection I felt with that bear and I tested that connection 
many times during my youth. I found my calling one afternoon. Twas a clear 
day and I heard a woman's voice call me (twasnt my ma's..I knew that one 
well enough).  I ended up in the middle of a field with only the clouds above 
and the trees around me. I spent hours waiting to see if it would speak again. 
It did not. 
	I went back to Kelethin and found my house; it was burnt nearly to the 
branch; was void of life. I found, after hours of searching, a piece of 
paper charred, I almost couldnt read it. It read, "The Orcs of Crushbone will 
no longer tolerate your interference".  That was all said. I cried out..to 
the only Goddess I knew. She granted me her power. I know not if my parents 
are living or dead, but I serve Tunare now....the Goddess will lead to 
rightousness.


          
Sasfai Cinderhands
