    Charter
      Always lend yer fellow guild members a helping hand.
      We are here to help all guild members to obtain thy highest potential.
      When possible, lend a helping hand to those in need!
      Take the time out of yer busy schedule to equip those not of yer experience. Such as armor, weapons and so forth.
      Being a clan based on respect and honor - we can't have ye slaughtering innocent citizens and guards. (Freeport guards are corrupt - have fun smiting evil!)
      If ye see a battle going on, do not interfere without that citizen asking! For all ye know, their diety may be testing him or her...

    Induction of New Members

      Must have attained 20th season
      Have a one-on-one session for at least one hour (real time) with a guild officer or the guild leader
      Been seen performing at least one chivalrous deed
      Majority vote of the existing guild members
      Current guild members have one week to form their opinion - vote is at following guild meeting.

          If your character is currently on the Erollisi Marr server and you feel this is the guild for you - please fill out the online submission form. You will be contacted within two (2) days to schedule a meeting with a Guild Recruiting Officer.

    Clan Mentorship

      Purpose: To more fully integrate new members into the Clan as well as provide training to younger members.

      Qualifications:

      Mentors:
      Should have attained their 30th season.
      Should have mastered at least one skill (trade, weaponry, or research)
      Proven skill in their field in battle (Determined by Lord Battleaxe or Officers)
      Should have been in the Clan for at least 30 days (ooc: real time)
      Interview with officer/ Lord Battleaxe to determine mentoring ability Trainee
      Any new member of the Clan
      Any Clan member under their 20th season

      New members over their 20th season:

      New members of the clan that are over their 20th season will be assigned a mentor that is familiar with the class and the role of that class in a group. They will continue with their mentor, until the mentor feels that the member is ready to join the Clan in battle. In addition, the mentor will insure that the member has been introduced to a majority of the current members in the Clan and clan policy. Finally, must have a knowledge of three languages, two of which must be Dwarvish and Elvish.

      Members under their 20th season:

      Both new and existing members under their 20th season, will have a mentor assigned to them. The mentor will stay with the member until their 20th season. After their 20th season, if the mentor feels that the member is ready, the mentorship will be ended. The same conditions mentioned above will apply. If not, the mentor will discuss with an officer or Lord Battleaxe, why the member is not ready. At this time, the mentor may elect to stay with the member, or request a different member

      Role of the mentor:

      The mentor will be available (IG, IM, or Email) to the new member to answer any questions that a new member will have. In addition, will spend a minimum of 2 hours each week with their members, in battle training. This consists of taking the member to hunting ground of the appropriate level, both for soloing and finding appropriate groups and supervising the member in the group. In addition, they will insure that the member has a firm grasp of Clan protocol and policies. Further, they will assist the member in finding a tutor for the languages. Finally , the mentor will determine when the member is ready to fully join the clan.

    Promotion to Guild Officer

      Have been a member of ClanBattleAxe at least one (1) month real time.
      Must be in good-standing with the guild.
      Induction decided by majority vote of existing Guild Officers.
      If a tie results from Guild Officer vote, Lord Battleaxe will make the final decision.

    Removal From Guild

      When ye is spotted not acting such like a member of Clan Battle Axe should be, thou will be given a warning. Once this warning has been issued, ye are recommended to reflect once again on the tenants of our clan. If ye are spotted doing it yet again, a vote shall be cast at the following meeting with all guild members present. Ye peers shall decide thy fate.
        Examples:
        Kill Stealing
        Ninja Looting
        etc...
