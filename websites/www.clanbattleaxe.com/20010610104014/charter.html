The Clan's Charter
Charter Induction of New Members Promotion to Guild Officer
Removal from Guild Charter Amendment Procedure

    Charter
      Always lend yer fellow guild members a helping hand.
      We are here to help all guild members to obtain thy highest potential.
      When possible, lend a helping hand to those in need!
      Take the time out of yer busy schedule to equip those not of yer experience. Such as armor, weapons and so forth.
      Being a clan based on respect and honor - we can't have ye slaughtering innocent citizens and guards. (Freeport guards are corrupt - have fun smiting evil!)
      If ye see a battle going on, do not interfere without that citizen asking! For all ye know, their diety may be testing him or her...

    TOP

    Induction of New Members

      Must have attained 20th season
      Have a one-on-one session for at least one hour (real time) with a guild officer or the guild leader
      Been seen performing at least one chivalrous deed
      Majority vote of the existing guild members
      Current guild members have one week to form their opinion - vote is at following guild meeting.

          If your character is currently on the Erollisi Marr server and you feel this is the guild for you - please fill out the online submission form. You will be contacted within two (2) days to schedule a meeting with a Guild Recruiting Officer.

    TOP

    Promotion to Guild Officer

      Have been a member of ClanBattleAxe at least one (1) month real time.
      Must be in good-standing with the guild.
      Induction decided by majority vote of existing Guild Officers.
      If a tie results from Guild Officer vote, Lord Battleaxe will make the final decision.

    TOP

    Removal From Guild

      When ye is spotted not acting such like a member of Clan Battle Axe should be, thou will be given a warning. Once this warning has been issued, ye are recommended to reflect once again on the tenants of our clan. If ye are spotted doing it yet again, a vote shall be cast at the following meeting with all guild members present. Ye peers shall decide thy fate.
        Examples:
        Kill Stealing
        Ninja Looting
        etc...

    TOP

    Amendments to Guild Charter

      Any changes of tenants will be done by yer guild officers at the weekly clan meeting.
