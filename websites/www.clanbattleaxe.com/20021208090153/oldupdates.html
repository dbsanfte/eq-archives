12-21-01
Zabin spent a long and tedious week awaiting the spawn of the elusive Lodizal to complete this Eyepatch of Plunder quest....And it finally paid off! Lodizal popped and we were able to gather many of our allies of Vivix Protectorate and several other friends in order to take the big mean turtle down. A big thanks goes to Blackmoonx, who was nice enough to play our main tank and also to Celoria, who was wonderfully prepared with her clicker-stick to rez the first group that jumped in a bit soon. /giggle
Winners were:
  • Zabin (CBA)-- Map piece (quest item)
  • Crombattleaxe (CBA)-- Belt of the Turtle
  • Siilverain (CBA)-- Piece of Lodizal's Shell
  • Myzzan (VP)-- Knotted Ring
  • Siilverain (CBA)-- Protection of the Glades
  • Emberstar (PC)-- Cani IV
  • Crombattleaxe (CBA)-- Divine Strength
  • Raypierre (VP)-- Call of the Predator
