Talmon's Biography
Clan Member

Greetings dear friends, comrades, brothers, and sisters of the Clan I thank you for your time. It has come to my attention that many or perhaps all wish to know a little more about me, and I shall endeavor to relate a summary of my life so far. So with out further ado I shall tell my story from the beginning.

Life began for me harsh, for I am half elven and an orphan, though not by choice of my parents but my cruel fate. My parents were killed on the road from Highhold to Qeynos, I can only assume to escape persecution of their mixed marriage and to find acceptance in the peaceful city of Qeynos where all are accepted if they follow the Light. They were set upon by a band of marauding gnolls, whether the Blackburrow or the Splitpaw none can say, and were cruelly slaughtered. And I would have joined my parents in death if not for the timely arrival of a patrol of Jaggedpine rangers. With both parents dead and the rangers not know who they where and I with out any family took pity upon me bringing me with them to their home in Surefall. There I was raised by all of the Jaggedpine folk to studies the ways of the forest. I lived there happily and was accepted even though I was a half-breed for there where many like me there. For those in Surefall as well as in Qeynos are truly noble and great people.

When I was old enough I was pressed into service of Qeynos and Surefall to eradicate vermin from the lands surrounding the city and the Glade. One day on patrol around the city I happened upon the most beautiful woman I had ever seen at the time. As I was about to introduce myself I was set upon by gnoll known as Fippy, a member of the Darkpaw family. He was by far a more experienced fighter than I and was quiet a bit stronger; it was all I could do to keep my life. Eyes searching vainly for help from the guards I once again noticed the half-elven lady. I called wildly for assistance for I saw the killing blow rise to finish me off, I closed my eyes waiting for it. A few moments passed and nothing. I opened my eyes to see that the lady had engaged the fiendish Fippy in combat, I took courage grabbed my scimitar and rush to rejoin combat. Now it was the gnoll's turn to be pressed and though the fighting was fierce we managed to vanquish our foe. Once combat was finish thanked her, giving her my name and that I was training to become a ranger, I found out her name was Sharval and that she was born here in Qeynos and she was also training to become a ranger. We became fast friends and ended up patrolling the lands of Qeynos and the Karanas. And over time my feeling for her started to grow but she was more seasoned than I and she started venturing out further and longer way from Qeynos until she disappeared. My heart was saddened, no goodbye, no farewell, nothing she just vanished as if she never was. I retreated from the civilization and journeyed forth for I had grown discontent with my home. I traveled to many lands and meet many peoples.

I had finally made my way to the other stronghold of humanity, Freeport. I was quite unprepared for the difference this city had over Qeynos. Well Qeynos seems to be clean, happy, accepting, and the heart of beauty, Freeport seemed cold, businesslike, dirty and apart from nature. And well the humans I meet in Qeynos, Halas, and Highhold did not look twice at me half-elven nature the one here seem to look at me askance and distrusting. With my service to the guards of Qeynos and my loyalty to the Jaggedpine and Antonious the paladins of the city welcomed me with warm arms, these guards did not.

Seeing as I was to reside here for a while I once again came into service helping the people and the city. I eventually gained the trust and respect from the guard by eliminating the bandits on their roads. Growing homesick I returned home to Qeynos to see how old friends where doing.

Upon my returned I was informed that the gnoll of Blackburrow where smuggling into the city their intoxicating brew. The Folk of the Jaggedpine suspected some one in the city was helping the gnolls, but unable to discover who sent me and others to find out this traitor. As it turned out I happened upon the human miscreant, though he did not suspect me of aiding his would be captors, he took me into his confidence. He wanted me to pick up his shipment of Blackburrow Stout from a gnoll late that night. So I joined forth to my appointed meeting with the gnoll I then quickly killed him taking his head and the note connecting this fellow to the gnolls. I brought the evidence to my leader in Surefall and he sent me with the evidence to the Captain of the guards. I was awarded the Medal of Merit for my actions in bring down this smuggling ring. Growing restless once more I wished to see the home of the elves so I packed my things and headed to their isle.

Upon reaching the city of both races of elves I was very impressed by their city large and magnificent but I was once again greeted by rejection. In the city of the high elves while greeted with smiles and welcome I saw their barely disguised disgust at what they considered an abomination, half an elf. My heart sank lower, but I moved on to the city of the wood elves. There again I was greeted with smiles, but the look in their eyes was pity and sorrow for though they will never admit it to me or to themselves I was just half an elf. It seemed to my I had yet again to prove my value to this self-important elves my worth and that the sun does rise and set with them in mind. I journeyed forth into the den of the orc and spent many of night killing these foul monsters.

Then one night in my hunt I came upon a trio of wood elves working together killing an orc though it look like the orc was giving as good as he got. I stood by and ask if they could use assistance and soon I had joined the fight with them. That night we killed many an orc and rescued many slaves these beast took. We four quickly became friends; the two wood elven woman happened to be sisters, one named Ananiel and the other Draewyn, while the other was named Kerth. Together we had many adventures on the isle of the fae folk, and then we journeyed once more to the main continent of Antonica we once more join combat against evil. Until once day I had taken a mortal injury and well Draewyn and Ananiel tried their best magics of nature to cure me I had to remain in the city of Freeport of many a month to recover my health. And so now I have fully recovered to find that my only friends came upon some differences and split up. I have yet to find Ananiel or Kerth but I did find Draewyn and found out she had joined a group of friends in the fight again darkness. Renewing our friendship she brought me before you to join you my new brothers and sisters of the Clan. My only friends. I have had many other adventures dear friends and if you wish to know more about them or me feel free to ask and I shall gladly retell all to you.

Talmon Lonewolf
Ranger of Karana
