Date: The Twelfth Day of May in the year Two Thousand and One Time: 13:00 CST (GMT – 6:00) until 17:00 CST (GMT – 6:00) Place: Ye may hunt anywhere in Norrath, but the items must be turned into a guild member at one of the following 3 locations: Toxxulia Forest: Location: 0,0 West Commons: Location: Entrance to Befallen Greater Faydark: Location: Hills in front of Felwithe Object of the Hunt: To obtain the most bear, wolf, and cat pelts as well as the silks of Spiderlings. Details of the Hunt: Scoring: Scoring is as follows for each item obtained: Low Quality Pelts - 3 points Medium Quality Pelts - 2 points High Quality Pelts - 1 point Spiderling Silks - 2 points Silk Threads - 3 points Winners : There will be 6 winners at each collection site. The prizes will be distributed to the person with the greatest total points in the following Categories: Levels 1-16 in the following Divisions: Wisdom-based casters Intelligence-based casters Melee Classes Levels 17+ in the same Divisions Winners need not be present at the end of the Hunt to win. You can pre- register by sending an email to Dararu Swordmaidan (Swordfalcon) : dararu@clanbattleaxe.com otherwise register by giving your email and name to the collector at your chosen site. The collection points are mutually exclusive. We will not combine a hunter’s points from multiple locations. Prizes will include: a variety of armor and weapons for the melee classes, robes and jewelry for the casters.
