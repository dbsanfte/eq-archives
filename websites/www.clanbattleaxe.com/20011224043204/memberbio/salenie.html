Salenie's Biography
Clan Herald

So, gentle stranger, you wish to know of my background, of my history, of the experiences and thoughts that make me me?

Then, gentle stranger, you obviously do not know the ways of the noble born elves very well. I take it not as a discourtesy as much as ignorance. If you wish to know more of Salenie Mithrilweaver, then seek me out, and gain my trust. Treat me with decorum and courtesy, and perhaps you will get the answers you seek.

For now, suffice it to say that I am a High Elf, based in our serene city of Felwithe, possessing the will of a magician and the skills of an artisan.

I look forward to our meeting. Perhaps when we can converse personally and not through anonymity I will feel compelled to move our relationship to stage where you can get the answers you seek. Until then, may Tunare guide and protect you.

Salenie Mithrilweaver
High Elf Magician
Member of the Magicians Guild of Felwithe
Fellow of the Academy of Arcane Science
