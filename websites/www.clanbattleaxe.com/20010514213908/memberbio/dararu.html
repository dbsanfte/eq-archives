Dararu Swordfalcon's Biography
Officer : Clan Herald
 


	:: Slowly and with a warrior's grace, Dararu rises, disentangling
herself from her Ashke's embrace::

So you want to know a little, about me dear visitor? Where shall I
begin...

At the beginning? Tis a long story and one we have no time for at the
moment...

My beloved shall be rising soon for the eve's hunt, so a brief synopsis
must do till ye can meet me in battle... For that is my tried and true
profession, That of the warrior's path

The battle rages in my blood , descending from two lines of dwarven
warriors, paladins and clerics... My path was virtual predestined as the
blood of the warrior's path was born and bred into my family. 

I came late in the lives of my parents... My father a Paladin of Brell,
my mother a Cleric in the same company. As I came along, my parents were
looking toward their retirement from Brell's service. After I came, they
both went into early retirement and we traveled to the Antonica
continent to visit their old friends that lived in the Commonlands
surrounding the city of Freeport.

It was this move and the events that occurred thusafter that
inextricably placed my feet amongst the path of the Warriors. For my
family became victims of betrayal, costing me the lives and love of my
Parents at the tender 5th season of development. 

I would have joined my parents in Brell's arms, if an old friend of my
father had not taken me to Kaladim. At Kaladim, I was placed in training
with the young paladins out of respect of my parents. Eventually, I had
caught the attentions of a Lord amongst the paladin, inheritor to
Brell's holy crusade, Crombattleaxe.

Under the tutoring of Lord Crombattleaxe and his druid companion
Siilverain Britecloud, I grew strong and proficient in the Arts of Sword
and Axe. The two guided my early steps and as I came into my 20th
season, I was invited to join their Clan, Clan Battle Axe.

The Clan became an adopted family as she learned a variety of secondary
arts from its members. Languages from Salenie and Siilverain and
smithing from Lord Crom. From the clan, I had also come into knowing
Love. Love to temper the desires of vengeance at those who betrayed my
parents. Love of a family as well as love from a true mate came me
through Clan. ::Smiles brightly at the half-elven male that still laid
curled up asleep::

Tis about all I have time for at this time dear Visitor... If ye would
like to know more of this dwarven warrioress, search her out, if not in
battle I shall answer thee. 

::Turns around and gently awakens her mate with a soft kiss as they
ready themselves for the eve's battles::
