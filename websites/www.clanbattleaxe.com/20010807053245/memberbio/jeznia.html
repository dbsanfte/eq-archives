Jeznia's Biography

To my brothers and sisters of the Clan Battle Axe, I give this evidence of my intention and good faith, may love guide you:

I wish to tell you in this letter how the paths of my life have led me to the road I travel and to insure with you that my intent is sound and good, in faith to you my brothers and sisters.

I come from a poor, but good tribe; that of the Labeel'Sca. As has always been for as long as anyone among the Labeel'Sca could remember, lots were cast to decide who would go in service to the gods and goddess. My people worship the gods and goddesses of the Triumvirate of Water and all their allied gods and goddesses, but our primary homage is to Karana, the Rainkeeper, and the Twin god and goddess, Mithaniel Marr, the TruthBringer, and Erollisi Marr, Queen of Love. Every ten full movements of the twelve months a lot is cast and one child is chosen, by the rule of the lots, of the eldest child of a mated pair of the tribe for each of the three primary gods or goddess. This child's life is given in service to one of the gods or goddess. Gender is not considered, but age, from age of weaning to just under the age of maturity, by two full movements of the twelve months. (Usually 5 to 10 years old.)

I was aged six when I went to live as an initiate of Erollisi Marr. My father had entered our tent and with mingled tears of joy and sorrow told me that my lot had fallen to the Lady of Love. I would leave the next day to serve the Lady for as long as she wanted me. So after packing what few personal items I owned, the tribal elders sent my father, along with two other chosen children, and their fathers, to Freeport, the closest city to the desert lands our tribes wandered. I was left at the temple of Erollisi Marr to learn, live and represent the Labeel'Sca as an initiate.

I was the only child of my father; my mother long ago having passed into the next life, and as I stood in the courtyard of the temple I cried. It was an honor to be chosen, certainly, but I was sad to leave my father and people as is natural for a child. My father kissed my forehead and wished me well and as I watched him go the priestess who stood beside me leaned down to whisper, "He will always be in your heart, young one, and you in his." I learned that day, and over the course of years, that those who love do not leave but are always a part one of the other. And if I learned in my young age the value of survival and helping those of the tribe so that all may survive, in my age after, I learned love in service to Erollisi Marr. I learned over the years the value of compassion, of giving of your self, your possessions, your talents, of the gift of healing, and I learned of the love between a man and woman, a parent and child, a brother to sister and sister to brother, and a friend to friend, as well as the love and forgiveness that is bourn to a repentant enemy. Though I saw my father again before his death, the priests and priestesses of Erollisi Marr had become a family to me, and a part of my heart, as my father always will be.

Once I had finished my initiation, I longed to see my people again, and I visited my tribe as was my desire. The people were joyful and greeted me with open arms. And though the love of Erollisi Mar is always with me and of it I freely give, I no longer felt as one of the Labeel'Sca. A chapter of my life had ended. A new one was to begin.

It was on my return back to Freeport from the tents of the Labeel'Sca that I heard the whisperings of word of the clan of Battle Axe. I began to dream and see portents that pointed me to the clan and shortly after I met Draewyn of Clan Battle Axe. I believe that m'Lady goddess was calling me to Clan Battle Axe and answer her call I must. I know not all her reasons, but she always leads me in nobility and love, and I welcome the path she asks me to take.

I am happy to be a part of the Clan of Battle Axe and I welcome it's friendship and familial love.

Your sister of Clan Battle Axe,
Jeznia d'al Labeel'Sca
Cleric of Erollisi Marr, Queen of Love
