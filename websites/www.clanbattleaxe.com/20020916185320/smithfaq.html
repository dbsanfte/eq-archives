FAQs
What is the difference between 
*Underfoot Plate, *Brellium Plate, *Dwarven Plate, *Enchanted Dwarven Plate & 
*Imbued Dwarven Plate? 		     Underfoot Plate is the newest armor known to the Dwarves.  It is created by forging armor with Brellium, Velium, Rubies (Blessed by priests of Brell Serilis) & Blue Diamonds (to name the most expensive components) and can only be worn by worshippers of Brell Serillis. 
     Brellium Plate is also made by forging Brellium, Velium & Blue Diamonds together.  Unlike the Underfoot Plate, this armor does not need to be worn by a worshipper of Brell Serilis.
     Dwarven Plate is non-magical armor made solely from Brellium.  Non-magical Dwarven Plate does not need to be worn by worshippers of Brell Serilis.
     Enchanted Dwarven Plate is made from Brellium that has been enchanted by an Enchanter/Enchantress. Enchanted Dwarven Plate does not need to be worn by a worshipper of Brell Serilis, much like the regular Dwarven Plate.
     Imbued Dwarven Plate is also made from Brellium that has been made magical, but it is also created by forging a ruby, blessed by a priest of Brell Serilis, into each piece of armor. Imbued Dwarven Plate is the most powerfully enchanted of the three Dwarven Plate sets.
Who can wear the Dwarven cultural armor?

     Underfoot Plate, Brellium Plate and all versions of Dwarven plate can be donned by pure-blooded Dwarves and their Gnomish & Halfling cousins who follow the paths of Clerics, Warriors and Paladins. (As a side note, Gnomish Shadowknights are capable of wearing the Brellium Plate as well.)  The Underfoot and Imbued version of Dwarven plate can only be donned by pure-blooded Dwarves or their Gnomish & Halfling cousins whom follow in the footsteps of Brell Serilis.

[Underfoot & all 3 Dwarven Plates: Dwf, Hfl, Gnm / War, Clr, Pal]
[Brellium Plate: Dwf, Hfl, Gnm / War, Clr, Pal, Shd]

     Those that have chosen the path of a Warrior (or Shadowknight) would benefit greatly from the Brellium and Enchanted Plate. While those that have chosen the path of a Paladin or Cleric of Brell Serilis would reap the greatest benefit from the Underfoot and Imbued Plate due to it's additional capacity to enhance your spell casting ability since it is blessed by a priest of Brell Serilis.     

Can this armor be blessed to another diety?      The plate armor created by a master Dwarven smith can be imbued by either Brell Serilis or Bristlebane.
Are you interested in purchasing components for your smithing?      Yes, I am interesting in purchasing Blue Diamonds, Lava Rocks and fully made Dwarven Tempers. I am not interested in purchasing rubies or drops of mercury at this time.

Blue Diamonds - 1,500/gem or 30,000pp/stack
Lava Rocks - 50pp/per OR 1000pp/stack
Dwarven Tempers - 50pp/per OR 1000pp/stack

     Yes, the platinum value of the Blue Diamonds, Lava Rocks or Tempers turned in can be credited towards armor.

Will you click combine for quest items if I bring all the components?      Yes I will combine quest pieces for 500pp per success.  I highly recommend bringing 3x the necessary components in the event of a trivial (or non-trivial) failure.
Will you click combine on weapons or armor items if I provide all the components?      Yes I will combine weapons & armor for 5,000pp per success.  However, if you require my services beyond just clicking combine for the final product, this price will change. I highly recommend bringing 3x the necessary components in the event of a trivial (or non-trivial) failure.
