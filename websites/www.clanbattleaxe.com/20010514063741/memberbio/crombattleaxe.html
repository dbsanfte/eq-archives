Crombattleaxe's Biography

Bah! I, Crombattleaxe, feel no need to explain myself to you! Suffice to
say I am a dwarven Paladin of Kaladim and a proud follower of Brell Serillis.

Through many years, I have mastered the art of slaying undead - which are
the epitome of all that is evil in this land. I fight to keep the evil banished!
This is a lifetime job!

I have been handed the opportunity to lead this Clan against the evil that has
plagued Norrath and I have accepted this honor with great pride. This Clan
shall fight until we are unable to fight anymore!

Crombattleaxe
Leader of Clan Battle Axe
