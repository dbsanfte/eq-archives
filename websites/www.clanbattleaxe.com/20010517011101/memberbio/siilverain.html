Siilverain Britecloud's Biography
 

           

	Greetings fellow traveler and welcome.  Since I expect complete honesty, I will 
be honest in return. I do not usually take kindly to strangers for they are unknown and a 
possible threat.  We Druids tend to keep to ourselves and visit only with those we know 
and trust (not to mention all the natural creatures of the forest).  To begin a hopeful new 
friendship with you in honesty, I shall reveal some personal thoughts as well as some of 
my background that has made me who I am today.  Conversely, you will hopefully return 
the favor....

	Growing up in the wonderful city of Kelethin was a exhilarating experience.  
Feeling everything sway with the wind while sleeping and listening to the denizens of 
the forest through out the day and night.  I have always felt so close to those creatures 
of the forests and of course the the forest herself!  While walking through the forests of 
Greater Faydark, I always marveled at it beauty and yearned for the power that I always 
felt all around me.  

	Then came the day I was wondering through the forest (as I usually do) and 
came upon a great injustice!  An Orc, one of the most vile creatures in Greater Faydark, 
was beating a helpless wolf!  Now I have always felt closer to the wolves than the other 
creatures of the forest, so I of course wanted to help!  While helping my wolf friend battle
this Orc, I felt the power of the forest gather around me.  Then suddenly, the Orc burst 
into flames!  I was so shocked I almost dropped the mace my father's friend had made me!  
Of course, good prevailed and my wolf friend and I did overcome the vicious Orc.  Again,
I felt the power of the forest, yet this time it was different.  I laid my hands on my friend's 
wounds - and they HEALED!  I repeated the same motions on myself, felt the power 
gather and my wounds too were healed.  It was a wonderous day!

	Since that day, I have practiced cleansing the forests of unnatural creatures.  
Not just Greater Faydark's forests, I have since traveled worldwide to further the cleasing 
of the forests.  Unfortunetly, I am not always successfull in my attempts , but I realize 
everyone has this problem occasionally.  As my age increases, so does my ability to gather 
the forest's power to get what I need accomplished.  I also LOVE learning new languages. 
I love being able to converse with everyone in their native tongue.  I find that usually puts 
them more at ease and they are more willing to open up.

	Now, friend, it is your turn. Seek me out in the forests and we shall further our 
converstation. I look forward to meeting you!



          
Siilverain Britecloud
