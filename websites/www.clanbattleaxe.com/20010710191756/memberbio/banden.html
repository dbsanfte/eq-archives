Banden's Biography
Member in Training
 



	From day one life was precious to this dwarf.  He felt a calling like 
none other from deep with in that he could never understand.  His quite 
inquisitive manner made him the object of many jibs from those more
interested in the fighting guilds and he was forced to seek solace in the
temple.  Once there he found a great hunger satiated and soon was studying
diligently to become a member of the great guild that worshiped the Duke
of Bellows.  

	Upon coming of age he was inducted into the order and sent out into 
the world to do good and to fight the evil that was invading the lands around 
Kaladim.   He soon found that his training and calling had paid off as he 
could keep people alive and do mass damage to the force of undead that were 
present around the entrance to his city.  Soon he became curious and 
traveled further into the world and that was when he encountered his first 
orc.  

	A hidious creature that attacked without provocation and had to be 
smited.  He started to search for these acursed creatures and found that their 
cess pool was not far from his home land being kept at bay only by the elves that 
occupy that area.  From then until now he has fought to rid the lands of 
these awful creatures until which point some else comes along that requires 
his attention more.  

	Those in need and those who are hurt are his specialty.  If you are hurt and 
he can help he will.  Just call out and Banden will be there.



May the Duke of Bellows 
watch over you and bless 
you,  Banden
