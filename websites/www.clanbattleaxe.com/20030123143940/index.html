July 14th, 2002
     After 13 long and tedious months, the quest for the Lore of the Magi'Kot has come to an end for our beloved, Salenie Mithrilweaver.

    In the wee hours of the morning on July 13th, we gathered on the 7th isle of Sky in hopes of obtaining the Elemental Crown of Mastery. On the 2nd to last remaining inhabitant on the 7th isle, the Crown did indeed drop. Now all Salenie needed was the chaining of the Pegasus Cloak.

    Very early this morning, Jvani of *Absolute Madness* logs in ready to chain the cloak. Less than 30 minutes later, Salenie had completed her quest and is now the proud owner of her epic.

July 5th, 2002
     This night was certainly a lucky & joyous night for us....As per the ritual for the last month, each Friday night we have been venturing to the Plane of Hate in search of Magi P'tasa and his elusive Staff of Elemental Mastery: Earth.  As our Lord was pulling (Monks? Who needs monks?), our ranger Adaianu shouted, "Magi P'tasa has been spotted!".  We sent our rogue, Ilsidar, to locate him...As our luck would have it, Magi P'tasa was on the first floor in the church that Crom had *just* completed clearing and he was wielding the staff.  

     Crom pulls Magi and Magi does a quick death at the hand of our 12 member raiding party.  Salenie waited anxiously over the body of the dead mage to loot her staff.  Once the body opens up, we are surprised to hear that not only was Magi hoarding the staff, but also the Shattered Emerald of Corruption!  Adaianu was in shock for quite sometime :)

     Now Salenie is one drop away from wielding her epic...The Crown of Elemental Mastery is next on our plate. Congratulations to both of my wonderful clanmates in their drops tonight.  Definately deserving!
