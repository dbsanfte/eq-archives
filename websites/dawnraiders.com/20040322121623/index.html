15-2-2004 The Story of Fenin Ro!

So it has been 2 weeks now since the brave band of adventures, lead by one great person whos name we shall not mention here (just look at the botom of this post), killed solro and ventured into the elemental plane of fire. Its been 4 weeks even since they slayed that foul god Rallos Zek and well many felt it was time to slay another god. "Its been 2 weeks!" someone said with a loud voice. "aye" one replied, "2 weeks since we killed a god!". The adventurerers had been working hard slaying all kinds of stuff before, but most were minor things. "Well ok", said the fearless leader (look at bottom of post again), "lets kill us a Fenin Ro". And the band of friends moved into the plane of fire again where the tyrant would await them. The tyrant had put up some defenses tho in the form of birds, big frogs, giant spiders and even demons but nothing could stop these adventureres. They went past the first defense wall into the second castle. Here at a table they took a brake to eat first tho as its unhealthy to slay gods on an empty stomach. Then they started a war on the giant more deamons birds spiders and other trash launches at them, even other bands of adventurerers came laughing with them thinking they would never succeed. Fenin himself thought he was safe but was he?

At this point the band of adventureres was scared at the sight of fenin ro, many wanted to run to their mommy, but the fearless leader said " Hold we came this far lets kill the basterd!" (bottom of post again). The result was soon known 1 dead god and no dead dawnraiders.

Shouts of congratualtions came waving thru norrath as these proud men and women walked out of the plane of fire wearing these wierd shields.

A big gratz to everyone there and just as a reminder there are still some spots open to join one of the finest guilds on EQ. Cya soon for the story of xegony the bitch with too big boobies!
