In order to become a member of the guild, the following procedure has to be met.

1. An existing Clan Bloodclaws member must bring you up for consideration.  In order to make a request for consideration, that member has to provide a background history as to what experiences he/she has with you and detailing why you should become a member of the guild.

2. Before your request to join will be voted on by the member base, 4 other members must vouch for your quality and integrity before the guild, stating why you should become a member of Clan Bloodclaws.

3. Once the above requirements have been met, your application will be brought before the guild at which time a vote of yes or no will take place.  If one member declines the application with a given reason, the application will be vetoed until such time as you have been able to resolve your issues with that member and that member grants you consideration into the guild, at which time the voting process will take place again.

4.  Once the voting process is concluded, you will be guilded by the first available officer, at which time you will be granted access to all Clan Bloodclaws resources.
