Ingredients

  • 6 tablespoons olive oil
  • 4 cups chopped onion
  • 7 to 8 cups (packed) mixed bitter greens, washed, dried, and coarsely chopped (1 medium bunch spinach & 1 medium bunch escarole works well)
  • salt to taste
  • 3/4 to 1 1b. penne, fusilli, shells, or another kind of short pasta
  • 1/2 to 3/4 lb. feta cheese, crumbled
  • freshly grated parmesan cheese, to taste (optional)
  • freshly ground black pepper
  1. Heat the olive oil in a deep skillet or Dutch oven. Add the onions and cook for about 10 minutes over medium heat, stirring occasionally. Meanwhile, start the pasta water.
  2. Add chopped greens to the skillet, salt lightly, and stir until the greens begin to wilt. Cover and cook 10 to 15 minutes over medium-low heat.
  3. Cook the pasta until al dente. Just as it becomes ready, add the crumbled feta cheese to the sauce. (Keep the heat on low as you add the cheese.)
  4. When the pasta is done, strain and add to the potful of sauce. Mix thoroughly.
  5. Cook the completed dish over low heat for a few minutes. Add a small amount of parmesan and a generous amount of freshly ground black pepper. Serve immediately.
