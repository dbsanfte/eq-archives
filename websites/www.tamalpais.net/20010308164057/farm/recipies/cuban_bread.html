Ingredients

  • 1 pkg. active dry yeast
  • 2 1/4 c. lukewarm water
  • 1 tbsp. sugar
  • 1 1/2 tbsp. salt
  • 6-7 c. all-purpose flour
  • 1 tbsp. cornmeal
  • 1/2 stick melted butter
Dissolve yeast in lukewarm water. Stir in sugar and salt, mixing well. Use wooden spoon to blend in 1 c. flour. Beat thoroughly w/ spoon, add another c. flour, and beat again. Continue adding flour, 1 c. at a time, until dough is smooth. (Knead last few cups if it becomes hard to stir.) Knead for 5 mins. Cover & let rise until double (1 1/2 hours). Lightly flour pastry board. Place dough on it and divide in half. Shape each into tapered loaf. Sprinkle baking sheet w/ cornmeal. Cover & allow to rise for 5 mins. Score tops w/ sharp knife; brush lightly w/ water. Put loaves in oven before turning on heat.

Place a small pain of boiling water on bottom of oven (important!). Bake at 400 for 45 min. Crust should be browned & crunchy when done.
