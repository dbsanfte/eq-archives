Ingredients

  • 4 salmon steaks (take the bones out!)
Mix and pour over steaks:
  • 1/2 c. apricot jam
  • 1/2 c. balsamic vinegar
  • 2 tsp. horseradish
Bake at 375 in a lightly oiled baking pan for 15-25 minutes, depending on thickness of steaks. (Sauce makes it less likely for salmon to dry out, so don't be afraid to leave it in.) Test w/ fork for doneness.
