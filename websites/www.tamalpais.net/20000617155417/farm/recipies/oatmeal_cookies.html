Ingredients

  • 1 c. (2 sticks) butter, softened
  • 1 c. firmly packed brown sugar
  • 1/2 c. granulated sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 tsp. vanilla
  • 1 1/2 c. all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp. baking soda
  • 1 tsp. ground cinnamon
  • 1/2 tsp. salt (optional)
  • 3 c. oats (Quaker is good)
  • 1 c. raisins (I don't put these in, but some people like them this way)
Heat oven to 350.
Beat butter & sugars until creamy. Add eggs & vanilla; beat well. Combine flour, baking soda, cinnamon, & salt; add to butter mixture, mixing well.* Stir in oats** (and raisins, if you're using them) and drop by rounded tablespoons onto ungreased cookie sheet. Bake 10 to 12 minutes until light golden brown. (Less done cookies will be very chewy -- this is ideal.) Cool 1 min. on cookie sheet; remove to wire rack. Store in airtight container.
Makes about 4 doz. 2 1/2" cookies, unless you eat some of the dough.

*This is a good time to sample some of the dough.
**This is another good time to sample some more dough.
