The top of the page
  Hi, and welcome to my home page. If you've found your way here, I can assume one of two things: either a), you know me personally, or b), you're really bored and have no idea where you are. If the answer is a), I don't have to explain much, but if you fall under the b) category, you're probably wondering why this page is here, why I made it, and whether it can do anything to make your life better. Sadly, I don't think I can help you with the latter.
  This site was born out of my experiences wandering around the vacuous daisy chain of the Internet, usually finding nothing, but occasionally finding something pretty good. I hope to create a time-saving collection of links for those looking for quality on the web, as well as provide some content of my own.
