say (za LON eet za)
Really, really bad for you. Serbian food similar to Spanakopita, but better! This is a Malden family recipie. As with all Malden recipies, quantities are approximate (this has never really been written down).

Filling:

  • 3 pkg. frozen chopped spinach (drained)
  • 1 carton cottage cheese
  • 2 lbs. fresh spinach, chopped (all water gone)
  • 1 brick jack cheese, grated
  • 6-8 eggs (it just keeps getting better)
  • 2/3 tbsp. sour cream
  • 1/2 stick melted butter
  • Grated onion
  • Nutmeg
You also need:
Phyllo dough

Mix all ingredients together to make filling. Spread over 4 sheets of phyllo that have each been buttered w/ melted butter. (Make this in a big casserole dish.) Put 4 more phyllo sheets on top w/ butter on each layer as well. Bake at 350 for 45 minutes. Cut into squares and serve.
