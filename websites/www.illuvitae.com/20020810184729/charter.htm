Illuvitae Charter

The following is an OOC (Out of Character) description of our guild's goals, purpose, and policies.  The roleplaying description can be found on our main page.

Illuvitae is a loosely roleplayed guild on Vallon Zek.  It is made up solely of the Elven team.  Unlike other "all-elf" guilds, Illuvitae actively tries to keep the Elven territories free of foreigners through PvP means.  Also unlike many other "PK" guilds, Illuvitae has strict policies governing its members' conduct.  The following policies must be adhered to by ALL members.  If anyone believes an Illuvitae member has violated any of these policies, please contact Tulweyen or Darstiff in game, or at tulweyen@illuvitae.com.

Illuvitae Policies and Rules

(1)  Race:  All members must be of the Elven team:   Wood Elf, High Elf, or Half Elf

(2)  Level Minimum:  To join  Illuvitae, your character must be level 15.  The purpose of this rule is to keep complete "newbies" out of the guild.  There is nothing wrong with new players, but Illuvitae tends to be a high profile guild, and we want our members to have at least SOME experience before joining.

(3)  KOS Policies:  Anyone not on the Elven team is KOS to us.  We are the only purist Elven guild on the server, and as such, we have no allies.  If they are in range and able to be hit, they are KOS.  Buffing other races is only permitted when the person is on a CR - as an OOC courtesy.  Binding other races is a personal decision, but may never be done in Felwithe, or Greater Faydark. 

(4)  Grouping:  The Elven race is in all ways superior, and has no need to ever group with anyone of another race.  Grouping with other races is strictly prohibited.  Grouping for the purposes of binds or teleports may be done, at the players discretion, but ONLY if this for the purpose of generating income, and is not in Elven lands.  Aid should only be given to other races if it benefits the Elven team somehow, such as in paid teleports.

(5)  Trading:  Trading with other races can bring benefit to the Elven Kingdom.  Therefore, it is the choice of each member as to whether they want to trade with other races or not.

(6)  LnS:  Loot and Scoot policies are enforced within the guild.  What this means, is that after you have been defeated in PvP battle, you must loot your corpse and leave the area.  Meditating, talking, or any other activity after you have looted your corpse is not permitted.  You can zone, camp, or play another character if you wish.   After vanquishing a foe in PvP battle, you may kindly request that they LnS as well.  If they refuse to loot their body, or refuse to scoot after looting, they may be killed again.

LnS rules apply if you are engaged in PvP battle, and die for ANY reason.  For instance, if you are engaged in PvP but your actual death occurs by falling, drowning, mob hits, or any other reason, you still must scoot.

A general guideline for the length of your "scoot" is 60 minutes.  However, if the victors have left the zone, they are obviously not concerned with "holding" it, and you may return.  Also if your killers have been kiklled themselves, you may return.  Please use good judgment in this area, and bow out gracefully when you have been defeated.

(7)  PvP Conduct:  This is Illuvitae's MOST IMPORTANT RULE.  It is also the hardest to enforce and describe.  Basically, we want to earn the fear, but also the respect of our enemies.  Therefore, every effort should be made to act in a mature and dignified matter while engaging in PvP.  Trash talking, and profanity of any type is strictly not permitted in the ooc and shout channels, as well as in personal tells to your enemies.  

Obvious, immature PvP behavior is prohibited.  Exploits, using trade windows improperly, LoS exploits, or other unsportsmanlike conduct should not take place.

Please go here if you feel you need a lesson in Vallon Zek PvP attitude... ;)

(8)  Immortal and Out of Range Healing:  As a guild, we are here to help each other.  Healing fellow elves is encouraged.  Healing or buffing OUTSIDE of direct conflict is always allowed.  However, to keep everything fair, we enforce specfic rules about healing during pvp.

It is against <Illuvitae>'s policy to NOT OOR or immortal heal, regardless of whether or not they are the attacker OR defender. OOR/immortal healing is not to be done by any <Illuvitae> member. The ONLY exception to this rule is against guilds or people that continue to immortal/oor heal against us

(9)  Twinking:  If someone wants to twink their Illuvitae character, that is their personal decision.  However, EXTREMELY unbalancing equipment on a low level character is not allowed during pvp.  Again, the purpose of this is so as not to ruin the gaming experience of other people.  Being killed by unbeatable opponents, due to their equipment is not fun.

(10) Guild Tags and Grouping:  Guild tags should be worn at all times, unless there is a specific and urgent need to turn them off.  Acceptable reasons to turn off your guild tag would be during a sneak attack of an enemy.  Unacceptable reasons would be because you are "just trying to get exp".   Be proud of your heritage. If you want the safety of XP without pvp, this is neither the guild nor server for you. 

These policies are subject to change.  If you would like to propose a change to Illuvitae's charter, please send an email to tulweyen@illuvitae.com or post within our private message board forum.
