Baronesse Piterpater Drizzlepaw

once there was an inquisitive wood elf womanchild. Before she could even walk her time was spent in the woods around Kelethin. Her mother would often let her roam for hours as the child was a hellion indoors - but in the woods the baby would forage and smile all day.

The child had become friends with the wolves that inhabited the area. She had become such a common sight among the wolf litters that the wolves had stopped thinking of her as an outsider at all. They dubbed her Pitterpatter, as she made the sound of both wolf paw and rainfall. If you weren't looking the right way you wouldn't know she was around till she bit your ankle!

The child knew more wolf speach than elven when the woods grew dark. Wolf and Elf alike had begun being hunted by outlanders. The woodland creatures that were both brave and hardy took to paw for friendlier earth. Piter, not questioning her wolfhood, took flight with her pack. The journey was long, and Piter took her place with the hunters as her limbs grew strong.

One day the struggle was suddenly over. Having crossed a terrible dessert, the entire pack was slaughtered by a hunting party of lizards. Only the speed of her kinsman Brokenfang brought her out of range of the lizards spears, but ultimately they were cornered. They turned to make their last stand, eyes glowing with the outrage of their dead kin and wisdom that these next acts would be their last.

Or so it seemed. The lizardmen fumbled around awhile, seemingly uninterested even when growled at. In short order they organized and scampered back toward the original battlefield - for pelts and meat no doubt. Piter had found her calling, and could now go where she wanted unnoticed when she wanted too.

And so time passed, the pair passing through camps taking what they wished and hunting in the swamplands. Piter cared for her kinsman through his declining years, and then was alone with the scaly and the fleshless creatures of the swamp.

The swamps were a hostile place. The only standing law was that the big ate the small, if they could catch them. The biggest were the Ogres, they were often the fastest also. Fortunately for Piter, they respected the way of the wolf - fast moving hunters. The Ogres were basically mobile food processing units - Piter suspected this similarity is what made wolves a welcome companion while catching lunch rather than a nice side with lunch. Unless lunch was hard to find. NEVER trust an Ogre, their feelings don't extend much beyond 2 things - ME hungry, ME sleepy. Piter, who simply was a wolf - albeit a clever and magical one - found an uneasy peace with the Ogres. She could sleep safe in some corners of their defended city, and the Ogre hunters would fight over who got to take the wolf hunting with them. Whoever did invariably brought home the big game, and a bag of bright shiny loot besides.

One day, an Ogre returned from a long journey. An educated Ogre of unusual strength and purpose. Not a hunter, but a conqueror. An Ogre with aspirations. He strode up to the hunting parties collecting outside Oggok. Seeing the tracker wolf, he strode up to the Ogre nearest it and without a word smashed his face with his glowing shield, rendering the surprised Ogre helpless. 'Wolf is mine now.' And that was how things were settled in Oggok.

The Ogre left immediately, heading back into the dessert. This time it wasn't about survival though, it was about slaughter. Apparently the Ogre had an issue with Orcs, and he pressed them relentlessly. They left behind a bloody trail of mostly Orcs and unfortunate wanderer. Its important to understand that Piter didn't fear the Ogres. This one in particular had risked his own life to save hers, though his motivation was personal greed, still he was unlikely to cause her harm. Piter stayed with him primarily because her alliance with the Ogres was useful to her - even here in the dessert the fleshless creatures didn't take much notice of a wolf when there was a huge noisy war machine to worry about. In her world there had only been the big eat the small for a very long time. She had long since stopped caring about who ate who. Except maybe the marsh bears - there were so few animals. She had never led an Ogre to a Bear.

It came to pass one day, long after they had crossed the dessert and were back in field and forest, that they were preparing for war. The Ogre wanted something from inside a Gnoll cave, and there were some peoples outside it he felt were worth looting. Piter cared little for the motivations. She had never even spoken to an Ogre - she was a wolf after all. She hadn't spoken at all since Brokenfang left her, actually.

The Ogre charged the unsuspecting trio. He liked to kill the biggest first so the rest would run in fear. Piter enjoyed another minute of sunshine before coming over the ridge to see if old 'ME BIG!' needed any help. The much smaller opponents were putting up a good fight, the battle would last another minute or two. Too her surprise though, as she approached, one of the Peoples saw her and exclaimed 'Please! We need your help!'. She recognized the language, from long long ago. Nobody had ASKED anything of her since Brokenfang so many years ago. Ogres only gave commands. Another word came to mind from long forgotten memory, in a language she had barely known.

'Kinsmen'.

The ogre shifted and, with a mighty bellow, swept the Elves aside with a wide swing of his Naginata, turning to face Piter. 'HEAL ME!'. But it was the ice cold eyes of a Wood Elf he met, carrying all the compassion of a wolf sizing up their prey. Those eyes widened a little. She felt no pity, no fear, no obligation. Nothing at all really, just that her nature called upon her take action, and so she would. This may be the day she returned to the earth, but that had always been the way of nature.

The Elven Warrior marveled at the scene. Never had he seen such an expression cross Elven features before. In some way, this Druids calm, placid expression was more fearsome even then the gigantic ogre.

'ME NOT PREY!'

But Tunare's Earth had already claimed his feet.

ME! NOT!

But the Elven Warrior had already recovered his Blade.
