Some clarification about Luvitea

We drop items on luvitae for one purpose, sharing items with other guildies. Sometimes, items just can't be sold, either because they are too common, or just too plain cheap. Sometimes, you find a very good deal and you decide to buy it and know someone will need that item later.

Free items on Luvitae are marked at 500 000pp. Anything higher then that means the item is for sell to guildies at (price - 500 000). For example, there is currently 2 spells i put on Luvitae i bought 50pp. I put the price at 500 050pp. So people from the bazaar won't buy it, and you know you can have it at a low price of 50pp when you ask me or Luvitea.
