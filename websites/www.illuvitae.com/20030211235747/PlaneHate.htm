Illuvitae - Hate
Preparation guide by Sylveron

Rules of thumb:

1.  When porting up to hate, invis before hand and ensure the wizards have plenty of hate stones available from neriak's 3rd gate in the spires of innoruuk (the cleric guild).

2.  Once you port up, if you are not attacked by an npc (undead may see you and attack), then move out of the entry building to the sharply inclining wall leading to the zoneline.  Move halfway up this wall, turn right, then run to the corner just ahead (the se corner), then continue just past the sw corner (moving clockwise around the zone).  Just past the sw corner you gather and move down as one to take on the first few mobs that aggro.

3.  If attacked while moving to the camp spot, stop and die.  If you're a cleric, root it and move a ways from the mob and try to camp out (we'll need a cleric camped out at all times if possible just in case).

4.  If you go afk, tell the grp you're in.  If the raid moves, everyone must move as one, leaving no one behind.

5.  Lag reduction:   Shut down all particle effects, /shownames 1, turn off other's attacks and others spell effects and bard songs.  Get everything that slows down your comp turned off.   Turn off your sky and your clipping plane.  We can't afford to lose someone in a raid from lag, as everyone has a job to do.

6.  Listen:   Someone will handle cash items, someone else will call out loots, another will be managing pulls and one will oversee things to ensure it all hangs together.  We'll have to pay attention, so as not to miss important instructions.  Instructions such as "Pull away from the mob so it comes down from the wall."  If there's poor listening, people die.  People die, their morale drops and the thin skinned ones begin to look for a way to log off and then we dont' get the big mobs dropped or get to the 2nd floor or get a shot at dropping Innoruuk.  Pay attention, people live longer, die less and we have more of our resources focused on the task and our odds to succeed improve dramatically.  Since we'll have just elves, it is paramount that everyone listens and reacts quickly to what they're told.  There is always a learning curve to ramp up when folks raid together the first time, so don't worry about it if you mess up.  But mess up the same things time
and again without any sign of learning or effort or improvement, then you get a translocation to bind point.

7.  Be on time:   The time is set to ensure we get up there before some other guild does.  If we have cleared the first floor in a few hours, and someone ports up for a look at who's in teh zone, then they won't want to come if we've taken their drops.   Unless they just want to pvp for the hell of it.  If its at 9am, Pacific, in Southern Felwithe, then be there before 9am.  We have to see who's on hand of the various classes, form up the groups and start porting up and then rezzings the dead.   Fast mobilization and departure are the key ingredients for this raid and any others you may do in your future.  Now is a good time to make being on time a habit.   If we haven't the classes needed to raid in a reasonable window around the start time, then the raid is called off.  Sounds harsh and it is, but if people are late there has to be a consequence for wasting everyone's time who was one time.

8.  CH chain.(Complete Heal chain)   If we go after Innoruuk, we will have to have a complete heal chain.  Druids or clerics, either or both can do this.  A main tank engages Innoruuk after the ch chain has begun. Clerics have to have FT gear if possible plus a mod rod.  They will have 2 hot keys:  one for fast heals, one for slow.  Prior to Innoruuk being slowed, the fast ch chain hot key is used, afterwards, the slow is used.   Here is how you'll set up your hot keys for the ch chain, line by line. 

First the fast CH hotkey:

/cast 1
/pause 30
/rs Inc FAST CH on %t !!!!!
/rs Landyin you have next CH, CAST NOW!!!


Next the slow CH hotkey:

/cast 2
/pause 30
/rs Inc SLOW CH on %t !!!!!
/rs Landyin you have next CH, CAST NOW!!!

The MT engages until aggro is achieved.  Then the MT calls in the melees to engage.   The melees set up at max melee range, testing the limit for out of range to hit the mob to ensure they are as far from it as possible.  If hit, they will stop attacking for 10 seconds, back away a few steps, then return, then start again.  Its key that you not take aggro from the MT or our dps and mana usage begin to suck hind tit fast.   Lots of melees never ever learn how to do this and they die from over aggro over and over and over because they're too stupid to listen and follow instructions.  When Innoruuk enrages, all melee turn off attack, all pet owners back off their pets.  You wait til enrage passes, then attack again.  Don't worry about rampage tanking for Inny.  Everyone has a job to do, even the melees who no longer can hit autoattack and push their thumbs up their ass til the mob drops.  The MT or raid leader may direct the melees to position to push the mob in a certain direction (eg. "We're getting to close to those dragon constructs, melees push the mob away from them!")  You have to pay attention.  See item number 6. on listening.

You need 4 clerics for Innoruuk (or druids also, but clerics ch is best). 
You need to land slow on Innoruuk.  If you land slow on him, he is dead!!!

9.  Pulling.   We've done careful pulling for low levels on hate raids, and just straight up plowed our way to the 2nd floor to take what we want.  Depending on what we have on hand, we'll adopt a strategy that will work for us. 

My favorite method (I don't know why) is to have 3 enchanters on the wall ahead of the raid force while bringing a train of mobs on the first floor.  The enchanters mez like a giant ass catcher's mitt for the train.  In a very rapid time, the first floor is cleared, everyone's pockets are loaded with cash items and new gear and we head to the 2nd floor.Careful pulling. 

We had Tulweyen scout a mob, Vealok assist to target and harmony the surrounding mobs, then pull the mob.  Hate is outdoor so harmony works.  Hate is highly suitable for elves to raid.  This method works well if you have a small or lower level raiding force.  Its damn safe.

10.  Key classes:   Clerics, enchanters, rogues, wizard.Clerics - Your CH takes the MT to full health, reducing the risk for death and the mana cost.  Depending on your level, your fast heals, group heals and buffs are very useful.  The CH chain though, is where no raid can succeed without you.  Druids can step in, but not as well since their CH isn't 100%.  Druids can do many other things to contribute though, but this
is when clerics begin to shine.

Enchanters - No shaman for elves, but your slow isn't very far below the almighty shaman's.  Kei, crack the speed the ever important mezzing of big nasty pulls are where everyone soon learns who the good enchanters are and, once in awhile, who the really great enchanters are.  Sometimes an enchanter
has to learn the hard way, but eventually emerges from a hate raid on top of their class.   Hate is where enchanters really shine.

Rogues - Pulling, dragging corpses and damage per second (dps) are essentials brought by the rogue.  Hate means wipeouts typically, and rogues have to be there to drag people to the camped out cleric to rez.  Rogues save everyone from having to take a bite from a shit sandwich over and over (porting up naked for CR = not fun and expensive).   Rogues will save your bacon in many ways, you have to have them for hate breaks in case groups get wiped out.  Sometimes justthe rogues survive the break and drag all the corpses.  Everone on the raid ought to know who the rogues are to /consent <rogue name here> whenever it gets ugly.

Wizard - You can't go to hate without a wizard.  You have to have a wizard to port up.  If you allow stragglers on the raid, they continue to shuttle people up who arrive late.  No wizard armor drops in hate, so compensate them with any other useable wizard items that may drop since they get no planar robes, arms, boots, etc..   Take care of them, you need them to shatter the fabric of space and time again and again, to transport everyone to the outer plane of hate. /roleplay OFF
