Wartime Charter
Time to get the big guns out

You have been mean to us or one of us! We can be mean as well.

It has been reported that this charter might have been against the game policies. Has it wasn't our intent to go against them,  i had to correct section (4) to be respecting those rules. The original writer not having a full grasp of the rules at that time, and myself that let that part slip trought, we felt the need to bring the corrections in order to really represent what the wartime charter is.

War time Illuvitae Policies and Rules

(1)  Race: We are Elvin Purists, and as such, we have made, and will continue to make enemies.  We will never group with Non-Elves, we do however, recognize that our enemies have enemies, and as such may cooperate with non-elves without grouping.  This means if you have made our Declared  Enemies List*, wewill use your enemies against you.  If we see you in a group and feel outnumbered, we may call on others, Elves and non-elves alike, to vanquish you.  If we initiate a Battlefield Truce with another race it means we can cooperate with those individuals to put down our common enemy, and for one hour after the battle the truce remains.  Which means that we do not have to immediately engage with each other after our enemy is downed.

(2)  KOS Policies:   

KoS-All non-elf team Races in Greater Feydark and Felwithe are KoS, these same races are 'Kill When Able' outside Greater Feydark and Felwithe.  We are not a suicide squad however, which means that stalking our enemies until they are weakened, and then attacking is perfectly acceptable.   Using Hit-n-Run tactics, like Concentrating Fire on a particular enemy, like a healer while in group, is a prime target, and should be exploited while main Tank is off pulling, but before MoB is upon the enemy party, is a  perfectly good and acceptable Wartime strategy.  We Elves are not the 'Chilvarous Knights' human's claim as honorable warriors, we are Guerilla Warriors, with our own form of Honor

(3)  LnS:  LnS- 'Loot-n-Scoot' applies when engaged with any honorable enemy.  If you or your guild has earned your way onto our Declared Enemies List* than you must PK every Illuvitae member in a group, before any of them must LnS.   Those Illuvitae members defeated may not re-enter the current PvP battle, however if that individual is attacked by you or your guild while CRing you will face concequences.  You are not honorable, or you would not be on this list, so there will be no quarter asked or givin.   If you are an honorable person our policy of LnS remains the same as always, defeated Illuvitae members will LnS for one hour, regardless of the fact that you might not have defeated us all. If we win the battle than you you wold do well to LnS, because not doing so will earn your name on our Declared Enemies List*

(4)  Immortal and Out of Range Healing:  Immy Healing and OOR healing are one and the same to all
Illuvitae members.  We do not engage in these practices except against those who use it against us.  If you OOR or Immy against an Illuvitae member we will re-pay that favor in kind.  If you are an elf and Immy then of course you leave us few options, so when you get hunted down by darkies we hired, no need to cry foul, cause you will not recieve any mercy, as a traitor to your race you deserve to be hunted down those of us who are able to slay you.

(5)  Twinking:  Twinking is a dishonorable practice in PvP, and as such Illuvitae does not use twinks in PvP against honorable opponents.  If you are on our Declared Enemies List* then this courtesy does not include you, regardless whether you Twink or not.  You have commited crimes againt Illuvitae to be on the list so any equipment is acceptable to use against you.

(6) Corpse Camping: CCing is a practice that is done to prevent defeated opponents from reclaiming thier possessions, and is not done unless you do not LnS after you have been defeated, or are trash talking us for PvPing you on a PvP server.  If you are on our Declared Enemies List* you may be CCed at any time for up to 1 hour per violation against us.  Which means if you are repeatedly offensive you may be CCed for many hours, even having our guild take shifts to do so.

(7) Griefing: Greifing is not tolerated, and may result in you being CCed, refer to Article Six.
