Amathar

Part 1

As a child, Amathar was very precocious, and had showed latent talent for the Arcane Arts. He had a wisdom beyond his years, as compared to most other elves his age. Though somewhat jovial at times, he would often times remove himself from others deeply immersing himself in books, on history, geography, literature, poetry and other things. Indeed there were very few topics that he didn't find time to persue, but he mostly studied books written on the arcane secrets of Magecraft.
His devotion to learning did not go un-noticed by the local Magician's guild either. At a very young age, he was apprenticed to Guildmistress Niola Impholder. Niola found the young elf a delightful and apt pupil, whom she doted on. Amathar came into his own, as a mage, and soon was mastering all the training she could pass onto him.
After a time the Guildmistress wanted to begin testing Amathar, and decided that it was to time for him to put his talent to work. She directed him to go out and meet an associate of the guild to get directions on constructing his very own Art Keepers Uniform.
Amathar went out and met with this guild associate and was soon out in the forest of Greater Feydark, seeking out the materials he needed to construct the uniform.
The forest of Greater Feydark is a dangerous land, where the Orc's of Clan Crushbone are at war with the elves. It is also a battleground where Dwarves, Humans, Ogres, Trolls, and others come to seek the destruction of the many Elves who make thier lives here. The Elves meanwhile being a stalwart race and a bastion of honor, engage thier enemies almost daily. Elven society, however, has endured within this dangerous land despite its many hardships.
It was during an excursion within this forest that Amathar met and fell in love with another High Elf, Jasmarillith, the Enchantress.
He had been out for days hunting and engaging with the Elvin enemies, when he came upon the young enchanting apprentice, whilst she was in a heated battle with a Dwarven warrior, who was fighting along side an Orc! Rushing to her aid, Amathar commanded his earth pet to attack the orc, while he concentrated on casting the most powerful offensive spells he had learned upon the Dwarven invader. The battle raged on for some time, but in the end Jasmarillith and Amathar prevailed against thier common foes. After the battle they introduced themselves to each other, and Amathar guided, the now lost, Jasmarillith back to her home in Felwithe. They were soon nearly inseparable, as the bond forged in blood soon bacame a bond of the heart.
Together they completed the quest not once but twice, once for each of them. It was during this time that Amathar first met the Venerable Tocap Val'dor, a great defender of the elvin lands. Amathar soon learned that this great warrior was the leader of an elite group of Elves who would combat the Elvin enemies, not only within this homeland, but take the fight to the enemy.
The young mage was still a novice adventurer, put wanted to prove himself to Tocap and the Elves of Illuvitae. Tocap had instructed Amathar on Illuvitae Philosophy, and showed him the elite guild's charter. It was a structered and well disciplined order with honor, and prestige. It was at this moment that the young mage had a catharsis. It was from this moment his life's void was filled, and he set out to live and if nessessary to die by the codes set out to him by his great friend, though he was not yet qualified to become a member of Illuvitae's illustrious ranks.
Amathar and his young companion set out to explore more of thier world together, to experience it many wonders, dangers, and peoples. They were never disappointed wherever they wenmt together, as adventure it seemed was all around them no matter where they travelled.
They came in time to a land known as the Butcherblock Mountians, the homeland of the Dwarves. It was here that the two companions found that they were quite a formidable team, and they learned to depend on each other, for a great many things. Amathar could provide food and drink, and some often needed bandages, while Jasmarillith found that she could negociate better prices for the items they collected to sell, she was also a tremendous help in combat, as she could distract thier enemies. Amathar was a good leader and under his direction they had many successful campaigns together.
Soon they found thier way to the docks in Butcherblock, and there while Jasmarillith was shopping Amathar encountered an Aquatic Goblin whose name is now forgotten.
He was sitting on the ground deeply immersed within its arcane secrets, when suddenly from behind he was ferociously attacked! His pet Kobaner the earth elemental quickly interceeded and engaged the foul smelling Aquatic Goblin, using his powerful earth magic Kobaner caused the goblin's feet to sink into the earth, rooting the foe in place.
Meanwile the mage jumped to his feet, cursing himself for not being more aware of his surroundings. He hustled a short distance and turned to see if he need to go get the guards to help with the foe, but instead he cast a flaming shield upon his pet, and then cast a powerful fire bolt on the goblin. It was looking bad and Kobaner was nearly dead, though the goblin was rooted in place he was nearly uninjured. It was then Amathar sat down to conserve some mana, and watched closely as his pet was being repeatedly pierced by the wicked spear that the vile goblin wielded with great proficiency. Amathar had a plan, but timing would be everything. Just then it happened, Kobaner, his trusted pet was slain. Amathar sat quietly looking on as the Aquatic Goblin pulled a hunter's bow and began firing a volley of arrows at the vulnerable mage. Then with purpose the mage stodd and began casting a summoning spell to replace his pet with a new manisfestation of elemantal earth, and soon he was directing his new pet to engage his enemy. A few spells of powerful fire damage later, and the powerful goblin went down. Amathar let out a sigh of relief, and walked over to inspect the humanoid to make sure he was indeed dead. Then, even though he had no use for it, he clamed the Riptide Spear the goblin wielded against him as a trophy. Later when he and Jasmarillith regrouped she bandaged Amathar's wounds, and she was relieved theat they were not serious. They then tracked down an encampment along the shore, where they together battled many more those fierce beasts.
That evening, as their battles with the goblins began to break up, they were set upon, and ambushed by an alliance force, consisting of Dwarves, Humans, and a Vah'Shir, where the two young elves were badly beaten and forced to withdraw from the battlefield.
After their defeat at the hands of thier allied enemies, the two elves journed back to thier homeland, wanting nothing more than to rest, recouperate, and tell tales of thier successes and thier narrow escape at the hands of thier enemies.
Fate, however, never rests and the young elves would soon learn this the hard way.

Part 2

It was supposed to be a mundane journey home, and after crossing enemy territory the two elves were back in thier homeland, Greater Feydark. The great forest was thier home, a place where elves could celebrate, dance, sing and be merry, and that is what Amathar was looking forward to. He had been away from his home for several weeks, and the time away had been hard. Onward they travelled deeper into the forest, heading for thier home in Felwithe.
It had started to rain, and the two elves hurried thier steps trying to get home before nightfall. Amathar indicated he knew a short cut, so they left the trail and followed a ridge fo a while. Then as they reaced the summit of the large hill, they happened upon two dwarves talking in thier native tongue. Knowing that these two dwarves were obvioulsy up to no good, Amathar approached them and switching to common tongue said "You dwarves look lost, I will escourt you to Felwithe so that you may pay your tribute to the Elvin Nations." They quickly denied that they were a tribute party, and that no self respecting Dwarf would pay an Elf any tribute al long as they were around.
"Then it is time you pay yor 'Safe Passage Tax', or perhaps you Dwarves would rather pay the 'Blood Toll, instead!" Amathar returned with an indignant and condescending tone.
With that the dwarves looked at each other and attacked, but it was to thier folly, far the Elvin duo put them down, though with some effort. Then Amathar liberated any coin the two were carrying, and the two elvin travellers continued on.
He should have known something was up, but the thought of returning home and a good meal near the fireplace was all he could think about. The rain had by this time stopped, and night was fast approaching. Amathar looked at his rain soaked companion and then ahead, "Not much farther, my love."
Then they were all around us, Dwarves, a Human, and a Pech. The two elves must have been mistaken about those Dwarves being dead, for here they were with coalition force of mixed races! They were all around and attacking feverishly, trying to seek some unfathomable revenge. Amathar and Jasmarillith ran, having the wits to know that they were outmatched. The lesser races put forth a chase and nearly put the tired elves into the Dead Book. Then several elves near Felwithe, who were travelling to the spires heard Jasmarillith call for help. These elves were not associated with any Adventuring Guilds but were none the less very helpful in fighting off the infidels. After the battle these noble elves helped Jas and Amathar return to Felwithe safely, and warned them that the Dwarven forces have been more and more aggressive over the last few weeks, and that a war with them was likely brewing.
Now safely back in thier home, the two elves went to thier respective guilds to give an up tp date accoount of thier travels.
Amathar had by now proven himself in battle and would soon seek out The Elves of Illuvitae, for it was his destiny to join thier ranks....
