Wednesday, may 14, 2003 - Roster update - Guild alliance - Black lists
Roster Update

That new guild tool Dump command works very well. I should be updating the Roster a bit more often from now on.

Guild alliance with Quendi Nosse

It was indeed a great night, when Leaders and officers of both illuvitae and Quendi Nosse met to form an alliance. Quendi nosse is an all elf guild that will benefit from a non aggression pact. Their dark elves will have safe passage in all zones except Greater Faydark and Felwithe where they remain KoS. This association was mainly done to allow both our guilds to find more light elves to group with and also to eventually raid together in the planes of power. Again! there is no grouping,  buffing or healing of their Dark elves.

Black list

Both black list have been put public. They will be receive a major update soon with the coming of our War Time charter.

04/29/2003 - Purism - Questing and leveling
Purism

Even when loosing members, illuvitae is still there to keep the purism alive on vallon zek. We got few news guys in the last 2 weeks. Hopefully the are with us for a longer time. We have to disband another guy that was caught grouping with pecks. There is no second chance on that. If you find yourself attracted to do grouping with enemy, please talk to an officer and /guildremove yourself if you really can't avoid it.

Questing and leveling

Why just sitting in a cave, killing the same mobs over and over again,  when you could be hunting specific mobs to get a good quest going at the same time. The goal, questing gives you, will make the game seem less repetitive and a lot more enjoyable. Here is what is suggest: Find few quests that works well for the next few levels, using allakhazam for example. And get the xp as you do the quest!!!

Tocap Val'Dor
04/16/2003 - Sad Sad week - Abusive PvP
Sad Sad week

We lost many friends and members this week. Even if those leaves were done inappropriately, we still have to move on and get the guild going. Twisla, Noyer, Candydare and Piterpater all left.

Abusive PvP

It is time again to remind people that illuvitae is OVER with getting abused by people in PvP. We will not tolerate getting CC,  immy healed, bind rushed, or jumped while on mobs. We will respond harshly to any of those. Illuvitae had his good and bad time, but it's now time that people start fearing us again and stop abusing the fact we have rules to follow that nobody else is bound to. If any illuvitae members have anything against that, we can talk about it. So dirty xteamers,  be warned. B)
