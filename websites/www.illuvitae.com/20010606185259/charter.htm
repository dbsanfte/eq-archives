Illuvitae Charter

The following is an OOC (Out of Character) description of our guild's goals, purpose, and policies.  The roleplaying description can be found on our main page.

Illuvitae is a loosely roleplayed guild on Vallon Zek.  It is made up solely of the Elven team.  Unlike other "all-elf" guilds, Illuvitae actively tries to keep the Elven territories free of foreigners through PvP means.  Also unlike many other "PK" guilds, Illuvitae has strict policies governing its members' conduct.  The following policies must be adhered to by ALL members.  If anyone believes an Illuvitae member has violated any of these policies, please contact Tulweyen Venemus or Ceescrit Bossalini in game or at tulweyen@illuvitae.com or ceescrit@illuvitae.com.

Illuvitae Policies and Rules

(1)  Race:  All members must be of the Elven team:   Wood Elf, High Elf, or Half Elf

(2)  Level Minimum:  To join  Illuvitae, your character must be level 15, or, you must have another character on Vallon Zek who is at least level 15.  The purpose of this rule is to keep complete "newbies" out of the guild.  There is nothing wrong with new players, but Illuvitae is a guild that centers around PvP and should be made up of mature players who have had at least some experience playing on a PvP server.

(3)  KOS Policies:  Greater Faydark, Lesser Faydark, and the cities of Kelethin and Felwithe are Elven territories.  It is the responsibility of Illuvitae to rid our lands of unwelcome foreigners.  Any non-elf within these boundaries should be attacked and either killed, or forced to leave the area.  No other races should ever be buffed or provided with services such as binding within Elven zones.  In other zones throughout Norrath, non-elves may be attacked if desired, but it is not a guild requirement.  At any time, regardless of location, evil classes should be KoS.   This includes dark elves, ogres, trolls, and any shadowknight or necro.

(4)  Grouping:  The Elven race is in all ways superior, and has no need to ever group with anyone of another race.  Grouping with other races is strictly prohibited.  Grouping for the purposes of binds or teleports may be done, at the players discretion, but ONLY if this for the purpose of generating income, and is not in Elven lands.  Aid should only be given to other races if it benefits the Elven team somehow, such as in paid teleports.

(5)  Trading:  Trading with other races can bring benefit to the Elven Kingdom.  Therefore, it is the choice of each member as to whether they want to trade with other races or not.  However, if you do choose to trade with another race, it may NOT be done within Elven lands.  Our homelands are not a center for foreign commerce.  Any non-elf trading must be done elsewhere.

(6)  LnS:  Loot and Scoot policies are enforced within the guild.  What this means, is that after you have been defeated in PvP battle, you must loot your corpse and leave the area.  Meditating, talking, or any other activity after you have looted your corpse is not permitted.  You can zone, camp, or play another character if you wish.   After vanquishing a foe in PvP battle, you may kindly request that they LnS as well.  If they refuse to loot their body, or refuse to scoot after looting, they may be killed again.

LnS rules apply if you are engaged in PvP battle, and die for ANY reason.  For instance, if you are engaged in PvP but your actual death occurs by falling, drowning, mob hits, or any other reason, you still must scoot.

LnS rules also apply regardless of your opponents behavior.  Even if they did NOT LnS, or if they are bindrushing, or acting in any other immature way, we still must LnS when we are killed.  We must lead by example, and thereby gain our enemies respect.

A general guideline for the length of your "scoot" is 60 minutes.  However, if the victors have left the zone, they are obviously not concerned with "holding" it, and you may return.  Please use good judgment in this area, and bow out gracefully when you have been defeated.

(7)  PvP Conduct:  This is Illuvitae's MOST IMPORTANT RULE.  It is also the hardest to enforce and describe.  Basically, we want to earn the fear, but also the respect of our enemies.  Therefore, every effort should be made to act in a mature and dignified matter while engaging in PvP.  Trash talking, and profanity of any type is strictly not permitted in the ooc and shout channels, as well as in personal tells to your enemies.  

Also, because we are a new guild, if foreigners are using an Elven bank, it would be an ooc courtesy to give everyone a warning before engaging in PvP at the bank.  This rule will be changed once people learn that foreigners are not welcome in our banks.

As well, obvious, immature PvP behavior is prohibited.  Exploits, using trade windows improperly, LoS exploits, or other unsportsmanlike conduct should not take place.

Please go here if you feel you need a lesson in Vallon Zek PvP attitude... ;)

(8)  Immortal Healing:  As a guild, we are here to help each other.  Healing fellow members is encouraged.  Healing or buffing OUTSIDE of direct conflict is always allowed.  However, during a conflict,  if the healer is 8 levels higher than both the guildmate, and the enemy in the conflict, then healing/buffing is not allowed.  This is simply because it can ruin the gaming experience of other people when they have absolutely no chance of winning, or of stopping the "immortal healer".

(9)  Twinking:  If someone wants to twink their Illuvitae character, that is their personal decision.  However, blatant and EXTREME twinking is not allowed.  Again, the purpose of this is so as not to ruin the gaming experience of other people.  Being killed by unbeatable opponents, due to their equipment is not fun.

 

These policies are subject to change.  If you would like to propose a change to Illuvitae's charter, please send an email to tulweyen@illuvitae.com or post within our private message board forum.
