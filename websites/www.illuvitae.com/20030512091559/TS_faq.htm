Success

Success = Skill - 0.75*Trivial + 51.5 (rounded up, for Trivial>= 6
192 * 1.05 = 201.6 (effective skill)

If Trivial is 251 then
success = 201.6 - .75*251 +51.5 = 64.85 %

If Trivial = 301 then
success = 201.6 - .75*301 +51.5 = 27.35 %

Its all up to Luck at that skill. (They are trival > 250).
