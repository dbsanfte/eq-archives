Lulanyan

When Lulanyan was young he would love to go and play with the other wood elves. But something always went wrong. They would accuse Lulanyan of stealing something or cheating.
After a while Lulanyan got tired of being wrongly accused (though nowadays I’d watch out for him) and decided to play alone. He would go out and stroll through the woods listening to the birds and watching the wolves run by. Lulanyan thought he might want to become a druid. Nature loved him and he loved nature. On these walks of his he would always head a certain direction, he didn’t know why, but he did.
Lulanyan finally decided to pack up and head out in that direction to see what was pulling his soul there. Along the way he would just act like he was on a normal walk but he was hurrying all the while. One day he strolled into a clearing. There was a cave there. He set down his pack and took out his staff and walked in. Right as he walked in the scenery changed and he saw a golden room with some dark blue armor lying on the floor.
He put the armor on to try it out. It seemed to fit him perfectly and looked pretty good to.
He also looked around and found a Bile drenched main-gauche and lying not a foot away was a Blood point! These were very good weapons at the time. That night he set out having silently decided to follow the arts of a rogue.
