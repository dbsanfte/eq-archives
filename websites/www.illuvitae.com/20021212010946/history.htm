Illuvitae - History
In an age gone by, it was the elves who ruled supreme over the great forests of the Faydark.  Despite their relative seclusion, their beauty, intelligence, artistry, and loyalty became renowned throughout all of Norrath.


Then the foreigners came, invading their peaceful forest and turning it into a center of commerce.  The elves welcomed the foreigners into their lands.  However, before long, the ancient peace of the Elven Kingdom was shattered.  Overrun with foreign races, the forest was beaten down, leaving fallen elves of all kinds.


It is now though, in this time of turmoil,  that a unique group of elves arise.  These elves remember the old times of fame.  They remember what their homelands were like before they were turned into a world center of commerce.  They remember when the elves were proud and dignified.  These elves rose up, and journeyed forth to restore the Eleven Kingdom to its former glory.


This elite society is made up of only the most dedicated and bravest of elves.  Striking fear into the hearts of their enemies, they battle forth, defending their Elven heritage.
