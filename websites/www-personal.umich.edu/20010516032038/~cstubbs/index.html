This is our working charter, it explains what makes us tick(well some of us at least) and will give you an idea of our goals and fundementals.

The Legion of Apathy(LoA) was formed many months ago, back in the earlier days of Test (but not the REALLY early days). "The Legion of Apathy" was named by one of our less active, but highly cynical members variously known as as Smegmodon, Pustullo (on test), and Bkuk on Fennin Ro.

Two of us from Fennin Ro (Snag and Gilligan) had intended to form a guild from the beginning. Snag and Gilligan are the sort who can intend to do something for a very long time. They had moved to Test permanently back in September of '99 to escape the increasingly busy rat-race(Proddies)on Fennin Ro. Glina, Keldarez and Smegmodon were also Fennin Ro Escapees in our crew. We met Kirska and Udal (Quellios Refugees) in Highpass, and quickly became frequent partners. Manae we met in our days of Cazic Thule cleansing. The guild was formed sometime soon there after with these members as our core "founders".

Our "Guildmaster" is currently Snag Glepus, a rogue. This came about more because of his persistence in locating and pursuing a GM to form the guild than anything else. The actual leadership of the guild is by consensus. Our current active officers are Gilligan, Glina, Kirska, Jaloo, Phantasy, Kreator and Kuhma.

 

Our Philosophy

We think that the attitude of apathy is important on Test. It doesn't mean we don't care because we do, very much. Our apathy manifests itself in our healthy "Jimmy Buffet" approach to life on Test. Server downages, roll-backs, bad patches, item loss, mass item wipes and the threat of character purge - all these are easier to weather with a healthy dose of Apathy. If we complain , it is generally not bitter, doesn't last long and often will be in the form of humor. Whining is almost unheard of in the LoA, and we tend to ridicule those who do.

The LoA is about the people that are in it. We play because we enjoy the game, and especially each other's company. We are like a really violent, fun loving, happy family (most of the time :) )

LoA does not tend to form enemies, but instead we would simply avoid contact with someone we dislike. If someone does not impress us with their integrity, we do not attack them publicly, we simply do not group with them, nor assist them when they have a problem. We try to avoid airing conflicts on public channels, and our members tend to avoid such incidents. It is rarely an issue, thankfully, as we are easygoing, and Test is pleasingly mellow.

The LoA is a guild that will help someone in need at the drop of a hat. If Timmy is stuck down a well - we're there! We enjoy situations of rescue, or questing for an item.

We will camp for items, but not with manic determination. We camp as long as it's not boring, then we tend to move on. And try that camp another day.

Generally, we try to camp for things the people in the group need. We don't usually camp for items for our secondaries or guildmates. If we encounter extra items along the way those may end up on a secondary or guildmate, or given away. But we tend not to go hunting specifically to twink. It's more fun to get an item for your character WITH your character, and we will eagerly help a friend trying to do just that.

We don't overly twink our lower level members. But, we also don't hoard items our higher level characters can't use. Items don't tend to gather dust in the LoA, we gift them to guildmates, or friends. People in the LoA are not grabby. None of us have ever been beggears, and while we appreciate gifts. We don't ask for them in advance :)

When we group, items go to those that can use them with their character in the group first. Those that can (and intend) to use the item will roll. If nobody in the group can use it, those with secondaries or needy friends roll. If nobody has any need for it, we roll for fun, or gift it to someone we just happen to find. This is the prevailing attitude to item drops on Test, and we follow it as well.

The LoA is *funny* all of us have a good sense of humor, and you'll be snorting milk out your nose on occassion. Many a monitor and keyboard have been assaulted with laughter induced spray at our comments and antics, pants have undoubtedly been wet.

The LoA is a very cohesive group. We play well together, and internal strife is rare and short-lived. Group with us! You're bound to have a good time!
