Now Kabba Rant....

For those of you not well versed in the history of Norath there is something I need you to know. The Gnomes have built and maintained a race that balances on the threshold of all other cultures by one thing "loathing politics". The Gnomes have gone as far as to actually build a clockwork King. Trust me when I say this is true. For any who doubt me visit Ak'Anon and you will see with your own eyes.

Our king has ruled for nearly 5 centuries without issue. There has been great turmoil in all of Norath with fighting between races and even single races fighting amongst themselves. Somehow the Gnomes have been left out of most of this and have thrived. Most gnomes would agree that this is due largely to the leadership skills of the king. Still more gnomes give credit to themselves for being wise enough to stay out of politics.

I come from very large family. We as a guild loosely toss the word "Family" around. In light of all that has happened I have a hard time believing that most of you who use this word know what it means. You can hate your family and disagree with everything they say but you cannot leave because you need to trust that the strength of the whole is stronger than the parts. You can love em you can hate em but in the end they are still your brothers and sisters and no matter how big the feud someday you will have to make peace.

I do not hold it against any one for leaving the guild and would welcome back most of you without question. I consider myself friends with a lot of you and will remain that until the end of Norath. I hope that some of you will stay and rather than leaving the problems behind try to correct them here. Honestly there will be a whole new set of problems wherever you go and I don't know about you but I would rather face these problems with "Family" than without them.

I do know this, many guilds have come and gone but for some reason the rag tag bunch called the Fallen Wolves have survived. Why? Because one person always cared eneough to stay!

Kabba Oom
