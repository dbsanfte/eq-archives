ENTER THE WOLVES DEN


Origin of the Fallen Wolves

The great silver wolf moved amongst the bodies of those that fell in the great battle. Every so often she would pause and utter a howl to honor those that had died valiantly. It had taken all the native races of Norrath to break the yoke of the dragonkind. Elves and halflings, ogres and humans had fought and died together to gain freedom.

The great wolf suddenly paused and sniffed the air warily. There was a scent that did not belong. Treading carefully around the wreckage of a catapult she came across the bodies of the other wolves. The great wolf spoke:

“Rise, spirit of the hunter. Heed my call for I am Tunare, mother of all. How is it that you lie here among the bodies of those who build?”

The shimmering spirit of the fallen wolf rose. It spoke not in words, for his kind did not use them. It locked gazes with the great wolf and the words came.

“The scaled ones do not belong. A sickness they are. We came to drive these away as did the forest tenders and the cave builders and the earth shapers. It is the way of the pack.”

Tunare considered these words for a long moment. The dragons had been defeated, but they would return one day. The younger races would soon forget that which bound them together and the alliance that won the battle today may never again come to pass. Tunare’s eyes hardened as she came to a decision.

“You did well spirit of the hunter, but the hunt is not over yet. Those that stood as one today will no longer have reason to work together. This cannot be allowed to happen, for I foresee the great wurms will one day return and try to reclaim their ancient glory. You and your pack will make sure this is not so.”

The form on Tunare blazed with light and in her place a beautiful high elven woman stood. Chanting softly, Tunare gathered and wove the strands of fate.

“Go forth from this place and choose children of the races that stood together here. Inhabit their bodies and guide their souls. Bind them together into a pack as you once were. You will recognize each other by deed, thought and demeanor and keep the alliance that was forged here today, lest the children of Veeshan rise again. Thus will the sacrifice of those that fell here be honored. Rise, Fallen Wolves, and stand watch until the world is made anew.”
