New Memberships Info


To become a member please read the following information and then fill out the registration form.

 

All applicants will be required to have obtained a level of 20 and must complete a guild registration form on this website.

One existing member must sponsor and agree to be the mentor of the applicant during the application process.

A 30 day probationary period will be allotted before permanent admittance to the guild is granted.

Any difficulties experienced with the applicant are to be reported to an officer. The officer will be responsible for informing the applicants sponsor to remedy the situation.

A group of officers will vote on the applicants membership at the end of the 30 day period. The applicant may be given 15 additional days to correct any existing problems or be removed from the guild.

By accepting admittance into the Fallen Wolves, a new recruit is agreeing to follow the principles and ideals discussed on our new members web page.

 

Secondary Characters

If an existing member wishes to have a secondary character join the guild, they should be permitted upon reaching their 20th level. No guild help in gaining items is to be offered or expected until they reach the 20th level.
