GUILD RULES OF CONDUCT

The Fallen Wolves have a fairly simple set of easy to follow rules:

Characters joining the guild must be of level 30 or above. Exceptions can be made for Family members and alternates and special circumstances(will be discussed on individual basis)
No /anon if there is a reason pst to any online officer and explain why otherwise /role-play. Wear the Fallen Wolf tag with pride.
NO KILLSTEALING or CAMP Stealing...this will not be tolerated under ANY circumstances, a single offense will lead to exclusion (mitigating circumstances will be taken into account).
Love thy neighbor (be kind to all u meet even if you don’t like who you meet you represent this guild and must uphold that which this guild stands for) do not train, be rude in ooc/shout channels etc.
No Arguments made with other players in open chat channels. If another player is abusive to you leave that zone. Do not engage in behavior that is unbecoming of a Fallen Wolves member. No vulgarity (language/ explicit sexual references etc) in guild chat (insults on other members will not be tolerated) Take disagreements with other guildies to tells or contact the nearest online officer.
Assist your guild brothers and sisters. Each and every guild member is expected to assist their fellow members to the limit of their abilities. This does not mean that you should give up the 4 hour camp to drop everything and help someone, but use your common sense and if you can help then do.
No PL begging, No item begging, from other guild members. All guild members are encouraged (read expected) to help each other. Guild members will help when they can. Begging excessivly is grounds for removal.
Resale of items. There will be occasions when a member may pass on equipment or items to another member. This is right and proper and is part of what the guild is about. However these items may not be resold outside of the guild for a profit without the express permission of the guild officers. The idea of this is to stop people exploiting other members by begging them to obtain items for the sole purpose of reselling them for profit. Raid rules always go Need before Greed, all items to class and individule that can use first.
Don’t sell in guild chat. Selling items obtained outside of raids or Fallen wolf groups or items that you no longer need to other guild members is permited. Please do not sell in guild chat. Remember that item should probably be given to a member that needs it not sold to them.
Guides and GMs are to be given respect at all times, even when you do not agree with their decisions!Follow Sony's EULA, breaking any of the EULA rules outlined is immediate grounds for dismissal.
NO fighting, arguing or belittling other players on public channels (say, auc, gos, ooc, shout) will be tolerated under any circumstances! Whenever you speak on a public channel, you represent the ENTIRE GUILD! Please maintain a polite decorum on these channels at all times!
HAVE FUN!!! Yes that's an order!
