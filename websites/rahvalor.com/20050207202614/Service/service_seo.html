Why do you need a Search Engine marketing program?

Did you know that over 75% of visitors to your site will reach it through a search engine? With a statistic like that, who can afford not to have a search engine marketing program?

No matter how terrific your site is, if nobody can find it reasonably quickly on a search engine, you are losing a major potential customer base.

You need to spend time, and sometimes money, to make sure your site can be found. It’s not enough to put it out there on the web where every day the big engines are bragging about the millions of new documents they’ve added to their indices.

How will we get your site listed on the search engines?

We use two strategies for maximizing exposure to your target audience. One is to “naturally” optimize the site and the other is to simply pay for ad space. At Rahvalor Interactive we focus on natural optimization, and we will recommend paid options that may benefit you and fit your budget.

To naturally optimize your site and gain you a “free” listing we:

Develop Keywords – We analyze your market and target audience to determine appropriate keywords and phrases.

Develop Content – We analyze and edit the content of your web pages to be sure it works within search engine guidelines and includes keywords in the right places.

Analyze Technology – We make sure that everything “under the hood” is in the best interest of your search engine marketing program. We will advise you on any technologies that interfere with search engines.

Develop META Tags – Some search engines use them and many people will see them as part of your listing. We make sure this invisible code is appropriate and effective.

Analyze Graphics – They look pretty but sometimes they affect how you are ranked. We advise you on the best way to treat graphics.

Set up Links – We will explore opportunities to place links to your site on other quality sites. If other sites point to yours, search engines view yours as more popular and will rank you higher.

Recommend paid options – We strongly recommend including paid options in your budget. We will recommend those that we feel will most benefit you. Should you choose to use paid options, we can set up and manage your paid accounts.

What we won’t do

There are a number of “tricks” that can be used in search engine optimization, but they will often get you banned from search engines altogether. At Rahvalor Interactive we employ only trusted, search-engine-endorsed methods for listing a site. We will not…

Create “doorway” pages – Search-engine-specific pages, generally stuffed with keywords and considered spam by the search engines.

Join “link farms” – Search engines penalize sites that set up links indiscriminately to boost rankings. If you are linked to a site that violates search engine rules, you will be considered in violation also.

Use automated submission programs –Some search engines disallow automated submissions and others ban sites that employ them. We submit key pages of your site individually to the major search engines.

Do anything that can be considered "questionable" - We follow only approved methods of optimization set forth by search engines and the search industry.

Are you going to be number one?

While no reputable company can offer this guarantee unless you pay the search engines to list you in a particular position, we can tell you that we have achieved a number of top ten and number one positions for customers who subscribe to our search engine program, leading to increased exposure to a targeted audience, increased site traffic and finally, increased business leads. The key lies in the marketing.

You might not see a number one position unless you target the correct niche. For example, imagine you are selling flowers. You would expect “flowers” to be a very competitive keyword – lots of people are selling, showcasing or talking about flowers. To get into a number one spot for a keyword that general, is, dare we say, nearly impossible - and not necessarily beneficial.

However, if you have a rare orange African daisy, there’s a much greater chance of being number one for a search on “orange African daisy” than “flowers”. Our goal is to find the niche and put you at the top of it.

Once you're in a good position, can you forget about search engine marketing?

We cringe to think of it. Search engines are dynamic, always changing entities. Today you’re number one, tomorrow you’re gone. At Rahvalor Interactive we offer ongoing site maintenance services to ensure that once we achieve an optimal rank for you, your site stays there. This requires monitoring and tweaking of your site to please the search engine gods. We will check your rank across a number of keywords and repeat the optimization process on a regular basis to ensure that you are reaching your target audience.

We also submit your site to a number of common secondary engines and we may submit your site to industry-specific engines. We will research options and submit your site as necessary to engines that represent your market.
