Case Study: Centro streamlines business processes and takes advantage of new technology

Project Components

  • Web site design
  • Electronic claim reporting and retrieval system

The Client : Centro Inspection Agency

Centro Inspection Agency is a network of certified mechanical inspectors operating throughout the United States and Canada, with headquarters located in central New Jersey. Centro offers mechanical expertise through inspections and verifications of mechanical failures, providing claims documentation review and forensic services.

Goals and Challenges: Making use of emerging technology

As Centro's business expanded and new technologies emerged, Centro looked for ways to improve communications between its inspectors and its corporate headquarters. We identified the following areas for improvement:

  • Successful businsses take advantage of new technology in order to streamline day-to-day operations.
    When we met the administrators at Centro, inspectors used phone, fax, postal service and traditional print photography as their primary means of documenting and filing claims. A growing business meant a growing cost associated with paper, shipping, film developing and other media. Centro needed a more cost-effective method of communicating with its inspectors.
  • Increased business also meant more time spent on error-checking and quality-control. This created a lag between the time an inspector filed a report and the time Centro could submit the report to an insurance agency. Additionally, claims and photos had to be shipped to insurance agencies, creating further lag time. Centro needed to streamline its business process in order to provide a more efficient and quality level of service.

The Project: Developing electronic communication with employees

To streamline information distribution and improve communication between Centro and its inspectors, we designed the following:

  • A web interface that allowed Centro's administrators to electronically assign claims to and retrieve claims from inspectors - administrators were able to review reports immediately after they were submitted so that errors or omissions could be checked quickly.
  • A web site targeted toward inspectors that allowed them to electronically retrieve their assignments and subsequently file their reports - the interface provided inspectors with the ability to upload and submit digital photos with their reports.
  • A similar online interface for insurance agencies - we equipped the insurance agencies with a similar application on the Internet, allowing them to view and print completed reports immediately upon submission.

The Benefits: Streamlined business processes

  • Save time. Instead of claims taking days or weeks to become available, depending on film processing times, shipping delays and error correction, reports and photos were at the fingertips of any staff member who required access. Centro Quality Control was able to check reports immediately upon completion, then submit approved reports to insurance agencies. The entire process, from claim to final report, now takes less than 48 hours.
  • Save money. A fully electronic system for filing and submitting claims substantially reduced the cost involved in processing claims. Postage, film developing and other costs were reduced or eliminated.
