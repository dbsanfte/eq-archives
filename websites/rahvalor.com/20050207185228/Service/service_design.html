Design and redesign goals

Whether you are just starting out in the online arena or your current site needs improvement, Rahvalor Interactive, Inc. will build you a site that is…

  • Easy to use
  • Search engine friendly
  • Organized
  • Informative
  • Valuable to your business
  • Part of your overall marketing and branding strategy

In short, our web development goal is to bring together all the elements of a “good site” in order to create a coherent, effective online presence for your business. We strongly believe that a web site is one piece in a complete marketing and branding strategy and should fit with the rest of your marketing materials. We do not simply build you a web site, we build you an image.

Notice that “aesthetic” is absent from our list of goals. The reason for this is simple: ask ten people what a good site looks like and you will get eleven answers. Our ultimate goal in terms of aesthetics is to build a site that meets the expectations and preferences of our customers while maintaining usability.

The difference between design and redesign

Fundamentally, design and redesign encompass the same goals and development process. We simply refer to redesign as the re-thinking of a site that already exists. Hopefully your business is continuing to grow and change and your site should change with it. Redesign comes into play when your site needs a facelift, either aesthetically or functionally. Maybe you want your site to be easier to use, look more modern or offer a service feature to your customers or employees. Whatever the reason, a redesign takes your site one step further.

When “more” is not always “better”

One step further should always equate with one step better. Yes, we can make your site flash, blink and sing. But there are reasons not to add extra features to your site. By “extra features” we mean things that do not affect your site’s usability or functionality but are simply surface enhancements. Music. A twinkling star. Graphics that fade in and out. All of these things may impress you but they may not impress your visitors. Extra features can…

  • Slow your site down so that people with slow Internet connections might never get through your opening animation
  • Stop or inhibit search engines from listing your site
  • Annoy visitors to the point that they leave your site before finding out how truly wonderful you are

Flashing-blinking-singing things can be distracting, repetitious or detract from the real message within the content of your site.

That isn’t to say that these elements should never be used. Each has its place and when done tastefully, can enhance a site. But any enhancement should advance your message and goals. The purpose of a web site is not to leave your visitors star struck but to convert them into customers.

The myth of the $300 site

Your nephew in college can do it. Your IT guy can do it. Some guy who sent you an e-mail last week says he can do it. We wouldn’t dream of doing it.

If someone offers a truly mouth-watering price tag like this… run. The truth is this: a $300 site is going to look like a $300 site. Our goal is not to slap up an online brochure with a flashing banner or two, but to market your business. We plan, set goals, explore options and bring years of expertise and experience to every project we undertake. If you want a $300 web site then stop reading! Go directly to your nephew. Maybe a couple of pages and a banner are all you need. But if you are looking for an opportunity to market and grow your business, we can help.

What do you get and how much is it going to cost?

Every web project we undertake is 100% custom. We do not have standard per-page rates because we feel that this limits the effectiveness of a site. If you are paying per page, you might feel compelled to cram as much information onto as few pages as possible without regard for structure or usability.

As part of our process, we meet with you to discuss goals, options and budget and to determine expectations and costs. Once we establish a clear direction through consultation and discovery, we will present you with a complete written proposal outlining what you can expect from your web site and associated costs. Your marketing goals will determine the depth and complexity of your site, which in turn will determine the cost. At Rahvalor Interactive we give you clear project goals and terms and we are with you through every step of the process.
