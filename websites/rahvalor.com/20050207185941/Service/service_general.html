Help!

If the possibilities are a little overwhelming, you’re not alone. Marketing requires a lot of exploration, plenty of imagination and a commitment of time and money. You may be tempted at times to simply cut marketing from the budget altogether, especially during times when the economy is a little tougher. Our advice, and not entirely from a selfish standpoint is: don’t do it! Marketing can be the most important thing you do, especially in a tough economy.

If you’re not sure where to begin, our team of consultants will sit down with you and discuss your goals (or make some up if you don’t have those either), and lay out a plan for the short and long term. We will help you set budget, outline expectations and develop a strategy to market your organization one step at a time.

To develop a marketing plan for your organization, we can help to…

  • Explore new ways to bring more customers to you
  • Develop more effective ways for you to manage your existing customers
  • Determine the medium to best convey your message, whether web site, brochure, CD-ROM or any other digital or printed material
  • Create a unifying, unique image for your company that is reflected in all print and digital materials
  • Set step by step goals that can be accomplished in phases for both short term accomplishment and long term success

Whether you are ready to begin marketing your company or simply want to discuss plans and develop an outline, we can help you plan and advise you on strategies that will (and won’t) benefit you. Before you embark on your marketing journey, we will present you with a complete written outline and proposal.

Contact us for a free consultation and to find out how we can help you improve your existing customer relationships and build new ones!
