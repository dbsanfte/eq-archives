Ode tu Ootok



Ootok is eh trollie
she is eh purdy trollie
She hafes green eyes
she attracks flys
purdy green skin she gots
me makes me feel hots
She got big boobs
sells stuff tu noobs
she doos sell tings
me give her rings
she buys me lewt
me give he eh flewt
"Umm.. Thanks" she sey
me marry her un dey
me in luv with her
me give her som fer
you mess with me woomun
gona get me fewmun
me fite fer her luv
dont mess with me bub
Ootok mine me bashy jou gud
