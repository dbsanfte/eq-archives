The Officers and Leadership of the Vanguard of Norrath would like to announce an official update regarding our allegiance with the Order of Loki.

Our Alliance with the guild Order of Loki has been ceased.

Citing several incidents involving vulgar language and other incidents that will remain private, Adeon has decided that we will end our friendship with the Order of Loki effective immediately.  They will no longer be welcome at 'official' guild events.  No one should treat a member with disrespect and if anyone chooses to group with a member of the Order then that is a personal choice. 

We wish them well as we part ways.
