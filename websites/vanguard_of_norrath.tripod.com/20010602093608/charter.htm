Charter

In presence of Master Adeon Mathedras

In agreement with the Council of Three
begun and held in the Tavern of Morshia , Regions of Greater Faydark
and under the eyes of god.

The Conventions of the Vanguard of Norrath, having at the time of their adopting this Charter, expressed a desire, in order to fortify a collection of orders, bylaws, and establish a structure by which the "guild" may be known and run.

This orders, bylaws, and establishments may be amended as seen fit by its majority membership.

  1. As this is a MMRPG (Massive Multiplayer Role-Playing Game) we, the Vanguard of Norrath (hereafter known as VON) recognize that there are many players, both in and out of the order, who wish total immersion in the world of Norrath. Therefore, a member should at all times "role - play" his/her character in public.

    Please stay 'in character' at all times when dealing with others in a public forum. When someone meets a member for the first time, we want him or her to know that we are a guild worth considering.

    Any 'out of character' conversations should be kept for private /TELL's, /OOC's /GUILDSAY's or /GSAY's. Under no circumstances should /SHOUT be used for any conversations that are not in character and game related. As is the practice, in guild conversation is traditionally much less formal and may not adhere to the role playing issues set forth earlier in this statement.
  2.  
  3. As this is known as a guild of Honor, Pride, and Protection; this Guild is primarily one designed for the "light" races. Races and Trades that make practice with the dead, engage in dealing with black gods, or otherwise belong to a guild, order, race, or practice that could be considered by our membership as being detrimental to the overall good of the light races and their cause will not be admitted.
  4.  
  5. The use of vulgar or foul language in open text (ie: /OOC, /SHOUT, /AUCTION, or regular /SAY's where a third party/parties could hear it) will not be tolerated, and repeated offences could be grounds for dismissal from the guild.
  6.  
  7. Harassment of another player will not be tolerated. If a dispute arises between a guild member and another character, and it can not be resolved by either party, then the guild member should then /Ignore the offending character and, if necessary, report the reason for the dispute to a GM. As well, a list of players that are well known for causing trouble will be maintained by the guild.
  8.  
  9. The practice known as "kill stealing" will also not be tolerated and is grounds for dismissal.
  10.  
  11. The outright trickery, murder, or thievery of another player character, regardless of circumstance is grounds for immediate dismissal.
  12.  
  13. We will only accept applications from people that have achieved the minimum level of 20. We feel it is necessary for a new player to become familiar with the basic mechanics of the game and the structure of the world before attempting to join into a guild.

All new members will have to read the guild Charter, and agree to these rules before they are accepted into the guild. They will then be considered on probation for their first few sessions, during which time the guild can get to know them and visa versa.
