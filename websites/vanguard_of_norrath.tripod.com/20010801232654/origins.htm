Origins

The Vanguard of Norrath was founded out of principle. Created at a time when the safety of those who wished peace and a noble exsitance could not be guaranteed such liberties. It was this fear and insecurity that brought about the creation of the Vanguard. A Vanguard is a troop. It is a first line of defence. It is a legion who represents the greatest and strongest of it's sponsored principles.

The Vanguard ofNorrath was formed by a band of adventurers hunting in the forests of the Greater Faydark, the Vanguard was founded in an effort to join the forces of light against those creatures and beasts who attempt
to destroy all that defines honor and loyalty. Our founding member, and now leader Adeon Mathedras gathered himself a band of individuals. It is this original group who while seated around a fire in a quiet inn in Kelethin, wrote our charter , swore to defend and uphold this rule, and laid the foundations for
our brotherhood.

Throughout the years the Vanguard has gathered a significant number to it's ranks. The ranks swell with individuals bornm into the light who wish to defend it's principles, and contain others which have learned from their past and wish to make peace with themselves.

Of this group some still remain. Some of whom are unfortunately no longer with the order. These lost souls fell to the shadows of lies and deceit, some of these individuals have simply lost the way. We pray for these souls daily.

Saedean Dane

Grand Council Steward of the Vanguard
