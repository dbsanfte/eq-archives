A Courtyard's in Session!!

The Vanguard of Norrath headed to the evil grounds of Mist Moore
Castle to aid one of their members in obtaining a component for
the Burning Rapier Quest. The Group assembled at the zone line
and began with the usual buffs and group layout plans.

The item in question was a Bottle of Blood Spirits dropped by a
Mist Moore Denizen named "A Hemo Encologist". The evil creature
was conveniently located in the very Courtyard of Mist Moore
Castle and so our parties headed there. They carefully cleared
the path, easily destroying any foolish undead and evil
nasties they encountered. Climbing the cliff path towards the
Castle, they encountered heavy resistance which forced our warriors into a pitched battle.

They proceeded to enter the Castle itself
and having cleared the entrance room, headed towards the Piano
Room and hence the Courtyard. It was here that the group was ambushed
by a large amount of Mist Moore inhabitants who cunningly
used various magics to quickly slay some of our party members
before we even were aware of the intensity of the encounter. The
party retreated and the wounded were brought back to the
edge of Lesser Faydark. Here they regrouped and tried again. A few more
brothers and sisters joined them in the drive to the courtyard. These
memebers, and the fact that they were more cautious then before
allowed them to access the Courtyard rather quickly. The fighting was
fast and bloody and many evil beings were slain in quick order.
Finally the object of thier endeavor appeared and they quickly took
it down. The Blood Spirits were acquired and the Vanguard proceeded stay
and give the evil beings a taste of justice.
