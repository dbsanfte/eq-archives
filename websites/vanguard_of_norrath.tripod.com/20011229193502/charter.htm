Charter

In presence of Master Adeon Mathedras

In agreement with the Council of Three
begun and held in the Tavern of Morshia , Regions of Greater Faydark
and under the eyes of god.

The Conventions of the Vanguard of Norrath, having at the time of their adopting this Charter, expressed a desire, in order to fortify a collection of orders, bylaws, and establish a structure by which the "guild" may be known and run.

This orders, bylaws, and establishments may be amended as seen fit by its majority membership. This charter is written OOC (Out of Character) for ease of explanation

  1. As this is a MMRPG (Massive Multiplayer Role-Playing Game) we, the Vanguard of Norrath (hereafter known as VON) recognize that there are many players, both in and out of the order, who wish total immersion in the world of Norrath. Therefore, the VON leadership encourages role-playing wherever possible. (Ammended by SD 10/28/01)

    We encourage staying 'in character' at all times when dealing with others in a public forum. When someone meets a member for the first time, we want him or her to know that we are a guild worth considering. In an effort to signal this act, please make use of the "roleplaying" flag/status while in game. (Ammended by SD 10/28/01)

    Any 'out of character' conversations should be kept for private /TELL's, /OOC's /GUILDSAY's or /GSAY's. Under no circumstances should /SHOUT be used for any conversations that are not in character and game related. As is the practice, in guild conversation is traditionally much less formal and may not adhere to the role playing issues set forth earlier in this statement. Any "informal" guild chat should remain in guild chat and should not be made privy to those outside our ranks so as too preserve the "role playng" integrity we are trying to achieve.     (Ammended by SD 9/9/01)
  2.  
  3. As this is known as a guild of Honor, Pride, and Protection; this Guild is primarily designed as a "Light Order". Individuals, Brotherhoods, and Organizations that make practice with the dead, engage in dealing with black gods, or otherwise belong to a guild, order, conclave, community, or practice that could be considered by our membership as being detrimental to the overall good of the people of Norrath may be admitted under special conditions as detailed here:(Ammended by SD 10/26/01)
  • Recruitment and membership requirements are as follows.  Membership is open to any High Elf, Wood Elf, Half Elf, Dwarf, Human, Barbarian, Erudite, Halfling, Gnome, Vah Shir.  The following races may also be recruited under additional conditions: Dark Elves, Iksar, Ogres, and Trolls.  These last four races need to be able to prove that the races of the light accept them.  A test of faction will be required beyond, and in addition too, any other entry quest that is given.  The potential recruit must be able to enter without magical assistance the city of Felwithe, or Kaladim and be witnessed by Adeon or an officer.  Necromancers and Shadow knights will not be allowed into the guild under any circumstance
  1.  
  2. The use of vulgar or foul language in open text (ie: /OOC, /SHOUT, /AUCTION, or regular /SAY's where a third party/parties could hear it) will not be tolerated, and repeated offences could be grounds for dismissal from the guild.
  3.  
  4. Harassment of another player will not be tolerated. If a dispute arises between a guild member and another character, and it can not be resolved by either party, then the guild member should then /Ignore the offending character and, if necessary, report the reason for the dispute to a GM. As well, a list of players that are well known for causing trouble will be maintained by the guild. Harassement includes, but is not limited to Begging for Cash, Pestering for Services (SOW, Teleports, Resurrections, etc.), Inscesant Browbeating or insulting, Open Ridicule, and Entrapment. (Ammended by SD 9/9/01)
  5.  
  6. The practice known as "kill stealing" will also not be tolerated and is grounds for dismissal.
  7.  
  8. The outright trickery, murder, or thievery of another player character, regardless of circumstance is grounds for immediate dismissal.

Officer's Code of Conduct

1) At all times adhere to the charter of the guild and uphold its standards with the membership.

2) Seek out potential recruits at all times, and encourage them to join if they are worthy players and meet the requirements of membership.

3) Maintain an air of civility to members and non-VoN folks at all times.

4) Officers are REQUIRED to attend all 'Official" Vanguard events unless they are not online at the time of the event or given leave to be absent by Adeon.

5) When inviting recruits or alter egos into the guild, officers must record the name, race and class of that person into the guild membership thread on that same day.

6) Officers are expected to assist the guild leader in inviting, disciplinary issues, quests for recruits, and be vocal, active participants in the guild.  This involves participating in online discussions, whether it is in guild chat or on our message board.  Officers should always keep the guild leader abreast of any issues of concern from the general membership.  Officers should encourage grouping with guild mates whenever possible to foster a sense of community and cooperation.
