Landril Steelwind

In times of war, it is the duty of the Captain of the Guard to safeguard the warlord at all times. He
will choose and command three guardians to assist him in this duty. These will be chosen by him from the
guild.

I grew up in amongst the trees of the Greater Faydark. As a child in Kelethin, my life was not that of fun and games, or at least not the normal fun and games that children of other races enjoyed. With our neighbors being the Crushbone Orcs, our most hated foes, the games I was taught to play were meant to teach me the skills I would need to defeat this enemy. All Wood Elf children of Kelethin began early with their instruction in the art of warfare, since we were constantly having to defend our home from the advances of these loathsome creatures. As I was one of the few of my kind not gifted with the ability to tap into the magic in the land or the gods, my instruction was given over to the Emerald Warriors when I reached early adulthood. I spent my days being taught how to use every weapon and skill of fighting. I honed these skills on the field of battle, slaying my foes in there encampments when they dared to tread to close to our city in the trees, and on numerous raids into the enemies territory near their stonghold. During this time, I also discovered an aptitude for the crafting of armor and weaponry, which were the tools of my craft. I sought and recieved further instruction in these skills and eventually attained the ranking of a Master in the skill of smithing. I worked long and hard to craft weapons and armor, both to aid my brethern in there efforts against the orcs and for profit, selling to foreign peoples who visited our lands. Eventually I tired of the constant warefare with the minions of Emporer Crush, though I still slay them when I come across them. I decided that I would like to travel; visit many of the places I had heard of from travelers and merchants who came through from time to time. As one of a long-lived race, I had plenty of time to see as many of the sights in Norrath as I wished. I had also heard of many of the evils around in many spots on Norrath. I decided that I would use the skills I had aquired battling the Crushbone, to vanquish as many of these evils as well. Thusly, I have left my home amongst the trees and wander the realms of Norrath, vanquishing evil whenever I can. Helping those weaker and less fortunate than myself, hwo are oppressed by the minions of dark. I am glad that I have found others who share many of my same beliefs and who I can band together with to defeat theses dark minions.

For honor, for glory, and for valor,
LONG LIVE THE VANGUARD!
