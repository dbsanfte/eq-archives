Adeon Methedras

Warlord - (guild leader)
Director of all guild operations either directly or thru councilors.

Born in one of the noble houses of Felwithe, Adeon was raised to be a Knight of House Methedras. Following in his father's footsteps, Adeon is honing his skills of battle and ambassadorship in the expectation of assuming his rightful inheritance one day. Traveling the length and breadth of Norrath has wizened this High Elf to see the world beyond the walls of his Elven city as one of wonder and great beauty. He also has seen great suffering and evil in many lands. Adeon has vowed to do all he can to stem the tide of darkness and spread the light of Tunare's justice to all peoples. He has met many folk from far off lands and his vision is often spoken of by these travelers. Knowing that there is strength in numbers he created a guild.

A group of heroes that will lead the way.
A legion of justice and honor.
An army of strength and bravery.
A Vanguard.
Let darkness beware for the dawn is nigh.
