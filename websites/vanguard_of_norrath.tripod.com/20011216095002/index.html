The Vanguard is a collection of Individuals resigned to uphold the virtues of Honor, Justice, and Integrity. The Vanguard looks to assist those in need, To defend those who ask, and to put down those who defy these principles.. Welcome to our Hall. You are invited to tour this site, see for yourself what we represent, who we are, and how we view our world of Norrath. When you are impressed, feel free to contact us. We look forward to hearing from you and trust that you are worthy to uphold the same ideals which we treasure and hold dear.

Saeadan Dane
