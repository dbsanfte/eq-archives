Saedean Dane

Second in command to Adeon. In his absence he will assume command of the Vanguard.
He also acts as Advisor and Guild Historian.

Saedean Dane was born the bastard son of a Freeport Barmaid and a Traveling Mapmaker. He spent his formative years mostly alone, working as a candle bearer in the Tower of Arcane Sorcery. It is here that he first learned his craft. It appealed to his curious nature and his general adversion to the militia lifestyle. A life which many of his friends drifted toward. His role in the Vanguard is one he treasures. He enjoy's his brotherhood as it allows him to give honest input and fill a pivotal role in the decisons of a guild of friends, brothers, and peers. He will do anything for his guild, for that is where he is most wanted. In fact, if you ask him, it's the only place he is wanted.
