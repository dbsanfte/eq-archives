Angar Descreamer

Angar grew up parentless in Freeport. He was put to work from a very early age tending to the gladiators in the arena. He watched and he learned. When he came of age he entered the competitions himself. It was during this time he came to worship Rallos Zek. He agrees with the basic principal that the strong will survive. But he does not believe the weak should be eliminated. The weak should be made strong. This should be done with honor. A trait he inherited from many of his gladitorial companions. Angar is an honorable individual who is always willing to support friends in anything they may need. Once he has given his word he keeps it at all costs, even at extreme risk to himself.
