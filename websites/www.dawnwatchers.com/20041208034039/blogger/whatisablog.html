What exactly is a blog???

From whatis.com (a well known IT resource for terminology and fact finding):

On a Web site, a blog, a short form for weblog, is a personal journal that is frequently updated and intended for general public consumption. Blogs generally represent the personality of the author or the Web site and its purpose. Topics sometimes include brief philosophical musings, commentary on Internet and other social issues, and links to other sites the author favors. The essential characteristics of the blog are its journal form, typically a new entry each day, and its informal style.

The author of a blog is often referred to as a blogger. People who post new journal entries to their blog may often say they blogged today, they blogged it to their site, or that they still have to blog.  Blogs are not related to the forums on this forum.

So be sure to post your blog every single day, use the editor to install links, graphics, or other HTML.  HTML other than that generated by the editor will be stripped, for security reasons.  Your blog may be syndicated to other sites on the Internet, consult the forum administrator for details.


So what is a BLOG posted on this forum?

Let me expand on that textbook edition:

A user's personal thought of the day, or funny quote, or anything on their mind they wish the world to know about that particular day. There are no restrictions on blog content but keep it to 2 pages of single spaced text or less per blog. It can be personal, it can be a diatribe, political or otherwise. It can also be filled with curse words, or with witty commentary about anything. It's a chance for the author to express something within them, externally, in the form of writing, for public consumption.

OUR 2 RULES ALL MEMBERS MUST FOLLOW:

1) Do not address your blog to other specific members of this forum!
2) This is not a message forum - no comments on other blogs here!

If the author posts a blog here but wants feedback, then that author must post a new topic on this forum in the message forums and link to their blog, here.  Remember, the blog page itself is NOT interactive, because that is not the spirit of true bloggism!  ;-) <wink>
ADD A BLOG EVERY DAY!  USE THE CALENDAR TO READ!

Be sure to add your blog (one or more) every single day or at least every so often on this forum! Use any of the calendar links to navigate days and months forward or backward to view any other blogs that may have been added.  The calendar is how you navigate. The default view is for any blogs added during the currently selected calendar month. To view blogs for a certain day only, if desired, click on any day links within the calendar, if any.  Use the calendar to change months and days as desired.

ADDITIONAL NOTES:

Any calendar day not linked means no blogs were added that day. The default view when entering the blog page from this forum forum or home page is for the current month and year. Remember, all blogs are in saved with the GMT time zone, not your local time zone, please note. For some of you, the date may have changed since most folks in the United States are numerous hours behind GMT.  So keep in mind all times are not local unless you have your offset set in your User Control Panel on the forum.  Only then will times reflect you local time zone. 

The reason all blogs are timestamped in GMT is because the blogs posted here may be syndicated internationally and GMT is the accepted World Time Zone.  Local times have no relevance globally.

Blogs are pruned automatically (the cutoff age in days is reported below the calendar). A "view" is any member other than the author who reads that specific blog. I did this so the numbers are a more accurate representation of actual views, by preventing the author from refreshing the page to skew the numbers by repeatedly viewing their own blog.
