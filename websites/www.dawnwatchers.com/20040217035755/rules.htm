Guild Objectives

Dawnwatchers aims to bring together those who want to explore the content while levelling in Norrath as well as having fun, gaining experience and making a bit of loot on the side! Boring XP grind in a mad race to mid 60's is not what we are about, we like to have fun while levelling. This is a brief list of things we think are important.

Helping each other.
Being polite to people.
Playing by the rules.
Gaining XP
FAIR distribution of loot.
Organised events.
Active participation in the running of the Guild.

If you agree, we'll get along famously and would love to welcome you!

How to contact us - Recruitment

Dawnwatchers welcomes new members in the range of level to 20-50. If you wish to join us, please register in our forums and post in the applications section giving your details. One of our officers will contact you or you can approach them direct. Current officers are :

Dwitherpot/Cromicon (Guildleader & Treasurer)
Klik (Chief Officer)
Elrosius (Main Raid Coordinator)
Paull/Paullette (Low Level Raid Coordinator)

New members initially have a pass a 30 day probation period during which participation in 4 grouping sessions or raids with guild members is required. We have a code of conduct for guild members as explained in the guild rules and the main purpose of the probationary period is to see if you can abide by these rules. Every member is expected to be completely familiar with the guild rules and these are shown below.

Guild Rules

Dawnwatcher members are expected to conduct themselves in a manner that will reflect favourably on the guild. To help you understand the requirements of our members and the conduct we expect you to display, we have designed 10 easy to remember common-sense rules, based on respect for others and fair play.

1. Guild tag and attendance.

We expect members to be proud to be a member of our guild and to display their guild tag at all times. This means you cannot go anonymous. To protect your privacy, the use of roleplay is allowed.

You must attend at least TWO events in any FOUR week period. We always endeavour to organise dates favourably and will make exceptions if need be, however blatant absenteeism will be noted and membership status will be reviewed.

2. Forum use

In our forums you will find details of planned raids, information on raid tactics, and other important information, which all members need to be aware of.

New members are expected to register in the forums on joining or with 24 hours, visit the forums at least twice a week and provide feedback to posts when requested. If you are offline and not in game, you can of course register when you are next online. This doesn't take long and you can use the forums to talk about the things that interest you, or to ask questions when you have a problem. Officers check the forums regularly and you will always get a quick response to any question.

3. Game channel use

The broadcasting of profanity, offensive language or racial slurs through any game chat channel (shout, occ, auction, guild) is forbidden.

Cybersexing is strictly forbidden as you don’t know how old other players are and we'll take a very dim view on any player who does this.

4. Fair looting

We believe in fair distribution of loot at all times. We have clear rules on this for guild raids – see raid loot rules. When grouping with non-guild members, its down to the group how the loot is split, but we expect all guild members to be fair in any arrangement.

Ninja looting means looting a corpse you didn't kill. If you didn’t kill it, you have no right to it, unless you have the express permission of the person who did kill it. At the very least the practice of ninja looting is extremely bad manners and it's often better to let it rot. Perhaps someone left the corpse because they didn’t want the loot, on the other hand, they may have not been able to loot due to aggro. Always ask first before looting it- this simple courtesy goes a long in enhancing the guild reputation and preserving your own.

5. Guild peace

Members are expected to maintain peace within the guild. If a dispute does occur within the guild, this should be carried out in /tell channels, not in guild. Officers will become involved if things get out of hand and take appropriate action as they see fit.

6. Peace outside the guild

Members are expected to maintain peaceful relations with all other people in Norrath. If you become involved in a dispute external to the guild, you should seek to resolve it amicably and quickly. Officers will become involved if things get out of hand and take appropriate action as they see fit.

7. Scamming

Members must never scam other players out of money or equipment. Bazaar trading or selling the fruits of hard earned skills is of course perfectly acceptable. More than one guild officer has a 24/7 bazaar trader and members can make a request to put some items on these traders.

8. SOE and UBISOFT conditions

All members will abide by SOE and UBISOFT terms and conditions. We do not tolerate any use of hack software and if that sort of thing appeals to you, you will not only be promptly ejected form the guild but your activity will be notified to GMs.

9. Chain of command

All members are expected to respond promptly to officer requests and to comply with instructions from officers. One of the roles of officers is to ensure members abide by the guild rules. If an officer considers that a member is in breach of the guild rules, they will discuss this with you and seek to ensure the situation is resolved fairly; officers may ask for an apology to be made or for a reparation to be paid, or take further action as they see fit. Officers are themselves subject to rules and if you disagree with an officer, you can refer the matter to the guildleader. Any decision by the guildleader is final.

10. Code of conduct

Dawnwatcher members are expected to conduct themselves in a manner that will reflect favourably on the guild at all times. We canot list and give examples of every possible situation, but hope the 10 rules we have laid out give members a clear picture of what we are about. If a guild member's behaviour does not match our requirements, we reserve the right to remove that member from the guild without any notice, although we will always endeavour to resolve a situation fairly before using this last resort.

Member Ranksand re-entry to guild

There are 4 ranks within the Guild.
1: Probation - not allowed any alts - awaiting review based on forum participation, raid attendance and guild chat participation.
2: Casual Member - allowed one alt in the guild, not allowed to be an Officer. This is based on forum participation, raid attendance and chat participation.
3: Full Member - allowed any number of alts within the guild. Allowed to be a candidate for an Officer rank. This is based on forum participation, raid attendance and chat participation.
4: Officer - All the rights of a Full Member but with additional responsibilities.

Re-entry to the guild must be approved by the guild leader and will not be allowed if a member has been removed on disciplinary grounds, joined another guild or left in a fit of pique.


Raid Loot Rules

There is usually one Master Looter at a raid. The person(s) will be designated by the Guild Leader, Chief Officer or Raid Coordinator, whoever is most senior attending.

The loot is collected throughout the raid and can be categorised as follows:

Vendor Trash
Nice Loot
No Drop Loot

Vendor Trash is sold at the end of each raid and the proceeds split amongst attending members. If you can't stay to the end you lose your share.

Nice Loot is announced by the Master Looter and can be rolled on at any convenient time but mainly at the end of the raid in the Bazaar/Nexus . All classes and races listed on the item can take part in the roll IF THEY ARE IN ATTENDANCE. /rand 3 333. Highest winning. If a draw, the darwing parties roll again, no others.

No Drop Loot needs to be rolled on by characters that can use the item in attendance at that time before the corpse rots. No Drop Loot must be announced and left on the corpse by the Master Looter so it is available for looting. If stuff rots, it rots.

Need Before Greed

The intention is that if a class that could use the item 'passed' on it and no other eligible classes wanted to roll then it is unclaimed for the ATTENDANT class specific roll and open for a free for all roll.

BUT, If there are classes eligible still present and they'd like to roll for it then only they can roll for it, as only they can use it.

If a class could use it but have a better item anyway, they should pass.

This will help distribute loot to those that can use it.

Ninja Looting.

This is against everything the guild stands for. It is depriving other members of upgrades and also depriving them of fun by ruining their enjoyment of the game.

Ninja Looting 'Nice Loot' and 'Vendor Trash' is subject to a warning and returning the looted item for proper distribution. Three such warnings and you are guildremoved.

Ninja Looting No Drop items results in immediate guildremoval. You have to take three actions to loot a No Drop item, including a confirmation window, and there are no excuses or exceptions. The Master Looter has to be especially careful.

Officer roles

All Officers are required to read and respond to the posts in the Officer Forum at least three times per week. This is an integral part of the Officers duty and is vitally important to the running of the Guild.

Guild Leader:
Writes policy for approval by Officers then Members, administers sanctions against members for violations of policy, ensures fair treatment to all members and assists all Officers in fulfilling their roles.

Chief Officer:
Aids the Guild Leader in decision making, upholds the written policy and acts as Guild Leader in his absence. Reports events and logs to the forums. Assists recruiting efforts of qualified members, generates prospects and follow-up on leads for new membership. Greets new members to the guild and ensures they are clear on the rules, who the officers are, and who the guild leader is. Ensures all new members go through an induction process. Monitors guild probationary members and assists deciding when they become full members with a post in the Officers forum for each case. Updates and maintains the guild roster.


Main Raid Raid Coordinator:
Organizes and conducts guild raids, researches potential raid sites based on level of possible attendees and posts concise, relevant zone, loot and mob information 7 days prior to raid taking place. Advertises the raids on the forums, raid calendar and Guild Message Of The Day in game. Assists recruiting efforts of qualified members, generates prospects and follow-up on leads for new membership. Greets new members to the guild and ensures they are clear on the rules, who the officers are, and who the guild leader is. Ensures all new members go through an induction process. Monitors guild probationary members and assists deciding when they become full members with a post in the Officers forum for each case.

Officer Rules

No person, because of his/her rank within the guild, is exempt from these standards of conduct. Officers especially are held to a higher criteria, and must take the responsibility for following, implementing, and enforcing these standards.

It is an Officer's duty to visit the boards more often than members and to provide requested feedback in a timely manner. This is necessary to the functioning of the guild and an essential task in an Officers role.

Abuse of authority is specifically prohibited.

Officers must investigate and report, as appropriate, alleged incidents of misconduct or violations of the standards of conduct and post all information in the Officer Forum.

Abusive or profane language from an officer directed toward a member is prohibited.

Officers who fail to perform tasks inherent to their positions will be considered as delinquent in their duties.

There are many tasks that are necessary to properly manage the guild. Such responsibilities include, but are not limited to:

Enforcing rules, policies, and guidelines, and taking decisive actions to preserve the integrity and effiency of the guild.

Investigate and follow-up complaints of members and the general public.

Pass-on needed or important information to the members in a timely manner.

Invite new recruits into the guild and insuring that recruits meet the minimum qualifications.

Notifying the guild Recruiting Officer of new members and alternate characters that are invited into the guild.

Officers will be attentive to their duties. Inattentive behavior includes failure to perform assigned tasks or those actions that negatively affect the operation of the guild.

Any other action deemed necessary by the guild leader.
