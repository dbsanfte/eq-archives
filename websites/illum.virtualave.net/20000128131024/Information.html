Some Thoughts Information

I think it would be a good idea that once people begin hitting levels 35+ that they should begin spending time in dungeons where you cant use SoW and other abilities. Its actually really good exp if you find a good group and it'll help us get to know our jobs in a party better. Things like the Mistmoore trip the level 30s took together last week were cool too. Id rather see our guys working to form groups and such together so that we will be able to handle item camps soon so this kinda stuff would be good practice. Anyways just some thoughts. Btw, for those of you who want to kite and such all the way up your levels, the Butcherblock Guards and East Karana Guards work till level 48. At that point you have to move to gnomes and freeport guards or mebbe the occasional spectre.

Ill be using this spot to post things like where people of level ranges and such can fight. As well as where are members are frequenting so others can find groups. This will take some time, and I hope it comes out well. -Fayvren
