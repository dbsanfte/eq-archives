Loot rules for NK members.

 

(1) FOR NO-DROP ITEMS: Loot will be distributed to the classes that can use it and are attending the raids. Individuals who can use a loot drop but have a comparable or better item of that type are expected to defer to those individuals who can and will use the item. “There will be a specific class loot list for items dropping from the planes. “

 

To see how the list is made go here.

(2) FOR REGULAR DROP ITEMS: These items are to be looted by the group leader in each group that is attending the raid and gets the kill, NOT the raid leader.

(3) Loot will be random after the raid is over, for everyone who can use the item. Individuals who can use a loot drop but have a comparable or better item of that type are expected to defer to those individuals who can and will use the item.

(4) In the case where items obtained from a raid have no class represented, then said items are to be distributed in an open lottery to all guild members in the end of the raid.

(5) If no one wants or needs an item, that item will either go into Guild Bank or be ran domed among guild members who are at the guild raid. Guild leader / raid leader will decide this.

(6) Those who win ran domed loots will be excluded from the random list until everyone else who is qualified to random obtain an item.

(7) Latecomers will not participate in the looting procedure. A latecomer will be counted as a person being fifteen minutes overdue after the raid is suppose to start. There might be scenarios when this will be nullified. The raid leader / guild leader / officer will have the final say if the person will be granted to be in the loot random or not.

(8) Early-goers will not participate in the looting procedure. Early-goers is a person that is leaving before the appointed time that the raid will last. This time limit will be set by the raid-leader in his/hers “checklist of the raid” that is being presented on the guild board. A person going link dead is not an early-goers even if he/she can’t log on again. If there is loot that the link dead person should be ran doming for, the raid leader / guild leader / officer will random for him/her.

(9) Events with good/rare loots (Gods/Dragons/Dungeon Bosses): Guild leader/raid leader will pick one item and tag it as good item. It will only be ran domed among those who are qualified to roll for it.

(10) Twinks / secondary characters will not get any loot from a guild raid. See rule 4 and 5 to see what will happen to the loot. If there is a NO-DROP ITEM and no one else in the guild can use it or already have it, then the twink may loot it after the guild leader/raid leader have said OK for the twink to do so.

(11) After the raid all cash and sellable items are to be given to the person that is the appointed banker to be kept in the guild vault.

(12) The guild leader and/or officers have the right to decide that a player should have a specific item instead of another player under special circumstances. The guild leader and/or officer will explain why the person got it on the guild board after the raid.


Failure to adhere to this policy will jeopardize participation in future Nordiska Kompaniet raids.
