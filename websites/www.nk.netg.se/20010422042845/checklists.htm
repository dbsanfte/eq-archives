A CHECKLIST FOR A RAID

 

This list is made so that people organizing raids will be able to get all the information out to the guild and that the guild members will be able to get some information about what the raid is going to be like.

 

WHAT:
What are we doing?

 

WHO:
Who are going to be in the raid? Might be more then NK going.

 

WHEN:
What date and time CET?

 

WHERE:
Where are we doing it?

 

WHY:
What are our goals on doing this?

 

HOW:
How will we reach those goals we have set up for the raid?

 

PETS:

Will pets be allowed on the raid? If they are will they only be used in specific spots or all the time?

 

LEVELS:
What level is the LOWEST to join in the raid? What level is recommended?

 

LEADERS:
Who is Commander of the Raid? He/she that set up the raid and that person is the one responsible for getting everything together. That also means that if we need a key class to make the raid he/she is responsible to get that class to come from out-/inside of the guild. Who is second in command – Lieutenant-Commander? He/she that is appointed by the commander that is holding the raid. Second in command is also tactical officer, you ask him/her about anything BEFORE you ask the commander of the raid. A second Lieutenant-Commander is also appointed for backup.

 

THE PLAN:
A ruff plan on how we will compose the groups, how many in the groups and how many groups will be needed to make the raid.

 

SPECIFIC TACTIC:

Any specific thing/-s you have to watch out for in the area? Traps, monsters, fake walls etc.... Is there specific monster that has to be killed in a specific way? Dose it have to be pulled some where special? Dose it have any special ability or spell that a player should look out for?

 

REVISED TIMES INFO BELOW:

This is a more in-depth information on when the raid will start, when to be there and where. What to do and not do. A ruff raid plan on where to go and where to camp. If a chat room will be established, if we need to log out and what the password will be. How long the raid will be, no exact time is needed. Tell what our prime, second and third objective is on the raid. Having fun is always a prime objective so that one do not count.

 

LOOT:
Some info on what items drops and from who will keep questions down to a minimum. I recommend that you go to www.eqmaps.com and look up the items list for the specific zone and put it here.

 

EQUIPMENT:
If there are special items needed for the raid like resistance stuff it will be written down here. 

MAPS:
Post link to where to get a map of the zone we are going to do the raid in.  www.eqmaps.com are recommended here to.

 

GUIDES:
Put a guide on what to expect on the raid here so people don’t go in totally blind.

 

RESERVING A SPOT:
Will be starting to take effect in the near future since we are growing again even if some have dropped out.
