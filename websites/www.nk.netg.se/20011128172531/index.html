[Tuesday, Nov 14, 2001]
NK moves server. - Zaba - 12:30
Tuesday, November 14th. Nordiska Kompaniet have moved to the Antonius Bayle server. Due to Verant technical difficulties, the guild will not be reformed until Monday the 19th of November. After that we will be ready to rock'n'roll =) Hope to see you all there.

We are accepting membership applications from characters L58 and above, please post on the board NK Forums

[Monday, Octo 29, 2001]
NK is back baby, NK is back. - Freyi - 14:33
Sunday october 28th. Nordiska Kompaniet comeback day! After nearly a month
of slumber we finally got moving again. And what a sunday it was.
We started off killing 2 Lord Doljonijiarnimorinar.
After that it was time for Velketor himself.
Boy what i fight it was, 10-12 people LD and among those the main tank
and second tank + clerics.
But we kept hitting him and in the end we had a total of 2 deaths and a very dead Velketor.

After these 3 nice kills resulting in some decent items we headed to
Dragon Necropolis to give this pile of dragon waste a try:

First a try to get our things in order, and on second try he went down pretty
smoothly. A first kill for NK which really is a milestone, for ST Key mob down.

The weekend have been pretty weird with many spawntimes beeing totally
out of whack.
Personally I know of several (5+) lozdial spawns and if not
more Venril Sathir spawns.
We did get a little peice of that action. A VS kill givin Efren and Elviis some
SS legs, a lozidal kill with map for Knus and Lozidial Shield for Nossy.
Saturday was also filled with some ToV action, drop rate was horrendous but we
scored some Leather Legs for Reptil, Plate BP for Varrioth, Plate legs for Zirek
and some other assoreted stuff.
