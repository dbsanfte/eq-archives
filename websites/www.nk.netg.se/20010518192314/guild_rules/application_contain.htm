What the application should contain.

 

First of all we have a lvl 35 limit on applying for guild.

1. Who your two sponsors are

2. The character you apply with...name, race, class, lvl
3. What you are looking for in a guild
4. Three things you DON'T want to see in a guild
5. Other chars...name, race ,class, lvl and if they are part of other guilds
6. Who you know in the guild
7. Who you talked to in the guild
8. The hours you usually play on, (GMT time, specify time zone or continent please). We are asking this question since we are a European based guild and that makes it hard for Asian and American players to enjoy the guild to its fullest. It is no fun to play the game if the main force are playing on the "wrong" time, all the time and you are no use to the guild if you can't do the raids. Trust us we know.

We play between 17:00 GMT to 23:00 GMT on weekdays and we want to see people play that time so we can team more with the guild. So if you are going to apply to NK please think about this before you hand in the application.
