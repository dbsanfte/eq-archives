The first Fear raid for a druid.

 

Sunday 27/8

 

Sitting down on the ground with between 20 to 30 people makes you feel mighty. This was the feeling that I had when I sat with NK and the icy-bunch. It was 11:20 in the morning and some people where trying to shake of there hangovers. Some made it, some didn’t, no names but they know whom they are.

We are divided in to groups and I get in to a group with a fellow druid and a SK. We are group 4 says the SK I go, k. The icy-bunch leader then says: the goal for the next hour is to get the clerics in to the north camp spot and camp them. Nothing else is important. Feeling good, fast and strong I think to myself.... he he easy as pie.

 

 

 


The icy-bunch leader says: We will go in to the zone one group at a time. Run to the NW corner if you are being beaten on, if not go to the N camping spot and camp. Group 1 make ready to start the first run.

Looking around all starts to move towards the portal that is gaping for our souls. I have a hard time seeing my group members but thinking that we are group 4 I will find them before we go in. Having 1000´s of questions I try to be silent and not ask my group members anything and sound like a newbie...I fail. Q: Are there more coming in to this group? A from druid: I hope so. A from SK: Don’t think so. A from me: Oh?

Group 1 RUUUN! 6 brave souls stair at the portal and takes a flying start. Group 2 make ready to go next on my mark. Feeling I have more questions I keep quit so I won’t miss anything now. Rumours trickle down threw the channels. All dead in group 1. They got a good pull though, taking many things from the portal. Not feeling so mighty anymore and wondering what how many are many? I look around starting to feel sweat forming between my shoulder blades.

Group 2 RUUN! 6 more brave souls start to run towards the portal and run in to it and vanish. Not being able to contain my nervousness I ask. Q: Just for your information I have never done this before so I will follow you guys. A from druid: Neither have I. A from SK: Neither have I. I only know we are suppose to run NW. I am thinking WHAT!!!!! No one in this "mighty" group has done this before!!! Looking at the map over Fear I see that the trail you should follow goes NE, then N, then follow the burning wall to the north camp if you are not attacked. I say to group: according to the map you take a little to the right and then you run straight north. Answer from druid: Okay I follow you. Answer from SK: Okay I follow you. I think to myself WTF?!?!? When did I get to be guide....!?!? We are sooo toasted. Trying to hide my nervousness I say nothing.

Information trickles down some in group 2 made it. YEAH I think we might make it. Another member joins and takes charge. He seems to have done this before, I hope trying to be quiet. I sit down to med up the missing mana. The newest group member says: I am going in with group 3 to try to pull some more stuff from the portal. I think to myself: NOOOOOOOO! And in the next second I think: Take a grip man what is the worst that can happen? A little voice inside me says: You can die. Starting to rethink this plain raid I have to steel myself to not to scream. Group 3 GO GO GO! 8 brave people start to run and one of the group staples turns from blood red to grey. I find myself thinking: Hope you make it.

Group 4 get ready to make the run on my mark. Group 4? Hey I am in group 4! I am going to run in there and die now? Eeeh don’t want to or do I? The druid in the group breaks in to my thoughts by saying: Any one else sweating? Without thinking I say: No kidding. The SK says: Me to. I feel like doom is coming in giant strides. SK says: Refresh my SoW please. Casting on him and casting on me even if I cast it a minute ago, I feel steadier. Information trickles down: Datortek made it!!! He is camped!! YEAH!!!!
