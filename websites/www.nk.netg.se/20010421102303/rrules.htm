Raid rules

 

With the start of guild events, raids and expeditions, Nordiska Kompaniet must establish uniform rules and policies for how we should conduct ourselves, both for our own benefit and organization as well as to demonstrate and practice common courtesy and etiquette, both in and out of our guild. These rules should serve as a comprehensive but not exclusive or limiting set of guidelines for composure and conduct. It should remain important that in all events, our conduct reflects the entire Nordiska Kompaniet guild, and thus, all members should conduct themselves honorably and in accordance with our principles, virtues and beliefs, but most of all, have FUN!


(1) Any full-fledged Nordiska Kompaniet member may lead a raid, either individually or in a group. Guild Events are open only to members of Nordiska Kompaniet, unless if explicitly permitted by the guild leader and officers. The raid leader holds the final authority on the conduct, but not necessarily procedural practices, of the raid itself. The raid leader should post information about the raid to inform the guild members of the coming raid/event: See the Raid checklist. In turn, the raid leader must provide opportunity for persons to sign up and reserve spots for participation in the raid.

(2) All members attending the raid should have told the raid leader on the board so that he/she knows they are coming and so the raid can be done.

(3) Be on time for the raid, be logged on for 30 minutes before the raid starts since there might be last minut changes. Tip: Make preparations and ask people that can teleport the day before the raid.


(4) There will be ONE leader of the event. The leader will appoint a minimum of one backup leaders prior to starting the raid. The usual way is that the group leaders in the other groups are backup leaders. There will also be one person assigned as the assist person and one backup person to assist.

(5) You should have the proper equipment coming to the raid and don’t expect the guild to provide it, it’s your responsibility that you have it.

(6) You must pay attention to the raid organizer at all times and keep up with what the organizer says. If you don't keep up, and you get left behind, you might a) die, b) start a train and kill us all and everyone else in the zone, c) lose your corpse. All 3 of these options are unacceptable.

(7) No extended periods of AFK. You got to go pee? Pee before hand. Need to walk the dog? Walk the dog before hand. You need to go hang out with the husband/boyfriend or wife/girlfriend? Put him/her to bed beforehand.

(8) You are to remain throughout the duration of the raid that is specified by the raid leader on the post about the raid/event: See the Raid checklist. If you leave early you forfeit the participation of loot procedures: See Loot Rules.

(9) If you have a question you ask it in this order: first: in-group say, second: in guild say, third: OOC, DON’T send tells unless they are most urgent. Only group leaders should talk to the raid leader. If you are not a group leader and want to pass some suggestions/comments to raid leader, tell your group leader to do so on behalf of you. Do not ask question about loot or "what if...." No one will use shout except for the raid leader, use only OOC to prevent spam and confusion. This allows anything done in shouts to be most urgent message form the leader, puller, /assist or any other key person on the raid.

(10) There will be no bickering/arguing about anything up to, and including loot. What the raid leader's say is final.

(11) Raids that are not typical, involve multiple guilds, or take place in areas to which these rules may not be entirely applicable, are to have unique rules drawn up by a committee of persons represented from all participating guilds and groups. Should Nordiska Kompaniet initiate a raid, it shall utilize its base General Raid and Loot Rules.

(12) Should the raid leader wish to utilize rules that are not standard and not general, the leader must obtain permission by getting a majority of votes of the guild leader and officers. Following the granting of permission, the raid leader must post any and all rule changes that deviate from the General Raid and Loot Rules. At any time, with the majority of the guild leader and officers, reserves the right to overrule specific procedures or rules advocated within a particular raid.

(13) At any time during the raid, the raid leader, and/or the guild leader or an officer, may dismiss any raid participant for any reason, but most notably for violation of these rules, or for either intentionally or unintentionally endangering the fluidity and the success of the raid. At the end of all raids, the raid leader must present a final report to be posted on the Guild Events forum before both all in the guild.


ALL rules are up for debate and reform, please post input. Please feel free to submit your comments, thoughts, reactions and suggestions for improving these "general" raid and loot rules. It should be noted that while these rules won't cover everything, they aim to be a standard for future raids and guild events.
