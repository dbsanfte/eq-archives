How the class loot list is made for ALL planes.

 

A Class Representative will be nominated, “if not already done”, to maintain an updated and fair loot list for his/her class.

The representatives sit down with the rest of the class members that are primary players and either random on who gets to start choosing when creating the list, or use level, all ready acquired plane items, how many times they have been in a plane raid with the guild, (any plane), etc… to decide who starts choosing.

 

Soon as it is done, a list is written down on all parts that are dropped.

 

The person that is first to choose say the item that he/she wants and then all go down the line of all members of that class. Then you repeat this until the list is full.

 

New players that enters the guild is added last on the list and if there are more then one joining us at the same time they talk to each other with the help of the class representative on who starts to pick one item. If you feel that this is unfair to the people just entering the guild I just want to point out that with out us they might not go to a plane raid at all, ever.... So we in the guild think it is a small “price” to for new member to be in three to five plane raids before they get there first peace.

 

If the person who is first on list isn’t on that raid the next person on the list loots the item.

 

The class reprecentatives will be the only once to be able to change the list. All the lists will be monitord by the officers and the class representatives and checked against the loot drop on the raid. If the change looks unfair or somting happens in the guild that makes so that the list should to be uppdated the officers and the class representative will look in to it and do the changes that are to be made, if any.

 

We will NOT fill out the list and add any secondary/twinks that the guild members might have. (This might change in the future, but for now we don’t).
