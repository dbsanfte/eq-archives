Temple of Droga - A Learning Experience
zdir - 2001-03-26 08:58:12

A Big Thank You To Those That Came Out

In a valley of the Frontier Mountains just outside the Overthere, some of HPAs best waited as thier leader hurried to make his own deadline. Many had gathered in advance to participate in the raid which made the registering (only 30 minutes late) much easier. After getting organized and picking up a few straglers the three groups headed into the Temple of Droga.

From the moment they stepped into the zone, this group showed they meant business as they made short work of every goblin that got in their path. The monks pulled rooms at a time but they were still no match. The groups ventured further and further into the temple laying waste to anything that moved.

After recovering from a couple organizational hicups the groups were once again ready to push even further and head on towards the chief. The raid pushed on to the "pool room" where they remained safely for a moment, a calm before the storm. On command, the monks pulled a goblin which in turn pulled more and more goblins. They came out of every corner overwhelming the groups. The healers cast selflessly to keep the tanks alive as they were beaten down by the enemy. Our groups were dropping left and right but still they kept fighting. Finally, for the sake of the raid, G1 with the only cleric evacced. G3, left in the depths, fought valiantly but could not fend off the legions of goblins. The CR mission was not easy but with some good organization and reliable members everyone got a rez and ported out safely.

Unfortunately no mentionable loot was obtained in the course of the raid, though we learned much about the zone for the future.

A special thank you to Nildadar, an HPA applicant, for joining us on the raid and doing a fine job for G1. Also thank you Myrr and Lexas, leaders of G2 and G3 respectively, for making my life much easier. Also thanks to the HPA members that joined our raids for the first time. A personal thank you to all the HPA members that showed up for the raid on time, you made things run much smoother.

If you have any comments on the raid, raid rules, or future raids please post them in the Raids area of our message board.

Upcomming Raids
zdir - 2001-03-20 16:06:58

New Raids - Fun for all ages

I'm trying to come up with a system that I might best be able to serve the needs of as many members of various levels as possible within HPA. As such I've decided to try to plan 3 raids of various lvl ranges to include more people during the month of April.

These raids do not have a maximum level cap as higher level members are always appreciated in any raid. But the raids have been chosen so that the members of the respective levels will get the most out of it. These raids are also to be designed so that the parties can get into the heart of the zone rather than just the tip, meaning that although a zone may be suitable for lvl 20s at the entrance, the raid may have a required level of 25 so that the parties can venture deeper.

These raids of course are not our only ones during the month, as we are always accepting CoE invites and planes/dragon invites as well as those raids planned by Drizden. These ones are simply HPA led and HPA focused.

Please take a look at the possible raid targets for the following dates and give some feedback.

Sunday April 8
Saturday April 14
Sunday April 22

The votes will stay up until the end of the month upon which the raid will begin to organize.

Please keep in mind that we are a guild and as such we aren't only here for ourselves. These raids are meant to help our brothers and sisters become stronger and in turn they will help us. Your participation in these raids is always taken into account when you need the guilds assistance. If you are online at the time of the raid and are capable of attending the raid, then you should as an HPA member feel obligated to attend. I can't make you, but I will ask that you make an effort.
