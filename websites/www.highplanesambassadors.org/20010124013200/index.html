RAID on PAW 27 Jan 2001
drizden - 2001-01-21 11:51:45

As told by Slogos Solaris (transferred directly from the Message Forums!)

Hey guys, I just wanted to let you know that I am planning a raid on Splitpaw for Saturday January 27th. I am recommending that only 30+ people attend, but all are welcome. The raid will be starting at 9:30PM EST, but it would be nice if people could arrive a little bit early. I would like to get this raid started on time.

So far we have 1 full group, but I personally would be a lot more comfortable with 2.

We will be going after quest items and camps that drop some good loot that you could sell. Don't expect to get much because a lot of the stuff is rare.

As for quest items, I know there are a lot of quest drops in SP. I think the casters would like to go after Kurrpok Splitpaw. He drops some jewel that is part of the "Jewelled Skull" quest, which is a pretty good item.

If there are any quests items that you would like to go after, please let me know.

Before I log on for the raid I will be checking this string. If there are any changes that need to be made then please let me know through here or in game (as soon as I log on please).

Now, before I logged off I went into SP to scout it out. I don't believe there is much, if anything that sees thru invis there as I went pretty deep. But I have spotted a few problems.

Ever since last patch, it has been pretty heavily camped. There are a lot of high twenties, low 30s ppl there, but if we go deep enough I am hoping that that will not pose a problem.

Also this zone is heavily populated with 29 - 32 mobs. That means that if we train, we are TOAST. There will be NO running to the zone, because if we are deep enough, I can almost guarentee you won't make it. I will try to get a druid in each group so the tanks can have someone evac them. Casters (myself included), I know you hear this time and time again, but I'm going to have to remind you again. Please do NOT panic. If you run even a few metres away, that could agro a lot more mobs due to the high mob population.

And one last thing. I am asking that everyone (if possible) print out a map of Splitpaw from EQAtlas as well as read the information from that site. It will be very useful for if you get lost. The info page is here and the map is here.

Well, I think that's about it. Time to go to work =(. Once I find out who all is coming I will have the groups for you. If you wish to add yourself to the list of people who are coming please contact Blitzen.

Thanks again everyone, and I hope this raid is a big success. (And please forgive me if I forget anything. This is my first time planning a raid)

-Slogos

Efreet Raid Results Saturday Night
drizden - 2001-01-21 11:43:47

As told by Ninam Skiewalker (transferred directly from the Message Forums!)

The results are in, but let me tell the story first.

I first met up with Hildadar in Nektulos Forest, to help his RL wife with a few buffs. En route to our rendezvous, I of course had to dispatch an inkie guard. Pansy. From there, Hild and I ported to Lavastorm. We met up with a 48 ranger by the name of Jarntik, and awaited the arrival of Terinay.
Terinay soon arrived, and we took a jaunt into Sol B. A few kobolds were just screaming to die !! Inside we met Meolan, a friend of the guild and Vokk. We had our full group, but didn't have a monk. However, a monk of the 59th season (Arlos) helped us up to the room. We kicked ass the whole way up there, and didn't even take any names. There we were, in the room where the Efreeti Lord was due to arrive. Many of his guards, imp protectors, died in the next couple of hours, but no sign of the Efreeti. It was soon after that our ranger friend had to leave, and a bard of the 60th season was waiting in the wings.
With the help of the bard (Shadowspear), we dispatched many more imps and lava guardians. Where the Efreeti was due to show, we obliterated 6 placeholders in a row. But finally, the Efreeti Lord himself showed up !! After a lenghty battle, the Efreeti went down, and a nice set of Golden Efreeti boots were rewarded to our group. I, Ninam Skiewalker, received the first pair.
Then we sat in anticipation for another showing.
We were rewarded for our patience, and two spawns later the Efreeti showed his powerful visage again. Except this time, he had company: a lava guardian and an imp protector. Shadowspear mezzed the Efreeti and charmed the LG to help kill the imp. Upon the imp's death, it was time to kill the LG. Then the glorious moment came upon us: DEATH TO THE EFREETI !!
The second pair of boots went to Vokk, and we were too tired to continue on for any more. So, I ported us outta there, and we bid each good night. Some members of that party would like to try again Sunday night, so please get in touch with Vokk, Hildadar, Terinay or Drizden to express an interest. Please be level 40+, because it is highly dangerous in there.
Again, thanks got to Arlos, Jarntik, Shadowspear and Meolan for their help. And of course, thanks to our own members Hildadar, Terinay and Vokk. It was a great night and I look forward to many more.
P.S. I know that this raid was kinda put together hastily, and I take full responsibility for that. I approached Drizden on this on late Wednesday night, so there was not much time to talk to anyone about getting group(s) together. I am sorry for those that were left out.

Ninam Skiewalker

Thanks to everyone who helped me and Vokk with our epic stuff.
