Joining
A little about membership..

Gaining membership in Storm's Fury is not easy. Our standards are high and recruiting opportunities are often limited. We are a close community of friends who enjoy the challenge of doing the most with the least, so we work hard to control our guild size.

We are taking applications now for anyone who is level 58 and up. *Only* exceptional players may be considered for membership on a limited basis. Applicants are expected to have a Cobalt Scar portal key, Sky Shrine key, Sebilis key, and resist gear to reach 115 resist in each area unbuffed.

Recruits should strive for excellence in their class and be able to dedicate a considerable amount of time to game play. We seek people that play daily and are committed to raiding. Acceptable candidates not only know their class but also mesh with the guild and demonstrate a well-developed sense of community. Conduct on message boards and with others in game plays a role in your membership.

New recruits will be invited to join us on a few raids so we can get to know each other. If all goes well you will be accepted as a probational member for a month. As a Probie you are expected to group with the guild, attend raids, and get to know us better. Probies are not eligible for raid loot unless the drop is a class specific item and all full members have it. After four weeks the guild will vote on your membership. If you receive 4 no votes then your trial period will be terminated. If it’s a mutual fit you will be awarded full membership in our family.

If you think we are the right place for you please send an E-Mail to Cerulean at cerulean@perfectblueproductions.com.

Good luck.
