Who is Storm's Fury?

Who is Storm's Fury? Well, Storm's Fury is a collective of friends in EverQuest who are out there to conquer the many creatures and challenges that EverQuest holds while bettering ourselves as a collective. Storm's Fury is composed of high level players only which is required in order to accomplish our goals in the game. We have no real set rules governing how we conduct ourselves in game or how the guild runs (other than recruitment). We are only expected to use common sense when it comes to how we conduct ourselves in game. We are not an uptight guild either. We cuss, smoke, drink, pull Innoruuk to the southwest corner, and anything else friends do. We take a light hearted approach to the game but we also take the game very serious when the time requires.

We have gone through many changes. We've had our down times, we've had our up times, but no matter what happens we will stick together. We have a guild leader and officers but there is no true hierarchy within Storm's Fury. We govern ourselves as a collective and each member is expected to do whatever keeps the guild running smoothly and abstain from anything that would hinder the members or our reputation.
