May 15--
Rules Change!
These are the first of some new rules that are being set forth but the council. They will go into effect on Monday, May 21. If you have any questions or comments, please contact one of the council members. The rules are presented here in the exact wording that they were voted on by the council. The vote passed with a final count of 5 out of 5 in favor.

OFFICERS
Officers of the guild must be Level 30 or over. 10 levels of that must have been gained while the member was in the guild. There will be a probationary period of 1 week, during which, the officer may be called upon for input when a council member is resolving a problem. This will give us an idea of how the officer will react if a problem is brought to his/her attention. We don't need to use their input, just get an idea of what they would do in a situation. (Edit: No current officers will be demoted due to this rule)

BEHAVIOR
There is to be no begging in guildchat. This means ports, cash, or powerleveling. This doesn't mean that you can't ask for things every once in a while, just that we should never hear "Somebody give me some pp, PLZ!" or anything similar. Asking "Is anyone porting?" repeatedly without getting a response, usually means that nobody is porting.

The main reason behind these rules are pretty simple. We've had way too many people begging in guildchat, and it has become disruptive. Also, making any member over level 15 an officer has left us with as many officers as we have members. As the guild grows, the conditions for Officership must be raised as well, or else we will all end up as officers, and that would defeat the purpose of having them at all.
Thanks,
--Lomein
