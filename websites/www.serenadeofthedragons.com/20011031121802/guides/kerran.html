Kerran Toy Quest Guide

Compiled by Lomein Noodles, with special thanks to Perfect Imperfections
This is a short guide to a short quest which results in a nice range slot item for monks. It's stats aren't much, but they're a nice bonus for a slot that is often empty at lower levels. This quest can be completed around 17th level or so. Once again, the maps aren't mine.

Kerra Isle


Near the docks in Kerra Isle there are several children (called Tesqs) sitting around a campfire. Talking to them will give you a little information about the Kerrans, and one will talk about wanting a gnomish toy.

You say, 'Hail, kerran tseq'

kerran tseq says 'Yes. You bring us toy?!

kerran tseq says 'Errr. You smell funny. You bring us [toy]?

You say, 'What about a toy?'

kerran tseq says 'You go, bring us gnome toy. You not come back or we eat you and then you die like short one.

kerran tseq says 'Gnome toy! You bring. You good person.

You say, 'i will bring a gnome toy'

kerran tseq says 'I not know where gnome toy from. I only heard of them, never seen. Where you find gnomes? Maybe they there. Go, hurry!

Where better to find gnomes then in Steamfont? Not much else to do here, in fact this step can be skipped, it just gives a little background for the story. Kerra Isle is a rather barren little place, and alomost always devoid of other players.

Steamfont Mountains


Now that you have reached Steamfont, you can go to visit Nilit Druzlit, who will tell you that one of his clockworks has gone on a rampage.

You say, 'Hail, Nilit Druzlit'

Nilit Druzlit says 'Eh? Eh?! Talk not now! My [creation] is running wild! Destroy it for me, please! If you do you can keep whatever parts you want from it.

You say, 'What creation?'

Nilit Druzlit says 'Did you kill it yet? What? Bah! Yes, I made it. Nilit's clockwork it is. I made it to carry my toys to and from Ak'Anon but now it's gone wild and started attacking people. Please, destroy it!

Head out in front of the minotaur caves and begin to kill everything you see. If you are a higher level character, please remember to have some consideration for the people that are still getting exp in the area. Eventually, a named clockwork "Nillit's Contraption" will spawn. The contraption seems to be around 16-18th level, and doesn't cast. It doesn't hit terrible hard, and shouldn't be much of a challenge. It is a random spawn, so don't get discouraged if it doesn't spawn immediately. There are some thigs that you can do to keep yourself occupied. If you are a smith, Iron Oxide, a componant used to dye armors appears on the ground round the giant gears and on the machinery, and if you are around level 40 or so, you may want to also try for a Hero Bracer (but that is another guide).
