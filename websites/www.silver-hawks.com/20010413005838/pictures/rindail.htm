Rindail

Rindail staggered back as the Iskar Necromancer Venril Sathir let out massive lifetap. Rindail quickly purged himself and his group of the poison which would have taken his life and his friends’ lives given the chance to take course. Venril was more than a match for Rindail and his mighty group of friends. Rindail had seen and done very much in his 52 seasons of wandering the lands of Norrtah. This battle by far was one of his hardest. As the after effects of the lifetap still made his head reel, Rin thought back to an important day in his youth......

Rindail did not have a normal childhood. His life began when the wealthy Elvish Ambassador to the Western Human Lands decided to “go out for a night on the town” in Qeynos 52 seasons and 9 months ago. To say the least he had a few too many elven wines and, since he could not stagger back to his horse, ended up staying the night with Sally the barmaid at the Fish & Ale. When he awoke the next morning at around noon he realized his actions and quickly paid a wizard to gate him home. Sally was left to raise Little Rin by herself.

Rather than deal with the bag of trouble that Rin posed as a youngster Sally threw him out into the wilderness at the ripe age of 1. The next day the starving Rin was taken in by a friendly family of bears who treated him just like a cub of their own. The family of bears treated little Rin well for the next 6 years of his life. At 7 Rin was discovered by the Druids. They took Rin from his family of bears, much to the bear’s disappointment. Their first action was to give Rin a bath, much to Rin’s disappointment, but everyone else’s (even the bear’s) joy.

He training started immediately following the bath. Despite his lack of wisdom in most scholarly terms Rin soon proved a quick learner and very intelligent. The druids, pleased with his performance quickly sent him out to rid the world of evil. Rin chose to start his riding with the evil gnolls that lived in BlackBurrow near his home. Rin took to killing gnolls with enthusiasm. He even got the nickname of Rindail The Dentist because of his habit of pulling their teeth out as treasures and wearing the teeth around his neck.

One day while Rindail was traveling to BlackBurrow he was attacked by several undead. The dreaded boned skeletons hit Rindail before he could cast and soon had him down to dangerously low life. It was then that a mighty ranger steeped in, slaying the undead before they could harm the poor druid anymore. Rindail quickly thanked his savior by the name of HawkFeather. Rindail noticed the markings of a guild on his sleeves. When asked about this HawkFeather told him of a band of adventurers by the name of the Silver Hawks, and invited Rindail to join him and his guild. Rindail decided at that moment that the world of Norrath was too dangerous for one person alone, and if he were to survive he must have friends he can depend on. It was that day the Rindail became a lone wanderer no longer, and became a wanderer of the Silver Hawks..........

Rindail snapped back from his dream and turned his attention on Venril. Venril could kill him and his fellow fighters. There would be no HawkFeather to save them tonight. As word of their eminent downfall spread, Rindail quickly began to work the magic of his evacuation spell. “Oh well,” he thought as a shimmering portal opened, “guess I’ll head home for a few days. Mom always gives me free beer and elf wine.”

Sinudorm, November 2000
