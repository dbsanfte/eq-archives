GAMEPLAY SITES:

 

Of course we can't leave out the most important site of all, everquest.com! This is the official EQ site, though many sites are more detailed about the game. Here you can find posted down times, ask about technical help, and of course read all the fun posts on the message boards. Remember though, be polite when posting here!

This is probably one of my FAVORITE EQ sites because it is updated daily, and always has posts about the latest issues. Here is not only a good place to see what Gordon and other Verant employees have to say, but to also find out about events going on throughout the servers. Also, there is a quest sectin, bestiary, items database, server status with exact numbers, and the list goes on.

I would have to say this site ranks up there with Everlore. It is updated almost daily and has probably one of the best item databases out there. The message boards on this site are pretty active and there is always a quote of the day and some sort of fun poll to take part in.

 

Casters Realm is a wonderful site to visit when creating a character, wanting to know more about a class, or finding that impossible spell. EQ news is also posted on the first page, but there is a strong emphasis on each individual class and how to play them correctly. A very wonderful place to go when you're wondering how much stamina does a druid really need?

 

This site I am not too familiar with, but it seems to have EQ news, chat forums, and strategy guides.

This site has lots of armor, spell and class specific information.

EQlizer is a very well rounded site with daily news, information about classes, armor, spells and equipment. Also it has a bestiary, quest information and answers questions about class skills.

This site has news, quest informatin, class information, zone informatin, etc.
