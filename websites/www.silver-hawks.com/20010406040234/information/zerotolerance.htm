Zero Tolerance Policy

I have initiated a "zero tolerance" policy. As of 3/10/00 this policy will be in effect. 

Officers have full authority to remove any member for not following the code of the Silver Hawks and they have my full backing. The officers have their positions because I believe they will represent me as if they were me when I am not there. If there are "reports" of a member not following the code then there must be accompanying proof to justify removal. There will be ZERO chances given so I highly recommend that anyone wishing to be a Hawk fully review our policies.

This may seem harsh but it is necessary. Please feel free to email me with any concerns. 

Actions that are deemed as grounds for removal from the Silver Hawks:

1. Begging. From a Hawk, or anyone. This does not mean that you cannot ask politely for help. Asking repeatedly for anything is deemed as begging. Basically, the first time is asking, then second and subsequent times are considered begging. The person you are asking will help you if they can or want to the first time. 

2. Kill Stealing. Whether provoked or not, this is unacceptable. If you have any questions about Kill Stealing, read Verants policy. Basically, all Mobs are fair game, (no one owns a spawn). If the mob is green to you, or someone has engaged the creature already and you take the kill, that is a Kill Steal.

3. Swearing in public channels. Anyone saying She*t, or F**k, or other words known to be even more vulgar in these channels is unacceptable. OOC, Guild, Say, Shout, Auction. If you are grouped with someone that is offended by your language, and your reported, then that is grounds for removal as well. No exceptions. Basically, make sure you know who your cursing to before you do it.

4. Harassment of another player. This includes /shouting, following someone from zone to zone. Continuing to give tells to someone after they make it clear that they do not wish to speak to you. Retaliation is not an excuse. If someone does it to you, use the /ignore command.
