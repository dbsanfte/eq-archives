TGI...H? Thank God it's Hill Giant Friday!

Date: Friday,that's right every Friday! Starting at about 4pm and ending whenever!

Purpose: The Girl Scout/Silver Hawk alliance in selling cookies fell apart, and we're all strapped for cash. So on every Friday I thought we could have a couple groups of Hawks set up camp in the Rathe Mountains for fun times, decent experience and some great loot. Coincidentally, this zone will support low to medium level players with Cyclopses, and medium to high with Hill Giants.

Minimum/Maximum Level: 20-30(clopses) and 30-45(giants)

Needs: This is a lot less formal than an actual raid, so just come sometime during the scheduled time and we'll have some fun!

Resources Needed: Nothing really specific here, but EVERY group should have a druid(for snaring) and hopefully someone who can cast fear. It really does make them as easy as cake.
