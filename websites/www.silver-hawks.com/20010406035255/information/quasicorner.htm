Using /locs to Find Your Way

The /loc command can be quite confusing at first, but is very useful, and with a little practice not to hard.

When you do a /loc command, there are two numbers generated. These are your X,Y coordinates in the zone your in. The first number is the North/South coord., with North being positive (+), and South being negative (-).

The East/West coordinate (second number), is a bit more difficult to master. West is positive (+), and East is negative (-). The way I finally remembered which way it was is this. Verant kind of made it backwards! That's right, I know that's hard to believe, hehe, but true. If you face directly North, then turn left (West), you would think that the second coordinate should get smaller, but not so. To your left is positive, and to the right or East is negative.

Okay, so you're in Greater Faydark, and want to find Felwithe (High Elven city on the Eastern zone line, towards the South). You check your current location and come up with this: -200, +1500.

Okay, you've now determined that you're just South of the center of the zone (or close to that, not all zones are equally distributed), and all the way on the Western side. You need to change the first number a little (South a bit), and change your East/West loc a tremendous amount. Since Felwithe is all the way on the East side, you can just start to move and use your locs to make the second number get smaller.

Use /loc

Mover forward a few steps

Use /loc again

Compare both answers as follows:

If the first number gets bigger, then you are heading Northerly, if smaller, then South. The second number is your East/West, with East being positive, and West negative.

In this case, just make your second number get smaller and smaller, soon you will get the hang of auto-running, and hitting /l repeatedly. If you run all the way East, you should hit the zone wall around x, -2000 or so. Once you hit the zone wall, just head South (right), until you see the opening of the city.

I hope this helps you out some. With a little practice, you'll be able to find just about anything in the open terrain. 

Side-step Your Way to the Bank

Hehe - this is kind of a "cheat", but donut tell me that you've never been "cheated" by the stinkin' game!

When running long distances, or when encumbered, I use this tactic to help me gain that extra time to get back out there and start nuking stuff again.

While running forward (or walking when you've got all that loot!), side-stepping at the same time will get you further faster. The computer doesn't calculate forward and sideways movement together. The resulting vector means you get to where you're going quite a bit faster. Put yourself on autowalk/run, hold down the right mouse button, and press on the left or right arrow key (whatever key you use to turn). It takes a little practice to "aim" where you are going, and the slower you are running, the more you will be walking sideways. But the results are well worth it. Especially good for getting away from mobs in those open areas (Karanas), or just outrunning your buddy - hehe, he'll be scratching his head as you pass him by. The cool part about this is, the "sidestep" does not slow down, even when you are very slow from looting. Even when I can barely walk forward, I just side-step my way all the way to the bank!

Some things to remember:

When side-stepping, you can only turn using the mouse, using the keys to turn isn't an option Be careful around cliffs! It can be hard to remember to how to turn, and I've almost died falling off in High-Pass meself. When used with SOW, you go much much faster.

That's it. Its saved me a few times from dieing running from mobs, but it wont replace SOW by a long shot. Happy Hunting!
