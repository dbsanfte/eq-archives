Guild Rules

MEMBERS:

1. All guild members pledge to follow the Play Nice policies. These policies include no kill-stealing, zone disruptions, harassing of players, corpse camping, etc.

2. We are a family. We expect each of our members to treat everyone in and out of the guild with respect. Remember that you now carry the guild tag and as such are representing us anytime you do anything.

3. All guild members pledge to keep their conversations to a family level. Remember that there are players of all ages in EQ. Thus swearing or conversation of a sexual manner is not allowed.

4. Druids and Wizards are NOT taxi services. If you play one and want to port others, by all means do so. However, this is your choice. For those of you who cannot port, do not expect the porting classes to take you wherever you wish to go. You may ask, but do not spam or hound our other members. Remember that as a family we will help each other, but helping does not mean being somebody else's slave. If you constantly need ports that bad, consider playing a Druid or Wizard.

5. Clerics are not required to be at everyone's beck and call for rezzes. Normally our members will help each other, but you cannot expect someone to spend two hours getting to you so you can get an experience rez.

6. Do not expect to be power leveled by other members. If the offer is made, then it is a gift.

7. Begging is strictly prohibited. Everyone works hard for their items and plat just as you should. Repeated begging is grounds for dismissal. Asking how somebody procured some item, or if the item is for sale is allowed as long as it is respectful and you do not irritate the other player by repeated asking. Using the beg skill on NPCs is permitted.

8. Do not expect others go give you Loot just because you want it. Often times members will give each other items they no longer need or as gifts, but you are not required to do so. Remember that they worked for their items and plat, so you should also.

9. We do not require attendance at any functions, but it is great when you do attend. Remember that we are a family, and to develop friendships and group skills we do need to work together. We understand that our members hail from all parts of the globe and that no one time can be convenient to everyone.

10. If you will be away for an extended period of time, please notify the guild .


11. You are required to make fair trades with other guild members. This means that you are not allowed to cheat the other members of the guild. If a member wishes to pay extra for an item, then by all means do it. Also, if you value an item above its normal worth, then please tell the other guild member that you will only part with it for an inflated price, giving them the choice if they wish to overspend or not.

12. You are not allowed to outright cheat other players during trades or sales. This reflects badly on us as an organization. Cheating usually involves taking something good from a newer player for much less than it is worth or more rarely selling at an inflated price to a newer player. Established players typically know what items are worth and sometimes sell for less or pay extra because they like you, (e.g. Jdan once got a pair of Kobold Hide Boots for binding a level 40 SK and not asking for any payment). This also includes the economy of effort where players are willing to pay a premium to avoid the effort of doing something (e.g. selling bone chips or bat wings stacks for 5pp is allowed, taking a GBS from a newbie that killed Pyzjn for 1pp because the newbie does not know its value is not allowed).

14. We do not require you to level at any given pace. We realize that some of you want to get to 60 as soon as possible, while others want to take time role-playing and/or working on trade-skills, etc. As a guild we hope to provide a home for each type of player, as long as you follow our guild rules. Do not think that you can impose your play style on other members or that other members can impose their style on you. You can expect to be allowed to play in whatever fashion you wish as long as it does not defame our guild name or hurt other players.

15. We have no level or class restrictions on guild membership. At some point membership limits may be imposed based on the size of the guild. However, we understand that you may have RL (real life) friends in-game. Feel free to put forth them forth for membership, but be prepared to defend why they should be granted admission. We would rather take a player with skills we do not need than to lose a guild mate, but do not abuse this.

16. The guild has a neutral stance. We do not discriminate on the basis of class, race, sex, god of worship, or evil/good bent. We expect all members to treat each other with honor.

17. Do not expect help from guild mates if the actions would hurt their faction. For instance, do not expect Dark Elf guild mates to help you with a Dark Elf guard that proves stronger than you anticipated. Also, do not expect help from guild mates versus a MOB that would only get them killed. This does not forbid you from helping, but only means that you should not be expected to help.

18. Members are encouraged to plan events for the guild.

21. Members are expected to play with guildmates reasonably close to their level when possible. A level 20 mage adds nothing to a group of lvl 45-50 players crawling Sol B, and is a big asset that will cause more problems than not. You may ask to come along, but the guild will not be obligated to help with a CR if you are greatly under the level ranges of the area you followed a group into. Also, if you're asked NOT to follow the group, listen to them - they have good reasons.

22. Complaining in guildchat, ooc, shout, or even groupchat about a member's playing ability is cause for a reprimand. If you have issues with the way someone plays their character, let them know in a private tell. Remember this is supposed to be FUN, and complaining about a guildmember in public makes it much less fun for them. If there are extended problems with a member, let an officer know. They will deal with issues as necessary.

23. Guild members, no matter how high up the tree, are not obligated to help other members in any way. However, we strongly ENCOURAGE that you do help other members with reasonable requests (asking a lvl 60 mage to help you farm spiderling silk isn't really reasonable, and asking a group of lvl 30-35 guildies to take down Lady Vox isn't, either). Feel free to ask, but don't get mad if you get turned down.

24. Check the Webpage.  http://www.jim.tc
The guild Webpage was created to improve the sense of guild community. If it is not used by all members, that community dies. If you feel left out of the guild, it's because you're not participating in the community. Also please use our forum.


25. Leveling. This game is supposed to be fun. However, as we are right now fairly small, we request that you choose a "main" and devote more of your time to working on your main character rather than 3 or 4 alts. You'll be able to join our higher level raids and events, and be able to group with more members. A single lvl 45 is much more worthwhile to the guild than 4 lvl 22s.
