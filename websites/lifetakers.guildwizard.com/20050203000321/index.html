SRR's New Clicky Clerics - 1/17/2005 1:25:36 PM

SRR Gains Two Clicky Clerics in One Week

SRR took down Ragefire twice within just seven days to finish up epics for two or our clerics, Kelee and Kornin. After stomping the Skyfire dragon in less than two mins on Jan. 9 an impromptu assault was launched Jan. 16. We had fewer raiders but the same result. With the epic piece: Water Sprinkler of Nem Ankh, our clerics can now do quick manaless rezzes, (not that we will ever need them) during raid wipes, mishaps and well, Norrath is a dangerous world. You get the picture.

Congratulations to Kelee and Kornin and thanks to all the guildies and friends who came together and "kicked some arse". And by the way no one died but the bad guys in either encounter!

Rock on SRR!!!

Kelee, right , stands on the corpse of the dragon. She finished her epic on Jan 9.

SRR Art Controversy - 1/17/2005 11:37:50 AM

What Do You Say?
You say potato, I say potatoe; but at stake is the soul of our Guild.  In a recent thread and on the homepage of our site, members have discussed what sort of image our guild projects. At the heart of the discussion are two pictures that have appeared on our website homepage. They are shown below.

Some members like the aesthetic value of the dark skull image created by our own Dark Elf Lupnoir. They are attracted to the color scheme, the image of the horses riding out of the clouds, the lightning bolts crackling in the eyes, and well the hot chick. Others get a warm fuzzy feeling from the image of the lighthouse shooting its beacon into the darkness, the warm blue colors and feeling of calm in the midst of chaos. Others love the lighthouse but miss the hot chick.

Some say neither. Some say either. Some say both. What do you say?
