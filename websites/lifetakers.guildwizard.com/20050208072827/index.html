Storm Riders devastate the Plane of Fear - 1/20/2005 12:20:30 PM

STORM RIDERS RAID PLANE OF FEAR  


         
                                      

Storm Rider Rebellion, along with some great friends, raided the Plane of Fear on
Wednesday, January 19th.  The break-in was flawless without a single death.                     

It is fair to say we owned Fear and many epic drops were received. 
Tine did an exemplary job as raid leader.  Everyone did a great job and we had a good time kicking on Cazic's menagerie. 
Storm Riders Aye!
 

SRR's New Clicky Clerics - 1/17/2005 1:25:36 PM

SRR Gains Two Clicky Clerics in One Week

SRR took down Ragefire twice within just seven days to finish up epics for two or our clerics, Kelee and Kornin. After stomping the Skyfire dragon in less than two mins on Jan. 9 an impromptu assault was launched Jan. 16. We had fewer raiders but the same result. With the epic piece: Water Sprinkler of Nem Ankh, our clerics can now do quick manaless rezzes, (not that we will ever need them) during raid wipes, mishaps and well, Norrath is a dangerous world. You get the picture.

Congratulations to Kelee and Kornin and thanks to all the guildies and friends who came together and "kicked some arse". And by the way no one died but the bad guys in either encounter!

Rock on SRR!!!

Kelee, right , stands on the corpse of the dragon. She finished her epic on Jan 9.
 
Kornin, below, followed up just a week later. He is shown apparently reading over the body of our fallen enemy.
