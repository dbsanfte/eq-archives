Joining the Eye of Mordor

Welcome stranger!

The Eye of Mordor is interested in getting more members of all classes and we look for active players (who can be online at least 4 times a week). In general we look for new members with level 60 or higher, here is a list of the classes and what levels we are mainly looking for of each:

HIGH wanted (level 60+): Cleric, Enchanter, Ranger, Rogue, Wizard
MEDIUM wanted (level 62+): Bard, Beastlord, Druid, Magician, Shaman, Warrior
LOW wanted (level 65): Monk, Necromancer, Paladin, Shadowknight

We are a mixed guild of North-American and European players, that is why you have to be able to speak (write) english well enough to understand what is said and make yourself understood. Our major raid time is in the evening European time, from 8.00 p.m. to 12.00 a.m. Central European Time (CET) or 2.00 p.m. to 6.00 p.m. Eastern Standard Time (EST). Our major raiddays are tuesday and thursday but we also raid on other weekdays, if worthy targets are up and we have enough people online. On average we raid 10 to 12 times each month, never on weekends though. Please make sure that you can be online on at least most weekdays at this time, or you will have very small chances of becoming a Full Member of the Eye of Mordor !!

Of course all members of the Eye of Mordor are allowed to participate on all raids that we organize.

The Eye of Mordor is a guild that tries to uphold a friendly and familiar atmosphere like in a smaller guild - even when our numbers are growing, to succeed in greater goals like raiding in Vex Thal or hunting in PoP zones (up to Tier3).

    Requirements you have to meet to join EoM

    1. A full set of printed out maps of as many EQ zones as possible (or maps for the in-game map system)
    2. Magelo-link of your character
    3. Resist gear svAll 100 or better
    4. Gate ability (spell, potion ..)
    5. Permanent Enduring Breath (Item)
    6. Kunark, Velious, Luclin and PoP installed
    7. Desired: Elementalflags and VexThalaccess

    level 60+: Cleric, Enchanter, Ranger, Rogue, Wizard
    level 62+: Bard, Beastlord, Druid, Magician, Shaman, Warrior
    level 65+: Monk, Necromancer, Paladin, Shadowknight

    When you fit all these requirements, please post a 'hello'-message that contains the following info on our recruitment message-board: Where do you come from? What time zone? What time(s) do you play? What expansions do you have installed? Some interesting infos about your EQ life before applying to join us :).

    Notice please that you have to post on your own to be considered for trialmembership. If a friend or relative wants to join EoM also, there has to be a separate application for that person.

    After this 'hello'-message you will get invited and be a Trial Member the following four weeks. After that time all members of the Eye of Mordor can vote for you in a poll in the internal section of our message board. If you get at least 12 positive votes within one week you will be accepted as a Full Member of the Eye of Mordor.

    Why was my appliance for membership rejected even when I could fulfill all the requirements for EoM membership?

    Besides the obvious points like acts against our rules, our ethics or antisocial behavior, the main reason for a rejection as trial member may have nothing to do directly with yourself, but just be our self-chosen limitation to have a maximum of 8 trial members at a time.

    The reason for this limitation is that we want to enlarge our power without losing the character and identity of Eye of Mordor. By keeping a limited number of new trial members, we want to give all senior EoM members the chance to focus on our 8 new friends, to get to know each other and learn about them rather than being a bunch of strangers.

    How do I set up a Magelo-link?

    A magelo-link is a link to a website where you can create a virtual copy of your EQ characters. The copy can be shown to everyone via the magelo-link. This can be very helpful for the organization of raids and for sharing items within the guild.

    To create a magelo-character just follow the link http://www.magelo.com and create an account. After this, create a new character and name it like your EQ character. To make an exact copy of your EQ character you have to make some screenshots (or simply write down) the statistics of your EQ character. Important are the names of all your items that you wear. You need also the number of your AC and ATK when you wear nothing than your majorweapon in your primaryhand (important: To get the right number in ATK use a weapon with no stat-boni, but of course useable with the same weaponskill) and the pure numbers of your stats (like in level 1).

    Insert the correct numbers for your level, stats, AC and ATK and other information. Follow then the link 'Manage his profile(s)' and add all your items to your magelo-character. Save then your current profile. You will get now your number for this magelo-character (in the box at left bottom side). Send an e-mail to galam@gmx.net with this number and it will be linked on the guild roster.

    How do I make a gate-potion?

    Many do not have it, and many want it, so here are the step-by-step instructions to create a Thurgadin-gate- potion:

    The best zone to do this is the bank in Thurgadin. There you find all that you need: a vendor with all pottery items, a pottery wheel and a kiln. Additionally you need much water (buy 10 stacks, the butcher in Thurgadin sells them for example). All in all you will need around 2-4 hours time (depends on your INT/WIS, whichever is highest) and about 200pp.

    1.0. You need a pottery skill of 120. There are three steps to get this skill.

    1.1 First, to get to 21, combine 3 small blocks of clay and a flask of water in the pottery wheel. You may create some blocks of clay. When you run out of small blocks of clay combine a block of clay and a flask of water to get 3 small blocks of clay. Repeat this until you reach pottery skill 21.

    1.2. To get 102 (after that this combine will be trivial and increasing the pottery skill won't help further) combine a small block of clay, a bowl sketch and a flask of water. You will get a small bowl, destroy it, it is not useful for you anymore.

    1.3. To get to 122 (after that the creation of a Thurgadin gate-potion will be trivial) combine a block of clay, a medium bowl sketch and a flask of water. Again, destroy the result.

    2.0. When you have 122 in pottery you need the following items to create a single Thurgadin gate-potion:

  • 1 small brick of velium (not stackable)
  • 3 crystalline silks
  • 2 glass shards
  • 1 high quality firing sheet
  • 2 small pieces of velium (stackable)
  • 1 velium purifier


    • Here are my hints on how to get these items:

    - 1 small brick of velium: kill all orcs on your way down to the dwarf city in Crystal Caverns and you will get some drops / hunt in Velketor's Labyrinth, it can drop randomly off many mobs there / kill some sentries in Skyshrine. It seems that nearly each one of them will drop a small brick of velium

    - 3 crystalline silks: Deep in Crystal caverns is a tunnel full of spiders that ends in a room where the Spider Queen spawns. All spiders drop crystalline silk often. / kill spiders in Velketor's Labyrinth, but the drop rate seems not that good.

    - 2 glass shards: vendor sold.

    - 1 high quality firing sheet: vendor sold.

    - 2 small pieces of velium: kill all orcs on your down to the dwarf city in Crystal Caverns and you will get some drops / hunt in Velketor's Labyrinth, it can drop randomly of many mobs there / kill some sentries in Skyshrine.

    - 1 velium purifier: Find the vendor Frundle Frenkler (in a house on the opposite side of the paladin and cleric guild in Thurgadin) and ask her 'What velium vapor?' and you will get the quest container.

    Creation: Combine the small brick of Velium, the three crystalline silks and the two glass shards in the pottery wheel to get an unfired velium vial. Now combine this unfired velium vial and the high quality Firing Sheet in the kiln to get a Velium Vial. Put the velium Vial and the two small pieces of Velium in the velium purifier to get a Vial of Velium Vapors.

    The Vial of Velium Vapor has the effect: Thurgadin gate!

    When you go and look for the items, bring back many of them. You can only create one Vial of Velium Vapor at once (it is lore) but the Velium Vials are not lore.

    What resist gear can I buy to meet the requirements?

    A basic resist gear set is a combo of the following craftable jewelry:

  • 2 velium diamond rings: all resists+8 each

  • 2 velium blue diamond bracelet: svmagic/svfire/svpoison/svdisease+9 svcold+11 mana+30

  • 2 electrum blue diamond earrings: svmagic+4 svfire/svcold+6 svpoison/svdisease+11 mana+15

  • 1 platinum blue diamond tiara (headslot): all resists+8 each mana+25

  • 1 golden blue diamond pendant: svmagic+ 4 svfire/svcold+11 svpoison/svdisease+6 mana+20

  • 1 golden diamond mask: svmagic+3 svfire/svcold+10 svpoison /svdisease+5


    • All this will provide a total of: svmagic+36 svfire+52 svcold+54 svpoison+47 svdisease+47 (mana+90)

    Components:

    Every armor piece can be made combining one bar of metal with one gem. So to make a velium diamond ring, a jeweller will need to combine 1 enchanted velium bar with 1 diamond. All metal bars can be purchased from jeweller NPCs all throughout Norrath, except velium bars, which are only sold in Thurgadin. The prices vary with your faction and charisma: velium bar 262pp / platinum bar 110pp / gold bar 15pp / electrum bar 1pp / silver bar < 1pp.

    The two jewels needed for resist gear (diamond and blue diamond) are mob drops only, so you will have to hunt for them or buy them from other players. So to be, the prices vary a lot, being 300-500pp for a diamond, and 1000-2000pp for a blue diamond. You may get cheaper prices buying several gems at once, or if players are desperate for many and accept to sell to you for a low price.

    Making:

    For all resist jewellery you will need an enchanter to enchant the metal, and a grandmaster jeweller to craft the jewels (250 jewel craft skill). However, resist gear is the most challenging jewel piece to make, even at this maxed skill of 250 there will be a chance of failure and losing the components.
