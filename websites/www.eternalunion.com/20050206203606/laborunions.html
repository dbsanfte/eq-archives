The Eternal Union has many great crafters of many different types.
We pool our power and resources into one great furnace of creativity
and accomplishments. All of our Lords and Magistrates belong to one
of the following Labor Unions.


The Gathering Union

Has the Crucial responsibility to provide supplies and materials to other
members of the Eternal Union. These individuals take pleasure in gathering
herbs, leathers and ore for the crafters of the guild. Gathering Union
members are very well rewarded within the Eternal Union.

The Eternal Union Gathering Union is crucial to the success of all
the correlating Divisions. The Gathering Union has the critical duty
of supplying the materials and items that benifit all members of
the Eternal Union. Direct result of this is of course tremendous
prestige as well as the ability to trade many of their wares for
the hundreds of items and craft that will be created by the other Divisions
of Labor.

The Eternal Union Gathering Division will be responsible for attaining
Herbs from the Herbalists, Leather from the Skinners and Stone and Ore
from the Miners. The Praetor of this division will 'set' the trade
schedules with the other divisions through whatever diplomacy is available
to them. Their success is totally dependant on their ability to provide
for the guild - which in turn will provide exceptionally for them.


The Transmutation Union

Is to focus on the creation of potions and enchantments for both
the guild, themselves, and for profit.

The Eternal Union Transmutation Division is responsible for taking
the raw materials that are provided by the gathering divisions and
creating using their talents, potions and enchantments for both those
whom have given them their materials - in fair amounts - and enough for
their own use and profit.

The Transmutation Division and it's Praetor will be working directly
with the Gathering Division to attain it's components in trade for it's
craft, both for trade for the items to the Gathering Division, for their
own use, and again, enough for their profit.


The Creation Union

Is to focus on the creation of material items such as swords, breastplates,
bombs, trinkets and materials for engineering and smithing. For the guild,
themselves and for profit.

The Eternal Union Creation Division is responsible for the creation of items
of warfare to the guild. They have the critical duty to supply our brothers
with the weaponry and armor that they can use to advance quickly within the world
of Azeroth. Often these creators are underestimated, but their skillful presence
is never never missed.

The Eternal Union Creation Division Praetor will be responsible for making
arrangements with other divisions for trading guildelines. Trading of Wares
in excess will only bring more profit to those individuals as well as to their
divisions. Again, enough product will be traded for materials for profit to all.


The Artisan Union

Is to focus on the creation of materials made of leather and textiles.
For the guild, themselves and for profit.

The Eternal Union Artisan Union is a very important division as it will be
creating the armor, wares, containers and many other useful supplies for
not only those of the arcane pursuation, but all others within the guild.
Their function and ability to craft leather and textiles into usable flexible
armor and containers will be fundamental to many of the Eternal Union guild.

The Eternal Union Artisan Praetor will be responsible for trade guidelines
with all other Divisions. Tailors and Leatherworkers will be able to trade
their wares for profit or items to others, all benifiting the guild as a whole
as well as the individual.


The Elite Guard Union

The Elite Guard is special in a sense as any of the other union members
can also be apart of the elite guard. Their function is to mobilize when
available to protect other members of the Eternal Union from the onslaught
of the Horde and it's craft.


The Elite Guard of the Eternal Union is our members Sword and Shield.
Those that are unable to defend themselves from the onslaught of tyranny and evil
can see shelter behind the might of the Eternal Union Guard.

Their primary purpose is to provide protection and support to those who need it
from the horde. If they are out questing and a member of the horde comes to
intrude on their adventures, the 'calvery' so to speak can be brought in to
protect them.

This isn't for petty squabbles or raids, this system is in place to protect those
of the Eternal Union from unfair advantages. Those of the Eternal Union are expected
to be able to defend themselves reguarly as these onslaughts will be common place in
Azeroth.
