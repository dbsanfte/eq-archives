Recruitment

The Eternal Union is always seeking looking for our next valued Member.
Potential Eternal Union members must meet the following requirements:

A strong desire to have fun with others.
A Rugged Individual seeking to challenge themselves constantly.
A good sense of humor that makes playing fun for all.
Maturity and self composure.
Mastery of the English language (translated: no l33t kiddies)
Ambition to carve out a name for in the midst of nameless guilds and
organizations that fill the world of warcraft.

The benifits to joining The Eternal Union:

Having a purpose, a goal that can be fulfilled and enjoyed.
Access to the divisional labor unions - which will grant you
access to easy materials and trade partners within the guild.
The Ability to advance within the ranks of the guild.
Having the Battle Masters to protect you when those who wish to kill
you roam freely about you.
A sense of brotherhood, and unity with others.
A charactername@eternalunion.com email address.

If you feel that you fit these roles, how do you apply?

Step One: Take an Objective look at yourself

Read the history and goals of the Eternal Union, we are unlike other guilds.
We are a group that idolizes the power of the Individual but understands it's
power within a group. Whatever a new member brings into the guild, or achieves
within it, is his or hers to keep or share. We want people whom are motivated!

Our application process is for those who are serious about being involved within
the world of warcraft.

Step Two: Register on the Eternal Union Forums - read a little about
what we represent - and then create an application.

Step Three: Creating your Application

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS of the Eternal Union

Q:Why can't I access  the entire forum?
A:Only proven members, those who have completed the forum registration process
and have been accepted as members can view the private forums.

Q:Why can't I speak over guild chat?
A:Only proven members, those who have completed the forum registration process
and have been accepted as members can speak over the guild channel.

Q:I want to advance in status, how do I do that?
A:There are several ranks within the Eternal Union, and we urge all members
to advance when they can. The requirements for each tier are different and are based
on the individual who has shown upstanding integrity and honor.
 

Q:I like to PVP alot, does the Eternal Union have a problem with that?
A:No, if you wish to charge into a horde town and kill their diezens - so be it.
We do not condone corpse camping or griefing in any manner, however.

Q:I keep getting killed while questing by horde, what does the Eternal Union do?
A:We have an Union that will protect it's brothers of the Eternal Union
when possible.

Q:I got corpse camped, and when the tables were turned, I camped back, is that ok?
A:No.

Q:I don't understand the whole Labor Union Concept, what's it for.
A:The concept behind the Labor Union is to make trade and profits for our Guild
faster and more effectivly. If the gathering guild can gather a certain amount of
leather, but needs bags from the tailor, instead of selling leather at the auction
house, and purchasing bags from the auction house. The Magistrate can organize trade
between the Unions to facilitate profit within the guild, and conservation and gain of
capitol. In short, an internal trading function.

Q:How do I join?
A:Register on the Forums, fill out an application, and three members must approve your
application before you are inducted into the Eternal Union.
