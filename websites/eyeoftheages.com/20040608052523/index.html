E3 Info from around the Web
Posted by Sedius on 17.05. at 06:27
This year's E3 was packed to the brim (over 65,000 attendees, as stated by ESA staff, the guys that put on E3), and the SOE booth was a nice, prominent addition, even if the sound in that corner consisted of NCsoft's Tabula Rasa, and SOE's videos competing for eardrum rattling volumes. Anyway, lots of people were able to see EQ2 this year, in the SOE invite only booth (as usual, guarded by big intimidating looking bouncers), so lets get down to the coverage.


Gamespy has a quick preview based on their experience at the booth. Gamespot has some updated impressions, plus a few screens in addition to the E3 Press Release Kit. PC.IGN has a preview as well. Stratics has three different articles about the game: a short one from Zedin, an in-depth, long preview from Berek, and another smaller preview from Sylvene. EQ2Vault has a a couple features so far, one about E3 before seeing EverQuest II, and the other a shakeycam video of the movies being shown outside the booth. The japanese site forGamer.net has a bunch of content, including movies, on their EverQuest II Coverage Page (Untranslated, if someone can translate or finds an engine that will actually do the translation, let me know). Warcry has a couple articles as well, on the hub site as well as an article on the new EQ2 Warcry site.



Here's a quick roundup of screenshots from other sites:
  • EQ2 Epicplayer has a set of three shots
  • MMORPG.com also has a set of three
  • Ogaming has their saturday shot
  • Stratics also has their latest in the May Mania series
  • ToThe Game also has some, though I believe they've all been seen on other websites like ours
  • EQ2Vault has some new character creation video, gnome and iksar in particular
  • The Online Gaming Radio also did a show that I'm going to plug even if it didn't have anything to do with EQII
