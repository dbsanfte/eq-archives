Friday February 14th, 2003
War! (What is it good for?)

Well, a rather nice ring for one thing. What is still one of the most entertaining events in EQ for another. Ransac somehow managed not to die during the war, and was awarded with the Dain's ring for this amazing feat. We think he was hiding in a nearby cave the whole time. Baldric and several high-rollers that showed up to help made off with other neat trinkets.

Been securing a few more Sleeper's Tomb keys, and have succesfully laid the foundation for raids in there. Should start after getting a few melees in need of Prismatics keyed. Actually managed to catch Sontalak up one evening before anyone else. Only had 24 folks online for our first attempt at him. Was quite messy, but in the end 6 surviving FE stood over his corpse.

To further our preperations for big stuff, we started practicing the most important of raid actions -- that of proper corpse hiding. Here's an example from a late-night Vindi kill;
