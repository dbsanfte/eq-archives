Wednesday March 26th, 2003
F / E

One might wonder why there hasn't been an update for over a month. Well, there was a very good reason. A reason that damaged Fate Entwined greatly. It's name? Maelin Starpyle. Not the Gnome in Knowledge failing to give out flags, but the new server. When the announcement of moves to a new server was made, most in FE were ecstatic. This could be the answer! But it was not to be. Extensive CS policies regarding transferred accounts, accounts with too many CCs on file, accounts that smelled funny, etc. being denied passage quelled that idea for several key members of the guild. However the temptation of a fresh server was too much for many, and Fate Entwined was torn asunder, losing nearly half of it's membership to transfers.

Things were looking fairly grim. A maximum of 8-10 people on per night does not a raid make. It is mainly due to the amazing recruiting work of Kiriyea (Wait, FE has a Bard?) that there is still a guild left. Not only did she find people to refill our ranks, she found exceptional people to do so. We stumbled, but did not fall.

All in all, I truly believe Fate Entwined came away from this crisis stronger than when it began. Our new members are dedicated, skillful, and people I'm happy to call guildmates. Last night we got a taste of just what this new crew, this FE 2.0, can accomplish. I have to say I'm extremely proud of every one of the new folks. People listen, people focus, MOBs die. Entered the Earth Tower in Bastion of Thunder for the first time, and proceeded to lay waste to its inhabitants and its boss.
