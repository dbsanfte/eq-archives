- 10/04/01 - Ending of an Era

"This is what UO should have been!"

Those were my words and thoughts when I started plaing Asheron's Call nearly 2 years ago. It didn't turn out to be quite that way though unfortunately.

On the surface AC had everything I wanted: Skill-based system, spell economy, trade skills, a new way to do guilds, and the promise of new content every month. How could anything go wrong?

Well now I wonder whether the skill-based system can actually work outside of theory. We've seen it fail in two games, AC and UO. The same thing generally happened in both, Tank Mages were common and you needed magic to survive. That to me is a failure.

Trade skills in AC are laughable and hugely ignored by the dev team, though not nearly as laughable and useless as it is in EQ. At least trade skills here have SOME use, though no where near what they were in UO.

The new way to do guilds has breeded a new way of thievery, greed, and people lookin for quick xp without doin anything. 

And the spell economy, well that was another failure that looks better on paper, and I don't feel the need to go through explaining why because we all know the answer to that.

The only thing that I felt was hugely successful in AC was the monthly updates. And even know that there were some months that were skimpy, most were satisfactory and all were more than you could get for free from EQ or UO in 6 months.

Now despite all this I do think that AC was the best of the first generation crop. It had a cool new world, some pretty cool quests and alot of nice people. And I love not having to zone (EQ) or get killed (UO) every 2 seconds. 

But it is undeniable to me that this game has nearly reached the limit of it's life. The second generation games are slowly trickling out and on top of their magnificent new graphics, they are also learning from the mistakes that EQ, UO and AC have all made.

They are all promising new content throughout it's life like AC, a deeper variety of classes and races like EQ, most are trying to put a better emphasis on trade skills and they are all attempting to put up better customer support.

And with all this said, I do plan on moving on to Dark Age of Camelot next week when it is released. I plan on continuing to keep my AC account at least for a few more months and even plan on buying Dark Majesty when it comes out. (It's already pre-ordered.)

But it is time for me to move on.

These last few weeks I have found myself playing less and less. Heck I play more now as my mule in a vain attempt to get his strength up to carry more so that I might be able to start a new mage character. But the game just doesn't hold the same attraction to me that it did even a couple months ago.

Camelot promises to be alot of what all the big three are and seems to have a better end game than advancing to a level where I can play on aerlinthe or singularity. This new Realm vs Realm idea that they have put out has really caught my attention and I look forward to seeing if it's as good as people say it is.

Now I do worry about a few aspects of it. I hear that it forces you to group like EQ, just a little less strictly. Meaning you can solo, you just won't advance as fast. In fact I've heard alot of people calling it a tweaked EQ, but maybe that'll turn out to be a good thing because on the surface EQ wasn't so bad, it just needed ALOT of tweaking, more than I could put up with=P

So we will see how this new game fares. I hope at the very least that it entertains me to be a place holder for games that I look forward to even further in the future such as Star Wars Galaxies, AC2, and hell even World of Warcraft a little bit.

So if anyone else is going to Camelot, I will most likely be playing on the Palomides server in the Midgard realm. Give me a tell there or email me,  maybe we could hook up and go on a mighty quest to take over an opposing realm's fort.
