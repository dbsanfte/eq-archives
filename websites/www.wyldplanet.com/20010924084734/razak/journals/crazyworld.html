 - 09/19/01 - Crazy World
What a couple of weeks it's been. The world has literally been turned upside down. And right when you want to leave the real world behind and head for the stable environment of Dereth to take out your frustrations on some helpless tusker. Of course the world of Dereth gets turned upside down as well.

Poll

Where do you hunt in this new world?

Vote in this poll or discuss it on the boards.
 

Yes the spawns of Dereth have changed yet again and we must learn to adjust now more than ever. I don't know if these changes are permanent, I don't know if they are tweaked, and I don't even know where to hunt anymore.

Obsidian Plains, once known for being the only true haven for high level melee and the place to power level a mage is nowhere near as good as it used to. The plains are now frequented by groups of Diamond Golems, Mauler & Mangler Banderlings, Augmented Tumeroks, Olthoi Nobles, Virindi Inquisitors, Level 250+ Ascendant Drudges, and your occasional Hollow Minion.

While that is all well and good and on the surface actually looks like a buff to the plains, it is actually a nerf. The monster spawns are so far apart that hunting there is no longer worth the time.

Where once stood hordes of Tuskers, Virindi and Shadows, now stands 3 spawns of single Olthoi Soldiers. And I must say Woopidy Doo with a capital DUH. 

From what I hear the rest of the dires has turned into hell while the obsidian plains has turned into a cakewalk. Someone in some message boards said that you can literally fit a basketball court inbetween spawns and I'd increase that to say you can literally fit a football field inbetween spawns. 

The mighty has fallen, I'd say OP is now for people wanting to try their luck on the dires but not yet big enough to play it. Sort of like a trial dires area. Kind of odd considering it is surrounding the dires so you have to actually go through worse areas to get to an easier area. Just not well thought out.

I have recently started to look around the north dires from reports that you can find Sentient Fragments up there. So far the spawns have been closer together which is good but what spawns there is iffy at best. On a typical run I saw Tiatus Lugians followed by Sandstone Golems followed by Ulu Sclavus followed by Banderling Guards. Woot, this be da bom!

Now don't get me wrong, I applaud the effort Turbine did on this. There are tons of boss monsters to challenge everybody, from the lowly Master of the Herd (Auroch Boss) to the great Diamond Lord. And I also thought that the spawns did in fact need to be rethought. 2/3s of the game was generally for levels 10 to 25 (Osteth), yet 2/3s of the population of Dereth is over level 35. So the changes were needed and they are welcome to some degree. But they weren't totally thought out in my opinion.

Maybe it just takes time to get used to. I have one friend who hunts primarily by AB now and I heard that's where Fist De Yuma also hunts these days. So maybe that's where the good hunting is these days. I admittedly haven't really explored much since the update, maybe 4 or 5 hours outside of OP. And I'm hoping that I find a new haven for me to hunt in. I may also decide to move to Aerlinthe which I had been considering moving to at level 65 anyway before, but i think I should travel around the dires to make sure there's nothing of interest.

Plus I really want my Crystal Sword. I do give Turbine mad props for the new trophy items they put out. They are rare, hard to get, cool looking, and useful. They even went so far to put the Ursuin Helm back in and a heavier, higher AL Ursuin Coat.  All good stuff and much appreicated.

After the last couple of months which have been pretty decent all around, I have started looking forward to patch day a little more, they've done well of late. I also look forward to the new expansion due out next month. I hope it gives that breath of fresh air this game desperately needs.

Statage

Well it's been 3 weeks now since I've posted stats because I didn't even have a journal last week in respect to the WTC tragedy so it's about time. To be honest I really haven't been playing a great deal the last few weeks for various reasons including 2 auctions which have taken alot of time for delivering items. So not much has changed even with the time

As you can see not much has changed. I did get to 64 and I finally hit a natural 200 on all my magic casting stats. I'm just a few Mana Conversion points away from being able to use the Quiddity Orb buffed with a vitae which will be nice cause it's been kind of a burden when I die. I've also died a few times trying to adjust to the new life in the dires.

One such death occurred when I think I saw one of the new NPC monsters, so I stopped to take a quick look and a Destroyer Grievver jumped me and one shot me. Was on a corpse recovery and didn't have a bp, to top it off my buffs were runnin out.=( This was in South Dires.
