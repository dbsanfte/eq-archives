The Wyld takes place in an ungoverned world with loosely made towns that serve the most basic of functions. There are some, however, who are now looking to create a ruling government, with themselves as rulers, of course. But for the most part the land is still wild and untamed.

Which is where you, as the player comes in. You are a traveler who has stumbled upon a small town. But unlike most towns you have seen, this one is starting to develop.

Someone named Craglan Stormheart has decided to set up shop on an island in the mouth of a river known as Sharlon Isle. He is building a keep that can be the base of his operations.

No one knows where this Craglan came from, or where he got his money. But rumors abound on both.

The Wyld is being made by a single person using the MMORPG Construction Kit and currently can only be played by owning the same kit. The Wyld will continually be updated and will always grow and support the community that plays it as well.

In the future, The Wyld will be remade and updated to support NeverWinter Nights. It is unknown as to when this switch will happen and whether or not The Wyld will maintain MMORPG Construction Kit support at all.
