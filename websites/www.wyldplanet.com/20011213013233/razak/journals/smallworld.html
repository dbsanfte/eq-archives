- 11/06/01 - It's a Small World After All

As you get bigger the options for non-pvp hunting places get smaller. The idea I believe is to nudge you into RvR play ever so slightly. If you are 30 or higher there are very few places inside the realm that aren't crowded. And the higher you go, the more smaller the world seems.

This past week and a half has been a time of finding places to hunt, with or without other people. In fact, I've mostly been soloing because there aren't a lot of people around my level using the "find group" feature. It's a shame too, because it really is a cool feature that mythic added to reduce spam and to help people find others that they need to be with. 

Unfortunately most people I see on it are about 10 levels away from me and usually I wanna get xp for myself. I think part of the problem with the system is the lack of usefulness having someone so far away is and the fact that you can't find people outside of your area. If I'm in Vale of Mularn, and someone in Galphen wants to group, I won't know and I ain't gonna ride a horse for 10 minutes to find out.

I first started my search for a good place to hunt in Muspelheim with my friends, Fendain, Rixor, and Keerok. It was a good area for 20 to 32 for a group only I think. There are lots of pack mobs, and lots of scouts making it not as worthwhile for solo play. And even in group play this place is really packed these days, don't expect to find a bonus here for too long. 

I really think it'd help alot if Mythic made group cons instead of just solo for this place. I think 2 critters that just turned grey should have a green con to em, lets face it fighting 2 is harder than 1 and you should get some reward cause it still has a chance of killin it.

I next headed out into the frontier. Actually this one worked out well for me, I've spent the majority of my time out there hunting various creatures in a couple of key spots. I haven't run into anyone from an opposing realm as of yet, but am sure as time goes on your chances on meeting up with someone goes up. 

Almost everywhere you go you'll find some kind of bonus and if you find a secluded place you'll get really good ones. And the thing I like most about it is that it's all cash which means that the only trips into town you make are when you die. The one bad thing is that neither of the places are really all that close to a bindstone and it may be quite the run if you die.

As much as I like the cash thing of the frontier, my first trip to Spindelhalla does make me hope for itemization of the frontier. Spin is great, there are lots of critters on fast spawns that sometimes drop some really awesome items. Yesterday was my first trip there, and I got myself a Dark Steel Kite Shield with some pluses to shield, strength, light, and some other protection that I can't remember off hand. This shield couldn't have come at a more perfect time because my old shield went gray and I was having problems finding high level weaponsmiths.

Spin is great, especially if you get in a nice group that knows what to hunt, and how to hunt it. But, as with everyplace else, it has it's downside. Right now it is extremely overcrowded during prime hours because it's the only 30+ dungeon that's been itemized. Last night at 7pm EST there were over 50 people in that dungeon alone. So if you are gonna be goin there at night, the lag is gonna be bad. And chances are you won't be getting many bonuses for too long, with so many people there, there is very little chance of spreading out.

When Mythic decides to itemize the rest of the high level dungeons and zones I think the overcrowded thing will get alot better. I just saw that a mythic person said that Midgard is the furthest away from being itemized of all the realms so they will probably add at least one new itemization to a dungeon soon. I think the frontiers might be a bit further off as I don't think anyone's frontiers have been itemized as of yet.

As a side note, I'd like to congratulate and thank all the players involved in the fort raid of Sunday the 4th. Midgard took 5 Hibernian keeps in one swoop, everyone did a great job. I was unable to participate but it's very cool to see a fort finally get converted to another realm, think Palomides was one of the last of the starting servers to do it, and 5 in one night on top of that. good job!
