 - 10/30/01 - Let the Fighting Begin

I've gotten up to level 27 this week and more importantly have been able to get a measly 32 realm points. It isn't much but it's something. This past week or two the fighting has started to take place between all the realms and I was lucky to catch a couple of raids on the Hibernians.

The first raid that I took part in I believe took place around 10:30 EST last Monday or Tuesday night. I got a call from one of my friends that a group was gathering for raid on Hibernia in Vindsaul Faste and that I should get my butt over there. Luckily I was in a nearby town and was able to run over there just in time to catch the portal to the frontier. 

This was my first realm invasion so I was stumblin around like a fool askin what to do. Go to merchant and by a necklace to Hibernia? Ok. Don't put it on until we are ready to go? Ok. Put it on! Put it on! Ok! Ok! Sheesh.

Finally we are ready to go, have the necklace on and the 4 priests walk around the portal area and cast their spell to transport us into the frontier. This was a very cool way to do the portaling on Mythic's part but we quickly learned that we shouldn't have been in a group because we had to relog to get rid of the bug that screwed up the group tab.

Back in we all assembled our groups. There was a little confusion the whole night, think it was a lot of people's first times, not just mine, and we ended up not having a skald for the trip. After a couple minutes out the finally stopped and we got a skald moved to our group so we could run fast like the rest.

Now things got really confusing. We started running and somehow the leader of our group, Jayden, got left behind and was soon lost. Well because the frontier is loaded with level 50 critters they wouldn't stop for him. Even when we made minor pauses to let everyone catch up they wouldn't wait long enough for him. When we finally got there Jayden and the leader of the raid got into an argument and left. I think rightfully so, I would have been pissed if it had happened to me too.

So we finally are set up and we run down to our chosen attack point. There is a group of hibernians hunting in the area and we quickly swoop down and wipe out the party, a lurikeen is able to get away but otherwise was a swift victory. We get back and hide and let the scouts find another group to get.

We were able to kill quite a few Hibernians this way, but finally the Hibernians organized a rather large group and wiped us out. From what I hear of other raids, this is pretty much how every raid ends. The other side eventually gets a force put together and kills the raiders. But overall I'd have to say it was a successful raid, I got 27 points which isn't bad for my first time.

I won't talk too much about the other raid. Suffice it to say, it was a dismal failure. 

We ran out to Hibernia again, me and a few other members of my group were killed by the high level critters along the way. (Don't worry we got resurrected.) Hid for 20 minutes because someone thought they saw a large albion raiding party and we wanted to jump them. (No there actually wasn't another raiding party.) And then ran to the gates and waited to die. I was ressurected 4 times during the whole encounter, including the death along the way. And died from 2 critters, thus losing xp for it. All for 5 measly realm points.

The communication was horrible. Not many people knew what was going on because only the group leaders were in the chat channel. I personally had no idea of what was going on and why everyone was huddled close to the gates. I decided this wasn't a good tactic and generally stayed away from the group in case an elf wanted to throw an area of effect nuke and wipe out the entire raiding party with a few spells.

I must say that while over all I had a really good time raiding, something needs to be done about the communication in the game. The chat channel only can have about 20 people in it at the same time. This creates quite a dilemma when you have 40 people raiding. Mythic says that this will be sort of solved in the future where a person with high realm points will be able to organize more than one chat and make announcements in those at the same time. That would help a little, but I'm unsure if that would be the best solution, not only that, who knows when such a feature will actually make it into the game.

All that aside, if you hear about a raid I highly suggest you jump in one, no matter what your level. (Well you need to be at least 15.) They are alot of fun and there isn't a whole lot lost if you die, just giving someone from another realm a couple realm points.
