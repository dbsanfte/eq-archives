- 08/03/01 - Dark Majesty at GenCon

Gamespy has yet another article about Dark Majesty, this time their preview is from GenCon. There isn't a whole lot of new info released here but there is a few new interesting tidbits. Here is the main bit of new info I found interesting:

When I tried to pin down Nik Davidson (former producer of Asheron's Call Live and now working on AC2) about how many new dungeons will be available on the new island, he said that he didn't have the stats, so I tracked down Chris Lye of MS who did. Chris hemmed and hawwed and wouldn't give me a number; wouldn't even give me an estimate. His reason? Because Turbine has been over-producing! Yup, they have been giving MS more content than they have been asking for. So, while I can say it's currently somewhere in the 20-30ish range (unofficially speaking), chances are that there will be even more dungeons than that once the expansion pack is released.

This is very good news indeed. I would be happy with just 20 to 30 new dungeons if they were all used correctly (unlike most of the dungeons currently in the game), but I am also glad to hear that the DM team is working to get MORE into the game than MS is asking for. This is a good sign. To read more of the article, click here.

- 07/26/01 - AC:DM interview at Gamespy

Looks like Gamespy has had quite the week in getting some info on Dark Majesty out there. This time they have an interview with Chris Foster, Lead Designer of Dark Majesty. Here's the goodness that you want:

GameSpy: Can you clarify just how big the new landmass is? The original AC landmass was about as big as the state of Rhode Island. About how big is the new island?

Chris Foster: e've been adding land to AC since we shipped, but Marae Lassel is our largest addition yet. For fans of Asheron's Call, it's about the size of the Tiofor Woods or the Obsidian Plain. For fans of the metric system, it's around 37 square kilometers (or 23 square miles for us Americans).

From the sound of that it seems that the new island north of Teth will indeed be Marae Lassel and not the big one east of Eastham. Kind of disappointing if you ask me. I was hoping for a much larger new area given that we won't need to download the info for it. Oh well still new place to explore. You can catch the rest of the article here.
