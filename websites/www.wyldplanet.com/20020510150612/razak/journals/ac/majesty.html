- 11/07/01 - At Her Majesty's Request

Well here I am back again in AC, who'd a thunk. I got the call from EB that my preorder of Dark Majesty was in the Friday it was released and it actually caught me off guard. I was still formatting drives and reinstalling programs due to Windows XP installation and had forgotten all about it.

After installing it I go online and go through the whole logging in thing and then it tells me that I have to subscribe. First off, didn't I have a subscription for the last two years? Ok, guess not. I go to subscribe again and it won't let me. In fact I can't until the following Sunday! 

Turbine really dropped the ball here as many people couldn't get in the first 48 hours. I can't believe they had this many problems with a rush of people coming to sign up, after all wouldn't the initial rush of AC have been larger than the expansion rush?

Well I finally get in and download an extremely huge patch (one hour download for a cable modem) and then jump to holtburg and take the portal to redspire. First I took a look around, as the name of the town said there was a red spire in the center of it. I don't think it was a lifestone but I couldn't find a lifestone to tie to anywhere around either.

I went around to the west side of the island, making my way north just basically looking around. There were all kinds of new critters though I honestly can't even remember the names of them. There were mole-like critters, and the famous peacocks we have seen in the previews, as well as new NPC monsters, the new tummies, and new varieties of pretty much all the old stuff. 

I fought whatever I saw to see where I stood and what kinda loot they dropped. Nothing striked me as a big deal till I started hitting the northern part of the island. Now all of a sudden there were a bunch of Vapor Golems around. I decided to try one out cause I never did get a chance to hit Aerlinthe anyway, but after hitting if for maybe 10 points and getting blasted for around 80 I decided maybe they weren't for me.

I did however scope out an Armored Tusker and went to work on that. I couldn't land an imp on it and from I heard mages have a bit of a problem landing imps on em too. It got to me and I was hitting it for 4s and 5s with the occasional crit of 10 or 15. I thought I was in trouble but then I looked at what he was doin. I think he hit me like 50% of the time and when he did actually land a blow he was doin 1 or 2. When all was said and done I didn't take much damage and I got 25,000 xp for it.

I started working my way into the center of an island and found a plateau I worked my way around it and found a way up and then head back to the Lair of the Eviscorators (or however it's spelt.) I soon found these things are awesome. The dungeon itself is like 6 or 7 levels and full of some very cool looking bugs that drop better than noble loot and 15k xp a piece. They do only slashing and piercing so make sure you bring out the slashing prot stuff. This dungeon is gonna become hugely crowded in the future, of that I am sure.

I did a bit more sight seeing around this area. I had a look at the damn which was pretty large. And there were alot of really cool things to see and lots of things to fight everywhere. I did notice one thing. There didn't seem to be any portals you could tie to, which I actually liked that they did it that way because that makes the whole island seem a little bigger because you can't just hop in a portal and boom you are already where you want to go. You actually have to run! *Gasp* Still there must be some initial place to portal to even if its only to a couple of the basic cities.

So, what's my thoughts on DM? Well I loved it. It added alot to the game. There are lots of new areas to explore, there are lots of new creatures to kill, there are a ton of new quests to find (heck they even have a quest to get a skill point!), new titles to get, and then houses. I didn't get a house because I felt it was just too much work for a part time player like myself but I'm glad they are there.

However, I do think it is too little, too late. If Turbine had gotten this out 6 months ago when I was getting bored, or even 3 months ago when I was hugely bored I think it would have done wonders to help my interest in the game and may not even have given Camelot the chance that I gave it. But releasing it after Camelot... well I'm already hooked over there and DM isn't that much of a change to warrant me really coming back. Camelot right now is the better game.

I do believe in Turbine though. They, I think, put together some of the best quests and story lines in online gaming and also have proven to give the best new content every month out of em all too. But they made some glaring mistakes with AC that just can't be fixed with an expansion no matter how much we wish they could.

If you are sticking with AC and not moving onto another game, I do think Dark Majesty is a must have game. It adds so much to AC that you won't ever regret the small amount money that it takes to get it. But it offers no reason to return if you already are in a new game like Camelot. I will wait for AC2 before I truly return to AC.
