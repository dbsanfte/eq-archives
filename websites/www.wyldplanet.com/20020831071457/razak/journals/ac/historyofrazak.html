- 05/12/01 - History of Razak - printable version

If you have never been to other Journal sites, people who generally do these start from the beginning. Well when I was making Razak, the thought never even occurred to me. So to sort of compensate for the fact that you missed the first 44 levels, I thought I would sum up how Razak came to be and how he progressed as well as where he's at now.

My original character was another Swordsman named Kazar. I had gotten him up to 55 but I felt that I had hit a wall due to poor decision making on him and was getting kind of bored. I have been playing for almost a year and a half, it's bound to happen. So I decided to retire Kazar, and because I couldn't think of a new name flipped his name around and thus born was Razak.

I won't go into details on the template I used for Razak, for that you can go to the template section, but suffice it to say, he is an extreme character. I began in Lytelthorpe as I had with Kazar due to the fact that I know the newbie dungeons well and it is a hop, skip and jump from Eastham.

After dispatching enough Drudge Prowlers to bring me up to level 5, I ran to Eastham and started killing gromnies on the beaches. Once I hit 8, I started taking out the bandies in Thieves' Den until I hit about level 16.

At this point I decided that I am going to need my alchemist (whom I had made awhile earlier) to begin producing ingots so I decided the best way to do that was to find a way to swear to him. Luckily, one of my friends, Kohlia, swore her mule Oolong to Zartov (the alchemist) and I swore to Oolong. And thus born my monarchy with Zartov as the head. Thanks Kohlia.=)

At 16, I ran to the subway and hopped to Qal, running up to the forked trail. I tied my portal there, this used to be the big Luggie hunting spot back in the old days before the cits came about and still was great xp because not as many people go to it anymore. I hunted there about 30 to 40% of the time during the day when no one was there, the other times I ran east to a place I knew that a Gigas along with a bunch of other luggies spawn. 

It is at the gigas spawn that I finally died at level 23. I was feeling powerful because I was soloing the Lithos/Gigas spawn so I decided to play my luck against a Copper Golem. Not smart. It is when you feel powerful when you die the most. I played with these luggies until I hit 27 and then it was onto Olthoi.

I ran back up to Arwic, tied to the portal and headed due north where there is 6 workers that spawn in the middle of the wilderness. It's a bit of a run but you can spend a whole day here at level 27 because it takes a bit of time to kill them. I spent the next few levels north of Arwic and then came back and tied to the Mountain Pass where I spent a few more levels fighting the Olthoi in Lair of Death, finally soloing the Noble at 32 or 33 and then moving on to OHN at 35.

Now at 45 I am starting to get a little bored of Olthoi and have started thinking about places to go next. 

BSD kind of sucks because there are a ton of people there and not enough tuskers, even so I may go there and get my feet wet fighting a few tuskers to see how I do against them. I hate fighting them in open places, as a fighter you really do need the wall since we aren't draining health to replenish all the health we lose from gettin hit.

Another thought I'm having is finding a decent Extas Luggie spot in south dires, I don't know if there are any set spawns but those are what I'm looking for. I got my Magic D up to 137 now as you can see in my screenshot below so I may decide to see how well I resist some magic throwin crits. If anyone has a suggestion, feel free to email me or post it in the message boards and I'll give it a shot.
