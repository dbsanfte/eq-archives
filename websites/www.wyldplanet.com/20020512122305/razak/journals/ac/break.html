Atlan Tool

On Saturday after gaining level 51, I decided to go out and help Kohlia and Teufelhund get their Atlan Tools. They had asked me a month or two ago to do it and I just never had the time until now so I decided to do it quickly. The quest is really easy, you only face tumerocks and while the High Priests can be a little difficult at times, they are still rather easy.

You need to start by going to Glendon Wood and heading to the North Direlands portal at 21N, 25.9E. Once in the Dires you head north east to the Artifex Vault at 26.5N, 45.8W.

Once inside, you buff and head down the shaft. I didn't put on any special prots but magic resistance might be something to think about for the officers and high priests. 

Once at the bottom of the shaft you kill the Tumerocks and you jump in the pit. From here I take the right entrance and head left at the junctions, you do have a switch task to take care of in between. It is pretty self explanatory. Run right, flip switch, run back, flip switch, and then run right again. After you get yourself through the maze (again I take lefts), you'll find yourself in a room with a couch overlooking a room with platforms.

You wanna jump on these platforms and head right over the platforms. Don't worry if ya miss the first few, you can always start over, but the last one has a pit that you don't wanna find yourself in.

Once you make it past this you will find a room with a large pit, you can jump across different platforms so you don't take damage but I don't mind the 40 point fall so I just jump. You end up in a room with crystals on the floor and a door. You need to flip a switch on the opposite side of the room to open the door.

Once you get in, head down the hall dispatching the Tumerock Gladiators, and take the very last door on the right. This will lead to a room with Gladiators on perches shotin down. Flip the switch and head back a little bit to flip another switch in a room on the left. Then had back tot he perch room and the final door will be open, the tool on the ground.

Like I said, easy. =P
