- 07/04/01 - Changes

Change. Alot of people don't like change. This is especially true when dealing with MMOGs. MMOG fans HATE change. The only change that is supposed to come to these games are added new cool gee whiz features like the component buyer so that everyone can conveniently forget about the fact that they even did it in a month.

Not all changes were very well thought out. The new Olthoi helm and the changes to the Shadow Armor were two of the greatest misplaced thoughts in my mind. Olthoi went from an ugly purple, yet wearable helm, to a copy of an olthoi head that was great for Halloween but not much good since then. The Shadow Armor well everything with that could have been fixed in my mind if they just allowed you to cast on it once you put a stone in. I wouldn't even mind if they changed the prots on it with stone a little more to justify that.

Some changes were better thought out than most people probably realize. Weightless money seems to come to mind. I don't think it was totally thought out, I think Turbine KNEW people would exploit the money runs but I don't think they realized just how bad it would be exploited and just how much it would change the game.

The end result was a change in vendor rates that I get the feeling that they have wanted to institute for quite some time. Let's face it, people in this game are just way too rich, it is way too easy to make money, even WITH the changes.

Before the patch I could go to OHN and in one hour get 2 M notes, fill up my bag with mod charges, 20 health potions, and 60 stamina elixer... and my lifestone is in eastham. Imagine how many Ms I could get had I been in AB.

Now I don't go to OHN anymore so I can't give you a comparison, but on the obsidian plains fighting predominately Tuskers I am able to fill up my comps and get about 6 or 7 high charges and I don't need stam or health potions anymore. I do get some spare change but I usually use that to buy comps for my trademule. And keep in mind that Tuskers were originally the dev's idea to try to drain a little cash out of AC a year ago. (lots of xp, no money)

People are quitting over this vendor change and I just don't get it. These people are probably suicidal because they aren't rich in the real world either, either that or they are CEOs of some big company threatening not to pay taxes anymore because there was too much catsup on their hamburgers.

These people say that the vendor change was the last draw because of failures to fix gear or because of the lack of content recently. Well they are trying to fix gear and no they haven't yet but I doubt it's an easy thing to do to rewrite all your frickin servers.

And no content? This month we got the quid orb (best orb in game IMO), Niffis Helm/Shield/Pearl (all of which are great), Creature 7 spells, and new ways to chat in the monarchy (a feature people have been asking to get for a long time. All this forgotten.

Yes the past couple of months before this month have been a little lax. But Turbine has often taken a month or two off from big content to re-gear for something bigger and I am sure the fact that they have 1 or maybe 2 guys purely devoted to fixing gear has hurt their ability to make alot of content.

So rolling with the game and not letting greed get to me, I continue to play. I go along changing with the game because I feel I can do that without much problem. I move from Eastham to Mayoi bringing with me my alchemy mule. I plan on moving Kazar over to Mayoi too (he previously had AB but that town is dead now) so this will actually help me cause now I will have portals to New sub (Kaz) and old sub (Zartov) and OP BSD (me) all in Mayoi.

Meanwhile, getting sick of BSD I start to venture out on the surface and find things much better in the Obsidian Plains. I find many more Tuskers all to myself and am able to pick off the occasional Obsidian Golem or Altered Drudge to keep my comps full.

I have also found the goodness of L6 scroll chests turning in packs and packs of keys for them. So far I've been able to get Armor, Quickness, Coord, Rejuv, Defender, True Value, Acid Bane, Frost Bane and Sword Mastery just to name a few. I've used some of my spares to trade for additional keys and shards and have gotten Souther Quid Frag and a Creature 6 Sceptre along the way.

All in all it's been a good month I think. And I wish others could see on the brighter side of things other than change.
