 So you want your own personal avatar picture? I'll tell ya how.

If you are going to use your own avatar, the picture must be a 60x60 picture and  you gotta have it on your own server.

You don't have a picture for your own avatar you say? Well you're in luck. I have decided to offer a few generic avatar's so you don't have to make your own, find your own, or host your own. (or all of the above.) 

Here's what you have to do:

1) On the main message boards page click on "My Control Center" on the top of the page.

2) Once at "My Home", click on the "Edit Profile" button. 

3) Copy the address below and end it with the filename of the picture of your choice and insert it into the "Personal Photo URL" text box.

4) Make sure the "Use Personal Photo" box is clicked.

5) Click the "Save Changes" box at the bottom of the page.

6) You're done! That's all ya gotta do.
