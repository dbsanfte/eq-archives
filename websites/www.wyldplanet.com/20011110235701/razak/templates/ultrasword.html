The Ultra Sword Template
Str: 90
End: 17
Coord: 100
Quick: 67
Focus: 40
Self: 16		 Spec: Sword
Melee Defense
Trained: Item Enchantment
Mana Conversion

This template could easily be 100/10/100/70/40/10 that UA people use and I could see alot of Sword people do this as well. But I decided that the points would better suit me more spread out. 

I feel with a sword, given the slowness and stamina usage that it has over UA, you should at least take 15, I took the extra 2 to get that extra health point and stamina and am happy I did. I'd rather take the points from Strength than Coord because while you can make a little more damage with the Strength, Coord affects both Sword and Melee Defense.

Having a sort of high focus does help make up for the fact that I didn't start with Arcane Lore, it made it so that I could use that society stuff from the get go. And I took a little more than the standard 10 Self because I like to actually cast all 4 weapon buffs for the first few levels.

One of the newer ideas I've seen is NOT to spec in Melee Defense, I don't understand this. My last char didn't have it spec'd and I regret it. I now have about 30 points more melee D and 10 less levels (pumped). I can take a good dozen soldiers with only taking about 20 damage unless they start gettin behind me or I run out of stam. So believe me it DOES make a diff.

That being said if you do spec both Sword AND Melee D, you have to sacrifice something. I chose to sacrifice Arcane and Healing. I took Arcane at level 5, but I doubt I will ever get Healing which is fine because I don't take a great deal of damage and when I do I got potions of healing as well as stam to health.

That's right, I took Life at 27, and should get Creature at 60. That will make me a 3 school Sword with mana conversion at the level of 60, pretty much unheard of by those taking Healing. I would have had to wait till 90 to get all 4 of those AND healing. It's a sacrafice I was willing to make and have no regrets about.
