- 09/06/01 - Autumn Cleaning

Every now and then you have to take on the task of going through all the stuff on your mules and getting rid of the crap that you, and no one else really wants. After over a year and a half playing this game, I have amassed alot of items, filling up all my mules and even some on Razak. I have stuff that I thought looked good a year ago when I was still kind of new, decent leather stuff that just isn't all that useful since the armor patch, and even stuff I was collecting for that Mace char that I've always wanted to make but never did.

Now not all of this was bad stuff, it's just stuff I know I won't find use. After all, I've been hanging onto some of this stuff for almost a year in some cases and haven't used it yet. On top of this 95% of my friends are all sword users as well so if I don't need it, chances are they won't either.

So the first step is sorting through all my junk and deciding what I do and don't need. Luckily thanks to Decal and ImpInventory, this isn't as big of a chore as it used to be. I just cast focus and all the appraisal spells on each mule and press a button. Anything that still wasn't read I true value and it'll usually be ID'd after all that. Once I get all my equipment in text file out of AC, all that's really left is deleting what you want to keep, leaving you with a list of stuff that you want to get rid of.

The next task is figuring out how to dispose of this stuff. Like I said before, not all of it's bad, just not useful to me, so it could be useful to others. You don't really wanna get rid of something that could have a happy home with someone! I decided on an auction.

I have never done an auction before so I didn't exactly know what I should or should not do. I had my list of 200 items and made it a little easier to read, categorizing them better and taking out prices so that it wouldn't take as much time to load or take 2 lines to display. I told all my friends a couple days going into the auction about the list so they could get what they wanted while they could.

Then I posted the list and let it go for 3 days during Labor Day weekend. It was alot of work, I basically had to bump once an hour to keep it on the top few pages and settle alot of PMs from confused people.

It didn't go quite as well as I'd hoped. I knew I wouldn't sell all 200 items, but I was hoping for 50 to 75 items to go. I got rid of about 20 instead. I think I was a victim of having too many items so I think future auctions will be held with just the categories, such as scrolls.

During the auction I also realized that I may not have all that much room for all the cracks, keys and money I'd be bringing in, not really alleviating my original problem. So I traded for a BD6 scroll and brought it to the Essence of Artifice for my Infected Carress!

While I was at it I decided to take a tour of all the essences to turn in various scrolls that I have collected and make a little more space. I can't tell ya how fun it is to turn in a scroll and then see a new name like Executor's Blessing in your spellbook. As confusing as the different spell names for old spells can be, I am glad for it because it is something interesting at least and I am sure I will soon enough get used to all these new names.

While I was in the spellcasting mood, I decided that it was high time to learn me a few dispells. I ran to the Luggian Quarry to grabs some medium grade chorozite for comps. The dungeon isn't that difficult to navigate through, the hollow boulders do hurt from time to time but I just basically ran past the luggies without even stopping to bother with them.

I also realized that I had lost my Ravenous Sword so I decided to go a little further south the the Excavation. This one is a little harder with all the Extas near the bottom, but at 64 it still isn't that difficult for me and the design is still really simple so I didn't get lost.

Once I had the ore refined, crushed and made into potions I learned Dispel critter 4 and 5. All I can say is WOW. These are expensive! Not only are the comps kind of difficult to get with chorozite AND diamond scarabs, but Level 4 costs 200 and goes up 50 every level. Level 4s aren't actually too bad for me because my Mana Conversion drops it down to 50 for a use but that still is quite a bit really considering MC was used for it.

I've come to the conclusion that these dispels aren't really meant for melee, and maybe that's what they had in mind but man, seems to me that meleers end up with more spells on us to begin with due to our low self and focus. Oh well, no biggy, I decided to turn the potions into actual dispel potions and use those instead.

This week I also refound the DELETE button in the spellbook. After a year of playing this game I had totally forgot that that was even there! So in spirit of my Autumn cleaning & spell learning week I deleted old spells I wouldn't use anymore because why use BD1 when you have BD6.

I finished learning my main L6 spells this week too. While I probably won't use for a little bit, it's nice to get that learning part out of the way.

If you are wondering why I don't use more L6 spells, well let me fill ya in. Since L5 durations were increased there isn't a whole lot of reason to make the move. L5s for the most part last almost as long as L6, and they give you almost as much of a bonus, plus they are cheaper to cast! For the marginal increases in duration (sometimes by a whopping 2 seconds!) and bonuses, you get more expensive spells, more fizzling, and a higher burn rate.

So while I can cast them at a reasonable rate, I'd rather wait till MC comes up to better bring cost down and normal skills to come up to bring fizzling down.
