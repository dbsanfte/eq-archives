The Uber UA template

For a long time UA was known as being the most powerful type you could be, and while it may or may not have changed a little, depending on who you talk to, it still is one of the big 3 at the very least. With that in mind I asked Garr, a level 89 UA friend of mine, to share his thoughts on UA. 

It is from these thoughts that I derived the following templates. I am gonna do these a little differently as he gave me various stat templates and skill templates separately, not merging the two into a true template, so you can mix and match between the two basically to make your own template.

As you can tell, about the only must is how to do the first 3 attribs. If some of you question having a low quickness, Garr informs me that UA speed generally maxes out about 190 to attack at 0 without the use of Swift Killer.

As for skills I'm gonna try to keep it to Garr's own words as close as possible.

"There are many ways to do an UA char, since specing UA only
cost 4 skill credits u can train pretty much anything u want. Spec
UA/Life/creature or UA/Life/Creature/Lore, both of these are VERY extreme templates, good for the veteran player. Melee/UA/lore spec is a
good all around template. Melee/UA is good and u can take
bow/lore/healing/life/item/creature/manaC by lvl 80 if u just spec
melee/UA. Life/UA/Lore is the most popular UA template at the moment."

So there you have it. Personally I think UA's are just way too lucky because that 4 points does open up the game alot for them, I don't for the life of it understand why they whine that Sword gets double-strike when they can choose to spec and train in so much that I never will be able to do. If I had to choose from his above templates I would probably go with the first set of attribs and spec UA/Life/Creature, though of course I would modify a few things here and there to make it my own.=P
