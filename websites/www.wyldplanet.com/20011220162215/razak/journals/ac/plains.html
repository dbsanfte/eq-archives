- 07/10/01 - Plain Ol' Plains

The Obsidian Plains is currently one of the better places for melee to be after they hit 50, though that may change after the upcoming patch. They are covered with Tuskers and Virindi and also have quite a bit of Altered Drudges, Shadows, and Obsidian & Diamond Golems. From 50 to about 60 I think your best bet is to stick with Tuskers and your occasional Drudge.

Now at 57, I still am based out of the BSD on the plains. But I rarely go in and fight in there anymore. I have a set path that I run, it's only about 10 clicks long, I run  to the end where I fight awhile, run back and repeat. This I feel allows everything in between to spawn while I hit other areas and seems to work well.

I don't particularly advise it the best idea to start hittin virindi yet. They are a pain in the butt.  There usually 3 to 5 in a pod and they all start chain castin on ya and also call any nearby virindi and tuskers. This can easily be fetal if there are a few pods around. 

To pick out the tuskers from the virindi, the best method is to cast an imperil on em to drag em out. I usually hit the imperil about 75% of the time on tuskers so that first tusker will be killed usually in 1 to 3 hits. That's invaluable if there are about 10 comin at ya.

I have also more recently found out the beauty of taking out the occasional Altered Drudge. Now these things in general are a huge pain in the butt, but it is often well rewarded. They come in groups of 4, 2 usually have thrown weapons, 2 have swords, all lightning. They also debuff alot  and cast drains and lightning bolts so it's best to have some potions on ya and have lightning protection on to get rid of em.

I usually only get to do the two with swords because the ones with the throwing weapons are just too stubborn. You only get about 5k xp for each drudge killed but the loot you get is easily better than the Olthoi Noble even on bad days. I've gotten lots of different items that I've been able to trade, pass down on mules and use that are more than worth the effort. And the stuff that isn't keepable is usually worth alot of money making them a good compliment to the xp rich Tuskers.

Outside of those I do get to kill an Obsidian Golem sometimes. They aren't too difficult at this level but they only give ya about 5k xp and the loot is much to be desired. All the same it does add a little bit of pocket money and it also gives me motes and obsidian hearts from time to time. 

The one big negative I have with them is that they call all golems in the area. I have more than once been faced with 5 obsidian golems banging down on me and while a couple aren't too bad 5 constantly casting and draining is just too much. They also call Diamond Golems in this way, needless to say I only attack the Obsidian Golems if they come to me and it's away from other golems. This happens more than it sounds.

Stats!

Well this week marked a rather large landmark, I hit 100 million total experience! I've been mainly working on getting Mana Conversion and Magic Defense in hopes to be able to use the Quiddity orb and resist spells more. In fact i have been resisting a little bit, not much but when I do resist the occasional Altered Drudge or Obsidian Golem it is a huge bonus in the fight. I'm getting close to 100 in Loyalty which is going to be my stopping point in putting points into that skill. I think i get enough points for Zartov already and shouldn't have to put more points into it on this end.
