Links

Camelot Vault - Right now this is pretty much the main news site. The devs sometimes post on their developer forums, and they usually get the recent news up.

Camelot Guild - Mostly a site to find info on quests, items, and maps and other useful info on the game however it does carry some news.

Warcry - Formerly the crossroads network, they've been my favorite news site for awhile. Unfortunately they don't seem to have the popularity they did in AC, nor the staff or developer support. Still a very good site for news, and hopefully their content will get better with time.

Palomides.net - Source for news on what's going on in the Palomides server, includes up to date info on who controls what forts and relics and when it changed hands last. Also has some good message boards for the server as well.

Midgard Weekly - A site geared towards Midgard players that has short stories of various sorts. People can submit their own stories to be posted in the newsletter so if you got a story that you'd like to be told, this is a good place to do it!
