- 05/22/01 - Of Olthoi, Danby, and other doodads

Well despite the fact that it was patch week, it has been a rather slow moving week for me. Nothing especially interesting happened, but I did get to go out and see a couple of new things and thought I'd throw in my thoughts on the nest as well.

A trip to the Olthoi Horde Nest

You shouldn't be in a nest if you aren't at least about level 35, if you get much below that you become a skimmer and if that's all yer gonna do, go back to the cit where ya came from, we deal with too many skimmers as it is. You CAN fight olthoi before 35 though, there are many other places to do it, I just wouldn't suggest a nest because there are just SO many olthoi it is very difficult.

Now alot of people wonder how I do it so I thought I would take a little time to explain. I get in the entryway and begin buffing. I buff with Rejuv 5, Armor 5, and Acid  5 (i use a piercing 4 shirt), and then buff my sword with level 5s as well. On top of that i buff my mattey coat with acid 4, piercing 4, and imp 5, and my shield with acid 4 and piercing 4 and then add a regen 4 to heal me so that I won't have to. Obviously it takes me a little longer to buff but is worth it.

Upon going in you are given the choice of left or right. I go right, if you follow the right wall you eventually get to a crossroad with about a dozen workers straight ahead, 2 workers to the left side, and 3 workers to the right side. This was my main area until I got to about 45. Alot of people like soldiers better but soldiers only give 350 more xp than a worker and is alot more difficult than it's worth. The main thing you gotta remember here is to not panic. Every time I see someone panic, I see them die shortly after, if you just chill and stay in the narrow hallways and heal when ya get low in health, you usually do fine.

Once ya get done with those head to right and there will be a short hallway with 6 more workers, you can use the corner to drag them out so that you won't have to take them all on in the wider hallways (they have a habit of getting behind you if you do.)

Believe it or not just that alone at the smaller levels will take up all of your buffs and then it's time to rebuff.

At 45, I started to take the left and stayed that way. You get to two rooms, I clean out the room on the left, and then the room on the right. It's the room on the right that I usually buff myself when they run out, it's a pretty safe place if you keep it clear.

In the room on the left there is a hallway down. This is another good hallway for me, the entrance is more skinny so that if you get into trouble you can run back here and turn around and fight the soldiers well. There are alot of soldiers in this hall and I just run the length which extends down 2 levels and then run back up and buff. I can get about 500k an hour if I don't have to compete as much for them.

Danby's Outpost

Well the nests were kind of crowded one day so I decided to spice up the day and go take a look at the new city that the dev's have graced us with.

I portaled to the subway and took a ride to Zaikhal where I knew there was a portal to the Outpost. I ran to the Outpost portal at 16.7N, 1.6W (untieable) and soon found myself at the northern edge of the inland sea. There wasn't too big of crits here, was actually like the beaches of eastham with blue wasps, water golems, and gromnies running around. The portal exits right next to the Outpost so it wasn't that long of a trip at all.

The outpost was rather plain. There were alot of open spaces, the mage was right at the entrance, the provisioner in the middle and the armorer was in one of the towers. The buy rate is a good 130% which puts it on par with AB and Teth so I could see some bigger levels coming here when they are sick of those two cities. Otherwise I can't imagine why anyone outside of a newb would want to be here.

I checked ACexplorer and there were only two dungeuns in the area, a tumerock one and an undead one, I heard there was a new subway leading to various points in the dires and filled with virindi but I didn't go to it because I am not too confident against virindi. There are also no nearby lifestones or portals outside of the new subway so there really is no value of the place outside of high sell rates.

I decided to see how good of a place it'd be for hunting the dires so I headed out west. I ran for like 10 or 15 minutes and had just seen my first group of Extas Lugians. I decided this was way too large of a run so I think I'd rather be in AB if given the choice, at least then I wouldn't have to give up my portal tie.

Doodads

A couple other notes is that I ran to turn in some Large triangles for xp. I have heard some say that they aren't really worth much even at 45k, but they do add up. I turned in 8 + 1 small, all unlocked which got me 375k xp, getting me very close to a new level.

As you can see I've been working a bit more on upping Melee D, Sword, and Stamina since my last update. I also thought I'd include Loyalty, I do raise it because all the xp I get for it goes to my alchemy mule. I did die 2 times this week, they always seem to come in pairs for me, but hopefully I won't die for awhile now.

I also could not resist temptation and am now a certified Swordfighter. Good bye Adventurer!
