These are the various programs that I use in conjunction with AC and highly recommend. To ensure the safety of everyone instead of linking to the download directly or posting them on my site I decided to just have a link to the program's site to ensure that you aren't downloading a virus of some sort.

Spiltbeans - Spiltbeans WAS the best program out there for finding out the spell components that you needed for all your spells. But alas, the maker has decided not to offer it anymore due to players using speedhacks. Let's hope Turbine fixes this problem soon so that we may get a most excellent tool back.

Splitpea - The old must have for spellcasters, Splitpea is still useful for quick spell lookups if you are looking for descriptions as well as various skill calculations and starting new characters.

ACExplorer - This is a must have for your mapping needs. Contains pretty much every point of interest, portal, city and vendor in the game. Also helps you calculate routes to get to places and lets you download updates every month to find those new places that were just added in the last patch. I would just download the old version as I don't think it's worth the extras to pay $20.

ACTool - Yes I have been known to macro and this is the program that I use. I don't macro much and usually only to raise alchemy and cooking skills. I am not one of those freaks who macros because I don't wanna play but I want to have a big char, I just think the current trade skills rather suck and is way too repetitive for me to do myself.

Decal & Sixth Sense - These two programs are NOT made by the same guy, but for some reason the Decal page has not been working, but you can get both programs from the same page. Decal allows people to make plug-ins for AC, Sixth Sense is a plug in that will allow you to see various items, monsters, players and corpses without hitting ] or needing the radar, these have become almost as must have as splitpea/beans is. I must warn however that alot of people have difficulty getting these installed so read the instructions carefully.
