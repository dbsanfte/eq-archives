- 12/05/01 - Grouping Frustrations

I'm now up to level 41 and I must say my production has really slowed down now. This has happened for two main reasons really. 

The first is that it takes alot longer to get levels between 40 and 50, and this is a well known fact. To this I do say the half steps are very nice and was a good idea, though I do wonder why they just didn't make the xp needed to level smaller and raise the cap by 5 levels. Would have been same thing and less confusing when a friends says he's level 43.5.

The other real problem is the difficulty finding a group. At this point of the game it is more forced to find a group than at any other time. Soloing puts up such crappy experience now adays that I don't even bother playing if I can't find a group and there are so few people who are above 40, I usually just don't bother playing my main character.

For this I say shame on Mythic. I understand the need they have to make people want to group with each other more than play on their own but that should never be the only choice, and despite their reassurances that it isn't the only choice, it really is once you hit about 35. 

But the inherent problem with forcing people into playing in groups with no hope of spending quality time alone is that there aren't always alot of people on. I personally am a daytime person because I appreciate that there is less lag, when nighttime rolls around I jet and do some real world stuff. This leaves me with a dilemma because there are less people, and thus not as much opportunity for grouping, hence I need to solo more.

I'll say it right now that one of the main reasons I didn't go to EQ was that it forced people into groups. I could never understand how anyone could live with that. I've heard horror stories of people who have literraly spent hours in town begging for a group in EQ and when they finally get one not being able to spend much time because they spent so much just looking for one. No game is worth that.

Now granted DAoC has a nice tool to help people find groups. But not many people actually use it, and even if they did the high level areas are really spread out and if someone in Vanern Swamp needs a group and I'm in Spind, I will never know. I would gladly take the 5 minutes to run over there for the group but the stupid find a group thing would rather pick up a lame ass level 20 trying to pimp me for xp in Spind.

The main reasons I came to this game from AC was cause I had gotten bored with that game and the promise of not forcing people into groups the way EQ did. And to begin with it seemed to be true, grouping was a better way to go but soloing was still viable. Now if I were to try to solo it'd take me a month to get to level 42, but if I group it'd prolly take a week. That's just too much of a difference to me, and it's a waste of my time. I just assume play Civ3, which I have.

Meeting Old Friends

With all that out of my system, this has been a pretty nice week down memory lane. I met up with a few people from the olden days of Shadows of Yserbius and Emperiana. Now Shadows of Yserbius (YS) especially was a game that holds my heart, I don't think any of these modern games could even really compete.

It was graphical and massively multiplayer for the times all 8 years ago, and really was mostly about the people, as all these games really are. No matter how pretty they try to make a game, no matter how complex they say it is, it will never compete with a game that is so simple and so about the people more than the game. 

Those people were family and I truly believe we had a closer relationship between us because there were only around 80 people per host instead of 3000. We may not have all known each other but to this day if I meet someone from YS, I greet them as if they were a long lost relative talking about the good old days of yore and the yearning to go back to that time.
