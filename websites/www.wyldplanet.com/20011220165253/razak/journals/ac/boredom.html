- 08/16/01 - Boredom Sets

Boredom can be a dangerous thing, it starts ya actin a little more silly, thinking things a little more nutsy, and doing things you wouldn't normally do. Well believe it or not, I've still been feeling the burnout that occurred when I spent too much time in AC to get level 60. Even though I am already 62, I still feel bored with the game because of those couple of days that I was in way too much.

Boredom isn't a unique thing to MMORPGs but for many it seems to be amplified in them. Normal games you will get bored of usually in a month, it is a good one that keeps your attention over that month. This is not so in MMORPGs which are expected to keep our attention for many months if not years.

One of the mains ways people combat the stagnancy that is likely to occur is to make a new character. Well that was the reason I made Razak and I don't feel that doing that when is really going to help me in that area. I also don't wanna give up right when I'm getting to an area of, what I feel is, respectability level-wise and when I'm on the verge of being able to go to cool new places I've never been, like Aerlinthe. Plus, I have all my slots full of trade mules and I don't want to buy a second account just to mule, that brings my pack ratish stance in-game into real world money.

I started up Quest Day with friends as a way to get out of the normal routine every now and then and do something fun with a bunch of friends. You see I'm normally a quester and I normally hunt alone (there really is not much reason to hunt with others in this game). So doing this is supposed to invigorate the game.

Well this latest Quest Day was a bit of a wash, everyone was having real world problems that prevented us from doing it, or wanting to do it. I personally had a back problem that was making me more frustrated in the game and thus I really didn't wanna do it at this time.

And even so, it's only once every 4 weeks, at most that gives us something positive to think about for 2 weeks out of a month. Maybe I will change it to 3 weeks.

Patch days are another source of excitement. I actually consider them almost as equal as going out and questing. First you have excitement leading up to the patch, with the teaser and speculation. Then you have the actual patch with new stuff to go find and experiment with. Unfortunately, Turbine has failed us a bit recently in these areas.

While I personally found July's patch quite exciting, it didn't have a whole lot of long term excitement. I have plenty of purple plants and have since stopped picking em, I am still too small for Caul, and the new OP is not only more populated by critters but also people who have heard of the new population of crittters. The last one can make it very frustrating because no matter where you try to go, someone else seems to be there too.

The rest of the patches have been extremely lack in content. And because of that and in tangent with the fact that Turbine no longer talks about upcoming patches, the pre-patch excitement is almost entirely gone. And because the patches have been lackluster too, the only real post-patch excitement you get is the first few hours, after that you realize it's crap and continue to do what you've been doing for the past couple of months before that as if it never even happened. All this is quite shameful on Turbine's part.

Two other solutions to the boredom problem is vassals and PvP.

Vassals are a good idea. And in fact I've been looking for some decent vassals for quite some time. But HG is one of the oldest worlds alive and there are very few new people coming in. Most people will see the smaller numbers on SC and WE and go there and few older people will move to a crowded server either. And now almost two years into the game everyone has pledged and usually are pretty happy with who they are with. Even with good friends of mine, I find it difficult to try to pull them away from their monarchs, and maybe rightfully so but it is kind of frustrating sometimes.

On top of the above, I am also kind of strict on who I let in. I have to know people, I wanna know that it'll work between both of us. I wouldn't just let anyone in cause I don't wanna have someone who I totally despise under me, just don't wanna deal with it. And don't want someone who is only after loot or levels, get yer own, those things come, just not when looking for em. Mostly I'm looking for friends that I can help out when they need it, and maybe the vassal/patron thing isn't even needed,  but it does sometimes help to meet new people.

As for PvP, well I'm not a mage and I'm not lookin to die every time a mage comes on the screen so I just assume stay white for now.

The only other thing to do is to get out of the game for awhile. Take a break. Which is what I've been doing for the past couple of weeks. I've done this before since I started the journal and I will most likely do it again. Every few months you need to. This game just gets too repetitive and if you have a spurt where you are on too much like I did, you just NEED to get out. If you don't, you are not sane, I suggest going and seeking out help.

Statage
