These are some links to some of the more useful sites that I have found. I hope to add more soon.

Asheron's Call Tips - This site is good for most gaming news out there. It also contains Unofficial Build Notes that contains alot of what is in each build and also has some good quest info. Make sure to give one of their banners a click while you are there, they almost went under once and it'd be a shame if they had to for real. They especially suffer around patch time so keep that in mind.

Maggie the Jackcat's Fabulous AC Guide - This site has alot of really great info. Everything from quests, interesting spots, fashion tips, and patch news. Good site for finding info on pretty much anything, but no real news involved.

AC Vault - This is my source for news. Alot of people prefer COD, which is an ok one too, but AC Vault's URL is easier for me to remember and I can't be bothered by bookmarks. AC Vault also contains an auction which is kind of nice even though there aren't a great deal of people putting things in it. News-wise I think they are pretty much equal.

Dizzarian's Diary of Deretth - Reading his diaries gave me the thought of making my own. Pretty good reading, especially if you are considering making a battlemage. One negative thing is the frames, all his links stay within the frame so beware.

AC Stratics - They seem to put up more news about what Stratics is doing than Dereth, but then there are other times when they seem to be the only site putting up Dereth news. Iffy news site, but they do have other nice features. Of note is the AC radio show, ok show I suggest at least fast forwarding to the Dev talks. They also have a monarch of the month and good quest guides.

The Monster Attack Project - This is a decal program but if you don't use decal it is still useful site. The site contains all the info on monster attacks taken from the decal program. It is very useful if you want to find out what a certain monster uses to attack.

Planet GameCube - Not a related site on any sense of the word. Planet GameCube dishes out the latest from the GameCube and Game Boy Advance. Not AC related but I am a Nintendo loyalist and E3 is coming up and well I'm just pumped for this system to come out.
