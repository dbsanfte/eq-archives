3) Sound. I don't think I've heard anyone complain about the sound. Booth and company did a great job with it.
       4) The new interface to enter the game. While I wish that you could save your password (I know it's bad security-wise but I'm just so lazy!), and that there was a refresh button in the game world list, it is much improved over AC1. No more Zone crap littering your entrance into the game, and also no more .net! YAY!!!
       5) UI. I've seen a few people who don't like the User Interface that much, but I like it. It's small and has a lot of functionality. I do wish you could move things around a little and I think the arrows for the quick key bar move the bar backwards but those are minor quibbles, I think it's much better than AC1.
       6) New Critters. Like I said earlier, I haven't had the chance to really get out there yet only gettin to level 12 and all but what I have seen I do like. I like that they brought in dragons into the AC world because I do really love dragons and I missed havin them around in AC1. I don't really care for the Vermin but another type that I've seen recently was the Lasher which I thought were really cool. There are a few others too that I can't remember well, the Grutts come to mind, haven't fought them much face to face but boy are they big!
       7) Races. I Like the new races a lot. I kind of got sick of seeing humans in AC1 (and DAoC) and it's nice seeing something different. Everything about them is different too... the look of the armor and weapons they use, the skill trees and all that. I do however wish there was more variety in the race. For example, there only seems to be 2 kinds of armor for each race, the robe and normal armor... they need more different kinds.
