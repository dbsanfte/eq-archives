- 05/09/01 -

I am sure everyone here is sort of wondering what this is all about, or not if you read the title and have any skills in the reasonable deduction area. Well for those less fortunate, I will explain.

My name is Razak, and this website is designed to chronicle my life. It's a journal of what I do when I'm online. The hope in this is that people reading this will find out how to play the game better, as well as learn a little bit about me.

Most people start these Journals when they start their character's. Well this isn't the case with me. Razak is my second character and I have no interest in starting him over as of yet. I may later choose to add additional journals for my other character's in the future, but at this time it's just of Razak.

Right now this site is kind of bare bones. I intend to start with a History of me and Razak, links to sites that I find useful, and a message board. In the future I plan to have the true journals, which I hope to update weekly or bi-weekly, polls, additional links, templates, family info, and some other useful info. I may also decide to analyze patches and tell what it means for us melees, or more specific, swordsman.

I hope you enjoy my journals, if you have any comments or suggestions feel free to contact me.
