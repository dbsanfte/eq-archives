 Allegiance Theory

My theory on monarchs, patrons, vassals, and monarchies in general is that it isn't about the xp or the stuff that anyone gets, but more about companionship and mutual support.

To that end, as both a patron and a monarch, I'd rather provide services than to provide free loot or power leveling to my vassals. I feel that if you do these things for your vassals, you are actually doing them and yourself a disservice. By doing this, they become more dependant on you to play and know less about the game and respect for other players. They basically become spoiled children that  you end up having to cast out later because you did it wrong in the first place. I just assume keep all my vassals.

The services that we provide are simple:

  • Alchemy/Cooking Services - This is done through my alchemy mule (or the true monarch if you will). All services that he can accomplish be it HQ ingot, comp bow construction, or dying. He will do for members of the monarchy for free.
  • Fletching/Lockpicking Services (soon) - I will soon be making a Fletching/Lockpicking mule to be able to get those tasks done. This will be similar to the above services just for making arrows and such.
  • Monthly Quests - I try to arrange one major quest a month. Right now we are working through the atlan stones like in the Sparking Stone journal. I am hoping in the future that other members will start arranging their own quests in similar fashion but for now this will do well.=)
  • Housing (soon) - With word of the expansion pack we have begun talking about ways to get at least one prime house for monarchy use. Due to the earliness in announcement most of what we are doing is speculation on what will happen but we will adjust plans as news comes out.
  • Body Recovery - We can help aid in body recovery as long as you don't get yourself in too big of a hole.
  • Message Boards - I do provide a message board on this site that can be used for communicating with each other to set up events or to just chat and get to know each other. If the monarchy chatter becomes too much I will add a new board for only the monarchy but until then I like to keep things as consolidated and simple as possible.
  • Mules - One of the few rules that I have is that you allow the monarchy one mule. The reason for this is two-fold. First, it provides every body with an easier way to get up in rank, I would like at least every player in the monarchy to be at least rank 2 so that they feel they get something back for sure. The second reason is that I am going to use vowing mules as a way for members to get to know each other. I will match up your mule to members that you may not know or may not know well to help you hopefully get to know each other better. I hope this will help the whole group feel more tightly knit than the average allegiance would.

Loot

I can see people looking through this and saying "what no free loot?" Well no, not per say. I don't want anyone joining my monarchy to get stuff so I will not promise anyone anything. Does that mean you won't be given anything? Not at all. Ask Kohlia and she will tell you that she is kept well stocked.

I would rather have more self-reliant vassals than people who count on their patrons and monarchs for everything they get. But if you are having a difficult time getting something that you need, just ask I will see if I can get it for you and if I can't I will keep my eye out for it. And there are times that I will just drop stuff on people who didn't necessarily ask or even want it, if ya don't want it give it to your vassal or trade it.

What I expect from Members

I expect that members treat everyone with respect... that means no stealing kills, no stealing loot that's being muled, etc. This stuff is common sense consideration for people. Not only to people in the same monarchy but to people you don't even know.

No meetings! Yay! I hate meetings, in the past I have been part of guilds that had a meeting every month and I always felt them dull and rather unhelpful. I also think that my monthly quest is a good replacement for it. Now we have something where we all get a prize at the end and have fun doing it so we can bond. At the same time we can all talk about important issues that is bothering members of the group. I also believe in email and message boards.

In light of the fact that I have no meetings. I do hope that everyone gets to know each other on their own, using the message board, quests or whatever it takes to do so.

I expect that you help out fellow members, but not to the point where it becomes a burden. It's just a game and while I think that members should help each other, it should never get to a point where that's all any of us are doing in the game. Have fun damn it!

What I don't expect

I don't expect players to put all their time in the monarchy as stated above. But I do expect some.

I don't expect you to have to follow many rules. I try to keep things as simple as possible.

I don't expect you to do what you don't want to do. If I, or someone else, asks you for help on something and you don't want to do it, DON'T. I can always ask someone else and it won't hurt my feelings that much if you don't want to for whatever reason. I don't want something to get between people just because one person is asking another to do too much and that person is feeling like he is too burdened by it.

Anyway this has gotten way too long and while I like to babble I don't like boring people too much. (bad combination that is by the way.) So if you are interested in joining, @tell me or email me (or the person you want to join) and we will get in contact with you to see if you will fit in with us.
