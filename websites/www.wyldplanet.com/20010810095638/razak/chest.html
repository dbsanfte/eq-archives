 Community Chest
 

The Community Chest is a program designed by Ezboard.com, who hosts the message boards, to allow the entire community to chip in to help support the board and get more services.
Contribute here

I have signed up for this program in hopes to further improve the message boards without having to take the burden of payment totally upon myself. I hope that those who enjoy the boards and want more out of them will be willing to chip in a dollar or two to improve them.

The first goal that I have set for the community is Blue CSC membership. What this membership includes is basically the removal of popup advertisements while in the boards. Gold would further take out banners, but I felt we can live with banners alot easier than those annoying popups.

I hope that you decide to help out the community. I do not come in contact with any of the money, it is all handled directly by Ezboard to improve the service. Thank you for your support.
