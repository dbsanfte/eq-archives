- 06/12/01 - Tales of an Ugly Shield - printable version

 
Well first off, I found out Saturday morning that I am in Phase 4 of the Anarchy Online beta test. After 24 hours of downloading, I am now at 87%! You would think they would have planned it better, but I am almost done so no worries. I will give you more information on that game as I give it more time!=)

 

After another week of playing in the nest for almost the entire week, I finally was able to get a new tie to BSD! I had to wait in Subway for about a half hour before someone finally opened it and I jumped on through.

This time it is Obsidian Plains. Now I only have weekend experience to compare the two but it does seem that OP is a little busier than the teth one. This is most likely due to the fact that it is on the plains which mages.

 
While I was trying to get a ride to BSD this week, I was sitting around in Subway when someone came to me asking me if I would help him do the new Luggian quest.

 

Well for some reason I thought he had a corpse to recover and decided to help him get that. When I found out that there was no corpse and he just wanted help to do the quest, I decided to stick around anyway cause I said I'd help and I'd never done the quest before.

I didn't really care for the quest and to be honest wasn't paying too much attention so I can't give coords. It almost seemed like an errand quest to me. I think I missed the first part so I may not be totally knowing on this though.

First we ran to a camp filled with gigas luggies and a few dread matties on a 4 minute spawn. In the center of all this was a master of the hunt and a luggian Lt. I quickly dispatched the master but with all the rocks being hurled at me I had to run. Big mistake, I found out the Lt disappears if you don't kill him.

So we run to the second camp where this time we hide behind a hill and imperil the Lt to drag him over so that we can kill him easier. There are dire matties in addition to the others here, and accompanying the Lt is a Luggian Commander and a Gigas Raider. The lt, commander, and raider all have hollow weapons but the rest does not. wasn't too hard to beat em all and the commander I got to keep the shield.

 
Now this shield is a hideous looking shield and I don't know if I can do something else to make it worthwhile, but as is it is pretty useless. It weighs over 1.1k bu and only has a 130AL and average prots. All the same it is cool to have just because it is unique so I threw it on a mule.

We did go to a third camp which had alot of extas luggies in it, but given that I was pretty much soloing it I decided that we shouldn't take on this one. (no hills either.) The other guy was level 29 and wasn't gonna be much help here so we called it quits and the guy got the items he wanted anyway.

End of story. It hasn't been too exciting of a week otherwise, just been killing olthoi and tuskers. I did get to level 52 and have started raising health, stamina, sword, and melee defense a bit more so that I can take out the tuskers faster. My hope is to be on the obsidian plains hunting by 55 so that I no longer have to deal with the over-camped BSD.
