 Titles on these message boards are based mostly upon how many posts you have made on them. The exception to this rule is moderators and administrators who not only have posted a great deal but have also earned my trust. Moderators and Administrators are meant to help me manage the message boards so that it doesn't become too huge of a task for me alone.

Each Title is accompanied by a title graphic which is placed below the poster's title which is meant to further demonstrate a player's rank in the message boards.

Once a poster reaches 115 posts, that poster can get a custom title. To get one just email me a message with the title in which you want. If I approve of the title (nothing inappropriate please), then I will change your title with the next update at the latest.
