- 12/19/01 - An Epic of New

epic adj 1: surpassing the ordinary especially in size or scale; "an epic voyage"; "of heroic proportions"; "heroic sculpture" [syn: heroic, larger-than-life] 2: constituting or having to do with or suggestive of a literary epic; "epic tradition" [syn: epical] n : a long narrative poem telling of a hero's deeds [syn: epic poem, epos]

Blah. Mythic has got to go back to school if they think they know how to create an epic. I have read epics, in fact I was reading an epic most of the time I was doing the new epic that Mythic contrived... that epic? Lord of the Rings... Mythic take notes... Lord of the Rings = Epic... running around on horse for a few hours while reading Lord of the Rings = not an epic, sorry try again. There's enough to do in the game where you won't need to alt-tab my ass.

Ok so knowing they were finally getting the level 40 epics in this week I had set Tuesday as the day that I absolutely had to get to level 42 so that I could train and get the epic at the same time, I got there on Monday. See I hate traveling to jord, it takes too long. 

I realize one of the reasons that Mythic decided to have long travel times is because they created a game that is just way small and the only way to make it seem a little bigger was to make travel times more boring than the average person could stand. 

And I know what everyone is gonna use as argument against this... "With all 3 realms combined it's actually a pretty good sized game" well that's bull. By splitting up this game into 3 realms they have essentially made 3 games, and I commend them for that because it offers a bit of replay ability. But if I can't go there, it ain't part of the game, plain and simple. And to cover my basis, it's not a matter of level in that I could eventually get there, I will probably never get into the albion or hib gates unless I actually play those realms, or exploit a bug.

At any rate, I got 42 on Monday and waited in Jord till Teusday to get my new "epic". I go to the person who gives out the quest, they tell me i need to go kill a timber cat in Skona Ravine and return it to vindsaul faste to someone there. Well ok, I hop on a horse start reading my book... get there... bind... start running towards where timber cats are... read book... kill the low blue timber cat... run back to stable... read book... hop on horse to Vindsaul... read book... get to Vindsaul find the lady and give her the timber cat prize.

Well she wants a Yeti fur before I can get the book that the original person asked for. That's not too big a deal, this is a realm gate after all the Yeti aren't too far away. So I run a few yards kill another low blue Yeti and bring it back to the lady, get the book, yeah hop on horse back to Mularn, read book.

I get back to the enchanter who wants the book, very grateful get a small amount of xp and she tells me in order to make my magic item that she needs 3 more items and OMG I have to go all the way back to Skona into Varulv to get a werewolf lord pelt. ARGH! I just came from there why didn't you have me get that before you #*$&(#$&!

<sigh> Well I hop on horse and instead of reading I spend two minutes talkin to a few people to get directions to Varulv, since Mythic never bothered to give the dungeon items I never bothered to go there before. Ok directions got... read book. Get there.... run in general direction of varulv... read book... Get to Varulv and start runnin around looking for a Lord. Nothing aggros and it's all yellow at best, the Lord is *Gasp* a low yellow. Man this epic is really getting difficult. Kill it quickly, run back to stable, read book, hop on horse back to mularn, read book.

Ok now I Have to get a Taiga cat and a wyvern from Uppland. Well that's not too bad I suppose, they both in same zone and I don't have to come back to the enchanter in between.... head to uppland... read book... get to uppland and I realize I don't know where any normal wyverns are, ask a few people and a after awhile someone gives me some pretty good directions to one. Go to the wyverns, kill another low blue head to taiga cats, kill another low blue, head back to enchanter, read book...

Ok so I Finally got all this crap give it to the enchanter and I get a choice between 1h & 2h weapon, and another choice between sword, hammer and axe. I choose the 1h hammer because that's what I use the most. It's actually not too bad, it cons yellow has 100 qual and 20 bonus which actually make it a tad better than the clay hammer which had previously been the best 1h in game. It comes with +4 hamemr, +5 str, and +5 quickness (think those are the nmubers) which are also a little better. However the clay hammer still cons orange so I am guessing that I will be using the clay hammer soon anyway just cause it cons higher.

So the prize isn't too bad, but is it worth the 2 or 3 hours it takes to ride a horse? I don't know, think it's gonna come down to each person and what you get for the 50 epic, if the 50 epic is anything like the 40 epic is, it very well might turn out that no one does epics after say 25 or 30 because they just aren't worth the complete boredom of doing them.

Well ok, the quests in this game certainly aren't epic, but to Mythic's credit no MMORPG has really figured out how to do a quest in a multi-player environment. In my opinion the best one thus far is AC, but even that game really hasn't totally got it. So while yes the quests suck in this game, they really aren't much worse than any other game, maybe the boringness of them is just amplified by the incredibly slow travel times.

hmmm... maybe Mythic would consider increasing horse speed or having a faster horse option on some routes that cost 5 gold? Bah that would assume that developers made sense sometimes.
