 - 11/20/01 - Putting on the Brackets
Guilds I think is one of the more personal choices you make in an MMORPG game. By joining a guild, you can literally be choosing who your friends are, and you're enemies are. It decides whether you are respected, or revered. Unfortunately, making this choice almost feels forced because EVERYONE does it.

Are You in a Guild?

Cast  your vote in the message boards!

 

At 36, I was in the vast minority of people who had chosen a guild.  In fact, most people above 10 had already chosen. Fortunately for me, this is not a new feeling. In AC, I was without a monarch for over a year and a half of the time I was there. Something very unheard of in a game where getting patrons and vassals meant free xp and loot.

So I finally did choose a guild at level 36, not because I felt like I was forced into it, and not to get free stuff or powerleveling. I did it for the right reason, the people.

I had met alot of the people who were going to be in the guild in a random grouping back when I was still around 25, and have had many conversations with one of the "leaders" many times since then, not only about the guild, but also other stuff that had nothing to do with it.

Well they finally got off their lazy butts and actually formed the guild a week or two ago, and I decided that it had what I was looking for in a guild. No rules, no requirements, no recruiting, no ranks, no egos, no politics, just friends having a good time. Which really is all it was ever meant to be in the first place. I also liked the name, Requiem, and the fact that the guild had no affiliation with any previous game, it was made for this game and this one alone.

My joining this guild I'm sure came at a bit of a surprise and disappointment to a couple of my friends who had made their own guild, Rixor and Fendain. They tried to recruit me pretty well and I did feel bad about not joining them, but it just didn't feel right to me. Towards the end I found out they had some minor ranks to which I didn't like, and I also didn't like their name or it's affiliation with EQ. It just wasn't really for me.

Such a decision in which you have friends in more than one guild all of which are trying to get you in theirs is a bit difficult cause you don't wanna hurt anyone's feelings in why you chose X guild over Y. But like I said, it doesn't make any guild better than the other, it's a choice that each individual has to make and has nothing to do with any guild really.

So what now that I've officially "sold out" and joined a guild? I don't know, I'm still new to the guild so I don't know everyone real well but I'm also the highest level in the guild, now at 39.

I made it a point to the guy who recruited me that I didn't want anything expected  of me from the guild in any way, and thus far it hasn't. But you do feel the need to do things for everyone. I wanna be able to play with everyone because they are cool guys, and that requires me powerleveling them. The timing for powerleveling hasn't really been there as of yet, but I'm sure it will happen soon enough.

For the most part though. I remain doing what I've always done. I group with whomever is available wherever I am, no matter what guild I'm in. I feel like I know most people above 35 in my realm because of this practice at least a little bit. And isn't that the whole point of MMORPGs in the first place? Guilds are for your closest friends and their closest friends to have a good time together.

And in the grand scope of things, I feel my solo attitude even in a guild will help me alot. I've been in groups with alot of guild's other people whom leave because some of their other guildmates come on or refuse to join a group if their guildmates can't come too. While I find this trait admirable relying on a guild so intensely isn't good when we have to go fight a big raid in RvR and you don't know anything about anyone you are fighting with but your own guild. 

I think it drastically helps a realm for everyone to know and have played with everyone else so they know who they can count on and what someone is like when the big events happen. And while it seems obvious that forts can be taken with only a dozen or two high level people there will always be bigger raids I think. I still don't know how hard relic forts are and who knows maybe Mythic will put in a super fort that is extremely hard to get that only big groups can take. As a realm we are better if we know each other.
