3) Chat still not intuitive. Other games have all had better chat systems and so far AC2 doesn't take much a step forward in that department. There are 4 chat windows and allegiance chat (which isn't in yet) which are both major improvements, but we still lack a yell command, hitting the R key to get a full reply printed out in the text box (meaning when you hit R it'll put /tell Razak, automatically), area/city chat, or trade chat room. I could  do without the trade chat but I think they definitely need the rest to put em on par.
       4) Lack of headgear. I understand Turbine's reasoning behind this. In Ac1 almost everyone wore a Heaume, the only thing that changed was color. This also happened in DAoC. Letting other players see your face does make everyone look a lot less clone-like. However, I do wish that Turbine would include headgear that had no spells and no AL, the only purpose it would serve would be to decorate yourself.
       5) The Map. While it is a much better map than AC1, it also sort of takes away from the game to know where the major dungeons, vaults, cities, portals, and mines are. I don't mind some of the more major ones in but I hope there is a lot more that isn't there. Kind of on a similar subject I don't like how perfect roads go pretty much anywhere you want. If there is a vault or mine or quest shrine in an area, there is a road to it. All this just takes away from the exploration aspect of it, I thought this was a world in ruins, not a well known world where everyone knows where everything is? I would much rather have a map feature where you could input your own places in it thus you'd have to visit it first....
       6) Lack of Alchemy and Cooking. Alchemy I see a need for. There are potions in the game, tho not as useful as in AC1, they are still awfully useful, but there is no way to make them, you have to find them. As for Cooking, it would be nice if Turbine could find a way to make it a "fun" skill, meaning that it would have little to no use in the actual game, but would be fun to play around with. I mean heck, why not, Trade skills are free to train anyway.
