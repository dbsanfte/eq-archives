- 07/17/01 - Flaming Frustration

Well this week we did the second quest day and we had chosen to take on the fire stone quest. Now it should have been a hint that things wouldn't go our way from the beginning when Neowolf went MIA, Teuf forgot about it, Mythras had visitors to entertain, and Greystar had to work. This left us with only 4 people to do it: Stranger II, Kohlia, WGW Blayze and me.

We decide to do it anyway because Stranger is at 67 and I'm at 58 and we figure that it shouldn't be too difficult given that we are all sporting nifty frosty swords. So I log in with Kazar and open new sub to get us started.

We take the Neydisa Castle portal and run to crater where we commence having a little bit of difficulty figuring out whether the vault is inside or outside the crater rim because none of us are all too familiar with the quest. Unfortunately in this whole deal Blayze ends up dieing and I have to login with Kaz and summon new sub again.

Ok no big deal we decide to start over basically and finally get on track moving from the v slit in the rim around to the other side on the outside and we finally find Jahannan Vault at 66.9N, 15.0E.

We all get in the portal and buff  up in the entry room and continue down the corridor. Take the first left and jump down into the first pit. Killing off Zefirs and Mountain rats along the way.

We go down the small stairs in the main room and go in a tunnel that goes under the pathway above and there is a corridor with a steep ramp going up. There are a few flammas and more rats up here and we take a couple of lefts following the paths.

Here is where the dungeon gets difficult. You see this dungeon is loaded with a bunch of difficult jumps, and as we all know jumping with precision is very difficult in this game. 

There are jumps with chains blocking ya, backward jumps, semi-far jumps, and pit jumps to do. There was alot of starting over and because of missed jumps both Blayze and Kohlia died again, both of whom decided to leave after their deaths.

Finally we start to get the hang of the various jumps and we are able to get to the final pit jump. Now it is only Stranger and I but after 2 hours of doing jumps over and over again we are determined to get this frickin fire stone! So we buff up and make the big plunge.

The pit takes about 40 life away and then 2 zefirs charge us which are quickly dispatched. We heal and head into the next room filled with flammas, zefirs, and an inferno blocking the fire stone.

Stranger takes out the flammas and I take out the zefirs and we both meet at the inferno which is quite tough I might add. After a couple of minutes we take down the behemoth fire elemental and I scoop up the fire stone. Like the lightning stone, it is on a 30 second timer so Stranger picks up his stone shortly thereafter.

Now we plan to go back when Teuf and Kohlia are on together and have time to do it and hopefully we can Blayze to go too. I think we'll have a little better luck now that we all have a feel for the place. 

This quest really isn't that difficult critter-wise it's just frustrating with all the jumps. The more jumps ya miss, the more you get frustrated and the more you get frustrated the more ya miss. But if ya figure out how exactly to tackle the jumps it isn't really that hard, sure you will miss some but you have to keep a clear head or you'll end up missin more.

Stats

Woot, I can now activate the Quid Orb! The Quid Orb is awesome, casts Item 5, Life 6 and MC6. This makes it so I no longer have to switch orbs while casting plust before I could only get up to Life5 and MC5 so it gives me a bonus there. As you can tell Magic Defense and Mana Conversion continue to be my emphasis. In the future I am going to cut down MC a bit due to the Quid Orb but I think a high Magic Defense will help me in the future... especially without healing skill.

I am so close to level 60 that I can smell it. I am going to stop putting points into stats and skills till 60 because I will be getting Creature then and would like to get it up to where Item and Life are so that by 65 I will be casting all 6s.
