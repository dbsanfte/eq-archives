- 08/23/01 - Lag of Death
 
Well I wish I had alot of in game stuff to talk about this week, but unfortunately I ran into a problem on this end. Ya see, in order to talk about something in game, you must first have the ability to play in the actual game. Silly concept, I know.

Poll

How aggravating is the lag that was added during the August Patch?

Find the results of this poll and others in the Polls section.
 

With August came what really was the Virindi's secret weapon. They have learned the complex art of creating insane amounts of lag, client instability, and server instability and coupled that knowledge with the ability to keep us from even logging on into the Zone.

I gotta say, this was one of the dumbest ideas for a story-line I've ever seen and have no idea what Turbine was thinking. Did they take a look at AO and go "Well they seem to have gotten a lot of customers by not letting em play, lets try that and see if it works for us too!"

I heard there has been some interesting new quests added to the game.

Like a quest in the capital towns which involves low levels, mid-levels, and semi-high levels working together for about 12 hours to complete a quest for absolutely no reward. And I still haven't even heard if there is any new dungeon that spawns because of it, as far as I know it just allows you to start over.

They added some new mace/staff/spear love with the banners and the assault weapons. Not a bad attempt. The banners I think will mainly be a new way to pump up mules xp as some of the better ones give out 35k and unlike the triangles are actually droppable (not 100% on that one so don't sue me). I don't know how good the spears and such actually are because I've never played one of those weapon types, but from what I've heard there isn't anything special about them.

The Assault Weapons are actually kind of a nice try at getting a little bit of love to those who have been receiving very little love in the past year or two, mainly mace, axe, staff, and spear. The weapons are exremely huge variable damage weapons with really sweet graphics. Just as an example the mace does 2 to 24 damage

It is obvious that Turbine decided to give a high max damage at the price of a low minimum damage a shot. This is well known not to work and actually when the game first started the variance on the weapons used to be different and they decided to close the gap a little, this also was why Gerty's Dagger was so popular with a 1 point variance. The problem is emphasized by striking for 90 damage one time and 2 the next blow giving you an average of 46 which I doubt would be any better than you would with a normal weapon.

About the only real useful new feature added to this patch was the @corpse command, which really was supposed to be in last month. This allows you to find your coords to your corpse in case you get knocked offline or it's just off the text screen. Now you can find your corpse no matter what.

But the real problem remains to be lag and instability. This is a problem that must be fixed. While I did need a break from the game, a forced one because Turbine did something to totally screw up the servers is never how you should get it done.

Hell, as it is I only have a few hours a day now that I can play. From about 6am to 12pm CST, if that. Anything more than five or six hundred people and the game becomes unplayable and I am on a cable modem! If I decide to sleep, eat breakfast, take a shower, watch a little TV, or have to do errands, an entire day is shot.

I realize that I am not the only one who has ranted about this subject, nor will I be the last. And I also realize that Turbine has set up today as a day to patch this patch in hopes that it will fix the extreme lag and instability problem so this could all be moot. They also have a back-up plan in place in case it doesn't work, which is likely to take out the code that they feel is so vital to update.

All in all, I hope they fix it, but I'll believe it when I see it. They have tried to fix Gear for 4 months now and the best they could do is make the game unplayable to do so. I don't like Gear and wish they could find a solution to it, but I'm getting to the point where I'd rather just have Gear usable and have a playable game and have them put the guys back on making more content than trying to fix it. My patience is running out.

Statage

Due to the severe lag problems and the lack of playtime that I've been able to get this past week I have decided not to put any stats up as they haven't changed a great deal.
