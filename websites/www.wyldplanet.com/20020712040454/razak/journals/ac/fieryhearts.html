- 08/28/01 - Fiery Hearts

The latest hotfix has helped game play a bit. It still isn't to a totally tolerable place as of yet but it is alot more bearable.

With this in mind Kohlia, Teufelhund, and I had planned on finishing up the fire stone quest that we had started more than a month ago. Unfortunately Teuf quickly bowed out due to connectivity problems, leaving only Kohlia and I. We decided to go on without him and get the fire stone totally completed.

The way through Jahannan Vault was much more simple now that we had a better idea of what to do. It really is amazing what a difference just going through once can do for future quests. I only recall 2 times where we had any major jumping problems, one for each of us, unlike last time where it seemed to be more of a constant problem.

It probably only took 30 to 45 minutes to complete this time. I actually had planned on going to do the frost stone right afterwards, but with the group only consisting of Kohlia and I, and the guy I was counting on to help us through the dungeon, I decided to hold off on it till we can get a bigger group.

After finishing the fire stone, and a little bit of chatting, I decided to go out in search of wood hearts to give to my new fletching mule to aid his leveling. Remembering my times with my former character when I was exploring that the forests in northern Osteth seemed to have alot of Wood Golems, I hopped on the subway to Holtburg and headed north.

On my jog north, I noticed one thing more than anything else, Tumeroks. They were everywhere! For the first half hour I went around fighting some new Tumeroks I saw, mainly sticking to the Standard Bearers, Majors, Lts., Commanders and Controllers. I was rewarded with 2 Falcon Banners. After going to Maggie's site to find out what they did, I quickly threw them on a local town cryer when I had time because they were only worth 1000 xp and I didn't have the patience to go around trying to get the other components just for 2000 more.

I started getting annoyed with the Tumeroks so I quit hutning them and decided that this patch just made Tumeroks more annoying than they already were. Maybe the gromnie and maks ones are more worth the trouble that it takes. But considering the low drop rate of the components to make the completed banners, I doubt it.

I don't know if it was bad luck or bad timing or what though, but the amount of Wood Golems in the area seemed lower than I remembered. It seemed to me that Obsidian Golems were much easier to find the Wood Golems which seems wrong considering that Obsidian keys are worth 30k xp and Wood keys are only worth 2k xp.

Looking for Wood Golems the whole way I traveled north, then east, then south going all the way around the mountains back to arwic on. Basically I did a gigantic circle. Along the way I only got 11 wood hearts, not too bad really but not great either.

Once back in Arwic, I hitched another ride to Holtburg and dropped off the hearts to my fletching/lockpick mule at the casino. He then went through the various portals and found himself in samsur where he bought an intricate carving tool and headed off to the Mnemosyne Collection Site.

I gave the undead a few tiny triangles and pumped my lockpicking skill up, then cast focus, coordination and lockpick mastery and carved the hearts into keys. I had about a 130 lockpick pumped and was successful on all 11. I unlocked 11 tinies and gave it to the undead. All in all the tinies that he got from the casino got him from late 11 almost to 13.

Statage

It's been a couple of weeks since I have posted my stats but they really haven't changed all that much. This is for two reasons, because of last week's lag and because it's getting harder and harder to gain levels and up stats & skills.

I did however pass a few landmarks this week.

I hit the dreaded 1 mil to raise stats. I think this is the first real landmark in raising since 100,000 xp per point, if not even all the way back to 10,000 (that 10k seems like alot when you are small). Considering that Kazar was almost at 1mil for strength at level 55, I think I am doing well that I am just now getting it, I also feel well-rounded in doing it. I am however thinking that I will back off a little bit on raising Self, it is the least useful skill. I will still be raising it, but probably only half as much as the other stats.

My other landmark this week was a natural 300 in Sword. I now buff to 355 so I'm doin well here I think. It's too bad that we don't get the advantage that UA gets in that you do more damage with a higher skill. Unfortunately, the only thing you get for having a higher skill is the ability to hit & crit more. Oh well, I not complaining a whole lot=P
