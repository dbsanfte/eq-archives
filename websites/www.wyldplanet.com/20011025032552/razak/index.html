 - 10/23/01 - New Journal & Poll

Building the Night Away has been released. This week I built a character to be an armor crafter and share with you my initial thoughts on trade skills in DAoC. Here's your blurb:

With the rather large cost of playing in this game I thought it might be a good idea to start on a trade. Now you have to train in one of four trades: weaponsmith, armorcraft, tayloring, and fletching. The only hitch is you can't un-train a trade as of yet so I decided to start a whole new char because they are talking of adding new tradeskills in the future and I wanna make sure I get the right one on my permanent char.

I've also added a new poll to the message boards asking: What trade skill will you trade in? Make sure to stop by the boards and cast your vote or just leave an opinion or two on the subect.

- 10/16/01 - New Journal

I have released my first journal for DAoC entitled I'm a Newb! This journal takes a more review feel too it as I try to explain the basics of the game to those who don't have it yet and also try to play catch up to where I am because those first few levels go by so fast. Here's your snip:

One of the hardest things to do, from my perspective, is to shake off old habits and old stereotypes from past games.  There are alot of EQish things in this game so for an AC player, I have to put it in my mind that this is not EQ and look past those things. There are also alot of EQ players so there is alot of EQ lingo that I don't particularly understand, like why the hell are new critters attacking called an add? Got me, seems lame.

I'd also like to update everyone on what to expect in the future from this site. I am moving the journal back to it's original Tuesday slot, so make sure to come back every Tuesday instead of Thursday for a new journal. I will also be making a few cosmetic changes and also be shifting the site to DAoC instead of it's current AC slant. I do plan on keeping the AC info and journals I have up, and will probably be keeping an AC section for this purpose.
