- 05/28/01 - In Search of the Sparking Stone

A few weeks ago, me and a few friends decided that the best way for us to go about doing some big quests was to set a time in advance so that we could all be on at the same time, having set up ample time to go quest. After much deliberation and organizational skills we had our time and decided to start off with the Sparking Stone.

I had decided to find a tie the day before so that the process of getting to the Amperehelion Vault would be as easy as possible. But fate didn't side with me. Most of the day the servers were having hissy fits and I was unable to get in and then once they calmed down I no longer had a subscription!

Well, hoping it was a technical glitch I stayed offline for the rest of the day and checked in the next. Still had the same problems so I decided to subscribe again. That done I went in and found a portal that dropped off near Black Dominion. (49S, 66.4W) This is the dungeon that I found to be closest to Amperehelion Vault via AC Explorer. I tied and went back to our meeting place.

After getting together, doing a little shopping, and waiting for a late arrival. We all finally got grouped together and opened a portal to Black Dominion. We then pumped ourselves with Armor and a couple of prots for the journey through the dires and then preceeded to leave the dungeon and head southwest for Amperhelion Vault.

We didn't really have much trouble getting there, there were Virindi, Bandey Strikers, and Tuskers mostly. The usual dires fare. We got there  in about 5 or 10 minutes.

The Amperehelion Vault (56.5S, 72.6W) is just on the inside of the rim of the obsidian plains. It does take a little maneuvering to get there, you either have to go south a little or you jump a cliff to get down. The actual portal is at the base of a big Windmill which is surrounded by a good 20 to 30 nasties, I didn't stick around much to find out exactly what is there but there is a few diamond golems.

Luckily the entrance to the vault is empty so you can run straight in and buff, and let your vulns run out. I buffed my sword with 5s, also added Armor 5, Lightning Prot 5, Rejuv 5, and Regen 4, and then went on to cast Lightning Prot 5 on my friends.

There is alot of lightning elementals near the bottom and ash gromnies near the top, hence the lightning prot. I can't give you really detailed info on how to get through, there are alot of twists and turns. But you do usually find your way down because if you take a wrong turn, you just end up back where you took the wrong turn and make the right one instead.

We made it down fine, though we lost one to internet problems along the way and all got our Sparking Stones shortly. The stone itself is on a 25 second timer so if you are going with a large group you will probably never see more than 2 spawns before everyone has one for themselves.

We did go back for the last person as well. We met up with a few more hitches our second time out. We all got lagged out on top of a diamond golem and 4 of us died. We did manage to get our bodies back, and I would like to thank Garr for taking time out and trying to help out even though we ended up getting them without his help, it was cool anyway.

We finally did manage to get through the vault again and get the last person their stone and then we all parted ways with our lightning atlans in hand. I personally had a lot of fun and would like to thank Stranger II, Mythras, Kohlia, Teufelhund, and Neowolf for coming with. Is always great fun to do these quests in a larger groups like this.

As you can see I died a few more times. I have started to care a little less about dieing and have been trying to help friends out a little more, of course I find that I forget about myself sometimes. I continue to work mainly on health, stamina, melee defense, and sword, but my little romp through the dires recently has made me consider raising magic skills more, including magic defense. We'll see.
