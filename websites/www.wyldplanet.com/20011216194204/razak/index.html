- 11/27/01 - Slight Site Redesign

Due to the slower week this week & not being able to play as much, I have decided to update the website this week and take the week off from journals. Don't worry though, I'll be back next week. Below are some of the changes I have done with the site and explained a few.

First new thing you will notice will be more prominent advertising, like the advertising to the right. I, like everyone else, hates advertising but it's a must to make up at least some of the costs of running this site. So if you are going to be shopping from one of my featured merchants, it would be much appreciated if you went through one of my links.

Another change you will notice is that most of the AC related content on this site has been pushed into a separate area. The content is still available but for the most part has been consolidated to take as little space as possible. 

The last main changes include adding a little more red and the loss of most printer friendly versions of the journals. The red will be there to highlight certain sections and links to make them easier to see. The loss of printer-friendly versions will mainly just be old AC journals, even though I haven't gotten any up yet.

I hope that everyone enjoys and/or understands the changes, if you have any comments or questions feel free to leave them in the message boards.
