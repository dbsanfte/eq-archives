Save Money with the NextCard Visa

These are the various programs that I use in conjunction with AC and highly recommend. To ensure the safety of everyone instead of linking to the download directly or posting them on my site I decided to just have a link to the program's site to ensure that you aren't downloading a virus of some sort.

SaijinSkin Cobalt - Ever since I posted The Big 60 when I changed skins everyone has been emailing me asking me where I got it. This is it. Best skin in game IMO. You do have to replace some graphics in the portal.dat, it is kind of risky. I recommend that you make a backup of it until you are sure you like the skin.

ACExplorer - This is a must have for your mapping needs. Contains pretty much every point of interest, portal, city and vendor in the game. Also helps you calculate routes to get to places and lets you download updates every month to find those new places that were just added in the last patch. I would just download the old version as I don't think it's worth the extras to pay $20.

ACTool - Yes I have been known to macro and this is the program that I use. I don't macro much and usually only to raise alchemy and cooking skills. I am not one of those freaks who macros because I don't wanna play but I want to have a big char, I just think the current trade skills rather suck and is way too repetitive for me to do myself.

Decal - Decal is a program that allows other people to develop third-party programs that can be used inside AC.

Sixth Sense -  Sixth Sense is a plug-in for Decal that will allow you to see various items, monsters, players and corpses without hitting ] or needing the radar, these have become almost as must have as splitpea/beans is.

Kebar's Toolset - This decal plug-in does a number of useful things. It recasts spells that fizzle or low on mana, it calculates how much xp you are making per hour/minute/total, it logs critter hits and sends em to The Monster Attack Project, it handles AFK messages and logs any messages you get while AFK, and it has an in-game clock so you know what time it is.

Arcane Knowledge - This is sort of a better version of Spiltbeans if you ask me. It not only does what decal does, but does it in game and also gives you a button to push that will automatically put all components in place to learn the spell in question. Same problems as Spiltbeans in that if you get from wrong place, you could get a trojan so be careful.
