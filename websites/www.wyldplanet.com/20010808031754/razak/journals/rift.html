 - 07/31/01 - After the Rift
 
July brought about a very good patch IMO. And while I haven't even attempted the new island of Singularity Caul, I have gotten to experience quite a few of the new features.

Poll

What did you think about the July patch?

Vote for what you think and leave your thoughts on this month's patch as well as this journal here.
 

To me, the best new addition to the game wasn't the new island and didn't even happen until a week after the patch was released. It happened when a Shallow Rift opened up near Rithwic and some adventurers mindlessly killed Dirrich, the Warden without thinking of the consequences.

This single act totally changed the Obsidian Plains. Now there are more spawns than before as well as new critters in the form of Virindi Directors, Virindi Executors, Dark Marionettes, and Augmented Drudges.

 

The plains have become heaven to hunt, making BSD almost totally laughable. Tuskers are all over the place making the xp you get from hunting above grounds well worth the trouble.

And I have totally fallen in love with a drudge named Augment. Augmented Drudges are great. They offer 2k more xp than Altered Drudges with about the same loot, though a little more sporadic. They use fire instead of lightning but don't use missile weapons making it extremely easy to drag them out of their little groups. The only real negative they have is that they do cast Firebolt 6 which, coupled with imp5 and fire vuln 5, can easily do 120 damage in a single shot. They make you want to put more points into magic defense.

Another cool part of the new Obsidian Plains is the new purple Renalim (I think that's what they are called) plants. I am not certain if they appear in random places like the original 3 or not but they do seem to appear at random times and usually are treated like a steel quest. Meaning there is usually a nice sized group of critters surrounding it.

They are really fun to go get for me though. Whenever I see one, I run and grab it and quickly run out again. There have been very few that I won't get and I have never died trying thus far. I have about 65 of them already and am getting close with Zartov to a point where I am willing to dye my main armor. So expect some new colored armor soon!

 

Virindi Raids

A supposed Virindi Raid happened to me while I was buffing before heading out to east dires swamp on saturday. I got the mystical air smelling like ozone followed by a rift killing a couple of creatures nearby.

Knowing that a bunch of hollow minions, virindi, and tuskers are about to come in and rip me a new ass, I quickly gear up and wait. Only nothing spawns.

I decide, well maybe it's nearby and I just heard the message. So I travel around cycling through the monsters on screen. Nothing.

Now I am confused so I think that well maybe I heard it through the portal. Stranger things have happened. So I jump through the portal to east dires swamp and am greeted by a malus shreth. I quickly dispatch it and look around. Nothing.

 

Oh well, guess I'm not meant to get myself a fancy new Quiddity Sword.

Garden Swamp?

Since the patch was released I had started a new fletching mule which I have been hunting with in the Eastham Sewers so I have been killing alot  of Carrion Shreths and thus have been getting a fair amount of little green seeds from them.

I now have nearly 10 so I decide that I should go turn them in and try to get me some cool plants so I bring them over to Razak and hop over to Yanshi and make the run south to where AC Explorer says the Swamp Garden is. Nothing.

Deja Vu? What's up with this? I wonder if AC Explorer is screwed up so I look over on Maggie Jackcat's site to double check the coords. No this is the right spot... no portal. I sit here for a few minutes hoping maybe it spawns at random times but to no avail. I met 4 or 5 other people who were also disappointed with the missing portal while waiting.

Where did the Swamp Garden go and how long ago did it disappear? I heard that it was broken on HG, does this happen on the other servers too? ARGH! HG is always broken!

Stats

Well this is the first stat post since Flaming Frustration 2 weeks ago. And as you can see not a whole lot has changed since then. The xp it took to raise Creature to be equal with Life and Magic was probably a good 1/4 of all the xp I have earned since then, if not more.
