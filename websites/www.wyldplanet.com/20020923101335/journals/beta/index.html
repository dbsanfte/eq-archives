09.01.02 - Beta Edition

       Some of you may have wondered what happened to me and my journals. I thank those who have posted in my old message boards or emailed me. Suffice it to say, I took a bit of a vacation to DAoC and AO and I didn't have anything to really talk about during much of my time there, nor did I have much to talk about when I did finally return to AC.  However, I am in AC2 now. It's a whole new world, kind of, and thus there is plenty to talk about.
       There's been a lot of talk going into AC2 in the AC community. This of course is because we all love the world of AC, however with beta came the realization that this is a drastically different game that is being geared towards a wider audience, and in there lies controversy. People liked there system in AC, they were comfortable with it.
       But I would like to ask everyone to sit back and take a very very deep breath and just relax. 2 months is a long time for changes, a lot of people don't think so for some reason, but believe me it is. It will always be a different game than AC, if they were just going to update AC, they'd just do another expansion pack, this is supposed to be a new game. A lot of what was wrong with AC was with it's system and they did what they needed to in order to fix it, they started over.
       The thing that is most important is that the world is still there. Yes it is vastly different. What did you expect after 300+ years and a cataclysm, the same static world? They've added a bunch more traditional fantasy-style critters to make newbs feel more comfortable, but they do still have a ton of very unique critters so worry not, Turbine is still there...
