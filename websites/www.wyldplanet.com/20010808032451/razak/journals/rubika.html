- 07/02/01 - Razak Goes to Rubi-Ka - printable version
So I got an invitation to be part of Anarchy Online's Beta 4 testing. After 3 days of trying to download their 600MB game, I finally get it and jump in with Razak. Let me first say that this game is REALLY buggy. It brings me back to the days of the last game I beta tested, Ultima Online. A game so horrible by the end of the beta I had declared never again will I give OSI money for their buggy games. Well OSI no longer exists, good I guess. But Funcom seems determined to take their place as the buggy kings. =(

I am only going to talk about the last patch that I played which dramatically changed the game, but I unfortunately only got a couple days testing because they released the game way too early.

This game feels ALOT like Everquest but takes alot of AC and UO's ideas so it's like combining all 3. I also feel that the people playing it will generally be more of the FPS crowd due to the constant whining of a lack of good first person camera and FPS controls.
The game has some really good ideas and because of that I think this game has alot of potential. In fact I had been thinking about getting the game in 3 months once they got all the bugs worked out until AC announced their expansion pack.

The best idea is the Mission Generators. No more walk-thrus for missions and no more competing with people for kills, loot and quest items. This does have it's downsides though. The missions are very simple and rather easy to get through, they get very very boring after about an hour of doing them, they seem to promote anti-social behavior (the true form) because there is no benefit to getting group missions and now you find yourself spending 95% of your time in a dungeon with no one else. It also didn't really stop camping, just changed it. Now people camp the mission generators for the best prizes, this became such a problem that they instituted paying for each mission generated to slow it down which hurts newbs alot more.

Another good idea was how they instituted housing. Everyone gets a free house. It's rather small but you can decorate it, invite friends in and use it as storage. They are all part of a high-rise and you never see every apartment door, every high rise has about 6 doors, you choose one (though several people can have the same one). The downside is that they only have the one size and once you have it you can't get a new one so if you move you are out of luck. (they do say they will implement different sized apartments later as well as guild housing, another sign that they released too early.)

There is alot of variety in this game and the cities are gigantic. I never left the cities much in fact cause everything for the first 10 or 15 levels is right there. There are usually several stores in each city and about 15 high-rises (newbie zones). It is easy to get lost and the maps don't tell ya where stuff is. There are also alot of NPCs running around which I think are all part of mission generators (you have to find em in your mission).

 
The way classes and such are put up are very nice. It's not as restricted as EQ but not as free as AC. You can raise any attribute or skill you want, but how much it costs to raise them varies depending on your race and class. This is  kind of a nice happy medium for everyone involved. To limit what you can do further you can only raise attributes 3 points a level and skills up to 5 points a level, depending on their associated attribs.

Lastly the camera and keyboard control is really crappy. There is no good way to do anything really. In the last patch the camera did get alot better, you could switch it to basically AC's camera with the stiffness bar all the way up, it had absolutely no give to it. They also had a rubber band view with too much give. Camera they really need to work on the most, it is just really annoying to do anything in this game. And they Keyboard ya can't really program to do much with it. there is like a dozen things you can program, 8 things you can't, and then the rest of the keys are totally unused.

Like I said this game has alot of potential. Unfortunately it just sucks right now. Luckily there are a good number of people who are willing to shell out $50 + $13 a month to be beta tester (as in UO.) I am not one of them. I also heard reports that in order to sign up you have to give your credit card online, unsecurely... a big no no as well in my book.

The game has almost everything broken right now (as it were pets made by engineers couldn't cross zones and no trade skills otherwise worked) and it crashes every 10 minutes, not even mentioning the lag. The lag and crashing did get better in the last patch (instead of crashing every 5 you now crash every 10 minutes). 

But Funcom continues to say that their servers are not laggy, they say that it's a client-side thing. This appeases alot of people for some reason. Not me, THEY made they client too! If the client is laggy, it's their fault not mine. It's not this laggy with other games. I have what I consider a pretty average system (Athlon 650 w/ Voodoo 3 and Cable modem) it's not the greatest out there but it isn't as bad as Funcom thinks. If my system is below the minimum they are saying that it's designed for 1ghz, Geforce 3, T3 users only which is not gonna be a great number of people. This kind of ignorance from a company really pisses me off.
Oh well, it's not for me. Alot of people seem happy with it and if it was less buggy, crashy and laggy I would have been happier too. Maybe if Dark Majesty is a joke I will still give it a look but in the meantime I'm less frustrated with AC.
