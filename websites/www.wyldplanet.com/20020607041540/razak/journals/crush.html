- 12/11/01 - The Big Crush

I'm still at 41 and I still have been playing alof Civ3, but something did happen to me recently in DAoC that I thought was worth writing about... About a week ago I had perhaps the most fun thus far in DAoC, and it happened in a RvR setting. Heck in a way I didn't even participate but it was still fun to watch.

I was in a group with Belfin from my guild, and Sojourn from MRA and a few other MRA people that I just don't remember their names. (Sorry) We weren't alone, there were a ton of other people too and we were all attacking anyone who came out of the Albion Portal Gate in Hibernia. Now this is not a strange thing to happen and I think almost happens nightly but this night we have a purpose, and I don't remember what  it is.

Everything was actually going like it always had on the few previous times I had taken part in such a raid. I would step up and Summon Mjollnir on a few souls who got too close and his friends would suffer damage for him getting too close too. Every now and then they would make a rush and I would actually get to use my hammer on someone but for the most part they just kept sending out their stupid little pets. 

Their pets don't really do anything but annoy the crap out of me, they don't do much damage and they easier than hell to kill, but you do have to kill em because anything can kill ya if you just ignore them when they damaging ya.

What really hurt us was our own people. Sometimes our people were just getting too close and they started pulling guards because of it. I think some of the time people were just running in to commit suicide cause they were logging for the night and forgot to disband from their group. If you are in a group and you do this the guard will try to kill every single one of your group until they are all dead, and they have a pretty long follow range too.

Well one particular time more than one guard had come out and my group had gotten a little tired of it so we ran back to the albion milegate to let everyone take care of it and give us a little break. We were talking and dancing and whatever when all of a sudden about 30 to 40 Hibs come from the other side of the alb mile gate and wipe us all out. Well all except Sojourn, the fabulous skald who sprints back to the main group and tells them hibs are at the mile gate.

Soon enough our raiding party returns to the mile gate and I find myself being enveloped in Mjollnirs because hibs are pretty much standing on top of my body. I must say it was pretty weird being on the receiving end of this spell for a change.

Our raiding party was gaining ground, but then a problem occurred. The albions decided to follow us out and take us out from behind. We were now fighting a battle on two sides. I'm not sure if this was the case becuase I could only see what was going on from one side but I believe we concentrated on the Hibs, while a few groups stayed back to occupy the Albs.

Soon enough the Hib raiding party was dispatched and our full force turned around on the albions and quickly wiped them out as well. All the while I was dead on the ground watching as thought it were some riveting episode of ER.

It was hugely exciting and once the dust settled the area around the milegate was littered with dead bodies. I don't know how many of them there were but it seemed like there were at least 100. They didn't last too long though because we were res'd shortly and most of the other realm players realized they wouldn't see a res incoming anytime soon and released.

The whole night was a huge success in my mind. I got about 300 realm points, but to show you how little I actually do any RvR action, it came pretty close to tripling what I had. With the slowdown of leveling I wouldn't be surprised if I found myself doing more RvR in the coming weeks.
