- 07/24/01 - The Big 60

As the title of this journal clearly states, I finally hit 60!!!

Alot of people congratulated me for 50 alot more than most levels and I must admit to the majority of people 50 is a big landmark. But to me 60 is a big landmark and 50 is nothing.

 You see people all the time hit 50 but 60+ is more rare. I think it's when a player finally starts getting a little respect. Heck already I've had a bunch of newbs askin me for free stuff because I'm big and they aren't.

Early last week I started to feel the draw of getting to 60. I had gotten close to halfway to the level and decided to stop spending my xp and saving them up for the big level.

Once that happened I started realizing that I was getting close to getting creature magic and my wanting of it increased so I started throwing in more time. Usually I only spend a few hours every morning online and take off the rest of the day but now I was burning... I spent almost 2 entire days (took off for the nights) online in order to get to 60.

Finally on Thursday, I got it. I had amassed about 5.5 million xp and I immediately trained creature magic and brought it up to just 2 points below Life and Item. A short time after that I got the 2 points to make them all equal.

All this done, the next step is learning useful spells. I learned all 5s in Sword Mastery, Magic Resistance, Coord, Strength, Endurance, and Quickness. I didn't find a need to learn Invuln because I have it in level 6 on my shield.

That all taken care of, I felt my next step was to re-arrange my spell tabs. They were a complete mess before because they were mainly made up on the fly which left them entirely random in how they were sorted. You get used to this if you do it that way but it's a whole lot easier if you throw logic into it.

So I put Life magic in tab 1, Creature in tab 2, Item in tab 3, fighting spells in tab 4, and portals & true value in tab 5. In all of them I put Stam to Mana 5 in slot one and Stam to mana 4 in slot 9. I also arranged them according to what order I felt I should cast them based on durations and getting the most xp out of casting.

I soon found out that my decision to put Creature in tab 2 was a bit of a mistake because creature spells last on average 3 minutes longer than life spells making them the first spells that I cast. I also only cast Sword Mastery, Magic Resistance, and Strength out of creature because I dont' really wanna deal with stam to mana usage too much, though I left them in the tab just in case.

I have immediately noticed what the extra strength and sword skill do for me. I think I do about 4 more damage to hit and I hit alot more frequently with them. Buffed my sword skill is now close to 350 and I feel like I'm cutting through butter with a steak knife with tuskers alot of the time.

Needless to say that by saving my xp pretty much since my last journal and putting them all into creature, my stats haven't really changed. So instead of doing my usual rundown of stats I have included a before and after stats of Creature and the unnassigned xp.
