A force is spreading throughout Norrath, enveloping and
taking over the desolate countryside. No vile creature is
safe when the feared Survivors invade even the deepest,
darkest fortress.
The Survivors guild exists on the Tholuxe Paells server.
Members assist each other generously but not blindly.
Everycreature must forge his own destiny in the realm's
history.
Originally called the Minions of Doom, the group was
reformed when its leader was overthrown in a rousing
display of tyranny by the adventurers who call the guild
home. Since that time, several changes of leadership have
taken place. Today the guild is led into weekly raids by
the diplomatic and feared necromancer Valkerine, with
help from the various officers and members that
make up this powerful force called:
Survivors of the Minion
Our Fearless Leader
