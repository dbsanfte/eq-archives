Membership Requirements and Guild Rules






1. Must be at least level 30+ or an alt of a current member.

2. Must be willing to follow the orders of officers as needed.

3. Must have interest in raids and participate in them on occasion.

4. Potential members will be subject to a 30 day probationary period. At the end of that time, if there are no objections, they will become full members.

5. Nobody is allowed to use anonymous while playing

6. Only Druids, Enchanters, Shaman, Wizzards, and Clerics can use Roleplay.

7. During raids though, nobody is allowed to roleplay.

8. To be promoted to officer, you must:
A) participate in guild functions
B) Follow ALL guild rules
C) Be registered at Guildmagic.com
