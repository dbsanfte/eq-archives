Rules for all guild raids


We will no longer roll on any items on any of Sotm's raid. All items will be voted by the Loot Council.

Items that are dropped on a raid will be awarded by Loot Council to the appropriate class based on a “Best for the Guild” need before greed concept.

BEST FOR THE GUILD
This policy is created to both reward members for longevity and activity in the guild. We strive to all work together to do bigger and better things and the only way we can all ensure this is by having a guild that works together as a team. We feel it is important to not just be in the guild but to take an active roll. So for this reason we have created a policy that works towards both of these goals. It is best for the guild to reward players that have gained sufficient levels to allow guild raids to happen, as well as reward players for loyalty and time spent helping the guild progress as a whole. All of these factors are considered when awarding drops to raid participants.

GUESTS ON RAIDS
The officers MUST first approve any guest. There may be times when in order to even do the raid we have to invite people of different guilds (or friends of Sotm) to fill these gaps in order for the raid to happen. In this case we will treat the guest with the same considerations as noted above, how often they assist Sotm in raids, current equipment and character level. This applies to any weapons or armor drops other than Robes Breastplates and quest items.

Quest drops as well as planar BP’s and Robes will always go to guild members first even if that guild member has received a prior item and guest has not. If there is no guild member present from the class or guild member already has the BP/Robe/Quest item it will be given to the guest that can best use the BP/Robe/quest drop based on above loot policy.

What is a Loot Council?

The Loot council will be accompanied by Leader's - Raid Leader's and Grp Leaders. Each raid we host will NOT consist of the same Loot Council Members. All item's will be voted by the LC Members on who gets the loots.

How will the items be distributed?
All "No Drop" items will be announced in Loot Council Channel by the Grp leader. The Loots will than be announced in raid channel by Loot announcer. By the following - slot/class/race/ and stats if possible. He or she will say, "WHO NEEDS PST NOW!" You will need to send a tell to let that person know that you need it. Once the announcer get all the names that was sent. He or She will list the names of the players in Loot Council. That is when all the LC members will need to vote on who will recieve the item(s).

What about tradeable items?
All tradeable items will "NOT" be announced but will be distributed at the end of the raid by the LC Members.

What do I do with all the money and Gems i have collected at the raid?
All money and gems are guild property. All money and gems will be handed to the Loot Announcer at the end of the raid.

Do I bring my own Peridots and spell Components?
You "WILL NEED" to bring your own Peridots and spell Components. All Peridots and spell Components used will than be refunded by the SotM at the end of the raid.

Who do I contact to get my refund?
The Loot Announcer will be in charge of refunds.


IN CLOSING
We pride ourselves on our need before greed values as a guild. We know that while any loot policy will not be liked by all we feel this is the most fair to individuals as well as helping the guild advance in their goals. We are in this together to help each other strive and grow and by working together and helping one another one day at a time we shall meet our goals as friends. The true way to succeed as a guild is to always support each other and work together. While we may not be the fastest to get there we will find the victories all the more sweet working together as a family to achieve them.

As always please be on time (early if possible) for all raids, and sign up on our message board to guarentee a spot for you. Late comers may or may not be able to attend.
