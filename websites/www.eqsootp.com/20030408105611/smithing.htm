Smithing

Miscellaneous Blacksmithing Recipes

  • Block of Brellium = 3 Large Bricks of Brellium + Dwarven Ale
  • Bread Tin = Metal bit + ceramic lining + bread tin mold + Water Flask
  • Cake Round = Metal bits + Cake Round Mold + Ceramic Lining + Flask of Water
  • Chain Jointing = Metal Rings + file + smithy hammer + water (Chain Jointings are used in making Field Plate and Full Plate)
  • File = Metal bit + File mold + Flask of water
  • Folded Medium Quality Metal Sheets = Block of Medium Quality Ore +  water + Smithy Hammer
  • Folded Sheet of Metal = Block of ore + Flask of water + smithy hammer
  • Folded Titanium Sheet = Block of Titanium + Flask of Blood Water + Forging Hammer
  • Lantern = Metal bit + Lantern casting mold + Bottle + Flask of water
  • Lockpicks = Metal bits + Lockpick mold + Flask of water
  • Medium Quality Block of Ore = 3 Large Bricks of Medium Quality Ore + Water  (may be barbarian only)
  • Metal Bits = 2 Small pieces of ore + Flask of water
  • Metal Containers = Metal bit + Hinge mold + Appropriate Container Mold (s,m,l) + Appropriate Container Lid Mold (s.m.l) + Flask of Water
  • Metal Rings = Large Brick of Ore + File + Flask of Water
  • Muffin Tin = Metal bit + Muffin Tin Mold + Ceramic Lining + Flask of Water
  • Pie Tin = Metal bits + Pie Tin mold + Ceramic Lining + Flask of Water
  • Pot = Metal bit + Pot Mold + Standing Legs mold + Flask of Water
  • Scaler = Metal bit + Scaler mold + Flask of water
  • Sewing Kit = Metal bit + Needle Mold + Thimble Mold + Flask of Water
  • Shaped Cookie Cutter = Appropriately Shaped Mold (barbarian, etc) + Metal bit + Flask of Water
  • Sheet Metal = 2 Small Bricks of ore + Flask of water
  • Skewers =  Metal bit + Skewer mold + Flask of water
  • Smoker = Metal bits + Skewer + Smoker base mold + Smoker support mold + Flask of Water
  • Steel Boning = Small Brick of ore + File + Flask of water
  • Studs = 3 Metal bits + File + Flask of water
  • Titanium Scales = File + Large Brick of Titanium + Flask of Blood Water
  • Tool Box = Metal bit + Flask of Water (no longer profitable)

Blackmithing Weapon Recipes

  • Field Arrowheads = metal bits + water + file
  • Forged Bastard Sword = Dual-Edged Blade Mold + Hilt Mold + Pommel Mold + Metal Sheet + Water Flask
  • Forged Battle Axe = water + hilt mold + axe head mold + metal sheet
  • Forged Dagger = water + metal sheet + hilt mold + pommel mold + dagger blade mold
  • Forged Halberd = Halberd Head Mold + Water Flask + Metal Sheet + Oak Shaft
  • Forged Javelin = water + metal sheet + javelin mold
  • Forged Long Sword = Long Blade Mold + Water Flask + Metal Sheet + Hilt Mold + Pommel Mold
  • Forged Mace = Mace Head Mold + Water Flask + Oak Shaft + Metal Sheet
  • Forged Morning Star = Spiked Ball Mold + Hilt Mold + Metal Sheet + Water Flask
  • Forged Pick = Pick Head Mold + Water Flask + Oak Shaft + Metal Sheet
  • Forged Rapier =  water + metal sheet + hilt mold + pommel mold + thin blade mold
  • Forged Scimitar =  water + metal sheet + hilt mold + pommel mold + curved blade mold
  • Forged Short Sword =  water + metal sheet + hilt mold + pommel mold + short blade mold
  • Forged Shuriken  = shuriken mold + metal sheet + water flask (makes 10, trivial at skill 85)
  • Forged Spear = water + metal sheet + spear head mold + oak shaft
  • Forged Throwing Axe = water + metal sheet + throwing axe mold
  • Forged Throwing Knife  = throwing knife mold + metal sheet + water flask (makes 10, trivial at skill 85)
  • Forged Two Handed Sword = Heavy Blade Mold + Hilt Mold + Pommel Mold + Metal Sheet + Water Flask
  • Forged Warhammer = water + metal sheet + hammer head mold + oak shaft
  • Sharpening Rusty Weapons = Rusty Weapon + Sharpening Stone
  • Silver Tipped Arrowheads = silver bar + file + block of medium quality ore + water.
  • Skinning knife (used to lower the quality of a pelt) = 1 small brick of ore + 1 dagger mold + 1 hilt mold + 1 water
  • Steel Arrow Shafts = water + file + 2 large bricks of medium ore.

Blackmithing Armor Recipes

When making armor, be sure to use the size mold that fits the race you are making it for.

  • Banded Gorget = Gorget Mold + Metal Sheet + Water Flask
  • Banded Bracer = Bracer Mold + Metal Sheet + Water Flask
  • Banded Boots = Boots Mold + Metal Sheet + Water Flask
  • Banded Sleeves = Sleeves Mold + 2 Metal Sheets + Water Flask
  • Banded Helm = Helm Mold + 2 Metal Sheets + Water Flask
  • Banded Belt = Belt Mold + 2 Metal Sheets + Water Flask
  • Banded Mask = Mask Mold + 2 Metal Sheets + Water Flask
  • Banded Mantle = Mantle Mold + 2 Metal Sheets + Water Flask
  • Banded Cloak = Cloak Mold + 2 Metal Sheets + Water Flask
  • Banded Gauntlets = Glove Mold + 2 Metal Sheets + Water Flask
  • Banded Tunic = Tunic Mold + 3 Metal Sheets + Water Flask
  • Banded Leggings = Leggings Mold + 3 Metal Sheets + Water Flask
  • Colored Fine Plate = Normal Fine Plate formula + dye + lacquer + acid
  • Fine Plate Boots = Smithy Hammer + leather backing + water + Plate Boot Mold + 2 medium quality folded sheets of metal.
  • Fine Plate Bracer = Smithy Hammer + leather backing + water + Plate Bracers Mold + 1 medium quality folded sheet of metal.
  • Fine PlateBreastplate = Smithy Hammer + leather backing + water + Breastplate Mold + 3 medium quality folded sheets of metal.
  • Fine Splinted Cloak = Smithy Hammer + leather backing + water + Splinted Cloak Mold + 3 medium quality folded sheets of metal.
  • Fine Plate Collar = Smithy Hammer + leather backing + water + Plate Collars Mold + 1 medium quality folded sheet of metal.
  • Fine Plate Gauntlets = Smithy Hammer + leather backing + water + Plate Gauntlet Mold + 2 medium quality folded sheets of metal.
  • Fine Plate Girdle = Smithy Hammer + leather backing + water + Plate Girdles Mold + 2 medium quality folded sheets of metal.
  • Fine Plate Greaves = Smithy Hammer + leather backing + water + Plate Greaves Mold + 3 medium quality folded sheets of metal.
  • Fine Plate Pauldron = Smithy Hammer + leather backing + water + Plate Pauldrons Mold + 2 medium quality folded sheets of metal.
  • Fine Plate Helm = Smithy Hammer + leather backing + water + Plate Helms Mold + 2 medium quality folded sheets of metal.
  • Fine Plate Vambraces= Smithy Hammer + leather backing + water + Plate Vambraces Mold + 2 medium quality folded sheets of metal.
  • Fine Plate Visor = Smithy Hammer + leather backing + water + Plate Visor Mold + 1 medium quality folded sheet of metal.
  • Forged Buckler = buckler mold + metal sheet + water + smithy hammer
  • Forged Kite Shield = Water + Smithy Hammer + Kite Shield Mld + 2 medium quality sheets of metal.
  • Forged Round Shield = Water + Smithy Hammer + Round Shield Mold + 2 metal sheets.
  • Forged Targ Shield = Water + Smithy Hammer + Targ Shield Mold + 1 folded sheet of metal
  • Forged Tower Shield = Water + Smithy Hammer + Tower Shield Mold + 1 medium quality folded sheet of metal.
