Raids

1. First and foremost, RAIDS ARE FOR FUN, not for loot. Loot is just an added bonus to a fun time. If you understand this, loot will be more abundant and you will have a better time over all.

2. Unless otherwise noted Fourteen Chambers raids are for members and prospects of Fourteen Chambers only. Any exceptions must be agreed upon by guild counsel.

3. Loot rules on Planes raids are as follows:

Armor: Rand 0 - (100 + 5 per piece you already have BEFORE START OF RAID)
**explained below

Your last piece of planes armor will default to you, in the event 2 people have default, roll will be 0 - 100

Weapons will simply be 0 - 100

When an item is no drop, you are not to loot until Sathe, Whom, or raid leader (if not Sathe or Whom) instruct you to do so.

Because of looting issues that may arise, you may not pass on armor items that drop that you do not have, unless everyone in your class (including those that have the item already) agrees to allow you to pass on the item. In the event everyone passes, those that do not have will roll 0 - 100. It will count against the winners loot.

Gems on the raid will be handed to Sathe, Whom, or raid leader. What we do with them will be decided on that raid. (Some examples include, but are not limited to: Letting people that didn’t win anything on raid roll for them; Making jewelry and awarding as presents to members for contest or other events)

4. Regular raids loot will default to people that WILL use items, if you will not use an item, you can roll for it, but please do not ask for default on an item you wont use. If more than 1 person would have default rolls will be 0 - 100. Once defaulted an item, you cannot be defaulted or roll on any item until everyone in your class has won an item. On items that no one has default ALL that haven’t already won something will roll.

5. While we are open to disagreements about loot, arguing over loot is not acceptable. If we have made a mistake in calling the loot winner, please feel free to tell us. We will hear your case and deal with it as fairly as possible. In the event that a no drop item is looted improperly please inform Sathe, Whom, or raid leader and we will handle the situation for you. Remember we came for fun, not loot.

6. We will have a loot master whose name is TBA. He/she will keep track of what is looted by who and when it was looted. Until everyone in your class has won an item from that zone, you cannot roll for future loot. (remember our goal is to get everyone at least 1 piece of armor) Loot WILL carry over from raid to raid. And because loot carries over from raid to raid, new members will not be allowed to roll on planar armor until they have been a member for a period of at least 1 month. (This is to prevent people from joining guild for loot and then leaving after gaining an item.)

7. Members are expected to be at preannounced raids if they are online. While we understand certain things do come up, if you are online and available to play EQ, we ask that you do show up to raids. Even on those raids in which you can gain nothing. Remember raids are for our enjoyment, and not for loot. We need your help if we want to continue doing the good raids. An example of an acceptable reason not to show for a raid is faction (PoG for example). While we will not punish you for not showing at a given raid, excessively missing preannounced raids may result in further punishment or lead to removal from guild.

8. Planar raids are to only be scheduled by guild leaders. If you would like to raid a particular plane, please contact your chamber leader. Although you are free to join others planar raids we ask that you let us schedule Fourteen Chambers raids, so we can ensure loot rules are followed etc.

**** Reasoning for planes armor loot rules ****
Our goal is a guild on a planes raid is not to get everyone the best piece of armor they can, although we would like to. Our goal is to have everyone leave with at least 1 piece if possible. Although the rules appear to be stacked in the favor of those that already have, here is how it works:

When you have no planar armor, you get to roll on every piece. Lets make an example and assume 5 pieces drop, BP, Arms, Bracer, Legs and Helm.

Now you who has no armor gets to roll on 5 pieces, all 0 - 100.
Lets assume another of your class has BP legs 2 bracers and gauntlets.
Based on his equipment he will roll 0 - 125 but he will only roll 2x
The person with no armor yet has a better chance of leaving the raid with something than the person with 5 items already. And as I said it is our goal that everyone leave with at least 1 piece.
