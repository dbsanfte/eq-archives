Recruiting=OPEN

Ballzz - November 22nd, 2004

Recruitment is now open for selected classes. We are looking for people with the desire to raid (ideally 4-5 days per week, roughly 7pm-12am EST even though we don't always raid that much). Vita focuses on being a team so if you can't raid or would rather play your alt while the guild is raiding this isn't the guild for you. You must also have a STABLE internet connection/computer system. If you can't stay connected or lag horribly on raids we can't use you. Level 66 is required and NO DRAMA/DFL WHORES!

Please note that you will need OoW and GoD expansions and will be expected to be lvl 70 now or soon. Also, the length of our recruit process varies so depending on our need, your effort, how well you fit in, number of friends in guild, server reputation etc..all will play a role in the length of time you will be expected to remain as a recruit. For some the process has been 1-2 weeks while others have taken as long as a couple months depending on the circumstances.

Flag and AA Requirements
We are looking for Time/KT flagged people but will consider some unflagged people as well (see forums for more info on possible reflagging events/times). You should have a minimum of 150AA with all your standard class AA's.

If you need out of game contact you can email Kyrel at millar156@yahoo.com or Pugslee at pugsleeeq@hotmail.com. Please also go to the Application section of our forums and formally apply by clicking here. When you post please include a bit about yourself including previous guilds, AA's, flags, playtimes and a link to your current magelo profile. If you are lacking in some area of the listed requirements or are not a listed class but think you are an exceptional player feel free to make your case either on our forums or in game with one of the aformentioned officers.

In game please /join virecruits:hi to mingle with various Vita members during the recruit process and find out what we are up to nightly.
