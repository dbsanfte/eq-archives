Twilight On Vex Thal

Magg - May 20th, 2003

Last night we cleared Vex Thal from top to bottom on our first real visit.  Regardless of how long it took, and how mind-numbing this zone can become, Uthal wouldn't let us quit!  After many hours, many trains, and the obligatory wipe, we finally made it to Aten Ha Ra and dropped her on our first attempt.  There are too many loots to list from the night, so I'll just list AHR loot with lazy links: Armguard of Shadows - Cassandria, Leggings of Secrets - Brillanie, Aegis of Judgement - Zilpa, and the Soul Essence for Talisman of Vah Kerrath - Stylo.  Congrats to everyone who won a new item today, you earned it.  Good job on sticking in there until the end.
