Twilight On Vex Thal

Magg - May 20th, 2003

Last night we cleared Vex Thal from top to bottom on our first real visit.  Regardless of how long it took, and how mind-numbing this zone can become, Uthal wouldn't let us quit!  After many hours, many trains, and the obligatory wipe, we finally made it to Aten Ha Ra and dropped her on our first attempt.  There are too many loots to list from the night, so I'll just list AHR loot with lazy links: Armguard of Shadows - Cassandria, Leggings of Secrets - Brillanie, Aegis of Judgement - Zilpa, and the Soul Essence for Talisman of Vah Kerrath - Stylo.  Congrats to everyone who won a new item today, you earned it.  Good job on sticking in there until the end.

Aten Ha Ra down!

Magg - May 8th, 2003

I guess it's been a bit since an update, so I think this one fits rather well.  Last night we ventured into the Temple of Marr and made a test run on Mithaniel Marr.  We didn't get the kill, but what we did get was all the information we needed.  Tonight we did a quick back flag mob, then it was time for Lord Mithaniel Marr round two.  We came in with a solid strategy and executed perfectly.  On our second attempt Vita Infinita sat Lord Mithaniel Marr down in his place.

 This is Lord Mithaniel Marr

This is Lord Mithaniel Marr on pot


Loots were this gem times two.  Grats to some mages, no one really cares about them so...moving on.
 

Aside from Mithaniel Marr, it's been much of the usual lately.  Cursed died a few times, but with Mithaniel Marr, Emperor Ssraeshza and the Zek twins on the front page, who really cares about Cursed?  Emperor Ssraeshza gave up the goods a number of times, and rest assured we'll be putting those Vex Thal keys to good use here in the not-so-distant future.  Agnarr went down again too, that's always a fun fight.  The last couple of months have been good to Vita, and there is definitely more to come soon.

p.s. - Uthal, you're not human, we love you.
