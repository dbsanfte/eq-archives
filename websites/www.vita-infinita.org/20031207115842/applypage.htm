Selective Recruiting

Ballzz - November 11th, 2003

In order to augment our existing forces, and better prepare for progession to the Plane of Time, recruitment is now open for selected classes.  Playtime should accommodate raiding 5 nights a week from roughly 7pm EST - 1am EST.  Level 65 is required to be considered.

Flag Requirements

Required Flags: Behemoth, Grummus, Aerin'Dar, BoT key, Carpin (lower CoD)

Recommended: Bertoxxulous, HoH trials, Agnarr

Class and AA requirements

Beastlords [1]- Mass Group Buff, Paragon of Spirit

 Clerics- Spell Casting Mastery 3, Healing Adept 3, Mass Group Buff

Enchanters- Gather Mana, Total Domination, Mass Group Buff, Spell Casting Mastery 3

Rogues [1]- Ferocity 3, Shroud of Stealth, Ambidexterity

Wizards- Spell Casting Fury 3, Spell Casting Mastery 3, Spell Casting Subtlety 3

Some AAs and flags may be negotiable.  Please contact Ballhaus, Neesha or Dimentia in game to inquire.
