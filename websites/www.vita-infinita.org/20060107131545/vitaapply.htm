Vita Infinita Recruitment Policy:
This is a guild of skilled players that are friends both in game and out. This is also a guild of honor and integrity. As such, if you wish to become part of this fine guild you will need to show a high level of quality as a person/player and also fit comfortably into our guild. It will not be enough that you are good at your class as we value having an enjoyable guild environment in which to play. The guild is mostly more mature players and we have a ZERO tolerance policy for drama/loot whoring or general idiocy.

If you are interested in becoming a member please check our needed classes below and post on our WoW forums in the application area with an account that reflects your in-game name and states your class, level, anyone you know in the guild, why you want to join and any other information you think relevant. Bonus points for anything witty or humorous!
