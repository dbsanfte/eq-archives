The Rainmaker

Magg - March 2nd, 2003

Deep in the Bastion of Thunder, past fallen lieutenants and guardians of the elements we were led.  Askr the Lost assured us at each turn that the worst was yet to come.  Many battles ensued before we came to face that which he feared.  Finally we stand before Agnarr, the Stormlord.  There would be no turning back the tides of victory.

For though he sought to destroy us, his fate was sealed from the start by Vita Infinita.

This truly is an amazing fight, and we are all very proud to have been a part of this accomplishment.  This was our second attempt, and we pulled through every adrenaline pumping second of it to victory.


Congratulations Nyrogen.
