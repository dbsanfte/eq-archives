Recruiting=OPEN

Ballzz - June 21st, 2004

Recruitment is now open for selected classes. We are looking for people with the desire to raid (ideally 4-5 days per week, roughly 7pm-12am EST). If you can't raid or would rather play your alt while the guild is raiding this isn't the guild for you. You must also have a STABLE internet connection/computer system. If you can't stay connected or lag horribly on raids we can't use you. Level 65 is required and NO DRAMA/DFL WHORES!

Flag Requirements
We are looking for Time flagged people but will consider EP flagged people as well if you are a quality player. Any GoD flags is a big plus as we will be raiding KT soon.

Needed Classes and AA requirements
Clerics [?]- Spell Casting Mastery 3, Healing Adept-3 (Advanced Healing Adept 3, Healing Gift-3 also preferred), Mass Group Buff

Rogues [1-2]- Ferocity 3, Shroud of Stealth, Ambidexterity

Warriors [1-2]- If you have at least 9500hp buffed, all your required tanking AA and meet other listed requirements we could use you. Only considering EST/CST warriors at this time.

Vita is currently lacking only a Quarm kill to complete PoP progression. We have also started making headway in GoD. If you fit the listed requirements please contact Ballhaus or Neesha in game for more info. Please also go to the Application section of our forums and formally apply by clicking HERE. When you post please include a bit about yourself including previous guilds and a link to your current magelo profile. Please also /join virecruits:hi in game to find out what's up and chat with us.
