Another First

We headed into PoNb tonight for a run at Terris Thule.  Everything went very well.  So, Vita has yet another first for the week.  Grats Fistuv on the cloak, and Spikester on the veil.  Sorry, but TT didn't faceplant so well for the cameras.  Maybe next time.

Long Overdue

Magg - February 4th, 2003

Patch days are never short on excitement, and today was no exception.  We hit up Kael for Statue / Tormax and spawned the Idol.  AoW is a mob that should have been down awhile ago, we just haven't had luck with finding the Statue up.  But enough with the could have, should have and would haves.  Vita Infinita presents the Avatar of War.  Congratulations to Annastazia on a Ring of Destruction, Nosylla on the Helm of Rallos Zek.  That's not it though - we have the first member of the honorary "Vita Infinita BoC brigade", grats Kaalan.  "Rez the dead, go go go!"

The story doesn't end here though, because we rapidly mobilized the newly formed BoC brigade in Ssra.  High Priest and Arch Lich both down.  Grats Lotwell on the Belt of Mystical Protection, Greymulkin on Ivory Crystal Greaves, Seliel on Sword of Lost Crusades and Fildorf on Shield of Auras.  That's not all though, tonight we also have a special segment, devoted to a "special" rogue - Heidee.  The High Priest was especially kind today.
 

And last, but not least, a second Vita first for the day, Xerkizh the Creator.  Another mob that should have been down a long time ago.  Congrats Strangey and Xorcerer on shields, and Raymcp on the mage dagger.
 

Xerkizh the Creator
