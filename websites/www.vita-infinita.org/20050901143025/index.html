Vita progress continues..
-Ballzz August 20th, 2005

First thing I want to mention is the merge of Legendary Heroes into Vita. We have had great success raiding together and I'm happy to report that LH is now officially part of Vita! Welcome to the family LH! We are happy to have you onboard with us to conquer WoW! We still need to fill some spots so if interested in joining click Apply on the sidebar for more info.

Now on to the MC stuff.. we had our fourth raid in MC today and made record time to Luci downing him in less than 1.5 hours. We then got Mag to 20% which I think is pretty damn good considering our lack of experience and tranq shots. Great job Vita and a big thanks to the open raiders that did a great job on the raid! Gratz to Remlor on the first Vita MC loot-Arcanist Boots, Smuu on his Felheart Gloves and Fizben on his Tranq shot!
Lucifron deados!
-Ballzz August 14th, 2005

Vita's third trip to Molten Core with Legendary Heroes (and others) was productive indeed! We made a lot of progress learning the zone and got Luci to assume the dirtnap position on our second attempt for the night. We also got two attempts on Mag and got him to just under 60% health on our second attempt ever. Gratz to Lortik on the gloves and to the loot n' scoot on his Choker of Enlightenment! I want to thank our co-raid leaders at LH and all of the open raiders that helped us pull off a great raid!
