Recruiting=OPEN

Ballzz - August 31st, 2004

Recruitment is now open for selected classes. We are looking for people with the desire to raid (ideally 4-5 days per week, roughly 7pm-12am EST even though we don't always raid that much). If you can't raid or would rather play your alt while the guild is raiding this isn't the guild for you. You must also have a STABLE internet connection/computer system. If you can't stay connected or lag horribly on raids we can't use you. Level 65 is required and NO DRAMA/DFL WHORES!

Flag Requirements
We are looking for Time/KT flagged people.

Needed Classes and AA requirements
Bards [1]- Epic, IM3, Fading Memories, Ability to max singing (prior to attaining Ayonae's Tutelage) with gear/AAs

Beastlords [1]- Mass Group Buff, Paragon of Spirit

Clerics [1 meeelion]- Spell Casting Mastery 3, Healing Adept-3, Advanced Healing Adept 3, Healing Gift-3, Mass Group Buff

Druids [1-2]- Mass Group Buff, Spirit of the Wood-3, Radiant Cure-3, Healing Adept-3, Advanced Healing Adept-3, Healing Gift-3

Enchanters [1-2]- Mass Group Buff, Spell Casting Subtlety-3, (Gather Mana, Lightning Reflexes-5, Natural Durability-3

Necromancers [1-2]- Spell Casting Mastery-3, Theft of Life-3, Lifeburn

Rogues [1-2+]- Ferocity 3, Shroud of Stealth, Ambidexterity

Warriors [1]- If you have at least 10000hp buffed, all your required tanking AA and meet other listed requirements we could use you. EST warrior strongly preferred!

Wizards [1-2+]- Spell Casting Fury-3, Spell Casting Mastery-3, Spell Casting Subtlety-3

Vita is currently working on GoD as well as clearing Time. If you think you fit the listed requirements please contact Ballhaus or Neesha in game for more info. Please also go to the Application section of our forums and formally apply by clicking here. When you post please include a bit about yourself including previous guilds, AA's, flags and a link to your current magelo profile. If you are lacking in some area of the listed requirements or are not a listed class but feel you are an exceptional player feel free to make your case either on our forums or in game with one of the aformentioned officers.

In game please /join virecruits:hi to mingle with various Vita members during the recruit process.
