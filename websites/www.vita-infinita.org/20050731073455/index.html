Vita's first MC raid!
-Ballzz July 19th, 2005

Vita had it's first MC raid this last weekend. It was a joint raid with 2 other guilds Legendary Heroes and Thieves and Charlatans. Considering we were 1 group short of full, limited on healers and had a lot of first timers we did very well. We didn't down any named but got to Lucifron before some ppl had to leave forcing us to call it a night. We have another MC raid planned for this weekend so hopefully we will get to Mag this time.

We are still recruiting quality players so if you are looking for a guild please click the Apply button for more info.
