Outstanding effort!

Ballzz - April 3rd, 2004

After our first Rathe and Coir kill there was some stupid chatter from some dumbfuck about us "not being ready for Time" but within 3 days downed Rathe and Coir again to show it wasn't some fluke and then nabbed Xegony and Fennin to round out our Time reflagging in less than a week.

Yesterday and today was our first trip to Time and I have to say we did an outstanding job. Most of us had little knowledge of what to expect beyond a very basic idea. In our first 2 days we almost got to tier 4. We got through 6 waves of Tier 3 on our first attempt and with only 64 people. Still much to learn but we certainly are "ready".

As many "uber" guilds that go belly up and "family" guilds that don't make it very far, Vita defies description and I am very proud that we have not only had the longevity to stick around but the determination to make it this far. Thanks to all the loyal Vita members new and old!
