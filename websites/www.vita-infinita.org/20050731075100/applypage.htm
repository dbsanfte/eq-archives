Vita Infinita Recruitment Policy:

This is a guild of skilled players that are also friends both in game and out. This is also a guild of honor and integrity. As such, if you wish to become part of this fine guild you will need to show a high level of quality and the ability to fit comfortably into our group. If you are offended easily or don't get along with the guild it won't be enough that you are good at your class. We are searching for quality players that are also quality people. The guild is mostly more mature players and guild chat can reflect that so please no children. We also have a ZERO tolerance policy for drama/loot whoring and general idiocy.

If you are interested in becoming a member you must post on our WoW forums in the application area with an account that reflects your in-game name and states your class, level, anyone you know in the guild, why you want to join and any other information about yourself you think relevant. We are currently considering the following classes (bold text indicates greatest need):

Druids
Priests
Paladins
Rogues

but will consider other classes if they can show an exceptional level of quality. At present there is a level 35 minimum for consideration.

If the initial criteria stated above is met you will be given an invite to the guild as an initiate for up to 30 days or longer if needed. During this time we shall get to know one another to determine if you are a good fit for us and for you to decide if you would like to stay. If at the end of the initiate period you would like to stay and leadership feels you fit in well with us then you will be promoted to full member. Long time valued members will become veterans at the discretion of leadership.

Please note that any sort of drama/loot whoring will be dealt with swiftly. Our guild chat and forums will entertain a myriad of topics and discussions but will never be a place for member's personal agendas or a platform for foolishness.
