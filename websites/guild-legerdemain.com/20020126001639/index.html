    Hate!Saturday - January 12th 2002    
GREAT day in Hate folks. We had the usual scattered splatter when breaking in to the safe spot, but once we got set up, things went well. I'd like to post the list of what everyone got, but the person who handled lewt has yet to e-mail me the list. No names mentioned, but I'm sure ROLD will send it to me soon :P~ Here's what I remember:
ZzeepEtherial Mist Breast Plate
Etherial Mist Leggings
AlashaEtherial Mist Gauntlets
TienenHauberk of Harmony
RoldaniousStaff of Elemental Mastery:Earth
GanodenCuller
NindenSome weapon who's name I can't remember
SkramboldBand of Discipline
FuttalHand of Maestro
I'm sure some of these may be incorrect, and I'm also missing several drops. I'll update this news report as I get more info. As a side note, Roldanious now has a six-note blade, and is working on a full set of insideous armor :P~
We got an unexpected visitor at the safe spot. Skrambold had been pulling a path towards the throne room, when he found that there were about 5 spite golems in Maestro's hall or some such. Well, on one pull, Skram's agro got transfered around enough to land smack dab on top of Maestro. He came a'running and Skram did the ~hit Stone Stance and MASH FD~ dance. Well, all was well and Maestro started heading back to his crib when BAM! That's right, Maestro smelled sitting casters and floated on in the side door. Wu oh... here's snapshot Joredor got:

I snapped off a tell to Zzeep asking him to camp out. He logged off as soon as he could. Much to my suprise, though, we didn't end up needing him to do mass rezzies. Yup yup, we spanked him at the safe spot hehe. It was a thrilling battle.
Our members eventually got the hang of using an assist button, and mezzies stopped getting broken. This is a good thing as it helps to keep me alive hehe. Of course, the fact that we had to spend time and extra mana learning that whole assist thing slowed us down. Squishy chanter broken mez = mana sponge. We still ended up with a clear path to the throne room and set up camp there. One one particular nasty pull we got 2 spite golems, 2 forsaken revenants, a loathing litch, and a rat. We had a few fatalities while mobs were getting mezzed but we double tanked the spites and got them splatted. It was riding the hairy edge for a while, but we got it done. Was another very exciting battle.
We ended up killing almost everything on the first floor and headed to the 2nd floor. The mobs are much tougher up there and we were killing mobs at a much slower rate then we had been on the 1st floor. One particularly nifty event was the killing of ~Corruptor of Life~. He was level 60 with roughly 20k hp. Was a nice battle indeed. Unfortunately, ther ewere no Paladins of Tunare available to lewt the quest piece. Keymarad now has it for MQ. The quest will result in a NICE 2hs for a pally at some point.
People began dropping out of the raid due to RL commitments and being just plain tired. We were about to call the raid after 8 hours time due to the loss of our raiders when our tired monk pulled 3 2nd floor mobs. I tried to mez!~splat~ Key tried to heal! ~splat~ Alasha got camped out in time.. while Zzeep went ~splat~. That left the rest of our raiders up there with a few druids and shaman available for healing power and 3 2nd floor mobs chomping down on them. THEY WON! OMG I was so suprised hehe.
I'm not exactly sure how they did it, and I'm quite disappointed that I missed the majority of such a heavy battle, but I'm sure glad they won. Had to have been some great fun.
We ported out shortly after though.. but not before finally nailing that damned spamming evangelist guy. We have another hate raid on Feb 16th. Invited friends sign up on the message boards.
Special thanks to our friends Faang, Tealamar, and Zukum who stayed for the entire raid. Also an enchanter friend of Alasha's stayed for as much of the raid as he could, but I can't remember his name for the life of me hehe. Sorry friend /cry
------submitted by ) Terrenoth
