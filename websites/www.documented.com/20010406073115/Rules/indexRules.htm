Policies and rules:

Guild goals:

The goals of the guild are quite simple. The first goal and most important is to have fun. The second goal is to make this guild an elite fighting force, capable of taking out any target, yet maintaining a low-level family oriented membership. The third goal is to make our hunts and time together variable and not static doing the same thing over and over again.

Structure of the guild:

1) There will be one GM. The guildmaster will be responsible for the well being of the guild. The guildmaster will guide the guild and change guild policy as deemed necessary. The guildmaster will work close with the officers in all ways before making decisions. Once the GM makes these decisions the officers and members will follow these decisions.

2) There will be two Battle officers. These officers will be responsible for scheduling raids, executing them, and making them a success. They will also be responsible for making sure every member online is at the raid and on time.

3) There will be one internal affair officer. This officer will be in charge of communicating with guildmates. If arguments or miscommunications develop, this officer is the first point of contact. If this person is not available, then the GM should be contacted

4) There will be one ambassador. This person will be responsible for communications between guilds. The purpose of this position is to keep track of what guilds have scheduled and correlate this knowledge with what we wish to do so that we can reduce the possibilities of fighting over targets. With our intentions made clear in advance, we should almost never move aside while we intend to take out a target.

5) There will be one person selected from each class to be the representative of that class. Their duties will be to keep themselves informed of what the needs and wants are for that class and each individual in that group. They will be responsible for knowing what items each member of their class has as well. They will also be responsible for educating others and helping them learn their class in a productive manner where they see fit. The last task would be to actively recruit others of their class that they feel would make a good addition to the guild, if that class were needed. Class representatives can be looked upon as mentors of the class.

Loot:

Loot will be handled in the following manner. Each class will be given the opportunity to show that they can determine “CLASS SPECIFIC” loot in a civil manner amongst themselves. If they demonstrate that they cannot, then the decision will be made for them by the GM and other officers available. This last resort should be avoided at all costs. It makes life a lot easier if people can get along and discuss these things amongst themselves in a civil manner. ALL LOOT will be handed out at the end of the event unless it is to be held for a member not present.

1) CLASS SPECIFIC LOOT – We will allow the members of their class to determine this themselves. If we see a problem with this group of people handling this on their own then the decisions will be made for them by the officers and GM. If the class representative is present, he/she will be the person to take charge of the discussion. Example: Warrior breastplate drops. Three warriors are present at the event and only two of them need this item. The three of them would talk amongst themselves to determine who should get it. If there is any argument or dispute then the officers and GM will make the decision.

2) NON-CLASS SPECIFIC LOOT - Loot that is not “NO DROP” will be handed to the officers. They will then talk and award the loot on a basis of merit, who has seniority, who puts time and effort into the guild, who needs the item most to improve the quality of our guild, and many other factors, including the class representative’s input. This loot will be given out at the end of an event.

3) NO DROP LOOT - The GM and officers who are present at the event will determine “NO DROP” items that are not class specific. The class representatives can add their input as well if they feel their class should have the item.

Membership:

Membership will be by referral only. This means that if there is a candidate who wishes to join our ranks they will either have to contact the GM personally or by word of mouth by a fellow guildmate. The person then must hunt with the guild on a few raids, get to know some of the members, and have at least one sponsor. The sponsor must make them aware of all our rules and how the guild works. After the candidate hunts with us they will then be interviewed by the GM. As long as the interview goes well and nobody has a major issue with this person they will be added to the roster. The minimum level requirement for the guild will be level 49; however, anyone joining under the level of 54 will be rare since we will be focused on a smaller type guild capable of taking out big targets.

Hunts:

The battle officers or GM will schedule official guild hunts. All must attend these hunts when online. If the GM calls a spur of the moment event, it will be considered an official event as well. All members are required to drop what they are doing and gather ASAP for that event. This is critical as for fast mobilization is crucial to taking out big targets before others. All other hunts, whether among guild members or a mixture will be considered every day hunts with no rule enforcement’s regarding loot.

1) PLANES (Fear and Hate) - This is how the guild will work these two planes. Fear and Hate will be raided only on the weekdays. We will make these raids short and sweet, about 4-6 hours long. On rare occasion we will make these trips on the weekend, but this will not be a common occurrence. The reason for this rule is simple. Many are burnt out on planes. It doesn’t mean we will stop doing them but we will not make them entire daylong events. We can clear out Fear and Hate rather quickly with everyone there. On non-scheduled days you are all free to raid the planes if you can get enough to do so.

2) EPIC QUESTS – Epic quest help is a very difficult subject to cover. Most everyone wishes to get their epic quest weapon but they must realize that these are very difficult to obtain and time is needed. Many members ask for help on a daily basis. I think the best way to handle the epic quests are for those individuals to request a specific raid on a target and post their desire to do this on a particular date. If it can be arranged, we will make it an official hunt. The guild will dedicate to a specific class every week to help with epic quests.

Encouragement’s:

The guild encourages you to level. Levels are a necessary evil that we must accomplish to realize our goals. You are encouraged to hunt with guild members as much as possible. You are encouraged to share your loot with members who can benefit from an item rather then trade it for a twink item.

Removable offenses:

You may be removed from the guild for anyone of the following reasons. The GM and officers will discuss such events when they deem it necessary to do so:

1) Blatant disrespect to guild members.
2) Causing harm to the reputation of the guild through various acts.
3) Not coming to guild events while online.
4) Selling on e-bay before you guild remove yourself.
5) Showing a bad attitude towards the guild on a regular basis.
6) Posting for membership on another guild board.
7) Whatever else the GM and officers may deem worthy of possible removal.

Conclusion:

The guild will be based on having fun, yet at the same time focus on a elite fighting force capable of most anything in short notice. We will be more level and quality oriented with our membership. With the right people and skills, we can do virtually anything. Simply be the best, respect your guildmates, demand respect back, and most of all, have fun!
