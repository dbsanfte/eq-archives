======

Everybody is free (to Break It on Down on the West Side)
(a parody of everybody is free (to wear sunscreen) by Baz Luhrmann)

======


Ladies and Gentlemen of the class of '99 ...
Break It on Down on the West Side.

If I could offer you only one tip for the future,
the West Side would be it.

The long-term benefits of the West Side have
been proved by the Old School Gansta's,
whereas the rest of my advice has no basis more
reliable than my own meandering pimpin'.
I will dispense this advice now ...

Enjoy the power and beauty of your Gat. Oh,
never mind, you will never understand the power
and the beauty of your Gat until you wind up using it
on the wrong end of a drive by.

But trust me, in 20 years, if you still haven't been shot,
you will look back at photos of your gun collection and recall,
in away you can't grasp now, how blissfully hip you
were, and how fabulous your 'fro really looked.

Your bandana does not look stupid

Don't worry about the Police -- or worry, but
know that worrying is as effective as trying to
do a drive by on a unicycle. The real
troubles in your life are apt to be things that
never crossed your gangsta' mind. The kind that
fire a machine gun into your living room at 4 p.m.
on some idle Tuesday.

Do one thing that scares a crack whore every day.

Chill.

Don't disrespect yo momma ; don't let her
smack up your homies who disrespect her.

Get Down.

Don't waste your time with chicken-head ass bitches.
The war is long and in the end, it's only with
the East Side

Remember your dead homies,
forget all the punk-ass East Siders you wasted
If you succeed in doing this, tell me how.

Keep your old Tek-9,
Throw out your old crackpipe

Break it on Down

Don't feel guilty if you only killed 13 East-Siders this week
The most interesting people I know haven't killed more than 10
East Siders in a week yet. Some of the most interesting
40-year-olds I know still haven't.

Get plenty of crack.

Be kind to your heart, you'll miss it when it gets shot.

Maybe you'll marry, you probably won't. maybe you'll have 7 illegitimate
children, may you'll have 8. Maybe you'll get locked up for the long ride
when you're 22, maybe the cops won't catch you till your 25.

However much time you do, don't congratulate yourself too
much, or berate yourself, either.


Your choices are all wrong. So are all your homies'.

Enjoy your Gat. Shoot as many people as you can.

Don't be afraid of it or what other people think of
it, if they give you shit, just turn it sideways and pull the trigger.

Pop a cap, even if you have nowhere to do it but in the movie theater.

Learn how to read, even if you never use it.
do not get shot in the face, it will only make you feel ugly.


Get to know your children.
You'll never know when they'll join the East Side.
Be nice to your siblings, unless they're East Siders,
then you can kill them.

Understand that homies come and go. But with a
precious few, you should always roll with.

Work hard to bridge the gaps in drug preferences and
shooting style, for as the older you get,
the more you need your homies to do your dirty work for
you while you're in the slammer.


Live in Brooklyn once, but leave before you get shot.
Live in Compton once, but leave before you get shot.

Kick it.

Accept certain inalienable truths: crime rates will fall,
the East Side will get stronger, you too will get old,
and when you do, you'll fantasize that when you were young,
crime rates were high, the East Side was a bunch of bitches,
and children respected their momma's

Word to yo momma.

Don't expect anyone else to bail you out of jail.
maybe you have a rich crack-dealing uncle,
maybe you have $75,000 stashed in your sock drawer.
but you never know when the cops are going to take your
money and shoot your uncle.


Don't flash your gang signs on the East Side,
or by the time you're 20, you'll be dead

Be careful whose advice you buy, but be patient
with hos who supply it.

Advice is a form of weapondry. Dispensing it is a
way of fishing your Gat from the disposal, wiping it off,
putting a new clip in, and capping someone's ass one last time.

But trust me on the West Side.
