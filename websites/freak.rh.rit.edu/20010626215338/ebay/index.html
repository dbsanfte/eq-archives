Accept only the Worst!

Wi is not only the worst mage in all of FrostFell, but probably the absolute worst character ever created in any game of any kind!

Wi is shown above in his natural surroundings

Yes you're reading it right, this robe, which you CAN NOT TAKE OFF, has an Armor Level of -400. AND YOU CAN'T TAKE IT OFF!!!

That's not the worst of it. You may be familiar with bug that allows enchanments to remain permanent, well Wi is affected by that bug, unfortunately when it stuck him he had just finished running by a Dark Revanant! Consequentially, Wi has the following permanent enchantments on him at all times:

  • Vulnerability Other VI (-35 Melee Defense)

  • Slowness Other VI (-35 Quickness)
  • Acid Vulnerability Other VI (+200% damage taken from Acid)
  • Blade Vulnerability Other VI (+200% damage taken from Slashing)
  • Bludgeoning Vulnerability Other VI (+200% damage taken from Bludgeoning)
  • Cold Vulnerability Other VI (+200% damage taken from Cold)
  • Fire Vulnerability Other VI (+200% damage taken from Fire)
  • Lightning Vulnerability Other VI (+200% damage taken from Lightning)
  • Piercing Vulnerability Other VI (+200% damage taken from Piercing)
As far as I can tell, Wi is cursed in the following ways by the evil turbinites:
  • Maximum Vitae is higher than most normal players - Wi is currently at 295% vitae, all of his skills are close to -2 times their normal values (as an interesting side effect, you can run backwards very fast)
