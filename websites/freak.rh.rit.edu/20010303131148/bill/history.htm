I was born on a Saturday, the second of June in 1979, three minutes past the stroke of midnight.

My parents are Jerry William Seely and Nancy Leah Seely, who at the time lived in Lyons, NY (a small village in central New York)

They had a second child a year later whom they named Amber Rae Seely - my only true-blooded sibling.

When I was 3, my parents divorced and I stayed with my mother. She then married Louis John Salerno.

When I was 5, we moved to Clyde, NY (an even smaller village)

Louis and Nancy then had two children: Crystal Leah Salerno and Louis John Salerno, Jr.

Around the same time, my father was dating Laurie Brunner Cole, whom he later married.

Laurie already had two children from a previous marriage, Daniel Robert Cole and Shannon Mary Cole.

After everyone thought that things had finally settled, Laurie and Jerry had Alyssa Laura Seely.

All this leaves me with one sister, two half-sisters, one half-brother, one step-sister, and one step-brother

Plus a slew of aunts, uncles, cousins, and grandparents. And now you know my heavily abridged immediate family tree.
