Search Engines that I Dig:

Altavista - my all time favorite search engine - read the FAQ's, they tell you how to use some of the really cool search patterns. This one comes complete with the Babelfish, a five language translator (From Hitchhiker's guide to the galaxy - love that book).

International Lyrics Server - the most comprehensive archive of lyrics to date, unfortunately I think it's still out of order (legal complications). Until it's back up, you can still search for lyrics at the Lyrics Headquarters (not as comprehensive a site, but it'll have to do for now)

Where I get my news from:

MSNBC - sad but true, I use MSNBC. It's a pretty good all around news site.

Pointcast - need stock quotes, news, etc. pushed to your computer? download the free software for Pointcast.

Slashdot.org - where I go for my geek news. If it's about a computer, you'll find it here.

At times, I have been known to run the God of all OSes: Linux, and this is where I went to get drivers, libraries, software, and information:

Linux - the linux site, what more can I say?

Linuxberg - the definitive site for RedHat RPMs!
