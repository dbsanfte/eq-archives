Comedians:

There's a lot of them out there, some are funny, some make my head hurt, but these one's are the best of the best:

Chris Farley - The Late, Great Grandmaster of Comedy. Chris Farley was by far the funniest comedian that I have ever seen. I've watched all of his movies as far as I know, and even though people say that half of them sucked, I still laughed my ass off at every single one of them. My favorite character for Chris Farley was Matt Foley the Motivational Speaker.

Dana Carvey - This guy was really funny back in his prime, he used to do Wayne's World and the Church Lady (never liked the Church Lady). Now he's older and has a wife and kids. His acts are still really funny, but he just goes a little overboard on the jokes about his kids.

Chris Rock - great comedian, his mock music videos were excellent.

Craig Kilborn - Former host of the Daily show, has his own late night show now. Very funny guy, unfortunately, I haven't had time to catch his new show yet.

Books

Hitchiker's Guide to the Galaxy "Trilogy" - This is a trilogy of 5 books (that's right, 3=5) by Douglas Adams, the greatest author ever. The books are, in order, "The Hitchiker's Guide to the Galaxy", "The Resturaunt at the End of the Universe", "Life, the Universe, and Everything", "So Long and Thanks for all the Fish", and the final book (written years later) "Mostly Harmless". If you like Comedy and you like Sci-Fi, well, more like don't hate Sci-Fi, then you need to read this set of books - they are drop dead hillarious. Caveat: Douglas Adams is British, and this book is British comedy, so if you didn't laugh at any of the Monty Python movies, you probably won't like this book.

Dirk Gently Books - these two books are also by Douglas Adams, and they are equally funny. They include "Dirk Gently's Holistic Detective Agency", and "The Long, Dark Tea-Time of the Soul", both starring the wildly humorous detective, Dirk Gently. These books are unique in that they use a very odd style of writing where the author seems to have no coherent relationship between chapters, and then some time around chapter 35 or so, everything seems to just fall together.
