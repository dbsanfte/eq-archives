Meetings Archive

Welcome to the Council of Guilds web site. We are here to further the player community on the Everquest server, Karana. We will work to create and maintain communcication between all the guilds on this server, both member guilds and non-member guilds. We want to provide events of all types to the players of Karana, including role-playing, wars, storylines, competitions and fun activities that all players, guild members, independents, and friends will enjoy.

8/14/00 - The Council of Guilds will hold a meeting on Thursday, August 17, 2000 at 8:00 p.m. Central Standard Time in the Lake Rathe Arena. The meeting is to be focused on what the Council can do to sponsor events for characters under level 20.

7/22/00 - The Council of Guilds held it's second Dungeon Guild Mixer Hunt on Saturday, July 22, 2000. We began at 1 pm Central time, and three dungeons were assaulted, Permafrost, Splitpaw and Kedge Keep. See the Mixer Hunt page for details.

7/06/00 - The Council of Guilds held a meeting last night in the Lake Rathe arena. The Log of that meeting is here.

6/05/00 - The Council of Guilds held a Guild Mixer Dungeon Hunt on Saturday, June 3 at 2 pm Central Standard time. The page listed above lists all the intial information about these hunts, including leaders and staging areas, and also has a debriefing on each of the hunts that took place, with screenshots where possible.

5/30/00 - The Council of Guilds held a meeting on Tuesday, May 30, 2000 at 7:30 p.m. Central Standard Time in the Lake Rathe Arena. The meeting was focused on the upcoming Guild Mixer Dungeon Hunts. The meeting log is available here. There have also been new guilds added to the Member Sites.

5/13/00 - The Council of Guilds held a meeting again on Thursday, May 11, 2000 at 7:30 p.m. Central Standard Time in Freeport City Hall in North Freeport. The meeting went well, though attendance was small, and the log is available online here. I've also added a Meetings Archive page which archives all the previous meetings. The Member Sites page has been updated with the latest members, as well as the names of the representatives and their email addresses where available. I also want everyone to know that my email address has changed. It is now haemish@mail.com.
