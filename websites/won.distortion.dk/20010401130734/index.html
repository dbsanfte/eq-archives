Guild calendar :

20/09/2000 - Guild raid at Orc camp in East commonlands - A success.
26/09/2000 - Guild raid at Orc camp in East commonlands - Was moved to an Orc camp in West Commonlands, but was quickly moved to a Dervish cutthroat camp, as we turned out to be overkill at the orcs. Almost everyone leveled, so the evenings raid is considered a success.
04/10/2000 - We made our move towards the Oasis of Marr , where we encountered mobs that were more of a challenge than the orc and dervs. Though we had some trouble with Deep Water Crocodiles, we killed and killed lots of other crocs, mymmy's, Madmens's ...... . A single member had a little "accident" fooling around with a non-friendly guy called Cazel. All in all it was a great evening which told usd that the Oasis of Marr will grant us several levels to come.
10/10/2000 - Another guild raid against the various mobs of the Oasis of Marr. All participating members died at least once but we came back and got our revenge - 10 times fold. This was one of the best radis so far, based on the fact the several members gained a level and most others are very close.
17/10/2000 - We stayed we fought we lost. well we all died once or twice some even three times (well one more than 10 times LowerGuk is NOT for lvl 11). We will have our revenge. the good stuff. Nanna got new spells. Weland got dual wield and Sindre one step closer to new heal spell.
24/10/2000 - Quiet day, one little guy who was alittle less experienced than the rest cought up and gained a lvl (Sleipner). Another little story is that using an escape spell (Shadow Step) can cause unforseen trouble, like ending up several hundred yards off shore with a madman on tail - outcome if you cant outswim the mob you will learn this lesson the hard way (Frej). In this state of our experience we often get new skills/spells, this has made the group unstable meaning that when one or two members are missing the rest can get into trouble which the full group easily could have handled. The authors advise is: If you know that u will lvl into a new spell-level then buy the spells the day before play-day so you wont have to leave the group to purchase them. If you level into a level with a new skill then wait till the group logs out and then get it or just get it before the next play-day. I know that skills is a bit different from spells due to the fact that you cant take it with you in a backpack but most people are past the "nessesary" skills like DualWield and meditation (Tjalfe though first attain Meditation at lvl 14) so if you get a skill that is not a absolute MUST then wait and stay by the group. This will make us stronger and cause less deaths and the person who must wait for a skill gets it till next play-day.
01/11/2000 - Even though the evening started with half of the group dying or running for their lives, this was probably the best evening so far. We worked as a team (Sadly Tjalfe was missing) and that payed off. We earned experince fast with very little need of healing and very little downtime (A thankfull thought goes to a lvl 55 ENC who delievered haste and clarity). Not much more to say .... we kicked ass.
07/11/2000 - Didnt happen... cause Verant updated 3 servers including Brell Serilis.
14/11/2000 - Quiet day - only 4 members could play. Made some desent exp. few died and some party members harassed highlevel TALL people.(Dont forget: Be small, be proud)
22/11/2000 - We bashed every orc at "orc highway" - was almost too easy.... Sadly we had a few deaths at the end of the day ... but now our chanter is within one lvl from BREEZE. We talked about leavint the Oasis of Marr and move on to Upper Guk.
28/11/2000 - We decided to stay in the oasis due to the fact that our tank was unable to participate. We were not a full group and we had just invited a gnome magician (Phae) who we ended up PLing a bit. Phae is not yet as experienced as the rest of the guild but she will be very soon... The most experienced member of the guild (Qurty) left us recently to join another guild.
