Introduction

Banished Souls is a young guild on Tarew Marr, formed in Feb. 2002.

The basic premise behind the guild is:

1) We are people who have limited play time during the week
2) We dont want that to inhibit us from trying new things


We are small, and we strive to extract the very most out of each of our players to allow us to try encounters normally reserved for much more "populated" guilds. We work at understanding game mechanics and strategies to allow us to make honest attempts at these encounters. We focus all our members on a guild-selected common goal and work as a team to accomplish this goal, then we move on.

Although sometimes, we just go attack something ludicrously beyond our abilities to see how it kills us. =)

We look for players who are level 55 and above, who like to play and advance their characters and who like to learn about the game. We realize the high end game is vastly different from the experience grind that got us here, requiring tactics, coordination and planning to be successful.

Guild Rules

You play with your main on raids, unless your alt provides a missing component to a raid. If thats the case, you decide beforehand which character you want to roll on equipment for. Not for both.

Each player is allowed one Alt in guild.

Each player is required to create a Ranger and monitor a guild chosen "uber mob".

The guild votes on an experience zone for the week as well as a weekend Raid. You are strongly encouraged to play with your fellow members during the week when you can. The weekend raid, your attendance is expected. Its not neccessary to make ALL functions, as we all have lives to attend to and even varying play hours, but we are here to make the most use of our player base.

The guild decides if an epic step is worthy of being designated as a weekend "raid"...based on player level, nearness to completion, and benefit to guild and time that player has given to the guild in helping others. No posting about setting up epic steps (get the help from direct tells)...final epic fights can be suggested as weekend raids.

Conflicts

Inevitably in a game like this, we will come across situations where people get upset with each other. We realize this, and also realize that EQ is a place we all want to have fun. You should make every attempt possible to rectify the situation directly with the other person. If no accord can be reached, speak with an officer. Most in game conflicts stem from miscommunication or misinterpretation. Keep this in mind before you spam guildchat with nonsense.

Guildremoval - Before you /guildremove yourself, make sure you have carefully considered this decision and aren't acting in the heat of a moment, because it is permanent. It is important to the cohesiveness of the guild that we work out our issues and carefully consider the impact of our actions.

Loot

Recruits do not roll on loot. If they are the only representative of their class on a raid and a no drop class item drops, it is theirs.

Gems that drop on guild functions are donated to the guild (Blue Diamonds, Fire Opals, Diamonds, Ruby Crowns etc)..to be used for raid funds (coffins, peridots, make resist jewelry etc) or purchasing needed upgrades for people(decided by guild).

Guild members can purchase gems from the guild at half the going rate in EC. These purchases are not for resale , they are intended for making resist jewelry, armor etc.

Spell Scrolls are given to the person in attendance who can scribe them. Spells are always given to the person on raid where it will do the most good. This is to insure our level 60s have all their spells (where it does the guild the most good). If more than 1 can use, the higher level person gets the spell. A random roll is done to determine winner when equal levelled characters can use. Un-used spell scrolls are donated to the guild for resale or banking until a guild member can use.

Epic Pieces - Are given to the person nearest completion of their epic at the time. If two people are equally along the epic quest, the piece goes to the higher level member.

Armor, Items, Weapons - Any in attendance of raid and who will USE FULL TIME as an upgrade can roll. Resist gear can be rolled on by anyone at a raid if it is an upgrade. If an item is found that has Stats and Resists, it will be considered a stat item first.

Example: Imbued Granite Spaulders 20ac +10 to resists shoulders...this item would be considered a stat item as the ac is good for melees.

Haste items- Any in attendance of raid and who will USE FULL TIME as an upgrade to their current haste can roll. No other stat is considered on the item. You can not roll if you have equal or better haste.

If you "win" an item from any of the above classes (ie armor, weapons) you abstain from further rolls in that class on a given raid. Let everyone have a chance to win. Trading of items won on a raid are to be worked out between the 2 winners only if the other person is willing to trade.

Open Rolls - If no one present at a guild function can use an item, an open roll will be done. Once you win an open roll you are out. You may roll up only once per guild function. Items not included in an open roll because they fall under different rules already specified or go to the guild bank:

  • Planar gems
  • Quest Gems
  • Quest Armor (example: MQ SS, Thurg)
  • Spells

Loot Disputes can occur, even with a solid set of rules in place. If this happens..final judgement will be left to the Guild Leader and Officers. Their say is final.

Raids

Raids are voted on by the guild, majority vote decides which raid will be occurring.

Raid Rules will be discussed on the board prior to the raid. Puller, MA, Coordinator will be assigned prior to arrival. You are expected to be at the raid site at the designated time.

During a raid, talking thru guchat will be kept to a minimum, it is our raid channel.
