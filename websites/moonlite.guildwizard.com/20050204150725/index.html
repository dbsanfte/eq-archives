Welcome - 1/13/2005 3:50:00 PM

We are a family, taking all the bad along with the good. We may bicker, bute're always there for each other. We treat one another with dignity and
respect. Our family grows stronger each time one of us succeeds.
     Our Goals         


Moonlite Redemption was established for well seasoned players with the common and innate devotion to helping each other within our ranks. It is however encouraged that we help other players in Norrath, especially those with sincere need. We will enjoy EQ to the fullest with a devout sense of family ties within our guild. We have many individual and group goals, and we shall accomplish those goals as a team. Through questing, raiding, xp grouping, and flagging we will form a kinship that will be loved and honored by all who know us. We will uphold the rules of the land, touch the lives of many, and respect ourselves and others on our search for the righteousness that we will gain out of the battles we forge our friendships upon. 

~Masterfu~
