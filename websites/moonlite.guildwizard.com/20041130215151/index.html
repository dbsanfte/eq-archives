Welcome - 11/25/2004 9:15:00 PM

 

We are a family, taking all the bad along with the good. We may bicker, but we're always there for each other. We treat one another with dignity and respect. Our family grows stronger each time one of use succeeds.

 

         

 

Our main goal as a guild is to have fun. We may raid, but we still operate as a "family style" guild. Helping each other learn to play better is one of our top priorities, as even the most seasoned of players still has much to learn! Never be afraid to ask for help, that is what we're here for!
