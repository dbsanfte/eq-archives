The Rathe Games - 9/28/2004 12:00:00 AM

   
The Rathe Games    
Sunday, October mes10th, at 8pm cst, the Rathe Games will begin.  These games will be much like the old Seru Games, but they'll take place in Lake Rathetear, and The Arena.  Invite your friends, your foes, your aquantices, and potential members to meet us in Lake Rathetear at 8pm cst. 
The Scavenger hunt
The scavenger hunt consists of about 10 items you'll need to find throughout Norrath.  They're not located in any zone you need a certain level or flag to enter.  You may have a partner.  You're not permitted to enter the Bazaar, but you're allowed anywhere else in Norrath.
The Duel
This will be an open duel.  There will be 3 prizes for each of two categories: those above level 50, and those below it.  I don't care what alliances/agreements you make with anyone, just keep in mind that you'll have to be the very last one standing to win 1st place.
The Race
The race location will be announced just before the race begins.  Everyone will get sow, no bard speed, or soe is allowed.
