When was this album recorded?  Sometime in 1999.

Where was this album recorded?  In my basement with a computer, a mackie mixer, and not much more.

Who played the instruments and wrote the songs and recorded the music?  I wrote the music and played all of the instruments and did all of the mixing and recording.

Why haven't you recorded another album?  Damned if I know.  I'm lousy at hyping myself up.  I spend more time breaking things down.  I have 30-something songs lying around in unfinished states of disrepair.  Work in progress?
