About MMM Rebalance:

This mod modified various skrit to balance the spells in dungeon siege. Great care was takent to make each spell enticing to use, as well as have its own niche. This mod does not make you into a god - but it makes Dungeon Siege fun again! (at least it did for me)

All aspects of a spell were considered as well as it's relation to other spells of both magic classes in order to rebalance them. Some useless spells are now pretty good (the impetous for making this mod in the first place) and those that you wouldn't think of ever using were given a purpose (debuff spells, faster casting spells, heavier hitting spells, etc).

I've played a caster several times now to level 100 or so, and being a fairly active gamer, as well as designer, I thought it would be a great idea to resurrect this game by making magic spells how they were supposed to be. Unique, purposeful, and fun to look at ;)

The mod is still a work in progress as I haven't completed the damage dealing nature spells for the most part. I hope to get this done soon.

-Maendark
