Nameless Love Guild Charter

"Then sighing, said the other, 'Have thy will, I am the love that dare not speak its name. ' "

- from "Two Loves" written by Lord Alfred Douglas, printed December 1894

Purpose:

  • 1 - We provide camaraderie for male and female adventurers (and their supportive friends) who find their heart's love amongst their own gender.
  • 2 - We hold a celebration for each new member who joins the guild.
  • 3 - We provide occasional non-combat activities for entertainment.
  • 4 - We encourage members to roleplay their characters.
  • 5 - We strive to teach Norrathians the importance of sharing diverse strengths.

The Story of Souldreamer

During the time of the rabbit's moon, in the year of the Windy Harvest, a travelling storyteller entered our village in the mountains of the north. She entertained us with her wondrous stories, tales of adventures across the lands of Norrath. The entire village gathered to hear her words. Everyone was there; both our shaman and our tribal chief attended as well.

Among the tales that the woman told to us was a different kind of story. She called it a poem, and she told us that it was about "Two Loves". It was a sad story; it told of one Love which was misunderstood and silenced by another Love. After she finished this poem, our shaman left the village circle to walk among the mountains. The people of the village did not understand why he left before all of the stories were told, but we seldom understood the ways of the mystical shaman and so did not think much of his wandering.

A few days after the woman left to continue her journey, our shaman returned to the village and approached me. He told me that spirits followed the storyteller, and those spirits had spoken to him of an important need in the tribes of Norrath. Not just our tribe, he said, but all tribes. The people have forgotten the power of their diverse strengths, and they need a reminder of their value. Only the united tribes could survive an approaching conflict.

"Of what use is a strong arm or a fleet foot", he asked, "if it is no longer attached to the body? You know that a group of adventurers will die if they do not have one among them who is uniquely prepared for the each of the problems they encounter in battle. So too will devastation come to the lands if the people continue their way of separation from each other: separation by race, by gender, by guild, or by temperament. The people should gain strength in their diverse abilities, not strive to expel the various talents from their lives."

"You should go to Halas and begin to gather others who can help you teach this lesson across the land. You will need to gather people like yourself, berdaches and shay'a'chern and other two-spirits from each of the villages and cities. You understand your own worth in a way that other people rarely have to investigate, so you and your new family are uniquely suited to this particular challenge. You know from experience that an entire village is made stronger because of the differences found within its people."

"As you travel the lands, remember the story that the two-spirit bard brought to us. Her words will remind you that you must teach others to understand their true strength and show them how they can value the talents of others. Use her story to warn others of the danger of remaining silent. By living outside_of the common life, you and your guild can show the people of the lands all that is possible for themselves. Remember the closing words of the poem she told to us about the Two Loves..."

'...What is thy name?' He said, 'My name is Love.'
Then straight the first did turn himself to me
And cried, 'He lieth, for his name is Shame,
But I am Love, and I was wont to be
Alone in this fair garden, till he came
Unasked by night; I am true Love, I fill
The hearts of boy and girl with mutual flame.'
Then sighing, said the other, 'Have thy will,
I am the love that dare not speak its name.'

And so, with these words in mind, I ventured to the nearby city of Halas among the Everfrost Mountains. I began my search for other adventurers like myself, people of strong heart who happen to find love amongst their own gender. Does your life also lead you down this path, and will you join me in this new family as we travel the lands? It takes a daring soul to enter the dungeons in Norrath to fight the beasts within. Are you also brave enough to do so as a member in the guild of the Nameless Love? Your distinguished company is welcome, and adventure surely awaits us.
