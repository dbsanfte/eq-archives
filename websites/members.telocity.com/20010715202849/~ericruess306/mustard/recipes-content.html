Chicken
Cajun Chicken Burgers
Serves: 4

1 pound ground chicken
3 tablespoons dry bread crumbs
1 egg
3 medium scallions, chopped
1 tablespoon and 1 teaspoon cajun seasoning
1 tablespoon spicy brown mustard
2 teaspoons vegetable oil
4 slices Muenster cheese
1/4 cup barbecue sauce
Lettuce, sliced tomatoes, sliced red onion
hamburger buns

1. In medium bowl, combine chicken, bread crumbs, egg, scallions, 1 tablespoon cajun seasoning, and mustard. Shape into four 4-inch patties.

2. Heat oil in large nonstick skillet over medium-high heat. Cook patties 3 to 4 minutes per side until cooked through, reducing heat to medium if necessary. Top with cheese, cover, and melt.

3. Mix barbecue sauce and 1 teaspoon cajun seasoning. Serve burgers on buns with lettuce, tomatoes, onion, and sauce.

Crispy Chicken with Honey Mustard Sauce
Serves: 16

olive-oil nonstick cooking spray
1 3/4 cups walnuts
1 cup plain dried bread crumbs
1 1/2 teaspoons salt
1/4 to 1/2 teaspoon ground red pepper (cayenne)
2 large eggs, beaten
8 medium chicken-breast halves (about 3 1/2 pounds), skin removed
8 medium chicken drumsticks (about 1 3/4 pounds), skin removed
Honey-Mustard Sauce

1. Preheat oven to 450 degrees F. Spray two 15 1/2" by 10 1/2" jelly-roll pans with olive-oil cooking spray.

2. In food processor with knife blade attached, blend walnuts with 1/4 cup bread crumbs until walnuts are finely ground. Place nut mixture, salt, ground red pepper, and remaining bread crumbs in medium bowl; mix well.

3. In pie plate, with fork, beat eggs. Cut each chicken-breast half crosswise into 2 pieces. One at a time, dip chicken-breast pieces and drumsticks in beaten egg, then into walnut mixture to coat; place in jelly-roll pans. Spray chicken pieces with olive-oil cooking spray.

4. Bake chicken, on 2 oven racks, 30 to 35 minutes, rotating pans between upper and lower racks halfway through cooking, until chicken is golden brown and juices run clear when chicken is pierced with a knife.

5. While chicken is cooking, prepare Honey-Mustard sauce. Cover and refrigerate sauces if not serving right away.

6. Serve chicken hot with Honey-Mustard dipping sauce. Or, cool chicken slightly; cover and refrigerate to serve cold later.

Baked Honey-Mustard Chicken and Vegetables
Serves: 4

1 1/2 pounds small red potatoes, each cut into quarters
1 jumbo onion (1 pound), cut into eighths
6 teaspoons olive oil
4 medium chicken-breast halves, skin removed
2 tablespoons honey mustard

1. Preheat oven to 450 degrees F. In 13" by 9" metal baking pan, toss potatoes and onion with 4 teaspoons olive oil, 3/4 teaspoon salt, and 1/4 teaspoon coarsely ground black pepper; bake 25 minutes on middle oven rack.

2. Meanwhile, place chicken-breast halves in small roasting pan; coat chicken with 1 teaspoon oil. In cup, mix remaining 1 teaspoon oil with honey mustard; set aside.

3. After vegetables have baked 25 minutes, remove pan from oven and carefully turn vegetables with metal spatula. Return vegetables to oven, placing pan on lower oven rack. Place chicken on upper rack.

4. After chicken has baked 10 minutes, remove from oven; brush with honey-mustard mixture. Bake chicken and vegetables 12 to 15 minutes longer until juices run clear when thickest part of chicken is pierced with a knife and vegetables are golden and tender. Serve chicken with vegetables.

Ham
Brown Sugar Glazed Ham With Mustard Sauce
Serves: 24

1 fully cooked smoked whole ham (about 14 pounds)
1/2 cup packed brown sugar
1/2 cup honey Mustard Sauce

1. Prepare Ham: Preheat oven to 325 degrees F. Remove skin and trim some fat from whole ham, leaving about 1/4 inch fat.

2. Place ham on rack in large roasting pan (about 17" by 11 1/2"). Insert meat thermometer into center of ham, being careful that pointed end of thermometer does not touch bone. Roast ham 2 1/2 hours.

3. After ham has roasted 2 1/2 hours, prepare glaze: In 1-quart saucepan, mix brown sugar and honey; heat to boiling over medium-high heat. Boil 1 minute. When bubbling subsides, brush ham with some glaze. Roast ham 30 minutes to 1 hour longer, brushing occasionally with remaining glaze until thermometer reaches 140 degrees F.

4. Meanwhile, prepare Mustard Sauce. Cover and refrigerate until ready to serve.

5. When ham is done, place on large cutting board; let stand 20 minutes for easier slicing.

6. Serve ham with sauce.

Prosciutto Rollatini
Serves: 12

6 thin slices prosciutto or smoked ham, about 9 1/2 x 3 inches
3 tablespoons toasted onion mustard, or flavored mustard of choice
Freshly ground black pepper
2 ounces mozzarella, cut into about 24 2 x 1/4 inch strips
1/4 cup matchstick strips red bell pepper, each about 2 x 1/8 inches
1/4 cup matchstick strips fresh fennel, each about 2 x 1/8 inches
fresh fennelfronds (for garnish)

1. Place prosciutto slices on work surface; cut each in half lengthwise. Spread each strip with 3/4 teaspoon mustard, to cover; generously sprinkle with pepper.

2. At narrowest end of prosciutto, place one strip each mozzarella, red pepper, and fennel. Roll up tightly to contain. Garnish with small frond fennel. Serve immediately, or cover with plastic wrap and refrigerate up to 3 hours. If refrigerated, let stand at room temperature 10 minutes before serving.

Orange Ham with Horseradish-Mustard Sauce
Serves: 8

1 4-pound fully-cooked boneless ham
Horseradish-Mustard Sauce
4 large oranges
1/4 cup light corn syrup
2 tablespoons sugar
watercress sprigs for garnish

1. Place ham on rack in small roasting pan. Insert meat thermometer into center of ham. Bake ham in 325 degrees F. oven 1 1/2 to 2 hours until thermometer reaches 140 degrees F. (15 to 18 minutes per pound). If ham browns too quickly, cover with a tent of foil.

2. Meanwhile, prepare Horseradish-Mustard Sauce. Cover sauce with plastic wrap and refrigerate.

3. Prepare orange glaze: With knife or vegetable peeler, cut peel from oranges into long strips. Trim off white membrane from peel. Cut peel into matchstick-thin strips to make about 1 cup, firmly packed.

4. In 3-quart saucepan over high heat, heat orange strips and 1 1/2 cups water to boiling; cover and cook 15 minutes. Drain; rinse. In same saucepan over high heat, heat corn syrup and sugar until mixture boils and sugar dissolves. Add orange strips to corn-syrup mixture; over medium heat, heat to boiling. Reduce heat to low; simmer 10 minutes. When ham is done, spoon orange glaze over ham to cover.

5. If not serving ham right away, keep ham refrigerated. To serve, arrange ham on large platter. If you like, slice some of the ham ahead of time for easier serving. Garnish platter with watercress sprigs. Serve ham with sauce.

Marinades
Honey Mustard Dressing
Yields: about 2 cups

1 1/4 cups mayonnaise
1/3 cup honey
2/3 cup vegetable oil
1 tablespoon white vinegar
1 teaspoon minced onion flakes
2 tablespoons chopped fresh parsley
2 tablespoons yellow mustard

In a medium bowl, whisk together all the ingredients until smooth and creamy. Serve immediately, or cover and chill until ready to use.

NOTE: This is great tossed with spinach or mixed salad greens and also in seafood or chicken salads and over grilled chicken or steak.

Mustard Shallot Vinaigrette
Yields: about 1 2/3 to 2 cups

3/4 cup olive oil
2/3 cup red wine vinegar
3 tablespoons Dijon mustard
1 large shallot, minced
1 teaspoon salt
1 teaspoon coarsely ground black pepper
1 teaspoon sugar

In large jar (about 2 cups) with tight-fitting lid, combine all ingredients; shake well. Refrigerate if not serving right away. Bring to room temperature and shake well before using. Serve dressing with greens. Refrigerate any remaining dressing to use up within 3 days.

The Very Best Vinaigrette
Yields: about that much

3/4 cup extra virgin olive oil
2 tablespoons balsamic vinegar
3 tablespoons Dijon mustard
1 teaspoon lemon juice
1 large crushed garlic clove
1 dollop mayonnaise
a glass jar with a screw-on top

1. Combine all ingredients in jar.

2. Shake vigorously.

3. Add salt and pepper, if needed, to taste.

4. Enjoy drizzled over a Salad.

Can it be prepared ahead of time -- Yes, the flavors should have time to mingle and grow. Can be refrigerated up to 4 to 5 days before use. Give it a good shake before serving.
