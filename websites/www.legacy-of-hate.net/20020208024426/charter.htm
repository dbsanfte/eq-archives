THE HISTORY OF LOH

As told by Drael

To know where you are going, you must know where you came from.

Being a Dark Elf warrior and entering my teens, I decided to hunt Orcs in West Commons. Upon arriving, I met up with a magician named Darquan and a necromancer named Innana, both of Dark Elven blood. We fought and learned together, hunting anywhere we could, the Commons, Lavastorm and the Deserts of Ro. After a few days of grouping together, Darquan and myself talked about how creating a guild would be the thing to do. We sat for a few hours, both deciding on what to name the guild-Shadowlancer being a real life friend-I asked his opinion on a name, and he without thought came up with "Gaylords," so I left him out of the naming process.  It was then that Darquan said Legacy of Hate-no further brain storming was needed, the name had been chosen. Now, we needed ten people to help us create the guild and at the moment it was only I, Darquan, and Shadowlancer-Innana being a member of Denizens of Darkness at the time and after convincing her to relocate, we had four loyal members and still had six to go.  Shadowlancer sent a Dark Elf warrior named Zakers a tell whom had grown up with us and had him meet with me at the Neriak tavern where we talked about the new empire over a cold brew. He agreed to dedicate his life to the guild and we now had five.  Next was an enchanter named Amathistia and an Ogre named Clover-the last three members were nobodies from the chat lines.  It was the next day that Legacy of Hate came to life in Norrath.

Once the Legacy began, Darquan decided to recreate his character.  Darquan went into retirement and Saroz was created.  I too, decided to recreate my character Drael, a Dark Elf warrior of the seventeenth season, and deleted him.  Then Drael, the Dark Elven shadowknight, was born. Leadership was split between the both of us, but Saroz would hold the title by default. After his decision to leave, we decided on letting Shadowlancer hold the reigns to the empire. Shadowlancer, Rikraiz, and myself then tried a tri-leadership known as the Circle of Hate. Though it may have worked, Rikraiz and myself had no consistent schedule, which lead Shadowlancer to lead the empire alone. Many have their own versions of how the Legacy got started, but this is how it truly began.

From the start it was meant to be Evil only and to this day it continues to uphold that rule. Many have joined and many have defected but the true Haters are with us.

Purpose of Legacy of Hate

We gather together to form a united front against those races who have chosen to persecute us strictly based on our collective races. We do not seek to actively destroy those races that discriminate against us, but as well we do not shy away from battle.

For centuries we have been at the mercy of their blatent hatred against us. Now we the descendants of those before us are the result of their hatred.

We are the Legacy of Hate.

--Mujer De La Muerte

THE LEGACY AS IT STANDS TODAY

The Charter

This is what is expected from every Hater.

Once you are invited into our family, you have committed yourself to the Hate. To wear the Legacy of Hate tag does label you as a Hater, but it takes more than just a banner above your head to truly become one.  You must understand that a Hater is someone who will consider each of their guild mates as their brothers and sisters.  The fact that we are an all-evil guild is something to be proud of.  Therefore, we must treat our fellow Haters like we owe them a part of our existence, because without them, we would be just like the rest of the world.  We expect that everyone in the guild be loyal to the Legacy since all Haters have worked hard to get to where we are.

How to Join Our Family

A guideline to become a Hater.

There are only a few requirements to join us. The golden rule is: You must be an evil race--which means only Iksar, Dark Elf, Ogre, and Troll. You can be any class you want to play and can have as many alts you want in the guild. Also as important as the golden rule, if you choose to have different characters in different guilds, we will not honor you as a true Hater, which means you may never be qualified for officer status or have access to guild vault items. The only exception to having a character in a different guild is if that character is a light race. As it is no secret that we are a close-knit family, in order to join our ranks, you must group with LOH members for at least one game play. Have a member who knows you well enough to sponsor you. Make sure it's a Hater who you are in good terms with because they will be the ones to tell the inviting officer whether they think you would make a good addition to our guild. Lastly, we require all new members to feel at home as soon as they join--that is all we ask.

Participation

What to expect once you are in the guild.

We expect full participation of you once you are in the guild.  You shall attend events and raids whenever possible.  You are also expected to visit the message boards regularly as important information about the guild is constantly posted.  All the officers work hard in keeping the guild taken care of, so the message boards are the only way to effectively communicate.  There is also a calendar that will be accessible by all members, to post important dates such as birthdays, vacations, raids, events, and quest days.  There should be no excuse why everyone can't participate in our events and raids.  If we find that you are not participating enough, the officers will have a discussion about your removal. 

Also our guild has a wide spread distribution of levels, which means that there are officers who may be in your level range no matter what level you are.  There is always room for growth, which is why our level limits are not high.  We believe that in order for a guild to last as long as we have, we have to teach younger ones our ways so that the Legacy will continue to prosper.

How to Become an Officer of Hate

To become an officer, one must prove themselves not only loyal, but dedicated in the guild.  A member hoping to become an officer must be actively involved in helping fellow Haters in every way possible.  Over time, such efforts will not be ignored, and the current officers will decide upon a discussion whether that person has the qualities to become an officer of Hate.  Just because a member has been with the guild "for a long time," it doesn't mean they are entitled to a promotion.  To work hard in the guild, and do it because one just wants to be an officer, will only get them very far.  It is the pride that we take in helping other members that define the loyalty of our officers.

Other Issues

The Guild Vault Rules, Loot Distribution, Guild Donations, and Beggars.

There is a Guild Vault Forum in our message boards.  There is no actual guild vault, instead all members will act as a 'guild vault.'  When they have something to donate to the guild, they should post it up so that there is record of it.  Not doing so will result in other Haters not knowing what you have to offer for them.  All items posted in the Guild Vault Forum can be claimed by anyone who needs it.  Remember, it should be understood to pass an item on to another brother or sister when one has better equipment to use.  It is the responsibility of everyone posting in the Guild Vault to update his or her posts frequently with the "edit" option.

Keep in mind that the guild is still growing and is learning many, new things everyday.  When we do have the opportunity to raid for loot (items that are not obtainable by a single person), we will pass on the loot according to who is present at the raid and on a need basis.  Although with some exceptions, if there is a non no-drop item and a class best needing that item drops and who isn't present at the time, the attending party can hold it for that person.

We do not have time to waste for beggars, greedy people or stingy alike.  When any officers find that you are constantly begging for items or disrupting Guild Vault standards, you will be given a warning or an immediate removal depending on the circumstances. 

We are a very open guild, our chat is not censored in anyway so that our game play may be enjoyable for everyone.  Everyone knows when to cross the line.
