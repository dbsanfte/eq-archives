Guild Leadership  

Leadership is done by Council.  No one person should be in-charge of making big decisions that will affect the lives of many, nor should they have that burden on their shoulders. To this end, a council of four will be made to make most major decisions affecting the guild.  The guild leader is above but also part of the council for tie-breaking purposes.

Leader

The leader is in-charge of the guild as far as the EverQuest gods are concerned. The leader shall be responsible for acting on all decisions made by the council and casting the deciding vote in case of council ties. The leader has the privilege of making critical on-the-spot decisions on the behalf of the guild (in the guild's best interest).

Council

A party of four officers will vote on any and all major decisions within the guild brought forth from any and all channels. The guild leader shall be considered a member of the council while still holding executive power over the council.  

Council shall recommend changes to the guild and the bylaws as necessary and vote on them as appropriate.  

Council members are elected by officers or council members and voted in by all remaining council.

Class Leaders

Class Leaders are officers and/or regular members appointed by the guild council to be "the best of the best." These members are specially designated because of their "knack" at training or assisting members or recruits within their class.  

Specific Leadership Positions:

Raid Coordinator - In charge of promoting, coordinating, and providing information for guild raids.

Recruiting - In charge of answering all questions regarding membership and in sponsoring new recruits.       

Webmaster - In charge of running the web site and updating all necessary information there.

Trade Skill - In charge of providing current and up to date information regarding trade skills.

Treasurer - Will hold monies and potential guild equipment for use at a later time. If items cannot be used by members and are sold, these members will be in charge of the sales aspect. These members will also be in charge of holding items to be used in newbie-type events for distribution at a later time.
