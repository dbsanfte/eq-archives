Principles and Values  

Our goal is to become one of the more organized and friendship/team oriented guilds on the Morell Thule. To see all areas, destroy all mobs, and have fun doing it. These rules/guidelines are to make members feel they are in a fair environment. These rules/guidelines are here so that each member has the same view and goals as all other members and will enable success for our future. Time builds friendships, friendship builds stability, stability creates a successful guild. Your Acceptance to this guild is based on everyone's opinion.  This is to ensure the family community we already have and hope to maintain.

Membership in The Forgotten means that you agree to follow these basic rules of conduct. Most of these are common sense, and should take no effort for any honorable person to follow.

All officers have the ability to answer most questions regarding the guild and its policies. If an officer is not able to answer a specific question, then they will know who can and will direct you to that person.

GENERAL:

All officers have the ability to answer most questions regarding the guild and its policies. If an officer is not able to answer a specific question, then they will know who can and will direct you to that person.

Remember you are a representative of The Forgotten. You will conduct yourself in a mature but silly manner. This means things like shouting obscenities to the zone, intentional training, kill stealing, ninja looting, etc are not acceptable. 

Members are expected to take an active part in the guild.

We help our fellow guild members, but we also respect their right to play the game themselves. Guild members try to help each other out with teleports and resurrections when able, however guild members also use sound reason when in need of those favors. Use sound reason and remember!! They are doing YOU a favor. Be nice. Return the favor when needed. And use your head. ;) To be a martyr for the cause of good is both respected and condoned by the guild. Better to help when there is a chance you could have made the difference than let a fellow guild member die. Do NOT let fellow members die when you can help.

GUILD CHAT:

Guildchat is a public channel just the same as /ooc and /shout. Joking and fooling around ( i.e. guild bonding ) is encouraged.

Arguments, threats, continuous whining and complaining may be a /Guildremove offence. Any disagreements within the guild should/will be taken to /tell and if not able to be resolved addressed with any Officer and/or Aryel.

You may discuss issues with officers, but it is expected that you will treat all officers with due respect. Disregard of this rule is grounds for immediate removal from the guild. If a discussion creates conflict between a guild member and officer, it is suggested you move the discussion to a chat room with a neutral officer present to observe.

No obscene remarks. This applies to all communication channels. Not all members may be adults and those that are, may not want to read such things anyway.  If you have a conflict with a Forgotten member, officers cannot effectively intervene if you have dropped to the level of making obscene remarks.

While on raids and setting up events guildchat will be limited and in some cases closed off to all people in the guild.

GROUPING:

All The Forgotten members are encouraged to group with other members when ever possible. However this is not a requirement for guild membership.  You may group with who ever you want.

COMMUNICATION:

Guild Meetings:

There should be a monthly or all-hands guild meeting to communicate any current issues. The meeting should be in such a location as to be as central and neutral as is possible to all members. There should be at least one member of the Council present along with a majority of officers.

Generally following the meeting will be a guild raid, time permitting.

All scheduled and unscheduled guild meetings will be posted on the web site as well as in the guildmotd.

Members and officers will be notified of meetings through the guild mailing list whenever possible. We can not be held responsible for lost or delayed email.  

Council Meetings:

Council members are responsible for knowing what is going on within the guild and, to that end, should hold regular weekly or bi-weekly meetings to discuss any ongoing events, issues or problems. Part of being an officer is being able to commit to such a schedule.

GUILD TAGS:

Guild members are encouraged to wear their guild tags. We want our members to be proud of who they are. They are part of The Forgotten family. Always remember that the actions you take reflect upon all of us.

Guild members can use the /roleplay command in situations where they are constantly bugged for Teleports and resurrections.  

ALTS/TWINKING:

There will be times when people need a little break and wish to play around on a lower level character to relax. This is acceptable.

Alts are allowed in the guild regardless of level.

Their will be NO loot awarded to a main character’s twink or secondary unless the loot would rot otherwise or unless those characters are on the raid or at the guild swap

GUILD FUNDS/BANK:

Each member is neither required nor expected to pay any taxes to the guild, membership is absolutely free.  If a player wishes to make a donation this will be delivered to one of the officers and used for the common good of the members.  Donations will be distributed free to those in need.  Every once in a while when the guild coffers run out, we will embark in a joint adventure to fill them.  Players will again be expected, but not required to participate.

You are to contribute as well as benefit from the guild. That means this is NOT a one-way deal. As well as other guild members will be there for you we expect you to be there for them. Have a spare Lamentation? Maybe one of the new members can use it. Need a Lamentation? Perhaps the guild can provide.

The Guild Bank has many items that can be borrowed.  When you can no longer use an item, please return it to the bank so another may use it.  The list of bank items will be posted on The Forgotten Message Board.  The Treasurer, Aryel and Guild Council can access the Guild Bank.

ALLIANCES:

We are ever working on a Norrathian Alliance among some smaller guilds to prevent all the "uber" guilds from swallowing up everyone and leaving a few people out to dry (I laugh when I see that specific guilds will NOT take any Rangers below like 50+ level - WE welcome all levels 20+).

AMENDMENTS:

Amendments to these bylaws shall be made through the contacting a council member to get on the agenda for the Council meeting or by bringing such up at the regular guild meeting.

Amendments should be approved by a two-third majority vote of all guild members in attendance at a guild meeting or by a majority vote of the Council.  No group in either case may be represented by less than a quorum.
