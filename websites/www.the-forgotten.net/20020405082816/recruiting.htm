Recruiting  

Our membership is comprised of many different classes and levels and as such it is often challenging to find a group within the Guild in your level range, this is an inevitable result of a Guild with so many members at so many different places in the spectrum. Part of the reason this occurs is that your membership request will not be judged on your class and level, but by your relationships with guild members and their knowledge of you. The Forgotten is family, a weird family, but family nonetheless.

So many people think that EverQuest is about where you can go and what you can do. Well, it is about that, but it is also about having a good time with people you know.  Come see what we are about and group with some members. You will be pleasantly surprised.

          Membership is not automatic nor guaranteed.

Invitation of a Member

You must be of a sufficient level of experience. Although we have some members under the current level limit, we have decided to implement a level limit of level 20 for new recruits. Further we reserve the right to change this limit from time to time.

We require that you group with other members of The Forgotten before an invitation to join is offered, preferably with an officer.

New recruits may apply to the guild by any one of the following means. 

Petition the guild leader, an officer or council member. This officer shall be deemed a sponsor.

Speak to a guild member.  The recommending member is deemed the sponsor.

New recruits must post a message to the "Membership Applications" message board with the at least the following information: class, race, level, tradeskills, and a few words about their character.  

New members can be added (/invite'd) to the guild by approval of Aryel or in her absence two or more officers.

New recruits should be announced through guild say and the guildmotd.

Once you are in, you are part of the family and we take every precaution to ensure the happiness and friendly feeling within the guild.

Removal of a member

If a guild member is accused of violating any of our rules, that member will be considered innocent until proven guilty of the charges, Guild officers will investigate any claims of wrong doing. Should the party in question be proven to be in violation of our rules and ethics, the guild leader and officers will decide what punishment is appropriate not members. Please note that actions considered serious will result in removal from the guild.

Removal of a member is a last resort, extremely serious action and the Council must be in complete agreement. Forceful removal from the guild/family is a stiff penalty that is taken very seriously. The council will review all information available, hold a vote and make their recommendations to the leader. The leader shall be responsible for removing the member should that be the decision of the council.

Duties of the Sponsor:

A sponsor is a guild member who has talked with the recruit and gotten to know them. The sponsor should be willing to state that this is a good person, I have gotten to know them and can attest to their good character and vow that they will be a good addition to our guild.

The sponsor is the point of contact for any guild-related information a new member might need. The sponsor can help make the new member's adjustment to the guild easier.

If any member has a problem with a new member then go to their sponsor. Talk to the sponsor about your problem or questions then the sponsor can have a chat with the new recruit about the behavior or action in question. It is the whole intent to keep things on a friendly level.
