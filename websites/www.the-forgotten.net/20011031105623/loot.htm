L o o t  P o l i c y  

As many of you know, we have avoided the point system in favor of rolls/leader decisions and sound individual judgment.  There are many reasons for this, including the fact that many of us are casual players with family, jobs, classes, kids, etc that don’t allow us to play 24/7 and therefore can’t obtain the kind of points necessary for uber loots, since a point system favors those of us addicts who ignore all else and play anyway. Not to mention the administrative nightmare.

Having said that, our current system only works if people are fair generous and defer when others are in greater need.  For that reason, outlined below are some dos and don’ts so you know what the guild expects of you when it comes to loot.

On all raids loot will be distributed by the Guild Council or the Loot Officer.  Consideration for making such decisions will be dependant on a need versus greed basis. 

All decisions are final and are not open for discussion. Arguing, complaining, whining over loot is not acceptable.

We are still examining various loot issues in an effort to minimize loot disputes.  While we are examining these issues and until further notice loot will be handled at guild events as follows:

1.      If an item drops, all classes who are listed on the item as able to use the item will be able to roll on that item if they choose and if they do not have better.  However you may only roll on an item your character on that raid will actually use and equip.

2.      Please note that selling any item won on a guild raid is very disfavored, the preferred course of action is to always hand down an item you replaced to another guild member of your choosing or to Raid Leader or the Guild Council to be distributed to another guild member.

3.      You are also expected to exercise sound and generous judgment and to defer on items which, while you can technically use as stated by Verant on the item, is really more appropriate for another class. For example a armor piece drops with 25 ac and 30 cha and it is melee and clerics, while under these rules all who can and will use may roll if they choose, we would expect almost everyone to defer to bards since the +cha is clearly best for them.  Again this is NOT an absolute rule but a suggested exercise of your judgment.  If you wish to roll and will use the item and you are listed as a useable class by Verant then you are within your right to roll on that item under these rules and we will not tolerate anyone bad mouthing you because you choose to be in.

4.      Similarly while we do NOT have a seniority system, you are expected to exercise sound and generous judgment and defer on items that are last pieces or clearly the only item sought repeatedly by a guild mate, or one of the few pieces left in the game for that person.  For example, if a helmet drops on a raid which is one of the last three pieces a warrior needs, all melee who need it will be allowed to roll on it if they choose and no pressure or repercussion will be had if you do, on the other hand sound judgment suggests that you defer if you win and allow the warrior to hand down an item to you instead. This makes sense for several reasons: (1) the better equipped our higher levels are the better we succeed on raids; (2) the higher level who receives an item will pass down the replaced item which will still be a very nice item for the guild; and (3) many of the raids at which great items drop would not be even within our reach but for well equipped high level players.  Now do NOT misread this, there is no automatic deferral rule, you will be allowed to roll if you choose and that is your right under these rules.   We only ask that you be generous, and consider deferring on occasion.

5.      At each event there will be a designated loot officer, ie person handling loot for that raid, which may or may not be the raid leader or the guild council, all decisions by that person will be final.  If there is a serious objection you may ask that person to submit the issue to the Guild Council.  However, unless the Guild Council overturns that decision --which will be extremely RARE-- the loot decision is final. Whining about loot decisions and causing loot disputes will be cause for immediate guild suspension or removal. Again do NOT misunderstand, we welcome constructive ideas and discussion and suggested improvements on how loot should be handled, please post those ideas on the board in a non-flaming, constructive manner and they will be read and considered.  However griping about a particular item during or after a raid is unacceptable. We have seen too many guilds get too big and greedy and fall apart over loot. Accordingly, such rants will be grounds for guild removal.

6.      Once you have received an item on a raid, you are out unless no one else wants the item, all defer to you or all have received an item in your class and we move to round two of loot for that class.

7.      Spells drops do not count as loot, spell drops are handled as follows: if u are on the raid and u do NOT have the spell and u can scribe it, you get it hands down, if more than one in that category, then roll.  If no one can scribe it, then it goes to roll among that class present. However again sound and generous judgment will suggest that you may wish to defer to the person who is most likely going to be able to scribe it first. For example a level 60 spell drops and no one can scribe it, so it.  It is fully rolled among the class and a level 54 wins, that person may in sound judgment choose to defer it to the level 57 person on the raid or defer it to the guild spell bank to be given to the first person who can scribe it or the person may choose to exercise their right under this rule to simply roll, win and keep it.  All spells which are not usable by anyone present will go to the spell bank for the guild's benefit. The preferred course here is to get spells into the hands of those who can actually use them NOW rather than have them sit in a bank account for the simple reason that it helps the guild more if we have all our spells we can use and we will likely get another by the time you get to the level to use it.

8.      On multi guild raids (defined as raids where non guild members are present), Aryel or Raid Leader will continue to negotiate loot rules on a raid by raid basis. Be forewarned that any and all of these rules may be suspended at any time for purposes of a multi guild raid. There are simply somethings that require other guilds to assist and when they do, Aryel or Raid Leader will negotiate and/or elect to implement different loot rules than these posted in order to facilitate a good joint raid.

9.      Droppable loot will be handed out at convenient breaks through out the raid.

10.  You may only be in on loot for the character you have present. The only exception is if the raid leader or the Guild Council specifically asks you to bring another character for the raid and we agree in advance that you may loot for the other character. For example we are low on clerics, you are at the raid with your tank, we beg you to get your cleric, you do but wish to get items for your tank, we agree and you are OUT on cleric items.  Such special deals will be rare and will be announced in advance whenever possible.  You may not ever loot for more than one character on a single raid unless the item would otherwise rot.

11.  Gems will go to the Guild Bank.  Gems can be purchased from the bank for a reasonable fee.  We will post a price list in the guild bank forum.  Again need before greed.  Gems are used by many classes.  Other classes beside enchanters use gems.  Mages need certain gems for their focus items.  Warriors need gems for their epic quest.  Gems can still be claimed by everyone.  Excessive abuse of the guild bank will result in a suspension of privileges by the guild council. 

12.  The last and foremost rule in this guild on loot is fairness, generosity, and respect.  Need Before Greed.  We should all endeavor to be fair, generous and respectful. Your turn will come and when it does we will be happy for you, so share and rejoice in all of us getting better!!!

ALTS/TWINKING:

There will be times when people need a little break and wish to play around on a lower level character to relax. This is acceptable.

Alts are allowed in the guild regardless of level.

Their will be NO loot awarded to a main character’s twink or secondary unless the loot would rot otherwise or unless those characters are on the raid or at the guild swap.
