G u i l d   R a i d s  

All The Forgotten members are encouraged to attend all guild raids whenever online for their appropriate level.

All raids will be setup and lead by Officers. There are a million and 1 things that everyone wants to do. Raids will be organized in order of importance and capabilities.  Suggestions should be forwarded to the Master Raid Officer or your Class Leader.  We understand that people have lives outside of EverCrack (errr, EverQuest) and not all leaders may be available for all raids. To that end, one Council member should always be available and present during each official raid. Raids will start small (with the likes of Guk and Cazic Thule) and end up big in times to come (like clearing out Kithicor Forest at night or killing Lady Vox).

Raid etiquette will be very important. How well and fast we can organize will determine how successful we will be. If there is a planned raid ahead with a day+ notice every individual is expected to be camped out, or bind near the location of the raid for the following day. All grouping for raids will be organized by the Master Raid Officer, the Guild Council, or their designee.

For raids all groups will have an assigned "Group Leader". Each Group Leader will have responsibilities. They will be dependant on to inform everyone in a public channel that group’s status (i.e. mana). They will be expected to answer all questions for the other people in that specific group. If they are unable to answer a question it will be up to the group leader to ask outside the group, and ONLY the group leader.
