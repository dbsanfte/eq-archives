C h a r t e r

The Forgotten is a guild built upon the foundation and belief that Everquest should be fun. Making friends, chatting, helping others, and grouping together are what we are about. Giving to and helping other guild mates is a belief, not a rule. Officers work to incorporate the views and opinions of the guild members in which they serve.

The Forgotten is a guild built upon good natured people, NOT necessarily upon good players.  Learning how to play EverQuest can be taught, while having the mettle to be a member of The Forgotten is genetic.

As a member of The Forgotten you are...

       Expected to work as a team player. Help and build strength for all other members when ever possible. Every person has a contribution to make. There is no class not needed and that cannot be utilized.

       Expected to treat all members as you would your brothers and sisters. Build relationships and trust that will enable a group to achieve any goals they set their mind to. Accepting both the good and the bad moments.

       Expected to teach people well.  Rather than complain about all the problems facing the players of Norrath, we should lead-by-example and try to teach others proper methods of fighting (the in-game character) as well as proper conduct (the out-of-game player). We should help teach others how to effectively use the skills that they have, thereby assisting in the fight against the evils of out land.

       Expected to encourage good deeds throughout the land.   Much like Random Acts, we'd like to be kindly-considered across the lands of Norrath. This is not saying that we can't accept donations, but good should prevail over financial gain (for example).

       Expected to Acquire Knowledge.  Effort should be made amongst guild members to make sure that other members are able to acquire the best reasonable equipment and supplies as possible. This is not to say that one player should coddle another, but we should help each other out with general niceties and the like (eg. bringing back supplies from distant lands for our casters and master craftsman (possibly for reasonable compensation), helping out newbies, etc). In short, we want to be there for our guild mates and, to that end, help each other prosper and stay healthy.

                    Expected to have as much fun as possible and in good company.
