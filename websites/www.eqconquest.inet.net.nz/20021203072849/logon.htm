Logon Problems
You have probably got Logon to Network ticked in your dialup preferences. To fix this just double click on My Computer then on Dialup Networking then click with the right mouse button on the inet icon and select Properties from the menu list and click on Server types. Just untick Logon to Network as shown below and click on Ok to save the changes and you should logon normally again.
