Hosting your webpage
Note: You must be a customer of iNET to be able to host your web site with us.

Before you can put your web pages on our web server, you need to get an File Transfer Protocol (FTP) Program to upload the files to us. We highly recommend using CuteFTP which is available from our ftp server here (1.2 megabytes).

Once you have downloaded CuteFTP, double click on the file that you have saved, and this will install it. Just follow the on screen instructions, its fairly simple.

Now that CuteFTP is installed, you can start it up. It will bring up a menu of sites you can connect to.


(click image to enlarge)

Click on the "Personal FTP Sites" option, and press the "Add" Button. This will bring up the an Add Site window. Fill in the details as follows.

- Site Label: iNET Webserver
- Host Address: ftp.inet.net.nz
- User ID: Your username
- Password: Your password
- Initial Remote Directory: /public_html
(Note: your username is the first part of your email address, so if your email is bob@inet.net.nz your username is bob)


(click image to enlarge)

Now press OK. This will bring you back to the connection window. You will see on the right side of the window, your iNET Webserver connection is now listed.


(click image to enlarge)

Highlight the connection and press the Connect button. If everything is set correctly, you will now be logged into the server. If you have any errors, edit your connection and make the appropriate changes. When you are connected, it should look something like the following.


(click image to enlarge)


Looking at CuteFTP, you have your local hard disk in the left window, and our web server in the right window. So, in the left window change to where your webpage documents are stored, select them, and holding down the left mouse button, drag the files to the right window, and release. You will now be asked if you really want to upload these files to your web site. Select Yes, and your web site will be transferred to our web server.


(click image to enlarge)

Make sure that all of your file names are in lower case, as our web server IS case sensitive. Once you have uploaded your web site, you will need to make sure that one of the html files is called index.html. This is the page that is defaultly loaded by our web server when someone goes to your address. Load your web browser, go to the location bar and type in the following address http://www.inet.net.nz/~yourname but replace yourname with your username.

If at any stage you get stuck following these instructions, please feel free to call our office on 377-4103 (Mon-Fri 9am-6pm) or email help@inet.net.nz

If you are interested in getting a domain or subdomain, please refer to our pricing page for further details.
