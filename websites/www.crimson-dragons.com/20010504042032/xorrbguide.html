The Gorge of King Xorrb ( Beholder Maze )

by Vanzant Bravesword

INTRO

    During my adventures in Norrath, I have had the distinct pleasure of learning the ins and outs of the Beholder Maze. This maze is a huge canyon with high rock walls and shear cliffs. It contains many a rare beast and treasures that can make you easily wealthy. "The Gorge" as many of us have come to call it, is a very diverse zone, wich contains creatures for a wide range of levels. If you are looking for a profitable and fun zone to hunt in, I highly recommend it!

LOCATION

    The Gorge is located in fairly close proximity to the city of Rivervale. I would reccomend that you bind in RV if you plan on hunting there. From RV, you just take the tunnel across from the bank to Misty Thicket. Follow the trail through the thicket, make sure you keep to the right and you will come to a small gate with two Halfling Deputies. From the gate, head straight out through the forest until you hit the zone wall. You should hit the wall rather close to the goblin camps. Follow the bottom of the wall to the left and you will run into the tunnel that leads to the Runnyeye Citadel. Zone through to Runnyeye and stay tight against the right wall. Follow the right wall around several turns and twists and you will come to an alcove wich leads to the Beholder Maze. Watch out near the alcove, because it is a very common place for adventurers to lead very large trains to. You will usualy be able to pick it out by the people sitting there resting. This zone is the first of two that leads into The Gorge.

    You may also reach the gorge from East Karanas if you are traveling in that direction. From the bridge in EK, head straight out through the plain until you run into the zone wall. Then follow the wall left and you will pass through a small merchant village and then come to the gorge entrance. The entrance has a smooth, rocky appearance and is rather large. You may have to run down the pass aways before you zone into The Gorge. This EK zone is important to remember, as it will be a very important escape route for you if you ever need to zone in a hurry.

CREATURES and STRATEGY

    Goblins- There are three types of Goblins in The Gorge. Ranging from least to highest difficulty, they are Goblin Lookouts, Goblin Sentrys and Goblin Veterans. These Goblins are great to hunt in a group from levels 7-10. Around 10th level you should probably even be able to solo them with caution. These goblins are located all throughout the maze, but they are most commonly seen at 3 spawn points right outside the Runnyeye Zone. Loot on these goblins isn't extremely valuable, although I have seen Goblin Veterans loot an occasional Bronze Sword. A great strategy is for the group to stay close to the zone and let one person lure goblins there. Playing my Ranger, I was able to gain 6th to 9th level there with a group of 2-3 people. Just don't be afraid to zone early on if you pull too much, and be very careful to avoid the trains of Muddites and Minotaurs that get pulled frequently to that zone.

    Muddite Minors- These "muddies" are quite prevalent in The Gorge. They can be found wandering up and down the passageways almost anywhere in the gorge. After level 10, you should be able to kill these creatures easily with a good group. You can lure them to either the RE or the EK zone, neither is safer that the other. The disadvantage to hunting muddites is that they are very aggressive and will attack you on sight, as will the majority of creatures in The Gorge. They often join in on a fight unexpectedly and they hit extremely hard for their level. They also have the ability to throw mud in your eyes, temporarily blinding you. The major advantage to fighting muddites is that they are extremely slow. You will take very little damage while pulling them, and if you aren't to low on health, you can easily outrun them if you're in trouble. The loot on these creatures can be small blocks of clay, used in pottery skills and small pieces of ore, used in smithing.

    Muddite Elders- The "Elders" are of the same make as the minors although they are slightly larger. These creatures have the same abilities to blind as their smaller counterparts and also posess the same crushing blows that seem to do way more damage than most creatures at their level. These creatures are good to fight around level 12-15 in a good group. The loot on these guys can is variable. They carry large block of clay (pottery), large pieces of ore (smithing) and the occasional silver, electrum, gold or platinum bar used in jewelry making. Always be careful when fighting these creatures, because the lesser muddites and goblins are always proned to join in on the fights.

    Minotaurs- Minotaurs are an incredible source of experience in the Gorge. There are two types of Minos, weaker Gorge Minotaurs and the deadly Chasm Minotaurs. From my experience, a well sized and equipped group is safe hunting Gorge Minotaurs at around 16th level. I would recommend pulling the Minos to the zones to fight. I would also advise you to avoid the Chasm Minotaurs, who usualy range from level 23-25. Around 18th level, it is safe to begin camping the throne room, wich I'll describe later. At this point it shouldn't be too hard for a full group to bring down Chasm Minotaurs with relative ease. The loot on Minotaurs is usualy a fair ammount of cash and the possibility of Minotaur Horns or Minotaur Battle Axes. The Mino axes are favorite weapons for low level players in EQ and can sell for around 4-5 PP on the open market.

    Evil Eyes- Evil Eyes are possibly the most feared creature in The Gorge. The Eye itself is not all that difficult to defeat, but it has the ability to charm a character, making you lose control of yourself and attack your friends. You can slow yourself down by sitting and standing repeatedly, but otherwise you will be charmed until someone dispells you or the effect wears off. The evil eyes also have pets that look much the same as an enchanters pet. These arent too hard to kill. Evil Eyes don't loot especialy well, and are killable after 17th or 18th level. Just remember, its not a very nice thing when you kill your friends who have been charmed by the EE... so avoid it at all costs.

CAMPING and SPAWN SPOTS

    The best camping spot in The Gorge of King Xorrb, other than the RE and EK zones, is an area referred to as the Throne Room. This Room contains several rare spawns and is a spot where you can level extremely quickly if you use proper caution. To get to the Throne Room you take the left path coming out of RE. Stay on the left wall to avoid the lesser monsters and roaming Minos and EEs. Keep following the path, and you sill see a wide passage that vears to the right. Ignore that passage and stay straight. Begin to pay close attention to the right side of the trail as you progress. The next pass you will see is a very narrow one in the right wall. That passage inclines downward into the Throne Room. At the entrance to the throne room there is a spawn point for a Minotaur on each side. The common spawn is a Gorge Mino, but chasms spawn quite often as well. The entire room is shaped like a large circle, with a Giant "T" shaped alter in the center. As you enter the room with the mino spawn points on either side, you will be facing the flat part of the T. A good idea is to sit against the T and pull each mino there to fight as they spawn. The spawn's are often spread out and the respawn rate is extremely quick, so you can gain a lot of experience here with a good group. On either side of the T, is the spawn point for an Evil Eye. Sometimes the evil eye will be a regular one, and sometimes it will be a named one. You should always make sure these eyes are camped, because if they aren't, they become a real problem when you are trying to camp Minos. Often times, groups of wizards sit along the outside wall of the room to camp these eyes.

RARE SPAWNS AND LOOT

    Lord Svirr and Lord Syrkl- These are the two named Evil Eyes that spawn in the throne room. These eyes are extremely nasty and can cause major problems if they arent taken out quickly after they spawn. These eyes are good to hunt for 20th level and up players. The eyes are known to drop Polished Bone Bracers on occasion, an item that brings quite a few magic users to The Gorge.

    Qlei- Qlei is a Giant Muddite who spawns somewhere in the gorge. I have only seen him on a few occasions when he was trained to the zone by fleeing players. The one chance I had to kill him, he looted a Large Block of Ore and a Silver Bar. I'm not sure what the con was, so you will need to be careful in attacking this guy.

    Spin Flint- An extremely rare spawn that looks a lot like one of the Rock Dervishes in Lavastorm. SpinFlint is killable around level 18 and drops some type of special jewel that supposedly increases magic resistance. I'm not sure where this creature spawns and during all my time in the gorge I have only heard of one sighting.

     Brahhm- This is a named Minotaur who spawns randomly and wanders around the zone. He is slightly smaller than a Gorge Mino and is killable around level 18 or 19. The Minotaur drops a special Lore Item called Brahhm's Horn that is used in some kind of quest for Bards.

    Yyimp the Infernal- Yyimp the Infernal is a small creature that looks like a winged demon who hovers just above the ground. I have heard rumors that he spawns in the throne room, but both of the times I have seen him, he was roaming the passages throughout the zone. It is an extremely rare spawn and drops the Minotaur Ribcage, wich is a very valuable piece of armor for the chest area. This creature conned blue to me at level 18.

    King Xorrb- Now, I have never seen King Xorrb and have only heard him casting spells as I passed through the zone early during the morning. I have heard rumors that he spawns in the Throne room whenever both Lord EEs are present, all the Minotaur spawns are present and there is no one in the room. I tend to believe this as I have heard it several times and have never seen King Xorrb when someone is in the room. The loot on King Xorrb is rumored to be nothing significant.

MAP and MORE INFO

     The great folks at Everlore have a fairly well done map of the area as well as some other information on the Gorge.

SUMMARY and ADVICE

    All in all, it is quite possible to go very far in this zone. The biggest thing to remember is not to get discouraged when you die. There are a lot of circumstances in the gorge that will cause a player and a party to be overwhelmed and dieing is something you have to get used to. Always be prepared to run, and never be afraid to sound the retreat if you feel you have bitten off more than you can chew. Once you begin to learn the ins and outs of the zone, it is one of the most lucrative and fun zones in the game. Patience, Caution and Strength in Numbers is the way to succeed in The Gorge!
