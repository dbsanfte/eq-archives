Born in the wilds of the Karanas Tinaran was taught at an early age by his father and the treants to respect everything in nature.

His facination with magics and the mystery of the ruins throughout the land influenced his move to Halas the city of the barbarians to learn more than just natural magic from the shamans.

Once becoming a man Tinaran has traveled the land seeking knowledge and a belonging unlike most druids. At one with nature but completely at peace in a city or a deep dungeon he welcomes all the mystery and magic that is Norrath.
