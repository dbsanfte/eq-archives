Raised by the followers of the Warlord, Axemdraike had been predesignated in the ogre society to be a shaman. Little did they know the resourcefulness and cunning he would grow up with. After exhausting the ranks of the lizards that plagued his forest, he moved on to see the rest of his world. Wittingly, he helped all the other races he found in their wars to make himself welcome in their ranks. Elves and Humans alike no longer shudder when they see him approach. They will even do business with him and let him use their banks.

They are too foolish to know that all he is doing is getting on the inside. But one day their folly will be made known to them, when they are not expecting it at all. Their homes will be burned and the children slaughtered, for the might of the Warlord is supreme.
