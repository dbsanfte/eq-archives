Dragon's Inn




Welcome in from a long journey across the lands of Norrath. The Inn is an escape for members and friends of the Crimson Dragons to plot future adventures, trade stories of cunning and battle and heal their wounds. Worry not, because here you are among friends and the food and ale is of unlimited supply. The Lounge has set up some priveleges for its members. Pull up a seat and trade stories of cunning, bravery and adventure in our Chatroom. Perhaps you would rather test your wits or cast your ballot in our Weekly Poll & Quiz section.

Walk over to the bookshelf and peruse some of the many Member's Writings that are available. While at the bookshelf, you may choose to look back at all the famous Dragons that have been recognized in the Dragon of the Week section. Also archived is our Quote of the Week. View the many battle scars and bruises on your fellow guildmates in the Gallery. Above up, enjoy your stay !!
