The history of Steelmantis PrayingFist is truly an odd one. Most think it's quite unbelievable when they hear it. However, one does not choose how our lives start. Fate does that for us. We just choose where to go from there.

It was two Dark Elves on a journey through the West Karanas that found a human child the only survivor of a werewolf attack. The child's parents were slain. The two Dark Elves, a man and a woman, had recently lost a son by the hands of humans. Seeking some sort of revenge, the Dark Elf couple took the child with a plan in mind. They would take this human child as a replacement for their own lost son and raise him to someday betray the humans in some manner.

After secretly raising this human child for some years the Dark Elves were finally confident in sending him out on his mission to betray the humans. Steelmantis was the boy's new name as he arrived in Qeynos to train with the Monks there. The plan of his foster parents were to have him train with them and learn the arts of the human Monks. Once finished, he would return and share the mysterious Monk fighting techniques with the rest of the Dark Elves.

After years and years of training in Qeynos, Steelmantis grew to become more comfortable with his fellow humans in the city. It was different than growing up alone where his foster parents wouldn't let him interact with other Dark Elves. After time he started to question his own mission to some day betray his guild masters. He had grown a sense of loyalty to his own Human peers.

Eventually his own master sensed the conflict Steelmantis was having. The master asked what the source of his inner conflict was. Steelmantis tried to lie at first, but the master couldn't be fooled. The master can see every tell tale mannerism of a man who lies. Finally Steelmantis told him of his mission from his foster parents. Surprisingly, the master was not upset. He found the whole thing quite fascinating.

After talking about it at quite some length, the master finally told Steelmantis that in the end it will be his own choice as to what to do. The master told him that unlike most of his students, Steelmantis has two opposing essences within. The Dark Elf way of thinking and the Human training of Monks. He will have to find inner balance before he can make a choice.

So, taking his master's advice with him, Steelmantis leaves Qeynos to explore the rest of Norrath. He hopes that through experience of seeing as much as he can see, he will find balance and someday choose which side to join. The evil or the good.

In the meantime, the second born and true blood of his foster parents, Snowtyger Winterknight is given orders to keep an eye on Steelmantis. He must not be allowed to abandon his original mission. He must be made to see that if he decides to betray the Dark Elves instead of the Humans, then it will cost him dearly.
