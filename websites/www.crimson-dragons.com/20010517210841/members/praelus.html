Praelus was born in Freeport, son of one of the richer families of the Coalition of Tradesfolk. From an early age, he was told to only help himself and those who would be able to return the favor... manyfold, to remember that he was much better than the "riff raff" and that he should never let those lower than him forget that. This never did sit well with him, but it was all he was ever taught.

When Praelus was 12 years old, his father began to spend more time away from home. Sometimes, Praelus's father would come back home and act very nervously for week or so, then go off again for another few days. Then, one day, as Praelus was on a business trip with his mother to the docks in eastern Freeport, he accidentally became seperated from her, and found himself in the slums. Wandering aimlessly, and somewhat scared, young Praelus happened upon a begger, just as another man in a black cloak snuck past. Seeing the cloaked man, the begger asked for a few spare coppers, when the man in black flew into a rage, unsheathed two daggers and brutally murdered the begger. Too terrified to move, Praelus watched was the murderer walked nonchalantly over to a water barrel to wash his hands. The cloak's hood got in the way while the man tried to clean himself, so he brushed it back, and Praelus let out a scream when he saw the slayer was his father. The man looked up at Praelus, and beckoned him to come near... but the terrified lad ran.

Praelus ran through all of Freeport, not sure of where to go. Eventally, he found himself in North Freeport, and it was getting dark. Still running, Praelus suddenly bumped into an armored figure. Even in the faint light given by street lamps, the man seemed to gleam and shine bright as some star at night. The man was Valeron Dushire, leader of the Knights of Truth, and he took Praelus to the Hall of Truth. Praelus told Valeron what he saw, and the Paladin offered Praelus shelter among the Paladins and Clerics of the Marr twins, Errolisi and Mithaniel.

As Praelus grew, Valeron realized that the boy's heart shined with light. Even having lived all his life with a greedy and degenerate family, Praelus showed kindness, compassion, and a strong desire for truth. Merkon Quetalis, a trainer and the tester of Truth, finally asked Praelus if he would like to train to be a squire, and perhaps some day become a knight of truth. Naturally, Praelus happily accepted.

Finally, the time came for Praelus's training to end, and he was sent forth into the world as a Squire of Truth. Praelus journeyed to the city of Men and Half Elves on Antonica's western shores, Qeynos. There, he became fast friends with the town baker, Voleen Tassan. Praelus asked Voleen to teach him her trade, and she agreed. Since then Praelus has always specialized in baking all manner of pies.

Eventally, Praelus realized it was time for him to take the test of truth, so he returned to Freeport. After a difficult quest, Praelus emerged from the test successful, and Valeron watched with pride and Merkon pronounced Praelus a knight of truth, and gave him the title "Braveblade".

Praelus has had many other quests since then, as well as trials and troubles. Through it all, he has always been there, sword in had, and pie in belly, ready to take on whatever evil may show itself.
