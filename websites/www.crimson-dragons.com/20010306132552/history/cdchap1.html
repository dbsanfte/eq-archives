The History of the Crimson Dragons

  

Chapter I - The Rise of Garath
————————
The first Dragon was born into a working class family, in lands where only wealth and power were used to determine one's worth.........

It was a prosperous time for the young city of Freeport. Merchants from all of Norrath gathered there to pawn their goods and enjoy the company of others like themselves. As a bustling trade city, Freeport was truley a success... but where there was trading, there was money... and where there was money, there were always the unsavory characters that followed it. Freeport was divided between the government, the traders and the criminals. Caught in the middle of it all, were the small families of laborers, sailors and craftsmen who called Freeport their home.

It was from one such family that Garath was born. Son of Freeport's hardest working blacksmith, Garath was brought up to know that hard work really did pay off in the end. His father taught him that nothing was more important in the world than to be a man of character. "Without character," his father used to say, "a man is only a face in the crowd." Garath rememberd those words always, and learned to view people based on their character more than on what they owned or how much money they had. That trait would eventualy lead young Garath to become the leader of one of the most remarkable groups of people to ever exist in the world of Norrath.

Garath had always worked in his fathers smithy since the day he was old enough to carry the large bricks of ore from the store rooms to the forge. This had made him a strong young man, and had taught him that a little bit of pain at the end of the day was well worth it if the job he sat out to do was complete. When Garath was old enough, he became fascinated with the Freeport docks. He took a job with the local harbor master when he was 16 and was responsible for going to each new ship that docked and signing them in. Garath loved meeting people from new places and new lands, and eventualy made many friends amongst the Captains and sailors of many ships.

One problem that all of Garath's seagoing friends complained about was piracy. The many islands in the Ocean of Tears were safe havens for brigands, pirates and cutthroats that often posed severe problems for ships passing through. Garath, concerned with this problem, approached the Captain of Freeport's City Guard and asked what he could do to stop the villains who plagued the passing ships headed to Freeport. The Captain agreed with Garath and began to form a group of guardsmen to venture into the Ocean of Tears to fight the pirates. Since the Captain was busy maintaining order amongst the growing population of Freeport, and Garath had the most knowledge of the different islands in the ocean... he was placed in charge of leading this party.

Garath was no expert in the ways of combat, but he had learned some tricks from his sailor friends and new how to handle weapons from his many days working at his father's forge. He gladly accepted his new position and lead the group of warriors with vigor. On one of his first patrols on the islands off the coast of Antonica, Garath and his men encountered a fearsome group of pirates lead by a man known as Sarnas. Sarnas was a villain in every sense of the word and Garath had heard stories of the many sailors he had killed while raiding their ships. Garath's troop watched the deadly pirate and his men in their island camp for several hours before they finaly decided to make their move.

The sneak attack was brilliant. Garath's men swam and crawled through the rolling, evening waves on the shores until they were close enough to the pirate's camp to hear their harsh voices. The Pirates, not expecting a seaside attack, had posted no guards and were caught completely by surprise. Armed with a heavy, steel battle spear that his father had crafted for him, Garath lead the charge into the camp. The battle was not a long one. Many of the pirates broke and ran when the howling Freeport Guardsmen swept into the campsite, but the cruel and cunning Sarnas would not be driven off so easily.

Sarnas attacked savagely with his wicked cutlass, killing two of the brave guardsmen in one volley of brutal sword strokes. Several others tried to subdue the enraged pirate only to meet the same fate as those before them. It seemed as if the brute was invincible. Garath, realizing that Sarnas had to be stopped, stepped into his deadly path, allowing the camp fire to flicker off of his simple ringmail coat. The shinning metal caught the eye of Sarnas, and he charged like a raging bull with a newly aquired target.

Garath, under threat of being cut down by those terrible swords, raised his spear above his head and threw it with all his might. The spear hit home, he knew, but the massive Sarnas' weight carried him crashing into Garath and sent both men sprawling into the sand. For a brief moment Garath saw blackness, thinking to himself that one of the pirate's swords must have run him through and he was about to die. Then Garath heard the victorious cheers of his men and knew that everything was alright. Garath regained his feet and removed his spear from the dead Sarna's chest. It had been a clean hit, straight to the heart, and one that Garath would become famous for.

With their leader dead, it was not hard to round up the rest of the pirates and return them to Freeport to be jailed. Garath and his men spent several years sailing the waters of the Ocean of Tears and protecting the merchant ships from Pirates. Garath became an accomplished sailor and an even more accomplished warrior. He was a scourge to all of those who dared oppose the will of Freeport on the seas, and his devotion to his work soon earned him another very important duty.

With piracy becoming a rather unsafe venture on Garath's ocean, the Prime Minister of Freeport, a man called Heratio Rall, decided that the young warrior should focus himself on other threats for a while. As Freeport gew, so did the Freeport Guard. Their Captain was growing too. He grew older and slower by the day. The City Guard had become out of shape and unfit to protect Freeport's Citizens. Getting them back into shape, would be Garath's next great adventure.
