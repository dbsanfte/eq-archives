The glory of Hindolar Thalornor who I am was grand in my mind untill I opened my eyes to see Drillz Thakir standing above me. Only then did I realize I lived a dream of anothers life and mine was just about to begin for the very first day.

Drillz was wondering what art of magic I was going to learn, but became suprised when I replied that I wanted to become a noble cleric of Brell and heal the wounded and sick. Drillz could only shake his head as he chanted a few words and vanished before me.

I found the temple and High Cleric who trained me in the basic arts of protection and told me to go forth into the world and bring pride upon our kind. I could only nod as I watched the power that flowed from the hands of the High Cleric and began to make my way to the entrance of Ak'anon.

What wonders would I see? What will become of me? Will I ever get to see the things in my dreams?

As I left Ak'anon and took my first step outside, the dream of Hindolar Thalornor vanished into nothing

Now begins the first day of my true life and journey
