Becoming a Member of Crimson Dragons

So you want to “Quest with the Best”? You think you have what it takes to adventure with the most unique group of guildsmen and friends in Norrath today? It isn't easy to become one of us, but if you truly want it and believe you can do it... nothing should be able to stop you.

The Steps to Becoming a Member

1. Obtain a minimum level of 30.
2. Obtain 3 member sponsors, one must be a Guild Officer.
3. Complete the guild application form and submit it to the Guild Initiation Officer.
4. Watch for your application to be posted on the Recruiting Forum.
5. Post a reply to your application, introducing yourself to our members.
6. Follow up with your 3 sponsors to have them post a recommendation for you on the forum.
7. Monitor our Forums and attend guild-sponsored events during a one-week Petition period.
8. If you are accepted after 1 week, complete your initiation quest and seek out Garanth for your official invitation.
9. Submit a screenshot of your character.

The Minimum Level

The reason for having a minimum level of 30 is not so the Crimson Dragons become an all-powerful guild or uber-guild. The level 30 minimum enables us to plan events that all of our members can attend with little risk to themselves. In the past, before our level 30 limit was set, problems arose with some members feeling left out because they couldn't attend events that were held in certain zones and dungeons due to level restrictions. As a guild that’s focussed on teamwork and friendship, that isn't how we want our members to feel. The level 30 minimum promotes the spirit of teamwork and friendship that the guild was founded on.

Obtaining 3 Sponsors

Obtaining member sponsors is just a way of saying that there are people in Crimson Dragons who know you and who will vouch for you. Before you list a member as your sponsor, you must make sure they have agreed to it. Once a member sponsors a new initiate, that initiate's behavior and participation will be a direct reflection of the sponsor. Our members tend to be rather picky about who they will grant their sponsorship to, they are using their own reputation to back you on your quest for guild membership. At least one of your sponsors must be a member of the Dragonian Council.

Sponsorship adds prestige and credibility to your initiation and provides an in game source for any questions and guidance you may require during the initiation process and throughout your life as a member of the Crimson Dragons.

The Membership Application

Your Membership Application is one of the most important steps of your initiation process. This form, which can be found linked to this page, contains valuable information that we require about you and your character. Failure to complete the application in its entirety or failure to take the application seriously will automatically render you ineligible for guild membership.

When filling out the information, you should take plenty of time and elaborate on your answers where it is necessary. You should pay special attention to your character's biography, as we take this part very seriously. An inadequate or incomplete biography may lead to the rejection of your application. Your bio should include interesting role-playing information about your character and anything else that you want to add. Your biography should be well-written and well thought out.

The Recruiting Forum

The Recruiting Forum is one of our many Crimson Dragons message boards. We use this forum in particular to post the applications of potential members for everyone to see. The reason for this is simple. Being a guild of friends, we try to avoid problems between our members at all costs.

By allowing all the members of Crimson Dragons to view your application and respond to it or ask questions about it, we provide an opportunity for them to voice their concerns or recommendations regarding your potential membership in the guild. Any concerns that are addressed on this forum must be resolved between the applicant and member before the initiation can proceed. The forum is also there for you to introduce yourself to the whole guild and allow your sponsors to write recommendations for you. Remember your introduction and your sponsor's letters on the forum are a requirement for your initiation, so you must see to it that they are written within 7 days of your application being posted.

The One-Week Petition Period

Once your application has been posted to the forum, it will remain there for one week (7 days). During this week you must monitor your application on a daily basis, and answer any questions the members may ask you or address any concerns they may have. You should also monitor our events calendar and participate on our other forums during the week so you can attend events and allow our members the opportunity to get to know you better. At the end of your one week petition period, if you have responded satisfactorily to any concerns posted by our guild members, received 3 posted letters of recommendation from your sponsors and have received adequate member support, the Guild Initiation Officer will send you an email informing you that you have been accepted to the guild.

Initiation by the Ghost of Garanth

Garanth was the founding father of the first Crimson Dragons. They say his ghost still roams the areas around Freeport, and if a man or woman is truly worthy of becoming a Crimson Dragon, he will appear before them and initiate them.

Upon gaining acceptance to Crimson Dragons, you must seek out the current Guild Leader and he will give you the quest you must complete to find the Ghost of Garanth. When you find the ghost... Garanth will then judge to see if you are worthy of wearing the Crimson Dragons guildtag. He may ask you questions about guild history and guild procedures and policies. Only when Garanth is satisfied that you are worthy will he make you a Crimson Dragon. Your sponsors are encouraged to stand with you as you face Garanth as a show of their support for you.

Character Screenshot

We’re proud of every one of our members and have designated a personal webpage for each of them to list their accomplishments, biography and character information. You will be required to submit a screenshot of your character with your new guildtag to the Guild Page Keeper within 24 hours of receiving your Crimson Dragons guildtag. The Guild Page Keeper will create your personal page for you and add you to the official Crimson Dragons roster where you will find a link to your page. Failure to provide a character screenshot may result in the removal of your guild tag. Your screenshot may be submitted by clicking on the e-mail link on this page.
