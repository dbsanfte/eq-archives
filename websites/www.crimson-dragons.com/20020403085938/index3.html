Sunday, March 3, 2002
Subject: CD takes Lodizal to dinner

Saturday afternoon, Lodizal spawned in Ice Clad. A call went out in guild chat and all those who were not in TOV made their presence known. (Well all except for Agathar, he's fat and was a lil slow.) It was a good showing timely showing by CD once again.
