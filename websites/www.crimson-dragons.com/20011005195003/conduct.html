The Crimson Dragons Code of Conduct

"Have character, but don't be a character." -- Richard Marcinko

  1. Don't Steal Kills....EVER. It is a violation that is subject to immediate termination.

  2. Don't engage in arguments with other players or Guild Members public channels. This makes you and the guild look bad and is generally annoying for everyone. If you feel it necessary to discuss differences then deal privately with the issue in /tell.

  3. Don't hog loot! Whenever you as a Crimson Dragons Member are in a group you are representing us as a guild. Don't be greedy and give everyone a fair chance to loot. Be respectful of the loot order that has been decided on by the rest of the group. Don't loot out of turn! Be aware of the costs incurred by Clerics and Enchanters for symbol and rune buffs. Be honorable and make sure that these classes are compensated for the costs incurred. When hunting in a group of Crimson Dragons members, need before greed is the preferred way of loot distribution. In groups of non CD members, the group will decide the method of distributing loot/items.

  4. Don't over-use guildsay. This is a great channel for asking questions, seeking advice or looking for a group, but don't have active conversations with a single person, the green text can get excessive and be an irritation to others. If you have to talk to a single person, take it to /tells. Never use foul or abusive language on guildsay.

  5. Don't beg for items. If you are at a level where there is an item you should definately not have, then don't beg for other members to get one for you. If you are at a level where that item is within your grasp, then others will be more then happy to help you get it as long as you have patience. The good of the guild comes before the good of the individual.

  6. If a member breaches any of the general guidelines or displays behavior unbecoming to a member of the Crimson Dragons, they can expect to be warned and counseled by an Officer of the guild. Repeat offenses by the same member after a warning will result in that member being removed from the guild.

Member Responsibilities

  1. Do your best to attend all guild functions. If you are online during the time of a guild event, you should do your best to attend. The guild that plays together stays together. If no effort is seen on your part on a continual basis to participate in guild events then action may be taken by guild officers.

    Most events will be scheduled well in advance and there is no excuse for not knowing about them. CD is a very active guild and expects it’s members to be very active and involved in guild events. We are counting on you to be there.

  2. Check the website regularly! Guild members and officers often post news and events on the guild forum. You should spend a few minutes at least every other day to browse the website and see what is going on. You are responsible to know what’s going on in the guild and what events are upcoming. Logging on and asking in /gu what events are planned is not acceptable. Event dates and start times are posted on the event forums and on the guild MOTD. Sign up for events. Events will be cancelled 12 to 24 hours in advance if enough people don’t commit to participate by signing up on the event forum. If you sign up, make sure you attend.

  3. Be helpful to other members. If you can provide a service or information that another member needs, be willing to do so. Helping each other is what we are here for, and favors to others are always looked on in a good light by other members and officers. If you are requiring a favor such as a resurection or port, it is frowned upon that you ask in open /gu. Send private tells where possible. If another member can help you with your request they will. If they can’t you will need to look outside of the guild for someone to perform the service for you and incur any costs associated.

  4. Be a good representative of the guild. This is an open ended responsibility, because all members should know what it means to be a good EQ player. Do your best to be the best... thats all it takes.

  5. Have FUN!!! This is the most important aspect of the game. The challenge and excitement of the game should always stay fun. If it isn't, then you're doing something wrong.

Policy on Secondary / Alternate Characters

Many members have secondary/alternate characters. Crimson Dragons will allow you to have as many characters in the guild as is allowed by the game dynamics. Secondary characters must be listed on the roster as belonging to you before they will be invited. There is no level restriction placed on secondary characters.

Although we allow alts in CD, we encourage primary character development. This ensures that we have members who can participate in successively more difficult challenges and encounters. We ask that there be some continuous improvement in the development of your primary character, so that all members can contribute to the success of the high level encounters.

As a members of Crimson Dragons you are part of a family. It is expected that all your characters will be part of the family. The Officers frown upon having characters in guilds other than the Crimson Dragons and will rarely make an exception to allow a player into the guild that has characters in other guilds. Too many questions of conflict of interest can arise when a player belongs to more than one guild.

Rejoining CD after You Have Left the Guild

In a guild as large as Crimson Dragons, it is inevitable that some members will eventually want to leave for various reasons. Members who do will be treated fairly and respected for their decision, but we encourage that they take ample time to think things over before removing themselves from the guild. When a member leaves the Crimson Dragons, they will lose all access to the member's only section of the message boards, as well as their standing invitation to any "guild only" events.

In the past, many members who have left the guild have come back. It should be understood that once a member leaves for an extended period of time, they will only be allowed to rejoin the guild after they have completed the regular joining process as if they were a brand new member. This policy helps to ensure that people will not leave the guild hastily without thoroughly considering their choice, and also gives the general membership a voice as to whether or not the departed member should be re-guilded.

Open Door Policy

Officers of the guild are here for your assistance. If you have a problem or concern, you may approach any officer. If he or she can not solve it, then they WILL find someone who can. No problem is too small or unimportant to seek advice and you will never be turned away. Be prepared to be part of the solution and to take action on the recommendations the Officers offer you.
