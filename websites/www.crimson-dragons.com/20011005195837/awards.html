Crimson Dragons Awards

"He who stops being better stops being good." -- Oliver Cromwell


To recognize all your hard work and talents, the Leadership of the Crimson Dragons has decided to sponsor an awards program that recognizes individual members.

Role Playing Award

This Award goes to a Crimson Dragon who shows exceptional role playing traits. The recipient of this award goes to great lengths to play in character and never lapses into OOC while in the game. This Dragon is a role model for roleplaying, and should be emulated by their peers and fellow players.

Guild Service Award

This Award goes to Crimson Dragons who play a special role in maintaining and preserving the guild. These recipients range from Guild Officers, to Guild Mentors and have all shown great dedication in playing an integral part in running the guild.

Founders Cross

This very special award is a valuable momento, representing the ten original members of Crimson Dragons. The founders who wear this vaunted symbol are responsible for everything that we have today. Younger Dragons will look upon this award with awe and inspiration, for within it and within all our founding members, lies the history and the core of our guild.

Master Tradesman Award

This Award is presented to Crimson Dragons who have mastered any single trade skill. These Dragons have shown shear determination and discipline in mastering their art, and are duely honored by our guild. This award may be presented once the character reaches Master level in any trade skill.

Good Will Award

This Award goes to those Crimson Dragons who show numerous acts of kindness and charity beyond the call of duty. These members far exceed expectations when it comes to helping others, both inside and outside the guild. These members are good role models for younger Dragons, and represent our guild with honor.

Good Humor Award

The members who recieve this award are always fun to have around. They are the ones who make us laugh till we cry, and always face difficult situations with a smile. Their comical antics and glowing personalities make the game funner for those around them.

GuildMaster's Leadership Award

This award is sponsored by the guild leader and is awarded to those Dragons who show exceptional leadership ability. These Dragons are respected and revered as true leaders by their peers. Their opinions and ideas are among those most respected in the guild.

Crimson Dragon Legends Award

This award is awarded to those Dragons who rise to and above the 20th tier. These Dragons show a true love for the game, and have become true adventurers and heroes in the Everquest community. This honor is to coincide with the choosing of the surname.

The Neriak Raid Patch

This campaign patch has been awarded to all members of Crimson Dragons who have accompanied our forces into the vile bastion of hate known as Neriak.

The Sacred Fyre Campaign Patch

This Campaign Patch has been awarded to all members of Crimson Dragons who participated in our day long war and gloried victory of the Sacred Fyre Guild.

The Nagafen Raid Patch

This Campaign Patch has been awarded to those guild members who have accompanied us into the depths of Nagafen's Lair to face the mighty dragon himself.

The Legions of Fyre and Ice Honors Award

In March of 2000, the Legions of Fyre and Ice and Crimson Dragons completed a successful merger of the two guilds. The Crimson Dragons name was chosen to remain as the guild tag but the spirit of LoFI lives on. This award is presented to those original members of LoFI who had the courage and wisdom to be a part of something great.
