Harpi's childhood was filled with laughter and funfilled days playing in the streets of Qeynos. Both her parents were halfelves and make a nice life in Qeynos. Harpi found her time being consumed by visiting the local bard guild. She sat for hours listening to tales of heros and songs of bravery and sadness. Her happiest day is when her father first bought her a wonderous lute. She played it till her fingers bleed. She always knew that songs held special powers, but it wasn't till she was accepted into the bard guild that she would truely learn of that power to heal and inspire.
