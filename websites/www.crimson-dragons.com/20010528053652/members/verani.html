Verani is a female half-elf born in Felwithe. Her strong will and belief in the generosity of her diety Tunare lead her follow the rigorous path of the Paladin. Meeting her share of visitors in the lands of Faydwer, her heart found love in a native of the area, Kahlea ( a high elven cleric). Working together, Verani and her wife spread the faith to all they can - one diety at a time. One day, Verani will succeed in her ultimate goal and enter the gates of Neriak unharmed by the dark elves. Only then can the faith be spread to those that need it most.
