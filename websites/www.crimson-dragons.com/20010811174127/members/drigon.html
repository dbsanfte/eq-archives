I was born in the city of Kelethin. As a young woodelf, I was always fond of weapons, but my father and mother both being druids did not know how to train me. They then sent me to the Emerald Warrior's guild hall, were I was kept and trained in the arts of a warrior. My parents were killed in the Crushbone wars, so I now not only fight for myself, but for the revenge of my parents.
