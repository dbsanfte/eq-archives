Felwar set out from Ak'Anon in his youth, bright eyed and full of wonder at the world of Norrath. His goals then were to acquire knowledge and to fight evil in the form of goblins, kobolds, orcs, and the other dark races. His early days were thus spent questing for the library Mechanimagica and the Gemchoppers. He fought the minions of Meldrath in the minotaur caves of the Steamfonts with his companions ( Mooshie the ranger, Teralan the bard, and Sabaoth the druid, and learned the history of the gnomish race.

When his knowlegde here could progress no further, he headed out into the world, marvelling at the Spires of Felwithe, gaping in awe at the grand statue over the gates of Kaladim, and learning about the concept of gravity in the treetop city of Kelethin. In these places, Felwar found many quests and learned even more of the history of these races. Their battles became his, and so he journeyed to the stronghold of clan Crushbone. There, he learned many a hard lesson at the hands of the orcs, and developed the skills he needed to battle alongside other heroes of the realms of Faydwer. Many a grand battle was fought, once again alongside his erstwhile companions, until in one final and climactic battle in the tunnel from Greater Faydark, the forces of light, led by Teralan Half-Elven, succeeded in slaying Emperor Crush. Many had fallen in the conflict, and Felwar decided to head to Antonica, having learned all he could of the elder races of Faydwer.

At this point in his carreer, Felwar set out to explore all that he could. However, after one disastrous attempt to lead his companions across the plains of Karana to see the city of Qeynos, he learned of his need for greater power to safely travel the lands. So he journeyed to the dungeons of Guk and Befallen, and into the blistering heat of the Desert of Ro. When he had attained a level of power sufficent to continue his exploration, he once again headed into the western lands, visiting Qeynos, Erudin, and Halas. And once again, here he settled down for some time, learning the histories of these western human races, and questing to further the goals of the good factions of the west. He battled in the maze of king Xorbb, and in the frozen castle of Permafrost, once again alongside his companions, Mooshie, Teralan, and Sabaoth. However, as he was ever seeking new knowledge and adventure in the world, he headed south, and explored the lands of Rathe, and the jungles of the Feerrot.

It was while fighting the aviaks of south karana and the orcs and gnolls of highpass hold, that Felwar's attitude toward the world of Norrath began to darken. First, he recieved word that his friend Mooshie was retiring, but would be sending a Dark Elven Necromancer (of all things!) to replace him as his companion. Sabaoth had already begun to leave his woodland home less and less frequently, and the loss of his second friend saddened him, but he was determined to continue on with Teralan the bard at his side. Also during this time, Felwar learned that many of the greatest evils in Norrath are not the orcs or goblins or dark races, but among his fellow adventurers. He returned to his home in the steamfonts to find the realm in chaos. The evil druid Therin had apparently started a trend of corruption based upon the murder those not of his own race. Felwar found gnomish gaurds being slaughtered in the same fashion as he himself had killed so many orcs and goblins.

And the worst part was that these were not minions of evil killing his countrymen, but elves and humans, druids and magicians. These were people who should have been the allies of his race, but instead chose to murder them for a profit. Upon investigation, he found that the vile practice had also spread to the dwarven and human guards in other realms. Felwar was greatly saddened by this fact, and immersed himself in the quest for the power to confront these corrupt individuals and crush them.

He then journeyed to Highkeep itself, and learned of a goblin infestation of the basement, and so decided to fight there for a time. It was during this time, that he learned that his friend Teralan had also decided to set aside his blades and his lute, and would be settling down for a time. And he also sent forth a Dark Elf to replace him as my companion, this one a mage.

However, in this saddest time, Felwar found he had gained a reputaion as a good spirited gnome of great skill, and he made several new friends in the cellars of Highkeep, including Brinstormsong the bard, Aalarye the cleric, and Kilkin, a fellow gnome who has taken up the sword of the Gemchoppers. Having his spirits once again lifted, Felwar began to devise some method of bringing hope back to the world of Norrath, even if in some small way. It was during this time that he achieved the ability to teleport others along with him in his journeys across norrath, and while journeying through everfrost one night, came up with a grand plan. Over the next two weeks, he consulted with his friends, and stockpiled items. Then, when the time was right (OOC:Christmas Eve), his companions Teralan, Sabaoth, and Mooshie, came out of their retirement for just one night to accompany Felwar around the world. And so his plan was revealed, as Felwar used his newfound powers to travel to Freeport, Ak'anon, Felwithe, Kelethin, Kaladim, Erudin, Rivervale, Qeynos, and Halas with his friends carrying bags of goods, and proceeded to distribute presents to the fledgling adventuerers of each realm. Several others, inspired by his actions, also joined him along the way, or began to give out gifts in turn. The journey took a long time, and several battles were fought along the way, but Felwar still considers this the defining event of his wizardly carreer. Because it was not a quest for glory or treasure or powerful artifacts, but one for spreading joy and hope, the very things Felwar had found lacking in the world.

Since that time, he has journeyed deeper still into the dungeons of Guk, Permafrost, and Cazic Thule. And as his power grows, he is confronted with more and more people who see norrath as nothing more than a place to be pillaged and squeezed for every last copper piece and trinket they can find. He has also begun to do further quests, many for the temple of Solusek Ro, his god, in order to outfit his friend Teralan in an attempt to pull him from retirement. It was during one of these trips that Felwar stumbled across the meeting held by Jubal and Jythri, outlining their vision of a better world and a thing called The Collective of Fennin Ro. It is his belief that this plan may be the best hope for improving the state of his world, and has since sought a group to join who also suppoerted this.

And so, after a legthy conversation with his friend Kilkin, Felwar has found what he hopes is such a group.
