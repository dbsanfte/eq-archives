The story of Myka so far...

Myka is a sturdy and friendly looking girl and from the beginning of her childhood she always helped the other children who got beaten or needed comfort in bad times. Her parents and the Shaman of her Sted (The Sted of the Polar Wolf) saw in her a potential Shaman. After much debate she was allowed to do The Tests to become a shaman. She passed them all easily and was made the youngest Shaman ever of her Sted. Because the Elders saw that she still lacked experience, they told her to go and adventure the world until she would see the sign of the Polar Wolf beckoning her to come home to her Sted and lead her people.

From that day on she walks across the world learning everything she can in benefit for her People. Because she is still young she doesn't see the difference between Good and Evil yet, but she does see people in need of help. She will help everyone the best she can and always will be very observant.

After some time she met Gance in Freeport and saw the powers and experience this King and his followers has. Because of the benefit to her people she joined his order and set to her mind to become one of the most respected members.


After some year she became a Quest Knight, but then something horrible happened to her:
You look up when you hear some girl crying hysterically while running through the corridors of King Gance's Castle.

The door to the throne-room bangs open and Myka comes running to Gance. Her face all wet with tears and her cries echo through the vast halls. She falls down in front of Gance's feet sobbing.

"I.. I... sniff..was robbed!" more tears stream down the sobbing face of Myka. "I was robbed and lost all my items!" After that Myka starts even crying louder and louder until every single one in the castle has woken up.


She travelled back again to Halas to only find more questions:
While being busy for some days to get some new cloths after the robbery in West Commons, Myka finally had some money again to buy a decent weapon. 'This will quicken the income again', Myka thought. She already had sold many bear-hides she had taken from polar cubs when suddenly she saw something familiar.

Ducking behind a rock she saw one of her robbers! Here, walking in everfrost! 'Maybe they are after my family or are searching something special?', Myka pondered.

Suddenly she felt a hand on her shoulder. She turned around quick and was already beginning to form a magic spell on her lips when she saw the face of her Shaman Elder.

"Ssshh" the Elder told her and made a motion to follow her closely. Myka followed quickly and after some hour of walking she finally decided it was time to get some answers.

"Excuse me Elder, but.. umm.. what is happening? Why are they after me? The already robbed me bare-naked!"

"Look at your arm Myka, see that sign? You had it from your birth on. I can't tell much about it, but that's why we send you away from Halas. Unluckily they found you even outside Halas.".

Myka frowned. "But.. but why? I'm not special, am I? What to do?"

"So many questions Myka, not so many answers though. It has something to do with the Polar Wolf. The symbol you have on your arms is the same as the stars that form the Polar Wolf in the sky. You are destined for something Myka. But now you have to hide my child, for you are not strong enough yet, you have much to learn yet.", the Elder stopped by a igloo-tent and walked in.

Myka followed and smelled some real good Wolfpaw-tea. She really was thirsty and hungry. She didn't talked much that night because of all the thoughts in her mind and went to bed expecting to have some nightmares again about those robbers.

While Myka was sleeping, the Elder began some old ritual, throwing ice dust over spider legs and beetle eyes, mixing it in with a boiling soup of something that wasn't alive anymore (hopefully). After she was ready she walked over to Myka and sprinkled some of the stuff on Myka. Before her eyes she saw Myka transform!


Myka is Kendolin now:
After a good rest Myka woke up with a headache. She hadn't had any nightmares, in fact she had a real good sleep, but something told her something was different today. Was it the blanket that she now could use as a private tent? She pulled one leg from the blanket and immediately pulled it under again. Her heart pounded in her body. "By Tunare what has happened to my leg, it looks blue!". She now pulled both legs from under the blanket and stared at her blue slim legs. "Those aren't my legs, and not my arms.. what happened?"

She got up quickly and walked to a mirror. She almost fainted but her Elder Shaman got hold of her just in time.

"Good morning Myka, I see you are full of questions, so I tell you right away. I transformed you Myka to a Dark Elf so you won't be bothered by your robbers for now. I also transformed you from a strong lady to an agile one so you can find out yourself what's behind the robbery. And if I may say I did a fine job!", the Shaman Elder smiled when she looked Myka up and down. Only Myka didn't like it and sat down on the bed crying.

"Come on Myka, I did this for you with love, I don't want you to be hurt and you need time to find out what's behind your little tattoo. I only changed your form, not your soul Myka.", "As for your name, you're better off with a new one. I will call you Kendolin. Now straighten up, we are going to equip you and then you're off."

I went all too quick for Myka, now Kendolin. She rather went to bed again and have it being a big nightmare, instead she found herself fully packed outside of Halas. She walked to a guard in order to ask if he knew where King Gance is, but instead she had to run for her life when the guard started yelling "A dark elf! Kill her!".

She run and run till she was in the tunnel from east commons to north ro, there she waited for the regular meeting of King Gance and his followers. Should she tell them and ask for help? Or would they kill her on sight?


The guildmeeting:
The guildmeeting was already started. Kendolin hid herself in a corner, still waiting for a good moment to reveal herself to her brothers and sisters. This would be very difficult!

"Excuse my me King Gance for intruding and for not showing myself. Something terrible happened to me and I don't look the same anymore." She slowly revealed her and gasps where heard throughout the room. Gance came up to her and said he would listen to her. She sat down and started her story about her robbery and her transformation to a dark elf, so (in her Elder Shaman's words) she could easily find out why they where after her.

Not everyone trusted her because of her new form. A week's trial would be started so Kendolin could prove that she really is Myka, instead of some dark elf that was doing as if. Kendolin was soon 'accepted' as no sudden threat and the meeting continued.


Found!
After getting used to her new body, Kendolin (Myka) went to the very heart of evil. The evil city of Neriak! She heard many rumors in bars about a recent robbery (that could have been hers) of some barbarian.

She went through the tunnel to Neriak. She shivered. "What if they see through my body right into my soul? They would notice I'm not a real Dark Elf!" She quietly walked through the dark Nektolous forest. The strange moanings and groanings from everywhere made her jumpy.

Suddenly there was someone that lay his hand on her shoulder. She quickly turned around and saw a giant stone guardian rise above her. "Woods dangerous, keep to path" was all that the guardian said while spilling little stones from his lips. "Oh. uh ok, thank you very much you are very kind sir!", Myka said. The guardian hesitated, but then walked away after some skeletons that where having a chat by some tree.

Kendolin ran the last bit to Neriak, ignoring the many guards and dark elves around her that were saying things like "Vendui!" and "Hail Dark Sister!".

She kept to the path until she saw the volcanoes of Neriak. Then she turned right to the Neriak's gate. She went for the first local bar and tried to begin a chitchat about the rumors of a robbery of a barbarian. She was taken to the back of the room by some sneaky little dark elf. "I know where they hold the barbarian items. Wanna split it? I need someone to look at my back when we steal it!". "Oh! Steal?", she quickly recovered her mistake, "Of course! let's do it, it will be fun!".

That night (or day, she couldn't tell because it was always dark in Neriak) they both sneaked through some backyards and little streets to some old building. "Now comes the fun part!", the dark elf said. He took his pick locks and skillfully unlocked the door and slipped inside. Myka took one last look outside and went after the dark elf.

Inside there were different rooms with labels on it like "Barbarian stuff", "Filthy dwarven stuff", "to be cleaned gnome stuff" etc. The rooms were very big, easily for Ogres and Trolls to walk around. In the room with the label "New Tinks" she found her belongings. "There it is!", Myka said and pointed to a far corner where she saw her belongings. The dark elf looked at her strangely. "How did you know?", he took out his dagger and slowly walked towards Myka.

Suddenly there was a big explosion. Somehow the dark elf triggered a trap. Kendolin and the dark elf where thrown against the wall. Kendolin (maybe because she was really a barbarian) didn't take much damage and wasn't knocked unconscious. On the other hand, the little dark elf had broken his neck and was instantly dead. She did a prayer to her god and closed the dark elf's eyes.

Kendolin rushed to her belongings and took all her items. "How strange, I would have thought I couldn't carry all my items in this Dark Elf form." Then she noticed she wasn't a dark elf anymore, she was in her Barbarian form again!

She quickly dressed herself and took the secret tunnels out of Neriak. (It was the flee-route they would have taken both after stealing the special items.) She quickly ran along the hill side to the volcanoes. She knew she could hide there till some party would come along. She hardly was at the spot or some Paladin in all shiny plate mail came up to her. "Hello my Lady, you look lost! May I escort you to the Eastern Commons?". "Thank you! That would be nice great Paladin". She blushed. This guy was soooo handsome!

They quickly went along the path. They came at the bridge and suddenly guards from all sides came rushing forward while shouting "Kill that Paladin and that worthless Barbarian women!".

"Quickly, run for the east commons, I will keep the guards busy, now go!", the Paladin raised his great two-handed sword and with one blow knocked a guard off the bridge, giving an open spot for Myka to run. She ran and only took one look behind. The Paladin was overmatched but still stood ground. He truly was great!

After she was safe in the eastern commons she waited. She heard a little shuffle and turned around. It was the Paladin, all bashed up and covered with blood. His two-handed sword was broken in half and most of his armor had taken severe damage. She quickly healed him and helped him out off his armor. "That was a great Battle Myka. I did honor to my people by killing all those guards. Alas I had to run when the Captain of guards came. But I'll be back for him!". Myka hushed the paladin and covered him with a blanket. "Of course great Paladin, but paladins have to sleep also. Now sleep, I will take guard my Paladin."


My Baby!: It were fast years for Myka, she came closer and closer with Gance BraveHeart and eventually they married together in front of the South Tower of Everfrost. The following months were like fresh lands with blossoms and not soon after their marriage Myka announced to Gance she was pregnant. A contest was written out for the People to come up with a name for th enew Prince or Princess, but it never came to the point of a big celebration because something horrible happened again...

Myka was heading for her parents in Halas, she knew this would be the last long trip she would be allowed while being pregnant. King Gance and all the Advisors of the Kings court insisted on her to keep calm and safe in the Castle, but she was stubborn and really wanted to see her parents again before giving birth.

On the way to Halas she felt as if someone was following her. She urged the driver of the coach to go faster and sent a guard to take a look. She made it into Halas safely and also the guard couldn't find anything peculiar.

She was enjoying the 'sky of Halas that night, the cool breeze and the stars that could be seen so clearly. She put her hands on her big belly and was proud of the baby inside her. She was day-dreaming a bit and started to walk to the nearby Inn.

Suddenly before she knew it she was grabbed from behind by someone. She wrestled loose and started to run, but a second dark figure jumped on her and knocked her unconsious.

Duke Falconas was just traveling to Halas for supplies when he heard some kind of scream. He thought it was his Queen and started to look for her. He had seen her coach, but from his Queen no signs. Even the guards didn't know, after he had awakened them!

The last place he searched was the home of thieves. The guild of thieves have ears and eyes everywhere, they should know something, but knocking on the door only revealed that the door was open and the corridor behind it empty and dark.

He started to walk down the corridor when he suddenly saw Queen Myka in the distance, lying naked on a table in a round chamber. Blood was dripping on the floor and he saw her belly was cut open. He didn't hesitate a second and used all his strength to cast a powerful healing spell on his Queen.

"Are you alright my Queen?", asked Duke Falconas. Myka was confused, but almost shouted it out from the pain she felt inside. She saw her mantle stained in blood lying on her. Her hands going to her belly and she felt nothing! Her baby was gone! She yelled and screamed for her baby, while Falconas tried to comfort her the best way he could.

It took Falconas some time to make sure Queen Myka was alright and started to investigate and interrogate the members of the Bank. Surely they would have seen something strange! But they only gave some vague description of some dark people carrying her and them fleeing in some kind of magical fear.

Even the Guildmaster of the rogues that came some hour later with a group of young thieves was shocked that something like that had happened in his home. Someone was trying to blame his guild the guildmaster thought and quickly offered Duke Falconas his help to go and search for the dark creeps, for an award ofcourse.

Duke Falconas turned to Queen Myka again, who was screaming again for her baby. Only now did he see the strange tattoo on her arm. He had heard about or seen that tattoo, which were merely some bright spots, somewhere! It was some myth he couldn't recall. He set it to his mind to order a search in the Castle's Library for some clues. But first he led Queen Myka to a nearby Inn and posted a double guard at her door. He was smiling when he saw her falling asleep. "Rest my Queen, we will find your baby, our Prince or Princess!"


The Search:
Duke Falconas started the search in the dusty Library of the castle accompanied by an enthousiastic gnome, who wanted to index the library in every way he could. The gnome was a bit nerving Falconas in his search, wanting to search quietly, but this time the gnome brought some strange device. "This is the fastest index-maker-and-search-machine-in-one!", the gnome said while starting the device. Big black smoke was coming out of the device while it groaned and began flapping some tentacles while making a deafening noise.

The little gnome was so excited that he jumped around the machine, pulling levers and pressing buttons, not seeing that at the same time he accidently pushed Falconas away, who fell against the machine and pulled a lever to get up again. "NO not that lever!!!", the gnome screamed, but it was already to late. "KABOOOM!" Parts of the machine flew in the air and a small fire broke out. Before Duke Falconas knew what happened the gnome already had the fire extinguished with his 'instant-fire-away-device'.

On his lap, Duke Falconas found a half burned book. He opened it and saw it was the book he looked for. While the little gnome started to make new sketches of an indexing machine, he began to read it, trying to make out the words written in the old language: "the Polar woman, recognized by a very peculiar tattoo made-up by dots that resemble the stars that make the constellation of the Polar Wolf, will give birth to a powerful man. This man should not fall in the wrong hands or...", the rest of the pages were burned away.
