There was a brave paladin name Baron Edward who lived in the outer realm where the ancient beast Diablo dwelled. Edward drawing on his mighty sword was able to slay the beast Diablo and save the realm from any more of his foul creatures.

Shortly after this event Edward and his wife Gwenifer gave birth to their son Tacticus. They saw great potentional in their son Tacticus and vowed he would never touch a blade in his life. But when Baal had arisen from Diablo's ashes Edward took his son to the realm known as Norrath. He knew his infant son would never be able to surrive in his realm with Baal and wished his son goodluck as he placed him through the portal. Just as he placed his son through Baron Weaponrack, Edward's half-brother had delivered a fatal blow with his devastating whirlwind attack. Edward fell to the ground and took his last breath as he saw his son Tacticus go through the portal.

As Tacticus entered the realm known as Norrath he was surely left to die without any support from anyone. But when things looked bad a dark elf warrior had come by and seen Tacticus lieing on the ground. The warrior whose name was Mephfeesto took this boy into the city of Freeport. Once their he heard the stories of the boy and vowed he would keep his father's promise of never allowing him to touch a blade. Mephfeesto took the boy to the Ashen Order where he honed his body into a natural weapon in which he never needed to arm a blade.

Tacticus grew strong very quickly and soon became a formidable force and caught up to his orignal trainer Mephfeesto. Now the 2 travel among the land of Norrath striking fear into the eyes of enemies. Tacticus does vow though he will return to the Outer Realm when the time comes to slay the evil beast known as Baal and to avenge his father's death caused by the evil Weaponrack.
