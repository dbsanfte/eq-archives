A Wizards Academy


by Galilo D'Artemis

When the word wizard crosses our mind, the first thing we think of is master of sorcery, the plane walker. Wizard is a scholar man/woman who spends most of his/her time in the art of arcane and has the ability to call up the raw power of the universe and concentrate the force upon a subject and destroy it in a blink of the eyes. Wizard has the power to drop a mob in the shortest amount of time. On the other hand, Wizard does not have the healing power of the priest, does not have charm power like an enchanter (this is one of the main reason why wizard didn’t have any date), and does not have pet to command like a magician or a necromancer. The trade off is Wizard is the only class that can gate to planes, and travel across the world in a short amount of time and the power to disintegrate an object to crumble (this is the second reason why ladies stay away from wizard).

Wizard is a very difficult class to play. It requires a lot of patient and effort, which goes hand in hand with the fact that we are the only pure caster class that does not have pet, and would have to rely on the root line spells and fade line spells to evade major damage. In addition, we have the power to escape the wrath of all evils - the mother of all survival - EVACUATION - use it wisely.

Now, lets start begin with character creation. There are limited races in Norrath that can be wizards - Human (blind fool), Erudite (stuck up blind fool), High elf (Pale face), Dark Elf (inky), and Gnome (shorty). Each race will start with some default stats and 30 points to distribute among all the stats across the board. My recommend is to put every single point into intelligent (25 points) and the left over (5 points) you can put wherever you wish. The current limit cap for intelligent is 200, but who knows, the cap may be raised in future (as we all know - Verant is very unpredictable, indeed).

There is not much to tell you between levels 1 to level 16. People can zoom thru it within a few days. I have seen someone got from level 1 to level 20 in a day with quests and some minor hunting. From level 16 to 24, the best place to get experience is Lake Rathe. Aviak guards give best experience for those levels. At level 16th, you will have your first absorb damage shield line spell, shieldskin. It uses cateye agate as reagent to process the spell. USE IT!!! You also get the invisible spell (no need to tell you what to do with it). At 20, you will get force shock spell, but take my word of this, don’t bother using it (as you will also find out that it gets resisted more often than your mana and hit points can afford). Your best option is to stick with either flame shock (110 damage) or lightning bolt (120 damage) - both are level 16th spells. At 20, you will also have the enstill spell and three self-gate spells to North Karana, Tox, and Greater Faydwer. Enstill is very useful in the situation where you have multiple mobs to deal with and you want to keep them out of your hair while you concentrate on one of them. It can hold the mob for a good amount of time but it will break away if it gets attack so use it with care.

When you reach 24, Aviak guards no longer yield measurable experience. It’s time to move on. Solusek Eye Dungeon (Sol A) is your ticket to ride all the way to 29 easily. At this level, you have frost shock spell. This spell works great on Fire goblins. DO NOT use flame shock on them; they are immuned to firebase spell. You also get your second absorb damage shield, Leather skin (it uses bloodstone as reagents to cast). The tactic here is simple -- stay close to the zone; take on the goblin one at a time. Fire Goblins drop a lot of fine steel weapons. You can take it to the druid ring in Lavastorm Mountain and sell to the vendor there to earn bread and milk. The experience for this place is great. I was able to get from level 24th to level 29th within one day (average 2 hours playtime per level). Before I forget, you get more self gate spells at level 24th: Cazic gate, Ro gate, Nek gate and West Karana gate. When you get to level 29, you will get more spells. Inferno Shock and Thunder Strike are the two direct damage, three group-gate spells. YAY!!!! I CAN PORT PEOPLE!!!!! (for a few bucks on the side), and a special: Bond of Force. This spell is similar to the druid’s snare, but it does not work as great. In my opinion, just forget about this spell and stick with root (level 4th spell) - a lot more reliable.

From 29th onward, the cake is done - you will now have to work. For those who does not care much about faction with the druids and Qeynos guard faction, kill the Treants in South Karana or druids at East Karana druid ring. I myself was the Drow so faction with the good guy does not mean anything to me; I went and nailed all those druids and treants. The best configurations for group at these spots are between 3 to 4 players max. Do not exceed this amount. Three to four people is more than enough to handle all situations at these two spots. Hang around there for a few days and you should have a couple levels out of those dead druids and treants. Many people consider 30 to be hell, but I don’t recall it as such. I flew right through 30. My average times for those levels were five to six hours each level. You can stick around until you get to level 34th to have a new set of spell or you can move to a different camp when you get to level 32nd. For those who need to keep their faction with the druid and Qeynos guard. You have to go to Temple of Cazic Thule. You may choose to join group or solo. It’s your choice. I prefer solo because I get the most exp from soloing than joining group. However, grouping isn’t bad either, since lots and lots of trains are being created. The best spot in Cazic Thule is around the base and mid section of Archon temple. You will find many Protectors, Fanatics, Heralds, etc. Just pick a mob that does not have his companion around and dump your mana on him. When you finished him up, go invisible and get up the wall to meditate. I have tried myself and it works great but if compare to killing druids, this is a torture. Either case, when you get to level 32nd or higher, move down to lower Guk.

Live side of lower Guk is the heaven for Wizard. Your invisible spell works well here. Use the same tactic I was mention earlier for Sol A. Stay near zone and kill those mobs around there. Most of them are Shin Knights, the rest -- shamans. There is not much to say about this. Basically, root and dump your mana on the should-be dead frog. You should be able to get up to level 38th or 39th within a few days. The average for me in this area was one level per day. At level 34th, you earn a new circle of magic. There are a few important spells for this level, Ice shock, Steel skin, greater shielding and Nullify magic. Nullify magic is the research only spell for wizard (not sold by wizard vendor), but to get around the research, you may purchase it from the cleric guild. Steel skin is one of the more popular one. Once you have this spell, spend some cash to get jaspers. Even it is a bit costly but worth the money. It is much cheaper than paying the cleric 100pp for the resurrection, so don’t be too thrifty. I always pack at least 60 jaspers with me all the time. Remember to check your supply and don’t let it run low on you. When you get to 39, you must go to Erudin to get spells. Erudin library is the only place in Norath that sell wizard spells for 39 to 49. Once you have all the spells you need, go to Oasis or Ocean of Tears (oot). In Oasis, spectres on the isle are great exp. Those spectres yield great experience for these levels up to 49th. There is not much to say but sit and camp the spectres. It will be very boring but the experience is incredible. In OOT, do spectres on spectres isles or seafury cyclops. Specters have decent drops so you can have some cash flow. At 44, you get a new fire base direct damage spell, Conflaguration. You also have Arch Shielding and Diamondskin, requires Peridot - expensive (10pp-14pp ea.). This is the reason why many wizards and Necromancer stick to the level 34th absorb damage shield. Once you reach level 49th, YAY!!!!! ICE COMET BABY!!! Not much to say at this point. You just got a most powerful spell of Norath. It does 1110 damage at level 49th and 1120 damage at level 50th.

Well folks, it’s time to go around and have some fun, camp for some nice things or duel with friends to earn some respect or better yet, let the Gods taste a piece of your mind

Notice: Root - A level 4th spell works well, no matter what level you are. There are very few mobs require you to use Immobilize (level 39th research spell, upgrade version of Root spell). Paralyzing Earth is a level 49th research spell, an upgrade version of Enstill. So don’t mix up between the two different lines of root spells. Basically, Root and Enstill are two different types of spell. They are not the same.

POWER LEVEL GUIDE FOR KUNARK YET TO COME NEAR FUTURE.

A few items you need to get as soon as you can:

  • Journeymans boots - Extremely Useful, unless you have so much time to kill.
  • Staff of Temperate Flux - Works Great when you need to kite mobs with journeymans boots. The rest? It’s yours to find out.
  • Mystic Cloak - Wahoo, who need Molten cloak?
  • Weeping wand - Yes, Baby. Focus!!!

Good luck and safe journey to the masters and soon to be masters of the arcane power.
