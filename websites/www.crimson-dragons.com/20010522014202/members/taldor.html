Favourite Skill Evocation

Taldor is a Gnome that has always been jelous of other casters. He still prays that one day he can have a pet that he can call his own. He knows he is a tiny bald midget and gets frustrated when people remind him. He is one of the deadliest Wizards in all of Norrath because of his Gnomish Fu knowledge, taught to him by his father, Turgan Fizzalot. He grew up in the hills of Ak'Anon and is a fun but crazy fellow.
