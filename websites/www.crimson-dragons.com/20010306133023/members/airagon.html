Airgon also known as Strider and Elessar among other names. He is the last surviving heir of Isildur of the Dunedain (the faithful Numenoreans) and therefore, the rightful king of Gondor and Arnor, although only a few persons such as Elrod and Gandalf knew it. When his father, Arathorne II , was killed by orcs, Airagon was raised by Elrod (leader of the Elves). As a man, he fought against Sauron in many places and under different names. He is the chieftain of the Dunedain (Rangers) who guarded the shire secretly. He carried with him the broken sword of his ancestor Elendil in expectation of the fulfillment of the prophecy. The sword was called Narsil by Elendil; when it was reforged in Riverdale it was named Anduril. Like the Numenoreans of old, Airagon could expect a life span three times longer than that of ordinary men. Airagon would like to provide his talents, dedication as well as his never ending thirst for knowlede and adventure to the Crimson Dragons.
