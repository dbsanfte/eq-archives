Galden, like most wood elves, was born in Kelethin. But beeing the adventurous soul that he was, he quickly took the long journey to Freeport. The first time he heard about druidic magic, he knew he'd found his life's calling. He wanted to explore the world, and being a druid would give him that ability. He spent many seasons fighting in the Freeport area, traveling to Rivervale for training and spells. The journey was easy back then, as the undead had not yet invaded Kithicor. Galden learned many things during that time, but one of the most important lessons came one day in Freeport. A stranger (someone who could have killed him with one swing of a sword) approached him and gave him a magical tunic. A blessing from Tunare indeed!

The stranger had only one request: do a similar thing for others in the future. With that simple act of kindness, he taught Galden that something insignificant to one person can be a blessing to another. As time passed, Galden heard many stories about lands far away: about a city of birds in the Karana plains and flying rock creatures in the lands beyond Nektulos Forest. Once he was able to run like a wolf, he started exploring those far away lands. He grew in strength and in wisdom, but he never forgot that lesson he learned in Freeport. To this day, Galden often travels back to those lands and shares gifts and magical abilities with others.
