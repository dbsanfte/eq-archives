Throughout my travels I have met many creatures, befriended many brave adventurers, and completed many side quests, yet I still yearn for more information regarding the mysterious magics of old.

Most recently I can be found on the newly rediscovered continent of Kunark, more specifically in Karnor's Castle and the hidden labyrinth of Old Sebilis.
