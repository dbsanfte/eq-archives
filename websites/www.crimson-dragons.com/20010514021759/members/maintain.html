Biography

Maintain's father, Main, always wanted something better for his kids than he had for himself. So one day while getting run down and killed by a snail- Main got the idea to enter Maintain in training to become a Shaman. With Spirit of Wolf, Main figured, his son could breeze through his life of 50 levels with relative ease compared to his speed-deficient neighbors on Fennin Ro. Everyone dies of course, but Maintain has done well to avoid getting his spirit broken- as was the case with his father. His underacheiving Ranger father Main, always felt inferior since he didn't earn his speed at level 39. If anyone is wondering how a Barbarian is the son of a half-elf, well that's another show, suffice it to say he was adopted. *S* Maintain holds the values of the Crimson Dragons close to his battle hardened heart. He will die a Crimson Dragon, but more importantly- he lives as one now. As long as he is breathing- no Crimson Dragon in his vicinity will have to walk again!

Keeping the peace while fighting the war-- your faithful Shaman, Maintain.
