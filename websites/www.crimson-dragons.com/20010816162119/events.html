Event Planning

Crimson Dragons is a multi faceted guild that participates in many types of events. Any member may sponsor an event, but these events must be approved of by the Guild Leader and Guild Events Officer before they are declared official. Crimson Dragons also holds several weekly and monthly events that are posted below. To sponsor or submit an idea for an event, please email our Guild Events Officer, Maintain Statusquo.

Event Calendar


The Event Team

The Event Team is a group of guild members who have volunteered their time to help plan, organize and run guild events. This team is captained by the Guild Events Officer, who is responsible for appointing team members and overseeing the progress of events planning. The Events Team has their own private forum in our forums section to be used in the planning of events.

The Fennin Ro Calendar

The Fennin Ro Calendar is a resource hosted by the Mercenaries of Doom for the purpose of coordinating events on a server wide scale. This tool prevents many conflicts between larger guilds in scheduling events and keeps conflicts from happening ahead of time. Not all guilds use this calendar but for the most part it is well regarded. The link to the Fennin Ro Calendar may be found below.

Weekly and Monthly Events

Guild Social Night

Guild Social Night is held every Thursday and all guild members and guests are welcome to attend. The event usualy consists of a party where prizes are raffled off and members can enjoy a wide variety of activities from trading and selling to dueling. The start time for the event is always 8PM EST and the usual location is the Oasis of Marr Gypsy camp. Alcoholic beverages are permitted and can be purchased from the gypsies rather easily. Guild Social Night is also a time where we invite new members who have completed the application process to join our guild. Any time and location changes for the party will be announced well ahead of time.

Friday Night Inter-Guild Events

Every Friday Night, members of Crimson Dragons get together with members of allied and other various guilds to hold events on a larger scale than what we can typicaly do alone. While the events planned are usualy for higher levels, such as dragon raids, plane raids, etc... our lower level members are encouraged to hold their own events such as dungeon and zone raids. These events may encompass a wide range of activities and will be announced a week ahead of time with times, level limits, etc....

Monthly Officer Meeting

Our Monthly Officer Meeting is a time for the guild officers to come together and discuss ideas, concerns and plans for the development of the guild. Only officers are allowed to attend but minutes of the meeting will be posted to our public forums by the Guild Elder on the following day. This meeting will be held in a private Everquest Chat room on the first Monday of every month. Times will be scheduled prior to the meeting.
