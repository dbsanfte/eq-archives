The History of the Order of Disorder

  
As told by Jadon Astbury
————————

Upon surveying the lands of Norrath, Kilnor Shadowdagger came to the conclusion that what was lacking was a networking of the assassins and mercenaries. The life of a rogue was difficult enough without the support and strength of others to aid him in his tasks. With the simple goal to form a loose group of the shadow walkers, Kilnor set about to forming the Order of DisOrder.

Seeking out the very best for his guild, Kilnor scoured the darkest regions of the lands and came up with nine elite rogues. From this small but dedicated group, the Order of DisOrder quietly spread across the land and made its presence known. Never working out in the open, the guild was nevertheless able to spread its influence across all the towns of Fennin Ro.

Neither a good or evil based group, the members of the Order choose to work by their own rules and morals. They would not let the laws of others govern their actions. The Order of DisOrder believed that actions were the mark of a man, not a badge or title. The Order of DisOrder did as it saw fit with the one and only rule being an undying loyalty to one another.

Under Kilnor's brilliant leadership and guidance, the Order's ranks swelled with rogues and as time went on, other individuals that were deemed worthy were allowed into this ring of rogues. Yet, a time came when the Order's dreams and goals could no longer be accomplished in the guild's present state. It was at this point that the opportunity presented itself to affiliate with one of the mightiest and bravest groups in all of Fennin Ro, the Crimson Dragons. This proved to be truly beneficial for both groups as the Dragons gained a hearty band of rogues and assassins and the Order was able to gain the diversity and numbers which it had previously lacked.
