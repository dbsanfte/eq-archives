Sargman was born in the frozen land of Halas, as a young Barbarian lad he was a little bigger and stronger than the other boys. As he grew he developed more and more of an affinity for weapons, and unlike his siblings showed absolutely no magic ability. Disowned by his parents, he was taken in by Kylan O'Danos and he threw himself into his studies as a Wolf of the North to prove to his family that one could fight physically just as well as with magic. As his skill increased his purpose was revealed to him.

He began to wander the land meeting kindred souls, and those who would oppose him in almost equal numbers. From the Gnolls of Blackburrow to the Drolvargs of Dreadlands..he has worked hard to make his name one that evil creatures should fear. With his purpose clear to him, Sargman continues to go forth and do battle to protect those weaker than him, and make the world of Norrath safer for those who would do no harm to others.
