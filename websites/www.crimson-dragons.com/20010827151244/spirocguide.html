The Spirco Hunting Guide

by Hexer Winzig

Recommended Level: 40-50

    The Spirocs are actually rather simple to hunt and kill. The Spirocs spawn in Timourous Deep. The best way to get there is to take the barrel barge from Oasis docks. I suggest binding here at the docks if you can. Don’t worry non-evil races you wont be KoS at the island you get off at. The approximate location of where they spawn is 5800, 1200. First a little information about the Spirocs. The highest level is the Proven. These are approximately level 40-41 and have roughly 3000 hps. Next are the Watchers. These are approximately level 38-39 and have roughly 2500 hps. Lastly there are the Lightfoots. These are approximately level 36-37 and have roughly 2000 hps.

Soloing: Being a wizard, I found it easiest to find someone to SoW you and then kite them with the Bonds of Force spell to snare. Druids can follow the same approach. Kiting works extremely well here. For Enchanters, I have seen Adrecia use a mez, nuke, mez method which appeared to work well. For Clerics, I think I saw Doogan solo these using the root nuke method. I would discourage the root and nuke method because it can be exceedingly dangerous. A note for Enchanters and Clerics though is that Doogan and Adrecia were both above 44 when they tried this so I suggest waiting until then to try and solo. Necros can use the old dark and fear approach with their pet dishing out most of the damage. As for Shamans, I saw Keeka using the root and DoT method and it seemed to be working very well. She said that after 3 DoTs you can stick the pet on and it shouldn’t taunt the Spiroc. Tanks, don’t even try soloing.

Grouping: I suggest having at least one person have snare in the group. Keep the mob snared and have it always aggro on the caster. The tanks should follow the Spiroc slashing as they go. Don’t taunt tanks! As for a group of tanks, you can try but I doubt you will be able gain good experience with all the tanks you will need.

A few more things to know about Spirocs: The Provens can triple attack for 99 damage so I recommend not getting hit. (I’m not bashing on tanks btw I just doubt they can sustain this much damage very long). Don’t turn your back on these bastards! They are capable of some serious backstab damage. I have personally gotten nailed for 357 so be very careful. Also, they will attack in groups. Usually there are 2 Spirocs that spawn on each platform (except for the very top area). There are certain locations where you can only pull 1 at a time. The 3rd platform on the tower (you will know it when you see it) is a good spot to pull 1 at a time. (Pull the one in the front first silly : P) There is also a single house at the location 6270, 1830 where only 1 will spawn. Be careful here though because another will sometimes be another that will wander by but you can usually time it so you only pull the one. The Spirocs don’t see through invisible so that is always nice. Unfortunately, the Spirocs have a nasty habit of “poofing” or disappearing when they are about to die (no doubt this is a bug). If this is the case, simply go to wherever the Spiroc was pulled from and he will be waiting for you there to finish up the kill. Finally, I met a lot of really nice people here. One of which, Willful, actually said “I love Gnomes.” So if that doesn’t convince you to try this place I don’t know what will. Well that is all I can think of.
