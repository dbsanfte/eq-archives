The Introduction to Tahnlu

By : The Interviewer

Tahnlu was born in San Diego, California. You may know it as, Qeynos. What do I mean by San Diego? Simple. Unlike the rest of you, Tahnlu wasn't born to this date. As a matter of fact, according to the History Charts, Tahnlu hasn't even been born yet. To tell you the truth, he shouldn't even be up and walking around. As we know it, Tahnlu doesn't exist. Yet he does? Yes Ladies and Gentlemen. Tahnlu was born over five thousands years, from now.

How is that possible you ask? No one has ever figured that out yet. Little is know about Tahnlu himself. The only way we will ever know about Tahnlu, is by talking to him. Problem is, Tahnlu keeps to himself. He seems very hesitant towards what he says. He gives the impression that, he wants to tell us, but for some reason he can't. He does however, give us many hints and tips about the future. He seems to know things before they happen. Well Historical events I should say. Not just every day things.

So why is Tahnlu back in this date? What drove him to come back? Questions that I still seek the answers to myself. Fear not though my friends. Tahnlu is a man of peace. He has not one Cruel intention in his heart. He strives to rid this land of evil. He seems to never want to rest, until he frees this land of the Hatred, Greed, Pain, Disease and Suffering that fills it. He knows he will Never be able to do this all by himself. Yet he still tries hard.

Even though I know little of Tahnlu. I still have managed to get together a nice collection of information about him. I have broken this information down into, what I feel, are different events in his life, different times, that all lead up to this date. Some information maybe missing or cut short. Please do not be upset. That information I am still trying to get myself. So hang in there. The pieces of Tahnlu's puzzle, to who he is and why, is slowly coming together.

-The Interviewer.
