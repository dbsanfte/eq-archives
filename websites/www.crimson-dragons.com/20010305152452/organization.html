"Lead, follow or get out of the way!" -- Lee Iacoca

The Officer Structure of Crimson Dragons is designed to minimize the amount of work that a single player must do to keep our guild running and maximize the amount of playing time for us all. Crimson Dragons Officers choose to sacrifice a small amount of their time to help keep our guild fun, active and constantly changing. Every Guild Officer has an important set of duties to perform and will execute them without hesitation. The Guild has ten main officer positions and several support officer positions. The ten main officers, or "lead" officers, comprise the Dragonian Council. The remaining support officers comprise the Dragonian Guard.


The Dragonian Council

The Dragonian Council is the body of officials in Crimson Dragons who make all major policy decisions and keep the guild running on a day-to-day basis. The Dragonian Council is comprised of ten officers, lead by the Guild Leader. Major decisions are voted on by the council with each member casting a single vote. In the event of a tie vote, the Guild Leader has the responsibility of making a final decision on the issue. Decisions voted on by the Dragonian Council may include removal of guild members, additions and changes to official guild policies and rules, and any other issue that is deemed important to the maintenance of the guild.


Members of the Dragonian Council

Guild Leader

Current Guild Leader - Keeny Cubscout

The Guild Leader is the end of the line for all decisions made within the guild. As the Chairman of the Dragonian Council, it is his or her responsibility to bring issues of importance to the attention of other officers and guild members. He or she is the keeper of the guild tag and is responsible for maintaining it always. The Guild Leader acts as a coordinator for every other Crimson Dragons officer and member. It is his or her responsibility to delegate authority to other officers and members of the guild. The Guild Leader has the final say on all entries and removals of members from the guild. The Guild Leader must be well spoken, ever vigilant, highly active and extremely knowledgeable about the Everquest gaming community.

Guild Initiations Officer (GIO)

Current GIO - Treawna BraveSword

The Guild Initiations Officer is responsible for supervising the recruitment and initiation of all new members into Crimson Dragons. The Initiations Officer must check initiations mailbox for new applications on a daily basis and post those applications on the Recruiting Forum. He or She must seek out and interview new applicants as well as their sponsors. The GIO is the official moderator of the Recruiting Forum and must track applications and see that they are processed in due time. The Initiations Officer must be a dillegent, dedicated officer with good organization, interviewing and administrative skills.

Guild Ambassador

Current Ambassador - Adrecia Tru'Ril

The Guild Ambassador is responsible for all diplomatic relations between Crimson Dragons and the Everquest community. The Guild Ambassador serves as a public relations hub for reporting the activities and events of the guild to the various Everquest news boards and information websites. The Guild Ambassador must always represent the guild in a favorable fashion and be ready to answer any questions that players or other interested parties may have about Crimson Dragons. The Ambassador must be an excellent writer and be quick in his duties of reporting noteworthy guild news to the public. The Ambassador is also the first point in establishing communications and relations with other Everquest guilds.

Guild Events Officer (GEO)

Current GEO - Maintain Statusquo

The Guild Events Officer is the person in charge of scheduling and organizing all guild events. It is his or her responsibility to conceptualize, plan and organize guild events for Crimson Dragons as well as assist others who wish to plan events. This Officer will also take ideas for events from the Crimson Dragons membership and work them into existence. The Guild Events Officer must actively assist the Guild Webmaster in updating and maintaining the Events section of the website and event calendar. The GEO must be an extremely active member of the guild, who has a nack for planning and a good imagination.

Guild Pagekeeper

Current Pagekeeper - Sheyla Fyreblade

The Guild Pagekeeper is responsible for the design, construction and day-to-day maintenance of the guild website. The Guild Pagekeeper oversees any decisions made regarding the web site and is responsible for keeping it well guarded and safe from outside intrusions. This officer must be extremely proficient in web design technology and be available to make updates to the site on a daily basis. The Pagekeeper must have excellent communications skills.

Guild Treasurer

Current Treasurer - Yurialdo Merchantmen

The Guild Treasurer serves as a bank, storage facility and merchant for the entire guild. The Treasurer is responsible for taking up donations of equipment, items and money from guild members to be stored in the Guild Treasury. Members may ask the Treasurer to sell or buy items for them under the rules set forth by the treasurer. The Treasurer is responsible for maintaining an accurate log of all items within the treasury and keeping that information posted on the guild website. The Guild Treasurer must have excellent communication and bartering skills, as well as plenty of time to devote to auctions.

Guild Tactical Officer (GTO)

Current GTO - Kilnor Shadowdagger

The Guild Tactical Officer is the strategic backbone of the Crimson Dragons. In situations where heavy strategy is required, the first person to call on should be the Guild Tactical Officer. Thus, this officer must have knowledge of general strategy, as well as knowledge of different areas of the game. This officer should be able to evaluate a raid party and determine their strengths and weaknesses somewhat quickly, and based on that, evaluate their limitations in the situation they're in. This officer should be attending most of the events where he's needed. Finally, the Guild Tactical Officer should be available to answer common questions about different areas of Norrath.

Guild Security Officer (GSO)

Current GSO - Vanzant Bravesword

The Guild Security Officer is the enforcer of rules and policy within the guild. In the event that problems, complaints or issues arise between members of Crimson Dragons, the Security Officer is responsible for investigating the case and reporting his findings to the Dragonian Council. The GSO must be a member of strong moral character and unfaltering integrity.

Academy Headmasters

Current Headmasters - Senine Deathwind, Hexer Winzig, Suriel Spiritweaver

The Academy Headmasters are the three officers in charge of maintaining the Academies of Melee, Sorcery and Mysticism. These three Headmasters are responsible for supervising the placement of class mentors as well as the promotion of individuals in rank within their academies. These officers must look after all the members in their academies, making sure that they are making progress, receiving adequate help and attention and having fun. The Headmasters must be experts in their respective fields and be able to devote ample time to speaking with and assisting the members who fall under them.



The Dragonian Guard

The Dragonian Guard is an honorary, elite group of Crimson Dragons who have proven themselves to be worthy of this highest honor. The Dragonian Guard may be called upon by the Dragonian Council in times of need to help make decisions and maintain order in the guild. The members selected to the Dragonian Guard all have specific duties, much like officer positions, and have access to officer forums and meetings to voice their opinions. In times of war or great danger, the Guard serves as the personal protectors of the Guild Leader and his family.


Members of the Dragonian Guard

The Sergeant is responsible for maintaining order at guild events, as well as helping the Security Officer enforce the rules and policies of the guild. The Historian maintains the History section and Photoalbum section of the guild website. The Guild Historian must always be ready to answer questions that others may have about the history of Crimson Dragons. The Historian must be skilled in web design, photo editing and in-game photography.

Guild Awards Officer (GAO)

The Awards Officer assists in handing out awards to members who have earned them and must actively work to see that this is done on a regular basis. The GAO may also create new awards and modify existing awards to be given out to members. Assistant Pagekeeper

Guild Quest Master (GQM)
