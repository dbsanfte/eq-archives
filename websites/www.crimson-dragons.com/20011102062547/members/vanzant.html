Sit back, relax... I'll tell you my story. Don't be dissapointed though, because where I have been is far less interesting than where I plan to go. I am known now, by the name of VanZant BraveSword. VanZant is actualy my surname, but during my time serving with the Steel Warriors of Freeport, it is what I was most commonly called. The surname BraveSword was bestowed upon my wife, Treawna, and I on the day of our marriage and it is what I have used ever since.

I was born Jonathan Alexander VanZant in the slums of Eastern Freeport, amongst what most people would consider the scum of Norrath. My very first encounters as a boy were with beggars, rogues, cutthroats and scoundrels. I simply came to know them as my friends. You would be surprised how friendly and welcoming those types of people can be when they look on you as one of their own. I still have many connections there and even though the Freeport Militia no longer accepts my presence there, I view it as my home.

My father was never a large influence on my life. He was a sailor, and when he was not out to sea he could almost always be found in the local taverns. He used to tell me that his sea-legs were no good on land, and he didn't feel right if the world wasn't rocking and swaying like the ocean. From him, I did inherit a love for the sea, and have always enjoyed my journeys across the deep bodies of water in Norrath. Unfortunately, I also inherited his affinity for the drink, although it isn't quit ethe necessity for me that it was for my father. He also taught me to fish. That is a hobby that is and will always remain my favorite pass-time. When I was twelve years old, my father vanished from my life. Word came to my mother and I that his ship had sank off the coast of Northern Ro. I was told that the survivors made their way westward through the blazing desert, but encounters with roaving bands of dervish cutthroats, orcs and other monsters of the sand scattered them and only a handful made it back to Freeport. I like to imagine that my father made it to a safe place... but he was really never the fighter that I would later become.

My mother was a calming influence on my life. I was rowdy as a youth, but I loved her dearly and would not disobey her word. She worked as a seamstress in East Freeport, and I often helped her in the shop. I could never quite get the nack for sewing, so I handled the manual labor. I stacked pelts, bundled cloth, measured customers and did just about anything else my mother asked of me. Treawna often teases me that I spent so much of my youth in a tailors shop and can not sew to save my life. My mother was not terribly old when she passed away but she was overcome with illness. She is buried in a small, grassy plot in East Freeport. I still visit it on occasion... when the militia are looking the other way.

I was 14 when my mother passed. I was ready to start experiencing "the real world." The real world for me was The Bunker. For those of you who do not know it, that is the name the Steel Warriors gave their famous arena in West Freeport. At first, I was hired on as a laborer. I cleaned up after the fights, sharpened weapons, sold tickets and performed whatever duties were needed of me. I picked up a few pointers on how to handle a blade and how to fight. When I was old enough, I began to enter matches where the opponents fought to first blood. I wish I could say that I was a champion from the start, and never lost a fight. That would be far from the truth though. I took more than my fair share of beatings from the veteran fighters. I was asked more than once why I didn't just give it up. Well, I've never been much of a quitter and in all honestly, I didn't have anyplace else to go.

I believe it was that determined attitude and subtle desperation in me that eventualy caught the eye of Cain Darkmoore. He was the leader of the Steel Warriors and took charge of training many young individuals in the ways of the blade. Cain was a good teacher. He pushed all his students hard and kept us extremely busy in the Hall of Steel. It was during that time of my life that I learned who I really was. I met Treawna there. It is even where I lost my right eye... but that is a different story, albeit an important one. Eventualy my training with Cain was complete, and I set out with Treawna to learn more about the world.

It wasn't long after starting our adventures together that we met and befriended many of those individuals who would become the first Crimson Dragons... but that my friends, is another story altogether!
