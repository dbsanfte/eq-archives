Biography

Keeny, like most Wood Elves, was born in the tree city of Kelethin. Like most Wood Elves, and most Rangers, at a young age, he was left to grow on his own. He met many friends along the way, of all types. One friend in particular, Foresee Ghostfaced, taught him the ways of Norrath.

Keeny, at a young age spent a lot of time killing off orcs in Clan Crushbone, that haunted the great city of Kelethin. One day, while hunting down orcs, one dark elf with the name of Dvinn, decided it was his job to slaughter many of the nearby fighters. Keeny took a beating and vowed to one-day kill Dvinn with the very dagger he injured Keeny with.

Unlike most in the area, Keeny ventured far away. He spent time learning the ways of Norrath by seeing most of it, and getting stronger, and wiser all the while. He saw the strange cat people of Kerra. He saw the dog-like Blackburrow gnolls. He saw many clans of Orcs and Gnolls along the way. When he reached adulthood, Keeny went with his dragoon dirk in one hand, and an axe he bought in his other to finally bring revenge to Crushbone’s Ambassador. He waited in the Ambassador’s favorite spot for a few minutes, and then the being appeared.

The battle ensued, and blows were landed back and forth, but Keeny realized, this man was very agile, and even without the knife he once possessed, he had quite a punch. Finally though, Keeny, with the help of a Paladin who Keeny had grown up with, Dvinn fell.

Keeny has since this battle grown stronger, and more wise. He has courage and honor when he fights, and always puts others before himself in any situation.
