Born in Freeport, Oonalik had a pretty typical beginning. Running around the Freeport gates, whacking on a bat, and running for dear life back to the guards, Oonalik managed to grow a bit and learn how to slip a dagger between the vertebrae of unsuspecting monstrosities. As Oonalik aged his desire to travel did also.

Oonalik wandered through the orc camps of the Western Commonlands, and learned that the orcs arent friendly. Once the orc camps lost his attention, Oonalik traveled to the Oasis of Marr where he got up close and personal with the Caiman population. On a slow desert day Oonalik wandered further south to the Southern Desert of Ro and bumped into Kilnor Shadowdagger. Kilnor was a seasoned Rogue and took Oonalik in.

Oonalik soon joined Kilnor's guild, the Order of DisOrder, a group of fellow Rogues who helped Oonalik to better himself and his skills. Then without warning Oonalik vanished. For about six months he was no where to be seen. Upon hearing of Oonalik's absence, the caiman population of Marr rejoiced, pronouncing the day of Oonalik's departure a day of rest for all reptiles. Just as suddenly as was his departure, Oonalik came back to Norrath. His arrival was quiet, and went unnoticed by many. Rumors spread throughout Norrath that during his absence Oonalik had grown more powerful and more adept at his trade. Caimans everywhere cower at the mere whisper of his name. When the time comes, you'll find Oonalik's blade nestled snugly between your ribs.
