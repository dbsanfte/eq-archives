Biography

Among the bangs, whirls, bells and whistles of the mighty underground Gnome city of Ak'Anon, there ran in playful innocence a young and spirited gnomish lad by the name Kilkin, only son of Kilkur and Yulaplik Cobblepot.

Between his seemingly mundane gnomish studies of arithmetic, valve carving and basic steam vent control, his father would often make Kilkin stand beside him and watch him tinker with his various contraptions. "One day, lad, this will all be yours. Watch and learn and make the name "Cobblepot" one that all Gnomes look to with pride and thanks. And if you do your work well, then all will look upon you as you pass and say to themselves, "Now there's a great Tinker!"" Such talk would send Kilkin into fits of giggling, but he wanted to make his father proud and would try to help his pop as best he could. Many times they would both come home with burnt eyebrows and broken spirits, but there was, thankfully, the occasional success. Momma Cobblepot would have a hearty meal prepared each night so that no matter what mood her boys came home with, it was soon joyous and buoyant nonetheless.

But Kilkin knew from an early age that Tinkering was not in him, and would hide as best he could that knowledge from his father for fear of his anger and disappointment.

What really inspired Kilkin was watching the gnomish guards, especially the Warriors; the famed Gemchoppers! Seeing them practice their drills and combat maneuvers and reading about their exploits against the terrible Minos of Steamfont instilled in Kilkin his real and truly felt pride of being a Gnome. He supposed that someday he would have to tell his parents his desire to become a Gemchopper when he got older, but that day was probably long off and he could continue, for the time being, his general gnoming about.

One day while enroute to the Steamfont Windmills in order to get some metal rods and sprockets for his pop, Kilkin came upon a hermit assumed by most gnomes as being long dead. The hermit was obviously not a gnome, but this true height was concealed by a course light brown jerkin covering him from head to foot, and patched here and there with various colored fabric. Beside him lay a couple small bags. More amazed than scared at this local legendary visage, Kilkin approached this rather gaunt figure standing under a tree, himself watching Kilkin approach with interest. "You're taller than I imagined, Sir, that is, well, I mean, if you aren't dead. I mean, I thought you were dead; that is...well, are you? Oh! I mean, are you the old hermit Trum Fillinstan?" Kilkin ended his sputtering introduction and was preparing to turn and half run the rest of the way to the Mills, when the old fellow raised his hand and, before Kilkin had time to flinch, struck Kilkin on the top of his head! Not a hard hit by all means, but certainly not a soft, frail hit either. The old man was chuckling. "Get used to that, boy!", the old man said. "Oft'times, your head will be the only thing between keeping yourself alive and feeding the belly of some foe. You were brave to venture closer to me, I suppose, but foolish else wise, eh?" A pause and the old man continued, "And yes, Trum is one of my identities, I seem to recall."

Kilkin, taken aback at this sudden confrontation with this strange fellow, rubbed his head and said, "Beg your pardon, Sir. Meant no disrespect. Just that you were thought of as dead or at least no longer in these parts; and, well, you just surprised me, is all." The old fellow looked at Kilkin with deep thought. "You are certainly one of the smaller Champions I've been instructed to contact." he said. "A Gemchopper you wish to be, but that is also what must Be, Kilkin." Kilkin was dumbfounded the old man knew of his secret desire. "How can you know that!? Don't tell my pop! Besides, I'm not so sure I will even... Hey, you know my name?!". The chuckling was replaced by a serene and wizened countenance. "Ahh, Kilkin. My role in the Balance is nothing compared with yours. I happen to know many things of many souls and places. But yours is one of action; yours will be the means to maintain all you see around you and of places and realms unheard of." Kilkins head swam at these strange words and decided to take leave of this even stranger man. He was beginning to scare young Kilkin. He turned to go.
"Wait one moment." the old man said. "You will soon understand. Well, at least a moment of understanding before all is hidden once again. I have a small item of interest for you, Kilkin. Please take a moment to read it."

Thereupon, the old man reached into one of his bags and pulled out a piece of parchment. He handed it to Kilkin, who without even thinking, without even the slightest hesitation, took it. The parchment seemed old, but the writing had a silvery sheen to them. He read.

"The Eternal Champion is the Warrior of Fate that is Destined to War against the Extremes which Threaten each Cosmic Plane of Existence. The Champion exists in many Guises throughout the Spheres of the Multiverse. He works often times not even knowing that he is Serving any other Purpose but his Own Ideals. The Eternal Champion always Serves the Balance in some form as he Embarks upon Crusades through the Myriads of Planes seeking to Restore Balance to his World. With few exceptions, he knows not his Past Incarnations nor his Future Identities which are Inevitable and Doom-Ridden."

Reading them, it was almost as if Kilkin were seeing through them, as if looking through a window. The words suddenly became alive, pictures, thoughts, memories, deeds of valor washed over Kilkin; things that seemed familiar, things that have happened before, will happen, happening now. Were these dreams? Memories? Faces and memories half-remembered, others totally unfamiliar. People and places not of Norath; of places and planes thought the whole Multiverse...

He was the albino Elric of Melnibone riding the Ship of Fate bound for Pan Tang and woe, his friend Moonglum standing by his side; then he was Corum of the Silver Hand, last of the Vadhagh, riding a Broughette with Jhary-a-Conel enroute the City of the Doomed Folk, and to slay a god; then he was Dorian Hawkmoon, the Duke von Koln, riding against Baron Meliadus and the Host of the Dark Empire. Other names and memories came to Kilkin. Was he really all these souls? One soul? Aubec, Rackhir, Asquinol, Lincoln. And there were more, more. All seemed doomed. All seemed related. The last soul was the most tortured. Erekose the Eternal, forever doomed to know all his identities, all his deaths; to know nothing else but battle upon battle, blood upon blood, Woe and Dread to the end of time, all in the Service of The Balance.

Kilkin awoke as if from a deep sleep, but was still standing before the old man, the parchment gone. His mind was racing with what he just ... witnessed? Lived? The hermit looked sadly upon Kilkin. "It is true, you will be more than a Gemchopper, Kilkin. You are one Aspect of the Champion Eternal. You are to serve The Balance of Norath in this Plane of Existence. To maintain Good versus Evil. To Maintain the Balance. Were either to overcome the other, the Multiverse and all its Spheres of Existence would end either in utter chaos or in dismal stagnation. Either end would be the end of All. The Balance must be maintained!"

Kilkin was shaken to his very core. "Why should I know this? Why have you shown me this terrible dream? Who am I really? How can I continue to ...". "You will continue to do that which you have done before, that which you shall always do. Right wrongs, slow rights. Keep both Good and Evil in constant equilibrium. You shall know great enthusiasms, great devotions, and know the triumph of high achievement, as well as experience great failings and utter despair. You are to be that which you desire, that which you always were and always shall be; you are to be that for which the Balance created you in the Beginning. You are to be, again as always, the Eternal Champion, a Gemchopper, a Warrior!"

Kilkin sat down hard at those last words. He was both elated and frightened, happy and sad. And he Knew all that transpired here was true. It seemed this had all happened before. And suddenly the old man seemed more than familiar now; he seemed to be a long lost companion, confessor, friend; though different than the other identities he knew of him. Kilkin looked up at the old man and smiled. "Yes Kilkin, though we are not to be companions in this realm, we have our duties here also. And here in Norath there are more than Minos to be fought! There are Dark Times brewing here in Norath, too." Kilkin stood. "And I am to forget all that you have shown me, again, I now know." Kilkin sighed and continued, "It was good to see you again, my friend, and I wish you were to be a part of my life here, also." The old man nodded and said "The Balance has it's own purposes, I suppose, but know that your role here is just as important as in the other realms and you shall do well, even though we be separate this time. Fare well for now, friend Kilkin. It is time."

Kilkin began to bow and suddenly he found himself on the road to the windmill. He was lost in some forgotten daydream and realized that he was farther from the Mills than he should be. His daydream was of some old man and some freighting words, but it soon seemed to pass and he was happily enroute to the Mills. It was getting late so he hurried.

And it came to pass that Kilkin indeed became a Gemchopper; learned the tactics and strategies of the Warrior. He traveled far, made many friends, slew many foes. And Ak'Anon was proud of her son. Kilkins father, too, boasted of Kilkins travels and adventures, though he hoped that some day his son would at least attempt a Tinker or two.

Kilkin reveled in his many exploits of valor and despaired in his many gloomy defeats. And always, at the edge of his thoughts, were seeming memories of battles he fought, would fight, somewhere, some when, that crossed and melded into one. Thoughts that gave him strength. Though he didn't recall it, he was more than Kilkin Cobblepot, Gemchopper, Warrior. Unbeknownst to him, he was yet another Aspect of the Eternal Champion, Defender of the Balance. And the Balance was satisfied.
