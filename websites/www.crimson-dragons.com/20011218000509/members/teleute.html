Teleute's family was very different than most people's in the city, her parents were necromancers. Not necromancers though how most people think of them, they were hippie necros. She had a very happy childhood and loved her parents very much. As a child she played with the skeletons in her yard and would run around with her dog Boney. Her dad was a used necronomicon seller in the aquaducts of the city and her mother was a homemaker, who made the best pixie chip cookies in the world!

As she grew up Teleute was drawn more to the arts of conjuration and evocation than to the dark arts. Her parents were very supportive when she told them that she would like to be a magician. But she was a reckless youth and would skip classes and sneak out at nights to go drinkin with her friends. She got very behind and most people in her class had passed her up. Her parents weren't very happy with her. She decided to leave home and try to make it on her own.

She met lots of interesting people and had many failed relationships as the years passed. One day during her travels she met a man named Trann Thunderknife. They got to know each other rather well and he took her into his family. She was welcomed with open arms and knew what a "normal" family was like. She felt she could count on them for anything and wouldn't have to worry if they would get beat up for goin in a city where thier profession was not liked.

As she started to venture farther and farther in to the world she bumped in to a bard named Harpi. They became almost instant friends. They could almost always be found hanging out together. As time passed they got closer and closer. Teleute started to have feelings for Harpi and hoped she felt the same way. It turned out that harpi did have the same feelings for Teleute. They talked about maybe getting married one day and if Norrath could accept such a thing. Well they have decided to get hitched as soon as Teleute gets accepted into Harpi's guild. and that brings up to date in Teleute's life.
