The Crimson Dragons Code of Conduct

"Have character, but don't be a character." -- Richard Marcinko

  1. Don't Steal Kills....EVER. It is a violation that is subject to immediate termination unless deemed necessary by the guild leader or a guild officer.

  2. Don't engage in arguments with other players over /shout or /ooc. This makes the guild look bad and is generaly annoying for everyone. If you feel it necessary to discuss differences then take it to /tells.

  3. Don't hog loot! Whenever a Crimson Dragon is in a group you are representing us as a guild. Don't be greedy and give everyone a fair chance to loot. Be respectful of the loot order that has been decided on by the rest of the group. Don't loot out of turn!

  4. Don't over-use guildsay. This is a great channel for asking questions, seeking advice or looking for a group, but don't have active conversations with a single person, because the green text can get excessive and be an irritation to others. If you have to talk to a single person, take it to /tells. Never use foul or abusive language on guildsay.

  5. Don't beg for items. If you are at a level where there is an item you should definately not have, then don't beg for other members to get one for you. If you are at a level where that item is within your grasp, then others will be more then happy to help you get it, but we don't actively support excessive tweaking.


Member Responsibilities

  1. Do your best to attend all guild finctions. If you are online during the time of a guild event, you should do your best to attend. If no effort is seen on your part to make it there, then action may be taken by guild officers. All events will be scheduled well in advance and there is no excuse for not knowing about them.

  2. Check the website regularly! Guild members and leaders often post news and events on the guild forum. You should spend a few minutes at least every other day to browse the website and see what is going on.

  3. Be helpfull to other members. If you can provide a service or information that another member needs, you should be willing to do so. Helping each other is what we are here for, and favors to others are always looked on in a good light by other members and officers.

  4. Be a good representative of the guild. This is an open ended responsibility, because all members should know what it means to be a good EQ player. Do your best to be the best... thats all it takes.

  5. Have FUN!!! This is the most important aspect of the game. The challenge and excitement of the game should always stay fun. If it isn't, then you're doing something wrong.


Policy on Secondary Characters

Lots of members have secondary characters. However, the guild will only allow two secondary characters per member into ths guild. Secondary characters must go through the exact same initiation process as others, this goes for regular members as well as officers. An officer may NOT sponsor their own secondary character for admittance into the guild.

Open Door Policy

Officers of the guild are here for your assistance. If you have a problem or concern, you may approach any officer. If he or she can not solve it, then they WILL find someone who can. No problem is to small or unimportant to seek advice and you will never be turned away.
