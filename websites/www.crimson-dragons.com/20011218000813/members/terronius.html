THE LIFE OF TERRONIUS REX

Terronius's father was head guard. Then came Sir Lucian. When my father found out about Lucian's plot to take over Freeport he was murdered in his sleep. But his pregnant wife escaped. 9 months later she gave birth to not one but two strong healthy boys knowing Lucan would hunt them down and kill them she split them up. She sent one to live with her husbands best friend a cleric in a distandt monistary. The other lived with her brother not far from freeport. The twin brothers were kept secret from one another for if Lucan found one he might get info on the other. They each vowed to avenge there fathers death.

After they reached maturity they each set out for more training and adventuring. When one day this young cleric (Vageeta) stumbled accros a battered Paladin (Terronius) and something clicked. They later found out they were long lost twin brothers. knowing this they voued never to leave the others side. And revenge was the first thing on there list.

One day while aventuring Terronius met a mighty warrior (Vanzant) they hit it off immediatly. They seemed to make a good hunting pair. Vanzant had many friends that shared in the beliefs that he and Terronius shared so he formed a partnership. Hence starting the Crimson Dragons.
