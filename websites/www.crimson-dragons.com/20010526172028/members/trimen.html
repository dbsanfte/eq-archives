Greetings Traveler. I am Trimen the ForestTracker. Let me begin my story by telling you of my beginnings. I grew up in the City of Qeynos. My fater was a Rouge pirate, my unckle was once a member of Antonius Bayle's royal guard, and my mother was druid. Father was a trickster and thief he would rather guzzle down a few pints of ale then open a book and do some reading. He was also very charming. His gift made him irresistable to the ladies, no wonder mother fell for him. He traveled the seas seeking adventure, he was a wandering soul. It was on one summer evening that his ship docked in the harbor of qeynos and he did disembark. He went to the nearest bar and there he met up with his brother. The two shared a pint and moved out of the city. on a drunken binge he stumbled across the hills of qeynos where he was beset by a pack of wolves. too drunk to remeber where he put his dagger, he became easy prey, if not for my mother. She rescued the drunk pirate and healed his wounds in the sacred glade fo sure falls.

No sooner then father's hang over dissipate, did he woo my mother. It was not till the nightfall of the following day did he remember that Unckle was with him when the wolves attacked. He hurried back to where it happened with mother in toe. There was no sign of death there and mother said she saw no one else. Terrified he ran back to the city. There he found out that the once proud guard had made a fool of himself in the prescence of King bayle. His punishment was being striped of rank and office. As an outcast he felt that Qeynos can no longer be home, so he joined my father in the travels of the world. For some divine reason my mother joined them as well. Two seasons came and went and my parents married. It was shortly there after that I was born unto them, several seasons after my siblings were born as well. Seeing as how the life on the ocean was not good for a child mother decided to return to Surefalls to raise us along with the son of uncle's best friend a barbarian from halas who they traveled with. Uncle and father decided to stay on the open seas. he gave up the life of piracy thank to mother's persuasions and became a legitamate sailor.

It was on a calm day that followed a great storm, that word came to us that the ship father was on was late for port. Mother knew something was wrong, for some reason she knew he was in trouble the night they first met, some divine fate brought them together. A week passed and still no sign. ships were dispatched to investigate. they returned a week later after retracing the route and found several survivors. A strange storm destroyed the ship and most of the crew mysteriously vanished. among them my father. Uncle was one of the luckier ones. Mother felt that she could find them so she left to search leaving me and my siblings with my Uncle. That was the last we ever saw of her.

Uncle raised us in Surefalls for several seasons before abandoning us with the druids there. With no head of the household and me too young to raise a family, we were split up. Mater Sureshot took me in, my younger brother Treshem became one of the druids. The silent fist clan showed interest in my brother Lawreck. Young Cerus had a inner light that guided him to the temple of the prime healer or so the paladin said. Young Tetheral was sent back to the fronzen halls of Halas.

Life under the care of Master Sureshot was interesting. He showed me that with the gifts of my mother and father and with the traing of the ranger's of the Glade I could become one of them, a ranger of the Goddess of Tunare.

It was with the training I did become what I am today. In my early youths I did travel with several of my fellow rangers. One of which is my good friend even to this day. A Young beautiful ranger named Senine. She was easy on the eye's but deadly with a bow. We did hunt together in the home of the enemies of the glade, Black Burrow. There we had fun adventures. On one such occasion a little gnome pestered us showing that he can best us by slaying a gnoll before we can. Well neither of us enjoyed the idea that this brash spell weaver was using us as a unwilling human shield. It was here where the signs of my heritage showed. I devised a plan to get revenge on this person. I became the bait to this trap. I walked up a ramp the hung over a small lake to where two elite guardsmen stood. I asked them if they knew where Surefalls was since i was lost. They looked at each other and charged me. The gnome thinking i beseiged them began to cast a spell, I then dove into the lake below. The gnolls seeing that offered no threat turned their complete anger to the gnome below. When I surface I was met by my good friend and laughingly told me what became of the gnome. The gnome survived by running from the gnolls all the way to qeynos, and other adventures took out the guards in his abscence.

As Seasons came and went I joined a group of Fellow travelers who called themselves The Millenium Sodality. They looked to be a promising band of adventures. Shortly thereafter the truth came out. They were nothing more the a sniveling band of people wanting the best things in the world and not wanting to work for it themselves. I felt that their kind should be shuned so I left. Many seasons passed after leaving them and I was alone. I traveled the world with only the cold ground as friend. Even though you might think rangers enjoy the open, but even we get lonly too.

i traveled the world from one side to the other seeing many sites meeting alot of new people, but always I was alone. In a room full of people I was the lone wolf. Off on my own scavaging about, Until she found me. Senine had grown up to be a great ranger, her skill surpassed me her grace was still there and more over she was happy. Delighted that my childhood friend was standing before me in good health I asked her many questions that she was all too happy to answer. She told me of a band of adventurers that knew the true meaning of teamwork, of how they were as close to one another as atrue family should be. It was not the fact that these were the people my destiny pointed me to join, it was because she was part of them. She is like a sister to me. So if this group calling themselves the Crimson Dragons was good enough for her, then by Tunare they are good enough for me. Since I my family was broken up I have not known a true family since I lost my parents, that was until I joined their ranks.

That is my story friend, from humble beginning to well... tell you the end when it happens for now I must go. Good Journey traveler may you find grand adventure.
