Kaadil was born and raised in Surefall Glades and the surrounding country side. He grew up with the animals and nature as his companions, where he became one with them. He was taught his duidic skills from the druids of old who have been long forgotten and became nature itself in all its gradeur. As he grew older he realized there must be more to the world than what he had seen, so he set off on the grandest adventure of his young life. Along the way he met a grand warrior and they quickly became good friends and for many seasons they traveled and adventure together. But alas they were separated and kaadil became distrusting of the many races and adventurers he encountered and turned back to the solutide life of the land and nature. Then Destiny in her grand design cross the pathes of the druid and warrior once again, the warrior had grown much and become involved with a very close knit group of adventures and again showed kaadil that there were truly people worthy of knowing out there. So now I travel the worlds helping those less fortunate myself and marvel at the wonders i encounter.
