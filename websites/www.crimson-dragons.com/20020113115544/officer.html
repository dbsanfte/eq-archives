The Officer Structure of Crimson Dragons is designed to minimize the amount of work that a single player must do to keep our guild running and maximize the amount of playing time for us all. Crimson Dragons Officers choose to sacrifice a small amount of their time to help keep our guild fun, active and constantly changing. Every Guild Officer has an important set of duties to perform and will execute them without hesitation. The Guild has nineteen officer positions The officers are grouped into four teams. Each team has one officer designated as "team lead".






The Admin Team

he Admin Team was created to be the ruling council for the Crimson Dragons.

The Resource Team

he Member Resource Team is designed to provide a point of contact for every member in the guild. This process starts at recruitment with the Intiation Officer who screens the applications and posts eligible candidates to the forums for review by the current membership. Also as part of this team are the Headmasters of the various schools. The Headmasters are here to answer any questions members may about their respective classes, strategies and also to be a mentor to young dragons as they find their way in our ranks
