I formed many groups and more often then not they included members of the Iksar race. One of the people i met early on was Szarlok. We have spent more than a little time taking out all sorts of the lakes less hospitable inhabitants. I quickly found out that they were not the worst this place had to offer. I then changed my attantions to the Sarnak collective that seemed to control this area. After my many battles I kept hearing tales of other places in this dangerous place.

Ive met many different people in my travels, seldom leaving Kunark except when I heard tales of a warrios finely crafted armor. After getting a set of this armor, i returned to kunark till i was already 42 seasons old. I have now traveled many parts of the world and have been lucky enough to make many friends along the way.
