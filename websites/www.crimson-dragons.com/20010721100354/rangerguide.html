A Ranger Guide

by Trimen Foresttracker

Welcome to Sure falls young one I am Trimen, Ranger mentor of the guild "the Crimson Dragons". Like you I was once unskilled in the ways of the ranger. Come in and I will show you our path.

Take a seat on the floor as where we go chairs are useless. Let me first tell you who we are. We are hunters of hunters; we are slayers of those who defile nature. We are the eyes and ears of Tunare's wishes. We are fighter, and we are spell caster, we are Rangers. Our ways are misunderstood by even those who call us brother or sister, we are cunning, we are vicious, we are a mix bag of many talents. Let us begin training in those talents, but first I must school you in what we can do before you commit to any one thing.

Well first off we must begin our training by choosing a weapon to use. I see the goddess gave you a sword and a note read it first and do as it says. Master Sureshot is just outside the cabin if you are wondering. Ah yes there you go a nice shirt to wear. Rangers may be masters of armed combat but our armor is light and not as effective as the brutal warrior or even the pious paladin but it is enough to get us through the day. Remember a Ranger's best weapon and armor is the head on their shoulder's. Try not to over power you opponent through brute force, use some tactics if you're alone or work as a team when you are in one. Now that you have some basic essentials let me show you some skills you will need to learn as you become more skilled.

(Level 1 skills: 1hand slashing, 2hand slashing, Piercing, Archery, 1hand blunt, 2hand blunt, and throwing): First off that sword will do you no good if you do not know how to use it. Swords may be the most useful of weapons but there are other weapon types that may become useful in the future. For instance the bow is a vital part of our calling. With a bow a foe is rarely too far to injure. Once you become skilled in it you can strike vital parts of the creatures anatomy causing excessive damage. We can also use piercing weapons like daggers and spears; we can also become skilled in clubs and large hammers. The weapons you choose to master may not be the best choice in the future so you may need to become skilled in several varieties. As an added bonus we are blessed with the gift to use two weapons at once. This ability does not come to use till later in life but once it does your life will change for the better. Though we cannot don plate armor we can whittle down a foe fast with dual weapons. Two heads one may be better then one the same can be said about swords. Strike fast and strike many times.

Weapon combat is not our only ability; we can lay a swift kick to our foe. This may not be the most honorable attack but the fact of the matter is, we are not paladins. Honor and trust is their forte our is clandestine at best. Nobility will win you friends; deceit and tricks will win you the battle. We cannot afford to fight head on, we must do as rouges at times and use stealth and pick out our battles carefully. Once you get a feeling for weapons combat the goddess will grant you the ability to cast spells. This is a rite of passage for a ranger. We become distinguished among the others of the world by our skills. Be it two swords or a spell we are quite able to harm other effectively. Ok now that I gave you a run down let's get you started. Let's go hunt.

(Level 1 skills; track and taunt): Sure you can hurt things but finding them can be a problem. All neophytes of all the classes begin training by fighting things close to home. Make sure you know where a guard is when you get in over you head. Let start by using a skill only we can truly master. Come here see this on the ground, this smudge, this was left by a snake you see it? Good.

This tell us the snake went north, this imprint here tell us a wolf went east and this foot print tell us that a decaying skeleton is right behind you, please turn around and take it out. Here let me get his attention this is called taunting this can save your friends in battle but do not be too brave a brave ranger is a dead one, ok you ready to fight this take him out. Excellent work though you need a lot more training. Come over here by these guards sit and rest, my word your injuries are more severe then I thought. Early in life your body is frail and cannot sustain much punishment, as you become more skilled you become becomes tougher and more apt to absorb punishment. Well that does not help you now does it?

Ok there are three ways to heal one's self. You can sit and let your body heal itself, this can be time consuming as when your body become more stout the length it takes to heal itself become excessively long. The second method it to use bandages, as you become more skilled with bandages you can repair yourself more effectively. The third way is to use magic. A ranger can cast spells relatively early in life thus we can heal ourselves fast and be back in the fray in no time. Take head that our healing spells are nowhere near as effective as others who can do the same. Oh good you're ready for more training. Let us find some more tracks ok I will let you go on your own. Come back when you are more skilled.

(Level 3 skill: Forage): Hi again you no doubt worked up an appetite let see what there is to eat, what you say you see nothing look under that tree root there, you see it here take this root taste it good right, A ranger can live off the land. We can find food and water in the wild so we need not visit towns save to peddle our ware. We are survivalists so make sure you keep a good stock of food on hand and keep practicing you may find things other then food.

(Level 5 skill; Kick): Welcome back ready to learn a new skill I see well this one helps in a pinch. Take your right foot and kick this training dummy. No, no, put more snap into it, there you go good. With this skill you can add to your offensive potential by merely kicking them. This skill will help you your entire life appreciate it learn it master it, now go back out there and become more skilled, what's that you are learning soothing in the hills well let me show you a place to hunt now. This is Black burrow a cave In Qeynos hills over there beyond that rise is the city Qeynos itself go there for supplies or just to meet other adventures we live in the glade because it is a more natural setting. Ok let's go in to the burrow. The foul beasts in here are known as gnolls. The can be formidable to one such as yourself but in time they will become a minor nuisance I am going back to the glade, return to me when you are ready to learn more.

(Level 8 skill; dodge): Welcome back, good work now it time to show you a life saving maneuver. Armor can protect you from injury but not all the time. As you have noticed you have suffered some injuries in your fights. This skill will help lessen your injuries. Strike me go on strike me. Ha you missed because I dodged your attack. Using this skill you can simply avoid injury. Go on try it yourself, good work now get back out there and do not come back till you are ready for more.

(Level 9 skills; Abjuration, alteration, evocation, divination, conjuration): Welcome back my you are becoming a fine ranger the goddess must be pleased, ok now it is time to learn magic. While your weapon and skills can get you through a fight, a magic spell here and there will make life a bit easier. Here let me show you a few spells. Very good you do catch on fast you remind me of well me. Now go back to the burrow and show the gnolls what you have learned. Come back when you are ready for more.

(Level 10 skill; sneak): nice to see you again. Ok here's a cunning skill. You can sneak around not making a sound. This can be useful if shop keeps do not like you simply sneak up to them and make your transactions. Ok the gnolls beckon you again.

(Level 15 skill; meditate): Good work you no doubt noticed that regain you strength to cast spells can be tiresome, ok it's time to clear you mind close your eyes and think of calming thoughts good now you mana is increasing quick see there you are ready to cast more spells. Using this ability you are quick to re-enter the fight but be careful, as you cannot se what is around you when meditating. Ok young one return to your training.

(Level 17 skill: Dual wield): My you do learn fast do you ok you are very skilled now and the gnolls must be becoming a minor nuisance it is time for you to go out into the world and find more fitting challengers, but first let me show you something. Show me you weapon swing ok now use your other hand. That's right hmm needs a little work keep trying excellent now you no doubt picked up a few weapons from those dirty gnolls or bought one, now its time to get a second. Now young ranger you are truly fierce. Go back into the world and show them your true skill.

(Level 18 skill; parry): You back quick then again you do learn fast ok here is another skill to add to you dodge ability, strike me and I promise not to move out the way go ahead try it. See how I blocked your attack with my weapon. With proper training and a little luck you can parry an attack launched against you, let's teach you how to parry properly. Needs some work but you can learn that out there in the field go now practice that skill.

(Level 20 skill: Double attack): Well I see you journey brought you back ok show me your attack swing. Hmm nice ok try it this way see how I can bring my weapon to bare twice in one motion? Good now try it your self. Excellent, now using that skill you can inflict more damage as you can attack twice with both weapons. Do not forget that kick I taut you not too long ago. With more training you can strike a foe five time in the duration it take them to strike once.

(Level 25 skill; hide): Your back good I was worried that you had fallen in battle ok you no doubt felt a few dishonorable hits while meditating here is a useful skill when you cannot cast camoflage. Now you see me now you don't. Using this ability you can rest or should I say meditate easy unseen by normal eyes. Beware are the undead and a few others can see the invisible and lay waste to the unsuspecting.

(Level 35 skills; disarm, riposte, auto meditate): I have not seen you in a while and frankly I am running out of things to tech you fine let us finish your training. You doubt noticed that you can meditate now without need of closing your eye to the world this help you avoid those nasty surprise attacks, ok remember that parry I showed you long ago ok this is a add on to that maneuver. Strike me, see how I deflected your attack and in the same motion nearly struck you this is a riposte attack. Turning your opponents attack into yours'. Learn it well as it can help you win a fight. Now for my last skill to train you, try to hold onto your weapon tight make sure you cannot drop it. Ok now go pick it up I just disarmed you. This skill can be very useful though it is one we cannot master. It takes many tries to disarm a foe so keep trying a unarmed foe is not as dangerous as one with a weapon. I taught you all that I know Go now True Ranger go now into the world complete in the knowledge of our ways.
