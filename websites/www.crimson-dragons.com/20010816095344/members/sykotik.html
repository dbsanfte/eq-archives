Sykotik was born in Halas the frozen wasteland of the barbarians. His father raised him alone as his mother died during childbirth. One day when Barde, Sykotik's father was hunting on the tundra when he was viciously assaulted by a band of ice orcs. There were too many and Barde fell to their blades.

Sykotik after learning of his father's demise wept for days, he then decided to venture out into the world and earn fame and fortune that his father had told him in stories.
