My dear mother was abandoned by my father when I was young. She taught me never to trust any man to bring me happiness and every word she said, I listened. She had one quest in mind set for me--to break as many hearts as I could.

I grew up on the tough streets of Freeport, where corrupt guards run the town and all sorts of scums roam the under city. My playground is where creatures crawl or scurry along. While playing outside the gates one day, I saw a man and a rock looking thing that he called a "pet." I asked him if I could have a pet just like him. He replied, "Go to the Academy of Arcane Science and hand this scroll in. They will instruct you on how to become a magician." I thanked him kindly and went to the academy and started my training.

Many years go by as I've refined my skills hunting bats, snakes, orcs, and cutthroats. It was time to go further out away from Freeport, so I headed to Oasis.

What a sight it was when I arrived at Oasis! Crocodiles ruled the shores and Sand Giants roam the sand. There was people everywhere and I've never seen so many people running around! I found a group of people hunting the bad orcs so I joined in their battle. We faught long and hard, yet the train of orcs never seem to end.

One day, for some reason, two guys proposed to me. I didnt know what to say or how to act, so I headed to The Temple of Marr seeking guidance. I have been alone for a long while and was ready for company. I said yes to one of them, but somehow deep down inside, I knew this bard wasnt the right guy for me--no man can bring me happiness.

I continued fighting by his side against the infinite orcs. As time went on, more orcs fell, and the longing to catch a glimpse of the druid, who runs by and buffs, grew. I called the wedding off, my heart now belonged to a stranger.

The never ending orcs was getting old really fast, so I journeyed to the Karanas. I found my new hunting grounds in South Karanas and continued my training. The druid came by quite often and everyday I longed to see more of him. One day, while on top of the aviak city, he levitated me and told me to follow. We went to one of the birdhouses that none other can reach.

He asked me to marry him!! There was nothing in the world I want more than that, so I nodded and said, "Yes." The months that followed were spent planning the big day in Erudin Palace. Finally the date was set and our closest friends and family all came to witness the union.

And the saga continues...

Epilogue

With the preachings of Erollisi Marr, I have learned to love again. My dearest Fennon, there is no other place I would want to be, but by your side.
