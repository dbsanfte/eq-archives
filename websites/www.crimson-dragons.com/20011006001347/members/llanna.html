I have no memories of my early youth nor even my family name. I was tutored in the fine arts of magic by Zarcof of Qeynos. Preferring the outdoors to the confines of the city I often hunted in the hills outside the city, one day I returned to find that Zarcof had been slain. My search to find the persons who had done this led me to the sewers beneath the city where corrupt officials allowed all manner of evil to dwell. I spent many seasons avenging the death of my mentor, hunting the corrupt and their allies within the city and throughout the Karanas.

My journeys have since taken me far away, to the wilds of Kunark and the wastelands of Velious where I continue to fight against the evil and corrupt.
