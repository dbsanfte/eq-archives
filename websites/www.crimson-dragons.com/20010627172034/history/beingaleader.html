Being A Leader

  

By VanZant Bravesword
————————
Hail Crimson Dragons,

In guilds like Crimson Dragons, or any other group for that matter, you can't lead by taking votes and following poular demand. When you lead, you lead from the front and you lead alone. Even if a guild is successful, it won't survive for long without a strong, independent leader. It's often painful to lead from the front, by yourself.

To be a leader who shoulders responsibility and decision making alone means having people constantly second guess you and tell you what a dumb shit you are. It means risking your reputation and maybe even your friends. It means having people who aren't happy who need scapegoats, and it frequently means looking in vain for people to help.

To top it all off, it is a leader's responsibility to give full credit for all of his organization's acomplishments to the members, but to shoulder the organization's failures on his own. And if a leader is really good, after it is all over and people look back, they will naturaly think that they did it all themselves.

Just something to Think About,
