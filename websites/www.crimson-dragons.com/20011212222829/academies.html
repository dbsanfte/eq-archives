The Crimson Dragons Academy System

"Training should be so realistic that it makes real battles feel fake." -- Richard Marcinko

Crimson Dragons supports three Academies to help foster knowledge within the different schools of discipline. These three academies group our members according to classes, so that they may learn different tactics, tips and ideas from each other. This sytem provides for easy access to experts that are ready to share their knowledge with those of less experience.

The Academy of Melee

( The School of Fighters )

This Academy is comprised of Warriors, Rangers, Paladins, Monks, Shadow Knights and Rogues. The members of this academy are the front line soliders of Crimson Dragons. They strive to master the art of physical combat, tactics and warfare. These mighty adventureres, strong in body as well as in mind form the core of the Crimson Dragons Fighting Force.


The Academy of Sorcere

( The School of Magic )

This School is the home of the pure casters, such as Wizards, Magicians, Enchanters and Necromancers. This Academy fosters learning of the arcane and scientific arts associated with spell casting. These brilliant and ever curious minds form the inquisitive heart of Crimson Dragons. Their active pursuit of knowledge serves to benefit the guild as a whole.


The Academy of Mysticism

( The School of Theology and Healing )

This Academy consists of those who draw their power from spiritual, natural and mystical sources, such as Druids, Shamans, Clerics and Bards. These talented individuals are the heart and soul of the Crimson Dragons, striving to achieve a spiritual balance within themselves and the world around them. Their powers of healing and nurturing make them a most valuable resource for the guild.
