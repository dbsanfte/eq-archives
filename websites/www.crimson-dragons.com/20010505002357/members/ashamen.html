As a druid, I am an expert runner! I much prefer the hit-and-run-and-snare method of fighting over the toe-to-to bashing that the warriors do. My pointy ears indicate I am much too intelligent for that sort of brawling! Now worshipped in many continents as a "Master Kiter" because I began taking on 3-4 mobs at a time with my daring method *takes a bow* - I must admit I do get a rush of excitement when I see three ravishing drovlargs chasing me, S-L-O-W-L-Y.

My little sis Whispee has too many boyfriends to worry about fighting with me, so I have joined my big sis (big as in older and bone structure!), Laurenaya (also wiser and richer than me, and I often want what she has), in her group of friends and co-fighters known as the Keepers of Kithicor. Together, and often with the help of other new friends we meet, we strive to stamp out the evils that try to overrun our land. My dream has been to fight the evil incarnate on the planes, and with this group of fine teammates, I have finally achieved that and hope to continue to wipe out the terrors in these areas until the likes of Cazic Thule himself cowers when he knows I am in his domain!

I love to explore new places and go on new adventures (something I need to teach my big sis), and to be daring and blaze new paths. No doubt a remnant in my blood from a relative in a time far, far past, named Spock. Oh yeah, I also like cats of all sizes.
