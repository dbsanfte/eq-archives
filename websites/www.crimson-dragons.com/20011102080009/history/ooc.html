The Out of Character History of the Crimson Dragons

  

As told by Vanzant Bravesword
————————
Well, the idea of forming the guild originated with three of us, and soon grew to incorporate all ten of the founding members. Myself, Treawna and Greyraidr had all known each other for quite some time... going back to a Starcraft and Diablo Gaming Guild called the Crimson Knights. Grey and I had litteraly built that guild from the ground up, and lead it succesfuly for quite some time. However, when Everquest came out, We found our true calling. The three of us left Crimson Knights and ventured out to explore the world of EQ.

Treawna and I were the first to get started playing regularly. Both of us being new to the game, we started a pair of Human Warriors out of Freeport. Later on, Grey purchased the game and started his own character on a path of glory and fame. With Treawna's charm and charismatic ways, it wasn't hard at all to attract friends, and my cavalier style and flare for the dramatic kept things exciting. The idea of forming a new guild in EQ had always been in our minds and the idea started to take shape as we gathered an ever growing circle of close friends.

It didn't take long before we ran into three more of our friends from the past. My real life friend Deci, who had been one of our best members in Crimson Knights had decided to make the cross over to EQ as well. He took to the game instantly, and has been stuck ever since. One day when Trea and I had just zoned into West Commons, I saw a name that I recognized immediately. Sevilnatas had been a very old aquaintance from Starcraft, knowing Grey and myself for well over a year. It turned out he had been playing EQ for quite a while already, and had a very nicely equipped shaman to show for it. He also let us know that another old Crimson Knight member, Braeldiar, had found his way to EQ and the Fenin Ro server as well. Brae had a Dark Elf Shadow Knight, and was invaluable to getting us started on some of the evil races and classes. With Deci, Brae and Sev added to our list of friends, we had a solid start on the way to reaching the ten founding guildmembers required by Verant.

One day in East Commons while I was hunting by myself, I ran into this Paladin by the name of SirTerra (Terronius) who was an excellent roleplayer and damn handy with the Laying of Hands. We got to know each other fairly well, and soon became friends. At the same time, Treawna had befriended several good guys. Gurt (Aerno) was a crazy wizard that liked to kill dervs, he fit in with our crowd instantly. His good friend, Gerard, soon found his way into the mix as well. Last but not least, while hunting Dervs one afternoon, we happened across the coolest little Half Elf Ranger named Main. With all of us becoming closer as friends and most of us being new to roleplaying, we had a lot of great experiences and learned a lot of new things together. Pretty soon, we were on our way to becoming a true guild.

During June of 1999, a little over a month after I had started playing, we completed our official guild application and were accepted by Verant. Soon after that, the GM Elionia came to me during a fight with a Minotaur and several Muddites in the Beholder Maze. I was being beaten rather badly at the time and had to take flight for the zone rather than stand and speak with her. Elionia, finding this extremely amusing... Inducted me as the leader and first member of Crimson Dragons... right in the middle of my mad dash to safety, with a train of Muddites, Minotaurs and an Evil Eye right behind me. Needless to say, it may indeed be the least boring guild creation in EQ history. I did make it to safety though, and soon after sought out all our friends to begin the process of building our guild. As I write this now, we are at the 4 month mark of existance and have risen to more than fifty members. Even now, we are still learning about the game and how to be a guild... and indeed, we are still and always will be holding strong.
