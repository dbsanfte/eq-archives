Dragon of the Week

For the week of August 14 to August 20, Kalasha is the Dragon of the Week. Kalasha wins this award for being constant source of help, aid and fun to fellow Dragons. Always willing to give of her time and abilities Kalasha is a glowing example of what Dragon of the Week is all about.

For the week of July 31 to August 6, Jherge is the Dragon of the Week. Jherge wins this award for always freely giving of his time and efforts. Whether it be for a teleport, quest or other need, Jherge is always quick to jump to the aid of his comrades. For the week of July 24 to July 30, Hexer is the Dragon of the Week. Hexer wins this award for giving his time and effort in helping with research. Hexer is also a joy to group with and willing to quickly jump to the aid of any needing guildmate.

For the week of July 17 to July 23, Tuxxen is the Dragon of the Week. Tuxxen wins this award for his generosity and for his joy for the game. Tuxxen makes Norrath much more enjoyable for all those around him.

For the week of July 10 to July 16, Tahnlu is the Dragon of the Week. Tahnlu wins this award for all the help he has provided so many brother and sister paladins in achieving there Soulfire quest and helping so many other members with advice and friendship.

For the week of July 3 to July 9, Fennon is the Dragon of the Week. Fennon received this award for selflessly giving up his time to aid fellow Dragons in achieving their Journeyman's Boots.
