A twig snaps nearby waking me, and jumping up from the bed of thistles, the figure of Noleriand Grayelven appears to me, bent over a campfire no larger than the other two I saw nearby. Set a ways from the entrance to the city of Paineel, our encampment is small, but very well prepared for the upcoming attack on the Warrens, home of a primitive clan of kobolds. I can barely make out the small shapes of several of my guild's members scouting the surrounding areas for any dangers. Most of my companions are dealers in the dark arts, and very shady fellows at that. This would in the past been against my morals and beliefs, and, given the chance, most likely would have killed any one of them. Betrayel by one's closest friends and family does has it's consequences on the mind of the betrayed, however...

I flinch as Noleriand steps on several more small branches, knowing that anyone could hear him in the quiet of this forest, known as Toxxulia. An important factor in my plans for this day involve not being seen or heard by any...

Kneeling, I grab a cup of hot water from a stone set near the fire, and toss some tea leaves from my pack into the cup. Sipping slowly, I look over those put under my authority by the leader of this raid, Hazor. To my dismay, the term authority has little meaning in reality, my charges being all my senior, and not too keen on taking orders from others, especially from a naturewalker such as myself. I still have a great deal of knowledge and wisdom to attain before I will be a true force, but that day will come...

For now, the camp needs to be cleared, to cover our tracks. Our band was commissioned by both Erudin and the Heretics of Paineel to deal with this threat, although the latter was a very secretive dealing, as I am told by my darker allies. The rewards and consequences for dealing with such shady fellows as the heretics remains to be seen...

As we enter Paineel, avoiding the undead guards in all our own manner, we make our way to the entrance of the lair of the kobolds. What exactly might lay down there, we do not know, although rumor has told of a magical living stream, and strange forged weapons held only by the kobolds. What shall happen will be seen very, very soon...

-Kevdysseus Ivorydog
