Read these installation instructions if you have not used EQWatcher before, and make sure to read through the whole introduction.  Please bear with me since you may be incredibly confused by the introduction itself, and EQWatcher is becoming much easier to use.  You may not need to understand many of the concepts to benefit from this program.  In fact, just by running EQWatcher it will automatically make sounds from tells and says, group chat, and guild chat, and will automatically count your combat damage and report it after each fight.  All of these options can be easily changed.

If you are new to EQWatcher it might also help when you start it the first time, to load the "INVIS.EQG" trigger set (click File->Load Trigger Set).  This gives you a warning when you are appearing while invis and is a decent starter trigger.
