Hi Sarah!

This is my journal of my trip to meet your Aunt Erin and Uncle Todd in Fort Worth, Texas. I had a very good time and learned much about the City of Fort Worth.

Fort Worth is located in the United States in the state of Texas. The city is in north Texas and is only an hour south of the state of Oklahoma. It is also very close to the city of Dallas, which is a 45-minute drive to the east. Fort Worth is smaller than Dallas, but is still a large city. Fort Worth is similar to Calgary, Alberta. The cities are about the same size and are both called ‘Cowtown’ because of the importance of cattle and cowboys to the cities’ history. It even has its own BRAND for its cattle. Here it is:

Your Aunt and Uncle said that it takes over 40 hours (3 ½ days) to drive to Fort Worth from Langley. To drive to Fort Worth, you have to drive through many states – Washington, Oregon, Idaho, Utah, Wyoming, Colorado, and New Mexico.

Texas is the second largest state in the US. In the south it borders Mexico and the neighbouring states are New Mexico, Oklahoma, Arkansas, and Louisiana. The state capital is Austin. The largest cities in the state are Dallas, Houston, San Antonio, Austin and Fort Worth. Texas is also called the Lone Star State because the state flag has a single star in it. Texas was its own country at one time before deciding to join the United States.
