It was Arirangs turn in VT but...

12 long, greuling hours of clearing VT in the middle of the night yeilded us the sight you see before us. That's right, Aten Ha Ra, Queen of Vex Thal and Big Hooters, fell to Anthology at 7:30 in the goddamn morning after an all night clearing session. I was thoroughly suprised and impressed by the dedication put forth by our guild to make this happen. We saw our chance and took it, and thus reaped the rewards that we worked so hard for.

Our day had started about 16 hours previous, at 3pm EST or so, when we entered Vex Thal with the intent to explore the second floor and kill Thall Xundraux Diabo, one of the last named left up in the zone. We had explored alot of the first floor and had killed Diabo Xi Va, Diabo Xi Xin and the Thall Va Xakra brothers a few days before, but we were eager to learn more about the zone. We had just entered the main hall when bam, the zone comes down. We log back in to a assload of mobs and a wipe but our CR was a cheery one as every named was now respawned. The city was ours. So we began the long task of clearing our way through hundreds and hundreds of mobs to reach the jackpot, the big kahuna herself, Aten Ha Ra. With no real previous knowledge of anything beyond the first floor, we slowly worked our way upward, taking down the various named mobs. Every named we encountered dropped with ease to our force on the first try. It was still a learning experience though, we had a few wipes and a few close calls, and one or two experiences with the ToV-Style Oh-Shit-That-Gater-Just-Brought-The-Entire-Zone type of gating. 12 hours later, at 7 o'clock in the morning of the next day, our still strong force stood in front of the portal to Aten's chambers. The hours were starting to take their toll on us, many of us had work or school in a half hour or so. But we held strong, ported in, and dropped Aten in near flawless form. I wanna thank everyone who stuck through those long hours to achieve what I think alot of people didn't think we would do.


We suck at group pics, specially when we're all half-asleep...

In addition to our crazy VT stints, we've gotten quite a few Emperor kills, including a poofed Emp corpse thanks to server repop. We also took down Manaetic Behemoth a few times to gain entrance in to Plane of Tactics. Hopefully it wont be long before the Zek brothers get a good beatdown from us. We made a pretty good attempt at Agnarr the Storm Lord, got him to 40% on our first try, which is pretty good considering we really had no idea what the hell was going on till we started fighting him. We've also gotten two of the three Halls of Honor trials completed. Just need Rydda'Dar to get into HoHB and we can start our attempts at Lord Mithaniel Marr. Stay tuned!
