They said we couldn't do it...

Yep, all that hard work and long boring hours of farming that godforsaken temple finally payed off... Unfortunatly Eelyen's been too busy keeping our asses in line and/or watching porn to update the page, so here I am a week later to tell you that the Emperor Ssraeshza has finally fallen to Anthology!

We sat at the Emperor's portal for hours on end, waiting for Blood to pop so we could get our chance to do what we've been meaning to do for months now. We made a small detour to the basement of the temple to give Cursed a healthy beatdown, only to have two of our loots ninja'd by a jealous member of another guild that just didn't quite have the power to kill him yet. Anyway, we put that whole fiasco behind us (the loots were later returned to us by GMs) and ran ourselves back up to the 3rd floor to plop our asses down in front of that portal and wait longer. As the night trudged slowly on, you could definatly notice that the wait was getting to us, people were getting irritable, goofy, and just downright odd (or maybe just drunk). Finally, we heard the words we had been dying to hear all night: "Hail, Blood of Ssraeshza". So we got up, buffed, and stomped on into the Emperor's room and beat the living hell out of Mr. Ssraeshza and his little buddy. It was an incredible fight, Pyrho stayed up the entire time, our healing was awesome, and the CC was near flawless. A marvelous job by everyone! And since the Emperor is dead, you all know what that means:

So now we're farming shards like theres no tomorrow and within the next few weeks we will all be working on "bringing this server to its knees" in Vex Thal. In between our shard farming we've also had time to beat down Seru, Terris Thule, Burrower, Aerin'Dar, Arch Lich and our good ole friend the Avatar of War.
