Paradigm of Blood



Paradigm of Blood is recruiting!

If you would like to be considered for membership, feel free to e-mail me at iutav@aol.com and answer the following questions:
1) Why do you want to join?
2) Who do you know well in the guild?
3) What is your level/class?
4) Are you in a guild right now? Which one? Why are you leaving that guild for us?
5) Why do you think you would make a good match with us?

You are required to be L40+ to be considered for membership. You are also required to have a sponser, someone inside the guild who is a good friend of yours.
That is not to say if your uncle isn't in the guild, that you can not join. By going on a few guild raids, we will consider you for membership, IF you meet all the other requirements. Send me or another officer a tell, and we'll try to get you into a group, or on a raid.

For any other questions or recruiting concerns, post on our message board.
