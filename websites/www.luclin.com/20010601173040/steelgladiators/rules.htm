Network Status

With other guild members

Be friendly. Always greet the guild by offering a salutation when you log on and a farewell when you log off. This way, the rest of the band knows who’s on at any specific time.

Always respond to a guild mate in need. If you can provide a service and are close by, do not hesitate to answer a plea for assistance. These requests may include buffs, teleports, binds, partying, corpse retrievals, and healing.

Sell and trade items at cost. If you wish to sell an item that a guild mate of another class may use, give one of them first crack at the loot and make sure you offer them at a special price for your brothers and sisters in arms. Usually 25-50% of normal auction price is fair for fellow Steel Gladiators.
The Game

No unethical charity! There is nothing wrong with giving a small item or two to lower level guild mates, but the fun of Everquest is earning your own money, weapons, armor, and items. So overdoing it is not permitted. For example, giving a newbie a full set of banded armor because you have switched to plate or handing over 100 platinum pieces is overkill. Giving a ring of +1 STR or a pair of banded boots is all right now and again, but not too often. Hey, you had to earn it, right?

No arguing amongst guild members in /guild chat. It’s a given that there will be disagreements. If you need to debate with a fellow Gladiator, do so using the /tell chat. Griping at one another so the whole guild can hear you is annoying and immature. If you cannot resolve your differences, feel free to /tell an officer to help settle the feud, but be warned, the rulings of the officers are final and may not be appealed.

When greeting a Steel Gladiator Officer in person, it is customary to salute or bow. It’s simply a sign of respect, not fealty. When greeting non-officers, the option to bow or salute is yours.

Whenever possible, party with fellow guild mates, especially when they need help obtaining quest items. That’s why we are here, to work together and you never know when you may need the help. It also looks good to see groups of Steel Gladiators traveling and fighting in packs.

Profanity in /guild chat is not just discouraged, it is not allowed. Simply put, be polite to each other. The officers will not tolerate members saying offensive comments to one another. There is a lot of teasing that goes on among friends, and that's fine, but there is a difference between that and plain old maliciousness...and we all know what that difference is. Nuff said?

Post to the Message Board often. Use it to keep in touch with your guildmates.

With non-guild members

Be polite. The Steel Gladiators are dedicated to wiping out the plagues of the land. This includes Orcs, Gnolls, Undead, and other evil characters that roam Norrath. Although you should be on your guard when greeting other travelers, always offer the hand of friendship first. You never know when you might need outside assistance.

Do not steal kills! As tempting as it may be, KS’ing is not tolerated in the Steel Gladiators. There should always be a way to share MOBs and spawns. If not, just hunt somewhere else.  If you are having issues with another KS’er, simply /petition a GM and report the offense. Do not risk suspension by trying to “get back” at your competition.

Do not loot fallen monsters that you did not kill. Unless you have permission from the characters that killed the MOB, do not scavenge other corpses. If you see a lone corpse and there is nobody around, it is fair game, but if there is a group nearby, it’s a safe bet that it’s their kill.

Assist others when you can. Let’s give the Steel Gladiators a good reputation on Luclin. If someone needs a buff, help finding a corpse, a teleport, etc…and it is within your power to help, do it. Of course, there’s noting wrong with putting a little coin in your pocket in the process, just make sure the price is fair.

Party often and represent us well. Partying outside the guild is a great way to meet other adventurers that could be considered for membership. It’s also a good way to make new friends. When partying, loot responsibly and pull your weight. We want to show others that the Steel Gladiators know their business!

If someone wrongs you, note that person’s name and class and take a screenshot if possible. We will keep track of all scoundrels on the web site to ensure that other Gladiators can steer clear of them.

Basic Guidelines

Travel often. Norrath is a big place! Get out and see it. Teleporting is fine, but you don’t get to see the true majesty of the plains of Karanas or the Ocean of tears that way. As you travel, take the time to grab screenshots that we can use on our website. Writing travel logs that our other members can read is also encouraged. It’s good to know what to expect when we venture into a new and potentially dangerous zone.

If possible, take the time to quest for your items rather than buy them. If you focus on leveling all day long, you’ll miss out on a lot of bonafide adventure. Gather items and get your new armor and weapons that way. It’s a lot more rewarding, honestly.

Have fun! Everquest is a game, not an obligation. If you feel like you are getting burned out, take a day or two off. When you return, you will be recharged and ready for action!
