Bastar's Tale

Bastar Odahola was born into the war-stirring streets of Freeport, first and only son to poor West Freeport merchants who already had two beggar daughters.    While thin and silent, Bastar was keenly intelligent, and often sparked heated debates with other merchants about the political climate of the city, prompting his father to grab him by the ear and scold him for his brazen and outrageous comments.     Because of his sullen nature, Bastar was ignored by his family for the most part, except for this mother, who treated him with kindness and gave him whatever she could scrape together.    Fellow sons of successful merchants taunted the small boy, striking him with rocks and flicking copper coins at him while laughing, Bastar's mother bowing her head and grabbing the coins.  And when his older sister Jeslana died under the shadows of a tent from starvation, Bastar narrowed his eyes and swore that he'd find something different, richer, better than this life.

The Game

As the Odahola family grew poorer. Bastar grew taller and ganglier, he was regularly sent out into the grassy knolls of the East Commons to search or beg for some semblance of food.   Bastar welcomed the solitude, as his mother had begun pressuring him to consider joining the Freeport monks and salvaging his life.   “Monkhood?” he thought to himself, “They are a class of poverty and charity that ignore the self.” He saw it as a ridiculous idea, and mouthed his disgust as he strolled through the trees.

A sudden burst of force exploded to his right, and Bastar flew into the grass face-first, shouting and jumping to his feet to see a mighty Wizard strike a treacherous griffin, the same ones that were warned about in childhood ghost stories, with a blast of wind that took the breath out of Bastar's lungs and slammed the griffin to the hard ground, dead.    Open-mouthed, Bastar approached the mighty wizard and stared at him until the magic wielder turned to him with a smug smile.   Not a drop of sweat was to be seen on his brow.  Bastar asked him questions, hundreds of questions, and the human wizard Kyne answered them all, telling him stories of adventures in places Bastar had never even heard about, and mighty foes that had fallen under his own powerful hand.

Upon his return, Bastar enlisted in the Academy of Arcane Sciences in Freeport, ignoring his family's protests and eventually cutting off all contact with them as his research nights grew longer and his hand became more and more skilled.   He traveled deep into Antonica and Fawdyer, exploring the regions and testing his newfound spells on anything that moved. On occasion, he would saunter back into Freeport and its familiarity, if only to scoff at it.

The seasons passed quickly, and Bastar surpassed his fellow Wizards, although never making friends with a single one of them simply because he couldn't be bothered.       During this time, the disagreement between the Freeport Paladins and Militia was growing into civil war, and regular watches and riots occurred.  Protests were heard consistently, and everyone was choosing sides.  Bastar, idly blasting bats and rats with his powerful spells and chuckling at their instant demise, grew more and more impatient with the Freeport population, until a day came when members of the Freeport Militia approached the now-powerful Bastar and demanded that he choose his side in the upcoming war, spouting their reasons for domination and rights.

Raising his chin, Bastar Odahola narrowed his eyes and told the soldiers coldly that no one told him what to do, Paladin or Militia, so they could take their swords and ideas and shove them into an unpleasant place.

The soldiers verbally threatened Bastar with his life;  Bastar sat down and casually began to memorize a spell from his brown leather-bound spell book, confusing the soldiers.    As he stood up, swords pointed to his throat, Bastar raised his hands and unleashed a spell that knocked the soldiers off their feet and slashed abrasions across their faces.  One of the Militia managed to roll away from the crippling red blast and pulled out his dagger, leaping at Bastar and catching him with the point of his blade in the eye before Bastar blasted him away with another spell.

Blood dripping from his now-defunct eye, Bastar backed away and glared at the Freeport Militia, who were rising to their feet again and beginning their approach for the kill.  Drawing up all the mana in his body, Bastar released a spell of shocking, brutal ice, instantly killing all three of the guards, freezing them in their grotesque and horrified body expressions.   Bastar was banished from Freeport at that moment, although he himself chuckled at the folly of the soldiers and walked away without hesitation.

His eye now covered by a simple eye patch, unable to be saved, Bastar roamed the continent, teleporting to various locations and observing all the different races and classes that he now saw in battle on a daily basis.    On occasion, he joined hunting parties, although his brutal and merciless honesty did not make him a popular man.     During one teleport, however, Bastar found himself landing on the head of a light-hearted bard covered with blue lambent armor in the West Commons, who at that moment was slyly cavorting with a young wood elven druidess named Celandra under a tree.

Raventod Ivikkewikkae was not amused for once, not with the interruption and the sight of the self-confident Bastar laughing at him as he stepped off his head, and his party members soon returned from their hunting to see Bastar and Raventod facing off at each other, Bastar continuing to make fun of the seasoned bard and Raventod growing more and more angry.  Darkreaper instantly liked the cocky wizard, and drew his attention away from the fuming bard, and Bastar and Darkreaper became fast friends, each recognizing the enjoyable evil in the other.

While Raventod and Galathor were suspicious of Bastar's intentions, they let him hunt along side with him and were reluctantly impressed with his offensive capabilities.   As such, both were persuaded by Darkreaper to allow Bastar into the small guild on a permanent basis, and while Bastar Odahola preferred himself as company over anything else, he recognized the advantages of having a group of hunting partners that who knew better than to tell him what to do.
