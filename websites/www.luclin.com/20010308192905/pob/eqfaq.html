The dictionary definition of "paradigm" is:
1. An example that serves as pattern or model. ``The paradigms and patterns of all things.'' --Cudworth
2. A list of all the inflectional forms of a word taken as an illustrative example of the conjugation or declension to which it belongs.
3. To show by the side of, to set up as an example
4. An example of a conjugation or declension, showing a word in all its different forms of inflection.
5. An illustration, as by a parable or fable.
