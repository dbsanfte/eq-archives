The coming of Galthor

One day, the paladin Galathor was making his way through Kunark, cutting down evil creatures in his path. During his trek through the dense forests of the Warsilks Woods, Galathor accidentally stumbled upon a hostile lizard group,. Upon seeing him, over 20 lizards warriors and spell-casters began to attack the Mighty Paladin. 

Galathor slayed as many lizards as he could, but over the hillside he could see a cloud of smoke and hear a loud roar. With that cloud came a band of lizard reinforcements; Galathor was trapped, but kept fighting. 

Suddenly he was hit by a blast of force that knocked him to the ground, powerless and bleeding. An Iksar necromancer stood over Galathor silently, as he lay weakened after battling the lizard camps. Galathor stumbled to his feet, trying to swing his axe, even with no energy left in his bones. The necromancer, gathering his mana in his webbed claws and beginning to cast a killing strike, looked at the fearless human paladin.

He saw honor and bravery in this paladin's eyes. The lizard admired these qualities, as he too believed he carried honor and courage within him. Knowing that he would willingly die for his race, Galathor stood brave, knowing this last spell would be the end. The necromancer threw his hands back and powered his shock of poison spell for striking. 

The spell fizzled away into fading clouds of red. The Iksar necromancer could not kill the paladin. Unbeknownst to Galathor, the necromancer was tired of the killings, and fought for peace, not for blood. He had seen many deaths of family and friends, and desired to see them no more. 

Iksar warriors stood by looking in anger and disbelief. Peace was an uncommon, shocking and disgusting characteristic for a necromancer, they all hissed. 

The Iksar necromancer was banned from his army, but even as he departed from the Iksar camp, the necromancer protected and remained companions with Galathor, even accompanying him on hunting travels throughout Norrath. 

As they hunted and camped, the necromancer told Galathor how he wanted to show the humans and the Iksars that peace could exist between the two races. Unfortunately, many lizards would not have anything to do with the necromancer, but secretly, some did believe that the war should end, he told Galathor, who was shocked to discover this. 

The paladin and the necromancer believed in peace, and both worked to show other classes and races that this, in fact, could be possible. As a token of his respect and acceptance, Galathor gave the Iksar necromancer the name Galthor, and they continue to this day to show Norrath that they share the name of peace.
