Abrak’s Story

Fire. If there's anything that has always been a constant in my life it is fire.

I was born in a very small village in the East Commonlands of Antonica.  Everyone in the village was a family member of mine since it had been started by two of our ancestors many years ago. We were independent of towns and their laws because we survived on our own skills and traded for things that were not immediately available. Every night we would gather around the campfire and eat supper together, singing and laughing the night away. Our goddess Erollisi Marr blessed us with a great love for each other. Those were good times then, just me and my family and the fire, keeping me safe
and together with my loved ones.

My parents were the blacksmiths of the village. I remember at a very young age my father began to apprentice me even though I was not yet strong enough to even lift a hammer. I loved to stare into the forge as the fire heated the metal, making it glow bright red and softening it for my father to
shape. The flames in the forge danced before my eyes, as if they were calling me to join them. My mother always said I looked hypnotized and was surprised that I didn't turn in to a piece of toast after standing in front of the hot forge for hours.

Then came a most tragic event in my life, and it was then that my destiny was revealed to me. One night the Freeport militia paid a visit to our village, demanding that we pay taxes for trading within Freeport city limits. The elders refused, we provided many things not available within Freeport and only traded them for common supplies such water and paper. This of course enraged the militia and they decided to wipe us out. They set upon us with cold steel, possibly some of the very same weapons my parents had
forged and traded to them. 

The men of the village stood to fight while the women and children scrambled for cover. My mother took into the forging hut and told me to hide behind the anvil in the corner while she went back to help my father. No sooner did she exit the doorway then I heard her scream. I was too frightened to get up, I crouched behind the anvil and covered my ears, hoping it would all go away.

Even with my ears covered I could hear the battle raging still. But beneath that, growing louder and louder, I heard voices. Something was calling me, it was coming from the forge. Enthralled by the voice I came out of my hiding place no longer thinking of the danger outside. I walked in front of the forge and peered in, it was cold and black because it hadn't been used since the day before. I put my head inside and listened, I could hear small whispers inside, talking, laughing, crying and screaming. Suddenly a force
pulled my whole body into the forge, and it lit up with a mystical fire.  There I was standing inside the forge surrounded by fire but still perfectly alright. It was then that she appeared to me, the goddess Erollisi.

She was too beautiful to describe. While I felt very comfortable within the magic fires, when I looked at her my eyes started watering up and began to burn. Then she spoke directly into my mind, she told me that I was to become the avenger for my family. To do this she would grant me a special power, the power to summon fire. She kissed me on the lips for a moment, when she released me I felt a searing pain in my head and passed out.

I woke up the next day in the forge, it was cold again and I wasn't sure if I had dreamed the whole thing. I crawled out and went outside to see what had become of my family. There was blood everywhere but no bodies, only a big flagpole with a black flag that said "PLAGUE" on it. Those bastards of the militia had put this up as an excuse to why they exterminated my family like a disease. They must have taken their corpses with them and burnt them in an incinerator like garbage. I felt a burning rage building up inside me as I looked at the flag. Then with thinking I shoot a burst of flame at the flag, setting it on fire.

I stood there in disbelief, so it wasn't a dream. Erollisi had really given me the power of fire and it was now my duty to become strong enough to get revenge on the Freeport militia so that my family’s souls can rest in peace. I sneaked through Freeport and stowed away on the next ship to Faydwer, where I convinced the High elves of Felwithe to train me in the ways of magic.  One day when I am strong enough, I shall burn each and every person responsible for my family's death to ashes. Then they will know the power of fire.

Abrak
