Gnome Invasion, 2/17/2002

On Sunday, February 17th, 2002, Emerald Phoenix recruited a group of Gnome Mercenaries to raid Crushbone at the request of the Feir`Dal.

However, It turns out the gnomes were more interested in booze, cookies, and taking apart my pet to see how it worked. Next time we'll have to try Dwarves, as even though they too have a taste for Ale, they at least would only be interested in taking apart the Orcs. None the less, in spite of being easily distracted and somewhat scattered, the Gnomes were modestly successful in their attack. Many Orcs died, and those that survived will require large doses of Prozac and Valium for some time to come. It is said that D'Vinn himself left Crushbone in a hurry muttering under his breath something to the effect of "gnomes...gnomes...they're everywhere...must...find....mother..."

 

Role Call for the Gnome Mercs. While present, most were out of sight trying to determine the location of the nearest Ale House. NEVER pay Gnome Mercenaries in Advance! 

 

 

With the Help of myself, Daliala, and Etheria, we got the Gnomes grouped up for their assault. I attempted to use my pet as a threat to maintain discipline, however this only resulted in a flurry of requests for Bear rides, and wonderment about how it worked. I sent the pet away when they began pulling out their tool kits.

 

 After making the short trip from Ak'Anon to Faydark, the gnomes began to get a bit more serious and make their battle plans...Until someone mentioned cookies....

 

 

After a brief discussion of Tactics, The Invasion Force immediately decided to hit another bar to "finalize"...
