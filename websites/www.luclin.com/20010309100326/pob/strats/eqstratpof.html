2. Entering Fear


When you enter fear for the first time, you will run north by northwest. You will run on the east side of the FF house (you'll see it, due NW of the portal). Basically when you zone in, start running and don't stop. You should cast invis before you come in, and if it breaks, don't stop, keep running. Run first, check where the hell you're going afterwards. As long as you go around the west side of the fire wall, you should be fine. If you die anywhere north of the ff house... it's all good. Just don't train anyone. It is 90% likely that when you enter pof, you will have to die to join us. It's how we enter pof. Fyre will drag your corpse and get you rezzed, and you'll be good to go.

Spiders, Scarlings, and FFs see invis. If you go past the FF house on it's east side, you should be able to run past the TTs, who do not see invis, and head to the corner to die before the NW corner roamers finish you off.

You die at the NW corner, near loc 1300, 1300. We camp at the North west corner of the firewall, on the north wall. Roughly loc 1300 700.

Whatever you do, DO NOT TRAIN THE GROUP when you enter. It is paramount, better to wait 6 hours for your corpse than get 20 people killed, and a few of them bind point killed...

For the first group of people who are the "rezzing team", basically rezes everyone that dies in the nw corner. The goal is to get a cleric to the safe point and bound... preferably camped out there so they don't die. The bait will go in first... all our druids, followed by enchanters to mez if they can. Druids will train shit around till they're told to die. Meanwhile, the cleric and monk will zone in, and the monk will lead the cleric to the safe point. The cleric casts bind, then camps... while everyone continues to kite everything around. Once the cleric is camped, everyone besides the cleric and monk goto the NW corner of the zone, not the safe point, and dies. No questions asked... you can camp if you think you can... but you probably can't make it past 20. Regardless, you shouldn't gate out... because you still have to be rezzed in.

4. Fighting Draco


There is no real guild strategy on fighting draco.
He is pretty easy, and just requires some disease resist, and a lot of beating on. He is by far the easiest dragon, and can be taken down without much effort the same way any dragon would (IE 2-3 tank groups, pet groups, and nukers.) He has semi low resists as well.

The main problem with Draco is that it agros Cazic... who starts Death Touching... and agros the whole zone. Everything should basically be cleared first, except cazic, and draco should be pulled to West camp.
He requires about 18 people + death touch bait.

6. Exiting Fear


There are 3 ways out:
Death - Die and come back later for your corpse or get a rez.
Gate out - Use a gate spell or potion, or have a druid or wizard TP you.
Portal - There is a portal in the amy castle (middle of the ring of fire). It's a pain in the ass to get to, and is very very rarely used.

Our exit plan is to have a druid bind at the safe spot, and TP everyone out. Gating back for more people. Usually a druid is always bound at the safe spot, so people who log out at the safe spot before the end of the raid can be picked up later in the week... or if people are bound there and die, sendin them back while we're not raiding Fear, the druid can gate up and TP them out. This is really our only exit plan, and is also used when individuals need to leave fear before the end of the raid.
