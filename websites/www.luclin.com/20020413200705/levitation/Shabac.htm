Shabac's Story

Hail, weary journeyman and good day! I see you have taken it upon yourself to find out more about us.  Aye, and what a story it is.  Our story begins many years ago on a cruel day.  Some wanderers quite like yourself, happened to be traveling down the same path when they ran into each other and began a pleasant conversation.  Seeing as how they were quite young and very uninhibited, they had wandered a little too far out of the safety that the gates of Freeport provided.  For you see, when one looked up, there was a horrible green beast running directly at his newly formed friend.  In pure desperation, the young lad hollered for his friend to step aside while at the same time wielding his dagger.  He thrust the dagger in the creature.  The creature struck back and the young boy was met with intense pain.  However, it was at this time that his friend came to the rescue by kicking the creature, and attacking it with the club he had recently found laying on the ground.  What the two soon realized was that by themselves, their destiny would have been very bleak.  However, between the two of them, this battle was not only easy, but also enjoyable!!  The two decided to travel a bit further together. Soon, they came across a pretty young damsel in distress who was being attacked by a pesky wolf that had appeared from nowhere.  Without a seconds wait, the two friends immediately assisted the young girl in need, and between the three of them, a wolf was slain on that very path.  A few moons later my friends, and you see how we have come into existence.   You see, we all start out as tender, young, inexperienced lads.  Together, where one's weakness is another's strength, ahh, fore there is the true combination of a mighty team.  Thus, our guild was founded through the likes of people such as yourself who not only enjoy, but rely on others to accomplish what may seem impossible at first sight.  So, do I have your attention yet? If so, then please, please, come in, sit down, relax, and get to know us.  Maybe, if you like what you see, there could be a spot for you amongst us.  If you choose otherwise, well, still come in and look around to see our guild.  Feel free to make any comments in our Message Board Section, or simply email me.  Until we meet again, good day!
