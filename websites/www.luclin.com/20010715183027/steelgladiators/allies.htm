Alliances

The Steel Gladiators have alliances with other guilds in Norrath. Here are links to our brothers and sisters in arms' websites!

Silent Sentinels - The Sentinels are a small but tight group of adventurers among whom we count our greatest friends.

Dragons of Lore - The Dragons are a larger guild with the power and experience that only comes with dedicated leaders and loyal members.

Discordia Eternum - Our newest allies, these guys can take the planes of Hate and Fear better than any I have ever seen. Visit their site you will see just how organized this guild is.

Heroes - One of the most powerful guilds on Luclin, the Heroes are always there to stand by our side when we want to take on Everquest's greatest challenges.

Alliance Message Board - A forum for all of us to chat, share stories, plan events, buy and sell our wares, and talk about the world of Everquest.

Alliance Events Calendar - A record of all upcoming interguild events and raids. Check it periodically for new events added daily!

WTF - A rather grand experiment in bringing friends together to explore the Western part of Norrath, in other words, Velious.
