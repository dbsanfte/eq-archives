Network Status

In order to join this guild on the Luclin Server:

  1. You must read the Rules of Conduct.

  2. You must be at least level 15.

  3. You must be sponsored by an existing member for consideration.

  4. All new members must be approved by the Membership committee of Galathor, Miyon, Dakhanavar, and Fractional.

  5. You must fulfill a task set forth to you by the membership committee.

For information and consideration, send a tell in game to Raventod or any officer on the Membership committee or post your application to our Message Board or 

Things to include in your post:

  1. Your Character's Name.

  2. Your Character's Class.

  3. Your Current Level.

  4. Your Alignment (good/evil/neutral).

  5. Your home city in the world of Norrath.

  6. How long you play a week.
