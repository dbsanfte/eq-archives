Lokriek’s Warjournal

The sun rose slowly over the mountains warding off the morning’s chill. The sunrise in the forests of Greater Faydark was breathtaking, but Lokriek didn’t notice. He stood holding his cloak tightly around him to protect against the cold bite of the morning breeze. He took no pleasure in the beauty around him, he was too focused on the task at hand, the assassination of Emperor Crush.

For all of Lokriek's life, Emperor Crush has lead his followers in many battles against the Elven race, Burning villages, killing women and children, and destroying everything in their Orcish grasp. Citizens of Kelethin are forced to live in the trees in fear of the Orcish Raiders. Now the Champions of Faydark have charged Lokriek with this task.

Lokriek stood next to his traveling companions, Cregan and Qalamari, and said, “are you ready to die for the Elven cause?” Qalamari, a High Elf Paladin, clutched her magical mace tightly, and answered, “always.” Then the Barbarian, Cregan, spoke up, “I do not intend to die today, but I will die for this noble cause.” With that Lokriek smiled, and thought about what the feeling he was going to have when he looked Emporer Crush in the eyes and watched him die. The three gathered their bags, and armor and set off for the home of the Crushbone Orcs.

The path they followed was well worn from constant travel of adventurers, and merchants. They followed the path for most of the morning, until they reached a large cave, with 2 orc hatchetmen guarding the entrance. Lokriek raised his hand in silence, then readied his longbow. Cregan snuck around to the left of one of the hatchetmen and waited for the sign. Lokriek took an arrow, notched it onto his bowstring, and took aim. The arrow flew silently through the air into the unsuspecting Orc’s throat instantly killing it. Cregan then appeared behind the other Orc and brought his two handed warhammer down on its head, busting it wide open. The group looted the dead Orcs’ possessions, then headed cautiously into the dark cave.

Once inside the cave, the group found the tunnel to Crushbone. They quietly made their way through to the other side, then jumped into the bushes. Lokriek led the group along the outskirts of the Orc camp, careful to stay unnoticed. They pushed ahead until they saw a rather large castle with a dirty moat around it. The castle was guarded by a single Orc Legonnaire armed with a large club. Qalamari told the group, “I’ll take this one,” and Lokriek Camouflaged her. Qalamari quietly snuck up to the large Orc, and unsheathed her dagger. Quickly she jumped on the Legonnaire’s back and slit its throat, letting the black blood drip silently to the ground.

Lokriek then muttered some Elven words and Camouflaged the rest of the group, and they slipped inside. Once inside, there were Orcs everywhere. The group started to panic, but Qalamari casted courage to keep them focused on their task. They made their way down the great halls to the throne room of Emperor Crush. They stepped through the large doors of the throne room, and began to survey the room. The room was rather large, with thick walls and thick doors. In the back of the large room, the group spotted Emporer Crush. Along side Emperor Crush, two large Emissaries were documenting his orders. “No one will hear the Orcs scream here. Let’s bar these doors,” whispered Cregan. The group closed the large doors quietly, and took position. The invisibility spell started to wear off so Lokriek gave the signal to attack. Cregan casted poison storm on the two emissaries, causing them to choke, and Qalamari jumped onto the Emperor. Cregan slammed his hammer down on one of the emissaries bludgeoning him to death. The other Emissary yelled some obsenities and tried to impale Cregan with its spear. Cregan jumped back narrowly escaping certain death. Lokriek threw his tomahawk splitting the emissaries skull, blood and brain spewing out. Qalamari held Crush for the group to see, then slipped her dagger between the large Orc’s ribcage piercing his lungs and heart. Crush’s eyes grew wide as he gasped his last breaths, black blood spilled slowly to the floor from the Emperor’s throne. The group took Crush’s tunic and all of the money, and Lokriek Camouflaged the group for a clean escape.

Once back in the Elven city of Felwithe, Lokriek gave Crush’s armor to Princess Lenya Thex as a show of courage. The kind Princess awarded the group with new armor and gold for bringing peace to Greater Faydark.
