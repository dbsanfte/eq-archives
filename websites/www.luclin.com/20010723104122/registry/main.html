June 23, 2000 9:05 AM PST

The site is starting to come up now, all features will be live by tomorrow at the latest. For this site to work as intended it will require cooperation by all the guilds involved. I have created this in good faith, if I see that it is not working out, I won't bother wasting my time and take it down and reserve myself to the mercy of the message board reservation system.

I will need to meet with the interested guild leaders soon, and propose either in in game meeting where we can group up and discuss how this site will be used, and the rules that will govern it. Only after all interested parties agree on how we can use this site will we begin to use it.

After the site is up and running, I will be looking for someone to help me co-administer the site to ensure that reservations are posted in a timely fasion as there may be occasion where I am afk for a day or so. Additionally I will be looking for someone to moderate the message boards to ensure that only productive conversations are taking place. Given the nature of many discussions I have seen on the luclin.com board, the moderator will have thier work cut out for them.

Rules for the message board will be simple. There will be no tollerance for flaming of any kind. If you can't make a productive contribution to the discussions at hand, don't bother posting. People who wish to make a post slandering a guild or individual for their thoughts will be banned. Each post you make logs in IP address that only I have access to, thus you can be banned by your IP address. I sincearly hope that I never have to do this but I will without hesitation to maintain a good atmosphere here.

This site is created in good faith, and I hope we can all use it that way.

Ty.
