Myth or legend?

One morning, a man, a woman, and their son were moving along a path in the Karanas. They were traveling to their new home of Qeynos. Although they were born in Freeport, it was time for them to go to a new city. With only a trusted stallion at their side for the young boy to ride on, they walked in the blazing sun.

The man said to his wife, "Aleena, I wish that one day our son will grow to be a mighty hero of the land. He will fight and destroy evil wherever he visits."

"Like the great Dekan?" asked his wife.

"Maybe even greater than Dekan, but it is only a foolish wish of mine. And besides, no one is more worthy of wielding the Soulfire than Dekan."

For hours, the family traveled further west. The golden sun gave them no mercy. They were getting tired, so they decided to take a rest at an abandoned camp. As the many graceful birds flew by, they drank plenty to quench their thirst. They never felt they could be so thirsty.

The woman began to prepare a quick meal for her husband and son. As her husband started a fire, the sky became full of dense clouds and it began to rain.

"Papa! I'll help build the tent!" shouted the young boy.

BOOM.

"What was that?" said the woman, "An earthquake?" The ground beneath trembled again.

BOOM.

"OH MARR! A HILL GIANT!" shouted the man. The man grabbed his son and placed him on the horse. He then took his wife's hand and they all ran in a desperate panic.

BOOM...BOOM...BOOM. The tall creature had the family locked in his sight. Since they were in open wilderness, there was nowhere the family could run to hide. The pouring rain was of no assistance to them either.

Suddenly, the stallion was knocked over by the giant's enormous hand and the young boy was thrown in the air quite a distance. When the boy landed on the ground, his body remained motionless.

"AAAAAAHH!!" screamed the boy's mother. She began to rush toward him but the huge giant immediately blocked her way. The man stabbed his knife into the towering monster's leg, but it only made the giant more angry. The giant began to reach for the woman.

WHOOOOOOSH!

A blade of fire mysteriously appears and slices off some of the giant's fingers. Before the giant had any chance of screaming in pain, someone wearing a full suit of Valorium armor slashes away at the doomed foe. The hill giant drops to its knees and stares at the paladin.

The paladin raises his blade of fire one more time, and beheads the giant. The traveling couple cannot bear to look at its death. The individual in shining armor turns and peers at the couple. Holding his fiery weapon, the rain from above appears to never actually touch the shining individual.

The man and the woman run toward their son who is miraculously still alive but very near death.

"Papa..."

"NOOOO!" screamed the boy's father. "This is my only son, and now I am about to lose him!" Many tears run down their faces as the couple both hold their dying son. A loud crack of thunder dominates the sky.

A gentle hand touches the woman's shoulder and she looks up to see the paladin who is no longer wearing his helm to reveal his face. The man holding the flaming sword begins to speak to the couple.

"You will not lose your son, for I will not allow it."

The paladin takes his weapon and places it inside its sheath. The knight stands over the boy, who looks as though he has finally expired. The knight takes off his Valorium gloves and places them next to the child. He then looks up at the heavens and raises his hands high above his head. He does this with such grace.

The man in armor closes his eyes and kneels down before the boy. He then lays both of his hands on the child, one hand on the boy's head, and the other hand on the boy's heart. The rain immediately stops, the sky clears, the sun shines again, and the child's eyes slowly begin to open. A spectacular aura surrounds the boy.

The child stands up on his feet and looks over at his parents. The child gives his father and mother a glowing smile.

"Papa, what happened?"

The couple take their child and hold him like they have never held him before. They are so very happy. The woman kisses her son on his cheek. The father says, "My son, you are alive and well! By The Marr Twins, I cannot believe it!"

"Yes, he is," said the paladin, "and you must believe."

The father says, "You saved my son, you saved us all. How could we ever repay you for your most honorable deed?"

The paladin replies, "It was nothing. It was an honor to save your lives. I wish nothing in return. Now, I must go."

"Wait," says the father, "are you the mighty one who wears the great armor from the Planes, and is that the legendary Soulfire you wield?"

"Yes, I am, and this is my sword. I am Seibwen Dekan, Holy Knight of Mithaniel Marr. I have sworn to bring endless light upon the world of Norrath, and to protect our world from all evil. And one day, your son will take my place. This, I promise you."

The father says, "But how? Why my son?"

"Those answers will come to you one day. Time is one of your answers. When I Laid Hands upon your son to save his life, that was when I knew he was chosen."

The wind began to blow, and the family blinked their eyes. And in a blink of an eye, Seibwen Dekan disappeared.
