Divine Levitation Charter

       We, the members of Divine Levitation have one ultimate goal - to destroy the evil beasts that inhabit the lands of Norrath.  However, our approach is what separates us from other guilds.  You see, we realize that our guild alone could not possibly destroy the entire region by ourselves.  Thus, to accomplish such a task, everyone together must have the same goal in mind.  With that we realize, today's "newbies" are tomorrow's heroes!   Therefore, we will assist any new players anyway possible when it permits.  It does not stop there: we are a friendly guild, and will help any player in need to the best of our ability, regardless of your level!  Good luck to you, and we look forward to assisting you on the battlefield!
