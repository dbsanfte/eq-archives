Wanderlust. The name says it all. Simply put we have a lust to wander. Not content to stay in one place we plan on everyone seeing our members all over Norrath. We, of the Wanderlust Guild, feel we have a responsibility to the EverQuest community. We believe in honor, valor, and respecting the rights of others (even if they are wrong). As part of our pledge to these initiatives, we have agreed to abide by the following guidelines:

  • We are an honorable guild, therefor, KSing (Kill Stealing) is NOT allowed!

  • We offer our trade skills to other guild members at cost of components. Best way to do this is to get the components and give them to the trade master. Please bear in mind the time it takes to make things, so if you get someone to make you something that takes forever, i.e. a full suit of armor, please be kind and donate for the time.

  • We are a multi-racial guild and we are family, therefor, we will share our language skills if we have the time to teach them. (Please dont go running in to Karnors and say, "Hey, teach me Lizardman." Save it for boat trips or language fairs, or other such things)

  • Yes, we allow Power-leveling and Twinking.
