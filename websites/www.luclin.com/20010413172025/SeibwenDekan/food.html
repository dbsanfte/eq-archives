Over at the Newbie Zone, one of the most common questions is "I'm being told I'm hungry and thirsty, where do I find food?". The usual answer is "Get muffins and milk, they're cheaper and lighter". This is often followed with "over by...", "I think near...", "somewhere around...". Heh, I guess we forget where the cheep stuff is as we get older (and richer). This list will hopefully answer the question.

    First, some facts about food:
  1. All food and drink is consumed at the same rate, but the rate may change from zone to zone..
  2. Items that stack weigh the same no matter how many are in the stack. (a stack of 5 weighs the same as a stack of 20)
  3. In desert zones, water is consumed twice as fast.
  4. Alcohol will not help your thirst. You need water or milk.
  5. Being without food and drink will not kill you directly. However, you will lose your ability to regenerate HP and Stamina.
