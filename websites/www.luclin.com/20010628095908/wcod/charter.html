History.........

The War Council of Dune was founded over seven years ago on the online game The Kingdom of Drakkar. Over the years, the core members of the War Council stayed together through turmoil and strife and game changes too, only to find our way to Everquest. The War Council is truly a unique guild in the world of Everquest, we are not brought together strictly for the love of money or uber gear. We are together because we enjoy the camaraderie and friendship we have built over the years. We even go so far as to have an annual gathering in Atlantic City each spring to gamble, drink and of course play EQ. Say what you will about the value of uber guilds and phat lewt, we are here to have fun and enjoy time spent with friends.

Requirements.........

The War Council has little in the way of formal requirements. There are no level minimums, no class or race requirements or anything of that sort. The main requirement is simply this: You must know, or at least have met, a current member of the guild in person. Once that requirement is met, and the active member puts the person's name up for the guild, the nominee will be voted on and if accepted, placed on a lengthy probation (usually a few months). The only restriction during this time is that the nominee cannot bring anyone else up for membership during that time. While this may seem a little excessive for an online game, infighting, politics and all the other BS is virtually non-existent in the War Council.
