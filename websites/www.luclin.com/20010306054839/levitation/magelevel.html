Magician Powerleveling

 

Stay in your newbie area so you can get Meditate and your new pets to try out. Some newbie areas are better than others and can take you higher.

Levels 1-10

This is a tough call because you're almost stuck with your own newbie zone for the first few levels no matter what, unless you know Norrath well or have friends to port you around.

On Faydwer, Greater Faydark is actually a good newbie zone except for all the people who regularly flood it... if you're on a low-population server (such as the PVP/racewar servers) then it can be a good place to level. Butcherblock is also very nice, but I always got major lag there, don't know why, it was much worse with 30 people there than Greater Faydark with 100 people in it. Clearly, though, a gnome mage should leave Steamfont as soon as possible... it's not a great zone to hunt in.

Around Freeport, Eastern Commons is nearly as bad as Greater Faydark... nice zone to hunt in, but usually filled with idiots. North Ro is a good alternative. I like the areas around Freeport, good places to hunt for well past 10th level.

Misty Thicket deserves it's reputation as one of the best places in the game to level, and it's well worth the trip to Rivervale when you can make it. You can hunt to 11th level or higher there, all the way to 14th if you really want to, although gaining exp will become progressively more difficult after 11th. But some people like the comfort of a zone with practically no insta-death mobs.

On the far side of the world, Qeynos/Qeynos Hills are okay hunting grounds but the hilly part of Qeynos is buggy, mobs teleport constantly. Tox forest is without a doubt the worst newbie zone in the game, dark and empty and extremely buggy as well. Everfrost, on the other hand, is IMO a better hunting area from 1-6 than Misty Thicket even; I've taken my Erudite and Human druid characters there to level (assuming you can make it through Blackburrow and avoid the vengeful skellies in Everfrost). I really like Everfrost from 1 to 6. After that it's very difficult to find mobs that you can gain exp from and still solo... Qeynos Hills is better to solo, but if you group up you can hunt the Everfrost plains or go to Blackburrow or Western Karana. Usually, though, I find a way to get to Misty Thicket or Freeport, where the hunting from 6th to 10th level is much better, solo or grouped.


Level 8-11

Willowisps offer great exp and loot for about your level. They drop 3 different items. A BOLS (Burned out lightstone) , A LS (Lightstone) , and a GLS (Greater Lightstone). The BOLS are basically worthless. The other two are great for doing a quest for decent/great money and great exp at your level.

Level 12-16

In West Karana at the village nearest the Qeynos Hills zone, there is a Bard, Misty Storyswapper. Misty ranges from lvl 13-17, always drops a mandolin (1pp+), the occasional word, rune, page and the occasional Raw-hide armor. She is great exp.

When you first get there con her. If your faction is indiffernet, apprehansive or dubious, I would suggest going and doing the bard report quest in qeynos first. Get you faction to warmly, abouit a dozen or so bard report runs. Then come back here and kill Misty. The bard reporst quest offsets the faction hits taken for killing her.

I did level 15 about 4 hours and made over 50pp.

Level 16-20

Starting at lvl 17, I went to South Karana to check things out. What I found was a gold mine of experience!

From lvl 16-20, you can base yourself at the bridge and solo elephant calfs, then move up to elephants, then some of the lower centaurs. This works best with an earth pet, but will also work fine with a water one.

There has always been plenty of people that hang at the bridge that would be willing to sell you a SoW. Be prepared to pay them...it is well worth it.

If you get into trouble, you can zone at the bridge. You can also sell at the merchat at the second floor of the bridge.

I solo'd from this bridge from lvl 16-24. I mixed a little of the aviaks in for some fun, but soloing is where the exp is at.

I killed Crag Spiders from lvl 21 to 24. They are a tough battle but smart magicians can solo them without the loss of a single hitpoint. Just make sure you have lots of malachite because your pet will get poisoned and may die. They also give up words, runes, pages, venom sacks, and most importantly....a grip of spider silk. Up to 6 at a time. Tailors pay well for this and I made 2 full suits of raw silk armour with all the silk I got by killing Crags. Then just move up to Broon. Becareful my fellow magicians...and Good Journey.

Level 21-25

East karana has lots of stuff to kill in this level range. Carrion spiders are plentiful, you can fight them in the gorge area without interference, a zone is close by for emergencies, and they drop silk that can fetch you some nice cash. There are also snakes, lion mat/patriarchs, and wolves to fight. and no faction issues. the zone has merchants for buying food/arrows/bandages too.

Level 26-30

Think Kedge is for 40+? Nope. At lvl 29 you can solo the yellow and light red lancer's that are near the zone. Use a fire pet and a lower lvl nuke to keep the fishie off of you and alternate with a heal on yur pet. I personally used shock of flame (96dmg) as fire-based spells seemed to get through much more then shock of swords.

After the battle I would still have about a bubble of mana left - unless it was a light red.

If you pull an imapler or piercer by accident or more then one lancer - zone IMMEDIATELY. The loot stinks but the exp is awesome.
