Download and install PSP 7.0 from Jasc

Download the psp image to practice on here

Toggle the tool options window so it is visable

Zoom in several times with you left mouse button. (7 is good)

Tool Options Settings: Rectangle, Feather=0, Antialias=unchecked

Magic Wand Settings: Match Mode=RGB Value, Tolerance=100, Feather=0, Sample Merged=unchecked

I suppose the good thing about Everquest's graphics are that they are not antialiased. You will see what I mean after you have zoomed in on the image. There is a definite line to follow, which makes the selections easier to create.

Let's Begin
Select the rectangular tool option with the settings stated above. Make a small selection to start with by dragging your mouse under the grey part of the image, or anywhere you want. I was working on the Wurmslayer at the time so I am using this as an example. Hold down the shift key [this adds to your selection and the ctrl key will take away from it] on your keyboard and make another small selection following the outline of the Wurmslayer. Do small sections at a time. Once you have a few done, hit the delete key on your keyboard. It will erase the selection you just made and create the start of your outline. Don't worry if you have made a mistake, there is always the undo button.

Once you have made your outline around the entire area you wish to cut out, select the magic wand tool, with the settings stated above. Left click on the outside of your outline in the dark area [don't hold down the shift key when selecting this], you should now see the area around the outer edge of your outline selected. Hit delete.

Continue to do this around the entire image until you have the desired effect. Don't foget about the smaller areas, like the inside of the Wurmslayer. Save the final result as a psp file. This saves the transparent background information, making it easy to place on a new one. Just copy and paste it in what ever you like. Saving it as any other file type will put it on a default background color [white].

This is one way to cut out your character from a background, another alternative, [sometimes easier] is doing this by vector selections.
