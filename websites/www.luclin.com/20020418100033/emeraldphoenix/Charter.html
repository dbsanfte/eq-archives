HONOR ABOVE ALL 


The Order of the Emerald Phoenix has come into existence to defend Norrath against that which would imperil the free peoples that populate the world. Those evils that would enslave the people who have known freedom must be defended against, to that end we willingly pledge ourselves to go forth into the world, to seek out such evils and destroy them. As we travel about the land, we will aid, such as we can, those who are less fortunate or in dire need and assist as we may, whenever we can.


RACES


As Emerald Phoenix is a guild that was created to remove the evils from the face of Norrath, no Pure Evil Races*(See Below)* will be allowed to join up in our fight against evil. However, if a character of Evil Race can do the following they will be allowed to join the ranks of Emerald Phoenix.

1.) They must cease and desist the killing of any Good race NPC’s i.e. Druids, Guards in good cities, etc. 
2.) They must prove that they are not KoS in at least two good cities. i.e. Freeport, Qeynos, Kelethin, etc. The members of Emerald Phoenix feel that there may be a few Evil Born races that may feel compassion towards the good side and be swayed to turn against there evil counterparts.

If after these conditions are met, if at anytime the member kills any Good NPC they will be placed before the HC where a trial will be held to determine their fate. All Races of Good are welcome. Each new member will be asked to perform an entry quest that must be completed within a two week time period. During this time period the prospect will be asked to remain in anonymous mode.

 *Pure Evil is defined as a Player Character that kills good NPC’s


TRIALS OF JUSTICE


We do not Loot Steal (LSing) If you are caught stealing from members of Your guild you will be summarily dismissed. If there is a battle going on and a corpse is nearby it probably belongs to the people in that battle, leave it for them. Exception: Leaving corpses around for newbies to loot creates lag or people might be sitting around after a battle, ask first, then loot, help keep our world clean. Kill Stealing (KSing) You get one warning and then goodbye. Exception: in FFA areas go for it. Be advised this can come back and bite you in the you-know. 

Guild Chat is designed so that we can all keep in touch with each other easily while online.  The use of Guild Chat is highly encouraged to share adventures, experiences, help other members in the guild, look for a guild group, etc. Complaints and whining however will not be tolerated in Guild Chat. We all come to Norrath to escape the rigors of everyday RL. Therefore please spare your guild mates from having to hear someone complain or whine. If you do have a complaint please inquire if there is an Officer online in GC and then send them a /tell. Whining that there is no one to group with is not acceptable in Guild Chat. EP has a diverse range of players in different time zones. This may make it difficult at times to find a group to be with. Use of the Web page Message Boards is highly encouraged to arrange for times to group with other guild mates.

We are a close-knit group of people who are friends in RL or have met online and become good comrades. However we do know at times that players can outgrow the guild for whatever reason. If this is the case you may feel free to leave at anytime. If you threaten to leave the guild, consider yourself removed. Threatening just creates animosity amongst the members. If you leave you will be given one chance to return. We will consider this a cooling down period. You must petition the High Council for reinstatement to Emerald Phoenix. They will have the final vote as to whether you may return or not.

Anonymous mode is discouraged. (Unless you are in your probation period) It is acceptable to be in normal mode or Role-play mode. Violations will be handled on an individual basis. 


SUB-GROUPS

Throughout history there have been organizations that have many, many sub-Organizations to accomplish specific goals. The best example, is the Catholic church (i.e. The Knights Templar, the Jesuits, the various orders of monks, priests and nuns). All of their sub-groups are named Knights of This, Order of That. To that end, as we expand, Members, with the approval of the High Council may form a sub-group. An emerald is normally cut into rectangle so there will only be four sub-groups (one for each corner of the rectangle) of the Order of the Emerald Phoenix. Members do not have to join a sub-group; it is their choice to do so. Being a member of a sub-group is not a requirement for membership in Emerald Phoenix. These four will be formed on a first-come, first-serve basis. The Knights that are forming will name them. Two Knights are required to form each group. Each group must have a purpose that is approved by the High Council. Each sub-group will have a seat on the High Council when the leader has reached the appropriate level.
