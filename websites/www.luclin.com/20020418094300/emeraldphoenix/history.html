Up from the Ashes

 

Squire Agnino Wergeland pounded on the room's door again. The snoring coming from inside the room sputtered out briefly then started up again louder than before. "Sounds like the mating call of a couple of Emerald Drakes" he thought to himself Sighing softly he opened the door and entered the room. Inside he found a Dwarf sound asleep on the bed half dressed in his armor still but fully comatose. The room was a disaster, with bits of metal, armor and weapons, clothes, and several empty jack of ale scattered about. The Dwarf once again let out a huge snore and Agnino stopped himself from automatically covering his ears, between his knights snoring and banging his head on the low door posts of Kaladim he had a splitting headache.

"Sir Grezz" he said softly.

Suddenly the Dwarf sprang from the bed and seemingly out of thin air a sword appeared in his hand. The sword whistled through the air towards Agnino's neck, stopping mere inches from connecting. The High Elf didn't move he had been through this all before.  " By Brell!  Squire I have told you before not to try and sneak up on me like that" Said Grezz crossly.

 " Yes Sir Grezz, I know, I knocked rather loudly several times but..."

 " Nonsense" interrupted Grezz " A finely trained knight as myself would never sleep through someone knocking on me bedroom door."

 " As you say Sir Grezz"  The Dwarf looked around the room and then lay back on the bed with a sigh. "What is so important you felt the need to wake me? It's not even the crack of noon yet!"

Agnino again wondered at the Dwarves ability to tell the time in the perpetual darkness of the Dwarven City of Kaladim. Personally he never could tell if it was day or night except by the changing of the work schedules of the Dwarves themselves.  "Sir Grezz, a message just arrived from the Lady Pistol"

 "A message?  Wonder what this bodes?" Grezz open the small parchment and squinted at the tiny script. " Hmm it seems we will have to postpone our trip to those new lands good Squire, the Lady Pistol has summoned me to a meeting."

 "Very well Sir Grezz, where is this meeting?"
"'Tis in the common lands by Freeport and I have very little time, could you please prepare me some traveling rations from the Kitchen?"

"Certainly Sir Grezz."

As Agnino left the room Grezz sprang into action.  He very quickly had all his armor on, and weapons ready and set. He left the room, taking a quick draw from a half empty ale jar by the door to help wake him up. He moved down the stairs to the kitchen where Agnino had already assembled a pack for the journey.

As Grezz entered the kitchen Agnino let out a huge sneeze and began the sniff.

"Allergies still, Squire?"

"Aye Sir Grezz, 'tis the mold and damp of these caves."

"Caves! This is the mighty city of Kaladim, home and heart of the Dwarves, not a bunch of damp and moldy caves!"

"Aye Sir Grezz...your pack is ready"

"Thank you Squire.  I am not sure how long I will be gone.  So keep up the practice with your Mage spells while I'm gone.  Please, this time try not to break all the furniture in the living room" he said with a smile.

" Of course Sir Grezz, if you will remember, I haven't broken so much as a dish in days you know." " Aye I know, I shall return as soon as I can." As Grezz left the small house and headed for the city entrance he thought to himself that it was high time for Agnino to earn his spurs and become a full knight, he was really more than ready.  Grezz quickly passed the guards at the city entrance and headed out into the Butcher Block Mountains. He came to the path, one way leading to the port and the other leading deeper into the mountains. With a shudder to himself, he chose to go deeper into the mountains away from the port. Sir Grezz, the mighty Paladin, and bane of the undead became deathly sea sick on ocean voyages. Grezz moved quickly towards the circle of standing stones used as a reference point for teleporting druids, and within a few hours found himself deep in the mountains at the secluded spot. Grezz found a spot and made himself comfortable while he waited for the arrival of a passing Druid to see if he could beg a ride. As much as he hated to beg he hated being seasick even more.  As he pulled some rations from his pack he began to ponder all the mighty changes the order of the Emerald Phoenix had undergone in the last few weeks. It was only a few weeks ago that Sir Tyre the mighty wizard and one of the leaders within the Order had fallen and turned to evil. His fall had split the Order, Cadre men and Runners, Squires and Knights, had disappeared or fallen to bickering among themselves. Grezz had even lost heart eventually, as the once proud Order of the Emerald Phoenix seemed to be upon the brink of ruin. He considered retiring from the life of an adventurer and setting up his own forge in Kaladim. As he had walked the now silent halls of the Citadel that the Order had called home out of the shadows had stepped the Lady Pistol, the Rogue, with her cat Bullets cradled in her arms. Bullets sprang from Pistol to be caught by the startled Grezz who found himself suddenly holding a purring cat that tried to bury its head in his beard.  Grezz had smiled briefly and then the feeling of doom had returned. "Why so glum cute dwarf, frowns don't become you?" inquired the young gnome.  Pistol's lack of respect for authority again brought a brief smile to Grezz's lips. " I fear the end of our Order has come and evil has triumphed, we have failed in our mission to protect Norrath," he said sadly. " Nonsense" countered Pistol "who dares to say these...um...untruths?" " You can lead us to greatness again like our namesake the Phoenix!"   " Not I, I no longer have the heart for it, too many friends have disappeared and evil's power seems to grow all the time. I can no longer lead those of us that remain. I have asked Sir Sistik to temporally take over the leadership of our diminished ranks till a meeting of those remaining can be called." " I will be at this meeting and will spread the word" said Pistol happily "you will see, this is just a new beginning for our Order and all is not lost".

As Pistol and Bullets faded once again into the shadows Grezz had been struck with the thought that she would be the perfect leader for the Order. He felt hope again. A few days latter the remaining officers had all endorsed this idea and Lady Pistol the rogue found herself the new Leader of the Emerald Phoenix. The meeting had also restored hope to the order. In the beginning the gapping holes in the ranks of Cadre men, Runners, Squires and Knights had seemed like great chasms, empty where once strong arms had stood. The meetings end had filled these holes as the circle of the order had moved once again to join together in their crusade against evil. Many had even reported they had heard a whispered voice say, " The Emerald Phoenix will rise again!".
The arguing of two approaching 'tall folk' interrupted Grezz's reverie. Grezz smiled to see Sir Dracoil the Warrior and Sir Piemar the Bard approaching. "Well met!" Grezz called out. (OOC I know Drac was having nautical problems at the time but he was SUPPOSED to be there hehe)
 Startled both knights broke off their argument about the best way to split an orc, vertically or horizontally, to clasp hands with the dwarf. Grezz had to stretch up and Dracoil had to bend down in order to accomplish this task. "Sir Grezz, what brings you here?" Asked Piemar

"Lady Pistol has summoned me to the common lands" replied Grezz, thinking even when speaking that the bard sounded like he was about to burst into song. " I was hoping to snag a ride from a passing druid."

The other two knights exchanged a smile, knowing of Grezz's feelings about ocean voyages. Dracoil said "aye, she has summoned us as well, we are to meet my wife Lady Loletta here".

With the shimmering of lights a portal from elsewhere opened and out stepped the Lady in question. "Did I hear my name?", Loletta asked with a smile.

Hugs where exchanged all around, bringing a blush to Grezz's bearded face since Loletta constantly forgot that he was much shorter than her, and when hugging him tight she often made him forget that he preferred bearded women. Loletta was a very pretty Druid, if a little tall, the perfect match for the towering Sir Dracoil.

"Are we ready to go?  Lady Pistol will grow impatient." Loletta asked.

All three agreed they were and with several mystic passes she had again opened a portal to elsewhere. All stepped through and found themselves in the far off Common lands. "We must hurry or we will be late," said the Lady. The four friends quickly hurried to the appointed place where they found Sir Huntir, Sir Tullaan, Lady Larisian and Lady Tarnia also waiting. The eight knights and ladies where not the most powerful within the Order, but were all veterans of many encounters with the evil minions that roamed upon the face of Norrath. As greetings were exchanged and
assorted alcoholic beverages passed out by Lady Tarnia, Lady Pistol arrived upon silent feet.

"Greetings all, well met!" Pistol laughed.

All the knights and ladies bowed to the new leader of the Order of the Emerald Phoenix.

"We have assembled as you have requested Lady Pistol," said Lady Larisian.

"Here have a drink," cried Lady Tarnia has she passed Pistol a flagon of brandy.

Bullets ran out of the darkness to rub up against Sir Tullaan and purr. Sir Dracoil had a momentary flash of hunger pain, which he ignored; it would be a terrible thing to eat the Leader of the Order's cat.

"As much as I would like to partake of drinks with you all I have summoned you for serious business.  Please, all follow me," said Pistol.

The knights and ladies followed Pistol to a secret place high in the mountains, secluded and windswept.

"I would ask you all to put away all your weapons and come to this meeting with open arms in a spirit of friendship" said Lady Pistol.

After all had put up their implements of war Pistol began to speak. "The time has come for the order of the Emerald Phoenix to rise again and to this end I have formed a new High Council. After consulting with many of the members of the order you eight have been chosen for this task. Each will be given an office and duties to perform. These are grave
responsibilities and the future of our Order will rest in our hands, will you all accept this burden?"

All humbly agreed and Lady Pistol called each by name and appointed them Masters with certain responsibilities within the guild. (OOC Please see the web page for a list of masters and these responsibilities.) Long into the night plans where discussed and strategies made. Lady Larisian and Sir Tullaan both mentioned that much of the evil that plagued the
land seemed to be coming from the vile home of the vampire Mayhong Mistmoore. Plans were laid to raid this nest of evil in the near future. It would be more of a scouting raid than an attempt to destroy Mayhong himself since his magical wards are to strong.

As the meeting ended, new hope burned in each breast for the future. The adventurers took their leave from each other. With much clasping of hands and hugging they said their good byes as each went to prepare for their new duties. Grezz remained upon the plateau to camp for the evening before heading back to Kaladim. As he lay down to sleep he thought he spied a green star rising, one he had never seen before. He thought to himself, that bodes well for the future of the Order of the Emerald Phoenix.  Mayhap it is a sign from Brell himself to show his favor. Just as Grezz was about to sleep, just on the edge of his mind, he thought he heard a sibilant hiss and an evil voice whisper "I await your coming members of the Emerald Phoenix". The voice cut off with a dark chuckle. Grezz shrugged and thought perhaps some of Lady Tarnia's alcoholic refreshments might not have agreed with him. As the shadows grew he rolled over and softly began to snore.

 

 

 

 

 

 

A Change of the Watch

 

The day dawned as it had before in Norrath with the bright sun rising up over the Qeynos Hills. Only this day was a little bit more special than previous days. A raid was scheduled to take place on the Goblins of Permafrost by the Order of Emerald Phoenix with assistance from the Warriors of Faith.

Lady Larisian sat up from her bedroll, stretching and rubbing the sleep from her eyes, that she had laid out the night before outside the entrance to Black Burrow. She rose from her bedroll and wandered over to the fire poking at it with a stick to make sure the embers were still hot enough for a morning cup of tea. As she sat on her bedroll contemplating the day's events ahead she heard a rustle in the grass coming up over the hill. She reached for her sword that was tucked under her pack gripping it tightly unsure as to what or who was coming over the rise. As she waited attentively, peering over her cup of tea in the direction the sound was coming from, the sun began to rise up over the hill. As the sun raised a tall robed figure seemed to come over the rise at the same time making it very difficult to identify who or what the figure was. As the figure came closer she relaxed a bit realizing who the person was.

"Hail Lady Larisian" said the figure as it stepped out of the rising sunlight to reveal the face.

Larisian loosened her grip on her sword and stood to receive the figure in a quick embrace of friendship.

"Hail Squire Agnino, merry met."

"Indeed it is a pleasure to see you Lady", expressed Agnino. "Are you ready for our venture to Permafrost?"

"Aye Agnino, there will be much blood spilled today." She quickly thought to herself that she hoped it would not be their blood but that of the vile goblins of Permafrost. Not wanting to cause any bad karma she quickly dismissed the idea from her mind.

"Would you care to share a cup of tea while I pack my belongings and get ready for the run?" asked Larisian.

"That would be very pleasurable M'lady,"

As Agnino sipped his tea, Larisian hurried about packing her belongings into her pack making sure to keep her Saber-toothed Tiger Coat out. It would be cold on the road to Permafrost.

The two companions entered the burrows of Black Burrow and hastily made their way through the tunnel that led to the land known as Everfrost. As they approached this frozen northern land a chill went through their spines. Not because of the cold but because of the thoughts of the oncoming battle they were about to partake in.

Larisian led the way with Agnino in tow through the snowy passes of the Everfrost peaks. The countless passes were confusing and the party only got lost once, having to backtrack only once. Larisian thought she heard Agnino mumble something about a "Ranger getting lost" but she quickly dismissed it. Rounding a turn in the pass they came across a few Barbarian guards and realized that they had reached the meeting point of Halas where Lady Tarnia surly would be preparing the troops for battle and handing out some of her newest brew.

"Hail All Mighty Emeralds," exclaimed Larisian. From behind her Agnino let out his boisterous cheer that is his patent mark.

"Hail Lady Larisian," the throng of people echoed back.

Pleasantries were exchanged amongst all as newcomers from Emerald Phoenix and members of the Warriors of Faith arrived at the gathering point. Lady Tarnia quickly, efficiently and methodically grouped the people together into hunting parties. Sir Piemar excused himself and ran off to Black Burrow to pick up some of the members of Emerald Phoenix who had not traveled in these parts of Norrath before to make sure they arrived at the raid in one piece.

Larisian leaned back against the wall of the tunnel to Halas a little tired from her run to Permafrost. She slumped down into a sitting position, opened a bottle of Lady Tarnia's Honey Mead, and was just closing her eyes when out of the shadows stepped a little gnomish figure. Larisian quickly got to her feet.

"Well met Lady Pistol," she said as she knelt before the leader of Emerald Phoenix.

"Well met Lady Larisian," as Pistol stepped forward to embrace her.

Larisian noticed that Pistol had quite a concerned look upon her face. She thought about asking if anything was wrong, but figured that Lady Pistol would inform her if there was.

Pistol shivered. "Brrrr! It is cold in these parts."

"Aye that it is M'lady, however the goblin blood that we spill in the chambers of Permafrost will keep the chambers warm enough to melt the frozen walls."

"Indeed it will Lady Larisian, indeed it will," Pistol chuckled.

Pistol motioned for Larisian to sit next to her on the ground. Larisian offered Lady Pistol of bottle of Honey Mead, which she took generously. The two sat there for sometime admiring the gathering of Emerald Phoenix and Warriors of Faith. They conversed on how the guild had once again started to become strong and of future plans. It was at this time that Lady Pistol grew very silent. An expression crossed her face that Larisian thought was one of sadness, excitement, and fear all wrapped into one.

"Larisian," Pistol stated, "we must have a private conversation"

"M'Lady what is your concerns? Please, share them with me."

"I have been given an assignment by the King of Ak'Anon. It will be a very dangerous assignment. One that I do not know if I shall return from."

"Nonsense!" exclaimed Larisian. "We will put together the finest of Emerald Phoenix's members and accompany you on this journey. You will return safe M'lady."

"I am afraid Larisian that that is not possible. Fore I have been told to go alone. But alas M'lady, my leaving would leave Emerald Phoenix without a leader."

"Lady Pistol," Larisian says chuckling nervously, "You have faced many dangers in your time. Surely you will return from this adventure as well. There is no need to talk about Emerald Phoenix not having a leader. You will return M'lady, I am sure of it. In the interim I will watch over Emerald Phoenix until your return"

"Indeed you will watch over Emerald Phoenix Lady Larisian. However it will be as their new leader. Where I go there is no assurance that I shall arrive. Where I go there is no assurance that I will return. I go to a land that is uncharted and only recently the gnomish kingdom has received word of its existence."

Larisian totally astonished at this news stammered to try to get something out of her mouth. Finally after a moment she recovered her composure to ask a series of questions.

"Where is this place you speak about M'lady? What is it that you must do? Why must it be you that go?"

"The place, Larisian, is called Velious. It is a frozen land south of Antonica. I am to go and scout the region in hopes of civilized creatures on this land and to take note of any dangerous creatures that may pose a threat. I have been chosen by my king for my stealthy ability to move in and out without being seen. I do not know what fate has in store for me Larisian, but I do know I may not be back. I cannot leave Emerald Phoenix without leadership. It would undo all the work the officers and myself have put into her to make her strong again. Therefore Lady Larisian, you must take the reins of Emerald Phoenix and guide her, nurture her, and finish the job I have started to make her strong again."

Lady Pistol was looking sternly into Larisian eyes while saying this. The look was one Larisian knew all to well. It was the look of seriousness. It was a look that was not to be taken lightly or to be argued with. "As you wish Milady, as you wish."

Lady Pistol saw the concern in Larisian's eyes.

"Larisian I have the utmost of faith in leaving the guild in your hands. If I did not I would have left it in the hands of the High Council to elect another. You will be fine. You will do a great job. The members of the guild trust you and have faith in your leadership. You have proven yourself to the members that you uphold the motto of Emerald Phoenix to its highest stature."

"But...but...."

"Silence Lady Larisian, there will be no more discussion about this matter. You are the new leader of Emerald Phoenix and that is final. I will be leaving for Velious shortly after the raid today. Until then we have much work to do in Permafrost. We must rid that area of Vox and Nagaffens minions."

"As you wish M'lady Pistol. I will lead Emerald Phoenix to the best of my ability. I will finish the job that you have started. I will make Emerald Phoenix the best guild in all of Norrath. I will watch over her until your return. This I pledge to you and to its members."

"Aye Lady Larisian that you will. I know you will. If I do return..." "You will return Lady Pistol," Larisian interrupted, "you are sly as the fox and as wise as the owl." Pistol continued, "Thank you for those words of encouragement, however if I return we will discuss the leadership of Emerald Phoenix at that time."

"I must make an announcement of this to the members of the Guild. You Lady Larisian must take charge of your group while Sir Piemar runs to fetch the other members for the raid. Go now and prepare your group with their roles during the raid. I must make this announcement post haste."

With these words Larisian reluctantly went away to make preparations for the raid with her assigned group. A sense of sorrow and loss swept over here as Lady Pistol made the announcement to the guild. However, at the same time she was excited by the opportunity and challenges that had been passed on to her to lead many of her closest friends. The announcement surprised as many of the members of Emerald Phoenix as much as it had surprised Larisian herself. Larisian had been worried about being accepted as the new leader. But as sure as Pistol had told her the guild trusted her and quickly offered their support to their new leader.

During the next 24 hours Emerald Phoenix along with the Warriors of Faith reaped havoc upon the inhabitants of Permafrost. One Ice Goblin after another fell to the floor. With each death of the goblin the caverns grew warmer with the flow of its blood. So warm that some of the ice actually began to melt.

All during the battle Larisian and the rest of the guild kept a close watch on Lady Pistol healing her as needed, taunting the nasties off of her so they would turn to attack them instead, allowing Pistol to use her deadly backstab.

As the last onslaught of goblins attacked Lady Larisian, between attacks, looked around the room for Lady Pistol. Out of the corner of her eye she saw Lady Pistol move towards the door. As she reached the door the last Goblin fell. Amidst congratulations on a job well done, Lady Pistol locked eyes with Lady Larisian. She smiled at Larisian, gave a quick wink, and mouthed the words, "HONOR ABOVE ALL!!". With that she stepped into the shadows and was gone......................................................
