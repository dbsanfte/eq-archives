June 19, 2001
 

Things are growing.

Well, people are joining, our ranks are filling. While I am sad to say that we have had a few that did not quite agree to our ideals, that has to be expected in a new guild. The Message Board is up and running! Inside you will find a Raid board for planning Raids or events. A trading board where you can sell or look to buy items within the guild. A general board for well, general discussion. Plus a board where you can ask me questions publicly to have answered.

Also, something i need to bring up. While Kort is about having fun with other people within EQ, there are a few guidelines for the guild that need to be set. I hope that all members can understand why those guidelines are there. Check out the new Guidlines here. Please Email me with any questions or ask me in game.
