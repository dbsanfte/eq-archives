Druid Powerleveling

 
I won't need to go into detail about what to do for humans during your first thirteen levels, because everyone knows about Blackburrow. This guide will start at level fourteen and go on from there.

Levels 14-19
In my experience, the best place to go during levels 14-18 is the Lavastorm mountains. The Lavastorms have three monsters which are vulnerable to the druid's expulse summoned spell. I believe it does 90 or so damage to the monsters which are vulnerable to it. Those monsters, by the way, are the Lava Crawlers, Fire Imps, and Fire Elementals. Now, at level 14 we get our first DoT spell, stinging swarm. This is an introduction to the wonderful world of snare-kiting. The procedure is simple: Make certain that you have Spirit of Wolf on, and go find one of these creatures that cons yellow or lower to you. Begin by snaring the creature and the put some distance between you and the monster. Then cast stinging swarm on it and put more distance between you and the monster. Then cast expulse summoned until the creature is dead. Simple, eh? Our spell line-up makes us druids excellent soloists because of our snare kiting abilities. Of course, you will have to adjust your tactics to different spells that you get as you progress in levels. But I digress... Anyway, the loot here is excellent for level 14-18 characters. Lava Crawlers and fire elementals will often leave behind bloodstones and the Crawlers also leave various spell words frequently. The fire imps drop various gemstones, all of which sell for several gold each. And to make matters even simpler, there is a conveniently located gypsy camp that isn't very far away from the entrance to the Nektulos Forest. You more than likely won't get premium prices, but it's nice to have a good unloading spot within your hunting grounds. Doing this, I progressed a level a day until I hit 18.

At this point, it should be getting harder and harder to find decent quarry. Here, you can either move on to North Karana or Highpass Hold. Personally, I moved to the Karanas and used our charm animals spell on Griffawns because they count as animals for all intents and purposes (Griffawns, NOT Griffenes, or Griffons! If you do this make absolutely CERTAIN you are attacking one with the correct suffix). Simply find two that are relatively close to each other, charm one and immediately tell it to kill the other near it. In the ideal situation, the charmed griffawn will kill the other one, which will give you experience, and then the charm will wear off and you can finish off the weakened victor with relative ease. The experience for such an escapade is quite nice. However, charm is tricky and untrustworthy at best. Sometimes the spell will wear off before one has killed the other, and then you will have two angry griffawns after you! Be patient though. Trust me, this method works. To make life easier on you, I recommend that you rest at the guard tower which is near the gypsy camp. That way, you will have the guards to protect you in case things go sour. Of course, these guards are often dead, as they seem to like tangling with Hill Giants and Griffons that wander by them. And as a side note, you need to be warned about Highland Lions. These things run faster than SoW, so if one attacks you while you are low on mana, snare it and lead it to the guards. If you have full mana, you should be able to deal with it easily. They are just irritating.

I charmed fawns from levels 18-19. Of course, if you haven't the stomach for this kind of adventuring, you can always go to Highpass and group with people who are killing the gnolls and orcs at the gates. The leveling there is much slower, but it's also much safer.

Levels 19-24
After hitting 19, I moved to Highpass Hold and group-hunted until I hit level 20. Upon reaching 20, immediately run back to a druid guildmaster and throw a point or two into tracking (there is a druid trainer at the North Karana druid ring). A druid's tracking will never be able to match that of a ranger or even a bard, but it's still very handy when hunting. Soloing is much easier and groups without rangers or bards will welcome you too (more than likely, though, they will just want you for your SoW abilities).

From level 20-23, I recommend that you hunt gorge hounds in East Karana. They are found near the entrance to the Gorge of King Xorbb AKA Beholder Maze. Just snare kite using stinging swarm and careless lightening, and you will do just fine. Be wary of wandering evil eyes, though, as they will charm you and do all of the other nasty things that enchanters can do to you. Upon reaching level 23, I moved to South Karana and began to camp the Hermit. This guy gives excellent experience, and he's a push-over to boot! And if he's ever carrying a weapon, it's fine steel! Nice money, to be had there. And now that you can teleport, all you have to do to unload all of those gleaming fine steel weapons from yourself is teleport to the Toxxulia Ring (I just chose there because it's the safest of all of the rings. No wandering Hill Giants like in North Karana or the West Commons.) Be wary, though as this guy will occasionally root you. His spells are pathetic, but if he hits you, he packs a wallop. Anyway, this guy will get you to level 24, no problem. At this point, we blind human druids can take advantage of the blessing of Wolf Form, which grants our blind butts infravision! No more stumbling around in the dark for us! I also recommend that you buy the new DoT Creeping Crud immediately after Wolf Form, in case you can't afford all of your new spells. These are two that you can't go without. I can't remember, but I think that we get Spirit of Cheetah this level as well. You will have to go to your guild to pick this one up, not the merchants that are at the various druid rings. This is a great emergency escape spell. It gives kickass butt hauling speed for about 30 seconds, which is more than enough time to escape whatever was after you. Plus it only takes half a second to cast, so getting that spell in between blows isn't tough. Be wary, though as this spell overwrites wolf form and SoW, so if you can't make it to a zone boundary, be prepared to ReSoW or Wolf Form yourself if you plan to keep running.

Levels 24-29
At this point, I moved to Kunark and began to group at the Lake of Ill Omen just outside the Sarnak Fortress. Group with people who are hunting there until you hit 26. At this point, you can snare-kite Sarnak Recruits with no problems. I recommend that, when blasting them between DoTs, you stick with Careless Lightening, instead of Dizzying Wind. Although Dizzying Wind does a whopping 122 damage, and Careless Lightening only does a measly 99, Dizzying Wind is an all-or-nothing resist spell, which means it does full damage, or no damage. Careless Lightening is rarely completely resisted by the Sarnak, and it usually does full damage in my experience. Snare kite Sarnaks until you hit 29. Go buy your new spells (including your group teleport spells! Huzzah! If you are low on cash, buy these first, and offer to teleport people for ten plat a head, which is the going rate [I usually only charge five, but that's your call] and you should have the money to buy all of your spells in no time).

Levels 29-34 I can only give advice from an experience (as in my own personal experience) standpoint up to level 30, which is where I am now. From 29-30, I hunted Tesch Mas gnolls in SK. They are easily soloable if you snare-kite, and by now you should have snare-kiting down to an art. From 30-34, I plan to stay in the Frontier Mountains and Solo Mountain Giant Hillocks and various Burynai and Goblin warrior types. As any druid should know, DON'T EVER snare-kite a caster. More often than not, the monster will root you and laugh at you as he is picking bits of you out of his teeth.

I realize that this guide is a disjointed at best, but if you can get past my ramblings, this guide will hopefully prove useful to you for your first 30 levels. As I said before, my own experience runs out at 30, so anyone who wants to add on to this guide or submit an advanced guide for 34+ shouldn't hesitate, because from here on in, I've got no clue what I'm doing!

Of course, if you know of better places than these listed here, by all means, stick with them! Just because this worked for me doesn't mean it will work for you.
