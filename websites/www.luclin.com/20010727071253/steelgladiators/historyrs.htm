The story of Risa and Sinena

The story of Sinena and Risa is really two that need to be told as one. They grew up together under secrecy: Risa a Wood Elf from the high trees of Kelethin, Sinena a High Elf from Felwithe.

Always and forever wandering under the trees of Kelethin, one day young Risa was startled to see a taller, blond elven girl coyly talking to a familiar foreigner, a human warrior with good lucks and charm to spare.   He was a frequenter of Kelethin markets, and one that Risa herself had had numerous and wild romantic dreams about.    The blonde elf's hair was pulled into the trademark high ponytail of the regal High Elves, and immediately Risa was reminded of how her own parents told her about the snobbery of the city Felwithe and all its inhabitants.  The fact that this High Elf had seemingly won the affections of the human warrior did not sit well with young Risa, either.

The Game

Curious, but cautious, Risa approached the young elf, who saw her and grinned a mile wide, waving her over.   As soon as Sinena began to talk, the warrior left, disappointed, for he could see that the two pretty elves’ attention was on each other and not him anymore.    The blonde elf was named Sinena, and was unlike any High Elf Risa had ever met before; against her better judgment and any adult nature, Risa began to relax and talk back at Sinena.

Sinena herself was delighted, as Risa was funny and fun to be round, and the two of them laughed and played games in the grass.   Sinena told stories of the Magician's training she'd just begun in Felwithe, and how dull and strict all the High Elves could be, and Risa told her about the Druid guild in Kelethin and making friends with all the animals, even proving herself right to Sinena when a bear wandered past Risa and modestly and amiably brushed against her.

Even as the sun set, the two girls made a promise to meet again in the remote area of the Greater Faydark the next day at dawn.    Sinena made haste back to Felwithe just as the sun settled behind the horizon and cast shadows over the forest, and breezily told her guardian that she was practicing her mage skills just beyond the Felwithe gate.   They continued this tradition with deft sneakiness, meeting for gossip and secret friendship sessions as often as they could.

As the seasons passed, Risa grew extraordinarily skilled at her spell work, as did Sinena, and after a time both girls gained the freedom from their trainers to explore other regions of Fawdyer, taking on the dangerous warriors of Crushbone and ghouls of Mistmoore together.   They were a fun-loving, dangerously strong and amusing pair, and both beautiful enough to stop the hearts of all those who hunted with them.

Marriage proposals were not uncommon, but both waved them off with a wink, later giggling to the other as they camped in safe locations for the night.    Their sisterhood faltered now and again, as most do, and Sinena and Risa had their share of bickering and fights, but after a while one would approach the other and things would be smoothed over in time.  During times when they went separate ways to different and exotic locations with different hunting partners, they knew they would meet again soon.  Sisters forever, they vowed.

Over time, Risa and Sinena heard word of a new guild being formed in Norrath, and deciding to try out the guild experience for fun (and cute men) they tracked down the members of the small but strong Steel Gladiators and petitioned to join as a pair.  They were accepted in without question, and Sinena made quick work of fulfilling all her flirting obligations with all the male members of the Gladiators, while Risa hung back a little and made good friends and hunting partners with all.

Hunting with the guild and watching as new members were inducted into the Steel Gladiators, Risa began to grow close and fonder of another fellow druid induced named Dakhanavar.   While he was bold and mouthy with the others (especially Raventod, whom he jokingly called "ass" whenever possible) he was funny and well-respected, not to mention one of the most powerful members of the Gladiators.

As Risa and Dakhanavar grouped together and talked and grew closer, after a long courtship Dakhanavar asked Risa to marry him, and with the uproarious teasing and blessing of all, they were soon happily married by Thunderblade, the guild's most prestigious cleric.

Sinena herself stayed engaged to many men at the same time, always causing a stir and sometimes a battle. But after a long friendship with Mumsley, a rogue halfling of Norrath, she accepted a marriage proposal from him, surprising the entire guild with the sudden announcement.

Nonetheless, Sinena and Risa remain surrogate sisters, and vow that if one of them wishes to leave the guild, so will the other.  Sisters forever, they have vowed, and all have no doubts about their sincerity.
