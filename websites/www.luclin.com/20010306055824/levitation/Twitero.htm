Twitero's Story

 

Why did I choose to be a Human Rogue?

                Well my friends this is a question that has been asked of me alot, so let me relate to you my story.  In the tale you are about to hear, you will come to see that I did not choose to become a rogue so to speak.  It was more of a role that was thrust upon me.  So sit back and relax, grab an ale or two, and give a listen!!

              We shall begin with my earliest memories as a child.  I do not remember my father at all, for he died when I was very young.  According to what my mother would tell, he died in the Ocean of Tears on a merchant ship bound for Kaladim.   My mother was always the type to provide what she could for me, even though I grew up in the slums of Freeport.  It was a very tough time for her, as she was always working herself nearly to death to put foos on the table and clothes on my back.  People would tell her that she could send me to a better part of town to beg for money, but this she never allowed.  Poor as we were, she still had her pride!!

              By the time I was six years of age, the hard work my mother had done had caught up with her, and she became ill.  Not having any source of income now, we could not afford to have the doctor come and see her.  At this time, I was forced to go forth from our shack to try and earn something to support myself and my gravely ill mother with.  Alas, not able to find any work at such a young age, and with the morals of my mother to never become a "filthy beggar", as she said it, I was forced to start getting the money in more undiciplined ways.  My skills were not finely honed enough at this troubling time, and I had to start sneaking around wherever I went.  I also got caught many times, and alas I could not save my poor mother.   

            It was then that I was forced onto the streets.  I moved from neighborhood to neighborhood, trying to find a place to fit in.  It was very hard, because I was forced to make a living practicing the secretive ways of the rogue.  Of course I had no idea of this at the time, untill I managed to pick the pockets of a certain man who used to hang out near the theater.  He recocnized that I had some pretty good skills already, and was able to help get me into the Coalition of Tradesfolk, Illeagal at the tender age of 8.   It was here that I found my true calling in life!!

              I was able to better train myself with mentorship from this fine establishment, and with that training, I finally dared to venture forth from the city gates!!   A whole new world of opportunity fell into my lap. With help from the Coalition, I was able to get the guards to tolerate me again, making my life more comfortable!  Also, I found that by venturing forth from Freeport, there were alot of other places in the world to visit, and thus that much more money to make!! 

              One day, whilst I was adventuring in the northern dessert of Ro, I stumbled across the meager beginings of a guild.  I was willing to join, becasue I had come to realize the importance of a sizeable force to help back one when needed.  It is in this guild, which came to be known as Divine Levitation, where one will find me today.  I hope this little tale helps to shed some light on why I "chose" to become a human rogue. 

             Twitero
