1. Guildwide


1.1 Member Rules and Guidelines
1.1a. Though shalt not desecrate Norrath. Clean up your trash by god! Loot your corpses! ; )
1.1b. Work with your guildmates whenever possible. We understand that sometimes you can gain more loot/better exp without others, but in general that is not true. There will be a time when you need other people... and what goes around, comes around.
1.1c. If you have a problem with another guild member, officer, or guildmaster. Please talk to another officer/guildmaster in order to straighten the problem out. There is no reason to quit the guild or everquest because someone was drinking and flammed you.
1.1d. Going along with 1c, speak your mind. If you have a problem with something, or someone, make sure to talk to one of YOUR officers. Running is the easy way out. Show us you actually care and stand up for what you believe.
1.1e. We group with other guilds. If someone needs a group, don't exclude that person simply because their nametag is different than yours.
1.1f. People who leave the guild should be treated with at least some respect. Often people leave guilds because of differences of opinion, lack of groups, or simply made an error in joining. If they were your friend in the guild, they should probably remain your friend once they've left.
1.1g. Do unto others and all that crap. If you don't like being trained, don't train others. If you don't like people calling you names, don't call other people names.
1.2. Loot Policy 1.2a. All loot from guild raids (basically when there are multiple groups) such as FG raids, plane loot, god-dragon loot, etc. will be allocated by the guild officers.
All loot from a raid, and hand-me-downs as a result of a raid will be given to those AT the raid before anyone not at the raid will be considered. If someone went McDonalds or go linkdead, and something tradeable drops, they can still be considered for the item.
To explain how it basically will work is that the officers will decide what the loot order is. Example: Crown of tranix drops. Usually we'd random between me, yaen, fyre, and woofee. Is that fair? Probably not. Yaen may have been on 50 FG raids, while I may be on my first raid... how would he feel if I won the random? So, to eliminate this problem, the officers would determine who gets the item (out of those 4 people). They would base their decision on:
a) who has worked the hardest to get the item (basically seniority)
b) who needs it (You going to use it or put it in the bank?)
c) who wants it.
Idealy people who chose to difer loot to those who need it, but that doesn't always work.
Now what I mean by determining loot order is that the officers would decide who loots when. Going with the Crown example, the officers would determine what each person has done. Yaen: been on 50 raids. Me: on first raid. Fyre: Needs it bad, been on 49 raids. Woofee: been on 30 raids and needs it bad. They would look at that information and determine that the first crown which dropped would goto Fyre, then yaen, then woofee, then me.
That doesn't mean 1 person will loot 500 things in a row. As in plane armor... but mearly replaces the random system. Once your receive an item, you're basically not elligible for another item till all the other people who need items, receive theirs. Example, using the same info as the crown examples, but changing the crown to a bracer of tranix... fyre would loot the first, then yaen, then woofee, then me, then back to fyre... and so on. Meaning EVERYONE STILL WILL GET LOOT. It's just that the people who worked hardest to obtain that item, and need that item, will GET it before others.


1.4. Joining Requirements
1.4a. Upon joining you are required to at LEAST give the guild officers your e-mail address, and it should be one that you check.
1.4b. In order to join the guild you MUST have a character L40+. Twinks are automatically allowed into the guild.
1.4c. Any prospective member is REQUIRED to have a sponser who is in the guild. Someone to vouch for them, and help them get into the guild. Their membership application will then be reviewed by the Guild Officers, Captain of the Soldiers, or the Guildmaster before they are invited to the guild.
1.4d. All prospective members are required to group with guildmembers before the join the guild. Guildraids are the best way to show your stuff and get into the Guild.


2. Guildmaster


2.1. Rules, Guidelines, and Expectations
2.1a. The guildmaster will be subject to a monthly vote of confidence. The vote of confidence will consist of every guild member deciding whether the GM has done an acceptable job (YES you have confidence in the GM) or they have failed in their duties as GM and wish to elect a new GM (NO you do not have confidence in the GM). The person tracking the results of the GM vote of confidence will be the Seneschal (see Guild Special Officers).
2.1b. The GM has the ability to call for a vote of confidence of any officer except for the Seneschal.
2.2. Qualifications 2.2a. They must be an officer.
2.2b. They will not maintain their position of officer if they fail to become guildmaster.
2.2c. They must not know of any reason they will be inactive during their term, for more than 2 weeks.
2.2d. You agree that for any reason, if you leave the guild, or fail a vote of confidence, you WILL give your leadership to the new leader. If you are unable to wait that long, you will give temporary leadership to the Seneschal.
2.2e. A new Guildmaster is required to write a "vision plan" with details on exactly what they want the guild to be, and how he plans on making his vision a reality. (Corny, yes, but being a Guildmaster requires you to have some sort of plan on what to do).



3. Guild Officers


3.1. Rules, Guidelines, and Expectations 3.1a. Guild officers will be elected to represent a ratio of 1 officer per 5 guildmembers (including them).
3.1b. Current officeres will be re-elected every month, with the exception of their "Seneschal", the "Exececutor", the "Captain of the Soldiers" and the "Steward", who will receive a vote of confidence like the GM will. The GM will talley the results of the officers' votes.
3.1c. If any officer who is inactive (has not been online), they will be stripped of their position. They will not be eligible for re-election following that term. Any officer who will be going inactive for greater than a week must discuss it with their fellow officers and the GM. If it's an emergency, they must make someone aware when they plan on returning.
3.1d. If for any reason an officer quits or is removed from the guild, his position will come up for reelection. All of that officers recent decisions, votes, etc, will be reviewed to ensure that they were in the best interest of the guild.
3.1e. Any officer may nominate new members to enter the guild. A quick vote by the officers is needed to allow the entry of any new members. The only exception to this is if the Captain of the Soldiers or the GM allows them membership.
3.2. Qualifications
3.2a. You must have a public, regularly checked, e-mail address, and be available to answer e-mails from your guildmates.
3.2b. You must have been an active member for at LEAST a month, total (you could have been inactive during the time).
3.2c. You MUST not know of any reason why you should be inactive for more than 2 weeks of your (month long) term.
3.2d. You must post on the guild message board on a weekly (or more) basis.


4. Special officers:


4.1. Rules, Guidelines, and Expectations
4.1a. The Steward. The Steward is responsible for HOLDing all guild items, money, and do any guild transactions needed. They are responsible for the collection and distribution of guild items as the Guild officers see fit. He is responsible for formulating a guild budget, and a fees plan which will help fund raids, guild items, etc. This plan will be approved by the officers. The treasurer is REQUIRED to allow at least 1 other officer/GM to have his account pwd and information, so that if he is unexpectedly inactive, items can be moved around. --This position is yet to filled.
4.1b. The Seneschal will be the 2nd in command of the guild, and the officer in charge of administration. They will be in charge of maintaining records of all guild activites, and help with the vote of confidence and election of the Guildmaster. If the Seneschal is running for GM election, this duty falls to the Steward. If for any reason the GM is unavailable, the chair will be a temporary "GM". He will also facilitate in solving any problems between officers. The chair will also be responsible for conducting meetings between officers and the guild as a whole (along with the GM). This position is ONLY available to current officers, who, before their term, will elect this position from amongst themselves. The guild will then hold a vote of confidence to accept/reject that chair, and will not actually ELECT the chair. The chair is a representative of the officers, and only indirectly a representative of the regular members. --This position is yet to filled.
4.1c. Executor is in charge of all in game raids, strategies and plans. They will nominate "Generals" who will be in game leaders of battle. The executor will be the supreme in game commander of any battle he is at, and will be treated as the Guildmaster in game, for all intents and purposes. He will plan raids along with his Generals. The generals are basically "mini-executors" who are in charge of single dungeons or areas. IE Kunark, Karg's Casttle, Plane of Hate, etc. All generals are subject to the approval by the guild officers.
4.1d. Captain of the Soldiers will basically be in charge of training and recruiting. He will have the in game duty making sure players have groups, have the skills necessary to go on raids (along with the Executor), and determining who is allowed to enter the guild. The only members who can allow new members are the GM and the CoS. The CoS will formulate recruiting strategies, and also be in charge of maintaining order of guild members (Including enforcing disipline). The CoS will also introduce new members to the guild, and be responsible for making sure they fit in. --This position is yet to filled.
4.1e. If a special officer is inactive for any period of time, the GM will act as his proxy, or nominate a proxy for that officer.
4.1f. If a special officer fails a vote of confidence, they may be allowed to run for a re-election, unlike the Guildmaster.
4.1g. Special officers can NOT be removed from their position without a guildwide vote. The GM, or a vote of the other officers can result in a guildwide vote of confidence (In addition to their monthly vote of confidence).
4.1h. Special officers will be subject to all officer other rules.
