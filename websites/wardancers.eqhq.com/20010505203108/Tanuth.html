It was a good day for the Hunt. The humans had been on the run for five days but that time is nothing for the patient Ranger. We had come for them five nights ago when they were many and invading the Fay forest. The Wardancers rose to the call and crushed the pitiful human invasion. The remaining three humans who had escaped the initial carnage fled into the forest. My fellow elves thought that it was of no use to pursue the humans for Tunare would deal with them for there is no mercy for outsiders in the Fay forest. I refused to return home with the rest of the group and told them I would be back with heads of the reaming humans. They knew my inner grief and the torment I lived with every day of my life and let me hunt the humans. That had been five nights ago and again the humans would be attacked at night once again. It may have arrogance or the exhaustion of running for ones life for many days but the humans kept no watch and were asleep like babes in a crib. As I unsheathed my sword and began to let my rage for the humans blossom into an unquenchable fire I remeber the story. I remember the Story.

The Story which has led me to the Hunt for so long, and I believe will lead until I die, is a painful one. I know the Story by heart, ever since the one named Mauras told me it. It does not begin with me but with my parents. Tanaras a mighty woodelf Ranger and my mother Saira an elf so pure in heart that it was said that the sun rose to make her happy. They both loved to travel the the world of Norrath and behold all the glories that Tunare had created. Unfortunately it was this love of travel that spelled there doom. It was while they were on Antonica that the Great Wars started.

Stranded so far from home in territories that were no hostile towards them it was a wonder that they did not die the first few days of the Wars. As it was, it was Tanaras skill as a Ranger that helped them stay alive while they tried to get home. It was in the Commonlands that they were caught by a Human raiding party. Tanaras knew very well that he could not defend himself and his wife Saira against such a mob. He tried to lead them away from Saira but to no avail. They killed him, but not until he had sent three of their warriors to whatever perverse god they came from. A swift death would have been better for my mother but the humans knew no mercy. They took advantage of my poor defenseless mother and raped her. Let not be said that Tunare does not take care of her children for she does. It must hae been her that sent the wolves and bears to destroy the humans and help my mother. It must have been her who lead an elven raiding party to discover her and take her back home. The humans took away the innocence and purity my mother stood for in an mindless act of violence and for that I will never forgive them. This is not where my story ends however but you can say it begins here.

It was in Kelethin that my mother found out she was pregnant. The child that she had always wanted but never could have, would be a bastard. It was then that she had almost done the most heinous sin by elven standards, she almost took her life. She could not live with the thought that her child would have been born by an act of violence instead of love. For that she hated humans more than anything, for that I had almost died. It is said that Tunare came to my mother in a dream and told her that if I were to live I would be the means to the end of all humankind. I do not know if this is true or not but I have hunted them eversince I knew how.

The child birth for my mother was hard and she died shortly after I was born. I was taken in by the old elf maid Mauras who had thought she could shield me from my past, it didnt work. At an early age I knew I was different. I had the delicate ears and almond shaped eyes that all elves had but I was larger and not quite as graceful as my fellow elves. Unfortunately others knew my story before I did and shunned me and mocked me. It was a lonely childhood I had which is probably why I became a Ranger, for I found solitude easier to deal with than the accusing glare of other elves. It was on Mauras deathbed that I finally knew the truth to my existence and it was then that the hatred for humans took place in my heart. I enrolled in the with the Rangers to try and follow the tradition of my elven father and I quickly excelled in every aspect of Ranger life. It was then that Tunare came to me in a dream and told me I was ready, and to seek out Melige Stardancer the Keeper of the Dreams and join her ranks to protect all elves and destroy their enemies. I became a Wardancer and never regretted it.

Which leads me to where I began. I rushed into the camp yelling my battlecry, the last thing that any human hears, "For Tunare and Saira."

Tanuth
