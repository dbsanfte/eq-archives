Raid on Neriak!
Hear ye, Hear ye!
On Saturday, November's 4th 7:30 PM PST
, The Light forces will strike upon the Dark stronghold known as the foul city of Neriak! This is a call for the Darkies to protect their fort, and to all of the Lighties that did not know yet about the Raid to stand in arms and fulfill our ambitions! It has been long, VERY long since the last time we Lighties struck with all our wrath upon the Dark Side, and I believe it is time! I am very Satisfied to announce that most of the Light guilds I had addressed, had replied positively to my call! Currently Participating : White Dragons, Heart of the Dragon, Darkwatch, Alliance of the Light, Flamma Knights of the White Rose (all that can make it) and Lesser White Dragons (they prove courage and cunning when they said they'll come to take care of all the lvl 1-8 healers - I bow before them) I tried my best to contact other guilds such as Patryns, Windwalkers, The Highland Army' and every other light associated guild, but I could not address all. Nevertheless, this is a Call for ALL of those guilds and every guild that wishes to participate and aid their brothers! Please Guild Leaders contact me at Lightcast@email.com to hear more and be updated! To the Dark Side : Come and show just how CRUEL you can be! I really would love to see you trying to defend your home but I promise you you will fail and the glorious victory shall be ours in a battle spoken of for generations to come. I call all Dark Guild Leaders to contact me if you wish to update / reply, and to make sure your guilds hear about the raid and come to defend Neriak.
Goals:
1) Banker
2) Necromancer Guild Leader
Rules:
1) Zone Camping - yes (both sides).
2) Loot and Scoot - Modified - if you are killed you are to loot corpse and zone for 10 minutes and only then to come back to the raid. this war involves NPCs so we can't force Loot and Scoot on the Raiders / Defenders.
3) Corpse Camping - NO, HELL NO - fight with the living not with the dead.
4) in the words of me buddy Grimbrow - 'Conduct yerself wif honor if's ya gots it...'
5) Participating Fee - Courage and Good Spirits.
/ooc after we kill every single living darkie and NPC, we can go crash on Freeport Bars and Taverns :) /end ooc.
