Story: There is no record of Kinuvan actually being born in any of Kelethin's or Felwithe's record books, although there local folklore states the Kinuvan was born in the mist of the Glade of Tranquility, a holy place. Nothing is known of his mother and father or his upbringing although it is certain that Kinuvan was trained well in the skills of a Ranger. He is an excelent archery and fletcher, it is even said that he could hit a target 100yds away,blindfolded. Kinuvan has not neglected his melee skills either for he is a formidable opponant in any duel, he has also practiced spellcasting to some degree and is happy to aid someone in danger of sickness or death. After spending some time in the ranks of The Guardians of the Glade, Kinuvan soon realised that the guild's intentions were evil and that they were in alliegence with the wicked Tier'Dal. Kinuvan then moved to his position now in the ranks of Tunare's Wardancers as Marshal of the Feather, Master of Archery. After many seasons Kinuvan was finally promoted to Keeper of Words after performing great service to the guild.
