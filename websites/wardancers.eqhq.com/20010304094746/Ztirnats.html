Story: The gnomish warrior lay on the hillside, her essence of life leaking out from the wound in her side. She could see the Dark Elf Necromancer who had provided this fatal blow, lying face down, unbreathing in the damp morning grass, her dagger sticking like an obelisk from his back. Turning so she could see the sun as it rose, she prayed to Quellious to grant her a final peace. A great spasm of pain racked her body as the unborn life within her struggled to escape her fate. With her dying breath she concentrated her remaining strength and pushed the struggling babe into the world beyond.

The stench of the battlefield was stifling even to the veteran campaigner Naradrim. His dwarven battle group had stumbled across the remains of the great conflict while tracking down some dark elves in Lesser Faydark. The gnomes and dark elf bodies lay as they had fallen, scattered in the heat of battle, staring sightless at their hated enemy. The silence was absolute, for even the creatures of the forest had fled from the sounds and the fury that had consumed this dreary place. Naradrim looked carefully at the gnome faces, some he recognized while others remained unrecognized or unrecognizable. He suddenly dropped to his knees and stared, not wishing to see what was before him. Pulling the many dark elf bodies off the gnome he examined the warrior for signs of life. The warrior gnome looked unhurt but the grimace that the face had frozen into bespoke of a death that was sudden and painful. "Not Zalanats!", he cried, "Rallos Zek, you have let your best fall!". Gently he pulled the eyelids shut of the gnomish warrior leader and silently mouthed his revenge. He looked swiftly around for Casanats his warrior wife, knowing that if Zalanats had fallen, his wife would be by his side. A glint of red armor was high on the hillside with its own hideous pile of corpse surrounding it. Quickly he went to the side of Casanats, for the red armor was a sign of the house of Anats, and saw the damage that had been done to her. She showed no signs of life, though her mouth was frozen in a smile of peace and contentment. Naradrim stood back with the realization of the loss and as he did a small movement caught the corner of his eye. The fresh gnome babe was staring at him quietly from behind his mothers form.

Ztirnats grew quickly with Naradrim’s family, becoming an elder brother when Briladrim, wife of Naradrim gave birth to a large babe that they called Oadrim. The brothers wrestled each other at the least provocation and usually without any provocation, much to the dismay of Briladrim, who had given up on owning anything nice that was breakable. Naradrim when he came back from another campaign against the dark elves brought in another foundling, a halfling, who was small and weak and would not speak. The family named him Btirnats to give Ztirnats a sense of extended family and the little halfling had found a protector for life.

When Ztirnats was of the 11th season, he took to wandering the little known parts of Kaladrim, the back alleys, sewers and hidden rooms. He learned of the Followers of the Tribunal and began to sneak into their meetings to listen to their plans of retribution. This was not easy as the followers themselves were of the sneakiest vein, but Ztirnats did not let this deter him, even though it meant getting cuffed on the ear when he was caught. He found that the Followers of The Tribunal seek one thing above all else: justice. While some claim to express this desire by pursuing careers as guards or magistrates, true believers in The Tribunal do not recognize the rights of any court on Norrath and enforce the Tribunal's sense of ultimate justice on the rest of the population. Retribution, vengeance, and punishment are sacred duties. Followers of The Tribunal are methodical, patient and just. They must be so, for it is their belief that if they punish an innocent, The Tribunal will pass judgement against them, and doom them to an eternity.

Sadly Naradrim was killed during another dark elven uprising, this one supported by the malformed trolls and their large brethern ogres. Naladrim was carried to his home on his shield and Oadrim vowed a never ending campaign of death to the minions of evil. He equipped himself with his father’s armor and swords and joined the training guild of warriors. Ztirnats, while grieving, kept silent and taking the only discarded piece of Naradrim’s armor, a dented scarab helm, left the family dwelling and went to Freeport as he had heard of a rogue trainer there of exceptional skill.

Not much is known of Ztirnat’s life after that, it is clouded in mystery and secrecy. Know one knows what level of mastery he has trained to, nor are there many that could conclusively say they saw him. Whole beaches in Oasis have been known to be littered with bodies and the few survivors talk of a dark shadow with a green helm. He has once again been reunited with his brother Btirnats and the two cry out to each other each time they meet. The dark hordes have learned to fear their greetings as horrendous death is usually dealt out when these two are together, as Btirnats has become a caster of great repute and skill. Upon occasion even Oadrim has joined the two to become an invincible arm of the forces of light. But Ztirnats, shrouded in shadows, assassin of evil, is the nemesis of all that follow the forces of the dark.
