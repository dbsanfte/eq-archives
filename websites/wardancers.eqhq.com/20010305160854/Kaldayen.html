Hail to thee, my fellow elf friend. Here you'll find my interesting story through time and worlds...

Once upon a time, I was on a certain world named Fennin Ro, wandering in the forest, when I heard someone talking to me, asking me to join him in a new world in a wonderful group, This group, Tunare's Wardancer! Always ready for new challenges, i accepted Drathomire's request and, a few days later, we were both teleported in Tallon Zek, a world apparently known by me but with a lot of differences. There, i met all of the others adventurers who were in the same group as I and Drathomire and I started a new life, a life dedicated to nature and friendship. I'm a peace lover and I hate nasty conflicts but wherever you'll find a wounded elf friend, you'll find me beside, healing his wounds and taking care of the evil creature or opponent with Tunare's Fire and Lightning! If you ever see a weird looking wood elf with brown hair and green eyes, always weiring his staff, his backpack and surrounded by his magic, say Hail to me and I'll be more than happy to know you better and make you my new friend! May Tunare's healing winds always whisper in your ears! Kaldayen Wood Elf Druid Tunare's Wardancers.
