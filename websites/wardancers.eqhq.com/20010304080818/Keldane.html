Story: Keldane comes from a long line of dwarven smiths. His father, Krageur Ironforge, was a great smith within the dwarven stronghold in Butcher Block, Krageur had taught Keldane, and his younger brother Halobash, the workings of the Great Forges, before Krageur's untimely death by an unknown Dark Elf assaliant within the walls of Chrush Bone. Keldane has spent much time in the forge, honoring his fathers memory, he has even become a master smith. Alas though, he prefers the feel of a dwarven made bow and fletchings to that of the smithy's hammer. Keldane is a master fletcher, creating great bows and arrows that can hit an enemy from 200 feet away!! Like most dwarves, he is rather abrubt in his deeds and thoughts, but deep down has a tender heart. He is quick to offer Brell's blessings to any of Norrath's Light races. Keldane has a deep rooted hatred for the Dark Elves, not just for the slaying of his father, but for the unholy twisting of Great Tunare's spirit in the torturing the first King and Queen of the elves. Keldane is a wanderer of many lands, this so much unlike his dwarven heritage, he can be seen all over the great lands, from Kunark to Erudin. If you see him, give him a shout, for he rejoices in seeing those he calls friends!!
Skills: Blacksmith (MASTER), Fletcher (MASTER)
