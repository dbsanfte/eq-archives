    Three long years to wait she had to wait for her. She was once a little girl like you , young apprentice, who venture too far into the land of Norrath. Now the cells of the Indigo Brotherhood are her new home. Here goes her story.

    There is a mystery around the birth of lady Meilge Stardancer. Her parents were once poor merchants from the town of Felwithe, but as a merchant daughter, she was in obligation to help her mother maintain the house. Her mother care for the only daughter she had a lot and taught her the love mother can give to her children. She would be the perfect replacement for her mother when the old woman think the time is right.

    But Meilge never talk a lot about her past history, only her close friends know her past life before she became the person she became today. Many say she is a crazy witch, or a pathetic and violent warrior. And many are right, but if you like deeper you can see she is a tender woman with feelings and emotion like everybody else. But life sometime transform people into the worst beast.

    As the war rage on the continent of Antonica between the Ebon Army and the militia of Freeport, her in faydwer the place seems quite calm. The father of Meilge was named Meilseoir, a great merchant who seems not to be a good merchant because of his fairness to customers. Meilseoir went often to Kaladim to trade with the merchants there who receive goods form the city . The caravan were escort by the finest of the Emerald warriors of Faydwer. No one would ever dare to attack this caravan. And no one did, not even the dwarf bandits of Butcherblok mountain. They were receive at the dock by their contact, a shady dwarf named Rutgor Irontoe. This gloomy dwarf was rumored to be a fine cutthorat, but also a great smugglers of the finest wares in Antonica. Meilge was with this group to keep the wares in fine condition. This day would change her life . This same night they arrive at the dock, they made a great banquet to honored the great association of the Irontoe Smugglers and the Faydwer Caravan. Elven wine was drunk by dwarf as gnomish beer was drunk by elf.

    But Meilge was a lonely and sentimental girl, always waiting for her prince to deliver her from the grip of everyday life. She was sitting on the dock, alone as often, keeping to her lone dreaming. Waves were assaulting the dock a lot this night, and this was the reason Meilge didn't heard her kidnapper come. The sound of the wave that obfuscate her kidnapper was maybe the biding of Tunare? Who knows.

    She disappear without a trace, the water was cold a lot for her frailed body. She remember not much her travel to Neriak, but she remember passing throught three towns before entering into Neriak. There she was beaten many time on the common place for all the Tier'dal to see. She was place after three weeks of torture into a dark cell, who she now know to be the Indigo Brotherhood quarter. The Tier'Dal said she were a fine addition to the trophy of their many raiding party. She subsist for three years into the cells of the Indigo Brotherhood, torture every two night by the a necromancer of the lodge of the dead, rape by members of the Ebon Mask. In her mind, she remember and wait. She was fed with gross food everyday, sometime she eats orc liver, other lucky day she was allowed to eat snake eggs. But was she kept like this and not kill like all Fier'Dal prisonners. And she wait. A guy named Lorenzo of the Ebon Mask came each night to give her some love. She rapes her , and rapes, and rapes. You don't know the feeling of someone touching you , licking you, and so one. And there she wait, her rage temper …

    One night, she tried to take her life (suicide) away. But Meilge, rageful and desperate kill herself.

    She wake up in the field of Commonland, but with a big smile on her face. If animal could tell the tale of what they saw this night, you would be afraid of your guildmaster. She raise and the animals shiver. A monster was born. She runs as fast as the deer to the sewer of Freeport. There she stay for two weeks, mustering her force for the battle to come, Freeport and the dock. She killed some wandering skeletons and took is weapon. May told she swam to the continent of Faydwer , others murmure she fly on the wing of tunare herself. Her new warrior was born, the one that dance into the forest, the one that rage at the sight of hateful race, the one that hate battle .

    Meilge Stardancer is a very enigmatic woman that talk less about herself, as if something was holding herself to speak. Who know??? If you want to know more, ask her, maybe she will ignore you, but if you win her trust, maybe she will.
