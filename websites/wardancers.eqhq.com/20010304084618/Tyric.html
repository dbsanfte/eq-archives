Story: Tyric was found as a newborn by a poor farmer while tending his fields late one summer. Believing the child to have been abandoned at birth, the farmer and his wife took the child in and raised him as their own. As the child grew older, he began to manifest the ability to commune with the spirits of the forest and the tundra, both those friendly to the Northmen and those who held no love for the Wasichu (mortals). Frightened by the increasing presense of spirits around their farmhouse, Tyric's adoptive parents sent him off to the capitol city of Halas to study under High Justice Shamus.

He gained the surname Bjornande after defeating a corrupted bear spirit that had been terrorizing a small village at the northern border of the Everfrost, and left Halas at the age of 19 to distribute the justice of The Tribunal to the wild and untamed lands to the south. Since then, he has wandered far and seen many things, and his great affinity with the spirits of nature have led him to revere Tunare as well. He now travels the lands, with his many traveling companions in the Wardancers, in Tunare's service. Recently he completed the tests that allowed him to bond with a spirit companion and become a Heyokah (a spirit/mortal hybrid).
