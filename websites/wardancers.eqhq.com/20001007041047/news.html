Friday, July 21, 2000 - Kinuvan

Ok, I was going to write up lots of stuff about the raid last Sunday and the little event we had in Runney Eye on Monday (LOTs of dead goblins:P) but I have almost forgotton them as they yhave been over shadowed by the big Battle yesterday. Here is a brief summary:
I logged on and said hi to the guild, I was promptly told to get over to EC quick as there was a big battle taking place. i retrieved my corpse and proceded to shout for a TP. Luckily I managed to get one off a nice shorty name Akrin for a mere 10pp (mmm rip off mmm). Upon reaching WC i ran to Fp west gate to get bound by that lovely Ninyenu, while I was there Callorn was also bound. We ran over to Nek Forest to find a zone littered with corpses, a quick /who revealed that there were 180+ people in the zone! I immediatly invited Glethowen into our group and began looking for Quadin, it was then that I saw an Ebon Army member. I proceded in decking the daylights out of him but unfortuately the little runt ran to the bridge where he was quickly helped by a couple of casters with pets, luckily their root ran out in time for me to get away back to EC. I healed up and went looking for the bastard but I never found him, I gave up and proceded to help in the battle where I could. We were presently joined by Pageboy who said he was a very close friend of Harren's. The frantic (and laggy) action continued until the battle was cut off by an Emergency Broadcast saying that the servers were going to come down for 3 hours for and emergency patch in 10 mins. This pissed us all off considerably.
After we had all logged off we came to this site and had a could ol' chin wag in the chat room, a good laugh it was, damn you Smelly Barbarian I can still smell you!
It wasn't all fun and games though, myself and Harren did manage to get some admin done so expect a lot of promotions in the near future!
