  For my first subject, I will show my opinion on the subject of Race vs. Race and Good vs. Evil.  I've stated this before, but I figured I'd start out with it.
 

      A long time ago when the server was new it was a scary world to live.   Darkies ruled all the lands and the shorties would frequently raid Faydar.  These times were hard yet offered a challenge, it wasnt Good vs. Evil on the server, instead it was everyrace for themselves.
    Now, as Ive said before the Darkies are dieing out, most of the old ones have left and there arent enough new ones to take their places.  Shorties act rude and powerful in front of us elves while we watch.  The server changed into GvE out of necessity, now I say we change back.  I am saying that most shorties(maybe all) and most humans should be KOS.  This will even out the damage that Kunark did.

My thoughts.
