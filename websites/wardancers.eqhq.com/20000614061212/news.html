Tuesday, April 11, 2000 - Kinuvan

The chat meeting went well with quite a few members turning up, items in the discussion included recruiting, guild events and our hopes for the future.

Recruiting: we decided that the guild was in need of many more members, so all current members should start to ask people they group with if they wish to join a guild. Any people wishing to join should be reported over guildchat to an officer. REM: members should worship Tunare, also give potential applicants the address of this site.

Guild Events: It was generally regarded that we don't have enough of these, so people if you have an idea for an event post it up n the message boards and I will see that it gets considered.

The Future of TW: We all agreed that TW has a bright future although some people voiced the oppinion that we should have a guild leader who plays on the server full time (no offense intended, Con). Please post your oppinions on this issue on the message boards.

After the meeting we had a mini guild event of our own in SK, Inkie killing fun was had by all . A multi level team proves very effective, meaning no one was immune to blows of TW members!
