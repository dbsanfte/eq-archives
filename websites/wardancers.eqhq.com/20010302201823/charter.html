Tunare’s Wardancers Charter and History

The Duke of Spring has told you to meet the sage deep into the forest of the Faydark, somewhere where time has no meaning. As you run between the heavy bushes, you hear a presence following, but the Cousin of Fall has told you to ignore this presence. You enter a clearing and there is a strange sight awaiting you! There is two animals sitting on their butt playing an odd game of chess. The old wolf is licking his paws as the swallow is thinking for his next move.

"Well met, young one. The Baron of Winter has talked a lot about your prowess in combat. Also, the Brother of Summer told me you have a silk tongue, like all gentleman should." the swallow said, interrupting his game of chess.

"Come and sit with us young one, you have much to learn" responds the wolf. "We will tell you the rules as dictated by our great friend Tunare to all her army of Wardancers."

The first rule: Duality is Life, Singularity is Death.

Those who live a one way life are near to death. Tunare has given us the freedom to live our life in two ways. As nature ravages the countryside and burns all on her passage, nature makes trees grow back to begin the circle of life. You are of two natures, Wardancer. Remember always to be aggressive as the wolf to submerge your enemy, or be kind as the swallow to protect your sibling. Remember that always. Those who stick to only one way of life shall be erased by nature herself.

The second rule: Creation is All.

We are creatures made from the hand of Tunare; we must create to survive. As banality gets more and more present in this world, some people must strive to create something of lasting value to counter it. It could be everything from alliances with other guilds, creating magical rings to organizing social events – all in the name of one thing, creativity.

The third rule: the Greatest of All, Fellowship.

We are in the obligation to bind ourself closely to protect our friends from marauding super powergamers who only want to loot our stuff. Without friendship, we will surely fall and be crushed beneath the hooves of the others races. We dont have the might of the troll nor the flexibility of the human race. We must work closely to protect each other from our enemy.

The fourth rule: Constant Change, Adapt or Die.

Stability cannot exist in a world of constant change and chaos. Stability is an anathema. The king of the elves could at anytime transform himself into the poorest merchant in Norrath. Embrace change in all its glory, because it is Tunare’s will, so it shall be done. If something shall be removed in the guild, so it shall be done. If you think rules should be changed, why not? Change can do good to these old, crumbling traditions.

The History of the Great Wanderers

The young dark elf named Tie’Shak ran as fast as hell in the countryside of North Karana. Where was he, he didn’t know?

He was sent here to deal with a troublesome human named Raven, who had done wrong to the Ebon hand, the rogue guild of Neriak. The guildmaster himself, Tani sent this young recruit to assassinate this man. But as he entered the sewer of Freeport to get some food and drink for the journey, he sensed a presence following in his footsteps. "You can run but you can’t hide," he heard everywhere he went! He slept in the quarters of some outlaws for five days to get rid of this evil presence. He went on the road again, thinking that maybe he was paranoid.

As he ran silently into the plain of Karana, he forgets this presence that is following him. This was a fatal error. On the road stands a lone man, face turned down and armed with two scimitars. He didn’t move at all, but had a smile on his face.

"At last we meet, Tie’Shak" the man said.

The muscles of the dark elf were ready to battle against this intruder. But was he ready?

"Before I cut your throat open, let me explain why I am gonna kill you as painfully as I can. For 200 years now, the Tier’Dal have destroyed everything we hold dear. They have destroyed our forest, burned our villages and told us that we were weak and pathetic."

"But maybe , you are," Tie’Shak interrupted him.

"Maybe we are Tier’Dal, just maybe. Tunare has given us the gift of nature, a gift not to be taken lightly. Nature is violent and unpredictable. So are we. That’s why I am gonna kill you with as much bloodshed as I can. As a Wardancer, I have vowed to hunt Tier’Dal to death or my own brothers will hunt me to death like a poor old wolf. Run Tier"dal, you stand no chance, run!" he said as he readied himself for combat.

There was fear in the eyes of this dark elf and he began to run as three wood elves appeared from out of nowhere. But they didn’t touch him at all. The first man passed like lighting near the running dark elf and with one swift blow cut the knee of our poor fellow. Tie’Shak feel to the ground like a wounded deer.

"As we hunt in the forest of the Faydark, we often play a game with the beast we hunt. To respect Tunare’s will, we don’t hunt around killing everything in sight as you dark elves do so often. We cut their knees and dance around the fleeing beast as she flees for safety," said the wood elf as the dark elf was crawling along the road, blood dripping from his wound. "We give her the feeling she will get away and when she thinks she’s in safety, then we strike with fury," the wood elf said as he suddenly disappeared.

In the plains of the Karana, all was suddenly quiet, no sounds and no elves. Only a poor dark elf, with knees wounded. Tie’Shak mended his wound a bit with some bandages he bought at an expensive price at the merchant and ran in the direction to Freeport. He thought he was in no condition to continue his mission.

Many years later as an officer to the Ebon Mask guild of Neriak, Tie’Shak was strolling down the street of Neriak to the guild. He had heard that the guy name Raven had been seen in the bar of Neriak. "Poor fool," thought Tie’Shak, "right in the mouth of the wolf." He took his dagger and sword and went on his way to meet him. As he entered the bar, he was surprised to see Raven plain dead on the counter, belly open and gore all on the floor. All of a sudden he was frozen in place by some magic. He couldn’t move an inch, for the first time in many years he was afraid.

"Well met, prey, we said we gonna catch you a time or so!" as the wood elf appeared out from nowhere.

"The guard will kill you, you won’t be able to get out of the city," said Tie’Shak, voice filling with fear.

"Don’t you remember as I said that we were as unpredictable as nature?" as the wood elf cut Tie’Shak’s throat open, blood all over his face. "That’s what you call a weak elf you son of an orc", the wood elf cried all over the tavern, dark elves not trying to interfere at all. "Be warned you Tier’Dal, that the Fier’Dal has declared war on all your race. Your pathetic race will be exterminated to the seventh plane of Cazic-Thule. Tunare has declared us her holy warriors, her Wardancers!"

And he disappeared without a trace.

The tavern was so silent, people frozen like ice. The body of both Raven and Tie’Shak lay there, lifeless on the floor. The hunt for Tie’Shak was over.

Many years have passed but now the wood elves can sleep calmly, knowing that an enemy has died. But what was the meaning of both deaths of the dark elf and human? Who was this holy warrior of Tunare? Why didn’t the wood elf kill Raven when he had time?

There were many questions left to answer, but one thing was clear: light elves will surely win, because it is Tunare’s will. And we, Tunare’s Wardancers, will be at the front, fighting furiously and without fear.

The idea behind the guild

Nature, our natural habitat, is very diversified. A thundering storm and a beautiful flower is what nature is all about. So why should we be portrayed in only one aspect of nature? Could violent and passionate exist silmultaneously? The answer to this question is yes. Nature can be twofold. It can be serene, calm and wonderful. You can hunt deer in the forest and be surrounded by beautiful trees and flowers. But nature can also be unpredictable and violent as the hell of Cazic-Thule. So why bother with only one aspect of it when we can mix the two? If we can mingle the two of them in one great guild, we will be the best! We will have things that will interest both kinds of players. We will be heavily oriented on role-playing but at the same time, we will be very warlike. We also need people that want to participate in the elaboration of the guild. So that’s why there are many places in the guild to be filled. Remember that I am not proposing a guild of powergamers that only want to kill everything on sight. We will surely be at war with others guilds, kill people and loot them, but first we are a roleplaying guild. This guild will be very aggressive, but at same time open for others things than war.

Peoples of the guild

As our great ancestors wander in the great forest of Faydark, they defended and killed many beasts. On one of the great beasts they slew, named Ferak’ta the great dragon, they found some inscriptions written on the back of the flesh of the dragon. In the ink of a pixie’s blood we marked some ancients runes. The druids and clerics of Tunare quickly transcribed the words. See what these word meant…

The Keeper of the Dream (Guildmaster)

You shall lead your children to greatness. You shall treat them as your own children. Protect them with all your heart and fury. Lead them to new horizons, to new ways of life, to victory. If you fail in these goals, you shall be removed of your duty.

This role is not the one of a despot fool that dictates every will of the guild. The Keeper is there to help other members in everything, to get back their lost items or to simply organize social events for the guild. If you have any suggestions to ameliorate the guild, just ask the guildmaster, he will be happy to hear what you have to say.

The Council of Season (the four officers)

As long as the great wheel of seasons turn, the balance of things will endure. You shall divide those under you in four different parts. The first one shall be the Duke of Spring. He shall be the one who creates. The second one will be the Brother of Summer. He shall be the one to bloom. The third one shall be the Cousin of Fall. He shall be the one who criticizes. The last and not the least, the Baron of Winter. He shall lead our brothers to war and victory.

These four officers are what the guild is all about. In trying to keep a balance to keep the guild stable, we make sure everyone is satisfied. Let’s see what these officers need to do.Yet keep in mind, we are Wardancers, all members are the sword arm of Tunare. The Council of seasons serves a dual purpose, they are the legion commanders for the four squads of various strength. For each Councilor of Season, two tasks are required, one of War and one of Office.

The Duke of Spring: This officer is charge of creating all sorts of events for the role-playing aspect of the guild. Spring is a new start. This officer is in charge of recruitment of new members in the guild. As the war makes itself more and more violent, the brothers and sister of Tunare need help. So he is needed to recruit new members to our cause. And also, the officer of spring needs to work in conjunction with all the other officers to help them create things. He can help the master of war in his war effort or the brother of summer in his festivity creation. The Duke of Spring shall command all the forces of Tunare that range from the first circle, to the fifteenth circle. These Wardancers shall be divided into two squads. The first squad shall consist of circles one through eight, the second shall be circles nine through fifteen.

The Brother of Summer: This officer is in charge of all social aspects of the guild. Summer is the season of festivities and banquets. By creating social events for the guild, we strengthen our bond with all other players of the guild. It's fun to group and go for a hunt in a dungeon, but it's better when you know well the people you are with! The officer must also help anyone who is interested in trade skills, so this position is for someone who knows the trade skills very well. If we have a group who knows all the trade skills, we will we be strong. The Brother of Summer shall command all the forces of Tunare that range from the sixteenth circle, to the thirtieth circle. These Wardancers shall be divided into two squads. The first squad shall consist of circles sixteen through twenty-four, the second shall be circles twenty-five through thirty.

The Cousin of Fall: Nothing is perfect, only the gods themselves are. This guild and their members are not. This officer is in charge of judging all that he thinks is wrong in the guild. If so, when he thinks the time is right, he can call a meeting where every member must attend and propose change to the guild. The Cousin of Fall will propose what he thinks is wrong and all members will vote on the issue proposed. The officer must be like a philosopher and a critic at the same time. Autumn is the season where the alliance fell to dust. So the Cousin of Fall is in charge of creating a network of spy and assassin for the guild. Nature has given us an aggressive, yet nimble side, so why not take the war to our enemy in the form of spies. After all, Tunare's eyes and ears are everywhere! The Cousin of Fall shall command all the forces of Tunare that range from the thirty-first circle, to the forty-fifth circle. These Wardancers shall be divided into two squads. The first squad shall consist of circles thirty-one through forty, the second shall be circles forty-one through forty-five.

The Baron of Winter: War is sometime unavoidable. This officer is like winter herself. He is the destroyer. He is the voice in everything concerning war with other races; it can be only a small skirmish for the head of a betrayer, or huge war campaign against other guilds and races. This officer also has the last word concerning the choice of the champion of the guild. He is also the judge with the guildmaster of laws to be applied. The Brother of Summer shall command all the forces of Tunare that range from the forty-sixth circle, to the sixtieth circle. These Wardancers shall be divided into two squads. The first squad shall consist of circles forty-six through fifty-one, the second shall be circles fifty-one through sixtieth. The Baron of War is the second greatest authority in all matters of war, bowing only to the will of the Guildmaster. Where the Other Council members are the Generals, the Baron of Winter is the Secretary of War.

The ones we call assistants

The next eight people I will talk about are, for now, not officers. But as the guild grows, they will be upgraded to officer status to keep a balance between normal Wardancers and officers. Each of them, receives orders from one of the Officers of Season, they are like assistants. These individuals serve as squad leaders for each of the Council. Wardancer's of any circle may be elected to these positions by their respective Council and must be approved by the Keeper of the Dream. The Assistant's act as squad leaders for the combative side of our guild. The Champion positions will always lead the higher circle squads for a given Council's forces, while the office based assistants will lead the lower end forces for that given Councilor.

Tunare's Chosen (Masters of their trade)

The Chosen of Tunare are the Masters of their respective field. They handle the one on one training for Player vs. Player combat. These positions are a great honor, they show that the individual is highly skilled, and a true threat to any enemy. The only way to gain these titles, are through trial by combat. Any member may issue challenge to the current Master, and this challenge may not be refused. If a great disparity exists between the challenger and the Master (more then 4 levels), then a test shall be decided by the Keeper of the Dream to decide who should rightfully hold the position. There may be only one Master per field at any given time.

Master of Faith

The Master of Faith is a practitioner of the mystical arts governed by the Gods or Nature herself. Only Clerics, Druids, and Shamans may hold this position. The Master of Faith is responsible for giving instruction in the ways of combat to any of his fellow practitioners that request training.

Master of Steel

The Master of Steel is the supreme swordsman of Tunare. Only Warriors, Rogues, Paladins, Rangers, and Bards may hold this position. The Master of Steel is responsible for giving instruction in the ways of combat to any of his fellow practitioners that request training.

Master of the Arcane

The Master of Arcane is a practitioner of mystical arts that originate from the Arcane. Only Wizards, Magi, and Enchanters may hold this position. The Master of Arcane is responsible for giving instruction in the ways of combat to any of his fellow practitioners that request training.

Tunare's Wardancers Code of Conduct

The actions of individual wardancers are the actions of the guild. You will be expected to
    uphold the virtues of this guild and act accordingly with the following rules.

1. The following activities will not be tolerated. Kill-stealing, Intentional Training,Camping
    Corpses, Camping bind points and zone borders for the purpose of attacking people when they
    are defensless and finally bug exploitation.

2. OOC and Shout are not to be used for baseless arguments. If you have a disagreement
    with someone about something use tells dont bring the whole zone into your argument.

3. We are a roleplaying guild and therefor we are required to be in character as much as
    possible.  If you must, use tell.

4. Assist members in need. We are a close nit guild so keep in touch with everyone.

5. Never send a tell to a gm or guide use petition.

6. Don't attack anything that moves just because it's not an elf. This is not quake.(Darkies accepted)

7. Always be courteous and helpful to people.

8. If you have problems with anyone report it to the appropriate person.
