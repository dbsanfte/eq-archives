Story:Gilwain as a youth began a career in pilfering and deceit, he became so centered upon himself that the rest of the world became infantesmal in the shadow of his own ego. In his early years he began to worship Bristlebane, the lord of lies, this is where he attained his name, Gilwain is Elven for Hidden star, and this suited him. But once his cousin Quadin emerged from the Order of Holy Knights, he set a goal to convert the devious Rogue, and set him upon the right path. Years went by, with many friends and battles lost and won. Now Gilwain has renounced the ways of Bristlebane and took up the Holy banner of Tunare, but yet feels tarnished in his ways for all of his evil. Gilwain has vowed to become the dirty hand of Tunare, to do the unspeakable for His Lord and his fellows in arms. Now as Ghost of the Dream, he passes nimbly and deftly into many perils, to attain the righteousness of Tunare by purging Norrath of those seen unfit by The Mother of all. He has great love for his friends and allies, but his wrath is terrible and unexpected. He wears a smile, until his knife is between your ribs, but here his eyes burn with rage, fueled by the Holy fire of Tunare. Never a favor given, nor a wrong left unpaid in full. Gilwain remembers all who help him, and hunts those who dare to cross him or his allies. An act upon the Wardancers is considered an act upon Gilwain. Though he knows his limits, he is always prepared to fall for the cause, or help when needed. He will run as swift as he can to aid the righteous. Never a plea will fall on deaf ears when Gilwain is present.
