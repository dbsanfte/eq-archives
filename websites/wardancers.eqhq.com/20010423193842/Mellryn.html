Story: I was born just outside of Kelethin many winters ago. SummerWind, my father was a Ranger and Trapper loved to adventure and seek out new lands and discover wondrous things. My mother, Sil ‘ ithil, was a Druid in Tunare’s service and accompanied my father on many of his expeditions.   It was only about ten Winters ago that my parents last visited our homeland, and soon after that the letters stopped coming. My younger brother, SummerLeaf, only now about fourteen Winters old, has been left in my care, the fate of our parents, unknown. Their last letter had made it known that they had heard stories of a strange and mysterious land inhabited by Lizardmen. That is the last we had heard from them.
Mellryn’s Description :I am a Wood Elf in the service of the Great Mother. As a Soldier in her Service I utilize the magical knowledge She has blessed me with. As a druid, I thirst for information, and seek to expand my horizons. In my quest for knowledge, I seek out new places, and new peoples, for it is through observation and careful analysis one can learn a great many things. The Mother’s Creation is filled with wonder and diversity and it is those things I most enjoy. Nature is my home, and in it I find everything I could ever want. Like Nature herself, I am both kind but when needed, as relentless and unforgiving at the Storm.   Although, there is a blight on Her Creation; the nefarious Tier’Dal. These dark abominations were responsible for the murder of my Mother’s parents. The deeds of this dark race have been etched into my memory since I could first understand the words my parents spoke. It is they that I most fear, and most despise. It is their perversion of the Mother’s Creation that drove me to seek them out, and to cleanse their stench from Norrath, like a powerful Storm that cleanses the earth.
