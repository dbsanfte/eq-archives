Guild Charter

This is from and e-mail sent out by Nickle when she was forming the guild. It serves as our charter; although is not written formally, we are not a guild big on formality...

I would like [Azure Mist] to be a guild that everyone enjoys to be in. One that doesn't require a lot of work, not because I am lazy, but because we all work well as a team together. One that believes that it is never beneath them to help out a fellow member. And also never expects people to get them things, but is not afraid to ask for help earning it themselves. Then there is all the charter stuff... we believe that all races are created equal... blah blah... all members will protect and defend... blah blah... the bottom line is I want fun to be our first and major concern.

Being in a guild is great. I love the green spam, or just seeing fellow members on. I enjoy the humor that is put out and the long nights of camping an item where you chat just to keep from going insane on a half hour spawn. And it is a great source of information. Most likely someone has been there, killed it or been killed by it. And it is a ready source of people to help you camp the leggings you have been dreaming of since you were lvl. 5 =)

So this is my dream and hope as a guild, let me know your views, opinions and what you hope to get out of it. This is only the beginning and I am sure I am leaving a lot of things and details out, but that is only because it is 3am =)

Guild Rules (in no particular order...)

1) Be respectful.

2) Try to be respectful even if they are goat-sucking twit.

3) Try to help out when possible.

4) Please remember your guild mates when you do something that makes YOU a goat-sucking twit.

5) If you leave the guild, re-invites will only be made by the guild leader after a vote made by the guild officers.

6) Any and all disciplinary actions taken by the Guild Whip are fully supported by the Guild Leader.

7) Any decision made by Nickle is final. However any reasonable suggestions or arguments will not be ignored.

8) Do not beg for gear or powerleveling. If someone is in a genorous mood, they will offer, If they have spare stuff (or you are looking for something specific) post in the Baazar section of the message boards.

How to Join

1) Try to research our guild. Azure Mist might not be for everyone as with any guild. It is sad to see someone leave after being a member for only one week when they realize we were not what they were looking for. If you are looking for a guild to do big raids, we are not for you. While we do raid sometimes and are doing more, it is not the primary focus of the guild. Here is a short post from Nickle on choosing guilds you might want to read.

2) State a reason for wanting to join us. It helps to understand what you expect to achieve as a member of Azure Mist, for yourself as much as for us.

3) Give a yell to a guild member. Chat, group, ask questions, and find the best time to reach a guild officer.

4) Read this website so you know the rules.
