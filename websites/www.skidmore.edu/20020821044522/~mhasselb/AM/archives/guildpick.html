Re: Looking for guild. (As seen in this thread on the E-marr message board)

In finding a new guild, make sure you group with as many of the as possible. A guild will have a personality already and you want to make sure you fit into it, or they fit with what you want. Also if you are looking for people to group with you might want to check their lvl range to see if you would be able to get a group with them. Although you would also want some seasoned players int eh guild since youare new. Chances are that they have been there, done that and been killed by it. So they can give you pointers.

Most important thing I think is that you need to ask yourself why do you want to join that guild. Then when you know the answer to that question you are better able to see if that guild fits you. It is like a job interview. They might hire you, but do you want to work for them?

Some guilds have admission requirments and some don't. Some have play time and lvl quotas you need to make and some are more relaxed. Just remeber every guild is different. You can also ask for a trial membership, you trying them as much as they trying you.

Number one rule. Don't ever ask a new guild to powerlvl you, get you things or twink you. Nothing makes me want to boot someone faster. You joined a guild for friends and when they are comfortable with you, they will HELP you get stuff when you ask. I have no problems helping someone camp something, but I earned my lv and equip and so should you. I admit I woulnd't have gotten a lot of my items without help, but I know the value of them and the friends I made.

Just a few thoughts on choosing a guild.
