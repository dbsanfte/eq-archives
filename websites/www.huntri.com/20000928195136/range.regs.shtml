RULE 1 PURPOSE


The purpose of these rules and regulations is for the control, government, and use of state Management Areas, and other areas operated and maintained by the Division of Fish and Wildlife within the Department of Environmental Management.

RULE 2 AUTHORITY


These rules and regulations are promulgated pursuant to RIGL Chapter 32-2-4, in accordance with 42-35, Administrative Procedures, of the Rhode Island General Laws of 1956, as amended.

RULE 3 APPLICATION


The terms and provisions of these rules and regulations shall be liberally construed to permit the Department to effectuate the purposes of state law, goals, and policies.

RULE 4 SEVERABILITY

If any provision of these Rules and Regulations, or application thereof to any person or circumstances, is held invalid by a court of competent jurisdiction, the validity of the remainder of the Rules and Regulations shall not be affected thereby.

RULE 5 SUPERSEDED RULES AND REGULATIONS


On the effective date of these Rules and Regulations, all previous Rules and Regulations, and any policies regarding the administration and enforcement of
RIGL Chapter 32-2-4 Section 1 Range Regulations - R.I. Fish and Wildlife Target Range Facilities and in accordance with Chapter 42-35 Administrative Procedures Act shall be superseded. However, any enforcement action taken by, or application submitted to, the Department prior to the effective date of these Rules and Regulations shall be governed by the Rules and Regulations in effect at the time the enforcement action was taken, or application filed.

RULE 6 REGULATIONS
As follows:

iii

1. A range permit is required prior to use of shooting range by any individual.

2. Range may be used from 8:00 A.M. to 7:30 P.M., seven days a week, provided the shooter has a valid range permit.

3. Range is closed to the public during all hunting seasons.

4. Individuals using the range will assume responsibility for their actions.

5. Misuse of the range will result in the revocation of the range permit.

6. All minors (under 18 years of age) must be accompanied by an adult responsible for the minor's actions.

7. All shooters are to police their firing points for empty casings when they have completed their firing.

8. Park in designated areas only.

9. Guests will remain behind the firing line at all times.

10. PERMITTED FIREARMS:

a) Rifles - Standard velocity .22 caliber rimfire only, using .22 long, short or long rifle ammunition only. Stingers, expediter, yellow jackets or .22 caliber magnums prohibited.

b) Shotguns - all gauges, using shot ammunition only. Rifled slugs may be used only when sighting in at the designated firing point.

c) Muzzleloading firearms - all calibers and gauges.

d) Handguns - .22 caliber long, short or long rifle. Larger handguns permitted with wad cutter ammunition only. Stingers, expediters, yellow jackets or .22 magnum prohibited.

e) No firearm is allowed to be discharged more than one shot every two (2) seconds.

11. *Use paper targets and clay pigeons only. No metal target stands or targets.

12. Keep the muzzle of the firearm under control and pointed in a safe direction at all times. Treat all firearms as if they were loaded.

13. Actions of uncased firearms are to be left open except when on the firing line ready to fire.

14. Firing on all ranges will cease when it is necessary for a shooter to go downrange to the targets.

15. When the 50-foot firing line is in use, there will be no firing at any other distance.

16. Firearms being transported from the parking lot to the range will be unloaded and cased.

17. Shooting at objects on the range other than the prescribed targets will be construed as destruction of state property and will be cause for revocation of the range permit.

18. Primary use of the range is for Hunter Education Student Training. Range may be closed without notice for training. Secondary use is practice live firing by the general public.
