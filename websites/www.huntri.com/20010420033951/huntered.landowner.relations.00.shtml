DEPARTMENT OF ENVIRONMENTAL MANAGEMENT
DIVISION OF FISH & WILDLIFE
IS PLEASED TO OFFER A COURSE IN:

HUNTER ETHICS AND LANDOWNER RELATIONS

A course designed to foster better relations between hunters and landowners and to improve the public image of hunters. The course consists of home study, four hours of classroom instruction (including a written exam), and proficiency testing with bow, shotgun, or muzzle-loader. Students who successfully complete all phases of the course will have their names placed on a hunter registry which will be made available to private landowners who are seeking hunters. Students will also be issued a course completion card.

THIS COURSE IS OFFERED FREE OF CHARGE BUT PRE-REGISTRATION IS REQUIRED. PLEASE CONTACT THE HUNTER EDUCATION OFFICE AT 789-3094 FOR AN APPLICATION OR FOR ADITIONAL INFORMATION.
