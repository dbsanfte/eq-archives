 SPRAGUE FARM
Pine Orchard Road Chepachet, Rhode Island
An historic and environmental treasure, which has been preserved in its open and natural state through the efforts of the citizens of Glocester and the Rhode Island Department of Environmental Management.

Open year round to the public under the administration of THE GLOCESTER LAND TRUST

Sprague Farm includes within its nearly 250 acres, a variety of habitats for both plants and animals, including the Blackthroated Blue Warbler, long thought to be extinct in Rhode Island. Many of the trees have reached full maturity, among these is an imp ressive stand of Striped Maple. A pristine glacial fen and other wetlands support many flowering plants which bloom in succession from early spring through fall. A grove of Atlantic White Cedar occupies a permanent swamp in the north-central region of the site. Approximately 200 years of agricultural activity has left several open fields which help to diversify the habitat, as well as a clearly defined network of trails. At three different locations, these trails cross permanent streams via beautifully constructed stone bridges. The immense capstones of these structures are testament to the skill and ambition of the early settlers.

1996-1997 HUNTING REGULATIONS FOR LAND TRUST PROPERTIES -
SPRAGUE FARM
Hunting allowed only from November 1 through December 31 (see special deer hunting dates below).

Firearms or other weapons are not allowed at any other time for any purposes.

THERE WILL BE NO HIKING OR OTHER RECREATIONAL ACTIVITIES ON THE PROPERTY WHILE HUNTING IS PERMITTED

Subject to the Land Trust's permitted hunting dates; species, bag limits and other regulations will be in accordance with those published by the Department of Environmental Management.

Deer hunting; on Sprague Farm will be permitted only between the following dates:

  • Shotgun...................................December 7 - December 20
  • Muzzle Loading............................November 2 - November 24
  • Bow and Arrow. ...........................November 1 - December 31
The necessary written permission for deer hunting on Sprague Farm may be obtained at the Glocester Town Clerk's office with the presentation of a valid hunting license and deer permit and by submitting other requested information. Permission, valid throug h December 31, 1996, will be written on the reverse side of the deer permit.

Although there is no limit as to the number of owner permissions which may be issued for muzzle loading and bow and arrow deer hunting, shotgun deer hunting permission will be limited to a total of 30; to be issued on a first-come, first-served basis.

To preserve this site in accordance with Rhode Island law and the objectives of the Glocester Land Trust, please observe the following regulations:

  • Carry out what you carry in, and please remove any litter you find during your visit.
  • Walk on established trails wherever possible.
  • The use of any type of vehicle is prohibited.
  • Camping is prohibited, except with the written permission of The Trust.
  • All fires are prohibited.
  • Possession of firearms or any other weapons, except by licensed and authorized persons in the appropriate season, is prohibited.
  • The possession of alcoholic beverages is prohibited.
  • Disturbance of any natural materials -whether animal, vegetable or mineral- is prohibited.
  • Swimming and bathing are prohibited.
  • Sound-broadcasting devices are prohibited.
  • Fabricating or placing of structures of any kind is prohibited.
