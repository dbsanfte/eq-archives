Rules and Regulations: Seasons and bag limits for the taking of migratory waterfowl, the methods of taking, and regulations for waterfowl hunting statewide and within certain state management areas and cooperative private land units, 2000-2001. All proposed seasons and bag limits are subject to change(s) in accordance with federal seasons and framework alternatives.

The 2000-2001 waterfowl season for ducks will be 60 days in length with a six (6) bird bag limit — possession limit is twice the daily bag limit or twelve (12).

  1. SEA DUCKS
    The season for sea ducks (scoter, eiders, old squaws) shall be October 6, 2000 - January 17, 2001 inclusive. Except for the periods October 6 — 9, 2000; November 18 - November 26, 2000 and December 2, 2000 - January 17, 2001 may be taken only in coastal waters and waters of rivers and streams seaward of first upstream bridge.

  2. DUCKS, COOTS, MERGANSERS
    The season for ducks, coots, and mergansers shall be October 6 — 9, 2000 inclusive, November 18 - November 26, 2000 inclusive and December 2, 2000 - January 17, 2001 inclusive.

  3. DAILY BAG & POSSESSION LIMITS
    A. SEA DUCKS - The daily bag limit shall be seven (7), only four (4) of which may be scoters, eider or old squaw. the possession limit is fourteen (14), only eight (8) of which may be scoters eiders. or old squaw.

    B. DUCKS - The daily bag limit shall be six (6) daily including no more than four (4) mallards of which two (2) may be a hen mallard, two (2) wood ducks, two (2) redheads, one (1) black duck, one (1) pintail, one (1) canvasback, four (3) scaup. The possession limit is twice the daily bag limit.

    C. HARLEQUINS - The season is closed on harlequin ducks.

    D. COOTS - The daily bag and possession limit on coots are 15 and 30, respectively.

    E. MERGANSERS - The daily bag limit on mergansers is five (5), only one of which may be a hooded merganser. The possession limit is twelve (12), only two (2) of which may be hooded mergansers. five (5) mergansers may be taken in addition to the regular duck bag of six (6) ducks.

  4. CANADA GEESE & SNOW GEESE
    A. Canada Geese - Regular Season - Forty days with two-bird bag limit and a four-bird possession.
    Statewide. October 6 - 9, November 8 - December 13. South Shore management fields will be open November 8 - December 13. Special permits and reservations required.

    Experimental late season - January 15 - February 15, 2001. Thirty-two days with five (5)-bird bag limit and ten (10) birds in possession. Limited to Providence and Kent Counties and portions of Exeter and North Kingstown as depicted on a map drawn by the Division of Fish and Wildlife. Special permit required, obtainable at Division of Fish and Wildlife, Great Swamp.

    B. Snow Geese including blue geese - the daily bag limit shall be fifteen (15) birds for the season October 6, 2000 - January 17, 2001. The possession is unlimited.

  5. BRANT
    The season for brant shall be November 18, - November 26, 2000 inclusive, and December 8, 2000 - January 17, 2001, inclusive. The daily bag limit shall be two (4), and the possession limit shall be eight (8).

  6. SWANS
    The season on all swans is closed.

  7. SHOOTING HOURS
    Shooting hours for all species are one half hour before sunrise to sunset daily.

  8. NON-TOXIC SHOT REQUIREMENTS (STEEL SHOT, BISMUTH SHOT, and TUNGSTEN) REGULATIONS
    No person shall take or attempt to take waterfowl within the State of Rhode Island, including Narragansett Bay and all its coastal waters, to the three-mile limit, while possessing shells loaded with any shot other than steel, bismuth, or tungsten shot Steel, bismuth, or tungsten shot larger than BB is prohibited, including duplex loads containing shot larger than BB.

  9. CLOSED AREAS
    A. Providence, East Providence, Pawtucket - That portion of the Seekonk River north of the railroad bridge (a.k.a. Red Bridge) to a line across the Seekonk River running between the intersection of Gulf Road and River Drive (Providence side) to the southerly abutment of the railroad bridge at the outlet of Omega Pond (East Providence side), shall be closed to waterfowl hunting.
    The discharge of firearms while waterfowl hunting on the Seekonk River is restricted to discharge toward open water (seaward).

    B. Warwick - Brush Neck Cove, so-called in its entirety. Mill Cove, so-called, in its entirety, including the beach front Buttonwoods Cove. so called, in its entirety.

    C. North Kingstown - Mill Cove, Wickford so called in its entirety. Fishing Cove, Wickford, area west of a line to be determined bisecting Cornelius Island. All discharge of firearms while waterfowl hunting is restricted to discharge in an easterly direction from the above line. The line of demarcation will be established by the Division of Fish & Wildlife.

    D. Charlestown - Watchaug Pond and Poquiant Brook, or hunting within 100 yards upland of the shore or bank of same.

    E. Tiverton - That unnamed estuary in Tiverton, north of Seapowet Avenue and east of Jacks Island, bounded on the west, south, and east shore by the R.I. Audubon Society’s Emile Ruecker Wildlife Refuge, and as posted by RI. Audubon and Department of Environmental Management signs. The effected area includes the inlet of this estuary and marshland on the north side of this outlet.

  10. ADDITIONAL RESTRICTIONS
    A. Barrington The Barrington River between Central Bridge at Massasoit Avenue and Barrington Bridge #123 at County Road is closed to hunting, except for that section designated by DEM markers on the river off Puritan Avenue and Seabrook Park Hunting shall be restricted to the water areas below the vegetation line, and between the DEM markers. Discharge of firearms while waterfowl hunting is restricted to discharge toward open water (seaward).

    B. Bristol - Walker Island - discharge of firearms while waterfowl hunting is restricted to discharge toward open waters (seaward).

    C. East Providence Check local ordinances for restrictions.
    The discharge of firearms while waterfowl hunting is restricted to discharge toward open water (seaward).

    D. Warwick Rock Island and its adjacent groin extending northward off Narragansett Parkway. The discharge of firearms while waterfowl hunting is restricted to discharge toward open water (seaward).

    E. Tiverton All waterfowl hunters must have the following to hunt the Simmons Mill Pond Management Area: (a) waterfowl decoys; (b) retrieving breed of hunting dog, and/or a boat without motor, or canoe without motor.

    F. North Kingstown Waterfowl hunting on Belleville Pond is restricted to the northwest segment of the large pond as designated by DEM signs. The discharge of firearms is restricted toward open waters. Waterfowl hunting is prohibited after November 15 by Town Ordinance. Firearms must remain cased until individual reaches hunting area (blind or boat).

  11. YOUTH WATERFOWL HUNTING DAYS
    Saturday November 4 & 5, 2000 will be Youth Waterfowl Hunting Days in Rhode Island
    A. Youth hunters must be 12 - 14 years of age.
    B. An adult at least 21 years of age must be in the immediate company of the young hunter. This adult cannot waterfowl hunt.
    C. Both the youth hunter and the accompanying adult must have current Rhode Island hunting licenses and state and federal waterfowl stamps.

Regulations for hunting GEESE in Matunuck Hunter/Landowner Cooperative Area and South Shore Management Area (SSMA) 2000-2001

  1. Permits
    Permits to hunt the South Shore Management Area (SSMA) may be obtained by mailing a self-addressed stamped business-size envelope [9 1/2" X 4 1/4"], to Goose Permit, R.I. Division of Fish and Wildlife, P.O. Box 218, West Kingston, RI 02892. Include your telephone number. Permits will be available beginning October 16, 2000.
    1. SSMA permits must be carried while waterfowl hunting on the SSMA and must be shown to any DEM Environmental Police Officer or Division of Fish and Wildlife employee upon request.
    2. All party members must have a permit and must be registered when reserving a field.
    3. No hunting party shall exceed four (4) hunters.
    4. Permittees may only hunt in the fields reserved for their party.
    5. No waterfowl hunting in the SSMA October 6-9, 2000 inclusive.

  2. Reservations
    Reservations must be made by telephone (401) 789-0964 Monday through Friday between 9:00 AM and 12:00 noon daily (subject to change).
    1. Reservations may be made no earlier than seven (7) working days in advance of the hunt.
      1. Reservations for hendicapped hunters may be made no earlier than eight (8) working days in advance of the hunt.
    2. For the purpose of determining number of days for hunting, a week shall be Sunday through the following Saturday.
    3. November 8 - December 13, 2000, permittees will be allowed to hunt a maximum of one-half day per week (Sunday through Saturday). No one will be allowed to hunt two consecutive weekend days.
    4. All upland hunting will not be permitted during the time period November 8, 2000 through December 13, 2000, inclusive.
    5. If unable to hunt, please cancel your reservation so others may hunt.

  3. Blinds Portable blinds, nets, tarps, or similar forms of concealment are permitted. The use of natural vegetation to build blinds is also permitted, however removal of stones from walls or vegetation from hedge rows is prohibited.
    1. Pits Blinds Pits may not be dug. Pit blinds are available in state-owned fields (3,4,5, and 6).

    2. Handicapped accessable blind is available in field #5.
  4. Decoys A minimum of twenty-four (24) life size, or oversize goose decoys are required. The Division suggests full body or shadow goose decoys.

  5. Harvest Report A Harvest Record Report must be completed and returned whether or not you hunted and regardless of success. Failure to report will result in the loss of hunting privileges on the SSMA for the 2001-2001 season. Return the Harvest Record card to the Division of Fish and Wildlife, P.O. Box 218, West Kingston, RI 02892 no later than January 8, 2001.

  6. Shot
    1. No person shall take waterfowl while possessing shells loaded with any shot other than steel shot, or other legally-approved non-toxic shot.
    2. Non-toxic shot larger than BB is prohibited, including duplex loads containing shot larger than BB.

  7. Vehicles
    1. Vehicles may park in designated areas only.
    2. Overnight parking is prohibited in designated areas.
    3. Vehicles may not drive into or along the edge of any field for any reason. [except in an emergency, e.g., accident or injury], or as provided in section 8.2 of these regulations.

  8. Accessibility program for waterfowl hunters who are handicapped
    1. Field #5 will have an accessible above-ground blind. The blind will be established by the Division in either a hedge row or within standing crop rows (i.e., standing corn).
    2. Vehicles may be driven into the field to transport a handicapped hunter and his equipment only when field conditions are allowable, i.e., not during and after heavy rain conditions when the ground is very muddy.
    3. Reservations for the field/blind may be made by telephone or by walk-in. The reservation may be made by the hunter of his/her representative at the Great Swamp Field Office, Monday through Friday between 9:00 AM and 4:00 PM daily (401) 789- 0964.
    4. Hunter must make goose reservations for an accessible blind no earlier than eight (8) working days in advance of the hunt. All party members, if hunting, must be registered when reserving the field.
    5. Goose hunters applying for the accessibility program must provide a statement signed by a medical doctor stating that the handicap meets the following definition: A person temporarily or permanently handicapped (i.e., unable to walk without the assistance of crutches or wheelchair).
    6. All regulations for hunting geese in the SSMA shall apply.

  9. PROHIBITED The following is prohibited: Littering; fires; destruction of fencing, equipment, farm property, and standing crops; possession or use of alcoholic beverages; shooting within 500 feet of an occupied building; shooting near or otherwise disturbing persons working in the area; urinating or defecating in pit blinds.

  10. Ducks during legal duck seasons The taking of ducks during the legal duck seasons is permitted while goose hunting under the above regulations

  11. Violations of any above regulations, state management area regulations, and/or any state or federal hunting laws will result in loss of hunting privileges on the SSMA for a period of one year, beginning from the date of the violation.

REGULATIONS FOR HUNTING WATERFOWL
IN THE GREAT SWAMP MANAGEMENT AREA - 2000 - 2001

  1. All persons wishing to hunt waterfowl within the Great Swamp Management Area’s Waterfowl Impoundment must first obtain a Special Waterfowl Permit from the Division of Fish and Wildlife Office, P.O. Box 218, West Kingston, Rhode Island 02892 (789-0281)

  2. All waterfowl hunters must have the following in order to hunt in, or along, the impoundment:
    a. Waterfowl decoys
    b. Retrieving breed of hunting dog and/or a boat (without motor), or canoe (without motor)

  3. All waterfowl hunters must return a harvest record card (supplied with permit), REGARDLESS OF SUCCESS, to the Division of Fish and Wildlife PRIOR TO FEBRUARY 15, 2001.

  4. Waterfowl hunting will be permitted within the impoundment and along the dike from one-half hour before sunrise to sunset daily.

  5. Any person found in violation of these regulations and/or any State or Federal game laws, will lose their privilege to hunt waterfowl in the Great Swamp Impoundment for one (1) year from the date of occurrence. Any person failing to return a harvest record card PRIOR TO FEBRUARY 15, 2001 will not be allowed to hunt waterfowl within the impoundment during the. 2001 - 2001 season.

SPECIAL REGULATION
STATE WATERFOWL STAMP

Beginning with the 1989 - 1990 Rhode Island waterfowl season, hunters sixteen (16) years of age and older are required by law to acquire a Rhode Island State Waterfowl stamp as well as a Federal Waterfowl stamp.

The 2000 - 2001 stamp will be free of charge to all 2000 - 2001 hunting license holders. The stamp must be affixed immediately on the hunting license and signed by the hunter across the face of the stamp in the presence of the issuing agent.
