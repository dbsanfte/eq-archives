The HUNT Rhode Island Trophy Room needs YOU!

Despite what some people say about our small state, I know Rhode Island has some BIG hunting opportunities. Your help is needed to fill out this showcase of Rhode Island hunting.

Send your hunting photos (as attached documents) to trophy@huntri.com. Include your name, the date of the hunt, where you hunted, what you got, and what you used to get it.

Please send photos saved as JPG format. Photos saved as BMP files are usually about 4 times larger than those saved as JPG. I'd like to save you time and me time, thanks.

If you don't have your trophy photo scanned, it can be done, free of charge, by mailing it, along with the details of the hunt to:

HUNT Rhode Island Trophy Room,
PO Box 3712,
Cranston, Rhode Island,
02910-0712

Unfortunately, your photo cannot be returned unless you include a Self-Addressed, Stamped Envelope.
