Dear Visitor,

With the mutual consent of the principles involved, HUNT Rhode Island is packing up it's guns in lithium grease, unstringing it's bows, taking down the tree stands, folding up the camo and putting it on the top shelf of the closet, and heading to the beach.

It's been a wild ride and we've met some great people over the years, especially our early supporter, Anna Minicucci, but it's time to fold the tents on this site. HUNT RI started when there was no other source of information about hunting in this state on the internet, and now, falters in the glut of it.

If your looking for more, visit Mark Mingain's Rhode Island Hunts. RI Hunts came along after HUNT RI, and copied most of our ideas, but they do say that imitation is the sincerest form of flattery.

See you in the surf, and bring some cold Coronas.

Aloha,
Tom Quinn & Rich Barbour
