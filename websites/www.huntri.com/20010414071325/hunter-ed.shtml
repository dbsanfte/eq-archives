2000 Archery Proficiency Testing
No Proficiency Testing scheduled at this time. See course instructor.

Archery Proficiency Testing is required to bowhunt the following locations:

  • Prudence Island
  • Patience Island
  • Block Island
Testing Requirments:
  • Positive I.D.
  • Equipment belonging to the hunter to include 5 arrows equipped with fixed blade broadheads (minimum of 2 cutting edges, 7/8" or greater at the widest point)
  • Broadheads must be covered or in a quiver.
  • Each arrow shall carry the name and address of the person testing.

Test consists of 5 shots at a fixed distance of 20 yards. In order to pass, 3 of the 5 arrows must strike the vital area of a life size deer tartget. Only 1 attempt may be made per day.

Archers must re-test every two years.

Please call 789-3094 for additional information.

Hunter Ethics / Landowner Relations Course
This free course, taught by DEM staff, will consist of home study, four hours of classroom training, and proficiency testing using a shotgun, muzzleloader, or a bow. Registration is open to individuals 18 years of age or over who have successfully completed a basic hunter education course. Applicants must be free of DEM Fish & Wildlife violations for a minimum of five years and possess a currant Rhode Island hunting license. Upon successfully completion of the classroom portion and proficiency test, students will be issued certification cards and be placed on a registry available to private landowners.

Applications and additional information can be obtained by contacting DEM's Division of Fish & Wildlife at 789-3094.
