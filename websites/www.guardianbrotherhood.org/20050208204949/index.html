Welcome to the Brotherhood!
Hail and well met!  You have come to the home of the greatest guild in all of Norrath, the Guardian Brotherhood!  We are a moderate roleplaying guild on the Blackburrrow server, dedicated to helping each other, helping others and having fun!  Feel free to browse around the site, get to know the guild and it's members and post a greeting or two.  If you have any questions, please feel free to inquire with myself or one of the officers in-game.  Happy hunting!

~ Website Changes
I have been diligently working to improve our website and get some new features online.  You'll notice the new main page as well as the forums being re-done.  I was hoping to save the original member database as well as all the forums/posts, but alas, the fates have not smiled on us in that regard.  Nonetheless, I have rebuilt the forums, so please re-register and get back to posting!  The forums are only as good as those who post in them.  I will be monitoring the registrations closely for a while, so don't be surprised if your membership status is changed within 1/2 hour of your initial registration.  Please dive in and start posting in the forums, they can be an invaluable tool and are our primary method of communication about the guild. 

More changes are of course on the way, so stay tuned!
