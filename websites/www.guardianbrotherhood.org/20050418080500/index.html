Welcome to the Guardian Brotherhood!
 
Hail and well met traveller! Welcome to the home of the greatest guild in all of Azeroth!

We are the Guardian Brotherhood and long have we stood against the darkeness. We are a moderately roleplaying guild on the Lightbringer server dedicated to helping one another and enhancing the gameplay for all.

We hope to see you in-game. If you should ever have need, call upon the Guardian Brotherhood
