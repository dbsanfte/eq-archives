Membership Application

Heya, this is the revised version of the Pre-Application Information Sheet, or PAIS as I like ta call it, heh,heh.

So anyway, you've made it to the membership application - that's the first step to seeing if the Guardian Brotherhood is going to be the best guild for you. Before you do anything, look back to the main website page, there are a few of things you'll want to take a look at.  The first is the charter. Make sure that you agree with what you read there!  Another thing you'll want to take a look at is the Members section. In there you'll find links to the houses that comprise the Guardian Brotherhood. Take a look at each and understand what they stand for, and what kind of person they are looking for to join them. Lastly, follow the magical link to the forums where the Brotherhood gathers to share tall tales and guild business is done.  You'll also find a forum there for each of the houses where you can post questions or state your intention to join.

Now it is time to start your application. Just click the magical link below.  It will take you to a form for you to fill in all sorts of interestin information for us to look at and laug--contemplate.  One of the lords will be reviewing your application, and then one of us (probably me) will respond to your application. If no one contacts you or responds to your application within two to three days, you can post in the house forum of your choice, or send me an email.  Once your application has been accepted, you will be contacted by the House Lord or a Captain to arrange for the interview.  note: gaining an interview does not constitute membership!

Anyway, after all that - good luck!

-= Lord Pigoursh -- Minister of Applications =-
