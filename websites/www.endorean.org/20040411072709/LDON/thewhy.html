This site exists to track our personal raids for LDON.  This is a separate DKP system from that of the Endorean DKP system.  

Why the need?

 Firstly Endorean is a raid guild who's raids are geared for 72 people.  LDON of course, only supports 36 persons per raid.  A separate system was needed so that LDON raids would not unbalance the DKP of individual members in the Endorean DKP system.  Specifically, the planes raiders do not want a "clique" group being able to hoard DKP to be spent in the planes that was earned in LDON where half the guild was excluded due to the 36 person limit.  Of course, it also goes the other way, the core LDON people not wanting to do all the work of getting creamed learning strats and adapting and then having some old school planes raider coming in for the easy milkin.  

BOTH camps have legit concerns and in the interest of LACK OF DRAMA, this is what the LDON Raid leaders have decided as a solution.  

Ah, but there's another reason.  The separate DKP system is NOT Endo only.  It is for everyone who attends and both Hatchet and Tygger have a ton of non-Endorean friends whom we love to raid with.

   That said, we welcome you to our raid site!

 

   Our goal is to see these raids defeated and to see our friends prosper in the process.

   As we embark on this quest we do ask that you bear in mind the lack of info's on the different LDON raids.  As of the first posting of this website, none on Luclin have beaten an LDON raid that we are aware of.  We don't know the quality or the quantity of the loot.  Our goal is to have approximately the same DKP going in and out of our system so that loot gets distributed as fairly and evenly as possible. 

 If by chance you feel you've been slighted or something is unfair, feel free to drop a message to lufalynx at ltlc dot com.  We'll do what we can to decide if something is really wrong or if you're just a pathetic wanker who bitches if you don't get every third loot.  Seriously though, we want to see gear getting upgraded, everyone have fun and see mobs hitting the floor like Deris the Reaver FD training noobs.
