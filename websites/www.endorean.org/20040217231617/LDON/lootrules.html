First of all, please note that our rules will probably change somewhat as we figure out just what is available in LDON and what will make an effective DKP system that doesn't over or underinflate.

   Any DKP system must start somewhere of course.  All participants in a raid will receive 1 point for attending the entire raid win, or lose.  If the raid is won, an additional point will be given to all participants.

   The loot that drops will be priced between 8 and 16 DKP points.  This is because an estimated 6 pieces of loot will drop per successful raid.  A full successful raid will generate 72 DKP for its participants.  With the average drop cost of 12 and 6 drops, that will roughly equal the DKP generated by attendance.  What does this all really mean?  Well, hopefully it means that regular participants will be able to catch a drop every 6 or so raids and that's the intention of the system.  For those who can't do math and think a loot every other raid is what they deserve, you need to remember that 36 people / 6 loots = 6 raids.

   Now... To the bidding and please note that we'll probably find need to revise these rules as things progress!

   1. Bids will be submitted to the loot officer in charge of each piece of loot.  The bid will be for the price of the item and the winner will be the person who has submitted a bid and has the most DKP.  The price of the item will be subtracted from the winner's DKP total.  You cannot bid at this point if you are negative on DKP. (Ex...  Uberaug_01 drops.  Loot officer announces it and declares its price to be 8 DKP.  Anyone with positive DKP may bid, even if winning will take them negative.)

    A.  In the event of bids from those who have the same DKP point total, raid participation history determines priority and in a tie there, a random roll will be made.  

2. A second phase of bidding may occure if none bid for an item and that is to open it to those who have negative DKP allowing those who have been actively bidding to dig their holes deeper. 

3. Hatchet or Tygger will have the final say in disputes.  FYI.  Drama is highly disrespected and we WILL politely ask you to never attend a raid that we have put the effort into leading if you can't take a rational, adult, NON-FRICKING-OBSESSIVE attitude to all this.

4. Minor augment drops will be offered for cheap.  Remember, these are (currently) the best "minor" augs you can get.

5. Finally, DKP cannot be shared or traded.  The only exception is for alts.  We want to avoid 2 boxing, but if you want to play your druid one day and your necromancer the next, you can ask to have their DKP consolidated.

Finally, it must be stated that these are still private raids.  YOU and other members are encouraged to bid on items you can use.  We suggest NOT hoarding DKP as there will some day be a time when this system will be abandoned as we go to play EQ2 or WOW or whatever.  Reap what benefits you can!
