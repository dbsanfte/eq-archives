Participation Policy

 These LDON Raids are unofficially sponsored by Endorean and Endorean's members. The raids are open to any and all Endorean and Endorean apps except those requested to not attend.

 We only raid on official "Nights off" where we are not raiding the planes and we do so to provide those members who do not wish to lounge a structured raid environemnt. As well as the invitation to Endorean, the raid leaders also extend invitations to friends in other guilds. Where space allows, we will also be allowing participants to invite their friends as well! 

Anyone attending will be added to the DKP system. Please be aware that if the raids become popular, we may not be able to take everyone along.

 The process is like this however. The raid is announced to Endorean in GUILD and we begin forming at the appropriate location. We take a head count and reservations for those on the way. THEN, the raid leaders send invites outside of guild. The raid is formed and we look at our numbers and then allow others in the raid to extend invites as cleared by class by the raid leaders (ie, we may not need that 11'th class_X ). 

The raid is filled on a first come first serve basis. We are NOT kicking out those who were invited and arrived on time in preference for a late guildmate. If you feel disrespected because of this, feel free to lead your own raids. 

 

To attend, you MUST BE 65'th level. You MUST have 2 normal wins in the current theme we are raiding. You MUST be able to attend a 3.5 - 4 hour raid.
