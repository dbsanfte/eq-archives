The Senatus of Acervatim Amicitiae:

The Senatus is the Council of Elders. It is comprised of all ten founding members and is responsible for the developement of policy, the assignment of officer positions, and the approval of non-commisioned officer titles.

The Officers of the Senatus are detailed as follows:

Dominatus - The Guild Leader.
Current Dominatus: Hika Mindsong
The Dominatus, as guild leader, is responsible for the ratification of policy and other decisions (besides membership) of the Senatus.

Potestas Tabularium - Keeper of the Chronicles
Currently held by: Shaorri Spelldancer
This officer is the keeper of documentation and communications, and serves as second in command of the guild when the guildmaster is unable to perform their duties. Guild members wishing to have certain information stored in the archives that is of a general nature will submit it to the Potestas Tabularium. This position archives (and makes available) the various names, classes, and trade-professions of all members of the guild as well as any other record-keeping duties that should arise.

Magister Occultus- Master of Arcane Knowledge
Currently held by: Erathon Soulblaze
Representative of all magi classes. Position requires in-depth game knowledge of the spells and tactics for most casting classes. This officer will advise other members on the little known magical aspects of the game such as rune-combinations for spell research, quest hints (not solutions) for quest only spells, etc. Guild Magi will report informative findings to the Magister Occultus for collection into the archives.

Magister Votum- Master of Prayer
Currently held by: Grunthar Mindspirit
This officer is in charge of the priestly wing of the guild. Clerical, druidic, and shamanic members will consult with this officer for information on tactics, spell locations, and informations on quests, etc. Guild priests will report informative findings to the Magister Votum for collection into the archives.

Magister Gladius- Master of Sword
Currently held by: Ursin Baleblade
Representative of all non-caster classes. Position requires fundamental knowledge of the magic system and in-depth knowledge behind the combat system and appropriate warrior tactics. This officer will advise and recommend (on request) which armour types and magic items various members should pursue appropriate to his class and level.
