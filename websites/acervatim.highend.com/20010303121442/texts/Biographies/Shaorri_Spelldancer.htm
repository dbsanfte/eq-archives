Shaorri Spelldancer
Potestas Tabularium
Acervatim Amicitiae

History:
Born into the Spelldancer house of Tarew Marr's Felwithe, Shaorri was the first female child of Shallae Spelldancer and her consort Llyandar Manastryke. Both parents died in the Fourth Age of Norrath when Cazic Thule manifest himself upon the land to bring war and destruction to the face of Norrath. As is the calling of all who follow Tunare, the Spelldancer house was among those at the forefront of this battle to defend the lands of Norrath and drive the evil back into the beyond. A mere child then, Shaorri was kept safely behind in Felwithe under the stewardship of her current mentor, Tarker Blazetoss. Her hatred for all things from the hand of Cazic Thule is legendary and she pursues this vengeance with the Will of Iron and the Power of Wizardry that is the mark of the Spelldancer clan.

It was during these formative years when she was still dealing with the loss of her parents by consuming all the knowledge that Master Blazetoss would give her that she first recognized a distinct and separate voice within her mind. It was like her conscience but it was wild, free and lacked the tight control her training had provided her mind. As she grew in power so did this constant companion. She hid the presence of this being in her thoughts from others for many years. Eventually the presence grew to such strength that she could communicate with it. She called it Mindspirit and eventually came to know, through study and magics, that Mindspirit was a Barbarian in the far off lands of Halas, named Grunthar.

As she progressed through the latter half of her childhood, she continued to train and study under Master Blazetoss all the while fighting the creations of Rallos Zek, Solusek Ro, and the hated Cazic Thule. As her childhood came to a close, Mindspirit made a journey of fantastic peril from the icy lands of Halas to the hot forests of Faydwer. It was in the outlands of the Gnomes, the Steamfont Mountains of Faydwer, that Shaorri first met Grunthar.

A giant of a man, compared to the Elves of her homelands, Shaorri would have been terrified of Grunthar had not the rapport between them been so obvious in his voice and actions. In the Steamfont Mountains with the help of Grunthar and others she left her childhood behind having attained the 12th Rank in Wizardry. So began the current age of adventure, no longer a child in mind or body, Shaorri left the lands of her heritage to prove herself worthy of the title, Spelldancer.
 

Current Events:
After the dreary boat ride from Faydwer to Antonica, Shaorri bravely stepped forward into this new and unknown land. She adventured in the commonlands with Grunthar, Ursin and others even braving the haunted halls of Befallen. Her journeys took her all across this wondrous new land where she encountered not only many new dangerous, but also many kindred spirits. Traveling to the far side of Antonica to the law-held walls of Qeynos the number of her continued to grow and the mind set that was common among them all became clear. This circle of friends, under the leadership of Hika Mindsong continued to grow and form until it became the guild known as Acervatim Amicitiae.

Shortly after Acervatim Amicitiae was formed, Shaorri returned to the fertile lands known as the Karanas. It was here that she obtained the first Rank of Recognition and could at least rightfully claim her title, even though she was not yet Ranked well enough to assume control over the Matriarchy. Traveling under the power of Grunthar's magics she made her way back to Qeynos where the very masters of the realm came down and bestowed upon her, the name of her house; Spelldancer.

With her newfound power Shaorri journeyed all across Norrath, visiting her homeland and even far of Erudin within moments. She collected many spells of power during these magical trips. At last, she approaches the second Rank of Recognition where she might step forth and claim the Matriarchy of House Spelldancer as her own and begin the long process of returning it to its former glory.

The years since she first left Faydwer have not been all adventure, work, and glory. A great deal of time was spent with her constant companion, Grunthar Mindspirit. During these years a bond even more powerful than before began to form between the two even if Shaorri was slow to see it. In the early morning hours overlooking the harbor of Freeport, Grunthar hands trembling and tongue slow with nervousness asked Shaorri for her hand in marriage. Her heart swelled with a joy too long suppressed and unrecognized, the words sprung from her soul and she accepted him as her life mate, thus becoming the first Spelldancer to choose life-bonding over simple companionship. All was as it was meant to be.

Shaorri is currently setting about preparing for the wedding as well as looking forward towards the deeds and actions that must be done to rebuild House Spelldancer. With her life-mate Grunthar at her side, the journey, though difficult, will be joyous.
