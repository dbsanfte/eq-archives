Supplicant:

A Suplicant is not a member of the Acervatim Amicitiae.

A Supplicant is currently under evaluation by the Sentatus (see membership requirements for more details).

A Supplicant does not have the right to claim membership to the guild nor are they given any of the rights and privaledges of Sodali until such time as they have been approved for membership by the Senatus. Immediately upon approval a Supplicants status is raised to Sodalis.
