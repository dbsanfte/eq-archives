Acervatim Amicitae

Who we are:
Acervatim Amicitiae is a close group of friends and is intentionally a small guild. Our membership requirements are few, but must be followed explicity.


Our Mission:

Through extensive use of role-playing Acervatim Amicitiae intends to explore all of Norrath lending a hand to those in need as they go. Acervatim Amicitiae shall seek to improve the gaming experience of members and non-members alike.

Acervatim Amicitiae exists only on the Tarew Marr server.

Divisions:

Below you will find a list of the titles bestowed upon the various members of Acervatim Amicitiae.
Dominatus - The Guild Leader.
Senatus - The Council of Elders.
Sodalis - A Full member of the guild.
Supplicant - A prospective member.

There are Additional Titles that may be assigned to non-commisioned officers.


Guild Wars and Duels:

The guild is non-PK oriented. The guild has no desire to engage in guild warfare. The guild may, however, elect through normal vote to participate in certain arranged role playing events from time to time. At any rate, individual guild members are not required to participate in any PK duel or event, even if the guild as a whole votes to participate in the same. Acervatim Amicitiae honors and respects the desire of its members to be free of PK activities.


Expected Behaviour:

Guild members are expected to act with decency to their fellow guild members as well as the Everquest community as a whole.

Guild members are to be aware that they represent not only themselves, but all other members of the guild when playing or communicating as a member.

Guild members are to act with respect to the character and racial choices of other members. Acervatim Amicitiae does not discriminate based on race or class or color of armor, nor any other basis.

Guild members are free to role play their characters as they see fit, but should be aware that most members are of good or neutral alignment. Members should not interfere with the playing enjoyment of other guild members.

Guild members must not engage in illegal, exploitive, or questionable activites. This includes, but is not limited to: taking advantage of known bugs; taking advantage of NPC misbehaviours; delivering trains on people as punishment; interfering with the enjoyment of the game for others. Guild members must adhere to the terms of service as issued by Verant for the right to play Everquest.

Guild members are expected to uphold the charter, mission, and structure of the guild by deed and word.


Censure: Herein may be found the process with which complaints about guild-members are handled.
