Censure

Complaints of a minor issue are encouraged to be resolved among the characters and players involved. Should such resolution be unattainable, the principles may submit the complaint to the Senatus for adjudication.

If one of the membership of the Senatus is the subject of the complaint, said member will be temporarily replaced by another member of the guild, appointed by the consensus of the remaining Senatus members. Said appointment will not be permanent, and will not survive the termination of the complaint procedure.

Should any member of the Senatus be in conflict of interest in the complaint, said member or members shall be replaced by temporary appointments, as agreed to by the remaining members of the Senatus. Such appointments will not be permanent, and will not survive the termination of the complaint procedure.

Evidince regarding the complaint will be heard by the Senatus. The subject of the complaint will be informed of the nature of the complaint and will be given the opportunity to defend themselves.

Upon receipt of all information, the Senatus will discuss the merits of the complaint, and shall decide if the subject requires censure, or is innocent.

If censure is indicated, the Senatus will inform the member of the reasons, and will grant the member the opportunity to rectify the wrongdoing or behavioural pattern that is the subject of the complaint. A term of two weeks of real world time shall be assigned for this purpose.

If at the end of this period, the issue of the complaint has not been resolved, the Senatus is authorized to deliver a Writ of Censure to the offending party or parties. Censure shall take the form of a punishment that suits the offense. Censure can include payment of fines, suspension of membership, removal from office, and expulsion from the guild.

It is hoped that such censure shall not be required of the Senatus.
