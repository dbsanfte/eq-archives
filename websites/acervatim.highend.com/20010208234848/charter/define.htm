Just what does that name mean and where did it come from?

The name originated after a great deal of discussion between several of the founding members. It primarily came about as a way to describe who and what we are. The words are in Latin and probably look familar to many people.

Acervatim, roughly translates to "Large Quantities, as in heaps or piles" while Amicitiae is the Latin plural for "Friends or Companions".

I am the first to admit my Latin is extremely poor, but Acervatim Amicitiae is how the concept translated for me and so, now, it is our name.
