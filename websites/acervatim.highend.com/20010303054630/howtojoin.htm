Sponsorship:

Recently we have greatly revamped the proceedure for sponsoring new members into Acervatim Amicitiae.  The process has been streamlined and the burden of contact placed upon the current members of the guild rather than potential applicants.

In order to sponsor a new member to the guild, any Sodalis may post the potential candidates name to the Message Board along with a brief description of who they are and why said member wishes them to be a part of Acervatim Amicitiae.  It is then up to the various members of the guild to determine if there is any substantial reason why this person should not be allowed membership.  Such reasons might be a history of harrasment, kill-stealing, etc.  Assuming no current guild member presents sufficient evidence or argument against the supplicant, after seven days membership is granted.

It has never been the ultimate desire of Acervatim Amicitae to grow into a large guild; Nor is it the intention of the guild to immerse itself in the politics naturally associated with truly large guilds.  We are but a collection of friends that use this common communications medium to stay in close contact with each other.
