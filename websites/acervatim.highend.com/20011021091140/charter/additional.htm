On Additional Guild Titles:
On the recommendation of the Senatus, additional non-commisioned-officer titles and responsabilities may be assigned to Sodali. Such titles will be prefixed with the term 'Praefectus'.

Such titular offices exist primarily as an ehnancement to role play, and are not members of the Senatus. However, such titles are not to be taken lightly, as they are to represent certain responsabilities. The Senatus may wish to appoint an officer for the druids, for instance. Such a character might then recieve, as ratified by the Senatus, the title of Praefectus Silvestris.

There are currently no additional titles assigned.
