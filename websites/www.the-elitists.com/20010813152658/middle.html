Yelinak is a real interesting fight. I'll explain. Imagine, if you would, that you're a big bad bruiser in a bar and you've knocked a few back and you're looking to pick a fight. Over by the bar is a scrawny little twerp that gave you a funny look so you swagger over and start mouthing off. You get the guy outside and are ready to drop him to the pavement when he pulls out some kung-fu shobayashi crap on your ass and the next thing you know you're counting stars.
