Remember who the enemy is...

Ok, plenty of blood is boiling. Flags are flying, every red blooded American here is justly mad at this. But now is a time for caution and care. We are hypersensitive right now. It's to be expected. After something like this... anger, rage, disbelief, shock, hurt - these are the emotions rattling around in our minds. I understand that - hell I know because I am feeling it.

But we must be careful to know who the enemy is. I do not stand above you in saying this, but amongst you. I am as guilty as anyone here. Can I really say I blame people for those feelings? No, because that, is, as they say, the human condition. It is also how Americans respond to horrors such as this. We seem fat, lazy, and passive. And in most respects, perhaps we are. But, when roused, our motivation, will power, and resolve that we are right is unshakable. We will embark on ANY impossibility, and reshape the universe around us if that is what it takes. That is the way we are, and the way we have always been.

And this resolve, this confidence that we are right, this fanatic belief in the justification of our cause, also leaves us VERY unsympathetic to those who appear to support, or show sympathy for the people that so gravely wronged the citizens of the U.S.A. So, when someone tries to take a dissenting view, or out right supports the antagonists of America when we are this upset, they can become a pariah.

Know right now I am not standing in support of any of the vile things that were said. I stood aghast. And, I screamed back, as most of the rest of us have. It was sickening to see someone say that people deserved what happened - no creature on earth deserved what happened on Tuesday. (Except possibly the perpetrators of this genocide and their sponsors, but we all know their fate is sealed).

Yet, we must remember, this is America. Why do we allow sick groups like the KKK or the vomitus Aryan Nation to belch their vile slop in the streets? Why do we let Louis Farakahn spout his evil racist propaganda? Because, here in America, we let people say whatever they want. We cannot forget this.

Sounds odd coming from me? Admittedly their are times when censoring is necessary. Spamming for instance. Or when security dictates it. Trolling. Or when our hosts and providers, the ones who ultimately decide if we continue to have a home, demand it.

We need to stick together. Nobody here is the enemy. Some have no sense of what is appropriate, and they will pay socially for their that lack of common sense. But we all need to keep this rage focused. Directed. Direct it to helping those hurt by this, and punishing those responsible. Keep the rage in check - aim it not at each other, no matter how tempting, or justifiable it is. We are AMERICANS. We give to the world. Now is when we show the world that we will keep on giving. (And by making painful examples of those puke-balls and their hosts/backers). Now is when we show the world we really believe all those things we thump our chests about - those are not just words.

Our guild members are our brothers. Are some smarter than others? You bet. Do some have the common sense to see and understand grief, and know not to blow iodine on it? Of course. But even those that did not have the common sense to not kick the wounded lion, are still members. Brainless - perhaps, tactless - most certainly. But they are not the people that obliterated our loved ones.

We got to remember this guys. We have many of differences, many of fundamentally different political beliefs and ideologies. Normally, it is our policy to keep them off the board. Hell guys, we usually have a hard enough time keeping the peace when we are talking purely in virtual terms - frostburns, stones, hydrolisks, and orc grunts cause problems as it is. When we introduce the nitro of political/relgious beliefs into the situation, an explosion is inevitable.

This is my fault. I was the first person to post the subject on the boards, being too shocked and numbskulled to heed my OWN words - keep political stuff off the board. Was it inevitable given the scope of this? Perhaps. But I should have been more responsible than to have been the on to introduce it. For that I am sorry.

I am realistic. I know that this is huge, and topics will come up. But in this, lets all try to step back a few paces. I know it's hard. I am in this too. My emotions are running the same marathon as the rest of you. Lets step back, focus that anger to helping, and to paying back those that deserve it. Lets not focus it on each other. It's hard, I know it is - GOD I know. Be we are DETH. We are the best. And we stand together. Comrades. Our foes are a legion - if we battle each other, we cannot hope to face them. This goes not just for the guild, but for America and her allies as well.
