Rant Mode: ON

An open letter to Mythic:

Because of the negative changes caused by the most current patch, midgard's realm v.s. realm has been annihilated the Galahad server. The free /levels 30's did not provide an enticement, bringing people to our realm as anticipated - it merely allowed an easy escape from it. If you will look at the realm point totals for Galahad, (my server), you will see that midgard has collapsed since the 1.62 patch went live. (Also, please note, I play a buff botted thane, and a shamen, so none of my characters performance were directly "damaged" by your last patch). But you have crushed what was previously a balanced server, the realm point and population totals paint a clear picture of this.

For most of my time spent playing this game, midgard galahad was a punching bag for our opponents, but we stuck things out, hoping for improvements. Eventually, the server balanced out, with less than a 1 million rp difference between us, the albs, and the hibs. This continued for about 7 months time. Now, the dynamics of the patch have crushed the realm as a whole, as you can see from the realm point listed on the Camelot herald. You will also note, that this phenomenon is not restricted to Galahad, but has become an issue on most of the other servers, with the exception if Isealt, which has always enjoyed a great abundance of midgardian defenders.

These drastic changes are partially due to the effectiveness of the Midgard realm being diminished, and partially due to moral of the players being crushed. The general consensus amongst midgard players, is that every effective class we enjoy will be damaged in some way by Mythic eventually. This is generally assumed - correctly I might add, when Mythic's history in relation to midgard is taken into account - that the remaining effective realm v.s. realm classes will be damaged in some fundamental way at the request of our competitors in other realms. Thus, beyond the battleground vacationers, very little of the midgard core server base wants to pursue any of the classes that have had "the black spot" placed on them, branding them as overpowered, fearing Mythic will inflict damage to the class after considerable time investment by the player. I for one, do not blame them. I can honestly say, fear of your company damaging (or fundamentally altering) my character has stopped me from playing a berserker, shadow blade, savage, or bone dancer completely. It's not a lack of curiosity, or a lack of interest, it is quite simply that I, along with a significant portion of midgard, believed that you were going to damage these classes in some fundamental way down the road. As we can see, our concerns were proven right in patch 1.62. I find myself exceedingly pleased that I did not invest time in any of the aforementioned classes, and I believe wholeheartedly that your company will go on to damage the bone dancer and savage class in some way.

So, you are not see an exodus the numerous /level 30 midgard realms you have enabled. You are seeing a migration from them.

In light of this policy Mythic has on damaging one's characters (at the request of their opponents it would appear), I am afraid that I can no longer support your company with my time or my money. I have stayed with my character and my realm as long as I could manage. I have played a mediocre class with little complaint, and nowhere near the attention that already successful classes were shown from the development team. (For example, the enhancements to the already mighty enchanter class, while hybrids in general continue to perform very poorly seemed more akin to a "sarcastic joke" patch notes placed on the VN boards as a quip, not a reality). But, your company has damaged not me, and not any characters that I play, but you damaged my realm's moral in, from my perspective, unrecoverable ways. Your actions have driven away my guild and my friends, and with those elements to no longer hold me here, I cannot justify the monthly fees for your product.

Thank you for the hours of enjoyment your game provided me in the past, I will not begrudge what your company did right. However, this most recent round of changes has taken away my enjoyment of your product to the point that I can no longer patronize your business. I will not be returning for Trials of Atlantis, or Imperator, because I believe these same issues will become problems again in your other products.

Thank you for your time, and farewell.
