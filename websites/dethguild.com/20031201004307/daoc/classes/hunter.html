A huntress takes aim.
A Hunter is a mix between pet class, archer, and melee class. A hunter can use their stealth abilities to become invisible, attack from range with their bows, and melee effectively with the assistance of their pets. Primarily loners, hunters work best in solo encounters where they can damage a target from range, and then finish it off with the help of their pet in melee. Hunter make excellent scouts, but have little to offer a group besides recon.
