A Bone Dancer ready to command the dead.
A bone dancer is a high survivability, low damage output caster. He can summon up to 4 pets, and can choose between healing, buffing, melee and ranged attacking servants. Additionally, the Bone Dancer has a few insta-cast spells and weak dots, which help make it very durable. This class excells in 1:1 combat situations, but are hampered by weak area effect and direct damage spells. "BD" are very hard to kill in small enguagements, and very weak in large scale RvR.
