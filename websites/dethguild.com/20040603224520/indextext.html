December 10, 2003:

Updates:

Due to disastrously low sales, The trials of Atlantis expansion pack has been reduced to $9.99 (US) in a desperate attempt to move copies. According to the gamestats web site, less than 40% of the dwindling DAoC subscription base purchased the "Trials of Atlantis" expansion pack, as opposed to 89% of the subscription base who activated Mythic's "Shrouded Isles" expansion. Additionally, mass cancellations of long time subscribers, and a grand exodus from most Midgard servers has prompted three frantic letters from Mythic's president, Mark Jacobs, pleading with the remaining subscribers to continue to maintain their monthly payments. Filled with the typical Mythic "carrot waving" and promises that the game will get better if you just pay them some more, it is in no way a departure from what we have come to expect from this cooperation. If you would like to see Mark Jacobs prostrate himself before the remaining player base, head to the Herald, we won't be fouling up this web site with his begging.

Updates:

The Deth guild has officially closed it's doors in Dark Age of Camelot. Thanks for the fun all - the people I shared so much time with made this atrocity of a game fun.
