Dark Age of Camelot is an exciting, vibrant online game. Its high-definition 3D graphical engine presents you with a world that is so realistic, you almost feel like you are living your character's adventures. You'll start out with a low-level character in Camelot, but there's so much to do! As you quest, adventure, fight monsters, meet other players, make friends and enemies - eventually you'll grow in power enough to be able to go out and defend your Realm from your enemies.

Three Realms

The world of Dark Age of Camelot is comprised of three Realms at war - Hibernia, Midgard, and Albion. The struggle between the Realms resulted from the power vacuum created by King Arthur's death. Each Realm is geographically diverse from one another and follow a specific mythological tradition. Albion is the land of King Arthur's heirs. Hibernia is based on Irish Celtic legends, with a dash of high fantasy. Midgard follows the Viking Norse sagas and legends.

Many Classes and Races

You can choose to be one of 15 races and over 30 classes in Dark Age of Camelot. With this many choices, there sure to be one to meet your particular playing style.

Realm Vs. Realm Combat

A cornerstone of Dark Age of Camelot's gaming experience is Realm vs. Realm combat. This means that everyone in your Realm is you friend, and you are safe from enemy players as long as you are in you home realm - but if you venture out to a frontier, you'll be able to fight other enemy-Realm players. If you don't want to fight other players, you simply don't go out to the frontier. However, RvR combat in Dark Age of Camelot is fun and exciting - you can even build siege equipment and storm enemy fortresses!

3D Graphics

Dark Age of Camelot is presented to you in a rich 3D graphics engine that renders players, monsters, terrain, dungeons, and bodies of water using state-of-the-art graphics techniques. Everything looks like it is almost real, allowing you to become immersed in the ongoing struggle for supremacy among the Realms.

Constant Updates

The world of Dark Age of Camelot is always being updated and enhanced. Dungeons are added, new monster encounters and quests are scripted, new armor and weapons are created - in fact, so much new content goes into the game on a regular basis that it is impossible to list it all here.

Other features

  • Thousands of spells - most of the classes in the game have at least some ability to cast spells, with hundreds of unique graphical spell effects.
  • Melee classes get Combat Styles - special moves that you can use to do extra damage in specific situations.
  • Immersive quests that lead your character through the story of their Realm, as well as provide equipment, experience, and insight into the conflict.
  • Realistic weather fronts actually move across the world, producing rain and snow where appropriate.
  • An extensive Guild system allows you to group with your friends, make alliances, choose your symbol, and raise your banner over enemy territory.
  • Over 100 unique skills and abilities.
  • Thousands of objects to be found - treasure in the game includes armor, weapons, and magical items of all types.
  • Special servers with specific rule-sets to provide players who wish a different type of experience while playing Dark Age of Camelot. Currently there are hardcore Player vs. Player combat and Role-playing servers as well as the standard servers.
