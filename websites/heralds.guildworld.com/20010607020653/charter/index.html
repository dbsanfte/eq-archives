Guild Raid Loot Rules
All dungeon raids are organized on loot principles of NEED and PRESENCE. Rare loot is distributed on the follwing principles:
1) If an item is class(es)-specific, those classes present are the only ones who can roll for an item.
2) All groups present at a Guild Raid can share in rare loot, but you must be at THAT LOCATION (e.g., if a rare drop is found on the third floor of Dalnir, you must be on the third floor - not the second - to roll.)
3) Any friends joining us on guild raids must agree to loot sharing rules.
4) Non-rare but good loot is rolled for within the group that found it (e.g., a charred tulwar), unless a guild member states a NEED.
5) Winners of previous rolls sit out future rolls until everyone has won a rare loot.
6) Loot distribution issues can be decided on-site by the highest-ranking guild officer.
Current rankings: Lyzandia, Chany, Sheae, Ihnzo, Lyzza, Forged
7) The guild can agree before a raid that they are trying to get an item for an absent member. But this must be agreed upon before the raid.

Guild History: Chaos threatens the stability of our world as evil races prosper and gain footholds in the lands of Norrath. Crushbone orcs invade the very limits of Faydwer, the DeathFist and RunnyEye threaten the human cities in Antonica, skeleton armies amass in the Plains of Karana, the good Gnomes have rampaging minotaurs on their doorstep, the Barbarians of Halas are cut off and isolated from their kin, and even the stout Dwarves find marauding ogres and the undead stalking their mountainous land. Sadly, efforts to restore order are hampered by deep suspicions between the various races of Norrath, and anarchy prospers instead of united effort. From the humblest beginnings, a new force has emerged that offers hope and unity amid this climate of suspicion and mistrust. Born from a vision by a young half-breed girl of a world where races work together instead of bickering, come the Heralds of Destiny, a guild dedicated to truth and knowledge. Their members draw not from the ranks of the elders and nobles, but from the common people, unified by a passion to eradicate deception and deceit. Entry into this organization is predicated on courage and the demonstration of a desire to help others. The Heralds seek out information on their common enemies, such as necromancers, shadow knights, orcs, gnolls and goblins, and endeavor to share knowledge widely so that the good citizens of Norrath may profit. Their members are on a quest – a quest for self-knowledge and awareness – and they will scour the land seeking for clues to unlock the destiny written by the Fates. The Heralds of Destiny are a guild for the common good, and they strive to remain democratic and non-elitist. The worship of all deities except those that are evil is tolerated. Members are encouraged to gain useful trade skills so that they may share the fruits of their labor with other members. Adventures are gladly embarked upon, and the Heralds travel widely, spreading their message of unity and cooperation to the four corners of the known world. They even seek to find lands that are yet unknown. All good races are encouraged to cooperate, and the Heralds even seek to covert some trolls, ogres,dark elves and even Iksar to their cause. Search within ye to see if you have the qualities to gain entry into the Heralds. If ye do so, your life will change forever, and you must put all pettiness and personal ambition behind ye. Yet the rewards of seeing others thrive due to your efforts are great. Long live the good citizens of Norrath! Long live the Heralds of Destiny!
