Member Conduct
  1. No Begging. You may ask for assistance in leveling, items, teleports, and so on. If the member has time to help, I'm sure that that member will assist you. Note; Teleports are a very nice means of transportation. All members should offer a small donation for that service....if it is turned down then its the porter's choice. Remember, we've all walked across Norrath a few times.
  2. Language. Please use common sense while in guild, ooc, shouts, and say modes. There are people of all ages playing in the game. We should keep our tongues in check at all times. Remember, you can always go to tells if you wish to use such language.
  3. Kill Steal. Will not be tolerated. We are not desperate enough for experience to take it from others.
  4. Skills. Many of us work hard in our skills. We've put our own money into that skill, therefore we should be making any item at cost to Guild Members only. Example: If a banded helm cost 3p to make, you pay 3p for it. In other words, you pay for the cost.
  5. Loot Items. If an item is looted as a guild group we will offer it to the guild on a first come basis as a gift. A gift is a gift. When you are finished with it, you will offer it back to whoever gave it to you or pass it to a fellow member as a gift. If you loot an item in a non-guild group, these can be sold to fellow members, but at a lower selling price. We looted it. Example: Exe Hood sells for 200p. If a guild member would want one you should sell it to them for half.
  6. Divided We Fall, United We Stand. If you are in a group with fellow brothers and sisters we: Die together or Run together. Above all we Do Not Bail Out on our fellow members.
  7. Verant. Verant has rules. I expect everyone to adhere to the rules of Verant.
  8. Web Site. Everyone must visit the site regularly for updates and upcoming guild events.
  9. Email. You should check your email regularly. Upcoming events and guild matters will be done via email and web site.
  10. Training. We do not train other people. We ask for help or make sure that your path is clear.
  11. Levels. As you make a new level, you must notify Draconi ASAP by tell or by email. (to update the site)
  12. Anonymous. You may use Roleplay at all times. Noone is to use Anonymous for any reason. If your proud of the Holy Redemption, then you should be able to show it at all times. Roleplay makes you Anonymous, hiding your class and level, but you still show up on a /who all "Holy Redemption".
  13. Behavior. There will be zero tolerance for incorrect behavior in the Holy Redemption. One warning is all that will be given at any time.
  14. Raids. Raids are set up for acquiring items, quests, and guild company. EXP: If you plan on obtaining experience from a raid...don't. Remember, the first part of this statement. Loot items are broke into this: If a said class can use and is present, those in that class shall roll on it. If no class is present or the item is not needed, then everyone present rolls.
We are a unified force in Norrath. Therefore we need to adhere to the rules as stated so we become one to battle the evil that resides in Norrath.
May we all live long and happy in the Holy Redemption!!!!!!
