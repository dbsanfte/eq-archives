Member Conduct
  1. Anonymous. No one is to be Anonymous.You may use the feature Role-play at any time you wish.If you wish to use Anonymous,then use /guildremove. Being in Holy Redemption is Honor. Be proud by displaying the guild tag; Holy Redemption behind your name.
  2. Language. In appropriate Language such as; Strong Profanity,Sexual Remarks,Racial and/or De-grading Remarks will not be Tolerated in Guild Chat, Shouts, Says, and OOC.
  3. Behavior. We have Zero Tolerance for in-appropriate behavior in the Holy Redemption.
  4. Begging. There is NO Begging. You may ask for assistance in Items,Teleports,and so on.
  5. Kill Steal. Will not be tolerated. We are not desperate enough for experience to take it from others.
  6. Loot Items. Need Before Greed. Loot looted by the guild, will be rolled on by those in Attendence and those who can use.
  7. Divided We Fall, United We Stand. If you are in a group with fellow brothers and sisters we: Die together or Run together. Above all we Do Not Bail Out on our fellow members.
  8. Verant. Verant has rules. I expect everyone to adhere to the rules of Verant.
  9. Web Site. Everyone must visit the site regularly for updates and upcoming guild events.
  10. Email. You should check your email regularly. Upcoming events and guild matters will be done via email and web site.
  11. Training. We do not train other people. We ask for help or make sure that your path is clear.
  12. Levels. As you make a new level, you must notify Tyberias ASAP by tell or by email. (to update the site)
  13. Raids. Raids are set up for acquiring items, quests, and guild company. EXP: If you plan on obtaining experience from a raid...don't. Remember, the first part of this statement. Loot items are broke into this: If a said class can use and is present, those in that class shall roll on it. If no class is present or the item is not needed, then everyone present rolls.
  14. 3 Strikes. Once any member has recieved 3 strikes for volating our policies, he or she will be remove from Holy Redemption.
We are a Unified Force in Norrath. Therefore we need to adhere to the rules as stated so we become one to battle the evil that resides in Norrath.
May we all live long and happy in the Holy Redemption!!!!!!
