Recruiting Policy
  1. Everyone in the guild will assist in recruiting new members. Only officers will invite once the policies have been met.
  2. The character must be 30th level or higher. Note: This is for new recruits only. If you are a current member you may have 1 other character in the guild of any level.
  3. The recruit must group with a currnet member at least 3 times for no less than 1 hour each.
  4. The recruit must visit the web site, sign the guest book, as well as post a Hello welcome on the message board.
    Officers are responsible for getting recruit's Name (first and last), Email (and whether or not Draconi can post it on the site), Class, Race, Level, and any Trade Skills that they practice.

    Officers, please make sure an email is sent to Tyberias or Draconi as soon as possible to add the new members to our website.

    Once the new recruit has followed the policy, he or she will become a member of the Holy Redemption.

    We in the Holy Redemption strive for Quality and Quantity in members.
