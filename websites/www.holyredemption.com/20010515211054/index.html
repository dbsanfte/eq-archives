Welcome fellow traveler.
We in the Holy Redemption strive for Unity in all of Norrath. We feel that Good and Evil resides in everyone, therefore we in the Holy Redemption do not hold Race, Class, or Beliefs against anyone in Norrath. Our motto "United We Stand, Divided We Fall" shall ring throughout all of Norrath. Evil shall tremble as our battlecry is heard.
We strive for honesty and trust in one another. We do not look at one's faults, but at one's accomplishments. We live each day for Unity and Integrity in the Holy Redemption. We strive for Quality not Quantity within the guild. Therefore we strive not to better one's self, but that of the entire guild.
We stand as a unified force in Norrath, with the faces of evil cringing at our presence. We all have faced our innermost fears and have emerged victorious, and seek Peace among all Norrathians. Only a Unified Team will emerge victorious over the Evil that resides in Norrath.
Only those who strive for Honor, Justice, and Peace will find tranquility within the Holy Redemption.
As a member of Holy Redemption, you'll find the overwhelming feeling of being part of a force that truely stands Unified against the Evil of Norrath.

May your journeys be safe and fun.
