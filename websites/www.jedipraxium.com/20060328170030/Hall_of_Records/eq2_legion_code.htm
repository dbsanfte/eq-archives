Everquest II Legion Code
Article I - Authority of the Code


Section 1. The Code of the EverQuest 2 Legion of the Jedi Praxium shall govern the affairs of the EverQuest 2 Legion, including but not limited to the affairs of the EverQuest 2 Legion Council, the EverQuest 2 Legion Commander, EverQuest 2 Legion Officers, and/or the conduct of all Jedi and Jedi Apprentices in the EverQuest 2 Legion.

Section 2. The Code of the Jedi Praxium and the Charter of the Jedi Praxium are hereby recognized as the supreme authority and the Assembly of Jedi as the supreme ruling body. The dictates of the Code of the Jedi Praxium, Charter of the Jedi Praxium, and Assembly of Jedi will be honored and followed by the EverQuest 2 Legion and its members. Nothing in this Code shall be deemed to limit or supercede the authority of the Code of the Jedi Praxium, Charter of the Jedi Praxium, or the Assembly of Jedi.


Article II - Organization and Structure of the EverQuest 2 Legion


Section 1. EverQuest 2 Legion Council: The EverQuest 2 Legion Council (hereinafter "Legion Council") shall be the ruling body of the EverQuest 2 Legion (hereinafter "Legion"). The Legion Council shall have the authority to take those actions it deems necessary for the betterment of the Legion.

Section 2. Jedi Senate: In order to foster communications and ensure harmony between the Jedi Praxium (hereinafter "Guild") and the Legion, the Jedi Senate (hereinafter "Senate") was established in the Code of the Jedi Praxium to address and oversee those issues of special concern to both the Guild and the Legion. The Senate has the enumerated powers described in the Code of the Jedi Praxium, and in addition, those additional administrative powers necessary to conduct the affairs of the Senate. Since representatives of this Legion shall serve on the Senate, the Senate is hereby adopted as a governing body of the EverQuest 2 Legion.

Section 3. EverQuest 2 Legion Commander: The EverQuest 2 Legion Commander (hereinafter "Legion Commander") shall be the chief executive officer of the Legion. The Legion Commander shall have the enumerated powers described below, and in addition, those additional administrative powers necessary to conduct the affairs of the Legion.


Article III - EverQuest 2 Legion Council


Section 1. All members of the Legion shall have a seat on the Legion Council. Each active Jedi Legionnaire shall have one vote in the Legion Council, and an inactive Jedi Legionnaire shall have no voting rights in the Legion Council.

Section 2. An active Jedi Legionnaire may vote on a matter in absentia or by proxy. In either event, the Legion Commander must be informed in writing by the Jedi Legionnaire in advance.

Section 3. Legion Council meetings shall be open to all members of the Guild unless necessity dictates otherwise.

Section 4. The Legion Council shall meet at least once each month and notice shall be given at least24 hours in advance. Where it is at all possible, notice of a meeting shall be placed in the Praxium Post.

Section 5. The Legion Council meetings shall be conducted in keeping with parliamentary procedures.

Section 6. The Legion Commander shall preside over the Legion Council. In his or her absence the Executive Officer shall preside over the Legion Council.

Section 7. The Legion Council shall take official action by a majority vote of the entire Legion Council. In the case of a tie vote, the Legion Commander shall have the tie-breaking vote.

Section 8. All official actions shall be published.

Section 9. The Legion Council may take unofficial action on a majority vote of the Jedi Legionnaires present at a Legion Council meeting. Unofficial actions shall have no binding effect unless it is made official at latter time by an official action of the Legion Council except as provided in Subsection A & B below.

Subsection A. The Legion Council by an unofficial action may accept new members.

Subsection B. The Legion Council by unofficial action may adopt treaties if the same treaty is later adopted by an official action of the Senate.

Section 12. The Legion Council shall elect one person from its membership to serve in the Jedi. The Legion Council may only remove a Senator from office upon a two-thirds vote of the Legion Council. Senatorial elections will be held every 6months.

Section 13. The Legion Council shall elect a Legion Commander by a majority vote of entire Legion Council. The Legion Commander may only be removed from office upon a two-thirds vote of the Legion Council. Legion Commander elections will be held every 6 months.

Section 14. The Legion Council may reconsider any action taken by the Legion Commander or any Legion Officer. For an action to be reconsidered, five Jedi Legionnaires must inform the Legion Commander in writing that they want the matter reconsidered. The Legion Commander shall then forward the request for reconsideration to the entire Legion. If a majority of the active Legion membership votes in favor of reconsideration, the matter will be shall be reconsidered and the action in question shall be stayed.


Article IV - Legion Commander


Section 1. The Legion Commander shall maintain a Legion membership roster. This roster shall at least list the active and inactive Legion members and Legion Officers. The Legion Commander shall submit this membership roster to the Guild Master on or around the 1st and 15th of each month. (The Guild Master may relax or drop this requirement where necessity dictates.)

Section 2. The Legion Commander and the Guild Master shall jointly oversee the Legion website.

Section 3. The Legion Commander shall ensure that Legion's Code, Charter, rules, and practices are respected and upheld.

Section 4. The Legion Commander may appoint such Officers, with the advice and consent of the Senate, as he or she deems to be necessary and desirable. These Officers serve at the will of the Legion Commander.

Section 5. The Legion Commander may establish such committees as deemed to be necessary and desirable. The purposes and terms of such committees and their members shall be specified at the time of their establishment.

Section 6. The Legion Commander shall informally investigate Legion disciplinary matters. In doing so, he or she shall act in a fair and equitable manner. If for some reason the matter cannot informally be resolved, Legion Commander shall forward the matter to the Guild Master or the Senate for further investigation and resolution. In any case the Legion Commander shall appraise the Guild Master and the Senate via email of such disciplinary matters.

Section 7. Upon a showing of just cause, the Legion Commander may temporarily suspend a Jedi from the Legion until the matter can be reviewed and considered by the Senate at the next regular meeting. The showing of just cause must be explained to the Guild Master and the Senate. In addition, the Guild Master must witness, either in person or in writing, the Legion Commander's execution of power including the explanation of offenses to the offending party. The Legion Commander may take a lesser disciplinary measure, but the same procedures as outlined above must be followed.

Section 8. In cases of emergencies, the Legion Commander may take such temporary actions as are necessary for the good of the Legion AND the Guild. However, the matter must be immediately reported to the Guild Master, Executive Officer and the Legion. If necessary the Guild Master shall also report the matter to the Senate and/or Assembly of Jedi. The Legion Commander in his or her report shall succinctly state the reasons for the actions.

Section 9. Where the Legion Commander is temporarily unable to perform his or her duties, the Executive Officer shall carry out those duties.

Section 10. Where the Legion Commander has resigned or is permanently unable to perform his other duties, the Executive Officer will temporarily discharge those duties while the process to nominate and elect a new Legion Commander is under taken.


Article V - Duties of Jedi


Section 1. Jedi Legionnaires shall uphold all rules, regulations, practices, and customs of the Legion and the Guild.

Section 2. All Jedi shall act in a manner befitting the honor and dignity of this Guild and the Jedi tradition. A Jedi shall not be rude, boastful, mean spirited, or bad tempered.

Section 3. All Jedi shall fill out and update their membership forms.

Section 4. A person who has a Jedi character may have characters in other guilds.

Section 5. A person shall never use his or her Jedi or non-Jedi characters to cause SERIOUS HARM to another Jedi or the Guild or a Legion.


Article VI - Ranks & Titles


Section 1. The Senate shall oversee the awarding of ranks to ensure the integrity of the ranking system. Further, the Senate may veto the awarding of any rank to ensure the integrity of the ranking system.

Section 2. The Legion ranking system shall be set out and explained in the EverQuest 2 Legion Edict on Rank.

Section 4. The Legion Commander may bestow titles upon Legion Officers to reflect their positions.

Section 5. By official action, the Legion Council, with the consent of the Assembly of Jedi or the Senate, may bestow special titles upon Jedi for great deeds or service to the Legion.


Article VII - Jedi Apprentices


Section 1. A Jedi may sponsor up to three persons at one time for membership. An Officer may sponsor more than three persons temporarily until a permanent sponsor can be found.

Section 2. All persons who have turned in an application shall be known as a Jedi Apprentice.

Section 3. Before a Jedi Apprentice is considered, he or she needs a sponsor and three recommendations. One of the recommendations must be from an Officer.

Section 4. A Jedi Apprentice's application may be considered and voted on by Guild or the Legion. A Jedi Apprentice can apply for membership directly to the Guild or through the Legion.

Section 5. A Jedi Apprentice who is granted membership by the Legion also becomes a member of the Guild.

Section 6. The Senate may waive portions of this Article where it would create a hardship for the Legion.
