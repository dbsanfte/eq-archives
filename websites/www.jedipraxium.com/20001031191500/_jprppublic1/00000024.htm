Hail to the heros--Kanlain and Gothryd

From: Keela
Date: 1/31/00
Time: 12:17:47 PM
Remote Name: 198.99.113.173

Comments

The other evening I met a most well spoken young druid at the gates of the city of Freeport. We had a pleasant conversation, but he had to run off to fight evil in that dire labyrinth known as Befallen. I stayed at the city gate a while, helping Fariah as he assisted those less fortunate by casting useful enchantments on them. Soon, exhausted by the day's activity, I dragged myself within the city walls and slept.

At the darkest hour of night I awakened suddenly. In spite of the reassuringly rhythmic sound of a blunt object hitting flesh far in the distance, I felt that something was wrong.* I crept to the gate again and there was the druid, just as polite and helpful, but shivering without his clothing and equipment. I asked him about his situation, and found that he had fallen down a well in Befallen, and had no idea how to go about attempting to recover his corpse. He was willing to try it alone, but knowing the area I suggested he get help. There are some very dangerous things at the bottom of that hole, and the location isn't easy to get to, being behind several locked doors.

I asked my fellow Jedi for advice on how long one might expect a corpse to safely remain in such a place, and was overjoyed when several of them offered their services as well. Kanlain, Gothryd and I set out, confident that together we could do what needed to be done. Unfortunately I was struck down by Zonepunt soon after we began our journey, and was unable to continue. With my last breath I gasped out the name of the young druid, in the hope that the others might be able to help him. As I spiraled down into darkness, I was able to do nothing more for my friends than pray for their safety.

By the time I regained consciousness Kanlain and Gothryd had helped the young fellow to recover his corpse! I hope some day to discover the details of their journey, but for now I can only raise a shout for these brave and generous souls, who risked their lives to help someone they had never met.

Three cheers for Kanlain and Gothryd!

Keela

*As a cleric, I often find the sounds of battle reassuring. With a fight going on, I know exactly where my friends and enemies are. I can meditate through nearly anything, in fact one time.... but that is another story :)
