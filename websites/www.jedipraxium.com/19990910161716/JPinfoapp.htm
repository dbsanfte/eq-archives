Greetings!

    In real terms, the Jedi Praxium is a closely-knit association of gamers who believe in the Jedi ideals and are committed to carry those ideals with them into various gaming forums. Originally, the Jedi Praxium was started in 1995 as a Guild of Neverwinter Nights, an America Online RPG game. As a Guild of NWN, the JP gained great respect and grew to around 120 members. Of course, with the retirement of NWN, we lost some of our membership just as America Online lost many thousands of customers. Currently, we have over 85 members who are committed to expanding the JP into the various gaming forums which they adventure in. We have five established Legions in EverQuest, The Realm, Ultima Online, Panterra (formerly Lords of Empyria), and MicroSoft's Zone.  And of course, our members enjoy other games like Baldur's Gate, Space Merchant, and Diablo.

     In RP terms, the Jedi Praxium is an order of noble warriors who know the mystical power of the Force. We, Jedi, use the power of the Force to attain our objectives and goals in the various realms throughout the many planes. Specifically, we are dedicated to the endless search for knowledge, truth, and enlightenment. We are explorers, seekers of justice, defenders of the weak, champions of the Light, and ever vigil foes of the Dark Side.  After three and a half years, the Jedi Praxium has developed a membership with diverse interests. Some Jedi enjoy role-playing, others enjoy adventuring and exploring, and still others just love a good fight. However, all our members enjoy the comradery and humor of their Jedi brothers and sisters. Many friendships run deep in the JP, and we are proud of that. The Jedi Praxium is probably more about friendships than anything. Oh yes, and to be a Jedi, you must have a sense of humor and be a bit of a wisenhimer. And, unfortunately Jedi are constantly playing pranks on there unsuspecting Guild Master (even though they now that I could wipe the floor wif'em).

     We have no class or race restriction because we feel the Force can run strong in anyone. However, a Jedi must be of dedicated to the light side of the Force and NON-EVIL alignment.  All Jedi must adhere to our Code of Honor. As Jedi we stand against tyrrany, and so the Jedi Praxium is democratically structured and run. Our system of government is a bit complicated, so I won't try and explain it. But rest assured, all Jedi have a equal voice in the Assembly of Jedi - our supreme body.  We put out a weekly newsletter called the Praxium Post. Its not much but it keeps our membership informed of important issues and events. There are Guild meetings every week and all members are invited to attend.

     The following is a general description of the Jedi application process. First, you will need a Jedi to sponsor you. Second, you must fill out and return the Jedi application to me (Warderick@aol.com) and your sponsor.  Once, the application is recieved, you will informally and briefly meet with me (Warderick@aol.com) or another Officer just so we can get to know you.  Then you will need to adventure with your sponsor some. Your sponsor will help you get the 3 Jedi recommendations that are required before your application can be voted on. One of the recommendations must be from an Officer. This process can take anywhere from one week to the maximum time of four weeks. At your request we will extend the application process. Once you have the required recommendations, we will inform your sponsor and your application will be voted on. Getting recommendations isn't a difficult thing. Its just a way to ensure that you get to know other Jedi and we get to know you. Also, we like to see other Jedi vouch for our apprentices' excellent character and demeanor. If you are having problems getting recommendations, please contact myself and/or your Legion Commander and we will help you out. Of course, the application process will vary a little from Legion to Legion. And each Legion's application may be a bit different. Below you will find the general application form.

     Well, I think that should give you something to think about. If you are interested in joining, just fill out the application and return it to myself and your sponsor. If you have any questions, please email me. I'm here to help so please don't hesitate to ask!

May the Force be with You,

Warderick
