EQ Gathering 2002

Between August 1st and August 13th, 2002, several members of Praxium attended the gathering (a real life meeting) to meet, raise a pint and discuss whatever came to mind. Hopefully, we can find a spot for next year, and a volunteer to host it. All are welcome. 

This page has the pictures from "The Gathering 2002", hosted by Kanlain and his wife, Ceelani. 

We thank them both for their efforts in getting everything together for our guild mates to meet in real life. I have heard many stories that happened over the few days that they were all together, hopefully they will share with those who could not attend.
