The Celestial Vortex

 

Of Beginnings

    Long ago the FORCE began to pull at an adventurer by the name of Warderick. He left his home and country in the True World to follow a yearning he did not understand. He traveled across the Trackless Sea and landed on the Sword Coast near Waterdeep. After some time, Warderick took up residence in the City of Neverwinter where the FORCE was STRONG. Yet, Warderick still wasn't at peace nor did he understand the nature of his yearning. He began to fill much of his time helping other young adventurers and exploring the evil infested areas near Neverwinter.

    On a fortuitous day Warderick met brash young adventurer by the name of Wolf Jedi at Mataga's. Wolf and Warderick became fast friends. Then one day Wolf told Warderick about the FORCE and how it had drawn him to Neverwinter. Hearing Wolf's story, Warderick stood amazed as this is why he had come to Neverwinter. Wolf explained the ways of the FORCE to Warderick or at least Wolf told him what he understood it to be. As they talked, they agreed that Neverwinter wasn't the focal point of the FORCE. Warderick felt the most intense energy when he was exploring the Woods which he loved to do. So, Wolf and Warderick decided they would search the Woods for more answers.

 

Founding of the Keep & Exploration of the Catacombs

    After exploring the Woods between Nightsedge and Longsaddle for some weeks, they found a mystical entrance to some Catacombs in the Southeastern portion of the woods. At the entrance to the Catacombs the first "Jedi Camp" was established.  Others who had felt the power of the Force and migrated to Neverwinter like Wolf and Warderick began to mysteriously show up at the Jedi Camp almost immediately. They would explain how the FORCE drew them to Neverwinter, and they would tell of how they saw the Jedi Camp in a dream and struck out to find it. In the little Jedi Camp Uncle Ira, Hophal, Garlord, Ontharie,  Duke Tomas, Rakia, Sekitch, Baithobbit, Lok4slvr, Cpntherfan, and EW1211 along with Wolf Jedi and Warderick became the first residents. The ranks of the Jedi Camp grew quickly and it was decided a wall and permanent buildings should be built for defensive purposes. After the renovation the camp was renamed the "Jedi Keep" with its center being the entrance to the Oouter Catacombs. In time the noble defenders of the Jedi Keep came to be known as "Jedi Knights" by the citizens of Neverwinter.

    Throughout all of this, the exploration of the Outer Catacombs continued. The Outer Catacombs were twisting and complex, but easily mapped. One could feel the crackle of energy emanating in the Outer Catacombs. Ever in search of the source of this great energy, the Jedi searched the Outer Catacombs for answers.

    Finally, a Jedi discovered a very powerful illusion which hid an entrance to what came to be known as the Inner Catacombs. The Inner Catacombs held many mental and physical perils. The pathways of these Catacombs were constantly and mysteriously changing and so mapping was impossible. The Jedi began to understand the Inner Catacombs didn't work on simple linear principles. Instead, they could only be traversed by conquering various mental and physical perils. In time those who finally conquered the challenges of the Inner Catacombs became known as a Jedi Grand Knights. The Jedi Grand Knights are still the guardians of the Inner Catacombs today.

 

The Ancient One

    Far, far underneath the surface in a vast cavernous Chamber, the Ancient One existed. The Ancient One was a tree unlike any other in the world, and possibly as old as Toril itself. The Ancient One emanated its own light and wasn't one type of tree but seemed to have branches of every tree. The origins of the Ancient One were and still remain a mystery to the Jedi. But, its power every Jedi knows and understands as the FORCE. The Ancient One was the focal point of the FORCE on Toril and possibly the focal point of the FORCE in the universe.

    In the presence of the Ancient One, Jedi found the peace and serenity for which they sought. And, the mental powers of a Jedi grew exponentially when in its presence. Unfortunately, while in the trees presence, mental control became extremely difficult and exhausting. Only a few Jedi had the mental strength and stamina to master both the perils of the Inner Catacombs and the side effects of the Ancient One. These few became known as Jedi Masters. The Jedi Masters are the stewards and protectors of the Ancient One and its wondrous powers.

    The Jedi Masters developed a Creed to prepare prepare themselves for entrance into the Chamber of the Ancient One. Although all Jedi know the Creed only a Jedi Master truly understands its meaning.

There is no emotion; there is peace.
There is no ignorance; there is knowledge.
There is no passion; there is serenity.
There is no death; there is only the FORCE.
