I quickly learned the routine... imagine myself a warrior... kill a rat... carve off his whiskers or his skin or his teeth... return to town when my pockets overflowed... rest carefully (always sitting near the fine Militiamen)...

Eventually I had saved up enough to purchase another of those scrolls... I scanned the lists looking for the easy stuff... was rudely turned away by the magician trainer (what I'm not a magician?)... and finally found one I could handle... I went back out to my master...

"Now", he started, "I want you to imagine yourself... well... different."

"Like the blue guy?", I said.

"Well that'll do for now", he said.

I imagined myself dressed in embroidered finery, with blue skin, white hair... greenish bubbles streamed from my hands... and...

"I can't move!", I screamed.

"Look at yourself", he said, "It's okay."

I looked at myself... I was gone... there was just a wall sconce laying on the ground with a torch in it...

"Beautiful", he said, "You did it!"

"Did what?", I asked. "I just see this torch here and I can't move! Tell me this is good! I was trying to look like that blue guy"

"You will... in time... of course do it in front of me and I might be forced to kill you... just trust me on this one... you're safer as a torch"

"Okay", I said. I sat and rested, then stood up and had a drink. Well at least I wasn't TOTALLY helpless. Finally I decided I'd had enough. I started to feel like myself again... then the torch was gone... my feet were back on the ground... and I ran out to the gate to slay another rat...

Well now it's been a while since those days of my youth... I am a (somewhat) powerful enchanter... I can summon a sword and shield to defend me... I can make armor even if I can't seem to wear it... and I even visit my mom from time to time to make some brews to sell to my dear old dad...

Oh and the rats don't bother me anymore... nor the orcs (at least the little ones)... although I do still enjoy blasting their brains to bits and stealing their meagre orc-wages... but best of all I can look like my blue skinned friend any time I want to now... (but not around the birdman)...

But the funny thing is I'm not hardly grown up yet... I have a lot to learn... like never attack really big mean alligators... never attack really fast puppies that love to travel in packs... and never never never sun on the beaches of Oasis without plenty of spectre-screen...
