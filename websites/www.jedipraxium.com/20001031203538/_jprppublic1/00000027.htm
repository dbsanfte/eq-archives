Having served my Goddess Erollisi Marr for most of my young life, I grew up around the Mighty Paladins of North Freeport where I made my home. Training with them when they would allow it and running various errands for all the noble priests and knights in the great City. I grew up under their watchful eyes and soon I was a squire, then a knight myself under the glorious service of Erollisi Marr. During my many years spent there I have seen many things. As I grew older and more experienced in my service as a paladin I can remember one oddity that truly stands out in my mind as exceptionally unusual. It was a clear day and I was in the temple of Marr honing my skills, when I saw of all things a young Ogre squire there within the holy Temple of Marr. At first I was shocked and nearly drew my blade to defend the preist from this creature. As I went to draw my might blade SoulCutter, my hand was stayed by an old and wise priest who was standing near by. "Fear not Sir LyonsBane, for that is Sogrom, a friend of all here in the temple. He is noble of heart and kind in spirit. He has forsaken the ways of his race and dedicated his life to the service of good. Though he worships no Gods, he does not curse them either.Know him as a friend and one to count on in times of need." With those few kind words of reassurance, I relaxed and took an interest in this unusual Ogre. I spent time watching him and asking others in the temple about him. I talked to the many people living in North FreePort, the merchants and other travellers, regarding their opinion of Sogrom Goodsoul. Not a ill word was to be said by all I spoke too. I think took it upon myself to speak with Sogrom and even battle beside him against the vile DeathFist Orcs who plague the East and West commonlands. Many a vile and wicked orc has fallen beneath his blade and even more people have been helped by this kind and noble warrior. From this day forth let it be known....Sogrom Goodsoul is a warrior who is pure of heart and one I am proud to call my friend.
