In Remembrance of Columbia & her Crew

It is a tragedy whenever we loose men and women performing such noble endeavors or those in service to our country.  Yet, it is especially tragic when we loose brave space travelers and their  wondrous ships.  Perhaps it is the very nature of their endeavor that makes such a loss so very sad.  The marvel of space travel encompasses the most noble and admirable characteristics of all humanity.  And it is a loss to all humanity when such a tragedy occurs.

Our support goes to the people at NASA in these difficult.  We demand perfection of them in accomplishing the unthinkable and nearly impossible.  I'm not sure any endeavor is more demanding or difficult.

Our thoughts, prayers, hopes and sentiments go to the family and friends of the Columbia crew.  The crew of Columbia committed the ultimate sacrifice in pursuit of humanity's greatest dream - space travel.
