Section 1.  General Overview of the Jedi Praxium

In real terms, the Jedi Praxium is a closely knit association of gamers who believe in the Jedi ideals and are committed to carrying those ideals with them into various gaming forums. Originally, the Jedi Praxium was started in 1995 as a Guild of Neverwinter Nights, an America Online RPG game. As a Guild of NWN, the JP gained great respect and grew to around 120 members. Of course, with the retirement of NWN by AOL, we lost some of our membership just as America Online lost many thousands of customers.  As of Novermber 2001, we number over 160+ total members and 130+ active members.  We have established Legions in EverQuest, Dark Age of Camelot, and The Realm. And of course, our members enjoy other games like Asheron's Call, Baldur's Gate, Diablo, MicroSoft's Zone, and Ultima Online.  Currently, the most of our membership play either EverQuest or Dark Age of Camelot.

 

In roleplay terms, the Jedi Praxium is an ancient order of noble adventurers who know and cultivate the mystical power of the Force. We use the power of the Force to attain our objectives and goals in the various realms throughout the many planes. Specifically, we are dedicated to the endless search for knowledge, truth, and enlightenment. We are explorers, seekers of justice, defenders of the weak, champions of the Light, and ever vigil foes of the Dark Side.

 

Ok what does all that mean in real terms, you ask? We RP ourselves as the very FIRST Jedi.  We are the ancient and mystical Jedi of incredible powers that mysteriously vanished eons before the times of the era of the Star Wars Trilogy.  We are of an age that is pre-space travel, so we know nothing about space travel.  But,t we know a great deal about dimensional travel (which is better than space travel). And of course we do have Light Sabers. But, in games set in the medieval period, everyone calls them Flaming Swords. I guess one person's Flaming Sword is another person's Light Saber. :) So as Jedi we fit in quite nicely to any time era.

 

After nearly six, the Jedi Praxium has developed a membership with diverse interests. Some Jedi enjoy role-playing, others enjoy adventuring and exploring, and still others just love a good fight. However, all our members enjoy the camaraderie and humor of their Jedi brothers and sisters. Many friendships run deep in the JP, and we are proud of that. The Jedi Praxium is probably more about friendships than anything. Oh yes, and to be a Jedi, you must have a sense of humor and be a bit of a wisenheimer. And, unfortunately Jedi are constantly playing pranks on there unsuspecting Guild Master (even though they now that I could wipe the floor wif'em).

 

Section 2.  Basic Storyline of the Jedi Praxium

It is important we all know about the roots and evolution of our Guild in roleplay terms.  Many members have long developed and maintained a mutual storyline that we adhere to.  In no way does this mean that you cannot create your own storyline.  I only want to overview the Jedi Praxium storyline, as it has been developed through numerous posts.  I ask you to respect our storyline as we will respect the storyline you develop. With that said, it is important you decide how and why you became a Jedi.

 

The Force has existed throughout the galaxy since time began.  Basically, it is a belief in the Force of life and nature, so obviously many people on many planets believe in its power.  For example, the New Age religions currently in vogue share many of the same tenets with the Force, as do the more traditional religions to some extent.  Many Native American religions are also very similar in the belief that all living objects have an innate natural power.  With this in mind, we contend that there were Force believers on the planet of Toril long before space travel in the galaxy.  We further contend that the Force was very strong of the planet of Toril.  (Much as the Sith was strong on Yavin 4, the home planet of Exar Kunn.)  Further, we maintain that there was focal point or vortex of the Force on Toril near the City of Neverwinter.  The mystery behind the Ancient One (focal point of the Force on Toril) is revealed more fully in the Jedi Saga.

 

After the Founding of the Jedi Praxium and the construction of the Jedi Keep, the guild flourished.  However, it became apparent to the Jedi of Neverwinter that the fiber of Toril was unraveling.  On a fateful day the Jedi gathered in the secret underground chamber of the Ancient One.  Together the Jedi used the Force to save their keep and the Ancient One by ascending to the Celestial plane.  From their Jedi learned how to travel the planes for the purpose of telling others our ways and explaining the powers of the Force.

 

Well, that is a brief overview of storyline as it has developed.  We chose it because it seemed the most sensible given the Neverwinter era and with all that happened.  For more information on the Jedi Praxium storyline, please read the Jedi Saga.

 

 

Section 3.  Jedi of the Light

A Jedi must be dedicated to the light side of the Force and of a NON-EVIL alignment.  All Jedi must adhere to our Code of Honor. As Jedi we stand against tyranny, and so the Jedi Praxium is democratically structured and run, and all Jedi have an equal voice in the Assembly of Jedi - our supreme body.

 

(NOTE:  While all members of the Jedi Praxium must be Jedi of the Light, the EverQuest Legion attempts to accommodate all race and class types.  While your evil character cannot receive a Praxium tag, you can receive a Dark Praxium tag.  And characters of the dark races with a neutral god can receive a tag through the special exceptions procedures.  Please see the Jedi Praxium – Dark Praxium Affliation Agreement and the Dark Races Special Exception Procedure in the Hall of Records for more details.)

 

 

Section 4.  Conduct of a Jedi

We are noble adventurers of high intellect who seek serenity and peace through the all embracing and all powerful - FORCE.  With this in mind, members are expected to act in a manner that would bring credit to the individual and to the Guild.  Members are expected to act with purpose and forethought at all times.  Jedi always exercise self-control and restraint because a Jedi's greatest weapon is his or her MIND.

 

A Jedi should conduct him or herself in a most dignified and noble manner.  A Jedi should at all times take the high road.  A Jedi should not engage in trash talking or berating others.  If someone is harassing you, either ignore it or fight them.  A Jedi who lets his ego or temper get the best of him is letting the dark side take over.  Not to be corny but always remember how noble Luke acted when he fought Darth Vader in the presence of the Emperor.  For a more detailed discussion of conduct please read the Jedi Code of Honor.

 

The Jedi Creed and the Jedi Oath both describe what being a True Jedi really means. And so, the goal of each Jedi is to achieve peace, serenity, and knowledge. Always remember a Jedi's greatest enemies are ANGER, HATRED, and FEAR not other players. And a Jedi's strongest ally is the FORCE!

 

 

The Jedi Oath
To act not for personal power or wealth but to seek knowledge and enlightenment.  Never to act from hatred, anger, or, fear but only to act when calm and at peace with the force.  Never to act out of aggression but only in defense.

The Jedi Creed
There is no emotion, there is peace.
There is no ignorance, there is knowledge.
There is no passion, there is serenity.
There is no death, there is the Force.

 

 

Section 5.  Organization Structure - Guild & Legions
The Jedi Praxium's form of organization is a bit complex at first glance.  But, once you see it in practice you will realize its pretty simple.  Technically, our guild is basically a bilateral democracy with a twist.  There is a single overall Guild that we all belong too - the Jedi Praxium.  And there are various Legions that only some members belong to – the EverQuest Legion for example.  Just think of the overall Guild as the equivalent of the U.S. federal government and the Legions as the equivalent of a state government.  While we are all members of the Guild, you can also be a member of several different Legions.

 

Ultimate authority resides in the Guild as a whole (or the Founding or Sovereign Guild as some call it).  Since we are all members of the Guild, it only makes sense that the Guild should have the final say on critical issues when they arise.  However, it is a rarity that the Guild through the Assembly of Jedi can actually take official action.  Normally, the only time a majority of the members of the Guild act is to elect a Guild Master and Senators.

 

Legions are set up in distinctive gaming forums where Jedi desire to establish the JP.  Legions are simply a chapter of the Guild in a game.  And it is possible to belong to several different Legions if you play several games.  Legions exercise a great deal of autonomy in governing themselves.  For example, Legions can elect a Legion Commander; adopt their own bylaws; recruit and induct new members; and set their own requirements for the awarding of ranks.  Further, each new member voted in at the Legion level automatically gains membership in the overall Guild.  If you want to become a member of a Legion, all you need to do is contact the Legion Commander and the Guild Master so you can be added to the Legion roster and mailing list.  Currently, there are established Legions in the EverQuest, The Realm, and MicroSoft's Zone.


Section 5.1.  Code and Charter

The Code of the Jedi Praxium is the Guild's bylaws.  The Code contains the rules under which theGuild is run.  The Charter of the Jedi Praxium is a simple document that sets out the Guild's basic objectives and fundamental principals.  Together these documents act as the Guild' s constitution.  Only the Assembly of Jedi on a majority vote of the entire membership can amend either the Code or the Charter.


Section 5.2.  Guild Governance

There are three branches which govern various aspects of the overall Guild - the Assembly of Jedi, the Senate, and the Office of the Guild Master (including the Officer Corps).  The Assembly is the supreme body in our Guild and the highest authority.  All Jedi belong to the Assembly.  However, since it is extremely difficult to schedule a time when a majority of ALL Jedi can get together, the Jedi Assembly rarely is able to take official action.  Official actions are only taken by an email vote, and normally after a long period of discussion and debate about the issue.  Our Jedi democracy is fair, but its also time consuming.  There are some specific unofficial actions which can be taken - voting in new members and adopting treaties.  Further, the Assembly can reconsider any action of the Senate, Guild Master, Legion Commander, or Officer.

 

The Senate exists primarily to facilitate communication and cooperation between the Guild and the various Legions.  The Senate is made up of elected Guild and Legion officials.  The Senate deals with specific issues that are of concern to both the Guild and the various Legions.  Specifically, the Senate oversees the Legion approval process, the awarding of ranks, and formal complaint hearings.

 

The Guild Master and Officer Corps handle the day to day administration of Guild affairs.  The GM is elected, but Officers are appointed by the Guild Master and approved by the Senate.  The Guild Master can be removed from office only by a vote of the Assembly while Officers serve at the will of the GM.  The Guild Master also serves to ensure that the integrity of the Code, Charter, and practices of the Jedi Praxium are respected amongst the membership and the various Legions.

 

Finally, there is one more honorary branch of the Jedi Praxium – the Council of Jedi Grandmasters.  The Council of Jedi Grandmasters was the original governing body of the Guild.  Today, it only exists as an advisory body.  Elevation to the Council of Jedi Grandmasters by a vote of the Assembly is the one of the greatest honors a Jedi can receive.

 

Below are some notes that further outline the roles of the various governing bodies of the Guild.

Assembly of Jedi - Everyone is a member of the Assembly.  The Assembly decides the big questions.  However, the Senate and the Guild Master handle some specific matters where the size of the Assembly and its lengthy democratic processes would be terribly inefficient.

Primary Duties:
1.  The Assembly is the supreme governing body of the guild.  The problem is its difficult, time consuming, and a pain getting 51 percent of the guild to OK something.  So, the Assembly only acts on the major issues.
2.  The Assembly chooses a GM and Senators.
3.  Only the Assembly can amend the Code or Charter.
4.  The Assembly can take unofficial action in accepting new members and approving treaties.
5.  The Assembly can reconsider and undo anything that the GM, an Officer, or a Legion has done.

Senate - Membership of the Senate is a bit complex.  Membership in the Senate is comprised of the Guild Master, the two elected Guild Senators, the Legion Commanders, and the Legion Senators for each Ratified Legion.  (Legion Commanders gain voting rights when their Legion is Certified.)  The Senate primarily exists to institutionalize communication and cooperation between the Guild and the various Legions.

Primary Duties:
1.  The Senate oversees the Legion approval process.  Each Legion must meet certain objective criteria before a Charter, Certification, or Ratification is granted.  The Senate is the body that judges whether these criteria have been meet.
2.  The Senate gives advice and consent for officers appointed by the GM or an LC. (Normally, the appointments are granted liberally, but the Senate is still a check on appointment powers.)
3.  The Senate can waive the Apprenticeship sponsor and recommendations requirements where such requirements would create a hardship on a Legion because its lack of members.
4.  The Senate can veto the awarding of any ranks if it goes against the ranking system.  The Senate can waive the lower two ranks if a Legion wants to do things a little differently, however, the ranks of Jedi Knight on up must stay intact.

Guild Master - The elected chief executive.  The Guild Master administers the day-to-day affairs of the Guild and presides over the Assembly of Jedi.  The GM is further charged with ensuring the integrity of the Code and the Charter among its members and the various Legions.

Primary Duties:
1.  The Guild Master’s primary responsibility is the day-to-day administration of the Guild and to ensure the Guild and its various Legions communicate and cooperate effectively.
2.  The GM oversees the web site, the Praxium Post, and the Guild roster and mailing list.
3.  The GM is to ensure the Code, Charter, and practices of the Jedi Praxium are respected by its members and the various Legions.
4.  In cases of emergencies, the Guild Master may take the necessary actions for the good of the Guild as provided in Article IV, Section 9 of the Code.
5.  Where problems arise among members, the GM is to initially investigate the matter and attempt to informally resolve the problem as a neutral party before forwarding it on to the Senate.

Council of Jedi Grandmasters - This was the original governing body of the Guild.  Now it only has advisory powers.  The Assembly can elect Jedi to sit on the Council for their extraordinary service to the Guild.  Elevation to the Council by the Assembly is considered the one of the greatest honors in the Guild.  The Council hardly ever meets.

1.  The Council may take official action.  However, its actions are only advisory in nature unless later adopted by the Assembly.

 

Section 5.3. Legion Governance

Chapters of the Guild in different gaming forums are called Legions.  Currently, we have Legions in EverQuest, Dark Age of Camelot and the Realm.  The vast majority of members are in the EverQuest Legion.  Establishing a Legion is a rather complicated process and hard to explain.  But, if you are interested in establishing a Legion you should contact the Guild Master.  The democratic body of each Legion is the Legion Council made up of all members of the Legion.  The chief executive officer is the Legion Commander.  The Legion Commander oversees matters relating to the Legion and has responsibilities and authority, which mirrors that of the Guild Master for the given gaming forum.

 

 

Section 6.  Weekly Meetings

Meetings of the Assembly or Senate are held at 10 pm eastern time on Monday nights. The Assembly meets every week unless a cancellation notice is posted in advance.  Legions are to hold in game meetings once a month.  The time and date of those are posted in advance.  Senate meetings are All Jedi are invited to attend Assembly meetings, and every Jedi gets to vote on matters before the Assembly.  All Jedi are also invited to some attend Senate meetings, but it is asked you keep your comments to a minimum until the end of the Senate meeting when the floor is open.
 

Section 7.  Sponsoring an Apprentice
Any member may sponsor another person for membership.  However, as a sponsor, you must undertake certain duties and responsibilities.  Obviously, you should believe your apprentice would make a good member.  But, there are other responsibilities.  You must post your letter of reference.  You are responsible for helping the apprentice meet other members.  You should aid the apprentice in trying to group with other members.  And you should check the apprentice listing regularly to see how the recommendation process is coming along.  If for some reason, you don't want to sponsor the apprentice or can't undertake the responsibilities sponsorship requires, please notify an officer immediately.

 

Here is what you need to do when you have a prospective recruit that you are interested in sponsoring.  First, send the prospective recruit to www.jedipraxium.org and direct them to link – “About the Jedi Praxium” and Application Form – found on the main page.  Or you can email Warderick the name of the recruit, and he will email the recruit for you.  “About the Jedi Praxium” explains a little about our fine Guild and is a good introductory letter.  The online Application Form must be filled in and submitted before the recruit becomes an apprentice.   Please note that a person can apply to a Legion or to the overall Guild for membership.  It is entirely up to the applicant and the sponsor.

 

After the application is turned in, the Guild Master or Legion Commander or Master of Apprentices will post the application.  (The Master of Apprentices will email you and the recruit that his or her application has been received and they are now a Jedi apprentice if the Master of Apprentices position is filled.)  If you wish to sponsor the apprentice, you post a letter of reference.  You should post the letter of reference in the Jedi Praxium Messaging Chamber in the appropriate private forum for your apprentice.  If under the apprentices thread, there is already a thread started for your Apprentice, then you should post the letter of reference under that thread. If not, then you should create a thread for your Apprentice.

 

It is then the duty of the sponsor to introduce the apprentice to other members.  Each apprentice is tagged into the guild for a 30-day probationary period.  After 30-days the application will be voted on if the apprentice has received the necessary 3 recommendations.  (One recommendation must come from an Officer.) Those who want to recommend an apprentice should inform the Guild Master, sponsor, and appropriate Legion Commander (if applicable).  The probationary period may be extended 2 weeks at the discretion of the Guild Master, Legion Commander or Master of Apprentices.  However, if the apprentice still hasn’t received three recommendations after two weeks, the application will be tabled indefinitely and the apprentice detagged.

 

Section 7.1.  Family Member Policy

In order to ensure domestic tranquility, the Jedi Praxium has a policy specifically for real life family members.  If you sponsor a member of your family, the three recommendations requirement is waived for family members.  However, a family member must still fill out an application form, and a family member must still be voted in at a regular meeting before they become a member.

 

 

Section 7.2.  Apprentice Listing

An up to date listing of the Jedi apprentices can be found in the Messaging Chamber in the Private Forums.  This listing also has an up to date record of recommendations.

 

 

Section 7.3.  Recommendations for Apprentices

Recommendations should be emailed to the Master of Apprentices, Guild Master or Legion Commander.  In addition, recommendations can be posted on the boards under the Apprentices thread underneath the appropriate apprentices name.  For a post recommendation to be valid, you must be a registered user of the boards.  Further, you may recommend an apprentice at a Guild or Legion meeting.



Section 8.  Membership Forms
It is the DUTY of all Jedi to update their membership forms periodically.  You should submit a membership form whenever you create a new character, change your email address, or join the guild, so the Jedi roster can be kept up to date.  The URL for the online membership form is http://www.jedipraxium.org/Membership_Form.htm and it can be found in the Hall of Records.  So please don't forget to keep your membership form updated.

 

 

Section 8.1.  New Character Tag Requests

Whether you are requesting a tag for any of our Legions (e.g EverQuest or Dark Age of Camelot), you must first fill out a Tag Request Form, which is found in various places on the JP site, including Legion pages.  Once you submit a Tag Request Form, tag officers will be notified and you will get a tag.  No tag will be granted unless a Tag Request Form or updated Membership Form has been submitted.

 

 

Section 8.2.  Membership Roster and Mail String
The membership roster and mailing list is available upon request.  The roster is broken down into several different parts.  It is the DUTY of each member to check his or her information when each new roster is published.  So please when a new roster is published check your information over carefully and inform the Guild Master of any changes or corrections.

 

 

Section 8.3.  Member or Character Exit Policy
A member, who withdraws his or her membership or a character from the Guild or a Legion, shall give notice and inform the Guild Master and/or Legion Commander in writing. The member should state the reason why he or she left, and how they would like to be classified on the Guild and Legion rosters and mail strings. A member, who doesn't give notice, may be dropped from the Guild and/or any Legions without notice.

For a member who revokes his or her membership, a determination will then be made whether the member left in good standing and whether any conditions, requirements or restrictions should be placed on his or her membership. The Guild Master shall direct the inquiry and preserve a record if it is determined the Jedi did not leave in good standing or must face certain conditions, requirements or restrictions.

In any Legion of the the Jedi Praxium, a member, who withdraws his primary character from the Legion, may face conditions, requirements or restrictions on his or her Legion membership. Specifically, the member may be restricted from certain mail strings, private forums, calendars or others methods of communication. This is done in order to ensure the security of certain information. This is not done to punish the member nor should it be seen as a punishment by anyone.

 


Section 9.  Grievance Procedure

If you have a complaint or grievance concerning another member or even another player, you should report it to your Legion Commander or the Guild Master.  This report can be done verbally but MUST also be done via email.

 

The Guild Master or Legion Commander will informally investigate all reports of misconduct and make a determination whether or not the matter should be brought before the Senate. If for some reason the matter cannot informally be resolved, Guild Master or Legion Commander shall forward the matter to the Senate  The Guild Master or Legion Commander shall act in a fair and impartial manner when conducting an investigation.  Anyone accused of misconduct will have the right to now the nature of the allegations and the names of the accusers. Only those incidents that are considered major transgressions will be dealt formally. Serious acts of misconduct will be forwarded to the Senate and enforced in accordance with the provisions laid out in the Code of the Jedi Praxium.

 

The Guild Master shall keep archive of all reports of misconduct. Upon a showing of just cause, the Guild Master may temporarily suspend a Jedi until the matter can be considered by the Assembly at the next regular meeting. However, two Officers of the Guild must witness, either in person or in writing, the Guild Master's execution of power.
