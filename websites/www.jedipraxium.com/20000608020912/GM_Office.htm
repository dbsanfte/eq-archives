As you enter the Office of the Elder Jedi Grandmaster, you feel at ease.  The towering man behind the desk motions for you to take a seat.  Sizing him up, you sense that this middle aged man is much older than his physical appearance would indicate.  His majestic silver-blue eyes catch yours and reassure you as he begins, "Greetings friend, my name is Warderick.  I'm the Elder Jedi Grandmaster and Guild Master of the Jedi Praxium.  You look a bit confused, but traveling through the plane of Cyberspace can do that to a person.  Let me explain where you have landed.

    This is the Jedi Keep - home of the Jedi Praxium on the Celestial plane.  The Jedi Praxium is an order of noble warriors who know the mystical power of the Force.  We, Jedi, use the power of the Force to attain our objectives and goals in the various realms throughout the many planes.  Specifically, we are dedicated to the endless search for knowledge, truth, and enlightenment.  We are explorers, seekers of justice, defenders of the weak, champions of the Light, and ever vigil foes of the Dark Side.

    Well, enough about us Jedi, let me tell you about the Keep.   There are several Halls, Chambers, and other rooms open to the public, and all of them branch off from the Great Hall.  I invite you to wander around the Keep as you like.  Don't mind the Iron Golems standing watch because they are just here to make sure no mischief goes on.  And I'm sure you won't be getting into any mischief.

    In the Hall of Legions, you can learn about our Legions in various realms.  Each Legion maintains an informational desk to aid Jedi and other adventurers in exploring their particular realm.  Just check out the Legion desk for the realm you are interested in.

    In the wondrous Hall of Remembrance, you will find the Jedi Saga and other accounts of the Jedi Praxium and its members.  The Jedi Saga chronicles the early Jedi Praxium, and the exodus from our homeland of Neverwinter during the Cataclysm.  The tapestry in this Hall depicts the Jedi Keep as it now stands in the Celestial Plane after the Keep's ascent from its original home in the Neverwinter Forest.  And the painting you will find on the opposite wall is a rendering of "The Celestial Vortex."  Aslo, you will find various personal accounts and stories on the scrolls miraculously levitating above pedestals throughout the Hall.

    In the Hall of Records, you can peruse important Guild documents on public display.  Remember this is a working library so some of the books might be checked out.  And unfortunately, you can only view those documents in the Public Reading Room because without a valid library card the Iron Golem guards will prevent you from browsing the stacks in the Main Library.

     The Council Chamber is the Guild meeting hall where official business is conducted.  The Assembly of Jedi convenes here every Monday night to discuss and debate important Guild issues.  The Senate and Council of Grandmasters also meet here on occasion.  The Iron Golems will prevent you from entering this room if you don't know the password.

    Wolfie's Tavern is where Jedi go socialize. This place is a popular hang out, and you will find Jedi here at various times throughout the day and night. The drinks are cheap and the conversation is always interesting. Try a Prune Fizzy, the drink of Jedi Grandmasters.

    If you would like to leave a message, just stop by the The Messaging Chamber.  Our messaging system is simple just write your message and post it on one of   the boards in the Chamber. No spamming please.

    Finally, the mystical Hall of Portals is where you can find "links" to various places in the plane of Cyberspace.  Be careful of your first step because some times the landings aren't so soft.  And we are always looking for Portals to new places and worlds, so contact us if you know of one that might be of interest.

    Well, that's about all the information that I have for you. Enjoy exploring the Keep.  If you have any questions or comments just drop me, Warderick, a note.  I'll get back to you as soon as possible."

    With that he gets up and shows you to the door. As you leave he proclaims, "May the FORCE be with you."
