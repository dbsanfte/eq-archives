Frenzy

From: Wolf
Date: 6/4/00
Time: 12:20:55 PM
Remote Name: 207.144.153.101

Comments

It seemed like an eternity to Wolf, an eternity of fighting. Almost two days ago Wolf set off on his quest, and had been fighting his way through endless waves of undead since. His spirit strong, his body weakened at points to the degree where his consciousness would leave his body and it seemed that someone else was controlling his body.

Ghoul after ghoul came, in waves of three and four at times. Wolf determination kept him going, along with the encouragement of his friends, for Smallander, Moonraider, Valoran, Trojar, and Fiathiriel were fighting along side him, helping to drive him on. For hours and days the fight drove on, until at last the ghoul Known as Frenzy appeared.

The fight was furious, Wolf's twin blades moving in a blur, but faster yet was the beast. Calling upon Holy powers, Smallander Managed to keep Wolf alive, and as the ghoul was occupied, Moonraider summoned an Icy missile and slammed it into the beasts side. The beast survived the blow that would have crushed most mortals, and turned hissing in Moonraider's direction. Trojar's Sword flashed and sliced the ghoul's midsection, drawing it's attention back to the Warriors.

Turning upon the brothers, the beast hissed, charging them, but slowing to a crawl as Valoran's spell entangled it's feet in the roots of the dungeon floor.  Confused the creature looked around to find the source of this latest hinderance.  It was at that moment that Wolf struck, both blades scoring telling hits on the beast, summoning the dreaded Ykesha force. Surrounded in a green mist of death the ghoul turned back to Wolf, raised it's claw to strike, and fell over dead before it could connect.

Swaying on his feet, Wolf reached down to the corpse and removed the sash the creature wore. Doning it as a belt, Wolf's movements seemed to fill with energy and speed up immediatly. Turning to his friends, Wolf smiled and said "Our work here is finished, let's go home".
