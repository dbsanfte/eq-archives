Charter of the Jedi Praxium


On this 31st day of October, in the year 1995, the charter members of
the Jedi Praxium hereby establish and charter this guild for the BETTERMENT of ALL REALMS. The dictates below shall be the guiding principals of the Jedi
Praxium and its members.


Section 1. Goals
The Jedi Praxium through its members shall: strive for the betterment of
all realms; aid fellow adventurers; explore and map the various realms; accumulate and compile knowledge for the Holocron; enlighten each other and others in the ways of the Force; defend the weak and oppressed; champion justice; oppose tyrrany; stand ever vigil against the Dark Side; and have fun doing it all.

Section 2. Conduct
A Jedi shall not act for personal power or wealth but seek knowledge
and enlightenment. A Jedi shall never act from hatred, anger, fear, or aggression
but act only when calm and at peace with the force.
A Jedi shall always conduct him or herself in keeping with the
teachings and honor of the Jedi tradtion. A Jedi not acting in such a manner
shall be tried and punished in accordance with the Code of the Jedi Praxium.

Section 3. Light Side Alignment
Only Jedi of the Light Side shall be admitted into the Jedi Praxium.
No Jedi shall be granted membership if he or she is of evil alignment or aligned
with the Dark Side. A Jedi acting in an evil manner or turning to the Dark
Side shall be tried and punished in accordance with the Code of the Jedi Praxium.

Section 4. The Code of the Jedi Praxium
The Code of the Jedi Praxium shall govern the affairs of the Jedi Praxium
and its members. The Code shall be the instrument by and through which the
guiding principles of this charter are accomplished and sustained.
