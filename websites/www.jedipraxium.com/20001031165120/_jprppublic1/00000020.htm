Khalesi Dawnstar, you ask us to judge you as an individual, independent of your race. I ask you, do you take the time to judge each orc or gnoll you meet as an individual, or is the only truth you seek for them a swift death? You mark us as ignorant because we assume you are evil. Yet the truth is, we learned this only through deeds of the dark elves who sneak from the ground durring the night to murder our wives and children and steal our wealth. The dark elves have earned the reputaion of being evil as much if not more than any clan of orcs in Norath. You may have turned against you own kind and joined the side of light, but how long will they let you mock them? How long before they see your good deeds and power to be a threat? How long before they seek you out and try to destroy you? And when they do will you be within the walls of the Praxium and bring their wrath upon us all?

It is not only question of what you can contribute to the Praxium, but also what you may take away.

I have yet to meet you myself, but will do my best to keep an open mind when I do so. Karana has taught me that even the vengeful storms in the plains can become the givers of life. Let us all hope you are the bright star in an otherwise dark sky that you claim to be.

Rakia
