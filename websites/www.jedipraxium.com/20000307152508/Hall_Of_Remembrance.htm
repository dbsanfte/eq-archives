Upon entry into the Hall of Remembrance, you are taken by the serene tapestry on one side of this vast room.  The tapestry, entitled "The Ascended Keep", portrays the Jedi Keep as it now stands in the Celestial plane after the Keep's ascent from its original home in the Neverwinter Forest.   On the long wall opposite the tapestry, you see a surrealistic painting with runes engraved underneath it.   Although these runes are unknown to you, you sense that if you concentrate their meaning and story will unfold.  Do you wish to concentrate and read runes of the Jedi Saga?

    Looking around this vast Hall, you marvel at several pedestals each with an illuminated scroll wondrously levitating just above it.  As Warderick said, each scroll contains a story of significance or a Jedi wouldn't have left it here for all to find to find. Would you like to read one of the Scrolls?
