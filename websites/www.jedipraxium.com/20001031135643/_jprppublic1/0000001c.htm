Freeport Arena

Comments

Eyes squinting into the brassy glare, I hastily search for my opponent across the blood red sand of the Freeport Arena. Wondering what I got myself into I finally catch a glimpse of Crion circling around to the left. Crion, fellow member of the Jedi Praxium, a bard of somewhat considerable repute, and here I am about to take him on in PVP pratice. Shaking my head, the sweat flies off the end of my long walnut colored tresses and falls to the shimmering sand as I once again wish for the cool dark forests of my native Kelithin. Being the senior of the two opponents I allow Crion to strike the first blow and our battle is joined. Running across the hot sand I chase Crion desparately trying to land a good solid blow but he is ever so elusive sliding across the sand with that unmistakable stride that all Bards seem to have. I turn to more magic means to subdue my opponent but he has already out thought me because his bard song keeps interupting my casting. I then turn to my trusty bow that has saved me many a time but alas I find it hard to draw a good bead upon my foe as he weaves in and out of the numerous duels be played out upon the Arena's floor. My last concious thoughts are to close with Crion and use my dual wield skill to break through his defenses. I awake to see Crion's smiling face and his hand extended to help me up. Yup I learned a lesson that day.... Not to PVP with bards. Crion and I left Freeport that night on a grand adventure where he saved my life but then that is another story..............
