The rain was falling like teardrops from the sky. Tia glanced up and let the water wash away the grime that matted her face and hair. Her arms ached from the heavy burden she had been carrying. The inn came into sight and she frowned as she watched the painted ladies twirl their hair and call out obscenities to the young men walking by. Why he choose to meet her at this place she had no idea. She did not relish the idea of entering this establishment. She peered into the darkness carefully looking for enemies. She had made so many in her years she wondered why she was not dead.. buried in some shallow grave with no marker. She shrugged off those thoughts and approached the door hesitantly. A simple sign denoted the building as being the Seafarer's Roost.A notorious tavern, filled with bandits and other riffraff. She gritted her teeth painfully and entered slowly.

He was sitting at a table off to the side, his dark wet hair clung to his face and she could tell he had not been there long. He glanced around the room and his eyes stayed on her as she approached. She dropped the bundle at his feet. Glaring at her, he briefly looked in the bag. Finally, he motioned to the chair next to him. She chose one further away. He grinned broadly at her choice. He leaned closely to her and whispered softly. "Did ye have any trouble getting them?" She fought off the urge to smack the silly grin off his face and refused to answer. She was tired.. the kind of tired that settled in your bones and made you too wary to even move. Finally, she looked in his direction and asked why he chose this place. He laughed and asked what better place? She shook her head and watched as a young serving wench approached. She offered her wares and Tia declined. He ordered an ale and tipped the wench generously. Tia shook her head and watched the other patrons of the tavern. He muttered and said she should be more sociable. She glared and whispered through gritted teeth that if she wanted an ale she would get from the Jedi Keep. He just shook his head and drank his ale.

She knew she was tightly wound and decided to try to relax some. He glanced up and saw someone enter the tavern. Standing quickly he grabbed her arm and ushered her out a back door. He leaned very closely and told her to meet him at the Temple of Marr in one hours time. Then he pushed her off the dock and into the water. Anger and panic rushed through her as she swam furiously under the pier waiting for an unseen enemy to show. When none surfaced she slowly swam to shore. Her body was aching from the cold and fatigue. Her teeth chattered loudly and her wet leather boots were rubbing blisters on her feet as she made her way through the alleys of FreePort to get to the Temple of Marr. She prayed her companion was making it safely there. <TBC>
