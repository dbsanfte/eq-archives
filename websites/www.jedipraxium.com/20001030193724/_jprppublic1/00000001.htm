The ancient Jedi fumbles around looking for an old door he long ago magically sealed and hid. "Hmmmm...now where can that door be," the Grand Master rhetorically asks the young Apprentice who is holding the torch for him. "You see originally we created several small ante Chambers off from the main Messaging Chamber. These smaller ante Chambers were to be used for various communication purposes. But, they were rarely used so I decided to seal the doors and save money on torches and such." The young Apprentice tries her best too look interested but hopes the ancient Jedi will find the door and soon. While the his stories are a tiny bit interesting, her Jedi training is much more interesting and it awaits. Chuckling to himself as he hears the thoughts of the Apprentice, Warderick wryly says "And that's why I choose you to come with me, young Jedi. For every Jedi must learn patience, and listening to my old stories is one way I can teach you that." With that said, the ancient Jedi waves his hand over a part of the wall while whisper some words of an unknown language. Suddenly, a large oak door appears. Warderick and the Apprentice step inside the small chamber. With another wave of his hand all of the torches in the room begin to burn once again. The Apprentices smiles at Warderick thinking their work is done. "It's just a little dusty I think, so give it a good once over and return to your training," Warderick tells the Apprentice. Seeing the Apprentices smile turn to a frown he reminds the Apprentice, "Patience is the way of the Jedi, and there is a lesson in every duty we undertake. Ponder on that while you work, young Apprentice."
