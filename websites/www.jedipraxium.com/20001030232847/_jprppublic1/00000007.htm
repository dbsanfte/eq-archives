"Hello, Hello is anyone here?" I yell out. I've been away a long time and was lucky to find the keep I haven't seen in over two years. It did not look abandoned as it was absent of the thick layer of dust long forgotten places hold. Perhaps everyone was out on a hunt or at a local tavern... "What was that?", I stop and listen, my ears are trained to hear the sounds of movement in the woods but they server me well inside as well. I relax as I hear the sounds of laughter ahead. As I get closer I can sense the force, not the constant presense that is in the keep and sourounding lands but a stronger more active measure. The laughter turns to conversation and I can see a room up ahead that is well lit. As I turn the corner not knowing what to expect my defences drop as I see some familar faces enjoying spirits with a not so familar face cleaning up around them. I smile, somethings never change and enter the room. "Hail old friends and new, can you spare a tired traveler a drink?".
