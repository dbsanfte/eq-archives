Jedi Praxium

     Suddenly, you find yourself in an enormous, ornately decorated room with numerous columns and a high vaulted ceiling. The imposing Coat of Arms over the main fireplace immediately catches your eye. The magnificent tapestries covering the walls portray brave warriors fighting fierce beasts, exploring strange lands, and aiding those in need. Of course, the large blue iron "statues" also catch your eye, and you immediately recognize the "statues" as Iron Golems.

     As you glance around to further inspect the room, you see an open door to another room. The sign above the doorway says, "Office of the Elder Jedi Grandmaster - Warderick". Peering into the room you see a towering man working at a stately oak desk. He looks up at you from his stacks of papers and smiles. His majestic silver-blue eyes reassure you that if you have questions or need assistance that he can help.

There are many passages leading out of the Great Hall where do you wish to go?
