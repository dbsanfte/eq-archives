The Night of the Cataclysm, the Jedi to gathered in the Great Hall of the Keep.  Distraught by the impending loss of their beloved Neverwinter and fearful of what the future held, Warderick attempted to ease their hearts and minds.  Stepping onto a chair, he shouted:

    "Old men forget and yet all shall be forgotten. But, our story shall a good man teach his son. And we in it shall be remembered. We few, we happy few, we band of brothers and sisters. For he, that hath shed his blood with me, is my brother. Be he nare so vile, this day shall have gentled his condition. And adventurers now a bed in other realms shall think themselves a curse they were not here and hold their manhood cheap as any speaks who fought with us in Neverwinter!"

(Please give Mr. Shakespeare credit for these words.)
