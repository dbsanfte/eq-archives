A Tale of fletching....

Returning to the keep, Wolf walks purposfully to his chambers. He carries with him a nondescipt bag, and in his hand is a stave of wood. Looking at it, it seems to be grown into the shape of a bow, with nary a line in it to show evidence of being crafted.

As he shuts his chamber door behind him, the glow lamps placed within brighten, and heading straight for his workbench, Wolf places the bag and stave on the counter. As he doffs his armor and hangs his weapons in there respective places, the stave seems to absorb some of the light and seems to glow of its own radiance. From the bag Wolf produces a spool of wire that gleems in the light, a silvery disk, and a planning tool. With a word of prayer to Karana, he begins to assemble the components, and as night falls, the light from Wolf's window illuminates the night.

And as he is engrosed in his work, he notices not the clouds that gather over the great keep, wich was moments ago blessed with clear skies. Nor does he notice as the rain falls, on the keep, and through his open window.
