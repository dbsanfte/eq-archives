FAQ about the Realm Legion of the Jedi Praxium

 

The Jedi Praxium is a multi-game player's guild originally organized in AOL's NeverWinter Nights online game.  The Jedi follow the ways of the Force much as their distant decendants do in the popular movie series.  If you ever wondered where the Jedi got their start, the Jedi Praxium can help you gain understanding.

The Realms Legion is the division of the Jedi Praxium active in Sierra's The Realms online game.  The Jedi also have Legions in other games, such as Origin's Ultima Online and EverQuest.  Other games Jedi can be found in include SharePlay.com's Space Merchant, Microsoft's Internet Gaming Zone, and The StarWars Combine.

The Realms Legion includes members who were among the original members of the Jedi Praxium, and others new to online gaming and the Guild.  We welcome players of all levels of experience and from all backgrounds.  If you are interested in online games, and seek the friendship and company of individuals like yourself, then you may have what it takes to become one of the Jedi.
 

Requirements


The number one rule in being a Jedi is to be a good Jedi.  Imagine what a Jedi Knight might do in a given situation and ask yourself, "Would Old Ben Knobe twack me for doing this?".  If the answer is yes, you're not being a good Jedi.  If your imagination fails you (or for some reason you never met Old Ben) here is a short list of good ideas:
 

  • Be Honest.  A Jedi is never dishonest.  Your good reputation is your strongest ally.
  • Be Noble.  A Jedi never behaves in a childish manner.  You must be an example to others.
  • Be Loyal.  Your Jedi brethren must be able to depend on you as well as you on them.
  • Be Peaceful.  Anger is the enemy of the Jedi, and an ally of the Dark Side.  Also we have members and meet people of all ages, many of whom do not appreciate foul language and angry words.
  • Be Resourceful.  The Jedi is a lone warrior most of the time.  You must not grow to depend too much on the aid of others.  Ask not for that which you need....Earn it.
  • Be Generous.  A Jedi has no need for extravagance and wealth.  We exist for the service of others.
  • Be Helpful.  The Jedi often encounters others of less skill and wisdom.  Our mission is to aid them.
  • Be Real.  Nobody can possibly hope to be all of these things all of the time.  However we all must strive to uphold these virtues whenever we can.  Remember, "Try not, Do".

A few other things:

  • Character name -- we do not require that you change your character's name (especially since in the Realms you can't without re-rolling).  However if you get a chance to, it sure does help find you in a crowd to start your name with "JP".
  • Character alignment -- although Jedi are "Good" your alignment may shift -- this is not a problem although maintaining a "Pure Evil" alignment might draw a few stares at the Jedi Social.
  • Character class and appearance -- we do not have a "uniform" of any type, nor do we restrict acceptance of characters of any class or race.
  • Other Characters -- we do not need information for any other characters you may have (in other guilds or just plain evil scoundrels).  We just request that you never use knowledge or equipment gained through the Jedi against your fellow Jedi.
  • Real Life Identity -- we don't really care who you are if you wish to keep it a secret -- all we really need is an e-mail address (and you can set that up somewhere like Microsoft Hotmail if you wish to remain anonymous).
  • Family Members -- members of a Jedi's family may be taken into the Jedi Guild without going through the formal process -- although they should send in an application to make it official.  (this is officially know as the "keep your wife from getting mad at you" clause).
