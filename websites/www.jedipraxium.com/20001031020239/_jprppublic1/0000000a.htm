here's the scoop though on the RP tag in EQ...

you can turn it on or off just like anon mode -- by typing /roleplay -- it changes the color of the name over your head and as a (possibly unintentional) side effect also makes you anonymous (i.e. hides your class/race/lvl/location on a /who list)

in THEORY people seeing the roleplay tag floating over your head will realize that you are attempting to roleplay and therefore refrain from talking to you out of character... however usually they will just ask you why your tag is a different color :)

there isn't an actual in-game effect besides changing your tag color... it is only for information purposes (and presumably so that if you want to find other RPers out there you can scour the universe for people with the same colored tag)
