Greetings!

In real terms, the Jedi Praxium is a closely knit association of gamers who believe in the Jedi ideals and are committed to carrying those ideals with them into various gaming forums. Originally, the Jedi Praxium was started in 1995 as a Guild of Neverwinter Nights, an America Online RPG game. As a Guild of NWN, the JP gained great respect and grew to around 120 members. Of course, with the retirement of NWN by AOL, we lost some of our membership just as America Online lost many thousands of customers.  Recently, we have surpassed our old high and number over 130 total members, who are committed to expanding the JP into the various gaming forums which they adventure in. We have established Legions in EverQuest, The Realm, and MicroSoft's Zone. And of course, our members enjoy other games like Asheron's Call, Baldur's Gate, Space Merchant, Diablo, and Ultima Online.

(NOTE: Currently, the largest contingent of Jedi play EverQuest. We currently have nearly 80 active Jedi in the EQ Legion, and the Legion Commander is JP Wolf. Of course, you can also find me on most nights late as -of course- Warderick. Its not uncommon at all to find 15-25 Jedi on around peak hours. Also every Thursday at 9 p.m. eastern there is a Jedi Gathering scheduled - its sort of like Jedi Night in EQ.)

In role-play terms, the Jedi Praxium is an ancient order of noble adventurers who know and cultivate the mystical power of the Force. We, Jedi, use the power of the Force to attain our objectives and goals in the various realms throughout the many planes. Specifically, we are dedicated to the endless search for knowledge, truth, and enlightenment. We are explorers, seekers of justice, defenders of the weak, champions of the Light, and ever vigil foes of the Dark Side. Ok what does all that mean in real terms, you ask? We RP ourselves as the very FIRST Jedi. We know nothing about space travel, but we know a great deal about dimensional travel (which is better than space travel). And of course we do have Light Sabers. But, in games set in the medieval period, everyone calls them Flaming Swords. I guess one person's Flaming Sword is another person's Light Saber. :) So as Jedi we fit in quite nicely to any time era.

After four years, the Jedi Praxium has developed a membership with diverse interests. Some Jedi enjoy role-playing, others enjoy adventuring and exploring, and still others just love a good fight. However, all our members enjoy the camaraderie and humor of their Jedi brothers and sisters. Many friendships run deep in the JP, and we are proud of that. The Jedi Praxium is probably more about friendships than anything. Oh yes, and to be a Jedi, you must have a sense of humor and be a bit of a wisenheimer. And, unfortunately Jedi are constantly playing pranks on there unsuspecting Guild Master (even though they now that I could wipe the floor wif'em).

A Jedi must be of dedicated to the light side of the Force and of a NON-EVIL alignment.  All Jedi must adhere to our Code of Honor. As Jedi we stand against tyranny, and so the Jedi Praxium is democratically structured and run. Our system of government is a bit complicated, so I won't try and explain it. But rest assured, all Jedi have a equal voice in the Assembly of Jedi - our supreme body. We put out a weekly newsletter called the Praxium Post. Its not much but it keeps our membership informed of important issues and events. There are Guild meetings every week and all members are invited to attend.

The following is a general description of the Jedi application process. First, you will need a Jedi to sponsor you. Second, you must fill out the Application Form linked to this page and submit it.  (If instead you would like me to email you a copy of the Application Form just email me (Warderick@aol.com).  Then you will need to adventure with your sponsor some and meet other Jedi.. Your sponsor introduce you to other Jedi and help you get the 3 recommendations that are required before your application can be voted on. One of the recommendations must be from an Officer. This process can take anywhere from one week to the maximum time of six weeks. At your request we will extend the application process. Once you have the required recommendations, we will inform your sponsor and your application will be voted on.  The recommendation process is just a way to ensure that you get to know other Jedi and we get to know you.  And, we like to see other Jedi vouch for our apprentices' excellent character and demeanor. If you are having problems getting recommendations, please contact me and I'll help you get things straightened out.

Well, I think that should give you something to think about. If you are interested in joining, just fill out the Application Form linked to this page.  If you have any questions, please email me. I'm here to help so please don't hesitate to ask!
