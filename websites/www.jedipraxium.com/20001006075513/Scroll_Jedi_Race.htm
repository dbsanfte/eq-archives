(This story is by Laurell.)

I was in the town of Freeport just hanging around when I felt this mysterious "force" calling me. I was needed for an important race that was to begin in Freeport and was to take me to the never before seen Qeynos. I was led by this mysterious "force" to train as a warrior and to build up my stamania as much as possible for this mysterious thing called HP. So I went to the gates of the city where this group of people had gathered and was invited by a man named Warderick to join a guild by the name of Praxium. I figured I had nothing to lose so I accepted and then is when I learned my fate. I also learned that the force that had been pulling me was the force of the light side which worked to rid the world of Norrath from the evil that inhabited the diffrent lands. I was teamed up with two other experienced adventurers. One was a powerful wizard named Binghigad and the other was a healer by the name of Mutan. Their mission was to get me safely to the other side of the world. We were racing against another group whose members incuded a ranger by the name of Agradon and another healer named Keela who were to get a guy by the name of Baitjp to the same destination.

Just when we were ready to start the gods were not smiling upon us because there were mysterious things happening in the land of East Commons. People were mysteriously dissappearing from within this land. It was then decided that the race would begin on the other side of where all the troubles were so we set off through East Commons as a group and were to meet up at a place called the Toll Booth in West Commons. Since I knew not where this place was the man named Warderick said he would see that I got there safely.

When I entered the land of East Commons I sensed the troubles. My feet and hands felt as if they were made of lead. I was slowed by a diffrent mysterious force, which the others were calling "the dark side", but by putting one foot in front of the other I slowly made my way to where we were meeting up and then the race was to begin. All of a sudden people were hailing strange creatures such as Hill Giants and Griffons and I was told by those more experienced to stay out of the way. When I looked at these creatures indeed I was afraid, for they scowled at me and I felt the force telling me they would attack. Just when we were getting our final instructions from Warderick the warrior on the other team was gobbled up by one of these griffons in one bite! Thankfully I was able to evade the attacks of this fierce beast and the other more experienced adventurers make quick work of slaying this giant bird. We waited for the other warrior to return from his death someone cast this strange spell on me. My eyes began to tingle and I could see things that were not visable to others, mostly other people who didn't want to be seen. Then this spell was cast on me that made me unseen to others without the aid of the first spell. All very strange goings on I must say.

The other warrior finally made it back to the place where the race was to start and again spells were cast on me. I felt the favor of the gods upon me and with another wave of Wardericks hands felt more magnanimous of spirit. Once that was done the other adventurers were casting all sorts of spells on themselves but were told the only spells that could be cast on the young warriors were heals.

After all the casting was complete and those casting the spells did something called "med" the race began as soon as Warderick finished the count down. When he said go, we ran for the next land called Kithicor Woods. Through some mysterious power I could speak only to the 2 adventurers who were helping and likewise they could say things that only I could hear. Through this communication system I kept hearing the words "root and run." The running I could handle for I knew what that was, I figured they would take care of the root part since they kept bringing it up. We ran through the woods and made it to the next land called Highhold Pass. We steped through the door to this land and there were creatures called orcs everywhere. "Run past them, they are all otherwise occupied", the other group members were telling me. So I ran and got to the other side of these orcs only to find myself in one of the most confusing mazes I had seen in my short lifetime. The force was with us though for we made it through after only taking one wrong turn.

The door to the land of East Karana was upon us at last. It was here that one my other team members cast a strange spell on me and I felt bound to the area. I just shrugged and went through the door. On the other side these adventurers told me to start down the mountain for they needed mana and had to med. Seeing as how I knew nothing of mana or what it was for I followed their instructions and began slowly down the mountain stying close to the side because the path was very narrow and it was a long way to the bottom. I got to the bottom and everything was going well when we got word that the other team had lost their healer and were going to have to wait for her to return. We were able to slow down at this time and take things a little easier because they were no longer hot on our heels as before. I still kept hearing the others in my group chanting "root and run". All of a sudden out of no where this lioness began attacking me. Why I do not know for I had done noting to her or her cubs but I heard in my head the "root n run" So I kept running and Binghigad I think began casting. The lioness then stopped in her tracks and could no longer hit me so I kept running and in the distance could see the bridge that was the gateway to the lands of North Karana. We crossed the bridge and breathed a sigh of relief as the lioness was left behind and could no longer hurt us.

I looked up thinking of the relief I felt at evading the lion and saw more of those giant birds that had attacked the other warrior. My spirits began to sink until I remembered the others telling me of the root and run and I figured if it worked with the lioness it would work with these birds. Off we went through the land and I tried to steer clear of all the stuff the force was telling me would attack me on sight. This worked and the force steered us straight though without incident to the land of East Karanas.

We ran through a magical wall to get to this land and upon entering I heard of things such as bandits that would surely kill me if they saw me, so it was decided the safest route would be to travel down to the river and follow it to the next land. So with me pointed in the right direction we started out and again the force was with us because before we knew it we were within sight to the gate to the Qeynos Hills area. But all of a sudden we recieved word that the warrior from the other team had just up and disappeared! We stopped in our tracks until word came again that the warrior was back and had found his companions. We started off again and got safely to the door that lead to the Hills.

Upon entering this new land I was told the finish was near and now instead of leading the group I would be in the middle with the mighty wizard in front and the healer behind me. So off we went and once again the force was watching over us because we met no evil monsters in this land either. Before I knew it we were walking through another of those magical walls that landed us in the city of Qeynos and now all we had to do was make it to the finish. I ran along the wall with the other adventurers behind me and made my way to the gates of the city where I was told to find a man by the name of Trojar. Finally we made it to the gates and I found the man wating for our arrival. The race was over and our team had won the first annual Jedi Fun Run.
