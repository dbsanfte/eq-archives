I would like to humbly present myself before the Praxium as I am in the process of applying for a position within this noble Order. I will tell you of myself in the hopes that you can judge based upon my actions and my deeds, and not upon the circumstances of my birth.

I am Khalesi Dawnstar, I have taken my own last name as I would not chose the name I was born with ... for I was born in Neriak and I am a Teir'Dal. I began my training with the Indigo Brotherhood ... but NEVER did I aid them ... nor did I even wear the tunic they gave me ... I vowed to turn my back upon Neriak and its dark master, Innoruuk himself. I have done this ... and never looked back.

It is only with the aid of strong friends that I have been able to survive in Norrath ... as I am viewed as a traitor by my own kind ... and an enemy by most other races ... yet with friends and patience and dedicated work ... I am now a Knight of Truth in N Freeport ... and so am devoted to Truth in all its incarnations ...

I realize that your Charter does not allow for evil races ... but how do you judge a race evil ... for I have seen humans who worship the same god that presides over Neriak ... I am Khelasi Dawnstar, a Teir'Dal ... but I ask of you judge as an individual ... and to do that please do not be afraid to look me up and speak with me in the wilds of Norrath ... I am eager to make meet more noble members of the Praxium ...

May Truth and Passion fill your lives ...

Khalesi Dawnstar
