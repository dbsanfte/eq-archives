Jedi Praxium Code of Honor


The Jedi Creed

There is no emotion, there is peace.
There is no ignorance, there is knowledge.
There is no passion, there is serenity.
There is no death, there is the Force.


The Jedi Oath

To act not for personal power or wealth but to seek knowledge and
enlightenment. Never to act from hatred, anger, or, fear but only to act
when calm and at peace with the force. Never to act out of aggression but
only in defense.

 

Preface by Warderick

    The Code of Honor was written to aid Jedi in understanding what is considered honorable and dishonorable conduct. The ideas expressed herein haven't been officially adopted by the guild as a whole. Rather this Code of Honor is based on the knowledge and understanding some old and of some well respected and senior Jedi. While this Code carries no official weight, it does carry a MORAL FORCE. This Code contains the guidelines the Guild Master will use in determining acts of misconduct. The Introduction below describes the difference between Jedi of the Light and Dark Sides. But, its a Jedi's actions and conduct which define him. It is the purpose of this Code to provide some guidelines that will help you to define yourself as a TRUE JEDI of the LIGHT.


Section 1. Introduction

    The Force flows through each Jedi, allowing him or her to move objects without touching them, to see the future and distant places, and to perform amazing feats. But, there are two sides to the Force; the light and the dark.  Jedi, who are in the light, fight only in defense of another, themselves, or the guild. They control their anger and other dark emotions. And they treat all beings with kindness. But, those in the dark are hungry for power and are driven by anger, hate, and fear.


Section 2. General Statement about Conduct

    We, Jedi, are noble warriors of high intellect who seek serenity and peace through the all embracing and all powerful - FORCE. With this in mind, a Jedi is expected to act in a manner that would bring credit to the individual and to the guild. Jedi are expected to act with bearing and forethought at all times. Jedi always exercise self-control and restraint because a Jedi's greatest weapon is his or her MIND.
    The Jedi Creed and the Jedi Oath both describe what being a True Jedi really means. And so, the goal of each Jedi is to achieve peace, serenity, and knowledge. Always remember a Jedi's greatest enemies are ANGER, HATRED, and FEAR not other players. And a Jedi's strongest ally is the FORCE!


Section 3. Conduct at Meetings

    Jedi are expected to conduct themselves in a dignified and restrained manner at guild meetings. Jedi are to refrain from outbursts and other disruptive behavior during meetings. Weekly guild meetings are important and central to the success of our guild, so it is important that all Jedi act in a respectful manner during the meeting. Only when the Chairperson of the meeting grants specific permission to a Jedi to address the guild or asks for comments from the guild as a whole, should a Jedi address the guild. A Jedi wishing to address the guild on an issue, must first gain approval from the Chairperson by asking for it. Any comments should be relevant to the issue at hand.  Respect must be paid to the Chairperson and guild brothers and sisters when they are speaking.
    A Jedi should carefully consider the comments of his brothers and sisters during meetings. This is especially true when it comes to comments about Jedi Apprentices. Jedi should put great stock in favorable comments from a brother or sister regarding a Jedi Apprentice.


Section 4. Conduct on the Boards

    The folders are a privilege and not to be taking lightly. The folders are intended for comradeship, communication, and role-playing but not for bashing or trash talk. Jedi are expected to act responsibly whenever posting.  We, Jedi, respect the other guilds' folders as it is hoped they will respect ours.
    Jedi don't respond to inane posts that are intended to enrage.  Instead, Jedi always take the high ground which is in keeping with our high morals and intellect. All Jedi posts must be within posting guidelines of the given forum. Posting is a privilege all Jedi should take very seriously.
And it is the duty of each Jedi to post in the folder regularly.


Section 5. Conduct While Adventuring

    Jedi conduct themselves in a most responsible, mature manner when in the realms and conduct themselves within the gaming forum's guidelines. Jedi don't act out of aggression, hate, or fear because that isn't the way of the Jedi. Jedi are kind and conscientious of other adventurers. Remember you are a representing the guild so your actions will reflect on the guild.  And ask to help others because it is the way of the Jedi to aid those in need.


Section 6. Rules of Engagement

    There are five general RULES of engagement when a Jedi is fighting an evil foe. As with any rule concerning conducted, the entire situation must be taken into account when considering whether the conduct was appropriate or inappropriate.

1. DON'T CAST UNLESS YOU KNOW THEY ARE A FOE
    Of all the rules in this one is of the utmost importance. This rule above all shall be followed! If you don't know whether someone is an foe, JUST DON'T CAST ON THEM! Normally, you can ask an ally in the fight whether the person is a friend or foe. Just ESP the ally and ask them who are foes.  Or if your in an FFA just ask someone who looks like a goodie who the baddies are. But if you don't know, don't cast on someone unless you KNOW THEY ARE A FOE! An important corollary to this rule is NEVER CAST ON A KNOWN GOODIE.  Occasionally, some goodie might get confused and cast on you, but don't return fire. Just ask him or her why they did that. There is nothing worse than hearing about some Jedi casting on another goodie. So, just be patient and find out who your foes are then show them to the gate. So PLEASE
remember DON'T CAST UNLESS YOU KNOW THEY ARE A FOE!

2. Don't WHINE
    This is a simple rule and it should always be followed. If you lose, just shut your mouth and take your lumps. If someone acts dishonorably, just tell him how lame that was and move on. There is nothing some baddies like more than hearing you express dissatisfaction. (Yes, I know these people are warped.) But, if you deprive a baddie the pleasure of your "whine", they will soon seek it from another like a junky does crack. And they just might realize your to mature to play there game and respect you in the future.  So, just grin and bear it.

3. Don't TALK TRASH
    Let's face it TRASH TALK just isn't in keeping with the values we, Jedi, stand for. Personally, I think trash talk is just a product of a lazy mind. Trash talk is normally trite and boorish. So, if you really feel theneed to insult a baddie or to talk down to them, its completely possible to do so and still RP a Jedi, but you must use style and finesse. A Jedi isn't just some dolt spouting expletives in modern jargon. A Jedi is a noble warrior of high intellect who combines a sharp mind with a cutting with when he or she belittles an opponent. Of course, arrogant and tough talking Jedi walk even closer to the Dark side, but they are Jedi.
    Here is a possible dialogue that I consider lame but appropriate Jedi behavior.

Baddie: Warderick, you stupid goodie newb why don't you go home to your Mommy!

Warderick: Ye silly Wart. I possess the POWER of the FORCE and will smite ye for your evil doings and blasphemy.

Baddie: The FArce sucks loser! And Jedi are a bunch of wusses! Wheeeeee!

Warderick: Your wart marked face should nae talk of things your pea sized brain cannot comprehend. Be gone lil wart ;pp

Baddie: Shut up yer lame wuss Jedi mouth afore I...

Warderick: Ye are proof that Evil beings are born of incestuous relations.  Ye are not of the social rank to even speak with thee, so I refuse to talk to ye, my lesser!

    Ok, while I still consider this dialogue lame it was done with some style and finesse. Notice how Warderick stayed in character the whole time and spoke as a middle ages noble might have. Notice how Warderick veiled his insults so they weren't so trite. In this example, Warderick acts as an arrogant and temperamental Jedi. But, he did act in a way befitting a Jedi.  Finally, if you play just to exchange insults, your missing most of the fun. And if you actually find it fun to insult others, don't do it with your Jedi. TRASH TALK is un-Jedi like. But, everyone gets mad now and again so when you do remember to use some style and finesse and cut them down to size with your Jedi intellect. Again let me stress TRASH TALK isn't the Jedi way.

4. No Jumping
    It is inappropriate for a Jedi to lay in wait for some baddie and jump him. A Jedi isn't a cut throat or an assassin. This doesn't mean Jedi don't jump into fights. Heck, we jump into fights all the time to protect other goodies beset be evil doers. But, we don't hide out waiting to bushwhack
unsuspecting adventurers.


Section 7. Administration

    The Guild Master or Legion Commander will informally investigate all reports of misconduct and make a determination whether or not the matter should be brought before the Senate. The Guild Master or Legion Commander shall act in a fair and impartial manner when conducting an investigation.  Anyone accused of misconduct will have the right to now the nature of the allegations and the names of the accusers. Only those incidents that are considered major transgressions will be dealt formally. Serious acts of misconduct will be forwarded to the Senate and enforced in accordance with the provisions laid out in the Code of the Jedi Praxium. The Guild Master shall keep records of all reports of misconduct.

 

Addendum:  Practical Points about Conduct

1.  A Jedi is CHIVALROUS and KIND to others even those who are his/her enemies because HATRED, ANGER, and FEAR are a Jedi's only true enemies. We Jedi are GOOD people. We deplore and shun those who exhibit the dark emotions of HATRED, ANGER, and FEAR.


2. BE NICE AND LEAVE ATTITUDES AT THE DOOR! You all are nice people so this is easy. But remember if your in a mood DON"T BRING IT IN THE GAME because it only gets others in a mood. Then when I pop on I hear so and so is grumpy because whatshisface snapped at so and so. People, your suppose to play this game to get AWAY FROM REALITY. So leave your real life concerns out of your fantasy life fun. As the Fantasy Island guy said, "SMILES EVERYONE".


3. GET ALONG WITH EACH OTHER! If you have a real problem with someone else in the guild, its your duty to take the first step and try and work it out. There is no rule that says you have to love everyone in this guild. In a group this large there will be personality conflicts, and your not going to get along with everyone just peachy keen. But it is your duty to try and work out your own problems.
   Most often it is the case, where someone just rubs you the wrong way. Personality conflicts are just a part of life we all have to deal with. There is no need to assume that someone is a bad person just because they don't share your views or personality traits. In those cases where its a personality conflict, I suggest you just limit your dealings with the other person. 
   However, if you can't then you are to come to me, and I'll resolve the problem for you. My solution might not be what you want, but I promise you I will find a solution that is best for the guild. BUT REMEMBER JUST TRY TO GET ALONG!


4. FOUL LANGUAGE AND VULGARITY is offensive to the vast majority of individuals. Now we all slip from time to time and something slips out. And if someone ticks you off by kill stealing or something of the sort, that doesn't give you a license to be completely obnoxious. Vulgarity is offensive, ineffective, and low brow. We, Jedi, are high brow and quick witted. If you must trash talk, then at least be good at it. Nonetheless, lets all try and refrain from making foul or vulgar comments.


5. DON'T STOOP TO THEIR LEVEL if someone insults you or tries to goad you because truely they aren't worth the effort to bother with. You see, some poorly adjusted individuals get their kicks off insulting others and getting into a yelling match. They just want to push your buttons and rile you up. DON'T LET THEM! Instead, realize the person spouting off is a warped individual. The best way to deal with such warped stupidity is to ignore it. The worst way is to sink to their level.


6. A Jedi is INDEPENDENT and SELF-RELIANT. A Jedi takes pride in accomplishing things on his/her own. It goes without say that a Jedi likes adventuring with his/her brothers or sisters. And just as a Jedi enjoys offering assistance to those in need, a Jedi isn't to proud to ask for assistance when he or she is in need.
   HOWEVER, A JEDI DOESN'T CONSTANTLY PESTER HIS OR HER FELLOW JEDI FOR ITEMS, MONEY, OR EXPERIENCE. Making yourself a nuisance by persistantly whinning about not having "stuff" is no fun for anyone, and it makes others not want you around.
No one starts out with a maxxed character, all the best items, and a billion platinum pieces. THE OBJECT OF A GAME IS TO ACHIEVE THOSE ENDS!!! Just about everyone works very, very hard to achieve those ends. However, some people think its all going to fall in their lap. WELL IT ISN'T!
   Constant begging for items, money, and experience turns you into a nuisance. Its OK to ask once or twice NICELY. But, to ask and ask and ask is annoying and makes people view you unfavorably.
   (Also, getting a teleport is a priviledge and a luxury. There is nothing wrong with asking a guild brother or sister to teleport you somewhere. But, you should certainly be understanding and ready to take the boat, if they don't have time or simply don't want to. And if someone promises to teleport you, don't become impatient with them because they are doing you a favor. Personally, my first instinct is to hop and the boat because 9 times out of 10 it will end up being faster and less of a hassle in the long run. However, if you are in need of an emergency teleport, don't be afraid to ask but explain the circumstances up front.)


7. DON'T TRY TO ANNOY OR OFFEND PEOPLE. This is simple enough, but some people still do it. If your trying to get under someones skin, then I really think you might be better suited in another guild. Seriously, it just isn't the Jedi way to purposely try to annoy or offend another person.


8. RETURN ITEMS LOANED TO YOU. If someone gives you an item, you should assume you are to return the item when you are done with it. A friend gave me a bunch of bronze. I think it was an actually gift but am unsure. However, when I get better armor, I'll contact my friend and tell him he can have his stuff back now and thank him for its use. If he says he really meant it as a gift then I'll thank him for his generosity. The point here is when people give you something always consider yourself a "borrower" and offer it back when your done. If they meant it as a gift, then they will let you keep it. And if they want it back, then you won't make them mad because you gave it back. Truely more people have gotten bent out of shape over this type of thing then you can imagine. And with just a little extra effort and a willingness to return stuff, you won't make anyone mad and people will like you even more and loan you stuff all the more often.


9. DON'T DRAG OTHERS INTO YOUR DISPUTE EITHER BY WAY OF THE GUILD CHANNEL OR GETTING THEM TO SIDE WITH YOU. I'm really saying two things here. First, if your in an arguement with someone, its not to be done over the guild channel. Nor are you to work your disputes out on the guild channel. There are several reasons for this. No one wants to hear two people argue. Its unconfortable and embarrasing. We all have problems, and if we all brought our problems to the channel, then the channel will become a big Jedi group therapy session. NO ONE WANTS THAT! So if your having a problem with someone, please try to talk /tell to them. If they don't want to talk with you, then talking on the channel won't get them to. I suggest you email them. If there is a problem between several of you, EverQuest provides some very nice chatrooms which are also private.
   Second, don't try to get people on your side. This is divisive and will only make things worse and that I promise. Most of the time it totally complicates what could have been easily resolved.


10. TELL YOUR GUILD MASTER (OR LEGION COMMANDER) ABOUT WHAT IS BUGGING YOU. That's right if you got a problem or something is bugging you then contact me. (I prefer email.) I'll be honest with all of you - I don't like dealing with these types of problems at all. But, I like dealing with them early on a whole lot more than when they are at critical mass. And whether I like it or not, I choose to be your Guild Master and it goes with the territory. So if you've got a problem, don't wait and let it fester - drop me an email or hail me online. Even if its just a concern you have about something going in the wrong direction, I need to know its better to catch things early on.
   But, be aware just because you might think something is a problem, I might take another view. Or just because you might think someone is in the wrong, I might take a look at your actions. So don't expect to get the results you want because you come to me. But, I do promise to be fair, open-minded, and objective. Yet, I always must consider whats best for the whole guild.
