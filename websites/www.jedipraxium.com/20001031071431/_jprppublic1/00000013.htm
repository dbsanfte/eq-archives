Flight of the Bard

From: Thorondil Soothesinger
Date: 11/16/99
Time: 1:51:46 PM
Remote Name: 212.126.131.163

Comments

One day, whilst speeding through Dagnors Couldron, Thorondil was thinking of flying. He had been trying to perform this long-forgotten skill but had never actually managed to do it, but as he has his speed song, and Dagnors couldron provieds good running jumps, perhaps they would give him a head start.

So, Thor lines himself up on the biggest hill he can find, and runs... When he reaches the top, he jumps...and begins to fall towards the ground at an extremely fast rate, meanwhile, in the corner of his eye, he catches a glimps of a beautiful blue bag that he had lost many years back on the 59th meridian. In seeing this, Thor completely forgets that the ground is there and simultaneously begins to fly away from it. At this point Thor is obviously surprised and he seems to have managed to pull off one skill that bugged him for ages, he begins to do loops and swoops, trying not to look at the ground incase he remembers it is there, and so gravity once again bothers to put some force into him, it ain't easy, and he is no master at it, and he begins to fall....

So there you are, perhaps the secret to flying is to be able to forget that there is any ground beneath you at just the right moment, with just the right distraction, and maybe, just maybe, gravity will think 'Oh sod it, I'm fed up of laws, let him have some fun.' Or maybe not...
