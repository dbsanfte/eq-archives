Freeport was a nice place to grow up... I was born in Freeport, in a small inn in the West part of town... Dad is an innkeeper there... My dad, Swin Blackeye, runs the bar... My mom, Krystin, works the streets selling rice to needy brewers...

Of course Freeport is also a dangerous place... The minute you step outside the gate you literally trip over the rats, snakes and bats that infest the outskirts of town. Walk a little further and you get chased by every orc and wolf in the neighborhood. Needless to say I died several times growing up... sometimes three, four times in a single day...

Well I knew if I was ever to see the world I'd have to learn to defend myself. Of course I had seen all the signs... be all you can be... join the Freeport Militia! So I decided to give it a try... But when they found out I favored Erollisi Marr, they just looked at me apprehensively and muttered something about not hardly being strong enough to even lift a sword...

Well I'll show them, I thought... It was then that it hit me... literally... I wasn't sure exactly who it was... I couldn't really see it... but I decided to follow it anyway... The trail of swirling dust led to that seemingly endless tunnel that leads to the east side of town when suddenly... someone appeared!

He was odd, colorfully dressed, with stark white hair, an incredibly colorful outfit, and the bluest skin you'd ever imagine on a person... he stared at me for a moment, seemingly shocked, but waving his arms which trailed strange bubbles... then he vanished...

"Sir", I cried, but all I got in reply was a whisper.

"Kid, you never saw me", he said...

"But I..."

Then suddenly a lady outside an archway shouted something about an intolerable reputation... I felt something turning me around and push me towards the lady... then he vanished around the corner....

The lady stopped like nothing had happened. Then I saw my dad running towards me... he looked like he was after someone...

I said, "Dad!"

"Can't you see I'm doing something here?", he said, suddenly stopping.

"Dad!", I repeated, "It's me, Spammy"

"Oh sorry son I didn't recognize you for a second", he said.

"Dad", I said, "I just saw the coolest guy... he was all blue and had the wildest outfit... I wanna be like him when I grow up"...

"Son", he said, "I was chasing that guy... his intolerable reputation insults us all"

"Funny that's what the lady down there said", I told him.

"Son, stay away from that place -- it's not a good place to be... "
