It was the next day... Dad was showing me the ropes for what was to be my new career...

"Son, first take these", he said, handing me a pile of assorted drinks and a few empty bottles. "Now, when someone walks in, you simply look at what you've got and tell them about it... the stuff sells itself..."

"Uh, okay", I muttered... I was still thinking about that wild and crazy outfit and the guy disappearing before my eyes...

"Look here comes Dirk", my dad said, "stand up straight."

"How do you know he's Dirk?", I asked.

"Look above his head, son"

"Hail Dirk", I said.

"No no he'll never talk to you if you act like that!", Dad admonished. "Welcome Dirk, you look like you could use a Kiola Sap."

The man, well Dirk, ignored my dad's advice and proceeded to buy 20 empty bottles one at a time. He dutifully handed my father 4 coppers for each one, my father dutifully making change for each purchase. I knew at once I had to get out of this place.

....

I handed the stuff to my dad an armload at a time and he thanked me for each and every item... Then I bolted over the counter, out the door, and back to the place where I had encountered that strange person the other day...

A strange birdlike man I had never seen before walked next to me. "What's the matter, son", he asked.

"I", I started, "well the other day I saw this guy with blue skin... he disappeared right in front of me... "

"Aha," he said, "go on..."

"I knew right away I wanted to be like him", I continued.

"Good", he said, "go on..."

"But I really want to join the Freeport Militia! I want to use swords and shields, be big and strong, but they won't have me! They say I'm barely big enough to hold a sword!"

"Ah... my child... I think I can help you", he said... and he led me through the archway and down to the side of a big pool... which I suddenly noticed had a huge building FLOATING above it!

"I can help you be all you wish to be", he told me...

"I can get a sword! and a Shield! and some armor!", I exclaimed.

"The sword and shield will come", he said, handing me a dagger, a robe, and a couple rolled up parchments. "Well talk later about the armor"

"Okay", I said, sitting down to study the scrolls. "Where's my other stuff?"

"Oy!", the birdman exclaimed, "you tink this stuff grows on trees? First you go kill a few of those rats and bats and snakes running around outside the gates... peel off their skin, pull out their teeth, clip off their wings... sell them in town and soon you'll have all the stuff you need"

"Great!", I said.

"First, though, I want you to concentrate", he said. "I want you to imagine yourself a big strong warrior"

"Like the militiamen?", I said.

"Like the militiamen", he smiled.

I did what he told me... he showed me how to use the directions on the scrolls... and, as I moved my arms, bubbles started forming around me... and I felt armored!

I ran to the west gate of town. I looked at one of the accursed rats, perhaps the same one who had killed me in my youth... I remembered something it said in the other scroll I had read...

"Die!", I shouted. The rat exploded in a shower of red... I could tell he was suffocating... then he ran right for me. I tried to fend him off with my dagger as well as I could... he kept biting me... I kept stabbing him... then he died! I cut off his whiskers as my master had instructed, stuck them in my pocket, and glowered at another rat menancingly, ready to attack.

Well after suddenly discovering my newfound powers had limits, I stabbed relentlessly but to no avail.. I needed rest. So I ran to the gate... then I realized I couldn't see the gate! I ran as fast as I could but the rat kept up... then an orc came out of nowhere, spiked a pick through me... and I woke up in the desert a short time later...

I asked my master what had happened. He only told me to hurry and get my robe back before it went away... he told me I had to pace myself... rest between battles... kill more rats...

I ran out where I had been killed, keeping an eye out for the orc... I got my stuff and realized I had forgotten all about my powers... so I sat down to try to recall what to do when Blam!... I found myself sitting in the desert again...
