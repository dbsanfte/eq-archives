The Death of a Bard

From: Havlar Hymnsinger
Date: 11/18/99
Time: 3:07:03 PM
Remote Name: 152.163.207.73

Comments

It was a night like any other,Havlar Hymnsinger,Teller of Tales,and Jedi GrandMaster,was wondering the forrest of Greater Fay, insearch of whatever fate may bring him. Always willing to lend his song to the aid of any who may need, he feared not the many and great evils that stalked the land, for the Force was strong in this Jedi. Having heard of a once grand and noble estate whose true name has been long forgotten,that has been over ran with the vile evil of undead, forever infesting its once virtuous halls with unspeakable horrors, now earning it the name of Unrest, he decides to investigate this place to see if somehow he can ebb the flow of evil there, or perhaps lend his song to aid those who were attempting to restore the place to its former state. Before entering Unrest, he had called upon another Jedi to meet him there, to aid him in his upcoming battle. However, before his fellow Jedi could arrive,a proud and noble ranger sought the Bard's help in recovering the slain body of one of her companions, which was located deep inside the house, surrounded by the hordes of undead creatures that now made their home within. True to himself and despite the great personal peril he were to place himself by agreeing to go inside, Havlar could not refuse the call for help, and so he prepared himslef to venture forth into certain doom, lke the noble warrior that he is. The first few areas were clear of the undead,as the band of adventures entered the House. He was in good company, with him travelled a very powerfull cleric of Marr, and a very skilled and strong Ranger, both as determined as the Jedi to succeed in thier quest. Soon, after decending into the depths of the house, they were suddenly attacked by several of the swarming undead, and a battle for dear life ensued! Havlars songs were of little avail to him, so resistant to his magic were the undead, that the Jedi was struggeling to merely survive. The battle was fierce and luckily with the aid of the ranger and the mighty spells of the cleric, the three managed to defeat the onslaught of the first wave of undead. Near death, they all rested in one of the hallways, the cleric completely drained of his powers for the moment and the ranger as well in desperate need of time to regain her strength. Luckily as a Bard, Havlar's magic would not fail him as long as he was able to sing, and so he sang a song of healing and soon, all were near rested and restored when suddenly a druid came running for his life, chased by four of the various monstrousities plaguing Unrest. The three adventurers jumped to the aid of the poor druid, and with ease defeated the undead warriors. Now near full strenght and rested, they continued thier quest, and without event reached the location of thier slain comrades body. All was well or so it seemed, and then tragedy struck. As Havlar was attempting to cross over to where his two friends were resting after having reached the body of their fallen friend, he did not see the break in the path, and he tumbled into a pit, surrounded and momentarily stunned, he was quickly overcome and slain by 3 undead Zombie Hags despite his efforts to fight them off. However, with the aid of the mighty cleric of Marr, Havlar's corpse was recovered and he was soon ressurrected back to join his friends, who were now ready to depart this wicked place. Yet the undead there were relentless, and soon they were attacked again, another desperate battle began, and all was going well until Havlar was blasted by a tentacle horror, throwing him back down into the pit from which he had previously been rescued from, soon he was hit numerous time and a blacknees engulfed him and he knew no more. Then there was a light and silence. Havlar found himslef lost and wondering in a place from which seemed to be no return, a land of the dead perhaps. Concentrating he reached forth with his mind and tried to contact the cleric of Marr who had saved him once before, after several passing minutes, the Jedi recieved no reply. Nothing, but the silence of his mind to accompany him. Perhaps the others were dead as well, as it seemed the old Jedi was now too, forever lost. Struggling to try to return to the land of the living, Havlar focused his mind and called out the Quellious for salvation from his fate, his call was answered and he was returned to an astral body, where he heard a voice that said " Venture forth and recover thou's body, if thee can" Surely this was Quellious speaking to him, or one of his servants perhaps? He found himslef standing near Unrest yet there was no body to be seen. He called out again with his mind, seeking the voice that spoke to him before, only this time he did not recieve an answer, nothing but silence. He could not sense his body despite all his efforts. It seemed this time, all was truly lost for his body was nomore, apparently devoured by the unholy creatures within Unrest. Will this be the end for the old Jedi? Has he sang his last song? If so he died as he would have wished, fighting against the darkness, and never giving up until he could fight no more. To this day and forever more, he will wander the hills of Danur's Cauldron, singing his song in the silence of his soul.

Havlar Hymnsinger Jedi GrandMaster EQ Legion "The Lost Jedi" "Teller of Tales" Survivor of NeverWinter One of the 12
