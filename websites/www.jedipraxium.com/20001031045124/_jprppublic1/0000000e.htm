And as the morning mist begin to give way, a group of Jedi Apprentices head to the fields surrounding the keep, walking in the direction of the archery targets.

As they come upon the targets, on yell for cover as an arrow comes streaking from the keep and flies in there direction. From the ground they watch the arrow fly straigt at the first target, burring itself to the fletch, dead center.

Sheepishly the Jedi get to their feet, and as they do another arrow is sent towards the second target, again driving itself into the center of the target. Soon all the targets in the field have an arrow protuding form their centers.

One of the young Jedi approches the target and syas to his fellows; "Look! The fletch is bright green and black. Only Master Wolf uses those colors for his arrows". In awe, the apprentices turn their head to the keep and stare, as if hoping to catch a glimps of the elder Jedi.

Standing on his balcony, with the bow still in his hand, Wolf admires his shots with aid of a spell of distance seeing. Dispelling the magic with a word, Wolf comments to himself; "Aye it is truly a fine bow. I belive I will call it... Trueshot". So declaring, the Jedi Master turns and walks back into the keep.
