A New Beginning

From: Zyvinn AireMathar <Holy Warrior to the 34th Circle of light>

Comments

I sat upon the ancient pyramid looking upon the warm desert sands, watching the wicked infestation of creatures roaming the lands. A tear started to fall from my eye, as I saw the many slain bodies of the innocent young adventurers who had once been. I felt a sickness swell inside me, but I have been through much and was used to this pain. I stood up, and bowed my head in a silent prayer for the souls that have gone to a better place. As I began to sit down, I felt a wind at my back, and was momentarly blinded by a multitude of lights. As my vision began to clear I saw before me, my good friend Binghigad. I immediatly smiled and the pains of the land was forgotten for the time being. I bowed and shook my friends hand. "Greetings, my old friend, I see you are traveling as usual" I said. "Zyvinn!!" he said. "What are you doing here?" he spoke with a smile. "Well I've come to see you my friend" I spoke with a grin . "To see me?, Great!!" he spoke excitedly. "Where shall we go?" binghigad asked. "hrmm..you are the traveler my friend, I am merely a slow running cleric who has to beg for teleports" I replied with a chuckle. We both just laughed and then he began to weave his spell that would take us to a another land in the world of Norrath. Binghigad winked at me and said "I know where we shall go" and then we both faded out within the wink of an eye. Leaving the desolate desert planes with only the echoes of the dead to keep it company. .....To be Continued......
