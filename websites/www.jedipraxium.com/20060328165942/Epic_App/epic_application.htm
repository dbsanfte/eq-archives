EverQuest Epic Application

The EverQuest Epic Application is the important first step in the process. From this application, the Senate and/or EQ Epic Committee  gains a great deal of information to aid in determining your spot in the Epic queue. A multitude of factors are taken into consideration so every bit of information is helpful.  Please take your time in filling out the Epic Application below. Notice that your Epic application has been reviewed will be made via the boards, when the Epic queue is announced and pending applications are listed.
