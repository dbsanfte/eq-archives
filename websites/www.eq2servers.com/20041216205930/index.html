Welcome to EQII Servers

I'd like to officially welcome you to EQII Servers, the place to go for your EverQuest II server community experiences.

What we provide here is an opportunity for the community in all its forms to collaborate on ideas and events in organized common areas. What you often find in massive multiplayer online communities is the lack of sites that focus on one server. Guild events, raids, birthday announcements, server down times, and more are all focused into one area here, and YOU as a community member can participate directly by posting your own events.

We are here to take that experience a step further. Being a network fan site we provide each server a site dedicated to all those activities. If at any time you are interested in what is occuring on your server, you need look no further than EQII Servers.

Please take a look around, view the central site here and check out the test server site once it is live. Also please read the network FAQ to hopefully answer any further questions that you might have as well as familiarize yourself with the rules and regulations. Now that the game is released we are allowing guilds to establish themselves officially in two seperate server sites.

Please feel free to add a link to our database for your site as well as start up discussions in the forums. If you are interested in becoming an administrator for central or the test site, please read the network FAQ on how to apply.
