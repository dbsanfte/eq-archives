Update Notes: Update Notes 04/11/2005

*** Zones and Population ***
- Merchant Sali Vaah has restocked her inventory with merchandise appropriate for her location.
- Marius and Lady Darkblade have become more wary of adventurers. They may be seen less often within their area of influence.

*** Quests ***

- Brandus Levine's fourth and fifth quests will now award armor wearable at level 15 rather than level 24.
- Nethet will now award the Blackshield medallion instead of trying to give two bracelets.
- Several quests in Everfrost will no longer give food as a reward.

*** Tradeskills ***

- Player-crafted poisons will now proc twice as many times as they did previously.
- The recipes for Halberds, Imbued Short Bows, and Imbued Clubs will now all work properly.

*** Optional Voiceover ***

- Another 25MB of optional Combat voiceover is now available for download.
- To enable optional downloads, run EverQuest II and click "Download Options" on the launchpad. Select the optional audio packs you would like to download and click the "Save Changes" button.
- Note: This download is part of the 400+ MB optional voiceover update offered on February 16. If you previously downloaded the following files, you already have this optional voiceover data:

OP3englishVoiceOver17.vpk
OP3englishVoiceOver18.vpk
OP3englishVoiceOver19.vpk
OP3englishVoiceOver20.vpk
OP3englishVoiceOver21.vpk

Community News: EQ2Vault Ask SOE #34!
EQ2Vault has another Ask SOE up! This one is #34. As usual Sassee brings us some great questions. Below is a snippet:

"Sassee: There are currently a bunch of different titles one can receive in game, such as "Hunter of Undead", "Hunter of Orcs", and the like. Are there any plans to add more titles for variation, such as maybe some type of collection title for players who complete 50 or more collection quests or other things of that nature?

Steve "Moorgard" Danuser: There are actually over 60 titles that players can earn through a variety of means. Some of them come from killing a certain number of enemies, through completing quests, as awards in live game events, or by raising your guild to a certain level. We added some new titles with the Bloodline Chronicles, so you can expect to see more of them coming in the future. Titles for collection quests would be an excellent idea!..."
