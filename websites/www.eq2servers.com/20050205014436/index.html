Site News: An example on how YOUR news is posted here, and other events
To give you an idea on how community news is posted (by YOU) on each of the server sites, you can check out the Permafrost news at (http://permafrost.eq2servers.com).

To post news simply click on the Submit link under the news section in the main menu on the left of your screen. To simply post events in the calendar click on the Calendar link at the top and you will be able to submit calendar events. The same goes for our gallery as well.

What can you post? Birthday news, events, gatherings, raids, crafting parties, guild leader announcements. Almost anything is allowed as long as it's clean and relavent to the community (Note I say community, so if you have something that you think your server would enjoy but its for another game, post! We'll check it out. Maximum time news is posted from your submission in 20 hours.)

And don't forget about the other features such as submitting your guild for a guild slot in the forums on your server, chatting in dedicated server chat rooms (each server gets its own!), and much more. If you have any questions you may contact us at admin@eq2servers.com, or read our Network FAQ.

Community News: New Ask SOE, #30 up at EQ2 Vault
Ask SOE, a feature started by Deathstryker eventually moved to myself and now onto Sassee, brings a little different twist this time. Questions directly from the community were incorporated. Below is a snippet:

"Sassee: Is SOE looking into improving the quest journal in regards to proper conning of quests as well as sorting quests by group/solo requirements and possibly even sorting quests by zone involved at its current step rather than just the zone that a quest was started in? (Question originally submitted by Loki_d20)

Steve "Moorgard" Danuser: Quest con color is set on an individual basis, so if you have quests that are obviously tagged too low or two high, please use the /bug command to let us know.

While we want to keep improving the quest journal and making it easier to use, allowing you to sort by zone on a per-step basis has a downside. We don't want people playing the journal instead of the game. What I mean is, having a list of all the quest steps you need to do in a particular zone turns your journal into a shopping list, and you lose the story and flavor behind the quests. Though it takes effort to go through your journal and find quests in a particular zone, the very act of doing that makes you read through the steps and remember elements of the storyline that you may have forgotten.

Adding a tag for specific solo quests is an interesting idea that I'll pass along."

This is a message just posted by Moorgard regarding the character and guild transfer status. The original thread can be found here.

Official EQII News: Armor Appearances - More Soon!
Moorgard posted in the official forums some exciting news in regards to more choices for armor graphics. Right now you will often see duplicate textures, styles, and other elements on characters. Read on to see how this will change:

Moorgard: "Many players have pointed out that there is currently a great deal of duplication among armor appearances. Currently all pieces of a single type of armor in a given 10-level range share the same appearance. For example, all types of chain armor look the same from levels 20-29, all leather armors look the same from levels 30-39, and so forth.

Our designers have been working to change the look of many armor types so that they can take on a variety of appearances for each level range. The look of each armor type will reflect the overall rarity of the item. Thus, someone wearing a set of store-bought chain is going to look different from someone who has equipped themselves with crafted chain, or a chain item that drops from a boss encounter. While more common items may share the same visual appearance, most armor types will now be much more distinct.

The effects of the first phase of this change will be visible in our next major update, scheduled to land on the Test server this week. The appearance of many existing armor pieces will change. Going forward, additional appearances will be used on new items that are added to the game. In other words, as more and more items become available, the amount of visual distinction among characters will continue to grow.

This change will affect items in the head, chest, shoulder, forearm, hands, feet, and leg slots. Shields, weapons, and symbols will be undergoing similar updates in the future. We think you'll agree that these changes will greatly increase the visual diversity of the game and will give a sense of pride to players who have worked hard to equip their characters with rare and precious items."

Update Notes: Update! Downtime For Character Transfers
Here is an update to the current situation about today's downtime:

Moorgard: "The English-language servers on which character transfers are taking place (Runnyeye, Faydark, Splitpaw, Najena, Mistmoore, Unrest, Permafrost, and Grobb) are experiencing a longer-than-expected downtime as we work to ensure the moves happen as smoothly as possible. We apologize for the additional downtime and will have the servers back up as soon as we can. We will post another update when we have additional information.

In order to bring the affected servers back online as quickly as possible, we will be bringing up each origin server as soon as characters have been moved from it. Therefore the last two servers to come up will be the destination servers for all these moves: Grobb and Splitpaw.

Further updates to this below:

"As we posted on Friday, character transfers to our two newest servers occurred with Monday's server restart. Although the process took longer than anticipated, these servers are in the process of coming back up now.

If you requested a move to one of the new servers, your character and/or guild should now be located on the new server. However, we are aware of a small number of characters that were not moved properly, and thus remain on their original servers. Those characters should put in a new "/movelog add" request so that we can transfer your character in the next batch of moves, which will happen in the next few days.

We have compiled a list of characters that requested a transfer but were not moved yet. If your character was a member of a guild, your status contribution will be restored to your guild when your character is moved over. Please note that your status contribution will be current as of the evening of 1/23/05, so any guild that moved to the new server and lost status and levels as a result of members not being moved over will have its status and level restored when the next round of character transfers is complete.

We apologize to those inconvenienced by this issue."
