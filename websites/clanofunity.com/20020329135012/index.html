Hail, and welcome to the website for the Clan of Unity.  We are an EverQuest guild located on the Karana server.

We are primarily a social guild, a close-knit group of friends who put a priority on how things are done rather than what things are done.  We measure our accomplishments in game by the happiness of our members, not by the value of our bank.

The best way to get in touch with us is through our forum, which several of our members access daily.  If you require our help in any way in game, please contact any of our members, who will gladly put you in touch with an officer.  If you need us offline, feel free to send some mail to the fat bear.

The first batch of screenshots is in, courtesy of Ialdaas!  Check them out by hitting the link below.  If you have a perfect screenshot, story, or both, please send it to Grromp, and it will be posted here as soon as possible.  Also, if you send me a full body screenshot and profile, I will begin posting profiles of our members in a separate section.  This was a very popular feature of the old website that I'd like to bring back.
