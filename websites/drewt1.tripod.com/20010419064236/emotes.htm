Emote Codes
%T - Current Target
%S - Subjective Gender-Specific Target (He, She, It)
%O - Objective Gender-Specific Target (Him, Her, It)
%P - Possessive Gender-Specific Target (His, Hers, Its)
%R - Race of Target (only PCs)

Custom text for canned animated Emotes
format: /emote say [custom text.]
(example: /agree say agrees with %T to move the guild to a safe location.
displays: /agree animation and Drewt agrees with [Target] to move the guild to a safe location.)

Player Name Tags
/shownames (ON or OFF) turns on or off visible names above PC's and NPC's heads.
Red PC Names are PvP.
Blue PC Names are -PvP.
Purple PC Names are players in /roleplay mode. (/roleplay = /anon with Guild Tag.)

Macro Batches
/pause [1 to 600 tenths of seconds]  (/pause 100 = 10 sec pause)
/pause command used on same line as command must come first followed by comma.  Pause will occur AFTER the command on the same line.
/cast [1 - 8] play the corresponding memorized song spell.
/doability [1 - 6] performs the corresponding ability from "abilities" window.
/doability [7 - 10] performs the corresponding ability from the "combat" window.
/whotarget performs /who on target.
