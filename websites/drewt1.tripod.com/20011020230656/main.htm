From yesterday's Test Server patch:

* Bards *

Bards are indeed intended to have the least ability to deal damage
physically of all the classes we're discussing.  However, with their lack
of double attack they didn't meet even our intentions for their damage
output.  We will, therefore, be raising the offense and one-handed weapon
caps for bards over 50th level to match that of warriors.  This should
place their ability to do damage by means of melee within our intended
range without making them more effective than the knights at any given
level, since double attack is a potent melee skill that bards do not get.
09/18/01 Received an email from Dark Age of Camelot by Mythic.  I've been selected (one of about 40,000) as a Beta-4 tester.  If anyone is interested in this game please check out http://www.darkageofcamelot.com or ask me.  As of this Beta-4 release the non-disclosure agreement has been lifted so beta testers are free to answer questions regarding the game.  I'll try to put some of the highlights in my journals here as I play and learn it.  I have started many characters trying to get the full experience.  My favorite so far is my naturalist named Chonnur.  In this game you start for instance as a naturalist and eventually choose the path of a bard or other specialty.  I'm still learning so I'll leave it at that and elaborate later.

One of the coolest ideas I was impressed by, at first glance, was that equipment wears out in this game.  In other words as you fight your armor and weapons take damage over time and you need to return to town to repair and refurbish them to keep them at their stats.

The only problem I am having so far is rewiring my brain (and my fingers) for this game.  Not everything is key-mappable although I have changed as much as I could to make it "feel" comfortable.

Fear not fellow guild mates and EverQuesters I have still been online ..um.. EQ'ing.  

I had made my way entirely through The Warrens and come out in Stonebrunt Mountains.  *pats himself on the back*  I had camped here a few days and finally ventured out to see what kind of trouble I could cause.  I wandered out of the cave and straight out into the woods to explore.  I con'ed a blue Kobold off in the distance running toward me.  No problem right?  I cast Selo's Chain (bind) on the distant Kobold.  Suddenly, a Kejek Leopard comes out of no where, no problem he's green.  I strike at the Leopard backing toward the tunnel.  Next I hear casting (I had not seen another PC since I logged on - I'm thinking, "ok, this isn't good".)  A green Lesser Kobold shaman that was guarding the tunnel entrance had aggro'ed me.  By now the distant blue Kobold was slapping me silly.  And of course the other Kobold's guarding the tunnel entrance, all be it all GREEN, joined in.  I bailed and ran, got stunned at least once and cast poison on me.  Made it back to the Warren and zoned with about one bubble left.  Ok, so I wasn't impressed with my first experience in Stonebrunt.

Figuring hunting was not going to be successful in this zone I decided to sing Shauri's Clouding (Invisible) back to the Paineel zone where I was bound.  I was successful until the "thrown room" where a huge Kobold aggro'ed me (even invisible) and I got slightly lost on the way back out.  Luckily no casters got involved and they were all green so nobody hit very hard.  I made it back to the zone pulling a train, I warned as I approached but a least one lowbie was a casualty.

Interested in participating from the start in a new guild with loyal and honest people?  
Send me /tell in the game and I'll introduce you to the guild. 

My guild will be different than most other guilds on
the Veeshan server.  It will not mirror the common
goals of getting the best loot, the most loot, having
certain level requirements and obscure rules to obtain
permanent membership. Items and loot are only
side-effects of teamwork by good, decent players who
enjoy the game.  Instead, my guild will step away from
the politics and red-tape often associated with most
popular guilds. The guild tag will be more than just a
bunch of words next to your character name.  It will
have pure meaning.  It will be a guild where
characters can be with most of their best EverQuest
friends to have fun and have help in adventuring,
fighting, questing, searching, auctioning, etc.  The
guild will foster a type of structured, sincere,
organized, honorable, easy-going, small, effective,
respectful, and fun environment.  Characters of any
level, class, and race will find the freedom to be
"uber" in their own way and at their own pace without
feeling heavy demands and pressures of having to live
their Everquest lives the way others want them to. We
believe that to become a good player, you need
guidance when you first begin, and that only through
teamwork will you become great.  Most of all, the key
words here are LOYALTY and HONOR.

11/20/00 - 11/22/00

Drewt's Vacation

Drewt took long strides as he raced toward the city of Freeport.  Images raced through his mind of the mostly bear kills that earned him the new level of 15.  He was proud as he invoked Selo's song, speeding his way toward the gate.  A smile crossed his face remembering the fight with Gix in WC with Tugna, these were the best of times.  Now he had earned that one new level song -- Kelin's Lucid Lullaby, an AE Mesmerizing song-spell -- that would make him even more valuable to his group partners.  Adding also more experience training points to direct toward the new wind instrument skill and other skills.
 
As he came into Freeport he threw back a short ale congratulating himself on his accomplishment.  Calling out to his guild for the first time this night, he would seek the approval of his peers, having listened the entire evening anonymously so he could finally level up.  Drinking another short ale he joked of alcohol tolerance with guild members he did not recognize.  Drinking yet another short ale, running through the streets of Freeport now in a drunken stupor.
 
As the effects of the alcohol slowly wore off, suddenly Drewt realized perhaps he had gone a step too far.  Perhaps the ale had clouded his judgement, combined with the lack of sleep and excitement of the new level.  What did these new guild members think of the drunken fool that was an officer of this great guild?  Even if they didn't know of his role now, eventually they would.  This wasn't what Drewt was about.  Perhaps they would understand.  Perhaps the other officers and Tugna knew the situation and would not look discouragingly at his actions.  Besides no one knew how difficult it was to level a Bard's position.  Drewt didn't have the strength of the Tanks or the powers of the fingerwigglers.  He shined in groups, that is simply his role.  Now feeling foolish he crouched in a corner and camped for the night. 
 
The next morning feeling the effects of a mild hangover and the burden of his actions slowly made his way to an INN in Freeport.  He decided to rest in comfort for a couple of days.  Collect his thoughts, organize his inventory and prepare for a move into new territories with his new level of experience.  He breathed a strong breath vowing to make it up to the others, to make them proud.  As proud as he was of the new guild and the direction it was heading under Tugna.  Drewt would prove he knew what it meant to be a member of the NUO, let alone an OFFICER.  [[TO BE CONTINUED...]]
