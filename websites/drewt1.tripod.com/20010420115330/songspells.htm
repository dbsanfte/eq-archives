This is list of Bard songs spells notes I've gathered from various websites and strategy guides.  In the spirit of this website these are my notes.  Like in the game, I share this information with fellow Bards.  Accuracy is solely based on what I've learned from reading, talking to other players or personal experience.  I welcome any corrections, please feel free to set me straight if the information I've gathered and presented is incorrect or changed.  Thanks.

Bard Songs typically take 3 seconds to cast the first pulse and require no use of mana.  Each pulse lasts approx 12 seconds.

Melee Disciplines:
Command:  /discipline
Once used, you cannot use ANY other discipline until the reuse time of the last-used discipline has expired.
You can type command to view the disciplines available to you, and amount of time before you can use another.

RESISTANT (LEVEL 51 - Velious Only)
 +10 to all resistances.
Duration: 1 Minute
Reuse Time: 1 Hour (decreases as you level.)

FEARLESS (LEVEL 54 - Velious Only)
 Immunity to "Fear" and spells that cause "Fear".
Duration: 11 Seconds
Reuse Time: 1 Hour (decreases as you level.)

DEFTDANCE (LEVEL 55 - Velious Only)
 Auto Dodge and Auto Dual-Wield.
Duration: 10 Seconds
Reuse Time: 72 Minutes

PURETONE (LEVEL 60 - Velious Only)
 Makes it as if you are wielding the best instrument of every type regardless of what you are holding.
Duration: 2 Minutes
Reuse Time: 72 Minutes
