00/11/20 - 00/11/22

Drewt's Vacation

Drewt took long strides as he raced toward the city of Freeport.  Images raced through his mind of the mostly bear kills that earned him the new level of 15.  He was proud as he invoked Selo's song, speeding his way toward the gate.  A smile crossed his face remembering the fight with Gix in WC with Tugna, these were the best of times.  Now he had earned that one new level song -- Kelin's Lucid Lullaby, an AE Mesmerizing song-spell -- that would make him even more valuable to his group partners.  Adding also more experience training points to direct toward the new wind instrument skill and other skills.
 
As he came into Freeport he threw back a short ale congratulating himself on his accomplishment.  Calling out to his guild for the first time this night, he would seek the approval of his peers, having listened the entire evening anonymously so he could finally level up.  Drinking another short ale he joked of alcohol tolerance with guild members he did not recognize.  Drinking yet another short ale, running through the streets of Freeport now in a drunken stupor.
 
As the effects of the alcohol slowly wore off, suddenly Drewt realized perhaps he had gone a step too far.  Perhaps the ale had clouded his judgement, combined with the lack of sleep and excitement of the new level.  What did these new guild members think of the drunken fool that was an officer of this great guild?  Even if they didn't know of his role now, eventually they would.  This wasn't what Drewt was about.  Perhaps they would understand.  Perhaps the other officers and Tugna knew the situation and would not look discouragingly at his actions.  Besides no one knew how difficult it was to level a Bard's position.  Drewt didn't have the strength of the Tanks or the powers of the fingerwigglers.  He shined in groups, that is simply his role.  Now feeling foolish he crouched in a corner and camped for the night. 
 
The next morning feeling the effects of a mild hangover and the burden of his actions slowly made his way to an INN in Freeport.  He decided to rest in comfort for a couple of days.  Collect his thoughts, organize his inventory and prepare for a move into new territories with his new level of experience.  He breathed a strong breath vowing to make it up to the others, to make them proud.  As proud as he was of the new guild and the direction it was heading under Tugna.  Drewt would prove he knew what it meant to be a member of the NUO, let alone an OFFICER.  [[TO BE CONTINUED...]]
