9/4/02 Website update and more to come....
8/5/02 Jeko has moved!  I opted to join the Legend service.  I cancelled my second standard account (yup "Glance" is history! - I know, your saying "who?")  For the time being Drewt will remain on Veeshan and in HL guild.  That being said, Jeko may return to Veeshan at some point also (allowed 1x every 6 mos) or if I decide to cancel the Legend service.

The transition wasn't too bad, having only lost my "hotkeys", but they were a mess anyway, and I'm thinking they are client side anyway.    At first there was nobody in any of the zones.  I was in WFP when I left Veeshan, and when I brought Jeko up for the first time on Stormhammer, he was still in WFP.  I ran to NFP and can you imagine no shouts, no auctions, silence, I was the only one in the entire zone.  That made me nervous to say the least.  But upon exploring I think the Bazaar upgrade cleared that area out, later when I visited the Bazaar, it was PACKED!

I have not been able to participate in any GM/Server activities yet but what I'm reading they sound very cool. 

I have joined a guild called Riders of Kunark and have done some hunting in a few different groups, I had traveled from WC to Luclin to DP, where I didn't have any luck, then hollered to my new guild if anyone looking for groupies.  Mafoo/Mafoojoan invited me to Tenebrous Mtns!  (can you say "road trip"?)  So I hiked back across NL, Nexus, SH, PC, ST, SV, HM and chickened out at GF.  I didn't think I could do it solo without invis.  Well, they wouldn't have any of that so they came and got me!  We traveled across GF (with invis thanx to Mafoo) to TM then got a TP (or maybe it was evac?) to Katta entrance.   Next a short jog to caves, I was turned into a wolf (cat in wolf's clothing hehe - meo-woof, meo-woof) and hunted Grims.  Woot!  I've been on this server for all of 2 days and I've traveled more then I have in 6 months.  Needless to say I'm impressed so far.

The Legends web interface wasn't working at first but I found that it just needed to be updated.  Click the "Jeko" link at the top of this column and then the "Legends" link and you should be able to checkout the character interface.  I haven't played with it much but looks cool.  If anyone has any questions on the service, I researched it thoroughly and would be happy to help.

To my friends on Veeshan, especially Aa, Tugs and Vorth, I haven't forgotten you and I will keep track of you, I certainly invite you to join me on Stormhammer.  If that is not an option I will be on Veeshan as Drewt time permitting and I'll be keeping tabs on guild activities too or you can always email me.  I owe a lot to you guys in the world of Norrath so look me up anytime and I'll be there for yas.  

Check back for more.. as soon as I find GIFs of the new IU icons I will update the icon description at the right.

4/16/02  I got quite a surprise recently while visiting the awesome city of Sanctus Seru.  I had spent the day here fiddling with my inventory and tailoring skill.  I met up with guildmate Aaevalor and went searching for a soulbinder.  I started to head down by the arena (which later I learned is dangerous enough) but realized it was getting late and I needed to log, so I turned around and headed for the front of town.  Suddenly I was attacked by a Defender of Arx who called all the other guards in as well.  Killed me in seconds.  Also a cross town Aaevalor had just gotten out of the pool (an entirely different story!) and was attacked and killed also.  After saying a hand full of bad words and petitioning the GM (but none where online) I looted all but one earring hoping that someone would come along and rez me for what I could pay.  I then logged off for awhile to do the RL thing.

Later I came back on and was coordinating a guild rez when I found that the zone was full of Lv60's.  I let out a desperate /shout which was replied with "we're too busy".  Moments later the pop-up window appeared asking if I wanted a 96% rez.  HELL YES!  Poof!  There I was.. I received most of my yellow bubble back.  Many thanks to Talys on Veeshan server for the rez, which he would not accept payment for.  You've renewed my faith in how the game is played by higher levels.

Moral of the story, if you have to visit Sanctus Seru, make it QUICK.. and no guarantees you won't get dead.  A moody bunch, these folks are.  BTW, we never did find a soulbinder, I don't believe there is one in this city.  ??
