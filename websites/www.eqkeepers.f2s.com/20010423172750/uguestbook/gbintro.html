Welcome to the Ultimate Guestbook Control Panel!

Detailed installation instructions!

Use this area to perform all of administrative functions related to your guestbook. You can customize the Ultimate Guestbook in many ways- change nearly any of the text, fonts, colors, backgrounds, date/time display formats, etc.

If this is your first time setting up your bulletin board, you should click on the General Variables link to the left. Follow the instructions and you will be up and running in no time!

If you like the Ultimate Guestbook, you will love the Ultimate Bulletin Board, the most popular online discussion forum program on the internet! Visit www.ultimatebb.com for more info on the UBB.
