NEW MESSAGE BOARDS ARE OPEN

04/25/2001

I've just about finished the new message boards. They are hosted on our own server space. Over the coming weeks I will complete the transfer of the old stories and messages there.

Please stop by and register your main characters name. I will have to authorize you to access the Members Sections of the boards. So stop by as soon as you can.

Can't wait to see your posts there :)
