Guild Charter of the Keepers of Innocence
an Ever Quest Guild on the Lanys T'Vyl Server

 

1.. Recruitment

a.. None shall be inducted into the ranks of the Keepers prior to said persons 15th season, to include all sub-characters. This will insure that the stop and go, roguish members of Norrath, who would join for what they could loot from the brotherhood and leave, do not disgrace our code of honor or our body members.

b.. Prospective members shall withstand a 30-day (real life) trial period. Just as the knights of Camelot had to earn the respect of King Arthur and the Knights of the Round Table, so must new members of the Keepers earn the respect of the council and its members. During this time frame, you will be evaluated on your respect for both guild members and citizens alike and your system of honor.

c.. Upon request for recruitment, the new prospect will be honored with a grouping of one or more of the guild council. If a council member speaks for you, then that council member and another council member shall join you in travels. Together you shall roam and hunt side by side, with your merit at stake. If you request an audience to join the Keepers, then the nearest available council member will join you in the hunt.

d.. Once a citizen has voiced the request to join the Keepers, and has roamed with a council member, a vote among the council will take place. A majority vote is necessary to allow induction and any opposition will have voiced reasoning to sustain or discredit the accusations made.

e.. Citizens suggested for membership by body members must endure the trials stated above, with the following exception; you shall be required to make two (2) separate adventures with different council members, one of which shall include your sponsoring member.

 

2.. Trading with Guild

a.. Be it known, the acquisition of wealth should not be the main focus of ones life in Norrath, but acquiring a name synonymous with duty, honor and loyalty. That being said, it shall not be the responsibility of the council to regulate fair trade within, or without, the guild body. It is, however, requested that when trading items to a brother/sister-in-arms, that a measure of favoritism be imposed and the price reflected accordingly. This will allow for a more satisfying and profitable journey into the future for all members of the Keepers.

b.. On the subject of crafted items, any sales should take into account the cost associated with failed attempts at the trade skill. It will be suggested here that any member proposing a sale within the guild should factor in the average cost of two (2) failures per item plus the merch buy back rate (example: Jewelcraft- cost of 3 stones + cost of 3 bars + the merchant buyback rate = selling price of jewels). Items found during adventure should reflect a lenient price. A suggested outline to follow in selling found items could be at 40% below common asking price. This will provide for an inexpensive means for all to acquire more rare items and the selling adventurer will still make a profit while aiding the guild.

 

3.. The Council

a.. The members to the council shall serve as officers to vote in new members and guides to aid said new members.

b.. They shall hold the highest office in the guild and, by that right, hold the highest responsibility in the guild.

c.. The council shall be made up of an odd number of members at all times, to avoid ties in voting.

d.. They shall be responsible for the actions of the guild members and the rewards or punishments that should follow.

e.. They shall be responsible for setting quests, raids, etc and organizing the details of said events.

f.. They shall be responsible for promoting to duty guild members to the council, in the event of a retirement or step down. The council may also, at the majority discretion of the council members, elect to promote guild members to sub-council levels to assist in operations, and retain those services for an indefinite period of time.

g.. With great power comes great responsibility, and the greatest responsibility of the council shall be leading by example. No council member shall be allowed to retain his/her position among the council if found to be in violation of the code of conduct, the unwritten rules of honor, or this charter. The hearing of a council member shall be handled by the council body plus one temporarily promoted guild member, of the council bodies choosing. If found guilty of accusations leading to the trial, the council member will be stripped of title and power and reinserted into the guild body, with a permanent block from any future promotion. Following this action, any future acts leading to trial and proven to be true shall result in immediate and permanent expulsion from the guild.

h.. The council shall be responsible for disciplinary action towards the body members as well. Employing a bit more leniency on body members than on council members, a body member shall also receive a fair trial and majority vote, but shall have one warning, followed by a 30 day (real life) suspension, and then permanent expulsion.

i.. All disciplinary actions shall be handled in a face to face meeting. There shall be no long range chat used to handle such personal and urgent matters.

 

4.. Misc Information

a.. Beyond the 15th season requirement, prospective members may be of any race, class or religion. A sense of honor should be used accordingly. While it is true that all dark and light races do not get along, and it is true that all religions do not associate well, it is the goal of the Keepers of Innocence to bring together these varied factions for one united group. Certainly there shall be some unrest, and certainly there shall be some distrust, but as honorable members of society at large, we, as the most honorable of all, shall overcome these odds and prove that the races, classes and religions can get along and there can be harmony across Norrath once more.

b.. On the subject of banking, there shall be no one member assigned to handle the guild finances. It will be the duty of each member to maintain his/her own bank account and items that are within. Any donations made in the name of the guild shall be made directly to the person receiving the donation. This applies to coin and item. This rule effectively eliminates the measure of distrust that goes with one person handling all the donated items.

c.. As members of the most honor bound guild in all Norrath, it shall be the responsibility of each member to remember that all life in Norrath is under our care. If someone is in battle and needs assistance, it is the duty of this guild to assist, whenever possible. It is a given that none shallpass by a fellow Keeper without acknowledging one another and aiding if needed, to the point of your own mortality. "It is better that I die fighting beside my brother, than live to see him fall dead at my idle feet." This shall be the motto by which all should judge each situation.


Adherence to rules is what makes a society strong and united. These are both guidelines and rules to lead the members in a direction that will reflect a positive outlook on the Keepers of Innocence for many days to come. We would welcome you to our guild and into our family.

Charter Amendment 1.0

We are the Keepers of Innocence. We are honor bound and duty sworn to protect all those in need of aid. The name should forever echo in Norrath as synonomous with duty, honor, valor and peace. By that right, it is the way of this guild to forgo any attempts at gains over another guild. Regardless of race, creed, color, profession or religious following, no guild wars shall ever be suggested or campaigned. Attempts at instigating a guild war will be held as a high offense within the guild and punishable accordingly. This will insure that the Keepers of Innocence maintains good standing with all guilds and that none shall dispell our good name.

This amendment is not in place to keep some from having what they see as a "good time", rather to maintain a fluid measure of accountability and discipline within the ranks of the guild body as a whole and to maintain our primary principle of helpfulness to all.
