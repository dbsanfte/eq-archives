Pet Commands

Some classes within EQ have pets. Now that you have a pet, you need to know what to say to make them do what they need to do.

Command

Action

/pet as you were

This command clears all previous commands

/pet attack

Your pet will attack whatever NPC is targeted

/pet back off

The pet will stop attacking

/pet follow me

Your pet will follow you

/pet get lost

This command will remove your pet from the game

/pet guard here

Using this command will make your pet stand in one location, which it will return to after combat

/pet guard me

Using this command will make your pet attack any NPC which is around you (regardless if they are friendly NPCs or not...use caution with this command)

/pet report health

Your pet will tell you it's hit point status

/pet sit down

Your pet will sit and rest

/pet stand up

Your pet will stand

/pet target

Targets your pet

/pet who leader

The pet say who summoned it (this is just for fun)
