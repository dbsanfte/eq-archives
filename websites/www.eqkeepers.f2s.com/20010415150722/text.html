Non-Animated Emotes

The majority of emotes have animations tied to them, however some just create text. These are great for role-playing or just to say something in a much quicker way.

You can also create your own emotes by using /emote (or /e) and then typing your message. Example /emote kicks the tree. By doing this the message will say "YOURNAME kicks the tree." Or by targeting a NPC or other play and adding the %T it will include the others name Example /emote kicks %T in the butt!! By doing this the message will say "YOURNAME kicks TARGETSNAME in the butt!!." This also works great to setup an INCOMING Message to your group.
