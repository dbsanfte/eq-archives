Emotes & Commands

There are so many different emotes and commands which can be used within EQ to enhance your playing style. It's very hard to remember them all, so just take a look at this list and give some of them a try when you are in the game.

Action Emotes

These emotes not only say something but your character "acts" out the emote. These are great fun and a useful role-play tool.

Non-animated Emotes

The majority of emotes have animations tied to them, however some just create text. These are great just to say something in a much quicker way.

Communications

These commands are used as a means to help you communicate with others in a variety of ways within the game.

Miscellaneous Commands

There are so many commands within EverQuest that some are just hard to categorize. But don't be fooled ... just because they don't have their own category doesn't mean they are not useful

Pet Commands

Some classes within EQ have pets. Now that you have a pet, you need to know what to say to make them do what they need to do.

Guild Commands

If you are the part of the council then you are sure to need this list of commands which allow you to recruit new members, dump old ones and a lot more.
