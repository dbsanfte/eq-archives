Raid info

Hi all. I'm going to try to make this short and polite...I'm not sure how close I will be on either to be honest. Part of this is about the Raid on ToFS last night...the rest is general raid info.

Good first: there were more than just a few Keepers and friends involved in the hunt last night, which is always a nice thing to see. Deaths were kept low...only two in fact ( sorry to Hab and Clar, but luckily we had Dal the uber dorf cleric for rezzes /cheer Dal ) which is also a good thing. Once we settled down I think we had some fun and got more than a tad bit of hunting done.

Bad/Ugly: it took almost 3 hours to get everyone grouped up and fighting. It was difficult to get folks to listen. Lots of griping.

We are still new at raiding together, but this is something we truly need to work on. This point has been made several times in the past, but there seem to be some lessons that are not being learned...or are being forgotten. Dal has graciously taken it upon himself to write out formally a set of rules for raids which the officers will peruse and post as soon as possible. These are rules for the Keepers raids. If you are on a Keepers led raid, you will follow them. Failure to do so will result in not being asked to join in on other raids. Keep this in mind.

Here are a few that need to be pointed out because of their importance (the wording may not be the same Dal will use, but I'm sure you'll recognize some of them).

#1 The RAID LEADER is ultimately in charge. Listen carefully to his/her directions as they will possibly save your life or that of someone in your group. Raid leaders will post here on the Keepers board the specifics of his/her planned raid. Including (but not limited to) the following: levels needed, date, location of staging area (where we all meet up and get into raid groups), time needed to be at the staging area, location of raid and time for raid start. Basically, look at it this way. If Joe the FriendlyDruid decides to lead a raid on RunnyEye and posts that your staging area will be at the Runnyeye entrance in Misty Thicket at 6pm CST Monday night, just consider that the start time for the raid. The sooner everyone gets there, gets settled into groups, and gets buffed up for action, the sooner the fun can begin. This means that if you sign up for the raid and you know that you are in Kunark or Vel or the other side of Antonica log on early so you can get a ride. After a few practice raids that we'll be having, if you aren't at the staging area in time, you aren't going on the raid. I know that sounds harsh, but you can't ask 12 or 30 or 100 people to wait on 1, 3 or 6 others. So remember: if you aren't there, you aren't going.

#2 Each Group Leader is in charge of his/her group. Listen to them. Follow their directions. If your leader says "Stay here", do it. If your leader says "Follow me", do it. If you have a question, ask. Do not go off exploring on your own. Do not ignore the Group Leader. Do not ignore the Raid Leader. Doing either of these is frowned upon and if it is shown that this is a constant problem you have, you will be asked to leave the raid.

#3 Be aware of who the Raid Leader designates as MainPuller/Main Assist...and then make a hot button for this. (ex. /assist Bulldogbigbooty ) If there is an alternate Puller/Assist make a hot button for that as well. This will keep us on the same mob, be sure said mob is snared, and ensure that when we have chanters or bards with us that we are not breaking mezzes.

#4 You will have fun. If you are not having any fun it is your fault...even if Cindy the halfling cleric is bossy or Tyrone the high elf mage is busting your chops. Enjoy yourself. If you're having that much trouble in your group talk to the RAID LEADER during a Quiet Spell (not during a pull, or a room pop)and ask to be moved. It will be the Raid Leaders decision whether or not to move you. If you can't be moved, try to have fun anyway.

#5 You will die. Expect it, accept it. Also expect and accept that we won't have a cleric for rezzes. While we're at it... expect and accept that we will not have either a chanter or a bard for clarity or manasong--- learn to utilize your mana to the best of your ability.

#6 Be aware of what your job is in the individual group. For instance if there are two druids and no clerics and you are one of the druid. Find out who is main healer and who is Back-up/Evac person. (Personally, I would hope that both druids would keep Evac up if at all possible, just in case. But that's my preference.)

#7 Each Group Leader will MASTER LOOT for their individual group or will designate another Master Looter in their place. This means they will loot every kill their particualr group makes. Cash and non-rare items will be divided within the group at the end of the raid as decided withing the individual groups. Rare, non-no drop loot will be rolled on, on a NBG (Need Before Greed) basis. No drop loot will be rolled NBG before it decays. If no one in the raiding party needs/wants the item it will be destroyed. Should a party make a kill and a lore item drops which the master looter cannot loot (ie they already looted one or own one personally), the group leader will appoint another memeber to loot said item.

#8 You will have fun. Just believe it and live it.

#9 You will be curteous and polite to fellow raiders and to anyone else in the zone. You will not KS another group. You will not get into /shout /ooc wars with anyone. If there is a problem you will let the RAID LEADER handle it. Hot tempers only worsen a situation and making a raiding party/guild as a whole look bad.

#10 You will not get any exp. Not happening. If it does, be cheerfully amazed. Raids are not about exp, however, so do not expect any. If you don't get any don't complain. Just HAVE FUN.

#11 Do not have fun at the sake of others. This is highly uncool and will result in people not being really happy to have you in a group with them. Do not make snide comments. You are not perfect. I am not perfect. Fred the mighty gnome warrior is not perfect. In other words (and pardon my language): do not act like an ass. It will not be tolerated and you will be asked to leave the raid if it is apparent that it's an on-going problem with you. Also, do not question the RAID or Group Leader's authority or judgement in /say. If you have a question or problem. When until a quiet time and then send a tell.

#12 Guess what? You're gonna die. You will get no exp. You will not be rezzed. You will not get clarity or hear the lovely strains of the manasong. You will not recieve other cool chanter buffs or be delighted by the buff songs of a bard. Mobs will not be mezzed. Dig?

#13 These raids are NOT about LOOT, EXP or CASH! They are about: learning to work together well in larger numbers; how to do our jobs better; accepting ways of doing said jobs better and accepting constructive criticism on them. These Raid are also about BEING TOGETHER with friends and family to have fun while we learn useful lessons. What we learn on these raids can make us more desirable in groups and on raids in the future. I would much sooner take into my group Monty the 45 monk who knows what the heck he's doing rather that Cornelius the Clueless 50 monk who has no idea what's going on.

We can learn and hone the skills which will get us into better groups and eventually higher level dungeon groups, dragons and even Planes raids. But we have to learn to work together.

#14 You will have fun? Got it? I mean it. Do not make me repeat myself. Now you've gone and done it. Ok, fine have it your way....YOU WILL HAVE FUN! =) Understand? =P

#15 One more time. Will die. No Rez. No EXP. No Mana, no buffs. No Mezz.

Why do I keep telling you this? I want you to accept it. The moment you walk into a dungeon or any zone where there's a MOB or group of MOBS that can kill you just accept that you are going to die...and then choose to live bravely. Choose to fight and live by the sides of your friends, your family your companions. Yes, you might die...but isn't it better to die fighting (and probably where you can find your bod) to protect those in your group than to run like a scared bunny (and possibly get lost or train something worse on the groups)? Hopefully, your healer (or one from another group) will keep you from dying or if things are really nasty, hopefully you are close enough to your evac person to get pulled out. With luck we have a cleric who can rez with us if we do die. If not, don't gripe about it. No one likes to die, but pouting and griping will only serve to get on other raiders nerves.

Be happily suprized when you don't die or when you do and get a rez. Be gracious and thank the rezzer. If we have mezzers and mana batteries be thankful and show them you appreciate their efforts. If there is a tough pull or room pop and we survive it and can catch our breath afterwards...rejoice. Cheer. Shout "Yahoo" "Hooray" "We lived!" But do not become a SPAM nuscience. Compliment folks on a good job. It'll make a difference.

This was much longer than I had planned, but I think it says most of what I wanted to get across. Dal, I'm sure will do a much better job than I have, but I think all of this needed said...some of it again and again. We need to learn this. We need to grow in our jobs and become better at them. We need to remember it's just a game, but that gives no one a reason to ruin the fun for anyone else--- just common courtesy there.

Once again, these raids aren't about LOOT, not about EXP, and not about CASH. Deal with it, accept it. They are about friends and family, learning new skills and getting better at those we already possess, maybe being able to come up with a better item that someone can really use, but mostly they are about having FUN.

With much respect,

Giden "Gid" Skyefyre
