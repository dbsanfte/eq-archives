Dark Brethren Rules:

. Principal rules is common sense and consideration of fellow Guild members as well as acting in accordance to Verant's rules and regulations.

. All members must be willing to attend Guild Events if online during that time.

. All Guild Members must be courteous to other Guild Members.

. Guild members need to follow Guidline set by leader and officers.

. Must abide by the rules and guideline set by Verant for conduct regarding other (non guild) players.

. Loots are distributed by the Guild Leader and/or Officers, based on a merit system. This is determinated by how often the person attends events, how willing they are to help others, who it will benefit the most, and sometimes a judgement call by those determining.
