                          The Iksarian Broods

 

 

Welcome to the home of the Iksarian Broods.  Please feel free to look around a make use of our services.  

The Iksarian Broods is an Everquest guild which resides upon the Bertoxx server. We Broods are a very close knit family that stand by one another. Within the Broods, we are friends, family, and a team. We learn from one another and grow from our experiences. Friendship and cooperation are the values we hold in the highest regard among the Broods. We will lay our lives on the line to assist a fellow Brood as they would for us. A sense of humor is an asset among the Broods as we are constantly keeping our members on their toes. The requirements for membership are as follows:

  1. You must be of a level equal to or greater than 20
  2. You must be willing, helpful, and contribute to the guild
  3. You must register with the guild web site
  4. You must maintain good standing with the populace of Norrath.
  5. No Anon you should wear your guild badge proud. Roleplay is fine for those who need it. 
With some time we will grow to be the most formidable force upon the Bertoxx server... but this requires the guilds cooperation as a whole. What we cannot achieve on our own we shall achieve together
