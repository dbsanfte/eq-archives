Info on a New Verant Game, And It Doesn't Sound Like It Will Suck Posted Tuesday, November 7, 2000 by Buur
Daily Radar has a story on a new game that Verant is working on called Planetside. You can read all about it right here.

Now I am no big fan of Verant. Some might say I am the anti-fan. But this game actually sounds like it might be fun! For those of you too lazy to go and read the story at Daily Radar, here is a brief run down of it.

It is a tactical MMOFPS (that is Massive Multiplayer Online First Person Shooter). It is like a TFC server with 1000's of people instead of like 16. It is team based and there are 4 teams to fight for. You can improve your character and but not to such levels that you will have an unfair advantage over everyone else. It is basically a big old deathmatch with some extras that you dont' find in Quake.

I better stop this or I might get accused of being a Verant fan. Anyway, I encourage you to go read about this game. If you are into TFC or Counterstrike then this might interest you too.

Smedley Responds to Fatbabies Posted Tuesday, November 7, 2000 by Buur
John Smedley fired off an e-mail to the fatbabies:

I'm not sure exactly where you morons get your information, but PlanetSide does indeed contain everything listed in the December Issue of PC Gamer.
Nothing has been thrown out whatsoever, and the St. Louis office is not being shut down, nor have a bunch of people been laid off. As for Sovereign, we have indeed been reworking the graphics engine, something we've been pretty public about actually. One final comment - Brian Hook was never on Star Wars and the work he's doing is awesome. Please remove your heads from their current locations and save the fiction writing for the Great American Novel I'm sure you're writing.

Regards,

John Smedley
Chief Operating Officer
Sony Online Entertainment

Normally I would go off on John for writing someone and calling them a moron and stuff of that nature. But this time I actually think it is pretty funny. It is not as if fatbabies are known for their real journalistic integrity or anything.

And yes, this did happen today as opposed to 7 months ago. Some of you will get that reference.
