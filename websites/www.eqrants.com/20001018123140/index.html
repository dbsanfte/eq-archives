Verant with Guns! Posted Tuesday, October 17, 2000 by WhiteFox
Was hitting the boards today when I read a post by MechaCrash... You can check it out Here.

Specifically these lines...

Did you find that if you walk in a certain pattern, the critter you attacked will come for you without getting his buddies? That's a bug, you're an exploiter, here comes the ban. Find out that you can make a certain item with a tradeskill and sell it back at a profit? That's a goof, and will be patched, so make it while you can, kids, it's not going to last. Have a level 52 critter run across the zone to kill your level 5 self? Well, you better suck it up, because it's working as intended.

Goddamn... MechaCrash just summed up MMORPGs in general... but EQ is the ultimate incarnation of this... for some reason Verant is... very... um... shall we say, paranoid? They get cannons to kill flies.

I mean christ, YOU HAVE TO AGREE TO THE TERMS OF SERVICE EVERY GODDAMN TIME YOU TURN ON THE GAME!!! That’s fuckin worse than communism!

Think of it like a line of people with a bunch of other guys standing around the line with guns. The line of people symbolizes the game. The guys with guns symbolize Verant. Now most companies, when they see someone in that line they don't like they either move that person or shoot that person. Verant... goes fuckin nuts, wasting the guy, the two guys in front of him, the two guys behind him, randomly executing 5 people from anywhere in the line, and finally rearranging the whole area where the guy used to be. If nothing comes up before patch day they resort to "Random Public Executions." Meaning, even if there really isn't anything wrong with your class/race/trade/whatever, its gonna get nerfed/fucked somehow eventually.

Think Penny-Arcade did a pretty good job of summing it up.

To conclude, we here are Verant know where the line is, so don't cross it, or your house might accidentally catch on fire.

Although I doubt Verant could burn down my house... my... assistant... is deadly...
My name is Teka, and Teka OWNS you...

Well... Thank You MechaCrash for giving me the inspiration to rant. Got anything else to say on the subject?

You Won't Have Gordon to Kick Around Anymore Posted Monday, October 16, 2000 by Buur
