FireArms and Front Line Force Posted Thursday, January 25, 2001 by WhiteFox
About a week ago I downloaded these two half-life mods. I've been playing them off and on since then and I feel I'm ready to voice my thoughts on them both.

Think I'll start with FireArms... This mod will very much remind you of war. Gather around my children and I shall tell you a tale...


Oh goodie!

This would be one of my experiences in the game. I had joined a small game (4 or 5 people on each team) and I happened to join the team that consisted entirely of a friendly clan. Being so "friendly" they let me play in their reindeer games, including me in their plans and such (thx guys!). Any names I use will be made up (cuz I can't remember any of 'em) and any screenshots I use will be coming from the Offical FireArms Website. Now... pretend children... close your eyes and pretend... er, don't close your eyes... but pretend... you start in a mucky wet looking landscape. You’re stranded above a high a cliff. You grab your gear and jump, releasing your parachute (died 4 times before someone told me how to use it). You land with your little gang of four. Your objective? To capture each point. Points are located between your spawn and the enemy spawm, generally a string of them leading from your spawn to theirs. Your four companions?

LeaderDude(TSF)
OtherDude(TSF)
ThatGUY(TSF)
I'm a Target(TSF)

LeaderDude(TSF) actually acts like a leader and tells everyone to gather at the first points (Consider it home base). Doing as he says you rip out your trusty one-shot killa "Mr. Snipa".

You grab some bandages and head out, bringing up the rear. Following your little gang of doom you come upon the second point out from home... its controlled by the enemy... Nothing more than a hill with a flag on top of it surrounded by a few sandbags. Now, LeaderDude(TSF) stops and everyone else seems to freeze. What’s he do? He orders ThatGUY(TSF) to get on his belly and crawl his ass up there (these guys were big on communication, OWNAGE). So... ThatGUY(TSF) jumps on his belly and begins to crawl.

After crawling all the way over there he comes around the sandbag... and that’s when it happens... an enemy who had been ducking behind the sandbag stands! Everyone opens up while you stand there deaf and confused because your to much of a newbie to know wtf to do (I sucked ass when I picked this game up).

The enemy soldier EXPLODES. Blood everywhere. The enemy flag is taken down and yours put up. Quickly your team is on the move to the next point and your barely able to catch up... still shocked by the fact that guy is in a million pieces all over the last point. You follow your team around a corner just in time to see two enemy snipers caught with their pants down... both of them in the middle of moving from cover to cover... Seconds later both are dead at the hands of your "pals".

That’s when you see it... Center point... a small fort surrounded by a large clearing. That’s when you HEAR it... a small ping sound... all of your gang jumps behind a rock. You head for the rock when your life drops rapidly... yet even as your ducking behind the rock ThatGUY(TSF) is next to you, bandaging you. Stopping you from bleeding to death and healing you. While he does this I'm a Target(TSF) and OtherDude(TSF) jump up with their sniper rifles and "deal" with the enemy sniper. After all is done LeaderDude(TSF) orders everyone to rush to fort and take it. An easy enough task, the fort is now empty and all goes well. Soon your all on the move again, heading to the next point. You enter a narrow passage... and the bullets come. You and your team quickly dive behind a rock. LeaderDude(TSF) receives minor damage but is quickly attended to. Four enemies are at the point, dug in behind sandbags. Two snipers, two with machine guns of some kind... and that’s when you hear it... a screaming sound, growing louder and louder and louder... then it happens. Blinding fire and your life drops 75%. You look up... and you see...

BigAssMortarGuy ------> OtherDude(TSF)
BigAssMortarGuy ------> LeaderDude(TSF)

At this point all you can do is scramble for another rock... attempt to get away from the mortar target as fast as possible. You can already hear the scream of another mortar...

BigAssMortarGuy ------> ThatGUY(TSF)

You and I'm a Target(TSF) manage to take cover behind another rock. You look over to see a burnt patch of ground, covered in blood where your friends "used" to be. I'm a Target(TSF) peeks around the corner of the rock and nails someone in the face with his rifle... you attempt to do the same only to be pushed back down by a massive barrage of fire. He slides out again and nails another... the enemy fire stops. After a thorough look over I'm a Target(TSF) says we should push in. You hop up and follow him...

I believe Cail said it best, "Seriously, nothing hurts morale like 5 blood trails shooting out of the point man."

SnipaDude -----> I'm a Target(TSF)

You have NO clue where the hell that just came from... You take cover behind a sandbag only to hear the scream of mortar fire... and its all fuckin over.

Now... THAT SHIT WAS FUN. I didn't do shit but I had fun doin it! I really wish I had the names of those 4 guys... they really were an awesome clan. The amount of text communication these people used was awesome... always short, to the point, and highly useful. I really am pissed that I was so stupid as to not get the ICQ number of one of these guys.

Now granted... the awesome part of all that depended on a lot of unlikely factors... good teammates. Enemies who camped and didn't play "Quake" style. COMMUNICATION!

Anyway... my overall score of the game? Scale of 1 to 10, 10 being best I'd give this mod a 7.

Why so low for such an awesome game you ask? Well... its not a smooth playing game... the frame rates are choppy in areas... a lot of people DO play the game quake style (full servers suck). The game is still being worked on though, its relatively new... but as it stands, I'm probably done playing it for now... needs work. I just have no tolerance for a game with bad frames... really a pity to. Has a *HUGE* amount of potential.

As for Front Line Force. This game is much less complicated. Two teams start on a map, one defending a few points, the other attempting to capture those points. The attacking team only has so much to time to steal all the points and if successful, they win. It plays smooth but the weapons are a bit too accurate and people take a bit too much to kill. Its played Quake style but is still rather fun because of the games objective. It’s not as good as Counter-Strike in my opinion but it’s something to play when you don't "feel" like Counter-Strike. The BEST part of the game is going "Kamikaze", which I sooooo love to do. Hold a grenade, pull the pin, and literally throw yourself into a mess of enemies and let them cut you down. I swear... its beautiful... after you get cut down you often get someone who screams "Hahahaha, you dumbass!!!!" just before him and the 3 guys next to him explode. Almost as fun as my "Kamikaze Engineer" from TFC (I would hold an EMP grenade and run up to a HWG or something with a lot of ammo to burn).

Anyway... both are rather good... FireArms needs a little tweaking. Front Line Force probably won't be your "#1 Game" but it’s a pretty good relaxation "goof off" game.

Think I'm about done for today... now to get some rest... Comment?

EverQuest Changes Are Coming Posted Monday, January 15, 2001 by Buur

You can read the whole statement from Brad right here. You can read the summary and my thoughts below.

Summary

The changes that we are making in the next patch should have a profound impact upon the game:

- Classes no longer have a true class-based experience penalty, making it easier for people to play the class that they want to play, rather than the class that they feel compelled to play due to faster advancement.

- Race based penalties are no longer shared with the group, and in truth are not severe enough to greatly impact the advancement speed of any particular race. Even the race with the worst penalty will level no slower than a human monk does now, and in truth will level faster due to the other changes that went in.

- The grouping bonus increase, in addition to the other changes, makes grouping much more efficient, without reducing the current efficiency of soloing for those who prefer that route.

- Experience modifiers turned up in the other zones should encourage people to spread out, have more fun, and allow those staying behind in other areas to have more fun themselves since the area is less crowded.

- All players, assuming that they have an experience penalty, or ever group with anyone who does, will level faster.

Many of these changes are quite involved in regards to their implementation, and as such we want to be sure that everyone interested understands them. Alan "Absor" VanCouvering will be happy to answer any questions that you may have on the EverQuest Message boards. Until then, we sincerely hope that these changes will help all players, whether "uber" or "causal", experience and enjoy EverQuest to their greatest potential.

No One Lives Forever Thoughts Posted Thursday, January 11, 2001 by Buur
I really don't like doing full reviews, unless they are the fake kind that I totally make up. I find them to be pretty boring. Plus someone once told me that you should never do a review of a game you have to pay for. Since game companies don't appear to be in a big hurry to send me free games it looks like I will never do a real review.

Instead what I am going to do is just give you my thoughts on the game. You see, I am avoiding the 'no review if you buy the game' rule by calling it thoughts instead. Pretty clever eh? Anyway, enough with this babble, let's talk about No One Lives Forever.

No One Lives Forever (from here forth it will be called NOLF) is a FPS from the fine folks over at Fox Interactive. It is run on the Lithtech engine, which is most notable for being the constant butt of jokes for Lowtax.

The first word that comes to mind when I think of the game is 'Fun'. NOLF is one of the more enjoyable games I have played in recent memory. The best compliment I can give it is that it was probably more fun then Half-Life, in my opinion anyway. There are just a ton of cool missions and things to do. Right off the bat your first mission involves sitting in a window and sniping the enemy while trying to protect someone. Games need more sniping in them. Sniping = Fun. From there you go on missions at such places as airplanes, trains, sunken ships and, of course, warehouses. I know, almost every game has warehouse type levels. But not every game has levels that involve you plummeting from a plain with no parachute.

The voice acting in this game is also top notch. Which is good because the game has a lot of it. I can't tell you how much time I spent crouched behind crates listening to the NPC's talk to each other. They often have a long, humerous stories for you to listen in on. They talk about their job, their love life and their fear of being killed by some super spy. Sometimes the stories are a tad too long but that can be forgiven. In between each mission you get briefed back at base by your bosses. These scenes drag on and on. There comes a point where you just want to get back to the action but you don't want to miss the cut scenes. I wouldn't minded too much if they had cut just a bit of time out of them. The cut scenes do make the movie seem almost like an interactive movie (I would not be one bit surprised to see a NOLF movie eventually either).

The graphics in the game are pretty good, just not mindblowingly good. I feel bad for those of you that are unable to play with FSAA because this game benefits from it big time (as do most games, but that is besides the point). But it isn't the graphics that make this game, it is the fun time you have playing it.

I haven't tried the multiplayer aspect of the game yet. I've read it isn't so good. If I want to do online play I think I will stick with the tried and true Half-Life TFC (Yes, I will try CounterStrike again eventually).

All-in-all I can't recommend NOLF enough. If you are looking for a good FPS to play then give it a try. It's a fun game that doesn't take itself too seriously. Plus Cate Archer is a major babe...well, as far as computer babes go anyway. I need to get out more...

***Patch Day***

We're happy to present today's patch, which focuses primarily on addressing issues that have been brought forth by EverQuest players. We appreciate the opportunity to spend some time focusing on specific issues important to you. Today's patch includes the following:

This would be as opposed to the normal patches. You know, the ones that are released with the sole purpose of annoying the hell out of the player base.

- The "Resistant" Discipline now lasts 5 minutes instead of the previous 1.

Root-type spells and "snare" type spells now use a separate spell effect.

When I got my first PC back in 1996 or so the first online game I got into was a racing game called Pod. You could log onto a free service and race against people from across the world. Eventually I got pretty good at the game and a nice guy that went by the name Shiv asked me if I was interested in joining his race team. Friday night I got a message on ICQ. "Burr, it's a sad day for the Pod alums. Shiv died on Tuesday."

The message floored me. I just really couldn't believe it was true. After reading and writing about the big suicide hoax from EQ I just figured it couldn't be real. I realized that I was going through the normal grieving process and that losing this person was like losing anyone else in life. It's not easy to deal with the loss but life has to go on.

So Shiv, wherever you are, thanks for taking a chance on a newbie who was still learning the game. I'll never forget the opportunity you gave me. It was always fun racing with or against you. I'll always have those memories and you to blame for getting me hooked on online games.

Well in space and on ships (the games setting) you usually don't have very open places, as a matter of fact most of the battles are in corridors so your only option is to back up and start wasting them while going back. Well, you backup into 3 of your friends who don't know your trying to get past them so they just stand there (until you die, obviously)... or... you manage to push past them only to walk into a wall or hit a corner or something where your ready to make a stand. So you duck down, take aim, only to find you got 3 morons standing in front of you wondering what ya doin... since you can't shoot past them (the hitmodels in this game are THAT bad) you can't do much but watch as the enemy runs up to ya. Then you gotta try and pick the enemy off as it pushes between your allies to get you (enemy comes after you 90% of the time, usually ignoring your allies). Whats worse is the fact your allies have a TON of health, you can't make them wait or stop, and they do NO damage when they finally figure out how to fire their weapons (they take a short cig break between each fire). You would think on the hardest setting your allies would do no damage and die in the first 5 minutes of the level... sooooo untrue. In this game your allies take a SHITLOAD of damage before finally croaking.

All this combined really sucks, your allies are almost invincible, like to stand infront of your gun, have no brain, do no damage, and can't be killed/put away by you. This is not how a game is supposed to be hard. Most of my deaths were caused because of my allies.

Some of it is fun though, some of the battles are organized VERY well and the enemy WILL take cover if the situation calls for it. Don't mistake this for good AI though. When you play through one of these awesome looking battles it becomes obvious that the AI is taking cover behind barricades and such because the designers of the game told the AI to specifically use that object for cover. Your average every day AI in this game will resemble the AI of Wolfenstien3d (Does it get worse?). As single player went it got more and more boring. Seemed like I was doing the same thing over and over. No choice of direction. The maps were basically this,

Point A. - Big elaborate one-way tunnel with elevators and turns and shit - Point B.

In other words, there was only one-way from point a to point b and you FELT it. It didn't seem like you could go any other way, all doors you passed were locked. Just a one way tunnel from the beginning of the level to the end of the level. Toward the end, the game becomes very easy. Once you get your hands on the hard hitters and learn how to deal with your stupid ass allies the game is no longer challenging.

Multiplayer. OMG. Multiplayer version of this game IS Quake3. Same damn thing. I don't personally care for Quake3. Takes less skill than most FPS games in my opinion. How much like Quake3 is this game? On my second game with around 15 people playing I made an outrageous score. Lets be realistic people. Your first few times playing ANY game multiplayer you should NOT win. This game has the Quad damage and everything. Its not based on the Quake3 engine. It is Quake3.

My complaints with its Multiplayer version? Well obviously, the fact that its not original. Another complaint that I have with Quake3 (and thus, with this game) is the fact that everyone is soooooooo tuff. Only 2 weapons that can kill a person in a decent amount of time. The rest of them require a great deal of work. Hell, the sniper rifle only takes about HALF the person’s life. Come on! The sniper rifle is supposed to be a one hit kill weapon... or a weapon that does a shitload of damage and then you switch to automatic to finish the job. This is why I believe Quake3 becomes soooo chaotic (all the quake games are but q3 is bad). In a game like AQ2 or CStrike if you see someone off to the side of you, you panic. You know your dead (less they suck). You can't afford to just go charging all over the place, you have to THINK "could there be a sniper on that roof? And if there is, wtf do I do about it?". In Quake3 you can soak up soooo much damage (Specially with some of the shields and armor) you can almost ignore the enemy if your just trying to capture the flag and shit (if you get the speed boots you CAN just ignore them, your to fast to hit enough to get a kill)...

I'll probably play it a little more in hopes of finding something about the game that’s original but in all likeliness the game will probably be shelved in a few days.
