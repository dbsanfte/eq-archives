SAN RAFAEL, Calif.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Nov. 29, 2000--LucasArts Entertainment Company LLC today announced Star Wars® Galaxies(TM) as the formal series brand name for their massively multiplayer online role-playing games. On November 30 LucasArts, in conjunction with Sony Online Entertainment Inc., will launch the game's official information site, www.starwarsgalaxies.com.

The Star Wars Galaxies Web site will debut with three active community relations message boards and a list of frequently asked questions fielded by the game's development team. Each message board will provide a forum for feedback to and from the Star Wars Galaxies' team in the following areas: A General Discussion Board for all game-related issues, a Developers Board for game development queries, and a Role Playing Board for ``in character'' discussion.

``We see this Web site as an important step toward building a strong community for the Star Wars Galaxies line of games,'' says Simon Jeffery, president of LucasArts. ``We firmly believe that consistent and open communication with fans will be one of the keys to the success of the Star Wars Galaxies experience.''

``We're thrilled to provide Star Wars fans and the Station's users with a forum that will generate enthusiasm for the game,'' says Kelly Flock, president of Sony Online Entertainment. ``This is one way fans can be involved in the creative process of developing the highly anticipated Star Wars Galaxies online series of games.''

The Star Wars Galaxies game series is being developed by Verant Interactive, a division of Sony Online Entertainment, and will be distributed and sold at retail by LucasArts and available for play exclusively online at The Station@sony.com ( www.station.sony.com ). The game is expected to go into first round testing in late 2001.

-- Pixie Strike: Made it less resistible by reducing the secondary resist.
-- Song of Twilight: Increased duration. Made less resistible by removing the secondary resist. Allowed it to be cast at any time of day.
-- Fixed the effect on the Necromancer Epic Quest Item. The effect was checking resist on each tick instead of running full course.
-- Increased the duration of the effect on the Enchanter Epic Quest Item.
-- Fixed the "Modulating Rod" so that it can be used by the appropriate races.
-- Fixed the Lost Staff of the Scorned
-- Maniacal Strength will now correctly overwrite the level 49 strength spell.
-- The Death Pact and Divine Intervention spells will now stack appropriately with other spells that have similar effects.

-- Modified the "Jonathan's" line of songs to remove the "skin" effect in order to allow bards to use them more effectively in combat. In addition, we made them "Self Only" spells rather than "Single Target" spells in order to allow bards to remain in combat while singing them.

*General Fixes/Changes*

-- Fixed a bug that caused an NPC on "Sister's Isle" to attack people who were fighting pirates.
-- Removed Paladins as an authorized class for piercing weapons in game since they do not have the piercing skill.

-- Also removed Rangers as an authorized class in general, since most consider them useless.

-- The "Belt of the River" quest should now work correctly.

-- The "Suspenders of the River" quest, however, is still broken.

-- Fixed all known problems with the class-specific epic quests.

-- That isn't to say that there are a slew of unknown problems with class-specific epic quests. These will be addressed at a later date.

-- Implemented some new race-specific quests for Iksar.

-- We think you will find the 100 meter dash race quest very enjoyable.

-- Positively modified most Epic Quest Items. Please check your items to see your changes. Note: Not all changes will be evident by looking at the item.

-- "We did not nerf them. I repeat, we did not nerf them."

-- Fixed the NPC bosses in the Plane of Sky that were not dropping the correct loot.
-- Your bread tin will now be returned when baking bread.

-- This is more environmentally sound. The previous disposable bread tins were just littering up the landscape.

-- Fixed the Wellford Fargin keyword problem in Firiona Vie.
-- Made the "Gravebinder" a magic weapon.

-- Repaired the Forager Bag. It was causing people to lose weight when placing items in the bag. It is supposed to have a weight reduction component, but was not supposed to cause weight loss.

-- If you would like to lose weight we would recommend Jenny Craig or Slimfast. Perhaps even Deal-A-Meal.

-- Taunt: Increased effectiveness above level 50. Previously, creatures above level 50 could not be taunted by means of the taunt skill. Now, most creatures below the character's level can now be successfully taunted. As a note, the chance to taunt a creature above 50th level is smaller than that of a creature below 50th.


*Client Enhancements*

-- You now have 10 pages of 10 hotboxes each when in full screen mode.
-- You can now customize 10 pages of 12 socials each, increasing the total number of available socials by 108.
-- Fixed a bug with the keyboard.txt file causing some keys to translate incorrectly.
-- Fixed a bug that caused some window positions to save improperly at higher resolutions.
-- All key mappings are now stored in the EQCLIENT.INI file.

-- Fixed all known problems with the clipping plane slider. Users should now see differences for the entire range of adjustment in zones that support an adjustable clipping plane.

-- Players on PvP servers will no longer show "[LD]" over their heads when linkdead. In addition, these same players will no longer show as "LinkDead" in the /who list.

-- Players can now set custom text colors while in game. /usercolor: Usage "/usercolor UserColorNumber R G B" where "UserColorNumber is the number of the color as indicated in the EQCLIENT.INI file, and R, G, and B are red, green and blue respectively. All changes will be automatically saved to your EQCLIENT.INI file.

-- OPT files in your EverQuest directory now have a number after the character name indicating the server number. As such, you will now be able to have characters of the same name on two separate servers with different custom options.

-- The eqlog.txt file now has a name that includes the server number and character name. For instance, if your character is named "Bobb", and you are on Server 1, your log text file name would be eqlog_01_Bobb.txt.

-- Added two new entries to the "Defaults" section of the EQCLIENT.INI file: AttackOnAssist=TRUE|FALSE - Sets whether or not your autoattack turns on automatically when assisting. Defaults to "TRUE". ShowInspectMessage=TRUE|FALSE - Sets whether or not you see the "Soandso is inspecting you" message. Defaults to "TRUE".

The Fragility of Life Posted Tuesday, November 14, 2000 by Buur
With the info I have received from a couple of different sources, I feel pretty confident in saying that the story Lum broke is, unfortunately, true.

I never knew Sheyla personally. Very few of us did. From what has been said so far we can determine that she had a rough life. Her mother died 4 years ago and she had to go through the stresses of living with a foster family. Then she became a mother herself at the age of 16. She was relieved of her child when she was deemed unfit to be a mother. She was in the midst of a custody battle to get her child back. Not the type of things you would expect to have to deal with at the age of 18. That is the time you should be thinking about graduating high school and having fun with your friends. The real world shouldn't have such a tight grip on you.

We also have heard and seen that she was a very talented artist. She also was a wonderful singer.

Unfortunately, to her, the bad outweighed the good and she took her own life last weekend, shortly after being removed from the EQ guide program. Whether the two things are related isn't important. This isn't an excuse to flame Verant and I am hesitant to even mention them in this story. The sorrow in all this is that a young woman saw no hope in her life and rather then fight through it, she ended it.

I can't explain why this story has touched me so much. I will just say that I am well aware of the fragility of life. I have been down before, very down, but I have always had someone to turn to when all hope seemed lost and all life seemed unimportant. Shelya appears to not have had anyone to turn to, or just chose not to turn to anyone.

Life is fragile. You may have everything going right for you and no worries in the world. That could all change tomorrow. Maybe the person you sit next to every day but never talk to is going through something similar to what Shelya went through. Perhaps they have no one to turn to and feel lost and that all hope is gone. Next time you see them ask them how they are doing, show that you care a little bit. Something as simple as that could make a world of difference in someone's life.

Maybe some day they will be there to return the favor.
