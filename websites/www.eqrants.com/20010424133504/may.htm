People that bought the expansion late were left with none of the good stuff.

This is what the conspiracy theorists believe.

What do I believe? Do you care? Tough if you don't cause I am going to tell you anyway.

I believe that this is a result of the carelessness that Verant has exhibited way too many times before. Kunark was released before it was ready. The problem is that most of just accept it because we have been conditioned to accept sub par support and products. As for the conspiracy theory. It is entirely possible that they did do all that stuff. Wouldn't surprise me one bit.

Thursday, May 18th

Is EQ for the Casual Gamer?

Over on the "Official" Everquest message boards someone asked Abashi if EQ was designed with the casual gamer in mind.

I can answer that one. Hell no. Any game that requires two weeks worth of camping to get a good item isn't meant for casual gamers. Does that mean that casual gamers can't enjoy the game. No again. I am a casual gamer. I play maybe an hour or two every couple of nights. I log on, meet up with a friend and we kill a few things and try not to get too far ahead of each other in levels. We generally have a fairly good time. Do we hold any hopes that we can get one of the extremely rare items. Hell no. I don't have the time to sit and camp something for a week or two, I have what you might want to call a life. I work 40+ hours a week, I go to school, I have many hobbies outside of gaming. Should I be punished for that? Apparently so. The people that play the game 10+ hours a day get the good stuff. That is just the way it is. Do I wish I could get some of the good stuff. You're damn right I do. I'd love to have J-Boots or some other rare item. But the game just isn't designed that way. Abashi suggests this:

The best advice I could offer to people in your prediciment (his spelling not mine) would be to play the game first, and see what items you get, rather than deciding what items you want, and then playing the game to that end. Discover the items through your normal travels rather than scheduling your travels around items you want. You might find this path a bit more rewarding.

The problem with that logic is that anything worth having is already camped. You don't randomly kill an orc and get a fiery avenger. I don't find it all that rewarding to collect a bunch of spider silk in my random travels. 

Now having said all that, I don't think everyone should have access to every item. It wouldn't be unique if every player could get all the good items. It's just a shame that they can't find a way to balance it so that players who only play 5-6 hours a week could get some of the stuff that the uber-campers get. I'd suggest a way to balance it but that isn't my job. My job is to bitch about the fact that Verant hasn't balanced it.

But I want good equipment too!!
