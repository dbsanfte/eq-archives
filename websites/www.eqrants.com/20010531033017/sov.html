Scars of Velious Review


Overview

Scars of Velious is an exciting expansion for the very successful MMOG Everquest. It includes new features such as snow, ice, snow and ice related creatures and new game play bugs. There are no new playable races or classes in SoV and the level cap is still at 60. Basically what you are getting is an expansion for high level characters. 

The Premise

The Gnomish, god bless 'em, built an icebreaker ship to travel through the frozen sea south of Odus (not to be confused with Odie, the lovable dog from Garfield). They found the lost continent of Velious (it's amazing how lost continents continue to pop up) and decided that exploring it would be a good idea. What they found was a continent full of ice, dragons and a lost Dwarfish tribe. The dwarves had a flourishing town at one point but it had long been destroyed by ice giants (as opposed to fire giants, which will probably be making a cameo appear on the next lost continent). They had since moved to underground caves to avoid the dangers. I am thinking I should have moved to an underground cave to avoid this farce of a review. 

Gameplay

Having not actually played SoV I am going to have to venture some guesses here. I am just going to assume that gameplay is pretty much the same repetitive waste of time that EQ always has been. According to a few of the other reviews I have read it sounds like Verant tried to make it not quite as boring as it normally is. Some of the NPC's apparently now have better personalities and are prone to make a joke here or there. I don't know that the humor is on the same level as say, Monkey Island, but the effort is there. Well, so I read anyway.

The quests are apparently a little better this time too. I am hoping that they currently all work as intended, but I am not putting any money on it.

In the end, this is still EverQuest. Uber guilds are going to camp all the good spawns and items and the solo player is going to get screwed. Wait a minute, you can't solo in EQ. Nevermind. The players that don't belong to uber guilds are going to get screwed.

Graphics

The graphics are pretty much the same. The EQ engine is definitely starting to show it's age. While some of the new creatures do look decent, you can't help but notice the boxy edges. Some Anti-aliasing lovin' really helps this game out quite a bit.

Sound

Who the hell cares.

Overclockability

As hard as I tried I couldn't get EverQuest: Scars of Velious to overclock successfully. All attempts rendered my PC useless. Much like AOL 6.0

My recommendation is to not try to overclock SoV unless you are extremely comfortable with overclocking.

Verdict

EverQuest: SoV is just more regurgitated basic EQ. If you are a perma-lowbie like me then this expansion will do you no good. Anyone lower then level 30 will get nothing out of SoV, save for repeated deaths. If you are a high level player then SoV might be right up your alley. More challenging creatures, new quests and new zones are all there for your enjoyment. If you are level 40+ then go there immediately. Quit hogging up all the old lands. Players like me still need them to level up.

Score

2 / 5 - No real innovation. Just the same thing over and over again. Die hard EQ players will love it. Casual players shouldn't bother.
