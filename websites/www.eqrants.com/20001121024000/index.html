Can This Be Over Please? Posted Friday, November 17, 2000 by Buur
I just got word that this whole Sheyla thing has definitely been a work. My source on this story spoke with Sheyla's mother on the phone and her mother says Sheyla definitely alive. As for the person that pulled off the hoax, well they are alive too. A bit on the warped side, but alive.

I don't know that Vince McMahon and the WWF could put together something like this. It has truly been an odd week.

This Is Getting Weird Posted Thursday, November 16, 2000 by Buur
Two days after proclaiming that I had enough evidence to proclaim the Sheyla story as true, we are going to go ahead and push it back to the hoax stage. I am getting word that this thing is quite possibly a very elaborate hoax. One that started over a year ago and had one person assuming the identity of 5 different people.

Either way, hoax or no hoax, this is one messed up story. I will post more info on this as I get it.
