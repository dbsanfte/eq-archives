PlanetSide FAQ Update Posted Wednesday, February 21, 2001 by WhiteFox
PlanetSide FAQ was updated earlier today with five new questions.

* What happens when you die?
Players will have the ability to choose where they respawn, although the length of time that must elapse before a respawn can be initiated has yet to be determined.

* What if the enemy camps out at respawn points?
Multiple respawn locations will be available to minimize this problem. If all else fails, players will also be able to respawn at their Main Base which almost always will be safe from enemy attack.

* Will there be vehicles in PlanetSide?
Yes, player controlled vehicles will be available in PlanetSide. The emphasis will be on land vehicles, but there will be the ability to travel by air.

* How will the boarding process work for vehicles?
Mounting vehicles will be a very intuitive process utilizing a "Visual Mounting System" approach. For every position available on a vehicle, a colored ring will be cast on the terrain adjacent to the vehicle. Above this ring will be a text label (Gunner, Driver, Passenger). The soldier simply walks into the ring of choice and hits an action key to board the vehicle. Before a soldier can select the gunner or passenger position, there must be a driver. Gunner and passenger rings will appear a soon as the driver is mounted. This approach greatly enhances the quality of vehicle mounting animations. Since the position of these rings will be pre-determined, highly detailed animations of a soldier boarding a vehicle can be constructed.

* Multiple players can board the same vehicle?
Certain vehicles will have multiple player slots, although only one player can drive at a time. In some cases, players can choose to operate gunnery positions.

Vehicles don't sound to bad but I don't know about the spawn system they described. Sounds like it has a lot of opening for spawn campers. I can imagine someone sitting over a spawn with a one hit kill sniper rifle...

Lunar: The Silver Star Posted Friday, February 9, 2001 by WhiteFox
Yup, I finished the PlayStation version of Lunar1 Feb 8, 2001 at around 2am.

Going to do me a little review now! If you don't want spoilers and all that shit don't read. If you read anyway but didn't want spoilers, e-mail me at YouNewbieDumbass@ShitForBrains.com. I'm also assuming in this that you've played one of the goddamn versions (not that it’s a req. for understanding).

Anyway, I've got to say I'm impressed with the goddamn box. Holy shit, you don't see this kind of quality packaging every fucking day in the console biz. This things a goddamn collectors item! Hardcover instruction book in full color! Can't say that for most console games! Making of CD, Outtakes, music track cd... all kinds of neat shit!

On to the important part! The game. Originally I thought this remake would try and stay as true to the original game as possible, just a nice graphics upgrade. Well, I was wrong. The graphics have been upgraded, but not as much as I thought they would be. In fact, graphics for world map, towns, caves and such remain the same. The battle graphics have been upgraded, spells look better... in some cases. Nash for example, his lightning attack just plain sucks, it looks like shit. The original SegaCD version did a much better job with the spells, they had more char… its hard to explain. They just looked better. I'll go into this later...

The cut scenes... well, there is just no comparison. The cut scenes in the original were usually just still pictures, not much animation. In the playstation version there is around an hour of anime. Lots of talking, LOTS. While in the original you were lucky to ever hear a single word, they stuffed a shitload of singing, talking, and all that good stuff on this version.

Battle system, this is where I'm the least impressed. While Nash and Mia are no longer TOTALLY useless at the end of the game (I remember leaving mia dead for like the last half of the game in the original, she just wasn't worth the MP to rez/heal) they are VERY limited in spells. This game use to be PACKED with spells, tons and tons and tons and tons of spells, most of them useless, but they gave you options! Shit you could do when you got bored, and they all looked different! Every fucking one of ‘em! Now each char has a max of like 8 spells, and some of them don't even reach that. This gets VERY old. You'll go 4 or 5 hours where each char fires off only one spell over and over and over. The already unimpressive animation upgrades must be watched, over and over and over.

This brings me to my next complaint, RUNNING. I didn't always feel like fighting the random enemies that had no chance against me but felt it necessary to attack me anyway. Two ways to run in this improved version. The first is to click "run" at the main menu, telling all of your men to run automatically. This feature is USELESS. If you hit this, you'll get away like 1 in 15 attempts. Other way to run is to go down into every chars menu and manually tell each one to run. This will always get half or more of your chars away, but always leaves one or two behind, insuring you lose him/time.

They removed random encounters and added little enemy figures, if you bump into em/they run your ass down you go to the battle screen. I thought this was cool... thought. You can't push through monsters, so in the event you do run from the monster, you get a few seconds of invincibility (your escape time) but usually you can't push past the monster because it blocks you, or worse yet, another monster sandwiches you. Insuring that you have to fight two battles before you can return to your task. I've got to say, I'm unimpressed entirely with the battle system upgrades. The spells look worse (what spells remain that is) and its been made far to repetitive.

That’s not why people play Lunar though is it (Majority of the spoilers ahead)? People play Lunar to enjoy the story! The game plays out like a goddamn movie now, it surely does. Lots of cut scenes, drama, love, romance, people who piss you the fuck off! The story has gotten a major overhaul, while its still true to the basic order of things, how those things “get done” has been altered. Example, Luna isn’t left in Saith like she was in the beginning of the original… in fact, she accompanies you for at least half the game before finally being taken away. To say the least, this is a MAJOR change in the plot. Because of this one simple change, the city of Burg isn’t enslaved, a whole shitload of dialog is introduced, situations, the other chars of the game actually know who the fuck Luna is when she is kidnapped. She isn’t just “Oh yea, Luna… that’s Alex’s chick isn’t she?”

Vain puts up a fight in this version, it doesn’t just get blown out of the sky like it did in the original. Lots of changes… hell, new enemies are introduced. Royce and Phacia were never in the original (although they are pretty good additions in my opinion). So with all these changes, whats different about the chars? Well… Ghaleon has lost a lot of his charm. While he still a pretty good villain, I don’t think he is as good as he was in the original. In the original, Ghaleon had purpose! The bastard had motive! The fucker had ambition! In the original Ghaleon went insane when his best friend, Dyne, was killed while defending the Goddess Althena. He wanted revenge, revenge against the Goddess, the Dragons that protected the Goddess, and the world that Dyne had to die for. In this upgraded version, Dyne never dies and Ghaleon knows it (in the original he believed he was dead, duh). Instead Ghaleon goes after his crazy scheme fueled not by the need for revenge, but by jealousy of Dyne and Goddess… to me this just isn’t a good motive.

He still a pretty kick ass bad guy though. The fact that Luna is really a Goddess doesn’t seem to mean as much. In the original, Ghaleon enslaved Luna, and used her power to shoot Vain out of the sky all the way from the Frontier. In this remake, Ghaleon just drives the Grindery over to Vain and points a big ass gun at it. While Luna is still a Goddess, she never really has a good chance to demonstrate the power that Ghaleon is taking. She never does anything that makes you go “Well shit… hope Ghaleon doesn’t get his hands on THAT particular power!”

The Grindery itself… it was an awesome machine in the original. A fortress. Ghaleon used it as a tank, driving around and commanding Althena to fire at targets from the top of it. In this version, Ghaleon relies on mechanical guns mixed with magic. Still kick ass and all but the damn thing has the range of a squirt gun. It’s basically crippled in its first skirmish with Vain. While it still destroyed Vain, it took a pretty good hit in the process and its all because the bastard had to drive all the way up to Vain to fire… In the original the Goddess basically fired beam of energy across a few continents and wasted the city. Not like anyone knew wtf happened. Vain didn’t put up a fight… Vain didn’t even know someone was shooting at it till it was goin down! COME ON! The fact that Ghaleon made Althena do this to one of the best cities in the game just *SCREAMED* “I’m one kick ass Villain, and yes, I know it.”

Kyle… the man has more than two lines in the remake. They did a good job on Kyle in my opinion. Every time he opens his mouth you can guarantee something stupid is about to come out and your just gonna snicker your ass off.

Nall is STILL the shit. The best line in the remake?

Nall: “Kyle, you suck.”

All in all, I think the upgraded version is just as enjoyable as the original, more so really. The hour of anime just tops it off, this is the modern version. Still, there is something to be said for the original…

I could really dissect this game (I just fnished my 6th playing of the game for Christ sake) but I’m not gonna do that… I need sleep. I've started Lunar2, can expect my comments on it as soon as its been defeated.

Anyway, if anyone’s been around long enough to know Lunar… lets have some comments.
