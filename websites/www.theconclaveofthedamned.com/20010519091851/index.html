    City of Mist Raid
    Malikorr writes "Hail Brethren!

    We are mounting an expedition into Kunark, to the fabled City of Mist. The city was once the pinnacle of Iksar construction, and my scouts report that it is still impressive to this date, however it is also highly dangerous. This expedition will require our mightiest members, and will certainly provide great challenge and greater reward to our expeditionary force.

    We shall embark from the Overthere Outpost at precisely 3pm EST for the City, which lies deep within the Emerald Jungle. The trip will be perilous, and the destination even more so, but with a solid force victory will surely be ours!

    OOC:
    As I'll be gone this coming weekend, here's the event for the 20th.

    By request, it's a foray into the City of Mist. Should be interesting to say the least lol, as (1) never been there and (2) no maps available.

    According to eqatlas drops are pretty random, so it should be fun.

    This one is strictly level 30+, as the city itself is listed as 35+ lvl mobs.

    With decent turnout (which we always have with higher lvls) we should do ok near the entry at least. No clue how much farther in we can go, we'll just have to see.

    Looks like we'll be running the route: OT-WW-Field of Bone-Emerald Jungle-City of Mist.

    See ya then.

    Malikorr Mistweaver
    Lore Master, Conclave of the Damned
