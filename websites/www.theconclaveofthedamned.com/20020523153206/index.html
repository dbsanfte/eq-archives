    A bad dream...
    Malikorr writes "Tossing and turning in, sweat forming on his troubled brow, Malikorr kicked off his blanket in his sleep. Obviously, the Lore Master of the Conclave was being troubled by some most disturbing dreams...

    Standing atop a ridge, enshrouded in mist, Malikorr saw images swirling in the haze surrounding him. Innoruuk he instantly recognized, glaring down at him with eyes filled with hate. The disdain in that stare was tangible, disdain for a dark elf who had forsaken him and chosen to follow no religious path.

    Next he saw Tunare, Nature's Whore, smiling at him sadly, feeling sorrow not for Malikorr but for all her children he had slain. Her sadness turned to rage and lightnings danced about her, lashing out at but never quite touching Malikorr.

    Finally Malikorr saw swirling, not quite formed face who image never solidified. At this sight Malikorr knew fear, for he was looking at it's master. As he turned from this face the mists closed in, oppresive in their weight upon his soul. Surely this was the end of his existence, the "gods" he had refused to pay lip service to all his life had come to teach him respect.

    With all seeming lost, his will giving in to his inevitable fate, Malikorr dropped to his knees, the physical weight of the mists forcing him downward. Suddenly, with a cry that lifted his heart, a booming ogre voice was heard in the distance, muffled by the mists.

    "Rally to the Lore Master! Forward the Conclave!" Other voices could be heard echoing the first. Malikorr recognized the voices of his comrades, but there were other voices amongst them, voices not of the Conclave, but somehow recognizable though they escaped his memory.

    At the intrusion, the mists swirled even faster, bearing down on Malikorr. As the foreign voices drew closer, he was stunned to make out a clear shout of "For honor! Forward the Vanguard!"

    Thinking all certainly lost with the soldiers of light coming to aid in his destruction as well, Malikorr snarled with the rage of a cornered animal, angered and confused.

    Suddenly, without warning, the mist itself screamed and began to disipate. Standing there, surrounding Malikorr, was the combined host of the Conclave and Vanguard. Rather than warring, as he certainly expected, they were united with a common goal. A goal of standing up to the gods themselves. Shaking his head in disbelief, Malikorr blinked expecting the image to fade away, and yet it remained. The Conclave and Vanguard united...

    Waking with a start, Malikorr scrambled out of bed and went to his desk, uncovering the wisp stone kept there for better light to write by. Certainly this strange dream was not a dream, but a sight of things to come. Combined, with the cursed army of goodness, perhaps the Conclave could conquor the gods themselves.

    Aligning with the light was certainly the most ludicrous thought Malikorr, who prized logic above all else, had ever had. However, using the Vanguard towards the Conclave's ends, then discarding them like the trash they are... Now that was a thought befitting his heritage.

    Wars end
    gmog writes "The battles had been fought the fallen had been disposed of, and the Conclave of the Damned stood strong. They had defended their homelands and taken the fight to the very gates of Felwithe. None could deny that they had bested their foes. A Teir’Dal in the Blind Fish could be overheard telling the stories he had heard, they went like this.

    “Those Vanguard started it I say….. I saw it with my own two eyes, I was heading to Lavastorm on an errand for my master…. When I see a rather large group of Conclave at the Boarder of the forest. Being cautious I stayed back a bit. I saw mighty magic’s cast and Ogres, Trolls and others disappeared before my eyes. The next thing I see is a Wizard emerges from the haze, I heard battle cries and he fell before my eyes. Quickly others of the light races emerged, yet they drew a hasty retreat as the mighty Conclave beat them soundly. I heard a few fell from the Vanguard in that battle but I watched as the one they called Yanosh fell first.”

    “Well many of us saw the battle in East Commons Tunnels”, chimes in another patron. “There must have been 40 of them from either side, the battle was fast and furious, venders and costumers alike were getting caught in the crossfire. I was by Shady when I first started hearing things, a young dwarf ran by yelling that trouble was brewing, and the mighty Vanguard of Norrath was amassing further down the tunnel. I didn’t have time to even move when I saw more Trolls Ogres and the like around me than I had before in my life. I had just ducked to safety when I heard the clash of swords.” His tone turns sour, and it sounds as if he’s spiting the words out, “ It was then that I saw…. The HONERABLE forces of the Vanguard concentrate their efforts on the Warlord Gmog. There was so much magic cast at one time I lost all sight of what was happening, but when the light show and all the noise stopped I saw many dead.” Voice dropping some, “Among them was the Warlord.”…………. Returns to normal bar voice, “ It had cost the Vanguard dearly though I counted no fewer than 6 more bodies and only one being Teir’Dal”

    A beautiful Teir’Dal woman speaks up. “My friend runs errands for the Mistress Llarasandra…… She told me of the next day. The Mistress had stayed up all night with the generals of the Conclave, who had retreated back to our fair city of Neriak to regroup. She had vowed that the very gates of Felwithe would be scorched for the loss of Gmog. She personally lead a raiding party into the very lands of the Far’Dal, and extinguished the lives of a score of the Vanguard as the main army amassed for the battle to come. That eve she honored her pledge and led an assault on the gates of Felwithe itself. Leaving more Vanguard lying dead, and few Conclave.” “A the day was truly won by the Mistress and the Conclave.” A cheer is heard and more drinking happens.

    A scared older Teir’Dal, who was clearly an aged veteran of the guard spoke up next. “Ahhhh but the Vanguard resorted to rebel tactics the next morn. They divided into small raiding parties and caught no less than four of the Damned off guard. They regrouped but not in large enough forces. An epic battle happened that day in the Nektulos forest and numbers of both sides fell. With a final battle that I will never forget.” He drifts off for second then speaks again. “ I was fishing off the bridge when I saw the Old Troll Zkana run past, an Earth Elemental was close behind. She stood her ground and I saw a Magician, I would later learn was Malakuron, the battle between these two was awesome to behold. I saw Zkana fall, and thought it was over….. but as the Magician walked away I saw him stager, once then twice, he grabbed his side fell to a knee and finally fell over dead. It was the greatest battle this old man had ever seen.” He raises is glass in a silent toast and drains it.

    “But alas, with the strange rumors of the reemergence of Gmog and the others who had fallen, a strange peace has settled once again………. Who knows when it’ll explode again……..” With that a quite settles over the bar and people go back to drinking. "
