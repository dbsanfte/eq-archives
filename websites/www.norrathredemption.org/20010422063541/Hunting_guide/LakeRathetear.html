Guide to Lake Rathetear by Narayan



Lake Rathetear - In this lake there are several spaces where you can camp solo or in a group and soak up the XP. First at the entrance to the Arena sit 2 Aviak Guards (will provide XP till around 24). These can be pulled one at a time and you are very close to a zone if you need to run.

If you follow the wall to the right of the arena, past the barbarians, you will find a treetop aviak house. In here spawn 2 guards. Again, these guards are level 21ish so you need a group of 2 22's to be safe. They spawn every 5 minutes. If you can, try and break the spawn up. Root one, kill his friend, then kill the rooted birdie. This means that next time you only have to deal with one bird.  There are also a couple of sites like this on the islands in the middle of the lake.

Just past those birdies you will find a lone madman who spawns in a hut on a pier. He is level 21ish, spawns every 6 minutes, has no faction hits and drops only water along with the occasional magical research item. He is a monk so tends to hit fast... sor pure casters you will want to root him down as if he starts hitting you you will probably not get a cast off.

Finally there is the small gnoll camp at the beginning of the zone. At level 20 there will be things here ranging from white to red... so pull carefully and back away from the camp. Watch the shamen, they root a lot and can ruin your day. Even at 25 you will still get XP off some of the caster skellies who spawn at night. Below 23 you should probably stick to hunting here in the day (unless in a group) and over 23 hunt here at night.
