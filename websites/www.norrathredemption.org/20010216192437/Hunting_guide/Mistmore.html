Howdy all (Hawking),

I have read the two guides below and found them to be most informative. I will try to post my own guide to Mistmoore, but I warn you that it is nowhere as comprehensive as these 2.

This is a level 20+ zone. The least they hit for is (on their most powerful hits) is around 40.

most common baddies (in order of increasing difficulty): Pledge Familiar, Initiate Familiar, Glyphend Familiar. ( I was soloing pledge familiars at lvl 24 or so). Watch out, some of them are casters!

When you enter from L.Fay, you turn a corner and go down the ramp. on the ledge on the right spawns a baddie(one of the 3 mentioned above). Further down there is a lake and there is about 6 baddies that spawn around it. To the right is a tunnel and baddies spawn all throughout it. When you exit the tunnel you come upon a graveyard (GY)on the right and a second tunnel to the left. Within the GY a house (dont go in, its a trap). beside the tombstones there are spawn spots, which generate tougher creatures (glyphed aegis, glyphed sentry) which range from level 28 to 31. In the left corner behind the house is a secret door, which leads to a room with 3 or 4 doors, open the door on the right and they all open. within, more bad guys.

The KEY thing to remember when camping in the GY is to never let anything get away from your group and down the second tunnel!!!!!!!!!!! ( I dont know how I can stress this enough). Down there is the courtyard of the castle and contains VERY bad things ( red to lvl 30). about 6 of them come if you let something go down there.

I enjoyed soloing there for a long time, but the most important thing is to have SoW if you are alone. Hp go down supprisingly fast and the baddies have backstab which hits for over 100. Also the trains may not be very big, but they are amongst the deadliest in the game.

Some interesting drops are a wicked dagger (sorry dont know name or stats) that a fire imp drops. (I know its better that the dirk most wizards use).

Hope this helps ( if it does not make sense thats because it is 2 am here)



Hawking aka Taxi

I'm a veteran of mistmoore, stayed there for a couple of levels. I have heard people from here are going there or there now. Just some pointers from someone who was there alot.

The Imp Familar drops a Sacrifical Dagger that has 5damage +5 intelligence with a very low delay. Typical of any dagger. I gave the dagger to my Enchanter, very nice weapon. it also dropped ringmail gloves.

The mistmoore Shield is probably one of the best shields i've seen, i've held on to two of them so far. One I gave to a Barbarian Shaman named Somee (she quit playing somee and now plays rhaul) Rhaul is lvl 19 Cleric in full banded with screaming mace and Mistmoore Shield. The second time i got a hold of that shield i gave to a paladin named Drantos who is now lvl 32. I still group with him alot. The stats on the shield are so numerous that the text screen was too short to contain it. VERY nice shield. The glyphed guard dressed in what looks like chain armor has it at times. It's a rare sight, when it does appear. Beware of a free for all getting it (heard people scream "SHIELD") next thing i know i have four people jumping into the fight.

Some thoughts on Mistmoore. If you ever camp the graveyard, if you see a monster walking thru the graveyard DO NOT PULL, i repeat DO NOT PULL. If you do so, the monsters inside the castle see you attacking that monster and they all pull. VERY nasty train. It's a bug that veterans of Mistmoore know very well.

If your in your mid 30's or lvl 30 area in character levels, camp the graveyard or the passageway across from the graveyard entrance. If you go into the graveyard, do not go into the building, it's a trap and you will fall into some nasties. Behind the building in the corner is a false wall, go thru this and you will be in the prison. Nasties in the prison are mean. Beware that there is a monster here that killed me at lvl 26 or 27 in two shots. I mean two shots. I didn't even see him.

DO not go too close to the tower in the courtyard area, the walls like any walls in the game, can be see thru and the nasty there can kill in 2 shots.

Beware of gypies, i can't stress this enough. If you think you can take them, good luck because one they hit hard and two they charm you. If they charm you and everyone else zones, they eventually go back deep where they are and just TRY to get your body from there.

Glyphed guards and ancilles drop FS weapons most of the time, i thought i became a weapon dealer at one point. Newbies in crushbone thought i was santa claus.

I strongly recommend never solo here, get into a group. The imp familiars and all familars in general are tough for mid 20's level. THey stay blue a long time, quite a long time. I finally met up and started meeting up with more green familars and finally came to conclusion that if i'm going to get good exp i would have to camp the graveyard or the passageway across from graveyard. Groups are rare for that, if there are groups they are automatically six members.

I forgot to mention the gargoyles. Argh, how could forget about them. In the courtyard, on two corners of the tower. There are two spawn points. They only spawn gargoyles. Remember you HAVE to use magical weapons and they hit hard. If your lucky, you will get a prize, gargoyle eyes which are worth less than 7 to 8pp i could be wrong.

Also, on rare occasions it was very rare, you will encounter a werewolf here. I saw him a total of 3 times in a month.

On camping spots... Now if you plan on camping the courtyard of mistmoore, don't camp at the bottom of the ramp or near the tower. Only camp to the farside of the tower from the ramp close to the back. There is two spawn points there so beware. Also, camp behind the pond, about 5 or 6 spawn points back there. Veterans of mistmoore, way the risks of being behind the pond and fighting spawns there are fighting the trains that go by. We always pick going behind the pond. If camping the graveyard, there are six spawn points, two points at each set of graves. There are three areas where there are graves. I believe the imp familiar appears in one of the two spawn points near the back of the building. Words of wisdom,If you hit the spawn on the right side of the graveyard, go to that side to rest. The other side will then pop and keep shifting locations to one side or the other. You can hit the back two spawn points at any time.

Other areas of interest... If you go down the tunnel that is across from the graveyard. Be warned, the monsters down there are not for low 20's. If you do go down here, if you go up the ramp to the left of the clearing, you will see the castle itself and the entrance. I only saw it from a distance, i didn't have enough courage to try further. The monsters down here, (shivers), rooted and killed me in 6 shots when all of them came. Even sow didn't help me.

The cooridor to get to the graveyard is where most people grab the familars when none are in the courtyard. There are plenty of spawn points all along the cooridor. One spawn point is high on the ledge on the left side. So always glance to your upper left or they might attack you and you didn't know they were there. Now, as to the location of the Mistmoore Shield, It is in this corridor. It spawns anywhere down this cooridor, most of the time it's close to the graveyard entrance.

The monsters in mistmoore have a spell that will drive any duel wielding person or low stamina person nuts. They have spells that will zap all your stamina, you won't have any stamina at all at times.

The only time i ever used my ward summoning spell, was becuase of the gypsie pet or skeleton pet that appear with some monster i can't think of the name of. It's rare but i got to use the spell here.

One last note, in the false wall in left rear corner of the graveyard. Where the prison is, if you open the door on the right side when you enter the prison, it opens all the doors of the prison. The monsters don't come out at you initally. You can all jump on one of them at a time but somebody has to stay back and keep opening the door.

Hope this information is useful when any plans on striking mistmoore comes around.
