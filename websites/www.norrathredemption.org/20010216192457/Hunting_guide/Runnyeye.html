Guide to Runnyeye by Nehna



Ah the zone we all love and hate, mostly hate. And I've never seen a decent guide to it (not sure if I've seen any guide to it in fact). Anyway, this zone is well suited to the lvl 18-30 player, 30+ even if you're after Borxx and the Evil Eyes (which are of a much harder variety than those in Beholder). There's stuff here for below 18s but with the risk of nasty trains, there's no reason to be here at those levels--you won't be near the good loot and are safer elsewhere.

That said, the main reasons to come to Runnyeye are for Blackened Armor, Blackened Iron Bastard Sword (BIBS) and the lens of Borxx. There are two zone lines, one to Misty Thicket and one to Beholder, and they are not too far apart. Many people will be found pulling to group members at the Beholder zone line.

If you are not pulling to the zone or hunting Borxx, you'll probably be camping one of three areas. From the beholder zone, if you follow the wall right, you will be going on a path that leads you to a door and the option to turn right or left.

If you turn right at the door, you will be led to the area where the Goblin Knight spawns. I myself have never camped this area, as my last character (a wizard) never needed the BIBS and my present character prefers duel wield but I've been told he is on a 4 hour spawn and always drops the sword. I've also been told some elites and Dazed Goblin Guards spawn in that area. The BIBS is an excellent mid level 2H sword.

If you turn left at the door, you will pass a few goblins (green to you by the time you should attempt this) and follow the path til it ends. There you will find a couple of green goblin guards who are often AGGRO even though they are green. You really need someone in group who can snare or root in Runnyeye, as a stray runner can be BAD news! Go through the door you'll see in front of you and turn right. There will be a door on your right, through it is the Warlord room. At full spawn there will be one Warlord and 2 Elites, at lvl 26, the Warlord cons blue and the elites typically con blue or green to me (more often blue). These all potentially drop blackened armor pieces and the Warlord may drop the blackened medallion (+3 wis/int neck slot item). You can find blackened arms, legs, waist, gloves, boots, shoulders, etc. on the elites and the warlord. Also runes, words, etc.

If you do not enter the Warlord room there is a third door that is on your left (across from warlord room entrance). If you go through you'll see a pond. Go forward, keeping pond on your right, and go through the door in front of you. Be careful of aggro greens here, if you are lvl 26ish, they will still tend to attack and you really can't let them run. Continue straight ahead and and then around corner to left you will come to the Goblin King's room. At full spawn this room can have 5 or 6 goblins so be prepared. A group in the upper 20s is advisable if you have to clear it. The King is lvl 26. He drops the Blackened Iron Crown (very nice item...+5 strength, +5 to ALL resistances), the Grotesque Mask (also nice item...+7 dexterity, -9 charisma), random blackened items, and fine steel weapons. He can be a very profitable kill...once I killed him and got the Grot. Mask, and blackened leggings, and a FS Long Sword. The crown, if you can't wear, is worth upwards of 200pp on the market. Beware if you have one already, not to loot it, it is LORE.

Finally, Borxx is the feared baddie of the zone. He's a lvl 30ish evil eye that takes a team typically to take down (a lvl 32 tank could not solo him). He has the usual bag of tricks of an evil eye (charm, fear, etc.). He drops the coveted lens which is turned in for the Staff of the Observer. He is not a rare spawn, rather common in fact, just not the mob you want to run into if you're not looking for him LOL. There are also various generic 'evil eyes' in the zone who are much, much harder than those in beholder. They all conned red to me at lvl 26 though I believe it was low red.

That's about it. There's probably more to find down there. Other mobs you'll run into along the way include Dazed Goblin Guards, Dazed Goblin Shaman (take them out FAST, they are nasty caster/healers), Miners.

It's the place to go if you want the black look (the quest in butcherblock is not dwarven armor so the value of blackened is probably about to skyrocket and this is one of the only ways to get it). It's usually not very crowded--often 5-12 in zone. Just be careful of the horrible trains and know how to get to the beholder zone, it'll save your life.
