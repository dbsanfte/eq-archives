The Guild By-laws:

1. The guild is ruled by a council of 10 officers, NOT a guild master. It is majority rules between the officers and if there is ever a tie vote, one regular member will be picked at random to break the tie. All officers are of equal power.

2. Before a person can become a member of The Norrath Redemption they must be good friends with at least 3 members of the guild, be atleast lvl 20, and agree to abide by the bylaws. After these steps have been completed the new member needs to contact Qesta and Sonka with their e-mail address.

3. It is not required to donate your unneeded items or money to other guild members, you may do as you like with your items. But on a personal note, remember "what goes around, comes around", and the more you help out your guild members the more likely they will be to help you out.

4. We will try to have a guild meeting once a month, and a officer only meeting will be held only when the need arrives to have one. All meetings will be held at the shady swashbuckler in west commons. Real life is more important than a game, and also at times you may not find your self at the meeting place when it comes meeting time, this is not a problem, but if you miss the meeting you give up your right to have your opinion heard. All meetings will be posted on the web atleast a week in advance.

5. There are no divisions in this guild. Even though we are all in the same guild, divisions give a sense of separation, so they will be avoided.

6. There are no ranks in the guild, when you have alot of good people it becomes too difficult to fairly place ranks and some feelings may get hurt, so this will be avoided.

7. You may have only one character in the guild. If you wish to bring a new character into the guild, you must guild remove your old character. The lvl 14 rule still applies.

8. If you wish to plan a guild activity you may do so , but people are not required to attend. You will attend guild activities because you want to, not because you feel you have to.

9. Qesta works hard on the web site so please make it worth her time and effort and visit it often and use it. Any questions, comments or suggestions about the web site should be directed to her (Qesta our web master ).

10. Most importantly this is just a game, not a extension of real life. You spent good money on the CD ROM and to subscribe to the game so have fun. Real life is more important than the game, so If you are ever absent for several days that is no problem, but please post your absence on the web site so we will know that you are gone and not just tired of EQ, the reason for the absence does not have to be posted.
