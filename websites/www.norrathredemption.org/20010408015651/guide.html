Zone Level Guide

    The suggested levels are approximate based upon the levels of the majority of creatures found in the zone, and, except for the newbie zones, assume that you will hunt with a group.  Most zones also have some higher and lower level monsters roaming the area, and can thus be selectively hunted at different levels.  Follow the links to get more complete descriptions, and maps and other links for the individual zones.

If their are any players guide it is linked by zone name for you to review.
