Solusek's Eye:
Level Range:      Above level 30


Typical Creatures:   Plenty of Fire Goblins (Starting from the least toughest to the toughest, are Young Goblins, Cinder Goblins, FLame Goblins, Fire Goblins, Inferno Goblins, and Solusek Goblins.  There are shamans and wizards of each variety), Kobold predator, goblin bartender, goblin drunkard, gnomes, clockworks, and a goblin king, Singe (a named fire drake around level 28)  and a named imp.

Description:   The home to a tribe of fire goblins.   This is a very tough dungeon.  It's entrance is found in the zone of Lavastorm.  The goblins range from the low 20's up to the low 30's, and often drop fine steel weapons as loot.  There is a section called the Solusek Mining Company full of gnomes and clockworks, which are in the high 20's.  If you are hated in Ak'Anon, you will be attacked there.  Some unique loot you can find here:  The Kobold has Kobold hide boots;  Singe has Drake scale armour and Obsidian bead hoops;  The imp has Imp hide gloves;  And the Goblin king drops the ring of the goblin lords (6 ac 3 str and 3 int).
