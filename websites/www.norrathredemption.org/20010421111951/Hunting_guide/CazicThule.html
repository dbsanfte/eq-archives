Guide to The Lost Temple of Cazic Thule by Ramza



This dungeon is great for players level 20-40. It's possible to solo here, but extremely hard. Everything explained here will assume you're in a group.

Level 20-25: You will want to be in a full group and have someone pull from the maze to the zone border. Once you come out of the big double-doors and into the courtyard (CY), make a right and the maze is through the door directly in front of you. If you're the puller, do not go too deep into the maze. It gets very confusing and you don't want to get lost inside. Inside near the entrance of the maze you'll find (from easiest to hardest) lizardman warders, defenders, and sentinels. A good full group can probably take on 2 or 3 of these at a time. Defenders and sentinels always drop fine steel weapons.

Level 25-30: You can still pull from the maze, but warders will no longer give you exp. You can also try pulling from the closer alligator pool, but it's a long way to the zone border and they attack others in their path. Coming out of the big double-door into the courtyard, strafe slightly right and go straight ahead and up a ladder. The gator pool is the one on the right. The best thing to do is to wait near the wall on the maze side and pull the gators there. A full group can handle 1 or 2 gators at a time. If you're in a full group of at least upper 20's, you can camp the throne room (TR). The TR has spawns that drop the Rubicite Greaves and Bracer. See below for more info.

Level 30-35: You can pretty much camp and hunt anywhere besides the Avatar of Fear room.

Level 35+: You can camp and hunt anywhere (with a good group of course). :)

Now, onto the Rubicite places!

Rubicite Greaves: This can be found on Tae Ew Templar in the throne room. To get there, come out into the courtyard (through double-doors again), go straight and make a left. Go through a single-door, follow the path, make a right, and through another door. The TR is split mainly into 2 sections: the actual TR and the TR courtyard. The trick to camping the TR is to be able to "control" the 2 sections. You have to clear both sections before begin to camp it. Start by killing everything in the TR courtyard, then rest. Next, pull the lizards inside the TR, but try not to pull any of the things near to the actual throne. After that, rest and kill everything else. The resting part will separate the spawn times into managable increments. Now rest in the TR near the throne area, and have at least one person scout every once in a while for any spawns. You have to kill anything that spawns in both sections ASAP. Also, I do not recommend pulling from the 2nd floor if you're going to camp the TR. Tae Ew Templar occationally spawns on the throne. The Lizardscale Cloak (8 AC, +5 DEX) is his common drop, with the Rubicite Greaves his rare drop.

Bracers: This can be found on Tae Ew Diviner, who can also be found in the TR. If you're looking at the throne, he occationally spawns to the right of that. The Divniner is an enchanter and can charm you. The Lizardscale Mantle is his common drop, with the Rubicite Bracers his rare drop.

Helmet: This can be found on the Stone Golem on the 2nd floor of the TR. The lizards that spawn here are very dangerous (some are level 34 or 35). Plus alot of them have greater healing and usually you can't interrupt it. The best thing to do is to have a cleric or paladin stun it if you see it cast any healing spells. At the TR, the stairs to the 2nd floor is to the right (when facing the throne). A full group of levels 30-35 is recommended here. The Stone Golem's common drop is a potion (1 charge of Skin like Stone) and it's rare drop is the Rubicite Helmet.

Gauntlets: This can be found on the Steel Golem deep in the green pool (past the 2nd gator pool). To get there, come out into the courtyard and climb the ladder on your right. Make your way to a well, go in, and swim out into a tunnel on the surface. Past the tunnel is the green pool. You will want to constantly pull gators from there and try to keep it clear. A full group of levels 30-35 is recommended here. The common drop is a wand (5 charges of Pillage Enchantment) and the rare drop is the Rubicite Gauntlets.

Vambraces: This is found on Tae Ew Archon in the secret temple past the maze. I've only seen the temple once, but I never found my way through the maze to go there again. The lizards that spawn around there are tough (same ones in the 2nd floor of the TR). I've never seen the Archon, but I heard his common drop was a Lizardscale Belt, and of course the rare drop is the Rubicite Vambraces.

Boots: This is found on Cazic Cenobyte in the room with the Avatar of Fear. To get there, go the same way as to the TR, but make a left turn after the first single-door. I've never been there so I have no more info except that someone trained him to the zone and he hit me for double attacks in the upper 70's in damage.

Breastplate: This is found on the Avatar of Fear. I've only seen him twice and lived once because for some reason (I think a GM wanted to have some fun) he spawned at the zone border, went on a rampage, and I zoned out fast enough. You need a full group of upper 30's to camp him. His initial attack is a 400+ dmg harm touch and then regular double attacks of around 90 dmg (and bashes). His common drop is the Bladed Thulian Claws (5 dmg, 19 delay, 1H slashing) and the rare drop is the Rubicite Breastplate.

TIPS

-Try to have someone in your group who can snare (not root). Nasty trains can start if lizards get away. Root doesn't last long and the enemy can fight back while rooted.

-Use the /assist command. With a large pull, you will want to make sure everybody in your group is attacking the same enemy. Usually it's best for the puller to choose the targets (use F8 or target healers first) and then have the others assist him.

-The healers are the Disciple, Probationer, Page, Herald, Justcar, and Judicator (from easiest to hardest). Kill those first.

That's all I can think of for now. Good luck!
