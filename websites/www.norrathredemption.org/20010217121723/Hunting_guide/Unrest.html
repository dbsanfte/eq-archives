Guide to Unrest by Cayne



Note: Factor in that this description of Unrest is from a cleric's point of view.

Cayne's Home(I have a cot in the basement with the hags) UNREST!!.

First off this zone is geared for players lvl 15 -30(and maybe all the way up to 35).

Grouping is a MUST in this zone. Dont even try to solo unless you are 30+ or just have a death wish(that and soloing sucks anyways).

15 - 18 Should concentrate on not getting trained on and killed. They should be either killing yard trash(tormented dead, greater skeletons, or death beetles) or pulling from the 1st floor of the house to either the left wall or right wall by the gazebo. This zone is very nasty at these levels as the yard trash can give you hell. Try pulling a lurking mummy and end up fighting 1 mummy 3 beetles 2 tormented dead and a ghoul. Quick and painless death.

19 - 22 Its gets alot better at these levels mainly because all the yard trash is very green to you and will not jump you or your party members anymore. You can start pulling from the 2cnd floor at these levels and really rake in the loot and exp. Make sure you have a really good puller because pulling from the 2cnd can also pull all the lesser monsters from the 1st floor. Carrion Ghouls are very good loot and exp for this level, very little downtime after the kill. Barbed Bone skeletons are also good exp but with poor loot... sometimes nothing. Be careful as the Barbed Bones have a natural damage shield on so those monks might want to shy away from these. 5 damage a hit i think. Skeletal monks are yellow to me at lvl 22 so they should be killable at 20 with a good group. More than one of Carrions, monks or Barbed may be deadly so watch out. I forgot Dry bones. They range in 20 - 27 and are a very good fight for the exp and loot that they give, which is usually some money and maybe Small Bronze Armor. There are 2 "special" spawns on the second floor: Ghoul magi(a ghoul necromancer) and the reanimated hand(this one might be on the 3rd floor though). The Ghoul magi drops the Savants Cap +3 int and +3 wis very nice item and the Dusty Bloodstained gloves + 20 mana. The Reanimated Hand(lvl 24 - 25) drops Ivory Bracelates +4 agility and +20 mana. Since im only lvl 22 i cant comment on the floors of the house above the 2cnd since ive never been in a group that pulls from there.

This zone is one of my favorite and i always find myself coming back here. Also this zone introduces you to the "good life" with Fine Steel and Bronze armor as loot, with some of the mobs dropping upwards to 3 plat. I recommend anyone 18 - 25 to come and reap the rewards.
