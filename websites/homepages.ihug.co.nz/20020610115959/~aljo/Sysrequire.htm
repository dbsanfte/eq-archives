System Requirements

Minimum Requirements:

1)  Pentium 166

2)  32 megabytes of ram

3)  Windows 95 or Windows Nt

4)  Direct3D compliant video card

 

Recommended Requirements:

1)  Pentium 200 or greater

2)  3dfx based video card.
