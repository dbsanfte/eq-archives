Paladin

Paladins are the very picture of a holy knight, fighting for the cause of good in all aspects of his life. They share some of the power of a Cleric, and a good deal of the fighting ability of a Warrior, but have a few powers all their own.

  

 

Player Guide: Paladin By Caedmon


Hail young knight, so your wish is to become a holy warrior? One who fights for the innocents who suffer? And is just in cause? Well you must work hard and stay pure in heart to take on such a responsibility, look within yourself and see if you a strong enough to be a Paladin.

Benefits and Disadvantages
As a Paladin you are part cleric, part fighter. Not exceptional in either, but the two combined make you a formidable foe. You are blessed with the "Lay on Hands" skill exclusive to the Paladin class, this is a very powerful skill for it allows you to heal yourself or others completely, but can only be used once per game day so it must be saved for extreme circumstances. Paladins have a large amount of hit points, which allows you to get up front and close to battle.

The major disadvantage is that until ninth level (when you get your spells), you are basically a fighter but without the kick skill and the larger amount of hit points. As well you do not gain any new skills till ninth level so what you start with for skills is it for awhile.

Lets Get Started
First you must choose your race, Paladins can only be Humans, Half Elf, High Elf, Erudite, and Dwarf. Once this decision has been made it is time for stats. The important stats are Str, Sta, Wis, and Cha. It is suggested to put your points into both wisdom and strength because these will help both the fighter and cleric part of you character. Now on to the land of Norrath…

The Cold Harsh World
Upon entering the world you have a starting sword, a guild note, some food and water and a PK note. You should go look for your guild as soon as you get acquainted with your surroundings. There you will get some experience and your guild tunic. Take this opportunity to use you five starting skill points training. A good suggestion is to put them into a fighting skill, this will make it easier for you once you are out fighting.

Now its time to fight, work at you levels and get some money, because you are going to need it for some equipment.

Equipment
As a fighter class you can wear any armor and use any melee weapon. The best thing to do is get some armor as fast as you can because this will help you greatly when out fighting. Patchwork Armor is cheap and easy to find. One piece of Patchwork armor costs around 2 gold pieces, depending where you buy it. Also get a better weapon so you can dish out more damage. These two things are very important since melee combat is all that will help you for the first nine levels.

Tactics
Fighting for a Paladin at lower levels requires knowing what can and cannot be taken down. You are not as powerful as a fighter in combat so you must be careful, it will not be as easy to kill foes. Consider any opponent you want to attack by targeting them and typing /c. This will give you a good idea how you will stand up against them. Remember that when you die at early levels, you will keep your newbie gear and receive 5 bread and 5 water. This ends around the time you reach level 5. You must just be cautious and I suppose that goes without saying but make sure you watch you hit points and if you are in a sticky situation you can use your Lay on Hands (just try not to get in another sticky situation for awhile). This will change once you receive your clerical magic….

Magic
After being a warrior for nine levels it is nice to finally have something to back you up. Your magic is clerical, Paladins specialize in healing, protection ad undead spells. These are a valuable addition to your arsenal but beware your spells are relatively weak because you haven't had the last nine levels to power them up like a cleric gets. Best advice work on your magic skills as much as possible.

Spells
Lets go buy some spells, they are very cheap at this point because you can easily make the money to buy them. Once you have a spell go over to that spell book that has been gathering dust and move the spell onto one of the squares. At this time the red bar to the lower right corner will begin to slowly drain. Once the bar has gone clear again the spell will permanently inscribed in your book. Now click on the spell and move it to one of the eight ovals to the left. This time the little red bar to the left will begin to drain and once this is done your spell will be memorized for quick use

The first level of Paladin's spells are as follows:

    1. Minor Healing This spell allows you to heal a small amount of damage.
    2. Courage + AC & HP
    3. Cure Poison Anti Poison
    4. Spook the Dead Fear vs. Undead
    5. Yaulp 18 seconds of + AC & + STR - Self Only

Conclusion
What's next, its up to you. You will be able to gain some new and useful skills upon reaching higher levels such as dodge, bash and double hit, these will just augment your fighters side and at fifteenth level you will gain five more spells which will help your cleric side.

It's a tough road to follow being a Paladin, both with its upsides and downsides but there is great versatility and once you gain clerical spell it makes the long wait for them all worth it.

Safe journeys young Paladin, and bring pride upon our name
