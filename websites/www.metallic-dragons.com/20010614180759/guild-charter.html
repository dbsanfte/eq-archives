 Guild Rules



Metallic Dragon our pact. It is our bond. Through the fealty we swear, one guildsman to another, and the Grace of the Gods of Norrath, we shall forge a House unrivaled by those who hold together based on glory or greed. Though our guild may be directed by her Leader, and her officers, Metallic Dragon's guildsmen are it's heralds. It is by their works that word shall spread of our compact. For this reason above all else we prize every voice that speaks in her behalf. All men and women may raises their voices in her cause, but only if they swear to hold one to another as their brothers or sisters, even should this break prior vows of vengeance, or righteous wrath.

Each guildsman swears the following:

                               I.            I will not permit the Guild or any of her guildsman to come to harm through my actions or inaction.

                             II.            Should any member of Metallic Dragon question me on a topic of trade, quest, or skill I shall answer honestly and to the best of my ability so long as answering does not endanger my fellow guildsmen in any way. (i.e. they are not actively hunting or laying in wait for a creature, or leading/guiding themselves or a group through hostile terrain).

                          III.            I shall make every reasonable attempt to gather with my brethren at hunts and meetings in a show of solidarity to the Metallic Dragons.

In return, every sworn guildsman can expect:

                               I.            That the guild shall provide him or her with the knowledge to peruse fields that furthers his or her development of skills and trades, even if such information must be requested and sent by missive due to conflicting schedules (i.e. even should the questions need to be responded to by e-mail).

                             II.            The officers and guildleader of Metallic Dragons will always be willing to listen to suggestions and concerns put to them so long as discussion does not endanger other guildsmen in any way. (i.e. they are not actively hunting or laying in wait for a creature, or leading/guiding themselves or a group through hostile terrain).

                          III.            The officers and guildsmen will give him or her the benefit of the doubt in conflicts with those outside the guild relying on honesty that he or she would expect in return if the roles were reversed.

                          IV.            That any guildsmen should attempt to deal fairly and honestly in terms of trade or sale of items giving him the most reasonable price possible in an effort to strengthen the guild as a whole. (NOTE : This does not mean that a guildmate must sell an item within the guild. It simply means that should he FREELY CHOOSE to do so, he must offer it at good price and in good faith to his or her brethren).

 

This pact is a living document, we shall revise it should the circumstances arise to make it better able to serve the needs of the sworn guildsmen of Metallic Dragons and her officers. We hold to this written word as the means through which we may best serve one another, the Gods of Norrath, and ourselves.
