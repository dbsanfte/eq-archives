The Call of Abraham

Posted by Mark under The Narrow Path
 

I’ve always thought that God called Abraham out of Ur to go to the land of Canaan (Gen 12:1-3). Imagine my surprise when I found that wasn’t the case. Going back to Gen 11:31-32 it states that Abraham’s father Terah had it in his mind to go to the land of Canaan, but they ended up stopping in Haran and stayed there. It wasn’t until after Terah died that God came to Abraham and told him to complete the journey his father had started.

So now I wonder, why did Terah wish to go to Canaan? I suppose one reason Terah wished to leave Ur was because of his son’s death, but I can’t be certain. Did God ask Terah to begin this journey to Canaan?
