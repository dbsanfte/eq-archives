Wed 4 Aug 2004

The Fear of David

Posted by Mark under The Narrow Path
No Comments 

Recently I’ve been trying to learn the guitar. I’ve been practicing on praise music, as the songs seems to be easy and I want to learn them so I can play during my own personal worship times with God. One of the praise songs I’m learning is “Create In Me A Clean Heart” which is based on Psalm 51:10-12. As I was learning the song I was suddenly struck with, what felt like, the emotions David must have been feeling at the time. Let me explain.

David was a shepard who was recruited into King Saul’s court to play music for him. David had probably seen King Saul as he was filled with God’s Spirit. David probably also saw when God had taken that Holy Spirit from King Saul. Now go forward a bit. In 2 Samuel 11 David sins against God by killing Bathsheba’s husband so that he could make her his wife. After this happens, Nathan the prophet goes to David and rebukes him for what he’s done (2 Samuel 12). David’s eyes are opened and his spirit is pierced. Seeing his sin and knowing how God took away His Holy Spirit from King Saul, David cry’s out to God in fear and repentance:

“Create in me a clean heart, O God, And renew a steadfast spirit within me. Do not cast me away from Your presence And do not take Your Holy Spirit from me. Restore to me the joy of Your salvation And sustain me with a willing spirit. ” (Psalm 51:10-12, NASB95)

 

Tue 3 Aug 2004

Spiritual Laws

Posted by Mark under The Narrow Path
No Comments 

I wonder at times what the Spiritual Laws are that Spiritual beings are bound by. God seems to do most of His work through creation. For instance He uses one nation to punish another nation. He speaks through the prophets. What I find really interesting, though, is that Satan seems to act in the same way. For the most part, Satan doesn’t come out and start enslaving people or cause supernatural things to happen, he does his mischief in more subtle ways…using other people, planting certain thoughts in our minds, etc. Satan, who is in every way contrary to God, is using what seems to be the same methods that God uses. I wonder if the spiritual realm is much more complicated then we could ever imagine. While the physical can affect the spiritual and the spiritual can affect the physical, Scripture seems to put ALOT more weight in the spiritual.
