If God is for us…

Posted by Mark under The Narrow Path
No Comments 

“…If God is for us, who is against us?” ( Romans 8:31, NASB95)

These famous words of Paul has often been an encouragement to believers everwhere. These words rang in my ears today as I was reading the accounts of John and Jesus birth in Luke. Jesus had just been born when an angel was sent to “shepherds living out in the fields nearby”. After telling the shepherds of Jesus’ birth…

“Suddenly a great company of the heavenly host appeared with the angel, praising God and saying,

‘Glory to God in the highest,
and on earth peace to men
on whom his favor rests’” ( Luke 2:13-14, NIV)

The angels praised God, which I understand, however they did something else…they blessed men. They wished that we would have peace. This caused me to realize that it wasn’t just God that was looking out for us and wishing the best for us, but the angels in heaven as well!
