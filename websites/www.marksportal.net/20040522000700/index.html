Communication

posted by: Mark @ 11:24 pm

Ever feel like you have something to say, but you just don’t know how to put it into words. This seems to be happening to more and more every day. Someone will say something or do something that doesn’t sit right within my spirit and when I try to verbalize it I’m given blank stares. Sometime’s it is funny, other times it’s just frustrating. Actually, come to think of it, this happens to me when I’m speaking with God as well. My spirit will be overcome with some sort of emotion or my mind will have a certain thought, but I’m unable to translate it into words. It reminds me of this verse in the Bible:

In the same way the Spirit also helps our weakness; for we do not know how to pray as we should, but the Spirit Himself intercedes for us with groanings too deep for words;

New American Standard Bible : 1995 update. 1995 (Ro 8:26). LaHabra, CA: The Lockman Foundation.

Let me tell you, these “groanings” are not fun, if that is indeed what I’ve been experiencing.
