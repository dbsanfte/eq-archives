Nothing in particular

Posted by Mark under General
 

I was reminded recently how much I enjoyed the movie “Almost Famous”. I’m not sure why but it seem to really resonate with me. Maybe resonate isn’t the right word, but it sounds like it is. “Twister” did the same thing for me. I really am not sure why I enjoyed these two movies so much, yet there was just something to them that seemed to touch my heart. They were “feel good” movies for me that left my heart aching. Whether that’s bad or good I can’t say, but that aching can sure act like an addictive drug sometimes.

Remember when I talked about “signs” in a previous post? Well, today I recieved more signs that were contrary to the first ones. I think I’m reading into these “signs” a bit too much. I guess it’s human nature. When we want something bad enough everything we see becomes associated with that thing. I haven’t decided yet whether I am going to go through with this thing I’m nervous/scared of doing. What a chicken I am!
