Mon 14 Jun 2004

Happenings

Posted by Mark under General
 

My first instinct is to say that not too much went on this weekend, and while that is true, I’m not too sure that is accurate. I had spent some time with friends on Friday. We just hung out and didn’t do to much, which was nice. I was feeling worn out and wasn’t up for anything too involving.

Oh, actually something happened when we went to dinner that night. After some discussion we decided to keep dinner simple and went to Taco Bell. As I sat at the table waiting for my friends to get their orders and join me a woman walked up to me. She didn’t look homeless, but she didn’t look healthy either. She told me that she just came from the hospital from across the street and needed money for food so she could take her pills. I immediatly closed down my emotions and went into suspicion mode. As I studied her, she started rummaging through her bag and showed me her pills. Then she proceeded to go through another bag and pulled out these calendars. Glancing at the covers of the calendars I saw that they were of scantly clad women, she then proceeded to make a sales pitch, hoping I’d buy one from her. I instantly replied, “No, I’m not interested in them.” That didn’t convience her, so she opened the calendars up and said, “Oh, their not naked.” Again I refused the calendars…..”I’m not interested, but here is a $1 for food”. She thanked me, then as she walked off she asked, “Are you a Christian?” I said nothing. In fact I was even more suspicious of her now. I just looked at her, not knowing how I should answer. Then she quickly said, “Or maybe your just a really kind person.” Then she walked off.

As I sat there, irratated by the events, my spirit started speaking up a storm, as it usually does in cases like this. “Mark, why was it such a big deal giving her money? You offer your money freely to many people. Why was it so hard to give it to her?” I wasn’t unwilling to give her money because I thought she was going to use it wrong. I was just not willing to give it to her in the first place….and I’m not sure why. It still bothers me. I can easily throw money towards computer hardware, to waiters/waitresses, to friends who ask….but this person, who might have needed it, I INSTANTLY was opposed to it. Like I said, it wasn’t because I thought it would be put to bad use, I just didn’t want to give it to her. Ugh, that bugs me. Maybe it’s just because I hate confrontation, and while this wasn’t exactly that, it was threatening in the fact that I wasn’t expecting the encounter. That event will linger in my mind for quite some time I’m sure.

Since I’m on the topic of money, guess what I did on Sat…..I bought a BBQ Grill. I could barely give the lady one messly little dollar, but I had no problem at all going out and buying this grill. BAH! Anyways, I bought my very first grill. It’s a Coleman Roadtrip Grill LX. Quite a nice travel grill. I’ve never grilled before so this will be quite the experience. My first night I tried hamburgers, which turned out well….and I had fun! I’m looking forward to trying it again. Not sure what I’ll try next, but it should be fun. It should get alot of good use out of it with the potlucks and events I have with my friends.

I also spent Saturday trying to build a computer for my mom’s quilting store. For some reason it’s been giving me a lot of trouble. I can’t tell if it’s just a slow network connection or if the hardware is flawed somehow. I hope to find that out this week. It’s just acting strange.

Sunday evening my roommate told me a couple of people were getting together at someone’s house to make Indian food. I was a bit nervous going over because I didn’t want to intrude as I wasn’t really invited by the host of the event. He assured me that I wasn’t intruding, so I timidly tagged along. It ended up being alot of fun. I knew(or knew of) most the people that were there. We all had a great time talking and joking around while we made the food. I was responsible for cutting the chicken breasts and dicing some garlic. You know, the easy stuff. When it came to rolling the bread dough I thought I was doing well, but then was told that I should let someone else do it. Really, how hard is rolling bread? hehe….I guess it’s harder than I thought. An art I have not yet perfected obviously.

The food was REALLY good. Afterwards we all sat around and talked. It was obvious that people were tired from their active weekends. The company was great though, so it seemed like people didn’t really want to leave. Someone had a guitar there and people would pick it up and mess around with it. In the past I had taught myself some very basic stuff that sort of sounded like a song, so I started playing around with the guitar to see if I could remember it. It took a bit, but the fingering seemed to be coming back to me. It was nice to hear an acoustic guitar again. When I lived at another place my old roommate would play guitar nightly. It was so soothing to just sit back and listen to him play. I enjoyed his playing so much I went out and bought my own guitar, but I haven’t really learned how to play it. So it sits in my closet collecting dust. After playing this other guitar briefly I’ve been inspired to try and learn again. We’ll see how long that inspiration lasts.

Well, that was my weekend. Like I said at the beginning, it seems uneventful, but it really wasn’t.
