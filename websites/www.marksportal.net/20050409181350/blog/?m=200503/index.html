Spontaneous Redesign

Posted by Mark under General
No Comments 

Funny how so many good things I do end up being spontaneous. Take this website redesign. Earlier last week I had no intentions of spending my time on it, yet all of a sudden I found myself working on it Saturday afternoon. Then by Sunday evening, I had completely changed everything and am VERY please with how it has turned out. I still need to work on making the photo gallery flow better with the front page, but I’ll hit that another time. It’s not that important.
