Personal note (ooc): My name is Chad.  I began playing EQ in the beta 4 phase and became quite addicted to the game.  My friends Qanamin, Aarist and Torien were the reason I played... I play for role playing and for social reasons and not to level quickly.  In fact the only reason TO level is if members of your party are levelling and you wish to be able to explore with them.  This original foursome was very fun, but with the advent of my wife and I about to have a daughter, I quit EQ.  It wasn't until my friends announced that they wanted my old character the cleric known as Kast to marry them, did I entertain thoughts of returning.  To my surprise Kast still existed and although he had no belongings (because he gave them away), he still had his spells and experience level.  Thus enters Dirg... My new character, a Bard.  He is much different than Kast, mostly because he has no sense of humor at all.  He is very straight laced and very polite.  It is with the advent of Re-joining this game that I find I desire not to level hardly at all.  I am comfortable where I am and will not let this game become an addiction.  So if you see me online, as Dirg or Kast you will find someone who'd much rather spend time talking or playing than killing or hunting.  I love people and hope this game to be a pleasant encounter with people.  My daughter is almost one now, and I much prefer to play with her than to be online for hours, so if you see me it'll be a rarity, but I still hope I get to meet you.

I have noticed that playing a human after a gnome, that the eyes are the biggest issue.  You can't see as well at night, but your line of sight is MUCH higher up.  You can see people's faces instead of their knees. (smile).

Also on comment on the economy, I notice that a lot more money is floating around and people are more generous to newbies.  I remember Norrath being a very poor economy, but things seemed to have shifted since then, and it is comforting to see people let you take a loot instead of them.  I also notice however that with the increase in the amount of players playing, that there is an increase in the amount of RUDE players playing as well.  Pay attention to your etiquette, and be forgiving of other players.  My reason for playing is to affect and be affected by people in a positive way, not to camp grumbling about what so-and-so did.  Have fun with it and don't take it too seriously.
