It’s Been Awhile

Posted by Mark under General
 

I’ve been meaning to post for awhile but things just have been too busy. Let’s see….what has happened since I’ve last posted. Well, I mentioned that I bought a grill. This last weekend I had a friend come over and show me how to make steaks. They were VERY good! We grilled up some steaks, potatoes, and corn on the cob. The steaks were pretty spicy, but we all liked it. So we all had a fun time doing that. I haven’t been much of a cook. Most my “grown up” life I’ve relied on fast food and microwave dinners. However this last week I’ve dived into trying to cook the easier items: Chili, Spaghetti, hamburgers, and steaks. I hope to try cooking some teriyaki chicken and fish soon. Perhaps this next week. I’m discovering that cooking isn’t as hard as it initially seemed. I guess that’s true with most things though.

In other news…I’ve been obsessing about some things. Things I wish I didn’t obsess about. It’s quite irratating actually. I’m finding that every time my life starts to get balanced, something pops up that I obsess over which throws me off balance. I think this obsession is starting to leave though, at which point things should balance out again. Hopefully I’ll be a bit more aware of it before it pops up again.
