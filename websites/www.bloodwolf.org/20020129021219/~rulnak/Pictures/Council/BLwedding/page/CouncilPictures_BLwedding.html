Bridgit and Leyor's Wedding






Bridgit and Leyor, from the Circle of Eternity, are good friends to many people of the Council; here are a few pictures of their wedding, which took place on sunday, march 26th 2000 near the lake of the West Commonlands.
 
 

 This event was an occasion for many old friends to meet.
I had not seen many of these people in a long time... 
Many people showed up and the atmosphere was festive. 
 
 
 
Eventually, the groom made his appearance; he was dashing in his red armor, wielding a mighty sword of flames. 
 Boburt, the Holy One, ordered the crowd into a row...
 And we sat, waiting for the bride and groom.
 
 
 
 A GM even attended this event.
 So it came to be Bridgit and Leyor walked down the aisle, in the company of their close friends.
And they spoke the vows before Boburt. 
     
 
And they were married on that day! As they kissed passionately, cheers resounded all over the commonlands!
A lumbering Giant tried to ruin the festivities, but alas he chose is foes poorly that day, and his demise was fast.
