Rulnak's Everquest Page
 
 





This humble page is dedicated to my favorite computer game, Everquest. Hopefully people I meet in the game can come here to learn a bit more about my characters and about the game.

Some of you may have met me in Everquest as some other character and may be wondering, "Who the heck is Rulnak??" Well, Rulnak is a character I played in the pen-and-paper RPG called Rolemaster. He is one of my favorite characters ever. My friends even call me Rulnak sometimes. Believe me, it's a much better alternative than Tony Boy!

Rulnak is also an everquest character I play on the Karana server. However, I don't play him often, so it's unlikely you've ever met him.

If you have anything you'd like to add to this page (screenshots, trade skill guides, game-related info, info about your characters, etc.), just let me know!
