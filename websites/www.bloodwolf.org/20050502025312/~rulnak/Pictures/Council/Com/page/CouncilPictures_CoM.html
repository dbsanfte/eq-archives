The City of Mist





Deep within the Emerald Jungle, there lie great old ruins known as the City of Mist. They were once a thriving city of one of the elder civilization of Kunark, but have fallen in decrepitude long ago. Now, the dead walk its streets. Eerie whispers echo all around, and vague shapes seem to move at the edge of one's vision...
A force of adventurers went into the City of Mist to try and destroy the source of the Evil the reigns there. (or to get phat l3wt, I forget which. :)
 
 

 The dark ruins.
Mysterious magic still exists inside. 
 The adventurers fight evil undead that guard the place.
 
 
 
 A strong force of adventurers is put together by Leyor.
 So many heroes together are certainly a force to be reckoned with.
We move toward a dark temple. 
 
 
 
 Once inside, we move cautiously...
And slay those who attack us. 
We see that a strange elevator is to take us to the second floor. We all climb on it. 
 
 
 
 But something goes terribly wrong, and half of our forces is butchered above while the other half is waiting downstairs, trying to make the lift work... Then the monsters come down and finish us off.
Result = Light Blue Bra of Death 
For pretty much all of us. 
 
 
 
 After hours of difficult corpse recovery, we finally got our corpses back.
And gave the denizens of the City of Mist a piece of our mind. :) 
All in all, this raid was an event to be remembered. All who were there showed great bravery in the face of adversity.
