Adventures in Kunark




A few moons ago, the bold adventurers of Norrath started to explore the mysterious continent of Kunark. The rode on mighty ships, and strode across the dunes and plains of that uncharted continent. They faced many dangers, but reaped great rewards as well.
Of course, The Council launched quite a few expeditions into these strange lands. Here are some pictures of their travels...
 
 
 

I began my journey aboard a small shuttle heading toward the Ship bound for Kunark. An ominous red light filled the western sky as the sun was setting for the night.
With a gasp of awe, I beheld the mighty ship built by my people.
It was vast beyond belief ! As large as a castle, I tell you !
 
 
 
After a long, yet comfortable, trip, we arrived in the outpost of Firiona Vie. I was surprised to see how fast the army had built the encampment there. In the distance, I could see a huge statue of Firiona Vie, the sister of my friend Ettel.
I set foot on the docks and started visiting the outpost.
And I knew that now it would be difficult to return back to my homeland.
 
 
 
My friends and I traveled many leagues in Kunark... We strode the skies themselves...
We stalked dark, eerie forests...
We delved into caves of ice...
 
 
 
We ran over plains of dust...
We walked through dark tunnels, filled with unnerving echoing sounds...
We beheld strange sights... Like this giant hive, home of wasps the size of a grown man, or larger !
 
 
 
Strange remnants of old civilizations... The Iksar and the Sarnak, among others.
And we found strange artefacts of ancient magical power.
Perhaps they had even been created by the Dragons themselves...
 
 
 
I was glad to have my friends along on these journeys. Morley, the rouge rogue.
My courageous friend Galilee.
Antoninus the Enchanter and his faithful pet wolf... Wait a minute?...
 
 
 
Ah! It was my friend Morgannawin the Druid, lover of animals. :)
And of course, Razael Grimsoul, the powerful warrior. As well as many others.
Tunare had indeed blessed me with wonderful companions.
 
 
 
But alas, the local inhabitants were not quite happy to see us...
And the Sarnak warriors attacked us viciously.
We faced the wrath of Living and Dead alike.
 
 
 
We fought beasts as well, from the wasps...
To the mighty Wurms !!
Those were truly epic battles.
 
 
 
But, luckily for us, these distant cousins of the Dragons had neither magic nor fiery breath.
From Yetis in the snow...
To strange lizard-like creatures in the jungles.
 
 
 
Kunark is indeed a dangerous place!
The beasts there love the taste of human, elven and wolven flesh alike.
 Some of our foes were twisted hybrids, part Teir'Dal and part giant spider.
     
Others were just mindless creatures.
The giants there follow a different fashion then the ones from our homelands. :)
A giant fighting a pack of wolves.
     
We recovered from the wounds of battle with my healing prayers and Morgannawin's regeneration spells.
We also found some strange items, like this staff that Morgannawin got.
She let me use it a bit. Looks nice, huh?
 
 
 

The exploration of Kunark goes on... There are still many secrets to uncover!
