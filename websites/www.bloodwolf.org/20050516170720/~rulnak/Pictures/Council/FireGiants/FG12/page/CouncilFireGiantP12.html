First Fire Giant Hunt (2000-02-21)
 
 

Page 2



 
 
 

The first assault against the giants was frightening for those of us who'd never done it before
A vicious battle was raging in the tiny room. Several of us fell, and, sadly, some not even to the blows of giants... But to a feral Stone Spider.
The event attracted the attention of two guides, one of whom was this gorgeous lady. One day Alruna will look like this!
     
So we prepared ourselves for the fight ahead...
We healed, buffed, rebuffed and reorganized the groups..
With all those preparations, nothing could stop us, right?
     
WRONG!  Nothing could've prepared us for the five giants that attacked us then. The poor pullers had an accident and a horde of giants fell upon us.
Try as we might, we could not defeat them all...
And soon our broken bodies littered the ground.
     
Slowly, we began the resurrection/healing process. Luckily some of the Clerics had camped during the fight!
Once we were all ready, we swore vengeance upon our enemies, and the brave Seymore brought more of the red behemots to us.
This time they were less numerous and we used our numbers to our advantage.
     
Magic and sword blows assaulted the Fire Giants. They pounded mightily on their opponents, but the Clerics healed the fighters...
Clerics like the bold Garvok here
And soon the last giant found himself facing a horde of bloodthirsty adventurers.
     
 
He roared in anger and pain...
And he fell down before our might!
