First Fire Giant Hunt (2000-02-21)






The Council and the Circle of Eternity joined forces to raid the fire giants that guard Lord Nagafen's Lair. The first such raid took place on monday evening, february 21st, 2000. Here are the pictures of the heroic actions that took place.
 
 

Page 1
Many great heroes gathered that day...
There was tension in the air while the small army fought the first foes. It was all very chaotic at first...
... but Leyor, guildmaster of the Circle of Eternity, did an excellent job managing the event and soon got things well under control.
     
He received help from Rutgger, the guildmaster of the Council, who coordinated the efforts of our guild.
Spoffid, greatest warrior of the Council, received the daunting task of protecting the spell casters from the giants.
Korinn made quite an impression with her useful enchantments, Clarity in particular.
     
Many strong blades were there to do serious damage to the giants... Ebumar's Ghoulbane...
... Saldrin's twin Ebony Blades...
And whatever Selindar was using ;-)
     
   
Galilee and Alruna wait to join a group.
