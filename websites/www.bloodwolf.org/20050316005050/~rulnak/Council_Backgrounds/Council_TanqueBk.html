Tanque's background

Tanque Stormhammer, Prince of Kaladim




Tanque was sent into the world by his father, Kazon Stormhammer, to experience the world of Norrath and learn the ways of the other races. This experience was to better prepare to serve as king of Kaladim when his time comes. Soon after leaving his home, he joined a guild of fellow short people, called the small chaotic gods of war. It was with this band of adventurers that Tanque earned the right to take the surname Stormhammer and declare to the world his birthright as Heir to Kazon and Prince of Kaladim. Now he seeks a new band of companions to further explore the wonders of Norrath and the citizens therein.
