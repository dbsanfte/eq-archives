Soulfire Quest




Two of the Council's Paladins, Onusar Peacebearer and Galilee Morningsong, have bravely completed the Soulfire quest - an endeavour that tests their Faith and Courage. Of course, they had a little help from their friends - the pictures below show some parts of the Quest, in particular the battle against the vile Sir Lucan, the leader of the corrupt Freeport Militia
