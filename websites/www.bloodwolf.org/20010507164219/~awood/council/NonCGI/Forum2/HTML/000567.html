I wish you had known the Council as it had been created. Those two cohorts of barbarians who did nothing but go out of their way to help out someone in distress. Their names have long since been forgotten but I can repeat them. Sulla and Axkel. Sulla was my brother, my hero. Everytime that little scared Rhyannia found herself in trouble it was he who she called upon for help and guidance. It was not a guild about how much could one accumulate, but moreso how much one could contribute. I have yet to see anyone as generous as the two of them.

In the recent past and as time evolved and people left and joined, something within Council changed. All good things must come to an end, but we could have hoped for a better ending to Council.
