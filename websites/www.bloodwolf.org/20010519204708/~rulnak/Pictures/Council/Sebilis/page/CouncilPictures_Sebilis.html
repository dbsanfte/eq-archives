Sebilis



 
 
 
 
 

Sebilis is a very difficult dungeon. It's also very difficult to access it. In order to enter it, one needs a key from Emperor Ganak. And to get the key, one must give the Emperor two medallions found in Trakanon's Teeth.
Here Leyor leads us in our very first Sebilis raid.
We follow him into the unknown.
 
 
 
I proceed with caution, not knowing what to expect. Here I am in the ruined outskirts of Sebilis.
And soon we see a strange pulsating red sphere: the magical entrance to Sebilis proper.
Inside Sebilis. Here I am with 2 other people of the Corpse Recovery team.
 
 
 
Later, the Council came back with fewer people, for raids of smaller span.
As you can see, casters cannot bind in Sebilis. So if you die inside and you did not leave a key elsewhere, you are in deep trouble.

And boy did we fight! It was continuous pulling for several hours.
Everyone gave a superb performance, tanks fighting, healers healing, casters nuking, Timot pulling, Korinn mezzing.
We got some nice loot that day, for example Rutgger (and myself) got Donal's Vambraces of Mourning
     
 
Faedrin got a black robe that makes him look like a Necromancer. :)
All in all, we the Council proved that we are now a force to be reckoned with. We were very organized that night in Sebilis, and we decided that we were ready for an even bigger challenge: breaking the Plane of Hate!
