- Level
This is the approximate level of the characters, to the extent of my knowledge at the moment I updated the table. I will try to update this reasonably often. If you see that one of your characters is in the wrong level category, you can let me know by email.

- Trade skills
This is, again, to the extent of my knowledge. If you need me to add a trade skill of yours, just let me know by email.
 

- Activity
Inactive: The person logs in rarely (less than once a week) but has made an appearance in the last 2 months or so.
MIA (Missing in Action): I have not heard from the person for several months nor seen them in Norrath... If you read this and are one of these people, please do send me an email to let me know what's happening!
