The Plane of Fear







"Velkom to ze Plane of Feeeaaaar, Mortal.... Mwa ha ha ha ha ha ha !!!!"

That place is really scary. I went there 3 times. The first 2 times were ok, there were a lot of deaths but overall it went well. They were led by Circle of Eternity. The second time, we tried to attack Cazic Thule and got destroyed. Later on they finally killed him, but I had left by then (13 hours of PoF is more than I can handle).

The third time was a total disaster, we all died 1 minute after zoning in because one clueless boob trained the camp spot. We spent hours trying in vain to do a CR by ourselves, but finally, FoH gave us a hand and we got our corpses. (btw very few Council members were in that last raid)
