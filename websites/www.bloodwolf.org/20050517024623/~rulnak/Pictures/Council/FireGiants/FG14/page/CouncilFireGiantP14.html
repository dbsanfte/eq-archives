First Fire Giant Hunt (2000-02-21)
 
 

Page 4
So I found myself in front of this bridge over Lava.
The heat was scorching, even through my fire resistance prayers.
With awe, I stepped on this ancient contruction...
     
And could not help but wonder who had built this, and how long ago?
After a fight too brutal to be depicted here (many more of us died), we found ourselves closer than ever to the lair of Nagafen
Carved in rock, the entrance to his lair was shaped into the likeness of his head. We were quiet, all of us. Were we truly bold enough to enter this great beast's abode?
     
We meditated before the final fight...
And soon found ourselves in battle against powerful fire giants: Magi Rokyl and a Fire giant wizard!
They used deadly spells just as we used ours. Spells like Gravity Flux, for example.
Ouch.
     
But in the end, we were victorious. And we eagerly entered Lord Nagafen's lair!!
A bridge over lava lead to the arena where the majestic beast often rests....
But, luckily for us, he was nowhere to be found.
     
   
We all gathered together one last time to bid each other good bye, and we departed by magical means. This was a great event, and all did a numero uno job. Especially Leyor who managed us excellently, as well as Seymore and the other fearless pullers!
