Expedition in Steamfont Mountains






Some Council members ventured into the mines of Steamfont Mountains for a quest. There, they found several useful items, including one for Alruna's Solusek Ro armor. In the end, they even faced and defeated Meldrath the Malignant himself!
Cinders, Rutgger, Spoffid, Galilee, Saldrin and Alruna were present for this adventure.
