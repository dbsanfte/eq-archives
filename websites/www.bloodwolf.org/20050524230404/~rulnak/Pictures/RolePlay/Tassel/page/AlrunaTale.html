Alruna tells a tale of a fictional Troll named Gargrim (based on one of my other character's past lives) who meets a tragic, yet noble, end.
 
 

THE GOLD HEARTED TROLL
 
 

There was once a Troll by the name of Gargrim
He came from the Swamps were the Frogloks do swim
He liked not his life, his fellows, his Home
All he wanted to do in his life was to roam

He had a sister, the fair Nukalu
Who brought him more joy than a fine Dwarven brew
He killed many Frogloks, slew many an Orc
But then came time to move to Freeport

He heard of an army for the Great Dark Master
He sought to join them, for gold they offered
There he met Teir'Dal and fought by their side
But he met Human Folks and they touched his heart

Wounded in battle, Gargrim then knew fear
A priest of Erollisi and his cures were quite near
Then Gargrim knew Love in his ears and his Soul
The touch of Erollisi had attained its true goal

Soon came a Great Battle: Evil fought Good
Mithaniel and his Knights struck where Innoruuk stood
On the side of Evil, Gargrim fought hard
Once the battle was won, he felt very sad

"Why the killing?" He asked, now that he knew true Love
He saw a shape move, her eyes like a dove's
'I must save this maiden', Gargrim decided
She lay among the corpses but was yet not quite dead

He kicked to his right and bashed to his left
Teir'Dal and Undead all 'round him were cleft
"Traitor!" they cried, as he grabbed the young girl
Hard he ran through the night, as the shadows swirled

He ran for the Temple but the distance was great
They would not let him go, as they knew only Hate
Arrows pierced his back and his legs and his arms
As he fell down they both lay on cool grass

His head was turning, and one thought was his last
Unto Love I go now, no matter my past
