About me




By now, you must be thinking, "I wonder who is this witty, charming, mysterious being who calls himself 'Rulnak'?". Well actually you're probably not wondering that at all, you must've clicked on the wrong button and ended up here.

Here's a bit of information about me, that you can skim through before you hit that 'Back' button.

My real name: Antonin Cote
My age: 25
My gender: Male
Where I come from: Montreal, Canada
My job: Software developer at Ericsson
Languages I speak: French and English. A teeny tiny bit of German.
General description: 5'7", 140 lb., Caucasian, short dark hair, dark eyes, glasses.
Lifestyle: I live at home with my mom and my younger sister. I have a female cat named Fiona.
Marital status: Let's just say that there is no woman in my life right now who's complaining I play too much EQ. ;-)
Interests: Mostly computer games and role-playing games. And, as almost every human being on the planet, going to the movies, watching TV, listening to music.
Favorite music: Pink Floyd, Faith No More, Genesis, Peter Gabriel, Jean-Michel Jarre, Tangerine Dream, The Cure, Tears for Fears, The Chemical Brothers, Rush, Red Hot Chili Peppers, Metallica.
Subjects that bore me to death: Cars, sports, investments, cooking.
RPG experience: My favorite RPG's are Rolemaster, Warhammer, Amber and AD&D (good old 1st edition). I've played some other fun ones too but these are my favorite.
Favorite computer games: EVERQUEST. Diablo, Doom, Mortal Kombat, Warcraft 1&2, Starcraft, Heretic, Hexen, Might & Magic 3, Pool of Radiance.
 

My everquest history: My good friend Alban Wood got me interested in Everquest last year. Thanks to him, I managed to get accepted for Phase 3 of beta testing around early december. I will cherish the memories of that month of december for a long time! Discovering Qeynos, Everfrost, Innothule Swamp... Ah, those were the days!
After having had a blast with Phase 3, we moved on to Phase 4 with some other characters. I was very busy at work during January and February so I could not play as much as I'd have loved to. So phase 4 finished and I still had not had a taste of real power within EQ, my highest level character so far having been only level 10.
Then came the release of the game. I played the first three weeks, enjoying myself fully, because I knew I would have to leave soon. Indeed, I had to travel to Ireland for business. There, I could not play EQ (no computer, Internet connection or anything... Didn't even meet a soul who had even HEARD of Everquest back there). I was in Ireland from early april to mid june. Argh! You can imagine how hard it was for me not to play for 10 whole weeks.
Obviously, by the time I came back, my level 8 and my level 5's looked kind of pitiful compared to the level 40's prancing around in their silvery plate armors. Nevertheless, I patiently set myself forward once again, and started wandering Norrath once more.
This is partly why, 10 months after I started playing this game, I've only reached level 22! (Like I need to give excuses to anyone... :-D)
