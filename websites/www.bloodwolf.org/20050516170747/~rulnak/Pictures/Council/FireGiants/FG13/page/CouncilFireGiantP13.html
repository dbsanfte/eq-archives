First Fire Giant Hunt (2000-02-21)
 
 

Page 3



 

 Having vanquished King Tranix, we made our way to the Throne room.
 There we prepared to fight Warlord Skarloon, the King's right-hand man.
I managed to enjoy the moment between two buffs. ;-)
 
 
 
 Soon the battle began...
 Roars and magical incantations echoed in the massive halls...
 Our warriors suffered Warlord Skarloon's mighty berseker attacks
 
 
 
 But they bravely fought on, confident that the healers were backing them up. And we were...
 Even though one of us was taking a few screenshots... She also made sure her warriors weren't hurt :-)
 Hey! It takes skill to be a multi class Cleric/Photographer :-)
 
 
 
 With a loud thud, the final giant fell to the floor.
 We healed our wounds; luckily this time, we had suffered no casualties.
 And then we fought the guardians of the bridge that leads into Lord Nagafen's inner sanctum!
