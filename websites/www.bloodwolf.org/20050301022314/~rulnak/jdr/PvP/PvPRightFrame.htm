PvP

 

PvP is a hilarious comic strip on the web done by Scott Kurtz. It describes the every day life of the staff of a computer games magazine called PvP online. The jokes and situations that Scott describes in PvP are often things that the real geek can relate to - computer games, role-playing games and difficult relationships with the fair gender.
