Bazaar in West Freeport





The Council and the Circle of Eternity organized a Bazaar in West Freeport in late november. The event went well, there was much trading, arena fighting, laughing and drinking.
Many of my friends of the Council, as well as from other guilds, were there and even though I did not trade much, I sure laughed and drank a lot. And, as I often do, I took some pictures. I hope that those of you who attended this event will remember it fondly as you take a look at a not so distant past... :-)

        - Alruna

This was the original announcement on EQvault:
 

What: Trade/Language bazaar Where: Veeshan server, W.Freeport
When: Sunday the 28th Nov. 2pm EST Greetings!

"Council" and "The Circle of Eternity" will be hosting a small trade "bizarre" in W.Freeport sunday the 28th Nov. 2pm EST. In an effort to make playermade events more common on the Veeshan server.

The event will not be as grand as the previous FOH event, but the booze will be distributed freely =)

The event will include:

CRAFTSMENS CORNER;
Guild craftsmen will sell their wares at lowcost to all interested.

SELLOUT:
Various items will be auctioned for highest bid, starting at 5pp with a minimum bid increase of 5pp. The items (which include Skullshaped barbute, Serpentine bracers, rubicite bracers to name a few)will be sold no matter HOW low the highest bid is.

NORMAL TRADING:
Hopefully the event will attract tradesfolk from all Norrath who will auction their wares.

LANGUAGE BAZAAR:
Languagemasters will be available to teach languages at no cost.

LOTTERY:
People will be able to purchase a lottery ticket for 5pp, the winner takes all. The winning number will be chosen by the guildleader of "Council" doing a /random.

MISC.:
There will be drunk arena battles, Strip the woodelf and other happenings to spice the event up.

Sincerly
Leyor M'Chanté
Guildmaster of "The Circle of Eternity"
