WEST BAY ANGLERS, THE FEDERATED: REACH OUT AND GIVE!©

For the past few years West Bay Anglers (WBA) have extended their club’s resources to significantly help charities, and within the past few, the club donated more than ten thousand dollars to the local Muscular Dystrophy Association, and other groups. WBA is a Warwick based, saltwater fishing club, and has accomplished its good deeds in a variety of ways. Among those ways, is a weekly "Lobster Raffle" which begins Saturday, 1:00 p.m., November 4th, and will continue through March, 2001. WBA club members give up their time each Saturday to feature the raffle which is open to the public. Huge lobsters, whole hams, beef briskets, televisions, VCRs, and many other attractive prizes are offered. Food and beverages are available.

The WBA, "Lobster Raffle" is held at the FOP Lodge, Tanner St., in the Apponaug section of Warwick. For more information, or directions call 401-463-7532.

Also, be certain to catch West Bay Anglers fishing trips on cable television, "Interconnect A". Check programming schedules for days and times. The program features a variety of club fishing activities, and includes children’s fishing days aboard local charter boats. Visit WBA’s web site at: http://www.westbayanglers.org.

FEDERATED SPORTSMEN’S CLUBS FOOD DRIVE

Dan Insana, president, Federated RI Sportsmen's Clubs, announced that the organization’s food drive is underway and donations will be accepted through December 2nd for holiday distribution. Canned or dry packaged foods will be accepted. Those who wish to donate food stuffs, may arrange for a pickup by contacting Insana at, 401-739-7238. The Federated is a long established sportsmen’s and sportswomen’s, "club of clubs," and through its early efforts helped to establish many of Rhode Island’s fishing and hunting regulations. The Federated continues to maintain a strong interest in advancing the promotion of wildlife management issues concerning: fishing, shooting sports, hunting dogs, hiking, trapping, and regulations attached to those outdoor activities. Visit the Federated’s web site at: www.frisc.net.

UNITED FLY TYERS OF RHODE ISLAND: NEW PREZ, NEW MEET PLACE

United Fly Tyers of RI sports a new president and a new location for its monthly fly tying sessions. John J. O’Meara, is the group’s new leader, and an acknowledged romantic when when it comes to whipping up a fly, and he’s especially enamored of wrapping deer hair around a hook.

UFTRI meetings will now be held at the Aspray Boat House, Broad Street, right after "Pawtuxet Village", in Warwick. Located at water’s edge, a public dock and boat ramp is available for UFTRI member use.

If readers see this column before October 4th, that’s the date of UFTRI’s next meeting at its new quarters. Fly tying instruction begins at 6:30 p.m. For info about what tying tools to bring, contact, Jim Barr, by e-mail at: flyrod48@aol.com, or call 401-465-8751.

Mike Martinek will be the guest tier presenter, for the October meeting. Martinek brings streamer flies expertise, with a Carrie Stevens style. Martinek’s fly tying articles have been widely published. He is featured in New England Fly Fishing magazine. His techniques are available on video, and he is frequently a featured tier at national, international shows, radio, and television guest spots.

CAPT. JIM WHITE SEMINAR FISHING SHOWS AVAILABLE

Captain Jim White, White Ghost Guide Service, offers an educational, seminar series, on shallow water fishing. White said the seminars are interactive and contain questions, answers, and discussion after each presentation. Instructional presentations cover how to fish for stripers, bluefish, and weakfish with fly fishing and/or light tackle gear, and show steps to become more proficient and catch more fish. Included in the presentation, is White’s "Jardines de la Regina-10 Days in Paradise," a fishing trip with Lefty Kreh. It shows Cuba’s fishing opportunities, and a slice of Cuban life today. White is a member of the Ranger/Evinrude Saltwater Pro Team, and charters fishing trips in Narragansett Bay coves and inlets for at ease fishing trips and successful catches. To schedule dates and for information, contact White at 401-828-9465, or write to Capt. Jim White, 43 York Drive, Coventry, RI 02816.

FISHING ACTIVITY ON A BUZZ

Saltwater shoreline fishing can’t get any better as bonanza bluefishing took over at most of the state’s shorelines. Sturdy, surf rods were actively engaged by "sand chair" and wading anglers who brought in 6-7 lb. fish. Fly fishers abandoned freshwater trout angling to line up in attempts to hook-up to toothy, schooling, 2-3 lb. bluefish.

Along Connimicut shores, baitfish, anglers and bluefish were plentiful under sunny skies. Stripers cooperated with anglers as well, and made for successful fishing opportunities. Early morning fishing allowed Frank and Pam Tameo, using plugs, to pull in a fair share of blues and stripers. The fishing couple can be quite competitive, however, when the fishing’s that good there’s no time to kibitz.

E-mails from Wood River anglers, rightfully boasted of great fishing finds with two rainbows caught within 20 minutes by Bro Kevin, and later a 1/2 lb trout. Jeff F, fishing with his son landed an 18 inch rainbow and reports they had the whole place to themselves. Other anglers expressed fishing pleasure at the Division of Fish and Wildlife’s late harvest, trout stocking management program. Apparently, it works!
