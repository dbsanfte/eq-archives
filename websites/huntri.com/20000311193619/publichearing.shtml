The public hearing for the Proposed Regulations was held June 23rd at the Warwick City Hall.

It was sparsely attended, with only 14 citizens showing up to comment on the proposed laws.

Issues commented upon were as follows:

  • The shortening of the Prudence/Patience Island Bow Season was objected to, on the grounds that the length of the season is irrelevent as long a quota is in place to regulate when the season should end. Comments were made that, having shortened the season, it would be difficult to return it to it's prior length if the deer population of the islands turns around.
  • The removal of seven (7) days from the length of the muzzle-loader season was objected to, reasoning that with the increased numbers of muzzle-loading hunters, reducing the number of hunting days could impact dangerously putting more hunters in proximity, overloading certain hunting areas.
  • It was requested by several attendees that shotgun hunters be able to purchase a second tag at any time, as was the case last year, rather than being made to purchase both tags prior to the season.
  • A proposal was made to abolish the license and tag fee for the special paraplegic hunt, so that disabled hunters may recieve free reciprocal licenses and tags from the other New England states. It was responded to that this would have to be addressed legislatively, rather than through the Division regulations.
  • Requests were made to ease the firearms restrictions regarding the taking of coyotes. This was also refered to a legislative solution.

A correction was made regarding the closing of the management areas for pheasant stocking. The Proposed Regulations noted that management areas would be closed to upland bird hunting on Wednesdays and Fridays between October 20th and November 26th. This should have read that the management areas will be closed to all small game hunting, Fridays between October 22nd and November 19th and on Wednesday the 24th for the stocking of pheasants.

The regulations on this site have been updated to reflect the correction.
