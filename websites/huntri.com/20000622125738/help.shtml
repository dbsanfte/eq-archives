Many of what most consider the most important documents on this site; the current laws, and public hunting areas, can be accessed from the home page, the Hunting Laws page and the Wildlife Management Areas Page.

Other content, despite efforts to organize the site, may be harder to find.

When in doubt, even though many people express a dislike of it, the best solution may be to try a search for the topic your looking for. This site can be searched from the home page, or for your convenience, from below.

Start by choosing a Standard Search and in the text field, enter one or more words that may be related to the topic your interested in. Choose whether to look for ALL of the words or ANY of the words. There is no difference for capitalization. Click the SEARCH button. A computer program on the HUNT RI server will scan all documents for the words you enter, and print a page from which you can access these documents.
