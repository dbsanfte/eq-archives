Jester's Masquerade Guild Description

We are a FFA Guild! What does that mean? FFA (Free For All) = We are NOT an Uber guild, no regularly scheduled high lvl raids. This is a Fun, Family and Partay guild! Be kind to guildmates and other players, do NOT cause Jesters to get a bad name as this may be considered grounds for your removal from the guild.

Jester's Masquerade Guild Charter

In this charter you find common themes from other guilds. We seek to practice honor, commitment, fair play, tolerance and respect. These noble ideals are easily attainable when the guild shares them. We at Jesters Masquerade believe that these precepts can go hand in hand with having a REALLY good time.
At this time we have no minimum level requirement but do ask that you appreciate respect the fellowship, friendship and humor that drives this guild. We meet to play and if we are not having fun, why bother?
What sets us apart from a number of the guilds in Norrath is the fact that we believe that, while there are guild leaders and officers, it is the ENTIRE guild that should make decisions for the guild on important decisions. To that end, a guild vote will take place on issues affecting the guild as a whole. We hope that this will foster the idea that this community is one where the individual has a say in the direction of his or her guild. Above all, we stress Respect, Fellowship & Friendship over power playing.


 Recruiting Practices

Jesters Masquerade relies as heavily on its members as the members do on it. What the hell does that mean? As such, there are certain procedures we wish to follow to ensure that we are the right family for you.

  • Once you are tagged there is a 14-day probationary period for both you and us. This gives both the guild and you a chance to get acquainted and decide if we are right for one another.

  • We request that you have no disputes with any guilds at the time you petition for membership. Differences of opinion occur, we ask that you do not bring them with you.

  • Respect your fellow players. We are all equals and are due that.

  • Be generous with your alcohol. After all, battles come and go. What lasts forever is the hangover.
