Guild Charter
In this charter you find common themes from other guilds. We seek to practice honor, commitment, fair play, tolerance and respect. These noble ideals are easily attainable when the group shares them. We at Jesters Masquerade believe that these precepts can go hand in hand with having a REALLY good time. We have a low level minimum but do ask that you appreciate the role-playing aspect of the game. Role-playing, fellowship, friendship and humor are what drive this guild. We meet to play and if we are not having fun, why bother?
What sets us apart from a number of the guilds in Norrath is the fact that we believe that, while there are guild leaders and officers, it is the ENTIRE guild that should make decisions for the guild on important decisions. To that end, a guild vote will take place on issues affecting the guild as a whole. We hope that this will foster the idea that this community is one where the individual has a say in the direction of his or her guild. Above all, we stress role-playing over power playing.

Recruiting Practices
Jesters Masquerade relies as heavily on its members as the members do on it. What the hell does that mean? As such, there are certain procedures we wish to follow to ensure that we are the right family for you.

For mains, we ask that you be a minimum of 10th level. If you are of a lower level and
     wish to join please meet with a guild member, spend some time, and attend a fete. We can put you on a
     probationary status until you achieve 10th level, at which time you become a full member.
There is a 14-day probationary period for both you and us. This gives both the guild and you
     a chance to get acquainted and decide if we are right for one another. After this period, your
     potential guild mates have an opportunity to voice their opinions. Once all is settled, a welcome party
     is set up and the new member is tagged into the guild.
We request that you have no disputes with any guilds at the time you petition for membership.
     Differences of opinion occur, we ask that you do not bring them with you.
Respect your fellow players. We are all equals and are due that.
Be generous with your alcohol. After all, battles come and go. What lasts forever is the hangover.
