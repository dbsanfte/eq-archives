SoD Discovers the Planes of Power!

The fourth and newest expansion is out, Everquest: The Planes of Power, and SoD is there, discovering and uncovering the many secrets that the genious' at Sony have created.

According to EQMAPS.COM there are 22 new planar zones included in this expansion (listed below).

From the Nexus (and many other towns) you can simply click to port up to the Plane of Knowledge which is available to anyone with the expansion installed and registered on their machine. The Plane of Knowledge includes the Great Library which contains all spells for every class at every level, including some brand new spells just released with this expansion, even one or two for level 61 that you may purchase for just over 250pp.

Surrounding the library in the Plane of Knowledge you will find convenient portals to every town in the game. With a simple click, you can teleport to your favorite destination. This will become a favorite bind spot for many adventurers, I'm sure.

One of the portal stones takes you to the Plane of Tranquility, which is a beautiful landscaped plane, and home to dozens of portals to the other zones included in the expansion. Instead of walking thru a cave along a zone wall to get to another zone, there are new, ingenious ways to enter the planes, by jumping into a vat of scum for example, or clicking on a cog, or a rock, but only 3 of the planar zones are available at first, until you get "flagged" to enter the others, by completing some quests, which are unknown at this time.

The Plane of Disease is full of diseased insects and rats which hit for 300+ and con blue to 55, yet some named bosses which con red to level 61 players, which SoD is eager to explore further. I have noticed that the experience is very good in these zones, but certainly takes a well balanced group of experienced players to survive.

The Plane of Innovation is an outdoor, dirt-lined valley that you creep through, killing "metal inventions" as you progress, with increasing difficulty and some interesting loot, mainly tinkering and quest-related items.

The Plane of Nightmares is an outdoor, grass-covered zone with a dark, eerie sky inhabited with spiders, ravens, and hobgoblins, to name a few, which hit in the high 300's and also wield very good experience to a well balanced group or raid party. I have seen some high level wizards quad-kiting, and some druids charming the ravens in this zone already.

It's going to take weeks, even months before we know all the secrets contained within the Planes of Power, including some zones which are recommended for level 65 adventurers, but SoD will be there, in due time. Get your expansion asap, and join us in the new planar zones for great experience and new planar loot, recommended level 55+ for the three available planar zones, but I have seen some level 50's in groups that have some hearty tanks to take the blows.
