Duffy Woodland

Born and raised in southern California, Duffy Woodland has always been an avid fan of cartoons and comics. He enjoyed many an afternoon in front of the television watching Bugs Bunny terrorize Elmer Fudd and Daffy Duck, and he began drawing characters a soon as kindergarten. As the last of seven children, Duffy always had plenty of brothers (and a sister) to show his drawings. However, they were none too happy to see that Duffy had drawn his cartoons all over their favorite books or record covers! (Oops!) Shortly thereafter, Duffy's mom and Dad bought him his first sketchpad.

Duffy first drew his favorite cartoon characters, which included Snoopy, Charlie Brown, and other Peanut characters. He really went off the deep end at about the age of eight when he discovered The Amazing Spider-Man! After that, comics and drawing became a full time obsession.

The good news is that Duffy received plenty of high marks in his art classes. Unfortunately, there were other classes Duffy took at school that weren't quite as fond of his drawings. There were plenty of notes sent home from his math teachers, which read, " I called on Duffy in class today, and he was drawing…AGAIN!" Duffy continued to draw his cartoons throughout high school and college.

After graduating from the University of Colorado at Boulder in 1989, Duffy worked for a number of years as a drummer, and holding down various retail jobs. A few years later, Duffy started a "real" career in professional sales. Since then, Duffy has worked in a number of sales positions, which include liquor distributing sales, rental car service, litigation document support service, temporary staffing service, and food and beverage container sales. In 1997, Duffy met Steve Dubinskas, also working in professional sales. Together they came up with "In The Field" as a way to poke some fun at some of the situations and people they experienced while advancing through their various selling positions.

Still residing in Southern California, Duffy currently works as a Pharmaceutical Representative…in sales! He has a lovely wife, Tricia, a beautiful baby boy, Davey, and two pesky cats.

Steve Dubinskas

Born in Hawthorne NJ, Steve Dubinskas was raised with his younger by his loving parents, Bridget, Albert, and their dog, "Dutches". Even from a young age Steve displayed signs of his jovial sense of humor and creativity. This was best demonstrated by Steve's practical jokes on his family. "The old bucket of water on top of the door trick" was a particular favorite of Steve's, although his Mother disapproved (especially after falling victim to the prank!) Other examples of Steve's notorious pranks include slicing up his sister's spaghetti dinner with a pair of rusty scissors, and tormenting his unsuspecting childhood friends by hurling eggs at them!

At about the age of six Steve became addicted to drawing in his favorite coloring books. Many a day he spent coloring in front of his favorite Saturday morning cartoons and programs. "Super-Friends", (that dune buggy show), "Magilla Gorilla" and "Land of the Lost" were among his favorites. Steve's creative side soared as he grew up enjoying "Star Trek", "Star Wars" and other science fiction favorites to accompany his comic and cartoon hobbies.

Steve graduated from William Patterson University in Wayne, NJ in 1991. He worked as an Insurance Salesman until he headed west and moved to California in 1993. Steve, like Duffy, has worked in a number of different sales positions. His past experiences include liquor sales, consumer healthcare products, and pharmaceutical sales. Throughout his career, Steve's creative side was always present helping him to develop his reputation as one of the funniest and most enjoyable sales representatives to work with! Despite his selling success, Steve always endeavored to pursue a career utilizing his humorous and creative talents.

In 1997 Steve and Duffy developed the idea for "In The Field" as a laughable way to make fun of what they experienced in their selling careers. Today, Steve works in the Pharmaceutical Industry…as a Professional Sales Rep! Steve currently resides in Southern California with his lovely wife Sandy, his precious boy Brandon, and their family dog, Rielly. All of whom still endure Steve's practical jokes and humor!
