Friday, August 25, 2000

Wish List Update: Anthony Pearson has informed us that he will be attending Gathering of the Guilds. This makes him our very first confirmed guest! We're looking forward to planning some discussion panels on guild formation and management so check back for more details as they're ironed out.

More Shadowbane news here: The Shadowbane newsletter is now available in both plain text and html formats. I strongly suggest that you check out this game, especially if you're looking forward to an MMORPG that is primarily geared toward fostering interaction within and between organized groups (i.e.: Guilds). Sign up for the monthly newsletter here.

Many people have been asking me why I tend to cover Shadowbane more than some of the other games. Shadowbane is the first MMORPG to be primarily designed to take advantage of peoples' tendencies to form up into groups. Guilds are going to be THE focus in this game. Don't get me wrong; I fully intend unleash the fury of my siege machinery on YOUR city so don't think I'll be all-work-no-play :) We feel that Shadowbane is going to offer a lot to the Guild community and therefore deserves the extra attention.

We here at GotG are also keeping a close eye on the development of WWII Online. There are many groups already forming for this game so it looks promising for those that find it a lot of fun to play within a group. Remember, if it affects the Guild community, you'll find the details here first :)
