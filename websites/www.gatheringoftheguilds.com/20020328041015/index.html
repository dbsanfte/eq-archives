Saturday, February 16, 2002

The Dragon*Con 2002 website has recently been updated with a the preliminary guest and band list. It's already looking like its going to be another great show so if you haven't already, get your memberships and hotel reservations made soon. We're in full gear now gang, look for more updates soon...

Monday, January 14th, 2002

Ah yes, back in Atlanta. I'm posting the remaining pictures as you read this and will also include some links to other sites that feature coverage of the event. My main purpose of attending the Shadowbane preview party was to cover the event and the people that made it happen: Wolfpack Studios and the throngs of rabid fans! I don't have any shots of the game or information about the game but don't worry. There were plenty of people there covering that including a few from Shadowbane Warcry and Phyzik.

Vosx, Ashen, Chewy, Meridian, and the rest of the gang were a great bunch. I did get to speak with them about the possibility of coming to Dragon*Con this year and things are looking fairly good. If I get a definite answer, you'll know here first.

Sunday, January 13th, 2002

What a night! Didn't get the chance to update yesterday but I've put up many of the pictures I took of the event. Go check them out! There are more to post but I need to check out of the hotel and hit the road. Look for another update sometime this evening.

Saturday, January 12th, 2002

Welcome to today's first update! I went to the Copper Tank last night and yes, they have good beer! I can totally understand why this place was chosen for the party.

Well, I'm heading on in to see how things are going. If I can find a network connection while I'm there, dialup or otherwise, I'll post updates from there. If not, I'll make a stop back by the hotel before things really begin to get crazy :)

Thursday, January 10th, 2002

Hey gang, here's your update as promised :) I've checked into a hotel in New Orleans and am looking forward to getting something to eat. The temporary page for pictures is up and there's even one posted, albeit just a shot of me testing the camera out in this lighting :)

Well, that's it for now, but more will certainly follow. I'll be arriving in Austin sometime tomorrow around 7:00pm local time. If anyone reading this is also planning on being there tomorrow, drop me a line and let me know!

Tuesday, January 8th, 2002

Welcome to the first installment for the 2002 convention season! As many of you already know, Wolfpack Studios and Ubi Soft are throwing a Shadowbane preview party at the Copper Tank this Saturday, January 12th. What you probably don't know is that yours truly is on the list to attend this even :) What does all this mean to you, the dedicated reader? It means you'll be able to check here for candid snapshots and updates galore!

I'll be departing the fair city of Atlanta, GA Thursday morning, stopping over in New Orleans, LA, and arriving in Austin Friday night for some pre-game festivities. I know, spending two days on the road to get there only to stay a day and then drive two days back seems like a lot but the idea of taking a road-trip through the deep-south to Texas is something I've been looking forward to since Wolfpack announced the event.

In other news...

Dragon*Con 2002 will be held August 30-September 2 (Friday - Monday). There isn't a main hotel listed on their page yet but chances are it will return to the Atlanta Hyatt Regency. For the complete story, head over to the official Dragon*Con website. Things are still pretty hush-hush concerning guests but that should all change soon as they continue verifying commitments. Personally, I'd love to see The Misfits come back as they put on a very high-energy show certain to keep you going long after it's ended. They've been to the convention a few times before and (*gasp*) actually stick around after the show to meet and chat with fans!

Dragon*Con isn't the only show we'll be covering this year so check back for updates about when and where we'll be hittin' the road.

Tuesday, September 4th, 2001

An unbelievable show! Unfortunately, my aging camera decided this was the event it was going to fail for. Not to worry, however, as there will be plenty of Dragon*Con photos posted at the convention page very soon. Special thanks to those that came to the panel and gave us your support. You had some great ideas about what we can bring to the even next year. All we know now in terms of a schedule is that next year's Dragon*Con should be sometime in August.

I'd also like to thank friends and fellow guildmates Freejack, Dr.Buzz, Merad, and Lars for attending the convention and supporting us as well. I think we made a believer out of Freejack this weekend which says a lot about just how large a variety of gamers and hobbyists this convention attracts :) Most people have hobbies but those who collect know how good it feels to come across a rare find after a long and diligent search. Dragon*Con is where many of them go to find those things most elusive.

Also very deserving of our gratitude are Elonka Dunin of Simutronics Corporation and Stephen "Lockeworth" Cairns. My inexperience in the discussion panel business was quickly dwarfed by their professionalism and ability to keep the conversation flowing. Good speakers truly are worth their weight in gold so if any aspiring journalists out there want to send in articles about events you've been to or spoken in, send in some of your work and I'll put in a good word for you :)

That's all the news for now but check back for story and photo submissions. If you have anything interesting to send in, please do so. Having said that, let's try and keep the material rated PG -those of you who went know why :)
