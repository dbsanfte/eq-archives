Friday, July 7, 2000

Welcome to the home page for Gathering of the Guilds. First of all, this effort would not have been possible without the vision of one man: Brodan of the Lost Order of Akalabeth. His desire to see this gathering happen sparked off what we hope will be a yearly event attended by MMORPG gamers and designers alike.

I recently attended the gaming convention Dragon Con 2000. I've been attending this convention for a number of years and always have an absolute blast. This year, over 20,000 people attended the event at the Hyatt Regency in downtown Atlanta. The address for the convention is www.dragoncon.org. Take a few minutes to check it out, you won't be disappointed.

Brodan was the first person to ask me what I thought of a gathering that was primarily geared toward the communities that owe their existence to the online gaming world: the Guilds. In the months that followed the initial release of Origin's Ultima Online, nobody could deny the trend that began to develop as thousands of people from around the world began to form groups with common interests "in-game." Realizing the potential this held, OSI's Ultima Online team implemented a system by which players could establish and maintain their guild presence using mechanics within the game. Since the rise of the guilds, many more features have been added to enrich their experience, such as formal titles and the ability to wage war -or ally with- other guilds. Sony Interactive and Verant's Everquest also features a system allowing for the formation of player-governed guilds. In the future there is no doubt that the concept of fostering this trend for people of like interests to form groups within the persistent-world environments will continue.
Gaming conventions have been flourishing for decades. Large technology and entertainment conventions like COMDEX and E3 are attended by more people every year. It is because of this seeming inevitability that the concept of this type of gathering makes perfect sense.
The variety of genres catered to by DragonCon is wide to say the least, a perfect environment for a large gathering. Since its a gaming convention, many of us would have a great time playing old favorites and new. As large a convention as DragonCon is, it would be very feasible to expect the likes of OSI and Verant to send their best, if they were extended an invitation.
This is where our work is cut out for us: getting the MMORPG teams to the convention as guests to meet the gamers. We need support from the gaming community. The man that runs the show at Dragon Con is Ed Kramer. The dedication this man has put into his convention is without equal. Its this level of dedication that inspired me to give consideration to trying to get the convention to host this gathering.
Mr. Kramer is probably a very busy man right now so I'd like to ask that if anyone would like to encourage him and his staff to give consideration to our most humble request, that he/she send e-mail to dragoncon@dragoncon.org with the words "Gathering of the Guilds" as the subject line.
The convention for next year runs from August 31 to September 3 so we have a nice, long time to plan for it. Let's make it count :)
