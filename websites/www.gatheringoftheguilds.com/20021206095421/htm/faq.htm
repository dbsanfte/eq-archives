What is Gathering of the Guilds?

Gathering of the Guilds is an event that will be held at the gaming convention Dragon Con in Atlanta GA, USA from August 31st to September 3rd, 2001. Our goal is to attract members of the guild community as well as MMORPG players in general. To facilitate this, we will be inviting (as formal guests of the convention) organizers of some of the more highly recognized guilds and members of design teams from companies like Verant Interactive, OSI, Turbine, and more. We're hoping to have these teams demonstrate some of the games they have in development, hold discussion panels, and meet the MMORPG community.

Who may attend the Gathering?

Anyone who wishes to attend may do so. Please remember that a membership to Dragon Con is required to take part in any convention functions. For more information about Dragon Con registration, please visit their site at www.dragoncon.org. The sooner you register, the more you save. Also note that the main hotel sells out of rooms very quickly so make your reservations soon.
