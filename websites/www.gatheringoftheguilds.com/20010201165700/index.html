Saturday, December 9, 2000

Familiar with Gathering of Developers? If you've been watching Shadowbane you are. This ones from their company info page:

"Gathering of Developers, a Texas-based computer and video game publishing company, was founded in January 1998 in response to the industry's need for a publisher who understands and respects independent game developers."

Independent game makers are a very important part of the industry landscape. This is why we feel it would be appropriate to invite representatives from G.O.D. Games and some of their talent to the event. Head on over to their page for more information about some of the independent game developers they publish for and some of the games we can look forward to.

Tuesday, November 14, 2000

We've been in touch with Wolfpack Studios and Cornered Rat Software. The formal invitation to the event via Dragon*Con will be completed this week. Will they accept our invitation and attend the first annual Gathering of the Guilds...?

Allow me to reintroduce you to the Forum and Wish List.

The Forum's use is obviously to let people post their ideas about the Gathering and who they'd like to see attend. Its also a great way to let the world know if you and/or your guild plan on attending the event. There will be more sections added to it as we draw closer to the convention so start posting!

The Wish List is where we'll be posting updates concerning those we've approached about attending as guests to the convention. This area is going to see some design changes very soon as it will be end up being a very long list to scroll through if we just continue adding names and bios the way we are now. The current plan is to segment it into groups for companies and guilds to allow for easier management and browsing. We always look forward to updating the Wish List so check that one often as its where all the latest guest information will be posted first.
