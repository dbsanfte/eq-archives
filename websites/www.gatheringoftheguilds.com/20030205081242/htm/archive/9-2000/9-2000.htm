September 2000

 

Wednesday, September 27, 2000

Check out all the latest updates at Shadowbane for more character class descriptions. There are updates nearly every day there now so it seems they're gearing up for something. We're looking forward to some guild structure updates so if you're on the Shadowbane design team and you're reading this, know that we're waiting eagerly :)

Monday, September 25, 2000

Sorry for lack of updates but since I've changed my position at work, I now work days for the first time in over ten years. Needless to say its taking some time to adjust to the new sleeping schedule. Also, we'll be establishing a new point of contact at Dragon*Con soon as Mr. Kramer is unable to continue his efforts with the convention. Dragon*Con will still be held on the same dates; other founding staff members have decided to continue the effort.

More updates soon...

Wednesday, September 13, 2000

We've been receiving correspondence from Verant Interactive about the possibility that they'll be sending two representatives to GotG. As far as names go, we can't say yet but we'll be posting them as quickly as we receive confirmation. There's also the possibility that they'll be bringing information about some of their upcoming titles to the Gathering as well. Need I remind anyone that Verant is working closely with Lucas Arts on a Star Wars universe MMORPG? I didn't think so...


Friday, September 1, 2000

The United Norrathian Brotherhood is hosting an Everquest gathering at the Texas Renaissance Festival from November 3 to November 5, 2000. Renaissance festivals are a lot of fun and from what I've heard about the Texas fest', you don't want to miss it. Two-thumbs-up for the Untied Norrathian Brotherhood!

Also in the news...

There are more screenshots and movies available for download from the WWII Online page! Get 'em in the download section. These are the first clips to show actual ground troops so pay close attention :)
