Richard Garriot

Need we say more?

Anthony Pearson

Aka "Brimstone" of The Lost Order of Akalabeth: Anthony Pearson is the first founding member of the immensely successful guild The Lost Order of Akalabeth. The list of accomplishments held by this organization is too long to list here. LOA began as a small group of players in the MMORPG Ultima Online in 1997 and has since grown into a number of other online games including Everquest and Asheron's Call. They will no doubt hold a strong presence in Wolfpack's upcoming release Shadowbane and Cornered Rat's WWII Online. Will they also form within Origin's "UO2"? You can count on it :)

Todd Pratt

Todd Pratt is the Founder of the Knights of the Gold Rose, soon to be called Oracles of Virtue. He also performs as back-up catcher for the New York Mets. Look for him in Ultima Online as Ian Stormbringer

Wolfpack Studios

You've heard the name, you've seen the game. Make plans now to attend the largest gathering of the online RPG community ever and meet the designers of one of the most highly anticipated MMORPG games to date: Shadowbane!

Origin Systems

Ultima Online is a household word among gamers far and wide. This team of writers, coders, and artists brought the first MMORPG to market in 1997 and we still can't seem to get enough. What do these seasoned veterans of the online RPG have up their sleeves now? See for yourself at Origin!

Verant Interactive

The makers of Everquest see no end to the projects they can take on. Not only do they continue to develop expansions to the world of Norrath, they've starting working with Lucas Arts on a Star Wars online RPG! If that weren't enough, they've recently announced another grand project called PlanetSide. Don't miss out on the latest announcements by Verant Interactive!

Turbine Entertainment

Turbine Entertainment: It took the award for "Best Game" in Eurogamer's Gaming Globe 2000 Awards. It won Adventure/Role Playing Game of the year from The Third Interactive Achievement Awards. Lets not forget the C/NET Gamecenter RPG Game of the Year Award. The list goes on and on for Turbine Entertainment's Asheron's Call!
