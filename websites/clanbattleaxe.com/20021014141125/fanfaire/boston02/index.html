Outside -- Inside 1 -- Inside 2

Bar 1 -- Bar 2 -- Bar 3 -- Bar 4 -- Bar 5 -- Bar 6 -- Women of CBA/VP

Welcome 1 -- Welcome 2

So Giah & Frekkels promise me we'll go dancing tomorrow night and go off to their hotel to retire for the evening. Mmm meditiating....To the bar!

Saturday: August 3 2002

10:00am - Armed with my Emarr sign again, we have breakfast. Nothing overly interesting happens until the Live Quest... 
11:30am - Frekkels gets to watch Giah stuff money in my (Siilver) blouse.

Breakfast 1 -- Breakfast 2

1:00pm - We gather in the banquet hall, again. Our speaker tells us (in her "mother voice" no less) that the
Everquest Fanfaire sign has disappeared. Across the hall you hear a few shouts of "POVAR!". Tsk tsk. Introductions of GMs & Guides & Web site people....What fun. After everything else, our speaker informs us of two new events happening this evening: a dance & a trivia gameshow. 

Mad mob 1 -- Elusive Affliction 

2:00pm - Live Quest begins. Unfortunately, I miss the first 10 minutes because I'm lost. I can't find anyone. How embarassing. I eventually get directed to them. Everyone is huddled over a table working puzzles. Occasionally, a very sweaty Hagaly runs by us to pick up completed puzzles & drop off new ones. After awhile, I get pulled from the table to "teach an NPC to dance". Wheee!
3:00pm - After teaching the NPC to dance, I get pulled along with Novichok & Hagaly to the rest of the NPCs. At one point Hagaly whispers to Novichok, "Tell (NPC Name) that I'll do an impression of a dorf roll for money". The NPC agrees and there goes Hagaly running to do his dorf roll on the red carpet out in back of the hotel...Funny stuff. Next impression to get paid for was a FD in Werewolf form (which I might add was really funny).
3:15pm - Hagaly says, "Hey Novi! You forgot this NPC!". Novichok responds, "Nah, I already did her". I swear, the NPC (femme I might add) almost fell on the floor laughing so hard. Novichok got paid 1pp for his umm "services". 
3:30pm - The Live Quest ends and the forum discussions begin. Several people head off to the discussions while the rest of us head to the bar...(See a theme here? ::snickers: 
7:30pm - The trek begins from the bar to the banquet hall. Crom & I decide to go "meditate" and proceed to sleep through the banquet. From what I hear, we didn't miss a damn thing. 

Dinner

9:30pm - Wake up...whoops - missed dinner. Dress up in my school girl outfit and walked over to the PC room. Novi falls off the bed and Hagaly runs out of the room saying, "You're killing me!". Oopsie 
10:00pm - Go down to the ballroom to check out this dance. Get this ... They have stopped the dance to host the trivia gameshow. ::rolls eyes:: These were the dumbest questions. The one saving grace is the announcer actually prompted the crowd several times to yell, "You SUCK!". So it wasn't all bad. Hagaly tries to get chosen for the last round...he doesn't get picked ...then he proceeds to sit down in front of the stage for the rest of that round. It was pretty funny.
11:00pm - The dance restarts. At first, no one besides myself, Giah & Frekkels would dance from our server. Within an hour - everyone was dancing. At one point, there was a contest (by the DJ) to see who could do the best "worm". And that turned into who could slide across the floor the furthest. Hagaly won Marines...who would have guessed it. Lets see...we did a kick line....we did a conga line...and a particular lizard and I danced to the Partridge Family. The fond memories that were formed.
2:00am - We're all invited down to the Terrance room for continued drinking and talking. I only lasted about an hour...man I was beat.
