Greetings Brothers and Sisters of Norrath...

 

We, Carpe Noctem of the Innoruuk Server Welcome you to our site. We are, and forever shall be based in Neriak, our dark homeland. Our membership includes those races and classes that are welcome within our forests. and can pass our guards without use of Illusion or invisibility. Those who wish to join our ranks must be able to do so, in addition to being a minimum of 20th lvl. and have two members vouch for their honor and skills. Many of us are true role-players and We have bonded together in a bond of Blood to serve our Queen and race above all else. Those races that have joined us have done the same. Its is our Queen's will we follow, and her desires are our own.

 

We have sworn to aid those of our kind when they are in need, so long as they do not break with our ways and associate with our enemies. Among these foul ones are presently the Halfling presence that has plague our beloved Nektulos. Their foul shrines and temples are a abomination and we look to the day when they will lay in ruin, their blood on our blades and our land reclaimed. They have no right within our lands, and should be driven out like the infestation they are. Currently the only others that we call enemy are the Class of Paladin. Their self righteousness shall be their downfall, and their wondrous halls within Northern Freeport and the rest of the world shall burn. Too many times their prejudice against our race has caused them to strike at our kind on site. Let it be known that when our strike comes, it shall not be so forward...but shall be when least expected. Look to the shadows my paladin friend....for from them shall come your deaths.

 

Although we are one of the newer Guilds in Norrath, our ranks have grown quickly. We may still not have the numbers of some of the more established Guilds we have many Allies, both independent and in factions such as the Guild the Iron League. We may not have the elders that many of the other more established Guilds, but we are a tight brood and watch out for one another above all else. Our members are expected to stand by one another, and so it comes to our Officers to settle our disputes within the Guild. For they are of the Council, and the Council's will is law.

 

Should any seek further knowledge, send a message to me at the following address and I shall replay as soon as able.

Mezzlan M`Larrn
