July 21, 2000

Idle Minds is back!

As you can see, the comic is now inked and colorized! Over the next few weeks, I will be redrawing my old strips, as well as producing a lot of new content. Of course, my artwork isn't any better, but I hope the lack of pencil and notebook paper will make the comic a bit more attractive.

What made me decide to renew my efforts on Idle Minds? Well, two things, actually...

First, I've always thought my comics have a good concept, and I like to think that occasionally they're genuinely funny. I wanted to take Idle Minds to the "next level", but I never knew how. Then one day, I was reading one of my favorite online comics, PVP by Scott Kurtz. Scott had been nice enough to post some of the technical details regarding drawing/scanning/colorizing his comic on the website. These tips were directly responsible for what you see above, so I must give infinite thanks to Mr. Kurtz. Go read his excellent strip, now. I'll wait.

The other event that made me reevaluate Idle Minds was when I was crusing the online comic "circuit", looking for tips and ideas. Though I don't care for the comic, I stopped by User Friendly. It was then that I had a revelation -- I thought, "This is one of the most popular comics on the net, and I can draw better than that!" So there you go. I'm not in this to be popular, and certainly not to make money, but the User Friendly experience gave me a little more courage to put my artwork online.

I hope you all enjoy the product of my little epiphany.
