About the Strip
Idle Minds first appeared on the web on January 1st, 2000. However, its origins go back to 1998, most notably the fall semester.

Imagine, if you will, three bored college students sitting in the back of a 9:00am Astronomy class. Having spent the collective previous nights playing Lunch Money and watching Space Ghost Coast to Coast, the three are certainly in no condition to pay attention to a lecture on globular clusters. It is important to note that one individual in particular has quite a twisted sense of humor, and a general sarcastic apathy toward college life. And so, in the natural progression of slackers everywhere, notetaking rapidly devolved into miscellaneous doodles. Inspired by classic comic strips such as The Far Side and Meg!, these doodles began to turn into real cartoons.

It is also important to note that these comics were drawn on notebook paper, in pencil. Many were even interlaced with various Astronomical formulae and diagrams of planets, stars, and flying weiner dogs (wait, those were the comics). Certainly, this person never expected that his scribblings would ever be shown to anyone. The student continues to doodle throughout his many long, arduous classes -- never sacrificing his trademark lack of drawing skills.

Fast forward to Christmas, 1999. The would-be cartoonist receives an unexpected gift from his mother -- a flatbed scanner. He shuffles through piles of paper, looking for interesting things to scan. He finds piles of his old comics and begins to import them into Photoshop. Before long, Idle Minds was born -- a website displaying the more humorous cartoons, shown in their original college-ruled form.

So there's the story of the comic's origin... If you're new to the site, you've probably noticed by now that the comic is no longer in pencil. In an attempt to improve the visual quality, I invested in some real art supplies (you'd be amazed what $15 can buy). My badly-drawn pencil comics have now evolved into... well... badly-drawn ink comics. Regardless, I think they're funny... and hopefully, you do too.

My name is Christian Wilson, and I do the scribbling you saw on the front page.

A little about me: I'm a perpetual Sophomore at the University of Alabama in Huntsville. In theory, I'm a Computer Science major, but as you've probably picked up from the cartoons, I'm not a huge fan of math and I'm trying to avoid taking math classes at all costs.

My first love in life (*ouch* aside from my girlfriend, of course!) is gaming. PC's, console, pen-and-paper RPG's -- you name it. I have never found a greater reliever of stress nor such a great assassin of excess free time. From my first computer back in the early 80's (long live Radar Rat Race!) to my new uber-machine (which became obsolete as I'm writing this), I've spent thousands of hours blowing things up, building things to blow up, interacting with things in the hopes that I can blow them up, and shooting for open nets (unfortunately, they don't blow up). Speaking of which...

I'm a complete hockey fanatic, to the point where I DJ the arena music at my school's hockey games for free, just to get a good seat. Aside from my UAH Chargers, my team allegiance is to the San Jose Sharks. They're not the best team, but they have heart -- and they genuinely love the game. That makes for more exciting hockey than a hundred clones of Eric Lindros.

My musical tastes are many and varied. I usually prefer fun music, such as They Might Be Giants and the Dead Milkmen. Even a little Weird Al is okay, on occasion. The rest of the time, I'm listening to classic alternative (Pixies, R.E.M., Smiths, etc.) or upbeat modern stuff like third-wave Ska.
