March 7, 2000

I'm baaack!

Sorry for the lack of updates... I haven't had much time to draw new comics lately, and a friend of mine has several dozen pages she was going to scan for me. Now she can't find them. *sigh*

Okay, that's just a lame excuse. Actually, I've spent two weeks straight playing The Sims. Whether or not it's really a "game" is open to debate, but whatever it is, it's addictive as hell. I don't know about the replay value though -- it seems like I'm already pretty close to doing everything there is to do. Plus, Maxis really pissed me off with their newest patch. It adds a cute little guinea pig that your Sims can feed/play with/abuse. However, if it happens to bite a Sim, they can catch a deadly virus and die! There was no warning of this in the patch description. Considering how many hours a lot of people put into developing their Sims, this is a pretty dirty trick to pull. Shame on you, Maxis.

While you're here, check out the newly updated Flying Lemurs site! That's right... The best band you've never heard of now has a home at Idle Minds. And check out the Idle Minds forum, too. Not a lot of posting so far, but that hit counter keeps going up (despite my wonderful ISP resetting it twice)... So talk back already!
