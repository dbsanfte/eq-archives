Aalinya

High-Elf Enchanter

Aalinya was taught the ways of Karana from a very young age and has become a devout believer.

She has been said by many to understand Karana in ways that no one else can. But this has brought her utter frustration as she has tried to bring this understanding to the rest of the world. She has tried through her kindness and example time and time again to help others see his ways, but has only met with apathy and confusion at best. While she still tries to lead by example, her frustrations led her to become an enchanter. She has grand dreams that one day she will be able to magically make everyone see the world the way she does, although she is beginning to see the futility in attempting such a task.

So for now, she wanders the world disillusioned, waiting for someone who understands the world as she does and knows what lurks in her mind and soul.
