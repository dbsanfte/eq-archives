Monday, July 23, 2001 - 11:00am

It's over!

As you probably gathered if you play on Test Server, I haven't been playing lately, nor have any of my guildmates. I think most of us have simply lost interest in EverQuest and have moved on to other things. I'd like to thank everyone who has ever grouped with a Rainkeeper, helped one with a problem, or even just been a good friend to the guild. I may be gone, but I will always remember the friends I made on Test Server. Good travels to you all.

--Galofel Trunkrunner, Guildmaster of the Rainkeepers
