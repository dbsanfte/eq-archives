August 4, 2000

As I promised, here's a new comic hot off the drawing board. Actually, my "drawing board" is really a door lying across two bookshelves, which has become a makeshift desk. Fortunately, I'm not above blaming this work environment for my lack of artistic prowess. But I digress...

The Idle Minds site is slowly changing over to a new look. It's mostly finished, though I need to update the Links page and add an "About the Artist" page -- though why anyone would want to know about me is a great mystery. I'm also going to try to get some life into the Forum. If you haven't checked it out yet, please do. If nothing else, post and tell us who you are, how you got here (to the page, I mean -- I know how you got here), and what your favorite mammal is.

Here's a little rant of interest to gamers: I've been playing Diablo 2 almost nonstop for the last month. Unless you've been living under a rock, I'm sure I don't need to go into detail about how this game is the best thing since British metal band Spinal Tap. However, Blizzard released version 1.03 of the game today, and ever since I installed it, the game looks like... well... monkey ass. The colors are too bright, and the light sources look all washed out. Even playing with the Gamma setting didn't fix anything. *sigh* I have a feeling we'll be seeing version 1.04 tomorrow, eh?

Update: Wow, Blizzard hosed more than the graphics. They neutered some of the base skills for Barbarians and Necromancers. I do think some of the skills were a little overpowered, but these were very drastic changes, and I feel bad for anyone who built a high-level Realms character around these now-broken skills. Fortunately, I play a Bow-using Amazon. *grin*
