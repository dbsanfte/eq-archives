Tenalin

Barbarian Warrior

In his younger days, Tenalin was infamous among the members of the Warriors Guild for having an insatiable sense of humor. He is a prankster at heart, and will do anything for a laugh (even if only his own). From practical jokes to horrible puns, nothing is off-limits to the charismatic warrior.

Tenalin first joined the Rainkeepers as a joke -- he bet Galofel that he could beat the Halfling in a footrace. Being a Barbarian, Tenalin wasn't familiar with Druidic enchantments, and was predictibly left in the dust. As a jest, Galofel said the warrior would now be indentured to the guild after such a shameful defeat. Tenalin remains with his new friends to this day.

The mighty warrior now roams Norrath with his guildmates, crusading against injustice and dishonor while still managing to keep smiles on the faces of everyone he meets. Many adventurers have witnessed a huge mass of rainbow-hued plate mail rushing by as Tenalin continues his journeys and makes a name for himself in the realms.
