July 28, 2000

Honestly, I expected to give you a brand new cartoon today.

Unfortunately, nostalgia got the better of me, and I redrew a few of the older comics that I'm more attached to. One of them turned out so well (especially after being colorized) that I really wanted to get it posted in its true form.

If you haven't been a college student in the last decade, the Dr. Staff reference will probably go over your head. In the course catalogs at most colleges, many of the entries in the "Instructor" column simply say "Staff". My friends, Bill Sabados and Chris "Toehead" Dillard, pointed out that Dr. Staff must be a very busy individual. Thus, he's a frequently recurring character in my little cartoon.

Anyway, I hope you enjoyed the look back at the history of Idle Minds, and please check back next week for a completely new, never-before-seen, and dare I say, virgin piece of... comic.
