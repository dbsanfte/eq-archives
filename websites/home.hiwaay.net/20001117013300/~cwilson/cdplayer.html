The Best of the Best
Usually this is where I put a listing of the CD's I've been listening to over the past week or so. But since it's the end of the year, I thought I'd post my favorite album released this year, and the ones that came very close. These are all INCREDIBLY good musical works, and I highly recommend that you pick some or all of them up. Without further rambling, here are my top three album picks for 1997:

Performer Album Comments
Information SocietyDon't Be Afraid ALBUM OF THE YEAR!! InSoc has put out what will probably be one of my favorite CD's of all time. Go grab some sample sound clips at www.insoc.org, and then go buy the album.
ToolAenimaAmazing CD, stayed in my car for weeks
Regatta 69Prime TimePossibly the greatest Ska album ever
