August 11, 2000

Oh my, what a long and boring week. My girlfriend's up in New York, and The Man keeps telling me to finish working on his website. Even my sweetest drug, Diablo 2, can't seem to break through the malaise hanging over my house this week.

*taps microphone* Is this thing on? I was hoping to get some activity going in the Forum, but everyone seems too shy to make the first post. Either that, or they come to my website seaching for naked pictures of Kevin Bacon (yeech), and flee when they see the comic starting to load. So come on, already. Post. Talk about your dog, or a movie you saw, or why you think granola will be the key to mankind's struggle for peace on Earth.

I keep trying so desperately hard to get into Icewind Dale, but I just can't. Vo0do0chile was nice neough to buy it for me, and the gesture was greatly appreciated. I enjoyed Baldur's Gate somewhat (though I didn't beat it -- in fact, does anyone know anyone who finished it?). I think my problem is that I don't particularly enjoy AD&D. Oh, I love fantasy roleplaying. It's the system -- levelling is painfully slow, finding magic items is very rare, and (this one's going to be unpopular) the spell memorization system is just lame. Now, I'm not one of those uber-power-gamers who gets off on a "Monty Haul" D&D campaign. But for some reason AD&D, especially on the computer, doesn't have a fast enough pace to keep me glued to the computer screen. This probably sounds strange, coming from someone who spent the better part of two years playing Civilization 2.

As usual, thank you for visiting my little corner of Hell. Oh, and go post in the forums. Come on, you know for the last five minutes you've been chanting "Granola... Granola..."
