News and Updates

August 20, 1998 - 12:04pm

Some people have been complaning that the walkthroughs on this site don't work perfectly for them. I've figured out that the reason is that FASA (in their infinite wisdom) made some of the missions harder in the version 1.1 patch. Some of the computer-controlled units will behave differently with the patched version, so not all of the walkthroughs are exact for version 1.1. My suggestion? The game is already hard enough without the patch, so unless you're experiencing crashes you should probably play through the single-player campaign with the retail 1.0 version. Mike Shanovich sent me some details on changes he noticed in the patch:


Just a quick FYI. The 1.1 patch does seem to change the game in some scenarios. The ones I noticed the most on my first time through were:

Mission 2-6: Minor change making it harder to kill all the firestarters seperate from the Catapult.

Mission 3-6: Harder to keep the Mad Cat from coming down the hill without support. Seems like someone else always wants to help it out.

Mission 4-4: The hunchbacks will all come back to the base after wandering off. Jumped my Raven over to an island for another salvage opportunity.

Mission 5-3: Minor change in that the Masakari shows up even if you don't capture #6.

Mission 5-4: Major change here!! All the Masakaris will find you no matter how long it takes. Only the first 2 at station Alpha will stay on post if it isn't destroyed (It's now the last one I take out). Best way is to burn through the forest take the turret station from behind, play bait and run with all of alpha's forces, take Alpha sensor control, leave intact, and then slide around to the bottom of the map and work around to take Beta first. Then you can repair after every Masakari battle.

Mission 5-6 Minor difference with the Lokis in the battle at the gate (they seemed more concerned with the turrets than my mech's----go figure?).


Thanks Mike!
