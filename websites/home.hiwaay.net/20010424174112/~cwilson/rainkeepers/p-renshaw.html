Renshaw

Wood Elf Druid

Renshaw was a pupil of the Heartwood Master's training, until the day he fell in love. The other Elves refused to accept his forbidden union with a dark elf. Ren sent himself into exile to be closer to his love.

It was only after a few days of his exile that the dark elf's true purpose was revealed: Ren was to be the sacrafice to her dark god in a ritual of blood. Only with the blessings of Tunare was he able to escape the dungeon within the walls of Neriak, and make his way to Rivervale.

He currently is doubting his own self, and wonders why Tunare still blesses him with her grace. He seeks to find a way to prove himself to his teacher, his Goddess, but most of all...to himself.
