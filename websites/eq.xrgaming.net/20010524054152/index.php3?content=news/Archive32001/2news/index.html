Guild Disbanded For Name?
There was some recent controversy when the guild Tolkien's Revenge for violating Verants naming policies. Some players got mad and Alan replied:
Let me wade in here with my opinion.

First off, keep in mind that any naming convention must be ruled over by people, and judgement calls must be made.

Tolkien's Revenge, in my opinion, as a guild name clearly violates the naming rules. Tolkien is a famous figure in fantasy literature. Arguably the founder of the whole genre (I've read that it was because of The Hobbit that book stores created fantasy/sci-fi sections in their stores). Using his name in a guild name is similar to using our president's name (even if we don't know who he is for sure).

There is no question in my mind that the guild name violated our policies, in fact and in spirit.

Scars Review
The folks over at PCIGN have released a review of the new Scars expansion pack scoring it 8.5/10 Here are some highlights:
The icy continent of Velious lies to the South West of Antonica, and is a little smaller than Kunark, yet larger than Faydwer. Adventurers can reach the frozen land by either boarding a boat in Northern Ro, or getting a teleport from a friendly taxi…err…Druid or Wizard. Just as with Kunark, Velious is a land that has been completely isolated from the rest of the world for as long as anyone can remember. On the continent three races have been warring with each other for thousands of years.

Before Velious was released, one of the big rumors going around was that the expansion would feature a linear zone structure much like the Plane of Air. In that zone, you must complete each island before moving onto the next. The first few zones of Velious aren't entirely linear, but they can be tricky to get to. For example, to get to the Wakening Land (the only non-snowy zone in the expansion and the location of the portal to the Plane of Growth) you have to pass through Kael Drakkel, which can be a very big pain if the giants do not like you. Once you get past those zones, you do get into the typical "kill mob, find key, enter zone" type situations that many areas of Kunark have.

That brings us to one of the cooler (no pun intended) things about Velious. You can choose to side with one of the three warring races to make yourself more welcome on the continent and to make traveling a little easier. The exact way to do this hasn’t been discovered by players yet, but if it works like faction in the rest of the world all you will have to do is kill certain creatures or NPCs or do a quest or two.

While on the subject of zones, there are also two brand new Planes for the high level players to have fun in. In addition to the aforementioned Plane of Growth (where Tunare resides), there is also the Plane of Mischief where players can encounter the King of Thieves himself – Bristlebane. You can access the Plane of Growth by touching a flower inside a tower in the Wakening Land. As for the Plane of Mischief, no one has found the portal yet, but word is that it will be a dollhouse-sized castle that randomly appears in two different zones. Both of these planes are good aligned so the evil players won't have to worry about going to a plane to kill one of their gods.

Graphically, there have been some new improvements. Previously, all armors in EQ have been just a new mesh texture. In Velious there are helms that have look a lot more elaborate than stuff from the normal game and Kunark. Zones themselves have received a facelift with even more detail, tress, and special effects. The waterfalls that hide the Coldain city of Thurgadin are especially nice looking. While Kunark shipped quite silent (new sounds weren't implemented in time), Velious doesn't share the same fate. There are plenty of new ambient sounds such as swift wind and falling waterfalls, all of which sound great in comparison of what was in the previous game.

Who Is Gordon Wrinn?
He features prominently on many Verant replies to player issues but who is he really? Verants Harpys Head Tavern released a writeup about him. Here are some highlights:
Gordon Wrinn is the Associate Producer of EverQuest Live. Also known by many as Abashi, Gordon has been a well-known spokesperson for EverQuest for well over a year now.

Gordon is originally from Michigan and, as he puts it, not from a single place, but from all over the state, having moved everywhere from Detroit to the rural areas of Webberville and Fowlerville. He attended Howe Military School in Indiana where he obtained his high school diploma, then the University of Arizona for a period of time.

Working with computers has been a long-time vocation. His first position was technical - fixing computers and printers at a small retail store in Tucson, Arizona. He learned a great deal on the job to supplement his own body of knowledge, gained from working with bulletin board systems (BBSs) at home on his own time. His management skills were noticed there, and within a few months he was promoted to Service Manager, supervising seven other people. Networking became his primary interest within a couple of years and he began to move into positions where he could further that aspect of his career.

After becoming a Microsoft Certified Systems Engineer (MCSE), Gordon moved into system design and management, in both freelance and employee capacities. After a former employer’s corporate headquarters shut down his local office, they retained him as a consultant for several months. Meanwhile, Gordon’s involvement in the EverQuest Guide Program and his astute assessments of various problems and situations brought him to the attention of Verant management. The rest, as they say, is history.

Gordon served as Community Relations Manager for Verant Interactive, Inc., until just recently, when he was promoted to Associate Producer for EverQuest Live. His management and coordination skills have proven to be valuable assets to the team.

Gaming has been an interest of Gordon’s ever since the days of Lawnmower on the Commodore PET 14, growing nearly to obsessive stages (almost a pre-requisite for getting into this business) at some points in his life. After going cold turkey for a few years in order to get through high school, he got started with bulletin board systems and from there into multi-user dungeon games (MUDs) and eventually online games.

In addition to computers, Gordon has also been an avid pool enthusiast, even to the point of competing professionally for a time. From there, he went on to pursue and obtain his pilot license. Since that time, he hasn’t found a new hobby to occupy his time that isn’t related to his job, though he keeps searching! He currently lives in the San Diego area with his wife of seven years, Marie.

How Did The Scars Signing Party Go?
Verant held a Signing Party in San Diego yesterday for Scars. They have put up a site talking about the event complete with pictures. Check it out. Some highlights from it are:
The signing party was scheduled to begin at noon and run until four in the afternoon. At 10:30 AM, when Cindy Archuleta and a other staff members arrived to begin setting up, there were already players waiting, anxious to get free posters, t-shirts, mouse-pads, and pewter action figures. Luckily, the rainclouds held off until the event was scheduled to end at 4:00 PM, at which time the very happy crowd of EQ fans dispersed and headed home to enjoy their collection of new EQ goodies.

Patch News
Here is what was fixed in yesterdays patch!

Today's patch addresses a few issues brought up by players over the last several days:

- Fixed an issue with /split that would cause people to go linkdead in the event that they used the command in quick succession.
- Fixed a bug where you could not edit socials or skills (via the hotbutton window right click) in fullview mode if you had hidden your "MainMenu" window.
- Fixed a bug where you would not see hotbuttons in "normal" mode if you had hidden that window in fullview mode.
- Fixed it so you can't use hotbuttons when looting, trading, banking, or using a merchant in fullview mode (which is how it is in normal mode, but with the inventory working in fullview mode now it wasn't blocked properly for that).
- Fixed the message for the /dynamiclights command to report properly.
- Fixed a problem where INI setting were not being read properly if you had camped or went LD to server select then reentered the game. This was causing various UI problems with window positions and colors being wrong.
- Fixed a problem with Sirens Grotto - Some people entering the zone were getting stuck.
- Fixed a problem with passage from Skyshrine to Cobalt Scar. Travel in that direction was supposed to require a key.
- We believe we fixed a problem where NPCs sometimes would hit a pet-owner no matter how far away the owner was from the pet and the NPC.
- Corrected loot drops in Thurgadin

We again appreciate patience while we stabilize following Velious launch.

SOV Teleporter Change!!
Velious is supposed to be more linear of an area than others. You solve quests and find keys and such to advance through most zones. There was an omission in one teleporter that didnt require this. Verant says:
The teleporter that takes you from Skyshrine to the Cobalt Scar is being changed. This was intended to be a keyed teleporter (a key that you need to look for).

Unfortunately, it wasn't set properly, and characters were able to use the teleporter without the key. It was intended that characters acquire this key in order to make their way into the Scar.

So we are fixing the teleporter so that it will now require a key to enter. This will happen very shortly.

I'm sorry about the error.

Dernit! You Nerfed My Sword!
There was a "nice" item being dropped in SOV on some mobs that Verant didnt feel should have been dropping such an item. They changed the items stats and, of course, there was some fallout from that (it was called an injustice) as always seems to happen. Here is Verants response to that:
This was, by no stretch of the imagination an injustice to anyone.

We changed the weapon into something that was appropriate for the creature that it dropped from. The item that was dropping there shouldn't have been dropping.

We corrected our mistake.

Leaving it as it was would have been the greater injustice. It would have promoted the idea that getting the expansion first entitled a player to items that they gained due to an error in our data.

I can't comment on the reasons for past actions about matters like this. I personally feel that this is the proper response to this situation.

I am sorry that we had to change that item. It wasn't done to anger anyone. It was done because it should have been done, to keep things in balance. There was no intent to 'bait and switch' anyone here. Yes, we missed this problem, but we fixed it as fast as we could. I'm sorry if any of you had your items changed.
