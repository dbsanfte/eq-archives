Interesting Thoughts on Exp Loss
A player posted an interesting "logical" argument against losing exp when you die. Brad took a rare opportunity to reply. The post and what Brad said are as follows:
POST: If i kill 5000 mobs and i die have i not the exp from killing 5000 mobs? Even if i die i still killed 5000 mobs and i have gained that exp.

I am a better fighter for what i have done. I should not be told that i am not as good as i once was just because i was trained by 15 mobs.

Brads Reply: The fundamental advancement mechanism in MMORPGs is Time Invested; therefore, Time Lost needs to be the fundamental death penalty if the principle of risk vs. reward is to be preserved.

The time lost can be in the form of experience loss, an experience debt (a cool idea, btw), items lost (providing it takes time to acquire items), corpse retrievals, etc.

As a corollary, being efficient in terms of time spent is the quickest way to advance (assuming advancing quickly is your goal, of course). Downtime, which is likewise often debated and criticized, is also a penalty based on the Time Invested/Time Lost principle. Minimizing or even eliminating downtime using intelligent tactics in a well balanced group is an efficient time investment. Failing to do so incurs a Time Lost penalty, though probably not as severe as dying.

Yet More Shaman Chatter
Shamans are a hot topic lately and here is the latest word from Alan:
Essentially, these are the key points that I've gleaned from this post so far (at the expense of doing much else this morning…):

Group buffs suck. You'd like them to be, basically, like casting the normal buff on everyone, but just all at once. I can easily advocate having them the same as regular buffs, with maybe twice the casting cost or something, and the same duration as the regular spells.

Healing: You'd like to be more useful healers, better able to reduce the burden on clerics. The idea of another spell at about 56th and move superior heal to 51, while reducing the fizzles on it (and the new spell), would probably be enough. Though better regens would be nice, you're aware that they aren't likely.

Pets: Enough already! :)

Alchemy: more stuff and a better return on the items made from vendors. And you need more How To books. Kilva's Skin of Flame is producing the same effect as Kilva's Blistering Flesh. Also, some potions require components, and they probably should not (levitate and water breathing as examples)

Fizzles on higher level spells: You don't like this…

DoTs: They should not be causing corpses to disappear.

Specific spells:

Torpor: "Torpor does not stack with the regen line. At the level you obtain this spell, as a Shaman you almost always (and I mean this, I have these spells on 99% of my online time - I NEVER take them off unless I've been newly resurrected) have PCP (Pack Chloroplast), regrowth of the grove, or regrowth on. Torpor will overwrite all of these spells, causing you to have to recast them. Thus reducing its overall usefulness because although Torpor is only 200 mana, if you have to then re-cast Regrowth for an additional 300 mana, you just cost yourself 500 mana for a 1200 point heal. You might as well have cast two superior heals. It'd have saved you a gem slot and casting time. Here is the important part: Torpor lasts 4 ticks (24 seconds). During those 4 ticks, at most you can hope to regain 60 health back with Regrowth. There is no exploit whatsoever in regenerating a puny 60 health back while under the effects of Torpor. Please make it stack with the Regen line so I can keep Torpor memorized and use it at will, instead of just when Regrowth wears off."

Tigir's Insects: The spell is cool, the drop is too rare.

Pox of Bert: Also too rare. But also perhaps not effective enough, might need a bit more damage output per tick.

Bane of Nife: too rare of a drop as well.

Voice of the Berserker: didn’t get fixed with the other Rage spells…

Shaman Changes
There have been alot of things discussed about shamans lately. Alan sums up alot of it with this:
I've taken a bunch of topics out of various posts and cobbled together this monster. I ended up not getting the names of the original posters, I hope you can forgive me if I don't attribute your posts or your ideas. I guess I'm more interested in addressing the ideas than giving credit…

First, some news about a few things:

1) Despite what I previously wrote about our satisfaction with the 55th level pet spell, we are still looking at it. I'm not promising any changes. But the debate continues…

2) The Rage Line: All the spells in the line should be working along the same lines as Rage now. But we did find an error. Fury was (is) giving a dex debuff instead of a dex buff. That should be fixed with our next patch (though there is no date set for a patch yet). There were some stacking issues to consider. We've looked them over, and they are working the way they should work. So, yes, it does overwrite some stat buffs and not others. That's the way it's going to stay. Changing anything about the way they stack would undoubtedly cause other stacking issues, and nobody wants that. :)

Ok, on to the various post snips. Keep in mind that this is sort of a first run through. You're getting my ideas and comments here, not company policy. I'm trying to get my ducks in a row so that I can present cogent ideas to the team.

Luclin Server

Hoping for GBS 0r journeyman boots.

we all settled down for a long, frenzied night.
