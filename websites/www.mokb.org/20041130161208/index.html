A Quick Update

It has only been a little over a week since the last update. The last update was not a standard one so I thought a generic, quick update was needed.We have been raiding steadily for about 4 times a week. The raiding committee has concentrated on tactics, variety, flags, and items. Grinding the same zone or the same set of flag mobs gets tedious plus everyone does that. MoKB likes to be different. MoKB has been hitting Sol Ro for farming, the standard PoP flags for progression, GoD and the trials for variety, and doing some Non-PoP mobs to add in some change.


Some screenshots I had laying around.

For the last month our targets have ranged from Mithaniel Marr and Saryrn to farming mobs such as Arlyxir and others in Sol Ro as well as the BoT Towers. We are also gearing up for VP and have some things in the works so our SSRA keyed members can do some raiding. We have also thrown in raids that are purely for tactics or purely for social fun. We expect each of our members to perform a variety of raid roles (some traditional and some non-traditional for each class) in order to stretch each of our member's capabilities. Recruits are treated in the same manner. We want people that are skilled, flexible, and active on raids instead of being a raid zombie or expecting to be the main assist. We are constantly shifting our key roles to different people of the guild so that everyone can experience a raid from many perspectives, optimize their skills, and basically keep things interesting.

It Came From My Garage

We have not done an update for awhile. The last one that we had done involved Gravelbeard's alter-evil ego, but that was temporary. I was cleaning out my garage this weekend and stumbled on some old books. One of them was Brer Rabbit and the Tar Baby. It was a leftover from ancient times. I was a bit curious to see if it was collector's item so I did some digging. I stumbled on the usual links. (I had time to kill since I was not eager to return to the garage.) I learned my book would probably be worth something in another thousand years. I also learned that Brer Rabbit was just a "modern" adaptation of a long line of "tricksters" that include Loki, Ananse, and the coyote in Native American folklore. While cruising around and killing time, I stumbled upon this from a Cherokee web site:


Yes, another rabbit.


Another garage oddity. 		An old Grandfather said to his grandson, who came to him with anger at a friend who had done him an injustice.

"Let me tell you a story. I too, at times, have felt great hate for those who have taken so much, with no sorrow for what they do. But hate wears you down, and does not hurt your enemy. It's like taking poison and wishing your enemy would die."

"I have struggled with these feelings many times. It is as if there are two wolves inside me; one is good and does no harm. He lives in harmony with all around him and does not take offense when no offense was intended. He will only fight when it is right to do so, and in the right way.

But...the other wolf... ah! The littlest thing will send him into a fit of temper. He fights everyone, all of the time, for no reason. He cannot think because his anger and hate are so great. It is helpless anger, for his anger will change nothing."

"Sometimes it is hard to live with these two wolves inside me, for both of them try to dominate my spirit."

The boy looked intently into his Grandfather's eyes and asked, "Which one wins, Grandfather?"

The Grandfather smiled and quietly said, "The one I feed."

So, what does this have to do with EQ? Well, I never cease to be amazed at some of things that happen. I have seen a single person totally disband a guild for some perverse pleasure, seen guilds roll over others for greed, and seen GMs follow a guild around due to all the trouble the guild has caused others. This is just within the last week. I always ask myself, "Why do people put up with it?" I guess there will always be people that are "tricksters" or are "feeding the wrong dog", and there will always be people that turn a blind eye. Anyway, enough about my garage.

MoKB has had its share of problems over the last month like most of the other guilds. A lot of guilds have had to drop their standards due to the losses. Everyone has seen mass exoduses due to promises of a greener pastures, greed, desire to see something new, or just tiredness. As for MoKB, we are holding our own. We are currently raiding Sol Ro and other PoP areas. We are also raiding targets outside of PoP for some variety. It has not been easy but we have a core of dedicated people and are recruiting people with similar qualities. Most of us are working professionals with families and all of us have real lives. We enjoy a good tactical raid, a good group, and a good beer on the weekends. We don't accept people that are guild hoppers, ksteal, roll over others, greedy, selfish, etc. We have not reduced our standards since we are a raiding guild that expects certain values from our members from a raiding and social viewpoint. We also have not reduced our standards in how we interact with other guilds.

We often stay out of the petty arguments between guilds. We have better things to do. Occasionally we make exceptions when we have to pull up our bootstraps when the crap gets too deep. We do not flag any competing guild that does not have an agreement. Not having an agreement causes arguments. On July 4th, our member that handles agreements was in discussion with a Cursed Brotherhood officer. A standard agreement was reached (must have killed the mob before, start with 5 people for backflagging, no gimping, each guild gives a heads up about opportunities, etc) and we were informed that their guild would have to vote on it. This is somewhat typical for most guilds. On July 6th, we were informed that the CB officers voted down the agreement:

"Hi xxx, this is xxx from CB. I am going to have to cancel our agreement, the majority of the officers voted it down for some reason. I'm really sorry to waste your time and maybe things will change in the future.

Good Luck and again, I am sorry for wasting your time. I wish it could have turned out different."

On Monday, CB approached us asking for flags on a raid. Our guild leader told them, "no" because they (the CB officers) had voted down the agreement. It never got implemented and stopped with the CB officers. We then learned that the CB officers were telling their members that "MoKB voted down the agreement".

Time to clear the air. MoKB will not be a part of CB's officers trying to placate their members. We will not pit our members against one another. Deal with your own tar babies instead of dumping it on someone else.

Welcome our Newest Members

It has only been a little over a week since the last update. MoKB has been steadily progressing and quickly flagging our new additions to the guild along with our other new-found friends. Last month, we welcomed members from Spiorad Arsaidh and all of them quickly became part of the family. This month, we have welcomed the remaining members of The Syndicate. They are also quickly becoming part of the family and, even though it has only been less than a week, many of us already feel like our new members have been here a long time. We are now 20 members stronger than we were back in January.

Many of us have learned that EQ requires patience. MoKB has always been stubborn and ever pushing towards its goals. Since many of our members have jobs, families, and other real-life demands, patience is already well established with many of our members. We learned a long time ago that making a journey while having fun with friends is as important as completing an objective. The incident that occurred in March paled in comparison to what happened last year, however, both incidents were related to patience. Last year it took 6 months to rebuild. This time we got back on our feet in less than a month. All of this relates to something someone said last year. What was true back then is still true today.



We have had our share of set backs, but with each set back, along with some patience, we are now stronger than before. We will be spending the coming weeks continuing to flag our new members along with our allied friends. As with any growth, we will continue to develop stronger bonds with one another and steadily march MoKB forward.

Happy Easter

A man was driving along the highway, and saw the Easter rabbit hopping across the middle of the road. He swerved to avoid hitting the rabbit, but unfortunately the Easter rabbit jumped in front of the car and was hit. The basket of eggs went flying all over the place, candy too. The driver, being a sensitive man as well as an animal lover, pulled over to the side of the road, and got out to see what had become of the rabbit carrying the basket. Much to his dismay, the colorful rabbit was dead. The driver felt so awful, he began to cry.

A woman driving down the highway saw the man crying on the side of the road and pulled over. She stepped out of her car and asked the man what was wrong. "I feel terrible!" he explained, "I accidentally hit the Easter rabbit and killed it. Kids will be so disappointed. What should I do?"

The woman told the man not to worry. She knew what to do. She went to her car trunk, and pulled out a spray can. She walked over to the dead, limp rabbit, and sprayed the contents of the can onto the furry animal.Miraculously the Easter rabbit came to life, jumped up, picked up the spilled eggs and candy, waved its paw at the two humans and hopped down the road. Fifty yards away the Easter rabbit stopped turned around, waved and hopped down the road. Another 50 yards down, he turned, waved and hopped another 50 yards. And waved again !

The man was astonished. He couldn't figure out what substance could be in the woman's spray can!!

He ran over to the woman and asked, "What is in your spray can? What did you spray on the Easter rabbit?" The woman turned the can around so that the man could read the label. It said:

"Hair spray. Restores life to dead hair. Adds permanent wave."
