Rydda'Dar Dead

Rydda`Dar at the feet of MOKB
Well they say the third time is the charm. In the case of Rydda’Dar it was for us. Our first attempt went very well considering it was the first time we had faced a mob with such high DPS. That attempt we got him down to 50%. We learnt a lot and then came back stronger and put everything we learnt into practice. Our second attempt went almost flawlessly. RD was going down very fast. However our MT eventually died and then all hell broke loose. We wiped with RD at 2%, quite infuriating. On our third attempt we came back again, put everything we learnt from the past 2 attempts into practice and after a very fast battle we walked away victorious. Great job all.
