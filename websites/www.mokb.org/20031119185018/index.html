Thursday, October  30  2003  - posted by Hyrin
We will follow the path of The Fallen.
Last night is the reason why we play EverQuest. Last night’s raid has become my most memorable event in this game. The previous event that used to hold this title was the old MoKB’s first Hoshkar kill in Veeshan’s Peak, probably 2 years ago. I think it is also fair to say that the rest of the guild feels similarly. Because this raid was so important to us, I am going to take a little bit longer to tell you the story of what happened than normal.

This was our first attempt at Agnarr, nearly everyone had never stepped foot inside the very upper levels on BoT. Most people didn’t fully know what to expect. Instructions were given and we went up. Level 1 went pretty sloppy and many fell during that fight. We defeated that level and everyone now knew how the floors operated. While people were resing and rebuffing we refined the details and filled in any gaps for people. We then ported up to the second level. This floor went very smooth. The experience gained from the first floor made this one a cake walk. The portals were killed before you could blink and Emmerik fell to a dazzling barrage of arrows and spells.

Now came time to face Agnarr and rescue Karana. We activated the Chaotic Vortex and prepared to go up. Something went wrong though. We didn’t realise that you had to right click the Vortex to go up. In the confusion we thought someone who got up had done so by hailing. In the spam half the raid ended up going up and half didn’t. We then realised what happened and got everyone up. People were dieing. We managed to get Agnarr and half a dozen adds kited around. Those alive proceeded to kill the portals. Once all the portals were dead we recovered everyone’s corpses while the mass of mobs got kited around. Due to lag and other factors the kite aggroed on the raid, people started dieing.
MOKB vs Emmerik Skyfury

MOKB vs Agnarr		 No one panicked we got the mobs quickly back under control and again resed the raid. Just when we had finished recovering our kiter died and again the mobs came and started killing us. Again we recovered. This time just as we were about to start peeling off the extra mobs with Agnarr the portals began to respawn. We quickly destroyed them again and regrouped ready to begin peeling the mobs off Agnarr. Disaster struck again, our kiter went link dead. Again we recovered. This time however we were able to start getting the mobs peeled off and killed. The first two went easy. The rest ended up all in camp together. Agnarr was then pulled back out. The rest of his friends were then killed and we were left with just Agnarr.

At this stage the zone was starting to make a lot of jokes. After all we had been giving them Agnarr spam for the last 2 or 3 hours. All that was left now was Agnarr. We engaged and with clock work precision we took him down. The mini bosses spawned and were killed with ease. I don’t think a single person died during the actual Agnarr fight.

Then after hours and hours of pure unrelenting focus and determination Agnarr lay dead at our feet. And that is the story of the sweetest victory this guild has ever felt.
