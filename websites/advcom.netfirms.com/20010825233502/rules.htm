Way of the Adventurer

Let these words hereafter define who we are to those who would know us. Let no one say that we are anything less than that which is held in these words.


Conduct

We in the Adventurers Company are Adventurers, Scholars, Hunters, Merchants, and Entertainers. In addition, and most importantly, we are, by all rights, Heroes. We are proud, and we are strong, with wisdom of travel and passion of heart; we stand against those that would be otherwise.

As such, no member will antagonize another player to duel; we are not about challenging others to prove that we are better. Let no one ever challenge your worth with words or taunts. Let no one ever get the better of you by being beneath you and bringing you down to his or her level. However, should the time come that someone does harm to you, and they choose to take from you or show you dishonor, then be not afraid to stand for yourself in combat and duel for your honor.

As Adventurers, we are not a family of beggars. We do not stoop to pestering others for items; we rise to the challenge and go out and win them for ourselves.

Courtesy should always be the first action towards any player in the game and to any member. When requesting items, buffs, ports, etc. from another guild member, please ask as a general question in guildchat. Another member may want to help or be willing but not able to help at that moment. If they say they can't or can but not right at that moment, that is them taking you into consideration. Please show them the same consideration. In addition, when requesting favors such as ports, buffs, rezzes, etc. from other players, be considerate of their situation and be understanding if they are unable to assist you. We do not want to be looked upon as demanding or ungrateful.

No member is ever expected, nor should they be asked, to leave a group they are hunting with to aid another member. If you make a request in guildchat for help, other members will let you know if they can help you or not. We don't want to be the guild that no one wants to group with because we leave a group in a bad situation to run off to locate a corpse, rezz someone, port a member on CR, etc. Those are the guilds that are not Norrath friendly. We are not elitists. We are just family.

KSing is not acceptable. If you are being KSed by a person, speak to them and try to reason with them. If reasoning does not work and they are in a guild, notify their guild's officers or leader. If the KSing is a real problem, petition. Do not turn around and get your higher level character and KS them. Anyone found to be maliciously KSing will be removed from the guild. You may send a rebuttal regarding your removal to the guild leader. However, you will remain outside the guild until a final decision is reached.

Any abuse of the guild or its members is grounds for removal. Negative attitudes towards other members will not be tolerated.

The use of the option, /anonymous is not accepted as we expect members to display their guild tags with pride. If privacy is desired, please use the, /roleplay so other members can see you are on when doing a, /w all adv.

No member should ever reprimand or counsel another member. Period. If a member feels there is a problem, they should address it to an officer, who will decide what action to take. No officer will reprimand or counsel a member without due cause, and then they must make a report to the officers' board after doing so.

All officers will report on the officers' board any violation of conduct or attendance. All violations will be addressed by the officers as a whole. No officer will council a member or officer before reporting the violation to the other officers available and discussing it. If, after discussing it, the officers find that the situation requires counseling or even guild removal, the officer taking this action will report on the officers' board the actions taken and the reasons why. Any member who is counseled by an officer for conduct or attendance issues can send a rebuttal in their defense to the guild leader, but they are not to approach another officer about the actions taken. They must wait for a response from the guild leader regarding the situation. This prevents any biased thoughts until they are reviewed and presented by the guild leader.

Title of Officer

This is simple. An officer of this guild works at making the guild, as a whole, better for its members. Officers make sure that events and raids get planned, and that issues that members may have get dealt with in a friendly and direct manner.

Officers of this guild are members, as well as servants of the guild as a whole. No officer will show favoritism to another member for any reason. Officers pledge that the good of the guild will always be put before the desires of the individual.

Any time that a member feels that an officer has misrepresented them or that the officer has broken the rules that he/she is entrusted to uphold, that member should speak to the guild leader about what has transpired, in game or by e-mail.

Guilding

1. You must have achieved 15th level.

2. The applicant must apply on the message board to request sponsorship.

3. The applicant is allowed 7 days to become sponsored.

4. If an applicant is sponsored, then the name, race, class, and level of the applicant are posted in the officers' forum. The officers then have 3 days to post 'aye' or 'nay' on the guilding of the applicant. The reason why or why not is not required. Not posting will count as a vote of agreement.

5. Should the applicant receive a unanimous vote from the officers, then the applicant's information will be posted for 3 days for the members to vote upon. Again, silence will count as agreeing. Members may post on the boards, or e-mail their vote to the guild leader or officers.

6. Should the applicant receive 1 negative vote, they will be declined membership in our guild. Why or why not has no bearing on this decision.

7. Should the applicant be unanimously voted for, then the applicant will be guilded and put on probation for 30 days. After the probation period has ended, they will be considered a full member and will receive all rights and benefits as a member.

8. Any officer may reserve the right to post a “field sponsorship” if they so choose. The officer will be allowed to make the decision of temporarily guilding the recruit prior to the recruit’s information being posting on the board. Once sponsored and guilded by the officer, the recruit will still go through steps 4, 5, and 6 as any other applicant, but, obviously, they will not have to seek sponsorship on the boards. The sponsoring officer will post the recruit’s info on the officers’ board and on the member's board immediately after guilding them for voting. Again, should this type of recruit receive 1 negative vote, they will be denied permanent membership and will be removed from the guild.  If the recruit does not receive any negative votes, then they will continue in the guild, observing the 30 day probationary period.


9. At any time during the probationary period, the guild leader or officers may decide that the new member should no longer be guilded. After a discussion in the officers’ forum regarding the reasons, the new member will be informed of the reasons why, and they will be removed.

Guild Removal

Removal of a member of the guild must be voted on by the entire officer council. If any officer fails to vote, then the guild leader will make the final decision as to what action is to be taken.
Removal of an officer will be voted on by the members. They will send their votes to the guild leader and he will then make a decision based upon the guild’s voice.
The guild leader retains the right to remove or demote any officer or member. The guild leader has veto power on all guild removal and demotions.
The guild leader may be impeached by a vote of the entire guild and officers. The votes will be sent to the 1st officer to tally, and the officer will then pass the judgement of the guild. If the guild leader is removed, he or she will become a regular member unless otherwise decided. At this time another guild leader will be voted on and promoted.

Dues

The incorporation of dues will ensure that on raids or at guild events, items, ports, or rezzes are available. Every month, all members will contribute 1pp per level of their primary character. These funds will be held by an official mule. The balance will be posted on the website, and it will be called the Bank of ADVCOM. This will not be used for any individual; it is for the guild as a whole only. The officers and the guild leader will determine what events or raids qualify for the funds.

Alliances

Any guild that we are in an alliance with will abide by the same rules of conduct that we do, as per the agreement drawn between our guild and theirs. No alliance will be accepted by ADVCOM with out the following stipulations.

1. In the alliance, neither guild will benefit more than the other. No guild shall offer their friendship to the other without knowing that the same is given in return.

2. Each member shall offer to the allied guild’s members the same comradery and courtesy that they offer to their own. The alliance provides both guilds with wells of information to draw from and friends to raid and hunt with. Remember, they may not bear our tag, but the alliance shows that they have the same heart and soul that we do.

3. There shall be equal invitation to any event or raid that is held. For events that are formally for members only such as ceremonies, weddings, promotions, or change of guild leader, the guild holding the event will have the right to decide whether or not to open the event to allies.

4. The benefits of this alliance are not to be abused or taken for granted.

5. No member will be asked to change their membership to that of an allied guild. Any member who wishes to move to an allied guild must remain guildless for 30 days before petitioning an allied guild to become a member. An allied guild will not accept an ex-member’s guild application prior to 30 days. This is to assure that there will be no hard feelings on either side regarding the loss of a member of their family. This also prevents animosity between guilds.

6. Any guild that we are allied with will remain its own separate entity, and so in turn do we. Adventurers Company will never enter into an alliance where we absorb another guild, or are absorbed by one. Any alliance formed will always respect the Charter and Individuality of the guilds involved. No guild will have say over the actions of an allied guild. If the actions reflect in a manner unappreciated by the alliance, then the alliance can be dissolved.

7. The guild officers of each guild involved will resolve any conflicts or differences of opinion between members in the allied guilds. No member will aggressively confront a member of another guild that is allied with ours. Officers are to be notified of anything that requires their direct involvement, and the officers will endeavor to deal with the situation in a diplomatic fashion.

8. With regard to guild wars, this alliance does not convey responsibility for any dispute or retribution that one guild may accrue. Should either of the allied guilds go to war, the other guild is in no way required to join in. It is entirely the allied guild’s decision whether or not they choose to join in. Should they decline involvement, it will not be looked upon as a failure to uphold any part of the alliance. The guild’s decision will be respected. Should an allied guild go to war and invite the allied guild’s members to join their ranks to fight, this change in membership would be for the duration of the war only. If at some point an ally requests to hold a Guild War as an event, this must be agreed upon by both guilds involved. If this event suggestion is declined, once again, no lack of respect will be implied. This being said does not suggest that any alliance would exist merely for the guilds to have mock wars or to support each other in the event of guild wars breaking out. However, it is stated here to make sure that, in the event that one does occur, there is no animosity on either side due to lack of involvement.
