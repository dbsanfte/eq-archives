  Our guild

    The Adventurers Company exists as a family of friends who have bonded in battle and quest, in times both good and bad, having found a common link which fuels our distinctive camaraderie.  Adventure being our focus, we seek the adrenaline SURGE, that legendary acceleration of blood flow stimulated by victory in battle and completion of our quest.  It is Adventure that drives us to master the quests of Norrath.  It is said not all people are cut from the Adventuring mold, and grow weary in the time of the SURGE, therefore a selective eye is placed on those who wish to join our ranks.  The Company strives to gain experience that is necessary to achieve loftier aims.  We will arm our membership with the finest tools with which to accomplish our set upon purposes.  And upon successful achievement, and even on the path to achievement, we will celebrate each victory with the finest among the fine blends of distilled and fermented libations available in all the lands and seas.  For when the mortars light the Norrathian sky, and cheers deafen its citizens, all will know it is the Company who prevailed that day.

 

Our Mission

 

To pursue Adventure through mastery of the quests of Norrath,

To acquire experience from Adventure,

To be victorious in quest and battle,

To acquire wealth from our victories,

To celebrate and libate at every possible opportunity,

To strive for quality membership,

To structure our ranks as we grow in number, yet maintain a loose and welcoming camaraderie,

To coordinate events which enhance our brotherhood and sisterhood in spirit and wealth,

To plunder those zones and exact revenge on NPC’s who tormented us in our youth,

To encourage skill development among our ranks to develop self reliance,

AND

To maintain our friendship and camaraderie to spite all odds.

 

The Battle Cry of the Company:   Hail the Hooch!!!
