September 12, 2001

With the tragic events that happened yesterday in NYC, D.C., and passengers on board the airplanes, I would like to extend my deepest wishes as well as the entire Adventurers Company to all those that have family or friends that were involved.  Our prayers are with everyone.  It saddens me to realize that there are people in the world that have no value on human life.  Having a war is one thing, you are fighting against a known enemy, but what happened yesterday was the act of true cowards that need to be brought to justice.  My wish is that someday everyone in the world can accept that everyone is equal, regardless of race, religion, sex or nationality and finally have a "Peaceful" planet. 

Then again, not all wishes come true...............
