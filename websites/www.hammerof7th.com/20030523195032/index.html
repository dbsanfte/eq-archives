Hammer of Retribution is Recruiting! ~ May 21, 2003 by Unikron

The fastest-growing guild on the Maelin Starpyre Server, Hammer of Retribution is continuing its glorious campaign across the four elemental planes in our quest to reach the Plane of Time. Young but determined, our guild has quickly established its presence on the server as a powerful and resilient entity. 

As we make our journey through Air, Earth, Fire and Water, we seek a few more good people to join our team. We welcome the interest of all classes. At this time, we are especially interested in Clerics, Druids, Rogues, Shamans and Wizards.

Our raiding begins at 6:00 pm Eastern daily.

If you meet the requirements and are interested in joining please send a tell to Clarea (or any officer) in game or email her at ms_clarea@hotmail.com. You can also post an application on our forum. Click here to find out more information about our requirements and policies. 

 

En Fuego: The Fall of Solusek Ro ~ May 17, 2003 by Clarea


You may have seen Alaticky's update a week or so ago. The wannabe-gnome usurped my own update with a blinding display of gay pride in the form of a pink-dyed Yeyeud. The exhibition was so flaming it made Will & Grace look like Monday Night Football. Our bards loved it. I know Bryslin did.

Sadly, we had to take down a beloved symbol of bards and paladins everywhere. Solusek Ro, the flaming prince himself, was on our hit list. Compared to Rallos Zek, this fight was easy. Even the path to his room was short. After grinding through some events in Plane of Earth and mindless clearings of ghostly looking Akhevans in Vex Thal, this was a welcome change.

For those who have yet to take on this challenge let me just say get your hit points up! You'll be needing everyone of them. After a few delays with piggy-backing and some "practice runs" (/chuckle), we won and the guild rejoiced. The guild earned access to Doomfire, The Burning Lands (the fancy name for the Plane of Fire). Oh yeah, stuff dropped too.




Two of these knives dropped. I think they're good.

 Of couse now with Solusek Ro dead....


Our first trip to the Plane of Air was a nice one. We started in Earth, which is considered the most difficult one to master. Air is supposed to be the easiest one, and we agree. We spent a few hours there and defeated Baltador the Cursed, the Avatar of Wind, and Pherlondien Clawpike. They yielded nice items. Among them were elemental molds and tons of Runes.



Finally...

Tragedy struck our guild this week with news that Tarkum and his son died in a car accident. He was a fine magician, a beloved guildmate, and a dedicated father. Simple words cannot express our grief for his family.
~Yeyeud

Suitcases For Everyone ~ May 14, 2003 by Clarea

It's been a busy first two weeks of the month. With our victory over Rallos Zek the Warlord, it was time to go through all of the lesser PoP gods one more time and get it over with. Every god up to and including RZTW fell to us in the last two weeks. Now only Solusek Ro stands in our path to obtaining access to all of the Elemental Planes.

Well, first things first. We've started exploring Vegarison, the Earthen Badlands. Our first named mob, The Living Earth, yielded an Earthtoned Band. Not bad for a one-group mob. It was a sign-good times were ahead of us.

The Plane of Earth is a nice zone. Nice music. Lovely exp. I only have two gripes. That annoying random explosion in the background and the mount bug. If you're not careful you can form your own CoH chain. I'll let you figure that one out on your own.

Yesterday we threw ourselves at Tantisala Jaggedtooth. Remember Lockjaw in Oasis? Remember how big we thought he was compared to the other gators in the zone? Well, let's just say T.J. is no Lockjaw. All in all, it's not that tough of a fight. It just takes a bit of diligence. Actually, it requires much more. But, again, you'll have to see for yourself.

T.J. the Friendly Crocodile made Dmiss a very happy cleric. Not even the Lakers' loss to the Spurs could ruin this. Congrats to Dmiss on her Shroud of the Stonecrafter. Congrats to Tattoa on her Primal Mask of the Earthcrafter. We also got a bunch of runes, including 4 off some insignificant mob. Easy kills and nice stuff. It's like Vex Thal, without the annoyance.

Tantisala Jaggedtooth

The Warlord Falls... ~ May 1, 2003 by Clarea

Our second victory over RZTW was even greater than our first. The flawless victory gave us two Blades of War. Congrats to Ardenia and Zech on their new weapons.

Just a little over two months since the formation of our guild, Rallos Zek the Warlord fell and granted a majority of our members access to the Elemental Planes.

The victory is twice as sweet for us, however. Some of us came from guilds that already had access to the Elemental Planes. Others could have joined one easily. Our members chose the unchartered waters of a new guild on a new server striving for great things.

Tonight, more than ever, our faith and commitment is rewarded. The Hammer of Retribution has swung once again...and victory is ours.

The fall of the Warlord caps a magnificent day in which Emperor Ssraeshza and Lord Mithaniel Marr also fell to our mighty guild.

Rallos Zek the Warlord
