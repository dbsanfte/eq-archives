1. Loot.
A. For now Brenos will be distributing loot, and maintaining a Log.
B. After a couple weeks and the Class Officers have had the opportunity to know their people they will be distributing loot for their class, I recommend that everyone reply to the wish list post in their class forum.

2. Twinks / 2boxers.
A. Only one main per Person! No exceptions!
B. Only Mains are eligible for loot, if no one wants a nodrop item it will be lotto’d to Twinks that are present and taking part in the raid, droppable items will be added to the guild bank.

3. First Time Removable Offences.
A. Ninja Looting.
B. Intentionally Training another raid.
C. Any VI bannable offence.

4. Expectations.
A. All members are expected to behave in a Dignified manner at all times, no excessive OOC or Shouting, no excessive careless or deliberate training. No kiting mobs in particular GY’s. If in need help your fellow guildies and they in turn will help you.

5. Guild Goals.
A. Primary – Flagging through to Elemental Planes.
B. Secondary – Gearing up members

6. Server Policy.
A. While you have a HoR guildtag above your head you will treat Everyone from Newbies to Ubers on Maelin with respect, you will not KillSteal, you will not CampSteal, you will not take part any sort of OOC or Shout flaming. If you come across someone you do not like for whatever reason then ignore them. If you have a dispute regarding a camp or loot with out of guild groups then resolve it peacefully or move on. Tarnishing Guild image will not be tolerated by anyone.

7. Guild Policy.
A. Treat everyone with respect period. If you have a problem with someone bring it to their Class Officers attention, if that does not resolve it to your satisfaction bring it to my attention. No flaming in GuildChat for any reason. Repeated offenders will be removed.

8. Raid Policy.
A. When raids are called everyone is expected to be there in a timely manner, in some cases this is not possible, CR ect… If you are going to be unduly late give an Officer a quick tell.
B. Raid Dodging will not be tolerated, if everyone only showed up for raids that dropped loot for them we would never kill anything.
C. Raid Chat is reserved for “Raid Chat”, reserve all chatter for GuildChat and keep the Raid Chat as clear as possible.
D. PAY ATTENTION, not doing so is the single most important reason raids fail.
E. AFK, if you go let someone know! Everyone has a role and everyone else is depending on you to be there for yours when needed. All PoP raids require a lot of movement if you must go AFK put the flag up and inform your group.
F. If you have a recommendation mid raid pass it to a Raid Officer, don’t express in it Raid/Guild chat during the raid, nothing is more confusing for newer people that hearing conflicting instructions while under stress.

9. HAVE FUN!
