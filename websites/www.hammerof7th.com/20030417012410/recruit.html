Welcome to the Hammer of Retribution's recruitment section. Hammer of Retribution is a high end raid guild on the Maelin Starypyre server. We raid daily and expect members that are looking to join also have high attendance, good knowledge of their class, sharp focus on raids and dedication.

Requirements:
1. Must be level 65 with at least 70aa's.
2. Must have these flags: PoS, PoV, BoT, *HoH, *CoD, Tactics, and Torment.
3. Must play Daily between the hours of 7pm and 12am EST.
