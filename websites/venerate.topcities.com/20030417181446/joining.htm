To ensure the quality of recruiting, the guild leader of Venerate has came up with this simple document to help reserve the rights to only skilled and qualified applicants. If you are interested in joining, it's advised you read the whole document thoroughly and understand it all.

In order to join Venerate, you must a certain amount of requirements.

+1
-You must of the specific level to join for your class. If you are of a lower level, this requirement CAN possibly be bent, but under very few circumstances.
Cleric - 45
Enchanter - 49
Warrior - 52
Ranger - 52
Bard - 45
Druid - 55
Shadow Knight - 50
Paladin - 50
Beastlord - 45
Monk - 50
Wizard - 50
Necromancer - 49
Magician - 50
Shaman - 49
Rogue - 50
-You must have a Sebilis key. If you do not have a Sebilis key, you must acquire one.
-You must have an signiface of your epic done.
-You must come on at least THREE raids and TWO events in order to apply.
-You must have a cool attitude about all things.
-You must be aligned with the Coldain and Claws of Veeshan. Indifferent or better is required.
-You need resist gear. Getting all resists 80+ is practically a must. Getting all resists over 80 at the same time is even better.

+2
-Initial screening will take place after you apply. This will last a duration of five days and will include any comments on you between guild members. It would be best to raid with us as much as you can, and meet as many people from
  Venerate as possible.

+3
-After this, you will need a sponsor. The sponsor will have to know you and will talk to the guild leader about you. We will then let you into the guild for a duration of seven days, and in that time if anything happens, you will be
  rejected from entering Venerate on a permanent basis. If no complaints come up against you, you will enter the guild.
