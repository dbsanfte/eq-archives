Velketor's Labyrinth


Velketors Labyrinth is an upwards zone in that you have to go upwards to progress in. Dead Man Floating and Levitate are disabled in the zone and will be stripped when you enter. You can enter from the Great Divide.

The entire front area consists of icy slopes and ramps with clever traps scattered around that consist of holes in the path that will plunge you to lower levels (or back to the floor). You will take approximately 220 points of damage for each level you fall, with a max of about 880 points if you fall from the top.

Most mobs in the front end consist of mid 40's spiders, however there are many that are either invisible, can see invisibile, or both, so it is difficult to proceed without fighting your way every step. Also, some spiders are rogues that can backstab for in excess of 260 points of damage - you do not want to be trying to run back down icy ramps with these things at your back.

The fun really starts, however, when you get to the Keep. Once you reach the upper levels of the front end, you will be able to see the Keep, but will not be able to get to it because there is a transparent wall blocking access. There is a very difficult transition that allows you (via a jump in a secret area from one icy slope to another) to move beyond the front maze area to the Keep.

However, be warned. Mobs in the Keep area are mid 50's and up. They have huge aggro radii, can see invis, can summon, and basically mess up your day. There is a nasty spawn right past the icy transition area, so that by the time you realize you have figured out how to get to the Keep, you are usually dead. Toss in a couple of areas that are very tough to retrieve a corpse from, and you will see that this zone should really only be attempted with at least two full parties of level 50+ persons. You can hunt the spiders in the front with a lesser group, but the loot is very lean (the spiders do not drop any cash), and the risks are great.

Velketor himself is deep within the keep.  Little is known except that he hits for 850 (sometimes doubles) and casts more spells than you can possibly imagine.  A very tough encounter for a very challenging zone.

Zone Desc by Kragmire Thundertow, Tunare.
