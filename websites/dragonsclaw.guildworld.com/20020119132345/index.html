Guidelines

Dragon's Claw has developed a set of guidelines that we believe every member should follow.  It is important to understand that these are guidelines and not rules.  We expect everyone in the guild is mature enough to represent the Guild in a respectable fashion throughout Norrath.  With this in mind, here are some of the guidelines to follow:

  • Maintain your pledge of life to the Lady Valacian and her Guild
  • Respect all other characters in Norrath
  • Follow all rules and policies the gods of Norrath have set out
  • Guild Officers are high level members within our family.  Treat them with the same authority as you would treat the Guild Leader
  • Guildsay is not to be used for flaming others - if you have a personal problem with a member, take it to a tell.  If ye cannot resolve the issue, bring it up with an Officer
  • Profanity is not accepted within the Guild
  • Extended absences should be reported to your Guild leader to insure you are not removed from the Guild for being inactive for a long period of time
  • The Guild leader has every right to remove you from the Guild as he/she sees fit
  • Third party utilities used with Everquest are prohibited.  If you are suspected of using such a utility,  you will be reported to a GM.  If the GM finds reasonable evidence that you have been cheating, you will be removed from the Guild and most likely punished by the Gods of Norrath.
