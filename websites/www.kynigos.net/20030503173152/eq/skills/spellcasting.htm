The major schools of magic are: Abjuration, Alteration, Conjuration, Divination, and Evocation. Each spell belongs to one of these schools. Every time you cast a spell, you have a chance to improve that school's skill. A higher skill means less chance of fizzling. A fizzle costs you a fraction of mana for the failed spell.

The official formula from Verant is:
  • There is always a 5% chance of fizzling.
  • There is always a 5% chance of not fizzling.
  • For INT based casters. INT is the prime stat; for WIS based casters, WIS is the Prime stat; and for bards, DEX and CHA are prime stats.
  • For a typical spell/song gained at level L, with an average Prime stat of 75, a channeling skill of 5xL, there is a base 20% chance to fizzle.
  • Each additonal Prime point beyond 5xL decreases fizzle chance by 1%.
  • Higher Prime stat scores (formula unknown) reduce fizzle chance.
  • Some spells/songs (unspecified) have reduced fizzle chance.

Each time you cast a spell, your channeling skill can also improve. A higher skill means less chance of interruption. Interruptions are caused by monsters hitting you or other distractions. Contrary to popular rumor, DEX does not improve your chance to avoid interruption.

The meditation skill lets you regenerate mana faster. It has a chance to improve every time you open your spell book and hit the meditate button. You must invest one skill point to learn this skill from your guild master, usually around level 4 for pure casters.

The above skills are capped at a skill of (level+1) * 5. Whenever I gain a level, I usually take a break from adventuring and practice my skills in town. This is a no-risk way to improve your battlefield performance. Find the lowest mana cost spell for a school in your spell book and keep casting it until the school maxes out. If the skill needs a target, you can use yourself if it will not harm you; or you may use your pet, a corpse, or another PC if it is an offensive spell, since you cannot hurt them. Don't cast offensive spells on NPCs in town; they will get mad and kill you. Also, do not practice on other PCs if the guards can see you, or they will hunt you down.

Buying Spells

Spells can be expensive, so I highly advise you to become proficient in baking. Some spells are not available at your local guild, so you may have to travel to other towns to buy them. Higher level spells may sometimes only be learned through research.

Mana

If you sit, you regenerate mana twice as fast as if you were standing. If you meditate, you regenerate at twice the sitting rate (but you will not be able to do anything else). Once you are 35th level, you will automatically meditate while sitting.

Pets

Pets can be very useful for guarding you while you meditate, and for being your tank when soloing. Remember to buff your pet if you can.If you zone or go invisible, your pet destroys itself. Enchanter pets are dumb and cannot be commanded; something must hit you first for them to engage it. Other pets can be commanded, but tend to fight to the death once they engage.

Pets are bad news in tight spaces like dungeons... they tend to attract too many trash mobs on your party (because they are lower level than you), and you could die under a mountain of green mobs who decided to pick on your pet!

Charming is a special case for pets. You may not charm if you already have a pet. Higher CHA scores improves the charm success and duration, as does the Tashan series of Enchanter spells. Also, the charmed creature will be extremely angry when the charm wears off! Be prepared to deal with it, by killing it, or stunning and re-charming it.

Research

Once casters reach level 16, they may put a single point into spell research. There are some spells which cannot be bought from a guild, and must be researched instead. The research is performed with a special book container purchased from your guild. Wizards use a Lexicon, Mages use Elemental Grimoire, Enchanters use Tome of Endless Enchantments, and Necros use Book of Dark Bindings. The appropriate ingredients must be combined in these containers to create a spell. A good reference for research recipes is .

Research is a skill, so you have a chance for failure. At level 16, you may only invest one point. At level 20 you can finally start puttin more points into it. While normal research gives you a chance to improve your skill, there is a cheaper way to improve your skill. The Concordance of Research can be obtained from Myrsila, a gypsy in North Karana, by giving her a greater lightstone. The concordance is a six-slot container that creates research runes. Here are steps needed:

  • Combine ingredients for an Azia rune in the concordance. For recipes, see .
  • Combine ingredients for a Beza rune in the concordance.
  • Combine the Azia and Beza rune in your normal research container (this is the step that actually improves your research skill).
  • Sell the completed rune (it has no other use).

Specialization

At level 20, pure casters can specialize in Abjuration, Alteration, Conjuration, Divination, and Evocation. Other classes can do this at level 30. The specialization skill reduces your chance of fizzling and reduces the mana cost of a spell.

One of these schools of magic will be your prime specialization, which is determined by the first specialization to go over 50; it will be capped at (level+1)x5. The other specializations will be capped at 50. When you first put points into specialization, you should load up a low mana spell of the school you want to be prime, and practice it until it goes above 50. See for some suggestions on good choices for prime specializations for each caster class. If you later change your mind about which school should be prime, there is a quest in the Temple of Solusek Ro to change it. An NPC named Ostorm can reset your specialization skills to 50 if you give him a ruby and 50 gp. You can then practice the one you really want to have raise it above 50.

Bard Songs

Songs are a special kind of magic that usually require no mana, and typically affect the entire group. However, a bard can only keep a few songs active at a time. Each song has a small duration, so a bard can keep switching between songs (using hotkeys) to keep two or sometimes three active at a time. The key is to watch the message window to see when the song has taken effect and then switch to the next song.

Bards who are tanking for casters can use their healing song as a way to taunt mobs off the casters. Bards never get the taunt skill of warriors.

Bards can save a group from a hopeless fight by using the speed song and running the group to the nearest guard or zone. The speed song can also be used for "kiteing", where a caster damages a mob, then runs away using this song; lather, rinse, repeat... very slow but it works well for tough mobs.

Tactics

Conserve mana! Ideally, you should regard your mana as equivalent to your tank's hit points. A good rule of thumb to keep slightly more mana than your tank's hit points. This minimizes downtime and keeps a reserve for critical situations. While the tanks beat on a mob, you can meditate your mana back and monitor their health bars to see if they need more help. This is especially true if crawling a dungeon; most of your mana regen will be done while standing, which is pretty slow unless you have a bard or enchanter to speed up regen.

Let your party know how much mana you have, so they can adjust what they hunt until you have fully recharged. In combat, "OOM" is the dreaded out-of-mana message, which means that it is up to the tanks to finish the fight or it is time to run!

Don't burn a mob too fast. Otherwise it will leave your tanks alone and come after you! If one does come after you, let your tanks know. If you continue to burn or hit the mob, it will make it harder for the tank to pull it off you. So usually, just stay calm, back away from melee a bit, and wait for the tank to pull it off you. If you run, the tank will have a hard time pulling it off you, while the mob will beat your backside for even greater damage!

A great spell for groups that pulled too many mobs is Fear or Blind. This sends the mob off running for a while, reducing the damage done to your group while it is gone. However, it will come back to beat up the caster when the spell wears off, so be ready to dispatch it when it comes back.
