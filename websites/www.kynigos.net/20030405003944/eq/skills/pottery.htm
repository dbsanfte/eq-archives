A complete link to baking recipes and supplies can be found at . This guide is geared to mastering the skill.

The only use to train in pottery is that items we make are used by other trade skills: baking, jewelry, tinkering, tailoring, and poison making. This is also one of the fast skills to reach master in.

Pottery takes two stages to complete a finished product. First is to make an unfired product. This is accomplished by mixing clay, sketch, water and sometimes other items in a potter's wheel. The second stage is to take the unfired product and some firing sheets and combining in a kiln. If everthing went well you should have a finished product. If you fail at any point you lose all materials used to that point.

First place only one training point into pottery. Any more would be a waste since this is so easy to do. Start by making medium jars, which become trivial around 36. Then large jars, which become trivial around 41. Now I started to work on bowls. So far i have not found out where these become trivial. At present my skill is 105. I bought everything in HH pass as well as crafted the material there. One vendor sells the sketches and has a wheel and kiln near by. Another vendor sells the clay, firing sheets and glass shards.
