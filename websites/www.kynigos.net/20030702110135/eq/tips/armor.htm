Silk can be worn by monks and pure casters.
Cured silk can only be worn by monks.
Patchwork, Leather, Rawhide, and Reinforced can be worn by:
    Druid Bard Cleric Monk Paladin Ranger Rogue ShadowKnight Shaman Warrior
Studded leather has the same stats as normal leather armor.

Cloth and patchwork cost a few gold per piece.
Silk, leather, studded, and rawhide sell for a few plat per piece.
Patchwork, Raw Silk, Cured Silk, Studded, and Reinforced are tailored items.
