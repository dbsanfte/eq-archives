This is a relatively easy way for low level characters to make money without risking death. A fishing pole costs about 1 gold, and bait is about 3 coppers each. You can catch fish, rusty daggers, or tattered sandals which can be sold back for profit. You may occasionally lose your bait, and sometimes break your fishing pole. Be careful not to eat your fish! Place your primary food in a higher inventory slot.

Fishing is very boring, but lots of folks do it to pass time while waiting for the boat. Personally, I prefer to bake or practice spells instead to pass the time!
