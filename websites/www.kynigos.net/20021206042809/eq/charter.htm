I. Introduction

Norrath is a land fraught with dangerous and powerful creatures. In order for the civilized races to flourish, cooperation is critical. The Order of Kynigos realizes that it is through teamwork that we triumph.

II. Meaning

When the children of Rallos Zek invaded the plane of earth, some knowledge of that plane was retained. Among those are several pieces of languages strange and ancient. The word Kynigos comes from one of those tongues. It means hunter. Thus the Order of Kynigos can be thought of as the Order of the Hunt, or the Order of Hunters.

III. Virtues of the Order

The Order of Kynigos recognizes the following Virtues:

Cooperation

Members of the order realize that a team can often defeat what an individual would surely fall to. Members of the Order of Kynigos should look for opportunities to create and be part of parties.

The Hunt

Members of the Order of Kynigos are hunters. The chase of game is a way of life. Game has open interpretations, of course. Fulfilling a quest or researching some arcane piece of lore are in face equal to the hunt for game itself.

Charity

Members of the Order should help wanderers when possible. This may be in any form and is up to the individual Guild Member to decide.

IV. General Order Policies as related to EQ issues

Role-playing

While EQ is a Role playing game, the Order of Kynigos will take a neutral stand on the issue. Role play, done well, adds wonderful flavor to the game. However, it is absurd to assume that everyone in our world can or will role-play. The actions, not the diction, of the individual shall prove their worth to our order.

Twinking

In general the Order of Kynigos stands neutral on twinking. Since one of our primary virtues is Cooperation, members will often provide some benefit to other members. This may even at times include a member assisting another of his own characters. Moderation in this act is encouraged and every effort should be made to make certain that the member is worthy of the gift being bestowed.

Camping

This is a fact of life in Norrath. As creatures and quest items are frequently found on certain monsters at certain times, camping exists and will continue. All members of the Order will certainly camp at various times over there careers. Fairness is encouraged in the Order and all campsites should willingly be shared with interested parties of adventurers. At times, seeking out cooperative parties is even desirable as a jointly camped site is safer to the whole.

Kill Stealing

This is an abomination to our world and should not be tolerated. Make certain that the incident is not isolated or accidental before taking action. The Order completely understands that KS sometimes occurs through error and that should always be forgiven. If a member of the Order accidentally KS's, some measure of recompense should be made to the victim to demonstrate our goodwill and sorrow for our error. If another is definitely KS'ing, /Shouts, /Petitions, and /Reports should immediately be filed. It is our responsibility to police our own world.

V. Who may join the order

The Order of Kynigos is open to all good and neutral aligned races and classes. Shadow Knights and Necromancers, being individuals that align themselves with the undead, are never to walk in our Order and should never be included in a hunting party. The inherently evil races should be approached with grave caution. While not all of their members are evil and some will certainly convert to the good, their vile races carry their reputation for a reason. Always consider when working with them that they may someday be the target of a hunt... The proposed change to this section is as follows:

The Order of Kynigos is open to all good and neutral aligned players. Shadow Knights and Necromancers, being individuals that align themselves with the undead, must continuously strive to prove their worthiness to the suspecting people of Norrath, but provided they can demonstrate the willingness to restore their faction are welcome to the Order. The inherently evil races should be approached with grave caution, but never avoided; let the character stand on merit of his actions.

VI. The Circles of the Order of Kynigos

Guild Leader

Appoints Guild Officers, can raise initiates to accepted, and can bring in initiates.

Guild Peers (Officers)

Can raise initiates to accepted and can bring in initiates. Any guild Peer may assign the Initiates quest and becomes responsible for making certain that the quest is completed. Peers can make recommendation for an accepted to become a Peer when that accepted has made great progress in his hunting. In general, a Peer should have attained level 10 and should complete a quest to be promoted. The quest should be in line with the capability and level of the prospective officer. Since the Guild Leader must appoint officers, the Guild Leader should issue (or at least approve) the Quest. As with all quests assigned by the Order, a guild hunting party can be formed for the quest.

Guild Accepted

Can recommend newcomers for initiate status. An accepted has completed his initiate quest.

Guild Initiate

The first level of the guild, may recommend newcomers for initiation. Initiates must complete the initiate quest. The quest for an initiate should be attainable for the level of the character. A general recommendation is that the Initiate quest should involve the return of an appropriate ruined pelt to make armor for them, or two pieces of spider silk. The quested item should (if possible) be converted to a piece of armor or equipment that the quester can use. The proposed quest for accepting formerly evil characters:

Evil players who wish to reform may join the Order of Kynigos. They must prove their sincerity in their initiate quest, by improving their faction in one of the Good cities (Akanon, Felwithe, Kelethin, Kaladim, Freeport, Highpass, Rivervale, Qeynos, Halas, Erudin, or Firiona Vie) to the point where they are not killed on sight by the guards. For a player that has alternate characters in the guild, we typically only require the primary character complete the quest, and promote all the alternates to the same status.

Due to the restrictions placed on evil characters who want to reform, the evil alternate characters of existing officers will not have officer status for that alternate until they have completed the reformed faction quest for that alternate.

Joining the Order

Any Peer of the Order can bring in an initiate. Outsiders interested in joining the guild should familiarize themselves with our charter and a few members. At that point they should seek out a member of the Order to join.

Note to Peers: preferably you will group with the prospective member before initiating. That is not always possible, due to level constraints. The very minimum that should be done is to have them look over the charter and get back in touch with you. Make certain that they understand that they will be initiates at first. This is a trial period with no responsibility and either party can back away if the Order isn't a good fit. (Whether or not you actually give them a guild tag at Initiation is up to you. I did with Rafa, so I guess that is the precedent)

Households

A Peer becomes responsible to any Initiate he/she brings in. That responsibility carries forward until such time as the candidate becomes a Peer himself/herself. You will be that candidates primary contact (after all, they know you first and foremost). You will assign their Initiates quest (and make certain that they fulfill it). You will be responsible for all the good (and potentially bad) things that occur within that candidates guild career. Ultimately, it should be you that recomends them to the Leader for Peerage.

Initiates you bring in (and Accepted) consititute a household per se. How formal this arrangement is will vary by Peers. Understand that you owe as much to your Initiates (in guidance, etc.) as they owe to you. Do not enter this arrangement lightly. However, do recruit good members. Part of the survival of any organization is sustaining membership.

The guild Leader will not take any Initiates. By default, the guild leader is the backup for all Peers (Rafa will be the only Initiate I take on. After that I will refer candidates to Peers that I think they will match up well with). Should a Peer be unavailable, the Guild Leader will be the Initiate/Accepted's next contact. Members should feel comfortable with as many Peers/Accepted/Initiates as possible, but as the guild grows, there may come a time when not all are quickly recognizable. To this end, I am recomending two components to the Initiates Quest:

The Initiates Quest

To become an Accepted, an Initiate must complete several tasks. While the exact nature of the quest is up to the responsible Peer, the following two components should be addressed:

  1. The Initiate should complete a quest for an item beneficial to the Initiate. This may be ruined pelts for armor, spider silk for armor (for casters), special weapons, etc. This component should be fairly easily attainable by the Initiate (based upon their level and equipment). As with all guild quests teamwork is encouraged. Other guild members may assist.
  2. The Intitiate should find and meet at least 3 officers of the guild. One of the people met must be the guild leader (As the leader of the guild is their backup contact anyhow, they should easily know who that person is and feel comfortable talking to him/her).

Any other requirements for Accepted status are the responsibility of the Peer. Please send the details of all quests to the guild leader (currently Nicophorus) and the Lore Master (currently Keleron). Also, once the Initiate has completed the quest, the Peer should notify the Lore Master to raise them to Accepted on the membership list.
