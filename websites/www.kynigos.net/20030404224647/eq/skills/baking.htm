A complete link to baking recipes and supplies can be found at . This guide is geared to mastering the skill.

This is the skill that can actually make you a few platinum once you master it. Your goal is to become proficient at making fish fillets; a stack of 20 fillets can sell for over 1p to the vendor.

If you can afford to buy a portable spit (from Traders Holiday in East Freeport), do so. That way you will not have to haul everything back to an oven in town.

Many baking components are stackable, so you will need to pick one off the stack to do your baking. Just control-click on a stack to pick up one item, so you don't have to fiddle with the stupid slider bar.

Remember to keep your primary food in a higher inventory slot than your baking items; that way you will not eat your profits!

The first item you must master is edible goo. You combine one fire beetle eye and one rat ear, both of which are freely obtained if you kill the mobs yourself. Or, if you have money, the vendors near the newbie areas usually have lots of these in stock. I have also heard of some folks employing newbies to do the harvesting. Edible goo becomes trivial at skill 21.

After edible goo, you can make sandwiches. Combine a piece of bread with rat meat or wolf meat. This becomes trivial at skill 26. If you don't feel like hunting the meat, you can find rat meat near the newbie areas and wolf meat in East Commons, typically around inn1. If you have trouble finding meat, skip this step.

Next you can master batwing crunchies. Combine one batwing with one frosting. Batwings can be found in the newbie areas, or you can hunt them yourself. There is a lady on the second floor of the mage guild in West Freeport who has an unlimited supply. Frosting (and many other baking supplies) are bought at inn1 in East Commons. this becomes trivial at skill 46. There is also a lady in East Commons inn3 with unlimited bat wings.

Next you make beer-braised items. Combine a spice, short beer, and either wolf meat, lion meat bear meat, or rat meat. The beer can be bought at any inn. This becomes trivial at skill 68.

Pat yourself on the back. You can now work on fish fillets. Combine one sauce with one fish. The fish can be obtained the slow way (fishing) or just go the port vendors in East Freeport and buy them. A successful combine gives you two fillets, which is why you can make a profit once proficient. This becomes trivial at skill 82, and you should slowly start making money. You may want to buy some more containers, so you can carry more sauce from East Commons back to port. I bought several sewing kits from the inn in North Ro, because they are light (0.4 pounds) and have 8 slots. Also handy if you want to learn tailoring.

You will still have only a 70% success rate. To improve the odds, you can practice fish rolls. They do not produce much profit, but do raise your skill. Combine one batwing with one fish. This becomes trivial at 135. I myself have only gone up to skill 110, which translates to a 96% success rate when making fish fillets.

Sometime in November a patch to EQ seems to have nerfed success rates on baking. This isn't 100% confirmed, but both Keleron and I noticed it. As a level 90(ish) baker, I used to get about an 85% success rate. Over the last 1000 or so filets, I have noticed that my success rate is down in the 75% range now. This did not effect trivial levels or profitability, just your success role. End result, I am finally starting to work on breaking the 100 level with Baking so I too can be a Master Chef... For high-level bakers, a more convient source of unlimited fish is sold at the Barbarian huts north of the lake in West Commons. This way you only have to zone once to get your supplies. I just bind myself to East Freeport when I bake. I pick up sauce in East Commons, then gate back to Freeport for the fish. Master Baker for the Impatient:
I went from 0 to 115 skill in two hours. My intelligence and wisdom were around 100, and I spent a total of 20p on materials.
  1. Buy a portable spit in East Freeport
  2. Make room for 2 or three empty backpacks in your inventory.
  3. Make batwing crunchies (batwing from EC inn3 + frosting from EC inn1) until trivial (46).
  4. Make fish fillets (sauce from EC inn1 + fish from Freeport dock) until trivial (82). Expect lots of failures at first.
  5. Make fish rolls (batwing from Freeport mage guild + fish from Freeport dock) until master baker (100+).
