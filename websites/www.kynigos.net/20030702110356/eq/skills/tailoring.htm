The main tool you need for this trade is a sewing kit. Freeport only sells small ones (2 slots), but the 8-slot large sewing kit is sold in the North Ro inn. Almost all recipes require a pattern for the piece to be made, which can be found in the East Freeport leather shop and the North Ro inn.

The size of the armor made depends on the pelt used:
  • Wolf pelt: small (dwarf, gnome, halfling, elf)
  • Cat pelt: medium (elf, human, erudite, half-elf, barbarian)
  • Bear pelt: large (barbarian, ogre, troll)
This can be a very slow skill to develop, because the components are dropped from animals you kill. In particular, spider silk, medium quality pelts, and high quality pelts tend to be in short supply.

No skill points need to be invested if you start by combining silk swatches; this never fails, and gets you to a competent level before trying patchwork.

Studs and steel boning are made with the smithing skill, and heady kiolas with the brewing skill.

Silk Swatches

Combine two spider silks.
Swatches are stackable!


Patchwork Armor

Combine a ruined pelt and pattern.
Almost as strong as leather armor.

Ruined pelts typically auction for about 1g each,
but they are a fairly common drop off of animals
in East Commons.


Shadow Silk

Combine shadow wolf pelt, silk swatch, and Necromancer scroll of Gather Shadows.
Add another pelt to make large shadow silk.
Add another scroll to make inky shadow silk.

Shadow silk is needed to make Necromancer Shadowbound armor,
which also requires some quest item components.


Raw Silk Armor

Tunic or legs: combine 2 silk swatch and pattern
Others: combine silk swatch and pattern

Wearable by monks and casters, and very light (0.4 weight).

Spider silk auctions for as high as 5g per silk.
The silk armor typically auctions for about 1-2p per silk swatch.


Tailored Whips

Combine whip pattern, medium quality wolf pelt, and 4 heady kiolas.
Not common near Freeport since wolves there do not seem to drop MQ pelt.
Also not a terribly useful item to other players.

Brewers auction Heady kiolas for about 5g each.


Studded Armor

Combine MQ pelt, pattern, and variable number of studs.
Same statistics as leather armor.

mask:                       1 stud
belt, bracer, gorget:       2 studs
shoulder:                   3 studs (this recipe is broken)
cap, sleeves:               3 studs
boots, cloak, gloves,pants: 4 studs
tunic:                      5 studs (this recipe is broken)

Smiths auction studs for about 5g to 1p each; they are not stackable.
You may see MQ pelts auctioned for 5g to 1p each.


Cured Silk Armor

Combine a pattern with some silk swatches and heady kiolas.
Usable only by monks, and very light (0.1 weight).
It also gives them the monk guildmaster look.

mask:                           1 swatch,   1 kiola
headband:                       1 swatch,   3 kiolas
cloak, gloves, mantle, sleeves: 2 swatches, 2 kiolas
tunic:                          3 swatches, 2 kiolas
gorget, sandals, sash, bracers: 1 swatch,   2 kiolas
pants:                          2 swatches, 3 kiolas

Cured typically auctions for about 1-2p per AC point.
Brewers auction Heady kiolas for about 5g each.

Wu's Fighting Gauntlets:
These are magical gloves that glow like a greater lightstone.

Combine glove pattern, 1 silk swatch, 4 kiolas, greater lightstone, vial of viscous mana.

You will need a level 12 enchanter to create the vial of vacuous mana,
which typically auctions for 10p per vial.
Wu gloves are expensive to make, and auction for 30-75p.


Handmade Backpacks

Combine a backpack pattern with a high quality bear skin.

HQ bear skins usually auctions for 10p, because folks know
that 10-slot backpacks sell for about 50p. They are useful
for high level characters who have to store lots of looted
items in the bank.


Reinforced Armor

Combine a high-quality pelt, patter, and variable number of steel boning.
Not commonly seen because HQ pelts are so rare, and superior banded or bronze is
easier to obtain. The only reason to make these is to advance your skill.

bracer, gorget:                         1 steel bone
mask:                                   1 steel bone (this recipe is broken)
cap, cloak, gloves, shoulders, sleeves: 2 steel bone
boots, pants:                           3 steel bone
tunic:                                  4 steel bone

You may see HQ pelts auctioned for 1p each (except bear, which is 10p).
Smiths auction steel bone for about 1p each; they are not stackable.


Quivers

Combine high quality bear pelt and quiver pattern.

DO NOT WASTE HQ BEAR PELTS ON THIS RECIPE!
Quivers can be bought at vendors, and the HQ bear pelt is far more
useful to make 10-slot backpacks.
