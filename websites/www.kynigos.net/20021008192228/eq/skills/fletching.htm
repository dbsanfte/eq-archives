With this skill, you can make your own bows and arrows, with custom damage and range properties to suit your hunting style. It is relatively cheap to work your way up to average skill.

Perhaps the hardest part of this trade is locating a vendor that sells the components you need. The fletching kit, bow, and arrow supplies are available in only a few locations:

  • Kelethin: Merchant Sylnis at loc -411, -520 (watch your step!)
  • Surefall Glade: Tonsia in Ranger's guild
  • South Karana: centuar merchant Ulan Meadowgreen at loc 123, -2185

There are several vendors that sell just the arrow components; I use the one at Hidden Inn in East Commons. Go to to get a list of all the vendors.

Arrows

To raise your skill cheaply, practice making arrows. An arrow is composed of a point, shaft, fletch or vane, and nock. A successful combine in your fletching kit yields 5 arrows. The difficulty of the combine is determined by the most expensive component, so just buy the cheapest components for the remaining parts. Follow this table:

Bows

When you have enough skill, you can make your own custom bows. Each bow consists of a staff, a string, plus some optional tooling and cams. The base staves are shown below: 
Staff   Damage  Range   Delay   Cost
------- ------  -----   -----   ----
Hickory 10       50     50        0.211p
Elm     13       75     51        2.11p
Ash     16      100     58       15.75p
Oak     21      125     65       69.3p
Drkwood 25      150     68      226.8p
The string type reduces bow damage in favor of a better delay. Most folks are not too concerned about bow delay, so it is likely you will just make simple hemp string bows. The optional tools and cams also have this effect: 
String  Damage  Delay   Cost
------  ------  -----   ----
Hemp    -       -       10c
Linen   -1      -4      21c
Silk    -2      -8      52c

Tool    Damage  Delay   Cost
------  ------  -----   ----
Knife   -1      -4       2.11p  (for elm or better, makes carved bow)
Plane   -2      -9      10.71p  (for oak or better, makes shaped bow)

Cams    Damage  Delay   Cost
----    ------  -----   ----
1 cam   -1      -5      37.8p   (for oak or better, makes compound bow)
2 cams  -2      -10     75.6p   (for darkwood, makes compound bow)
