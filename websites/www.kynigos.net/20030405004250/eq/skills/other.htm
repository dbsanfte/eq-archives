Most other skills max out at (level + 1) x 5. The text description of your skill corresponds to these numeric ranges: 

Awful                0 - 10  
Feeble              11 - 20
Very Bad            21 - 30
Bad                 31 - 40
Below Average       41 - 50
Average             51 - 70  
Good                71 - 80
Very Good           81 - 90
Excellent           91 - 100
Master              101 +

Swimming

You can learn this skill by jumping into a pool and swimming around. As your skill increases, your speed will too. Watch the green air bar, and remember to surface for air as needed. Also watch your yellow fatigue bar and rest before it runs out; a higher STA score allows you to swim longer. A good practice place is the pool under the mage guild in West Freeport, because you can swim in circles there. A safe place for beginnners is the pool that surrounds the Ashen Order (monk's guild), because it is not deep and presents no risk of drowning.

Sense Heading

This skill lets you figure out which direction you're headed, without needing to buy a compass. At first it will fail a lot, but it recycles very quickly. Your best bet is to make a hot key for it and keep pressing it until it maxes. You will appreciate this skill later when you get lost!

Sneak and Hide

These skills can be practiced while sitting, so you can practice them while waiting for your hit points to regenerate. It is best to assign a hot key to these. They act as a toggle; the first press turns it on. If successful, the second press turns it off. You will know if these are successful if aggressive mobs become indifferent to you. Also, you know sneak is working if when you notice that your movement rate is slowed down.

Beg

This is not a terribly useful skill, since it only yields 1 copper on a success, and on a bad fail, the NPC will kill you! However, this skill can be used as a porr man's taunt for classes that do not get Taunt (monks, bards).

Bind Wounds

This is a useful skill for classes that do not have healing spells. You can heal a person up to half their hit points with it. It heals about 1 point per 5 points of skill. It uses a bandage from your inventory, and the person being bandaged must stay still. If they move, you will get stuck; hit your stand key to get back up. Since bandages are expensive, it is handy to have a magician friend summon them for you. Summoned bandages disappear when you log out, though.

Lay on Hands

This Paladin skill heals 25xlevel hit points, and takes 24 game hours to recycle.

Mend Wounds

This Monk skill is self only, and heals 1/3 of your hit points. It takes 6 game hours to recycle, so the skill advances very slowly. If your skill is less than 100, there is a chance for failure, and also a chance to worsen your wounds.

Taunt

Warriors should constantly practice this skill in battle; keep it hot keyed. This skill makes a monster attack you instead of some other party member, which helps keep your casters alive. You will need to invest one skill point in this to get it started. Taunt works by inflicting "virtual damage" on the mob, so it can take several applications of it to pull a mob off someone.

Safe Fall

Monks, bards, and rogues can reduce the falling damage they take with this skill. Invest only one point into it, then go out and practice. Find a spot where you can fall for minimal damage (zero damage is best!) like the East Commons tunnel entrance. Then keep falling of it (you can see yourself stumble if you watch from behind).

Languages

You can learn languages from other players in your group by listening to them speak it. They right-click their chat window to select a language, then assign some group text to a hot key and spam you with it. You skill can improve up to their level. You will eventually be able to understand them, and others can understand you in that language. This is a common thing to do while on the boat on the Ocean of Tears.
