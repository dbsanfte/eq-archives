This can be expensive to learn, but pays off when you can finally create banded armor; it usually sells for about 1p per AC, and is lighter than bronze. The hardest part is finding an available forge! Here are the ones in Freeport:
  • North Freeport near bank
  • North Freeport by Groflahs Forge
  • West Freeport in Militia
  • West Freeport in Steel Warrior guild
  • East Freeport near Ithkars Armor
  • East Freeport slums (watch out for Scraps the dog!)
It is not necessary to put any points into this skill to start it; I started by sharpening rusty weapons into tarnished weapons.

Most recipes require metal bits (which you must create yourself), water, and specific types of molds. Ore can be bought at Groflahs Forge in North Freeport. You can find molds in Travellers Holiday and the tavern next to it in East Freeport. (Needle and thimble molds come from the vendor at the North Ro inn.)

It is useful to have several 8-slot containers, because molds and products you make usually do not stack. I usually organize each container to hold all the items I need to make a particular product. My sale prices are the same for boning and studs. 1PP each for orders under 5. Orders over 5 are 8GP each (so 4PP for 5 studs). I carry this pricing through 10 parts. I will not likely lower the price on larger volume orders as these things are majorly tedious to make (studs require two separate steps and boning require excessively heavy components). Both items cost about 4GP to make anyway, so an 8GP price (on volume) is very much in line with the 2X Cost pricing model.

I will take orders overnight or longer. I usually keep 5 studs and 5 boning inventory at the vault.

The forged mace is rumored to be trivial around 65.  My skill is 64
currently and I have about a 50% success rate on maces (which would lend
credence to a trivial in the 60's).  This is a very nice weapon for the
younger characters.  Stats are the same as a Mino Axe and it can be made for
under 4PP.  Sale prices of 7PP would be appropriate.  At any rate 4-6PP is a
decent profit.  These are interesting idea's for low level quest items.
