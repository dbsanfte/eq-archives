Much of this comes from the Caster's Realm site, but I have combined some of the tables into one big one. Each combination lists the raw cost for one creation attempt, and known trivial levels are annotated.

Each gem has a consistent effect, although the kind of jewelry it makes varies with the metal type. More expensive combinations have greater effects.

Abbreviations used in this table are: 
    str: strength        hp:   hit points       
    sta: stamina         ac:   armor class      
    agi: agility         fire: save vs fire   
    dex: dexterity       dis:  save vs disease
    int: intelligence    cold: save vs cold   
    wis: wisdom          pois: save vs poison 
    cha: charisma        mag:  save vs magic
I generally auction pieces for 150% to 200% of raw costs. That helps cover the occasional failure. Vendors usually like to mark it up to about 400%, unfortunately they only buy at about raw cost.

If you're learning jeweling, be prepared to blow 100's of platinum. Also, it's not worthwhile unless you're an enchanter... otherwise the items have no magical properties. Very simple to make items: just combine an enchanted bar and gem in jeweling kit.

Your best bet is to follow the trivial list until it is more economical to practice the next metal. For example, after mastering Silver Pearl, I switched to Electrum Malachite, because failing on Peridots was much more expensive. It is not necessary to enchant the metal if you are just raising your skill; the vendor buys them back at a small loss. You can make a 4% profit if you enchant the metal. There is always a 5% chance of failure, so this is not a quick way to get rich off the merchants =)
