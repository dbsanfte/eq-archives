Experience Awarded

Hackers have used packet-sniffing programs like ShowEQ to figure out how experience is awarded. The experience formula for soloing a blue/yellow/red mobs is: 

    E = 75 M2,
        where M is the mob's level.

* In some of the harder dungeons, the multiplier is increased from 75 to 80.

There is a cap on the amount of experience awarded by red mobs; no defienite numbers exist, but observers have mentioned it amounts to about a quarter bubble of experience.

If the mob is high green, only a fraction of experience will be awarded. Estimates are 1/2 to 1/4 normal experience.

Experience Required

The experience needed to gain a level is given by this formula: 

    E = 1000 C R H L3,
        where C is class modifier (base is 1.0)
              R is race modifier (base is 1.0)
              H is hell-level modifier (base is 1.0)
              L is your current level

If we assume base modifiers of 1.0, then the amount of exp required to advance each level is given by calculating E(L) - E(L-1), which simplifies to: 

    Experience needed for level: 1000 (3 L2 - 3 L + 1)

Group Experience

Players who group split the experience from a kill in proportion to their total accumulated experience. So higher level players and race/class combos with experience penalties get a larger share so they don't fall behind their peers. There is also a grouping bonus estimated at 2% to 5% per additonal group member.

If a pet does more than 50% damage to a mob, it counts as an extra member and eats a share of the experience.

Experience Loss For Death

Level 5+ characters lose experience for dying. The amount lost is based on the width of the previous level's experience bar. All races and classes lose the same amount, so the race/class modifiers do not factor in these calculations: 

    previous bar B = E(L-1) - E(L-2)
    percent lost P = L * 0.45 (L is capped at 25 for this formula)
    experience lost = B * P / 100
