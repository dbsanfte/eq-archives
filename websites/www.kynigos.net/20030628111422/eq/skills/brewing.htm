This is not a skill that will make you a worthwhile profit. You can almost always buy the final product cheaper at a tavern than you could make it. The only reason to pursue this skill currently is to make Heady Kiola, a required component for some tailored items.

No skill investment is necessary to start; begin by learning to make bog juice. After that, you may want to try fish wine, because the other intermediate recipes are expensive.
