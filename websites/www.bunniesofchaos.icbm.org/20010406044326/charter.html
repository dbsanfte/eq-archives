Bunnies of Chaos Charter

When Veeshan rended her claws through the planet that was to become what we call Norrath, she destroyed a great, thriving empire.. the empire of the Bunnies. Their cities teemed with life and commerce, and their people lead a happy life. Those claws tore asunder everything the bunnies had built.

In the Age of Turmoil, when signs of this great Bunny Empire have been discovered, a small group of adventurers learned of the terror and horror forced upon these peaceful creatures. Filled with hatred and the need for revenge they formed an alliance, to take back the land so forcebly taken by the bitch Veeshan. They are... the Bunnies of Chaos.
