Membership

What does being a Bunny mean?
As a bunny, you have every say in how the guild handles any situation. Your voice counts in any guild decision. This is your guild, treat it as such. Don't be afraid to speak up in support or in contest of anything the guild may do. Your willingness to speak up is your vote. Take advantage of it.

So you want to be a Bunny?
If you want to be a Bunny, then get to know as many Bunnies as you possibly can. We value friendship and we value our family, so of course we want to get to know you very well before deciding whether or not to make you a part of that family. Once you feel comfortable with the Bunnies, feel you know enough, and have obtained a sponsor into the guild, then the following will occur:
  1. A poll will be posted in the Bunny Cage message board by your sponsor.
  2. You will need to accrue a total number of 16 yes votes within a period of seven (7) days.
  3. If you get your 16 votes, then welcome to the family!

Again, it is imperative that you get to know the Bunnies before you even think of applying.

What would keep me from being a Bunny?
If you do not get your 16 yes votes within that time, you will not gain admittance into the guild. You do, however, get one chance to reapply to the guild, but you will be re-voted on and will only get five (5) days to aquire those votes, and it will be your last chance. So make sure to get to know as many Bunnies as you can! Also, if you obtain any "no" votes, you will not gain admittance in the guild.

As to classes and levels, the minimum level of entry for the guild is level 55, unless you are a cleric or enchanter. Please keep note of what classes we're accepting on our message boards.

What's a Sponsor?
A sponsor is required for admission into the Bunnies of Chaos. They should be a friend of yours that will help introduce you to more Bunnies, arrange for you to join in on events and raids, and familiarize you with guild conduct. This sponsor will also be responsible for all your actions while in consideration, and on probation.

How do we use the Bunny Cage?
The Bunny Cage is for full, approved members only. This means that it is your forum for speaking your mind about potential members in private. If a recruit upsets you, or does something incredibly wonderful for you, post a message about it in this board. During the voting and consideration period, use the poll with the recruit's name to post messages regarding that person. Remember, your vote is important. This recruit is a prospective family member, and treat it as if you were adopting your new pet chincilla. You're going to have to spend your active playing time with this person, so it's in your best interest to find out everything you can and share it with the guild.

Any "no" votes should at least be expressed personally to the guild leader or admissions officer. Also, each "yes" vote this individual recieves should be accompanied by insight (examples of their behavior, a story of what happened with them in your recent group, etc).

What does each vote mean?
  • YES
    I'd like to remind everyone in the guild that your YES vote is far more powerful than your NO vote. Yes means "Yes, I want this person to be part of our family, and I won't be upset if s/he gets loot over me, preference over me by level, because I think s/he is a great person!" Ask yourself if you'd feel that way before you put down your YES vote.

  • NO
    The standing rule is one NO vote means you're denied membership. That NO vote will be under scrutiny by every member of the guild, since they will be arguing for the person they voted YES for. Please remember all players have bad days, including you. NO votes will be left unchallenged if there are more than one or if your single veto is based and supported by MULTIPLE personal interactions with the member. Please only vote NO under the circumstances of these kinds of examples: witnessing ninja looting, offensive behavior, or they killed your firstborn child.

  • Not Voting
    Not giving a person their YES vote is just as powerful as a veto. If they do not receive the neccesary votes within a total of 12 days (initial 7 day then 5 day vote) poll time, they will not gain membership into the guild.

What happens if a Bunny sponsors a real pud and gets them into the guild?
  1. Bunnies shouldn't be voting in puds to begin with.
  2. That Bunny gets a HUUUGE turd and will be flagged as "ThatBunnyTurd".
  3. That Bunny shall be suspended from sponsoring any other recruits indefinitely, with a minimum of 90 days... so chose wisely.

THE BUNNY BOOT (Removal from the Guild):

Three Strikes
This is a process for removal from the guild of any new member, or current member that acts in a way that displeases the guild as a whole (loot disputes, inappropriate treatment of other members, etc.).

    First offense: In game warning by the guildleader or officers.

    Second offense: An officer's meeting will be called to discuss the offending member. Member will then go on probation for four weeks and will be excluded from special guild privledges, including epic raid requests and special loot.

    Third offense: A poll shall be held to the membership whether to keep this member as a Bunny. This vote will last for three (3) days. If the majority vote is to exclude them, they will be detagged and will not be allowed membership into the guild again.

So you want to be a Big Bunny?
Being an officer is an honor given to those that show their dedication to the guild through their participation in events and vocal nature in guild business. An officer should hold the respect of the entire membership, for their position is voted on by the membership.

Member determined officerships will be posted on the message board for nomination, and then those with a minimum of 3 nomination votes will be placed in the final vote for the position. Terms will be determined by the officer's ability to perform the duties of their position.

Loot Policy

The Bunnies of Chaos have a blanket policy of "with, not for", meaning that those that are present are in consideration for ownership of items that drop. Items that our guild acquires go primarily to those that demonstrate a need for that item. If members are not present, they have no say in what is done with the item. Preference always goes to those that can actually use the item (caster items, slashing weapons, "wisdom" items, etc). Remember, though certain items can be used by many classes (for instance Tranix Crowns, Cloaks of Flame), they will be given to the member for whom the item is most suited. Anything that cannot be immediately used by the members present goes into the "spoils" category, meaning it's offered up in a general lotto, or if no member wants the item, it can be placed in the guild treasury for distribution or the acquisition of other needed items. When a spoil is won in a lotto, then the winner is free to do with that item as they please.

Primaries come before alts. Period. Primaries can be declared on the guild member database on the web site. If a member is playing an alt more often to level them up, that does not determine "primary" status. Please keep your character information up to date on the web site for this purpose. Switching "primaries" for a specific raid is not allowed. The concept of "simulprimes" is still under discussion.

Planar loot is handled a bit differently, considering the NO DROP nature of the items. Handling of this type of loot should be determined beforehand by the represented members of the classes. Looting rules should be crystal clear before the guild begins the raid. Any items that cannot be used by primaries will then go to alternate characters.

Guests invited on raids by the Bunnies of Chaos will be given the same consideration as any "primary" member of the guild. Those that join a raid "just because [they] are there" or ask to join are considered in the same category as "alts". This guild is "Bunnies first". Guests on raids need to have their invitation approved by the guild leader, raid leader, and representatives of specific classes at least a day before the scheduled raid. Those invited to unscheduled raids should have their invitation cleared by those attending.

Raid Handling

How do I schedule a raid?
Those who want to have a specific raid must submit the raid date and time to Jihan in email (jihan@raveworld.net), along with any supplimental information in regards to the raid. They must also put up a corresponding post in the Raids board so that those who can help can express their interest in doing so. Those names will be listed on the page for the associated raid. Please submit emails with the name of the player, mob, and date/time to me in the subject with the body of the email including the following information:
    Player Name:
    Time/Date of Raid:
    Goal:
    # of Bunnies Needed:
    Zone:
    Additional Information/Maps:

Epic Quest Raids:
Epic Quest raids should be scheduled directly through Jihan, just as any other submitted raid. It's very important that we space out raids and she is the primary keeper of the raid calendar at this time. No more than 2 raids will be scheduled weekly (I don't want to swamp everyone with constant raiding), and under some circumstances (multi-day raids), we can stretch it to 3. It is highly encouraged that if you would like a raid in a zone that has many epic quest mobs in it that you schedule with the associated classes so we can "kill two birds with one stone".

Random Epic Quest Items:
"Randomly dropped" epic quest items (Corrupted Emerald off Innoruuk, Blood of Cazic Thule, gems off VS, etc.), without question, will go to guild members who are furthest along in the quest itself. If there is more than one Bunny who are at the same point, that quest item shall be lottoed or its placement will be decided by those members. Any sort of disputes over epic quest items shall be decided by officers present.
