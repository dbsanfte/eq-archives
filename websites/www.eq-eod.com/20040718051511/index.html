Introduction to NSAi

As a leading IT service management consultancy focused on infrastructure engineering, enterprise management and security for Global 1000 clients, Network System Architects, Inc. (NSAi) has the right combination of people, process, and technology to ensure the success of your most critical IT initiatives.

NSAi provides a full range of certified skills to direct and deliver a successful Best of Breed solution including strategic evaluations, network and system design and implementations, and network performance upgrades. We have developed a comprehensive, proven methodology based on past engagements with Global 1000 clients in a wide range of industries. NSAi consultants average more than a decade of experience with the relevant technologies.

NSAi is a highly focused consulting and systems integration services company with a diverse customer base of Global 1000-class organizations. Our world-class Enterprise Management and Security practices build upon our strong network and system expertise to enable our clients to develop and maintain effective and efficient IT service infrastructures. Our contribution to our clients' business value include strategic staffing, project based engagements and complete outsourcing services.

NSAi has a heritage rich in experience and talent. Our pragmatic approach to partnering with - and becoming an extension of - our clients' internal teams has earned us a reputation of unparalleled dedication. Our innovative and creative work environment has attracted top talent from many disciplines. Our team includes technology consultants and architects, project managers, e-business consultants, application developers and systems integrators.

We work closely with the leading technology vendors including Hewlett Packard, Sun Microsystems, IBM, Cisco, Foundry Networks, Veritas, and Agilent. We have the focus, the experience, the team, and the partnerships to ensure the success of your next IT infrastructure project.

Additional information regarding NSAi is available in the NSAi Capabilities Briefing and the NSAi Overview Brochure
