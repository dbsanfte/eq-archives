 !  Who we are...

Bound by loyalty and honor to our brothers and sisters, we strive to explore and conquer the lands of Norrath for the betterment of our kinds.

Begun in the dawning of the lands of Norrath, the Edge of Darkness sprung from a small band of friends tied together by years of comaraderie and kinship. Their love for adventure quickly set them forth on a path to explore the very corners of the world, to gain as much knowledge and wisdom in that world as their lifetimes would allow. Quick to accept others into their fold whom had similar interests and the lust for enlightenment, the guild has grown to it's present size, large enough to overcome most any obstacle that presents itself, however, small enough that the feeling of family and kinship is not lost.

The Edge of Darkness hosts a wealth of races and classes in it's members. We would be considered a very small, tight-knit guild, who enjoy the social aspects of adventure as much as the power it provides. Membership is not closed, however recruits are required to have an existing member to vouch for them. This is most often done by grouping with an EoD guildmember. See the public Recruitment section of our forums.

The Edge of Darkness is a proud member and supporter of the Guild Coalition Project. More information on the GCP can be found on the GCP forums link above.
