 !  Noteworthy Feats...

Archived below is the noteworthy history of the guildmates of the Edge of Darkness. Many of their honorable accomplishments could not have been possible without the efforts and dedication of their friends and brethren in the alliance of guilds.

Bringing down the evil namesake of their server, Terris Thule.

Purging the waters of the scourge of Untel'Dak in the Plane of Nightmares.

Sending the Terror Matriarch to her grave in the Plane of Nightmares.

Surpassing the trials in the Plane of Justice.

Murdering the contemptuous Spiritseeker Nadox in Dulak.

Crushing the mighty Derakor the Vindactor in Kael Drakkel.

Besting the seldom seen Lodizal Turtle in Iceclad Ocean.

Ending the reign of the elusive Phinigel Autropos in Kedge Keep.

Slaying the foul Magi P'tasa in the Plane of Hate.

Conquering the evil Lord Nagafen in Solusek.

Felling the mighty dragon, Lady Vox in Permafrost..

 

And the adventures continue....
