Frequently-Asked Questions:
             
Q. Where the hell's your mission statement? Your fancy "charter"?

A. We're just not sophisticated enough to have a charter in a spiffy font detailing our to-the-death commitment to valor, honor, truth, justice, and the American way. But we are a bunch of generous, fun-loving gamers with wry (and sometimes utterly goofy) senses of humor, who play for good times, adventure, and making friends. We never forget that there are human beings behind all these avatars, and we really try not to take it too seriously.
             
Q. I'd never join a club that'd have me as a member, but... how do I get in?

A. Becoming a recruit The Far Eastwood requires that you be level 46 or above, unless you are the PERSONAL (and that means in real life!) friend of a current member who will sponsor you. If you meet these criteria, please post a message on our Message Board forum, The Good, The Bad, and the Ugly and introduce yourself. Say hi to us in-game or ask to join us in a group or on a raid (our raid intentions are posted in High Planes Drifter. If interest is mutual, you will be contacted by a member of the recruitment committee for an interview. At the end of a successful interview, you will be offered a temporary tag to join us as a recruit. At the end of 30 days, a review will be made by the membership, and if no objections are raised, you will be invited to join our family as a full member!
             
Q. I hate rules. Do you have any rules?

A. We do actually have some rules, but you'll find them pretty common-sensical, and not involving bizarre hazing rituals or filling out any forms in triplicate. They are:
  • Act like a civilized being. Don't harrass, belittle, kill-steal, ninja loot, or otherwise engage in unbecoming behavior while you wear the E tag.
  • No whining. Other characters in the guild, despite your possible belief to the contrary, are actually there to play the game too, not drop their group in Sebilis or jump out of the Plane of Sky to res your level 9 twink. You will find the Es an amazingly generous group of people, but constant badgering others to camp things for you, do your quests, port you to and fro, res you, etc. and laying on a guilt trip when your guildmates are unable to assist, is grounds for dismissal. Don't believe us? We can refer you to a few people we kicked out for just this very behavior.
  • The minimum requirement for membership is that your main be level 45 or higher, and we do not accept alts unless your main is in the guild. But once your main is in the guild, then we will accept all of your alts in perpetuity, regardless of their level. We all love playing different classes, and some of the odder folks among us have up to eight different characters. Come and experiment!
  • Members are required to check in to the Message Board regularly to stay informed of guild news and events. Our guild is growing, and it's very inconvenient to have to explain things multiple times in /guildsay and /tells. Be proactive!
  • Find out that this guild is not for you? These things of course happen. However, we ask that in the unlikely event you might ever want to de-guild, that you speak to an officer prior to quitting as a courtesy. Not doing so makes you ineligible to rejoin at a later date, and also makes us cranky.
             
Q. What about friends? Do you have any friends?

A. We are a CoV guild, which means we suck up like mad to the Velious dragons (and Coldain dorfs), and enjoy beating the bejesus out of the giants.

As far as non-NPC friends, we currently are not formally allied with any other guilds, but enjoy friendships and activities with our old guildmates, the wonderful folks of Vallis Scortor. The Far Eastwood has also been been a pivotal player in some exciting inter-guild movements, such as the now-famous AoE Superfriends, a cadre of players who enjoy the fast-paced lunacy of area affect fighting, who meet on a regular weekly basis.
