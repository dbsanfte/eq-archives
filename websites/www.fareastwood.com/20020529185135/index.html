In the Beginning...

|e|
 Once upon a time in the wild, wild, west, there was a bunch of little boys (and one little girl) who played Quake II. A lot. One might say to excess. You might remember some of us; we were the folks with the |e| tag. Yes Clan Eastwood ruled the OGL ladder, well okay, competed admirably on the OGL ladder, and had the cool server that all the other little boys and girls wanted to come play on (Feel Lucky? Q2CTF), and fragged with a fervor not seen since Dirty Harry.

Other Dramatis Personae from the Incestuous Quakeverse:
The Association
If you don't remember Clan Eastwood, you might remember the Carats, that deadly and ubiquitous non-clan clan with the ^ tag in front of their names. Clan Eastwood ultimately borrowed three from these ranks, but membership in The Association is for life; sorta like getting a tattoo. Or going to hell. Thus our aptly-named Q2 servers, Inferno, and later, Purgatory (both now Q3 and RTCW servers; visit http://www.carat.org for more info).
Clan Indra
Some folks you surely never heard of is Clan Indra, which, while sporting a professionally-designed Web site, collapsed quickly into oblivion when our most dedicated (or at least most obsessed) player defected to The Association. However, the ISP (Indra's Net) for which it is named later became the home of Inferno, and also plays host to this very Web site you are viewing right now.

So What The Hell Happened?
History may never know who or what was ultimately responsible: The lame security inherent to Q2 that made every 12-year-old kid without an ego worthy of his own bot detector? The fact that Q3 flat-out sucked? The cosmic convergence of trolls in Hawaii? Some blame the little girl as a jezebel temptress, having bought EverQuest at the urging of some of her Association cohorts, then proceeding to persuade everyone she knew that they simply had to have it.
But one thing is certain, in the course of these events, the Quakers (no, not that kind of Quaker, you boob) were brought far -- far from their original course. Thus was hatched The Far Eastwood on Cazic-Thule in early 1999. But we've come even further today: we've joined so many new friends along the way who can snipe "MAKE MY DAY!" at a mob with the best of them. And two Es even liked playing together so much that they ran out and got married.

When Cazic-Thule split in 2001, most of us moved to our new home on Ayonae Ro (aka Annoying Ro). In the fall of 2001, we merged with that noble guild of sluts, Vallis Scortor (Quake reference: founded by the Q2CTF clan QSA!), for whom we have to thank for many great times and the slaying of many vile mobs. But, missing that cozy family feeling, we unmerged in April of 2002 to re-form The Far Eastwood, and here we are!

That is our story, and every bit of it is true. Except the part about the trolls.

Even in times of mirth, The Far Eastwood remembers and honors those who lost their lives and their loved ones on September 11, 2001. Please continue to support The American Red Cross or your favorite disaster relief organization.
