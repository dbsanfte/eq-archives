Azzarack Island Cleared 2-26-2000 11:00 PM MST
Today members of Immortal, and friends completely cleared the Azerack Island in the Plane of Sky. To the best of our knowledge, this is the first time anyone has done this on Xegony. A total of 28 people participated in the event. We killed all of the Azeracks, as well as the Protector of Sky. It was done without so much as a single mass-death. All told, it was phenomenally successful. Everyone there did a fantastic job. As I write this, we're gathering people together again, and moving over to the Temple Isle, and getting ready to continue the fight.

Congrats to Mcfinn who won a necklace. I think the stats were something like +15 AC +5 str +5 dex. I could be slightly off on that though.

Izzin also obtained the last piece to his Sphynx Hair Cord quest, and will be completing that shortly. Congrats to him as well.

To quell any silly rumors before they start, this was entirely 100% legit. We ported over to Azeracks, and beat the snot out of them. There was no LD cheating to get to the third isle, or any of that. We flat out *KILLED* the Azerack Isle.

The raid was led by Adon, who did a wonderful job of organizing, and making things go well.
