We in Glory of Quellious want to make ourselves, and the force of light, more powerful. In order to do this, we must do all that we can to fight, train, and assist for and with eachother every day. The battle can only be won with a constant blade at the throat of evil. We also take pride in assisting others we come across within the continents of Norrath, as long as they have the same ideals as us, and are fighting against chaos. In the guild, we will assist fellow members get items suitable for their levels, complete quests, and replace lost items (failed corpse retreivals). To be invited within the fold, one must have known a GoQ member for at least 10 levels, or have hunted with a member for at least two hours. That member sponsors you, and recommends you to be invited within the guild. If you accept the invitation, you become a "junior member". After time and deliberation you will either become a "regular member" or be asked to resign/booted from the guild. Noone remains a "junior member" for ever. You can avoid being booted from the guild through frequent cooperation, help (physical or verbal), a positive attitude towards your guildmembers, and by being a good representative of the Glory of Quellious wherever you go.
