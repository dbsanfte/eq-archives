The Glory of Quellious has a mission: to spread light throughout Norrath through the destruction of evil. To acheive this goal, not only must we slay the fiercest of beasts, but we must assist and heal those we come across that have the same desire to eradicate chaos from this world! We unite as family, and though our races may vary across the spectrum of noble creatures, we are all bound by blood. The stronger we become, the stronger becomes our will! Hail to be Glory of Quellious!
