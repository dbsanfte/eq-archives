Join Primal

Thanks for your interest in Primal Brood!

Read stuff you should know about us and if you're still interested fill out the application form and hit send. Your application will be sent to Foofee and the Officers for review and posting in our members section. Barring any objections you'll be invited for a one month trial period during which the guild is getting to know you better.

You will be expected to seek out and meet the members of Primal Brood and group with them for us to get to know you after you submit your application. After members have grouped with you enough to feel they know you they will vote. Once the majority of officers vote in your favor you will get a guild invite. If you receive one objection from a member during application review or during your trial period, depending on the severity of the objection, we may choose to discuss it with you or deny you full membership and remove the initiate status.

We do not consider "Guild Shoppers" as being serious in their application to join PB. So if you've posted on many boards don't bother.

Read the PB loot rules.

Our loot rules may not suit you and it would be a waste of both your time and ours applying then finding out you disagree.

Your primary character must be level 54 (there are few exceptions).
If you have a family member or real life friend in PB we will consider lower level entry.
You must have a Cobalt Scar tooth and a Sebelis Key (we encourage having your HS key as well but not required).

The following are some reasons for denied membership:

Using the name “Primal Brood” to intimidate others.
Ninjalooting, Killstealing & Training.
Sharing our strategies and tactics with people outside guild.
Sharing things said in /gu with people outside guild.
Constant negativity (especially concerning loot).
Defaming the Primal Brood name with rude and insulting conduct to others, or by badmouthing Primal Brood.
Only participating in raids that can further your character(s).
Selling items on ebay.
