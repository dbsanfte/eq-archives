Primal Broods Loot Rules

Primal Brood uses Seniority Loot. We've been raiding for awhile now and happily alot of the front of the list has their items. Seniority is defined by time in guild and contribution. Both are determined by the GM and Officers based on raid attendance and proactive interest in guild. These rules apply to planar armor and weapons as well as God and Dragon loot.

When we are grouping in small groups for xp or pleasure we follow a need before greed guideline.
