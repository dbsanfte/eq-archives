Primal's Charter

Primal Brood is an Everquest Test Server guild for serious high level players interested in exploring and adventuring in some of the most dangerous places in Norrath. Members of Primal Brood endevour to play skillfully, and work together as a team to accomplish progressively greater challenges. Every member of The Brood is important, respected, and appreciated. To join The Brood, we recommend grouping with our members to get a feel for how we play. If you feel that you wish to join us, let a guild member know of your interest.

Several things to know about Primal Brood:

- We are a guild for players interested in exploring and appreciating all that Norrath has to offer with a focus on zones deemed for very high level organized players.

- We seek to play together regularly.

- All members have a "level focus", and are actively seeking to reach level 60.

- We play skillfully, and look for others that do the same.

- We have a low overhead, politics reduction, guild management system which we ask all members respect.

- Every members opinion is important and should be treated with respect.

- Members of the Primal Brood wish to play the game with friends without being involved in the politics of a guild. As such, we seek "like minded" players that agree with our playing style, and share our vision.

- Members of Primal Brood agree to put "need before greed" in respect to loot distribution.

- Members of Primal Brood endevour to be honorable, kind, and respected by the Test Server community.

- Members of Primal Brood are close friends and protect, defend and care for other members as such.

Membership Requirements
-----------------------
Minimum level requirement: 54*

* exceptions are occasionally made for people over level 50 with a commitment to this playing style.

Simple rules for Smart people
-----------------------------

We expect every member of Primal Brood to be respectful, polite, and empathetic to other players on the Test Server. Our members are mature, responsible, and caring and it is expected that they will act that way. Any inexplicable reports of bad behavior (i.e. disrespect, rudeness, disregard for other players safety) will result in warnings and possible expulsion from the guild.

We have a simple system for the creation of Rules and Guidelines that is designed to prevent arguing or disruption of game play. All Rules and Guidelines are clearly posted and are expected to be upheld. The guild leader is entrusted to protect the interests and vision of the guild, and has final say on any proprosed rules or guildlines which would threaten to damage the vision.
