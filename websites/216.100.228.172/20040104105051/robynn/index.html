How to use the Configurator
Stats and Modifiers
Enter values that you feel reflect how important a stat or modifier is. Defaults have been provided. These will be multiplied by the stat or modifier value on the item and totalled. (e.g. If you score strength at 3, and an item has 5 strength, the total strength score for the item is 15.)

Effects
Enter values that you feel reflect how important an effect is. Leaving the score blank is an effective score of 0. These values will be added to the stat and modifier total.

Filters
You can narrow your item listing by choosing a combination of filters. You can change the range of scores displayed or narrow to a specific slot, race, or religion. Choosing the Instruments slot on the Bard config will show you all items with an instrument modifier. Choosing the Primary or Secondary slot will include weapons unless you further filter them out on the Sources list. Checking a checkbox will include that set of items in the list; unchecking the box will exclude those items.

When you have finished entering your values, click the Calculate button to see your scored list.
