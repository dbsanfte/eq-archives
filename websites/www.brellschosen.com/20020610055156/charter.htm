Since time immemorial have both dark and light existed. In the Nameless One all things exist. In Norrath, all things exist, but in a constant state of flux. From the Age of Elders to present runs a common thread of conflict. Good and evil, light and dark, peace and war, love and hate - these diametrically opposed ideas are what drive the nature of the ever evolving world around us as we strive to find some sort of balance. In struggling towards this realization we have but one true weapon: knowledge.

Brell's Chosen hearken back to a time before history was written - a time when the balance was maintained. To this end we have banded together beings of all race, religion and creed in pursuit of the utopia we believe possible. Though we do not agree on all the aspects of our lives we have established a harmony between ourselves to better understand the path that fate has placed us on.

Guild Rules

All members are expected to conduct themselves in a manner which exemplifies the rules of fair play. Harassment and disrespect of others, be them guildmates or outsiders, will not be tolerated. Any violations will be dealt with quickly and decisively.

Loot Rules - Raid specific

-Gems and cash items go to guild coiffures for compensation (port stones, peridots)

-CAWU use on all items with one exception for epic pieces (i.e. Guild objectives will be met first, for example if we head to Fear with intent to kill the Shissar for the Enchanter epic piece for one person, then our directive will be stated in the raid sign-up rules and so forth.)  

-Epic pieces, unless specifically stated beforehand, are rolled on with a two to one advantage in favor of the class that can use it for epic.  (Example: the White scale that Lady Vox drops can be used for the Bard epic and also for a caster quest cloak.)
