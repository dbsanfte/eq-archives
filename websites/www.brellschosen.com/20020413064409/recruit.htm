So.... you want to become a member of Brell's Chosen?

Please read and follow the steps below.  Brell's Chosen is always looking for new members for our family, so come on in, take a look around.  

STEP 1: Go to the message board (http://pub66.ezboard.com/bbrellschosen) and introduce yourself to the guild. Please include the following items:

  • Name

  • Class

  • Level

  • Race

  • Any Info you think is relevant

Keep in mind that though we aren't labeled as a ROLE-PLAYING guild, our members are extremely fond of roleplaying. If you wish to write a story about your new recruit, please do. It is highly encouraged. If you don't have a lot of access to the internet and would prefer to do this in game (in EQ) then feel free to contact one of our officers.

STEP 2: Once you have completed Step 1, it is encouraged that you group with members of the guild and get to know some of those people. Many of us have alts of various levels so this shouldn't pose a problem. We think this will work the best simply because we want to get to know our recruits a little before accepting them. Also, if you are interested in us as a guild you may group with our members and find that it's not quite what you are looking for.

STEP 3: Members of the guild who have grouped with you will then post in the members only area of the guild and relay their decision(s). If things look positive, the new recruit will be allowed in on a probation basis. If things look disagreeable, the recruit will be informed of this and will be given the opportunity to make another impression.

STEP 4: Induction into the guild. New recruits will be on probation for 2 weeks time. After the 2 weeks they will be come full-fledged members.

F.A.Q.

Q. What kind of guild is this?

A. We are a family-oriented guild first and foremost. Our goal with Brell's Chosen is to offer a place for anyone--of any race or religion. We hope to be able to help our members with items and quests and information as best we can and work together when we can. This does not mean that we are a guild set up to hand out a bunch of "Phat lewt". Those seeking to join the guild solely to gain in possessions will need to look elsewhere. However, we do encourage helping one another obtain sought-after items by working together on projects.

Q. Will your guild be a "Raid Guild"?

A. Eventually, yes. We hope to gain enough members and levels in order to partake of Brell's raiding system. Brell is one of the few servers in EQ where reservations are honored. Take a look at Brellserilis.net's raid message board area: http://pub13.ezboard.com/bbrellserilis 

Not only do we  someday wish to participate in some of the bigger raids (Fear, Hate, Vox, Naggy) we also want to be active in smaller raids such as: City of Mist, Mistmoore, Najena, Runnyeye and so forth. Until we get bigger in number, however, we will be limited to our raid capacity. This shouldn't prevent anyone from participating in other guild raids. There are a plethora of guilds who host raids all the time.

Q. What level do I have to be to get into the guild?

A. At this time we have no minimum level requirement.  We feel that everyone should have the opportunity to join a guild and become part of the Brell Community.
