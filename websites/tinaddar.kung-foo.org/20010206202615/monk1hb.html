This chart uses (seemingly) more accurate equations I found at The Ranger's Glade.

It incorporates strength (assumed to be 130), max weapon skill for each level, max double attack skill for each level, and max dual wield skill in off-hand for each level.

It also assumes you land approximately 60% of your swings. I'm told accuracy is closer to 55%, but it doesn't make much difference. This chart is for comparisons between weapons. Secondary use is to give a rough idea of how much damage you're doing per second.

Please email any errors or additions to me at omnibus@kung-foo.org.
