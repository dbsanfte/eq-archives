INTRODUCTION:
Our primary goal is to have FUN in the World of Norrath. In order to promote this goal, it is desired to organize and maintain a support structure for the members of the guild. This support may take many forms such as accompanying other members into the darkest depths of Norrath, assisting in the recovery of worldly remains, or in the pursuit of the finest equipment obtainable. This is a team effort! When the whole team pulls together, there's nothing we can't do.

CODE OF CONDUCT:
Guild members are expected to conduct themselves in an upright manner at all times, whether their business is within the guild, with other players, or with the Guides/Game Masters of E'ci. The guild does not tolerate kill stealing, swindling, false witness, nor any other dishonorable behaviour.

Guild members are strongly encouraged to avoid players who have earned unsavory reputations, as such association may ultimately reflect unfavorably upon the guild.

Likewise, any accusation of misconduct formally made against a guild member will be taken seriously, and investigated thoroughly.

The reputation of the guild is based upon the actions of its members. Have fun, play well, and the guild, the game and the Community of Norrath will be better for it.

MEMBERSHIP REQUIREMENTS:
To become a member of the Core Alliance, the prospect must:

  1. Be recommended to the membership by a member in good standing.
  2. Not be known to be of dishonorable character.
  3. Read and agree to the Charter of Core Alliance
New members are accepted under a probationary status. The period of probation will last two months. Upon the close of the period of probation, there being no objection from the membership, the new member will become a full member in good standing.

MEMBERSHIP RESPONSIBILITIES and EXPECTATIONS:
Members should register on the message board, and are encouraged to read the message board regularly. We encourage this in order to ensure that you are aware of what's going on in your guild. The message board is our primary means of communication outside the game. It also happens to be full of good information that can make your time in Norrath more enjoyable.

Members will provide assistance when called upon by the GM. And yes, this may at times require that you drop everything. These occasions are rare, but what goes around, comes around. Someday, someone will drop everything to come help you. It is expected that you should make any reasonable effort to help a guild mate in need at any time.

Members are encouraged, but not required, to:

  1. Assist your fellow guild mates. Well OK, you're EXPECTED to do this! It is, after all, the basic reason for having a guild in the first place.
  2. Attend Guild functions whenever possible. This serves to bind us into a coherent body of friends who know and trust each other.
  3. Recruit quality members. It's always easier to succeed together than to succeed alone. If you meet someone whom you believe to be 'Core' material, invite him or her to join us. The more the merrier!
  4. Make yourself available to mentor new members. The process of introducing new members to the joys of our guild is important to the continued growth and health of Core.
  5. Serve on any special committee, as requested by the GM.
  6. Assist your fellow travellers in Norrath. Build our good name in the world! Courtesy and helpfulness are contagious.
  7. Practice trade skills. It's always nice to be able to buy trade goods from guild mates at reduced prices, rather than the going rate. However, when buying trade goods from a guild mate, be sure to toss in a little extra. Remember, the Trade Masters in your guild spent a LOT of time and money to achieve that status.
  8. Share your wisdom and experience with other members. Knowledge is life, and life is MUCH more fun than the alternative.

MEMBERSHIP TERMINATIONS:
Any accusation of misconduct on the part of a guild member will be investigated, and any member found responsible for inappropriate behavior will be reprimanded and made to make amends as applicable.

Any member who shows a consistent pattern of misconduct may be subject to discipline, as determined by the GM based upon his/her Good Judgment, up to, but not including, termination of membership.

At any time, at the GM's discretion, the CAL may be convened to discuss ongoing investigations, advise on possible disciplinary actions, or determine any member's continued status as a member of Core Alliance.

Membership in the guild may be terminated by a simple majority vote of the CAL.

Probationary membership may be terminated by the GM at any time, and for any reason, based on his/her Good Judgment.

GOVERNMENT:
The Government of Core Alliance shall consist of a Guild Master, elected by the membership, and as many officers as are deemed necessary to carry out the business of the guild.

OFFICES:

  • Guild Master (GM):
    The GM will serve for a period of one year as defined by the Election Process, and will have the following powers/duties:
    • Appoint officers as needed.
    • Appoint an Assistant Guild Master (AGM)
    • Assign duties to the officers and members of the guild as needed to ensure the smooth running of the guild.
    • Direct the overall course of the guild toward the common goal of increasing our ability to have fun in the world of Norrath.
    • Appoint the Election Committee from the membership.
    • Hold power of Veto over proposed changes to the Charter of the Core Alliance.
    • Serve as point of contact for negotiations with other guilds, except as outlined in the responsibilities of the Events Officer (EO).
    • Provide names and dates of appointment of officers to the Web Administrative Officer (WAO).
    • Call special meetings of the CAL, or entire membership, as needed.
    • Can create at any time, at his/her discretion, or at the request of at least three members in good standing, an independent board to review any decision made by the CAL and/or the GM.
    • Is responsible for disciplinary action, up to but not including termination, for members in good standing. For members in their probationary period the GM is responsible for disciplinary action up to and including termination.
  • OFFICERS:
    Officers will be appointed by the GM. Officer postions will include, but are not limited to:
    • Assistant Guild Master (AGM)
    • Website Administrative Officer (WAO)
    • Admissions Officer (AO)
    • Mentor Officer (MO)
    • At least two Events Officers (EO)
    • Any other positions as deemed necessary by the GM. These will be referred to as Officers at Large, or such title as the GM deems appropriate.

REQUIREMENTS FOR HOLDING OFFICE IN CORE ALLIANCE:
To hold office in the Core Alliance you must:

  • Be a volunteer. You will be held to a higher standard than the general membership. You may be asked to give up some of your game time for the betterment of the guild.
  • Be a member of the Core Alliance for at least three months.
  • Not be an officer in any other guilds.
Appointments must be submitted by the GM to the CAL. The CAL does not vote on the appointment, but will use this opportunity to voice their individual opinions on the matter. The GM has the final decision. Appointments, with the exception of the WAO, will be for a period of nine months and may be renewed by the GM upon expiration of the current term.

An officer in Core Alliance is expected to:

  • Coordinate rescue efforts for difficult corpse recoveries.
  • Guild members' secondary characters as is convenient.
  • Guild new members as requested by the AO.
  • Perform duties as assigned by the GM.
  • Act as a conduit between the membership and the GM.
  • Act as an arbitrator between members during disputes.
  • Remain courteous to all members at all times.
  • Investigate accusations of misconduct thoroughly and efficiently.
  • Publicly support the decisions of the GM and the CAL (even if you personally disagree)
  • Serve as a member of the CAL.
      ASSISTANT GUILD MASTER
      The AGM is appointed by the GM as his/her right hand person. The AGM:
      • Holds all the same powers as the GM (with the exception of in-game commands that only the GM has) during any period in which the GM is unavailable.
      • Serves as a member of the CAL.
      WEBSITE ADMINISTRATIVE OFFICER
      The WAO is responsible for:
      • Upkeep of the Core Alliance website and all associated message boards.
      • Providing up to date membership information to the Election Committee.
      • Appointing an assistant if necessary.
      • Creating the members only message boards as needed for elections.
      • Creating special voting windows and/or message boards as requested by the GM or CAL.
      • Serving as a member of the CAL.
      The WAO is a special position. The nature of website maintenance is such that it is better to keep the same WAO for as long as possible. Continuous change in this position can be disastrous to the website, which is our primary means of providing information to the guild. Therefore the WAO may continue in office, at the discretion of the GM, for so long as they are willing to fulfill the office.

      ADMISSIONS OFFICER
      The AO is responsible for:

      • Guilding new members, or delegating the authority to do so to another officer when appropriate.
      • Present new members to the MO.
      • Keep track of new members' probationary status, including starting and ending dates of said probation.
      • In conjunction with the new members' mentor, provide the final recommendation of whether a new member will become a member in good standing at the end of his/her probationary period.
      • Inform the WAO of new members admitted, for the purpose of keeping the membership list up to date.
      • Keep an accurate membership list as a backup to the WAO membership list.
      • Serve as a member of the CAL.
      MENTOR OFFICER
      The MO:
      • Maintains a ready pool of volunteers to serve as mentors.
      • Assigns mentors from the pool of volunteers to new members. Assignments as a mentor should as closely match playing times and personalities as is reasonably achievable, and should not be the same person who sponsored the new member to the guild.
      • Resolves conflicts between new members and their mentors.
      • Monitors the status of new members to ensure their smooth integration into Core Alliance.
      • Serves as a member of the CAL.
      EVENTS OFFICER
      The events officers are responsible for:
      • Coordinating events that involve the entire guild.
      • Coordinating between Core Alliance and other guilds for multi-guild events.
      • Coordinating guild meetings once per quarter, or as called by the GM.
      • Serving as a member of the CAL.
      COUNCIL AT LARGE
      The CAL consists of all officers of the Core Alliance, excluding the GM, and serves as a system of checks and balances between the GM and the membership. The CAL is responsible for:
      • Maintaining the smooth functioning of the Guild through the discharge of the Officers' individual duties and through the conduct of the group of Officers as a whole.
      • Advising the GM on disciplinary action for members who do not adhere to the Code of Conduct of Core Alliance.
      • Debating the merits of proposed changes to the Charter of Core Alliance, and presenting a formalized proposal to the GM.
      The GM may participate in any discussions within the CAL, but normally holds no vote. In the event of a tie, the GM will vote to break the tie.

      INDEPENDENT REVIEW BOARD:
      The Independent Review Board will be convened by the GM, at his/her discretion, or at the request of at least three members in good standing, to review any decision made by the CAL and/or GM. The size of the board will be whatever the GM considers reasonable, but shall be no smaller than three persons, and no larger than seven persons, chosen from the regular membership. No Officer may serve on the Independent Review Board.

      The Board has the power to halt implementation of the specific decision for which they were convened pending further investigation and review. The GM may participate in any discussion held by the Board, but has no vote.

      The Board may challenge the decision on a simple majority vote. Implementation of the challenged decision is halted for a period lasting no longer than one month. A challenged decision will be brought to the regular membership for open debate. The regular membership will then be polled, and a decision will be made based on this poll. The Board will select one of its members to moderate this debate. It is the responsibility of the Board to ensure that the debate proceeds smoothly and concludes in a timely fashion. If no decision is made within one month, then the original decision shall be implemented.

      The Board may overturn the decision on a unanimous vote. Implementation of an overturned decision is immediately halted. Additionally, the subject will be considered closed for debate for a period of one month.

      ELECTION PROCESS:
      Each year, an election will be held for the GM position. Beginning on the first of April of each year, the floor will be opened for nominations to the position of GM. The period for nominations will last three weeks, ending on the 21st day of April. At this time, the nominations thread will be locked down by the WAO. All nominations will be submitted to the members only message board that will be created for this purpose by the WAO. After the close of nominations, the candidates will have one week to campaign.

      Voting begins on the first of May and closes one week later at midnight the 7th of May (East Coast time U.S.). Votes will be submitted by one of the two election processes as defined below. The GM will select the election process to be used.

      PROCESS A
      A secure voting booth will be established on the members only message board previously created by the WAO for nominations. Voting may begin as soon as the voting booth is made available, but will end at midnight on the 7th of May (East Coast time U.S.), and the winner will be announced immediately thereafter. (The nature of this voting process is such that all can see the progress at any time, so no delay is needed to establish who the victor may be.)

      PROCESS B
      An Election Committee (EC) will be appointed by the GM. The number of the EC will be equal to the number of candidates plus one (i.e. If there are three candidates, the EC will number four members). Any member in good standing may serve on the EC. Each member of the EC must place a valid e-mail address on the members only message board. Voting will occur as follows: Each member will send a single e-mail addressed to all members of the EC. The e-mail will contain the member's Primary Character name, the names of all secondaries in Core Alliance, and the member's voting preference. The EC will match names against a membership list provided to them by the WAO. Any vote that is not addressed to ALL members of the EC will not be counted. Any vote received from a non-member will not be counted. Multiple votes from a single member will not be counted.

      Voting will commence on the first of May and will end at midnight on the 7th of May (East Coast time U.S.). At this time, the EC will independently tally the votes and each send a separate e-mail of the results to the WAO who will ensure that the tallies are in agreement. The WAO will then announce the victor on the members only message board.

      EMERGENCY TRANSFER OF GM STATUS:
      Being as we all know that Real Life frequently changes our gaming experience, the GM must have an avenue available to him/her to rapidly turn over the office to a new GM. This Emergency Clause is provided so that in the rare event that a GM must resign immediately, he/she will be able to turn over the position as rapidly as possible to a suitable successor.

      Normally, the outgoing GM will transfer GM status to the AGM. If this is unmanageable due to circumstances, the GM may appoint any available officer as a temporary successor, and transfer GM status immediately. It is that officer's responsibility to transfer GM status to the AGM as quickly as possible. The new GM will then be charged with starting the election process on the first of the month immediately following his/her appointment.

      Any election called for by the Emergency Clause will be carried out in accordance with the Election Process as defined in the Charter of Core Alliance. The only difference will be the month in which the various steps occur. Any GM elected by this process will serve out the remainder of the original GM's term, and then start the Election Process on the 1st of April as called for in the Charter of Core Alliance.

      In the event that the new GM is appointed less than four months from the end of the original term, no election is necessary. The appointed GM (formerly the AGM) will serve out the remainder of the original term, and then commence the Election Process as defined by the Charter of Core Alliance. Any GM elected by this clause less than three months before the date set by the charter for commencement of the next election will instead hold the GM position until the next following election date set forth in the Charter of Core Alliance, in effect skipping the election process for one year.

      CHANGES TO THE CHARTER OF CORE ALLIANCE:
      As the size of the guild changes, so do its needs. In the light of this Truth, the Charter of Core Alliance must be a flexible tool to ensure that our needs will continue to be met. Changes to the Charter of Core Alliance may be accomplished in the following manner:

      Ideas for changes to the Charter of Core Alliance may come from any member of the guild. The member will post his/her proposal on the Members Only Board, allowing for comment from the regular membership. If any Officer (including the GM) likes the proposal, he/she can sponsor it by posting it onto the Officers' Board, where its merits will be debated. Based on this debate, the CAL may modify the proposal as needed and present a finalized version to the GM. The GM can either approve or disapprove the recommendation, or return it to the general membership for further discussion, at which point the process will repeat.

      Any Officer who wishes to submit an idea must follow the same process of first putting the proposal up on the General Board for review and discussion. In addition, the Officer making the proposal cannot subsequently sponsor the proposal before the CAL, but must wait for sponsorship from another Officer.

      If the idea passes the CAL and the GM, the WAO will then modify the Charter of Core Alliance to include the new proposal
