Links!

There are a lot of excellent EverQuest resources out there, quite a few of them very public. So instead of reinventing the wheel, we've just included a couple of our favorites.

  • EQ Traders - THE definitive resource for EverQuest tradeskills. If it's not listed somewhere on this website, it's not in the game.
  • EQ Atlas - One of the better resources for maps of Norrath
  • Norrath's Studio - A website for the artistically inclined to both display their work, and view the work of other artists that play EQ.
  • E'Ci boards - The IGN EQVault boards for E'Ci
  • E'ci Photos - As the title suggests, RL photos of other E'Ci players.
