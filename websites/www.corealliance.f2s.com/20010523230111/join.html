Join Core Alliance

Core Alliance is a small, friendly guild that thinks of its members as family. We do not expect our members to pay tithes in time or money, only ask that members treat each other with respect. We are not loot oriented, and we are not uber raiders.

As such, we realize that Core is not for everyone, and everyone is not for Core. When any member is approached about possible guiding the prospect should be directed to the website and instucted to do the following:

  • A. Read the charter to make sure the Core Alliance is what is best for them.
  • B. Post in the "I want to join the Core" message board and be sure to provide us with character name, level, class, current location and usual play time.
  • C. They should be made aware in the original contact they should check back to the message board in 3 days for possible groupings with core membership over the next 2 weeks and that a determination of guilding will be made at the end of that 2 week period.
  • D. They should also be instructed that they should as much as possible to introduce themselves to membership as much as possible. They should also remain in contact with the message board and feel free to post as often as they wish.

    The Admissions Officer(s) will post on the member board as soon as possible about the prospective member and that groupings need to be arranged with characters of the prosective's level. The members should then post of their availability to group with the prosective in the prospective's original post.

    Members should post their experience with the prospective on the member board after their groupings with them.

    At the end of the 2 week period if the prospective is unable to acquire a sponsor it should be left to officer vote based upon reports of groupings and any personal experience with the prospective.

    Upon vote of approval, the prospect's name will be added to the Approved list for guilding by any officer available. A time and place should be posted on the prospective's original post by the Admissions Officer(s) for guilding.
