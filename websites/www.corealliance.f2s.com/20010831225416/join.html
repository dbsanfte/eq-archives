Join Core Alliance

Core Alliance is a small, friendly guild that thinks of its members as family. We do not expect our members to pay tithes in time or money, only ask that members treat each other with respect. We are not loot oriented, and we are not uber raiders.

As such, we realize that Core is not for everyone, and everyone is not for Core. When any member is approached about possible guilding the prospect should be directed to the website and instucted to do the following:

  • A. Read the General Boards to get a feel for what we're about. This isn't just about us liking you; we want to make sure you'll find Core a good fit for your own designs.
  • B. Post in the "I wanna join up!" message board and be sure to provide us with character name, level, class, current location and usual play time.
  • C. Please be sure to follow up that original post by checking back on the message board in a couple of days for an appointment with a Core Officer.
  • D. We encourage prospects to introduce themselves to members as much as possible, by /who all Core while in game and freely posting on the general boards offline. We want to get to know you!

    Each Officer in Core has the power to guild new prospects, so make an appointment with any of them.
