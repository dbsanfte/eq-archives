INTRODUCTION

Heartwood and Core Alliance are two faces of the same guild. Core Alliance is the junior guild, and is continually trained by the officers and members of Heartwood to become the most effective players and teammates that they can be. Members of Core Alliance are admitted to Heartwood in accordance with the rules listed later on in this Charter.

Our primary goal is to have FUN in the World of Norrath. In order to promote this goal, it is desired to organize and maintain a support structure for the members of the guild. This support may take many forms such as accompanying other members into the darkest depths of Norrath, assisting in the recovery of worldly remains, or in the pursuit of the finest equipment obtainable.

This is a team effort! When the whole team pulls together, there's nothing we can't do.

CODE OF CONDUCT

Guild members are expected to conduct themselves in an upright manner at all times, whether their business is within the guild, with other players, or with the Guides/Game Masters of E'ci. The guilds do not tolerate kill stealing, swindling, false witness, or any other dishonorable behaviour.

Guild members are strongly encouraged to avoid players who have earned unsavory reputations, as such association may ultimately reflect unfavorably upon the guilds.

Likewise, any accusation of misconduct formally made against a guild member will be taken seriously, and investigated thoroughly.

The reputation of the guilds is based upon the actions of its members. Have fun, play well, and the guilds, the game and the Community of Norrath will be better for it.

MEMBERSHIP REQUIREMENTS
To become a member of Core Alliance, the prospect must:
A) Be recommended to the membership by a member in good standing.
B) Not be known to be of dishonorable character.
C) Be adventured with by at least one officer, who may then at his/her discretion guild the prospect at any time. New members are encouraged to read the Combined Charter of Core Alliance and Heartwood at their earliest opprtunity. The officer admitting the new member to the guild is expected to tell him/her of our website and message boards.
To become a member of Heartwood, the prospect must:
A) Be recommended to the membership by a member in good standing.
B) Not be known to be of dishonorable character.
C) Read and agree to the Charter of Core Alliance and Heartwood
D) Be level 40 or higher
Members of Core Alliance will automatically be given full membership status in Heartwood upon reaching level 40, barring any objections from the officers. Members of Core Alliance graduating to Heartwood are exempt from the period of probation required of new members.

New members are accepted under a probationary status. The period of probation will last two months. Probationary members are entitled to all the rights and privileges of full membership, and are encouraged to enthusiastically participate in all guild activities, but will be excluded from voting on guild issues. Upon the close of the period of probation, there being no objection from the membership, the new member will become a full member in good standing.
MEMBERSHIP RESPONSIBILITIES and EXPECTATIONS
Members should register on the message board, and are encouraged to read the message boards regularly. We encourage this in order to ensure that you are aware of what's going on in your guild. The message board is our primary means of communication outside the game. It also happens to be full of good information that can make your time in Norrath more enjoyable.

Members will provide assistance when called upon by the GL. And yes, this may at times require that you drop everything. These occasions are rare, but what goes around, comes around. Someday, someone will drop everything to come help you. It is expected that you should make any reasonable effort to help a guild mate in need at any time.

Members are encouraged, but not required, to:
A) Assist your fellow guild mates. Well OK, you're EXPECTED to do this! It is, after all, the basic reason for having a guild in the first place.
B) Attend Guild functions whenever possible. This serves to bind us into a coherent body of friends who know and trust each other.
C) Recruit quality members. It's always easier to succeed together than to succeed alone. If you meet someone whom you believe to be guild material, invite him or her to join us. The more the merrier!
D) Make yourself available to mentor new members. The process of introducing new members to the joys of our guild is important to the continued growth and health of the guild.
E) Serve on any special committee, as requested by the GL.
F) Assist your fellow travellers in Norrath. Build our good name in the world! Courtesy and helpfulness are contagious.
G) Practice trade skills. It's always nice to be able to buy trade goods from guild mates at reduced prices, rather than the going rate. However, when buying trade goods from a guild mate, be sure to toss in a little extra. Remember, the Trade Masters in your guild spent a LOT of time and money to achieve that status.
H) Share your wisdom and experience with other members. Knowledge is life, and life is MUCH more fun than the alternative.
G) In addition, members of Heartwood are required to keep a secondary character in Core Alliance, and to play that character on a regular basis as well.


MEMBERSHIP TERMINATIONS

Any accusation of misconduct on the part of a guild member will be investigated, and any member found responsible for inappropriate behavior will be reprimanded and made to make amends as applicable.

Any member of Heartwood who shows a consistent pattern of misconduct may be subject to discipline, as determined by the GL based upon his/her Good Judgment, up to, but not including, termination of membership.

Membership in Heartwood may be terminated by a simple majority vote of the CAL..

Membership in Core Alliance may be terminated by the GL at any time, based on his/her good judgement. The GL should (but is not required to) post on the officer's board for input first, if possible, before terminating the membership of any member of Core Alliance.

At any time, at the GL's discretion, the CAL may be convened to discuss ongoing investigations, advise on possible disciplinary actions, or determine any member's continued status as a member of Core Alliance/Heartwood.

Probationary members may be removed from either guild by the GL at any time, and for any reason, based on his/her Good Judgment.

GOVERNMENT

The Government of Heartwood shall consist of a GL, elected by the membership as prescribed by the Election Process, and as many officers as are deemed necessary to carry out the business of the guild.

The Government of Core Alliance shall consist of the GL of Heartwood, and as many officers as are deemed necessary to carry out the business of the guild. It's important to note here that the goverments of the two guilds, Heartwood and Core Alliance, are completely different. While Heartwood is run by democratic process, Core Alliance is not. The GL has total control of Core Alliance and may act as he or she sees fit at any time to ensure that the guild continues to run smoothly.

REQUIREMENTS FOR HOLDING OFFICE IN CORE ALLIANCE/HEARTWOOD
To hold office in Core Alliance/Heartwood you must:
A) Be a volunteer. You will be held to a higher standard than the general membership. You may be asked to give up some of your game time for the betterment of the guild.
B) Be a member of the guild for at least three months, unless you are coming directly from Core Alliance as an officer.
C) Not be an officer in any guilds other than Core Alliance/Heartwood.
Appointments must be submitted by the GL to the CAL. The CAL does not vote on the appointment, but will use this opportunity to voice their individual opinions on the matter. The GL has the final decision. Appointments, with the exception of the WAO, will be for a period of nine months and may be renewed by the GL upon expiration of the current term.

An officer in Core Alliance/Heartwood is expected to:
A) Coordinate rescue efforts for difficult corpse recoveries.
B) Guild members' secondary characters as is convenient.
C) Guild new members as requested by the AO.
D) Perform duties as assigned by the GL.
E) Act as a conduit between the membership and the GL.
F) Act as an arbitrator between members during disputes.
G) Remain courteous to all members at all times.
H) Investigate accusations of misconduct thoroughly and efficiently.
I) Publicly support the decisions of the GL and the CAL (even if you personally disagree)
J) Serve as a member of the CAL.
OFFICES
GUILD LEADER (GL)
The GL will serve for a period of one year as defined by the Election Process, and will have the following powers/duties:
A) Appoint officers as needed in either guild.
B) Appoint an Assistant Guild Leader (AGL)
C) Assign duties to the officers and members of the guild as needed to ensure the smooth running of the guild.
D) Direct the overall course of Core Alliance and Heartwood toward the common goal of increasing our ability to have fun in the world of Norrath.
E) Appoint the Election Committee from the membership.
F) Hold power of Veto over proposed changes to the Charter.
G) Serve as point of contact for negotiations with other guilds, except as outlined in the responsibilities of the Events Officer (EO).
H) Provide names and dates of appointment of officers to the Web Administrative Officer (WAO).
I) Call special meetings of the CAL, or entire membership, as needed.
J) Can create at any time, at his/her discretion, or at the request of at least three members in good standing, an independent board to review any decision made by the CAL and/or the GL.
K) Is responsible for disciplinary action, up to but not including termination, for members in good standing in Heartwood. For members in their probationary period OR any member of Core Alliance the GL is responsible for disciplinary action up to and including termination.
The GL of Heartwood will also serve as the GL of Core Alliance, with all the powers of the office as outlined in the Combined Charter of Heartwood and Core Alliance, those powers being conferred onto one of his/her secondaries in Core Alliance. Within Core Alliance, the GL has total control over the functioning of the guild in all ways.

Officers will be appointed by the GL. Officer positions will include, but are not limited to:
A) Assistant Guild Leader (AGL)
B) Website Administrative Officer (WAO)
C) Admissions Officer (AO)
D) At least two Events Officers (EO)
E) Any other positions as deemed necessary by the GL. These will be referred to as Officers at Large, or
such title as the GL deems appropriate.
These offices may be appointed in either guild, or both, as deemed appropriate by the GL.
ASSISTANT GUILD LEADER
The AGL is appointed by the GL as his/her right hand person. The AGL:
A) Holds all the same powers as the GL (with the exception of in-game commands that only the GL has) during any period in which the GL is unavailable.
B) Serves as a member of the CAL.
WEBSITE ADMINISTRATIVE OFFICER
The WAO is responsible for:
A) Upkeep of the Core Alliance/Heartwood websites and all associated message boards.
B) Providing up to date membership information to the Election Committee.
C) Appointing an assistant if necessary.
D) Creating the members only message boards as needed for elections.
E) Creating special voting windows and/or message boards as requested by the GL or CAL.
F) Serving as a member of the CAL.
The WAO is a special position. The nature of website maintenance is such that it is better to keep the same WAO for as long as possible. Continuous change in this position can be disastrous to the website, which is our primary means of providing information to the guild. Therefore the WAO may continue in office, at the discretion of the GL, for so long as they are willing to fulfill the office.
ADMISSIONS OFFICER
The AO is responsible for:
A) Guilding new members, or delegating the authority to do so to another officer when appropriate.
B) Keep track of new members' probationary status, including starting and ending dates of said probation.
C) In conjunction with the new member's mentor (if one is assigned), provide the final recommendation of whether a new member will become a member in good standing at the end of his/her probationary period.
D) Inform the WAO of new members admitted, for the purpose of keeping the membership list up to date.
E) Keep an accurate membership list as a backup to the WAO membership list.
F) Serve as a member of the CAL.
EVENTS OFFICER
The events officers are responsible for:
A) Coordinating events that involve the entire guild.
B) Acting as a liaison between Core Alliance/Heartwood and other guilds for multi-guild events.
C) Scheduling guild meetings once per quarter, or as called by the GL.
D) Serving as a member of the CAL.
COUNCIL AT LARGE
The CAL consists of all officers of the Core Alliance and Heartwood, excluding the GL, and provides a system of checks and balances against the GL in the governance of Heartwood. The CAL serves as an advisory body to the GL in the governance of Core Alliance. The CAL is responsible for:
A) Maintaining the smooth functioning of the guilds through the discharge of the Officers' individual duties and through the conduct of the group of Officers as a whole.
B) Advising the GL on disciplinary action for members who do not adhere to the Code of Conduct of Core Alliance/Heartwood.
C) Debating the merits of proposed changes to the Charter of Core Alliance/Heartwood, and presenting a formalized proposal to the GL.
The GL may participate in any discussions within the CAL, but normally holds no vote. In the event of a tie, the GL will vote to break the tie.

INDEPENDENT REVIEW BOARD

The Independent Review Board will be convened by the GL, at his/her discretion, or at the request of at least three members in good standing, to review any decision made by the CAL and/or GL. The size of the board will be whatever the GL considers reasonable, but shall be no smaller than three persons, and no larger than seven persons, chosen from the regular membership. No Officer may serve on the Independent Review Board.

The Board has the power to halt implementation of the specific decision for which they were convened pending further investigation and review. The GL may participate in any discussion held by the Board, but has no vote.

The Board may challenge the decision on a simple majority vote. Implementation of the challenged decision is halted for a period lasting no longer than one month. A challenged decision will be brought to the regular membership for open debate. The regular membership will then be polled, and a decision will be made based on this poll. The Board will select one of its members to moderate this debate. It is the responsibility of the Board to ensure that the debate proceeds smoothly and concludes in a timely fashion. If no decision is made within one month, then the original decision shall be implemented.

The Board may overturn the decision on a unanimous vote. Implementation of an overturned decision is immediately halted. Additionally, the subject will be considered closed for debate for a period of two months.

ELECTION PROCESS

Each year, an election will be held for the GL position. Beginning on the first of April of each year, the floor will be opened for nominations to the position of GL. The period for nominations will last three weeks, ending on the 21st day of April. At this time, the nominations thread will be locked down by the WAO. All nominations will be submitted to the members only message board that will be created for this purpose by the WAO. After the close of nominations, the candidates will have one week to campaign.

Voting begins on the first of May and closes one week later at midnight the 7th of May (East Coast time U.S.). Votes will be submitted by one of the two election processes as defined below. The GL will select the election process to be used.

Only members of Heartwood, and the appointed officers of Core Alliance are eligible to vote in the annual election.

PROCESS A
A secure voting booth will be established on the members only message board previously created by the WAO for nominations. Voting may begin as soon as the voting booth is made available, but will end at midnight on the 7th of May (East Coast time U.S.), and the winner will be announced immediately thereafter. (The nature of this voting process is such that all can see the progress at any time, so no delay is needed to establish who the victor may be.)
PROCESS B
An Election Committee (EC) will be appointed by the GL. The number of the EC will be equal to the number of candidates plus one (i.e. If there are three candidates, the EC will number four members). Any member in good standing may serve on the EC. Each member of the EC must place a valid e-mail address on the members only message board.

Voting will occur as follows: Each member will send a single e-mail addressed to all members of the EC. The e-mail will contain the member's Primary Character name, the names of all secondaries in Core Alliance, and the member's voting preference. The EC will match names against a membership list provided to them by the WAO. Any vote that is not addressed to ALL members of the EC will not be counted. Any vote received from a non-member will not be counted. Multiple votes from a single member will not be counted. Voting will commence on the first of May and will end at midnight on the 7th of May (East Coast time U.S.). At this time, the EC will independently tally the votes and each send a separate e-mail of the results to the WAO who will ensure that the tallies are in agreement. The WAO will then announce the victor on the members only message board.

EMERGENCY TRANSFER OF GL STATUS

Being as we all know that Real Life frequently changes our gaming experience, the GL must have an avenue available to him/her to rapidly turn over the office to a new GL. This Emergency Clause is provided so that in the rare event that a GL must resign immediately, he/she will be able to turn over the position as rapidly as possible to a suitable successor.

Normally, the outgoing GL will transfer GL status to the AGL. If this is unmanageable due to circumstances, the GL may appoint any available officer as a temporary successor, and transfer GL status immediately. It is that officer's responsibility to transfer GL status to the AGL as quickly as possible. The new GL will then be charged with starting the election process on the first of the month immediately following his/her appointment.

Any election called for by the Emergency Clause will be carried out in accordance with the Election Process. The only difference will be the month in which the various steps occur. Any GL elected by this process will serve out the remainder of the original GL's term, and then start the Election Process on the 1st of April as called for in the Charter of Core Alliance/Heartwood.

In the event that the new GL is appointed less than four months from the end of the original term, no election is necessary. The appointed GL (formerly the AGL) will serve out the remainder of the original term, and then commence the Election Process as defined by the Charter. Any GL elected by this clause less than three months before the date set by the charter for commencement of the next election will instead hold the GL position until the next following election date set forth in the Charter, in effect skipping the election process for one year.

CHANGES TO THE COMBINED CHARTER OF CORE ALLIANCE AND HEARTWOOD

As the size of the guild changes, so do its needs. In the light of this Truth, the Combined Charter of Core Alliance and Heartwood must be a flexible tool to ensure that our needs will continue to be met. Changes to the Combined Charter of Core Alliance and Heartwood may be accomplished in the following manner:
Ideas for changes to the Charter may come from any member of either guild. The member will post his/her proposal on the Members Only Board, allowing for comment from the regular membership. If any Officer (including the GL) likes the proposal, he/she can sponsor it by posting it onto the Officers' Board, where its merits will be debated. Based on this debate, the CAL may modify the proposal as needed and present a finalized version to the GL. The GL can either approve or disapprove the recommendation, or return it to the general membership for further discussion, at which point the process will repeat.
Any Officer who wishes to submit an idea must follow the same process of first putting the proposal up on the Members Board for review and discussion. In addition, the Officer making the proposal cannot subsequently sponsor the proposal before the CAL, but must wait for sponsorship from another Officer.

If the idea passes the CAL and the GL, the WAO will then modify the Charter of Core Alliance/Heartwood to include the new proposal
