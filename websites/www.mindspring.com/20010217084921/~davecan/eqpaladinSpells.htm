Paladin Spells

SPELL LEVEL SKILL MANA DECRIPTION

 

Courage 9 Abjuration 12 Courage the first in a line of buffing spells for the cleric class. This line of spells adds both hit points and armor class and can be cast on yourself and on others.
Cure Poison 9 Alteration 20 This spell will counteract poisons.
Flash Of Light 9 Divination 12 This spell will temporarily blind your opponent.
Minor Healing 9 Alteration 10 Minor healing is the first in the line of healing spells.
Sense Magic 9 Divination 12 This spell will give you information on magic items.
Spook The Dead 9 Alteration 10 This is an undead fear spell. When can upon the undead it will cause them to turn and run away. This can be a useful spell when used in conjunction with lull as you can lull one of the undead to prevent him from attacking you and spook the other one away from the area and get a chance to fight it one on one.
Yaulp 9 Abjuration 5 This spell endows the caster a brief surge of strength which not only adds to the casters strength but also to his armor class. The duration is very short but the spell also takes very little mana.
Cure Disease 15 Alteration 20 This spell can be used to cure disease.
Hammer of Wrath 15 Conjuration ? This spell summons a dmg 6 delay 32 magical warhammer that can only be used by the player casting the spell and will disappear when you log out or die.
Holy Armor 15 Abjuration 20 Holy armor is the first in the line of armor spells. This spell adds additional armor class to a player.
Light Healing 15 Alteration 25 Light healing is the second in the line of healing spells.
Lull 15 Alteration 25 This is the first in the line of spells which are aimed to calm your foe. This spell can be used to lower your opponents rage and(hopefully) prevent him from attacking you.
Sense The Dead 15 Divination ? This spell will point you in the direction of the nearest undead monster.
Ward Undead 15 Evocation 30 Ward undead is the first in the line of direct damage undead ward spells. These spells only affect undead. Ward spells are important because at the level which you attain these spells they are the most efficient direct damage spells in the game at this level.
Center 22 Abjuration 40 This is an upgrade to the courage spell. It will not stack with courage but can be stacked with the holy armor and the symbol of transal line of spells. It grants +ac and +hp.
Endure Poison 22 Abjuration 20 This spell will increase your resistance to poison as well as curing it.
Halo of Light 22 Conjuration 40 Creates a light source.
Invigor 22 Alteration 20 Invigor will restore a players stamina.
Invisibility To Undead 22 Divination 40 This spell renders you invisible to undead mobs.
Reckless Strength 22 Alteration 30 Reckless strength gives a burst of strength which slowly dies out.
Root 22 Alteration 30 This spell will hold your opponent in one place. It serves two different purposes. The first is to keep a monster from running so your party can finish it off. The second is that it prevents the monster from getting near you, this is very important if you plan on soloing at higher levels.
Expulse Undead 30 Evocation 60 Expulse undead is an upgrade to ward undead. It has a bit better damage to mana ratio and also does more damage
Hammer of Striking 30 Conjuration 100 ?
Healing 30 Alteration 60 Healing is the third in the line of heal spells. It heals around 80 points of damage and is more mana efficient than it's predecessors.
Soothe 30 Alteration 30 Soothe is an upgrade to lull. It will reduce a monsters aggro range and perhaps prevent it from attacking you.
Spirit Armor 30 Abjuration 75 Spirit armor is an upgrade to holy armor. This spell gives an armor class bonus.
Stun 30 Evocation 35 ?
Symbol Of Transal 30 Abjuration 55 Symbol of transal is the third line of buffing spells that clerics and paladins get. It requires a component and grants hit points only.
Cancel Magic 39 Abjuration 30 Cancels Magic on others, or yourself.
Counteract Poison 39 Alteration 50 Cures Poison.
Daring 39 Abjuration 60 Daring is an upgrade to the courage->center line of spells. It grants armor class and hit points.
Endure Disease 39 Abjuration 20 Adds to your Disease stats.
Greater Healing 39 Alteration 150 The largest heal Paladins get.
Symbol of Ryltan 39 Abjuration 111 ?
Yaulp 2 39 Abjuration 15 Adds to strength and armor class.
Bravery 49 Abjuration 70 Adds to your AC and HP.
Calm 49 Alteration 50 ?
Dismiss Undead 49 Evocation 90 ?
Guard 49 Abjuration 80 ?
Holy Might 49 Evocation 60 ?
Revive 49 Alteration 300 Bring a player back to life.
Symbol of Pinzarn 49 Abjuration 200 ?
