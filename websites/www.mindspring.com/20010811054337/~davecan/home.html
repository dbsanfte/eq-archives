Welcome!

We are The Companions, an online guild of EverQuest on the Veeshan server. We are a gathering of people who want to adventure, explore, and socialize together in Norrath, the online world of EverQuest.   Our group is good in nature and we strive to uphold honor and justice in all we do.  

Our Goals:

     1) Explore as much of Norrath as we can, aiding others when we can.
     2) Discover the complex history of Norrath by completing as many quests as possible.
     3) Socialize with guild members and non-guild members alike and have lots of FUN.

We value friendship and loyalty.  While we won't start fights with other groups, neither will we avoid them or surrender meekly.  We are and look for dedicated role-players to join our ranks.  If you see a member of The Companions in Norrath, stop and talk a while.  If luck be with us, maybe our paths will cross and run together for a while!

Our Charter:

1. The Companions are a force of good. We adhere to the following principles: Respect, Honor, Support, Truth, Justice, Fair Play, Fellowship, and Generosity.

2. Uphold the principles of the guild in all your dealings.

3. Attacking others is looked upon as an abomination in the land. Killing or attacking others will not be tolerated by members of this guild.

4. Supporting or allying with PKer's will not be tolerated and is cause for immediate dismissal.

5. Involvement in other's disputes other than to try to peacefully mediate is to be adamantly avoided and can be cause for dismissal.

6. Petty wars will not be tolerated. Only the High Council will decide to involve the guild in any dispute or war. A vote among the guild may be taken if the High Council deem it necessary.

7. Duels should only be conducted for appropriate reasons in a manner consistant with good role-playing.

8. Conduct yourself in a mature manner, rude and antisocial behavior does not befit a member of The Companions.

9. Only the High Lord or High Council may dismiss members.

10. All decisions for the guild shall be reached by the High Lord and the Councilors, other members shall be involved as deemed necessary by the High Council.

11. The guild is your family. Always remember that and strive to make it so. Make every attempt to make this land a better experience for your family and fellow traveler's.

12. Always help a brother or sister, defend those weaker, and offer a helping hand to new travelers. Put helping others before your own gain whenever possible. Support guild members in times of need and always give them the benefit of the doubt.

13. Strive to be the best you can be, but be happy no matter what level that takes you to. Enjoy the experience of this land.

Amendments:

1. Guild Dues are 25 plat. a week per member. (not per char.)

2. To leave the guild you should speak with Tosari or a High Council member. Failure to do so would require a vote of  The High Council for readmission  to the guild.

3. Minimum level to join The Companions is 35.
