Flame Lick 1 Evocation 10 This spell is used to lower an opponents armor class, making him more susceptible.
Lull Animal 1 Alteration 10 This spell will can cause animals that are normally aggressive to not attack if successful.
Minor Healing 1 Alteration 10 Minor healing is the first in the line of healing spells.
Panic Animal 1 Alteration 10 This spell will cause an animal to run away in a panicked state. Beware that after this spell wears off(approximately 30 seconds) the animal will return.
Sense Animal 1 Divination 5 This spell will point you in the direction of the nearest animal.
Skin Like Wood 1 Abjuration 10 This is the first in the line of druid buffing spells. It adds to both the armor class and hit points of a player.
Snare 1 Alteration 15 Slows the creature down, when low on hits it will stop attacking.
Burst of Fire 5 Evocation 15 Burst of fire is a direct damage spell. Basically it is an upgrade to Burst Of Flame.
Camouflage 5 Divination 15 This is an outdoor only invisibility spell. You must be grouped to cast on others.
Cure Disease 5 Alteration 20 This spell can be used to cure disease.
Cure Poison 5 Alteration 20 This spell will counteract poisons.
Gate 5 Alteration 70 The gate spell will teleport you home to your bind point.
Grasping Roots 5 Alteration 35 Grasping Roots is an upgrade to snare. This spell actually roots the monster in place(instead of slowing him down) and also does a small amount of damage for the duration.
Harmony 5 Abjuration 25 It is like a better Lull spell.
Invoke Lightning 5 Evocation 32 Invoke lightning is an outdoor only area effect spell.
Whiling Wind 5 Conjuration 24 Whirling wind is a direct damage spell. While not as mana efficient as Burst of Fire it does more damage(and uses more mana). For that extra bit of mana you gain a short stun effect.
Enduring Breath 9 Alteration 35 .Water breathing spell
Enduring Cold 9 Abjuration 20 This spell will increase your resistance to cold based attacks
Fire Fist 9 Evocation 30 This spell will add damage for your offensive blows.
Ignite 9 Evocation 30 Ignite is a fire based direct damage spell.
Invisibility vs Animals 9 Divination 30 This spell will render you invisible to animals. It is not very useful as you will usually prefer to use camo or superior camo instead
Light Healing 9 Alteration 25 Light healing is the second in the line of healing spells.
Shield of Thistles 9 Abjuration 40 This spell is a damage shield. It does more damage than thistecoat but has a much shorter duration.
Star Shine 9 Conjuration 50 Starshine is an outdoor light spell.
Strength of Earth 9 Alteration 40 This spell will add to a players strength ability score.
Thistle Coat 9 Abjuration 25 This spell is a minor damage shield which also adds armor class.
Treeform 9 Alteration 40 Treeform will allow you to take the shape of a tree. It is really a rather useless spell.
Befriend Animal 14 Alteration 70 Befriend animals is a charming spell which will work on animals only.
Bind Affinity 14 Alteration 100 This spell is used to change a players bind point. This is the point where a player will return to if they die or gate.
Cascade of Hail 14 Evocation 62 This is an outdoor rain spell.
Expulse Summoned 14 Evocation 60 Expulse summoned is an update to the ward summoned line of spells which does direct damage to elements.
Halo of Light 14 Conjuration 40 This spell summons a magical headband which casts light as a greater lightstone.
Invigor 14 Alteration 20 Invigor will return a players stamina much like a heal spell returns health.
Levitate 14 Alteration 30 This spell will allow a character to levitate. It is useful for travelling over water or lava and in situations where you are afraid you might be knocked off of a ledge.
See Invisible 14 Divination 25 This spell will allow you to see invisible beings.
Sense Animal 14 Divination 5 This spell points you into the direction of the nearest animal.
Skin Like Rock 14 Abjuration 60 Skin like rock is an update to the skin like wood spell. It adds armor class and hit points.
Spirit of Wolf 14 Alteration 40 This is one of the most useful travel spells in the game. It allows you to run at double speed in outdoor zones which is great for escaping attack or for reducing travel time.
Stinging Swarm 14 Conjuration 65 Damage over time spell
Summon Drink 14 Conjuration 10 You  can summon your own drink when not able to buy it or forage it. It will disappear when you leave the game or die.
Summon Food 14 Conjuration 10 You can summon your own food when not able to buy it or forage it. It will disappear when you leave the game or die.
Barbcoat 19 Abjuration 50 This spell is an upgrade to thistlecoat. It adds armor class and a small damage shield.
Calm Animal 19 Alteration 45 Calm animal will calm animals and lower their aggressiveness.
Cancel Magic 19 Abjuration 30 This spell will cancel a magic effect cast on you or one of your party members. It is useful if someone is rooted, charmed or has a nasty DoT spell cast on them.
Careless Lightning 19 Evocation 70 This is an outdoor only offensive spell. It will hit more than one target in a group of creatures.
Dizzying Wind 19 Conjuration 80 This outdoor only spell will do extra damage to flying beasts. It is an upgrade to the whirling wind spell.
Endure Disease 19 Abjuration 20 This spell adds to your resistance to disease.
Endure Poison 19 Abjuration 20 This spell adds to your resistance to poison.
Feral Spirit 19 Alteration 50 Feral spirit is a buffing spell which can be used on your pet(a befriended animal perhaps). It will make them much more formidable.
Healing 19 Alteration 60 Healing is the third in the line of healing spells. It heals roughly 80 points of damage.
Rings Of Butcher, Tox, Karana and Commons 19 Alteration 150 These are the first of your personal travel spells. They will teleport you to one of the above locations
Shield Of Barbs 19 Abjuration 60 Shield of barbs is an upgrade to shield of thistles. It creates a damage shield.
Superior Camouflage 19 Divination 40 This is an invisibility spell which works both indoor and outdoor. You must be grouped to cast on others.
Terrorize Animal 19 Alteration 30 This spell is a fear spell that works only on animals.
Charm Animals 24 Alteration 120 This spell will charm animals and allow you to use them as pets.
Creeping Crud 24 Conjuration 100 This is an upgrade to the stinging swarm spell. It does damage over time.
Dismiss Summoned 24 Evocation 90 Dismiss summoned is an upgrade to the ward->expulse summoned line of spells.
Ensnaring Roots 24 Alteration 60 This spell not only roots your opponent but also does damage while rooted.
Pogonip 24 Evocation 125 This spell is a direct damage offensive spell.
Resist Fire 24 Abjuration 50 Adds to your resistance to fire based attacks
Ring of Feerrott, Lavastorm, Ro, Steamfont 24 Alteration 150 These are the first of your personal travel spells. They will teleport you to one of the above locations
Skin Like Steel 24 Abjuration 100 Skin like steel is an upgrade to the skin like wood->rock line of spells. It adds armor class and hit points.
Spirit Of The Cheetah 24 Alteration 20 This spell allows you to go into super fast sprint mode. It is much like spirit of the wolf only faster and with a shorter duration.
Sun Beam 24 Divination 40 Area affect spell, sends a blinding light over the creatures.
Tremor 24 Evocation 200 Tremor is an area effect spell which has a large area of effect. In exchange for its effect radius it sacrifices mana efficiency
Wolf Form 24 Alteration 60 This spell allows you to assume the form of a wolf.   It raises your attack. You can not have SoW up at the time of casting this spell.
