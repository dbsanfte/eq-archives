Greetings,

First let me say Congrats! to Balboa he made level 60 recently. Balboa has been very helpful to others in the guild, he has helped others also by giving out many spells he has found while hunting, he also gives a lot of ports to others. Thank you Balboa for being a TRUE Companion!

As you may have noticed your name above your head now reads "Companions" instead of "The Companions" we have reformed the guild and left "The" off on purpose. We have had the tag bug so many times we are hoping this will keep it from happening again.

We have downsized our squads, we don't need as many as we thought and so we have combined them. If you do not know what squad you are in please contact one of the Officers as soon as you can.

We will start having regular meetings again very soon. I will post them on the message board and announce them on guild chat. Everyone should make every attempt to attend these meetings, of course real life comes first, but if you are online you should be at the meeting. Everyone's input is welcome and needed concerning your guild.

Please check the new roster I have put up, I am updating it as often as I can. If any corrections need to be made you can post those corrections or email them to me, the email addy is listed on the roster page. As you all know by now I do have a job out of our home now but I will try my best to update every week at least. ;)

As many as you know several of our long time friends have joined forces with others and formed "Pravus Dei" we wish them all the best of luck on their journey's. There may have been some hurt feelings but we still consider them our friends and we consider their  new family friends as well.

Remember also that Ring of Shadows would like to hunt with us and go on raids together, we will probably be planning something soon with them or they with us.
