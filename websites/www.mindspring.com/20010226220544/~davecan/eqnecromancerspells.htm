Necromancer

SPELL LVL SKILL MANA DECRIPTION

 

Cavorting Bones 1 Conjuration 15 This is the first in the line of necromancer pet spells. This spell summons a level 1 decaying skeleton.
Cold Light 1 Conjuration 15 This spell summons a dim light which can be used as a lightsource.
Disease Cloud
 1 Conjuration 10 This spell will disease your opponent and do a small amount of damage initially as well.
Invisibility To Undead 1 Divination 40 This spell renders you invisible to undead mobs.
Lifetap 1 Alteration 9 This is the first in a line of spells which is unique to necromancers. This spell will suck hit points away from your opponent and give them to you.
Locate Corpse 1 Divination 5 This spell will point you in the direction of the nearest corpse.
Minor Shielding 1 Abjuration This is the first in the line of the wizards shielding spells. This spell adds armor class and hit points. It can be cast on yourself only.
Reclaim Energy 1 Conjuration 5 This spell is cast on summoned pets. When cast on a pet this allows you to reclaim the mana you spent on him to a certain extent and "unsummon" him.
Sense The Dead 1 Divination 5 This spell will point you in the direction of the nearest undead monster.
Siphon Strength 1 Alteration 5 This spell will lower your targets strength and add it to yours.
Clinging Darkness 4 Alteration 20 This is a duration of time spell which causes damage over time. It also slows the targets speed down as it is somewhat of a blinding spell.
Endure Cold 4 Abjuration 20 This spell adds to the targets resistance to cold.
Fear 4 Alteration 40 Fear will cause your opponent to turn and run away at full speed. Be warned however that he will return when the spell wears off and he may bring friends.
Gate 4 Alteration The gate spell will teleport you home to your bind point.
Grim Aura 4 Alteration 25 This spell grants the caster a damage shield. Whenever a monster hits you it will inflict some damage upon him.
Leering Corpse 4 Conjuration 40 This spell summons a level 3-5 skeleton.
Lifespike 4 Alteration 18 Lifespike is the upgrade to lifetap. This spell takes transfers some of the hit points of your opponent and adds them to your hit points.
Numb The Dead 4 Abjuration 20 Numb the dead is an undead calming spell. By casting this spell on undead beings you will cause them not to attack players they would normally attack.
Poison Bolt 4 Conjuration 30 This spell will poison your opponent doing damage to him over time.
Sense Magic 4 Divination This spell will give you information on magic items.
Bonewalk 8 Conjuration 80 This spell will summon a level 7-9 skeletal pet that has the ability to Bash, Double Attack, and Parry.
Dark Empathy 8 Alteration 20 This spell will heal your target while damaging yourself.
Dark Pact 8 Alteration 5 Converts Health into Mana
Deadeye 8 Divination 35 Deadeye grants the caster not only infravision but also the ability to see undead beings. This is important for races who do not have the benefit of infravision.
Gather Shadows 8 Divination 35 Gather shadows is an invisibility spell that can only be cast on yourself. This is very important to evil players as it will allow you to travel places you previously were cut off from.
Impart Strength 8 Alteration 15 This spell will give some of your strength to your target.
Lesser Shielding 8 Abjuration This is an upgrade to the minor shielding spell. It adds armor class and hit points.
Mend Bones 8 Alteration 25 This spell allows you to heal your pet.
Shadow Step 8 Alteration 10 This spell is a short mini teleportation spell that will teleport you a short distance away from your current location. It is mainly used as an escape spell.
Vampiric Embrace 8 Alteration 30 This is an more powerful version of the lifetap spell. This spell will drain hit points away from your target and give it to you. This has the benefit of not only damaging your enemy but healing yourself as well.   You must hit the monster to drain.
Ward Undead 8 Evocation 30 This is a direct damage spell for use versus undead beings. It is the first in a line of spells.
Bind Affinity 12 Alteration This spell allows you to change your binding point where you will return if you gate or die.
Convoke Shadow 12 Conjuration 120 This spell will summon a level 11-13 pet.
Endure Disease 12 Abjuration 20 This spell will increase your resistance to disease.
Engulfing Darkness 12 Conjuration 60 This damage over time spell is an upgrade to the clinging darkness spell.
Heat Blood 12 Alteration 72 This is a damage over time spell. The importance of it is that it can be stacked with the clinging->engulfing darkness line of spells which will allow you to do a lot more damage stacking the two of them.
Lifedraw 12 Alteration 63 Lifedraw is an upgrade to the lifetap line of spells which will heal yourself by stealing a monsters hit points.
Sight Graft 12 Divination 10 This somewhat useless spell will allow you to see through your pets eyes.
Spook The Dead 12 Alteration 10 This is an undead fear spell. When can upon the undead it will cause them to turn and run away.
Wave Of Enfeeblement 12 Evocation 40 Wave of Enfeeblement is an area effect weaken spell.
Banshee Aura 16 Abjuration This is an upgrade to the grim aura spell. It allows you to cast a damage shield around yourself which will punish your opponents for making contact with you.
Cancel Magic 16 Abjuration This spell can be used to cancel magical effects that have been cast on you or your party members.
Cure Disease 16 Alteration 20 Cure disease will allow you to cure yourself or another player of a disease.
Feign Death 16 This spell allows you to play dead. If it works the monster will assume you are dead and will leave you alone. You can lay around and regain hit points and then get up and run.
Heart Flutter 16 Alteration 16 This is a triple threat spell. It not only weakens your opponent and lowers their armor class but it also does damage over time.
Hungry Earth 16 This is a root spell which works only on undead.
Infectious Cloud 16 Conjuration 78 This is an area effect disease spell.
Restless Bones 16 Conjuration Restless bones will summon a 13-16 lvl skeletal pet.
Shielding 16 Abjuration This is an upgrade to the minor shielding->lesser shielding line of spells. It adds not only armor class and hit points but magic resistance as well.
Shieldskin 16 Abjuration 41 This spell allows you to absorb a certain amount of damage without being injured.
Spirit Armor 16 Abjuration 75 Spirit armor is a buffing spell which can be cast on yourself and others which will add armor class.
Voice Graft 16 This somewhat useless spell will allow you to speak through your pet. It is great for toying around with(especially when you are invisible) however it does not have much use.
Allure Of Death 20 Alteration 5 This spell transforms the caster into a skeleton. It is a -hp and +mana spell.
Animate Dead 20 Conjuration This spell will summon a level 20ish pet.
Dominate Undead 20 Alteration 100 Dominate undead is a spell that can be used to charm undead monsters and use them as pets.
Expulse Undead 20 Evocation 60 Expulse undead is an upgrade to the ward undead spell. It not only does more damage but is also more mana efficient.
Harm Shield 20 Abjuration Harm shield grants the caster a brief period of invulnerability.
Identify 20 Divination This spell identifies magical items. I really do not see much use for it in this game.
Siphon Life 20 Alteration 20 Siphon life is another upgrade to the lifetap line of spells which drains a monsters hit points and gives them to the caster.
Word Of Shadow 20 Evocation This is an area effect damage spell that centers around the caster.
Breath Of The Dead 24 Abjuration This is a water breathing spell.
Haunting Corpse 24 Conjuration Haunting corpse is the level 24 upgrade to your pet summoning spells.
Intensify Dead 24 Abjuration This spell allows your pet to dish out more damage and it also acts as a regeneration spell on your pet.
Leatherskin 24 Abjuration 24 Leatherskin is an upgrade to the shieldskin spell. It absorbs more damage.
Major Shielding 24 Abjuration This is an upgrade to the minor->lesser->shielding line of spells.
Resist Cold 24 Abjuration 50 This spell will add to your resistance to cold.
Rest The Dead 24 Abjuration 75 I have no information on this spell. Anyone who does please mail me.
Shadow Sight 24 Divination Shadow sight grants ultravision to the caster.
Shock Of Poison 24 Conjuration 100 This spell does a bit of direct damage and also poisons your victim.
Blood Boil 29 Alteration 150 This spell is an upgrade to the heat blood spell. It can be stacked with darkness spells for maximum damage over time.
Dismiss Undead
 29 Evocation 90 This is an upgrade to the ward->expulse undead line of spells. It is a high mana high efficiency spell which is very useful versus undead foes.
Dooming Darkness 29 Conjuration 120 Dooming darkness is an upgrade to the clinging darkness->engulfing darkness line of spells. It does damage over time.
Panic The Dead 29 Alteration 50 Panic The Dead is an upgrade to the spook undead spell. It has a longer duration and will work more on higher level foes.
Renew Bones 29 Abjuration Anyone who has information on this spell please mail me.
Spirit Tap 29 Alteration 144 This is an upgrade to the lifetap line of spells.
Summon Dead 29 Conjuration Summon dead is the level 29 pet summoning spell.
Vampiric Wave 29 Evocation This is an area effect spell similar to vampiric embrace.
Beguile Undead 34 Alteration 170 This is an upgrade to the charm undead spell.
Call Of Bones 34 Alteration Not really sure about this one. Mail me if you have info.
Greater Shielding 34 Abjuration Greater shielding is an upgrade to the lesser->minor->shielding->major shielding line of spells.
Invoke Fear 34 Alteration Invoke fear is an upgrade to the fear spell. It has a longer duration.
Invoke Shadow 34 Conjuration This is the level 34 summoning spell.
Resist Disease 34 Abjuration 50 This is an upgrade to the endure disease spell. It adds more resistance.
Root 34 Alteration Root will prevent an NPC from running.
Steelskin 34 Abjuration 149 Steelskin is an upgrade to the leatherskin spell. It absorbs damage.
Surge Of Enfeeblement 34 Alteration This is an AE weaken spell.
Venom Of The Snake 34 Conjuration 160 This is a poison spell which will do damage over time.
Augment Dead 39 Abjuration 100 Anyone who has information on this spell please mail me.
Counteract Disease 39 Alteration 50 This is an upgrade to the cure disease spell. It is more effective with high level poisons.
Drain Spirit 39 Alteration 189 This is an upgrade to the Spirit Tap spell.
Expel Undead 39 Evocation 130 Expel undead is an upgrade to the ward->expulse->dismiss undead line of spells. It does DD to undead foes.
Malignant Dead 39 Conjuration This is the level 39 summoning spell.
Nullify Magic 39 Abjuration Nullify magic is an upgrade to the cancel magic spell.
Scourge 39 Conjuration 170 This is a disease attack.
Word Of Souls 39 Evocation Word of souls is an area effect spell which centers around the caster.
Arch Shielding 44 Abjuration Arch shielding is the final upgrade to the shielding spells.
Crackling Bones 44 Conjuration Crackling bones is the level 44 pet summoning spell.
Dead Man Walking 44 Divination This spell is an upgrade to the level 20 allure of death spell. It will turn you into skeletal form.
Diamondskin 44 Abjuration 236 This is the final upgrade to the shieldskin->leatherskin line of spells. It absorbs damage that would otherwise hurt the caster.
Banish Undead 49 Evocation 200 This is the last in the line of the ward->expulse->dismiss->expel undead line of spells.
Cajole Undead 49 Alteration 245 Cajole undead is a high level undead charming spell.
Cascading Darkness 49 Conjuration 300 Cascading darkness is an upgrade to the clinging->engulfing darkness line of spells. It does damage over time.
Drain Soul 49 Alteration Drain soul is an upgrade to the lifetap line of spells.
Expel Summoned 49 Evocation Expel summoned will do good damage to summoned beasts such as elements.
Invoke Death 49 Conjuration This is the level 49 summoning spell.
Lifetap AEIV 49 Alteration This is an area effect lifetap spell. Since there are no level 49 necromancers in the game we can only ponder how nice it must be.
