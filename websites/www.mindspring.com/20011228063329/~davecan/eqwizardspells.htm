Evocation

 10 The frost bolt spell is the first in the line of bolt spells. Bolt spells generally do a higher amount of damage than other lines of spells.
Minor Shielding 1 Abjuration 10 This is the first in the line of the wizards shielding spells. This spell adds armor class and hit points. It can be cast on yourself only.
Numbing Cold 1 Evocation 13 This spell is an area effect spell which is based on the caster.
Sense Magic 1 Divination 5 This spell will give you information on magic items.
Shock Of Frost 1 Evocation 8 This is the first in the line of direct damage shock spells. Shock spells generally do not do as much damage as a bolt spell of the same level will do however shock spells are attained frequently and do not need to be aimed.
Sphere Of Light 1 Divination 10 This spell will create a glowing sphere in the casters hand.
Fade 4 Alteration 10 This spell is a great escape spell for a wizard. It causes you to randomly teleport from spot to spot in a close location.
Gate 4 Alteration 70 The gate spell will teleport you home to your bind point.
Glimpse 4 Divination 30 This spell lets you take a brief glimpse at objects far away. Basically it gives you a 15-20 second period where your sight is zoomed in to the distance.
Ice Strike 4 Evocation 30 This is an area effect rain spell. As with all other rain spells these can be effective if you have a large group of monsters as it can damage them all at a single time. Be sure to cast this on the "tank" and not on the monsters.
O'Kiels Radiation 4 Abjuration 15 This allows you to cast a limited fire shield on yourself or another person. For the duration of the spell every time a monster hits the radiating player it will suffer damage. This is important as it is one of the few spells that wizards have that are actually beneficial to others.
Root 4 Alteration 30 This spell will hold your opponent in one place.
See Invisible 4 Divination 25 This spell allows you to see invisible beings.
Shock Of Fire 4 Evocation 15 This is the second in the line of shock spells(all of which do direct damage).
Column Of Frost 8 Evocation 39 This is a cold based area effect spell which centers around your target.
Eye Of Zomm 8 Conjuration 30 This spell creates a moveable eye which you can control and use to scout out dangerous areas.
Fingers Of Fire 8 Evocation 47 Fingers of fire is a fire based area effect spell which centers around the caster.
Firebolt 8 Evocation 40 This spell is the second in the bolt line of spells. It is a high damage spell which is unguided meaning that you can easily miss if you do not line up correctly.
Lesser Shielding 8 Abjuration 25 This is the second in the line of shielding spells. It cannot be stacked with other spells in this line(such as minor shielding)
Shadow Step 8 Alteration 10 Shadow step is an escape spell. This spell will teleport you a short distance away to avoid your attackers.
Shock Of Ice 8 Evocation 30 Shock of ice is an upgrade to the shock of frost and shock of fire spells. This spell is a direct damage spell.
Sense Summoned 8 Divination 5 Casting this spell will point you in the direction of the nearest summoned monster(element).
