August 5,2000

Greetings,

First off I would like to congratulate Tallana, and Sylyna on becoming level 60!! :)  They have worked very hard on this and finally have reached their goal. :)

Welcome to all the new folks that have joined us in the last 4 months. :)

We have had a very busy summer, our guild has been on several Hate runs and we have gotten several pieces of great armor for our companions.

There have been several spells gotten from Kurnark dungeons and handed out. We have some that are in safe keeping for certain class's to be given out when some one in those class's reach the level of the spells.

I would like to thank Shaihulud for allowing us to use his Lake house at Smiths lake in Alabama for our get together. He has a great place on the lake and every one enjoyed the weekend.

We played pool, went boat riding , watched Tosari, Methose, Morijac, Bador, Daelon, Myfisstow  all fly off of the huge innertube, hehe, should have been there just for that!  And of course there were the jet skii's as well. The next get together we have Shaihulud asked that we have folks talk about their class's. We did sit and talk and a lot was learned from the chats on the porch.

Hopefully we will be able to meet again in the Fall when its not so hot, it should be beautiful at the lake with the colors of Fall all around....druid thoughts ;)

The new rosters should be up very soon, Tos has several links to rename and then it should be up. Thank you for being patient with me. ;)

Safe Travels,

Silvarel Mistmoon
