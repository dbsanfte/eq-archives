Don't know how to use this site? Maybe you need some guidance on how and where to register. Or maybe it's just a forum problem that requires an admin's attention. This is the place to find out the questions to the most common requests we get. Please read this section before contacting us about the site. Any further questions please visit the contact section.

I. Forums

  • Question: How do I access the forums section?
    Answer: Click on the menu link that says "forums." A new window should pop-up with our forum community. If you cannot access the pop-up window, your browser is outdated. We use JavaScript technology, go to www.internetexplorer.com or www.netscape.com to download the newest browser avaliable.

  • Question: When do I get the option to post?
    Answer: Once you click on the forum link, you enter our forum community. From there, you have to click on a specific forum link (each made for different discussion). You can then post a message within that forum.

  • Question: It says I need to be "registered" to post. How do I register?
    Answer: You may register here. The registration link is also avaliable on the main forum page.

  • Question: How do I post a reply?
    Answer: Within specific forum messages you have the option to reply. Note: You must also be registered to reply.

II. General

  • Question: How come the Raid Calendar, Register, and Member Listing sections are broken?
    Answer: We simply haven't figured out how to configure the CGI yet. Give us a week and they should be up.

  • Question: Are you guys accepting volunteers?
    Answer: YES! We are currently accepting vollunteers to moderate our forums. All moderators must follow SullonZek.net policy. Please submit an e-mail to webmaster@sullonzek.net with the title [Application: Moderator]. We are currently not accepting applications for other positions.

  • Question: How may I help this site?
    Answer: As it costs money for bandwith and to host the site, you may make a small donation to us. We will then list you as one of our sponsers, and give you a gold icon next to your name to show that you support your community.

  • Question: How come this picture or link is broken?
    Answer: Even though we try our best to have no errors on all our pages, sometimes typos evade our work. Please, warn us of our errors immediately.

III. Guilds

  • Question: How do I get my guild listed?
    Answer: Contact the webmaster at webmaster@sullonzek.net titled [Guild Submission]. We need the full guild name, guild leader / leaders, and the guild website link. Also, we need a little description (max 200 char) about your guild. You will then be e-mailed a confirmation letter once your guild has been posted.

  • Question: Do you host guild websites?
    Answer: We need to reserve our bandwith for the automation that this site requires. Therefore we have stopped hosting websites. A good website host is www.geocities.com and www.homestead.com.

  • Question: How do I advertise my guild and recruit personel?
    Answer: You could post in our guild section on our forums. Just click on "Forum" and then "Guild" and post a message there. An attractive post will bring in more players.

  • Question: How do I add a member to a guild roster.
    Answer: Sorry, this option is currently not avaliable. Our programmer is currently working on the code and it should be working relatively soon.
