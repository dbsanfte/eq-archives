Teakken's Workshop

You enter a chamber filled with all sorts of things... blocks of clay, empty vials, various brightly colored gems, jugs of water, blocks and scraps of metal, bundles of long wooden shafts, stacks of various animal skins, spools of thread laying about, pile upon pile of sketches and sewing patterns to name but a few. The chamber is well lit, with globes emanating a bright light hung from the ceiling. The air is musty, but you notice the floor, what little you can see of it with the small walkways winding between the piles of tradeskill supplies, is swept clean. This room, unlike many of the others in Teakken's den, is lined with wooden floorboards.

"Welcome to my workshop." Teakken greets you, with a broad smile upon his small face. "Sorry for the clutter, but I work on quite a few things, and I find it best to have everything out in the open where I can see it. Please do be careful, it wouldn't do to have you knocking over my supplies."

"So tell me. What are you interested in today?" he asks.
