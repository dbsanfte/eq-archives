About Us
Lor Mani is a guild on the Quellious server in the game of Everquest. We go beyond being a guild... we are family. Our friendships within the guild are lifelong, anyone who is, or has been in Lor Mani, is a Lor Mani for life.

Making the most out of Everquest is our goal. As small as the guild is, we are all bound together as family, sticking together, doing what is right to help each other as well as the rest of Norath.
