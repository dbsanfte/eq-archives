Guild Charter
The members of Lor Mani are not merely members of a guild, but are also a tightly knit band of friends. With the goodness in our hearts, we together have found ways to overcome our differences and the obstacles that stand in our way. We are dedicated to the following Code of Honour:

- We will always offer to help others in need as best we can
- We will give freely of our knowledge of Norrath and its denizens
- We will set an example by our actions and words for the rest of Norrath;
- We will be fair and just to the Citizens of Norrath, regardless of race or class
- We will never engage in behaviour that is abusive, offensive, dishonest or discourteous to any other player. This includes an absolute veto on comments or terms which are intended to denigrate another player on the basis of race, or sexual or religious preference.


Government

Each and every Member of Lor Mani is an integral part of the Guild's success. Member participation in group activities (such as hunts and events) and votes on issues such as policy is critical to the health of the guild and its continued success.

The Guild Leadership will be comprised of the Guild Leader and one Officer for every five (5) members of the Guild. Prior to making policy decisions, the Guild Leader will confer with the Guild Officers and, having reached a consensus, address the issue on the Lor Mani message board for discussion and a vote, if necessary. In any situation where an immediate decision is required, the Guild Leader will confer directly with as many members as possible in the time available.

Issues on which the General Membership will always vote include, but are not limited to, Guild Officer Appointments/Nominations, Alliances with other Guilds and Amendments to the Guild Charter.

New Members

In order to maintain the cooperative spirit of the guild, and ensure that members wearing the Lor Mani title follow the Code of Honour, new members must meet the following prior to being invited into the guild:

- As a general ethic, act with kindness, compassion and fairness to others
- Clearly indicate a priority on working together, playing together and having fun with the other members of the Guild
- Make a genuine effort to meet the other members of the Guild and earn their trust and friendship
- Act at all times within the guidelines of the Code of Honour, regardless of the presence or absence of Guild members


Members are encouraged to invite potential new members and friends to Lor Mani hunts and events; if an event is members-only, this will be clearly stated in the announcement of such event. Potential recruits can be sponsored into the Guild once they have read the Charter and agreed to abide by it as if they were a full member and after they have attended several Guild events. The initial sponsor will introduce the recruit on the Lor Mani message board and they will have full posting privileges. In order to gain acceptance into the guild, a recruit will need one additional sponsor for every five current members in good standing. Once the necessary number of sponsors have posted their support of the recruit, the Guild Leader will post on the members-only voting board and members will have one week (7 days) to raise any concerns or objections.

Membership shall not proceed while there is a concern from a member has not been thoroughly addressed and resolved to the satisfaction of the dissenting member. Once a recruit has been accepted into the Guild, they are a full member in good standing with all rights and privileges and therefore a high percentage of members responding is strongly encouraged for every new member sponsored.

Any discussion of a recruit's acceptance will be held in confidence, and only the final results of the discussion will be posted to the public board.

Honourary Membership

The membership may propose a past or current member to be named as an Honourary Member when they feel that such person remains part of the guild regardless of whether they currently play online games. A unanimous vote is not required, but there must be clear majority support from the current membership.

Inactive Membership

1) A member may go on inactive status at any point if they will be unable to play online for an extended period of time, or have other priorities at any point. A member who chooses to go on inactive status will not be guildremoved, and can re-activate membership at any time.

2) A member may be placed on inactive status if they have not been in contact for a period of 4 consecutive weeks. If possible, members will be emailed prior to being placed on inactive status - if there is no response after 2 weeks, they will be guildremoved 'without prejudice' (meaning that they are not banned from attempting to re-earn membership status).

3) A member may be placed on inactive status if they have not participated in the guild for a period of 6 consecutive weeks. As there can be many reasons for this non-participation, (such as developing a new char, working on trade skills, conflicting play times etc.) this action will not be taken without several attempts to determine some of the reasons for the lack of participation, and under no circumstances will anyone be arbitrarily guildremoved. There should be a clear indication that the member does not wish to participate in guild events, and does not intend to in the near future.

Honourary and Inactive members retain the right to participate in guild policy discussions, and have message board privileges, but will not affect the number of required officers or sponsors, and will have no voting privileges unless granted such for a specific issue.
