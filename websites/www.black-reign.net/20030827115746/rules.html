Black Reign

An Evil EverQuest guild.
 
 
 
 
   

 

Rules

- General Policies -

NOTICE: We are currently using our message board for primary communications outside of the game.

RECRUITING: Candidates "main" guild character must be level 45+ and be evil to be considered for membership. We Would like to have all new members sponsored by at least 1 members, having had some time to group with the individual and seeing how they play and general attitude. Then the sponsors could recommend membership at that time. A probation of 60 days for each new member.

BEHAVIOR: As a general rule: lying to guildmembers, begging for or demanding guild services or items, kill stealing, camp stealing, training other players on purpose, ninja looting, and other generally hated behaviors will not be tolerated, as it is against our basic purpose. Guildmembers are expected to conduct themselves in a manner that promotes the values of the guild charter. Violations should be reported to your local Officer, and remember that the Guild's reputation is also your own. However, you are expected to stand up for your own name and the honor of the guild when faced with such behavior from others, or if you are unable to do so please fell free to call upon your guildmates for assistance.

GUILD SERVICES: Many tradeskill masters and porters make their skills available to guildmembers at cost. They are under NO guild obligation to provide these services, but do so as a benefit to members. You are generally required to provide ingredients to the master for making items, unless other arrangements are made at the masters discretion. Tradeskill Masters and porters are a valuable asset of the Guild, and abuses to these members will not be tolerated.

POWER LEVELING : This guild does not support power leveling or Twinking of any kind.

GUILD CHAT: Guild chat is generally reserved for guild business and/or information that guild members would find useful or entertaining. Individual conversations should be conducted using tells.

RAIDS: Raid leaders have the responsibility of conducting multiple-group raids, setting up the groups, and camping locations. Members wishing to hold a guild raid in a certain zone should contact a raid leader and/or post a request on the message board. Ad-hoc raids, recons, and small party raids may be conducted by any member. Members who wish to participate in a Guild sponsored Raid are expected to arrive at the location prior to the raid start time and to research the zone (eqatlas, etc.) before the Raid to be familiar with the zone. Be on time! Plan ahead to be at the raid formation location at the correct time, the correct time being 15 to 30 minutes before the raid start time. You are late if you are there at the raid start time. All items obtained through a raid will be given out on a Need Before Greed basis determined by the Raid Leader, and/or designated Officer. Raids will be announced on the message board, and by the MOTD prior to the event. Raids are not for exp they are for items that you can not get in a normal group.

RAID Chat Channels : Before the raid begins, the Raid Leader will initiate a private Raid Channel for the raid. All attendies will, upon their arrival, be notified of this channel and will immediately join it. This channel will be used for all general chatter pertaining to the raid such as incoming messages, Leader orders, and so forth. Shout and OOC will be kept clear for emergencies such as TRAIN & ZONE! During larger raids, at the descretion of the Raid Leader, a second raid channel may be opened which everyone will join but will be for the exclusive use of the Raid Leader(raid master)/group leaders.

LOOT RULES: Looting - ONLY Group Leaders will loot, or only the assigned person(s) for loot. Group leaders will then inform the raid officer (RAID MASTER) of any drops that need to be lottoed

There are two main types of loot distribution used in raids: 'Need Before Greed' and 'Greed Rolls'. 'Need Before Greed' will be the standard for all raids unless noted in the initial raid post in the forums by the Raid Coordinator.

Drops: 1. All rolls will be for characters in attendance on the raid. Rolls to "twink" alternate characters not present on the raid are prohibited in all rolls except 'Greed Rolls.' 2. If an item drops for a specific class(es)/race/Diety, it will be lottoed off to the members of that class(es)/race/Diety that require that item or can use that item ('Need Before Greed'). The only exception to this is if one of the people who can use the item opts out of the lotto. Items that are droppable, which no one wants will be held for a 'Greed Roll' at the end of the raid. 3. By "require" we mean people that do not already have this item and will actually equip or use the item, or need the item to fulfill a quest. 4. There is always going to be some debate as to whether all classes that can physically use the item should be allowed to. For example, just because a Tranix Crown drops on a fire giant raid, does that mean everybody should be allowed on the lotto when an enchanter could possibly put it to the best use? To be fair, we are going to say that people who can use an item, as listed by the description on the item itself, can be entered into a lotto if they are actually going to use the item. However, we would like to heavily stress that if you know this is an item that you're not really going to use and that a particular class is very excited about getting in on, please reconsider whether you really want to get in on the lotto or not. If you are a wizard and a sword drops that's great for rangers and druids but happens to be ALL/ALL, do you really want to enter that lotto just because you can? Be considerate of others and use good judgment and some common sense. 5. People seen abusing this rule will not be invited on future raids. The determination of this will be made by individual Raid Leaders in conjunction with Guild Officers. This will include people who are seen auctioning won items from the current raid or from raids in the recent past. 6. If a different Loot Rule is going to be used by the Raid Leader, this fact should be posted when the announcement for the raid is posted in the forums. People are going to expect the 'Need Before Greed' rule so if you're leading a raid and you are not going to use it, make sure you state this clearly beforehand so people can decide whether they want to come on the raid. 7. All cash items (jeweled necklaces, opal bracelets, etc. - basically things that sell for a lot but can't be used for any other purpose such as jewel craft) go to the Raid Leader. The Raid Leaders will see that this is distributed to reagent casters and used to cover any other costs associated with the raid.

'Greed Rolls' will be held at the end of the raid. Those who have yet to win anything are eligible for the rolls or, if everyone has won something, it will be open to all who care to try. You can roll for your own greed. You can roll and hand the item over to the Guild Vault or to a friend. You can roll and then try to turn a profit, or you can roll and give it away to your twink. It is a greed roll, plain and simple.

CORRECTIVE ACTION: After repeated violations/warnings, or if it is determined that a member does not live up to our Charter and ideals, that member may be removed following the removal policy guidelines.

REMOVAL: No member shall be removed from Black Reign without the Guild Leader's and Majority Officer's approval. Under extraordinary circumstances where the Guild Leader and a majority of officers cannot be consulted, two Officers in agreement may remove a member who has violated the guild policies or charter. However that member has the right to appeal to the council. If that member appeals they must do so within 5 days of being removed from the guild.

COUNCIL : The leader of the council will be the guild leader. Sitting on the council will be an even number of officers. This will make the total number an odd number, this will keep a ties from happening during voting. The power of the council is final.

DISPUTES: It is understood that disputes will arise between people. Try to resolve the disagreement so that both parties are satisfied. If you are unsure of what you should do in a particular situation, contact an officer for guidance. 1) If you have a dispute with another member, please try to resolve it in tells. 2) Do not air grievances against another guild member in guild chat, regardless of whether or not that person is online at the time. 3) If you are unable to resolve the dispute, contact a guild officer for help. If a situation arises which involves another guild, contact a guild officer immediately.

Good communication is essential to the process of evolving as a group. This includes the feedback of ALL members - ingame, at meetings, and on the Guild Forum Site - and ALL Guild members are responsible for participating in the decision-making of the guild. We may disagree at times, but we must and will continue to discuss and resolve the issues.

Please note these rules are subject to change. The council can at anytime change the rules without notice.

Thank You

The council
