 My life
So you want to know more about me? Then read on and I will tell you a bit more about my life.
I was born in Greater Feydark and lived in Kelethin throughout my childhood. My parents where simple people who lived in peace with the wood - like most of our kind do.
During my growing up - It became evident that I was very musical and it was quite naturally for my parents to take contact to Sylia, the head of SongWeaver's Guild.
I was accepted as a student and started singing for my life... Literally because Greater Feydark is infested with Orcs Skeletons and other evil beings who only have one goal. To destroy our beautiful forest. Thus every elf with fighting capabilities helped rid the forest of evil. I fought epic battles around Kelethine and even entered the Home of the Crushbone orcs together with an army of elves in an attempt to destroy them completely.
Until now we have failed to completely rid our home of those wicked beings, but atleast we don't have much trouble with keeping them away from our city and forest now. So I left Greater Feydark knowing that our town and forest would be in safe hands with the guards. Today I am wandering around Norrath entertaining and helping people whenever I can. With a great desire to see the world before returning to my hometown.
During my travels across Norrath I ran into people who had the same urge to help others as I do. So I joined them in the search for the one true creator.. Creators Path a guild set to help people all over Norrath.

May the Music guide you.
- Musiker Silverdrum -
