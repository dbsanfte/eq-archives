You stand there on the beach gazing out at the waves. They call to you, they always have. So powerful is the ocean's spell, that you barely notice the almost silent wings above your head. Before you can see the bird, it is gone - replaced by a tall dark woman who stands at your side. She smiles and regards you with wide golden eyes.

"It's beautiful, isn't it?" she asks. You nod and she continues in a voice like the surf itself, "Out there the waves can rock you to sleep or drown you in an instant. And yet, many will brave them for the sake of riches, adventure and freedom that can only be found on the open sea aboard a pirate ship."

Her words ring true, it's what you've looked for, longed for. A ship comes into view, its dark flag snapping in the breeze and she waves to it, a gold ring on her hand glistening in the sun. She drops a seashell onto the sand at your feet.

"If you'd like to join us, send me word with this. I think you'd make a fine sailor." Smiling, she winks at you and in an instant is gone, replaced by a small owl. It flies off toward the ship.

The tide laps at your feet and you can almost hear words within its sound. "Come with us.... Come with us..."
