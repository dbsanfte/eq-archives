This is our Code for conduct in character as Pirates. All members are expected to adhere to that described within or pay the penalty.

We are the Pirates of Neverwinter. We're reavers, pirates, buccaneers, corsairs, thieves, sailors, freebooters, gentleman (and ladies) of fortune, pickpockets, sea wolves, smugglers and more. What we all have in common is a thirst for adventure and treasure wherever we can find it, and a spirit unshackled by any code but our own.

Most of us are driven by desires few understand - we have no real home but the sea and no family but our shipmates. Survival is uncertain when those you trust with your life may slip a blade between your ribs for a bit of gold or when the ocean that last night rocked you to sleep threatens to reduce the ship to splinters.


  1. There is no allegiance to a king or ruler who is not our own. If we serve such a power it is temporary and only for payment, be it gold, land or something we prize.

  2. Our ship(s) belong to the captain and crew. Our home upon the water cannot be the property of a single person.

  3. The authority of officers it to be respected. Those who disobey direct orders or challenge that authority shall be reprimanded and or punished.

  4. Treasure is our main objective. Finding new sources of wealth will be rewarded with extra shares or better position. Stealing from shipmates will not be tolerated.

  5. Shipmates are the closest thing we have to family. We help each other whenever possible.

  6. Mutiny is a serious undertaking. When successful, it shall provide the crew with a new leader. A failed mutiny provides only disturbance and shall be punished severely.

  7. A ship sails smoothly only when everyone carries out their duties. Find a job and do it well. There is no such thing as a passenger!


Revised by Captain Kiko Hibou d'Hiver ~ October 5, 2001
