A fair young lad set foot upon the dock, clearly cut from a proud, seafaring stock.
Today, at last, he'd sail the open sea and then, a sailor, he could claim to be.
But as he planked, a gentle voice he heard. He paused and strained to pick out ev'ry word.

I am the singer on the shore. I pray your voyage comes with ease.
Until you dock, I will emplore, "Gods, grant a strong and peaceful breeze."

The captain called for anchors to be weighed. His deep, booming voice all the men obeyed.
He breathed in deep to taste the salty air and tapped his breast and knew the flask was there.
Then as he turned, a distant voice came near. It carried on the wind and drifted to his ear.

I am the singer on the shore. I pray your voyage comes with ease.
Until you dock, I will emplore, "Gods, grant a strong and peaceful breeze."

Atop the mast, the lookout gives a grin. He feels the tossing waves, the rushing wind.
He looks 'cross the waves through his glass, and swears she'll always be his only lass.
But as he pledges honor to the sea, he falters when he hears this melody...

I am the singer on the shore. I pray your voyage comes with ease.
Until you dock, I will emplore, "Gods, grant a strong and peaceful breeze."

The sailors who don't know her, number few. But none know her by name, Kiko Hibou.
And none will ever claim her as a wife. She doesn't love the men, she loves the life.
More than one have sworn love, for evermore, but still, she is the singer on the shore.
