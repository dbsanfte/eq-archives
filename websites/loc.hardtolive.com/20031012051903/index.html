July 1

On 28 June at 5 PM EST LoC held a guild meeting in EC at the tunnels. Approximately 10-15 members attended. There was a lot of talk and it was late in my time zone so please if I have left anything out post a thread below!

OFFICERS

The naming of new officers is on a temporary hold, except for filling the positions of Guild Raid Leader and Banker. Bacce did nominate himself for the position of Raid Leader and was the only member to do so. Concerns were raised about the lack of playing time that Bacce is on. Bacce is an alt character of Iceonyx, who is in another guild. All present at the meeting seemed to feel the position could be better filled my a person's main character that will get maximum playing time. Therefore we are still searching for a Raid Leader. Tulenia (our new guild leader) will be fulfilling the role in a limited capacity (planning and organizing events....more below in that) until we get the position filled. There were no nominees still in guild for the banker position.

MEMBERS COMMENTS, CONCERNS AND THOUGHTS

All present were asked to "speak" their minds about the guild.

The loot conflict between Charmonz and Inyar was discussed. It is hoped that people will try to work out differences independently in a mature manner. Should members find that they are unable to resolve a conflict then a guild officer should be approached for assistance in resolving the issue.

The lack of guild grouping was discussed at length. Some felt there was some animosity towards forming guild groups. All seem to feel that attempting to form guild groups would be an ideal way to strengthen our guild. It was suggested that logging on and saying hi in guild and stating your class and level would be a good way to get started on forming more groupings of guild members. While all felt it would be ideal to have guild groups in no way is it a requirement. All realize that there may be times people prefer to group with non-guilded friends and so on. No one should be made to feel the need to leave an already formed group to group with guildmates unless they freely chose to do so. The variety of character levels in the guild was also a factor in the discussion. LMAO...I've forgotten the formula for grouping levels!

Many of us get confused as to which character belongs to which player. With that in mind if people will go to the guild management tool and add Main and Alt of XXXX (your main character's name) under the public notes area to the roster it would certainly help a bit!

Several points were raised as to the variety of guild types. It was felt that ours is a cozy friendly type guild not a hard-core raiding guild. That is not to say we won't raid but that did not seem to be the focus direction wanted for our guild.

Many were concerned over the disbanding of several members recently. This was a tough issue....we cannot control the actions of our members....we're not communists! We are striving to make changes to strengthen the guild. People leave a guild for a variety of reasons....as the saying goes "you can please some of the people some of the time, but you can't please all of the people all of the time"! The main point is for us to have fun.

Looking at those registered on GuildMagic (PLZ be sure you've registered your characters here!) we have a bit of a diversity problem. Our guild is weak on tanks and clerics. All members are asked to actively recruit members of these classes especially. While we certainly do not want to discriminate against any classes or levels mid to high level tanks and clerics are going to be a necessity for conducting the raids and events mentioned next in this posting.

EVENT/RAID IDEAS

Tulenia had several raid/event ideas. He will be posting a voting poll to determine the best days and times for these raids/events to assure maximum participation. It was discussed that both a weekday and weekend day would be chosen according to your votes. This is in accordance with our democratic approach to guild management.

While we are not limited to the following ideas these should get us started on a road to new events/raids for the guild. We hope you will present new ideas or suggestions in threads below.

1. Level 1 War. Guild members would create level 1 characters, leave them untwinked and have wars. Maybe someone else can explain this further. I'm not sure I fully understand this! Days and times will be determined by the voting poll mentioned above.

2. Baby Raids (lvl 30-40). Regularly schedule raids for lvl 30-40 characters. Days and times will be determined by the voting poll mentioned above.

3. Big Raids (lvl 45+). Regularly schedule raids for lvl 45+ characters. Days and times will be determined by the voting poll mentioned above.

4. Roleplaying Events. All characters would roleplay their characters. Maybe someone else can explain this further. I'm not sure I fully understand this one either! Days and times will be determined by the voting poll mentioned above.

ALLIANCES

Last, but not least, alliances were discussed. Endless Society has expressed an interest in allying with us and it was felt that Dracos Infurnum, Madcap Elite, and Sjendarim might also be interested in forming an alliance. Work will begin immediately on forming some alliances to assist us with the ability to have raids and events in the guild.


There is bound to be something I forgot or missed....looking over the jumbled notes I took it was a challenge to get this out. LMAO....if you think my typing is bad you should my handwriting. I think I deciphered most of the scribble, but I may have forgotten or totally missed some points or issues that were raised. Please post anything you think of that I missed below and certainly add any comments, ideas, or thoughts you may have on the guild too! We are trying to make this a stronger and better guild, but it requires the work and dedication of everyone to succeed!
