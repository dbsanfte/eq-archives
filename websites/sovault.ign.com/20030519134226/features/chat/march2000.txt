<Aurum> Exactly how much are the vehicles and weapons in Sovereign going to be based on real life vehicles and weapons?  Will the weapons be sort of parallel to real world ones, or will they be actual ones in use today?  -laphter
<SovProducer> The units and weapons are taken from real life coounterparts. We didn't want to use modern names because the units are altered somewhat, but theu are immediately recognizable which makes it easy to identify what unit or weapon they are. We did have to use some creativity in the expansion of the technology tree to add gameplay, aside from that everything is based on modern day technologies and vehicles.

<Aurum> Question> How will the movement of units that move on more than one plane be handeled. Such as planes and submarines, that have the ability to move up and down. -Inferno
<SovProducer> The actual movement of the units will be in 3D space like the unit type that they represent. The units are all 3D models so having planes flying and tanks driving across the ground is quite easy.
<SovProducer> Once again thank you for joining the Sovereign developers chat tonight, be sure to check out www.station.sony.com/sovereign for the latest information on Verants upcoming RTS.
<Aurum> QUESTION:  How does one control territories in Sovereign?
<SovProducer> Territories are controlled by the possession of cities. There are no "borders" in Sovereign. Since the units can move freely where the users order them to move the concept of "borders" is nearly impossible to enforce.
<Aurum> Does the image of a soldier in game represent one soldier?  Or will it represent a squadron?.  eg:  The StarFighters in Rebellion, 1 fighter represented a squadon of 12 fighters.  -Krichak
<SovProducer> 1 = 1 simple math. We do not want to confuse the player by representing a false number of units. 
<SovProducer> Be sure to check out the redesign of Sovault.com, new information with exclusive interviews are posted on the main page. Also be sure to check out the message boards,development teams post there daily.
<Aurum> QUESTION: In a previous posting , you mentioned that a player who wants to can build up large armies of units, like tanks for instance, and then march them across to battle. With up to 500 players in a game, will this possibly lead to lag as it does in some traditional RTS's?
<SovProducer> We have learned quite a bit from EverQuest as well as Tanarus. The design of the server load balancing takes the possibility of thousands of units moving across the globe at any one time. It was a hurdle that we had to cross for this game but we have succeded and are moving forward.
<Aurum> Follow up> So, say, an Airplance will be able to move freely through the air, not in, say, 2-3 predefined levels? -Inferno
<SovProducer> We are currently keeping air units moving on one level to keep the information required for movement that is sent to the server at a minimmum. Don't get me wrong, planes will avoid other planes by adjusting their altitude but this adjustment is made by the game not the user.
<Aurum> Question - Will troops have a morale system?
<SovProducer> Troops have morale. The morale is tied to each individual unit, so it is possible for units to retreat during battle. This is a consideration that must be taken into account when forming battle groups.
<Aurum> Will enemy detection and surveillance play a big part in Sovereign? -laphter
<SovProducer> It is one of the 3 tiers to gameplay. #1 Diplomacy, #2 Espionage, # Battle. These are the design focuses for the game and what the entire game revolves around.
<Aurum> Welcome GunnerX!  GunnerX is Sovereign's Assistant Producer.
<Aurum> QUESTION: How will damage affect units?  Will it be bar damage? Will damage lower the effectiveness of a unit?  And how will damage be shown on the units?   - Thanos
<SovProducer> Damage is handled in a traditional form health bars that are asociated with each individual unit. Damage is displayed by damaged textures and particle animations on the individual units. Damage can reduce the effectiveness of a unit so it pays to be able to repair.
<Aurum> About the fog of war.. Will it be even in it?  Or will it just be radars, and maybe weather effecting visibility like storms etc.
<SovProducer> We are currently expieriementing with fog of war. Since the game is 3D there are quite a few different ways of implementing it.
<Aurum> Speaking about technology, there will probably be nukes involved.  If so, and a city/territory gets "Nuked", will it comform to comventional rules, and make that area, unihabitable for a certain time frame?   -Tobias
<SovProducer> YES, YES, did I mention yes? Nuclear warfare is going to be different in Sovereign. No more nuking tanks and their armor drops to half, nuclear weapons and nuclear warfare are dramatic and change the gameplay for the parties involved. And sometimes for some parties that aren't involved. 
<SovProducer> Players will have to consider all of the options when launching a nuclear weapon. They are not intended for hourly launches.
<SovProducer> besides
<SovProducer> you wouldnn't want a accident at the nuclear site to cause contamination of YOUR city, would you?
<Aurum> QUESTIONS:  If my unit survives a battle.  Will he gain skill and improve his battle and tactics.  eg: Myth and Myth 2 has something similar.
<SovProducer> Units gain expierience and that expierience translates into rank. Rank translates into higher offensive and defensive attributes. There are also bonus modifiers that are associated with each rank. The original goal was to make the player WANT to keep his units alive, instead of sending them into the fight without carring.
<Aurum> Question> Clint, I will once again bring up the subject of Sovereign time vs. Real Time, how will the two compare? -Inferno
<SovProducer> It all depends on the definition of real time?!? There will be no stopping or modifying that actual time in sovereign, so you cant pause the game an pick your moves and hit play. 
<SovProducer> The game plays out in "real time". A real day will not mimic a sovereign day, due to the fact that most people play these games around 4-6 at night
<SovProducer> You wouldn't want to play the game if everytimme you log in it was nighttime.
<SovProducer> We have to keep everyone in mind on issues like that and try to please the mass market.
