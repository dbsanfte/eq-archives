Wednesday, February 12, 2003

Sovereign is Officially Vaporware

What many have suspected has now come true, SOE has cancelled Sovereign. Although it would have been nice if they sent out a press release rather than letting people find out second hand in this CNN Money article on SOE.
Work on "Sovereign," a massively multiplayer real time strategy game, has been terminated after more than four-and-a-half years of development. Ambitious in nature, the game had hoped to replicate a continuous global war that supported up to 500 players. Diplomacy would have played as significant a role as the player's tactical abilities.

"We came to a decision that it was not going to be what we wanted it to be," said McDaniel. "It never really had the magic."

I'd like to thank the Sovereign community for staying behind the game even when there was no news on it and to Jonric and IGN for never closing down Sovereign Vault. Thank you all.
- posted by LAR @ 3:32 PM PST

Single Character per Server

This is only indirectly related to Sovereign, but the following entry showed up on the Star Wars Galaxies FAQ yesterday(which is also being developed by SOE). Is this a hint of things to come for Sovereign? Only time will tell, but a precedent for it has now been set.
Question
Name: Jarik
Asked: Jan 6, 2003 (20:03)
Category: Players
Title: Can we have Multiple Characters?
Question: Will I be able to have more than one character per account?

Answer
Name: Gonk
Answered: Jan 7, 2003 (11:06)
Currently, no. The developers have stated that SWG is going to use a Single Character per Server system. (Typically called "SCS" on the forums.) This means that you would have only one character per server. If you wanted another character on the same server, you would have to buy another account.

Life on the official forums

A sign of life reappeared Wednesday on the official forums when Froech made the following post on why he was MIA.
I really think you guys are reading way too much into the fact that I've been a bit too busy to be posting much.
Things haven't changed in my ability to post information. That doesn't mean that the information hasn't changed.

Yes, Kelly Flock isn't here any more. That hasn't changed SOE's commitment to this project one iota. I'm just not in a position to release additional information yet... no matter how much you all harp on me to.

We're still working away like busy beavers. We haven't been canceled, nor has there been any discussion about canceling us to my knowledge. We think this product will be the end-all of Massively Multi Player Online Strategy Games, once we've got it completed.

Yes, it's taking longer than we originally thought. Why? I've addressed this before. We're solving problems that no one else has had to deal with before. We underestimated the difficulty of taking on those problems. Does that mean we're going to tuck our tails between our legs and run away?

Nope. We're going to continue our efforts, until we get it right, and get this game out.

Sure, it's hard for you folks... no news coming out, no new stuff to discuss. But, in this case, no news is good news. We've not been canceled, I'm not here to shut down the boards...but without any significant new information to give to you all, there isn't a whole lot of purpose for me to be in here every day, chatting up a whole bunch with you. I've been quiet for a while, and I'll be quiet for a while longer.

But that doesn't mean I don't care about you, individually, or collectively. It means I'm working hard on getting this project done. And to be truthful, the amount of time it takes to be in here, read, and compose responses is time that I could be getting the game done. (Now, don't you all feel guilty? :-) )

I'm not trying to hurt you all by starving you of information. I am trying not to mislead you by giving you bogus information, or teasing you by dribbling out stuff that doesn't matter. We are all serious about this game. I don't want to waste your time, just as much as you don't want to waste mine.

If you really want to help, stop over in the General Discussions area, and give me some feedback over there. You have been a vital source of information in the past, helping guide our thoughts on some systems. We're soliciting more thoughts from you over there.

For now, I'm gonna head back to some work on a build of the game.

Froech

Wednesday, January 23, 2002

Link Death

After a brief leave of absence, Froech was back on the boards today commenting on dropping your connection.
Indeed, this is a concern of both our engineers, and our designers.

It wouldn't quite be fair to just implode all your units the second we determined you were no longer connected.

But then comes the side issue... how long do we wait? 1 minute? 3 minutes? An hour, even?

We're going to have to determine some appropriate time, likely in testing, both alpha and beta, that is appropriate.

Then there's the issue about how does that impact your play... say I'm in a game teamed up with Fist of Iron, against larrypeters and Usambara2, and I go link dead. Where does that leave Fist? What options should we provide in that battle?

Something to think about, no?

Froech
