FAQ

Last updated: May 26th, 2001

What is Sovereign?

Sovereign is a massively multiplayer real-time strategy game being developed by Verant Interactive. It has been described as a Diplomacy/Civilization mixer with a shot of Command & Conquer, with emphasis on making and breaking alliances. Play will take place on a world similar to Earth, where you will control an empire consisting of cities. Sovereign will break new ground in the RTS genre by introducing elements such as a persistent gameworld, massive amounts of simultaneous players, and dynamic changing of game features.
3D or pre-rendered 2D?
All units and map elements will be rendered real-time in 3D. Individual models will be animated -- watch copter blades spin and gunship turrets swivel.
Sovereign is an on-line only game?
Yes. All games will be played over the internet and on Verant's servers.  You will not be able to host one on your local computer.
Sovereign is subscription based, then?
Yes. The model will be similar to that of EverQuest. 
What are the system requirements?
PC hardware requirements are unknown at this point. The game will require you to have a connection to the internet. Verant has also said that a 3D graphics accelerator will be needed.  "Every game that Verant develops is 3D only due to the next generation graphics that we are known for."
What can you tell me about a beta test?
There will be an open beta test. A date for this has not been established, but it is some time off -- at least until the end of 2001 and maybe not until 2002.
Will there be a limit to the number of games that can be created? Will there be one game per server?
Empires will be involoved in only one actual game per server. Each game will also have will have multiple excursion worlds, the RTS part of Sovereign. There will be multiple excursion worlds that will be being played on each server at any one time. So you can log into your empire and then you select the excursion world that you would like to play.
Can I join an excursion game at any time?
Yes. When an excursion world is created the number of available players that can be on that world at any one time is determined. When the game is underway, new players are able to join a game as long as there is an open resource spot.  Players can also be involved with multiple games at one time by creating different empires. Verant is currently planning on allowing players to have at least two empires per server, the final number has yet to be determined.
How many players can be on one excursion world?
Between 4 and 500. The word thousands has been uttered, but Verant has gone with the smaller figure after running game tests.
How long will games last?
Verant is currently planning on having games last between a few hours to two or three days.
That's a lot shorter time frame then what I heard before. Weren't games originally suppose to last up to a month?
Verant has decided that getting attacked when you weren't online wouldn't be very fun. So they've made it so that players won't need to play on excursion worlds when they're not online.
What kind of terrain is there?
Mountains, deserts, icy wastelands, rolling hills, forests, tundra, oceans, etc. In other words, all the extremes found here on Earth. It has also been said that there the game may contain planets that aren't similar to Earth.
What kind of units are available?
Verant is "using a storyline style called future regressive. Which is there was a more advanced society, they regressed somewhere, and it's pushing it's way back forward." So expect to see modern units as well as futuristic units.
Are there different classes and technology trees?
Sort of. There won't be classes per say in Sovereign. Instead there will be what are called traditions, which are essentially classes. Each empire will have both a military and a civilian tradition which will define what "skills" that empire will have and what area's of the technology tree it'll be able to research.
Will the pace of play be similar to C&C?
This remains to be seen.
What makes Sovereign "persistent"?
Verant has moved away from a Massively Multiplayer Real Time Strategy type game and is now designing a Massively Multiplayer game with some RTS part to it. Which means that the game is now focused on being a Massively Multiplayer game first, and to do certain things in the game you'll need to go to a sub, RTS, game. The center of a player's empire will exist on what's called a homeworld, which will always be in the game. From there players will store their population, resources, and units. Players will then land on excursion worlds for the RTS part of the game.
Can't I just bring all of my units with me to invade an excursion world?
No. The transport you take to an excursion world has a limited amount of space on it. Players will only be able to bring an equal amount of units and resources with them. The same is true for the trip back to your homeworld.
Will it be possible to invade another player's homeworld?
No. You won't be able to land on a player's homeworld and conduct a military campaign. You will however be able to conduct more discrete actions against a player's homeworld.
Will there be an in-game chat system?
Yes, there is an in-game email system that is very similar to a standard email system that you would use for normal messages. Since the game is persistent Sovereign allows the players to send messages to other players and if the other player is not online it will be sent to their in-game email box. The players can check their emails when they log back into the game.
Is guild/clan play available?
Sovereign is going to be centered around team play. Upon creating an empire a player will become a member of what's called a great house. Each of these great houses will consist of hundreds of players. In addition to being a member of a great house players will also be members of a guild in each house, each consisting of between fifty and one hundered players. Each of a house's member guilds will also be fighting for control of the entire house.
Will there be nuclear and chemical weapons like in other RTS's?
Verant has said there will be weapons of mass destruction in the game, but they are still designing them.
