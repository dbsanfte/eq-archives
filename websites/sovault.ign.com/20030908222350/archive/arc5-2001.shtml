Thursday, June 14, 2001

Lets all work togeather

Froech made this post about build Sovereign's community today on the official forums.
OK, lets see if I can take them one at a time....
You're partially right, GotheJ, but the fact is we want to encourage participation of a good kind on both sides of the fence. A new player always has incentive to get together with an older player, mostly for protective reasons (and yes, I'm relating this in my mind to EQ type games, although it is applicable across genres). But as we've been talking, we don't particularly want the griefers to reign supreme, so we need to figure out a way to make it advantageous for that alliance to take place on the Vet's side.

Remember, you want to have mixing of the levels, to engage the new players into sticking around. Get them involved, get them to stay to become a vet, to be your ally or your enemy, a real challenge. If we segregate the two groups completely from each other, then I suspect that neither will ultimately be happy, and we won't have a successful game.

Second, those "mechs" you see are just engineers. They run around and build things, or fix things that are broken. They don't have weapons (at least right now), although we've been joking about letting them carry a drill that will allow them to drill you to death. Kinda like an instructor.... (ok, bad pun).

Thirdly, as LAR's been saying, all of the stuff you see in game right now is merely concept stuff we put in for E3, not final units. Now, they may stay in, or they may not. That decision hasn't been made, nor will it be for a while. The artists are hard at work coming up with other concepts for each tradition/style/'insert appropriate name here'.

Hope that helps.

Froech

- posted by LAR @ 6:31 PM PDT

What are you willing to pay?

Froech wants to know what we think is an acceptable price for Sovereign. Go here to check out the thread.
I'd certainly be interested in what you all think is an appropriate amount to charge for a game. We haven't yet set pricing for this game (nor would I be involved in that), but it certainly could be a useful discussion to point people to at some point in time.

And of course, you should know that the right answer isn't "free". We have to pay for our work, so let's be honest with ourselves here.

Thanks for the input!
