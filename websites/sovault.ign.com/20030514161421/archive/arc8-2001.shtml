Wednesday, September 26, 2001

What is the definition of the word is?

Froech had the following comment to add to his post on victory conditions yesterday.
I did say X weeks. And I perhaps didn't phrase that sentence as clearly as I should have. I was talking, I believe, about controlling Y amount of territory for X time. What I didn't clearly state is that it would be your House, not necessarily "you" the Sovereign state.

My apologies for being unclear!

Froech

Tuesday, September 25, 2001

A possible slip of the tounge?

I found this reply from Froech of interest today, in which he makes a comment about controlling territory on an excursion world for more than a week. This is in stark contrast to what Geoffrey Zatkin said during the E3, that excursion world battles would only last "anywhere from half an hour to two or three real days."
It might be entirely possible that some planets have "victory conditions" that relate to resource gathering, while others have "victory conditions" that are to control the maximum amount of territory for X hours/days/weeks. There may even be other ways to "win" a world!

One of the advantages of having separate worlds is that you can set up for different styles of play on each one of them.

Froech

- posted by LAR @ 4:23 PM PDT

Monday, September 24, 2001

Stay in formation?

Froech has the following to say when asked if players would be able to put units into a formation.
I wouldn't be suprised to see that kind of thing happening in our game.

The guy doing the coding on pathing is pretty good. He also did the pathing on Age of Empires.

There's been quite a bit of discussion between he and the designer as to what is appropriate, and what isn't. And discussion with the artists, in terms of clipping and occlusion of objects. This guy knows what he's doing, and is doing it well

Froech

Saturday, September 22, 2001

What you see is not what you'll get

There's still not much in the way of news on Sovereign coming from Verant, but the following was posted by Froech on the official forums yesterday.
In fact, none of the art from the show at E3 except for the Engineer unit itself is in the game.

All units are different, all buildings are different, all terrain is different, the worlds are different, and a significant portion of the user interface is different.

We're moving forward. We want to show you how much as soon as we can. I know you guys want to kill us, but trust me, it will be worth the wait.

Froech
