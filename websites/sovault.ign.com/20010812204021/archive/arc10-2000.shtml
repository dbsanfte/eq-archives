Saturday, November 25, 2000

GZ Working on Sovereign

Sovereign Lead Designer Geoffrey Zatkin, whom many of you will remember as Norrath's Arch Mage, was given a prominent role in a recent article published on The Station. The article in question discusses the role of a game's lead designer. Here's a clip:
    “The Lead Designer is the person who works with the overall design goal of the game,” says Geoff Zatkin, Lead Designer for Sovereign. “They are given a task from the producer who says, ‘We’re making this kind of game, here’s our basic idea, take that and turn the idea into a 200 page document detailing every step along the way of how to make it.’ This is basically the blueprint of the game. The lead designer with the team takes the game and parses it into systems, and says, ‘okay, we’re going to have combat, we’re going to have technology trees, we’re going to have each of these systems,’ and then they subdivide those. Design of the game then begins. They hand it to the rest of the team, the programmers and the artists, who divide up their tasks. While this is going on, the design team is continuing to flesh out all the systems that they’ve designed. And once they have an engine, they start play-testing it, to see which of the things that looked great on paper don’t actually work, and which things do.”
Special thanks to IronFist, of Sovereign Empire, for passing this info along.
- posted by Aurum @ 7:58 PM PST

Thursday, November 16, 2000

New Producer Letter

Also new on the official Sovereign page is a brand new Producer Letter written by the one and only Clint Worley. Here's a complete copy of it:
    Wednesday 11.15.2000

    Welcome to the Sovereign Web page. Allow me to introduce myself, my name is Clint Worley a.k.a. CobraJet. I have been in the industry for 6 years, starting at the bottom and working my way up. My most recent projects included Tanarus and Cyberstrike 2 and now Sovereign. I have a heavy console background and I enjoy the freedom that PC's give to designers as well as programmers when making incredible games.

    Sovereign offers a unique concept, which in turn offers a unique game. We feel that Real Time Strategy games are enjoyed by millions and the next logical step in the genre is the massively multiplayer environment. One of the charters in SOE's goal is to offer massively multiplayer games set in persistent universes. Sovereign offers this, and opens the doors for the genre to evolve into the dreams that we have always had.

    Hundreds of players waging war against each other, trading resources, spying on each other, researching technologies and backstabbing close friends. These are the elements that Sovereign brings to the table. We understand that the RTS genre is crowded, we understand that many of you feel that you have played them all and they are all basically the same with different graphics. This is why we offer you a new experience unlike any that you have had before. A RTS game set in a persistent universe, empires that grow and citizens that react to your every move. Trades that sacred and friendships that will last a lifetime.

    Please check the updated FAQ. We have included new information that you will find intriguing. We have also included new screenshots displaying the new graphics engine that we have been working on. Look forward to interviews with the team members discussing their role on the team and their thoughts on the industry and the RTS genre. We are also implementing a polling feature so that we can get feedback from our fans on various topics of discussion. The message boards are a great place to post thoughts and visions and I welcome all to post there and become a regular.

    "Welcome to Sovereign, the only Massively Multiplayer Real Time Strategy game on the planet set in 3D. We offer you the world……. All you have to do is take it!"

    – Clint Worley
    Producer Sovereign
    Sony Online Entertainment
