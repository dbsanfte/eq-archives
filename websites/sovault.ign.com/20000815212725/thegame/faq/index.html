FAQ

Last updated: July 31, 2000
Thanks LAR!

What is Sovereign?

Sovereign is a massively multiplayer real-time strategy game being developed by Verant Interactive. It has been described as a Diplomacy/Civilization mixer with a shot of Command & Conquer, with emphasis on making and breaking alliances. Play will take place on a world simillar to Earth, where you will control an empire consisting of modern-day cities and technologies. Your sole mission: conquer all other players. Sovereign will break new ground in the RTS genre by introducing elements such as a persistent gameworld, massive amounts of simultaneous players, and dynamic changing of game features.
3D or pre-rendered 2D?
All units and map elements will be rendered real-time in 3D. Individual models will be animated -- watch copter blades spin and gunship turrets swivel.
Sovereign is an on-line only game?
Yes. All games will be played over the internet and run on Verant's servers. You will be able to start a game there, but not host one on your local machine.
Sovereign is subsription based, then?
Yes. The model will be simillar to that of EverQuest. You buy Sovereign at retail, which includes one free month. After that you pay a monthly fee for unlimited play. Discounts will be availble with pre-payment.
What are the system requirements?
PC hardware requirements are unknown at this point. The game will require you to have a connection to the internet. Verant has said a 3D graphics accelerator will be needed.  "Every game that Verant develops is 3D only due to the next generation graphics that we are known for."
What can you tell me about a beta test?
There will be an open beta test. A date for this has not been established, but it is most likely some time off -- the tentative release date for Sovereign is for the second quarter of 2001.
Will there be a limit to the number of games that can be created? Will there be one game per server?
Sovereign's GMs are responsible for the creation of the games and will decide when new globes are to be added to the available games. Servers will have multiple games that are being played on them at any one time. So you can log into a server and then you select the game that you would like to play. Players will also be able to move their characters from server to server and from game to game also. However, there will be consequences for doing so.
Can I join a game at any time?
Yes, when the game is created the number of available players is determined. When the game is underway, new players are able to join into a game as long as there is an open city.  Players can also be involved with multiple games at one time but there is only one limiting factor, once you register one of your characters into a game that character is not available to join other games. If you would like to use that character in other games you will have to resign from the game that he is currently registered in and then move him to the new game. Since you have 6 different slots for characters you can use a different character or create a new one to play in a different game.
But what if I enter a game and there aren't any resources near by?
Cities are tied to resources. The collection of resources is done with the construction of a city. There will not be any resource "collection" vehicles. 
How many players can be in one game?
Between 4 and 500. The word thousands has been uttered, but it appears that Verant has gone with the smaller figure after running game tests.
Will you always need to "kill" everyone else to win?
No.  When you select a game to join you are briefed on the victory conditions, once these victory conditions are met the game will "end". That game ends and all of the players that are registered in that game will be taken back to the game selection screen. All of the experience that you have gained or lost from the previous game will dictate the available technology and skills for your empire in the following games.
How long do games last?
This will mostly be determined by the type of game being played. Some games can last as long as a month, with typical games ranging from two-three weeks. Shorter scenarios are also possible.
One month? But I need to go to work!
You will have the option of allowing the server to control your empire while you are logged off, presumeably in a mode you select (defensive, agressive, neutral, etc.). You may also elect to have your resources controlled by an ally. Additionally, there will be game types that limit the amount of moves/attacks you can make in a 24 hour period, thereby limiting powergamers and those with more time to spare than you.
What kind of terrain is there?
Mountains, deserts, icy wastelands, rolling hills, forests, tundra, oceans, etc. In other words, all the extremes found here on Earth.
Will the units travel at the same speed over different terrain?
No, the terrain does effect the speed and the overall distance that a unit can travel. Each terrain type has a speed associated with it that dictates the overall modifier that will be placed on the unit’s total speed. This creates a unique situation where some units can move across specific terrain types faster or slower then others so planning attacks becomes more strategic. There are some other areas that are tied to the terrain that must be taken seriously like the unit's fuel consumption.
What kind of units are available?
Units will be similar to our present day weaponry. Expect to see tanks, battleships, helicopters, ground units, missiles, and even nukes. What you can produce is tied to your current level of resources and technology.
Are there different classes and technology trees?
Yes. There is a baseline level of technology that all players posess. After that, different classes will progress on seperate technology paths. This is expected to make espionage and alliances all the more critical.
How does the map interface work?
Sovereign is played on a 3D globe. You will be able to move about the globe, zooming and rotating freely, selecting and commanding units. As you climb the technology ladder, your monitoring abilities will increase as you pick up aircraft and satellites. At advanced levels, you can zoom from your tank up through the clouds and into space, then back down again.
Will the pace of play be similar to C&C?
No. A slower pace is expected in Sovereign, with more emphasis placed on diplomacy than most RTS games. Units will also be more valuable and less likely to be expended without blinking.
Are there any role-playing elements?
Yes, the character that you are playing as will gain experience from game to game. Verant's designing the experience model in a way that would be familiar to RPG players. You gain experience and you lose experience depending on the actions taken in the games. Overall the character is constantly gaining experience.
Will there be an in-game chat system?
Yes, there is an in-game email system that is very similar to a standard email system that you would use for normal messages. Since the game is persistent Sovereign allows the players to send messages to other players and if the other player is not online it will be sent to their in-game email box. The players can check their emails when they log back into the game or they can have them forwarded to their own private email box.
Is guild/clan play available?
Probably. The mechanics of this feature have yet to be determined/revealed.
What's going to happen when large guilds ally together and take over a game? 
Players will be able to make alliances but there will be a limit to the total number of players allowed in the alliance. This forces the players to really care about who is in their alliance and it also makes the other players in the game strive to be the best at what they do so that they are a prime pick to be picked for an alliance.
Will units and cities be micro-managed?
The control that each user has over his units is the exact same as traditional RTS games. You can select units, issue commands, and group them. Verant decided to stay true to the standardized layout when it comes to the unit control. The player is also in control of the build-out of his individual cities. Verant has removed most of the micro-management but they do make the player accept some responsibly for their empire.
Will there be nuclear and chemical weapons like in other RTS's?
Yes, but don't think you'll be able to just go off nuking people.  These weapons are "powerful and threatening" but the morale of your empire will react accordingly to the production, stockpiling and use of these weapons.
Do my units also have morale?
Yes, morale can cause units to think twice about attacking or even moving. Units can be lost to the enemy also in dire situations where they have no other choice but to surrender or parish.
