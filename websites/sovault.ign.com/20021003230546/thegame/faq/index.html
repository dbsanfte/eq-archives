FAQ

Last updated: December 31st, 2001

What is Sovereign?

Sovereign is a Massively Multiplayer(MMP) Real-Time Strategy(RTS) game being developed by Verant Interactive(aka Sony Online Entertainment). I would describe it as being akin a web league based on a Real Time Strategy game all wrapped up into one neat little package. Players will have their empires based on what are called "Homeworlds" while RTS battles take place on other planets called "Excursion Worlds". Sovereign will break new ground in the RTS genre by introducing elements such as a persistent gameworld, massive amounts of simultaneous players, and dynamic changing of game features.
3D or pre-rendered 2D?
All units and map elements will be rendered real-time in 3D. Individual models will be animated -- watch copter blades spin and gunship turrets swivel.
Sovereign is an on-line only game?
Yes. All games will be played over the internet and on Verant's servers.  You will not be able to host one on your local computer.
Sovereign is subscription based, then?
Yes. The model will be similar to that of other Massively Multiplayer Games, such as EverQuest. 
What are the system requirements?
PC hardware requirements are unknown at this point. The game will require you to have a connection to the Internet. Verant has also said that a 3D graphics accelerator will be needed.  "Every game that Verant develops is 3D only due to the next generation graphics that we are known for."
What can you tell me about a beta test?
There will be an open beta test. A date for this has not been established, but I would expect it to be sometime mid-2002. Please note that this is only my opinion and it is not a date established by Verant Interactive.
What makes Sovereign "persistent"?
Verant has moved away from a Massively Multiplayer Real Time Strategy type game and is now designing as a Massively Multiplayer game with some RTS parts to it. As I've said before, it looks similar to a web base league you might find for other games, but with everything contained within one program. This means that the game is now focused on being a Massively Multiplayer game first, and in order to battle other players one will need to go to a sub, RTS, game. Sovereign will also have more to it then just RTS combat. Players will also have things like a "Galactic Trade Commission" to trade on, the ability to conduct espionage against other players, and the need to deal with inter-Guild politics.
Will there be a limit to the number of games that can be created? Will there be one game per server?
While it's not known if there will be only one gameplay server, Empires will be involved in only one actual game per server. Each game will also have will have multiple Excursion Worlds, the RTS part of Sovereign, that will be played on each server at any one time. So you can log into your Empire and then you select the Excursion World that you would like to play on.
Can I join an Excursion World at any time?
Yes. When an Excursion World is created the number of available players that can be on that world at any one time is determined. When the game is underway, new players are able to join a game as long as there is an open resource spot. However, once a player decides to end their current session, and log off, they will need to pack up their base and transport their units/resources back to their Homeworld. Players are also limited to being able to play on only one Excursion World at a time.
How many players can be on one Excursion World?
Between 4 and 500. The word thousands has been uttered, but Verant has gone with the smaller figure after running game tests.
Will there be any limits to the number of units a player can have on an Excursion World?
Yes. Due to bandwidth constraints players will be limited in the number of units that they can have on an Excursion World at any one time. The current limited being uttered by Verant is 100 units per player. However, this doesn't mean that a player couldn't store more units in their Transport.
What this "Transport" you talk about?
Players are required to "rent" a Transport to move their units and/or resources between their Homeworld and Excursion Worlds. The catch is you have a limited amount of space on a Transport and you can only bring one Transport to an Excursion World.
How long will battles last?
This part of the game is a little fussy now. Originally Verant had planned on the larger games taking up to a month to play, then they said Excursion World battles would only take a few hours to two or three days, now a few weeks is being spoken again. The important thing to remember is that even though it may take more than a day for an Excursion World's victory condition to be met, each player's base will be "dismantled" once they log out of the game. This means that players will need to work together in order to meet the victory conditions of some Excursion World.
Will there any "formal" way for players to group together in Sovereign?
Yes. As a matter of fact players must become part of a group when they create their empire. There are actually two types of groups in Sovereign: Great Houses, and Guilds. A Guild will consist of up to about fifty players and the Great Houses will be made up of a number of Guilds. It's expected that there will be three or four Great Houses for a player to choose from and any number of Guilds to join. It is not yet know whether or not players will have the ability to create their own Guilds in Sovereign, although I would expect they will. It has also been said that players will also be able to create "mercenary" groups, but not much is know about this yet.
What kind of terrain will there be on Excursion Worlds?
Mountains, deserts, icy wastelands, rolling hills, forests, tundra, oceans, etc. In other words, all the extremes found here on Earth. It has also been said that there the game may contain planets that aren't similar to Earth.
What kind of units are available?
Verant is "using a storyline style called future regressive. Which is there was a more advanced society, they regressed somewhere, and it's pushing it's way back forward." So expect to see modern units as well as futuristic units.
Are there different classes and technology trees?
Sort of. There won't be classes per say in Sovereign. Instead there will be what are called Traditions, which are essentially classes. Each empire will have both a military and a civilian Tradition which will define what "skills" that empire will have and what area's of the technology tree the player will be able to research.
Will the pace of play be similar to Command & Conquer?
This remains to be seen. The pace of the Excursion World demo shown at the 2001 E3 was similar to the pace you'd find in a Command & Conquer type game.
Will it be possible to invade another player's Homeworld?
No. You won't be able to land on a player's Homeworld and conduct a military campaign. You will however be able to conduct more discrete actions against a player's Homeworld.
Will there be an in-game chat system?
Yes, there is an in-game email system that is very similar to a standard email system that you would use for normal messages. Since the game is persistent Sovereign will allow players to send messages to other players and, if the other player is not online, it will be sent to their in-game email box. When a player logs back into the game they will see any messages that were sent to them while they were away.
Will there be nuclear and chemical weapons like in other RTS's?
Verant has said there will be weapons of mass destruction in the game, but has yet to say what kinds.
