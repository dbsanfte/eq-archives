Clint Responds To Skepticism

Kumquat_HB posted his concerns that Sovereign will be another cookie cutter RTS where tactics are unimportant and combat is unrealistic. Clint's reply:
I understand your concern and I believe that there is a happy medium between realistic damage and "fun" damage. The one point that I completely agree with you on is that the beta will determine the true feelings of the players. It is a interesting discussion, I mean if you have realistic damage being done then the battles are over really fast, but if you have game style battle that is being done the battles last longer but you lose the risk associated with combat. I would be interested to find out what some other fans think about the topic.

-Clint Worley
Producer/ Designer Sovereign
Verant Interactive Studios
Share your ideas by adding to this message. Thanks Clint!

    CobraJet Infobits

    More new information about Sovereign is being uncovered all the time! Sovereign Producer Clint Worley made several posts recently to the official boards:
      Influences for Sovereign
      Empire, Civilization and Command & Conquer were the main influences for Sovereign. We all loved playing those games and dreamt of a game that could bridge the gap between these great hits. The game is here!

      Newbie Strategies
      Keep in mind that your actions in Sovereign are constantly at the approval of your empire population. The government that you select along with the style that you play the game will dictate the freedom that you have when attempting to do whatever you want. It is true that a smaller empire might be expanding in areas that you may want at a later date but they are also absorbing the maintenance cost associated with that expansion and you aren't. The diplomacy between players is essential to the success of each individual empire. In other words... Let him take the territory but then demand a tribute be paid for your protection.

      Will Sovereign work on a 56K connection?Yes! Although the game is transferring a ton of information modem users will be able to play the game the way that the game was designed to be played. Although the connection speeds that are offered in various regions are improving we must design for the average connection to keep the mass-market player in mind.

      On New Screenshots
      We just released a new screenshot on Sovault.com. It displays some tanks and infantry. Trust me the combat that we have in the works right now will blow your socks off and the wait is worth it.
      --
      There will be a steady flow of screenshots between now and the launch at E3. Check the official webpage along with the fansites to get the most up to date screenshots.

      World Generation
      The worlds are randomly generated using fractals that decide where the terrain is placed in relation to the location on the globe. It is quite a tool, it allows us the most flexibility possible when we are creating and modifying the globes. The creation process when designing a globe is simple and straightforward which will translate into easy adjusting and tweaking when it comes to the beta, allowing us to have the game as balanced as possible when we launch.

      We are experimenting with the Fog of War. It is a little tricky because the engine does not NEED fog of war to hide the clipping so we don't want to include it in the way that is has been used traditionally. We are focusing on the battle right now for E3; fog of war is planned to be implemented after the show. Look for screenshots after the show with new features and battle displays.

      Unique Naming
      We recognize the desire to create unique names in games and we are catering to this desire in Sovereign. The only catch is that when the user logs into game they are logging in as a character, so the character will dictate the name of the empire. The player will be able to select the default name that is presented when they create a city, or select one of their own.

      Email Warning System
      When I set out to design Sovereign I asked myself a question, why aren't online games more "connected". Thus the implementation of the email warning system, the messaging system on your desktop, and we are exploring other areas of "connecting" to the game. We do understand that not everyone wants to or has access to these features and are taking that into account when we are implementing them.

      On 'Guilds'
      First off lets change the title from "guild" to alliance, and lets forget the huge numbers of people that are allowed to be in "gangs" in games like Everquest. I am aiming for a much smaller grouping system that forces the players to carefully consider the members of the group.

      More on Alliances
      We are looking forward to the closed and open beta testing to determine the correct numbers for an alliance. I understand the potential abuse that could happen from MEGA-Alliances and I personally like the idea of a much smaller alliance created by players. The idea of a mega alliance is a game-balancing nightmare; I am creating a game that I want to play. I would not want to play a game when I log into for the first time I get crushed by a 50-man alliance. Teamwork is the idea, not abuse.

      Newbies and Veterans
      The combination of newbie players and veterns in the same game is often looked at as "trouble". I welcome the challenge and in return offer gameplay that forces the veteran players into cooperating with the new players which will make the overall playing expierience better for all the players.

      SK2 and Sovereign
      I do agree that SK2 is an awesome game. However one of the biggest things missing from the game is the persistence that Sovereign offers. Logging out and your empire remaining in the game. Pager support, messaging systems, the whole nine yards. Sovereign will be bringing the term "real-time" strategy to a whole new level.

      Camping in Sovereign?
      Camping will not be a problem due to the fact that the resources have a limited life span. The resource that everyone is trying to obtain will only be conquered by one players but the resource will eventually run out and force the player to defend the location. The resources life span regenerates slowly allowing a well defended city and/or empire to maintain ties to the critical resources.

      Diplomacy:
      I like both of your examples for diplomacy in games. I however am a fan of the diplomacy that is found in board games like Diplomacy. I love the idea of players being forced to work together in the beginning of the game to survive and then the longer the game goes you begin to see people "potentially, friends" start cracking under the pressure and backstabbing you. If I were going to give a comparison of the style of diplomacy that you will be able to find in Sovereign it would be that exact example. BTW of you haven't played Diplomacy the board game, PLAY IT! It is a great game and plays completely different every time.

      Empires
      The overall design of the game caters to the helping of new empires. We reward the players that help smaller empires and create a bond that ties the two types of empire together. We understand that a smaller empire should get some kind of a reward for the defeat of a larger enemy, but keep in mind the situation that the defeat happens. Did the player defeat the enemy single-handed? Did he use nuclear weapons? Chemical? Was there a joint attack by his empire and his allies? These are some of the examples that come into the equation when we discuss balance between newbies and vets.

      Smart Bombing?
      A partial answer is YES! You can attack specific areas of the cities in Sovereign. Due to the engine and the layout of the cities you cannot however attack specific buildings in the city. You are attacking the area and the buildings that reside in that area will be damaged according to the effectiveness of your attack. This is a great tactic to use to cripple an enemy’s production or resource collection.

      Soundtrack
      Let me clear up what seems to be a little bit of confusion. We are working on the actual music for the game. It is the actual music for the game that I was referring to in the aforementioned comment. We have not yet discussed a soundtrack that will be available as a separate item.
      --
      I was talking about the music for the game also. I would never do MIDI files for the music. It just can't do music justice and besides with the media that people are using these days there really is no reason not to do .wav files at least. My personal favorite music from a game was the music in Total Annihilation. They did a great job on the tracks in that game and the power of the music made you feel like playing the game. Besides it was one of the first games that I know of that had a full orchestra perform the music.

      Alpha Testing
      Well first off the only alpha testing that we do is in-house. Once the game gets to a beta stage that we feel comfortable with we invite a small number of people into the game to conduct load testing and make sure that the game is stable in all aspects. Once the game is in a stable form we release it to the public in the open beta stage. This stage is still somewhat limited but allows WebPages and magazines to give away copies to the public. We welcome people in and ask for honest testing and cooperation with the dev team so that the final product is as bug free as we can get.
      --
      We really aren't discussing the beta-testing phase right now. We want to make sure that the battle as well as the trade and city simulation is up to speed for E3. Since we are showing the game there for the first time. I am thinking about getting a mailing list together for weekly updates, as soon as I get it together I will be asking for people to sign-up. I am planning on discussing development team status and progress as well as include various artwork from the design phase. I am sure that if you are interested in Sovereign you will want to be on the list.

      More on Globes
      Actually let me clear up the confusion. The globes that are the playing fields in Sovereign are complete globes. The engine that we have designed allows us to zoom from the battlefield level to orbit. It also allows you to rotate the globe at any level without loading new terrain. We designed the engine with this ability in mind. The technology of the user when he is playing the game dictates the total zooming and rotating ability that the user is capable of. We are working on the controls for the camera to keep the view easy to understand, due to the 3D engine the camera can get confusing at times.

      Timezones
      We are not planning to support any type of "time zones" in Sovereign. We do realize that there are people that cannot give their life and soul to the game and for those people we have timed games. The timed games have a set movement time per 24 hour time period. Example: a 2 hour time game will allow each player that is playing that game a two hour time limit per 24 hour day to play the game. Much in the same way as a game of chess is played with a timer. Once the player has reached the limit of his daily playing time he will be locked from the game until the following day. We encourage this style of play; it opens up the possibility of a player participating in multiple games at the same time. This allows the user to use each of the classes in separate game and be able to focus of the development of each character.

      Character Creation
      We are allowing up to 6 characters per account. This allows each user to have at least one of each class in his character selection. Aside from that if a player would like to create a seventh character he must delete an existing character or purchase another copy of the game.

    Multiple Games

    Can Plyaers engage in more than one game at a time? That's a question asked today on the Developers Board. Here's Clint's response:
      Players can be involved with multiple games at one time but there is only one limiting factor, once you register one of your characters into a game that character is not available to join other games. If you would like to use that character in other games you will have to resign from the game that he is currently registered in and then move him to the new game. Since you have 6 different slots for characters you can use a different character or create a new one to play in a different game.

      -Clint Worley
      Producer/ Designer Sovereign
      Verant Interactive Studios

    Sovereign's AI

    Verant's Clint Worley talked on Developers Board about Sovereign's AI, a subject not talked or mentioned a much as of yet. Here's his post
      The AI level of interaction is set by the player when they log out of the game. Each unit in the game has a stance that is associated with it. This stance dictates the AI of that specific unit. There is also general logging out options that the user sets to dictate the overall empire AI. We are not offering any type of scripting options for the players to make AI for their empire. The logging out options will give the players options when logging out of the game that create opportunities and disadvantages that the user must decide between, this creates a situation where the players decide what state they leave their empires in when they log out. Some people are more risky then others and some will makes sure that every city and unit is protected. We want everyone to have his or her own style reflected.

      -Clint Worley
      Producer/ Designer Sovereign
      Verant Interactive Studios

    Clint on Terrain

    Clint Worley, Sovereign's Producer, responded to a post regarding Sovereign's terrain on the Developers Board today:
      The terrain does effect the speed and the overall distance that a unit can travel. Each terrain type has a speed associated with it that dictates the overall modifier that will be placed on the unit’s total speed. This creates a unique situation where some units can move across specific terrain types faster or slower then others so planning attacks becomes more strategic. There are some other areas that must be taken seriously also like unit fuel... wait did I say that? Well there is unit fuel and its consumption is tied to terrain also. THIS GAME IS HUGE!

    Questions Answered

    The General Board is alive once again! Recently, Clint Worley took some time to answer a few questions in a recent post there:
      1)When a player is out of the game, will communications from other players(ie trade proposals, peace proposals) be put into a queue for the first player to review later or will there be an away message displayed or both?

      There is an in-game email system that is very similar to a standard email system that you would use for normal messages. Since the game is persistent we allow the players to send messages to other players and if the other player is not online it will be sent to their in-game email box. The players can check their emails when they log back into the game or they can have them forwarded to their own private email box.

      2)Will there be a limit to the number of games that can be created, so that the server's don't get overloaded? Will there be one game per server? Our GMs are responsible for the creation of the games and will decide when new globes are to be added to the available games. Servers will have multiple games that are being played on them at any one time. So you can log into a server and then you select the game that you would like to play. We allow the players to move their characters from server to server and from game to game also. There are consequences when moving that we will discuss at a later date.

      I hope that these have helped answer some of your questions! Please in the future ask one question at a time. It makes it easier to post the answers and the people that visit the board will be able to track the posting easier. Thanks!

      -Clint Worley
      Producer/ Designer Sovereign
      Verant Interactive Studios
