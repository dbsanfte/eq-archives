Saturday, February 24, 2001

Big Brother Is Watching

In a few weeks Verant is going to require people to sign up to post on the official forums, just like they did with Star Wars Galaxies. Froech explains why on the official forum:
    Ladies and Gentlemen,

    I just wanted to give you advance notification of some changes I've requested here on the Sovereign boards. Hopefully, it shouldn't impact you at all.

    What I've asked for is a sign up/registration system like what they're using over on the Star Wars Galaxies pages. If all you do is read, you won't have to sign up (and I know there are a lot of you lurking out there). If you post, you'll need to sign up.... once. After that, you shouldn't see anything different.

    And to answer your questions, it is so that I can track what's going on over here in the boards more effectively. So if you want to blame someone, I'm your man!

    It'll be several weeks before this gets implemented, so if you want to discuss it, now's the time.

    Froech

Visit the official forum to find out more and to post to this thread.

- Reported by LAR
- posted by Aurum @ 9:26 AM PST

Verant Wants Low Modem Speeds Too

Froech posts on the official forums why it's advantageous to both the end user and Verant to keep modem requirements low:
    I think you all have hit it on the head. Some games work well across certain "pipes", others don't. We're certainly sensitive to those issues (and with EQ on our side, we have a wee bit of experience).

    Of course, I can't tell you anything concrete at this point in time, as our code has all sorts of extras for bug tracking in it, but we're certainly aiming for lowest possible connection speed, to maximize number of potential customers. Of course, we have to balance that desire with the amount of information we want to pass down, so that game play is engaging, and fun. We not only want you to try the game for a month, but continue to play for the rest of your lives.

    You might be suprised, but some of the limitations imposed on us are not the size of the "pipe" leading to the client, but how many people are playing, and how much the total information is (we have to fit X number of people's information through our own "pipe", before it gets spread out to your individual "pipes".

    But we are certainly cognizant of the issue, and continue to do everything possible to maximize the number of people who can play, without impacting the "fun" factor of the game.

    Froech
