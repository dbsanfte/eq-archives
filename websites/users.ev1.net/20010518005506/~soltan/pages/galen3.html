Galen Meets the Wild Knights...Part 3

"I hate gate travel," thought Corran as he stepped into the blue sphere.   It took a moment for his eyes to adjust.  Silen was beside him, helping Taj to his feet.  Ten paces behind their position a stranger remained motionless against the ground.  Forty paces in front of them, Corran saw Soltan trying to shield Sativa and Silver from the attack of three ettin’s. Corran abruptly realized that the gate had closed, SangDrax was left behind. Sang had a shorter fuse than Sativa, once his mana was replenished he would join them. Deciding to act now before the condition of his brothers became critical, Corran started to issue commands. "Taj tend to Silver, keep him healed and protected, I’ll assist our Knight Superion." "What about the stranger?" Taj uttered. The butt of Corran’s halberd whacked his helm; he quickly learned the answer to that question. Silen’s eyes grew wide at the carnage around him; the boy started to salivate. Corran knew the wisest course of action was to let Silen loose. SangDrax was the only person other than Lord Sativa that could keep that blood thirsty wild child under control. So he unleashed the dog of war and yelled "Silen, Attack!"

"That has to be Silen" thought Sativa when he heard the screaming howls behind him.  He turned and watched Silen streaking toward his position at top speed.   Silen was chanting some old Celt war song "something about open the gates of hell company's a coming."  "The boy is in beserker mode, better be getting the hell out of his way.", muttered Sativa.  If Death had a poster child it was Silen.  He wasn't very bright, he didn't believe in soap, but the boy had a talent for killing.  Judging by Corran's stupid grin, he purposely released the dog of war and waited for the dust to settle before he reached the battle.  Silen jumped on the carcass of a fallen giant.  He used his elevated position to climb the back of an attacking ettin.  Silen drove his sword into the beasts back, burying the blade deeply.  The giant bucked and arched his back vainly trying to dislodge the foreign object.  Grasping the giant's neck with one hand, Silen drew his hand-axe.  From his perched position Silen started hacking at the giant's throat; gushers of blood spurted everywhere.  The ettin finally grabbed Silen and threw him down on the ground; the monster tried to stomp him but he just rolled before the giant's foot reached him.   Back on his feet, Silen resumed his attack whacking the monster's knee with his axe.  The poor monster knew his doom was at hand and began to retreat, Silen grew his crossbow and sunk three bolts into the giant's back, sending the behemoth crashing down.

Corran and Taj finally reached the locale and recited the words "In Vas Mani".  Both Silver and Sativa felt their bodies become rejuvenated. "Vas Ort Flam" whispered Soltan , an explosion ripped open the second giant's chest dropping him to one knee; Sativa saw the opportunity and took it.  He swung his hally at the giant, shifting his weight giving it an extra bit of inertia.  The polearm ripped the Ettin's left head wide open, brain and bone fragments exploded everywhere.    The late ettin's body started a series of seizures that continued even after it fell to the ground.  The remaining giant paused and saw that he was alone.   SangDrax finally arrived; he was carrying the unconscious Galen with him.  He stared at the remaining ettin and  unceremoniously dropped Galen to the ground.   Sativa grinned and spoke "For those who are about to die, We salute you".   Like a haunting chorus, five voices cried out "Corp Por" one voice, Taj's, weakly called out "Vas Flam".  The bolts of energy and fire ripped through the giant, most of it's flesh ceased to exist.  He dropped like sack of flour.  The enemy was annihilated, the stranger was brought back to consciousness, and he told the Knights his tale.  The Wild Knights began to trace the path towards Galen's fallen friends.  Two dark shadows flew overhead and landed in front of the party.  Two hideous Drakes with wingspans that blocked the sun approached the Wild Knights.  The Knights spoke out in unison "SWEET"
