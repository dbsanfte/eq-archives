The Summonings

Smiling softly, Jaeya read the missive in her hand. It seemed her halfling life companion was anxious to meet with her in the Eastern Plains of Karana as he had a surprise planned for her. Shaking her head at his impatience (he wanted her to get there RIGHT now), she turned to the bard awaiting her response.

"Tell Goonier that I will come to him just before the sun falls to the earth in slumber"

The bard nodded and bowed. Jaeya, remembering the times of hardship as a young mage, reached into the pouch at her side and withdrew five gold coins. Eyes widening with pleasure and disbelief, the bard gratefully took the money from her outstretched palm.

Jaeya turned to her group members and told them of her impending departure. They smiled and wished her well, as if they knew something she did not. Wondering for a moment, the world seeming to conspire against her, she brushed aside her speculations and made sure her bags were packed.

As she was about to depart the room, a druid in her group called out, asking her if she had forgotten the goblins standing out in the corridor. Blushing at her lapse in memory (very dangerous in the Runny Eye Citadel), Jaeya sat down and withdrew her spell book to memorize the words to an invisibility spell.

Realizing that her earthen pet could not follow her into the mists, Jaeya hugged her faithful pet goodbye then stood. Chanting softly, Jaeya raised her hands above her head and was enveloped by a veil of fog. The earth pet, saddened that he could not follow his mistress, slashed himself until he lay dead, a heap of drying mud.

Walking swiftly, even more than her normally long-legged stride due in part to a spell cast on her by the druid, Jaeya reached the top level of Runny Eye and made her way to the entrance.

Once outside and facing the Gorge, Jaeya took a deep breath. Finally she was free of the incredible stench emanating throughout the halls of Runny Eye. Looking in both directions, shuddering slightly at the screams of dying creatures rebounding through the canyon, Jaeya set off towards Eastern Karana.

By: Jaeya Pyrostryker
