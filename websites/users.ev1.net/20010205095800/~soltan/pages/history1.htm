The Wild Knights History...Part 1 

Chronicles of the keeper of knowledge, the entity known as Thoth

In the multiverse, one ever vigilant and watchful deity recorded all that had transpired. Thoth as the patron of knowledge knew of everything and anything that occurred across the span of hundreds of planes. The concordant plane served as his home, which he manifested as a great library that expanded to infinity. As a greater power among the gods, he produced hundreds of avatars that traveled the vast planes of the universe recording all that occurred. Thoth regarded the forces of law and chaos as components of the complex machinery that was the multiverse. All creatures ranging from the bellowing fiend’s of the Abyss to the jovial satyr’s in the Happy Hunting Grounds recognized the avatars by the ankh symbol carried on their person. Since the dawn of time a decree was made by Thoth "Whoever slays my servant, vengeance will be taken on him ten fold. Let this mark give fair warning to those who would harm my followers". All creatures’ great and small gave a wide berth to the avatars; one story recounts the tale of an avatar that was killed. An ancient Wyrm crossed paths with an avatar and consumed him; Thoth learned of the event immediately. He quickly stripped the knowledge of sorcery from all Wyrm’s. To this day the race of Wyrm’s cannot wield the arcane powers of magic. Thoth sat upon his study writing the rise and fall of civilizations when a ripple cascaded across the planes. An event of significance was about to manifest. Thoth summoned his envoy; the avatar took the form of an elderly frail man clutching a crimson book. Each of Thoth’s corporal emanations carried the book of ages. The ancient tome contained all the knowledge and events that came to pass since the dawn of time. Thoth sent his emissary to the planes of Arcadia, to a world named Britannia and waited for the event to transpire. The emissary arrived on the world called Britannia, the land was barren and clouds shrouded the sky. The overcast horizon prevented the sun from reaching the ground below. Streaking bolts of lightning illuminated the disfigured land; skeletal remains of animals adorned the landscape. The roaring wind produced a haunting wail like a plea of help from nature. The world around the avatar was dying and he wondered what chain of events brought this calamity to this world. He opened the book of ages and read the history of this planet.

Britannia was a land pulsating with magical energy. The people of this world shunned the use of sorcery. They prospered cultivating the region to serve their simple needs. A golden age was upon them, man intertwined with nature resulting in a bountiful land where all people had their fair share. During the next hundred years Britannia knew only of peace and harmony but it would soon change. Across the ethereal planes came the Lord of night, the God of evil, Set. Set’s dreaded talons streaked across the land and planted the seeds of chaos on a man. The name of this man was Mondane, a bargain was struck and he became Set’s devoted follower. Mondane would conquer this land; in return the enslaved people would worship the dark deity. The vile Mondane was taught the art of sorcery and alchemy. A plague of death and misfortune raced across the continent. Great lords and ladies mysteriously disappeared or simply passed away in their sleep. Chaos and turmoil struck the citizens of Britannia. People of the community began to bicker and fight each other, the bonds of friendship were lost and man turned on man. Cities were looted and set on fire, citizens ran to the farmland for safety. Mondane choose this time to send his messengers and proclaim himself ruler of the world. Learning the source of their misfortune and strife, the remaining nobles gathered all their armies and rode to battle the evil Mondane. Set learned of this impeding threat and bestowed a gift to his minion, the gem of immortality. The gem gave Mondane complete command of the forces of magic and nature of this land. Mondane wasted no time and made an example of the army marching against him. The earth beneath the gathered warriors began to quake; suddenly the ground erupted with fierce tremors sending man and beast of burden to flight. The earth violently ripped itself in half, swallowing hundreds of warriors. Screams of men plunging to the belly of planet filled the air. A wave of missiles from the sky began to fall on them. Bolts of lightning and meteors landed on the remaining soldiers. Every man who marched against Mondane was struck down. Flying vermin circled the battlefield; hundreds of bodies began to rot under the burning sun. A feast began as the carrion crawlers of nature consumed the bounty provided by Mondane. News of defeat spread like wildfires across the land, the people of Britannia were beaten and they submitted to the rule of Mondane. The freedom, hope and dreams of the inhabitants of this world were stripped from them. The God of evil’s grand scheme was unfolding before him. The assertion of a whole planet of worshippers would exponentially expand his sphere of power. No force in Britannia could stop the reign of Mondane. The people stripped the land of its resources, building shrines and temples for Set and showering Mondane with gifts of gold and jewelry. Anguished cries of the innocent reached across the planes to the cliffs of mount Olympus.
