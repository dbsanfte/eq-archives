The Wedding

Staring at her hand, that now bore traces of blood from her nails clenched into her palm, Jaeya relaxed her fingers and calmed herself. Crossing the border into the Eastern Plains of Karanas, just as the sun began its descent into slumber, Jaeya eagerly scanned the area for signs of her love.

Jaeya looked down at the slight cough she heard and into the eyes of her life companion. Joy flooded her heart as she bent down and enfolded him in a hug. Words of love and apologies for her tardiness tumbled from her lips. Laughing, Goonier hugged his love fiercely before disengaging himself from her arms.

Taking her hand, he led her to a tree near the end of the road, where he motioned forward a figure silently standing there. Embarrassed for not seeing the person earlier, Jaeya was pleasantly surprised to see her old friend Sobana the Cleric of Felwithe.

Both women exchanged hugs and hellos before turning back to the halfling druid patiently watching them.

"My sweet, since our first meeting so long ago in the city of Rivervale, you have owned my heart. I knew despair at your love for the monk and joyous pain at your separation. We have come so far together, shared many adventures, and nurtured a love within our hearts. I stand here today to ask you to wed with me in the beauty of Tunare."

Jaeya, finally diving the reason for Sobana's presence, cried tears of joy at his words of love. As she placed her hand in his, turning towards the cleric, visions of her first love slowly faded, replaced instead with a green-eyed brown-haired halfling who heart was larger than himself.

Gladly speaking the words of the ancient ritual that would bind her to Goonier forever, Jaeya smiled softly as the wind stirred her hair.
