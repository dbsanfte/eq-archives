The Betrayal

Please Identify yourself ... Jinx
Please state your business ... death
Please state your alignment ... evil

Jinx shook her head as she whispered the last words, glancing over her shoulder nervously. She felt conspicuous standing beneath the large gold sign "Aqueducts of Qeynos". Anyone with half a brain would realize that she was up to no good. And considering her recent escapades, "good" was not a term used to describe her anymore.

"Evil", such a small four-letter word, but one capable of changing lives. She couldn't believe that she, once a law-abiding citizen had now turned against all that her goddess upheld and was no better than those who followed Innoruuk. True she still worshipped and revered Tunare but her actions certainly had not endeared her to her goddess. Or to the residents of Qeynos or the plains of Karana. She grimaced slightly as she took note of her surroundings.

The cold bare stone beneath her feet was dirty and spotted with dark stains she feared were blood. Despite her recent murderous tendencies, Jinx was squeamish to the thought of the activities her new cohorts and partners in crime were accustomed to. Shuddering but unwilling to give up the experience and money she earned, Jinx slipped into the secret passage way opened before her.

Once inside she followed the young woman before her through a maze of tunnels until they reached a room filled with rough looking characters. Straightening to her full regal height, and smoothing her rumpled green robe, Jinx coolly nodded to the people standing before her.

Jinx stiffened imperceptibly when one of the bandits stepped forward. He assessed her beneath hooded lids and barked a laugh.

"This is our new recruit? A high elf mage? One so fair and lovely has joined our ranks? Ha!"

The others surrounding her laughed, but she sensed not with humor. Wondering what she had gotten herself into but not willing to back out, she addressed the man standing before her with all the haughtiness bred into her from centuries of royal blood.

"I have come to embrace your ways, sirrah, and what's more I have convinced another to join with me. He awaits me in the glade. Are there any orders that you wish to be passed on?"

The bandit smirked before bowing mockingly.

"If it pleases milady I want the swords of Chanda and Baobob Miller, but you may keep the money looted from them. Secondly, recently the guards have made trouble for my people. I want you to kill Guards Phil and Bixby that stand at the entrance to Surefall Glades."

Staring into the malevolent eyes of a killer no longer frightened Jinx. She saw the similar look in her own eyes and all those who hunted with her so she was not intimidated by her boss. Nodding her consent she asked if she might leave now. Eyeing her once more the bandit motioned her off. Jinx muttered a few words beneath her breath and soon was enveloped with silvery blue light. Within seconds she disappeared.

The bandit's hatred, carefully hidden from his newest employee, was apparent upon his rough features now that she had left. Turning to the man standing next to him he uttered one command.

"Kill her."

 Jinx relished the rush of adrenaline that coursed through her veins. Calling upon the fire gifted to her at birth, she listened to her victim scream as white-hot flames licked at his clothing and boiled his flesh.

Smiling softly, her beauty at odds with the carnage her actions produced, she brought forth more fire and finally the screams were silenced. The smell of burning flesh drifted to her on the breeze, but well accustomed to it now, Jinx ignored it. The heady sound of coins clinking captured her attention and she eagerly looted through the dead man's pockets.

Standing up, Jinx grinned in victory at her hunting partners and held several coins in her hand. They laughed and the loot was divided amongst them, the larger portion going to her since she had made the kill.

Standing to the side she noticed a young bard. For the last few days Jinx had become accustomed to spectators. She cynically mused that he was one among the latest looking for loot she and her group might've overlooked. Or perhaps he was looking for the shaman for a few protection spells. But to her complete surprise he called to her by her name. Her real name.

Her name, the ones her parents had named her, hadn't been spoken since that awful day she had shed the beliefs of her childhood and accepted the path of evil. A piercing regret filled her heart for an instant then she dismissed it.

Motioning the bard closer, she asked him to state his business. In a shaky fear-filled voice the young bard introduced himself as Karllyx and told her he had been sent to find her on guild business.

Starting violently, Jinx remembered that a meeting had been called by the elders, set in the Aviak Arena of Lake Rathe. Frustration filled her, as she didn't want to leave her current location. But knowing if she didn't questions would be asked, and not wanting anyone to know of her recent activities, she regretfully told her companions goodbye.

Motioning to the bard she thanked him for delivering his message. Gifting him with coin for his troubles she swore him to secrecy of what he had seen. But as he walked off Jinx become troubled. Would he really keep this news to himself? Knowing she couldn't afford to find out, Jinx waved forward the evil druid in her hunting party.

He came to her side and she whispered her dilemma into his ear. Eyes glinting, he promised her he would take care of it. Trusting that he would, Jinx thanked him before leaving the area.

As luck would have it her husband, recently from the dungeon Cazic-Thule, came to her rescue and offered a portal that would lessen her travel time. Hugging him, Jinx again felt another twinge of guilt at her latest adventures but brushed it aside. They would go to the commonlands to pick up another guild member then head to the arena. She agreed.

 As she started across the grassy plains of North Karana towards the bridge into South Karana anxiety began building. Her husband and guild had no idea of her activities therefore her urgent need to be invisible seemed pointless. But as she drew closer she could hear the grunts and curses of the guards posted at the entrance to the bridge as they fought off some creature attacking a traveler.

Fear and panic began coalescing and spread throughout her body. Stopping completely Jinx muttered a quick spell and soon was cloaked in a veil of fog. This fog would shield her from the guards' keen eyes and allow her to pass over unmolested.

Sighing with relief, Jinx began walking again but soon realized she had lost her companions. Shrugging slightly, she continued on and approached the bridge confidently.

Within moments she realized that the gods were never fooled for long and that the retribution she had always half-expected for killing innocent men was now at hand.

As Jinx silently walked to the entrance she heard a shout behind her. Ignoring it in favor of getting over the bridge she soon realized her mistake.

Pain came to her in crushing blows as razor sharp bladed edges cut deeply into her side and armored fists beat her to her knees. Knowing that life was flowing from her as fast as the blood spilling to the ground, Jinx attempted to gate herself to another location. But the gods had deemed this to be Judgment Day so her attempts failed.

Just as death came to her on the wings of agony, one of her killers leaned down to whisper last words into her ears.

Understanding and betrayal flashed across her face before the blankness of forever closed her eyes.
