The Wild Knights History...Part 2

From the silver city of Olympus came a champion of light. Lady Athena daughter of Zeus came to the world of Britannia and sought to free the people from Mondane’s dark reign of terror. The goddess traveled to the planes of Elysium, home and resting place of the faithful worshippers of her pantheon. A call reached out to all the great warriors, before Lady Athena stood hundreds of souls ready to again champion the weak and protect the innocent. Pride filled the goddess as she gazed at the vast host of souls before her. Some of the greatest heroes in recorded time were assembled before her, mighty Achilles, clever Odysseus, Ajax son of Telamon, Perseus, Diomed son of Tydeus, and Jason of the argonauts. Athena focused and listened to the hearts of the assembled shades of fallen heroes. One spirit’s immense desire for this quest caught her attention. She addressed the assembly of heroes and spoke " Step forward Diomed son of Tydeus and share your desire to champion my cause". Diomed advanced but turned his gaze from his goddess. Her radiant essence left her humble servant in awe, thus Diomed keep his eyes to the ground and spoke. "Great goddess I fought and died far away from my home, family and friends. I fell in a war that was forged by vain and arrogant men. No great happiness or achievements occurred in my life. I seek to redeem and restore my honor by becoming a champion of the innocent". Lady Athena knew this was her champion and spoke "Diomed, son of Tydeus, you will become a creature of flesh and blood, subject to pain and suffering. The knowledge of this life will be stripped from you; thus all memories will be forever lost from you. This world is devoid of shrines or temples erected in my name consequently I cannot aid you in any matter. With this enlightenment will you champion this land?" The answer came in the form of a simple nod from Diomed. A smile crossed the goddess face "Step forward Lord British and prepare thyself to protect the lands of Britannia"

Athena transported the shade of Lord British to Britannia. She provided her champion with clothing, food, weapon and armor. Lord British retained the knowledge of war and tactics from his previous incarnation but had no memory of his past. He felt the presence of evil emit from the land. Across the murky horizon a storm was raging, a swirl of clouds circled a dark tower. Lord British felt drawn to that tower; he proceeded towards the spire. An echo from the past resided in Lord British thoughts, he knew the source of the chaos around him was inside the tower. Thoth’s avatar closed the tome of ages, taking a transparent hue of light he transported himself to the vicinity of Lord British. The task to observe and chronicle history was at hand. A great event was about to unfold and he would witness it. The entrance to the tower was unguarded; no doubt the owner felt it was unwarranted. Up the steps Lord British climbed, the overwhelming sense of power grew as he reached the apex of the structure. He opened the door and found a wretched robed man clutching a giant gem, inside the gem a world was slowly dying. The mage turned to Lord British and spoke to him in a hissing tone "You dare to disturb me foolish cretin, a most excruciating death will await you. It seems the people of this land must be taught a lesson again. Rivers of blood will flow because of your impertinence". Mondane's hand cradled a flaming ball of fire that he threw across the room. Lord British dove to the side avoiding the fireball, the clothing he wore was singed. Desperate to close quarters with the mage, Lord British leap toward Mondane’s position. Back on the offensive Mondane cast another spell, a shimmering hand appeared. The hand grasped Lord British and began to crush him. A wicked smile cracked Mondane's face; He summoned an energy bolt and sent it flying to his captive. The bolt ripped into Lord British; blood began to flow freely from his mouth. A gleeful Mondane spoke "I should finish you now, I do look forward to bathing in the blood of newborns." Lord British fought to remain conscious, despite his best efforts the magical restraints holding him would not budge. A vortex of energy now surrounded Mondane, his next incantation would rip Lord British to pieces. A prismatic spray streaked towards the helpless Lord British. A second before reaching their target, the shimmering hand holding Lord British vanished. Dropping to the ground, Lord British rolled and watched the ray blast devastate one side of the tower. Mondane was frozen from the sight he just witnessed. The gem of immortality had magnified his power so exponentially that his spells had never failed or expired. A stunned Mondane was unaware of his peril, Lord British struck. Sinking his blade between the mage’s shoulder, a cry of agony ripped from Mondane. Mondane was mortally wounded and sounded a desperate cry for help for his dark deity "Master of damnation, your servant calls for your divine intervention in his time of need". Before Lord British could act a glow surrounded Mondane’s body. The wound he received was sealed and restored. A source of searing agony cut through Lord British’s body, the overwhelming pain dropped him to the ground. Mondane slowly approached his adversary, picking up the sword Lord British dropped. Watching his opponent writhe on the ground, Mondane addressed him again. "You have brought great agony to my person. I usually disdain from physical exertion but in your case I’ll make an exception." Raising the newly acquired sword Mondane quickly brought it down on his opponent’s head. The killing stroke never arrived, for the second time today Mondane was at a loss. His hand was held frozen, before he could act he was thrown to the other side of the room. Lord British’s pain had passed and he quickly retrieved his sword. A gentle voice sounded in Lord British’s mind "The gem, the gem is the key". Focusing on the dark crystal, Lord British threw his blade at the gem of immortality. The sword and crystal impacted, sending dozens of shards across the room. The link between Mondane and the gem was severed; as a result Mondane’s life force ceased to exist. He collapsed among the gem shards. Lord British stood puzzled wondering how or what came to his aid. Nevertheless it was time to rebuild this world…The avatar witnessed the event and recorded it. Closing the tome of ages, he returned to the concordant plane. Thoth was pleased with his servant and dismissed him. Sending the avatar to chronicle more stories in other planes. Thoth began preparations for the coming event; it’s been a millennium since a god had been put to trial. He was looking forward to witnessing that affair himself.
