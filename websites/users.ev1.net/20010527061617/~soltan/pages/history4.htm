The Wild Knights History...Part 4

Kraanan, demigod of Meridian greeted his visitor "Hail Lady Athena, we are pleased to have you as our guest." Lady Athena joined Kraanan and his wife the goddess Shal’ille. A banquet was held in honor of Athena’s visit. The demigods of Meridian had gathered to toast the occasion. At the head of the table sat Kraanan and to his side sat fair Shal’ille his wife. Athena sat opposite of Kraanan and to her sides sat the goddess Jala sister of Shal’ille and Faren the patron god of war in Meridian. An empty chair marked the absence of Qor the demipower of discord and death. Riija demigod of scholars had urgent matters to attend and forwarded his regret for missing the gathering. As the festivities drew to an end, Lady Athena decided to plead her case to Kraanan. "Great Lord Kraanan, I have learned that your land has many great warriors that defeated the forces of chaos and hold the upper hand the in battle for law and order. A distant world and people cry out in pain and suffering. The lands of Britannia are on the verge of collapse, the evil Set will soon consume this land and leave its empty carcass for the vermin of evil to fester and violate. My family has been denied the power to aid this world. In this time of great need perhaps you could send some of your champions to aid the world of Britannia?" The jovial atmosphere of the party had passed; a quiet Kraanan sat pondering what action to take. A gentle smile and a firm nod of his wife Shal’ille cleared all doubts from his mind. "Lady Athena, we shall aid this land, the greatest of heroes will begin a journey that will take them from the lands of Meridian to Britannia." An exodus of heroes began. Many joined the forces of law, but some were lured by Set’s promises of riches and power. The battle for Britannia began. Heroes and villains alike forged great guilds. The forces of law and chaos fought throughout the land, but it seemed that Set’s minions had gained the upper hand. The chaos guilds mounted victory upon victory, while the forces of law barely held their own.

Athena grew distressed as she learned of Set’s minions repeatedly beating the heroes of Britannia. She needed to contact the three who are one, The fates. They could reveal to her how to best aid the people of Britannia. She traveled to Gladsheim and met the three sisters, Urd, Verdandi, and Skuld. The three fates represented the past, present, and the future. Lady Athena approached them and greeted them. "Hail Ladies, I seek your wisdom and guidance in matters of great importance." The sisters turned and spoke "Behold, the daughter of mighty Zeus. We are honored by your presence. We know you seek to overthrow the wicked grip of Set in the world of Britannia. What do you offer in exchange for this information?" Athena stood rigid and addressed them "I am a sovereign power in the realm of deities. I am entitled to three answers from you noble fates. I would also be grateful for your guidance and be in your debt." The Three spoke as one "We acknowledge your request and will provide you three answers young goddess of Olympus." Athena proceed with her questions "Urd, which guild will play the greatest role in defeating the forces of Set?" The young fate turned and spoke "The Wild Knights." Athena turned to the fate Verdandi and spoke "but who are the Wild Knights?" Verdandi began to recite her answer "Many of the Wild Knights ranks originate from the planes of Meridian, but a few are offspring’s of the noble families that were driven underground by Set’s inquisition. The remainders are normal men who have chosen to fight against the abomination of Set. A bond exist among them that is unparalleled a brotherhood of comrades and friends who stand ready to protect the people of Britannia " Athena accessed her information and gave her final question "Skuld, what is the greatest threat to the Wild Knights?" The oldest of the fates slowly turned and spoke "One among the ranks of the Wild Knights will turn and betray them. Set’s evil talons have planted the seeds of jealousy and hatred upon him. He plots against them and his only desire is to utterly destroy and wipe out the Wild Knights." A startled Athena cried out "Who will betray the Wild Knights?" A soft cackle broke from the sisters. "We’re out of answers for you dear, one question; one answer. "Athena sent news of her newfound knowledge to her allies. The Wild Knights were the keys for victory in Britannia. Only a short span of time passed before Thoth began to chronicle the victories the Wild Knights earned against the vile scum of the land. Many brave deeds and countless acts of heroism were documented. The people of the land began to hear whispers concerning a band of heroes destined to turn the tide against the forces of darkness. The people of Britannia finally began to hope and dream to someday wake up from this nightmare and wake up to a dawn of peace and happiness. Only one person remained deeply troubled, one question remained in Lady Athena’s mind "who would betray them"…
