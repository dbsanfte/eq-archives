The Paladin

The symbol of all that is holy in Norrath, Paladins are warriors first and Clerics second. At high levels of dedication they become able to wield some of the Cleric's power and soon become a force to be reckoned with. In addition, their unique ability to "Lay Hands" on the injured have saved many a life in our dangerous lands.

Spellcasting:          Level 9
Primary Attr's:      STR, STA, WIS, CHA
Allowed Races:    Dwarf, Erudite, Half Elf, High Elf, Human
Unique Abilities:    Lay Hands

1 1H Blunt Ability to use a blunt object that only requires one hand to wield.
1 1H Slash Ability to hit others with one-handed blades, such as small to medium swords and axes.
1 2H Blunt Ability to use two-handed blunt weapons, such as Warhammers and Great Staves.
1 2H Slash Ability to use two-handed slashing weapons, including the larger swords and axes.
1 Archery The ability to use the bow. This includes the Hunting Bow, the Short Bow, and the Long Bow. Crossbows are currently not available as weapons.
1 Taunt Allows the user a chance to enrage the target to such a degree that it considers the user enemy number one and will try to kill them before killing everyone else. This is a good way to save a comrade's life when he is down to a sliver of health.
6 Bash Meant to be used with a shield, this skill allows one a chance to temporarily stun an opponent as well as inflict a small amount of damage.
9 Abjuration A skill only available to users of magic. The ability to cast spells of a defensive nature
9 Alteration A spellcasting skill. This is the ability to alter reality and make changes to already existing targets. Good examples are the Teleportation, Gate, and other types of 'ability' spells (run faster, see better, etc).
9 Channeling A magic user with ability in this skill will be able to cast a spell faster no matter the spell's type.
9 Conjuration A spellcasting skill. Allows the user to call upon the arcane forces to summon something into being. For example, a magical pet, food, water, weapons, etc.
9 Divination The spellcasting skill of making things visible or invisible.
9 Evocation A spellcasting skill. This is the skill that allows its user to call forth tremendous forces of energy in order to cast purely offensive spells such as Lightening Bolt, Shock of Frost, etc.
10 Bind Wound Allows the user a chance to begin to heal wounds. May be used an unlimited number of times, but immobilizes the user for a short period. May only be used on a target who has below half of their total HPs.
10 Dodge Simply put, a chance to get out of the way of your opponent's weapon. A very useful skill for all classes, but extremely useful for magic users who do not like being interrupted mid-spell.
12 Meditate Magic users should get accustomed to using this skill. Allows faster regeneration of mana. Don't waste training points on it at the trainer, as you will use it so much that the skill will go up on its own.
17 Parry Ability to deflect blows with one's weapon. Makes a cool sound in-game.
20 Double Attack Allows the user a chance to double their weapon's attack speed, thereby attacking twice in the time that it would have taken them to attack once.
30 Riposte Special attack.
40 Disarm A very useful skill that actually causes the target to drop its weapon. This is often the death knell for your opponent, especially if you pick up the weapon before he or she does.
