Soulfire Walkthrough

Step 1:
You don't need the Zimels Blades scroll to complete the Quest.  Go to the Seafarers Roost in East Freeport by the docks and an NPC named Tykar Renlin will eventually spawn to the left of the bar.

Hail him repeatedly to keep him from disappearing.  You will need to give him 4 Drom's Champagne's.  These are Lore Items and must be purchased one at a time from the barkeep.  After you give him the 4th Champagne, he will give you a blanket.

Step 2:
Go to the prison located behind the arena in West freeport and give the Blanket, Edible Goo, and Bog Juice at the same time to the prisoner (Zimel) and he will give you a key.

Step 3:
Go to High Pass Keep and find Bank Clerk Jaylin in the bank and give her the key.

She will give you a rolled up note.

Step 4:
Go to Paw and find Brother Hayle and give him the note.  He will tell you to prove yourself by getting 3 items yourself.  He demands the Hilt of an unholy sword, a Brilliant Sword of Faith and your Testimony of Truth.
