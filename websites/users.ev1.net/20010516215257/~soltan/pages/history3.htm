The Wild Knights History...Part 3

The plane of Nirvana, home of the entity known as Destiny, the cosmic judge of all matters. A balance had to be kept between law and chaos. Destiny enforced and judged the symmetry between all the different deities of the multiverse. The arrival of Thoth heralded the beginning of the trial. Thoth would witness and chronicle the trial of a god. Destiny called out the accuser, "Set, Lord of the night, stand forth and present thy case." A solemn Set kneeled before Destiny and spoke. "A great crime has been committed, a greater power of law interfered in a realm that I controlled. No other religions existed on this world, thus my claim to this world was complete". Destiny acknowledged Set’s accusation and called out again. "Athena daughter of Olympus grace us with your presence". Lady Athena appeared beside Set. Destiny addressed the defendant. "Child of Zeus, do you admit to interfering in a realm that Set controlled?" Athena paused gauging her words and spoke. "Lord Destiny, a grave injustice was taking place in the realm of Britannia, the world was dying and the people were suffering. How could I just stand idle and watch them die?" Destiny quickly replied. "I acknowledge the balance of nature, it was your responsibility to stand against Lord Set’s goals. But in this world no shrines or temples existed in your name, therefore you could not aid them. We find you guilty of the crime and we will decree our judgment. "Lady Athena and all members of her pantheon are barred from interfering in the realm of Britannia and all it’s additional shards. Should another incident occur, your pantheon would be banished to the realm of Olympus, denying you access to your followers for the span of a hundred years?" A shocked and fuming Lady Athena streaked home to Olympus. The malicious Set started to howl in delight at this turn of events. The world of Britannia was his to conquer and nothing on this multiverse could stop him.

Athena entered the hallowed halls of Olympus. News of her punishment had reached the ears of her brothers and sisters. A solemn mood was apparent throughout the kingdom. Her brother Apollo gave her a hug and spoke in a low tone. "I too fear for the sake of the mortals in Britannia, but we are bound by Destiny’s judgment." Brother and sister quietly walked towards Athena’s home, fair Apollo gave his sister a farewell hug and parted her company. Athena never felt so defeated; her actions only delayed the inevitable. Her champion would stand his ground but the forces of Set would eventually crush him. Her train of thought was disturbed by the sudden appearance of Hermes the messenger god of Olympus. The anxious Hermes spoke in a blur " Lady Athena, Mondane’s followers are forging a new clan called the followers of Armageddon. This vile scum seeks to bring a second age of darkness upon Britannia. Lord British has rallied the people to rise, but they are farmers and shepherds. They will be crushed when Set’s minions strike " Athena bolted from her chamber and raced to her father’s home. She had to act but needed her father’s guidance. Upon reaching the sky father’s hall she was greeted by the step mother Hear " Child, you seek to bring down our great family. Your concerns over the land of Britannia are overzealous. Destiny’s judgment is final and we cannot act to aid this planet you wish to save. Your father has departed our home, probably seducing some mortal females in another plane. Go home and forget this insignificant world". The dejected goddess walked slowly back to her home, her failure to protect the innocent and weak brought great pain to her. A resounding crashed broke the peace; Athena rushed to the source of the commotion. She entered the hall of her little brother and saw two deities locked in combat. Hercules was wrestling the Aesir god of thunder Thor. The two brutes were exchanging blows and punches. The empty barrels indicated that they were intoxicated. A roaring Thor screamed "time to show you little demigod what a greater god is made of!" Thor’s fist impacted with Hercules jaw, sending the god through a wall. Hercules rose from the rubble "What was that, a love tap?" Hercules charged Thor tackling him and sending both brutes through another wall. The two combatants rose from the wreckage and broke into laughter. Athena saw an opportunity and waited for the drunken warriors to settle down before speaking with them. Hercules saw Athena and addressed her "Hail big sister what bring you here". Thor staggered to his feet and also spoke "Hail great lady, you honor us with your presence." Athena took this opportunity to speak with them "Hail brother Hercules, might I have a word with our guest of the Aesir pantheon?" Hercules nodded and returned to the few remaining barrels of ale. "Thor, son of Odin, I seek your aid in a grave matter." Thor noted the serious tone of her voice and sobered up a little "But of course Lady Athena." Athena recounted the recent events that transpired in Britannia and pleaded to Thor "Could your family of Deities aid this planet in our behalf?" Thor spoke with a heavy heart. "Alas Lady Athena, we are in constant battle with our enemies in the plane of Gladsheim. The frost and fire giants of our realm repeatedly assault our homes and I must stand ever vigilant against them. We cannot aid these mortals and the heroes in Valhalla must ever be prepared for Ragnarok, thus we cannot send them to aid your champion." Hercules joined the two saddened deities and spoke "Thor, what about those new gods of Meridian?" A puzzled Athena looked at her brother "Meridian?" Thor gave a smile "Great idea Hercules, a new pantheon of gods exits in the planes of Twin Paradises. All the gods are demipowers, but the heroes of the land are extraordinary. Contact them Lady Athena, you will find help in Meridian "
