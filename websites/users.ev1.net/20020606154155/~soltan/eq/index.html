Wild Knights do it again!

We had a fairly strong force show up for the raid into CoM Friday night.  Slightly weak on the melee side, but strong overall.  After getting to the castle, we were able to single pull the Reavers, and after drop kicking 5 or 6, the elusive Jade Reaver dropped! Congrats to Galwen for finally getting this rare epic piece.  A special thanks goes out to our friends from KoS, and the few others that showed (Saedian, Levlin, Serenyu [sp?], and any others I failed to mention).  Galwen is now only one step away from getting his epic and having the WK step up with other guilds and be classified as an Epic Guild (sort of). I ask that you mark it on your calendar and make plans to attend the coming hunt to take down Faydedar.  We will be receiving help from NB, Tindi, and KoS that I know of, so our odds are looking better.
