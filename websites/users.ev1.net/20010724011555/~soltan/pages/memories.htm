Memories

Stepping carefully around huge boulders in her path, Jaeya watched as a stream of various people raced past her in either attempt to avoid certain death or running to it. Sighing silently, always aware that eyes might see her, Jaeya wondered if she was ever that young. Smiling as visions of past adventures flashed through her mind, Jaeya ruefully admitted it was so. The mist of time seemed to part and suddenly she remembered her youth.

Life for her began in the beautiful high elven city of Felwithe. Daughter to a Sol, the immediate family of the Royal Blood, her life had been filled with leisure and luxury. Her father, a man of conviction as evidenced by his Clerical aspirations, had spoiled her shamelessly, probably to make up for the fact she was without a mother.

Had Tunare, the Mother of All, not stepped in the breach, there was every indication that Jaeya would've ended up a beautiful but useless ornament to dangle at the end of a Royal's arm.

In those dark days, the ones bards still hesitate to remember, the evil band known throughout Faydwar as Crushbone Orcs had begun an assault upon the elves. Though they had been enemies since before the first written word, never had orcs ventured much past their own gloomy home surrounding an ancient castle. Now they were even daring to come to the lifts that led to Kelethin the treetop city of the Wood Elves.

Angered by the loss of people to this evil, the Royal Blood called a Meeting of Councils. Never in the history of elves had the High Elves ever consulted with their Lesser cousins of the forest. They planned a direct assault upon the castle itself, recruiting anyone who could heal or wield a sword. Much to their surprise and dismay, the battles they waged were not easily won. In fact often they lost more than they won.

Soon it became apparent that direct attacks were not going to work, so those with any knowledge of the ancient crafts were employed.

Jaeya, heavily cosseted though she was, could not help but hear some of the gory details of battles as the men came streaming in through the gates. Since she had been born into a time of war, Jaeya, like most of the High Elven children, had never seen the forest that surrounded their home.

Curiosity burning a hole in her heart, Jaeya snuck past the guards and ventured into the forest. The canopy of the forest was so thick and heavy there was little distinction between night and day. But Jaeya found beauty in the foliage and began wandering. Too late she realized she was lost.

Frightened as she had never been before, she began walking around some more until a sudden rustle in the bushes froze her. Turning around, her dress whirling with her movement, Jaeya looked into the eyes of death.

Heavily panting, obviously wounded, the blue skinned creature facing her glared at her from stormy red eyes. For a moment both were too surprised to move but a cry from a passing bird shattered the moment and they were released from their paralysis.

Terror lending her speed, Jaeya picked up her skirts, knowing with every beat of her heart that she must reach help soon or she would die. But she had no knowledge of the outdoors and soon became even more lost turning in circles. The creature behind her, sensing her distress, pushed itself harder then threw its body atop of her. Staggering with its sudden weight Jaeya nearly went down but, even though she was a maiden she was still taller, righted herself. Despite being nicked and cut in several places, bleeding heavily, the creature rammed its fists into the tender and unprotected flesh of Jaeya until she began to feel lightheaded.

Moaning in distress and pain, Jaeya soon fell beneath the orc to the ground. As she looked into the top of the trees, she whispered a prayer to her creator, closing her eyes in preparation of death.

Suddenly the world around her exploded into a flaming ball of fire that passed over her but touched the creature on her. Within a second or two extricating pain made Jaeya aware she was alive. For wasn't it said that within the embrace of Tunare you felt no pain only happiness? Pain was only felt when one still walked the face of the Mother.

Willing herself to open her eyes but afraid of what she might find, Jaeya was shocked when her wounds mended themselves and it was as if she had never been hurt. Puzzled, Jaeya opened her eyes to see a black robed person bent over the still steaming corpse laying at her side. Standing behind was a grizzled old man holding a mace and shield.

"Eh lassie what ye be out here by yer lonesome?" a gruff voice asked. Trembling, Jaeya slowly sat up from her prone position and pushed back her hair, the net to hold it back lost somewhere in the depths of the forest.

A hand reached out from beneath the folds of the robe and extended to her. Dazed Jaeya reached out and was quickly brought to her feet. The hood fell back and Jaeya gasped. The rich loam brown skin marked the man as an Erudite as did the symbols etched upon his brow.

Bowing, he spoke "Milady it is not safe for children this far out. I, Sorcerer M'Ewen of Erude and my companion Kathlor the Cleric of Kaladim will escort you to your city."

Nodding slightly, Jaeya remembered her manners drummed into her since birth. Curtsying low, sweeping her bloodied skirts out, she introduced herself. "I am Sola Jaeya, Blood of the First House. I thank you for your timely assistance and will gladly take your escort."

It was in that moment, staring into the eyes of the mystic, that she realized that Tunare indeed did work in mysterious ways. For the path of the future was opened before her and Jaeya knew she would become a sorceress.
