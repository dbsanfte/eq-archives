  Anarchy Online Grid Requirements
Page last updated: 09/18/2001


The Grid is a way to rapidly travel across Rubi-Ka. Using various terminals located in most cities and outposts, you can instantly travel to other areas of the world without slow modes of transportation such as using a whompa or running. It is like the internet of today, except that you can use various nodes inside to actually be teleported to the location of your choice. Inside of the Grid, you have an avatar that represents your character, so that you can navigate to the various nodes. Use of the grid requires a high computer literacy skill, with some terminals and nodes requiring higher skill than others.
