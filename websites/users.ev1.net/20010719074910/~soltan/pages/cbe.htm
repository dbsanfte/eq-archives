Tales from the Crazy Blue Elf

Inside a tavern in Vesper a poor orphan boy fiddles with an empty bowl. The boy slowly walks up to the fat bartender, looks up with pleading eyes and asks,
"Please sir, may I have some more?"
The bartender turns around with a scowl on his face.... "More?!?"
The bartender angrily throws the glass mug he was cleaning at the boy, missing him by inches. The boy runs off quickly, sobbing loudly. The bartender grunts as his assistant cleans up the mess caused by the incident. Into the bar enters a powerful, but odd looking warrior wearing a jester's suit over his armor.
The warrior, covered in a magical archer's suit, looks straight at the bartender. The bartender gestures for the warrior to sit. Instead the warrior quickly walks up to the bartender and pulls him with one arm over the counter easily. The few patrons in attendance put down their drinks to stare at this angry warrior. The warrior lifts the chubby bartender a foot off the ground and in a low, yet squeaky voice speaks...
"Give the kid a bowl of soup."

The bartender, still in shock looks at the boy standing at the door with a tear in his eye. The warrior repeats in a louder voice, "Give the kid a bowl of soup!"
"Y-Y-Yes sir!" the bartender says, gesturing for his assistant. "W-Who are you...?"
The warrior, still holding the large man of the ground removes his helmet. The bartenders face goes pale and his eyes wide.
"I'm Elfman..." the blue elven warrior replies, "NO, you DOPE! I'm Gamma the Crazy Blue Elf, MoooOOOoooo!"
The bartender stares blankly for a few seconds then yells, "GUARDS! Arrest this man er... elf er.. whatever"
Four guards run into the tavern, pushing the orphan out of the way carelessly. The Sargent orders Gamma to drop the man and give up. Gamma merely smiles. Faster than a flash of lightning, Gamma throws the bowling ball of a man at the guards and unsheathes the katana from his pack. The blue elf, still smiling, raises his index finger on his free hand and wiggles it tauntingly. The first guard charges, Gamma ducks the halberd swing and guts the poor fool like a cow. The other two underling guards rush in, only to be met with a powerful left hook punch. A few patrons cheered the elf as he jumped back and loses his smiling appearance.
"You naughty boys! You first push a poor boy, then try to kill me. Go run now and I won't need to make you die laughing."
The captain raises his hand, and the two living lackeys return behind him. "I shall defeat thee, foolish naive! And as for the boy... well..." He turns to the child still setting on the floor. "No soup for you!"
Gamma, now grinning almost evilly, points at the leader of the shamed royal guards. "Too bad you aren't in their world now." The guards merely stare at Gamma. Gamma quips "You guys look like Monty Python rejects with your pathetic garments." This angered the guards.

The Sargent yells out, "Die you incomprehensible bastard!" But it was too late; Gamma had already shot an arrow at the fool's eye a moment earlier. The leader falls backwards stiffly without uttering another sound.

The bartender screams in a high pitched voice, not making any sense. Gamma pulls his katana out of the first kill and pulls on his helmet only to say in the low voice, "Give the kid some soup.... now."
The remaining guard’s back away confused and walk slowly backwards out of the tavern while the bartender personally pours warm, delicious soup into the orphan’s bowl. The elven warrior, sheathing his blade orders the bartender, "Now you idiot, give ME a bowl of soup before I turn you into another blue cow..."
The bartender slowly inquires, "Another?"
Gamma sidesteps to the left and lo and behold a bright blue cow stands where a customer once sat. Gamma explains in a higher sarcastic voice, "This little piggy thought he would hit me from behind during the little training exercise." Eventually, as Gamma and the orphan boy ate their soup, the mood returns to as normal as it can possibly get with a blue cow and two dead bodies on the floor.

Gamma leaves the tavern, proud of his accomplishment and head to Redwind's. Redwind's has been gaining decent supplies recently Gamma thought to himself. And perhaps the elf could purchase arrows needed that somehow made earth elemental crumble and liches bleed. About halfway out of town, a loud voice commands Gamma to surrender. Once again, the two remaining guards had returned with a "few" friends. Gamma roughly counted 15 or 20 fierce guards clad to the maximum. Gamma's smile faded inside the hiding mask. Gamma stood for a moment, then throws of his helmet... the sky blackens to that of Blackrock nearly. Fierce winds begins to blow Gamma's long grey hair to one side.
"Guess what guys," Gamma said in a low voice, "this time it won't be a "You are dead." message you'll hear; but that of a "Game Over" that will mark the final end of your lives."
With all said, Gamma raises one arm to the cursed sky, smiles, and chants. The wind howls and echoes his every syllable with passion and horror.
"Vas Corp Grav Hur Ort Por" (Meaning "Great Death Energy Wind Movement). After the last word is chanted a tremendous bright flash engulfs the squadron. The sky turns back into a blue hue, yet darker than before and the winds dissipate into a gentle breeze. What was once a powerful groups of men now only remains small piles of ash.
"I won.... They could not withstand the power of the Divine Bovine that I possess..." Gamma said quietly while falling to one knee.

Then he heard one man laughing loudly far in the distance. And then another, until a chain of men were heard laughing.
"You FOOL!" one man said approach Gamma... " You just used that awesome spell on a bunch of training dummies you lamer!" "LOL"
Gamma looked up at the man. Gamma wondered to himself, was this really a squadron of Lord British's stupidest? Of course not, most guards just merely try to whack you, then stand there mocking your dead body. No, this was much worse. Gamma's thoughts are interrupted when this man kicks him in the face, sending Gamma to the ground with a bloody lip. No, it was him, the craftiest, yet most cowardly killer in the land, Ho Chi Minh.
"Now to kill you, loot you, and use your head as a bowling ball you dumb ass role-playing wannabe elf loser." Ho Chi muttered as he raised his warfork high into striking position.

Then with a mighty blow... Ho Chi Minh's weapon is struck by a longsword, sending the fork several feet into the air! A mighty, heroic, yet somewhat familiar voice bellows from a horse, "Not today fool one!" It was D'Artagnan in a green robe and full plate armor.
This, of course, angered the PK'er who waved his companions to attack. The first attacked leaped into mid-air at D'art, only to be struck down fiercely by a pillar of flame. cHip, as he was known, had also joined the battle. Other evil and Wild Knights joined the battle one by one until it appeared to be a laggy 8 on 8 combat. Gamma, now alone and lying weak on the ground, was approached by the same orphan boy. Gamma turned his head to the boy and gaspingly pleaded, "Please young one, feed me one of my magic nachos in my backpack. It's in... in the right pouch next to the yellow healing potion... I need... that too."
While the battle raged on, neither side with a kill to its name (it's the lag folks). Gamma opened his mouth to receive the magic nacho and the yellow liquid (it's lemonade folks). Almost instantly, Gamma jumped up to his feet and screamed in his normal semi-sarcastic squeaky voice, "Sonic MOO!". A powerful blue blast of energy left Gamma's fingertips, killing a murderer. Hopie noticed Gamma was ok and yelled for him to execute a new spell they had been working on.
"Kitty Vas Flam!" "Vas Corp Moo!"
A steady stream of small flaming cats and energy cows rushed the scene.... killing two baddies and wounding others. Ho Chi Minh saw this, and was not happy. "Kal Ort..." Too late Ho Chi... today you got blasted by Dallben's and Eric's crossing flamestrike, and you forgot your sunscreen, SPF 20,000. The remaining murderers saw this too, one wet himself. They start to run screaming obscene complains. Wild Knights Jestor and Wallace teleported in front of the lame men, one died of fear, another soiled himself. After the final kill, Gamma being sly as ever, starting singing one of his many weird songs...
