Galen Meets the Wild Knights...Part 1

"I'm dead"; the thought raced through Galen's mind as the four two-headed giants drew closer. Galen's limbs ached; his breath was cut short due to the cracked ribs he received from one of the giant pursuers. He knew there would be neither glorious death nor honor in his passing, for he had failed his friends and would soon join them. Poor Laurana, her precious silver bow did not save her, nor did young Kocurek’s enchantments prevent his death. Burly Cronus lay in two halves. Galen’s limbs gave in to the fatigue and pain. He crashed to the ground in a heap of flying blood, sweat and dust. The ettins were a mere hundred paces behind him. He muttered a quick silent prayer and braced himself for the end. A moment of silence and a blue sphere shimmered into existence. A lone figure emerged; he wore a suit of plate mail and held a well-worn halberd. Galen’s pain was overwhelming and he was becoming incoherent. He thought he heard the stranger mutter "sweet ". The warrior rushed pass the injured one and took a defensive stance glaring at the charging behemoths. Galen leaned on his side and saw the four creature’s charge towards the stranger. The warrior turned and spoke to Galen. Galen thought he intended to promise him salvation instead heard him say, " You really pissed them off ". The warrior quickly disarmed his halberd, and began to recite a spell. The air around him surged with energy, the forces of nature bending to his will. He gazed at the lead giant; several arrows protruding from his torso and a smoldering burn mark where a bolt of lightning had struck him. The warrior grinned and spoke " Kal Vas Flam ". A pillar of flames erupted from the very earth itself; the screaming giant was the nexus of this fire. The bellowing of two heads ripped through the air, the stench of burning flesh was overwhelming. The walking pillar of fire staggered a few steps then gave one final primal scream of agony and fell to the earth. The remaining giants glanced at the charred pile of flesh, the site of their fallen comrade drove them into a blood frenzy; they reached the brave warrior in two strides. The valiant warrior spoke two words before they reached him " Flam Sanct " and drew his halberd. The three giants surrounded him and started to land blow after vicious blow upon him. Galen’s broken body prevented him from protecting himself or reaching safety. Darkness was beginning to set over the fallen Galen, his last thought: "I hope this brave fool knows what he is doing "

A steady flow of pain passed over Sativa’s bruised body. The glancing blows from the giants didn’t bother him; it was the solid crushing hits that worried him. One ettin was seriously wounded; the remaining giants were at full strength. Several thoughts raced through Sativa’s mind at this time of great peril. "Where are those sorry punks. I told my brothers to meet me here for some training ". Sativa struck the wounded ettin again, this time the halberd dug deep into the monsters leg cutting through muscle and reaching bone. The monster staggered and gave a loud moan as he fell to the earth. The joy Sativa felt was short lived; the loud sounds of battle caught the attention of three more ettins who all broke into a sprint heading towards him. "I’m a dead man, I’m out of mana and my protective spell just dissipated". He suddenly sensed a ripple of magic behind him, like a tear between two planes was just opened and closed. A familiar voice cried out " In Vas Mani ". Sativa felt surges of energy engulf his body, the essence of life was restored. He turned around "So nice of you to join me SilverLeaf, where are the others?" SilverLeaf crashed into the giants yelling, "They will be here soon…I hope."

"Where are they!" growled SangDrax staring at Soltan and Corran. Soltan shrugged and avoided meeting SangDrax’s glaring eyes. SangDrax, the most seasoned warrior in the Wild Knights, paced back and forth like a caged animal. Corran moved clear from his path, Sang was known for his short temper and his quick backhand. A veteran of countless campaigns, Sang’s armor reflected his stature. His suit of plate was covered from head to toe with metal scars and chipped steel. A scrapper, Sang fought like a man possessed. In combat, he summoned the forces of magic and steel to destroy his enemies. It seemed like a good time to step outside, catch some fresh air and reach safety. Corran reached for the door and salvation, unfortunately Sang’s gaze turned towards him. "Corran, where do you thing you’re going?" the cold spoken words of SangDrax paralyzed him. He turned around, gave a weak smile and spoke "I’m going to look for Taj and Silen." He waited patiently for a response but Soltan broke the silence "That may be unwise brother, something is wrong and we should depart to join Lord Sativa. " A puzzled look grew in Sang’s face "Explain yourself Soltan and be quick about it. " "As you wish battlemaster, we all know Lord Sativa is not known for his patience, if he wasn’t already occupied he would have returned. I recommend leaving now to join him." Soltan finished his sentence and quickly drew a bolt from his quiver, armed his heavy crossbow and donned his bone helm. Soltan gave an evil grin quickly returned by Sang. The malicious grins gave Corran a chill, he felt sorry for any man, woman or creature that crossed our path today. The feeling abruptly fading as he sharpened his halberd.
