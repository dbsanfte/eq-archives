This guide is just that, a guide to baking for fun (lol) and profit.  If you know of a better, faster, or more profitable way please let me know and I will be more than happy to try and post changes.

Don't waste skill points putting them into Baking unless your in a hurry (it's probably the easiest AND it is the cheapest skill to advance).

If you are in Freeport you'll have an extremely easy time baking since Rat meat, Rat ears, Fire beetle eyes and Bat wings are easily had simply by killing the respective creatures. Otherwise look around for ANY creature that gives you one or more of the required ingredients.  I started baking right away and killed rats to get meat to increase skill.  However you may want to train up at your guild if you don't want to kill rats for several hours :-)

The only recipes you'll need to get to Master are:
Rat Sandwiches/Edible goo until trivial Rat meat + Bread/Fire Beetle Eye +  Rat Ear (or train to 21)

Batwing Crunchies until skill level 45 (Bat wing + Frosting) Bat wings from Academy  of Arcane Sciences or From Erudite Female @ Last Inn in EC Frosting from 1st Inn (Parthar)

Fish fillets until skill level 82 (Fresh Fish + Jug of Sauces) Fish from merchants @ East Freeport Docks Sauces from from Povar? in EC

Fish rolls (I stopped at 110 skill) Fresh Fish + Batwing

The profit margin for fish fillets is very good...
10cp per jug of sauce (cast Minor Illusion before buying from Parthar if he sells at 11cp)
26cp per fresh fish
114cp per two fish fillets sold to vendor
profit with 100% success = 78cp per attempt
I usually get 95% success.
This means an adjusted profit of 74cp
I can make 30 plat an hour easily and 35 if I haul ass.
Sure you can make a ton, but man, this has got to be the most tedious and boring way to go about it.

Take my advice and buy a Spit.  That way you can bake anywhere.  You can purchase them at the Traders Holiday from Tislan in East Freeport.

I take 7 backpacks to EC and FILL with Sauce (except for 3 slots) and then go to E Freeport Port and buy 20 fish and make 40 fillets.  Then buy 40 fish, then 60 etc as I make more room in packs. This stops me from zoning so much.  I also bind at merchant who sells fish so I can gate back after buying jugs of sauces :-)  I take 15pp with me to EC, when I go to bank after finishing batch I usually have around 80+ plat.
