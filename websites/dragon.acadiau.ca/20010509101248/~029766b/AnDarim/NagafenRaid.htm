Lord Nagafen Raid

On March31, 2001  Goemassun Grundy and Caleryn Artavia proudly represented The AnDarim in a raid on Lord Nagafen himself!  Teamed mainly with members from Temple of Dawn and Coeur de Lion (and a few others), 36 brave adventurers entered Nagafen's lair in search of glory.

The following are a series of pictures recording the event which was a total success!  Enjoy, and a great thanks to all who participated, especially to Mallo, Kuroni and Harmonious for inviting Goe and I.

And of course special mention to our brave yet somewhat psychopathic pullers Mastere and Gritzknoc who sacrificed themselves for the greater good! *salute*

Nagafen dropped:  a Cloak of Flames, roll won by Fentres, a Heirophants Crook, won by Tangena (sp?), and 2 Orbs of Tishan, won by the two enchanters Meznjessor and Elliphaz
