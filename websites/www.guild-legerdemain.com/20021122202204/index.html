    Plane of Nightmare!Wednesday - October 30th 2002    
I just wanted to share a couple pics with ya'll. Some of you may know that Imp form is non-kos in some parts of some planes. Here are a couple of the results from a little exploration in PoN. As always, you can click the picture for a larger version.
First, I found a mean lookin gator named Untel'Dak. The red thing growing out of his back is me hehe. He's red to a 60 and looks mean.

Another fellow I came upon was Vhaksiz the Shade. He looks pretty cool imho. Reminds me of the Lord Soth guy from the Dragonlance books. He's red to a 60 and has some horsies with red burning feet that guard him.

I did find some pleasant folk during my journey. These tadpoles don't hit nearly as hard as some of the other mobs in the zone and rarely drop some decent loot. They are casters, but resists help out quite a bit. Blue at 60.

For those of you that are PoP enabled, have fun exploring, but remember this; there's a whole lot of content on the mortal planes that is now unoccupied :>
