    Renux down!Friday - August 16th 2002    
Friday night Hriyn, Joredor, Zzeep, Kennington, Samanthea, Sweetblithe, Bubben, and yours truly assembled a force and headed for Steamfont Mountains intent on taking out Renux Herkanor.

Our plan was simple. Once we all were buffed and ready Bubben was to kit Renux around while everyone else smacked her around. Bubben performed an excellent pull once Renux was spawned. After a few rounds of nimble kiting Renux saw and opening in Bubbens defenses and hit with deadly precision. Seeing our valiant druid in peril Samanthea unleashed her healing powers, but alas the swift blades were tainted with a deadly poison...

Renux filled with anger and struck out at Samanthea to claim yet another victim. The attacks were relentless and quick. Seeing the fall of their comrades Joredor and Sweetblithe went into action casting with all their might to gain the attention of Renux. Good and evil magic combined together left Renux enraged to the point of confusion. Filled with rage, inspired with the deaths of their two friends, Hriyn and Ninden took this opportunity to unleashed a flurry of blades. Zzeep and Kennington were there to aid in our attack using their healing powers to assure Renux shall not claim another life again.

After a short while our force accomplished what they set out to do.....
Renux is no more.


Grats Hriyn on your epic pieces!
Loot dropped: Jagged Diamond Dagger, Translated Parchment, Trochilc's Skean, Ravenscale Neckguard, and Ravenscale Mask.
------submitted by Ninden

    New Interface!Thursday - July 25th 2002    
Welp, the new interface is out. It's still in "extended beta" so it's optional for the moment. You can read about some of the info on Casters Realm for a while.

While the new interface introduces some really nice features, it's a little difficult to get use to. At least, it's difficult for someone who's been playing for 3 years hehe. One of my big gripes is the lack of the /hitsmode command. Sure, you can create a new chatwindow that filters ALL of the melee spam into one window, but it's not the same. /hitsmode offered so much more versatility.

There are a few other gripes about the interface (like having to reorganize the spellbooks), but it's not that bad. For those interrested in a different look and feel for your EQ experience, you might check out EQ Interface

Just FYI.. like with many other major patches, they've re-incorporated several bugs that HAD been fixed. There was a problem with mob HP being reported to the client that had been fixed.. it's back. There use to be a problem where if you typed /camp desktop it would take you to server select that was fixed... it's back. There were a couple others that are now back that I can't recall at this moment. In any case, be wary of re-occuring problems. (Like that one where you die just after getting a CH.. if that's back too)
