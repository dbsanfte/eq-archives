    Halls of TestingSaturday - September 21st 2002    
Thanks, Ninden, for another great raid :>
We had a rough start, but eventually the raid took off. We really got moving about 2 hours after start time. We all did very well. Everyone was following istructions quickly and without question. There seemed to be quite a bit of laughing (and probably some drinking) going on. We even started playing with CH orders to get our clerics familiar with the idea. I'm sure we'll be using it quite often in the near future. A total of 10 hours went by before we gave the dragons a break hehe.
We decided to give Dozekar the Cursed a poke before we called it a day. That's a mean leezard. There was a little confusion over the timing of the CH chain which caused a slight speedbump in the healing process hehe. This cut our Dozekar adventure a little short. No worries though. For many, it was the first time we'd gotten to see him. For several, it was the first time to ever get devoured by him hehe. All good learning experiences. I'm sure we'll all be a little more wary the next time we play with Doz.

    EQ Fashion!Sunday - September 15th 2002    
It's a little known fact that high elven womens' helms were in fashion shortly after the Luclin models came out. That's right, they were something to admire, indeed. Don't take my word for it, here's Keymarad modeling a Thuradin brand helm back in-the-day.
Nice huh?
Unfortunately, these helms became a thing of the past; a rumor, a myth. As quickly as they came, they were gone =/ I assume that VI removed them because of the horrible load times that came with all the Luclin models. When it came down to it, womens' fashion just wasn't that important and was cut out of the picture.

Recently, we had a CT event. During that event, Samanthea's Skyshrine hat looked a little different than it normally does.
What does this mean? Are the old hats back? Was this just a fluke bug interracting with the new UI and shrink or illusion? Who knows. At least we know that the models are at least still in EQ somewhere hehe.

I certainly hope the hats make a come back. There are some things, though, that are deffinatly fashion no-no's. Here's Wuddar modeling an... interresting ensaumble of boxers, belt, hat, and boots in DN.

    ToV - HoT Success!Saturday - August 31st 2002    
Ready to test our might against an ancient evil we all headed to Legerdemain's first Halls of Testing raid in the Temple of Veeshan.

Our first pull of the day proved what dangerous place ToV truly is, we all were killed rather quickly due to pets gaining agro before Wuddar. Not willing to accept defeat so easily we prepared our second attack with newly gained knowledge and vigor.

On our next pull everyone ran for cover from the wurms deadly area effect spells while Wuddar stood his ground, not willing to succumb to this menacing wurm. Once the wurm was intent on making Wuddar his next meal we braced ourselves and charged into battle.....

Group one was Keymarad, Wuddar (MA), Sweetblithe, Embren, Joredor, Tileren.
Group two was Zzeep, Futtal (SA), Xardan, Quila, Gann, Skrambold.
Group three was Samanthea, Kabniel, Ninden.

The wurm fought fiercely but was not prepared for such powerful adversaries. Soon the wurm was dead and we prepared for the rest of our assaults.

All I can say is, truly an amazing job everyone. After a few pulls everyone was performing like a ToV pro! I didn't see one mistake, no over nuking, no early assists, nothing, nada, zip! We had a little over two groups and kicked wurm butt big time!
