Fallen Brethren and Nobles of the High Sky JoinMonday, November 27, 2000 1226 ESTBanyon

Yesterday on Sunday November 26th at 2:00 there was a meeting between the 'Fallen Brethren' and 'Nobles of the High Sky' in the Arena off of Lake Rathe.

The purpose of this meeting was for the merging of these two wonderful guilds under the name of 'The Fallen Brethren'. Those of us who have been in the guild for a while welcomed the newcomers with open arms, and intense carnage (dueling).

The Arena party ended with a group going off to visit the Efreeti and see if he had any more boots. Alas we never saw him. (7 placeholders in a row!)

A complete list of all the new Brethren will be forthcoming, but heres a short list of the new officers.
* Samdori, 48 Cleric
* Kronadin, 49 Paladin
* Sofya, 30 Shamaness

For those new Brethren out there, please make sure to send an eMail to fallenbrethren@hotmail.com with your characters name, level, class, and any alts you might have so that I can update the members page.

Again, let's all welcome the newest members!
