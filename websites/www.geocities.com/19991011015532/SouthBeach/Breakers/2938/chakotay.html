Chakotay
======
 
 =^=
Data Log
=^=
Name: Chakotay
Rank: Commander
Position: Second In Command
Training: Starfleet Academy
Race: Human
=^=

     A graduate of the Star Fleet Academy, this descendant of Native Americans went on to serve Star Fleet and achieved the rank of Lieutenant Commander. When his home world became part of the demilitarised zone and under the threat from Cardasia, he placed his people's well being above his own and joined the Marquis.
     Inevitably becoming an outlaw, he waged a terrorist campaign against the Cardassains, until his Star Ship was spirited to the Delta Quadrant by the Caretaker.

     He now serves as First Officer aboard the U.S.S Voyager, where it is hoped that he will exert a calming effect on the Marquis members who were forced to join the Star Fleet crew.
