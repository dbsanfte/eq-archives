Tuvok
====
 
=^=
Data Log
=^=
Name: Tuvok
Rank: Lieutenant
Position: Chief Security Officer
Training: Starfleet Academy
Race: Vulcan
=^=
     Tuvok has served under Captain Kathryn Janeway for four years as her tactical/security officer, including a period undercover, when he infiltrated the Marquis. His Vulcan logic renders him an excellent tactician and, combined with his high intelligence ( Hey...I'm just writing what he's supposed to be...it doesn't mean that I agree with it! ) provides him with umparelleled powers of deduction.
     His Vulcan approach to problems can sometimes cause him to be overly pedantic in his explainations! ( As if you hadn't noticed!!! ) Nonetheless, his Captain has come to depend upon his steadfast nature and sound advice. True to his Vulcan nature, his demeanour remains composed, no matter how disatrous or disappointing the situation becomes for the U.S.S Voyager and her crew.
     Thus his presence has a calming effect on his fellow officers. ( But not on me. Personally I find him obnoxious and irritating. Does anyone else agree with me that they should kill him off? )
