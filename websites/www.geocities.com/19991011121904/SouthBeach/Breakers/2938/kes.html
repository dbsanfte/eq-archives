Kes
===
 
=^=
Data Log
=^=
Name: Kes
Rank: Hydroponics Expert
Position: Trainee Nurse
Training: Field Training
Race: Ocampa
=^=
In keeping with her people, Kes has a very gentle demeanour. Unlike most of them, she is a free thinker and has a daring streak which led her to reach the surface of her planet, escaping her subterraneon home. She is sensitive and mildli empathic.
The Ocampa live for only nine years but in compensation are capable of quickly absorbing huge quantities of information and knowledge.
She has brought with her a thorough understanding od hydroponic food cultivation and is supplying her beloved Neelix, the chef, with ingredients. She has now set her mind to the study of medicine to help the doctor in the capacity of nurse.
