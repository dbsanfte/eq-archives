RANKS

High Council

High Lord/Lady :

The High Lord/Lady controls the website and is the figure head of the Raven Guard. By no means is he/she the supreme ruler. The 3 Senior Lord/Ladies can rebuke him/her in any decision. But he/she does have the power to promote or demote anybody at any time. (except the Senior Lord/Ladies) He/she helps keep running things smoothly at all times if he/she can.


Lord/Lady of the Treasury :

This position is in charge of the Raven Guard's monetary and equipment caches. This is ranked so high, because it takes complete trust and a stout heart to carry out the duties. He/she must decide who needs the money for loans, emergency equipment for cases of unretreivable bodies and all that such. 1 of the 3 Senior Lord/Ladies.


Lord/Lady of Foregin Affairs :

The Lord/Lady of Foreign Affairs sets up alliances, treaties, trade and hopefully prevent wars. Most likely the most important role in the Raven Guard. To strive, we need to get along, if we do not, we will be hard pressed to even survive. Also a Senior Lord/Lady.


Lord/Lady of the People :

This Lord/Lady is the surgeon of the morale department. He/she tries to intercept the problem with the troops before it begins. He/she is their main mouth piece. The Lord/Lady speaks the general feelings of the people. Also to set up official dungeon dives and ceremonies. One of the Senior Lord/Ladies.


Lord/Lady of the Warrior Caste :

This Lord/Lady represents the melee fighters (Rangers, Paladins, Shadow Knights, Rogues, Bards, Monks and of course Warriors). Since this is the largest group, this postion is the toughest for the Lord/Lady. But he/she also have the larger group to represent.


Lord/Lady of the Magi Caste :

He/she represents the pure Magic Users (Necromancers, Enchanters, Magicians and Wizards). To tell the High Council what the Magi want done or changed, what they are looking for and so on.


Lord/Lady of the Priest Caste :

The Lord/Lady represents the Healers of the Guild (Shamans, Clerics and Druids). They have the smallest group to represent, but they have it the easiest. Just like the other 2, they voice their opinion as the group of Priests.


Captain of the Guard :

The Captain monitors the members during ceremonies and during Guild Chat, to make sure they dont insult people within the guild or make trouble during the ceremonies of any kind. The Captain has no say what so ever over what members do outside the guild. If they kill steal or some other "un-honorable" thing, no action shal be taken and shall be ignored. We are about the guild, not what happens outside of it. That is not the duty of the Captain and will not take it on him/herself to try to reprimand the brother or sister of the Raven Guard.


Supreme Justice :

The Supreme Justice will decide cases that is too delicate or too vague for immediate decision by the main body of the High Council. He/she may also veto a High Lord/Lady's decisions ONLY if it goes against the Charter of the Raven Guard, and must point out where and how it interferes with our constitution.




MEMBER RANKS

Member ranks can be given Senior Officers at any time, and regular officers may request for approval of promotions for the members. Also, members, if they feel they deserve it, may put in a request to be taken under consideration. Warning, if a member bothers an officer everyday about the said promotion, don't hold your breath for the promotion any time soon...
