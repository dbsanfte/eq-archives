Tom Paris
=======
 
=^=
Data Log
=^=
Name: Thomas Eugine Paris
Rank: Lieutenant
Position: Conn Officer
Training: Starfleet Academy
Race: Human
=^=
Tom Paris began a brilliant career as a pilot with Starfleet but made a mistake which cost the lives of three fellow officers. Although he owned up, he was court martialled and cashiered. realising he had dissappointed his father, a Starfleet admiral, he bitterly turened to the Marquis as a mercenary only to be captured on his first mission. Captain Janeway sprang him from the penitentiary, needing his expertise and his knowledge of the Maquis but, under her fair command and their enforced circumstances, the good in the man and his brilliance as a pilot have resurfaced. His fondnedd for the ladies gets him into trouble but he has instinctively put his life on the linee to help others.
