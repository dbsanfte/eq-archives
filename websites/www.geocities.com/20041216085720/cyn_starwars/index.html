Welcome to the Web Site of Phaedra Whitlock.

Some Personal History.

I was born in Peoria, Illinois 1968, and named after a Phaedra in Greek Mythology. My family moved to several different places around Illinois as I grew up and I ended up back in Peoria to go to college at Bradley University. Graduated with an Art degree and became a graphic designer for the Illinois Department of Transportation, Bureau of Water Resources, which July 1,1995 was moved to help create a new department, The Illinois Department of Natural Resources### (Office of Water Resources).

After moving here I met Cathy Mosley### through a local Star Wars### gaming group/roleplay-by-E-mail-and-Live-Action game [LARP], and she introduced me to the Star Wars roleplay-by-mail club Forces of the Empire### where I met Patrick### and Dora. href="http://www.deathstar.org/~sparrowh">###. Patrick and Dora got me on-line where I have found other friends in Alt.Dragons-Inn###, a fantasy D&D roleplay by E-mail and newsgroup group and from there on to other games and friends.

I like gaming, roleplaying, reading, and visiting on-line museums, I like walking around Lincoln Memorial Gardens (a nature trail by Lake Springfield). I read lots of science fiction, fantasy and romance books, but these days have been reading more history than anything else. Science Fiction [the Lensman series by Doc E.E. Smith###, Robert A. Heinlein, Lord Peter Wimsey mysteries by Dorothy L. Sayers, the DragonLance trilogy, Darkover by Marion Zimmer Bradley, and the usual assortment of magazines and Harlequin Romances###, and Mercator's World### about maps. I also like skimming the Web and reading what looks interesting, and I read PC Magazine### so that I might learn something about computers.

I claim two cats as my own, and have a tentative claim on two more at my parents house. One younger brother named Charles, two live parents, three live grand-parents, 6 cousins who have 2 children so far, and twin niece and nephews. Charles is married to Jennifer, and they and my mother and two cousins are all nurses.

Favorite foods are steak (medium-rare with Ranch dressing, soy sauce, or au jus) and macaroni and cheese. Fried chicken. Sausage Lover's pizza or Cheese Lover's with extra cheese. Colby, Meunster, and Cheddar cheese. Fast food. Chocolate brownies. French Silk or peach pie. Pepsi, mild, hot cider, hot chocolate, chocolate milk, orange juice (not from concentrate), and lemonade that has actual lemons in it. I dont much like spicy foods.

I like space very much and one of my favorite books is rather technical one on the logistics of setting up a moon colony and what it would be like to then live there. In the meantime, there is NASA### and the Jet Propulsion Laboratory###.

I've always liked languages, took french in High School and College, and had Spanish in High School as well (do NOT take French and Spanish at the same time). Would like to learn Klingon###, Romulan (Rihannsu), Japanese, Arabic, Russian... Just about anything really. I'm not picky.

I have played D&D ###, Vampire the Masquerade ###, Champions ###, and other roleplaying games when I get the chance.
