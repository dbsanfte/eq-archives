The Reckoners History

The Reckoners first formed around 1993 during an AD&D game. The characters needed a name for their adventuring band and so the Reckoners we became due in large part to the warcry of Hilda the barbarian paladin, "Now Comes... The Reckoning!". (Hilda also devised the 'Circle of Doom' now known as bard kiting.)

The little group continued in play for several months before each character went their seperate way, and a new adventuring band was formed.

The Reckoners were reborn in the fall of 1999 when Nimarok, a friend of the Reckoners began playing Everquest on Bristlebane and incited a few of them to try it. Addiction set in, but by mistake the Reckoners had landed on Tunare server, being easier to remember and spell. Eventually Nim bribed them into restarting on Bristlebane where they discovered it was much easier than they'd feared to hit 25 the second time around.

Mirabella Ebonheart established herself as our first guild leader. Asiah X'Rhai and Sharien Songraven served as the first guild officers.

Guild membership was at first restricted to the original Reckoners only, (IE friends in real life) plus Andromada, the wife of one of the originals, but after grouping regularly with us for over 20 levels Vuurl became the first of the new Reckoners followed by Chiba. Bigburp Scuseme and Belgerard Oldewulf the mage also grouped regularly from Overthere on, but did not join the Reckoners tho some of their alts did in the end. Both departed for Dark Ages of Camelot when it came out. Mukk joined next, and soloed his way up to finally group with the Primes. (Go Mukk!) Nimarok eventually joined the Reckoners in late 2001.

Asiah became the first Level 50 character, followed by Mirabella and Sharien. Vuurl soon passed them and has held onto his lead through 54. Terece and Perisina are the closest, but unlikely to catch him. The bard stalled at 52 due to excessive dragon raiding.

Currently the guild consists of the core group of 45+ characters: Mirabella, Asiah, Sharien, Vuurl, Terece, Jasada, Aleriel, Biffur, Andromada, Mukk, Nimarok, Chiba, Evian and various younger alts of these Primes. The number, name, class and level of alts varies wildly, but seems wood elf/erudite heavy.

After the original group of Reckoners stalled out due to lack of an enchanter, a second group was formed to level one, along with a new cleric to take over for Asiah. Most of the alts from the first and second groups are 45+ and together form a fluid rostered group, based on who wants to play the cleric that evening, and who will be snaring or mezzing.

Due to varying schedules, several other Reckoner groups have come and gone. Currently the lowbie group created for Heidie's play schedule is approaching 17 and scheduled to merge with the mid-20s Warslik Woods group that is currently on hold. Chodo may be joining this group as well. The high levels are busily chain raiding Tserrina Syl'Tor in order to get Perisina the robe, and spiking dorfs, wurms and Hole golems for variety.
