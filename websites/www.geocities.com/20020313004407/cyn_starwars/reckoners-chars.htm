The Reckoners Character Bios

Level 50+

Mirabella Ebonheart: Lvl 54 Reaver , Erudite Shadowknight
"She who pushes bards off docks" and "She who's vanity exceeds all others", Mirabella is the leader of the Reckoners. From Erudin, she took up the sword in order to one day confront her dark god Cazic Thule the lord of Fear and no doubt complain bitterly about his ignoring her. Mirabella has slain his avatar many times in the Temple in Innothule and taken his loot for herself. Mirabella is the third highest Erudite Shadowknight on Bristlebane, not to be mistaken for Mirabelle the druid. Mirabella is extremely vain and if not praised regularly grows insecure and homicidal.
Hobbies: Buzzing the Platform in NRo with the Bard's 51 Selo's song up.
Official Quote: Mean people suck >p.
