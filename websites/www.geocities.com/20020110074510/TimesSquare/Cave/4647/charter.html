CHARTER

The Raven Guard stands for honor, loyalty and unity. To always to be a body to never be defeated. If we are seperated, we will be thrown apart by even the slightest winds. We are here to uphold and to help each other. Like the name suggests, we will stalk our prey and destroy them without mercy. But, we are a peaceful guild above all. If we are attacked, we will defend our own at any cost. Our honor derives from helping our brothers and sisters, if something simple to give them some much needed food and water, to helping them from a creature. We will always help. Long live the Raven Guard, Cuervo Guardia!
