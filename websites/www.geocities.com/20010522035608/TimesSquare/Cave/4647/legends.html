LEGENDS OF THE RAVENS


Kienea

If I, Gadas, am the father of Raven Guard, then surely Kienea is the mother of the Guild. She is actually the one that concieved the essence of Raven Guard. From her mind sprung the rules and back bone that binds and nutures this Guild. We all owe her our respect and gratitude for this great deed she has wrought. Also, it was her determination that pushed me to finally put the motions of the Raven Guard dream into a reality. That is why she is a Legend of the Ravens.

Gobsmaka Toofsmasha

Gobsmaka is the "posterboy" of the Raven Guard. He is fiercely loyal and will always defend the honor of the Guild. Also, he always, no matter what the cost, helps his comrades. He gives without asking for reward or even thanks. With him as one of the Senior Officers, the Raven Guard will always prosper and overcome anything we have to face. He is a Legend because he knows no bounds when it comes to helping the brothers and sisters of the Raven Guard.

Leani Firefoxx

The fiery red headed Magician is close to my heart. When I first joined Wrath of the Dragonclaw Clan, I only heard of Leani as an upstart...a rebel even. By fate, it seems, I met her in a remote area of the world. We became fast friends and agreed to build a Guild together. That was long ago and the dream of a Guild was forgotten, but she is the one that started the fire. When finally Raven Guard was brought to life by Kienea and I, she was the first one in line to join. I owe....no, we all owe her a debt I fear can never be paid. She truly is a Legend of the Ravens.
