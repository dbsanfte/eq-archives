     Harry Kim is an extremely enthusiastic young man from a close Asian Family. He sets himself very high standards and is determined to be and exemplary Starfleet Officer. Voyager is his first assignment since graduation from the Academy.
     His position as Ops. Officer reflects him academic success and his ability to analyse and solve problems. He has faced the situation of being stranded in the Delta Quadrant, so far from home, with strength of character and courage beyond his years.
     He is earnest and eager to please.
