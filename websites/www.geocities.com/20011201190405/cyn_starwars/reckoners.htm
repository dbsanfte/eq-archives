    History

    The Reckoners first formed around 1993 during an AD&D game. The characters needed a name for their adventuring band and so the Reckoners we became due in large part to the warcry of Hilda the barbarian paladin, "Now Comes... The Reckoning!". (Hilda also devised the 'Circle of Doom'.)

    The little group continued in play for several months before each character went their seperate way, and a new adventuring band was formed.

    The Reckoners were reborn in 2000 when the same players assembled online to play Everquest on first the Tunare server then again on Bristlebane.

    Both times in EQ, Mirabella Ebonheart established herself as our first guild leader. Asiah and Sharien SongRaven served as the first guild officers shortly followed by all their other numerous alts.

    Guild membership was at first restricted to the original Reckoners only, (IE friends in real life) plus Andromada, the wife of one of the originals, but after grouping regularly with us for over 20 levels Vuurl joined the Reckoners. Bigburp Scuseme and Belgerard the mage did not but their alts did after they too spent several thousand hours with us.

    Asiah became the first Level 50 character, followed by Mirabella and Sharien. Vuurl soon took the levelling lead and has held onto his lead.

    Currently the guild consists of the core group of 50+ characters: Mirabella, Asiah, Sharien, Jasada, Vuurl, Aleriel, Biffur, and various younger groups of alts. The number, name and level of alts varies wildly.

    The Guild's main law is 'Need before Greed' when divvying loot.

    Reckoners participate in Rordin's Everquest Raid Calender X, and the Bristlebane Dragon Calender X.

    Mirabella Ebonheart: "She who pushes bards off docks" and "She who's vanity exceeds all others", Mirabella is the leader of the Reckoners. Mirabella is the third highest Erudite Shadowknight on Bristlebane (Lvl 52 Reaver), not to be mistaken for Mirabelle the druid who wears whatever gear without caring how it looks.
