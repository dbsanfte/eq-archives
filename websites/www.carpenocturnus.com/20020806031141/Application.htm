Applications

 

Membership applications are open again, and we are currently accepting applications for all classes and level 50 or above, with a strong preference for level 55 and above. But before you fill in the electronic form below, here's a little bit of information about our guild, Carpe Nocturnus.

We started out as a small guild, filled with friends made both on-line and in real life. We grew to be a family of people who enjoy one another's company just as much as we enjoyed the game we all play on. Over time we have kept that closeness with one another as we grew, adding new members to the ranks who were also looking for a place to play with friends and generally have a good time on raids and other events. We tend not to be a guild that is heavily into raiding, though we may add more events as we gain more members of raid level and ability. Our scheduled raid times are Sundays, starting at 12:00 EST, and then again Wednesday nights. In some cases, these raids are for epic pieces for a guild member. Our only requirements are that you play actively, attend raids, and want to play the class you are applying with regularly on our raids. You're no good to us if we can't rely on you to be at most of the events held.

If you're looking for a place to just hang out, we're likely not for you. If you're looking for a place to 24/7 raid, we're definitely not the right guild. But if you would like a combination of casual grinding and weekly raiding, then fill out the form below and send it off to us. We ask our applicants to come to a few raids and grinds so that we can all get to know each other and see how well we work together. We're a tight family here in CN and we want to make sure that we think this is the guild for you.
