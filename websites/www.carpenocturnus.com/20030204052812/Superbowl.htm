COASTAL CLASH - Place your bets!


And from around the worlds, people from Norrath, Luclin, and the Planes, and beyond, all joined in to celebrate a special day, one that would hold the attention of all who participated for hours on end. Good and evil clashing on the battleground, their prize first a small, brown, off-shaped, boarskin sphere throughout the conflict. After fighting for their goal, only one group, the devils or the angels, will receive their ultimate prize, a set of powerful and much coveted magic rings.

The dark army, clad in black and silver, marched down from the north, even as the young privateer navy sailed into port . Both knew that only the other could stop them from claiming the jeweled rings they had seen in their dreams, the rings of destiny. The field of battle would be close by to the One True Temple of Norrath, and even the lofty temple keepers would watch the coming turmoil.

The onlookers cheer for their Pirate favorites, and platinum is exchanged as swiftly as it is found deep from the pockets of the observers. You too can support your favorite seamen, and buy, for 20 PP each, a place among the cubes below. Boxes will be assigned randomly. The team who bests the other will bestow upon the winner a sum of 2000 PP.

If you want to play, contact Quella either in-game or by e-mail. Guild Members only, please, unless too many boxes go unfilled. Then we will open it up for others. PP to enter will be expected at the time you purchase your boxes.

Disclaimer: As not all of the boxes were filled before kick-off, if no one wins, all monies will be returned.
