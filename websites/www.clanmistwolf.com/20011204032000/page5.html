Who: Clan Mistwolf and friends
What: A low-level fun raid (?)
Why: To have fun and all get to work together
When: Each Wednesday at approx 9:30 CST
Where: Look for current data posted by Degres

How:
1) Create a new character.

a) Must be a human.
b) Cannot be a solo class (no mages, or necros)

c) Must be different from your primary character's group (healers = Clerics, shaman, druid. Caster = Mage, Necromancer, Wizard, Enchanter. Melee = everyone else.)

2) Contact a guild member and let them know you have made a newbie. Guild guides are Lalani, Karaglen, and Aggony.

a) Complete your newbie note quest.

b) You will be supplied with our starting armor and weapons as well as money for your first spells. It may take a while on the armor, but we will at least try to get you spell money as soon as possible.

c) Get spells and mem them, Caster (bards too)will have their first round of spells bought for them.

d) Level your character up to the appropriate level to match the rest of the guild. Look at the most recent newbie night post to see what level you need to be.

3) Group up and start the Hunt. Join us at the next event and we will get your character guilded.

a) We'll group up by class coverage trying to form 'ideal' parties.
b) Many of our events will be role playing type events.
c) Trying to work on group command skills and some basic tactics.
d) Events will last for about 2-3 hour each, try to be there the whole time.

The objective of this event format is so that we can all play together and really get to know each other. Hopefully it will be a learning event and help to promote teamwork and communication with in large groups and multiple parties.

Some of the other rules on these characters are that I would like them to remain pure, only used for these events. Anyone caught playing them outside these events will be beaten with a wet noodle. No twinking them either outside of their initial issue.

I would like 100% guild participation in this event, meaning that if you're online when we do it I would like you to be there.
