Guild membership

Clan Mistwolf will be an open membership guild. All races and classes will be considered for membership. All applicants must be approved by current members of the guild in a bi-monthly mem

bership vote. Membership votes will be held at a prescribed time and it is the responsibility of the guild members to vote and express their opinions.

Basic format for membership will be as follows:

1. Prospective member is posted on the guild web site a minimum of a week prior to the membership vote.

2. Potential guild member will be invited to guild functions prior to guild vote. Current guild members are encouraged to group with and interact with potential candidates at said function. If applicant is unable to attend the function, it is the applicant's responsibility to make themselves available to a variety of guild members.

3. Guild will vote on applicants on a prescribed vote date.

4. Two no votes will be considered as a vote of no confidence. A no vote must be accompanied by a reason or explanation.

5. If a vote of no confidence is received, an appeal may be made directly to the guild officers for reconsideration.

6. Upon guild approval, a guild officer will invite, welcome, and introduce the new member. New guild members will have a 14 day probationary period (members receiving an invitation after one no vote will have a 30 day probationary period). During said probationary period the new member will be expected to group with an officer of their class group and as many guild members as possible. At the end of the probationary period, guild officers will meet and discuss the status of the new member.

7. Any guild member whose behavior reflects poorly upon, or brings discredit to the guild, will have their membership status reviewed by the guild officers. If guild officers find sufficient grounds, the guild will be asked to vote upon continuation of membership.

Flying above, so high on wings of bone and feather
Looking down upon the earth below
High above any one man's tether.

I look up and see this majestic sight,
This beautiful omen of love and style.
A creature so perfect, no man could have conceived,
An angel so lovely, my heart is beguiled.

She comes to me,
Her words like a symphony of sainted musicians singing,
She touches my arm, the cool touch of a wind,
Caresses my arm, always pleasing.

If only I could say a word, a phrase, a lyric
I would then be found in honor in her sight.
But alas I am but a poor lowly man,
Dreaming of ways to be in her arms throughout her Perpetual Flight.

***Real life family members - brothers, sisters, wives, husbands, etc. are exempt from this process and will be admitted after a cursory introduction to the guild, provided there are no strong objections to membership. Objections should be addressed to a guild officer.

***New guild membership rules will go into effect on July 1, 2000. New membership before that time will be handled on an individual basis by guild officers.
