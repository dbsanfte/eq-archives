About us:  Clan Mistwolf is an Everquest guild that has been on the Test Server for several years.  We have about 30 regular members ranging from beginner level through the low 50's.  We have events almost weekly where the guild gathers for raids and parties.  We hold a role playing event each Wednesday which we call "Newbie Night".  The newbie night characters are only for those events and allow the guild to play together regardless of our primary characters levels.  You can get more details in the "Newbie Night" link at the left.
Joining us:  Party with our members regularly and you are likely to be invited to our events.  If that works out, the guild may vote you in.  Also see our "Charter" link at the left.
