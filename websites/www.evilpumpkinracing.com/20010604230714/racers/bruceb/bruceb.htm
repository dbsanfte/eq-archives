Daytona and DeLand March 2001

Wow, it’s time to pack up and head for Daytona again.  I spent hours and hours this winter working on the Skorpion motor, not!  Actually the motor maintenance was just like last year, change the oil and adjust the five valves.  Yep, this Skorpion Cup racing is a lot of work.  Oh, I did replace the OEM chain, wash the bike, and safety wire the oil line bolts. 

This year I decided to go as frugally as possible to Florida and only race with AHRMA in the Skorpion Cup events.  I departed for DeLand, FL  at 5am Thursday March 1st, arrived at the  airport/temporary racetrack about 3pm, went to registration, and then wheeled the bike through technical inspection.  Being the frugal guy that I am (some say I’m just a cheap bastard) I slept in the back of the trailer Thursday night.  I almost forgot, about dusk I met a guy on a Skorpion Replica.  His name was Abel Genes and he said he just flew in from Brazil to race the Skorpion Cup events at DeLand and Daytona.  Here’s a guy who just got off a plane, went to storage to retrieve his bike, used it as transportation for the weekend, and it was also his race bike.  Talk about incentive to keep your bike on two wheels.  I really thought Abel was pulling my leg but he really did race the bike and use it for transportation to the hotel.

Did I mention I was too cheap to sign up for Thursday practice, and by doing so I would only have one practice session Friday morning before the race but that’s OK because I’ve raced at DeLand in 1999 & 2000.  No change in the track conditions, DeLand is still better suited as a motorcross track with all the rough pavement and loose surface material. I was lucky enough to get the holeshot at the start of the race and lead by a small margin over Trent Thomas for the first 3 laps.  On lap 4 I finally decided to relax and if Trent was faster he could take the lead.  Funny thing happened, by relaxing I dropped another second off my lap times and pulled away to win by 20 bike lengths.  Trent and I both bettered our lap times from 2000.  I ran about 2 seconds quicker with a best lap of 1:34.5.   Kevin Brown took third place in the race. 

We spent Saturday & Sunday enjoying the Daytona wildlife.  I could tell you more but my wife might read this.  Monday morning we arrived at Daytona International Speedway and it was very windy. Since the AHRMA races were spread out over two days we would get 3 practice session before lunch.  My first two practice session were uneventful but the final session was red flagged due a crash.  This seemed to hurt Kevin Brown the most since he missed much of the first session and all of the second after he threw a chain.  Green flag, everyone makes it through the first couple of turns on two wheels, YEAH! Kevin Brown got the holeshot followed by cousin Bruce Brown, and Trent Thomas. However, going onto the west banking for the first time Trent became the only Skorpion bodywork scrapper of the day. He and the bike survived without serious injury but the fractured foot bone will take some time to heal. I passed Kevin for the lead running towards the chicane for the first time, while we both put some distance on the rest of the guys. The remainder of the race for Kevin and I was lonely since I had a gap on him, and he had a gap on Craig Hunley in third place. The race for third was the one to watch with Craig, Alex McLean, and Paul Conley grouped close together for much of the race.  I found out after the race that Kevin was hitting the rev limiter about 200 yards before his braking market for the chicane due to the tail wind.  My lap times on my on-board timer were in the 2:28-2:29 range which was 3 seconds slower than the 2000 race (best laps in the 2:25’s).  In the 2000 race there was three of us drafting each other for the lead which helped lower our lap times. 

OK this settles it I’m only racing in Florida during March in the future.  I’m 4 for 4 in Skorpion Cup at Daytona & DeLand.  I guess luck is better than skill any day.  Only entering the Skorpion races, skipping the extra expense of practice, and sharing a hotel room almost saw the prize money pay for all the bills.  Wow, that will never happen again.  Next race should be with CCS at Roebling Road the last weekend of March.

Playing with Sprockets

This race report is going to be quick because I'm racing again on April 14-15 at Virginia International Raceway (VIR). Saturday March 31 I started the day using Daytona gearing (16/39) just to find out what it would do to lap times. My best lap time on Saturday (1:28.92) was 3.9 seconds off my times from the October 2000 race at Roebling. I did finish 2nd in Lightweight Sportsman but it was a weird race with a waving yellow in turn 9 and some passing under yellow as the race went along. I held my position through the waving yellow and lost touch (got beat) with the first place rider.

After the race I decided to put 16/40 gearing on the bike which I used during the fall AHRMA race. Not one to leave well enough alone I started thinking about the gearing which caused me to study a gearing chart at 11pm. 10 minutes later I decided to try 16/41 gearing which I installed Sunday morning.

Sunday morning started out windy and the wind got worse as race time neared. I would guess we had a head wind up the front straight of 30-40mph. I got a good start for the SuperSingles race and lead into turn one by out braking a "real" supermono bike (Honda 600cc stuffed into a 250 GP chassis). I would repeat this same out braking maneuver every lap during the race as I traded places with the Honda rider. He would trade places by out powering me on the front straight before turn one and I would out brake him and lead through the infield turns. As you might have already guess I lead out of the last turn but got passed just before the finish line. The other rider was so happy to beat me I didn't have the heart to tell him he just passed a bike that weighed 80-100 more pounds and probably had 15-30 less horsepower (FYI, my bike weighs 370lbs and has 43hp). The gearing helped, especially with the headwind, but my best lap time (1:26.7) was still off my best from the fall.

Other items worth noting. I lost another trailer tire on the ride home, just like the Daytona return trip. Track time was very limited due to all the crashes and red flags during the weekend. I only got about 90 miles of track time from Saturday morning through Sunday's race, geez!
