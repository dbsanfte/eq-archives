Welp...now that we have got a few of the gems for the quest armors. We need to figure out which faction(s) to go with.

I'm suggestion Coldian as a definite and Dragon as a maybe. Oh okay...kill them damn dragons if ya want...some darn nice stuff drops in SkyShrine. Stuff our Alpha Group could do too...right now! :)

Here's the discussion I started on the Praetoriate site about it...figured we should all get on the same sheet of music too. I don't really expect much trouble, but wanted to at least let you all debate and maybe get more input too. I can be wrong, just doesn't happen that often! :-P

1) Coldain
2) Giant
3) Dragon

Quest risk -vs- reward pretty much follows the order. Of the three faction/quests the Coldian faction/quests offer a good reward and the least risk. Killing giants to raise Coldain faction is almost as easy of a desision to make as killing orcs. Killing giants also raises faction with ClawsofVeeshan ( Dragon ).

Giants offer real nice quests, but require you to kill dwarves and dragons to raise faction to get the quests. Giant faction also requires you to kill dragons in the "easy" part of the Temple of Veeshan for the quest armors. Going with Giant faction allows you to kill dwarfs in Icewall for the real nice plate armor that they drop. Going with Giant faction also makes hunting in Skyshrine a non-issue. Some real nice stuff drops in Skyshrine too.

Dragon offers some real ultra-uber quest armors...a few AC points better then giant, but still real nice. Dragon faction also allows you to use the Wakening Lands port and hunt in Cobalt Scar without having extra wyverns of cobalt drakes aggro on you. Does make Western Wastes a tad more difficult to hunt in as you can't kill the dragons there, but there is plenty else to kill for exp and loot there.

I suggest that a direction is picked now. It really stinks to have half of your guild on one faction and the other half on another. Really limits the things either side can/will hunt together.

Myself, I'm thinking Coldain & Dragon is the way to go. Just makes Skyshrine out of limits for hunting. Reason I think coldain/dragon is the way to go is for both short term and long term.

Short Term: Killing Giants to get coldain quest armor is something a small guild can do. 1-2 group raids on giant town can outfit the guild with the coldain quest armor fairly easily. 1-2 group raids can get everything except for maybe the chest. I think you need to kill the giant king for quest chest armor drops, but could be wrong.

Long Term: Using the Coldain quest armors, you can then work on the Dragon quest armors as the guild power develops.

Easy Loot Greed Problem: /drool The darn dorfs and Skyshrine mobs drop some darn good armors and weapons. Just a few stats short of Hate/Fear gear for the melee types.

Part II:
Did some comparision "shopping" with the quest armors...all armors have the same spell effects per peice. Some effects are rather nice. All of the armors would be nice to have. Some peices are better then others for a particlar class. Druid bracer stats and effect is rather nice, while all the other priest class bracers pretty much stink stat wise.

Looking at pure stat values. In order of best stats to lowest.
1) Dragon
2) Giant
3) Coldain

AC bing one of the few stats that really matter...in order of best to worst.
1) Giant
2) Dragon
3) Coldain

In order of most obtainable to least:
1) Coldain
2) Giant
3) Dragon

Dragon Faction Reality Check time...

A show of hands for who where will regularly if ever kill 65th lvl dragons in the Testing Hall ( deep inside ) of the Temple of Veeshan.

Unless we all join Divine or some other uber-guild...I don't see any hands coming up for this poll.

So..I want Dragon Faction so I can port into Wakening Lands without Woushi killing me.

Cobalt Scar portal is an excellent alternative. Yes...there are agro Cobalt Drakes that path close, but you can survive them if not inviso. Buff cold resists. Zone to Skyshrine is very close to CS Port Circle and Zone to Wakening Lands is very close to SS/CS zone point.

Based on reality ( as I see it ) and greed for the nice drops in Skyshrine. Verser is going for Coldain and killing giants and dragons. They are all evil and must be stopped. Sleethr will probably still try and get good coldain and dragon faction though, but not going to stress out over the dragon faction stuff like I used to.

As a guild, Division 13 will go with Coldain faction. Giants will be fair game and Dragons will still be open for debate.
