Just wanted to relate a little about my weekend in EQ.

Friday night spent the evening with connection issues that affected anyone trying to play on Bertox or any of the CerfNet servers. The raid for Vox that I was supposed to attend got delayed by about 1/2 hour or so I was led to believe. The raid itself didn't start until 1am. We started clearing all the Giants and the guardians of Vox around then and after we had cleared her lair it was 3. After being selected for the buffing group we started buffing the 55 attendants. IT took about an hour ro get everyone buffed. Then we all camped out and went to chat. After we were all there everyone went back in and we reformed into our groups and rushed in to defeat Vox. She managed to get off 1 complete heal. We only lost 6 people.

The loot was the biggest issue with people bitching about who got to roll for what.

Anyway once it was all over it was 5:30. I had been up for 24 hours.

Saturday work up at 12:30 and went to play EQ. I managed to play until 5:30 and was falling asleep so I decide to take a nap until 6:00 am Sunday :)

Well I played until about 9:30 Sunday night so over 12 hours in Norrath and for that dedication we got some more Epic armor gems, some wicked nice armor for Tenlaar, some stuff for some nice Tailored armor for Ghall and some decent exp.

My thanks to Gammashot from Ancient Lightning for the invite to Vox. To Nef for all the ports and for Curastik for all the Mana, to Foggy for Tanking, Caldron for Nuking, Sariig for the crack and Sleethr for kickin serious ass with his pets .. and the free goodies.

Hello Division 13 :)

Heres a recap of my weekend in EQ, (only played on Saturday), in case anyone was wondering what the long absent Kalaan/Wardak is up to these days. Hehe

Logged on in the afternoon, and tried finding a group in Dreadlands to get some exp with Sendil. 2 hours later, with no group, I called it quits and logged on my twinky Shadowknight. Got him to within half a bubble of 15 then logged off for dinner. After dinner I logged on Lonar to get him from Skyshrine to Sebilis for the Juggie raid. At 8pm 6 full groups of Blood Tide, all 53+, zoned into Old Seb and started clearing our way down towards Trakanons Lair. As we got about halfway down, we found out that Midnight was attempting Trakanon since he was up. We decided that we would hold up just down the hall from Traks’ Lair and pull juggies and reets until Midnight was done with Trak, then move into the lair. Well, things didn’t go as planned for Midnight and they got wiped out. After doing CR, they tried again. Total Wipeout. They gave him a third try, third full wipeout. After the third CR they called it a night and we were left to continue doing juggies and reets from the tunnels around the lair, as there was NO way we were going to attempt Trak. If everyone had brought their resist gear, I think we would have given it a shot, but since we didn’t plan on Trak being up, we weren’t prepared for him. As it was we had an excellent night for drops, and I think we are planning to head back to that spot this coming Saturday. Our drops in 8 hours of killing juggs/reets:
