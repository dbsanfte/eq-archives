What I noticed the other day (on Test):

Steaks typically add +1 to a stat. Bear steak is strength +1. Mammoth steak is strength +2. Didn't check lion or wolf steaks (or I forget).

Beer braised meat is typically one + the stat for steak, plus a -1 to Intelligence. So Beer Braised Bear is +2 Strength, -1 Intelligence. Beer Braised Mammoth is +3 Strength, -1 Intelligence. Beer Braised Rat is +2 Dexterity (if I remember correctly) and -1 Intelligence.

Those are the only foods I noticed with stats so far. No sandwiches have stats that I could determine, and I made gator rolls and fish rolls but those also do not have stats.

You get the stat changes just by having the food in your primary food slot (the first slot with food in it, so that you are "eating" it). So it's not cumulative at present but placing a 20-stack of mammoth steaks in your top slot will give you +2 strength until the stack is used up. ^_^
