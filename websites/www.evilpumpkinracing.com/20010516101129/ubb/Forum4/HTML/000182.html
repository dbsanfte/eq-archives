Sleethr posted 04-06-2001 08:03 AM CT (US)     
Our stalwart band of heroes/fools formed up at the entrance to Nurga. Blessings to Brell and Tunare were said upon each member of the group and with spirits lifted ( literally by Brinne ), thoughts quickened and bodies energized we desended the treacherous waterfall and started the cleansing of this evil den. The Goblins attempted to bring us down with numbers, but their advances were easily stopped by the combination of Brod's amazing fists, Erlas's mighty determination and Gastro's Jedi Mind Tricks(tm). Oh wait, wrong game...sorry. :)

One of the brainwashed goblin slave burnyai, not happy to see us there to free him, foolishly attacked our party. Sadly, we had to kill him. We made his death as painless as possible and where rewarded by finding a Gi among his slave gear. The Gi told us that he must have been an informer for his fellow slaves.

Finding the Nurga foes little challenge and wishing to rid the lands of Kunark of even more villians, the team marched bravely into the center for Goblin evil, The Temple of Droga. Based on all the horror stories and fearing the worst from this vile place, blessings and prayers of protection were said again. We desended into the first goblin room after being made light by Brinne's songs. We quickly overpowered this first batch of goblins and then proceeded further into the goblin den. Our efforts soon paid off as we discovered a nice Bard weapon hidden upon one of the goblins.

The goblins provided us with many opportunities to rid the land of their kind and despite their best efforts, we proved to be too strong for them. Desiring even more goblin blood, our band cautiously made their way to the goblin Dining Hall where the Goblin Warder is said to reside. Our caution proved well founded as Brod scouting the room. There were goblins everywhere! Brod managed to trick a few from the horde into following him to our ambush and via his tricks, we were able to steadily eat away at the goblin's strength in numbers.

Our over-confidence would lead to the goblin's retaking their dining hall. After, clearing enough of the goblins around the hall, we moved into the goblin's large room. We kept the area nicely clean of goblin's until one goblin decided to run down a hall to gather some reinforcements. He came with about 6-8 of his friends and together, they managed to cause our retreat. Leaff's evac spell managed to gather everyone up and wisk us safely away to Medec's Butcher Block homeland.

The goblins of Droga may think they have won this battle, but one day...their cheif will fall to the might of our arms!
