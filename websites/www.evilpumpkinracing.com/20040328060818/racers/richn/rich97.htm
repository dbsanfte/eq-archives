May 9th - 11th: First Race Weekend

The first weekend! It's about time! I fixed a idle/rich mixture problem with my carbs a few weeks before this weekend. Talk about the last minute! The problem turned out to be bad float needles. $34 each thru Metropolitan Hitching Post in St. Paul. I also ordered a jet kit and my tires thru the web via the Motorcycle Accessory Warehouse. I was pretty bummed that a jet kit was cheaper than two little OEM float needles. I decided to go with the same tires that I was running last year, and it looks like just about everyone except Jason ( #197 ) went with the Metzler ME1 CompK for the Rear & ME33 Comp K for the front. ( I wonder if his crash prone laps had anything to do with tire choice :-) )

After talking to Glen Christianson, it looks like I got the tire pressure just right for the Saturday races at 29 front/ 32 rear at about 65 degrees and sunny. I raced in 3 races for the Saturday activities. I even managed to get a second place trophy finish out of the race in my class ( Ultralight Super Sport ) Even with that...I'm not at all happy with my performance. I think I've finally reached the point where I need to get a pipe to be more competitive. It was pretty darn depressing passing other GS's in the infield, just to be re-passed on the straights.

Sunday was a whole different story. We woke up to 40 degree weather and overcast. It was miserable! I froze my way thru one morning practice session -vs- the three I could have taken. The track was cold and the tires where having a hard time biting. A lot of other people made that observation the hard way by crashing. After I got done with that practice I dropped my front by 1.5 +/- .5 lbs and the rear by 1 lbs. It seemed to help for the race I had in the afternoon ( when it was even colder that the practice) I witnessed about 3 - 4 racers picking themselves up from the grass in that race. I think just about everyone was having trouble staying on two wheels and trusting their tires.

Now to make my bike faster, then watch out Marty! ( #84 ) :-) See ya'll next race weekend.



September Endurance Race Weekend

Got to the track around 8:15 after checking into the hotel first. Jason, Jim & I got our bikes thru tech fairly quickly, so we decided to be nice and get Chris's GS thru tech also. ( Chris had some lame excuse about needing to work on Friday :-) ) We then headed to the hotel and jumped in the hot tub! Hotels RULE!!!

Friday morning we all meet back at the track and got ready for practice. I managed to turn 2:12's fairly consistently. :-( I was hoping that the Aug race time of 2:07 wasn't a fluke! Jim ran into a clearance problem ( see earlier story ) and Mike, Jason & I went to the hospital to see if he was all right, then call him a wuss for not racing w/ a broken collar bone like the pro's do & drive him back to the track. Mike took his F2 out for practice and managed to turn some 2:02's ( his goal was 2:05 ) He was VERY happy to have most of his speed back on the 600.

Saturday: We all meet for the riders meeting at 8:30, then signed up for our sprint races. I registered for the three Ultralight races. Jason managed to crash on the second lap of his last practice session. That is of course, after he had already put his money down on all his races for the day. Mike & I had fun racing each other in those races. The first race had some good action during the first 2 laps w/ Mike ( 192 ), 107 & I doing the passing battle. Unfortunately I got past them and sort-of check out on them. I looked back after passing 107 and couldn't see anyone for 3-4 turns behind me, while 229 ( Fausto ) was 100 yards or so infront of me. Not fun! I did manage to catch him and even put a pass on him in turn 6 w/ 2 laps left. He motored right on by me half way down the straight, bummer. I wasn't able to close the distance in time to make another pass before the checkered flag. I don't remember much about the 2nd race except that Mike & I fought it out again, he passed me once in turn 4 when I accidentally downshifted twice under braking and almost lost the rear end. 107 ended up checking out on both of us. I ended up being able to re-pass Mike, but don't remember the gory details...sorry. I think I need to keep a log of the races for next year's reports.

While we where waiting for the third race, I kidded Mike about letting him lead until the last lap, then passing him to take his position. Darned if that's how it ended up going! Mike thought I was playing with him during the race, but he was awful hard to keep up with and set up a pass. I was just glad I was able to back up all the trash talk. Marty #84, ended up crashing during the first race in turn 4 and he also broke his collar bone. Two pumpkins down. Chris will have to give the details on that crash. ( I think I heard some talk about him and two others forcing Marty off the track ) We had planned on racing Jason's bike for the endurance race, so Saturday night saw all of us working on Jason's bike. We managed to fabricate a replacement shift lever for his rear-set, we attached Chris's tach to replace Jason's totaled tach and straightened his twisted clip-ons. We got it thru tech around 9pm, yeah! I ended up buying an infra-red on bike lap timer from Brad LeBlanc, so we ended up zip-tie'ing & duct taping the unit to the bike too.

Sunday: Again the rider's meeting at 8:30. Mike took Jason's bike out for the first practice session & he ended up falling in love with the rear-sets. I managed to con Mike into letting me take his F2 out for the next practice session. What a difference! Blasting thru turn 1 at 135+ MPH -vs- the 115 MPH on the GS is very eye-popping! I took it around for 5-6 laps and managed to run a 2:06 as my fastest lap. On the last lap, A squirrel ran out onto the track at the entrance to turn one. I just managed to miss the little bugger and thank God, 'cause I had visions of an off-track excursion from hitting the little dude while leaned over at 130+.

The endurance race was very chaotic. We had almost nothing ready, Mike didn't know his grid position & we where rely'ing on the untested timer to tell the rider when to come in for a pit. Mike managed to run fairly consistent 2:07's on Jason's bike. Mike was pretty darn happy with those times, while was was anxiously waiting for my turn to see if I could do the same. Jason got on and managed to eat thru the brake pads during his 20 lap session. When he came in, Chris looked at the pads and said "Shit, the brakes are gone!" We scrambled to find a set of pads and luckily Mike had a set in his tool-box. Slapped the pads on and Chris went out to set 'em and run his 20 laps. Chris managed to turn a few 2:07's just before he crashed. He ended up lowsiding it in turn 3. The pipe & rightside clip-on got totaled and the tank got a good dent in it. On the plus side, my timer was unscathed! :-) That pretty much ended the festivities for the weekend. We hung around long enough to help Jason pick the bike up, then back to the cities to dream about all the modifications we are going to make for next year!
