Gastro posted 04-13-2001 06:45 AM CT (US)     
The air was cool, a gentle breeze wafted through the air. The team gathered up in there usual place, the druid ring in West Commons. Brief exchanges of "hello" and "good to see you again" and such nots where given. But they were truelly ready to depart on there mission. The lovely Druid of the group was in need of a new shroud, and we knew just the one to get. So we gathered around her, as she called up her magic and took us to the Dreadlands. Once there we quickly made our way to Frontiere Mountains, well, most of us. One Enchanter, whos name I wont mention, Kinda got turned around and made the trip alone. But all was not lost, for he did make it safely.

The team gathered around the entrance to the mine, of wich they were quite familiar with by now, and made a plan. They exchanged buffs, wished eachother good luck, and charged in to the depths of Nurga.

The trek into the bowels of the mines was uneventful, and safe. We rid Norath of a bunch of vile goblins. Once we reached the entrance to the Temple of Droga, we steadied or nerves, and carfully entered the evil temple.

Once in the Temple, it was easy going, slowly making our way to the dinner hall, seems we were not invited or welcome for the evening meal however, so we had to slay our would be dinner partners ( those Goblins are so rude )

The Warder came strolling in to see what all the comotion was, we killed him and took his Shroud. There was no debate as to who would don the great shroud. Our Druid accepted the shroud with a smile on her face.

We stayed in the dinnig hall and slew all Goblins that were brave enough to stand up to us. And we were victorious once again.

We did cringe a couple times when our noble Monk came close to death, but our vigilant Cleric and Druid kept him healed, while the Bard and Enchanter kept the Goblin occupied and the Warrior beat the living daylights out of him.

All in all it was a great night and we made killer experience and mucho loot. It was a very possitive experience for the now tired and worn group.

/OOC

Well, another great night last night. I LOVE our group. I am spoiled with our group. I find it hard to get in a group as good as ours, sometimes they can come close, but we are like a well oiled machine. We survive when death is certain. We prevail when failure is around the corner. We overcome all obsticles, and we do it with style!

Ok, there are the very few times when something goes real bad, but comeon, we handle sticky situations efficiently, sometimes with out verbal ( typed ) comunications, because we know our jobs, and we know others jobs so well.
