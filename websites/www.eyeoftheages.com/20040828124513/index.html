    Pre-Order & Character Creation

    I purchased the preorder disc today from my local GameSpot. The disc only allows you to generate your appearance, not assign stats, but you can save your work. The creation also happens on the well-known tutorial boat with full camera manipulation available using the mouse. I recommend getting this for no other reason than your character will start with Journeymen boots.
