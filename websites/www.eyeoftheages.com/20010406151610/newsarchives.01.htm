December 13, 1999 - Wild Round-up on The Weekend
Phew!! What a weekend it was!! Let's see if I can remember all the events. Saturday found us all logging in and guildless. The guilds were being reformed but that took time and we finally got our guild status back and our tags! Yay! We are no longer a part of the Unknown Guild.

Woooo!! Najena Successfull

Reforming our guild took place in Najena. Gamlok and Semoan were officially invited at this time as well. Gamlok is a shadow knight of the 19 season and Semoan, a warrior of the 33rd season. Welcome to our lil zoo..*grins*.

After the invites were finished, we went on to search for Najena. Our mission: to get a robe for our ever lovin' Sadyk.. With us on this trip was Shadox, from eht sdrawkcab guild, (The Backwards) and Daymender. We managed to get not only 1 Flowing Black Robe, but 3 Flowing Black Robes were gotten from the nights efforts. Xy received the second robe and the third robe went to Zed. The BoneCracker happened to spawn for us, and he dropped a barbed whip for Wuulf.
Trazdon also made an appearance wearing the Dark Circlet, which Xy had been searching. Najena was good to all this night.

Next, we decided to go to Cazic Thule's Temple, in search of the Crusader, who carries the ShadowKnight quest items and experience for all. Here in Cazic Thule we met Vanir, a good friend and enchanter and we asked him to join us in our ventures. We killed the Crusader but all he had was lizard meat..Oh well, another day we will come back for him. We then went to the Throne Room were we found the Tae Templar on the throne. The Tae Templar met his death and surrendering his lizardscale cloak.

The cloak was randomed and Bayla was the the winner of the random.

On Sunday, Shai, Bayla, Mistressia and I went to Guk to look for the ancient crocodile. We were joined a few hours later by Arithon. Finally the ancient spawned, and he dropped the gatorscale sleeves. So instead of new leggings, I have some new arms now. I did not get a screenshot of the ancient as I went link dead *sighs*. If anyone got a screenshot send it to me and I will post it.

Later that night found us in The Keep killing more pickclaws. Many people leveled this week-end and congrats to all who did!!! *cheers*

Well, I think that is all for the moment. Thanks to Sadyk for the Najena pics and Strive for the group shot in The Keep. I have some other things I will posting in the next day. So stay tuned =)

Good hunting to all!!
