Nameless Server

For those seeking to join Eye of the Ages, please read the following:

1. Eye of the Ages does not seek to be a large, powerful, or rich guild. If your primary purpose in Norrath is to increase in leveling or in acquiring magical items, then we will not be the right guild for you. There are many other fine guilds that could suit your needs better if they are as such.

2. If however, your primary desire in the game is to assist other adventurers, and to consistently character-play in a noble and honourable fashion, we warmly welcome you to join us.

3. Those seeking entrance into the guild are reviewed with the participation of all members, and every nomination received will be given careful consideration. As such, there could be a short delay while your nomination is reviewed.

4. Membership into the guild is not automatic. The candidate seeking admission will be required to have a supporter from within the guild. This is to ensure that only adventurers of good stock are invited into the guild.

June 1, 2000

As of this day membership to Eye of the Ages has been closed for an indefinite time. If you are seeking membership with us please check back with us from time to time or inquire within the game. In the meantime, grouping with us is also strongly encouraged as you will learn more about each other this way.

Thank you for your interest in joining us.
