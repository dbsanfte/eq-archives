November 30, 2000

Hail Eyes!

The last two weeks have been extremly eventful for most members of the guild. So full of accomplishments that I might actually fail to list them all. I will make a good try though however.

Regarding slayings we added Trakanon to the list this month. Sanctuary were our allies and they performed as required. So far Trakanon has fallen three times before the force of EotA and Sanctuary, and many more are to follow. Something to smile about.

Eye of the Ages the proceeded to down Venril Sathir, being the first guild to show Meail that other guilds indeed have the power and the will do slay Venril Sathir. After having killed him three times one must say that his drops have been most discouraging.

We also banished the Emerald Jungle dragon named Severilious two times until todays date. A very entertaining fight as well where Durck recieved a scale for his warrior Epic quest. Who is to receive the secondary scale is yet to be decided upon. Nonetheless the fights with Severilious are a blast, funniest fights that I ever had

As smaller news we did kill the disdainful Overking in Chardok multiple times for our clerics. It's a hard battle indeed but it is surely going smoother and smoother everytime now. We also killed Ragefire multiple times along with the General in Kithicor woods and the monk Epic boss mobs in Lake of ill Omen.

Congratulations to those who have completed their epic quests thus far:

Cleric: Nylan, Terv, Xandralia
Paladin: Moonwalker
Monk: Trinity, Lenardo
Rogue: Kolienu
Ranger: Tegian
Shaman: Seanlan


Also it's time to congratulate Durck on reaching 60 as did Seanlan early this month. Many are struggling in 59 right now and also quite a few in 54, just keep enjoying yourselves out there and the levels will come with time. Personally I am happy to be hitting 56 and if anyone happens to see the 56 pet spell Servan of Bones around I would be VERY interested in obtaining it.

All in all I think we could say that the Eyes really started rolling these last few weeks, it has been a blast and with Scars of Velious around the corner we are merely scraping off the tip of the iceberg.

Yours sincerely

Majico Boneherder

October 17, 2000 - A guest reporter makes a few statements
Well met travellers!

This is Majico reporting. It has indeed been a along time since dear brother Danbei update this journal. So I have taken it upon myself to spread some of the recent news. Even though I myself have been less active in the world of Norrath the Eye of the Ages have not. In fact you all seem to have been very busy indeed. So what’s new then?

The first thing that comes to mind is that or dear and old friend Moonwalker finally aquired his Fiery Avenger! A quite impressive weapon indeed and quite a tool in the hands of such a paladin. Congratulations Moonwalker! Although the last trip to Air led to some turbulent about how such raids are to be handled in the future, I do belive it was one of the most successful ones yet. Good work and well done all that participated!

The Eye of the Ages also killed that vermin Nagafen a few more times, and naturally the fearsome Lady Vox as well. We actually killed Vox twice and the last run at her showed exceptional patience and devotion from many members of the guild. But I fear now that we have outgrown those lizards and need to move on to bigger and better things. Already the members of EotA are sharpening their swords and picturing the death of Trakanon.

As far as character development it’s worth to mention that more people now have aquired the rank of level 60. Ailly followed Terv and made 60, so now EotA are supported by two High Priest/esses. Qumelwen also reached level 60 and became our first Enchanter to reach that level. (What are they called anyhow?) As I write this Seanlan may already be 60 with his two barbarian friends just hitting 58, two very impressive warriors indeed.

I would also like to congratulate Lenardo for aquiring both a Runed Fighters Staff and the robe of the Whistling Fist. I might just have to say that you look pretty darn spiffy mate.

Personally I’ve taken some time off Majico and playing my monk a bit more. But I will be returning to Majico now and then so that I will eventually manage to squeeze through 54.

Kayla was away for an extended period of time while moving in real life. Naturally the telecom tech people had to delay her return but as of today we have seen her back for real. Welcome back Kayla, you have been missed!

The guild as a whole is now undergoing some changes, we are taking a more professional look at how to handle our recruiting and our growth. We are also focusing on how to prepare for our future goals and conquers, while keeping the community feeling alive. More about this in the guild forum, please come and state your opinion.

Well, that’s all for now. Yours sincerely:

Majico Boneherder
September 27, 2000 - Current events and other silly stuff

This is your gnome reporter checking in again for another edition of the news. Since my last update, the Eyes have made a number of successful raids into the Planes and into the lairs of dragons. We've managed to slay Lord Nagafen on no less than three more occasions (how the heck you slay something three times is beyond me, maybe he was just faking it so we'd stop beating him senseless), and also slew Lady Vox for the first time. We'll forget about those other failed attempts, the last time I woke up feeling so badly was after a serious drinking binge in Halas. But, that's another story...



Our crew also paid visits to both the Plane of Air and the Plane of Fear over the last month. In the Plane of Air, we managed to make it as far as the fifth island, and slew the Keeper of Souls a few times while also trying to avoid serious cases of vertigo. Plane of Fear also went off without much of a hitch, and while we killed Cazic-Thule, yet again, there was something very special about this particular slaying...

The killing blow was delivered by a gnome.

The newest patch has brought a little excitement into the guild with many people scrambling to find make armor and find quest items. Personally, all I need in life is a life time supply of Gnomish Spirits (TM) and a bevy of beautiful women to keep me company, but if these trinkets make you happy, then I wish you all the best of luck in your endeavors.

Adso sent me a bit of personal news. Apparently the big lug has headed to Overthere and joined the Marines. He was kind enough to write me a letter from boot camp, and included some shots of his training.

We wish you the best of luck in learning how to take your head off, Adso. But, you may want to talk to your commander if he is sending you into battle armed with a fishing pole...

That's all for this edition, except to thank my two staff photographers Adso and Bender for the provided pictures. Now, I'm off to find myself a warm fireplace deep in Ak'anon with a potent drink and my trusty toolbox by my side. Until next time...

Danbei Hayami - World's smallest ogre and wearer of the Magical Undies

August 24, 2000 - A new reporter takes over the news
This is your roving gnome reporter in the field signing in for the first time. (Although why the heck I have to rove in a field instead of being behind a comfortable desk in Ak’anon is beyond me.) My first tidbit of news is to let you all know we have, over the summer, added some new slaves... err, members to our family. Everyone give warm welcomes to all the new recruits into the zoo. Don't tell them about the secret guild hazing, yet, though. I believe this time the initiation is "give a massage to a gnome", isn't it?

Since the last update, the Eyes made four Planes trips. The first trip was to Fear, where once again Cazic-Thule was slain. This guy has died so many times at our hands now, I wonder why anyone bothers to worship the wussy boy anymore. Ah well, some people will follow anything, I guess.

We also made our first trip to the Plane of Air, and while I couldn't be there, I understand that everyone had a great time, and on this first trip we managed to kill the Noble Dojorn and the Keeper of Souls. Next time we go there things should pr etty much be a breeze. (Get it? Air? Breeze? Bah!)

The next trip was to Hate, where Innoruk finally met his maker at the hands the Zoo. And, about danged time if I do say so, myself. I must say, though, he's smarter than Cazic in the fact that he's managed to hide from us a few times now.

And the final trip was to Fear again. In Fear, Kayla got a Vermiculated Tunic, and then decided to switch classes from druid to ranger. Why on Norrath she would want to do that is beyond me, but to each her own, I guess.

Possibly the biggest new, though, is that we have finally taken down a dragon! Big, bad old Nagafen suddenly doesn't seem like such the tough lizard to crack to the Eye crew. The next lizard we take on is Shard! Well, maybe he gets a break and we'll take down a different one, instead. At any rate, something cold-blooded is going to meet its doom at our hands again sometime soon.

And finally, the Eyes, with the help of a couple members of Unity of Spirits, went after that mean old Freeport Militia leader Sir Lucan, and kicked his butt royally. As a result of this, Tellurn is now the proud owner of his very own Soulfire. And he looks quite dashing with it, I must admit, even if he is a smelly dwarf.

There have been a number of queries about who the guild officers are lately, and with good reason since many officers have retired or cut down on their time in Norrath considerably. The list of current officers includes: Terv, Bayla, Majico, Caladar, Qumelwen, Beshaba, Calbik, and Danbei.

Kayla also wanted me to remind everybody that there is currently an error that appears once you submit the changes to your profile. The form is still being sent, so there is no need to resend it. She's got the problem on her "to-do" list to work out. Until then, make sure when you submit the profile, you send it only once, and that your name appears on the profile, otherwise it will be trashed. Thanks.

Signing off until next time,

Danbei Hayami - World’s Smallest Ogre and wearer of the Magical Undies
July 13, 2000 - New Look.... and other news
Well, a lot has been happening since the last update a month ago. I apologize for the lack of updates. First of all, what do you thing of our new look ? Let's all thank Shard for our new logo, he did a great job on our new header. There is a new page that has been added to our roster, the character profile page. Just click on the member name to bring up the profile on the individual. Updates to your profile will be made from the link that is located on this page. I think that is all on what is new on the site.

The month of June also brought us some changes in our membership, as we invited a few of our close friends from Fires of Wrath into our family, as well as adding the exceptional people from the Council of Redivivus. The Eye's would like to give a warm welcome to you all... *cheers*

The first 2 weeks in June found us deep in the Plane of Fear and the Plane of Hate. We killed the Maestro of Rancor; then in the Plane of Fear, we killed Dracoliche and Cazic Thule for the first time. Everyone there did a great job, many lessons were learned by all; and I, for one, am looking forward to the day when we add Innoruuk to our list.

The 30's and 40's crowd also made a number of trips into the Temple of Droga, and have found their experiences there to be very fun and exciting, as well as a great chance to work on their skills as a team. Not all forays into Droga's were successful, but on at least one trip everyone managed to come out of the dungeon with a very nice item, including a few never heard of before by the Eyes.

This weekend will find us back in Hate for Friday, Saturday, and Sunday, so if you are interested please let me know.. Please see the Planes board for any other information, and review the looting if you need to. Lets make this a great weekend. Watch out Maestro, the Eye's are coming back for you!

As a final note, Danbei is taking over the writing of the news on the front page, so if you have any accomplishments, events, stories, or any news at all that you would like to let him know about, you can email him at Danbei or click on the news link in the navagation bar.



Kayla
