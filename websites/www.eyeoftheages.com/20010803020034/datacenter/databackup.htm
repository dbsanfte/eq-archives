    Data & Webpage Backup

    Website pages and data files are is jeopardy from a variety of sources, mostly human. A Webmaster may inadvertently corrupt and upload a page or an entire site. And on very rare occasions a hard drive fails, taking with it all files on the server. Fortunately as equipment and software reliability improves, total data loss is rare. Unfortunately it can and does happen.

    To insure the safety of our client's data in the event of an equipment failure, DaySite uses an automated backup systems. All client files are saved to a separate storage device on a nightly basis. These files are then kept for 4 days before discarding them. Should the need arise, our clients can also request copies of their backup files.

    Additional Backup Protection

    For clients with more critical data, DaySite can provide additional backup protection. Multiple daily backups or hourly backup can be arranged. And if your data cannot be lost, we will place your site on its own dedicated Webserver with continual redundant backup systems, with data integrity checks. Just contact DaySite if you have special backup needs.
