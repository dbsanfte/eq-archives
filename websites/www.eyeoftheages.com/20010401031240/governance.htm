The Governance

Any member of Eye of the Ages is an honourable being; and should be treated as such and given the necessary respect and leeway to make decisions with intelligence, wisdom and foresight.We are a guild governed by principles intended for thinking people; not a guild governed by rules that must be blindly adhered to.

This does not mean that we operate without rules; rather that we will operate with the expectation of applying good, common sense, and a distinct sense of honour and righftulness in our dealings with other adventurers.
Objectives

To create an order of adventurers of unquestionable virtue and character, and in so doing, all adventurers in Norrath will be able to look upon any member as a good adventurer of the highest order, one that can be trusted upon to render assistance and help.

Rules of Conduct

Adventuring

1. Participation in any of the following activities by any member of the Eye of the Ages will not be tolerated, whether for vengeful or any other purposes:

Kill Stealing: this is the deliberate snatching of a monster or being that is currently being engaged by other adventurers.
Loot stealing: this is the taking of monies or items off corpses of beings that do not belong to the group with which you are in.
Training: this is the deliberate luring of monsters to another adventurer for the sole intention of harming that adventurer.
Verbal abuse: at no point in time is a member of the Eye of the Ages to use uncouth language on another adventurer.

2. Where there is space, you are encouraged to give preference to fellow Eye of the Ages members in grouping together for common activities.

3. While adventuring, you are expected to display the highest level of moral conduct.
1. Politeness to other adventurers
2. Respect for their adventuring space and the area in which they may be camping

4. There will be no abusive or uncouth behaviour demonstrated while participating in the guild chat channel. One of the common failings of other guilds is that the healthy debate on the guild chat often denigrates into mud-slinging and verbal abuse hurled at other members. Anyone found guilty of breaking this rule will be required to render apologies to the aggrieved persons.

5. Members are required to display his/her guild affiliation in Norrath. If any member wishes to disguise his/her specific seasoning and profession, this may do so by setting the "roleplaying" flag on.

Use of third-party programs and other exploits

1. As Verant does not support or allow the use of third party utilities in the game itself, all members are expected to follow this regulation as well. The use of an in game bug to duplicate items will be cause for immediate dismissal from the guild.

Membership

1. All beings of virtue, honour and character are eligible to become a member of Eye of the Ages. These include beings traditionally thought to be evil, including adventurers from the dark races. The guild governance confirms the invitation to all adventurers with virtue and the willingness to uphold fair and honourable existence with others, regardless of race or profession.

2. However, in order that a new member does not feel out of place, it will be a preference that all new adventurers seeking membership into the guild be the ninth season of profession. This means that all adventurers petitioning for membership will be measured on character first and foremost, and this leveling requisite second, and all adventurers will be strongly encouraged to meet this requisite before petitioning.

3. Members may nominate adventurers of good stock for membership into the guild. The candidate will be required to know the guild governance, in particular the charter and rules of conduct before seeking membership.

4. Membership into the guild will not be automatic however. The candidate seeking admission will be required to have a supporter from within the guild. This is to ensure that only adventurers of good stock are invited into the guild.

5. Once the candidate has been confirmed on his/her eligibility, and approved by the Founder and co-founder, the founder or co-founder will meet the candidate, and formally extend the invitation to said individual.

Leadership

Leadership within the guild is essentially from two persons, the Founder and the Co-Founder. There will be a group of Elders who will provide counsel, advice, support, and moderation if ever necessary to both co-leaders. In addition as well, the core responsibilities of the Elders will encompass:

Assessing of new candidates for membership
Providing a middle layer of leadership
Organization of Guild Events

The Governance

1. The articles documented in this Governance will be subjected to a periodic review of once every month, wherever changes may be considered and effected were appropriate.

2. All members may propose for changes to the guild governance.

3. All requests for changes should be submitted in writing to the scribe. Matters to do with style of representation that do not change in effect the article will be effected immediately. Matters that require a change to a rule will need to be considered upon and voted upon where necessary.
