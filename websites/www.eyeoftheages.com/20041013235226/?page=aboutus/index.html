About us


Through all the Ages there has been the Eye. Chosen protectors of the Eye’s great purpose have always been those loyal and courageous few who keep its secrets and herald its work. In times past and places without name that purpose has brought the Eye into the light for the masses to see, although that is not its home. In most recent memory its banners were called to bring together a group of stalwart adventurers so that they could form their own great powers. Then with a terrible shock and a soothing remedy the Eye returned to darkness so that it could watch and choose its time again. That day has come; Norrath is a shattered image of itself. Much knowledge and power have been lost to the world but the Eye has seen and forgets not where they are hidden. The banners of the Eye of the Ages have unfurled and leading the charge is Sedius, our counselor and commander. He shall show us the way to those buried things and the Eye shall not retreat until the world is again restored.

Just as the Eye belongs in a seat of darkness overlooking the ebb and flow of time, so do we, its members belong in Norrath. Founded as a new guild in 2004 by Mike McDaniel, the Eye of the Ages (EotA) is a private Everquest 2 guild and, simply put, a gathering of friends. Consisting almost entirely of former Everquest Live players from the Nameless server, EotA draws members from the original Eye and many more from its children, Shock of Swords and Remedy.

We believe in loyalty, camaraderie, and excitement. If we are merely a gathering of friends; why then is our guild a force to be reckoned with? Take a look at our member’s list for a moment and I think you’ll see.

Stefi: If you never crossed this ranger's path then you have surely missed one of the server's great treasures. Was it only possible that she were as competent as she is kind, this wood elf set foot on the Nameless soil and witnessed its very first dawn.

Joust: One of the servers most well respected and knowledgeable druids, his halfling was constantly sought after for all manner of help.

Sedius: A barbarian shaman of great power and skill, his might rivaled that of any other. However, when paired with our next member his talents were truly brought to light.

Windeyes: Previously Yetmasi of the original Eye of the Ages, his monk made furious use of fist, foot and tail to the terror of all his enemies. These four, and all of our members, enjoy nothing more than making the very best use of their talents and excelling in everything they do. To that end, our goal is to enter a position of prominence among the guilds of whichever city we choose to affirm our allegiance through tireless questing and raiding while never forgetting our purpose. Eye of the Ages: Loyalty, Camaraderie, Excitement.
