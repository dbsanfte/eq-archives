BIG BIG BIG NEWS! (Good and Bad)

First the bad news:
The Lords of the Undead, our trusted allies, our friends, our
brothers and sisters in evil, is sadly soon to be no more.
LotU was truly a bastion of the Quellious server.  Many young
necromancers (including myself) grew up learning from, and 
idolizing most of LotU's members.  It was a classy guild,
comprised of classy people, and it will truly be missed.
