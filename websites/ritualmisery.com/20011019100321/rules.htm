The Rules of the Guild

 


I. Rules of Recruitment and Membership

1. Ritual Misery shall preserve its evil purity by only allowing 
completely evil beings into its ranks. By birthright, Dark Elves, Iksar, 
Ogres, and Trolls are acceptable characters to recruit, providing that 
they meet the remaining standards of the guild. By trade, 
Necromancers and Shadowknights are also acceptable characters to 
recruit, again, assuming the remaining standards of the guild are met.

2. Half Elves, High Elves, Wood Elves, and Dwarves shall never be 
allowed to attain membership to Ritual Misery. These races are not 
only disgusting pieces of vermin, scattered about Norrath; they are 
also incapable of being  completely evil, no matter how many evil 
deeds they perform. Light Elves and Dwarves shall always remain 
at the end of our blades and under the wrath of our magic.

4. Humans are not totally incapable of being consumed by the 
darkness. However, because of our past experiences with these 
weak minded, weak bodied, and all around weak beings; humans 
will be required to achieve a special level of evil. First, and foremost, 
only human Necromancers and Shadow Knights will be allowed into
the guild. Second, any human Shadow Knight or Necromancer 
seeking membership, shall first attain the scorn of ALL non-evil cities 
in Norrath, including their own city. This is to ensure loyalty to the 
guild and the darkness. Third, humans must increase faction with
the nobles of Neriak (murder a countless amount of Halflings), 
increase faction with the...eh...nobles of Oggok (murder thousands 
of Lizardmen), and increase faction with the......nobles of Grobb 
(murder tons of Froglok's and Fungi). Fourth, Humans must bring 
the meat/parts of dead high elves, wood elves, half elves to one of 
the RM officers or leaders. Fifth, any human who wants to join
Ritual Misery, must also have an existing guild member sponsor them. 
Beyond the initial sponsorship, humans must fight alongside an officer 
of the guild to prove their skills and loyalty to the guild, and to evil itself. 
Only after all these requirements are met, will a human be allowed to 
reap the benefits of joining our guild. 

5. Though prototypical Gnomes are quite putrid, a few Gnomes  have
earned the respect of the elders of Ritual Misery.  Because of this, the
Gnomish Death Squad was started.  The GDS is a murderous bunch
of Gnomes that share the views of Ritual MIsery and is led by the
mighty Mordox.  Final approval of a Gnome attempted to join RM must
be given by Mordox.

6. Erudites are not ideal allies of Ritual Misery, however, they are not 
quite as repulsive as humans. Erudites have secluded themselves 
from the light skinned races, and with their high intelligence and 
general casting abilities, the Eruds have earned a modest (very 
modest) amount of respect from the elders of RM. Of course this 
respect only applies to the Heretics and Reavers of Paineel. 
Residents of Paineel interested in joining this guild will be looked 
upon as an evil character, since they are KoS to almost all of 
Antonica, and are welcome in Neriak.  Other classes, excluding 
paladin, will be allowed to join a mercenary group, if deemed 
worthy.  Mercs will have to become KoS to 2 light skinned cities on 
Antonica, Rivervale being one of them.  All Mercs will be under the 
command of Rypaton.

7. Barbarians, at first glance, may just seem to be large humans. 
However, the elders of Ritual Misery have fought countless battles, 
side by side, with these less than smart beings. Their simplistic, 
uncivilized ways seems to have sheltered them from the arrogant 
ways that Humans, Halflings, and other Light races have adopted. 
We have witnessed many Barbarians murdering the Dwarves and 
Wood Elves (their mortal enemies) in the Ocean of Tears, simply
for the fun and profit of it. There have also been numerous times 
that Barbarians have aided members of Ritual Misery in killing Druids, 
Rangers, and Treants. We have also discovered that Barbarians 
have a natural bloodlust and are inclined to be filled with hatred. All 
things considered, Barbarians we have encountered have been 
marginally evil by nature. BUT, this is not quite evil enough to join 
Ritual Misery. Barbarians that intend to join our guild must achieve
the following tasks, before joining: First, Barbarians must become 
Killed on Sight in 2 of the 4 "good" cities in Antonica. One of these 
cities MUST be Rivervale.  Furthermore, Barbarians that join Ritual 
Misery will be Mercenaries and will be under the command of 
Rypaton.

8. Halflings, under normal circumstances, are not allowed into the 
guild. However, Halfling rogues murder their own kind in cold blood. 
By this aspect, we share a common interest. There are a few deeds 
that must be done before a halfling rogue will be allowed in the guild. 
First, Halflings must prove their loyalty to us by becoming Killed on 
Sight in all the non-evil cities in Norrath. Second Halfling Rogues 
must obtain a Mask of Deception, and keep the Dark Elf Illusion 
active at all times.

9. All non-Dark Elf rogues in the guild must obtain a Mask of 
Deception and keep the Dark Elf illusion up at all times. They must 
also become Killed On Sight in all non-evil cities.

II. Rules of General Gameplay

1. A member of Ritual Misery must always help his or her guild mates.
This does not mean you have to give your last copper piece to another
member of the guild so they can get a new item. Nor does it mean you
have to take a 4 hour trip across Norrath to help somebody camp an
item. The meaning of this rule is as simple as it sounds. If somebody 
needs your help, and you have the time and means to help them...do it. 
If a younger member asks for your advice, take a few seconds to 
answer their questions. This also means that if you are in battle with a 
fellow guild member, and you can give them a tip or two on fighting
tactics, please do so.

2. A member of Ritual Misery must never turn their backs on, or 
criticize another member for anything. For example, if a member you 
are fighting with, fears a mob in a dungeon....instead of saying 
something like "YOU IDIOT, WHERE DID YOU LEARN TO FIGHT?", 
explain why its probably not a good idea to fear a mob in a dungeon. 
Then promptly run for the nearest zone, gate, or camp, because you 
are about to die. Remember, nobody starts up their first character 
and automatically knows everything... .they must learn by trial and 
error and other people's advice. Criticizing others does nothing but 
weaken the guild...conversely, helping others and offering advice 
strengthens the guild.

3. If a member of Ritual Misery has a problem that can not be solved 
by themselves, they are to talk with an officer or leader to try and 
come up with a solution. For example, if a member has a problem 
with another player in the guild that can not be solved between 
them, they should send a tell to any of the officers and explain the 
situation.   At no time will there be an argument or anything of the
like  between two members over guild chat. Doing so is grounds 
for immediate guild removal.  If such a problem arises, it will be 
taken care of in tells and with the aid of an officer if necessary. 

4. No member of Ritual Misery will Kill Steal from other players, 
purposefully train other characters with the intent of killing them, 
beg from people in or outside of the guild, or ninja loot. Also 
spamming stupid things in a zone and just being a general piece 
of crap will not be tolerated.. These acts are inexcusable! They 
give the guild a bad name and will be rectified by guild removal! 
Remember, members of the guild represent themselves as well 
as the rest of the guild.

5. Members of Ritual Misery are encouraged  to group with other
players from the guild in their level range (whenever possible). 
This is to promote closeness and friendship within the guild, and 
to make guild groups for guild raids, item camps, and planes raids, 
the best that they can be.

6. Members of Ritual Misery are expected to be at all raids and 
events if they are online.  This of course does not apply if there 
is a level or member count restriction on the raid.

6. All members must kill Travis Two Tone at least once it their EQ 
life. This is mostly because I like watching him bleed.

7. All members must understand that BLOOD MAKES THE GRASS 
GROW...punishment for not understanding this is suffocation by
Uhgly.
