Raid on Mistmoore - Wednesday May 16, 6pm PT

Friday, April 27th-

More Events Coming Up...But We Need to Know About Them!

We intend to post all upcoming Raids and other Guild Events, but we request that you drop us a short note at news@ritualmisery.com with the following information, so we don't have to guess:

Point of Contact: Who is in charge? Who do they need to contact if they have any questions?
Type of Event: Such as a "Raid on the City of Mist", "Research Event", etc.
Location: Where all attendees are expected to assemble. "SRo Wiz Port", "West Commons, at Nektulos Zone", etc.
Date and Time: This means The Month, Day of the Week, and Date, and the Time AND a Time Zone.
Level Restrictions (if any): 46+, mid 30's, All Levels, etc.
URLs for Sign-Up Sheets (if any): This is the Message Board URL, if there is one for the event.

We appreciate your cooperation on this point. The way I figure it, if I don't see it on the EVENT CALENDAR, I can't be sure it's a "go". There has been more than one aborted Raid in the past, and it's good to make sure there's a green light.
