Mercenaries
 
Through the years, Ritual Misery has fought side by side with
many different races.  Some of them demonstrated just how
truly inferior their race is (Wood Elves, Half Elves, and our 
Albino cousins for example).  On the other hand, some of the 
other inferior races have earned the slightest bit of respect 
from RM.  For example the impressive casting abilities and
intelligence level of Erudites and the brute strength and 
ruthlessness of Barbarians.  Recently a few elite members of 
these lesser races have approached the Seraphs and Ritualists
of RM and pledged their allegiance to us.  For the most part we 
laughed in the faces of these lesser beings (and on a few 
occasions slit their throats). However there were a select few
that impressed us so much, that we couldn't deny their skills or
evilness, or what they could do for the guild. In these cases
we gave them an opportunity to show their loyalty to Ritual
Misery, by killing leaders of their own race, and sharing
information with us that will come in handy when we eventually
take over their home cities.

Once they have shown they are loyal, skillful, and evil enough
join our ranks, they are to follow the normal rules of the guild
except during raids they will fall under the command of our
original mercenary Rypaton O'Booty. Rypaton has proven his
skills, loyalty, and ruthlessness, 10 times over.

The mercenary groups will be limited to 2 full groups of lvl 40
or higher.  No Wood Elf, High Elf, Half Elf, or Halfling will
ever be allowed in.  No Druid, Paladin, or Ranger will ever be
allowed in Ritual Misery. Only on rare instances will a Dwarf
cleric or warrior be allowed in.  This will require A LOT of 
work to show their loyalty to Ritual Misery, our allies, and
evil in general.   

To begin the evaluation process, a mercenary wannabe must 
first prove themself to an Arcane Ritualist of the guild, and then
take the blessing of said Ritualist to one of the Dark Seraphs 
for final evaluation.  Quests and tests of loyalty are not 
uncommon during this process.
