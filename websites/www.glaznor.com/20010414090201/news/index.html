Souls 1, Cazic Thule 0!

The time came for the Souls to take on the Plane of Fear, and Verant Interactive, in their infinite wisdom and compassion, had patched the servers once again the day before. Luckily for the Souls, the Plane was not completely cleared the day before, so our time had come! Cazic Thule was UP! So, armed with a list of willing competitors, and a large dose of courage, we gathered at the Portal to Fear.

Eventually.

After a bit of delay while groups were rearranged due to last-minute changes, we managed to scrounge another enchanter (big thanks are due to Llissa of Clan Impact for all her help) and convinced our pull team of Tolstoy (independent) and Rykar (Nore Edelo) to join us once again, along with Sheqessa (Norrathian Knights), Handor (Justifiers), and 25 or so Souls. Two by two and three by three, most of us ran to the camp spot, and exactly three hours after starting, we were rezzed, revved and ready to fight. Yes, dragging corpses around Cazic Thule was more difficult than we thought.

 

Cazic Thule says, "BRUTO!"

Cazic Thule says, "SOREFIST!"

LOADING, Please wait...

 

Not much to say about the main bulk of the raid, as the pulling was good and swift, the fighting well organised, Gart's bare butt was ever-present (even if it changed colour sometimes) and the few necessary Ressurections were clean and painless. Well, except for the single Total Party Wipeout we had, which was very quickly recovered from. After this, we remembered to kill off all the mobs' pets rather than let them aggro Cazic again (thanks for the idea to Llissa, and the execution of pets was well done by Creeping).

After about 7 hours of clearing the Plane, it was time to take down Cazic's Avatars Terror, Dread and Fright, which involved us running around from corner to corner of the zone, kicking the sleepy bears when they needed it, and sacrificing Darkded more times than had to, simply because he likes the attention of Gods. We had to log a couple of times to be safe, but safe we were. With Cazic's avatars safely buried, we turned to the Amygdalan Temple for the first time.

Clearing the temple floor by floor, with more necros eating Harmtouch for us - see, they are good for something! (thanks to Briladven, Nalthek and Darkded) - Tolstoy kiting, and Rykar peeling, we managed to clear the Temple smoothly, with more nice L3wtz dropping for us. So we ran around again, formed an orderly queue, and buffed up.

We were ready to take on the God of Fear!

 

Fobben says, 'CAZIC THULE! >:x'

Cazic Thule says, 'FOBBEN!'

 

and the battle commenced. Yes, we had some deaths, we had some LinkDeaths (four of the High Council, sharing the same connection, went at the same time), but the important death was the Big Mudball himself!

Some survived the monster fight with CT, and some managed to run back inside fast enough to then find and take out his friend, the Wraith of the Shissar, who only spawns when Cazic Thule dies, and we somehow managed to take down the Wraith (lvl 55) as well, with only a few more deaths, due to some great tankage.

In all, it was a great raid, we completely cleared the Plane of Fear, and inside our booked time too. Some decent loot dropped from mobs, although we got no fancy breastplates this time, and of course we got God Loot, which was allocated by the High Council. Ratinga and Kantia got some of the hardest parts for their Epic Quests (so they better make use of them!), Kelval, Nedd and Briladven got some fancy goods to show off, and I think we all had a great time. We did very well with only two clerics and two or three enchanters!

Thanks to all who turned up, especially those guests whose presence was certainly felt and appreciated.
