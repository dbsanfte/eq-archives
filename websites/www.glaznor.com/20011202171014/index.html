The Good, the Bad and the Fugly (Gart Egilsson - House of Blessing)

Quite a weekend for the Souls. I think it's going to take us a week just to recover.

The Good

We smashed through Fear on Sunday. We got a little too aggressive with the pulling at one point and bit off more than we can chew, but frankly there's good wipeouts and there's bad wipeouts - and that was a good wipeout. So, we got back together and went at it.

We were motivated by our eagerness to kill the Wraith of the Shisser. Yes folks, that's right. The Shisser was up when we zoned in. Breaking our usual clearing patterns, we reversed tracks a bit and went North. And the Shisser went down like a little punk.

Then because we wanted to end Yizzy's incessant whining, we cleared to the Broken Golem so Yizzy could obtain part of his epic. He spawned the Enraged Golem and in a perfectly executed fight, we took down the Enraged Golem. Not a single casualty, proper aggro the whole time, really our best fight yet in terms of smoothness. In all seriousness, grats to Yizzy, who now joins Ratinga and Shalimar in the 3l33te club of guys with dead skins on them.

Loots basically were lousy, except a guest Shadow Knight walked in and came out pretty loaded. Yes Shintou, we did pull the temple. amy tendril - Seri cleric belt - Ressur cryosilk choker - Kinity (really crappy item) flux axe - Dlmystic flux axe - Rodov monk gloves - rotted natilius shield - Hookups necro arms - Syner necro boots - Syner sk arms - Graffyx sk bracer - Graffyx sk breastplate - Graffyx wiz bracer - Teca wiz hat - Teca wiz gloves - Teca

Epic Drops - Nomand (Shissar Head) and Yizzy (Enraged Golem)

The Bad

This one is pretty bad. Not much to say. After another 70 hour camp, Ragefire spawned for Annea early in the morning. We thought we had plenty for the fight, but apparently we didn't. We lost the fight, and Annea suffered the worst thing that probably could have happened in this game. I'm sure all of us are second-guessing this one. I'm sorry for Annea, and for all the folks who spent so much freaking time in Sol B. After a little break, I expect to see Souls flock to help Annea camp and/or keep her company if she chooses to restart this thing. "Sucked ass" was a phase heard in /guildsay, and probably sums things up fairly well.
