Guild Charter
What is Norrath's Hope about?

Norrath's Hope recruits members level 10 and above. 

Norrath's Hope works very hard to create an enjoyable, friendly atmosphere for all members. We are more than a guild, we are friends and family. Everyone in Norrath's Hope is equal, regardless of level or rank in the guild. We are here to help each other and have a good time!

There will be no disrespecting other members in the guild. repeated offenses will be cause for removal from the guild. This is our home, and no one gets disrespected in their own home!

On the message board there is a place to post all items you wish to sell within the guild for a discounted price. this will be where our bartering is done!
