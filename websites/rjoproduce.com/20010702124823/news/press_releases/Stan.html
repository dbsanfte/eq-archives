Stan Ford leads RJO Web Development

FRESNO, CA—Stan Ford, long time member of the RJO team, will be posting current news on the RJO website.  Stan will continue to be the translator for RJO's Latin American business.

Founded in 1993 by brothers Rob and John O’Rourke, RJO Produce Marketing offers comprehensive sourcing, distribution and category management services for tree fruit, strawberries, citrus, grapes and melons grown around the world.  The firm has earned both the Red Book “Business Character Award” and the Blue Book “Trading Member” status.  For complete information on RJO Produce Marketing, visit www.rjoproduce.com or call 559/447-3100.
