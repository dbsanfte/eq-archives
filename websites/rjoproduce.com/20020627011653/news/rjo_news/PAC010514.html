RJO Continues On Track as Field Rep for Customers

by Rand Green
excerpted from The Produce News May 14, 2001


Fresno, CA – RJO Produce Marketing has no immediate plans to replace Ray Spinelli and Todd Klassen who recently left to form their own sales company, K&S Sales in Sultana, CA.  “Our strength is in our inspection program,” said John O’Rourke, an owner of RJO along with his brother Rob O’Rourke.  So rather than hire more people on the sales desk, “we’re going to get more inspectors and have more people looking at fruit,” he said.

The company will also be expanding its application of technology in order to improve its efficiencies, he said.

He does not expect any reduction in business volume, nor will customers see any reduction in the level of service as a result of the personnel turnover, he stated.  On the contrary, “we have had customers we’ve been doing business with for 10 to 12 years.  I think if anything, we will be able to do a better job for them, just using the technology and the knowledge that we have.”

One immediate impact of the turnover is that Rob O’Rourke, “instead of being in the background, is going to be more in the forefront actually on the sales desk” once again.

“One of Rob’s strengths,” said his brother, “is that he has always had an eye to the future.”  That is a valuable commodity in a business such as produce which tends to be “focused on today and trying to do today’s business,” he said.

Although Rob O’Rourke will now be helping to manage some of the day-to-day activities of the business to a greater degree than he has been recently, he will also continue to travel and focus on customers as well as to maintain that broader vision for the future of the business, John O’Rourke said.

The recent changes within the company do provide “an opportunity to evaluate the way we do business and try and find the most efficient, best way to do it,” Mr. O’Rourke continued.  “That is something we are definitely doing.”  But is it something “we are constantly doing anyway,” he added.  “So it is really an opportunity to take it one step further.”

RJO will continue “to focus on what we do best, and that is service our customers and essentially be their buying office on the West Coast,” he said.  “We will [continue to] be their eyes and ears in the field, which is what we have always done. … We are going to continue to be the field rep for our customers and relay market information on a day-to-day basis to them so that they have the information they need to make the decisions the need to make.”

RJO has become increasingly involved in category management over the last several years.  “One thing we have been doing with the category management, “Mr. O’Rourke said, “is taking it one step further,” by “trying to fine-tune what category management is for the different customers whose programs we have” and then determine “how to best utilize” each customer’s category management data and “tie it back into our procurement practices … to make our customers’ job easier.”

The company is now in a position to take “some hard core data that we’ve collected over the last couple of years” and use it to help those customers make better decisions.

On the technology side, he said, “we need to find out the best areas that we can focus on” to use technology to “help us and ultimately our customer.”

There has been some redistributing of responsibilities among the various personnel at RJO in recent weeks.  Matt Ruegge, “who has been doing a lot of category management work for us” since coming on board a year ago, will now have “more of a presence on the sales side,” Mr. O’Rourke said.

Mr. Ruegge’s category specialist position will be expanded “to actively support” the sales side of the company and “tie the two together,” he said.  “What we are really looking for him to do is to help bridge … the category management information and data that we have” and tie it back to the customers, so they can compare what they did last year with current information on markets, timing and availability.

The whole concept, according to Mr. O’Rourke, is to provide customers with tools that will help them make smarter decisions.
