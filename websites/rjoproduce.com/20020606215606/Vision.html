RJO Blueprint for Success

Vision

To be the very best and most successful marketing partner in select produce categories in the US retail food industry.

Mission

To help our clients optimize business results through the most sophisticated understanding of the key performance drivers in select categories.

Company Philosophy

We believe our long-term value is founded on our core values, culture and the manner in which each individual reflects those in their day to day activities.

RJO is dedicated to building long-term, sustainable, mutually beneficial, value-adding business relationships.  All energies of the company should be spent on activities that move us in that direction.  Activities focused on delivering short-term results, or results that benefit RJO to the detriment of our clients, are discouraged.

Our core values provide guidance in all situations, but especially those situations where uncertainty prevails:

    Integrity

We will exercise the highest degree of integrity in the conduct of our business.

 

    Partnership

We will build long-term mutually beneficial relationships with out customers and suppliers.

 

    Continuous Improvement

We will ceaselessly seek to improve our services, our professional expertise and development, our competitiveness and our profitability.

 

    Employee Participation

We will work as a team.  We will treat our employees with dignity and respect.  We believe in mutual loyalty and commitment to the accomplishment of our objectives.
