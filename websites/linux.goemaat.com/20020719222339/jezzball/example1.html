Jezzball Example 1

Last updated September 15, 1999

To start out you create the stovepipes. To do that without losing a life on these easy levels, do the following:

Place your vertical wall builder on the line on the right side of the play area:

When you click, the bottom wall will immediately form, and the top wall will start heading for the top of the play area. Now the next time you click, ONLY THE BOTTOM wall will start forming. This is because the top wall from your first click has not reached the top yet. So place your wall builder with enough space to create a channel two-lines wide and click again:

Now you are ready to trap a ball. This is the real trick to Jezzball. The blue wall (the one that travels down when vertical and right when horizontal) is stronger than the red. If the blue wall hits an atom head-on, it will trap it. If the red wall hits an atom head on, you will lose a life. Now that we have a stovepipe made, if we center the arrow on the left line (there are two lines running down the stovepipe), there is no way we can lose a life. This lets us trap an atom with the space above it open and we can close the board without a single open square on it. Your timing must be perfect to trap a ball and leave it open, but if you move up one you can either close or trap it and still be guaranteed not to lose a life.

Here you can tell that I missed it the first time (the bottom is closed off), but I didn't lose a life (still 2) and I was able to nab it the second time. You can see that even though the ball was directly under the center of the wall builder, it got closed off anyway. If your arrows had been centered on the right line, you would have lost a life. Now the atom cannot move and will wait paitently for you to close off the other one.

When I have a ball trapped but open, I prefer to get the game over quickly, as time counts for points also. To do this, i create wider stovepipes that are more likely for the ball to get into. If you start near the bottom, you can get several lines out before the top wall reaches the top.

Then I trap the atom in one of the larger boxes and widdle it down to nothing. Just be sure you don't close off enough of the board to equal 75%, or the level will end and you will get a very SMALL bonus.

Widdling down a box like this takes some practice and timing. What I try to do is place the arrows centered on whichever way is smaller, so that the wall will be as short as possible. Also, if I am going vertical, I try and position the arrow on the side that has the ball hitting the bottom. That gives you a better chance since the blue wall is stronger. I click as soon as I see the ball hitting that spot. My reactions are just slow enough to let the ball pass by and to get it fast enough so that the ball will not come by again before the wall is finished. I usually count the ball hitting the surface (the one my arrow is near) in my head "1, 2, 3, 1, 2, THREE".

Once you get to this point (everything is trapped in a channel that is two wide), there is absolutely no reason you should die. Just remember, keep you arrow on the top or left line of the two-line channel. Above you can see how I am about to slice the little area in half. As it turns out, the ball got trapped. Now as long as we keep the arrow on the top row (for vertical), we cannot lose a life:

Now for the finale, we close off the one we left open, and finish level 1 with 99 percent! (not much time though because of the screen captures...) Here I keep the arrow on the left side, which just so happens to be directly above the atom. If the atom was trapped on the right side of the stovepipe, it wouldn't matter. The arrow should remain centered on the left line.
