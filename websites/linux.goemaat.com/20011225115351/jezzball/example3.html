Jezzball Example 3

Last updated September 15, 1999

Welcome to Level 16! Here you have no chance of making a channel without losing a life, so you had better plan on it. Here you can see I am about to make my second stovepipe. Atoms are obviously going to kill the wall that is going up, but the one on bottom is safe and will be completed. You can also see how perfectly I closed off the first stovepipe. At level 16 you start with 17 lives (always level + 1), as you can see I have only lost one life creating the first stovepipe. I trapped all four balls that would fit in there, then capped it off. The reason I capped it off was so that I could later use the top of that area as a starting point for creating stovepipes without losing a life. It's much to early in the level to think about that now though, so let's create our second stovepipe and lose another life.

Here you can see I made the stovepipe and lost a life (15 from 16). I lost a life when a ball hit the wall moving up, but I planned on that. If balls hit both of your walls, you lose two lives.

Now I've closed off the second stovepipe and taken almost half (8) of the balls out of play. Now things are starting to quiet down enough where if I'm careful I may be able to create pipes using the staging area above the pipes I've closed off to start a wall going up, much like I did at the beginning of Level 1.

I am a chicken however and lose two more lives creating the next two stovepipes:

Here I have all but five balls trapped. Two pipes are open also, so I can start maybe creating bigger boxes (on the lower left) and trapping atoms, then trimming them down - 96% and 13 lives at Level 16, not bad!.
