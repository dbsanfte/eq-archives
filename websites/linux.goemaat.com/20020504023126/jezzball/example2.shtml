Jezzball Example 2

Last updated September 15, 1999

Here you can see that everything is trapped. There is a group of two in the lower right that looks impossible to close, until you remember the secret!

The secret is that the blue (down or right) wall is stronger than the other. As long as you have your arrow centered on the open square, you can go horizontal or vertical, the blue wall will save you both ways:

But what if the open area is to the right and below the balls? What then?

The answer should come to you - you place the arrow directly over one of the balls. This way you produce only the blue (powerful) wall.

Now all that is left is closing the last trapped ball off:
