Everquest

  • EQ Casters - GREAT site for pure mages (and clerics too) - spell and eq lists, plus MANY guides on game play.
  • EQDruids - THE Druid site.

  • EQMaps - Great site with maps and zone descriptions.
  • Japanese Maps - A Little hard to understand, but you can find some nicely done maps here.

  • Allakhasam Everquest - If you're lookign for quests, go here. Great quest by item list for those no-drop items you find.
  • EQLizer - GREAT site with quests, beasts, equipment, and more.
  • EQ Stratics - Good general site with message boards and news.
  • EQ Stratics Spell List - Awesome spell list.
  • EQ Stratics Item List - Good general site with message boards and news.
  • EQVault - Another good general site with message boards and news.
  • Jewelry - EQ Casters' Jewelry pages deserve a link of their own - VERY informative.
