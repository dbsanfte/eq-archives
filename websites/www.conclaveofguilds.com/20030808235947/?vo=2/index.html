Everquest II News: New Happenings Abound
Posted by Mayhem at 10:41pm on August 5, 2003
Well, I mentioned it on the forums, but Moorgard has been appointed Everquest II Community Relations Manager. So far I've noticed that he's been over at Everquest II: The Age of Destiny posting on their forums. This is nice to know. :) Even better would be if he wandered over here, but we're still "new kids" right?

There have been new screenshots put up. Both EQ Lounge and IGN's EverQuest Vault have eye candy to check out. For those of you wondering why we don't host media here, it's simple. Everyone else does. When there is something real to host (i.e. people can send in their own shots of their guilds in action) that pertains to the theme of this site, you'll see it. Until then, enjoy what the other fine sites in the community have.

EQ Square has some information from Bill Trost available now. As always, this information WILL find it's way into our FAQ as soon as humanly possible. I hope this means less than 24 hours. (I typically take some time during slow times at my "day job" to update the FAQ, then upload a new version when I get home.)

Finally, not really new news, but just me thanking those of you who have visited so far. Please take the time to email me with any requests, ideas, feedback, or whatnot you may have. I'm also still trying to get in touch with the dev team to ask some questions, so if anyone can help out in that department it'd be appreciated. :)

General News: Roleplaying in an MMOG article
Posted by Mayhem at 9:02am on July 31, 2003
There's an interesting article from Dave Cerra, the CEO of Sojourn Development up at the Stratics main site. It deals with the ideas behind various roleplayers attitudes, and talks about how designers of MMOGs need to incorporate more depth into their offerings in order to make people more interested in truly playing roles, without forcing it upon them.

All in all, while I may not agree with everything said, I do think it's a good read, and provokes some thought. So go ahead and take a glance at it.
