MARK OF DRACONIANS


The Draconians are the people of Norrath who share a common bond. They have been touched by Dragon Magic.
How have you been touched by their magic? Settle down and I'll spin you a tale.

A war has been brewing long between the good and evil gods, a game of war that has begun to play upon Norrath. It is believed by some, that the Teir'Dal, led by their evil gods, are the true puppetmasters of all the evil races of Norrath. What ever the cause of evil's sudden upsurge, the gods of good are fighting back.

A chosen few have been marked by Veeshan herself, each born with a dragon-shaped birthmark. It is this chosen few who seek each other out, to join in the battle for Norrath for all that is good and holy.







The Tale so far:
I. The marked were joined to form their army against darkness.

II. The Draconians freed the slaves in the mines of Crushbone. The Teir'dal demanded their return or a payment of 50-100 platinum. We, of course, did not comply. We left the freed people to the tree-city of Kelethin to rest and heal and strengthen themselves at the care of the wood-elves for a time until they would be strong enough to take to their home cities.

III. The Draconians joined at the border of the land of evil to warn those Teir'dal that their plans for controlling Norrath is to come to an end.

IV. The Citizens of Halas aided us in taking and holding the bridge of Nektulos. We took a stand against evil and would not let them cross. On that eventful eve, it was discovered that our own mage, Nazriel, was in fact the son of Knarl of the Order of the White Hand. Many duels were fought, including a duel between Cyrric and Eisabeau. Eisabeau strangely enough, won the duel, but why?

V. Nazriel seeks to find the answer to his heritage and is given a strange neclace by his father. He distances himself further and further from his comrades in the Draconians. Often he is found staring out into nothing, his eyes glazed.

VI. Eisabeau finds a Crystalized Sword bearing a tag with her name on it tied with a silk ribbon. She finds this on the railing of the podium in the Hall of Dragons.

VII. An attempt is made to kidnap Danthur. In a horrible struggle, Danthur throws himself into the sea, commiting suicide. Fortunately the clerics of Marr find him and restore his life and tend to him. Strangely however, he has since had the strangest bad luck suffering death after death.

VIII. The slaves are readied to move from Kelethin to travel slowly through Faydark and to their homes. The Citizens of Halas join Thorban on the endeavor. The scouts sent ahead are ambushed by the Order of the White Hand. The party guarding the slaves is also attacked, and with the attackers is an Iksar named Zildrax, sent on loan from Lady Ravensky of the House T'Vlos. One of the slaves is killed, but two make it to their dwarven home in Kaladim. A gnome makes it to the docks to return to his friends in Antonica, as is a human freed woman, valiently guarded by an Elven Elite, Kadniar. And where is Nazriel? Standing back, his eyes glowing.

IX. Nazriel is found again with his father where he falls into a trance and discovers what had become of his mother. In a rage, he kills another Draconian, Railana. Soon after, the weekly guild meeting is held and Nazriel is called out to explain himself. He is exiled by the Draconians.

Meanwhile, Kragga hurries home from seeing her father in Halas to ready her troops for the upcoming attack on Mistmoor, and Eisabeau recieves a note from her old nemesis, Drahkanis.

X. Looking to broaden their horizons, Jalyna, Tabrina and Danthur set about exploring in the new world, Kunark. Not much further into Firiona Vie, the new outpost, and the trio run afoul bad luck, again and again. Their woes are compounded when Fatan tries to rescue them, and he too, is stricken with bad luck. Tabrina cries out, lost, lonely, cold and wet. The Seneschal hears her cries and asks the aid of the Hessian, Anae to step in and help the four companions...which she does...for a price. Anae, the Hessian smiles a secret smile and says she knows she will be able to collect from the Seneschal, in the near future......


What is next?
The call has come from the other good guilds of Norrath. The evil of Mistmoor threatens to spill out into lesser Faydark and we have been asked to help push them back within their own borders. While the ranks of the other guilds go within the vile land of the vampires, we shall help those of like seasons near the border.
