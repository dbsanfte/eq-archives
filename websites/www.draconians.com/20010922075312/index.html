MARK OF DRACONIANS


A chill wind has blown from the twisted canyons of Highhold Pass while the Draconian Seneschal silently wove a prophetic tapestry in a high tower within the Keep.


The Duke Nazriel Nemesis has fallen to the Dark-side forever and Eisabeau has been baptised in fire and paid the price with the permanent sacrifice of the personality she once was. She bears the permanent scar of one white eye, an eye that acts as a mirror to the will of the gods of Norrath.

With visions being continually cast upon the inside of the eye and shining within the mind of the Seneschal, Eisabeau cannot help but have one foot into madness, a madness that is kept in check by the presence of the Drake, another soul that was a prophesy of the gods.

General Jalyna Valeur thinks to call the Marks to war, but knowing the prophesy to draw those born of the Mark to war with the evil of the White Hand has failed to flower beyond the gathering of the chosen, she calls for the army to disband.

That done and the finish of the chosen, she calls upon those who will chose for themselves, both with the birthmark and without, to swear their loyalty and kneel to be knighted to the reborn Mark of Draconians, and proudly bear the brand seared onto their right arm by the Seneschal, herself.
