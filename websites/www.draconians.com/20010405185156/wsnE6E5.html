DRACONIAN CHARTER

 
 

I. The Draconians are members of a roleplaying guild. We may be of any race, and a member of any class upon Morell-Thule that uses no evil (such as necromancers and shadowknights. Rogues, however, are quite welcome). Typically evil race characters that wish to join must be agnostic with the exception of Iksar (who have been given no choice by Verant programing...see amendment I. Our common bond is that we were marked by Veeshan at the request of our individual gods to signify that we were chosen by our god in the battle of good vs. evil. Agnostics that are marked may chose which good god chose them.

Amendment I:
A) The mark was placed upon the baby with birthing and therefore had to be different from his siblings at birth in attitude SINCE BIRTH. No having an evil mentality and then changing their ways. That might work if it was good guild accepting ALL who live in the light regardless of WHEN they found the light, but not those CHOSEN to follow the light at BIRTH. So, at the birth of the creation of the character, they have to have been made agnostic. With this reasoning, they cannot have been interested as children in apprenticing into evil classes such as necro.

B) Iksar are the only exception because Verant does not allow for this and I feel it is a programming hand-tying.
They must place in their character "consider" screen that they are Exiled, and from the get-go work on "good-faction" as they were born, at the get-go as being different than their siblings.



II. How has this "Choosing" come about? Before the birth of the chosen, the gods and goddesses of Light asked Veeshan to place a dragon-shaped birthmark upon the chosen, those they would send onto Norrath so that they might find each other. Now these "draconian born" seek each other out, to fend of the onslaught of evil brought upon us by the conspiracy of the Dark Elves to control the evil ones to rule Norrath.

The FIRST goal of this guild is to seek each other out as the birth of this guild is to be roleplayed out IN GAME. The point of this guild is ROLEPLAY.
The next set of goals shall be in the campaigning of battles and quests against the dark ones as set forth by the Elders of Class and the Trades Elders.

III. The Laws of Draconians:

Our purpose: to join together IN ROLEPLAY to hold back the onslaught of evil. The Seneschal (guild-leader) will control a main story line direction, while the players and their groups control their personal chapters to their perspective and experiences that they have while roleplaying out this story. It is encouraged that they relate their personal view of the story as it relates to them (in roleplay) in the Journals area of our scroll board.

RULING STRUCTURE: A COUNCIL OF ELDERS shall be made up of chosen leaders of each class so that all classes shall be represented. Voting shall consist of classes giving blind vote to their Elder. This Elder shall give blind vote to the Seneschal. These Elders shall be responsible for working ooc with their evil counterparts to DM adventures and campaigns with class questing for our members that share their class.
Example: the council elder of the rogues shall represent that class in guild vote, and shall be responsible for all the spy missions set forth by guild. Elder positions cannot be held by members who also have an evil character to stave of any question of loyalty by complainers

AMBASSADORS: We will also have ambassadors, representatives of each race to be ambassadors of our guild to their race and country and work as guilding officers

TRADES ELDERS: Elders shall be chosen to lead other members training on like tradeskills and shall work with their evil counterpart ooc to provide DM'ed roleplay adventures against the enemy. Trades Elders CAN be members that also have characters of evil alignment in other guilds.

HIERARCY: Blessed by the royalty of the governments of Norrath, the "titles" of hierarchy are bestowed upon us by the royal houses or Norrath (ex. the Feir'Dal)to signify experience (level) achievement only. We shall never have anyone holding the title of King or Queen as they are already placed in the game. The heirarchy in NO WAY reflects political clout in guild over another player.
Squire: level 1-8
Knight/Dame: level 9-16
Lord/Lady: level 17-24
Baron/Baroness: level 25-32
Marquis/Marquise: level 33-40
Count/Countess: level 41-48
Duke/Duchess: level 49 and up
**special title "Seneschal": as Eisabeau Willows is the "dungeon-master of this roleplayed story using Everquest as our "AD&D kitchen table", She reserves the right to hold the special title of "Seneschal". She has seen too many guilds fall to weak leadership. Though we will have a ruling body of the Council of Elders, the Seneschal shall have veto rights as she is the one directing the main storyline and knows what little twists and turns are coming up in the path before the rest of you are. That is, after all, the role of DM, is it not?

Tome Master: a special member that shall attend all council and ambassador functions shall be the Tome Master. This position shall be voted on by council members as to who is the best writer in the guild and shall become responsible for being our official transcriber.

A character may hold more than one title or position. It may be decided at a later date when the guild is larger on how elections shall be handled.
