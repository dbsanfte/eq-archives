Velketor the Sorcerer dead! (Utrellien Pentragon - House of Guards)

I loged in to quickly say Hello to the Souls before I went to bed and guess what? It took me 5 more hours till I loged off :)

Velketor the Sorcerer was up and so we gatherd quick to try and kill him. It was quite a task to get people together since not many was online at this time. After a while we had at least a couple of tanks and damage delears and well three clerics.

So we made our way to Velketor. The way there was quite easy since we had a good wizard force with us and just AE'd everything wich was in our way. After maybe 30 to 45 minutes we were ready to engage Velketor.

One task though was left to split his guards out of his room to kill them first. It took us a moment to fiddle that out but we got it done than. It was truely a Knight task to split the guards since all our Shadow Knights and sadly even a Paladin had to die for the task to be finished. But than with three Shadow Knights and three Paladins what raid can go wrong anyway? :)

PinK LadY TuGuRuk'sTM time was due! Together with the clerics and Paladins our very own PinK LadY TuGuRokTM rushed Velketor. Paladins Layed Hands in the beginning and healers kept pinky boy alive. After a longer fight Velketor felt against the Souls!

Not a single dead on our side ;)

Congrats on a very great fight and congrats to Endrin for the Orb of whatever DD I click in 10 seconds and Syner on the spell let me look like a spectre - Arch Lich :)

It's Raining SOULS!!! (Nedd Neddley - House of Guards)

As I dropped from the sky into Freeport harbor, I struggled under the weight of my armor to pull myself up to breathe. Breaking the surface, I was amazed to find that there was more than just a cool morning drizzle falling from the sky. There were in fact PEOPLE falling from the sky, and moreover, the SAME people who were my guildmates that had just finished cleaning up in the Plane of Sky. A wonder that even when one takes into account all the factors of chaos - changing winds, air density, varying air resistance of the falling people - that they should all fall in the exact same place! I was able to capture these astounding images, and compiled them into this montage:

Overall we had a great turnout for that dreaded raid that somehow always rears its ugly, logistical nightmare of a head every so often, Plane of Sky. Not only was the turnout great, but this time so were the rewards, amazingly enough! Congrats to the following people on their rewards:

- Syner on his Black Silk Cape
- Mystrunner on his Fine Velvet Cloak (from the SAME Keeper of Souls nonetheless) and thus a completed Dark Cloak of the Sky
- Hotsteel on his Ivory Sky Diamond, and a completed Girdle of Faith
- Maelle, Efrieum, and something on the order of SIX other warriors on their Spiroc Wingblades

Not to mention, as if just those weren't reward enough, both Maelle and Syner's epic pieces were the final piece they needed, so....

Congratulations Maelle on your Blades of Tactics, Strategy, and your Jagged Blade of War!

Congratulations Syner on your Duck of the Shadowed Soul!

A great raid overall, thanks to all who attended! And as a parting gift, I leave you with this picture, a very old image of two halflings having way too much fun when they should be fighting. ;)
