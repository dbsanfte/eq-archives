July the 24th, 2003
The most selfless of Souls! (Nedd Neddley - House of Guards)

I'd like to take a moment to point the spotlight on perhaps the most selfless Soul ever to grace the hallowed halls of the guild. No doubt you've seen him before, a dwarven fellow with a heart of gold, willing to put himself in harm's way to the benefit of all, and asking nothing in return for his great contributions.

Of course I refer to Balar, or Jebus as he goes by sometimes. How is it that such a wonderful fellow could exist? Whenever a raid is short on healing, out of the ground he springs, ready to do battle in the name of victory. Whenever someone has fallen valiantly in battle, in even the most exotic locales, out of the cracks in the walls he emerges, ready to resurrect their body. Whenever Donetta has fallen valianty running through the commonlands, the plains of Karana, the mist-shrouded forests of Faydwer, the steaming jungles of Kunark, the icy shelves of Velious, ... or the phantasmal outer planes themselves, he will make the necessary trek to recover and rejuvenate her body.

Somehow he's always there, just when you need him, and assists with a smile and a kind word. One wonders if perhaps the magic of more than just one dwarf is in that glowing face and healing hands.

Recently we showed our gratitude to the little guy, completing his epic which he now uses to benefit everyone else even more. Porteous snapped a shot of the glorious moment, portrayed below:

Wherever you see a mob beatin' a guy,
Wherever a hungry newbie cries,
Wherever there's a fight against the blood and hatred in the air,
Look for him, Souls, he'll be there.
Wherever somebody's struggling for a place to stand,
For a winning group or a helpin' hand,
Wherever somebody's strugglin' to be free,
Look for him, Souls, that's where he'll be.

Balar, we salute you!
