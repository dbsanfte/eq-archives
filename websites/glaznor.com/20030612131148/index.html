February the 26th, 2003
Velketor the Sorcerer dead! (Utrellien Pentragon - House of Guards)

I loged in to quickly say Hello to the Souls before I went to bed and guess what? It took me 5 more hours till I loged off :)

Velketor the Sorcerer was up and so we gatherd quick to try and kill him. It was quite a task to get people together since not many was online at this time. After a while we had at least a couple of tanks and damage delears and well three clerics.

So we made our way to Velketor. The way there was quite easy since we had a good wizard force with us and just AE'd everything wich was in our way. After maybe 30 to 45 minutes we were ready to engage Velketor.

One task though was left to split his guards out of his room to kill them first. It took us a moment to fiddle that out but we got it done than. It was truely a Knight task to split the guards since all our Shadow Knights and sadly even a Paladin had to die for the task to be finished. But than with three Shadow Knights and three Paladins what raid can go wrong anyway? :)

PinK LadY TuGuRuk'sTM time was due! Together with the clerics and Paladins our very own PinK LadY TuGuRokTM rushed Velketor. Paladins Layed Hands in the beginning and healers kept pinky boy alive. After a longer fight Velketor felt against the Souls!

Not a single dead on our side ;)

Congrats on a very great fight and congrats to Endrin for the Orb of whatever DD I click in 10 seconds and Syner on the spell let me look like a spectre - Arch Lich :)
