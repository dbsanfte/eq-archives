Patch Rant™ Posted Wednesday, August 30, 2000 by Buur
Verant didn't give me much to work with this week. I think I start every Patch Rant™ by saying something really similar to that don't I? Regardless, here we go.

*Today's Patch*

Today we modified the EverQuest client to implement the following changes:

- Corrected a bug that would cause players to crash if they entered the game, exited to the server selection screen, and tried to reenter the game.

- That's good. I was personally getting a bit tired of logging on, then immediately logging off to see if I could try to relog on, only to crash. It just got tedious.

- Corrected a bug that caused delays when switching between UI modes due to the text wrapping change implemented last week.
- The /book command (/book #) now accepts pages 1 through 50, and will switch to the proper page.

- Because switching to the wrong page was confusing. The real problem is when you actually switched to the wrong book. I once tried to switch to page 12 of my spellbook, the next thing I knew I was reading 'The Catcher in the Rye'. And no, it isn't about baseball. Yeah, I was disappointed too.

- Opening your spellbook will automatically cause you to begin meditating providing that you have the skill.

- If you don't have the skill you will just kinda sit there, you know, not meditating. Makes sense I suppose.

- Removed the /meditate command since it is no longer necessary.
- You can now activate a hotkey while the spellbook is open by pressing the corresponding number-key.

- Just, whatever you do, don't try an activate a Hot Pocket!!!!!!

- The "/target [name]" command can be used to target other players by name. Please note that this ability is subject to the range to the target.

- That would be as opposed to the "/target [name]" command targeting trees. Please note that this ability is subject to the range of the tree.

- EverQuest will now write the original default colors when creating the EQCLIENT.INI file, rather than the new colors that were put in a few weeks ago.
- The @-character can be used in emotes to indicate a target. For instance, if you target "a gnoll" and type "/emote laughs cruelly at @", the output will be, "Playername laughs cruelly at a gnoll".

- To which the gnoll will reply,"Hey, screw you buddy. I got it bad enough already, I am a frickin' gnoll! You don't have to go around cruelly laughing at me. Asshole!"

- People with the /LFG (Looking for Group) flag set will now have their over-head name prefixed with an asterisk. Remember that you can search for people looking for a group by using the "/who" and "/who all" commands. Typing "/who all ranger 40 50 lfg" will return a list of all rangers between 40 and 50 that are looking for a group.

- And people with the new /LFGT (Looking for Good Time) flag set will now have their over-head name prefixed with an exclamation point.

- The "Sense" series (Undead, Animal, and Summoned) will no longer point you towards people's pets.

- Thank god. Whenever I cast that spell I find myself drawn toward my neighbors dog.

*Note on Voice Recognition*

Last week we implemented voice recognition technology in EverQuest. To use this new feature, please refer to the EQROKManualSupplement.doc file in your EverQuest directory. Please note that you must already have a voice recognition engine that is compliant with the Microsoft Speech API (SAPI), version 4.0. The voice recognition technology was tested with Dragon NaturallySpeaking(r) v4.0. Check with the developer of your software to find out if it is SAPI 4.0 compliant.

I had been meaing to talk about this anyway. Has their ever been a more blatant case of discrination then this! Sure this helps out those with handicaps that can't use a keyboard and a mouse real well, and that is a noble thing. But what about the blind players? Where are their braille controls? I am sure many of my blind readers would appreciate braille controls in EverQuest! You know how hard it is to navigate when you can't see where you are going?

And what about those that have a disablity and are mute? Can we get some retinal controls for them? Come on Verant! Get with it! The players won't take your abuse anymore!!
(The above was humor. If you were offended by it then too damn bad.)

- The EverQuest Team

Gordon backs off Posted Monday, August 28, 2000 by Buur
Everone's favorite sock puppet, Gordon, rescinded on some statements he made yesterday. He said he was going to check with the programming team to see if items that were stored in unproper containers, i.e. sewing kits, quest bags, etc., could be destroyed once someone logged out. You see, by doing that Verant can punish those players that have the audacity to be smart.

Hello all,
The original thread on the Partisan pack shifted later to a topic about combinable containers in general. I wanted to separate out the two issues and discuss them individually. I've started a separate thread on the issue concerning the unannounced change of the partisan pack, which is a bag given by an NPC for the purpose of completing a quest.

In this thread I'd like to discuss my comments where I said:

"Concerning items in combinable containers, I think that I'm going to chat with the programmers Monday and see if they can make the containers eat any items in them upon logoff/Linkdeath. I think that there'd be no question of the intent of those bags then."

Patch Rant™ Posted Wednesday, August 23, 2000 by Buur
It is time for everyones favorite EQ Rants feature. No, not 'Buur bitching about his new apartment'. It's time for this weeks installment of 'The Patch Rant™'.

August 23rd, 7:00am
------------------------

*Level 1 Character Purge*

We've decided to postpone the previously announced purging of level 1 characters that had not been played in the past 30 days. Should we reschedule this process in the future, we will again give fair warning prior to implementing the purge.

- Good, I really wanted to keep my level 1 Dark Elf ShadowKnight around. I have never played him, and he is at level one, but I have so much time invested into wanting to play him that it would be heartbreaking to have him deleted.

*Today's Changes*

Today's patch consists mostly of content changes within EverQuest, such as new quests, corrections to existing NPC texts, and adjustments to spawn times associated with quest NPCs and other NPCs in general.

- It is good that todays patch isn't fixing things within Ultima Online. I am sure that the Origin guys would get pretty pissed.

- /help will now display emotes, guild commands, and normal commands separately.
- /sit will cause your character to sit. '/sit off' will cause your character to stand.

- /screw off will cause your character to....ah, nevermind.

- '/cast #' (where # is 1 to 8) will cast the spell memorized in the corresponding slot.
- '/book #' (where # is the even displayed page number divided by 2) will take you to the corresponding page of your spell book. For instance, to flip to page 27/28, type '/book 14'.

- Hey, that is cool. Flipping through all those pages gets to be a real bitch. Especially for my warrior character.

- '/doability #' (where # is 1 to 10) will fire the specified ability. Abilities 1-6 are those that are set on the 'Abilities' page; Numbers 7-10 represent those that are set on the 'Combat Skills' page.

- Where I come from 'doability' means something completely different. Although I have never assigned numbers.

- /whotarget can be typed to do a /who on the targeted PC.
- '/language #' (where # is a number corresponding to the desired language) selects the chat language to use in general communication.
- '/language help' displays a list of available languages and their corresponding number. '/language' alone displays the current language.
- /meditate causes you to sit down, open your spellbook, and begin meditation.

- As opposed to making you run around in a beserker rage.

- In 'HUD' mode, the Chat Box will now take advantage of higher resolutions to display more text before wrapping down to the next line.
- Fixed a bug that allowed users to /quit out of the game immediately without penalty. Normal operation is supposed to cause /quit to leave the character in-game and linkdead for a period of time to discourage this action.

- Now the penalty for trying to /quit out of a game will be 5 minutes in the penalty box for Unsportmanlike Conduct.

- Again changed the functionality of the 'U' ('use') key. It will no longer inspect players or loot corpses. All other changes implemented in the last patch stand.
- Added new customizable color options to the EQCLIENT.INI. The working documentation for this is below.
- Corrected a bug that caused items that ran out of charges to 'disappear' when the last charge was used. The item never really disappeared, and would reappear after re-logging. It no longer appears to disappear.

- I know this is worthy of some sort of comment, but such a comment escapes me right now. Oh wait, I think it is reappearing. Oh no it didn't, nevermind.

- The EverQuest Team
- The EQ Rants Team

---
