FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE
September 15, 2004
Contact: John O'Rourke
559/222-7200

Fresno, CA - To better meet the needs of its customers and provide flexibility for growth and development RJO Produce Marketing is separating its retail service offering said RJO President John O’Rourke.  Dan Lyman, Vice President of Business Development for RJO, will create a separate retail services company called FreshEDGE, Inc.  RJO will continue to provide year around on-ground field representation services including inspections, market analysis and product procurement in key summer fruit categories.

“The marketplace is telling us they see great value in the services we offer, but the retail service value goes beyond our core categories” said O’Rourke.  “The FreshEDGE approach helps improve the performance of more than just the products we represent.  It benefits peaches coming from Georgia or blueberries coming out of Michigan.  This move gives Dan the flexibility to combine multiple categories under the FreshEDGE approach which better fits with how our customers want to do business. The knowledge gained looking across multiple categories will help us better understand the role of our products within the overall produce department strategy so we can do a more effective job in the field.”

“Today shoppers have more options and are finding more reasons to get their groceries at warehouse clubs, health food stores or supercenters.  More and more supermarkets are desperate for new ideas to defend their market share and differentiate from these competitors. At the same time shipper/growers are looking for ways to increase their returns and elevate their relationship with key accounts” said Lyman.

FreshEDGE will offer a tightly integrated framework of services that focus on three areas; (1) helping clients develop and deliver relevant shopper solutions (2) making these programs executable at the store level and (3) strengthening their abilities to collaborate with their trading partners.  Services will include sales and consumer strategy and analysis, research, retail audits, training/education and strategic customer business development.

RJO and FreshEDGE have created a strategic alliance agreement that will maintain seamless support to RJO’s customers. “The creation of FreshEDGE, Inc. is an exciting development” said O’Rourke. “It let’s both organizations focus on their strengths, but still provide a complete solution that links supply and demand and delivers greater satisfaction to the consumer.”   FreshEDGE will be based outside Chicago.

Founded in 1993, RJO Produce Marketing offers comprehensive market analysis, inspection, procurement, and category management services for summer fruit categories grown around the world. The firm has earned both the Red Book "Business Character Award" and the Blue Book "Trading Member" status. For complete information on RJO Produce Marketing, visit www.rjoproduce.com or call 559/222-7200.

FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE
October 7, 2002
Contact: John O'Rourke
559/222-7200

FRESNO – RJO Produce Marketing announced the appointment of Dan Lyman to Vice President of Corporate Strategy and Business Development.  Dan will report to RJO’s President, Rob O’Rourke.

“Dan is an exciting addition to our team” said Rob O’Rourke.  “In this newly created position,  Dan will be responsible for shaping the company’s vision and the development of customer services designed to impact profitable growth and shopper satisfaction.  He has a unique background and brings a well-rounded set of skills that we need to take our organization to the next level.”

Dan most recently served as Partner, Education Alliance and Resource Group, LLC where he worked with school districts to build stronger public/private partnerships with business and community groups.  Prior to that, Dan had the position of Vice President, Willard Bishop Consulting, a leading consulting firm in the retail food industry where he specialized in retailer/supplier trading relationship development, perishables category management and activity-based costing.  In addition, Dan worked for 12 years in the candy industry for Hershey Chocolate and Leaf North America in national accounts, broker management and sales operations.

“RJO has succeeded by being customer focused and innovative in the service and support we deliver.  We have believed for several years now that our industry was changing and that we would need a deeper understanding of our customers’ business – their shopper, their competition, their market.  Today it is not enough to put fruit on a truck and send it across the country and hope it meets the customer’s needs.   Dan’s retail sales and marketing experience will help us get to the next level of adding value to our customer service and relationships” said O’Rourke.

“I’ve worked off and on with Rob for nearly four years now and am impressed with the culture and performance of the organization” said Lyman.  “I see produce as a strong growth category.  Given the health needs of aging baby boomers and the recent news regarding obesity in America, demand for fruits and vegetables will only continue to increase.  I also believe retail customers will need more support from their trading partners to capitalize on these opportunities.  Summer fruit is a substantial part of the retailers business and RJO’s expertise in these categories and the strength of their existing supplier relationships provides a great platform to build a unique “virtual” service company – one dedicated to helping retail customers understand, predict and respond to shopper demand.”

RJO changes its name to better reflect current and future operations

FRESNO, CA—RJO Produce Marketing is the new name for RJO Produce Distributing. The new name better describes the company’s commitment to year-round sourcing, category management and emerging technology. The company’s logo has been updated as well.

In 1999, the company’s inspectors started traveling outside California. RJO bird dogs have inspected Florida strawberries, and produce from Chile, Mexico and Central America at various American ports of entry.

“We now have a lock on our five produce categories. Wherever they come from, anytime of year, we’ll be there” says Rob O’Rourke.

“There is simply no substitute for being in the field,” says O’Rourke. “Our inspectors carry cell phones, laptops and digital cameras, but everything comes down to their good judgment. We simply want the best produce for our customers.”

From the beginning, RJO has functioned as the “branch office” of each customer’s buying department. RJO procurement specialists stay in constant contact with both supermarkets and the growers. But in 1998, RJO strengthened its partnerships with supermarkets by adding services such as consumer research and promotional analysis.

“Supermarkets have welcomed our category management skills,” says O’Rourke. “We track all the factors that influence produce quality, price and availability, then evaluate that against scan data and recent consumer behavior. We distill a large volume of information into something our customers can use to make better decisions. “

RJO recently hired its first category specialist. Matt Ruegge will concentrate on category management activities exclusively. Among the tools in a category management plan are produce branding, display design, and promotional analysis.

Tying all RJO services together is the strategic use of technology. The company was first to embrace the most advanced agricultural software and make use of electronic data exchange with its customers. In the months ahead, RJO will enhance its web site to deliver more information and functionality to its customers.

“Technology has been a great tool for RJO,” says O’Rourke. “But what has built our business is getting results for our customers.”
