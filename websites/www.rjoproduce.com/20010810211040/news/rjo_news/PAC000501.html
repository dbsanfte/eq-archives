Firm Changes Name to Fit Services

by Lisa Lieberman
excerpted from The Packer  May 1, 2000


FRESNO, Calif.-RJO Produce Distributing has taken a new name-RJO Produce Marketing-to go along with its new global identity, said Rob O'Rourke, partner in RJO.

Instead of selling produce on a seasonal basis, the company now provides buyers with year-round supplies, O'Rourke said.  The company also offers buyers new services, including category management as well as merchandising and marketing assistance.

O'Rourke, along with his brother John, founded RJO Produce Distributing in 1993.

The focus of the company was to act as an independent buying office for retailers in five commodity groups-strawberries, stone fruit, grapes, citrus and melons, O'Rourke said.

The company primarily procured produce from California and Arizona.  Last year, though, RJO began purchasing Chilean tree fruit and Florida strawberries in volume.  RJO also purchased small amounts of melons out of Central America and grapes out of Chile, he said.

This year, the company has plans to increase its off-season imports of grapes, citrus and melons, he said.

"We've changed our emphasis from being just a West Coast procurement center to being a year-round procurement center," said O'Rourke.

To go along with its new global image, RJO has also changed its logo.  Previously, the company had the state of California as part of its logo.  Now, the logo features a sunburst.

RJO has been offering retailers category management services over the last three years.  But this year, RJO is planning to offer a number of other value-added services to help retailers move their fruit, O'Rourke said. 

"We feel it's very important to know our categories completely.  We want to know how the product is grown, where the product is grown, and who grows the product.  In the past we focused on the procurement end.  Now we've taken a big step to finding out what happens to the product once it reaches the store level. ... We want to offer to the retailer full support -- from procurement right through merchandising, marketing and consumer analysis." he said.

To that end, RJO has hired Matt Ruegge as a category specialist for the company.

While many shippers are exploring category management, O'Rourke said his company offers buyers something more.

Rather than just trying to promote a commodity from a specific region or grower, RJO gives buyers a more objective analysis of how a commodity or variety is doing, O'Rourke said.
