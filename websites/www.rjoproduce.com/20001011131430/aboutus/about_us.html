Founded in 1993 by brothers Rob and John O’Rourke, RJO Produce Marketing offers comprehensive sourcing, distribution and category management services for tree fruit, strawberries, citrus, grapes and melons grown around the world.  RJO's team of inspectors personally evaluate the produce at its source daily, thus insuring our customers receive the best quality products available.

RJO Produce Marketing has a trading office in Central California and a technology office in San Francisco.  The firm has earned both the Red Book “Business Character Award” and the Blue Book “Trading Member” status.
