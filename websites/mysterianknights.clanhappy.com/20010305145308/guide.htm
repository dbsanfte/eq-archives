New Members Guide

Welcome to the Mysterian Knights Guild! By now you will have read the Guild Charter that gives an overall view to the nature and purpose of the MK. This guide is intended to brief you on the history of the MK and the guild rules that are NOT posted within the charter.

The Mysterian Knights Guild originated about 4 years ago Earth Time in a land far, far away :). We migrated to Norrath when the doors to this world first opened, and have made a new beginning. We have grown from the original ten founding members (Dantagnan, Mysteria, Kilsen, Joanne, Chikii, Amano, Sloan, Aevernil, Jeremiah, Nocturne) to a staggering 90+ as of this time.

 

Rules of Conduct

1. No swearing on guildsays! Dantagnan buys tasty soap wholesale, and Joanne uses it liberally when this rule is broken.

2. No random Player Killing. The MK are an anti-PK guild. Yes, we DO kill players, but only in reaction to injustice perpetrated by the player.

3. No alternate characters that are Player Killers on the same server as the MK are allowed. Discovery of an MK with an alternate PK character will result in immediate dismissal from the guild.

4. No assistance of any type to known Player Killers. Do not sell to them, trade with them, teleport them, heal them, group with them, etc.

5. Always help a guild mate whenever feasible. This does NOT mean that members are required to drop whatever they are doing to assist a member with something the member should be capable of doing themselves, nor does it mean the guild will give away valuable items to new people just because they are in the guild. To paraphrase a cliché, ask not what the guild can do for you, but what you can do for the guild.

6. NEVER harm a guildmate, or through inaction allow a guildmate to come to harm. This is grounds for immediate dismissal.

7. Do not steal. This is self explanatory.

8. The guild is NOT a democracy. The officers are to be obeyed, and the final authority is the Guild Master.

9. No race wars. The MK judge individuals by their actions, not their race or creed. We are the proverbial melting pot, and include among our ranks members from nearly all of Norraths diverse races and cultures.

10. All members will be required to assist in any call to arms issued by the GM or officer of the guild. If your nature does not permit killing for any reason, you will be expected to lend a hand with healing, or tracking, or whatever particular skill you can contribute.

11. Group whenever possible with other MKs. This builds unity and provides safety. This does not mean you cannot group with other friends, but whenever possible try to include your guildmates as well. We protect each other AND the other innocents in Norrath from the evil PKers, as well as helping each other level.

12. Please do not spam the guildsays. They are busy enough as it is :)

13. Teleport rides are a privilege, not a right. Do not expect the guilds few teleporters to come running every time you call. You have two feet. Use them :)

14. No internal bickering. This brings disunity. If you have a problem with a guildmate, speak to an officer about it and get it settled. Don't let it fester, and don't drag it out in guildsays. One for all and all for one!

Promotions

New members are always interested in moving up through the ranks to officer levels. The guild does encourage this. To be promoted to officer status you must demonstrate leadership qualities by helping others and assisting in fighting our foes. Officer candidates must be nominated by fellow guildmembers, and are elected by the current officers. As the guild continues to grow, we will create new clans and each will need new officers to lead them. If you recruit your OWN clan, and have demonstrated the leadership qualities we seek, then you will be considered first for that position. We require a minimum 6 new members to start a new clan.

 

Recruiting New Members

The MK are always looking for a few good people :) If you have a friend you would like to recruit, or are approached by a player interested in joining, first spend a little time playing with that person or observing them to see if you think they would be a good fit for the guild. Ask them to read our charter, and after they have then refer them to an officer. Only officers can invite someone into the guild. The officer will question you and the prospective member, and ask around the guild for any additional information available on the prospect.

 

Leaving the Guild

As with any large, vital group our membership does change from time to time. New people are admitted, old friends go. The guild understands and appreciates this. If you feel for personal reasons that you must leave the guild there are no hard feelings. If you leave the guild because you have decided to embark on a life of Pking then you join the ranks of our enemies and will be treated accordingly.

If you have any questions not covered in this brief guide please feel free to contact any officer online.

Best of luck in your future journeys and safe hunting!
