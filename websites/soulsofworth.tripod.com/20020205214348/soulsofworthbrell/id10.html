Souls of Worth - Brell

We are always looking for people interested in joining our guild.

How Can You Join?

Just simplay hail one of our members in game, and they will direct you to one of the officers. All new guild members will be subject to a trial period where we can get to know them better, and be sure that they show the same qualities that we wish to have representing our guild.

Who Can join?

We are currently seeking those of the 35th season or greater to help us make Norrath a friendlier place, while at the same time relieving it of a few ne'er-do-wells =)

Benefits of Joining

There are several benefits to becoming a member of our guild. Our members are close friends and we have a great time when we get together. Of course we also have a common interest, and we hope to further our cause.

RULES OF RECRUITMENT

You must read the Guild Rules and decide if the Souls of Worth is indeed the right guild for you.

If you are accepted into the guild you will become a trial member for 30 days. In those 30 days the officers will evaluate your progress and decide whether you will become a full member.In certain circumstances your trial period may be extended i.e.; RL issues prevent you from playing, etc. Remember be patient

During your trial membership members will have a chance to discuss you with officers, if there are any negative tells about you they will post them on the officers board for discussion between the officers.

During the entire length of your admittance procedure, members are encouraged to group/raid with you, and give opinions about you to the officers which may affect your final admittance. However, it is ultimately YOUR responsibility to try and group/raid with as many members as you can, and make your self heard on the board. While we don't look for spammers, we are looking for people that will be active in our guild. After all a guild is but the sum of its members.

After your trial period is over, if all goes well, the post will be deleted from the Trial Members section, and you will become a full member. You will gain access to the Vault section. In the unfortunate case that after your trial period it is decided that you are not Souls of Worth material, you will be removed from our guild and your board privileges will be denied.

We look very dimly on Ninja looting, KSing and being and all around beggar this is a deterrent in any guild and will surely jeopardize your life here in Souls of Worth. We are one of the strongest guilds in Brell and it is an honor for you to be invited to our family and you will be glad you did. We are strong for one reason we get along and HELP each other this is our creed and as a member you must live by it. One last thing your officers are there for your help in making your guild experience one of the best you will have. Please treat them with the respect they have earned.
thanks and I hope to see you on line! have fun this is NOT a game to be taken lightly.


One Requirement is that you have a member sponsor you before you are eligible .. 1 member and 1 officer/elder/leader .. YOU are responsible for meeting and grouping with such members . You must interview with Layha, Selia, or Targys before trial period will begin.
