Souls of Worth - Brell

These are basic rules and can be modified at anytime to fit the current raid.

These rules are at the descretion of the Raid Leader, SoW Officers and Guild Leader FINAL WORD on any loot is up to them.

These rules apply to all SoW hosted and funded, open invitation raids. Open invitation raids are those that we as a guild post public sign-ups for. The rules apply to all participants, including both guild members and our guests. Any deviation from these raid rules will be posted with the raid signup before the raid with permission of the Raid Leader or Guild Leader. If, at any time during the raid, the Raid Leader feels it necessary to modify these rules, the raid leader will make an announcement to the entire raid specifically outlining the change and the reason for the change.

First Rule

The first rule of SoW raids is: the Raid Leader makes the Rules. The second rule of SoW raids is: the Raid Leader makes the Rules. This means that all participants need follow and obey any and all instructions from the Raid Leader. Disagreements with the Raid Leader's instructions during the raid should be put off until the raid is over. It is not our intention to slight players on our raids, but we ask that you respect the responsibilities that the Raid Leader has taken on. Arguing with the Raid Leader during the raid will result in exclusion from future SoW raids. You are free to make your case at the completion of the raid. If you have specific questions, please post them or ask the Raid Leader before the raid begins. The Raid Leader reserves the right to remove disruptive, abusive or argumentative raiders from the raid at any time.

Sign Ups

SoW Raid Leaders will fill their raid rosters at their own discretion, following these priorities:

1. Guild members who desire to go on the raid.

2. Friends and allies.

3. Players who have supported our raids previously.

4. Players from a sign-up list.

The guidelines that SoW Raid Leaders will use to fill out the raid roster are as follows:

- Key classes needed for a successful raid.

- No more than 3 or 4 raiders of each armor-dropping class for plane raids.

No consideration is given to sign-up order for roster invites. Any sign-ups not chosen may be given alternate status, at the Raid Leader's discretion.

Preparation for Raids

- Be at least 1/2 hour early and prepared to begin (supplies, resist gear, etc.).

- Be prepared to stay the duration of the raid.

- Players showing up late may be subject to replacement by an alternate.

- Fear: Run-ins must be accomplished before the day of the raid. Otherwise, you may be subject to replacement by an alternate at the Raid Leader's discretion. Exceptions are made for raiders filling slots for no-shows.

Guild Funding

Raids hosted by SoW will be funded by our guild Treasury. This specifically includes spell reagents and coffins. The Raid Leader has the authority to reimburse incurred expenses, with oversight by the Raid Coordinator and Guild Leader.

Awarding Loot

SoW follows the system of CAWU (Can And Will Use) random lotteries as the guiding principle behind awarding loot at our raids. Please do not ask to be included on rolls for items your attending character cannot or will not use. Claiming raid loot for the purposes of selling or trading them is forbidden. Do not ask to roll on items you have already won with this character in the past, even if you do not own that item now. Do not ask into the roll for an item with the intent of giving it to another character, even if that character is present on the raid. Giving an awarded item to other raider is cheating on the lotto by skewing the roll. Violation of these principals will result in exclusion from all future SoW actives, and likely those of our friends and allies. Exceptions must either be agreed to in advance by the Raid Leader, or by all participating players affected by the exception.

Grey areas or cases of ambiguous fairness fall to the Raid Leader's discretion. SoW encourages in the strongest terms that respect for class utility be observed by all raiders. We trust in your common sense not to ask in on rolls for items that are of marginal utility to your class as compared to others. Although we value the CAWU principle, in such cases the Raid Leader may ask you to reconsider if we think certain classes should receive priority on an item.

We also support limited swapping or trading of select items for the greater good of all raid participants. Examples include Tinker Bags for the dragon bags, or high-end caster cloaks for a White Scale. In general, drops for Epic Quests are encouraged to go members of the appropriate class.

Group leaders are responsible for looting the kills for their group. Loot should be turned in to the Loot Master throughout or by the end of the raid unless instructed otherwise by the Master Looter. No one will be allowed to loot for a character not present unless there is no character present that can and will use the item.

SoW claims all coin, gem, and cash item drops for our Treasury. Any drop not claimed by a player under CAWU is subject to this rule. This is how we fund our raids. The Raid Leader has complete discretion over these drops, and may use them to reward players for outstanding service on the raid.

Misc. Items

Minor item drops from unnamed mobs of limited demand (planar weapons, shields, wands, etc.) may be awarded as the Raid Leader sees fit, usually by a simple CAWU lotto. These awards do not count against any loot rotation (planar armor or boss loot).

Planar Armor

- Last Piece Rule: if prior to the start of the raid you can show that you are down to your last piece of planar armor, you may invoke this rule to receive priority if your last piece drops.

- Skipping: you may pass on a class armor drop if all players of characters in your class agree. This allows members of a class to arrange the armor looting amongst themselves, as long as they remain unanimous.

- Rotation: once you win a roll for class armor, you are out of any subsequent rolls on your class armor until all members of your class receive such an item. At the Raid Leader's discretion, this rotation may also include other highly desirable armor drops, such as belts in Fear.

Boss & Quest Drops

Boss drops (dragons, named planar mobs, etc.) are on their own rotation, so winning class armor or other gear on a plane raid does not count against rolling on drops from featured encounters, for example. Winning any loot from a boss does preclude you from further rolls on this class of loot (from the same or subsequent boss mob encounters). This includes the quest items dropped by these mobs.

Spells

Spell drops are on their own rotation separate from other rolls. Those who can scribe on the spot are given priority. If no one can scribe on the spot, all members of the appropriate classes will be included in the lotto. If no characters present can use the spell, it goes our guild treasury.

If any spell research component drops are contested, they will be randomed on their own rotation.
