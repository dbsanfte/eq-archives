No Time Posted Thursday, August 10, 2000 by Buur
I don't have time for a full fledged update or rant today, sorry about that. Instead I will leave you with a classic EQ Rants moment. Err..well it may not be classic, but it was the first thing I found in the archives that I thought was worth reposting.

This first aired July 21st, 1999.

Let's see, where to begin today. I really don't have a whole lot to talk about, so I will ramble on and see how long I can keep you with me. Oh, I made it to level 14 today and then at level 15 I get some new spells. Woohoo! It's a pretty sad commentary on ones life when he gets excited about magic being acquired in a computer role playing game. Ah, here is an idea, lets see what a day in my life would be like if I could live as my EQ Ranger for a day.

I would wake up in the morning, in a sitting position, and then I would go and forage for my breakfast. Maybe I could find some roots and a pod of water, maybe not the best get-up-and-go breakfast, but neither is Pop Tarts. I would then slip comfortably into my banded armor and brush my minotaur axe and be off to work.

I wouldn't be driving or anything, since I would have nothing to drive, and since there is no water between here and work I couldn't take the ever annoying boat (look at post on July 7th). I guess I would be stuck running, maybe I could ask my neighbor, I am pretty sure she is a druid, if she would cast Spirit of the Wolf on me. I would offer a couple of gold, just to be nice. Neighbor looks at me like I am crazy, hmm..she won't be getting my gold! Well time to sense my direction and be on my way.

Morning commutes are a real bitch so I would have to stop and fight a few of the locals who decided to cut me off, and what do you know...the guy conned blue so I gained some experience for my efforts, let's check his wallet. Woah! He has 5 gold, 6 silver and a leather belt! My lucky day! After that little detour I would be running late so I would track my way to safety and proceed on to work.

Damn, it is getting dark out...I had forgotten about how short an EQ day is and my having to run to work made me quite late. The boss is furious! A quick con and I see that he is yellow, not good news for me. I cast Skin Like Wood on myself and Fire Protection since the flames coming out of the bosses ears have me worried. I also decided to cast a snare on the boss, but wouldn't you know it...he resisted it. Damn spell never works when I need it. Seeing that I am not going to be able to avoid this one I spring into action with my trusty axe. Damn, my boss sure is tougher then he looks. I watch my life bar, that conveniently floats over my head, quickly start to diminish. Hmm...let's heal! Interrupted, interrupted, interrupted...ugh..hey wait, that one worked...oh Christ, only healed 10 points. That does me no good! Time to flee and mark this one as a day that I should have called off. Hmm..not gonna be that easy, the boss is quickly on my tail! Stunned. Dead.

I would be warped home with none of my equipment. Damn, it sure is gonna be a long walk to get all my items back, I sure hope the boss has gone home. Maybe the neighbor lady will reconsider that Spirit of the Wolf......

Patch Rant™...kinda... Posted Wednesday, August 9, 2000 by Buur
What a dilemma. Here it is patch day, one of my favorite days of the week, and I can't find much to rant about. This patch is pretty good. I will give it a go anyway, just because I know that at least one of you enjoy the Patch Rants™ (yes, I am trademarking Patch Rants™. If 'The Vision™' gets a trademark then so does Patch Rants™). As always, I will pick and choose what I want to mention. You know where to find full reviews of the patches.

August 9th, 7:00am
------------------

*Guide Application*

The Guide Application is now back up at http://guideapp.everquest.com. If you are over 18 years of age, have a good customer-oriented attitude, enjoy helping your fellow player, and are interested in joining the Guide Program, we will be pleased to review your application.

If you are interested in spending all your in-game time answering dumb questions, please apply. You'll maybe even get a free poster out of the deal.

*Kunark Users*

A new file, emeraldjungle_chr.s3d, is included as part of todays patch. This patch adds additional displayable NPCs to the Emerald Jungle. Please run the 'Kunark Patch' by pressing the 'Kunark Patch' button on the bottom of the patch program UI prior to entering EverQuest.~

Instead of hitting the 'Cancel' button that would probably save us all a lot of trouble.

*Patch Day*

Greetings everyone! Today's patch contains some exciting new changes.

Shouldn't they have said this before they started quoting patch items? I know, I am being nitpicky.

*General Enhancements*

- Rangers, Paladins, and SKs now have mana equivalent to that of a pure caster with the same stats. This will give those hybrids a good deal more mana, regardless of their stats.

- w00t! Suck it down casters!

*Changes/Fixes*

- Small Studded Tunic recipe now works

- I'll just continue to eat foraged berries, thanks though!

- The Rusty Rapier now looks like a rapier.

- As opposed to looking like a spoon or other kitchen utensils.

*Dragons?*

Rumor has it that some new dragons have been spotted in Kunark. It should be noted however that the dragons can be engaged from much further away than is intended. Rather than hold off implementing these dragons for another week, we've decided to implement them now and fix the problem in the next patch. Enjoy :)

- Those rascals for Verant are letting us get away with something for a whole week! They are crazy I tell ya! Not that my level 19 ass could touch a dragon (Yes Findail, I am still level 19. Sucks eh?).

*Client Customizations*

Upon starting EverQuest, a new file (EQCLIENT.INI) will be created in your EverQuest directory. This file contains customizable settings for:

- Colors for text messages in the in-game chat window
- Default screen viewing mode
- Default travel speed (run or walk)

RunMode - TRUE for run, FALSE for walk.

- I could care less about the color thing. Blah, blah. But damn, is it nice to be able to default to run now. It really isn't that big of a deal to have to click on 'Run' when you log in. But it is really nice to be able to have it set as the default.

Another interesting point is that in the last Patch Rant™ I mentioned something about a run default, and now it is implemented. Coincidence? Well ok, most likely. But for ego sake I will assume they did it just for me!

More notes from the new expansion Posted Tuesday, August 8, 2000 by Buur
I just scoped out the official press release for Scars of Velious over at Verant.com. They cover some basics, such as when the expansion will be released (Winter, 2000), pricing (still being discussed), number of new zones (16), and some other tidbits.

The thing that caught my eye was the breakdown of some of the new creatures. I'll just cut and paste here for you:

New frozen continent "Velious" located south of Antonica.
More than 16 new advanced adventure zones including: ice caves, crystal caverns, crypts, dungeons and frozen towers.
A new cast of visually stunning non-player characters including frost giants, ice dragons, snow orcs, cave bears, Snowfang gnolls, storm giants and vicious ottermen.

Ok, they had me with the snow type creatures. I'll buy into ice dragons, frost giants and snow orcs. Where they lost me was with the vicious ottermen. Ottermen? Vicious? Hmmm....



Does that look vicious to you?

I will quote this directly from the National Geographic OtterCam site:

Found in or near rivers and wetlands throught the U.S. and Canada, North American river otters are powerful and graceful swimmers - and are known for their playfulness.

You read that! They are known for their playfulness! Not their ferocity or their viciousness! What are they going to do? Play Twister with you to death? Perhaps hide and go seek? Oh, I know, since they are river dwellers they will probably play a rousing game of Marco Polo until you drown. I certainly hope you guys know what Marco Polo is, if not that joke just bombed.

Anyway, I for one am just a tad bit disappointed in ottermen.

Walrusmen I could have handled, but not ottermen.
