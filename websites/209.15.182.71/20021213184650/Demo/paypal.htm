Step by step Tutorial

 

Welcome to Paypal step by step tutorial at mysupersales.com this tutorial will guide you how to make new account on Paypal l. By becoming a member of Paypal you can not only purchase on mysupersales.com but you can send and receive money online without a credit card (U.S. only). international Subscribers have to additionally give a credit card number for verification purpose only.

 

Step 1

On the main page of Paypal click on the signup button or free signup banner to make a new
account.

 

Step 2

On the form page you will see the following fields to fill in. For detail we have divided
the form in 3 steps , for your convenience.

Form part 1

On the fields below fill in your details your name , address, state and Zip etc.
If you live outside USA the click on (switch to international registration).


Form part 2

-In the fields home telephone fill in your telephone numbers as dialed within USA.
- In the Email Address field fill in the email address you wish to use . It will be your login name at Paypal
and you will be notified by Paypal at this address whenever any transaction takes place.
(NOTE: email address is which ever email address you use most and can be of any domain eg hotmail, yahoo etc
it need not be yourname@paypal.com)
- On the password field fill in your password of at least 8 characters long.
- The secret question menu has two questions which Paypal will ask you incase you have
forgotten your password. Choose the questions from pull down menu and fill in appropriate
answers.

Form part 3

-After filling the answers to questions. You have to fill the text field in security test field.
You have to fill the characters appearing in the yellow box.
as in this case is NNEERJDL.
- Click on the No radio button if you want to use totally free service, if you want to use
Paypal to accept credit card payments and for high quantity of transaction click on YES.


Click on the radio button at the bottom of form to indicate that you have read agreement.
And click on Signup. The next screen is optional as you can fill in credit card details any time.
