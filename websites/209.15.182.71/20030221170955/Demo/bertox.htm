Step 2

On clicking the server you will see the list of items available for that server.
Along the list you will see icons and detailed description of the features , quantity
and price of the item.


On the item list page click on the button to include the item in your shopping
cart.

Step 3

Upon clicking the add to cart button a pop up window will appear listing the items you have
added to the cart you can add or remove items .

If you want to purchase more items you can click on continue shopping , this will close the window
and when you add any new item to the shopping cart it will automatically add the new item to the
previous list and calculate new total amount.

After you have done with your shopping click on Checkout button.
