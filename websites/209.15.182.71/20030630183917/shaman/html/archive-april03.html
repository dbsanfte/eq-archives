Sunday, April 27th, 2003 - New Server


First of all, I upgraded the layout of the news site a little, as you can probably see. Your thoughts are welcome. I moved all of the March news to the archives. Also, the skills page is now 100% complete. Next up - the alchemy section. I have decided to delete the equipment section as it is badly out of date and to keep an accurate database of shaman armor is an enormous task. Check out Allakazams for an excellent database of equipment. Also, a new server is coming:

SOE celebrates the rapidly expanding EverQuest world by opening a brand new server. Join now and be one of the first to experience EverQuest from the ground up! Each player joining this server will be starting at level 1, so now is a great time to jump in and play. All characters on this server will be new and transfers to this server will not be allowed until at least six months after it opens.

The new server fulfills two purposes. It offers more room for our player base to grow as the number of subscribers continues to increase. It also offers a fresh starting point for players of all types. It gives players, new and old, the opportunity to lead the way, to build a new community on a fresh new server. Be among the first people on the server to explore the haunting castle of Mistmoore or the eerie Tower of Frozen Shadow in Velious.

EverQuest is a game about shared adventure. An exciting journey into the heart of the dragon's lair is a lot more fun if you have friends to share it with. This new server is an excellent opportunity to gather up your friends from other servers, or even those that have never played before, and experience new adventures together.

In appreciation for those that have been EverQuest players from the very beginning, this server will be named after one of these long-standing player's characters. SOE will randomly pick 8 of the first 1,000 players who subscribed to EverQuest and who are still playing and then SOE will select one character name from each account. Current EverQuest players will be allowed to vote on which one of those names should be used for the new server.

Please stay tuned to EverQuest Live site for further details.

Thanks,

The EverQuest Team

Then, Sony put out this additional statement:

Thanks for all of the feedback we've already received about the new server! It seems that a lot of people are interested in playing on a fresh server. Several folks have suggested that we remove the "transfers to this server will not be allowed until at least six months after it opens" wording entirely and make it so that this server never allows transfers.

We'll take a look into this next week as we work on finding out the name of the server. The idea certainly has merits, and several eyebrows were raised in interest at the appeal of it. I'll post again when I have more information next week.

Alan

Lets hope Sony can learn from what happened on the European servers. I hope they follow through with this.
