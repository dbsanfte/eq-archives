How orders are handled : 
 
1> Visit your server page to select the items you want.
2> Click on check out and pay for the items with the Paypal shopping cart (if you can not use Paypal then just send an email to yantis@mysupersales.com and we will find another way to arrange payment).
3> Immediately after sending payment, the items will be removed from the site, and an email sent out to you verifying your order.
4> Then send us an email with your characters name, or any other characters you may be playing on, the server the order is on, as well as a list of the items you bought.
5> We then gather up your order and then wait for you to send us an email that you are going in game to pick up your order.  We prefer meeting in the Bazaar but if you do not have Luclin we can work something out.
6> Once you have notified us that you are going game, we will log right in and deliver your order.
 
It is as simple as that. You pick the items you want and we deliver them to you.  I hope you enjoy our services =)
