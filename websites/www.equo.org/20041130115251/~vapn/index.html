The Quixotic Union of Optimists are caught up in the romance of noble deeds and pursuits of unreachable goals. We are idealistic without regard to practicality. We are dedicated to questing and the exploration of the unknown, the saving of damsels, and the righting of wrongs. We are committed to defeating unbeatable foes, throwing wild parties at the windmills of Ak'anon, and most importantly being excellent to others.

The following statement of "who we are" was put together by QUO to illuminate to outsiders who we are, what we are, and how our "guild" works. If you have questions, feel free to chat with Vapn, Bayd, Sorronn, or any other QUO members in game.

What is QUO?

The Quixotic Union of Optimists (QUO) is not a guild in the traditional sense of guilds from EQ or any other Massive Multi-Player Online Role Playing Game. QUO is a collection of friends and even real life family who have gotten to know one another through various mechanisms and over the years have become good gaming friends. Our first concern is always each other, not uber_loot_sword_076. QUO is not optimized for EQ (nor was it optimized for UO). QUO is optimized for our friends.

QUO's emphasis, therefore, is on long term friendships for the purpose of enjoying role playing games together. This means not just EQ but whatever games we find ourselves playing, for years, even decades to come.

The core of QUO consists of a small group of friends who played tabletop RPGs together 20+ years ago in high school. As the years passed, college came and went, and life settled into jobs and lifestyles across the country, these friends stayed in touch and developed more friends.

Then in the 1990s, the MUDs of the 1980s evolved into commercial online gaming in a large scale. Many of these people naturally migrated from table top gaming with friends in the same town to internet gaming with friends from all over the country.

With the emergence of games like Ultima Online, and later, EverQuest, these friends found entire online worlds where they could spend time together, game together, and accomplish goals together.

QUO consists of real people with real lives, not gamers who can game 24/7. As such we are not likely to ever be to the first to kill a certain dragon, or enter some exotic dungeon, but we are exceedingly skilled players. In time, as our real life schedules permit, we will rise to those challenges and we will go to these places. If you are someone that has the burning need to be first everywhere, then perhaps QUO is not for you. But if you are the type of person who values long term friendships along with the knowledge that the people you are playing alongside are both skilled and talented, and you have a desire to have a consistent set of friends to talk to, game with, and take on challenges with, then perhaps QUO is a place for you.

QUO has a diverse range of ages and backgrounds. As such we endeavor to be extremely conscious of what might offend our friends. The use of cursing in guildchat or public channels by QUO members just doesn't happen (though a few of us can vent quite creatively in private tells - we just won't do it in a place that we know might offend a longtime friend). And that is a key thing about QUO - we place respect for one another above "winning" the game, getting loot, or being first at anything.

QUO has no application process. There is no admission process per se. You cannot ask us to join QUO. Instead you need to play alongside us, laugh with us, cry with us, succeed with us, and sometimes fail with us. And you need to be patient about this. Eventually, one of us is going to ask you the question "Do you want to be in QUO?"

When we do, then you will know you've come home.
