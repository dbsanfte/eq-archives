February 28, 2002
PoF and Terror
Submitted by: firius
On Tuesday night, ADD killed Terror in PoF for the first time. After AOE nuking our way thru ¾ of the zone in record time and taking out the broken golem for Emandar, we got ready for the fight with Terror. It didn’t go so well the first two times as we were not sure what to expect. Learning from our mistakes, we killed Terror on the third try swiftly and without any casualties. Grats to Kaastle on the ball of everliving golem and Verbo on the weight reduction box. We then took out the Iskar child for Uglii and called it a night.

January 31, 2002
ADD conquers Island 7 in PoA!
Submitted by: Firius
ADD reached a new plateau last night by fighting to island 8 in PoA and doing it 100% guild only. The much-anticipated fight against the bees on island 7 went very well. ADD defeated the island boss bee Bazzt Zzzt and company in a nearly flawless battle. Tonight ADD will start clearing island 8 for mage epic crowns and other drops. Epic drops so far include Rakle on the necro epic item from Keeper of Souls, Kazama and Thorongil on the ranger swirling spheres, Draconus on the SK epic blade, Zordac, Fenoorn, Droog, Kaastle and Calidin on the warrior blades from the Spiroc Lord. We have also looted numerous quest items, haste belts, spells, etc. Grats to all members that have advanced in their epic quests and grats to ADD for a great raid thus far.
