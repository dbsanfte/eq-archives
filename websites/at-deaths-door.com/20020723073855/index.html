July 2, 2002
ADD Changing Factions
Submitted by: Garaddor Ghoulbane Guild Leader At Deaths Door
This past weekend was ADD's last days in HoT and on dragon faction. While, as you know, with the insept of new recruits, folks will always want to get SS armor and in essence you can basically raid HoT all the time.

We, as a guild, decided to move on, so from here on out, we are starting to kill the dragons or WTOV, driving for Sleepers keys and all first brood dragons.

Eventually, we will be working our way into the NTOV rotation, so if any guild would like to partner with us on this or wish to raid with us, give me a shout in the game. We will be trying to do it alone or with an alliance, not sure yet.

I also would like to take this time to say thanks to the Quell community for the understanding and patience with ADD thru the years. We had some bad apples as of late, as do all guilds, and in hopes of creating better server harmony, got rid of them.

Should anyone have any questions regarding this switch please contact me in game.
