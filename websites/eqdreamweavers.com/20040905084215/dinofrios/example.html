LAUGHINGBIRD BLACKJACK HOUSE RULES
                    •Winners will be paid the bet amount PLUS time and a half for ANY combo of 21.
                            • Cards are dealt from 6 decks
                            • Dealer will stand on any 17 (soft or hard)
                            • You may Double Down at any time
                            • You may Split at anytime
                            • Surrender is not allowed when dealer has an Ace showing.
                            • Pairs may be split only once.
