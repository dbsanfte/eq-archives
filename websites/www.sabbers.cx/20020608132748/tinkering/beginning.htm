You are in for some hard working?...
To beging with tinkering you need two things:

1] You are a gnome
2] You have alot of platinum and time to spend...

To start, you need to bee in Steamfont Montains and go to the thired windmill.
There you must buy a toolbox (around 1pp). You should buy the Tinker books too, with the recepies in (new books can bee bought inside Ak'anon, near the Cleric guild). Here you can also buy all comon tinker components.
Now it's just like all trade skills, find the stuff you neeed, put 'em in the toolbox and press 'combine'. Hopfully you have created something new....
