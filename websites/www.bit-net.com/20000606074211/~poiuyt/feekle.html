Feekle Log!

Official Web Site

Feekle Log! is a program that will take the mess that is the Everquest log files, and turn them into something that is interesting. To turn on logging, just type "/log on" while in Everquest. Also, when you are all set with Feekle Log, you can take a look at the site that gave me the idea to make this program. His site is http://jeffs.hardwarecentral.com/cgi-bin/eqstats.cgi and it is great!
