In the spirit of Norrath, our pledge is to carry ourselves with the utmost honor and integrity. Race, color, class, creed or religion does not bind us.

Let it be known through the land that our guild is fair and benevolent to all around us. It is our goal to strive to achieve this and extend a helping hand to all those in need.

We endeavor to scour the lands for knowledge and wealth in our search for the most secret and sacred items known. In pursuit of these goals, utmost and foremost, we desire having a good time and just plain fun.

Thus, being valiant explorers of like mind and similar goals, we have united!

So let our epic adventures begin, and let the following govern us.

Let it be known that the following are the conventions that govern all members within Vallis Scortor.

  • 1. Vallis Scortor shall be resolved to, and forever in its purpose uphold the virtues of camaraderie, honor, and truth.
  • 2. Vallis Scortor with all that follow proclamation one, shall form its guild center in the arena. This shall be where our official functions as a whole are made.
  • 3. Each person's opinion is equal so long as it does not conflict with proclamation one. Spirited debate is encouraged of course, but when the debate reaches a fevered tempo and three guild mates say stop, it must be taken to tell, message board, or all with an opinion adjourn to the arena with a neutral mediator to talk things out.
  • 4. All members are required to check the web site and message board daily, or before each time they log on, whichever is less frequent.
  • 5. As members of Vallis Scortor we are not a family of beggars. We do not stoop to pester others for items. We go out and win them for ourselves.
  • 6. Courtesy should always be the first action towards any member. When requesting items, buffs, ports, etc. from another guild member, please ask a general question in guild chat. Another member may want to help or be willing but not able to help at the moment. If they say they can't or can but not right at that moment, that is them taking you into consideration. Please show them the same consideration. In addition when requesting favors such as ports buffs, rezzes, etc. from other players not of our guild, be considerate of their situation and be understanding if they're unable to assist you. We don't want to be looked upon as demanding or ungrateful.
  • 7. Every member is never expected nor should they be asked to leave a group they are hunting with to aid another member. If you make a request in guild chat for help, other members will let you know if they can help you or not. We don't want to be the guild that no one wants to group with because we leave a group at the drop of a hat. The only time you are expected to leave a group is to join on an official guild raid.
  • 8. Official Guild raids are to be posted on the raid forum for discussion. There are different kinds of raids, single party, multi-party, planes, uber mob, and events.
    • a. If you post that you will be in attendance at the planned raid, it is expected that you're on time and ready to go. We understand that RL issues may delay you.
    • b. It is expected that you be at the raid gathering point 30 minutes prior to the raid start time. To facilitate this please camp at the gathering point before you log the day prior. If your just logging on and request a port 2 minutes before the guild raid you will be left behind.
    • c. Major guild raids which will be classified as multi-party raids, planes and uber mobs fall into this category, will state who of the appropriate level must be in attendance if you are logged on during the planned event.
    • d. Official guild events are planned and scheduled by the high council and will state if attendance is mandatory by all logged in during the planned event.
  • 9. No one other than the raid leader(s) communicates with other guilds/groups when a spawn/zone/camp is contested. This means no shouting "YOU SUXOR" on zone-wide channels.
  • 10. When on a raid, it is your responsibility to follow instructions of the raid leader(s), and to pay enough attention to know those instructions. Repeated failure to do so will send you packing.
  • 11. Kill stealing is not acceptable. If you are being KS'ed by a person speak to them and try to reason with them. If reasoning doesn't work and they are in a guild notify their guild officers or leader. If KS'ing is a real problem, petition - don't turn around and get you're higher level character to KS them. Anyone found to be KS'ing will be removed from the guild.
  • 12. Any abuse of the guild or its members is grounds for removal. Negative attitudes towards other members will not be tolerated. Nobody deserves to be talked about behind another's back.
  • 13. No member should ever reprimand or counsel another member. Period. If a member feels there is a problem, they should address it to an officer who will decide what action to take. No officer will ever reprimand or counsel another member without due cause and then they must make a report to the lower or high council after doing so.
  • 14. All officers will report to the lower or high council any violation of conduct or attendance as appropriate. The council as a whole will address all issues.
  • 15. Always remember, all actions you take when grouped or soloing reflects upon your guild.
  • 16. Harassment, threatening, racism/anti-Semitism, duping, ninja looting, blatant exploiting are all grounds for immediate removal.
  • 17. Try to keep a positive attitude on guild chat, even if the raid/day goes badly. If you don't have something nice to say don't say anything.
  • Guilding and Recruitment policy.
    • 1. The petitioning member must have achieved 55th level. This is a soft limit and exceptions can be made.
    • 2. The petitioner must apply on the message board and have read the FAQ.
    • 3. The petitioner must group with at least 2 different full members.
    • 4. If the petitioner becomes sponsored, the petitioner is not given recruit status until such time as he/she is interviewed.
    • 5. After the interview the petitioner is guilded if the interviewer deems fit.
    • 6. The petitioner is given a trial period in the guild for a period of no less than 4 weeks.
    • 7. At the end of the petitioner's trial period a vote shall be taken to admit the petitioner to full member status. The results of the vote will then be tallied by the recruiting team and the petitioner told via in game contact.
    • 8. Typically, the recruits get no loot.
  • Planar Loot

    • Class Armor

    • 1. All individuals on raid are in lotto, if they so choose, on their class armor
    • 2. Once an individual has won a common piece (helm, bracer, gloves, arms) he/she is out on all other common drops. This is in effect until all members of the same class have won something. If it's a duplicate piece that everyone has, an exception is made.
    • 3. BP's and Legs are ultra rare and as such they will be awarded to the ranking member of the class that can use them first if they have need of that item, otherwise the following looting rules apply.
    • 4. Loot shall always be given out in the following order.
      • a. Full members (going by seniority, AND if they have come along on previous raids)
      • b. Recruits (by seniority)
      • c. Guests and or Alts.

  • Any person with better armor than the planar armor that drops is out on rolls for that piece.

    • In the case of Quest armor (Kael, Thurgadin, Skyshrine), only people that are 50+ may loot any armor drops. Weapons and other Armor that is not no drop may be loot-granted to mains present or lotto'd to any mains that will need it.

    LOOT GRANTS

    Items/Weapons may be granted to a raid participant if he/she has been on many raids and has not won anything. The loot trustee, raid leader and officers present will decide if the raid participant is worthy.

    Loot grants are there to encourage attendance, and try to guarantee that the most raid participant possible will win loot.

    FUNDING THE GUILD

    Donations from members/guests on raids are welcome. Donations will be used for hate/sky stones, coffins, peridots, etc. You are never required to give any money to the guild. Donations may be made the guild trustee in the form of an item or money. All amounts given to the trustees either via planar non-magical jewelry or donations are to be kept and posted on the message board. The amount a non-magical item sells for are posted also, always sold by a person with high charisma. The guild treasurer on the message board will post the balance. This is for the guild as a whole only. High council will determine what events or raids qualify for the funds. All jewelry loot on the plains of non-magical nature shall be sold to vendors and put into the guild coffers. Gems that can be crafted into jewelry are considered magical.
