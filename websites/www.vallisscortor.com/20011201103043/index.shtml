Hello all fellow Norathians...

Vallis Scortor is growing. We'd like to welcome into the fold the Far Eastwood. The Far Eastwood, led by Drucilla merged with Vallis Scortor this month expanding the Ranks of Vallis and our ability to take on ToV halls of testing.

Now some exciting news...

Shadows of Luclin will be released on December 3rd World Wide

Ubi Soft today announced exciting developments for EverQuest®, the world's #1 multiplayer online game. In an unprecedented move, it has been decided to coordinate all countries with a simultaneous GLOBAL release date of Monday 3 December for the latest installment. Everquest: Shadows of Luclin. This is in direct accordance with customer demand.
In keeping with this massively multi-player on-line title, the community will be able to experience all the wonders bestowed on the continent of Norrath at the same time. Key enhancements provide new player models, additional monsters and the ability to purchase horses.

"We are committed to bringing online gaming to everyone, everywhere," said Kelly Flock, President, Sony Online Entertainment. As such, EverQuest, the world's #1 massively multiplayer online game, already boasts more than 410,000 members across the world and is poised to grow as servers are deployed into new nations. With over 6 million online gamers in Europe alone, localized servers will offer lower latency for a heightened interactive experience for this growing market. With the first overseas hub in the U.K., additional servers will rollout as each market ripens.
