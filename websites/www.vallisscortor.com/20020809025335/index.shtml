The 8th Shawl is the end of the Shawl Quests. Posted Thursday, August 8, 2002 by Silvermaine
Absor posted on Graffe's Forums explaining that there will not be a 9th or 10th shawl added to the game. Here's what he had to say:
I'm glad you liked them.

But, well, after a god comes by and hands you the last item in your quest... there isn't too much further you should expect the quest to go.

Sorry folks. We're glad you liked the quests, but there aren't any plans to add to that series. It had a definite end, and we reached it.

We'll just have to make new cool stuff for you in Planes of Power.

- Absor
