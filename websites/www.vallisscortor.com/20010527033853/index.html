More Skyjinx Posted Tuesday, May 22, 2001 by Santerre
Last night was proof that alcohol can indeed be consumed responsibly. And apparently it gave our team of 12 a bit of courage last night, as we cleared the entire gorgalask island save for the meteors and the avenging gazer.
We logged in to an empty island courtesy of Darkwind, but Nelith was able to make the time pass by summoning lots and lots of booze. Our first major accomplishments of the night were Larlaana and Tiphae hitting 200 Alcohol Tolerance! Always scary when the women in the guild can outdrink ya...

After about an hour of that, respawn started, alts were camped, and we got to business! We oscillated between 11 and 13 people all night, and had some very nice pulling done by Usulson, Sakra, Gertrude, and Tiphae. Drops were meager but standard with only several minor quest pieces dropping (pali girdle quest globe, monk eyepatch quest ruby, cleric shield quest wood, etc.). The only nice drop was a Bracer of Quintessence, awarded to Zigfree.

Finally the island was clear and it was time to pull the windmill/temple/building thingee. The Heart Harpy came clean after a successful sakra-fice bunt by Berfoot, who may or may not have been acting of his own accord. Heart Harpy fumbled up an elite SK 1-hander, awarded to /destroy.

After one aborted pull attempt and subsequent rezzes, Mr. Gorgalosk chased Gerty into the camp solo. There waited 10 members, with Lesias the Paladin cheering us on as an insurance policy, and Gorgalosk managed to die fairly quickly. The only casualty was Zobekn the cute little bear, who, under the orders of his callous master, kept attacking when Gorgy enraged - for like 1/2 a second.

Before dying, Gorgo handed Gertrude a much-deserved Belt of Contention. Larlaana found a leather cord on the corpse, and we let her keep it as apparently it is part of the shaman Test of the Well-Endowed Bosom.

We plan to clear that island again tonight, and if YOU are there, we can move on!

Day 3.. Vallis Owns PoS. Posted Sunday, May 20, 2001 by Silvermaine
Hello fellow readers..

Today Vallis entered the Plane of Sky at 12pm CST. First plan of attack was to kill all the Thunder sprites for more keys for those that were inserting today. After successfully killing all the Sprites, we took on the princess and soundly trashed her. She dropped a nice haste belt for a Bard, sadly we don't have any within our ranks at present that could have been awarded this item.

After getting buffed to the hilt, we inserted to Azarack isle. We managed to kill all and lost only 1 or 2 people to those filthy imps. After soundly defeating the protector of sky and getting Sirran to spawn we moved to Gorgalosk isle. There we managed to kill everything except the Shimmering Meteors. Those we just decided weren't worth the trouble. Only one major wipeout and that was an easy recovery due to Nitrains ablility to camp out. Yeah Nitrain. With a regrouping we owned the rest of the isle. All the mini-bosses dropped with ease, and Gorgalosk was handled swiftly and soundly.

A big hand to our monks Sakra, Usulson, and Fryarr. They did a tremendous job of pulling. Also thanks goes to our Rangers for their fine assistance.

We will be back at it at 10pm CST or so for anohter round of trashing of Gorgalosk isle. Sadly this cronicler didn't get the uber loot from the boss as of this writing, but will post it with the next installment from the Plane of Sky.
