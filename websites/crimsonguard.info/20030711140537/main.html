A few more first kills - Update by Nottall ~ 7/02/03
 
We finally got our shot on the cursed rotation in Ssra this week.  We started by clearing the 7 taskmasters then we took down The Glyph Covered Serpent with ease, which spawned Vyzh`dra the Exiled, he to went down with ease.  Since we failed on the Cursed we were forced to try again.  We were able to take down both the Runed Covered Serpent and Vyzh`dra the Banished with ease.  We will get the Cursed next time!  Last night we headed back to Ssra to take on Xerkizh the Creator.  He to went down with ease. 

Grats to all of you for getting these mobs down.  I am positive that we will take down the Cursed next time.

Vindi, Statue, Idol and AoW down - Update by Nottall ~ 6/30/03
 
It was our turn on the AoW rotation, so we gathered in Kael to work on faction and keep the area clear for the expected spawns. About mid after noon we got the call that the statue has spawned. We pulled him and dropped him easily. As we moved on clearing the way to where we would fight idol and AoW Vindi spawned right on top of us. Our MTs were LD so Polyx stepped in and tanked him for us.


Once we finished CoHing a few members not yet there we pulled in the Idol. Naerron was doing a great job tanking. I wish I could say the same for our CH rotation, somebody was not paying attention, if Liatvin had not of healed Naerron he would have dropped. With about 10% left on the idol we did a mana check for the clerics, one cleric was below 90%, so we finished the idol off and fed mana to that cleric.
3 Minutes later and the AoW was pulled in. Again Naerron did a great job tanking him the entire fight. He almost went down 1 time, again clerics need to pay better attention, but thanks to Liatvin and myself we got some quick heals off and the rest of the rotation got back in the groove of things.
When it was all said and done we killed 4 of the 5 boss mobs in Kael with out losing our MT 1 time. Funny thing was, the rotation was having a bad day yet we still kept the MTs alive. Yep we're just that good!!!

Farewell Unleashed - Update by Nottall ~ 6/17/03
 
Unleashed is no more......  To some this was good news to others it was bad news.  As a rival guild to Unleashed, my first thoughts were good that is one less guild to compete with, then I sat back and thought about what it must be like to work your ass of for a common goal and then one day it all be taken away from you.  I know it would not sit well with me, as I am sure it is not sitting well with the former members of Unleashed.

Many of the things that Unleashed accomplished was impressive and should be recognised as such..  Many rival guilds would not say such things about another guild, but I felt that today was a day I would set aside our differences.  As an officer of Utopian I wish all of you well and wish you all good luck wherever your paths shall take you.
