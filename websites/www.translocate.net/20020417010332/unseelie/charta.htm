Guild Charter

Guild Rules -

Membership is by invitation only, you must gain a sponsor with a rank of Noble or higher. Once this person has sponsored you before the guild you will be guilded at the rank of Peasant for 30 days. During that time your conduct will be noted by everyone in the guild, and at the end of that time there will be a vote whether you are a good addition to the guild. The Subjects of the court will get a say in the decision but not an official vote only Dukes, Dutchesses or higher get official votes for membership. There is no OFFICIAL rule on how many times you have to hunt with someone to sponsor them into the guild, or what type of people we are looking for.. Use your judgement! Be aware however that those you sponsor reflect upon you! If most of the people you recruit never make it through their 30 days then that will reflect poorly on you, so make sure you are willing to stake your name on someone before sponsoring them!

We do not restrict membership to race or class, all are welcome, the only limitation is that you must be over level 20.

There are very few mandatory rules for membership, pay heed to them. If you are not willing to do these things then you are not the type of person I want in the guild..

  • Message Board - The guild board is there so we can communicate out of game, plan events and guild meetings.. Checking it is mandatory! Ideally I would like everyone to check it once a day but sometimes that is not possible.. As a rule of thumb I say if you have time to log on that day then you have time to quickly hit the site.. You do not have to post, but at least browse and make sure there is no one asking you anything.. Posting and being a part of the community IS encouraged however..

  • Guild Services - It is expected you will offer services you have (Trade Skills) to guild mates at cost, of course the guild mate will pay for failures but I should not see a guild tailor selling backpacks at a 100% markup to guildmates. Standard rule should be going rate or better (within reason) and on custom work such as jewelry pay for all material (Including material eaten by failures)

  • Conduct - Guild members will conduct themselves with honor at all times! I do not ever want a report of someone with my guild tag engaged in a cursing match in OOC or SHOUT. I do not want reports of you training someone or doing anything to try and ruin the fun of another player. I will make it simple I DO NOT CARE why, just do not do it! If someone harasses you or is making your day unenjoyable then tell us.. Do not stoop to their level.

  • Participation - I want everyone to be an active member of the guild.. Participate on the message board in game. Attend meetings and generally try to be a part of the guild..

  • NO BEGGING - Whether it be for ports, resses, power leveling whatever it will not be tolerated! Of course if a cleric is available they will come res you and if a druid or wizard is available it is expected the port you at no cost but do NOT expect a guildmate to leave a group or drop what they are doing to come if they cannot! It is unreasonable and it is not fair to them. The same goes for powerleveling, I am not against it but do not hound the higher levels for it.. Powerleveling is NOT fun so I do not want to see anyone being guilt tripped into coming to help you. ***The ONLY Exception to these rules is if you have an UNRECOVERABLE corpse and need the services of either a Necromancer or a high level guildmate to get your body it is expected that you drop what you are doing and come to the aid of your guildmate!

Guild Ranks -

  • King and Queen - Have final say in anything that affects the guild.

  • Prince and Princess - Have official control of the guild should the King and Queen be away.

  • Duke and Dutchesses - The lowest rank for Guild Officers special titles are conferred to Dukes and Dutchesses indicating their responsibilities.

  • Nobles - The highest non officer rank. While not Officers Nobles are able to sponsor new recruits.

  • Subject - The lowest rank for a member of the guild. Subjects are not allowed to recruit new members on their own word but on the word of any of my members I will take candidates under advisement. A good word from even a subject will go a long way towards someone getting a sponsor.. Just as a bad word will go a long way to them really having to prove themself.

  • Peasant - This is technically a non guild rank.. Peasants are guilded after gaining a sponsor and evaluated for 30 days. At the end of that span they are either voted into the guild and promoted to the rank of Subject or disbanded from the guild. If a peasant does not make the cut I will make it a point to give as much reason without breaking any secrecy over who may have cast the deciding vote.

It it important to realize that although Subjects and Nobles do not get an 'Official' vote in guild matters that we always ask for feedback before a vote.. We DO value the feedback and often will act on the majority of the guild's feelings. But however the officers do still lead the guild, that is the responsibility that comes with the rank.. We ask only that you place your trust in us to run it well.

Guild Coffer and Dues -

  • We have no official dues, no one pays anything to be a member of the guild.

  • Donations to the guild are appreciated and will either be given to guildmates who need them by the guild quartermaster or traded away for plat or items we can use.

  • If you need gear post what you need and what purpose it serves in the trading forum, within reason we will do everything in our power to see to it you get the gear you need.. Do NOT be insulted if you do not immediately get what you ask for, I try to see to it that everyone gets what they need but in the end it is the responsibility of the quartermaster to do the trading.. Trust me it's better that way, I would get schooled trying to trade for zombie skins. If you feel your being discriminated against or feel you really need something and it's not being bought feel free to bring it up with me.. I will do everything I can to work it out for you.

  • Above all I want to see everyone getting stuff... I do not want the impression that only certain people get equipment and others do not.

  • A VERY important thing of note!! Most equipment purchased for you by the guild is owned BY THE GUILD!! Unless specifically stated it is not yours to trade away and is being given to you in good faith that you are not planning to jump ship, and if you do decide to leave then guild owned equipment will be returned..

Guild Hunts -

  • We will try as often as possible to host guild run events.. Whether they be Big Game Hunts, or Dungeon Crawls your participation is not required but encouraged..

  • If there is a level limit on a raid do not be discouraged or take it personally if you do not get to go.. Some zones it is perfectly acceptable to have a character 10 levels too low and no one dies (Except maybe that person) However there are other zones where such a person can place the entire raiding party at great risk.. In these cases level limits are spelled out and are NOT NEGOTIABLE do not have hurt feelings, the level limits are there for your protection and the protection of those on the raid..

  • We will try to have personalized awards or ribbons for each raid of note we host.. Some small bit of prestige for your character.. 

Guild Awards -

  • This is not set in stone yet, I will elaborate further in the future BUT we will have an award system.. usually just ribbons awarded to your character on the roster for acts such as unprecedented valor in the face of danger, attending raids, helping the guild, etc.. We have not settled on details but if guildmembers do something extraordinary I want them recognized for it.

Meetings -

  • The guild holds meetings about once a month, these meetings are MANDATORY! Barring RL complications such as having to work or an emergency.. If you cannot make it to a meeting at the posted time then post with WHY before the meeting.

  • Post after the meeting if an emergency happens and you cannot post beforehand, or e-mail me.

  • If you make a habit of not showing up to meetings for no reason other than you simply forgot or did not feel like coming then you will be disbanded. I plan meetings around times people can make it, if enough people cannot make it then I reschedule.. It is VERY rude to the guildmembers who are online at the set time and travel to the meeting spot or leave groups to be there just to have people not show.

  • I will be clear, if you have RL Obligations such as work ETC I have no problem with you missing the meetings, I just like to know beforehand.. The only time this rule applies to you is if you do not post and just do not show up at the meeting.

Discipline -

  • There are several levels of discipline involved in the guild, the first would be verbal warning, the second demotion in rank (Yes a subject CAN be demoted to Peasant in which case they are under evaluation again for possible dismissal from the guild), and dismissal from the guild. 

  • A warning will be given on any minor occurrences such as missed meetings, or conduct that does not reflect the values of the guild, such as Killstealing, Cowardice, or Non participation in guild events.

  • Demotion can occur if enough warnings are given on a specific problem, or if your actions do not reflect your post.. IE I expect much better manners and conduct from my officers than my subjects, such is the burden of the role you should be looked up to. For a demotion to occur it must pass by 3/4 vote among the officers, if an officer is being voted on they do not get a vote in the matter.

  • Dismissal from the guild will occur only in the most severe instances, such as disrespect towards a fellow guildmate, insubordination towards an officer, or flagrant violations of Verant's rules. Dismissal from the guild must pass a 100% vote from the officers. In the event you are dismissed from the guild you do have 30 days to appeal the matter to the Guild Master if you feel it was wrongfull.

  • In the event of disciplinary action the matter will be handled in e-mail privately.
