The Birth of the Immortalis Extrahi

Written by Shanar Nefandus

When Norrath was young, during the age of legends, when mankind was just beginning to walk the earth there was a great council of dragons.. Elder wurm of all colors were seated in the temple of veeshan, Veeshan herself the mother of all wurms there at their head, all of them to discuss these new races of mortals, Elves, Humans, Dwarves, and more.. Veeshan knew she could destroy them all while young but risked the wrath of the other gods to whom these beings were as sacred as her dragons were to her..

As a whole the council denounced the mortals, some with lifespans less than 100 years, as pathetic and meaningless to the dragon race, praising Veeshan as the wise broodmother, and turning a blind eye to mortals as a whole.. Judging them not worthy of the attention of their race, they would always be the dominant race on this planet. Such it had always been, such it would always be.. Surely none were mightier that veeshan or those she had created in her own image.

Each of the Elders echoed this in one voice except one.. Now they may be pathetic and weak, but one day I fear they will destroy us all he said, one day we will be hunted as they now hunt and kill wolves and bears.. There was a great rumbeling through the assembelage as more dragons courage was fueled by this one elders words, they spoke mirroring his fears.. Something must be done, the Mortals must not be left to grow unchecked! Soon the roomm was in chaos, both sides arguing back and forth, until above it all Veeshan herself howled, a piercing roar, all even the oldest of dragons crouched in pain at the powerfull roar.. This was the most angry any of them had ever seen her since the creation.. With a massive claw she stomped shattering the marble floor in the council room, and speaking in a roar which shook the room she addressed the one elder.. You think Mortals are a threat to us? to ME?! You DARE speak as if you know something I do not? YOU DARE QUESTION ME?!

No one spoke in the room, An aura of power and fear cloaked Veeshan's mighty form, her anger was awesome to behold, none even the eldest of dragons were immune.. All stood transfixed as she roared her judgement.. YOU who have dared speak that these pathetic and ridiculous creatures are worth any concern at all, will now live among them AS them, I banish you for all time from my temple, I give you their 'powerful' forms, sneering as she said the word 'powerful' to do with as you will.. And with that and a roared word of power each that had spoken to support the one elder were banished from the hall.. Each fell to the earth in mortal form, bodies twisted and broken, they each learned to their bitterness that while veeshan had indeed cursed them with mortal form, she had left them Immortal.. Each slowly healed, alone and lost in the world of men, shackled to the earth learning to live lives as Elves, Humans, Dwarves, and other races.

Shanar the one Elder who had dared speak against Veeshan landed among the dark elves.. As his body healed and he began tolearn the shadow arts from the house of the ebon mask in Neriak, he watched.. Years passed, Friends came and went, Many he knew when he was yound, now dead.. Jagd the human Monk, Citrine his partner, both human and both gone now.. Jaedran his childhood friend, a dark elf but gone the same.. The long lifespan of an elf means nothing when you make your living as an adventurer.. More friends than he could count all gone, but he watched as these mortal beings started in the forest he himself landed in body twisted and broken, and then becoming more bold these people ventured into the lavastorm mountains.. And then into Soluseks Eye.. And he knew who waited for them there, many who found him died.. Lord Nagafen, one of the many who said nothing when he was cast from the temple and cursed, one who eventually fell mortally wounded by these pathetic and wretched mortals.. It had begun, the Mortals grew in strength, grew in power, and grew in greed for more.. A lust for power never seen by dragonkind, these mortals possessed, no one can say why, but they never stopped.. Ever forging more powerful weapons, reserching magics so deadly they threatened to challenge the gods, and still they grew.

Then one day something happened, Shanar met another.. Valhalan who he found that he knew.. Looking into his eyes he knew Valhalan as one who had stood at his side the day of the banishing.. One who had the courage to speak with him, one of the cursed.. Slowly as time went by more of the cursed came to Shanar, no one can say after all this time what force has begun bringing us together, but one things is clear.. Veeshan was wrong, we were right.. Now we will have our vengeance..

The Dream

Written by Pyrrhic

I had a dream the other day, that I was a dragon trapped in a man's body. Now that I am awake, I'm not sure if this is true, or that I am a dragon dreaming that I am a man....

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------


"Spirits, why have you brought me here?" I groaned out loud, "I'm cold and tired, but mostly just confused."

'Always asking questions. If you stop talking once and awhile, you might not have to ask so many questions.'

Sighing, I sit down in the snow, shaded by the cliff wall behind me. Wisdom, the attribute most prized by shamans, I have found is usually comes hand in hand with patience. How long I sit, how many questions I ask, how much I yell, nothing will help me understand. Some things you just have to go through I've found.

I am awakened from my dozing by a loud crashing noise. Instantly awake and aware, I peer through the fog caused by the hot sun on the cold snow. Is this what has brought me here I wonder? As the mist part, I see the source of the noise, and instantly shrink back. Out of fear? Respect? Yes. For before me, crashing through the mists on land that is his by right and force, is the dragon.

Sontalak.

As the Great Wyrm makes his way back to his accustomed place, I cast a quick spell of invisibility upon myself and sit back to regroup. Sontalak? My reason for being here? Spirits, what could you be thinking? Then slowly, as dusk approaches, I come to my answer.

"So this is it, eh? After all this time, after all that has happened, you bring me to this?" I ask aloud in a weary voice. "They won't come, it's a waste of time you know."

/sigh

"Okay, okay, I will, if only because I don't like the silence."

somewhere, a spirits smiles....

Reaching out through arcane means, I initiate contact with the one I know I must talk with first. "...Trickster's Son, are you there?...I have a message from the spirits for you...hello?..." After a pause, I recieve my answer from the suprised Favoured Son of Bristlebane.

"Pyrrhic? It's been a long time old friend. What can I do for you?" he asks.

"I have a message for you from the spirits, make of it what you will. Sontalak is awake and roaming the world again."

"So? I'm not your leader anymore, remember?" he replies crisply.

"You never were my leader Shanar, you old rogue, I followed you. It's a very different thing. Any twit with an attitude can call himself a leader. It's in the hearts of those who follow where the real power lies. Who cares what title you give yourself old one, you are still the Shanar that followed."

Silence of an indeterminant length followed.....

"Pyrrhic, you spend too much time in Spirit World, you know that? Okay, so Sontalak is awake. What do you want of me?" comes the eventual reply.

"I think you know old dragon. Of the First Brood, Sontalak is the only one who escaped our wrath. We may be cast down by Veeshan herself, brought low into these mortal bodies, but Dragon at heart we still shall always be. And as you know, chief amongst our attributes is Pride, both our source of power and our greatest weakness as well. It is in the name of pride that we must put out a call to the others. There must be a Reckoning with Sontalak."

"Pyrrhic, what have you been smoking? Do you remember what was said? The insults? The accusations? Why would I put myself through that again? Are you insane? Nevermind that, we both know the answer, bloody half-crazed mystic! They won't come, you know that, don't you?"

Smiling a small smile to myself, it is my turn to stay silent. Let's see how someone else likes it...

"Alright, alright! FINE! Be that way!" comes the angry message. I think to myself, "Now Shanar knows why shamans are so cranky half the time, it's not getting killed alot that does it. It's the silence you can't fight that does it." Grinning an evil grin, I sit back and wait....

...and jump completely out of my skin as Shanar taps my shoulder. Bloodfire and Brimstone! Bloody sneaky rogues! Scaring a poor old man like that! "I'm gonna put a curse on you if you do that to me again!" I sputter in my shock. "Well, at least you brought Mianya. She can always rez me if you scare me to death again." She smiles her coy little smile at me, and we share a hug. "Glad you kept him safe all this time" I whisper in her ear so low that even his sharp ears won't pick it up. As we part, I turn to Shanar, his arms crossed, glaring at me. "I know, I know. I dragged you all the way out here on some fool quest, and no one else is going to show. I told the spirits this would happen, but they never listen."

"Well, almost no one" Shanar replies, looking behind me. Turning around, I wonder how I could have missed the steady stream of muttering behind me.

"Bloody hell, what am I doing here? What am I supposed to do? Bleed all over him? One little hammer and one glowing sword, and you expect me to stand face to face with a dragon and live. One little dark elf against the raging hordes, and what do I get to help me? Nothing, that's what! I probably won't even get my heal in time and I'm gonna die again, aren't I? Bloody hell!"

I swallow my smile as I step forward to grasp Slandius's hand. "Ever reliable Sal, should have known you would come too. By the way, you sure you are full-blooded dark elf? Shanar has all those masks, and sometimes you sound like a dwarf."

"Shut the fuck up, idiot shaman! Go eat yourself! Better yet, turn into a bear, I'm hungry and haven't killed anything all day!"

Harsh words, but I know with family you can talk like that. I know he doesn't mean it any more than I do. "So, four of us eh? Steep odds again I guess" I say.

"Five, six, seven and eight too, or has all that smoke blinded you too?" comes a voice from the fog. "Like we are going to let you guys have all the fun!"

"Woot! Zupdok, Alcaron, Copperman and EvilMiker! Glad you finally made it!"

"Sure, ignore the gnome, everyone else does. Small in stature, large in power, Master of Mana, still no hero's welcome!"

"Shush Ittie, they were getting to you I'm sure" Lillyian says as she smacks him on top of the head.

One by one, group by group, more old friends begin to show up. Ranger, paladin, beastlord and bard, our little circle begins to fill. Too many to name, but all just as important, my old friends gather here in the mist. It is awkward for some of us, remembering the harsh words spoken in the full heat of shock and anger. Mortals we may be, but only a dragon feels the heat of emotions, the chaos of passion! We all feel this, and whether we want to or not, we see the same fires burning in one another. Like a magnet, it is what draws us together, and eventually pushes us apart. How can we all be so proud, so assertive of our own individual importance, that we ever got together to begin with I wonder? How did we ever get anything done with all these strong personalities and vocal opinions? I guess when you see the same faces all the time, you forget about the spirit of the person behind the face, and just think about your own. I wonder if it's how we keep sane, keep ourselves happy. It's too much to ask of ourselves to think about anothers happiness when we can barely look after our own.

I look across the Wastes to Sontalak, and wonder if that is why he stands guard alone, why all dragons seem solitary. Maybe getting cast out by Veeshan was a blessing I muse, maybe these mortal forms hold more power than even we knew of? Why else do us mortals constantly group up, guild up, form friendships? What is it that draws us to each other when we are all just looking out for ourselves? Maybe there are lessons still to be learned, more of the Spirit Path I have to travel before I can understand. But before that, there are spells to cast, a battle to fight.

"Everone ready?" Mianya asks us all. We nod or mumble yes, giving her our assent one by one. A calm overcomes this valley, in expectation of the battle about to be fought. We all eventually turn towards the one who kept us together all that time ago, in happier days, and the silence is deafening me.

"All right! Let's make this shit dead!" comes the battlecry, and we all begin the dance of battle, each contributing in our own unique way. Why, even the rogues are doing more than running around looking busy!

Minutes, hours, who can keep track of time in the midst of a battle? Does time even exist? Finally, with a roar of suprise and shock, the one dragon that has eluded our grasp falls to his side and shivers in his death throes. What a mighty battle! Let the bards tell the tale, I have not the words!


In the aftermath of the chaos, I find myself beside a bloody and even blacker Shanar. We sit in silence for a few minutes, our gaze falling over our comrades on the battlefield. Finally, he groans and begins to stand.

"Guess we better go tally and give out the loot, eh?" Shanar wheezes. "Isn't that why we do this again and again? Just imagine how powerful some of us will get with this dragons hoard! What do you want out of it anyways?"

I pause at the world-weary voice of my old companion-in-arms. I look at the solitary dragon corpse, dead and suddenly seeming very alone. I look around at all my old companions, see them talking, sharing tales of their prowess, and I think of the Path that the spirits make me walk. I have a small flash of insight, and I begin to understand anew. Spirits above, I remember now!

"Well shaman? You gonna sit there with a grin on your face all day? What do you want out of this mess?"

"Shanar, you old trickster, I have what I came here for already. I had it all along, and got too caught up in this game of life to see it. I have great memories, alot of friends I didn't have before, and a few stories to share with those willing to listen. Thank you for this grand adventure you have taken me on, and the kindness you have shown to me when you didn't have to. Thank you for giving me a family to call my own, even for an all too short a time. Let those who care for it claim the spoils of battle, for I remember what truely has value in this world. I am, and forever will be at heart, Immortalis Extrahi."
