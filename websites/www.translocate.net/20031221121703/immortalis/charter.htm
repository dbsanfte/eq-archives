This guild was founded on a concept many believe in. That you can raid, accomplish big things in EQ, and also have fun and do so without EQ becoming a 'job'. So we raid 1-3 times weekly, these raids are NOT mandatory but strongly encouraged and your time is essentially your own.

Now for the Important Stuff, Our Charter, Rules, and Requirements for Membership!

Recruiting -
Membership in the guild Requires a main who is at least Level 54, if you are at least level 54 then you post an application in the Lost Dragons forum and work on obtaining 2 sponsors in the guild if you have not already.. Upon gaining 2 sponsors you will be guilded as a probationary member.. This period can last anywhere from a couple of weeks to several months, it really depends on how quickly you make an impact on people. Now that is not to say you can apply then remain an applicant for 4 months never playing. If you are not SERIOUS about pursuing membership in our guild then do not waste our time, we are a fun and friendly guild and are looking for more members who want to be part of that, not just another tag in game and a tick on the roster. our current "need" for classes is as follows:

Conduct -
We expect every member of this guild to conduct themselves with honor at all times. We should NEVER see any of you engaged in a shouting match in a public channel, nor should we ever get a report that you are training people, or doing anything because 'someone else started it' your actions reflect on all of us.

We expect each of you to act like the family you are, of course you will fight, fights happen. Keep it civil though, remember you are all part of the same guild and are working towards the same goals. Too easy people get pissed off and just toss away months or years of friendship in anger.. Think before you speak, and cool off before saying something you may regret.

Trade Skills We expect will be offered to your guild mates at cost, including failures of course.

Porting and Ressing.. Yes you can expect to get a port or a res if someone is available.. NEVER expect someone to leave a raid or a group to come res or port you RIGHT AWAY though.. Be patient.

Above all though look at the first line, conduct yourselves with Honor.. and Make us proud.

Participation -
We expect people to be active members in the guild.. Posting on the message board, grouping in game, and attending guild events all plays into that.. If you are the anti social type or prefer soloing to actually playing with your friends in game then your probably not going to fit in here.

We EXPECT people to be checking the boards, Nothing and we mean NOTHING pisses us off more than planning a raid for a week on the board, in the middle of forming up at the planned time having people sign in and ask "Hey is anything going on, or Why is everyone in X Zone.." So CHECK THE BOARD! Simple rule of thumb we go by. If you have time to log into EQ then you have 5 minutes to browse the board to see what is going on.. Posting is not required but it is encouraged.

Raiding -
We raid 1-3 times weekly depending on demand and our raids are NOT manditory but ARE strongly encouraged so please make an effort to attend them if at all possible.

Raid Loot -
Loot will be distributed according to the members wish lists handed in quarterly and the officers reserve the right to default any items not on wish lists to members we feel who may benfit from them most. (for more infromation about the wish list system please contact your class officer)

Discipline -
Possibly the shittiest part of running a guild, but alas a necissary one.. There will be times when a member of this guild just crosses the line, and something needs to be done.

There will be varying levels of discipline, usually starting with the guild leader talking to you or your class officer and explaining what the problem is. Whether that be a personal problem, or a complaint made against you from within the guild or outside guild. Most people once they know there is a problem work to remedy it, we do not ask anything unreasonable. If they choose to ignore the problem then it should come as no surprise if further action is taken.

After a verbal talk this progresses and can very well skip ahead based on severity. Actions include Verbal Warning, Official Warning, Demotion, or Probation, and Dismissal from the Guild.. Do NOT think for a moment that anyone is above the rules in this guild. Our loyalty and dedication lie in making this an awesome guild to be in.. If someone is not on board with that then we will address the problem.
