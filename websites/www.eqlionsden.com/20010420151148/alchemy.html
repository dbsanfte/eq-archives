Alchemy

  

 

Alchemy is a very important trade skill available to only the shaman class. Alchemy is a trade skill in which the shaman can produce magical potions with several different effects. The effects mainly have a positive effect, however, some potions carry a harmful side-effect. Alchemy, isn't exactly very cheap. Beginning the trade alchemy can cost lots of time, and platinum. Choosing to pursue alchemy to its maximum extent can ultimately cost you lots of time and money, but it does have its rewards in the end.


In order to use a potion after you make it, you must right click on it. This will result in a small loading time where you are actually drinking the potion. The effect of the potion is instant once you drink it. 


What do I need to make potions?

The first thing you need to make a potion, is that you must be at least level 25. 


Once you do that, go to your hometown or any other town with an alchemist and see your alchemy dealer, and buy a "medicine bag". The last thing you need are the mixing ingredients, which can vary for different spells.


How do I make a potion?

To make a potion, you need to buy the correct ingredients for the potion you want to make. Lets say for instance you want to make a "Potion of Lesser Stability." This potion requires the two ingredients of Lucerne, and Sage Leaf. It also requires a skill of '25' to be trivial.  The more you make a potion the more your skill increases. However once you reach the trivial level, you stop gaining point increases in your skill. This means its time to create more difficult potions to raise your skill.


Step 1. Place your ingredients into your "medicine bag."


Step 2. Click the "Combine" button on your "medicine bag."


Step 3. Dependent on your skill, you will either make or fail the potion.


Where do I buy ingredients?

You can buy your alchemy herbs and ingredients at several different locations. Some locations sell different ingredients than other locations. It may require some traveling to get the exact ingredient you want.


When buying ingredients, make sure you keep your charisma in mind. The higher your charisma is the better prices you will get on your alchemy ingredients. The maximum charisma you can have to get better prices is 110 charisma. We recommend  you max out your charisma or bring it relatively close before buying too many major ingredients. Shaman also have several charisma boosting spell buffs that can help as well.


Faction is also relatively important. Try to keep a positive standing with most merchants so you get the best possible prices with the dealers.

  • Grobb - located near the shaman guild.

  • Paineel - In a store called "Poison Petal" in an upstairs building near the bank.

  • Halas - located right next to the shaman guild.

  • Oggok - located near the bank in a room with decorative skulls.

  • Kerra Ridge - located right next to the Temple of Kerrans.

  • Fironia Vie - located on the docks .

  • Cabilis - in the middle of West Cabilis in a small hut.
