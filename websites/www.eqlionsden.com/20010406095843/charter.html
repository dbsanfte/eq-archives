Lion's Den Charter

  
 

Charter

Loyalty, pride and bravery are three words, which describe The Lion’s Den.

Members of The Lion’s Den are loyal to each other and are there for one another in there time of need. We are loyal to our beliefs in that everyone should be treated in such a way, as we would like to be treated, with respect and maturity

We take pride in our Den and our skills and abilities as individuals. Each person brings about his or her own characteristics and skills, yet not one person is above any other. Once welcomed into our Den…our Pride …our Family, one’s skills, abilities, and strong characteristics are fused to the over all bring of the Den, thus making it stronger. A strong and highly organized pride thrives and lives to hunt another day!

Bravery! Carpe Diem, seize the day, willing to charge into the great unknown and reach new and thought to be unreachable heights, forever broadening our horizons and setting new standards, and forever raising the bar and reaching ever so higher. A Den member has the readiness to sacrifice one’s life so that another may see another sunset or accomplish a long awaited goal, and yet knowing when admit defeat and to retreat so that he or she may live to fight another day.

This is The Lion’s Den. We are The Pride.

Amedeus Leonidas
Paladin of Truth

" Loyalty, pride, and bravery…..No less, forever more!"

Rules of Conduct

No begging Nothing in life is free. One is rewarded for hard work! To be rewarded for hard work, instills pride in one’s self. Begging shows feebleness in one and as well as one’s associates.
No rude or disruptive conduct Everything you do reflects upon the guild, your brothers and sisters. Disruptive or obnoxious behavior will not be tolerated! It embarrasses yourself as well as the guild. No obscene language in guild chat! We have members that are young and may be monitored by parents.
Dueling Dueling is not allowed within the guild to settle any disputes or to try and overpower a fellow guild mate. However it is allowed for educational emphasis. Reason being that hardships may develop amongst members.
Dueling outside of guild If you chose to duel outside of guild vicinity, you are to do so with honor and maturity no matter the conduct of your adversary. Pride members are to always promote themselves in such a way. If you agree to a duel, you must fight to the death! You may not flee (unless the duel has been declared by both to be Non-death)! Should you die in combat, die with dignity! Recover your corpse, bow before your opponent and go about your business. NO rude comments OR gestures!
Power leveling  A member of this pride is not to ask, beg or pay to be power leveled! However another member may offer out of generosity.
Trade skills Members are to charge other members the cost of materials or have the buying member purchase the materials himself. Let it be known that a tradesman does fail! Be prepared to purchase additional amounts! Master tradesman may decide to form quests to be completed by fellow members in order to obtain needed materials. All quests are to be posted in full detail, along with rewards and materials needed.
Grouping Try to group with other members of your level as much as possible. Get to know each other and each other’s weaknesses.

 

Positional Roles
Leaders
  • Monitors conduct of officers, and members
  • Forms guild alliances
  • Finalizes dates of events or raids
  • Assigns officer hood to members
  • Has final say in removal of a member if needed
  • Recruits
  • Forms meetings for officers
Officers
  • Monitors conduct of members and composes reports to be given to leaders
  • Recruits
  • Votes on events and raids
  • Must attend all all meetings, events, and raids
  • Makes suggestions for events and raids
Members
  • Recruits others but only an officer may formally invite a person into the guild
  • Makes suggestions for events and raids
 
*When recruiting a person, question their character! Explain the rules to him or her or have them read it on website. Should they see the rules to be unviable,  they are not welcomed.
