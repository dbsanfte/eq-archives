Blacksmithing

  
 
   Blacksmithing is a skill which involves ore, water, molds, and a forge to create a diverse amount of items.  These range from arms and weaponry to  pots and cookie cutters.  This is a trade skill that takes plenty of time and platinum to master.  Below find all current recipes as well as where to get your supplies.
