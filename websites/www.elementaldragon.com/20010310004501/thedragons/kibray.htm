Bringin' Back The Gods

 





















 
Kibray

Dark Elf Enchantress

 
Dark Elves, as you may know, have always disturbed me slightly. None more so than the Enchantress Kibray, who's disarming intellect and dry wit can flummox even my over active tongue. Draw your own conclusions (and draw your sword to be on the safe side)

Same old thing...stare at the spellbook, ready the dagger, stand up and summon into the world the only protection I have left beyond my own ability to manipulate the world.

"Hello, Gebarn. You're not so weak as the others. Let's go."

I veer off the path towards the corpse of a hapless human who'd wandered too close to the Guards. Stupid. Deserved to die. They all do, really. That's what my brother said. He hated them so--but that didn't keep them from killing him. That all-so-powerful hate of the Father--it was nothing in the face of stupidity. It hadn't saved him. It never would. It was empty. So my brother left me too. And Gebarn will perish tonight. And the bone chips I harvest--they may be my brother's or my mother's or my father's. It doesn't matter.

If I know anything, it's that faith will not protect you. It will only lead to the kind of stupidity that leaves a corpse like this. Faithless Innorouk! You abandoned my people! You abandoned my brother--and I will not forgive you. I will twist the world and see you punished...betrayer!

So, purposefully, I grind my heel into the corpse's back. His god had abandoned him too. See? And, I repeat the rhyme my brother taught me:

Trod upon my foe
Deal the killing blow
Trod upon my kin
Toughen my own skin

It's hardly a step away that a fallen Teir'dal lies--cousin of a cousin's cousin. And it's just as satisfying to hear his bones crack too. But the rhyme still seems incomplete...something about those dreams I've been having.

Trod upon the earth
I will see the dragon's birth

That's it. Sounds right.
I step back onto the path--and walk out of the forest. The earth is everywhere.

 I stop to grind my heels into it--just to make sure it knows.
