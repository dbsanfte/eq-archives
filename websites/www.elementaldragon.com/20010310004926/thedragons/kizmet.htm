Bringin' Back The Gods

 





















 
Kizmet

Human - Bard
Bards, a most unusual class of people. Flighty, frivolous, smart and keen. They offer spirit to any saddened company, and life to any party. None comes better than our dear Kizmet, she of the flashing green eyes

You enter a tavern in North Freeport… Your journey has been long, your battle weary. Ale is ordered and you begin to erase the haunting calls of ghouls and skels that plague your mind… You sigh…Weary, oh so weary… You slam back your ale and order another… and for an instant you hear
music in the distance… You almost think you are losing your mind when suddenly the tavern doors open and a woman strolls in, wrapped in mystery, singing a song the lifts the hearts of all those who hear…. Her smiles are generous, her way irreverent… Her eyes sweep across the room and settle on
you… Merriment, laughter and something else… She winks at you and you feel a warmth rising in your cheeks, battles forgotten your interest exists only for her and the stories she weaves… The bar begs for song after song… She switches from baudy to romantic in an instant … Her laughing eyes
find everyone in the room, drawing them closer, more drinks are ordered and the atmosphere becomes electric… Wit and grace her only garb, her cup is never empty! It is after the 12th song just as you begin to realize that the evening is late and your bed soft that she sits down on a center tabletop, high above the crowd… Her music softening, growing serious in rhythm and rhyme… It is as if the song itself has acquired a soul of its own, wrapping the entire room in the hushed air of
unspoken things… You realize this is her last song and the sadness of good things ending fills your soul. The crowd quiets as she begins her sing … It is a tale of a dragon, a symbolic song of elements… Your whole soul resonates to this music… And her words burn your being… Myth yet not quite myth… You find yourself confused, enthralled and strangely curious… On the finishing note, her eyes meet yours, and she smiles… Standing gracefully, she bows with flourish, accepting
coppers and silver from all those around… You feel reality and begin to question the magic of moments ago with a hard cynicism that comes with long battles and even longer travels… You slam another ale, and stand, throwing some coinage on the table… It is as you move towards the door that you find yourself staring into a pair of devilishly green eyes… She bows to you laughing, taking in your battle worn exterior… "Milord Warrior, I hope you too enjoyed my song… For you have the appearance of one who has fought hard with the need for much joy and happiness at the end of your day…I am Kizmet, Minstrel to the Wyrm and Friend to All…"
