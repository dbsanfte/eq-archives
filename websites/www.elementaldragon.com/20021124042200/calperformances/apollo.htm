Press Release from Cal Performances

AUDITIONS FOR APOLLO THEATER AMATEUR NIGHT ON TOUR
SATURDAY, DECEMBER 14, 2002, AT ZELLERBACH HALL

Harlem’s Original Amateur Artist Showcase Comes to Cal Performances as part of a
40-city Nationwide Tour, Friday, January 31, 2003 at Zellerbach Hall

BERKELEY, October 22, 2002—Whether you’re part of a gospel group, a jazz trio, a barbershop quartet, or a jug band; if you’re a medium, a comedian, or a concert violinist; if you can break dance with the best of them, play baroque in your Birkenstocks, or just want to sing your heart out, you’ll have your shot at the “Big Time” when Apollo Theater Amateur Night on Tour comes to Cal Performances.

Local auditions for Apollo Theater Amateur Night on Tour will be held Saturday, December 14, 2002, from 10:00 a.m. – 6:00 p.m. in Zellerbach Hall (intersection of Telegraph Ave. and Bancroft Way) on the UC Berkeley campus.

Harlem’s Apollo Amateur Night is perhaps the edgiest and most famous amateur artist showcase in the world, having launched the careers of so many superstars that it has a legitimate claim to being the most successful star search vehicle in show biz history. Now the original amateur competition takes to the road in a national star search tour, presented in the Bay Area by Cal Performances at 8:00 p.m., January 31, 2003 at Zellerbach Hall. The Apollo Amateur Night show began in 1934, and is celebrated for discovering and launching the careers of some of the biggest stars in entertainment, including Ella Fitzgerald, Billie Holiday, Sarah Vaughan, James Brown, Gladys Knight, Stevie Wonder, The Jackson 5, Luther Vandross, D’Angelo, Lauryn Hill and Sisquo, to name only a few.

AUDITION INFORMATION

Talented amateurs from all walks of life are encouraged to polish their acts and take them to Zellerbach Hall on Saturday, December 14, 10:00 a.m. – 6:00 p.m. for Amateur Night auditions. Variety is the operative word in selecting Amateur Night contestants: such diverse acts as dulcimer players, blues singers, Appalachian clog-dancers, female impersonators, rock bands and performers of the most unusual instruments have been known to share the Amateur Night stage on the same evening.

A piano will be provided during auditions, and contestants must provide their own accompanist. Tape and CD sound systems will also be available for applicants using recorded music to accompany their audition. A house band and back line will not be provided for the audition. For the final performance on January 31, vocal performers are encouraged to perform with the house band. If the use of the house band is required, musical charts or sheet music and a professional quality tape must be received no less than two-weeks in advance of the show date.

From the audition process, 10 to 12 acts will be chosen to compete at the Apollo Theater Amateur Night on Tour performance on Friday, January 31, 2003 at Zellerbach Hall. The winner of the Apollo Theater Amateur Night on Tour contest will receive a $1,000 cash prize, two American Airlines plane tickets to New York and the opportunity to compete live on the world famous Apollo Theater stage in Harlem.

The Apollo Theater defines an amateur as an aspiring performing artist who does not have a recording contract with a major record label, has not headlined at major performing arts venues, has not performed in major motion pictures, and is not known beyond the local presenter’s region within the genre for which they are auditioning. The decision as to whether or not someone is an amateur will be made by the Apollo staff based on available information.

Auditioners will be scheduled in two-hour time blocks on December 14. Auditioners are encouraged to sign-up in advance; walk-up auditioners will be accepted only on a “time-permitting” basis. Amateur performers and groups wishing to audition for Apollo Theater Amateur Night on Tour may call the Cal Performances, Amateur Night talent coordinator, Laura Abrams at (510) 642-3691 to receive an audition application and to schedule an audition.

APOLLO THEATER AMATEUR NIGHT ON TOUR

Cal Performances’ presentation of Apollo Theater Amateur Night on Tour will take place at 8:00 p.m., Friday, January 31, 2003 at Zellerbach Hall. Apollo Amateur Night personalities include the New York Kings of Comedy/Def Jam comedians Talent and Capone (“The Gangster of Comedy”) as show co-hosts; C.P. Lacey as “The Executioner” aka Sandman, who ushers acts off stage that can’t quite cut it; and Monijae, from TV’s “The Parkers,” the hostess with the mostest. The Amateur Night house band, Ray Chew & the Crew will be on hand to provide musical inspiration and accompaniment for the raucous and fun-filled evening.

Apollo Amateur Night raises the performance bar for up-and-coming talent. Its goal is to test the metal and skills of a diverse line-up of amateur talent as they compete against one another to win the approval and support of the legendary star-making—but discriminating—audience. Never a shy crowd, Amateur Night audiences are as legendary as the stars they have created over the years as they bluntly vocalize their pleasure—or displeasure—with each contestant act. Performing before an Amateur Night crowd can be daunting for the less than determined, as many have been known to quake in their boots as they await their chance before the footlights.

TICKET INFORMATION

The Apollo Theater Amateur Night on Tour auditions on December 14 are free to applicants; no tickets required. Auditions are closed to the public. Tickets for Apollo Theater Amateur Night on Tour at 8:00 p.m. Friday, January 31, 2003 are priced at $20.00, $30.00 and $40.00, and are available through the Cal Performances Ticket Office at Zellerbach Hall; at (510) 642-9988 to charge by phone; at www.calperfs.berkeley.edu; and at the door. Half-price tickets are available for purchase by UC Berkeley students. UC faculty, staff, senior citizens and other students receive a $2 discount. For more information, call the Cal Performances Ticket Office at (510) 642-9988 or visit the Cal Performances web site at www.calperfs.berkeley.edu.
