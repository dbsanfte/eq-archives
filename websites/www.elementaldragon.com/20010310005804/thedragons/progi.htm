Bringin' Back The Gods

 





















 
Progi Smith

Gnome Necromancer

 

Dark, never ending pain.

The kind of pain that makes you wish you could end it all.

It grows and consumes your every thought! PAIN, PAIN? Suffering and misery. PAIN, PAIN?!!

Quite slumber.

The pain is somehow lifted, peacefulness has been granted.

Now that death has taken your body, your soul remains. How do you feel about the pain of your life, how do you seek revenge on those that would have caused you such pain. Somehow you manage to find your way into the darkness? seeking answers and working on solutions.

In the most dank, disgusting crawl spaces of putrid waste, you discover the answer. What you thirst for is the life of others, to inflict pain and to drain the very thing that they find as most precious. Life.

I awoke amidst the muck and stench to find that the new smells and textures had been drawing me too them while I had been trying to avoid them. There were others like me, small in stature. Quick to judge and manipulative in all that is unnatural. Living in darkness, Traveling in the shadows - Has a certain quality in it.

Over the years, many mistakes have occurred. I thought that being within a clan, a brotherhood you might say of same sized and evenly focused minds would provide some of the answers I seek. Alas, this has not been the case and the clockwork brotherhood has become more of a legend, a dead myth.

Many seasons had passed and something was missing from my soul. A community, a kinship is something that I know I lack. In passing I came across a tall furry, unevenly bumpy figure that looked down at me and said. Oh? How cute! A black widow spider that can talk! Holding back the pain I wished to inflect, I informed this female that I was not a spider. But that I was the darkest of all creatures to ever walk the face of Norrath.

She giggled back at me and said, "ok, Spidyperson".

A magical essence sprang forth from her fingers that made me feel almost alive.

We spoke and she told me of stories of things I did not know and I did the same. We talked and talked and when it was over I said that this has been a wonderful feeling. She informed me that there was others that had not come across my race before and that she would like for me to meet with them and possibly join a family. A kinship, a community of sorts that do not discriminate against others just because of their height or beliefs.

Friends of old and of new were part of the family. Elotapper, Kiko, Makebleed and Winnie to name a few. Things were doing well and then the leader of the family decided that we should join forces with another clan. One that I did not know, but decided that if the family was going to join, so would I.

The total amount of people that made up this combined troupe was awesome! The power! We could form a family function and swim into the very depths of evil without worry. Even though the experience gained was less than I had started and the loot even more so, it made for enjoyment.

Raiding the undead or assisting others in quests became a heavy chore. The isolation that I needed to fulfill my own desires came with the discovery of a new playground for my pet and me. A wonderful place for all that was evil and corrupt.

I spent much time in the darkness of overthere and may return from time to time to play with the kitty's and to water the cactus.

Apparently while I was away, there was a cloud of dispair hovering around the family. The darkness consumed the family and the stench laid most all to rest. The plague is my friend and I know of its smell, because it was what I was born from.

Those mentioned above and many more passed away. Some ran off into the darkness to find a lost passion.

I began to wander the mountains of Rath and all the Karana's looking for a companion a new lease on life. As I was walking, I stumbled across a cave. Inside there was a motley crew of humans. All clean shaven with smiles on their face. Made me sick to glance at them!

I offered my services and in turn they provided me with a reason to continue in my quest for pain.

 With a mighty yell and fingers in a knot, I tore all that was good and wholesome away from me.  Piled in a blob of shiny and new was a pool of life.  I laid down two bone chips beside the goo.

 Upon my lips, in a voice so loud, I did say:  Cronos Uranus HECATONCHEIR !!!

As the goo soaked into the bones they started to form a mass that moaned before me.  I looked down at what I created.  Naked and pale, I helped its bloody mass into the lake.

Humm… I do think this may just work.  A part of me that I do not have need for can do my bidding.  Its not a son, Its not a brother.  Its me, but not me.

I shall name it Igorp.  Training on walking, gathering of food and hunting should be it’s basis for existence as the good side of me had no real use for magic.

Left loose in the mountains it should be able to survive or die, either way it is of no reflection of me.
