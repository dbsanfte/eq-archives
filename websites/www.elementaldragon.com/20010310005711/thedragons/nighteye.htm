Bringin' Back The Gods

 





















 
Nighteye

Dark Elf - Rogue

 

My story starts when I was abandoned as a young child in the streets of Neriak. With nowhere to go, I was forced to thievery to survive the day-to-day trials of life. For three years I prayed to the gods for a better life. Instead, I was captured and enslaved by a greedy merchant and forced to do his bidding. I barely escaped with my life after a year of his tormenting me. I found myself again living with the rats of the city. I prayed, oh how I prayed to the gods, but my prayers fell on deaf ears. However, as time went on, my skills in thieving increased and I had learned how to fight from two local rogues, Braeven and Dradk. Soon my skills had began to match those who had taught me. I had stopped praying and now decided to take matters into my own hands. I had risen from the depths of Neriak and realized that I had completed that task on my own and without the help of the gods. But I still felt empty. Why had the gods forsaken me and let me suffer? I need an answer, for my soul shall not rest until I have faced the gods and tell them to answer for their ignorance. I believe the ELEMENTAL DRAGONS can and will help me and everyone else who wants an answer from the gods. With this, I sincerely request a chance to prove myself worthy in aiding in the DRAGONS' quest to bring back the gods.
