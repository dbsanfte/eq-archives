Bringin' Back The Gods

 





















 
Kiko Trueheart

Half-Elf Druid

Keeper of the Square

 

When a person is unsettled in their early years, often they will become shy, retracted and untrusting. Often they will lack morals having had no teacher, and no role model. Despite Kiko's difficult past, he conducts himself with a reverence and discipline. After much pushing, he eventually revealed his story to us. It reveals many a great hardship that we had all suspected.


Some questions arose last eve about my past. It seemed to me that if it were a concern before now you would have asked and not questioned me at such an inopportune time when I was neither willing or ready to speak of such matters.

Because I consider some of you companions and others as true friends I ask of you now only one question. Why haven't you queried before of my past? And since the topic ranged so last eve I shall sit here now and tell you my story.

My story is not full death or murder or unhinged hatred to the GODS who left us to our despair on this world. No it is not a sad tale at all. But a true tale, of people, of family, of friends, and of rage.

My father you see was a nobleman of Qeynos, named Alexun Tuyre. A well respected land owner and partaker in those doings which seperate Noble blood from peasant blood. Not a giver at all but a taker of, well whatever he wanted. Taxes from those that lived on his land. Taxes from those that
crossed his land. Taxes from those that shopped in his stores, and bought the fruit of his land at the markets. The one thing though he never had was love. Not to say he did not have women. He had plenty of those. The whores from qeynos adored my father. And the Peasant women from his land were, well lets just say not willing but assumed the responsibilty of paying their families debts when they were charged. My fathers reputation was well known throughout Antonica and no maiden or Courtier of any dignity would been seen with my father. My father I am sure begat many sons and daughters before me but claimed none of them and I know not of them till this very day.

My mother, Neriah Espeeri was a Wood Elf from Greater Faydark who made her home in the Tree city of Kelethin. She was also of Noble blood. A daughter of an Elven Prince. She learned the ways of the Forest and so became what they, the elves, call a giver of life. She spent most of her time hepling the younger Elves of Faydark learn the history of the Elven Nation. And learn the lore from the other continents. She was a healer and great teacher.

So you ask how they came to meet, My father on one side of the world, and my mother the other.

The elven King sent his son to Antonica on what he called a peace and good will misson. A mission to spread the Elven culture thorughout the Human lands. And since his daughter, my mother, was a great teacher at the time he brought her with him on this journey along with a league of other Elven Masters. They traveled to the Cities thoroughout Antonica spreading the Elven Word. To freeport, To rivervale, to all the quaint Inns between them. Then to Surfall Glade and finally a stop in Qeynos. This is where it all began. The Elves Set up a camp on the land of My father, for adequate compensation of course. And every night the Humans of Qeynos and the surrounding hills would come to the
camps for song and dance and lessons in the arts of the great Elven Nation, some of which taught by
my mother.

One night Alexun ventured into one of my Mothers classes. Now dont get me wrong, my father was a Handsome and Charming man, and better at wooing women who did not know his reputation. And so the courtship began. My mother was not akin to being wooed in such a manner. Alexun began
spending more time with Neriah and as time passed Neriah and Alexun fell in love, and were soon to be married. The people of the Elvish community could not believe what they heard but they also respected Neriahs Decision.

The elves packed up and left, Neriah stayed behind and moved into the Big house. At least I remeber it as being real big. Especially for the Tiny Wood elf. As for being married I beleive no religion would accept the two so they lived together out of wedlock. Which left many openings for my father to continue in his ways. They soon had a child. Yes you guessed it. They named him Kiko. A fine strapping baby boy with the looks of his father and the wisdom of his mother. It wasnt long before My mother got wind of My fathers doings. She stuck it out for five full seasons before she fealt it best that she packed here stuff and take us back to her home of Kelethin.

My father did not like this at all. She threw Neriah out and kept me with him. Just to hurt her I suppose. Heartboroken Neriah left to return back to Kelethin without her son.

Now my father decided I was too much to handle at age 5. And when he started being chastised and started to realize that a Half-Elf child was not welcome in his social circle he cast me out to the peasants on his land to be raised by them. At age 6 I learned to work the land and respect the land
and at age 8 my adopted family packed up their things in the middle of the night and moved us to a place called the Common Lands.

Several Years passed and I took an internship with a Druid who frequented the ruins in the Commonlands. It seemed to be a place of great magic but I just used it to think. When I reached 17 I decided I was strong enough to leave the CommonLands and try and find my Mother.

I ventured through Freeport where I learned the wrath of the Dark Elves. And rode a ship to what they call the Butcher Block Mountains. I learned Swordsmanship from the Dwarves and also the allure of the brew. Then I travelled to Kelethin, the great Tree city. And soon learned that Half-Elves were outcast by both humans and the wood-Elves. I found the home of my Mother but it was too late. She had died a year earlier from an unknown disease. I also learned that I had two brothers, of full Wood Elf blood. Shanden and Gherak. When I went to meet them they chastised me as being the reason their mother was not happy. They allowed me stay in the home for they knew my mothers love for me. But always looked down on me. I tried to win them, for i needed a family but it did not work. So I left there also and returned to Freeport to call that my home.

Now since then my father has died as well. But I am not allowed his wealth and I have no other family. I do though on occasions write to my wood elf brothers and tell them of my journeys. And by messenger send them notes of events in my life. And each time receive no reply.

I cannot say I have had a hard life. But as to a good life that is not true. The fire that burns in me is fed by my desire to be accepted by someone. The humans of Qeynos call me a half breed and the Elves of Faydark call me a half-breed. I am not a trusting soul. Nor do I have any reason to trust anyone. I am with you now because we seem to have a common destiny. And that in itself makes us the same. I cannot give enough, nor can I expect anything from you. I may be fair hearted at times but that never clouds the hate that drives me to be better a being than my Father and the Humans and the Half Elves that shunned me and cast me aside.

That is my story.
