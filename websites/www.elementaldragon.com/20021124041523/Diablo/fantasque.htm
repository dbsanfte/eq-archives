Press Release from Diablo Ballet

Diablo Ballet Launches 2002 - 2003 Season with Nikolai Kabaniaev's
La Boutique Fantasque (The Magic Toy Store)

Expanded Two-Act Production To Debut November 29th At 8:00 P.M.

WALNUT CREEK, CA—Diablo Ballet presents its annual Thanksgiving Family Performances in Walnut Creek on November 29 and 30 featuring Nikolai Kabaniaev's charming La Boutique Fantasque (The Magic Toy Store). To the delightful Rossini/Respighi score, La Boutique Fantasque is a tale about toys coming to life in a toy store after closing time. The San Francisco Chronicle called it, "A crowd-pleasing hit … tender and funny. The sets by Guy Buffet are fantastic, a huge red lacquer box of a shop with sliding toy boxes that open up to reveal surprises." For this revival Kabaniaev is adding new material and turning his original one act ballet in to two acts. Diablo Ballet will perform the original one act ballet at all matinees and debut the new two-act production for the Company's evening performances only.

Guy Buffet, best known for his work depicting French sommeliers, chefs and waiters, consistently captures the spirit and passion of the French people. Buffet said, "I have admired Nikolai's work as a dancer and a choreographer for many years and I am excited to collaborate on this project with him." About his own work he says, "I am an optimist. When I paint, I invite you to share my experience. I take you into my world like a guest in my home. I want you to be comfortable, relaxed and happy, able to forget about your problems and sorrows."

Diablo Ballet was founded in 1994 to present innovative dance to Bay Area audiences. Diablo Ballet is especially known for recruiting principal dancers throughout the U.S. and internationally, and for performing bold, original contemporary ballet and superb classics.

Artists performing with Diablo Ballet include Artistic Director Lauren Jonas, Co-Artistic Director Nikolai Kabaniaev, Tina Kay Bohnstedt, Karyn Lee Connell, Grethel Domingo, Erika Johnson, Viktor Kabaniaev, Miroslav Pejic and Edward Stegge.

Diablo Ballet's Thanksgiving Family Performances at the Dean Lesher Regional Center for the Arts are at 12:00, 3:00 and 8:00 PM. on Friday, November 29th and Saturday, November 30th, 2002. Individual tickets for the Matinees range in price from $15 - $25 and $22 - $28 for the Evening performances. For tickets call (925) 943-7469. The Dean Lesher Regional Center for the Arts is located in downtown Walnut Creek at 1601 Civic Drive.
