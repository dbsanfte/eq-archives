Other Animals: Drawings and Journals by Merce Cunningham
Twenty years ago, choreographer Merce Cunningham had a few unexpected hours of time on his hands, and on a whim spent it sketching. Fortunately, Cunningham continued his new-found art, which has been collected with some of his journal entries in a new book Other Animals: Drawings and Journals. Cunningham's animal sketches and journal entries reveal the artist's affection for life, as well as insights into the travels and people of his world-class and world-travelled dance company. Some pages of the journal are shown in Cunningham's cryptic handwriting, which is definitely worth the effort to decipher for the interesting details of his daily activities and reflections. Other Animals is interspersed with performance photos of his work, and includes a postscript by writer David Vaughn, which thoughtfully includes definitions of the initials by which Cunningham refers to the people he mentions. Merce Cunningham's Other Animals: Drawings and Journals, is a colorful, imaginative and entertaining surprise. Michael Phelan, BayDance.com
