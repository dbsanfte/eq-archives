Bringin' Back The Gods

 





















 
Rufio
Note: I will never disclude Rufio as one of the Dragons.   He may have left us in body but we still strive to keep his soul.

The pleasant chattering of the young wood elves tossed emotions in Rufio's heart as he sat in the shade of a great oak tree. Why where they in butcherblock? Their cheap padded cloth armor gave rise to the notion of young adventurers out on a journey.. but so far, so young? But their happiness was intoxicating, he found it hard not to keep a smile on his face, sharpening his axe and listening to their conversations.
"Peregine! Give me back my shield!" wailed a pretty wood elf as a handsome High elf ducked and dodged her lunges.
"Tis only a buckler, Lily.. It shan't deflect a pebble, and for that matter an orc's blade!" the one named Peregine responed with an older brothers voice.. both wise and mocking
"Please you two, you are making us look bad," a quiet figure in a purple robe idly drew grand designs in the dust of the road, "other travelers will make sport of us.."
"Oh Razcel, you are never any fun!" Lily called back waving her arms as if she meant to fly. Rufio sighed, he remembered how simple life had been at that age...
"Oh look.. a human! But he has MUCH darker skin.. do you think he has some of the Tier 'dal blood in him?" whispered Lily in a NOT too quiet voice. Rufio's eyes widened, now HE was to be made sport of?
"Looks like you have more admirers, Rufio.." a dry voice inside his head intoned "hurry lest they follow us, you have much to do"
"Yes, master.. I am ready" Rufio stood up, slung his axe into his shoulder harness and straightened his armor. The banded chain he had received from saving a Teir'dal armorer was a surprise, lighter than average chain armor.. but still much heavier than his old leather relics of his father's days of glory. "And it doesn't look as heroic either.."
"I do not think we were chosen for our fashion sense, Rufio.. carry on."
*sigh* "Yes, master.." And as Rufio started down the road towards Kaladim, he heard the voices titter behind him still..
"He speaks to himself.. what an odd one!" the light female voice
"Stay away from him.. he made be diseased!" cautioned the haughty male's voice
"Why must you always point out other's differences, you two?" the quiet male voice plaintively asked
"And why should you want to cover his differences, half-man?"
"Perry.. that was MEAN!"
"It's ok Lily.. he knows I.. URK!"
Rufio's footsteps stopped and he turned around to see the blonde elf's body hit the ground.
"heh heh heh, see what a pretty wench we have here! She'll fetch a nice price in the slave auctions.. eh Peg Leg?" a squat dwarf leered at the girl and played with her hair, as another one with a wooden stump for a leg pinned her to the ground.
"'haps we should "test" the product before we sell it.. see if it's quality is up to their standards.." responded "Peg Leg"
"No! they'll now she's been used then!" spake a third as he approached from behind, "Peg, Dwain, truss 'em up and ship 'em out.. we have more work to do.. we'll show these strangers not to ruin our homelands!"
"Nooooooo!" Lily screamed and thrashed about, as the other elf, Razcel, looked as he was about to wet his robes.
"Rufio.. GO! Kill the dwarves!"
"But Master.." Rufio asked quietly, "isn't that a good deed? I thought we must balance the deeds out.."
"You shall see Rufio, go now, show them the darkness!"

The dwarves laughter was cut short as they spotted a shadowed figure running straight at their midst
"What the.."
"Peg, get 'em!"
"Right, Lynnar!"
Peg Leg, whipped a dagger straight at the figure, who casually knocked it aside with his shield as he lept up, flipped and tossed a hand axe just as casually into Peg's head.. and the one with the wooden leg fell over.. almost as casually.
Rufio landed, wrenching his axe from it's holster and lept into Dwain, swinging as hard as he could. The dwarf parried with his short sword, and quickly cut downward, into Rufio's leg gashing through the armor and into the tender flesh beneath.

"Qvert Sal Flomeg WEXEN!" and with a flourish a rush of fire exploded from Razcel's cupped hands and slammed into the face of Lynner.. causing the burly dwarf to scream and lash out with his axe, cleaving into the elf maiden below him..Lily screamed and went into spasms. Peregine, slowly rose up and drew his blade, obviously only a tarnished long sword, and ran towards Lynner screaming.. tears and blood mixing on his brow and into his eyes.
"You bastard.. you short.. stumpy.. BASTAAAAAAARRRD!" and Peregine cut mightly down and sliced open the Dwarf's arm and received a sneer for his effort.
"Get ready to die.. boy!" Lynner switched his axe to his off hand, kicked Lily's body away and swung from left hip right to Peregine's face, and only the elf's natural grace saved his face from being cleaved from his skull. Peregine jumped back and prepared to leap forward again, Razcel begining yet another arcane spell, when the other dwarf's body was tossed in front of their feet.

"Take the girl and run.. this is not your day to die.."
The erudite stood unsteadily, one hand to his leg and the other clenching his axe.. and a burning fire in his eyes. "Come dwarf, the Dark one awaits!"
