News - 1/24/00

Our Roving Reporter Zastrum Shatterspear Has News for us.

First off, congratulations to Aonadora for getting a Lockjaw Tunic for a very fair price!  Way to go to one of our new members!

Congratulations are also in order for Malafon; when you see him, compliment him on his new last name!  I won’t tell you what it is; it would ruin the surprise!

Thank you goes out to Kiko for acquiring a horde of wonderful items I am currently doling out, from a flowing black robe, to intelligence earrings, to magical weapons that, as Shan says, “do something more than pook!”  Included in these is a nifty magical short sword, named at that, and the first one I’ve ever seen.  If you want a unique item warrior and rogue types, let me know and it is yours!

Finally, a report from a long hunt Sumelin and I ventured upon.

Sumelin, as you may or may not know, needs his totemic helm.  We know where to get the items, from a big ice bear under the river in Everfrost.  It is a tediously long spawn, seven and a half hours, and there are placeholders.  We discovered two things.  First off, the guild will have to band together, and endure sitting for possibly that long to help Sumelin get his helm.  The reason is, the spawn is timed.  High powered casters jump in and take the spawn, regardless of how long you’ve been waiting.  We experienced this last night, with a necromancer named Zyzx, the guild leader of Shadow Born.  She was harvesting for her other character, a shaman, and though she originally admitted it was kill stealing, she later changed her tale and said it was not.  Apparently, it is how the spawn is camped.  At any rate, be wary of the dishonorable dark elf.

Well, the placeholder spawned, so after four and a half hours of talking and sitting, we got nothing, not even the chance to kill the placeholder skeleton.  Disheartened, we prepared to leave, when a shaman in full totemic named Torlan gated in.  Sumelin at first, angry at the waste of time we’d gone through, thought not to tell Torlan the spawn had occurred, but I told Torlan anyway, and we gained some very good feelings from the outcome of our conversation with the big Barbarian.  His opinion on the camp is that he who comes first, owns the spawn, seven and a half hours or not.  He spoke very well and at length with us, and has said that if we are to camp again and he is there, he may allow us to work on a lotto system, him and Sumelin, to see who gets to loot the quest item.  He has also said he would aid against kill stealing harvesters.  At any rate, Torlan is a very well spoken and honorable man.  Should you meet him, treat him well.

Last in the news, the new mage spells are out.  A level eight and level twenty pet healing spell, and a level forty four weapon.  At first, I thought the healing spells were very poor.  At second look however, they are spectacular.  In two casts of healing, I was able to bring a half dead earth elemental of the twenty-ninth level (around twenty fourth in reality) to full health.  I grow more and more impressed with each casting.

Safe travels!
