Press Release from Diablo Ballet

Diablo Ballet Announces New and Departing Dancers

Walnut Creek, CA, Tuesday, November 12, 2002,—Diablo Ballet Artistic Director Lauren Jonas announced today that Miroslav Pejic has joined Diablo Ballet for the 2002-03 Season. Pejic comes to Diablo Ballet from Ballet West in Salt Lake City. Miroslav started training at the age of ten in his hometown of Santiago, Chile. Upon leaving the English National Ballet School in London, where he was on scholarship for two years, he joined the Ballet de Santiago. He danced with Ballet de Santiago for five years under two Artistic Directors, Marcia Haydee and Ivan Nagy, and while there was promoted to Soloist. Miroslav was a Principal Guest Dancer with Ballet Contemporaneo de Caracas in Venezuela for one season. Upon moving to the United States, Pejic danced in Ohio with the Cincinnati Ballet and for two seasons with Ballet West in Salt Lake City. He has danced principal and soloist roles in the ballets of Ben Stevenson, Robert North, John Cranko, Ronald Hynd, Andre Prokovsky, Maurice Bejart and Natalia Makarova.

Also announced are the departures of three dancers: Diablo Ballet Assistant Artistic Director Kelly Teo and Christopher Young have retired and left to pursue new interests. Richard Marsden has joined the Sacramento Ballet.
