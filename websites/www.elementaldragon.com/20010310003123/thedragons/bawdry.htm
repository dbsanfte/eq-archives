Bringin' Back The Gods

 





















 
Bawdry SwyftBlayde

Wood-Elf  Scout

 

 

I was born in the great city of Kelethin. My mother was a merchant in the town, her name Mackensea Swyftnote. She was a beautiful Elf, with hair as yellow as the sun’s rays...*sigh* I remember her well. Her life was not ready for children though, and she felt it best to send me away to Freeport when I was in my 4th Season. She explained to me that without a husband she could not raise me and my father would not take her. He said it was a disgrace to his family, to marry a merchant. She never told me his name...*stares away for a moment*...

Alas, in Freeport, I met an awkward couple at the port : A Drow, Whytehawk Lytfingers, and a Half Elf, Saelailu Stealzwell. I had never seen a drow before and the wonder in my mind led me to stroll right off the docks and plummeting into the water, for I was too busy staring. Surfacing for air, I found that Saelailu had jumped in to aid me and carry me to shore. Whytehawk, as vain as he was, did not want to ruin his armor and walked all the way around and down the stairs to meet us. It was then that I found a home...

There was a door under the dock, which they led me too. However, once inside it was barren and empty. The walls were plain and a stale air filled the room. It was then that I missed home...Not but two breaths later, Whytehawk had swept me off my feet, cradled me, and approached a wall. I was confused and lost when he said, “hold on dear child.” It was then, when I realized what they were...Whytehawk and Saelailu carried me through the wall and down a narrow hallway to their home. It was their guild; a guild of thieves...Rogues...

The next morn, when we awoke, they asked me my name and why I left.... So I told them the forbidden love my parents shared and why I had to leave...When I looked up Saelailu had a concerned look on her face and pulled Whytie, as I called him, into the adjacent room, where I overheard their every word...

“Could it be, Whytie? Could that be her?”
“If it is, how did she get so far? She cannot possibly be any further along than her 5th season.”
“Whytie, we need to know...We should contact Mackensea...”
“Saelailu, I hope you are not considering that we raise her? What Would Tyflon do if he knew?”
“He cannot do anything to us; we are his kin. He is a fellow rogue and...”
“He is the Woodland LEADER of the Rogues. There is no telling what he will do if he finds that we are harboring his forbidden child...”
“Well that’s a chance we will have to take!”

Those are the only words that were ever spoke of my supposed father. From then on, Whytie and Saelailu took me as their own, leaving me with the name of my mother, SwyftBlayd. They taught me to fight. They taught me my skills and trades. They taught me the ways of Bristlebane. They loved me...

It was so soon into my 19th season that I had to say goodbye to them. Saelailu and Whytie were always up for a challenge, and they decided to go explore the new lands found in Kunark. I begged them not to go, but they did not listen. They had heard of the dragons and the snow and all the beautiful nature to see. It was those dragons, that snow, and all else that lays in Kunark that killed them...*wipes a tear from her eye*

After not hearing from them for many moons, I decided to travel to Kunark to look for them. When I found their bodies near Karnor's Castle, I knew not what to do. I sat there with Drolvag Ragers and Drachnid Silkmasters surrounding me...crying...crying...crying. Then something lifted me up and carried me to safety, in an icy cave. He was brave and strong. His hair was gray with experience, a Wanderer. When I asked him his name “Kiko Trueheart,” rolled of his tongue. However, I refer to him as my guardian, still to this day!

He is my love, my savior, and soon we will be wed...He brought me into this family, and here I will stay!
~~Bawdry “SwyftBlayd” Trueheart~~
