Bringin' Back The Gods

 





















 
Diavlo

Human - Monk
It is said that none are more wary than the Dark Elves of Neriak. I would disagree. Having met Diavlo, I can honestly say that he steps with trepidation, trusts no one and fights for himself. But he is one of us, and for that we should be grateful

It's a quiet place in the hills where Diavlo stays.

Sitting by a tree, the only thing he sees is the tree. Others may see the tree as an outcropping of the earth's power or perhaps a thing to be protected. To Diavlo there is only the tree.

"Remember, to others there are forces at work in the world. Disregard these people. They are fools. The only thing that counts is what strength lies within your mind and fists. Things are things. People are people. Nothing more."

Words from his father, the only person he ever knew for most of his life. These words, in one form or another, were the clarion call of his training.

It's a quiet place in Diavlo's soul.

All that disturbs this are those who seem not to know his father's words. New grass grows on his grave. Time for mourning has passed. The hill holds little more for thought.

Perhaps it is time to leave this place. Perhaps travel will take his mind from these dreams. Perhaps people will see the tree for the tree. Perhaps . . . Freeport.
