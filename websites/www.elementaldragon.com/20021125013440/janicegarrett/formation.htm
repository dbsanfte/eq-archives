Press Release from Janice Garrett and Dancers

Janice Garrett & Dancers Celebrate Formation of New Dance Company

ODC Theater, December 5-7 & 12-14
Special Benefit Reception Immediately Follows December 7th Performance

Packing a hefty kinesthetic punch, Janice Garrett & Dancers, a new San Francisco dance company, will perform their Fall season at the ODC Theater, 3153 17th Street (@ Shotwell) December 5-7 & 12-14. Having dedicated the last decade to choreographing, performing and teaching throughout the US and Europe, Garrett is putting down roots in the Bay Area and forming her own company. Her choreography has been characterized as “finely crafted, wonderfully detailed and musically attentive” (the Independent-London). The concert, with two premieres and two audience favorites, will feature the dancing of some of San Francisco’s finest dancers: Kara Davis, Jenifer Golden, Brian Grannan, Bliss Kohlmyer, Dana Lee Lawton, Heidi Schweiker, Nol Simonse, and Heather Tietsort.

Season Highlights

The evening will feature the world premiere of Unmarked Boxes (Working Title) a movement commentary on human emotions and the constant interplay between thought, feeling and the physical body. Set to Francesco Geminiani’s Concerto Grossi (after Corelli, Op. 5), this new work centers around four characters and their psychological idiosyncrasies. Through these characters, and the relationships that develop between them, the piece explores the delights and dilemmas of the ever-changing human condition.

Ostinato, premiered this July in San Francisco's West Wave Dance Festival, charts the course of the company’s eight dancers as they traverse a world of their shared creation. The music, composed by assorted 16th and 17th Century I berian composers (Valente, Falconiero, Ortiz, Marini, and Merula), is performed by viola de gambist Jordi Savall and Hesperion XXI. The lush score transports the ensemble through an exuberant and highly interactive landscape. “They flood the stage with swirling running patterns that only amplify the immense vitality of the early Italian and Spanish baroque music… They chase each other, and flutter their hands as though shaking with joy, and raise their eyebrows at one another’s vanity as they pose for an imagined photographer.” (The Examiner).

Hither Thither (World Premiere), is the culmination of Garrett's collaboration with the music of Finnish women's vocal ensemble, Värttinä--an exploration which began in 1999 when she created Wayfaring. This work was begun at England’s Northern School of Contemporary Dance in October 2001 and has been completed with Garrett’s own company members this fall. The piece is driven by lively ancient Finno-Ugric melodies and incorporates the vocal talents of Tuvan throat singers, Yat-Kha, with whom Värttinä began an association in 1998. Both music and dance speak to age-old themes of love, loss, sadness, joy and celebration.

Garrett will revisit her quietly compelling, Otherwise, premiered in May, 2001 and nominated for three Isadora Duncan Dance Awards. This somber duet examines themes of intimacy and loss while highlighting the distances spanned and spaces shared by two women. This dance is choreographed to Arvo Pärt's Für Alina and features costumes by Michael Kruzich and lighting by Matthew Antaky.

Janice Garrett

Janice Garrett is a choreographer and dance educator based in the San Francisco Bay Area. In 1990, following a ten-year residency in New York where she performed and toured extensively as a member of Dan Wagoner and Dancers, she began choreographing, performing, and teaching throughout the US and abroad. In May 2001 she presented her first full evening of choreography in the Bay Area and in March 2002, she formed her own San Francisco based dance company, Janice Garrett & Dancers. Her choreography has been presented in San Francisco at Theater Artaud, the Cowell Theater, ODC Theater, and Brady Street Dance Center, as well as the Palace Theater in Los Angeles, The Place Theater, London, the Nott Dance Festival, Nottingham, and Danse Scenen, Copenhagen. She has received commissions from various companies and performance groups including Scottish Dance Theater, 4D Performance Group, London Contemporary Dance School, and Skolen for Moderne Dans i Danmark. She has been a frequent guest artist with the internationally renowned London Contemporary Dance Theatre, London Contemporary Dance School, Dansens Hus and Skolen for Moderne Dans i Danmark, as well as guest teacher for numerous dance companies including: Rambert Dance Company, DV8 Physical Theatre and Adventures in Motion Pictures.
