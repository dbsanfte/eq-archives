JOINING TWELFTH NIGHT

Welcome those who seek to join our family. The requirements are outlined on this page. Joining however is only possible while playing Everquest on the E'ci server. Allthough contacting us here and shareing information on the times you play will allow us to meet more easily in game.


Requirements...

Greetings!!

Thank you for your interest in Twelfth Night! As stated on the home page we are a small family-like guild. If you are interested in friendships we are a good guild to join. If you are interested in uber raids, then you are in the wrong place. This is a decision that must be made by the individual player and I strongly urge you to think about this before asking to join us. We are in general a friendly bunch and we like to help out fellow members. This, however, is not intended to imply that we are here solely to power level or twink everyone within the guild.

The requirements are fairly simple. If you are a complete stranger to most members of the guild, then we ask that you group with some of us so that we can get to know you. Many of us have characters of assorted levels and grouping should not be that difficult. We do have a general level requirement of 15 for your first character admitted into Twelfth Night, but all alternate characters can enter at level one. The level 15 requirement has been known to be ignored for close friends and family members of established members on occasion though .

We have a few basic rules for guild members. 1) No swearing in guild chat, out of character or shouts. 2) No begging, in or out of guild. This includes items, money and power-leveling. (I personally take this rule very seriously and have been known to remove characters for doing this). 3) All members are expected to behave with honor. Stick up for yourself when necessary of course, but remember that it's sometimes better to back down then have an argument get out of hand

In general these rules boil down to one thing: Always behave in a matter that is a tribute to our guild. Remember that while wearing our guild tag you are a representative of our guild and I expect all members to behave as such.

If you feel you can live with these requirements we would love to have you. Quality people are always welcome with us. Feel free to contact me with any further questions any time within the game or via the email link on this website. All of my alts are in the guild and their names are as follows: Aluiena, Hanaa, Katas, Mezmarize, Midnyte, Mantyss, and Violynn (although this one is my selling mule so I most likely won't be avail if this character is on, please feel free to try).
Walk Safely,

Crystyl Phoenix
