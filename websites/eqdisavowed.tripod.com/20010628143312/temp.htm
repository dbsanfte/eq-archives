    Guild Recruitment System     - Last Changed - January 24th - 2001

      Guild recruitment works in a probation system. The following sequence of events is the method we use to administer recruits.

      Officer Review: The officers of Disavowed will consult with each other and determine whether a recruit is acceptable for a 2 week probation period. If a player is clearly unacceptable, the officers will save him/her and the guild trouble by filtering them out early. If the player is accepted by officer review, they move to the next step in the recruitment process.

      Two Week Probation Period: In this designated time, the recruit will be temporarily guilded. He/she is expected to participate with the guild in events, the general purpose for this is to determine the caliber of the player.

      Voting Process: Once two weeks expire, Disavowed will vote on the recruit. If anyone objects to the recruit, they will send a tell to the Recruit Officer with an explanation depicting why he/she feels that way. When the negative votes are tallied up, they will be reviewed by the officers. The officers will then determine if the negative votes are legitimate. To be accepted into Disavowed, a recruit has to be approved by 90% of the guild; meaning that if more than 10% of the guild votes 'No', the recruit is rejected.

      For Example, assuming Disavowed has 20 players, the recruit is allowed up to 2 'No' votes. We do round the figures, so if Disavowed has 25-34 players, 3 'No' votes are tolerated.

      Disavowed has strong filtering techniques to assure excellence. We are proud of our system and we feel that it makes the guild more fulfilling.
