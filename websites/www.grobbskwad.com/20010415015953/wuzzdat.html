Hail and well met! You have found the homepage of our guild in the world of Everquest, a multi-user online roleplaying game, played over the internet. This guild unites players from all around the world on the Bristlebane server with the intention to adventure together, share experiences and especially to have fun.

Our guild consists exclusively of trolls, one of the races you can choose to walk the fields of Norrath, how the universe of Everquest is called. Trolls are per definition an evil race, warlike and primitive. That's why you will see this horrible slang all over the place, it is called "Troll Tok". Every real troll is able to speak like that, even when from time to time interaction with the human races require more common language. 

Even though our tribe is considered evil, trolls are known for their cleverness and valor in battle. Annoy one troll and you better be prepared for the whack, also called "barsh", on your forehead. Prove yourself worthy and a troll will respect and protect you with his life. Trolls don't steal or backstab, they prefer open confrontation. The stronger one prevails and it's strength, not good or evil, that determines the rightful victor. 

This guild is called the "Grobb Skwad", named after our hometown in the swamps. Today we recruit the finest members of our race and are well know beyond the southern borders. Previously only lightly organized the skwad is now about to develop its' full potentional under the recently created warchiefs. With a strong and firm hand our leader Snottigrub Rotbreath will lead us to conquer the dragons and the planes, for the Troll Horde shall raise again and make our enemies cower in fear!
