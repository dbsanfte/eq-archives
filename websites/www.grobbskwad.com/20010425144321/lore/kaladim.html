DWARF PICKLES

Anodda messik frum Trubble how ta mak dwarf picklez:
De raid went as planed and de guards fell under de mighty hands of de skwad. We did de hit n run tingie till we get to de city deep in dere stronghold. So far so gud...

When we enter de city sumtin bad happen - de skawd all swell up to normal size and we was stuck inna tunnel like a cotton ball in de asprinz bottle. Me was no seein de target me had till me look down an see me was standin on him head. Me barsh an barsh wonderin how de rest of de troops gonna get in dere. Me bounce in and tink me see mighty Snotti at me feet ded ded. Dis brave troll general was on de point an fought to de end in dis tight spot. Me not hab time to cry fer him.

Me knew it was time to try an get back where dere was more space only to find de way back was plugged up too. Dere was a fight goin on in de rear but we was packed in tight an me only git to see de back of de helm of de big troll in front of me. Sumbodiez say heal Trubbs and me look an see how bad me was hurt. De healers could not see me in de mass of troll flesh but sumbody cast invis on me. Bless dat smart person who ever dey be.

We waited in dat hallway helpless to assizt de troops fightin a way out fer us. We wait an wait till finally we can pop out. Me hurt an invis so me can only watch else me die right away so me watch de great battle in da foyurway. We gots sum brave trollies in de skwad an many of dem gave up dere lifes so udders might escape. We make it back to zone and wait fer de recovery. Elyysa bring back many a troll frum de dead and Dethfist searched out corpse aftah corpse. Him hab more consent dan a GM.

Finally fren Volon shows up fer de transport back home. Him tank us fer flyin "Colon airlines" which me tink is funny cause him name not colon - heh heh. It was a great adventure and me glad me was dere to take sum picturz an tell de story. We goin back me bet but dis time we pack a can opener fer dem tight spots.

Luv an hugz to all trollies,

Trubble Galore Combat photograbber fer de skwad.
