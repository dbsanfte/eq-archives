2. RULEZ OF CONDUKT

General policy on begging, trade and assitance in guild (/ooc)

We need to clarify the difference between assistance and annoyance. It is good for the guild to be helping out its members, but it's bad when members attempt to demand and abuse the generosity of brudda trolls. Here are some general guidelines:

1. First of all as general rule, I find the best form of in-guild help is gifts, when a older skwadie will offer it unsolicited just out of the blue :) It's always great to see a skwadie come in without being asked in guildchat and offer to help. This is really great :) and it makes me happy to see it whenever it occurs in guildchat.

However if a skwadie wishes to ASK for an item for the guild these following rules must be observed:

2. A younger troll should not become a serial beggar. There is no shame in asking your troll brudda's and sisdas for help when starting out, but a troll should not see this as a open excuse to treat his guildmates as a ATM to leech off.

3. If a request for an item is turned down or a trade agreement cannot be reached, a skwadie must not harass the other troll for the item. A troll must maintain respect for his fellow troll.

4. It is ok for a troll to ask for help but it should be a reasonable request in Relation to the trolls level. This is really relates alots to higher end items. An example would be a level 9 recruit should Not be asking the guild to give him a dark reaver.

5. A troll must not automatically expect to get a item for free (that is the descression of the troll who has the item). If the troll is selling the item he will however be expected to to sell it no higher than the current market rate and will be a encouraged to sell at discounted rate to his/her brudda or sisda troll.

6. A troll when conducting trade with a brudda troll, will not  knowingly charge amounts clearly over the market rate. This is NOT to be done. If a troll wishes to make a better profit he is always free to find a hooomie to sell to.

7. Trolls are free to trade on the open market, However trading in the guild as long as it follows the above guidelines is to be encouraged, as a way of building a sense of guild community.

To conclude I want to see the skwad  able to offer support to younger trolls in a considerate but fair trading enviroment. My message to younger trolls especially is don't demand or beg, however if you wish to make a polite and responsible request in guild chat feel free (in troll tak polite meh meanz :), and as snotti wud sayz, uze gits betta remba no matter wat,  respect ur brudda's and sisdahs!
