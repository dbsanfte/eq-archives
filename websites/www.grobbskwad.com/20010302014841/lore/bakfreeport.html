Arooo Skwadies,

We make de run in formation pass de Oasis and North Ro and we be screamin dat hoomies gonna die all de way. Sumbudy brung us two guards to warm up on and den come de orderz from DaBoss. Zone into Freeport an group by de wall. De shamanz get busy bindin eberybody to dis city cuz if we fall in battle den we be close by fer to come and barsh dem sum more.

We bash sum guards dat Snotti pull into us and de crowds are startin to come watch. Den we move closer to de city and den we take de gate. We keep control of de gate fer over an hour and de guards trow dere best at us but dey wuz eezz prey fer de trolls of grobb. Lot of dem hoomis watch an see what fun dis be so dey pretend dey be troll (dem wish) an bash dem guards along side de mighty skwad. Dere was two casualties an dat was de Doctor Feelgood name of Kven - him said him go link dead an den dere was de brave Runtt Izbiggadenyoo who always manage to make de sacrifice fer de skwad. Him always be back fer more cause him like da combat. We salute ya Runtt as a brave an honerble trollie.

Den we all break up an sum go ta arena to fight while udders get redee fer de quest dey left fer glory wid de skwad. Me was happy me went an if you want gud time you come next time cause me know fer sure de Grobb Skwad not rest til de whole of Norath be troll or waz wishin dey wuz.
