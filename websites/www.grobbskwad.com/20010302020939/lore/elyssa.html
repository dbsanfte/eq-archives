DA STOREE OF ZACHARIAH

Altho dis be not scribblinz by a fello Skwadee, dat be very important piece of Grobb historee anyway. Read why da hoomie ladee Elyssa Brigantia be helpin us wif da city raids...
My ancestor, Zachariah, who, like all teenagers, was young and foolish had set out to have adventures in the deserts of Ro when he was rescued from the clutches of a very angry sand giant by a trollie warchief named Ugh. Ugh took the  beat-up and starving hummie to the village shaman who used his mojo to heal the young and foolish Zachariah. As Zachariah was mending (and being fattened up) the trollie warchiefs were debating (well, debating for trolls means beating the snot out of each other until the last one standing wins) on how best to use this young and foolish hummie.

 Should he be roasted? pickled? stewed? It took several days of..um..debating before Ugh announced he would be marinated in a delicate halfling liver sauce and barbequed to a golden honey brown. The other trollies (the ones still standing) acknowledged the good tastes of Ugh and eagerly awaited the complete recovery of the young and foolish Zachariah. 

 Well, needless to say, young and foolish Zachariah who didn't know the ways of trolls was very grateful for the care and mojo the trolls so unselfishly gave him. He chatted happily with the trollies who came to check on him several times a day (they were hungry and trolls aren't known for patience), and it wasn't long before the shaman pronounced he had made a full recovery. The trollies cheered and the trollie cooks began their preparations. Of course, young and foolish Zachariah had no idea what the real reason was behind the trollie cheers and was even more grateful. And so this is what the young and foolish boy did...

 He stood upon NoArm Hill, so named for the brave and selfless warrior Oz NoArms who lost both his arms to a cyclops after he single-handedly (no pun intended) battled a cyclops on that very hill. I know, you're wondering what a cyclops was doing in the depths of Grobb. Well, it all has to do with a trollie, who like all teenagers, was young and foolish and had dreams of being the first circus ringmaster in Grobb. He thought a trained cyclops would make a nice addition to his little circus, and, well, he was wrong. Anyway, back to young and foolish Zachariah. Zachariah stood on that very hill and made this promise:

 "I do hearby vow that from this day on all of my descendants will answer the call of all trollies if trollies should ever need help, cross my heart and hope to die."

 The trollies gasped. To make such a vow and then to seal it with a crossed-heart gesture was big, real big. What to do now? Should they eat him anyway or let him live to make a bunch of descendants to serve them forever and ever? Of course, the young and foolish trollies wanted to eat him anyway, but the warchiefs, who were very wise (as far as trolls go anyway), foresaw the possibilities of Zachariah's young and foolish vow, and declared that no one shall harm a single hair on his young and foolish head. Well, to make a long story short, Zachariah returned home to Freeport and promptly married a very fat baker's daughter. He had lots and lots of children who all had lots more children and all the while the trollies cheered.

 Yes, I am a descendent of the young and foolish Zachariah and I too must come when the trollies call. I continue to search the lands of Norrath for something that has the power to release the chains of the vow made by young and foolish Zachariah. I have heard of an ancient idol which has the power to break these bonds, and am still searching for it.

 Well, at least I can be grateful that I have not yet been called to a troll's frying pan, unlike poor Aunt Sophie, who was the main course at the annual "Runin uv da kobolds" festibal several years ago.

 And that is why I will be there when for the next invasion, wherever it may be...

 Elyssa Brigantia  Cleric of Marr
