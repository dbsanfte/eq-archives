RAZIN FELWHYT

Diz whut sistah Trubble skribbled on dem postkardz frum brashin in elfie land:
De trip was great. Since dis was de furst time me was abroad wid out gittin kilzed me had lots of fun. Dem elfies was sure terrofied of de hoard. Me tink sum of dem nebber seen a troll before let alone a army like de skwad. When we killed dem furst guards dey knew we wuz serious. Dey grouped together to try an block de gate. Some of dem was as brave as us trollies - but lots smaller. Den we blocked de gate wid only a few trolls standin side by side. Dem little peoples taught we was gonna keep em frum dere home. We hudda charged dem a troll toll to pass. *self smack* Me sily troll sumtimes.

Dere was sum shoutin goin on bout dem meanie trolls and how we shud leave. Meantimes me was gittin private talk frum lots of folks sayin dey luv de show we put on an dat dey remember us frum Freeport. Dey also mentioned dey was healin us up as well as cheerin fer dem magnificantious trolls while we was barshin dem guards. Frum what I see des raidin partees is de skwad doing gud will ambassitor wurk an makin de hoard wurld famose. Me bets lots of dem wishes dey grew up to be troll like us.

Me stinkie hummie user is sneakie one cause de nex day a new elfie was made to look about de elfie city. Dem peeples was still asking bout what happen wid dem trolls de nite before. Dem usin wurds like awesum to describe de power of de skwad hoard.

Long lib de General Snotti and de risin of de hoard! Me hope to see yuz at de next barsh an me hope it real soon.

Lub-n-hugs to all de bruddahs an sistahs,

Trubble Galore

And da Skwad mad it happin!
