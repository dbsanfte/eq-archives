DA FINDINZ OF DA IDOL

Dis beh journal meh find off humiee git dat meh give dirt nap too, He now just anotha skull in da cave like all da others. Dead humies tell no tales.
The personal Journal of Lieutenant James King, Freeport Militia.

12th of November 3185.
Finally it appears my efforts have paid off! A commission which involves more than just watching the south gate for fat merchants trying to avoid the trade dues. I don't know the details yet but Sir Lucan has summoned me to lunch tomorrow. I am sure this is the moment I have been waiting for.

13th of November
Yes it is official, I am now writing this journal as a full commissioned lieutenant of the watch, And better yet my duties are to involve much more than just baby sitting a gatehouse watch. Sir Lucan has entrusted me to lead a detachment to protect the expedition of a Master William Kemmler ,a noted archeologist. In deepest confidence I must admit I was hoping more for commanding a hunting party against the dervish raiders or Deathfist thugs. 

14th of November
We left at first light, my first command with over a dozen militia at my disposal!, Pitty my orders are only to baby sit this Kemmler character. About 7 hours south of Freeport, We stopped at the sandfishers village along the coast. The troops were well fed, and more importantly my meal was superb, delicate white fillets of finest fish, washed down with generous helpings of Elven wine. I must admit I am truly enjoying the life of an officer.

15th of November
I have not much time write, our journey through oasis has been swift our column moved down the coast with all the celerity we could muster. As I write this note we are passing the large canyon walls which mark the beginning of the southern most reaches of the ro desert .

16th of November
We are here! At the dig site in the southern most tip of the desert. Kemmler set up work soon after we arrived. I gave the order to set a open camp near the dig site, and posted one sentry, there is no need for much fuss around these parts. Tonight over dinner Kemmler confessed to me the nature of his work. Apparently there is a big gap in the historical records of Freeport during the first 200 years of its settlement. Kemmler has this bizarre notion that the first settlement of Freeport had "disappeared", he mummbled on about distinct difference of design in some of the ruins he uncovered in the old slum quarters of Freeport. He believes an military outpost of the old Freeport of a similar architectural design was located in so.ro and too disappeared around the same time. When I questioned him on what he meant by "disappear" I was met with silence,! Babysitting this aloof academic is a waste of my time and abilities.

17th of November
Guard Robertson did not return from sentry duty last night. But knowing Roberston the drunkard is probably all the way to the oasis inn by now. I entrusted Guard Failren with the duty of retrieving the drunkard fool. No use sending out a large search party. Kemmler as usual is to absorbed in his work to know or care about any of this, he is digging away like a mad groundhog.

18th of November
Failren has not returned, I don't understand, Failren is a competent guardsman not like Robertson! I asked kemmeler about it this morning but he looked half dead, he has been working on the dig non stop for over day now with no rest, maybe that explains his strange agitation. It appears he uncovered some clay idol of non human construction. It is a rather distasteful piece of primitive art it appears to me. It shows a large hideous creature holding a some form of war axe, I bet it is trollish in origin yet for some reason the figure unsettles me,...its face, the eyes are calm and silent but seem almost to glare to with a unknown intent. Well enough of this silly banter, it is only a lump of clay.

19th of November
Kemmler too has disappeared and the idol with him!!! I sent out a search party immediately. Sargent O'conner protested he wishes me to courier freeport for reinforcements. But I have no time for that, We will locate kemmler ourselves no need to let Sir Lucan know of our little problem just yet., it would not look good for my first commission..

20th of November
Two more guards have disappeared today when scouting around the valley to the swamp. This brings the total to four. 1/3 of my total detachment! This expedition is turning into a disaster, O'conner is almost pleading that we return to Freeport and muster support. But I must find kemmler now if not I am almost certainly facing dismissal when I return. My mind is set, I will take all my remaining forces into innothule swamp at first light. There should be no problems if we move in force.

21st of November
This swamp is an hideous place I see why the men fear it, even in the full light of day visibility is barely 10 feet in the thick sickening mist. I must ensure we stay close in this place. We have already lost guard Mallory. I must confess this swamp is being to work on my nerves, am I seeing shapes in the mist, its almost like shadows are moving around us....toying with us....I swear at one time I almost spied the hideous form of that clay idol in the mist.

22nd of November
The shadows are ALIVE, I viewed the shadowy outlines of large troll forms in the mist just before it had happened......Sargent o'connor and 3 guards must have been only 50 feet away in the mist. I heard their screams, followed by a brief clanging of swords and then the shrill sound as the air screamed with magical discharge. I had moved sword drawn into the mist but there was no sign of anything, no sound, no movement and no bodies. It had stopped almost as soon as it started. My handful of remaining guards are almost in a state of panic. We will not rest here tonight we must withdraw at once, lest we meet the fate of our companions. 

23rd of November
I have become separated, I cannot say what is happened to the others, for now I am totally lost, cold and alone. My food and water provisions have spoiled, I have not slept in over a day now, fear keeps me awake. I know I am not mad, But by Marr, the form in this idol lives! I can almost him see him in the shadows, directing his troll minions as he laughs at me, playing with me! My pride and faith in Marr is no protection. To the person reading this this journal. I swear under oath that I speak the truth. The form in the idol lives, and the trolls are massing in the swamp. They seek to follow him, what ever this holds for the future I cannot say....

24th of November.
Marr has saved me! I have found my way back out of this nightmarish swamp! My eyes feel like lead weights and my stomach growls in emptiness. I will rest for now tomorrow I will write more once I reach  the oasis inn.
