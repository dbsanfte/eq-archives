Welcome to the Hobbit Patrol's nexus on the magical web!  Come, sit, and partake of our tales and the bits of wisdom we have gathered in our jaunts across Norrath.  As hobbits are basically humble (and lazy) folk, this magical site will not be flashy or impressive.  After all, that's time that could be better spent with ale and a good cheese in a warm, cozy hobbit-hole.

Hobbit Patrol exists to enrich the lives of our folk all over Norrath.  We put little stock in extravagant gifts or shepherding the young through battles they are not fated to win.  (OOC: We don't twink or powerlevel.  Much.)  Instead, we serve each other and all good hobbits through our friendship in times of  peace and our steadfast alliance in times of battle.

While most members are the folk of the River Vale, some other races have been  admitted as Friends of the Hobbits.  We do not tolerate murderers among our ranks, and all reports of misconduct on the part of a Patrolman are  taken seriously.  We try to recruit only the finest hobbits for our guild, and people of poor conduct are often encouraged to find a guild that better fits their personality.

I am glad you have come to visit us!  Make yourself at home, just don't eat all the dwarven cheddar.  May Bristlebane watch over you.

-Sergo Cheddargnobbler
