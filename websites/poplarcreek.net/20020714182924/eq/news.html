5-7-01  A guild meeting is hereby announced for Friday night, May 18th, beginning at 8:00 eastern time and lasting at least a couple of hours.  There will be socializing, personality (OOC=roleplaying) contests, perhaps some drunken hide-and-seek, and of course, battles against the forces of evil.  If anyone has prizes to donate, please contact Sergo in Norrath.

4-30-01  Earlier this month, Syeerilearned a seat on the Hobbit Council.  The keeper of the mystical web apologizes for the delay in this announcement.

4-17-01 Our alliance with Court of the Crosswinds has been formalized.  Please extend every courtesy to nobles of the Court.

3-29-01  The Hobbit Patrol message board  is now operational!  It is for guild members only and has a password.  Just  ask in magical guildspeak while in Norrath.  Someone there should know the  password.  To use the board, you will have to register (it's free) with  gamers.com, which you can do when you follow the magical link above.  I  hope this helps us all to get to know each other better and strenghtens  the guild.  Be sure to sign your Patrol name to you posts if you register  under a different name for some reason as I did.  (OOC I can already see  I'm going to have to rework the home page to put up a permanent link to  the message board and reorganize some other things, but I'm basically lazy,  so don't look for that right away.)

3-28-01Sergo has penned an editorialaddressing    some issues facing the guild right now.  Please take the time to read it    and comment on the message board.

3-27-01  Hobbit excursions into Vellious have begun! A small team of councillors and Guildleader Gercheckhave begun forays across this icy continent.  It appears they will be able to bring back a trove of magic vellium weapons to share with younger patrolmen.  (Please don't pester Gercheck, as it is Sergo who is collecting most of the weapons for gifts.)

3-25-01  Congratulate Toddin on his promotion to the Hobbit  Council.  Toddin's tireless effots to aid fledgeling hobbits in the Thicket  caught the attention of the guild council, and he has been rewarded for  his service to the Patrol.

3-21-01  Dagers has turned up alive and well!  It seems he was doing some "information gathering" in the hobbit fashion for quite some time, but as this image shows, he is healthy and prosperous.  We hope to see more of him in Norrath soon!

3-18-01  The Patrol was amply represented at yesterday's Pan-Antonican  Pub Crawl.  Leibolmai, Minymee, Biggelman, Montange, and Sergodrank, sang,  danced, fled, and occasionally died in a manner befitting good hobbits.   Some magical imagesof the events have been culled from the addled minds of the participants.

3-17-01  Patrolman Wowzas was expelled from the Patrol this morning after a unanimous vote of the Hobbit Council.  He was found guiltly of murder without just cause and actions unbecoming of a Patrolman.
               Also today, Councillor Sergo conversed with former Councillor Samsiz who has left the Patrol voluntarily to pursue justice in a manner more in accord with his own conscience.  Samsiz reports he has applied to the Typhesian Guard, a guild dedicated to thwarting any dark races attempts to dominate Norrath.  The meeting was quite amicable, and may lead to a guild alliance in the future.  Samsiz's departure should in no way be viewed negatively, and he is still considered a friend of the Patrol.

3-10-01   Congratulate Leibolmai and Biggelman on their promotion to the Hobbit Council!  Both were recognized for their service to the guild, and assisted young hobbits in our most recent guild gathering in the Misty Thicket.  They are fine representatives of our guild.
              
3-9-01     The Hobbit Patrol is officially participating in the  Pan-Antonican pub crawl!  Please see the detailsand  join us.  Let's show Antonica that no one can party like the hobbits!  This  can get our guild some great publicity, so we need a great turnout.  Note  that slaying of other pub crawlers if prohibited, even if they would normally  have KillOnSight status.  Even (and I want to make this perfectly clear,)  even if they DO say "Jehovah."
