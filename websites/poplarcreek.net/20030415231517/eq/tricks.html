Our first tale comes from Runnyeye.  After a fierce battle against a beholder and its goblin attendants, a battle in which brave Councillor Biggelman had fallen, Sergo was alone with Councillors Corrik and Leibolmai in the beholder's lair, hoping to deliver a nasty surprise to any dazed goblins who might report in.  The next thing Sergo heard was:

Corrik tells the party, "Leib are you STUCK down there?"

I'll let Corrik tell the story from there...

As I looked at the poor, problem-glanded hobbit in the pit, I recalled a similarly horrifying incident in Befallen which saw me stupid twin brother fall through a hole in the floor.  That incident, of course, led to the gruesome death of both Lardbottoms.  After having vowed never to let that sort of lapse in judgment occur again, I sighed, clearly disappointed and somewhat shocked when Councillor Sergo leapt into the pit yelling "All for one and one for all!"  Usually, I'm not prone to let that sort of peer pressure curb me unwillingness to risk DEATH.  However, having not seen our ugliest Patrolman in quite some time, I felt obligated to help him in his predicament.

After having taken the necessary precautions of hiding and sneaking, I loathingly dropped meself into the pit to help me fellow Patrolmen.  Still recovering from me deep wounds inflicted by the elite guards, skin still smoking from a blast by the evil eye, I was particularly annoyed when I then saw Sergo nibbling on a bit of cheddar and rehearsing his Gate spell.

Our third tale occurred in Cazic-Thule.  Corrik alone has lived to tell thee...

My third excursion into Cazic Thule saw me and several other Patrolmen, as well as a couple of non-patrolmen, exploring the depths of the gator tunnel.  Things had gone fairly well, and we had quite a powerful party, so me confidence was pretty high when I eyeballed an alligator bathing lethargically in his pool.  With our group, I had every confidence that we'd dispatch him with relative ease.  So I let him take a chunk out of me leg before heading back to me friends with him.
 
I didn't happen to see his family lurking about in the shadows.
 
Needless to say, I was somewhat nonplussed upon turning around to see 3 more of his ilk trailing behind me, drooling at the thought of a hobbit dinner.  I was also rather displeased to see that yet another gator had decided to attack from the other side of the tunnel.  This would be tough, but like I said, me confidence was high.
 
Mass confusion ensued.  Who was assisting whom?  Who was getting hit hardest?  Who do I assist?  Above all, what's the quickest way out?
 
Hobbit blood splattered and spattered about like a bad paint job.  Sergo's head rolled lazily across the tunnel like a tumbleweed.  Alligators feasted hungrily on me friends' little limbs and torsos as I turned and jumped into the pool to make a mad swim out and up.
 
Biggelman - gone.  Poor guy had already died five times today.  Sergo - resting quitely in the stomachs of several putrid reptiles.  Oh!  The humanity!
 
Flanda, bleeding profusely, swam up the well after training a few more gators onto him.  Behind/below him, I struggled for breath and had to find me way past the gators, through the crimson wash.  Thinking quickly, I used his near-dead body to hoist meself up and out.  Unfortunately, that gave the gators more time to shred him.  His sad little corpse sank down the well and through some digestive tracts.
 
Better him than me...
 
Implementing me Hobbitspeed, I managed to make it to the safety of the outside, where I saw the re-established Sergo.  He generously healed me up before heading back to retrieve his corpse.  The treacherous and ill-fated expedition was over...  for me.
 
I felt a strange combination of guilt (for causing the demise of so many of me friends and guildmates) and relief (after all, I DID live...)  Then Sergo had an epiphany:
 
"You mean Corrik was the only one who DIDN'T die?"
 
I hung me little hobbit head in shame.
 
(But I got over it.)
