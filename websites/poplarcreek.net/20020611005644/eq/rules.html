Our guild rules are written with our reputation in mind.  In the past year, we have become known as a helpful nonPK guild, and as such, often receive aid and support from other adventurers.  As a Patrolman, you benefit from this reputation, and it is in your interest and ours to uphold it.

If these rules seem too confining for you, for instance, if you like to cuss a good blue streak every now and then, perhaps Hobbit Patrol isn't the best guild for you.  There are guilds to fit all kinds of people on Rallos Zek.  If we seem to sedate or restrained, then we wish you well in your search for more fun.


Rule 1:  No asinine behavior.

Rule 2:  No killing without a roleplay reason.  Someone insults you, fine, it's Rallos Zek, let him have it.  Known murderer?  Fine.
