Gallerie 1, being the earliest known images of the Patrol, many taken during the very first guild raid, an inconclusive skirmish in Runnyeye.

Here's a marvelously foreshortened image of a young Minymee fearlessly taking on a goblin guard. Miny was at or near his 14th season in this image. No, he's not slaying it singlehandedly, a large swarm of hobbits was just out of the picture.

Several of these Patrolmen have not been     seen in some time.  Dagers is occasionally about, but often involved in various "information gathering" activities which make him scarce..  Corrik, in this image, has just thrown out his back trying  to loot anotherblock of ore.

Toehealer, shown here, was our guild's first webmaster.  He, too, may have gone missing, though speculation leans more toward him having traded the cleric quest armor (shown here) for a backpack full of Tumpy Tonics.  He may yet turn up in one of the more remote hobbit holes around the Rivervale, reeking of alcohol and incense.
