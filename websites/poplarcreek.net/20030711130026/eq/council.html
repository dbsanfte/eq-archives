Council Chamber

Herein is news and guidelines for members of the Hobbit Council. 


Your eyes adjust from the bright light outside in the River Vale. The council chamber smells slightly of fresh cheese.  Someone has been here recently.  But, alone now, you look around the room.  You see the glint of Corrik's spare daggers hanging along the wall, and what, if you didn't know better, would look like froglok finger bones on a dirty plate in the corner.  You pad across the cool earthen floor, and pull up a chair to the table.  The notes from the last council meeting are still there.  You begin reading...

ITEM-Recruitment:   When you invite new members, ask them some questions in the hobbit tongue.   If they can't answer, they may be a guild crasher.  Murderers usually give  themselves away if you ask this question, phrased exactly like this:

"How much do you PK?"

Sometimes honest hobbits balk at this.  I tell them to answer honestly and I will know whether they are a good fit for the guild.  This is not to say that some murderers haven't slipped past me.  Dumbrowski was my fault.  But I have also detected more than my share and gently suggested that they'd likely be happier in another guild.

Tell prospective patrolmen right up front that we don't twink or powerlevel much, and that we're not merely a source of  free teleports.  This will weed out most of the people who are joining only for powergaming reasons.

All recruits must apply on the public message board and receive the endorsement of two Patrolmen.  More details are on the public board in a thread titled, "How to apply for membership."

Given how many dOoDs are in Rallos Zek, we have done well to find such a core of fine players and stable, thoughtful officers.

ITEM-Guild Alliances:  Councillor Corrik is recognized for a report on guild relations.

You locate one spot at the where the oak planks of the table's surface have been pocked in a fan-like pattern.  Remembering Corrik's penchant for mumbley-peg, you begin sifiting through the papers there. Sure enough...

We still have alliances with Knights of no Remorse, Vendetta Factor, and the Elite Crusaders.  Members of these guilds should be viewed most favorably.  And while no formal declaration of war has been issued, enmity still runs high with Reign of Pain and Short Militia.  Members of these guilds may be slain on sight.  By choosing such a vile organization, they have revealed their character, and we had best not wait for THEM to start the battle.

about him.  Hopefully with the website up now
and directing more hobbits to it we can get more people to understand what our
guild policies really are.   I personally don't attack anyone first unless they
are establish pk guild AND they personally have attacked me before.  There are
only two pk's I have on my kos list that are not in guild, Rallyd and
Shorttears, both for exploiting game dynamics and exp killing.  The key is, you
shouldn't really take a persons word on somebody being a pk or not.  Just cause
Rallyd and Shorttears are people I would kill on site, doesn't mean I want the
rest of the guild to actively hunt them or take my word on it.  Maybe
cautionary warning, but not saying "Let's kill so and so cause he killed me

unfortunate circumstances.  One thing I stress, is /guildstatus any or all
people that you intend as targets or any pk'ers attacking you.  You just need
them targeted and have /guildstatus hotkeyed to get what guild they are in,
regardless of anom/roleplay tag.  Also, /guildstatus "name" will work if they
are in the same zone as you.
