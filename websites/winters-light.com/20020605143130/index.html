A few mobs killed..., May 27, 2002

 by Kalle

Necklace of Wishes [Yaladan], Ring of Ages [Senuvina, CT] and Tunic of Kinship [Sphinxd, CT]. I guess there are way more dead mobs within 2 weeks, but thats all I know so far. Blame wrong infos on Mojoo as well :) . Anyway , grats all winners !!
