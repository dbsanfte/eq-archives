House Archon Charter
House Archon is a guild of players who seek this game to relax and enjoy ourselves. We are a family of individuals who strive to support one another in achieving our goals. We of the House are seeking to establish ourselves within a comfortable place of the end game. We are training ourselves to be able to complete different high-level objectives with ease and efficiency.

Every member of the House shares a collective responsibility to the other members in terms of respect and appreciation. We are founded under the premise of establishing a sound foundation from which we can enjoy every aspect the game has to offer.

House Archon feels that there are several methods of enjoyment with in the game world. One of which is the ability to contribute to the success of the end game. Another would be to reap the benefits of those encounters that occur in the end game. There are some sacrifices that will be asked of members to fulfill the necessary requirements to achieve these methods of enjoyment. While we are achieving these goals the players are asked to contribute as best they can with the knowledge that everything has its purpose. The House wishes to equip the members with the finest wears that can be acquired, but all must come in good order to continue to develop.

House Archon is founded under the premise that with enough effort and mutual assistance a casual players’ guild can participate in the game that requires a great deal of aptitude. We believe that these goals are achievable as well as being desirable. Within the game we seek out individuals who are willing to sacrifice some for the benefit of the guild, and understand the reasons behind these actions. We also seek individuals who can contribute of different levels of the game. Whether it is by educated feedback to help organize the guild to possessing a regular player prowess to assist in our field campaigns.

We are a talented guild with a great deal of knowledge of the game and how to perform well within. The House will continue to grow and define itself as we age with the rest of the server community.
