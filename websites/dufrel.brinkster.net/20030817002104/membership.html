Guild Recruitment Policy
Currently our needs are listed on the guild messageboard. They change weekly.

We are looking for players who are first and foremost good people. This means being patient, kind and courteous to our fellow players in all situations.

We ask that you group and raid with us as much as you can. This will let both sides know if we are right for each other. Just ask any member of House Archon for grouping and what guild events we have planned.

Also, you must read all of our rules and agree to live by them. If you have any questions or concerns feel free to ask them of the GL or an officer.

There is also a 2 week probation period. This is the "last check" to find out if the House is right for you and vice versa. You are free to leave during this time with no questions asked. After the two weeks are up, you are then able to bring in one alt.



We have a quota that we maintain. If your application is not accepted, it may be because we are simply full.

Loot Rules
To begin, our loot rules are designed with the guild in mind first, and the betterment of the guild. We are not here to equip just your character, but will work in order of importance to the guild. If you have to ask why the main assist would get his Thurgadin BP before a cleric, you have no business here.

All items will be given on a need base system. That is, the people who, with that item, will benefit the guild the most...not the person who has the lowest stats. As such the more active you are, the more likely you are to acquire items from the guild. The more often you raid with us, the better giving you the item does us.

Guild Raid acquired Dropables.
  1. All items acquired through Raids is the property of the Guild. Unless otherwise stated by the Leader before hand. It is not the property of the party or parties at risk, it is the Guilds. As such it will be used in the best interest of the guild. Either awarded to a guild member who needs it or used to acquire needed items.

    Once an upgrade is acquired, you are required to return the dropable item (be the replacement dropable or not) and regardless of how you acquire the upgrade (unless through trade of the dropable item). We are working for the betterment of the guild, and as such trading a dropable item for another item to aid you may be allowed (after officer approval). Again, upon upgrade you are required to return this item also.

  2. Non-Guild Acquired Dropables and Guild groups.
    We strongly encourage anyone with dropable items they don't need to donate them to the guild. We will not and cannot require anyone to do this, of course. But there are people who may need the item in the guild.

  3. No Drop items.
    The only string attached is, regardless of the item you are getting being dropable or not, you are required to give up any Guild acquired item it replaces.

  4. Complaints.
    If you have a complaint about loot, please contact an officer and he/she will deal with your situation. Let it be noted that constantly complaining about loot yet not attending any raids may go as far as to get you removed from the guild. Never at any point should complaints about loot be either in public chat of any kind/Guild Chat/or any message board. We will not have any arguments on the boards about loot, though constructive criticism is good.

The only exception to any of the rules above regarding giving up guild loot is in the following cases:

  1. That item has resists on it that would be used on a raid in the future.
  2. That item has effects that are used by the character in question (dealt with on a case by case basis).

  3. That item has stats that would be used for another form of hunting (eq. solo gear for AC).

In all these cases though, the guild comes first. It would not be fair for you keep a cloak if you only plan to use it twice a month if a member would use it as a permanent item. Rule II also comes before everything.

Invites :
People from outside the guild will be treated as fairly and decently as possible. However, House raids are to equip and better the guild. Only if the guild doesn't need a item will it go to invites.
