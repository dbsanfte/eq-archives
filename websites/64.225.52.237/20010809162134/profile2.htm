Imagemotion's success and our ability to provide dynamic web solutions is due to the strength of our leadership and vision:

Coco Bermudez:
President, Co-Founder
As a founder and President of Imagemotion Inc., Coco has played a key role in building the company and its vision. Coco received his B.S. in Telecommunications and Associates Arts degree in Graphical Design and Electronic Media from the University of Florida, and then spent over 10 years in video production for companies such as PBS affiliate WUFT and Ultimate VDO Productions. Coco also served as Advertising Production Supervisor for the award-winning Florida Alligator, the largest independent newspaper in North America, where he managed a staff of 12. Before founding Imagemotion with Larry Elliott, he founded and directed the Non- Linear Division for Digital Video Systems Inc. Coco's responsibilities included making recommendations concerning commercial non-linear digital editors for various broadcasting networks and production houses in the Caribbean, South, Central and North America.

Lawrence Elliott
C.E.O., Co-Founder
As a founding partner and C.E.O. of Imagemotion Inc., Larry has an impressive track record as a project manager, branch manager, account executive and entrepreneur, with over 12 years of experience in technology-based industries. A graduate from the Ft. Lauderdale Art Institute with an Associates degree in Science, Larry began his career in video production and transitioned into producing large-scale corporate events for companies such as Colgate-Palmolive, Citibank, and Wyeth-Ayerst International. He was quickly promoted to branch manager, and in his three years, he quadrupled annual sales, while managing a staff of 8. Larry moved to Tampa in 1998 to provide high-level consulting and project management in the broadcast integration field. He founded Imagemotion in 1998 with Coco Bermudez, as a production resource for high profile clients.
