Company Profile
In 1998, partners Lawrence Elliott and Coco Bermudez founded Imagemotion as a broadcast video production company based in Tampa, FL. Imagemotion quickly established an impressive client roster including Bennett, Kuhn, Varner (BKV), Crispin, Porter and Bugolsky and Save the Children. As the Internet began to change the face of communications, we recognized the significant multi-media and design opportunities emerging through the web, and incorporated Imagemotion, changing our focus to a full-service multimedia and web development firm. We utilized our core foundation and assembled a talented team of writers, designers and developers. Together, we provide our clients targeted turn-key Internet solutions, utilizing streaming audio and video technologies to create an online advantage for our client companies.
