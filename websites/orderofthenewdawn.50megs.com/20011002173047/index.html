Welcome to the Order
This site is the temporary home for The Order of The New Dawn.
We are a new guild in the game of Everquest, found on the Mithaniel Marr server.
Since this is a temporary page, please, excuse the mess.
Charter
We few have gathered to create a commonplace for Wood Elves of the Bard, Druid and Ranger classes in an effort to learn from one another and to explore and catalogue the lore of Norrath.
The world we inhabit is vast and to many, unknown. Our race has forever been directly connected to the land itself and, those who follow the paths of the Bard, Druid and Ranger are attuned to it in a way that many could only dream of being.
We have rallied the followers of these paths because of their affinity for and pure dedication to our world.
Druids are the keepers of Norrath's spirit. Through their wisdom and guidance we will teach others to see the essence of the land, the life it gives, and how its will affects us all.
Rangers are the embodiment of Norrath's strength. From thier experience and knowledge we will teach others to respect the land and its inhabitants, and guide them along the safest paths.
Bards are the unerring memory of Norrath. Through their songs and tales we will remind others of the history of Norrath and the legends who have walked before us.

Each member of The Order is dedicated to learning all they can about our world, sharing that knowledge with all who would benifit from what we have learned.
We will strive to assist each other and advance each member's knowledge. We will experience all we can in order to learn more about ourselves and Norrath. In doing this we will enable ourselves to be more capable of helping others, guiding them safely through the lands and perhaps, if we are fortunate, we may also teach them something along the way.

The Order of the New Dawn believes that all denizens of Norrath should be treated equally and fairly. Never shall a follower of The Order condemn another on a basis of race, gender, class, religion, lack of experience or knowledge. Nor shall any follower ever resort to treachery, deceit or violence toward innocents to achieve their, or The Order's, intended goals.
Any follower of The Order found mistreating, misleading, abusing or otherwise acting in a dishonorable fashion toward another (whether guild member or not) will be brought before the Council.
The offender will be granted the opportunity to present their case but, if evidence and witnesses show their guilt, they will be placed on probation.
If the offender does not display a change of character and continues to act in the same manner, they will be removed from The Order for a period of time agreed upon by the council. This removal can range from days, to weeks or even permanent expulsion.
We will harbor no hate or ill will. We know only peace and wish to spread this peace through the lands of Norrath.
The Order of the New Dawn represents the Wisdom, Strength and Memory of Norrath. We live to learn, teach, advise and help those who are in need.

May our deeds and words help to usher in a new era of peace and understanding.
And may our actions please the Gods who have blessed us with life.

Siobahn Soulshadow
25th Season Ranger
Guild Leader, The Order of the New Dawn
