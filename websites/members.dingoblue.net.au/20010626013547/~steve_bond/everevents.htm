This page will announce upcoming events. It will post all the information that might be needed.

We have a special event planned as our next get together.
You will notice on our Forum a thread posted specifically to find out what people would like to see happen at this event. We have in mind a FAIR of sorts. Thus we would like to include the following events:

 

Swimming (Please note that the Iskars will be excluded from this one, sorry ) - This is as it sounds, a swimming race.
Archery - A target character will be created. Rules for this event will be posted here.
Drunken Footrace - Drink Drink Drink then race to a defined destination.
Drunken Brawls, Single and Team - Drink yourself silly then duel.
Market - Auctioning items that you have for sale.
Treasure Hunts - Items need to be collected within a defined timeframe.
Pet Fighting - Match up of pets as well as members vs pets.
Gambling - Who has the luckiest die may win a fortune.

The majority of these events you will have to pay to enter. From this a jackpot will be created that the winner/winners will receive. I am hoping that donated items can be given out as rewards as well.

There will be rules, these are yet to be finalized.

I need some referees for this event as it is too much for one person to organise. If you wish to particate as a ref then email me.
