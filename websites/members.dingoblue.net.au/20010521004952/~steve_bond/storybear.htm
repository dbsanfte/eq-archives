* The Incredible Journey of Bearheart Talesmith. *

 

Or

* Great ways to spend 8 hours on EQ and advance only 2 levels. *

 

By Bearheart Talesmith

This is the story of a young wood-elf and his journey, nay, pilgrimage to his parents birthplace. You see, young Bearheart, although brought up in Faydark, was born in Surefall Glade, where he spent his early years running with childlike abandon through the rich and bountiful fields. Then disaster struck; His parents died by the hand of poachers whilst protecting the Glades’ bears, and so our young hero’s uncle came to fetch him back to the tranquillity of Faydark. Several years later, now a member of the Faydark Warriors, Bearheart draws plans to return to Qeynos and avenge his parents. And so the story begins:

Sighing to himself, Bearheart wiped the wasp drones thick blood from his blade. His wounds were hardly worth mentioning as the armour his uncle had given him had taken the brunt of the animal’s attack. Still, he would be careful next time. Start slow, live long. Straightening up, he gave a slight shrug and set off towards the Butcher Block Mountains and the port it held.

His wandering across Butcher Block had been largely uneventful, which, Bearheart reflected, had nearly been his downfall. Knelling down to clean the roots he had scrounged for his meal, Bearheart had failed to notice the goblin stalking him, coming ever closer. Step by careful step, the foul creature inched his way towards the wood elf’s exposed back. Dropping the root, Bearheart cursed and bent to pick it up. The rusty blade swept straight through the air where his head had been just moments before. Rolling and coming up with short sword in hand, Bearheart new he was doomed. An apprentice ranger was no match for a fully-fledged goblin. Still, he would go down fighting. They circled around each other exchanging blows. Soon, Bearheart began to get tired, began slowing down. His many cuts were sapping his strength faster than any leech could. Feint, parry, dodge, block was all he new for the next few minutes of his life. Suddenly, his world exploded; a blinding light issued from somewhere over his shoulder, and where the Goblin had stood was a pile of ash, scattering on the breeze. Amazed, all Bearheart could do was stare at his saviour, a huge barbarian heavily draped in animal skin. "Watch yourself pup, next time you may not be so lucky" Shaken out of his trance, the young elf found his tongue. "Much appreciated friend, but to whom do I owe thanks?"

"Names Gehiznik. Druid by trade. You’re far from home for a young elf." Gehiznik’s gruff voice was a change from the melodic sounds of the elves. "I seek the port of Kaladin; I’m to travel to Qeynos to avenge my parents" Bear replied. Kindness touched the old druid’s eyes. He was genuinely moved by Bearheart's plight. "Here then young one, ill give you some things to ease your journey." And with that, Gehiznik began to work his magic.

With the druid’s help, Bearheart soon found himself standing on Kaladin’s docks. It was here that he met with a lone stranger, a man of imposing proportions. A man with such strength of character that none could deny his greatness. "Names Haplow young one. You waiting for the ship?" Jolted out of his doze, Bearheart looked up at the armoured man. Standing close to 7 feet high, he certainly was impressive in full armour. "Yes. I journey to Freeport and then on to Qeynos." Bearheart shrugged deeper into his cloak, uncomfortable with the attention. Haplow was not to be put off however: "Ahhhh….so you are the one Rulsidar told me off" Bearheart sat bolt upright. What had his uncle to do with this man? The imposing man grinned. "Yes, your uncle said you would be coming this way. He petitioned me to initiate you into our guild. There is great strength in numbers little one." The young wood-elf relaxed. "And what guild would that be, friend?" Relaxed, but still cautious. "Why the Raptors of course!" came the curt reply. Bearheart was stunned. To be given a chance to join the Raptors! The guild of heroes such as Lorewolf, Foleage, Mulamber and the mighty Crushem to name but a few! "I’ll take your stunned silence as acceptance then?" The seasoned cleric was grinning again. Bearheart could only nod in bemused silence. "Well then young lad, it seems your boats arrived. Here is where we part ways for now. Be safe. And remember, you’re a Raptor. Do us proud." With a final wave, the elf climbed the gangplank and sailed into fate's arms.

Snorting, Bearheart came awake. "DAMN!" running to the fore-deck he cast about frantically. Unable to see anything in the heavy darkness that shrouded the ocean, Bearheart grabbed the nearest passenger by the collar. "WHERE ARE WE??!" The poor human could only stare at tiny ball of anxiety that had him lifted 5 inches above the deck. "WHERE ARE WE DAMMIT? HAVE WE PASSED FREEPORT?" Bearheart was red in the face from screaming. Finally the man found his tongue. "Y-Y-Yes…we passed the Freeport docks 2 hours ago….." Cursing himself for a fool, Bearheart realised he had slept straight through his stop. Muttering to himself, he began to think of ways to pass the time on the round trip back to Kaladin. A timid voice interrupted his thoughts. "Er…could you put me down now please…?"

Gaping in amazement, Bearheart watched Lorewolf saunter up the gangplank from the Kaladin docks. Jaw slack, he stared as Lorewolf came right up to him, him, a lowly Apprentice Ranger!, and introduced himself. He could only nod as Lorewolf spoke to him of his adventures with the Gnoll's of Black Burrow. Bearheart was jolted out of his amazement when Lorewolf offered to teach him the language of the Gnoll's. Learning was always a passion and this had more than piqued his interest. For the next few days, he sat, learning much from the wise old Raptor. Finally, on the third day, Freeport edged into view. It was time to part ways. Standing on the docks, Bearheart waved to the champion of the woods as he departed from view, drifting into the amber sunset. There he stood, motionless, until the final speck of the S. S. Stormbreaker disappeared over the horizon.

Bearheart stood at the entrance to Highpass Hold. He had come far, but the hardest part lay ahead of him now. While pondering the problem of sneaking past the filthy orcs blocking the passage, Bearheart failed to notice the small presence by his leg until it spoke. "Ugly buggers ain’t’ they?"

Bearheart had his sword halfway out of its sheath before he realised that the speaker meant no harm. Infact, he was chuckling. "Oh put that needle away lad, ye be makin a fool o’ ye’self. Me name’s Folage ArmpitFeet. Please ta make ye acquaintance." The gnome held out his hand in greeting. Stunned, Bearheart shook it. Too meet two legends in one day! Amazing! "Well mist country bumpkin, ye gunna stop ye gawkin so I ken get ye outta here an onna road ta Qeynos?" The little gnome smiled, taking any sting out of his words. Bearheart realised he was still staring. I seem to be doing that a lot of late, he thought to himself. "You’re guiding me to Qeynos?" A nod. "Really?" Another nod. "I mean, REALLY?" Yet another, slightly frustrated, nod. "Oh." But the little man had already walked off mumbling about youngsters and standards. The diminutive druid paused to yell over his shoulder. "Ye comin’ or whut?"

Bearheart shook his head to himself as he stared at the gates of Surefall Glade. He had made it. Between the magnanimous members of the Raptors and his personal drive, there was no way he could have failed. He had passed many obstacles, killed many monsters and run away from more still. But he had made it, and he would have his vengeance on the Millers. He knew he had to bide his time; gain more experience. But he would have his vengeance. Oh yes he would. Bearheart smiled to himself as he stepped under the great arches of Surefall and into the future. He had but one regret. He wished he had asked why Folage’s last name was Armpitfeet.
