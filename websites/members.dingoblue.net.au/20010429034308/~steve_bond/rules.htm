RULES OF CONDUCT

Every member of the RAPTORS must abide by certain rules that have been put in place to protect and enhance our reputation online. Rules dont need to get in the way of the game as it is afterall a game. Some members have already been removed for violating these rules.

 

1.    You are a RAPTOR so behave online, no swearing, insulting or other bad behaviour.

2.    KSing is something we do not tolerate and in fact will go to war against another clan if it happens to us. KSing will result in possible removal from guild.

As you can see there are not that many rules, and these rules make sense.

 

If you would like to see more rules added or modified then please email the webmaster. Please note, the PANEL will be deciding the rules from now on and they will be the adjudicators.

RULES FOR OFFICERS

The RAPTORS officers operate under a guidance system that they use throughout their careers. These 'rules' they accept when they are promoted and should not be there to hidder or prevent them from roleplaying their characters in Everquest.

1.    Being an officer is a symbol of rank and as with all ranks you carry the RAPTORS reputation with you at all times. Misbehaviour will result in loss of officership or worse, removal from the guild.

2.    Officers can and will remove members that breach this code and damage the reputation of other raptors.   Please see the RULES section to find what causes a breach. Note: I would advise the recommendation of at least 2 officers for this to occur.

3.    Officers can add members to the 'army'. Please be careful when adding members to our ranks as they also must abide by the rules set forth. Before members can join they must be sponsored by another member, this member does not have to be an officer.

4.    Officers can organise 'EVENTS' and are called upon to assist with guild events.

 

Please email me any suggestions you would like to see addes to this page.

PANEL MEMBERS

As with our other guilds we have what is called a PANEL and it is made up of members from the Guild. These select members are made up of those that originally created the guild as well as the first few members. Please note that I am not a panel member but reserve the final vote in the event of a tie.

 

1.     Panel members will be responsible for new rules, modifying of old rules.

2.     The eviction of members for violating the rules.

3.     The organisation of EVENTS and special events.

4.     The Declaration of WAR.

 

If you would like to see more rules or modification of these rules, then please contact the webmaster and he can forward them to the panel.
