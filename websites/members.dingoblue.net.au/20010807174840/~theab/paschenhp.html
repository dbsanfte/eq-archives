Paschendale - Game Related Info.

PD, or Crocodale Pee Dee , came across from Xegony to The 7th Hammer in March of 2000, after a year as Paschen du Pays Sombre.

Unimpressed with the outlook for necros, PD was born, and went off to do a lot of unusual things. PD is the ultimate minority. Female, Human, Bard. She was the 1st bard to 60, and has, in some forms, been paying for that. Swords in hand, more often than lute, PD has adventured far and wide. She has participated in many of the server "firsts" even though we are a "backwater server".
Such firsts included, first Virtuoso, first Twilight armor, first ST weapon, first Trak, Gore, Lord D, Velks, Vindi, Statue, Woushi, Zlandi, Klandi, Master of the Guard, Progenitor and Final Arbitor killings on Hammer.
She is currently in The Dark Exile , the premier guild on Hammer, and married to a wonderful cleric, Zellidon Soulifter.
I cannot imagine a world without his friendship, companionship, devotion and kindness.



Thea - the real person

I currently live in Southport on the water, and too close to the Mall for my own good. I am a Change Management Specialist for a locally based communications company, a very satisfying, if time consuming position.
My true addiction is chocolate, though it is much easier not to buy chocolate than it is not to play EQ.
