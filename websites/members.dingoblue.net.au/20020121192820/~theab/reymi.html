Reymi



Reymi is the young and impressionable High Elf Cleric on Vallon Zek, who is still a novice in the world of a full time PvP server. Originally a bard on a blue server, Reymi took the plunge and went red, hoping to find enjoyment and happiness again.
These she found in abundance, through the friendships she had previously made with Fasol/Karn and Dirks/Xtream both of whom she knew before venturing into the realms of PvP. This has led Reymi into Ancient Orden one of the best "light" guilds on Vallon.


Ancient Orden is a fiercely light 3 team guild that openly opposes 4teaming, "darks", griefers and any and all that assist the aforementioned. AO is KOS to many on the server, and Reymi has found the tag to be close to an invitation to get attacked by those opposing the guild or playing outside the PVP ruleset. Happiest in the company of Fasol, Reymi continues to adventure with AO and her friends in a world of "relative" safety.
