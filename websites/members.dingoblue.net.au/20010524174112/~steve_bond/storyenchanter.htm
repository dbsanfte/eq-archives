Enchanters have the worst pets of the pure caster
classes. I kinda suspect this is some type of
indication that we are supposed to use Charm. However,
Charm requires a good deal of organization. And since
I consider myself lucky, if the group is smart enuf to
realize how Mezz works, Charm is pretty much out of
the question.

One funny thing that I have noticed, is that NPCs
don't have the same restriction that us players do.
NPC enchanters can have their pet going AND charm
people too, no freaking problem.

I knew about this, of course. I'd heard about it. But
I'd never seen it before.

So, one day, in Cazic-Thule, I got to see it.
First-hand, so to say. It wasn't a very long
experience; about 4 nano seconds or so...

I was in a full group, in the Throne Room. We'd been
there all day/night. And every time a "named" (Tae
Em/Tae Ew) showed up, the other group took it, in
spite of the fact that we'd been there the longest.
Every time this happened, the guys in the group would
bitch and moan, and say things like "They'd better let
us have the next one, or else it's group war" and
similar intelligent and mature things. I tried to
suggest for the team leader to go tell them that the
next one was ours, but they seemed to prefer to just
bitch about what a bunch of nasty KS the other group
was. Eventually, a guy in our group, who knew some of
the guys in the other group, went over and explained,
and the other group was cool with it. 

Then, by the time the next "named" showed up, the
other group had more or less become a different group,
as everyone had been replaced, and they damn well
ended up taking the freaking thing again.

The moral here is of course that you gotta tell people
up front. The moral is of course also that had people
been decent, it'd be enough to tell em "BACK OFF --
IT'S OURS!" during the battle, or even to tell them
that the spawn WAS ours, after the battle, and have em
hand over the loot. But well, greed always win over
decency (I hope to hit the dragons or planes one day,
so I bend over backwards to be non-greedy -- and that
is of course why most people laugh at the measly
equipment I stroll around in, but uh, I'm rambling).

So... After having lost the freaking "named" one THREE
times (and died once, at lvl 30), our lizzie finally
came to pay a visit.

The Tae Em Diviner, it was. The enchanter.

The pull was a complete disaster. There was a great
big sea of undulating green lizzies everywhere.
Someone said, in group "TEMPLAR!" and I noticed the
enchanter pet somewhere in the crowd. Seing as we had
lost the mob on three prior occasions, I had loaded up
my burn, and let everyone know that I'd burn, instead
of crowd control, for this occasion, and everyone were
ok with this...

I jumped, scampered, and hopped around, clicking again
and again, on the lizzies, trying to select the Tae.
My /assist so-and-so hotkey (for the main tank) failed
to find the Tae, so I knew they hadn't managed to find
the bugger.

After a few quick seconds, I lucked out, and had Mr.
Tae Em Diviner in my target window, and shot my Chaos
Flux, 150 dd off.

I think I lasted uhm, nearly two seconds or so. I had
to scroll back in the log to see what had happened. I
barely had the time to say "SHIT" and barely the time
to THINK about asking for a heal, let alone typing the
plea in...

The Diviner was on me. And the Diviner pet was on me.
But he had also charmed the group's mage, so I had a
little nasty oracle robe wearing gnome beating me with
a stick and casting shit at me. And the Diviner had
also charmed the mage's pet, so that goddamn blob
thingie was on me also. When I thought GODDAMNIT GIMME
A HEAL, I just noticed that there was a fifth person
beating me up: our cleric.

All the ones that were charmed, made it out alive.
