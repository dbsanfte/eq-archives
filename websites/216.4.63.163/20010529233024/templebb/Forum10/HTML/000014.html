PLAYER-TAG CHANGES

As part of a continued development by Verant in improving gameplay and communication between players some significant changes are apparently about to go on the Test Server to enable players to more easily find each other. Here is the summary:

The player tags for /anonymous and /roleplay are gone. It will no longer be possible to hide your class or guild from a /who. This has apparently been done to help Clerics, Druids and Wizards be more easily accessible to players.
New tags of /help and /nohelp have been introduced. These will display above your character as a + or - symbol to reflect if you are currently acting as a player who will assist in general or are preferring not to do so. They will not however show up in a /who.
It is apparently hoped that these changes will help make the community of players on the servers tighter and more group-friendly to one another.
