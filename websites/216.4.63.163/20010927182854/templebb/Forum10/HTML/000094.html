Over the past few days I have received a couple of e-mails and some tells, the few times that I have been on line, from people asking me why I left Temple of Dawn. When I posted good bye I was torn between my feelings of wanting to point out the problems vs just leave and don’t worry about it. I guess I have been persuaded to give an explanation.

Temple of Dawn is facing some very serious problems. Some prefer to hide from these problems instead of facing the hard work and hurt feelings that would result in putting in place some sort of fix. As an example I point out Klailie’s reply to my good bye post. My answer to you Klailie is yes, I have been approached by numerous other guilds with offers but so what. That does not explain why Kellewic has quit EQ, to start new with AO, as has Findy (last I heard she might come back to EQ one day but doubts it), why Goldmane no longer participates in ToD groups or activities, why I have left and others are wrestling with that same decision and why Phinn now plays AO. Earlier this week his Ranger equivalent AO character dinged level 14.

When I left, Temple of Dawn had 44 members listed (non alts) who were level 49 or above yet could not muster two groups to go into Kedge. This decline in interest is not server wide or connected to summer time activities.... the number of players logged on each night seems about the same and other guilds do raids just like always. This decline is Temple of Dawn. Trying to pretend otherwise will do nothing to fix the problems. The officers of ToD must work to generate guild pride by accomplishing things that “the guild” can be proud of. Instead... ToD will now focus on the beginning steps of people’s epics and relatively easy quest.

This demise began with the SB Alliance and I have expressed my views on the subject many times when I was an officer. The lack of moral and continuity continues and is nourished, when one person’s goal is to stand out and above all others no matter what. Finally, the dishonesty; How many of you knew that I resigned as an officer over a month ago? Or that Kellewic and Findy quit playing EQ and that Klailie has been guild master for over two weeks? These issues and developments are being hidden from the membership in an attempt to paint a pretty face on serious problems so that all can pretend “nothing is wrong”.

Real life people join groups and clubs to feel a part of something they can be proud of. Those are the same reasons why real life people join guilds with their pretend characters within the pretend world of EverQuest. These interactions form a bridge between the make believe world and the real one. That bridge is the accomplishments and pride experienced by the real life people who play the make believe characters. The guilds are formed to enhance and further those experiences. There is no pride generated for ToD when the SBA goes to Kael. Limiting guild activities to those that can be accomplished by a single group or two of who-evers simply says, we are not capable of more. For me to acquire the three books dropped by Lord Ghiosk in the City of Mist (for my epic) I went to Karnos and auctioned for help. No pride for the Temple of Dawn in that. My final step was accomplished by me tagging along with a group of Reviction people, as they farmed the Black Reavers for one of their Druids and getting a little special help from a very good “non-guild” friend.. Again, no pride for the ToD. The one step that I did receive help with was the guild raid to “the Hole”. In return, I did my best to use that event and experience to generate pride, enthusiasm and support for the guild. Unfortunately, any positive outcome was undermined by those who focus on being “the star players” as opposed to being “team players”.

These are the reasons why I have left Temple of Dawn. I am sorry if I have stepped on some toes here but I guess that’s just the way I see it.

Conaan
a barbarian shaman

The point I'm making is that ALL guilds are seeing a decline in motivation, in participation and in loyalty. Members drift in, get thier epic, and leave for bigger and better things. Long time members leave over the stress of HAVING to be on raids and getting burned out, or are just tired of fights. Some come back, some don't. It's a natural part of the dynamics, as people struggle to see where they fit in.

People want to raid, to explore, to take on more dangerous missions, to get phat lewt, to meet new people, to hang out with friends, to explore relationships, build bonds, accomplish things together in a cohesive group, to feel special. Not all of these things can be met all of the time, and not everyone wants to do all of them. So there have to be compromises and there has to be patience. And sometimes there has to be a time when you realize that a guild isn’t the perfect fit for you.

ToD, from what I've read and what I know of the people, have never fought about loot. They fight over people. This tells me they care a great deal about their members...the reason I came here. Raids have happened pretty frequently. Bigger raids will come with time. I don’t really see the rush personally.

Klailie has taken on the role of leader during this time of transition, and I applaud her for her bravery. She has a level head and a huge heart and personally I think this guild is lucky she cared enough to assume the role. I love her for caring so much.
