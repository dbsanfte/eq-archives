Saturday, June 16, 2001

It was interesting have house guests.

My brother-in-laws came up for a visit last weekend… they had called to ask if they could… TJ and Chuck…

Phil is the oldest, and his younger brothers are… still growing…

TJ is getting divorced from Amanda, and while I feel sad on the one hand, I feel more sad over the fact that I knew even the first time I met her that it would end this way. Sometimes it sucks being right.

Chuck, just graduated from highschool, and is going to move out to Alabama – in fact, as I write this he is out there already – to live with Gary, his new wife, and their baby – who will be born any day now.

They had asked to come up and visit for the weekend, and I thought nothing of saying yes… I like having them visit, Phil likes it and it makes him happy… and that makes my life easier.

Austin decided, after having an invitation open for several months, to finally come and visit… as he needed an asylum, I am thinking… before leaving for his trip.

It was interesting. I always loved having company… there is a part of me that was raised to be a hostess… and it feels very much at home with people in the house.  That part of me has not had much to do … in recent years.

I no longer, thrive on having people here… it isn’t that I did not enjoy the company, or that I had a horrid time… it is more rather, that I no longer need that to satisfy myself… and it was therefore, tedious to have someone in the house.

Rick is living with us, but he is not like a house guest, he is a roommate, and he looks after himself, and cares for himself, and I don’t feel the urge nor the need to spend my time reassuring myself that my guest(s) are contented with him.

It is nice to have the quiet back in the house, and have the time back to myself. Its funny – truly so – I never would have thought that I would grow to love the quiet moments with myself.  In fact, I would have assumed that I would still be afraid to be alone in the house, but this is no longer the case… I can be… content. Truth be told, I am far more productive alone in the house.

It just sort of… odd, the way things happen to change you.

Perspectives that is… objectives…. What you use to base your reality upon…. Mine is in flux at the moment, and I don’t know yet if that is good or bad… but it is the case regardless.

Its sort of like the writing… I want to be a writer… but I am finding it difficult, infact excessively tedious to devote the time and effort to the pieces that I want to work on for writing… it isn’t that I don’t like the stories, in fact its far from that – I love them… its that they seem like they are inside a fish bowl….

I am wondering if this is someone’s way of telling me this is not really what I want to do… or if this is just another attempt at me to make distraction from accomplishing my goals.

*sighs*

Another day, another thought.

 

~ Alethia Signing Off
