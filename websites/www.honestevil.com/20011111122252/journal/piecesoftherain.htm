Pieces of the rain....

 

And I sit here waiting for the light...

My light having burned so dull of late so as to force me to seek...

Seek solace in the light of others to remind myself of that warm glow.

I have not lost my light but its times like this where it burns brightest...

Times in the dark, where the recesses of my mind are wanton for fantasy.

And gathering cobwebs to fuel the flame...

Its a bright light, but only until the dust burns off and then I shall be left with...

 

The embers....

 

The embers remain to watch over the flame...

To protect and guide it through the light.

Warm kisses and a soft g'night

 

Rain splatters echo in the silent room - Old songs warm my hands and hearts as I think on you.
