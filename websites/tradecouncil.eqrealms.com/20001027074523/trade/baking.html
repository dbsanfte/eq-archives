A complete guide to all trades



Like several other skills, baking is not for people who want profit. Baking is a fun skill, especially for those of us with a slightly odd sense of humor :) You have to be a little nutty to look forward to making roast wood elf on your brand new spit =P

If you want to bake, you should start off making edible goo, or any of the crunchies and candies. Those are inexpensive and will raise your skill for awhile. I'm not entirely sure what to make after that because my chart is far from complete, so please send me any information I don't have. Personally, I made fish fillets until they were trivial, then moved on to fish rolls and beetle eye pies :)

I have heard rumors of being able to make pies a different way than what the recipe books state. I haven't confirmed this, and I haven't confirmed what kind of pie it makes, so I'm putting it here instead of on my chart. Mix 2 rat ears, a cup of flour, and baking spirits (can use a giant rat ear instead of 2 rat ears) in a mixing bowl. This will make an uncooked pie. Take the uncooked pie, bake it, and you should have a pie. This doesn't need snake eggs for dough or pie tins from a smith, so if it's not a rumor, bakers everywhere will be happy. Please, if anybody confirms this recipe, let me know!! :)
