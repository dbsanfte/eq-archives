A complete guide to all trades



Brewing is strictly for fun, and for "player factions". Have a good time, and help people like you better. After all, who doesn't like getting a couple free brews now and then? :) You won't make any money, but you'll have more fun than any other tradesman out there.

Unlike with other skills, brewing isn't one that you'd want to use the cheapest ingredients with. Why? They're all cheap! It's MUCH easier to simply make fierce brews from the beginning. You get used to ingredients you have to buy, become accustomed to where you have to click, etc. Things go a lot faster and you don't have to adapt to changes (in other words, make lots of mistakes =P). After fierce brews, make Ginesh till 168. I've made a chart of recipes and trivial points. You can also download the full spreadsheet in excel 97 format. Hold shift and click the link to download
