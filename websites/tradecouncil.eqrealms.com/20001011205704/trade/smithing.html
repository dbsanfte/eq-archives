A complete guide to all trades



Smithing is a good, profitable trade, but it's overcrowded. It's difficult to sell at good prices because there are several smiths who sell for very little over cost just to corner the market. This means if you want to get into smithing, be prepared to master it, or don't expect any profit. At standard banded prices, you can't afford to fail.

Still want to go ahead? First, you have two options. You can either spend 10 to 15 practice points into smithing, or you can sharpen your way there. I started from zero and it cost less than 10 plat to get around 20. Now take a look at the smithing materials and costs chart. Obviously, you want to make the cheapest item on there that isn't trivial yet. As people send me information, I will be adding it to this chart to make it more complete. If you follow what I have so far, though, you will get to 135 skill eventually. That's the best time to start making your banded, and you can hope to make a little profit. You can make banded as early as 80 skill or so, but I wouldn't recommend it until at LEAST 115. You will lose money up until that point.

Now that you're a master smith, you need to determine prices. I won't tell you what to sell for, but I will give you advice. Take a look at my banded chart to get an idea of costs. All banded mail requires the appropriate mold, the appropriate number of metal sheets, and water. You should determine your prices by how difficult it is for you to get these things and by how often you fail. Don't throw money down the drain, sell to make money, or don't master smithing in the first place. It's just too much platinum to waste for any other reason. Sell where you feel comfortable selling. It doesn't matter if someone is selling every banded piece for 7pp. Just move to a different zone and sell for your prices. You aren't ripping anybody off by being fair, it's the other smiths who are ripping themselves off by charging so little for their time and mastery. They obviously take no pride in their work and you shouldn't follow in their footsteps.

Remember, as the smith, everything is up to you. You make the armor, you regulate the price and the rules. Some people take trade-ins, some don't. It's your business, you run it however you want to. I'm just here to give a little advice and guide you to being a master smith instead of a 16th level adventurer demanding a banded tunic for 3 plat.
