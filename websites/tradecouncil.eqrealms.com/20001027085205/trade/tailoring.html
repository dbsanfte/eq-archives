A complete guide to all trades



Tailoring is currently more or less broken. The difficulty to raise your skill past 27 (the trivial point of patchwork) is way too high for what it returns. You will have to spend lots of platinum, lots of time, or both just to raise your skill. This is because Medium and High Quality pelts are very difficult to get in the large quantities needed to raise skill. Seeing as studded and reinforced leather are not very good, I guarantee tailoring won't be worth this effort until it is improved upon. The only useful thing so far is tailored backpacks, which have 10 slots each. These require a skill of above 40 to make (or so I've heard). If you're still interested in Tailoring, see my size chart, item chart, and studs and boning chart. You can also download the full spreadsheet in excel 97 format. Hold shift and click the link.
