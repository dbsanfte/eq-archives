A complete guide to all trades



Fletching is my personal favorite among trade skills. I enjoy a challenge, and this was tough for me to get skill in. It took a LOT of combines and about 700 platinum to accomplish. If you want a trade skill to really be proud of, this is the only one there is. You also might make a few bucks while you're at it :)

To raise fletching skill, just make arrows. Lots and lots of arrows. The difficulty is based off of the most expensive component, so use 3 of the basic components and one skill raiser. Always use the cheapest one that will raise skill. Beginning fletchers will want to hang around surefall glade for cheaper prices, master fletchers will prefer kelethin for a larger market and an excuse to charge more money :)

I have to mention one other thing on fletching, and that's the ceramic vane bug. Ceramic Vanes are NEVER trivial! Even at the maximum of 200 skill, ceramic vanes aren't trivial. You can make 10,000 class 6 arrows at 200 skill and not gain one skill point... but the arrow isn't trivial :) It will also take you up to 200 skill level, provided you want to combine a lot. If you're looking to save money, use only ceramic vane arrows after 162 skill. If you'd rather have some speed, then I'd try making steel shaft arrows after the 160s or 170s, and bladed tip arrows after that. They'll cost more, but you'll get the skill faster than you will with ceramic vanes. It took me 200 combines (yes, 1000 arrows) to go from 199 to 200 skill with ceramic vanes. It's dependant on how much patience you have :)
