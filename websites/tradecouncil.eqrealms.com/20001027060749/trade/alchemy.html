Alchemy is broken. Severely broken. It's supposed to be a skill that shamans get at level 25, but the guildmaster will not train it. He tells you that you have already learned too much and cannot train it any further. Potions CAN be made by combining one bought ingredient (usually several platinum, ranging up to over 100) and one ingredient you find off of a monster which will be green to you by the time you hit 25th level. Combine these two in a medicine bag to get the potion. Unfortunately, the potions are really not worth what they cost. Your spells are much better by that time. The only concellation is that you might be able to use alchemy before 25th level, but this is an unconfirmed rumor so far. Another note, all potions that have been made have been trivial.
