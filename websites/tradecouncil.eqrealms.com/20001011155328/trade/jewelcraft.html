A complete guide to all trades



Jewelcraft is the most expensive, most profitable trade you can do. You can make tens of thousands of platinum if you work at it, but that will just cover the cost it took you to learn. Don't expect to make back what you put into it for awhile, but when you do, you can expect to be making more money than you would with any other trade skill. I don't recommend trying this skill unless you're an enchanter. You'd be better off making an enchanter and twinking it.

Not much I can say as far as raising skill. You want to make the cheapest item that isn't trivial to you until it's trivial or very close to trivial.
