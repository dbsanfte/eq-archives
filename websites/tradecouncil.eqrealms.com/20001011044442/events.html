After the newbie tournament I went into the regular tournaments. Only one person participated in the tournament of shadow, so Eredium won that. The tournament of Radiance was interesting, but the level gaps were too great. The end was interesting, though, when Murtik (level 17 wizard) managed to defeat Roop (level 20 bard). The tournament of Neutrality easily went to Gilthas, though. He even resurrected Hallen and killed him a second time =P The King of the Hill was interesting. At first, Murtik defeated Gilthas, which was surprising considering Gilthas was a level 23 druid. Upon a rematch, though, there was no competition whatsoever. Gilthas then faced Eredium (level 24 necromancer) and promptly died. Murtik requested a match with Eredium for fun... I don't have to explain the outcome. Eredium chose a Flowing Black Robe and a Jagged Band for his prize.

Hunt for the Horn

A powerful Arch Magi of the Eastern Continent has researched a powerful newspell. Combining theories from the great texts of Cazic-Thule, the tomes ofNeriak, and the personal notebook of Miragul, the greatest spell in all the circles of magic has been pieced together. All the Arch Magi needs are ingredients to seal the words of power onto his scroll. Posted on all the messageboards across Norrath, etched in every bathroom wall, scrawled in the dust on every path...

Spell Ingredients Needed:

1 Koalindl Fish
1 Dwarven Wire
1 Human Head
1 Tarnished Halberd
1 Blackbox
2 Ogre Heads (one must be Pungla's)
1 Tergon's Spellbook
1 Dwarven Head
1 Illegible Cantrip
1 Troll Head
1 Barbarian Remains
1 Locked Strongbox
1 Gnoll's Head
1 Giant Fire Beetle Leg
1 Erud's Tonic
1 Catfisher Tail
1 Rat's Head
1 Tattered Pouch
and most importantly,
1 Minotaur Horn

To be turned into the Arch Magi of the East Continent in no more than 2 backpacks with no loose items for a reward of 1000 platinum pieces.

ooc: The scavenger hunt from Hell. Collect the ingredients and notify a Trade Council representative. A designated member will arrange a meeting time with you at the pillars of Greater Faydark to exchange the reward for the ingredients. The reward will NOT be given until the ingredients are given to and checked over by the representative. As stated 'in-character', the ingredients MUST be given in no more than 2 backpacks and no loose items will be allowed. Yes, that means at least 2 10-slotted backpacks. Good luck! You'll need it :)
