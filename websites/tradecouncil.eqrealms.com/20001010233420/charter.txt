Trade Council Guild Charter

Guild Purpose

Trade Council will have the ability to make or buy any item
and sell it to the player community for a small profit. Extra
charges will be added for distant item deliveries. Other 
services include guides to anywhere on Norrath, all languages
taught to excellent, and a guarantee on all services the guild
offers. Festivals, activities, events, and other promotional 
deals will be held whenever the guild can afford them.

General Conduct

- Be friendly and courteous
- No spamming
- Help other guild members

Trade Conduct

- Advertise only once every few minutes. Do NOT spam an advertisement
- Sell at a fair price
- Encourage return customers by giving alcohol and/or baked goods
  with purchases
- Make the customer happy

Guild Rules

- No more than 3 characters of one person may be in the guild
- All of a person's alternate characters will be treated as the same 
  person
- Only 1 of a person's characters may receive guild loans
- Do not clutter the /guildsay channel with useless messages. Use 
  /tell or /gsay when you can
- Do not take advantage of the kindness of other guild members
- The guild charter may only be modified by a high council vote

Division Breakdown

- only High Council members vote on guild changes
- All High Council members need to have a title related to the work 
  they do
- Trade Division members will gather items and sell them at profit
- Guard Division members will deliver items, gather items, and help 
  other members gain levels
- Public Relations Division members will help promote the guild name to 
  people and other guilds
- All new members will be in the Initiation Division during their trial period
- Initiation Division members need to decide which division they would 
  like to be a part of when their trial is over

New Members
- All new members will be in a 'trial period' for 2 weeks.
- At the end of a guild member's trial period, he/she may be removed from
  the guild if there is sufficient reasoning.
- Guild members must choose a division to belong to after their trial period
- During a member's trial period, he/she may be asked to help out several
  different people to prove loyalty

Charter in effect as of 06/12/99
