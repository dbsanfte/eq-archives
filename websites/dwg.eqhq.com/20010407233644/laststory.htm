This is the last known recorded history for the Deathwatch Guard.

HOME

   Far in the distance Castle Karnor can be seen. It sits ruined surrounded by a wasteland of fields and gullies. Farther away near the edge of the wasteland an important event is unfolding. This is the known record of what became of the ancient and recently reformed Deathwatch Guard…

   The cold air can be felt, sweeping down from nearby snow-covered mountains. The sky overcast with snow threatening to fall. Belshin takes a long look across the field at the approaching band of oversized Gnolls called Drolvargs. His breath can be seen in front of him like an ethereal mist in the cold air. He looks over his shoulder at all that survived the first wave. Not many are left … mostly his command group and a few others. The first wave has nearly wiped out the Guard.

   Azarial, blood dripping from a gash in her hand, gives him that familiar warm smile. Ever so steady, like a rock in the midst of blowing leaves. Bair standing there, weak from using her powers in the last battle. Her body covered with sweat and blood. Sedai, robe torn and face covered with soot, looks stoically forward. Her powerful magic the only thing that has saved them so far. Mithaneil, the noble Paladin, stands protectively over Caerrie. His once bright and shining armor covered with mud, and among that blood is spattered all across the front of him, but thankfully it does not look like his own. Caerrie simply stares at the oncoming force and readies her bow. Her fate was sealed until Mithaneil rescued her from two of the foul beasts in the last wave. Off to the right Belshin can see Glithoniel and Cravin waiting to pull a flanking move when the next wave hits. He takes a deep breath, not knowing what to say.

   "They are approaching from the woods on the left!" shouts Glithoniel.

   Belshin looks to the direction of Glithoniel’s warning, a hard, almost desperate look on his face. He mutters to himself, "This is it." His strength was beginning to fail. Azarial moves over next to him, taking on that protective warrior stance she always uses when preparing for battle. She gives him a quick sideways glance and says, "My heart and soul will be with you always my love." Belshin cannot find words to say before the first volley of arrows lands around the group. Sedai begins her now familiar chanting and hurls a lightening bolt at the archers, a loud crackling sound following it. The hairs on Belshin’s arms stand on end the bolt flies by so close. Sedai is quite accomplished with her spells and lands a lethal bolt right in the middle of them causing them to scatter. With the archers down or wounded, the warriors of the group move up and charge. Just then Glithoniel lets loose with his arrows, the steadfast ranger never misses and fells many of them in a few shots. Cravin, next to him calling upon Tunare’s might to rain fire down on them.

   The enemy is falling in great numbers, but there seems to be two for every one that falls. They are almost among the main group. Belshin stands back and begins to draw upon his nature magic. "I call for the spirit and body of Tunare to help us in this, our gravest hour." A light flashes outward quickly and suddenly everyone around him turns into wolves. "Glory, Honor, and Justice for the Guard! May Tunare watch over us!" Belshin shouts as he runs towards the enemy. The Drolvargs are somewhat taken aback by the sudden change in the group and slow their approach. A voice is heard, one which no one can understand. They renew their assault with even greater speed. The two forces slam together. Wolves biting everything in site, casting everything they can. The forces of the Drolvargs overwhelm the small unit like a flood from a broken dam. Belshin takes a serious wound to his arm and then is knocked unconscious by a vicious club to the head. His body collapses to the ground and everything goes dark….

   Belshin wakes to the warm sun on his face. He has returned to his Elven form again, the spirit of Tunare has long since passed. He lifts his head slightly to look around the area. All he can see are the bodies of fallen Guardsmen and strange shapes of the dead Drolvargs. He does not see any faces from his command group before, once again, slipping into darkness…

   A cold spike hits Belshin face…another one…he opens his eyes to see the beginnings of a storm. Sitting up, a little dizzy from the blow to the head, he looks around. Not much has changed since the first time he came to. Strange large birds are picking at some of the bodies. Their rainbow colors overwhelming the eye. He thinks to himself, "I have failed you Tunare". Looking down at his arm, he sees the terrible wound. It looked bad and was beginning to bleed again from his movement. He tries to summon some strength and draw upon the nature elements of healing. Only a trickle of power flows through him, his wounds are so severe. He manages to stop the bleeding on his arm for now. Standing up on uneasy legs, he begins to walk the way he came. Towards Kelethin … so far away on another landmass in the world …
