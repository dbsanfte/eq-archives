These are the guidelines for the Deathwatch Guard.  As mentioned in other places, if you break these policies you will will be removed from the Guard after the second offence.  Even though it is easy to become a member, we expect our members to behave properly and help keep the gaming environment fun for everyone.

1) Everyone is expected to participate in the weekly role-play sessions if they are online.  Currently these sessions are scheduled to happen on Saturday nights.  These sessions can range from a few minutes to several hours depending on the situation.  The sessions also do not have to exist in one large group, and can happen in smaller groups at the same time.  The simple rule is that, if you are online those nights you will be expected to participate if something is going on.

2) Rank structure will be respected at all times.  We want to express a very disciplined and organized group.  It will be expected that a lower ranking member salute a higher ranking officer.  Remember an officer is a Lieutenant or higher.

3) Promotions will be handled by superior officers/members.  You need at least two people to recommend you for promotion. An exception to this is that the Knight Commander and any Knight Captain's can promote with out the need of a second person to approve.   I would like to have formal role-play promotions carried out at the weekly meetings when this happens, if possible.

4) Missing, or skipping out on role-play sessions because you want to camp some spawn or 'powerlevel' will not be tolerated.  If this is the case, then you will receive one warning.  If it happens again then you will be removed from the guild.

5) If you avoid role-play sessions for no good reason then you will receive a warning.   If this happens again you will be removed from the guild.

6) Everyone is required to give me (the GL) some sort of e-mail address you can be contacted at.  This address does not have to be published on the web site.  You just have to let me know when giving me the address if you want it on the site or not.   (If not it will show up as 'Logged'.)

7) You will not harass other members to help you get a piece of equipment or to camp something for them.  It is their own prerogative to help you out in those matters.  If they don't want to do it then fine, don't harass them about it.  If it becomes a problem you will be notified by me (Belshin) that there are complaints of such matters and it will also serve as your first warning.  The guild does not exist so you can pimp out your own characters.

8) It there are complaints that you are KSing someone, then you will be given a warning.  The second offense and you will be removed.

9) Respect all other members at all times.  We are here to play a game and have fun.  If someone in the guild pisses you off then don't act like a four year old, there is no need to fight or argue among ourselves.  There have been many situations in the past where members have left for unknown reasons, to then later find out it was because of someone in the guild.  The sad thing is that the person that left never told the offender (or anyone else), and thus the situation was never taken care of.




Remember, this is an role-play guild.  We do not exist to be the highest players on the server or to have the best equipment.  We all do have our own personal goals, and that is why there are only certain nights where one is expected to role-play.  If you have any comments or questions about the rules laid out here, then e-mail Belshin.
