Rothsamus Shiatan

Of The Deathwatch Guard

   Many years ago in the quiet town of Qeynos a child was born different from others in breed. From the doomed love of a barmaid for a wandering Elf from across the sea I came. My father, a brave elf my mother always said, was from Faydwer and the city of Felwithe. He had traveled the dangerous seas and braved the karanas to make his way to Qeynos on a quest for Byzar Bloodforge. From what my mother recounts to me she was working in the Fish Ale when my father came to exact Bloodforges revenge on Trumpy Irontoe. When he saw my mother there, a simple barmaid but beautiful beyond compare to him. He some how managed to get Trumpy drunk and angry. Trumpy left and my father following him home made the dwarf pay for his crimes and took his head. Instead of returning to Kaladim he sent the token of his deed with a dwarf heading home who promised to deliver it to Byzar. Thus he stayed for my mother and their love grew and I was conceived. But this happiness would not last for the corrupt guards plotted against my father unknown to him. He was asked to slay Fippy Darkpaw, in what they said was a easy kill much like the Gnoll pups that had been terrorizing the North Gate. So my father took up his tarnished longsword and headed out. From what the good guards at the North Gate say he fought valiantly but was slain in battle before they could aid him. Thus when I became of age I joined the local Paladin guild, being one of the places that accepts my half-breed kind. They taught me about honor and strength, valor and kindness, and above all truth. Thus the time came when I laid down the rusty short sword given by my guild and took up my fathers tarnished longsword for it would soon taste gnoll blood and would help me gain the skills I needed to make Fippy and those guards pay and pay dearly. After a time I had finally reached manhood and was made a true Holy Knight and had acquired new armor and a fine weapon, so I passed my sword on to a knight in training, wished him well. I then headed out into Norrath to seek for new weapons and skills to further my goals of revenge as well as be a shining light in a shadowed world.

   After journeying through the cold north I happened upon a mighty evil skeleton said to be the vengeful lyrist. A furious battle insued and while what little blood I had left slowly flowed from me, I saw a large man in leather armor and shimmering with a blue light. I began to feel a little better and my wounds healed some I then felt as though I could run like a wolf! He quickly grabbed me a yelled "RUN TO THE CAVES!" I managed to make it a safe place in the caves with him, then he told me his name, Galamorf. A young Shaman and a traveler as well much the same age as I, he afford his companionship and magic to our mutual betterment thus we have become old friends and have traveled much together. After a time I grew home sick and returned home to Qeynos only to find my mother dead, she not being a fighter by nature had been slain by a swarm of giant rats. When I heard this news I slew every giant rat I could find till their blood baptized me and I was red with it head to toe. When I returned home Galamorf said there was a visitor in my mothers room. There I gazed at a beautiful half elf and a druid by the look of her, her name Midana. She then told me her story and that she was indeed a sister to me of my fathers’ house her story is one best saved for her to tell. Thus I found out of her being in a powerful Guild one I shall now never forget the Deathwatch Guard and her engagement to Ralifar a noble soul. After meeting with her Lord Belshin he made the pronouncement that though I was unknown to him was worthy of entrance into this mighty guild. Now the wedding of Ralifar and Midana was not to be a secret love kept even from me, had grown between her and Galamorf my good friend. I was unable to attend the wedding but was told that during the ceremony Galamorf objected to the union of Midana and Ralifar and then took the bride for himself (not against her will I might add). Now Ralifar is gone and many new faces mix in with the old but something still wasn’t right in my heart old vendettas remained. As always Fippy Darkpaw and the corrupt Guards in Qeynos weighed on my heart so I returned to Qeynos.

   There I waited for Fippy I could feel him near then trough the rain I saw him, charged him blinded him with my magic, he ran from me by the guards but they knew my ends and stayed away from him to let me finish it. There I struck him taking off one of his furred legs; he was dying but no longer blind. I forced his eyes to mine and said "My name is Rothsamus Shiatan, you killed my father, prepare to die!" Then with a merciful stroke not shown my father I took off the foul creatures’ head. I still have a flame of hatred burning in my heart for those guards who conspired to kill my father but for now I am satisfied but someday they will pay as well.

   The time for my tale has ended the time for glory and honor has come, we the free peoples of Norrath have banded together for a common good and now as I take up my weapon, and pledge my life to you all I feel the pride of our friendship and strength and secure in the knowledge that we shall be victorious!
