The History of the Deathwatch Guard

(The history section is somewhat small.  If anyone would like to embellish the story please let me know.  My history background tells on how the DWG came back.)

  It was between the Age of Blood and the Lost Age that existed the once coveted Deathwatch Guard.  this elite unit of men and women were the last line of defense for the High Elven king Taliz Dal'mah.  Dal'mah was one of the wealthiest kings of this period and could easily afford to maintain the elite group.  They had everything from the best training, to the best equipment, and even the best lodgings.   There was not one faction in the entire world that would dare to personally challenge him because of the Guard.  They were the best of the best.
   Things change though, and when the great forests and rivers of his land dried up the kingdom stumbled, and then fell.  He could no longer afford to maintain the Guard.  Out of honor for the king, the members of the Guard remained with out any monetary support.  It was not enough though.  The entire land was beginning to change, and even the Druids could not keep things fertile.  The great nation of Taliz Dal'mah fell, not to a formable army, and not to some great creature of Myth, but to the very land they lived on.  The Guard remained until Taliz died, but by that time they had become more of a rag tag group.  Even though Taliz had a successor to his throne, there was not throne to rule over.  The Guard finally disbanded, and the once great kingdom faded into history and eventually was forgotten.

                                                                                        - Dalmac, High Elf Historian

OOC: I took the setting directly from the Verant sight that explains the history of the world.  If you read that you will see that a desert (Ro I believe) was once a lush forest but was thrown into chaos by the gods during that time period, this is where the old empire existed.
