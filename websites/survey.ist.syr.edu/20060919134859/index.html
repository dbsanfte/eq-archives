Thanks for choosing NSurvey as your prefered survey and form engine software.
  • If its your first start login as admin, password admin.
    Once logged and once you have created a survey / form, you can change your password, username from the user section at any time.
  • You can also run NSurvey in a single user mode (anonymous), open your web.config and find the FormUserProviderSingleMode element for more information.

  • Change your mail server settings from the web.config at any time using a text editor

  • You may start now using the online administration tool.
     Log into the administration section

  • Ready to import Xml form samples are available in the FormSamples directory or on nsurvey.org's Xml online form directory, you can also share and distribute your own forms models there ...

Last but not least ... Don't forget to leave your suggestions / bugs reports on http://www.nsurvey.org
