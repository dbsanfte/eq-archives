The Art of ePvP
July 16, 2001
  This came from one of our subscribers. Want to get a very high rating on BG? Try these tactics...

Tired of looting cloth, killing naked players or getting only 15 copper for your kill? Instead, would you rather be one of the richest players in the game and still have many players to group/level with?

The Everquest PvP servers have less rules than other servers and this combined with the player-killing aspects, make these servers ripe for the ePvP player. Unlike the disgusting truth and justice seekers, or the righteous revenge-only killers - ePvP stands for "exploiting PvP." This player does anything and everything to amass their wealth and by far, builds more wealth faster than any other role player. Server rules are pushed and ignored whenever possible to quickly amass wealth. ePvP players can still easily level since there are a number of guilds that support ePvP...making these playing techniques even stronger.

So, here are some VERY profitable techniques for the ePvP player looking to exploit and share his "grief" with others. Remember that the GMs can't actually issue a warning or ban a player unless they personally witness the event. This is due to everybody's frequent in-game lying and rather than play judge, a GM must witness the infraction themselves. So, try them out a few times and go to the next one...and watch the pp and items just roll in.

Fictitious Teleporting
This is really how to get your start as an ePvP and get some base financing setup. Players line up to invest in your success. It is quite simple...auction and sell teleports for 15p and up, really high in uber zones, and then gate out with your free money. Casting classes definitely look better on the exterior, but plat can be taken for teleports from any class. Just go "anon" and if you're a mage, wizzie, necro or enchie it is like taking candy from a baby. When you log on, teleport for a few hundred plat just to get you in the spirit of ePvP. I have even received money for teleporting as an ogre warrior, there is no limit to someone's stupidity.

Fictitious Buff Casting
Advertise "WTS SOW or Clarity Group Buffing, make offer and I come to you". Get your plat first, gate back to a safe spot. I normally fake-buff a group for 50p a shot.

Kill in Town
The towns are a very rewarding killing experience for the ePvP. Players in town tend to be fully dressed and carry loads of pp on them under the false sense of security the town walls offer. Once a ePvP understands casting ranges within towns, players will drop like flies and the yard-sale will begin. Great opportunities in town include: players on the way to the bank, caster's guilds where players come to buy spells, forges, on docks, or any other place where a guard's pathing can be timed or aggro range eliminated. Bottom line, people are very sloppy players in town...kill em, loot their best items and then type /ignore.

Zone Camping
 set up your partner on one side of the zone and you on the other. As people come through, cast or melee before they even get initialized in the zone. Many times you can take half their health off before they even get control of their char. The first reaction for the one being killed is normally to zone again and go back to the other side - "Meet my friend" - Eureka, another yard sale.

Selling Summoned Weapons
yep, that is right, players will buy summoned weapons at the lower levels. This can turn 50p sales many times over in the lower zones. If you can't summon them yourselves, get a mage to summon some swords for you. Talk it up a bit and settle for 50p each cause you need to get rid of them. This is great cause it leaves players complaining to the GM that their weapon was lost by Verant the next time they log on.

Sell Items on Other Chars
The oldest trick in the book. Sell a high-end item and ask for the money first because you need to log on to another character and give them the item after. Insist on this "as you have been burned too many times." This astounds me to this day that this still works. Build trust in conversation. You can even offer lower priced items for them to hold...anything to build your profit margin.

In Combat Killing (not an exploit)
When groups are in combat fighting, the group talk and text is flying by so fast that it is very easy to "lurk" in the background. Once their "shitstorm" begins, step up and drop the most vulnerable player. 75% of the time you can step in and slaughter the unsuspecting, loot their body, and leave - and the group is still fighting the mob. This is because in battle, when you get hit, you assume it is from the mob in front of you and even if you type a message to your group for help, most players aren't smart enough to turn down their messages so their pleas for help go unnoticed. Miscommunication is everywhere.

Kill the same player multiple times
All too many times after a player has been killed, people come back to their body, pick up the remains, fully don their equipment and armor, then off to being the same sloppy-ignorant player they were. Especially after they have been reassured that you have gotten what you need and will leave them alone. So after your ePvP kill...rinse, repeat.

Fake Grouping
One of my favorite techniques. Shout in the zone that you need one or more additional members. Schedule the incoming players to meet at different spots. Make your rounds, killing them one by one. If done correctly, you should feel like a Brinks truck, making your pickups for the day. As a healer class that has infiltrated a group or formed a partner, you can withhold heals from the target so your partner can be sure to have a weakened target. While the target is being slaughtered and you aren't healing, simply claim you were AFK. But if your partner starts losing health, heal him up to full and then claim you fat-fingered your targets.

The VERBAL GAME
Never, never forget the importance of the verbal game. Anyone that has played sports understands this concept. A good verbal game can incite anger and even more stupid decision making leading to multiple kills. After every kill, make sure you throw in at least one verbal jab. Some of my favorites include:
- "Geez, you dropped faster than your Mom on nickel night"
- "My God, did you scream loud when you died!"
-"This is like a shopping spree! Weeeeeeeeeee!

After dropping a player and looting an item, immediately auction their item in the zone. "Now selling: Bashur's Crafted Greaves". Be sure to fire a few auctions off once Bashur has reentered the zone too. If anyone asks where you got them from (thinking its a new item), just tell them honestly "They drop off a named PC called Bashur".

Enjoy your wealth :)
by Tony
