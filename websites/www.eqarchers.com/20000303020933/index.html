Who we are

This guild on the Solusek Ro server of the Everquest game world is undergoing major structural changes, regarding guild government, guild events, and guild goals.

We are a role-playing guild which believes in honor, justice, kindness, fairness, balance, and friendship. Many of us are like family, and if you are a member you are encouraged to speak up and talk with other members of the guild. So please do.

Once, we were a guild of archers and fletchers, but no longer are we restrictive towards just those types of people. We also try to lobby for improvements to the game. And we try to be witty, especially in public channels, such as auctions or shouts, for this helps for us to role-play at all times.

Goals as a guild

We strive to be honorable, and uphold our tenets. We are role-players. We strive to be examples of what people can be in the world of Norrath; building friendships, families, while upholding honor and fairness (and having fun).
