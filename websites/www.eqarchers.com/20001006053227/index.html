Who we Were

The Archers were a guild on the Solusek Ro server in the game Everquest. We strove to be more than the average player morally and as friends to those around us. Throughout the time that the guild existed we have made many friendships, strong alliances, and luckily very few enemies.

Over time, we lost members for various reasons, and after a while the guild we once knew slowly faded away. Our beloved founder and leader Medea left under harsh and perhaps the wrong reasons. The core groups that had held the guild together and held friendships together slowly disbanded and over time the Archers beccame but a shadow of its former self. After a mass exodus of members or two, I, Kuchulahn was left holding the pieces of a guild that i loved, and through it all was torn between friends that had left, and a guild i thought i could sustain and strengthen. It worked. The guild got stronger, we grew once again. But, those who remembered the golden days of the Archers could never be satiated with this new guild with an old name.

And so, it was decided that it was better to burn out than to fade away, and the Archers became a guild of ghosts, a disbanded guild, with many alumni. And for all those who were once an Archer, I have redone the members page to encompass as many of the alumni as i can remember.
