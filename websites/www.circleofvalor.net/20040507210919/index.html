Monday, April 19, 2004
New faces and Interesting places
By Knevil
Untitled Document

Over the past month we have spent a lot of time in EP's, Earth, Water, Air and Fire have all seen mobs drop to our combined effort. We're now taking down the upper level mobs on our way to finishing off the bosses. We had a run at Fennin and learned a lot but due to the time of day had to leave him standing.

Mobs we hit were varied and most with great sucess, even our failures are rare and we have learned a lot from them.

Down went VT, RZ, MM, TT, Bert/CD, RD, Earth Rings, Air Rings, Pyro, Babs, Birds, Druav, Reparm, Kriizk, Ofossaa, Hydrotha, Rinturion, Gakamenial, Escalardian, Calebgrothiel.

Congrats to the Clerics for a chain so perfect it made Fenthen cry infront of FenFen.


Welcome to our new members and apps, this month we have opened our doors again and are recruiting.

Welcome Camin, Fleece, Zberyn, Kanguran, Vihanna, Soffty, Mulaz, Telzard, Gildar, Karisuto, Solnos, Mochiach and Drefanrahl.

Also a shout out to our apps it's been great having you along to the raids and we will be doing a week of backflags a month till were straightened out that way.
