Thursday, July 10, 2003
Hear ye, hear ye…
By Reny

RD

Late on the eve of July the 10th CoV, UD, and DI rallied in masses to destroy Rydda’Darr. The guardians where killed in a matter of seconds, though some where held onto for pets. Hey, who can resist a guardian who calls you "master"? The battle was short with only a few bodies to be found. All where ressed and made it back in time to obtain the flag. The loot for Rd was Elven Bow of Mysticism (X’s 2) and a spec. Those where won by, Tanadrr (CoV/bow), Dilph (DI/bow), and Gradlin (CoV/spec). WTG you three!

Princess Trial

Custodian Marr fell fast and the fun began! Many mobs spawned, but with our great strategy where no problem to soon defeat. Each mini boss (if you will) was brought to a swift death. The main boss fell only moments after. The loots where ornate leather tunic pattern and ornate silk robe patens. They where won by Suar (DI/robe) and Falshir (UD/ tunic). WTG you two.

Villager Trial

The mobs went marching one by one… and they died that way too! Thanks to the excellent teamwork and some fancy footwork by our kiters this trial was a walk in the park. The loots was 2 specs which where won by Graydane (CoV) and Sarbosia.


Monday, June 30, 2003
The Great Assling Race
By Reny
Holy Halflings!

CoV members got some funny looks in Rivervale on Thursday the 26th. Why? Everyone was a halfling! Not just ordinary halflings though... everyone who attended Thursday night's "raid" was a PVP level 1 druid halfling. The bojective was to run across Norrath and die. The path of the race was: Rivervale too... Misty Thicket book too... PoK ( get secret item from Biddio ) too... Greater Faydark (Felwithe) too Gfay/Butcher Block zoneline (get secret Item from Tevixa) too... Kaladim book (get secret item from Biddio) too.... PoK too... Tox Forrest too... Paineel newbie area (Grab secret Item off Wyeen) Jump into the Hole!!! (Die Die Die) Things started a bit later than planned. The race was supposed to start at 8 PM EST. We didn't actually get started till about 8:30. Everyone gathered and turned in their books to become PVP. A few people had to make new toons. Yeah, killing rodents at level 1 is awesome exp. More awesome than some people thought it would be. Everything went smoothly for most, some got lost in Butcher Block. Guess it has been a while sense we have traveled through that zone. There where a few deaths along the way, mostly people forgetting they do get agro in newbie zones. The winners for the night where: Chuwy in first place winning; 1kpp and a ton of booze to share. Samadcit in second place winning; 500pp, a dueling weapon and an AC buff. Fen in third place winning; 250pp, a dueling weapon and an AC buff. Fen won the duel between him and Sam. Way to go winners! Can't wait till the next run across Norrath... maybe next time everyone will be gnomes! ;)
