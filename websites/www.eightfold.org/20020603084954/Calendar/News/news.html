December 23, 1999

Eightfold Path will be holding a special event on Thursday, December 23 at 7:00pm Pacific Time (GMT -8).  As the holiday season is fully underway we will be keeping a low profile on this one.  Goguyo Efftieyen will be inducted into the rank of Grandmaster of Flowers to serve as our third guildmaster.  This will take place in the East Commons tunnel.  Please stand by me for his induction - he will be a great asset to us all!  Afterwards we will travel to the Oasis of Marr for some celebratory Orc hunting.  In January we will resume our high energy get-togethers with another all out battle against evil!  All are welcome.

November 17, 1999

Eightfold Path will be holding their first event - the Drunken Guildmaster Bash.  This is your chance to beat up your guildmaster and practice up on that ever-important drinking skill.  This event will take place Wednesday at 7:00pm Pacific Time (GMT-8) at the beautiful Lake Rathetear Arena.  Bring your friends, win valuable prizes - all are welcome.  Liquor provided courtesy of Eightfold Path.  Email Riun Qi for a detailed set of rules or if you need assistance for travel.

October 28, 1999

The Founding Circle of Eightfold Path is complete - thank you to all of our new members who helped build our foundation for a new beginning!

October 21, 1999

Eightfold Path forms an alliance of friendship with the guild Tunares Vengence.

We have formed an alliance with the guild Tunare's Vengence.  Please treat their members as you would your brothers and sisters in Eightfold Path.  They are a guild dedicated to protecting the land and people of Norrath and have many of the same values as ourselves.  Eightfold Path looks forward to a long friendship with Tunares Vengence including joint events and helpful exchanges.

October 18, 1999

Eightfold Path is officially a guild on Rodcet Nife!  We welcome all to apply for membership.
