If you wish to participate in the BattleCom communications system please fill out the form below and I will send you a registration number.  The following is a detail of what BattleCom is and how to get/use it:

BattleCom is a client program that you run before intering Norrath.  It allows voice communication between players to alieviate that annoying typing in the middle of a battle (among other things).  The system requires a full duplex sound card to funtion to its full potential.  After that, all you need is a pair of speakers (which you all ready have) and a mic (a headset can also be used) connected to the sound card.  Simple as that!  For those of you connecting at 56K this is a very low bandwidth program so, never fear, it will not interrupt of slow down your EQ connection.  At that point, everyone connected to the server can talk as if we are all in the same room...
