We offer email to all guild members who attain the rank of Master and above.  Upon promotion to Master you will be notified of your address and password.

There are three ways that you can use your email account at eightfold.org:

- Have all email forwarded to your regular email box (this is the default)
- Use the web interface to send and receive all of your email from any web browser
- Setup Outlook Express or the like to send/receive your email

You can select between these options by clicking the "Manage Box" button below.  You can also change your password, add a last name and enable an auto-response message there.

To use the web interface to send and receive mail be sure you have the proper selection in the management section and then click on "Check Mail" below.  You must be sure to first remove the entry from "Forward Email To:" in the "Manage Box" section so that email will not be forwarded out of your guild mailbox to your regular email box.  You can then go directly to mail.eightfold.org to check mail in the future.

Additionally, you can use a standard mail program such as Outlook Express to handle all of your email.  The necessary settings are as follows:

POP3 or IMAP server = eightfold.org
SMTP server = eightfold.org
Username = Your first name in Norrath
Password = Your assigned password or whatever you have changed it to.
