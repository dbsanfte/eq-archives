The Eightfold Path

"We are all but snowflakes, directed by the winds of fate, falling from the heavens to our final rest in the earth.  The seasons cycle, the flowers hide from winter, and again we are snowflakes, falling to kiss the earth… unable to see the cycle, not knowing that we are born in heaven, unaware that we are not snowflakes at all, but something far greater…"

      - The Book of Flowers

The Eightfold Path is a brotherhood of kind souls, united in their desire to achieve enlightened perfection, and to bring peace and joy to all of Norrath.  It is a path of service to one's brothers, for true wisdom is gained only through courage, generosity, forgiveness, trust, and love.  Followers of the path adventure through the world, freely helping those who need guidance or aid, whether it be treasure or deed.  A brother of the Way commits their life to perfecting their body and mind, so that they may be strong enough to vanquish implacable evil, and wise enough to see the ethereal path leading to enlightenment.

The Eight Values

To stay on the path one must adhere to its values, which teach how to hone the spirit and lead a noble life.  The Eight Values are:

Right Seeing

See things how they truly are, not how you expect them to be.  Greatness and nobility may take a displeasing visage, as is sometimes the case among our Troll, Ogre, and Dark Elf brothers.  Right seeing is seeing the soul's intent.

Right Thought

Avoid angry or envious thoughts, as they are distractions from the true Path.  Some will always seem unjustly fortunate or act in a way unbecoming of their standing; you must be forgiving of these brothers, lest your thoughts be drawn from that which leads to wisdom.

Right Speech

A noble person has no need to lie or slander, and will speak only that which he believes to be helpful and true.

Right Action

Do not steal, and freely protect and aid those in greater need.  Strike down those who are irreversibly evil; it is part of their soul's penance, and removing them from the world eases the way for the innocent.

Right Living

Cherish your days as a chance to learn and enjoy; strive to live your life in Norrath so that it brings as much joy to others as it brings yourself.

Right Effort

Be brave, for fear weakens the spirit and encourages evil.  Seek adventure to strengthen your spirit, and strive to master all that you may learn.

Right Will

Be content with the path you have chosen, and stay true to it.  There are many ways to greatness, but it is a goal that is only gained through steadfastness and patience.

Right Meditation

Reflect on the world about you, enjoy its splendor!

 

Brothers of the path usually come from the monk calling; however, purity and nobility are not bounded by race or caste, and some of the most great and wise brothers and sisters have come from other disciplines.  All who yearn for peace and happiness within and without will find a place in the Eightfold Path.

 

Rankings

Within the Eightfold Path, members are given names indicating their progress towards the ideal of joyous enlightenment.  Higher ranks do not command lower ranks, all may speak their minds freely and do as they choose, as each must find their own path.

Initiate

This is person who has just joined the order, and has yet to grasp the true significance of the path.  They are treated with exceptional kindness as they grow in understanding; they are often jokingly referred to as "grasshopper".

Immaculate

This is a member who has proven their spirit is ready to undertake the path, and is sincere in their desire to bring happiness to the world.

Master

A brother who has shown extraordinary courage and generosity is given the title of Master, as they have defeated the worldly delusions of greed and fear.

Master of Dragons
Master of the Lotus

These two ranks are equal, though opposite in nature.  A Master of Dragons is one who has attained mastery in combat, and thus has true understanding of death: it's inevitability, and its frequent necessity.  A Master of the Lotus is one who has attained mastery in the healing or craft arts, and thus has true understanding of the creative essence, and recognizes the impossible level of genius in the design of the world.  Both of these understandings are necessary for later ranks.

Master of the West Wind
Master of the East Wind
Master of the North Wind
Master of the South Wind

Members that have mastered the Dragon and the Lotus may undertake a quest to gain mastery of one of the four winds.  These quests are extremely dangerous, sometimes overwhelmingly so, as they are tests more of steadfastness to the Eight Values than of might or guile.  Each quest is a guarded secret, and is centered around a particular region of the world (either in the north, south, east, or west).  One may become a master of the winds in any order, but no matter what the order taken, the quests will increase in difficulty.  They may culminate with treks into Cazic-thule, or the Plane of Fear.

Master of Winter

This title is given to one who has mastered all of the four winds.  They have shown purity of spirit and true understanding of the Way.  They have gained near-ultimate wisdom.  But they have not yet achieved enlightenment…

Master of Autumn
Master of Summer
Master of Spring

These ranks do not have requirements easily put into words.  They cannot be earned by conscious action, as they are names for masters who act as if guided more by spirit than the mind.  The rank is not something describable, but it is knowable.  Once one has gained mastery of the four seasons they will become an assistant officer within the guild, having the privilege of inviting in and mentoring new members.

Grand Master of Flowers

This is the highest rank one may obtain in the Eightfold Path.  It is true enlightenment: the total mastery of mind and body, the perfect purity of intent.  It is the rank of the leaders who's responsibility it is to guide the guild and its members in the Way of the Eightfold Path.  Only monks may test for this most honorable rank.

 

Guild Operations

Guild Restrictions

The guild will be open to all good classes/races who pledge to follow the Way of the Eightfold Path.  The guild is not open to evil characters or any class that traditionally practices skills contrary to our beliefs – these include Necromancers, Shadow Knights, and Rogues.

Officer status is considered to achieved with the mastery of the four seasons.  These individuals will be re-invited into the guild as assistant officers, their duty to promote the Way by increasing membership to the guild.

One must be of monk class to attain full leadership status.  This is due to the nature of the monk's calling and that since birth they have followed the Way.  Monks have a natural spirituality which allows them to achieve oneness with the Path and must always be the "overseers" of this process for those who have newly dedicated themselves to enlightenment.  Officer status is considered to be at the level of Grand Master of Flowers.  Any individual monk attaining this rank will have full leadership privileges and senior officer status within the guild.
 

Guild Initiation

All new members will undergo an initiation process.  In this "interview" they must agree to follow the Eightfold Path.  All perspective members will be asked these questions prior to being invited into the guild as an Initiate:

    1) Do you know the basic values of The Eightfold Path?

    2) Do you promise to follow these values with all of your spirit?

    3) Will you accept full responsibility and punishment for violation of these Ways?

    4) Above all, do you pledge to honor all life and always aide others in need - even at risk of death?

The first 12 new members will begin with the rank of Immaculate and attain the rank of Master after having adventured with one of the guild founders.  They will only be expected to undergo the first part of the initiation and may disregard the 2 steps necessary to progress in rank to Master.  This is a benefit of being a founding member of The Eightfold Path and only applies to these individuals.  They must attain all additional ranks through normal means but may always refer to themselves as members of the Founding Circle.
 

Guild Dues

All members are expected to donate a portion of their income, either monetary or looted items, to the guild.  The frequency and amount of the donations will be up to the individual – anything will be graciously accepted and no one will ever be personally solicited for donations. All dues collected will be used for the purpose of organizing guild events, stipends for newby members and to promote the general good.  We recommend, but not require, that guild members donate their used items to the guild armory when upgrading instead of selling them - this will give us the ability to help out the newer members to better protect and defend themselves.  Donations in any form will not go to personally benefit the guild leaders in any way.  The guild leaders will be expected to donate a larger portion and have established a fund of 100pp and a few misc. items to open the guild treasury.
 

Guild Meetings

The Eightfold Path will hold open guild meetings once a month to be announced to the membership.  At these meetings we will discuss general operations, future plans, any concerns related to the guild and all issues observed within Norrath which are in need of our attention.
 

Guild Events

The guild leaders will organize guild wide events for the purpose of the betterment of the land or Norrath and the promotion of the Way of the Eightfold Path.  Members are invited to participate in the creation of these events by suggesting worthy undertakings.  We will notify the members of upcoming events via e-mail and message board postings.
 

Guild Ranking Determination

Guild members will have the opportunity to follow the Way of the Eightfold Path by progressing in rank within the guild.  These ranks may be obtained by questing, performance of heroic deeds, approval of officers/members, or the like.  At the rank of master one has proven their dedication to the guild and will receive an e-mail address for guild members only (name@eightfold.org).  The evolution of the member will be determined as follows:

Immaculate – Must be an Initiate for a minimum of 1 week; must choose one of the eight Paths that best symbolizes one's outlook within Norrath; must petition an officer with a request for a change in rank and explanation of why you chose the Path that you did.

Master – Must be an Immaculate for a minimum for 2 weeks; must prove your intentions by seeking out and aiding a non-guild member of any race/class who is in need.  This can consist of preventing (or trying to prevent) the certain death of a person fighting for their life while putting one's own life at risk.  The task may be considered successful even if you end up dead.  The guild leaders will accept proof of completion of this task solely on the honorable word of the Immaculate and will then promote you to Master.

Master of the Dragons – Must prove a mastery of fighting both solo and in a group.  This will be determined with input from the members of a party that you travel with, input from other guild members, or journeying with a guild leader.  A member must also be at 'Master' skill level in at least 2 offensive/defensive skills.  When it is time, one must petition a guildmaster with their readiness to advance and references.  This is not a rank per-say but a symbol of achievement - you may attain this title as soon as your skills merit, regardless of your current rank.

Master of the Lotus – Must demonstrate mastery of the craft arts by hand making a set of Cured Silk armor (at least Tunic, arms, and legs) to be donated to a worthy new Initiate.  The set will be given to the guild treasurer for keeping in the guild armory.  Our non-monk members may choose to master the skills of an alternate craft(s) - these skills can include higher level enchanted jewelry making, advanced brewing and drinking skill (cheers!), or a like craft that demonstrates mastery of an art.  Talk to a guild leader with your ideas.  This is not a rank per-say but a symbol of achievement - you may attain this title as soon as your skills merit advancement, regardless of your current rank.

Master of the Winds – These 4 quests will be personally issued one at a time when the member has proven ready for the spiritual and physical challenge.  All quests will increase in difficulty as you progress through them.  Each quest will take place in the appropriate hemisphere of Norrath.  You must be a Master of the Dragons before commencing with these four tests.

Master of the Seasons – These ranks (Autumn, Summer, and Spring) are given to members as they further prove themselves worthy to continue on the Eightfold Path.  A guild leader will contact you with promotional information when the time is right.  Be patient, enlightenment takes time.  You must be a Master of the Lotus before commencing with these four tests.  You will know when you are ready...

Grand Master of Flowers – The final test of a member on the Eightfold Path is one of selflessness and virtue.  Its origin and secret is shared by only the guildmasters and the few who aspire to take its challenge.
 

Violation of Guild Values

Anyone violating the Way of the Eightfold Path will undergo a tribunal of their peers consisting of at least 1 officer and 2 members to determine the fate of the individual.  The punishment, if necessary, can range from a monetary donation (in an amount determined by the tribunal), item donation (any item owned by the offender as determined by the tribunal), fulfilling a special quest or dismissal from the guild.  All new members must agree to abide by the decision of the tribunal when accepting new membership.  Failure to comply will result in immediate banishment from the guild with no chance of return.

 

The Way of the Eightfold Path will not be easy but if followed it will set the way to enlightenment...  Above all, enjoy the game!  We want you to have a good group of friends to provide advice, support and great adventures.

Safe Journeys to you all!
