We offer email to all guild members who attain the rank of Master and above.  Upon promotion to Master you will be notified of your address and password.

There are three ways that you can use your email account at eightfold.org:

- Have all email forwarded to your regular email box (this is the pre-configured default)
- Use the web interface to send and receive all of your email from any web browser
- Setup Outlook Express or the like to send/receive your email

You can select between these options by clicking the "Check Mail" button below.  This is your online web interface that allows you to check mail and manage your own box.  You can also change your password, add a last name and enable an auto-response message there.

To use the web interface (or a standard email program) to send and receive mail be sure that you disable forwarding to your RL email address in your account.  This is becuase your eightfold.org account is currently set to automatically forward all new mail out of the box to your RL address.  You can do this by simply clicking the "Check Mail" button below (or going to mail.eightfold.org), entering your username and password, clicking on "Rules" at the top of the screen, unchecking the "Redirect All Mail to:" and clicking "Update".  You can then go directly to mail.eightfold.org or use a standard email program to check mail in the future.

Additionally, you can use a standard mail program such as Outlook Express to handle all of your email.  The necessary settings are as follows:

IMAP or POP3 server = eightfold.org
SMTP server = eightfold.org
Username = Your first name in Norrath
Password = Your assigned password or whatever you have changed it to.

If you wish to use the above method, we recommend connecting to your account using an IMAP compatible email client such as Microsoft Outlook Express (instead of POP3).
