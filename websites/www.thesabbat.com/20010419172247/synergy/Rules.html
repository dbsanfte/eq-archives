INTRODUCTION

A new planes group is starting. We will give you the basics here and post the rules we will follow after. This group is being formed to challenge the new Kunark Boss mobs as well as the old Deity/Dragon favorites. The charter members will be by invitation and we never intend to have an inordinately large group, we will deal with new members under the rules section. No one is being told to quit their current planes groups, if you are happy where you are by all means stay; if you want something new and different we will by happy to have you. Since we can't think of a better system for loot than the hour-based system we will use that. All charter members will be given 50 hours to start; all new members will start at 0. The only reason charter members will start with hours is so we won't have to deal with negative hour looting when we begin.


RULES

1.Hours will be submitted within 48 hours of end of event. Failure to report hours in this time will result in 0 hours for you.

2.Loot you receive is to be reported within 48 hours of end of event. Failure to report will result for double hours charged for any loot.

3.Regardless of what character you play you will receive hours for yourself. So for example, if you play Joe's cleric because we need one you will receive hours not Joe.

4.Please leave personal and guild wars outside the event. If another person/guild decides to try and disrupt the event by coming after you we will ALL deal with them.

5.Primary and Secondary characters will be decided by you when you join. If you wish to switch primaries there will be a 40 hour penalty (that's a full week's work at a regular job, choose wisely). If you switch primaries your characters become locked for 30 days, no switching back and forth. Any change of primary/secondary characters must be made prior to event start. All primary characters will loot ahead of secondary (even if the primary is not there). If a secondary gets a chance to loot you will be charged half normal hours for the item.

6.Events outside SYNERGY. Feel free to attend any event, this includes guild events. Any loot you receive is yours at no cost to your hours with the exception of Kunark Boss mobs and Deity/Dragon loot. This means that planar armor is free but loot off the god will cost you normal hours. This is to try and keep hours balanced within SYNERGY so you cannot gain any advantage over others. It also promotes doing large events as a group.

7.New members. We will use the sponsorship approach. Once you have attained 200+ total hours you are in a position to sponsor someone. You can only sponsor one person at a time and each sponsored person will be reviewed at 50 hours. At this point they will be either approved, denied or remain sponsored to be reviewed again at a later date. New members will be added at the discretion of majority vote by council until people have sufficient hours to start sponsoring.

8.Council. Those people in charge of planning events and enforcing rules. For the time being we will limit this number to 3 people.

9.Ebay. If your character or items you received through SYNERGY are sold you will be removed.

10.Have fun. We are here to have fun, please act accordingly. Becoming abusive and insulting quickly detracts from the fun and makes events unenjoyable. Don't be jealous or greedy, show some enthusiasm and respect and its guaranteed we will all enjoy ourselves.

11.Have fun. Please re-read rule 10 to make sure you fully understand it.
