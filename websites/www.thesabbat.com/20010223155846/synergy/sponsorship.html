Sponsorship Guidelines

Current members can sponsor new members once they reach 200 total hours.

Warrior, Cleric, Enchanter and Wizard classes will be limited to 20 members per class.  Minimum level to sponsor someone  from these classes will be 52.


Bard, Monk, Rogue, Shaman and Necro classes will be limited to 15 members per class.  Minimum level to sponsor someone from these classes will be 54.


Druid, Mage, Ranger, Paladin and Shadowknight classes will be limited to 10 members per class.  Minimum level to sponsor someone from these classes will be 56.
