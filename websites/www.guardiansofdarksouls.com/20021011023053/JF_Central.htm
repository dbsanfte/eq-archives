JetForm - Central™

FORMS OUTPUT THE WAY YOU WANT IT


Have you ever thought about what your customers see when you send them important business documents such as invoices, purchase orders or shipping documents? These documents are touchpoints to your customers — it’s how they view your company. Do these form-based documents portray a high-quality, professional image of your business?

With JetForm Central you can “forms-enable” your ERP, financial, health care or other line-of-business applications and generate professional-quality, laser printed documents. With JetForm Central, you won’t be locked into outdated print technology or software designed for old impact printers and preprinted forms.

POWERFUL, FLEXIBLE OUTPUT
JetForm Central provides the high quality and ease of use you demand in a print/output solution. Regardless of the application software that produces your data, you can print electronic forms, checks and shipping documents quickly and easily using JetForm Central. With JetForm Central’s Transformation Agent, you can convert the current data output from your application into JetForm field-nominated data — a format that allows for sophisticated output processing capabilities. No messy spool file data mapping is required and the forms are tightly integrated with your line-of-business application. Multiple forms can be printed from a single field-nominated data stream, such as invoices in multiple languages with different terms and conditions or invoices with credit and debit memos. You can dynamically embed different logos for your subsidiaries, without changing the form or data stream. You can also select the delivery method to output and transmit forms in a way that best meets your needs — by printer or fax.

DYNAMIC FORMS
 Perform arithmetic calculations on your form data — create subtotals, perform currency conversions or add data from an external source.

JetForm Central supports powerful dynamic forms capabilities which allow portions of a form to appear or disappear depending on whether or not they contain data. For instance, an invoice can be printed with 10 lines or 50 lines, depending on how many data items there are, with no unused white space. Implement market-focused, personalized, messages on your customer documents with dynamic forms capabilities that allow a customer message area to appear or disappear, based on whether or not there is a message to print.

DELIVER DOCUMENTS WORLDWIDE
 To support your businesses operating in multiple countries, JetForm Central can print documents in any of the following languages: French, German, Swedish, Spanish, Italian, Finnish, Norwegian, Danish, Brazilian Portuguese, Czech, Polish, Hungarian, Russian, simplified Chinese, traditional Chinese, Korean and Japanese.

JetForm Central is the ideal way to leverage the data in your existing business applications and produce high-quality professional output.

EASY GRAPHICAL FORMS DESIGN
 Forms are created using JetForm Design™, a comprehensive WYSIWYG graphical design and development tool. With it you can create electronic replicas of paper forms. There’s no programming required to use it — just point and click. Field “objects” are placed on the form to identify the position and location of data that will come from your line-of-business application. Easily customize forms with company logos, complex graphics and bar codes, or create multi-page or multi-part forms.

No more waiting for programmers to modify source code in order to make even a minor change to your forms. You decide where fields, logos, signatures and bar codes print, making changes in minutes, not hours or days.

UNATTENDED PRINTER MANAGEMENT
 JetForm Central supports the unattended downloading of forms to the memory of the targeted printer and controls flash memory and other storage media on today’s sophisticated printers. JetForm Central maintains a printer control file that monitors which forms are resident in each printer.

LEVERAGE EXISTING HARDWARE
 JetForm Central eliminates the cost of adding hardware by using your existing printers or fax servers. JetForm Central supports almost any PCL or Postscript laser printer. In addition, thermal transfer printers and high-speed production printers are supported, such as those supplied by Zebra or Xerox Corporation. With JetForm Central’s powerful print drivers and sophisticated forms architecture, you can print your forms at, or near, the rated speed of the printer.

JetForm Central supports CommercePath, OmTool Fax Sr., Teubner & Associates FaxGate, V-Systems VSI-FAX, TopCall, RightFax, Faxination and Merkur from SCH Technologies.
