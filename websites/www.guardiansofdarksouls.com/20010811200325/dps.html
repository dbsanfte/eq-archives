The first question most people will ask is, 'What are Dragon Points?' The simple answer is that they are our fair system of rewarding loot to our guild members. We do not believe in merit loot, nor do we believe in randomly selecting someone to win a prized item. Instead, for each raid you participate on as a member of GoDS, you gain a set amount of Dragon Points. These points are then later used to bid on items the guild gains. The complete system and a FAQ follows:

How Dragon Points are earned:

As a member of GoDS, you will earn Dragon Points for every raid you participate on that is hosted by or sponsored by GoDS. The Guild Leader and the Guild Masters are the only people who can award Dragon Points. While the total award for any given raid can be any number, the minimum is always announced before the start of the raid. In order to be awarded Dragon Points, you must participate for the entire length of the raid. Depending on whether or not the raid has a foreseeable end, you may be awarded less points if you can not stay to the raid's completion. Any raid that has a set goal (such as to kill a dragon) is considered to have a foreseeable end. In most cases, you will not be awarded anything if you leave before the end. Raids which do not have a foreseeable end (such as camping a plane for armor or a re-spawning mob such as The Efreeti) will typically carry a small DP award which will be granted after the initial break. For extended participation the Guild Leader may increase awards.

How Dragon Points are spent:

On raids in which only members of GoDS are allowed to participate, items will be awarded only by purchase with Dragon Points. Any item which isn't NO DROP will be bid on using Dragon Points. Should no one wish to buy the item, it will be put into the Guild Treasury to be sold for supplies or given to a guild member at a later time for a set amount of Dragon Points. The minimum bid for any item on a given raid is the total amount of DPs awarded for that raid (ex. if you earn 10 DPs for a raid, the minimum bid for an item on that raid is 10). The person who spends the highest amount of DPs wins the item and that amount of DP is then subtracted from their current total. Should an item be found which is NO DROP and more than one person wants it, DPs will be spent with no minimum bid. Should only one person be able to use such an item, it will be awarded free of charge. Items are to be distributed following the policy Need before Greed.

FAQ
 (DPs=Dragon Points, CDPs=Current Dragon Points, LLT=Life Time Total)

When are Dragon Points Not used?
 When GoDS hosts or sponsors a raid and invited non-guild members, Dragon Points can not be spent. On such raids, Loot Rules will be agreed on before the raid begins and all GoDS members are expected to obey them. Dragon Points will still be awarded on such raids.

What if no one wants an item?
 If no one wants an item it will go into the guild Treasury for future use or for sale/trade to pay for Plane Keys, Cleric Gems, Coffins, Resist Gear, and other needed items. Items will be held for one week before it will be sold or traded. Before an item is sold, it will be offered to the guild to be bid on with Dragon Points. The minimum bid still remains as the total amount of DPs awarded on that raid.

Can I give my dragon points to another person?
 No. In an effort to maintain fair play and keep the system as honest as possible, Dragon Points are non-transferable. Dragons Points are given to people for their participation in GoDS events. They are a reward for helping your guild. A person who has more points than another deserves the item.

Can I bid on an item and give it to someone else?
 Once an item is rewarded to you, it is yours. However, it is bad form to bid against a guild member only to give the item to someone else. Repeated incidents of these practices will more than likely result in your removal from the guild. As stated in the guild charter, we value Need Before Greed.

What if I'm the only one who wants an item and I don't have the minimum DPs?
 Should a situation arise where someone is the only person who wants an item but they do not have the minimum amount of DPs, the item will be held for one week at which time it will be offered to the whole guild for the minimum DP value. If at that time, a person still wants it but they can't afford the minimum DPs, it will be held for another week. If after two weeks the minimum DPs are still not met, the item may be awarded to you and your DPs will go into negatives AS LONG AS your life time DP awards exceed twice the items minimum DP value. (ex. If an item is worth 10 DPs and you only have 6 DPs you will be allowed to go to -4 DPs as long as your Life Time Total is equal to or exceeds 20 DPs)

Why can't I go into negatives if my Life Time Total isn't double the cost?
 We don't allow negative numbers to prevent people from joining our guild just for one event, taking a really nice item, and then leaving the guild. Our guild rewards members for their participation. The more you participate, the higher your LTT will be. Even if an item is sold because no one can afford it, it will benefit the guild as a whole better. As stated previously the proceeds go towards purchasing needed items and reagents for the guild.

How do I know how many DPs I have?
 CDPs and LTTs are kept track of by the Guild Leader. They will be posted on the members section of this web page. CDPs and LTTs will be updated after every gain or loss.
