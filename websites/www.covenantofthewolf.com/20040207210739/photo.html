PACK CHARTER

"A wolf is a beautiful, fierce, wild creature that lives in a pack and also on it's own. Within the wolf pack it's members care for, defend, and hunt together, and that is the purpose of our guild. We level together, protect each other, look after each other's needs, and always have a friend to group with. Some of our members may be lone wolves, but we all share a pack spirit throughout the guild. We are a neutral guild. We don't murder innocents. But if someone threatens a guild mate or ourselves we defend and hunt the offender as necessary. This is our promise, to uphold the ideals of the wolf." - Raveena

Theme

The theme of our guild is the wolf pack. We try and run our guild like a pack of wolves. Pack elders and an "Alpha Wolf" to make important guild decisions. But like a wolf pack all members are important in seeing that guild goals are met.

Purpose

The purpose of this guild is so that friends online can find each other and have fun. We define fun as roleplaying, grouping together, raiding together and joking in guild chat. We are a laid back guild.

We Are

  • Friendly. Nice, Courteous, polite, caring, generous, patient. Avoid profanity, trash talk and other rudeness. Respectful of each other and other players. Attentive to each wolf's needs and the pack as a whole. Treat each other as friends treat each other. Like wolves we hunt together, defend each other, group together and help each other. A few wolves in this pack prefer to be lone wolves, but they remember where home is. They come to the aid of the pack when called upon, and the pack comes to them. We try to only invite nice people. We try to kick out any mistakenly invited mean people. If you enjoy being friendly, and getting to know nice people, you will enjoy being in this guild.
  • Fun loving. We "woof" to each other in zones as a greeting. We tease each other in guildchat. We love to have fun as a guild. We have fun exploring new areas. While not a pure raiding guild, we spend a lot of our time raiding, and trying to run each raid more smoothly than the last. Working together and having fun are the goals. Many of us have families, and other real life time constraints that take precedence over playing. We understand that. Even if it means less raids, members are never "required" to attend our raids. Encouraged: Yes. Required: No.
  • Fierce. We often travel in "wolfpack" groups of six, for mutual defense and betterment. We generally do not go around looking for fights. We like peace, and typically try to get along with everyone. However, in the world of Rallos Zek, fights have a way of coming to us often enough. We like to mix it up with enemies for fun, excitement, and variety.
We Are Not
  • Powergamers. We do not obsess with being "uber". Sure, we like to get experience points and items like every other EQ player. Some of us enjoy those things more than others, but neither is the primary focus of the guild. If your main source of fun is getting experience, we may not focus on that as much as you would like. If you feel it is a guild's job to focus on getting you all the best gear, this guild may not be for you.
  • Warlike. If you like to fight all the time, you may find this guild too peaceful.
  • Troublemakers. You may find we encourage you to "cool it" too often.
    • We respect other playing styles, and wish everyone to have fun in the way they see fit. However, not all styles fit perfectly with our guild, and people who like these styles may wish to seek their fun in other guilds that focus more in that direction.

    Expectations of All Pack Members

    Suggest to the officers what you find the most fun. Keep an eye on the private message board for upcoming guild events, or feel free to run your own. Show up to guild events whenever you can, and encourage your packmates to do the same. Uphold the ideals of the pack in yourself, and in those pack members around you. Come to the aid of the pack when called upon.

    Additional Expectations of Pack Elders (Officers)

    Keep an eye on the pack, make note of who needs what, and what would be fun to do as a pack. Organize events a little bit more often than other members. Know, uphold, and encourage proper pack behavior. Invite in good fits. Kick out bad fits. Provide and maintain message boards, calendars, and other things needed to maintain a healthy and fun pack. Make wise decisions in areas that effect the whole guild.

    Additional Expectations of Alpha Wolf (Leader)

    The Alpha is expected to be the chief source of vision and direction for the guild, and to set guild goals.

 

PACK LAW

This wolfpack is made up of wolves. Wolves are unpredictable. They sometimes behave however they feel like it at the moment. This book of laws is intended to let new recruits know what is expected of them, and outsiders learning about us to better understand us. However, it is not binding, especially on the pack elders, as it is their sound judgment that the pack relies on more than any written law.

Organization
  • The guild has three tiers, members, pack elders (officers), and the alpha wolf (leader).
  • The pack elders have a private message board they use for discussing the running of the guild. They hold votes there on any important issues that come up.
  • The Alpha Wolf is the same as any other officer, except they carry the "guild leader" tag, and hold the exclusive authority to declare war. The Alpha does not need elder approval to declare war.
  • There is a public message board for the members to voice their concerns, ideas, and just wolf around. Elders will generally post announcements there.
  • Especially newsworthy accomplishments of our guild or any of "our" raid organizations are sometimes posted on the news page.
General rules
  • The pack comes first above all things.
  • Get to know your guildmates. Group with them. If you do not like what they are doing at the time, encourage them to come with you somewhere more to your liking. If you do not like them, maybe you or they do not belong in the guild. If you are sure it is they, bring it up with an officer.
  • This guild does not require membership in any outside organization.
  • This guild does not prohibit membership in any outside organization, unless that organization requires our member to violate any of our other laws.
Foreign relations
  • We participate in various raiding organizations, raiding alliances, and have friendly relations with other guilds.
  • When the Alpha Wolf decides using their powers over guild warfare that a military alliance is required, they will observe the following rules...
    • Such alliance must be an equal alliance. The requirements on our guild must be identical to the requirements of all ally guilds in the alliance.
    • The alliance agreement must be posted publically.
    • The only concrete requirement can be automatic declaration of war by our guild on any guild or organization that has performed an attack against any member of the alliance. Any other requirement must only require "good faith" compliance by guild members.
    • Any alliance agreement must automatically be voided if the ally gets into a conflict with any other guild we have an alliance agreement with.
    • The Alpha Wolf holds exclusive authority to enter into or cancel any military alliance.
Raid rules
  • If you raid with an outside organization, we encourage you to be very familiar with that organization's customs and rules. Being a bad raid group member reflects badly on you and your guild.
  • Guild only raids have the following expectations:
    • Avoid PVP or otherwise disruptive behavior during raids.
    • Listen and do what the raid leader asks you to do.
    • Be on time.
    • Tell the leader when you arrive, so they can assign you to a group.
    • Tell the leader if you need to leave before the raid has ended.
    • Complaints about how the leader is running things? That's what message boards are for to use afterwards.
Recruiting Policy
  • Required minimum level to join: 58.
    • This guild is predominantly level 56 and higher.
    • We do not have much to offer those lower as far as grouping or PvP support.
    • Some of us may on occasion enjoy the memories of a lower level dungeon long enough to help you get some item, but do not count on it.
    • If you see a large number of guild "lowbies" on, you probably have stumbled on a semi-organized "twink night" event.
  • If you like the guild, tell your friends. Encourage your friends to join.
    • Officers can invite anyone they feel like inviting whenever they feel like it.
      • They tend to tell the recruit a few things about the guild, to make sure the recruit knows what they are getting into.
      • They may ask the recruit a few questions, to ensure the officer knows what they are getting into.
      • They sometimes decide to discuss among themselves before inviting. There is usually a reason for this, and they will usually tell you what the reason is, but there are no guarantees.
    • We like to see new recruits post an introduction to themselves on our public message board, and keep up on posts, as announcements and such are posted there. But visiting the message boards is not required to join the guild.
    • New members can consider themselves "on probation" for a while after being invited. They can consider this a time to get to know the guild. Typically removed if they cause any problems so soon after being invited.
  • If you do not like the guild, tell the officers. If you are simply a whiner, they will probably remove you on the spot. If you have constructive ideas on how to improve the guild, they may open a discussion among the officers. Who knows, they may even nominate you to join their ranks.
  • Officers may remove anyone they feel should be removed whenever they feel like it. The member of course is welcome to plead their case on the message board, which will result in officer discussion, and possible re-invite.
Officership
  • Nominations require one officer to nominate and another officer to second.
  • Promotion and demotions require a unanimous officer vote.
  • Removing an officer: Except in case of emergencies, cannot occur without a discussion thread to remove them. Vote can occur anytime after any of these conditions are met...
    • That officer replies with their side of the story
    • One week has elapsed since the thread was posted.
Alpha Wolf
  • The leader is voted in by majority vote of elders.
  • The only way to remove a leader against their will is by voting in a new leader to replace them.
  • There is also an "alternate" leader voted on seperately. The alternate becomes the leader if the current leader steps down voluntarily. The identity of the alternate is secret until that time, and the alternate has no powers other than a normal officer.
Member Removal
  • We respectfully ask that you post a message on the public board if you leave the guild voluntarily.
  • Officers are authorized to at any time remove without warning anyone who they believe is a detriment to the guild.
    • Officers are typically not appointed that are known to make rash, hasty, or selfish decisions.
    • If a removed member feels the officer has made a mistake or acted in a malicious manner, they are encouraged to explain their take of the situation on the member's board. The officers will discuss among themselves the situation and come to a determination. Typically the removal will stand or the member will be offered a re-invite.
    • Officers should not take this authority lightly, because depending on their actions and intentions, the other officers may decide to take action against the officer.
Officer Removal
  • Officers that wish to become normal members are encouraged to seek an officer to be re-invited as a normal member. If you wish to remove from the guild entirely, we ask that you post a message on the public board just to let us know.
  • Blatant violation of the pack law will result in removal of the officer.
  • Mistakes deemed minor might result in an officer being demoted to normal member status.
PvP
  • We are a neutral guild. We do not mess with those smart enough not to mess with us.
  • Fair game...
    • (Obligated) Members of guilds or organizations we are at war with.
    • (Encouraged) Those who have earned a spot on our KOS list due to messing with us in the past and have yet to make amends or be forgotten.
    • (Overlooked) Members of guilds who encourage random player killing, and "well known" solo random player killers. They will probably enjoy someone fighting back for a change, and fun is to be had by all. We are not anti-pk, but simply recognize these as folks who are less likely to be offended by ambushes.
  • We do not random PK. If the officers are convinced you are guilty of attacking anyone that is not on the fair game list (or assisting others who are), without them attacking you first, you will be removed from the guild and added to our KOS list.
  • Defend first, ask questions later.
    • When guild members are attacked, other guild members are expected to assist if...
      • They can get there quickly.
      • They can get there without too much trouble (e.g. Would have to leave a raid).
        • If coming to the assistance of guildmates being attacked in your mind will ALWAYS be too much trouble, then we suggest looking into...
          • Another guild.
          • Another more "trouble free" server.
    • The idea is to make sure those who mess with us are killed or forced to leave the area bleeding.
      • Ideally this occurs right after the offense.
      • Putting them on the KOS list until game mechanics and schedules are more cooperative sometimes has to suffice.
  • Anyone attacked without provocation may report the incident to the guild. Try to have a screenshot of the offense, just so there can be no mistakes about name, etc. If the accused is a member of a guild that frowns upon such actions, the screenshot becomes that much more important to convince them their member is in the wrong. If the officers agree there is no question the attack happened, the offender is put on the KOS list.
  • KOS list.
    • Characters placed on the KOS list remain there until they are killed at least once, or until a long period of time elapses.
    • We periodically organize hunting packs to chase these down.
    • If somone on our KOS list joins a friendly or indifferent guild, we continue to KOS the individual. If the new guild approaches us, we will explain why the individual is KOS to us. If the new guild asks us to not KOS their new recruit anymore, we will consider removing their new member from our KOS list. If we decide not to do so, we will continue to KOS them, with no regard for what the new guild thinks about it, and let the chips fall where they may.
  • Third party guild wars. If there are other guilds or sects at war, as a neutral guild, we stay out of it. It is recommended not to group with people known to be involved in wars. If one of your group members is attacked by a guild they are at war with, you stay out of it. No heals, no buffs, no evacs. Aiding either side in a war can cause tensions between our guild and the warring guilds, and may drag us into the war. Intentionally aiding either side constitutes putting your group member or anger about group disruption ahead of the pack, and will result in your guild removal underscoring our apology to the offended parties.
