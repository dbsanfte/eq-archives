More Cleric Epic Work!

We successfully killed the fire elemental, Ixiblatt Fer, twice tonight. The first time for Ephanie and the second time for Riclore. 21 people turned out tonight from the following guilds: Order of the Righteous Blade (ORB), Celestial Empire, Temerity, Arcane Dawn, Covenant of the Wolf, No Tears Initiates, and one guest from Norrath Knights. Our Main Assist was Bogim (AD), secondary assist Gnad(NK), and 3rd assist Darkgolem(AD). The puller was Graywolf (CotW) and the cleric epic advisor was Bisax (CE).

Everyone did a fabulous job. The second time we killed the elemental we did so with no loss of life. As Cutty said, "It was perfect."

Besides the cleric epic piece the elemental dropped the following items, both of them no drop:

Crown of Fire - won by Zantara (CE)
nice monk Mantle - given to Sahsha (CE)

Again thanks everyone who came. It was a good time.

If any more clerics of TRG need this epic mob killed please post a message on the TRG board.
