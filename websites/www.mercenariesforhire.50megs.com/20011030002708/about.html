CHARTER
The Mercenaries for Hire (MFH) are devoted to devising and implementing strategic approaches to game warfare (combat) both to increase hunting efficiency and to aide in PVP battles.
The guild charter is as follows:

Mercenaries Creed
I swear to uphold the Creed by being an example of the following:

1) To always complete my contract once accepted, regardless of cost in time or gold, to my employers exact specifications.

2) To always get paid.

3) To never leave a companion in danger, to show no cowardice in battle or business dealings.

4) To honor other Swords for Hire, in friendship, competition, or battle with utter professionally. To deal honorably and fight dirty.

5) To operate by the MFH Handbook.

The MFH is dedicated to making Ever Quest an *enjoyable* Role Playing experience for those that desire such things, and break new ground in Everquest. RP is heavily encouraged, and use of the /emote command is appreciated. Remember, you are a Merc, so act like one... chew iron and spit out nails. May your hunt be profitable.


~Kardde Gnollsbane

GUILD RULES
Rule Number 1: All rules set by Verant and 989 Studios are to be respected by the members of Mercenaries For Hire. Breaking them will result in discharge if it is brought to our attention. However, since you may be getting banned from the game, this is a moot point. It must be stated that we will support the rules of Variant and 989 Studios.
Rule Number 2: Game Fun Must Be Maintained. Our guild is based on role playing being a mercenary, we must remember that not all people playing Everquest are as avid about RP as we are. If any one that tells you out of character (OOC) that something you are doing is offending them, i.e. Your Role Playing a Necromancer hating Paladin and they are playing a Necromancer but not into the role playing aspect of the game, you must desist immediately. As long as they are in the spirit of the RP, and you let them know OOC that it is RP before you get into a heated disagreement, then game fun is maintained.

Rule Number 3: Cursing and use of vulgarity is not banned by MFH in guild chat due to the spirit of our RP. In shouts, auctions, and out of character chat channels, it will not be tolerated. Some are offended by the use of such language. We maintain a zero tolerance for those that insult or offend others by the use of such language.

Rule Number 4: The Mercenaries Creed must be observed. MFH members do not beg for anything, We always offer compensation. If a deal is made, you MUST complete it unless you are released from it by word of mouth. For out of guild dealings, the MFH expects its members to deal honorably. For guild dealings, anything from trades to lending of money, you are advised to have a witness present so that any disputes that come up later may be smoothed out by a third party. It is recommended that you use the /log command before and after each time you are negotiating or transacting a business deal. This provides a record of the transaction to reference if a problem arises later, it also provides us with a history of contracts and prices that can be posted on the internet for other MFH to use as a guideline.

Rule Number 5: Role Play is enforced in this guild. We are a Role Play guild offering our members a Role Play environment to explore and enjoy. Out of character communication should always be set apart by the indicator OOC, and should be kept to a minimum. /guild chat channel is for in character discussion.

Rule Number 6: Real life and fun take priority. Guild Events and Training Maneuvers are open for all to attend and participation is completely voluntary. You may at any time during a guild event leave the event, if you have to. Please let the commanding officer know. **No MFH member may be promoted, participate in guild events, be issued guild equipment, or enjoy any of the privileges of their rank until they are listed on the MFH internet site with their valid email address, character info, and they have edited their bio on EQ.**

Rule Number 7: All Ranks in the MFH should be respected. Each rank is important and carries with it certain privileges, duties, and authorities. Officers are allowed to give orders to lower ranking members because the lower ranking member has AGREED to take orders from that officer for a set time (guild event, training maneuver, until the hunting group disbands, etc.).

DUE PROCESS
This is very important to ANY guild. I want everyone to know their rights when dealing with problems in or outside of the guild. When left to question, who should deal with a problem can be the cause of more problems than the original one. Due process must be observed.
What can you do if you have a disagreement, or feel someone has done you wrong? All Ranks have to follow this Process. The offending and defending members need to agree on their means of settling the matter.

1) Superior Officer
If it is a small thing, perhaps an RP argument (small one), or a simple misunderstanding. You may take it to an officer currently on-line who outranks both parties. If no officers of higher rank are present then one of equal rank to the highest of the arguing parties will arbitrate. If this is decided upon mutually, then all parties must abide by the assessment and orders. The officer would be selected by being the first officer that meets the above stipulations in a /who all Mercenaries for Hire.

2)MFH Legal Department Forum
Any guild member is welcome to post an overview of a dispute that arose within the guild or outside of it with the guild. Others will review the matter and comment usually making suggestions. It is expected, in the event that any officer is called upon to settle a dispute or otherwise make judgment call, that they post an account of the matter here.

3)Standard of Refusal
You may request from your commanding officer written orders at any time (he will repeat his orders after you have activated /log). present such written orders to any Lieutenant and have them reviewed. If orders were found to be unreasonable, the commanding officer responsible for them WILL BE DISCIPLINED. If you refuse to follow the orders, you are not necessarily in the wrong if the orders ARE unreasonable. The Commanding Officer and/or you may make a report of the situation. If you do, the orders will be reviewed, if they were unreasonable, the officer giving them will be disciplined, if not, the Lieutenants will decide what shall happen next.

4)Tribunal
Guild Tribunal is used to arbitrate disputes and even possibly fights within the guild between it s members. Anyone at ANY time may call for one. The first Captain going down the list of names in a /who all Mercenaries will be the Arbiter and organize it, if none are present, a Lieutenant will, if no Lieutenant are present, then the first Sergeant, if no Sergeants are present, than the first Trooper on the list will organize it. New Recruits may neither organize nor sit on Tribunals, but they may request them. If not enough MFH members to make up the five person Tribunal council are present the accusing party is asked respectfully to email MercenariesForHire@bigfoot.com detailing the problem so that a Guild Tribunal may be organized at a convenient time.
The person organizing the Guild Tribunal is referred to as the Arbiter. A Guild Tribunal consists of five hopefully objective Guild Members who were uninvolved with the disagreement that are chosen by the Arbiter. All parties involved are given uninterrupted time to relay their side of the issue at hand to the Guild Tribunal with the Arbiter overseeing and assuring an orderly process. After all involved parties have said their piece, the five sitting on the Guild Tribunal, who have been listening quietly to the proceedings, will confer amongst themselves and decide the matter. All involved parties must agree to abide by the Guild Tribunal decision if they request the matter to be solved by a Guild Tribunal. Tribunals (if held in game) must be logged and the transcript emailed to MercenariesForHire@bigfoot.com
Formal Tribunal is brought together when someone outside the guild has accused one of our guild mates of some crime or wrong doing. The Arbiter for a Formal Tribunal is selected as follows: The first Captain going down the list of names in a /who all Mercenaries will organize it, if none are present, a Lieutenant will, if no Lieutenants are present, then the first Sergeant will arbitrate, if no Officers Sergeant rank or higher are present, or not enough MFH members to make up the five person Tribunal council are present the accusing party is asked respectfully to email MercenariesForHire@bigfoot.com detailing the problem so that a Formal Tribunal may be organized at a convenient time. They may choose the five people to sit as Formal Tribunal from within the ranks of MFH. Those who sit as the Formal Tribunal must be at most the rank of Trooper. The offended party is given a chance to relay his side of the story first. He may be invited into the guild for the purpose of speaking his side of the story, or if that is not possible due to his involvement with another guild, the whole group; the offended, the Arbiter, the five Tribunal members and the accused guild mate may go to the EQ chat room and the Arbiter will create a chat room called Tribunal which will be private and the password is the name of the arbiter. The offended will not suffer ANY repercussions from the guild but must agree to abide by the guild decision in order for his accusation to be given weight. The Tribunal will listen to both sides of the story, starting with the offended, and then ending with the accused. During each accounting, the other side MUST remain silent. Each is given full freedom to explain their side of the story. The five Tribunal members are then to come to a decision. Discharge is NOT a sentence that can be acted upon by the authority of a Tribunal. But they may suggest that to the Lieutenants as a possible sentencing. Any equipment or money that were a part of the disagreement will be handed over to a neutral party (generally the Arbiter) before the Tribunal is brought to order. It is to be given to the party whom it was decided it belonged to AFTER the Tribunal has arrived at a decision. Tribunals (if held in game) must be logged and the transcript emailed to MercenariesForHire@bigfoot.com.

5)Board of Inquiry
The Board of Inquiry is one of the most respected and feared forms of justice in the guild. This is never to be called for small crimes or accusations. This is for major crimes against the guild. The Board of Inquiry consists of ALL Lieutenants. Most of the inquiry and process may take place on-line, but all decisions, evidence, and statements must be posted on the MFH internet site. This is a serious process. The Lieutenants are completely free, once all sides have been presented and considered, and all evidence examined, to decide where guilt lies and make ANY sentence they see fit, up to and including dishonorable discharge. The Board of Inquiry has a process that must be followed. If any steps in the process are skipped or ignored, the process must begin over, it does not let the offender off the hook.

A Any member may REQUEST that a BOI convene. The request must go through their C.O. if the dispute is about their C.O. they may take the matter to the next ranking Officer. Lieutenants either decide or are requested to form a Board of Inquiry (BOI). This must be done through the MFH internet site, though it may be addressed in game. The lieutenants are listed on the MFH internet site. Each one must be made aware that a BOI has been called.

B The charge must be stated. At this time the CIC must be alerted to the fact that a BOI has been formed via email.
Heresy - Breaking Rules set by Verant and 989
Discordance - Breaking Guild Rules
Dishonesty - Answering falsely to direct questions
Dishonor - Failure to finish a job to employer specifications without refund
No Confidence - Misuse of authorities or abandonment of duty
AWOL - /Guildremove-ing yourself without notice Away With Out Leave. Such removal is considered voluntary dishonorable discharge

C Evidence is requisitioned and examined. During this time, a special rank may be created by the guild Lieutenants. It is the rank of Inquisitor. They are charged specifically with gathering and logging information from other guild members and non-guild members who they believe may be involved or witness to the situation. All their logs will be submitted and posted on the MFH internet site unedited as replies to the original board of inquiry post. The evidence gathering period will take a minimum of 2 days, and a maximum of one week. After that time, any evidence or testimony that was not given shall be rendered null and void. All evidence will be viewed by the Lieutenants. Only the CIC may state that evidence be inadmissible. All evidence must be submitted to the CIC for approval.

D Testimony is Given. One of the Inquisitors will be named prosecution. The accused shall make his own defense. The prosecution will state the evidence in support of the charge first by post on the MFH internet site. The defense shall make a reply to the post on the MFH internet site in defense of the charge and evidence brought forth.

E Sentencing. The Lieutenants will then consider evidence, prosecution, and defense. They will all vote in favor of or against the prosecution. The majority vote wins. If the number of votes is equal in favor and against, the CIC shall cast a tie-breaker (this is the only time the CIC may vote on such a matter). Then the Lieutenants will decide upon and impose a sentence. The sentence is to be carried out immediately or upon the earliest opportunity.

6)Duel of Honor
Any guild member may forego complete Due Process in this manner. It is when things need to be settled and temperaments are high. The duel is the way to go. You and your opponent will either duel each other, of if there would be no contest, you may each pick champions of roughly equal level to fight for you (You must work out what payment you will give to your champion for the fight, if any). A duel of honor should be just that; Honorable, no outside help should be given to either contestant from other PCs. Duels should take place in an arena when possible. The terms of the duel will be set, and If the matter warrants an Officer is to oversee the battle. Any exchange of equipment or moneys to be decided by the duel will be held by the Officer for the duration of the battle. Command has no say in the decision that comes from a duel, but both parties must agree that the winner wins the dispute. The terms of the duel agreement must be logged and submitted to the MFH internet site.

DISCIPLINE
1) Fines
Forced Requisitions and Issuing Donation
The amount of donation is to be decided by a C.O. or BOI, and may be no less than 10pp and no more than 100pp or a comparable item. Adequate time will be allowed for payment. Refusal to pay is considered automatic dishonorable discharge. You are not required to pay, or will be refunded if a BOI finds your c.o.s decision for fining you faulty. All fines go to the Quartermasters for addition to the R&I department.

2) Forcible Demotion
Only those of Lieutenant rank or higher, or order of a BOI may result in forcible demotion. This is usually in response to a claim of no confidence. A demotion may not reduce ones rank to less than Trooper.

3) Dishonorable Discharge
Dishonorable Discharge is only possible by order of a BOI. Upon such discharge order, the first officer to confront the sentenced, is to target him, and type /guildremove (be careful, do NOT do this while targeting yourself as you will remove yourself from the guild). This individual will never gain support nor aid from MFH members, and he is banned from ever charging our members or calling for Tribunals against our members. His testimony is no longer valid (that does mean that you will not be charged for crimes as above against this individual).
Only by unanimous anonymous agreement by the board of Lieutenants can a Dishonorable Discharge Status be rescinded.

Troopers -- Troopers make up the bulk of the guild. They are the main fighting force and the most numerous rank in the guild. No Trooper ever need accept a contract they do not want, and unless on a guild event or training maneuver, do not NEED take any orders. The officers are only officers if a Trooper volunteers to be under their command, and then only during the set time frame of the contract, event, or maneuver.

Privileges: The guild is going to supply them with one magical weapon, a uniform, and a good ranged weapon if they can use one. (NOTE: Equipment will be given out as supply makes available, not as demand increases.)

Duties: To work with other Troopers and add their strength to the overall strength of the guild. To role-play a mercenary, get that alcohol tolerance up, and find their niche in the guild, whether it be a spell casting mage, or a dagger slinging back stabbing thief, or a straight up hard core warrior. To make as much money as possible and help their troupe in leveling and working as a smoothly oiled team. It takes work, dedication, and the desire to have fun at all costs and show the monsters in Norrath that you are a force to be reckoned with. If you are asking to join a Troupe you are EXPECTED to follow the commanding officers orders, and do your best to conform to the manner he runs the Troupe.

Authorities: A Trooper DOES NOT have to follow any orders of ANY superior officer unless they have volunteered to do so. By accepting Guild Status you have already agreed to follow any reasonable orders from a Lieutenant. You are perfectly within your rights to request such orders in writing. You may if you feel it is warranted enact the Standard of Refusal. If you are asking to join a Troupe, or are invited to one and accept, you have the RIGHT to leave at any time, though it is strongly requested you request to be dismissed and not just disband. You also have the RIGHT to join ANY EVENT as long as you understand the aforementioned requirements. You have the RIGHT to attend training sessions, if you wish. You can SPONSOR New Recruits. Once you have sponsored 3 - 5 new Recruits, your performance will be reviewed and you most likely will be promoted. You have the authority to request a Tribunal.

Specialist(1,2, or 3) --
A Specialist is a group member of a MFH troupe that has distinguished his/herself in combat WHILE hunting in a MFH troupe. Specialists can also be given this rank if they have at least 101 points in a trade skill and they have become known for it. The promotion must be sponsored by a member of the applicants Troupe, seconded by another Troupe member and approved of by the applicants commanding officer.

Privileges: Additional to what Privileges you had as a Trooper, Specialists will get preferential access for receiving MFH equipment when supply makes items available. They get the honor of being the troupe authority on a certain type/area of combat or skill.

Duties: Additional to what duties you had as a Trooper, you must respect the ranking system and procedures of the MFH. If the group leader falls in battle the ranking specialist will be in command of the group (The fallen leader may still give orders to the specialist).
return to Rank

Sergeants -- Sergeants are in command of a Troupe. Though it is advised that a troupe consist of regular members; any Trooper available may take up an empty spot in a Troupe if the Sergeant is willing to accept them. The condition is they must abide by the sergeants style of command and not interrupt the troupes usual operating habits.
Promotion to Sergeant: Promotion to Sergeant requires that you meet the following requirements. You must have been in the guild as a Trooper at least two weeks (giving you a total time in the guild of one month). You must have sponsored enough people in the guild to form a troupe (i.e. 3 - 5 people). If all of these conditions have been met, it does not guarantee you a promotion. You are free to tell your direct C.O. that you are desiring promotion, if he considers you worthy, he will make the official request, or go through the channels necessary.

Privileges: They may organize and command troupes during events, training maneuvers, and guild wars. They are officers of the guild. They retain all of the Privileges of the lower ranks.

Duties: To create an effective troupe and be able to coordinate with other troupes in guild events and maneuvers. They must be able to RP a paramilitary leader to some degree (or at least try -- that does not mean be a hard ass... that means sling the lingo, or learn it, and keep your men in good spirit and coach them in working as a group. You decide if you are the hard-ass drill sergeant or the more quiet, yet calm and polite Starship Captain... it does not matter as long as your men follow you right?). Recruitment of new members.
Authorities: Sergeants have the authority to decide if they will accept a volunteering Trooper into their troupe. This can be permanent or temporary (for a guild event or a day of hunting). A sergeant can declare at any time, event or not, that they are organizing a troupe for a hunt/event/etc. The troupe will fall under event rules, which means that the Sergeant decides if you need be in uniform, and Troopers under him are expected to follow his orders. He is leader of the group (to organize it by inviting all members) and decides on the place and time of hunting, and when the group will disband (formally that is, any member may take his leave by requesting to be dismissed... which is to BE HONORED.) Sergeants may freely invite people into the guild, but in doing so, the New Recruit is THAT SERGEANTS responsibility. Those New Recruits must be properly logged (see recruiting) and will be the first the sergeant is expected to pick when organizing troupes -- unless level differences dictate otherwise. A sergeant does not have the authority to reprimand a Trooper. They may choose at anytime to relieve someone of duty who refuses to follow orders (i.e., disband them) and is expected to make a report of this to the superior officer on duty (on line) or if none are, email it to a lieutenant. He may suggest disciplinary actions be considered if this is a reoccurring problem. Under NO CIRCUMSTANCES can a sergeant DISCHARGE a guild member OR refuse them leave from a guild event. This IS grounds for DEMOTION, and possibly DISCHARGE . By accepting the Rank of Sergeant you have VOLUNTEERED to accept commands from Captains at any time, regardless whether it is an event or not. The Standard of Refusal still stands for you. Sergeants may request a Tribunal.

Captains -- A rank of some prestige. Many disciplinary questions and ALL POLICY DECISIONS for each Captains Company is that Captains area. Policy for the company CAN NOT supersede the policies laid down in this document and set by the lieutenants.
Promotion to Captain: In order to be promoted to the rank of Captain you must meet these requirements. You must be a Sergeant. You must be suggested for promotion by a higher ranking officer. You must have invited at least 3 troupes worth of members into the guild (9 people).

Privileges: A Captain may take command of a Company. It is up to him to personalize it, and make it unique in the guild, including naming it. He is in some ways like a mini-Commander In Chief. He also has the privilege to form any troupe of any unassigned Troopers and sergeants who are willing or reorganize his Company if he feels he needs it. A Captain is encouraged think of a theme for his Company, do you specialize in trade skills, insertion and extraction, vanquishing elite enemies, or as a captain would you like a troupe of each? He is also in charge of recommending Troopers and sergeants for promotions. He retains all of his Privileges from the lower ranks. Captains may promote Troopers to Sergeants, and even suggest Sergeants who they feel are ready to take the office of Captain. The CIC will make all promotions official.

Duties: He must be able to coordinate large groups of people (anywhere from 9 to 18). It takes the ability to inspire people, gain the respect and friendship of the men below you, and all ways remember that this is a game. We are here to have fun. They are in charge of making sure the Sergeants do not get too bossy (if the Troopers bring you a complaint, address it immediately, and with diplomacy... i.e. use mostly tells to discipline the men under your charge, be fair, and be lenient -- we re a Paramilitary force, not the ARMY of Antonius Bayle. We hire, not conscript. So we got to keep our employees from quitting) He must choose a name for his company and do everything to add more numbers to his company and the guild. Oversee his Company during events and guild wars, and ensure the Sergeants can coordinate with each other and him during training maneuvers. He must remember who is in what troupe, and ensure the levels of those under him compliment each other during training and events. He ensures everyone in the Company knows what their rank is, what company they belong to, and that they display it properly. These officers are in charge of forming Tribunals for dealing with outsider complaints. Officers are also given the authority to RECRUIT RECRUIT RECRUIT.

Authorities: First and Foremost is they may build and take charge of a Company. The company will be logged under the name that the Captain chooses for it. During an event, a Captain is in full control of his company (consisting of those active in the event, not those who are not participating). A Captain has the authority to recommend a guild member in his company for promotion. He may invite New Recruits without permission and may set his own companies pre-requisites for membership in his company (a captain is not to turn down those who want in to the guild, but to merely decide whether or not they will be in his company). A captain is the one in charge of organizing tribunals (see Guild Rules). A captain may arbitrate minor disputes between his company members, and a Captain my reorganize his Company at any time (during events). A Captain my give orders to Sergeants OUTSIDE of events ONLY if they feel that that Sergeant is doing something that may not necessarily be breaking any rules, but may be making the game unenjoyable for someone else, such orders should be reasonable, and usually done via tell (of course, if your people love you and your all into RP enough, as long as they will follow them, you can order them to practice playing Leap frog at your whim... I do not, however, suggest this unless you are REALLY LOVED). No one is under any obligation to honor your orders, but if they do not you may report them and the matter will be looked into. I weigh heavily in the idea that the less NON-EVENT orders given, the better... and only for small disciplinary matters.

Lieutenants -- The MOST prestigious rank in the guild. These officers DECIDE guild policy and have FINAL SAY in disputes. They have the most responsibility and do the most work for the guild.

Promotion to Lieutenant: There is no quick and easy way into this position. You must be held in very high regard both by the men under you and the officers who have commanded you. Generally, as long as you are willing to do what it takes to help the guild and get things done, and are 100% reliable, you might find yourself in this prestigious position. The other way is by working your way through the executive staff. Chief Recruiters and Chief Quartermasters are up for consideration of promotion to Lieutenant when their terms of service are completed satisfactorily.

Privileges: Lieutenants will be assigned a Battalion consisting of 1 - 3 Companies. These people are their charges. They have the right to organize events for their Battalion and anyone else who may wish to join. ORGANIZATION
The guild is organized like a paramilitary fighting force. Though these groupings will not always be in effect, they do define the positions, ranks, and feel of the guild.

Solo
The smallest unit in the guild is a solo mercenary. Called a Solo for just that reason. If you re solo, any jobs you take and business deals you complete are your own business, and all profit and favors are yours to keep. You do not even have to let the guild know the deal went down without a hitch, though you are encouraged to submit records of your jobs for posterity, and stories and good RP material will be rewarded. The Solo can be of any rank, takes no orders from anyone, and basically does as he pleases as long as he does not violate the guild charter or its IRL rules.

Troupe
The troupe is the smallest tactical unit in the guild, consisting of three to six mercenaries. During guild events or training maneuvers, this will be led by an officer of no less than Sergeant ranking. During non-events this may consist of anyone in the guild you choose to take with you, though rank should be observed, during regular hunting, it may not necessarily be.

Company
A Company consists of 1 to 3 Troupes and is led by an officer of rank no less than a captain. Captains of companies are encouraged to name their companies, give them a theme, and personalize them. In effect, a Captain may customize a company in any way he sees fit, running it as his behest as long as his changes do nothing to interfere with the guild rules set down here. Some Ideas for a Company theme are Insertion/Extraction, Looks and Attitude to advertise the guild, or Combat on a certain terrain.

Battalion
A battalion consists of 1 to 3 Companies and is the largest tactical unit in the guild. Company A and B are regular Companies, Company C is composed of irregular members of the MFH that may not be able to attend regularly, and additionally First Battalion will have a D company, the training company, that all new recruits are assigned to for training. A battalion is led by a Lieutenant, and is only used during guild wars and rare training maneuvers. A few events such as massive raids on cities will use a Battalion or two if enough of the guild is present for the event.

GUILD POLICY
The Board of Lieutenants has collective control over Guild Policy.
In order to amend this Handbook (guild policy) the amendment must first be written out in the final exact wording. Several changes to different areas of the Handbook may be made in one amendment.
The board of Lieutenants must pass this amendment by near unanimous vote or unanimous vote; 4 yea/1nay or 5 yea.
The CIC has no voting rights on the Board of Lieutenants. If there are less than five Lieutenants then the ACIC shall vote as an acting Lt. The CIC may submit desired amendments written in their exact final wording to the Lieutenants for a vote of the Board.
The CIC has the right to veto an amendment to the Handbook after it has been passed if he is wholly or in part unsatisfied with the content or wording.
The Lieutenants may request a reason for his veto and attempt to re-write the amendment and pass it again as many times as they wish.
Votes are held for a time of two weeks in the Legal Department Forum, after two weeks time abstaining votes are counted as yea votes.

Guild Events
Officers must make an attempt at organizing events regularly. During events the accumulation of experience may not be the main concern. Officers should assure that each participant benefits in the form of experience or loot from events.

Rating System
1 untested
2 blooded
3 proven
4 veteran
5 elite
These are the ratings that can be awarded to deserving guild members to raise morale and give other MFH a reason to excel. They may be given by a 60% yea vote from the troopers own company, and then approved or vetoed by the CIC or ACIC.

Voluntary Demotion
There is no dishonor or reprimand if you request a Voluntary Demotion from your current duties and rank. If IRL situations have hampered your ability and time to devote to your current duties, or you just feel they are taking away from the enjoyment of the game you may request a demotion. We will return your previous rank and keep you in excellent standing with the guild for you honesty and responsibility in alerting us.

Honorable Discharge
At any time, if someone requests to leave the guild, and gives proper notice via EQ chat, email to their superior officer, or posting on the MFH internet site, they are considered to be Honorably Discharged with Re-hirable Status. If they do, at a future date, wish to return to the guild, they enter at the rank of Trooper.
