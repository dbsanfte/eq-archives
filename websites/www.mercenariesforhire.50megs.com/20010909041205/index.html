Our Guild Charter:


The 'Mercenaries for Hire' (MFH) are devoted to devising and implementing strategic approaches to game warfare (combat) both to increase hunting efficiency and to aide in PVP battles.

Mercenaries Creed

I swear to uphold the Creed by being a living example of the following:

1) To always complete my contract once accepted, regardless of cost in time or gold, to my employers 'exact' specifications.

2) To always get paid.

3) To never leave a companion in danger, to show no cowardice in battle or business dealings.

4) To honor other Swords for Hire, in friendship, competition, or battle with utter professionally.

5) To deal honorably and fight dirty.

6) To operate by the MFH Handbook

The MFH is dedicated to making EverQuest an *enjoyable* Role Playing experience for those that desire such things, and break new ground in the world of Everquest. RolePlay is heavily encouraged, and use of the /emote command is appreciated.

From the Commander In Chief


Our guild does not have a lot of lip service. We are based on action, and reward action. We do see those who tow the line. I want you to join the guild, have fun, make a name for yourself, and leave your mark.

"Remember, you're a Merc, so act like one... chew iron and spit out nails."


Membership in the MFH

If you play Everquest to meet friends and to have fun then become a part of one of the most in depth role playing guilds in all of Everquest, Join Mercenaries for Hire.

Anyone interested in becoming a Guild Member please go the JOIN page for details.

If you have questions email the MFH, contact us in EQ, or leave a message for us on ICQ #115599338.

Official attire for MFH is an all black uniform.

Our Guild is based in Brell Serilis. Mercenaries for Hire plans to start branches of our guild on other servers.
If you are on another server and would like to bring a branch of the MFH there, learn how!
