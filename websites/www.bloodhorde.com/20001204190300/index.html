Members Offering Services (2 Replies) Posted by Runja on 2000-11-26 17:51:55
Me would just like to clarify sum tings about da services that some of our members provide to da guild. Weez seem ta have some people demanding services recently, and me juss wanna talk about sum stuffs.

-ooc-
We have members that lend their time to the guild providing Blacksmithing, Jewel craft, Alchemy, and many other useful services. The members that provide these services are being generous with their time. I want to remind people that the members are providing these services as a favor to us. They are not required to help people every time they need something "right now", and are not required to provide the services at a discount. Most of the time, members will be more than happy to help and provide the services at cost or at a discount for guild members, but they are not required to do this.

As a guideline, if you want somebody to make something for you (especically in jewelry, or alchemy), find out what the components are for that particular item, purchase them yourself, and bring them to the person providing the service. They will usually be more than happy to make the item for you. This really helps the person providing the service from having to constantly run to town to buy components and such. Note that failure is part of the game, so it is possible that the jewel crafter can fail on your platinum sapphire necklace and eat your 200pp+ worth of components.

Also note that member services are not to be used as a way to get items for your non-Bloodhorde alt at a cheap price. If you are caught doing that, you will be cut off from guild services. The time that members spend providing their services should go to benefit the guild.

Just remember that the services the members provide are provided as a favor. If ya got questions, post'em.

RARR!
- Runja
