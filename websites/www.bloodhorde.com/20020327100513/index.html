Sunday mid-level TOFS Raid (0 Replies) Posted by Drachon on 2002-03-22 13:38:17
Greetings Comrades,

The Tower of Frozen Shadow has long been a successful hunting ground of the Horde, and it has now come time to bring a new generation of Hordelings there to spill blood.

The recently returned Shaar will be spear-heading a mid-level Raid there for those of us in the Horde who are unable to attend the veteran's Plane raid, due either to experience or time constraints. Mid 30's to mid 40's is the recommended seasons to participate, but all will be welcomed and none turned away.

We will meet at the Gnomish Pirate Outpost in the Iceclad Ocean at 4pm EST. If you think you are to be in attendance and have not yet spoken either to myself or Shaar, please Email me to let us know, so that we can plan our groups accordingly. It looks as though we will have at least two full groups there.

The roll-call thus far includes: Abrethel, Alexandreana, Klaxz, Shaar, Uteu, Drata, Obelisk and myself, Drachon, but we hope to add even more to that in the next few days.

I have posted a PDF map & info sheet to the Tower for download here, and recommend that those who have not been to the Tower before go to the message archive on this site to take a look at Kartane's excellent primer to the TOFS, posted under a previous TOFS raid announcement.

Also, a special note for the SK's coming: Many of us have neglected practicing our Evocation discipline, which our most powerful spells against the waking dead fall under. If you have a few minutes before Sunday blast some Decaying skellies with Ward undead, so that we can use this very mana efficient line of spells to compliment the Necromancer and Cleric's (more powerful) versions.

In hatred,

Drachon Arkanis
Knight of Innoruuk

new blood... (4 Replies) Posted by Dizeez on 2002-02-28 10:30:19
A dark trio set up camp near a halfling village in the murk of the Nektulos Forrest, boasting of the trophies they will carry home. The two young Tier Dal resemble each other close enough to be twins, while the hulking troll strikes a disturbing contrast to the eerily handsome youths.

Unnoticed by the three, a dusty skeleton shambles by, grinning to itself. Upon passing out of sight the decaying creature stops and seems to make outline a mystic symbol in the air. Slowly, the flesh creeps and crawls from the earth, coalescing into the form of a familiar Necromancer. Watching from beyond the rise, the Haggard looking Dark Elf watches the three and, nodding his approval, steps into the shadows and dissapears.
