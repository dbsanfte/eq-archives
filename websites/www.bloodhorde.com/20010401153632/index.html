New website stuffs (0 Replies) Posted by Runja on 2001-02-19 02:21:51
RARR!

-ooc-

I spent a little bit of time this evening sprucing up the Bloodhorde website. The first thing I would like to point out is that we now have 3 new message boards. Due to popular demand, each battalion has their own message board to discuss their hunting spots, issues, or what ever they like.

The other "enhancement" is also by popular demand. If you go to the battalion pages, it will now list battalion members with their level, and now, class too. I had several requests from Battalion leaders that it would be useful to see everybody's class on the list. Hopefully, this will make group orginization easier for people.

Lastly, I would like to point out a new box on the right (if you are logged in). This new box named "Newest Posts" shows the 15 most recent posts made to our discussion boards. No more needing to post messages on the "Front Page" to get something noticed (*grins*). I'm hoping this will allow members to see what the current hot topics are and draw attention to some posts that may normally go unnoticed.

One thing to note about the "Newest Posts" box. It only updates every 3 minutes (saves the server from having to generate it every page load). So, if you post a message, your message may not show up immediately. Just be patient, it will show up within 3 minutes.

Please feel free to email me if you have any questions or more suggestions.

- Runja
