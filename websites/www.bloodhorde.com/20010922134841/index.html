As the newly appointed Blood Bringer of the Horde, it now falls within my realm to arrange the pairing for Trials of Blood. In order to perform this duty, I will need the help of all guild recruiters, officers, and minions. If you, as a guild officer, see fit to honor an outsider with the name of the Horde and bring them among us, I need to be notified of this. Please send me word, via carrier goblin (furryblueweirdo@home.com), detailing the new recruit’s name, profession, and season. If you are currently a minion who has not undergone his/her/its Trial of Blood, send me word of this as well.

I know that in the rush of blood and pain, it can be difficult to keep ones mind on the finer points of protocol. But the Trial of Blood is a tradition as old as the Horde, and one that has grown with us. It is this show of loyalty, this willingness to kill or be killed at the behest of the Horde, that finally makes us one of the family. So, you beautifully blood-thirsty mongrels, do keep me posted so that I can ensure the battles roar on. I’m sure you don’t want this Rake finding you in the darkness, giving you a reminder… in the kidneys.

Remember to keep your information on the Guild Roster current. This list serves as a valuable tool for our guild only if it is accurate. Remember that where you walk you represent each and every one of us. Temper yourself accordingly. Remember not to anger your healer before a fight. Never venture near kiting druids. Don’t forget to wipe the nose of the ogre next to you, because I’m sure they’ve forgotton. And finally… the most important thing to remember…
It’s all about style.

Kedge Keep on Saturday, Sept.15 at 6pm EST (4 Replies) Posted by Pustule on 2001-09-11 23:02:47
Greetings to the Horde,

Since we will be busy this Sunday, ive decided to organize a small raid into Kedge for Epic pieces this Saturday at 6pm EST. Ive already gotten verbal commitments from some of you, and have made a sign up for it at everquestraids.com. I'll be on a bit earlier for ports, but Id appreciate it if we could all meet at the Gfay spires. I know this is a horrible place to meet, but as I figure ill be porting many of you its as good a place as any. We can then move down to Dagnors together and form groups.

A great guide, as well as drop listings, can be found at:
http://www.xegonyalliance.com/resources.html
Our main objective will be to try to take out Phinny for the Mage/Rogue/and Wizard Epics, and the Corrupted/Tainted Seahorse for the Druid Epic. On our way we'll try to take out as much nameds as we can for loot.

Because this is my first time into Kedge, im urging that everyone signing up read the FAQ on Kedge. Im keeping it at 18 people max (3 full groups) for the following reasons: 1. Phinny isnt that powerful (51 caster), 2. From what ive read theres alot of tight corridors, the less people to congest them the better, 3. The more people that come, the more disorganized it gets no matter how well you try to control it, 4. Its an entirely underwater zone and many of the mobs are casters who like to dispell (byebye dmf and enduring breath), having less people to worry about 'whos got a waterstone' the better.

As of now, im sure of at least 1 mage thats coming. This means that if you dont have a fishbone earring or other EB device at least you can rely on waterstones. Also, try to get your swimming up to par if its lacking (~150 should be great).

Loot Rules: Subject to change, right now im leaning towards NBG, with the understanding that once youve rolled on/looted something, your out for future rolls UNLESS everyone has already looted or its class specific/nodrop. If there is a piece that noone wants (since this is all old world stuff) they will go to either SU or BH guildbanks. Epic pieces (Robe of the Kedge, Staff of Mastery) will be rolled on by the 2-3 highest level people of that class.

*insert fine print*
As the organizer and probable Raid Leader, im going to be reserving the Wizard Epic piece should it drop for myself (Blue Crystal Staff). By doing so I will forfeit on any other loot that might drop during this event.
