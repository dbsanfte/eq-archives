I am sure it will come as no surprise that I no longer seem to be able to muster any interest in playing EQ. Some of you have known of my disdain for the actual game for over a year. I never enjoyed the game so much as I enjoyed the Bloodhorde. I thank you all for making that possible for so long. But now, I find myself choosing to play solitaire rather than log into EQ and I believe that means it is about time for me to call it quits.

Consider Bloodhorde disbanded. In a few weeks I will log in game and perform the disband guild command, but I will allow a few weeks before doing so as to allow you all time to easily chat with one another as to what you will all do next. I have spoken with Vengerax who has told me he shall bring back Quellar del Oloth should I disband the Bloodhorde and that all Bloodhorde would be welcome among his ranks. I have also spoken with a few others who have told me should the Bloodhorde disband they will probably join an uberguild. It does not seem viable that all of the horde will find itself in one guild upon me leaving but I hope that most of you stick together. We have a good bunch of people among the ranks of the Horde. I was very proud of the Bloodhorde and I am convinced that through and through we had as high quality guild as could be found anywhere in Everquest, on any server.

I admit I feel a bit like I am betraying my friends here, but I hope you all can understand that I can no longer feign interest in EQ. I invite all of you to join me in my invasion of Shadowbane in early 2001 when it is released.

There are members I would like to thank individually but I fear that virtually every member of the Horde deserves an indivual thank you, so I will thank but a few individually and hope the rest of you understand why I stopped there. Runja deserves endless thanks for maintaining this website and making this guild substantially easier to run with his beautifully programmed website. Kolu served as a constant leadership presence and much of the reason I've played this game as long as I have. Susserak, Boilybut and Renadin were all founding members under various names that were with us from beginning to the end. Their loyalty to my vision is something I cannot allow to go unacknowledged. And of course there were many many more of you from the tops of our ranks to the very bottom that made the guild what it was, and I thank you all. This was the first guild I've ever been a part of that I liked every member and constantly enjoyed them all. (except Zhalazaim who I kicked out in the early days of the guild. ha ha)

I hope that this is not the end of my friendship with any last one of you. I hope we will all fight beside one another again in the next game worth playing. (not that I ever thought EQ was worth playing. hehehe)

Front Page Message Board (0 Replies) Posted by Runja on 2000-08-20 12:24:56
RARR Horde!

Originally, only the "guild leaders" where able to post new topics to the front page message board. This was just to allow guild leaders to announce events or other items that were of interest to the entire guild.

After much request, I have made it so that any member can post new topics to the front page. However, please note that new topics on the front page message board should be limited to guild announcements or discussions that are of interest to everyone. You should not post general questions, stories, or other such topics to the front page message board. We would like to reserve the front page message board for "news" type topics that are of interest to the entire guild.

All other topics should be posted to the general discussion or stories message boards. Please keep the front page so that it only contains news for the guild. Examples of news would be any type of guild event (raids, meetings, wars, weddings, etc), announcements about EQ itself (game changes, server moves - if we ever do that again...), suggestions for the guild, and other topics along those lines.

Again... please keep the front page news oriented. Have fun!

- Runja
