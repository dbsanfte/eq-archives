Current Events
Well, our plans for HoT were stripped when some plat hungry ubers decided they wanted to farm HoT to sell off the MQs. Dissapointed but still thirsty for blood, the Horde headed into the Crypt of Nadox and brought down one of our oldest adversaries, the Broodmother of Nadox (aka that big shin in front of me that I am hacking away at). Congrats to Grothia on Bone-Carved Boots and Frostooth on his new Razorfin Earring!

Also in recent weeks, The Horde cleared Fear and then called in reinforcements to take down the lord of terror himself, Cazic Thule. Congrats Ozzwald on his new Robe of Inspiration and our High Priestess Rejara on her new Halo of the Enlightened. I'm sure she'll taint that Halo in no time... Also congrats to Aruut and Gasaraki, who picked up a few pieces of Umbral armor while we were showing Cazic's minions what FEAR really meant.

Cyranyl had a bone to pick with the the Teir'Dal military command that screwed up the great crusade of Hatred in Kithicor and left Innoruuk's daughter, Lanys T'Vyl, splattered accross the forests floor... Took us a few tries to get the hand of the pull, but once our SKs got rolling ther was no stopping us - General V'Ghera was no match for Boilybut's skill in battle (well, at least not while Boily had the guild backing him) and Cyranyl proudly walked away with the General's Pouch. A few robes and trade ins later and she recieved the Rogue Epic Ragebringer. Congrats Cyranyl!!!

And last (but not least), we returned to our favorite pillaging grounds deep beneath te waves of the Lake of Ill Omen, exorcizing the tortured souls of the lost Iksar city and plundering their artifacts. Gloves were on the menu today, with Drachon, Sythin, Naldaaeliu, Frostooth, Gnarishal, Aruut and Razorr all now wearing matching sets of Kylong Gauntlets. Rejara picked up a pair of Kylong Boots, meanwhile Naldaaeliu was the biggest scorer of the night with an awesome Hooked So'Shar (with Aggro proc!).
