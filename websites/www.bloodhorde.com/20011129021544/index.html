Inevitable (5 Replies) Posted by Gnormagn on 2001-11-19 09:44:23
Well, you all know it’s been coming… suppose I’ll make it official.
I was going to get creative, and post a cool in-game death for myself, but the truth is, I just can’t bear it. It’s been over two years since I first installed EQ on my computer and took up the persona of Nulliira, the dark elf necromancer on the Karana server. Now I sit back and look at the “Create a New Character” button where Gnormagn used to be. It’s just too much to roleplay.
For those of you that don’t know, I was hacked for the third time over the weekend of November 2-4, a mere 5 days after my 27th birthday. Happy birthday to me… your account’s been destroyed. Once again I found Gnormagn naked and 45th level, destroyed from 54th with lots of planar gear and quest items. I also found all of my worthwhile alts deleted. I asked Verant for help, and once again I got a cold response at best… two weeks later. It’s all been horrible for me, and after going through this again, I just cannot bear to play EQ anymore. I uninstalled the game the night Dana destroyed Kartane… I’m only thankful that I wasn’t home to watch her do it.
Gnormagn and Kartane have been alive for just about a year, and after almost 80 days of play time, they’ve become very prominent parts of my recreational life. While I may run a LARP (www.lionerampant.com) and I may part-own a gaming company (www.livingimagination.com) nothing can compare to the roleplaying experiences I’ve had across 2 years of EverQuest. I’ve met dozens of people in the world of pixels and dots, and every one of you has meant something to me in some way. I’m going to begin my list of thank you’s, and I’m sorry if I forget a name or an incident; there’s just so much to cover and I want to get as much covered as possible. This letter’s been long enough in coming, I don’t want to delay it any more while I ruminate on what is, essentially, the death of a friend. Here goes;

To Zhokaii, Vengerax and Kolu: Thanks for forming Bloodhorde. I was there from the beginning, and this guild has been through a lot. You guys have always been a part of my EQ experience, even all the way back to the Guardians of Innoruuk, the rise and fall of Quellar del’Oloth, and the eventual split from Karana. Of everyone, you three are the true EQ vets, and I have to thank you first and foremost. You kept me playing when I began to hate the game, and you helped me realize that there ARE roleplayers out there… they’re just hard to find.

To Runja, Voness, Ulul, Darakryn and Boilybut: You kept the guild going when it looked like it was going to fall apart. You welcomed me back to Bloodhorde as a rancid little gnome when my dark elf was left on Karana, and helped me get on my feet when I was a newbie again. You guys were the heroes I aspired to hunt alongside, and the practitioners of skill that I hoped to compare to. I don’t think you four will ever understand how much I looked up to you as a young gnome, and how thankful I was to actually fight in the same group as you, or help coordinate raids with. I’m honored to have served with all of you.

To Ithgor, Kloth, Jury, Izayo, Nerotic, and the rest of the Black Venom veterans: You may have left the Horde, but you all still played a role in my advancement. Whether it was to invite me on a raid, jump in on a pickup group, or if it was just to say Hi at an appropriate time, I count you all as friends and brothers. Some of you lost the kindred spirit that made you Horde in the first place, but I don’t hold that against you. Being a roleplayer in a small guild when you’re surrounded by “uberness” is a hard thing to do, and not everyone can stomach it. I’ve said it in the past; Bloodhorde is not a prison. You join to have fun. If it’s no longer fun, you leave, no questions asked. You were all still on my friends list.

To Inertia: You’re the most recent guildmaster of a sinking ship… that’s the thing about Bloodhorde; it’s always a sinking ship. Our standards and requirements are much higher than other guilds, and almost none of them have anything to do with your level. We require a level of competence, a level of dedication and a level of performance that few other guilds even care about. You’ve admirably maintained those requirements, and will admirably lead the forces of darkness, even when they leave as soon as they become interesting. I’ve watched a lot of “50+ migrations” in my time, but I never thought I’d start one. I want to thank you for hunting with us when we were only 30th level, thank you for roleplaying with me when I seemed to be doing nothing but rant insanely in guildchat, or fauning over you in a stupid little gnome way… thank you for everything you’ve done for this guild and for me. I’m sorry, Inertia. I wish I could stay and help you plug some of the leaks in the hull of Bloodhorde. I wish I could keep roleplaying with you… I wish fate didn’t deal me this cruel hand. I’m sorry.

To Rekka, Pustule, Freyja, Vanc, Achzizra, Lazier, and the rest of the recent Black Venom battalion: You grew up admirably; all of you. You grew from the ragtag bunch of lowbie hunters to the lifeblood of Bloodhorde. We served together, organizing raids, hunting together, supporting one another, and generally being the type of core that the old guard would have admired. We backed each other, even when it didn’t make any sense to do so. I know most of you have left the Horde by now, or are planning to go sometime soon, but the same applies to you; Bloodhorde is not a prison. We all had fun while it lasted, and I bear no ill-will to any of you. You, more than anyone else, were the people I sought to hunt with and the ones I could count on when the chips were down. All I can say to you is “RARRRR!”

To Deftnoes, who must stand alone: You were the best damned taunter and Shadowknight I could ever ask to hunt with. You, me and Kart were the best three-person team Bloodhorde has ever seen, and I dare anyone to challenge that. There was nothing we were afraid to try or unwilling to dive head first in to. We cracked the Tower of Frozen Shadow wide open, we swam into the unknown waters of Siren’s Grotto, we strode boldly into the Dragon Necropolis. You made me be the cleric I grew to be; I owe every ounce of skill I have to you and your own skills. I can only hope that you can find a cleric who can keep you alive; I know how much you hate dieing in stupid situations (like your cleric is too busy arguing with Rekka to notice you’re on the verge of death). I don’t think I’ll ever find a more capable tank in any game I play. I salute you higher than anyone else. Words will never capture it.

To the rest of the Horde: FIGHT ON! Find solace in your brothers and sisters. You can and WILL do it! You don’t NEED an enchanter everywhere you go (though it does make it easier, I’ll admit). Find a dungeon for the love of God, and stop hunting on zone walls! The world is HUGE, explore it! If there’s anything you can learn from me and my legacy, it’s that the game is what you make it. Don’t be slaves to the grind; BE PROACTIVE AND GET OUT OF THE OVERTHERE!

A special word to those of you who were never in Bloodhorde: Thanks for the memories. This list includes, but is not limited to; Kitzu, Sirik, Nori, Kroden, Lildyanu, Dopple, Dogge, Takatok, Glicknib, Canddie, Bearwolf, Driftingsoul, Wandor, Platnm… and more I can’t remember. You’re all awesome in some way, or I wouldn’t have noticed your names.

And finally…
Gneil, Razze, Frostooth, Razorr, and Gnadira: You’re all IRL friends of mine, and you, more than anyone else, know how hard this is for me. You’ve joined my hunts, helped me rebuild the last time this happened, you kept my heart for the game beating. I’m sorry I couldn’t keep it this time. Best of luck to all of you in your future EQ/MMORPG endeavors. You know where to find me.

Ramazam, Horde.

- John

Honestly, I adored some of you, admired some of you, was annoyed by some of you, and down right wanted to strangle some of you. But, I always wanted to do right by all of you. Because you are Horde. And if this guild knows one thing, it’s the meaning of family. From the angry bickering to the undying loyalty.
