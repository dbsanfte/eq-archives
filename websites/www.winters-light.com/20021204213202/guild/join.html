Join us!

  
Must be 63+ have Comm 1, Comm2 and bag (or emp key), PoV flag.

Must have at least 3 references from guildmembers regarding your moral character, once we have decided whether you fit the guild or not we will add you as a trial member for a period of 2 - 3 weeks. During this time attend all raids that you can make so that we can get to know you better. We try and raid all the time, we are NOT an xp/social guild, if all you want to do is log on and chit chat with friends then i suggest not applying. When we get together we have one goal to do and that is kill whatever is in our way and have a good time doing it :) No application will be turned away if you meet requirements all you have to do is send me an email at matt_abear@hotmail.com and you must send me a tell in game formally applying for membership to Winter's Light. Good Luck all that apply and hope to see ya in the game.

Mojoo Rising, Leader of Winter's Light
