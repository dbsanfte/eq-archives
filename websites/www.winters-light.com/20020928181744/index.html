A Little Ass Kickage..., August 12, 2002

 by Aenene  

The night was young. The word was out, and the call was made. "Move to Kael!" Mojoo shouted. The super force gathered at arena for what would be, we hoped, our final showdown against The Avatar of War. We first conquered Derakor the Vindicator (Grats Parri[CT] on boots), then Statue of Rallos Zek (Grats Bruuss[CT] on Cloak), and then moved down to our Idol/AoW prep spot.

As the leaders went over the strategy so that all would know, the Cleric team fixed macros, made sure everyone was on the same page and hashed out the MT list. After much discussion, the time had come to test out our strat. A fantastic pull of The Idol of Rallos Zek seemed a precursor of what was to come. We downed him with only a few Linkdeaths and finished our prep for the Avatar.


The pull was made, fantastic job monks! Azureblade engaged Avatar as the clerics pumped him full of life. Engage was called and within seconds, he was already down to 90% health. The 4 Mage team did a fantastic job with popping out those mod rods for the clerics. The shammies healed the clerics beautifully. Everything was just right. It all fit. The fight was one of the fastest and cleanest we had done for AoW at around 8 mins.



Grats to Alucardex on winning the oh so leet Blade Of Carnage, Xylor[CT] on his kick ass Helmet of Rallos Zek, and a double grats to Raahn and Brutis[CT] on their War Bow of Rallos Zek. Officially, there is no mob that we have engaged that we have not also sent to its grave. Grats WL and CT for being the second people on Drinal to kill The Avatar of War!!!
