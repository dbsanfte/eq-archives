A new dawn is upon us.


A Special Edition of Reality Checksum, by Mike Talon.


 

“These are the times that try men's souls. The summer soldier and the sunshine patriot will, in this crisis, shrink from the service of their country; but he that stands it now, deserves the love and thanks of man and woman. Tyranny, like hell, is not easily conquered. . .”

 

Thomas Paine, referring to the depths of the American Revolution, wrote these words in his work, “The Conflict.”  He was referring to the fact that a battle for freedom is never quick or easy.  Paine recognized that the people of the American Colonies were growing weary of the fight, they were ready to say that it was simply not worth it, there must be other ways to gain what they wanted, there must be other alternatives to the fight.  His words, then important, have become prophetic.

 

At shortly after 9pm, Eastern Time, on Wednesday, March 20th, 2003; the coalition which had amassed in the Persian Gulf fired and opening salvo of missiles at Iraq, initiating what the White House termed, “The disarmament of Iraq.”  Mere hours later, Saddam Hussein announced on Iraqi television that he and his forces would simply wait out our forces, wait until our patience fails, until morale drops, until we simply give up the fight.  This statement, and it’s implications, have prompted me to once again pick up my pen, and do the only thing I can in this situation.

 

I have seen the peace protests, I have heard the arguments against warfare in Iraq, and these arguments have some merit.  They have also been voiced before the government we elected, and before the governments of the other countries in the Coalition.  They did not fall on deaf ears, regardless of the fact that they were not directly acted upon.  To all those who strongly feel we should not be at war, do not feel you have been ignored.

 

This is not, however, the time for protests and protestations, for public displays of disunity and derisiveness.  The lessons of the Vietnam Police Action and other military actions prove that this does nothing but harm the morale of our own troops, your brothers, sons, sisters, daughters and friends. Their lives are tied to your actions, now is not the time to fail them; and the enemy is counting on you to fail them indeed.

 

During the second World War, Britain was decimated by constant attacks from Germany.  The Luftwaffe was so brutally effective that entire neighborhoods were wiped out in single bombing raids.  However, the spirit of the British never failed them, and they defeated the Germans (with help) because of that spirit.  Regardless of the level of attacks, regardless of the devastation and destruction, regardless of how hopeless the battle appeared to be, they never gave up.  Germany lost the war with Britain specifically because they did not fight just the military might of England, they fought every man, woman and child that still breathed air!

 

America must now take on this same attitude.  We must show the world that tyrants and terrorists do not fight simply the military of the United States, but that every man, woman and child in this country stands firm against them.  That we, each and every one, support our troops, support their fight, and will do whatever it takes to bring them home after a successful fight.  Most importantly we must never falter in our resolve, for in doing so we will surely see those feelings echoed in our troops, in their morale, in their willingness and ability to defend themselves and us.  If this happens, Saddam’s threats will ring true, and the world itself will lose this fight.

 

Make no mistake, from this moment on, you – as an individual – have a duty.  You have a role to play and a responsibility placed on you.  You may feel this war is unjust, you may feel we don’t belong there, but we are there now, and someone’s son must go out into the desert and fight.  Our moral will echo through to him, and our insistent protests will strike him harder than any Iraqi weapons.

 

And make no mistake, you will not only feel this through the eyes and ears of men and women thousands of miles away.  We no longer have that luxury.

 

As I write this, the Homeland Security Office is warning all Americans that acts of terrorism here at home are a real threat.  Once again, arguments that we bring this on ourselves are moot, they are coming, and they’re probably already here.  Once again each one of us must stand firm and do everything we can to stop these terrorists dead in their tracks. This doesn’t mean turning in your Arabic neighbor, but this does mean calling the authorities when the guy next door (regardless of his race) start stockpiling fertilizer when he doesn’t have any room for a garden.  This responsibility falls on your shoulders and mine, no one is immune from terrorists acts, and duct-taping the windows is a rather “head in the sand” move at best.

 

We stand at the dawn of a brand new era, on the brink of a new World War.  Allies stand with us, enemies against us, and the battle lines are clearly drawn.  This is not the time for semantics – there will be more than enough time when everything is done to point as many fingers as you care to point.  This is not the time for protests – they are ineffective at best and devastating to troop morale in any case.  This is not the time for hysteria – the woman in the hijab is most likely not your enemy.  This is not the time for bigotry – most Muslims do not harbor any ill will toward others.

 

These are the times that try men’s souls.  Whether or not we survive the trial depends on you and me.
