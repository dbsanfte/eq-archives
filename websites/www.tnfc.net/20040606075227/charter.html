Guild Charter

The Tuesday Night Fight Club is a guild on the Bristlebane Server. Our purpose is to come together every Tuesday and adventure as a group, without twinking, to experience Norrath among friends. We aspire to be a place where members are always assured of a group (both on Tuesday Hunts and off-Tuesday hunts), and where they can experience the game without pressure to level quickly or acquire uber gear. We are a democratic guild, and our purpose is, first and foremost, to have fun within these basic guidelines.

Also, read our Rules.
