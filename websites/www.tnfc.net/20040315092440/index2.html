Welcome

Tuesday Night Fight Club is a unique guild on the Bristlebane server, whose sole purpose is to bring friends together at least once a week to hunt and have a good time. We love to explore Norrath and try to visit zones that are less well-known. Many of us came from different servers to participate in TNFC on Bristlebane. We gladly welcome anyone who is friendly and easy going, and enjoys exploration, new challenges and a good laugh.

If you're interested in joining our guild, please review our Charter, Rules and then fill out the Form. Then come be a part of a great group of people!

If you have any questions about TNFC, please feel free to visit our Forums and post your question there.
