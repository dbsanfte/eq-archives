History of the TNFC

The history of the Tuesday Night Fight Club is a murky one. There have been many legends passed down through time, mostly orally, regarding how it came to be. But there is no definitive tale that has been accepted as truth, as too many varying accounts of the deeds of these strange and unlikely allies seem to exist. Here are a few of those legends which have survived:

According to Tarrax Ironwolf, Beastlord of the Tribunal:

The winter night air blew blissfully around the shadowed figure as it crunched through the snow to the towns activity board. A frayed but leather colored parchment flapped rapidly in the wind. The figure reached up and placed his weather beaten hand to calm the parchments anxiety. The darkness around him was too much as he reaches into his tanned-hide backpack and pulls out a small unseemingly white candle stick. He whispers softly into the breeze and the candle lights up instantly, its little red flaming dancing wildly in the wind, but never going out. The figure looks up at the parchment once again and proceeds to read.

'I am seeking up to 5 other travellers who are willing to start on a long lived life of exploration and adventure. These hunts will be on the most holiest of days on Norrath, which is Tuesday. The Day of Battle, as deemed by the Gods of Norrath. These gatherings will be our way to show the Gods respect on the holy day. And any other day you are free to do as you wish, be it plundering for coin, working on a trade, and so on.

The acquistion of Seasons in your profession, be you mage, cleric, shaman or what have you, should remain constant and within reach of other members' Seasons. This way we are able to sanctify the Holy day together and vanquish our enemy in honor of it. If this be what you are looking for, ask about the Tuesday Night Fight Club and you will find us.

-Rawlden, Mage'

The sound of footsteps echo off the walls to the figure's right as a patrolling guard, Alec, approaches. "What say you at this hour. Is everything alright?" he says to the figure.

The figure still shadowed by his cloak and hood turns to the guard and smiles weakly. "I am fine. Just catching up on some posts since the last time I was here." His hand sliding down the parchment slowly.

Alec looks to where the figure's hand was and nods knowingly. "I see... Are you looking to join the Tuesday Night Fight Club?"

The figure looks back at the parchment and shrugs, "It's a possibility? What do you know of it?"

Alec fidgets for a moment, hand to his chin as he looks down at the ground trying to recall something. "Nothing more than that these posts are all over the known world of Norrath. I do remember a small gnome coming here a fortnight ago and posting it. He hasn't been back since."

"Interesting... Thank you. You have been most helpful."

"Anytime." Alec said, pausing for a moment, "Journey well and travel safe, Tarrax." then continues patrolling his watch.

Many days pass when the arrival of Tuesday came. Tarrax could feel the anticipation building as he quietly waited near the stone pillar book which brings you to the Plane of Knowledge. Occassionally a lone individual would arrive, at which he would politely ask, "Are you part of the Tuesday Night Fight Club?" Response after response came a resounding 'No.' Tarrax began to grow fustrated with each person came and went, when a small halfling appeared out of nowhere.

Standing up, Tarrax, walked up to the halfling and with a half-fustrated voice, asked "Greetings and well met. Are you the Tuesday Night Fight Club?"

The halfling looked up at the barbarian, and nodded. "No."

Tarrax at this point dropped his arms to his side, "Thanks my friend." and started to turn away.

The halfling then pipped up and said, "Wait, I just meant that I'm not the only Tuesday Night Fight Club..." Upon which Tarrax turned back and saw a crowd of people standing around the portal. Vah Shir, dwarves, halflings, humans and more. "All of us here are." He said smiling. "We've been expecting you Tarrax."

According to Rawlden, Founder:

Deep within the bowels of Ak'Anon a Magician sat and brooded over his ale.

"I have no friends." he muttered to no one in particular.

"Then why don't you get off your tiny butt and go find some?"

The mage started at the voice and looked around. There was no one else in the room save the clockwork bartender.

Muttering to himself at the barkeep's impudence, he lifted his glass to take a draught.

"Click, Whirrr, Bzzzz. You won't find any friends in there."

"You mind your own business, clockwork! I'll mind mine."

"Click, Bzzzz, Clunk. Well lately you've been most of my business. Sitting here day after day crying in your ale about how lonely you are. You're depressing my other customers."

The mage looked around. "What other customers?"

"Clank. Wizzz, Well I used to have other customers, but you've driven them all away with your meloncholy. Get out of here now, and don't come back until you can bring a friend or two to make up for all the business you've driven away!"

As the mage strolled thru the streets of Ak'Anon, an idea was born. Running over to the messenger service he hailed a clockwork scribe.

"Take a letter!

"To whom it may coincern,

"I am seeking up to 5 other travellers who are willing to start on a long lived life of exploration and adventure..."

He filled in the details. Tuedsays, Adventure, Comradarie, Loot. Why he settled on Tuesdays was his own business, but he added some high sounding language about Tuesday being a holy day of some sort just to make it sound good.

"Allright, I'll need a hundred copies and I'll pay to have them delivered and posted in every village and town on Norrath and Luclin."

"Click, Clank, That will be 100 platinums for the printing and delivery..."

The mage checked his purse.

"Hmmm... and if I deliver them myself?"

"25 Platinums."

"Very well, here's your 25 Plats."

Over the next week the mage traveled the length and breadth of both Norrath and Luclin posting his notices. From Halas to Shadow Haven. Then on the appointed day at the appointed hour he sat by the stone book in Steamfont Mountains and waited.

And waited...

And waited...

Just as he was about to give up and go home he heard a voice.

"Hail Rawlden!"

and another..

"Hail Rawlden!"

"Hail Rawlden!"

Sixteen adventurers had answered his summons. Enough for not one but three groups of hunters.

"Where shall we go?", they asked.

"Well, for our first hunt I suggest the Grikbar Kobolds to the west of here."

A stout Dwarf named Bordlumer scoffed, "I know these beasts, They present no challenge. I say we travel to slay the Gnolls of Blackburrow. With our numbers, surely we can clean their den of life from top to bottom.!"

The Mage shook his head. "We must first learn to work together. The kobolds will give us that opportunity. And besides, did I mention they have Phat Lewtz?"

"To the Kobold camps!" the croud roared as one.

Many Kobolds were dispached and nuch loot was gathered. Even the dreaded Bugglegupp was slain. Although not without a cost.

After an hour or so, Rawlden gathered his new charges about him and said, "We are now ready for the Gnolls of Blackburrow."

"Woot!" shouted the croud! And off they ran to Blackburrow where many gnolls were also destroyed by the Army of Rawlden.

Much later that evening, Rawlden cautiously entered the tavern he had been so rudely ejected from a week earlier.

"Click, Whirrrr, bzzzzzzz, You again! I thought I told you not to come back wihout a friend."

Rawlden smiled and made a gesture towards the door.

"I've brought a few." he said quietly as the mob tumbled in behind him...
