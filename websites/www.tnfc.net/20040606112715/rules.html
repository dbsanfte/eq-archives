Guild Rules

:: Rule #1 is Have fun!!

:: Be courteous and respectful of everybody, not just fellow guild members. We want to maintain a good reputation on our server.

:: Explore the less travelled regions of Norrath. We have decided to avoid places like Paludal Caverns for Tuesdays, but feel free to visit there to "catch up". Our sights are more on those places that are not well travelled and require a full group to visit when "level appropriate".

:: Re: twinking – Please, no donations from high level non-TNFC characters that belong to you on Bristlebane. Use "reasonable gear" at the Tuesday night events. This means try to avoid being overequipped for the Zone/Mobs we are hunting that night. If you have doubts about a particular peice of gear, leave it in the bank on Tuesday or ask a guildie for an opinion. There will be no "Gear Police", so don't worry. This is strictly an honor system. In any event, feel free to farm plat with your TNFC character! Also, feel free to use any gear you have obtained to assist with off-night plat farming, catch-up leveling and such. If this is confusing to you, please ask an Officer, they will be more than happy to clarify.

:: Stay together - Do not exceed the weekly level cap. This is part of the core ideal to ensure we have a group to join each Tuesday. Thus, we want to avoid anybody getting too far ahead or too far behind. Characters who exceed the level cap will not be allowed to participate on Tuesdays until the level cap catches up with them.

:: Tuesday Nights at 7 p.m. CST – be prepared for large scale fun. Check the boards for deciding where we go.

:: Off night activities – catch-up, work on skills, tradeskills, participate in guild-organized contests and games, play on a different server, group with TNFC members and explore, farm, consume a few Short Beers...have fun!

:: Guild Organization: We are democratic and casual in terms or organization. Officers are elected but the guild leader is an indentured servant for life.

Now that you've read that, please read our Charter.
