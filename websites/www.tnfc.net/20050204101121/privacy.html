TNFC Privacy Policy

The members of TNFC are regular people just like you, and your privacy is just as important to us as our own. Therefor, we assure you that any information you provide on the TNFC website or forums will be kept confidential. We will not email you (except to inform you that we have received your application to join) or share your email address with anyone. Your first name will be kept confidential unless you decide to share it with others yourself.
