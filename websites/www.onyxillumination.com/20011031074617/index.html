October 29, 2001
The Undead of Unrest see the Light of Onyx.
Friday Evening, A large mass of Onyx Illuminated Lay seige on the Estate of Unrest.  For more than 2 hours the Undead minions of Unrest knew no rest while the Onyx Lyght Shone Bright and true.  With minimal casualties The Onyx Handed out Damage and revenge for the Way it treated the Onyx Illumination on its very first raid as a guild.  To walk memory lane, Unrest was the place Brewnor Onyx First gazed upon the Light of Onyx and Eagerly joined that very night.  To follow suit on Onyx growing Stronger with visiting Unrest, Tinkertot Has now stepped into the light that is Onyx.  Please make sure to say hello to the Newest Illuminated one.
