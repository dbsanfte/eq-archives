  Sacrosanct accepts applications for membership from players who meet our level and class requirements. Your application must include your class, level, raid experience and everquest experience. Applications whose class and level do not meet the current recruitment requirements will most likely be discarded.

If you meet all the Requirements above, please email Slaas to get involved in Sacrosanct raids. Please let him know your main char's name/level/equipment and background of character. Also include a Sponsor if you have one.
You are not required to have a sponsor to join Sacrosanct, but having one will increase your chances drastically.
