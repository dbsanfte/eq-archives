Back to Everquest

What an interesting week this has been for the guild since our last update. We started the week off with another AE Plane of Fear clearing, to wax that big pile of shit Cazic Thule. It was a pretty smooth raid all over, plane cleared in our fastest time I believe, and CT downed on the first attempt again.
Shortly after we ported to thurg to visit our new friend Dain, although we were greeted with an unexpected 425 dain, which for some reason was a huge bitch to pull, but we managed to finally get that down. It was a pretty insane fight, and rocky at the start since Dain decided to kill 2 clerics right off the bat, which crippled us for awhile. We pulled together and managed to get him to no visible life remaining before he finally did the last of us that were standing in. It was a very good first attempt on this bitch of a mob.

Pumped from our Dain near-win, we decided to attempt Tormax the next day, and what an attempt it was. When we first attempted the statue, we had people bind near the fight so when they die they come back FM, and ready to go. For the statue though, once a warrior dies and comes back, they are still not a tank, just basically another melee. (Btw, if you have never had people bind at the fight to come back fm or full health, you are a lying bastard). We tried the same plan for Tormax.. which had different results, much different.

Normally once all the warriors die, there is no chance to win, which is usually the point when you call camp (or zone). For our fight though, we got KingT down to 25% life before this happened, and a zone was called.. but the sheer determination of the guild refused to listen and they just kept hacking away. Not one member wanted to face defeat, so once they died, they came back, looted and started going at it again. Tormax' health slowly continued to drop, which pumped us even more to keep going.

It was the sloppiest zergfest I have ever been involved in, but no other victory felt better than this. Just the rush you get from your guildmembers who are willing to sacrifice all that just to see this guy die is a feeling I cannot describe. Sure, our future Tormax victories will prolly be a snooze, like our current Statue fights.. but this one moment is when the guild was actually closer than it has ever been.

We also picked up a few ST keys this week killing Keeper, Klandicar, Sont, maybe Zlandicar too.
