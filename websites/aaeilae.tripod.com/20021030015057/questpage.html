This was the moment that i decided to commit myself to the cause of good. I became a Paladin of Tunare.

Falcion of the Koada'Vie
Here you see me carrying the Falcion of the Koada'Vie.
How do you get this sword?

It's a 3 part quest, the first two parts of this quest is in Crushbone Citadel. The last part is in Ocean of Tears.

First you must talk to General Jyal (he is in the basement of the Paladin Guild in Felwith) He will tell you about the orc oracle's en the orc runner. If you complete the orc runner quest (sealed note) He will talk about the Falcion, and how to do that.

The dangerous part of the first item is Ambasador Dvin Heart (nodrop) This is always on his body if killed.
Becarefull Dvin is very dangerous.
Dvin is a lvl 20 warrior (atacks as a lvl 30)

The second part is the blue Orc Head. This will be dropped by the Prophet (becarefull this is a caster)
There will be also a note there to tell you where to find the Suply crate. (If you can read Orcs)
Prophet is lvl 19 shaman

The note tells you where to find the Ogre Brawn. Brawn is very difficult to find. He spawns only on 5 AM replacing a siren, but he is not alone the other two sirens change into Darkelf Dragoons.
Brawn is a lvl 22 SK and the dragoons lvl 16-18

Brawn spawns on the sirens islands in Ocean of Tears. If you travel from the docks of Kaladim to Freeport jump overboard by the islands of the sirens (the first you encounter) swim towards the largest and walk cloakwards to the back of the island. There you find a smaller island in front of you whaere Brawn spawns.
