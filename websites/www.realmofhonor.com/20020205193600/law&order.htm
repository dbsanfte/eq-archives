Law and Order

Current guild rules:

1. Any level, class and race are allowed into the guild. If you are interested in joining, I (Tugna, the Leader) would like you to /tell me and arrange times to group with one or more of the guild members as well as attending one or more of our activities. After doing that, we will see what happens from there and I will discuss the matter further with you in game. I want you to get to know us as well as us getting to know you.

2. Regarding ports and rezzes -- do not assume that rezzers and porters in the guild will always be there to rez and port you. It is understandable that we all want to save money and time, but rezzers and porters are also trying their hardest to gain experience just as you are. However, if all else fails, do ask the guild for help. We are here to help each other, after all, just don't take advantage of the situation.

3. Power leveling. It's cool. Same as number 2, though, just please don't take advantage of the situation.

4. General conduct. Please be respectful of others. Conduct yourself in a considerate manner. If a hostile player confronts you, please remain calm and if you need help from me (Tugna) or the current Officers (Artagel, Drewt, Maddelf, Sinthetic, and Namila), do not hesitate to ask.

5. /anonymous vs. /roleplay. Please no /anon. If you are not proud to be in RoH then please do not join or please remove your tag. Roleplay works as it still shows guild tag, but allows you to go anonymous, where as going /anonymous covers your guild tag.

6. Leaving the guild. Just as a courtesy to the whole guild, let me know either in game or through email. We need to know why someone leaves so we can attempt to fix the problem and therefore not have anyone else leave from the same problem. We also need to know when you leave so we can remove you from the guild list.

Guild meeting rules:

1. Please do not stand or move about during a meeting, unless you have the floor. Simply sit and listen to what is being said.

2. Please do not speak during a meeting, unless you have the floor, or have been asked to respond to the speaker's comments. If you wish to speak please /raise your hand or request in a /tell.

3. Please do not drink alcohol during the meeting.

4. Please use emotes sparingly during the meeting.

5. Please do not cast spells during the meeting.

If we all utilize these rules to the fullest, then we stand to have a quick, un-dragged out yet efficient meeting and can get back to gaining experience, auctioning, etc.
