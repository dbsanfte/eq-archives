Welcome to e107

e107 is what is commonly known as a CMS, or content management system. It gives you a completely interactive website without the need to learn HTML, PHP etc.
It has been in developement since July 2002 and is constantly being updated and tweaked for better performance and stability.
Some of the features of e107 are ...
  • Secure administration backend allows you to moderate all aspects of your website, post news items etc
  • News item commenting, chatbox, forums, poll etc make your site totally interactive to visitors
  • Totally themeable interface, change every aspect of how your site looks
  • More themes and plugins available to download, dynamic recognition of new addons means extremely easy installation
  • Allow users to register as members on your site, and allow comments from members only or anonymous users
Your admin section is located at /admin/admin.php, click to go there now. You will have to login using the name and password you entered during the installation process.
If you would like to see something added to the core, or coded as a plugin please visit jalist.com and leave a message on the Requests forum, or alternatively email the developer jalist (Steve Dunstan) here.
If you have created a theme or plugin for e107 please consider sharing it with the rest of the community - send it to jalist who will upload it to the main e107 site at jalist.com.
Thankyou for trying e107, and have fun with your new website!
