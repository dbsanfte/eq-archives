Application for membership in Imperium is a four step process.


Step One

Visit our documents section and read all sections. If you find that you like and agree with them you are one step closer to membership. If, on the other hand you do not agree with our policies it may be best to seek another guild.

Step Three

Our Senate will review all prospective members and either approve or deny the application. All applicants will be notified of the decision either in game or via e-mail (that valid e-mail thing). In the event you are not approved for membership an explanation of our decision will be included.

Step Four

An Officer will invite approved applicants into the guild to begin thier 2 week trial period.
