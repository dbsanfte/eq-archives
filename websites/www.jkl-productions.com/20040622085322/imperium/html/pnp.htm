The Vision and Mission of Imperium

To become the dominant Guild on our server(s) of choice, if not the whole of the EverQuest II Universe. We shall attain this goal by adapting, aligning and utilizing our resources wisely while making practical use of our abilities to best accomplish our goals. We shall demonstrate expertise in attaining these goals by use of evaluation, research, and steadfast determination while working cooperatively as an integrated team. We shall all be whole heartedly committed to the vision that is IMPERIUM.

Recruitment and Trial Period

EverQuest II has been scheduled for release in the last quarter of this year (2004). At the this time we are accepting members based on prospective class and name. Please note that the 2 week trial period will not begin until we have officially established the guild and your character has been invited.

The basic minimum requirements follow:

Prospective Character Class: Any
Character Level: 20, you must be of final class level (to be imposed 1 month following the release date.)
Prospective Character Race: Any
Prospective Character Alignment: Any

** Note: Evil aligned classes/races may have to shift thier allied city to Qeynos, as we are based in this city

These requirements will change quickly as the guild grows during the two week period following release.

IMPERIUM shall set limits on the number of main character class membership slots. Certain classes may have more slots available than others depending upon function. No matter how qualified a character may be, we may simply have no room in that class at the present time. Recruitment notices will be posted on the major EverQuest II forums sites..

Honor, Strength, and integrity above all

IMPERIUM is a guild made up of like minded players who seek to reach goals which we have set for ourselves, and to overcome significant obstacles through the superior use of tactics and strategy as the Roman Legions did in many battles in the ancient world.

During the early stages of the release of EverQuest II we will be filling slots in the guild. However, all new members will be subject to the following:

Sign up – 2 week trial - The trial period may be extended at the discretion of the Senate and does not start until you have passed the initial screening.

At the conclusion of your 2 week trial period the guild council will vote on your permanent member status. This vote is influenced by our observations with regard to your ability to play your class, overall compatibility to the guild and its environment as outlined in in the rest of this page, and by input from guild members.

If you are voted down you will be removed. This removal can occur during the 2 week trial or at the termination of the trial period. It will be at the discretion of the Senate to provide you with a reason for the removal.

The Senate reserves the right to remove any character at any time.

If you are made a permanent member of the guild, you will still be subject to removal should you be identified as a problem or for violation of any of the guild rules/charter. The rules of the IMPERUIM are non-negotiable.

Code of Conduct

In order to provide the best Guild and gaming experience possible, we have established these guidelines for our members:

1. Membership is a privilege not a right. - Have Fun, Be Nice, and pay attention to these guidelines.

2. IMPERIUM expects a minimum standard of maturity from its members. The ability to act as a cohesive unit is mandatory. If you feel that you cannot or we decide you cannot fulfill the functions necessary to achieve our goals while maintaining a mature attitude, remove yourself from the guild, or we will.

3. If IMPERIUM is holding an Event (to be termed Raid when we are sufficient level to Raid) and you are online, you are AT the Event. All Events are mandatory.

4. We are civil to each other. Pretty simple, but we need to keep in mind that we are doing this for enjoyment, and we should respect each other's ideas. We can disagree without getting personally aggressive about it.

5. We Communicate. Communicate with your fellow members. A greeting in guild chat of some kind is appropriate when you log in. Make use of the forums, as much , if not more than Guild chat. Announcements, notices, and scheduling will be posted there.

6. ONE GUILD. IMPERIUM is your guild. In EverQuest II guilds are competing with one and other and solidarity is a must. Alts will be welcomed into the IMPERIUM Auxiliary gladly, but must make use of the /roleplay command until of appropriate level. If you feel that your alt needs to be in a different guild then by all means take your main there too! Those found to have alts in other guilds shall be considered to be involved in espionage and will be removed from our roster and replaced, with or without notice.

7. ONE PLAYER. We expect only YOU to be playing your character.

8. We keep the Guild's "secrets". This just means that we don't give away what we know or what we plan in IMPERIUM to non-Guild members. The Leadership determines the Guild’s public or private status.

9. We resolve Guild problems within the guild. We do not air dirty laundry in public. When differences arise, as they often do between talented and intelligent people, we try to resolve them in a way that is fair to everyone. We have tried to structure the Guild to allow for maximum freedom while still providing foundations to get things done. Should you have a problem with a guild member then present the problem to an Officer. If an Officer cannot resolve the situation they will present it to the Senate who WILL.

10. We are open to change. There is nothing about our little group that is beyond improvement and change. Every member may contribute in many ways, and we welcome participation and innovations. Please let your ideas be known. If you have suggestions for improving the Guild, bring it up for discussion in the forums in order for all to participate in the discussion.

Raid Rules

Imperim will follow the FiF (First in Force) rule when raiding.

1. If you are online and IMPERIUM is holding an Event, you are AT that Event. ALL Events are mandatory.

2. BE ON TIME!! At a scheduled raid it is your responsiblilty to be at the appointed starting place. If you need a port arrange it BEFORE raid time. We set a start time and place for each Event – at 30 minutes past that time we move. If you are late, we will not send someone back for you. In the event of a Called raid, transportation will be arranged by the Leadership.

3. All Event communication shall be within the event channel.

4. Absolutely NO spam in the Event Channel..

5. No direct messages to the Event leader unless you are a group leader.

6. No questions in Event channel. If you have a question, ask your group leader, that is what your group leader is for. Alternatively we will have a “spam” channel set up and you may ask there.

7. LISTEN - FOCUS - FOLLOW INSTRUCTIONS

8. Never go /afk without letting your group leader know. When going /afk you must stand and autofollow another member of the goup. Please let this member know that you are /afk and on autofollow.

9. Never question an order by the Event Leader. Follow blindly.

10. Your priority is to IMPERIUM. If you are on a pickup raid/group and an IMPERIUM Event is called or starts, you will be expected to leave your current raid/group.

11. All loot shall be distributed according to the Point Participation System (PPS).All droppable loot on an Event will be looted by a designated member. At the end of the Event we will begin distribution of the spoils using the Point Participation System (PPS). If you do not know how this works then visit the PPS System link. All monetary loot from an Event will be placed in the guild bank to facilitate the purchasing of needed reagents and necessary items, as well as contributing to the purchase and upkeep of guild property.

Point Participation System

IMPERIUM makes use of a Point Participation System (PPS). By use of such a system we can alleviate the problem of bickering over loot and the appearance of unfair distribution of the fruits of our labor.

The PPS link on the left will allow everyone to know exactly where he or she as well as others stand in the system.

During a raid a designated looter will pick up any and all droppable loot. All of the droppable loot will be distributed by bidding on it in the end phase of the raid. No-drop loot will be bid on at the time of drop once the area is clear and it is safe to do so.

Bidding will be conducted in a designated bid channel. You may be up to the amount of Participation Points you have. You may not overbid the amount you currently have and have it deducted from future raids.


Rewards/Definitions:

4 PP – Raid – A guild level function where we will have a specific target or targets. This type of guild activity will last a minimum of 4 hours.

3 PP – Guild Level Epic – A major step in a player’s career as they earn their epic weapon. This encounter will be one that requires multiple groups to accomplish.

2 PP – Guild Level Quest – A quest that involves the entire guild. This type of quest may require one or more events to complete.

All Events will have a pre-determined length, and it will be posted when the event is announced. The rewards will be pro-rated for those who do not remain for the entire event. Should you remain for less than half of the allotted time (from start time of the event) you will receive 0 (zero) Participation Points. Should you remain for more than half of the allotted time, but less than the entire event you will receive 1 Participation point. You must remain for the entire event to gain the full reward.

Exceptions to the above rule will be made for extremely long events such as multiple event quests, etc. Exceptions may also be made in extenuating circumstances as deemed necessary by the leadership.


How you may spend your Participation Points.

You may spend your points in any way you see fit, provided you keep the following four things in mind.

1.) Points are awarded to Player not the character.. (Double boxing will not get you double points)

2.) Bidding for your Main Character is on a 1 to 1 basis (a main character is the one you have designated as a main on the roster at the website, and you may not change your main status in mid raid).

3.) Bidding for any of your characters other than your Main Character is on a 2 to 1 basis. (i.e. bidding for you alternate character will cost you double the Participation Points.)

4.) You may not bid for no-drop items for an alternate.
