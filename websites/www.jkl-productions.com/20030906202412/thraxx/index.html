Thraxx Industries Intergalactic

Thraxx Industries Intergalactic is a Player's Association loosely designed around the concept of a Corporation, based on the Naritus Server in the Star Wars Galaxies Universe. At Thraxx we care little for the sucess or failure of the rebellion, and live by the philosopy "War is GOOD for business". Therefore, our faction alignment is truly Neutral.

We are made up primarily of Artisans and other classes that can craft items. We do, however accept members from all classes as a Corporation includes many diverse divisions. If you want to get in at the creation stages of a Player's Association that is bent on control of the economy of the Naritus server, NOW is the time to do it !!
