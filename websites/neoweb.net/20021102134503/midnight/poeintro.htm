Poetry

Some people believe that poetry has to rhyme, others believe that it has to have a certain number of lines, or sound a certain way when spoken. I, on the other hand, beleive that poetry can be anything, as long as it is heartfelt. I believe poetry is truth, not to be contained or inhibited by rules or filtered by unwritten laws of the art. Poetry is freedom of expression, the best and purest way to speak your heart. It is in that spirit that I have collected these works from my visitors, and hope that you will add to the works in the days to come.

All the people who have submitted their work to me are listed in the menu on the left, feel free to browse the current writings, or add works of your own.
