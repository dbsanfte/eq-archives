So, you choose to be a rogue? You like to lurk in the darkness…. backstabbing unsuspecting prey, and hiding as those volatile  trains come by. Well, good choice. Rogues can dish out the most damage on the opposing threat undisputedly; especially in groups with a taunting tank.     Well, the first thing to do is make your character. If you have already done so, scroll down to the next paragraph. When making a rogue, make sure you put ALL of your points into strength. If I am not mistaken, you will have a few points left over. Put these into dexterity or stamina; or both. Dexterity affects the character's capability to use weapons and armor, procs of magical weapons, and is more important for a rogue because it also affects the time it takes to learn roguish capabilities such as backstab and pickpocket. Stamina affects the amount of hit points you have, one of the major weaknesses toward rogues. Strength affects how much of a load you can carry and how hard you hit. During your career as a rogue you should always work for strength items, as that is also a rogue weak point. The wood elf rogue base skill in strength is only 65. The next step is to choose a tight little rogue name and the hottest face you can find and a religion and you are off. The first thing you do upon entering Norrath is getting to your rogue guild, called Scouts of Tunare. I think to get there you go over one platform from the mail lift and go up from there. If I am mistaken, hail someone and ask him or her to lead you there. At your guild, train some points into piercing and give the note to your guild master. He will give you some crappy but temporary armor. Hail your guild master. He will give you a quest involving 2 gold pieces and two rusty daggers to prove your worth. Do as he requests. Levels 1-5     Ok, I'm a little rusty on this area of experience but I'm pretty sure you can get to level 6 by killing little stuff in Greater Faydark such as bats, snakes, and maybe even orc pawns. You might even want to take a visit to the bandit's camp. You will level fast, and improvement will be evident. You may even get a twink or two if you are consistent.
