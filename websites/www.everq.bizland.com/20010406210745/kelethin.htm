 The beautiful city in among the trees, Kelethin is where Wood Elves are born to be either as a Bard , Druid , Rogue, Ranger and also a Warrior.

Level Range: 1 - 10

What to kill in the Kelethin Zone, What level do you kill them at?

Note: The table below is for people that solo and group, apart from levels 9 and 10 who are 'solo' only.

Level   Creatures Of Kelethin
     
1 - Bats, Decaying Skeletons
2 - Bats, Black Wolves, Decaying Skeletons
3 - Bats, Wasp Drones, Black Wolves, Decaying Skeletons, Widow Hatchlings
4 - Wasp Drones, Black Wolves, Decaying Skeletons, Widow Hatchlings, Pixie Tricksters,  Orc Pawns
5 - Widow Hatchlings, Pixie Tricksters, Orc Pawns, Orc Centurions also Bandits (better to group to kill bandits at this level)
6 -  Orc Centurions, Orc Shamans, Orc Oracles, also Bandits (better to group to kill bandits at this level)
7 - Orc Centurions, Orc Oracles, Orc Shaman, also Bandits (better to group to kill bandits at this level)
8 - Orc Centurions, Orc Oracles, Orc Shaman, also Bandits (suggested solo only)
9 - Orc Centurions, Orc Oracles, Orc Shaman, also Bandits (suggested solo only) NOTE: You will only find a few creatures that you would get experiance of though.
10 - Not many Creatures You Would Find At Level 10, So Time To Go Into The Realm Of CrushBone!

The table above may be inaccurate if so please contact me, Thanks.

Creatures That Spawn: 

 In the area around Kelethin is are many creatures to kill. So these are the most common creatures to look out for:

 This is by the two lifts known as 'Newbie Lift' and 'PoD Lift' Black Wolves, Wasp Drones, Bats, A Willow Hatching, Decaying Skeletons and bixy fairies. As you go to orc lift and go up the track you will end up at orc hill which you find Orc Centurion , Oracles, and Orc Shamans. Also by Orc Hill there is an entrance to Crushbone! There by the entrance to Crushbone you will find Orc Centurions, Orc Oracles, Orc Shamans and Orc Pawns and IN Crushbone there you will find would be Orc Pawns, Orc Centurion , Oracles, Orc Shamans, OrcSlavers and Orc Legionnaire's and the great Lords of Crushbone.
