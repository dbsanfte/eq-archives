Bards are a class that are always wanted in a group due to there abilities to heal and also to do lots of of nifty things they can do with their instruments they get. But they are not the most popular solo class as they are not the most strongest class along with the lack of hit points. It if sure that Wood Elves would be the best race you could be if you were to be a Bard. Being a Wood Elf Bard you will find that it is more of a Magic Supporter than a Tank. This class is a class that isn't specialized at anything but since it uses no mana its very handy as it doesn't have to meditate unlike other classes which takes hours of meditation to get to there full peak. The bard can only use one song at a time but can continually playing it on and on, they can use a special skill that makes them use two songs at a time. This class is really for people that are confident about the chat bit and as being a Bard you will need to group, most of the time.. Over all this class can turn out to be a very powerful class, specially to be in a group as you should always be welcome into a group, always.
