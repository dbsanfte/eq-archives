  Wood Elf druids are the most popular class in all of Norrath! Druids you will find are very popular because of their awesome spells they can get, such as healing and travel spells such as SoW -Speed Of Wolf and being able to Teleport is very good! They are also a class that is popular in groups with there buffing spells and healing spells. As you get higher in the game past the nebbie levels (1-12ish) you will get spells which will be good for travelling such as Sow and camouflage. Camouflage you can get a very low level but as you get better at it becomes very handy to a druids travell. Druids have very  powerful attack spells which if you don't have a magician or wizard around druids are right up there ready to attack! Giving time to meditate druids can be a very powerful solo class. With its spells and hit points druids  can regenerate very well also! One of the many reasons why Duids are such a popular class because they have such a wide variety of skills and spells, Druids are one of the better solo classes out there and that's a fact! Druids have great strength to run across field due to having Sow and other spells you can get making you very fast and a great traveler. Unfortunately most of the Druids Attacking spells are only workable in the out doors so you cant cast in dungeons and places under ground. I would recommend this class to people that are generous (as in SoW'n people as a request which you can make some money out of!), friendly and also like to do a mixture of things as you can use this class to either be a Healer, Tank, Magic Support, Magic Attack or all of them at once!
