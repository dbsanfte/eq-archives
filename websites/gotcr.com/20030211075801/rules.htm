General Guild Rules:

These are the rules. We expect everyone wearing the GotCR guild tag to be familiar with them and uphold the image and reputation we are proud of. Anyone who chooses to ignore the rules, or exhibit behavior unbecoming to the guild will be removed post haste.

1. All guild members will be expected to check the Raid and Amor Vincet Omnia Boards twice a week. The website is our Central Communication Center and our only way of communicating with the entire guild at once. On these two boards you will find guild issues and current events, as well as all Raid notifications and sign-ups. The passwords to these two boards can be obtained by emailing Weber Soze (mark@weber.org). If you do not check, at least, these two boards regularly--sadly, you will miss out on many guild events and raids. Any other content you choose to read on the website is up to you. It is there purely for the entertainment and interactive discussion of our members.

2. All people interested in becoming a Guardian should be referred to the Recruitment board at www.gotcr.com. If guild invites are open, they will be assigned a sponsor and have a two week “getting to know you” period. At the end of that period, the guild leader, sponsor, and recruitment officer will decide whether or not the individual will be tagged. Alts will always be tagged even if Invites are closed. Sometimes guild invites will be closed, but if we are actively seeking certain classes to make up for a lack in our roster, appropriate characters will still be assigned sponsors and tagged.

3. We are a family guild. We accept members from all age groups, classes and dedication to the “game”. This means that some of our members may be very young or easily offended. Therefore we ask that everyone refrain from using any type of language/ subject matter that could be considered offensive or objectionable in guild chat or on our website.

4. We expect our members to always conduct themselves in a reasonable and courteous manner. Shouting or OOC rude/offensive remarks across zones will not be tolerated. When in doubt, take it to tells.

5. Personal attacks/ Hazing/ Intentional Training are not tolerated. If a problem arises, contact an officer and let them try to intervene either by contacting the offender or the offenders guild leader/officer. Sometimes we can help, sometimes we can’t, that is just part of EQ. No matter what the situation, we will always expect you to acquit yourself in a manner becoming to the guild.

6. All members must designate a Main character. You may have as many alts as you would like wearing the guild tag, but only your main character will be counted in the official roster, and only your main character will be allowed to receive guild help on Epic battles. This does not mean you can’t take your Alts on guild raids and events should you choose, only that Main characters will be given consideration over Alts in cases of Uber/Epic/Class loot distribution.
6a. Alts left behind when a Main Character leaves: When a guildmate chooses to take their main character out of GOTCR, they are permitted to leave their alt characters guilded, provided their exit was in a clean and professional manner. Remaining alts of a former main will have access to guildchat and the message board like any other Guardian, however, if your main focus is elsewhere, expect your alts to retain the status of alts in all guild loot or epic consideration.


7. Every member will be expected to sign up all characters wearing the GotCR tag at www.guildmagic.com. Please be sure to pick the one that lists Evee as Administrator.

8. We encourage all our members to plan and host raids in zones they are familiar with/have led raids in the past. However, all Guild Raids must be emailed to Weber Soze (mark@weber.org) for approval before being posted to the boards to prevent conflicts with other planned guild events. Guild leadership will add your raid to the existing queue and get back to you with possible raid dates/information. Sometimes, this may take a bit of time, as the queue is sometimes long, so be patient, we are trying to get things done in as timely a manner as possible. After arriving on an agreed date, Guild leadership will post it to the Raid Board with all pertinent information. Smaller raids hosted by yourself can be posted under Guild Events, but please take care to check the Guild Raid board first to avoid "double-booking".
8b. Raid Level Limits: Guild Raids are not meant to exclusionary, but there are some places where even well-played attendance by a player of a lower level than the raid party can cause a wipeout. Setting a level minimum on a Guild Raid is the responsibility of the Raid Leader, and will only be used in circumstances where it would be dangerous to have an open invitation. For Planes raids, Guild Members should always be at least one yellow bubble (20%) into Level 46 before they can attend.


9. All members are responsible for themselves, their actions and ultimately, their happiness within the guild. Guild leaders try very hard to make this a fair, enjoyable environment for all our members, but we will never be able to meet everyone’s needs and wants. If you find yourself unhappy with guild policies, find that we just don’t raid enough for you, or we play at times when you just aren’t available - maybe this is not the guild for you. If guild leadership can’t offer suggestions that help or can’t reasonably accommodate your schedule, maybe it is time to /guild remove and hunt for a guild that can. Constant complaining and dissention in guild chat won’t be tolerated. We will always wish you well in your endeavors and hope that you find a guild that can better suit your needs.

Any questions, concerns, or ideas can always be taken to an officer or the guild leader (mark@weber.org).
