Loot Rules
These are the rules. We expect everyone wearing the GotCR guild tag to be familiar with them and uphold the image and reputation we are proud of. Anyone who chooses to ignore the rules, or exhibit behavior unbecoming to the guild will be removed post haste.

EPIC QUEST PIECES

When a piece to an epic quest drops on a guild raid, it belongs to the attending character currently furthest along in their epic quest (unless the raid was specifically set and announced as being for someone else). If two Guardians are at the same stage it will be lottoed between them. If it’s possible for the piece to be multiquested, and no MAINS present need it, it will be put into the Guild Bank for a non-present guildmember. If the piece cannot be multiquested it will be offered in this order: to present guild alts, to guests on the raid, then shouted to the zone. Recently tagged Guardians can expect to wait two months before receiving looting rights on epic quest pieces.

LOOT RULES

These are the set rules for any GOTCR Guild Raid. They may be adapted as necessary by the Raid Leader, but in every case it’s important to make the loot rules broadcast and advertised both in your post and before the raid begins. GOTCR subscribes fully to the “Need Before Greed” slogan. When an item drops, it will be lottoed among the classes who can use it, and any raid present main character who can use it as part of their everyday armor or weapons set will roll. If no one needs it as part of their everyday gear, but can use it for resist gear, they will roll next. If no main character can use it at all, then present alts will be allowed to roll. If absolutely no one present can use it, it’ll go to the guild vault.

SPELL LOOT RULES

With the abundance of expensive spell drops, it’s vital we all understand the rules concerning scroll lottos on guild raids. A scroll drop will be lottoed among present mains who can scribe the spell right away. If there are no present mains who can scribe the spell, it will be offered to main characters with two levels of scribing it. If no present characters fit either criteria, the scroll will go to the guild bank.

Any questions, concerns, or ideas can always be taken to an officer or the guild leader (mark@weber.org).
