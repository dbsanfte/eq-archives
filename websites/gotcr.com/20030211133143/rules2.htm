Multiple Mains
These are the rules. We expect everyone wearing the GotCR guild tag to be familiar with them and uphold the image and reputation we are proud of. Anyone who chooses to ignore the rules, or exhibit behavior unbecoming to the guild will be removed post haste.

If you split your time between multiple characters you either have “Alt Characters” or “Multiple Mains”. The difference depends on your division of time and experience.

If you have characters above level 50 and WITHIN TWO LEVELS OF EACH OTHER, you have Multiple Mains.

Players with Multiple Mains will be allowed to bring either to a guild raid and to participate in loot lottos. If the raid leader of guild leader requires the attendance of one of your characters over another (like Trekkdala over Crimthann or Doppler over Chikote for pure example) you are expected to bring the requested character to help insure the success of the guild raid.

Each player is still required to have a Primary character – that’s the one listed on the official roster and the one who can expect unquestioned guild help towards his or her epic quest. Each player is also required to list their Multiple Mains on the Multiple Main thread on the Alternate Personalities board.

“2-boxers” are welcome to attend any raid and to enjoy the full backing of the rest of the guild, as long as running two characters simultaneously does not hamper the raid’s effectiveness. Players who CHOOSE to “2-box” must post on the web page PRIOR to the raid stating which character will be eligible for lottos that night. I acknowledge and respect our handful of “2-boxers” and hope this will facilitate their inclusion at guild raids while leaving everyone with the same fair chance at any items that drop during our events.

Any questions, concerns, or ideas can always be taken to an officer or the guild leader (mark@weber.org).
