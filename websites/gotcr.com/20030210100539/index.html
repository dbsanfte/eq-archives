February 5, 2003  � Burning Woods

Ixiblat Fer Times Two!

 

We had assembled at chardok for the schedule Royals Raid. It was quickly seen that we didn't have sufficient forces 

for the Royals so Chardok was called off. That is when someone informed us that Legion of the White Rose was 

doing Ixiblat Fer for Kendricke and could use help. After a while setting up, Ixiblat #1 goes down. Grats Kendricke! 

Then after some people left those that remained reformed to take down Ixiblat #2 for Neelyx. Grats Neelyx!

 

Side Note: Druids are very underestimated...we only had 2 clerics left for 2nd attempt so we had the 

druids all throwing fast heals on Kyrelth. I cast Complete Heal once, then watch and never bothered to 

cast again as he simply wasn't being hurt. To paraphrase Kyrelth: "I was Complete Healed for 173 pts."
