October 24, 2003 · Plane of Fear

One Down, Many to go...

 


 

Friday night was winding down before Madkanki offered his weather forecast - seems it was the 

perfect temperature to kill a GOD. The Legion of the White Rose joined in (along with some other 

riends) and within the hour Cazic Thule was defeated and GOTCR had their first (assisted) 

GOD KILL. Kudos to Madkanki the patient for assembling and spearheading the raid, and congrats 

to all who came, and who worked so hard to make this sort of raid possible. Magworth won a 

Bile Etched Obsidian Choker and Seracid took home a Robe of Inspiration. Once again, it was a 

great day to be a Guardian!

September 13, 2003 · The Hole


The Hole Shooting Match

 

 

On Saturday, Hungaz turned the Hole Zone into his own personal bowling alley - notching 

strike after strike and spearheading a Guardian assault to reach Jaeil the Wretched. The rules 

have changed for Jaeil, allowing just one Ranger at a time to fulfill that step in the epic quest.

spirit in deferring to his brother Ranger. Thanks to all that attended!
