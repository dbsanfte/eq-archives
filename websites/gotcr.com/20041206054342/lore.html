The Crystal Rose and its Guardians
Not long after Veeshan, Crystalline Dragon and ruler of the Plane of Sky deposited her blood onto the world of Norrath, staking her claim and thereby beginning the Age of Scale, it came to pass that Brell Serilis, Duke of Underfoot, approached The Great Mother, Tunare, and Prexus, The Ocean Lord, and Rallos Zek, The Warlord, to form an alliance to keep the Wurmqueen in check. Each of these gods would create a race of beings to watch over Norrath and keep a vigilant eye on Dragon kind. All, save for Rallos Zek agreed, and he abruptly departed the conversation. With the agreement struck, Brell Serilis reached into his pocket and withdrew a shard of crystal from his labyrinthine chamber of mystical Living Stone. Its shape appeared to resemble a flower bud and his touch caused it to glowed with a ruby light. He passed it to Tunare, whose own magicks caused the rock to glow with a molten emerald light, unfurling its petals into a perfect rose. She passed it to Prexus, whose glowing sapphire-hued touch cooled the blossom in its crystalline form. The Rose was then entrusted to Tunare to find a secure location to house the precious stone. And thus the first pact between the gods had been struck and so began the dawn of the Elder Age.

        The release of such magical energies involved in seeding planets with life soon attracted the attentions of even more gods. These oftentimes petty and jealous gods decided to leave their marks on Norrath by creating their own races. Soon, many of them learned of the Crystal Rose's existence and the pact that had been made. Among the first was Innoruuk, Prince of Hate. He learned of the pact and cursed his fellow gods for excluding him. Yet several others, in turn, sought to join the alliance and to endow the Rose with their own touch. Karana, the Rainkeeper, bathed the stone in an aquamarine light. The touch of Rodcet Nife, the Prime Healer, cause the crystal to glow with amber hue. And the magicks of Quellious radiated through the gem with a topaz brilliance.

        Unbeknownst to them all, a second pact was then struck between Brell Serilis and Fizzlethorpe Bristlebane, King of Thieves, and Cazic-Thule, Lord of Fear. Even Rallos Zek was convinced to join the dark pact this time. And thus the die was cast and the seeds of deception were sown.

        In due time, Rallos Zek created armies of Ogres, Orcs and Goblins and led them in invasion against the lands of the greater gods, in part to uncover the hidden location of the coveted Crystal Rose. Learning of Brell's duplicity, the others of the Alliance of Light formed their own legion of handpicked followers to protect the valued object; and these individuals were dubbed The Guardians of the Crystal Rose. The war waged on between the gods and with their followers alike; yet the Rose remained hidden and safe. Eventually, the greater gods forced Rallos Zek back to the Planes of Power. And thus began the Age of Monuments.

        Soon the Marr twins, Gods of Valor and Love, created their own race and joined the alliance with the other forces of good; endowing the Rose with their magical energies of indigo and citrine hues. And the Age of Blood was born. In turn, Solusek Ro, Lord of Fire, joined the Dark Circle and soon overtook the vast forests with his desert curse. The period which followed became known as the Lost Age.

        This next period in Norrathean history is the least known. It is surmised by most knowledgeable historians that the elder gods regrouped and reestablished themselves. Ruins litter the lands from Odus to Faydwer and very little history of this period remains. Yet even fewer mortals know of the immortal activities which took place to caused such events to transpire.

        For during this Lost Age, the hidden location of the Temple of the Crystal Rose was uncovered. The Guardians were slain and the crystal had disappeared. Yet little was know of what had transpired until Innoruuk and Brell Serilis approached their fellow gods. It seems that the dark conspirators had enlisted the aide of Bertoxxulous, the Plaguebringer, in claiming the stone for themselves. Realizing the error of their ways, Innoruuk and Brell tried to assist the Guardians, but alas the followers of light were no match for the forces mustered by the dark alliance, and they fell. Yet when Bertoxxulous' minions entered the rubble to retrieve the Crystal Rose, they found it was already gone. With haste, Innoruuk and Brell Serilius sought out their fellow gods to right the great wrong. For here too, the truth was finally learned. Not only had Innoruuk cursed his fellow gods for their disregard of him in the original pact, but he had cursed their jeweled creation as well. Bathing it in his own dark amethyst magicks, his curse was that this crystalline rose needed to be eternally surrounded by those who sought to protect it. Should that not come to pass, the gem would gradually lose the light that was endowed in it by the gods and would eventually implode. He continued to say that if the Rose were not recovered, all of Norrath would be destroyed by such a magical disruption.

        Realizing the severity of the situation, the gods once again joined forces, this time with the aide of Innoruuk and Brell Serilis, in an attempt to reclaim the Crystal Rose. Each sought out the bravest and most honorable followers and appeared to them in a dream; enlisting them to reform The Guardians of The Crystal Rose, to search for the prized possession. And so it came to pass that through the Age of Enlightenment and into the current Age of Turmoil, the war between the Alliance of Light and the Dark Circle raged on. Prexus, the Ocean Lord, seemed to all but vanish from involvement in the affair. Homelands, like those of the reptilian Iksar, began disappeared from the maps of mankind; lost in the memory of most until now. Yet one question always remained. Where was the Crystal Rose?

        And thus, the true course of the Guardians of the Crystal Rose was set in motion. Their mission became to seek out and destroy the sources of evil that still seeks to possess the shining gem which embodies the light of the gods. And in the course of time, perhaps they will uncover the final answers to the Rose's location, and the identity of whomever took it that fateful night. For the wounded heart of the universe...this land called Norrath...this world of the Crystal Rose...shall one day be mended and the shadow of true evil will never be seen again ... the forces of evil and good, of light and darkness will coexist in perfect balance.

And in that, these valiant individuals truly are... The Guardians of The Crystal Rose.
