Regrettably, the Kinship of Valor Guild disbanded July 27th, 2000.  KoV was one of the greatest and oldest Guilds on the Karana server and I will miss all of the Members immensely.  KoV Members are still out there in the lands of Norrath fighting against the injustices of the world and bringing balance and harmony to all.  However this time, instead of being in the KoV family, they are benefiting another family with their loyalty, kindness and valor.  Some new homes for KoV Guild Members are Allied Giants, Phoenix Arisen and the Empyrean Army.

The Members of the Kinship will always live in my heart....and who knows...one day KoV may rise again.

Empress Sareana Empyrean
Kinship of Valor Guild Leader
