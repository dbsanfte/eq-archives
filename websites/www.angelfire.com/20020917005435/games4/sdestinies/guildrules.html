Guild Rules

This is a listing of the guild "rules" that Shattered Destinies follows. We don't believe in Draconian rules - after all this is a game. But we do believe that in order to maximize everyone's enjoyment a few guidelines are needed. The rules posted below are in effective as of August 13, 2002 and have been drafted by the officers of Shattered Destinies and read/acknowledged by guild members.

1. Recruitment: The level limit will be 35, until October 1, at which time it will be moved to 40. ONLY exceptions will be RL friends/family who can be accepted at any level but must be /role until they hit the required level limit. Online friends must wait until they hit the level limit to be guilded.

2. Alternates: Main characters may have their alternate characters join SD. All alternates under 35 will be /role until they hit the required level limit. No exceptions. It is the policy of SD that if the main is removed for any reason, that the alts must be removed also.

3. Chat Channels: Guildchat (and /ooc & certainly /shout and any UoH chat) is not the place for profanity or discussions of your favorite porn sites. If you want to have such a discussion, create your own chat channel using the "/join" command. Let's keep guildchat at a PG rating please.

4. Item Camping: Initially a guild member should make a post on the message boards giving around 5 days notice of any camp. This may be followed by requests in guildsay. In any case the item must be one that is suitable for your level, one that you could normally obtain in a group. The point of our involvement is purely to save you having to roll on it.

5. Courtesy: We (the officers of SD) expect at all times that guild members show great courtesy and respect to all players. In other words treat others as you yourself would wish to be treated. This extends outside of the guild as well as internally. Actions such as ninja looting and kill stealing are not welcome in the guild. We pride ourselves on our reputation and all members should strive to uphold our name.

6. Porting: If any member is in need of a port they should request ONCE, over guildsay only, for a port. Anyone who is available and happy to port should then respond. Please do not send direct tells or spam repeatedly. Also be advised that in general porting should only be requested to avoid awkward journeys such as boat rides and corpse retrievals - i.e. - asking for a port from West Commons to Southern RO is, in my mind, unnecessary. Remember these people play for their entertainment too and constant interruptions for porting can become wearisome.

7. Respect others decisions: If you ask for anything and are politely turned down, this does not necessarily mean that the person involved is being unreasonable. If you honestly feel that cooperation is being unnecessarily withheld, then contact an officer or the guild leader and discuss it. In any case an officers decision will always get the guild leaders full support and should be considered final.

8. Officers: Please feel free to contact any officer for any requests, advice or arbitration. I (as guild leader) have full confidence in each and every one of them and will wholeheartedly support their decisions.

9. Anonymous: Anyone who wishes to hide their class or level should use /role instead of /anon. This is purely out of respect for the guild as /anon hides any association with guilds. We would ask that guild members always show their affiliation with Shattered Destinies.

10. Disregarding of rules: In instances where rules and/or policies are found to be disregarded by a member of Shattered Destinies, disciplinary action will be enforced on a case by case basis. This may include anything and everything up to your permanent removal from the guild. The guild officers will review each case as it happens and depending on the severity of the situation, will determine what action, if any, needs to be taken. Our goal is to maintain Shattered Destinies' reputation as an honorable group of fun loving people.

The list of officers are as follows: Sirq (guild leader), Terzik, Jerle, Foodawg, Garibaldi, Kinesis, Bealonna, Machalek, Yendatin, Lowon, Krioni, Lowthar and Vareth.
