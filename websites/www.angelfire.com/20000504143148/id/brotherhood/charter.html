The Brotherhood Charter
Visari founded The Brotherhood on March 26, 1999. 
The Brotherhood is not yet an Official EQ Guild.

Guild History

 The Brotherhood has long been a secret society. An underground group of individuals that share a desire to rid the lands of evil by whatever means available.
 The Brotherhood  is now ready to surface as and retake these lands where vile creatures now roam...

Guild Policy/Goals

The Brotherhood is a group of brave adventurers from all the lands of Norrath who have abandoned their racial prejudices for the common good of all of Norrath. 

The Brotherhood is a group that will seek out and defeat the most vile of all foul creatures inhabiting the lands of Norrath. 

The Brotherhood is a guild that will aid the weak, confront the wicked, and accept only honorable players as members. 

The Brotherhood is a multi-racial, by invitation only guild that is formed for the brotherhood of all Norrath inhabitants. A guild that has a prime goal of uniting all the races of Norrath. 

Guild Structure

The Brotherhood is an organization run by its own members. However to address the need for some type of authority and responsibilty of delicate issues within the guild,  the Council was formed.
The Council consists of members whos dedication toward The Brotherhood drives them to make The Brotherhood the best it can be. They together to maintain the website as well as the order in the guild.

Guild Membership

Currently The Brotherhood is not recruiting Members. This is an Invatation only guild. Invitations are generally delivered in Game.
