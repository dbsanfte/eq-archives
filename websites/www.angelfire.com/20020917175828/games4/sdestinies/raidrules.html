Raid Rules

The raid rules are still being worked on, but as of August 13th, the following rules have been accepted by the officers/members of Shattered Destinies.

All raids will have a goal in mind, specific loot, experience, etc. Aside from those specific goals, a general goal for all raids is that we all have fun, and hopefully as many people as possible get something positive out of the raid experience. The following raid loot guidelines are presented in order to hopefully meet this general goal. Note that epic drops should be considered completely separate from this loot system, and distribution of those are decided upon by the Raid Leader and Epic Leader.

Drops will be rolled upon and distributed in the following order using the follow guidelines:

During the raid:

NO DROP Items - Those attending the raid who will USE the dropped item on their character participating in the raid will be allowed to roll on the item. On lower level raids, if no character participating in the raid will use the item, then the item may be rolled on and used by an alternate character, are allowed to roll if they will use. If no one will use the item, the item's location should be /shouted to the zone. Once you have won a NO DROP item, you will not be allowed to enter a roll on other no drop items unless 1) no one else will use it, or 2) all those eligible have already won a no drop item. Breastplate pieces will be open roll by the class that can use them, otherwise open to all.

When you win a roll on an upgrade item, if the item you are replacing is NOT NO DROP, it is suggested (but not required) that you offer up that item for roll by those who can use it as an upgrade to their equipment. If no one at the raid wants the item as an upgrade, then the item is considered an open roll item (see below). If you don't want to give up the item you are replacing (perhaps due to resists, situational use, RPing, etc.) it would be a nice gesture to give up something else for roll by others, perhaps even some pp if there's nothing you can spare. This should be considered optional, but would certainly go a long way in helping to improve our guild as a whole, and would also increase the number of folks who finish up the raid with some loot.

At the end of the raid:

Cash, gems, & crap weapons/drops worth less than 100pp - goes to the guild bank to fund the cost of raids, spell components, coffins, etc.

Droppable Quest Gems/Items - goes to bank until someone needs

Regent Costs - will be covered for all casters that use regents (ie clerics for buffs, wizards for tl shards)

Droppable equipment upgrades (rolled on from most valuable/desired to least valuable/desired) - Those attending the raid who WILL USE the dropped item on THAT character will be allowed to roll on the item. On lower level raids, if no participating character will use the item, then it is allowed to rull for alts are allowed to roll if they will use. If no one will actually USE the item, the item is considered an "open roll item". Once you have won a droppable equipment upgrade item, you will not be allowed to enter any other rolls unless 1) no one else at the raid will USE the item in question, or 2) all those eligible have already won a droppable item. The same suggestions regarding passing on the item you are replacing also apply here as they were described earlier.

Open Roll Items - Items that will not be USED by anyone in the raiding party as well as gems worth more than 100pp, will be rolled on by those at the raid who have not already won something (excluding epic items). Again, these will be rolled on from most valuable/desired to least valuable/desired.

[NOTE: it has also been suggested that the first "open roll" item default to the bank...or that the bank gets a chance to roll in the open roll. these are two more options to think about.]
