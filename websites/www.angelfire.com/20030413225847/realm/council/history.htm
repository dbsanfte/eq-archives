The Council Reborn

A New Light Shines Across Norrath

Many years ago two of the five Wizards that were part of the Council of Istari traveled far away. They were known as Ithryn Luin (Blue Wizards). Their names were Alatar and Pallando. They were never heard from again.

Recently a young lady named Sirus made a grueling journey to Norrath with a mission given to her by her father, Pallando the Blue Wizard, to follow the ways of the Council of Istari.

Upon arriving in Norrath she discovered that the Council was no more. After discovering this she knew she had to do something. She had promised her father she would continue his battle against darkness. So she began to assemble a small but dedicated group and teach them the ways of the Council. Together they seek to bring honor to the Council of Istari and be a beacon light in the crusade against the evil creatures of Norrath.

Sirus began to learn that some of the members from the old council were still out there somewhere in Norrath. Realizing she and her few members are young and very week in their abilities she begins her journey into Norrath to seek the Wisdom of those who were before her.  

 

The Disappearance of a Leader

 As Sirus has been gathering forces in preparation for the unknown evils at work in West Commons, she suddenly vanishes.  A young paladin by the name of Galdor located her corpse and brought Andromada to it.  Andromada attempted to revive her.  As she was reviving her an evil force began to pull Sirus away.  It was to great a power for Andromada.  Sirus immediately handed the guild over to Andromada before she was pulled away.  After investigation by Andromada, she determined that the evil creatures of Najena that were residing in Befallen were responsible for this treachery.

As Andromada was preparing for an attack on Befallen it seems that the forces have fallen back to Najena.
