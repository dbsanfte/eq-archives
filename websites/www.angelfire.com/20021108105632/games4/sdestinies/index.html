Welcome

Welcome to the Shattered Destinies webpage. We are a guild on the Everquest Seventh Hammer server. We hope that you enjoy the site and hope that if you have any questions, you will visit our forums.

The members of Shattered Destinies have come to realize that the selfish egotistical goals of an individual are not what makes one great, or remembered. We seek only to find the true purpose of our lives, not to make our lives more than they are ordained to be. So strong is the drive to find our eventual destiny, that we receive as much satisfaction from helping others along the path as they would themselves. For we know that our path will cross many others, and no one path is more important than the other. They merely lead to different destinations.

The members of Shattered Destinies consider themselves to be kind, considerate, and unselfish. When we get online, we have fun, talk to friends, maybe hunt with them. When we get into trouble or have a stupid question, we know that we have friends that we can count on for an answer or help. While we may like to get experience and loot, it's not really our primary goal, in fact our biggest goal is to have fun.

Our main goals for our guild, are as follows:

1) Be kind, considerate, and unselfish, bring honor to the Shattered Destinies name. Let actions speak louder than /shouts.
2) Recruit others who share the characterstics of #1.
3) See number 1 & 2.
4) If it happens that in doing 1 & 2 we become uber...then that's nice too, but certainly not required.
