December 3, 2002

The guild planned a nice surprise for when Sirq came back from the Thanksgiving Holidays. Sirq had recently reached level 60 and as a chanter we all know what that means, KEI. KEI is a pretty hard spell to come by and he had left money with one of the officers to purchase if it came up in the bazaar. It became available, and the guild jumped at donating their own money as the officer was not online. Sirq was lured to Thurg after he logged back in, and unbeknownst to him, part of the guild was waiting in the zone to surprise him. This was a very moving moment for SD, and we are all very proud of the fact that we could do this for Sirq, as he is what holds the guild together.

As a guild we have been really working on exping so that we can get up in levels and accomplish some of the things that all guilds would like to do, such as Dragon raids and ToV. Keep a close eye on the board to see what is going on soon; upcoming events include Dragon Necropolis, Practice Raid in the Hole, Cazic Thule, Hate, and a few raids with UoH.

We are requesting that all guild members post their email and their equipment on the message board so that we can better determine what needs to be upgraded and have the ability to send out event emails when a new event is added. This is required, so please post as soon as possible.
