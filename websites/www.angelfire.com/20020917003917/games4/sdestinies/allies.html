Alliances

Shattered Destinies is part of two different alliances, one is the Union of Heroes (UoH) and the other is the Elders of Honor (EoH). The purposes of these alliances is to provide Shattered Destinies and their alliance members a chance to do the things that they might not accomplish otherwise. Each alliance does has it separate message board that is linked below:
