Image Upload

Use the following form to browse and upload your images. Please only upload JPG and GIF images, and try not to make them too big! You must be a member to upload images here.

Use the Browse button to select the image you wish to upload. Then enter a Title (max 60 characters) and a Caption (max 255 characters). Next enter your username and password for authentication. When done, click on "Upload!" to add your image to the database. After you press "Upload!" it may take a few minutes to upload the image, so please be patient and don't hit upload more than once.
