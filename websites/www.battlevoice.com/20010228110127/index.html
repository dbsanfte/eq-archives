We are a group of software developers and gamers who have decided to make a product to serve the EverQuest community.  This product is called BattleVoice.

BattleVoice is a highly customizable and powerful voice recognition engine to be used while playing EverQuest.  When you are in that tough fight with your group and are near death, do you want to take the time to type out "I need a heal!"?  Well with BattleVoice, you just say what you want into your microphone and it shows up in your chat screen.  Need a buff?  Need to tell everyone to run or gate?  It can all be handled with your voice and a microphone.  No typing required.

The beauty of BattleVoice is that you customize your own lists of commands and phrases.  Going on a raid?  Think about all the things you need to say, add them to your phrase list and you're done.  Log in to EQ and say your phrases and they are typed right in your chat screen for you. 

The BattleVoice interface makes it easy for you to build a list of phrases to use.  BattleVoice already has lists of commonly used phrases that you can edit, as well as word lists of all EQ mobs by zone, and lists of spells and skills for your class that you can use. 

BattleVoice can be a life saver during combat, saving you critical time from typing, and making life much easier for the puller or group leader who needs to shout out commands.  Free up your hands to handle the important stuff, like movement, casting and attacks.  Let BattleVoice be that extra set of hands you always feel you need at those critical moments.
