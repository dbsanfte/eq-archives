THE PURPOSE OF BATTLE VOICE

BattleVoice was designed to allow EverQuest users to use voice recognition to display text in the chat screen in order to eliminate the need for typing.  We are gamers ourselves and all play EverQuest.  As we played we recognized the need for speech recognition in a MMORPG such as this.  There is just too much to do during combat.  You have to control your characters movements, attacks and spell casting, all while trying to communicate with the people you are playing with.  BattleVoice was designed to allow you to communicate with your group during combat without using your hands!

HOW IT WORKS

BattleVoice was designed using a command recognition system as opposed to general English recognition.  What this means is that BattleVoice will recognize the specific phrases in its dictionary once spoken into the microphone.  If a phrase spoken into the microphone is not found in BattleVoice's dictionary, then nothing will be typed in the chat screen.  Using a command based system allows for much more accurate and much faster speech recognition and also allows for the ability to say words and phrases that are common to EverQuest but not necessarily common  to the general public.

CUSTOMIZATION

BattleVoice was designed from the beginning to be completely customizable.   When you purchase BattleVoice, there will be a dictionary already included which has a general phrase as well as word lists of all the mobs in EQ by zone and lists of spells and skills used by each class. The general phrase list contains hundreds of commonly used phrases like:  "I need a heal", "run to the zone" and "incoming!!".  With BattleVoice, you, as the user, can edit these phrases and also create your own. 

WORD AND PHRASE LISTS

BattleVoice includes a general phrase list which can contain all the phrases you want to use throughout the game.  You also have the option of creating zone specific phrase lists.  When you enter a specific zone you can say "Load Unrest" for example, and your phrase list specific to that zone will be loaded as well as all the specific mobs that live in that zone, so that you can use those mob names in your phrases.  Phrases can be simple like "I need a heal", or they can make use of word lists that are included with BattleVoice or that you create yourself.   A phrase that uses a word list would look like this:  "I need a <buffspell>".  A phrase like this will allow you to use the <buffspell> word list to have one phrase recognize a multitude of commands.  With this one phrase, BattleVoice will recognize when you say "I need a heal" or "I need a clarity" or any other <buffspell> with only one phrase in the phrase list.  BattleVoice includes word lists for all the spells in the game as well as character skills, mobs for each zone, and other common actions or commands.

BATTLE VOICE CHAT AND ZONE COMMANDS

BattleVoice has a list of its own commands that allow you to use all of the different chat modes for EQ such as group and guild say.  BattleVoice also has its own built in commands that allow you to turn it on and off by saying "listen" and "stop listening" as well as load specific phrases for the zone you are in such as "load unrest" or "load east commons".  We have also added a "command mode" command which allows you to take advantage of the newly added features such as /cast # or /target npc.

TEXT REPLACEMENT

BattleVoice also allows for text replacement from what you say, and what appears on the chat screen.  You can program a command "cast root" and it could type "/cast 1" and allow you to cast the root spell in your first spell slot.  You could also use this feature to role play your character.  If you are a troll or ogre that likes to role play, program the command "I need a heal" to type out "ME NEEDUM HEAL!!" and that is what will appear in the chat screen.

SYSTEM REQUIREMENTS

Minimum System Requirements: Pentium II - 300 MHz, 96 MB RAM, (real) full duplex Sound Card (SB Live!)

Recommended System Requirements: Pentium III - 500 MHz, 128 MB RAM

BattleVoice will run smoothly without adversely affecting the performance of the game when using the recommended system requirements.

GAME COMPATIBILITY

Currently, BattleVoice is designed to run with EverQuest only.  In the future we hope to provide dictionaries for other games like: Anarchy Online, Shadowbane, and more!
