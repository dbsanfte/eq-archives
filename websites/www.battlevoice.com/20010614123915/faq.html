What is BattleVoice?

BattleVoice allows you:

1) To communicate to other players in Everquest using speech-to-text recognition, or

2) To perform actions using the command shortcuts.

By setting up phrases before play using BattleVoice, text phrases will be typed out allowing you to continue to react to the situation. For example, the puller has pulled too many MOBs and the party looks like it may die. You, as leader, need to try to help your group mates (casting spells, taunting, etc.) while issuing instructions to them. With BattleVoice, you can say, “Run to Zone!” and it will automatically type that out to groupsay because you’ve set that up prior to playing.

If you choose to use command mode, verbal commands will make your character perform actions. For example, if you have your Ice Comet spell memorized in the first spell gem, you can set up the command “Cast Ice Comet” to enter the command “/cast 1” or use “Speak Erudite” to enter the command “/language 3”.

Who is BattleVoice?

BattleVoice is a group of programmers that work at a small software development company located near Columbus, Ohio.  We played Everquest and loved it; playing at the office, we had the luxury of screaming down the hall to each other when we had to.  Many times the communication made the difference between life and death.  At home, we did not have this luxury, so we created BattleVoice in our spare time.

What’s the difference between BattleVoice and my hot keys?

Hot keys also allow you to type canned phrases or commands at the click of a button. However, there is a very small amount of phrases and commands that can be associated with hot keys. With BattleVoice’s vocabulary building technology, you can issue commands that include zone specific MOBs. For example, you may say, “Taunt this barbed boned off me” or “Taunt this ghoul off me” or “Taunt this death beetle off me”. Instead of having three hot keys, BattleVoice can use one phrase with the option of inserting any MOB from Unrest. 

How do the word lists work? 

Word Lists are listings of words that can be substituted into a phrase. For example, instead of entering: 

“Lost strength buff”, “Lost quickness buff”, “Lost regeneration buff”, etc., BattleVoice Phrase builder allows you to enter:

Lost <buffspell> buff

where <buffspell> can be a list of all the buff spells you can think of. In addition, this <buffspell> word list can be used in any other phrase you create.

Is this considered a cheat or hack?

No, BattleVoice allows recognition and typing to the various communication and command modes present in EverQuest. It does not allow the repeated pressing of hotkeys, etc. like a keyboard macro. It does not invade the EverQuest program at all. It simply listens to your speech and types out what it recognizes to the EverQuest chat window. This is not a cheat; BattleVoice only allows you to “type” as quickly as you can speak.

Can I get rid of my mouse and keyboard?

No. BattleVoice does not replace the commands and actions of EverQuest. Fighting, moving, etc. are still done using your keyboard and mouse. You will be able to speak commands and warnings as shouts, groupsay, etc. as you use your keyboard and mouse.

How complicated is the training? Can I just install and go?

The training isn’t that hard to do and doesn’t take too much time. After installation, you will be guided through the training. We recommend you follow this completely to help BattleVoice recognize your speech patterns and speaking environment for better accuracy. However, if you choose to skip the training, accuracy should be at an acceptable level as long as you speak slowly and clearly.

What are the requirements for BattleVoice? Will it slow down my game performance?

BattleVoice will run on a Pentium II-300 MHz computer or faster with at least 96 MB of RAM. In addition, you need to have a fully functional full duplex sound card that allows you to record and play at the sample rate; SB Live! works well while older models that advertise full duplex are really not. BattleVoice was designed to not affect your gaming performance. If you’re presently running the Kunark expansion, BattleVoice should work very well.

How do I increase the word recognition?

The easiest way to increase word recognition is to fully complete the training provided; both microphone and voice training are available. Also, you have the option to control the speech engine accuracy but will increase the recognition time.

Another way to increase accuracy is to remove phrases from your phrase list that you do not use. The smaller the phrase list results in better accuracy. The phrase list provided as a default has been tested and proven to be very accurate; however, if you feel there are many phrases you will never use, we encourage you to create your own customized phases and wordlists.
