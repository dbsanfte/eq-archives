THE DIFFERENCE IS QUALITY AND COST

The main difference between the voice recognition engine that EverQuest has implemented in the game is their use of General English verses a Command based system like the one in BattleVoice.  Because they made this choice, you don't have the ability to customize the VR engine to fit your needs, and the quality, speed, and accuracy of recognition is much lower than that with a system like BattleVoice.

The second most important reason is cost. You need to purchase a voice recognition engine before you can use the system in EverQuest. Buying the required voice engine will cost you $50.00 to $100. BattleVoice has the voice recognition engine already included, and for a much lower price.

THE PROBLEMS WITH GENERAL ENGLISH RECOGNITION

General English recognition engines are designed to recognize normal everyday speaking, and they don't even do that well.  Imagine this engine trying to understand you when you are talking about "the ghouls in unrest".  It will probably interpret that as "the girls in Budapest!".  You can add word lists to this engine, but these lists will ultimately pollute the language model even further and decrease performance of the game and of the speech recognition.

General English voice recognition engines are available for the general public to be used with word processing applications and operating systems, but they have not caught on.  The reason:  they just don't work well.

THE BENEFITS OF USING A COMMAND BASED SYSTEM

By using a command based system, the recognition speed and accuracy is maximized.  If BattleVoice doesn't find the phrase you speak into the microphone in its dictionary, it doesn't type anything.  With general English, something will always be typed, and probably not what you intended.

The BattleVoice command system is completely customizable.  You can edit the phrases included with BattleVoice or you can make your own lists of phrases, for use all over the game, or for specific zones.  You can use text replacement to role play or use game features.  You say "cast root" and BattleVoice could type "/cast 1" and activate that first spell slot. Anything that you want typed in the chat screen, whether it is a phrase in any of the chat modes or a 'slash command' that is part of the game, BattleVoice allows you to do it.

WHY CHOOSE BATTLE VOICE?

BattleVoice was designed to give the players what they want and really need.  Customization, accuracy, and speed.  What a player needs is to have those key statements and phrases like "I need a heal" or "Run to the zone" or "Incoming red mob" to be typed out for them, quickly and accurately just by saying them. That is what the EverQuest system can't do, and what BattleVoice was built to do.
