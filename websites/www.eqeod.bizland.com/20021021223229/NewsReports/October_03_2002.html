October 03, 2002

     Once again, the web site has undergone reconstruction. The new format will allow more information to be shown for the news updates. Hopefully, we will have more screenshots to post with news updates, than previously (limited, to save load times).

     The new web site is also now being hosted on 3 sites. Preiously we had 2 (the original Bizland site, and a mirror hosted by Starigon/Plexi). Due to the annoyance of pop-up adds, and the cost to have those removed was not something I am willing to pay for, I have pestered Xingtau to host a mirror on his server. This site may load a little slower, and may have more down time than Bizland, BUT it is free from pop-up adds (the link will be posted in the forums for guildmates only. The rest of the world will suffer the pop-ups for now. I will re-post some of the old news archives later.

     Xingtau is also working on getting some BBS software to work on his server. We are going to test out the software for a bit, to see if we can move off of ezboards (course, we still need to get approval of the Officers). That will give us greater control over the forums, better security, and NO pop-up adds.

     Please let me know what you think of the revised format for the site. If you have any Screenshots, news, updates, comments or suggestions, feel free to email me any time (email link is at the bottom of every page.
