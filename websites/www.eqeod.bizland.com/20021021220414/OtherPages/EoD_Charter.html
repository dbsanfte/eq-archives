ARTICLE I (ELIGIBILITY)
Essence of Darkness will not be race, class or religion specific. Members should be at least level 52 and preferably a master in some trade skill, but at all times acceptance will be based the views of the guild about the individual. A guild member must sponsor a person into the guild, and then current members online at the time will give their views of the person. If one EoD member disapproves of the person then the person will be denied membership. Real life friends, family and alternate characters of current guild members will be invited into the guild as long as they are level 52+. Those real life friends under level 52 will be referred to Dark Conscripts of Essence guild. Dark Conscripts of Essence member who reach level 52 will automatically be invited into EoD without prior approval of current EoD members. Members who have left the guild will not be invited back into EoD unless extenuating circumstances exist. All new members will be on a probation period for 2 weeks and may be removed by any officer if the values and goals of the new member are not consistent with those of EoD.

ARTICLE II (OFFICERS)
Any member of EoD who is nominated to be an officer by a current officer will be discussed in privacy among the existing officers. If any current officer disapproves the promotion then the nominated member will not be promoted. A reasonable amount of time for discussion will be given, but normally will not exceed 1 week. In the event of a guild merger, the leader of the merging guild will retain officer status. For every 10 members above level 52 of the merging guild the merging Guild Leader may appoint an officer.

ARTICLE III (REMOVAL)
It is inevitable that with so many members from different cultures and countries around the world that personality conflicts will result. At no time should a member be removed from the guild simply because an officer "doesn't like the member." In most situations prior to guild removal the member will be warned that some action he/she has done may be grounds for removal. This warning may be done by any officer having knowledge of an offense using a direct tell to the member. 3 officers must be in agreement before a member will be removed from the guild and the guild leader and 2 officers must be in agreement before and officer is removed from the guild. Offenses that are grounds for removal are, but not limited to: Taking NBG items while on a guild raid solely for the purpose of later selling Arguing with a guild mate in GUILD TALK Attempting to organize a mutiny KS'ing of a guild mate Repeated begging of a guild mate or non-guild mate Harassment of a guild mate to include harassment based on sex, rl race, rl religion or sexual orientation. Repeated harassment of a non-guild mate (except during times of war) Fraud to a guild mate Excessive use of bad language Our name implies that we have a dark nature to us and good role-play is always encouraged, but at no time should a member violate the norms of the Norrath community. Members that have been removed from the guild under circumstance outlined in this article will not be invited back into the guild.

ARTICLE IV (ANONYMOUS AND ROLE-PLAY)
Members above level 52 should not use anon. If it is essential for a members race, class or level to be unknown then role-play will be used. Exceptions would be during times of war or special missions, which will be coordinated through an EoD officer.

ARTICLE V (RAIDS)
Raid ideas will be proposed by an officer and officers will discuss various location and vote on the raids for the following month that will most benefit the guild. All members who are on during the times of the raid and meet minimum level requirements for the raid are strongly encouraged to attend. This is the one day of the week when we can all get together for a common cause. Many times there will not be experience to be had by all members, but senior members of the guild should still attend and assist our younger members. The raids date and time will be posted and announced preferably 2 weeks prior to the day of the raid.

ARTICLE VI (INACTIVE MEMBERS)
A member will be considered inactive if he/she has not been seen for 2 weeks unless prior knowledge has been stated. If the member is an officer his/her officer status may or may not be retained. Inactive members and officers will retain their current position if they return at a later date.

ARTICLE VII (BEGGING)
In all cases begging is discouraged. However master tradesman should offer services to guild members at a reduced rate. Also, if a member has an item for sale they should offer to sell it to the guild prior to auctioning it to the general public. Members should not expect a rare item to be sold to them at a reduced cost and should be thankful if a member does choose to reduce the normal cost of the item or give it to them. However, if the guild is responsible for you getting an upgrade to an item then the old item should be given to a guild member having a use for it. This will make us stronger as a guild and promote cohesion among guild members. A mule will be maintained by the Guild Leader to hold items for the guild. If the item is not of use to EoD it will be filtered down to DCoE for use.

ARTICLE VIII (VOTING)
As the land of Norrath changes and EoD continues to evolve there may arise a need to change various articles of this constitution or the norms of EoD. Changes will be proposed at announced officer meetings. A majority of the officers present at the officer meeting will decide if the change will proceed.

ARTICLE IX (LOOT)
Loot will be determined by a point system referred to as loot points. The formula will be (current level) + (number or raids 2attended)/2 = loot points. Person with most loot points will be given first opportunity to accept an item, but may pass. The items will always be on a NBG basis. Only one item per person will be awarded on any raid until all everyone of the usable class has received and item with the exception of breast plates, robes, epic items and uber items. You must have attended a minimum of 5 raids before a BP/Robe can be awarded and will be awarded by the Guild Leader or Officer in charge of the raid. Receiving another item will not eliminate a person from receiving a BP/Robe. Epic pieces will be awarded by the Guild Leader in all cases. Uber items will be designated prior to the raid start and will be awarded by the Guild Leader or Officer in charge of the raid in the same manner as BP/Robes. Errors made in loot will be corrected if the item is droppable. If the item is no drop then the looting will remain final and no type of corrections will be made.

ARTICLE X (TARDINESS)
To encourage members to be on time for raids any member that is late for the start of a raid for any reason will go to the bottom of the loot list for that specific raid regardless of their current loot points. However those that are late, but come and stay a reasonable amount of time, usually 4 hours, will receive credit for attending the raid. 15 minutes prior no more port request will be honored so that our wizards/druids will have time to prepare themselves for the raid. Members that request ports after the "15 minutes to raid" is announced will need to arrange their own transportation to the raid, outside of the guild.
