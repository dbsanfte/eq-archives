September 30, 2002

     There has been a lack of updates to the web site for a while now. This is not due to a lack of EOD events, but due to a busy web administrator. Updates will be on a regular basis once again.

     Essence of Darkness has been very busy in the last month or so. The guild continues to mobilize quickly, to take down some rare named mobs. The guild is constantly checking to see which mobs are up, and hunting them down, to gain some of their rare and valuable loot. Recently, the guild has decided to begin acquiring keys for the Sleeper's Tomb.

     EOD's 2nd birthday is around the corner now (October 5th). In anticipation of this event, the guild is going to change it's Velious Factions. We will be entering NTOV at the end of September.

     EOD is looking for a few more members. Recruitment is open to the following class/levels:
