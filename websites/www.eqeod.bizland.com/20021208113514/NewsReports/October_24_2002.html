October 24, 2002

     Now that Planes of Power is out, both EoD and DCoE have a number of players out exploring these last few days (some players got their copy Monday afternoon). The Plane of Knowledge is a great spot for trade skills, and for getting around (got to love all the portals). If you don't have it yet, go grab a copy. This week, the guilds will be grouping, just for the fun of exploring new zones (and the new raid XP system should be nice for this).

     DCoE related news: Epic nights are back! DCoE is going to work on getting epics for the guild. Wednesday evenings will once again become epic nights (starting in November), thanks to the efforts of Feallun. Go read his post in the forums, and post the necessary information. Help your guildmates, and they will help you. The raid scedualed for Saturday (Kael) has been postponed. Instead, DCoE will join EoD and explore the new planar zones. Next Saturday is a scheduled raid to the Plane of Fear.

     EoD related news: EoD has taken a number of named mobs down in the last 3 weeks. EoD is still going to concentrate on taking named mobs down, but will be doing a little exploring this week (PoP). Also, EoD will be moving to the new DKP system very soon. Waldoo has given me the mob ranking information, and a link to the information is now posted in the raid forum. There have been a number of new updates to the web site, but they are only available to members who visit the forums (and read through some of the posts). The pages are only for EoD and DCoE members. This is temporary, until Xingtau gets around to updating the web server (he has been working on it, but keeps running into glitches). The links will be added to the navigation bar, as soon as we can protect the pages.
