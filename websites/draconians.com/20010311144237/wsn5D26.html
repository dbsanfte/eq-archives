Draconian Charter Ammendments


 
I. Ammendments and Motions passed on January 21st, 2001

A. Guilding shall consist of the following steps:
1. Applicant must be directed to the web site first. They must agree to the charter and then make it be known that they are interested by posting on the Scroll board.
2. Applicant shall contact any member of the guild in-game and game with them until that person believes the applicant has or has not a true mark.
3. If mark is deemed to be true, the closest officer shall be contacted, and that officer will game with applicant for a time until they agree that the mark is indeed true.
4. Applicant will then be guilded on a two-week probationary basis to make sure they have a true mark.

B. All officers of any position must be of that players MAIN character. Only Elders of Class may have key to private War Room.

C: Duel challenge may not be done indoors in highly populated areas, such as taverns due to the safety of innocent bystanders (in actuality, this allows for other types of competition, such as drinking contests and other such acts of bravado to be RPed against the evil ones.

II. TITHING: On the 15th of every month, each character will make a note of what their level is (i.e. 23rd level). On the 15th of the FOLLOWING month, they will owe the treasurer 23pp regardless of what their level is at that time. Tithing begins once a member knighted (9th level).

III. WAR RULES:Loot was given a Mute Point as it is believed that looting is only possible on pvp servers and not on blue server even during war. Cyrric and I will be declaring a quick war and testing this theory.

Attacking within a certain level was discussed but undecided as of yet. Blue or stronger was suggested. Those of higher levels disagree as if we con the enemy blue, they may be 10 levels beneath us and that would start hard feelings.

NOT attacking when the enemy is camping a mob was approved with the following definitions:
Mobs are monsters. Camping races and their guards give the enemy the right to defend their race and their guards.
NO attacking an enemy when they are low in hp due to finishing off a mob.
NO camping zoning areas and blind-siding the enemy

No attack in public places was approved. Public places defined as where innocents can get hurt such as cities, taverns, the Bazaar.

Using DoT, poison, etc IS allowed due to a)too hard to prove it wasn't used on you and thus would start arguments and b)it is in character for people to use these things.

NOT DISCUSSED: Loot and scoot
NOT FINALIZED: Con level for attacking
NOT FINALIZED: Not having to con during a mass brawl
