Against All Odds…

__________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________

 

Since the creation of our guild in early March we’ve tore through the Planes of Power…pretty much like the Allied Forces through Iraq. Aerin’Dar, Agnarr, Behemoth, Grummus, Mithaniel Marr, Saryrn, Terris-Thule, and The Zeks have all fallen like a house of cards. Now, after two months of intense raiding and preparation, there is only one target left before the Elemental Planes. A single obstacle that stands before us. His name…

 

 

Baghdad Bob???

 

Sadly, it’s true. Baghdad Bob, not Rallos Zek the Warlord, is our true enemy in the Planes of Power. Baghdad Bob has taunted us from the beginning, exposing us for the weaklings that we are. As we inch closer to success (26 percent!), we realized it was completely fake. We were nowhere near Tactics!  Nowhere at all!

 

Consider these sad truths, finally exposed by our feared enemy:

 

"These cowards have no morals. They have no shame about lying"

Yes…no shame.

 

 

“Their infidels are committing suicide by the hundreds”

Ah well, we try.

 

 

"The louts of colonialism."

Yes, and France is next.

 

 

"They do not even have control over themselves! Do not believe them!"

 

It’s true…all true!
