    POH Wed May 9

    Posted by Lyzandia on Thursday, May 10 @ 09:47:12 EDT (0 reads)
    Last night 7 of the Heralds joined on a TSO raid lead by Otter Antilles to the Plane of Inoruuk. After a very slow start (we tried a portal break and I think it was too confusing) we finally got rocking after about 3 hours and really ripped through the ground level. We killed at least the Lord of Ire, Magi P'T'sora, The Coercer, Mistress of Scorn, The Grandmaster, and of course the Maestro of Rancor. However Jaysonn insisted on rigging all the rolls so that the Heralds never won. Congrats to Nosylla, Underfoot, Grumpy Aanas, Jaysonn, Noyr, Sammeal (and me) for a very successful raid. I got a pair of planar bard gloves out of it! Hope Fear on Friday goes as well.
