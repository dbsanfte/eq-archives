Commands: (Is this what you were looking for Jean-Remi? :)
/me , the CTCP ACTION command. Ex: /me looks at the ceiling
/ping , PING someone. Note: To see the reply you must either be in the same channel as whoever you are pinging, or have a open PrivMsgChat with the same person as target. Ex: /ping l00nie
/version , check what IRC client someone is using. Same note as for /ping. Ex: /version l00nie
/msg , send a messge to somebody. Opens a PrivMsgChat window. Ex: /msg l00nie Hello l00nie, whats up?
/nick , change nick. Ex: /nick mikael

Some notes

The PrivMsg button on the Setup tab opens a PrivMsg-watcher. All PRIVMSGs sent to you will show up here, only the latest from each nickname is displayed. To send a message to someone in the list, select his/her nick and type the text in the input control and press Enter. To open a dedicated PrivMsgChat window with someone, double click the nickname.

Another way to open a PrivMsgChat is to double click a nick in a channel user list.
