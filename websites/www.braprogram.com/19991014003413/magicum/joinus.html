Join Us!

 

To apply, please read all of below carefully before applying. Some titles are links, please investigate them further for detailed information.

 

 

1. The apprentice fee is half your level in PP, and is to be given to the GuildMaster or an appointed vice or Guild Cashier. Please remember that this is -not- to make money from anyone, these money are invested in -your- studies and learning. The money are also in the common bank, for members of the guild to be able to borrow from in dire need.

2. Common Bank. The guild has a Guild Cashier that keeps track of how much the guild has in its treasury. This also includes weapons and artifacts for members to use and loan in need. When loaning money from the bank, the interest is 10% of the sum. That is, you loan 10pp and pay back 11pp. There is no set time for payback, but in reasonable time.

3. Fighting a guild-war includes everyone, even apprentices. If the guild goes to war, so do you. The guild must always support and help each other, no matter what. There is no leaving the guild in wartime.

4. Research Materials that are looted by our members should, after using the ones needed for personal use, be transmitted to the guild Library instead of selling to merchants.

5. Disbandment and being kicked out from the guild can be handled by the High Council and noone else. If by some reason you are found guilty of an offense to the guild, you may be disbanded for lifetime from the guild.

6. If called upon by the guildmaster you must drop what you are doing and meet as soon as possible. If you cannot, ask help from the guild to free your hands.

7. When fighting in groups and partys, always do your best to help the other members. The party-leader should sacrifice him/herself to save the others from certain death. Such heroic deeds are always rewarded in the end.

8. If fighting amonst other guild-brothers or sisters, do only consult the High Council when all other methods fail. But do not duel until the High Counsil has given permission to do so. Fighting in the guild is nothing we want, so such incidents should be approved before acting.

9. Auto-split, fair looting and good spirit is vital to everyone in the guild. Keep it that way.

10. If finding treasure or great quests, you are obliged to tell the guildmasters about it. You will ofcourse keep everything found if so wished for, but the knowledge is valuable for other guildmembers in seek of adventure. Any knowledge is good knowledge, so please share anything you feel of importance.

11. Donations are ofcourse a valuable income to the guilds wellbeing. Senior members usually give donations when having more than they need, but don't be shy to donate anything. All donations are of great importance, and keeps the guild going uphill. You do not, of course, need to donate anything at all. It is just good will.

12. Spread the word. Ars Magicum has its sails set on being the supreme magic force on Norrath. Tell whomever you feel might want to join, and let our ranks fill with great heroes and magnificient spellcasters.

 

 

To join, you need to email the guildmaster and state your personas name, level, race, class and server. You will then get an appointment back to meet with the guildmaster and to be approved into the guild.

As for now, the guild is only active on the Cazic-Thule server. If enough appliances comes in from other servers, we will start strong there too, so please apply no matter what server you are on. You might even become a guildmaster on a server?
