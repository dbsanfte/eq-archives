The Library of Ars Magicum

 

Welcome to the Ars Magicum library!

This is a new service in the lands of Norrath, a library at the service for all arcane researchers! It contains Runes, Words, Pages, Research Components and magical artifacts. All of which can be traded for by our librarians in place at the arcane headquarters of Norrath.

This service can be used by any arcane brothers and sisters, although Ars Magicum members do trade at a better ratio. If you wish to encourage the continued growth of the knowledge base of our world, you can also donate your leftover components and artifacts to the Library. In some cases the Library might even buy them from you with our treasury.

We do not sell our components (only artifacts), they are only Trade. This to ensure the continued growth and wellbeing of the Library, to one day become the central part of Norrath researchers.

 

This service is only available on the Cazic-Thule server.
