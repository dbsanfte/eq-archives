KassaSystem  
A computerized POS (Point Of Sale) system under BeOS environment. Will not be available for download, commercial for sale. Features include client-server modell software, crash-safe database, ultra fast EAN queries, inter-server communication via TCP/IP (internet compatible) and much more.
Expect a dedicated homepage in the near future.
