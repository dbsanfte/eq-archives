What is the High Council?

The High Council is the deciding force in the guild, and its member form the Hierarchy of decision. The council consists of 1 member from each represented class in the guild. That member views the decisions about the guild in their own classes perspective and point of view, and thus will all decision made in the guild be much more fair to all the members. All decisions, actions and problems are submitted by the members to their respective Council, and then the council's meet and make a democratic decision.
