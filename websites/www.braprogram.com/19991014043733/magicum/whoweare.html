What is the Ars Magicum?

The guild is for magicusers of all kinds. Those are the leading powers of the Ars Magicum.

The Ars Magicum guild strives for power through magic. Within our ranks we will sometimes pick out Arch-Mages and WarLocks with a handpicked few of our Elite fighters to form a party to gain artifacts and arcane knowledge. These partys are carefully considered and weighed, and also in some degree equipped by the Guild. When a party is created for a great purpose, all belongings of the guildmembers are available for equipping the selected heroes.

We strive for knowledge, adventure, roleplaying and progress!

The ranks of Ars Magicum are like a small family, we all try to look out for each other and helping each other in every possible way. To maintain this closeness, accepting new members is a very closely inspected matter. The High Councils, and no others, are legitimate of signing up new members after thorough interviewing. If you wish to join, contact the Council of your class. As of now only magic-casting classes are accepted, although the officers of Ars Magicum can appoint body-guards of other classes.

Members gain access to a members-only login area, where all members are listed with statistics and contact information as well as pictures and biographys. This method ensures easily updated information about what is happening and the status of the guild.

 

 

What is our purpose?

We ultimately strive after the ultimate power, a divine perfection of magic. A combination of all of our greatest spellcasters, working together to cast the spell that will change the world of Norrath forever. We strive after the power from The Nameless, who created all and everything. It is said that these powers are to be hold by Veeshan, the crystalline dragon. Our purpose is to conquer the Dragons and bring an end to the Age of Turmoil, and bring forth the Age of Perfection! With the Divine Creation spell and power, we will enlighten the world of Norrath and settle it to ultimate beuty and wisdom. Our goal is to enter ourselves and Norrath into the Age of Perfection.

 

 

The guild has a common bank-system for members to use, that also has weapons and items for our members to loan and use as needed. The guild has a Deed Point System, that gives out and withdraw points depending on members actions ingame. All members start with a Deed status of 1. If the Deed counter is lowered below zero, the member is dismissed from the guild.

This Deed system proves to ensure that our members always work in honorable ways, help each other and bring pride and glory to our beutiful guild. All different Deeds are listed here.

As well you might know about our Guild Library, where members trade their research components with a 1 to 1 ratio!

We hope you are more enlightened in our ways. If you feel of worth, join our ranks! As of still we are a growing force, and we need the powers you can provide! Help us bring forth the Age of Perfection.
