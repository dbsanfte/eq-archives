I welcome thee to our temple.

In the vast world of Norrath, there is one force that supercedes all others. There is but one force that controls, helps, destroys, rebuilds, enforces and create. A force that rules all of our daily lives, at night and at day. It is present on FayDwer, it is present on Antonica and at Odus as well. Even the small islands in the Ocean of Tears or the Buried Sea. This force is all. This force is everything. This force controls.

This force is magic.

The Ars Magicum brothers and sisters know this. That is why we worship it. The beuty of the arcane does not blind us, but strengthens us in our missions and goals. The powers of magic fullfills our souls and bodies, it builds up inside us and in the end it makes us complete. The blade of the sword shall one day melt down from the warm rays of magic, to let the world be totally consumed in beuty and insight. The wars shall be won, the wounds shall be binded. The cracked surface of our world, Norrath, shall heal up under the powers from hundreds of magicusers working together to mend the wrongs and fulfill the rights. This time will come. But we must make it do so.

The brothers and sisters of Ars Magicum is the magical force on Norrath. We will strive to gain all knowledge gainable, no matter what is positioned in between us. We will work together, help each other, distinct our enemies and bring knowledge and magic to the center of our beings. But we will not just read books, we will cross the continents and seas of our beutiful world in our search for the powers we have yet to find. We will fight our way forward, until one day. The day we are complete. The day we have the knowledge of mending the wrongs. The day we can work together and cast the greatest spell to exist. The spell that will ensure wisdom and beuty for all eternity. One day, my brethren. One day.

I welcome thee to our temple.

 

/ Gerlakin, GuildMaster and High Counsil.
