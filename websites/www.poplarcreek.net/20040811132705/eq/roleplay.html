Hail, Hobbits!

Hobbit Patrol is a hobbit-only guild on Rallos Zek. We do not admit murderers and only kill in self-defense or in defense of another hobbit.

Following are some suggestions to aid you in your quest to roleplay (and realize the comic potential of) your hobbit character. No one is "forced" to roleplay, but many find that EQ is much more fun when people rp. Feel free to add your suggestions.

1) We aren't halflings. We're hobbits. Always refer to yourself and others of our race as hobbits, including in the guild name, Hobbit Patrol.  The gods call us Hairfoot because they fear the attorney-demons.

2) Our guild battle cry is, "Hobbit Patrol, ATTAaaaaaaaaaaaaCK!!!" Hotkey it twice, once for /shout and once for /guildsay. Don't spam it, but use it every hour or so, or _especially_ when rp appropriate, like taking on a nasty monster. It will build a sense of camaraderie, make new members feel like they are a part of something organized, and help our guild gain recognition among PCs, especially around the River Vale.

3) Our guild salute is, "HOBBIT HOP!" When we score a great victory, or are otherwise overjoyed (like when an old friend appears,) someone says, "HOBBIT HOP," and we all jump around exuberantly 3 or 4 times. We also use this when we are in a group and a non-member makes a comment about "all the halflings." I use this on /say most times, or on /guildsay when someone levels. Know that whenever you level, hobbits throughout Norrath will be bouncing like idiots in your honor.

4) Pick one character trait and make some emotes for it. Corrik is uncouth, Sergo is a glutton, etc. A few emotes like

: uses his dagger to dig the crud out of his toenails, or
: takes a bite of pungent blue cheese

can go a long way. Halflings are also known as cowardly, shifty, free-spirited, and other things. Pick one and have fun with it. It will make your character much more memorable.

5) Avoid OOC things like

Goober tells the party, "class/lvl, d00dz?"

and replace them with things like, "What is your profession and experience, fellows?"

Come up with RP ways to describe game mechanics things. Some of these can be clever or funny. Here are a few examples:

12/cleric = I am a priest in the 12th circle of the temple of Bristlebane.
zone = magical barrier
lag = sacked by a lagbeast
afk = trance
SoW = Spirit of the Hobbit
spawn = reinforcements arriving
no spawn = they have heard of our mighty reputation and fear to face us
camp = leave the realm

6) Fake your own quests. Pull/kill/repeat is boring as hell. Learn how to run around a little bit without dying. Even if you aren't as brave as that, come up with an RP story. Why are you standing at the zone in the gorge and killing goblins over and over? Perhaps the Runnyeye goblins are receiving reinforcements and supplies from the Pickclaws, and you are trying to disrupt this aid that threatens Rivervale.

7) Call it, "the River Vale", not just Rivervale. Hobbits would see their home as a place in nature, not a political entity. They would think of the natural vale itself.

8) Speak hobbit, not common, when possible. Of course, when you are in a mixed adventuring group, you can't do this. The hobbit language is especially useful when recruiting to screen out guild-crashing PKers. I used to recruit zonewide, sometimes inviting new recruits without ever laying eyes on them. One guy tried to get in, but couldn't answer my questions in hobbit. Heehee.

9) Refer to Gercheck as guildleader, (chairman sounds communist,) and to other officers as "Councillor so-and-so" Its' hard to keep track of which councillors are still active.  Corrik, Flanda, Leibolmai, Hemmil, Derreni, Xamar, Franklins, and Sergo are all councillors as of 4/15/02.  Any guild member may be referred to as a Patrolman, or Patroller for the ladies.

10) Refer to the spell SoW as "Spirit of the Hobbit." I did this with a human druid who thought I was a newbie who didn't know what the spells were called. I replied, (and feel free to use this one,) "If you think that's spirit of the wolf, you've never seen a hobbit being chased by a minotaur." In general, our time honored racial defensive tactic is RUN LIKE A GAZELLE. Play this up some when RPing whether with other hobbits or non-hobbits.

I hope these ideas make the game more fun for you. Thanks to all of you for supporting Hobbit Patrol!
