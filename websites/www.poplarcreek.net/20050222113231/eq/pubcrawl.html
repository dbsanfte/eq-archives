The Patrol sent 5 members to the Pan-Antonican Pub Crawl.  Leib, the most distinctive of our members is easy to pick out in this photo.  His weapon's just a wee bit larger than any of the others.





The crawl started in the Free Port, with representatives of some of Norrath's finest guilds there.  Now just imagine this crowd stumbling drunkenly across the Commons, yelling, "Whersh da good schtuff?"

Here are our intrepid Patrolmen whooping it up at the famous Tiger's Roar in the Highpass.  They were going to let us pet the actual tiger, and we were drunk enough to do it, but then Leib quaffed a few more Halfling Stouts and started roaring, "Heeeeereesh da kitty!  Wheresh da yummy kitty?"

Montagne and Biggelman quickly hustled us out before the authorities could be alerted.  Note Minymee listing a bit to port in this image.  He'd had his share of the stuff too.







Councillor Minymee reported that he had come across signs of a giant's recent passage through the Commonlands, and wandered off to the north.  We assumed he was making a polite excuse to go... you know...  Good hobbit manners and all.  Anyway, imagine our surprise when he comes back being chased by this nasty fellow.

The giant seemed rather intent on slaying people, and we couldn't let that happen.  Tempers were high.  This had to be resolved in a hurry.  If you study this image carefully, you'll see Lieb attempting a little creative diplomacy on the giant's backside.



Unfortunately, other obligations prevented us from following the crawl all the way to Halas.  That may, however, have been just as well.  The crowd was getting awfully rowdy and starting to talk about ways to get the dark elves past the guards in Halas.  Some of their methods, were, frankly, rather direct and lacking in finesse.

The crawl, frivolous as it seems, was not all fun and games.  Considerable research was conducted into what kind of alcohol goes best with cheese.
