Hail, hobbits!  I am Sergo Cheddargnobbler, priest of Bristlebane.  I am fond of good cheese, good drink, and a warm evening in front of my hobbit hole, gazing at the stars.  I am also occasionally dragged off on adventures.

You may have met me sitting on my stump by the Archgate, healing and blessing young hobbits.  While I have many fine friends who are druids, I prefer to remain closer to home.  The portal-hopping life is not for me.  (Take note... teleporting is especially disagreeable with a stomach full of sharp dwarven cheddar.)

Many people ask me what wisdom one gains at the temple of Bristlebane, since they are too busy drinking to seek any wisdom themselves.  I reply that Bristlebane teaches patience.  I have learned how to handle the throngs of ignorant doublings who ask me for Spirit of the Hobbit whilst I'm wearing a full suit of metal armor.  Their brains are too far from the ground... I guess the blood just can't make it up that high.
