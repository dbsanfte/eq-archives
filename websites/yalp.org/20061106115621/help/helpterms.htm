YALP Online Help

Damage By:

The Damage By column displays the grouping of damage for the current damage list. Damage lists are either grouped by damage type, or party name.

back to Top

Kills:

The kill statistic refers to the number of kills or the number of deaths for the selected party. Kills listed on offensive damage lists are kills by the selected party, while kills on defensive damage lists show deaths of the selected party from opponents.

back to Top

Total:

The total column on damage lists displays the total amount of damage for the current grouping.

back to Top

Duration:

The duration column on damage lists displays the total amount of time that was taken for this damage to be inflicted. Duration is listed in HH:MM:SS format so it is easier to understand.

back to Top

DPS:

The DPS column on damage lists displays the average Damage Per Second for the amount of accumulated time and total damage for this grouping. This can be read as the actual DPS for this aggregation while doing damage.

back to Top

ADPS:

The ADPS column on damage lists displays the Adjusted Damage Per Second for this grouping. The duration for the ADPS is always the duration for the [ Total ] line on the damage list. This provides a sort of "normalized DPS" that works for all damage types, some people refer to this as "caster DPS".

back to Top

Attack Delay:

The Attack Delay column on damage lists displays the overall delay between attacks for the current damage grouping.

back to Top

Maximum Damage:

The Max column on damage lists displays the maximum amount of damage for the current damage grouping over the time it was accumulated.

back to Top

Average Damage:

The Avg column on damage lists displays the average damage for the current grouping of damage over the time that it was accumulated.

back to Top

Minimum Damage:

The Min column on damage lists displays the lowest amount of damage done over the time that the current grouping of damage was accumulated.

back to Top

Accuracy:

The Accuracy column displays the percent of all attacks for the current grouping that landed on their target.

back to Top

Evasion:

The Evasion column on damage lists displays the percent of attacks that missed their target due to the effects of defensive skills. This percentage is out of the total number of attacks for the current grouping.

An evasion value of 33 percent would mean that one of every three misses was due to a defensive skill. Offensive evasion is a measure of how good the defensive skills of opponents are, while Defensive evasion shows how good the selected parties defensive skills are.

back to Top

Defense:

The Defense column on damage lists displays the percentage of all misses from the total number of attacks for the current damage grouping.

back to Top

Hits:

The Hits column on damage lists displays the number of attacks for the current damage grouping that landed successfully.

back to Top

Misses:

The Misses column on damage lists displays the total number of attacks for the current damage grouping that did not land successfully.

back to Top

Blocks:

The Blocks column on damage lists displays the total number of attacks for the current damage grouping that did not land successfully due to the defensive skill Block.

back to Top

Dodges:

The Dodges column on damage lists displays the total number of attacks for the current damage grouping that did not land successfully due to the defensive skill Dodge.

back to Top

Magic:

The Magic column on damage lists displays the total number of attacks for the current damage grouping that did not land successfully due to the employment of magic spells such as HarmShield.

back to Top

Parries:

The Paries column on damage lists displays the total number of attacks for the current damage grouping that did not land successfully due to the defensive skill Parry.

back to Top

Ripostes:

The Ripostes column on damage lists displays the total number of attacks for the current damage grouping that did not land successfully due to the defensive skill Riposte.

You should note that Ripostes have an effect on the overall Attack Delay for the current grouping of damage because they are additional attacks.
