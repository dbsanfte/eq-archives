Welcome to our guild


 

 

Quest for Fellowship is a guild for friends who enjoy the fun of gaming for its own sake.  Race, class or level do not matter, only the quality of the person. It is not a guild of power-levelers, sappers, or people who are not interested in experiencing the fun of grouping with like-minded individuals. This guild is for good people to come together in friendship and community to enjoy the camaraderie of playing with unique individuals who’s goal is to enjoy and experience the full flavor of Everquest through the benefit that comes from the strength of a powerful guild. Everyone is viewed as an EQUAL, but the leader and officers words are final. We are all about having fun and experiencing the joy of play that can be found through team-work with members of all ages, races, classes & skill level.
