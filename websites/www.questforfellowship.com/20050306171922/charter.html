Quest for Fellowship is all about the following:

  1. Helping another guild member achieve something they could not do alone but may accomplish with the assistance of a fellow guild member.
  2. Assisting each other within the guild and those outside it through the various means of buffing, gear provision, rezing and porting at the discretion of the individual whom is being asked for assistance.
  3. Providing all characters with the ability to contact any officer and have their issues raised to the guild leadership.
  4. Providing a refinement and atmosphere of fun and enjoyment in the game where people who play full time or part time can still benefit from the abilities and zest of being a part of the guild.
  5. Providing a pool of trade skills that can be called upon at the discretion of the character for use by the guild.
  6. Providing a community of relaxed and enjoyable fun for all levels, races & classes within the guild.
  7. Contributing to a place where no foul language or inappropriate behavior to others within the game will be tolerated especially on guild chat.
  8. Assisting in creating a haven for those who are seeking a guild where they can function within a community to make the whole greater than the individual.
  9. At no point in time will anyone within the guild be obliged to assist in any manner that they do not wish; including buffs, ports and rez’s.
  10. All maters of importance will be voted on.
  11. Guild members should realize that their actions and behavior reflects directly on the name and reputation of the guild. Guild members should follow proper (mature) etiquette at all times.

 

Guild Leadership

The guild is run by a number of guild officers and one guild leader. The power of the guild is in the officers, with the guild leader being a single deciding vote should the officers be unable to make a decision on a matter.

The guild officer is voted in by at least 5 other guild officers. Any member may make a petition to the guild to become a guild officer. Once a guild officer petition is received via the message board, a vote is taken after consultation of the guild officers and a decision made. The decision is made based on the commitment of the individual to the guild and is final. An appeal of being tuned down can be made to the guild leader but not to the guild officers. This decision is final but does not stop another petition for guild officer status to be made in the future.

The guild leader is elected via a vote by the guild officers and this position is for a random period of time. Change is made where there is a need or as requested by the guild.

Guild Invitations

There is no level or class requirements to join the guild. Participation in mandatory guild raids are a requirement of membership. If you are online during a mandatory raid you are expected to attend.

New / returning members will be on probation for 30 days. During this probationary period a guild officer may, at their discretion, remove you from the guild having consulted with the guild leader or  other officers online. You will not be able to roll on or receive any items obtained during official guild events while on probation. This rule does not apply to guild pick up groups or events that you organize and lead yourself. Violation of the no-loot rule will result in immediate removal.

Guild Removal

Forced removal from the guild may be made immediately if circumstances warrant it. Eg, the individual is conducting him or herself in such a way as to bring substantial disrespect to the guild. However, the majority of guild remove circumstances will be based on a request from any member of the guild to one of the officers with a full explanation as to why. The request will then be addressed by the officers who review the case and to make a decision of if removal from the guild is appropriate. There will be an opportunity for all of those who have had a complaint issued against them to explain their side of the issue.

Once a request to remove a member is made, the guild leader will discuss the reasons with the individual, will give him the opportunity to correct the basis for the request. If that corrective action is not taken, they the guild leader will remove the individual from the guild. All characters for that individual are then removed from the guild. You can not have one character removed and keep others in the guild.

Upon your termination of membership with Quest for Fellowship (voluntary or involuntary) all characters in the guild must be removed as well. We ask that you make a good faith effort to remove any reference to your character being a member of Quest for Fellowship guild. This includes, but is not limited too, web site, forum, signature blocks on other web sites and forums.
