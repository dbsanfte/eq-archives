Guild Raids


 

The guild will hold raids as published on the MOTD and based on the number of members in the guild. These raids are the focus of the entire guild and we need the power of the entire guild to be successful. These raids are all about improving the power of the guild and thus special rules exist for them.

  1. Raid Loot rules are very different from the usual rules for grouping or personal raid set up. Loot from these raids is designed to benefit the GUILD power core or an individual who need something specific. Thus the RAID master looter will dispense all loot and will direct who gets what item viewing what the GUILD needs or the need of  an individual needs. Other Loot will be sold at the Bazaar by me or someone else. The money form this will be either put in guild bank so members can use it if they need it, Or can be split up to the members that where on the raid.

Raids are the opportunity to do something that we couldn't do alone or just in groups. Sometimes raids will be held to help someone get a quest piece, and other times, to help bolster the guild bank with money and items. People should not expect to see a significant amount of drops or plats from raids.

 

If anyone has suggestions please feel free to post it on the message board so that the raid organizers can them set up.
