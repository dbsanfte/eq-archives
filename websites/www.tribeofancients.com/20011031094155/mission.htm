When Norrath was young, and mankind and light skinned races younger, a group of idealists formed to bring culture and civilization to a fledgling world. They came from all corners of the charted regions; man, elf, dwarf, and gnome alike with a common goal.

To spread virtue and tame an unbridled wilderness.

To blaze trails where no other child of the gods had stepped foot before.

They relied on their friendships and years of experience battling the forces of evil that constantly threatened each of their homelands. They placed their faith in each other and with their common goals they persevered. They conquered great evil and mapped a world where others only dared to tread.
Code of Conduct

The following should be the moral compass that players use for their participation in the guild and the EverQuest game in general. Violation of these tenants shall result in disciplinary action, suspension, or expulsion. Please read the following carefully. Should you find problems or irregularities, please inform a member of the counsel so that changes may be made.

  1. 1. Conduct yourself in a way that brings honor to the Tribe.
    2. A Tribe member does not speak ill of another member in public.
    3. The decision of the counsel is final and will be honored, without question.
    4. As a guild we fully support the Need before Greed model of loot distribution,     and this is the default model we will follow unless agreed upon before any event takes place.
    5. Begging of any type will not be tolerated. To do so will be grounds for immediate suspension and or removal from the guild for repeated offenses. Begging is defined as repeated requests for Power Leveling (PL), requests for items, or requests for assistance getting items that are beyond the ability of the individual to attain.
    6. No kill stealing, Nina looting, or deliberate training.
    7. Member must make every reasonable effort to attend Tribe of Ancients guild events and festivals.
    8. Items that are handed to guild members from other guild members as "gifts" are considered guild property and cannot be sold. The item when not used any longer is to be handed down to another member in the guild or follow the wishes of the person that gave you the item in question. That member must also hand the item down. When an item cannot be handed down further, it is given to a "mule" character for storage in the bank until such time a new member may need it.
Guild Charter
  • Goals
    To provide a unique and FUN experience in the EverQuest game.
    To provide both camaraderie and help in making the current playing experience even better.
    To provide a sense of family and social belonging that contributes to a lasting friendship.

Article One: Guild Leadership
A core group of players, consisting of the GM and 9 officers, will comprise a "Counsel of the Ancients."

This counsel is the ToA's governing and legal body and as such, has the right and responsibility to create, modify, or rescind rules and or policies as it sees fit. The counsel also has final word on punishment as pertains to ToA members who knowingly break those rules and policies (see rule infraction).

Above all else, the purpose of the council is to insure equality, fairness, impartiality, and to lighten the GM's workload considerably.

Article Two: Membership/Sponsorship
(ToA has the right to not admit a sponsored player into the guild for any reason; they have the final say.)

Membership into the guild is by invitation only.

Members may sponsor others for membership but realize that they are always responsible for the actions of these individuals during the probation period. Additionally, there is a minimum level of 15 for acceptance into the guild. It is highly recommend, that members be seasoned players, whether that experience comes from the current server or another. There shall be no restriction based solely on level.

All recruits to the ToA are accepted on a 2 (two) week probationary term. During this time, it is strongly recommended that the recruit group with ToA members so they may observe the playing style and conduct of the recruit to ensure they are ToA material, know how to play their class, and understand what it means to be a member of ToA. This also give the recruit time to see if ToA and it's members are right for them. If there has not been enough time to make an informed decision by the members (i.e. recruit did not group with members enough) then that probation period can be extended until such time that ToA members are comfortable making a recommendation.

The Guild Recruiter (GR) will start a thread in the recruiting topic to track comments and opinions of the recruit. The recruiting thread will be password protected for access by ToA members only. When the probation period is completed and before the recruit is admitted to the guild, the GR will remove the thread concerning that recruit to protect the privacy of those who commented.

Should recruit violate Terms of Service (ToS) or the Code of Conduct (CoC) at anytime during probation, the punishment will always be immediate expulsion.

After the probationary period, it is the responsibility of the sponsor to ensure feedback from ToA members is submitted to the recruit thread. Then that information will be gathered by the GR who will submit a final recommendation to the counsel. The counsel will then vote on whether to admin. the recruit or not. At that time based on the vote of the counsel, the recruit will be officially invited into the guild by an officer using the /guildinvite command.

Once a recruit is approved by a majority vote of the officers conducted on the ToA web site, and they have signed the Agreement to abide by the ToS and CoC by posting in the appropriate thread on the ToA web site. They will have full and complete access to the benefits of the online community starting at that time. The new member has 5 (five) days to enter their character information into the GuildMagic™ web site and apply to the ToA guild through GuildMagic's guild system which will update the guild roster.

Another membership type is that of Alternates. Alternates are defined as characters played by the SAME person that plays a fully guilded member. Alternates are automatically accepted into the guild without a vote required, but must supply a password created by the Recruiter (this ensures that they are actually alternates). If new alternates are created after the initial invitation into the guild, the person creating the alternate must notify the GR before an invitation to the guild is extended to the alternate. The GR or another officer will see to it that the alternate gets guilded at a time convenient to the player and the officer.

Votes will be counted on a 1 vote per EQ Account. Alternates do not have a separate vote, but will be counted as the vote of the primary member in the tribe. 1 EQ Account = 1 Tribal Vote.

Article Three: TOS and Guild Rules of Conduct
Violation of Verant’s ToS (Terms of Service) is punishable by expulsion from the guild. There is no excuse for violating the ToS as this can reflect badly on the guild as well. In addition to the ToS, there is also the Guild Code of Conduct (CoC) Document. Failure of any of these tenants may result temporary expulsion from the guild, permanent expulsion, Disciplinary action, or any combination of the above. It is the decision of the counsel that determines what action disciplinary action will be taken.

At any time, a member may have their relationship terminated; membership in the ToA guild is a privilege not a right.

Article Four: GuildMagic Character List
As a matter of policy, all guild members will be required to maintain a list of current characters on the GuildMagic web site. This site is used to track the class, level, and skills of a character, and will be used by the CO as a link for the guild roster. Failure to maintain characters will only make it more difficult to have a who's who list up to date.
It is the responsibility of all guild members to enter their primary and alternate characters within 5 (five) days. If a character is not added, the CO will give a warning. Multiple warning will be grounds for probation or removal from the guild.

Only the minimum information is required, but it is recommended that skills be kept up to date, as this will help guild members know who to go to for a particular skill.

Article Five: Dispute resolution
In the event that another player or guild needs to contact the GM or council members about a problem or issue the council members that are currently online and available* will attend a discussion room in the chat area of server selection. The person or persons involved will also attend, and any reps from the other guild. We will all sit down and have a nice friendly chat with all of us present. If the situation cannot be resolved at that moment due to no/not enough officers being online, and the other party does not mind waiting, the situation will be discussed at a later time when officers can be present.

(* If in a place like a planes raid the officers involved with a raid would not be considered available)

Article Six: Guild Events
(It is a given that Real Life (RL) will take priority over Everquest, all we ask is that ToA members take an active role in the guild. ToA understands that RL will have precedence over EQ, it is a game after all.)

On occasion the guild will hold events such as "Whack a Guildie" or guild meetings. Some events may be deemed mandatory. Attendance to mandatory functions shall be recorded. Mandatory functions will be announced for 1 week (7 days) prior to event using the guild web site and the MotD to give reasonable time for everyone to plan accordingly if possible. Missing a mandatory function without prior notification will result in that member’s absence being noted. Repeated failures to show at mandatory events will be grounds for Disciplinary action by the counsel, which may include probation or removal from the guild.

Article Seven: Guild Message of the Day
The message of the day will be used for official Guild messages such as Raid announcements or meeting reminders.

Article Eight: Leaves of Absence
(This paragraph borrowed from another web site, it is a good way to deal with burnout)

This section is intended to deal with the inevitable occurrence of the guild leadership being away from the game for an extended period of time. The guild is best served by having a leadership that is playing the game regularly, and is available to members within the game as well as outside of it on the web. In the event that a Council Member will be unable to have a major presence in the game for a period of time (due to illness, work, burnout, whatever), that Council Member should select a regular guild member from the membership at large to serve as an "acting" council member. The Council must approve this member by a simple majority vote. If this exchange continues for more than 60 days, then the acting Council Member will resume his/her regular membership and a new Council Member election will be called. The former council member's status will revert to that of a regular guild member.

Normal members experience no penalty for being "away" from the game for any period of time. It is encouraged however that before a member decides to take a break, or even retire from the game, that he/she notifies the guild membership that he/she will be absent. This is not required, but we are a group of friends, and we worry!

Counsel of Ancients Charter
(You are the compass that guides the Tribe)


  • · The need for centralized leadership is extremely important in order to ensure the timely resolution of disputes and activities.
    · The counsel is the central body of the organization. It’s responsible for creation of amendments to the charter and the final vote for acceptance of recruits.
    · They are the governing body of the Tribe. Depending on the vote, they have the authority to expel members based on charter, Verant TOS, or Tribe of Ancients CoC violations.
    · All proposals created by a counsel member must be voted on and must have a minimum of 4 counsel members approval to be ratified.

Article One: Guild Leadership
The role of GM is a position of leadership in name only. The GM exists only because, as a game mechanic of EQ, it is required. No special rights or privileges are given with the office of GM.

The counsel is comprised of 10 (ten) members. The Tribe votes these members into that position. Counsel members MUST be active in management of the guild. If a counsel member no longer wishes to be on the counsel, then a replacement will be named and a vote will take place at the next Tribal meeting. (Ties must still be addressed.)

Counsel members are required to attend all Counsel and Tribal meetings unless prior arrangements are made.

Article Two: Tribal and Counsel meetings
Counsel meetings will be held a minimum of once a month or as needed to resolve disputes. Tribal meetings will be called as needed.

Article Three: Dispute resolution
All disputes between guild members will be handed privately by the guild. At no time will disputes of this nature be taken to a public forum, to do so will be grounds for disciplinary action by the counsel.

The decision of the counsel concerning a dispute is final. ToA members must accept the decision as it is handed out.

A decision cannot be made until all sides have had their say in the matter, to do so would be against reason, and could be seen as favoritism. Everyone has a valued opinion, and the more input we get from the parties involved and their witnesses, the better we will be at making an informed decision.

For disputes involving parties outside of ToA, the matter will be handled as quickly as possible. Any counsel member getting a tell in game from a nonmember will ask if it is ok for us to call a meeting to discuss the issue. The parties involved and no less than 3 counsel members must attend this meeting and try to come to an agreement as how to proceed. If they are unable to get three counsel members to attend, then it will be requested that we have a time setup a meeting that will include all parties.

Article Four: Special assignments
(These duties are in addition to the normal Counsel duties described above.)
Communications Officer (CO)
The office of the Communications Officer is responsible for the managing the Message of the Day (MotD) and as the primary Webmaster of the guild web site.
Guild Recruiter (GR)
The office of Guild Recruiter is responsible for watching and keeping track of those interested in joining or those who have already joined the guild. The GR is also responsible for keeping a List of the Damned (those who don’t exhibit the traits that make a good Tribe of Ancients member) and a List of the Chosen (possible recruits who do show the traits that make a good Tribe of Ancients member). These lists will be maintained on the guild web site in protected forums and only Tribe of Ancients guild members will have the password to access this forum. An additional responsibility is that of the official point of contact for new and potential members. Their Sponsor should direct any potential new member to this officer.
Event Coordinator (EC)
The office of Guild Event Coordinator is responsible for official or mandatory guild social functions. This includes any gathering and awarding of prizes and other particulars of a specified event. Any other events and the like shall be the sole responsibility of the sponsor for that event.
