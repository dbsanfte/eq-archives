SPIROCS FALLTO SURPRISE INVASION

HORIZON AND BREGAN D'AERTH COMBINE FORCES

On September 2, 2000, in a well planned and choreographed event, members of Horizon and Bregan D'Aerth teamed up to attack the Spirocs on their small atol in the middle of the Timorous Deep.

Bregan's  roster included Verinsolen (GM), Kulann, Naersyl, Turiya, Krazie, Lazzerwraith, Heidin, Marduk, Slaadie, and Ishmail.

Representing Horizon were Mordra (GM), Yallayen, Sumtra, Aorthin, Sethalon, Chiun, and Aanelin

The encounters were fast and furious thanks to the constant pulls by Verinsolen and Naersyl.  Not only did they have the Spirocs pleading for mercy, but the casters, healers and tanks as well. There was hardly time to med and heal.

This combined event was the first for these two allies.

Members of one of Bregan's teams led by Kulann are poised in anticipation of the ensuing battle.

Amidst a barrage of nukes, and weapons, a Spiroc Proven is taken down as another has been stunned while awaiting his fate.

The pace was so fast and furious that many casters and clerics had to med between nukes and heals to keep up with the constant stream of birds. It is rumored that the local vendors ran out of "Dr. Procto's Extra Strength Odorless-Greaseless Butt Blister Cream".

Not a one was left standing after Bregan's teams were finished. There was barely time to clean up before the next wave.

In a rare moment, Nearsyl is seen without a Spiroc "in tow". We managed to keep him down for a few seconds when he ran out of levitation. But as soon as Yallayen summoned him a few "Rings of Flight" he was back in business. Some members of Horizon used this time to take a small breather.

Barbecued, Tenderized, Sliced and Diced was the order of the day. I'd prefer a leg myself, Anyone for the breast or wings? (And we don't use MSG)

Mordra is seen resting and looking over the battlefield after a recent slaughter. She must be wondering if all this fowl will fit in the freezer.
