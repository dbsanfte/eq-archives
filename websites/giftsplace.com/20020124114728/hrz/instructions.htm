Here is explanation on our e-mail service as well as instructions how to set it up with your E-mail Client

What is Horizon E-mail ?

Horizon e-mail is an address provided by Horizon Guild, that you can use to send / receive information to other Horizon Members.

Can it be used for my other e-mail needs ?

Yes, But please do not abuse it .  If you have questions on how to use it, please send me e-mail to tinyone@giftsplace.com  and I will be glad to answer your questions.

Can Ii be forwarded to my regular e-mail address as well ?

Yes, When you fill up request form, please type in an address you want it to be forwarded to.

How can I set it up with my Regular e-mail Client i.e. Outlook Express?

It very simple, just use  the following in your e-mail client setup:
POP3 (Incoming Mail) - www.giftsplace.com
SMTP (Outgoing Mail) - www.giftsplace.com
then use Login Name and Password that we assign to you
Then select 'Leave a copy on the server' in Advanced Tab, otherwise it will be moved to your PC when you read it.
