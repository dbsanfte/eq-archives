Guidelines

Overview

Horizon has been founded, and our guidelines have been developed, based on
the premise that the Guild is our Everquest family. The Ruling Council shall
endeavor to act in accordance with this ideal. We will not be the biggest
and most powerful guild in Norrath, but we are the best. All members are
requested to carry themselves accordingly.


Online/Offline Communication

Communication is an essential part of being a successful guild.
Horizon Message Board ( http://www.giftsplace.com/cgibin/UltraBoard/UltraBoard.cgi
)is choosen the primary mean of communication between Horizon members. Also
guild wide e-mail address can be used as a secondary mean of communication
(currently horizon-guild@yahoogroups.com). Guild MOTD will be used for
important announcments and Raid reminders. All members are required to check
Horizon Message Board on the regular base.
All members are to conduct themselves in an appropriate manner. The chat
channel is our living room and as such it is a family meeting place.
While frustrations abound in game and we all know about the "filter"
command, vulgar words and innuendo will not be tolerated. All members are to
be treated with respect. Any problem, arguments or conflicts are to be
discussed in private "tells" between the parties involved. If the conflict
cannot be resolved, then an officer is to be contacted. If no officer is
available the parties involved should contact an officer via e-mail, state
their case and the council will resolve the issue. If an officer is involved
in a hunt that is in itself a dangerous position for said officer, then they
are to be considered "unavailable"!
 

Play Nice Policy

In our effort to build the best guild possible, we are asking everyone to
remember that his or her’s actions, comments, remarks, etc. reflect on our
guild name. So please play nice, watch the language, and respect all other
players in the game. If for any reason, anyone is caught by or reported to
any of the officers for abusing these policies, guild membership will be
reconsidered for that individual. This is not something any of the officers
want to have to do, so please just follow the guidelines.
In the case of conflict with another player, either just back off or contact
an officer to deal with the situation. That way your rear is covered and the
officer can handle the situation in the best possible manner.
 

Recruitment

Before any member is to be guilded, they are to read and abide by the
charter set forth for the guild. That person can be asked questions by any
guild member at any given time pertaining to such charter.
If a member of the guild recommends an individual whom wishes to be a part
of Horizon, then that person will automatically be guilded.
If an individual wishes to join that was not recommended, then they will be
guilded immediately but shall be on probation for a period not to exceed
three weeks. Upon the third weekend, that person will be deguilded so that
the members can vote on that person. The voting will be conducted by those
individuals present on that day. This person can and should be asked
questions about the charter until such a time that he/she is guilded and is
wearing the name Horizon with pride.
A new family member should endeavor to group with the other members as much
as possible.
 

Family Inventory and Treasury

There will be no central collection of items and or coins at this time,
except the situations described in Guild Loot section.
 

Guild Raids

Our guild will participate in two types of raids: Experience Guild Raids and
Fun Guild Raids.
Experience Guild Raids - These types of raids will consist of groups of
similar level as to allow for exp to be gained by all in the parties. The chosen destinations for the raids should be appropriate to the levels
participating in the raid. For example: Befallen would be a good raid for parties consisting of levels around the mid teens. (In the old Befallen at
least)
Fun Guild Raids - These types of raids are for the whole guild to
participate in. The level ranges in the parties may or may not allow for all party members to gain xp. The purpose of these raids is for fun, excitement,
and overall camaraderie. Examples of fun raids would include low to high
level dungeons and also item achievement raids. (Item achievement raids are
for obtaining quest items and/or specific class/race items)
All guild raids will be organized and ran by an officer (or an officer’s
alternate char) of the guild. Exceptions to this rule will be allowed upon the consent of the guild master. In other words, if one of the guild members
would like to organize and run a guild raid, a request would need to be made
to the guild master for approval. At least a week’s notice should be given
before a guild raid is to take place. This will allow everyone plenty of
time to prepare for the raid and for updating the calendar of events on our
web site. We will also try to remind everyone of upcoming raids a few days
in advance through the guild MOTD messaging. So it is everyone’s own
responsibility to keep an eye on our website and the MOTD for upcoming
events.
 

Rules to the Guild Raids:

Guild raids are to be ran in the most efficient and organized manner as
possible. All participants of the guild raid are to remain patient so as to let the raid organizer do his job.
Looting of corpses will be done by the guild raid organizer or by an
appointed individual(s). At the end of the raid all loot will be distributed equally amongst all participating members.
All players’ corpses must be recovered before the guild raid ends. It is
everyone’s job to help in the recovery of corpses.
Guild Loot and Distribution
 

Guild loot:

First and foremost, we are here to acquire equipment for the guild as a
whole and not just a single member. The better we equip our guild then the
better off we are for doing those increasingly harder raids that only a
guild can do together. Greed of any member will cause hardship and
resentment within the guild and therefore will not be tolerated by any.
Secondly, looting will not be conducted while a battle ensues unless told to
by the raid leader or an officer. All looting will be done after battle or
during the time in which an Evac is called.
All loot that is acquired from guild raids will be considered guild loot.
These raids are as follows:
1) Was a scheduled guild raid.
2) Was a raid conducted by the huntmaster or one of their chosen aids.
3) All loot obtained during a specific equipment raid, i.e.… we conduct a
raid in order to obtain Golden Efreeti Boots for our guild members, all loot
obtained from the start to the end shall be considered guild loot.
Distribution: All guild loot that is not distributed during or directly
after a raid will be handed over to the treasurer for future distribution to guild members or put up for sale. If the treasurer is not on hand then the
loot will be given to any officer on site, if no officer then it will be given over to the person in charge of that particular raid or the Huntmaster.
Guild loot will be awarded to the individual that can most use it that is
present during the raid. If that person already has a piece of equipment that was obtained from a previous guild raid then the item that was replaced
will be given back into the guild for redistribution to other members in
need.
When more then one person that is present during the raid is in need of the
item obtained then the standard in game random role system will be used by
those individuals.
When an item is obtained that no one wishes to have or can use then that
item will be rolled on by whomever desires that is present during the raid.
The individual that wins will not be in any other rolls on such equipment
described within this paragraph in order to allow others some personnel
treasure. Also, if an item is obtained later that can be used by this winner
then that winner has an option to give up that loot and receive the item
that they can use. That item given up will then be relottoed amongst the
members there.
If there is a questionable item, an item that can be used by either fighter
or caster, then it will be dealt with as regular loot and the rules put forth earlier in this section.
No Drop loot will be randomly rolled upon only by those classes that can use
that item if there is no one that is in need of such item. To prevent the
rotting of loot, if no one present is in need of such item or the item is
not class specific, then those present who wish to can roll for that item.
 

Policy for Hand-Me-Down Items

Guild members are strongly encouraged, but not required to hand down
'inferior' items when they receive guild-gained items.
Guild members who receive a guild-gotten item that replaces guild-gotten
item, should hand down the 'inferior' guild-gotten item.
Some members of the guild hand-down items with the provision "hand down this
when you are done with it." It is required that if you accept an item given
in this manner, that you hand down that item when you receive a better one,
whether that be from your own efforts, a guild raid, or another
hand-me-down.
Special situations will occur, and they will be handled on an
incident-by-incident basis in a manner that best benefits the guild as a
whole
