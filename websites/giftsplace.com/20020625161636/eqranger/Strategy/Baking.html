Feeding your Ranger

What would you pay for an item that gave you +3 to strength and stamina without needing to be equipped or triggered? What if this item wasn't Lore, and could give you your stat increases simply by being in your backpack? 200pp? 500pp? Well, take heart rangers because with a bit of patience and 100pp this item and many more like it could be yours.

What I cam taking about of course is baking. Although not profitable, baking is an extremely useful skill. Combined with foraging and brewing (once certain items are fixed), baking will ensure you have unlimited use of stat enhancing food. Here's how. 

How food is consumed

The first food item in your inventory is considered to be the food you are currently eating. Only after you are finished eating it will it disappear from your inventory. You can speed up this process by placing the food item in an unoccupied slot of your inventory and right clicking it. This will result in the following message.

Munch, munch. Cannan takes a bite of a ration.

Right-clicking an item makes you consume the whole food item immediately, instead of snacking on it for a while as you normally would. Eating food this way means you will take longer to consume your next food item, since you are now no longer hungry. However, if that food item you right-clicked on was a stat enhancing food item (such as Gator Steaks), you will no longer get its benefit since the food is considered eaten. 

But, if you put the Gator Steak in the top slot of your inventory (in a backpack if you desire) and don't right click it, then you will gain the stat benefit until it is finally consumed. That means you will get increased stats for at least 20 minutes, or however long it takes to eat it. Further, food items are classified according to how long it takes to consume them. There are 4 food categories: snack, meal, hearty meal, and banquet. A banquet meal lasts the longest; I believe at least twice as long as a meal.

Keeping your stat food forever

So, how do prevent this top-slot food item from being consumed? Simply, you make sure you are never hungry. Let me explain. Let's say you place a mammoth steak (str+2) in the top slot of your inventory. As soon as you do this, you will notice your strength increases. Now lets say you place 20 rations in an unused inventory slot. Before you begin your adventures you right click on the rations multiple times until you receive the following message:

You couldn't possibly eat anymore - you'd explode!

Not only are you no longer hungry, but you are positively stuffed. After every battle thereafter, say every 15 minutes, you right click your ration stack again and try to eat another ration. By the time you go through your food stack, hours will have passed and your mammoth steak will still be sitting in your top food slot, giving you your +2 strength bonus. As you buy more food and get into the habit of eating it once and a while you will notice that you never consume the mammoth steaks. +2 strength always.

Can this get any better? Yes! If you are a ranger, or any other class that has the forage skill, you don't even have to worry about buying the food. As long as you make sure you are foraging regularly, you will have an endless supply of food to eat. Further, this will ensure your foraging skill goes up, which will vital when you begin your epic quest. The Ranger epic requires rarely foraged items, and you won't be able to forage them without a high foraging skill. 

Baking to 135 the fast way

It is extremely easy to raise your baking skill to 135 - all you need is 100pp and some patience. I should note that there are many ways to raise your baking skill, but this is by far the most rapid. The only recipe you need to know is this: 

1 batwing + 1 fresh fish = 1 fish roll (combined in a spit).

Before beginning, make sure you have at least 6 empty backpacks on you, and one free inventory slot. Start in East Freeport and buy a spit - you can find one in one of the vendors by the docks. Then head to the vendor immediately on the dock (you pass through the
store on the way to the boat) and fill up at least 5 back packs with stacks of fresh fish.
Then, head to the East Commons to the gem vendor in the hut by the WC zone line. She sells batwings, the other needed component. Buy as many batwings as you have room for, and have a seat. Put one bat wing and one fresh fish in your spit and hit combine. Now do this several hundred more times, buying more batwings as you need. 

Fish rolls are trivial until 135, so after a few hours of making these food items you will find that you have become a master. More often than not you will fail at making fish rolls, but you are only trying to raise your skill so that doesn't matter. Keep the Fish rolls you manage to make - they are a banquet size meal and are perfect for filling you up!

Stat Food 

Now that you have achieved master status in bakery and know exactly how to keep yourself full, only on thing remains: making your stat food. Fortunately, if you have been following the above guide you will have the skill to make any stat food you want, and, even better, you won't have to make much since you won't be consuming it! Perhaps the best recipe guide can be fond on EQ Traders Corner. There you will an exhaustive list of baked items, with information on where to find the needed components.  Experiment a little until you find the food you want.  

Good luck, and I hope this guide has helped!
