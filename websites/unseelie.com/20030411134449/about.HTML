Kami the Faerie is actually a young artist from W. Virginia. Her talent has been building for many years trying to "out-do" her father, who was also a capable illustrator. Kami moved to Atlanta in the summer of '97 to attend college and become a computer animator. She met Tim here, as her RA in the school dorms. They hit it off, and it wasn't long before they were an item. She also met a strange guy named Matt, or as he calls himself, Matho. The three moved in together and had several adventures, both real and imagined. Life being what it is, Kami had to stop her schooling. But she continued to draw and learn computer techniques to enhance her work. Living the life of a "normal" young adult, Kami spent plenty of time with friends having fun, battling demons, and working a part-time job. Eventually she found herself a web designer at a small company in Atlanta with Tim. It was a boring job with little reward and a lot of stress. That job came to an end in 1999. At the beginning of 2000, she moved with Tim to Washington. Now, Kami spends her time working mundane jobs to survive, doing the fantasy art she loves, organizing roleplay guilds in EverQuest, and writing stories.

Hobbies: Playing video games, playing with faeries, dancing in thunderstorms, reading, writing, and role playing.
Goals: Learn to play guitar, learn to kick ass, and to make great video games.

Tim the Shadow has no homeland. He is the product of a military father, and as such has moved so many times he cannot name a "home town." But he graduated high school at Turner Ashby High School in the Harrisonburg, VA area. After graduating, he went to college in Atlanta to become a computer animator. He became an RA of his dorm and in the summer of '97 he met Kami. She, like many others in the school, was a young artist. But unlike the others she could actually draw more than just comic book characters. So a mutual respect formed that blossomed into something better: love. Tim graduated with a great demo reel (compared to his classmates, anyway) and hit the job market as fiercely as he could. Unfortunately, Atlanta wasn't looking for new computer animators at the time of his graduation. Becoming desperate, he took the first job offered to him as a web designer. Tim had no knowledge of the internet, HTML, or how to design a web site, but for some odd reason he got the job. Maybe it was his exceptional demo reel, maybe not. Most likely he was hired because he was the first to interview, showed up in a nice suit, and had a reasonably good knowledge of Photoshop. Also unfortunate, Tim managed to get Kami hired here. Over time, the job became more and more horrid: Too rigid for creativity, and too loose to make real progress. At a time of corporate restructuring, Tim and Kami were laid off. No harm done, the job sucked anyway. After many attempts to fit in at other web design jobs, Tim decided that he was never meant to be a web designer. He was a storyteller, and let's face it, corporate web design doesn't offer that. So now he survives with Kami in Washington, writing, drawing, and relearning the skills he once had as an animator.

Hobbies: Playing video games, writing, reading, role playing, 3D modeling and animation, and dreaming.
Goals: Animate his stories, release a good RPG, make great video games, and maintain sanity.

Matho the Mage (a.k.a. Matt) is not only the spiritual leader of countless numbers of pagans, he's also a damn good GM too. He too attended the Art Institute of Atlanta for a short time, but unlike a majority of the other students, he had imagination, vision, and spirit. With that imagination Matho started the world of Meledeam. Slowly, he swept his friends into it, and the world grew to be a rich tapestry of fantasy.

Hobbies: Challenging Bryan to Street Fighter, video games, writing, role playing, spirituality, and various mind expanding activities.
Goals: Beating Bryan every time at Street Fighter, releasing Meledeam, and ascending to another reality.
