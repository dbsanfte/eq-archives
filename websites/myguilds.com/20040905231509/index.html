This week
Posted: 8/27/2004 2:53:00 PM by Aaron Lewis
This week, many enhancements to existing features were made in order to further improve performance. Of particular note are load times on pages with external news feeds, and the default roster content type (especially noticeable to those guilds with hundreds of characters).

Also, officers and higher can now e-mail everyone in the guild by clicking on "New Message" in a inbox content type.

App question/answer pairs are now sorted properly in the e-mails that go out to member admins, and admins can now see join/promotion/disband dates for individual members by clicking on Tracking when viewing their information in the member admin tool.


Updates this week
Posted: 8/20/2004 9:30:00 AM by Aaron Lewis
Some folks were having difficulty with cross-frame stuff when trying to change their domain name in the Control Panel, under Subscription Services. This has been fixed.

Medals now sort correctly in "Manage Medals" in the control panel.

Renaming a topic in a forum will now also rename the first post in that topic, so that the Most Recent Posts display will show the new title.

The Raid Points content type will now only show members, as opposed to applicants.

When a member removes themselves from a guild using their account settings, it's now logged and can be seen from the Activity Logs in the Control Panel.

Application e-mails that go out to member admins now include the questions configured by the admins, and the requesting user's answers.

Importing to Outlook is now supported in the "Calendar - New" content type.


News Enhancements
Posted: 8/8/2004 10:01:00 AM by Aaron Lewis
Several enhancements have been made to the large Guild News content type. First, when editing an individual news item, news admins can now specify whether comments are allowed from members of the guild. Next, from the main news edit page (where you are able to add a new item or modify existing ones), a new link called "News Display Options" is available.

Within the News Display Options, you can specify how many news items to display in a large Guild News content type (previously, it was hard-coded at 5). Finally, you can specify whether news items are always fully displayed, or whether they are cut off with a "Read More" link at the end. Guilds may want to use this functionality if their news items tend to be lengthy.


Quests and Characters
Posted: 8/7/2004 9:30:00 PM by Aaron Lewis
Individual characters can now be marked as having completed steps of a quest, including multiple characters per member. When viewing quest details, each step shows a list of who's completed it, and a link is available at the bottom that says "Manage Character Completion."

When clicked, the user is taken to a list of their characters for the game the guild plays, where they can select as many of them as they'd like.
