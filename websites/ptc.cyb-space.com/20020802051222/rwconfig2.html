When running Roger Wilco the first time, you will need to register. I will describe how to run and connect to the PTC voice chat server, and how to register Roger Wilco

Initially this is what Roger Wilco will look like when you start it. To join the PTC server, click the [Join] button.

Type in the above in the Join Channel prompt, and click the [Join] button, and you should be in the chat channel.

What ? - Registration required. Oh no - more expenses ! - Now don't worry, its free and its easy. Press the [Yes] button. (Sorry my operating system is localized and therefore the weird text on the bottoms.

You will be presented with a screen like the above, and you will have to select the link Visit Registratyion server.

Now fill out the form. It requires only a click on the Agree, and select your country and sound card infromation. Then click the [Proceed with Registration] button.

Hmmm .... They are kind of slow in there - the registration information to review is massive - Was it the right country you typed ? [Click here to register]

Which makes you ready to go. Roger Wilco is now running, connected and ready to work. Click the minimize [_] button, and the program will be in your system task tray, in the lower right corner of your screen (where the clock is displayed).
