Clopsofobia...

This story does not reflect any facts or any real happenings. It is purely my own conclusions based on the screenshots that i have received from Eriick. Reference to "I" in the following is to mean Eriick and not me, the author of this nutty story.

Boober our fine and smelly ogre, had approached our lovely leader Laurefin "goldielocks" Lonestarr for some serious talk about the dreaded Clopsofobia that he has been having since his childhood. Laurefin, being the local shrink of our guild, imidiatly understood the severity of this and started to talk very seriously with Boober.

I and the rest of us, were kind of left behind and were wondering what was going on, and most of all, "Where are we going?" - We followed the two as they walked in front of us and talked very silently. Little did we know that Laurefin had already plotted a way of getting rid of the dreaded Clopsofobia.

The two were completely lost in their talks, that they didn't realize how much we had to fight just to keep all the mobs away from them while they were walking. But hey, since they were so into the talking, it must have been important, we thought. We crossed a third of Norath and entered Ocean oif Tears, heading straight towards Clops territory, and then it happened ...

A 'Clop obviously happened to cross the way of the two talking parrots and there was little the rest of us could do than to just stand and watch. Laurefin did make a move to show us that this was intentional though. Boober was screaming so loud that it went through our bones and marrow. - You know the kind of scream only an Ogre could make.

The Clop on the other hand, probably got an even bigger shock than the two of them. We still don't know for sure weither it was Boobers scream or the smell that made the Clop try to run away in terror.

Allthough it was obvious to us that Boober was scared, the talks with Laurefin must have done something to him. He imidiatly charged the clop. Screaming, kicking, biting, bashing, slashing, hacking, whacking he was determined not to let the clop ruin the day. Allthough the most probably cause was that he wanted to ruin the clops day. Boober was singlehandedly managing this clop, and he didn't even notice in his battle fury.

The battle was over in a very short time. Boober was just standing there, watching the dead Clop, and Laurefin turned to him and said. "See there, Boober. You did that alone. Now why is it that you are afraid of those ?" Boober was standing there watching the dead corpse of a clop, not knowing what to do about it. "Your kill Boober, so it's your loot" said Laurefin.

Boober walked cautiously towards the clop, he slowly realized that his clopsofobia had goine with the kill. He traightened up and firmly began to go through the clops's pockets.

We all sat down to talk with Boober now. He explained the whole thing and we all had a good laugh about the Clopsofobia. Laur on the other hand looked somewhat puzzled. She has later told me that she had heard a lot of strange voices "Like as if it was Clop voices" she said to me, "Asking for help.".

A new dreaded disease has been roumored to have infected most clops in Ocean of Tears ... Booberfobia.

Moctemoc,
High Elf Mage.
