GUILD NEWS FLASH JUN. 2000
 
June 17, 2000
Hear Ye, Hear Ye,

Tales have been told, and fortunes read. I have had the possibilty of looking into one of those greater lightstones, and it amazed me what I could see. Allthough my eyes were burnt, my soul was touched, I saw a prosperous future for two of our guild members, united in holy bands in the near future. A wedding is due within the ranks of the Phoenix Trading Company. I could not make out any faces, but I saw a human female. I will bet the vision gets clearer within the next three to five weeks. Make room in your calendars as the invitations soon will go out.
