Moctemoc’s story of his life.

Part 2, The land of Norrath.

Moctemoc was standing in front of the gate, he didn't know what was going to happen once a living object passed the gate. He hesitated at first, checking that he brought everything with him that he would need in this strange land. Then he took a firm decission and stepped into the gate.

Everything went dark. He felt as if he was torn apart while he was tumbling down from a high cliff. It was as if his conciousness had left him and he was dreaming a bad dream. He tried to open his eyes, but they were open. He pinched himself and it hurt. He was sure it wasn't a dream.

Suddenly there was a bright light. It took some time for his eyes to get used to the light. Even though it felt like several minutes, it must have been just a few second, for when he could see again, he saw the gate close to his homeland and captured the last glimpse of his old world.

Moctemoc was looking around. He was in a city. He asked the first person he met where he was. "You, my friend are in the fine city of Felwithe" came the answer. "And i am a merchant. You look new to this city." he continued. "You look like you could use some shoes. It's not the hottest season to be running barefoot."

Moctemoc was looking at himself, and to his amasement he discovered that he somehow had changed. He was no longer the same person, as if he had assumed or claimed someone elses body. His face was still the same, maybe slightly more gray hair as before, but still, the same. He had new clothes. He checked to see if he still had all his possessions from his homeland. Phew, he had them all. A knife, some food and milk, a couple of notes, one of which was a letter he had to give to someone in this new land, and some magical spells that he had researched. Moctemoc now eqiped himself with the knife, and started to find his bearin gin this new city. A city he was to claim his home from now on. He was walking along in the city trying to figure out where the person he needed to talk to was. He finally found him. As a matter of fact, he was amased that it wasn't a man, but a woman. He introduced himself, and was greeted kind of cold. He gave the woman the note he had received earlier, and he was imidiatly accepted in the guild of the mages, and was given a guild tunic. He was to learn more of his skills and magic capabilities before he was to leave this place.

He studied, and was warned that Norrath wasn't the most pleasont of places he could have choosen. He tried to contact the guild of the wizard's to learn more about his father, but no one wanted to talk to him, except the merchants. He did not learn more about Moc, nor where he had been or what he had done. No one here seemed to have any knowledge of him.

Moctemoc soon thought it was time to leave and search for his family and perhaps find his fathers remains. He thought he was a great mage, and would do very well in this land, even if he had been warned. Moctemoc knew that he had a power that was far stronger than he could imagine, and he chose to go adventure in the land. Moctemoc was soon to learn the true nature of this cruel and evil infested land of Norrath.

He entered Greater Faydwer, and saw an enourmous amount of strange beings fighting, just outside the gate. He turned to the guards and asked them why they didn't help. "But we do help" the guards assured. "It's just, there are so many of these beings, that we cannot run around in the land and kill them all. Anyway who is to guard our wonderfull city while we do this." Moctemoc nodded. There was something right in what the guard said. "So you will aid us if we are in danger, and you still have the ability to guard the city?" Moctemoc asked. "Yes", said the guard. The two guards at the gate looked at eachother, as if they said "Oh dear, yet another one trying to leave the city, and we have to take care if him too".

Moctemoc carefully walked into the forrest. He tried to figure out what kind of beings where there. None of them seemed to want to attack Moctemoc. So he walked further. "Hey", he thought "this is great, easy as a piece of cake. You leave them alone, and they leave you alone." So Moctemoc walked around outside the city, avoiding all fighting. Suddenly he was struck from the behind. He swirled around and looked into a little ugly create. It was only a third of his height, but had a punch that allmsot threw Moctemoc to the ground. Moctemoc grabbed his dagger, and started to pierce the being. But he couldnt hit him. The Orc pawn, thats was what this being was called, was much topo fast for Moctemoc. Moctemoc tried to use his strongest magic to kill the Orc. And he did hit him once, but it was a very strong little fellow, and Moctemoc had to run towards the guards. Yelling for help, and just before Moctemoc went unconcious the guards reached Moctemoc and the Orc, and slayed it. Moctemoc had to sit for a long time, to regain his breath and then asked the guard what was going on. The guard said "Oh those Orcs. They are pretty annoying, but the small pawns usually leave us alone. You should see the ones in Crushbone, that's some mean orcs."

"Yeah, I understand", Moctemoc said, "But why are people fighting bats and those giant wasp's rather than ridding the land of the Orcs?" The guards both laughed at Moctemoc. "Well my friend", one of the guards said, "To be able to fight the orcs, you wil have to learn how to defend yourself, and there is no better way to learn that, than fighting. But choose wisely who you fight with. A too strong oponent may kill you, and a too weak one will not make your skill in fighting any better." Moctemoc suddenly understood. "I see, so Everyone here is fighting the bats and wasps to learn how to fight. Well then, if thats what it takes to be able to kick those orc pawns back to Crushbone, then that is what I will do." And Moctemoc started to work his fighting skills and magic capabilities.
