Starting Tailoring

Tailoring is started by aquiring a sewing kit of any size, preferably the size of the items you want to manufacture, but any size will do for starting up the tailoring. Obtain Spider Silks (unstackable), remember not spiderling silks, and combine two of these. This will produce Silk Swatches, which are stackable. To get further recipies you must buy books sold by vendors, and then obtain the different items required to tailor the appripriate item you want to manufacture. Remember that large size items usually require more stuff to be combined than small size items.

Mail recipies to ptc@cyb-space.com to get them included here in this list.
