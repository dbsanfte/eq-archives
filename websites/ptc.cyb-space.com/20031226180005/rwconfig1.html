GUILD IN-GAME VOICE CHAT
 
If this is your first time running the program, jump directly to the configuration section.

To enter configuration at a later date, you must select the Adjust tab, and press the [Configure] button.

Configuration section.

First time installation and configuration of Roger Wilco requires a registration and test of the sound card and microphone. Before you run the Roger Wilco software, you should be sure to have everything ready. Allthough it is not neccesary to have everything connected and ready, I would recommend that you have this, since you will be able to test that your system will work with the equipment you have.

Insert the headphone/speaker in the sound card jack marked as speaker, alternately it may be color coded with the color green. Your microphone goes into the sound card jack marked Mic, or alternately colored Red. The color codings are the PC97 or PC99 standard color codes, and should be used on all computers of later date.

You may have to upgrade some drivers for the appropriate sond card that you have, but this is only if you have any problems during the configuration and running the program. In such a case, I would recommend that you contact me, and I will then be happy to guide you through the process of testing and upgrading your sound card drivers.

After installing start the roger wilco program, by double clicking its icon on the desktop or selecting it in the Start menu.

Initial start of Roger Wilco will initiate the configuration Wizard, which is made to guide you through the configuration of the system for the first time. This wizard can be restarted later, in case you need to reconfigure the system. Press the [Next >>] button.

First configuration option in Roger Wilco is how to record from the Microphone. You have two options, either Wav recording, which is the default, or DirectSound Recording. Initially you should select Wav Recording. However, some sound cards have an emulated Wav recording device, which is not fully compatible with Roger Wilco, and you may have to select DirectSound Recording. I will try to compile a list of Wav recording sound cards and DirectSound recording sound cards, but for now, leave it at Wav, and press [Next >>].

You will now have to select the device that is for playback and recording. The picture above shows the settings for a Creative SoundBlaster PCI128 sound card. You would normally leave the Playback Device at Primary Sound Driver, and the Record Device at the default settings. The Microphone Selection determines if Roger Wilco will try to determine the Microphone as the default recording device. Since most sound cards have several recording facilities such as Line in, CD In... leave this setting at Automatic. Pressing the [Verify Settings] will bring up the Mixer, where you may select the Microphone as the recording device. When ready, press the [Next >>] button.

Now comes the time for test and adjusting the input level and playback level of your sound card and settings. It is also in this section that you may receive your first really tricky error message. First of all, you must be sure that the speakers/headphones are working, and that the output level is adjusted. If you have had sound on the speakers/headphones when you switched on your computer or has been using a game with sound, this should be the case. The lever at the left, adjust the input level of the microphone. For a start, just leave it at the mid position, we wouldnt like to fall down the chair when you herar your own voice wouldnøt we ? To test, press the [Test Mike] button, and speak into the mike in a normal voice. Depressing the [Test Mike] button, will dim this button and change the name to [Recording]. You may or may not be able to hear yourself in the headset while speaking, but don't worry if you dont. The recording will take about 5 to 10 seconds, and Roger Wilco will then try to playback what you spoke. If the sound level is too low, adjust the lever upwards, and test again. Do this until you reach an acceptable level. In case you have a great distortion, try to adjust the lever down, until the distortion is gone. Distortion is quite normal with different microphones, and can be caused by the microphone being way to sensitive. There are several ways to adjust this, but I will not comment any further on this here, but leave that to a later discussion, should the topic come up.

When you are happy with the voice level, press the [Next >>] button. Pressing the [System Settings] button, will bring the mixer up, for you to select the correct recording source in case you missed that previously.

You must next select full or half duplex mode. Half duplex only allows you to hear everyone else, whereas full duplex allows you to use the microphone to spam everyone with your stories and your singing. The Roger Wilco Wizard, should have Full Duplex preselected. If not, then your soundcard may not support full duplex mode, which unfortunately is a requirement if you want to speak with the other guild members. Select the [Next >>] button to continue.

To speak, you will have to press a key. I recommend this rather than having voice activated detection. Imagine you have just finished eating and you suddenly burp loudly. Ok, it would be quite a laugh, but continiously hearing everyone burp around eating time would probably become rather annoying. Click with the mouse in the white square, where it says F12, and press the key you want to activate voice. I have personally selected the Scroll Lock key, which is not used in EQ. But you may select any key you like, as long as EQ does not require that key. When done, press the [Next >>] button.

You will now have to select the speed of your modem, and basically how much bandwidth the Roger Wilco program will take away for the transmission of speach. I recommend that you leave this at the Slow Modem setting to start with. If you don't feel any worsening of the gaming speed, you may select a higher value at a later time. Remember though, that when you feel the game speed is suffering from the speach, run the wizard again and select the speed one lower than you had. The slower the modem connection is selected the lower quality will the speachh be. The UDP port is relating to the transmission area of the IP protocol. When using the Internet all communications is done using TCP/IP or UDP, both are essentially the same, but UDP is better for sending information that are not required to be totally perfect. Everytime you connect to somehwere in the Internet, you use a small part of the TCP protocol, and you can have a theoretic maximum of 65535 concurrent connection going. The Roger Wilco program, will use one of these 65k connections as default. Just leave the value and click the [Next >>] button.

Now to select how much power will be consumed from your computer when Roger Wilco is running. You must select how much CPU power will be used to run Roger Wilco. I recommend the High Priority setting, but you may require to have it set at low. Experiment with this value once you start playing with the voice in-game. [Next >>]

You must now select your callsign. This is probably the most unimportant thing of the whole, since we will be running a game, and will not see any callsigns when in game. However, it is wise to choose a name here, at least then I can see at the console who has connected. [Next >>]

Thats it - Take a vacation, you have just increased you technical skill by 274.
