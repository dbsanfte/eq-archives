May 25, 2000
Kunark CD Key Problem

If bying the Kunark CD, full version, the latest key codes are only ten digits long, and will not work when upgrading an existing account. Creating a new account with these codes will however work. It is, accordiung to the European distributor, the last digit or letter that is missing, and in theory you can try out all possible solutions, but - do NOT attempt to do it, as Verant may think you are trying to break their keys, and therefore may ban you account.

All servers will be coming down at 8am Pacific on Thursday the 25th. The expected downtime is 4 hours.

- The EverQuest Team

I will try to be on-line in the voice and chat room, to answer any questions of technical nature in the four hours of time we have to kill. While on-line, I will naturally monitor the status of the servers, as to tell you when the system is up and running again.
