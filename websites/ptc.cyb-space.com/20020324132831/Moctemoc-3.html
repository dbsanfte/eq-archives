Moctemoc’s story of his life.

Part 3, Beyond the home town.

Allthough Moctemoc was training hard as he could, and ventured deeper and deeper into Orc land, he never felt comfortable with the Orc's running around him. He was also intrigued by the information that came from furthest away from Norath. Erudin, was supposed to be the last place where someone had seen Moc last.

Moctemoc wanted to get stronger, but was very afraid to travel too far away from his home city. One day came a Druid to Moctemoc and said he knew where his father's remains where. Moc was thrilled and sad at the same time. Had his father really died, and was this the proof that he needed. The druid offered to take Moctemoc to the land where he had seen Moc's remains, but Moctemoc protested and said he was not ready for such a long travel. "But I'm a druid. I will take care of you and being one with the nature, I have ways of traveling the entire world in a few seconds. Besides, you want to find your father don't you? And if I can show you his remains, you will know for sure what has happened." Moctemoc finally agreed upon going with the druid.

"We're going to High Hold. Thats where I have seen Moc." said the druid. "Ot will be dangerous, but don't worry. I will take care of you all the way. And I will take you back if you do not choose to stay there." Moctemoc was happy with that explanation, and since he thought he had grown to know this druid he accepted.

Druids surely are wise and strange beings. They can travel all over Norath in a few minutes. The druid took Moctemoc to West Commons, and they both headed towards the Forrest of Kithicor. It was night, and the druid said that the forrest was haunted by undeads. Moctemoc grew more and more afraid. But the druid assured him that with the magic of the Wolf and invisibilty it would be no problem to cross the Forrest of Kithicor even at night. Moctemoc allthough sure that the druid spoke the truth was still not comfortable at all. But they traveled on towards the city of High Hold.

Arriving at High Hold the next morning, the druid guided Moctemoc into the Keep to show him where Moc's remains were. Entering the Keep, a messenger came running past the two travellers, and said that the Druid must come to the rescue. The druid started to work quickly, and found a friendly soul that would organize for Moctemoc to be inhabitant of High Hold from now on. And so it happened, Moctemoc was outside of his home town, being an alien in another town, but accepted and forced to stay there for a while. The druid quickly departed to help his friends and was never seen again by Moctemoc, allthough the druid promised to return.

Moctemoc was longing for his own home, Felwithe. He was very young and still unable to travel and settle down somewhere else by his own. Did he have the guts and the strength to travel back all by himself ? Yes he did. He was able to get from Kramned to Norath, follow this crazy druid to High Hold Keep. So why shouldn't he be capable of returning to Felwithe by himself. He started packing all his gear, and began the long travel back to his home town.

Going out of High Hold was tricky. Lots of very strong Orc's where guarding the town exit toward the dreaded Kithicor Forrest. But Moctemoc had learned from the druid. He tried the magic of invisibility. It din't work. He tried again, and this time it was working. He then ran - the fastest he had learned, and he ran and ran. No way was he going to stop and look back, just run until he could see some friendly people again.

He fainted when his strength was all used up. And the next morning when he woke up to a nice and sunny day he could recognize the area as a place he had travelled through a few days ago. West Commonlands. Ah nice he thought. No problems here. I can just walk through this wonderfull green land, enjoy the nature and continue onwards to the Human city of Freeport. He walked towards Freeport unafraid of anything and not knowing that he was followed. Some undead from Kithicor Forrest must have caugt scent of Moctemoc and followed him half way to Freeport, for it was just before entering the East Commonlands, that Moctemoc was jumped by this undead Zombie. The Zombie was hitting hard on Moctemoc. Who first didn't know what to do, no where to run. No one to help him. All alone. Moctemoc's brain started to work. And he concentrated. All powers put into his magic, he tried to cast the fire of hell onto this Zombie. Hoping that would make it stop attacking. SMACK! The Zombie hit Moctemoc hard. Almost threw him off his feet. The Fire that Moctemoc tried to cast hit a nearby tree that started to burn. Moctemoc quickly got back on his feet and tried again, allthough weekened and badly hurt. The Zombie tried to strike again, but as if a miracle had happened or the gods of Norath had kept an eye on Moctemoc, the Zombie didn't hit. Ka-Boom - Fire! - and lots of it hit the Zombie. It was just standing there, now knowing what had hit it. It probably didn't think that such a stupid looking creature would posess these magic powers. Moctemoc began the casting again, knowing that he had nothing left in him if this didn't work out. The Zombie just stood their enveloped in flames. Ka-Boom. Direct hit. The Zombie fell to the ground. The spell that had it bound to the undead was gone. It died a natural death. Moctemoc was close to loosing his life as well. No more magic left in him. Allmost no more life force was left. Moctemoc moved away from the spot, afraid that there might be more of these undead. He didn't stop until he was in East Commons and he fell down completely exhausted.

Allthough many people passed him on the road. No one seemed to take notice of him. No help from anyone. Moctemoc just sat there and waited for his life force and his magic strength to return. He rested for almost a day before he was ready to continue his travel. It was not before late evening that he came to the borders of the city of Freeport. He dared not go into freeport, but just sat there at the border, watching the guards watching him. He moved cautiously moved close to the guards, and hailed them, but they just looked dubiously at Moctemoc and didn't say a single word. Moctemoc was cold, hungry and thirsty. He simply had to enter town to find someone to help him through the rest of his travel.

He was in luck. A friendly half elf hailed Moctemoc and started to talk to him. Probably because Moctemoc looked alone and scared. The friendly Half elf explained the ship, and the traveling to and from Freeport The half-elf even followed Moctemoc down to the docks and showed him where the boat would come in. Moctemoc sat down at the docks, wating for the boat and staring into the water. He fell asleep.

"BOAT - BOAT Approaching", he shouted. Moctemoc woke up. Looked around and didnt see a boat nowhere. He scratched his head, stood up, and looked towards the sea. There, far away was a shimmer of a ship sailing towards the docks. It grew bigger and bigger until it was at the dock. Moctemoc was the first to enter the boat. Eager to return home to his own city. Thing was unloaded and other things where loaded on the boat before it sat sail towards Faydwer again.

People were talking all kinds of languages on the boat. Some of which Moctemoc didn't understand a single word of. The boat crossed the Ocean of Tears and stopped once to pick up mail from a wood elf outpost. There was lots of talk about Sea Furies tha roamed the waters and everyone where afraid that one would attack the ship and everyone would drown. The trip went on but this time, it took very long before the sailor's shouted "Land Ho!".

Moctemoc jumped off the boat and looked around. He was standing on the docks of Kaladim. A dwarf city that he only had heard about. But he had investigated the maps of the area and knew how to go through the land and get back to Greater Faydark and the city of Kelethin. And so he traveled back towards his home town. He came a little down the road and found himself puzzled with a great statue of a dwarf standing over the entrance to the city of Kaladim.

"Nice one. Isn't it ?" a voice said behind him. Moctemoc nodded. "It's been there as long as I can remember and I'm growing old. Oh, sorry friend. My name is Drolgin. Drolgin Steingrinder. Proud of that name." Moctemoc turned around and saw a stout little dwarf standing there looking at him. "Oi, mate. Come down 'ere". Moctemoc sat down next to Drolgin. "So you're a dwarf. And you live here." Moctemoc sort of stated. "Thats goddamn right. I'm a dwarf and I'm proud of it. But I'm also a Paladin, and I'm proud of that too. Steingrinder is the name, and I'm proud of that too." Drolgin replied. "So what's a Dwarf Paladin doing in this land?" Moctemoc asked. Drolgin sarted to laugh loudly. "Well, the same as everyone else in this 'ere land is doing. Slaying all the bad guys and helping all the good guys." Moctemoc nodded, allthough he hadn't figured out the good and bad guy thing yet. "Mighty fine sword that you have there my good Drolgin. What does it cost ?" Moctemoc asked, just to keep the conversation alive. Drolgin replied,"Mmmm. Yes the sword is a mans best friend. But you wouldn't know what I am talking about. You look like some kind of magic user to me, and you will never get to wield a sword." Drolgin sighed. "So I won't try to sell you this sword, even though I am a member of the mighty fine Phoenix Trading Company." Moctemoc added "And you're proud of that." "Aye" Drolgin replied.

"You look mighty young to be traveling to Kaladim by yourself. What might you have been doing this far from youre home?" Drolgin started to question. Moctemoc answered promptly; "Oh I've come from High Hold just now. Was sort of kidnapped by a Druid." Drolgin began to laugh. "Har, har. I think I know who that would be. Do not listen to that guy. He is crazy. As long as you are alone and have't joined a community or guild or anything, you be carefull who you follow around. Don't take me wrong. Most people are ok, but you never know when you are up against someone who has too much on their mind to keep a promise."

"You don't need to say that twice." Moctemoc replied. "But how do you join a comunity or guild. I mean I am a member of the Mage guild. Does that count?" Drolgins eyes grew bigger, then he burst into laughter. "No my friend, being a member of a mage guild simply gives you a pass to enter the mage complexes around Norath. It does not help you in your everyday life and keep you company. For that you must find a group of people that you like and that fits to your way of spending your time." - "I see" Moctemoc said, but he didn't quite understand that yet.

"Oi mate. I gotta be running along now. My guild needs me. We gotta slay some of the bad guys, and save the fair maiden." Drolgin waved goodbye, leaving Moctemoc to think about what he had just been told. Moctemoc continued his journey back to Felwithe, having thoughts about guilds, friends and what to do next. Moctemoc reached Felwithe in safety and had to bribe someone the amount of 5 platinum pieces to be able to move back into his own town again. That was all the money he had, and now it was as if he had to start his life in Felwithe all over again.
