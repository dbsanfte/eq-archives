GUILD RULES

Membership: From this day forward we will only accept for membership levels 15 and higher. People who have personal friends wanting to enter guild will be placed on a honorary members list till they reach 15th level.

Officers will hunt with prospective members during a two week getting to know you period before a guild invite will be given. Your name will be posted on the message board as prospective member. Before and during this getting to know you time the guild will need information about you for the membership list. All prospective members are required to send e-mail to ptc@cyb-space.com and leave an introduction on the message board. Please include information of class, level, and preferably a screenshot of yourself.

Officers and members with secondary characters can select to get these invited in guild without the probation period, but knowledge of class and race must be send to the e-mail above.

Officers who have RL friends may have their friends invited directly, but still on probation for a period of 2 weeks, and the prospective members area must still be used, as well as the e-mail.

No officers have been appointed as special recruiting officer.

Duties are, however, as follows:

Wrynkle - Hybrids (RNG, SK, PAL, BARD)
Kwikster - INT based Casters (WIZ, MAG, ENC, NEC)
Oter - Support (CLR, SHA, DRU)
Palwen - Melee (MNK, WAR, ROG)
Code of Conduct Members will be expected to act with dignity and honor.

No kill stealing. No cussing out loud or being rude to other players or guild members. No spamming or showing disrespect. Never gate out and leave group members to die. No ninja looting. All are assumed to act with maturity and remember you carry the guilds name that is to be kept from being tarnished in any way. Officers are expected to conduct even more by these rules.

Members are expected to put the guild next to themselves. Lend out items to guild members that you dont need yourself, and preferable sell or give items to guild members rather than selling them for some easy cash. Items given to you from guild members are expected to stay in guild, or be returned to the guild or the one who gave it to you.
Guild Dues

10 pp for levels 15 to 19
25 pp for levels 20 to 29
45 pp for level 30 to 39
75 pp for levels 40 and up

Treasurer:  Laurefin and Wrynkle
Guild Day/Event No specific guild day is assigned, however a guild day will generally be held twice a month. Around the 15th, dues are collected in a guild only day, where anything can happen.

Around end/beginning of month a dungeon day or perhaps week will be held. Attendance is not mandatory to either of the guild/event days, but paying the dues are.
Be a buddy Higher level characters will try and pick a different members each week and help them work their levels. This can be done by healing while they fight, showing them good places to hunt or offering advice on skills and training.

GUILD BACKGROUND

The Phoenix Trading Company was formed by Dinthek Everhott. Dinthek came from a family whose skill in blacksmithing were famous in the Kingdom of King Stormhammer . The Everhott family has been furnishing armor and weapons to the soldiers of King Stormhammer , in Kaladim for centuries . The young Dinthek decided to open a Trading Company that could offer not only weapons and armor but goods of all kinds. Dinthek's father was none too pleased with this and refused to loan Dinthek the gold needed to get started, so Dinthek found his own backer. Kaladin , son of King Stormhammer and best friend of Dinthek , offered to buy into the PTC and become a silent partner . Dinthek then sent word out to his Pack of friends who all came from far lands to join the company , thus was born the Phoenix Trading Company. The Company's goals are to offer fast , friendly services of all sorts. To take orders of rare and hard to find items and to use the skills of the members to better the members and for selling in the open market. Our long range goals are to buy a warehouse in Freeport and to have a fleet of ships to transport these products.

Dinthek however had build up an empire that was evelving on its own, and had to leave this empire he had build up, to seek new grounds of hunting. The new leader of the guild was assigned to be Laurefin. Since the day of Dinthek leaving the guild, the original sales and trading has been kept alive, allthough impossible to keep as it was, it still exists. The guild has very good number of members all working on their skills to perform and craft the best possible out of the nature of Norath.
