Research

Research is the spellcaster prime way of aquiering spells after level 16, and especially after level 24. Every full caster type must obtain a special research book or Item, which lets them combine certain components to fabricate a new spell. The fabrication is not allways succesfull, but depends on the skill aquired in research. To practice research, one may get hold of the Concordance of Research, which is used to make so called practice runes. This item can be obtained by venturing to North Karana, and visiting the Gipsy camp. You must give a Greater Lightstone (obtained by killing Willowisp's) to one of the gypsies. Be sure to check which one it is by hailing, and asking the appropriate question (The quest wasn't broken when I tried). After you have aquired the Concordance of Research, you must collect different items to produce practice runes. Practice runes are NOT dropped by any mob in the game and can only be made by using this Concordance of Research. The practice runes must be paired, an Azia type rune, with a Bezia type rune, of the appropriate level. I assume there is a trivality list, which I will try to make up according to my findings.

To produce a practice rune, you will require a Quill and a piece of Parchment, which can be bought in the guild hall. You will only need a single Quill. Each time you create a rune, the Parchment will be destroyed, but you will keep the Quill.

The product (Azia and Bezia) runes, will have to be combined in the research book i.e. NOT in the Concordance of Research. This should quickly raise your skill in research.

Starting Research

I placed a practice point in the Research as soon as I got to level 16 (I am a mage), but did not research any spells before level 24, since the level 16 and 20 spells can be bought from a High Elf vendor in oot. Obtain Concordance of Research. Collect and create the Azia and Bezia runes. Creating the practice Runes does not require any skill, and does not fail. Combine these in the appropriate Research Book. This however requires skill, and will fail randomly, even if trivial. Combining the practice runes creates a Practice run (complete), which use I so far have only found to be to sell for the few silver you can get for it.
