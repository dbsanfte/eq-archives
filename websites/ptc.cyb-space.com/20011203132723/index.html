Wednesday, May 16, 2001 Volume I Issue 2
Goooooooooooooooood Morning PTC!!!


:: NEWS ::

Well it was quite a week here in the PTC. Our Vox raid didn't go as planned but we almost had that dragon!
Palwen spent most of last tuesday running from zone to zone trying to put together a small army to take on Vox. Golddigger also lent a hand raising troops. Well when it got closer to the time of the Raid we ran into a small problem. There was a naggy raid going on at the same time
Well Palwen contacted the leader of that raid useing his trusty gnome globe of seeing, talking, shouting, whispering viewing free long distance 1000 minutes weekends 500 minutes weekdays free sundays comunicator. {phew damn gnomes get a shorter name} After discussing with the Naggy leader they made a pact that they'd join forces to slay both beasts. So while Palwen stayed in Perma he sent many adventures and treasure hunters to lavastorm to face the Red dragon. Due to the unseen difficulties of getting to the entrance and slaying Naggy, Our Vox raid was delayed for a few hours. When we finally got our forces strong enough we began out assault on the Ice giants who kept us from the entrance of her lair....
{editors note: This was the last transmission I received from Palwen after many hours of silence I packed lunch grabed me trusty dagger and took to the road to check up on them}

=

A awesome spectacle was it as dwarves, humans, elves, darkelves, halflings, Iksar, and gnomes, all as one, fuaght these giants and made our way to the front of the lair of this gigantic lizard. All looked well for us as we moved a large rock away from the lair thinking we would catch Vox unknowingly. Alas she knew we were here for a Giant had been dispatched to warn her of the our approach. As the First few valiant warriors entered it would be the breath they would breath. Vox was upon us instantly, slaying many in the few seconds it takes to blink your eye. The Icy breath Froze one barbarian instantly {for he is now just a statue for her lair} another tryed to jump away was frozen in midair. Recovering from the intial surprise we rallied what was left of our army and managed to push her back deeper into her lair or we thought. Lady Vox was just lureing us back to allow her pet Icy Terrors to take us from behind. Our spell casters were surprised from behind as The giants we had thought were all dead sprang from hiding and tore through their ranks. Slowly but surely we held our lines but Being caught in the middle of the lair with no escape They knew death was near. In heroic fashion we charged the Dragon hoping to take her with us. It was a ugly sight as talons and fists slashed and beat flesh, Steel fell from hands and our Magic faltered out.........
I came upon the seen to late to see this glorious battle, but learnt of armies fate from a dieing dwarf who somehow managed to survive the genocide his Brothers and Sisters in Arms met. He told me this story begging me to raise another Army to come back and avenge their deaths. I swore that I would return one day and avenge the death though my words fell to deaf ears, for the dwarf who's name I never got had passed on to the next life. The carnage I saw was unreal bodies laid in pieces about, what was once white snow was now red with blood, swords and spears lay broken about. It was an ugly sight, such a site that has brought sorrow to my cold heart why did these men and women have to die such Horrible deaths, was this all worth it. That is when I heard the laughter, bone chilling laughter, echo through the caves of Permafrost laughter of an evil beast who had enjoyed the suffering she had cause, at that moment I knew this land must be rid of the dragon, Lady Vox must be slained. I am now training with many armies learning all I need to know so that I may one day come back and complete the promise I made that night. Oh Vox will die, but it will be a slow and painful death, one does not anger a Rogue who specializes in poison and torture.....

=

So it was that the raid was a failure but with failure comes strength, new friends and allies, we know now what we must do that was wrong the 1st time, and hey it was fun

This past Weekend was an exciting one for us here in the PTC.
