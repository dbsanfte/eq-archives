Inklevasion...

This story does not reflect any facts or any real happenings. It is purely my own conclusions based on the screenshots that i have received from Eriick.

Inkle's are a returning phenomen in the Norathian wildlife. Allthough thought to have been extinct, they seem to return every hundred years or so, and this is why we have set out to explore these due to recent roumors of sightings in Rivervale. To show you what Inkle's are and what they do, we have the pleasure of following our professor of Norathian natural history, William Bernard Straw from the Nuttural History Museum of Freeport, on one of his expeditions to investigate the natural habitats of this wild little critter.

Inkle's are some of the most strange inhabitants of the Norathian world. They come in all shapes and forms, but their most common form is defenetely the small Clerical Halfling types. I had the great pleasure of following two Inkle's for some time, a male and a female. I specifically selected the two based on their youth, since they would probably be less sceptic of letting a stranger come into their normally very private life.

Inkle's are know to be very fond of water, and that is probably why they are in and around the city of Rivervale with its many ponds. Inkle's also have a very good ear for music, and the music in this city probably also attracts these little critters a lot. I found the yoiung pair of Inkle's on one of my dives in the seas. At first they seemed very uppset that I, a complete stranger, tried to press myself into their most private life, but I managed to catch a nice photo of them fleeing away from me after their initial encounter with me.

To approach an Inkle requires lots of skill, and they far more dangerous than they seem, but I really needed the plat that I got from this photo expedition to buy myself some new scuba gear, so I opted to go to their home place to try to sneak up on them and gain their trust. At night I sneaked into Rivervale and was waiting for them to show up at dawn. And I was right - there they where right in front of me.

Cautiously I got closer to them, sneaking the best I've ever learned. They were close. I wasn't hiding anymore, I thought that now was the time to show myself. I thought they didn't want to blow the Inkle cover here in the middle of the town. As soon as the two could feel my presence, they spun around, and I managed to get this first picture of them close up.

By the looks in their eyes I realised that they recognised me from the day before where I found them swimming around. But it was too late for me. They spun around again and did the incredible Inkle attack. I must warn everyone of this form of attack. Inkle attacks are furious - Ogre's and Troll's are nothing compared to this. Never underestimate the power of an Inkle - and I had two on me right now.

Of course I passed out before i knew what was going on. I woke up a few days later in one of the houses of Rivervale, and asked what had happened. No one seemed to know, and when I asked about the Inkle's everyone shook their heads as if I was talking about something they never have heard about. I had to return home, but fortunately when developing my fil, I realized that I had taken a final shot of the Inkle attack. I must have done that just before I passed out, probably unknowingly. Let this be a warning to everyone: If you ever see an Inkle in this position, it's too late to run.

Moctemoc,
High Elf Mage.
