Saturday, March 9, 2002
A funny thing happened on our way out of Skyfire last night... Laurefin and I, Wrynkle, were preparing camps in a lovely patch of rocks when suddenly along comes a dragon ;p. Though we were tired, we still had enough courage to run up to him and snap some pictures... looking back that was a really bad idea lol. The dragon fear wasn't so bad... nor was the area effect 500dmg spell... twas the 4k fire dot that was the killer ;(. Poor Laurefin died from pain and suffering while Wrynkle barely survived to res her.
