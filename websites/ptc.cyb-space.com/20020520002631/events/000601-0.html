GUILD NEWS FLASH
 
It started ominously...

I was grouped in Rathe Mountains with my good friend Messmer, a fellow Viking from the fine guild Nordic. We were laying waste to the evil trolls that guard the tower near the named Scarabs.
Suddenly, a message popped on to our screen...
“A young elf girl screams in terror from the horrible vision, as she darts by”

Scary. We are envisioning Cazic Thahulala popping on us. Naggy deciding Sol B isn’t good enough for him any more. Vox looking for warmer skies. Doomsday stuff, you know...
Nothing happens. There is a lot of speculation over /ooc, but silence. For a few minutes...
Then another message comes.
“A lone wolf howls as the trees seem to come alive”

More speculation - a Treant invasion?
I decide to run for the Unkempt druids - maybe that’s where the action is. But no, nothing out of the ordinary.
A third message comes, more ominous than the first two...”Terrified adventurers scatter as the earth begins to shake”
And then it happens...

7 or 8 named Hill Giants in differing sizes, all named, all more than slightly tipsy, and all toting drinking steins come bouncing towards the gypsy camp near the Lake zone.

Sprout, Tim the Tiny, Tree, Bregg the Barkeep, Golbeg Berash and some names that I forget now.

They start goofing around, one of them emoting “Golbeg Berash sneaks up on Drolgin” and hitting me for 12 points of damage while laughing proudly “Me sneak bery gud! Hehe!”.
The same Golbeg tried to pick up a female warrior called Pearl, saying that “Dat pearl look gud round wifes neck!”.

Unfortunately, none of the hill giants responded to hails, emotes, or any kind of . They seemed more intent on drinking their brains out than anything else. Sprout complained loudly that he didn’t get any booze - so I ran to the gypsies and bought some for him. Bleah. HGs have no manners, he didn’t even thank me for it.

It ended as such events unfortunately often (what am I saying? ALWAYS) do - a lagfest where adventurers from all over port in to attack the named NPCs in the vain hope that they’ll drop something nice. What did they drop? A few longswords and lots of Ogre Swill. But that doesn’t stop your average EQ player, no. Always ready to turn a quick plat, he auctions “Selling Ogre Swill dropped from the Named HG!!!!”. Bah.

After Tree, the last standing HG, had gone to HillGiantHeaven, a last message came across our screens.
I don’t remember the wording exactly, but it too had an ominous tone...something about the “Wives wondering what was keeping their husbands...they should have been home by now...”

Drolgin Steingrinder
