GUILD IN-GAME VOICE CHAT
 
Yes, a new type of guild chat channel is available. You can talk with fellow members of the guild directly while playing. What benefit is this? Well, first of all, you don't fall asleep when you are camping stuff, because the guild members will yell at you when you are attacked. Also you can ask questions and still watch the screen or fight. Not the big necessity to stop and write. All in all more fun playing.

Is this illegal and bannable ?

No it is not. This is like making a phone call, except it is a free international call. Abashi (Gordon Wrinn) has himself said that it is perfectly ok to use Roger Wilco with Everquest.

What do I need to do ?

Download the Roger Wilco program. Install it. Get a license code (free). Configure the system. Play and talk. For further information and a complete walkthrough of the setup, read the help section to the right.
