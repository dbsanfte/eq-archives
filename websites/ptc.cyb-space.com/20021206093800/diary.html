Diary of a Halfling

"Many are the strange chances of the world," said Mithrander, "and help oft shall come from the hands of the weak when the wise falter." - Tolkien, The Silmarillion

Oct. 31

The shadows grew long and twisted in the forest as evening was already beginning to fall. The pine needles were soft under my feet and there was a heavy, sweet smell of autumn in the air. It was unusually quiet in Kithicor; not a beast in sight. As I went on my merry way I pleasantly distracted myself by reading the newest letter from my dear friend Bunsun. His previous note had urged me to join him in Freeport, if only for a few days. I’m sure he really didn’t expect me to go (as you well know, halflings rarely ever travel far from the comfort of their hobbit holes), but this time I decided to give him a surprise visit. I whistled a little tune as I read:

Dear Wrynkle,

How fair thee my furry-footed friend? Since you have yet to explore much of the world outside your lovely town of Rivervale I will update you on my adventures from abroad. I have recently joined a wonderful new guild called the Phoenix Trading Company. They could use some more trades folk and your tailoring skill would come in handy. Perhaps you should think about joining as well. I am also working on a quest which will eventually give me a stein of intelligence (how a stein makes you smarter is still a mystery to me). I wish you well little friend, and Oh! By the way, traveling in Kithicor at night has become incredibly dangerous, what with the recent undead upheaval and all. Not that you need to worry since you never travel far.

Ever At Your Service,

Bunsun

I thought to myself, "What could he possibly mean by "undead upheaval?" I stopped suddenly, stunned and motionless. Up ahead, standing in the middle of the path, was a very evil looking skeleton. One look at him told me I was no match for him. At this point I was half way through the forest in the dead (pun intended) of night. I was forced to make a quick decision that drastically changed my sheltered hobbit life. The monster hadn’t seen me yet, so I summoned all the courage I had (which wasn’t much), crept around him, and then ran like the wind! I could sense undead all around me as I ran blindly through the darkness. The gods must have been smiling upon me because by some miracle I found myself on the outskirts of the West Commonlands without a scratch on me. Now it seems that my visit to Freeport will be extended so that I don’t have to pass through that cursed forest again. Sigh. When will I ever be safe at home again snuggled in my warm bed?

 

 Dec. 28

Being forced out of my hometown has made me realize that there is a huge world out there just waiting to gobble me up. How can a little halfling take on Norrath by herself? I am alone, overwhelmed, and terribly afraid, yet I must be brave. I can find little comfort in the vast City of Freeport because most people here seem to dislike halflings. There also seems to be a preconceived notion that all halflings are druids. Although speed would be a nice skill to have I am quite proud of my cleric distinction. Since the surrounding faces are all unfamiliar I can only trust my new guild mates for advice (yes, I happily joined the great PTC). I should thank all the guild members for being so kind and patient with me. If I ever get back to my hometown I must also give thanks to Twippie and Meeka Diggs, who so long ago, suggested that I take up tailoring to provide myself with some small patchwork armor. I am now an average tailor working my way up to mastery. I have been roaming the Commonlands and seen glowing globes called Willowisps. I can’t hit them with my fine steel morning star, only with my feeble spells. I have heard of a magical weapon called a "Screaming Mace" which would be useful. Alas, I am but a poor cleric with hardly a copper to my name, so I must travel even further from my home to equip myself properly; it is found on the distant continent of Faydwer.

Jan. 12

It was a cold and rainy morning when I finally arrived on Faydwer. The dwarves in Butcherblock Mountains are stout, friendly folk. My sense heading is awful and the kind dwarves have helped me more than once with pointing me in the right direction. I have also met our faithful guild leader Laurefin. In my humble opinion, she is a beautiful and intelligent wood elf who is definitely worthy of her title. The tree city of Kelethin is truly remarkable unless you are afraid of heights; then I’m sure it would be terrifying. The land of faydark has been invaded by orcs and in the heart of Clan Crushbone is where my quest for a screaming mace leads me. It will be dangerous and difficult to gather all the quest pieces but I have come too far to chicken out now.
