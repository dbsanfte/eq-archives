Moctemoc’s story of his life.

Part 1, The Beginning.

Moctemoc a mage, who has been in Norrath for quite some time, has come there due to the fact that his father, "Moc" visited the entire world through an interdimensional gate from the far away land of Kramned.

Moc was a wizard, and he left the home just after Moctemoc’s birth. Leaving his wife and newborn son alone, he chose to try his luck in the land beyond this gate that he had opened. He did not know if he was ever to return to his family ever again. Moc however was sure that his family would be taken good care of, since he left his brother in charge of his wife and son. Well Moc left, and was never seen again.

Moctemoc grew up in his father’s footsteps, and even though he was never told much about him, not even the fact that his father was a great Wizard, Moctemoc himself turned magical in a way his mother did not hope would ever happen. Moctemoc took up the skills where his father had left them. By studying old notes found hidden in his home, he managed to gain some of the skills his father had. He learned of the magical land of Norrath, he learned about his father’s dimensional gate, and finally when he was old enough and assumed himself skillfull enough, he took his guts together and asked Moc’s brother what had happened to his father.

At first Moc’s brother did not want to share what he knew, and he had to consult Moctemoc’s mother. And after a long discussion, they decided to tell Moctemoc what had happened, hoping that Moctemoc would accept the fact that Moc was gone and probably would never return home.

Moctemoc however had inherited his father’s urge to adventure, and against the will of Moc’s brother and his mother experimented with all the found information from his father. Moctemoc managed to open a gate. He managed to contact the other side. He even managed to get words and items transferred from his world to Norrath and back. He was still too inexperienced to travel, and kept the gate secret to the rest of his family. Until one day, the notes he had passed through the gate, and the notes he had gotten back, proved to him beyond doubt that his father was or had been out there in the unknown. Through his communications with the other side, he had managed to contact his father or perhaps some friends of his father, and they immediately identified Moctemoc’s skills as those of a mage. Not as powerfull as his father, but still with very high powers. He received a note, which he was to bring to a guild master of a mage guild, if and when he would decide to enter the path of his father and come to Norrath.

Moctemoc told his family about the gate and that he had been in contact with the "other side", but he left out the part about the invitation to enter the world of Norrath. Moc's brother, and Moctemoc's mother wasn't very fond of what they heard, but accepted the fact that the gate had been opened again, and they pleaded with Moctemoc not to enter the gate.

Moctemoc, still not sure about himself, did not want to leave his family, and continued to research his skills, and through the gate, trying to locate his father and friends. Little did he know that his life soon was to change.

Moctemoc never did receive a note directly originating from his father, but he learned that his father was unable to create a gate back to his dimension, and therefore decided to marry again and make a family in the new land. Moctemoc received note that he had a half-brother in the land of Norrath, and that Moc was notified of the gate that Moctemoc had created. Moctemoc hoped that his father would come to this gate and return home to his dimension.

Meanwhile in Kramned, Moctemoc’s mother turned very ill, and died. This completely devastated Moctemoc, and he was unable to open the gate to Norrath for several month’s. When he finally managed to make a gate, and this was stable enough for him to get notes across, he learned that his father had died in combat with evil, while looking for Moctemoc’s gate. Quickly and full of resolution, Moctemoc decided that now was the time. He notified Moc’s brother that he would travel to the "other side", and seek a life there in his father footsteps and searching for his half-brother.

After having taking this decision, it was not hard to create the gate for the last time. Moctemoc knew that he would never return, unless someone from his dimension would open up the gate. So he said goodbye to Moc’s brother and left the dimension, firmly determined to revenge his father’s death, never give up in a fight, and protect the people that have helped his father through the hard times he has had in this new land.
