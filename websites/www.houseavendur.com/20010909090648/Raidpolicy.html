House Avendur is based on friendship. Friends like to see their friends succeed. To that end there will be many hunts to help us better equip our guildmates, our friends, as this is an enjoyable experience for us all. It is our hope that everyone will be able to enjoy each other's good fortune as if it were their own.

 
1. Need Before Greed: Any person present may roll on an item their class and race may use, provided it is an item they will use.

2. To make sure all present at a raid have a chance to go home with something, each person may only win one class item, or loot item, until all of their class have something. If something "better" comes along and the item they looted was exchangeable, they may random on the better item. If they win the random they must return the lesser item to the raid leader to be redistributed.

3. The Raid Leader is required to post on the sign up sheet the details of loot distribution if that should differ in any way from the above stated guidelines. An example of a loot distribution difference is the "bad luck rule". The Raid Leader may designate one person a recipient of one item to offset their run of bad luck on past rolls. This must be clearly posted on the raid sign up sheet so that all in attendance will know going in that this individual will be awarded a specific item should it drop.
