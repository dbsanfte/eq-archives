September 18, 2002

Closing Time
Those of you who know us know something went wrong this weekend. The family and fighting force that was House Avendur dissolved into a jumble of old friends with heartaches. Some people have left the game, others have left the guild, some are simply stunned and don't know what to do.

In Farewell, a toast!

To Duraen and Kelvani Avendur: for dreaming it up, and making it happen.

To the officers of the guild: for helping it grow and keeping it strong as long as they did.

To every member: for being there when your friends needed someone, for your honourable actions that gained us the respect of the server, for enabling us to do great things, for making the House a home with your good cheer and kind words.

A million thanks! Best wishes and loads of /hugs to everyone, where ever you may go now.
