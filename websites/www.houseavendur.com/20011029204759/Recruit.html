A candidate may be of any class, race, or level. Any member of the guild may bring a prospect to the attention of the guild.
1. There will be no open invitations. A current member of the guild must sponsor the candidate. Those interested in joining House Avendur are recommended to form bonds of friendship with our members, and seek their support of your candidacy. Once the Guild Master has confirmed your candidacy, you shall be known as a Squire until the completion of your candidacy period. The candidacy period may differ from Squire to Squire, and is determined by the speed in which the Squire's Quest is completed.

2. The Sponsor will post an intent to sponsor on the Guild Board under the forum entitled "The Squires Quarters". Sponsorship threads should include the candidate's name, class, level, and reasons why you think this person would make a good addition to the guild. You obviously like them, let us know why! Each member may only have one Squire at any time. Once a squire is posted on the squires quarters they will be contacted in game and given their quest. From that date they will have a minimum squireship of three weeks. If they recieve 100% membership votes before the three weeks are up they will be invited imediately. Once the three weeks are up if they have recieved 75% vote or more and no concerns have been voiced, the Officers will take the squireship in hand. If the officers are in 100% agreement on the squire they will be invited at that time. If the squire does not have at least 75% votes or they have a concern voiced about them they will remain as a squire indefinitely until such time as the concern is lifted, the sponsor withdraws them from consideration, or they reach 75% membership votes. At that time the officers will take the squireship in hand.

3. Each guild member is charged with actively participating in every Squire's Quest. As a Squire contacts a member they should post under the Squire's thread their thoughts about this Squires worthiness to join the guild. Honesty is STRONGLY encouraged. This thread will be archived before a Squire is invited to join the guild to avoid any ill will. It is expected of all members to keep any information upon these threads private and internal to the House. Failure to do so is grounds for removal for violation of our trust.

          a. One or two "no" votes shall constitute removal from active consideration as a Squire. A Squire who has recieved under three "no" votes may be reconsidered if the parties who felt their membership unacceptable remove their objection to the candidate, or leave the guild. We recognize that people change, and thus we are willing to offer a second chance. The Squire's thread will be archived and their name and reason for removal from consideration posted under the Squires Quarters until withdrawal of objections to them.

          b. Three "no" votes shall constitute permanent removal from consideration as a Squire. There are NO exceptions to this rule. The Squire's thread will be permanently closed at this time and archieved.
