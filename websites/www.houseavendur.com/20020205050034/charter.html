House Avendur is not just a Guild. We are not about loot. We have no desire to be "uber". We are a group of friends, a family, first and foremost. As a family cares for its members, so shall we. As a family grows together and forms firm bonds by shared experiences, so shall we. We shall always strive to be at peace with one another, and work and play together in harmony. Friendship and loyalty provide the glue that bonds this family together. Trust is the key to unlock the door to the House. Honor provides us our meat and drink. Our foundation is strong, rooted in a past that spans worlds. Our present is secure, as knowledge of the past uplifts us, and new bonds are forged in steel. Our future is bright, for when friends unite there is nothing under heaven or earth they cannot do.
The Guild Leader, Duraen Avendur, will have the responsibility of upholding the charter and laws both in letter and intent. He will be the final authority on all matters. This is not a democracy. Leadership in Norrath can not be done by committee. If at some time in the future Duraen's time in Norrath comes to an end, as all things must, he will name his successor. This person will be someone he feels has the strength and moral fortitude to uphold the original ideals of House Avendur. At such time his successor should also leave then he/she will pass on guild leadership to someone they feel will uphold the original ideals of House Avendur. Each successor should do the same. If ever the majority of the House no longer follows the original ideals laid down in the charter it will be disbanded.

The duties of the Guild Leader are extensive. There will be several officers to aid in the administrative duties. Should the guild leader be absent from Norrath, he/she may chose to name one of the officers as Chancellor in their absence. The Chancellor would be authorized to deal with the day-to-day business of the guild until he/she returns or passes on guild leadership.

In order to retain the family feel of the guild, and prevent size from causing members from losing touch with each other, the guild shall be no larger then 50 active members. Alternate Characters of members will not count against this total sum, as they do not represent "real" people. If a member goes inactive for more then a six-month period they shall be considered retired. We wish to limit growth not choke it.
Alternate characters can enhance and enrich the playing experience of our members and therefore we welcome their existance. Each member of the guild shall be listed in our Roster, both main and alternate(s).
Characters that are guild "banks" or serve the guild as teleporter's, or in other needed capacities as defined by the Guild Master shall be listed as such, beside the name of the person that holds them for the guild.
The guild will gather in Norrath approximately every two weeks. All members are encouraged to attend. Sponsors are encouraged to invite their Squires to the meetings, as it will help them complete their Squire's Quest. Friends are always welcome.
1. Those who are members of the House will not show its members dishonor by commiting any of the following acts: Kill Stealing, Training, or Farming Items for profit. (We understand that some members enjoy hunting items for resale, and though this is strongly discuraged we do not consider it to be a crime against the House, provided you do not ruin someone else's fun by monopolozing a green camp.)

2. Members of the House will do their best to see that all fallen party members, including non-guild members, are aided with corpse retrieval. No one should be left to deal with difficult corpse retrieval or stuck deep within a dungeon without help. It is understood that link death, server crashes, and power outages may make this impossible.

3. Members are encouraged to practice sharing of loot fairly among party members including those who are not in our guild. Greed should never come before family and friends!

4. Verbal abuse or harassment towards anyone will not be tolerated. This especially applies toward family members. Foul language and conversations considered inappropriate for minors are expected to be kept off the Guild Chat-line, as members may have young children that enjoy watching them play.

5. Stealing from another player will not be tolerated.

6. Members will not beg. Begging is an act defined as asking to be given the unearned. It is expected that members will be able to pay fair market value or help in obtaining the item in question. There will be many events scheduled by the guild to help members obtain items.
