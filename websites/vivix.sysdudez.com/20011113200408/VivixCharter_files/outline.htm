Vivix Protectorate
Guild Policies and By-Laws
Tenet Principles
Treat all guild mates and players with respect
Work as a team, not as enemies
Stealing, be it kill-stealing or loot-stealing, within the guild or without is forbidden
Help others in need whenever able, not only when convenient
Fides, Probitas, et Virtus.  Honor, Honesty, and Courage
Vivix Protectorate Charter
Vivix Protectorate was founded in order to create, preserve, and cultivate a close-knit fellowship of characters and players on the EverQuest Erollisi Marr server.  The founding members came from the League of Omisan, where they met and found a good and nurturing environment.  Vivix Protectorate was formed in order to take those principles one step further.
Vivix Protectorate seeks to grow in both strength and numbers through integrity, fellowship, and fair play, and through no other means
Vivix Protectorate Rules
Vivix Protectorate will organize raids, events, and hunting parties for all levels of its membership at least once a month
Vivix Protectorate will make a commitment to every one of its members, and demand the same from them
Vivix Protectorate will foster teamwork, not animosity
Vivix Protectorate will not participate in harassment of any form
Vivix Protectorate will have lots of fun
Guild Leadership
Member Guidelines
Any new member must have grouped at least two realtime hours with a member of the guild
A new member must be sponsored by a guild member
Unless sponsored by an officer, a new member must be at least Level 10
As a courtesy, every member should send notice if their playtime is expected to fall below five hours per week
Every member will place priority on guild events when held
Members are discouraged from using /anon.  /roleplay is preferred
Guild Council Guidelines
In addition to membership on the Council, each officer shall have a designated duty
An Executive Officer will be nominated by the Presiding Officer to serve as his/her second.  The XO will rotate weekly
Any officer taking an extended leave of absence (over two weeks) must nominate a delegate to serve in his stead.  This appointment may be overruled by 2/3rds majority vote of the Council
Guild Council Guidelines
After a leave of absence, Officers will be re-instated to their position.  Their substitutes may be considered for promotion when positions become available.
The number of the Council shall always be an even number
The member:officer ratio will be one officer to every 10 members.  Officers are promoted for every 20 members inducted.
In the event of deadlock, the Presiding Officer will cast the deciding vote.  Otherwise, he/she will abstain, deferring to the rulings of the Council majority
Guild Council Guidelines
Every officer must be available to all guild members via e-mail
Every officer must maintain at least five hours of playtime per week
No officer shall play /anon.  They may use /roleplay instead.
Every officer must regularly check in with guild members whom he/she serves
Every officer must plan and hold at least one event per month with the guild members they serve, be it individual coaching or hunting parties
Presiding Officer Guidelines
After a leave of absence, the Presiding Officer will be re-instated to his/her position.  This may be blocked by 2/3rds majority of the Council, in which case the Acting Presiding Officer retains the position, and the Former Presiding Officer takes his/her place on the Council
The Presiding Officer may be removed by 2/3rds majority vote of the entire guild
The Presiding Officer’s vote will be used to solve deadlock situations in the Council
