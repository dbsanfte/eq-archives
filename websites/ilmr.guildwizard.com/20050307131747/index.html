Inverse Logic has moved~! - 3/2/2005 12:21:18 PM

Inverse Logic has a new website and new forums! Please visit us at http://inverselogic-mr.com and register so you can stop by and say Hello!!

Here are instructions on how to register and/or get forum access:

Register to the website
You must register to the site. To register, go here: http://inverselogic-mr.com/modules.php?name=Your_Account&op=new_user

Once you have registered you will be sent a confirmation email. You must go to the link in the email, HOWEVER, you cant click it, you must copy/paste the entire link into the address bar and go there that way. This will complete the registration. Then you are registered to the site. If you go to the "Menu" panel on the left of the site there is a link to the "Members List" - this is a list of all registered users of the site and you can go there to find someones user name to Private Message them, etc.

If you are having technical difficulties with the confirmation email
If you receive an email that has no link, or has just a bunch of symbols mixed with text... copy and paste THAT into the address bar and put the "http"and the "www" stuff in front of it and try THAT as the link. Some email programs dont recognize it for some reason.

HOW TO GET ACCESS TO FORUMS
To get to the new forums, there is a link to "Forums" on the "Menu" panel. The URL is: http://inverselogic-mr.com/modules.php?name=Forums .

Once you click into the Forums there is a horizontal menu bar across the top. Click the "User Groups" button. Choose a user group that applies to you from the drop-down button (Officers are in the Officers User group, Members are in the Members User group, Probies the Probie user group, etc). Then click the button to the right "View Information". Then click the button that says "Join this Group". It will notify me that you have requested to join this group and I will add you, and then you will have access.

DKP Page
The DKP page is accessed by scrolling down the left side to the "Main Menu" panel and clicking on "DKP". The URL is: inverselogic-mr.com/DKP/viewnews.php

You must register for this site also! Click the "Register" link. Once you are able to login, click "Standings" to see the overall DKP page. From there you can sort standings by the various categories listed there. If you do not see your name, scroll all the way to the bottom and look to the right and find the "show all" link. Click it and all names should come up.


If you have any questions about the website, forums, DKP page access and/or registration, please let me know.


--Aandara
