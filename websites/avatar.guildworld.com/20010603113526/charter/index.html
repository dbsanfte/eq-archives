OUR HISTORY

The Avatar of Terror was founded by Seeka on the realm we call Luclin. His parents named him this because he was a seeker of new adventures. As he grew in power, he became more curious about the outside world and needed new adventures to fullfill his craving for knowledge.

One day he happened upon a great cave filled with many treasures. His curiousity was great so with a brave heart he ran into the cave and saw the biggest dragon he ever laid eyes on. Our hero was not afraid and immediately lept into battle.

The battle went on and on. There have been rumors that it went on for days and days. Seeka fought long and hard but in the end the dragon proved to be to much for him.

At long last the battle ended. Seeka slew the mighty dragon during his last and final breath. When he did the people in the Avatar of Terror could feel a huge power surge through them. This was to be his last gift to the guild.
