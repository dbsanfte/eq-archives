Battle Island Preview Posted Monday, October 30, 2000 by jcsmith
There is a preview of Battle Island: The Androsian War up at Gamespot UK. For those who have never heard of Battle Island it was an award winning turn based strategy game for both the PC and the Amiga platforms in the early 90's. I loved the earlier incarnations as well as Blue Byte's Historyline series which employed similar gameplay but using historical units. This could be an RTS to look out for.

Battle Island: The Androsian War will be a mix of real-time and turn based combat. How will they accomplish this? It's covered in the article and quoted below...

Each combat turn takes 10 minutes. During your opponent's go, you have 10 minutes to look after the economic and developmental side of the game - basically anything that isn't combat. Naturally this includes resource gathering, construction of power plants and the like, but it also includes unit construction and research. If you so choose, you won't have to immediately flip back to the combat mode once the 10 minutes is up, but you can hang around on your base island and carry on with essential tasks. Of course, this will leave you less time to execute your combat orders, but the flexibility is there.
