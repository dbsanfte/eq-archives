Raven Blade Cancelled for Gamecube
 Gamecube - 23:23 EST - jerjer234

Retro Studios' Gamecube project, Raven Blade, has been officially cancelled due to financial reasons. Here is a quote from Gamespot.

Nintendo of America has informed GameSpot that the upcoming GameCube RPG, Raven Blade, which was being developed by Austin, Texas-based Retro Studios, has been officially canceled. As a result of the game's cancellation, the development studio has reallocated its resources and has laid off an unspecified number of employees. According to an NOA spokesperson, Retro is currently focusing its resources entirely on Metroid Prime for the GameCube.
"Raven Blade has been canceled," Nintendo of America said in an official statement. "It was a business decision and was made clearly based on the need to focus on Metroid and to ensure that it is a great game. We wanted to consolidate the resources that would have been needed to finish Raven Blade."

Raven Blade, which was shown only in video form at this year's E3, puts the player in the role of a sword-wielding medieval knight who must battle dragons and demons. It was one of the first projects initiated by Retro Studios.
It is always sad to see a good game like that go to waste, but at least we know it was for a good reason. :)

    Creating Game Environments
         NeverwinterNights - 13:25 EST - jcsmith

    The third Neverwinter Nights Adventure Creation Guide at Gamespy targets "Creating Game Environments". Here's a sample of what's inside...

    Adventure areas should be exciting, filled with challenges and the opportunity to explore, and they should have a definite direction in which you want the action of your story to flow. That action might have multiple paths or it might have lots of twists and turns along the way but, for the most part, you'll typically require two fundamental locations: a starting point and an ending point. Of course, there should be some space in-between these two points, space filled with the various elements that make for an entertaining tale -- we'll discuss this topic in a later conversation. But it is important to note that your adventurers should rarely, if ever, have to backtrack through spaces they have already explored. Unless the previously explored territory has somehow been made new and exciting, such backtracking tends to make your adventure seem stale and monotonous. For this same reason, I would urge you to use mazes sparingly.

    7. What can you tell us about special skills in Earth and Beyond?

    Fighting Legends Interview
         FightingLegends - 13:52 EST - jcsmith

    There is a Fighting Legends Interview up at RPGVault. Here's a highlight...

    Jonric: Let's move on to relics. Please describe the relic system, how relics are obtained, what benefits they provide, etc. Is the full relic system in the game now, and if not, what are the highlights to come? And are you doing anything different from the relic or artifact systems other online games?

    Brian Wood: Relics are rare items that you can acquire through completing quests, trading or recovering from fallen monsters in the world of Exisle. Relics will increase damage done, make you invisible, increase toughness, increase your size, speed you up and more. Relics provide that extra variable that may determine the outcome of battle and show your experience and expertise in the game. We want to avoid this problem by making relics available in several circumstances. While relics will still be difficult to acquire, we do not want to make the game unrealistic by limiting where you can get relics. So expect to be able to acquire the same relic through different, but still difficult, means.

    Update on AO
         AnarchyOnline - 13:12 EST - jcsmith

    This was posted to the Anarchy Online community page this morning...

    We have lately experienced an even greater need to inform you about the issues arising in Anarchy Online on a daily basis. We thought that a good and effective way to do this would be to select a few issues aired on the forums every day and address them in the required fashion. We will therefore introduce a Q&A from the forums every day here at our support/community site, and hope this will go a long way in providing you with some of the answers you are looking for and deserve to get. For obvious reasons we won’t be able to address every topic discussed on the boards, but we do look at them every day, and we hope this will give us a chance to respond more effectively to some of the questions asked.

    We are aware that there are some exploits, and we are working on localizing and removing all of them one at a time. The ones we have seen that we considered very serious, we managed to remove there and then, taking the money away from the player in the process. We advice people to think twice before purchasing, say one million credits, on Ebay. One million credits might sound like a lot of money when you first start playing, but the fact is that that amount of money won’t really get you anywhere when you start trying to maintain a high level character, and see what kind of prices you’ll have to fork out for the really cool stuff.

    That was all for this update, but I hope there was some useful information in it for you, and that you check back for another update from the forums shortly.
