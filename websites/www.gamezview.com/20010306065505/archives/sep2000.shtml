Zeus Interview Posted Saturday, September 30, 2000 by bullseye
Chris Beatrice of Impression Games has been interviewed at Daily Radar. He talks about Impressions upcoming strategy/god game, Zeus: Master of Olympus. Here's a small portion of the interview:

    DR: It was easy in Pharaoh to get a city completely out of whack economically. Build too many houses, build too many industries, and everyone in the city would be happy, but your city would slowly be going broke. Trade was one option, and taxes, but it could be a really tough road. Have there been any improvements to the economy model?

    CB: While I wouldn't necessarily characterize all the changes you're talking about as "improvements," the economic model in Zeus is considerably more forgiving than its Pharaoh counterpart.
