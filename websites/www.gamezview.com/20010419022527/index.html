Additionally, the player is given only general goals to accomplish. Battlecruiser Millenium Dev Diary
 MMOSim - 13:48 EST - jcsmith

I missed this on Monday, but a new Development Diary for Battlecruiser Millenium is up. You want graphics?

The entire game is now 100% DirectX 8 compliant and sporting all the bells and whistles (apart from the mostly useless DX features) that are needed to keep the graphics kernel on the cutting edge. We've implemented vertex buffers, hardware Transform & Lighting, among other kick-ass high-end features. Also, a lot of optimizations have been done. To the extent that the game is so fast now, its kinda ridiculous, considering the amount of work the game is doing. In fact, the AI kernel currently takes up approx. 50% (yep, thats right, I said fifty) of the game processing the last time it was profiled. This is not strange, considering that all BC titles have always placed strong emphasis on the AI kernel upon which the game runs. In the coming weeks, SMP support for WIN2K will be completed and activated. This will allow the AI kernel to get thrown into a separate processor thread. The game code already supports this and was designed as such, several months ago.

    First, Warden discusses why there is not a 1st person view in Shadowbane, and the slight possibility that one could be added in the future:

    We think that 3rd person lends itself better to strategic combat, for one. That's why you don't see many 1st person RTS games.

    Battle for Dune Preview
         RealTimeStrategy - 0:31 EST - jcsmith

    A preview of Emporer: Battle for Dune was posted to Firing Squad. Check this out...

    What sets Emperor apart from most RTS games is its use of a strategic map for players to choose their next battle. Those who have played EA's Shogun: Total War are already familiar with using a strategic map. Instead of a map of Japan divided into fiefdoms, what players in Emperor will see is a circular "map" of Arrakis divided into 33 territories between the three houses Atreides, Harkonnen, and Ordos. Mission selection is a matter of choosing an enemy controlled territory that borders your own.     The overall job system will be a mixture of dynamically created missions and player-generated missions. Essentially whatever’s happening on a continent will be queried on the server on a regular basis, and dynamic missions will use the results to spawn themselves logistically.
