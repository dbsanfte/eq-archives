SWG Developer Ideas
 StarWarsGalaxies - 14:36 EST - jcsmith

Haden Blackman gave some juicy information in this thread on the Star Wars Galaxies boards earlier...

Bajeezus suggested that we developers try to post messages about what we're currently discussing/debating amongst ourselves. I think that's a really fantastic idea and we'll try to do it whenever we can, without giving too much away.

So, here's something that's been a recent debate... As I've said in other posts, we've been finalizing our list of planets and locations. The planet that has caused the most discussion is Hoth. Here are some pros and cons to Hoth:

Pros:
  • It's familiar to everyone and is a film location
  • It has an interesting climate and terrain that's different from other planets
  • The terrain itself would probably be fairly easy to generate
  • The few sites that are on Hoth are compelling (the Rebel base, wampa caves, etc.)
  • There's plenty of reference from the Illustrated Guide to the Star Wars Universe
  • It would be really cool to hunt wampas

    And the Cons:
  • There's not much else to do on Hoth besides hunt wampas (and maybe Rebels)
  • Hoth is meant to be isolated and unsettled; it would be weird to have many players visiting
  • Hoth probably wouldn't provide any real estate for settlements or buildings (again, because it's supposed to be harsh, unpopulated, etc.).
  • How do you handle the fact that Imperial-aligned players might find the Rebel base at a point in the timeline that's well before the Battle of Hoth? (SW timeline experts will remember that the base took about three years to construct, so we could have some form of base there - the Imperials just couldn't find it).
  • There's very little in the way of flora and fauna
  • Although the few sites we know about are keen, there's not very many places to visit

    I'm not revealing whether or not Hoth has made our short list, but I just want to let you know the issues raised by the debate.

    SimCoaster Review
         Simulations - 1:12 EST - jcsmith

    Happy Puppy has posted a SimCoaster Review. They gave the theme park based simulation a 7/10 mark. Here's part of the reason why...

    This coaster game is what you would call, "sim lite." In fact, it’s so light on coaster simulation, it’s a wonder the name contains the word "coaster" at all. This isn’t a bad thing, though. It’s nice not to have to lug around a physics textbook to build a successful coaster. However, if you’re feeling creative, the game offers you a coaster design kit where you can go to create your coaster without any interruptions, i.e. “old goats” yelling at the employees and children setting trash cans on fire. In the design mode, you will be able to build track supports to your specification and loop-the-loops to your hearts content. It’s pretty easy, too. As long as your tracks don’t cross one another in an odd place and meet at the ends, your coaster will be ready to go. It’s a far cry from the detailed construction in Roller Coaster Tycoon, and that’s just fine. Better yet, if you’re just not in the mood, you don’t have to build a coaster at all. There are plenty of pre-made coasters to go around. There’s a reason games like SimCoaster appeal to a mass audience: it’s good, clean, accessible fun.

    Atriarch Linux Support
         Atriarch - 13:53 EST - jcsmith

    I noticed this interesting tidbit at Atriarch Stratics. Arc made an appearance in this thread at the VNBoards. He delves in to Linux Atriarch support. Here's what he had to say...

    Development for GNU/Linux is proceeding quite well. GNU/Linux is the target deployment platform for all of the Atriarch servers. The Shell (what we call the client program that players use) currently runs on Windows and is being developed to run on GNU/Linux as well.

    We have only been building for i386 at this time, but there is no technical reason why we cannot build for others since we don't have any code that is CPU specific. Binaries for GNU/Linux will be distributed most probably as both .tar.gz and .rpm (which are supported by multiple GNU/Linux distros).

    We are trying to keep a very clean installation that doesn't involve registry entries on Windows or dependencies on shared libraries (.so or .DLL) in common system directories. Under Windows, we don't depend on DirectX; if it is available we do take advantage of it (such as DirectSound), but it is not required. We prefer to leverage open, platform independent standards such as OpenGL and OpenAL.

    We are using some Free Software and Open Source licensed under the GPL/LGPL. We will also be contributing our own Free Software as enhancements as well as original work. All GPL/LGPL source will be available from our web site once the game is released and possibly included on the distribution; certainly on any CD/DVD released, but probably optional for downloads so as to reduce bandwidth consumption.

    Personally, GNU/Linux is my preferred platform. Since I plan to run Atriarch on it, you can be assured that our GNU/Linux support will be strong.

    Everquest Test Server Changes
         Everquest - 2:24 EST - jcsmith

    The Everquest test server was updated earlier today with the following changes...

  • Fixed a bard stacking problem that previously resulted in mana and regen spells not being able to be cast on the player when the bard was singing Cantana of Replenishment.
  • Added the chance for level 60 players to be able to taunt mobs in the range of 60 to 65. This is for only those players with the taunt skill and are level 60.
  • Corrected a problem that was allowing NPC's to pass on their hate lists when the player was out of range. This resulted in ghost faction messages and unusual NPC agro. Now, NPC's will not add you to the hate list if you are out of their awareness range.
  • There was a bug in the calculation of hate when players were sitting next to agro NPC's. This is fixed.
  • Made a small change to the way hate is calculated for healing spells.
  • Doubled the chance to be able to learn skills that are above 200. Such as defense.
  • Fixed it so that /book and alt+b no longer take non-spell casters to the spellbook screen.
  • The bandaging skill will now give a message to the player that is being bandage that he is being bandaged.
  • Fixed it so that merchants will recognize iksar as a race when displaying their bad faction messages.
  • Raised the skill cap in tracking for druids to 125. Previously when you could not scroll through the tracking list, druids preferred a smaller tracking range so they wouldn't see so much clutter. Now that they can scroll, druids have been placed in-between bards and rangers in skill.
