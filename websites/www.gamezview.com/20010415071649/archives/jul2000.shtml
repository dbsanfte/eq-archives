    IGNPC: Apart from the campaign, are there any other missions or modes that the single player can get involved in?

    Todd Vaughn: There really are no other modes, but there is a historical scenario so that once you've completed a mission you can go back and re-play. Sony Posts Losses, Not Gains Posted Thursday, July 27, 2000 by mage
It seems as though the release of the Playstation II did nothing more than lose money for the electronic superpower Sony. Yesterday, they reported a whopping $820 million loss for Q1 2000. The company itself may not be entirely at fault really, however, the mass expense that goes towards the development of a new console took a huge bite out of their net profit. The software sells that were expected to counter the loss did not deliver either, as people flocked to buy the newest DVD movies, rather than the latest games. Currently Sony loses $112 to $121 for each new Playstation II sold.

You might remember that the company posted a gain of $171 million this time last year. Quite a big drop. We will continue to monitor this story as it develops.

A Sony spokesperson later stated, "We do not know exactly when the game division will start turning a profit. Most Likely, the profits will be marginal this year if at all. The PS2 sales in Japan were as we predicted, but software and PS sales fell. In Q1, the cost of PS2 production caused the gaming division to post a loss."
