Deathfist Citidal

            Emporer Fyst Slain!

 Seekers of Truth recently put together a raid force and were able to enter the Deathfist Citidal.   With many members entering for the first time it turned out to be a very productive raid.   Several members were able to complete heritage quests as well as many other completed quests within the zone for some very nice armor pieces.   Most of the zone was unworthy of our raid force and we quickly tore thru named after named to advance within the zone.   After slaughtering most of the Orcs within we meet up with the Emporer himself.   He at least poised some amount of trouble and turns out he does have a perticular interest in female swashbucklers.  Hollian unfortantly meets this description and was quickly sought out by Emporer Fyst and was shown some tough love.  Despite the lose of Hollian the rest of the raid was able to avenge her and defeated Emporer Fyst.   Thanks to all that attented and stay tuned for future raids.
