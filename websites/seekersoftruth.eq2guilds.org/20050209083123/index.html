Cauldron Hollow Guild Raid!

Seekers of Truth guild put together a small raid party to enter Cauldron Hollow.  There were many many Group x 4 mob's that came in groups of 4 to 5.

As it turned out the zone was slightly lower in level then hoped and all mobs were too low of level to yeild any loot from the actual mobs.   Although several members were able to advance thier goulbane heritage quest.  Future trips are in the near future for remaining guild members interested in obtaining the elusive goulbane.
