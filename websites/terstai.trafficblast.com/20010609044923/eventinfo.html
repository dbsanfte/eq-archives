The Great Merger

Date: Sunday August 27th, 8pm CST
Location: East Karana

ALL are welcome.. 

*It is a foggy morning in the woods of East
Karana, the sun has only begun to rise and the roaring might of the waterfall
can be heard blazing in the distance.

*The mighty council of the Lords of the
Ters'Tai has been gathered this morning.  In the distance High Commander
Toke spies a large group moving close.  It is the Guild of Viators come to
offer aid in the battles to come.

*This will truly be a day to remember.

 

"Hail! Who comes before the High Lord of the Lords of the Ters'Tai?" 
 says Toke, High Commander of the Lords of the
Ters'Tai.


"The Guild of Viators approaches, to join with our Brothers, the Lords of the Ters'Tai, in a fight against our common cause."  
says Gozaro Sehalfling, Grand Master of Viators.


The Guild of Viators, having discussed the matter with their allies the Lords of the Ters'Tai, and after having discussed with jointly and separately, have agreed that upon this day, the two guilds shall become one.

For it came to pass that as the Guild of Viators thought more and more about the evils that Innoruk was bringing upon the world, they realized that there was little that they could do about it without help. Alliances are helpful, but yet, alliances do not bring the bond of being guildmates. The way to increase their numbers, and to become a more powerful influence in Norrath, was to join with another, and to become one.

Above, my dear reader, is part of the transcription of the ceremony which will take place upon that fateful day,
27 August 2000 8pm CST, in the East Karanas, just north of the Barbarian Village, when the Guild of Viators and the Lords of the Ters'Tai become one.

The Guild of Viators approached the Lords of the Ters'Tai from the west. They were led by their Grand Master, Gozaro, and then by the Guild Masters Raladdar, Siene and Tpar. 

The Viators approached, halted, and then seated themselves on the ground, forming a three ranks behind each of the Masters of their guild with the Grand Master standing ahead. The High Lord approached, and after giving time for the Viators to become accustomed, opened the meeting using the ancient form.

If a friend of Viators or the Lords of the Ters'tai  you be, please accept this open invitation to attend our ceremony.   All are welcome.
