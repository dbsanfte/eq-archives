Statute of Membership

 A Statute providing for the conditions and terms of membership in the guild, the process of becoming a member of his Guild, and for the recruitment and training of new members.

The Council of Lords, in accordance with the Great Charter, and being mindful of their sacred responsibility to guard zealously the reputation and honor of the Guild, enacts herein the following Statute relating to membership in the Lords of the Ters'Tai:

I. Elgibility to Membership.

  1. All free-born or freed persons are eligible to membership in the Lords of the Ters'Tai, upon recommendation by at least one member of the Guild.
  2. No person shall be denied membership based upon their race, class or gender.
  3. Persons who refuse or fail to meet the requirements of honor and dignity set forth by the Council of Lords may be refused membership.

II. Membership Process

  1. Membership as a Knight or Dame of the Lords of the Ters'Tai is a privilege extended to those who meet the requirements of membership, including but not limited to satisfaction of the Council of Lords of their honor and integrity as a member.
  2. Any person wishing to join shall first ascertain the name of the guild member, of at least Knight or Dame's rank, who agrees to sponsor their name before the Council of Lords.
  3. No Knight or Dame shall sponsor anyone with whom they have not fought alongside at least once, for a period not less than one hour.
  4. Upon acquisition of a sponsor, an applicant for membership shall apply for membership with the guild's web site, using the forms provided herein.
  5. Upon receipt of the application, the Lord of Instruction shall forward the application to the sponsor, and shall indicate to the sponsor the next meeting of the Council of Lords for presentation of the inquirer.
  6. If, in the opinion of the Council of Lords, the applicant is worthy of consideration, they shall be adjudged a Page of the Lords, and shall be assigned to the sponsor for further training and instruction.
  7. The Sponsor shall ensure that the Page is versed in the history of the guild, its law, requirements and codes of honor. The sponsor shall also ensure that during the period before initiation, the Page shall have grouped with at least 3 other members of the guild, who are not related to the Sponsor. During the time of such status, a Page shall not wear the guild-tag, shall not participate in guild events without their sponsor, nor sponsor any person to membership.
    1. In the event that a guild-event is required, any Lord of the Council may agree to take responsibility for the supervision of the Page and/or pages in attendence pro hoce vice.
  8. Upon the conclusion of the allotted time, (which shall be set for each applicant individually), the sponsor and the Page shall again be summoned before the Council of Lords, and inquiry shall be made of their progress. If in the opinion of the sponsor the Page is ready for membership, the Council or its representative shall examine the Page upon the history, traditions, membership, laws and customs of the guild, and the Council shall then vote, by secret ballot, to determine if the Page is worthy of initiation. A unanimous ballot shall be required for initiation into the Lords of the Ters'Tai, unless the High Lord shall exercise his discretion to waive the requirement, in which case a vote of three-fourths shall be necessary.
  9. The Council of Lords shall then schedule initiation for the page as an Knight or Dame of the Lords of the Ters'Tai.

III. Membership in the Lords of the Ters'Tai shall be defined in the following levels or ranks:

  1. Lords of the Council - A past or current Lord, sitting on the Council of Lords, or who sits on the Council ex-officio.
  2. Knight or Dame - a member in good standing in the guild, and who has all of the rights, privileges and responsibilities of membership. Only Knights of Dames or above may sponsor new members, or bring in a member through the privilege of transfer.
  3. Page - One who has been accepted and who has pledged themselves to the Guild, but who does not have the privilege of wearing the guild-tag, of participating in events without their sponsor, of sponsoring new members or bringing in a member through the privilege of transfer.
  4. Inquirer - One who is interested in membership in the guild, but who has not yet been invited into the guild for as a Page.

IV. The Lord of Members, acting on behalf of the Council of Lords, may approve and invite into the guild, at the membership level of Knight or Dame, the secondary or subsequent character of any Knight or Dame in good standing, after said Knight or Dame has made proper application.

V. The Lord of Members may, from time to time, petition the Council of Lords to waive the necessity as a Page for someone who has otherwise met the requirements of honor and integrity, and who is presently a member of a guild which has standards equaling those of the Council.
