Lords of the Ters'Tai
An Exclusive Guild on the Mithaniel Marr Server

Code of Conduct


SECTION 100 - Offenses and Solutions

ARTICLE I - Offenses against individuals - Definition

A. Definitions

  1. Kill Stealing - Kill stealing is the act of causing sufficient damage to a NPC or other monster that the actions cause its demise, and, prior to causing said action, another player, with whom the offender is not grouped, has already started on the process of killing. It shall not be considered Kill Stealing if the other player has asked for help, in any public way. 
  2. Inappropriate Language - Inappropriate language, includes, but is not necessarily limited to, curses, racial slurs relating to the real world, description of bodily functions either real or fantasy, description of explicit sexual activities, and any other language which is not appropriate for the situation and/or the character being played. Inappropriate language is a violation of this code whether in Shout, Tell, OOC, Auction, Say or Group mode.
  3. Discourteous Behaviour is behaviour which is loud, obnoxious, rude, and otherwise socially unacceptable for the player's class and/or race being played.
  4. Disrespect is the act of showing a lack of courtesy, obedience, honor or fidelity to any person to whom such a duty is owed. As an example, but not by way of limitation, it is a disrespectful action to take arguments further than needed, or to inspect a person, intentionally, without their first consent.
  5. Stealing/Dishonesty - This offense consists of depriving any player, in guild or without, of any article or sum of money to which they are rightfully entitled, without their consent. Stealing/dishonesty shall include the offenses of fraud, extortion, deceit and robbery. 

B. Solutions

  1. Offenses under this article shall be resolved through restitution to the aggrieved party, unless the Judicial Lord shall find the element of fraud, in which case the offense shall be resolved as a capital offense. Stealing or dishonesty shall be tried as capital offenses.

ARTICLE II - Offenses against Morals - Definition

A. Definitions

  1. Misrepresentation of Self - This offense shall include misrepresenting the class, race, or level of a player character to anyone, and shall also include misrepresentation of possession of any item.
  2. Cheating shall include the acts of farming, i.e. taking advantage of a bug to obtain money or items to which you are not entitled, and shall also include the use of any third party "hack" to deprive others of a fair and equitable game. Cheating shall also include the acts of intentional and repeated violations of the server rules, as published by Verant, Inc.

B. Solutions

  1. Offenses under this article shall be tried as a capital offense.

ARTICLE III - Offenses against the Guild

A. Definitions

  1. Guild Property. Guild property is defined as property which is purchased, won in combat, created by quest, purchased by guild funds, or which is donated to the guild. Any item which is given to a member of the guild, excepting those items which are won as the prize in a guild event, remain the property of the Guild, and shall not be sold, transferred, traded or otherwise disposed of by the recipient without permission of the High Lord or his designated representative.

B. Solutions

  1. Violations of Article III, Section A shall be treated as a serious offense, and shall include restitution of the item, and/or expulsion of the offending party from the guild.

ARTICLE III - Miscellaneous Offenses

A. Definitions

  1. Repeat offenses - Repeat offenders are those who have been found to have committed any offense which was not adjudged to be capital, in a number exceeding three (3) in any calendar month.

B. Solutions

  1. Offenses under this article shall be defined as a capital offense.

SECTION 200 - Procedures for Violations

ARTICLE I - Mediation for Resolution

All offenses arising herein, excepting those designated specifically as not being eligible thereof, shall be first resolved by mediation between the offending party and the aggrieved person. The High Lord, or in his absence any member of the Council of Lords, shall appoint a neutral person to mediate the matter, and resolve the matter to the satisfaction of the aggrieved party, with the intent that the offense is not repeated. Repeat offenses, or matters which cannot be resolved by mediation, shall be referred to a Judicial Lord for determination in accordance with this Code.

ARTICLE II - Trial

          All matters herein shall be tried before a Judicial Lord, sitting as trier of fact and of law. In all matters herein, the accused shall have the right to summon witnesses in their defense, shall have the right to be present at proceedings, and shall have the right to have anyone of their choice defend them herein. The Crown shall be required to present evidence to the satisfaction of the Judicial Lord that the accused committed the offense, and it shall be further explained to the Judicial Lord why mediation was unable to solve the problem. The Judicial Lord shall have the right and responsibility to question witnesses, summon such persons who may have knowledge of the actions in question, and may do so without the approval of the Crown or the accused.
          The decision of the Judicial Lord may be appealed to the Council of Lords within one week of being decided, after which time they become final. The decision of the Council of Lords shall be deemed to be final. Any decision which calls for the removal of a member of the guild shall be automatically referred to the Council of Lords, which shall have the sole duty and right to impose final sentence.
