Who is Ellegon?

So, you are interested in learning more about me? Thanks very much. I hope to not disappoint you :-))

 

Ellegon is basically a character out of a series of books called "Guardians of the flames" written by Joel Rosenberg. It is the name of a very young dragon who is just lovely. Since I fell so in love with him, I borrowed his name for several occasions. One was my hero character in Illusia. Illusia is an online RPG currently in it's Alpha Stage. Being a hero is a responsible job as well as a pleasant job. My main duty was to help new players with a first tour around the lands and give them a short instruction of how the game works. Beside of that, I was able to change the names, descriptions and icons of items, as well as the icons of players. To find out more about Illusia, please visit there official Web-Site. Unfortunately I had to quit there, because I have not enough time to be there regularly and fulfill my duties.

I also use Ellegon in Everquest. Everquest is a Multi-Player Online RPG as well. It's a great game and I already met some nice people there. If you are into RPG's you should definitely check it out.

Since every character there needs an icon, I searched the web for one. I found a pretty cool pic from a grey elf. You have seen it on my main page. The picture was drawn by a great talent called Raina. Because I admire her work, I asked for her permission to use it and got her ok. Thanks again Raina! Please visit her site if you want to see more of her great skills.

Another character name I often use is BalthusDire. BalthusDire was the name of a character in a book from Steve Jackson called "The Citadel of Chaos". It is a powerfull sorcerer there. I liked the sound of the name and it's not that often like Gandalf.

I have an OOC live too. OOC is RPG-language and means "Out of Character". My real name is Eugen Weber and I live in Allschwil, Switzerland.I was born on the 23rd of December, 1970 in Basel (Switzerland) and work as an Internet/Intranet Consultant at Informatis Ltd.
