Guild Treasury

A guild treasury will be maintained for guild-related purchases and expenses. Due to the inherent logistical problems, the guild will not provide loans, but does encourage members to assist each other as much as they feel comfortable doing so. The guild will also maintain an inventory of useful items for purchase by guild members, to use as prizes for events, or as newbie give-away items. These items will be stored by the guild treasurer on a “mule” character, created specifically for this purpose.

There will be no dues as such. Instead, funds will be generated in two ways.

 

Guild Fund Hunts:
Periodically, guild fund hunts will be organized as a way to raise money for the guild while at the same time providing an opportunity for fellowship and fun. Groups will form to hunt profitable monsters with one guild officer or designated leader per group. To avoid confusion, all guild members should deposit their own coinage in the bank prior to the hunt. The groups will venture out, slay monsters, and gain experience and loot. Upon returning to town, all members should sell all loot and give the coinage to the guild officer or leader of their group, who will in turn give this coinage to the guild treasurer’s “bank” character for deposit. During fund hunts, special items found as loot should also be turned in and can be sold by the guild treasurer or stored for later use. If sold, the member who originally found the item will be given first opportunity to buy it from the guild.

 

Item Recycling:

Useful items can be donated to the guild for use of other guild members. When the using member outgrows the item, he or she is expected to donate the item back to the guild to be used by another member.  The donating member will, of course, receive recognition for his or her generosity.

 

Raffles:

Periodically, the guild will collect donated items and raffle off these items as prizes.
