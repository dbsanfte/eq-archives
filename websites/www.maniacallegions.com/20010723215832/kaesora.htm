Maniacals pay a visit to Xalgoz

 

After spending a week in the Dreadlands kicking Drolvargs, Giants, Chickens and Brutes around we decided on a good ole dungeon dive.  Keasora was a nice little under used vacation spot we heard so we went to pay Xalgoz a visit.

Zoning into Karnor’s Castle of a port to Emerald Jungle is always a dangerous thing to do and this trip was no exception.  As we were zoning in along came a Drolvarg and beat Yarrum to the ground as Juniper let loose the Winds of the South to carry us to the Emerald Jungle.  Thankfully, Yarrum got a resurrection and was spared the run from the Hidden Valley to the Castle.  A few minutes later we were back on our way.

The only incident in the Emerald Jungle was with Kildem.  Being the kind and gentle Ogre he is, he stopped on his way to smell the flowers.  Unfortunately this flower didn’t appreciate it and tried to bite his face off.  So much for kind and gentle.

Once inside there is a deep pit that we had to get down.  Looking into the pit, all I could think about was Raiders of the lost Ark when Harrison Ford tossed the torch to the floor and everything moved.  In our case, snakes would have been welcome.   The room below was full of Straithbone spiders, of which some were healers!  Steadying ourselves we plunged in only to land in front of the Runelord!  What an unexpected surprise.  A long hard battle ensued ending with many dead spiders and a battered, but not beaten, group of Maniacals.  The Runelord was good enough to give up a Dagger of Frost for Yarrum.  Pressing on to the next room we encountered more spiders and one of the oddest creatures we had seen yet, a Ravener.  This thing is all teeth and attitude!

Now we were clear of the spiders and were pressing into the undead.  Wanting to avoid the vast majority of ghosts along the way Durell made everyone invisible to the undead.  This made our trek to the temple much faster.

Once at the Temple we cleared the platform in front of it and began pulling to there while waiting for Xalgoz to join us.  We faced off against many Guardians, Minions and Warders of Xalgoz and were rewarded with 3 Dust Ransacker’s Packs.  We found the elusive Giant Guardian video bug as well.  Xalgoz finally made his appearance and he was not as happy to see us and put up quite a fight.  In the end he was no match for us as Kildem knocked a Xalgozian Fang out of his head.

After Xalgoz recovers a bit we shall return for more fangs on our roar to Howling Stones.  Rest for now Xalgoz, the Maniacals will be back.
