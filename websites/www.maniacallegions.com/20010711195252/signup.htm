MANIACAL LEGIONS

Recruitment Center

Membership in the Maniacal Legions is, as with any guild, an honor to be taken seriously.  Not only is it about people helping each other but the friendship that comes with being part of a larger whole.  We are pleased that we have a great group of people and are always looking for others who enjoy EverQuest as much as we do.

New members shall undergo 30 days of apprenticeship.  Apprentices will be required to participate in 2 events (Guild raids/groups or Guild Quests) within the 30 days.  Raids will be posted on the Events schedule and Quests in the Guild Hall.  After completion of apprenticeship the Apprentice will be deemed a Legionnaire in an appropriate ceremony.
