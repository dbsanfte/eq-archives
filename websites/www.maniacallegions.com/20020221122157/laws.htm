Laws of the Guild

Recruitment:

New members shall undergo 30 days of apprenticeship.  Apprentices will be required to participate in 2 events (Guild raids or Guild Quests) within the 30 days.  Raids will be posted on the Events schedule and Quests in the Guild Hall.  After completion of apprenticeship the Apprentice will be deemed a Legionnaire in an appropriate ceremony.
No Items shall be gifted during apprenticeship.

 

Public Conduct:
Arguing in public chat is not tolerated; take it to /tell if you have issue with someone.

 

Loot Rules:
These are guidelines to follow for fair looting of items.  These rules may be modified on a group-by-group basis if all members agree and rules are set at the beginning.

Categories:
There are 2 categories of loot:  Tradable and No Drop.  These are handled in much the same way with some exceptions.

Tradable:
Loot will go to the group member who can and will use it at the time of drop.  If more that one member will use, it shall be lottoed among those members.  If those that can use choose not to use, the item shall be group lottoed.   All else will be group lottoed.  If you got an item by use you are out of lotto till all members have won an equal number of items.

No drop:
Items will be lottoed between the group members that can and will use the item.  If only one member can or will use, the item defaults to that member.

Note:  Receiving an item by Default  does not remove you from lottos and is not considered winning for the One Item rule.

Loot Master:
If need be, a Loot Master will be assigned.  This person will be the only one looting and responsible for ensuring loot is divided evenly.  The Loot Master will lotto items, Tradable and No Drop, by randomizing between group members based on the eligible number in group (i.e. 1-6).  All tradable items will be divided during a break in the action to prevent rotting and respawn trains.  Cash will be divided evenly at the end of the group.

One item per member till everyone has an item:
Once you win an item in a category you are out of the lotto in that category until all members have won an item in that category.  If an item  drops later and you would prefer to have that item more than the item you previously won, you may enter the lotto for the second item only if you can lotto off the first item you won to the rest of the members.  If many more No Drop items are dropping than Tradable, as a courtesy, you may refrain from rolling on Tradable at your discretion.    If a bracer drops and you want to exclude yourself from the lotto because you might want to get in on a possible BP drop later in the day, so be it.  The choice and suppositions are yours to make. If you win a bracer and a breastplate drops later, sorry, but that's the luck of the draw.

Nothing rots:
Nothing rots, ever. Not ever. If something drops and somebody can use it, it's getting lottoed and looted, no questions asked. If nobody is around that can use it, hell, somebody can loot it just to freak out their friends with it, but we never get ourselves so wrapped up in policy that we forget to give somebody something.

 

“Ninja” looting not permitted and grounds for removal from the Guild.

 

Corpse Recovery:
No one leaves a CR unless released by the member on the CR.  As courtesy to others if a member can’t help let them go if they need to.
