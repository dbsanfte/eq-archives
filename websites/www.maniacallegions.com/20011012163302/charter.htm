Guild Charter

The Maniacal Legion is an EverQuest Guild based on the world of Xegony.

We will always strive to better ourselves as a whole and not necessarily as individuals.  We will defend our Guild brothers and sisters and assist them in times of need at all times.  In doing so we grow stronger as a Guild.

These are living documents and will grow with the Guild.
