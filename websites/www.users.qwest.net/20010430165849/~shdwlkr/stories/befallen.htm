The Road to Befallen, or, How I fell

 



Gruesome and I were hunting wisps and young kodiaks in West Commons on Sunday, attempting to hone our skills ... and make some cash, when the allotted time of the trip to Befallen arrived. We headed for Nektulos Forest, the arranged launching point of our journey, and finding no one there, we departed for Befallen. A courier met us on the way to inform us that most of our party had already arrived and were waiting for us.

Grue and I were separated in East Commons, and of course, I got lost. After wandering around in the desert, I finally discovered the entrance to Befallen where I was greeted by Tiladdar and Utanadar. Grue arrived shortly after me and we all headed down into the realm of the dead to test our mettle.

Plague rats were the first thing to assault us, but we made short work of them.

Grue found them to be a bit of a nuisance as their corpses prevented him from moving down the corridor to the next room. Til and I carved up the corpses in short order and our party continued.

Liilithe joined us shortly thereafter and produced a key to one of the locked doors. After fighting our way through some rather large skeletons, we happened upon Tolmet and his party trapped in a hallway by skeletons and an Necromancer Apprentice. Liil, Til, and Ut's favorite pet hacked away while Ut and I pounded the attackers with the forces of the Arcane. I am glad to report that all escaped with bodies in tact. Grue saw to our wounds and made us ready to continue.

Unfortunately, it was not fate's plan to allow Grue to continue with us. Some force of nature had dropped a beam in his path and he could not circumvent it. He bade us continue and he would remain so that we might return for healing when needed.

Again we waded through the skeletons, Apprentices and rats and found our way to a rather large, bare room. It was here we all took up positions along the wall to wait for our prey. Long we waited, and patient we were. Suddenly, we were surrounded by huge skeletons. We took them down one by one until they were nothing but a pile of bones on the floor.

Again we waited, and again we were confronted by skeletons. Finally, we were rewarded for our patience. Our target, the Shadow Knight Ogre made his presence known. Til, Liil, Ut, and I attacked him with a vengeance. He was a worthy advisary. He fought valiantly, but we were too much for him. We were rewarded with Spiked armor, which Til won in a game of dice.

We decided we would wait to see if this Ogre's brethren should come to avenge his death. Long we waited and the hour grew late. Tiladdar and Liilithe were required elsewhere and so we bid them farewell. Utanadar and I remained. We were soon joined by Ayalintha. Happy were we to accept her company.

Soon there after, the Ogre's brethren arrived. He ate my pet for lunch and had me for dessert. Try as they might, Ayal and Ut could not distract him from my corpse. My spirit heard the sounds of their valiant fight as it winged its way home to Nektulos, that I might be resurrected.

Again I made the journey to Befallen, again getting lost. It seems that getting my body out of Befallen was a much greater chore than getting it in. Ut and Ayal were assaulted at every turn and my body was lost ...er... misplaced... several times. Ut and Ayal finally made it to the foyer with my body. Upon arrival, Ut granted me invisibility to the minions of the dead, that I might reclaim my possessions.

A member of Tolmet's party found us, reporting that Tolmet had fallen through a hole in the floor and was having a difficult time retrieving his belongings. Ut happened to have a full set of keys and gave them to her, wishing her luck.

Deciding we had had enough of Befallen, our party split up- Ut and I to make some money stealing mandolins from a bard in Freeport, and Ayal to journey the land testing her skill.

We look forward to returning to Befallen, but have decided to wait until our skills are much increased, and perhaps the size of our party as well.

Written by Kelila Bloodfacet
