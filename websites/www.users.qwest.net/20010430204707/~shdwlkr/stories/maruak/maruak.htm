Fatal Enigma

The Court of Ruins is deadly host,

to those who have no need to boast,

and feared from the desert to the coast,

it harbors those of power grim.

But we, who many call an evil power,

who live our lives in ruined towers,

are like a dark and deadly flower,

and follow our own whims.
