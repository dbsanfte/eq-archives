What is an "Easter Egg?" No, we're not talking brightly-coloured poultry products. We're talking tidbits of humour that the programmers have hidden for their own egos or for our amusement. EverQuest, like many other popular computer programs, has it's share of Easter Eggs, and this site is an attempt to document many of them.
