Nyneve Al'Vere

 

 

 

Nyneve is a lonely but loving young Teir'Dal who searches the world for her lost companion she knew so long ago. She lives and hunts with careless abandon, and hides behind her cheerful silliness, for deep down within her is a pain, a need to be accepted where she is not, a longing to be loved were they cannot, and a hope to find the strength within herself.
