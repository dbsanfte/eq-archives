Recruiting the Vaultkeeper



Being a proud Tier-Dal, he maintained his stoic demeanor, refusing to show a hint of the humiliation he must endure. For reasons only the Gods themselves knew, he had been cursed by being born into the family of an ambitious minor Noble, who had a penchant for debauchery and ill-advised investments. Triel grudgingly had to admit,however, that given the circumstances, he would most likely have undertaken the same tactics as his mother had to save House Baenre from a terminal loss of face. Weakness was a liability. His father had been weak. Worse yet, he hadn't been discreet. His carousings, and drunken gambling had begun to become a source of inter-House gossip. The last straw had been virtually bankrupting their House by taking out ever-growing loans from the banks. The solution his mother had come to was elegant in it's simplicity, and yet ruthless enough to leave no doubt in the other Houses as to the continued strength of House Baenre. His father had died from a particularly 'potent' drink. He, had been removed from the Warrior's Academy and placed into the 'service' of the main bank in Neriak.

Having strength and intelligence made him an asset. His lineage made him a bargaining chip. And so, he continued the tasks he was assigned without complaint or visible signs of contempt. His musings were interrupted by sudden darkening of the vault he was presently working in, and a thunder-deep voice booming,"Vaultkeeper, me want talk wid' you now!"

He turned around quickly in his wooden chair and was astonished to be looking into the face of a particularly immense Ogre! He knew that his usual non-expressive demeanor had been broken by the grin the Ogre gave. He stood over ten feet tall, with his horned helm brushing the ceiling as he bent over, his massive body in deep black and vivid blue armor effectively blocking off the entry, and exit from the vault. Triel, attempting to recover his composure, raised an eyebrow and stated smugly, "You are obviously lost. The Foreign Quarter is above and in the other chambers. I'm sure you will find someone more SUITABLE to aid you there." He made no effort to conceal the contempt from his voice, and turned his back to the Ogre with a dismissive turn.

"Perhaps you would prefer if I spoke to you thus, in your own language, foolish coin-counter?" The voice had boomed out in the same tone, but in flawless Tier-Dal. Triel spun around with a look of utter surprise on his face. "Oh yes. Not all Ogres are as stupid as you would believe. But those who have wisdom, feign oafishness, as having both strength and intelligence, is to be feared." "Enough prattle, I am in need of one such as you, and I have come to enlist you into my service."

Triel cleared his suddenly dry throat. "Ah, I am currently under contract to the Bank Manager here, and it is a non-conditional term."

The Ogre looked amused at Triel's obvious discomfort.

"It has been taken care of little one. Your House's 'Blood Debt' has been paid-in full. You are mine now." With that he grinned revealing rows of meticulously sharpened teeth between too gleamingly white savage tusks. He reached into a pouch at the side of the blackened belt upon which swung small skulls and produced a glowing black medallion bearing runes and symbols. Triel recognized these as being the heraldic symbols of the Court of Ruins, a guild headed by House Bloodfacet. Triel blanched, at least as much as one of his kind could. He had heard tales of other 'recruitments'. He shook his head stubbornly. "No, I am free now, my debt paid by your own words. My family will be expecting me, I will just be on my wa.."

His words had been cut off by the flashing spin of an immense axe from the back of the Ogre's trappings, stopping mere inches from his throat. "Perhaps you misunderstood me. You are MINE now! You belong to Fleshrender Bloodwine, and the CoR."

Triel tried to muster his courage. "I will summon the Dragoons, you misbegotten blight upon the world. They will come here and teach you the sovereignty of Neriak!"

Fleshrender, to Triels' surprise, chuckled. "Aye, you could do that, but not before I turn you into a cloak for my personal use!" He underscored the statement by pressing the edge of the blade slightly into Triel's neck, producing a thin red line. "I see now that you understand. Your duties will be made known unto you. In return, you will be equipped and PROPERLY trained as a warrior."

Fleshrender noticed his look of puzzlement. "Aye.You are of no use to me as a simple coin-counter Vaultkeeper."

Trying to retain a last vestige of pride, Triel began to state, "My name is Triel, of the House Baenr.."

"Your name is Vaultkeeper, until I decide you have EARNED the right to be known otherwise." "Now come with me, we have much to discuss."

Later that evening, as Triel thought upon his new-found service, a splendidly dressed Tier-Dal from the Guild known as 'Legacy of Sorrow' strode up to him. "Vaultkeeper, yea, you..CoR, come here, I have some things for you." He then gave the astonished Triel a complete set of bronze armor. Hmmm...perhaps this wouldn't be as bad as he thought.
