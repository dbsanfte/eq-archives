This just in!

Some of you may have heard that Wizards will be given the ability to summon Familiars when Shadows of Luclin is released. Here, for the first time, is a screenshot of the new Familiar. Minor Familiars will only be able to equip one weapon while Lesser Familiars will equip two. Familiars, like the one seen here, will have the ability to equip three weapons while Greater Familiars will be able to equip four.


If you haven't done so already, you can now pre-order Shadows of Luclin through Caster's Realm's shop. They offer ordering through both EBWorld and Amazon.com. EBWorld is including a Tome of Knowledge with each pre-order.

Everlore has seven more screen shots of the Stoneburnt Mountains.

Auric has consolidated many of the scams going around to obtain your username and password. Most involve email scams, but one is an in-game scam with a twist.

There is a nice little editorial over at Lum the Mad about character transfers. It gave me a little laugh and made me wonder just how well thought out this character transfer idea was.

The Sleeper has Awakened! Check out the EQ News forum of the Guild Hall and Mobhunter for more info and screen shots.
