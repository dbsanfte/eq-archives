The Abduction of Tyre Dreadslayer

 


She moved through the guildhall of the Order of the Emerald Phoenix as if she belonged there, with a casual ease one could say, she was however, not oblivious to the barely controlled panic that surrounded her. It only made her job that much easier.From what she could see, the first attacks had gone very well, many wounded and maimed, few deaths, good for morale to remember how your buddy became so mangled and that you could be next.

 

The readiness of the Dwarven guards has always been a source of pride in Kaladim so it was no surprise that the guards on duty immediately formed up for action and descended upon the Emerald Phoenix guildhall, prepared for anything. What they found was less than they expected but more than hoped for. The giant emerald green phoenix had been blasted to the ground. 

 Phoenix Guards, dressed in their green livery lay injured and dying. Wisps of smoke drifted lazily up towards the roof of the mighty cavern. Something had hit this place and hard. The officer in charge, a mere lieutenant, orders his men to advance with caution. Lord Grezz, the commander of the Order of Emerald Phoenix, met them.

" Lieutenant, would you send your men to the front gate of Kaladim as soon as possible, we have a missing gnome, in fact it is Sir Tyre Dreadslayer. He must be stopped at all costs."

 Just three weeks promoted, Lieutenant Axehome, was a little unsure of what to do, no, he admitted to himself he was very unsure of what to do. He had sent a messenger off to wake the guard commander, but these things just were not supposed to happen here.

" Lord Grezz, I beg thee, I must wait until I hear from my own commander, till that time I will deploy my men to guard the entrance of your mighty hall."

 Grezz fumed. " Lieutenant, Sir Tyre Dreadslayer may have gone crazy, even now he could going on a rampage through other parts of Kaladim. What if he gets loose? Think of all the people, the farmers, the merchants, the innocents he may kill in his current state of mind, what are you going to do about them?"

 Axehome thought to himself for a moment and then dispatched runners to alert all guard posts on the way to the port. He then ordered his sergeant to deploy troops in a defensive position around the entry.

 Lord Grezz was beside himself. "Why don't you send a messenger to Firiona Vie? DO YOU THINK THAT A WIZARD OF HIS POWER IS GOING TO TAKE A BOAT?! HAVE YOU BEEN DRINKING?"

 Lieutenant Axehome was concerned, Lord Grezz was obviously distraught. " M'Lord Grezz, perhaps if you would calm down, I am sure that Colonel Swordforge will be able to give you all the assistance you need, in the mean time, why don't you see to your wounded."

 Lord Grezz had never been so causally dismissed before and was about to increase the level of the discussion when a messenger came from within.

 "Lord Grezz!", the human was out of breath, "m'Lord, Sir Dracoil has sent me to you, there is word." The messenger looked over at the dwarven guards watching the drama unfold. He was unsure if he should speak in front of them, that notion was quickly dispelled.

"Do not wait! Speak!" Grezz was in no mood the be trifled with.

 "Sir Dracoil begs to report that he is finding dead and wounded in other parts of the hall as well."

 Grezz sighed. " Sir Tyre apparently visited a number of places in the guild before leaving."

 The messenger looked perplexed for a moment and then realized his error. " No, m'Lord these injuries have come from sword thrusts, Squire Agnino who is with Sir Dracoil says there are indications of necromantic magic's!"

 The dwarven guards started to murmur amongst themselves. Lieutenant Axehome shouted something, several messengers ran off, one toward the guard barracks, the other toward the temple. If he had seen it, Lord Grezz would have been pleased.

 " What? This is insane." Thinking for just the barest moments Lord Grezz made a decision; " Get to Sir Huntir, tell him I said to round up as many spare men as possible, then reinforce the city gate. Someone has to do it" Grezz glared at the guard officer.

 Lieutenant Axehome was a little concerned. Should he prevent the Emerald Phoenix reinforcements from going to the front gate? Could he? Come to think of it the messenger he sent to the front gate should have returned by now. If anything had happened…….

 Within minutes Sir Huntir was leading a patchwork force of eighteen cadre out of the front gate. " This is all that could be spared, Dracoil is doing a room by room search."

His eyes said there was more to the story than he was willing to say in front of the city guards. "Bombast and the others are tending to the wounded and dead. Bombast wants to see you as soon when you have time." Grezz nodded. Huntir left to rejoin his troops as they marched towards the city gates..

 "WHAT IS GOING ON HERE!"

 More of a statement than a question, Grezz turned to face Colonel Swordforge. Rumor had it that he took Swordforge as his name because he made very good weapons in his youth. Other rumors said that he had never seen a battle. Grezz looked at his escort, four guards in light battle gear, he believed the rumors.

 "Colonel, we seem to be having a problem and your good lieutenant here is either not experienced enough in battle or is not taking it seriously enough to deal with it."

 Fate has a strange way of coming into play, of bringing to light the realities that exist. It was at this moment that fate chose to intervene in the form of one of Sir Huntir's men shouting at his ran to his guildmaster.

 " Lord Grezz! Lord Grezz! They are all dead, Kaladim's outer guards have all been killed!" shouted the messenger for all to hear. A small contingent of paladins, along with several clerics arrived just in time for the latest in what was turning into a never-ending maelstrom of darkness.

 "Calm down" Grezz commanded; " Tell me slowly what happened"

 "As we arrived at the city gates we found the guards all dead. Sir Huntir chose to continue to the main entrance. There we found the last of the guards fighting a losing battle. We charged towards the battle as the last of the guards fell, but they retreated and vanished into thin air."

 Within moments there was pandemonium among the city guards. Actually Grezz thought, the lieutenant looked the calmest of all.

 Lord Grezz looked back at the messenger; "Ok easy there lad, tell me how many there were, what they were armed with, everything you can remember." Grezz thought that if Huntir had sent word back upon encountering the first of the dead they might have been able to do something. Upon reconsideration though he realized that even if he a runner had come back for reinforcements there were none available. No competent ones at least, as he looked over Kaladim forces. The runner calmed down, took a deep breath and began.

 " There were some with our livery, about five, though I could not see who they were, based solely on size though, one was certainly Sir Tyre. The others though.." The runner shuddered as if suddenly chilled.

 "Take a deep breath there", Grezz tries to reassure the runner, but her had a very bad feeling about what was about to happen.

 The runner took a deep breath before continuing; " Ti'er Dal, several of them, I saw a magician and enchanter, I could tell by their pets! There was an ogre with a great big hammer and a necromancer, escorted by two great skeletons. m'Lord there was also a Shadowknight with a sword the glowed black, (the clerics made warding signs) I can't describe how it glowed black, but it did. Sir Huntir and his men are in defensive positions around the outer entry.

 The runner continued to babble, falling more and more into hysteria. One of the clerics had sent word back to the temple calling for help. Members of the Stormguard had been arriving to offer their assistance to their sister guild, now they sent word back to armor up for a full confrontation. Colonel Swordforge sent word to turn out the entire guard. Citizens locked their shutters and went to their cellars. The sounds of battle were nowhere to be heard, yet Kaladim geared for war.

"Lord Grezz, you the rest of Emerald Phoenix will remain in your guildhall until we can sort this out, if Kaladim is under attack we will call for your assistance."

 "As you wish Colonel" Grezz turned and walked silently into the guildhall. This was unbelievable. Somewhere in the confusion, he remembered that Bombast wanted to speak with him as soon as possible. He went in search of his friend, wondering what else could go wrong.

 

Continuing through the hallways of the guildhall, she gave the impression that she belonged there, in all the confusion no one paid her the least bit of attention. Her mission, the most difficult of all, provide information to the retreating forces. Would Emerald Phoenix put together a pursuit force? She doubted it, things were chaotic. Kaladim had the forces available but for whatever reason, the dwarven guards rarely if ever left the confines of the great city.

"milady?"

She turned to face her questioner, it was a sergeant, one of the old ones.. It was bound to have happened eventually. "Aye?" Best to play it off like nothing was wrong at all.

"What are you doing here? Your not supposed to be here at all, especially with everything that is happening!"

She raised a single eyebrow in question yet in doing so, imparted all the answers to all the questions he had. The sergeant drew his weapons and began to retreat. He needed help, and fast.

 

Squire Trem was pleased. He had gathered sixteen members of the cadre to 'assist' him with the dwarven bandits that plagued the roads. So far he had brutally killed three of the buggers and was looking forward to hunting down the others and killing them like the dogs they were. He smiled as he remembered the last one, he had laid down his weapons and surrendered only to butchered. Trem smiled.

" You said something m'Lord?"

It was his personal retainer. "Nay, I was just thinking happy thoughts". A mounted rider thundered toward them, still far enough away not to be a threat. As his men scrambled to prepare in case of attack, Trem sat there watching. It was obvious to him that this was a mere messenger.

 Even before he could even give the proper greeting, Trem had commanded that he speak. The story the messenger told was one of woe, Tyre Dreadslayer gone crazy? The possibilities were endless. Trem smiled. He instructed the messenger to continue his journey and began to think, where would he go if he were on the run. With sudden inspiration he bade his men to mount up, for he knew exactly where they would be found and that destination was not far away.

 

She did not have time for this. The sergeant was good, real good, all he did was fight a defensive action while looking for someone to help him. Good thing they were in an apparently deserted part of the guild. This had to end and fast. It is said that halflings of Rivervale make the best throwing knives on Norrath, enhanced with poison, they made for the most deadly of missiles. One of the two she carried found it's way into the chest of her most worthy opponent. She had all of the information she could gather, it was time for her to meet with the others.

 

It was less than an hour's hard ride. Squire Trem and his men arrived at the ruins of an ancient, long forgotten tower. Some long dead Lord ruled from here, not a dwarf, but something much more powerful he mused. Guarding the ruin was a single skeleton, a weak undead, he had been victorious over them many a time. He slowed his advance, there was something not quite right, the skeleton was blurry. Trem signaled. Four of his men readied bows, three more began casting protective spells (they were druids), the rest readied swords. Trem and the two swordsmen advanced with caution. The skeleton turned to face his attackers.

It was carrying a book. It cackled at them in challenge.

 "Choose the manner of your death"

 Trem's men murmured, they had all fought the undead before, but none had ever heard a mere skeleton speak.

 "The only thing that will be destroyed here today is you" Trem answered.

 "Not likely that, me thinks Trem that your death will be as painful as can possibly be made." The new voice was as familiar as any, Trem had been right all along, the price of being right was going to be high indeed. They all turned around to face Lord Tyre Dreadslayer.

 "Lord Tyre, I request that you return with me to Kaladim, you are obviously not well and the ministrations of Sir Bombast will be of help to you."

 "Your actions have sealed your fate, it is just a matter of how much it is going to hurt. I suspect quite a bit actually."

 "Your not serious, you think that you and your friend over there can actually stop all of us?"

 "Why don't you take another look."

 At that moment, several sharp intakes of breath from his men alerted him the something amiss behind him. Turning, Trem saw that the skeleton had changed into a fearsome Teir Dal dressed in robes of the darkest black. Four spearmen stood in front of a standard bearer, holding the colors of the Court of Ruins. Trem understood that he was facing Lord Utanadar, their leader. There was a tug at his sleeve. Trem turned back to face Tyre, only to find that he too was no longer alone.

 A stunning lady, her skin of the deepest blue had joined him. As had what was obviously another wizard and of all things an Ogre! A big ogre, with a very big hammer. This was not good. All of the casters began to weave their hands in the begins of spells, Trem charged Tyre only to be stunned by a blast of magic. He could not move, his men were dying fast, he felt as though his insides were on fire, he saw his leading sergeant fighting to get to the standard, he was sure his blood was boiling, it was agonizing. A loud explosion from the area of the ogre ended the struggle for one of the druids. Trem saw Tyre smiling. With a quick prayer Trem felt rejuvenated, he broke free and headed for Tyre at the very least he could kill him.

 The spellcasters had been using up their power. The ogre was still killing his men. He had a chance. He had always underestimated everyone else thought Tyre; 'and over estimate himself' he said under his breath. The first spell knocked him back, wrecking his insides in the process. The second spell, coming from the wizard he identified as Dismar made him feel as though he was in the wastes of Everfrost. He lost track of the other spells and what they did, all he knew was that he was going to die, that he was sure of.

"You did not have to kill him so slowly."

"He deserved it"

With the very last of his ebbing strength, he turned. There stood Lady Serri. He could not see how she was bound. If he had known she was there he could have freed her, she could have changed the tide of battle. His final thoughts were that he had not alerted his brothers and sisters in the guild and that he had failed,… everyone.

 "Are they all dead?"

"That or dying, come we must not tarry."

"Just a moment" Lord Utanadar spoke with quiet authority, "Before we go Tyre, I must ask you to accept this." He handed Tyre a small wand, it was a badge of office.

"Let all within the Court of Ruins know that hence forth you are the Lord Wizard of the Court of Ruins."

"I accept with honor my liege." Spoke the gnome wizard. "Long have I waited for this day, that it has finally arrived fills my heart with joy. I would hasten to say that we should depart, for these lands can be very unfriendly."

"Agreed"

Tyre and Dismar both open portals so for those who could not gate, those who could vanished of their own accord.

 Not everyone had died, not everyone had even participated. One person had watched the events unfold from the very beginning. To have engaged in combat would have meant death. The concept of death was not one that was frightening, but someone had to bear witness to the tragic events that had befallen these people.

 

Grezz's mind refused to accept what Sir Bombast had just told him. " You are going to have to say that again Bombast, for I am sure I did not understand you correctly." Grezz looked around, there was no one in the room but them. " Bombast I am but a simple servant of Brell, please, please say what you think in simple dwarven so I can understand."

 "This required an enchanter, and a powerful one at that." Looking first to confirm they were still alone, Bombast muttered a few words and waved his hands. As gentle blue light washed over the body it slowly changed. What lay before Lord Grezz was no longer the body of Lady Serri, but that of a chambermaid. Grezz was stunned. Bombast stood there in silent prayer.

 It was a harsh decision, but it had to be done. "Bombast, you must leave Emerald Phoenix, let all know that you have left. I think your search will be easier if you are not a part of the order. In fact, tell whoever you trust that they too must resign. Search the lands, when you have word or Lady Serri or Tyre for that matter, return with haste."

 " I shall find Sir Tyre and I shall do what has to be done" Bombast grimaced. It would be hard and he would ask for forgiveness afterwards, but Grezz was right, it had to be done.

 "No, when you find him, return to the hall, we will have to gather our strength, especially if he has joined the Legion of Ka."

 "…and what of you m'Lord?"

 "I too shall sally forth to learn what I may, but first I shall turn over the scepter to Lady Pistol. Have those you speak with use the secret passages to leave, lets not alert the guards. "

 With a nod Bombast departed, wondering how long it a time would pass before he saw his friends again.

 Her goal was just ahead, the no-so-secret-but-rarely-used lower entrance to the guild. From the second basement it was possible to leave by means of a passage that led outside of Kaladim to one of the merchant houses. Usually it was lightly guarded and the portcullises could only be raised from the inside. Given the situation upstairs, her best hope was to sneak past the guard, raise the first portcullis and roll under as it fell back down. The guards, having figured out something was amiss, would have to spend a few precious moments dealing with gate and sending a message.

 "HALT! HALT!" the voice of the guard was clear and unmistakable, she had no choice but to obey. "Who are you and what are you doing here?" It was clear that in all the confusion, no one had bothered to reinforce this position.

 "Who are you and what are you doing here?" one of the two guards repeated. Both looked at her warily.

 " I have been sent to reinforce you. In case something happens down here."

 "What did you say your name was?" the senior man repeated

"Bah." scowled the youngest; "nothing ever happens down here, me sergeant gave me thi……"

 He was unable to finish. The poison on her blade attacked his central nervous system. Paralyzed, the young guard fell to the ground, barely able to breathe. Her second sword missed its target. The senior guard, with reflexes seasoned by many combats, jumped back and drew his weapon. The fight was on. Or rather would have been, for as he moved forward, confident in his ability, she, confident in hers, easily tossed her other halfling throwing knife into her attacker's throat. His swords clattered to the ground.

 "Sloppy" she said to no one in particular. The guard, choking on his own blood was trying to say something. Curiously, but with great caution she bent over to hear his last words. There was a faint smile on the dying guards' face as she complied with his last request.

 " Imoen,…… Imoen Windreaper"

 With that she disappeared into the tunnel.
