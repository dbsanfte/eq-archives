Dino Frenzy!

I was on an expedition in the Ocean of Tears with a trusted friend called Antin Derov . Antin disappeared for a few minutes. We all asked "Where are you Antin?", to which he did nothing more than to reply with a chuckle. Shortly after he mentioned that he was bringing a friend to visit us. His friend was a rather large Dinosaur Lizard.
