Fledgling Members: When a person first joins RA,
they are a Fledgling Member. Before they can
become a Fledgling Member, they must read The Call
and The Charter and see our message board.
When they apply, they must give their email address.

A guild officer will find them a sponsor. The Fledgling
Member is required to attend guild events and
group with guild members, when possible. The
Fledgling Member will complete the Adopt-A-Newbie
Quest, and be recommended to be a Regular Member
by their sponsor and two Regular Members.
The Fledgling Member is encouraged to post messages
on the Message Board. A Fledgling Member
thus becomes a Regular Member through demonstrating
the willingness to practice random acts to
kindness while playing EverQuest.

The Adopt-A-Newbie Quest

You Fledglings out there can get started on
                      the quest that will help give you Regular
                      Membership: the Random Acts Fledgling
                      Adopt-A-Newbie Quest!

                      It is your task to find a newbie, I mean
                      a real newbie: someone who is new to
                      EverQuest.

                      Note: These newbies are not necesarily being
                      brought into Random Acts as Fledglings. We
                      are just helping them learn EverQuest.
 

                      Then do the following:

                         Get their permission to help them.
                         Tell them you will show them the ways of
                      Norrath (or your own phrasing) and all you
                      ask in return is that they report your help
                      to a Random Acts Council Member.
                         Teach them how to use the Chat screen and
                      the basic commands (eg., /loc, /r, /t, etc).
                         Teach them how to hunt if necessary.
                         Teach them how to use the internet for information
                      (eg., Allakhazam's, EQVault, EQAtlas, etc.)
                         Check with them daily for a minumum of three
                      days to see if they need any help and to
                      answer their questions.
                         Giving them items is not required nor is
                      it forbidden.
                         Make sure they know the safe and unsafe
                      places to go when traveling and hunting.
                         Teach them the mail quest (if of good race)
                      and at least one other newbie quest.

                      By the way, I find newbies every day without
                      even trying. I run into them everywhere.
                      They always look a little dazed--which is
                      amazing because they don't change their
                      facial expressions!

                      If you try to find one and cannot, perhaps
                      someone in the guild knows of one who needs
                      a little help getting started in EverQuest.

                      Fledglings....on your mark, get set, go!

                      Good Journey!

                      Hamadryad for Random Acts
