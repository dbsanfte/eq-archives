How to Join

Membership in Random Acts is by invitation only. That means you will need to seek us out and get to know us. There are a few different ways to find us. Do a "/who all random" and strike up a conversation in tells. Group with us--all are welcome at our hunting parties and raids, provided there is room. Or you can always attend one of our scheduled events--just let someone know you are there to meet us, and we will invite you to join the event chat channel.
Suggestions, comments, questions? Send them here.
