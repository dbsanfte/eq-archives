Who We Are

Random Acts is a society based on spreading kindness throughout Norrath, wherever and whenever possible, as detailed in our Call and Charter. We have over 100 members (and more alts) of almost every level and description. Our guild will turn two years old on June 5, 2002.

Our focus as a guild is to help others, wherever and whenever we can. Kindness to others is our highest standard. We expect every member to adhere to the philosophy outlined in our Call and Charter, and to uphold the Guild Ethical Guidelines while playing a tagged character.

We are dedicated to helping each other as well as the rest of the players on the server. We share our knowledge of the game with each other, and help each other with hunts and quests. Active members who like getting involved will be very happy in our community, because we strongly believe that you get out of Everquest what you put into it.

Random Acts is not a roleplaying guild, but we do welcome roleplayed characters as long as the character's personality does not conflict with our mission of spreading kindness.

What We Do

Any kind of character can uphold the principles of Random Acts, regardless of class, race, level, or equipment. Therefore, we do not restrict membership based on any of those factors. Since our focus as a guild is not leveling or raiding, but service, the decision of how to spend time in Everquest is left up to the individual member of Random Acts.

Unlike many other guilds, Random Acts has no system for pushing people into the higher levels of the game. Members are not required to be present at any guild hunting parties or raids. We have no requirement for how many hours per week a member must play--real life comes first, always. We do not use a point system, nor do we require members to finish their Epic Quests.

In other words, if you decide to join us, you must be prepared to organize your own higher-level activities. Doing so can be frustrating. Planning an encounter and recruiting people to join you requires much more work on your part than just going along on the scheduled raids.

But if you want to try something scary, you can count on our support. We have built a strong core of 50+ members who know their classes and enjoy meeting tough challenges. And since we are here to help others, you can bet your last platinum piece that your party will get you out of that dungeon with all your equipment intact--or literally die trying.

Beyond hunting and raiding, we hold events that benefit others on the server. First Aid Camps are held on the second Saturday and fourth Sunday of each month. Attendance at the camps is required if you are online. Details will be posted on the Events, Hunting, and Gathering board. We try to schedule the camps at times when members of all real-life geographical locations can attend. Advance transportation is available through guild porters. We encourage friends and other interested parties to join us at the camps.

We hold other kinds of events as well. In the last year, we've had weddings, surnaming parties, story circles, a language fair, a swap meet, an Olympics, a scavenger hunt, a bat-bashing event, and a whack-the-gnome contest, among others. These events happen when there is enough interest among guild members. As with First Aid Camps, everyone is invited to attend.

Still Want to Join?

Membership in Random Acts is by invitation only. That means you will need to seek us out and get to know us. There are a few different ways to find us. Do a "/who all random" and strike up a conversation in tells. Group with us--all are welcome at our hunting parties and raids, provided there is room. Or you can always attend one of our scheduled events--just let someone know you are there to meet us, and we will invite you to join the event chat channel.

We're looking forward to meeting you!
