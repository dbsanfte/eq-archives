Guild Activities

  1. We spend 4 nights a week, typically Sun-Wed, pursuing XP and other personal goals. A key component of the guild's long-term success will be to build a foundation of skilled higher level members that can prevail in most zones against most mobs.
  2. We spend two nights a week gearing up. Another key component of the guild’s long term success will be to get the best armor, weapons and equipment for our members. Core equipment raids are HoT farming and 2 FFA nights.
