THE HOLE
One fine day in Norrath Banthaf and I, Quaymar, decided to join some fellow guildies in the new zone called Stonebrunt Mountains. It sounded like lots of fun and an opportunity to explore some areas of Norrath that we had never seen before. So off we went! We got a port to Toxx and headed for Stonebrunt through Paineel. Upon zoning into Paineel we noticed this cool looking tower off in the distance. Since we were waiting to hook up with some other people, Banthaf and I decided we would take a look at this cool looking tower and off we went. Well Banthaf saw the cliff (Maybe because he is so short...hehe) and I did not. So off I went into "The Hole" !!! AAAAAAAAHHHHHHHHH My first thought was WTF?? Then, OMG I'm falling REALLY FAR, then, whew...I'm zoning (Did I find a shortcut somewhere??) Then, OMFG I'M FALLING MORE!! And finally, SPLAT!! 10,000 dmg right in front of the main mob in the Hole. So, naturally, I found myself sitting back at Firiona Vie wondering how in the hell am I going to get myself out of this one??

After my fellow guildies finished laughing (Which took quite some time I might add..lol) they got serious and started to try to figure out how to get me out of that god forsaken place! While there was much chatter about Rogues going in and dragging my corpse, me getting invis and going in, etc.... I was petitioning for some help and, of course, was told that I was pretty much on my own!! Meanwhile, Banthaf and I were sending tells back and forth about how I was going to quit EQ if I couldn't get my 46 Pally that I worked soooo hard to develop out of that F*&%^ ing Hole!!! Btw....Banthaf is my RL friend and he was on the phone with me for awhile laughing so hard I thought he was going to go into seizures!! So, anyway, after many ideas that didn't or wouldn't work, just as I was about to give up, Alexbacal, my hero!!, said hey wait a minute! I know a 55 Necro that can summon your corpse for you!! THANK GOD!! Well, Alexbacal, my hero!!, managed to convince the Necro to help me out for a fee of course, which Alexbacal paid and I later reimbursed. So the Necro met me at the entrance to Paineel and I followed him down to the CORRECT way to get into "The Hole" and he showed me how to squeeze my way in through the rocks (which only works one way!). And then he summoned my corpse for me.

Then he said BYE and left me standing there staring at these mean looking ELEMENTALS that were looking at me like I would be a tasty snack for them!! So I tried to swim back out the way I came in and could not squeeze my way back out the way I got in....DOH So i gave up at this point, after 4-5 hours of this stuff I was exhausted, and I logged. Luckily when I logged on the next day there was a group just starting a raid on "The Hole" and I was standing right there in their midst and one of their friendly wizzies TL'd me out of there.....THANK GOD!!! Thank you Alexbacal for saving my arse, I'll never forget that!! I LOVE THIS GUILD!! I could tell quite a few stories where this guild has saved my arse when I got in a tight spot and I have saved a few myself as well. For example, getting feared in PoH....OMG!!! I shiver at that memory...but that's another story!
