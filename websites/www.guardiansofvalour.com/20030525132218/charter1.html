Member Conduct

  1. Guardians should think very carefully before using /guildremove. Take a break, cool down, talk to officers, etc. before making a final decision. Personal guildremoval will be taken very seriously by the officers.
    (a) Players asking for a re-invitation to the guild after a voluntary /guildremoval must be voted on by the officers of the guild. Re-invitation may be issued after a majority vote.
    (b) Players reinvited to the guild will be subject to all new recruitment rules such as probationary and epic waiting periods.
  2. Guardians will NOT engage in kill stealing (KS'ing). If there is a dispute over a camp, please try to conduct yourself with honour and work it out. If necessary, please use the /REPORT command or turn /LOG ON to record the event in case a GM needs to be called.
  3. Guardians will not use the /ANON feature as that does not show the Guild tag. Instead, please use the /ROLE feature.
  4. Guardians will not engage in duelling out of anger or revenge. All duelling shall take place in a PvP area such as the Arena.
  5. Guardians will not beg for items or PP in any zone. If you have need of some item or PP, please contact your guildmates or the Guild Treasurer.
  6. Guardians will abide by all posted SONY/Verant EQ rules.
  7. Guardians are encouraged to refrain from vulgar and profane language in guild chat. There are rare occasions when such language is appropriate, however, members are encouraged to keep such occurrences to a minimum.
  8. Guardians should always be courteous and respectful of other guild members. Whining or flaming of other guild members in guild chat or on the message boards will not be tolerated. If you have a concern, please state it in a courteous and constructive manner in /TELL first to the guild member involved and then to an officer if necessary. If you post a courteous proposal on the boards, the officers will bend over backwards to make things right for you. Please read the Dispute Resolution policy for further information.
  9. Disciplinary action will normally consist of one or more warnings before a player is removed from the guild. However, guild removal may or may not be preceded by warnings if the infraction(s) is(are) serious enough to warrant immediate guild removal. Guild removal shall be preceded by a statement from the removing Officer stating the reasons for removal.
  10. During the probationary period (two (2) weeks), a member may be guildremoved without notice.
