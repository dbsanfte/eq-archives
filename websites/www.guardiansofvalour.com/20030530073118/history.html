The Legend of Guardians Of Valour
(The Account of Ristor and Foen)

Long ago, in the city of Qeynos, a mighty Paladin was born into the world of Norrath. Born to poor parents of a lower class, the mighty Ristor strived to set the wrongs of the world right. Poor, unarmored, and defenseless, he went to the Paladin Guild in Qeynos. There he recieved his first training in the arts of Battle. Now was his time to go out into the world and find adventure. Never forgetting what his father said on his dying bed, "Be Humble, Be Noble, Help Others, and Bring back the Honor to the Nobleheart Name!", the might Paladin set out. Ristor was not alone though. He was joined by the kindest of Druids, Foen Wolfrunner. Foen hailed from the city of Surefall Glade. As Ristor's most trusted friend and healer, he followed Ristor out into the wilds surrounding Qeynos.

As time went by, the two battled through many a fight and had many a death. Until one day, when they found themselves able to adventure into the depths of Blackburrow. Little did our heros know this is where the seeds of brotherhood would be planted. This is were they would meet the first members of the Guardians of Valour.

It was a dark evening in the tunnels of Blackburrow, Foen had fallen to the evil Scrawny Gnolls. Ristor, low on health and alone, thought of running. Then out of nowhere, the healing light fell over Ristor. When the gnoll died by his sword Ristor turned to see a Shaman of Barbarian descent. The Shaman told Ristor his name was Ashden. Ashden had traveled to Blackburrow to fight with his wife Aleena, who was a cleric from the same race as Ristor and Foen. Ashden and Aleena Joined the mighty heros and thus, the chains of brotherhood were formed.

Days passed in the world, until one day Ristor (whose only weakness is woman), running through the hills of Qeynos ran into a long haired Goddess, wearing the robe of a Wizard. Ristor stopped to admire her figure. When she turned he felt like it was only right to hail her. She told him her name was Missusbon Wigglefinger. She was related to a mighty Wizard named Bonanon Thunderfist. Bonanan had conivnced her to go into the wizardly arts with him. Our hero saw a spark in her eye and knew that they would be friends till the world of Norrath exsists no more.

The five friends set out together, headed to the bandit country of West Karana. Our heros were compelled to find another battler to fight along side of them. Hearing the call, a mighty warrior came to them. Matrumlus the EarthKeeper fought well. Brandishing his swords in the defence of them all, he won their faith and trust.

Days passed and the group slowly grew into a more formidable force. Then the time came for them to let all of Norrath know of their faith and trust in each other. They set out to build a Guild. A guild like no other guild, one that would mean friendship, trust, honor, and would help defend the weak and sick against the attacks of evil. To do this they would need to find more heros to join with them. Enter the mighty Enchanter Gilauran, the mighty Paladin Pelles, the noble paladin Landrover, the dark and mysterious Zilth, the Druid Umbreon. And many others joined them. Thus on this day was born the Guardians of Valour!

I hope this tale instills the feelings of Honor we felt when this guild was started. I hope that the tale of our two heros will prompt other great and noble Guardians to scribe their own legends!

Ristor Nobleheart
