Welcome to the Guardians of Valour website! We hope you have learned enough about our guild to be excited about joining us. We are currently recruiting active, main, Planar capabale (46+) characters from ALL classes.

If you are interested in joining GoV, please read through our Charter and our Raiding Guidelines. All Guardians are required to register their character information on the Member List within 2 weeks of joining or will default their membership.

If you would like to continue with the membership process, you need to post in the Applications for Membership area of the forums indicating your character name, level, class, race, your GoV sponsor (if any), previous guild membership and your acceptance of the Charter policy to be considered for membership into GoV.

A reply will be posted to your application outlining the process again. A thread will be created in the Members Only section of the guild to notify members of your application. If there are no objections within 2 days, an invite for probationary membership will be given by an officer of the GoV.

If you have any questions regarding the guild or guild membership please contact our Recruiting Officer, Judagirl Desade BY EMAIL.
