Member Conduct

  1. Guardians will NOT engage in kill stealing (KS'ing). If there is a dispute over a camp, please try to conduct yourself with honour and work it out. If necessary, please use the /REPORT command or turn /LOG ON to record the event in case a GM needs to be called.
  2. Guardians will not use the /ANON feature as that does not show the Guild tag. Instead, please use the /ROLE feature.
  3. Guardians will not engage in duelling out of anger or revenge. All duelling shall take place in a PvP area such as the Arena.
  4. Guardians will not beg for items or PP in any zone. If you have need of some item or PP, please contact your guildmates or the Guild Treasurer.
  5. Guardians will abide by all posted SONY/Verant EQ rules.
  6. Guardians are encouraged to refrain from vulgar and profane language in guild chat. There are rare occasions when such language is appropriate, however, members are encouraged to keep such occurrences to a minimum.
  7. Guardians should always be courteous and respectful of other guild members. Whining or flaming of other guild members in guild chat or on the message boards will not be tolerated. If you have a concern, please state it in a courteous and constructive manner in /TELL first to the guild member involved and then to an officer if necessary. If you post a courteous proposal on the boards, the officers will bend over backwards to make things right for you. Please read the Dispute Resolution policy for further information.
  8. Disciplinary action will normally consist of one or more warnings before a player is removed from the guild. However, guild removal may or may not be preceded by warnings if the infraction(s) is(are) serious enough to warrant immediate guild removal. Guild removal shall be preceded by a statement from the removing Officer stating the reasons for removal.
  9. During the probationary period (two (2) weeks), a member may be guildremoved without notice.

Member Responsibilities

  1. Guardians must create a login name and password for the site. This will give the member access to the Member List section where they must create and keep updated, a Member Profile. Members have TWO (2) weeks from invitation to complete the profile or forfeit the invitation.
  2. Guardians should be skilled EverQuest players that know how to play their class: eg Tanks know how to MA; clerics know how to buff, heal, and manage mana; Mages know how to control their pets, etc.
  3. Guardians should know how to find, research and post information about quests, dungeons, equipment and boss mobs on their own.
  4. Guardians should be able to find their way around Norrath. eg If there is a Chardok raid, it is expected that people will be able to get to Chardok on their own.
  5. Guardians should be able to assemble small groups on their own initiative to achieve their mid-level goals in-game. The guild will not be organizing official raids and having the entire guild show up to take out mobs that only require one or two groups.
  6. Guardians should render aid to other guild members where possible and or necessary. If someone needs help killing a quest mob and they do not have enough people, go help. If someone needs a rez and they cannot get their corpse or find a cleric after making an honest effort to do so on their own, go help.
  7. Guardians should respect the game time and personal goals of their guildmates. While every member should try to help out when and where necessary, members should not expect every officer, cleric, porter, etc. to be available for every event or crisis.
  8. Guardians should understand that guild goals supercede individual goals. Individuals should be willing to give up an armor drop to an unarmored guildmate, let the person farthest along their epic quest have the epic drop, or reschedule their planned raid to kill a rare spawn.

Dispute Resolution

1. Disputes between members:

Disputes between members need to be referred to an officer if they cannot be dealt with in a civil, mature manner. Your first course of action should be to try to work it out with the person via /tell or email. If that does not work, refer the problem to an officer to mediate the situation. Any of the officers are perfectly willing and capable of handling disputes between members. You may be asked to drop into a chat room to discuss the situation privately. This is to avoid involving the entire guild in what is most likely a private dispute. Private arguments over guildchat or in the forums distract others from enjoying their gaming experience and will not be tolerated. That being said, no dispute should be allowed to fester nor should a member feel they are unable to post on the boards. Find some way to work it out. We want every member of the guild to be a happy and productive member of Guardians of Valour.

2. Disputes with someone outside the guild:

Generally, the same rules apply as with intraguild disputes. If you cannot resolve the problem by yourself, the officers are more than happy to work it out with the other player's guild leader. Please feel free to let an officer mediate your dispute with another player. We want GoV to be presented in the most civil manner possible. Again, just be sure to let an officer know. Officers will gladly answer /tells or emails regarding issues with other guilds or its members.


Recruiting Policy

With the goal of having an eventual elite fighting force where all members can participate in raiding a plane or killing a mob, the following level restrictions will be placed on new invitees. Those who are already members are grandfathered, and will not be guildremoved for failing to meet the level requirement.

As of April 1st, 2002, the level requirement is 40.
As of May 1st, 2002, the level requirement is 46.

Alternate characters must have achieved level 20.

  1. Membership in the Guardians of Valour is by CHARACTER only, it does not preclude ALT characters joining other guilds in Norrath.
  2. All candidates shall have read and agreed to comply with the entire Charter before applying by posting their application in the proper forum or by email to Judagirl.
  3. All candidates shall have played with a Guardian for at least one week prior to invitation. All Invitations are given with a TWO (2) week trial period. During the probationary period (two (2) weeks), a member may be guildremoved without notice.
  4. All candidates shall email the Recruiting Officer an application to start the process.
  5. Joining members are subject to the approval of the officers and guild at large.
  6. If you have a potential Guardian or question on the process, please contact Judagirl Desade, our recruiting officer.
  7. Exceptions may be made to the enrollment limit upon the approval of the Council.
  8. No discrimination will be made regarding a character's race or gender in EQ or RL. Guild recruiting restrictions based on class are based on numbers of current players in each class.

Loot Distribution

  1. Unless an item has been posted as reserved in the raid announcement, it will be randomed by class. It is our hope that a need before greed philosophy will be upheld by the winner, and if the drop is not an upgrade to your current gear, that you will pass it down to the next person.
  2. It is our policy to reserve a small number of drops for guild members who have contributed greatly to past raids, but have not won drops with random. These reserved items will be posted with the raid announcement.
  3. Epic drops are awarded via our epic drop policy, please refer to that section for info.
  4. On small raids, items will be randomed and looted immediately. On larger raids, no-drop items will be randomed and looted immediately, gems and dropable items will be randomed at the end of the raid. Once you win an item, you will not be eligible to win another item that same raid until everyone else has won an item or has passed on the roll.
  5. The guild will reserve a quanity of sellable items and/or gems from the raids to reimburse gem/compenent costs from casters.

The Loot Policy is subject to change as deemed necessary by the guild and its officers.
Please continue to read this page to keep current on GoV's loot policy.


Raid Policy

Attitude

We raid for fun, camaraderie, and for a sense of accomplishment at conquering challenging dungeons and monsters. Do not expect to get any experience on a raid. Do not expect to win any rolls for loot on a raid. Expect to get killed many times. Expect to sit around a lot waiting for group assignments, buffs, or rezzes.

Listen carefully to your raid leader and your group leader. Follow all instructions from these individuals immediately. Respond to tells for buffs or assistance immediately. Stick with your raiding party, do not run ahead, fall behind, or go wandering off. Do not complain or be disruptive during the raid.

If you go into a raid with the right attitude, work as a member of a team, and have the patience to endure rough spots, you may be pleasantly surprised at the things that can be accomplished with a good raiding party.

Preparation

Guild raiders and guest raiders should sign up for the raid in the raid forum. This allows raid planners to gauge the strength of the raiding party, and saves time with group assignments at the beginning of the raid. Indicate class and level in your signup.

All raiders should familiarize themselves with the zone to be raided. People are expected to have either personal knowledge of the route, or maps to and from the raid zone in, as well as a map of the raid zone. Maps can be obtained from EQAtlas.com (lowbie zones) or EQMaps.com (most other zones).

Casters who require spell components (clerics, chanters, shammies, etc) should come with one stack of their required components. Other classes are encouraged to bring gems (jaspers for lower level raids and peridots for higher level raids) in case any casters forgot to stock up. Gem costs will be reimbursed at the end of the raid.

Timely Arrival and Departure

The guild will generally make ports available to members 15 minutes before the raid start time. Just go to the nearest druid or wizard ports 15 mins before the raid and we will pick you up. Guest raiders are welcome to use guild porters where appropriate.

Please show up for the raid on time, at the designated assembly point. Porters will not be leaving the raid to pick up latecomers. We will not be re-arranging assigned groups to accommodate latecomers. Raiders will not be leaving their groups to escort latecomers from the raid zone-in to the camp.

As a rule of thumb, you should be prepared to stay at least two hours at a low level raid, four hours at a high level raid, and six hours on a planes / epic raid. Showing up for a high level raid and leaving early unexpectedly is discouraged, as it may significantly disrupt the raiding party (particularly if you are a key cleric or enchanter).

Raid Conduct

Raid leaders will be using the /SHOUT channel and group leaders will be using the /OOC channel for communication during the raid. As such please refrain from using these channels unless necessary. The use of emotes, spam, or other distractions should be kept to a minimum to all for effective communication. All requests for haste, clarity or buffs should be sent in /TELL to the appropriate person. Group leaders will use the /OOC channel to call out LDs, dropped loot, or group readiness. If you send a /TELL to someone and do not get a response, please try again! Please remember that at times there is alot of battle spam going on and single lines can easily be missed.

For most raids, it is advised that you

  1. turn /showspelleffects off
  2. turn socials off
  3. turn Luclin models off
  4. /hidecorpse group
  5. /showname off (or at least /showname 1)
  6. turn other misses/my misses/other hits/my hits/mob hits OFF
  7. /serverfilter ON

Last, but no means least, NEVER EVER EVER train the camp! Make a hotkey that has the following 2 lines:

        • /loc
        • /g Stopping and Dying. I have my LOC
Recovery of a single corpse is fair easier than recovery of the entire raid party. Be kind to your fellow raiders!

Epic Policy

We assign epic drops according to the procedure outlined in our Epics section of the forums. Each guild member working on an epic must create a post describing their epic progress in this section of the web site. Drops will be awarded in the following order of priority.

  1. Who has posted their epic progress in a readable form (used a template).
  2. Who has been with the guild for more than 2 months.
  3. Who has the most raw number of "dones" posted in slots that can't be easily soloed.
  4. Who is level 51+ (the level required to recieve your epic).
  5. Then we will random if everyone is still tied.

A master list of what spot in line you are for epic drops will be maintained in the Epics section of our forums. Each player can only have one character in the epic progression at any given time. Alts will be put at bottom of the priority list. Questions or disputes regarding your spot in line must be posted 24 hours in advance of the drop to allow time for verification. It is each member's responsibility to verify that their spot in line is correct, as drops will be awarded based solely on this list.

As new members join the guild, they will be assigned a priority number below those already assigned, until the end of the 2 month period. After 2 months, priority will be assigned according to the above guidelines.


Guild Goals
Long Term Goals

  1. Explore every zone, kill every boss mob, and get all the best armor and gear that is reasonably possible with a small number (24 - 30) of casual players.
  2. Provide an environment where Guardians can achieve all their questing, leveling, epic questing, and personal goals by having well-defined loot, raid, conflict, and epic policies in place and understood.
  3. Do all of that with great team of high quality, self-motivated people who are fun to play with, know their stuff, are there to help when you need them, and have a positive attitude.

Guild Activities

  1. We spend 4 nights a week, typically Sun-Wed, pursuing XP and other personal goals. A key componant of the guilds long-term success will be to build a foundation of skilled higher level members that can prevail in most zones against most mobs.
  2. We spend two nights a week gearing up. Another key component of the guild’s long term success will be to get the best armor, weapons and equipment for our members. Core equipment raids are Kael, Growth, Hate, Fear.
  3. We spend one night a week doing epic mobs. A key goal of the guild is to equip members with epics as quickly as possible.
