1.0 Code of Ethics
1.1 The Guardians of Valour is a guild built to help in the enjoyment of the game. The guild is not here to diminish the experience of the game by giving quick and easy solutions.
1.2 Officers of the guild are guides, as they are the older members of the guild with some experience, and as such they will answers questions and lend aid as needed. However, first and foremost, officers are PLAYERS. They are not here to finish your quest, provide loans, leap over tall temples, slay your dragons. But if asked politely and given a choice of date and time, most will help you gladly and kill that dragon with you!

2.0 Member Conduct
2.1 Guardians will NOT engage in kill stealing (KS'ing). If there is a dispute over a camp, please try to conduct yourself with honour and work it out. If necessary, please use the /REPORT command or turn /LOG ON to record the event in case a GM needs to be called.
2.2 Guardians will not use the /ANON feature as that does not show the Guild tag. Instead, please use the /ROLE eature.
2.3 Guardians will not engage in duelling out of anger or revenge. All duelling shall take place in a PvP area such as the Arena.
2.4 Guardians will not beg for items or PP in any zone. If you have need of some item or PP, please contact your guildmates or the Guild Treasurer.
2.5 Guardians will abide by all posted SONY/Verant EQ rules.
2.6 Guardians are encouraged to refrain from vulgar and profane language in guild chat. There are rare occasions when such language is appropriate, however, members are encouraged to keep such occurrences to a minimum.
2.7 Disciplinary action will normally consist of one or more warnings before a player is removed from the guild. However, guild removal may or may not be preceded by warnings if the infraction(s) is(are) serious enough to warrant immediate guild removal. Guild removal shall be preceded by a statement from the removing Officer stating the reasons for removal.
2.8 During the probationary period (two (2) weeks), a member may be guildremoved without notice.

3.0 Member Responsibilities
3.1 Guardians must create a login name and password for the site. This will give the member access to the Member List section where they must create and keep updated, a Member Profile. Members have TWO (2) weeks from invitation to complete the profile or forfeit the invitation.
3.2 This website is PUBLIC RESTRICTED. Anyone can look at the site but may not post or alter the site unless they join. Guardians may not alter posted items or messages.

4.0 Membership Requirements
4.1 Membership in the Guardians of Valour is by CHARACTER only, it does not preclude ALT characters joining other guilds in Norrath.
4.2 Guardians of Valour is currently in LIMITED enrollment for main characters of levels 46+ from the following Classes: Shadow Knight, Rogue, Necromancer, Wizard, Enchanter, Beastlord.
4.3 All candidates shall have read and agreed to comply with these Code of Ethics as well as the Raiding Policy before apllying.
4.4 All candidates shall have played with a Guardian for at least one week prior to invitation. All Invitations are given with a TWO (2) week trial period.
4.5 All candidates shall post an application on the forums to start the process.
4.6 Joining members are subject to the approval of the officers and guild at large.
4.6 If you have a potential Guardian or question on the process, please contact Judagirl Desade, our recruiting officer.
4.7 Exceptions may be made to the enrollment limit upon the approval of the Council.
4.8 No discrimination will be made regarding a character's race or gender in EQ or RL. Guild recruiting restrictions based on class are based on numbers of current players in each class.
