Member Responsibilities

  1. Guardians must create a login name and password for the site. This will give the member access to the Member List section where they must create and keep updated, a Member Profile. Members have TWO (2) weeks from invitation to complete the profile or forfeit the invitation.
  2. Guardians should be skilled EverQuest players that know how to play their class: eg Tanks know how to MA; clerics know how to buff, heal, and manage mana; Mages know how to control their pets, etc.
  3. Guardians should know how to find, research and post information about quests, dungeons, equipment and boss mobs on their own.
  4. Guardians should be able to find their way around Norrath. eg If there is a Chardok raid, it is expected that people will be able to get to Chardok on their own.
  5. Guardians should be able to assemble small groups on their own initiative to achieve their mid-level goals in-game. The guild will not be organizing official raids and having the entire guild show up to take out mobs that only require one or two groups.
  6. Guardians should render aid to other guild members where possible and or necessary. If someone needs help killing a quest mob and they do not have enough people, go help. If someone needs a rez and they cannot get their corpse or find a cleric after making an honest effort to do so on their own, go help.
  7. Guardians should respect the game time and personal goals of their guildmates. While every member should try to help out when and where necessary, members should not expect every officer, cleric, porter, etc. to be available for every event or crisis.
  8. Guardians should understand that guild goals supercede individual goals. Individuals should be willing to give up an armor drop to an unarmored guildmate, let the person farthest along their epic quest have the epic drop, or reschedule their planned raid to kill a rare spawn.
