Norwest Associates is a full featured web design and hosting firm with nearly 2 decades of experience serving clients' computer related needs right here in Pierce County, Washington. We opened our doors in 1991 with Nexus Software; an enterprise specializing in application development for small business owners. 

By 1994, we recognized a need to expand and created Two Guys From Tacoma Computers to handle projects for a financial educator who needed exposure on the Internet using the latest multimedia resources and encryption techniques for his 3000+ students. In addition, we served as a low cost alternative for computer owners by offering high quality computer repair and upgrades without emptying their pocketbook. 

By 1998, our company grew to include out-of-state clients and businesses with more than one satellite office. This prompted us to take the name Norwest Associates.

Today, Norwest Associates carefully plans each project one-on-one with our clients. We pride ourselves on detail and quality. We offer visual appeal and effective "information architecture." The secret to a successful web site is more than just looks... it must be easy to use.
