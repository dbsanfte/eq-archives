Billing Policies
1. Account Payment


1a. Company Check or Cashiers Check

Norwest Associates, accepts payments by personal check, company check, and money order. All funds must be payable in US dollars. Norwest Associates does not accept international checks in foreign currencies.


1b. Returned Check Fee

In the event that your check is returned by the bank, a service fee of $25.00 will be accessed against your account. Your service will be immediately suspended and the customer will be sent a email outline procedures to remedy the suspension. If within thirty (30) days the customer is unable to pay all services fees, including a reactivation fee of $150.00 and late fees, customer's account will be turned over to collection, and interest will begin to accruing at the rate indicated in the hosting contract. After sixty (60) days delinquent, Norwest Associates reserves the right to dismantle client's server, including the removal of all HTML pages, graphics, scripts, and data. This policy is non-negotiable. Recurring charges will continue despite any suspension.


1c. Credit Cards

Although Norwest Associates does process credit cards for payment of its services, it is customary for Norwest Associates to work with clients one-on-one. We prefer payment by check, cashier check or money orders for any contractual or web design invoices. Any monthly fees or reoccurring fees will be handled by credit card for accounting purposes.  Please talk to your account associate for more details.



2. Extra Charges


2a. Maintenance Fees

Client agrees to pay all preauthorized maintenance fees as per the client's hosting contract. Norwest Associates will provide the client with an estimate prior to any work that is not covered under the maintenance agreement which is listed in the hosting contract between Norwest Associates and client. If service is performed outside normal business hours this is not covered under the maintenance contract unless the client opt for on-call service. Failure to pay all fees, charges and invoices per your hosting agreement, please refer to Account Payment listed above. This policy is non-negotiable. Recurring charges will continue despite any suspension.


2b. Excess Bandwidth

Each account is allowed a predetermined amount of bandwidth. Bandwidth is the measured amount of data that is transmitted or received to the server. Norwest Associates affords client the ability to shutdown bandwidth upon reaching the amount of bandwidth allotted. Additional bandwidth is billed per the contract agreed upon. Please refer to the hosting contract for more information about bandwidth.


2c. Server Upgrades / Service Changes

Norwest Associates monitors servers on a regular basis. If it is determined that your account is exceeding the limitations of a shared account on a virtual server plan, you will be notified by email about the possibility of an upgrade. If the client does not wish to upgrade, the client has 24 hours to comply to server requirements. Please refer to our Quality Assurance for Service for more information. Client may pay for server upgrades or service changes by check or money order. Such payment must be received in advance.

3. Late/Non-Payment

3a. Accounts with Non-Usable Credit Cards

Credit cards on file are not usable when any of the following occur:

The card has expired;
The card declines the charges;
The card is reported lost or stolen;
The card is declared invalid.

Norwest Associates charges credit cards seven (10) days in advance of the new billing term. If a credit card payment fails, the customer will be sent an e-mail notice allowing seven (10) days to rectify the situation either with a new payment method or by updating the credit card. Client will be afforded the opportunity to correct the situation at anytime during the seven (10) days. If the client fails to correct the situation during this time, Norwest Associates will reattempt the charge on the first day of the new billing cycle. If the card still does not accept our charge and no other payment has been received, the account will be suspended without exception. The customer will have the option of reactivating the account after paying the full amount of the invoice due. An additional fee of $150.00 will be assessed for reactivation. The reactivation fee is non-negotiable. Upon suspension of the account, Norwest Associates, will send an e-mail deactivation notice to customer. All accounts more than thirty (30) days past due will be charged interest from the due date of the lesser of (i) 2% per month on the past due amount; or (ii) the highest legal rate of interest. Accounts past due more than thirty (30) days will be turned over for collections.


3b. Late Check/Non-Payment

If payment has not been received prior to the renewal date of an account, the account will be suspended on the first day of the new billing cycle. Upon suspension, Norwest Associates, will send an e-mail suspension notice to customer. The customer will have the option of reactivating the account after paying the full amount of the invoice due. An additional fee of $150.00 will be assessed for reactivation. All accounts more than thirty (30) days past due will be charged interest from the due date of the lesser of (i) 2% per month on the past due amount; or (ii) the highest legal rate of interest. Accounts past due more than thirty (30) days will be turned over for collections.

4. Cancellation Policy

4a. No Cancellation Allowed.

Currently there is no cancellation procedures.  Norwest Associates does not allow cancellations.  All fees and charges for hosting are paid in full in 3, 6, 12, or 24 increments.  Norwest Associates DOES NOT provide a refund for hosting services. A hosting contract signed between client and Norwest Associates explains this policy in more detail.  The client is encouraged to read the hosting contract in full; all policies concerning clients rights and procedures are within the hosting contract.

4b. Approaching Billing Cycle

Client may discontinue hosting with Norwest Associates when their current hosting contract expires.  Client must provide in writing a cancellation notice 30 days prior to their next billing cycle.  For example:  ABC Corp.  signs a hosting contract with Norwest Associates for 12 months.  They pay in full for 12 months of hosting.  On the 10 month of their hosting they receive mailed an invoice for the next billing cycle.  They have 30 days from the date of the invoice to cancel their service with Norwest Associates.  Otherwise they are automatically renewed for another 12 months and Norwest Associates would expect full payment of invoice.

4c. Change Hosting Service

Clients have the right to change their hosting service with Norwest Associates per the hosting contract.  Please refer to the hosting contract or contact your account representative for more information.

5. Refund Policy

5a. Refunds within the First 30 Days of Service

If an account is cancelled within the first thirty (30) days of service, the customer will receive a refund of all monthly fees less one (1) month. Set-up fees will not be refunded for dedicated servers only.

Refunds will be credited to the credit card on file for credit card paying accounts. Accounts paid by check or money order will receive a check via postal mail within sixty (60) days of cancellation.


5b. No Refunds Outside of the First 30 Days

Norwest Associates, does not offer refunds for services after the initial thirty (30) days of service.
