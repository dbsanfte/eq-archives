 Application for membership in Stone Guard

Enter your information below to apply for membership in Stone Guard. We will contact you within 72 hours with our response by email. To expedite the process, contact Paldarien, Syri, Syke, Badwood, Zelious, Sorcera, Elphame or Barrymore in game once you have submitted your application. If you happen to join another guild, please contact us by e-mail to withdraw your application.


Already a member of Stone Guard, click here.

Membership Requirements:

  • We are looking for players level 52+ with good raid skills and whom work well with others.
  • The following classes are frozen as of May 20, 2001: Druids, Paladins, Necromancers and Warriors
  • We do not require that you own a Sebilis, Howling Stones, or Veeshan's Peak key to join the guild. We do encourage you to acquire them as soon as possible and use the guild to help you.
  • You must have keys to the Velious portals (Great Divide is absolutely required - we can assist in obtaining the Wakening Lands and Cobalt Scar keys).
  • You are responsible for obtaining resist gear. Our guild jewelers can create resist gear at a fair price. The guild will cover 25% of the cost of resist gear, once you have been a member for 90 days. An unbuffed magic resistance of 100 minimum linked with any other resist at 100 is desired.

We have a few expectations from members:

  • By joining Stone Guard, you are making a commitment to participate in activities to accomplish the guild's goals.
  • Our members are high quality people who embrace honor, integrity, and fun over levels and loot.
  • Participation on the web site is essential. A valid e-mail address is required and should be checked daily to keep up with news and event notices. As a member you will receive access to the guild calendar, roster, schedule, and forums. It is recommended to visit the web site forums daily -- a separate registration for the forums is REQUIRED.
  • Learn the raid policies -- members are required to follow them. We have rules to make raids run smoothly and increase enjoyment. Continual infractions against raid policies will not be tolerated.
  • Learn the loot rules --  as you will be required to work with the guild and members using these rules.
  • Show up early for events, no matter where we are raiding. We do not require 100% attendance, but continual absence or avoidance of certain zones will not be tolerated.
    Pull your own weight. This means something different for everyone. We intend its definition to include all potential interpretations.
  • Experience is your responsibility. We have experience parties from time to time, but do not count on them for advancement. We encourage members to get together and form their own experience groups. It is mandatory that you work your mains to 54+
  • If you are joining us to get your epic or some other special item, you found the wrong guild.  We work in Epics weekly and encourage the guild to help whenever possible.
  • Guild chat, the mailing list and forums will remain civilized. These lines of communication are available for guild issues and friendly discussion.  We welcome constructive criticism--and feel that if we do not know what you are feeling, we can not improve our guild with you as a member. Voice your opinion at the appropriate times (i.e. not on guild sanctioned raids, fun events etc, or in guild chat--contact an officer).
  • Do Not Train other players in Norrath.  Respect 'camps'.  Do Not Kill Steal from other players. As always, log your text if you feel you need to dispute any trades. personal tells or problems such as stolen camps, kill steals, loot problems or other disputes you may experience by any players.  Our officers pride themselves on investigating matters as they are reported.  However favorable decisions are rare unless a log is made of the incident.

If your application stands out, we will invite you to join us on a couple weekly skirmishes.  The following days are good to join us; Friday evening 6pm PT, Sunday 10am PT, Monday 6pm PT.  Saturday reserved for ALL DAY SANCTIONED RAIDS.   This allows us to evaluate you - your skills, your attitude and willingness to be part of the team.  Contact an officer to see where we are raiding.

Thank you for considering Stone Guard.
