What has Stone Guard done in the last 30 days

The majority of our members live in the Eastern and Central time zones of the United States and Canada. We maintain a timetable favorable to as many as possible. The official timetable starts at 5 pm Pacific GMT -8 (8 pm Eastern GMT -4) Monday - Friday, All Day Saturday, and 12 pm Pacific GMT -8 (3 pm Eastern GMT -4) Sunday. Unofficial skirmishes occur during the week ALL DAY.

We have two types of raids; daily skirmishes and major events. Our raids help gather items needed for quests. We believe in NEED before greed.

Skirmishes last 3 - 4 hours on weeknights and have 1-2 groups focusing on learning, gathering items, or gaining experience.

Major events last several hours and include encounters with boss mobs, epic quest npcs, and trips to the planes. In addition, expanded skirmishes push deeper into zones.

Current Raid Goals

We are working on Thurgadin quest armor, spell and gem gathering. Our skirmishes take us to the zones that drop the items needed.

Zones Visited Recently

In the last 30 days, we visited Kael Drakkel, Velketor's Lab, Icewell Keep, Skyshrine, Wakening Lands, Chardok, The Plane of Hate, The Plane of Fear, Old Sebilis, Karnor's Castle, The City of Mist and Dragon Necropolis. We make monthly raids to Sol B for Golden Efreeti Boots and other prized loot items in various dungeons.
