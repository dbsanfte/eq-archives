 Recruiting Policy

AS OF MAY 20th 2001

Class Freezes: Druids, Paladins, Necromancers and Warriors.

Any member not on probation may propose a recruit to Stone Guard.
1)   Potential members must meet criteria to become candidates
a)   Meets minimum level requirement presently 52+. Druids,  Paladins, Necromancers and Warriors not accepted at this time.
b)   Recruit’s reputation must be in-line with guild philosophy.  We check EMarr Msg Board for any indication of your reputation.
c)   Recruits must possess knowledge of their class and be able to raid effectively.  Following directions and showing  up on time for raid's they commit to.
2)   A candidate must fill out an application for membership.
a)   A candidate update is distributed to the officers.  The officers will inform the guild in chat.
b)   Guild members may respond with concerns and comments through guild chat. There will be no need to respond in forums as those who are online at the time will be consider the majority.  Normally between 6 pm PT to 12 am PT weekdays.
c)   Barring conflict, the candidate will be moved to probationary membership.
3)   Probationary Membership
a)   The probationary period lasts four weeks for individual invites.  If you come from a guild with more than four (8) people (including your guild leader) the probation period does not apply.  However your ex-guild leader will be appointed as an officer and at their discretion may remove you from this guild without notice during this merger trial.
b)   Receives probationary access to the web site. Which includes access to all areas except posting events on the calendar or news.
i)    Must read and acknowledge all of the guild policies.
ii)   Must participate on message board on a regular basis.
iii)  Must post a schedule and update their roster.
c)   Eligible for most raids, although not for item loot, except as specified in raid policy.
d)   Officers vote on membership status upon completion of probation period.
i)   Candidates may be rejected for acting contrary to guild policy.
ii)   Rejected candidates are ineligible for future candidacy and restricted from future raids.
4)  Full Membership
a)   Receives full member access to the web site.
b)   Eligible for all raids and item loot.
