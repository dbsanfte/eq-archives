The Diary of an Aenjel

My Path Chosen

 

I am the daughter of my father, a fearless Dwarf they called Edeno.  Along with other courageous warriors, he traveled the lands of Norrath helping to ward off the evil which is always lurking in the shadows.  He swore on my mother's tomb that he would always fight against the evil ones - those who took her from him, whoever and wherever they might be.  He traveled with many; large barbarians from the cold lands, the nimble elves that make their homes in the trees, and even the few humans that found an uncommon strength outside of their race to face the evil ones.

 

My father hoped that I would follow in his footsteps.  He trained me to use a weapon, to spot the evil ones, and to use all my strength and will to disperse them.  I was with him during his last battle...against those gruesome undead creatures buried in the hills just away from the human city of Freeport.  Unfortunately, I was unable to save him, and he died saying the words I shall forever live by:

 

"Lejnea, it is now to you I pass my legacy.  The evil ones must be destroyed.  Look around me, and you shall see many brave ones who will give their lives for this purpose.  There is no lack of might.  But had I near me one who could mend my wounds, I might have survived this day to fight one more battle.  You shall now be known as Aenjel, Lejnea in reverse, for you shall do the opposite of what I have done – and that which I have trained you to do.  I no longer can cause pain and suffering; neither will you.  Aenjel will, from this day forward, heal and mend, where have I cut and wounded.  She shall save lives, where I ended them.   She will dedicate her stay in this world saving those dedicated to saving Norrath.  She shall be the savior of the saviors.  Aenjel Ofmercy you shall be from this day forward."

 

After we buried my father in the tomb below the earth next to my mother, I sought out the council of those knowledged in the gifts of healing.  I studied well and began my work in the city of Kaladim.  I nursed the injured soldiers that fought to protect Kaladim from the pests that continually attempted to invade her from ButcherBlock.  As I grew in my abilities, I ventured out of Kaladim into the mountains of ButcherBlock, searching out those who needed my touch. From there, I traversed the many lands of Norrath, aiding wherever and however I could any of the great warriors, shadownights, monks, and, yes, even those that performed their protective services by way of the mystical gifts of magic.

 

When I had reached my 28th year in the services of those who battle, I focused my studies on the  art of reviving, a skill I wish I had the day my brave father left me.  It is for him that I continue to work, to study, and to travel, aiding those who carry on the great deeds of Edeno, the great Dwarf warrior.

The Diary of an Aenjel

 

Entry #1:

Being bored with the hills of ButcherBlock, I have decided to once again venture out farther from home.  I have heard rumors that there is a need for healers in the areas surrounding the human city of Freeport.  I’ve packed my backpack with food and water, the necessary equipment to perform my duties, said my goodbyes and am heading for the docks to board the ship to Freeport.  The adrenaline is pumping through my body, as I sit on the pier waiting.  Have I made the correct choice?  Am I prepared for what I shall encounter? 

My teacher, Princess Ghalea, has found a cleric’s training center in North Freeport and has given me a scroll of introduction, which I am to present to the master cleric there upon my arrival.  My curiosity is getting the better of me, however, as I try to not open and read it.  I wonder what the Princess has said inside.  She didn’t tell me I couldn’t read it.  I’d sure like to know what is written there…  Perhaps it will provide  me with some valuable insight… It could provide quite useful…

 

 
To:     Tholius Quey

 

“Before you stands Aenjel Ofmercy, follower of Brell Serelis and student of the art of healing.   She is a quick learner, works hard, and has proven herself quite a capable cleric.  She tends to get lost easily, so I urge you not to allow her to lead or scout ahead of her party.  She also is quite chatty, but never allows that to interfere with her clerical duties. 

 

Aenjel loves all who love goodness and righteousness.  She is not adverse to any race or class of being, as long as they share her goal of ridding this world of the evil ones.  I only hope that her trust of all will not endanger her or her fellow party members.  Often times, I have found, beings will misrepresent themselves to infiltrate their enemy’s camp…well, you know all about these things.

 

If I can be of further service, please send word.  I pray you find Aenjel of good service to you and those in your care.

                                               

          In the pursuit of goodness,

                                                          Princess  Ghalea

 

 

Chatty?  Easily lost?  Wow, I never really thought about those things…
