Joining The Lost Cabal

 

    Before joining The Lost Cabal, there are a few things you must know and do to be considered for membership.

 

    We are not an uber guild. We don't expect our members to spend their time power-levelling anyone or getting them "ph4t l3wt". If this is your reason for joining a guild, we are not for you.

    We are not bullies. We will not back up a member if they are being a bully to others, nor will we participate in vendettas for our members. Personal disputes are just that, personal.

    We ARE a supportive and nurturing group, we will help you whenever we are able and it is within our power.

    We ARE a family, and as such sometimes have our disputes, but we expect our members to behave as adults and solve their differences without resorting to childish behavior.

    We get together every weekend (or try to at least) to work on quests, get experience, or just chat. Every member will get a turn to pick what they'd like to do on a weekend. This weekend is determined alphabetically by the name of their main toon.  This can be anything the member likes, from power-levelling, quest item gathering, or a language exchange.  Anyone who wishes to have their time to choose should participate in other members' choices. We do take note of who doesn't participate, and these members won't get a turn. (So far, everyone has come when they could. =) )

 

    Now that we got all that out of the way, there is a process to become a member.

 

First: Find a sponsor that is already a member of the guild. This sponsor will talk to me about your possible membership, and should refer you to this page.  Please don't spam the officers with tells about wanting to become a member.

Second: When you find yourself a sponsor, click HERE and fill out the short form. It contains some basic questions that will help us get to know you a little better.

Third: Join us on one of our weekends. This will be the deciding factor. After you've joined us for one or more weekends, the guild as a whole will vote on your membership. A single no vote will stop the process, but you can try again if you so desire.

Fourth: After the voting has come up with allowance for your membership, you will need to email Aerich HERE and provide your main toon's name, race, class and level so it can be added to the memberlist as well as an email address you check regularly to be added to the guild mailing list.

 

And finally, when all of the above is done, you will receive a warm and hearty Lost Cabal welcome to the guild (hope you have a high alcohol tolerance) and will become a member of the finest family on Norrath.

 

    We look forward to hearing from you soon.
