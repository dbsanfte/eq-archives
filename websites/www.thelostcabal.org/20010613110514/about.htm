The Lost Cabal

The Beginning

 

 

    The real beginnings of The Lost Cabal can be traced back to two people joined in a mutual desire to form a guild that could, through its members, make Norrath a fun place to visit.  These two dreamers were lucky enough to find friends who shared that quest.  Good people. People you can trust to guard your back in a fight or to share their last rations during a lengthy adventure.

 

    As The Lost Cabal, we try to remember and encourage people to remember the effect they can have on other Norrathians.  We have few guidelines and regulations. Play nice and help others about encompass the restrictions on our members.  We try to be helpful to any in need, even those outside the guild.
