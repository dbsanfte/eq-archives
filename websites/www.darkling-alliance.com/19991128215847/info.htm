Guild name:  Scabrous Raiders
Scabrous:  scaly, rough surface, indecent
Raiders:  one who makes a sudden assault or attack
Guild motto:  Hic et Ubique - Here and Everywhere
Guild Server:  Cazic-thule

 
Charter
Our Guild is based on the savage practices of the Old World Vikings. Bloodthirsty warriors who not only plundered the coasts, but also explored more than they were given credit to. They took honor in death, and death in battle was glorious. To die in defense of a fellow Raider is even more of an honor. We are a Neutral Guild, we take care of our own and those who do not respect our presence will be dealt with in whatever way we chose. We may be kind one day and leave you to rot another if you are not a Raider. We do not run from enemies, we give nor recieve quarter in combat. We will travel throughout all the Lands of Norrath, as the Vikings did. We will be feared or welcomed to the lands we travel to. When the Scabrous Raiders are on the move a watchful eye must be kept.

 

Laws
Raiders stick together, they never ditch and run as cowards.

Raiders are allies of the the sisters of Elg'Caressen and will support them whenever needed.

Raiders do not whimper, if you have a problem you come out with it.

No harsh cussing or abusiveness on Guildspeak or to Raiders who do not want to hear it.

Raiders will stand up for themselves and know they have the backing of the guild if their cause is right.

Raiders are not dirty players, unless first blood is drawn against us, then No Holds Barred tactics will be used against our enemies.

Play to have fun, that is why the Raiders are here, well that and to get really big toys and crush our enemies, but that is fun as well.

 

Rankings
Leader:  Holds final say over all Guild dealings, also the Guild Treasurer.
Warbosses:  Mentors and seconds to the Leader.
Advisors:  Guides and have direct contact to the Leader and Warbosses.
Ambasadors:  Members of Elg'Caressen within our guild.
Raiders:  The backbone of the Guild. The Fighters, Casters and Healers who keep the Raiders growing and strong.

Style and Role-play of the
Elg'Caressen Guild

Elg'Caressen is Drow or dark elf for female dogs swear word or Bitches. This guild is in basic terms and for all intents and purposes an evil role-play guild comprised of all female characters. We do accept light races into our numbers, on the condition that they understand it is evil role-play. We have liberal attitudes towards kill stealing and other negative but controversial issues within Norrath. We enjoy parties and celebrations, and believe in having fun while leveling. This includes such things as dueling, drinking and general debauchery. Some among are number are very industrious, others are not. The guild does not pressure its members to rise in levels, except to the point of lvl 10, an adequate expectation in order for members to attend guild events such as raids. It has been noted by other guilds that we are sometimes overly helpful and loyal to our allies and friends, to the point of dying for them. This is our way, and the guild will stand by any sister who can reasonably justify her actions. Something else we are known for is our intense desire to group with each other and fight together. Sisters often prefer to group with other sisters, and the guild admires and supports and encourages such action. There is an ultimate plan behind the creation of the guild, but that purpose remains hidden, for the ears of those that need to know. In every day play, Elg'Caressen is a format designed to help sisters play with sisters, and play evilly. All Hail Elg'Caressen!!!
 

House Factions

Within the guild there are many Officers, in order to help the guild grow faster. We have a focus on numbers, believing that all are possible guild material, and that while the curds sink to the bottom, the cream will rise to the top. In order to better deal with the ever-increasing numbers, each Officer is in charge of a faction called a House. Each House has a different name. Permanent Houses in the guild are the Temple of the Moon, and the House of Doom. Each recruit that is invited into the guild becomes a member of the House belonging to the inviting Officer. Thus, it is easy to see by inspection who belongs to what House. Each Officer is in a minor way responsible for the members of her House, though the guild continues to support all Houses and all members.

NOTE: It is important to note the role of males within the Elg'Caressen guild. We are not men haters, as some would have us portrayed as. We are merely following one aspect of traditional Drow role-play, that being the rule of female over male. To compensate our sisters and their male friends and counterparts, it is possible for Officers to have males within their Houses. That is to say, males are allowed to carry the House name in their profile. However, they do not get a guild tag, nor do they get guild privileges such as guild speak or opinions therein. Most of these males have taken up a guard position, escorting and partying with sisters at their leisure. At some point in the future, they may be formed into a guild that brothers our own. As yet, they remain loyal to our cause and support our own goals.
 

Role-play
It is the guild's desire to role-play traditional Drow society. However, such role-play is difficult to achieve and so the guild struggles on. There is a downloadable Drow dictionary available at www.cazic-thule.com that can assist any sister with learning basic Drow words and etiquette. Such information is not necessary, but is a wish of the guild's. Because we profess an evil role-play base, there is much we are able to do within Norrath that others may shy from. Indulge yourself, have fun, and the guild will support its sisters.
