Emyr

Emyr Ambermist :

I was born on the lush green island of Faydewr, in the tree city of Kelethin. My mother, Zelli Starsfire, is a spell merchant in the druid guild, although she does not train prospective druids, she does know the ways of the Soilders of Tunare,the druid guild in Kelethin. My father, the more aggressive of the two, is a guard of the city, known as Guard Sunweaver. He opted to remain close to Kelethin after I was born and protect the home front. I myself was always ready for a good rough-houseing session as a young elf and when it came time to train in a class, I chose to be a ranger. I began my training with Ran Sunfire in Faydarks Champions, which is the ranger guild in Kelethin. After I became skilled enough with the bow, meaning I didnt accidently shoot into huts anymore, I bid my parents fare well and got on the next boat to Antonica. Although I am still learning much of what it means to be a ranger I am also learning what it means to serve Tunare. Tunare the mother of all guides and protects me on my journeys, but all can not be left up to her.. after all.. as the name suggests.. she is quite busy. I was fortunate to meet Balis, leader of the guild, and join the Knights, for they are very much the type of family that I was begining to hope for after my long time away from my home land.
