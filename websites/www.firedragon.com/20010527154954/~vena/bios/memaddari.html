Memaddari

Memaddari grew up an orphan in the dismal Antonican city of Freeport, with no memory of her parents, and only the knowledge revealed by her face that one of them must have been Elven, and one human. She led a happy, if unstable, childhood within the city’s walls. As a girl she learned to do odd jobs in exchange for a night or two of food and lodging, and so grew up under the watchful eyes of the town’s merchants and residents, who came to regard her with the love and concern that one would accord a neighborhood stray. While many would find this way of life to be intolerable, Memaddari knew no other way; her natural cheerfulness allowed her to view each day as a new adventure. Every dawn heralded the promise of new friends, and every night she dreamed of a quiet, dark forest, heavy with greenery and ripe with magic, quite unlike the arid flatlands surrounding her home.

Happy and likable though she was, the fact remained that she dared to be a half-elf in a human city. Often, her human peers would tease her about her sharp Elven features. Memaddari was proud of her Elven heritage, ignorant of it though she may have been, and frequently these altercations escalated to blows. Naturally strong and agile, the girl found herself victorious quite often, and gained a reputation for being quite a skilled fighter. It was this that caught the attention of Cain Darkmoor, guild master of Freeport’s Steel Warriors; knowing talent when he saw it, he invited her to join the guild as an apprentice. When it became obvious that she displayed absolutely no talent for magic, she was officially inducted into the guild. In this way, Memaddari began her career as a warrior.

Life with the Steel Warriors grew stultifying for her, though; rather than feeling safe and secure within the walls of the guild hall, she felt confined. Cain knew that she would never be happy as long as she was required to remain in one place. As soon as he was confident that she had mastered the basics of her training, he permitted her to wander as she wished, so long as she agreed to uphold the credo of the Steel Warriors wherever she went. Memaddari, confident in her skills and eager to travel, packed up her meager belongings and caught the first boat to Faydwer, a land she had heard was the home of the Elves. Perhaps, she thought, she would meet someone who would recognize something in her violet eyes that called to mind a long-gone friend, some key to her past. She imagined that maybe her father had been a powerful magic-user, or her mother a gifted healer… During the long boat ride, she stayed curled in the hold of the ship and entertained herself by daydreaming romantic scenarios about meeting magical Elves.

When at long last she came to Faydwer, she wandered into the dwarven town, where she met a kind dwarf by the name of Bordaaf. He was a curious stout fellow on his way to Kelethin, which he said was the home of the Wood Elves. He offered to guide Memaddari there in exchange for nothing more than her cheerful company. The two trekked through the Butcherblock Mountain range together; he pointed out native flora and fauna to the wide-eyed girl, and saved her from more than one marauding goblin. She told him Antonican jokes and tales. The dwarf, a seasoned traveler, had heard them all before, but he laughed for her sake, and was genuinely entertained by the novelty she must have felt at their telling. When at last the two came through the mountain pass into the forest of Greater Faydark, Memaddari recognized at once the lush green land of her dreams. She felt that she had come home. It is here that she has remained since, occasionally returning to Antonica to visit her friends in Freeport, for she is nothing if not loyal to those she loves. She has taken up the fight against the orcs of Crushbone, and is currently harboring an unrequited crush on a high elf enchanter, who, with his beautiful features and great talent for magic, embodies her romantic ideals about Elves.
