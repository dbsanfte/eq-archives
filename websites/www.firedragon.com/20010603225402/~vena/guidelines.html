Our stance on Powerleveling:

 No hard core powerleveling, yes you can help out a friend, but there will be no all out powerleveling. Ex: Balis and Jala were at the aviak village together, when something that gave her experience spawned I would help her if she got into trouble with it. And conversly when something that gave me experience spawned she would help me out with it if I needed it. So helping is ok to a point, but a level 50 powerleveling a level 4 is a little outrageous.

Our Stance on Twinking:

 If you earned it with one character and you want another to have it, then more power to you.
