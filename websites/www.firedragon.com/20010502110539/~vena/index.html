Charter


The Gnostic Knights are a peaceful guild built on the idea of bringing Norrath together. We do not seek to make any race or class of creature an outcast unless they intend to cause harm to us or to other peaceful peoples. We pride ourselves on the caliber of our members and let it be known that we are available to help all in need. The Gnostic Knights have also come together on Norrath to begin a tradition of worldwide events and bazaars. We believe that merth and merriment that involves all the peoples of Norrath is the basis of peace and Understanding between us all.
