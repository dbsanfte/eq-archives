Chapter 5

After packing up Salia, Faloria swung herself on the beasts back and motioned Crystavia to join her. Crystavia swung up easily, as if it were second nature to her. Faloria’s eyes swept over the spot where they had stopped, making sure nothing was forgotten. Satisfied nothing had been left behind, Faloria nickered to Salia and off they went, heading east.

“We’re going to make a stop in Redridge Mountains” Faloria said “I heard a friend of mine was near there and may be able to create a portal to Ironforge, which would save us some time”. They rode in silence for awhile. Eventually Crystavia noticed some buildings to the north of the road. “What’s that over there” Crystavia asked, pointing. “That’s Eastvale Logging Camp” Faloria frowned “I didn’t know it was still active. Last I heard the loggers had abandoned it due to the Defias Brotherhood swarming in and taking over. We need to be on lookout” Faloria sighed “Defias have no concern other then their own gain. They won’t hesitate to try and attack”. Crystavia continued to look towards the logging camp “What are Defias anyway? How can I tell who’s Defias and who’s not?”

“Believe me…you’ll know a Defias when you see one” Faloria stated, matter of factly “First, all members of the Defias Brotherhood wear red bandanas across the lower part of their face. Second, they will not hesitate to rob you and they definitely won’t hesitate in trying to kill you” Faloria looked around “I think we’ll be ok as long as we keep a safe distance from the camp”

They continued on their way, alert for any danger when suddenly something came whizzing towards them and knocked the Night Elf off the horse. “Oof!” cried Crystavia as she landed on the hard ground “what in the world?” Faloria instantly jumped off Salia and stood next to Crystavia “Are you alright?” Faloria asked, her eyes surveying the land. “I...I think so” Crystavia stammered. “What was that?” She started to get up, which was when she noticed that her arm was bleeding. She looked around and saw the knife. It was wicked looking and stuck firmly in the tree behind them. Crystavia looked at her arm and saw exactly where the pain was coming from. Crystavia closed her eyes and murmured something under her breath and a peaceful green mist surrounded her, completely healing the wound.

At that moment, there was thrashing in the bushes next to the road. “Look…fresh meat” someone growled from the bush “give me all the gold you have and maybe I’ll kill you quickly”. There was laughter from several others as they came into view. “Defias!” Crystavia thought. Faloria stepped in front of the Night Elf and said in a firm voice “We are traveling, out of our way so that we may continue”. This brought laughter and derision from the four Defias standing in front of them “ho! The gnome wants us to move!” said one “oooh I’m shaking in my boots” sneered another. The four men circled Faloria and Crystavia.

Faloria leaned closer to Crystavia and whispered “keep them occupied for a moment” Crystavia nodded slightly and suddenly, became invisible. “Hey now, where’d she go?” cried one of the men. Crystavia walked right past them and spoke “here I am you twits, come and find me!” The four men turned and began stabbing the air around them. Crystavia saw them coming and deftly moved out of the way each time “is that the best you can do” she taunted. She snuck around and smacked the back of the head of one of the men and jumped aside. She laughed when she saw him turn quickly and stab at the spot where she had been. “A bit slow, aren’t we” she laughed, all the while keeping an eye on Faloria.

While Crystavia was keeping the dregs busy, Faloria closed her eyes and quickly murmured a spell under her breath. Suddenly, in a sea of blue and smoke, there stood her trusty voidwalker. He stretched lazily. Faloria pointed towards the group of men “That’s them…dispatch them quickly”. With that order, the large blue cloud moved quickly towards the men and before they knew what was happening, he was upon them, attacking. The four Defias never noticed the gnome casting spell after spell from the distance. At least they never noticed until their health was just about gone and they fell to the ground dead, their eyes still open and wondering how this had happened.

Faloria dusted off her hands and motioned to Crystavia to come closer. Crystavia, still invisible cautiously walked over. “What is that” she asked in awe “I’ve never seen a monster like that before”. “That’s not a monster…well, it is a monster but it’s my voidwalker” Faloria said “He’s been trained to serve me when spells just won’t do”. Faloria snapped her fingers and the large blue voidwalker slowly disappeared. Faloria walked over to Crystavia and took hold of her arm and looked at the spot where the wound had been “very good, you’re learning to heal quite well” Faloria said “but I still would like to have you talk with my friend Peacia. She’s a great druid and I think you’ll learn quite a bit from her”

Faloria whistled and Salia came running. The two swung up and continued down the road towards Redridge Mountains, leaving the pile of dead Defias in the road. Neither of them noticed the one defias who had remained cloaked during the exchange. “That gnome will pay for her treachery” he fumed “I must alert the others. That gnome will pay”
