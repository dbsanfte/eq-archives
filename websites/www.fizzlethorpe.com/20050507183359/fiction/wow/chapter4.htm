Chapter 4

They had been riding for hours. Crystavia, who had never ridden before, found herself stiff and sore. Faloria could feel Crystavia moving around to find a more comfortable position and decided to stop near Stonecairn Lake for a spell.

Faloria pulled back on the reins “Whoooa Salia” she said. Salia stopped near the shore of the lake and lowered her head to the water. Faloria and Crystavia slid from the back of the horse. Faloria bustled about, removing packs from Salia’s back so that the animal would be more comfortable while they rested. Crystavia, unused to riding, sprawled on the ground. “I don’t think I want to learn to ride anymore” she moaned “my backside is killing me”. Faloria laughed and threw a pack to Crystavia “You’ll change your tune when you realize that walking takes you forever” Crystavia caught the bag easily and set it on her lap. She opened the bag and pulled out thick coyote steak sandwiches and a flask of melon juice. She also pulled out a blanket and spread it on the ground. “Ok. Lunch is ready” she called to Faloria as she set out the sandwiches and drink.

The gnome and night elf sat eating in comfortable silence, both engrossed in their own thoughts. When lunch was over, they packed the leftover food and drink away and lay in the warm sunshine. “Since it doesn’t look like I’ll be free of you for awhile” Faloria grumbled but with a twinkle in her eye “why don’t you tell me what sort of training you’ve had so far”

“Well, when I was 6 my parents decided the time had come for me to begin my studies” Crystavia said “My mother, being a healer, naturally wanted me to follow in her footsteps. The same went for my father. They got into such arguments over what I would study” Crystavia smiled to herself “I finally decided for them by declaring that I wanted to be a druid and that by being a druid, I would have the best of both worlds”. It was decided I would travel to Darnassus to begin my training.

“It was a beautiful spring day” Crystavia remembered “I had my pack filled with supplies and a list of certain herbs to get for my mother. I started walking west, knowing it would take the better part of the day to reach the city.” Crystavia leaned back and stared off into the distance “My father had been teaching me how to hunt since I was a little girl so I knew I would be somewhat safe as long as I kept to the roads”

Crystavia leaned over and rummaged through the pack for the flask of melon juice. She raised it to her lips and drank deeply. “After walking for several hours, I finally was able to see Darnassus taking shape” Crystavia’s eye’s brightened “I was so excited to begin my training, I practically ran the rest of the way. “My first sight as I passed through the gates of Darnassus was the tallest tree I had ever seen…but that wasn’t the amazing part. The amazing part was that it walked around, patrolling the area”.

“I continued walking through the city. I came upon a guard, who pointed me in the direction of the druid trainer” remembered Crystavia. “After another 30 minutes of walking, I came upon a group of Night Elves and several large Nightsabers. I stopped to pet one and asked if the druid trainer was nearby. The one in charge turned and pointed to a tree even taller then the one I had seen at the entrance” Crystavia’s eyes grew wide at the memory ““Top of the tree behind you” he said”.

“I walked over to the entrance of the tree and peeked inside” Crystavia said “Inside I saw a few vendors and a spiraling ramp that led up to what I had been searching for. I walked up and after a few moments, entered a large, open room with an intense looking Night Elf in the middle. His full name was Arch Druid Fandral Staghelm. I remember being frightened yet curious. I walked over to him and stated who I was and that I was here to begin my training as a druid.”

Crystavia lay back on the blanket, her hands clasped behind her head. “The training I had was not easy. I learned how to hunt using spells and weapons instead of weapons alone. I learned about what druids can do. Did you know that some druids can even change their form” Her eyes grew wide at the thought “I heard stories about druids who were able to change into cats and even bears!” She sat up suddenly “That’s what I want to do someday. I think I would like to be able to turn myself into a cat” Crystavia smiled at the thought.

“When the horde invaded my village and killed all those dear to me, I had nowhere to go, no one to turn to” She sighed deeply “so I made myself invisible and started walking, not sure of where I could go but instinctively heading towards Darnassus, towards my teacher, Fandral. Before I knew it, I was standing outside the tree, the tree where I had spent so many joyful hours learning my skills. Fandral was the one who suggested I venture towards Stormwind to further my training. He said I needed to see more of our world then just the village and Darnassus, so I got a ride to Stormwind and well, you know the rest”.
