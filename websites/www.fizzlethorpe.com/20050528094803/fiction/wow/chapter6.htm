Chapter 6

The lone Defias sergeant hurried back to headquarters. He stormed inside the once grand home, which was now the headquarters of the Defias Brotherhood. He walked in and slammed the door behind him. The man and the woman standing at the window turned sharply at the sound of the door. “That…that gnome” was all the man could utter “That gnome killed my men. She must pay!” He stormed around the room, oblivious to anyone else in the room.

The man and woman looked at each other. “Calm yourself sergeant and tell us what happened?” asked the woman, who was the picture of beauty. The sergeant looked her in the eye and seeing her soulless gaze, turned away and spoke “My men were ambushed, M’lady” he said “four of them…killed by that sorry excuse for a gnome and her voidwalker pet”. He pounded his fist against the desk.  “I want her dead!” He continued to pace frantically around the room.

“Who was this warlock” asked the woman, though her tone suggested she already knew the answer to that question. “You know there’s only one such gnome who dares to hunt Defias” the sergeant huffed “It was that warlock Faloria…only she wasn’t alone. She had a night elf with her”. “A night elf, hmmm” mused the woman “we’ve been wanting a night elf to study for quite a long time”. She snapped her fingers at the sergeant “go. Bring me the gnome [b]and[/b] the night elf. And bring them back to me alive. I have plans for both of them”

Meanwhile, Faloria and Crystavia continued riding, eventually reaching the town of Lakeshire, in the Red Ridge mountains. Faloria suggested that Crystavia do a little fishing while she made some arrangements for their trip. “I heard my friend Malestrom was in the area. Since he’s a mage, I figure he could open a portal to Ironforge for us and it would cut down on our travel considerably” Faloria said as she unpacked the bags in their room in the inn. “I think we’ll stay here for a day or so to rest. Besides, after our run in with the Defias, I’d like to keep off the roads as much as possible. I have no doubt they are quite angry at me…and you as well for what happened”

Faloria continued to rummages through her bags and pulled out a fishing pole “ahhh…here it is. Why don’t you go out to the dock and catch us some fish for dinner?” Faloria sat down at the table in their room and pulled out a pen and some paper and started to scribble something. When she was done, she snapped her fingers and suddenly her black crow appeared. Faloria tied the note to the crow’s foot and said “Take this to Malestrom right away. Last I heard he was over near Stonewatch Keep doing some hunting. Wait for an answer and come right back” with that, the crow was off, flying out the window.

Grabbing the other fishing pole, Faloria motioned to Crystavia to follow “come on, let’s go fishing or else we’ll have to have leftovers for dinner” The two walked down the stairs and out of the inn, neither one of them noticing the 2 sets of eyes watching their moves, nor did either one of them hear the mutterings of the Defias hidden in the bushes "there they are. The sergeant will be quite pleased..."
