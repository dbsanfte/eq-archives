Chapter 1


It was the moaning that woke her...

Faloria struggled awake, her joints stiff and sore from sleeping in the hard chair next to her sister's bed. Opening her eyes was a struggle but finally she was conscious, then she remembered what had woken her and she reached for her sister Crystavia's hand. It was cool to the touch. She gazed at her sister, who lay moaning in her sleep, the deep gashes on her arms and chest covered in thick bandages made by Faloria herself. She noted that they would need to be changed soon...but decided to let her sister sleep for awhile longer.

After making sure her sister was sleeping, she sighed softly and dropped back into the chair. “Why did I let her follow me? She's way too young to be wandering around that god forsaken area. Why didn't I stop her?" she thought, berating herself. "If she doesn't make it, it will be my fault."

Faloria sat slumped in her chair, listening to the soft sounds of Crys's breathing, assured that the night elf was still with her. As she sat there, she closed her eyes and thought back to that fateful day when the gnome and night elf first met.

That day started out like any other day...Faloria, not used to being awake during the day and not liking it one little bit, was rushing to meet with Gakin the Darkbinder to discuss her training. Normally they met late in the evening, when the rest of the city was asleep. "Stormwind" said Gakin, “tolerates us...barely. They turn a blind eye to The Slaughtered Lamb and the warlocks who inhabit the cellar. We're good enough to protect their city...but not good enough to acknowledge" Gakin always grumbled.

As she made her way through the quiet paths of the Mage Quarter, Faloria's mind was on her training. "soon...very soon" she thought "soon, I'll be a done with my training and I can put my talents to good use". So engrossed with thoughts of her training, she didn't notice the gangly Night Elf until she ran into her, knocking them both to the ground.

"OOF!" said Faloria as the breath was knocked out of her. "What in Elune’s name do you think you're doing? She stood up and brushed off her robes and picked up her stave. "Don't you know better then to knock a gnome down?" Faloria turned around in time to see several Stormwind guards running towards her.

"You, there...warlock" stated a guard, contemptuously "have you seen a Night Elf run past here?" the guards looked around, sure that the gnome was hiding the Night Elf. "Are you blind as well as stupid? Can’t you see...?” Faloria said as she turned and pointed to the spot where the Night Elf was last, but nothing was there.

"You best hold a civil tongue in your head, gnome. We don't much like your kind here" snarled one of the guards. "Just tell us where she went". Faloria scratched her head and mumbled something about daylight being the scourge of the devil. "She went...that way" Faloria said, pointing south. Before she had even finished her sentence, the group of guards took off, in search of the Night Elf.

Faloria stood there, lightly tapping her staff against her foot. "Ok, you can come out now" she said to the empty space "they're gone". She waited a moment and slowly, the Night Elf came into focus. She was tall and gangly, just like her kind. With long purple hair and glowing white eyes, she was quite a beauty. She was also quite young, no more then 14, wearing clothes that Faloria wouldn’t even allow her Felhunter to sleep on…and she didn’t even like her Felhunter.

“So tell me, little one” Faloria began “What is your name and why were you being chased by the guards?” At this question, the Night Elf’s eyes filled with tears and she sat down hard on the ground. “My name is Crystavia” sniffled the Night Elf. “I just wanted something to eat” she cried “How was I to know that squirrel was somebody’s pet?” “Crystavia, hmmm” pondered Faloria “Well Crystavia, I suggest you find another means to fill your stomach. Guards here lack, shall we say…hearts” with this, the gnome turned and started walking towards The Slaughtered Lamb.

Crystavia looked up, alarmed. “Where are you going” she asked? “I’m going in there” Faloria said, pointing towards the tavern. Faloria turned and continued walking. “Wait” cried Crystavia “Let me go with you”. Crystavia scrambled up and ran towards the gnome, skidding to a stop and looking down at Faloria with sad white eyes. Faloria sighed “I knew I should have stayed in bed today” she said, cursing softly to herself.

Sighing deeply, Faloria motioned for the Night Elf to follow her. “Keep your head down and your mouth shut” she said “The masters don’t much like strangers wandering around so if anyone talks to you, say nothing.
