Chapter 3

At that moment, Faloria’s crow flew back into the kitchen with a note tied around its ankle. Faloria reached over and untied the note. She quickly unrolled it and began to read. “Ah, good” she said “Maura and Kullom will be able to help” Faloria put the note aside and stood up from the table “I’m sure you must be exhausted. Why don’t you go upstairs and rest. You can use the room at the end of the hall” Faloria picked up the plate and walked towards the sink “We will be leaving in the morning for Thelsamar, where Maura and Kullom live. It’s a long ride, I’ll wake you when it’s time”

Crystavia stood up from the table and slowly walked to the stairs. She stopped at the bottom and turned and looked at Faloria “I don’t know how to thank you…you have saved my life and I am indebted to you.” With that, the Night Elf turned and walked up the stairs.

Faloria walked back into the kitchen and fixed herself a plate of food and a mug of melon juice. She carried it over to the table and sat down tiredly. “It’s been quite a day” she thought to herself. “I never thought I’d be taking care of a Night Elf, much less a Night Elf druid”. With that thought, she quickly finished her meal and drained her mug. She placed the dishes in the sink and made sure things were locked up.

When Faloria reached the top of the stairs, she could hear the deep, even breathing of Crystavia, fast asleep. She walked to the end of the hall and peeked her head into the open door. She saw the Night Elf sprawled on the bed, dead asleep. She hadn’t even taken the time to undress or get under the covers. Faloria walked over to the closet and pulled down a thick comforter and covered Crystavia with it. “Sleep well little one” she thought, as she brushed a lock of purple hair away from Crystavia’s face “you’re going to need it”.

The next morning dawned bright and early. “Wake up Crystavia…we need to be leaving soon” Faloria called up the stairs “I left a pitcher of water on the dresser for you and some clean clothes on the chair”. Faloria hurried back into the kitchen to finish packing some supplies for their trip. Crystavia woke and stretched and after looking around the comfortable room, she got up and poured some water into the bowl and proceeded to clean up. She pulled off the dirty rags she had been wearing and reached over and grabbed the clothes off the chair. She held them up to her and noticed that while they were old, they were clean and well made. She quickly pulled the tunic and leggings on and bent over to pull her boots back on. With a quick glance in the mirror, she hurried out of the room and down the stairs.

“Ah, there you are” Faloria said “sit and eat something before we leave”. She placed a plate in front of Crystavia with some leftover omelette and some hot wolf ribs. “There some melon juice and milk in the icebox, would you pour us both some while I finish up here?” Faloria asked, rushing about the kitchen with practiced ease. Crystavia got up and pulled a large pitcher of milk from the icebox and two mugs from the cupboard and placed them on the table. Faloria walked over with a plate and sat down at the table and started to eat. “Have you ever been to Thelsamar?” Crystavia shook her head, her mouth filled with delicious food. “It’s about a day’s ride northeast of here. Its easy riding…just long. It’s a beautiful area…I can see why Maura and Kullom wanted to make their home there”

They both finished their food at about the same time. Faloria jumped up to gather more supplies while Crystavia took their plates to the sink and quickly cleaned them and placed them in the rack. “Ok. I think we’re ready” said Faloria. She walked outside, followed by Crystavia. She stopped and closed her eyes for a moment and softly recited the spell that Crystavia had heard earlier. Salia appeared before them. Crystavia smiled at the thought of riding the magnificent horse again, wondering if she would ever own anything as grand. Faloria swung herself on to Salia’s back and reached down to offer the night elf her hand. Crystavia grabbed her hand and swung herself up with practiced ease and without further ado, the two rode north towards Thelsamar.
