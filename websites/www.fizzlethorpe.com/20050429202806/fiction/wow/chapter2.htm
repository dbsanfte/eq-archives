Chapter 2

The gnome and Night Elf walked out of The Slaughtered Lamb, blinking in the fading light of the day. Crystavia stretched and tried not to let Faloria hear how much her stomach was rumbling, but no amount of coughing would cover that sound. Faloria started walking and motioned to Crystavia to follow. Crystavia rushed to keep up with quick paced gnome. She was anxious and hungry, but loath to question the gnome for fear that Faloria would send her away. She continued to follow Faloria through the city until they reached the front gates.

They continued through the gates and once they were clear of the guards, Faloria looked around and satisfied that they were alone, closed her eyes and recited an age old spell under her breath. Suddenly, in a burst of light and fire, there stood the most magnificent horse. It was black like the midnight sky and had fire coming from its hooves and eyes. Crys’s sharp intake of breath and slight step backwards did not go unnoticed. Faloria reached up and stroked her trusty Felsteed on the nose. “This” Faloria said “Is Salia, my felsteed.” Faloria walked towards Crystavia, leading the massive horse. “she won’t hurt you…go on…pet her”. Crystavia extended a slightly shaking hand out to the beast and was rewarded with a neigh & a shaking her the head. Crystavia smiled brightly and continued to scratch the steed’s nose.

Faloria stood and watched the night elf. She noticed how thin the child was. The fact that Crystavia had slipped into invisibility whenever they came upon a guard did not go unnoticed either. “If I’m not mistaken” Faloria thought to herself “that one has had some druid training…what am I getting myself into?”

Faloria climbed on Salia’s back & motioned to Crystavia to come closer. Crystavia walked up, grabbed Faloria’s hand and swung herself up behind her. Crystavia was in awe…she had never been on the back of such a magnificent animal. She held on for dear life as Salia reared, neighed loudly and took off running.

The two made quite a sight, the tiny dark gnome and the willowy purple haired night elf, galloping through Goldshire towards Duskwood. Several people stopped to admire the horse and the rider. They never saw the night elf, who had instinctively cloaked herself in invisibility, concerned that a Stormwind guard would catch sight of her.

They rode for what seemed like hours but in fact was only about thirty minutes. Suddenly Salia pulled up to a stop and Faloria swung her leg over the beast’s massive back and jumped to the ground. “Come along” said Faloria as she walked towards a small house nestled in the woods. It was small and purple, something that Crystavia had never seen before. She flung herself off the horse and hurried into the house after Faloria.

“Go ahead and have a seat. I’ll get you some food and drink…You must be starving” said Faloria as she busied herself in the small kitchen. Crystavia walked to the table and sat down, taking in her new surroundings. She noted that the inside of the house was a lot larger then the outside let on. Off of the kitchen was a large living area with several comfortable looking couches and chairs. There was also a large fireplace that kept the room warm and cozy. Off to the left were stairs that led to the bedrooms upstairs.

Faloria walked back to the table with two mugs of ice cold milk and offered one to Crystavia. Crystavia took it gratefully. Faloria went to the other end and sat down. “Dinner will be ready shortly. I hope you like Monster Omelettes…I just got some nice eggs from my friend Peacia”. Faloria took out some paper and a pen from the basket on the table. She tapped the pen against her teeth, deep in thought. She looked at Crystavia, thought for a second then started to write. She wrote for what seemed forever. When she was done, she snapped her fingers and suddenly a large black crow appeared next to her. “Here, take this to Maura” Faloria said, rolling up the paper and tying it with a string “Wait for an answer and bring it back to me immediately”. With that, the crow took off through the open window above the sink.

“Maura is a good friend of mine” stated Faloria, matter of factly “She is a master tailor and I asked her to make you some decent clothes and some bags, since I’m sure you don’t have any to speak of. I also asked her husband, Kullom, to forge you a dagger for protection” Faloria put the pen and paper away. “So tell me how you came to have some druid training?” Crystavia nearly choked on her milk “wha what do you mean” she stuttered “I’ve never trained to be a druid”. Crystavia looked around nervously, as if she was afraid that she would be caught. Faloria rolled her eyes “Don’t play games with me, elf. I know a druid…or at least a druid in training, when I see one” Faloria got up and checked the omelette. She put it on a plate and brought it and a fork over to the table. She placed it in front of Crystavia and almost before she pulled her hand back, the ravenous elf was attacking it.

“Slow down…you’ll make yourself sick” Faloria said “it’s not going anywhere so take your time”. Crystavia slowed down, savoring the first taste of good food she had had in days. “So tell me how you came to be chased by guards in Stormwind” Faloria sat down and took a sip of her milk.

Crystavia took the last bite of the delicious omelette, took a big sip of her milk and wiped her mouth. “Well…I was born in a small town called Ameth’Arn on the outskirts of Auberdine almost 14 season’s ago. My mother was a woman of healing. My father... a hunter. They were good, decent people. Always willing to help when needed” Crystavia reached over and picked up the napkin, toying with the edge. “We lived there until the Burning Legion ravaged the countryside, killing everything…and everyone, that had the misfortune to come across their path. We packed up what belongings we could and set out for Starbreeze Village, just outside of Darnassus. My parents had family and friends there.”

“I loved living in Starbreeze Village. The woods were my playground, my schoolhouse, my life. I never thought I would leave, never wanted to leave. Until the day the horde came to my home” Crystavia sighed deeply, her eyes brimming with tears. “They came in the dead of night, like cowards. They slaughtered the guards while we slept. Then they started on the townspeople. I woke to the sounds of their screams” tears began to stream down Crystavia’s cheeks. “I ran outside to find…death and destruction. There were people everywhere. They were killing those that could heal first, then they started on the warriors and hunters”

“All I heard were screams. All I saw was death” Crystavia picked up the napkin and wiped away her tears. “I heard the horde commander tell his men to kill all the healers. I couldn’t let them know I was a druid in training. They would have killed me too”. Crystavia stared off into space. “I could have helped but instead I slipped into invisibility and ran” Crystavia lowered her head. “As I was running towards the edge of town, I heard my mother calling my father’s name weakly. I turned and ran towards her, but I was too late. She lay there dying” the night elf dropped her head into her hands. “I came back into focus for a moment to let her see I was there, then I made myself invisible again. I sat with her until she passed”

“I sat there next to the body of my mother for what seemed like days, but in reality was only about an hour” Crystavia said “By that time the horde had tired of their sport and had left to wreck havoc elsewhere. I got up and slowly surveyed the damage done” Crystavia cried harder “It was horrible. All my friends were laying in the streets dead. I kept walking, looking for my father. I walked up the hill and that’s where I found him. The horde had taken their time with him. It was horrible. That was when I fled my home, my life forever.
