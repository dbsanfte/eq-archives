This page will be where you can send me some of your really cool screenshots and we can share them with everyone. It can also be a scrapbook of raids and things you've done. It's up to you! All screenshots will open up to a larger window with the screenshot in it.

If you have a screenshot to add, just e-mail me the screenshot. I'll convert them to JPG if you cannot, but I'd prefer them im JPG if you can. You can also ZIP them up to make the file smaller to send. Just e-mail me your screenshots to cyranith@home.com and make sure you include who you are so I can give credit!
