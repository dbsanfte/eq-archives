Welcome to the Risen Phoenix Web Site

We are not the biggest guild, nor will we ever be. What we are is a family. We help each other, we are social, and it's friendly over guild chat. We don't force people to do anything they don't want to do. We do not run the guild like a military operation (except on raids where it's a requirement) and we don't order members to be on raids or else.

We give members the freedom to be an individual, yet part of a family. We will eventually do planes, dragons, and the like. But we will do it at our own pace. We have grown much lately and are working to improve as a team. But we will reach these goals at our own pace, staying centered on our core value of family and friendship.

People stay in Risen Phoenix not because of the loot we can get and of the places we can go, but because they can remain an individual yet be part of a family. No one is ignored, and everyone has a name. It's like "Cheers"; everyone knows your name. Everyone says "Hi" when you come on, and everyone congratulates you when you do something that's a personal accomplishment.

We invite you to browse our boards and get a sense of whom we are. If you come here looking for loot, level or fame, you are probably in the wrong place. If you come here looking for friendship, exploration, and to learn a little of the world that is Norrath, come on in.
