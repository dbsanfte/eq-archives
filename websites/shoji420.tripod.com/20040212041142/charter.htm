Guild Charter

 

    The Twilight Marauders are a group of people bound together by the common ideals of
Teamwork, Respect, Fairness, Friendship and Goodwill.  These ideals form the backbone
of our guild and are the qualities we look for in every member.  As a guild our purpose in
Norrath is the exploration of the world, the sharing of knowledge, and to provide a network
of people and support to all of our members.  Loyalty to the guild and its members
transcend any issue of race or class, and we freely accept members from all
areas and professions.

 

    “We are a guild that stands by our members, always striving to provide teachings and
guidance as they make their way in Norrath. Members pledge that they will aid their
fellows in these endeavors and all else, to the best of their ability.”   Kinadur

Code of Conduct

    All members of the Twilight Marauders pledge to follow the Code of Conduct to the best of their ability.

 

1)       Treat everyone, guild members and others, with courtesy and respect

2)       All disagreements between members are to be kept private between individuals and off guild chat, save when an officer is needed to intervene.

3)       Guild member’s item distribution, though encouraged to stay within the guild, is at that member’s sole discretion.

4)       Concerning items found on a guild raid.  First choi ce will be given to a suitable class participating in the raid.

5)       Items donated within the guild are to stay within the guild except at the original owners discretion.

6)       All members are encouraged to master a craft skill to benefit the guild.  Other members are expected to pay the cost of materials for any crafted items plus a reasonable gratuity to cpensate the craftsman.
