.: May 15, 2001 :.  
  Due to the growth of the guild we are now limiting the entry of new members.  By reviewing the roster we have come up with the following list of characters that we will continue to accept:

Bards - 46+
Rogues - 51+
Monks - 51+
Mages - 52+
Clerics - 52+
Wizards - 52+
Shamans - 54+
Enchanters - 54+

The above characters will still be allowed to apply, with a sponsor, as normal... 9 votes, 1 officer vote, no veto.  If your character was not listed above or does not meet the level posted then your application will have to be reviewed and approved by the officers.  A 2/3 vote of the council will be required.  This can be done if the player is truly outstanding or is a real life relation to a current guildmember.

Applications will be by invite only.  The invite immediately shows that at least one of us sees potential in the individual to be a great addition to our forces.  We want people that will contribute to the guild rather than use it for their own benifits.  When you see someone that displays good qualities in game play and attitudes then feel free to invite them to join us.  You can direct them to our web site and the application.

Even though we choose people rather than them choosing us, it is still possible for someone that knows no one in Arcis to join.  If they have shown interest in applying then simply have them get around as many members as possible.  Introduce them to everyone you see and ask them to post something in the General forum describing themselves and why they'd like to join us.  If they are what we are searching for then likely someone will extend an invitation to them.

Anyone can invite new members.  When you sponsor someone to apply then you are accepting responsibility for them and their actions, so choose wisely.  It is also up to you to help them get through the process in a timely manner.  Do not leave someone hanging or they will lose interest and go elsewhere.

 
.: Always check first :.  
  Put up a thread in the Members forum of our board asking if anyone knows so-and-so.  This gives the guild a chance to discuss them before the invite has even happened.  If someone in the guild has some information why they should not be invited, it can be freely expressed at this time.  Perhaps they have been known to KS or have a bad reputation with many people.  If someone in the guild has a problem with them then please respect that or perhaps find out what the problem is and see if it can be fixed.  Do not invite someone until all differences have been resovled!  If the response has been positive, or even neutral, then you may carry on to the invite.

 
.: Fill out an application :.  
  They can find the form below.  Have them copy the questions and paste them into a new post in the Application Forum.  The title of the post should be Name, Level, Class for easy reference.  By inviting the person to fill out the form you are taking responsibility for them and the process.  It is you that must look after the votes and encourage them to hang around us as much as possible.

 
.: Get them around the guild :.  
  As their sponsor it is your duty to help them meet as many members as possible.  The more people meet the applicant the better we can get to know them.  Invite them on our raids and other events.  In general, encourage them to group with Arcis as often as they can.  Once people get to know them they can decide if they want to vote for them.  As a guild you should pay attention not only to quality of the player but to the attitude as well.

 
.: Voting :.  
  I must stress here that you should only vote if you have spent time with the applicant either in discussion or in battle.  Do not vote because someone else thinks they are cool.  I have seen people get in without prior contact with members and due to uncontrolable issues they leave because no one really knew them and they felt like observers rather than active participants.

If you think someone is fit to join us then by all means head to the Application Forum and add your vote simply by posting a new message in their thread saying so.  Please don't just post "This person is great."  Be clear that you are voting for them and include a brief comment why you decided to vote.  The sponsor also votes! As the sponsor, you must identify yourself as such and include a comment about why you have chosen to sponser them.

This whole process is made easy or difficult in the voting.  Be sure of your votes!  Think quality control!  Applicants need a total of 10 (ten) votes to be guilded and at least one of those votes must come from an officer, this is to prevent chain-invites.  There is a time frame for this.  The application will be scrapped after one month.  The more the sponsor gets them around the guild the faster it will go.

If there is a negative vote or dispute regarding the person invited then the process is halted.  If you are the one to make the negative vote be sure you have a solid reason for it.  Be able to explain what they have done or not done to make you feel they should not proceed into the guild.  It is up the the guild as a whole to either correct things and carry on with voting, or to decide that the person is not to continue any further.  Each case will be handled individually as they come up.

 
.: Guilding :.  
  When all votes have been collected then it's up to the sponsor to notify an officer.  The officers may know already, but it's you that should be monitoring the votes etc.  At the soonest oppertunity they are to be guilded along with their alts.

Now that they are new members of the guild the sponsor should continue to intorduce them to more and more members and answer any questions they may have. 

Good luck to all and let's build a strong and cheerful guild!
