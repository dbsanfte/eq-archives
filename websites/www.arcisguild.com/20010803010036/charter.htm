.: Who We Are :.  
  Arcis is a group of friends on the Povar server that grew from a group of guilds that banded together in order to hunt bigger mobs and in bigger areas.  Aqua Vitae, Fireblades/Off The Wall, Wolf Hounds, Hands of Faith, and Knights of Povar merged to form Arcis in an effort to improve communications and relations between the various members of the guilds involved.

 
.: Why We Exist :.  
  Arcis believes that its members are all individuals and have an inherent freedom to participate to whatever extent that they feel comfortable in the guild.  They may be very or minimally active, as they choose.  We want people to enjoy their experience with the guild.  We encourage active participation, involvement, and comments but no member will be alienated or removed for not doing these.

Arcis is first and foremost a family of friends.  We have formed this guild in order to better communicate and enjoy our playing experiences.  Guild members are highly encouraged to help as is within their powers but will not be required to do so.

 
.: Guild Leadership :.  
  The officers of Arcis are here to provide support for our members and to create a more enjoyable playing environment for not only our guildmembers but also the rest of the residents of Norrath.  Please feel free to contact any of them with complaints, issues, problems or compliments whether you are a member or not.

In addition to the officers, those who join the guild by invitation of a current guild member will be a ward of that member.  These Mentors will be the primary point of contact for the new member for answering questions, directing them to the guild webpage and message board, and collecting all of the information about the new member for the guild records.

Lastly, one need not be an officer or a mentor in order to be a leader within and outside of the guild.  Those who can lead should lead.  Raid leaders, group leaders, and event leaders are always needed and welcome.  You are encouraged to take the opportunity to lead when it is available.

 
.: Member Responsibilities :.  
  All members understand that their actions and comments will now carry the weight of our guild's name as well as their own.  They also understand that their actions reflect upon their Mentor who are also responsible for their Ward's actions.

Strive to be the best player and person you can be.  Know the abilities and limitations of your characters and try to know those of the other classes you group with.  Follow directions when appropriate and give directions when the situation warrants it.

As a general rule we would prefer if guild members did not use /anon or /roleplay as they both hide your class and level, as well as your location.  It is much easier to group with people when you know if they are online and in the same part of the world that you are in.  Because we are a fairly large guild, both /anon and /roleplay will hide you from the /who GUILD all lists when broken out by level and we cannot easily tell if a given member is online.  There ARE times that /roleplay is appropriate (trying to bank in Greater Faydark for example) but in general we would like you to not be anonymous or roleplaying.

 
.: What Not To Expect :.  
  There are class dependancies that exist and while other guild members may have abilities that you are in need of, it is not reasonable to expect others to drop everything in order to satisfy your need.  We encourage Druids and Wizards to provide transportation when they are available to do so.  We encourage Clerics to Ressurect their fallen companions when they are able.  We encourage Necromancers to summon corpses when guildmates get in over their heads.  But in all cases, none of these are expected every time.  It is expected that those wanting help put forth at least as much effort as those providing the help.

You should not expect to be power leveled or twinked.  Arcis discourages power leveling other members of the guild.  Players that have been power leveled rarely reach their full potential and miss many important and fun portions of the game and we do not want to deprive our members of those experiences.

 
.: What Is Not Allowed :.  
  All members of the guild realize that each and every individual in this game has their own right to enjoying their time and we by no way own a "camp", a "quest", or any locations or creatures in Norrath.  If a guild member is denied the right to something of the like listed above then no member will take matters into their own hands.  We believe the best way to reach others is not by threatening them or alienating them but by demonstrating effective cooperation.

No member will practice deliberate training.  Accidents happen however and the guild will not suspend a member because of this without investigating the incident(s).  Training constitutes anything that the trained person believes it is.  Guild members realize it is within their power as a human being to resolve these conflicts with simple reasoning and compromise.

Threatening language, excessive profanity, and other bannable speech such as sexually explicit depictions or other extreme abuse will not be tolerated via any communications channel.  We expect members to treat others with respect at all times in the interest of providing the most enjoyable play experience for everyone.  We realize people become frustrated so minor infractions, so long as apologized for, can be overlooked but continual disregard for the rights of the others can constitute removal or suspension from the guild.

Guild chat is here to help us communicate not to spread hate propaganda or used as a trading post for the mistreatment of others.

 
.: Removal From The Guild :.  
  Membership in the guild is based on past actions and intentions.  Comments about our members are welcome, however, members will not be prosecuted unless their actions are extreme or part of an ongoing pattern.  Our guild allows and expects individual freedom so the views or ideas of any single member may not necessarily reflect the supported views of the guild itself.  We work as a group and think as a family, we are not a collective.

Suspensions from the guild will be temporary removals and can be done with the agreement of any two officers.  Suspensions will last one week, in which the suspended individual will be given a chance to state their side and attempt to resolve all conflicts.  Permanent removal from the guild will require a 2/3 vote from all officers and final approval from the guildleader.

 
.: Base Loot Rules :.  
  These rules are the general guideline assumed to be in effect within the guild unless additional rules or changes are announced in advance of a raid or a piece of loot dropping.

If you are able to use the item, will use the item, have a need for the item, and feel you have done enough to deserve the item, then you may roll for the item.

All loot should be rolled for on the basis of the needs of the character in attendance unless agreed upon in advance.  If noone in attendance is able to use a given item, that item will become available for general roll.

Only the first roll over the correct number range will count.  Those who accidentally roll over incorrect ranges will be allowed to roll again over the correct range.

Ninja-looting will not be tolerated under any circumstances.

Arcis would like to encourage members to first attempt to give away or trade items within the Alliance before selling or trading them outside the Alliance.

Finally, it is considered to be good form for a portion of all money loot to be donated to the Clerics to help cover the spell component costs incurred to help the group as a whole with buffs.  This can be done in many ways and while not required in pick-up groups, it is still encouraged.

 
.: Joining Us :.  
  Those wishing to joing our ranks should first actively seek out guild members of the appropriate level and spend time grouping with them.  The more you can group with the better, as one of these people will need to sponsor your application to the guild.

Next, copy the questions in the Application Form to a new post in the Application Forum on our message board and fill in your responses.  Please use your name, level and class as the subject header.  This application will be evaluated along with the the impressions of those guild members you have grouped with.

In order to be invited into the guild, at least 10 guild members who have grouped with you must approve of your request - including at least one guild officer - and none must object.  You will be evaluated based on your personal character, your ethics, your play ability, and your in-game reputation (including actions prior to your application).
