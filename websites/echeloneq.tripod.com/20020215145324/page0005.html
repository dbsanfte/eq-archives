Formation
I don’t wanna form a guild that turns into a bureaucracy of rules and red tape. I’m doin this to help ppl play better. I want us all to keep playing as much as possible, anything that takes away from that is to be avoided. Fancy websites, mandatory guild meetings, weekly newsletters, and such are definitely out.
We need to be diverse. We need all races and classes represented. We cannot afford to be one dimensional or weak in any area. I propose a guild leader or officer, for each race and class. All members will be subject to judgement of the leaders of their race and class as well as any senior officer.
