Kedge Raid

The following is meant as a guide for the upcoming Kedge Raid.  However, for a complete guide to Kedge Keep be sure to read PhreeKitty's Guide.

General Rules and Tips

   - Bring a stack of Fish Scales with you.  This is most important, as we will be casting enduring breath a lot.
   - When we buff up, make sure your top buffs are junk buffs.  We will then negate the third and cast enduring breath.  That way  we can keep enduring breath from being dispelled.
   - No Area Effect spells.  Kedge is a 3d maze and as such AE spells can aggro mobs above and below us.  Also, there are sailfins which explode upon death and blast everything around them for 400points.
   - Hot key a social before hand that tells your group that Enduring Breath has dropped.  When you see it drop or it starts blinking, hammer on this hotkey!
   - Looting will be done in a 'need-before-greed' fashion. Only the group leader loots.  Then, either after the raid or during a break, the loot will be divided.  You can only roll on something you can use.  If no one can use it, or those that can don't want it, then you and anyone else interested in the item rolls.
  - Finally, remember to have fun!  

The Route

  We will attempt to establish 3 camps in succession in this raid.  Camps A, B and C are marked on the map below, followed by a brief description of each camp.  First some general remarks.  We will hold each camp for as long as necessary, and attempt to acquire as many as the major items as possible in each camp.  When we are finished with the camp, we invis, then proceed to the next one.  Only the puller leaves each camp.



Camp A

  In this camp we will be pulling Mobs from the center of the main room. The major pull is Cauldronbubble, located at the bottom of the shaft marked 'D' on the map. Cauldronbubble is a 49th level shark who drops the Sharkbone Warhammer (1HB 8/26 +5 Wis, War Clr Pal Rng Shd Dru Mnk Brd Rog Shm) as a common, and the Hammerhead Helm (AC 13 Str +7 Sta +7, War Clr Pal Rng Shd Brd) as a rare. The puller will swim straight down from the Camp A room and will pull Cauldronbubble or his place holder.  There is another shark, usually a Cauldron Hammerhead, but occasionally a Cauldron Shark with him, so we will be fighting two sharks when we pull.

Camp B

  In this camp we will be pulling the Golden Hair Mermaid's room, located up the shaft marked 'C'.  There are 3 other spawns in this room along with the mermaid which we will also tackle.  The Mermaid always drops a lock of her hair, which is need for the Raincaller bow quest.  Note that the Raincaller does sell for a fair amount of cash, so even if you can't use it, you may want to pick up the hair if you can.  The 2nd component of the Raincaller (Gorilla hair from Cazic Thule) is a joke to get, and the 3rd, the Trueshot Longbow, I can make for you. 

  The only other thing to mention in Camp B is the presence of Shimmering Sailfins.  Do not attack them.  They will blow up with an 400 point AE spell if you do.  As such, there will be no area effect spells used during the Kedge raid.  Along with possibly provoking a Sailfin nuke, it will also attract the attention of mobs either below or above us.

Camp C

 Finally, we will move to the lair of CauldronBoil.  Here, we will set up camp in one of the jail cells, and pull CauldronBoil, and one of the 3 other sharks patrolling with him.  CauldronBoil drops a Sharkjaw Cutlass (1HS 10/32, War Pal Rng Shd Brd Rog) as a common and  Kedgemail Gauntlets (AC 11 Str +3 Dex +5 Mana +25, Pal Rng Shd Brd Rog Shm) as a rare.   

  Once we have had our fill of Camp C, the planned part of the raid is over.  From there we can either call it a day, or continue on to other prey.  One suggestion is to set up camp in the 'ferocious room' (see PhreeKitty's Site).  I guess we'll see how it goes ;)

....Thats it!  I am sure we will have fun.  If you have any suggestions or ideas, please email me.
