Level 34 - 40 Stonebrunt Mountains (solo)

Note: I wrote this guide to assist rangers and guild-mates who wish to hunt for experience 
and the major items in this zone. It is written from the point of view of a solo ranger, 
but can be applied to groups as well. I am sure it is by no means complete, so feel free to 
email me any additions or corrections you feel appropriate.

Introduction

The first thing I will say about this zone is that it is a ranger's dream. All the mobs of 
any significance are animals (read: fodder for the fear spell), and the major quests require 
tracking and SoW. There are merchants in zone, and a bind point merely one zone away. 
The forage skill is more than a means of sustenance, it is an important part of the 
wakizashi of the frozen skies quest. Also, in this zone you learn how to manage two 
annoying hardships; rugged terrain and multiple adds. Ultimately, the realm of the Kejek 
can provide a mid-level ranger was both experience and an amazing amount of platinum.

The Landscape

To say Stonebrunt is rocky is quite an understatement. The flat wooded area in the zone is 
small; dropping quickly in to the water in the East, and rising sharply in the north towards 
the Kejek outpost, your new home in Stonebrunt. The area spanning from the zone walls in 
the north and west, to the stream in the east, and the jungle in the south is where all the 
action is, and thus where you will spend most of your time. It is all rocks and slopes, and 
as such you would be well advised to load up on batwings for your levitate spell. You will 
be praising this spell when you get it, of that you can be sure.

The Prey

There are seven major Mobs in Stonebrunt; pandas, gorillas, kobolds, tigers, spiritlings, 
titans and the elusive heretics. For the most part, however, you will only be focusing on 
the pandas, tigers and heretics. The tigers and pandas range from low to mid-twenties to 
mid-thirties in level, while the titans range from mid-thirties to mid-forties. The 
gorillas share the same level range as the pandas, so they can serve as a good exp source.  The spiritlings and kobolds will not hold out as a source for experience as they begin to green-out by the time you are level 36. Finally, the heretics are roughly mid to high 
twenties, and as such pose no challenge to you. 

So why only focus on tigers, pandas and heretics? Read on.

The Quests

There are a few quests in Stonebrunt, most of which are quite good. The quests involving 
the titans can net excellent items for higher level characters, including the Astral 
Leggings of the Titans, which I believe is the highest droppable int legs for casters 
currently in the game. However, your focus will be on 2 quests: The quest for the 
wakizashi of the frozen skies, and Heretic heads quest. These two quests will take up the 
vast majority of your time, and provide both decent items and decent experience. Tracking, 
Foraging, and SoW are the tools you will use to complete these quests, while Panic Animal and Snare are your weapons.

Wakizashi of the Frozen Skies

This quest involves two NPCs in the Kejek village (lovingly called Kitty City), Khonza and 
Jali. Say "I will fetch them" to Khonza, and you will receive an eight-slot bag. Fill this 
bag with 2 panda claws, 2 ASP poison sacs, 2 tiger skins, a Kejekan Palm fruit, and a 
bamboo shoot. Hit combine and return it to Khonza to receive a vial of medicine, some 
faction and a little bit of experience. Give this to Jali in the next hut and you will 
receive a wakizashi of the frozen skies.

The Wakizashi of the Frozen Skies is an excellent little sword. It has a damage of 6, a 
delay of 22 and a 81 point DD damage proc. This proc starts at level 20, making this an 
excellent weapon for your lower level alts. Because of its high 'twink' value, the Waki 
sells for around 700-1000pp (more in trades) and from my experience, it sells quickly. 
As you progress through to 40, you can easily gather 10 - 15 of these weapons, and if you 
play your cards right, in your favorite auction zone you can net between 15 to 20K in 
platinum and gear. A virtual fortune to a ranger of this level. On to the components...

The components required in this quest are relatively easy to acquire with a little patience. 
With one exception, they are all available in the zone, and drop on mobs you are hunting for 
exp anyway. The asp poison sacs are the only components not available in Stonebrunt. 
Fortunately for you, the poison sacs are abundant and easily acquired in the Oasis of Marr, 
so before you head to your new home, spend an hour or to in Oasis loading up on poison sacs. 

The Panda claws drop from any of the pandas, and the tiger skins drop from any tiger (yes, 
from the tigress as well). Based on experience, I would say the Tiger skins are uncommon 
drops (getting about 1 in every 5-6 kills) while the Panda claws are rare (about 1 in every 
10-15 kills). Finally, the palm fruit and bamboo shoot are foraged, so be sure to be 
constantly foraging!

The great thing about this quest is that the mobs you need to hunt are fear-able, and provide decent exp. The strategy is simple, hit track, locate the next panda or tiger and hunt it down (foraging and checking track for heretics as you run). When you find your target, 
simply snare it, fear it and attack. Keep applying fear as needed and before you know it, 
its on to the next target. The only problem is wandering mobs that will join the fray. All 
you need to do is fear it and it will run away, hopefully forgetting about you. If it comes 
back fear it again and it will eventually get the point. As you progress, you may find 
yourself becoming proficient at fear kiting, taking two on at the same time. I found I did this when another panda joined the fight and I didn't want to lose it. If things do become to much to handle, run away (you are sowed remember) and when you are far enough away, camp to clear aggro. When you re-enter Norrath, its back to basics.

Heretic Heads

This popular quest is easy to explain, but time consuming to do. Saying "what heretics" to 
Shazda will get you a four-slot bag that you are to fill up with heretic heads. These heads 
are no-drop, and come from 'a heretic invader'. The invaders are easy to kill, but not easy 
to find. They only spawn about once every Norrathian day, and don't breathe mountain air 
for long. They are aggro to everything, so if another PC doesn't kill him first (and there 
will be several of players hunting them), wandering mobs will. Remember how I said you have to always be checking track while you run? That's why. As soon as you see the heretic on track, stop what you are doing and immediately go get it. I have found that staying in the 
area south of Kitty City allows you to always pick up heretics on track while you are 
hunting, and you will generally not be too far way from them. So, be sure to stay in that 
area; there are enough pandas and tigers to hunt there anyway.

When you finally get 4 heads, hit combine, cross your fingers and return the bag to Shazda. There are two rewards you will get; a Kejekan Tribal Headband (Int + 3, Wis + 3) is the common drop and the Swiftclaw sash (AC 2, Agil +2 Effect Haste - about 15%) is the rare. Obviously the sash is what you are looking for. It is lore, so if when you go for your second, be sure to drop your first off on an alt or with a friend. You will want to keep your first sash, and will probably sell any others you get - if you are lucky enough to get extras, that is. I have found that they sell for about 2 - 2.5k, a healthy amount of plat at any level. I have given 9 bags of heretic heads to Shazda, and have been lucky a third of the time. 

The Titans
There are five Titans in Stonebrunt:  A panda (Giang Yin), a tiger (Snowbeast), a leopard (Prowler of the jungle), an ape (Old Ghostback) and a snake (Slider the Ancient).  The Titans are much more powerful than anything else in the zone, and should not be engaged until you are past level 45, at the very least.  The first problem is that they can not be snared. They can, however, be feared so all is not lost.  Secondly, killing them will get you a negative hit with the Kejekan people, but if you have done the other quests in this zone the hit will not be that bad.  Also, a wandering Kejekan will heal the Titans and attack you if they see you battling them.  Root-park them and finish them later if they interfere.

The key to successfully fear-kiting them is to find a decent path.  I have found that the barren rock northeast of the Kejekan city provides a good fear-kiting path, with few wandering mobs in the area.  Start at the top (where the mountain begins), and the Titans will always head in the direction of the Kejekan city when feared.  When fear breaks, run back to the top again and recast fear.  Keep doing this until your prey falls to the ground.  

Along with a few good items, the Titans drop pelts which are used for a quest in Erudin.  Turning in the Giang Yin's and Old Ghostback's pelt to Akbaq Salid in Erudin will get you the Astral Leggings of the Titans.  Turning in Snowbeast's and Prowler's will get you the Astral cloak of the Titans.  Finally, turning in the skin of Slider along with some platinum thread will get you the Scaled Robe of the Elder Serpent.  Item descriptions will follow at the end of this guide.

There is some debate on how you get the Titans to spawn.  And, after days of hunting them, I don't know that I have the answer.  Some say you have to kill a lot of animals of the given type (i.e. apes for Old Ghostback) to get them to spawn, while others maintain the key is the rabbits.  All I can suggest at the moment is to go on a rampage and kill anything you see.  By the time you can tackle the Titans, everything else in the zone will be easy, so keep on slayin until you see a Titan appear on your radar.  And if you do unlock the secret, please email me - I'll make surre you get all the credits and applause you are due!

Conclusion

I should conclude with a few remarks about banking.

Since you will have to make the run to Erudin once and a while, you may as well make some use of the time. On your way through the warrens be sure to take out a few kobolds and harvest the Kobold meat. Why? Have a read of my guide to food for rangers. And, in the Paineel newbie grounds there is an NPC right in front of the guards you can kill for a quick Erudin faction boost. 

So there it is. Hopefully you have this guide useful, and your time in Stonebrunt is 
successful. Take care, and may your arrows always find their mark!
