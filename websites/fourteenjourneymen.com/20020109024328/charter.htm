Here are the laws that govern our guild. Only those who follow the ways of The Fourteen Journeymen are allowed to fight under the <Fourteen Journeymen> tag.

1. Above all - HAVE FUN! The ultimate purpose of this guild is to enhance the enjoyment of EverQuest for those of us who play it.

2. Because this is an RPG and there are so many players who wish total immersion in the world of Norrath, a Journeymen should at all times "role - play" his/her character in public.

Please stay 'in character' at all times you are dealing with others in a public forum. When someone meets a Journeymen for the first time, we want him or her to know that we are people they would like to play with.

Any 'out of character' conversations should be kept for private /TELL's, /OOC's /GUILDSAY's or /GSAY's. Under no circumstances should /SHOUT be used for any conversations that are not in character and game related.

3. The use of vulgar or foul language in open text (ie: /OOC, /SHOUT, /AUCTION, or regular /SAY's where a third party/parties could hear it) will not be tolerated, and repeated offences could be grounds for dismissal from the guild.

4. Harassment of another player will not be tolerated. If a dispute arises between a guild member and another character, and it can not be resolved by either party, then the guild member should then /Ignore the offending character and, if necessary, report the reason for the dispute to a GM.

5. The practice known as "kill stealing" will also not be tolerated. That is to say, the intentional taking of a kill from someone else who was fighting it first. This is an ambiguous area, and common sense and respect for others in a group situation must be used. For example - You are in a group that is camped at an area and who are using a rotation to take turns there, when someone arrives who attacks out of order. In this case, "stealing" the kill back from them would be justified.

Under no conditions is it acceptable to play using tactics that are considered "dirty" or "unfair". Role playing an evil character is no excuse for this type of behavior as there is no provision in the game to defend against it. Also, your actions reflect on the guild as a whole, and therefore you must act in a manor that won't adversely affect our image.

6. Please fill out the member roster so we may have an accurate count on how many members we have. Be sure to add any alts that are in the guild.

7. We will only accept applications from people that have achieved the minimum level of 20. We feel it is necessary for a new player to become familiar with the basic mechanics of the game and the structure of the world before attempting to join into a guild.

However, if someone is an experienced player, having another character(s) above level 20, and who wishes to enter our guild with a new character, we will accept their application.

8. All new members will have to read the guild Charter, and agree to these rules before they are accepted into the guild. They will then be considered on probation for their first month, during which time the guild can get to know them and visa versa.
