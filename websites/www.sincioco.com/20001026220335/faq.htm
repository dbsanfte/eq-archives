FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQ) Version 1.5


Why do you want to leave your present job?

All good things must come to and end...

Personally, I like to learn new things and face new challenges. When things become routine and predictable, I take that as my cue to go.

What is your desired salary or hourly rate?

Always more than the last...

I'm interested in contract positions only. My hourly W2 rate is $85 per hour, and my corp-to-corp rate is $110 per hour.

These rates are non-negotiable, so if you are from an employment agency, make certain your clients can afford to pay for a top notch programmer.  :-)

When are you available to start?

There is no time like now...

I'm available today, but will not accept just any contract work.

I'm not interested in startup companies or stock options!  Don't get me wrong. I plan to get rich just like everyone one else, but I want to do it the old-fashion way:  saving and investing—and by getting into real estate.

When can you come in for an interview?

When you've got clients waiting for me to show up...

I'm available for an interview given 24 hours notice, although I would prefer a phone screening first so the client and I can decide whether we should proceed further or not.

The proper attire to wear should be communicated to me ahead of time, as some likes it casual while others formal.

The client's name and phone number should be provided, in the event that I'm going to be late, or get lost on the way

I will only interview for the same project once. I will not come back for a second or third interview. A relationship should start with the understanding that everyone's time is valuable. If you deem the client's time valuable, what do you make of mine?

Describe your ideal work environment?

Do what you love, love what you do, everyday... (Microsoft)

My ideal work environment would be at Microsoft, but an all Microsoft shop will suffice. Using Microsoft's cutting edge technology to deliver real world solutions.

I would like to continue working on web development, but since I'm also an accomplished client/server developer, a position that utilizes both skills-set would be excellent.

I would appreciate an environment where I can grow as a professional and as a person. Money could be made anywhere, and I'm only young once, what makes your company unique?

I would like to work for a manager/project lead who actually did software development or familiar with the software development cycle. Let's face it, programmers are logical beings. How logical is it to be led by someone who hasn't got a clue about what it takes to create great software?

What are your strengths and weaknesses?

Say what you mean, and mean what you say...

I usually speak my mind freely, which counts both as a strength and a weakness. I believe in honesty is the best policy. If the truth isn't good enough, then ask for a lie.  :-)

I'm also an optimist and an idealist. Don't tell me it can't be done, because you'll just challenge me to do it. Don't give me the it's always been done this way excuse, because I'm going to tell you how it ought to be done today!

I'm a quick learner and bore easily. Like a child stuck in a young man's body, I can learn and pick up things quickly. But don't expect me to get excited over the same things for more than 12 months. :-p

Where do you see yourself 5 or 10 years from now?

As your boss...

All joking aside, for the next 5 years, I want to gain as much experience as possible doing consulting work. This will let me see how various businesses operate and develop a network of contacts.

Having co-founded Online Creator Corporation, I'd like to put into practice all the things I've learned in the past 6 years as a consultant, and eventually work for Online Creator full-time.

Alright, alright... Here's my ultimate goal: I want to someday be a game developer.

...but, one has to pay mortgage and bills...
