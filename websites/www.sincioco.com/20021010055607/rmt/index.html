RMT is a FREE tool designed specifically for raid leaders who need a tool to do the following:
  • Assist in raid sign-ups and group creation.
  • Manage loot drops during and after raids.
  • Export groups and loot drops as text files.
  • Historically track players' raid attendance.
  • Historically track players' loot awards.
  • Historically track all players who got a particular loot.
  • Time when named mobs and buffs are due.
  • Import LFGs using your EQ log file.
  • Track Raid points, per player, per raid, per item awarded.
