In preparation for our deployment to Iraq, we learned how to protect a convoy.

Crew Served Weapons, November 21, 2004.

In preparation for our deployment to Iraq, Bravo Co., Data Platoon attended a course on how to assemble/disassemble a 50 caliber, a SAW and a 240G.
