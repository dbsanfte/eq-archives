No alliance will stand without a Charter; the foundation under which the alliance is formed to begin with. Liberation of Povar is no exception...it was created with a purpose...that being:

We shall gather together as comrades, drawing strength from the union, and accomplishing that which we cannot accomplish alone.

We shall be one, we shall be many. None shall lose their identity or the uniqueness that makes them who they are.

We shall journey on the Path to Greatness, known not for what we have, but for who we are, separately, and as a whole.

We shall remember the spirit in which we travel Norrath, and share that spirit with others.
