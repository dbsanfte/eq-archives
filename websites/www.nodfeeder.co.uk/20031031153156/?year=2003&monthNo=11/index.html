Weclome to Dreamers of Nexus website........

Over the last couple of months this guild has established itself on the Antonius Bayle server. The 2 fundamental reasons it was created was

  1. To provide help and introductory knowledge to new EQ players to the game on a larger scaled basis, and
  2. To increase the intake of members joining our 'father' guild Nexus of Dreams.

The guild has rapidly grown in its short period of existance to now claim to have over 50 members. THe new members of this guild have shown amazing enthusiasm and keeness to help one another, learn together and move on as a friendly group to bigger and better things.

Recently our guild achievements have been 3 fun raids to Runnyeye, Mines of Nurga and Dalnirs Crypt. The later of which, found us in a hilarious situation, where a level 50+ mob called 'The Kly' had charmed the whole raid and slowly but surely we began killing one another. Oh how we laughed ;-)

Not only this though, we were able to learn a valuable lesson for our future. Some of these zones are totally new to people and it is a great benefit to be able to enjoy doing such things with such friendly people.

Thank you all for taking the time to read this. If you find yourself engrossed in DoN activities and would lke to givve us a try, please register on our forum and post as our guild rules suggests.

Many thanks
