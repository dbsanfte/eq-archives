Friday, April 19, 2002



The Yakuza



 VS.



  Derakor the Vindicator


Expectations were high.  Excitement was at a peak.  And the
pressure was on for YKZ.  With an unprecedented turnout of 56 members, we
proceeded to lay the smack down on Vindi.  It took a couple of tries, but
the dynamic pulling duo of Kaiy and Xeno did a masterful job of separating
Vindi's guards, leaving him...unguarded!  Our first attempt took him down
to literally no hp's showing at all.  I personally nuked him for 3700 dmg
AFTER he was showing no red health bar...and the bastage still lived and took
down the remaining peeps there.  I swear he only had maybe 100 hp's left.


Our second attempt proved far more successful.  Even though our first
few MT's were wiped quickly in the beginning (sorry Linudan, but what's a good
raid without you dying quickly?), the clerics rallied and kept Blimp alive long
enough as the rest of the guild proceeded to pound on the enemy.  Our
wizards, druids, and mages gave Vindi a full body cavity check from every angle
as the meleers wreaked havoc slicing, dicing, and punching away.


The fight seemed to take an eternity, but only really lasted about 5
minutes.  With a mighty blast from Fallor, Vindi tumbled to the ground
defeated.


His BP went to Jayz and his head to Pendarin.  Gratz guys!

Early Friday morning a rag-tag YKZ crew of 12 (5 of which were bots) tried to take on Sev. It was a valiant effort, though 2 tries proved unsuccessful due to unusual lag. Losing a few peeps who succumbed to the Sandman, we decided to pair up with Soveriegn's Might. With a few mishaps, we still managed to bring down the giant leezard.

Drops included were: 2 Kavruul's Mystic Pouches, Orb of Tishan, and Shield of the Green Dragon. Spells included were: Mask of the Hunter, Talisman of the Raptor, Word of Redemption, and Winds of Gelid.

Splitting the loot with SM, YKZ ended up with: 1 Kavruul's Mystic Pouch, Shield of the Green Dragon, Talisman of the Raptor, and Winds of Gelid.

Unfortunately, everyone was too exhausted to stay on much longer as the loot was being decided, and most everyone had logged by the time Sev's corpse poofed. So the items are currently on me in the guild bank.

I have a couple screenshots, but a bit tired atm to post them. Will probably tomorrow. =)
