What do we believe in?

Our motto is "Integrity, Honor, Unity, Power".

Some guilds are created with the purpose of conquering new areas and lands. Others are created to excel and celebrate the trade skills in Norrath. Still others are created to equip the most characters as quickly and as superiorly as possible.

Ykz is none of those. We are a family first and foremost. Only second are we a guild. We have a relaxed atmosphere and enjoy having fun. We do not dream of being "the best" or the most "uber". We do not pace ourselves according to the accomplishments of others, but rather set our own goals, often greatly exceed them. While Ykz is seen as a powerful guild on Xev, our greatest pride is not the prestige that comes with our accomplishments but rather the humbling respect of the general public on Xev as an honest and fair guild.

We believe in need before greed. Quite possibly more than any other guild on Xev, we keep our gear within the guild. While there is no official rule stating as such, it is generally expected that any gear obtained within the guild (be it a majority guild group or a full-scale raid) will be kept within the guild. That is to say if an item is obtained by the guild it is the guilds' property, not the current user's. We believe that this is in the best interests of the guild in every aspect of practice. It makes already large accomplishments all that much greater and in the end the guild is geared to its very best ability. The same goes for equipping alts before others' mains--while we will not kick you out of the guild for doing so, it is generally frowned upon and will not make you a very popular member within the guild. This is a fairly radical idea for some to adhere to, but it is one of Ykz's defining qualities.

Just as a family strengthens each other through property so too does it assist its members with aid. Ykz is not a guild for loners and those who prefer to solo. We are constantly raiding, whether it be a small two group raid or a full-scale dungeon crawl, we are actively hunting nearly every day--not alone but as a guild. Whether working on epics or advancing our experience bars --- we are together. A family that hunts together stays together.

What is "The Code of Ykz"?

This is a question that many people that do not know about Ykz or are new to Ykz do not fully know yet, so since we have several new members in this guild I feel it is very appropriate that I take the time out to inform you of the four most important words in this guild.

The Stages of Development

1.Integrity
2.Honor
3.Unity
4.Power

These words are our code.

Integrity is something that every member of this guild or candidate wishing to join this guild should have. This is not something we can really make people have but we can help develop it. Integrity is understanding your own personal code of morality, of what’s right and wrong. This is your ability to stand by what you believe in but not only should you believe in it, you should also be willing to listen to others so you can mold your beliefs as needed because we are all human and thus we do not know everything so we must allow ourselves to be able to understand others and change our morality if required.

Honor is our ability to not only do what’s right all the time but our ability to respect others as we wish to be respected. We are a family of friends and like any friends that I know, we will not always get along but we need to look beyond our disagreements so we can understand each other and respect each other to achieve our goals. Honor and respect go hand in hand they are not given easily, they are things you must work hard for to earn.

Unity is our ability to function as a team and over come personal differences to reach our desired goals. Teamwork is important in this goal because we are not able to do things by ourselves and we must rely on each other at times.
Power is the final part of our code. This is the Shangri-la of Ykz. This is what we aim for each and everyday. Power is our ability to overcome any obstacle that is put in our way and pull through to the end with success behind us.

If these ideals could be summed up into one sentence to give to people considering Ykz as their home, it would be this:

You get what you give.
