What A Night


We had a pretty spectaular turnout for a weeknight. The server had just been patched early Thursday, so pretty much everything was up. Nagrach popped over to EJ to see what was going on with Sev, and found that he was up, but KoR was raiding him. Given the forces they had, we guestimated that they wouldn't be able to take him, so we began proting over just in case. We prepped up and waited to see how they'd fair. Given their low average level, they did extremely well on him, taking him below 50% (mind you many players were under level 50, and I remember losing to this guy with all 55+). By the time he finished them off, we were up to 31, which was pretty overkill, so the fight lasted all of 30 seconds.

Having so many people on, I popped back into Ssra temple to see if AF had finished off Rhag`Mozezh. Turns out after finishing Rhag`Zhezum off, they hadn't had the forces for the second Rhag and had left, so we hauled ass over to Ssra to give him a shot. Unfortunately by the time we all grouped up there, we were down to 21 (lot of east coast people had had to leave), which wasn't enough. So with some quick planning and communication by Nagrach, we soon had a group of about 20 Odysseys on the way to do a joint raid with us. Once everyone arrived, we quickly plowed through the mobs up to the second floor and into Mr. Mozdezh's room, which was strangely devoid of guard snakes (???). Oh well, easier for us. After a failed first attempt (in which Linudan went down faster than Simmie at the tittie bar's all-you-can-eat special, then everyone else fell to the rampages), we regrouped and mobilized with some new tactics, one of which which included watching Aalandan nuke Rhag`Mozdezh with lifetaps causing
