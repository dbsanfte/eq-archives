SO YOU WANT TO BE A HERO?

Step 1 - Basic Requirements & Ground Rules

These basic requirements are in place to assure that you will be able to meet, group, and have fun with Heroes as a guild. You will not be able to start your application until you meet all of these requirements.

1. Read everything carefully! Please take the time to read all this information carefully and make sure you understand it. If you have any questions, please don't hesitate to contact me.
2. You must be level 60. Please feel free to meet as many Heroes as you are able before you are level 60, however your application will not begin until you are level 60.
3. All applicants must be unguilded from any previous guild before applying.
4. You must have a sponsor before you can submit your application to applyheroes@yahoo.com. No application will be posted until there is a verified sponsor.
5. You must have basic resist gear. Some of the raids you will attend will require resist gear, and you need to be prepared. As a general target, you should be able to get MR/FR/CR all above 100 unbuffed.
6. Applicants will not receive any loot items on raids unless it is a NO DROP item that would otherwise rot. Please do not send tells for items you are interested in, we will offer it to you if appropriate.
7. Family members of current Heroes may be able to have some of the requirements or steps in the process waived. This is a case-by-case basis, please talk to me directly.
8. Applications can be closed at any time for any reason.
9. Expect applications to take 3-6 weeks. If you are not ready to commit this much time to your application, then Heroes is not the guild for you.
10. All new members will be subject to a 3-week probationary period. During this time you are unable to be a sponsor or a recommendation to any applicants.
11. Due to the limited number of flags available in some PoP encounters, applicants will only receive flags after all the current Heroes members on the raid have received theirs. Please make sure it is alright for you to receive the flag before doing so.


Step 2 - Finding a Sponsor

All applicants must have a current Heroes member who will agree to sponsor them before the application is opened. Please use the /join heroeslfg:lfg chat channel and the general section of our message boards to help find groups and to meet Heroes. There are a few basic requirements here too:

1. Heroes members may sponsor one person at any one time.
2. People without sponsors are not included in raids unless specifically asked by one of the raid leaders. If you do not have a sponsor, please make that your first priority and do not worry about trying to attend raids.
3. Unsponsored people may be asked to leave raids by the raid leaders.
4. Heroes officers may not be sponsors.


Step 3 - I have a sponsor, now what?

Once you have a sponsor, you are ready to have your application made official. Please write up a few paragraphs that introduces yourself to Heroes and email it to applyheroes@yahoo.com. The sponsor also needs to email me or otherwise contact me in game and confirm they will be your sponsor. After receiving your email and the confirmation from the sponsor, I will post your application in the "Becoming A Hero" section of the Heroes message boards.

Once the application is posted, other Heroes may reply to that application with comments, questions, or post their recommendations for your application. You may also post any additional comments or information that you would like us all to see.

1. Sponsored applicants are cleared to attend raids. However, it is at the raid leaders discretion to include or exclude applicants from a particular raid.
2. Your sponsor is the main contact for you during the application. Please find a sponsor who has a similar schedule and play style as you and can try to keep you informed of Heroes events and help you to find groups with other Heroes.
3. Your class rep officer may not be one of your recs.


Step 4 - Gathering Recommendations

Gathering recommendations from current Heroes is the next step in the application process. Recommendations can be from any current Heroes in good standing, and for it to be an official rec, they need to post on your thread in the "Becoming A Hero" forum and clearly express their desire to be a rec for you.

Generally, it should take about 3-4 weeks to gather the recs for your application. Applications that are "dead in the water" after the 3-4 week mark, will likely be closed unless you have talked to me specifically.

Depending on your character class, there are different amounts of recommendations you are required to gather. The classes with the higher requirements must be very active in gathering their recs in order to keep the application from being closed, 3-4 weeks will pass in a hurry. Additionally, we hold applicants within each demand tier to different standards and expect the applicants in the low demand classes to be truly outstanding people who can meet our highest standards in all aspects.

**High Demand Classes - You must have a sponsor and 5 recs from Heroes in good standing.
**Medium Demand Classes - You must have a sponsor and 7 recs from Heroes in good standing.
**Low Demand Classes - You must have a sponsor and 9 recs from Heroes in good standing.

1. Heroes members may only have 3 recommendations on the boards at any one time.
2. Heroes who are currently sponsors for other applicants may still rec up to 3 people.


Step 5 - Class Level Interviews

Every class has a Heroes officer who acts as their representative. Your class officer (or someone filling in for them if necessary) will conduct the class-level interview after you have completed gathering the required number of recs. Please don't be afraid to get to know your rep while you are still in the beginning stages of your application, they are all very friendly people and willing to talk and answer questions.

The class interview should be held while you are logged in to EQ and should include the sponsor. EQ chat channels make it very easy to include 3 people in the interview process. At their discretion, class reps may hold 2 interviews, one with the sponsor and one without, or may ask the sponsor to leave for part of the interview.

I understand the logistic problems you may encounter while trying to schedule an interview time with 3 different people to attend. If all three people have been making a genuine effort to meet and it is just not working, the sponsor may be excluded from the process. However, I don't want this to become a regular thing, sponsors are a valuable part of the process, both for helping the applicant to feel more comfortable during the interview and to help make sure the applicant is being honest with the class rep.

Sponsors, please feel free to ask and answer questions during the interview, you are a part of the process and not just there to listen in, please be active.

After the class interview has been completed, the class rep will let me know and post on your application on the boards. Please keep an eye out for me and we'll schedule the final chat.

Step 6 - Membership Level Interview

The final interview is one with me! Stick me on your friends list and keep an eye out for me and don't be shy. My regular play times are in the evenings PST between 6pm and midnight.

It is optional for the sponsors to be included in this interview, and it is at the sponsor's discretion. If the sponsor did not attend the class level interview then I may ask them to attend and take away some of the discretion.

If you have any questions please do not hesitate to contact Whirl online or email me at applyheroes@yahoo.com
