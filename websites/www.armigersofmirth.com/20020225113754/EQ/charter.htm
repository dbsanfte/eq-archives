Armigers of Mirth Charter

e are an EQ guild located on E'ci, consisting of players from any race and/or class, dedicated to exploring the taverns of Norrath, stretching the limits of recreational drinking, playing fair, and generally having a good time.
layers are at all times required to carry at least two drinks with them. Drinking is a great way to solve any in-game problems. Remember when in doubt about how to resolve a situation, offer the other player a drink.
layers are required to take up brewing, at least as a hobby (minimum skill rank of 1). A Player that becomes a Master Brewer will be awarded a personalized shot glass acknowledging their illustrious achievement (okay its a virtual shotglass).
ever drink alone, unless you're celebrating with a Guild Member or Members located in another zone, and always bring enough for everyone.
While we don't require role-playing at all times, we believe that is adds fun and flavor to the game and certainly recommend role-playing as much as possible.
ill stealing will not be tolerated. Your actions reflect on The Guild as a whole, and therefore you must act in a manner that won't adversely affect our image and reputation. When in doubt offer the other player a drink.
efore selling any major item check with the guild members that are online first, another guild member may need or want it. Guild members should get first crack at any such items. (Armigers, check out the new Trading Post section of the site.) This doesn't mean you have to give your stuff away for free. A drink should accompany every trade and transaction so that a toast may be made to its successful completion.
 
Guild looting policy
In a group composed entirely of Armigers (or primarily of Armigers where the outside party or parties agree):

* If an item is dropped that is either unusable by all group members, or there is general agreement that the item is only going to be used for resale purposes, then all interested group members will participate in a roll-off to determine who gets the item. Nobody should be excluded from such a roll-off unless they choose to be.

* If an item is dropped that is usable by one or more members of the group, and the people who could use the item plan to do so, then...

* If there is only one group member who can (and plans to) use the item, it is theirs.

* If there is more than one group member who can use the item, a roll-off will be done between interested parties to determine who gets the item.

* A player receiving an item in a 'contested' roll-off should refrain from participating in other roll-offs that do not involve the entire group (minus those who don't wish to participate) until all other players in that group have gotten something. The intention being to facilitate a somewhat even distribution of high-value items among the group.

[In a 'pick-up' group, all bets are off and/or players agree on other looting rules as required.]
