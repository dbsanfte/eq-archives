Joining the Armigers of Mirth

eing a social lot, we are happy to consider new players for memberhship in the Armigers of Mirth. For the benefit of all concerned, we do however have a few requirements.
pplicants must read, understand, and agree to abide by the rules and behavior as explained in our charter. The rules are not negotiable and repeated failure to abide by them may result in removal from The Guild.
hile we do not require roleplaying at all times, we believe that role playing adds flavor to the game and certainly suggest role playing as much as possible. In light of this, applicants must have a name the reflects positively on The Guild and therefor doesn't mock EverQuest©, imply vulgarity, plagarize, steal proper names, or resemble something other than a reasonable sounding fantasy name. For example, StudMan, KoolDewd, TygerWoodz, Skrewu, etc. would all be unacceptable.
ince we hope to bring in members that fit well into our Guild we require a "getting-to-know-you" period before membership. This entails the applicant grouping and playing with other Armigers on no less than 3 different occassions. Additionally during that time you must have played with at least 4 different Guild members. The purpose of this is to determine if our Guild and style of play is a good match for the applicant.
fter having played with Armiger members for the prescribed time, the players who have grouped with the applicant will take a vote on membership. The acceptance vote must reach a consensus, any 'nays' and the applicant's request for membership will be refused. Acceptance requires a good toast and several rounds of drinks - to be supplied by the new member of course!
embers may be promoted to Guild Officers if they have shown good judgement, fair play, and are living by the goals of The Guild. Promotion requires a consensus vote by all current Guild Officers. Promotion requires many a' good toast and a drinking party afterwards!
