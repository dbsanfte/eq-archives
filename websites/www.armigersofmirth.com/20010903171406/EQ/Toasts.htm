Every good drink should be accompanied with a good toast,
and every good toast accompanied by a good drink.
Here's a fun collection suited for battle, victories, new friends
and well wishing; many of which could be hotkeyed for in-game use.
Most of the toasts (some with minor modifications) come from a great site,
- check out Tom's World--Sláinte! Toasts, Blessings, and Sayings.
Thanks to Tom Donaghue for giving us permission to use these!

In Vino Veritas
In Cervesio Felicitas
("In wine there is wisdom, In beer there is joy.")
  

Slainte!
(Gaelic: To your health!)
  

Céad Míle Fáilte!
(Gaelic: One hundred thousand welcomes!)
  

On the chest of a barmaid in Rivervale
Were tattooed the prices of ale.
And on her behind,
For the sake of the blind,
Was the same information in Braille!
  

Let's drink to Kaladim, not far from the sea,
Where a woman's ass, and a whiskey glass,
Made a horse's ass of me.
  

For Kelethin hath no sober man,
Or none of milk sop thinkers,
And no philosophical fools,
But great and glorious drinkers!
  

The Gods invented Ale
to keep the Armigers from ruling Norrath.
  

An Armiger is never drunk as long as
He can hold onto one blade of grass and not
Fall off the face of the earth.
  

Four blessings upon you...
Older whiskey
Younger women
Faster horses
More money
  

Here's to being single...
drinking doubles...
and seeing triple!
  

There are several good reasons for drinking
and one has just entered my head.
If a man can't drink when he's living,
then how the hell can he drink when he's dead!
  

Be one who drinks the finest of ales.
Every day without fail.
Even when you have drank enough,
Remember that ale is wonderful stuff.
  

Merry met, and merry part,
I drink to thee with all my heart.
  

Here's to those who wish us well,
as for the rest, they can go to Hell!
  

May misfortune follow you the rest of your life,
but never catch up.
  

Here’s to a sweetheart, a bottle, and a friend.
The first beautiful, the second full, the last ever faithful.
  

May your home always be too small to hold all your friends.
  

May your laugh, your love and your wine be plenty,
thus your happiness will be nothing less.
  

Here’s a toast to your enemies’ enemies!
  

A toast to your coffin.
May it be made of 100 year old oak.
And may we plant the tree together, tomorrow.
  

Who is a friend but someone to toast,
Someone to gibe, someone to roast.
My friends are the best friends
Loyal, willing and able.
Now let’s get to drinking!
Glasses off the table!
  

Here’s to a fellow who smiles
When life runs along like a song.
And here’s to the lad who can smile
When everything goes dead wrong.
  

Here's to cheating, stealing, fighting, and drinking.
If you cheat, may you cheat death.
If you steal, may you steal a woman's heart.
If you fight, may you fight for a brother.
And if you drink, may you drink with me
  

Here’s to a long life and a merry one.
A quick death and an easy one.
A pretty girl and an honest one.
A cold beer—and another one!
  

Here’s to fine wine, women, and song.
And here’s to workdays that aren’t too long.
Here’s to shoes that always fit.
And here’s to you, you silly shit!
  

May the winds of fortune sail you,
May you sail a gentle sea.
May it always be the other guy
who says, "this drink's on me."
  

For every wound, a balm.
For every sorrow, cheer.
For every storm, a calm.
For every thirst, a beer.
  

When the hour is nigh me,
Let me in a tavern die,
With a tankard by me.
--Archpoet, "Confesio," 12th century
