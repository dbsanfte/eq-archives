        Proposed Motto, Charter and other information gleaned from the discussions on the Message Board

        Guild Motto:
        Virtus Aquirt Honorem (Virtue Procures Honor)

        Guild charter
        The Adventurers' Phalanx will endeavor to promote fair play, honor, and enjoyment for all citizens of Norrath. We will defend our homes with vigor against any enemy. We will seek knowledge where it may be found, and we will strive to promote commerce throughout the world. We will give aid to those in need, and adventure where we may. Above all, we will work to improve ourselves for the betterment of our guild. The ideal member of our guild would also be an ideal candidate for a Guide in the world of Norrath.

        Guild Code of Conduct - Proposed
        Violations of the code by guild members are to be reported to Telchar , at least until an official Enforcer of Rules is appointed.

        1. Assist in battle.
          If a fellow guild member's life is threatened and you can safely assist, you must do so (waived if said member were doing something in violation of the rules of conduct).
        2. Heal, Guide and Buff.
          Help those who ask for healing, buffs, binding or assistance whenever possible. Charge as you wish to those over level 5, give preference (and perhaps a discount) to guild members.
        3. Selling Items.
          When auctioning, offer your item to guild members first, then public auction. Set your price as you wish.
        4. Act Honorably.
          Do not commit any acts which will knowingly bring dishonor to the guild (kill stealing, foul language, unnecessary shouting, etc). Tell directly to someone that has offended you or another guild member, avoid shouting.
        5. Offer Services.
          Upon entering a city, it is a courtesy to announce your presence to the highest ranking guild member in said city.
        6. Participate.
          Make every effort to participate in guild events, contribute to guild knowledge and membership and generate ideas.
        7. Recruit.
          Be on the look out for good potential guild members and invite them to visit our page and consider joining.

        Guild Membership Process - Proposed

        • All guild members can suggest or invite people to join the Legion.
        • You must fight and adventure with them with at least one other guild member for 2 real-time hours.
        • Then you both send a report to the Recruitment Officer for approval. (Level, class, etc. should be considered in recommendations)
        • Their name would be posted for all guild members to see and comment on (a way to catch known kill-stealers, etc.), then they'd be approved.
        • Prospective new members can be tested by anyone in the guild - approach them asking for help with a low level character, etc. and comments sent to Recruitment Officer.
        • Entire recruitment process should take less than one week as a maximum.
        Later, as we get larger, we may want to tighten this up more, perhaps have 3 guild members fight with them on two different occasions.

        Guild Dues and Treasurer Role - Proposed

        • We pay dues per real-time month to the guild treasurer.
        • Suggested starting dues level: 1gold per level (So a level 2 character would pay 2gp per month dues, a level 17 character would pay 1pp 7gp per month)
        • The treasurer will keep all monies for the guild. To keep the guild money separate from his personal fortune, the Treasurer will create an separate character to act as a banker.
        • Treasurer may grant loans to guild members at a standard interest rate, to be determined. Perhaps 10% to make calculations easy?

        Guild Positions - Proposed

        • Leader: Role is to guide guild operations, suggest new guild activities, manage filling of other officer positions and take care of any correspondence with Verant.
        • Recruitment Officer: Role is to take comments on prospective members, review and approve or disapprove membership.
        • Events and Communications: Role is to maintain guild communications - web site, mailing list, etc. and to plan and aid in implementing guild events. (This could be combined with Recruitment at the start...then broken out later)
        • Treasurer: Obviously a person of demonstrated virtue and honesty, but also someone good at organizing and able to be online at specified times to allow for exchanges, etc.
          Duties - outlined above under Dues section.
        • Armorer/Weaponsmaster/Loremaster/Tradesmaster (four roles here, they could be combined at the start and broken out as we have volunteers): These officers will be responsible for holding onto the guild's armor, weapons, and research components and books. Ideally, guild members should purchase rare items whenever possible for sale at a small profit to the guild, thus ensuring all guild members of a supply of needed equipment. These officers will, like the Treasurer, create extra characters to act as holders of these items.

          Armorer would become knowledgeable in various types of armor, what classes, races they are useful for, where they may be acquired, etc.

          Weaponsmaster would become knowledgeable in various weapons, what classes, races they are useful for, where they may be acquired, etc.

          Loremaster would become knowledgeable in the general area of magic, spell components, and the classes and races that may use them.

          Tradesmaster would become knowledgeable in trades like Baking, Pottery, Jewelcraft, etc.

          Miscellaneous Thoughts - Proposed

          *Warfare*
          It is possible in an extreme event that we will be called upon to defend the honor of our guild in a guild war. These PVP wars may only be begun on UNANIMOUS consent of all guild officers, or a two-thirds majority of ALL guild members. Guild members may opt out of the guild war (conscientious objection) and the opposing guild will be informed of these non-combatants.

          *Dissension*
          Disputes between guild members on any subject, including rights to a certain item in the guild inventory or a direction for a crusade direction for the guild as a whole must be solved by non-lethal duels.

          Disputing guild members will face each other in combat until one yields or is unconscious.
