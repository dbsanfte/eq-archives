Kelek's Revenge

Kelek was at the last of his rope, but it had been an easy climb to the top of the building. He paused on the windowsill and scanned the dark room beyond. His low light vision, a trait of Half-Elves, was enough to tell him there were no threats present. With the skill of his namesake, Kelek Quickfingers picked the lock with ease, and slipped silently into the room.

Like death he crept from shadow to shadow, searching for his prey. With his keen ears he heard heavy footfalls coming his direction. With the grace of a cat, Kelek slipped silently into the nearest shadow. The door opened and he saw a huge man enter the room. Kelek had purposely left the window slightly open, a gamble that did not always pay off, but this time it did. The man walked right to the window, and paused to look outside. Kelek, silent as death, crept up behind him. Even though the huge man did not know it, death was upon him, smiling at him from behind. Kelek swung, and with a slight thud dropped the man with his sap. He had entertained the idea of killing him, but that was not his style, and this man had done nothing to deserve death. Kelek quickly dragged the man to a corner of the room, and preceded though the open door.

He stopped at a large gaudy wooden door, and thought, "This must be the place". He placed his ear to the door and listened. Snoring was all that greeted him, and with a smile, he entered the room. He silently shut the door behind him, and swiftly moved next to a bed in the middle of the room. Sleeping on the bed was Rolo, a real slime. Several months ago, Rolo had been snooping around the dock area, looking to hire a skilled Rogue. That is when he ran into Kelek. He hired Kelek to retrieve a piece of jewelry, a necklace with a huge gem in the middle. It was gaudy, just like Rolo. The theft was easy, but when Rolo refused to pay Kelek, Rolo had his thugs attack him and take the necklace. As if that was not enough, he told Slagg, the original owner of the piece, that Kelek was responsible for the theft. Kelek was not interested in the money, but rather the challenge of getting into Slagg's home, a fortress in itself, but when Slagg's men tracked Kelek down, he was in a bind, and the only way to save his hide was to agree to Slagg's terms - kill Rolo, and return his property.

With his dagger ready to strike, Kelek bent low over the fat form of Rolo. With one hand on the fat man's mouth and the dagger pressed next to his throat, Kelek shook Rolo from his slumber. Rolo's eyes snapped open, and horror played across his face. With his cloak pulled over his head, Kelek spoke.

"Hello Rolo. I have come to kill you." Rolo's eyes went wide. "I want the necklace, and I want it now. Slagg is quite displeased. When I have it, we will discuss your life."

A smile played across Kelek's face. He was enjoying himself. He had no intention of killing Rolo. Well, at least not yet. Rolo and Slagg represented the scum in the city, and with time, all would be forgotten. They would have more jobs for Kelek and his friend Ralgus. Rolo was a greedy coward, but received warnings well. He would not retaliate, and would remember well the night his life was in Kelek's hands.

Rolo eyed a small box next to the bed. Kelek removed his hand from Rolo's mouth, and opened the box. There it was, his prize. He nimbly placed it in his pouch and moved in close to Rolo.

"So what should I do now, fat man? A quick flick of my wrist, and your life would spill from your veins.", Kelek teased.

"No no, please... I should have known not to cross you Kelek. Please forgive me, I will work it out with Slagg, honest!" Rolo stammered.

"Why should I trust you? I end your life now, and I would be rid of you." Kelek said flatly.

"But I know a secret. A rich merchant will be returning to the city soon, with an ancient treasure from the Galin Dynasty. More wealth then you can dream of." Rolo said in desperation.

"Hmmm. Who is this man?" Kelek asked. Rolo motioned to the drawer in the nightstand next to his bed. Kelek opened the drawer and removed a letter. A quick glance the letter revealed times and places, names and dates.

"Your life is spared Rolo Baumhousen. I take my leave."

At that moment the bedroom door flung open, and there stood the huge man from earlier. He rubbed a large knot on the back of his head, and anger played across his face. In rage he rushed in. Kelek easily slipped to the side as the large man barreled though, crashing to the bed. Rolo went flying to the ground. Kelek's rapier was out now, and he stood ready to strike.

"I was just leaving, no need to show me out!"

But a growl of rage was the huge man's reply. He pulled a sword from his belt and attacked with a wild swing. Kelek dodged easily, and returned his strike with a pierce to the leg. The huge man growled in pain, and struck again with an overhand chop. Again Kelek was too quick, and the huge man's sword was buried deep into the wooden floor. The huge man struggled with removing the sword from the floor, but Kelek was not going to give him a chance and struck again, this time a poke to his upper weapon arm. The pain caused the huge man to release his grip on the sword, and with a kick to his head Kelek knocked him to the floor and fled.

Later, when Kelek met Ralgus, they studied the map with eager eyes.

"Do ye think this map be worth Rolo's life?" Ralgus asked.

"We will see my friend, we will see." Kelek answered.
