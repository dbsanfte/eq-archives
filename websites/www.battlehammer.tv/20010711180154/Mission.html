Our mission is to combat the threat of evil, while helping our people grow as a dominant force in all of Norrath. We believe that evil magic is an untrustworthy source of power, and those who use this power can be easily corrupted. We believe in BATTLE and WAR, and will lay down our very lives to protect and rid the world of the "Dark races" and their vile plans to despoil the good people of Norrath. Strength is one of our driving goals. Strength through adventure, and the desire to seek out and find others who share our desire! Constant training, through contests of might within the guild are commonplace. In order to defeat your enemies, you must know your allies' strengths and weaknesses and how to better use them in battle! When the battle is done, we will hold our tankards high and roar in VICTORY! Long live Clan BattleHammer!

Codes of Conduct:

1: No Barbarian may purposely harm citizens of Halas. (You may if you wish hunt other races, but be prepared for the faction loss. This belief does not include "Evil" Barbarians, such as Basil and Paglin for the Langseax quest, or any Barbarians who are Bandits or Dervish Cutthroats. Bloodguard members are encouraged to follow this rule, but they do not have to, just don't let us catch you hunting our people.)

2: No more then 1 alternate character in the guild. All alternates MUST be level 15 or higher.

3: Members of Clan BattleHammer consider Dark Elves and other "Dark Races" to be enemies of Halas, and should be treated as such! (I know that for the most part you may have friends that are Dark Elves, Ogres, and Trolls, and this is ok, but I would like to make raids into the Dark Elf forest, as a guild! We have members in the Bloodguard, only because they have earned our trust.)

4: Members of Clan BattleHammer consider Dwarves as allies of Halas. Dwarves are the only race we would consider equals to Barbarians, They refrain from the use of Evil Magic, and they are mighty warriors!

5: Only the STRONG survive! Raids into the lands of evil are encouraged.

6: This is more of a suggestion. Role-play! Role-play your Barbarian as a gruff and mighty warrior, be proud of your race, and make sure others know how proud you are, but do not disrespect others. I have found that when you role-play others around you begin to role-play with you, and the game is that much more fun!

7: Be nice! Represent BattleHammer well, we are good players, who respect others, and help others, play that way!

8: Guild Chat is to be used to communicate with guild members, about the game, grouping, and friendly rivalries. DO NOT use guild chat to disrespect the guild or its members. If you break this rule, you will be warned, and you will have 3 warnings, and then you will be removed from the guild. If you have a problem with a guild member, contact an officer, and explain the situation to him/her.

9: Have fun!
