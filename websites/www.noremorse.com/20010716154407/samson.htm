Sampson Simpson "The Berserk of Doom"

Hiyas! I'm Samsonsimpson, and my friends tell me Sam. My original name was Samson Simpson, but when I entered in Norrath my perception about it was changed to Samsonsimpson (Verant sucks!).

 

I was born in a little town of the Hollow Lands called Bluecloud's Hills. In my teen age I joined a party of adventurers in search of richness and fame. When I left the town, I was leading the Rogues' Guild of there (the guild was composed for me and an old friend who fired me). With my new friends I lived some nice adventurers, but I don't want to bore you explaining on them.

 

Only say you the first of them: when all my party was slept (was my turn to do guard, but I was reaaaaally tired) we were attacked by a troll (not a Norrath's one, the trolls of the Hollow Lands are really ugly, and without brain, like Fabel), was our first hard fight, but in the critical instant I jumped in the back of the troll and pushed my short sword through its neck. The mob falled to the ground and my groupmates could take fire on it. Was my first kill, and I took the surname "The Berserk of Doom". But that happened too many years ago.

 

About two years after left BC's, my group found a cave with some strange fog on the entrance. It seemed extremely dangerous, and for that reason we entered in. After a few seconds of lights'n'sounds, we found us in a strange world... in the existence plain called Forgotten Realms. We found some great adventures there too, and I found some new friends: two intelligent swords (cool both, but they were ever discussing between) and a efreeti, some kind of genius of Air. We traded our musical instruments (I was a famous pan flute-player years ago): my pan flute for a whistle. A nice whistle who gives me the power to call my new friend once per year.

 

Being missing my old town, I created a new settlement and called it as mine: Bluecloud's Hills. There I developed some jobs, in this order: major, sheriff, rogue's guild leader, drunkard, begger, mendicant. But the greed of my co-citizens was stronger than his love to me, and they fired me FROM MY OWN TOWN!! I returned to my globetrotter existence, having more fun, but.

 

A year ago, bored from the Realms, I used the whistle to ask my friend a favour: he hided all my stuff under a mountain in Forgotten Realms and tp'd me to a new plain of existence... To a wolrd called Norrath. In Norrath I found a lot of fabulous friends, but the rest of the history you know...

 

Love you all,

Samsonsimpson <Knights of no Remorse>
