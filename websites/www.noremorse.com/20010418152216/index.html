Declaration of Alliance
 
    Be it known that on this day of 12/19/2000 an alliance between the honorable guilds KNIGHTS OF NO REMORSE and THE CURSE has been declared. In the spirit of brotherhood and justice for which both guilds stand, the following pledges will be observed by all members of the alliance.
1. Guild members will not harm, or by there actions allow to be harmed, alliance guild members.
2. Guild members will assist members of an alliance guild as if they were in the same guild.
3. Alliance members will not recruit or allow the joining of members from alliance guilds.
4. Alliance members will follow the anti-pk doctrines for which both guilds stand.
5. Alliance members will assist in the transferring of messages between guilds via guild chat, or tell.
6. Guild Officers are answerable only to the leaders of their corresponding guild.
7. Members of an opposite but allied guild are invited to guild events upon discretion of the leaders of the guild holding the event.
 
Agreed and attested to by guild leaders:
