Faction, how to get yours



Freeport, west, east, ec, and wc
Death fist belts plain and simple, for most it will take under 40 belts to the captain
on the balcony in the militia house. for ogres and possibily trolls you may need to do the
rebby's whiskers quest.
get a caster who can gate and if possible sow.
go to east freeport to the seafairers roost.
under the bar is a hidden door, go down this and throught the door at the bottom of the
stairs.
to your right is a door open it and hail the de inside, follow the leads she gives you til
you get the note. make a hotkey with the phrase on it,
e.g. "I will deliver the note to rebby" (I am not sure this is the phrase but its close)
bind yourself infront of her.
take the note to rebby and get his whiskers in return, do not give the
whiskers back to the de just keep collecting rebby's whiskers til you have 101 of them.

sneak your ogre through the tunnels and turn them in to the de with your ogre (or troll)
this should get you to dubious and now you can turn in df belts as per above.

North Freeport, ec, and wc
get a mule and give them 8 10 slot containers and 30 plat,(this should be enough even for
low charisma chara's) make sure they are welcome in kaladim.

get on the boat in efp,
take it to sister isle, get off and run to the big hut near the
docks, open the door and look behind it (the shes behind the now open door) quickly purchase
kiola nuts (they are in the top row look like acorns kinda) until you are full. run back to
the boat, yes you have time if you dont play around.

now go to kaladim and find tumpy irontoe (oh yeah you will need about 4 stacks of water
I would put 3 in the bank and carry one stack with me)
give mr. irontoe one water and one kiola nut. you can give him 2 nuts and 2 waters but you
have to have a water and a nut in the top 2 lots and the same on the bottom or youll mess
it up. he will give you a tumpy tonic in return, keep these.
return to freeport with your 78 (or so) tumpy tonics, give them to whoever wants the
nfp faction,
go to seafairers roost
when you enter the bar there should be one of the following standing at a table A female de,
a barbarian, a human paly, or if your one lucky basterd groflah steadheart.
I think (havent had a chance to test this yet) that groflah spawns in the bar at 9 pm on
friday (eq time) nights, he doesnt seem to spawn in the day time at all, so just kill place
holders until he does spawn, the de and the barb are fairly low lvl,(under 20th each)
give groflah 4 stacks of 4 tumpy tonics at a time until youre out. and you are now allied
with nfp. (fair warning Kill place holders only at night and the de will give some ugly fp
faction hits)


Neriak
you will need someone who can invis others to do this, somone who is welcome in neriak.
(or 22nd lvl or so to keep loktar from aggroing on ya if kos)
how to get your very own invis caster, get a friend to make a de enchanter.

load up on gls and ls's and go to sro and turn them in til you hit 4th.(the chanter that is)
sell the books and containers.
run like a scared little 4th lvl chanter in sro should.
stop at the zone mem gate and cast it.

invis the person who wants neriak faction and run like mad for third gate. (hint you should
learn the way intimately neriak is confusing as hell at first)

once in the library have the chanter go out and get you 40 pp worth of red wine (thats alot)

get the wine from the chanter and give loktar to`biath (close hes in the only room with a
door in the library)

put 4 stacks of 4 red wine in his trade window (DO NOT PRESS TRADE!)

tell the chanter yer ready and have them invis you while the window is open

once invis press trade

do this the first 3 or so times you turn in wine then break your invis (however you want to)
and con him

once hes indiff or better just turn in all the wine you have then find a guard and con him

be warned it could take another 40 pp (or more depending on who you worship) worth of
wine to get amiable with the guards.
the necs, rouges, clerics, casters, and warriors are
all on their own faction but mostly dont roam around much
psan punox(sp) walks from the clerics guild to third gate propper and appears to be on his
own faction...he hates EVERYONE (amusingly enough my barb has better faction with him
than my de's do)

Qeynos,
if your indiff to the qeynos rogues you should start by saying to the guild leader
"carson has a mole in the high pass militia" just say it over and over for good faction hits
then after getting to warmly there go to one of the buildings on the docks and find
captain soandso (inside a building hes not a guard) and he likes brandy, cant remember
how much but its a good amount, and will get you good faction with half of the qeynos guards.
the other half (the corrupt guards) are a bit more difficult, find trumpy irontoe and give
him honey mead...ALOT of honey mead, he needs 4 to get one faction hit so when you give him
4 stacks of 4 you only get 4 faction hits. it becomes a balancing act as you need to keep
good faction with 2 groups but the captain gives you better hits so it wont be to much
trouble.


Highpass/Highkeep
in the qeynos rogues rogues guild you have been telling zanns (or is it hanns?) about the
mole now do what he tells you, go to hanns (or Zanns) and tell him what the first guy said
to say. said to say, he will give you a note, this note is not nodrop nore is it lore. so
load up on them you will need perhaps 16 of them. go to highpass from the keep you go right
and stay right to find the golden rooster, once inside you will find prak on the first floor
in front or more likely in the back, con him. he wont do the quest if your dubious, I am
not sure about apprehensive, so if needed bust out that pled enchanter and do the same as
you did in neriak.


Rivervale
if your kos to the clerics and cant invis/fd your pretty much gonna have troubles in this
town on the upside faction is just a few (hundred) bandaids away. get a pile of bandaids
like 2 maybe 3 10 slots full and head out to misty thicket, from rivervale take a left and
follow the wall till you find a hut in the corner. there is a little halfling in there just
hail him he will say OWWWWW or some crap, give him 4 stacks of 4 bandaids until warmly.

buncha elf faction
pandos flintside wants muffins, so give him say 700 if your glares. I dont even know who
all this helps you with but its good with the sisters on sister isle.

there ya go quick easy faction for the pvp masses
