    Welcome to the Unbridled Aggression guild home page. UA is an Everquest guild located on the Tribunal Server. Please have a browse around and feel free to sign the guestbook. If you're a tribunal player and want to join the guild, give one of the officers a /tell in game. Officers are listed off the members section.

    If you are a current guildmember and want to get a username and password to join the members list, send Elb some e-mail. Send your real name, contact email and character details (name, level, race etc...).
