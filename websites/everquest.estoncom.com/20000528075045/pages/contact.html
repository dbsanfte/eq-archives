Pic/Screenshot Submission

To submit a picture of yourself or a screenshot simply attach the image (in .gif or .jpg format) to an email including your player name, class and race (optional), real name (optional), and a description of what was happening during the pic/screenshot if you like. I may edit the image or description so that it "fits" well. If need be I can reformat your .bmp to a .jpg.
Example:

My name: Jacob Giddens
Player name: Tiyr
Class: Ranger
Race: Half-Elf
Description: This screenshot was taken in the Qeynos catacombs. I call it "Smuggler SuperMob"....
(attached file: screenshot.jpg).

Submit a pic/screenshot to tiyr@estoncom.com
Hint Submission

To submit a hint please include as much detail as you can (area/zone, suggested level, etc.) Leave me your player name if you want credit, or specify player name as Anonymous. I'm really big on spelling and grammer so if that's not your strong point I may modify your submission to make it all pretty like. Hints can include quest spoilers or just suggestions on good spawning points for a certain level character.
Example:

Player name:Anonymous
Hint: Gnoll pups are great for newbies in the Qeynos area. Keep the teeth even though you can't sell them. They are actually part of a quest that is really easy to do and can end up being profitable for a young adventurer (around 5 silver per tooth). What you do first is take the teeth to.....

Submit a hint to tiyr@estoncom.com
Story Submission

This one is really up to you. If you like include your character name, class, race, etc. I probably won't take too much time editing these stories so please be careful with your spelling and such. Try not to get too crazy with your stories; anything in bad taste won't be posted. If formatting is important (paragraph breaks, etc.) make sure you make it obvious in the email.
Example:

Player name: Yoda
Class: Jedi Master
Race: Little green thing
Story: A long long time ago, in a galaxy far far away....(of course your story should be EverQuest related)

Submit a story to tiyr@estoncom.com
Trade Master Submission

Consider this the yellow pages for people who spend a good deal of time selling items, as opposed to a quick turn-around trading post. Please leave your character name so people know who to try to contact. List what items you generally have for sale and their prices if you like. It might also be a good idea to say what area/zones you are usually in so you won't get /tells from the other side of the world from people who don't plan on travelling to you.
Example:

Player name: Tiyr
Items:Banded helm - 6pp
         Banded leggings - 2cp
Location: Usually around Freeport, will travel some for large sales, otherwise forget it.

Submit your trade master listing to tiyr@estoncom.com
