Welcome to the EverQuest site that is dedicated to the adventurers of Erollisi Marr.
On this site you will find player pics, screenshots, item lists, player hints, trade masters (players advertising their commonly sold/traded items), and more to come all the time.

On Erollisi Marr I am a ranger that goes by the name of Tiyr. I love a strong sense of community and hope this site can add to that. I'm always willing to help out others (as long as they aren't little twits) so feel free to /tell me anytime if you need a hand.

This site is still in the infancy stage, but I hope that it will soon be able to make your adventuring days on Erollisi Marr more enjoyable. Please leave me feedback on what you like or don't like about the site and any suggestions on what could be added to make this site more useful.
