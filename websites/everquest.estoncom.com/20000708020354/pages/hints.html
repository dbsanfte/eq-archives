Player name: Chumas
Hint: This one is for newbies in need of cash and you're itching to get rid of that SOW you picked off a dead skeleton. First, find a Sharpening Stone (about 3-5 copper), usually available in a smithy. Buy two. Head outside to the forge (looks like a garbage can) and right click on it. Put in the SOW and the sharpening stones and hit Combine. If the gods of Norrath are pleased with you, you'll get a Tarnished Weapon instead of just Rusty. The sharpening to Tarnished will decrease your attack delay and increase the weapons selling price.

Quest: When you obtain gnoll teeth, do not destroy them. Instead, either bring them to Qeynos or Halas. In Qeynos, bring them to the Captain Guard (Captain Tillin) guy in the Arena. He will give you a moonstone per tooth. When giving them to him, only give him one per give slot, or you will lose them. Take the Moonstone, and go the Fish's Ale. Go to the back room and find a bartender, I forgot the name (Mcneal Jocub). Give him the moonstones. He will give you Black Burrow Stout, which you can drink or sell for about 7 sp with good faction.
In Halas, find the Woman trainer (Lysbith McNaff) in the warrior's guild. Give her 3 at a time. With a high enough faction, she will give 1 pp and iron ration for each 3 teeth you give! The only problem is getting lost in Everfrost (being human I did that a lot). I learned that if you "hug" the left wall, you will be fine.
Player name: Lamil
Hint: Just a suggestion, when you are auctioning for a item that you might not have enough money for, role play. You could say something like this, if you wanted a FBR. "Any fine adventurers out there who have happened to obtain a FBR in their recent travels and would be willing to sell it to a low level character in dire need of such an item but without large sums of money at my disposal. Please give ma a tell. Thank you!" This would probably help you get the item you are looking for. I have not been many places, but I have discovered that this method works very well in Greater Faydark, as the people there are very friendly. Also, I would suggest lower level players, because a level 40 player would pity a level 10 player, but not a level 39 player.
