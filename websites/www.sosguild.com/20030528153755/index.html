Need Mages
We arent uber, We arent great big loot whores, and we arent your ticket to being uberfied over night. We want to have fun.

Now that is out of the way, we really need a few Mages who play consistently. Its very frustrating to have to log on a mule parked in a special zone, suicide him, log in a rezzer camped in same zone, rez in, gate mage out, COH for 2.5 hours as people trickle in for long raids, then rez him back in, camp and close CoH for the night telling members to go exp or something because no mages are online. It is also VERY frustrating that the guild has to cancel events because we NEED a mage to get someone out of a tricky situation and we cant.

Now dont get me wrong. Mages are MUCH MORE than CoH or Vending Machines. Damn good DPS too. You have a very needed role and right now, we are suffering bad. Everyone has a role in this game, and realizing that a mages CoH gets shit done faster is important. You also help things die faster, and provide other benefits to the guild in your summoned items. Talk to Ttam or Verash, our two mages and ask them if we make them sit around and COH and hand shit out then ignore them. Two mages who are well taken care of just slightly busy in other things as well and cannot be expected to be there 24/7.

So unless you are a mage, dont apply. That is all we are currently seeking.
