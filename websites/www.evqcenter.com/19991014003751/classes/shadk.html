Shadow Knight

Mykil's View
The stormy night sees very few travelers. The rain, paired with the fear of death by lightning or the claws of the night monsters, generally keeps the populace off the roads and in their beds where they think themselves safe. But a small group of dark people, faces and bodies concealed by their black shrouds, brave the storm towards the fulfillment of their equally dark purpose. As they near a small unsuspecting burg whose name has been forgotten amidst the mists of time, the one at point, their leader, stops. The others follow suit, and the leader pulls off its death shroud, revealing the face of a female dark elf. The others also pull of their shrouds, revealing the faces of humans, trolls, ogres, and other dark elves. In unison, they raise their arms and chant in voices that would freeze the bones of all but the strongest of warriors. And all at once, their numbers double as skeletons, ghouls, zombies, and spectres materialize around them from thin air or the ground. Then, the dark people pull out bows, knives, swords, and all manners of other types of weaponry, and with deadly silence they and their unnatural pets decsend into the unsuspecting village. The next day, the village is gone. Wiped off the face of Norrath, leaving only suspicious looking charred areas of ground behind. No one will miss it, as no one ever knew it was really there except for a few local fishermen who have been "taken care of." At night, ghouls and zombies now come out, with scores of skeletons besides. Skeletons are skeletons, but look closely at the faces of the other undead, and you will see what some of the inhabitants of this village used to look like.

Shadow Knights are the warrior order of necromancers who command the undead as well as varied fighting techniques. The "dark knights" of Norrath, this brotherhood's members range from those who would steal away maidens in ancient fairy tales to those who would raise the dead to dispose of innocent townspeople before adding them to their ranks. Sharing the powers of warriors and necromancers, the shadow knight is a fighter whose image inspires fear. It shares the shadows with rogues, often hiding within them to conceal their presence from townsfolk until they can walk about unnoticed. They can raise the dead just like their necromancer brothers, as well as share some of their vampiric tendencies, but come into their minimum magical potential later on in life than necromancers. They cannot raise the dead until rank 2 (level 15), but like the other hybrid classes, get a few special tricks of their own.

As warriors, they excel in melee combat forms. Able to learn the arts of the warrior, they can serve on the front lines and soak up some damage. When you need healing, summon a pet and set it at the enemy while you evade it and regain some HP, keeping the pet healed along the way. With a decent number of direct offensive spells at their disposal, they can serve as long distance spell casters who can destroy monsters that seek to flee the wrath of your group. Death Knights are often shunned by NPCs because of their social standing. Although they do not rise in power with the same speed of a necromancer, they still raise the dead, which is an unwholesome ability indeed. But, if necessary, they can always return to the shadows which they call home, and wait till nightfall to raise some undead assassins to deal with those who give them trouble.

Bottom Line
Shadow Knights are made for those people who want to play "dark knights." The image this character presents alone shows off the roleplay abilities of this character without even delving into its roots. One can roleplay the guardsmen of their necromancer masters, traveling accross the lands providing protection while their masters weave spells to cause all sorts of evil mischief. A group can claim a forest or region as theirs and raise a small army of undead to protect their claim from NPCs and PCs alike. Shadow Knights make the perfect terrorizers of the weak if you wish to play that type of character, and as people come out to end your life of terror, you can smite them with an undead minion to fight. For maximum enjoyment, a dark elf would provide excellent roleplay opportunities, especially if you decided to play a good dark elf. Imagine the surprise on the faces of people, NPCs and PCs, when they see a dark elf shadow knight, sworn enemy of all that is good and wholesome, take down that beast that was about to wipe out an entire herd of cows. Shadow Knights, however, are creatures of mystery. No one knows whether they are really an order of necromancer warriors, or powerful undead servants pulled from the earth in the same manner as a ghoul or spectre. Perhaps, maybe, they are both. Beware them nonetheless, lest ye find yeself in the ranks of their undead armies.
