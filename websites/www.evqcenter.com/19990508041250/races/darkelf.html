Dark Elf

Mykil's View
Evil born of goodness is the cryptic way of putting the origins of the dark elves. They are the products of ages of repression, hate, and fear. The Tier'Dal, as they are properly known, are the children of the Prince of Hate Innoruuk's rage and hatred, and his children mimic his feelings in their lives. Created from the original elven king and queen whom Tunare, The Great Mother, planted on Norrath as part of her pact with Brell Serelis and Prexus, most Dark Elves are as sadistic and evil as high elven and sylvan kind are kind and good. Their darkness is expressed in all places where they roam. It permeates every pebble in Neriak, their labyrinthine cave city, and follows them to wherever they go, spreading the shadow and Innoruuk with their presence and actions of pure hatred.

The dark elves are a beautiful race. Their elegance is unmatched by any but the highest of elven kind, and their physical beauty is reminiscent of their sylvan (wood elf) cousins. Their city, Neriak, is the darkest and gloomiest of habitats in Norrath. Every step into that hole in the mountain is more depressing as the last. Yet the city has an alien beauty matched only by the beauty of its inhabitants. It is a gloomy place, yet how the dark elves have adapted to the dark is unknown. Have they developed ultravision slowly over eons of living in caves such as this? Or was this clarity of sight a gift from Innoruuk to ensure their faithfulness and enable them to do evil in the concealment of darkness? Either case is just as likely as the other. Their continued evil over the centuries is not a surprising thing. The gloominess of their cavern colors the soul if one stays within its shadows too long. Most dark elves have been colored too thickly with its depression to ever see the light of good again.

Ages of isolation and depression in their underground city have not uncivilized the dark elf. On the contrary, the dark elves are merely evil, not uncivilized, and their minds are highly developed. They make excellent spell casters with their high intelligence and wisdom. Their abilities as rogues are also legendary, as their ability to see flawlessly in the dark enables them to do their dark work, with less chance of being noticed, at night, and their agility enables them to evade most attacks in the event that they are noticed. Creatures of darkness, they are at home in the absence of light, making them the perfect thieves and brigands. Yet this comes at a price, as they are intolerant to intense light. Yet beware, because as surely as there is a thief lurking in your shadow, even during the day, the chance of that thief being a skilled dark elf rogue is equally as great. Woe betide you if you don't keep one eye on your back in the presence of dark elves.

Bottom Line
Spell casters and rogues are the callings of a true dark elf. Creatures of darkness, they can make use of the shadows to exact the use of their skills on anyone unwise enough to not watch his/her back. It is for this reason that the dark elves are branded as evil and uncivilized by other races, for their abilities inspire fear in even the hardiest of men. Only the trolls and ogres consider them friends, and the reason for that can be either a common situation of being considered "evil," or that the ogres and trolls are too inept to realize how much more evil most dark elves really are than them. Whatever the case, there are few races that the dark elves consider friends themselves. Roleplaying possibilities are interesting for this race. Being considered "evil" the first thing to come to mind would be that these characters were the type to slaughter townsfolk and kill soldiers in defiance to the law. But actually more interesting twists can be taken. One could roleplay the introverted dark elven who, although friendly enough, keeps a lot to himself. While posing as a "good person," you could secretly plot against the time when you have enough power to reveal your nature. You could also roleplay a lone assassin for hire, evil because of his dedication to Innoruuk as well as the money people pay it to kill their enemies in cold blood. Or, better yet, if you want a challenge, roleplay a good dark elf. You heard correctly. This is something that might be worth trying. Although dark elves are idealy suited to the lives of spell casters and rogues, they also make very solid warriors. Although people would argue this point based on their strength and stamina, their agility is high. Being able to avoid a blow is better than being able to withstand it, and when a warrior's staple power skills such as dual wield come along, they will be able to attack with double frequency. With this in mind, you could set out to become a legendary dark elf warrior or fighter-magi hybrid like a shadow knight, championing Innoruuk and his hatred wherever you go.
