House Quests

All these quests can only be done once for the points and all will usually need a picture of when you turn in the items to the NPC to show that you have completed and finished the quest.

1. Travel to the Steamfont Mountains and battle the minotaurs within their caves to obtain one of their axes. Must get a picture of yourself fighting the minotaurs. Then turn in the axe to an officer. (points 150)

2. Get the letter from Kizdean Gix to turn into Loveal S'Nez (25)

3. Gather 10 Fire Beetle Eyes for Noxhil V'Sek, take a picture of the 10 fire beetles before turning them in to Noxhil (25)

4. Gather 4 Kerra Isle Beetle eyes, and one Mammoth tusk to be given to Noxhil V'Sek (350)

5. Trizan N'Tan needs you to get 4 orc pawn picks (25)

6. Find Vurgo within the Temple of Solusek Ro and complete for him the task to obtain the Harvester (500) info here

7. Talk to Sylina within the Temple of Solusek Ro and complete the tasks for her to obtain the Robe of Enshroudment. (600) info here

8. Go to Lon the Redeemed in the Temple of Solusek Ro; he will want you to find to gems, one of Stamina the other of Righteousness follow the tasks set upon you to gain the Burning Rapier (900) info here

9. Talk to Sylina within the Temple of Solusek Ro and complete the tasks for her to obtain the Shadowbound Gloves (400) info here

10. Talk to Sylina within the Temple of Solusek Ro and complete the tasks for her to obtain the Shadowbound Boots (400) info here
