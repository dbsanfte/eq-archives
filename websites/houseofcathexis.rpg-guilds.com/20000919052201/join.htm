Joining the House

How to join

Ogre,Tier'dal,Troll- Meet the evil intent based on race

All others- A demonstration of loyalty to the ideas of the guild must be proven via quest

Exception - NO Paladin or Druid may be admitted....all other classes and races are taken on individual circumstances.

There will be an admission process (a quest)...concieved and ran by the LoreMaster and his/her assistant. After the process there will be a vote....if the vote is favorable the new member will be admited for a probationary period which is 30 days...after passing the probabtion period the new member will then be given full membership with all priviledges and responsibilities.

There shall be no more than 30 members in the guild.

New Members

Regarding the application of new members - Any guildmember shall have the responsibility of forwarding prospects to the Master of Arms. No invitation shall be made without the consent of the Master of Arms.

No removeal shall be made without the consensus of the Senior Officers.
