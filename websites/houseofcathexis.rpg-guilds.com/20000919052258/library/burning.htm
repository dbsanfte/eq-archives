Burning Rapier

ThThe Burning Rapier Quest

 1) Goto Lon the Redeemed in the temple of Solusk Ro. He wants you to quest to find 2 gems       (Stamina and Righteousness) which you are to give to Ortalius in S. Ro. Talk to Lon and  he sends you with a note to his friend the prisoner.    

2) Goto find Conium Darkblade in Dagnor's Cauldron(on isle in the middle of lake). Give him  the note and he gives you an axe called " never stops chopping" and a clue about the top  of the world.    

3) Goto HH, and find Bryan on the deck of the lumbermill. Give him the axe and he gives you a container to fill with 10 different beverages from around the world. (be careful here, he killed me just after giving me this container for i had no faction there). These are:  

 Underfoot Triple Boch -- Kalidim, Trumpy Irontoe      

Tunare's Finest -- Felwithe, Wela Muselender      

Vasty Deep Ale -- Sister Isle in oot or in Erudin      

Lendel's Grand Lauger-- Rivervale, Mac Deeppockets      

Gator Gulp Ale-- E. Commons Tunnel, Bubar      

Frozen Toe Rum --- Halas, Scon Macdaniel      

Oblong Bottle(lore item/ no drop)-- Loc is n1611, p2924 on the ocean floor  

BloodSpirits-- Mistmoore, a "hemo enologist" in the garden(surrounded by 8 others)      

Clockwork Oil Stout --- Sol a, Clockwork CX ( this is pickable)      

Blackburrow Swig ---- need a blackburrow cask, found on gnoll

brewers/master brewers in Blackburrow, a common spawn at loc p32,p32 on level 3 by the pond. Place-holder is a gnoll guardsman.

Combine in the Blackburrow cask(no skill required) the following:  

 4 BB skins- rare drops off elite and commander gnolls(can buy for 5 pp off of  PC's)  

2 BB gnoll scalps(off of pups) 2 casks of OL' TJ's fierce brew  The BB cask disappears after combining the ingredients correctly to make the swig.    

4) Goto Bryan, give him the container(after combining the beverages i believe) and he gives  you the Gem of Stamina.(note: Bryan did not kill me here for i must have built up enough faction)       

5) Goto the sewers of EFP and find Toxdil. Ask him about gems, then about Gem Case. He  tells you that he has a key, but will only give it to for the gem inside the case. He says he  sold it to a jeweler( i skipped this step)         

6) Goto Noresa Sparkle in WC. She asks you to save her from Kiz Gix. She also tells you of a gem case which you will need 2 keys to open, and would have to return both keys and the case to the 'original boxcrafter' to find out what's inside.          

7) Kiz Gix roams around befallen side of WC(usually by inn). Upon killing him, you receive a note in goblin, telling you of an outcast who stole a sparkling purse (i skipped this step  because of the faction hit and it is unnecessary)         

8) Go find the outcast( Lydia the exiled) in Sol A, in the High Shamna's room. Upon killing her, you get the sparkling purse.        

9) Return the purse to Noresa Sparkle and she gives you one of the keys needed to open   the gem case. Also buy a sparkling sapphire from her at this point for approx. 580 pp   (this is the gem that Toxdil wants)    

10) Find the Gem Case. It is hidden behind a locked door in the slums of EFP that you must  pick(approx. 150 skill is needed) It is guarded by a dog named Scrapps. Spawn time unknown( I found it as a bag on the floor in the room)    

11) Goto Toxdil and give him the sapphire you bought from Noresa, he gives you the other key necessary to open the gam case.    

12) Goto Bubar in  the E. commons tunnel and give him the gem case and both keys. He   opens the case and you recieve the Gem of Righteousness.    

13) Goto Ortalius in S. ro( NW corner of zone) and give him both gems. You reap the rewards
