Welcome, traveller ! My name is Tarron, a bard from the city of Freeport. Many cities have I seen on my travels, but none as beautifull and dangerous at the same time as the city of the Dark Elves: Neriak.
Join me and enter this strange and mysterious place, in which the shadows embrace you like a caring mother. But beware, for this city can be dangerous to the unprepared, and deadly to the unaware. Poison is like the kiss of death, and a silent blade is your comrade on your way to survival.
Come with me then, stranger, and experience a visit you will never forget.
