April 04, 2001 : SITE UPDATES
I have now got around to doing a first version of the new membership page. A number of our members have been deleted from this page but it is becoming increasingly obvious that a large number of our current members are either not listed or that we have insufficient information about them. I would therefore like everyone to check their details on the members page and email me with the following information.
* Full name
* Level
* Character picture (zipped bitmap or jpeg please preferably at 1024x768 or 800x600)
* Charcter Race, Class, Gender, Deity
* List of trade skills and level achieved in these
* Short character biography
* Email address
Please note that all this info is for the MAIN character you have in the guild. If you have additional characters in the guild then please let me know all these details for them too but please state that they are an alt and not a main. I also urge anyone guilding people to ensure that all of this informaiton is gathered when entering someone into the guild.
