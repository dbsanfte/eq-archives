Two people can get along.  (Even trolls) Get more than two and there are bound to be arguments and halfling-eating.  At the same time, a group of dedicated people can forge alliances that are strong in all areas, from combat to simply kicking off your shoes and having fun.  

Emerald Legacy is not a guild wrapped up in dozens of obscure rules.  We promote friendship, adventures, and of course, travels throughout Norrath.  However, as we grow, the need for certain rules becomes evident.  

1. Any member engaging in foul ethics will immediately be removed from the guild. Foul ethics are unacceptable and include kill-stealing, abusive or excessive foul language, and disruption of other player's game enjoyment. "I was role-playing." is not a reasonable excuse.

2. Members may have as many characters as they wish in Emerald Legacy  - names may be any they wish and need not be similar.

3. Members may belong to other guilds as well as Emerald Legacy.

4. While not a rule per se, members are encouraged to assist one another when they pass by such as by offering buffs, or even assistance in a particularly tough battle.

5. We do not have "official" guild meetings at the time of this writing, therefore, members are asked to use the message boards on this site to swap information, stories, and generally have fun. 

6. Raids, when conducted, have a tendancy to be brutal in nature.  Members are required to support one another and adhere to the raid plans as laid out prior to the raid.

7. "Uber-Item" quests and raids are subject to the loot order and/or procedures established prior to the raid.  In most instances, if the group consists of 3 Druids and a Paladin, and the raid nets a highly desirable item for Paladins only, members are expected to allow the Paladin to loot the item.  If a lottery or dice loot procedure is in place, then members should adhere to that rule.  Keep in mind that this only applies to your fellow guild members.  Emerald Legacy will not take any action in mixed guild groups.
