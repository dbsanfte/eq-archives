Wisten Evercross

Now, following the fall of the great Combine Empire, the Erudites became embroiled in a great war of magic. As the Erudite Necromancers fled to southern Odus, one woman, Evidra, fled the raging conflict and took to sea. Her small boat was tossed about the waves in the grip of a great storm. Finally, after many weeks at sea and near death, she came within sight of jagged rocks. The horrors of war were still fresh upon her mind and she prayed to Tunare for a quick death.

Tunare had taken pity on Evidra and beseeched the Ocean Lord Prexus to bring her to Faydwer. Prexus agreed. Unconscious and guided and sustained by Tunare and Prexus, Evidra eventually beached on the shores of Faydwer. A band of Dwarves, Wood-Elves, and High-Elves found Evidra and began ministering to her health. Delirious and rambling Evidra spoke of dark majiks and incredible power. It is rumored that at one point Evidra began speaking in the language of the Gods and all that were present gained the abilities to travel vast distances even as Erud had done.

Evidra had mastered all forms of magics, from necromancy to theurgism, yet as her strength grew, she became enamored with the forces of nature. Karana saw this and began instructing Evidra in heretofore-unknown magics. Evidra's power rivaled that of Erud, though without the fear and paranoia. With her band of followers, she was content to study the workings of nature in the vastness of Norrath. She and her band of followers, identified by a small emerald amulet, traveled from Faydwer to Kunark and watched the trials of the Iksar nation. It is rumored that upon their council, the Emerald Circle was formed to restore prosperity to the Iksar Empire. Upon the departure of Evidra and her party, the Iksars soon fell to disarray.

Evidra and her band travelled to Antonica, discovering the Halflings, much as Erud had done. Rather than be fearful of the inquisitive little Halflings, (who had a propensity to ignore some of the finer points of property ownership), they found the Halflings to be an amusing race, and a potentially powerful race. It is then that they began instructing the Halfings in the arts of magic. Halflings, like the Wood-Elves, became masters at transportation and constructed magical ports throughout Norrath. As the Age of Turmoil ensued, general peace settled over Norrath. Evidra, sensing that her many years on Norrath were drawing to a close, gathered her band about her and shared the last of the great secrets of the gods with them. Chesta, who had ever been Evidra's guardian and protector, and Mishele, having sustained her with powerful magics, lovingly and tearfully eased Evidra into her final sleep.

Innoruuk studied the activities of the Emerald Band and resolved to destroy this band. Mishele, a shaman of incredible power descended upon a group of Dark Elf necromancers who had been slaying halflings at the Nektulos Forest ring. Innoruuk became enraged at the thought of these mortals who had seen fit to dabble in the affairs of gods, and sent his greatest magic users in search of this elusive band of travelers. Rhac'Tull, a necromancer whose power was infused with the might of Innoruuk and Cazic-Thull summond legions of undead to begin the search. The halflings and wood-elves, already warring with goblins and orcs, formed alliances with virtually all other races to attempt to destroy this threat. To this day, the minions of Rhac'Tull still travel Norrath in their blind search for followers of the Emerald.  Ultimately, Rhac'Tull and his giant undead discovered Mishele and attacked.  Having been hidden by Mishele's powerful magic, Chesta erupted from the shadows and with a war cry heard throughout Norrath, laid waste to the minions of Rhac'Tull.  Chesta and Mishele, though wounded gravely, turned to face their nemesis.  In a flurry of magic and the ringing of steel, an epic battle ensued.  It has been said that Chesta's sword lit the night sky in flames, each slashing blow's energy so bright that even the gods had to avert their eyes.  Mishele weakened unto the point of death, cast her last spell in a vain attempt to heal Chesta's many wounds.  As Rhac'Tull gathered ethereal energy about him for yet another assault, Mishele finally succumbed to her grievous injuries.   With a cry of rage, Chesta launched herself at Rhac'Tull, but alas, while her heart was great and her determination unquestionable, she too fell before Rhac'Tull's relentless onslaught.  Emerging from the field victorious but weakened, Rhac'Tull presented the corpses to his followers.  Innoruuk was pleased and led Rhac'Tull to what some say is the core of Norrath so that he might receive his reward.  

Unbeknownst to either Innoruuk or Rhac'Tull, Mishele had given birth to a daughter in the frozen city of Halas, as had Chesta in the fishing village of Freeport.  It is there that her lineage was hidden from the watchful eyes of the Teir'Dal. Each first borne daughter to that clan was named Mishele and was instructed in the saving arts of the Shaman. Likewise, Chesta's family were sworn to protect Mishele and each daughter was trained in the arts of war, philosophy, and diplomacy.  Each generation of Chesta's family moved throughout Norrath, keeping and ever watchful eye on their Shaman charge.  After five hundred years, a girl-child was born whom, upon her first birthday, summoned flames to warm her tiny body against the unforgiving cold. It was then that all the descendants of the Emerald band began gathering at Chesta's instruction. As Mishele matured, she drew about her great warriors, wizards, druids, and casters of all types. In homage of their heritage, they called themselves the Emerald Legacy.

Now, be it known that Sassiful, the ever-watchful druid, studied the history of the epic contest between Rhac'Tull and Mishele and Chesta.  Great was her grief that her ancestors could not intervene, for surely they could have averted the disaster in the plains.  For generations, Sassiful's family sought out other druids with great potential and guided them in the use of magic that might someday be used to destroy the Teir'Dal.  Upon the re-joining of Mishele and Chesta's prodigy, Sassiful traveled to Halas in the company of the warrior Glymm, the druids Wisten, Darrj, and Slendergrass.  Joining them were the power shamans Asphen and Halora.  Together they began journeying Norrath, learning the forgotten secrets of magic and war.  Others soon joined the Emerald Legacy from all disciplines.

Even today, they bide their time, admitting all who would follow goodness and mercy for the greater good of Norrath, even to the inclusion of Dark Elves, Ogres, and Trolls. As they grow in numbers and power, Innoruuk and Cazic Thull tremble at their coming. For it is written that the Age of Reckoning is at hand and the Emerald Legacy shall be the harbingers of the destruction of the dark forces.

This is your call to power. The Emerald Legacy once again strides across Norrath, carrying the power of gods and the wisdom of the great crystalline dragons.
