Welcome! - 03/26/02 - Quella

 

A fond welcome to all of our recently added members to Carpe Nocturnus, and a welcome back to old friends Nunya and Vember! We have added a number of great new people to our ranks and look forward to the many days of grinding and raiding together to come!

After including all of our new folk, we find that our ranks have swelled to full capacity for the time being. We regret that we are no longer able to accept new applications from anyone interested in joing the Carpe family, and as of today we will not be adding new members to the Guild until a later time to be announced here. Guests, however, are always welcome to join in on our weekly Sunday afternoon and Wednesday evening raids.
