Member Survey - 05/07/02


 From time to time a question will come up within the guild that needs to be polled in order to get a majority opinion. Recently a matter has arisen that has the guild divided in their opinions and before the officers can make all the necessary decisions needed, we would like to get everyone's input.

All submissions shall be kept private, and the names of the people who sent the mail will be stripped off by the single recipient before information is disclosed to the rest of the officers and discussed. It is very important that the members state their true feelings on the matter or the poll will be useless.

Survey question:

How will you feel, good, bad, or indifferent, if Carpe recruits people of your class that are of a higher level than you are?

Examples:

Will you be excited by the new people?
Will you be unhappy that your specialness has decreased?
Does it matter to you at all either way?

Click here to answer the survey question, and please remember to include your character name so that we know who has yet to respond.
