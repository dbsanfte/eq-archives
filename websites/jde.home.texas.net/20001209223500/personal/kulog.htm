Kulog Kidlat

Order of the Fang

 

 

 

"Of Thunder and Lightning…"

Flashes of light followed by deafening explosions of thunder were the most vibrant memories of my youth. The southern portion of Felwithe, which contained the great Elven houses of Geomancy, was always enshrouded with this awesome display. I remember spending my childhood there, watching the great Magi hone there skills and at the same time pestering them with my impertinence.

My parents, seeing that I was gifted in mind and had quite an interest with the Arcane, decided to have me educated at one of those great houses of Geomancy. This is where Tarker, guildmaster of the House Wizardry took me in as one of his pupils. One would ask why I chose Tarker and the Wizards instead of the other houses. That choice was simple because in all my time that I had spent watching the great Magi perform their skills, Tarker and his pupils always displayed the finest and most wonderous displays of raw, sheer, magical power. The fact of the matter is that after watching his students perform (at ranges not always exactly safe to view from), the flashes of lightning and peals of thunder of their displays always left me in awestruck wonder (and on occasion stunned). However if one were to ask Tarker as to why he sponsored me, he would probably snort and say it was because my impertinence at bothering him and his students was best served in his classes rather than out in the practice yard.

And there I did study under Tarker’s watchful eye. Many a time did he say that I should stop being impertinent and pay attention to my sorcery (ah the memories!). I learned the great art of Geomancy as it pertained to Wizards and its craft. The ability to channel titanic proportions of raw, pure, power and unleashing it in numerous ways was the core of my studies. In my spare time (when Tarker had other pressing concerns other than to entertain my impertinence…) I had the time to study the history of Geomancy. It was here that I learned of the fallen Combine Empire whose magiks rivaled those of the Arch-Wyrms from the Age of Scale. That was the time I became extremely interested in finding ways to rediscover the lost power of the human mages that help establish the Combine Empire. And I secretly hoped that those discoveries would shed more light on the Elder races whose legends in magic were only found in myth.

As time passed and my rudimentary knowledge in Wizardry drew to a close, Tarker began testing and evaluating my skills and powers. After grueling sessions of channeling, casting, and memorizing Tarker and some of his more burlier adepts instructed me on the rudiments of physical combat. I learned during that time that as a sorceror, my true strength lay in my ability to unleash my geomantic talent upon my foes rather than using my physique to hurt them. However, Tarker also taught me that being handy with your fists and a weapon is also a good secondary if you should find yourself without mana to channel. I can recall the times where my impertinence (a talent I had not shed in time spent with Tarker and his adepts) about which weapon or technique was better. Whether or not use my physique at all if I could simply just cast would cause him to fret with annoyance!

And at last the time came for me to test my skills outright… Tarker requested me to perform a minor task for him. Some black wolf pelts were needed by the guild, so many of the novices who were judged capable (me being one of them!) were sent to do this task. My excitement at the anticipation of earning my keep for the guild I had spent years learning from could find no expression in words. Perhaps when at last I have returned from completing this and many of the other ‘tasks’ I have taken on, I will have that essay of words…

‘Safe travels and good hunting…’
