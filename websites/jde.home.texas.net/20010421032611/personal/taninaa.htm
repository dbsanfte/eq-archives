Taninaa

Order of the Shadow

 

 

I was born as a cobblers daughter. My father was a proud and wealthy vendor. We had a large butique in the Foreign Quarters of Neriak. People came from all over Norath to purchase the wonderfull creations my father had produced.

We were a happy family. My father continously tried to teach me his skills. I was meant to be his successor. His daughter. His pride. His future. His only child.

My mother was the beauty of Neriak. She was the light that filled the streets when she passed. She was the head of every beneficial organisation that I can think of. People adored her and her efforts. It was a good family. At least when you spoke with my parents.

I never played with the other kids. No, I wanted to explore the surroundings of Neriak. I was sneaking out during night time to adventure the Nektulos Forest in the star light. Duing daytime I was hiding in the shadows of the houses, spying on the other kids who played happily. Happy kids unaware of the great forces of this world. They were taught the basic rules of right and wrong. I believed in the power by knowledge. Nothing else.

My father never stopped believing in me. One day my father hired a merchant to go to the east coast to sell his wares.

I was told to ride along and learn the basics of the trade. The wagon was filled with sandals and shoes in high qualities.

Aelrik was the name of the merchant. He was taller than my father and he was no drow. He had blond hair and spoke many languages. We left my parents in april that year. We had to wait for the snow to dissapear. It must have been around 17 years by now I recon. Aelrik could tell so many stories of countries far far away. Creatures and monsters that had never visited Norrath. We travelled for many days before we had to cross the High Pass. We were attacked by orcs, but Ailrek fought with the strenght of many men. He was a great warrior. He called himself a druid. Guardian of the forests. He told me about the power of magic. This was a turning point in my young life. I studied everyday and everynight while we travelled. I wanted to learn. I wanted to be as powerful as he was, but his magic would not work with me.

In the middle of the summer we hit the east coast. Ailrek knew of an island located just east of Quenos. "Sales are good out there", he told me. This was my first and only time on this island. We sailed into the habour late one evening. The skies were black as heavy clouds shielded us from the moonlight.

We headed up a small path towards the city of Erudin. We didnt get far, though. The horses started to neigh. My elven sight allowed to explore the area, so I jumped of the wagon and ran into the bushes. I could hear Ailrek yell at me, but I could feel something was about to happen.

Zombies, skeletons, vampires, ghouls, and mummies were gathered around a pair of very tall dark people. More and more stumbled over the hills. The man in the middle was a powerfull spellcaster. Many undeads were slain by his spells. But he was outnumbered. I could smell his fear. The fear of the undead. The fear of not living and breathing anymore. It was a beautiful sight. Fear can kill a mans mind.

Then I caught sight of a little boy. A little scared boy who was soon to be an exliving. The woman was saying something to him before resuming her casting.

This faces on the couple changed. I think they started to wither. A giant reptile birdlike being approached the couple triumphing. The woman knealed over the kid said some words and cast a final spell. The boy vanished.

The battle ended soon thereafter. The man had died. The woman followed the rest of the undead speaking the language of the dead. I never spoke to my parents again.

"Death is just the beginning"
