Enchanter Research

 

Here you should find all the information you need to research Enchanter spells. Please let me know if anything is missing or needs changes.

This information provided courtesy of Zehnman at The Mythiran Tower.

Please note that if a single page is listed, it means you must recover both the right and left side of this particular page, then combine them to create the spell. I realize this list is far from complete, and I understand animated hands drop the enchanter recipe books, so PLEASE, if you can get me more info, send it in ASAP! If I know a spell is broken or if there are any special notes about the spell I'd like you to condider, I will annotate it with a "**" and a note will follow that level's spells.
