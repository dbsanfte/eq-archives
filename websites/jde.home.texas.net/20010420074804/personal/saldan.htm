Saldan Shessair.
It is now 17 years ago, that I was left behind. I don't remember all the
details but I will do my very best to tell you the truth. At that time I was
only 5 years old and my mother,Silvara Moonbridge, and my father, Soacker
Shessair, were taking me to the all mighty magician, Seodorex Planewalker
to be given my first name, Saldan. It is a tradition in my family first to
be named after 5 summers. Seodores Planewalker was the most important person
in Erudin by that time, and mages came from all over Norath to seek his
wisdom. We had to cross the forests at night to be able to be in Erudin by
dawn. I remember it as beeing the darkest night of all my nights upon Odus.
The green fog was thicker than ever and evil was in the air. We left
the path to make a shortcut. Both my parents were were quiet and nervous. I
could feel that they knew something was about to happen. And then it
happened. It was as if the time had stopped. I could see my breath freeze in
the air and I could smell death and the fear from my parents. It was huge
and I remember that I tried to scream and run away, but my tounge was
paralyzed and my throat was dry. Me feet began to tremble as it roared. Or
at least I think it roared, because the only thing I could hear was my heart
and the only thing I could feel was my blood flowing like madness in my
veins. We stood like statues for hours, or at least I felt it that way. It
might only have been second or two. Then things happened fast. Zombies,
skeletons, vampires, ghouls, and mummies came from all over the place. They
came stumbling over the hills, hands, arms and then hole bodies appeared
from beneath the ground. I didn't know much magic by that time, but I did
know that the spells that my parent used that night was draining their mana
and their lives. They were not fighting to survive. They were fighting for MY
life. Their son, who had not been named yet. If I would not be named, I
would be no one and wouldn't belong in any society. An outcast. I have
never seen such powerfull magic beeing wield in my 22 years of life, as I
saw that night. The earth was trembling and the forest was on fire as far as
I could see. I didn't know what to do, they were everywhere. After some long
minutes, the undeads stopped attacking. My father was gasping for air and
blood came out his mouth even no creature had been close enough to touch any
of us. They had fought well. The undead made a circle arround us, but too
far away to be harmed by spells. And there it was again. I had forgotten all
about it while the battle was going on. It smiled. Almost laughed, but there
was still no sound coming out of its mouth. My parents looked at each other.
I was shocked looking at them. They didn't look like my parents anymore. The
spells they had used had tormented them. My father was all covered in his
own blood and my mother look 40 years older. She almost looked like a mummy.
They spoke in an foreing language that I had never heard before. The beast
that I now know as a dracolich, undead dragon, looked at them with triumph.
Time had stopped again. My farther began walking towards the dracolich. He
looked tired. Very tired as if he had not slept for years. Only a few minutes
ago they were young and healthy. Now they were both old, sick and wounded.
Mother kneeled beside me and whispered something that I did not understand.
She told me to memorize it and then she began casting again. I was engulfed
in a Shessair and I felt a burning from my stomach. The world began to
vanish and I felt dizzy. The last thing I saw was my father being crushed by
a giat claw as the dracolich tried to prevent my escape. He exploded. My
father simply exploded in a huge blast that tore the leg that, had crushed
him, from the dracolich's body.
When I regained my concience, I found myself lying on a cold stone. I sat up
and vomited. The burning in my stomach had stoped, but the world was still
turning. I tried to focus but my young body was not used to being teleported
yet. I think I lost my conscience again. When I woke up again I
found myself surrounded by tall men and women. They were talking to each
other in that strange foreing language that my parents had been talking.
"What is your name young one" a man steped forward and asked. I shook my
head as to tell him that I had been given no name yet. He looked sad at me
and turned to see the others. I still couldn't understand what they were
talkning about, but they did mention my parent's names. The man turned to me
again while some of the others ran out of the room. "You know the rules
young one and your parents are the only ones who can name you. I'm sorry". I
did know the rules but I was not sure what I was to do now. Then I remembered
what mother had whispered in my name. I was not too sure how to pronounce
the word as it was in foreing language, but I did my best. The man
brightened up. "Come with me young one. You sure do have a name then, and I
shall see to that it is given to you in the right way."
I think I'll stop my story now. I was given my name, Saldan. Shessair will
be my last name the day that I have revenged my parents. I was on my own for
many years. I was given a mentor, but he was no good to me so I decided to
live in the forest in the old house. I never saw my parents or their bodies.
Well, my mother's corpse, as my father was destroyed. I have studied
the words of magic for many years now, but there are still alot to learn. I
recently joined a guild called Conclave Of The Staff. Here I find the
friends that I never had in my childhood and here I find answers to my
questions. I shall revenge my parents and kill all the undead I meet. Some
day when I'm ready, I will track the dracolich and destroy it for good.
Revenge and hatred is not good, but that is what makes me get up in the
morning and continue my training so that I will be prepared for the day that
has to come.

~Saldan
