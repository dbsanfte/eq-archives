Necromancer Research

 

Here you should find all the information you need to research Necromancer spells. Please let me know if anything is missing or needs changes.

This information provided courtesy of Zehnman at The Mythiran Tower.

All components listed are "Words." As I'm informed a spell is broken or any special notes you should know about the spell, I will annotate the spell with a "**" and a note will follow that level's spell listing. Also, the various tomes of ingredients list some extra spells that are not on this list. The reason I have excluded them from this list is because they have been removed from the game and the recipe books have not yet been updated.

**There are two Words of Sentient currently in game. They look the same, they are named the same, but one is a lower level. Attempt to stack them to decide if you have two different words. If they do not stack, they are of different levels. Next, take them to a vendor. The low level one will sell for around 3 gold, the high level for around 7 gold. This spell requires the LOW level word.
