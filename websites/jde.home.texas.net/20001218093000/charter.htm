The Conclave Of The Staff

Charter

 

Here in you shall find the charter for the guild, the Conclave Of The Staff.


Article 1 - Founding Ideas of the Conclave Of The Staff

The Conclave Of The Staff (hereafter referred to as the Conclave) was formed in an effort to share the strengths and weakness of the true spellcasters of Norrath.  One of our primary goals is to share information and resources with our Brothers and Sisters. Our aid is offered to any of the true spellcasters, if it is within our abilities to help.


Article 2 - Our Role In Norrath

As a source of knowledge in Norrath, the Conclave realizes the need to work with all true spellcasters of Norrath regardless of race or class. The Conclave welcomes members from all four of the true spellcasters; Enchanter, Magician, Necromancer, and Wizard. The Conclave also draws members from all five races; Dark Elf, Erudite, Gnome, High Elf, and Human. Together we shall become a resource all true spellcasters seek in order to maintain their current skills and seek out new knowledge.


Article 3 - Code of Conduct

The Conclave is an honorable guild. We shall not lower ourselves to the status of petty thieves and vagabonds. Knowledge is the source of our power and with said knowledge we shall be able to overcome our adversaries and attain items of great power. All other true spellcasters are to be treated with respect until they prove themselves to be unworthy of such respect. The Conclave does not discriminate membership based on race, religion, alignment, or persuasions of most sorts. The Conclave believes that actions reflect upon the individual and the Conclave as a whole and that any actions that bring dishonor upon the individual and subsequently the Conclave will be dealt with under the provisions of this charter as defined in Article 4.


Article 4 - Disciplinary Actions

If it is discovered that the actions of a member of the Conclave have brought dishonor upon not only the member, but also the Conclave, disciplinary action will immediately be taken by the Council of the Staff (hereafter referred to as the Council). The Council currently consists of Pegasus, Techy, Keila, Bairn, and myself, Listanus. The Council will convene, and disciplinary actions will be discussed and voted on. A member that is charged will be able to bring witnesses before the Council in their defense.


Article 5 - Amendments and Changes to the Conclave Of The Staff

All amendments and changes to the Conclave shall be voted on by the existing members of the Conclave. A meeting will be called and all present shall
have one vote towards the issue. A 3/4 vote in favor of the issue will decide the outcome.
