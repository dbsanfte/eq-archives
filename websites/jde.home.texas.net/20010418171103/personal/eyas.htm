Eyas

Order of the Eye

 

"Why it must be this way…"

I am and have always been the keeper of my destiny. That being the case, I must always remain true to my convictions. As it is with my mother, so it shall be with me. An elven magistrate is my mother. She believes and lives her life according to the principles of Law and Order. My father, like my brother, on the other hand believe in the individual circumstances of any particular situation. No doubt a contradiction in terms but alas that is how life is sometimes. Do not mistake me though, I do love my father and brother as I should, but where they would see nobility and chivalry, I do not. What’s wrong is wrong and thus consequences must be accepted for the given actions…

The next question one would ask is why I chose to be a student in the Geomantic Arts of Illusion? One would naturally assume that being a staunch supporter of Law and Order, my paths should have pointed me to that of the Knights or Paladins of Felwithe. Perhaps, but if one were to look at the great magistrates and commanders of Felwithe, one would notice that behind these great figures an enchanter was there providing them with key information, advice, and support. For who none better than the Masters of Illusions could go forth and mingle with the commoners or the enemy to gather important information? The fact that most accomplished enchanters, asides from becoming master jewelers, become magistrates of the Law, advisors to the King, or leaders in Felwithe’s Army and Militia speak of the usefulness of our trade.

And so there I went when I was a young maiden, to the Great House of Illusions and Enchantments. It was one of the four great house of Geomancy in Felwithe. My time spent there was unremarkable. I learned to channel geomantic power to alter the forms of many things including my own body. Also I learned how channel that same power to alter the environment in a given area. The instructors at the school also taught me some useful self-defense techniques should I be in a situation without mana to channel. In the end they taught me that being able to alter what a person perceives as real is convincing enough to be real…

When the time came for me to graduate and to choose a profession, my mother came to me and asked if I wanted to serve my King. I felt compelled to honor her request for what service is far better than to serve the King? As it turned out, the King’s Advisor of Foreign Affairs need a young enchanter to gather information on the strength and capabilities of the infamous Crushbone Orcs. I gladly accepted. I had no love for the ignorant and malevolent Orcs. When my briefing was finished, I realized that not only was my assignment going to take me Crushbone itself but, later on it was to bring me to far reaches of Oggok in the land of Ogres and eventually to Neriak in the land of the Fallen Ones…
