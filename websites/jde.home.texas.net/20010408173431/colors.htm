What the spell colors mean

 

Listed here are what the colors mean for each of your spell icons. This was taken from the EverQuest Manual, page 42. This proves most useful when determining what Area of Effect spells you will use.

 

- Yellow means the spell will be cast on you.

- Red means the spell will be cast upon the target.

- Green is an area effect spell which will spread out from the target.

- Blue is an area effect spell which will radiate out from you.

- Orange is an area effect spell that will affect everyone in the area.

- Purple is an area effect spell that will affect everyone in your party.
