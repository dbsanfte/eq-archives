Project Lightning** Rune Of Fulguration, Rune Of Periphery

**There are two different runes in the game listed as "Rune of Fulguration" and they look identical. They are of different levels. To determine which rune you have (if you have two) try and stack the runes. If they do not stack, take them to a vendor and offer them for sale. Whichever rune is worth more is the higher level rune (used for Energy Storm). This spell require the LOWER level rune.
