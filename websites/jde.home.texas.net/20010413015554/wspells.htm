Invisibility

30

Divination

Target becomes invisible. To determine if a monster can see invisible, target the monster and the type /con. If the monster cons as Indifferent, it can not see you. The duration of Invisibility is random. It can last from 2 seconds on up. You will know the spell is about to end as it will say "You feel yourself starting to appear". Once it has given this message, you have 5 seconds to before Invisibility totally fades. Spellcasting and melee combat will also cause inisibility to fade.
