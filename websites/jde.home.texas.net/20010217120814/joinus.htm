The Conclave Of The Staff

 

Thank you for your interest in join the Conclave Of The Staff!

The Conclave Of The Staff currently only resides on the Innoruuk Server.

 

We have somewhat revised our recruiting process. In order to gain admission into the Conclave you must follow these steps:

1) Find a sponsor within the Conclave. This can be anyone in the guild except for the 4 officers, the 2 recruiters and myself, Listanus. It should be someone of relatively the same level. Once someone has agreed to sponsor you, you need to group with that person for a minimum of 4 hours. This does not have to be a continuos 4 hours. The time can be split up between multiple days, but the 4 hours must be accrued with the same member of the Conclave. That member will then relate to the respective Recruiter about his journey with you.

2) Grouping with the recruiters. Once you have grouped with a member of the Conclave, you will then be refered to a recruiter. The recruiter for Enchanters and Necromancers is Keila, and the recruiter for Magicians and Wizards is Lyrren. If your time with the regular member of the Conclave went well, you will then be asked to group with your respective recruiter. This will be for a minimum of 2 hours. As with the initial sponsorship, this does not have to be a consecutive 2 hours, it can be split up over several days.

3) Voting. After you have grouped with the recruiter, they will relate their experiences with you and the experiences of the intial sponsorship to the members of the Council of the Staff. The Council of the Staff currently consists of Bairn, Pegasus, Keila, Techy and myself, Listanus. We will vote on your membership at the time and determine if you will join our ranks.

 

If you have any questions about this process, please feel free to email me.
