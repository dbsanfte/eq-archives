Research Library

 

Welcome to the Conclave Of The Staff Research Library!

 

This library is resource of research components for the use of all the true spellcasters of Norrath. The library is TRADE only. To ensure the continued growth and wide selection of the library, non-members of the Conclave Of The Staff trade at a 2 to 1 ratio. This means that you give the Librarian two research components and recieve the one of your choice in return. Guild members of the Conclave can trade at a 1 to 1 ratio.

Currently Librarians reside in Freeport and Qeynos. Also, expect soon to see a library open in Felwithe!. For races or clases that are KOS in Freeport, feel free to send a /tell and we can set a meeting outside of Freeport. The same also applies to races or classes that are KOS in Qeynos.

Please Note: the Librarian can only trade for items listed in the Freeport Library. Qeynlibrarian can only trade for items listed in the Qeynos Library.

The Freeport Library is run by Librarian. You can contact myself, Listanus Mytherian, to arrange a meeting with Librarian.

The Qeynos Library is run by Qeynlibrarian. You can contact Bowan D'Black to arrange a meeting with Qeynlibrarian.

The Conclave of the Staff Research Library only resides on the Innoruuk server.
