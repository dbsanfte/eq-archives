Guild Events

 

Spellcasters Battle:

We are planning a Spellcasters Battle! We will try and draw spellcasters all across Norrath to this grand event. So far we have the rules of the event set up. They are tenative rules for now. We are going to do a practice battle with just the guild members to see how it goes. No one is required to enter, but I would like to see everyone there when we run it.

Rules:

1. There will be no starting buffs.  If you feel that
you need them you have to use the manna to cast them
once it begins.

2. No melee combat.  The point of the Spellcasters
battle is to use your spells.  You should be able to
rely on your spells, for we are true spellcasters.

3. No pets.  This brings your knowledge of your spells
to the playing field.  Plus eliminate hand to hand
combat.

4. Must be of comparable level.  Example: Range of
spells 8-11, 12-15 and so forth.

5. Must be 1 on 1.  No groups or outside help.

6. No looting of fallen persons corpse.  This is out
of respect for the other person.  This is only a
contest, not a loot fest.


Prizes

I think that they can be items that we make, or items
that are donated.  Any donations for the making of the
jewelry would be appreciated.  Keila has offered to
help me make the jewelry.  So the money can also be
donated to her.
