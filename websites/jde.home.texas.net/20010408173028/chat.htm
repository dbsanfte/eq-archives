Conclave of the Staff Chat Room

 

Thanks to Leahcim  for his great chat applet.

Keep in mind that you cannot see other people in the chat room until you press connect.

Use a Java-enabled Browser to Chat!!!

Instructions for using chat
  • it should take about 10-20 seconds for the client to load
  • choose a name and type it in the box where "yourname" appears
  • press the "connect" button and wait a couple seconds
  • your name will appear in the right side, along with anyone else who is chatting
  • type in the bottom area and press enter to send your message
Special Commands
  • "/nick newnick" changes your name to newnick
  • "/msg otherperson" sends a private message to otherperson
  • "/wake otherperson" gives a message in his terminal and playes a sound
  • "/ping otherperson" tells the delay of the messages you send to otherperson
  • "/me thinks" gives "yourname thinks" in the chat area
  • "/ignore otherperson" ignores messages from otherperson. use "/unignore" to undo it
  • "/whois otherperson" gives some info about otherperson
