Crew News 17/10/03

 Hi All

First of all, a big welcome to all the new guildies that recently joined us from Fate of Blades and Covenant of Honour, I hope you soon find yourselves feeling at home.

Feedback from Officers from the Plane of Hate raid was very positive. I am pleased to note that a lot of the new guildies played like experienced raiders. I did not have to repeat myself very often and as far as I can tell nearly everyone was following raid protocols. A few people were still not listening, and were talking over the Raid Leader towards the middle and end of the raid, but this did not happen too often.
As for the future, please keep your direct tells to the Raid Leader to a minimum. Casual chat and requests are not helpful and I often find them distracting when trying to issue instructions. I would prefer if raiders can stick to topics relevant to the raid and only if they are important.

Aside from these niggly things, I am fairly confident that we have a strong core raiding force. Of course this will truly be tested in the coming months as we raid. Our raiding prospects are looking very promising and we can start to tackle more difficult boss mobs very soon.

Our focus for the time being is still to accomplish more Epics and now includes leveling for those below level 55. As we will soon be tackling much more difficult mobs, we will be looking to raise the raiding level requirement to 55+, those below 55 will be ineffective against these mobs and will be more of a burden (constantly needing rezzing) than being a help.

The level requirement for Guild acceptance is now being raised to level 50+. Special consideration will be given those classes that we lack, e.g. Rogues.

Last but not least, congratulations to all those that have been confirmed as Officers. Kobite - Cleric Officer, Nemex - Assistant Raid Leader, Bloodshade - Rogue Officer, Tinytori - Warrior Officer and Masran - Necro Officer.
