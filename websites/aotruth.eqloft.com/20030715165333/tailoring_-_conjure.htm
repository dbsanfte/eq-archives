Tailoring - Part 1

FASTEST ROUTE TO BECOMING A MASTER TAILOR
by Conjure

Due to a request from a guildie, I have prepapred this guide. I'm sure many of you are trying to do your Coldain Prayer Shawl quests and will find this very useful.

PART 1: to Skill 76
(Please print out the map from the web address below, it will help you locate vendors.)
http://www.eqatlas.com/velious/thurgadinmap.html

All (Loc #) numbers are references to the map

This is expensive but fast. You will need about 350pp and some nominal skill in Smithing.

Go to your guild trainer and put 20 points in Tailoring.

Now make your way to Thurgadin and be prepared to spend a lot of money. All of the following can be done in Thurgadin

MANDRAKE WEAVE
In the bank (Loc 17#) there is a merchant (fourth from the left) called Rexx Frostreaver, purchase from him a Coldain Tanning Kit (it is a better version of a large sewing kit, 1.0 weight lighter, and yes you can use it for general tailoring). Then you need to make mandrake weave in the Tanning Kit from mandrake roots. These can be purchased from a merchant (second from the right) in the bank for approx 2-3pp depending on your charisma (minimum 104 CHA for best price).

Combine the following:

2 x Mandrake Root (stackable) = 1 Mandrake Weave (stackable) Trivials 66

Make as many as need be to trivial, this should be approx 6 stacks which will cost you approx 300-360pp

Next you need your Smithing skill to make a File, which will enable you to make Steel Boning which trivials at a fairly low skill. Steel Boning is required to make Picnic Baskets with the Mandrake Weave.

To make a File
This is a good way to start your Smithing Skill while you are at it, please note that these item need to be combined in an ordinary forge (Loc 11#) (it will not work in a Cultural Forge!). First of all you need to make some metal bits from which the file is made of.

2 x Small Piece of Ore + Flask of Water = Metal Bits (stackable) Trivials 18
(Loc 2#) (Loc 10#)

Then make the File:

Metal Bits + File Mould + Flask of Water = File Trivials 21
(Loc 17#)

To make Steel Boning
Combine the items below in a forge, note that the file is returned on each combine regardless of failure or success, so you do not need to make more than one.

Small Brick of Ore + File + Flask of Water = Steel Boning (stackable) Trivials 37

Make as many Steel Bonings as you have Mandrake Weaves.

PICNIC BASKETS
Here you get to recuperate some of your outgoings, approx one half.

Mandrake Weave + Steel Boning = 10 x Picnic Baskets (stackable) Trivial 76

Theses Picnic Baskets sell for over 4gp each!

Tailoring - Part 2

PART 2: to Skill 131

Unfortunately this is the hard part before you reach Master status, you will need to camp the spiders in the Crystal Caverns for approx 8-10 hours. Being a level 49 Mage makes it rather easy, you should be able to solo the whole of the corridor and Queen Dracnia’s Chamber without too much effort. Not sure how this applies to other classes.

You need approx 14-15 stacks of swatches to reach skill 131, making Crystalline Silk Armour. Once you have harvested the silks follow the 'Making Crystalline Silk Armour' guide below.

2 x Crystalline Silk (stackable) = 1 Crystalline Silk Swatch (stackable) Trivial 15

Note: It is probably best to carry a sewing/tanning kit with you, this enables you to make the swatches while you are hunting and hence, maximise your carry capacity.

Making Crystalline Silk Armor

First make Crystalline Mantle
*I found both the shoulder pad and mantle pattern works

1 Swatch + 1 Silver thread + 1 Shoulder Pad Pattern = Crystalline Mantle Trivial 118
(Loc 25#) (Loc 17#)

Then make Crystalline Gloves

2 x Swatch + 1 Silver Thread + 1 Gloves Pattern = Crystalline Gloves Trivial 124
(Loc 25#)

Finally make Crystalline Robe
*I found both tunic and robe pattern works

3 x Swatch + 1 Silver Thread + 1 Tunic Pattern = Crystalline Robe Trivial 131
(Loc 25#)

Congratulations! You are a Master Tailor skill 131 with minimal effort. Obviously there are alternative routes to 131 however; you will spend endless amounts of time hunting for pelts of the appropriate quality and getting over loaded with weight. With spiders you at least get a good yield, can be hunted fairly easily in one place and the silks are light weight. Those of you that have hunted pelts, imagine the time it would take you to collect 220 high quality cat pelts for combines!!!!
