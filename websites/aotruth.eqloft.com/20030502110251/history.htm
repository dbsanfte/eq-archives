Ambassadors of Truth (AoT)

Guild Moto; “If its worth hitting its worth dying for!”


The guild was set up to help create a community of like-minded peeps, all striving for Exp, Loot, Plat and a decent corpse recovery team. Corpse recovery is by far the skill we are best at; in fact, upon guild membership new recruit skills are automatically increased to Corpse Recovery (100).

Probably, what we are most famous for though is our exploits in Befallen. At lvl 15ish, where most Players feared to go in you could often find a group of founder guild members tearing through befallen corridors with large trains biting lumps out of there ass’s. Inevitably, this is where we began to hone our corpse recovery skills.

In short, guild members are brave, daring and a little stupid; if there is a place in EQ with large aggroying monsters and only one way out you’re sure to find an Ambassador of Truth member there, or at least his corpse will be.
