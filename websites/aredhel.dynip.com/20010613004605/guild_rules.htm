The Aredhel Member Rules

  1. Treat others as you wish to be treated. This is a simple concept that take's all of us a long way.
  2. No Foul, sexually oriented, or discriminating language should be used on the Forums, in Public channels or in Guild Chat. All 3 of these can be offensive to others and will not be tolerated. Please refrain from this type of language and attitude while wearing The Aredhel tag.
  3. Help others when you are able, but you will never be expected to drop everything to help others. We all do our part to help each other in-game and out for that matter. The more involved and helpful you are in game, the more respected and liked you tend to be.
  4. No Ninja Looting, Kill Stealing or Generally Intolerable gameplay will be tolerated. We don't like it when others do it to us, so don't do it to others. This is dis-respectful of others and affords nothing but bad feelings and problems in-game.
  5. The Aredhel is a well respected guild, by wearing The Aredhel tag, you are automatically given a level of respect that is not afforded a lot of people online. Respect the Tag you are wearing and be weary of your actions, as they affect the rest of the guild.
  6. Lastly, Have fun!!! We are an extended family in The Aredhel. Our main objective is to have fun and do our best to enjoy our online gaming experience. When you lose sight of that basic part of Gaming, you lose sight of what The Aredhel is about.
