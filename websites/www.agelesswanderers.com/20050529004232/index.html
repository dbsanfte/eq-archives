Ageless Wanderers is an Everquest Guild that was originally formed on the Innoruuk server in the Fall of 1999. We were moved to The Nameless server on May 2, 2005 as part of the ongoing server merges.

The guild has certainly seen its share of ups and downs over the years since it was first formed, but has slowly evolved from a casual, mainly non-raiding guild to a larger, more organized guild that (usually) raids regularly on Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday nights. Exceptions sometimes occur during slack membership times such as over the summer or when new expansions/games suck away the attention of our members.

We've been a late-night raiding guild for most of our existence, which has had both drawbacks and advantages in terms of mob availability and membership attendance.

We are not a DKP guild. If you are rabid about loot allocation, then our officer-assigned loot system is probably not for you, and you may wish to check into other guilds.

For information about our membership requirements and current class wants, please check the public section of our forums. You can see what we've been killing recently in the Recent Activities section. and who our current members are under Roster.
