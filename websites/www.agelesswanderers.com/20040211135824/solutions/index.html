Marble Solutions:

Marblehead.com was found and run by students of the Marblehead High School since 1995. The original founding students were Jeff Alexander, Russ Goodwin, Tim Angle and Dan Stein. This site gives the students an opportunity to earn credit while learning some of the most popular Internet services including Linux, Apache, MySQL, PHP and more.

I am currently seeking new students interested in getting involved. If you are a Marblehead High School student or know of a student at the High School that may be interested please contact Jeff Bickford, Technology Director, at 781 639 3100 x5124.

Current Projects:

  • Building Marblehead.com into a search engine for information in the Town of Marblehead
  • Documenting free services offered to Marblehead businesses and organizations
  • Ensuring current businesses are keeping their content up to date on Marblehead.com
  • Building an off site backup server for email and web services
  • Writing the history of Marble Solutions to include in this web site
