Recent Events

Tuesday, April 19 - Drunder and Nobles' Causeway - We began the evening by paying a call on Vallon Zek in his comfortable residence at Drunder, Fortress of Zek. He left his spiffy belt hanging in the closet it seems, but we did walk away with an Obsidian Scimitar of War (grats Microndew) and a Furious Hammer of Zek (grats Rains). We also picked up an Ornate Greaves Mold from Anival the Blade enroute. Next we went to Nobles' Causeway and farmed a couple more Greater Muramite Runes off of The Murkglider School Leader (grats Ganbor and Tanamenx) as well as a nice Tri-Spined War Cudgel (grats Konvoy).



Saturday, April 16 - Tower of Solusek Ro - As a break from rune farming, we decided to go to Sol Ro and drop in on Xuzl. He coughed up a Greater Rune Word, Ornate Breastplate Mold, and a Lance of the Flame Knight (grats Poscha). Along the way we also picked up a Huntsman's Cleaver (grats Ginwedar).



Thursday, April 14 - Nobles' Causeway and Ruined City of Dranik - Do I even need to say that rune farming has continued? The Murkglider School Leader again fell to our forces, as did Tiorpat Tornwing, netting us a Glowing Muramite Rune (grats Dagreth), three Greater Muramite Runes (grats Forterix, Nokax, and our guest Eckish), more Slime-Etched Epaulets (grats Zarbask) and Loyalist Spaulders of Defiance (Grats Siimon).



Tuesday, April 12 - Nobles' Causeway and Plane of Justice - Once again the Murkglider was schooled, gaining us another two Greater Muramite Runes (grats Azeyn and Ganbor) and a Slime-Etched Epaulets (Grats Snikch!). We then strolled over to the Plane of Justice and dropped in on The 7th Hammer. Unfortunately, someone presented us with a classic example of why it's a Bad Idea to pre-target a named mob, and we ended up sitting in the graveyard watching our corpses rain down out of the sky. Perhaps we'll pay another visit on him at a later date.



Saturday, April 9 - Nobles' Causeway and Ruined City of Dranik - Getting more runes is always good, so once again we slew the Murkglider School Leader and Tiorpat Tornwing. MSL dropped another Glidergut Neck (grats Dandera) and Tiorpat coughed up an Ornate Blade of Revolution (grats Konvoy). We also scored four Greater Muramite Runes (grats Jurell, Succachorro, Valley, and Syrath). Congratulations also go out tonight to Jammiee, who completed his Epic 1.0 quest for the Spear of Fate before tonight's raid.



Thursday, April 7 - Nobles' Causeway and Ruined City of Dranik - After some initial difficulties with push and adds we again dropped the Murkglider School Leader, obtaining a Nauseating Bilestone (grats Jurell), and two Greater Muramite Runes (grats Aneoc and Physician). We then dropped in on our old friend Slavedriver Menlo, who rolled over to get his tummy scratched and thereby lost some Frayed Footpads (grats Moretsu) and two more Greater Muramite Runes to us (grats Altarial of ML and Jammiee). To round off the night we went to the Ruined City of Dranik and remonstrated with Tiorpat over his lack of hospitality on Tuesday, and so walked away with an Idealist's Woven Bracer (grates Deklin of ML) and a further two Greater Muramite Runes (grats Tanvas and Toradorina). Congrats to Ganbor on the Map of Old Kuaa that Ornaia the Foul dropped just afterwards.



Tuesday, April 5 - Wall of Slaugher, Ruined City of Dranik and Nobles' Causeway - We started the evening by trekking to the Wall of Slaugher, where we killed Velitorkin. It was an interesting fight, with Warstorm managing to stay alive until the mob was at 22% even with his healers being tossed like salad. In the end it died after dropping much of the raid, as well as a Bazu Hide Tunic (grats Aneoc) and two Greater Muramite Runes (grats Poscha and Tanvas). We then moved on to the Ruined City of Dranik, where we sought to reprise our previous success at the Tiorpat event. Saddly, we must have frightened the poor mob dreadfully, as he chose to despawn literally as he was about to be pulled (BOOOO!). For our final kill of the night we moved on to Nobles' Causeway and finished off the Murkglider School Leader, obtaining a Glidergut Neck (grats Altarial of ML) and two more Greater Muramite Runes (grats Dagreth and Microndew).





Sunday, April 3 - Ahkeva Ruins and Ssraeshza Temple - Our allies Mystic Legions asked us to turn out and lend them a hand offing the Itraer Vius in Ahkeva Ruins. We happily obliged, netting a Collar of Travuel Xakreum (grats Prunejuice) and a Crown of the Itraer Vius (grats Kijutii). We then moved on the Ssra, and paid another visit on Vyzh'dra the Cursed. Congratulations to Warstorm on a Bloodfrenzy, Siimon on Circle of Smoke, and Reiahla on Black Runed Pants.





Friday, April 1 - Impromptu Epic Raid - Okay, so it wasn't a real raid, but a couple groups of us decided to run around after Epic 1.0 drops for a couple of our members. We went to Chardok in quest of manisi herb for Evuull, and instead found some bizarre aggro and the Fabled Bridgekeeper, who dropped a nice Fabled Stained Cloth Mask, grats Herrkulease. After recovering from the weird aggro (can you say "overwhelming train?" I knew you could!) we were about to take a second stab at it when Hammosa happened to mention he had some manisi herb he could MQ to Evuull...grats Evuull and thank you Ham. We then moved on to The Hole and went in after High Scale Kurn. After another "wtf did all these mobs appear from!?" moment we eventually offed the beggar, grats Jammiee on epic drop.
