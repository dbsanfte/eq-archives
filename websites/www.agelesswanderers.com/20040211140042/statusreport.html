Sunday January 9, 2004

It has been determined that the initial failure of the web server drives was due to excessive dust buildup in the vents. This caused our 5 drive RAID array to fail completely including the backup drives. The server has been cleaned and all new drives have been installed. We are currently working on rebuilding our numerous web sites from backups. We are also in the process of setting up off site backups to ensure this problem will not occur again in the future.

The current web server is now once again a 5 drive RAID array with 2 backup drives, located in a climate controled closet with power backup and surge protection. We are trying real hard to add a network support position in the School Department so we have someone to monitor the 25 servers we operate on a regular basis. In the meantime we are utilizing our High School Computer Support Specialist, Craig Gerrish, to monitor and maintain our web server.

Our drives are still off at the emergency recovery specialists and we are waiting on a status report from them.

All School Department staff that do not have thier web sites back online please contact Craig Gerrish at 781 639 3100 x4126 to have your web site re-configured. If you have worked with Barbara Schwartz please contact her for help restoring from your backups.

All business clients that still do not have their web sites back online please contact Jeff Bickford directly for further information at 781 639 3100 x5124. Do not contact Marble Solutions at this time.
