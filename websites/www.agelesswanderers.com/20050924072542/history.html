A Brief History of Ageless Wanderers

Ageless Wanderers was formed in the Fall of 1999 on the Innoruuk server. Its original guildleader was a human paladin named Pathwarden. Some months later a new guild, Fortress of Honor, was formed from a merge between Ageless Wanderers and several other guilds, with many members moving to the new guild and only a portion remaining in AW. A wood elf rogue, Korinna, became guildleader of the now smaller guild.

The guild had its share of ups and downs over the next couple of years, seeing members continue to drain to Fortress of Honor, Alliance of Magic and Steel, and Silent Justice, among others. A barbarian warrior, Cuhulain, became guildleader for a time, and then after the collapse of Silent Justice and the return to AW of a number of ex-members, leadership passed to another barbarian warrior named Rogosh.

With the influx of returning members and the friends they'd made in other guilds, AW finally began to grow again, and over the next few years gradually developed from a small, very casual "family" type guild to a larger casually raiding guild. We began to claw our way up the Tiers.

Pathwarden, who'd quit EQ for a while, returned around this time in the form of a gnome paladin, Miniwarden, and again led the guild. After he again departed a couple years later, leadership bounced rapidly between several players (Rogosh, Synvai, Goen, among others) before AW settled for a while on a council style of leadership, where the actual Guildleader title was stored on an officer's twink and the officers work in consultation with each other to lead the guild.

On May 2, 2005, the guild was moved to The Nameless as part of the ongoing server merges that Sony had mandated. On June 1, 2005, a large chunk of the currently active membership of Ageless Wanderers, including the current guildleader, Tanvas, a halfling druid, decided to /movelog to the Luclin server. Snoww, a halfling rogue, became guildleader of those remainng on The Nameless server.

Tier Timeline

The following is the history of how we wandered up the Tiers, as recorded on Innoruuk.com in the Guilds forum:

Thursday, February 13, 2003
Ageless Wanderers has killed both Trakanon and Venril Sathir, bringing them into Tier 1. Welcome to the Tier listing

Thursday, March 20, 2003
Ageless Wanderers have slain Velketor the Sorceror and Vindicator, moving them up to Tier 2. Very nice job AW

Thursday, August 7, 2003
Ageless Wanderers have killed Lord Yelinak, for a two tier jump to Tier 4. Very impressive!

Thursday, September 25, 2003
Ageless Wanderers slew Tunare, the Trial of Heroes mob for Tier 5, moving them up a tier. Great job on the kill and the immense clear AW!

Sunday, December 14, 2003
Ageless Wanderers have slain Arch Lich Rhag`Zhadune and Grummus to bump them up to Tier 6. Excellent progression AW, keep up the good work.

Saturday, March 20, 2004
Ageless Wanderers killed the High Priest of Ssraeshza and Aerin`Dar, moving them to Tier 7. Nice to see a guild that's been around awhile still very capable of trying new things and progressing wonderfully.

Saturday, April 3, 2004
Ageless Wanderers have killed Tallon Zek and the Manaetic Behemoth, to qualify them for Tier 8. Great start on PoP AW!

Monday, November 8, 2004
Ageless Wanderers have defeated Rallos Zek the Warlord, the Trial of Heroes mob for Tier 10, to move them to that tier. Congratulations AW, enjoy the elemental planes.
