Archive of Events from March 2005

Thursday, March 31 - Sol Ro Tower - Tonight we and Mystic Legions went and paid a call on Rizlona in the Tower of Solusek Ro. She eventually coughed up a glyphed rune word, Basalt Plated Pauldrons (grats Mercuryknight of ML) and a Mithril Shod Staff (grats rots, /sigh).



Tuesday, March 29 - Ssraeshza Temple - Along with our friends from Mystic Legions we headed down to the basement of Ssra Temple and spawned Vyzh`dra the Cursed (note to Snoww - lockpicks are to remain in inventory at all times!). Grats to Galindryl of ML on the Claw of Vyzh`dra, Littlemant on the Cloak of Dark Shadows, and Aimbuk on the Breastplate of the Void. We then headed upstairs and attempted Xerkizh the Creator, but due to a small tactical error (shaman are for slowing, Ham!) had a lengthy wipe and ended up doing some extensive testing of the corpse summoning NPCs in the Guild Lobby.



Saturday, March 26 - Nobles' Causeway and Ruined City of Dranik - We made a second appointment with Slavedriver Menlo tonight, and he fell for it. Grats to Famena on Brass-Buckled Girdle, and Snoww and Konvoy on Greater Muramite Rune. We then moved on to the Ruined City of Dranik and did the Tiorpat Tornwing event, netting a Glowing Muramite Rune for Tanvas, a Greater Muramite Rune for Gimgulch, and a Dragorn-Inscribed Caduceus, grats Valley.





Thursday, March 24 - Nobles' Causeway - Tonight we went to NC to try and learn strategies for dealing with Slavedriver Menlo. It was an interesting night, with Stone Thrower popping unexpectedly out of a gorge while we were setting up for one of the Menlo attempts. The Stone Thrower managed to smush most of the raid, but fell over dead in the end, netting us a Lesser Muramite Rune and a Blacklink Cape. During setup for our final attempt, we also had a Dragorn Battleleader wander into camp, dropping a Minor Muramite Rune and a nice Blacklink Band, grats Echofourteen. On our third attempt at the Slavedriver, with a by now much-reduced force, we succeeded in getting his health down to 4% before wiping, and are now confident that the next time we encounter him, he'll be the one doing the dying. We are rather amused that we handled the unexpected 3000+ hitter Stone Thrower, but failed repeatedly on the much gentler Slavedriver.



Tuesday, March 22 - Mistmoore and Ferubi - We went exploring tonight, visiting the revamped Castle Mistmoore (which many of us haven't been back to since we were young'uns) and then moving on to Ferubi. Grats to Aimbuk on the Jointed Ivory Gauntlets we picked up off of Ra'tuk Kjyt Gykk in Barindu on the way to Ferubi, to Tanamex for the Scaled Sash of Battle Hunger from Rav Ptvin, and to Dragonfear for the nice Daunting Opaque Orb, an AC 15 aug that dropped from Pixt Elite Brawler. The force in Ferubi shrunk considerably by the end of the raid, but we succeeded in planting a mage where we wanted, so future events here should be rather faster.





Saturday, March 19 - Shaman Epic 1.5 - Grats to Hamosa on getting his Epic 1.5, and on those who got drops during the event.



Thursday, March 17 - Vallon Zek - Since several of our members needed backflagging, and we can always use more FT belts, tonight we travelled to the Fortress of Zek (aka Plane of Tactics), where we sought out and ultimately killed Vallon Zek. The necromancer Nrojos got taught how to pull (and, umm, how not to pull) this particular personage. To our pleasure, VZ dropped a Girdle of the Tactician (grats Nokax) and an Obsidian Scimitar of War (grats Atharion).



Tuesday, March 15 - Lord Inquisitor Seru - We decided to do something a little different tonight, and trekked over to Sanctus Seru to pay a visit on the Lord Inquisitor. After a lengthy ranged fight, he mysteriously fell over sideways. I guess he was tired of toting that big sword around. Drops included a Bow of Storms (grats to our guest, Galindryl) and a Glowing Amber Band.
