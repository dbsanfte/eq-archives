Recent Events

Tuesday, July 26 - Cobalt Scar, Burning Woods, and Skyfire - we decided to tackle a bunch of quest kills tonight, starting with Shaman Epic 1.5 sharks in Cobalt Scar (grats Xatraris) followed by Ixiblat Fer in Burning Woods (grats Hopefor), and finishing up by clearing the guardians around the VP quest container guy in Skyfire (grats Treek on completing his key and others on acquiring the quest container).



Saturday, July 23 - Exp. Night Called - low turnout tonight, as it seems half the guild had plans for this weekend.


Tuesday, July 19 - Temple of Cazic-Thule - We attempted the Ring of Fear event in CT tonight. The first few waves went well, but by the time the golems started becoming active, we were being overwhelmed with mobs. We still succeeded in killing one of the golems before finally wiping, and overall rating of the event seems to have been "damn that was fun". Grats to Magrint on Ring of Dreadful Dreams and to Shoulynn on Pearl of Dread.



Saturday, July 16 - The Deep - The Thought Horror Overfiend fell to us again. Saddly, he failed to cough up much of anything worth looting apart from a handful of spells. Grats rots on the rest of it!



Tuesday, July 12 - The Karanas, The Accursed Nest - The evening began with a little Necro Epic 1.5 action in the Karanas (grats Sumanau) follwed by a trip to The Accursed Nest, where Kami and Elureh coughed up an Azure Dragonkin Ring (grats Patreous) and an Earring of the Vortex (grats Dezmers) respectively.



Sunday, July 10 - Acrylia Caverns - We held an inpromptu raid versus the Evolved Burrower tonight. Congratulations to Sumanau on the Bile-Stained Robes and Orb of Luclinite, and to Letz on the Strands of Power.



Saturday, July 9 - The Deep - We paid a call on the Thought Horror Overfiend tonight. At first he was overly surly but on a second try he warmed up to us. Congratulations to Sumanau on Orb of Lost Souls, our guest Soninya on Veil of Hidden Thought, Curelean on Spell: Hammer of Souls, and Xatraris on the Orb of Luclinte for VT Key.



Tuesday, July 5 - Exp. Night Called - Nothing up worth trying, at least not with the numbers on.


Saturday, July 2 - No Raids! - Due to this being a long weekend in both the U.S. and Canada, no raid was held. Happy Canada Day and have a great 4th of July.
