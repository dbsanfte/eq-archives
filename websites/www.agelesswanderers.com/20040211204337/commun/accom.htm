ACCOMMODATIONS

Marblehead's hospitality is offered in a variety of cozy, elegant and comfortable
accommodations. Many of our bed and breakfasts are home stays: accommodations
in private homes with a portion of the site devoted to lodging. Our list includes
accommodations from studio apartments to larger inns. Each offers unique charm
and amenities.

All of the accommodations listed here offer caring and gracious hosts. We recommend
them with confidence and pride, and know that your visit to Marblehead will be
enhanced by your experience as an overnight guest.

This list is compiled for you to select the accommodation most appropriate for you.
