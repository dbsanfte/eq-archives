Playstation 2 controller:
   The PSX2 controllers look about the same as the first one. My guess is that  they didn't want to change what people are already use to. Its a lot  easier to go back to your PSX game with a controller that is almost identical. Sony has only put 2 controllers ports on the PSX2. Here is the main reasons why. The architecture of the motherboard left only enough room for 2 controller ports, leaving us to shell out more money for a new multi-tap. PSX multi-tap are not compatible with PSX2. Also, the PSX2 controls have analog sensing device. Analog sensors measure how hard and how fast you press a button. The faster you push on the button, the stronger the signal gets. Your PSX controls can be used on your PSX2, but the PSX2 controls ARE NOT backwards compatible to the PSX.
Playstation 2 memory card:
   The PlayStation 2 will come with a HUGE 8MB memory card while still being compatible with your current memory cards. It has a data transfer speed of 250 times faster than the original. The card will use "Magic Gate authentication and encryption security system" to ensure it only works on official PS2 games. Magic Gate has also been employed in interests of data security for the future network applications.
DVD player and hard drive:
The PlayStation 2 will have a DVD drive that is capable of reading both CD-formatted (650 megabytes) discs as well as DVD-formatted discs (4.7 gigabytes). Most of the initial games will be straight CD games, however, as time progresses, more and more games will be produced on DVD. The first DVD game is by Koei, entitled Kessen. Sony announced that it has developed a specially designed spindle that can read both DVDs and CDs from the same laser. Normal DVD readers have two sets of lasers. One to read CDs, and one to read the finer pits of a DVD. This measure of combining the laser functionality will reduce the cost of the PlayStation 2's manufacturing in the long run. Sony has said that, right out of the box, the PlayStation 2 will indeed have the ability to play DVD movies. Sony has official plans to release a hard drive-like peripheral in the year 2001 to coincide with its broader e-commerce plan. It is also reported that the drive will be a massive 50 Gigabyte model. This will enable players to buy games, or perhaps updates to games, as well as movies, and music online. The system may have MPEG2 capabilities also.
- PIMPimitsu (05-17-00)
