The Druzzil Ro Alliance Charter


Formed by various guilds all interested in working together of one mind and accord to bind together with the emphasis on large scale high level raids such as dragons and planes.

This Alliance will allow everyone to keep their guild tags without having to wear the guild tag of a larger scale as well as should better promote unity amidst the people of the server as well as better instill camaraderie and enhance overall gameplay.

People will have a choice in being in a guild and can simply look to the guilds that are members within the alliance, no longer being subjective to join a large scale guild and deal with any unpleasantries that are accrued therein.

Above all, this is meant as an even playing field for all parties that may or may not become involved. These forums and the proposed alliance is created in the interest of the common good and fairness to all.

Any guild leader/master will be allowed operative privlages for the message board, if he/she asks for it. It is urged however that if you request OP privelages, you be willin to offer your support to the alliance and it's cause... If ya do want to be upgraded send me an e-mail at: sparaz@druzzilroalliance.com

Long Live DRA
