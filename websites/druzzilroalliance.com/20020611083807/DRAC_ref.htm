Section A - D.R.A. Council
 


(Part A.I) Council/Council Members -

The D.R.A. is composed of a combined management team. Each guild involved within the Alliance must present one of their officers (or their leader) to act as the councilman representing their union. Each councilman will act as the voice for their own guild, operating as the will of the overall guild itself. Every guild only is allowed only one councilman, regardless of their size or power.

As a council, the council members from every guild work with each other to ensure overall fair, equal, and unbiased treatment as well as benefits from the alliance's entity. Any disagreements, or other possible situations and issues always will be worked out through the will of the council. In the event of a systematic clash of interest or stale state of being the nominated speaker of the council must present discuss possible solutions or options to decide on.

On alliance raids, due to the very complexity of managing a multi-guild event, council members from each guild will form the means of overseeing the raid as a group (although as described in Sec., Part B.III, raid leaders will still manage battle tactics and control. *Raid leaders will ONLY be council members).

* see also, Part B.III.

(Part A.II) Guild Representatives -

Just as their is one council member of every guild, there are only two representatives of each guild. Each guild's representative will act as a advisor and link to their own council member. Representatives should manage the opinions, wants, needs, etc. from their guilds members and relay the data to the council members attention. The representatives & council member can work as a team when discussing issues on the table when in council.
 
Section B - Raiding Relations
 


(Part B.I) Protocol -

In order for large multi-guild raids to be successful, everybody involved must abide by a certain standard of organization during the course of the event. This section is a short designation of the primary points of cooperation required to execute the events.

- Abide by your group leader
In large scale events such as which we will be conducting, leadership and organized control is a difficult task to master. In order to avoid dangerous mishaps or disasters and to ensure fair execution of the event, listen to your group's leader, he/she will be in direct contact with the leading person or body.

- Keep communication to a bare minimum
Please refrain from OOC's, shouts and auction's unless under dire circumstances. Although this protocol is primarily geared towards shouts, OOC's and auctions you should also try to keep normal non-group speech down to avoid added confusion & lag (of course a raid is no fun without some communication, so don't think you have to be dead silent).

- Stay with your group
Do not wander astray from your leader and group at any time during the raid without specific and circumstantial permission. People who wander around because they are impatient have many a time destroyed an entire raiding party.

- Route feedback, opinions, etc. through your leader
Please try not to message the leader or leaders of an event directly, they will be bogged down and under enough pressure as it is. Direct any feedback, comments, questions, or displeasure through your group leader. He or she will relay the message.

(Part B.II) Looting Overview -

This department of events always tends to be the area of disruption. Since many people of the player base are driven by greed and lust for loot, controversy always sticks it's head out from those involved in the event. Understand this is a council and all council members are equally divided between the alliance's individual guilds. The council as it is comprised of has the power of democracy to conduct the fairness of equal loot distribution.

Each guild is allotted it's own share of looting rights, the council decides on which guild gets a certain dropped piece of gear (Weapon, armor, items etc.) after the raid one piece at a time. Equal and fair distribution will be ordained by the will of the council in respect to what and if a guild has already been awarded.

Factors involved in the initial awarding of loot depend on several factors. Level, class and the use a member of a guild will get from any specific equipment will primarily decide the award of equipment during the beginning stage of distribution. The higher a level is a deciding factor mainly because of the help/power that a higher level adds to the raid....

For example if a semi-uber wizard cloak drops the higher wizard will normally receive the item so long as: A. his guild is eligible for a loot item (meaning they haven't already recently been awarded an item for the guild) & B. He will use the cloak, replacing whatever he already is using (if someone is caught selling anything they will be banned from ALL future events to come).

There will be situations where multi-class gear will drop, it is then up to the council to agree on the awarding.

No drop gear will be distributed on the spot. It is not to be looted by anyone except those that the council decides on.

(Part B.III) High End Raid Organization -

Simply a quick breakdown in the management teams and means that will be followed to run the events.

Raid Leader/Leaders: In charge of orchestrating the execution of the event. Commands each group through communication with the groups leaders and with shout/ooc commands. Oversees general timing and placement of group actions as well. Leaders are primarily members of the council unless none are qualified for the zone being raided.

Council: Controls post-raid loot distribution.. Concludes solutions to critical problems or issues during or after the raid. Controls distribution of no-drop flagged loot.

Representatives: Representatives serve a basic role of overseeing the general status of the raid. They also will use auction channel to communicate statements, reminders, or warnings to the zone.

Group Leaders: In charge of keeping their group in check and ensuring that protocol is being obeyed. They serve as a relay-person for the leader/council to their group (communication and orders likewise). Group leaders must also stay watchful of their groups actions, and watch for unjust looting.
