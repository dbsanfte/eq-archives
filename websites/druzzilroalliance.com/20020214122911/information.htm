We are at WAR and HERE is all the Info! - Coming of the Plaguebringer
Greetings everyone. I have spent the past few weeks or so looking into some rather strange happenings in the game that seem to hint there will be another war in game. War? No not a player run war. How many people ever heard of what was called "Bloody Kithicor"? It was the GM run event of 1999 that ended with the plane of hate opening. The core of the battle involved Lanys, Innoruuks daughter...pitched against Firiona Vie and the result of that battle was a fabric torn in reality that left open the plane of hate for adventurers from Norrath.

Now as of late I have been following things that have been happening. Lately the appearance of a mysterious bard who keeps contacting me named Melondar and "forsoothes" of a great war to come that is to pinnacle Bloody Kithicor and end in the plane of decay opening, the thesis...the attempt to summon Bertoxuulous into Norrath by the Bloodsabres.

Now I know alot about this storyline as I had the luck and pleasure to become directly involved in it. I ended up being commanded by word of Mithaniel Marr to raise an army to defend high keep...so that being the case...I ended up learning every detail of the war. I know things most don't and to be honest I wouldn't be surprised if I knew things no one else knew on any server...I know some very obscure facts...but I also know that from my role as leader of the defense forces of high keep when Lanys attacked and we met her forces in Kithicor. What strikes me the most odd is thie Melondar seems to know these same facts...and FYI...Druzzil Ro started as a split server...the war never happened on Druzzil Ro...but it did happen on Solusek Ro and Fennin Ro of which Druzzil Ro was made initially for.

9. The day before the war. Scouts report the dark elven army assembles for battle in the Nektulous Forest. Scouts I sent report General Laarthtik, the Avatar of Hate, Lanys T'Vyll, Donk Crushbone the son of Emporer Crush and an unknown Troll from Grobb also known to be present. The Avatar of Hate and General kill any spies and scouts found on site and all those that appear at the Wizard pyramid.

I deploy all assembled fighting units to the commonlands. War breaks out. Faydwere remains silent all the while. The core of the battle takes place in Kithicor woods. My unit held the gates of high keep and kept the generals from advancing into the karana plains. My group summoned reinforcements to keep the gates of high pass secure as we began our fight against various generals in the pass and succeeded in keeping the gates of high pass secure. The remaining feir dal forces engaged the bridge that lead to north karana to secure it.

Guild Looting - Message from Banegrivm

Now not everyone made this meeting. But the founding guilds that were all present, this was what was AGREED to and something we requested be agreed to by ANY other guilds that joined the Druzzil Ro Alliance. I thought I would repost it all so there would be no confusion.

Basically it was agreed to, looting for general raids went highest level, most in need, those present. A good example being a low level group of people from the alliance go to raid upper guk. They camp the Shin Lord and Ghoulbane drops. Their are two paladins present. The higher level paladin would get the Ghoulbane. Keep in mind that paladin with the ghoulbane will be wielding it later to help other people get items THEY need. So no wizards walking away with warrior weapons etc etc etc. It all goes full circle.

Items go down the line until someone is found in the raiding party that can use the item to replace an older piece of equipment hence bettering themselves.

What if no one needs a drop? The guild leaders get together, lotto the item. Winner takes it and is out on the next guild lotto and the item in question goes to that guild specific. Keeps everything fair and even. Before it comes up, if you have alts and you want something from a raid...bring your alt. Alts that arent present for a raid wont be considered in for the loot. You have to be physically present and physically contribute. It makes it one hundred percent fair to all involved.

A suggestion was made (note a suggestion) that people who receive loot from raids to replace gear might wish to hand down their old items to a guild mate. Keep in mind, this also helps not only your guild and your friends, but the alliance as well.

Dragons and Gods and Special Entity Like Mobs, such as Venril Sathir (sp). Loot from these creatures is lootoed since its the rarest and most prized of all in game. Only those in NEED that can use the items they would be rolling for would be rolling, I.E. no wizards rolling on a cloak of flames, no warriors rolling on the drums off of nagafen etc etc etc.

What constitutes need? Again, replacement current items with said item that dropped. Need doesn't mean to take and sell, doesn't mean to put on an alt that hasn't contributed, it means using the item to replace a piece of older equipment that's not as good as the loot in question. A BIBS replacing a BBC as an example.

The ONLY exception to this rule is when a person ASKS for help to obtain an item and then a raid party is brought together to explicitly help that individual. Meaning when the item is found, it will go by default to the person who the raid was put together for, all other loot and loot drops work as per these loot rules. Paladin X wants to go fight Lucan and a raids put together, paladin X gets the testimony. Later that same paladin arranges a raid to Mistmoore. That same paladin gets the sword hilt off of Xicotl but all other drops follow loot rules.

I think I pretty much summed it up from the meeting.

I briefly spoke with Tair as I thought of something that creates a complication with the later part of the rules. What do we do with people OVER 52 that want to fight nagafen and vox? It isn't fair they are not allowed to fight them because of character advancement. It was suggested "we help them" which I am all for. But at the same time I was wondering...if they help clear and secure the area wouldn't it also be fair that they get a roll?

Helping them is one thing and would be a DIRECT result to it. I am all for that. I am not over 52 so I cannot speak but I have been made to be under the impression that loot in the planes and on kunark and vellious dragons is better then that of Vox and Nagafen, so that being the case if it is indeed true im all for the helping end of it. I am also curious as to furth imput and thoughts as well.

KoS fix to the Freeport Militia - Message from Thallius Trueheart

This faction can be repaired quite easily. I went from KoS to warmly with them as well as the FP thieves, FP merchants, and the Corrupt guards of Qeynos.

You need Amiable faction or better with the craftkeepers in Erudin to do this quest. To get that, kill Elial Brook at the dock in Tox several times. Save the bones he drops and give them to the Mage guild leader in the Erudin Palace for even more faction, but with the Erudin guards, Mages, and Council instead.

Go to this link for the quest that will repair these factions: eqdb.allakhazam.com/quest.html?quest=141

To make this go faster you need 2 people, 1 of whom can root or mez/charm. Have the rooter attack Slansin and get him to follow you up to the third floor near Toresian. Root Slansin there and then get out of cast range and camp. All this time the second person should be hailing Slansin so he will not leave and return to the potion shop. The rooter then logs back on and joins the hailer in doing the quest. Be sure to hail Slansin every minute or two or he will leave the area and you will have to attack him again to get him close to Toresian.

Stand close to Toresian but face Slansin so you can give Slansin the potion immediately after Toresian gives it to you. I was able to achieve approximately 20 faction hits per minute using this method.

Hot keys to make this easier:

Make a Toresian key with the following commands.. (1st line) /target Toresian_Fhabel00
(Second Line) i wish to assist toresian This key will get you the potion with a single click or number shortcut push

Make a Slansin key with the following commands.. (1st line) /target Slansin00 (Second Line) hail Slansin
This key will make it easier to hail Slansin so he will not leave the area.
