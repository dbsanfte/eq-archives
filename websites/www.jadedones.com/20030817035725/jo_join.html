Join Our Guild

Here are the requirements for joining the guild...

  • Be a nice person
  • Be honorable and have a sense of personal integrity
  • Don't be focused on loot
  • Don't be a whiner

Here are the steps for joining the guild...

1) Read our Charter, the Frequently Asked Questions, our Rules and follow the instructions on this page.

2) Post in the Visitors Center and let us know you are interested in joining.  Please answer the questions below to tell us a bit about yourself.

Main Character's Name:
Names of Often Played Alts:
Race/Level/Class of Main Character:
What about our guild caught your attention that you want to join?
What guilds on Zebuxoruk have you been in?
What prompted you to leave them?
Age in Real Life: (this is optional, but its helpful to know)

3) Contact our members in the game and ask to group with them.

4) An officer will contact you when we see you on and ask a few questions to make sure you've read our website and understand what kind of guild we are.  The last thing we want is to have someone join our guild who doesn't understand what we're about.  It is too easy for people who haven't taken the time to read our website to get frustrated by our playing style and then leave, causing a ruckus on the way out.

Note: It is to your advantage (and ours!) if you message us when you see us in-game.  We sometimes forget about the fact that we have applicants in the queue and it slips our mind to look for them when we log on.  So help us remember to get with you and get you tagged.
