Frequently Asked Questions

What kind of guild is Jaded Ones?

We are a small guild that values friendship, honor and integrity above things like racing to see the high end of the game or getting the next piece of uber loot.

Why was the guild created?

The founders of Jaded Ones, as the name implies, got fed up with all the petty politics, insane join requirements and silly complex loot rules normally found in a lot of guilds today.  They wanted a guild where the rules were simple; a place where the people were friendly and of good character; and an environment where they could have fun without the pressures often present in most "uber guilds".

Are you accepting applications for new members?

We are always looking for good people to join our ranks.

What do you look for when considering new members?

The simple answer is:  We only want nice people who aren't worried about loot or gear for their characters.  Please see our Join page for details.

But don't you need good gear to experience the high-end of the game?

Without a doubt, hunting in the high-end zones will require that characters be adequately equipped.  Everyone who plays the game, including us, would like to see our characters decked out in the best gear the game has to offer.  But if this is your exclusive purpose in playing, you will not be a good fit with our style of play.

I don't understand what you mean.  Can you explain this further?

We play to have fun and simply enjoy our online time.  For us this does not mean we have to be focused exclusively on acquiring gear and phat lewtz.  Sometimes we just want to sit and camp greens for faction.  Sometimes we just like to sit at the gates of Qeynos or Freeport and buff newbies all night while we chat and cut up with one another.  Other times we want a good old-fashioned dungeon crawl.  Basically...we enjoy all the variety of things to do that the game offers.  Hunting is fun and so is leveling.  But sometimes our members just aren't up for high-intensity hunting.  Sometimes they just want to kick back and relax while they mindless kill saltwater crocs in Lower Guk waiting for the Ancient Croc to spawn. 

Doesn't sound like you're even interested in seeing the high-end of the game.

Make no mistake, we'd like to go places like Sleeper's Tomb and Vex Thal and the tier 3 planes of power.  We just don't let it dominate our every waking moment of game play.  There is a lot there to enjoy besides just mindlessly plodding on in pursuit of seeing them.  Besides, once you've killed a couple dragons...*shrug*...killing a few more isn't all that exciting.  But if that's what trips your trigger, then go for it.  There are a lot of guilds whose focus is to do just that: loot uber drops off dragons every night of the week.  We just aren't one of them.

Do your members play one character most of the time? Or do they have alts?

Most of our members have a number of alts they play.  In fact, for many of them it's really hard to say which character is their "main".  There are a few members who tend to play one or two characters almost exclusively, but most of us have a variety of alts of varying levels.

What time zone do most of your members live in?

This is one of the really cool aspects of our guild.  We are currently about 50% U.S. and 50% Australian.  We have a couple of European players as well.  So, for the most part, there is someone online around the clock.  Since we are a smaller guild, there may not be a lot of guildmates online, but there are usually a few who you can at least chat with and share your successes with; someone is almost always on to answer questions, cheer and congratulate, and to chat with during lulls in the action.

How do I join?

See our Join page for the details, but the short answer is...post on our website to introduce yourself, do /who all jaded when you're online and ask to group with us.  There's not a lot of pomp and circumstance.  If you've read this page, our rules and our join requirements, an officer will get with you to ask you a few questions and get you tagged into the guild.  But please read our Join page for the details.

How do I become an officer?

As we've said elsewhere on this site, we try to keep things fairly uncomplicated.  We decided a long time ago that you don't need a lot of officers, but you do need enough to make sure there is one around when you need one.  The Guildleaders will decide when it's time to add more officers.  And if an officer leaves the guild or steps down for any reason, it's not an automatic assumption that they will be replaced.  An assessment will be made as to whether or not we feel we need one.  So when the occasion arises that a new officer is needed, the Guildleaders will choose a member who they feel would be a good fit for the role. 

Yes, but what do I need do to be chosen?

Be active.  Follow the rules.  Contribute to the guild in helpful ways.  But even so, this in no way guarantees that you'll be chosen.  Please don't take it personally.  This is just our way.  We've been a solid guild for several years and we want to keep it that way.  Good contributors are not automatically good officers.

What is the role of an officer in this guild?

We tend to view the role of officer as someone who can fill two roles:

1) Someone who can tag new members into the guild

2) Someone to be on hand when situations arise that require an officer and/or guildleader to step in and deal with. 

When would they "step in"?  I mean, what kinds of "situations" are you referring to?

The most obvious situation is when there is a blatant violation of our rules.  It is the officer's job to try to gather information to understand what happened.  If something is getting out of hand, such as someone just being incessantly vulgar and rude in public chat channels--especially guild chat, then the officer's role is to address the situation.  Some people are more comfortable dealing with confrontation than others.  And in any gathering of people, regardless of how like-minded they are on the general issues, sometimes the specifics and details can create some contention.  It happens.  *shrug*  But when it gets out of hand, we expect our officers to step in and help get a lid put on it.

The other situation that comes quickly to mind is when someone outside of our guild needs to talk to an officer about something.  Hopefully we will have an officer online during most of the prime gaming ours for the major time zones in which we have players.  We may not have around-the-clock everyday coverage, but we try to ensure that there is an officer around most of the time.
