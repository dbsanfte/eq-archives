Lanys F.A.Q.

Files

  • Where can I download programs like winzip, icq, or mIRC?

This is a pretty simple one.  First thing you should try is to find a search engine (the answer to 90% of your questions) like Google and Yahoo and do a search for that file.  If that turns up nothing, try other download sites such as C|Net's Download.com. Most of the software found by this method is freeware, shareware, or trialware (software only good for a limited time).

If your looking for full versions of software (better known as warez), your better off asking your friends -OR- do a bit of detective work on the search engines. Don't ask the boards to help you find this stuff.
