Video Card Problems:

Where can I find drivers for my video card?

This is really an easy question, as there are many different ways to do this. Your first stop by any method would be to a search engine like Google or Yahoo.

1. You can do a search for the exact video card you are looking for, i.e. "NVidia GeForce Ti 4200 drivers"

2. Run a search for the video card manufacturer, and that site will normally contain some sort of "downloads" or "support" page that will contain information on how to obtain the drivers in question.

3. Try a drivers site .. just do a search for "drivers", and you'll find a couple of the more popular ones.

If you are still stuck, you might throw up a question on the TECH HELP section of the boards to see if anyone else has the drivers you are looking for.
