New Member Application
Congratulations on choosing to join our guild!  We need more elves like you who will stand up for our homeland!  You must realize by now that the path of an Illuvitae Liberation Fighter is not an easy one.  Every Illuvitae fighter must have complete trust in his comrades.  Therefore, only the dedicated are accepted into our ranks.  If you still think you have what it takes, complete the steps below to be accepted...

(1)  Charter:  New members must read and agree to abide by all of the policies listed in our charter.  Please read the charter in it's entirety before applying.

(2)  Dedicated and Proven:  Illuvitae only accepts those elves that have proven themselves in PvP combat.  To prove their dedication, they must perform the following:

Fight alongside an Illuvitae member who can vouch for your dedication and PvP prowess. Or engage the leader or an officer in a duel.

Also, please visit the roster section, and submit your character's information once you are accepted into the guild.  Thank you, and we look forward to having you join our noble cause!
