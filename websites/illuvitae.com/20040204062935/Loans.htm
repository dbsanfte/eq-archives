Member Loans

 

In occasion, you might want to share some wealth,  for a period of time with friends and good Illuvitae Soldiers. This is a suggested place we keep track of those items. Items cannot be requested. They are lent by the owner to the person fits better. Some people showing more dedication or a strong sense of defense are good candidates. Items should be returned on the owner's request. Members leaving the guild are requested to leave the item to the owner or an officer. Items are not transferable between members. They must be returned to the owner, which in turn will dispatch the item again.
