So, new things have been happening. Very good things. Gotten a lot of "most excellent" people to join us, and we're killing more stuff than before. Case in point; in the first week of Junne we've managed to acquire almsot 30 drops for the guild, all great quality items that were gladly gained. Contrast that to the month of May, where we gained 39 items in the entire month, and you can see why we're upbeat and happy with recent turn of events. Everyone's getting something, and if they haven't yet, it's certain that they will, and given our current rate, they'll probably end up getting 2 or 3 top notch items this month alone!

So what where these upgrades I'm referring to? Here are a few "phat lewt pics" for those that love the stats. I know I do.

Thank you for your interest in joining Menacing Visage!

Before posting your application, please read the following requirements/information:

You must;
- read, understand and accept our DKP/grant system.
- read, understand and accept the guild charter.
- have Resist Gear (100 MR with 100 PR/DR/CR/FR is the requirement with no buffs)
- Choose a Primary and apply with that one character.

MV members vote you in, there's no automatic acceptance, you have to earn the respect and the votes of the guild. Only with a considerable number of votes will you be admitted. It is up to you to contact MV'ers in game for grind groups and raid info to show that you are willing to bust your ass, because we will bust ours to help you. Our "primetime" for raiding and such is approximately 5pm EST to 1am EST.
