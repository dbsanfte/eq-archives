Burrower Dead!

Ring events are a mixed bag. On one hand you have to dedicate hours at a time to a specific event and absolute crap may happen, you wipe and end up with a waste of 3 or 4 hours. On the other hand, it can test your guild as a whole. Making everyone equally responsible for a win or wipe.

We gathered about 35 or so people in the deep and headed to the burrower ring, having seen the event pop earlier that afternoon. For a first shot attempt it seemed like a perfect number for the event since we thought he was slowable.










First few waves go without any serious bumps, we lose a tank and a paladin to waves 4 and 5. It's going smoothly, Rudan and Gagman tank waves 6 and 7 awesomely.


Kill the last wave and we sit and wait for the big worm to pop. Diamsorn sets up to WeaponShield him so we can debuff and slow.

...

Much to Diamsorn's and our dismay... he's not slowable. Yay?

Yep, Diamsorn gets snacked on and Grewd starts tanking. Kudos to our cleric rot crew, 1k flurry mobs must hurt. I turned off my hitpoint bar about 4 minutes into the fight since it was bouncing up and down like a 13 year old loaded up with Mountain Dew.

End result of this luclin version of AoW?



Dead, dead, dead!

Appalon got the FT2 ring, I'll add his little gnomish mugshot here with his loot later. Gratz gnomage!

Congratulations to our badass crew in the Deep, well done all. This was a win made possible by everyone being on the same page. For our first attempt with 35 people, we did better than expected.



Why wizards should apply to MV!
 
Applications!

Thank you for your interest in joining Menacing Visage!

Before posting your application, please read the following requirements/information:

You must;
- read, understand and accept our DKP/grant system.
- read, understand and accept the guild charter.
- have Resist Gear (100 MR with 100 PR/DR/CR/FR is the requirement with no buffs)
- Choose a Primary and apply with that one character.

MV members vote you in, there's no automatic acceptance, you have to earn the respect and the votes of the guild. Only with a considerable number of votes will you be admitted. It is up to you to contact MV'ers in game for grind groups and raid info to show that you are willing to bust your ass, because we will bust ours to help you. Our "primetime" for raiding and such is approximately 5pm EST to 1am EST.
A minimum two (2) week application period is required. MV's officers reserve the right to extend that application period if needed.
