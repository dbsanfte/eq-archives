Recruitment

Menacing Visage is a guild based on simple truths. Honor, Integrity, Family, and a feverish desire to challenge ourselves. We have a great cross-section of EQ players ranging from the part-time dabblers, content to chit chat with good friends when they get the chance to log on, to the hopelessly addicted, ever striving for that next flag and the joys of end-game. We have been growing a lot and thus are able to take on harder and harder challenges. These challenges require discipline and loyalty and Menacing Visage has these traits in spades.

To assure that we stay disciplined and loyal we have certain guidlines for recruitment:
1) There are level limits in effect; the level required is dependent on the class and our need for that class. For the current level limits, please read the sticky topic in the Applicants forum.

2) When applying, a post on the forums in the Applicants section is mandatory. This is so all members have a chance to see who is currently up for application and voice any concerns or praise they might have.

3) After posting in the Applicants section of the forums, a minimum of two weeks hiatus time is also required so that current members can, as mentioned above, discuss the applicant. Note that this is not a maxiumum time; it is a minimum. MV reserves the right to extend the time an applicant is up for membership.

4) Direct approval from three members and one officer is required before an invitation to the guild will be extended. Obviously it is in your best interests as an applicant to get out and group with us, so we might better know you. We tend to recognize and gravitate towards people that take an active interest in the application period. Proactiveness goes a long way.

5) Raiding with MV is optional, but very reccommended, for the application process. Check the Raid Info forum for more information about upcoming raids. We will often go after raid targets without prior warning so it suits you to be in contact with members regularly.

Recruiting directly from other guilds is bad karma, and a practice that will be avoided by all members Menacing Visage. If you wish to say that we would be glad to have them apply, that is certainly another story, but we will avoid pulling members directly from other guilds.

Membership

Members of Menacing Visage will conduct themselves with honor and dignity at all times. We are not a guild of assholes. If you want to be an @#%$ to other players, go play on a Zek server where they can PK you if you lip off. Complaints of verbal abuse, including but not limited to: swearing in public channels, inappropriate conversation in any channel, and arguments in public channels, will all be taken very seriously. In addition, all guild members are expected to not only uphold the Rules of Conduct set forth on the Everquest website, but to encourage others to follow them as well. No ninja looting, kill stealing, fighting over spawns, etc. Use your head. Failure to observe the rules of conduct will result in dismissal from Menacing Visage. (note) As a fiercly loyal group of people that take pride in our screening process we require that any complaints directed against a member in good standing be backed up by logs, screen-shots, etc. submitted in a timely manner. We will not become embroiled in the game of "He Said, She Said" and will always defend our guildies tooth and nail until the preponderance of evidence supports their wrong-doing.

Raids

Our desire to take on challenges and equip our members for even tougher challenges makes raiding a regular event in Menacing Visage. Many raids are planned in advance but due to the nature of some spawns many are impromptu. Raiding is not mandatory but is HIGHLY encouraged. We have instated a Dragon Kill Points (DKP) system that rewards those that are actively involved in improving the guild. The DKP system is designed to benefit perseverance. The greater your attendance at raids the more DKP you earn. Thus, the more use you are to the guild. Thus, we will want you better equipped. More information about the DKP system can be found under the "MV Points" forum. Discipline and knowledge are two keystones to raiding. All members are expected to do their homework regarding specific targets. Members are also expected to be familiar with specific raid tactics and environments. This reduces the number of deaths due to stupid mistakes and cuts down on prep time and recovery. The officers have put many hours of work into providing easy access to this information and it is expected that you to use it.

Equipment

Any loot that drops on guild-sponsored raids will be distributed in one of two ways: according to the DKP system, or at random to those that need it. If there are no members present who need the item, it may be added to the guild fund or banked for multi-quest by someone who can use it. Any item in the guild fund may be used by any member's main character for an indefinite period of time. After two weeks in the guild fund, any item will be open for distribution to twinks that are also members of Menacing Visage, or to be sold. If the item goes to a twink and the need arises for it on another player's main character, the item is expected to be promptly returned. If the item is sold, the money is expected to return to the guild fund. Any members that replace their exising equipment may opt to add their old equipment to the guild fund. In this case the item may not be sold, it may only be used by another member. The original owner will have the right to recall their item at any time. Cash in the guild fund will be used primarily to reimburse casters for reagents used on raids, or as deemed neccesary by the guild council.

Again, thank you for your interest in Menacing Visage. We love this game and those that we consider to be part of the MV family: Remember above all else: It is a game. If you're not having fun, then you're doing the wrong thing.
