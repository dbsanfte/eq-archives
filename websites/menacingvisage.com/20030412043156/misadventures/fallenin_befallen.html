Well I am not sure how much of a MISadventure this is but well it is a shot. Now this may be a bit rusty cause it was from 2 months ago in a "way" n00b guild. We decided to try a CR for a fellow guildy in Befallen, now of course i've never been there but hearing "It is like level 5 to 20", and me being Level 25 was very reassuring. So once the 'few' guildies are together we head into Befallen. We enter the place and me and my friend (The trusty Cleric from my Bio) follow a guild member through the first level door. With turns and twists and Necro and SK fights which were all easy my EGO was booming and I thought I owned the place. Now with only the three of us getting deeper and deeper it never crossed my mind (and I will cover that later). After the other guild member (Well I guess for the sake of ease we will call him BOB), after BOB opened the door he backed up. And in it I saw a lowly green con necro, piece of cake.

"Charge guys", said Jaytaiem triumphantly, <run run run> Now all of a sudden in a flash of an eye the necro is gone and i took 50 points of damage. On top of that I am one level down (Damn Pitfalls). Suddenly 4 green con Skellies agro and of course they double attack, strike for 25 dmg and don't want to die (Again). Friend is bewildered as to where I am at and runs into room. Down he comes.....so now this is where i think the MISadventure has began to start. Of course we die... and of course we are smart enough to bind out side of Befallen... ironic thing is it went from guild corpse rescue to personal corpse rescue.

The score is now Skele 1 PCidiots 0. We run after being hastily escorted to the area...so we decide to mem spells and charge in magic blaring.... we jump down and Xennos grabs his body and runs into next room (lol much to my humor...I'll explain in a bit). None of my spells are working due to me being hit I don't fight back after a bit cause I want to find my body and maybe run. Well I think some of the staff at Sony and Verant were watching me. My body is no where to be seen. Yup that is right it glitched on me. So I try to run and die yet again.

Now we are peeved, went on friendly raid...got hammered 2 times..havent gotten bodies back for anyone.. and oh yeah..my friend died in a hallway that has, like 10 skeles in it...he came out of doorway and was in the middle of a surprise party and dead. With hope running low finally the cavalry arrived (the entire guild a few of which were 50's) and took us in. Feeling safe we ventured in, and well we felt safe again. We got our bodies and were in full group. The 50's went to check a named spawn and left us in a easy room to fight. Well thanks to our ranger puller he goes and pulls a yellow which in turn runs around like an idiot and pulls everything in zone (metaphoricaly speaking). And well everyone dies but me who pushed this little button called "Gate".

Well to end this horrible story they got their bodies back,  Xennos and I went to BB to get boat to FV to never go to Antonica again. And got out of that guild fast as a Gnome being chased by a Dragon.

Thank you for listening to my story it wasn't to good but I hope it does something for you all (even if it is to read to your kids at night when they wont fall asleep). And i hope you all send yours in as well i know there is a MISadventure in all of you.
