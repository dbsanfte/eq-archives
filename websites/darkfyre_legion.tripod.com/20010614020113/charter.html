Now viewing...

 

 

 

 

The Darkfyre Legion, based on Everquest's Luclin server, was originally created back in the beginning of August 2000. Shortly after it's conception, the original guildmaster, Darkrealm, mysteriously vanished and the guild was never fully established. While originally created as the evil counterpart and antithesis of the good-aligned Wildfyre Legion, the guild was re-established on November 16, 2000, with new directions.

Darkrealm and Zerion, two original Darkfyre members voted to have Vendeia, a Cazician Shadowknight with attitude, take the helm and spin the guild in new directions. With the help of her diabolical assistant, Moribond, the foundations for the Darkfyre Legion are being cemented. The following charter has been developed by Moribond, and revised by Vendeia:

***Basics: Darkfyre Legion will now be more than just the antithesis to the Wildfyre Legion. We hope to attract more evil-hearted roleplayers who are intelligent, mature and knowledgeable of key aspects of the game such as grouping. We shall endeavor to seduce more seasoned evil characters to join our fight! Though most of our original members (those who are listed on the roster) have officership status, we should try to limit officership to a selected few, who will each have a set of specific responsibilities (which will be determined at the next guild meeting).

***Meetings: we could have maybe one optional meeting every month and one mandatory. Since we have no druids that can possibly be in the guild and a very limited few wizards, it might prove essential that we set up a Guildhall in every single evil town, and perhaps some other safe, neutral meeting places. Moribond has already found a nice one in Neriak (the Forge House). In Paineel we could possibly meet in the Fell Blade which houses Paineel's smithery, Shadowknight guild, sleeping room and arena! Now in the land of the Iksar we can choose to either befriend them or hate them (this can be discussed at next meeting). If we choose to let the Iksar join us, Moribond has ideas for locations of Guildhalls in Kunark. The advantages to having guildhalls in every town is that we can choose a few meetingplaces and send a high ranking officer to each. All of the officers can be grouped so that they can discuss things in secret and communicate each group's concerns; this will help us with our travel needs.

***Events: In Moribond's words, "We can have an event every month that we have worked out at the mandatory meetings. We could do many things for newbies, many contests, raffles, fundraisers, and various "lets-see-who-can-jump-offa-this-without-dying" contests. Then there is always the beer and fighting. Besides this there could also be a raid every month. [For example, we could] do something every Friday of the month (1-meeting, 2-event, 3-raid, 4-opt. meeting). Then we could do raids on [places like] the West Commonlands where we kill guards (although i'd rather kill Qeynos guards and the like because it fits in better with my character and it is a crappier town)." Thus, this shall be discussed extensively at our next meeting.

***Recruiting: When we are stabilized, we should develop a rigorous training and questing method to gain acceptance to our guild. We should be the best of the best of the bad. Moribond has some interesting ideas such as a ten question quiz, a day's long quest, and training from experts in different fields such as crafmanship and dueling. Then we could hold guildwars!!! and win!!!... we could pick desolate places such as the Everfrost peaks or the Lavastorm mountains.

***Goals: We will plague Norrath with chaos, fear and malice. We will become the most respected (through the havoc we induce) evil roleplaying guild on all of Luclin! We will DOMINATE the Planes bringing back RICHES to all of our guildmates.
