Introduction

Sahiba Serene, from House of Serenity founded this Alliance, Along with Proull and myself, we established the Liberation of Povar.   More as to our beginning and how we got to where we are today can be explained in the LoP History.

The goal of the alliance is to Liberate Norrath of evil. This process is called purification. Purification is accomplished by peaceful means if possible, but more often then not, force is required to achieve our objective.

  Guild Structure

The Alliance is a guild-based structure of command indicated by the leaders of each adjoining house. Membership is decided by each participating house, which instructs and cares for its own members. Currently there are eight houses, which make up the Alliance. The leaders and/or seconds and the council preside over all the houses, although those with rank of guild second are also in command as officers. The leader of each house leads in unison for the better of everyone, through the council.

  Objectives

The Alliance is bent on the liberating of the realm of Norrath, Luclin and the Planar Realms of all evil. This is done through the skills, and methods granted to us by the gods.

The Alliance is composed of individuals who have sworn their loyalty to the alliance and have abandoned their past lives to serve the greater goal.

  Expectations

All members of the Alliance will respond to situations in a professional manner. Do not lose your cool. As often times others will wish to bring out the worst in you. Loyalties to other members and to the Alliance’s ideal are paramount. No member of the Alliance will ever speak out against another in public, as this would harm the Guild’s reputation. Role-play is not a major requirement, but most practice it, and it always adds to the fun.

As an alliance we are opposed to hacking of any sort, and will not condone such actions. The Alliance consists of a group of brothers and sisters who share a common goal, the liberation of Norrath, Luclin and the Planar Realms. Respect is expected of all the members and is given in return. We are the Alliance! We will be victorious!

Recruitment

Guilds desiring to join the Alliance must have a sponsor and complete an application, which will be forwarded to the Council Coordinator of Recruiting. The application will be reviewed, and the sponsors contacted to verify the information provided by the applicant.

The Council Coordinator of Recruiting will interview the guild for further information, and to show support and to schedule the assistance of such guild to join us in raiding and the like.  The Council Coordinator of Recruiting will notify the entire alliance to announce the new guild. He/she will also notify the leader of that guild, that they have been accepted and will be added to our alliance.

 Role-play

The Alliance role-play is that of a superior being. We have boards available for posting at our Web site. As for the compound this is where most of the role-playing is done.   Your Role-playing is not mandatory but as you will and should be fun

Trash talk is NOT appropriate posting for The Alliance. As superior beings, we do not feel it necessary to "run our mouths" at the drop of a hat. We prefer to argue with logic, and to back ourselves up with action, not easily spoken words. Clearheaded, well thought out, posts is the Alliance’s means of argument in the public boards.    

Alliance Code of Conduct

Kill stealing or monopolizing of an area of green cons, preventing some one else from gaining experience will not be tolerated.

Use of zone channels such as /ooc or /shout to argue or state opinions that would reflect badly on the guild in the alliance will not be tolerated.

Use of alliance channel to complain, argue, borrow money or be disruptive in any way is forbidden.

Use of alliance channel to discuss illegal activities is strictly forbidden.  

Foul language is not permitted on alliance channel

Begging, or spamming in alliance channel is prohibited

Unless told otherwise, the Leaders/Officers should be addressed as "sir or ma'am".

There are other things that you will find that are more or less rules, but this is a general set of guidelines to follow for all members. In or outside of a raid environment. There are raid specific guidelines that are accessible from the main page, the button is named “Raiding”.
