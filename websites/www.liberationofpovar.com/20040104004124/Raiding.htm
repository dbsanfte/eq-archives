Introduction

Please keep in mind, these are not the only rules and they are not set in stone. These are simple guidelines and I am not the raid God. Not all of the raids run will be like this, but we decided to throw this out so people can get their "game faces" on and know what should be expected during a raid.

A raid will only be successful when it’s members do what they should be doing without being asked. We don’t have the time to hold everyone’s hand and tell them what they should be doing. I will put it down in writing what is expected of raid members in general, and from each of the classes in hopes of keeping everything crystal clear.

Raids will at this point have no standard set day or schedule. We will all meet at the appropriate ring/wizard spire, and go from there. Raids will be mobs/areas that we know we can handle. Its foolish to think we’re going to run right out and start slaying dragons or doing ToV. We must crawl before we can walk, and we must walk before we can run. We are not Triton, R.A., or B.o.L., We will get there, but first we must learn to raid together and work as a team.

Some general rules of Thumb prior to raids are:

Be there 15 minutes early

Don’t ask until raid time where we are going to raid

You must be appropriate level to participate in the raid being hosted.  Example would be if we are doing a planar raid, you must be at least level 46, and if it is a lower level raid then you should be that level, as a level 60 would not do more then be someone who buffs.

Make sure if you need casting components to have them on you. If you need arrows, throwing weapons, or other weapons on you, make sure you have them.

Make sure to have your spell particle effects off and your settings turned down to prevent lag.

Be prepared to leave the raid with less experience than you started with ROFLOL J.

Have enough food/water on you to survive for the duration of the raid, now the raid may not last that long, but you don’t need to be asking for food or drink in the middle of a battle.

Make sure your backpacks are empty and everything is banked that is not needed for the raid. Our buffers should not have to continually cast strength buffs on you because you are over encumbered from other battles.   

If we are doing an epic mob, and you're the one whom we are killing the mob for, make sure that you have all the needed components in your bag that may be required to summon, pop or ready a mob for the take down.

Make sure you have any resist gear you need on you.

Make sure you have paper and pen handy. You won’t always take notes, but it’s nice to have if needed.

Make sure you use the restroom, make phone calls, and inform your family/friends that you’re busy, and have enough munchies and drink on hand before you start to raid. We don’t need a puller dying because his/her cleric decided they needed a soda or just had to use the bathroom when they were pulling a mob with 10k + hit points.

Arriving at the Raid:

Disband groups and put your /lfg on, the Raid leader will assign groups, based on class/ability

Check your ego and personal problems at the zone and simply listen to the raid leader. There can be only one Raid leader, and you are not it. Don’t question, just do.

The raid leader will some times appear gruff, bossy, demanding, they don’t always ask please, Remember, they are the one with the plan, and if you don’t follow the plan, it will fail. That is their job, they will not cuss you out, but they will tell you to do things to make the raid work, and work smoothly. Don’t take it personally.

Listen, Listen, LISTEN, I cannot stress that enough. If you don’t listen, you could die, or be the cause of whole groups dying.   Do you really want to be remembered as the one who got the raid wiped?

If you are a caster .. Make sure you bind!!!!! We need this of Druids, Wizards, Mages, and Clerics especially

Mages start summoning resist gear and mod rods , Clerics start group buffing when you get your groups, Chanters start handing out Clarities, haste, etc, Druids start casting circles and buff, Shamans start buffing and shrinking. DO NOT summons pets, they are useless in raid environments.

Casters make sure to have simple /assist keys set up, not a lot of garbage on them. Casters that are casting on mobs to slow them, cancel magic on them, snare them, de-buff them, have simple keys set for this, and have them set to /g /rs, /ooc, and room.

Melee have simple assist keys, stating who you are assisting, and what you are assisting on set up. Have a key set for Off tanking, to inform if you are doing so, Pullers have simple pull keys set up. Everything scrolls enough in this environment without added words and junk.

Clerics, Druids, Shamans, and Paladins, have simple /assist only keys set up so you can target the mob and heal whomever has agro if needed. Also have a key set to /target (main puller) and /target (main assist) and /target (main cleric)

Group Leaders:

Always report when you are asked about group status. But do not just throw it out there when not asked.

If some one in your group, or your whole group needs a buff, please ask that buffer in tells, DO NOT clutter Alliance or Raid channel with requests for buffs.

Make sure your group has Assist keys set for the MA, and MA Cleric. Also make sure they have one simple /assist key made so that they can target the mob, and assist to see who it’s aggroing.

Make sure to let the Raid leader know if there are any changes in your group, such as a LD, or some one having to leave.

Do Not change the make up of a group without the Raid leader’s approval.

Group leader will be that groups speaker, and will turn over all looted items to the RL for /Random If it’s no drop, DO NOT loot the item, just list in /ooc or /rs for the RL

The group leader is assigned by the raid leader. Period, no questions asked, and the group needs to listen to the group leader as well as the Raid leader.

The group leader is responsible for the whereabouts of their party, If someone is link dead shout it, if some one is afk .. Shout it , if some one is lost, or lagging, or preoccupied .. Shout it.

Groups:

Group make-up will be determined by the raid leader. Do not come into a raid expecting to be grouped with your friend, this may not happen. Of course it would be poor form to complain about this, because you’re here to raid not to socialize.

Ideally, there will be groups of 6 people each, but this may not always be the case. If you don’t have 6 then don’t worry about it because the leader is aware of this.

Keep in mind there are never enough of each class to go around, so you may have a group with 1 druid, 1 mage, 1 wizard, 1 cleric, and 2 warriors. But hey it’s a group and you’re included. Don’t complain

Do not change the make up of the group without the leaders approval

Be prepared to die more than once.

Don’t complain when you do

Don’t complain about the length of time it takes for CR’s

Party members should refrain from reporting the status of the group unless asked to do so by the group leader.

Come prepared, if you can print maps, do so.

Pulling:

There will be one main puller assigned by the raid leader

You should never suggest to the main puller what to pull, If there are 12 mobs out there that will drop stuff for your class, don’t tell them that. The puller does not need a backseat driver.   If you are found doing this to the puller you will be removed from the raid, no questions asked.

When a pull is brought to camp, do not run up to the mob and meet the puller to attack. Please allow the puller to position it so it is a safer fight

If you see a wandering mob, alert the raiding party, but DO NOT pull the mob in to camp

Looting :

One person will be assigned as the Master Looter (ML). This person is responsible for knowing who is in each group, their class, and their level. The ML will be responsible for retrieving items of value from group leaders, and /Random them.

When the corpse is looted, the ML should state in /rs what was looted. In the case that it’s money, simply state money.

If an item that drops is class specific then the ML will ask for tells, from those that can use the item. If you are able to be in on the roll, and want to be in on the roll, you will then need to send a tell to the ML .

If you do not send a tell (and only a tell) to the ML you will not be included on the random. If you say it /ooc, /rs, /shout or anything else .. You will not be included.

Once you win a roll for a class specific item, you are out of class items until everyone in that class has gotten an item.

Just because you are at a raid, does not mean you’ll get an item, its all luck of the draw.   Make sure to make a hot key that is set to /Random 1 1000

These are the raid rules, study them, follow them and be prepared.  If you are one of the few that are constantly being reminded of the rules, during the raid you may be asked to leave the raid.

Thanks and may the Gods bless us with successful raids.
