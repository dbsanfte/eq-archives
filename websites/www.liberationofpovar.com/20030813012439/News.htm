NEWS April 2nd, 2003

Grats to Laliana and Kaitain!

After getting friends together and hiring mercenaries like 60+ warriors and a cleric, they were able to go speak with Venril Sathir's remains in Karnor's Castle.  After speaking with the remains, Laliana summoned a globe of the fireflies in agreement that he would help.  But then VS proceeded to go back and rest in piece in his throne room, not holding his end of the bargain, complaining about not being alive as he once was.  But then the hired hand gave him a resurrection spell scroll.  Then suddenly after getting what he wanted, VS after being summoned made the mistake of attacking us.  We were prepared for we did not trust this cursed being.

It was not long before he was put back to rest and paid for his crimes of deceiving us.  Laliana & Kaitain were able to get the much desired Pulsing Green Stones from him.  Laliana & Kaitain must now go to City of Mist and fight off the dark forces there to retrieve a Jade Reaver, one of the dark lords servants, the Black Reavers weapons, if in tact after hard battle they remain, they shall loot one from them in order to please their goddess in hopes to receive their prized gift.  But not before they finish a few more tasks.  Like all travelers who seek their Gods approval and prize, there is always a quest to take.

NEWS March 28th, 2003

Last night 3 groups from various guilds of the LoP banded together to go and help Willian go after the seahorse for himself.  This is starting to look like a routine raid for the LoP.  The poor Tainted/Corrupted Seahorse has got to be sick of seeing us.  The look on his face when he thought the ranger Kaitain was alone, chased after him, finding 13 people waiting to jump him.  Then when the Corrupted spawned, the real villain of this story, they took him out as well.  Then Willian looted the Kedge Crystals and is readying himself for Faydedar tonight.  Best Wishes to Willian tonight as he confronts the giant lizard, that some have mistaken for a dragon.

There was one casualty last night, as poor Healary faced a few bully fish and mermaids who decided to make a delicacy of her.  But after a few issues she was able to be revived after Kaitain retrieved her corpse from the gangly shark who was hovering over.  Sorry Healary, glad we could get you back after a few problems, you help out a lot being the one of two clerics at the raid.
