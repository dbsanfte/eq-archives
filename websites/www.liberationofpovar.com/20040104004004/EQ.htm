If you stumbled onto our web page and are wondering what exactly this is all about, then this page will explain and direct you to a wonderful, fun and adventurous game.  Click on the icons to be teleported to information about each land you read about here.

The game is set during a time when all the races of Norrath are caught on various sides, each not only defending their lands, but also venturing off to do what their heart desires, whether that be for good or evil, is yet to be known.

Throughout the years many battles have waged and many lands were destroyed, some had to move onto other places to live, to start over after experiencing the affliction of the evil ones.  Some even facing off the Gods.

Some lands were thought to have been destroyed, disappearing for many years to only become myth.  It was the world as we knew it, Norrath.  Then thru the courageous few, Kunark the mythical island was discovered.  Along with this discovery, we found and opened the books on the long lost Combine Empire, and the finding of the lizard people, the Iksar.

Here we find rumors of the fabled Shissar, a other race of lizard type race, but more snake like in story then the Iksar.  Here we find more dragons, and the many ruins which show the lost lands to be a dreadful place to venture.  It is here the High Elves, in search of finding their Queen, Fironia Vie, and a statue erected in her remembrance.

Then it was some time later, that the mysterious and frozen lands of Velious were discovered, and an old race of dwarves, were found.  But these dwarves were calloused and frozen in their ways, having built for themselves, Thurgadin, a city, built in the frozen mountains of the Great Divide.

Here in Velious we find that there is a great war between 3 factions.  The Dwarves, the Giants and the Dragons.  Which side will you choose in your struggle.  Much is to be gained in the lands of what was once home to the great dragon, who scared the lands after a great war.

Meanwhile the great wizard Al'Kabor was researching what had been revealed about the Combine Empire, and the mystery behind the Wizard Spires.  Through careful study the wizard was able to discover the power, and using the help of other wizards, they opened the portal and discovered the Nexus.

In the Nexus were pathways to the city known as Shadowhaven, and the lore about the truth to the empires demise was found.  What was left of the empire had fled to the ringed moon and then from there, separating and dividing themselves into 2 factions.  It is here the city of Shar'Vahl, the cat people known as the Vah'Shir were discovered.  A Godless and very neutral race.

During this time the rumors about the supposed snake people, the Shissar were found, and their emperor, who according the the Iksar, was thought to be dead, was found alive and well in his temple.

It was then about a year later the mysterious gates were opened and the Gods seem to beckon us.  The portals lead to the Plane of Knowledge, where even more lore can be found about the history of which I have explained, and even more as to the great wars, and history of each race.

From there one may venture if they are so brave, as to face the Gods on there own lands, each full of its guardians, and those who would meet such protectors, should they pass without permission.

During this time where many search their way into the Planar realms, the trolls were forced out of their home shortly after facing a battle with an unknown faction of pirates, who seek a stone of great power.  Now the Frogloks after being blessed by the God Mithaniel Marr, The Lightbringer.  Have claimed Grobb to be their new home.

Now the Trolls live in Neriak, home to the Dark Elves, and it is there the dark races of Norrath now look, in anticipation of what may come. Will there be more war, or will more unexpected lands be discovered, realms opened?  

As the world turns, the moon holds it's shine and the Gods beckon, I shall continue to write about the coming years.
