Through your journeys in Norrath, you have often heard of whispers, strange tales in old tattered bars.  Then you happened upon a scroll, something of mystery in old elvish, hard to read.  Though for a price you found the translation, the location of such an underground passage leading to a great city, The Shadowhawkes Haven.

  Now upon entering the cave, after going around and under a harsh, yet beautiful waterfall.  You discover a forest, rich and lush, some parts of it even have fresh fruit growing.  Then you see it, the huge gates of emerald.  Covered with vines to show its life pouring from around it, as if magical.  At the gates stand camps with fires, guards ready to face off any opposition, which might dare.

  The guards seem to be all the same, yet once closer you notice, humans, elves and other familiar races, even some of the darker races, all together, enjoying the company as if your eyes were cheated by some spell.  It is here you’re welcomed into the Thirsty Dragons Tavern.  , enjoy your stay and if you should need anything, don’t hesitate to ask.  Our home is your home. If you should be wondering about our alliance, or have a need or request; use the magic jewels here to be teleported to the appropriate destination of your desire.
