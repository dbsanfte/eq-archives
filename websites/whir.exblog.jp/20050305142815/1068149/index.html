Ranger Epic 1.5 Walkthrough >> Step 1-11
Epic 1.5 LONG walk thru (work in progress)(Ranger's Glade)

Step 1.
---------
East KaranaのAlthele (Loc = n3650 n1600)と会話。会話をするにはSwiftwindが少なくとも必要。Earthcallerは必要なし。

You say, 'Hail, Althele'
Althele says 'Hello, friend. Beautiful is what I would call such a day normally, but lately? I sense that something is [out of balance].'
You say, 'what is out of balance?'
Althele says 'I don't know for certain what is wrong. It feels like the start of a cold, a sort of tickling at the back of Norrath's throat, if I may make such a poor metaphor. In much the same way that I can sense that you have wielded the power of nature, probably in the form of the weapons known as Swiftwind and Earthcaller, I can sense a power of illness creeping upon the land. Please, if you come across anything suspicious bring it to me. I am worried, this does not feel like a natural sickness to me. Go with the blessing of the Mother and the speed of the Storm, my child.'

Step 2.
--------
Rathe MountainsでForage

Sickly Maiden's Hair
LORE NO DROP
WT 0.2 Size: Tiny
Class:ALL Race:ALL

Step 3.
-----------
EKのAltheleの所に戻り、Sickly Maiden's Hairを渡す。

Althele says 'This plant has an illness that I have never sensed before.' Althele pauses in thought for a moment. 'You are a hunter, so I shall put your skills to work. First, take this to Corun in Surefall. He is an expert on animal illnesses. Then put your hunting skills to work and see if you can capture any animals that may have eaten this plant. Corun will want to see them to help him discover what the sickness is.'

以下のItemを受け取ります。

Tuft of Sickly Maiden's Hair
WT 0.2 Size: Tiny
Class: ALL Race: ALL

Step 4.
--------
Surefall GladeのCorun Finiscの所に行く。Druid Guildの近くをRoamしてます。
Amiable以上のFactionがあることを確認。
Top Slotに空きがあることも確認。Containerを渡されます。

Tuft of Sickly Maiden's HairをCorunに渡す。

Corun Finisc says 'Well, this is certainly an unpleasant sight. If Althele sent you to me it must be because I've had a lot of experience with sick animals. If you can find me some animals that have become ill by eating this plant perhaps I can discover something about this disease. You must not kill these animals. It is never good to harm an animal if you do not need to. It is also important that the animal be alive when you take samples of their blood. If any animals have eaten this plant and become ill, you need to find a way to capture them and use your fletching kit and these vials to gather blood samples. Althele is rarely wrong about what she senses from Antonica. Be carefull with that medicine bag, I borrowed it from a friend in Halas a while back. I'll need you to use it to store some samples as you capture the animals. Don't lose it, you don't want Lars angry with you!'

以下のItemを受け取ります。

Borrowed Medicine Bag
LORE NO DROP
WT: 0.6 WR: 0%
Capacity:6 Size Capacity: SMALL

と、以下のVialを6個受け取ります。

Magical Sample Vial
NO DROP
WT: 0.2 Size: TINY
Class:ALL
Race: ALL

これらのItemはしばらく使わないので、Bankに入れてOk。ForageとMob Killがしばらく続きます。

Step 5.
-------
Fletching kitと、Smithing Hammerを準備します。Fletching KitはPlanarかCorrupsedでもokです。

Step 6.
--------
Dranik's ScarでForage。
以下のItemを手に入れます。

Fallen Tree Branch
LORE NO DROP
WT: 1.0 Size: Medium
Class: All Race: All

もし、以下のItemを拾ったら、壊さずに保管で。

Golanda Nut
NO DROP
WT:0.5 Size:SMALL
ALL/ALL

Step 7.
-------
以下の、High Level OoW zoneに行きます。
Wall of Slaughter
Muramite Proving Grounds
Harbingers Spire
Nobles Causeway
etc...

Golanda Nutsを8個Forageします。DSでも出るのですが、High Level zoneの方が拾いやすいです。

Step 8.
--------
Jaggedpine ForestかNedarias Landingで以下のItemをForage。

Pine Sap
LORE NO DROP
WT:0.2 Size:TINY
ALL/ALL

'Jaggedpine Sap' も拾えますが、これは使えません。

Step 9.
-----------
Dragon NecropolisでHunt。

Phase Spidersから以下のItemを4個入手します。

Phase Spider Silk
NO DROP
This item can be used in Tradeskills
WT:0.4 Size:SMALL
ALL/ALL

Step 10.
---------
Abysmal Seaでお買い物。
Bankの正面にいる、以下のNPCから以下のItemを買います。

Tasec Dreeng (-80, 205)
Duath Untez (-60, 240)

Whittling Knife
LORE NO DROP
WT:0.3 Size: TINY
ALL/ALL

価格は5SP。Listの下の方にあり、「Shiv」と同じIconです。

Step 11.
--------
Mobを捕獲するためのItemを2個作ります。
全てのCombineは、Trivial15で、NO-FAIL Combineです。

Note：Tradeskill UI interfaceで、RecipeをSearchして作った方が良い。Experimentで作るには、最低でもFletching Skillが100は必要である模様

Smithy HammerとGolanda NutをFletching KitでCombineすると、以下のItemが出来ます。

Fibrous Rope
NO DROP
WT:1.2 Size: SMALL
ALL/ALL

Smithy Hammerは返ってきます。
これを8回繰り返します。

Fibrous Ropeを8個Fletching Kitに入れてCombine。以下のItemが出来ます。

Eight-loop Lasso
NO DROP
Slot: PRIMARY
Skill: 1H Slashing
15/40
Effect:Eight-loop Lasso (combat)
WT:10.0 Size:LARGE
ALL/ALL
Slot 1, Type 4


Phase Spider Silk(unstacked)とPine SapをFleching KitでCombineすると以下のItemが出来ます。

Phase Netting
LORE NO DROP
WT:1.5 Size: MEDIUM
ALL/ALL

Fallen Tree BranchとWhittling KnifeをFletching kitでCombineすると以下のItemが出来ます。

Long Net Pole
LORE NO DROP
WT 0.9 Size:MEDIUM
ALL/ALL

Phase NettingとLong Net PoleをFletching KitでCombineすると、以下のItemが出来ます。

Large Butterfly net.
NO DROP
Slot: PRIMARY
Skill: 2H Blunt
15/40
WT 10.0 Size:LARGE
ALL/ALL
Slot 1, Type 4
