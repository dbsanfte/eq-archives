Kaylie Miragrel

 

Ht: 5'1"
Wt: 71lbs.
Build: Corpse-Like
Hair: White
Complexion: Pale
Eyes: Blue
Age/Level: 24
Race: Dark-Elf
Class: Necromancer
Diety: Innoruuk
Marital Status: Single, never been married.
 
 

I was found one day wandering through Neriak, unknown to anyone. I didn't know who my parents were, nor did I know where they went. I was cold, weak, hungry, and above all, I was without clothes. Illiana found me and helped me regain my strength. She gave me food and shelter with her in her home in Neriak. She started instructing me on the ways of the Tier'Dal, as she called it, and taught me some of the powers that Tier'Dal children have, and of following the god Innoruuk. I chose all the powers that came with being a Necromancer. Illiana taught me the ways of the Necromancer and got me my first robe, and instructed me how to use my spells. She helped me all through my early years as I grew in power. Illiana was like a mother to me; she gave me lots, and helped my mind grow stronger, to the point where I would be able to venture off on my own. I wandered through the lands killing animals for food, and killing the undead that wandered in front of my path. I studied words and scriptures and grew in power. I was able to conjure up skeletal minions to do my bidding as I stole life away from my targets. I finally moved away from my home in Neriak at the age of 15. Exploring, in search of wisdom, and all sorts of artifacts. I ventured through oceans and continents. I found another evil city called The Overthere, where I set up my new home. I was all-alone for the first time, but constantly surrounded by other Tier'Dal travelers. I hunted in these lands, purchasing scriptures in order to gain more power and conjure up more powerful evil minions to do my bidding. I am at the age of 24, and that is still my home today.

Death brings light to the lucky,
Death brings darkness to the cursed.
And should  the darkness seize control of your last breath,
I will seize control of your fears.
I will seize control of your soul.

I am Kaylie, evil child of Innoruuk.
