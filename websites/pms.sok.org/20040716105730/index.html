The End of SOK in SWG
Posted by Alex on Thursday, May 27 2004

The time's come to close the doors on the Sterling Order of Knights branch in SWG. Surefoot and I wanted to wait until the space expansion but the knight support in this game is too lacking. It was good while it lasted, and I think it's still a wonderful game. I personally wish it wasn't scifi, but the systems are great. I think the game is too casual-focused and ingame-reliant while encouraging solo gaming. Our crew tends to be more social and lifestyle-gamers. More importantly, I think in our time in Star Wars SOK has done everything we wanted to do or could have wanted to do. We achieved all our goals, we've slain those imperials, built a successful city from a deserted island, killed all the baddest creatures in the galaxy again and again from Endor to Lok to Dantooine while exploring the galaxy inside and out, partied with our friends, mastered all the classes and skills, made tens of millions of credits, crafted all our own weapons, food, homes, furniture, armor, droids, medicine, etc, made quite a few good friends for the future, and even had a jedi in our ranks. We pwned the game and moved on. So it's with no regret that I give this announcement. See you in the next game.
