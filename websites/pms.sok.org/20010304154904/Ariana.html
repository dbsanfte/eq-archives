Name: Ariana
Age: 18
Home: Felwithe
 

I was conceived on the eve of May 1st, amidst the dancing and merrymaking happening around the bonfires. My mother Gladiolo, who was the fairest in the land and the May Queen of that year, died while giving birth to me. I am a  merry-begot, much beloved by the citizens of Felwithe. No one knows who my father is, it could have been a villager, or a man of noble blood. So the citizens of Felwithe raised me, and a very sheltered life I led. I never knew about the world outside of the city gates, everything I needed or wanted was provided for me.

I kept hearing whispers and gossip that made me curious of Faydark Woods.  So one night, I made a plan to see the world. I went to bed fully clothed, waited until my caretakers fell asleep and snuck out of my cozy home and
past the city guards. What I saw beyond those gates enthralled me, I felt so free that I found myself dancing and singing throughout the woods! Ahead of me, I saw a few campfires lit, and was elated! New people to meet! I danced
right over to the fires and my blood froze at the sight that I encountered.  People  were in battle with ugly creatures, and screaming "Kill the Orcs!" At this point one of those creatures turned to me, and grinned evilly. As he swung
his sword at me, I ran, ran back to my beloved city, praying to Tunare as I fled. This beast was draining the life from me as he hacked and as I fell, I realized my life was over. Knowing that I was dying, cursing my first and last encounter with evil, flashes of light seemed to come out of the sky.

My next memory is of waking up in new surroundings, what seemed to be an Inn of some sort, and my wounds were healed. Looking down upon me, an elderly wizard. He calmed my fears, and told me tales of Norrath that I'd never
fathomed. His stories fascinated me, and as he told them, I had the growing conviction that I would do what I could to combat evil. I learned of the Paladins and knew that was my calling. The elderly wizard, who's name I have sworn never to reveal showed me the ways of the Paladin, or what he knew of them. When the time came that he felt that I could hold my own against most foes in the land, we said our goodbyes. It is the first, and the last, time that I shed tears.

I have been roaming around Norrath on my own, fair to say, I have few friends, and every new battle seems to harden my heart abit more. What a far way I have come from that long lost innocent merry-begot. During my
travels, I stumbled across a strong group of women I have grown to admire. They told me of a guild of Sisters, bound together against all conflict. I set upon my next quest, to join them...
