Naladieni

How to describe my life?  How to describe what I know so little of?

I guess I can begin by Introducing Myself.  My name is Naladieni, and I am a High Elven Cleric of Tunare.  How did I come to take this path in life?  Well, I was once just a little girl, playing in Felwithe, when I walked into the Clerics guild.  At first, I thought "Whoa, those sure are some pretty lights that they are makin'!"  I asked what they were doing, and they said they were healing a injured man.  I looked to the corner and saw a High Elf, Wounded in battle.  As they cast their magics on him, I saw his wounds disappear.  I was in awe.  I asked then if I could learn these magics, as one thing I loved to do was to help people.  They told me to come back when I was older.

So I did.  I went back and learned all that I could from High Priest Yeolarn Bronzeleaf.  He taught me all that I needed to know to become a Priestess of Tunare, A cleric.  I practiced day and night, until I felt comfortable.  I then travelled over to Antonica.

After I arrived, I found more things to try to show to Tunare.  Alas, the Deathfist Orcs would not listen to me, Nor would the Dervish Cutthroats. None on the former continent of the Elves would listen.  Dejected, I headed back to the city, and heard laughter and song coming from a tavern.  Being one of the few places open late at night, I walked in...

And that is how I found the Sisters.
