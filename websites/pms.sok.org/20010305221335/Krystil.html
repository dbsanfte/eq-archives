Krystil
Dark Elf
Necromancer

    I was born in Neriak sixteen years ago.  My parents left me when I was ten, they went to join a hunting group that was traveling to Kunark. I have not seem them since.

    They had left me in the care of a friend of theirs, a member of the Lodge of the Dead. His name was Kilanth and he was a very powerful necromancer. He decided, after a year had passed since my parents departure, that I was now his child and that he was going to teach me to become a necromancer as well.  Kilanth was a wonderful teacher, even when the guild leaders would grow angry at me for messing up on a spell, Kilanth would softly tell me try again. He never showed any anger towards me.  When he finally decided to take me out of the city and into the forest I saw his dark side emerge. He waved his hands in front of him and out of the ground a skeleton seemed to "grow". Its yellow eyes gleamed with fury and its bones creaked and groaned with each movement.
 

     The pet watched over me as a practiced my spells on Moss Snakes and Black Wolves. I gained seasons fast under the watchful eye of Kilanth , he saw always there to protect me. Once I reached my fifth season Kilanth told me that I must now start to hunt on my own. I was scared when he told me this, but I did not show it. I did not want my "father" to think me weak. After giving him a brief hug I summond a pet of my own and took off in the direction of the Commons.

     Upon arriving the mountain pass that would lead me to the Commons I saw something I had never seen before. It was a young woman with the same build as me but yet her skin was pale. I had heard stories of these creatures, but in the books they were described as being ugly. A woodelf.  The woodelf was meditating but opened her eyes as I neared her. She stood quickly and glared at me. "Ugh, disgusting Inky! What are you doing this far from your cave? Pathetic worm!" She cast a spell and she disapeared...her laughter echoing all around me. I ran from the sound and out into the open air of the Commons.  I was shaking with fury, 'How dare she call me that!?' I have done nothing wrong to her.

     I suppose I was so angry that my judgement was impaired, for out of no where it seemed a huge beast leaped at me. It growled and spit at me, it held a huge two-handed sword that it raised at me. I sent my pet to attack it but it seemed to be of no good. The beast brought its sword up then smashed my pet to pieces. I tried to back up but tripped on a fallen branch. I landed on the ground with a heavy thud and groaned in pain. The beast came at me with its sword and cut deeply into my leg.

     I screamed loudly in pain over and over. Screaming for someone to help me. The beast drooled as it smiled evilly and brought its sword up again. Then I heard....her.  A loud yell filled with fury coming from behind the beast. "DIIIEEEE!!!!!!!", the woman yelled and swung her sword at the beast. It cut completly through the creature...it fell in two pieces on either side of me. That is when I fainted.
 

     I awoke to find myself in a room above a tavern. My rescuer sat across from the bed in a chair. She stood up when she saw my eyes open and walked over to me. "Well, hello there. I thought you might never wake up", she grinned and pointed to my leg. "I had one of the Sisters heal your cut for you." I looked at my leg then back at her confused. "Sister?" The woman smiled again, "Oh, I'm sorry dear, I havent yet introduced myself. My name is Androdameia. I am the guild Mistress of the Pretentious Malevolent Sisters. You are in out Tavern at the moment. When you feel well enough you might want to go and introduce yourself to the rest of the guild." I nodded briefly and spoke. " I really ought to get home, my father will be missing me...". Androdameia answered,"We can send him a message if you like. I really would like you to meet the rest of the guild."Androdameia smiled again, " I think you might just fit in here."
 

And now here I am, a member of the Sisters. *grins*
