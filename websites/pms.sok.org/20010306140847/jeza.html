Jeza
Human Necromancer

Dalharil of shadows, robed in black,
Pale-skinned child of Neriak;
Her anger flaming, hot as coals
She is valsharess of decaying souls

Of ssins d'aerth born to this earth
She learned to weave and chant the curse
She is jabbress of the undead forms,
Ulathtallar of diseasing storms.

Colnbluth in the only home she knows
Yet, in her heart, like them, deep indigo.
Og'elund, always, yet not of the light
Inside, her soul is dark as night.
