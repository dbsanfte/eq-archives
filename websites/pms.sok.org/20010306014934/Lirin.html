Lirin Khilaenm’ea
Daughter of Truth's Fire
Half-Elf Paladin of Mith Marr

Background:

Lirin's mother didn't even bother to put her name down on her birth certificate. She listed her race - high elf, and her occupation - enchanter. The clerics at the temple of Marr in Freeport, where she was left, said that her mother wanted to have nothing to do with a child of rape. Her father's name was listed, Korbac, a human necromancer. But Lirin didn't learn these things until she was 16 and the clerics of the church deemed her old enough to know

So, the young paladin went off to seek some type of family. Fortunately, her birth father Korbac, had been in the news lately, and she realized that the leader of the Pretentious Malevolent Sisters, Androdameia, was her half sister. Having heard of the guild and believed I their goals, she decided that was the place to turn, a place she could call people sisters.

Appearance:

Her dark brown hair is cut short, with long bangs creeping down into her eyes. She is fairly tall for a half elf, and is slim like her mother's race. Her eyes are dark and serious - black or dark brown, it is hard to tell - perhaps these are her heritage from her father.
