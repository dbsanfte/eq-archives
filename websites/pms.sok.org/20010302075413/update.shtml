Farewell PMS! Thursday, February 15, 2001 -- Cynarra z'Imp
The PMS is disbanding as of March 1, 2001 to work on the NWN project.

I know that we have all worked hard towards fostering an RP environment in EQ, but there are new RP opportunities ahead for all of us.

Due to my limited resources, I am going to focus my energy on the NWN project, with incidental RP in EverQuest, as I am able.

I would like to again thank everyone for an excellent RP and hope you will join the rest of us in the next world.
