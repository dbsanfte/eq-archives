Name: Margali Govannen-Zek
Race: Demigod - Human
Class: Monk
Age: Unknown
Follows: Agnostic

Margali was conceived during one of the Sister's darkest hours.  A Dark Order roamed the lands, seeking to destroy their allies, The Sterling Order of Knights.  Margali's mother was believed to be insane. However, the truth behind Ethlinn's "insanity" is that the warrior was being possessed by a sword, which had been made by her father, Crowan Govannen.  The sword
had been hidden for some time.  News had passed Ethlinn's ears that a powerful sword forged by her father's hand and infused with Rallos Zek's hand was being passed along throughout Norrath.  She sent several of the younger Sisters to speak to an oracle of Zek, named Rahne Warsong, a gnome.  The oracle told the Sisters that another warrior they were familiar with had taken the sword.  Aluinie Aspenleaf, a wood elf, was confronted by the Sisters and gave the sword to them.  Unbeknownst to the Sisters and Ethlinn, Rahne manipulated the wood elf to give the Sisters the sword, as her patron commanded.  Once Ethlinn was in possession of the sword, she studied it, shutting out all other Sisters.  As the days passed she became erratic, and blood thirsty.  Any Sister that tried to confront her, was either slain or beaten badly.  The warrior, in possession of the sword, had no superior. She then left the Sisters, running freely amongst Norrath, and promoting Rallos Zek's wars against the other gods.  As the Sisters strived to cure this oddity, once they discovered the true power of the sword, to possess Ethlinn, they set forth to cure her.

Meanwhile, Ethlinn left Norrath and met Zek at the entrance to his Plane of War.  They entered together, and soon after, the Warlord impregnated Ethlinn.  Time in the Plane of War is far faster than in Norrath, and the child was soon born, and Rallos Zek, satisfied with his new child to mold, left Ethlinn.  Shortly after the Sisters cured her and she had no recollection of Margali's birth.  Meanwhile, the newly born demigoddess was trained by Zek's finest warriors, to be his ultimate heiress.  Her powers, if any, to this day, are unknown, and unrevealed.  However, Ethlinn's stubborn spirit burned brightly in Margali, and eventually the demi goddess would escape the Plane of War, with the aid of a secreted follower of Quellious.  Margali entered Norrath for the first time, and was guided to the Temple of Quellious in West Freeport, where she was taken in by the grandmasters of the guildhouse, and trained to be a monk.  Margali strongly rejected any weapon, and used her own body to combat those that would wrong her, and continues to do so to this day.

She met her mother a few times before Ethlinn's demise, and the memories of those meetings still pains the monk.  It was then that she decided to join the Sisterhood, as she felt it would honor her mother's memory.  She also learned of her half sister, Aweane, who left the lands soonafter. She strives to be the best she can possibly be, to combat Rallos Zek's force as they continue to try to return her to their deity.

Personality
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Margali is a naive, kind, young woman.  She is learning the ways of life as she progresses in her profession, and is very intrigued.  Margali looks foward to a fair fight, to prove her worth to her allies and friends.  She despises weapons, and refuses
to participate in any honorless act.  She has learned much from the Temple of Quellious, and although she feels she can never trust any deity for the cruel games they plan on their devouts, she follows many of the Tranquil One's philosophies.  She strongly enjoys serenity, and looks foward to the day when she will meet a man that can best her in any field.
