Kenia Fedaykin
Erudite Paladin
Follower of Quellious

My name is Kenia. I was named after my father, Kenar and my mother Trudia. I was born in the beautiful city of Erudin but I was born under shame. For you see my father chose the path of a warrior. This was unheard of to Erudites and in their eyes my father had forsook the path of his forefathers. So with my mother he left to venture the world of Norrath but alas his death was untimely and my mother in shame returned to her homeland. The grief was unbearable and her family feared for her health and it was not until she realized she was with child that she truly felt alive again. For this child would be the continuation of the great Erudite warrior, Kenar and she made sure that though his name could not be spoken in his child they would be forced to see him and say his name.

After I was born my mother returned to her training in the cleric's guild where she met a wonderful and kind man. Boman was a highly respected priest in the guild and he soon fell in love with my mother. By wedding my mother he helped her to retain respectability amongst her people. But in me there still harbored the anger that my father though a great warrior who had helped many people would never be respected, even in his death. So I decided that at my time of choosing I would choose the path of the paladins. This was virtually unheard of for girls, for they were expected to become pure casters. There was much debate but my mother and stepfather stood strong in my decision and so at the age of 15 I began my training at the Deepwater guild. I soon excelled and it could not be denied that I was borne with my father's gifts of a warrior. Soon I was leading hunting expeditions outside of the city and it was while on hunting that we happened upon Elial Brooks.

Now we knew of those that studied the darker art of magic but were told that such ones had been banished from Odus. Thinking Elial to be a friendly man we approached him but before we had time to react we were set upon by his pet. My hunting group barely escaped and as I lay dying from his powerful poisons I wondered how one could be amongst us. A search party was sent to find me and through the blessings of the gods and the magic of my parents I was saved but I had many questions. Finally, when my health returned I learned that these heretics did in fact live amongst us. The great civil war that had been fought had not truly been won by either those good or evil but more or less a truce had been drawn. The heretics were allowed to reside among us but they must practice their art outside of Erudin. I learned that they had rebuilt their own city of Paineel. I felt betrayed. Here were these heretics that would as easily take the life of their own fellow countrymen and yet be allowed to live in peace among us and yet my father a good man had been looked down on.

That night I swore an oath; death would could swiftly to any heretics that crossed my path. I left my home and traveled to Qeynos were I continued working on my skills. I met a human ranger named Carasmar who encouraged me to continue in my training and when I was ready he would take me to the city of Freeport. As each of my seasons passed I would ask him, "am I ready now?" but he would only shake his head and tell me to work harder. I was half tempted to leave without him but alas I did not know my way. Finally, after my 8th season he told me I was ready. I was terrified and excited at the same time for I knew that I would be leaving my family far behind but there would be more to learn in these new lands.

Upon my arrival to Freeport I immediately set to venture these new lands. I had been warned of the evil dark elves and to stay away from the tunnel that led to their city. I also knew of the tunnel to North Ro but in fear of wandering in the wrong tunnel I stayed away either. One night as I was out venturing I heard shouts of joy and partying. There was a guild called the Pretentious Malevolent Sisters inviting everyone to join in their Happy Hour but I was fearful thinking it to perhaps be a trap. Carasmar arrived and asked me if I'd like to see the sisters of PMS and I said yes. There among them I saw an Erudite woman so I approached her and spoke to her in our tongue. She told me about these sisters but I was not too trusting for among them I saw dark elves and practicers of dark magic. However, as I passed my seasons I would often see the sisters of PMS and soon realized that they were honorable and highly respected women and so I too came to realize that not all practicers of dark magic nor dark elves, trolls or ogres are evil. For the most part we all fight the same fight and that is for honor and the eradication of evil that dwells in Norrath. Today I am proud to call myself sister to those of PMS and I have made many friends in my ventures. Though Carasmar is no longer with me I have met a wonderful gnomer named Filikkin. Where he goes I go and where I go he goes. This is my short story and as each day passes I know I will have many more stories to tell.
