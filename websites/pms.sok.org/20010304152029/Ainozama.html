Ainozama Amansule
Ranger

Greetings, I am Ainozama Amansule I am a ranger of 40 seasons. I was born to a happy family close to Surefall Glade; at least I thought they were. My mother was a Wood Elf Ranger of Tunare and my father a Human Cleric from Qeynos. I was raised to enjoy and respect the great outdoors and all the creatures of Tunare. I always liked helping others so at an early age I would run small errands or deliver a mail pouch or two to Qeynos for the bards in Surefalls. This was my excuse to go in and see the big city. I still enjoy the occasional trips to town but always make my camps in the wilderness. I learned many of my fighting skills in the aqueducts of Qeynos fighting the thieving thugs and Smugglers that hide out there. As I grew older I heard of undead gnolls roaming the Plains of eastern Karanas so I spent a small amount of time there before moving on to High Pass and battling the foul Gnolls, Orcs, and Goblins there.

Around this time it was clear to everyone that my mother, who had gone out adventuring, had found her way back to her home in the trees of Kelithin and had no intention of returning to Qeynos. My mother was always the adventuring type roaming the far corners of the known world protecting the wildlife and doing her best to rid the lands of the evil that has arisen in so many places. My father was a temple priest at the Temple of Life. He did very little roaming. This is how my two younger half sisters came to be but if you ever meet them they can tell you more about their own trials and tribulations in this world of ours. I will just say that my mother and father both found happiness with their own kind. Partners that had more in common with what they enjoyed doing. My mother found a wood elf Druid that she adventured with from then till now and my father found another temple priest to his taste and they are all happily engaged in their own life's work.

One-day word came of a new land that was discovered and infected with many many evil creatures from Giants to the abominations of the drachnids, half spider half elf. I packed up my belongings and set out for this new world. I had heard and seen the sisters of this strange all woman guild before and I always held them in high admiration. Fighting in these new lands I came to know some of them even better and fought along side them for a long time.
I gradually became more entrenched in their world and I discovered that I  liked it that way. These women were a lot like me. I finally left my previous alliance and joined the sisters.

Through all of this I still enjoyed running errands and finding the rare items that the people in the towns could not leave to go find themselves. This is how I became known by many of my friends as the great quester. I love questing and hunting for that elusive piece of equipment that many seek but few are able to obtain.
