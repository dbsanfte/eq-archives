Tuesday, August 31th 10:50 pm -- Iolanthe
The Pretentious Malevolent Sisters present:

Happy Hour and a Drunken Biathlon

On the Fennin Ro Server

Friday, September 3, 1999 at 6:00 PM PDT

Meet at the fishing village in the Northern Ro Desert. We will be serving drink and food for the occasion.

At approximately 6:30 PM PDT, the most hardy of the party attendees are invited to join in a "Drunken Biathlon" (the festivities will continue in the Northern Ro Desert during the race). The racers will all meet on the shore of Oasis  (Northern Ro Boundary). Once there, entries will be accepted and the race route will be announced. There will be numerous check points along the race route - at each check point, each racer must consume at least 5 drinks - a racer may consume more and at the discretion of the checkpoint official, a racer may be required to consume more if they are deemed to be not drunk enough. The first portion of the race will be a swim. The second part will be a foot race. No SOWs or similar spells or magic items may be used during the race. The race officials are not responsible for any loss of life, limb or possessions during this race. If a sand giant, spectre, orc, crocodile, or any other foul creatures decides to have you for lunch, then it's your problem. The winner of the race will receive 100 free drinks. Second takes home 50, third, 25.

At the end of the race, the participants and partygoers will proceed back to the Northern Ro Fishing for more drinking and eating. All attendees are welcome to auction items at this time.With all the drunken revelry, the sisters expect many great deals will be available.
