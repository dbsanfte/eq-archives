Vladara
Tier'dal Cleric
 

I am a child of love, disguised in the mask of hate, conceived of an oath to the Gods, spoken on the lips of my shadow father to my sorceress mother....

Bristlebane, Protector of Shadows, and Erollisi Marr, Goddess of Love, bind our souls together, that my strength might protect my beloved.  My strength is your strength, take of it what you will.  For without you, my heart would break asunder.  Our souls are as one, for I am yours.

My name literally means: possesses peace, heart of wisdom & compassion.  My looks are deceiving, I am Teir'Dal, dread race, worshippers of Hate.  I have learned from the darkness of my heritage how to conquer hate and make its power my own.  From the love of my conception and birth, I am given great powers of healing and understanding.  Both emotions are mine, for neither controls or possesses me.  For I have learned when to use them and to what purpose.
