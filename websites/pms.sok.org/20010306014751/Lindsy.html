Lindsy
High Elf Cleric

BEGINNING:

It was another beautiful day in the Greater Faydark today, and Lindsy and her family were going on a picnic.  She wasn't ever afraid, has her Dad was one of the royal Paladin Guards, and her Mom was a very powerful Mage.  They finally set up camp, and talked and had fun while they ate, and afterwards Lindsy wanted to play.

"Mom, could you pleeeeeeeeease make a pet made out of dirt for me?"  Lindsy asked.  "Sure honey," She replied.  Her Mom stood up, and began her casting, creating one very large pet.  "Thank you Mom!  Come on Gobeker, lets go play fetch!"  Her parents smiled at her, for she had promises of being a kind Cleric, always helping others out.  She had promised her Mom she would be the best and nicest Cleric in all of Norrath!  She wanted to be just like her Grandma, who was also a Cleric.

While she was playing with Gobeker, she heard a faint sound, and this startled her.  Lindsy turned around, and she gasped to see what looked like an Elf, but was of a very much darker skin tone.

He said to her, "Hello, Lindsy.  How are you?"  Lindsy was scared, but now believed that he came in peace, being this kind and all.  "I...I am fine....thank..you."  she said.  "Good good.  I have someone here who would like to see you."  He said next.  Lindsy replies curiously, "Who pray tell?"  "His name is Jarbon, and he is the last thing you will ever see, little girl!  Pet, attack!"  Out of practically no where, a large, red skeleton came running out from behind this darkened creature.  "Aaahhhhh!" Lindsy screamed as the skeleton charged at her.  Just then, Gobeker came back and saw this, and attacked the skeleton with a blow to the right shoulder blade, and the pet was shattered into small bone chips on the ground as it screeched in the air.  Then Gobeker rooted the creature, so it would not move.

But then, Gobeker looked very worried, and very scared at the same time.  He then raised his arms, and stuck himself in the chest, exploding into many little pieces of mud and dirt.  The Dark One grinned at Lindsy, and started walking to her.  She jumped back, and tried to remember the spell the Clerics showed her in case of emergencies.  She performed the spell, and large balls of light formed around her as she gated back to the Felwithe gates.  She than hear a scream of a most disturbing language, which sounded like the voice of the dark Elf.  She then ran back to the camp, to get her parents help, but alas, when she arrived, she did not see her parents at all.  The last thing she saw was a dirty, hairy Orc run off over the hill into the distance.  The only thing she saw was the picnic site, trashed, and blood on the ground...she assumed the worst for her parents, and swiftly ran back to town, into the church, and prayed to Tunare, about what and why this happened.  Normally, Tunare would not directly speak and interfere with her Elves lives, but today, Tunare said something to Lindsy.

Tunare said, "Lindsy, my dear Lindsy.  I know you grieve over the lost souls of your parents, but you musn't grieve any longer.  You are the key, Lindsy.  You are the key to the death of the dark god of these evil elves.  Though not all of them are against you, you must still smite the ones that do.  In reality, you must smite anyone and anything, whether it be Human, Gnome, Dwarf, or even another Elf.  I give you my blessing to do this.  You are to gain power, and to do this you must travel the world, using your skills to manipulate others and ultimately smite all of the foul, unnatural living dead that roam the land with you powerful Clerical abilities.  You will not be able to do it alone.  You will need to find the lost souls of your parents, Lindsy.  Yes, they are still alive, but are lost in a twist of hatred, and you must free them from this.  Today, you were targeted for dead, not your parents.  They want you, you are the most important of them all.  As long as you live, Norrath still heads hope.  Go now, train in you studies, and gain the power you so rightfully deserve and will get!  I will watch over you as you cleanse the land for me."

After that day, Lindsy trained hard in her arts, and traveled afar to kill all and every thing in her way.  She was driven though by anger, the total opposite of what Tunare wanted.  She felt that her race was superior to all other races, and all other High Elves were under her as well, even there king.  She started to take joy in killing things, and was even thought to be a High Elf with the mind of a Dark Elf.  And with this came great power and greed.  She claimed every thing she did was for Tunare, and in her name, even though she has twisted every thing that Tunare had told her.  She still remembered what Tunare had said to this day, and had headed to Befallen to gain power in the dismissal of undead.  Thus, she traveled to Befallen, and an event changed her life forever....

ENTER WHYLL AND THE SISTERS:

As she approached this place, she saw a Half Elf and a Barbarian, blocking the way.  She immediately scorned them.  And told them to move and bow to her, in so many words.  They refused, and this angered her more.  The Barbarian was quick to attack, yet the Half Elf was more calm and related to peace.  The Sisters soon came to defend there friends.  Lindsy was used to being taken advantage of by large males, and would slay them for this.  She then lunged toward Whyll with her mace and spells, and though the battle was a fierce one, she finally had fallen.  In her crippled state,  she say visions of Tunare in her head, and remembered her mission.  She then noticed the Sisters, and how strong they looked, and determined.  She was used to being the only strong female around.  These women made her think.  All her life she had been protecting and defending her beliefs and defending herself only.  She wanted more, and she also new that Tunare spoke of friends who would help her.

She asked around and got information of this 'all woman's guild', and it actually excited her, and made her happy.  She thought, "All these strong females, fighting for a cause and for each other, and Whyll, he won because he fought with real spirit and compassion...what have I been fighting for?  What?  I need answers!"  She then got in contact with the leader of this guild, and spoke to her of what she has been doing, and the leader accepted her, through all she has done, and all the blood on her hands, and told her to come to there tavern one night.

She knew she wouldn't be readily accepted, and it would be hard for her to turn from her old ways, but she will always have a taste for blood, and an un-dying unconscious to fulfill her mission to smite Innoruuk and find her parents.  But yet, she was willing to change, and become the key Tunare wanted long ago.  Then after weeks of thinking, she came and entered the tavern, and then slowly took of her helmet, revealing who she was, and shocking everyone, even Whyll, who was there also.
