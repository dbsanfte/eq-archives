Brinna
High Elf Cleric

    Brinna walked into the Tall Tale Ale, a tavern she had heard that the Pretentious Malevolent Sisters owned and operated, and walked up to the bar.  A young woman was busy wiping down the counter with a washrag, but stopped and smiled when she saw the visitor.

     "Hi there stranger, welcome to the Tall Tale, how may I help you this afternoon?" Brinna smiled at the warm welcoming and requested a bottle of Berry Juice. The barmaid nodded and went to get the bottle and a glass.

     Adjusting her pack on her shoulder, Brinna looked around. The tavern was nicely kept, not a speck of dirt anywhere. Torches were lit in the entryway and the seating area. Not many of the seats were occupied, the young cleric assumed that was because it was only 2 p.m. and the guild members where off hunting.  Some of the wooden tables still had chairs on top of them, most likely so the barmaid could sweep the floors.  To the far left of the common room was a set of stairs leading up into a dark hall, the separate rooms for each Sister was Brinna's guess.

     "Here you go My Lady.", the barmaid smiled and set the Berry Juice bottle and glass on the counter. "That will be two gold pieces please." Brinna set a platinum piece down and told the barmaid to keep the rest as a tip.  The barmaid smiled gratefully and went back to cleaning behind the counter.

     Brinna took the bottle and glass, walked into the eating area, and found a table well lit by a window that the warm afternoon sun shone through. Sitting down, she took off her backpack, set it before her and rummaged through its contents searching for a piece of paper, her quill pen, and ink vial.  After emerging victorious in her search, she carefully set each item before her.  Now all that was left was the pouring of her beverage.

     Carefully, Brinna pulled the cork out of the Berry Juice bottle and poured the ruby-red liquid into her glass.  She set it to her right and placed the piece of paper in front of her.  She had to write and autobiography, a story of her life, if she was to be considered for pledgeship in the Sisters guild.  Taking a small sip of her drink, Brinna figures the best way to start was from the beginning.  She dipped her quill pen into the ink vial and began to write in beautiful, educated strokes.

     I was born to a middle class family in the city of Felwithe. My parents, Nithen and Marlanna Fyremoon, are both clerics of Tunare and are well known in the community for their healing talents. Both work in the cleric guild as medics, something they hope I will do when I become older. I on the other hand, would rather marry an Orc then work in the guildhall for the rest of my life. I want to travel the world and see everything, but that is getting off the track if my story.

     My schooling was the same as the other children of Felwithe. I arose early each morning to walk to the school hall where I learned reading, writing, mathematics, history, and economics. All which I excelled at, but all which I found boring. The only part of the school day I really enjoyed was when we all got to go to our guild halls to learn out new skills and practice spells. My parents were always there but they were not allowed to interfere with my training. Which I found that I liked very much. I tend to grow impatient and rude when my parents try to correct me when I do something wrong. Where as when my guild leader corrects me, I tend to show more respect. I assume this is because I am not as comfortable with him as I am my parent.

     It has been two years now since I have graduated from school and am now on my own. I like in Freeport with a friend I went to school with, we have been warmly welcomed by the cleric hall here and we visit the leaders often, sometimes to train, get advice or sometimes just to chat about life in general.

     Now to write about how it is I came to know about this guild. About a month back I was hunting alone in the East Commons. I was running about killing snakes, spiderlings, black bears, and anything else I thought I might be able to handle. After about an hour of hunting I decided to take a rest. Still a little new to the Eastern Commons I didn’t realize that the spot I had chosen was very near to the entrance of Nektulos Forest.

     I was digging through a small bag I had hooked onto my belt  for some food, when I heard footsteps behind me. Putting my pack down I turned to see whom the footsteps belonged to. A DARK ELF!! Shrieking with terror I scrambled to my feet  to face the elf. As scared as I was I couldn’t help but notice how beautiful she was. Emerald green eyes set against the blue hue of her skin. Her white hair flowed softly over her shoulders, contrasting elegantly over the ruby red of her robe. A large skeleton, as tall as me, stepped up behind the dark elf and, in a deathly whisper, spoke, "Guarding you. . . Master."

     The dark elf cocked her head to the side. "What are you doing here, so close to my people's home? You could easily get killed here." I shook my head, confused, terror still etched on my face. The dark elf straightened up and with a wave of her hand, motioned for me to follow her. "Come with me High Elf, I will show you a place to rest." I hesitated, unsure if I should believe her or not. But seeing the look of growing impatience on the dark elf's face made me reconsider. I stooped down to grab my pack and nodded that I was ready to follow her.

     We walked together, neither one saying a word. I suppose I was still in shock that a dark elf was helping me. She led me east towards Freeport for almost a mile, through thick trees and vegetation, until a large building came into view. Pointing towards it, she spoke, "There are guards at the Inn, you can hunt fairly well around here and when you wish to rest, simply sit by the building. The guards will protect you. And again I give you this warning: stay away from Nektulos Forest if you value your life. This is not a threat, do not look so afraid of me, it is simply a warning."

     I thought hard, what could I do to repay her? In quick, clumsy motions, I reached into my pack, withdrew my purse and pulled out a few platinum pieces. "Here, take this. . . you have helped my greatly."
The dark elf turned one corner of her mouth up, almost like she was trying to smile but did not have enough practice at it to do it right. She held up her hand and shook her head. "There is no charge High Elf. Just stay. . . safe."

     It was only now that I noticed that she had something pinned on her robe. It was high up on the left-hand corner of her robe, it was a guild pin. Squinting, I tried to read the tiny print but all I could make out was the word 'sisters'. Looking up at her face I questioned her about the pin. Cocking her head to the side again, she gave me a half smile. "I am a member of the Pretentious Malevolent Sisters, my name is Krystil." She extended her slender hand and I grasped in my own. Her grip was firm, more so then I expected and I raised my eyebrows in suprise. "And what is your name? Or should I continue calling you 'High Elf'?"

     Blushing at my rudeness, I stammered out my name. Krystil nodded, "Ah, Brinna. A beautiful name for a beautiful girl." I blushed again at this and looked shyly down at my boots." Placing her hand on my shoulder, she squeezes slightly and asked, "Would you like me to stay and hunt with you Brinna? I am free for the rest of the day.." Looking up, my jaw dropped in shock. This had to be the oddest day of my entire life. A dark elf helping me find a place to rest, TEHN offering to hunt with me. We were supposed to me mortal enemies, not friends! But there we were...

     Looking back on that day now, I realize how blind I was to anything NOT taught to me by my elders. Krystil and I have known each other for a month now and we have become good friends. She was the one that gave me directions to the Tall Tale Ale and told me to write an autobiography and how I came to know about the Sisters.

------------------------------

     Brinna sat back in her chair and stretched her arms upward. Relaxing forward again, she drank the rest of her Berry Juice and re-read her story. After correcting a few grammar errors, she folded the paper three times and slid the pages into an envelope. Dipping her quill pen into the ink vial once more and wrote out the name Krystil told her to send it to. . . . Cynarra.

     Brinna gathered her supplies and placed them back into her backpack. Lifting it up on her shoulder she grabbed the envelope and walked over to the bar. The barmaid was sitting on a stool, reading, but jumped up when she saw Brinna. "How can I help you again, my lady."  "Can you please give this to Cynarra when you have the chance?" Brinna spoke as she handed the barmaid her story. "I sure can, the Mistress is off in Kunark at the moment, but we are expecting her back any day now. I'll run up and put it on her desk for you." Brinna smiled and thanked the young woman.

     Turning on her heel she walked to the front door, and walked out into the sunshine of a beautiful Norrathian day. Breathing in the sweet scented air, Brinna smiled and headed back to her apartment, silently praying to Tunare that her story would get into the Mistress' hands.
