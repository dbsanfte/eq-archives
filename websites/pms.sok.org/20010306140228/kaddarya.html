Kaddarya Nae'blis
Dark Channeler of Souls
16th Season

I am born from the once great House of Nae'blis. My life started as any  young dark elf noble girl would have, I had the best clothes, best teachers, and the best foods. I had everything I ever wanted in life but never knowing what was important to me. I was quite the wild one growing up and very spoiled, managing to disobey my parents any way I could. Especially with my necromancy tutor Xon Quexill. I'd often skip out of my sessions and go out to play with my sisters, since I was the only one old enough to be tutored. 

I hated being the eldest. I always had the responsibility of being a role model to my younger sisters. I wanted to shun any responsibility I had and feel free to explore whatever I wanted!

My life changed drastically when my parents Asertak and Meshah were killed in a raid from a rival house. My cousin, Gravewolf valiantly pulled my sisters and I out of the attack. Everything I hated about being the eldest child bore down me.

I wish I could say I had a wonderful childhood after that event but everyday was a struggle. For the first time in my life I had to resort to begging and stealing to keep my sisters alive.

Knowing I had to replace my mother and my father's role, I grew up much too fast than I had wanted to. I used some of my basic teachings of necromancy to keep us alive. How ironic it is that the things that you hate most in

life, you end up being the best at?

When my sisters slept I used to create tiny skeleton animals to keep watch over them. As my powers grew and grew, I knew I had to rebuild and restore power to our once great house.

Gradually my sister Graviata looked over my younger sisters Zephiris, Erisia, Kallima, Sillith while I ventured out into Nektulos and earned money by selling spider silks and various hides. Soon I had enough money to for them to learn the various mystical arts. I was never as strict as my parents were nor as narrow minded so I let them choose what they wanted learn instead of binding them to the art of Necromancy like my parents did. I always felt as though my sisters follow their own path instead of someone choosing one for them.

Now as my sisters venture forth in to Norrath alone, I can finally find my own destiny. My own path.
