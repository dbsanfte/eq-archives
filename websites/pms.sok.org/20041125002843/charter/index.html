Mission Statement
The Sterling Order of Knights strive to maintain excellence and professionalism within the online gaming community. It is important that every member understand the principles that have made the SOK who we are today. It is our goal that every single member of the Sterling Order of Knights is able to completely respect, trust, and assist another member in every way possible. This is the way of the SOK.

All guild members are required to respect each other at all times. We are unable to build a strong fellowship without this. It is important to build friendships amongst your guildmates. This cannot be accomplished without respect.

In order to build trust it is important to be active within the guild. As your fellow members interact with you, you will be able to prove that you are able to be relied on and are willing to help. Examples of this can be as simple as being a grouping partner, or offering assistance when needed, advice on the message board or even helping at guild meets. Whenever possible, group with fellow SOK, it is always worth the trip and it is why we are here.

When a guildmate is in need, It is our responsibility to assist them. Our guild is here to help us, and we must contribute to that the best we can. Helping a fellow member is the single most important thing you can do in the game if you are SOK.

Wear the name Sterling Order of Knights with pride, we are a cut above the rest. This is what we are about, this is who we are, this is SOK.

Participation
The most important aspect of our guild and our survival is participation. We are building a community, and it is necessary that every member participate how they can in order to make our community stronger.  There are many ways to participate within the guild in-game and out of game. It is understood that not everyone will participate in every way, or be able to participate in every guild meet. We realize that not everyone has the same time available online, but we expect you to do your best to participate.

Message Board
 All members must register to use the SOK Message Board. Once registered, contact Jubal for your Guild Member status and access to the Private Forums.

The message Boards are moderated by the Officers and any post therein may be deleted or moved.

SOK Chat
Members are encouraged to visit the SOK Chat Room, accessible through the web page, or via IRC (minin.sok.org, #sok). SOK Members must register their name with nickserv. In order to do so, type

/nick Yourname

/nickserv register (pick a password) yourname@sok.org

This will register your name within the sok chat. then send an email to officers@sok.org so we can reserve your name.

Chat is moderated by the Officers (designated by an @ symbol) and members may be kicked or banned from the channel.

Roster
 It is important to us that we have a current Roster. You should make sure that you are on the roster, and that your information is current. Currently we are keeping rosters in our private forums of our respective games. It is your responsibility to make sure that your roster information is up to date at all times.

SOK Email
SOK provides you with an email address of yourname@sok.org. In order to receive this address, send an email to Jubal or post on the thread for this in the private forum. However, if for some reason the sok.org site has trouble or gets moved, this email may have downtime. Doesn't happen often, but it does happen. 
Guild Meets
It is fairly easy to start a guild meet within SOK, and all members are encouraged to do so. Pick a place and time, and post it in the respective forum. It is expected that if you lead a Guild Meet that you post your intentions on how the raid will be handled, and how items will be distributed. 

Officers
The Sterling Order of Knights is managed by our team of Officers. I learned the hard way that I cannot handle everything required to manage a well run guild. For this reason SOK has a small team of Officers which work together in managing the guild. SOK is not a democracy, and important decisions will be made by the Officers based on the best interests of the guild. Input by the guild is appreciated and valued and certainly has impact on the decisions that we make. Decisions made by an Officer is final, and will be upheld by Jubal and the other Officers.

You can contact the Officers via email at officers@sok.org

Privacy Policy
The application that is required for membership asks many details that many wish to remain private (Due to the 1998 Children's Online Privacy Protection Act, SOK will not accept applications from anyone under the age of 13). This information is used to learn more about you to determine whether you are a good fit for our guild. This information is shared amongst the Officers within the Officers Forum. Information regarding your character name, race, class and level is provided to our members in order for the purpose of grouping with SOK members in order to gain sponsorship. Your personal details are kept private and are not shared or redistributed in any way.

In order to effectively operate and manage the guild, Officers must discuss incidents and members within the Officers Forum. Officers have a responsibility to be honest and open within this forum, so discussions with an Officer that are guild related may be reposted therein. This information is kept private and is not shared or redistributed in any other way.

Interaction within the Guild
Members should be respectful at all times to each other. If you have any problem or dispute within the guild, do not bring this discussion to guild chat, but instead contact an officer via tell or email. Constructive criticism is welcome however, but please post it in the Private Forum.

Our main form of inter-guild communication is through guildchat and the message board. Guildchat and the Message board is rated pg-13 and should be free of profanity and obscene comments. If you wish to curse or be obscene, do it in tells or email.

Interaction with other Guilds
There will be times in which disputes occur with other guilds. If such an incident occurs, it is your responsibility to immediately inform  an Officer of what is going on so that the dispute can be handled by SOK management. If an Officer is not online, then defer the problem to your group or event leader and allow them to handle it. A member of SOK is expected to be professional and mature at all times when interacting with other members of the online community. Disputes should never be brought to Public, hostility and insults in a public channel reflects poorly on the guild, and is unacceptable.

If the dispute occurs in game, immediately defer to an Officer online and allow him to handle the situation. Some guilds have a reputation as being unreasonable and possessive. It is not our place to return to them the same attitude they give us. Keep a level head. Remain calm, and allow the Officer to handle the situation.  Anything else will only make a bad situation worse.

If a dispute occurs on a Message Board, email the Officers and send him the URL of the problem post. We refuse to be involved in the flaming that occurs on some other guild message boards, so allow an Officer to post an official statement.

Adherance to The Game Host's Rules and Policies
SOK does not condone anti-social, dishonorable, or game-destructive behavior from its members. The behavior of one member reflects on us all.

Whether or not you personally agree with any or all of The Game Host's policies, as a member of SOK you have an obligation to the rest of us to follow the rules set forth in the TOS as best as you can. There have been numerous instances in the online community where entire guilds have been banned based on the actions of only some of its members. Whether or not this is 'fair' or 'right' isn't relevant. It happens.

Therefore, in the interests of protecting the group as a whole, if after an appropriate investigation one of our members is found to be deliberately engaging in activities which could result in his banning, SOK will be forced to take disciplinary action against him, up to and including removal from the guild.

If you have any question whatsoever about whether or not an action could get you banned, feel free to contact an officer, or better yet, a guide. Remember, even if you feel you are completely in the right, when it comes to being banned your interpretation of the rules doesn't matter. Only The Game Host's does.

It takes months and months to build a good reputation, and only one bad day to blow it forever. Let's all try to work together to make sure SOK is always known for its unquestionably honorable members.

SOK in PvP Situations and Games:

Even in the cut-throat world of PvP(Player vs. Player) SOK retains our sense of honor and integrity. Our goal in PvP is to kill the enemy, but our Members do not run cheat programs, corpse camp, bind point camp or in anyway grief other players. For those that wish to try our patience however, we will keep a private list of those people and they will be dealt with in kind.

A Members success in PvP games will also be rewarded by the Officers. Whether it be a ranking or an item for the accomplished Knight.

Rule and Policy Violations
Sometimes members may act in a manner that opposes our guild policies. Depending on the severity of the incident, the Officer may make a decision to issue a warning, or remove you from the guild. Such decisions are never made on hearsay alone, and you will always have the opportunity to discuss your case and present your side to the available Officers.

If there is ever an issue that requires the Officer to discuss an issue with you further, they may ask you to discuss it with them in chat. It is at this time that you must log out immediately. If you are in a situation that does not allow you to log out immediately, inform the officer of your situation and take the appropriate measures needed to log as soon as possible. Refusal to do so will result in your removal from the guild until such time that you are willing to discuss it. Any decision made by an Officer will be upheld by Jubal and the other Officers. The Officers of SOK are Androdameia, Donnan, Erolotis, Jubal, Leod, Minin, Shalafi (Alex), Surefoot, Thadicus and Trubador.
