Elani

At the hour of sunset, on a hot summer day my story began....


I cannot speak of my birth parents as I have never known them. I have spent my 24 years living in the city of Qeynos. My father being every rogue that lurked in the shadows. My mother being every heart that beat within the city gates. For I have learned from them all. The streets and Taverns were my home, and often gave me comfort. They nurtured, raised and cared for me. They taught me the life of a thief with the heart of a warrior.

On  one particular summer day, as I stood in the shadows of my dear city I felt an uneasiness.  The air around me wavered as if with intense heat, and before me takes the shape of a young man.  A tall man with eyes as black as a rare pearl. I could not decide if this man was a living being or not.

His words were like a piercing dagger, yet his voice enveloped my soul.
His lips never moved yet I heard him as clear as any man talking.
He never gave me his name, yet I seemed to know it.

I could here him saying...
"There is neither a beginning nor an end, only what shines in between matters".
As his words faded, so did he. I realized that day, it was my time to move on and find others like me.
And so I find myself traveling Norrath, now and again I return to my family.....
my city.
