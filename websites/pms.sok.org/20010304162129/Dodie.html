Dodie
Agnostic Warrior

Well Dodie don't have much to say about herself.   Dodie born in Ak'anon...Dodie runaway...now Dodie in
Freeport...that's all.

"Uh-oh, Dodie see Miss Kenia not happy so guess me better say more.  Dodie now read her bio to you all."
 
Dodie was born in the city of Ak'anon, an only child. Her parents were killed or ran away or disappeared when she was but a baby.  As an orphan she was raised by a kindly but stern old woman and her husband who had made it their life mission to help parentless children.

It wasn't that her homelife was bad but the other children bullied Dodie unmercifully.  In defense of herself she fought back and the bully always left with a bloodied nose, black eye or some other battle scar.  Unfortunately it was Dodie that was punished and as a  result deepened her resolve to leave these lands as soon as she was able.
 
As the kindly old couple grew older they were less able to manage all the children...foremost Dodie who never seemed to stay out of trouble.  It was decided that it was time for the children to begin their chosen paths.  Dodie was sent to the warriors guild  where she fared no better.
 
Barely was she there a day when she bit the guild leader who was trying to break up a fight between her and another trainee.  Before the week was out Dodie decided it was time for her to leave.  Packing the few possessions she owned she made her way out of the safety of the city and into the darkness.

There was no fear only the feeling of freedom and she ran as fast as her legs would take her.  Using her  keen senses she avoided harm and found her way to the docks of Faydwer.  It was morning by the time the boat docked in Freeport and Dodie was tired, cold and hungry.
 
"Wake up...wake up little one...it's not safe for you to sleep here."
 
 Dodie woke with a start and looked up at the regal, beautiful and most tall woman she'd ever seen.

"Your awfully young to be here...are you alone?"
 
Dodie simply blinked in reply.  The woman smiled at her and introduced herself as Kenia.
 
"Come...let's get you cleaned up and fed.  The Jade tavern will be a safe place for you."
 
Though Dodie has made few friends since leaving her home of Ak'anon she has finally found a family.  The sisters of PMS are not only her family but her friends and it is here that Dodie has found happiness.
