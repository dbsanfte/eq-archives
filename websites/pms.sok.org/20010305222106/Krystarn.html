I am Krystarn V’lorlich.  Born when the world was young, amid turmoil and hate.  I stood in the forest of Nektulos when the great Houses’ formed their ranks.  Now I stand outside a city that has taken its toll on my life, I stand looking at the gates of Neriak.

When I was young, I was vengeful and full of hate.  I would kill without reason and battle without real thought.  In the Greater Faydark I battled many foes and made many enemies.  It was there I turned to Chaos and Discord. 

I learned that while the forces of the Faydark would invade the castle of Emperor Crush, a stand off generally occurred in the throne room.  Standing beside my allies in the tower I would strike a slave in the pits causing that slave to charge into the castle.  The forces of light seeing a dwarf or elf charging into the ranks of the orc legions would flee out of every window and door they could find.  Thus allowing the orcs to replenish their ranks.

When I left the Greater Faydark, I ran across a guild called the Masters of the Blood.  They also served Chaos and Discord.  Over the next few weeks I watched and hunted them, supplying my own people with the equipment I was able to loot from their bodies.  The battles culminated to a massive hunt for me that went from west Freeport to High Pass Hold.  It was satisfying to know that in the end… I escaped while hiding in the chambers of the master of the pass and left that battle with more than five hundred platinum worth of items. 

For a time I traveled the lands, never staying in one place long.  I sat and drank among our allies in the swamps, learning of the ways of the Ogre and Troll.  When the cold winds tore across the frigid land of Everfrost I was there stalking the mammoths.  I walked the halls of Halas, mocking the people of that savage and barbaric land.

My time in the west was most enjoyable.  I walked the streets of Qeynos, finding the weak and weeding them out.  Humans are a feeble race, their continuing efforts to build without corruption is laughable.  In a city that is hailed as the glory of their race, failure and corruption is their heart.

I joined the great House Teken’Duis, under the leadership of Zaknifien.  There I learned about the movements of Innoruuk in his people.  I watched and waited but the House I was a part of fell to ashes.  In anger I withdrew again from my homeland and traveled to spread hate and death. 

It was not long before I discovered the Pretentious Malevolent Sisters.  I traveled with many sisters and then joined them in their ranks.  It was not long after that the Gresh’Maj raised its head and enslaved many people, including myself.  I was sent to distract and destroy the ones that were thought to be a problem for the Gresh’Maj.  I battled my own sisters in blind rage and anger.  When the Gresh’Maj were destroyed, I returned to the sisters, with the blood of many on my hands.  In the cleansing of that I found myself cut from the power of Discord and Chaos.

It was not long after that Iolanthe died and another took over.  During those times I found myself under the lure of another great Teir’Dal power, House Arkhenneld.  The scent of war and more battles drew me from the ranks of the sisters back to the arena of the Father. 

In the months that followed, battles were fought and power changed in Neriak.  Then suddenly House Arkhenneld fell, its leaders left for what they deemed greater things and lands.  Left with an empty shell of a House, we attempted to rebuild but the bitterness left by its one time leadership left most if a bile taste for this House and it folded.

Darmendrax met me in Neriak one dark evening after the disbanding of Arkhenneld.  After some conversation I was introduced as a member of House DeVir.  In the following months I rose in the ranks until I stood second only to Darmendrax.

In the dark recesses of Nurga, in the new lands of Kunark, I met Vampirro.  We stood side by side for many weeks, battling goblins and their slaves in those dark mines.  It was there that we formed a relationship that was later sealed by a cleric of Innoruuk. 

But all things must come to an end and so it was to be for Darmendrax.  Bitterness and betrayal broke the ranks of the House and he stepped down.  Vampirro stood and claimed it as his own.  Since then he has built a stronger House but it was not for me.  It was then I bore twins, one blessed by the Father and one blessed by the Mother I have hated all my life.  My daughters were as divided as light and darkness, one, a Teir’Dal and the other a Feir’Dal.

It was then that I returned to the chambers of the Tall Tale, to the very room that Cynarra slept in.  It is there I struck a deal that would save my child.

Now… I am back.  All my hate… my anger… my skills… stand among those who would protect my daughter from the very thing I helped mold by the actions of my life.  I stand with the Pretentious Malevolent Sisters.
