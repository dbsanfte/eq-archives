Janea Northwind
Age: 22
Race: Barbarian
Class: Shaman

So, ye wants ta know me story?  Well, I be from th' far north, from that fair land called Everfrost.  Me story don't seem ta have all th' blood an' glory some a th' others have.  I was raised by me ma an' pa ta be a dutiful  follower of th' Sixhammers, so when me time a callin' came, I said was ta be one a th' hands a th' Tribunal, ta wander th' land and be dispensin' their  justice, where I may find it.

So's I started me wanderin's down ta th' South, since it seems that most a th' justice was takin' care a by me seniors in me homeland.  Th' first place I found was a foul den filled with gnolls, name a Blackburra'.  Well, I started a whompin' and a wailin' on ever one I could find, aidin' me bretherin that was a fightin' next to me as best I could.  They felt th' hand a justice that day, let me tell ye.

Me travels took me farther and a farther from me home, till I heard of a group a feminin ladies like me, who's all stood together and a helped each other, likes I was a doin' with th' gnolls.  I sped me way here, bringin' a friend I met on th' way, seein' that she would be a big help ta these Sisters.
