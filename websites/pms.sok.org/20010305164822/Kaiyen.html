Kaiyen

Race: Wood Elf
Class: Ranger
Age: 18
Ht: 5'4
Wt: 130lbs
Build: Athletic
Hair: Auburn
Eyes: Golden
 

In the Sylvan land of Faydark within the green canopied village of Kelethin, Kaiyen was raised an orphan.  She was brought to the treetop village by travelers who found her in the nearby forest, alone and playing with woodland creatures.  With no family to call her own she was raised by the kindness of strangers, fostered from home to home.  It was clear even at her young age that perhaps she had spent too much time being wild and free.  After many failed attempts to tame her, she had rejected the yoke of training to become a Magical caster, or Priestess...as well as chaffed at the strict codes of the Paladin, and a life of crime and thievery was not her style.  She found it difficult to make friends, and would always sneak out into the forest to play and sometimes sleep. It was during this lost and defiant stage that she met a tall handsome Elf, golden in complexion by the name of Artimis.  He called himself a Ranger, and she admired not only his strength, cunning and intelligence but also his sense of freedom.  He knew the forest better than anyone, and began to teach her to respect the wild, not just play there. Many days were spent in his company, learning the secrets to the woods and it's inhabitants.  It was during this time that Kaiyen, for the first time in her life, felt a purpose. Felt happiness.

Artimis spent many weeks in the area of Faydark, teaching and training Kaiyen, preparing her for the journeys yet to come.  One damp and misty morning Kaiyen awoke to find Artimis gone, a note left behind wishing her well and reminding her to remember his teachings.  She was heartbroken, but soon had the realization that this is the way of a Ranger. She respected and admired him for his help, but also knew he craved freedom and yearned for adventure.  It was then she too decided to strike out and start living.  With what meager possessions she had, she left the only home she had ever known.
It was more than she could have ever hoped for...For she discovered the wonders and dangers of the Butcher Block Mountains and Steamfont Mountains.  She met Dwarves and Gnomes, and many other races as well.  Kaiyen then decided to strike out and leave Faydark, for her thirst for adventure cried for more.  Freeport held the golden ticket....

It is during this time of exploration and adventure, in the lands around the grand city of Freeport that Kaiyen discovered others with the same fire for adventure. They possessed a bond of kinship that she only dreamed of alone at night.  It was then that Kaiyen found her place, and sought to enriched the ranks of the Pretentious Malevolent Sisters.
