SOK Now Has Upper Black Rock Spire Access!!
Posted by Uanadven on Thursday, February 10 2005

(From Thadicus)

After countless weeks of dungeon crawling to collect the parts, last night Rhoshin assembled the key to Upper Black Rock Spire that we have been working so hard on.

In the beginning we died and died a lot. After a while we got use to the pulls and developed strategies. We worked our way deep into the lower bowels to collect the key parts needed night after night. We had a long and unfortunate string of bad luck. We had 13+ runs with Voone as the single target to get our last part and he never dropped it. Last night he dropped it.

Thanks to all those that came out and raided with us. I know it got boring a lot of times. I know it was rough sometimes. It was worth it.

It is important to note that this is just a single step and a stepping stone to Onyxia. Now that we have Uppert BRS open, we need to get each member a quest done in Upper BRS. Only one person can get this item per raid. So while this is the end of our quest for the key to Upper BRS, the journey for us has just begun. Onyxia, the Molten Core, and beyond calls us!!

Talk about OBNOXIOUS paladin bodyguards! Thoradin himself would've been stunned by paladin Uanadven's uptight security when he chowed at The Copper Kettle, the trendy cafe on IF's lower west side, where A-list SOKers like Jubal, Rhoshin, Sraaz, Androdameia, etc. - even demi-celebs like me - drop in daily.

First, the lovely Mrs. Paladin (a.k.a. Egeria) rode up on a Mercedes Ram with a bevy of twittering dryads. Moments later, several tricked-out Stallions swooped up and out leaped two beef-o's in full Secret Service/drama queen mode - dark armor, goggles, chattering nonstop on goblin ear walkie-talkies as . . . (drum roll) . . . His Heineken Highness, The Artist Formerly Known As "Hey Hon, will you get me another beer?" disembarked and swept inside like . . . well, like a Paladin.

Then the fun began! As Uana sat quietly noshing, the owner - a kindly, but famously feisty dwarf lady- was shocked to see Beef-o #1 ordering annoyed customers to move their tables back from the entrance, so they couldn't peek in at Pretty Pally. Then Beef-o #2 went in her face to complain that a customer had . . . Horrors! . . . passed within 10 feet of Uana's table, without a beer for him! The owner finally erupted when one dryad-in-waiting demanded of her: "Stop STARING at Uana!" Collaring Beef-o #1, she snapped: "How dare you harass my customers. We're very used to SOK celebrities here, and they're never bothered by any of us or the press. But guess what - I can send a message  and have Medivh here in five minutes" WHOA! The warning worked. Good thing it was off peak hours for the Auction House. She got an apology - and Uana even smiled and winked at her when he left two hours later. And what did he tip for their trouble? A Paladin-ly 4sp, folks!

Tragedy on Fennin Ro
Posted by Jubal on Tuesday, February 1 2005

Jonathan and Andrea Parmer, known as Erkantos and Laeenu of CI and TMO in EQ, suffered a great tragedy this weekend. A fire broke out Saturday morning from their heater which destroyed the entire house and all of its contents. They, along with their five kids are ok, other than some burns on Jon's head and upper body. The house was insured, but nothing inside was. They are staying with her parent's at the moment.

Some of you may know of them in game, some may know of them from last year's SOK Camping trip. Jon won the chubby bunny contest.
