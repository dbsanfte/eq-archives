Ygraine

In a time of peace and prosperity, the grand city of Felwithe stood like a shining citadel among the deep mysterious forests of Faydwer.  Long known as the seat of knowledge and culture in the land, High Elves have fiercely protected their capital from their enemies.  High Elves have always prided themselves on being superior to their uncultured primitive cousins the Wood Elves, and mention of half breeds and dark fallen ones is above them.  It is during this Golden Age that Ygraine was borne....Daughter to D'anton (the fierce, just and  beloved Captain of the Guards) and Isabeau (the beautiful High Priestess in service to the crown).
It was no secret that D'anton wished for a boy, someone he could train to follow in his footsteps, and someday ascend to the highly coveted rank of Captain.  He loved his daughter mightily, but did not wish that life for her (high bred women were not meant for a life of hard training and never ending service).  Thus, Ygraine was chosen for a life of intellectual pursuits within the Church, to follow in her mothers footsteps.

It was on an ambassadorial mission, returning from the Dwarven capital far to the west, that everything changed.  Talks between Kaladim and Felwithe had begun, as a trade agreement was about to be signed.  Isabeau was among the delegates, representing the Holy Order of the High Elves.  Still deep within the Butcher Block Mountains, it is said that the retinue was attacked by Evil Undead creatures, Ghouls the only survivor reported.  Everyone, except a blessed merchant, were slain.  News of the tragedy reached Felwithe, and D'anton was heartbroken...as was the Holy Order.  None were more stricken than Ygraine, still a mere child, now without her loving mother.  Learning how her mother died, a vengeful hatred welled inside her for these undead creatures!  She begged her father to train her in the ways of combat, so that one day she could venture to these mountains and rid the land of them, and in someway murder the pain of her mothers death.  D'anton could not refuse the plea of his only child, who he could already see Isabeau's beauty in.  From that day forward Ygraine was to be trained in the ways of combat, in the ways of justice, courtesy, chivalry and honor.  To one day be a Knight.

The years past quickly, and Ygraine honored her father by becoming the most adept squire in Felwithe.  She spent long hours training her strength and endurance, for she had to compensate for being a female.  She spent even longer hours honing her skills with weapons.  But never did she stop training her mind, as her mother would have done.  She studied languages and religious texts...all in an attempt to please the memory of her mother.  Many a night did she go off on her own in search of these dreaded undead, but none were to be found.  The city of Felwithe was well guarded indeed.  When D'anton knew that there was no more he could teach his child, he brought her before the Holy Order.  Ygraine had chosen to no only be a warrior, but a champion of justice.  A protector of the weak.  The bane of evil everywhere.  She had chosen to join the Holy Order and become a Paladin, like her father.

Ygraine spent the first few months learning the land, traveling to nearby cities and meeting new friends.  But never did she forget her true mission.  She quickly learned that Orcs were an unwanted threat to the land, and it is here that she tested her mettle.  Traveling to Kelethin she learned of this place called Crushbone...the heart of the Orc infestation.  It was here that Ygraine left her youth behind and honed her skills as a Knight. Having stood in the Throne Room and slayed Emperor Crush himself, she knew it was time to continue training elsewhere.  Her journeys took her far to the south, for she had heard tales of fearsome Minotaurs deep within the Steamfront Mountains.  Ygraine discovered the city of Ak'Anon while there, and made friends among some of the Gnomes.  Ygraine discovered others who adventured like her, and formed groups.  She was asked to join other causes, enter pacts with groups of adventurers...but none offered her the justice and honor she was seeking.  None held the sanctity which she held true.  It was during this time that she befriended other adventurers, ones she had seen time and time again in Faydwer.  She longed for the times of laughter and friendship back in Felwithe and sought to have this again.  It was during this time she traveled with a Druid named Keaerin (a mercurial wood elf always testing her patience, much to Ygraine's humor) and a fellow Paladin named Fabyan (a High Elf like herself who remembered her from the academy) and last but not least, a Wizard (a lot she never quite understood) named Stourm (another High Elf from Felwithe, but younger than she).  Ygraine learned of this place called Unrest....the corrupt heart and source of the undead.  But she also knew she was far too weak and untried to venture there.  So, she decided to continue to strengthen her sword arm and mind.

With her new found companions, Ygraine was in much better spirits, but she still hungered for adventure.  They traveled through the Butcher Block Mountains and onto Kaladim.  It was here she discovered the ocean....and the promise of distant lands.  New People.  New Adventures.  She persuaded her friends to journey with her, for the only way she could honor her parents and serve the church was to become a fierce and strong Knight.  These new lands promised that.

It was while exploring these new lands, very different from her beloved Faydwar, that Ygraine found what she was looking for.  A Cause and ideal she could give her heart too.
A group of noble, honorable adventurers had time and time again given aid to Ygraine and her friends.  Always expecting nothing in return.  She learned that they lived by the motto "Justice by the sword and scroll".  When she learned that they lived by the tenants to protect the innocent, destroy evil, defend the cause of good, assist others in need, combat injustice and cruelty, shun all things unclean and always uphold the sanctity of justice....Ygraine knew she had found her place.   She knew her father and mother would approve.  The Sacred Guardians were the answer......
