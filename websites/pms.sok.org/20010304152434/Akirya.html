Akirya

Ht: 1.70m (5’6”)
Wt: 48.9 kg. (108lbs.)
Build: Athletic
Hair: Platinum-Blonde
Complexion: Fair
Eyes: Blue
Age: 19
Race: Half-Elven
Level: 15th moon
Class: Ranger
Diety: Karana
Marital Status: Single, never been married.

Profile: She’s feisty and headstrong and always tends to take on more than she can handle. She would never betray someone she calls a friend and will help them in everyway she can. Not reckless in battle, but very aggressive. She can follow orders, but can lead if need be as well. In times of relaxation she is a party girl and lets her hair down. She can be very sweet and sultry when she wants to be, but get on her bad side and she has the fury of a tigress.

Background: Born on the streets of Qeynos to a drunken, and abusive father and a submissive mother Akirya came into the world. She had watched her mother suffer greatly at the hands of the man that gave her life. Her mother, Talania, a wood-elf, was a merchant in town and her father, Rigelios, a human, was a gate guard. Day after day he’d come home complaining about the people who’d come into the city, and that the pay wasn’t enough – often drunk, often angry. Talania would walk on eggshells around him, terrified of the man she had come to love and bear a child with. He’d often beat her infront of Akirya and soon she grew to hate the man she’d come to call father.

Her only childhood times of happiness were when her mother used to take her Surefalls Glade to visit her cousin, a local ranger. This place was a paradise, with many protected and docile woodland creatures. Akirya would often come here to think and cry. She wished her mom and her could come to live here and leave her father. Talania however was a faithful wife. She prayed to Tunare everyday that things would change and that the man she loved would come back to her – that day never came.
 
 

Rigelios beatings increased and soon started to include Akirya when she tried to defend her mother. One drunken night, Talania caught Rigelios molesting Akirya, that was the last straw for her. No longer would she suffer anymore indignities from this man and she would not allow this to spread to her daughter. She summoned up Tunare’s divine might and attacked her husband. He fled from her very intoxicated and severly wounded and sounded for the guards. Talania told her daughter to run and she would hold them off, but Akirya begged her mother to come with her. Talania cast a spell that made Akirya move a lot faster and she ran off towards surefalls glade. The last image she ever saw of her mother was her bathed in a golden glow with her hands blazing crimson, as the guards began swarming her like locusts.

It was 10 years now since that incident. Akirya had trained in Surefalls Glade to become a ranger. She vowed that she would find out what happened to her mother when she got stronger and if her father still lived, she would kill him. The rangers and druids were very kind to her and she advanced in her skills very quickly. Upon her third moon of advancement her trainer told her he had a gift for her. Her mother had provided a few things for Akirya so her life would be easier. The bundle contained a various pieces of valuable armor, magical jewelry, and a fine assortment of weapons. She was now ready to find her mother and challenge this world.

She trekked back to Qeynos to learn her mother had died two years prior in a Qeynos prison cell. All alone her feelings turned to anger and vengeance. She now only sought the death of her father. Akirya turned against Tunare that day. Seeing her as having forsaken her mother, she felt the words of Karana better suited her. She learned that her father had been transferred to Freeport and had assumed an alias – Lindeal. She vowed to go to the city and that she would see him dead. She knew she was not yet strong enough, but needed to keep an eye on her enemy.

The trip to Freeport was hazardous. Travelling through the lands of her god’s namesake was dangerous and she almost died twice. As luck would have it Karana provides. A kindly druid named Redhare cast a spell of speed on her, and a cleric whose name she forgets healed her in a fight with a lion. She arrived in Freeport and was bound, ready to gain power and seek her revenge. She has been fighting in the lands of the commons, the groves of misty thicket, and the deserts of Northern Ro and Oasis. She always keeps an eye out for her father, waiting for her chance to strike. So ends her tale for now…
