Requirements to apply:
  • You  character must be female.
  • You must role-play.
  • You must have ICQ.
  • You must have e-mail, internet/web access.
  • You must create your character on the Fennin Ro Server.
  • Your character must be at least 10th level.
  • You must read all the requirements below and be willing to adhere to them.
  • You must then, fill out the joining application, HERE.
Once you're willing to meet the above requirements, and after applying you will receive an e-mail conformation.

Things that do not matter:

  • PvP status
  • Race
  • Class
  • Alignment
What you can expect:

Dues: Guild dues are mandatory.  Each Sister must donate 5 gold to the guild mistress upon pledge induction.
And the following applies to mandatory guild dues:
  • For Levels 10 - 20: 1 pp
  • For Levels  21 - 30: 5 pp
  • For Levels  30 - 40: 10 pp
  • For Levels  40 - 50: 20 pp
  • For Levels  50 - 60: 30 pp
These fees will go towards guild member trade skills and guild sponsored events.

Meetings: Once a month the guild meets to discuss upcoming changes, events, thoughts, suggestions and other issues that might come up throughout your adventuring. It is also at these meetings that those who have met requirements will go through the Ceremony of Sisterhood and receive their Sisterhood Title.

Levels of Sisterhood: (what to expect)

[Pledge] --

  1. You will recieve an e-mail after you apply going over in detail everything you will need to do to begin your pledgeship.  If we do not know you, we suggest you attend at least one Happy Hour, and try to spend some time hunting or chatting with some guild members so we can get to know you and you can get to know us.
  2. You must write a biography for your character.  You will not receive your guild tag until the guild mistress has received your character's biography.
  3. You must write in your journal (in the private section) each evening you play and share what you have learned.
[Whelp] --
  1. Once the Guild Mistress and the Pledge Counselor decide that you've proven your worth and compatibility with the guild you'll be promoted to Whelp.
  2. You must write in your journal (in the private section) each evening you play and share what you have learned.
  3. You must attend at least one guild event and Guild meeting
[Wench] --
  1. Once the Guild Mistress and the Pledge Counselor decide you've proven your worth, compatibility, trust, honor and dedication to the guild, you will be contacted and sent on a quest.  Each quest will vary for each potential member.
[Sister] --
  1. Once your quest has been satisfactorily completed, you will now be eligible for the rank of Sister.
  2. Your membership will be voted on upon by all officers.
  3. You will go through the Ceremony of Sisterhood and receive your Sisterhood Title.
