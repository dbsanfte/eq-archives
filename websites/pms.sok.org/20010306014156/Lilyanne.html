Lilyanne
Class: Shaman
 
 

 I was born in the frozen city of Halas just north of the towering Everfrost Peaks. A land that is filled with crystals and diamonds that fall softly from the dark sky above. The wind whispers, telling its secrects that it has learned, as is blows by you...  My land is beautiful, it will always be my home.

 I remember only bits and pieces of my early childhood. Memories of my mother, her sweet face..her loving arms that would hold me and comfort me in my times on need. My Father who was a brave and fierce warrior who loved my mother and me dearly. I try to recall more but when I do everything turns grey in my mind...except for the red...the burning, blistering, pungent red.

 The fire..it was the fire that stole everything from me. My soul...my heart...and my parents. They said it was an accident..someone had not put there fire pit out correctly..it was an accident...only...an accident. But that did not comfort me, it could not bring them back.

 I was alone. Yes, the villagers tried to take care of me and treat me like I was their own daughter, but how could I accept that? These people, yes the may of been friends, but they were not my parents. And how DARE they try to take my mother and fathers place?!

 I ran.

 I ran until I felt that my heart would explode....  Then "she" found me.

 A woman in her early 30's.  She looked wise for such a young age. She found me hudled under a tree in the western Karanas, crying and cursing the gods for what they had done to me.  I remember feeling a hand placed on my shoulder and seeing a female human face look down at me. "My dear, why are you crying?"
 

 Her name was Kiya. She took me to her home, a cottage she had built with her husband. She told me of her story, her life, her loves and her loses. She told me that no matter what pain and torment I was feeling now that it would not last forever. The pain, she said, will slip more and more with time. Kiya was right.

 I still continue to live with Kiya and her husband, it has been 9 years since my first encounter with them. She told me a few years ago of a guild that she was once a member of. A guild of women that gathered together for the sake of helping each other. The Pretentious Malevolant Sisters. I have come to Freeport to seek them out and their guidance as I grow to understand myself more. I hope to grow wise with the knowlage that they share and in turn share my knowlage of life and of magic. And hope to become friends with them. Without friends..what is this life for?
