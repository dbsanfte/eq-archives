Stormiee
Wood Elf Ranger

My name is Stormiee. My Parents abandoned me at the age of 2 months old, in a dark and evil swamp, with only a note saying: take care of our daughter Stormiee, we love her very much......

I was raised by trolls; they treated me horrible, like a slave, a piece of garbage. So the day I turned 9 years old, I was able to escape while they slept, and ran away. I ran for days and days. I was able to reach a place called Freeport. There I saw this man, he looked scary at first, but he was so sweet and nice.  He saw that I was ill, and took me in.

He healed me and gave me clothes and weapons and food.......I asked him to help me find out what I was and who I was. He said, "You are a wood elf ranger.  Your home is Kelethin, the Tree City, in Greater FayDark." I told him of my horrible life and he promised to get me home to find out where my parents were. He never gave his name. He said, "You will find me again and then I will  tell you my name."  All I knew was that he too was a wood elf, but a druid with magical powers.  He cast this spell and we disappeared and reappeared in this beauitful city called Kelethin,  my home land. There I found my own kind and they taught me how to fight and be a ranger.

I never told anyone else but that kind druid who helped me get home of my life history.......

Then one morning months later, I was depressed and thinking of my horrible life before I found my home land.  That day a woman heard me crying and came into my home and asked if I was alright? I told her no, and then I told her my horrible life story.  She was scared and afraid and started crying. She said, "Are you Stormiee?" and I told her yes.  She said, "My name is Crysta, I am your Mother!"

I was mad and angry and happy, and I wanted to hug her,  but then I told her, "I hate you, you left me to be treated like an animal, GO AWAY!" I yelled.

She said that she loved me and told me why she did it. I told her I did not care about why she did it, but that I wanted to meet my father.  She said that she just heard he was taken prisoner by the trolls, that he has been looking for me for years...Now I have to find him, and forgive my parents for what they have done...

I heard of a rumor of women warriors, called the Pretentious Malevolent Sisters.  I need to join them so they can help me
look for my father and rid the world of these trolls who raised and hated me, who treated me horrible.  I also need to forgive my mother but not now, not yet, I have a quest to do.  I hope the sisters accept me and help me...maybe I can find the druid and he can help too....
