Xyna
Wood Elf
Ranger
Age 42
 
 

I, Xyna was very young when it happened.  My family resided in Kelethin, fair city in the trees.  Most of my family were trades people and traveled all of Faydwer selling our wares.  I always looked forward to visiting Lesser Faydark woods, because the caravans traveling from all of Faydwer would meet for the Summer Solstice Fair, and my gnomish friend’s family would be there to meet us.

We were adventuresome.  We love traversing the nearby stream and following it to the Fairy City.  It was one such escapade that saved our very lives.

It was a typical Fair day.  Warm, bright and sunny, we sought escape from our chores by grabbing packs of bread and honey mead, then heading into the nearby thicket.  We fished and swam after eating our fare.  Then took a nap under the trees.

We awoke to screams and cries of terror.  We peered up over the tree roots from our resting-place.  What I witnessed was horrifying.  Our lovely camp was on fire; dark elves and orc raiders were pillaging our Solstice Fair.  Everyone in the camp was slaughtered, our tents and wagons looted.

Together we ran as fast and far as we could.  We tried to get back to Ak’anon, her gnomish home, and give warning to someone.  But the evil necromancers of the Eldritch Collective captured us.  Meldrath the Malignant killed my friend and decided to use me in an experiment to create a lich slave.

She gave a good show of becoming a promising acolyte.  But she had plans for Meldrath.  She had discovered she was a wizard, not a necromancer.  She studied from books Meldrath forbid, and learned her true calling.  Then, one night, she put a sleeping potion in Meldrath’s evening cup of wine, but before he fell asleep, he discovered her treachery and crushed her with his necromantic powers.

Later, I heard someone burn off my shackles with a burst of flame. She was a powerful wizard, completely covered from head to toe in her wizardly robes. I could see nothing of her mysterious features. She had come to Meldrath's caves seeking a magical item. When she saw me, she freed me! I was shocked to say the least, but followed her to my escape from the caves of Meldrath and his evil minions.

As we journeyed, I found that I had an uncanny sense of direction and could also tell who was following us.  With my skills and my strange new friend's magic, we made our way safely out of the mountains and back to the forest of Faydark.

Cynarra fed me, clothed me, and offered to accompany me to a city called Freeport.  There she said were other women, young women like us that had joined together to help each other.

Imagine my shock and surprise to discover Cynarra was the feared and hated Teir’Dal!!!  Cynarra helped me to understand that all women have a connection and we must stick together to help each other.  She took me to Freeport and introduced me to Lillith and Iolanthe, the founders of Pretentious Malevolent Sisters, and to Ethlinn, our Guildmistress.  The rest is history.
