Ayeesha

Hi.  My name is Ayeesha.  I don’t really know what it is I am supposed to say here but I guess I should tell you a little bit about myself.  My name is Ayeesha, oops I guess I already said that, hehe…anyway I was born in Felwithe which is the capital of the High Elves, which is good since I am a high elf.  My mom and dad are great parents and have always taken real good care of me.

I was tested when I was young and it was decided that I should become an enchanter, I think it was because of how I look, I mean most people say I am really pretty, hehe.  So I spent a lot of time with my instructors and even though I frequently made mistakes I tried real hard to do well and eventually they told me that I had to go out into the real world to try out my talents.  I don’t think it had anything to do with what happened in class the day before, despite what Charmda says.  Charmda is my oldest and dearest friend.  The day before we were all in a lesson to learn how to turn a knife into a pet, you know not like a dog or cat but something that we could use to help protect us, I don’t really know why they call it a pet, but anyway…so the lesson was going well until it was my turn to try.  I said all the right things, I think, but my mind began to wander, which is what my instructor always tells me to watch out for, but I couldn’t help it, he had this huge stain on his robe from lunch and I just thought that was so funny but when I finished my incantation the instructor had turned into a plate of elf stew, which was what the stain on his robe was from.  Well the fuss they made about that was not really necessary, I don’t think….they all ran around yelling about this and that, but finally someone turned him back into himself so there was no real harm done.  And I don’t think that had anything to do with them sending me away, I mean things like that have happened to me my whole life, like that time everyone in the town lost their hair…I was running to class and reciting the lesson we were suppose to know but then I tripped going up the steps to the class and I still don’t know what I said but suddenly everyone in the town was bald, except for me of course.  Or that time we were practicing our levitation spells and I got to thinking of all the nice things I could buy if I only had enough money….well I never saw it myself but they eventually got the bank building back down on the ground.  So I think Charmda is wrong, but don’t tell her I said that she might get mad at me.

So here I am out in the real world and becoming a real good enchanter.  At first it was kind of scary what with all the Orcs and other bad things that are just outside Felwithe but eventually I learned to use my magic and it isn’t so bad now.  I did start that one little fire, the one that burned down the forest next to Felwithe but I hear the trees are coming back really well now, even if they won’t let me back to see for myself.  I can even work that pet spell now, well most of the time that is.  So if you see me around say hi, my name is Ayeesha.
