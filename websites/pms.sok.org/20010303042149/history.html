Legacy -

A commitment to the ideals that this guild of woman was established for.

It all began with two woman, Iolanthe and her sister, Lillith.  Iolanthe had just came through a bad period her life and was reunited with her sister Lillith.  Iolanthe saw a need.  A place, an organization, something, for the Woman of Norrath to go to, an organization which would protect her fellow woman from the prejudices of the male dominated world.  She formed her vision, and it became the Pretentious Malevolent Sisters. After the loss of Iolanthe and Lillith, the responsibilities of guild mistress fell to Ethlinn, whose vision of the guild and what it should be helped the Sisterhood grow even stronger.

Now, the responsibilities of guild mistress have come to rest on my shoulders. Needless to say, these women are a hard act to follow. They have left behind a legacy of strength and honor that I strive to uphold. In honor of the women who came before me, and as a guide to those who will come after, I demand and expect of myself and all sisters that they live up to the legacy.

A sister must be respectful of all her other sisters, regardless of race or religion.
A sister must respect and defend her sisters, through whatever means necessary.
A sister's word must be dependable beyond question.
She must never flee from the face of her enemies.
She must be generous and kind to her fellow sisters.
And always, she must be the champion of all that is a strong and formidable woman.

Androdameia Pallida`mors, Former Guild Mistress

My Vision of the Guild -

Are we an evil guild? I cannot count how many times I have been approached with this question. It could have to do with the fact that one of our founders was a very opinionated shadow knight, and a devoted follower of Innoruuk. It could also have to do with the fact that a number of our sisters are Tier'dal. These explanations, however, don't account for people who don't know us at all assuming we are an evil guild as soon as they see our guild name. On its surface, I suppose I can understand that assumption, but lets dig a little deeper, shall we?

Why would a group of women who strive to be respected in this land choose to affiliate their guild with the letters PMS? Definitely it was intended to be a play on words, a bit of a joke if you will, but there is much more to it than that. Lets look at it this way: Let us say a man is confronted with someone or something he does not agree with. He assesses the situation, then issues his strong opinion on the matter, stating it simply and with conviction, and offering no apologies for feeling that way. Nothing out of the ordinary about that, right? Now lets say a woman behaves the same way...how often does she get a raised eyebrow and the thought, "What's her problem? What's with the attitude? She must have PMS." Too often a woman with a strong opinion and the courage to stand behind it is relegated to being some bitch with hormonal problems. If this is how we must be seen, so be it. We embrace it and areproud to call ourselves PMS. We are strong, opinionated women, and we offer no apologies for that; by taking on the initials PMS we openly state the kind of women we are. Does that make us evil? I think not. But to some, the whole idea of a women's-only guild is scary, how dare they exclude men? Some people can't handle the idea that a woman does not need a man to protect her; it worries them and makes them question their self worth. Rather than face the fact that if we are with a man it is by choice rather than out of necessity, they decide we are evil.

This same train of thought can be used to explain the first word in our guild tag, Pretentious. Defined as: expressive of affected, unwarranted, or exaggerated importance, worth, or stature. Since the beginning, this guild has thrust itself into the forefront, saying we are the Sisters, fear us, we are to be respected. We gave ourselves the importance and stature we felt we deserved...the importance and stature that too many fail to give the women of Norrath. We are often seen as just pretty things to look at. This is in part due to the way we were created. How hard is it for a female wood-elf to be taken seriously, when all she gets is constant ogling and catcalls? There is nothing she can do about the fact that the gods who created this world decided no matter what she wears she should look like a scantily clad nymphette.

I remember when the time finally came for me to get my banded armor. Iolanthe chipped in and Ethlinn forged it for me. I was so excited at having my first armor, and I stripped off my leather and slipped into my newly forged banded armor...only to find that I looked like a chorus girl from the Maiden's Fancy! I put on my banded tunic only to find that despite its looks before I put it on, it did not protect my chest in battle, it merely gave the oglers more to look at! I put on my banded pants expecting to have protection for my legs, only to find when I looked at my reflection that I was wearing high cut metal panties and see through glittery stockings. I am a shadow knight, not a chorus girl, I thought. No self-respecting shadow knight would be caught dead in this get-up. Needless to say, I quickly found some bronze to cover my legs with.

Those of us who have worn banded, blackened iron, or blood stained tunics all know what I am talking about. How hard is it to get respect when your breasts are falling out of your supposed "armor"? Well, that is where the pretention comes in...

Finally, malevolence. Malevolence suggests a bitter persistent hatred that is likely to be expressed in malicious conduct... but it is also desire to cause pain, injury, or distress to another. I, for one, have this desire often in Norrath. Like when that annoying halfling keeps strafing around to stand behind me so he can look at my black panties (even though I wear darkforge greaves, the gods couldn't resist making me pull tiny black panties over them). Or when that warrior comes up to me and says "U r so hot!" or "U r so sexy." Or when one of my wood elf sisters has grown so tired of the ogling, staring, and harrassment she gets in Oasis that she mostly just goes under the dock and fishes now. A bitter persistent hatred? You bet your arse. And I don't know about the rest of you, but I desire to cause pain, injury, or distress (preferrably all three) to anyone who doesn't respect the women of Norrath.

So what are the Pretentious Malevolent Sisters? A group of women who refuse to be treated as trinkets, sex toys (well, except for Cynarra heh), or pretty things to look at (well, except for Pixa). A place where women have banded together, respect each other, and put each other first. A place where they can go and express their opinions and thoughts, and the reply is not "oooh, u r sexy when u r mad." Are we evil? Well, I suppose that is all a matter of perception. Respect us, and you will find true and loyal friends who will stand by you at any cost. Disrespect us, you'll find out what pretentious malevolence is all about.


- Androdameia Pallida`mors, Former Guild Mistress


 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 


Origional History, Written by Iolanthe Demonblade

Iolanthe and Lillith have now  found each other after so many years apart.  They've had many adventures together, and apart.  But what could possibly make these two so adamant about proving their genders abilities?

When one understands where the creators of this guild came from, one understands why the guild was formed.  From birth Iolanthe and Lillith were both viewed as subservient and weak.  They left home to over come that stigma.

However, Lillith and Iolanthe found that some still looked at women as though they are weak, sniveling, ignorant and substandard.

Realizing this Iolanthe and Lillith set out to make everyone in Norrath understand that women, too can be the most respected of warriors, role models, mages, priestess, or whatever they choose to be.  Regardless of race, class, age, background, or skills.. they knew that it was time everyone knew that females could be whatever they wanted to be.

Through the oppression and abuse of their fellow female companions, Lillith and Iolanthe see that much of Norrath needs to learn about respect.

Iolanthe is malevolence, heart and soul.  Her hate, in the worship of a Dark god Innoruuk powers her every breath.

Lillith is pretension right down to her toes.  Her manipulation of minds, her open loving but always alert heart brings a breath of fresh life into Norrath.

Together with the help of their follow sisters in Arms, they are, The Pretentious Malevolent Sisters.


Guild History from Ethlinn:

I can describe the history of the guild from my perspective, I'm sure there are members that could do at better job describing the beginnings of the Guild (perhaps Aeneldile, our first officer) and the current events in our guild (Phaera, Valkryst, etc.), but I'm one of the few who has been here since the beginning.

The early days of the Guild in Norrath were hard days.  Full leather at the 12th circle wasn't all that bad, banded was a luxury.  Many things have changed; and somehow the world has become less frightening as I have gotten to know it better (with perhaps the exception of the Kithicor Forest which now frightens me more now).

Even though my birthplace is Freeport, when I was still very young my father left the city  and traveled to Qeynos (I wonder how horrible travel was in those days - my father must have been a 50th circle warrior).  It was in Qeynos where I learned that even though you might die, your god will bring you back.  I also meet Iolanthe here.  In those days the sister who traveled with me was Kalynn, half elf treehugger extraordinaire (AKA Druid, for those that never meet her).  Iolanthe and myself tried to travel to Freeport in our youths to meet with the rest of our sisters in Freeport (mainly Aeneldile and Narna).  This was an adventure.  We saw many a creature that with one swipe of their claws could kill us.  These were the days when they was nary a soul hunting in the Karanas, especially the North and Eastern Plains.  Somehow we arrived at the ramp up to Highpass Hold and made our way up.  Well, needless to say, Iolanthe made it to Freeport, and I went back to hunting with Kalynn near Qeynos.  Kaylnn and myself became full sisters after traveling over to Erudia and killing a troll that was harassing some friends of the guild.  It was really fun adventuring with Kalynn in those earlier days.  We developed a deep bond and trust.  Unfortunately, one of our major goals remains unfulfilled to this date - neither of us has grown our hair longer or acquired a ponytail.

One day, while adventuring by myself, I was contacted by the ethereal messenger service by guildmembers/Sisters Narna (a wood elf ranger) and Aeneldile (a human monk).  They were wondering why I was still up in Qeynos and not down in the Freeport area playing with them.  I said to myself "Why am I not in Freeport?"  So I just took off.  Well, I was wetting my pants all the way there.  Those creatures were still scary.  I somehow got to Highpass Hold, sat down and couldn't move any more.  This was my first great sisterhood experience.  Aeneldile and Narna came to my rescue.  We somehow found someone to bind us to the keep.  And we began the run to Freeport.  Short one.  We got Narna killed by an orc.  Then we started again.  We followed Narna the Lost Ranger through the forest and eventually found the exit into the Commonlands.  We ran through the Commonlands, where Iolanthe joined us, and eventually made it to Freeport.

Why do I relate these stories?  Because they all say something important.  How important we are to one another.  Whether it means fighting side by side, or just giving support, we are here for each other.  This condition continues today.  Whether it's Kalendra rescuing Valkryst from Neriak, or Dalane and Mystie killing gators together in the Oasis, similar stories are occurring all the time.   Tales of sisterhood.

The guild was small when we started but in time our membership grew.  We have gained sisters and we have lost sisters, but in the long run we have gained strength.  We have also gained much respect.  There are few people indeed that have not heard of the sisters.  And I myself would say most of these people would say they respect us and respect our strength.

We have experienced many hard times.  The worst of which is recent history, the horrifying murders of Sileas (Kestra's daughter) and Lillith (one of our founders) by Iolanthe's father.  And, of course the loss of our  founder, Iolanthe, the one whose ideals this organization were built on.  She too fell, but in the way every sister wishes to...Triumphing over their most hated enemy and driving out the fears and nightmares that haunt their lives.  She died to save us from her own worst fears.  For that we are forever grateful.

But we have had good times.  Sisters have loved good men, and married them.  We have had many exciting Happy Hours, where all of Norrath was invited to join us.  And we do have good memories of Iolanthe.  Her son is but one of those happy thoughts.

And I will strive myself to make this a strong organization of the women of Norrath.  I do so in Iolanthe's memory for all of my sisters present and future.  We will go on.  And we will gain strength.

This guild is for you, my sisters.  May it always support and protect you.

Ethlinn Govannen
Guildmistress, Pretentious Malevolent Sisters
