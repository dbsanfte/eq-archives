Viktoria


I was born amongst a happy family of Wood elves in the city of Kelethin. We were considered a bit eccentric by a few of our neighbors but loved by all. My family consisted of two loving parents [who made us look and laugh about any troubles that we thought life threatening at a young age] and one elder sister, two younger brothers.

My elder sister is the material one. She was always willing to share her loot with us and then some. I was the second born and forever a problem for my mother. I believed in the goodness of all things, shape, size and form not important. This led to many false threats from my mother that she would leave our home if I brought home one more stray. The memories make me laugh!

My younger brothers were like oil and water. The youngest should have been, may be to this day, a Rogue. He has the swiftest hand and was scolded many times over it. I believe that his good looks let him get away with it!

I find myself here because of what fell on the third born, first son. He was the " Family Jester " with a wit as fast as his anger. If he took to you, he made you laugh endlessly. If he didn't, he made sure that revenge would be
his!

We lived like this for many happy years, as a family.

Until the day that my brother came home diseased and his humor could not help him. The healers in the city did all that they could, to no avail. He informed us that a great evil was following him, wanting his bloodline erased from Norrath. He told us that our family should part ways for now, avoid the danger that he knew would take his life soon. "Never let it be known that we were related! "

I looked into the fading light of his eyes and lost part of my soul. One look at the others and I saw the same hollowed look in their eyes.At just that moment, a dark shadow seemed to fill the room. Evil. My little Jester had just enough breathe in him to tell us to run! We did.As much as it hurt, we scattered. We never said " Goodbye " or " See you soon. "

This is how I found myself in this great tavern, with women of all races speaking to me. I do not remember many details as to how I ended up here.
