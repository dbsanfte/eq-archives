Kililye
Wood Elf Druid

Well I guess you all want to know who I am. This is a hard question as I don't really know who I am. Let me start with my parents. My mother is a wandering woodelf druid who met a handsome ranger in her home town of Kelethin. One thing led to another and I was born. I was conceived at a great festival. I never knew my father, each handsome woodelf ranger that passes by I wonder if that may be him. Perhaps one day I will find out who it was. My mother had been married to a human before and had a Daughter, Ainozama.

I studied the arts of magic and being a druid in and around the tree city. I learned fairly fast and quickly discovered that I needed to move on to another city to advance my skills.

I wanted to find my sister. I knew of here and had heard stories but had never laid my eyes on her. I left Kelethin after my 10th season and headed for the human city of Freeport. I had heard that she was part of a great all woman guild in this area. Finally I stumbed into a tavern called the tall tale ale and found who I had been searching for. I also met many of her guild sisters this is where you find me today fighting beside the sisters my only wish to serve and protect them.
