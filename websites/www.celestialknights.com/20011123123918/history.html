Our History
Sometime in 1996, a few guys started playing together often in a game known as Multi-player Battletech on America Online. A few more joined, and then more, and the Celestial Knights were born. Based upon the ethics of a chivalrous, medieval past, they started to form the core ideas and values that would become the mainstay of the Guild.

AOL institued an impeccably stupid hourly tithe for the Celestial Knights to be able to play MPBT, which sent the entire Battletech:Solaris gaming society into a panic. As many had predicted, the Golden Age of Battletech began to crumble for no one could pay the outrageous fees accompanying the game now. The Celestial Knights fought off obscurity by hastily erecting a web page, but the inevitable still occurred... THE CK HAD PERISHED and the DARK AGES had begun.

Six months after the Dark Ages had begun for the CK, a yong knight named Edrick returned to the once glorious CK HQ's back in AOL. After navigating through all the rubble, Edrick went to the drop room to see how his babies were doing. There he was assaulted by the unmistakable stench of alcohol and standing at the door was Dark Hart, a box of donuts under his left armpit and a bottle of Jack Daniels in his right hand. After much talk and a few games here and there, the two decided that Gamestorm would be the perfect place for the CK to recapture their former glory. They had some cool games in addition to MPBT, and several of us also began playing Legends of Kesmai. By this time it seemed that MPBT was dying again, and there was news on the horizon of a new game.

After much hemming and hahing, and reluctance slowly CK started to migrate to Everquest. Alas, when there were enough of us to think about making the guild on EQ, someone had already taken our name of Celestial Knights. Edrick contacted the administration at Verant, but it was decided that it would be best for us not to try to co-exist with another body of CK, so we gathered suggestions and voted on FIRST DEFENDERS to continue our legacy of honor and chivalry.
