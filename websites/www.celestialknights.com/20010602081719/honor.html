Honor Code

Ever vigilant we are the FIRST DEFENDERS and the last hope in humanities darkest hour. We are the champions of the old code of Honor and Chivalry; Guardians of Peace and Virtue and the Avatars of Truth and Justice. We are the beacon of light that shines upon the path of righteousness so others may follow. With the wings of an eagle and the strength of a lion, justice is delivered swift and sure. Though at times the dark forces seem relentless in their pursuits, we shall never yield to those that attempt to extinguish the light of virtue.

A First Defender is sworn to chivalry
Undaunted they follow the path of Honor
Valiantly they defend the Helpless
Unfaltering he pursues the Truth
Their spirit knows only Virtue
Their blade vanquishes the Wicked of Heart
Their might upholds Justice

The FIRST DEFENDERS is a stable that carries on the tradition of the more honorable days of Medieval Kighthood. Honor above all is held in high esteem. It is the foundation of our very being. For without Honor, our destiny becomes void and meaningless. Determination is the force that drives the Defenders to victory. For without determination, ultimately all would succemb to failure. Skill is a learned process by which all Defenders are trained to their fullest potential. For without skill, honor and determination become mute, unable to fulfill their roles through action. These are the virtues that form the very cornerstone of our credo.

Justice...Valor...Truth...Vigilance...Humility...Righteousness...

All together these qualities give DEFENDERS their strength, power, and is the basis of what it truly means to be a FIRST DEFENDER!!

Code of Conduct

Whether on the field of battle or awaiting fellow adventurers, zoning, or merely travelling the land of Norrath, a FIRST DEFENDER without falter conforms to these virtues. As such a FIRST DEFENDER will maintain the highest caliber on conduct and respect for others as well as his fellow FIRST DEFENDERS.

Our steeds may have changed though the course of the Millenia, but the heart of a FIRST DEFENDER remains the same...TRUE TO THE CORE!!!! Combat in Norrath is the honorable contest to prove ones honor and skill against the considerable and mulit-faceted foes encountered throughout the land. With this in mind, a FIRST DEFENDER will commit to not obtaining PvP status unless on that specific server. Tis also recognized that throughout Norrath, the balances of good and evil are necessary to balance the land, so it is acceptable for a FIRST DEFENDER to roleplay an evil alignment or study the darker Arcane Arts, as long as their conduct is still worthy of the stated virtues.
