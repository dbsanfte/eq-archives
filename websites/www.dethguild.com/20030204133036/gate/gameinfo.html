This area is full of useful game info to help prevent you from becoming monster bait.

Character Classes:
Name: Fighter
Description: Fighters are the best melee battlers in the game. They are the only class capable of weapon mastery, and as such, no other warrior can stand against them in terms of pure combat ability. Fighters gain more attacks, inflict more damage, and gain special bones to hit, making them formidable killing machines. To maximize your fighter, you will want high strength and constitution. Having ample health will insure you can take a ton of abuse, and a high strength will assure that you deal out more than you take.
Restrictions: None
SilverDeth's Notes: Nobody can stand toe to toe with a fighter. Any class going heads up with one of these killing machines is going to get gutted and hung like a trophy. But a fighter is also very limited. They hack and slash darn good, but lack any other abilities, making them somewhat less interesting than other classes.
   
Name: Paladin
Description: Paladins are special holy warriors. While they lack the raw combat prowess of a fighter, a paladin is granted many clerical abilities that more than make up for the loss. A paladin can heal a person by laying his hands upon him, and can also turn demons and undead as a cleric. At higher levels, a paladin can actually cast clerical magic. He is constantly protected by a magical aura that helps defendhim from poisons, disease, and many other maladies. Like a fighter constitution and strength are very important, however, in order to use his clerical prowess, a paladin must also have a decent wisdom score.
Restrictions: A paladin MUST be lawful good in alignment.
SilverDeth's Notes: A paladin is very hard to kill with magic. Spells and curses run off them like water, and they tear undead or evil creatures apart. However, in terms of melee power, they cannot compete with a fighter.
   
Name: Ranger
Description: The ranger is another fighter subclass. Rangers tend to be masters of wilderness survival, not to mention skilled archers. While a ranger cannot whip a fighter in terms of pure battle prowess, they have a number of special powers that make them worth playing. A ranger can hide in shadows like a thief, as well as creep silently in for the kill. Woodland creatures are the allies of the ranger, and he may call on their aid in battle, and no naturally occurring animal will attack a ranger unless provoked. A good ranger has both a high strength, constitution, (like a fighter), but a ranger also needs a high dexterity score, to help him with his thieving skills and archery.
Restrictions: A ranger must maintain a good alignment.
SilverDeth's Notes: The ranger is a great person to have along in wilderness encounters. They also make awsome archers, and you will NEED missile weapons.
   
Name: Thief
Description: Thieves are a very diverse class. They lack any real stand up melee power, but possess a number of very specialized skills that make them a critical addition to any party. A thief can steal items from people or monsters, (earning otherwise unattainable loot), slink unseen in shadows, detect hidden traps, and unlock barred or locked doors. Also, thieves have a special power known as a "backstab," an ability that if successful, can inflict a mortal wound on almost any foe. A thief should concentrate on dexterity above all other traits, as it directly improves his/her chances of successfully using their abilities.
Restrictions: A thief cannot wear metallic armors, or use heavy weapons.
SilverDeth's Notes: What can i say about a thief except this - yeah anyone in the party can whip the thief, but without a good thief along, you are guaranteed to die - all of you. Nuff Said.
   
Name: Bard
Description: The bard is the jack of all trades, but master of none. A bard character can do just about anything. They can equip good weapons and armor, cast magic spells, steal like a thief, and their legend lore ability allows them to identify items like a cleric. A bard is a great solo character, due to the wide range of powers and abilities. A bard always has the answer to a situation. However, the diversity of this class is ultimately it's weakness: A bard excels in nothing. He is balanced evenly in all abilities, and thus, never really shines. He is a good place filler for parties lacking some character class, as he can easily emulate a fighter, wizard, or a thief. A bard desires a good balance of all ability scores, which will help him capitalize on his diversity.
Restrictions: A bard MUST be partially neutral in alignment.
SilverDeth's Notes: Bards rock at low levels, but their power diminishes greatly by the end of the game.
   
Name: Cleric
Description: Clerics are the healers and spiritual leaders of a party. A cleric can fight modestly, and has a great selection of spells, the majority of which heal damage or cure maladies. Clerics can also turn undead and demons, driving away, or sometimes, even destroying the creature outright. (An evil cleric has the same chance of bending the creature to his will, making the monster his loyal servant). A group cleric is essential to long term survival, as without magical healing, the party will quickly find themselves dead. A cleric should try for a high wisdom score, as this will directly effect the number of spells he/she may call upon in a day. Strength is a useful ability as well, for a cleric can don heavy armor and weapons, making them a reasonable fighter should the need arise.
Restrictions: A cleric may only use bludgeoning weapons.
SilverDeth's Notes: Like the thief, a cleric may not be a killer, but without one, you die. Simple as that.
   
Name: Druid
Description: Druids are nature priests. They call upon the power of the land to smite foes or heal allies. A druid cannot turn undead like a normal priest, however, they can shape-shift into different animal forms, gaining the natural attack and movement abilities of the chosen creature. A druid's spells tend to be more offense oriented than a clerics, but they are forbidden the heavier armors of the clergy, so they match up evenly. A cleric is a more powerful healer, but a druid can call down more offensive magic to use against enemies. As a druid , one should try for a high wisdom attribute to gain extra spells.
Restrictions: A druid MUST be True Neutral in alignment, and may only wear leather armors.
SilverDeth's Notes: Think of a druid as an earth-firster from hell. They can almost compete with a wizard in terms of destructive magic, but in a pinch, a druid makes a reasonable healer.
   
Name: Wizard
Description: Wizards are the spell castors royal of Baldur's Gate. They lack almost any shred of combat ability, but, they make up for this by being able to deal tremendous amounts of damage over a wide area. Wizards can memorize scrolls found on their travels and add them to their permanent spell book. From then on, a wizard may cast the spell as needed. Wizards can use wands as well as cast spells from scrolls, making them very dangerous foes. As a wizard, you need a high intellect, so as to memorize the higher level spells. Also, try to concentrate on constitution, as your meager hit points can use all the help they can get.
Restrictions: A wizard cannot wear armor or use heavy weapons.
SilverDeth's Notes: these guys are the masters of the one-hit bang. If a wizard gets the drop on anyone, they are a crispy critter. However, their low hit points will assure that they are always a step or two behind a big, buff fighter.
   

Available Weapons:

Name: Battle Axe
Description: The battle axe is a powerful weapon. It deals ton of melee damage, and is still relatively fast. It's a good pick for any fighter. Only fighters can use a battle axe, and its type slashing damage makes it a poor choice on undead creatures. Unlike Diablo, a battle axe can be combined with a shield.

Name: War Hammer
Description: A war hammer is a bludgeoning weapon, and as such, is very effective against skeletal undead. It's damage is somewhat less than other melee weapons, but it is fairly fast, and fits easily within a cleric's weapon restrictions.

Name: Mace
Description: A mace is a good melee weapon for a cleric or fighter. It is roughly comparable to a long sword in terms of damage and speed, while remaining a viable weapon against undead creatures. The mace is usually the weapon of choice for clerics.

Name: Halberd
Description: This is a towering axe, standing taller than a man. As such, it must be wielded with both hands. It is the most damaging non-magical weapon in the game, and it will quickly shred any foe in a fighter's hands. However, it is also very slow, and wielding it in close quarters will incur penalties.

Name: Dagger
Description: While the dagger may have rather pathetic damage potential, it is one of the fastest weapons available. Due to they stringent equipment restrictions applying to wizards, it may be the only weapon a mage can use.

Name: Short Sword
Description: The short sword has plenty to offer, especially to the rouge class. It offers reasonable melee damage, while at the same time being very fast. Additionally, the short sword is fairly light, always a consideration for the weaker character classes.

Name: Long Sword
Description: The long sword is the standard adventurer's weapon, and we see it glamorized movies and stories. It is relatively light, has great damage potential, and sports a good speed. The long swords only downside is it's general ineffectiveness against skeletal undead.

Name: Bastard sword
Description: For the guy that wants a bit more damage while still packing a shield, there is the bastard sword. It is a both heavier and slower then the long sword, but it also hits harder. A bastard sword can be used either one or two handed, making it a very flexible weapon.

Name: Two-Handed Sword
Description: This monstrous weapons stands nearly 5 feet tall, and inflicts terrible damage. Each swing can nearly cut a creature in twain, but this power comes at the cost of weapon speed and the use of a shield. This sword's users are less interested in saving their asses than in taking an opponent's.

Name: Morning Star
Description: The morning star is a piercing weapon that also combines the bludgeoning characteristics of a mace. It is fairly quick, and inflicts as much damage as a long sword. It is a wicked weapon, and in the hands of a master, is as deadly as any sword or axe.

Name: Spear
Description: The spear, while slower and less damaging than a sword, has the advantage of range. It can strike a foe down before they can close into melee. Also, a spear may be set to receive a charge, inflicting double damage on the charging opponent/creature.

Name: Short Bow
Description: The short bow is a compacted version of the long bow. It still inflicts the same damage as it's larger relative, however, the short bow suffers from a greatly reduced range. Bows fire two shots per round, and are excellent additions to a player's arsenal.

Name: Long Bow
Description: The long bow is identical to the short bow, except for the substantial increase in range. Bows fire two shots per round, and are excellent ranged weapons for a character to learn. Masters of the bow gain additional attacks, and bonuses to hit.

Name: Light Crossbow
Description: The light crossbow fires very slowly, but it drastically out ranges a bow in both damage and distance. Crossbows can be fired once every round, but, if a quarrel is loaded, it can be fired at a speed factor of 1, the fastest in the game. Reloading takes a long time however.

Name: Heavy Crossbow
Description: The heavy crossbow reloads even slower than the light crossbow, however, it shoots farther and hits harder than any other ranged weapon. Crossbows can be fired once every round, but, if a quarrel is loaded, it can be fired at a speed factor of 1, the fastest in the game.

Name: Sling
Description: The sling is a small, yet fast weapon capable of inflicting so-so damage to a target. It is also the only missile weapon available to clerics, who are banned from using blood shedding weapons. While not as damaging as other missiles,a sling is better than nothing at all.....

Name: Throwing Axe
Description: Only a heavy crossbow hits harder than a throwing axe. Although it has a limited range, any character proficient with axes can throw them, allowing a player to use both a melee and a missile weapon without having to divide his limited weapon proficentcy slots between several different arms.

Name: Dart
Description: This weapon suffers from a very limited range and poor damage. On the plus side, it is lightning fast, two can be hurled per round, and wizards can choose a proficentcy in darts, allowing them a combat option that will keep their fragile asses off the front lines.

Name: Sling Bullet
Description: These smooth metal or stone pellets are the ammunition for slings. Sling Bullets are collected in groups of 20, cost almost nothing, and are readily available, Halflings in particular are known for their prowess in the sling, and a sling master can be very deadly.

Name: Flight Arrow
Description: The flight arrow is the ammunition for the long and short bow. The arrows are cheap, easy to come by, and caches of them are often found on fallen foes. A diverse variety of magical arrows are available adding to the power of these weapons.

Name: Quarrel
Description: The quarrel is the ammunition for the crossbow. As easy to come by as arrows, quarrels are fairly common. They are equally cheap, and almost as many magical variants of crossbow quarrels exist, keeping this weapon evenly balanced with it's contemporaries.

Name: Flail
Description: A flail is a heavy metal ball attached to a length of chain. It is a bludgeoning weapon, so it lacks the damage that swords cause, (unless your dealing with undead, which this weapon will absolutely crush, no pun intended). A good buy for a cleric.

Name: Quarter Staff
Description: A quarter staff is the bludgeoning equivalent to a long sword. It causes respectable damage, and drifts toward the middle of the pack. It is a two handed weapon however, so the user cannot wield a shield while equipped with a quarter staff.

Name: Wands
Description: Wands are weapons that only wizards can employ. Each wand has a set number of charges, and at a cost of one charge, can cast a magic spell instantly. Unlike normal magic, wands can not be interrupted, and their casting times are far quicker. Once out of charges, a wand crumbles to dust, so use them wisely...
