Here in the Game Info section, you can find tips and tactics for mastering the five character classes of diablo 2. Included here is a general overview of the each class, a listing of the skills available to them, and great player v.s player tactics. Additionally, there is a section dedicated to trading in the "SOJ" market, listing prices and values of rares in stones, and a table of every known horodric cube recipe.

A dexterous archer, and wielder of the spear, the Amazon is a master of evasion. She is a skilled warrior, relying on cunning and agility to wear enemies down.

A brutal warrior, the barbarian is the king of melee combat. His weapons are his brute strength and his iron constitution - he uses them to grind enemies to dust.

The Necromancer is a scheming poisoner and summoner of minions. He can quickly summon a huge army to do his bidding - and destroy his foes.

A noble party leader, the paladin is a selfless warrior in all respects. His true strength lies in his auras, and they power they grant his allies.

The elements are the sorceress's playthings. She can conjure fire, ice, or electricity to obliterate her foes.
