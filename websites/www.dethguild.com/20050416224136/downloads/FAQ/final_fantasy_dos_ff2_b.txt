Press Ctrl+F and type in the following:

Enter.  Make sure you don't drop the ^ or you'll end up back in the Table of

: Skills & Attributes    ^[MSKL]
------------

Final Fantasy 2 has no levels at all; your characters gain skills based on how
you use them in battle. For example, a character who focuses on magic use will
in turn, gain skills that improve their casting abilities.

The Status Screen looks something like this:

: White and Black Magic    ^[MMGC]
------------

Spells are divided into two categories: White and Black Magic. Black Magic is
(mostly) attack magic whereas White Magic is (mostly) defensive and support
magic.  Given, there are exceptions to both types (Confuse, a Black spell,
does 0 damage, but confuses the target and Ultima and Holy are both attack
spells).

Any person can learn any spell, but having one character cast one color of
magic will allow them to become more powerful than if they cast both types
equally. Try to keep your characters pure Black or pure White Magic.

Here is a list of all the spells, arranged by color alignment, effect, family,
and Tome price. Tomes are always the same price btw. [QRWJEARCTKYSYOUNI]

Spell Families are also something you should watch. Some enemies can be harmed
more by certain spell families. Fire, Ice, Lit families are self-explanatory,
but some of the trickier ones (Body, Mind, etc) are listed here. If "None" is
listed in the "Family" section, the spell has no inherent family.

: Character Status    ^[MCST]
------------

There's more than one way to skin a cat, or damage a party member. Status
effects can completely change your characters' abilities by hindering certain
aspects of your powers.  A spellcaster is ineffective when inflicted with
Silence; a fighter is ineffective when inflicted with Darkness or Confusion.

A list of all Status Effects is included here, along with their effects and
whether the Status is Temporary or Permanent.  Permanent Status Effects can
only be removed with the Esuna spell; Temporary Status Effects can be removed
with the Basuna spell, but lose their effect after the battle ends.

 Status       Effect                                           Temp / Perm
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 Amnesia      Prevents a character from casting spells         Permanent

 Confusion    Character turns on his/her party members         Temporary

 Curse        Attack and Defense drop considerably             Temporary

 Darkness     Reduces Accuracy                                 Permanent

 KO           Character has 0 HP and cannot partake in         Permanent
               battle until revived; if party suffers KO,
               the game is over

 Mini         Character is shrunk and reduces power of         Temporary
               physical attacks

 Paralysis    Paralyzes character so they cannot attack        Temporary

 Poison       Damages character after every turn and after     Permanent
               every few steps

 Silence      Prevents a character from casting spells         Permanent

 Sleep        Puts character to sleep so they cannot attack    Temporary

 Stone        Character has been turned to stone and cannot    Permanent
               attack or be damaged

 Toad         Character is turned into a toad, Accuracy is     Permanent
               reduced, character cannot cast spells

 Venom        Damages character after every turn               Temporary

The 64-item limit in the original FF2 is no longer existant. Instead, you can

manually arrange your items, you can do so by selecting the Use option.


------------
: Formation    ^[MFRM]
------------

You must have one character in the front row, period. Your other three party
members can either remain in the front row or switch to the back row. When
placed in back, characters are unable to be hit with physical attacks, as long
as there is one character in the front row that isn't KO'ed. Magic attacks hit
front row and back row characters for the same amount of damage. Unless you
have Shell in effect, but that's another story. [1R23JAXC56KASEOQN]

Place your magic users in the back row and your fighters in the front. If you
have fighter/mage hybrids (I highly recommend you do), you can solve your
problems altogether and place everyone in the front row. :)

: Key Term System                                  ^[KYTM] :
------------                                    ------------

Final Fantasy 2 was the first and only game that allowed you to learn special
terms and use them to activate special events. Although there are only fifteen
Key Terms, it was the only game to ever have this feature.  Here's a list of
Key Terms available, and where you can learn them.

To learn a new Term, use the Learn option when prompted. You can then ask other
people about the Term and learn what they know about it or maybe even learn new
Terms. Be sure you ask your teacher about the Terms they teach you.

Note: Some NPC's don't let you ask about Key Terms, others do.

 Key Term             Location
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 Wild Rose            When you first begin the game, Hilda teaches you the 
                       password to get into any Rebel hideout. The Wild Rose is
                       the insignia of the Kingdom of Fynn.

 Mythril              After you infiltrate Fynn and retrieve the Ring. Return
                       to Hilda and she'll teach you the Term.

 Dreadnought          After you recruit Minwu into your party (right after
                       learning Mythril), talk to Hilda again and she'll teach
                       you Dreadnought.

 Airship              When you first enter Poft, go into the Pub and speak to
                       Cid. You can learn the Term from him.

 Sunfire              After the Dreadnought attacks, speak to Cid in Poft and
                       learn about the power source of Airships, the Sunfire.

 Goddess's Bell       After the Dreadnought attacks, speak to the King, who's
                       bedded up in Altair and ask him about the Sunfire to
                       learn about the Goddess's Bell.

 Dragoons             Once you destroy the Dreadnought, speak to Gordon after
                       he assumes control of the throne and he'll talk about
                       the mighty Dragoons.

 Wyverns              Ask Gordon about the Dragoons and you'll be able to learn
                       about the Wyverns that the Dragoons rode into battle.

 Mysidia              After you free Fynn (much later in the game), talk to
                       Hilda and learn about the land of mages, Mysidia.

 Mask                 Ask Hilda about Mysidia and she'll tell you about two
                       Masks that are needed to break the seal in Mysidia.

 Ekmet Teloess        Ask Hilda about the Mask and she'll tell you the password
                       to open the door that holds the White Mask.

 Ultima Tome          Talk to Gordon about Mysidia and he'll tell you about the
                       ultimate White Magic, Ultima.

 Cyclone              After obtaining the Ultima Tome, talk to Gordon and he'll
                       tell you about the Cyclone that's wreaked Altair and is
                       heading for Fynn. [IREJSADFCFKHSNOJN]

 Palamecia            Hilda will tell you about the only place the new Emperor
                       could be hiding: Palamecia.

 Jade Passage         After Ricard leaves the party, talk to Hilda and she'll
                       teach you this Key Term.

: Characters                                       ^[CHAR] :
------------                                    ------------

Three characters that will always be with you are Firion, Maria, and Guy. Your
fourth character will change at times; sometimes you won't have a fourth
character at all.  Each fourth member of your party will already be adept in
some form of fighting or magic.  It's your call whether you want to exploit
their current abilities or teach them some new ones.  Be warned though, your
fourth character will leave the party eventually, taking with them any new
tricks you've taught them.  Oh, and be sure to unequip their armor before they
leave. You'll never see it again otherwise.

------------
: Firion    ^[CFRN]
------------

Firion starts with the most balanced stats in the party. His physical strength
is matched by his magical knowledge. It's your call on whether you want him to
be a mage or a fighter.

STARTING STATS:
 HP   MP    ATTACK    ACCURACY    DEFENSE    EVASION    MAGIC DEFENSE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
 30   10      10       1-70%         2        1-14%      1-22%

 STRENGTH    STAMINA    SPIRIT    AGILITY    INTELLIGENCE    MAGIC
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
   10          10         10        10         10              5

 HANDS    KNIFE    SWORD    STAFF    AXE    SPEAR    BOW    SHIELD
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
   1        1        1        1       1       1       1       1

STARTING EQUIPMENT: Broadsword, Buckler, Leather Armor

STARTING ITEMS: None

STARTING SPELLS: None

I made Firion into a White Mage/Fighter type character.  I recommend taking off
the shield he starts with and putting another sword in his left hand.  Teaching
him Cure (among other White Magic) is a good move, depending on what you want
to do with Maria.  If you make Maria a Black Mage, make Firion a White Mage,
and vice-versa.  However, Maria's better suited for Black Magic, so I suggest
making Firion a White Mage.

As for weapons, if you find yourself no longer leveling up in Swords, stick a
Spear or an Axe on him instead. [WRAJSADCWKASSODN]

Teach Firion spells such as Protect, Shell and Blink.  Sap is a must-have for
long journeys.  You can give Guy all the status-inflicting spells. Note, if you
plan on making Maria your White Mage instead of Firion, just swap their Spell
recommendations.

: Maria     ^[CMRA]
------------

Starting out with a Bow and a head start in Intelligence, Maria is most
convinient as a Black Mage.  Keep her casting spells as much as possible to
skyrocket her spell abilities.

STARTING STATS:
 HP   MP    ATTACK    ACCURACY    DEFENSE    EVASION    MAGIC DEFENSE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------

 STRENGTH    STAMINA    SPIRIT    AGILITY    INTELLIGENCE    MAGIC
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
    5           5         10        15         15              5

 HANDS    KNIFE    SWORD    STAFF    AXE    SPEAR    BOW    SHIELD
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
   1        1        1        1       1       1       1       1

STARTING EQUIPMENT: Bow, Clothes

STARTING ITEMS: None

STARTING SPELLS: None

Screw the back line, put Maria up front so she can get hit and gain HP.
Seriously.  Having JUST Firion and Guy as your front line will allow their HP
to go up, but it'll also leave Maria to be hit by nothing but spells. Which is
okay, until you get spells that do ungodly amounts of damage later on in the
game.  Put her up front; none of the starting enemies can kill her that easily
anyways.

Teach Maria some elemential spells.  Fire, Blizzard, Thunder (you get a Fire
Tome later on in the game, but I suggest buying one right off) should be her
biggest three spells.  Teach ALL your Black Magic (found in the [MMGC] section)
to Maria to reap some sexy results.

Keep the Bow on Maria to begin with, so you can stick her in the back row and
still allow her to attack later on in the game.  Also, upping her Spear and/or
Unarmed skills is a good idea.

: Walkthrough - Travel Notes                      ^[WNOTE] :
------------                                    ------------

In case you're new to the Final Fantasy world, there are a few things you
should ALWAYS be aware of. As a matter of fact, I'm so confident in these notes,
I'm going to say they apply to just about every Final Fantasy game in existance.

1. SAVE!!!!! I cannot emphasize this enough. Save early, safe often, SAVE
    BEFORE ENTERING A BIG BATTLE. Through the process of saving, you'll save
    yourself a LOT of headache and a LOT of lost time. Whether you save at a
    Save Sphere (FFX) or at an Inn (FF1), you should save whenever you can.
    In Dawn of Souls, you can save wherever the hell you want, so don't worry
    about getting back to the Inn. Trust me on this, even the best FF players
    can mess up every once in a while and lose their entire party.

2. TALK TO EVERYONE!! They'll (usually) give you clues as to where you should
    go, who to talk to, etc. Plus, some of them even blurt out funny quotes,
    like the infamous "This guy are sick", or perhaps they'll provide clues to
    hidden treasures. Who knows? All you gotta do is talk to them.

3. LEVEL UP!! Unless you're doing a low-level game, you (probably) want to make
    your characters more powerful than the guys they fight. It's not smart to
    take your 55 HP characters into a battle against an enemy with 1,000,000 HP
    and an attack of 30,000, and it doesn't matter how high your evasion is.
    To do this, just fight the majority of your battles instead of running away
    all the time. If you find yourself getting your ass handed to you each and
    every battle, then yeah, you might want to backtrack and beat up your own
    characters if you're really getting your ass kicked.

4. CURE IS YOUR BEST FRIEND! Right next to Ultima, Knights of the Round, etc.
    More often than not, you'll end up using Cure more often than every other
    spell combined, unless you're doing a special game type, but that's totally
    besides the point. Buy your Cure spell(s) early. [YRTJEASECKXSXOVNNB]

= B1 =
Upon entering Semitt Falls, head west and make note of the blue block that sits
inconspicuously. You'll use it later when Josef enters the party. Continue on
west until the path splits. Head west and then north to pick up 200 Gil. Go
back and head south until the path splits to an east-west split. Head east and
pick up 400 Gil, then head all the way west to go downstairs.

= B2 =
Get out of the water and head north, and then a bit west to pick up some Eye
Drops. Head east from the Eye Drops (you'll have to backtrack a bit) and you'll
go through some water and pick up a Potion.  Go south and you'll be able to
either go back upstairs or head down a righthand path. Take the righthand path
and keep going straight. Pick up the Hi-Potion and stop at the four doors. The
left-most door leads downstairs. The other three lead into dead rooms that
contain nothing more except a lot of random battles. Stay away unless you want
to up your stats. [ORIJUAYCTKRSROSN]

= B3 =
You'll end up heading down a set of stairs before you turn up at the north-
eastern corner of B3. Go straight south and you can score 200 Gil. Go back
north and then west, ignoring the east door (it's a random battle room) and go
across the southern bridge for 250 Gil. You don't have to go farther than the
first bridge, as there's nothing below it anyways. Go back north and west until
you reach three doors, and you should take the left-most door once again to
continue on.

= B4 =
You'll come upon the prisoners that were taken from Salamand. Included in the
mix is Paul, the greatest thief in the world! Also included is Nelly, Josef's
missing daughter. Talking to Paul will allow the prisoners to escape, and open
up the way to the next floor down. Take the stairs at the eastern part of the
room to continue.

If you use the Teleport spell, you can go back to Salamand and everyone there
will be singing you exuberant praises. You'll also be able to talk to Josef,
who will finally open his damn pie hole and tell you more about the location of
Mythril, as well as give you an update about the Dreadnought.

= B5 =
Take the bridge directly north and pick up the Fire Tome. Then go back (don't
backtrack across the bridge) and head east across a small bridge and stop in
the crossroads. From there, go north and follow the path around until you find
a stairway going down.

entrance, using the left-hand door. If you do decide to go across the bridge,

Say, this path looks awfully familiar. Follow the stairs down twice, and then
across the water. Follow the stairs up twice, then down the next two sets of
stairs down. The next set of stairs up leads to a Silver Cuirass. Backtrack
down the stairs and across the water, then down the stairs. If you go west, you
will see a waterfall. If you remember the real waterfall, you remember the
spell merchant that's inside. Go talk to him.

= B4 =
Go north and slightly west to find a Hi-Potion. Keep going north until you hit
the wall, then head west to find a door. Enter it and get a Golden Shield, some
Eye Drops, and a Demon Spear. Head out the door, then go west to find an Aura
Tome. Go straight south and enter the door to continue.

= B5 =
Follow the path until you pick up an Ether. As far as I can tell, you can
completely skip the Roundworm battle and move on. However, since Ricard is far
more powerful than any of your current characters, I'd highly suggest talking
to the Roundworm and activating a battle.

: Soul of Rebirth - Machanon                      ^[MACH0] :

First things first: ARMOR. If you left ANY of your characters naked, now is the
time to put some decent armor on them. The weapons you found (or should've
found) in The Unknown Cave should work for now. The various portals around
Machanon lead to all the corners of ... whatever this palce is.

After you purchase the best armor you can afford, you still won't stand much of
a chance against the enemies here. Go back into The Unknown Cave and find some
weak enemies, then level up your characters until they're battle-ready. You'll
also want to convert all your fighters into hybrid fighters/mages, and turn
Minwu into an experienced fighter.

Here's what I had my guys level up in. Keep in mind, you're free to do as you
wish, but here's an outline if you wish to follow it. (btw, I love using the
original FF1 class names ^_^)

Minwu: Fighter/White Mage. Axe/Sword/Mace/Staff (take your pick) + Shield.

Scott: Fighter/Black Mage. Sword + Shield.

Josef: Monk/White Mage. Unarmed, no shield (you could put one on for extra
    defense if you like).

Ricard: Fighter/Black Mage. Lance/Sword + Shield

For White Magic spells, Cure is a must have, as is Life. I consider Blink,
Protect, Shell, and Esuna to also be must-haves. Holy is a great spell to own,
as is Teleport. Barrier isn't as useful, as it guards against special attacks
only, and Wall is just like Shell. Other spells that cause status effects, like
Silence, Fog, etc. aren't completely necessary, but feel free to add them at
your own leisure.

For Black Magic spells, I very highly recommend the four elemential spells:
Fire, Blizzard, Thunder and Scourge. Aura, Haste and Beserk are also high on
my ranking list. They amp up the attack of your party, which is useful even
against high-defense enemies. Drain and Osmose make your Black Mage a self-
sufficient fighter, as your BM can absorb HP and MP. Flare is a nice spell to
have, as long as it's at a higher level, and your INT is high. Death is okay,
but it doesn't hit against most bosses. All other spells can be added at your
own leisure.

As far as I can tell, the entire point of Soul of Rebirth is to level up your
characters so you can advance through the story. When you're good and ready,
you can go into the portal on the southeastern side of town and enter the
Chamber of the Soul


============================================================
: Soul of Rebirth - Chamber of the Seal           ^[CHAM0] :
------------                                    ------------

Upon entering the Chamber of the Seal, Minwu quickly realizes that this place
is where he gave his life so that Firion, Maria and Guy could obtain the Ultima
Tome. This time, there's no random encounters, and only five items to obtain.

When Minwu reaches the door, he'll break the seal again. Can you imagine Minwu?
KAAAAAAAAA-MEEEEEEEEE-HAAAAAAAAA-MEEEEEEEEE-HAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAA!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!
*ahem* Anyways, when Minwu breaks the seal again, go inside the door and obtain
the four orbs on the corners that contain boosts for your Strength, Agility,
Spirit, and Intelligence. Then go to the middle orb and talk to it to activate
one of the hardest battles in this game.

=======================================================

:-----------------------------------------------------:

Ultima WEAPON is easily the hardest boss in the entire game. And you know, he's
completely optional, since you don't require the Ultima Tome to beat the game,
it's just a nice perk.

Anyways, Ultima is almost completely invincible to physical attacks. And he's
also very resistant to all magical attacks. However, magic is the only way to
hurt it, so boost up your party with Blink and Protect, then start hammering
away at it with your strongest spells. If you can outlast Ultima WEAPON, the
Tome is yours for the taking. :)

different this time. Watch the cutscene, then get ready to rock!

The battle with the Emperor is much different this time. Although his stats are
very similar (save HP), the way you go about beating the Emperor is completely
different. You now (should) have 4 kickass weapons at your disposal. Use them
to wreak great havoc on the Emperor. Most of the Emperor's attacks target your
team as a whole. However, he can cast a Lv. 16 spell against a lone character,
so watch out. The Emperor can slap your characters (depending on your stats)
anywhere between 1000-2000 HP. Although high level spells work rather well, the
weapons you have work well if you have Beserk+Aura+Haste in place. One last
thing, the Emperor uses Dispel 16 every once in a while, making all the defense
you (should have) put up useless. However, Dispel DOES NOT WORK AGAINST Haste,
