A troll skald looks for something to flatten...
Skalds are midgard's music class. They are hybrid melee/support casters. A skald can train midgard's weapon lines, learn to parry, and be a potent boon to his groups with his battlesongs skills. A skald is not as durable as other tanks, but makes up for this with the fastest movements spells in the game, insta cast direct damage spells, and even insta cast crowd control abilities. Skalds are considered indespensible by midgard RvR groups.
