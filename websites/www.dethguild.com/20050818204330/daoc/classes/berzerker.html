A Berserker is a light tank - a melee class that trades heavy armor and shields for a fast, hard hitting attack with 2 weapons. Berserkers can enter a "frenzy" which will cause every one of their attacks to crit - however, they loose all ability to defend themselves while "berserking" so one must use great care when using this ability. "Zerkers" are strong RvR and PvE characters, and make great melee peels or main tanks.
