Rules:
1:
 The beta testers MUST abide by the rules of the blizzard TOS, included with the beta CD.
2: The beta testers MUST write an article describing:
a): Their overall impression of the beta - (Play balance, graphics, sounds, play control, fun factor).
b): Their favorite race.
c): Something that was annoying - if anything - (Bug, glitch, sound, ect). If you found nothing wrong, say so.

d): Capture 10 screen shots of epic battles, builds, and any other eye candy that you find cool.
e): Provide a minimum of 5 strategies or tactics you used frequently in your time playing.

Your articles are due the day after your resting period ends. Please submit the screen shots and article to jlprocto@hotmail.com. They will be poster here shortly there after.
3: The beta testers MUST send the beta through first class postage on the FINAL day of their testing schedule Packages postmarked after the tester's time has expired will result in the individual being forbidden from participating in future beta tests. Be respectful of the next guy on the list guys - please. Each testing period is designed to give you at least a Saturday and Sunday to play the game.
4: You MUST send all materials, (manuals, paperwork, CD's, ect), that your received in the beta test package to the next person on the list. The addresses and contact information for each person on the list will be provided in the great hunter's forum, which you should all have access to.
5: Once the beta test has concluded, the beta CD is to be returned to SilverDeth by 1st class mail, posted marked the last day of your testing Period.
