Here is a detailed blueprint of our Baldur's Gate Headquarters: Durlag's Tower. This ancient fortress will be the starting point for all our adventures on the BG server. Here you can find useful equipment, wages, as well as helpful NPC's to adventure with you.

Exterior View:

Durlags tower is featured on the "Tales From The Sword Coast" expansion CD. Thus, starting with this disk loaded will greatly speed the game set-up time.
From time to time a magical arms merchant will stop near our tower. He offers a plethora of magical items, from weapons, to potions, to powerful wands.
The area surrounding the castle is not safe, and large packs of gnolls often prowl about. Exploring alone is a bad idea.

Interior Blue-Prints:

Floor 1 - The main foyer.
Features: The First level of Durlag's tower features a large central meeting area, a wine stocked kitchen, and several lounges where one can escape from their partners for a bit. Most of the chests contain an unlimited supply of potions and scrolls, an invaluable commodity for your adventures ahead. Beware of a the chest in the north-east drawing room, it is used to store dangerous cursed magical items. Wages and provisions are distributed here, and the spacious hall is perfect for guild meetings.
Accessible Floors: Catacombs, Floor 2.
   

Floor 2 - The great hall.
Features: Besides the huge dining hall, this floor sports the master kitchen, which is comfortably provisioned with magical foods and the finest wines. (We even have rude maids -just like home). Several community chests are filled with equipment donated by other members, and two vacant bedrooms are open, granted on a first come, first serve basis.
Accessible Floors: Floor 1, Roof-top battlements.
   
Floor 3 - Hall of secrets.
Features: This is the tower's main library, laboratory, and chapel. Numerous magical scrolls are made available to spell castors, as well as scores of tomes containing virtually any game knowledge a player could ask for. Also, this floor houses the chapel, where an injured character can be healed or raised free of charge. The master bedroom is located here, and is occupied by SilverDeth
Accessible Floors: Floor 2, Floor 4.
   

Floor 4 - The hall of war.
Features: The hall of war contains the tower armory, filled with virtually every type of weapon or armor imaginable. All of a DETH Guild member's starting equipment is provided free of charge from this store. There is an open bedroom, awarded on a first come, first serve, basis.
Accessible Floors: Floor 3.
