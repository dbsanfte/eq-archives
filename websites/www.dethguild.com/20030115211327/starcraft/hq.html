Terrain: Space
Players: 2-5
Resources: Limited
Races: All
UMS Required: No
Tips For This Map:
The tips vary, depending on whether you are the defender or the aggressor. The aggressor VASTLY out numbers the defender, but the defender has the home court advantage. There are limited units, so it becomes a nasty fight of who has the best micro management skills. If the aggressor looses all his units, it is game over. If the defender's leader dies, he looses. Remember to repair damaged units with scvs, every unit needs to count, and terrans repair fast.
Map Info
Name: Deth Base Alpha (Interior)
Terrain: Installation
Players: 2
Resources: None
Races: Terran
UMS Required: Yes
Tips For This Map:
Remember to use your early tech advantage to buy upgrades. You're in the middle, so long term defense will be problematic, don't even try. On the bright side, the base is totally impervious to nukes/tanks, so ignore the terrans. Stay aggressive and try to wipe out whoever you can at the beginning. Be cautious of drops or heavy air attackers. You will want to expand into the burning wreckage of one of your opponent's bases as fast as possible, as your own minerals are very limited.

Tips For This Map:
While your tech may be SUPERIORITY protected, (barring a major guardian hit or corsairs), you must not loose your land foothold or you will loose in the end. You need the cash on the mainland to survive. Players: 2-5
Resources: Limited
Races: All
UMS Required: No
Tips For This Map:
Build several barracks and machine centers outside your base, and use the enemies high positions against them. Lift off buildings and float them until you get a few air units to spot for you. I have never been a real fan of money maps, but this is an interesting variant of the "classic" (rrrretch) BGH theme. Think of it as a cross between BGH and Lost Temple.
