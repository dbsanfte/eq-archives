New tactics are always welcome, and if they are good, they will be added. If you have a new tactic or two, e-mail them HERE.
The Zerg rely upon masses of cheap, expendable units to overwhelm their adversaries. While individually a zerg unit might be canon fodder, your ability to create waves of vicious killers makes the them a horribly effective force. When playing zerg, prepare to loose units en mass. But their cheap cost and fast build times makes this an acceptable battle strategy. Zerg are the best attackers, and no race can sustain a prolonged "pressure cooker" offensive like the zerg can. Fear the swarm.
The "rouge" of starcraft, humans are well rounded in all ways. They have a great combination of weak and powerful units, and possess the ability to counter any other race. On the down side, the human bases tend to be spread out over large areas, and are difficult to defend. Terrans are considered the masters of map control and counter offensives.
As far a units go protoss are almost everything you could want. They are fast, powerful, durable, and expensive. (Well, almost everything...). You can build 8 zerglings for the cost of two zealots, but odds are, those two zealots can TAKE the 8 zerglings. Although protoss units are spendy, their ability to consistently win against three to one odds more than makes up for this failing. Protoss players tend to be cautious and extremely tactical in the nature of their attacks, as they are unwilling to squander expensive units on futile attacks.
