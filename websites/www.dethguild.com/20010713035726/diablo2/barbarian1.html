Barbarian Overview:

The barbarian is the quintessential fighter. He is built to wade into the middle of raving packs of enemies, where he proceeds to rip them limb from limb. A "barb" is the master of the one hit kill, as once his combat mastery is combined with a high damage output weapon, he becomes capable of leveling almost any creature with a single blow. (Players included, thus making the barbarian the undisputed champion of mid-game duels once they have a high level jump attack and full weapon mastery). Once he reaches level 30, a Barbarian is able to handle packs as well, thanks to whirlwind, and he becomes a true menace on the field of battle. When playing the barbarian, one should have three goals - equipping a weapon with the highest possible damage, (weapon speed is NOT a priority), getting equipment with life/mana leech, and maxing each resistance. Skills like iron skin will optimize barbarian defense, so equipping the best armors should be secondary to a HUGELY damaging weapon. A simple 7% mana steal ring will allow a barbarian to set his special skills to left click, and his attacks will recharge his mana with every kill. Typically a "barb," (as they are called by b.net players), will smash most monsters in small groups or bosses with ease. However, the barb is not invincible - magical foes and extremely large packs tend to give him trouble, so he must beware, lest he find himself fried by a lightning enchanted creature, or felled by a mob of damned. Thus, a great deal of time should be spent tracking down items that give resistance bonuses, such as iratha's finery, (one of the few GOOD sets out there.......), or prismatic rings. (A prismatic ring of the vampire/locust is a barbarian's dream toy). As far as character point allocation, the early levels should be spent building strength as high as possible. Jumping strait to 75 strength is not a bad idea, as it will allow one equip almost any gear found in normal mode. After reaching the 75-100 range in strength, a good character point distribution is 2 to strength, 1 to dexterity, 2 to vitality. Energy is never a problem once a barb secures mana stealing equipment. This path can change however, depending on a character's weapon choice. If a barb is a sword master, he will need to add MUCH more to his dexterity if he is to meet the heinous requirements of the massive executioner's sword. A Mace master will need 169 strength to even pick up the crushing martel de fer, and the dexterity requirements on polearms are equally stringent. An axe master gets off by far the easiest, as the (relatively) low character point pre-recs on an ancient axe will allow him to add many more points to his vitality. (Axe master's with 1000 or more hit points are not uncommon among the level 75-80 crowd). So, pick a mastery, then build your character's attributes up to the required levels to use the most exceptional form of that weapon.

Barbarian Quote: "If you didn't kill it in one hit, you need a bigger axe."
