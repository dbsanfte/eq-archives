How does the game calculate my total damage?

The Game takes the base damage of your weapon and adds it to the sum of all increase damage percentages times that damage. If you use a weapon, then your strength will add a percentage damage equal to its value, i.e. 100 strength = +100% damage. If you use a Bow or CrossBow, then your dexterity will add a percentage damage equal to its value, i.e. 120 dexterity = +120% damage. (btw, 25% = .25)

Damage + Damage*(Percent+Percent+Percent...) = Total Damage


Example:

A Barbrian has a maul with 164 to 260 damage, and he has a level 20 Weapon Mastery(123%), level 20 WhirlWind(100%), and a 170 strength(170%).

Min = 164 + (164 * (1.23 + 1.00 + 1.70)) = 808.52 Damage
Max = 260 + (260 * (1.23 + 1.00 + 1.70)) = 1281.8 Damage
