Changes to 1.09 NOT listed in the Read Me Text

I have compiled a list of all the changes that took place in Diablo 2: Lord of Destruction, and Diablo 2. This data came from a variety of sources throughout the internet, and has for the first time been compiled here. Enjoy. - SilverDeth -

Data pulled from the .mpq of Diablo 2: Lord of Destruction:

Monster levels are capped at 65 in nightmare, and 75 in hell (Edit: maybe not).

There's a new cube recipe for runes.

Maul, Frenzy, Arctic Blast, and Double Swing were all changed.

Daggers, throwing weapons, and claws have had their damage improved (indirectly).

Some monsters (including Diablo!) had their nightmare / hell treasure drops boosted.

CHANGES IN ARMOR.TXT

The following items should now all be useable with Iron Golem:

Bracers(M), Light Gauntlets, Gaunlets(H), Heavy Bracers, Battle Gauntlets, War Gauntlets, Vambraces, Crusader Gauntlets, Ogre Gauntlets

The following items each went from a maximum of 2 sockets to a maximum of 3:

Tigulated Mail, Mesh Armor, Cuirass, Russet Armor, Sharktooth Armor

Bone Visage, Troll Nest, and Blade Barrier should now each be able to spawn with a max of three sockets.

The following paladin shields are now nameable:

Targe, Rondache, Heraldic Shield, Aerin Shield, Crown Shield

Several items had their cost reduced, including Circlet, Coronet, Tiara, Diadem, and Demonhide Gloves. Demonhide gloves also had their level requirements reduced.

The circlets all had their 'magic lvl' reduced, I'm not sure what this does.

CHANGES IN CUBEMAIN.TXT

This file changed dramatically. The way in which recipes are listed is now completely different.

There is a new family of recipes: 3 of any rune = 1 of the next higher rune. This only works for the first 9 runes, however.

The elite crafted item recipes have vanished from the file.

CHANGES IN DIFFICULTYLEVELS.TXT

Hireables now do less damage to act-end boss characters. In Normal, damage went from 90% to 50%, in nightmare, from 60% to 35%, and in hell, 30% to 25%.

CHANGES IN GEMS.TXT

All of the emeralds now do less poison damage per second, but they do it for a longer duration.

Chipped Emerald: Used to do 5.86 damage/sec for 1 sec, now 3.42 damage/sec for 3 sec.

Flawed Emerald: Used to do 11.72 damage/sec for 1 sec, now 5.08 damage/sec for 4 sec.

Emerald: Used to do 17.58 damage/sec for 1 sec, now 8.40 damage/sec for 5 sec.

Flawless Emerald: Used to do 23.44 damage/sec for 1 sec, now 10.06 damage/sec for 6 sec.

Perfect Emerald: Used to do 29.30 damage/sec for 1 sec, now 14.36 damage/sec for 7 sec.

Tal Rune: Used to do 19.53 damage/sec for 1 sec, now 15.04 damage/sec for 5 sec.

Jo Rune has been renamed to Jah Rune, now has effect of ignore-ac.

Cham Rune now has effect of freeze target.

CHANGES IN HIRELINGS.TXT

It looks like the experience system changed a lot for hirelings, as all of the Exp/Lvl values changed dramatically. The range used to be 100 - 960, now it ranges only from 100 - 130.

Also, the desert mercenaries now only have 2 skills each - jab and one aura. They apparently will never gain a second aura as they did in 1.08.

CHANGES IN ITEMRATIO.TXT

Lots of change happened in this file, but I have no idea how to interpret them. There are several new fields, including 'UniqueMin', 'RareMin', 'SetMin', and 'MagicMin'.

CHANGES IN ITEMSTATCOST.TXT

Lots of changes happened in this file, they appear mostly to reduce cost of several affixes.

CHANGES IN ITEMTYPES.TXT

Bows, Swords, Spears, Polearms, and Amazon Spears now can have up to 6 sockets.

Throwing potions are no longer repairable.

Orbs can be spawned non-magical, and can have up to two sockets (three in hell).

Necromancer Totems can now have up to two sockets (three in nightmare and hell).

Circlets can be spawned non-magical.

CHANGES IN LEVELS.TXT

Classic Diablo2 monster levels are returned to what they were in 1.06

Expansion monster levels are now capped differently - level 65 in nightmare, level 75 in hell.

Edit: I'm not sure how the monster levels in this file interact with the monster levels in Monstats.txt. Those haven't changed, and still go up to 100 for hell difficulty.

CHANGES IN MAGIXPREFIX.TXT AND MAGICSUFFIX.TXT

Elemental damage on charms should be less common now.

Many melee modifiers that were previously not allowed on orbs are also now prohibited on wands and/or staves as well.

Resists will no longer show up on orbs.

There are a number of new very,very low-level affixes. Well, they aren't exactly new, since they are just weaker duplicates of other affixes. For example, there's a new 'of strength' suffix that can appear on rings that adds +1 strength, that can appear on items starting at ilvl 4, wheras normally, you'd have to wait all the way until ilvl 5 for the +1-3 strength rings. Whee!

Self-repair items should be more common now.

Affixes that expand throwing item's stack size are now about twice as effective.

Self-replenishing throwing items should be more common now, and they are about twice as effective.

The 'gain X health / mana per level' items should be better now - 'of the Kraken' is 50% stronger, 'of Memory' is 50% better, and 'of the Elephant' is 100% better.

All of the poison mods have been changed dramatically. The length on all of them has been greatly lengthened, while the damage per second has been decreased on the low-end affixes, and increased on the high-level affixes.

The 'Of Blocking' and 'Of Deflecting' suffixes now have additional fields, which I assume is related to blockig speed.

CHANGES IN MISC.TXT

Rejuv and Full Rejuv potions will now be placed in your belt as you pick them up.

Malah and Jamella should now sell thawing potions.

CHANGES IN MISSLES.TXT

A lot of missles had the field 'pierce' change from 1 to 0. Not sure what this means, but it might be related to the bug where a certain uinque piercing bow worked with sorceress skills. Interestingly, Guided Arrow had 'pierce' go from 0 to 1. A new field has been added, as well, called 'Damagerate', which serves an unknown function.

CHANGES IN MONSTATS.TXT

It looks like some boss monsters in nightmare and hell were sometimes dropping the wrong treasure classes before this patch (they were dropping their normal difficulty loot).

They are: Andariel, Duriel, Radament, FlyingScimitar, Mephisto, Diablo, Summoner, Council Member, Griswold, TrappedSoul, The Feature Creep, Nihlathak, Baal Crab

The Siege Beast class of monsters now give experience, when before they did not.

The Overseer class of monsters now only give about half as much experience as before.

Radament and Griswold now may be considered Act-End bosses.

The druid's vines had the field 'Align' change from 0 to 1, which might be to fix some of the weirdness with monster shrines and such.

CHANGES IN NPC.TXT

Vendors will now pay up to 30000 in nightmare, and 35000 in hell.

CHANGES IN RUNES.TXT

The following runewords are activated in single player:

Ancient's Pledge, Black, Fury, Holy Thunder, Honor, King's Grace, Leaf, Lionheart, Lore, Malice, Melody, Memory, Nadir, Radiance, Rhyme, Silence, Smoke, Stealth, Steel, Strength, Venom, Wealth, White, and Zephyr.

CHANGES IN SKILLS.TXT

Poison Javelin, Plague Javelin, Lightning Fury, and Immolation Arrow all had their damage changed; now the base weapon base damage counts.

Fire Wall does less damage now, but only about 25% less. (The damage scaling went from 9/18/27 to 9/14/21).

Double Swing has a new parameter, which is labeled 'Attack Rate Bonus', and is set at 50.

Arctic Blast has increased chill duration per skill point past skill level 8. (15 duration / lvl, it used to be 10 / lvl).

Maul should now add 20% attack rating + 10% per level (it used to be 3% + 3% / level).

Hunger changed big time. It now steals a smaller percentage of life and mana (about one half to one third as much), but should do more damage, as it only has a 75% damage penalty (it used to be 90%)

Fury now deals a lot more damage, it now gives 100% damage bonus + 17% per level (it used to be 50% + 5% per level).

Changes to % chance to find magic item

Magic Find in single player v108 has never worked at all. So if you are playing off of the Realms, you'll notice it being much much better now.  On the realms, MF% has gone from full for everything to diminishing returns to find Rares/Sets/Uniques.  You can see exactly how much in the table below. Thanks to Jarulf for the stats.

Note that the maximum possible is a bit under 1300%, so the 1500% is just for comparison's sake.

The diminishing returns start after 100%, and really kick in past 200%.  The result of these now is that most characters with decent equipment will have about the same MF for Uniques.  200% isn't that hard to put together, and you get 171% MF for Uniques at that point, while you need to go all the way to 700% to increase just 100% more on Uniques.

This is an odd way to modify this property, since really going all out for the huge MF is sort of pointless now, or not well-rewarded.  It had been expected that MF would be lowered by a steady rate, so you'd still be rewarded as you went up and up, and it wouldn't be so easy to double or triple your total.

Explainations of Further 1.09 changes:

Unique Conversion Recipe: The new recipe will be something like the unique + a Rune around #20, and will re-create the Unique with the new v1.09 stats.  This is not in the game yet, but should be added in a server side patch on the Realms soon.  No info on when/if it will be enabled for single player, possibly in v1.10?

Ladder Reset: There has been no word from Blizzard about a ladder reset.  D2X just came out less than two months ago, and they've done D2 ladder resets about every six months, so there's no real reason to expect one.  Sorcs totally dominate the ladder now, and there are a ridiculous number of lvl 99 characters, so some people are asking for a reset.  However v1.09 didn't change anything in terms of the ladder.  The fastest ways to level are still 1) be a sorc.  2) party with a sorc, and there aren't any known changes to monster experience awards, or exp gain rates at very high levels.  So even if there were a ladder reset, they'd just look the same as they do now in a month.

Sorcs Nerfed: Not really.  Firewall damage is down slightly, but it's still enormous. Two second kills instead of 1.5 seconds. 

Memory Management: This is a listed change, but some reports are that it really helps a lot.  Depends on the system specs.

This will be of great use for people not on realms.  You can set the game to simulate 2-8 players, which ups monster hps 100% per extra character, and ups the items dropped as well as the experience awarded by .67% per additional player.

Poison Damage: Lots of changes to how poison damage works and it's much less damaging.  No point in carrying around 10 small poison charms anymore.  You want just a few high damage poison things like perfect gems, not a ton of little damage ones. Also reports that Venom and Poison Dagger are just pathetic now, actually lowering the damage your weapon alone would do. 

Simulate Extra Players: Something we didn't comment on on the readme page is this great change for play off the realms.

Typing "players X", where X is a number between the current number of players and the maximum (8), in the message box will now set the effective number of players in the game (in single player, open Battle.net and TCP/IP games only).

This means you can simulate from 2-8 players in your game, which will make it harder but give you much more experience and more item drops.  Each additional player adds 100% to the monster's hps, .67% to the experience awarded.  So simulate an 8 player game they'll have 8x normal hps and give 5.67x the normal experience.

Mercenaries

Life Regeneration: The Barbs regenerate hit points slower than before, but the others seem to be faster.  More or less players in a game doesn't seem to effect it much if at all.

Exp Gain: Everyone reports much faster leveling for Mercs now. They need more exp per level, but they gain a lo more per kill.

Shift + Potion: This works fine, even with the hotkeys you have for your belt, and should more than make up for the lower regeneration rates.  Try and get used to shift+1, or Shift+left click on potion, rather than running or teleporting back whenever they are near death.

Others: There are reports of -magical damage not working for mercs anymore.

Exp from Minion Kills: The Patch.txt says mercs will gain experience from kills by other minions, but we've had some reports that this isn't working.

Economy

Sell Prices: The 30k on Nightmare and 35k on Hell are indeed working.

Gold Sharing: Gold sharing for what you pick up off the ground (from monsters/chests) is still in, for parties.  However there is zero gold shared for items sold, so if you are used to making money just by being in a party and not going back to sell often, you are out of luck.  Also you'll want to be quick to grab any valuable stuff that drops, so you can sell your fair share.

Gold dropped by other characters, or from deaths, is still not shared when picked up.

Gambling: Mixed reports, no one has really gambled enough yet to say for sure if Rares are boosted as the patch.txt claims.  You would need to do literally thousands of trials to really be able to tell if there were more, and how many, rather than just knowing if you'd been lucky or not.  I did 33 amulets and got just 1 rare, for example.

Coronets were cheaper for my lvl 84, they used to be 175k or so, and were only 140k now.  There should be a number of other noticeable changes in gambling prices, due to changes in their base numbers.  All gambling prices per item jump up when your character reaches a Clvl to get the exceptional or elite version of that item (watch as you level and suddenly one type of item will increase a lot), so this might be a factor as well.

Circlets and/or Coronets are not offered every time, as had been considered, but some reports are that these items come up in the random items to gamble more often. This needs more testing though.

Repair Prices: Most weapons cost a lot less to repair.  People with Cruel weapons and other big damage ones are very happy.  Items with charges cost more to recharge, especially with useful high level skills like Lower Resistance or Teleport.

Monsters

Full Rejuv Drops: Bosses on hell drop a lot fewer full rejuvenation potions now.  They formerly dropped at least one every time, and often more.  The same goes for all types of Champions, and there seem to be fewer purples dropping in Nightmare also.

Nightmare: Nightmare is MUCH easier for physical damage characters, with the buggy added physical resistance removed.  My bowazon was able to fly through item runs in Nightmare using a 160 damage Shae(l) Grand Matron bow.  Stone Skin bosses went down in a few shots, SS Specter boss packs, and other things that would normally require a lot of shots with an elemental damage bow were seconds of work, far far easier than in the past.  All other monsters die more quickly too, including bosses, and all normal monsters that don't have any special resistance tags.  With a big damage weapon in a solo game, Nightmare plays almost like Normal now, with one or two shot kills on most things.

Characters with a decent damage weapon won't need to worry about having an elemental skill or weapon switch until Hell now, though there are still monsters that don't leech, and they have some physical resistance, just much less than before.

Super Unique Drops: All SuperUnique bosses seem to have a much improved drop now, with better odds of getting rare/set/unique.  These include the quest ones, such as Blood Raven, The Countess, Izual, Hephasto, Shenk, etc, as well as others you see every game in the same place, but don't have to be killed for some quest, such as Rakanishu, Treehead, Pindleskin, will drop better than normal random bosses.

Rune Drops:  The Countess does indeed drop a Rune when you first kill (completing the quest) her on each difficulty level.  She doesn't drop every time, but seems to have a decent chance of dropping a Rune, better than other monsters.  She's basically another Hellforge now, and you should be sure you are not in a party when you kill her, and that someone else doesn't kill her and tak your quest reward while you are elsewhere in the Act.

Most people report finding low level Runes from her most times.  El, Tir, Eld, Ort, Ral, etc, with one better one the first time. In four tries on Nightmare solo I saw Dol, El, nothing, and Eld.  One try in Hell with three people in the party she dropped and El and a Tir. 

Also just in general, more Runes seem to be dropping.  Just doing the Black Marsh and the tower on the way to the Countess, several Runes were spotted each time, including two Hel's inside the tower from monsters, and a Shae from a chest on tower level five.  This might just be luck though, the sample size isn't large enough to conclude anything. 

Items

New Cube Recipe: Three of the same Rune => 1 of the next higher quality.  This only works for 1-10, unfortunately.  So 3 El's in the cube => 1 Eld.  3 Eld => 1 Tir.  And so on, up to 3 Ort => 1 Thul.  This is as high as it goes, 3 Thul doesn't make an Amn, and nothing higher combines to make anything, nor are there any other combinations that work.  For now, anyway.

This is sort of neat, but not really of much use, since Runes 1-10 are all quite common in Normal, but if you really need a lot of runes, you could save up and keep upgrading to get more Orts if you were doing a lot of Blood Crafting, for instance. (Though now most Crafted recipes are kaput, for the time being.)

Keys in Cube: You can no longer store keys in your Cube and have them accessed automatically to open locked chests.  Most people never knew you could do this anyway.

Hover Box Appearance: The pop up box with the item description text in it is now smaller, with the wording somewhat rearranged.  This is purely a cosmetic change, but people seem to like it.

Socketed Added: The patch lists normal Circlets and Orbs, and yes, these are in and can be found with Sockets, have sockets added, and can probably be made into Runewords.  Another item not listed in the read me are the Necro totems, these can get sockets added now as well.

Retroactive Throwing Items Stacks: The patch.txt said that the larger stacks of throwing weapons wouldn't be retroactive, that you'd have to find new ones to get bigger stacks, but email reports are that this isn't true.  Check the stack size of any throwing weapons you have from before, and you might be pleasantly surprised.

Uniques: There are tons of them with changed stats, we'll have our Uniques section updated very soon with all of them.  One interesting thing, the Gull now gives 100% better +MF%, now that it's nerfed, of course. ;)

RuneWords: There are no new ones yet found on the Realms.  Blizzard has told us that there will be more added soon, server side, and to look for clues in upcoming "Magazines/Comic Books". Blizzard is doing a comic book on Diablo II, and that'll apparently have some clues to RuneWords.  Unknown about the magazines, probably some preview or feature in CGW or PCGamer or Gamestar.

Crafted Items: We've got lots of reports of them not working.  Caster Items and Blood items especially.  We'll be compiling the info and updating the appropriate section soon.

Skills

There are lots of changes to skills that people are reporting. Consider these as "maybe" until there is more confirmation, or you test it yourself.  

"Werebear seems to have been nerfed with the 1.09 patch. I lost around 400 hps with a 50% loss to life with max lycanthropy and werebear. Lycanthropy seems to still be adding the same % to life, but the werebear has dropped. It is only around 170% max life now."

Guided Arrow is reported to now Pierce sometimes.  This can be very powerful if it works, and it's not just a display bug.  Especially since the arrow would hit a target, Pierce, and reverse to hit it again most of the time.  So the OK or Imp or other single target you were aiming for would take multiple hits. We've not observed this in our testing though, so it seems unlikely.

Berserk on Barbarians now makes you lose defense for longer at slvl 1 it takes 2.3 sec in v108 and now it takes 2.7 at slvl 1. 

Find Item for Barbs when used on Bosses gives a chance for a full boss drop.  Reports of multiple items with it, including Rare/Set/Uniques are coming in frequently.

Diablo II Classic Changes

- Magic find is NOT nerfed in classic. With the new socket item cube recipe, 300% MF is possible.

- Selling prices have been raise to 35,000.

- Gambling odds hae been MUCH improved, however, exceptional items cannot be gambled.

- The physical resistance has been removed in hell mode - ww/multishot fun time again.

- Super Uniques now have drastically improved drops.

- Repair costs have been lowered drastically.

- Shield blocking is no longer nerfed.

- Monster level have been lowered to their post 1.07 levels.

- Most of the unique items have new stats.

- New cube recipe: 3 perfect skulls + 1 stone of jordan + rare item = adds a socket.
