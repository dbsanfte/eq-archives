Dark Age of Camelot class benchmarks and skill directories.

A healer is quite possibly the ultimate support class. Despite having no offensive capability, a healer is the cornerstone of any midgard groups - sporting three very potent group support spec lines. Pacification spells can debuff, root, stun, and mezz opponents. Mending is filled with life saving heals and resurrection spells. Augmentation spells grant the party movement and attack speed buffs, as well as numerous self only buffs. With chain armor, and many insta heal spells, a healer is indespensible to any group.
