Tactics: Possibly the ultimate necromancer killer, the Amazon's skills are wicked at handling anything he can throw. Slow missile renders Bone Spirit, the necromancer's big player v.s. player ability null, and his creatures will have an impossible time striking her with such high degrees of evade. Even bone wall will not save him from the amazon's lethal ranged attacks. Use your decoy and valkyrie as targets for bone spirirts while you close, as this is the most dangerous moment. Once you slow the necromancer's missile attacks, he is a sitting duck. Be careful on any approach, as often times packet loss will cause bone spirits to become invisible. As long as you keep moving however, these should still miss you.

VS

Tactics: Amazon v.s. paladin combat is difficult, but if played patiently and carefully, it CAN tip in the amazon's favor. Playing games with guided arrows and multi arrow will help a great deal against a paladin, but spear use is asking to eat a charge + concentrate combo. Accept that the amazon's melee skills are inferior to the combat abilities of the true fighters, and concentrate on her range game. A paladin will typically use the skill holy shield, which will boost his blocking percentage to 75%. They usually use the aura vigor, and then proceed to charge at you, depending on their shield to carry them through your arrow swarm. Once they close, you have deadly problems. If you stop to shoot, the paladin's charge will hit. Once you are on-screen with a paladin, you are forced into a game of running around in a panic, slowly watching your stamina drain towards the inevitable. Your one chance is to get him on the approach, otherwise, he will probally kill you. A decoy or your valkyrie might buy you enough time to let fly a few more guided arrows, some of which, (hopefully), might connect through that 75% block.

Tactics: This battle can be best won with guided arrows. A few quick shots will bring down a sorceress, and slow missile can turn frozen orb into a humorous spectical, to say the least. However, DO NOT let the sorceress close on you, which they will attempt to do. She will approach in a random, zig-zagging pattern of teleports, climaxing with her appearance right next to you. Here is where she casts three orbs, one to the north, one to the east, and one to the south, and that's where things get ugly. Even slowed, the orbs at this range are horribly leathal, and will make short work of a high dex amazon. Stack fast attack items, and try your hardest to keep her stun or block locked. Death's Gaurd is a no brainer here. You can't afford to get chilled at any point.

An amazon duelist needs to be a patient and tactical killer. She lacks the one strike kills of the other classes, but makes up for this by being extremely difficult to land a blow against, and guided arrow.. well, it's the best missile attack in the game, hands down. An amazon duelist needs to constantly keep on the move, and her evade skills will assure that even on the run many attacks, INCLUDING spell effects, will miss her. The only time an amazon needs to stop is to recast slow missile or briefly fire off another barrage of guided arrows. Using this running offensive, an amazon can wear an opponent down, while rarely ever taking a blow in return. The valkerie is a distraction tool, something to occupy your opponent for a few moments while you pepper him with a few more arrows. On key factor in dueling with an amazon is using the mini-map to target foes. Guided arrow can strike an opponent far off screen, and seek very rapidly - unless your opponent has fastest run walk boots, they WILL be hit - so keep them off screen and aim using the map. Fighting on screen with a sorceress is certain death, kill them before they get close enough to box u in with frozen orbs.
