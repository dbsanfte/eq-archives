Quake III is all about the toys. If you have the best toys, you get the frags. However, it is also about the right toys for the right situation. (It is amusing to see a fool cut loose with a BFG in a narrow corridor). Here is a list of the toys, and hints on where to use 'em.

Name:

 Gauntlet

Description:

 The only decent thing about the gauntlet is that it costs nothing to fire it. Beyond that, there is no reason in you would ever want to use this, besides humiliating a person. Its harder than hell to hit a moving target, and the damage, while decent, doesn't make up for this things all around CRAPPINESS.

Best When:

 Never ever ever used. If you are so crappy as to need this weapon often, you deserve to get fragged out of the gene pool.

Ammo:

 None
   

Name:

 Machine Gun

Description:

 Well, if your opponent is similarly armed, you got a chance with the machine gun. It kills relatively quick, and is pretty accurate. Really deadly if you got quad, but then again, what ISN'T deadly when you got the quad? Strafing attacks are pretty good with the machine gun.

Best When:

 You manage to close the gap on a grenade or rocket launcher packing spawn camper. You can do enough damage on the way in to make it suicidal for them to shoot.

Ammo:

   

Name:

 Shotgun

Description:

 The shotgun has been improved from the previous quake games. At point blank range, it can gibber an opponent in twain. It's performance drops WAAAY off at a distance however, so you want to be close. It is usually easy to find ammo, and almost every level has a shotgun or two.

Best When:

 You sneak up on a camper and place both barrels on the back of his head and then pull the trigger. Good for charging people with heavy splash weapons too.

Ammo:

   

Name:

 Rocket Launcher

Description:

 This honey packs a wallop. Its the weapon of choice among most quake players, firing fast and killing with a few hits. It can be your best buddy or worst nightmare however, so don't get yourself caught in a tight space against a shotgun or lightning gun armed foe.

Best When:

 You have some person pinned behind a wall and you can splash damage him to death. Aim at the floor near an opponent unless your are a REAL good shot, you'll be more consistent in the damage you do.

Ammo:

   

Name:

 Grenade Launcher

Description:

 This is an interesting weapon, it fires a pipe bomb that bounces about a bit, before exploding with roughly the same splash and "oompf" as a rocket launcher. Should your opponent contact one of the bombs it detonates automatically, making this a fun weapon to fire into a fracas or at spawn points. Don't let people close in or pin you against a wall however.

Best When:

 You see three or four people engaged in an all out brawl, and you begin strafing the area from above. It really complicates their lives, well, those that don't explode into bloody chunks anyway......

Ammo:

   

Name:

 Plasma Gun

Description:

 This beauty is basically A++ version of the machine gun. It's fast, lethal, does splash damage, and sucks ammo. Fire short bursts at a distance, or lay on the hog if you close.

Best When:

 You always have it. Its a great multi purpose weapon, with awesome damage, great firing speed, and enough splash damage to be useful, but not so much as to make you scared to lay into a guy point blank.

Ammo:

   

Name:

 Rail Gun

Description:

 In the hands of an expert the rail gun is the most deadly weapon in the game. It takes more than a little of practice to get the hang of it, but considering the EXTREME damage it does, and it's almost instantaneous impact,the rail gun is a toy you need.

Best When:

 You have a nice cozy place high up on a level where you can camp out and snipe at people. It reloads slow, VERY slow, so don't get cornered.

Ammo:

   

Name:

 Lightning Gun

Description:

 This hold-over from the previous quake games as the ultimate up close and personal weapon. In seconds, even a stacked opponent will be gibbers, as the electric lashes of this weapon will tear them apart. Its only good at point blank however, so get in on an opponent fast, and watch the ammo - this thing sucks it like nobody's business.

Best When:

 If you can trap a person in a narrow hallway or dead end, you will waist them in moments. This gun is not so good in the open, as it can be hard to track an opponent.

Ammo:

   

Name:

 BFG10K

Description:

 This thing is all you would expect from something called the "Big Fucking Gun 10,000." It has a massive splash radius, and now, (an improvement from the doom games), fires INSTANTLY, no charge up. Also, it uses ONE shot, instead of 40 charges. Those bastards at I.D. always make the damn thing hard to find though.

Best When:

 You get it.
