What is the Fastest Cast rate I can get?

The Game has 8 steps of cast rate. 0 is the slowest without any fast cast rate items on, and 7 is the fastest that you can cast. To find out what you cast rate is simply add up the items you have on, where:
1 = Fast Cast Rate
2 = Faster Cast Rate
3 = Fastest Cast Rate

*Note*
You'll have to have on at least 3 item with a fast cast rate on them to achieve the fastest possible. Those combinations are:
3-3-3, 3-3-2, 3-3-1, 3-2-2, 3-2-1.
Also note that to achieve the fastest possible cast rate you will need at least 1 item that grants Fastest Cast Rate.
