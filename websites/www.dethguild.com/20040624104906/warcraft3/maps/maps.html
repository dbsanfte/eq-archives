Big Snow Hunters
Tips for this Map:
A remake of the classic starcraft "Big game Hunters" this map features a nearly identical layout, and veins of nearly unlimited resources. Each starting local has access to a mercenary outpost, and the near-by expansions have goblin merchants and alchemists close at hand. Beware the tribe of yetis that have occupied the center expansion. They are very hungry after a long, hard winter. Each town starts with a custom hero and a small cache of upgrades for him.

Players: 1-8
Resources: Limited
Silver's Crucible
Tips for this Map:

After playing the map "The Crucible" I felt it was on the verge of greatness. In a very offensive based game, this map strikes me as one of the few with truly defensible starting positions. I added some nice touches in terms of custom units, custom treasure, and a small casche of loot at each base to help your fledgeling heroes. The fountains in the center of the map have been replaced by piles of treasure and a flight of not even remotely pleasant dragons.

Map Info
Terrain: Azeroth Summer
Players: 1
Resources: Limited
Silver's Survival
Tips for this Map:
How long can you survive 5 insane computers? You begin in a well fortified base, but you will need to quickly bolster your defenses, or you will be destroyed by wave after wave of ravaging computer controlled enemies. If you manage to exhausted the computers and claim victory, you can choose to continue your attacks against the neutral undead city to the west, or the hero populated island nation to the south. If you thought surviving 5 computers was difficult, your definition will be re-defined upon launching any assault against the two optional cities.

The Twin Cities
Tips for this Map:
The ultimate map. Postured for intense PvP warfare, beware the map is full of NPC's, treasure, traps, and hidden secrets. Featuring six rescuable unique NPC's, a rescuable city, over two dozen custom magical items, an interactive NPC city, an optional undead city to wage war against, as well as scores of hidden shrines and scenic locations, this map can literally take days to complete. Fear the wrath of "The Developers" inhabiting Mt. Arreat... Note: For those of you with low end systems, this map is quite resource intensive.
