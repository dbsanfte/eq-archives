Sorceress Overview:

The sorceress is a powerful spell castor, who's specialty is crowd control. She is the queen of spells that deal terrible damage to a HUGE area. But, she pays for this at the cost of combat skills and durability. The main concerns for a sorceress are mana bonus/regen gear, and fastest run/walk equipment. To stay alive, a sorceress has to totally fill an area with damaging attack magic, and this gets spendy in the mana dept. Acquiring anything that increases her rate of mana regen or total mana pool is a no brainer. Also, when the baddies DO survive you dropping everything up to, and including the kitchen sink on them, you need to be able to get out - fast. (Or become monster chow, your choice). Playing a sorceress is an exercise in patience. All character points should be saves for the lvl 30 skills and masteries. With a few exceptions, (such as static field and mana shield), all the low end skills are a total waist of points, and should only be bought as prerequisites. A series of staves should be utilized to "crutch" up her abilities till lvl 30 - when she gets to have fun. The lvl 30 spells, when pumped to high levels, and combined with masteries, are true room cleaners, and even hell/hell monsters will quickly drop due to the scope and power of her spells. The "sorci's" weakness will become apparent if the baddies ever GET to her however. A few quick blows will make her into maggot feed, and the sorceress is easily stun-locked. Point is, if they GET to you, you die. Another point to consider, pick two skill trees, not just a single one to specialize in. Diablo 2 is filled with elemental resistant creatures, and having a back-up skill is EXTREMELY valuable against a resistant monster. Ice mastery with a lightning back-up is VERY common among the higher level players. Character points, as should be obvious, need to be MASS dumped into mana, but early on, placing 50 or MORE in strength is an excellent idea. This will let her don some of the heavier armor, and this will be an asset early on, when a sorceress is at her weakest.
