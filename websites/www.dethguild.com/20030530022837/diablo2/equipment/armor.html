Armor:
An object of unparalleled might, weapons and armor of this quality drop with the frequency of meteorites. These are easily most potent items of the Diablo 2 universe. If you manage to acquire one, you have gained entrance into the upper ranks of the Diablo 2 elite.
"A" class items are both superbly powerful, and extremely useful. While not as rare as "S" class items, they are still among the most powerful items a character can wield. These items are very popular, and widely considered the best gear a player can hope to find.
An item from the "B" class is solid in both form and function, but is rather common. They might have one or two useless powers, but for the most part, these weapons will work quite successfully for any character well into hell mode. These items are usually fairly easy to acquire, making them popular choices for the common player.
A solid choice for normal or nightmare, "C" class items have at least one useful power, but usually posses some drawbacks that regulate them to secondary roles. Most "C" class items are very good in the right situation, but also, leave you wanting in many circumstances. You should try to replace "C" class weapons after nightmare difficulty.
"D" class items represent most of the unique gear of the Diablo 2 universe. They are strictly the venue of the very low level or very poor character. These item feature few, if any useful powers, and are hindered by level requirements that make them trail behind even low powered rare equipment.
Weapons and armor from this selection is almost categorically garbage. No character would ever use these weapons unless presented with absolutely no other choice. Your better off dead than wearing these stinky pieces of vomit, and if using them, you will likely find yourself exactly that. Newbies are likey to complain if you try to GIVE them this gear.
