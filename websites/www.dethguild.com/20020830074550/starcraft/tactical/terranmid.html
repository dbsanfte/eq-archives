It is in the mid range that the humans are probably at their most lethal. They can mount a fast, well balanced attack, which is hard for any race to fight off. It is here that terrans win battles. A terran mid-tec offensive is fast, relatively cheap, and well balanced.

Tactic#1: "LET'S DISPENSE SOME INDISCRIMINATE JUSTICE"
Submitted By: -=ZEALOT=-

1: At this point in the tec-tree realize one thing as a human: The machine center is your friend. Begin ordering all your SCVs to harvest. Do not begin construction on another SCV. Save the 50 mineral at this point.

2: As soon as you get 150 mineral, order one of your SCV's to build a barracks. By the time this is finished, you will have another 150. Build a bunker right next to your minerals. Make a marine in the barracks. This will hold off any opponents rushes.

3: Build four additional SCV's and three marines. Build a refinery and a machine center. As soon as it is finished, construct the add on. As the funds become available, make another machine center with add on. Place the three new marines in the bunker. Finally, build a supply depot or two and an armory.

4: Make 8 siege tanks and 8 goliaths. Research siege mode, and upgrade your vehicle weapons. Build three more supply depots and an academy. Construct the comsat station add-on for your command center and four more SCV's.

5: After you have located the enemy with your comsat station, advance your goliaths and siege tanks on their position. Move three siege tanks up, with goliath cover. Activate siege mode. Now, advance the other three tanks in front of the three in siege mode. The rear tanks will cover the front two, while the goliaths murder any air unit that flies by. Repair damage with SCVs as needed.

6: Continue "leapfrogging" your tanks. As the opposition's units try to attack your advance force, the group covering your lead will drop the hammer. Air attacks will not be an issue, as even protoss scouts will have problems against the goliaths, especially with SCV's constantly repairing them. In the mean time, you should be well on your way to making air units. Wraiths are especially helpful at this point.

7: With the tanks, blasting their defenses apart, choke points will be no problem. In siege mode, they out-range every ground unit in the game, and even reavers are hard pressed to beat this formation. In no time, the enemy will be pulverized. Ahhh, the sound those big guns make is sooooo nice. Coupled with the chorus of your enemies screams, it makes for a great opera.

Tactic#2: "Golaith Swarm"
Submitted By: WhiteDeth

The goliath is a well-rounded unit that is deadly against ground and air units and if they are in large herds, can take out even a Battlecruiser with ease. As with any "swarm" tactic, you need to allocate an SCV to all mineral fields in your base and 3 SCV's to each base and have a stable defensive network of bunkers and missile turrets.  Build 1 or 2 other mining bases to meet the expense of the war effort.   When you can afford, build 3 SCV's and have 2 build factories until you have at least 6 and have the other build 2 armories and then help the other 2 SCV's build factories.  The armories will allow Goliath to be available and also use them to upgrade land weapons & armor to level 3.  It may not seem significant, but it really does help.  When you are done building factories set a common rally point for the factories and begin building Goliaths.  When you think you have enough, you can send them in to the enemy base.  A ComSat station is nice to use to detect ghosts or wraith that are cloaked.  If the enemy defenses are too strong, use a nuke to soften them up.

(*SilverDeth's Note: I have found two groups of 12 Golaith's are sufficient. They get stuck easy so be careful!)
