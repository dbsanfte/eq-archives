Ok, we'll give you instructions that even a dumb whirlwind barbarian could understand. That set aside, if you will stop scratching your head with that 323 lance, and come over here and listen, we can explain how to play the game. Would be a darn (tarn?) shame if you got chucked out for breaking a rule. So read up, twice if you need to get it through your thick, meaty skull. You will be expected to know the rules - ignorant people get the boot right along with deliberate cheaters.

Introduction: How good of a Diablo 2 player are you? What determines this - How well you can kill monsters? How well you can kill players? How well you can forage? Tactics? Character build? The purpose of this tournament is to test each of these areas. All the skills that make Diablo 2 a great game will be tested to the limits. Your skill, build, tactics, and luck will all be tested in this event.
1): All players MUST be character lvl 60 or higher, any class, any build.
2): Players will be stripped totally of all their usual gear, jewelry, scroll books, and gold. They are allowed to bring the following items, depending on class:
A): *Paladin A: Non-magical exceptional weapon + exceptional shield.
B): Paladin B: 2H non-magical exceptionalweapon.
C): Necromancer: None.
D): Necromancer B: Non-magical exceptional dagger.
E): Sorceress: None.
F): Barbarian A: Non-magical exceptional weapon + exceptional shield + Cathen’s Seal.
G): Barbarian B: Non-magical exceptional 2 H weapon + Cathen’s Seal.
H): Barbarian C: Non-magical exceptional throwing weapon (2 Stacks).
I): Amazon A: Non-magical exceptional bow/x-bow + 1 stack of arrows/bolts.
J): Amazon B: Non-magical exceptional throwing weapon (2 Stacks).
K): Amazon C: Non-magical exceptional 2 H weapon + Cathen’s Seal.
L): Amazon D: Non-magical exceptional weapon + exceptional shield + Cathen’s Seal.
*Paladin scepters with any bonus skills will not be allowed.
3): All participants will enable loot to all other contestants. Not only with this discourage cheating, but the victor of any 1:1 battle has the right to the loser's possessions. When a contestant dies, he is required to remain in the game until the tourney ends. Eliminated contestants will help the line judge watch town way-points and exits.
4): The six contestants will all precede to ACT 3 in the hell mode of the appropriate game. They will wait in the Kurast Docks while all contestants are tested and viewed for illegal gear and assigned a way point to start from. Travincal and the Durance of Hate way-point will not be in the draw. One contestant will exit from the Kurast Docks via the town exit.
5): You may fill your character's 4 belt slots with any combinations of the following items prior to the game:
A): Super Healing Potions
B): Super Mana Potions
C): Full Rejuvenation Potions
D): Scrolls of Identify
E): Antidote Potions
F): Stamina Potions
G): Thawing Potions
6): Once the characters have proven their legitimacy, the way-points are drawn randomly out of a pool, and assigned to the six contestants. Once out in the field, the objective is to be the last player left alive. Potion use, traps set at stairs, monster kills, player killing, whatever gimmicks imaginable to kill one's adversaries is not only legal, but encouraged. As long as a contestant does NOT use a way-point after the initial drop, town portal, leave the game, ally, or try to return to a town, anything goes. The last person alive at the end of the tourney, along with the last two to perish, proceed to the next round.
7): The players have 4 hours to eliminate each other through any means at their disposal. If more than one person is left after the time limit has lapsed, the pot is forfeit, (to be added to the next event's pot), and all remaining contestants are disqualified.
8): Loosing your Internet connection is the same as being eliminated - you will be allowed to rejoin the game, but you cannot reenter the competition. Lag deaths, while unfortunate, as ALSO the same as dying to a competitor or monster. Maintaining a stable Internet connection, and a computer with sufficient RAM, video card, and processing speed maintain a decent playing environment is YOUR responsibility.
9): Team play is allowed if agreed upon WELL BEFORE tournament time. (Must be at least 2 days prior, and it all enrolled contestants MUST be in consensus on this issue). Otherwise, all whispers, team hunting, or alliances during the competition are forbidden. Anyone caught double teaming, whispering, or in any way helping each other in a non-team game will be ejected by the line judge, (along with those he was helping), and their admission fee forfeit. Throwing the match or splitting winnings as reward FOR throwing a match will result in being barred from further tournament play by i.p. Address.
10): Horodric cubes must be emptied prior to testing. They will be dropped on the ground, and then reissued to the contestants shortly before the contest begins. Any attempt to smuggle items in your horodric cube will result in expulsion and forfeiture of your entry fee.
11): The bracket shows your standing on the DethBash ladder. As the players fall, the weak will be culled from the skilled, and we will see how smart you are, the quality of your tactics, your player vs. player moves, and your character design talent.
12): Failure to show up at the pre-scheduled time will result in expulsion from the tourney. Acts of God, natural disasters, family problems, childbirth, or personal death, will NOT be grounds to avoid expulsion. Show up, or get the axe.
13): Returning to town, for ANY reason, is forbidden, as is the use of town portal scrolls and way points after the initial teleport. (The line judge will be randomly cycling through the way-points, and anyone caught cheating will be disqualified without remission of their admission fee).
14): Server Shutdowns will result in a rematch of the remaining contestants. All participants will be given exactly 1 hour to re-equip themselves before the tourney restarts. Players that have been eliminated prior to the server close may reenter the game, but may not participate. All characters will be re-screened before the match starts.
Admission Fee: Admission fees will be paid on game day prior to entering the gaming field. Failure to pay at this time will result in expulsion from the match. Credit is NOT extended to anyone, nor are other items that are "worth" a stone. Please have the ring in the possession of the character that intends to participate.
1): All players wishing to participate in the tourney must submit an entrance fee. This will take the form of a single Stone of Jordan. The winner of the tournament will be awarded most of this "pot" as part of the grand prize.
