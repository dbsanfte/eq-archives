Dual claw non-elemental melee assassin
By: SilverDeth

Right now, I am going to challenge the concept of the shield + claw elemental damage assassin. It just does not strike me as.. well, pretty. And I am going to TOTALLY dispel the myth that dual claw assassins are vastly more powerful, because they are not. The build I have worked out compares favorably to the current shield + phoenix strike + dragon tail assassins running rampant all over act 5. Melee damage is still wonderfully viable, and an assassin is a formidable killing machine, comparable to any other melee class in terms of durability and killing power.


Planning.

The most important element to a melee assassin is a planned and skillful approach to your entire character. From build to skill points to charms, every point needs a specific focus, and to achieve success, you will need to follow your plans to the letter. Plan out your character on a piece of paper before hand, and stick to the recipe. Here’s a sample recipe, which is realistically possible for any player that invests a moderate amount of play time:

Headgear:
Rare +2 assassin helm, Tal Rasha's Death Mask, Vampire Gaze, Pretty Much anything. The nice toys are nice, but you can pretty much slap on whatever you want.

Amulet:
Mana leech. For cheap, you can snagg a blue res all + mana leech amulet. The rich can shoot for a pre 1.08 prismatic dual leech. Just be sure to get a good share of elemental resistance and some mana leech. 4% works fine, assassin skills are cheap.

Rings:
Life steal and elemental resistance. Blues will work just fine for the poor player, exotic people can shoot for those wicked dual leech prismatic rares. But a simple pre 1.08 prismatic 5% life steal or 40% + single res ring will work wonders.

Belt:
The belt is another freebie. Look for a fast hit recovery, 50 life with one or two good resistances. A good idea is to spend your free money gambling belts. Life and elemental res a must. You can gamble a good one eventually, so keep throwing down the cash on plated belts.

Boots:
Hands down natalya's soul. Fast running, and some good resistances, and they trade cheap. Those of you with a few stones to burn can shoot for something more exotic - like dual boots with 2 high elemental resistances and life. In a pinch, tearhanches work well too.

Armor:
Lionheart, is in my mind, the best armor for this character. Resistance, life, + to attributes, and enhanced damage. I challenge you to find a better rare. If your into min max, hunt down a 3 socket barlrog skin or other light elite armor, and socket it, but pretty much any exceptional or elite suit with 3 sockets will work just fine. Hel, Lum, and Fal trade pretty cheaply, and you will probably find a few of them on the long road ahead of your assassin.

Weapons Set #1:
2 Bartucs talons. You cannot compromise on these. They inflict serious damage, and have a great bonus to skills and attributes. Additionally, they are exceptional items - meaning they are WORLDS more common than elites with comparable damage. Nightmare mephesto can drop these puppies, and they trade cheaply in the trading rooms. Socket them with a sheal if your a cheapskate, or a wicked IAS jewel. If you need to make some capital for them, use gems to re-cycle your small charms. You'll eventually nail a 6-7% small mf chams, which will easily trade for one or both talons. Don't let people sweet talk you - they are QUITE common.

Weapons Set #2:
I prefer 2 venom runic talons I docketed, but the runes involved there can get spend. (Mal). Just look for any arching of anthrax, or similar high end elemental damage weapon. Elite or exceptional status takes second stage to the raw elemental damage the weapon provides. Rich people can afford to socket with extreme elemental damage jewels. But not required providing your getting 200+ elemental damage out of your back up claws. With your charms, you'll be doing over 1k mixed elemental damage, which is enough to mince death maulers in 1-3 shots.

Gloves:
 I like + to martial arts skills with mana leech and 10-20% ias. But your pretty open here to use whatever you want. IAS or skills. You might get lucky with a good blood craft, gem re-roll imbue, drop or trade. You have plenty of easy options open, even if poor, cheap, or both.

Charms:
Only small charms here. Ignore bigger ones - the only really worthwhile grand charms are + to skills, which will not play a huge role in your characters effectiveness. Save 12 slots of inventory space for 12 small charms. Look for elemental damage on them. (Poison damage works as well). Stockpile those that inflict elemental damage, and discard, (or trade), the rest. IF your having difficulty finding those arching small charms of anthrax, you can stick 3 perfect gems in the cube with a stinky small charm and re-roll it's attributes. Repeat until you get 12 really good elemental or poison damage charms. 1-27 lit and 100 poison damage charms add up FAST. Cheap - and they are critical to your success.

Merc:
Hands down an act 2 nightmare Holy Freeze mercenary. These guys are incredible at debilitating your opponents. They are simply SO good that I am surprised blizzard allowed a skill as crazy powerful as holy freeze to exist on a merc. For gear, get them any old armor with tons of poison, life, and lightning resistance. Crown them with a 3 socket helm, (with 3 perfect rubies), and do whatever you have to get the unique bill. These trade cheaply for now, (because people have not realized how powerful they are in the hands of an act 2 merc). This item casts weaken, which combined with JAB ASSURES that any pack . you engage will not only be frozen by the merc's aura, but weakened by the curse. Might, extra strong, extra fast are nothing when you use this set up. Get that bill. Trust me.

Character Points:
Dexterity: 113
Strength: 113
Vitality: EVERYTHING ELSE
Energy: Base

The strength and dex will allow you to use a few elites should you run across anything better than Bartuc's Talons, but more importantly, raise your melee damage and raise your chance to hit. This will also get you into a few of the elite armors (with some nudging with +30 to strength and +30 to dex from your dual Bartuc's talons of course). Everything else gets stuck in vitality. 1,300 hit points is not uncommon with this build, even higher if you can afford better gear. You need to get over 1K life, which will put you out of danger of 1 hit kills from fanatic enhanced monsters. Once your over 1,000, you can play the leech "juggle" game with your red orb, and rarely ever have to touch a potion.

Skill Points:
(Note with Bartuc's Talons you will have a minimum of + 6 to your martial arts skills, the lists below do NOT account for this.)

Tiger Strike: Level 20
Dragon Claw: Level 20
Dragon Flight: Level 1
Cobra Strike: Level 1
Fists of Flame: Level 1
Claw Mastery: Level 20
Claw Block: Level 9
Burst of Speed: Level 9
Fade: Level 1
Shadow Warrior: Level 1
Psychic Hammer: Level 1
Cloak of Shadows: Level 1
Mind blast: Level 10
Shadow Master: Level 10

(Anything after these pre-recs should be dumped into an elemental attack of choice for added physical immunity squashing power.)

As you can see, tiger strike and dragon claw are your primary attacks. The damage you start to do with Bartuc's + claw mastery + tiger strike + dex + strength + dragon claw gets OBCENE fast. Tag on another thousand in elemental damage small charms, and your now doing damage that would make a barbarian blush with envy. Burst of speed will keep you slogging FAST with your already very fast attack speed claws, whish you juiced up MORE with a sheal rune. Even in 8 player games, your non-charge up damage will be hovering around 3,000. As high as 5,400 with a 3 charge tiger strike.

Execution:

Now that you have carefully and exactingly planned out your build, you need to learn how to play with her. You will have 45+ resist all in hell mode, excellent leech, (which you can hyper augment with cobra strike if you must), a good deal of life, and a kick but mercenary, with some real staying power. Right off, don't be timid. You can play much like a frenzy barb. Dragon flight into a pack of enemies, and start swinging. Tiger, tiger, tiger, claw - bang one dead. Click fest heaven. She can kill as fast as you can click. If someone walks buy an does not mistake your rapid fire mouse clicking for an epileptic seizure, your not clicking as fast as she can swing. The enemies are all at 53% slower (courtesy of holy freeze), and their damage is drastically cut, (thanks to that godly weaken enhanced bill your got for a steal). And lets not forget the havoc your shadow master is playing with mind blast. (They seem to do this ALOT). And your in the middle of it all. Three sturdy targets are reducing the number of hits individuals are taking, and life regen/leech will keep your mob running unscathed. In the middle of it all is you, shredding monsters left and right, with one or two clicks. Typically, I totally ignore using tiger strike if there are less than 4 people in the game, as dragon claw will shred most creatures on one blow without ANY use of charge ups. It's WILD how well this works.

Expanded Strats: There are times you need to shake it up. Fanaticism + extra strong + cursed bosses. These guys can finish you off fast, unless you turn their powers against them. Mind blast the hell out of them. Convert them all. Auras are sticky, and the boss pack will stay "fanaticized" while ripping a serious chunk out of the life of the unlucky boss sitting in the middle. Keep this up, it's not hard with a few blue pots and some help from your master. Once they get pealed down a bit, you can point black melee the boss. Your dragon claw with 12% life steal will refill your life orb with every strike, so as long as the boss cannot kill you in 1 blow, you can "click and splat" him to death. Simply avoid bosses like this completely if cold enchanted, extra fast, or stone skin are also on this list of attributes. (Those guys, are, for most practical purposes, unkillable to all but parties - large, powerful parties - or a single sorceress and some advantageous terrain).

Multi LEB: Well, if you don't care about your merc buying it, and your lit res is decent, simply cast fade, point dragon claw, click, and hold. If you stay on the top left side, you can avoid most "cotton balls of death", and your critical damage will leech back whatever pain the boss can put out. If this creature is stone skinned or conviction enhanced, this also becomes one of those "Only an insanely high level power group of 5 or 6 people would be interested in screwing with this thing," type situation. And believe me, many, many, many of them would DIE in the attempt. If it's an oblivion knight on TOP of those mods, just forget it. Nothing can take that. (Really sucks when it's Lord De Seis).

Physically Immune: Yuck. You will hate these things. I mean, when your used to killing a monster in 1 or 2 dragon claws, spending the time to charge up elemental attack,, unleashing the shot, and repeating once or twice more, your kill rate really starts to drop. The better your backup elemental claws and charms are, the less of an issue this is of course). A back-up elemental skill will go a LONG way towards hastening the death of immunes as well. The fire and lightning claw skills are amazingly effective, even at low levels, and allow you some elemental punch without having to invest heavily in phoenix strike, which requires a LARGE skill point investment to be made effective. Stick to blades of ice, claws of thunder, or fists of flame. With 2-5 skill points, and your +6 thru +8 to martial arts skills, you will have a handy weapon for dispatching death maulers, swarms, specters, and Stygian Furies.

Staying alive: There will be times that you are mobbed and totally outgunned. This is where dragon flight comes in. Use it to zip to the outside of the pack, and thus, freedom. Your merc and master will follow, so you can use this skill to re-position them around you to fade the heat. Also a must for taking out unravelers, who will otherwise mire you down in herds of burning dead.

Super Uniques: Think walking full rejuvenation potion. Click and hold. The only thing you need to worry about is a weapon breaking. Diablo's blood lightning, Dureal's Charge, Mephesto's "frozen orb," and Baal's ubercheeze "super inferno" are not issues point blank - they either don't use them, or in the case of Diablo and Baal, the attack never hit at range 1. Hephasto is the only boss to be careful of, as he has a tendency to be surrounded by Abyss Knights, whose elemental projectiles will end you fast while convicted.

Leveling:

Well, there is the tried and true power bloody hills run. You can leech of sorceresses for million and millions of experience points in moments. If under level 70, you will go up 2 or three levels every game. (Which last an average of 10 min). If you want to solo 8, the world stone keep, Nithlak's temple, ancient’s way are all great places where you will be harassed by a minimum of physical immunes. (You will level slightly faster solo, but only if your 100% by the book. If you on the short end in any of the suggested areas, your exp per minute ratio will be better in the company of blood runners).

Have fun - the dual claw assassin is both versatile and powerful.
