Sabranic's thane fixes: Final Post

I’d like to start of with a quick thanks to Sheena, Pakka, and Aulrich, for all of the hard work, toil, and outright frustration they had endured trying to fix the thane class. Thanks, you all shot your hearts out for the thane class, and while not much has actually changed for thanes, nobody can dare say it was for lack of trying on the part of our great team leads.

This will be my last report, as I am no longer able to continue to play my thanes, pay mythic, and at the same time, maintain my sanity. At the end of this week, I will be deleting the number 1 and number 20 thanes on the Galahad server. (One of the thanes is currently not listed on the herald due to account expiration, Kancer is his handle, so friends of Negativ need not worry, Nny is in no danger of deletion nor has Negativ quit). Some will be sad, and I am sure many will cheer, as I have been vocal, harsh, and sometimes dogmatic, in my opinions and views. But I have never wanted anything more than to see the thane class improve in a way that would allow thanes to be a desirable component of a successful RvR group, while not leading to the creation of Savage 3.0.

My reason for leaving the game, these message boards, and destroying the highest rank Galahad thane is completely due to the feedback our team lead Aulrich received from the TL Coordinator, Mackey, formerly the Druid Lenslar. His feedback was contradictory, condescending, and most importantly, not possible under the current game mechanics. Mackey’s suggestions for a thane’s group role best illustrates these three issues in my opinion. In light of the fact that Mackey will be filtering and acting as a roadblock to any potential thane improvements, I have given up all hope that this class will be substantially re-evaluated into a strong contributor in RvR groups.

That being said, I am thankful that Mythic has finally publicly stated a group role for the thane, and that the class is not slated to be expanded or improved upon, and is working as intended. We have asked for years that our intended role and functionality be explained to us. And for that, I will give thanks where thanks are due, even if I happened to hate what I have been told, I did ask for it, and I won’t complain about getting what I asked for.

On that same note, I have no faith or hope that the thane class will ever be adjusted in such as way as to be able to successfully carry out the role stated by the TL Coordinator. Issues with crowd control, shield guarding, spell resists, immunity timers, the speed at which opponents close into combat, the effectiveness of /assist trains, as well as the thane’s inability to deal “ranged damage” while maintaining guard range on a kiting support class/caster, have remained undressed, and will remain undressed for the foreseeable future. Other issues such as a lack of evade and broken RA skills that have remained undressed have plunged overall thane moral further into the proverbial ceptic tank.

All of these problems are compounded by the TL coordinator’s unwillingness to accept that thanes have serious issues, and his obstinacy in passing the information collected by our team lead to the developers. It is my opinion that the thane is a crippled class, that under performs in every aspect of realm vs. realm combat in relation to it’s peers in other realms, as well as it’s fellow mids. It is ill suited to it’s intended role, and other midgard classes will out perform the thane this role, due to the issues I have addressed earlier.

So, my diatribes, thanks-you's, and general paddling aside, here is what I feel, would make the thane a strong competitive, realm vs. realm class, who will be able to function in it’s intended role as a support class/caster guardian:

- Issue 1: The ability to guard a caster or support class from an assist train.

1: Access to Large shield – the thane needs to be given access to a large shield, as it has an increased chance to block multiple opponents. This will help a thane better protect his charge, deal more damage with slam attacks, and place him on par with his Albion hybrid counterpart, the reaver, who also has access to large shields.

2: Advanced Guard – The thane needs access to an advanced form of guard, with an enhanced range. Latency, reaction time, and crowd control make it extremely difficult for a thane to maintain guard range. Additionally, the necessity of support classes/casters to kite to avoid /assist trains make it very difficult (nearly impossible) for a thane to both maintain guard range AND be a contributor in terms of castable ranged damage. I propose thanes be given an advanced guard skill at level 40 or a shield spec of 30, which doubles or even triples the range of his ability to protect another person.

3: Advanced Intercept - The thane needs the ability to intercept multiple attacks against a protected caster/support class. Since their role has been defined as a protector, please grant them an advanced form of intercept, which allows them to continue to intercept attacks against a protected target until the thane is dead or cancels the intercept. The same armor factor and absorption penalties that apply to intercept in it’s current implementation should remain in place. This will allow the thane to contribute in a meaningful way to the survival of his charge, and to be superior to a warrior in this regard since it is the thane’s intended role.

- Issue 2: Crowd control and the thane.

1: Mezzmerization reduction buff - The thane class is very susceptible to crowd control skills, for four reasons:

A: A general lack of spirit, body, and energy buffs available to mids. (This is a realm wide issue)

B: Expensive RA skills which limit investments in skills like “the empty mind” and “avoidance of magic.

C: Lack of access to determination.

D: Difficulty in spell crafting the thane’s many attributes and skills while maintaining high resists. (This is a general hybrid issue).

Therefore, the thane class would like access to a self buff that reduces the duration of mezzmerization spells cast upon the thane, which prevent him from going about his duties as a caster/support class guardian as efficiently as a warrior could. Without this crucial ability, the thane will be cripple by crowd control and will be unable to perform his intended role as well as a high level determination warrior could. We ask that the sorcerer self buff be used as a model.

2: Access to a reduced cost purge – The thane class would like access to a reduced cost purge, to help them perform their intended group role of guarding and protecting casters/support classes.

- Issue 3: Giving and taking.

1: 2.5 specialization points for the thane class - There are three aspects to the thane class. His defense, in the forms of shield, and parry. His spell casting, which effects the potency of his self buffs, the damage, and resist rates of his magical attacks. And finally, his prowess with a melee weapon. Every thane, will have to pick one of these attributes to sacrifice.

Due to limited specilization points, a thane player will always have to sacrifice one aspect of his ability to function in his intended role. A thane must:

A: Sacrifice his spell casting to maintain a strong weapon attack and shield guard defense, hurting his ability to contribute as “a form of ranged damage while guarding the rear lines.”

B: Sacrifice his melee skills in order to maintain a strong spell arsenal and shield guard defense, hurting his ability to directly protect his caster/support class once the fight comes to the rear lines. (Which happens very rapidly in most RvR encounters).

C: Sacrifice his shield guard defense to maintain a strong melee weapon attack and potent ranged damage arsenal, which will harm a thane’s ability to function in his intended role as a caster/support class guardian by lowering his ability to block attacks.

The thane will either have to lower his spell casting, his guard defense, or his melee skill. This becomes an issue in the thane’s intended role as a caster/support class guardian. This is a problem that all hybrids face, but none of the other hybrid classes are designed or intended by mythic to perform the same role as the thane has been shoehorned into. In light of this, thanes ask that they be given 2.5 specialization points so that they are better able to perform in all three of the aspects that their role, as defined by Mythic.

- Issue 4: General Issues

1: Evade 1 – The thane is the only non plate wearing hybrid class that does not receive the evade ability. (Warden’s are a base naturalist class in addition to having have PBT). Thanes would like to be given parity with these other classes. This is a simple and long standing issue, and while not a major factor in class balance, it falls under the category of “should have been there from the beginning.”

2: Recast timers – The thane’s request that their insta cast spells be lowered to a 15 second recast timer to mirror the comparative insta cast spells of other hybrids. This will improve a thane’s damage output slightly, and add more utility to his insta spells, which, while trying to maintain guard range on a kiting support class, quickly become the only feasible form of ranged damage a thane can provide. It also must be remembered that each of the thane’s spells is situational, so one has two factors that limit their usefulness – recast timers and propriety. Thane insta spells are much more limited than the direct damage spells of other hybrids both in terms of damage, and in terms of when it is acceptable to use them.

3: Weapon skill improvements – The thane class was placed on the same weapon skill per strength table as a bard. They would like to be moved to the skald/paladin/reaver weapon skill table. This will slightly improve the thane’s weapon skill, and allow them to better shield guard, melee stun, and damage their charge’s aggressor.

4: The thane class would like access to the Mastery of Arcane realm ability – this will allow a thane’s self buffs to approach/exceed the effectiveness of a similar specced shamen. This request is in light of mythic’s stance in firm support of buff bots, and the impact these characters have had on all self buffing classes. This is an issue that all self buffing characters need to have addressed, as their self buffs become a liability rather than an advantage in a heavily buffed environment. I believe the ranger and cleric team leads have brought up this issue as well. Note that this realm ability would also allow the thane’s damage add to be improved in it’s effectiveness somewhat, addressing some of the valid concerns of the current thane and ranger team leads in regard to damage adds.

5: Class specific RA skills – The thane class specific RA skill, static tempest, is badly in need of a redesign. Numerous other classes of all realms also suffer from similarly broken and useless realm abilities. Each class’s realm ability should be viewed as potent and effective, and should thus be made a very desirable aspect of that character, not something that is commonly passed over for generalist skills. This includes the melee styles from all realms, midgard RA skills in general, and scores of other class’s abilities, which all need to be reviewed, and normalized in both realm ability point cost, and their utility.

That raps up my final report. Thank you for the past 2 years of reading, replying, yelling, and tolerating me. To those of you masochistic enough to continue, goodbye and good luck, to those of you who have left already, here I come!

Special thanks again to Sheena, Pakka, and especially Aulrich, who have maintained their composure, wit, and tact in some of the most trying circumstances I can possibly imagine. I salute you, and I admire the professionalism you display. I know I could not have done the same in your position. Thanks allowing me to be part of your community, but I have to take my leave of it now.
