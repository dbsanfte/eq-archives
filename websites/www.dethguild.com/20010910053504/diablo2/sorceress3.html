Sorceress Player V.S. Player:
VS
Primary Skills: Frozen orb, firewall, static field, blizzard, teleport, ice armors, energy shield, passive masteries

Tactics: Be very aware - a sorceress is two or three guided arrows from death here. To win against an amazon, your mission is simple - fill the air with so much damaging magic that even an amazon's IMPRESSIVE dodging abilities can't save her. Teleport when it gets hairy, and then fill the screen again. When teleporting v.s any homeing missile, it is critical to change directions every jump. Teleporting in the same direction will only delay you death. Try teleporting in a circular pattern around the amazon - this should throw off many of the guided arrows, then quickly appear as close to her as you dare. In the few moments it takes her to retarget you, fire off three orbs - one above, one below and one to the right of her. Even if you are under the effect of slow missile, the amazon will be very fortunate to live. The initial approach is where you are in the most danger, and perfect timing with your teleport is essential. Twitchthrow, Siagon's Guard, and Culwin's Point helps alot v.s. an amazon.

*Probability of Victory: 4 of 10 matches
VS

Tactics: This fight is all about patience. A typical barbarian wiill have max resistance to your elemental attacks, 2000+ hit points, and an instantly fatal attack. Fortunatly, through the use of teleport, the barbarian will never hit you. Sit tight, using yourself as a lure. As the barb charges at you, launch a barrage of frozen orbs, then, just as his whirlwind is about to slap you silly, teleport to safety. Two or three shots of this will kill any barb, simply be ready to teleport at a moments notice. You can combine teleport with almost any spell, and beat a barb stupid with it.

*Probability of Victory: 8 of 10 matches Primary Skills: Frozen orb, firewall, static field, blizzard, teleport, ice armors, energy shield, passive masteries
VS

Tactics: The necromancer has a great difficulty dealing with area effect spells, which tend to drop most, if not all, of his servants at once. Accommodate this weakness with a mass of frozen orbs. Teleport away from bone spirits or out of bone prisons, and keep blasting frozen orbs or stacked firewalls at him. Don't sweat it if a few bone spirits hit you - energy shield splits the damage down the middle, and Nightsmoke will give you a better return on your investment. Thunderstorm is also a real killer, as many necromancers totally neglect elemental resistance.

*Probability Of Victory: 8 of 10 matches Primary Skills: Frozen orb, firewall, static field, blizzard, teleport, ice armors, energy shield, passive masteries
VS

Tactics: Paladin's are a real pain in a sorceress's pretty little arse. They tend to crumple her into a heap with a single blow, and holy freeze causes REAL problems. Blaze can help alot, and a sorceress can play keep away - running circles around the paladin, and he is forced to follow her through her flaming wake. Thunderstorm is also very useful, as it will allow a sorceress to play evasion games and still deal lethal damage. Teleport into rocky ground, this will hamper a paladin's ability to "lock on" with his charge. Orb him as he charges, then dart away to safety. Holy freeze will make fast escapes very dicey however, so plan ahead on having a slow casting time.

*Probability Of Victory: 5 of 10 matches Primary Skills: Frozen orb, firewall, static field, blizzard, teleport, ice armors, energy shield, passive masteries
VS

Tactics: Well, its all about mana management here. There will be fireworks, explosions, chaos, lightning bolts, followed by mass teleporting and blue mana quaffing. The player that most effectively managed her powers will make the other player, (i.e. the one out of mana) into a frozen, fried, or glowing husk. Telekinesis can be really nasty in these fights, as it inflicts physical damage, not to mention stuns, which can set up an opponent for a firewall or frozen orb.

The sorceress is a player versus play killing machine. Her main equipment consideration should be speed - she needs to keep those melee types from hitting - one love tap from a paladin, amazon, or barbarian and it's a K.O. Teleport is NOT an option - its a means of survival. Use it to get away from a charging paladin, after being decrepified, or to out pace a speed enhanced barb. Also, unless you like amazon's peppering you with guided arrows, get a high blocking shield, and for god sakes, stay away from those bone spirits. Thunderstorm will be leathal to a necormancer, but frozen orb, with a high level of ice mastery is the real killer. At almost 240 damage per ice shard, orb is fatal at close range. And even if they live, they are frozen and easy targets for another one. Hydra and fire wall can be useful "passive" addon's, similar to thunderstorm, but are not as effective. Most people cannot be drug kicking and screaming into a firewall, and placing them under a person's feet is unlikely in a running fight. Mana shield + nightsmoke will keep you alive against other "sluts" and necromancers, as their damage is effectively reduced by a quarter thanks to the "juggling" the belt does. Don't count on orb's freeze to always slow your opponent - players are getting smart and wearing set and unique items that prevent freeze status.
