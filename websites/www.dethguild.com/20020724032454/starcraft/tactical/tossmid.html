Having been on the receiving end of a protoss reaver drop, I can vouch for it's effectiveness. Short of an all out air raid, few things can actually advance on two fully loaded reavers. On top of this, the protoss can acquire reavers in a few short minuets, surprising many players with the speed and ferocity of the assault.

Tactic#1: REAVER DROP
Submitted By: SilverDeth

1: Start by ordering all your probes to harvest. Do not begin construction on another probe. You will want a fast extractor for this one.

2: As soon as you get 100 mineral, order one of your probes's to build a pylon. By the time this is finished, you will have another 150. Build a gateway right next to your minerals. As soon as it is completed, warp in a zealot and begin constructing a cybernetics core. This one zealot will be able to handle four marines or six zerglings.

3: After the first zealot is finished, build two more. Also, warp in four more probes and a refinery. Have the workers gather resources. As funds become available, create two more pylons. Begin building 2 robotics bays. Send your zealots on scouting missions. Remember a good worker to min ratio is 2.3 probes per mineral.

4: As the robotics bays are completed, order them to make a shuttle. Take a probe and create two robotics support bays. When they are completed, research the reaver damage and capacity upgrades.

5: Begin building four reavers and one more shuttle. Construct a forge and begin making photon cannons in case of an attack. By now your zealots should have found the enemy base. As your reavers finish, fill them with scarabs. Time is critical - you must finish before they complete a strong air defense grid.

6: Load the reavers in your shuttles, and fly toward the enemy base. Maneuver around their anti-air units, and drop all four reavers just BEHIND your opponent's minerals, being careful to avoid defensive emplacements. Laugh as any units they try to send at you get blown to pieces. Order your reavers to hold their position, and you opponent's command center will be rubble in seconds. Be sure to keep them full of scarabs. You can use your shuttles to pick up the reavers if they get in trouble, and against tanked terrans, try dropping the reavers right next to unguarded tanks. The tanks and any marine escorts will get blown to itsy bitsy chunks.

Defense #1: Stopping the REAVER DROP
 
  Any protoss ground unit is going to get smashed by the reavers, so ignore building them beyond a small defense force. Instead, go for an air attack. In the same time it will take your opponent get reavers, you can have two or three scouts ready. Kill the shuttles first, and if they still get the reavers out, dismantle them from the air. Have your probes flee if your opponent gets close to them. The nimble workers can easily outmaneuver the lumbering reavers. In a few moments, the scouts will demolish them anyway.
 
  Being able to blow a bunker all to hell in three shots, the reaver is a serious threat to any terran base. Your answer to this attack lies in your machine center. Research siege mode and begin building siege tanks. Four of them parked by your minerals will be a rude shock for the person dropping the reavers. Most likely, the reavers will get blasted before they get a single shot off. As they slowly try to advance on the waiting siege tanks, the 70+ damage of the terran super guns will reduce them into expensive scrap.
 
  Against this tactic, the zerg must rely upon their base attack units - the zerglings. Make certain you get the speed and damage upgrades as fast as you can. Also research the burrow upgrade. You will need roughly 30 zerglings, but this is still cheaper than the cost of the protoss units. Burrow the zerglings behind your minerals and wait for the protoss to land the reavers on top of them. Unburrow them, and the reavers will destroy themselves trying to kill off your zerglings.

Tactic#2: TEMPLAR DROP
Submitted By: DevilSmurf

Build as though you were going to reaver drop, but instead of getting a robotics support bay, build a citadel of adune and a templar archives. Build one shuttle and 2 templar. Research psi-storm while your temps charge. Fly your units close to your opponent's workers, (if done quickly enough your should be able to catch him before he gets a massive anti-air force built). Your target: His harvesters. Two well places psi storms will decimate his harvesters. In the mean time, be building a large mixed group of zealots and dragoons. Take advantage of having the citadel of adune and upgrade zealot speed. As soon as the victim rushes troops in to kill the templar, use psi storm on them as they blunder close. You will inflict a heavy toll on his troops. If he just has ground attack units, scoop up the templar with the shuttle. Take advantage of the time it will take him to rebuild his harvesters by climbing to carriers, or expanding. If you got away with the templar, try another hit and run on his workers. It is hard to kill a templar before he can let loose the worker killing psi-storm, and "poof!" No harvesters!

Tactic#2: DARK TEMPLAR TEMPLAR DROP
Submitted By: FugBastard

Build as though you were going to reaver drop, but instead of getting a robotics support bay, build a citadel of adune and a templar archives. Build one shuttle and 4 dark templar. Fly around whatever choke your opponent has constructed, and drop the dark templar in or near his workers. If your opponent is terran, got for the comsat or any near-by stationary cloak detection. If you are careful to keep out of sight, this attack can ruin an opponent.

Tactic#2: DRAGOONS & CORSAIRS
Submitted By: SilverDeth

The success of this tactic is dependent on two factors - 1: It is still mid-game, and 2: You are not playing a zerg opponent. You will need to amass a fleet of 12 corsairs and 24 dragoons with all possible speed, while still maintaining a reasonable defense/expansion effort. As soon as the prescribed units have been constructed, carefully scout a weak point in an opponent's base. (Using the corsairs, or better yet, observers). You should do you best to acquire as many armor and shield upgrades as possible, the dragoons is not so much known for its attack as it is for it's durability - capitalize on this factor. You will need several templar along as well, unless you like m&m, or superior numbers of goons wreaking your day. Use the corsairs to rape any opposing air, they do well against most mid ranged fighters, and d-web any particularly obnoxious chokes. Two templar will handle all the marines or zealots a person will likely have, and goons can easily jockey out of opposition psi storms. D-web tanks, but you should get this going no later than mid game. An opponent with heavy air will slaughter you.
