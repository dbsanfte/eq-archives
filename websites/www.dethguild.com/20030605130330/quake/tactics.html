Of course, even in a game like quake, (where the only thing flying faster than the action is body parts), there's room for tactics. This section is dedicated to tricks, both dirty and honorable, that will keep your frag count high, and your limbs attached to our torso.

Tactic# 1: Kill the weak. Is it nice? Nope. Is it honorable? Nope. Does it help you win? Yup. This game is not about being nice, it is about quickly dispatching your opponent (s) in the most painful and humiliating way you can find. It's survival of the fittest, so don't go after the stacked freak with quad and a rocket launcher. Chase down the newbie and blast him into a pulp.
Tactic# 2: As you get to know where all the spawn points are, you have a tremendous advantage over any opponents you have just fragged. Quickly run to where you think they will spawn, and splatter them all over the walls. Cheap playing? Oh yeah, but Quake is all about cheap, and besides, he would do the same thing to you in a heart beat.
Tactic# 3: Camping on good toys is irritating to other players, so normally, I would recommend it. But it leaves you very open snipers, and on levels with the rail gun, the last thing you want to EVER do is stop moving. A better tactics is to set up a "patrol" where you run around the level in such a manner as to snag all the fun toys, while presenting a moving target.
Tactic# 4: Get a good keyboard configuration! Ideally, you to be able to switch weapons, move, sidestep, jump, and use items with your left hand, reserving your right hand for flashy mouse work.
Tactic# 5: FOR THE LOVE OF GOD DON'T STOP MOVING!!!!! Move when you shoot, move when you MOVE, move when you die, MOVE MOVE MOVE!!! I see plenty of newbies STOP moving to fire,and it makes them easy targets. The only time you should ever stop is when you have a sweet beyond sweet sniper position HIGH in the air.
Tactic# 6: Turn off the walk option, set it to always run. The ability to walk slowly is basically pointless in Quake III. Sure your quieter when you walk, but your also THAT much easier to hit.
Tactic# 7: Jumping is a great way to evade some of the splash damage from weapons like the rocket and grenade launcher. Most players aim at your feet with these weapons, so launching yourself airborne keeps your farther out of the blast, and thus, reduces the damage taken.
Tactic# 8: You can hurl yourself to incredible heights with the rocket launcher or BGH10K by shooting at the ground at the apex of a jump. This is a great way to reach places, and it is also a great way to kill yourself. Practice a bit with it in single player before adding it into your strategy. Timing is critical, and if you screw up with the BFG10K, you're fly bait.
Tactic# 9: Quad - get it. Camp on it if you can do so safely, even the machine gun gets dangerous with quad damage. The rocket/grenade launcher, BFG10K, and lightning guns become almost certain 1 hit kills.
Tactic# 10: If you are totally stacked, do not just leave weapons or ammo lying about. Fire off a round and then pick up the weapon/ammo. Deny your opponents the toys, make then attack you with shotguns.
Tactic# 11: In close combat, resist the temptation to fight two dimensionally. Moving up/down/left/right is a nice way to give people kills, but it does little to improve your standing. When you break into a melee, strafe from side to side, facing your opponent while moving laterally. This presents them with a moving target that is still facing them and firing back.
Tactic# 12: The DETH circle: Once you get strafing attacks down, you are ready to try the DETH circle. This maneuver works best with the plasma or machine gun, and involves you running lateral circles around your opponent, while pelting him with everything up to and including, the kitchen facet. Keep them in your cross hairs, and switch directions occasionally, or they may try to lead you with a rocket. This is a good way to handle a stacked opponent, or just confuse the shit outta someone.
Tactic# 13: Jump up short stairs and ramps, you slow down when you walk or run up them, and the last thing you want to do is make yourself any easier to hit. On short ramps or low stairways, jump over the obstacle. This will also help you evade if a camper is waiting at the top of the stairs for you.
Tactic# 14: Often you will see bridges spanning the "red fog of death." Now, a fall from their is a suicide, and costs you a frag. Or your opponent. A rocket launcher has been known to bounce people around, and a well timed shot at your opponent's feet could very easily send him teetering off the ledge, and COST him a kill. Very situation specific of course, but sooo worth it for the piss off value alone.
Tactic# 15: Be cheap! Use any unfair advantage that you can. This is the ultimate advice in the game of quake. Predators don't hunt down the strong and healthy, they kill the old and sick. If a guy just finished a nasty fight and has 5% health left, it's your DUTY to cull the herd. If you know a player just spawned in a dead end hallway, don't be afraid to shoot twenty or so rockets against the facing wall, slowly wasting him with the splash damage. The person with the nastiest tricks wins in Quake III, so be mean, be cheap, and most of all, be dirty. Vai Victus.
