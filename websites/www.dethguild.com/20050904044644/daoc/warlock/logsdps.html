Presented here is information regarding the Warlock class, for use as a refrence and resource for those wishing to present a case for the adjustment and balancing of the class to sane levels.
Method to the Madness:

My logs are very simple, and my process strait forward - I wanted to show how much damage a Warlock was capable of unloading on a target before killing it, how quickly target death occures from moment the first spell strikes the it, and how the level of resists on the target impacted it's survivability against a Warlock assault. Also - keep in mind, how long a support class has to respond to the Warlock fontload if he expects's to actually heal the target.

I find it troubling how quickly someone with 26% spell resistance is killed by magical damage. Observations:
With 50% spell resistance - thus, players with maxed equipment resists and have the benifit of the top resistance buffs - it is within the realm of possibility that friendly support will be able to react to a Warlock frontload quickly enough to provide healing. The extra 3-5 seconds of life purchased with the presence of red specced resistance buffs should grant a primary healing class enough time to heal the Warlock's target - with this level of damage output, I do NOT feel that the secondary healing classes (Warden, Mentalist, Bard, Shamen, Friar, or Heratic) could reliably keep a Warlock's target alive - even with resistance buffs. This should be of great concern for groups unable to find a support class capable of providing the very best resistance buffs, as it will be very difficult to keep members of their group alive with only their base (26%) resists.
