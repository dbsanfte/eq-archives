The Members Area is dedicated to helping the members of the DETH Guild interact. Here members can post messages for one another, view hidden files, or arrange times to meet. Below is a full index of the contents of the members section:

Members - This area includes a total listing of the guild members, as well as their e-mail addresses and merits. Members are listed according to rank, with the higher ranks receiving more privileges.
Mission - What is the DETH Guild about? What are the rules? Who are our enemies and allies? Fear not, all the answers and more can be found here.....
Forum - Here you can post a message for the members of the guild to read. It is our primary means of communication, and active members are encouraged to read and post often. Any subject, from technical aid to game tips to jokes could turn up......
Vault - The vault is a high security storehouse for a multitude of sensitive information. Members with the rank of Hunter better are given access, and it is the front door to the Great Hunter's Vault.
