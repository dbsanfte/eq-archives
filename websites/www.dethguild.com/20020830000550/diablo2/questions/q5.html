Which boots are better, Treds of Cthon or Vidala's Fetlocks?

Vidala's Fetlocks at early levels.
Treds of Cthons at high levels.


Vidala's Fetlocks:
  Defense: 9-11
  Durability: 30
  Fastest Run/Walk
  +150 to Maximum Stamina

Treds of Cthon:
  Defense: 8-9
  Durability: 80
  Required Strength: 30
  50% Stamina Drain
  Fastest Run/Walk
  +50 Defense VS. Missile*
  +10 To Life

It breaks down like this:

The only real difference is how they affect your stamina. Vidala's Fetlocks adds 150 to stamina, and Treds of Cthon drains your stamina half as fast. If you have more than 150 stamina, then Treds of Cthon are better. Otherwise Vidala's Fetlocks are better. This is because Treds of Cthon basicly double your stamina, and if you have less that 150 stamina, then Vidala's Fetlocks gives you a higher total stamina.

Example 1:

Your character has 95 stamina:
Vidala's Fetlocks: 95+150 = 245 stamina
Treds of Cthon: 95*2 = 190 Stamina

Example 2:

Your character has 201 stamina:
Vidala's Fetlocks: 201+150 = 351 stamina
Treds of Cthon: 201*2 = 402 Stamina

Example 3:

Your character has 387 stamina:
Vidala's Fetlocks: 387 +150 = 537 stamina
Treds of Cthon: 387*2 = 774 Stamina


*Note*
The stamina listed above for Treds of Cthon is virtual and is for comparison with Vidala's Fetlocks. It will not display that stamina in the game, but it will act like you have that much stamina.
