Amazon Overview:

The amazon is possibly diablo 2's most versatile class. She has two open roads, each of which are playable to higher levels. She is unmatched with a bow, and at the same time, is quite able to mix it up in a melee with a spear or javelin. It should be noted, that while an amazon CAN hand-to-hand, this is not necessarily the OPTIMAL role for her. Her melee skills, particularly Jab, truly shine v.s. boss monsters, and are best when reserved for such occasions. For the rest of the game, a great bow will keep your amazon dropping besties like flies. The priorities for an amazon are - The wickedest, most damaging bow she can lay her hands on, items that increase running speed, hit recovery, and stamina, and, did we mention a really freaking awesome bow? Only a rare bow will fit the bill, so gamble, imbue, trade, kill, DO WHATEVER you have to get a bow in the 45-150 dmg range. After nailing a weapon like that, she is set all the way through hell mode. Now, there is the spearazon, the pike wielding equivalent of the bowazon, but thanks to bad coding, the lance, (a gruesomely damaging exceptional pike), cannot drop - ever. This totally renders the spearazon ineffectual in the later difficulties, as she cannot find an effective exceptional weapon. So, if you make a spearazon, don't expect to play them much past mid-nightmare. Continuing on, the art of playing an amazon is learning how to fight an effective running offense. Attack, fall back, attack, fall back, rinse and repeat - it becomes obvious that items granting the best possible hit recovery, extended stamina, and enhanced running speed are CRITICAL to the success of these fighters. An amazon's true strength lies in her passive skills - critical strikes, dodges, and her decoys, which begin to stack very rapidly, and the overlapping bonuses make even a mediocre weapon like the bow into a devastating engine of destruction. An amazon can survive being swarmed briefly, as her evasion skills will keep her alive for a WHILE, but she has no where NEAR the life-span of a barb or a paladin in a crowd, so you will want, (NEED), to keep moving. Attribute points are difficult to allocate for an amazon, as she is so multifaceted that every skill needs some attention. But her dex should receive the lion's share of the power, as it will not only drastically up her chance to hit enemies, but also increase her base damage. A decent strength is good, but don't overdo it - lighter armors reduce the stamina her drain, and she NEEDS to run. Passive skills will do most of an amazon's evasion anyways.

Amazon Quote: "You can't HIT what you can't CATCH."
