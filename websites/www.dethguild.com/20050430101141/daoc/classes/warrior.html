A warrior salutes, ready for a fight.
Warrior's are the quinessential tank. A great starting class in DAoC, the warrior is simple and strait-forward. Warriors have the most armor, hit points, and survivability of any class of arguably any class in the game. Warriors are loved by PvE raid leaders for their ability to absorbe damage - warriors are not damage dealers, but they are damage sponges, holding aggression from very high level mobs. Their lack of ranged attack is very limiting in RvR however.
