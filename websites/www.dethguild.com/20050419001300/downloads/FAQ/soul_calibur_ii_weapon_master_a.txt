Hello and welcome to my latest and greatest FAQ, this one covering Soul Calibur
II’s Weapon Master Mode. Although I do own a Dreamcast, I got to play neither
the original Soul Calibur nor Soul Blade for the PlayStation, so SCII was a
godsend to me. While I’m not the world’s biggest fighting game fan, I do enjoy
playing one every once and a while, and SCII is easily one of the best fighters
I’ve ever played (if not THE best).

So anyway, for this FAQ I’ve decided to leave out all that preliminary stuff I
usually throw into all my other work, such as characters and game basics. Why
did I do this? Simply because just playing through the Weapon Master Mode
(hereby WMM) doesn’t require much knowledge of every feature in SCII’s fighting
system. Besides, the first mission tells you everything you need to know. So
without further adieu, here we go!

Enjoy!

  --Chris Noonan / VTS

    a) Regulus
*******************************************************************************

Proving Grounds
******* *******
Opponent: <Edgar> Samurai of the Fire (Mitsurugi)
Objective: Clear all the exercises!
Experience: 400 | Gold: 600
  This battle is nothing more than a basic tutorial on how to play the game.
  Your opponent (Mitsurugi) will give you some instructions and you’ll then
  get to try them out. Here’s a blow-by-blow description of the battle:
  *First press A to hit him with a horizontal attack.
  *Second press Y to slash him vertically.
  *Third press X to perform a kick.
  *Next guard against his attack by pressing either B or one of the triggers.
  *Now just circle around him a bit.
  *Throw your opponent by pressing either A or Y whilst guarding.
  *First use any attack against him and he’ll Guard Impact it. Now you get to
   try it: just watch for his attack and press B and either left or right. If
   you’re anything like me, it’ll take a few tries before you can nail it down.
  *Finally, you are asked to perform a Soul Charge. You can do this by either
   pressing up on the C-Stick or by pressing Z.

  **Extra Mission**
  Opponent: Random
  Objective: Combatants are launched high into the air when knocked against
             the ground. Both combatants’ health recovers. Defeat the enemy
             under these conditions!
  Experience: 800 | Gold: 1200
  You just need to use some simple knockdown moves here for an easy victory.
  You can also win just using normal attacks, as they work perfectly fine here.

Bulwark Ruins
******* *****
Opponents: Kilik, Xianghua, Maxi
Objective: Utilize all that you learned to defeat all the enemies!
Experience: 400 | Gold: 900
  This is a simple three-stage battle meant to help you hone your skills
  before taking on some of the more difficult missions. You’ll have to fight
  Kilik followed by Xianghua and finally Maxi all in a row, but they aren’t
  very powerful opponents and your health will be replenished between each
  fight. Easy stuff.

  **Extra Mission**
  Opponents: Ivy, Astaroth, Nightmare
  Objective: Defeat all the enemies
  Experience: 800 | Gold: 1800
  There’s nothing special about this mission –- it’s just a simple ladder
  match.

Warrior’s Trial Grounds
********* ***** *******
Opponent: Character Specific (each character has a different enemy)
Objective: Overcome the enemy to clear the trial!
Experience: 400 | Gold: 1500
  Who you fight in this battle depends upon which character you chose to use.
  For the sake of simplicity, I’ll just refer to this opponent as “Character
  Specific”. Anyway, this battle isn’t too hard, so just apply everything you
  learned in the first mission and you shouldn’t have any trouble.

  **Extra Mission**
  Opponents: Sophitia, Seung Mina, Necrid, Link
  Objective: The enemies have strange weapons. Defeat all the enemies under
             this condition!
  Experience: 800 | Gold: 3000
  This is a pretty funny mission, as all of your foes are equipped with their
  joke weapons! Anyway, there isn’t much to say here, as the battles don’t
  really have anything to give them a twist.

*******************************************************************************
   b) Thuban
*******************************************************************************

Thuban Ferry
****** *****
Opponent: Voldo
Objective: Land 20 hits on the enemy and survive the round!
Experience: 500 | Gold: 400
  This battle may take awhile if you aren’t too skilled yet. You have a paltry
  20 seconds to nail your opponent with 20 strikes. Just use some rapid
  attacks and avoid blows that knock your foe down and you should do fine.

  **Extra Mission**
  Opponent: Taki
  Objective: Land 20 hits on the quick enemy and survive the round!
  Experience: 1000 | Gold: 800
  This battle is pretty tough, mainly due to how quick Taki is and what little
  time you are given. You have 20 seconds to land 20 hits again, but this time
  your opponent will block often and put up a good fight. I’ve found that Link
  has a really useful move that helps greatly with this: repeatedly press
  forward+Y.

Village of Arche
******* ** *****
Opponent: Yunsung
Objective: Strong gusts constantly push you and prevent you from stopping at
           the edge of the of the stage. Defeat the enemy under this condition!
Experience: 500 | Gold: 800
  This is just a normal battle...Except that the wind is very powerful, so
  you’ll be pushed towards the edge. Deplete your foe’s energy if you want,
  although circling around him and knocking him off the ledge is quite a bit
  easier. Just make sure you don’t get too close yourself, as the enemy can
  get a ring out on you, as well.

  **Extra Mission**
  Opponent: Mitsurugi
  Objective: Strong gusts constantly push you and prevent you from stopping at
             the edge of the stage. Furthermore, both combatants’ speed is
             increased. Defeat the enemy under these conditions!
  Experience: 1000 | Gold: 1600
  Just like in the normal mission, the best way to complete this is to simply
  strafe around your foe and go for the ring out.

Minkar Mine
****** ****
Opponent: Boss - <Cherot> Chief of Minkar (Yoshimitsu)
Objective: Navigate through the enemy-infested dungeon and defeat the boss.
Experience: 400 | Gold: 1600

  Here’s a crude map of your first dungeon.

  Entrance ----> [ ]-[ ]-[ ]
                          |
                         [ ] <---- Boss

  Before you reach the boss, you get to fight against two weak, random
  opponents. It’s pretty easy to get ring outs, too, so making your way to the
  boss should prove to be a rather simple task. The boss himself isn’t all
  that hard either. If you’re having trouble beating him, though, try to go
  for a ring out.

  **Extra Mission**
  Opponent: Boss – Raphael
  Objective: Navigate through the enemy-infested dungeon and defeat the boss!
  Experience: 800 | Gold: 2200
  The dungeon layout is exactly the same as the normal mission, so I won’t
  bother with another sucky map.

*******************************************************************************
   c) Benetnasch
*******************************************************************************

Carnival
********
Opponent: Charade
Objective: You cannot hold your footing at the edge of the stage. Defeat the
           enemy with a ring out!
Experience: 700 | Gold: 1000
  This isn’t too difficult. Just get your opponent near the ledge (most
  characters have a throw or two that will do the trick) and knock them off
  with a kick.

  **Extra Mission**
  Opponent:
  Objective: You cannot hold your footing at the edge of the stage, and the
             gravity is weaker. Defeat the enemy with a ring out!
  Experience: 1300 | Gold: 2000
  In this mission, your normal attacks will barely scratch the enemy, although
  you can send your foe higher into the air due to the weakened gravity.
  Getting a ring out shouldn’t be excessively difficult.

Talitha Gambling Den
******* ******** ***
Opponent: Character Specific (remember this guy from Regulus?)
Objective: Receive heavy damage when knocked against the ground or wall.
           Defeat the enemy under this condition!
Experience: 700 | Gold: 1000
  Normal attacks hardly do anything here, so you need to knock your enemy
  against the wall/ground to cause some heavy damage. If you’re new to Soul
  Calibur and don’t know any moves that will do the trick, switch over to
  Mitsurugi and use his back+X move -- it works wonders.

  **Extra Mission**
  Opponent: Three random characters
  Objective: Combatants receive heavy damage when knocked against the ground
             or wall. Furthermore, your appearance has changed. Defeat all the
             enemies under this condition!
  Experience: 1300 | Gold: 2000
  I cannot stand playing this mission, probably because I totally suck with
  Lizardman, who is the character you’re forced to play as. I’ve compiled a
  few of his moves that work well in certain situations though, so here they
  are:

  *Use these when you’re near the wall:
   Back-back-X

  *Good knockdown attacks:
   A+Y
   Up+A

  Other than those, though, I really can’t help you out here. Button-mashing
  and a lot of luck is all that got me past this mission.

Diphda Temple
****** ******
Opponent: Raphael
Objective: Throws are more effective. Defeat the enemy!
Experience: 700 | Gold: 500
  As the objective suggests, throws work very well here. That’s not to say
  that you can’t win using normal attacks, because you can, but throws just
  tend to work better here.

  **Extra Mission**
  Opponent: Charade
  Objective: The enemy will continually guard. Cause a Guard Break and attack
  while the enemy’s defenses are down!
  Experience: 1300 | Gold: 1000
  Another tough one. You have only a mere 20 seconds to deplete your foe’s
  energy meter, while it guards nearly the entire time! I recommend equipping
  a weapon that penetrates your opponent’s defense, so that you’ll cause a
  little bit of damage even when it guards. Just try to catch Charade off
  guard and you should win eventually.

Benetnasch Harbor
********** ******
Opponents: Maxi, Ivy, Voldo, <Alfred> King of the Pirates (Cervantes)
Objective: The enemies’ weapons have poison, so you will go numb if you are
           damaged! Defeat the enemies under this condition!
Experience: 800 | Gold: 2500
  A ladder match against the four characters who are, in my opinion, the most
  difficult to fight against, eh? This CAN’T be good. To make matters worse,
  you’ll be stunned each time you’re hit, giving your foes more time to strike
  again. Think it can’t get any worse? You’re dead wrong, pal, since you only
  have limited health replenishment between fights. My advice: guard often and
  go for ring outs against everyone -- it’ll make life a lot easier for you.

  **Extra Mission**
  Opponents: Ivy, Raphael, Cervantes, Voldo
  Objective: Defeat all the enemies!
  Experience: 1500 | Gold: 5000
  Thankfully, this mission isn’t too difficult. It’s simply a ladder match
  against four enemies. No special qualities that make this any tougher. Yay.
  Also, your health will be totally restored between matches.

*******************************************************************************
    d) Mizar
*******************************************************************************

Al Gieba Ruins
** ***** *****
Opponent: <Hobb> Servant of Veral (Assassin)
Objective: Force the bomb off onto the enemy with your attacks! You win if the
           enemy has the bomb when the timer runs out.
Experience:
  Ever play hot potato? No? Well, you’re about to! Basically, you just have to
  make sure you get the last attack. The best way I’ve fought to do this is to
  wait until there are only a few seconds left and then hit your foe with a
  knockdown attack. This way, they don’t have enough time to return the favor.

  **Extra Mission**
  Opponent: Random opponent
  Objective: Force the bomb off onto the enemy with your attacks! You win if
             the enemy has the bomb when the timer runs out.
  Experience: 1500 | Gold: 2400
  Almost the same thing as the normal version of this mission. Easy as pie.

Windmill
********
Opponents: Yoshimitsu, Taki
Objective: Your health is low. Defeat all the enemies under this condition!
Experience: 800 | Gold: 1200
  Okay, so you have half of your energy meter to defeat two enemies. No
  biggie, we’re just going to regain some of it after the first battle. I
  don’t really have much to say here; just try to not take too much damage. As
  always, ring outs are terrific ways to preserve your health.

  **Extra Mission**
  Opponents: Assassin, Assassin
  Objective: Your health is low. Defeat all the enemies under this condition!
  Experience: 1500 | Gold: 2400
  Same thing as above, only significantly harder. Nothing new, here.

Merope Monastery
****** *********
Opponents: Yunsung, Ivy, Mitsurugi, Astaroth, Nightmare
Objective: The timer increases with each enemy you defeat. Defeat all the
           enemies before time runs out!
Experience: 800 | Gold: 3000
  There really isn’t anything to say here, as it is just a basic battle
  against several opponents with any real twists. The timer will increase
  after each battle, so as long as you don’t take too long you should be fine.
  Also, I shouldn’t need to mention this, but I will anyway: ring outs save
  TONS of time. As an added plus, you’re health will be fully restored after
  each fight. Nice!

  **Extra Mission**
  Opponents: Mitsurugi, Voldo, Taki, Nightmare, Cervantes
  Objective: The timer increases with each enemy you defeat. Figure out the
             best way to defeat all the enemies before time runs out!
  Experience:
  Simply put, this mission is HARD! Your opponents are relentless and the
  timer is only increased by 15 seconds between matches. Your best bet is to
  lure you first opponent, Mitsurugi, towards the edge and knock him off to
  save some time. In fact, ring outs will be integral to your victory.
  Hopefully you’ll have between 30-40 seconds for each of the two final
  battles so that you’ll be able to put up an actual fight.

Deserted Village
******** *******
Opponents: Mitsurugi, Raphael
Objective: The enemies favor Vertical Attacks and are armed with weapons that
           excel in Breaks. Defeat the enemies under these conditions!
Experience: 900 | Gold: 1200
  This isn’t much different from a normal fight. Just use your favorite moves
  and try not to lose an excessive amount of health during the first battle
  and you’ll win with little trouble.

  **Extra Mission**
  Opponents: Necrid, Astaroth
  Objective: The enemies favor Horizontal Attacks and are armed with weapons
             that are strong against Breaks. Defeat the enemies under these
             conditions!
  Experience: 1800 | Gold: 2400
  I just fight this like I would a normal battle and I do fine. I expect
  you’ll be able to do the same.

Charon’s Pass
******** ****
Opponent: Boss - <Leana> Paladin Knight of Charon (Sophitia)
Objective: Navigate through the booby-trapped dungeon and defeat the boss!
Experience: 1500 | Gold: 3000

  Here's my nifty dungeon map for you! Keep in mind that rooms marked “I” take
  place on ice, while rooms marked “Q” take place in quicksand. “F” signifies
  a stage that has fire surrounding the outer edge. The room with a “P” on it
  will yield a reward when you beat it. Unmarked rooms are just normal
  battlefields with no abnormal hazards.

                                [P]
                                 |
                                [ ]-[ ]-[ ] <--- Boss
                                 |
  Entrance ---> [ ]-[I]-[Q]-[F]-[ ]

  Until you reach the boss, you’ll be fighting against a set of randomly-
  chosen opponents on various different types of terrain. Battles that take
  place on ice can be especially tricky; I recommend going for the ring out.
  Quicksand doesn’t have much of an impact on the battle, nor does the fire.
  Stand in the flaming lava too long, though, and you’ll receive some damage.
  Not cool, but it works against your opponents as well. One last thing:
  almost all of the enemies in this dungeon are constantly regaining their
  health, so it’s best to take them out as quickly as possible.

  The boss fight is against Sophitia, who fights similarly to her sister
  Cassandra. Like most everyone else in this dungeon, her health is recharging
  all the time. Nonetheless, she isn’t a very tough opponent.

  **Extra Mission**
  Opponent: Boss - <Gerald> Warrior of Prophecy (Maxi)
  Objective: Navigate through the booby-trapped dungeon and defeat the boss!
  Experience: 1500 | Gold: 6000
  Until you reach the boss, this dungeon is VERY similar to the version seen
  in the normal mission. The boss is Maxi, but the catch is that his health is
  always regenerating. Nonetheless, a few good hits should take him down.
  Nothing too difficult.

*******************************************************************************
    e) Altair
*******************************************************************************

Nereid Gorge
****** *****
Opponents: Nightmare, Yoshimitsu, Assassin
Objective: Team up with the fort’s soldiers and defeat all the enemies!
Experience: 900 | Gold: 2100
  Depending on your skill with the three characters you are given (Xianghua,
  Taki, and Mitsurugi), this battle can be really easy or really hard. Pretty
  much, it’s your team of three against the enemy’s team of three. If you
  lose, you have to use your next character. There really isn’t a whole lot to
  say here other than try button-mashing if you aren’t particularly avid with
  any of the available characters. It may be cheap, but it works.

  **Extra Mission**
  Opponents: Three random opponents
  Objective: Control a special team and defeat all the enemies!
  Experience: 1800 | Gold: 4200
  In this battle, your team, comprised of Assassin, Berserker, and Lizardman,
  must defeat three randomly-chosen foes. If you aren’t particularly
  skilled with any of the characters given to you it isn’t really a big deal.
  Button-mashing and/or performing a few basic moves or two-button combos
  always works for me.

Paradise of Altair
******** ** ******
Opponent: Whoever you are playing as
Objective: You are your worst enemy...Defeat yourself!
Experience: 900 | Gold: 1400
  This is just a normal battle with no gimmicks, so it really comes down to
  whether you’re halfway decent and can beat this stage or if you really suck
  and can’t. There isn’t much of a strategy, other than maybe go for a ring
  out.

  **Extra Mission**
  Opponents: Mitsurugi, <Edgar> Samurai of the Fire (Mitsurugi)
  Objective: The enemies have excellent speed and health. The ability to knock
             back the enemy has increased. Defeat all of the enemies within
             the time limit!
  Experience: 1800 | Gold: 2400
  Since you can knock enemies back a lot further than normal, this is the
  perfect opportunity to go for the ring out. As per usual, it works like a
  charm!

*******************************************************************************
    f) Aldebaran
*******************************************************************************

Castle of the Dead
****** ** *** ****
Opponent: Berserker
Objective: Defeat the enemy within the time limit!
Experience: 1000 | Gold: 800
  At first, this seems just to be a normal battle with no gimmicks to spice it
  up. Of course, once you start you find that your attacks don’t do nearly as
  much damage as they should. Fortunately, there’s still plenty of time to
  kill your opponent, Berserker.

  **Extra Mission**
  Opponent: Assassin
  Objective: The enemy has excellent speed and health. Furthermore, your
             health decreases over time. Defeat the enemy within the time
             limit!
  Experience: 2000 | Gold: 1600
  Since your health is quickly running out, you need to end this battle
  quickly. Just use some powerful combos/moves or knock Assassin out of the
  ring. No biggie.

City of Nashira
**** ** *******
Opponents: 5 randomly chosen opponents
Objective: The prize money doubles with each win. Gauge your strength and win
           the matches!
Experience: 1000 | Gold: Ranges from 300-4800
  Winning this tournament can be kind of tough, actually. Getting ring outs
  and/or winning battles without taking much damage will be key should you
  choose to try to win this tourney, since your health isn’t restored between
  matches. Of course, a wussy player will just win the first battle, keep the
  pocket money, and move onwards to the next stage. Personally, I find that
  it’s much more rewarding to win the tournament rather than to run away.

  **Extra Mission**
  Opponents: Five randomly chosen opponents
  Objective: The prize money doubles with each win. Gauge your strength and
             win the matches.
  Experiences: 2000 | Gold: Ranges from 1000-16000
  As with before, you don’t HAVE to win the entire tournament; the first
  battle is all you NEED to win. Be a man and go for the gold, it just takes a
  little skill!

Seginus Temple
******* ******
Opponent: <Arma> Halberdier of Seginus (Seung Mina)
Objective: The enemy’s weapon has the ability to Guard Break. Defeat the enemy
           under this condition!
Experience: 1000 | Gold: 800
  This is your first battle against the Korean beauty Seung Mina, who happens
  to fight a lot like Kilik. Anyway, this battle shouldn’t be a problem unless

  This mission is comprised of a tournament-style battle against four

***How do I unlock Procyon?***

   Beat Stage 3 of Subchapter 3.

Muliphein’s Crypt
*********** *****
Opponents: Necrid, Lizardman, Charade, Astaroth
Objective: The ability to knock back the enemy has been increased. Moreover,
           you cannot hold your footing at the edge of the stage. Defeat the
           enemy under these conditions!
Experience: 2000 | Gold: 7500
  Although you can knock enemies back further than usual, I find that it tends
  to be easier to simply go for the K.O., since getting a ring out requires
  that you get near the edge of the arena, which can be dangerous. Why is it
  so dangerous? One slick move from your foe and you’ll be in the drink, as
  you cannot hold your footing at the edge. I’d only go for the ring out if
  you’re already near the edge; don’t go out of your way to try for one. Also,
  don’t worry TOO much about your energy, because some of it is restored
  between matches.

  **Extra Mission**
  Opponents: Assassin, Lizardman, Berserker, Necrid
  Objective: You cannot hold your footing at the edge of the stage, unseen
             forces push the opponents apart. Defeat the enemy under these
             conditions!
  Experience: 4000 | Gold: 15000
  Like this mission’s easier counterpart, I recommend avoiding ring outs and
  going for a direct K.O., since you still can’t keep your balance at the
  edge of the stage. As per usual, health is restored between battles so this
  isn’t too difficult.

Tower of Happiness
***** ** *********
Opponent: Charade
Objective: The strong gusts increase your ability to knock back the enemy.
           Defeat the enemy with a ring out!
Experience: 2000 | Gold: 3000
  Easy as pie. Lure your foe to the edge and use just about any kick move you
  want.

  **Extra Mission**
  Opponent: Charade
  Objective: The ability to knock back the enemy has been increased.
             Furthermore, receive heavy damage when knocked into the wall or
             ground. Defeat the enemy with a ring out.
  Experience: 4000 | Gold: 6000
  Same thing as before. Still works like a charm.

Mount Procyon
***** *******
Opponent: Yunsung
Objective: You will be launched high into the air when knocked to the ground.
           Defeat the enemy under this condition!
Experience: 2000 | Gold: 3000
  Normal attacks don’t do any damage AT ALL, so you’ll have to rely on air
  combos. Don’t know any good ones? Try Mitsurugi’s back+X then Y or you could
  use his A+Y. They both work perfectly in this situation.

  **Extra Mission**
  Opponent: Random
  Objective: Combatants are launched high into the air when knocked to the
             ground. Use air combos to defeat the enemy!
  Experience: 4000 | Gold: 6000
  The moves I listed right above still work fine. Use them.

Lynx Arbor
**** *****
Opponents: Four random opponents followed by <Unknown> Unknown (Link)
Objective: The monetary prize doubles with each win. Gauge your strength and
           win the matches!
Experience: 2000 | Gold: Ranges from 800-12800
  There really isn’t any strategy for this mission –- it simply comes down to
  pure skill. Of course, seeing as how I’ve beat this mission, it can’t take
  much too skill, because I’m pretty sucky overall. Anyway, if it’s too hard
  you can just quit after the first match or two.

  **Extra Mission**
  Opponents: Four random opponents followed by <Unknown> Unknown (Link)
  Objective: The enemies are quick and possess weapons that excel in Guard
             Breaks. The monetary prize doubles with each win. Gauge your
             strength and win the matches!
  Experience: 4000 | Gold: Varies from 1200-19200
  This is just a tougher version of the original, so there isn’t anything else
  for me to say.

******** ********

           health runs out!

  The thing that makes this mission different from your usual dungeon-crawlers
  is that you only have one energy meter for the entire level. In other words,
  you have to take as little damage as possible if you want to make it to the
  very end intact. Fortunately, your health is partially replenishes between
  fights and ring outs are easier to get than usual.

  The boss fight is against a very agile and potent Ivy. If your health isn’t
  at or near it’s maximum, I recommend retreating to one of the rooms
  marked  “R” and regaining some. Other than that, the battle is decently easy.

  **Extra Mission**
  Opponent: Boss - <Azami> Light of the Shadow
  Objective: Navigate through the booby-trapped dungeon and defeat the boss!
  Experience: 8600 | Gold: 16200
  Thankfully, the dungeon layout hasn’t changed a bit, so we can zoom right
  through this hellish place. The boss strategy is the same, as well –- just
  make sure you’ve got enough health.

*Q: Which character is the best? Which character do you use?

*A: I’m not at all positive, but I think that Cervantes is overall the best
    character, according to the tiers. Of course, your personal skill with a
    character is much more important that how a character ranks on a tier, so
    there is no specific best character. As for who I use, I was formerly a
    Link player, but then I started using Mitsurugi and began to see how bad
    Link sucks. So yeah, I use Mitsie.


*Q: How about the worst character?

*A: Not a clue. I’d guess either Link or Yunsung, since I hear a lot of bad
    stuff about them, but you can get pretty good with any character if you
    work at it.


*Q: What’s the best way to make money?

*A: Go to the second level of Chapter 9. You can rack up a TON of cash by
    doing this over and over again, either the normal version or the extra
    mission.


*Q: From which stages do I unlock the secret characters?

*A: Here’s a happy little list I made up:

    Yoshimitsu - Thuban Stage 3
    Charade    - Benetnasch Stage 1
    Cervantes  - Benetnasch Stage 4
    Sophitia   - Mizar Stage 5
    Seung Mina - Aldebaran Stage 3
    Assassin   - Beat Subchapter 3, Stage 2
    Berserker  - Beat Subchapter 1, Stage 1, the Extra Mission
    Lizardman  - Beat everything in Weapon Master Mode, including all of the
                 Extra Missions


*Q: From which stages do I unlock the secret arenas?

*A: I made a list for this too. Here it is:

    Lakeside Coliseum             - Regulus Stage 3
    Money Pit/Top Tier            - Mizar Stage 1
    Labyrinth                     - Aldebaran Stage 6
    Hwangseo Palace/Phoenix Court - Rigel Stage 2
    Egyptian Crypt                - Merak Stage 5


*Q: How do I unlock Subchapter 2?

*A: First off, you need to be at Level 72 (Iron Edgemaster) at least. Once
    you’ve attained that class, you need to go beat Chapter 4, Stage 3 (even
    if you’ve already beaten it you need to do it again). Doing that will
    unlock Subchapter 2 for you.


*Q: What is the highest class possible? What class are you at right now?

*A: I don’t know the class title, but I’m sure there’s a Level 99. From what
    I’ve heard, though, attaining that level would take something around 100
    million experience points, so there’s no way I’m going to try to get that.
    I’m only at Level 76 (Platinum Edgemaster).


*Q: How do I unlock the extra missions?

*A: Simply beat Stage 3 of Chapter 10 and the extra missions will become
    available.


*Q: Where can I find everyone’s last three weapons?

*A: You can buy them in Subchapter 2 and the two Extra Chapters.
