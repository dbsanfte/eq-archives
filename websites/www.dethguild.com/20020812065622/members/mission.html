Welcome to the DETH Guild Mission Section. Here is a breakdown of the guild rules and benefits:

Mission Statement: It is the ambition of the DETH guild to be the perfect mercenaries. We are as ever changing as the wind. PKs, PKKs, assassins, bounty hunters, treasure hunters, trackers, body guards, we are all - for a price. No task is too grand or too pathetic - as long as their is profit to be made.
Clan Motto: Vai Victus - Suffering To The Conquered. This Latin phrase was screamed by the Roman centurions as they charged into battle. Historically, the Centurions were the mercenary backbone of the fearsome Roman legions, an unopposable force, and much of the DETH Guild's martial philosophy was based upon the code of these excellent sell-swords.
Coat of Arms: Death's Head Bearing Scythe. The official symbol of the DETH Guild is the scythe. This farming implement has long held connotations of death and pestilence, dating back as far as the middle ages during the Black Plague. It represents the vital elements of life being excised by death. We combine this symbol with the Death's Head logo, adding to the already menacing appearance of the design. Resembling a devil's skull, the Death's head logo represents the almost demonic nature of the guild's vengeance. Never is a slight forgotten, or a transgression allowed to go unpunished.
.
Regulations:
1. We are first and foremost mercenaries - Do anything a client asks, as long as you or the guild profits. The only exception is........
2. Never kill or attack another member of the DETH guild, even if hired to do so by the client. Should this happen, use the client's confidence in you to destroy them [the client] in the most expedient manner possible. Friendly duels are permitted between members.
3. Demand compensation for any request. Even if you don't need it, take something any ways. We give no freebies. Exceptions can be made for guild members and trusted allies.
4. Follow the spirit, (but not necessarily the intent), of any agreement. Make it work best for you. If a person gives you vague instructions, oh well, they deserve what they get. They still have to pay your tab. And when they don't......
5. Destroy any client that cheats or steals from you. Slaughter them. If possible, humiliate them publicly, if not, tap the thief on the shoulder a couple of times - with an axe. We give bonuses for crushing thieves.
6. We will give special rewards for great accomplishments or actions that benefit the guild in a large way, (such as recruiting members), in the form of medals, which can be viewed in the medal cases, located in the members section.
7. Help any member of the DETH guild you meet. In this case the entire guild profits, so there is valid reason for the "freebie." If the person you are aiding gets overly demanding, charge half what you would a client.
8. You must pick a word and add "Deth" to the end or beginning of your character name. This will allow other guild members to recognize you. Only those people promoted to enforcers are allowed to have "individual" names. Examples of acceptable names are: CrimsonDeth, DethWind, SpawnDeth, GreatDeth, ect.... Do not add any crap after or before your name, as it makes looking you up on the b-net difficult. (Crap= !@+-=#$%^&*{}[].():;~). Keep it to the name and "Deth."
9. You can be a member of any other guild, providing it is not an enemy faction or it's requirements do not conflict with ours. Name requirements can be "bent" slightly in this case - but not much.
.
Benefits:
1. Members can download any items, trainers, or maps from the various archives for their personal use.
2. You can count other members to protect or aid you in times of need.
3. Upon reaching the rank of Hunter, one can petition an enforcers to accompany them on a mission. A member must schedule this two days in advance by e-mailing one of the Great Hunters. The request will be served on a basis of availability of the enforcer and rank points of the applicant. The enforcer will accompany the hunter as long as possible, and will dedicate himself to serving his [the hunter's] whims. Bad treatment of an enforcer will result in him leaving or attacking the hunter.
4. A member can "pull rank" on a lower ranking member of the guild. Compliance is an optional issue, but the ranking officer can lodge a complaint to one of the Great Hunters should the lower ranking soldier choose to disobey. The situation will be thoroughly reviewed, with possibly disciplinary measures taken. Care must be exercised with this benefit, and ranking officers can be punished for unfair, unreasonable, or abusive demands.
5. Upon reaching the rank of Assassin, a DETH member may requisition hacked equipment from the Great Hunters. A date and a meeting place will be arranged by e-mail. Only the extreme high end hacked gear will be denied, (as it is used as a reward for exceptional service to the guild).
6. At the rank of Merc, a member receive a +10% bonus to the points earned. This is added separately after the total points for the week are tallied.
7. After achieving the rank of Hunter, one can petition to become an Enforcer, a kind of secret service for the higher ranking DETH guild members. It is a great honor to serve amongst the enforcers, some of the most elite players in the DETH guild. Applicants will be tested on a basis of their skill and ingenuity. Those that pass will be given any legit equipment they want, and will have the privilege of taking a singular name.
8. As a guild member, you are entitled to a vote upon any future changes or amendments to the rules. Your rank will determine the number of votes you are worth. From this point on, any rules or regulations change will be followed by an e-mail. Each member can vote for or against the regulation. If those opposed outweigh those voting for it, the rule will be scrapped. You must reply to the e-mail no later than TWO days after the sending of the mailer to have your vote count. A tie will result in a revote 1 day later.
9. At the rank of Hunter, DETH members are given access to the vault, where they can acquire special files and archived materials.
10. Members are added to the semi-weekly mailer, filled with important guild news, events, and programs.
11. The great hunters are the chief administrators of the DETH Guild. They are in charge of much of the day-to-day organization, planning, and also, aiding the other members of the guild in their endevors. The great hunters are the most prominent signs of the guild's power, and as such, MUST be highly visible, professional gamers. (I.e. playing any of our sanctioned games ALOT). The position itself, is determined by popular election, as the masses of the guild determine who will lead them. Of those that make the pre-requisites, the top five in the polls will be given the position of Great Hunter. Once elected, the Great Hunters will hold their position for 4 months, or until removed. Should they become inactive, a majoriy vote of the Hunters can expell them from their position. No longer will inactivity be tolerated among the upper echathlons. (Mitigating circumstances can be considered). After their term is over, the Great Hunters will leave office, and a new election will be held. A former Great Hunter may run again if he wishes. During election time, we will allow candidates to post a total of SIX messages on the Forum, using any colors, graphics, slogans, or mottos they are capable of displaying. Canadites may send out a total of TWO mass mailings, detailing their views, stands on issues, etc. Use them tactically, but SPAMING IS NOT PERMITTED, under threat of removal from the running.

The pre-recs the Administrators will consider when choosing who will run are:
A. You must have 500,000 points.
B. You must have displayed a tendency toward level headed decisions, even when under duress.
C. You must NOT have a habit of bossing others around, abusing authority, and generally pissing others off.
D. You must pass a background check by the DethHunters.
E. You must be an active member, having attended at least two meetings in the last month, as well as having been a member of the guild for at LEAST 6 months prior to running.
F. You MUST be visible in at least ONE of the guild sanctioned games, playing frequently, organizing games, helping members, ect.
G. You must submit a weekly report, (can be pretty much anything you can think of or want to talk about), to be mailed out to the members in the form of a weekly mailer.

12. The Administrators will choose a guild member to be placed in charge of the mailer, a weekly publication that will inculde tips, quotes, status reports, hardware recomendations, bounties, and anything else guild related. We will choose this person based upon his dedication, maturity, and his ability to consistently deliver the mailer on a weekly basis.
