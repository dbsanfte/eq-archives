Barbarian Player V.S. Player:
VS
Primary Skills: Jump attack, whirlwind, battle command, whirlwind, passive skills, battle orders

Tactics: Fighting the amazon is only a matter of catching her. She can dodge, she can run, but in the end, one sad fact remains - one whirlwind, jump, or strait up melee hit will kill her. If she tries to melee, well, she did half your work for you. Just keep quaffing potions as needed and run her down. Sword and shield barbs are the only way to go here. Polearm or lance masters get slaughtered by the amazon's guided arrow. Wearing a Death's Guard is also critical, as most amazon's wear an Eye of Etlich, and if they can chill you, the battle is over.

*Probability of Victory: 7 of 10 matches
VS

Tactics: Well, strait up here - the first one to land a successful whirlwind dictates the outcome of this one. Often, both barbarians will kill each other in these types of duels. Weapon speed will probably not play a factor, but missile barbs are just flat out dead. Try to launch a jump attack to stun and close on your opponent, then a quick whirlwind follow up will end things. Pike, maul, polearm, axe, and throwing barbs are at a serious disadvantage over sword and shield barbs.

*Probability of Victory: 5 of 10 matches Primary Skills: Jump attack, whirlwind, battle command, whirlwind, passive skills, battle orders
VS

Tactics: One of the hardest fights for a barb to win is v.s. necromancer. The iron golem is your BANE, avoid it, it's thorns will kill you fast - and since it is a monster ability, (the golem counts as a monster), it is NOT reduced to 1/10 damage like a paladin's thorns - at a mere 150% return, any melee contact with a lvl 2 iron golem is suicide. Complicating things, there is usually a ton of skeletal mages about, getting in your way - and necromancer's are notorious for ducking behind their minions at the last second. You can still one hit kill a necromancer, but you will be very busy dodging 350+ damage bone spirits. If he uses Iron Maiden, don't even THINK of trying to whirlwind, just head back to town and talk to a healer. Battle orders is a must before this fight, and stack every piece of speed enhancing gear you can find. Many necromancer's use a tower shield, so you may need to land several whirwinds before one connects for a fatal blow.

*Probability Of Victory: 2 of 10 matches Primary Skills: Jump attack, whirlwind, battle command, whirlwind, passive skills, battle orders
VS

Tactics: The paladin lacks your melee muscle, but be cautious - a charge + concentrate can still spell instant death. As can a lvl 20+ thorns. Against a thorn paladin, go to town and buy a very low damage weapon. This will allow you to survive the damage being reflected back at you. Jump attack is also viable v.s. a thorns paladin, but landing a blow is very difficult. However, thorns damage is NOT reflected on a successful hit. Also beware of hammer paladins - they can creat a field of whirling death, and a boffed whirlwind through this region is almost assuredly fatal. Be paitent, and wait for him to burn his mana dry - he can only cast so many blessed hammers, and a few feignted charges will leave him a sitting duck. Reflexes will determine this fight, as both parties can perform one-hit slayings. The edge goes to the barb because of his superior combat skills and passives that are constantly active.

*Probability Of Victory: 7 of 10 matches Primary Skills: Jump attack, whirlwind, battle command, whirlwind, passive skills, battle orders
VS

Tactics: A barbarian v.s. a sorceress is fighting an uphill battle. For starters. wear a Death's Guard, or a funeral gown,. A sorceress will usually have thunderstorm active, which will punish you for any missed whirwinds, and she will be teleporting about, zipping away to safety whenever you draw near. Usually a sorceress will teleport in visible range, and wait, using herself be bait. As you charge in, she will launch several frozens orbs, then telport in a zig-zag pattern, as you whirwind through empty space. A few of these attack will finish even a very high vitality, battle orders enhanced barbarian. A lance or pole arm, combined with the armor blinkbats form can go a long ways here. To win, you will need to concentrait on ruining a sorceress's timing with overwhelming speed and range. Try to fly in and feign a few attacks, pulling out just as the sorceress unleashes her orbs and teleports away. Frozen orb can be out-run if enough fast walk gear is worn, and after several barrages followed by teleporting, a sorceress has to start playing more conservative, or she will totally burn her mana dry. Lagg is your ally, and your best hope of killing a sorceress.

*Probability Of Victory: 2 of 10 matches Primary Skills: Jump attack, whirlwind, battle command, whirlwind, passive skills, battle orders
