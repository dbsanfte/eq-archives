In a battle of technology, the protoss have an undisputed edge. At the pinnacle of their power, a protoss unit can throttle any other species warrior(s). A hoard of fully upgraded protoss war ships are all but unstoppable. Terrans and zerg alike can do little but watch in horror as their units get burned from the skies.

Tactic#1: INVINCIBLE ARMADA
Submitted By: SteelDeth

1: Begin by ordering all your probes to harvest. Immediately begin building probes.

2: As soon as you get 100 mineral, (while keeping a good stream of probes warping out), order a probe to build a pylon. By the time this is finished, you will have another 150. Build a gateway right next to your minerals. As soon as it is completed, warp in a zealot and begin constructing two cybernetics cores. This one zealot will be able to handle four marines or six zerglings if you use some workers as interference.

3: After the first zealot is finished, build two more. Also, warp in four more probes and a refinery. Have the workers gather resources. As funds become available, create Four more pylons and a cybernetics core. Begin building four stargates ASAP. Send your zealots on scouting missions. You will NEED to expand, so be on the lookout for more GAS.

4: At your cybernetics core, begin upgrading your air weapons and armor. As your stargates form, order them to make scouts as fast as possible. You should have the funds for four if you kept up worker production. Warp in a fleet beacon. Also consider making a few photon cannons for defense.

5: Build four more scouts and begin researching the carrier and scout upgrades at the fleet beacon. Build pylons as you need them. Your zealots should have found the enemy encampment by now. Continue to upgrade your air attack. You want to keep building workers this whole time. You want 2.5 workers per mineral vein. Build another cybernetics core.

6: As soon as the last scout is finished, warp in four carriers. By the time the last one arrives, your air weapons should have reached maximum power. Fill the carrier's hangers as soon as the funding becomes available. Construct 8 corsairs, and research their powers. They will build fast. use the fleet to defend until the corsairs are full charge

7: When the corsairs are fully charged, send them toward your attacker's base. Order the scouts to follow the carriers. Use the scouts for obliterating opposition fighters, as their ground attack is rather lack-luster. With the carriers, you can easily overcome ground attackers, and the corsairs will ruin chokes. When you actually get into their base, your carriers will turn your enemies fortress into a bloodbath. Hit their starport first, then the barracks and supplies. Fan out the scouts and search for expansions.

Defense #1: Stopping the INVINCIBLE ARMADA

 
  A protoss player will want a to quickly build a forge and a cybernetics core. Upgrade the dragoon range, ground attack and armor as fast as possible. Construct dragoons in large quantities. Also, be sure to acquire a healthy force of templar, with psionic storm researched. When the carriers hit your base, psionic storm them before the temps get obliterated. Even if this doesn't kill the carriers, it will put a nice ding in their armor. Now move the dragoons in and batter the left overs. Press a hard counter attack to win.

 
  As a terran, you will want to engage them from the air. Any ground units you have will be unable to cope with an attack like this. Begin by building a starport with control tower upgrade. Research the cloaking and reactor upgrades. Build 12 wraiths, and wait for them to attack you. Go after the scouts first, killing or driving them off. Remember, your ships last only as long as they are cloaked. (Scouts RAPE wraiths). Crush their carriers after wiping out the escorting scouts. Even if the cloak is out by now, 12 wraiths can handle the carriers. Lockdown is also effective.

 
 The zerg are best able to deal with this attack. Devourers backed up liberally with mutalisks and scourges can deal out more punishment than a toss fleet can deal with. Also, very creative use of dark swarm and a sizable mass of hydralisks can cause problems, but watch out for the corsairs, who may d-web the center of your swarms. 8 corsairs can cover a sizable area with disruption webs. You may end up watching your whole base fall apart while your defenders pick their butt....

Tactic#2: DEAR GOD THE DAMAGE
Submitted By: SilverDeth

To pull this, you will need to be in a team map, as you will be dedicating enough of your facilities to this attack as to be very vulnerable to virtually any offensive. So, right off, we are assuming that you at VERY least have an ally that can quickly respond to an attack against you. (Preferably, your ally has a large fleet massed over your main :-p). That aside, I'll tell you how to make an attack so lethal that you are virtually guaranteed to destroy whoever you use it on. Makes a nuke look like a firecracker. You will need the facilities to create 12 reavers, 4 arbiters, and 6 templar as quickly as possible, as well as researching all their applicable upgrades. Assuming your allies are doing a reasonable job guarding your defenseless ass, you should be able to get the required materials in less than 13 min. As soon as the four arbiters have 150 energy, you will want to make 16 hallucinations of the arbiters with 4 of the templar. Mix the four real arbiters liberally with the illusions, and make a mad dash for the middle of your opponents base - be sure the arbiters are hotkeyed to 1,2,3,4, and that the 12 reavers and 2 charged templar are clumped in tight groups. No matter WHAT kind of anti air, at least two of those arbiters will get through. Recall your mass of robotic deth into the middle of whatever you opponent has. It doesn't really matter what you're recalling them into, if each reaver only gets off 2 shots, (Virtually guaranteed), you will level half of his base. Aim the templar at his workers and psi storm away. Between the two of them you should be able to get every worker the sap has. Use whatever energy you have left to maim your opponent's army. Be building dragoons and corsairs while this whole attack is going on to use as a mop up force for whatever manages to survive. (Which WON'T be much). While your are insulting the idiot you just beat, remember to thank you buddies for keeping you ALIVE this whole time.
