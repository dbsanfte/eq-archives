What are some of the features of the expansion pack?

1): A quest that will allow you to socket ANY item you have. The number of sockets has yet to be determined.

2): Rare and magical items can have a prefix called "mechanist's," which grants a set number of sockets.

3): Hirelings and NPC's can now be equipped with items, gain levels faster, and you can give them potions to use when they get battered down.

4): The number of sockets on items has, in most cases doubled - imagine a SIX diamond shield....

5): Items that could previously not be socketed will now come with sockets - such as rings, amulets, armor, boots, and gloves.

6): Gems powers will be increased, and a new class of gem has been added - runes. Runes, when combined in proper order, will transform the item into a "unique item." However, the item will remain exceptional, thus, you could use it to create a lance with the powers of the Griswald's Edge.

7): Two new classes of items have been added - Elite and Ethereal. Elite items are a step above Exceptional weapons, inflicting two times the damage of Exceptionals, with higher requirements to match. Ethereal items have stats in excess of EIGHT TIMES the power of the normal item. However, Ethereal items have a set durability, and cannot EVER be repaired.

8): Two new character classes - the Assassin and the Druid. The druid is a hybrid between the necromancer, barbarian, and the sorceress. The assassin is a Mortal Kombat style fighter, who's special moves are linked into finishing attacks and special moves. Pure finesse this one.....

9): The hordric cube has dozens of new recipes, some of which claim to be useful.

10): BARBARIAN hirelings.
