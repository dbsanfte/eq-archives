Dark Age of Camelot class benchmarks and skill directories.
Warrior's are the quinessential tank. A good starting class in DAoC, the warrior is simple and strait-forward. Warriors have the most armor, hit points, and durability of any midgard class - and of arguably any class in DAoC. Warriors are loved by PvE raid leaders for their ability to absorbe damage - while kicking out respectable sums in return. While a warrior's lack of ranged attack is very limiting in RvR, with his Tetsuto realm abiltiy and the battlemaster skill set, a warrior is an unbelievable guardian of casters and support classes. His damage output with a 2 handed weapon also makes him a respectable addition to a melee train.
