*************************************
       Section 4: The Codes
*************************************

Make sure you input the master code into your GSA so the codes will actually
work :) A note on the card codes, they don't stop you from getting cards, they
just keep your total card amounts down to 0. Use the no Law code to get rid of
laws :-P. If you notice that one of my codes doesn't work then send me an 
email at Vegikachu@hotmail.com and I'll fix it. Also make sure that the 
version you input are the correct one. Most of the Japanese codes won't work
on the American game and vice-versa.

There will be some repeating of myself until I have the time to re-do the
layout my FAQ to make for less repeat codes.

One more thing, if you are trying to use my codes on an emulator please note
that you have to delete the space between the codes in order for them to work.
