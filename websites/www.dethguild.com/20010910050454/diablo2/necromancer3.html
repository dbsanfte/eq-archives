Necromancer Player V.S. Player:
VS
Primary Skills: Bone wall, bone spirit, iron golem, fire golem, skeletal mages, iron maiden, passive skills, decrepify, bone prison

Tactics: This is a BAD fight for the necromancer. Trapping an amazon in a wall only gives her something to leech life from, while her slow missile ability will drag bone spells to slug speed. Her seeking missiles will track a necromancer, and his useful curses, like Iron Maiden and decrepify will be mostly useless. A fire golem's holy fire attack can help, as it is the only ranged attack that will work well against an amazon. Your best shot is during the amazon's initial approach. She will have to get very close to cast slow missile, and during this time, you have the best shot at killing her. 2 Bone spirits will finish an amazon, but they have to hit before she gets off slow missile. Wear the unique armor twitchthrow, and a Siagon's guard to stand a fighting chance against her lethal arrows. Be sure to remember to wear a Death's Sash, which will save you from chilling damage caused by the Eye of Etlich. Decrepify can help in this battle, but an amazon's guided arrows are fast and decisive.

*Probability of Victory: 2 of 10 matches

VS
Primary Skills: Bone wall, bone spirit, iron golem, fire golem, skeletal mages, iron maiden, passive skills, decrepify, bone prison

Tactics: Fighting a barbarian is simply a matter of map usage. Don't let him get on screen with you. Keep running off screen, blasting bone spirits in his general direction. If he closes, a large number of skeletal mages and an iron golem will see to it his stay on this world is brief. If you expect a whirlwind, cast iron maiden, and laugh as the barb chews himself up on your skeletal mages. If he accidentally lands a blow on the iron golem, (which is considered a monster, so it's thorns is NOT subject to the 1/10 damage penalty), he has just killed himself. If you are short on skelemages, try to stay as near to your iron golem as possible. This will REALLY make the barbarian reluctant to whirlwind at you. Additionally, the golem will absorbe hits should the barbarian decide to kamikazi you, giveing you a better chance to block the leathal whirlwind. Or, in a pinch, you can summon bone walls in from of a whirlwinding barb, (providing he is cursed with iron maiden), and laugh as he is slain "by an evil force."

*Probability of Victory: 8 of 10 matches

VS
Primary Skills: Bone wall, bone spirit, iron golem, fire golem, skeletal mages, iron maiden, passive skills, decrepify, bone prison

Tactics: This fight is all about efficient use of bone spells. The necromancer that can most successfully trap, clog up, and curse, will win. Getting trapped in a bone prison is sure death, a flurry of bone spells are sure to follow. Bone spear may be more useful than spirit v.s. a General, but one would need the intellect of a wood mite to take on a necromancer while he still commands a signifigant number of revives.

*Probability Of Victory: 5 of 10 matches

Primary Skills: Bone wall, bone spirit, iron golem, fire golem, skeletal mages, iron maiden, passive skills, decrepify, bone prison

Tactics: The necromancer makes a paladin his plaything. With an iron golem to intercept a charge, all a necromancer has to do is trap a paladin in a bone prison, then victory is only 4 bone spirits away. A paladin's defensive auras are useless against the necromancer's piercing bone magic, rendering him, well, worm chow. Be wary of the charge + vigor or charge + holy freeze. They are QUITE deadly against people at the higher skill levels. Decrepify will turn a super speed paladin charging into a slug, which you can then pummle into oblivion.

VS
Primary Skills: Bone wall, bone spirit, iron golem, fire golem, skeletal mages, iron maiden, passive skills, bone prison

Tactics: This is a nasty fight for the necromancer. The sorceress can level almost all of the necromancer's minions before they get a SHOT off. Not only that, bone spirit is totally absorbed by her energy shield, and she will likely as not teleport before being struck anyway. Frozen orb, hydras, blizzard, all that mass destruction will leave you a charred corpse 9 times out of ten. This is one of the few instances where poision nova would be very useful, as the damage goes directly to a sorceress's health, not her huge mana reserve. Inflict a nasty shot of poision and RUN. If you are playing a "no potion" game, one poision nova at lvl 5 will leave a sorceress with 1 hp in about 30 seconds. A fire golem can be a good ally as well, for his holy fire, while not a HUGE ammount of damage, is still an effective way to harm a sorceress.

When creaming people as a necromancer, always remember, bone spirit is your bread and butter. It is devastatingly powerful, and it seeks targets to boot. Even a barbarian will die in a few hits, so this is DEFINITELY the skill of choice for you killers out there. Understanding what a necro does well, all other skills need to be geared toward making his [your] trump card more effective. To this end, remember your golems, skeleton mages, and the curse "decrepify." The various golems will, at very least, keep the heat off of you for a bit, (iron golem is especially good for those pesky barbs), and can always be summoned in the event corpses are unavailable. Skeleton mages are mainly fodder to absorb hits and area effect spells, (like frozen orb), but the poison and cold damage is very handy for debilitating your mark. Decrepify attacks every statistic - making an opponent's abilities worse - tons worse. Most notably, it slows them down to a crawl, making your REAL gravy - bone spirit - more useful. Grabbing many +1 to skills items is a must, but DO NOT neglect your resistance, ans your speed in the process. Goldskin is too heavy to wear in a duel, so blinkbat's form or twitchthrow is the preferred armor of a dueling necromancer - let those damn witches have their silks. Bone walls and bone prison are well worth investing in, as they not only impede movement, but create yet another target. If you can trap an opponent in a wall, they had better be fast with teleport or jump attack - as your flurry of bone spirits will cross their eyes and dot their t's. If it's still moving, you haven't shot enough bone spirits at it.
