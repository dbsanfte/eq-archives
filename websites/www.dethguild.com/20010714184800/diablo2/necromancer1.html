Necromancer Overview:

A specialist in poisoning and summoning, the necromancer is a powerful force on the b.net, combining ease of play with potent abilities. The necromancer is often nicknamed "The General" by on-line players - due to his tendency to collect a massive, lag causing army of minions that clogs up the screen, grind framerates into the single digits, and utterly pulverize any monster that shows up on the screen. One important facet of the necromancer to consider - his class is the most dependent on "cross" applications of a broad range of his abilities. While most other classes can "one-trick-poney" one 30th level ability, jack it into the 20+ range, and ride it forever, a necro MUST combine his bone, summons, and curses effectively to stand a chance. But, when he does this effectively, he is well-nigh invulnerable. (Only network lagg kills a high level necromancer). A necromancer's priorities are getting gear that adds bonuses to skills, and equipment that yields a high level of magic resistance . No class benefits as much from a +1 to all as much as a necromancer. He must spread his points about, so a +1 bonus could be as many as fifteen free skill points. Additionally, with each bonus, the number of minions a "necro" can summon increases. +1 means 3 extra minions to a high level character, and that is plenty of extra destruction. The resistance is a matter of common sense. With your minion army intercepting all the melee grunts, the only attacks leveled directly at a necromancer will be magical ones, so a high degree of resistance will greatly magnify his life span. A necromancer's strength lies in his ability to deal with the monsters of Diablo 2. Without question, the necromancer is the best equipped to handle the general rabble, and even high lag games are playable, as his minions continue to battle even after a screen freeze. For this reason, even the legions of hell melt like a snowball in.... well.... hell. But even the necromancer has a weakness - bosses. There is no "easy" method for a necromancer to dispatch a boss, especially on the hell difficulty. He is forced to transform his golem into a sacrificial lamb, while he casts iron maiden and slowly allows the boss to destroy itself. Nothing is as slow and arduous as boss killing with a necromancer, even bone spirit is pathetically underpowered in 8 player games. As a spell castor, the necromancer should be interested in allocating as many spare skill points as possible into mana. Lacking the warmth powers of the sorceress, a necromancer will constantly be challenged to keep his mana orb in the blue, so an extremely high energy count is critical. Also, do not overlook the value of strength. Unlike a sorceress, the necromancer has to "get down in it" with his minions, so he will need some decent protection. Plan on spending at least 100 character points on strength. Divide the remainder between vitality and energy, and you should have a powerful, yet durable character, who can dish out plenty, and even take some.
