Here in the Game Info section, you can find tips and tactics for mastering the five character classes of diablo 2. Included here is a general overview of the each class, a listing of the skills available to them, and great player v.s player tactics. Additionally, you can find out almost any useable piece of info you will need to dominate your foes and allies alike.
A dexterous and versitile warrior, the Amazon is a master of evasion. She is a skilled combatent, relying on cunning and agility to wear enemies down.

The master of speed and stealth, the assassin strikes from nowhere to dispatch her foes, and then vanishes back into the shadows.
A brutal warrior, the barbarian is the king of melee combat. His weapons are his brute strength and his iron constitution - he uses them to grind enemies to dust.
Comanding the forces of nature and animal compantions, the druid can also transform into a raging were-beast to rend apart all who oppose him.
The Necromancer is a scheming poisoner and summoner of minions. He can quickly summon a huge army to do his bidding - and destroy his adversaries.
A noble party leader, the paladin is a selfless warrior in all respects. His true strength lies in his auras, and they power they grant his allies.
The elements are the sorceress playthings. She can conjure fire, ice, or electricity to obliterate those foolhardy enough to challenge her.
