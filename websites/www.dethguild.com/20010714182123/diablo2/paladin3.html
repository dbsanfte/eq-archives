Paladin Player V.S. Player:
VS
Primary Skills: Charge, vigor, holy freeze, concentration, blessed hammer, holy shield

Tactics: Paladin's are natural amazons destroyers - IF built correctly. An enhanced block shield, combined with a lvl 20 holy shield skill will reduce her guided arrow to a mere 25% success rate. Vigor will help you close with a terrifying burst of speed, and charge will annilate the amazon in one or two strikes. She will get two, maybe three shots off before you have closed into melee range, at which point, the amazon is in grave danger. Once you have her on the run, switch from vigor to holy freeze for a breif instant, then back to vigor. Not even Death's Guard or Hawkmail can save the zon from turning blue, and now she has virtually no hope of escape. Try to maximize your fastest hit recovery gear, as the most dangerous time for a paladin is the initial approach. You cannot afford to get stun locked, or even worse, frozen, while you hurtle at the amazon like a guided missile.

*Probability of Victory: 8 of 10 matches
VS

Tactics: A charge and the concentrate aura will guarantee a one or 2 hit kill, but the difficulty will be landing the blow. A better tactic would be to activate thorns, and let the barbarian's own damage slay himself. One must still be careful however, as many barbs will purposefully equipt low damage weapons - enough to kill you, but not so much that they cannot survive, care of battle orders. Additionally, jump attacks by-pass thorns, and a lax pally might find himself crushed. Blessed hammer + concentration can also be effective, filling the air with damaging magic, much a a sorceress does, then running about, letting the hammers batter the barbarian into hamburger on a missed whirlwind. Care must be taken not to consume your whole mana pool however, leaving you a sitting target.

*Probability of Victory: 3 of 10 matches

VS

Primary Skills: Charge, vigor, holy freeze, concentration, blessed hammer, holy shield

Tactics: HARD hard fight here. The necromancer will try to trap a paladin on a bone prison, so it is imperative that he keeps moving as fast and erratically as possible. Vigor is a must, especially if facing chilling skeletal mages, and when charging, avoid the iron golem or die. Holy freeze may slow things down enough to land a charge against a necromancer, but, the odds are NOT good of picking him from among minions, and even worse on not getting trapped in a bone prison. A selfless charge, wielding a 2 handed weapon, fastets boots, and blinkbats, amplified with vigor is your best chance against the necromancer. Be VERY leary of decrepify, as it will turn you into a snail.

*Probability Of Victory: 1 of 10 matches

VS

Primary Skills: Charge, vigor, holy freeze, concentration, blessed hammer, holy shield

Tactics: An interesting battle to be sure, at least this one the people stand on equal footing. These battles are very amusing, as they usually involve both paladins squaring off, and charging one another. The one with the better dexterity, higher blocking shield, and most damaging weapon kills the other. Amusing. Holy freeze is very useful for tipping the odds, and keep-away games can be played with blessed hammer.

*Probability Of Victory: 5 of 10 matches Primary Skills: Charge, vigor, holy freeze, concentration, blessed hammer, holy shield
VS

Primary Skills: Charge, vigor, holy freeze, concentration, blessed hammer, holy shield

Tactics: A sorceress is trouble, all rolled up in a cute little green package. Her powerful elemental attacks will leave you chilled, slow, and dead. The use of teleport will making even a vigor enhanced charge difficult to land, but it is your best bet. Do not follow her into rocky terrain, as she can play teleport games around rocks and debris. Try too keep the fight in the open, where you can maximize the speed of charge, and minimize the role that teleport plays int he battle. Holy freeze will also go a long ways towards winning, especially with a charge + vigor follow up.

*Probability Of Victory: 5 of 10 matches

In a fight, [as a paladin], your auras will dictate the course of the battle. Each aura has a use, and when utilized at a grossly appropriate moment, will carry the day. Using the wrong one.... well, Van Gogh time bud. (For you uneducated louts out there Van Gogh cut off his ear). Vigor will assure you a crazy move speed, (add Death's Guard to keep from getting frozen), Concentration will cause charge and blessed hammer to become instant kills, and thorns will make those barbs regret spending a single point in whirlwind. When used correctly, auras can make a paladin absurdly powerful, but they need to BE USED AT THE CORRECT TIMES. Thorns does diddly against a sorceress, and salvation against a barb is amusing. Combat auras that really stink against monsters are very effective player versus player. Holy freeze in particular. Offensive auras may be pathetic versus a hell level beasts, but they can be quite effective for whittling down an evasive sorceress - or, as a means of debilitating a necromancer while you run away from bone spirits. Holy freeze is superb against players, as at IS NOT BLOCKABLE, even by Death's Guard, and after getting placed in the deep freeze, your challenger is charge + concentration bait. Keep your resists high - you need to be able to get survive a hail of magic to land that one fatal blow.
