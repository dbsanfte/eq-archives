Paladin Overview:

The paladin class has changed drastically since the release of Diablo 2 in June of 2000. At first, he was dependent on extremely heavy armor and high blocking shield to survive. This left the paladin with very low vitality, poor melee attacking abilities, and HUGELY item dependent. A player found his "pally" badly outmatched by the swarms in act 4 hell mode, and quickly gave up on him. Fortunately, a new tactic came to light, involving the use of the skills blessed hammer and aura concentration. These two powers combine to make the paladin a powerful fighter versus the mob, and 10x increased his survivability in the later difficulties. Points are now redirected from strength to vitality and mana, and wizardly gear, such as fast cast rings and amulets, as well as scepters, once laughed at, are now considered the finest gear for a paladin on the fast track. A superb "Hammerdin," (as they are called), outfit would probably consist of: a +2 scepter with fastest cast, frostburn gauntlets, a pair of rare fast cast rings, a +1 fast cast amulet, a tarnhelm, the belt nightsmoke, heavenly garb - goldskin - silks of the victor for armor, (each works well), a 3 diamond pavaise or steeclash, and a set of tearhanches. As for stat point distribution, a hammer paladin will want to balance life with mana. He has a great deal more life than other spellcastors, (as he is begin used as such), yet his mana is extremely low. Once 135 strength has been reached, it is wise to devote the rest of a paladin's points to vitality and magic. Without saying the skills concentration and blessed hammer will need to be maxed, as well as the skill holy shield. The auras redemption and meditation at medium levels are also extremely useful for keeping the mana orb in the blue, a challenge indeed, considering the paladin;s low mana pool. This new style of playing paladins has redefined the character class, and transformed it into a competitive, and FUN choice to play. The recent emergence of numerous hammerdins into the ladder rankings is proof enough of this.
