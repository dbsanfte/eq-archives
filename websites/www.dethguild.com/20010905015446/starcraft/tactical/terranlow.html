Terrans are often seen as the under dogs in starcraft. Usually, this is because they are played improperly. Most people "turtle" when playing terrans, trying to out-defend an opponent. But the terrans have a surprising capacity to go on the offensive, and their combination of low and high end units make them exceptionally versatile.

Tactic#1: "GIMME SUMTHIN TA SHOOT"
Submitted By: FugBastard

1: Start by ordering all your SCVs to harvest. Do not begin construction on another SCV. Save the 50 mineral at this point.

2: As soon as you get 150 minerals, order one of the SCV's to build a barracks. By the time this is finished, you will have another 150. Build a bunker right next to your minerals. Make a marine in the barracks, and place him in the bunker. This will hold off any enemies rushes.

3: Build four more SCV's and 15 marines. Construct a refinery, and build an academy. Research stim packs. As the funds become available, make another barracks. Place another three marines in the bunker. Build a supply depot or two.

 

4: Make 12 firebats and add a comsat station to your command center. Begin scouting with your army. Use the comsat to home in on your opponent. Speed is critical. If the enemy gets too dug in, your units are going to get blown into bloody pieces. Be sure to make another supply depot at this time.

5: Move your units into the enemy base, being careful to keep the firebats in the front to take on the enemy melee fighters. If you see zealots, hydralisks, or bunkers, shoot up with the stim packs. After destroying their primary defenses, split the group, ordering the marines to hold position close to the enemy barracks, while the firebats toast their harvesters. Any new units that are unfortunate enough to waddle out of the barracks will find themselves glorified target practice. Build replacements as needed, keeping the pressure on. While fighting, take extra money and build an engineering bay. Begin upgrading armor to give your marines one more hit from lurkers or dark templar.

6: Though you may loose soldiers at a scary rate, you are getting farther and farther ahead. While you are shooting up his harvesters, your's are adding to your gas and mineral reserves. While you pull farther and farther into the lead, your victim....er.....opponent slips into defeat.

Defense #1: Stopping "GIMME SUMTHIN TA SHOOT"

 
 You'll need to build a forge right off to survive this kind of attack. Construct 4 photon cannons and a few dragoons. The stimmed marines and firebats will do unkind things to your expensive zealots, so keep them off the front lines. The dragoon range upgrade is a life saver. Once these defenses are in place, you force them to find another attack type. Or, just get a single templar, and fry the bastards if you don't feel like fighting....

 
 Bunkers, bunkers, bunkers. Build three right off, and fill em with marines. Be sure to get the range upgrade at the academy. When the bad guys come calling, your bunkers will turn their attacks into massacres. Be sure to keep them close to your supplies, or your opponent may just circle them and continue on to your defenseless base. Or, punish them for the lack of medics with your own.

 
 The key here is to beat them to the punch. Get to your opponents before they get to you. Masses of stimmed marines and firebats kill zerg like penicillin kills germs. Cluster zerglings and hydralisks and research the burrow upgrade. Bury your zerglings, and let the hydralisks handle the firebats. After the firebats croak, dig up your zerglings and mob the marines. Keep doing this until you can get a large cluster of sunken colonies built. They are really effective against human units. Try getting on the offensive - humans are good a bleeding you out of money. Three lurkers also kills terran-scum darn nicely.

Tactic#2: Newbie Tips
Submitted By: WhiteDeth

There are two common mistakes newbies make when putting down Turrets-Misplacement and Over Turreting. Both of these are simple mistakes to make, but against a good player, mean certain death.

Misplacement of Turrets may be more common than Over Turreting, but it is just as lethal. Misplaced Turrets leave you open to Scout raids, An all-out air attack (common ones are BCs and Carriers), or just about any drops you can think of.

First of all, Turrets should NOT be placed directly around the edge of an island map. This is defense against drops and Guardians. Make the Turrets 2 spaces away from the edge. In between the Turrets and the edge of the map, place Goliaths (almost completely Guard proof, it takes a  a lot to break through). The Charon Booster upgrade is a necessity here, and the 2 spaces gives Goliath a little maneuverability in case of a breach in another part of your defense. Also, it gives the a little maneuverability vs a successful reaver drop, where mobility is key. (A reaver dropped successfully against this defense, however, is a rare feat, and anyone who accomplishes it deserves to be playing on something more than Big Game Hunters =}) Don't put Siege Tanks behind the Turrets in case of a drop, because they end up taking out Turrets for the enemy, and Goliaths to boot, but do use Marines or something else able to provide a little support for your Goliaths.

Turret over placement can be so.newbyish. The typical 3 rows of missile turrets around a BGH island is a sure sign you are playing someone who doesn't deserve to own starcraft. The only real problem with over placing Turrets is only a small one, really anything on the ground. Not only do Turrets do absolutely nothing against ground, but it takes some small amount of time to set up three rows of Turrets, time in which your enemy is racing up the tech tree, and if he scouted, pumping out Zealots or Tanks, or something else you wont stand much chance against. Mobile forces always have a higher priority.

Tactic#3: Bunker Placement
Submitted By: WhiteDeth

Bunkers have become a lot less necessary for Terrans in starcraft. Terrans have mobile power. Bunkers should only be used against human players. Whatever you do, do not rely on bunkers anymore. Put 1 or 2 marines in a bunkers near your bases entrance. 9 of 10 players will go straight for it, while with Medics, this is a deadly mistake. Bunkers strength is how they protect your minerals. Worker raiders have two choices here, and neither one is pretty. They can either take out the Bunkers that are killing them, or they can go after the workers and more likely get killed than kill. Those are the uses of bunkers. Anything else is pretty much a waste of time and money.

Tactic#4: "Strikeforce: Morituri"
Submitted By: ShadowDeth A.K.A. Deacon

Marines are great because they are cheap, they can attack both ground and air units, and with all the upgrades, they can decimate an enemy base in no time. This tactic works well when the enemy has decimated your more expensive vehicles and ships AND your only vespene gas refinery has been depleted. This assumes that you are still harvesting minerals and since Marines are cheap, this rush tactic should work most of the time.

Start by closing you base in to protect yourself from enemy rushes. Then if you don't have an extra barracks already, build one. Once that is built, and making sure your supply depots can support it, start training Marines like crazy. Have both barracks rally to a common juncture so that your strikeforce will be easier to manage.

Start with two sets of twelve Marines each hot-keyed to 1 and 2. It's really up to you how many stikeforces you want to cue up, but the longer you wait to attack, the more likely YOU will be rushed. I have found that the first two sets almost always survive to completion. However, I do have two more right at the entrance of the base I just invaded, just to be sure.

If there is a back way in, like an Academy, Supply Depot, Spawning Pool, basically a non-defensive structure that the opponent put up to impede traffic, pinpoint this. This is the reason for the many Marines, the faster you can break through this, the faster your forces can wreak havoc inside the base.

Use the MOVE icon to pinpoint key offensive placements, this allows the strikeforce to be focused and singular in the destruction of the enemy. This includes Bunkers, Sunken Colonies, Proton Cannons and any tank-like enemy forces, first and foremost. The move icon is especially useful because you can move your units right up to the "tanks" and with their longer range, they're going to have a hard time attacking you*. Then attack unit-creating structures like the Hydralisk Den, Barracks etc.

*Try going for pairs of Siege Tanks, because they'll probably aid you in destroying their own Tanks...along with you of course.

By this time your first strikeforce may have been whittled to half...send in the second wave of 24 Marines to mop up. Keep in mind that you have to hot-key each and every unit of twelve you create. THIS is important for success.

I have taken over many bases with just these hopped-up decked-out loyal Marines, so have fun.

Extra Tips: Be sure to back your Marines up with the Comsat Station. It's really sad to see ONE Ghost laying waste to two dozen Marines. Air defense units are last priority since you don't have any air units to be attacked. When in doubt, hit and run. This is a Strikeforce, not a slaughter.

Tactic#1: STOPPING A DARK TEMPLAR DROP
Submitted By: FreeMuRa

A tactic that has been kicking the snot out of terrans in Brood War is the much feared dark templar drop. The invisible assassins massacre terran units in a blow, and without a ground attacking detector, humans are easy prey for these deadly units. However, your salvation lies in the much ignored vulture. The spider mines this vehicle carries are capable of hitting any cloaked unit that wanders by, and the dark templar's slow attack rate guarantees that they will be unable to hit the mine before it kills them. Additionally, 4 vultures can easily kill four dark templar, and coupled with some clever comsatting, a suck unit can stop the otherwise insurmountable protoss advantage. So crank out a bunch of those 21st century hogs, and go mulch some templars. Also, try mining the protoss in their own base. Drop a few near his rally points. His units will blunder into the mines on their way to their destination. Drop a few gems near the other resource nodes. His probes wont set them off, and you get a free comsatt telling you where his expansions are. Rumor has it that reavers don't handle mines well either.

Submitted By: DevilSmurf

No matter what defense he's got you'll toast all those little workers before he can stop you, and odds are, the splash damage from his weapons will kill as many or more of his little miners as you.
