Dead Stuff
   


Fun day today; Terris and Vallon both gave up their loot for us. Terris was first up ... after the boring trek up through her guardians she went down easy - is it any wonder she comes dressed in purple and black? She gave up a couple Fearsome Midnight Cloaks - for some reason our clerics yelling "Proc!" sends the melee scrambling to collect regen items, so those were pretty contested rolls. Congrats to Dreamcatcher and Darcilla on some well-deserved back slot upgrades.

Moved to Tactics after, but only one Zek brother was up - quickly took care of that, and got a couple of nice weapons to fall. For once, no wake the dead pets to confuse things on Vallon. Congrats to Dumathin on a hammer as big as he is and to Bannin on the Obsidian Dagger's big scimitar brother.
