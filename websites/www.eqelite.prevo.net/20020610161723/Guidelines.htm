Guild Guidelines

 Article I

        Members shall treat all other inhabitants of Norrath in a fair, honest, and courteous manner.

 

 Article II

         We feel that all areas of Norrath are there for all players to enjoy, therefore we believe in sharing spawns whenever possible. If another person or group politely asks to share an area we have 'camped', we will do our best to work out some kind of agreement.  If we are the ones asking to share a camped area, we will abide by whatever decision the 'campers' make.

        Remember, 'camping' is NOT sanctioned by Sony/Verant and game GM's will not get involved in camping disputes  (See the PNP - Play Nice Policy).

 

 Article III

        Members shall conduct themselves in a manner that brings pride to this guild.  This means that profanity, dishonesty, discrimination and violation of any Sony/Verant policy will not be tolerated.

 

 Article IV

        Members must provide guild leadership with a valid email address, so we can contact you about guild news and events.

 

 Article V

        Newly recruited members will have 'probationary status'.  Full membership will be granted  to those who have proven that they can abide by these guidelines.

 

Officer Guidelines

Ø Officer status will be offered to those members who display loyalty and dedication to the members of this guild.  Examples of dedication include going out of your way to help someone, participating in guild events, and sharing information with others on the website forum.

 

Ø All characters of an officer that are members of this guild may hold officer status.  The honor is bestowed based on the deeds of the person, not a particular character.

 

Ø Officers must spend the majority of their playing time on characters that are in this guild.  Membership in other guilds is allowed,  but if you spend the majority of your time there, then you aren't  showing dedication to our members.

 

Ø Officers may recruit any player they feel is worthy of membership in our guild.  The inviting officer is responsible for informing guild leadership of the new members name, class, race, level, and email address. 

 

Ø Officers must display the highest standards of conduct at all times, in order to set an example for newer members, and to show the rest of Norrath what our guild represents.
