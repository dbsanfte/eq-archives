We then killed RZ and got Bert and CT both to 60% with a serious skeletal crew before calling the raid. So while it was disappointing to lose so much of our first day it was nice to see that we could get to Tier5 in one day on a weekday no less. We did in one day this week what took us both days last week so even with the bug we made good progress. Nice job Vita!
