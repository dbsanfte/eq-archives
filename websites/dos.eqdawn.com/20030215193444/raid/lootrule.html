Loot Rules

Each group on the raid will have designated looters. After the Raid the loot will be distributed.

Those who can use the loot for their main character or alt character on the raid, have first dibs on the loot. If there is more than one character that needs the loot, it will be rolled for by the characters that need it.

If no characters need the loot who are on the raid, it will be rolled for by those who want it to sell.

If no characters want or need it, it will be placed in the Guild Bank. Our bank character is Alliya's alt, Dummybank, in Kelethin.
