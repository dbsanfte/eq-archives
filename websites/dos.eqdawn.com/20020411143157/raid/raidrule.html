Dawn of Sovereignty Raid Policies

Attendance

It is hoped, but not required, that you attend as many raids as you can. The more people that participate in these events, the stronger the guild becomes as a whole.

If you do choose to attend, please notify the raid leaders as soon as possible prior to the raid that you are planning to go. This gives them time to coordinate and plan groups efficiently. All raids will be announced at least a week in advance. When signing up for a raid, either post on the message board, email the raid leader, or notify the raid leader in game that you will be there and state your current class and level if they are not aware. Level is important so they will know what spells/abilities you will have. If you have more than one guilded character, specify which one will be in attendance.

At least an hour prior to the raid it is preferred that everyone attending be waiting at the nearest port to the area and melees make arrangements to be bound in a convenient spot. Please be at the dungeon entrance at least 15-30min in advance. This is when raid groups will be formed and when people will be buffing up for their foray into the dungeon. If you are not present by the time the raid starts, things will start without you, so please be punctual. If you know you will be late, alert someone ahead of time so arrangements can be made. Chronic lateness for raids will not be tolerated. If you say you will be there please do so.

Lastly, most raids take a considerable chunk of time. Expect to spend at least a few hours at a raid. If you know you will have to leave early, please alert the leaders so it is not a surprise to them and arrangements can be made.

Groups

Groups will be planned in advance with a leader appointed to each one. Every attempt will be made to keep groups as balanced as possible. Raid participants are expected to follow the instructions of their group leader and keep all questions in group talk or /say. If you have a question for the raid leader, tell the group leader to pass it on. Guild chat should be reserved for group leaders or emergency shouts. Communication with others in groups should be kept to /tell or /ooc (though please do not abuse the /ooc channel if others are in the zone). Also please stay off /shout unless it is an emergency such as a train warning.

Guests

Non-guilded guests and friends may attend if cleared through the raid leaders ahead of time. Eligibility of these individuals to loot will be at the discretion of the raid leaders.

Things To Keep In Mind

Please remember that neither experience nor loot are guaranteed on any raid. When either happens it is a bonus but not everyone will get something every time. Please read over the loot rules and understand this when you attend to avoid misunderstandings.

You may die on a raid. It is regrettable and we do our best to avoid it, but it can happen. Please try to take this in stride and avoid taking your frustration out on other raid participants. Deaths will be rezzed and corpses recovered as soon as possible.

Some camps will have slow respawn rates and downtime. This can be boring, your group leader, and only he or she will decide when and if to pull from neighboring areas to lessen the boredom of it, but please do not wander from your camp even if it means you are sitting around a lot.
