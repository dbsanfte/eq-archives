Charter

I. The Golden Rules of Dawn of Sovereignty

•Be Respectful!
  All DoS Members must show respect for themselves, guildmates, other EverQuest players, and the game itself
•Be Polite And Courteous!
  DoS members can spread good feelings and merriment by being a model of courtesy to other EverQuest players
•Play With Honor!
  DoS will be admired, trusted, and respected by all EverQuest players if our intentions are good and honorable
•Be Honest!
  There will be NO excuse for deceit and dishonesty
•Be Loyal!
  We are a family dependent upon one another. That which affects one guild member affects the entire guild
•Have Fun!
  If you're not happy, We're not happy!


II. Dawn of Sovereignty Eligibility

• All races and classes Level 40 and up are welcome
• Prior to joining the guild, characters must be interviewed by a guild officer and should group or go on a raid with
  other DoS members
• Characters that have voluntarily left the guild less than two times are welcome to rejoin. A majority vote of approval
  by guild officers will be required if a character has left voluntarily but wishes to rejoin
• Any character that has been removed from the guild non-voluntarily is not welcome to rejoin DoS
• "E-Bay" characters are not permitted by the creators of EverQuest and will not be permitted into the guild. Any
  character violating this rule will be removed immediately
• To gain Officer status, a majority vote of approval by existing guild officers will be required. The guild leader has
  veto authority over any officer nominations


III. Removal from Dawn of Sovereignty

• Any guild member may leave the guild for any reason and at any time without fear of harassment or persecution
• Please inform a guild officer if you wish to leave the guild. Not only may we be able to help if there is a problem,
  but we also need to maintain record of who is and is not in the guild
• Any form of harassment of other members or other EverQuest players will result in removal from DoS
• Any blatant disregard of DoS values will result in removal from DoS
• Intentional kill stealing or loot stealing will result in a warning and possibly ultimate removal from DoS
• Failure to abide by any guild officer resolution to a conflict with any EverQuest player will result in removal from
  DoS
• A unanimous vote of removal, from guild officers will result in removal from DoS

Please understand that we do not want to remove anyone from DoS! These rules are here to protect the guild as a whole and we believe that most issues that arise can and will be solved in a civilized manner


IV. Raid Eligibility & Procedures

• In the event there is a level requirement for a raid, a member must be the minimum level to participate, unless   approved BY the raid leader!
• Non DoS members may attend DoS raids with prior approval of raid leader
• The raid leader has ultimate authority over participating guild members for the duration of the raid
• The loot procedure on raids is a ladder system. If you need an item and choose to roll on it, you automatically move   to the bottom of the list for rolling eligibility IF you win that item. This will carry over to subsequent raids if   there are still others who have not won a roll. This will allow for ALL members to have a chance to win loot. If an   item is not needed by anyone attending the raid, then EVERYONE can roll to sell the item for cash. In the event   that we do roll to sell for cash, then NO ONE will be moved to the bottom of the list. This procedure must be   followed in order to assure fair looting by all. Guild officers will deal with any loot issues that may arise after the   raid
• Members are STRONGLY encouraged to follow their designated role in a raid. People get killed when casters
  start tanking or people start running ahead, and no one wants DoS corpses all over a dungeon! : )


VI. Extras

•Crafted items
  A guild member may charge any price for crafted items to other guildmates, although it is hoped that guild members
  will sell crafted items to guildmates at cost or at a discount
•Ports & Buffs
  Whenever possible guild members should try, but are not required to help other guildmates with buffs and ports
  free of charge!
•Disputes between guildmates
  Any dispute or conflict between guild members is to be addressed to a guild officer for resolution. Disputes should
  be addressed in private using the /tell channel
•Freedom of speech
  Any thoughts, comments, ideas, questions, and criticisms are welcome in an appropriate manner
  Use of offensive or inappropriate language in the /guild, /shout, or /ooc channel should not occur
•Guild Anonymous
  The use of the command /anon is not allowed unless approved by an officer
  The command /roleplay (which still allows member to appear on "/who dawn all ") list is allowed

NOTE: Guild members are NOT REQUIRED to port, craft items, or assist guildmates in any way if it is not mutually agreeable to all parties involved


VII. Conclusion
This charter is the foundation on which Dawn of Sovereignty will stand throughout time, without end, as a shining light of hope and achievement for all EverQuest players. All Dawn of Sovereignty members are responsible for knowing and understanding the guild charter. All guild officers have approved this charter and any changes therein will require unanimous agreement by all guild officers
