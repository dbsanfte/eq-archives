ICQ

ICQ is a great communications package and can be downloaded here.

ICQ works from a contact list which is a list of people you know online who also use ICQ. When you are online ICQ constantly monitors the ICQ relay servers to see who else from your contact list is online and lists them appropriately. When you are online you can choose from several modes such as 'Do Not Disturb', 'Available' or 'Invisible'. The various modes change the icon next to your name and allow others to see your status. Invisible is cool as it allows you to be online and see who is around but you do not show up on anyone's list as online.

The contacts list can be managed and can be set in several modes. I highly recommend that you run in the 'Authorise' mode for new contacts. By default anyone can add you to their contact list, however with the authorise option set you are sent a message telling you who is trying to add you to their list along with a message from them, you can then vet who's contact list you want to be on.

ICQ can be used to send 'post it note' type messages to anyone who is online on your list, this is the most commonly used mode, however ICQ can also provide a chat room style interface where whoever initiates the room can invite as many people as he/she wishes to. ICQ is also a very good way to send files to people.

When you register with ICQ you are given an ICQ number, this number is your ID and is the easiest way to let people add you to their contact list, there are other searches but they tend to turn up hundreds of results where the ICQ number is unique. I would suggest that the best way for us to share our numbers is on the members only forum of the message board.
