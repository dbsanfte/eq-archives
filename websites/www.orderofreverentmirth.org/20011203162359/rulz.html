The Order of Reverent Mirth

Guild Rules of Conduct

 

Order of Reverent Mirth Guild Guidelines

 

1. Treat all players with respect.
2. All Everquest rules of conduct must be adhered to.
3. Guild chat is not to be used for begging, requests for power leveling,
selling (spamming) or arguing. Disputes with fellow guild mates should be
taken to tells, and if unresolved brought to an officers attention.
4. Any items given to you by the guild should be handed down, or given back
to the guild these items are not to be sold or traded for personal gain.
5. All loans made by the guild bank should be paid back immediately or as
soon as possible.
6. Last rule but the most important one - HAVE FUN!

Violation of these rules will be handled with warnings or an immediate loss
of guild status.

 

Order of Reverent Mirth Officers Guidelines

 

1. All guild guidelines must be adhered to
2. No officer should talk down about a fellow guild mate, if you have issue
take it up with them and clear the air, if the dispute cannot be settled
notify the guild master for help in resolving the issue.
3. All rule violations should be brought to the attention of the guild master
for documentation purposes.
4. Requests for help from fellow guild mates should be responded to
immediately.
5. No guild member shall be disbanded without at least 3 officers agreeing on
the matter at hand.
6. If a member is banned from the guild, the guild master must be notified
immediately via email or in game.
7. Rules of conduct and web site information must be reviewed with all new
members at time of recruitment.
8. All new recruit information should be emailed to the guild master as soon
as possible to aid in keeping the roster up to date.

Violation of these rules will be handled with warnings or an immediate loss
of officers' status severe violations can lead to complete loss of guild
status.

 

Order of Reverent Mirth Guild Raid Guidelines

 

1. Raid leader is in charge of being the zone expert.
2. Raid leader is in charge of group placement.
3. Each group appoints a master looter. All looting is performed by the master looter
4. All items are class rolled for first and if no member of that class is present then everyone in the group can roll for it.
5. If you win an item you are out of future rolls until everyone receives an item.
6. At the raids end each master looter reserves 20% of their loot and splits the rest with their group. This must be done at the time of the raid, if for some reason the master looter cannot attend until the end of the raid, loot is to be split (reserving 20% which is turned in to the raid leader) and another master looter is to be appointed. This must be done before the master looter leaves the raid.
7. The reserved 20% of loot is turned over to the raid leader and it is their responsibility to see that it is turned in to the guild treasurer. This money is used for loans, parties and purchasing items to be loaned out to guild members.

NOTE: These rules are for Mirth only raids and may be different if a joint raid with another guild is held.

 

Order of Reverent Mirth Recruiting Guidelines

 

There are three requirements to accept a player as a new member of The Order of Reverent Mirth.

1. The character must be level 20 or higher. Once the character has become a member of the guild, any alternate characters can be invited into the guild regardless of level.

2. The player must post a message in the Join Mirth Now! forum stating their desire to join. They should include some basic information about their character (e.g., name, level, race, class), and anything else they'd like to say.

3. The player must get recommendations from five current guild members. Recommendations should include a short description of the time you've spent with the person being recruited, and why you think they would make a good member of the guild.

Recommendations can be submitted through e-mail or in game through /tell, to any guild officer or the guild leader. Accepting the recommendation is voluntary on the part of a guild officer, but if they do agree to accept it, it is then their duty to get it to the Recruiting Officer (currently Manchy), again through e-mail or in game through /tell.

Recommendations should not be posted on the guild message board (this is to avoid personal conflict between members in the event that some members want a player to join and others do not).

If any guild member does not want someone to join the guild, they should submit their reason(s) using the same process as submitting a recommendation. This information will be handled on a case by case basis by the guild leadership.

Once all of the requirements have been met, the Recruiting Officer (currently Manchy) will announce the decision to the officers and any officer can then do the /guildinvite.

The officer doing the /guildinvite is responsible for collecting the players information for the guild roster (for main and alt characters guilded) and forwarding the information to the guild leader either through e-mail or in game.

Once the recruiting process is complete for a player their thread on the Join Mirth Now! forum will be deleted.

The only possible exception to this process is for players previously in the guild who left and wish to rejoin. Decisions on whether or not to allow these players to rejoin the guild will be handled by the guild leadership on a case by case basis.
