Lesson One: Dual Wield

Quite often we hear people ask in guildchat "Which weapon should I put in which hand?", a question which often results in many conflicting answers. Today, using my own class (The Monk) as an example, I will attempt to give you some information that will help you decide for yourself.

Dual Wield is a skill that allows your character to equip 2 weapons, one in each hand, with the purpose of being able to attack with both. The two weapons slots (Primary and Secondary) are completely independent of each other, and a dual wield check is made for your secondary hand based on the delay of the weapon you are wielding. So, for example, if you are wielding a 30 delay weapon in Priamry, and a 15 delay weapon in Secondary, your secondary hand would make a dual wield check twice as often as your Primary hand attacks.

Notice how in the example I said that your Secondary hand would "make a dual wield check". I did not say that it would attack, because your secondary hand only attacks on a succesful skill check, and this number will never be 100%. At it's maximum skill level (252) dual wield has roughly a 78% chance to be successful. Your Primary hand, however will always attack at least once, dictated by it's delay.

In addition to always attacking, the Primary hand has another advantage over the secondary hand that must be considered when choosing weapons. Every class that can dual wield recieves a damage bonus on the weapon in their primary hand. I don't know the bonuses for other classes, but a monks Primary hand bonus can be calculated with the following formula.

(Level-25)/3, with the result always rounded down.

So, a level 49 monk would calculate:
1) 49-25=24
2) 24/3=8
So the damage bonus would be 8.

This number is added to the damage rating of the weapon you are using. So, if our level 49 monk is wielding a Jade Mace, which is normally 9/18, the weapon would be the equivilent of 17/18 in the primary hand before any haste is computed. But if the mace was in the secondary hand, it would still only be a 9/18 weapon.

So, the best choice in a Primary hand weapon is one that has a low delay, which is more important than a good ratio. The damage bonus combined with the fact that the Primary hand always attacks will make up for the ratio in the long run.

Ratio is the most important thing for the Secondary hand, though. With no damage bonus to calculate, best ratio = best weapon. Over time, a Jade Mace (9/18) will average the same ammount of damage as a Knuckle Duster (14/28), because they have the same ratio.

Of course, there are other factors to consider too. If you have 2 weapons with similar ratios, but one has a proc, you might want to consider putting the procing weapon in the Primary hand, because more attacks = more hits = more procs. Also, since melee aggro is calculated based on the number of times a mob is attacked, 2 Jade Maces, while doing the same ammount of damage in the long run, will generate much more aggro than a Jade Mace/Knuckle Duster combo.

So, I hope that this guide helps you decide where to put your weapons.
