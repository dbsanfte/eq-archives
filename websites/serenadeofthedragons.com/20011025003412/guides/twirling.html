2\ When you manage to master your twirling technique you may be able to spin rather fast, a true master always picks a barstool that has a nice lip underneath to grab onto.

3\ NEVER, and I mean never whirl on the spot! Go with the seat, let it do the spinning, nekked bits on hot leather trying to spin doesn't bear thinking about. It's bad enough to get yer knickers in a twist, other bits in a twist are worse by a factor of ten. Remember, friction can be a bummer.

6\ Now, a true master will check the style of barstool nonchalantly beforehand, taking note of the twirling system it employs. The last thing you want is to completely unscrew the seat so always make sure your starting on a bearing style barstool as opposed to threaded.

7\ En route to mastering you may want to use protective gear (shame on you for any thoughts you just had) such as elbow and knee pads. A safety helmet is optional but just beware for anyone putting 'YMCA' on the sound system. Look out for sailors, policemen, Indians or biker types.

9\ For everyone's safety, remove those piercings!

10\ At this point of mastery you can try advanced techniques such as spin straddling, cheek surfing, counter spinning and the highly risky Kavorkian muffin.
