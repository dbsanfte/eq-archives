RULES
These are some new rules that were put into place recently by our Council

Officership
Officers of the guild must be Level 30 or over. 10 levels of that must have been gained while the member was in the guild. There will be a probationary period of 1 week, during which, the officer may be called upon for input when a council member is resolving a problem. This will give us an idea of how the officer will react if a problem is brought to his/her attention. (No current officers will be demoted due to this rule).

Behavior
There is to be no begging in guildchat. This means ports, cash, or powerleveling. This doesn't mean that you can't ask for things every once in a while, just that we should never hear "Somebody give me some pp, PLZ!" or anything similar. Asking "Is anyone porting?" repeatedly without getting a response, usually means that nobody is porting.

Council of Five
The leadership of Serenade of the Dragons is led by a council of five. The council consists of Lomein, Tluluilea, Mytharkayne, Zeex, and Miztral. Each member of the council is considered a guildleader, enabling the guild to have 5 viewpoints on important matters, and with an odd number, there will be no vote deadlocks.

Guild Events
Guild events will be held no more than 3 weeks apart. If you have an idea, or a guild event you would like to see happen, post it here, on the guild forums under Guild Events.

Majority Vote
Upon a majority vote of the guild, leadership may be bestowed upon any member.

Harassment Policy
"Anyone found to be harassing any other player or guildmember will be subject to IMMEDIATE removal from the guild, at the judgement of any council member."
Yes, "Immediate". That's no 3 strikes... so, simply put "BE NICE TO PEOPLE!"

Officer Behavior
Officers are expected to uphold and defend all the rules. There are NO EXCEPTIONS. Officers should be seen as an example for their fellow guild members. Remember, people see your guild tag and judge the name there by YOUR behavior.

Final Rule
The number one rule of all, is to have fun :) It is a game, after all!
