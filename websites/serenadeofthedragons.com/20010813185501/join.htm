The Serenade of the Dragons is a guild that plays on the Terris Thule server.
We have 60+ members, and our average level range is High 30's-Mid 40's. We are not an "Uberguild" and most of our members are close friends despite our large size. We are growing every day, and would welcome you to join us. Our only real requirement for joining is that you have reached level 20, and have an attitude that is in following with the guild. If you would like to join, please send email to our guildleader, telling us a little bit about yourself. Or, just contact one of our members in-game, and they will be happy to direct you towards an officer.
