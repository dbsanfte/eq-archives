PLANE OF GROWTH RAID INFORMATION

Here is a gathering of informations dealing withThe Plane of Growth. Please study this information you will be better off when trying to tame PoG. This information was compiled by online sources as well as through my own trial and error. :) HEHE! Good luck and be well.

MAP AND INFORMATION
The Plane of Growth zone in is located at the top of a tower on an island roughly in the middle of the Wakening Lands at loc p500, n1050. The bridge and tower base are guarded by faun rogues that don't see through invis. Groups can buff and re-invis at the top without fear of aggro, but be very careful with your mouseclicks when near the tree, it's very easy to accidently click on the Mushroom and zone into PoG unintentionally.

Groups will be formed, buffed, and invised before everyone zones into PoG. There will be a Main Assist Group as well as a Secondary Group for add support. A Druid or Ranger with good Tunare Faction will be asked to zone in and check out the zone in area for mobs who can see invisable. One such mob is the Farstride Unicorn. Once the area is clear the Druid/Ranger will let the raid leaders know and each group will be told to zone in 1 by 1. Each member simply clicks on the Mushroom to port into the plane. A Whorl of Energy (54th level mob) guards the portal. This mob does not drop treasure and can see invisable at times. This again is why it is important to have a Druid/Ranger scout the zone to see what we are up against before we move in.

CAMP SPOTS AND MOBS

Main "Safe Spots" - There are 4 Primary "Safe Spots (ya right)"used by most guilds when raiding PoG. A few of these are can be tough if we do not have an experienced PoG puller. Here are the 2 main camp areas we will focus on this time around.

  • West Wall - Right by the zone-in. Everyone zones in invis to avoid the Whorl and runs west to the zone wall. The mobs we can pull are:
    1. A rolling plains steed
    2. A gale wolf
    3. A spirit flux wolf
    4. A sanguine kodiak
    5. A glade stalker
    After clearing this area we will either follow the west wall North clearing everything on the way to the waterfall, or move to the Lake.

    Lake - We will make our way to this area by traveling south east from the west wall. You can also make it to the lake by following the river at the waterfall. This camp area is near the thifling camps.. In order to pull from the thifling camps we will either need to have a Druid or Ranger friend come up and keep them harmonized (they will not take faction hits) or have a Monk split them up - Lament sometimes works, as well. The mobs we can pull here are:
    1. A thifling sprite
    2. A contemplative thifling
    3. Ordro and his Assistant
    4. A serene forest spirit
    5. You will also find lots of flowers, sylvans, and wolves here.

    Waterfall - Keeping the west wall on ourleft, we will SOW everyone and follow it to the waterfall. The mobs we can pull are:
    1. An entoling essence channeler
    2. An entoling culler
    3. An entoling harvester
    4. A feral amalgam

    It should be noted that all Treants as well as Protectors of Growth will attempt to Gate when low on health. If they are successful Clerics will be asked to "CAMP". Casters may Gate if they are binded at the WL PoG Zone in area *Mushroom* All clerics must try to log out from the game.. Once these mobs gate they will begin summoning each player for a slow horrible death. Clerics need to erase the aggro for any players needing a rez.

    　

    Loot Rules for Raid - No Drop items will be rolled upon by those who can use (ie; race and class) at the command of one of the raid leaders. Items that can be picked up and passed to another will be held til the end of the raid.The same goes for spells and pp items. Gems will be split amoungst the clerics at the raid and any druid or ranger that decides to help us by scouting.
