Nagafen Raid Overview
Here is the layout, information, mobs, treasure, and loot rules for the Naggy Raid. This information is intended to help you know exactly what we will be doing and what you need to help make this a successful raid.
Lord Nagafen -- The Primary Target

  • Level 55
  • 32,000 HP
  • AOE fire breath that can hit for 800 at its maximum (Less with save versus fire gear and buffs)
  • AOE fear (Save versus magic gear and buffs will overcome fear)
  • AOE dispel (No counter measures other than junk buffs)
  • Claws those he likes best for some 255 per (Sacrifice your AC for resists for this cannot be "countered")
  • Immune to fire and casters outside melee range
  • Ports those over the 52nd season out of his Lair
  • Spawn time is one week
Fire Giants -- Bodyguards fit to protect a Lord

  • Level 47 to 51
  • Will hit you for 130+ per
  • Cannot be mezzed or stunned (can be charmed)
  • Fire damage does not hurt them (other than damage shields)
  • Immune to casters outside melee range
  • They number 21 and come in three varieties
  • Warriors (Warlord Skarlon being the best)
  • Wizards (Magi Rokyl leading this pack)
  • Shadow Knights (King Tranix invoking the touch of death)
  • Spawn about every 6 hours
Requirements

To attend you must have reached a minimum of level 40 except clerics who may attend at level 39 if they have CH, and good resists - if you get feared or die, you're not much help to your group.

You must have an unbuffed fire resist of 80 and an unbuffed magic resist 60. The higher the resists, the better off you will be.

You must have a minimum of three and a half hours available. It takes a minimum of one hour to clear the way to Naggy, another hour to buff, five minutes to kill, and about one and a half hours to rezz and distribute the loot. Those attending should realize if we fail to kill Nagafen a slow and long corpse recovery takes place.

You must also agree to the loot distribution.

Although this raid is open, preference will be given to those who sign up early and those of the highest level. This only applies if too many wish to attend (i.e. more than 50). Raids only proceed if the raid leader is confident that we have a chance. If there is not enough firepower, the raid will be cancelled.
The Loot

All loot of significance dropped by Naggy and the fire giants is up for grabs (unless otherwise specified). Once a raid starts, we WILL NOT change the loot rules (so don't even bother trying).

If you roll and win an item, you are not be eligible to roll again during that raid. There is one exception to this rule which applies to no drop items from the fire giants. These items are randomed immediately and winners are not disqualified from rolling on Naggy drops during that raid. If two of the same item drop, there will only be one roll. The two highest rolls would win.

You may only roll on items you need. Recognize that just because you can equip an item, it does not mean you will be allowed to roll on it. This may seem unfair to you, but understand the loot rules before you decide to attend.

Lavastorm Staging

All those attending will gather at the druid rings in Lavastorm Mountains before the raid start time.

At the start time, the raid leader will ask all those attending to put /anon or /role OFF and /lfg ON. A decision will be made at that time to either cancel or proceed. This decision is dependant on the number of people in attendance.

The raid leader will then request a FD puller form group one (cleric will lead so FD can /ex). This group will be responsible for pulling the fire giants and leading the charge into stoney when requested to do so by the raid leader. Several healer types will be asked to organize the remaining groups (grp numbers are assigned). These groups should be "filled" quickly and not "formed" since their purpose is only to get us to Naggy.

The final request will be for 2 or 3 volunteers who would be responsible for forming and executing the buff sequence. These individuals should organize during the FG run so buff groups can be formed quickly upon arrival at the buffing area.

A main assist (MA) and a secondary assist will be named. It is imperative that everyone create /assist buttons before we move into Sol B. Do not engage any fire giant unless you use these hotkeys.

A backup raid leader will also be named in the event the raid leader goes link dead.

All communication to the raid leader should be done through your group leader from this point on (i.e deaths, link deaths, info on those who become lost). The pulling group members and buff group leaders should also keep the raid leader apprized of anything of relevance (e.g. puller down).

Once these groups are formed, you should buff up for the short term (rebuffs will take place before Naggy so no component buffs are required).

Note:   The group leaders will be asked for information on who is in each group as the fire giants are being killed, this information will be used by a 3rd party (raid planner) for the final Naggy assault groups.
Stoney Assembly Area

Once group leaders have confirmed groups are formed and ready, we will move from Lavastorm to SolB and directly to the Stone Spider Room (Stoney).

Make sure you familiarize yourself with the route to Bat1, Nox, Room Before Stoney, and Stoney. The green path on the image shows the route we will take.

When progressing through SolB to Stoney ensure everyone keeps with the "group" so the fire and healing power to clear a few Kobolds, a bunch of Bat and some heavy hitting Bugs is available. This is somewhat adhoc because there are so many people.

Stop once you get to Stoney; do not run up the ramp (dotted line on map) to take a peek. You will actually be in the aggro radius of the first giants before you even see them.

No pets are used to clear the way to Naggy.
Camp 1

Once group leaders have confirmed groups have all safely arrived at Stoney, we will proceed to run up the ramp and storm the first two fire giants. It is imperative to run past the bridge and fight these giants in the room with the Large Urns (do not stop on the bridge).

From this point on, ensure you do not rush forward to meet incoming giants, fight only in designated spots and do not wander away from the group. If told to "BACK UP" then back up. Fire giants forced into walls, lava or dangerous aggro territory puts the entire raid at risk.

Be aware that some fire giants will be charmed and that the /assist is a must from this point forward.
Camp 2

When instucted, the party will move to the hallway. It is important to allow the puller to bring the Fire Giants into the hall before engaging and not to force giants backward into the lava. King Tranix will be killed in this camp.
Camp 3
When instucted the party will move up the ramp, and into the throne room. Stay on the left hand side of the throne room to ensure not to aggro Skarlon who is in the opposite room (depending on the pull group) and about to meet his maker.
Camp 4
Travel to the fourth camp is always fun because everyone must move across the drawbridge. If you happen to fall in, you will be happy to have read this and noted the point on the map where you can get out. There are levers, please make sure you don't touch. Also don't click on the bridge itself. The raid leader is the only one who shall handle that aspect of things.

Some times groups will stage on the platform just past the drawbridge until the first pulls are completed.
The pulls here are extremely difficult, if you happen to see a cleric running forward do not follow, he/she is going to heal the puller. The final pull (before Naggy) will be Magi Rokyl.

Once the last fire giant is dead all existing groups will disband.
Preparing to Kill Nagafen
At this point everyone is debuffed (ask someone in your group to debuff you or right click to remove them yourself). Buff groups are formed. The long task of preparing everyone for Naggy begins. Bard's are used as mana batteries, necro's pump mana, and mages summon modulating rods for same.

Those who can bind in this room should do so.

Fourteen buffs are given to each person. Once buffed you must immediately camp out to the chat room. The chat room will be named: VZNAGGY password: Naggy and you should be warned that the wait in this room can be long. Camping out ensures your buffs won't fade while the 30-50 other people are being buffed.

The Chat Room

Naggy groups will be communicated in the chat room. Final instructions are given and everyone is instructed to log back on. Group leaders should log in 30 seconds before everyone else.

Use /invite to form the group and inform the leader when all have been accounted for. Once all groups are ready, the raid leader will give the assualt commands.
The Assault
The leader and/or backup leader will shout attack instructions.

"/Shout prepare for attack". Bards should start to play Elemental Rhythms with a drum (which should add approximately 71MR and 71FR).

Pets can be summoned as soon as this instruction is given. Consider dropping a few pet reagents so you can summon a new pet without the need to loot in the event of death.

"/Shout group 1 and 2 attack". The main tank groups will rush Nagafen, activate disciplines (if you have them), and attack. Bards will not attack with hand-to-hand (insignficant melee damage and Nagafen ripostes can cause significant damage). The safest place for a bard to fight will be directly below the stomach near his hind legs. If lag is minimal and it is possible, twist Guardian Rythms (71MR and 10AC), or other buffs.

"/Shout group 3 through 4 attack". This command will follow the first quite quickly.

"/Shout pets attack now". Pets are called in to attack.

Healers for the tank groups will remain near the bridge.

80% HEALTH:

Healers should remain back at the bridge until needed. If there is only a single person in the group taking damage, they should move back to the bridge and get a heal. This allows the healers to stay out of breath range. If a number of people in the group have taken damage, the healer should come to the group and heal. The safest place for a healer to stand is on the bridge outside breath range.

Healers/Paladins should call out in OOC any LoH or CH.

Necros should pump mana to clerics.

"/Shout remaining melee attack now". All non casters should move in for the kill.


60% HEALTH:

"/Shout tash-debuff now". One Enchanter will Tash and communticate in /shout "Tashed". As soon as Nagafen is Tashed, a Shaman/Mage will attempt to Malosi/Malisement Nagafen. Sucesses will be communicated in /shout "Malosi/Malisement". If Nagafen agros and stays agroed on that enchanter who tashed, they should not be healed (hate will be transfered to healer and the healer will die). If the enchanter manages to live, they can then be healed.

Necros should not turn Nagafen into a skeleton. This makes it very difficult to find the mob with so many people.

Enchanters will have hard time landing any damage spell on Nagafen. Because of this, they are best to cast Rune on Nagafen's current target.

50% HEALTH:

"/Shout nuke now". Nukes and casters will be called in (use cold based spells). Nuke from the tail or below the stomach near his hind legs to land spells (melee range). Continue to nuke until Naggy is dead.

If a caster dies, memorize critical spells and rejoin the fight.

If a bard is nearly dead and cannot be healed, they can move away from Nagafen and suicide with Denons Desperate Dirge (dragon targeted) for 300+ damage.

5% HEALTH

Cheer cause Naggy is on the run and victory will be ours.
The Aftermath
Naggy's corpse should not decay for 30 minutes. The group leader of the killing group should inspect the body and tell the raid leader which items are present. No one else should inspect the body.

Rezzes are then first priority. Temporary groups should be formed with clerics & bards to minimize the time it will take.

Naggy's loot will not even be announced until this very important task is completed (so don't asked what dropped). With exception to the pally book which will be announced immediately so pally's can be rezzed first.

Once rezzes are complete, all loot will be announced.

Each item will then be randomed. The raid leader will anounce the item, inform on who is permited to roll, and the roll will take place. You cannot roll on items you already posess and NBG applies.

Those bound in SolB shoud remember to re-bind outside of Naggy's lair ASAP.
