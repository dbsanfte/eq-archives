*** Rules and Regulations ***
General Etiquette:

We want this to be a respected guild, therefore treat others as you would like to be treated.

Any complaints about guild members will be dealt with accordingly, and depending upon the offence may warrent being removed from the guild
{ see Code of Conduct }.

If you come across a guild member acting in a rude or inappropriate manner please report it to one of the Officers or the Guild Master.

Anonymous:

When playing, do not use Anonymous. From time to time we have unplanned raids. It makes things very difficult for the guild to help others out in these unplanned raids when your character is in Anonymous mode. So please use /Roleplay this will keep you anonymous, but you will still show in a guild listing.

Power Leveling:

Please do not spam the guild with requests to be Power Leveled.
When we hold lower level raids ( twice a month ) we try to get everyone in a group to get experience.

Guild Services:

If you need a port, ask in guild talk only once.
If some one has this service and is not doing anything, they normally will come right away. You must realize that most of us travelled around by foot because we did not have the luxury of having friends or knowing someone who could teleport us.

Get out and see the world. It is big and getting bigger with every expansion.

Looting:

There are many ways of looting in a group here are a few that may help if you are the leader.

    • Go in alphabetical order in the group this is a little harder then most but worth it.
    • Go in order of your Members list this is the easiest.
    • Lore, No Drop or both items Make a seed by using the /random 1000 command; then roll on the number generated.
