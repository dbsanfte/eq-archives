History and Charter

Our History

Ages ago, in the era of darkness, harmony ruled the world of Norrath
and was essential to its peace.

A council of gods and goddesses watched over the Norrathians, ensuring that nothing was going wrong. Every God and Goddess worked equally hard to ensure the balance of peace and harmony throughout the lands. All was as it should be, citizens living there daily lives and embracing all that Norrath had to offer.

But then some of the gods became greedy. Innoruuk, the Prince of Hate, joined forces with Cazic-Thule, the Faceless, and Rallos Zek, the Warlord. Together they began to infiltrate Norrath with there army of casters and warriors, destroying what they had taken eons to create and beautify. Bertoxxulus, the Plague bringer, also began to deposit his necromancers and harbingers of death onto Norrath, albeit separately and much to the anger of Rallos Zek and his companions. Chaos began to rule the land and it seemed as if the balance was bout to be no more.

The other gods did what they could to stop Innoruuk and his allies from completely turning Norrath into a putrid land, but they were not enough. Innoruuk and Bertoxxulus had worked long and hard on there separate plans to destroy what, to them, was hideous. They wanted to bring hate and disease down to Norrath and spread it across the lands whilst ruling all of there followers from there respective planes and crushing all who opposed them.

Alas, Innoruuk and Bertoxxulus were to fail by themselves from completely destroying Norrath. Without any alliances and several enemies, Bertoxxulus was alone in the Plane of Disease with no one to help him, but loyal followers still existing on Norrath and doing his deeds. Innoruukm Cazic-Thule and Rallos Zex persisted on, sure of themselves that combined, the three of them could do much damage and take over.

On Norrath, the citizens were taken aback, as the presence of Necromancers, Shadow Knights, and evil cleric began to invade there cities, steal there lands and murder them by the thousands. The citizens, who had know of only peace to that point, were confused about the sudden turn of there lives had taken. Druids, Shaman, Paladins, Warriors, Rangers and the other classes began to use there skills for more than beneficial attributes. Thy had to save themselves, there families, and there countrymen.

Tunare, Karana, The Tribunal and the rest of the gods knew that alone they could not stop what had already begun on Norrath. Innoruuk and the others behind this they would try and hold back, but down on the lands, more was needed. Divine intervention would not be enough and they knew that allowing the younger followers to see them in there true form would be detrimental and dangerous for them. Among the children of these gods, they chose a select few, those who would lead the quest in cleansing the land, ridding it of all impurities and restoring the critical Balance that was necessary to maintain the world.

And the was born the Sentinals of Norrath. These select few have received the calling that tells them to restore harmony to the lands. Along the way, the Sentinals have discovered that they are not the only ones to have received a calling from above. They have also discovered that several of the followers of Innoruuk and Bertoxxulus abandoned there beliefs, knowing somehow that it was not right. However, these rebels retained there knowledge of the dark arts and joined forcers with the Sentinals. Together they unite for one purpose: To restore Norrath to its original beauty, and to protect the lands from any harm or injustice that may come to it or its people.....

Our Charter

We the Sentinals of Norrath was founded on the basis of mutual friendship of adventurer's. We try to help out all those in need whenever not engaged in a group or saving our own lives. We bond together by Honor, Loyalty, Friendship, and Compassion..

Honor

High regard of great respect given, received, or enjoyed. A keen sense of right and wrong; adherence to action of principles considered right; integrity. (both definitions from the Webster's Dictionary)

Loyalty

Quality, state, or instance of being loyal; Faithfulness or faithful adherence to a person, government, cause, duty, ect... ( definition from the Webster's Dictionary)

Friendship

The state of being friends. Attachment between friends. Friendly feeling of attitude: friendliness. (both definitions from the Webster's Dictionary)

Compassion

Sorrow for the sufferings or trouble of another or others, accompanied by an urge to help; deep sympathy; pity. (definition from the Webster's Dictionary)
