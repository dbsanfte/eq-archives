Your Stats Explained

Strength -


1) Determines how much you can carry with out being encumbered.

2) Increases the maximum and average damage you inflict in combat.

3) Influences how quickly you learn many offensive skills.

Stamina -

1) Reflects ability to perform strenuous tasks with out becoming exhausted.

2) Is used to calculate Hit points.

3) Determines how long you can hold your breath.

Agility -

1) Helps determine you defensive abilities: it affects how often you are hit, and to a lesser degree how much damage you can take. The Higher Agility score the less likely you are to be hit in melee.

2) Affects how quickly you learn some defensive skills.

Dexterity -

1) Helps determine accuracy and spell casting capability in combat.

2) Helps calculate missile combat skills.

3) Helps determine if you're interrupted when you're hit while casting a spell.

4) Affects the success of critical hits. (for those who get it)

5) Affects how quickly you learn a weapon skill.

6) Affects how quickly you learn Rouge skills.

7) Affects how much damaged you do with missile weapons.

8) Affects how often your weapon Procures. (this is the affect your weapon may have)

Wisdom -

1) Determines how much Mana that a Cleric, Druid, Paladin, Ranger or Shaman has per level of Experience.

2) If higher then Intelligence affects how quickly you learn many skills.

3) Helps determine casting abilities of Clerics, Druids, Paladins, Rangers, and Shamans.

4) Helps resist spells that target the mind.

Intelligence -

1) Determines how much Mana that Enchanters, Necromancers, Shadow Knight and Wizards have.

2) Helps determine the casting abilities of Enchanters, Necromancers, Shadow Knights and Wizards.

3) Affects how quickly you learn many skills especially if it is higher then your Wisdom skill.

Charisma -

1) Used primarily to calculate how various NPC's (non-player characters) react to you. The higher your charisma, the less likely they will be adversely affected by actions that would lower you standing with a group. This modification is very limited.

2) Affects how much merchants will charge for items, and how much they will pay your for yours. This is also modified by your faction with the merchants.

3) Affects you chance of affect with mind altering spells (I.E. Charm, Enthrall and Calm spells)

4) Increases the chance you receive divine intervention with some very high level spells.
