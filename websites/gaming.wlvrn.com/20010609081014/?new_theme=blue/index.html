Game Title: World War II Online

World War II Online is a new MMOL game by Cornered Rat Software. It attempts to virtually re-create the second world war by combining air, land and sea simulations into one giant virtual world. Thousands of players will play in a 1/2 scale map of the world (you heard me right, world). CRS has said that they first intend release Europe, followed up closly by either North Africa or the CBI theatre and then the rest of the world.

" Blitzkrieg - WWIIOL's first retail release is set at the beginning of the Blitzkrieg, and all the major battle points are included: strongholds, fortresses, forests and rivers, just as it was in 1940. Can the RAF and British armies help the besieged French forces to re-write history? Could the Blitzkrieg have been stopped in France? With the dynamic flow of resources within WWII Online's strategic system, players will start the battles where they choose, and those choices will make a new history. "

Game Title: Aces High

Aces High is an online World War II combat flight simulation (a la Warbirds or Air Warrior). Currently, it's the most advanced online flight sim out there and boasts such features and player controlled ground vehicles and ships to go along with the fighters and bombers. Aces High has what I consider the best graphics of all the major WWII online flight simulations. Take a flight through a cloud layer at dusk and you'll see exactly what I mean.
