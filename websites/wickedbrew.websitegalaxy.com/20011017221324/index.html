Wicked Brew

 
History:

Due to the growing number of Guilds not tending to the needs of Guild members, a group of like-minded friends is formed...
old friends and new friends seek a place of belonging, a lupin rage has filled their hearts, wanting to be part of a wandering pack, forever joined in their quest to seek out adventure. We do not hunt alone, hoards of us fill bars, talk of armour, high elves and table dancing fills the air, we compete with darkest evil and brainwashed, moronic goody-goodies. We must arm ourselves, for with fortune and glory comes action and adventure. With each week, new expeditions begin.. are you ready to join with us on the trail of chaotic-neutral, our members vary, our tendencies are hostile but we quest for one thing... power.


Rules:
All members are to be over the age of 18, preferably 21 due to the content of some of the chat which occurs on Guild Chat.
It is a very rare occurance for a stranger to be allowed into our Guild, Wicked Brew has been developed of a group of old friends, meaning we can trust one another.
If you are interesteed in joining Wicked Brew, contact Skunkeen or Darkbear, or one of the commanding officers.
