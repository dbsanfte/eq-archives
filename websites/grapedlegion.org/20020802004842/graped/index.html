Netscape: Did I get the real version?
7/30/2002 12:04:58 PM
Author: Lunkk Smashmouth

Recently I've become employed as a web developer. Because of this, I had to go back on a promise I made to myself many years ago. I had horrible experiences with Netscape Communicator and vowed never to use the piece of shit again.

Unfortunately, I now have to "test" to make sure that the sites I create can be viewed using Netscape. For a while I simple asked other people to look for me, but I came across a bug that wasn't going away easily so I downloaded the piece of shit.

It took me 10 tries to download the product. It installed itself with AOL crapware attached. It loads extremely slow, displays pages extremely slow and video refreshes as if constipated. Does anyone use this piece of shit?

I know people download it to get away from Microsoft and to "test" that it loads pages, but does anyone actually use it on a daily basis as their default browser? From what I can tell its slow, buggy, and clunky. My opinion after giving it a second try years later is much the same as my first experiences. Its a piece of shit.
