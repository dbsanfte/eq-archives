Welcome to Council of the Fated Guild Homepage. We are a neutral guild that operates on the Bertoxxlous everquest server. We are a neutral guild dedicated to the spread of chaos through out Norrath. We accept all classes and races as members (though we do reserve the right to turn anyone away with or without cause). We only ask that you abide by the guild rules and that you do your best to work within the guild charter. Feel free to look around. If you have any questions or you wish to join us please go to the application section of this webpage and fill out the form then locate one of our officers in game and ask for a invite.
