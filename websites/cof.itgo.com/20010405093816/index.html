   From all walks of life they came with reckless abandon they chose to follow the way of the fates. To forsake all they had come to know and trust only the unseen. The ranger and his brother the magician who hailed from the lands of the west, The Paladin and Shadow knight from the lands of the east, The rogue and the brother the wizzard from the dark lands of tourment and chaos, and the Barbarian warrior from the frozen lands of the north.

   Forever drawn by a unseen force driven to the planes outside of the great city of Freeport this unlikely band of travlers forged an aliance. From this aliance was born a council dedicated to a lifestyle of the nomads a lifestyle of freedom. From far and wide people came to join the council all swearing that they would no longer live in fear. That their fate was written in the stars long ago. So it became that Council of the Fated was born and fates willing it shall always live on.

Armethous D'Synn
Book of the Ages



     The Council of the Fated is founded on the principle that the course of our lives is determined even before our conception. Whether one will live, die, succeed or fail is based purely on the weave of the fates.
