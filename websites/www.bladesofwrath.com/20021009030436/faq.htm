Q. How Does Blades of Wrath looting system work?
A. Blades of Wrath is a seniority based looting system.
 
In a system where the leader of the guild (or the raid) decides loot, he usually considers a few things when awarding loot :
 
  1. Which class can use the item to it's fullest?
Very important. A dragon weightless bag goes to a monk first. A item with haste goes to meleers first. A enchanter gets the best "caster" loot possible often because they are in situations to get hit a lot more and are more vital in many raids. Something with INT and mana goes to a caster first even if it's insane AC. Etc, etc.
    There is no rigid rules and most leaders think differently but the leader can always ask the guild what people think on some issues. And then apply what the guild feels is best.
  2. In which hands would an item contribute the most to the guild? or who deserves it the most?
Even if you have a level 50 cleric in your guild that REALLY wants his trakanon BP, are you doing your guild a service by giving it to him instead of a level 60? Because in most high level situation, the level 50 cleric will die very early in a fight or be of no use (dragon fear). Also, who's always there to help the guild for raids? for epic quests? to run somewhere to teleport somebody? to res somebody? to contribute with their time for JC, potions, etc, etc. Who gives his own item to the guild when he doesn't need it? Who goes out of his way to help somebody in need?
    The more you give in a seniority guild, the more you receive.
  3. Who doesn't have something similar on them right now?
Even if you're senior cleric, if you have trakanon BP you won't get PoG BP. Unless you pass down your trakanon BP. If you just got your epic warrior weapon, you're not getting the new uber slasher from uber dragon.
  4. Who cannot expect anything similar from another source?
If Cloak of flame drops, no rogue will be considered even if they don't have their epic. Why? Because they *can* get their haste from another source (epic). And of course the guild will surely help you if you work on your epic quest.
    If Staff of battle drops, no monk will get the item. Because you can get MUCH better weapons out there (even if you don't have a weapon that's better right now, it's common sense that there's like 3-4 better weapons out there that are easier to get). Which is not the case for the warriors.
  5. Who's closest to finishing X quest (Epic, sky, etc)?
If you're doing your epic quest and you only need 2 pieces to complete it but you're not the most senior player there, you're STILL going to get an item over somebody who hasn't started the quest.
    I find it funny when people want to roll on items that they might need 3 months from now when somebody can use it in 2 weeks. If you really want something, work for it.
  6. Who already has something No Drop that cannot be handed down?
That's a catch 22. If somebody senior gets a uber Breastplate but it's No Drop, he's probably going to see a SuperDuperUber BreastPlate pass him by to somebody who's got a Droppable Breastplate. Exemple : Player A has a PoG breastplate. Player B has a Trakanon Breastplate. Player C is using indo BP. (they are in order of seniority). When a new Veeshan BreastPlate of KickButt drops, Player A won't be elligible. Player B will get the Veeshan BreastPlate and Player B will get Trakanon BreastPlate.
