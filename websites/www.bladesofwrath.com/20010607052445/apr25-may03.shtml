Saturday, April 28  


Telkorenar falls! Trindade

Telkorenar reaches for a solution to the BoW machine


Today we decided to do some farming for Kael quest armour in ToV west wing. There were lots of drops. Today was silk and chain day. I think we had something like 6 or 7 chain bracers. It was freaky, all the same stuff dropping over and over and over again.

At the end of our farming session, we decided to have a go at Telkorenar, who we had never attempted before. The result of our effort? One attempt to test Telk, where we got him to 50%. The next attempt we killed him. Did I mention that we did it with only THIRTY people? I didn't? Oh, then I probably forgot to mention that we had ZERO deaths on our successful attempt. It was a pretty amazing fight. One might even be tempted to use the term "flawless victory" in regards to this fight. We were pretty damned happy with the result here. 

Loot from Telkorenar was a Black Rock Maul of Crushing, which went to Apocolypes, and a Transparent Eyepatch, which went to Sios. Normally, around now, I'd have a bitch about how shitty the eyepatch was from Telk, in terms of the risk required to gain it. However, the fight was just so amazing, the black rock maul so unbelievable (1hb 20/23 +75 hp 15 ac? yikes), that it would spoil the immense fun of the day to actually bitch about one item we gained.

Anyway, after Telkorenar, we decided to pay our old friend Gorenaire a visit.  The fight went something like this:

Free to roam Dreadlands! Gorenaire slain! Trindade

Kolas celebrates the victory

 We used to consider Gorenaire more trouble than she was worth. I was surprised at how easily we beat her. Oh well, we'll probably kill her a lot more in the upcoming weeks, as we have two bards sitting on white scales required to finish their epics. Unfortunately, no white scales from Gore today. One Cleric hammer (Clairia) and one Vox Book was the loot today. We had no Paladin's online, but we have two sitting on the Vox book to finish Fiery Defender, so it's all good. We'll just get it multi-quested over, I guess.

 All in all, I'd rate this a great day.

- Trindade
