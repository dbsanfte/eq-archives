Recruiting:

One of the most frequent questions that I am asked is, “Is Blades of Wrath recruiting??” Very easy question to ask, very hard question to answer. 

In short, “Yes, we are recruiting”.  In fact, we are always recruiting, even when we say we are “full” on a certain class…we are still recruiting…

Here is where I may lose some of you, so stay with me. This goes kind of fast…

Blades of Wrath isn’t for everyone.  In fact, it isn’t for many people at all.  More often than not, we aren’t the type of guild that you “leveled up”in.  We are a friendly guild, but we are not necessarily just a guild of friends. We come together for one purpose, and that's to kick-ass. Notice, I said "kick-ass" and not "get loot". If you want the latter, try someplace else.

Requirements to be a member of Blades of Wrath are steep. We only accept the best of the best.  Seeing as your applying to a guild seen by many as in the top 10 powerguilds in all of Everquest, we require our members to know almost everything there is to know about this game that we all play. We require our members to have extreme patience, because a lot of the time, the things that we do have never been done or have rarely been done. If you tire easily, if you complain, if you’re a quitter, if you’re a whiner, if you are a bad listener and/or have a problem following direction, if you aren’t willing to die multiple times a night(or even an hour)…Blades of Wrath isn’t for you.

Another stumbling block for future applicants is our play time.  We play a lot, in fact, we spend un-godly amounts of time strategizing and refining our skills in Everquest.  We raid daily, normally from 7est to midnight on weekdays. If you can’t handle that sort of time investment…Don’t apply.

If you can't handle bad language, you can stop reading here. If seeing the word "Fuck" in guildchat is un-nerving to you...this is NOT the guild for you.

If you can't handle being corrected when you make a mistake, or being down right told off and bitched at, you probably have the wrong guild as well. Everyone one of us had made a mistake, and everyone of us has been bitched at about it. It's part of how the guild works, and it's also part of growing and getting better at what we do. If you can't handle it, don't apply.

Please don't consider yourself on the "inside track" just because you know some of us. Like I stated before, this isn't <Gay and Friendly>...just because you know people in the guild by no way gives you an upperhand over anyone. In fact, it may work against you, as we will assume you should know what the hell you are doing. So don't "drop names", this ain't hollywood.

Please don't try to pull some "James Bond" stealth 007-ninja-application either. If your in a guild, don't bother applying. If your thinking about leaving your current guild. Leave it FIRST. There is no "testing the waters" crap. The only thing you sending me your "stealth" tells while your still guilded does, is make me think your conniving and sneaky. If you will ninja-/guildremove your current guild to join ours...how are we supposed to know that you won't do the same to us? And on the same line, no 'testing the waters' with multiple guilds, either. You either apply only to us, or you find a guild that doesn't mind you applying to multiple guilds. We look for dedication, we're not going to court you, it's up to you to court us.

Our stance on transferring servers:

This is one idea that is welcome. We love the idea that you'd be willing to forego ALL your current gear and your current server for a shot at joining our guild on Torvo. Anyone willing to do this, is probably the kind of player that we are looking for. There are stiplulations however;You must have good standing on your current server(ie. You aren't hated by everyone), It will help your chances drastically if either you belong or you have belong to one of the top 4 guilds on your server. If this fits you, and you wish to join us...log on torvo and drop me a tell, or email me. I will, when able, log onto your server and check you and your gear out. If your a level 60 warrior walking around in full crafted gear, your chances of being asked to movelog are shit. We aren't going to re-equip you with substantially better gear then you already have...so if you have plans on ebaying, you might as well stay home. Also, the rule about being un-guilded doesn't apply in this case, we won't "blow your cover" if your thinking about movelogging and we are checking out your reputation...no worries. I mean, fuck...we let Divius in, how bad could you be?

Here’s a few things among many that will get any applicant declined pretty fast:

1.      You have a bad attitude.

2.      You frequent message boards and make yourself and potentially us, look like asses.

3.      You fit into any of the descriptions outlined above of players we don’t want

4.      You’re a loot whore.

5.      You don’t come prepared.(ie. Clerics showing up to raids w/o peridots, mages w/o pearls, necro’s w/o coffins, etc.)

If you think you fit into our player profile, then continue reading and check out our class/level and item restrictions.

You must also have the following equipment:

  • Innate Run Speed 3 (AA)
  • Sebilis key
  • Some form of See Invis, be it an item, a spell, whatever.
  • Some form of permanent enduring breath item
  • Resist gear, MINIMUM of 100 fire/cold/disease/poison unbuffed, while simultaneously having 100 unbuffed in magic resist.  
  • Jboots
  • 100+ sense heading
  • 4 out of 10 Lucid Shards collected (Zones: ME, FG, SSeru, Ssra, Grey, DS, SD, Akheva, Deep, Acrylia)
  • Approximately 40 AA points

You must also have the following:

  • The knowledge and ability to travel safely to remote locations (ie.Temple of Veeshan).
  • Knowledge of at least one other class in addition to your main character
  • Luclin Expansion
  • Knowledge of Old World, Kunark and Velious landscapes
  • Knowledge of Luclin zones and locations or a map of Luclin zones.  We can’t hold your hand as you travel around luclin
  • A reliable connection.  If you go LD every 10 minutes, your useless

If you meet these requirements and wish to join Blades of Wrath, your next step is to post in our forum under the “Recruitment” thread and contact Jalce in game.

Please keep in mind that things in Everquest are dynamic, as is Blades of Wrath. Desireable classes this week, may be not need next week.
