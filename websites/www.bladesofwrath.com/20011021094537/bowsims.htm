10.12.01
Welcome to Sim-A-BoW!
What exactly is Sim-A-BoW? Well, I was pretty tired of EverQuest one night so I pulled out The Sims. If you don't know what The Sims is, check out the link. I don't feel like educating the masses with this site. What is on this page is a soap opera that you can read about and watch as it unfolds. You can also vote who stays and who gets evicted from BoW Sim City, who is added to the cast and some intricate plot developments. Just visit the Polls section.

So check out the character profiles for your favorite BoW members, familiarize yourself with The Sims and check out what BoW does when they're not pulverizing Norrath's wildlife. Who would ever suspect Jalce reads fine literature or Zophim wonders about his home's color scheme? Is Vardia really an organized crime leader? Will Ylaine become an Academy Award winning actress? Will Zenthar ever find true love and lose his virginity? Help us find out!

P.S. I'll be adding the links later. I just looked at the clock and I have a raid to go to now.
