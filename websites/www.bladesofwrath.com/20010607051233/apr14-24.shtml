Let the defiling begin!

 As per usual, he dropped an assortment of common Kunark spells, some west wasted armour that nobody wanted, and a Talisman for Sleeper's Key. Grats Calli on the key.

The rest of the week has involved a lot of frustration for us here at Blades of Wrath. Patches have really ruined the week for us, and we have not managed to accomplish much. Several times this week, we'd do some kind of mob clearing and setup, say, Dain in Icewell, only to then have the servers come down in the middle of it. We'd log out, wait the two hours for the server's to come back, and then have to waste all that time clearing again. At one point there was a clock rollback, and we had to re-clear, AND rebuff. It was very annoying. 

What takes the cake is that today we were fighting Cazic Thule when Torvonnilous came down. Infact, we had Cazic Thule down to 15% when the server came down. 3 and a half hours clearing the plane wasted because a server outage couldn't wait 5 god damn minutes. Oh well, we got some phat Dracoliche l3wtz. Grats Aalnayaelie on the hoss Zombie Flesh Bracer (I think that's what it's called), and Grats Ferrel on the Fearsome shield.

You know, a man of great tolerance and forgiving nature would not be inclined to say bad things about Verant in regards to what has been happening recently. Fortunately, however, I am not that man. FUCK YOU VERANT INTERACTIVE. Every single day this week, we've been screwed by the constant emergency patching and server downs. Do you people even test the new patches for more than say, a day? I don't know what they did, but daily I've been crashing from the game while zoning, and at the server select screen 15-20 times. It's ridiculous. Possibly the worst thing about all this patching is not even the fact that we wasted all our time this week. The worst thing that;s come out of this is that we got screwed out of 3-4 Sleeper's Tomb keys due to the f'd up timer resets.

Anyways, after the Cazic Thule event, nobody really felt like clearing Fear twice in one day, even if it would mean a second Dracoliche. So, we went off to exp, and that's when Rokar took the first group into Sleeper's. I don't have a key yet, so I have nothing first-hand to tell you about the zone. However, I do have this screenshot of Zeratos wielding the first Priceless Velium weapon on the server. As you can see, our group has had at least moderate success. W00t grats Zeratos.

Two Bile Etched Obsidian Chokers (Sayoxia and myself being the lucky recipients), and two Eye's of Cazic Thule (grats on the new fashion accessory Alakraim and Jigger). Stupid bish still hasn't coughed up any brains yet. Ahh well, no wonder the fight is such a walk in the park. Dis mofo needs to start thinking of strats to beat us.

Due to the fact that Rokar was at Icewell Keep when he logged on, it was determined that we should do Dain first (love the bias in the reasoning for that decision). Anyways, thirty minutes after we all arrived, Dain was pulled down. It was messy work, but the job got done. As per usual, Dain gave head (grats Rokar on the shiny new belt). He also coughed up his crown (grats go out to Senni), a Ring of Winter (I think that's the first on Torv? Grats Kenner), and finally a Shield of Gorenaire Smackdown (grats Kotetsu). 

After giving Dain his medicine, we made our way to Dragon Necropolis. Thirty minutes after our arrival in DN we were buffing and prepping  for Zlandicar.  For this fight, we were totally underpowered on Melee's. I think we had a little under 36 people today. Ahh well, we got the job done, and the end result looked a little something like this:
