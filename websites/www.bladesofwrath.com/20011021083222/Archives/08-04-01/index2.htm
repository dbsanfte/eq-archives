Saturday, August 4th, 2001  

You wouldn't have a CLUE of what's been going on if I wasn't here. The long-time chronicler of BoW events, Kilogramm, has disappeared. And no one but the Angels have stepped up to take charge of this important endeavor. Did Gnome Club volunteer to update our kills? Did those smolder-brandishing neandrothols come out of their caves to help? Even Soup ran and hid when there was responsibility to be taken. I think the Care Bear Regime was off at Shirt Tales Fan Con when Kilogramm went MIA.

So here I am, informing all you loyal readers exactly how much ass was kicked in the past couple days.

Oh, by the way, Kilogramm was spotted late last night being chased out of the Rosewood Funeral Home for, shall we say "interrupting" the embalming process. Local authorities stated "We're not even sure how he got up on the table."
