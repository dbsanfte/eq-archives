    Test of Tactics 2:

    Team Blades of Wrath came out of the gates on fire, blasting the competition and peoples expectations.  They blew up big, owning Bristlebane and the PvP server right off.  As mouths of the oposing team gaped in astonishment, BoW stuffed a foot in it and made them run home crying to mommy.
    It took Afterlife of Mithaniel Marr to put us down in the semi-finals.


"We did not lose, we were beaten by MM. 100% of the reason was their superior tactics that none of our team fully realized until halfway into the MM vs FoH final."

"Whereas ToT 1 was a test of toys, ToT 2 was a test of tactics and the best team won with the best strat/gameplan and solid exectution. Was a pleasure to be beaten by Afterlife."

"As far as us, we had a tremendous rough draw. Beat 2 pvp servers who no one in their right mind gave us a chance against (lots of people camped or went to eat during these 'pre-determined' fights. Had a blast and got among the top 3 out of what, 40 servers? As a ten month old server, thats satifying" 
- Envoy the Tactitian

"I'm not gonna blame it on them (Mith Marr) having better items though, it was all tactics. They didn't even lose a person against FOH in the finals, beat them 6-0."

"We actually did better against Mith Marr than FOH did, the bards were vital. I think we woulda had a decent shot to beat Tunare or Veeshan if we had had a diff draw.. our bracket was really hard, had to go through 2 PVP servers and Mith Marr."
