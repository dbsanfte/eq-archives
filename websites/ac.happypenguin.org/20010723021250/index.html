AC 1, Venril Sathir 0
Saturday, July 21, 2001 - 1:22 AM CDT - Ellsa
After a disappointing start to the evening when another guild got to an enchanter epic spawn before us, we rallied and came back in style! First, a superb take down of everyone's favorite giant monkey (no, not Grape Ape), the Tangrin - congrats Ama. Then off to KC where Hornfell did a little dance number for Venril Sathir's remains to cause the real deal (well, cloned version) to spawn for us to kill. After a frightening harm touch to Lindy (my heart is STILL pounding), the fugly lizard when down! Congratulations to Hornfell and Berron. Almost done guys!

Lindy Wyrmsbane, Warlord
Friday, July 20, 2001 - 7:40 AM CDT - Ellsa
A HUGE, although slightly tardy, congratulations go out to Lindy Wyrmsbane, who achieved level 60 and the title of Warlord this week. I hear she's already conquered several South American countries and the island of Wake. Congrats Lindy! Can I have one of the beach houses?

AC Cleaned Up Fear!
Thursday, July 19, 2001 - 7:35 AM CDT - Ellsa
AC had a great time clearing Fear on Wednesday night! Good old Cazic was too much of a wuss to show his face, so we've decided to relocate our guildhall to a new location!



Oh, did I mention we also got some loot? Congrats to Riek on his Verm... his green druid shirt thingy! Mandelbrot managed to fool all the other guild wizards on the date of the raid and got to keep all the carmine for himself... including a pretty nice robe.

Congratulations to everyone who got loot, experience, or just had a great time!
