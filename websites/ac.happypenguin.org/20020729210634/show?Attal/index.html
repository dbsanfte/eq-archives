Attal

 Version: 0.5.1
Author: Attal's team  
Category: Strategy Rate this game yourself!   Average of 0 Ratings:(not yet rated)

Turn-based strategy game

In Attal, you rule Lords (as Heroes in HOMM 3) and their units. They will earn experience thanks to artefacts, buildings, quests or in fighting other lords. You will also rule some towns and bases that you will be able to develop (in order to produce more armies, better units, ...) and you will manage ressources (mines, pits, factories). For each scenario, you will have a mission to achieve but some other subquests can be proposed during the game. The game will be themeable, in order to manage easily different graphisms, rules, campaigns, etc.

License: free

Additional System Requirements: Qt > 2.1
