  sad? posted by gablin on Wed, Feb 13 2002 07:16:17 AM  

Why sad?

I think the reason is simply that most Linux users are not "real" gamers, ie: that's not the main use of their computer. For this situation to change, well-known games have to be available, so that real gamers (ie: computer=game-console ;-) begin switching to Linux.
But why develop games if current users are not the aimed public...

You see? It's a circle (or rather a spiral?), and Loki knew it very well. I admire them, and I praise their courage for begining this circle.
They made Linux games for very few users, by trying to make as little expenses as possible, while trying to capilalize in reusable libraries for their future, with the goal to attract new clients.
And that was going to work! if only the summer did not mean that people play less, hence buy less (from the own words of Loki's manager).

I, for example, am not a gamer. I play very seldom on my computer. So I did not buy any game from Loki, even though I kept regularly informed about their releases.
Why? Because there is ONE category of game I would have bought: car simulation. Unfortunately, the current trend is rather war-/shooting-/strategic-games.
So I'm stuck with my (original of course) CD of Mc Rae Rally, which I cannot play because it won't work in Wine... too bad.

Yves.
