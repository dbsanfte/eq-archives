Tux the Penguin - A Quest for Herring

 Version: 1.0.13
Author: Steve Baker  
Category: Arcade Rate this game yourself!   Average of 14 Ratings:

A 3D search/explore/puzzle

Follow our Hero Tux the Penguin in a quest for eight golden herring.

Game is in full 3rd person perspective, It's written in OpenGL - and ports to several other OS's.

Initial release has only a handful of playable levels - but it's all OpenSourced under GPL and hopefully enough people will want to help with the development to turn this into something very special.

License: free

Additional System Requirements:

  • You'll NEED a 3D accelerator.
  • A six button gamepad is recommended but a two-button joystick or just the mouse will do at a pinch.
  • The PLIB >= 1.4.0 library
