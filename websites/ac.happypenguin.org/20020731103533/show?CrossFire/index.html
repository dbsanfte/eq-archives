CrossFire

 Version: 1.3.0
Author: Frank Tore Johansen  
Category: Role Playing Rate this game yourself!   Average of 22 Ratings:

A multiplayer network rogue-like game.

A multiplayer network game for X11. It's kind of like a cross between Gauntlet and Nethack.

License: free

Additional System Requirements:
