Abuse

 Version: 1.1.0
  Published by Crack dot Com
Category: Abandoned Rate this game yourself!   Average of 12 Ratings:

An excellent side-scroller from Crack dot Com.

This is a 2D platform game with a dark, gritty feel. The lighting is beautiful and the character animation is very realistic. This is the shareware version, and only comes with a few levels. Multiplayer (IPX or TCP/IP) is supposedly supported in version 1.1.0, but wasn't available in the version I tested (0.3.2).
