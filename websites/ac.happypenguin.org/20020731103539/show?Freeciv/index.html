Freeciv

 Version: 1.13.0
Author: The Freeciv Team  
Category: Strategy Rate this game yourself!   Average of 67 Ratings:

A free clone of Microprose's Civilization game.

Freeciv is a free turn-based multiplayer strategy game, in which each player becomes the leader of a civilization, fighting to obtain the ultimate goal: To become the greatest civilization. Players of Civilization II® by Microprose® should feel at home, since one aim of Freeciv is to have compatible rules.
