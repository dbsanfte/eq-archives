glTron

 Version: 0.61
Author: Andreas Umbach  
Category: Action Rate this game yourself!   Average of 33 Ratings:

tron-like lightcycle game with a 3D view

It is inspired by tron-like lightcycle games. With computer opponents. For 1-4 players You steer a futuristic bike, called lightcycle. Combat takes place in a rectangular arena. Your bike leaves a trail behind, which is like a wall. The goal is to force the other players to drive into a wall. The winner is the last player alive. Networking is still in alpha.

License: free

Additional System Requirements: OpenGL or Mesa
SDL
