Rocks 'n' Diamonds

 Version: 2.0.0
Author: Holger Schemel Published by Artsoft Entertainment
Category: Arcade Rate this game yourself!   Average of 21 Ratings:

A very good Boulderdash type game with high quality graphics and stereo sound.

Rocks 'n' Diamonds is a Boulderd*sh game for X11 with more than just the falling rocks and diamonds of its namesake. The object is still to collect all the diamonds (and emeralds), then get to the exit before time runs out. But your character must make use of bombs, spaceships, and many other elements in order to fill his quota of jewels. This was written by the same person who wrote MirrorMagic, and the graphics are of high quality, as are the musical soundtrack and sound effects. Well done.

Version 1.2.0 added multiplayer support, both locally and over a network.
Version 1.3.0 strongly enhanced the level editor which now has powerful drawing functions and the possibility to directly test new levels from the editor. The latest (1.4.0) version contains enhancements in the level editor and the highscore list, brushed-up graphics and new elements and over 10.000 new levels imported from "Supaplex", "DX-Boulderdash" and the "Emerald Mine Club".

License: free

Additional System Requirements: SDL needed for SDL version
