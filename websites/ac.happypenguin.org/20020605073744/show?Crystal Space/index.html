Crystal Space

 Version: 0.94r001
Author: Jorrit Tyberghein  
Category: Code Library Rate this game yourself!   Average of 7 Ratings:

A free, portable 3D engine with a ton of features.

Crystal Space is currently under heavy development, but it's already quite impressive. The engine supports colored lights, mipmapping, portals, mirrors, scripting, 3D acceleration and lots more. It compiles and runs on quite a few platforms including Linux and Windows. The Crystal Space project's aim isn't to produce a game, but rather a flexible, portable 3D engine.

License: free
