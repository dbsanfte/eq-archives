Fly

 Version: 0.3.0
Author: Rafael Garcia Moreno  
Category: Arcade Rate this game yourself!   Average of 2 Ratings:

Fly - The 3D spaceship combat game

Fly is an arcade game. You must kill all enemies as faster as you can.

There is a 3D scenario where you and your enemies can move in.

As my graphic card doesn't have a 3D working driver, the game runs smooth without 3D acceleration.

License: free

Additional System Requirements:

  • SDL
  • SDL-mixer
  • OpenGL
  • Glut

Gave me compilation problems, but that's probably Utah-GLX's fault (it's very broken). Also refers to certain files describing the keys, and how to create levels, but I couldn't find these (or did I miss something?)

There's also an annoyance that when you press return (which you have to do to restart when you die), you start again on a newly generated level, so if you want to play one of the included levels (there's only one...) you have to quit and restart each time you die ....

Having said all that, it is pretty neat, and has quite a bit of promise. I'll be interested to see how it progresses, and its at least worth a look for the moment. I'd give it a higher rating if it improves later.
