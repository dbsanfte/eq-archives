  What benefit? posted by aablackm on Fri, Mar 8 2002 11:36:11 PM  
Really, what benefit is there in using the Windows API? Other than the fact that it's native to Windows, I doubt even Windows developers see any benefit to it. Other libraries and APIs, such as OpenGL, SDL, OpenAL, etc., offer more flexibility, better documentation, more regularity, and much less in the way of odd side-effects and bizarre requirements. Plus, the above-mentioned are already cross-platform, while a WinAPI emulation layer that's actually usable has yet to materialize despite years of effort. And this is all without considering the license and standardization issues.
