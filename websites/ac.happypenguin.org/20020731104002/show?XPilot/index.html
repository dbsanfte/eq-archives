XPilot

 Version: 4.4.2
Author: Bjørn Stabell, Bert Gijsbers, Ken Ronny Schouten  
Category: Action Rate this game yourself!   Average of 22 Ratings:

A networked multi-player 2D space game.

This is an excellent multiplayer action game. It's sort of like a wireframe Maelstrom, only you can play against many other people either on your own or as a team. There's a large variety of goodies like mines, lasers, multiple shots, and cloaking devices. Requires a fast net connection and preferably an accelerated video card.

License: free
