First the rules specific to this raid, and there are a few:

  • Groups will be designated in advance and group leaders will be responsible for updating group mana/health status and any members afk or LD.
  • Follow directions very closely. They WILL make the difference between a succesful raid and hours of CR.
  • If you go afk in a non-designated area or suffer a linkdeath, try to catch up to the party. If repop has ocurred and there are mobs between you and the raid, DO NOT train the raid. Alert your group leader and if necessary take the death and your corpse will be retrieved by a member of the Ascendant Council Halfling Rogue Brigade: Hole Unit for a rez.
  • Due to the difficulty mezzing them, golems will always be switched to as primary targets.
  • All raid members who can be will be shrunk before we begin, excepting pullers, who will have the option. The ledge where we will be inivising to undead is not a large area and this will help us stay out of aggro range while folks head up to Kindle's room.
  • All loot will be handed out by Tossica following the raid, with the exception of Loam Boots (if they drop, they go to Bludaba). This will give me one less thing to deal with.
  • When we get to the Ledge, move as close to the ledge and corner along the rock (furthest from the Rev tower wall) and SIT DOWN until you have had ITU cast on you and it is your turn to run.
General Raid Rules apply and are:
  • Always use /assist . Have a hot key setup for both Primary and Secondary assists. Not doing so WILL result in enchanters dying and they don't like that. If you are uncertain if you are on the proper target, hit your /assist hot key, confirm target, hit ESC, then hit assist again. If it's the same target then you are usually good to go.
  • /ooc and /shout will be reserved for raid leader, group leaders and pullers. Please do not abuse these channels as communication is essential for success, and these are our primary means of communicating to all raid members.
  • Do not wander around or roam during downtimes. When told to move stay BEHIND the puller while transitioning through areas.
  • If the call to "CAMP OUT" is made, do your best to get any and all mobs away from the clerics. NO ONE should camp until the clerics are safely camped out. Once the clerics camp, if possible, try to allow the chanters/bards to camp next. Melee classes should be the last to camp during a crisis. If you're not sure why click HERE.
  • Loot Issues
    • Group leaders and only group leaders will loot. Any droppable loot acquired should be passed to Tossica (official Loot Leader on Paddy's raids :). If the loot is not droppable, inform Tossica ASAP and she will handle the distribution.
    • All loot will be distributed at the end of the raid.
