    Status of "Kohan: Ahriman's Gift" for Linux   16 May 2002 03:10
    Kohan: Ahriman’s Gift is a stand-alone prequel to TimeGate Studios’ highly acclaimed fantasy real-time strategy game Kohan: Immortal Sovereigns, previously ported to Linux by Loki.

    Now that Loki is not anymore, I have asked TimeGate Studios if there were any news regarding the release of a Linux version of Kohan: Ahriman's Gift, and this is what they answered:

    "While I can't reveal the information now, we are very close to having a partner that can get KIS and KAG to the market for Linux. Please stay tuned to our website for the official announcement."

    I am vividly keeping my fingers crossed they will finally update the Linux version of KIS (1.3.1) to match the Windows version (1.3.4). Besides, I can't wait to play KAG...
