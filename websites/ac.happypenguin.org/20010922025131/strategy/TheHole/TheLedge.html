The Ledge

The ledge is where we will Invis to Undead for the run up the tower. I've included some snapshots here of the outside of the tower and a couple different angled views of the ledge.

If you've never seen the ledge it is off to the right of the tower entrance

From this angle you can begin to make out where we will be regrouping.

And from this angle you get a nice view of the spot we'll call home while transition up the tower.
It is ESSENTIAL that we stay as close to the ledge and the corner as possible. Everyone will be shrunk to give us more room to prepare. Once you get to the ledge, SIT DOWN and don't move until you've had ITU cast on you.
