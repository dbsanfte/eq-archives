To Create an Ezboard Account
Please read all the steps before clicking the link at the bottom of the page to beging account creation.

1)After clicking the link at the bottom of this page, you'll be looking at the Create Account Screen. You will need to decide if you'd like a global account or a local account, the choice is yours. A Global account will work for all message forums, a local account will work only on our message forums.
2)User Name and Password: The username and password you provide are the ones that you will use to login to our message forums We suggest you use your character name as your username but it is not required, the password can be anything you want, it does NOT need to be the members password.
3) Fill in the rest of the information that ezboard asks you until you reach the page that tells you to wait for your vaildation email. Once you recieve the email complete the instructions inside and your done! You will then be able to login to our message forums

Now that you know what to do, click here to begin
