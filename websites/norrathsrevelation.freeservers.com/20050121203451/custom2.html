Prior to joining us we ask that you sign up on our guild roster so that we have an accurate list of who's in the guild and what levels they are etc etc.
To sign up you will need to complete the following 4 steps. Please be sure not to forget step 4, its commonly forgotten the first time and without its completion your signup will not be complete.


1) Create User: Click here
2) Create Character(s): on your My User page at GuildMagic (created in step 1 above)
3) Add Guild to User: On your My User page click the "add guilds" button, select the ECI server then select and save "Norrath's Revelation"
4) Add guild to Character(s): Click the "edit" button under the character name(s) that you wish to add to this guild.. There will be a guild selection drop down box, select "Norrath's Revelation" and save your changes. **note** The guild will not appear in the drop down box unless you have completed step 3 above (added guild to your user)

Once the above 4 steps are complete, you're done! A request to join the guild will be sent to the My User pages of Lauriella and Karaethon. If your request is accpeted, your character will appear on the guild roster under the "need guild invite" section and the Officers will be notified, as soon as your spotted in game you'll be invited :)

**notes**
--If you appear under the "need guild invite" section and have been waiting awhile, please let us know so that we can speed the process up
--You will not appear on the guild roster at all until your character(s) request has been accepted by Lauriella or Karaethon
