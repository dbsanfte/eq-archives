Before Joining
--Level Requirement You need to have at least one character joining us that is of level 10 or higher. If you have one character in this guild that meets the level requirement none of your other characters joining us need to meet the requirement.
--Guild Roster Registration You must be signed up on the guild roster before you can receive a /invite in game. For directions please visit the Roster Registration page of this website

As a Member of this Guild
--No Begging This includes repeated requests to guildmates and/or non guildmates for powerleveling, ports, money, items, etc.
--Attendance Please make every effort to attend guild events. We understand that real life events take prescedence but eccessive abscences may result in you being removed. **note** noone shall be removed by anyone other than Lauriella or karaethon, and such action is only taken as a last resort, members will be spoken to beforehand to try to come up with a better solution
--Treasury If you have extra items we encrouage you to donate the item(s) to the guild treasury so that they may be used by another member in need. **note** Once an item is donated to the treasury it belongs to the treasury for good, if you have borrowed an item you will need to return the item when you no longer need it and/or if you leave the guild
--Guild Roster Please be sure to keep your character level and other such information up to date on the guild roster. An update once a week is adequate
