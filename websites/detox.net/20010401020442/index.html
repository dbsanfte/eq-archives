Who is Brian Coe and what is CM Productions? Well first a little about me. I first got interested in computers in the 7th grade (1974) playing chess against the computer at lunch. It was a Timex Sinclair I believe with a program on a cassette player that was attached to the computer. I played with computers a little bit all through Jr High and High School. After High School I went to LCC to study Machine Technology. After that I got a job in a machine shop running automated (CNC) cutoff saws.

In 1985 due to an accident at work I had to give up my career working in the machine shop. In 1988 I moved to bend and started managing a futon manufacturing plant. I also started back to school in 1989 I wanted to get into the field of office machine field repair. I started taking computer classes again as part of the curriculum. In1990 I moved to Portland OR to continue my schooling and changed my major to Computer Field Service.

In February of 1994 I started working for Symantec Corp. at Symantec I have done technical support for many products ACT, (Norton Utilities Dos, Windows, Windows 95, Windows NT, and for the Mac), (PcAnywhere Windows, Dos OS/2, and Networking Support) I have trained other techs in WinFax Support as well as act as technical lead for WinFax. In the current group I am in I developed an Intranet solution for communication between techs and development for real-time feedback on issues that the TS agents are having.

In Feb of 1996 6 other Symantec Employees and I opened the doors on Digital Forest LLC. DF was a local ISP and a Galacticom BBS that also provided Internet access. DF was a good learning opportunity for me and the other founding members but as we learned that not having good leadership even the best of ideas will falter. In Feb of 1997 I removed myself for the business of Digital Forest LLC. But I gained enormous experiance in the world of the World Wide Web and the Internet in general. From that experience I started CM Productions. CM Productions is about helping you decide what best fits your needs. From system design and maintenance to web design and Virtual Domain name search and registration and Internet Service Provider recommendation.
