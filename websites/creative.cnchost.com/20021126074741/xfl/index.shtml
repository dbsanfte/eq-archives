Detroit gets back in the win column... 12-7-99

The Detroit defense was again the strong point of the game but the offense did get its wheels rolling. The defense forced 3 turnovers and returned one fumble for a touchdown.

The offense finally was able to get into the endzone more on the ground than through the air. Coach Simon said, "Coming into the season we invisioned a strong running game followed up by consistant passing attack, but it has been exactly the oposite. We may need to restructure our attack to utalize the best of both our running and passing abilitys."

The Lions play Chicago next week and with a win will be tied atop the central division with the bears. It should be an exciting game.

Downward spiral continues... 11-15-99

"As a coach, I just don't know what to do at this point," Coach Simon was heard saying as he watched his offense continue to sputter toward a complete stop. The Defense had another great game and once again left it up to the offense to win, they didn't.

The Defense held the Vikings to a TD on their opening drive and kept them out of the endzone for the rest of the game, they have only allowed one TD in the last 8 quarters, of course the offense has only scored two in the last 8 quarters and has not been in position to contend with the opponents kicking game.

The offensive coordinator said the following during a post game press confrence, "We never expected that after 7 games our only rushing TD would be by our QB. With weapons on the ground like Faulk and Alstott, and the emergance of Traylor we just expected to punch it in on regular basis from short and long distance on the ground."

The Lions will use the off week to try and regroup after 3 straight losses and get back some of that winning effort they had when starting off 3-1 in their divison. They really need to find some offensive explosion, on both occassions that they have scored 20 points or more they have lost.

D does its job, but slow start by O causes loss... 11-11-99

The Detroit Defense has been playing heroicly most of the season only to watch the offense sputter. The Lions Defense had two goal line stands on consecutive drives in the first half to hold Tampa Bay to 6 points going into the half. The Lions had an oportunity to put some points on the board at the end of the half but the 42 yard fieldgoal attempt by Peterson sailed wide.

The problems continued on into the 3rd Quarter with Jeff George throwing interceptions on the first two drives of the quarter that led to another set of fieldgoals for Tampa Bay which took the lead to 12-0.

With 8:26 left in the 4th Quarter the Lions were finally able to put points on the board when Peterson converted a 36 yard field goal attempt, the Lions were able to get the ball back with 6:52 left and drove 57 yards and scored a touchdown on a 1 yard TD pass from George to Moulds. The Defense held strong and got the ball back to the offense with 1:40 left on the clock at their own 22 yard line. The offense was moving the ball well with plays for 11,10 and 13 yards consecutivly. Then the Lions had a holding penalty giving them the ball 1st and 20 on their own 46, the next 3 passes were incomplete putting the offense in a 4th and 20 with 11 seconds on the clock. George pulled off a 30 yard run that would have put them in fieldgoal position but there was no time left on the clock.

Luckily the Bears lost this week also leaving the Lions still at a game behind in the NFC central division. They are looking to right their offense next week with a game next week in Minnesota who is also 3-3 in the division.

San Fransisco offense too much for Lions...11-9-99

Even the return of Marshall Faulk to the starting lineup couldn't help Detroit pull out a win against the powerful 49er's. The Lions continue to struggle offensively in the big games. While Faulk did provide Detroit with its best individual rushing game of the season, they can't seem to get into the endzone on the ground, they really need to find a way to spark the rushing game. The passing game does continue to be the strong point of the Lions offense, but even with the consistency that George has been passing the ball, without a running game to fear opponents can continue to focus on shutting down the air attack.

The Lions dominance in past games sure didn't show up today. This is the Second big game that the Lions D has allowed near a 150 yd rusher, 2 weeks ago it was Ty Wheatly for New England and this week it was Ricky Williams hitting the 147 mark. Donavan McNabb was hitting his marks when needed, putting up 4 TD passes against the Lions D, who failed to pressure him all day with only 2 sacks and 2 knockdowns.

The Lions need to win next week at home against Tampa Bay to keep pace with the Bears who head up the NFC central.
