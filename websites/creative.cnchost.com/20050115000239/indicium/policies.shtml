POLICIES

A Note on What Follows. . .

These rules are in place to assure a fair distribution of loot on raids that Indicium hosts, weather it is an internal only raid or a raid that we invite additional recruits along on. We want everyone to leave a raid happy and feel that they were treated fairly by Indicium so that there is no doubt in their mind that they do want to join additional raids hosted by us.

 Gems and Trivial Loot

On raids, rare gems, such as diamonds etc, will be kept by the guild and placed into the guild treasury. Cash loot will also be kept internally by the guild treasurer (fire emeralds, mithril amulets, etc). Of course, clerics and enchanters will be reimbursed for peridots burned on raids.

 Spell Drops

 Spells that drop on our raids will be randomed to all members of that class that can scribe the spell at that time. We do not want to see any spells sitting in banks when there will be plenty of future opportunities for a person to gain the spell before they reach the level to use it.

If no one can scribe, or the available participants that can scribe already have the spell than all persons of that class who do not have the spell will roll.

Any spells that drop that do not have a class in representation will go to the guild vault for later distribution.

 Will Use

All loot that is considered of high quality will be rolled on a 'will use' basis. If you will not equip and use this item imidiatly and consistantly then you will not be allowed to roll. Once you win an item, you are excluded from randoms until everyone receives something. If you win dropable loot and something you desire even more drops later you will be allowed to roll but will be required to put your previous win up for rerandom.

Class specific items will be rolled on by the appropriate class. If a specific class is not reprsented or all of the people available on the raid of that class have the item then it will be rolled on by everyone in attendance assuming the item is of a dropable nature. If everyone in attendance passes on this roll or is a guild member than the guild will retain the item for guild distribution.

If an item is dropped that no one will use, it is considered 'junk loot' and will be randomed to everyone who has not received something. If an item comes up and you already have a junk loot item, you may be included in the roll and if you win, return your junk item to the group to be rerandomed. It is your responsibility to tell the master looter you'd like to trade in your loot for something new that has dropped. If no one wants the junk loot it will go to EC tunnel for sale by the guild for the purposes of the guild vault.

 Weapons and Haste

Weapons that have a considerable dmg delay will be rolled on only by mele specific classes on the raid regardless of additional stats. If all mele on the raid have weapons better than the dmg/delay it will be rolled on anyone else who 'will use' at that time based on which class' would best benefit from the additional stats.

Haste items will be rolled on by all tanks that do not already have a better haste item. This includes items that do have other stats valuable to other classes. If all mele classes in attendance have a better haste item then the item will be rolled on a 'will use' basis to the class' that would best benefit from the additional stats.

If the item you win is replacing an item won on a previous guild raid it will be asked that you then put that item up for rerandom among the remaining class that can use it and that are in the guild. (Participants that are not members of the guild will not be included in any rerandom items. Specific situations may occure that will allow outsiders who raid with us on a consistant basis in on a roll for a rerandom item.) If a weapon is won that will cause you to move your current primary weapon to your secondary slot then this rule will only fall into play if your current secondary slot item was won on a previous guild raid.

If the piece you win is replacing an item that you came by of your own accourd outside of any guild raid then it is yours to do so with as you please.
DISCLAIMER: There may be special cases where items will be awarded to a specific person without following the pre-established rules. In any instance that this may occure the raid leader will try and make sure that everyone knows ahead of time. In the instance of planer armor, last piece rule will applay and be appropriatly awarded.
