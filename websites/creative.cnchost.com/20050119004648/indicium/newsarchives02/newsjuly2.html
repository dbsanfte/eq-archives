NEWS FLASH - Gwealin is a slut

This reporter is not usually one to state the obvious, but I found Gwealin's recent exploits to be quite news worthy.

Gwealin has recently been seen charming and fornicating with the Solusk goblins.  The Dark Elf seems to think that she can make Innoruk proud by serving as host to a new race in his name.  Some of the Solusk Goblins have been found sacrificing themselves to the fiery pits of lava when they see Gwealin trotting through the halls.  The Solusk Champion's have been the target of choice as she seems to think they have the best chance of getting the "job" done.

The Gnomish Mining Community has been seen franticly working of late in their corner of the fiery halls.  Strange shipments of a rubbery substance have been seen imported recently.  Is it possible the Gnomes are coming up with some preventative measure to Gwealin's diabolic plan?
