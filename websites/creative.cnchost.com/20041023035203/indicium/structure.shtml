STRUCTURE

Indicium is a gathering of knowledge and the study of the proper use of that knowledge. Many come to us who walk different paths in life. With the passage of time so comes the need to change structure and policy.  A new order has found its way into Indicium, a High Council has been established that contains the Master and Chancellor of Indicium as well as a sect leader for the Blades, Guardians and Magi.

In an effort to diversify and broaden our leadership there will no longer be Chancellors assisting each sect leader, but a new council that have a more specific role assigned to each member.   This council will be known as the Concilium of Elders and they will each maintain a specific role for assistance to our members as well as oversee and include comment on the rulings of the High Council.

The Blades of Indicium

Those who do battle with the sword, who stain the ground with the blood of their enemies find themselves within the Blades of Indicium. To the Blades falls the task of filling the front ranks of the war that rages all around. Together with the Shields they protect the Magi in their never-ending quest for knowledge.

To the Blades falls the responsibility for protecting Indicium and its members. The Master of War watches over guild security, keeping all within safe from treason's within and dangers without. Should the guild need to go to war the decision will come from the Master of War and the Master of Indicium only

The Guardians of Indicium

Watching over all of the guild are the Guardians. Those whose touch alone can bring relief from pain and suffering. Those who can breathe the spirits of nature into others, who call the power from the gods. These are our shields, these watch over and protect the guild.

Even as the Blades protect all of Indicium the Guardians protect the Blades. When the foe bears down upon them it is the warming touch of the Guardians which brings life back to the battered of Indicium. When the odds are too great, only through the power of the Guardians can the speed of nature guide the flight away.

And perhaps the greatest power of all, when the life is taken from one, it is the power of the gods, channeled through the Guardians, that can call the wounded soul back to Norrath.

The Magi of Indicium

In all of Norrath few seek to harness the powers of magic like unto those who call themselves pure casters. To the Magi do these go. The Magi serve with the others in the quest, standing behind their Blade and Shield, hurling death to the enemy from afar.

To the Magi falls the responsibility of gleaning knowledge from the world about us and with the Blades and Shields watching over them who can stand against them?

The Grand Scheme

Some, who would seek to sow strife might claim that one sect is greater than the others. That one is more prestigious than the others. But the truth of it is these are the calls of the ignorant. I have seen the casters of Norrath try to stand against their foes, and time and again they fall and perish. I have seen the warriors take sword and be trodden beneath the foot of their enemy.

Only together, with the combined might of all sects can Indicium truly realize its power. Only when the Blades, Shields and the Magi stand as one can the knowledge we seek be unearthed.

So if you would take up sword, shield or magic and stand with us then do so friend, for we are Indicium!
