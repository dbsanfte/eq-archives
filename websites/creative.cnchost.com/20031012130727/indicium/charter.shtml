CHARTER

Who are we that call ourselves Indicium

Who are we that call ourselves Indicium? What drives us? Should you look at our numbers you will quickly learn that it is not the good or evil that joins us together.  So what is it that binds us then? Quite simply it is a quest for knowledge. We understand the importance of gaining knowledge, we understand that the true power of Norrath lies in having it, and the true weakness lies in ignorance.

We of Indicium put aside the everyday strivings of good and evil, we try not to dictate the way in which a member should behave and instead focus on the pursuit of true power, the pursuit of knowledge.   Yet, we realize one additional truth, one that has led to the demise of many of the great and powerful Norrathians. Not everyone is ready for the knowledge that is at their fingertips. So with the struggle to learn, so also do we strive to use this knowledge wisely.

Let us consider the necromancer Miragul. At his fingertips he held more knowledge than whole libraries, and to what end? Eternal entrapment within the body of a lich. He lost everything because he was not wise. So it is that we of Indicium band together to gather knowledge and use it wisely.

So it is that we shall rise to become the greatest power in all of Norrath!
