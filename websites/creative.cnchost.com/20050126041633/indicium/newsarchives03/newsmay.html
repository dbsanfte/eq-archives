Sky's the limit  (5/31/03)

Friday night and Saturday afternoon the sky opened up with Wing Blades for all and then some.  All of our warriors got blades as well as some alts and some members of Illuminatis and Veteran's Crew, and even a few to fill some bank space.  Tealya was the first warrior in the guild to complete her epic and Llyd has all the items, just needs to log himself in to do the turn in.  Many others are a piece or two away from finishing up so look for little stars next to all those warrior's names on the roster soon.
