RECRUITMENT

Joining Indicium

A little about Indicium

We are a family oriented medium sized guild. Our goal is simple - to make our time spent in Norrath fun. We strive to challenge ourselves but not get bored. Our time is split between challenging EXP camps and raids - if you are looking for a guild that raids 7 days a week, you are looking in the wrong place. 

 How to apply

First of all, anyone applying for membership must be 55th level or higher. This rule is in place to ensure people can group together and not feel left out. Indicium is aligned with Claws of Veeshan - this means you'll have to be at least dubious or willing to change to dragon faction. We do not allow for any violations of the rules set forth by Verant such as eBaying, kill stealing, camp jumping and harassment.

If one meets these requirements then he/she should seek out current members of Indicium to group with. We want to know you! Sign up for our raids - talk to us. Find out if Indicium is a good fit for you.

If you like what you see, make a post in the Joining Indicium section. Tell us about yourself - your character's name, what your goals in EverQuest are, previous guilds, raid experience and maybe a bit about the person behind the avatar.

Also be sure to read our General Discussion forum. Read some of our crazy topics, post there! We want to get to know you!

 So you've applied. Now what?

Seek us out and get to know us. Keep grouping and raiding with us. We'll discuss your status and make a decision. You'll have to get the support of 5 members including one officer. People won't post support for you after one or 2 grouping sessions. But don't press the issue on your guild status. No one likes a person who is pushy or impatient. Check your application post often as we will many times ask questions trying to get to know you better. If there are any negative comments from members, you won't be invited. It is up to you and your 5 sponsors to plead your case and sway any doubter's view.

We have a recruit /channel set up in game. This will make it much easier not only to find out about EXP groups and raids, but also to converse with us. You'll have to get the channel name and password from one of us in game, but as long as you are interested in joining you can /join the channel anytime. We encourage you to /autojoin so you'll always be aware of what we are doing as a guild.

Stay with it! It won't happen over night, but if you are a good fit for Indicium you will be invited. Just stay patient. Even if Indicium doesn't fit your needs, you'll have at least made some great friends.

 Ok I've been invited! Am I in?

Congrats! you have the support of 5 members including an officer, you've raided with us, we know each other pretty well, and we've decided to invite you. The first 30 days will be a trial period where we can feel each other out in guildchat. You're not a full fledged member yet, but after a month you will be. Just be yourself and get to know us.

After the trial period is up, you will be granted access to the members section of the board and you can have any of your alts guilded, regardless of level. Welcome to the greatest show on Norrath!

Thanks for your interest! See you around!
