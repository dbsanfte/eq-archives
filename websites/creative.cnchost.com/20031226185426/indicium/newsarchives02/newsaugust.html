Wednesday August 21st

Couple of groups in CoM = good time.  Yeah Yeah we pulled a couple of n00b moves tonight but we had a good time and a few moments that really made us look like the well oiled guild we someday may be.  We recovered from our few rough points to round out the night with a spectacular job cleaning a 19 reaver pop.  In the end Tegerdar and Ainana walked away with Jade Reavers for their epics.

Tuesday August 20th

I don't know what it is about ranger's but we seem to have a bunch of half assed ones since they are so lazy it seems all they can do is complete half of their epic.  Grat's to Deavante who today completed, you guessed it, half of the ranger epic.  He now wields Swiftwind.

Saturday August 17th

Made an attempt at Chardok Royals today in hopes of getting Ladak his note from the King.  Things were moving along nice and smooth as we cleared our way down to the Royals and managed to take out the Prince with minimal problems.  Grat's go to Fayit on her part of the enchanter epic. 

We then started moving to the King room and ran into a few snags and started getting caught in repop, but struggled through it nicely.  When we approached the hall just before King is when we hit a snag that ended our night.  Somehow a mob gated or warped and started summoning people out in front of the locked door near the Prince area.  This put an end to our raid for the night but we owe much thanks to Olim <Veteran's Crew> for helping us recover from our wipe in a fairly quick manner.  Thank god for positive Chardok faction.
