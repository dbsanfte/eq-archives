Offense Sputters, D holds on for win... 11-5-99

It looked Robby Traylor was going to solve the problems of Detroit's running woe's when he started off with 30 yds on his first 3 carries, but the cold rainy day in Buffalo caused a big drop-off in offense for both teams.

The Lions defense kept the game in check and held Buffalo to 7 points while pressuring Doug Flutie all day. Simmeon Rice sacked Flutie in the endzone for 2 points in the 2nd quarter to put the Lions up for good. "It was a real defensive struggle all day, and also a struggle with the weather, but we prevailed" said the Lions Defensive Coordinator.

The Lions will be facing the San Francisco 49ers in San Francisco next week and will need to find some consistency out of their offense if they expect to win. The defense will have to continue their dominance if they plan to keep the high scoring San Francisco offense at bay in the Bay.

Lions make trade with Buffalo... 11-4-99

Buffalo's slow start this season has them already looking to next year! Buffalo sent DT Dan Wilkinson and WR Amani Toomer to the Lions in return for 3rd year DT Tony Williams, Veteran WR Vincent Brisby along with their 3rd Pick in the 2000 draft and 1st round pick in the 2000 Free Agent Draft.

Hoping to put some pressure on oposing QB's the Lions will Start Wilkinson Under Tackle, "He should draw double teams from the oposing Offensive Line and allow our Linebackers to get some free shots at the Quarter Back on pass plays and plug the holes a little better against the run" said the Lions Defensive Coordinator.

Amani Toomer will add another young talented reciever to the Lions deep pool of players at that position. The Lions may look to spread the field a little more with their passing game and go with a one back set which will really be effective once Marshall Faulk returns.

Running game struggles, Lions Lose... 11-3-99

The Lions suffered a loss at home in weak 3 to New England 29 to 20.

The Lions never missed Marshall Faulk more than this week. The insertion of Ceceil Collins into the starting linup did nothing to spark the running game. A dismal performance by the backfield that led to a total of 28 yds along with a few mental mistakes by George cost the game for the Lions.

One Lions coach was heard saying, "We did a decent job on defense of only allowing one touchdown, but their kicker had a steller day going 5/5 and there is nothing we can do to fault that." The Lions passing game kept them in the game, but it also gave up the game when Jeff George tried to force a pass in tight coverage to Mark Bruener that was intercepted by Jason Belser and returned 15yds for a Touchdown. "Turnovers always hurt you, turnovers that turn into touchdowns tend to hurt real bad," said coach Simon.

Lions win on arm of George... 11-1-99

For the second consecutive week the Lions have thrived on the arm of Jeff George. George passed for nearly 250 yds and had 1 touchdown. This weeks favored reciever was Eric Moulds who had 11 receptions for 167 yds and a touchdown vaulting him to the top of the receptions list.

The Lions are having a perticularly hard time getting the running game going with the loss of Marshall Faulk, coach Simon was heard saying, "We may have to go with the Rookie Collins in this next game to try and spark the running game until we get Marshall back."

Lions loose first pick in this years draft... 10-28-99

The Lions suffer the loss of their number one draft pick Marshall Faulk in their first game of the season. Coach Simon had this to say, "We lost a great talent in Faulk, but our running attack shouldn't suffer to much with Abdul-Jabar in there. He has to perform better than he did tonight but we should be all right." Faulk will be back in 4 weeks and the coaches confirmed that he will return to the starting lignup when he recovers.

Not suprising is the fact that the Lions were able to excel with the passing game in midst of the loss of their star running back. George's arm is very strong and he has good skills at the QB position, it looks as if this year he is displaying the kind of leadership that he has lacked in the past.


Lions get first pick in this years draft... 10-25-99

When speaking with the Lions coaching staff they said this, "We are very satisfied with our position in this years draft and are hoping to be able to establish a strong running game with our first few picks as the draft is deep in talanted runners. Were excited to make our first selection."

With their first two picks the Lions did what they hoped to, they more than solidified their strength in the running game by making Marshall Faulk and Mike Alstott their first and second pick respectively. They rounded out their top five picks with the following selections; Jeff George at QB, Eric Moulds at WR, and Simeon Rice as the 5th pick to shore up their Defensive Line.
