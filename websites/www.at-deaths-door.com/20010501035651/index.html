Happenings On The Test Server
Submitted by: Feadorn Lifegiver
Two interesting items on the Test Server currently:
  • Traveller Boots: These are a slightly less effective form of Journeyman Boots. They cost between 5200 and 7000pp depending on your Charisma, from the Tinker Bag NPC in Solusek A. Unlike Journeyman Boots they have a casting time (you can't click on the run).

    Note that the speed of the boots is the same as Journeyman Boots (which is significantly slower than an alchemy made Spirit of Wolf potion).

  • Lure of Insects: This is a new Shaman only slow spell on test. It is a magic based lure spell which is much less resisted on higher level NPC's. The slow is only 25%, but that is still very significant on mobs which could not be slowed otherwise.
As always these are currently only on test and may change before going live, or not go live at all.

Note: This story was taken from eqcastersrealm.

Quellious Quarters Closes Down
Submitted by: Feadorn Lifegiver
Quellious Quarters has decided to close up shop and disband. This in affect changes our server to a FFA system when it comes to dragons and planes. Sharac made this post on the Everlore website:
QQ was deleted due to the fact that Quell seems to prefer the FFA system over the Reservation System. In addition, we are tired. Tired of the flaming, tired of the accusations against us. We have taken 14 long months of it and several of us have had to give up a game we loved because of it. We dont see any point in continuing either the site boards or the message boards at this point.

It was not an easy decision but one that we needed to make. I am truly sorry but I hope you can understand.

February 11, 2001
ADD Kills Lord Nagafen !!!
Submitted by: Kataar Stormseeker
At Death’s Door, the slayer of Gods and Dragons. These heroes of Norrath have truly grown up and now dispatch the most vile and evil enemies of the land. The world is now a much safer place thanks to the courage and sacrifice of their efforts.

Haans/Kosten/Banelion, leaders one and all, led our charge into the wicked lair of Nagafen. Banelion assembled a strike force to dispach the evil Fire Giant minions guarding the way to Lord Nagafen himself. Meanwhile, Haans and Kosten prepared the onslaught for the Lord himself.

For their efforts, Sendaen got the PM mask, Magmel got the PM torque, and Camenla some Mith arms.

The buff team did a fantastic job for everyone in the raid. Their efforts in an extremely tough job did not go unnoticed. These folks made Naggy possible and we all owe them a debt of gratitude.

9 groups consisting of over 50 heroes took on the mighty Dragon in his lair. Haans and Kosten assembled the attack in two waves and it worked out to perfection. I led group 2 and we were all at 50% health or less (Nags at less than 50%) when the charge was made by the second wave. I believe that everyone in my group got disconnected (due to lag) as the second wave hit, but we had done our job.

Naggy knew from his Fire Giant commanders that an extremely strong and courageous group of heroes were on their way to dispatch him and he ensured that his best treasures were hidden from their grasp. The loot he dropped was 2- Treasure Hunter Satchels, 1- Prayers of life, and 1- Blight Hammer.

Congrats to everyone on the raid, Dragon slayers one and all. And a special thanks to all our L53+ mentors in the guild who were there to lend their advice, spells, and swords to the cause.

Kataar Stormseeker
Extremely proud officer, At Death’s Door
