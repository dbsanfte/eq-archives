Xylobot - a game tool that takes the grunt work out of playing your game

XylobotTM takes the grunt work out of playing your game, and makes it more fun.  Xylobot can record keyboard, mouse, and joystick sequences; and then play back those sequences to the game.  Advanced users can set up triggers and scripts to respond automatically to game events. 

Xylobot is a generic game tool that is compatible with most DirectX games.  Check the compatibility page to see if Xylobot will work with your game.
