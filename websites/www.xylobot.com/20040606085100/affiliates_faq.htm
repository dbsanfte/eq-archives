Affiliates Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is the Xylobot.com Affiliate Program and how does it work?
The Xylobot.com Affiliate Program is an online revenue partnership between Xylobot.com and you. You generate revenue through commissions earned on all sales made through a Xylobot.com link on your site. We provide banners and logos to place on your site which link to Xylobot.com. When a visitor from your site clicks through to Xylobot.com, we track the customer's purchase and pay you a commission if they make a purchase. The customer's order carries a special tracking code letting us know they clicked through a link on your site and made a purchase at Xylobot.com. We record every sale and the date the purchases were made. You'll earn a $5.00 commission on every new customer purchase. Every month we tally your commissions and pay you using PayPal.com. You must have a PayPal account to participate in the Xylobot.com affiliate program.

How much does it cost to join?
It’s absolutely FREE. There is no application fee or minimum sales requirement.

Who can participate?
Anyone may participate.  If you don't have a website, you can give your friends your affiliate code, and send them to the referral page.  If you have a website you simply place an affiliate link on your website.  Either way, we welcome you to become an affiliate!  Please visit our Affiliate Agreement for additional details.

Will I earn a commission on purchases made through my own link?
Yes! You'll receive commissions on all purchases made through your link.

How do I create links to Xylobot.com?
It's easy! Once you've been accepted into our affiliate program, we'll send you an email with simple instructions for copying and pasting the special html tracking code into your site's source file. You don't have to be an HTML expert - even beginners can create links in just seconds!

When do I get paid?
You will be paid approximately 15 days after the month in which you earned a commission.  Payment is made through PayPal.

How do I get started?

  1. Review our Affiliate Agreement.
  2. Fill out our easy Affiliate Enrollment Form.
  3. Copy an HTML Link to your website
