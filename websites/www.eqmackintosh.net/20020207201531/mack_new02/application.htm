Application Requirements, READ THIS FIRST

Welcome, and thank you for considering applying for membership in Clan Mackintosh. Below is listed the requirements for joining the Clan.


I. Potential member's name must be posted in the Hall on the message board. This allows for any interested Clan members to speak with the applicant (Click on Clan Members link on the home page and then on "Gather round the communal fire".  Post your request in the general message area.)

II. Trial Period (before induction)

    A. 2 Weeks
    B. Must hunt and/or spend time with at least two (2) Members
        (The responsibility to seek out and hunt with members of the Clan rest on the shoulders of the applicant, not the members of this Clan.  You cannot expect our members to drop what they are doing to go run hunt with you because you have applied.  If you wish to become a member, then you must take the appropriate steps necessary to accomplish that goal)

III. Voucher 

    A. Must have at least two (2) Clan members that are in good standing, vouch for potential member.

IV. Probation Period (after induction)

    A. 3 months
    B. During Probation Period either party (member
or Clan) can retract the induction.

        1. If the member retracts during the three (3)
month period, they have the choice to state why, but are not required to give reason.

        2. If the Clan retracts the induction of the member during the
three (3) months, explanation must be given. If the reason is for flagrant violation of the rights of others, or blatant disrespect of self and others, then counsel must have been provided to correct the behavior prior to retraction of membership. The right of the individual to receive counsel can be waived only by the individual


Note: If the person wishing induction is highly regarded and is known to be of good character, the trial period may be waived. There still must be at least 2 members who will vouch for the person's honor, and the probation period, if voted on would stand.

QUITTING THE CLAN

If a person chooses to depart from the Clan after
the three (3) month probation and then later wants to return: the
person must petition to the Clan an Order for Reinduction. NO PERSON will be accepted back into the Clan after leaving twice.
