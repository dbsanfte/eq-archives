Sept 8th, 2001

After the Nagafen raid ended, Lord Gojirax, Lady Kitani, Lord Askelon, Lady Laene, Prince Jhason and Lord Jardail travelled to Kaels to slay the Giants.  After several other deaths, they finally arrived.  During the time they were there Lady Kristana assisted them in several fights.  Her assistance was invaluable in helping this small band of adventurers to slay the Giant guards.  In the end however, the Clan was driven from the Castle where they were forced to regroup and try another tactic.

Sept 8th, 2001

Elements of Clan Mackintosh Sallied forth this eve into the Lair of Lord Nagafen.  During the prep phase, someone from the Raid angered a bunch of Imp Protectors.  In the ensuing melee Lady Kitani, Lady Laene, and Lord Askelon were slain.  After being Ressurrected, the Raid went off.  Many more deaths in the Clan were experienced but in the end Lord Nagafen was felled and there was much celebration.  Lady Kitani won a Heiro Crook (she was the only Druid on board the raid so it defaulted to her).  Lady Laene won a Gravy roll for Words of Life.  A Good time was had by all.

Sept 8th, 2001

Bagpipes blared and the Flags fluttered in the frosty air of Everfrost as the Mackintosh Clan arrived to answer the call of the people of Halas.  The Ice Giants that live in Everfrost had been raiding the outposts and waylaying travellers for some months.   With Warrior steel flashing and caster spells blazing, Clan Mackintosh dealt a deadly blow to the antics of the Ice Giants in their Icy Fortress of Permafrost.

Sept 7th, 2001

A courier arrives at the Clan Mackintosh Castle with the news from some group that the Granddaughter of Gildanni and Jardail has been Kidnapped.  The Courier is now in Custody and the Clan has mobilized for War.  The Thane and Queen are extremely upset by this turn of events.  Anyone that has information regarding this terrible event is requested to contact the Thane at their earliest possible opportunity.

Sept 4th, 2001

Younger members got together and assaulted the newly discovered area known as Stonebrunt Mountains.  The Kobold camp near the entrance was the scene of many a bloody fight and many deaths (all on the side of the Kobolds)
