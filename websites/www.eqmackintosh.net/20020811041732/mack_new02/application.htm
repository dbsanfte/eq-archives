Welcome, and thank you for considering applying for membership in Clan Mackintosh. Below is listed the requirements for joining the Clan.

Clan Mackintosh is a Role Playing Guild. We do organize Raids and Hunts from time to time but that is not our primary focus. Our Focus is to gather a group of friends that like to adventure together and Roleplay our Characters. If it is "Ph3t L33wt" you seek, then seek it elsewhere. What you can expect from Clan Mackintosh is a band of brothers and sisters that will go out of their way to help you and others in time of need. We are a "Family" dedicated to one another and to our Honor. What Clan Mackintosh expects of you. We are not a stepping stone to be used to move onto "Uber Guilds". If your desire is to gain Massive amounts of Loot and Items or Raid every day of the week, then again, we are not the Guild for you. We have a HIGH expectation of honor from the members who call our Guild Home. Before sending this application in you need to read the Standards of Conduct. Once this application is mailed in, it is assumed that you have read and agree to the Standards of Conduct that we have set forth.

Sincerely Jardail Mackintosh Thane of Clan Mackintosh

I. Your Character name must be posted in the Hall on the message board. This allows for any interested Clan members to speak with the you or agree to be your Sponsor (Click on Communal Fire link n the far left column and then on Apply for Membership. Post your request there.)

II. All applicants will have a sponsor. This sponsor must be a full member (ie having been in CM for over three months, making them no longer on new member probation).

    A. The responsibility to gain a sponsor rest on the shoulders of the applicant, not the members of this Clan. If you cannot gain a Sponsor within the halls of Clan Mackintosh (Full members in good Standing, NOT in their own Probationary period), then you cannot join this Clan. This may seem harsh and restrictive, but we intend to make this a fun place for all who wish to join, and letting "just anyone" in will quickly degrade us to a point it will be no fun for anyone

    B. YOU must seek out a sponsor and this person must agree to the responsibility. The sponsorship has to be a mutual decision and cannot be forced upon any CM member.

III. Probation Period (after induction)

    A. 3 months
    B. During Probation Period either party (member
or Clan) can retract the induction.

        1. If the member retracts during the three (3)
month period, they have the choice to state why, but are not required to give reason.

        2. If the Clan retracts the induction of the member during the
three (3) months, explanation must be given. If the reason is for flagrant violation of the rights of others, or blatant disrespect of self and others, then counsel must have been provided to correct the behavior prior to retraction of membership. The right of the individual to receive counsel can be waived only by the individual


QUITTING THE CLAN

If a person chooses to depart from the Clan after
the three (3) month probation and then later wants to return: the
person must petition to the Clan an Order for Reinduction. NO PERSON will be accepted back into the Clan after leaving twice.

STANDARDS OF CONDUCT (Filling out this Application assumes acceptance and understanding of these Standards of Conduct)


Clan Mackintosh's original Charter formed us as a Role Playing Guild. In keeping with that tradition the following Standards of Conduct are expected of ALL members be they Current or Applicants.

1. Guildsay is for Rolplaying purposes ONLY. If you wish to discuss the latest "Uber Loot", then form a channel and invite those who wish to discuss that topic to that channel. If you must say something out of character in guildsay, then use or something similar to that before you speak your piece. "Kewl D00d" type speak is an example of OOC and should be kept to a bare minimum or moved to a channel.

2. As a member of Clan Mackintosh you are expected to conduct yourself in a proper manner. This includes but is not limited to, Verbal shouting matches in OOC or Zones with members of other Guilds. Begging for "Stuff" whether it be in Guildsay, group or in /tells. If you want somthing, go out and put together the groups necessary to get it, or, submit it to the Clan Calendar as a planned raid that YOU setup and run.

3. Training of other players, be they Clan Mackintosh members or some other Guild or the general population will not be tolerated. If you are caught INTENTIONALLY training another player you will be immediately removed from the Guild upon proof of your guilt.

4. KS'ing (Kill Stealing), if it is done intentionally will also not be tolerated, but it will be judged on the circumstances surrounding it. KS'ing does happen and alot of the time we understand that it is not done intentionally. Accusations of this nature will be investigated.

4. The Guild does not exist to provide you with Loot or Items. We will be forming up groups and raids for members to go on, but Begging or asking for items from members of the Guild is not allowed. If a Guild member offers something to the Clan that he/she is giving away that is a different matter, but begging/harassing/cajoling another member for an item that they have will not be tolerated.

5. Conduct yourself as a Gentleman or Lady at all times. The Guild Tag above your head reflects all of us. What you do affects all of our reputations at all times. Going "anon" to avoid showing your Guild Affiliation is no replacement for good manners.

6. If disagreements arise between you and another Guild, or you and another player, please report it immediately to one of the Officers, and then let that Officer handle the situation. This may be hard to do if you are being personally attacked, but it is better to let one of the Officers handle the situation instead of further inflaming it.

7. Vulgarity and Profanity will NOT be tolerated on Guildsay. 3 warnings on this subject and you will be removed from the Guild.

8. Sexual Play in Guildsay is also NOT allowed. Flirting, kissing, hugging are NOT included in this description. We have young people that do play this game or see the screens that you are typing on. Keep it within reason. Nudity in EQ is unavoidable, overt sexual acts are.

9. Movies, Music (except bards), CD's, DVD's, the Latest RL fashions, Politics and Religion are real world topics unless you are talking about the Politics of the Everquest Game or the Religion of different races. This topics are not allowed in Guildsay.

10. Officers are the Law in Guildsay. If an Officer of this Clan tells you to take a topic to /tells then you must do so. If you refuse and continue you risk being removed from the Guild. Insubordination to Officers in the Clan cannot be tolerated. Arguing with Officers IN PUBLIC, also cannot be tolerated. If you have a disagreement with an Officers Decision, then take it to /tells with that Officer and try to reach an agreement. If this does not satisfy you then you have the option to bring it to another Officer's attention and the Officers as a whole will discuss it and make a decision. You will be HIGHLY encouraged to take part in the debate to tell your side and to bring witnesses on your behalf. Once the Council has made it's decision however, the issue is closed.
