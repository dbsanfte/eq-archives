Clan Mack and Friends take down Lord Nagafen!
Friday,July 19,2002

It was a glorious battle. Steel Rang! Magic sizzled the air! In the end, all the valiant melee fighters were corpses, and the clerics and casters who were left threw spells at the Fiery Red Dragon until their mana was exhausted. Still the Dragon stood! Lord Thane Jardail and Queen Gildanni then issued orders for the remaining clerics, mages and wizards to draw Steel against Lord Nagafen, who stood still, nearly at death. It is believed that the final killing blow was from the loyal pet of the only mage left standing. A Terrible toll, but Lord Nagafen fell!

Loot:

Cloak of Flames - won by Pumella!

Bladestopper - won by Drackonn!

Bard's Drum - won by Boneedee

Blight Hammer - defaulted to Queen Gildanni

Orb of Tishania (? No list of who won this)

3 gems or other vendor loot handed to Lord Iggy, the brave and valiant Cleric

Great fun had by all and our heartfelt thanks to Lord Igzidyous and Queen Gildani for the organization, Lord Daer and Lady Caeri for helping with dispensing the loot, and the many friends of the Clan who helped slay the Dragon! ~Clan Mackintosh
