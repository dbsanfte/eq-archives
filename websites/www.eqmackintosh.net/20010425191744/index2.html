Clan Mackintosh Charter

Clan Mackintosh is a guild promoting medieval roleplaying based in part on the old Scottish Clans
Clan Mackintosh promotes the principles of honor valor chivalry loyalty courage camaraderie
and MOST importantly family  This guild will
accept members into the guild and encourage many
Clannish names to grow into their own individual
Clans while uniting all the different Clans under one Clan in the pattern of the Scottish Tribes of old
