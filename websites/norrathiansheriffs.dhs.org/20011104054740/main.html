Welcome friend to the Guild of the Norrathian Sheriffs.

Our Guild holds to the ideal that Balance must be maintained in all things or chaos will result.

We are a friendly guild based on the Lanys Tvyl server. To this end, our Guild welcomes those of both good and evil alignment. Members of our guild are free to follow whichever path they desire, but are required to put aside all differences when dealing with other members of the guild. For this reason it is not uncommon to find the holiest of Paladins in company with those of dark souls, the Necromancers.

Apart from this ideal, we are not a strict roleplaying guild, and are open to all characters, regardless of race or class, so long as they wish to take part in a relaxed and friendly guild that will not place any requirements on them.

As Norrathian Sheriffs we strive to bring Peace Order and Balance to all the lands of Norrath.
