ARMOR QUEST NIGHTS

Once your character has reached their 30th Level, they are eligible to sign up for and participate in Armor Quest Nights.

Armor Quest Nights will be held every Friday night, and occasionally on other nights when enough members are interested.

On an Armor Quest night, all interested characters above level 30 may participate in the effort to obtain a Sheriff's Quest Armor.

The Sheriff whose Quest Armor is being sought will be determined by their precidence on the AQ Nights list below. YOU MUST BE LEVEL 30 OR ABOVE TO SIGN ON TO THE AQ NIGHTS LIST.

The person who is first on the list will be the subject of the Armor Quest. If that member is not able to attend, the next person on the list becomes eligible. At the end of an Armor Quest night, that person is moved to the bottom of the list. If you are not able to attend, your name remains at it's position in the list.

The subject of the AQ Night may choose to do the Armor Quest for any class of character.
