The Realm of Heroes

The Realm of Heroes can be found in most cities of Norrath. It is a place where the gods send the greatest of champions. Those who perform great deeds to the faiths of their respective gods and earn their favor are wrested from the prime material plane of Norrath to be deposited in this haven of bliss in the world above. Each hero oft has a tale to tell about how they ascended into the realm by their perspective gods, which in and of itself involves itself with the depth and histories of Norrath. The heroes each await a coin to be placed within their grasp from someone who has the eyes of the gods upon them. When such a coin is given them that person is declared a hero throughout all the realms and gifted with an item of great power worthy of that of a hero.
