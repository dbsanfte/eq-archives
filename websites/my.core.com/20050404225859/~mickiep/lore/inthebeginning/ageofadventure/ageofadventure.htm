And we yet see the elder races of Tunaria scattered in small villages and tribes all over the continent. The First Guild (not an official part of EQ lore, but nevertheless interesting)

The origins of the first unified pact known as a guild is yet uncertain. Guild scholars have traced the infancy of The Guild to the later periods of the Age of Monuments. A period where Barbarians first appeared on the face of Norrath, inhabiting the cold desolate lands of northern Tunaria. The history speaks vaguely of a few Barbarians led by one that sought a greater good. As the Barbarian race was conquering new lands and creating an empire that would eventually encompass most of Tunaria, a few Barbarians began to appreciate the harmony of peace.

While Barbarians transformed Tunaria through war, Solusesk Ro (God of Fire) began to scar southeastern Tunaria. His ruthless attack with fire transformed the previously lush homeland of the Elves into arid desert and smoldering mountains.

Amongst these tragic events, the first concrete evidence of The First Guild appears. A group of the enlightened Barbarians, with peace in their hearts, ventured to the land of the High Elves. There, they joined in the effort to save the land from the scourge of Solusesk Ro. Alongside the High Elf druids, the Barbarian shamans used magic to fight the wrath of the god of fire. The mighty Barbarian warriors, who had given up a war-like culture in hopes of fighting for peace, helped construct aqueducts in an attempt to bring evermore needed water into the vast High Elf cities from greater and greater distances. In the midst of this battle, enlightened members of other races began a journey to Takish-Hiz (the ancient High Elf City). Dwarves and Gnomes came from Faydwer; even Ogres and Trolls trickled in to help in the struggle against Solusesk Ro, duty-bound in an attempt to cease the hands of the pitiless god from further destroying the world in which they lived.

The seeds of The First Guild were sown. Individuals from all races bound together, not in the guise of war and false alliance, but in a fight to control their own destiny.

Some ancient scholars have speculated as to what led to the defeat of The First Guild. Some believe that Solsesk Ro would have been overcome were it not for the lack of action from the other gods. It is known that the Gods stopped meddling in Norrath even as the great Elven Cities crumbled to the ground. Seeing their creations’ desire to control their own destinies, the gods withdrew their hands, disrupting the balance of Norrath

The First Guild, dismayed by their failure, sought solace in the realization that they had in a sense accomplished their goal. The gods never again attempted to devastate or physically grace the Lands of Norrath. The First Guild was at its pinnacle, providing council to all the nations of the land. The Guild was at the heart of creating a new home for the High Elves in Faydwer. But alas, doing so caused great resentment among the other races of Norrath. Fighting began to break out between natural enemies such as the Trolls and Dwarves once again. Discord swept through the lands. The First Guild grew progressively weaker as they attempted to maintain a delicate balance of diplomacy between the increasingly feuding races. Alas, any further records of the First Guild then ceased.

The decline of the First Guild coincided with the beginning of the Lost Age and any accounts of subsequent events are as yet undiscovered. Humans began to appear during this period., though the denizens of Qeynos (known as Western Humans) and those from Freeport (known as Eastern Humans) would come to be as different from each other as day and night. The folk of Qeynos would generally be good natured, well mannered and hospitable. The people who now inhabit Freeport are indifferent to most things but some are disciples of dark and fell gods.

Some believe that the final direct intervention of the gods transformed a small number of the enlightened Barbarians into a more intelligent, yet smaller race. The few later, as yet unconfirmed, guild writings speculated that inter-racial breeding between Barbarians and High-Elves was the cause of the human emergence. The truth is yet a mystery. Guild scholars to this day quest to far regions of Norrath looking for relics that could illuminate the truth.

What is known is that the humans quickly created an empire, the Combine Empire. The lands stretched across Tunaria, Faydwer and little known Odus. The empire was not long lived, although a vail of mystery surrounds its demise. The ruins and relics of this empire still litter the lands of Norrath.

Through all the passing of the ages, the message of The First Guild remains constant. The First Guild demanded only that we attempt to maintain a balance in Norrath. We, the citizens of Tunaria, a land encompassing all races as in the beginning, must seek only to maintain the stability of existence in the land. Be it so hence our descendants and those that come ever after can enjoy the vision of the Ocean of Tears, the silken Plains of Karana, and the lush forests of Greater Faydwer, as we would enjoy them today changed only by the forces of Tunare goddess of nature herself
