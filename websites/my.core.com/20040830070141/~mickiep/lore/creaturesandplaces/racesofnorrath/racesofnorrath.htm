Of the Races of Norrath

Dwarves
Dwarves are a stout and hardy race whose temperament is jovial, though stubborn and unforgiving when crossed. Dwarves are short but extremely strong (much more so than humans) and dexterous. They appear to be small, burly humans, and males are never seen without beards and mustaches. Of course, dwarves do not see their lack of physical height as a disadvantage. To them, it is simply the way of things. Besides, it is just as satisfying to break kneecaps as it is to break noses.

The Dwarves of Tunaria dwell in the underground city of Moradhim where they work with many crafts in the name of Brell Serillis father of the Dwarves. All dwarves are followers of Brell no matter what walk of life they choose as they are dutiful sons and daughters. Dwarves are surly, loyal, and tough as nails. Dwarves are of course master drinkers as well as brewers. The gems, weapons, and armor the Dwarves produce are highly sought after by other races. Racial Tensions: Dark Elves, Trolls

Dwarves believe in many different things, and each is willing to die for their beliefs. One thing all dwarves share is a common belief in good, strong ale. It would not be strange to witness a dwarf in battle, a weapon in one hand and a frothing mug in the other. To better serve their dwarven patrons, taverns across the lands have specially constructed stools that can take a beating. Of their professions, dwarves may choose to serve their god as clerics, take on the mantles of paladins, harry the enemy as warriors, or pursue the refined arts of the rogue.

Kedge
Creatures of the waters, created by Prexus, description or way of life unknown, hearty aquatic beings of great mental power and stamina

Elves
The Elves of Tunaria are intelligent, wise and charismatic beings that hold the delicate balance of the natural world as sacred above all things. Elves can be Bards, Clerics, Druids, Enchanters, Magicians, Paladins, Rangers, Rogues, or Wizards. The blessed children of Tunare are one race dived only by profession and interest at this time. One group of elves are upright, noble, and civilized. The other group is free and wild. They reside in the cities of Fayspires and Tethelin. At this time the elves live close to Klick-Anon the city of the gnomes and far to close to their cousins the Dark Elves; whom were created from the nobles of the Elves after Innoruuk the Prince of Hate abducted them and perverted them with his foul magics. It seems that the elves are fighting a losing battle against the encroachment of the other races. Is this what drives them to flee Tunaria along with the Dwarves and Gnomes? Racial Tensions: Dark Elves, Trolls

Tier’Dal
The Dark Elves are unrivaled in their cunning, wit, and malevolent drive to fulfill their unquenchable desire for personal power. Dark Elves can be Clerics, Enchanters, Magicians, Necromancers, Rogues, Shadowknights, Warriors, or Wizards. The disciples of the Innoruuk the Prince of Hate are possibly the most evil and hated race in Tunaria. They are born of Hate and live his creed every minute of every day. Inside their dark city of Neriak deep within Nektulos Forest the Dark Elves twist and pervert all that is natural to their own foul ends in preparation for the day Hate will conquer Tunaria in Innoruuk's name. They have no true allies they see the Trolls and Gnomes as tools for the job, nothing else. Hate is the only emotion that fills the hearts of the Dark Elves. All other emotions are scorned and stamped out in the young leaving them a race of cold and violent killers working towards a common goal as well as fulfilling personal agendas. Only in the cities of the Trolls and Gnomes can the Dark Elves tread freely. Every other Race will kill them on sight. Racial Tensions: Barbarians, Dwarves, Erudites, Elves, Halflings, Humans

Dark elves (also known as the Teir'Dal) are the evil brethren of the elves. Their skin is bluish-black, their hair white, and they share the pointed ears and sharp features of their elven relatives. Due to their life under the earth and dedication to evil gods, they have excellent vision in the dark. Being of the elven race, the Teir`Dal have a natural magical ability.

Gnomes
The Gnomes of Klick'Anon are a carefree race. They are masters of ingenious design and creativity. Gnomes can be Clerics, Enchanters, Magicians, Necromancers, Rogues, Warriors, or Wizards. The Gnome’s genius has created machines, and clockwork automatons take care of all the menial tasks in their society. This has allowed the Gnomes to further their knowledge of more esoteric subjects such as enchantment and necromancy arts borrowed from other races. But all is not well when it comes to the clockwork creations of the Gnomes. Some have broken off and gone rogue fleeing Klick-Anon for the community of Rogue Clockworks near by. Gnomes are very gifted in the arcane arts and the mechanical arts but are also gifted rogues, clerics and warriors. Most Gnomes are well liked among the citizenry of Tunaria as they provide wondrous objects crafted of magic and inventiveness to amuse and delight. Currently the Gnomes live quite close to the hated Dark Elves. In fact the area they live in what will become the Lavastorm Mountains. what calamity will drive the Gnomes to undiscovered Faydwer along with the Elves and Dwarves. Racial Tensions: Dark elves, Trolls ( Necromancers can move about Neriak with few problems but are KOS to most Trolls.)

Gnomes spend most of their lives in their underground burrows and rarely come to the surface. They resemble dwarves to some extent, but have a ruddy brown skin and are more wiry and gnarled. Their hair is white, and somewhat sparse. Yet, their appearance is not what gets them noticed. Gnomes believe that building contraptions ranks right up there with saving the world. Renowned for their tinkering abilities, gnomes delight in the fact that they can build the right machine for any occasion. The many catastrophes, explosions, and traumatic amputations of limbs that have resulted from this pursuit of mechanics are not seen as tragedies, but rather as learning experiences.

Gnomes are an easy-going race, and most have a pleasant, carefree attitude. They do not believe in failures, only temporary setbacks. Their fascinations with gadgets, sprockets, springs, doodads, and-frighteningly enough--explosives, provide gnomes with an abundance of healthy respect (at a safe distance) from others. Not ones to waste time on such things as revenge, gnomes prefer to experiment. It cannot be helped if an experiment happens to go bad on someone who just offended them in some way.

These little people have many deities, among them Brell Serilis and Bristlebane. Some worship the fiery power of Solusek Ro, and of course warriors tend to draw strength from Rallos Zek. There are others rarely spoken of who are said to follow Bertoxxulous, the Plaguebringer.


Halflings
Agile and light of foot, Halflings are a friendly, jovial race that has an oftentimes-dangerous thirst for mischief and adventure. Halflings are a mystery, wrapped in an enigma and shrouded in trickery. The good natured, fun loving homebodies defend what is theirs as fiercely as a Troll guarding its food. Rivervale is home and all Halflings return when they are tired or home sick. The threat of the RunnyEye goblins looms over Rivervale and the Halflings like a shroud of doom. With the Wall under construction they hope to obtain some semblance of security for the population of Rivervale. The menace of the RunnyEye is a constant threat bringing forth a flood of Halfling adventurers who defend Rivervale to the last. Racial Tensions: Dark Elves, Trolls

Halflings are stocky little people who resemble humans, but are much more agile, dexterous, and light of foot than those clumsy humans. Their mischievous, jovial natures make them fun people to be around. Male halflings take great pride in their foot hair and strive to ensure that each hair is properly groomed.

Possessing naturally high dexterity and agility, halflings are viewed as quick little fellows who smile a lot. They have a love of travel, and their professions of cleric, druid, rogue, and warrior lead them on many an adventure. All is not fun and games for halflings, however. They take the encroaching goblin horde as a serious threat and defend Rivervale and Misty Thicket with all of their might.

Most halflings worship Bristlebane or Brell Serilis, unless they have chosen the druidic or warrior life. Halfling druids follow the call of Karana the Rainkeeper, and warriors swear to Rallos Zek the Warlord.

Trolls
Trolls are vile creatures ruled by feral and vicious instinct, making them the wholly unpredictable and savagely cruel predators of Norrath's swamps. Grobb is the crown jewel of Innothule swamp and the center of Troll civilization. Those who dwell in Grobb proudly are called Trolls. Within the confines of Grobb, Trolls live like the kings that they are. They fight and scrape for everything they have. Trolls are large and easily swayed by the Dark Elves and their promises of wealth and tasty gnome parts to snack upon. The Troll lives by the whim of his stomach and the lure of riches. The Troll wandering far from home is most likely still looking for a snack located preferably next to a pile of gold. Racial Tensions: Barbarians, Dwarves, Elves, Erudite, Gnomes, Halflings, Humans

Trolls typically stand about 8' tall, are extremely strong, ugly, stupid, dirty, and green of skin. With such characteristics, one can only assume that trolls are not pleasant folk. That assumption would be correct. Trolls bask in the glory of killing, eating their kills, and killing some more. They do this not out of instinct but out of purely evil motives. Their dark hearts pump malice and the need to destroy that which is not troll. They won't mess with ogres, though, as ogre meat is not good eating. If they could get away with it, they would dine on dark elves too (dark elves do not let them get away with it).

Being such evil beings, trolls pick up the weapons of a warrior, or follow the dark path of the shadowknight. Occasionally a troll with more than a minimum level of wisdom may become a shaman. Troll faith may be less than sophisticated, but their deities don't require much more than that they behave according to their nature. Their gods are Cazic-Thule, Innoruuk and Rallos Zek


Lizard Men and Iksar
The Lizardmen, Iksar, children of Cazic-Thule, are the only known survivors on the continent of Kunark except for the Frogloks that will come over from Tunaria. The Iksar had their Empire Serbilis and caused terror in a constant war. They were known for feasting on other's dead bodies as there was little else to eat on the continent of Kunark. The content was also a breeding ground for Dragons, perhaps the most dangerous creatures on the face of Norrath. This left the Iksar in the hands of the Shissar and their Dragon allies. The Iksar were quickly turned into slaves. Given the opportunity, the Iksar soon became the most dominant race on Kunark. They fought back against the Dragons and their empire grew quickly. Faced with the possibility of being destroyed the Dragons formed the Ring of Scale, an organization with members hailing from all of the races of Norrath. The evil children of Cazic-Thule-- The Faceless, live deep in the forest called the Ferrott. They love nothing more than to strike fear into the hearts of the hearts of people and wage war with whoever they find. The only other place Lizardmen can be found is inside the castle of Cazic-Thule himself. They are well known for being extremely dangerous hand-to-hand fighters and some can even cast magic as well. The Lizardmen are likely either the ancestors or the descendants of the Iksar. Though their size and skin colors have drastically changed and they have slightly different classes amongst themselves.

The god Cazic-Thule created them during the Elder Age, and they began a tribal existence on Kunark thousands of years in Norrath's past. Eventually, they united to form a massive empire, which later collapsed, leaving behind an intricate and proud history as well as a rich setting for adventurers to explore. The Iksar have a background that most races would consider evil in nature, in that conquest, enslavement, and general war and destruction were the norm.

The Iksar have an interesting variety of classes available to them due to their unique history and circumstances. Their current tribal nature limits their general mystic abilities to the shamanic arts, yet the fallen Iksar Empire's strong involvement in necromancy allows them to choose the path of the necromancer or shadowknight. They are also afforded the special opportunity that, until now, was limited only to the human race: Iksar may become monks. Their unique reptilian body type gives Iksar monks special magically enhanced attacks. And, of course, what warlike race would not have an abundance of warriors? These scaly beasts make up for the humans' complete rejection of Cazic-Thule. There are no agnostic Iksar, nor are there any who worship any other deity.


Ogres
Big, ugly, and stupid, but don't let them hear you say that last part. Ogres are barbaric to the extreme and are known to smash anything, even other humanoids and eat it. They have been known to eat other things as well that most would shudder to think of putting in their mouth. But this wasn't always true, at one time they were fierce and cunning warriors, the children of the Warlord Rallos Zek. They enslaved their cousins, the Orcs, who were a bit more intelligent but too broken to stand against the united Ogres. They also worked along side Rallos Zek's third race, the Giants, who had existed long before the Ogres had. The Ogres continued to conquer lands until all of Tunaria. They grew in strength and intelligence and were even capable of casting spells. Their Empire was large and unmatched. However they grew tired of Norrath and when they learned of the other planes they decided to conquer them as well. Rallos Zek was thrilled when his creations challenged the gods of the Plane of Earth and they managed to take, even hold the plane for a while. However they couldn't keep it up and eventually they were driven back by the gods. For their arrogance the Ogres, and the Giants and the Orcs by association, were stripped of their intelligence and magic. Their empire was destroyed and has never recovered. To this day they've never regained the arts of magic and live in the ruins of civilization.

Ogres are incredibly massive beings. With an average height of approximately 10' and weight well over four hundred pounds, they are truly immovable objects. Due to their size, they are the strongest of all the races and are tied with trolls in pure stamina. Conversely, they are the least intelligent race, whose lives consist mostly of smashing things and eating them. An ogre's strength is its strength. With the ability to use heavy weapons, ogres become either warriors or shadowknights. Whether they truly understand their vocations, the smarter races have not yet decided. But, thanks to the mystery of evolution, an ogre is sometimes born with a bit more smarts, and may decide--or fall into by mistake--the profession of shaman.

Orcs
Bred for battle and single-minded in their desire for conquest. Their fate after the defeat of Rallos’s forces is unknown. They are now scattered in isolation throughout Norrath.

Shissar
Race of intelligent snake men which explored dark rites and forgotten evils, the entire race was destroyed by the gods for their brutal actions towards the Iksar

Giants
Once lived in the fertile grasslands of the Northlands and once represented one of the most successful civilizations that have been recorded. They were strong and loyal to their creator Rallos Zek and it is said that they were cursed by the gods for following him in his attempts to destroy the plane of earth. The Giants were scattered to all ends of Norrath where they have adapted to live in the environment they've been placed in. The Giants to this day live in isolation and will destroy anyone they come across.

Goblins
Followers of Rallos Zek, no records of their ways remains, they also are only living in small scattered remains of what they once were. The Goblins have never managed to keep peace with anyone for very long. They were never a powerful people and tended to have short memories as a society. When the Ogres were forced from the Plane of Earth, the Goblins were punished alongside the Orcs, Giants and Ogres out of pure association of having the same forefather. Although exactly what was done to them has never been known, the Goblins have been split into many different factions all across Norrath much as the Giants have. The RunnyEye Goblins established the city of RunnyEye under the leadership and guidance of Brell who took them as his followers. They have managed to put themselves all across Antonica and even into Faydwer. The ice goblins of EverFrost have become light blue, those that live in Lavastorm Moutains have become red and are called Fire Goblins and those that have managed to make their home underwater have become blue-green and are called Aqua Goblins. The state of RunnyEye Citadel may well explain why so many of the Goblins are green as well. It has green water and glowing green walls. They may seem quite bestial and unintelligent until you see one of their wizards in action.

Dragons
Born of Veeshan’s eggs, they are powerful beings of great intellect, wisdom and strength and are capable of flying.

Sarnak
A result of a foul experiment by King Atrebe during his reign. The Iksar went about creating new races with their newfound magic. They created the Sarnak, a race of Dragonmen by combining one of their own and a Dragon. The Froglok and Sarnak slaves rebelled against their Iksar masters. With all these people attacking their cities and no single leadership the Iksars were soon pushed back to just a single city. Thankfully for them once the races had sufficiently defeated the Iksar they established their own homes upon the continent and began to war amongst one another.

Froglok
Frog men, a holy race, masters of the swamps for many centuries until the Trolls moved into Antonica from Kunark. Their home is Guk in Innothule Swamp. Their eggs need to be lain within the swampy waters to hatch. Peace with the Trolls is impossible, so it would seem their people are doomed unless the Trolls and their allies, the Ogres are somehow defeated by some outside force and driven from their lands. With their dead piling up, evil has cursed the dead Frogloks and turned them into Ghouls that now attack their former friends. Frogloks are near unmatched swimmers, very susceptible to magical lightening and fire, nor are they very strong. The Frogloks lived in capitivity on Kunark for many years as slaves through the Iksar's many wars. In both homes the Frogloks now find themselves under seige and looking for allies.

Sokokar
Also a creation of King Atreve, a cross between dragons and froglok

Gnolls
Jackal-men, Wolf-men, Doggies... There are many names applied to these canine humanoids who make their home in Qeynos Hills and the Planes of Karana. They are very much hated and have few, if any allies. They were created by Brell, no doubt to see that his influence reached all across the lands. When the humans came down from Halas to seperate themselves from their Barbarian cousins they made their home in Qeynos. However this city was discovered too late to be very close to Black Burrow, the home of the Gnolls. Originally the Gnolls welcomed the barbarians and humans with open arms and they could have lived in peace, however a barbarian cut off the paw of one of their high shamans, Opolla. This turned into an all out war that has been going on until this day. They've sent a pack into the Planes of Karana in hopes of finding a new place to establish home. These Gnolls call themselves clan Split Paw and have found a temporary home, but it isn't large enough yet to move all the Gnolls from Black Burrow into. To survive this age the Gnolls will have to get some powerful heroes, make some allies or somehow have the humans driven from their lands by some outside force. Bertoxxulus- The Plaguebringer has lead some of the desperate Gnolls away from their father, Brell Serilis, and created a group known as the Gnoll Reavers.

Vah Shir
The Vah Shir civilization is based upon strong tribal roots. The Vah Shir do not keep written records. Due to this, folklore plays a vital role within their society as does honor. Shamans and Bards form the focus of their civilizations political system as the lore keepers and teachers of the tribe. Their unwillingness to document research performed by Vah Shir scholars has prevented them from achieving the ability to explore any of the magics available to the pure casters.

Shamans and Bards are not the only classes that make up the Vah Shir community. A Vah Shir's superb agility lends well to the disciplines of the Rogue, and their endurance makes for a formidable Warrior. Being in tune with nature and understanding the ways of the beast, a Vah Shir Beastlord will always make a valued companion while traversing dangerous lands.
Since the Vah Shir are a feline species, they have innate abilities to see in the darkest of nights.

Barbarians
Barbarians are the hardy stock of Northmen from which all humans originated. They are the fierce, steely warriors of legend. The Wolves of the North inhabit and defend the cold and frigid wasteland that few would desire with great zeal. The Shamans of The Tribunal lead the folk of Halas spiritually. Warriors and Rogues provide the muscle necessary to survive in the Northern Wastes of Permafrost. Halas is where Barbarians begin their lives. From here Barbarians must make their way in the world surrounded by enemies such as vile goblins, simpering orcs, and perhaps one of the most powerful dragons on Tunaria Lady Vox. Luckily the Dwarves of Moradhin are nearby to aid their tall allies. Apparently the Barbarians isolationist polices do not extend to them. Barbarians also tolerate other humans to a point. Racial Tensions: Dark Elves, Trolls

A hardier variety of the human race, barbarians inhabit the cold and rugged northlands. Their city of Halas is located in the mountains of Everfrost. Though referred to as barbarians by the rest of the world, these proud people refer to themselves as "Northmen." Because of their harsh environment and warlike culture, they are well suited to being warriors. They are generally unwashed and rugged, possessing very few social graces. Barbarians take great pride in clan lineage, and eating, drinking, and battle are their preferred pastimes.

Although the vast majority of barbarians become warriors for the great honor this will bring to their clans, some feel the mystical call to serve as clan shamans, lending their magical abilities to further their clan's standing. Yet, there is one more class that a barbarian may become, and that is the class of the rogue. Barbarian rogues are usually loners who have decided to bring their clan honor through less respectable methods. Achieving honor is what is important, not how it is achieved. No matter their chosen profession, barbarians are a force to be reckoned with. The very love they have for a fight is what makes them a true danger.

Humans
Humans of the east tend to be drawn into the paths of the shadow, often binding themselves to the forbidden dark arts. They can be Bards, Clerics, Enchanters, Magicians, Necromancers, Rogues, Shadowknights, Warriors, or Wizards. Humans of the west are the most versatile in their innate knowledge of magic, faith, and the sword. They can be Bards, Clerics, Druids, Enchanters, Magicians, Monks, Paladins, Rangers, Rogues, Warriors, or Wizards. The denizens of the regal city of Qeynos and harsh Freeport are as different from each other as day and night. The folk of Qeynos are generally good natured, well mannered and hospitable. The people who inhabit Freeport are indifferent to most things but some are disciples of dark and foul gods. As of this time the Humans are treading a dark path that leads to civil war and unrest in Tunaria. The other races they share Tunaria with watch in fear as the impending war threatens to tear Tunaria apart. Racial Tensions: Dark Elves, Trolls ( Necromancers and Shadow Knights are like the other evil classes unwelcome in good aligned communities. )

Humans are the most abundant race in Norrath. Some would say only rats outnumber them, but then again no one has taken a census of either humans or rats. Good, neutral, or evil, humans pursue a gamut of philosophies.

With the exception of shamanic studies only, humans may pursue the professions of their choice undeterred by racial shortcomings. From bard to wizard, they can do it all. But if they are doing it at night, they'd best have a good light source, or else they will have to camp until dawn in order to see their hands in front of their faces. Humans are an open-minded lot. They accept almost any other race, and believe in just about any deity other than Cazic-Thule the Faceless. They occupy and dominate the two main human cities, Qeynos and Freeport. In these bustling metropolises, humans begin their journeys.

Erudites
Erudites are peerless in their affinity and mastery of the arcane sciences. The High Men of Tunaria, an off shoot of the Human race, the followers of Erud, the believers of his dream of a better place for his people far from the strife and civil war the lower races seem to reveal in. Erudites live a more comfortable lifestyle thanks to their mental capabilities. However they now shun physical labor as they consider it low and better left to their lesser. They also consider physical strength and muscles disgusting and crude. Currently the Erudites are as a race preparing to leave Tunaria for better shores across the sea. What shall become of Highbourne? Racial Tensions: Dark Elves, Trolls ( Necromancers and Shadow Knights have difficulties getting around just like any evil class.)

Being the exact opposite of the barbarian Northmen, the high man prides himself on his intellectual abilities and social graces, to the almost total exclusion of the physical disciplines. They take satisfaction in their superiority over all other races. Any obstacle can be overcome with intelligence and wisdom. Pick up a sword? Bah, leave that to the lesser, unsophisticated beasts of the land. The only exception is for Erudite paladins and shadowknights.

Erudites have very few friends outside their own race. Others find them difficult to associate with. Their snooty attitudes and overblown egos can be said to be the main factors in this. But only a party of ignorant adventurers would turn an Erudite away from joining them, for their high intelligence and facile use of magic comes in handy, especially when facing a powerful enemy. As clerics, mages, enchanters, necromancers, and wizards, Erudites excel when magic is needed.

Erudites--other than wizards--tend to ally themselves with either Prexus the Oceanlord, or Quellious the Tranquil. Wizards utilize the firepower of Solusek Ro in their magic and so gravitate toward him. There is a sect of Erudites who have turned to the evil Cazic-Thule, and are branded heretics.

Centaurs
Proud and strong, the Centaurs are among the oldest of the peoples of the World, and their kingdoms still stretch across the heart of the plains, little changed from the nations they built in the first Age of the World. Nobility and honor shine in the eyes of every Centaur, and it is said that the deeds of ancient Centaur heroes were the inspiration for the High King's conception of chivalry and the Code. Ages of war and strife have thinned the numbers of these mighty beings, but great Cohorts still ride to crusade from the depths of the plains. The children of Kenaryn the Hunter, Centaurs have inherited their father's sense of duty, and rarely feel content, even in times of tranquility. Centaur Warriors are always galloping away to chase the winds and hunt the enemies of the World. It is said that the Centaurs were the first to teach the ways of Law and War to the Sons of Men, and the Horse Princes are still strong allies of Humankind. Their feuds with the Elves are legendary, going back Ages to the time when the Beast Lords walked the World. Swift of hoof, true to their word and strong in battle, Centaurs are worthy allies and deadly adversaries

Minotaurs
Born of sorcery and bred for combat, the strength of these half-breed Giants is matched only by their brutality. Originally created in the frigid Northlands, the Minotaurs served as shock troops of the Deathless Empire, but have since broken away from their Elvish masters and carved out domains of their own. Driven by their abject hatred of all the World's children, the Minotaurs have fought legendary wars against the Elves that made them, the hardy Northmen, and the Centaurs of the plains. Where once raiding parties of Minotaurs were feared throughout the World, these bitter wars greatly thinned their numbers, and in the days since the Turning, the Minotaurs have rarely been seen in the lands of Men. More Beast than Man, the bull-headed Minotaurs are terrible to behold, and their incredible strength and stamina make them even more terrifying in battle.
