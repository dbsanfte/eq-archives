Welcome to the Tower of Lore

The Tower of Lore is a guild of scholars, dedicated to the discovery and preservation of knowledge. All are welcome within the Tower’s walls and everyone may have a purpose: be it safeguarding the Tower’s record keepers on their adventures, passing the knowledge gained from the Tower’s studies, or even combing the depths of long forgotten tombs in search of lost artifacts. The only requirements are a desire to learn and a willingness to help a guild member in need.

The Keepers of Knowledge are the record keepers of the Tower. They are made up of the Wizards, Necromancers, Magicians, and Enchanters of the guild. As the main researchers of the guild, the Keepers spend most of their days going over the ancient texts looking for clues to the whereabouts of lost artifacts and tomes of knowledge.

The Guardians of Knowledge have been charged with the Tower’s protection. They fill their ranks from the Warriors, Paladins, Rangers, and Shadow Knights of the guild. They are the sword and the shield of the Tower of Lore. Some knowledge was never meant for the minds of mortals and this knowledge needs to be safeguarded.

The Speakers of Knowledge are the voice of the Tower, much as the name suggests. It is their duty to communicate the Tower’s discoveries across Tunaria. They are composed of the Clerics, Shamans, and Druids, as the oratory skills of the clergy are best suited to this task.

The Seekers of Knowledge have the harrowing task of searching the ruins and dungeons in search of Knowledge that has been lost to the sands of time. Monks, Rogues, and Bards all belong to the Seekers. Their unique abilities allow them to perform their task with brilliant efficiency.

The four factions of the guild are each headed by an Elder. Each Elder is responsible for choosing one Officer of each class in their faction to aid in the factions duties. The Elders as a whole are responsible for making changes to guild policy and also choosing new Elders for any vacant faction seats. They will put the issue to an Elder vote, with the deciding vote going to the Guildmaster in the event of a tie. If a seat is currently empty the Guildmaster may choose a stand-in for the Elder’s position until the current Elder’s have decided on whom will fill the position.
