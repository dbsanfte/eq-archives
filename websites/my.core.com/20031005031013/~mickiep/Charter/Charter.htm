Welcome to the Tower of Lore

The Tower of Lore is a guild of scholars, dedicated to the discovery and preservation of knowledge. All are welcome within the Tower’s walls and everyone may have a purpose: be it safeguarding the Tower’s record keepers on their adventures, passing the knowledge gained from the Tower’s studies, or even combing the depths of long forgotten tombs in search of lost artifacts. The only requirements are a desire to learn and a willingness to help a guild member in need.

The Keepers of Knowledge are the record keepers of the Tower. They act as the main researchers of the guild, spending most of their days going over the ancient texts looking for clues to the whereabouts of lost artifacts and tomes of knowledge. The more adventurous of the Keepers lead expeditions to the ancient ruins and monsters layers in the hopes of recovering that which was lost.

The Guardians of Knowledge have been charged with the Tower’s protection. They act as the sword and the shield of the Tower of Lore. Some act as bodyguards for the Keepers on their expeditions. Others lead expeditions themselves, to prevent the ambitious from causing too much harm to the world. Some knowledge was never meant for the minds of mortals and this knowledge needs to be safeguarded.

The two sides of the guild are each headed by an Elder. Both Elders are responsible for choosing their Officer's to aid in their duties. The Elders as a whole are responsible for making changes to guild policy and also choosing the new Elder or Master for any vacant positions. They will put the issue to an Elder vote, with the deciding vote going to the Guildmaster in the event of a tie. If a seat is currently empty the Guildmaster may choose a stand-in for the Elder’s position until the current Elder’s have decided on whom will fill the position.
