Rules of Conduct

(1) Please keep your out of character (OOC) conversations off of the guild channel. Most of us are role players and we would appreciate it if you stayed in character as much as possible. This is a role-playing guild so use private tells or the group channel for your OOC business.

(2) Don’t be greedy. We have all grouped with that person that takes more than their fair share of the loot. Don’t be that person. There are as many ways of dividing loot as there are groups so I am not going to detail the many possibilities here. The preferred method within the guild is the honor system, don’t take more than your fair share and rare drops go to whoever can make the most use of them.

(3) No begging. We have equipment available for guildmembers and it is OK to ask for it if you want it. Don’t let it become a problem. I trust everyone in the guild to know the difference between asking and begging, just don’t cross the line and there won’t be any problems.

(4) If you go to your favorite spot and find that it’s already camped, move on. Some areas are big enough to keep two groups happy, just ask the group that’s there if it’s ok to share. If they say no, move on. You’re a representative of the Tower and you need to show some humility – it’s a virtue so I am told.

(5) Ask before inviting someone to your group. It’s ok to invite without asking if someone is shouting for a group. Any other time ask first.

(6) Training and Kill/Loot stealing: Don’t do it. I shouldn’t even have to comment on that one.
