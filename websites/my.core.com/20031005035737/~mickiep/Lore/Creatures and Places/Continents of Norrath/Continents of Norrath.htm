Of the Continents of Norrath

Norrath
The known world of EverQuest is referred to by scholars as Norrath, although very few are aware of its entirety. Five continents fill the hemisphere, and whether other lands lay beyond the oceans to the east and west is unknown. Here you will find the words of an ancient scroll regarding the five continents of Norrath, and the remnants of a history fortold many centuries ago.

~ Tunaria/Antonica
Tunaria will come to be known as Antonica, the largest and most populated continent on Norrath. This change will come as a result of a human ruler by the name of Antonius Bale from the Free City of Qeynos. It is on Tunaria where Tunare will first place Her High Elves.

~ Faydwer
I have had a vision, in which I see the High Elf civilization from Antonica relocated to Faydwer after the death of the Elddar Forest. Faydwer is also rumored to be the home of two of the oldest creatures on all of Norrath, Mayong Mistmoore, the Elf-Vampire and Phinigel Autropos, the last living Kedge. The name, Faydwer shall be given to this continent by the High Elves of Antonica when they land on its shores.

~ Kunark
Kunark is a continent of ancient ruins and hostile lands. Records tell tale of empires lost to time, their ruined cities and hidden tombs still filled with treasures. The evil hand of Cazic-Thule touched this land and spawned the Iksar, a powerful reptilian race. Once the predominant force, they now strive to rise from the ashes and reclaim their glory. The creatures in Kunark are quite formidable and regard all as invaders. Only the elven outpost of Firiona Vie shall offer a safe haven for adventurers. This is no place for the weak.

~ Velious
In ages past, the great crystalline dragon Veeshan chose Norrath as home for her brood, clawing the earth and creating great scars in which she deposited them, to grow and flourish. In later times, when ogres, giants and goblins had conquered much of the planet, their arrogance in attempting an assault upon the Plane of Earth brought retribution from The Rathe in the form of a great curse. The once fertile homeland of the giants was frozen along with those of its surviving inhabitants who were unable to flee. Only a group of shipwrecked dwarves managed to find their way beyond the Frigid Barrier to reach the icy continent of Velious, and they became the Coldain who shall be rediscovered by the gnomes of Ak`Anon, with the help of their marvelous ship, the Icebreaker.

~ Odus
Odus shall be founded by a human named Erud who, along with his followers, will abandon Antonica searching for a place to establish a new enlightened society. Like any "enlightened" conquerors, the Erudites will take it upon themselves to forcibly relocate the native inhabitants of Odus. The Kerra, a proud and noble feline race, shall be pushed from their lush homeland to a small island off the western coast of Odus.
