Fletching Arrows:

To fletch an arrow you need a head, a shaft, a vane/fletch and a nock.
Range and dmg is related to a Class 1 Wood Point Arrow whith large nock: dmg 1, range 50.
After the last patch the price for arrow components increased a lot, but you get now 5 arrows out of a set of components. If you put more then 1 of each component into the fletching kitt, you still get only one arrow.

Archery

The maximum damage is bow damage + arrow damage (as far as I experienced).
The total range is the range of the bow. Arrow range does not influence the total range.
But it seems that the chance to hit is much lower when you shoot over a higher range than the arrows range.
For example you use an 125 range bow and 100 range arrows. Than you can shoot at over a distance of 125, but you have only up to 100 range a good chance to hit.

If you find any mistake on this site or know anything I didn't mention here or have more exact values please e-mail me XizorTB@gmx.net.
