Event: Regatta im Ocean of Tears. Samstag 5.6.99 ab 20 Uhr

Am nächsten Samstag, dem 5. Juni, veranstaltet die Decent Folks Guild die erste Norrathsche Regatta! Das Ereignis wird stattfinden auf der Amazoneninsel der Erollisi Marr-Schwesternschaft im Ocean of Tears. Die Happy Hour der Gilde in Kaladim fällt an diesem Tag aus, die Gäste können uns wie üblich um 20 Uhr in der Greybloom Tavern treffen und mit uns zusammen die Überfahrt machen oder sich selbstständig bis 21 Uhr auf der Insel einfinden. Von Freeport aus ist es der erste Stop des Fährschiffes, von Kaladim aus der zweite. Jeder, der lebend sichtbar an den Inselwachen vorbeikommt, ist uns willkommen!

Die Regatta wird mit Ruderbooten durchgeführt und führt von der Amazoneninsel aus einmal um das Orakel von K’Arnon, das ist eine Strecke von einigen Minuten. Das Startgeld beträgt pro Boot zehn Goldstücke, es können zwei bis vier Ruderer pro Boot antreten. Wir werden vier bis sechs Boote zur Verfügung haben, falls es mehr Teams gibt - wovon wir ausgehen - werden mehrere Vorläufe stattfinden, bevor die besten Boote das Finale austragen. Das Benutzen der Boote ist sehr einfach, man klinkt sich ein und bewegt es dann so, wie man zu Fuß geht. Vor dem Start können sich die Teilnehmer mit ihren Booten vertraut machen. Magische Beschleunigung eines Bootes ist ein Disqualifikationsgrund! Das Siegerteam erhält das gesamte Startgeld plus einige nette Zusatzpreise.

Zur Unterhaltung der Zuschauer werden auch am Strand verschiedene Lustbarkeiten angeboten, u.a. das bekannte Kiolanuß-Tauchen. Die Amazoneninsel verfügt auch über eine Auswahl erlesener Getränke, wie das schmackhafte Black Burrow Stout. Nach der Preisverleihung ergeben sich Ausflugsmöglichkeiten z.B. zur nahegelegenen Pirateninsel...

Falls Ihr tropische Strände, gutes Bier und eine Insel voll leichtbekleideter Amazonen mögt, dann dürft Ihr Euch diesen Event keinesfalls entgehen lassen!

Für das Regatta-Team, Tolund, Decent Folks Guild.

The Invisible Guild

Recently my Gnome warrior was called away by a private message from another, higher level Gnome warrior. He gave me 2 pieces of very nice armor free of charge. Then he told me never to sell them. When I found something better I should find a lower level gnome fighter and give them to him. I swore that I would do this. Immediately I felt involved in something real, a genuine effort to help the few souls brave enough to play a Gnome warrior. Since then I've started going out of my way to help younger Gnome warriors. The game experience has been better than any quest. So I'm inviting all Gnome warriors on all servers to join this invisible guild. It isn't a guild in the official sense, just a collection of characters that agree to help each other. To join, pick some piece of equipment that you would normally sell and give it to a struggling fighter. Tell them about the Invisible Guild of Gnome Warriors and invite them to join as well. The won't get a longer name (it's invisible, after all) but they will get a purpose in life. Other races and classes are free to form their own invisible guilds, but I think it will be most beneficial (and fun) for unpopular race/class combinations.

EQ Zustaz Erweiterung: Neues hier nur noch in English. Mein Flieger geht in wenigen Stunden.

I got to talking with Brad McQuaid and Andy Sites of Verant while at E3 today, who very kindly provided me with some further details concerning the upcoming EverQuest expansion set (again, the working title). First and foremost among these is the fact that the current release of EverQuest is by far the DevTeam's primary concern at the moment. No staff has been pulled from performing bug fixes and code updates to the current game; all staff who are working on the expansion have not been working on the current game for some time. Currently there are 8 people concentrating on the expansion: 4 artists/animators/modelers, who are working on monster frames, character skins and zone graphics and 4 others who are concentrating on new zones. Again, these staff members have not been pulled from current projects in order to work on the expansion, in fact, Verant is in the process of creating a number of important features and improvements to the game.

Here's a rather large list of the information concerning the expansion set that I was able to obtain form the guys at Verant:

1.The new player race, the Iksar, is a completely new variant of the lizardman, complete with clothing graphics (I saw one equipped with a robe--tail sticking through :). These reptilian humanoids are distantly related to the lizardmen of Antonica, although much more advanced. 2.Currently only 1 zone has been created: "The Field of Bone", a scorched-earth type of land with Iksar ruins, a gigantic walled temple and a large tower, among other "to-be-added" landmarks and features. 3.The Iksar at one time ruled all of Kunark--an empire built upon the foundation of slavery of lesser lizardmen. Due to internal conflict and strife, the empire collapsed long ago. Now only vestiges and scattered tribes of Iksar stand testament to their once mighty ruling state. 4.Animated trees (sway in the wind and look great) will be included in the expansion. These new trees actually consist of fewer polygons than the current, non-animated foliage. 5.Immersiveness of environment is a key point in this expansion... Verant hopes to go beyond animated trees and create other interactive and/or non-static scenery and surroundings. 6.4 new monsters have been created so far: The Cockatrice, Leech, Mosquito (giant) and Giant Scorpion (also saw a huge scorp which may server as the basis for some kind of unique monster, ala Queen Klicnik). 7.All monsters created for the expansion set will be completely unique. This means new models and skins for each. 8.There will be over 20 brand new monsters in the new expansion. 9.The number of zones that will be included within the new continent is currently unverified, although reports indicate somewhere around 15 or 25. However, at this time, not even Verant knows how many they'll be able to squeeze in. Time will tell. 10.The new continent of Kunark will serve as a means to deliver more of the rich background story and plot that underlies Norrath's history and peoples. Brad and the rest of the Verant team look forward to opening up further interaction with EQ's back story and fiction through the expansion set, as well as future updates to the main game.

11.Programmers, who will be handling the core of the new expansions new features and improvements, will be jumping into the project within a couple of months, after the majority of EverQuest's current needs are taken care of. 12.The current slated release for the expansion set is the First Quarter or 2000. Verant was hoping to release the set at the end of '99, but that (very preliminary) release window has been pushed back in order to allow them more time to concentrate on the current state of EverQuest as well as the large number of features that they wish to include with the expansion. 13.The expansion pack will follow a release strategy similar to that used by Origin with "The Second Age" expansion set for Ultima Online; current players will be offered the expansion for a very nominal fee and future releases of EverQuest will be released at its current price but with the expansion bundled with it. Verant's main goal with this expansion is not to generate revenue (hence the low to free price structure), but to continue to develop the game in new and interesting ways so that we as players will continue to enjoy the world of Norrath. well, that about does it for the current expansion info that I posses... expect more soon! In addition, I got my grubby little hands on some general game information, which I'll be posting shortly. Thanks to Brad McQuaid, Andy Sites, Mike Hutchins, Ryan Palacio, John Smedley and all of the rest from Verant for both the tidbits and the courtesy shown to me at the Exposition! It was very nice meeting all of you, as well as the numerous fans of Stratics that I happened to bump into while visiting the different displays and booths.
