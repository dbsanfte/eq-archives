To be clear, there is no specific injunction against killing Karg. There is a overall prohibition against the slaying of Good-Aligned NPCS- period. To my personal knowledge this includes any creature or Named Mob who's faction is oriented along the Good Axis. This is *regardless* of whether or not said creature happens to /con dubious or such to you personally! For example- the Paladins in Felwithe /con apprehensive to mac. They don't like him. That does NOT mean I can kill them. They are clearly "good" guys.

THe clan was allways intended as a good-aligned guild. While there is no formal punishment for breaking this Prohibition, it's certainly not favored.
