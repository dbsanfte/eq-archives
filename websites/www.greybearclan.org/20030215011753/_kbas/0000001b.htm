CHARTER FOR THE GREY BEAR CLAN GUILD

PURPOSE

The Grey Bear Clan shall be an Everquest guild with the primary purpose of increasing the Members enjoyment of the world of Norrath, through companionship, cooperation and role play . We are primarily Barbarians, although the guild will not be restricted to Barbarians. Guild Members will be expected to aid one another, as well as developing relationships with other Norrathian guilds to promote a greater online experience.

NAME

The Guild shall be known as the Grey Bear Clan and shall play on the Mithaniel Marr Server .

MEMBERSHIP

The Clan shall initially consist of Ten (10) original Members, who shall each forward an appropriate E-Mail message to Verant (tm) for the purpose of establishing the Clan. Thereafter, Membership shall be by invitation only, upon the following terms and conditions:

1. The Clan is primarily intended for older players who may not have as much playing time available as younger players. Therefore, at least Fifty percent (50%) of the Members should be Twenty-Five (25) years old, or older. The Members shall endeavor to keep the Clan open, but with this principle in mind.

2. Clan Members shall, at all times, observe all known rules promulgated by Verant (tm), as may be amended from time to time. Any knowing violation of any rule will be grounds for immediate dismissal from the Clan. The final decision concerning dismissal shall be in the sole discretion of the Guild Leader or the Council (see below).

3. Clan Members shall immediately report any known or suspected bug to the Council, and to Verant (tm) as outlined in the Everquest manual.

4. The Grey Bear Clan shall not offer Membership to Player Killers.

5. Members shall ALWAYS help one another when possible. However, Members are not expected to place themselves in situations of sure death, or to help much lower level Members unfairly. Failure to aid a Member should be reported to the Council for discussion. Willful failure to aid may be grounds for dismissal.

6. The Council shall make rules as determined from time to time.

7. Ogres, Trolls and Dark Elves shall not be offered Membership.

8. Shadowknights and Necromancers shall not be offered Membership.

9. Members should not worship any "evil" deity.

10. While Members are required to assist one another, there is no such general requirement to help non-Members. In fact, many of our Shaman brethren, as followers of the Six Hammers, may be required to let others "lie in their own beds" rather than render aid.

GOVERNANCE

The Clan shall be governed by the Grey Bear Clan Council of Elders (the "Council"), which shall consist of the Guild Leader and at least Four (4) other Members. The Council will meet not less than every two weeks to vote on any issues to be discussed, and to ratify, or vote on the addition of new Members. An affirmative vote of any three Council members shall be a sufficient vote authorizing any Council action. However, any Guildwar decree, must be unanimous. Each regular Council Member will be assigned as overseer to one of the four Circles, as described below. In the event of a dispute which arises between members, and which the Circle leader is unable to resolve, the appropriate Council member shall attempt resolve the issue personally. In the event the Council member is unable to personally resolve the issue then the matter will be submitted to the full Council.

The Council shall be assisted by the Circle of the Clan, who shall advise the Council. Circle Members should meet at least once every two weeks, and shall be responsible for keeping the Lore of the Clan as well as any and all useful information, such as maps, other guilds, items. Etc... All Members should seek out a Circle Member as soon as possible to share such useful information. A Circle Member should attend the Council sessions to report back. The Circle shall appoint a Circle Member to attend each Council meeting, as convenient . The Circle will be run by the Circle Master, who shall be appointed by the Guild Leader. The Circle should consist of not less than five (5) Members.

Circle Leaders will be appointed to help in the recruiting of new members. At least two (2) Circle Leaders shall be appointed for each division of levels. For example, prospective members, or Members between levels one (1) through ten (10) would contact one of the Circle Leaders for that level range. The Circle Leaders shall recommend members, render aid as appropriate, and handle disputes as they may arise passing along such information to the Guild Leader or Council as necessary. The Circle designations shall be as follows:

A. Circle of Initiates (levels 1-9) B. Circle of Adventurers (levels 10-19) C. Circle of Masters (levels 20-29) D. Circle of the Grey Bear (levels 30+)

Circle Members will advise the Council whether Lore or information should be imparted to the Members as a whole, or only a portion thereof ( for ex: Members over a certain level). Circle Members will also be responsible to determine rules of conduct within the Clan, other than those listed in the Charter, and to submit same to the Council for a final vote.

Likewise the Guild Leader should be available at least once per week for the purpose of helping and information, in addition to other duties, including meeting with the GM for mandatory Guild meetings. The Circle Master and Guild Leader shall conduct all meetings and hearings in Halas, as shall the Council, or by the guild options available. In the event that the player is elsewhere, or in another part of Norrath, a proxy shall be appointed, and announced. The Clan will endeavor to establish a web site as soon as practical.
