Success in Rathe Mtns!  New event, Wed, April 19th....Be there!

My fellow Knights and stalwart friends in Grey Bear Clan…

Thank all of thee who came with us last night to the Rathe Mountains to hunt, and to search the mountains for word of the conspiracy that is even now being fomented against the Grey Bear Clan and the Knights of Marr. After my near brush with death just over a week ago in these same mountains, when I overheard talk against our guilds from a motley collection of creatures, Kilamor and I were most anxious to see what more could be uncovered.

We had great success in our foray into the Rathe Mountains this past weekend. Tyre, Kilamor, Renen, Gohei, Ceenedra, Cajun, Aaedor, Kaebil, Livia, and ????? from the Grey Bear Clan joined Basherbeau, Llyara, Shaphedra, Qila, Xanderax, Raelyn, Jaellra, Conundrum, Merrick, Linbyen, Solynx, Llywellen, Cmos, and Kailor from the Knights of Marr. It was a combined force to make all those of evil intent who beheld our might shudder and flee in terror.

After breaking up into four groups, we headed out to comb the mountains for sign of the conspirators, hunting down anything evil in our path. It was with great relief that we heard a shout go up from the group searching the Lizzie camps! Shaphedra and Raelyn sent out the call to come to their aid, for they had discovered members of the Dark Fallen lying in wait high on a mountainside.

As the single groups struggled to keep the Dark Fallen from fleeing, the other three groups converged on their location. Our group was one of the first to arrive and joined in the fray, swords and axes high. With so many brave combatants on our side, twas not long before the dark ones started shouting their surrender.

We were all under strict orders not to kill these vile cretins, for we needed what information they carried of their alliance with the Iksar, Dark Alliance, and the corrupt guards of Qeynos and Freeport. We knew that at least these groups were involved, as I had overheard them all talking in hushed tones in an earlier meeting in the mountains.

With many oaths and curses flung back and forth, the surrendered Dark Fallen gathered before the combined might of the Knights of Marr and Grey Bear Clan. Brokchop, Ngamok, Gramp, Domitia, and Agak comprised a scouting party sent to meet with their co-conspirators, and were among those seized for questioning.

Tempers of Bears, Knights and the Fallen were running high after such a battle, and several times during the following session of inquisition, we had to pull combatants apart, so the questioning could continue. Listen, and I will relay what was uncovered in this first foray. The questioning began with Llyara and Brokchop. The following is an excerpt from the questioning: ___________________________________

Llyara: "We had heard rumors of thee. Ulthas was shown visions of those marked with the signs of the Bear straying from the path of light. We prayed against hope 'twas not true. I see we were mistaken. Why hast thou betrayed thy Grey Bear heritage, and the faith that the Spirit of the Grey Bear placed in thee, honoring thee with his mark? Why hast thou desecrated his holy spirit by burning this mark from thy body?"

Brokchop spits in Llyara's face and snarls.

Brokchop: "Bah! De mitee Grey Bear Clan! Beter den us all! Innoruuk hev cleared me mind of de weekness u wud make me hev. Onny de strongess live, de week hev no place in Norrath. Me strong arm, and me big axe and de fear of me lurd Innoruuk, dese give us de glory! Join us now, befur de die!"

Llyara pulls a towel from her pack to wipe her face clean.

Llyara: "Never will we join with thee! We shall hunt thee and thy allies down. I have seen thee in thy secret meetings, plotting with the corrupt guards, Dark Alliance and even the vile Iksar. Who are thy leaders? To what purpose dost thou work? When art thou meeting again?"

Gramp stares fixedly at the ground and mumbles something unintelligible.

Basherbeau: "Speak up man, we cannot hear thee."

Gramp: "Cannut! Wud be me life if I tawk to yus, Lurd Iksar….."

Gramp trails off again into unintelligible muttering.

Basherbeau: "Speak up, we are fast losing patience! What are your plans? When are you meeting?"

Basherbeau threatens Gramp, pressing the point of his sword against his throat.

Gramp glares at Basherbeau, then looks over at Ngamok.

Basherbeau turns to Ngamok: "okay…you answer then!"

Ngamok: "Dunno! Dunno! I onny kno we wuz sent to a bigg place wit all dese lizzie men and tole to tawk to Lurd Iksar. He give us dis ting to take to Lucan in Fweeport den to some inkies in Newiak."

Ngamok fumbles in his pack, then holds out a slightly squashed, rolled scroll to Llyara.

Llyara takes the scroll, and unrolls it, holding it out for Tyre to read.

Tyre reads: "Greetings dark allies! This is an auspicious moment! I have finally gathered together enough of those who have fallen from the way of the Bear for them to band together in a guild. We must help them to gain strength so they can challenge the Clan and their allies and so we can move forward with our mission. I cannot say more here, lest this missive fall into the wrong hands. I would like to meet with thee all to discuss this. I will meet with thee all after dark ten days from now. My emissaries will look for thee in West Commons, to bring thee to meet me in the dark safety of Kithicor Forest. May Innoruuk and Cazic guide thee, Lord Iksar."

Tyre looks sternly at the Dark Fallen before him, then gestures with his hand.

Tyre: "Begone! Leave my sight. I cannot kill you. There may yet be some way to bring you back into the Clan. If the great Grey Bear saw fit to mark thee to join us, we may yet find a way to bring you back to the fold. Go forth and tell your brethren of the futility of opposing the combined might of the Grey Bear Clan and the Knights of Marr, and to reconsider their actions. We will welcome those back to our ranks who wish to rejoin us."

Gramp, Ngamok, Agak, Domitia and Brokchop slink off, muttering they cannot believe we let them go.

Llyara: "Bears, Knights…thou hast heard what the forces of evil have said this night. We must prepare, and we must uncover the details of this plot they are hatching! On Wednesday, April 19th, at 10pm eastern/7pm pacific, we will meet again. This time we will gather near the Kithicor entrance in West Commons. We will search these folk out and find out what they are up to! We shall need all the help we can get to thwart their wicked designs!" __________________________________

The combined Grey Bear and Knight of Marr groups headed back to the Feerrott zone to regroup, before heading out once more to hunt. At the zone, the cowardly Dark Fallen were lying in wait, and attacked as we arrived. Another brief skirmish ensued, before they scurried away through the pass into the Feerrott, shouting imprecations, and warning that we had not seen the last of them!

Many groups headed back out into Rathe Mountains after this excitement, appetites for battle having been whetted by these two brief skirmishes. Many a hill giant, giant skeleton, cyclops, lizardman, and both Sphinxes went down that night.

I hope thou wilt all join us this Wednesday, April 19th, 10pm eastern/7pm pacific for the next joint foray, as we seek to find out more about this next gathering of darkness.

Until then, be safe my friends. May Marr watch over and protect thee.

With Highest Regards, Llyara d’Avarlith Pureblade Knight Oracle, Knights of Marr
