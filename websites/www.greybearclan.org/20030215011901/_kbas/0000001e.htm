Proposed officer restructuring

Category: Category 0
From: Kilamor
Remote Name: 208.53.94.198

Comments

When we originally formed, all officers were divided by the various circles, which are level based. Initially, we had lots of members in the lower circles, now we have lots in the higher ones. In an effort to better streamline the duties and responsibilities of officers, and to get more involvement in the day to day running of the Clan, a proposal is being discussed to create some committees and groups to take on certain tasks. Here is the preliminary list:

1. Membership: Will handle all membership issues, including all invitations to the guild, recruiting, explanation of admission rules, and proposing any rule changes to the Elders. This committee will likely take over the responsibilty of voting on allowing re-admissions on a case-by-case basis.

2. Roleplaying: Responsible for all Clan storylines, information concerning hunts, and lore, and administration of the guild items through the loan program. This group will work closely with the other committees to insure consistency of purpose.

3. Raid/Events: Pretty self-explanatory, will organize hunts and events. The who, what, where, when and why of all such matters will be in their discretion.

4. CHG reps: Our representatives to the CHG.

5. Circle/Division leaders: Yes, we'll still have these. These will be the front line folks to resolve issues concerning people within their circle and division. They will also work with the other committees to plan rp events, raids, etc.

6. Honor Guard: Will make up the members of the honor court. This is the final line of dispute and problem resolution. Should the circle and division leaders be unable to resolve something, it will be submitted here, for a ruling. Also, the honor guard will propose any rules or policies necessary for the guild to the elders.

7. Elders: An elder will sit on every committee, and maybe multiple ones depending on the final number. Elders are responsible for all policy and rules, including any changes to the Charter.

More may be added, this is a work in progress. Hopefully we can get these up and running soon.
