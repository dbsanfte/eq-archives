It has become necessary for me to address the Play Nice Policy (PNP) announced by Verant, and what that means for us, especially during crowded times. Oddly enough, we have almost always had a form of PNP as our official stance. First, we try to work things out with a group contesting a spawn, and we typically offer a sharing arrangement. We do not take over camps where people are already hunting, and we will leave an area and look for better things to do in the event a matter is becoming too heated.

Our policy worked, and served us well for a long time. However, as the server grew, many times GBC have felt that our own honor system was being abused by others who would come into our well established camps, make a scene and rely on us to be the "bigger" person and leave. Surely this was frustrating. I made it clear that our policy was for honestly debatable camps, not situations where we clearly had a camp.

Verant then instituted PNP. This did not really mean much to us as we had never done anything but play nice. However, some members have raised the concern that the PNP rules could be used "offensively" to remove other groups from camps where GBC may want to hunt. For example, a clearly established group is hunting in Hate, and a group of GBC enters, and sets up shop next to them, saying no group owns a zone, etc.

These problems have occurred on MM as various threads on different forums can attest. I do not want us to be involved in these types of disputes. We should not use PNP as a sword. PNP was designed mainly as a defensive policy to stop folks from Kill Stealing, intentional training to clear out an area, etc. I don't believe it was designed to have groups move into OOT and say that they are there to share to Ancient Clops spawn.

Which brings me to the more difficult point, namely item farmers. I understand that when you go to see if Goonda is available, and everything near there (Froon etc) gives you exp, and 2 level 50's are camping items, and continue to do so for 10 hours straight, it is frustrating. I think these are case by case type things, and we should ask the guild what should be done. A hard and fast rule just won't work here. However, should there be any significant objection by even a few GBC members about use of the PNP rules, we should all be respectful of our fellow members and take the high road. This is now very long, so I will discuss any remaining points in follow-up if needed.
