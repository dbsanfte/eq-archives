As long as I can remember we have had a guild policy that we do not attack or kill good-aligned creatures or NPC's. Neutral creatures, or animals are fair game. So, Treants would be intelligent, good creatures. They conned "amiable" to me the first time I ever saw one. I think I may have even increased faction from there, but not sure how.

We understand that this can be a bit tricky, since there are some things you would expect to be good that are not, and vice-versa. When in doubt, con it and ask the guild to see what others con. Also, we do not kill NPC's, of any sort that are part of quests. Disrupting somebody's chance to initiate or finish a quest (except for those NPC's who are necessarily killed as part of a quest, i.e Xicotl) is just uncool. Due to the game world, these are not always written in stone, as the quests vary and are changed sometimes.

In the event you have damaged your faction with good groups, we would ask that you repair it. We have spent a long time gaining a reputation as good and honorable folks, and to have a GBC attacked by a Rivervale guard, or a good Qeynos guard, is embarrassing to all of us.
