The Lodge is situated on the outskirts of Halas, in the northlands. It is the home of the Grey Bear Clan, mortal enemies of the Dark Fallen and member of the Coalition of Honorable Guilds, and sworn to uphold honor and protect the innocent of Norrath. Each member has been marked by the sprit of the Great Grey Bear, avatar of the tribunal, as a sign of their faith, and as a pledge of their duty to serve Norrath.

The Citadel is perched on the cliff overlooking the pit in Paineel. It is the home of the Dark Fallen, mortal enemies of the Grey Bear Clan.
