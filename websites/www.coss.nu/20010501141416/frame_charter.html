History:

In days long since past, when the threat of attacks on the cities of the gentler races was great, and the humanoid races ran freely throughout the world, there was a group of individuals who were drawn together by the elders of their races. The elders charged these few brave adventurers with the protection of the weak, and defending the cities from these beasts. As a way of identifying these nameless heroes, the elders presented each with a star of the purest silver from the dwarven mines. In time as the tales of valor grew, and their exploits became legend, they became known as the Company of the Silver Star.

It is said that the final battle of the Company was in the Innothule swamp, far to the south of Antonica, where, as rumor goes, a thousand Orcs went in to challenge the last remaining members of the Company. While no sign of the thirty Company members was ever seen again, no one reported seeing any orcs in that region for quite some time.

Present Day:

Now it appears that the humanoid tribes (Orc, Gnoll, Goblin) have once again come forth to reclaim the land they once lost. We shall make sure the Company of the Silver Star is once again there to protect the weak, and defend the innocent. The humanoids shall once again fall to our might!

Purpose:

It shall be the purpose of the Company to confront the Humanoid menace, and once again send them into hiding, or to their deaths.

Structure:

Any race or profession is welcome in Company, as long as they posses the desire to help the weak, and accompany us in our quest to rid Norrath of this menace.

A council of the Guild leader and three other members shall be formed to provide the leadership of the guild. All major decisions of the guild will be carried out by a vote of the Council. Each member gets 1 vote, except for the Guild Leader who shall have 2 votes. In the event of a tie vote, the guild Leader shall make the final decision.

Recruitment:

The Company shall not openly recruit members. If the purpose of the group is successful, then word of our good deeds shall spread quickly, and those willing to join will seek us out. After a prospect applies for membership, he is given a quest to prove his devotion to our causes. If he passes the quest, the input of the members will be taken into consideration, and the Council will vote to admit him or deny him. After being admitted, he shall become a member in good standing.
