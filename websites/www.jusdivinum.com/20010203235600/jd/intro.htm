Jus Divinum's Mission
Jus Divinum has banded together to fight the evils in Norrath, right the injustices and work towards making this a better place for us all. We strive to stand strong in the face of terror, bring honor to our name, come together in friendship and to help those who are in need.

So it is written, so it shall be done.


Menu Options
- top -
The menu options are listed at the top of the website from left to right with a helmet located in front of each option.

Public

Guild - The guild menu option list all the issues that are related to the structure of Jus Divinum. Topics that you can find there are Membership (how to become a knight), Knights Code (guild rules), Judgement (disciplinary action), and Duty (member responsibilities).

Rosters - The rosters menu option list all the members of Jus Divinum and their positions they hold. There are many predefined roasters with more abilities to list the members by different categories.

Resources - The resources menu option leads you to an area that has a lot of different resource related to EQ for everyone to use. Some of these topics are links to other websites, guild calender, maps, Elendorn's Corner and much more.

Forums - The forums menu option is the area where players get together to share a variety of topics on the message boards or in live chat.

Vault - This menu area is for items that are specific to the guild.


Image Options
- top -
Image options are menu items that have no name. There is one image option in the menu area at the top of this site.

Lion - The lion icon in the upper left-hand corner will return you to this introduction page.
