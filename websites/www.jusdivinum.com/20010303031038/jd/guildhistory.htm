History of the Knights of Jus Divinum

    His first recollections were of running through the Hall of Truth corridors, playing soldier in the training yard, asking questions of the paladins in their shining armor.  It wasn't until he was six years old that he found out that he was an orphan.  He hadn't ventured outside the courtyard of the hall and had thought that this was how children were raised.  Soon after his sixth birthday a female vendor who worked in the hall brought in a child, her son, and Palliate asked who the child was and the woman told him about mothers and fathers and families.  Palliate was crushed, his world torn from him by the realization that he had been abandoned as an infant.  His outlook on life were forever changed.

    Years passed and Palliate, now more serious and self conscious, began to train as a paladin of Mithaniel Marr.  On his 12th birthday he was taken to the temple for the initiation into the Order of Truth.  The High Priest began the rites of initiation and augury to determine Palliate's worthiness and purity of heart.  During the augury the High Priest fell into an unusually deep trance and began to convulse.  The other priests became concerned and took the High Priest to his private chambers and the ceremony was stopped.  Palliate was again crushed, fearing that he was not worthy of the Paladin's guild he had lived in his entire life.  Fearing he would again be turned out and abandoned, he began to work harder than ever, reading and meditating on the teachings of Mithaniel Marr, and training his warrior skills at every free moment.

    Four more years passed and Palliate again was brought before the High Priest of Mithaniel Marr.  This time there was no augury into the soul of Palliate and the ceremony was performed privately in closed quarters.  Palliate was greatly confused by this and after the ceremony as Palliate walked down a corridor with the Guild Master of the Paladin's guild he turned and asked "Your lordship, why was the ceremony held in private and why was there no divination as to the worthiness of my heart?"

    "Palliate, the first augury showed you were worthy of membership with us, but there was more, and the priests wanted time to interpret the signs.", came the Guild Master's response.

    And so the Guild Master took Palliate aside and told him of the divination.  The priest had seen a vision of the future and the destiny of Palliate.  He would rise up from obscurity to lead a guild of great and honorable men and women.  The guild would flourish but Palliate would stumble under the weight of his responsibilities.  With his rank would come risk, and evil would attempt to subvert the guild and twist it to their ways.  It was said that Palliate would marry one of evil decent and bear a man child who would risk all that had been striven for.

    At hearing this Palliate was taken aback, shocked and fearful.  He fled the Hall of Truth and took up arms against the evil of Norrath, vowing to wipe the world free of the threat that the priest's vision had spoken of.  Over the next few years he fought valiantly and vengefully, beginning to quell his bloodlust.  As he fought he befriended other paladins and clerics of the various good temples and talk of a guild began.  Palliate was fearful of the vision, but knew in his heart that what was destined could not be turned aside.  So with the aid of Dakadin, Benger, Ashton, Steelweaver, Esmeralda, Collyne, Drell, Balanor, and Zerik the Guild Jus Divinum was born and the first Knights of Jus Divinum were granted title.

    As time passed the guild grew and became noted as one of the most honorable in the land.  Adventurers form all corners of the world came to be named as Knights and bear the crest of Jus Divinum.  Soon each city in Norrath had a Duke/Duchess from the guild to represent its members in that city.  The membership flourished and the people of Norrath had great respect for the guild.  All was well and Palliate began to think himself able to bypass the prophecy of his downfall into evil.  He ventured to Unrest and crushed the undead menace there.  Other Knights of the guild traveled deep into the dungeons of Norrath vanquishing any foe before them.  Even the planes of the evil gods and the lairs of the dragons were not safe form the Jus Divinum.

    Then came the grave day, the day of the prophecy.  Palliate was feeling very confident and took his group into Mistmoore feeling it was time to directly assault one of the bastions of the Dark Elves.  The hunt was going well, and the group was assaulting right up to the castle walls when suddenly a vast horde from within the castle issued from its gates.  The group of Jus Divinum was overrun and Palliate was taken, alive and charmed, into the castle, where a dark ritual was performed that began the evil possession of his soul.  A band of adventurers from elsewhere broke through the castle gates and in a last ditch effort to save themselves the priests of Innoruk commanded their charmed paladin to attack the assailers, but the hold of the possession was not yet complete and Palliate managed to escape into Lesser Faydark and rejoin his companions.

    As time passed however, the possession took hold and Palliate became mad with evil desires and bloodlust.  He fled his guild and joined the dark forces of Divine Chaos, taking refuge in the Dark Elf city of Neriak.  Not much is known of what happened to Palliate while he was in this state, but it was later confirmed that he sired a son with an Innoruk priestess named Raveena.  During this dark time a young warrior girl named Chrysla was granted leadership of the guild, which was now in great turmoil.  Chrysla had violent visions and insight into the future and aided in the finding of Palliate and his eventual capture by Jus Divinum members.  In a long and draining ceremony, Dakadin, High Priest of Jus Divinum, forced the possessing magic from Palliate's heart and Palliate returned to his place as leader of the guild.

    Rumor quickly spread of the dark child Palliate had sired and the thought of the destiny he had been told of actually coming to pass caused Palliate deep despair.  The destiny must not come to fruition.  It would be the end of him.  IN desperation Palliate sent one of his most trusted diplomats, Maravana,  on a dangerous mission to contact the dark elves and determine the truth of the rumors.  No clear answer could be attained however by speaking with the treacherous Dark Elves, so another means was formulated.  The shamans of the guild had heard rumor of a shaman in Lava Storm Mountains who could divine the truth of this matter.  A search was started and the shaman found.  Items were obtained to perform the ritual and the divination was completed.  A son was to be born, fathered by Palliate, born to Raveena, priestess of Innoruk.  Palliate fled the mountain temple in outrage and depression.

    The next week found Palliate and the guild officers in grave debate, at the end of which it was decided that Palliate should step down from the role of leader until it could be determined what threat this child held for the Jus Divinum.  Dakadin Lifekeeper was elected by the officers to hold the Arch Duke and Palliate assumed the new position of Chancellor to Dakadin.  Thus begins a new chapter in the annals of Jus Divinum.
