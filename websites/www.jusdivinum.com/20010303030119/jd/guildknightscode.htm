Code of the Knights of Jus Divinum

Aid Those in Need

The Knights of Jus Divinum must be known throughout the land as courageous and helpful.

At all times you should strive to aid those in need around you.  If you have spare food and someone is hungry, offer them something to eat.  If you see someone being slaughtered by foul beasts of the land, step in and lend a hand.  Spreading good deeds throughout the world is the number one goal of the Knights of Jus Divinum.

Aid to Fellow Guild Members

Special aid should be given to guild members in order to bolster the strength of the guild overall.

Knowledge of special places, quests, dangers and enemies should be spread to any members who lack such knowledge.  A web page listing learned guild knowledge will be posted among these pages.  Guild members who have craft skills or who have attained spare items are encouraged to offer them to guild members at discounted prices.  A web page will be posted here to publish member services and prices available to other guild members.

Rest for the Undead

Norrath is plagued by hordes of defiled undead.It is the goal of the Knights of Jus Divinum to return all undead to a state of peace.  Also, we will strive to destroy the faction of The Dead and all other defilers of the deceased.

Overthrow of the Freeport Militia

The Freeport Militia are a scourge to law and order. It is a direct edict from the Hall of Truth that we make every effort to undermine and overthrow the Freeport Militia.

Fealty to the Guild Leaders

It is of the utmost importance to the survival of our guild that the members of the guild uphold and support the rulings of the Guild Officers, and that the Guild Officers in turn support the beliefs and rulings of the Guild Leader.
