Roles and Responsibilities of officers and member ranks

ArchDuke - Update the Guild Web, Mediate Duke and Officer disputes, Monitor the activities of Dukes and Officers , Monitor the initiates

Elder Knights - Fill in as City Constable (Acting Duke) when the city duke position is vacant

Duke - Place initiate information into the roster listings, Mentor guild members and suspend membership of those  who do not follow the Jus Divinum code, Update "Local News" section of the web, Hold Town Meetings, Remain up to date by reading the web
