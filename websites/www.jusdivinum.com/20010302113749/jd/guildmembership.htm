Becoming a Knight

Welcome!

Welcome interested members! Please browse through our pages and familiarize yourself with Jus Divinum's people and their conversations on the public bulletin board.

Our basic philosophy is contained in the Knights Code. If when reading these words you find yourself agreeing, then possibly Jus Divinum is the guild for you. Our membership policy is centered around getting to know some of the people in Jus Divinum. The more people you meet on your travels, and the more often you demonstrate the values of Jus Divinum, the easier it is to become a member.

For those wishing to join, you will need to find someone to sponsor you. This should be someone who you have gotten to know. Someone would will stand beside you and declare that you would be an addition to Jus Divinum. This is precisely what they will be doing.

After finding a sponsor, that Knight will be required to perform a couple of actions on your behalf. Your sponsor will be your main contact and will be the person to talk with about the status of your application. The first act that the sponsor will have to perform is to inform the guild of your desire to join Jus Divinum. They will do this by posting a message in the Hall of Truth that states why you wish to become a Knight and why they feel that you should be granted Knighthood.

This will give the current Knights a chance to discuss your application. Assuming there are no major objections, then your sponsor will petition the officers to grant you a hearing. You and your sponsor will then appear before a quorum of the officers to discuss your knighthood. After this discussion, the officers will vote on your application and your sponsor will be informed of our decision. If you are granted Knighthood, then you will given the title of Knight Squire at the next guild meeting. If you are denied Knighthood, then your sponsor may appeal to the ArchDuke.

As a Knight Squire, you will be allowed into the guild with the requirement that are anon at all times. This probationary period will last at least one month. After the probationary period, your sponsor may petition the officers to have you granted full Knighthood. If this petition is granted, then at the next meeting you may remove the anon and be promoted from Knight Squire to Knight.

Each Knight may only sponsor one person at a time.
