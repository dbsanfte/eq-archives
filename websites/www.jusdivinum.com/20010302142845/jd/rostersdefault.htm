Rosters 

Roster Topics
Members - The list of all Jus Divinum Kights sorted by their status. Members can have multiple characters in the guild so we identify them one of three ways. The first is the voting member or primary character that holds a rank within JD. The second is the dual primary character that is played just as much as their voting character, but has no ranking in JD. The third is secondary character that is a character which is played now and then and holds no ranking in JD. All ranking in JD is done through the voting character.

Officers - The list of all officers in Jus Divinum.
