Grouping with an Enchanter

Enchanters are one of the most powerfull classes in EQ. They however depend a lot on their group. So I thought I'd give a few comments here on what other group members can do to help an enchanter in a group. If you are an enchanter, refer this article to your tanks. If you are not, you will group with an enchanter, and you need to read this.

  • Don't hit a mesmerized mob: That is a good start. A few reasons for this: if the enchanter mesmerizes a mob, it's probably because there is a main target for the group, and this extra mob is just causing damage. So it's not a good idea to wake the mob up. Second reason is the memory blur effect of the mesmerize line of spells: mes does mem blur from time to time, but not all the time. And both cases are bad in a sense: if mes does the mem blur, then if you hit the mob, it will be aggro on you and you only (VERY bad for casters, try to taunt a mob after you DD it for 500 HP); if the mes does not mem blur, then the mob is aggro on the enchanter (VERY bad too, I died a few times because of this).

    Then, when the main target is dead, you can break the mes of the next target. But it shoudln't be done by one tank only, if mem blur did not work, the mob will be aggro on the enc, very bad. All the tanks should taunt the mob at least once, then start hitting it, much more efficient. Ranger snare is also very good at that point to get the mob aggro on the ranger. Wizzie DD is dangerous to apply right away, even if it sounds simple because the mob is not moving: if mem blur worked, very hard to taunt the mob away (would you start a fight with a wizzie DD? no, you wait for the tanks to aggro the mob first).

    Only exception to this rule: when lots of tanks are involved, with multiple pets, and only a couple of mobs, it is difficult to assist one main tank, or one pet. The enchanter will try the mes, will be broken. No worries. The mobs will drop quickly anyway. The enchanter can get away with a few stuns, and the fight is over.

  • What breaks mesmerize and what doesn't: anything that causes damage to a mob will break mes: DD, attacks, or stuns with damage. Anything that doesn't won't: de-buff (shaman), motion debuffs (snare, root), or non-DD stuns (lower lvl pali stun, and enchanter AE stuns). DOTs are the worse: a DOT-ed mob cannot be mesed as long as the DOT lasts. Also, be very carefull with weapons that proc an AE spell (like fleshripper), or bard songs with AE DD components. (see AE tactics below).

  • How to help the enchanter mesmerize mobs: the puller is getting a few mobs. The enchanter starts a mes, gets resisted, a mob is aggro on him, he cannot cast any more. What can I do? The main goal is to allow the enchanter to cast mes again, maybe with a first Tashan to lower the mob MR. A tank can do that, but it's difficult to stop attacking right when the mes works, and usually a tank will attack once or twice after the mes, and break it (see previous paragraph, VERY bad). What to do then? Depends on the class, here are a few things I've seen work. Cleric stun is VERY good, it gives a few seconds to do both Tashan (lower MR) and mes. Same for wizzie/mage stun. Rogue backstab seems to work pretty well: one shot, the mob is aggro on the rogue. Ranger snare is a good taunt too, and will not break the mes even if cast after mes. If nothing better can be done, it is better to let the enchanter be pounded on a few times, he will eventually get the mob mesed. Be sure to heal him as he may have to do another mes quickly. Heal is a good taunt too. Personnaly, I like when the mobs are aggro on the cleric: he can usually take a good amount of damage (good AC), giving time for the enchanter to mes. If more than two mobs are on the enchanter, he will usually not be able to cast any more. Get one or two off him, that should work.

  • AE tactics: when both enchanter and wizzie are in the group, they can drop a lot of mobs pretty quickly. The enchanter mainly uses AE stun, the wizzard mainly uses AE DDs, the tanks try to taunt mobs off the wizzie as much as they can (each attacks a different mob), and the healer keeps the wizzie alive. It's mana efficient, very quick, and just works. Ask Cinius. All AE proc weapons and songs are then welcome as a bonus (and especially the sleep, chain and slow songs).

  • Ask the enchanter for buffs: the enchanter has a few very good buffs, like the speed line (quickness, alacrity, celerity, swift like the wind) that don't last very long, and like the wisdon buff that are of no use for the enchanter. It's very difficult to keep track of which buffs are on who, so it is VERY helpfull to remind the enchanter, like dropping a quick 'cele gone', or 'wis fading'. Maybe you won't get the buff renewed right away, but the enchanter knows where to spend mana next (please Esme, ask for celerity :). As a side note, buffs that are on the enchanters (like clarity) will get noticed VERY quickly by the enchanter, so reminding the enc to cast clarity is most of the time useless, he is already casting it on you.

  • Choosing the right target in a fight: without an enchanter, it usually makes sense to drop the healers first. With an enchanter, it doesn't matter, a mes-ed caster doesn't cast. It then makes sense to drop the mobs that are the most difficult to mes for the enchanter. Usually, that means the higher level ones. If pulling a red mob tank, and a blue healer, attacking the blue healer is suicide, the enchanter won't succeed in mes-ing the red tank, will be very tough. other way is a lot easier.

  • Charming mobs: charm is very powerfull (more than a mes, the charmed mob fights for you), but can be very dangerous. When the mobs breaks charm, or resists it, it will be very aggro to the enchanter. The stun strategies apply, until the enchanter charms again or can mes the mob. Also, it is a lot more difficult to charm a yellow/red mob. Blue are usually easy.

  • Don't panic: pulling a train of 5+ mobs can be very scary. If it happens with an enchanter, it is probably OK. My personal record is to break a eight kobolds train in king group in sol b, with only lvl 45- members, was fun. Enchanter mana is very important at that point. Don't pause between fights, the longer the fight, the more mana the enchanter needs to mes. For wizzies, it is important to drop the first couple of mobs pretty quickly, again to save enchanter mana.

    When the enchanter runs out of mana, he will usually tell the group pretty quickly, at that point it is crucial to let him mes as many mobs as possible before running. That will buy enough time to get out.

  • AE mesmerization: Enchanters have a very powerfull spell called Mesmerization, that will mes all the mobs in a given area. So the theory is that when a train is pulled, an enchanter can put to sleep a lot of mobs. This spell is however very dangerous, since the enchanter can mes himself if he is too close to the target, and all the mobs that resist it will be aggro on the enchanter. So most enchanters use it only in a few situations (like DT in Lower Guk). When they use it, the same rules apply as in the previous case: drop the mobs as quickly as possible. To ensure all the mobs are mes-ed, the enchanter will have to cast the spell over an over, which can be mana consuming.

  • Tanks taunt all the time: an enchanter is pissing mobs off just by breathing. So when he casts, the mob is really pissed. The only way around is for the enchanter to space the spells, and for the tanks to taunt all the time, even if the mob is already on them.

  • Enchanter DD have a mega-taunt built-in: Verant thought enchanters are not meant to DD, so every DD will most of the time result in the mob being aggro on the enchanter. Wizzies can do a lot more damage without the mob being aggro. Enchanters usually don't use DD, unless they have too much mana, which is a good sign. Only exception is the AE DD spell (gravity flux), which has the same taunt component as the wizzie one.

  • Try to spread the mobs in the fight area: When pulling a small number of mobs (two or three), they tend to all come at the same time and stack on each other. It is very difficult for an enchanter to target the ones he is going to mes. So backup up a bit from time to time will help the separation, and it will be much easier for the enchanter to target the mob to mes. Also, turning around the mobs may be enough, depending on the size of the mob, as soon as a first mob is mesmerized.

  • Nuking the pets: mem bluring a pet will result in its suicide when his master is dead (the 32k death). It however doesn't work all the time. So I end up never using it. Bards and clerics are great for that, enchanters not that good.

    That's all for now. I hope with all those advices, you can keep your enchanter and your group alive!
