The Code Of Virtue

Virtue defines who we are on the inside. It defines how we behave when nobody is looking. As we take steps to increase our ability to deal with the mobs in Norrath, we also want to take steps to increase our ability to deal with each other and the rest of the player community. We want Crimson Twilight to be thought of as a positive force on Drinal, and a friendly place for our members to spend time.

This code outlines the conduct each individual is expected to demonstrate as a member of CT. In no way is it expected to establish guidelines for every situation, rather it is to be a reflection of the golden rule, treat others with the same respect that you wish to be treated. While most of you will interpret it the same way that the officers have, we recognize that there will be some of you who choose to test the boundaries. The bottom line is that if your interpretation and an officer’s don’t agree, then you are expected to observe the officer’s interpretation.

Any infraction, even when provoked, will receive a reprimand from the guild leadership. This code applies not only to misconduct with our guildmates, but also to those with any character from level 1 to 60 in the game of EQ.

Punishment for misconduct is as follows:

At the time of the first infraction, the member will receive an official warning for misconduct. All officers are empowered to issue warnings. If an officer other than the guild leader issues a warning, the offender has the right to appeal this issue to the guild leader.

If your behavior continues after the warning, or you are involved in a second infraction within three months, your status as a member of CT will be removed. At the end of 7 days from your removal, you may re-apply to the guild, and the officers will convene to review your case.

Any infraction may be determined by the guild leader to merit immediate guild removal if the infraction demonstrates blatent disregard for Crimson Twilights policies.

The Code of Virtue
1. You are expected to remember at all times that there is a tag beside your name that identifies you with Crimson Twilight. Anything & everything that you do reflects in some way back on the rest of us and has an affect on how much pride we are able to wear this tag with and how effectively we are able to interact with the rest of the population on Drinal.

2. Obscene guildchat is not permitted. Think of a morning radio show. There aren’t many topics they can’t discuss, and there isn’t much that they can’t suggest; however, vulgar language isn’t a part of that. If your objective is to shock or gross out your guildmates, please keep it to yourself.

3. Harassment,of any kind, will not be tolerated. If you think it might be harassment but aren’t sure, then it is. If you’re asked to stop, then stop.

4. Official requests made by a guild officer are not open to argument. You may politely discuss any issue with any officer, but in the end, you are expected to comply with the officers decision. Should the officer request that you take this issue to /tell, then you are to do so immediately.

5. Any conflicts, with anybody, are to be dealt with in /tell and are also expected to comply with Virtue #2. If you are asked to take a conversation to /tell and you continue to use a public channel, including guildsay, then you can expect a /tell from an officer.

6. Attitude is contagious. Please keep ALL negative comments out of guildsay. There is nothing that can not be discussed in a friendly positive manner.

7. If a serious discussion is taking place in guildsay, which does not concern you, feel free to keep your opinions to yourself. If you feel that you have something positive to add to the conversation, then send a tell to the appropriate person involved.

8. Members are expected to help each other whenever possible and not just whenever convenient.

9. When asking for guild assistance with gates, quests, CR, rezzes, etc., if it becomes apparent that the guild is busy with their own issues and unable to help, do not continue to spam guildchat guilting people into dropping everything to come help you.

10. Power Leveling is not a practice allowed in the guild, nor should it be asked for in guild chat.

11. Whenever possible, assist others outside the guild.
