ToV Loot/Quest guidelines...

Loot

1. Armor drops will be lottoed to all those present who can use.
2. You cannot pass up on lotto for a drop if it is a piece you would wear. Doing so removes you from all lotto’s that raid.
3. “Final Piece” rule is in effect when someone’s final piece drops to complete their set of SkyShrine armor. You cannot call “final piece” if you only got to that status by looting another drop previously during that raid and others have not that could use. You can still call final piece rule if you are wearing 1 or more pieces superior to SS armor and this is the last piece of SS armor you would wear.

Velious Quest Gems

1. The guild gem bank is being done away with. All gems acquired during a multi-group raid will be lottoed to all those present that have the associated armor piece already first, and then to any that can use the gem if none present have the associated armor piece.
2. CT only single groups should abide by the same rule above when getting gems in their camps.
3. People are free to give or trade gems within the guild, but selling them is out of the question unless said gems were previously purchased yourself.

Quests

1. See the list of quest rewards at fthesaf...2144.topic and figure out which 2 of the items you would most like to get.
2. Choose one of the items as your PRIMARY choice and the other will be your SECONDARY choice. You must go to the Epic forum and post these or you cannot be in lotto’s for the quest drops.
3. When a quest piece drops, the quest(s) that it is used in is identified then matched to the associated quest rewards. All those who have the associated quest reward as their PRIMARY choice on the Epic forum may be in lotto for the drop.
4. “Final piece” rule is in effect for these quests as well.
5. The only time you can go in on an item that is for your Secondary choice is when no one in the guild needs the drop for their Primary choice REGARDLESS if they are present or not.
6. If you decide to change your PRIMARY choice you may do so at anytime if you have no quest drops for it yet. If you do have 1 or more quest drops then you must multi-quest them before you can start going in on lotto’s for the new PRIMARY choice. Any pieces you may have that can be used towards the new PRIMARY choice may be kept and do not have to be MQ'd.


If you have questions please ask, and I will do my best to answer you, or clear up anything. See the epic forum for more info @ pub83.ezboard.com/bctepics
