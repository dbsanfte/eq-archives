The Joining Process

  • In order to join Crimson Twilight you must be approached by one of the officers
  • If you are approached by them then you have either

    A.  Made a good impression on the guild

    or

    B. We need someone of your class and you have shown to be a quality person and a quality player that we would like to be a part of our guild.

 

Q&A

  • Is there a level requirement for Crimson Twilight?

    Yes.   The soft cap is a minimum of level 55.  However in certain cases we will go down as low as 50, however no lower.

  • Is there a member cap in Crimson Twilight?

    Kinda, we don't want to over recruit is all 

  • What if I want to join Crimson Twilight but I don't know anyone?

    Get to know some people and impress yourself upon them.  We are near our membership cap now, however we generally try to include quality people that will strengthen the guild

  • What classes is Crimson Twilight looking for?

    That varies with time.   Usually the officers will make known what we need and ask for suggestions.   Please only suggest people you know will make a positive impact on the guild and will be willing to participate in various activities even if personal gain isn't always likely.
