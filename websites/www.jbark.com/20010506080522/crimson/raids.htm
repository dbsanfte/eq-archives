GUILD GUIDELINES FOR
LOOT ON RAIDS
"Raids" meaning multi-group expeditions...*not* Single-Party camps.   Must be comprised of atleast 75% Crimson Twilight Membership

  • CT Lottos on certain items (high level items gotten during a CT Raid) will be lotto'd in the following fashion:
    • Once an item is gotten, the main characters who CAN AND WILL use the item will decide if they are in or not.
    • If they decide they are in then they will choose whether they want to use their points or not.  They can only use all or none of their points.
    • Then all those in the lotto will roll 25-200
    • If they decided to use points then the amount of points they have will be added to their roll
    • He or she with the highest resulting number will win the item and lose all points (if they decided to use points)
    • If you decided to spend points but lose, you will retain all your points for next time.
  • CT Member will have a slight advantage on CT raids because they will be able to apply their points to lottos
