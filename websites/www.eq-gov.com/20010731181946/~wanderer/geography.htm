Norrath is filled with interesting places to go and the most valuable experience gained by travelling is finding the interesting things to do and see. This section does not aim to describe Norrath as a whole, there are many sites available on the web for that information. Instead this section is to keep track of information about certain areas which make them special or otherwise stand out against the sea of normal camping spots.
