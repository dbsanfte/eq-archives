Welcome fellow druids. I am Cuddle the Journeyman Officer of Druids. Seek me out for entry into the Fourteen Journey. You will not regret your choice. We are a guild committed to helping each other. Read our charter and you will see right away that we honor and respect each other highly and that anyone who dishonors the guild or it's members will be dealt with firmly. This page will slowly evolve as the guild evolves. For now it is just a greeting welcoming any and all druids who wish to join the Fourteen Journeymen. I look forward to your requests to join us. We are still a some what young guild and have many low lvl characters. We do not judge any on what lvl they are or how much plat they have. Membership cost nothing but by entering you our bound to our charter. You agree to help your follow members when ever you can and they will help you when you are in need. As druids we will often be called upon for ports. This is one of our special abilities and is often rewarded. Remember as a guild we are much like a family. And all your good deeds will not go unnoticed.
