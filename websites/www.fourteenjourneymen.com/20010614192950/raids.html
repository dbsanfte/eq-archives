::::::Raid Scheduled On Unrest:::::

RAID ON UNREST

June 9th, 2001.
---------------------------
Greetings Everyone,
On Saturday, June 9th the Fourteen Journeymen will be raiding the Estate of Unrest. A time has not been slated for this event as of yet, but will be announced prior to the raid so that everyone can make arrangements should you wish to attend. This raid will be for levels 10 and up. It is preferred that you be at least level 13, however, level 10 should be sufficient depending on how many we have coming to the raid. You will be required to RSVP for this raid, so that we can plan in advance who is going to be in what group, and where you will be fighting. I will stop accepting RSVPs for this event 24 hours prior to the actual event taking place. This allows me time for any last minute changes, and also allows me to finalize most, if not all, of what will be happening on the day of the raid. So please, if you plan to attend, let me know asap! This event will be for the Fourteen Journeymen only. No outsiders will be permitted to join in on this raid, however, in the future we will have raids where anyone is welcome. For each of you who plan on attending this raid, please send me the following information Information Needed: *Character Name *Character Race/Class/Level. Please include the level you will be at for the raid. *Binding Location. Where is your character bound, and would you be willing to change it if necessary? *Time when can you make it to the raid, and how long can you stay? Please include time zone or convert to EST. *Do you have a magical weapon and/or spells as it will be necessary to have one or both for this raid. My next post will include the rules for this raid. If you plan to attend, please read them carefully as they will apply to all who attend this event. Failure to comply with the rules for this event may result in you being asked to leave the raid. Thank you for participation and I hope to see you all on the 9th! -Nooga
