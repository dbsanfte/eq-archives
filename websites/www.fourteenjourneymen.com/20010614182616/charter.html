Here are the laws that govern our guild. Only those who follow the ways of The Fourteen Journeymen are allowed to fight under the <Fourteen Journeymen> tag.


1. You are to treat your guildmates with respect at all times. If the officers hear any reports of members acting untruthfully or disruptively towards other guildmates, we will remove them from the guild.

2. Do not abuse guild chat. Guild chat lets us communicate with our comrades in a central forum that everyone can see. If members are observed being vulgar or unruly in guild chat, they will be warned the first time, and then they will be removed from the guild on any future offense.

3. Do not harass your guildmates. While we usually have no qualms helping each other, do not expect or demand special treatment from guildmates. Anyone seen or reported bothering other guildmates with requests for ports, money, power leveling, etc. will be warned on first offense and removed if found doing it again.

4. Try and settle your difference with guildmates through tells so that you do not disrupt the guild. If it is an extremely vulgar/scathing conversation, use the /log tool so that the officers can review the conduct and take the appropriate action.
If it is a player outside the guild, keep the conversation in tells. Do NOT shout or use /ooc to argue with the player. Doing this can reflect poorly on you as well as the guild. If the player is out of line, use /report and /petition to report the player to Verant and query guildmates for suggestions/assistance. Any members found starting or participating in fights with other players may be removed at officer discretion.

5. Members are not required to pay dues on a monthly basis. Dues are used to help further the guild by providing a resource for loans and prizes for guild events. Members that do not pay their dues will be unable to use these services in the future. Dues can be in the form of money or items that other guildmates can use.
