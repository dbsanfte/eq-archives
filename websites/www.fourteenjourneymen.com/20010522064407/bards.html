Welcome fellow bards. My name is Dalhari. I am the Bard officer for Fourteen Journeymen. I am here to make your gaming experience that much better so any ideas or questions you have please fell free to contact me at BardDalhari@sacred-love.com or just drop me a tell online. Being that we are bards one of our tasks in the guild will be assisting our fellow guild mates in corpse runs. That doesn't mean that every time someone dies you have to help then get their corpse. But if you see a guild mate on a corpse run help them out if you can. Help you fellow guild mates when ever you can. Trust me your kindness will not go unnoticed. Once the board is up we will have a section in there to discuss anything we want. Any planned raids that we do will be posted here. I am on mostly in the night but if you can't find me send me an e-mail.

Once we can will be going on guild runs through the various dungeons and collecting various bard items. These items will be given out to those bards that contribute the most to the guild. The will be figured out not by just how often you help the guild out or by how much money you donate to the treasury. It will mainly be figured out by how much you help out in the time you play and your need of that particular item. There is really no need for a lvl 8 bard to have a MM Drum. It would be put to much better use on a lvl 25 bard where the difference would be noticed. I am not against a little twinking as long as it's with in reason. There will also be rewards for those of you that go above and beyond a little help. Like if a low lvl bard helps to do a corpse location in a high lvl zone where he would be dead in two hits. Things like this are looked on highly. But he puts his guild mates life ahead of his own. Or a bard that always stays in a fight till the monster is dead or all his guild mates make it safely out of zone. As bards we are high on the hate list and monsters will agro on us. We must trust in out guild mates to save our lives and only run when we will die if we don't and not cause the rest of the party to die. The use of selo's allows us to outrun most mobs even when we only have 1 bubble of health left.

As bards we often underestimated in our true power and how we truly help the group. But once most people learn of all that we can do during a fight they always seem to find a spot in their group for us. What other class can heal, buff, and increase mana regeneration without down time. All other classes that do that need mana and often run out when it's neede the most. The only reason we need to sit is to heal and even that doesn't take long. We do not excel at any one thing. We are really good at everything. We are loved by tanks for our haste and healing abilities and by casters for our mana regeneration abilities. We are the jack of all traits and a much wanted class on most dungeon runs. Which we will have many.
