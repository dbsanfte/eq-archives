Vaniki, Lendiniara the Keeper, Gozzrem, Telkorenar, Wuoshi all went down in less than 6 hours
Posted by immort on Thursday, 17 January 2002 4:58 AM JST
 
Hehe today marked the first time we killed so many named mobs in a single raid day. Our raid plan was to do Zlandicar before proceeding to clear West ToV. However Vaniki was up and we decided to do it first. Not sure what happened the first time(I wasn't there :P) but Vaniki clearly had no chance against 35 of us the second round hehe. Grats Viola san for winning Willsapper and Bisi who got Vaniki's Heart.

We wanted to do Zlandicar next but LDT was already camping it so we had no choice but to proceed to West Tov hunting down the rest of our targetted victims(excluding our Victim hehe :P). Started with LtK and it went down with a convincing win on first try with 35 people. It was a good start but the loot suck(watashiwa evil hand TT). Anyway Grats Hideo for getting the ST Talisman, Baystars and Eminor for getting the Boots of Superiority.

Nxt Gozzrem the AE frost dragon, we were pretty confident as this fight was suppose to be a easier version of the previous one. Sure enough, Gozzrem gave us no trouble and it went down pretty quick. Grats Tadd with the food making Shovel of Harvest that she won't be hungry anymore and Andy with the Wand of the Black Dragon Eye that he can use for Zlandicar raid hehe.

Telkorenar was suppose to be the last dragon of the day or so it seemed. We were left with 28 people then and there I was getting a little worried. It would be our first serious attempt on West Wing named below the magical 30 mark. There was no time for hesitation and the fight started with uber Aterui pulling Telk out. The fight was wonderful like the previous 2... everyone was doing their role so well that it seemed like we had done this a thousand times... but this time it was Perfect! At the end of the fight Telkorenar lay dead on the ground failing to slay not even one of us. I wish I could record down the memorable and fun fight like a quake demo but alas. Anyway, grats Reiaku for getting the True Mithril Bp and Bisi with the Circlet of Suffering.

Raid officially ended here but quite a few of us were still in the "dragonslaying" mood. Someone mentioned Wuoshi and we started heading towards WL. By then DW had only around 15 people but luckily we had Ted Club friends online who were able to join us for the raid. The same usual buffing up and running over to tell Wuoshi that his days were limited. Sure enough, he dropped 3 emerald scales: Kimuri, Mahiru and DW guildbank, Skydarkener: Rafald, CBoL: Dyack, SotED: Henklin.

Overall it was a fun but tiring day. Now need sleep ZZzz ^ ^
