Patchday Fears
Posted by aterui on Friday, 22 March 2002 9:37 PM JST
 
Well due to an emergency patch, the planned raid had to be cancelled and DW decided to have some fun in PoF.

After entering the realm of Cazic Thule, who unfortunately decided not to grace us with his presence, we decided to dispatch his underlings thus Draco, Fright, Dread and Terror were sent to dream with the fishies!!

All in all it was a fun time in Fear, with many members taking new roles and responsibilities. Grats to all who got items that are not listed and to Jyphant for epic piece.

Loots included : Crimson Robe of Alendine [Told], Zombie Flesh Bracer [Hideo],Spine Dragon Claws [Qualan],Dawnfire, Morning star of light [Shashonee]Staff of the Earthcrafter[Lilianna & Kaitlynn], Carmine Robe [Oxox]
 
 
Sayonara Telk and LTK
Posted by aterui on Thursday, 21 March 2002 9:41 PM JST
 




Well VI might have made them more difficult, but with 32 people we decided that Telk and LtK can be successfully attacked. We first went for Telk, he had no chance and with one death we successfully dispatched him. Loots were, Circlet of Suffering [Sarenna], Black Rock Maul of Crushing [Fakekartang].

Next was to see if Ltk was actually that much harder now she has been upped.





Well all in all, the fight took a little longer .. but with very few deaths she was dispatched and DW lived to fight another day. Loots were : Gloves of Stability [Tadd],Silver Girdle of Stability [Rasis], Breastplate of Superiority [Syaka],Lendiniara's Talisman [Sharo],
