Magic Numbers!
Posted by aterui on Sunday, 17 February 2002 6:09 AM JST
 




Once again with less than 30 players and no shaman for HP Buffs and to slow. We marched into Dragon Necropolis to attack Zlandicar. So we ate him up in usual fashion to avenge our friend Jaled Dar. If you look closely above, we had a Chetari ratman join in the fight and cheered for Zlandicar. So we killed him too. While he dropped nothing,Zlandicar dropped some goodies.
The ninjaed items were Zlandicar Talisman [Huenry] Cracked Claw of Zlandicar [Qualan], First Brood Talisman [Sarenna], Zlandicar Heart [Rasis]





So we went to ToV to check out the newly buffed Gozzrem and Telkorenar. Word on the street has it that they have more HP and hit harder than ever before. But we had less people than any of our attempts. With 28 valiant people and our kamikaze attitude, we attempted Gozzrem since we wanted to gain major faction with the Kromzek of Kael Drakkal. Finally after a long fight, we know for a fact that Gozzrem will be sleeping with the fishes tonight. On another note, nobody on our end died. Gozzrem dropped us,Braclet of Protection [Osyou] Unopenable Box [Huenry]

I Had to leave the raid early but Divine Winds with a final crew of 26 - our lowest ever decided that it wont' be fair to Telkorenar if we didn't give him a try. So off we went and BOOM another dead dragon. Loots were,Emerald Bastard Sword of Purity [Kartang] Pauldrons of Blight [Hihix],Circlet of Suffering [Tutumkama].

On a final note, welcome Eason and Anson as full members of Divine Winds tonight.That's all folks. So much for the Verant upgrade!
