The End of an Era: Rest in Peace PSone
Story by: Nocte
Mon, 27 Mar 2006 @ 08:25:00
Discuss this story

While inevitable, Sony has officially ceased production on their revolutionary industry dominating 32bit console system. Some loved it. Some loathed it. Yet it was the undisputed champion of console gaming geeks for many years.

The PSone was this author's last console system as Everquest pulled me back into PC Gaming full time. More than likely my son will choose the next console for our household and that's probably 2 years off. I still dust off the old beast occassionaly for my not so secret affairs with Lara Croft or taking head shots at zombies in Raccoon City. Tonight sounds about a good a night as any...

Sony Playstation one December 1994 - March 2006

Rest in peace old friend. /sniff

Nvidia announces the GeForce 7600 and 7900 series
Story by: Ruccus
Thu, 9 Mar 2006 @ 10:05:00
Discuss this story

Today nVidia updated its GeForce 7xxx series of cards with three more offerings that are sure to interest most gamers' budget.  In the high end segment (nVidia has quoted a rather large range of '$499 to $649') there's the GeForce 7900GTX, dropping down the the $299 range for the GeForce 7900GT, and finally the new mid-range GeForce 7600GT weighing in at an expected $199 or so.

To add some fuel to the fire ATi also today introduced their $249 msrp Radeon X1800GTO, a 12 pipeline version of the Radeon X1800XL which fills the gaping hole in their SM 3.0 card lineup between the Radeon X1600XT and the X1800XL.
