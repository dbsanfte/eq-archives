Alliances

We will only ally ourselves with a very few guilds as we wish to keep quality rather then quantity. Also, we will only form alliances with Good guilds and Neutral if they are against the Shadow.

NAPs may be formed with those we have a closer relation to and consider trusworthy.

We will not publish our alliances as of yet, mostly for security reasons.

If any guilds wishes to form an alliance with us, just post your interest on the Public Forum, under the Alliance subject and the matter will from there be handled through mail. Those who have NAP propositions do the same but post underthe Requests subject.
