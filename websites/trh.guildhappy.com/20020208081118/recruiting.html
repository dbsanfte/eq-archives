Recruiting

Here are the requirements to join The Red Hand:

1. You must be of Good or Neutral alignment and wish to fight Evil.

2. You must follow The Red Hand, its ways and its rules.

3. You have to be active more then your inactive.

Before you send in your interest to join, remember to have these point in your mail:

1. Name (Ingame)
2. Race
3. Age
4. Sex
5. Occupation (Ingame)
6. Bio (Ingame)
7. Email
And answer this question:

Why should we acept you into the guild? What can you offer us in return?

Its optional to send in RL info such as RL name, location, age, ect.
