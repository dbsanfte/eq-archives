The Red Hand is mainly a Good aligned guild, fighting the forces of the shadow. We are a militaristic guild, but there is more to us then fighters and wars. The same way we fight the Darkness with steel and magic, we fight it with tools and gold, through trade and crafting. We recruit non-fighters as eagerly as fighters, as long as they can follow our ways and our rules.

We intent to be a force primarily against the Shadow and will pay little attention to what goes on among the other Good and Neutral, unless us paying attention helps to our cause and to the guild.

The Red Hand consists of two main divisions, both working on their own and together.

The Sun Division is the non-fighting, where everyone who wishes not to use direct violance can join and still be of great help in the everlasting battle against the Shadow, which we wish to end, to our favor.

The Deathwatch Guard is our armed forces, taking the battle at the more direct front. Those who wish to fight Evil directly, with either steel or magic, are stationed here. The main duty of the DG is to protect the Sun Division and the guild as a whole.
