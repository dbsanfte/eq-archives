Rules

1. Total loyalty towards TRH, you may not be a member of another guild if your already a member here. A member who commits treason against us will regret it. That person may never again join The Red Hand. Other members are free to pursue and kill him/her at any given given time. If a member wishes to leave the guild, consult with you superior and then with me.

2. Protect TRH and its members in any way possible.

3. Always show respect to superior ranked. And behave with respect even to those equal or under you.

4. Follow orders given by superiors, if you have complaints talk to me (General-Commander) or another higher ranking officer.

5. Never attack a member of another guild if its not personal, in that case, make sure the other player(s) knows so and know that guilds will not be involved.

6. Show no mercy to those of the Shadow..

7. Never let any outside conflicts come before the priorities of TRH. Blood and race wars shall not have any marks in TRH.

8. Never hurt innocent people.

9. All serious conflicts whether between TRH members or with outsiders are to be reported to higher ranking officer.

10. Always offer help wherever you can.
