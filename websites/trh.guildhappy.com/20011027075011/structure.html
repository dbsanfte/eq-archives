Structure

Those of no Division:

General Commander - Leader of The Red Hand


Military Adviser - Advises the GC in military matters, such as strategy and tactics.


Domestic Adviser - Takes care of many of everyday resposibilities. The main duty is to care for the guild as a whole.


Foreign Adviser - Leads the Diplomats and negotioations with other guilds, factions and societies.

The Deathwatch Guard:

Ranks:

Captain General - Commander of a Great Legion.

Lieutenant General - Commander of a Legion.

Banner General - Commander of a Banner.

Sword/Lance/Range Captain - Captain of a Troop.

Soldier - No commanding authority. (Though veterans usually have a higher status.)

Structure:

Army - The whole force.

1 Great Legion - 2 Legions inculding 2 Lieutenant Generals, 4 Banner Generals and 8 Captains.

1 Legio - 2 Banners uncluding 1 Lieutenant General, 2 Banner Generals and 4 Captains.

1 Banner - 2 Troops including 1 Banner General and 2 Captains.

1 Troop - 5 Soldiers including a Captain.

Sword = Infantry
Lance = Cavalry
Range = Archers

 

The Sun Division:

Ranks:

Head Administrator - Leader of a Society.

Commisioner - Leader of a Bloc.

District Commisioner - Leader of a Group

[SD] Cilivian - No leading authority, though skill in their trade is considered.

Structure:

Society - All players of a proffesion. Ex: All smiths belong to the same Society.

Bloc - All members in an Empire. Ex: All [SD]Civilians within a Empire.

Group - All [SD]Civilians in or near a city. Ex: Tazoon Group, all SD's in or near Tazoon.
