Mistmoore Raid (1 Replies) Posted by Kementari on 2001-01-31 05:53:42
Brothers and Sisters!

The Storm is unleashed and it is time to feel its power let loose across the face of Norrath. Show the lands a taste of the fury that is the Bloodhorde. The arrogance of the Mistmoore denizens has continued long enough. It is time for the Bloodhorde to claim the castle for our own.


Details:

Sunday February 4th.
We will meet promptly at two places at 3pm EST, 12pm PST
The Overthere Outpost or the Neriak Wizard Spires.
Our powerful wizards, Nerotic and Zandeth will generously be porting us to Greater Fay where we will group and march together to Castle Mistmoore.
Do not ask them for ports to join the raid after 3:30 EST, 12:30 PST.
Do not ask them for ports to join the raid from any other location.

The raid will be ENDING at 7pm EST, 4pm PST
Zandeth and Nerotic will begin porting us out then.
If you wish to stay later then you are on your own for transportation back. Please do not bug them during the raid or hours after the raid for ports.


You will need to have a map. Some good ones can be found at:
http://eqmaps.com/mapz/mistmoore3.jpg
http://www.eqatlas.com/mistmoore.html (click on the map to get the full size version)
You might also want to print out a Greater Fay map and Lesser Fay map if you are unfamiliar with these areas.

The main purpose of the raid is learning how several groups can work effectively together. A secondary purpose is to collect some quest items and treasure from this zone for our members that may be hard to gather on their own.

We will be following the standard Bloodhorde Loot Distribution rules. (found in the rules if you are unfamiliar with them)

If you do have a quest item you need from this zone though and there are several here send me an E-mail at Kementari@bellsouth.net and I will add it to my list.

Also please E-mail me if you have an idea for a raid or a place you need help to get a quest item from and I will keep a list. We may not be able to get to everything right away but I promise that you will be heard.

ooc - My poor webserver! (0 Replies) Posted by Runja on 2001-01-24 04:09:37
Just a little info. bloodhorde.com runs on an old P120 that sits on the other side of my 144k DSL line. Its not a fast server, but handles the small amount of visitors we get ok.

In the last few days we have been getting more traffic due to the larger number of posts people are making to the message boards. This has caused my poor server to choke. You may have noticed the website running a little slow, or not responding at all. This is due to the higher amount of traffic and how crappy my server is.

I need people not to hit the "reload/refresh" button on your browser if the website is slow. I know the temptation is great when the page isn't loading, but hitting "reload" will just put more strain on my poor server and possibly cause it to die.

So, please, please, please, don't use that "reload/refresh" button.

Thanks
- Runja
