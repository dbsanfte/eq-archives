Welcome to the Golden Society's official web-site. For everybody who doesn't already know, we are an EverQuest Guild (EQ). We are currently only registered on the Bertoxxulous Server and plan to stay only there.

    If you are on the Bertoxxulous Server and would like to find out more about us, please visit out Guild Charter. It explains our purpose in the grand World of Norrath and will help you decide whether or not to join.

    One last note, our guild is currently Monk-Bard-Cleric Only. If you are another class, you will have to look elsewhere for a guild, We are truly sorry.
