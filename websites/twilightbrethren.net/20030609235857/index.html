Changes to the site Posted Tuesday, April 22, 2003 by dangalak
The forum software is malfunctioning. New members are unable to register. Please post as a guest for now.

The forum software (UltraBoard) we have been using since the start of the site is no longer supported or being worked on. I believe the company has gone under. It is written in Perl, which I detest. I will be phasing out the old forums and we will be using phpBB2 for the new forums.

phpBB is open source and well supported with a large community. Unfortunately there is no converter available to move the old posts over or the user database. This gives us an opportunity to wipe the databases of ex-members and clean things up in general.

I will have to fix the member database to use the new forum's database. I will be wiping out the member database once this is done. After it's all working everyone will re-register with the new forums and then put their characters back into the database.

There will be some changes made to how the forum works. You will only be able to register as a guest. Then you will send an email or message to one of a group of officers to confirm that you are actually in the guild and they will put you into the appropriate group. Members of SOTO should have a delegated officer that can confirm their status as a member of their guild. This will enable us to have a secure forum to discuss upcoming raids privately.

More news will follow so keep an eye on the front page.
