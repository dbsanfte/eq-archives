Ack, I've sorely neglected this, haven't I? Well, I'll do better now that the kids are back in school.

I want to share why I joined the guild, The Claw of Nagafen. The Claw of Nagafen is not a raiding guild. They are a family guild who raids sometimes. One night a week they set aside to go to Temple of Veeshan. That's it.

If you see them all in a zone on any other night it's because someone said, "hey, Trakanon is up, who wants to try to take him down?" Last week it was oh, what's his name? The one who drops the cool enchanter robe in Katta? Anyway, someone saw him up and said, "hey Claw, here's something new, wanna take him down?" And everyone jumped at the chance to play the game side by side with friends doing something new and exciting. It was a blast. Another time we tried to take down Lcea Katta and she handed our butts to us on a platter, not once but twice. Oh and she charms. It was hilarious to see our gnome mage running from his epic pet, lol! We didn't succeed the first time but who cares. We had fun doing it. And the second time we had learned from our mistakes and handed HER her butt. :) The guild jumps at the chance to do stuff like that because it's fun. It's not boring long endless raid after raid. I couldn't even imagine doing that. ::shudders::.

They fight together most nights of the week for exp. Most willingly drop what they are doing to help someone get an epic mob because they want to, not because it's required. And no one is required to attend any raid if they don't want to. It's all about family and fun. What more could I ask for?
