... Tuesday, November 12, 2002 ...

We had a blast in San Francisco. OMG that city is SO cool! I had no idea. It never sleeps! I'm so used to sleepy Vancouver and Portland... where 9 PM everyone goes home and goes to bed. Not in San Francisco. There was NEVER a time the streets weren't buzzing with tons of people and cars.

And the cars.. oh my if you think I was afraid of the airplane, I have a whole NEW fear now. People drive like MANIACS in San Francisco. And they honk at everything!

As we were getting ready to leave our home, we noticed the ink exploding tag thing was still on the dress Jack got me last week for this trip. So we had to take a detour to get it removed. That would've been bad! Then when we got to the airport we found out our flight was delayed due to bad weather in SF. Then they changed it to delayed AND re-routed to San Jose. We goofed off in the airport a bit and got caught playing on the moving sidewalk. Hey, what can I say. Those things are kinda fun! hehe We landed in San Jose (I did great on the flight! I'm proud of myself!) and met a bus they had waiting for us. That was a fun ride. The bus driver was a hoot and we sat up in front talking to her the whole hour long trip to San Francisco. From San Francisco we called our hotel and found out they didn't have shuttles other than the city ones that transported people for a fee. We decided to take a taxi.

Ok, so WOW. We got in the cab and Jack said, "Marriot Downtown" and I swear before he finished the "town" part of "downtown" we were thrown back and were speeding towards the freeway. That was the most intense drive I've ever been on. He weaved in and out of traffic and did NOT like to use his brakes for any reason. He'd literally wait till the last possible second before SLAMMING on the brakes to avoid hitting anything. Didn't matter what. Jack and I were holding on for dear life and white as ghosts when the roller coaster stopped. The whole time the driver never uttered a word to us. We paid him the $34 then fled from the cab, lol!

We arrived too late to check in for the Fan Faire so we got ready to meet Lejax then head to the Hard Rock Cafe at Pier 39. We took a bus/streetcar thingie there. That was nice and mellow. The Hard Rock Cafe was cool! But the flamboyantly gay waiter thought I was pregnant. He squealed with embarrassment when I told him I wasn't. Hehe That was kinda funny. He was hilarious! We ate some incredible food, enjoyed the music then left. I have to say I was a teeny bit disappointed in it. Not too bad, just a little. I thought it would be bigger and have more people but it was fun just the same.

From there we went back to the hotel and changed into more casual clothes then met up with Ghitming, Glorfindal, Lejax from Claw then Demisoth, Rathool, and some others from Club Fu at the Sony Metreon. We were in a place called Portal One that was like a huge arcade but served alcoholic beverages. It was packed with EverQuest people and most were already pretty well lit up. And it was HOTTER than hell in there. After talking to Ghit and Glor we decided if we were gonna stay we needed to change into some lighter weight clothing. So we excused ourselves to change. We came right back but they left already. I think there was a miscommunication there. It was late, loud, and everyone was drinking, hehe!

More later! ~Kitani EverLost    /tell me...
