... Friday, June 21, 2002 ...

Last night a member of our guild was stuck in the Plane of Hate on an awful over three our corpse retrieval. We gathered two groups of Claw members and got him and his friend out. What a mess. We might've stayed longer and helped these people but there were too many not listening and doing stupid things (which explains why it was such a mess) so we helped our guildie then evac'd out of there.

I think I'm done! Yay! I've been working on this off and on for a while and never got around to finishing it till now.
