Core Rules

 1. Everyone in the Clan should be supportive of each other, If a given member is very close to leveling, Everyone who is available at the time, and in close proximity to the individual in need of assistance (within a 4 zone radius) should do what they can to assist. This increases the Clan's overall Experience and Strength.
2. If members disagree with one another, which will occur in any organization, Please keep it civil and between individuals, Meaning, Keep it out of Group or Guild says. If the dispute cannot be resolved, contact the Guild Leader or an Officer online, DO NOT bring other individuals into the dispute. If deemed necessary by the Guild Leader or an Officer, A Referee may be employed to resolve the dispute.
3. New members must read the charter and these rules before acceptance into the Guild. All New Members have a probationary period of up to one Month. The officer that invites a prospective member is that character's Sponsor. The "Sponsor" is responsible for that prospective member's indoctrination into the Clan, The "Sponsor is also responsible for making sure that all pertinent Character information, i.e.…. level, class, race….is reported via E-Mail to (maxine_cd@hotmail.com) or the Current Clan personnel Director.
4. "No Clan Member May Kill Steal another player(s) Kill, This is a Dishonorable act, will not be tolerated, and is grounds for IMMEDIATE removal from the Clan. If another player kill steals from a Member of Clan Donald, Report the offending player to the guild Leader or an Officer Immediately. If that player is a member of another Clan or Guild, Please report the Clan or Guild name as well as the offending individuals name."
5. Application for Acceptance into Clan Donald should be filled out prior to the Invitation of the proposed Member, It is the Sponsoring Officers responsibility to send the new member to the Clan Web Site (www.clan-donald.com) to sign up. If the proposed member is not known to an officer of clan donald, the individual needs to make an appointment for interview with Clan Donald, this can be done by contacting any officer on at the time to set up a date and time, if an officer is not online at the time, the proposed member's character's name will be taken and passed on to the appropriate individual so that a time for interview may be set up. Upon completion of the interview, the proposed member will be notified of the boards decision on membership within but not exceding 10 days real time.
6. "All clan Members should know who the Clan Officers are, if you do not know who the officers are, Ask, someone will be glad to steer you in the right direction."
7. All Members Should use the message board for information prior to logging into Everquest, There is A LOT of good information on the message board and could be important, this only takes a few minutes of time so please try to remember to hit the board prior to launching Everquest (NOT REQUIRED AT PRESENT)
8. When asking another Clan Member for Items (Especially Lore or Hard to get ones) it is BAD FORM to turn around and sell those items. This can lead to distrust in the Clan, Any Member caught doing so will be BANISHED from the Clan. Furthermore, Other Guilds will be notified of the offense. If you were given armor and now have better armor, please hand down any given items to lower members of the Clan.
9. The Exploitation of Bugs in the Game is against the Terms of Service (TOS) agreement with Verrant.com and will not be tolerated. Clan Donald is one of the MAJOR Guilds on Solusek Ro, and has many other guilds as her friends. Use of "Bugs" in the game is grounds for immediate banishment from the Clan, Notification to all other Guilds of the transgression and the incident WILL be reported to Verrant.com. THIS IS A DISHONORABLE ACT AND AGAINST CLAN LAW! RULES OF CONDUCT ON EVERQUEST Item # 11 : "You will not exploit any bug in EverQuest and you will not communicate the existence of any such bug (either directly or through public posting) to any other user of EverQuest. You will promptly report any such bug via the in-game /BUG command"
10. Clan Members will assist other Clan Members in helping to fulfill quests. Other Non-Member players may be given regular hints, otherwise how will they learn the game? This does not mean we are to give each other details on the quests, just that we will point you in the right direction. Upper level Clan members will not camp an item for you, if you are wanting a hard to get LORE/QUEST item you must be of sufficient level to help participate in the obtaining of said item.
11. Twinking:
11a. Clan Donald does not TWINK its Members. Twinking of Secondary characters by that characters primary character is allowed and practiced to some degree.

11b. It is bad form to loot items on a raid to twink an Alt unless there is no one on the raid who can use or wants the item in question.
(This is at the group’s discretion. At times the guild will organize a raid when it is seen several people within the guild are needing an item. This helps the overall effectiveness of the guild.)
12. Begging for Cash or Items in not allowed in Clan Donald and considered BAD FORM. Anyone caught doing so will be warned, If a second transgression is registered that member may be removed from the clan pending a Hearing of the High Council.
13. Powerleveling is not a standard practice in Clan Donald and WILL NOT be treated as such, On RARE occasions, Guild events of this nature may be held, however, No Clan member is to harass or pester any other Clan member to Powerlevel them, If the higher level character offers this option to another lower level member of the clan, then as long as both parties agree, the clan as a whole will not look down on this practice.
14. Ninja style looting: this type of looting, i.e...running in when the corpse opens up and looting is looked down upon in Clan Donald, Most individuals will not mind letting others loot a corpse or two, just be sure to ask if someone is going to be looting prior to just running in and doing it, this will alleviate confusion and unintended hard feelings between Clan Members.
15. Auctioning Items in guildsay is an acceptable practice providing the person auctioning the item is not SPAMMING the guild with the Auction request, Please limit auctioning in guildsay to once per Hour (Real time).
