"Clan Pride" Award
Awarded to members who show great leadership or community spirit outside of the clan, ranging from message board interactions to relations with other guilds and individuals in EQ. These members really define the spirit of Clan Donald and raise clan status by their behavior within the EQ world.

"Above and Beyond" Award
Awarded to members who will regularly and cheerfully drop all they are doing to come to the aid of other members, or work with other members to achieve needed goals and items for quests. Recipients of this award are helpful in the extreme; they continually roleplay their characters as rescuers and aids to other members in need.
