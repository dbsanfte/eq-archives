Battle Forged believes in peaceful play and interaction as a means toward good relations and a proud reputation.  We believe in handling ourselves with honor and fairness.  Kill Stealing, swearing, or any other unkind or immoral actions will not be tolerated and is subject for immediate removal.

All members invited into Battle Forged are on probation for one month to see how new members interact with other members.  All players wanting to become a member of Battle Forged must visit the web site and read the charter and rules.  They will file an application for membership to be gone over by the Recruitment officers.  We are looking to invest in the future with new members, so at this time no one will be turned away based solely on level.

Something that we hope will never happen, but may be necessary, is the removal of a guild member. If a member has a problem with another member, or complaints about another member, it is that member's responsibility to inform Severine, or an Officer.  In order to remove a member from Higher Powers, all officers and Council Members must agree to it.  We do not take these matters lightly and will attempt to view both sides of an issue before making a final decision.  This does not apply to members on probation.
