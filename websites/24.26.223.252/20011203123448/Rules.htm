General Rules (Preliminary Common Sense)

 These general rules are basically common sense.  They have a few good examples of what to do in certain events and here to give members an idea of what it means to be a member of Clan Donald.  These rules are to give players the enjoyment of playing, instead of having to spend their online time dealing with the off the wall B.S.

1.  Be courteous to your Clansman.  Remember, they are players just like you, and pay their $9.99 a month too.

2.  Follow the Policies and Procedures in the Game Agreement you "Agree" to when you sign in.  IF you haven't read it.  I suggest you do.

3.  If you are mad at the game.  Take a break.  Log off, get a drink.  Walk the Dog, and come back with a clear mind.  (if you do not have a Dog. Take the cat out for a Drag.)

4.  Know who your Officers are.  Each Officer knows their duties, and how to take care of issues that develop.

5.  Know and Read this set of Rules, and abide by them.  

6.  Training is not an acceptable practice.  /petition or /report it to a Guide online.  Inform the Clan that you were trained and by whom.  If you hear of a member being trained.  Inform them to do a /petition or /report and leave it at that.  Post known players that Train to the forum.  We do not want players that Train in Clan Donald.  BE SURE it was NOT an accident, and politely discuss the matter with the offending player first, before calling them a trainer.

7.  Kill Stealing is not an acceptable practice.  If you are KS'd, speak politely with the offending player.  IF they become insulting, do a /report and /petition to a Guide or GM.  Try to resolve the issue yourself.   IF matters become irrational.  Leave the area and hunt elsewhere.  "Discretion is ALWAYS the better part of Valor".

8.  Never demand assistance from a fellow Clansman.  It is inappropriate to think that people are expected to drop whatever  they are doing to help another Clansman.  (WITHIN REASON).   Assistance if needed, is rendered if available.  

9.  Rezzes and Ports.  It is not a good idea to bug a Wizard or Druid to port all over Norrath.  It is also not a good idea to pull a Cleric from a hunt to rez you when there is already a cleric in the zone that can rez you.  Denying any rez when given (i.e guild 51 Cleric comes to rez you, yet you want the guild's 56 Cleric to stop what he/she is doing to come rez you will get you immediately removed from the guild.). Also note Item #6 in Rules of Conduct in Guildsay

Rules on Raids (Preliminary)

Raids are anything from killing Vox, Naggy or Gorenaire (known as a Dragon Raid), Planes Raids, to a combined effort of Clan Donald to help complete an Epic Quest or just for hunting purposes.  This includes any raid that Clan Donald is invited on, to combined raids with multiple guilds.

1.  On any Raid, always pay close attention to Loot Policy.  If you do not know, ask who the Raid Looter is.

2.  If a disagreement between another guild, hunting in the same zone occurs, let the Guild Leader take care of it.  If the Guild Leader is not present on the Raid, the Raid Leader or a Ranking officer of Clan Donald take care of the situation.  Interrupting negotiations just makes matters worse, and can get you removed from the Raid.

3.  Everyone must participate in a minimum of 2 out of 3 scheduled raids.  Raids are important for developing cohesion and support for each other.  This is mainly directed at Epic Raids but goes along the lines for all Raids in general.  This insures that people do not just attend raids directed for their classes, and not show up for other raids.

4.  "Pick Up Raids" are Raids where you are looking for a raid to do, and get with another guild that is doing it.  Pick Up Raids can be dangerous to your character.  If joining a "Pick up raid", please make sure the Raid Leader knows that you are with them so you do not get left behind.

5.  Joint Raids are raids that are shared between 1 or more guilds.  These are the most common, as not many guilds other than the Uber ones, can field enough people at one time to attain a particular goal.  On joint Raids, the SENIOR OFFICER online or Guild Leader does the negotiating for the guild as a whole.  This includes Loot Rules and Policies for Epics agreed on by both Guild Leaders.  (reference item 2.).

6.  Ninja Looting is NEVER an allowable action on a raid.  Any member caught Ninja Looting, will be asked to leave the Guild.  If not leaving on their own, they will be removed by the Guild Leader.
*A message stating the name and incident, will be posted on the Solusek Ro Public forum*

Rules of Conduct in Guildsay (Preliminary)

Guildsay is a way for members to communicate with each other when online and in different zones.  It is used by many people at the same time.  The guidelines placed forth here, are to insure every member gets a chance to use it.  We will joke, play around, chit chat in guildsay.  Keeping Guildchat clean will keep everyone using it as a proper communications channel.

1.  Auctioning, Price Checking is NOT permitted under any circumstances within Guild Chat.

2.  Spamming Guildsay with information is also NOT permitted.

3.  Cursing and using flaming remarks will not be permitted in Guildsay.  (Unless there is some "ribbing" and joking going on).
Abbreviated "colorful metaphors", such as WTF?? and Shiete!! are permitted within reason.

4.  Guildchat is not for person to person communications, unless it is per a group oriented information, just joking around etc..  Please take other  subjects to tells.

5.  Whining is not permitted under any circumstance.

6.  Begging for rezzes or ports is not a good way to get the attention of the Clerics, Druids and Wizards.  Asking if there is anyone available is acceptable.  better to do a /w all "Clan Donald" to see who is on and sending that person a TELL.  If they are too far away, or not able to get to you do not start spamming the guildchat. (See #2).

7.  It IS acceptable to offer an Item in Guildsay to any member that needs it.  (i.e. Necro gets a Cleric Drop spell.  he/she may /guildsay "Any Cleric need the spell "Kill all Necros?")  Said Necro, may just decide to keep that one to his/her self.  (Joke)

8.  There is never a guarantee that a question will be responded to in Guildsay.  Other members of the guild maybe in battle, afk (without using the /afk), busy in other tells or groupsays.  So just because someone doesn't say "HI" or "BYE" or if no answer is recieve in Guildsay from a question, doesn't mean that people don't like you.  It just means they are busy doing other things.
