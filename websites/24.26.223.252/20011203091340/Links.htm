Higher Powers
The purpose of Higher Powers is for all members to have fun together. We are a family that holds guild events and rare item hunts together.Higher Powers is not the biggest guild on server, but we are a guild that works together to have fun and to kill mobs as a guild. What is not a challenge to some guilds can still be a challenge to us, and attempting and succeeding at overcoming these challenges brings a lot more fun to the game.
