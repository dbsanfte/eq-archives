The guild leader for Battle Forged is Severine Wolfe.  The assistant guild leader is Effan.  There are several officers who are available to help you.  Please check the website’s Member Listing to see who they are.

  Battle Forged is one of the newer guilds on the server, however, most of us have been playing together a long time.  We are looking to build the guild with players who play with as much honor and fairness as we do and who seek the companionship of friends to fight the mobs.

Please read the following carefully.  There are hard and fast rules for the guild.  Failure to follow these rules could result in removal from the guild.  Don’t come back and say “Oh I didn’t know I couldn’t do that.”  Failure to know the rules is not a defense.

1.  No spamming guildchat with anything.  This is annoying to everyone involved and a sure way to have yourself put on ignore.  If you have an issue with a guildmate take it to tells.

2.  Do not demand to be power leveled.  You may ask another guildmate to PL you, however, if they choose not to, end of story.  Do not beg for PLing in guildchat.  It is up to the individual player on whether they wish to do this. It is not a guild rule that you must be PLed.

3.  No harassment of other players of any type will be tolerated.  This goes for guildmates and outsiders.  If you are caught harassing another player and it is brought to the attention of an officer you will be removed upon sufficient proof being put forth.

4.  Always conduct yourself with honor and integrity, even when soloing.  If you get into a situation where you need help, ask in guildchat for assistance from an officer, or, barring that, petition a GM.

5.  Looting.  Don't be a loot hog, but don't let people walk all over you either.  Ask for loot rules the minute you join a group.  It is very bad form to loot an item for an alt when a person of that class is in your group and can use it.  Ask first, there is no excuse not to know what the loot rules are.  In guild raids the loot rules will be explained by Severine or Effan.

6.  Trades.  Trades between players are personal and I don't believe that we, as a guild, should get involved.  However if two guild members come to an impasse on a trade agreement an officer will be called in to mediate.  The officer's decision will be final.  If it is found that someone in the guild has trade scammed another player they will be summarily removed and their name blackened in the Auction zones.

7. Guild Bank.  A guild bank will be set up and in this bank we shall keep extra spells we pick up and wish to hold for guild members, stones for planes, coffins for summons,  gems for planes from resist gear and money to pay clerics for peridot and wizards for portal fragments and/or port stones.  Members 46 and above, who will be going on the raids in high level areas will be asked to put in at least 50pp monthly.  If you are unwilling to contribute to the raid fund, then you will be asked to buy a stack of peridot to donate to the clerics.

8. Guild Gifts.  It is the custom of most people to hand down equipment to other players that they no longer need.  We fully encourage this.  However, any equipment (armor or weapons) that is donated is NOT to be traded or sold.  If the player no longer needs the equipment then it should be handed down to another guildmate who may need it.  If it is not needed by anyone at the moment, it will be placed in the guild bank.  If a player is found to have sold or traded a gifted item they will be immediately removed from the guild.  Behavior such as this is not what we want in Battle Forged.

Please work on your epic as much as possible.  However, it is Battle Forged's policy to not organize large epic raids for guild members that are under level 53, unless it is for a class such as cleric (epic very helpful to guild) or monk (very easy epic).  Large raids (more than two groups) that take an enormous amount of time and require nearly all members to attend, will take time to plan, so give us advance warning and the Raid Leader will try to get the manpower.   Feel free to ask for one group to help you with a Mob for your epic.  Most members of Higher Powers are extremely friendly and will drop anything to help you. If you need a small raid for one of your items that does not require a huge amount of manpower we will schedule these for you as they come up.  Since Epic Quests were only meant for players above 51, we will stick with the Verant vision that help for epics will only come after level 51.  If you have a problem with that, speak to Severine or Effan for clarity.

The following are Battle Forged's policies for looting in the planes and on major raids.  If you do not agree with this policy then you are welcome to not attend.  Higher Powers recognizes three types of attendees at the raids:

1.       Battle Forged members.  These are current Battle Forged members in good standing.  They have the right to roll on all drops.

2.        Invited guests of Battle Forged.  These are friends of the guild who have been invited by the guild leader or the raid leader.  They have the right to roll on all drops except for the breastplate or robes which will go to Battle Forged members first.  If no one from Battle Forged needs the breast plate or robe, then the invited guests can roll on the item.

3.        Add-ons.  These are people who show up at the last minute and were not previously approved by the guild leader or the raid leader.  This will be decided on a case by case basis and only if no other class represented on the raid is there or does not need the item. 

4.  Loot for Alts.  I strongly discourage looting any items for an alt unless no one else can use it.  Logging in an alt to loot an item is forbidden and will NOT be allowed.  In other words, if that character did not participate in the kill, they will not be allowed to loot. 

When an item drops, all of that class rolls unless they are an alt of someone. In the case of someone's alt coming, they will get leftovers. Please do not bring three level 46+ characters, camp two of them there and expect to be able to roll on items for all three classes. 

Once you have won something for your class, you will be out of lottos until all of your class have won. If someone comes to the raid late, as in not the first or second day, it will be up to the raid leader's discretion if they count in this or not. EXCEPTION is if a BP or robe drops, which everyone that needs will lotto for even if they have already won a class lotto.

IF no one rolls against you for an item, it is considered a DEFAULT win, which means you are still in lottos until you actually win an item.

Crap Items: If there is a piece of gear that everyone thinks are crap and no one in that class wants, we will let them roll as a Default roll, in which case they would still be in regular lottos as in the previously mentioned Default wins.

Anyone who is down to needing their LAST piece of gear for that plane will get it automatically. This is NOT the last piece you WANT. It is the last piece PERIOD.

Only the group leader should check the corpse.  He or she should IMMEDIATELY shout out what is on the corpse so that lottos may begin if necessary.

Any gems or other droppable items will go to the loot leader, which will probably be Severine. Cash items such as cheap gems or jewelry that can be sold to vendors will be held by the guild and sold to help pay for raid costs, such as coffins, peridot, and portal stones. Any person that has paid for these items will then be compensated using that fund. If rare gems are dropped they will go to all Clerics and Wizards (if in a wizard port only plane) first to help cover the cost of symbol gems and port stones. The rest will then be zone lottoed.

Any spells dropped will be given to the highest person of that class in the raid that can use. Ties on level will lotto. Any extra spells that no one needs will be put into the guild bank.

Anyone not coming up from the beginning will be on a 3 hour loot restriction. This means that whoever did not help break in will have to wait 3 hours real time to roll on any class loots, unless it is something everyone in their class already has.

If you think these policies are unfair you are welcome to not attend. They are standard procedures in the planes, however. ALL outside invites go through  Severine or Effan. NO ONE will be allowed to come up that has not been approved by us.
