Greetings Traveller,

with a sad heart I am writing these lines!

As the Lorekeepers of Norrath on E'Ci have been officially disbanded I am closing down the website! This page will stay up until the 31st of January. Then I will delete the domain Lorekeepers.net from the SilverDay Network and release the name.

The Lorekeepers were the first Guild I was a member of during my years in EQ. During that time the Lorekeepers-Site was hosted here on SilverDay Network and has gone through many changes! I would like to thank everybody who has helped to maintain the site. Most of all I would like to thank Silverbeard who wrote the scripts for the last site.

I would like to close with the words of my favorite playwright William Shakespeare:

If we shadows have offended,
Think but this, and all is mended,
That you have but slumb'red here
While these visions did appear.
And this weak and idle theme,
No more yielding but a dream,
Gentles, do not reprehend.
If you pardon, we will mend.
And, as I am an honest Puck,
If we have unearned luck
Now to scape the serpent's tongue,
We will make amends ere long;
Else the Puck a liar call.
So, good night unto you all.
Give me your hands, if we be friends,
And Robin shall restore amends.

(Taken from A Midsummer Night's Dream by William Shakespeare)

Goodby, my friends. Hopefully we will meet again!
