My Q&A Session with Abashi

My questions in white.

Abashi's answers in the cool blue color.

 

*First thing I have to say is thank you Gordon, for joining us. It is always a pleasure to have a famous NASCAR driver participate in a Q & A session. Oh wait, wrong Gordon. Hmm...I am going to have to quickly change my line of questioning.

 
*laughs*
 
Dang it! You've foiled my plan.  I thought that I could get by talking about Daytona. 
 
 
*So, what is your official position with Verant? Responsibilities?
 
I'm the Internet Relations Manager for Verant.  I'm basically responsible for keeping an open line of communications between the public and the company concerning EverQuest, essentially insuring that information passes between the two.  I'm not responsible for writing code, making changes in the databases, making customer-service policy, or defining the direction of our games as a whole, though I do offer ample input into all of those areas, debate issues, and help drive the decision process along.
 
 
*Do you actually play EQ? If so, which class? And do you think your class is underpowered?
 
I do play EverQuest, though not as much as I used to.  Most of the time after a long day on the 'net dealing with various issues, the last thing that I want to do when I get home is sit in front of the computer.  My main character is a Necromancer of nearly 50th level.  As for your last question, I'll have to give a resounding "No" on that one. 
 
 
*Some people are under the impression that yourself, and Verant employees in general, are evil and perhaps demons from hell. Could you please clear up this mess. Are you indeed a demon from hell and, to your knowledge, does Verant actually employ any of hells minions? Or is that just a Microsoft thing?
 
*looks over his shoulder*
 
I'm sorry, but an NDA I signed shortly after being summoned...er...I mean hired... prevents me from answering.
 
Seriously though, we are sincerely aiming to please the largest percentage of our playerbase possible by keeping the game fun and interesting for them.  Though it may appear to some that our intentions are "evil", it wouldn't make much sense for us as a company to purposely alienate our customers.  The numbers, particularly the ones that show EQ's ability to retain subscribers, and the ones that show EQ's subscribers gladly recommending the game to others, clearly show that we must be doing *something* right. People may disagree on exactly what that *something* is, and most would agree that EQ isn't perfect, but in the end, the people are here, play the game nearly every day, and enjoy the heck out of it.
 
 
*Ok, enough with work stuff. I am sure the last thing you want to do is talk more about your job.
What are some of your hobbies? Things you like to do apart from getting blamed for all of EQ's problems.
 
My hobbies vary from time to time and usually run a bit on the expensive side.  I also tend to be a bit obsessive with my free-time, which doesn't make for a good mix, but it sure can be a lot of fun! 
 
Several years ago I played pool.  A *lot* of pool.  Leagues, bar tournaments, private games in the back room, name it I was there.  This went on for a long time, and one point I actually went out on the road to play.  That didn't last too long, I got the ultimatum from my wife, and gave it up.  For my next gig I decided to get a pilot's license.  Did that for a while, until my dog ate it (don't ask). 
 
After that it was online game.  Of course, now that I work in the industry, my wife tells me that I need a new hobby.  Any suggestions?
 
 
*My partner in EQ Rants, worthless, wanted me to ask you this. I think he is afraid he may be the only one. Do you find Bugs Bunny attractive when he dresses up like a female bunny?
 
Errrr, no comment.
 
*blushes*
 
 
*Complete this line. The perfect day in the life of Gordon Wrinn consists of _____________________ .
 
Hmmm, I think I'd have to go with "a full day enjoying good espresso, fine cigars, expensive cognac, my wife, and some thoroughly bloody prime rib".  Oh, and it has to be someplace without internet access.
 
 
*I know there is an EQ players gathering in Vegas in July (unfortunately I will be unable to attend). Do you consider yourself a gambling man? Plan on playing some cards while you are there? Or just checking out the ummm...local entertainment?
 
I've been known to gamble from time-to-time, per the "Pool" episode mentioned earlier. However, our schedules are all going to be pretty tight.  I'm not sure how much time we'll have to go out and patronize the casinos.  Perhaps I'll just go into a casino, hand the information clerk my money since they are going to get it anyway, then go sit at a bar and let everyone buy me drinks while grilling me for info.  *evil grin*
 
*Pick one: Queen Amidala or Buffy the Vampire Slayer?
 
That's a toughie, I'll have to go with Buffy.  Actually, can I expand on the choice?  Not Buffy as much as the actress' character in the movie Cruel Intentions. 
 
 
*Your phone a friend in Who Wants to be a Millionaire would be whom?
 
Well, it would have to be someone who knew something about sports, and movies, and perhaps sports movies.  Of course since I don't know anything about any of that, I wouldn't know who to name either. I wonder if Bryant Gumble plays EQ?

*(Actually I know a lot about sports, movies and sports movies. I'll volunteer - Buur)

 
*Ok, I don't want to keep you too much longer here.
Did you find this Q & A enjoyable and will you frequent EQ Rants and tell all your friends and family about it?
 
Hey! Sounds like a deal!  Keep your eyes on your hit counter for those six extra hits.
 
*Any parting shots? Anything you would like the public to know that hasn't been covered here?
 
Shots? No, but I'll certainly let someone buy me a couple.  See everyone at EQG3 in Vegas!. 
 
 
Thank you for taking time from you day Gordon. We'll see you on the track!!
