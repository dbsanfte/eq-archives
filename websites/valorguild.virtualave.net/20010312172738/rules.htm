Guild Loot and Distribution

Guild loot:

First and foremost, we are here to acquire equipment for the guild as a whole and not just a single member. The better we equip our guild then the better off we are for doing those increasingly harder raids that only a guild can do together. Greed of any member will cause hardship and resentment within the guild and therefore will not be tolerated by any.

Secondly, looting will not be conducted while a battle ensues unless told to by the raid leader or an officer. All looting will be done after battle or during the time in which an Evac is called.


All loot that is acquired from guild raids will be considered guild loot. These raids are as follows:

1) Was a scheduled guild raid.
2) Was a raid conducted by the huntmaster or one of their chosen aids.
3) All loot obtained during a specific equipment raid, i.e.… we conduct a raid in order to obtain Golden Efreeti Boots for our guild members, all loot obtained from the start to the end shall be considered guild loot.

Distribution: All guild loot that is not distributed during or directly after a raid will be handed over to the treasurer for future distribution to guild members or put up for sale. If the treasurer is not on hand then the loot will be given to any officer on site, if no officer then it will be given over to the person in charge of that particular raid or the Huntmaster.


Guild loot will be awarded to the individual that can most use it that is present during the raid. If that person already has a piece of equipment that was obtained from a previous guild raid then the item that was replaced will be given back into the guild for redistribution to other members in need.


When more then one person that is present during the raid is in need of the item obtained then the standard in game random role system will be used by those individuals.

When an item is obtained that no one wishes to have or can use then that item will be rolled on by whomever desires that is present during the raid. The individual that wins will not be in any other rolls on such equipment described within this paragraph in order to allow others some personnel treasure. Also, if an item is obtained later that can be used by this winner then that winner has an option to give up that loot and receive the item that they can use. That item given up will then be relottoed amongst the members there.


If there is a questionable item, an item that can be used by either fighter or caster, then it will be dealt with as regular loot and the rules put forth earlier in this section.


No Drop loot will be randomly rolled upon only by those classes that can use that item if there is no one that is in need of such item. To prevent the rotting of loot, if no one present is in need of such item or the item is not class specific, then those present who wish to can roll for that item.

Guilding of Future members

Before any member is to be guilded, they are to read and abide by the charter set forth for the guild. That person can be asked questions by any guild member at any given time pertaining to such charter.
If a member of the guild recommends an individual whom wishes to be a part of Soldiers of Valor, then that person will automatically be guilded.

If an individual wishes to join that was not recommended, then they will be guilded immediately but must remain anon for a period of three weekends. Upon the fourth weekend, that person will be deguilded so that the members can vote on that person. The voting will be conducted by those individuals present on that day. This person can and should be asked questions about the charter until such a time that he/she is guilded and is wearing the name Soldiers of Valor with pride instead of being anon.
