The Bard

A Bard is an entertainer, with all the powers of music to deploy.  You’re able to inspire yourself and your friends to a quicker pace, a more powerful and rapid attack, topped off with quicker recovery of expended energy and mana.  At the same time you can entrance your enemies with songs of wonder or terrify them with ballads of woe.
