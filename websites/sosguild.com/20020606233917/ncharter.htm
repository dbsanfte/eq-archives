CHARTER

Scourge of Shadow is a guild based upon what gaming is really about, having fun. We're not a roleplaying guild and we're not a guild that requires our members to play 24 hours a day. We are a hodgepodge collection of mature players (for the most part) that get together, joke around, and kill mobs.

Rules to live by:

We promote and encourage fair play. Training, harrassment, ninja looting, KSing, etc... are all frowned upon and will get you GRed.

Pay attention and use common sense... These are the most important attributes one can exhibit on a raid.

Heed the experience of others... We have people in this guild that have been here for a long time. They know how we do things. If you have a question feel free to ask a senior member.

Don't embarrass the guild... We expect people to show their guildtag, wear it with pride.
