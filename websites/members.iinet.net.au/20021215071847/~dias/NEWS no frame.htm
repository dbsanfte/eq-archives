20/07/01

hmm 3 months since update..I think that may be a record. Anyways....a few changes. Firstly, I have decided to stop supporting the frames option. Frankly, it's a bit of a pain in the arse. However, if people want it back let me know and I can reimplement it easy enough. Also today the members section has been updated. I have added 2 new parts to it: firstly a list of who is who and secondly a breakdown of the guilds basic level statistics.  The list of alternates I put up just so everyone knows who everyone is. I understand that some folks might not like this idea so if you want your alts taken off (or put on!) please just send me an email at dias@iinet.net.au and I will dutifully remove (or add) them.

The guild breakdown is very basic, and yes, I realise that the numbers don't quite add up. I didn't really see the point in going into decimal points. so anyways I put this up for everyones curiousity more than anything, though I guess it gives us a rough idea of where we need development.

So, if everyone could check out the changes and let me know corrections and whether they liked them I would appreciate it. I have started a thread on the messageboard for this purpose.

 

23/04/01

Well some news. First of all as anyone who has read the boards will know we now have a council of officers as elected by the guild. Congrats to everyone! In light of this I have placed up everyones names that sent me emails with their characters details, as well as the list of Officers.If I have missed anyone out my apologies, please just let me know. If you haven't sent me your details could you please send them to me so we can get our list up. Just so we know how strong our guild is, and where we may be lacking.

Secondly, as im sure everyone has heard Verant has issued a statement saying there will be another expansion to EQ, called Shadows of Luclin. Personally I think this sounds quite cool, and anyone who wants to can read the press statement and see some screenshots on Gamespot here.

Also, the draft version of the Charter as composed by Stormii has been posted. Enjoy ;)
