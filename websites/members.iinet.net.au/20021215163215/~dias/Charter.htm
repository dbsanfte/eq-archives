Charter

We at Edge of Dawn (EoD) wish to create a relaxed but unified group which consists of all levels. Our intent is to have enough members at all levels to allow us to group with each other for raids, quests, events, or to just play together on a regular basis.

All members are expected to abide by the rules the rules posted here as well as in Verant's Codes of Conduct and Fair Play.

Show that you are proud of your guild! Do not play under the /anon feature as this removes you from guild listings. You may however use /roleplay which will flag you as a guild member while eliminating your lvl and location.

Members of EoD should be organized and self-motivated (meaning-if you plan a raid, do the research and be prepared. If you are joining a raid, make prior arrangements to be there and if not on time, be early). It is your responsibility as a member to check the guilds' web-page and stay up-to-date on guild events.

Guild Events/Raids will be handled as organized as possible. Please sign up/register at the website or any other utility we choose to use for events so that the event coordinator can pre-plan as much as possible.

During guild driven raids the organiser or events officer will designate "master looters". No one else is to loot.

During guild hunts need before greed rules apply, meaning if a item drops and you cannot equip or use the item it goes to the person who can, unless the person(s) already have or turn down the item.

What a person does with there own time, gear, money, and/or any combination of the before mentioned is completely up to them. Everyone strives to their own individual goals. While EoD has no rules against "twinking" (meaning you can do what ever you want with what ever you have or aquire), be motivated enough to go after what you want on your own (If you want a SSoM-do the research-hmmm, the MOB that drops it is 10 level's higher than you?-organize a group to assist you, guild mates and/or non-guild mates--while your guild mates will assist when possible, don't depend on them to "get" you all kinds of stuff or PL you).

No begging of any kind in guild chat, ooc, shout, etc. Please, do not support people who spend time standing around begging rather than attempting to earn things on their own.

No badmouthing of anyone in public. You have a problem with someone 1) take it up with them in private tells 2) contact an officer of the guild or 3) contact a GM if the problem is something they can assist you with. This doesn't imply that you can't add someone to "@#%$-list" on Lany's's's if you feel the need, but please do this as a last resort.

Please be considerate of others. Different people will interpret certain language and characterizations as offensive. Please try to respect how others may feel and also try to accept that all of us are different (in other words try to overlook things that bother you). Remember people of all ages play this game, not all of them mature adults, so try to keep this in mind and act maturely even if others do not.

Prior to receiving an invite to our guild, potential members must visit the web page, read the charter and post a Message of Interest (subject line) on are web site. They must also group on a "event" with the Guild and/or group at least 6 verifiable times with member(s) of EoD and be certified by them as potential guild mate material (we are very choosy).

After your initial invite into the guild, you will be on a 30-day probation period. You can/will be removed from the guild without notice at any time during the first 30 days. Probationary recruits will be notified by the person who invited them to the guild at the end of the probation period.

Discipline will be handled by the officers. Violations of these rules can result in your removal from the guild without any notice!
