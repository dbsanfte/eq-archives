Charter

We the Chosen Counsel will assist all races and classes of Norrath regardless of level.  Defining help as this, Showing by example the ways to make the most of the lands of Norrath thru passing on information to teaching battle tactics, etc etc.  But we of the Chosen Counsel will not support the beggars of norrath, from time to time we may hold a event to bring the lower and newer faces of Norrath together and this should be the only time things are freely given away, after the are earned of course.

We the Chosen Counsel are mainly in this for fun.  Roleplaying is great but if you are a die hard Roleplayer do not expect others to be and do not force or try to impose your thoughts on others, it wont be tolerated.  This guild is about being able to do what you want with who you want there is very little to none EGO in this guild, if there is a problem with it check it at the door or the guild will check it forya.


To keep this short have fun, play fair, obey the rules not only by the guild but by Verant, be mature in the face of the immature, promote the guild by example and let the recruiting officers know if you want to sponsor a person

Rules

All members, are expected to abide by Rules of Conduct and Guild Charter and all potential members are expected to agree to them upon admittance to the guild.

All guild members are to abide by Verants Rules of Conduct.

Guild members are not allowed to keep their "anon" flags on, as it does not allow people to query for guild members. You should be proud of your guild, and want to have it appear in all queries. Role playing is acceptable if you are Role Playing as it still displays  your tag.

Do not use threats, bad language, or insults with any other players in game, regardless of circumstances. Though it is
understood that problems will arise within the game, we ask that
you show restraint and maturity in the face of immaturity and
disrespectfulness.  If a problem persists contact a guild officer.

Internal disagreements are kept in-house until their  resolution. If you have a problem with a guild member or part of the guild structure, we ask that you use our message board to
resolve them, or that you speak to an officer or to the offending
party in-game be mature handle it dont announce it to people who dont need to know.

Failure to comply with any guild rule may result in punishment up to and including :

Officers: 2 warnings, 30 day probation, demotion, guild vote on dismisal from guild
Non Officers: 2 warnings, 30 day probation, dismisal from the guild officer vote

All new recruits are on a automatic 30 day probation , hence it will be brought to a discussion amongst the officers of the guild they have earned there position and that is why they are officers.  If it is decided to part ways with a new recruit it will  be up to the officers to decide.  To find out your probationary status you will need to check with the Lead Recruiter.

If an Officer is absent, or away on extended leave, then their  vote does not count. All Officers are required to give notice to the guild before leaving for an amount of  time more than five days, this is to be sent by Email or posted on the MSG boards.
