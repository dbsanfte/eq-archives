Alladvantage pays money just to surf the web with their viewbar up.. it's not up all the time and can be closed if you don't want to use it or or not on the web. (can also remove it from startup folder so it doesn't start up when you reboot or turn system on)

I've been using this for awhile now.. it makes just enough to pay Everquest's monthly fee without referrals if you use it for the max payable time per month(14-15 hours in North America I think.. who doesn't surf this many hours a month if not WAY more? hehe). You don't even have to use the viewbar once you've already surfed the max payable hours per month for your country.

You can make even more money if you refer people and they sign up and use the viewbar while surfing the web. The internet paying for itself..not hard to get a few referrals to be able to cover ISP bills also I think.. unless you got some insanely huge ISP bill.

I've also looked at a bunch of other services similar to Alladvantage but this one is by far the easiest to use.
