Warrior Skills

All skills need to have at least one training point trained in it at the guildmaster to "learn the basics" otherwise it will not advance. Critical hit is not a skill but just something you get when you reach level 12.

Taunt
Skill gained at level 1. It is used to help keep creatures on the warrior leaving casters free to heal/dd etc by putting you at the top of the creatures hate list if successful. This skill should be kept at max for each level.

Kick
Skill Gained at level 1. An extra attack, can kick "magic weapon only" creatures if wearing magic footwear. Caps low before level 50.(I don't know new cap)

Bind Wound
Skill gained at level 5. Warriors only form of healing using bandages and can only heal up to 50% of total health. VERY useful skill until level 15-20 where bandaging to 50% starts getting expensive(5 silver per bandage approx). Caps at skill 100 I believe until post level 50.

Bash/Slam
Skill gained at level 6(slam is level 1 for large races but still need to train bash at level 6 to advance). This is an alternative attack to kick, meaning you can either use kick or bash but not both at the same time. Slam is the same skill as bash but doesn't require a shield like bash does, only the three large races can slam. There is a chance to stun an opponent with bash/slam and is great for interupting casting.

Dodge
Skill gained at level 6. Just what it sounds like.. a chance to avoid an opponents swing.

Parry
Skill gained at level 10. Another defensive skill but instead you deflect the swing rather than just avoid it.

Dual Wield
Skill gained at level 13. The ability to use two weapons at once(1 hand weapons of course)

Double Attack
Skill gained at level 15. Chance to attack twice in the same round rather than once. With dual wield you can get up to 5 hits in including kick/bash or two swings with a nice 2h weapon.

Riposte
Skill gained at level 25. When successful this skill lets you turn an opponents attack into an extra attack for you.

Disarm
Skill gained at level 35. Just what it sounds like. Currently useless skill against NPC's because they no longer use weapon delay for attacks.. might be useful if the weapon the creature is holding has a good effect(bloodboil, ykesha, etc)

General Skills

Offense - gain 5 points per level and raises ATK level in addition to weapon skills(see below)

Defense - gain 5 points per level and raises AC

Slashing/Piercing/Bashing - weapon skills which determines how well you use certain types of weapons

Archery - useful for pulling creatures from a distance but skill caps early compared to rangers I think.

Throwing - alternative to archery I guess..not much to throw really.
