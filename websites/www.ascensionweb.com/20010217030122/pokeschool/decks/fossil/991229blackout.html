Card by Card Strategy:
 
Pikachu / Raichu (Original):  Raichu plays an important role because he follows 
the strategy, does the most damage, and has the second highest HP.  Once I draw 
a Pikachu, if I don't already have a Raichu out, I will most likely use a 
Computer Search to get him.  I don't need Raichu in play right away, but I need 
him on the bench.  I would save Raichu for a deadly Pokemon like Charizard or 
Venusaur.  To find out why I didn't put Blastoise as deadly read Electabuzz's 
strategy.  When I am using Raichu I would mainly use Agility, which is leaving a 
lot up to chance, but it's a chance I am willing to take.  Once in a while I 
would use a Thunder, but not to often because Raichu can loose almost half his 
HP doing that attack.  That is what Raichu has to do when it's game time.
 
Pikachu / Raichu (Fossil): This Raichu is the warning before the storm.  When 
there is a "big-hitter" on the opponent's bench, I will get this Raichu going.  
It will deal some damage to the bench, so when the "big-hitter" comes out it 
will already be low on HP.  Fossil Raichu also must take some of the larger hits 
because it has the highest HP in my deck ( 90 ).  A short, but strategically 
important job to fill in this deck.
 
Electabuzz: Electabuzz, a must for any electrical deck.  I would use Electabuzz 
a lot against a Blastoise deck (Raindance), I would paralize Blastoise until it 
is knocked out, the attack would also do double damage because Blastoise's 
weakness is electric.  Why not Raichu, because the Raichu squad need to be saved 
for a real emergency, Blastoise would be to easy.  Electabuzz is the card that 
needs to come in during big runs by the opponent.  Then Electabuzz would simply 
paralize the opponent until the defending Pokemon is knocked out.  Electabuzz 
would play the main roll against a Lapras deck because a perfect Thunderpunch 
added to Lapras' electric weakness would knock out Lapras is one hit.  This is 
the role that Electabuzz needs to play. 
 
Magmar (Fossil): Magmar is the starter of my deck.  It would come in and stall 
while I get evryone else built-up.  It also follow the strategy of my deck when 
using Smokescreen.  I would use Magmar first to poison the defending Pokemon 
with Smog, then I would use Smokescreen and watch the defending Pokemon slowly 
and helplessly be knocked out.  Magmar's job is to stall and posion, not a hard 
job but a very important one. 
 
Scyther: Scyther is there to play the meat of the match.  I would use the 30 
damage on most occasions, but when I get a grass energy on the bench, I will 
power up Scyther and do 60 damage (After I attch the energy to Scyther of 
course).  Syther plays a lot more, though, against fighting decks.  My Raichu 
and Electabuzz are weak against the fighting decks, and Magmar might work, but 
that is a bit to much for Magmar to handle.  This where Scyther comes in, 
fighting is weak against grass, so I would use Syther to knock out the opponent 
in one or two hits.  Scyther will some in handy in any situation.
