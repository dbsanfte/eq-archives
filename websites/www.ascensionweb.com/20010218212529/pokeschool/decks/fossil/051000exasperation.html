"Exasperation" Psychic/Water Deck
[17 Pokemon]
3 Staryu
4 Seal
4 Dewgong
3 Jynx
3 Mr. Mime
[18 Trainers]
4 Bill

3 Gust Of Wind
3 Potions
3 Plus Power
3 Energy Removal
[25 Energy]
13 Water
12 Psychic
The deck itself is cheap.. simple pokemon and simple trainers, but in my opinion good enough to work well in the grand scheme of what is Exasperation. Jynx, Mr. Mime and Dewgong might well be the better pokemon in the deck, but staryu can dish out 20 damage (and be increased with first hand plus powers) with a single water energy, good for blitzing first turn if lucky enough. Seal, with a not sufficient enough attack of 10, has 60HP.. good enough to work up the time to get a dewgong (potions can work good to up the niftiness of seal). Jynx and Mr.Mime.. well that alone is another story, although Mr. Mime has the power of preventing any damage 30 or under, both him and jynx have meditate.. for 2 energies you get to grow 10x per turn on attack (unless potions are used, or it gets retreated), but that's the beauty of it all, with gust of wind you get to willingly shift back the pokemon.. either for a lesser pokemon or one with enough counters on it. Part of exasperation is energy removal, if you get these since the beggining, and the oponnent pulls out an active pokemon with a retreat cost.. you get to take away his energies.. making him powerless, no attacks and no retreat.. an easy prize if you have enough bundled up for a larger attack.
Overall.. i think it's pretty good, every card serves it's purpose and it has worked very good so far.. energies are never short, with any pokemon you get to do something (even if just make time), and trainers.. although simple, are good enough to exasperate yer opponent.
Laterz,
Jarl
