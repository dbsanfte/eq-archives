    • WHAT IS ASCENSION?
    Ascension is a Communtiy of Entertainment Websites geared towards the 14-24 year old age group. We give you the hosting, connections and audience you seek for FREE with no effort on your part. Now that you don't have to worry about your "Page Views" you can do what you do best - Build Websites.

    WHAT WILL YOU DO FOR ME?
  • Web Space - While we expect your web resources to be within reason, there is no limit on web space. If we find that we are running short on disk space, we simply install more drives.
  • FTP Space - Hosted sites may have a directory structure dedicated to them. Again, there is no practical limit to the allowed disk space.
  • Bandwidth - Ascension is on a 100% dedicated T3 connection. Response time is excellent. Ascension will allow a site to use as much bandwidth as they can generate. Should the bandwidth start to peak beyond the T3, more bandwidth will be allocated.
  • File Types - We support a large spectrum of file types for your site. Including CGI scripts, ASP, and Access databases. We do NOT support downloadable file types such as (.rm, .mpg, .zip, .mp3, .wav, .mid,) etc. Their are many great services out their that will provide you with free space for these particular files.
  • Getting Paid - We have special Profit sharing programs in place for all of our sites. (You must recieve in excess of 100,000 page views a month) This includes the splitting of profits from click-thru and impression based systems displayed via the Ascension banner system. Also, unlike other networks, you are allowed to use the rest of the page to generate more of a profit for yourself via buttons or more banners.
  • E-Mail - Your site can have multiple e-mail addresses on our server, so you can use yourname@AscensionWeb.com (Check out our list of Available E-Mail Domain Names or if you have your own domain, yourname@yourname.com). We can setup full e-mail accounts or it can forward to your current address.
  • Multiple NT Servers - This allows the best response time under high load situations while maintaining excellent security and reliability. Since NT is quickly becoming the standard on the Internet, you will find it compatible with web components that are freely available.
  • NT Accounts - There are several levels of accounts that are available to your webmasters. These include forwarding, e-mail only, FTP, and shell. The type of account is dependant upon the requirements for the user.
  • Services - Ascension provides a large number of services so that you may make your site the best it can be. These services currently include: SSI, CGI, Perl, and ASP. If there are other services that are required, request them and we will see about installing them.
  • Components - Ascension provides a number of ready made components to enhance your web page. This includes such things as a web boards, and a news posting front end. If there are components that you would like that you think may be of service to all sites, let us know and we may develop them for our growing library.
  • Domains - Sub-domains of http://yourname.Ascension.com (Check out our list of Available Domain Names) will be provided for all hosted sites who request them. Additionally we can host you under your own registered domain such as http://www.yourname.com.
  • Sub-Hosting - Hosted sites may host other sites. This is particularly useful for sites that cover a broad spectrum and have their own registered domain. They may have hosted sites at http://subhost.yourname.com.
  • Resources - Ascension provides several resources for the hosted sites. This includes such things as contact lists, a message board, and hot sites and rumors. If there isn't a resource we are providing that can be, it will be added.
  • Support - Ascension has three administrators for our servers. As such, around the clock support is available. You can contact the administrators via e-mail, fax, or telephone. Ascension is dedicated to providing the best support available.
  • Reliability - From past experience, Ascension has learned the importance of uptime. As such all servers are monitored and the admins will be paged if a server goes down. Remote reboot and control capabilities are available and full daily backups are made. Currently, Ascension has all of our Hard Drives on a Mirror set-up, If one hard drive goes down the mirror will instantly jump in and replace it. Death before downtime.
  • Dedication - The Ascension team is dedicated to provide the best home for all of it's web sites. We are also dedicated to expanding the Ascension name and image, including all of the hosted sites. We have full time members as well as part time members that are available at all times to help you make your site and Ascension the best it can be.
