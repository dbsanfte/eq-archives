Mewtwo's Wrath
Calvin Li 2/22/00

MEWTWO'S WRATH

Pokemon (11)

4 Movie Promo Mewtwo
2 Chansey
2 Mr. Mime
3 Basic Electabuzz

Trainers (26)

4 Computer Search
2 Item Finder
4 Super Potion
3 Super Energy Removal
3 Switch
3 Gust of Wind
4 Bill
3 Professor Oak

Energy (23)

2 Double Colorless
11 Psychic
10 Electric

This deck is designed to attack very quickly. First you should get out a Mewtwo on the bench or out. If you don't have one, send out Chansey or Electabuzz. Electabuzz uses high damage attacks, and Chansey stalls with scrunch. Finally whe that Mewtwo is ready send it out. It is immune to Energy Removals and Super Energy Removals. At this time, pump up another Mewtwo and Electabuzz. Mewtwo is the replacement for the guy up front. Electabuzz will kill anything Colorless or Psychic. Chansey will stall if needed or commit suicide with Double Edge. This high damaging attack does in all those with around 80 HP. That means that Fossil Magmar, Hitmonchan/Lee, Electabuzz, and Scyther are gone. Other Chansies will be easily killed by Electabuzzes, or gusted away. Mr. Mime can build up damage quickly (not Chansey though). And it can hide under it's Invisible Wall, destroying Raindance's edge on this deck. Stall decks will be decimated with the deck's speed. Haymaker's will have no chance. There are so many Computer Searches and Item Finders because this allows two energy to be discarded and then "absorbed" by Mewtwo. Same with Professor Oak.
