Why Scyther?
Vemco Van Stein 4/28/00

Hi, this is my deck, called: "Why Scyther?"

Why Scyther (by Remco van Stein Callenfels from the Netherlands)

Pokémon (20)
4x Alakazam
4x Abra
4x Scyther
4x Chansey
4x Mr. Mime

Energy (16)
4x Double Colourless Energy
12x Psychic Energy

Trainers (24)
4x Computer Search
4x Item Finder
4x Pokémon Breeder
4x Pokémon Center
4x Professor Oak
4x Super Energy Removal

This is just another Alakazam deck designed to deck your opponent by using Mr. Mime as your active Pokémon and switching any damage to Chansey (or Scyther) using Alakazam. That is, at the first glance. There are two things which might not be obvious. First there are the Professor Oaks. I have got something to say about that card in this deck. First of all, use it as less as possible. Most of the time you'll use it when you are sure your opponent has already drawn more cards than you have. Secondly, you might still use it when your opponent has drawn an equal amount or less cards than you have. In this case, there are two possibilities:
1: you see a quick kill. This means you're able to kill of your opponent quickly by knocking out all of your opponent's pokémon. Usually the suspect is Scyther, but it might be Chansey, too;
2: you need an Oak to survive. There are occasions when your opponent is powerful enough to slip through your array of defense (Alakazam, Chansey, Mr. Mime, Pokémon Center, Super Energy Removal.

Another thing which often puzzles people, is that I included Scyther. This is the most frequently asked question about this deck (hence the name of this deck). There are a few answers possible to this question:
1: Scyther has a reasonable amount of HP for a basic poké;
2: Scyther helps you out when your opponent has a poké out with resistance to psychic;
3: Scyther has no retreat cost;
4: Scyther has a resistance to fighting, so it's perfect to balance Chansey's weakness and against fighting it can hold you a while so you can wait to draw all the cards you need instead of having to use your Computer Searches.

Speaking of which, Computer Search is your key card. It lets you set up your combo. Do not be affraid to search, use Item Finder, and search again if you have one of the following ing your hand, but not the other two (because you NEED Alakazam in play)
1: Abra
2: Alakazam
3: Pokémon Breeder

The rest of the cards are self explanatory, but here are a few quick hints:
1: If you have Alakazam in play, attach one Psychic Energy to it if it hasn't got one already. Should your opponent play Gust of Wind, all you need is 1 (!) Double Colourless Energy to retreat it;
2: If you have Alakazam in play, and you have got another Alakazam in your hand together with an Abra and a Breeder, play another Alakazam, just in case your opponent plays Gust of Wind. This way, you'll really turn the lock on your opponent;
3: Use Super Energy Removal with care: most Pokémon that need more than one Energy card to attack can be stopped with Mr.Mime;
4: If you do not need a card in your hand immediately, do not play it (unless hint #2 is the case) This is necessary to keep your opponent from playing Imposter Professor Oak;
5: Never underestimate your opponent: overestimation always turns out better than underestimation;
6: Play Professor Oak very scarcely: if you do not need it you can always discard it to Computer Search or Item Finder;
7: Do not be affraid of a quick kill if you have a Scyther with 3 Energy out on the second turn (thanks to Double Colourless Energy.
