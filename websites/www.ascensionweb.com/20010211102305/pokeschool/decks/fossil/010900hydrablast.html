Hydra Blast (a hydra is a dragon(think Gyarados))

Pokemon
1 Blastoise
3 Wartortle
3 Squirtle
1 Gyarados
2 Magikarp
2 Lapras
2 Dewgong
2 Seel
2 Scyther
1 Articuno

Trainers
2 Computer Searches
1 Item Finder
1 Pokemon Breeder
4 Switches
4 Bill
2 Professor Oak
4 Super Potions

Energy
23 Water Energy

Yes it is me again who brought you the poor man's Damage Swap/Attackdeck. The whole point is to throw out a Scyther or Lapras. They'll take care of the enemy for a while. Especially because Scyther has resistance to Hitmonchan and his buddy Lee. Lapras's Confuse Ray is very annoying.
That is when you build up on Pokemon like Gyarados, or Blastoise, andDewgong.

What if they don't come up? Squirtle, Magikarp and Seel will not stand up to many things. Computer Search is supposed to take care of that. There is plenty of energy and a Wartortle should come up soon. If not Squirtle is a staller. Withdraw and Bubble are great for that action, unfortunately not it's HP. Seel has better HP so it will stand a little more beating. They both can be retreated for very little. By now you have a Dewgong, Gyarados, Articuno, or something else that puts up more of a defense and offense.
