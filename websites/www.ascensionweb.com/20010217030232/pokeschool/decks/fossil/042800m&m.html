3 Mr.Mime

3 Scyther

4 Scoop Up

4 Mysterious Fossil
4 Clefairy Doll
2 Imposter Professor Oak

27 Psychic Energy

THE GOAL OF THE DECK:
The goal of my deck is to stall 'til my opponent runs out of cards. I've given it the name M & M because the decks builds upon Mewtwo and Mr. Mime. Here's how to play the deck - stall with Clefairy Doll / Mysterious Fossil / Scyther / Chansey / Mr.Mime and make it even tougher for your opponent by using Scoop Up, Pokemon Center and Defender. In the meantime, put a ton of energy cards on Mewtwo. Eventually you bring him out and use his Barrier attack until your opponent runs out of cards. This deck's main problem is that it has a tough time playing decks with lots of Gust of Winds, because then the opponent keeps bringing in my Mewtwo all the time. But, it usually wins anyways...

Josh Petsonk
