The power of this deck comes with simplicity, i find it very easy to whimisically pound my enemies with energy removals and hyper beams. THis deck can remover so much energy it can actually make a rain dance deck useless, it gets so bad that one of my enemies actually ran out of energy completely, none left in his deck at all. I shouldn't have to explain or brag much more except about the breeders, they get the wraths up fast, wags are just there to evolve (and fight while waiting for certain cards) bill are there to draw what you need fast and get hyper beaming up to crack fast, double colorless to actually propel the pokemon faster. overall this deck rocks, has scattered weaknesses and is about the most annoying thing you'll ever fight.
