Pure Power III
Sal Farruggia 2/20/00

This was the first organized tournmnet I ever went to. It was held at Mark's Comics and Collectibles 109 North Centreat Ave Valley Stream, New York on Saturday 2/19. The registratrion was 5 dolars and 14 people showed up. I went with my friend Ryan, I made his deck. He wanted me to make him a 7 color deck with pokemon that don't evolve so I put in, Pinsir, Fossil Magmar, Lapras, Tauros, Hitmonlee, Jynx, and Electabuzz. It works good because it shouldn't. Well enough about him lets get to me. I played my haymaker Pure Power III. I have been perfecting it since Septermber '99

4 Hitmonchan- Pure Speed
3 Scyther- KIlls the Hitmonchan
3 Tauros- For the occasional Physic deck

12 Fighting- Don't need many
5 Grass- For that swords dance
4 DCE- for Tauros and Scyther

4 Bill- Gets the cards I want
4 Plus Power- That extra 10
4 Gust of Wind- Kill the Charmanders before they evolve
4 Energy Removal- Denyial
3 Pro. Oak- Gets the cards I want
2 Super Potion- Heals that Hitmonchan
2 SER- See ya later energy
2 Computer Search- Gets the cards I want
2 Item Finder- Gets the cards I want
2 Switch- When there parylized, alseep, confused and Tauros and Hitmonchan have a 2 retreat

60 cardz

The idea of it is to have out quick pokemon and make them even quicker with trainers. There is no need for energy becaue these traines get the energy quick. You wouldn't belive how many one hit K.O I have. I would send out hitmonchan agianst a weak basic. Attach a plus Powers. Pro. Oak then Computer Search and kill that 40 hp basic. It works great. Scyther is for protection from Hitmonchans and 60 damage in 2 turns. Tauros is there for protection from Physic

We played a Swiss Style tournmnet and the top 4 would go into a round robin.

This is my tournmnet report

He had a Moltres with 3 energies on it by I SER. won by prizes
Record 1-0

Sal vs. Andrew [(Kinda everything deck)Used Buzz, Chan, Chansey, Aero]
This guy was a grown man there with his wife. Everyone was afraid to play him, I know I was. It started out Scyther vs. Scyther. I won the flip and had those grass enegies in my hand to swords dance. But we both were in a Energy Removing frenzy. He played a SER and ER, and so did I. At the end though I ended up having more card advantage so I had the energies. I forced him to Oak and he was barley. Scyther won this entire match. He ended up killing a Ditto for the win. won by prizes
