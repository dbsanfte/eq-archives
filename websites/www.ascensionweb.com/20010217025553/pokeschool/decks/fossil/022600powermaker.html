Power Maker
Aaron Rosier 2/26/00

Hi my name is aaron rosier....
and here is my POWER MAKER.

POKEMON:16
2x scyther
3x electabuzz
2x aerodactyl
3x hitmonchan
3x fossil magmar
3x mysterious fossil

ENERGY:26
8x fire energy
6x electric energy
8x fighting energy
4x double colorless

TRAINERS:18
3x bill
3x plus power
4x super potion
2x professor oak
3x energy search
3x computer search

STRATEGY:
All of the pokemon have high hp and are basic (except the aerodactyl line) which means there is no time used building up higher evolutions. There are plenty of energy for each pokemon, so you will have what you need or get it soon by using the bills and professor oaks. The energy searches allow you to find the energy needed immediately and are much easier than hoping to draw the right energy. This deck can also deny the opponent of prizes by using switches and super potions. The super potions with high hp is excellent because they can eliminate a lot of damage to prevent a KO, and you can easily retreat a weak pokemon to the bench. The plus powers can be added to all of the attacks to KO weak basic pokemon (30 or 40 hp). the computer searches help you evolve aerodactyl a.s.a.p.

With a few good draws an aerodactyl can stop any high evolution deck. I choose to use the hitmonchans, electabuzzes and magmars because they are great to start with because of their one energy attacks with and possibility of preventing the opponents attacks (except hitmonchan) . The electabuzzes and magmars 2nd attacks only require 2 energy which are quick and give you time to charge a scyther aerodactyl. The hitmonchans provide for powerful attacks durning opening turns also,but aren't able to prevent attacks. Scyther is excellent because of his resistance and zero retreat cost his attack is good and should be built up while he's on the bench. I don t suggest starting with scyther or aerodactyl(unless you draw a dce quick) because their attacks both require 2 or 3 energy. Having 3 types is excellent because you can customize your attack to the opponents weakness or your resistance by switching or retreating for a powerized bench of a different kind. If you don t have all the cards machops can cover for hitmonchans, farfetched for scyther,and charmander or base magmar for fossil magmar. computer searches can be replaced with their less effective counterpart, pokeball. The fossil magmar line can also be compleyely replaced with jynx and psy energy, depending on what decks are popular in your area.

aaron
