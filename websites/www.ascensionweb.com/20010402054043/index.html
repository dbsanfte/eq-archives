Quake III Arena v1.27 released
After such a long wait, id Software has finally released the v1.27 point release patch, which is an official non-beta patch with major changes from v1.17 (the previous non-beta patch), including less dependency on frame-rates, increased smoothness, and no damage through floors (RL shots from below floors).
