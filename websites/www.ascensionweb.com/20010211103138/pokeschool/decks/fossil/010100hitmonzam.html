Hitmonzam
by Nolan Ogdahl
Pokemon
1 Alakazam
3 Kadabra
4 Abra
4 Hitmonchan
4 Chansey

Trainers
4 Bill
4 Energy Removal
3 Pokècenter
2 Professor Oak
2 Gust of Wind
1 Computer Search
1 Pokemon Trader
1 Scoop Up

Energy
12 Fighting
11 Psychic
3 Double Colorless

The main purpose with this deck is to hit hard with Hitmonchan and Kadabra while Alakazam is swapping damage to chansey on the bench and then pokècentering or scooping up the damage.

Hitmonchan: He is in here because he is a heavy hit with a respectable amount of hp. He can take damage and give some back.

abra/kadabra/alakazam: Abra is basically in here to evolve into kadabra and alakazam. Kadabra is in here to evolve and be a heavy hitter. Like hitmonchan he can take some damage and give it right back. If I don't have any chanseys out I can just move damage to kadabra and have him use recover so he is versitile in this deck. Alakazam is in this deck for one reason and that is "Damage Swap." This is probably the best power out there.

chansey: Chansey is in here to take damage. I can get rid of the damage one of three ways. One I can scoop up chansey. Two I can pokècenter the damage away. Or three I can bring chansey up on a suicide mission and get rid of it that way. The third way is a last resort only though.

Theres my deck I hope you enjoy it. Any questions or comments email me at mewtwosith@pokeschool.com
