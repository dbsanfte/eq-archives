STRATEGY
I'm gonna discuss the strategy first so you won't get totally confused by the pokemon combo in this deck.

Straight to the point…

If Kangaskhan is active and Dark Gloom is on the bench… you can use D.Gloom's Pokemon power to confuse the Defending Pokemon. You have a 50/50 chance. Regardless of this fact, that 50/50 chance doesn't effect Snorlax because he's immune to status effects! And if Snorlax is powered up, then you can get some serious attacking in!

If Gengar is on your bench at the same time… use his Pokemon power to distribute some damage everywhere to whatever pokemon your opponent is playing.

That's the main emphasis of the deck… Snorlax vs anything.

If Haunter is ready… then use the Trainer SLEEP and if it works, that's a 50 point attack right away. In any case, all of your pokemon can contribute to a series of attacks.

Having multiple Gengar's on the bench will help you get those KO's when needed.

Scyter can fly in and out at free will. If confused, he doesn't lose any energy trying to retreat so he helps out D.Gloom a whole lot as well and can assist Snorlax if you don't want to use up energy beefing up Snorlax's attacks. Just soak up damage and Scoop Up when you are fully powered.

Use Computer Search to get some needed Pokemon! You can discard Energy or even some NEEDED pokemon to do that. The reason I say you can discard NEEDED pokemon is because Nightly Garbage Run can get them back if ya need em.

It's not an extremely fast deck, but Boss's Way gets you what you need and Computer Search speeds your evolutions up for ya!

Snorlax CAN stall and Scoop Ups will save the day.

This deck is kinda a lock down deck or maybe a deck that really slows your opponent down from his offense… Just focus on Kang and D.Gloom and the rest should fall into place.

Let me know what ya think!
