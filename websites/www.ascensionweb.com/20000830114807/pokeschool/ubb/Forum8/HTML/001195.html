"Mother! Nooooo!," the young boy let out a deep moan as flaming beam fell from above and crushed the woman where she lay, stunned from the paralyzing blast let out by...him. The boy grabbed his father's blade from the mantle and lunged at the stranger. Are that could be heard was laughter.
The man awakened from his dream moaning in agony. His heavy breathing broke the cold silence. His Ninetales stared at him as he clenched his teeth in a rage. He stood up, his black cape billowing from under his red tunin. He began to walk when he collapsed against a tree. "Why did she have to die? Why? WHY!?" He began to beat the tree in his rage, his Ninetales just lay back down. "C'mon, we're going" he told it. It seemed emotionless as they walked along the forest. He noticed the abundance of wildlife, probobly due to the Mana saps that plaged the land. Those ****ed elementals. Sapping the world for their own personal use. He emerged from the forest entering a small village. Abandoned. His blade swung at his side as he walked. He glanced around, skeletons lay waste everywhere. He noticed a glint and reached down to pick it up. A rifle, perfectly usless against the Allience. Why did they even bother? He walked down the path as his Ninetales just followed along. All of a sudden he heard screams. So maybe there was life somewhere in this unholy wasteland! He started running towards the source. All of a sudden he began to glow and began to run at an unhamanly possible speed. He stopped. The Allience! He took shelter behind a tree as he watched the scene. A young woman was being prodded by one of the Allience's beasts, a Scyther. She screamed out in agony as it swiped a scar across her forehead. A stream of blood ran down his face.
One of the men there seemed to be the leader. "Tell us where the crystal is, NOW!" he yelled. She spat at him in defience. He wiped the siliva from his face and gave her a smack. "Tell me now!" All of a sudden a blast of flames lept out from the forest. It burnt the leader in the lower torso bringing him to the ground where he writhered in pain. Then the beast itself, as if possesed, left from the forest and gave another member a gashing cut across his head. Then, emerging from the forest came the man himself. He grinned, the look of hell itself frozen on his face. "Scyther! Attack that beast!"one of the men yelled. The Scyther leaped forward, both scythes forward ready to strike.
"Ninetales, Hellfire attack now!" The Ninetales reared its head accepting the command and began to glow a reddish glow. It snarled as chunks of the Earth rose from the ground, burst into flames and launched out at the Scyther. The Scyther let out a "SCY!" as it was struck by one, than another, of the flaming balls. It fell, dead. The men stood silent.
"You killed my scyther! you'll pay!" screamed one. He stepped forward, blondish hair, brown eyes, typical young male, only about 16.
"Shut up beginner, it was your own fault, fire destroys grass, plain and simple."
"Bring it on fool!"
"Is that a challenge?"
"Yes"
"Alright then, let's go." The two men unsheathed their swords. The young boy had a common katana. It looked worn, not fit for battle. This would be a short match. The man brought out the old family blade, still shiny brightly due to constant cleaning. He was awfully proud of it. He thought of all the cold blood that had touched it and shivered.
"What are you waiting for! Let's go!" The boy lunged forward but hit air. "Huh, where'd he go?" The boy looked around when he saw the man, up in a tree, it would be his last sight. The man leeped from the tree piercing the boy. The boy fell, match over.
"Anyone else want to battle?" They all stared in horror. How had he gotten into the tree so fast? The Ninetales began to blow a wind of fire scaring the men away. They picked up their fallen comrads and ran. The man walked over to where the girl stood, still bound to a tree. He cut the ropes with his blade and began to bandage her wounds. "That Scyther ruffed you up pretty bad back there huh?"
"Yeah but I'm ok, thanks to you."
"Thanks, so what were those creeps bugging you for?"
"The Elemental Crystal."
"What!? Do you have it!?"
"Don't you think I would've used it?"
"Oh, yeah..., then why were they asking you then?"
"They ask everyone, they really want it."
"I know, so what's your name?"
"Kiown Shoish, whats yours?"
"Blackheart, Mornstar Blackheart"
"That's your real name?"
"No, no one knows my real name, and if they did they aint living now."
"That's nice."
"Yeah, anyway you got pretty banged up but you should be fine, see ya."
"Wait!"
"What do you want now?"
"Take me with you."
"Why should I do that?"
"My entire family, village, everyone, they're all gone, I don't have anywhere to go." she began to cry. Ninetales seemed annoyed and was ready to leave already.
"Fine, you can come with us but we dump you at the first sight of civilization A'right?"
"Ok, fair enough." And with that they set off.

"Golduck! Retaliate with a Water Gun!" The Golduck nodded and fired short bursts of water from it's fingertips. Mornstar jumped but the high-pressure blast pieced his leg. Ninetales was down.
"Ninetales! Get out of here! I'll handle things" Mornstar shouted. He grabbed his blade and held it in the air. "Ninetales! Flame Sword Now!" he yelled. Ninetales shot a burst of fire at the sword, charging it. Mornstar ran forward, kicked an Allience member in the head, jumped off of the member and slashed another member through the leg. The Golduck tried to hit him but he was to fast and slashed straight through the Golduck's chest. The Golduck fell.
"Darn you! Go Nidoking!" yelled one in anger. He chucked another disc at the ground and a Nidoking appeared. It's muscled bulged.
"What you got these things on? Steroids?" asked Mornstar.
"Nidoking! Horn Attack." The thing fired the horn off it's head straight at Mornstar grazing the same leg that had been hit before. He fell.
"Looks like your screwed huh?" said one. He grabbed a dagger and held it towards Mornstar's heart.
"Freewing! Stun Spore!" While the Allience was distracted Kiown had freed her Butterfree! The giant winged creature began to sprinkle massive amounts of glittering powder over the group. They all fell to the ground paralyzed.
"Nice work." said Mornstar
"Thanks." Said Kiown "I still owed you from earlier today."
"Yeah I guess so." They proceeded to set the pokemon free. The pokemon left to reunite with their masters, if they could find them anyway. Kiown hugged her Butterfree.
"C'mon, we gotta go, and now we got a plane!" said Mornstar.
"Yes, let's go." Kiown said. They boarded into the plane and set off, with Ninetales staring out the window.

I hope you like it. Looking out over the horizion, Mornstar spotted nothing of interest. He lay back in his chair putting the plane on auto-pilot. "Some manual telling you how to make your pokemon stronger."
"Anything interesting?"
"It has a whole bunch of plants that make a pokemon's attributes stronger."
"I know those, Rabi Seed, Trawill, that's beginners stuff."
"You know all that?"
"Yup, been studing it for years." With a sigh Kiown went back to her reading. Mornstar laid back once more and paused to look out the window. He looked down at a great big oak tree. All of a sudden the tree fell limp and crashed to the ground. "Whut the!" he Exclaimed. Kiown looked at him.
"What's wrong?" she asked.
"We're screwed." he answered
"Why?" she asked. Right then a giant lightning sphere engulfed their plane in flames.
"That's why, now get out of here!" He opened up the hatch and Nintales and Freewing both went out. Mornstar and Kiown grabbed parachutes and lunged out of the plane right before it was hit by another ball of fire. They opened the chutes and landed in a small clearing. Ninetales and Freewing were already there.
"What the heck was that!?" Asked Kiown.
Mornstar paused to catch his breath before speaking. "That was Electra, elemental of lightning, she's sucking mana again, I don't think she wants us around."
"Not a very nice person huh?"
"Nope, anyway I bet she thinks we're dead. It is getting dark so she wont be able to see us. We should be able to spend the night here."
"Ok, let's go to bed then." The two found the planes wreckage and managed to pull a Sleeping bag and a Blanket from it. Kiown pulled out a coin. "Call it, for the sleeping bag."
"Heads" It landed Tails.
"Sucks for you I guess" Kiown said as she curled up in the sleeping bag. Mornstar lay down. Ninetales came over and lay next to him.
"Lucky you, you got yourself a built-in sleeping bag huh old fella?" He said scratching Ninetales behind the ears. "Good night he said and went to sleep." Late at night they heard a crash.
"What was that!?" asked Kiown
"It's Electra!, we gotta get out of here or we're dead, this time, try not to puke."
"Wait! AGHHHH!" Kiown screamed as they ran through the forest. They came to a river. "Oooohhh, my stomach." growned Kiown.
"Get some water but hurry up." said Mornstar. She waled over to the river, drank up some water, and then puked. The hurl floated around for a few seconds and when she saw it she puked again. Mornstar laughed. "Here" he said, handing her a bottle with some blue liquid in it, "drink this, you'll feel better." She gulped it down and stood up.
"Wow, it worked, were did you get that?"
"I whipped it up while we were in the plane, thought we might need it." Just than a lightning bolt came blasting out of the forest, hitting some trees overhead. "Crud! Let's go!" He grabbed Kiown right before a huge oak fell straight on their heads. They stopped in a cluster of trees. "Phew, I think we lost him." He said
"Uh, Mornstar, Where the heck are our pokemon!?"
"Uh oh. They must be trying to fight Elecrta! They're nuts!"
"We gotta go back there!" Yelled Kiown.
"I know, I know! Look, you stay here, I'll get those two."
"Wait!" She yelled, but Mornstar was gone in a blur. He arrived back at the river. Freewing and Ninetales were trying to do their best against Electra but it was no use. Both had heavy cuts and scars. Electra, in her yellow and black body suit was commanding lightning magic to use against the two, her eyes glowed a flashing gold color, with a scar resting on her right cheek. She floated a few inches above the ground. She was a powerful elemental indeed, one of the Power15, a group of the most elite elementals. They were extremally strong and commanded a vast army of pokemon. Electra seemed bored fighting off the pokemon as if they weren't even there. Mornstar creeped up behind Viastro and raised his blade. Electra whipped around and shot a bolt of electricity at Mornstar knocking him back a good ways.
"Ha! Think I couldn't sense you? Well you were wrong!"
"Shut up! You think you're so strong using magic on helpless people. I challenge you to a no magic duel! Right now!"
"You dare to challenge the great Electra, master of lightning?"
"No, the other electric elemental! Yes you, you stupid fool!"
"Fine! Let's go!" Electra whipped out a sword. She consintrated on it and soon it pulsated with electricity.
"I said no magic!"
"Too bad! Get ready!"
"Ninetales! Flame Sword!" yelled Mornstar. Ninetales charged up Mornstar's sword with a powerful blast of energy. "Now! Get out of here! Both of you!" Freewing flew off but Ninetales whimpered. "I said get!" With a sigh, Ninetales ran towards Freewing. "Let's do this." said Mornstar. He thrusted for Electra but she paried the blow and countered with a blow to the stomach. He got off with a scar but she was soon moving so fast that all he saw was a blur. He still managed to block most of the moves but suffered some nasty shocks from where the sword hit him. He jumped up and caught Electra off guard by giving her a kick in the head. She fell back.
"That hurt! Now you'll pay." She ran forward and thrusted hitting him straight in the lower thigh. He groaned and fell. Looking up he saw her looming over him ready to strike the final blow. Than he realized something. The Lightning Elemental Stone! It was embedded on the middle of her body suit. He grabbed his blade and thrust at the Stone chipping off a piece of it. "AGGGHHHH!!!" Screamed Electra. She was apparently recieving most of her power from that stone. "Oh, you just wait till I tell everyone about you! You'll pay!" She raised her arms up and flew off into the sky. Mornstar sighed with relief and limped back to the others.
"What happened to you!?" Asked Kiown.
"Ugh, Electra is pretty danged strong."
"Here, I'll bandage that wound for you, lie down." She told him. He obliged. She began to take some first aid supplies out of his backpack and fix him up.
"You remind me of my mother." he mumbled, and with that he fell asleep.
