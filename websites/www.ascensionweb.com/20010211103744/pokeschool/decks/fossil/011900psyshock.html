This is not a stall deck. Then why are Mr. Mime, Chansey, and Alakazam there then? It is a Haymaker/Damage Swap Deck. Haymakers have this one thing. They use high powered quick attacks which in two hits, if not one, will take out the opponent. So with those two hitters, like Scyther vs. my Electabuzz. I can damage swap and basically neutralize the damage.

First put down a Electabuzz, Mr. Mime, Mewtwo, or Chansey. With the first three attack, attack, attack! With Chansey, scrunch, scrunch, scrunch! Now while this is going on search for that Alakazam. but they'll murder that Abra! Well with this deck, there is a commonly used PLAN A, and a commonly used PLAN B.

PLAN A is to get that Alakazam to tip the scales. PLAN B is to use Abra as bait. While they get the Abra, you pick up your pumped up Mewtwo and blow him up next turn.

The enemy I have found always jumps on the first appearence. I send out Electabuzz first, he thinks instantly, HAYMAKER! I send out Abra, he instantly thinks STALL! That's when they get confused. Abra in Haymaker!!!!????!!!!????... They either jump and kill it wasting that Gust of Wind when they could have used it when I had a Mewtwo out against his Hitmonchan with a nice Electabuzz on the bench sitting there. Or when there is PLAN A, he thinks oh look, an Electabuzz HAYMAKER! I can almost sit there and evolve that Abra into a king size ALAKAZAM. By then he's lost all hope of killing Kadabra or even instant Alakazam (breeders). I don't know many Haymakers who can KO Alakazam in one hit.
