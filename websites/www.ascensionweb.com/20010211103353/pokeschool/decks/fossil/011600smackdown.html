4 Double Colorless Energy
21 Psychic Energy

This deck you use to bring out something weak on the 1st turn. The pokemon that you may have out are probably a Dratini, Gastly, or a Dodou. Soon you will evolve all those pokemon and here is what you do with each.

Dragonite: Use his Step in Pokemon Power if any of your other pokemon is damaged or status effected like Paralized or Asleep.

Dodrio: Use his rage attack and his Retreat Aid so you can switch all the pokemon in this deck (not including Dragonair, that bum)

Gengar: Use the Curse and the Dark Mind to possably KO two pokemon at one time! I guess the saying, hitting 2 birds with 1 stone works in this case. Very good card.

If you know what these cards do, you shouldn't have trouble with this deck.
