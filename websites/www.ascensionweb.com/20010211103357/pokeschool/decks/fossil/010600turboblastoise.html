Turbo Blastoise
Tim "Tech" Saad 1/6/99

This is my Turbo Blastoise deck..

4 Blastoise
4 Squirtle
4 Computer Search
4 Bill
4 Professor Oak
4 Item Finder
4 Pluspowers
4 Super Energy Removal
4 Pokemon Breeder
24 Water Energy

Okay. Your probably wondering, "You Fool! 8 Pokemon, 4 Basic!?". Well, it works, and quite well. Mulliganing is no problem and u will probably do it 2-5 times a game. your opponents hand can be very devastating to this deck though. If he's playing Haymaker, you can see those Pluspowers in his hand from your mulligan for sure and if he lays down a Hitmonchan or an Electabuzz, you lost. Aerodactyl? Good game. Muk? You can still win, but it will be a long game. I think that this deck can be MURDERED tur 1 and 2. But usually, you always get a Blastoise turn 2. Thats the beauty of it. Blastoise turn 2 means 60 per a turn then on. THAT means, you'll probably win. The thing is, with all the manipulation in the deck, 2 Oaks turn 2 is common, as well as 6 energy on a Blastoise. I went to a local tourney, and in the semi-finals, i got 2 Blastoise turn 2 and it was over. each had 6 on turn 3, and his Charizards were his DEFENSE. I think that deck plays itself, and you'll know when you get that winning hand. Just remember, if you are playing in an area where a lot of people are playing with one of the decks weaknesses, I HIGHLY recommend that Pluspowers become Defenders. In my area, Charizard\Venusaur and Mewtwo and Scyther/Pinsir Grass is all the rage, so my eck is quite viable. Remember, if you thnk your opponent has a Pluspower in his hand, play like you know there are two. Defense is most important. Overestimation always ends up better that underestimation. By the way, I am thinking about addig something big for defense like Kangaskhan for defense because of Scyther. E-mail me at NotQuiteAznBalla@aol.com if you think something else would be more ideal. Thanks.

Tim "Tech" Saad

Doctor's Notes:
Rating: 10
I usually don't make comments on the deck submissions but after looking over this deck I really had to. It's the InSaNiTy deck with an evolution!! InSaNiTy is built for speed. Battling against Energy Removals is just a battle you have to endure as an InSaNiTy player. But this deck alleves this problem. 2nd Turn Blastoise and then Raindance turns this into an extremely fast deck. What other deck could be any faster? What other deck could you have two Stage 2 Pokemon fully powered by Turn 2 with a chance of 70 damage? Electabuzz gets charged too late to beat the speed of this deck. Wigglytuff can't KO Blastoise in one turn. And the new Haymaker addition, Magmar, is no more than a match light!

I have yet to see the results of this deck but by the success of InSaNiTy, I think it's a pretty solid deck.

Look out for Muk and basics in your prizes! Other than that...

Good job with this deck!
