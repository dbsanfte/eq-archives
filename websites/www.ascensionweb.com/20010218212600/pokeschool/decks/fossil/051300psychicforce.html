THIS DECK HAS A LOW AMOUNT OF ENERGY. MY DECKS OBJECTIVE IS TO STRAIGHT FORWARD MAKE MY OPPONANT RUN OUT OF CARDS.
THE POKEMON ARE ALL FOCUSED TO THIS "DECKING" THEME WITH MANY SERVING MULTIPLE USES. CHANSEY PUTS IN DOUBLE DUTY AS BOTH A "SCRUNCHING WALL" IN THE EARLY GAME IF NESSESSARY,AND A BENCHED "DAMMAGE SPOUNGE" OUNCE ALAKAZAM IS IN PLAY. SOAK ALL OF THAT DAMAGE ONTO YOUR BENCHED POKEMON(AFTER BEING TRANSFERED THERE BY ALAKAZAMS DAMAGE SWAP POWER),JUST BEFORE WIPING THE SLATE WITH ONE OF YOUR FOUR POKEMON CENTERS.MR. MIME IS UNPRECEDENT WITH ITS INVISABLE WALL POKEMON POWER.DONT LET THE 40 HIT POINTS FOOL YOU.MR. MIME WILL PUT UP ONE OF THE STRONGEST DEFENSES THAT THIS DECK OFFERS!!!!
MEWTWO'S BARRIER SHOULD ONLY BE USED IN EXTREME CIRCUMSTANCES,AS THE AMOUNT OF PSYCHIC ENERGY IN THIS DECK IS SEVERELY LAKING.MORE USEFUL WILL BE HIS PSYCHIC ATTACK MOVE FOR PUTTING DOWN THE MORE DANGEROUS POTENTIAL ATTACKERS BEFORE THEY CAN PENETRATE THE WALL. THIS IS ALSO THE REASON THAT KADABRA IS FEATURED (ALONG WITH ALAKAZAM'S DEPENDANCE ON HIM.)SUPER PSY CAN CLEAR OUT MORE THAN ONE WEENIE OPPONENT WHO THREATENS MR. MIMES LOW DAMAGE REQUIREMENT.
USE YOUR OPPONENT CONTROL TRAINER CARDS WISELY. THE ENERGY REMOVALS AND GUST OF WINDS WILL DISAPPEAR FASTER THAN YOU MAY WAN'T IF NOT CONSERVED FOR THE MOST APPROPRIATE MOMENTS.THE ITEM FINDER WILL HELP RECYCLE THESE WHEN NESSESARY,BUT THE KEY TO WINNING WITH THIS DECK IS PATIENCE. LASTLY IMPOSTER PROFFESSOR OAK CAN BE USED FOR THE MOST ADVANTAGE WHEN YOUR OPPONENT IS HOLDING ONLY 1 OR 2 CARDS IN THEIR HAND. BE CERTAIN THAT YOU HAVE THE GAME IN CONTROL HOWEVER,AS THE ADDITIONAL CARDS THEY DRAW WILL OPEN MANY OPTIONS TO THEIR ATTACK PLAN.
YOU WONT MAKE MANY FRIENDS WITH THIS DECK,BUT YOU CAN LET THAT WARM,COZY FEELING OF WINNING CONSOLE YOU.
