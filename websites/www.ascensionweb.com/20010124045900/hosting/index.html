WHAT IS ASCENSION?

Ascension is a Network of Affiliated Websites geared towards the 16-25 year old market. We give you the connections, audience and hosting you seek for FREE with no effort on your part. Now that you don't have to worry about your "Page Views" you can do what you do best - Build Websites.

WHAT WILL YOU DO FOR ME?

Exposure - Your site will be given as much exposure as possible by Ascension. You will be listed in our Network and have a banner for your site shown on other affiliated sites. Site happenings may even be announced on the Ascension News page.

Support - Ascension has three administrators for our servers. As such, around the clock support is available. You can contact the administrators via e-mail, fax, or telephone. Ascension is dedicated to providing the best support available.

Reliability - From past experience, Ascension has learned the importance of uptime. As such all servers are monitored and the admins will be paged if a server goes down. Remote reboot and control capabilities are available and full daily backups are made. Dedication - The Ascension team is dedicated to provide the best home for all of it's web sites. We are also dedicated to expanding the Ascension name and image, including all of the hosted sites. We have full time members as well as part time members that are available at all times to help you make your site and Ascension the best it can be.

Getting Paid - We have special Profit sharing programs in place for all of our sites that recieve over 100,000 page views per month. Ascension has more leveraging power because of the diversity of the network as a whole. We in turn use that leveraging to generate more cash for your sites.

BUT, I ALSO NEED HOSTING!

Web Space - While we expect your web resources to be within reason, there is no limit on web space we can provide for you. If we find that we are running short on disk space, we simply install more drives.

FTP Space - Hosted sites may have a directory structure dedicated to them. Again, there is no practical limit to the allowed disk space.

Bandwidth - Ascension is on a 100% dedicated T3 connection. Response time is excellent. Ascension will allow a site to use as much bandwidth as they can generate. Should the bandwidth start to peak beyond the T3, more bandwidth will be allocated.

File Types - We support a large spectrum of file types for your site. Including CGI scripts, ASP, and Access databases. We do NOT support downloadable file types such as (.rm, .mpg, .zip, .mp3, .wav, .mid,) etc. Their are many great services out their that will provide you with free space for these particular files.

For a complete listing of what we do and do not accept visit our Affiliate FAQ.

E-Mail - Your provide all of our Host's with full support E-Mail via our partnership with Everyone.net. Of course you don't have to go through them, but you will be guaranteed unlimited E-Mail accounts and forwarding addresses

Multiple NT Servers - This allows the best response time under high load situations while maintaining excellent security and reliability. Since NT is quickly becoming the standard on the Internet, you will find it compatible with web components that are freely available.

NT Accounts - There are several levels of accounts that are available to your webmasters. These include forwarding, e-mail only, FTP, and shell. The type of account is dependant upon the requirements for the user.

Services - Ascension provides a large number of services so that you may make your site the best it can be. These services currently include: SSI, CGI, Perl, and ASP. If there are other services that are required, request them and we will see about installing them.

Domains - Sub-domains of http://yourname.Ascension.com (Check out our list of Available Domain Names) will be provided for all hosted sites who request them (sub-domain sites must still meet the criteria for being hosted). Additionally we can host you under your own registered domain such as http://www.yourname.com.

Sub-Hosting - Hosted sites may host other sites. This is particularly useful for sites that cover a broad spectrum and have their own registered domain. They may have hosted sites at http://subhost.yourname.com (sub-domain sites must still meet our standards for being hosted).
