Fossil Haymaker
Alex McCollister 3/3/00

My new deck the;Fossil Haymaker

Fighting
x2 Aerodactyl
x3 Hitmonlee
x3 Hitmonchan

Grass
x3 Scyther

Electric
x4 Electabuzz

Trainers
x3 Potions
x3 Super Potion
x3 Energy Removal
x3 Bill
x2 Proffesor Oak

Energy
x18 Grass
x9 Electric
x4 Double Colorless

This deck is my third deck and has worked really great for me. The basic idea behind this deck is to use Aerodactyl's Prehistoric Power to keep the opponent from getting too strong. And of course use Hitmonchan, Scyther, and Electabuzz to make quick attacks while I power up Hitmonlee to do High Jump Kick for 50. Then, to disrupt your opponent's every move with Energy Removal. Believe me, it works.
