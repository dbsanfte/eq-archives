CUT GRASS

4 Oddish (TR)
3 Dark Gloom (TR)
3 Dark Vileplume (TR)
2 Ditto (Fossil)
3 Electabuzz
3 Scyther

3 Bill
2 Professor Oak
2 Pokemon Breeder
4 Scoop Up
3 Item Finder
3 Computer Search
3 Full Heal Energy

12 Grass
6 Electric
4 DCE

Based around 2 Ditto's, this deck has some serious power after mid-game battles. Dark Vileplume stops the Trainers and can also dish out up to 90 damage with 3 good coin flips. Full Heal Energy cards save the day if you get a good attack plus assist on retreating when need be. Scyther, Buzz support the attacks of Ditto and Scoop Ups can be used for any basic in trouble. The Breeders just helps stop the 'trainer game' (because Plume will be evolved) and reduces the speed of opponent.

Best use for this deck is to take some damage with Ditto, Scyther or Buzz while building to a Trainer stand still and a solid attack by Plume, Scy and Buzz.

Let me know what you think!!!

I think that trying to play Ditto, under the influence of Dark Vileplume, may be really tough. As well, Electabuzz seems like a strange choice, since as a splas type it does not offer any direct weakness/resistance protection.

1) I am not sure how well this deck can cope in the early game, especially since it does want to get out the Vileplume lock. I would try to either lower the reliance on the lock, take itout altogether, or support it. Addin in RSA is probably the first thing I would do. This will disable your opponent in those crucial first few turns. Plus a first turn RSA shows your opponent that you mean business! 3 copies would be a nice start.

2) This deck runs a surprising 25 energy. This is a little much. If you are planning to use for DCEs for Ditto and Scyther, that's O.K.. But I would then take out some Grass Energy. You can charge up Dark Vileplume slowly.

This is where having a splash type like Electabuzz may not be the best thing. I think that you should consider taking it out for more Dittos, or a colourless attacker.
