Since just about everybodys deck has coulores pokemon I decided to add in some pokemon that would kill them in one turn. (except for Chansey).The purpose of this deck is to get your Hitmonles out to kill all of the opponets coulores pokemon. Then scythers ad Hitmonchans are for dealing with the rest of the defending pokemon. The Magmars and Lickitungs are for staliing while you power up your hitmonles. The gust of winds are for when you have hitmonle out and you whant to kill their couloress pokemon. The thing most people think about my deck is that It has very few energys and it does thats why I added in some Energy Search.
