HOW THE DECK WORKS:
Ok,well theres my deck i had to change it because the only decks people played at the tornament were FIGHTING,and RAINDANCE...and i got killed by hitmonchan...I originaly had 3 magmars instead of GASTLY and the MAGMAR's got killed by RAINDANCE too quick,but i useually won...and GASTLY's are sooo much better now because they got NO weakness and resistance to FIGHTING...And a free retreat!!!!ok sooooo here's how to play it...if you play against a fighting deck DONT ever PUT down a jigglypuff or a ELECTABUZZ...just drop a SCYTHER or GASTLYand killem like that till u get rid of there fighting pokemon...thats when you drop the jiggly,wiggly combo...if ur playing against water,then your gonna start out with a electabuzz,and get a wigglytuff out with a full bench ASAP,it's not very hard to do with this deck,most of the time when you computer search if you dont already have a P.OAK in your hand thats what u get!There ya go the rest is up to you...
