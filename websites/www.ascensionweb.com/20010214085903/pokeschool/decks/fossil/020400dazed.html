Dazed and Dazzled
Thorin34 2/4/00

Without further ado here's my latest deck idea

17 Pokemon:
Psychic
3 Fossil Ghastly
2 Base Haunters
4 Base Drowsees
Electric
3 Base Electabuzz
2 Magnemite
Colorless
3 Jigglypuff

22 Energy:
13 Psychic Energy
9 Electric Energy

21 Trainers:
4 Bill
3 Prof Oak
3 Super Energy Removal
4 Energy Removal
3 Gust of Wind
2 Defender
2 Scoop Up

Deck Strategy

Now before anything is said, let me explain how this deck works and how fun and efficient it can be to play. This deck is a disruption deck. Every pokemon has a status effect that can turn the tables in a game. I didn't want poison in this deck outside of sleep because then pokemon powers wouldn't be disabled and then it would look more like a cleaner. I wanted a deck of my own creation. By the way sleep is good.. 50/50 chance just like parylyze for disableing opponent because all my sleep moves are definite.

Trainers should be used only at a key time in the game. (how often i go to tourneys and see people dump their hand so quick. then their screwed) I suggest only using ERs and SERs on the bigger pokemon (u can see my guys have trouble with them = low hp). Then u status them with any of my guys. You also have Gust of wind which is a great card for easy kills or to delay ur opponent. For Card advantage i felt the 4 bills and 3 oaks were enough. One should expect on using atleast one oak a game to recollect a hand or start fresh from a bad one. Why didn't i include Computer search?? well i don't really know but it works fine with out it. Computer search used to be in it but the deck doesn't rely on one card and i get all the combos i need anyway for a good game. It wastes space in this deck too and the 2 card discard can be a burden sometimes becaue this deck needs all the cards it gets.

This deck does very well against some other great decks out there today. First of it covers its weaknesses great. Psychic covers colorless and electrics weakness to fighting (ghastly is great against hitmochan and other ground types strong to electric, and drowzee is good agianst fighters) while colorless covers psychics weakness to psychic (Jigglypuff can be devastating to mr mime and other psychics). With a low retreat cost for all the pokemon u always have the pokemon out that u want. (leave it up to ghastly to retreive energy but u won't need it because there is pleanty of energy in the deck) Against raindance electabuzz and magnemite can take it down, and the statue effects can render it and other pokemon power based decks useless. The deck has an edge against hay makers too with the psychics. Against damage swappers u gust out abra with your plentiful gusts of winds and smash em'. Against other decks all u have to worry about is keeping energy off the heavy hitters and it does fine.
This deck is fast. Its needs very little energy (All pokemon can use a 1 energy attack) and this makes it good for being able to discard the energy for SER. against energy denial decks its awsome. Ghastly can recover tons of energy and many of my pokemon can recover quick with little energy needed for thier attacks. usually my pokemon are too fast and disabling for most decks anyway. Ser/prof oak and ghastly would probably be my first combo to mention but its not important because again, the deck uses plenty of energy.

other combos are defender and one of these three = ghastly, magnemite and electabuzz. having their self enflicted damage prevented and then ur opponents pokemon damage too next turn is awsome. 2nd turn 40 damage from magnemite (plus 10 form first turn thunder wave) can send most pokemon to pokeheaven. Plus magnemite is saved with the defense.

jiggly puff and base haunter are a great team. First turn jiggle stalls w/hypnosis and u build up ghastly. when u have 2 energy on him and u get lucky with sleep and the other pokemon is asleep during ur turn (this should happen with in those two turns you build ghastly up) retreat jiggly for the 1 energy and bring out ghastly. evolve to haunter and do a 3rd turn 50 damage. not bad and besides i thought haunter was useless??

Also scoop up is a great card with all my basics around. plus i can scoop up a weakened pokemon but SER first so the energy is not wasted. This move rox. i bring out a fully pumped guy too. and maybe rebuild that scooped up pokemon. Gust of wind is a great card for any deck but it works wonders with magnemite. hurt a pokemon down to ten and next turn retreat for magnemite, gust him out in the place of another pokemon (preferably wiht only 40 hp left) and you can kill 2 pokemon!! excellent (defender here is great).

Drowzee is the only weak pokemon in this deck. Electabuzz is capable of 40 damage, magnemite 40 damage, Jiggly puff 20, Haunter 50. (low hp between my pokemon could be a problem sometimes though) I'm hoping a new set will give me more options for a good confusion basic pokemon instead of drowzee. The status effects that the pokemon use are pretty well balanced between them which is cool.

The number of cards in the deck is key too. Magnemites are weak, cut back on them and defenders, scoop up used sparingly. This deck isn't perfect but i'm hoping i can give it a cool name atleast (mine are dorky) and i'm hoping for suggestions on how to improve the deck. Thanks for reading.
