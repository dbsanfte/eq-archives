MARCH 2000 DECKS

To submit a deck please send to Decks@Pokeschool.com. All decks MUST include:

1) A legal deck with SIXTY (60) cards.
2) At least a paragraph writeup on how the deck functions and what it is designed to do (Your strategy!).
3) Make sure to include the COMPLETE card names. No Abbreviations.
4) A name. Don't spend the time designing a killa' deck just to have someone else name it for you.
5) A deck meeting my rating of 6 or better (out of a scale from 1 to 10) will be posted.
*If you don't see your deck posted, send it to the Deck Clinic Forum for friendly advice from a Doctor.
(Submission guidelines updated 1/2/2000)

Why doesn't your deck get posted?
 ~It's an illegal deck. I get tons of decks that do not have 60 cards in them!
~Unreliable evolution ratios. If there is a 1:1 evol. ratio in your deck, forget about seeing it posted!
~No strategy! If you don't write out a strategy, then how doest his help those who want to use your deck?
