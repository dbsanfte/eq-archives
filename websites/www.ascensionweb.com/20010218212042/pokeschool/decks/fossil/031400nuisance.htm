Nuisance
Eileen 3/14/00

I call this the Nuisance Deck because it is meant to annoy and trap your opponent. Here it is:

18 Pokemon:

4 Oddish
4 League Promo Mew
4 Snorlax:
2 Gourmet Snorlax (CD Promo)
1 Snorlax (Vending Set)
1 Team Rocket Gym Snorlax
3 Evil Vileplume
3 Aerodactyl

20 Trainer:

4 Pokemon Breeder
4 Mysterious Fossil
3 Potion
3 Super Energy Removal
2 Bill
2 Imposter Oak
1 Switch
1 Scoop Up

22 Energy:

8 Psychic Energy
8 Grass Energy
4 Double Colorless Energy
1 Potion Energy
1 Full Heal Energy

This deck is based on pokemon powers, attacks, and trainers that annoy and trap your opponent. I guarantee that your opponent will be utterly frustrated if you play with a similar deck against them. Pokemon Powers: Aerodactyl--No pokemon are able to evolve. Evil Vileplume--No trainers can be played. Vendor Snorlax--Opponent cannot retreat his or her active pokemon. Rocket Snorlax--While Snorlax is asleep, if Snorlax is attacked, the attacking pokemon takes 20 damage (BONUS: this pokemon's attack, Faint, puts it to sleep and does 20 damage to the opponent.). Attacks: League Promo Mew--Deevolution Beam- (two psychic energy) choose one of either yours or your opponent's evolved pokemon, return the highest stage evolution card to that player's hand. Gourmet Snorlax--Eat-(one colorless energy) put one food counter on Snorlax, you cannot have more than two food counters on Snorlax at the same time. Roll-(three colorless energy) does 20 damage plus 30 more damage for each food counter on Snorlax. Trainers: Super Energy Removal-discard one energy from one of your own pokemon and discard up to 2 energy fro mone of your opponent's pokemon. Imposter Oak-Your opponent shuffles his or her hand into his or her deck and draws 7 new cards. Switch-Switch your active pokemon with a pokemon on your bench (good if your opponent "gust of wind" 's up a pokemon you don't want active). Scoop Up-Choose one of your pokemon in play and return its basic card to your hand and discard all cards attached to that card (good if you're out of healing cards and a pokemon has a lot of damage counters on it). That's about it. The idea is to keep the pokemon with the pokemon powers on your bench to anger your opponent because they can't do anything except attack and draw, while you either attack them for 80 damage with the Gourmet Snorlax or deevolution beam them with the League Promo Mew. If you got Aerodactyl out after the opponent evolved a pokemon, you can deevolution beam it and then your opponent can't reevolve it.
