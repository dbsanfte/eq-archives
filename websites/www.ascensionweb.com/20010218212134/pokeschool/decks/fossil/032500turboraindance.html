Strategy

My strategy for this deck is to get my Squirtle and Blastoise out fast with Oaks, Bills, and a Computer Search. So I can use Blastoise Pokemon Power: Rain Dance so I can power up all my water pokemon fast and easy to kill my opponents pokemon. My Energy Retrieval is used to retrieve all my water energy because this deck ha to have lots of energy to use to use my 3 Blastoise attack that needs 5 water energy that deals 60 damage. The Lapras and Articuno helps me in stalling my opponent in killing all my Squirtle when I have a bad hand. Lapras with HP of 80, can stall my opponent by using its confuse ray that just takes two water energy and might confuse the pokemon which they have to flip a coin to see if they can attack. When the Lapras is out stalling me I power up my Articuno with the HP of 70 which its not bad for a basic pokemon. For its first attack called Freeze Dry (30) which takes three water energy and you can paralyzed the opponent if its heads. Its Other attack is Blizzard (50) is used to try attack my opponents bench and the active by when use this attack it deals 50 damage and you have to flip a coin, heads your opponent bench deal 10 damage to every pokemon in his/her bench, this the bad part of his attack because if it tails your benched pokemon deals 10 damage to every of my benched pokemon. The Plus Power are used for to kill the opponents pokemon that is higher HP than my pokemon attacks like when I attack a Scyther with the HP of 70 and I have a Blastoise as my active pokemon I use the plus power on my Blastoise and it pushes his attack by 10, to equal 70 and killing the Scyther. The Gust of Wind is to take out the weak pokemon or the pokemon that you think might make you lose and kill it fast. The Plus Power and Gust of Winds is like the best combo to use in killing pokemon in my opinion.
