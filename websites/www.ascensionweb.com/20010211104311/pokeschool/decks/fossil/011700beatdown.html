Here's my deck:

16 Pokemon

3 Arcanine
3 Chansey
3 Fossil Magmar
3 Lapras

26 Energy
4 DCE
13 Fire Energy
9 Water Energy

18 Trainers
4 Bill
2 Oak
3 Defender
3 Super Potion
2 Computer Search
2 Energy retrieval
2 Switch

Pokemon Stratgey: Arcanine is the pokemon this deck revolves around. 80 damage by possibly turn 3 is phenomanal. (2 Fire Energy, 1 DCE) Fossil Magmar is here for early damage and prolonged damage with poison. Chansey can stall if needed or can dish out damage with Double Edge. Lapras is good aginst other water with confuse ray, and great aginst other fire with water gun.

Energy Stratgey: Well, you do need energy to attack...

Trainer stratgey: Bill and Oak for card advantage, Defender and Super Potion for damage from take down and double edge, computer search to get what i need, energy retrieval for arcanine's flame thrower, and switch for lapras and arcanine's retreat costs.
