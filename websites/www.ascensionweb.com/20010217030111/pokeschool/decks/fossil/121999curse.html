Curse Deck

4 Abra
3 Kadabra
2 Alakazam
4 Ghastly
4 Haunter
3 Gengar
4 Mr. Mime
2 Mewtwo- 1 Movie Mewtwo
3 Chansey
2 Ditto

20 Psychic energy
2 Double colorless energy

2 Super potion
1 Energy retrieval
1 Pokemon breeder
1 Pokemon trader
1 Super energy removal
1 Gust of Wind

I have played in 5 tournaments so far and have won every one with this deck. The deck bases on; Gengar, Mr. Mime, and Chansey. What I do is put out Mr.Mime as a distraction. He he gets ready to die retreat him and put out a Chansey and then use him as a distraction. While doing that, load up on Psychic energy on Gengar. Use Gengar's curse to hurt the opponets benched pokemon. Then hurt the defending pokemon with Gengar's Dark Mind attack. If this does not work, (it didn't work for me one time) then use Alakazam's Damage swap on Chansey to heal your Gengar back. Make sure you have a couple of Super potion's in your hand before you try this strategy.
