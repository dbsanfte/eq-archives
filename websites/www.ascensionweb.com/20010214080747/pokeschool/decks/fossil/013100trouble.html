Trouble

Pokemon:

2x Ditto(Helps out when ever trouble comes, i.e. for damage swap, raindance, other haymakers, etc.)
4x Scyther(My Fav. card, hence 4 of them, i use them as colorless, and free retreat helps alot to stall)
3x Aerodactyl(ok card, 30 damage for 3 energy, pretty good, pokemon power also rules against damage swap and raindance)
3x Hitmonchan(What can i say?! My second fav. Card!)
1x Farfetch'd(I just use him as a backup for scyther after i retreat scyther, pot smash ain't bad eighter)

Trainers:
1x Energy Retrieval(just in case)

4x Mysterious Fossil(to stall when i have to and for Aerodactyl)
3x Gust of Wind(To get out the weak pokemon and kill em' in one hit, hehehehehehehe)
4x potion(i wish i could put more, helps keep those heavy hitters in there)

Energy:
20x Fighting Energy(i use them for the colorless and to keep it organized)
2x Double colorless(for the pokemon that require 3 colorless energy, i like to attch one, then on next turn attach a Double Colorless and attack on the second turn instead of the 3rd turn)
Stategy:
Although this may not be the original haymaker, i still like it and i play it at Pokemon Leauge, at friend's houses and at tournements. It's won every match almost. So it's total record is probably like 20-5. Enough Jabbering, my deck's strategy is to attack fast and K.O. pokemon as quickly as possible before they can evolve. If they have High HP then that's when Aerodactyl comes in (pokemon power). I try to keep him on the bench though just for the pokemon power. I use Scyther, Hitmonchan, and Hitmonlee as the "Big Hitters" of my deck. They hit really hard and energy need as that bad.

Retreat cost are also low.

When I'm in big need of it, I use this plan called, my hit and retreat. When my oppenet is starting to pull away with the lead, i hit as hard as i can, then retreat, and hit hard again trying to keep damage as low as possible without my oppenent getting more prizes, if all else fails, then i send out my Scythers to hit with 60 or 30, retreat for free, and send out another and keep doing this.

I hope you like this deck and post it up. It took me ALOT of trades to get the pokemon i need for this deck. If you have any questions, comments, or tips, email me at mongoosemaniac@hotmail.com
