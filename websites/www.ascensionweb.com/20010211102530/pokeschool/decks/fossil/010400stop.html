Strategy
Get Muk out fast against decks that use powers like Mime or Raindance, etc. Then get your Haymaker going. Or get your Haymaker Pokemon up and ready and evolve Muk when needed. Grimer is good because it paralyzes Defending Pokemon like Electabuzz would and if in trouble, Muk evolves and dishes out some Poison and still has the basic 70HP like most Haymaker Pokemon have
