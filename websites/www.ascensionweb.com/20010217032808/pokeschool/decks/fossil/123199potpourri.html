Haymaker's Insane Potpourri v3.0
Steve Farrington 12/31/99

Pokemon: 13
1 Ditto (Transform into something they have, but you don't)
4 Electabuzz (BBP)
3 Hitmonchan (For those Electabuzz's)
3 Scyther (For those Hitmonchan's)
2 Magmar Lv.31 (for those Scythers)

Energy: 25
6 Fire Energy
7 Electric Energy
9 Fighting Energy
3 Double Colorless Energy

Trainers: 22
4 Energy Removal
4 Super Energy Removal
2 Computer Search
2 Item Finder
2 Scoop Up
2 Gust of Wind
2 Plus Power
2 Bill
2 Professor Oak

The deck functions much like a normal Haymaker, but the basic object is to match up Weakness/Resistance with your opponent (i.e. You oppenent has a Scyther out - you send out your Magmar, not your Hitmonchan). Secondly, you want to try and oak with verly little cards in your hand. Do everything you can before oaking, to give you a better chance to win.
