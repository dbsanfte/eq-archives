Fire Starter
Andy Scruggs 3/2/00

Fire starter by Andy Scruggs at gengar234@hotmail.com

Pokemon=14
4xElectabuzz
4xScyther
4xMagmar (fossil edition)
2xlickitung

Trainers=20
4xenergy removal
4xsuper energy removal
4xitem finder
4xscoop up
4xgust of wind

Energy=26
4xdouble colorless
13xfire energy
11xelectric energy

Summary: This deck is made to desimate energy. After you use your energy and super energy removals you can itemfinder to get them back. Scoop ups work well because if you count double colorless on scyther then all the pokemon are fully loaded with two energy, and scooping them won't cost you as much. When you use this deck only put one energy on your benched pokemon so they are less apt to get super energy removaled. Use the gust of winds to your advantage and wait till your opponent sets down a pokemon that you can kill quick. If you face a psychic deck then pair likitung with scoop ups and a scyther in your bench. If you scoop lickitung send out scyther and lay lickitung back down then retreat scyther and send out lickitung. This wall will drive psychic decks mad. If there is a mister mime then i would recomend magmar's smog. Also always use the energy on your active pokemon for super energy removals right before you scoop them up. This lets you take advantage of all your energy.
