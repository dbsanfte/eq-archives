Layeth the Smacketh Down
Bert Hufnagel 1/16/00

Hi. This is my Deck: Layeth the Smacketh Down:

X3 Hitmonchan
X3 Electabuzz
X3 Scyther
X3 Mr. Mime
X1 Kangaskhan
X1 Ponyta

X4 Bill
X4 Gust of Wind
X3 Professor Oak
X3 PlusPower
X3 Energy Removal
X2 Super Energy Removal
X2 Item Finder
X1 Computer Search
X1 Energy Retrieval

X7 Fighting Energy
X6 Electric Energy
X6 Psychic Energy
X4 Double Colorless Energy

My Staretegy: My strategy is to get my Speed Pokemon out fast, if it's anyone but Scyther, Mr. Mime or Kangaskhan. I don't want Scyther, Mr. Mime or Kangaskhan out first because I'll have to wait at least 2 turns before I can deal any damage, which isn't good. My Energy count is balance in the matter of getting the right Energy at the right time. I have more Fighting Energy than the others because Hitmonchan needs 2 Energy of its type instead of 1. As for the Trainers, like you said 'Trainers win Games'! So, I figured I only need 1 Energy Retrieval because I have more that enough Energy for all my Pokemon. I also have 2 Item Finders for getting Trainers from my Discard pile. That's the same for Computer Search, but I usually get the card from my Deck to end the game in my favor. The Bills' and Professor Oaks' pretty much explain themselves, but I'll clarify. I use the Bills' when I don't have the cards I need, and I use the Professor Oaks' when my hand is getting low or it's the only card left in my hand. As for the Gust of Wind and Energy Removal/Super Energy Removal, they work together. I'll use the Gust of Wind to switch my opponents Pokemon with a high Retreat Cost on it. Or, I'll use Gust of Wind to switch a Pokemon with a lot of Energy on it and a high Retreat Cost from the Bench to Active, the I'll hammer it with tons of Energy Removals/Super Energy Removals. I could also switch a Benched Pokemon with a high HP and a Weakness to my Active Pokemon. Then I'll use a bunch of PlusPowers' to eliminate it from play.

That pretty much explains it, so I hope my Deck is good enough for you. :)

Eric Hufnagel
