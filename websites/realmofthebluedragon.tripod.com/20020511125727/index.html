Welcome to Realm of the Blue Dragon's Guild Homepage!

Realm of the Blue Dragon is an exclusive EverQuest Guild on the server Vazzelle.
We are the seekers of tranquility. We fight only the evil, both in our selves and in Norrath. Whether born High Elf, Dark Elf, Vah Shir, or Lizzy we look onto what is inside and not outside. We shall live and die by our beliefs. We shall protect our way of life and those we share it with. We shall revive a lost civilization, and seek it's revenge on the dark side of Norrath.
