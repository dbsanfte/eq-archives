Realm of the Blue Dragon

Welcome to Realm of the Blue Dragon's Guild Homepage!

Realm of the Blue Dragon is an exclusive EverQuest Guild on the server Vazzelle.

We are the seekers of tranquility. We fight only the evil, both in our selves and in Norrath. Whether born High Elf, Dark Elf, Vah Shir, or Iskar we look onto what is inside and not outside. We shall live and die by our beliefs. We shall protect our way of life and those we share it with. We shall revive a lost civilization, and seek it's revenge on the dark side of Norrath.

We shall love by the code
We shall hate by the code
We shall live by the code
We shall die by the code

Guild Code

We Realm of the Blue Dragon shall:

  • kill and destroy the evil which lays in Norrath.
  • protect our guild at all costs.
  • not ignore travelers in need our our help
