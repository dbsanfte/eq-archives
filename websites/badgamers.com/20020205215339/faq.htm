 I saw someone who was ranked out of spite here -- therefore this site is a farse.
 Well, we don't really buy into the logic pattern that because a few people were ranked out of spite therefore the entire BG engine is invalid. We realize that this does happen so be careful when you post not share data that would allow the person being rated to recognize you. Either way, you should have already learned the lesson that not everything you read is true! While we can't control the goofballs who post people here in an effort to just get ranked, we can give you all the information we possibly can so you can make intelligent decisions. So, when you read the posts, pay special attention to the ratings and the content so you can make the decision on your own as to whether the person truly sucks, or it's just a guild having some fun by harassing a member. You can also look at the ratings themselves to see if there is any unrealistic trends happening (for example, one person gets ranked 40 times with all 10's on the same day). We do make an effort internally to delete those characters that seem to have little or no supporting details to their high ratings, or exhibit strange trends with their ratings. Of course, we cannot catch them all so we rely on the intelligence of our readers to make the final decision on their own. Remember to resist the urge to rate all 10's out of emotion and try being more realistic. This will give the readers an accurate view of the person you are slamming -- as well as give your rating more credibility.

How do I sue you for slander?
 Class action lawsuits, slander, police department threats, national organizations, WASVC (Women Against Slandering Virtual Characters)...we've heard it all. Did you forget that these are "virtual" characters? Yep -- see, they are *not* real. So, suck up your feedback, take it like a grown man/woman and play your character differently.

Why do so many ratings have little or no comments?
We can't control the postings of comments. This ranking system is only as good as the data users put into it. All we can do is strongly encourage all of our users to include real scenarios and descriptions of behavior. The characters who truly suck will have loads of comments and examples of their areas for improvement. Other characters which have little documentation to support the ratings probably won't be so notorious. Comments like "He sucks" probably won't be as devastating as other, more descriptive comments. So the message is this: if you truly wish to slam a character and prevent others from experiencing the same crap you have, describe their actions (dates, times, places) in detail so there is no doubt who truly sucks. A top ranker with a hundred comments of real examples from players they have screwed and actions they have done is much more worthwhile than a top ranker with silly slanderous comments (although these are sometimes equally as funny).

Why do some character names disappear?
If a character has no ratings in any category as well as no supporting comments, it will be flagged for deletion after several days. If you accidentally create a character (on the wrong server for example) then you can update the categories to all zeroes and as long as no comments are made, it will be deleted. Also, early on we had several test accounts that had fictitious player names that we backed out after real data was coming in.

Why was this site created?
Those of us who are serious gamers really have no tolerance for suckage. We have met so many crappy players that we decided to do something about it. Furthermore, there seemed to be no repercussions for their actions. For example, there are some players who repetitively act like idiots and sure enough, the next day, there they are in the plane raids with another group causing havoc. Well, welcome to the first only feedback mechanism for MMPORGs. You want to play like an idiot? Fine, go nuts. Your ass is going to be rated here and good luck finding a group in the future. Eventually you will be so notorious you will either have to solo, switch servers or start another character.

What do I do if a guild got together and slammed my character for no reason?
Suck it up. We are of the opinion no one is really going to rally their guild to slam a character that hasn't done something stupid or malicious. Maybe you killed their leader, or maybe you just plain sucked and this resulted in a team member of theirs dying during a group outing. Either way -- be careful who you cross, and don't get in a group that is over your head. We want everyone in these games to start thinking about their actions and wondering if this will result in a BG (badgamers) rating. In the overall scope of this, one, two or even 10 ratings will probably not put you on the scoreboards. Those players who truly suck will rise through the scum to the top on their own accord. They will be receiving hundreds of ratings over time.

What do I do if I accidentally misspelled a name and want to delete it?
To control the rankings, deletions are not allowed. However, periodically the database will be wiped of characters with zero ratings and no comments. So, just zero out all the ratings and move on. Your misspelled character will end up being deleted in the next purge cycle.

How are the rankings computed?
First, every category score is totaled and stored. The numbers are displayed in (Total/Average) format. In each category, the total will be the total score of all the rankers combined. The average will be a number between 0 and 10, averaged across all rankers for that category. The total column is the summed total of all categories and the average in this column is the average across all categories (average for all rankers). This will also be a number between 0 and 10. Ranking is sorted in descending order by total score. One of the upcoming enhancements will allow a user to sort all the ranking lists by each category, and by the number of raters. So, for example, you can find the most dishonest player or the most ignorant one.

What do I do if I have a problem with the site?
If it is a fundamental problem you have with the site (e.g. you don't like the fact that your character was rated here), then we suggest you change your behavior in hopes the players on your server will lower your rating. If you would like to send us an e-mail complaining about your unfair rating, we will be happy to post it for everyone to see. And finally, if you truly have a technical problem, please shoot us an e-mail at support@badgamers.com.

If I offer money or plat, can my name be removed from the ranking lists?
No. But this site does run off of donations so if you like what we have here and you have an extra buck or two, toss it our way through Paypal! =)

I changed my ways but my rankings haven't changed. What do I do?
There is really nothing you can do. The only way for your ratings to be reduced is for the original raters to lower their scores. We suggest making a list of all the groups you sucked in, all the players you screwed over, and all the the other reasons why players hate you so much and trying to mend ways by grouping with them in the future and doing better. Spamming or harassing those characters to lower ratings is just going to get you on the ignore list and probably make your ratings worse. There are probably some players out there whose actions are so bad that their ratings will never be lowered. But, as they say, characters change - and stranger things have happened.

I registered, but now I want to change my e-mail address. What do I do?
 Periodically we will be wiping users who are not active (who have not logged in changed their passwords). We do not allow the changing of one's e-mail address since this is used during the registration process. If you want a different e-mail address, we suggest registering again.

How do you handle users who will log in 15 different times and rate the same player?
 There are some whose hatred will drive them to do this to up a particular character's rating. Heck, if you have the time to get 15 different e-mails then go nuts. Most people have better things to do with their time -- like play games. But to be fair, we do control the registration process by unique e-mail address as well as by IP address. If it appears that the same IP address is being used for multiple accounts that are rating the same players, these accounts (and all of their ratings) will be removed. Furthermore, these IP and e-mail addresses may be locked out from further access to the site.

What if someone rates themselves all zeroes just for the purpose of responding to comments?
We think players are intelligent enough to figure this kind of thing out. And, they are contributing to the ratings since we track the number of unique raters for a given character -- regardless of their rating. Since the comment area is mostly a chat area, we have realized that this kind of silliness is usually flushed out and results in the other raters just making their ratings worse (higher). In the end it all comes down to this: change your ways or enjoy your fame on BG. :)
