EQ FAQ

TRACKING
Here's a hint from one of our thumpers for your tracking ability:
1. Use the Newbie screen not the full screen
2. Put your tracking ability in slot 5 or 6
3. Position your pointer over the upper right corner of ability 5 or upper left corner of ability 6
4. Start your thumper, you will notice the cancel button is located in the same place as the ability.
This will allow you to thump your tracking skill!

LANGUAGES
In one weekend I managed to teach all my characters all the languages in EQ. Just set up a button to continuously group chat in whatever language you desire to learn. =)

FORAGING/CONJURATION
Raising conjuration for rangers with the firefly spell, you don't have to drop the globe it produces, it keeps raising your skill anyway. Perfect for the thumper...foraging is a good use for it also. ^_^
- Scott (Ranger Lvl 30)

FEIGN DEATH
At first I didn't think this skill could be thumped because you have to stand up -- but if you keep thumping it it will continue to make your character feign death (without the need for standing). Just pick a location away from monsters and you can max it in no time.
-Alejandro (Monk Lvl 27)

BIND WOUND
After a long battle (especially if I am soloing), I run to a zone border (preferably not right at the zone point but rather near a border) and bind myself back up to 75%. Make sure to have a bunch of bandages handy. This technique also saves the use of Mend for those nasty solo targets.
-Paul (Monk)

PICK LOCKS
OK you rogues are gonna love this one. Get your picklocks and go to Befallen. Get in front of the door and hide. Then, click on the pick locks so that they are on your mouse and position the mouse over the hinges of the door (this will avoid accidentally targeting a rat or player).
-Chris (Lvl 36 Rogue)

You can max your skill right at the Befallen door -- no need to go to Freeport.
- James (Rogue)

SENSE HEADING
Easiest skill to thump. Make a hotbutton, sit down (in a safe loc) and thump away. This one takes forever to max (many thousands of clicks) so do it while you sleep. =)
-Shawn (Lvl 18 Rogue)

SENSE TRAPS
Just sit down anywhere (preferably in a zone where there are no traps) and thump this baby.
-Chris (Lvl 36 Rogue)

INSTILL DOUBT / INTIMIDATE
 Target a player or corpse anywhere in the zone. Try to target dead players that have been there for a while =). Go to a border where it is safe and thump. I've tried this with corpses of creatures, but after they disappear you lose your target. Trick is to get a dead target that will be around for a while.
-Mark (Lvl 42 Rogue)

HIDE/SNEAK
Make a hot button, thump it until maxed.
-Joyce (Lvl 22 Rogue)

BEG
Pick a friend's pet! Just beg the heck out of him forever without the worry of getting aggroed.
-Elliott

BACKSTAB
If you are in a good group (one where the puller pulls to the same spot each time and has a great taunt), just thump the backstab constantly. Turn it off when its dead. Beats the heck out of constantly clicking and ensures the maximum amount of backstabs while fighting (no delay in between clicks). Just use the keyboard and to adjust your character and make sure a rooter is in the group.
-Mark (lvl 29 Rogue)

TAUNT
Just constantly taunt everything during battles. Start thumping once the creature is stationary -- especially when in a group with rogues! Turn off the Thumper and start moving with mouse during pulls. Use number keys to activate other skills that aren't being thumped (to avoid repositioning the mouse).
-John (Lvl 19 Warrior)
