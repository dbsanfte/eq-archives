 Hello and thank you for your interest!

We have developed this site in the hopes of improving the gaming experience of every gamer out there -- for any game. Our site is totally free and we have no current plans of charging for the services here.

Because of the costs of hosting, architecture, and bandwidth, we need your help to stay alive. We do our best to fund this site with our sponsors, but sometimes even that is not enough. Much like NPR and other public services, we survive on donations.

If you use the site and feel it is worthwhile, then please donate whatever you like. All donations will go towards supporting the operating costs of running this site, as well as the ongoing enhancements and maintenance. If, however, you cannot afford a donation, feel free to use this site and enjoy its content for free.

We do not lock you into any set amounts, nor to we force our members to pay any subscription fees. All we ask is that if you have the means, please contribute to our community (monthly if possible) based on what you feel these services are worth.

We accept payments via Paypal, the largest and most secure payment site on the web. Paypal has over 6 million subscribers and is safe and secure when it comes to credit card, check and money order transactions over the web.

Click the Paypal logo below and when you get to the payment screen, fill in the amount you wish to contribute. You can check off the no shipping address is necessary button, then click pay to confirm your transaction. You will receive an e-mail confirming your payment once the transaction is completed.
