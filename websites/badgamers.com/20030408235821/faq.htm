General Questions
What is a Thumper?
It is a custom made, electronic device designed specifically to meet the needs for the serious RPG player. Focused on character skill enhancement, our Thumper family of products allow you to increase your character's skills easily requiring no manual involvement. Top

Does a Thumper require a 9V battery?
Yes. All of our models require a 9V battery or a 9V battery eliminator (which allows you to plug your Thumper into a standard US power outlet in your house). Top

Can I buy a Thumper at a local store in my area?
Currently, Thumpers are only available online via our site or partner sites. Top

Does the Thumper have a mouse ball?
No. It is designed to be used in conjunction with a mouse. Top

If I order a Thumper today, how long will it take to ship?
Because of the high order volumes we are receiving, your Thumper will be shipped within 5-7 days (sometimes earlier). We are doing everything we can to expedite orders. Top

We have Paypal setup to manually approve unverified addresses. We understand that some customers may require alternate shipping addresses. After you click on the link to purchase your Thumper you can add in an unverified address. We will contact you via e-mail once payment is received if there are any problems. Top

How long does a Thumper last?
Our Thumpers are like any other device you would use for your computer. Treat it as you would any other peripheral and it will last accordingly. Every month we take one of our Thumpers and power it via a 9V power supply. We then turn them on forever. Our earliest Thumpers have been thumping nonstop since October 7th, 2000 (24 hours a day and still going). Top

How long does a battery last?
Make sure to use a new 9-volt battery. A new battery should last anywhere between 18-27 hours of use (this varies slightly by model). You can now purchase a 9V battery eliminator to run your Thumper indefinitely. Top

What does the green LED denote?
It denotes switch position (on is lit and off is unlit), not the battery condition. Top

No. GM Associates has a strict policy about confidentiality. Your name, address, shipping information, etc. will never be given out. You as a customer have the right to privacy and we intend to keep it that way. Feel free to anonymously use your Thumper in any way you like. Please see our privacy statement for more details. Top

Does the Thumper click at a constant rate?
No. Every Thumper is different. Randomly induced mouse clicks ensure that your Thumper is totally unique. And because of this, your Thumper is different each time you use it. We have built in a random timer to all of our models that introduces variations in the click cycles of all of our units. Top



Installation and Setup Questions
After I receive my Thumper, how do I install it?
First make sure to shut your computer off, then plug the Thumper into the USB or PS/2 port. When Windows is loaded, it will either sense new hardware or do nothing if you already have the correct driver loaded. If you are prompted to load a driver for the Thumper, pick a standard generic two button mouse for the V2 and V2 VT models. For the KB, pick a standard keyboard. If no prompt is given then you already have the correct drivers loaded to use the Thumper. Launch your MMPORG game and try it out! Top

What is the best way to test that my Thumper is working correctly?
 After installing your Thumper in Windows, load up either Notepad or Word for Windows. If you are using the KB, you will see a string of text appear once you turn it on. If you are using the V2, VT or Camelot version, place the mouse cursor over the File menu. You should see the File menu appear and disappear periodically. Remember that MMPORGs are very resource intensive so plugging in your Thumper while in game may cause a delay before it works correctly. It is better to install your Thumper while in Windows, then launch your game. Top

How do I avoid bending over to switch out my mouse?
You can now purchase the Thumper V2 ™ in USB or PS/2. We recommend using USB if your current mouse is PS/2. If you currently use a USB mouse and have an open USB slot, USB is the way to go. And, finally, if you favorite mouse is USB and you have no open slots, you can use PS/2. We also offer PS/2 extension cables which will make it easier when switching the mouse and the Thumper V2 ™ PS/2. Top

Can you still use your normal mouse?
Yes, you should be able to pick the appropriate Thumper V2 ™ for your configuration (USB or PS/2). This will allow you to use your Thumper V2 ™ with your mouse. Top

Does the Thumper go between the mouse and the PS/2 port?
No. The Thumper is a separate device than your current mouse. Both the Thumper V2™ and the Thumper V2 (VT)™ can be plugged in at the same time with your mouse. For the Thumper V2 (KB)™ model, the Thumper can operate in conjunction with any standard PS/2 or AT style keyboard and can only operate on the USB port. Top

If I am using a PS/2 mouse with my PS/2 Thumper, Will my PS/2 mouse stop working after I switch back and forth? Will I have to reboot my computer?
You can swap out your mouse with the Thumper PS/2 models then swap back to your mouse without losing navigation capabilities. We recommend the use of a USB mouse with your Thumper PS/2 models, but many users simply swap their Thumper in and out with their regular mouse. Windows may take several moments to resense your mouse, then your mouse should continue to function properly. Top



Questions Concerning the Use of Your Thumper
What if the Thumper works in Windows but doesn't work in-game?
Some users have experienced this problem when running Logitech's mouseware program. If you are running this, please shut it down before going into the game. Top

With the latest patch, if you use a Thumper will a GM receive a message causing your account to be canceled?
Well, we actually hear this with every patch. But don't worry, that is why we built in a randomized timer into all of our units. All of our products are designed to avoid eliciting any electronic signature that can be tracked -- in fact, each unit is different. Furthermore, the only signature our products do send out is either that of a standard, generic mouse (for the original V2 and V2 VT models) or a standard generic keyboard (for KB models). Even if an online game scanned for devices like this one, all it would see is a mouse or a keyboard. This means that your electronic signature will be exactly the same as someone else who is using a mouse or keyboard to play the game. You can thump without the worry of being discovered. Top

Why does my skill/spell button pop off the screen?
Some online games allow you to use hotbuttons that can be moved around on the screen. It is possible that at some random times this button may be lifted off the screen (instead of activated). This is a normal, rare occurrence (for the original V2 and V2 VT models only) and your Thumper will thump it back on the screen on its next click. This effect, combined with the question below, sometimes leads users to believe their Thumper is not working correctly. Top

While I thump, why does it look like nothing is happening.
Many of the online games give repetitive messages when you are practicing. Once these messages fill up your chat window, it will appear as if nothing is happening (especially in quiet areas where there are few characters). Just have your character say something so it appears in the chat window, then you will see the repetitive messages scroll it off of the screen. You can always put your ear close to the Thumper and listen for a tiny pulsing noise to make sure it is thumping. Make sure the green LED light is lit to make sure your Thumper is turned on. And don't forget to use a new battery. Top

What if the Thumper starts to act strangely?
Replace the battery. If you are using a 9V battery eliminator, unplug the mouse then plug it back in so that Windows can resense the device. Top

Why did my Thumper stop working after I opened it?
Opening of the Thumper may cause it to malfunction since components and wires that are attached on the inside of the unit may be permanently damaged. Opening of the unit may also cause damage to the main circuit board. GM Associates does not support units that have been opened or tampered with in any way. Please do not open your Thumper. If you have any questions, please e-mail us. Top



Thumper V2™ Specific Questions
 How does the Thumper V2 ™ work?
The Thumper V2™ sends randomly timed left mouse clicks to your mouse port (PS/2 or USB). This means that it is the closest thing to simulating real life mouse clicks -- just as if you were sitting at your desk clicking the mouse mindlessly yourself! So, go play with your kids, go read a book, go jog, or whatever you want while we take the boring aspects out of the game for you. It operates on a standard 9V battery and plugs into any PS/2 or USB port. Top



Thumper V2 (VT)™ Specific Questions
How does the Thumper V2 (VT)™ work?
The Thumper V2 (VT)™ sends randomly timed left mouse clicks to your mouse port (PS/2 or USB). It also comes with a controllable timer that allows you to set the range in between clicks anywhere from 1 to 7 seconds (approximately). The VT is available in both USB and PS/2 models. Top



Thumper V2 (KB)™ Specific Questions
How does the Thumper V2 (KB)™ work?
The Thumper V2 (KB)™ sends randomly timed key strokes (hence it is USB only). The KB sends approximately one lowercase keystroke per second and enables full mouse control while thumping (you do not need to position the mouse anywhere on the screen to thump). The actual keystroke used is clearly labeled on the side of each KB unit. Top

How do I know what character the KB will transmit?
On the side of each KB is a label which clearly identifies the key it will transmit. Map this key to a hotbutton and you're ready to thump with full control of your mouse. Top

What is the main difference between the KB and the other units you offer?
The main difference is the ability to have complete mouse control while you thump. In the original V2 and VT models, users must position their mouse over the skill button (or macro button) to thump. With the KB, you no longer need to position your mouse to thump a skill. Top

When I turn on my KB, why do my transparent polygons go on and off repetitively?
Some units may be mapped to the ` key, which is defaulted in EQ to turn polygons on and off. You must remap your Thumper's key to one of the user defined keys (1-6).Top



Thumper V2 (VT Camelot)™ Specific Questions
Does the Thumper V2 (VT Camelot)™ remember mouse movements?
The VT Camelot is simply a VT with a longer delay cycle. It is specifically designed for DAoC users and will not work with EQ trade skills. Top

Why can't I just return to my desktop and all my trade products are completed?
Remember that like all the Thumper models, the Camelot Thumper is simply a mouse that auto clicks. Trade skills in DAoC are similar in fashion to those in EQ, except they do not force a mouse movement after an item is made. This means that you can repetitively click a trade skill and fill your inventory with the end result. Most of DAoC's trade skills require the fashioning of several unfinished goods (blade, hilt, etc.). Once all the unfinished goods are in inventory, you can then make the final piece of armor (or weapon). Keep in mind is that DAoC uses an increasing time scale when practicing trade craft. In other words, the easy equipment can be made quickly, but the harder the equipment is to make the longer it will take. Master crafters can experience delays in upwards of 5 minutes just to fashion a piece of equipment. And, any clicking during this delay will cancel the creation of the item. So, as your character gets better in their associated trade skill, you will need to be well aware of the time it takes to make a product so that you can set the delay cycle on your Thumper appropriately. It is important to note that the Camelot Thumper will require more user intervention than our other EQ models. This means that the Camelot Thumper will save you time (especially if you are a serious tradesperson) but you will have to perform some trial and error to accomplish the right mix of products and timing for a successful use of our product. Users should *not* expect to simply plug in the Camelot, go to sleep, and wake up as a master tradesperson (unlike EQ where users can go to sleep and wake up as a master lock picker). Most of our users check back with their characters every 30-90 minutes or so to make adjustments and fine tune their trade practicing techniques. Top

I received my Thumper, it was recognized by Windows, but why doesn't it work?
When you first receive your Thumper, make sure the variable timer is turned completely counter clockwise (this is the minimum delay setting). When you first plug in your Thumper, the first few thumps will be longer than usual. On the maximum delay setting, the first thump (when first plugged in) may take upwards of 8 minutes, giving you the idea that your Thumper is not working. Starting the Thumper at the minimum delay will avoid this. Let the Thumper click a few times and then you're ready to go. Due to the nature of electronics, the first few thumps will always spike a little higher on the delay cycle. After the first few thumps, your Thumper V2 VT (Camelot) will settle down to the 40 - 350 second delay range. Remember, because of the built in randomizer in all our units, the delay in between thumps will never be exact -- it will vary plus or minus several seconds. On higher delay settings, this variation will grow to approximately plus or minus 8-12 seconds. Keep this in mind as you try to discover the correct range for your character depending on their level, trade skill, etc. Try to err on the high side of the delay so that you do not cancel your trade skills by clicking on them again before they are finished. Top

Why does my Thumper not make any soft pulsing noises like the other EQ models?
Unlike our previous models, the Camelot is virtually silent in its operation. This means that putting your ear up to the box is not an accurate method of determining if your Thumper is working correctly. A better method is to open Notepad within Windows and move the mouse over the File drop down menu. Then turn on the unit and watch to make sure the file menu is appearing and disappearing. Don't forget to use a new battery (if you do not have a 9V battery eliminator) and remember to turn the variable timer completely counter clockwise. Top

Why do my batteries not last that long?
The Camelot operates on a much slower delay cycle than our other units. This is due mainly because in DAoC, as you progress in levels the time it takes to actually make an item extends. At higher skill levels, users usually leave their thumpers plugged in for very long periods of time. Because some skills require delays in upwards of five minutes, this means our thumpers are only achieving 10 or 12 clicks per hour. So, thumpers stay on longer, skills increase slower, and batteries are simply sucked dry. If you are going to use the Camelot seriously, we recommend you buy the 9V battery eliminator. For those users who have already purchased their units without the 9V battery eliminator, simply send us a support e-mail and we will waive the shipping charge for your battery eliminator. Top



Thumper V2 (VT Camelot XLT)™ Specific Questions
Does the Thumper V2 (VT Camelot XLT)™ work with all trade skills in DAoC?
Yes, but remember the XLT was specifically designed for the extremely long time cycles required for the new trade skills in DAoC. Since the minimum delay cycle for the XLT is 7 minutes, this means practicing the simpler trade skills will take a long time. Users are recommended to pursue the VT Camelot to max their trade skills first, then utilize the XLT for expert level trade skills. Top
