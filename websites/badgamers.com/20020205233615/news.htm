7-11-01
SUMMARY OF FIXES/ENHANCEMENTS
- There was a problem that was fixed in the MyBadgamers area. Most browsers were having trouble with the multiple list boxes when more than 10 players were rated. This has now been fixed.

A note to our users:
We have enabled the comment posting area with some new features. We took a quick run through the multiple account registrations and comments and removed many multiple accounts (so those character's ratings are now lower). We also banned a few explicit posters, permanently disabling them from the site. For those accounts that have received warnings, if the behavior continues your account will be banned as well.

If you feel there is content that needs to be removed, please e-mail us at support@badgamers.com. Please be sure to include the time and date of the post, character name, as well as the text in question. We will review the postings for explicit content and remove it if applicable. We will *not* remove content that users simply do not like or content that users feel is false. If you feel the content is false, post about it. We will only be removing inappropriate content.

We are continuing to expand the comment area over the next few weeks to bring you a wide variety of rating mechanisms. The MyBadgamers panel will also be expanding to allow you a much more sophisticated control panel with which to rate your players.

As we add more characteristics to track players on, it should become readily apparent whose posts are accurate (e.g. if someone posts about a character but doesn't know their class/race odds are it is false or a simple flame from some bored 13 year old). We will be adding these kinds of categories to post on shortly.

Also, please remember that the characters on these lists are not real, and many players are role playing. This means that any comments about a character should not be extracted to relate in any way to the actual reality that we all live in.

And finally, thank you to all who have been with us through our growing pains. We are learning a lot about running a site in the MMPORG community and we are doing our best to achieve our original vision for this site. We will continue to do everything we can to improve this engine. If you have any comments or suggestions, please drop us an e-mail!
