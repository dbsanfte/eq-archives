18th April - Dragon Raid

Hi there groovers,
Its been bounced around for awhile that we try our hand at a dragon raid as the first of our major guild accomplishments. A few of our high level players are nearer the level 53 cutoff mark and they obviously would like the chance the chance to bump off an uberwyrm before the opportunity passes. I propose that we begin plans to have a Lady Vox raid in 2 weeks time (the weekend of the 28th & 29th of April) and invite our friends from The Raenore to help us in our quest. I have not been involved with a dragon raid before but plan to do as much research and planning as I can to get it to happen. Anyone else with knowledge of dragon raiding please let yourself be known so we can make sure that we do the best job possible. From what I know already we will need at least 3 full groups of mid 40s and higher players and the more tanks the better as the dragons are highly magic resistant. You will also need as much magic and cold resistance gear as you can get to hinder Vox's attacks. I will post a link to the Vox lair when I find a suitable one. Please post your interest here and if you have any friends outside the guild who are of suitable level and preferably with experience in this please invite them along. Also understand that donking a dragon on the head with a sword is not the safest pasttime so expect casualties. With the gracious help of our clerics we can provide resses but this raid comes with no safety guarantees. Having said that I know ten people offhand that would give up a death to get a shot at her Ladyship. This is going to be exciting. Well that's all for now. Lets get behind this and make our first step towards ubermob slaying.

Cheers,

Fildaren

Announcing the great Treasure Hunt. On the 20th January Dorfl and Odalian will be hosting the first of the Great Treasure Hunts.  This event will be open to all classes and levels within HoF.  Everyone will meet at the Wizard Spires in Greater Faydark at 7:30pm NZ for a start half an hour later.  Your local time will be  5:30pm Sydney, 6:30am GMT, 10:30pm Friday PST.
