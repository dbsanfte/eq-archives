Heart of Fenris
An EverQuest Guild of the Erollisi Marr Server








 
Our aim is to keep the Heart of Fenris beating to help keep the world of Norath alive. Our secondary objective is to help any new players who wish to succeed in the world of Norath.

History

The Guild was originally formed by Fornagath in Mid 1999 under the name of Heart of Fenris.

Since then the old Heart of Fenris Guild was deleted and the New and improved Hearts of Fenris was born.

Recently, the guild was wiped out by VI. Since then it has been re-created as Heart of Fenris.

About Fenris.

Fenris is a Huge Wolf that the Gods could not tame. So, one day in an effort to keep Fenris under control the God of Honor chained up Fenris to guard the great serpents, and when Fenris realized that he had been chained up he bit off the hand of the God of Honor. Ever since he has been guarding the great serpents and when Fenris howls it will mark the end of the world.
