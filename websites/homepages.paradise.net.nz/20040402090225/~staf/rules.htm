Heart of Fenris
An EverQuest Guild of the Erollisi Marr Server








 

HOF GENERAL RULES

1. Powerleveling and twinking is accepted within the guild but it is not a right, it is a privilege. Higher level players will happily give their time to help lower levels but it is understood that the higher levels must be free to do so and not be expected to drop whatever they are doing to come help. The guild is about introducing lower levels and assisting them in developing their characters but they must hold realistic expectations on this. Power leveling WILL BE OFFERED NOT DEMANDED.

2. When selling items members are expected to offer the item within the guild first at a small discount. If they have no buyers then feel free to sell it outside the guild. Giving away items for free to help other members is not expected but is encouraged.

3. Members should not use the /anon command but rather use /roleplay if they wish to hide their location or class. This way members always know who is on and it helps promote the guild name.

4. We will accept evil races/classes into the guild and they are free to roleplay their characters as long as they do not break any EQ player rules such as training or KSing.

5. It is generally accepted that HoF holds with all EQ player rules and anyone breaking with these are breaking HoF rules.

6. As far as discipline anyone breaking the rules should be reported to a guild officer. They will be spoken to and if they continue to break the rules or are someone found unsuitable for the guild they will be asked to leave.

7. Members should be FAIR and FRIENDLY in all their dealings in Norrath.

8. Read and participate on the web page forums as regularly as you can. Found at homepages.paradise.net.nz/~staf.

NOTE: We do not seek to have an overly regimented guild but it was thought that some groundrules must be set to make it clear to members as to what is expected.
