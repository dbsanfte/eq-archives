Joining MoD:

- Get to know the Members of our guild. Group with them if possible. You will need to have a Member sponsor you in order to be considered for the guild.

- Post your intentions on our Public forums and join in on our discussions.

- Griefers need not apply. That type of behavior won't be accepted in the guild, we are all here to have fun and enjoy ourselves.

- Once we get to know you, the current members and officers will discuss whether you are MoD material and we will make a decision about admittance.

Once you are a member:

The main thing once you become a member of MoD is to have a good time. Many of our members have been with us for about 3 years now. We are very laid back and do not impose heavy expectations on our members. The guidelines are as follows:

- HAVE FUN!

- Conduct yourself in an appropriate manner to others in the game, but don't let people walk on you. We will not tolerate you intentionally, causing trouble for others in our Realm.

- New members will start at PFC rank. You will have access to guildchat, but will not have emblem wearing ability. This is the only rank that does not allow wearing the emblem and most people will be promoted from this rank in a very short period of time.

- All members will pay guild dues. These dues are scaled per level. It is not a great deal of cash but the guild bank is a necessary part of the guild. We have purchased our emblem with these dues and will in the future use them for making Siege Engines, as well as, potentially funding our trades people.

- and Lastly........Have some more fun!!
