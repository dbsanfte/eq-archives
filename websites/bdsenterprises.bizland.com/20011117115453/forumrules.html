The KoV Tavern was founded around its Forums. They are a great place for both knowledge transfer and social interaction. Friendships have been born here and memories forged that some of us will never forget. We pride ourselves in the unique community we have all built, and because of that we have become very protective of it. In hopes of preserving what some call a "second home", we ask you to follow a few simple rules.

General rules:

  • Please post in the appropriate forum. Don't be upset if we move your post to a more approriate forum.
  • Avoid personal attacks. Debate opinions, not the people behind them.
  • Do not post links to pornographic or other inapropriate sites.
  • Do not use the Tavern forums for advertising purposes.

Signature rules:

  • One of the great things about the KoV Tavern is all the GREAT sigs. But with all the sigs, threads can get bogged down, take forever to load and look really cluttered. We ask that you adhere to these guidelines:
    • Sig MUST be under 300kb in size - this includes Flash, animated gifs, etc.
    • Images that distort the format of the forums (mainly personalicons) are not allowed.
    • Only post your custom signature ONCE per thread. Simply uncheck the "Include custom signature" box when posting more than once in a thread.
    • Don't post "test sigs", use the "Preview" checkbox to see how it looks.
