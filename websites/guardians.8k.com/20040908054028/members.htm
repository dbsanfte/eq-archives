The High Council
  The Council is comprised of members selected by the GM to represent the good of the guild in our closed forums. It takes a majority vote of online officers to approve new members. All other High Council deliberations and conferencing will take place in a private forum, never guildsay. See the Rank Structure page for more information on High Council duties, roles, responsibilities and conduct.

The Masters of the Arts
Any Apprentice who has attained the level of 25 may apply for this rank. After an intention is stated to become a Master,  the high council will evaluate your efforts as an apprentice and vote on your promotion. An intention to become a Master may be announced Via the message boards. Please see the Rank System page for more information on the duties, responsibilities, and conduct of a Master of the Arts.
