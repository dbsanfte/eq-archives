OR vs MB - 4/8/2005 9:12:35 AM

Friday April 8th Kinni led OR's first raid against MB, a resounding success!  After a little hangup on the south side with some errant spiders all four crowd control groups got in place and started killing.  After that it was just a few minutes before MB himself fell.  Total raid time of about two hours from start to finish.  Great job everyone!

The crowd control groups mezzed and memblurred their spiders so everyone could run hail the gnome for their Tactics flag, and then there was only loot to distribute.  The first distribution via dkp worked out well, Rakasaz and Fireandwater were our two winners.  The actual looting was a bit more complicated since the spiders were at this point zerging MB's room where they explode for a 4k AE.  Only one or two deaths later, though, and everyone had their flag, their loot, and quite enough of PoI factory ;) .
