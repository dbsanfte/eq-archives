Drinal and Tarew Marr to Merge - 3/30/2005 2:19:24 PM

On April 14th the two servers Drinal and Tarew Marr will merge (announced on EQ Live Forums under Town Crier).  Here's a short synopsis of the long post:

Merging Servers

4/14 Tarew Marr merges with Drinal and will be listed as Drinal.

If you have a character below level 10 that has not been logged on in over six months and that you want to keep, log him/her on before April 14th.

If you have characters on both Drinal and Tarew Marr, clean out your shared bank (platinum too).

If you have more than eight characters in total on Drinal and Tarew Marr, choose the ones you want to keep least and delete them before April 14th.

Make sure that on April 13th when you log out all of your corpses are cleared from the world.

If your name is taken on Tarew Marr, the character with the most played time will keep the "true" name and the other character will have an X appended on the end.  If you want to know whether you'll be keeping your name for sure, create a new character on TM with your name to see if it's taken.

The full post is copied on our forums.
 

Grummus Falls! - 3/18/2005 10:20:42 PM
