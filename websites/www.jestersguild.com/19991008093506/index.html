We are the Jesters of the EverQuest Tarew Marr server.
 

If you happen by a group of people fishing in Crushbone,
If you saunter by someone beating an orc to death with his fishing pole,
If you have ever seen someone on his last sliver of life, but laughing while unconcious,
You have just met a Jester.
The Jesters were founded to spread Goodwill, Honor and Valor throughout the lands. The Jester protects and looks out for all others of a kind and virtuous nature. The jester entertains and lifts the spirits of those who have nowhere to turn. The jester is everyone's friend, and a friend in need is a friend indeed to the jester. The jester knows joy and the jester also knows sadness and, as such, is an active adversary against all evil and those that would spread evil. If you feel the calling of the Jester and have a heart that is full of good cheer, please join us in our journeys!

We also do cheap parlor tricks and...

 
WE ARE NOT THE SYNDICATE!!
 

We're pretty proud of that fact...jhehe...
