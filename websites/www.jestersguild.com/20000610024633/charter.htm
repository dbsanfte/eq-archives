The Jesters Guild Charter

 

 

The Jesters Guild is a guild of Free spirited individuals of Good alignment

who are dedicated to adventure and fun.

We are a potent fighting force against those who walk the dark path, but we are also pledged to merriment.

NO GUILD knows how to have a good time like the jesters...period.

The Jesters run together, fight together, look after each other and generally are always there to help an adventurer in need.

We are loyal, honorable and amusing individuals who enjoy the company of others of similar mind.

But though we are pranksters we are driven to further the cause of Light and Truth .
