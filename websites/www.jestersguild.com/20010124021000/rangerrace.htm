Race Selection for your Ranger

by Namil Jackrabbit

 

 

Which race should you pick for a Ranger?

Well, Each race has its advantages when you choose to play a Ranger.

As you know, you can be a Human, a Half-Elf or a Wood-Elf (thats me).

Check these base stats....

 

Class STR STA AGI DEX WIS INT CHR +ADD PSA TOT AVG
Human 80 85 85 75 80 75 75 20 87.5 575 82.14
Half-Elf 75 80 100 85 65 75 75 20 85 575 82.14
Wood Elf 70 75 105 80 85 75 75 20 88.75 585 83.57

 

Lets Go down the list and then we'll do a comparison of the races.

Strength determines how much weight you can carry on your person. a strength of 80 means you can carry 80 pounds after which you will be encumbered. Strength also effects how much damage you will inflict with a weapon and higher strength increases your chance to hit an enemy. Strength can be increased with spells or with magic items.

Stamina effects how many Hit points are granted to you every level. The higher the Stamnina the bigger the hit point bonus. Stamina is the yellow bar beneath your Mana bar (the blue bar). You might notice in a fight that the stamina bar goes down a bit (especially in a long fight while dual wielding). It also goes down when your swimming or jumping or even while starving. The higher the stamina the more constitution you have. Some people have also suggested that there is a correlation between high stamina and faster meditating. Stamina can be increased with spells and items.

Agility- I am an agility freak. and here's why. Agility effects your ability to make attacks from MOBS miss. In essense you are so agile that you can avoid blows from monsters. Not only that, but agility will add some AC to your stats. This is a great attribute as even if you have a low AC the ability to not be hit and sustain damage is key in a battle situation. Agility can be increased with spells and items.

Dexterity is your coordination hand to eye. It directly effects your probability to hit. The higher your dexterity the better chance you have of making contact with that MOB. Also there are certain weapons that have what's called a "PROC", that is a special effect that goes off as the weapon is used. This could be an effect of a damage spell or an effect of Beserker strength. High Dexterity increases the chance of that weapons "PROC" going off while in combat. Dexterity can be increased by Spells and by items.

Wisdom is key when it comes to casting spells. Because Rangers are 25% Druid, our mana is based on how much wisdom we have. The higher the wisdom the more mana we have to cast spells with. Pretty cut and dry there. Wisdom also comes into play when it comes to Trade skills. Trade skills, be it baking or smithing or FLETCHING are skills that you choose to work on and become masters at. High Wisdom effects the success of creation of trade skill items. Wisdom can be increased by spells and items.

Intelligence effects how fast we can learn (and raise) Trade skills or other abilities like learning different languages. The higher the intelligence, the faster trade skills will rise. Other casting classes rely on Intelligence to determine Mana for spells like we rely on Wisdom. Intelligence can be increased with items.

Charisma is how attractive and charming you are perceived as. This directly effects Merchant prices when buying and selling. A character with low charisma will not be able to sell for as much as a character with a high charisma and vice versa. Charisma also effects the ability by certain classes to charm MOBS (and even players in PVP).Charisma can be raised by spells and items.

Now with an understanding of all the attributes you can see that Humans, Half Elfs and Wood elves differ in certain areas. You have to determine whats more important to you. That said, heres some extra information about each Race.

Humans Get the ability to Level FASTER then any other race. If your a human you will get slightly more experience in combat or quests then other races.This is important because as a Hybrid class (Rangers, Shadowknights, Paladins) we get an experience penalty.

Humans however are BLIND as a bat at night. The Elven Races get Infravision (and Ultravision if your a Dark elf) which is the ability to see much better at night. Humans, Barbarians and Euradites do not. You can combat this by carrying a Greater Lightstone or a fire beetle eye to light the way. * Rangers get the spell Dance of the fireflies at level 22 which allows you to summon a lightstone. Rangers also get the spell Eyes of the cat at level 30 which allows you to cast infravision on yourself (useful to humans only and may not be cast on others).

Wood Elves get the skills HIDE and SNEAK at level one. These are handy skills to have. Hide allows you to while standing still effectively render yourself invisible while SNEAK allows you to slowly sneak past a Hostile without being seen. Humans and Half Elves dont get these skills untill much later.

Wood Elves start out in Kelethin, The city in the trees located on the continent of Feydwer. Humans may start in either of the two human cities of Freeport or Qeynos located on the east and west coasts of Antonica. Half -Elves being of both Human and Wood Elf Stock may start their careers in ANY of the above cities mentioned.

There are two fully functional Ranger guilds. One being located in Kelethin and the other located outside Qeynos in Surefall Glade, so its not recomended to start as a Ranger in Freeport. There is a third Ranger Guild Outpost in Kithicor Forrest ( 3 zones away from Freeport) but i am not sure if you can do the first quest for the tunic there although you can train skills.

It should be noted that Half-Elves being a half breed (human and Wood elf) are treated with some disdain by certain NPC's around Norrath.

When you create your character you start with that races base stats and 20 points that you can use to increase any attribute(s) that you wish. Remember too that all Stats can be raised ingame by spells or magical items...either stat enhancing Armour or weapons or Jewelry. So the differences in attributes in the races isnt all that dissimilar when it comes right down to it.

I have raised my Strength in game by 50 points from when i started at Level one. The same goes for my other stats. If you feel weak in one stat attribute..know that you can raise it over time with items. Plus there are many other classes out there that will cast their Stat enhancing buffs on you that will raise certain attributes for a short period of time while you group with them.

As with any Race/Class Combination, choose the one that you can relate to best. These characters are more then just pixels on a screen. Over time they tend to become an extension of yourself.

Choose wisely and safe journies!
