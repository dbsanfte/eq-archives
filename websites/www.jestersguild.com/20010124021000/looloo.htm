Looloo - Tale of a Young Ranger

By

Looloo Dia'Anna

Norrath is a turbulent changing world filled with animals, monsters and beings of many races. And the bards shall someday sing of a young elfin girl who would try to change hearts of stone and hatred to love and light. Her name is Looloo and this is her tale.

Looloo is now a wood elf ranger in her 20th season. She has traveled far in Norrath and still has seen so little of her world. Born in Kelethin, Looloo was orphaned as a child when a band of evil orcs killed her parents. Tunare the Mother watched over Looloo and protected her as she grew. Looloo was given the gifts of foraging for food and tracking for danger and was completely at home in the forest. Kindly strangers often helped and fed and clothed the poor young girl, as she needed. Her cheerful, sweet nature earned her many strong friends and her laughter was a delight to the citizens of Kelethin that knew her.

Looloo was befriended by a traveling band of Jesters one-day and they enjoyed her sunny disposition and comical antics. They invited her to join their guild and offered her protection and guidance under their leader, Namil. Looloo and the Jesters had many adventures and successful hunting raids and cleansed Norrath of quite a few evil monsters. The Jesters taught Looloo how to fight and how to pick battles. They always fought for good causes and shunned evil races. Looloo learned fast and listened well to her friends and teachers, thriving under their alliance.

But one day when she was very young, as she traveled through West Commonlands, a vile undead creature attacked Looloo. She fought bravely, but was losing the battle and was about to die as the ghoul shrieked its hideous laugh. From the East came a young elf that healed her as she fought and helped her attack the fiend. When she had recovered her senses and stood to thank the person for their help, she was stunned to see he was a dark elf. She had never seen a dark elf close to her before and had always been told that she was hated by dark elves and was to return their dislike. He asked after her health, cared for her and gave her spells to strengthen her body. Looloo was very confused and pondered why a dark elf would risk his life to save hers. She thanked him politely but was very wary because of what she’d been told of his race. He giggled at her, introduced himself and bowed deeply to the frightened girl. He was a cleric; he said and healed when he was needed. After he made sure Looloo was healthy again, he waved and vanished around the corner of the hill as quickly as he had arrived.

Looloo traveled back to Rivervale and decided to read from the teachings of Tunare to see if she could explain to herself and others how she came to be saved by an “evil”dark elf. As she read, she suddenly understood how powerful The Mother is, that she could influence the heart of a dark elf to save one of her children. “We are all Tunare’s children. “ she read, ‘”The world of Norrath gave birth to Tunare. From Tunare the Great Mother all life has come. Worship and protect the land and protect nature in all its forms. Thus you will pay respect to the Mother and the Mother will care and provide for thee.” It was now very clear to Looloo, but she was unsure her guild would understand her new “friend.”

She often heard from her friend though he was always far away. Looloo feared bringing him close to her Guild friends would cause them distress and she was sure the Elfin Counsel would not approve. Looloo studied more as she grew up and became a voracious reader of history. She knew she was searching for a way to explain her actions. Looloo felt it was only a matter of time until the Elders knew of her unusual friendship. She was a not friend with all the evil races, but her kind heart saw good in everyone. Looloo hoped in some small way, by showing the evil ones the ways of Tunare, that Tunare would change their hearts to goodness. Looloo danced whenever she saw the Mother influence a dark race to kill the most hated Undead foes. She knew there was hope for the ones that were not lost to Innoruuk, The Prince of Hatred.

Looloo admired her Guild Master Namil and sought to be as powerful and honorable as he was. Namil delighted in another Guild Mistress, a powerful woman named Vyrinia who had befriended the Jesters and had sanctioned their name. Vyrinia would often visit Namil and he would share their talks around the campfires with the Jesters. One fateful day, Vyrinia herself was claimed by Innoruuk and transformed into a Dark Elf. Namil mourned her and vowed that the Jesters would save her from this fate. Namil believed as did Looloo, that the real Vyrinia was lost somewhere and within her was goodness.

Deep within a forgotten parchment, Looloo believed she had found an answer for her tolerance and a hope for Vyrinia and those like her trapped in dark elf form. Carefully she unrolled the scroll and read:

Towards the beginning of the High Elf nation, Felwithe was created, the High Elf capital. This Great City was constructed at the same time the Lord of Hate, Innoruuk arrived on Norrath. In a jealous rage, He captured the first King and Queen of the High Elves, took them to His realm, now the dungeon everyone knows As "Befallen" and tortured them for 300 years. The result of this realm of pain and anger From the Lord of Hate, were the first two Dark Elves. Yea verily, Dark Elves, our greatest Enemies, have the High Elves as forefathers. The King and Queen had not only been warped physically, but obviously mentally as well. These two were not only the first King and Queen of Felwithe, but also the first King and Queen of Neriak and the Dark Elf nation. Since we still hear of them, we assume they are still alive in zombie or skeletal form...

Looloo thought that perhaps together if an Elf worked with a Dark Elf and freed the King and Queen from their eternal torment in Befallen, that the Dark Elves would be freed of Innoruuk’s power. She tried this with her Dark Elfin friend, but both of them perished in the attempt. Perhaps it will take a stronger Elf such as Namil and the tormented Vyrinia to bring light into the dungeons of Befallen. The King and Queen of the High Elves may someday be freed and restored to their former goodness. Only the Gods know what will happen and sages will record it. May Tunare bless and keep you safe as you travel Norrath.
