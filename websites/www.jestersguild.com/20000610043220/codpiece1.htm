The Jesters Codpiece

By

Dudail Bellavoce

The sun had sets on yet another beautiful day in Norrath. The city of Freeport was a buzz with the evening traffic of people seeking entertainment after the hard day's toil. A handsome Half elf detached from the crowd and headed for his favorite watering hole, The Jesters Codpiece.

He strolled across the street and up the steps to the inn and through the weathered door. He paused to let his eyes adjust to the light level inside and to survey the crowd. The inn was set up like many others he had visited in his travels. The bar stood along the west wall occupying the entire wall but for a space to access the kitchen beyond. The whole of the North wall was a huge fireplace that doubled as heat source and cooking spit. The east wall was the highlight of the inn. Along it at even intervals were three rare glass windows. It is rumored that the owner and proprietor was once an adventurer and had done some favors for a band of Gnomes. They are said to be the ones that gifted him the glass when he built the Inn. Tables and benches are scattered throughout the room with the ones by the windows being the favored ones. The inn was full with the evening rush. Workmen, nobles and the odd adventurer shared tables and drink.

"Ahhh it's good to be home" Dudail sighed.

From beside the door a large shape appeared from the shadows and moved towards the new arrival. A hand the size of Dudail's head reached for his shoulder and as it is about to land, the shoulder gone.

"You have to be quieter, faster than that Emil hehehe" he jokes.

"Har Har Har.. You always slippery Dudail. Long time no see little man." Emil smiled his big gap toothed grin.

"Aye, has been a long time old friend. Do you like my new look?" Dudail smiles. He has known Emil for most of his life. Large in size and slow of thought, Emil appears to be human at first glance but upon closer inspection there are signs of another, more feral race from the swamps. Half breed like Dudail, though one half is Ogre where Dudail's is High Elf. Despite their differences, the two have been friends most of their lives. Steadfast and loyal to his friends, ferocious to enemies, Emil is not one to trifle with.

Emil peers at Dudail, looking him up and down. "Nice shiny armor. Me like shiny." He grins. "You have new pointy things too. Is not good for you to play with something sharp? har har har."

Dudail hands Emil his Tomahawk. "She's a beauty isn't she."

The Tomahawk looks like a hatchet in Emil's hand. " Har Har Har. Who you gonna hurt wit dis little thing? Har Har Har!" Emil turns and hangs the axe in a ceiling high locker behind him.

"Hey!! Be careful with that. Grenix Mucktail doesn't give that away. I had to persuade him hehehe" Dudail reaches for the sheath on his left side and pulls his Dirk. "I know the rules Emil." House rules at the Codpiece NO WEAPONS. Kind of ridiculous since swords and axes were the least of Dudail's weapons but rules were rules.

Dudail looked around the inn and turned to Emil. "Is he here?"

"You not gone THAT long!!! He always here." With a signal from Emil one of the pretty serving girls started to make her way through the crowded room.

Dudail starts to smile and strikes is most dashing pose. "Hi there sweetie"

"Hi Dud" she smiles "Don't remember me do you?" With that the pretty serving girl pulls her arm back and lands a full hand smack to Dudails cheek. "There, maybe that will refresh your memory"

He chuckles and rubs the red hand print on his cheek, "Now I remember you Sheena. Hehehe. Still upset about the little incident in the stable I see"

"Har Har Har. Me think so Har Har Har. Go get you know who but don't tell why." Emil asked Sheena.

With a glare and a wink Sheena glides through the crowd and slips into the kitchen. Maybe she was not as upset as she appeared. Dudail made a mental note to investigate further.

Dudail moved to the bar and ordered a Short Beer. Up went the glass and down went the beer, "Mmmm as good as I remember"

From the kitchen emerged a gray haired man. Though weather, there was no stoop to his posture nor hitch to his gait. Of medium height and average looks, the thing people noticed right away was his bearing. He held himself tall and proud with movements that spoke of quickness and strength. Scars criss-crossed both arms and hands and a nasty one tracked across his face from left cheek to chin. A warrior for all to see.

With a suspicious look in his eye as he looked around the room till he spotted Dudail, then they were alight with pleasure and pride.

"Bout time you showed your tail in here" he grumped in an attempt to hide his feelings.

Dudail sketched his best bow and said, "I have braved wind, rain, orcs and giants to once again be blessed by your company" . The bow disguised the glint of mischief in his eye and gleeful grin.

"Bollucks" roared the old man. "Git yer skinny butt over here and give yer father a hug". The old man beamed a smile as Dudail let out a loud whoop of joy and jumped on him, embracing him an a bear hug.

Heads turn and knowing smiles appear on the faces of those who know the tail of the wandering Bard and his father Captain Thalion (ret).
