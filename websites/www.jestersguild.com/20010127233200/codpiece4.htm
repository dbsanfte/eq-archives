The Jesters Codpiece, Chapter 4

By

Dudail Bellavoce

Chapter 4

Dudail related to his father what he had overheard…

"Sssss.. The meddler Thalion was supposed to be here. Sir Luc will have some explaining to do when the master learns of this. At least we have the elven bitch." hissed the Dark Elf captain. "The master has great plans for you." He leered as he pulled Iyrinia's head up from where she lay on the floor.

"Your master Innoruuk will never have me you abomination" she challenged through the blood and bruises that covered her face and body.

The captain threw back his head and roared with laughter, "We shall see hehehe we shall see."

The Dark Elf party then tied Iyrinia to a pole and carried her from the house.

Thalion's eyes turned dark with rage. Sir Luc has set them up to be slaughtered. His knuckles turned white as he gripped his sword. He rose and turned, determined to find Sir Luc and persuade him with red-hot pokers and sharp knives, to tell him where they had taken his wife. A small tug at his pant leg brought him up short. He looked down and saw his son's eyes staring into his, filled with tears.

"Don't leave me daddy." He cried.

Thalion's heart broke. He could not exact his revenge, now. His son needed him. Picking Dudail up, he headed back to Freeport. The battles in the Commonlands had ended since the real reason for the Dark Elves mission had been successful.

He entered Freeport through a hidden tunnel he knew the smugglers used and headed to a friend's house. Feleshia Starbright was an officer in the bards' guild and close friend. There he found shelter for his son. He sought the bards' advice on how he should proceed. Their advice was to take care of his family, bid his time, show no sign that he knew of Sir Luc's involvement, and learn where Iyrinia was taken by returning to work. Information is best learned in the lion's den, they said.

His first sad duty was to notify Finwe, his father in-law. He took this upon himself since he wanted them to know the truth even though his relationship with Iyrinia's family was not good since he took her away from the court and her home. With Sir Luc's involvement, there was no telling what Finwe would be told. Thalion went to the Wizards guild and paid to have a message sent to Felwithe.

Finwe's response was quick and efficient to no avail. A large force arrived in the Commonlands by magic and though a large force entered Nektulous Forest and tracked the raiding party, they had escaped through the druid rings. Finwe then advanced on Freeport to demand an explanation from Sir Luc. Fawning apologies and promises of retribution were made. Through it all Finwe glared at Thalion. He clearly blamed him putting his daughter in a situation where this could happen.

It was right after the meeting with Sir Luc that Finwe tried to take Dudail. He felt his blood needed to be raised properly. It was a near thing. Heated words were spoken and blades were drawn. In the end Dudail stepped between the two men.

"STOP" he shouted. Though only seven seasons old, his mothers abduction has aged him. "I will stay here in Freeport with my father." He glared at his grandfather, daring him to day different.

Finwe recognized the look in Dudail's eyes. It was the same one his mother had given him when he told her she could not marry Thalion.

"Very well grandson." He smiled, "But know that the doors to my home are always welcome to you." With a final glare for Thalion, he stalked off, leaving Freeport and Antonica the way he had come.
