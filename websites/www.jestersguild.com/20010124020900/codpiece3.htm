The Jesters Codpiece, Chapter 3

By

Dudail Bellavoce

Chapter 3

Years passed and though Thalion was not in favour with Sir Luc or his cronies his talent, honour and abilities singled him out and made him a favorite with the citizens of Freeport and the Commonlands. Sir Luc had no choice but to keep promoting him till he reached the rank of Captain.

One day while Thalion was taking on extra duties to help a friend; word arrived from the Commonlands. Dark Elves were invading from the Nektulous Forest. Fear flashed through Thalion. Iyrinia and Dudail where at home with no protection except the few patrolling guards. He raced from his post to their home.

As he entered the East Commonlands he witnessed a fierce battle raging. The forces of the Dark Elves were attempting to slaughter all the inhabitants of the East Commonlands while the few adventurers that happened to be in the area and shopkeepers attempted to defend themselves. With hardly a pause to take in the sight, Thalion plunged into their midst, heading straight for his home. Many of the forces of evil met their end when they got between Thalion and his goal.

As he neared his home he knew he was too late. The outbuildings were ablaze as was the north side of the house. More details came into view as he got closer; the front door kicked in, scorch marks on the front porch, a pool of blood in the front yard. His heart filled with dread. He raced into the house, sword drawn.

"IYRINIA!!!! DUDAIL!!!" he cried.

The only reply was the crackling of the fire and the creaking of timbers as they burned. He searched all the rooms. From the condition of the house he knew there had been a mighty struggle. Iyrinia had not gone quietly. The pools of blood and tattered pieces of uniform of the army of Neriak attested to that. She was no damsel in distress. Long had she lived before she settled down with Thalion and she had many experiences but that is another story…

In despair Thalion sunk to the floor. His beloved wife and son were gone! Then a sound reached his ear. It was the muffled weeping of a child. Hope leapt anew!! He began searching for the source of the sound calling for his son.

"Dudail.. Dudail. It's me, your father." He yelled.

In response he heard pounding coming from the kitchen. It was then that he remembered the cold cellar dug under the floor concealed by a trap door. Thalion raced to the trap door. An overturned table covered it. Soon the table was flying across the room and the trap door was opened. Inside was Dudail, scared and weeping.

Thalion swept his son into his arms and ran from the house. Outside he held and comforted him soothing his panic. After Dudail had calmed down, Thalion learned the awful truth. The Dark Elf raid had been a ruse. The real goal was to snatch Iyrinia. Dudail told his father he had overheard the Captain of the Dark Elves say that all had not gone as planned.
