The Jesters Guild .

The Jesters Guild is a loose amalgam of mostly New York City single professionals in their 20s and 30s devoted to our friends, our city and our fun. When we raise our glasses, we raise money for local charities. Our recent events include The Prom (for New York Cares), the Tiki Party (for the Covenant House) and Thirsty Thursday (for New York City Coalition Against Hunger) The Fall Rush Party and The Birthday Party, our signature Guild events, quintessentially represent our mission and philosophy: Welcome, Share, Celebrate. Sounds corny, we're looking for a better slogan.

Our members never earn money for their efforts. We are, however, personally enriched by meeting you and creating what are invariably some of the best evenings of our lives. Come join us for our events. We always welcome new members and charities to the fray.


If you would like to receive an invitation to our future events, send an email to: list@jestersguild.com

We reserve the right to expect you to dress for a theme, put on a costume or wear a dumb nametag. If you come to an event and don't want to dress the way we say, you may be publicly humiliated and/or heckled. We also may take pictures of you and paste prettier faces over yours. You are hereby warned.
