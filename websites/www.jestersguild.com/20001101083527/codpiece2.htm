The Jesters Codpiece, Chapter 2

By

Dudail

Chapter 2

Dudail was glad to be home and his father was overjoyed to have his only son back safe. He disengaged from his father's fierce embrace and stepped back to have a look at him.

"Lookin good Pops, except for that role of fat around your middle" joked Dudail.

Thalion was in good shape for his age, a hold over from his days in the Freeport Militia. He still followed his daily training ritual as some of the young men from the neighbourhood who trained with him could attest. Up before dawn. Stretching and running followed by fencing and shield play.

"Bah you insolent whelp!" said Thalion with a grin. "No more fat now than when you were a lad on your mothers apron strings"

At the mention of Dudail's mother, Iyrinia, both father and son's mood changed.

"Any luck with the search son?" whispered Thalion, eyes downcast and sad.

"I am sorry father. No luck as yet but I have not given up. There are many places I have not yet searched and many places the dark ones prevent me from searching. Some day I will be able to scour the world and the evil ones will not prevent me from finding what I seek or learning the truth." Fierce determination could be read in Dudail's every word and nuance.

The romance of Iyrinia and Thalion was the typical story of, the man from the wrong side of the docks and the beautiful elven princess. They met by chance when Iyrinia arrived on a diplomatic mission from Faydwer with her father, Ambassador Finwe, member of the Royal family of Felwithe and ambassador large.

Thalion was a junior guard assigned the duty of routine foot patrol in East Freeport. He happened to be on his normal route at the docks when the ambassador and entourage arrived. A Dark Elf assassin was lying in wait for the High Elf delegation. Thalion noticed something was wrong when he saw that all the normal street rogues were missing from the area and will be the first one to admit it was a case of, the right place at the right time. From where he was standing he saw the Dark Elf appear when he raised his arm to cast his dagger. Reacting on instinct, he leaped into the path of the missile and raised his shield. The assassin was no amateur. He tried to adjust his aim, but was not entirely successful. Though he missed his target, his dagger still found a home in flesh.

Thalion felt a searing pain in his right thigh then numbness. He leg buckled under him and he fell to the dock.

"What is going on?" he thought to himself. "I have been wounded by more than a little dagger and haven't been put down!"

True to his nature, the Dark Elf had coated his weapon with a virulent poison. The Ambassador's bodyguard having seen what had transpired surrounded the Ambassador's party and started to move them back aboard their ship.

Iyrinia noticing Thalion lying on the dock and having seen what he had done prevailed upon her father to have someone help him. Finwe listened to his daughter and although worried for her safety, dispatched one of his men to drag him to the ship with the rest of the company.

Once safely aboard, Iyrinia was allowed to tend to the wounded man, as she was skilled in the way of healing. And so it was that hers was the face Thalion saw when he awoke. It was love at first site. They had many obstacles to overcome and Finwe her father was not pleased, but that is another story…

At the insistence of Ambassador Finwe, Sir Luc promoted Thalion to Lieutenant for his bravery. In the fullness of time, Thalion and Iyrinia were married and they settled down to live just outside Freeport in the East Commonlands. Thalion continued his career in the guards and Iyrinia reveled in her role as housekeeper and soon they were blessed with a son.
