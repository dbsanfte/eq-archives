The Beginnning
As the sun set in South Karana Winneda bid farewell to her best friends Enus, Cyrth, Deban, Kaldazar, Bloodyscam, and Mardus after a long fight with the Split Paw.

Seeing how they all went their own seperate ways Winneda wondered if she would ever see this group of Extraodinary Knights again...

She prayed to Tunare that night, asking for a sign of what to do.

As Winneda slept a Wood-Elf in Ivy Etched Armor came to her dreams. He talked to her and said, "My child, I call upon you to form a house. Bring forth those dearest to your heart to help you fight the evil that lurks our lands. I have seen the good you bring and the teachings you give the young. Go forth and call upon your friends. Take heed Winneda! Be strong and grow with Norrath."

The years went by, Winneda had a new calling! She heard the cry of the Elemental Dragon. It spoke to her, "Winneda, Seek forth Kiko Trueheart. We need your help. I am dying, Winneda, I need your help to keep my power alive... I call upon my truest Druid to tell you a tale in hopes that you will help us." Winneda woke up quickly from her slumber, packed her bags, and left to find Kiko Trueheart.

Kiko Trueheart was found in the land of Antonica where Winneda met with him and heard his cries. She prayed to Tunare to give her the power to help these mighty Dragons keep their Faith.

Winneda then met with her brothers and sisters of Manifest Ascension and told them the saddened tale of the Dragon. With quick haste we took them into shelter and shared our home.

To this day both Manifest Ascension and the Dragons keep learning from each other in hopes to keep the faith alive and the Elemental Dragons' wise teachings.

United as one, we are strong and wise again!
