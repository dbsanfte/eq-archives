Tsunami is one of the oldest original lpMUDs in existance, having been around since at least 1990. It has a long and glorious history, which you can read about by taking one of the links to the left. The MUD is a virtual world constructed with an ancient Japanese and a medieval European world in mind, though there are exceptions. The game features many different types of play including hack and slash, questing, business and the ocassional war which grips the mud. Please follow the links to the left to learn more about this truly great game.
