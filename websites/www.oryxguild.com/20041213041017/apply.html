So you are interested in joining Oryx? Let me explain a few things about us.

Oryx is a hardcore raiding guild located on the Lightbringer server which is on the PST server list. Oryx is a Horde guild. We are a group of skilled players with a goal of seeing the high end game.

Our loot system is based on the RPP point system.

We raid 6 days a week usually from 5pm PST till whenever.

 

There are a few requirements you must meet in order to be considered.

First and foremost you must be able to dedicate at least 4 days to raiding with 5-6 hour raid days starting at around 5pm PST.

You MUST be level 60 and a Horde character. Healing classes may apply prior to level 60 if they show outstanding skill at their class.

You must show dedication and skill at your class. You must also know the world well enough to travel at a moments notice.

You MUST have 2 tradeskills selected and leveled.

NO DRAMA WHORES. If your going to cause problems save yourself the trouble and dont apply. We dont want you.

 

All applicants will have their app reviewed and will be contacted in game shortly by the recruiting officer after the application has been recieved and reviewed. You will then have a one on one interview and instructed what to do next.

We are currently not accepting applications at this time. The game is new and we are working on learning the game and leveling. We wont be recruiting untill sometime in mid febuary.

Of course if you are a player who shows great skill at your class and you want to level/group with Oryx feel free to apply. We will take your application into consideration.

Any questions or concerns please contact Renia in game or email her at Renia@Oryxguild.com .
