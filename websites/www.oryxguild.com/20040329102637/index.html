A setback.....and a new beginning
3/25/04 - Sola

You know SoE can go to hell for all I care. I started out leading this guild back before PoP, with 25 people running ragged in NTOV. Sure I knew how to kill the shit but with using half what I was use to was so damn fun. Planes of Power came out and it was like we hit a snag. March 2003 killed us. Some say our choice of doing a linear progression, and keying for Emp in Ssra was a mistake, PoP was the way to go. I turned the other way, pushing to finish Emp. April we rebuilt and keyed, May saw the death of Emperor Ssra. End of May to August we farmed the piss out of Vex Thal. We were having a blast, although 30 people VT clears got very, very old. I finally looked toward PoP and hit another snag. I just couldnt progress with 40 people. We recruited, and we recruited alot, I think. In the end there was a total of 85ish people. I was pleased.

September saw the death of Rallos, 2 weeks later we finished our Elemental Access with Solusek dying. I was on cloud 9. For 6 months we farmed tier 4, waiting for the right time to move forward with the raid force I was use to now (55-65 people). January Fennin Ro fell, 1 week later Xegony died. I couldnt ask for anything more.

And then it happened.

People got tired. Real Life caught up with others. Feb 2004 proved to be as hard on Oryx as March 2003 did. Longtime members of mine left, tired of people not wanting it bad enough. Tired of the endless nights farming scraps with 35-40 people, not making a shot at Time, and the rest of what Everquest has to offer. I almost folded. I almost gave up.

The answer was right in front of my face. I needed a mass of people that I could train, that would do exactly what I wanted when I wanted it. I found my answer in a little guild called Terrorized. Oryx has taken them under our wings and together we have forged a new beginning, for old members and new alike. The last week has seen a full reflag, and a very suprised guildleader, being handed a near flawless Rallos kill.

The footwork is almost complete. The pieces have been moved into position, and I'm ready to play the game again. Not for you, not for prestige, not for the top spot, but the way I have always played. For me, for my guild, for the game.

Nothing is stoping me this time.
