GENERAL INFORMATION ON DATA TRANSFER AND STORAGE:

Data transfer is derived from all the downloaded data from your web site. Email services are not included in the counts towards the data transfer usage.

Storage is calculated from total of Email, web space, logs and any files on your server. There are tools in place to rotate and archive logs to save space. It is recommended that you either download and delete the logs on a regular basis or set the system to auto delete the log files over a specified period of time.
