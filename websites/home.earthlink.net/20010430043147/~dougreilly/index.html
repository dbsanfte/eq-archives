News-February 2001
Monday, February 01,2001 - Fino
OK here's my first attempted at updating this site. As you all know we have had some changes. First and biggest Jhessal is our new Guildmaster, lets all congratulate her on this promotion. Second is I have been promoted to Officer and webmaster, and Phina has been promoted to Officer. Last but not least we are gathering some amazing new members, Kenmare has mentioned a few of them below, but we have more AoW welcome Valarie and Jerroth!!

OK so our activities recently include OWNING the Tower of frozen shadow and working on our Old Sebilis and Howling Stones keys. We have successfully killed Tserrina 3 times now and have really gotten this tower down to a Tee. Big ups to all involved (too many to list in AoW) and a Special thanks to Kraegar, Tossk, Rauq, Orcen, and Azie to name a few from other guilds.

Now with the shift in our guild we will also have some new traditions to add to the old one. We are still all about being the best we can in our classes. This means we are an asset to any group we fight with, that when we leave the group will compliment our actions during our time with them. Something new we are going to add is a private Guild forum, this will be used to discuss interguild activities. When this forum is created the URL will be mailed to everyone. So ending this update, Lets all get together and have fun and show Norrath how efficient AoW really is.

 

Sunday, February 04, 2001 - Kenmare

The AoW website has finally been updated!  I have been trying to keep our site current, however as of recent weeks, real life has been taking much of my time.  Fino has therefore volunteered his time and effort to help keep our website up-to-date (/cheer).  

Well, there have been many of us who have moved on to real life endeavors and others in AoW who have moved to other guilds.  We also added a few good people in our guild, namely Sagin, Aqualuung, Phina, Tarlain, and Kemon.  Welcome to Art of War!
