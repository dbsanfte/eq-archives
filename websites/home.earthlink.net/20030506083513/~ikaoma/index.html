We kick the Fritos ASS

Sounds like the Keepers handed the Fretti a sound ass whooping. Congrats to those that got their GEBs. Heres the LINK to the original post.

I have to say, I was most impressed with the Keepers as a whole this evening.

The following people showed up : Tigertank, Maddi, Jasariz, Zolog, Fayrlorn, Wantu, Gwarks, Melderous, Evandril, and Xeckis, a monk from the Stormseekers. We got into SolB about 6:30 PST, to find that the Efretti was already camped. We decided to stick it out and wait until they had finished their camp. We made our way to the DB room, where Tigertank pulled some imps to see how well we would do if and when the Efretti came open. We did very well, and at about 7:30 PST the campers left (their tank logged so they couldn't hold the room anymore).

We proceeded to break into the efetti area, killing imps as we went. However disaster seemed to loom on the horizion as 3 of the damned things popped on us as we made our way up the ramp. A frantic battle ensued as we tried to keep the cursed creatures from wiping us out. In a stunning display of level-headededness, everyone present kept their cool, assisting the main tank as we took them down one at a time. Sometime during the battle we agroed a 4th imp, making things worse. As the clerics' supply of mana dwindled, we cleaned up the stragglers, and made our way to the Efretti's room to rest. (the room had not yet respawned from the previous group)

We then settled into the camping routine, killing imps and the placeholders as they spawned. After seeing 6 or 7 placeholders spawn, we were overjoyed at seeing the Efretti himself spawn. We quickly dispactched him, and Melderous won the roll between her and Zolog, making her the first Keeper of the Blood to loot her GEBs. We stayed for 2 more rounds of spawns. In both cases the efretti spawned, was was quickly taken down. Zolog was awarded the second pair, and Xectis won the roll on the 3rd.


I wanted to take the time to congratulate everyone present on a job very well done. I also wanted to congratulate again Melderous, Zolog, and Xectis on their new GEBs.

Until the next raid!

Newest Patch

Heres the latest Patch Message.

August 22nd, 3:00 am
------------------------------


** Crashing Bug Fixes **

- Fixed the problem that was causing zones to crash in some instances.
- Corrected a bug that would cause some people to hang up while waiting for a sound event between the splash and character-select screens.


** Item Change **

- The Green Flower should be working again.


** Spell Change **

- Fixed a stacking bug that allowed multiple Levitation buffs to stack.


** Sullon Zek Changes **

- The Resist code changes we made with the last patch should be working properly now. Here is the text about that change:

"Changed the way resists are calculated when a Player Character casts a spell on another Player Character on Sullon Zek. Resist modifiers based on level will now be more smoothly distributed and will allow lower level characters a slightly better chance to land spells on higher level characters. At the same time giving lower level characters a slightly better chance to resist spells cast on them by higher level characters."

- Cancel Magic spells should now work across teams.
- Characters should no longer disappear when Mesmerized or stunned
