February 22, 1999. First News Posting. Well, looks like we have a Endurance Race bike courtesy of Chris "Moose" Furrows of Lunatic Racing. We will be running a 1994 CBR600 F2 with F3 modifications. Hopefully this machine will take us to victory in the OMRRA Endurance Season in Portland, Oregon and hopefully attend Willow Springs Endurance later this fall.

May 15-16th, 1999. Great Finish! The Lunatic Endurance Team took a AWESOME 2nd Place finish after 4 grueling hours at Portland International Raceway! Everything came together this day with no major setbacks. Our preparations had paid off! Unfortunately, we were unable to use our new race cams from Webcams because we were unable to rebuild the cylinder head in time. Even without the cams, we still beat out the 3rd place team by 1 lap! Needless to say, we were all estatic about our finish! Thanks to all those that helped out as our pit crew and our sponsors. Circuit One Suspension and Rose City Motorcycle.

September 18-19th, 1999. Well unfortunately we couldn't make this weekends Endurance race at Portland International Raceway. We were all very bummed out! Well, there is always next year! Look for the Lunatics coming up from the backside!

January 1, 2000. Happy New Year! Hopefully everyone had a safe and awesome New Year! I was stuck at work so naturally it sucked! Vrrrrrooom Vrrooom, fresh start from here.

February 28, 2000. Woooohoooo!! Looks like the Lunatic's got a new race bike curtosy of Chris "Moose" Furrow. Hopefully we'll be racing to victory circle on a 1999 Yamaha R6 600cc motorcycle!
