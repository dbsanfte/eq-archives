Here's a table of fletching information for you wanna be arrow makers. Notice that making arrows & selling back to merchants is a money losing business.

Explanation of Items Used:

1st number is the arrowhead. 1 represents Field Point, 2 is Hooked, 3 is Bladed.
2nd number is the shaft. 1 represents Wood, 2 is Bone, 3 is Ceramic, 4 is Steel.
3rd number is the fletch. 1 represents Round, 2 is parabolic, 3 is Shield. Shield gives shortest range but hardest hitting power.
4th number is the nock. 1 represents Large, 2 is Medium, 3 is Small. Small nock makes a faster arrow, but is more difficult to manufacture.
