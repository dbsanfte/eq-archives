News for Monday May 21st 2001

Wow, What a weekend.  After much deliberation, Forbidden Rites, Order of Hasson and Deliverance are now One Team.  The name has stayed Deliverance, but the guild is 100% new.  Cronar is guild leader, and we'll be publishing an updated roster with officers within the week.

Saturday the 19th was our Fear Raid.  The break went poorly, and Kathrynn from Knights of Eternal Good (KEG) brought in a group of her guildies to help us out.  Once it was broken, we cleared the plane.  Some good drops happened.  Unfortuantly, no one kept a list.  So, if you got Fear loot on the 19th, email me.  We also helped Passion from Realm Powers get the last piece for her Wizard Epic.  Apparently there was a problem with the turnin, and let's all hope the GM's fix it for her.  GL Passion.  We're here to help you!

Sunday the 20th Deliverance did Hate.  Don't know the drops yet, but they'll be posted on our forums.
