Purpose:
To aid and protect our brethren as they begin their new lives as adventurers. To increase our membership so that all races benefit from our knowledge and experience.

Membership:
All races and classes are welcome to join The Keepers. We pass no judgement on any race, nor any class. We all desire our fellow adventurers to prosper and grow. The Keepers act on that desire. Therefore, all races and classes are invited to join out guild. We are all brothers in this wonderous land.

Methods:
The Keepers act as guides and aides for those starting their adventuring careers. We provide assistance in the form of advice, directions, or minor magical aide. On occasion, we will band together to rid ourselves of a group that has earned our wrath. We will do so to provide young adventurers a safer place to practice their new-found skills. We do not force our methods or knowledge on anyone, nor do we keep our knowledge from anyone who seeks us out. If an adventurer seeks out a Keeper and requests his sagely advice, the Keeper is obligated to help the plaintiff, within reason. Perhaps guiding him to the nearest guild hall, or bank. Perhaps leading a stray adventurer back to a path or landmark. Minor healing or other beneficial spells are part of the obligation to our questing brothers.

Politics:
The Keepers maintain a distance from the political strife that infects other guilds. We honor all adventurers as brothers, and seek to remain neutral with all guilds and factions. However, we will not tolerate any guild or faction interfering with our brothers. We seek to protect our own with all the means at our disposal. Should we seek vengeance, we will do so with overwhelming force, and crush our enemy.

Benefits:
Guild members benefit from the brotherhood that is associated with being a Keeper. The knowledge and might of an individual is dwarfed by that of the group. While one member of the guild may not have the knowledge required, he has access to the wisdom of the whole. If a Keeper is being beset by vicious creatures, he can call upon the aide of his brothers. Such aide is not to be taken lightly, and it’s benefit cannot be exaggerated. Should a Keeper ever be slighted or cheated by a lowly being, the disrespect of the guild will be visited upon that individual. They shall know what it is to be shunned by the Keepers.
