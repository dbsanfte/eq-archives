Remembrance

Autumn in the Butcher Block Mountains was pretty much like autumn in any other place on Norrath. The leaves have started turning a shade of orange, a slight chill upon the wind could be felt, yep autumn was in full swing; thought Krasilin. "We best be heading to some place warmer ya hairless ape," Krasilin said as gazed over at his friend. "Ur kay ‘ittle guy" replied Tsaathagua.

Tsaathagua is one of the inhabitants off Oggok, the capital city of the ogre nation. It was an odd coupling, on one hand Tsaathagua belonged to an order of knights that opposed all that was good and just. Often enough he would drag Krasilin into a fight with holier then thou paladins of Marr, just for fun. On the other hand there was Krasilin a dwarven priest of Brell Serillis the mighty dwarven deity, dedicated to maintaining balance and good where necessary. Neither remembers why they became friends but both remember how they were once enemies…

Winter had settled over most of Antonica, not that you could tell in the Oasis of Marr. Krasilin was sitting near the docks enjoying a frosty mug of dwarven spirits, magically chilled to preserve the powerful brew no matter the heat. Across from him was Edyrnn the Lyon a paladin of Marr and a right stuffy, overzealous human if he’s ever known one. Edyrnn had the look of an epic hero, with his well-muscled body, square jaw, and handsome (as humans go) looks. Then there was Absalomn a young human; probably no more than nineteen summers, he too was well muscled but nearly a half the size of the paladin. Absalomn was a monk from the city of Freeport, who abhorred violence but could dish it out greater than anyone Krasilin knew. All three were heroes in their lands and had came to the oasis to purge the land of an orc temple and its high priest Broxk Moonripper. The map they obtained from the gypsy camp, outlined the whereabouts of the temple and the surrounding area, they would strike tomorrow around noon when the orcs were at their weakest.

Early dawn they set out for the orc temple, in hopes of reaching it by high noon. Without horses the journey was less then enjoyable, especially for me thought Krasilin damn humans and their long legs. The journey took nearly three hours over rolling dunes that seemed to have a life of themselves.

As they neared their objective, Absalomn scouted ahead since he was the only one not wearing plate armor. With a few hand signals Absalomn had indicated that only two orc warriors stood out front of the temple entrance. Krasilin and Edyrnn would move to the rear of the temple to surprise the orc warriors, as Absalomn would charge with his lightning speed from the front. Krasilin stood amazed that the orcs could have constructed something as amazing as the temple before him.

The temple stood on a rocky out cropping some fifty feet above the sand dunes. Four pillars, easily twenty feet tall, were erected to hold the roof of the exterior entrance. All along each pillar were golden orcish symbols carefully carved, not like the crude one found in the common lands near Freeport, but symbols crafted by a true and dedicated craftsman? The doors to the temple were iron bound, and bore the symbol of some dark deity the orcs worshipped here. Regardless, of the temples near beauty it was an affront to all the goodly races and needed to be purged. An evil grin rolled over Krasilin’s face, as he contemplated how many orcs he would be able to kill today.

With a surge of speed Krasilin and Edyrnn sprinted over the small ledge they were hiding behind, towards the unsuspecting guards. As on cue Absalomn charged the two-orc warriors, with a bellowing cry one orc charged Absalomn as the other made for the iron bound doors. The last thing the retreating orc saw was the head of Krasilin’s giant warhammer, just before its sickening impact. Blood and brain splattered Edyrnn’s armor, prompting a moan of disgust from Edyrnn and a giggle from Krasilin. The other orc warrior oblivious to his comrade’s demise charged Absalomn with its deadly scimitar. Ten feet from the orc, Absalomn leaped into the air and brought his foot crashing down on the orc warrior’s head. Dazed the warrior took a wild swing with his scimitar, that was easily deflected by the monk. As the two squared off, Absalomn searched for a weakness in his opponent’s defense, waiting to deliver the deadly strike he was known for. Thinking he could easily defeat an unarmed human, the orc warrior began slashing back and forth with murderous delight. With swift grace, the young monk parried and dodged the enraged orc’s attacks. Seeing a brief opening, Absalomn unleashed a flurry of blows to the bewildered orc; each strike causing the orc to stumble back. As the orc warrior brought his scimitar in a chopping arc over his head, the agile monk stepped into the orc’s reach and struck its throat with a piercing strike. The orc was either to stupid to realize he was dead or was possessed by his dark god, regardless it kept coming after an astonished Absalomn. Stunned that his attack didn’t work Absalomn was unable to avoid the vicious backhand from the orc warrior and was sent flying into the sand, dazed. Trying to recover from the stunning attack, Absalomn was unable to deflect the descending scimitar. The shearing sound of metal and bone had always been music to Krasilin’s ear but never has it happened to a friend. Absalomns corpse lay there in the sand with half his head cleaved, squirting blood and oozing brain matter.

"NOOOOooo!" Edyrnn screamed with rage. Leaping over the dead orc at his feet, the young paladin charged the staggering orc, sweeping the air with his glowing runesword. Three things dawned on the orc, he couldn’t breath, there was a dead human at his feet (that made him happy), and a crazy armored human was charging him. He thought it was kind of funny, even after the armored human sliced his head off, Grok had never seen his body from this angle, kind of weird he thought.

Krasilin moved to stand over Absalomns body, reciting a prayer to Brell to safely guide his fallen comrades’ body to his eternal reward. Edyrnn gave a warrior’s salute and said "Come on Kras, there’s a temple full of orcs and I’ve got a need for vengeance." The dwarf stood over his fallen comrades’ lifeless form, offering only a silent reply.

Krasilin replied softly, "Tis a shame I never got to know the lad better, he was to young to come, I should not have let him join us." "This is no time for feeling sorry for yourself Krasilin, the kid knew the dangers." Edyrnn stated, he couldn’t fathom why the dwarf was worrying over a fallen comrade this much, then it dawned on him…Krasilin had a son that died in battle along side him. "Kras’ Edyrnn began ‘he’s not your son, but we will mourn his loss later. Inside this temple is the cause of his death, lets go say ‘ello"

"Aye lad, lets go say ‘ello" Krasilin said with a sinister tone that sent a chill up Edyrnn’s spine.

Just beyond the front doors to the temple, Krasilin and Edyrnn made their way down the long hall, moving with as much stealth as two armored warriors could make. The rough-hewn hallway led into a vaulted chamber that reeked of spoiled food and sour drink. The ceiling was nearly fifteen feet high and decorated with all sorts of dark images of orcs in ritual slaughter around a great horned demon. Inside the chamber were three grand tables, each twenty feet long; near the back wall was a fire pit of enormous size. Set along the same wall near the right corner was a stout oak door; from somewhere beyond its threshold came that guttural chanting of orcs. In the opposite corner was another hallway that extended deep into the darkness. "Which way Kras?" Edyrnn whispered. "Well there could be a bunch of green skins down that hall’ Krasilin motioned to the opposite archway ‘that we could deal with first, or we can go smash a bunch of them through this door" Krasilin asked. "I think it best we take out the leader, the body cannot do much without its head." Edyrnn stated.

"Right." Krasilin said with finality right before he crushed the oak door with one blow from his hammer. Both warriors ran down the carved stairs, only taking brief seconds to observe the evil statues set in alcoves along the stairs. As they neared the bottom of the staircase the chanting had become much louder, Krasilin’s keen ears could pick out two distinct voices in the chanting. The stairs ended in a rather long hallway; the only room that could be seen was lit by green light. Krasilin and Edyrnn inched their way to the open doorway, and peeked inside.

Decorating the inside of this domed room was eight braziers alive with green fire, all at the tips of a large eight-pointed star on the floor. Behind each brazier was a pillar similar to the ones outside the temple entrance. In the center of the star was a massive orc, decorated with numerous tattoos along its face, arms and chest. On opposite sides of the large orc were two smaller ones chanting, green energy flowed from their eyes and mouth with each word. The baleful energy seemed to flow into the larger orc with each breath it took. Enough is enough Krasilin thought, with a slight hand gesture he told Edyrnn to take the one on the right and he the left.

Must be too distracted by their casting, Krasilin determined since he and Edyrnn were making no attempt at stealth. Both positioned next to the chanting orcs, giving the thumbs up to proceed. Then some unknown force knocked Edyrnn to the ground before he could strike the chanting orc. Materializing out of thin air was an enormous ogre warrior, in bronze armor wielding a massive two-handed sword. Along the blades surface were engraved runes glowing with a sickly violet light, with arms as big around as Krasilin’s waist, the ogre started swinging the sword in a deadly arc towards Edyrnn.

"Kill them Kras! Stop the ritual!" Edyrnn shouted as he barely parried the massive sword. Krasilin mumbles "right lad" sets his helm in place and charges bellowing "DIE HELL SPAWN!"

With one sweep of his massive hammer, Krasilin smashes the spine of the orc chanter sending him into the massive orc. Letting his momentum carry him, Krasilin brings his hammer down on the shoulder of the other chanter, smashing its collar and shoulder bone. The chanter goes down in a whimpering heap, baleful energy barely trickling out of its mouth.

Edyrnn grunted as pain shot up his sword arm from parrying another blow from the ogre warrior. Uttering a quick prayer, Edyrnn channels the power of Marr into his body giving him the protection of the gods. To his amazement the ogre likewise started to speak a dark prayer. Before Edyrnn could call out his discovery of the ogre’s nature, an evil darkness was clinging to him. Seeing his comrades’ danger, Krasilin intoned the prayer of cancellation, just before being struck by icy shards.

The darkness dissipated in time for Edyrnn to bring his guard up and deflect another killing blow. Relentlessly the ogre shadow knight pressed his advantage of size, strength and sure terrifying presence. Switching from sweeping arcs, to deadly chops the ogre wielded the two handed sword with the ease a duelist wields a rapier. To further his press his advantage the ogre managed to cast a summoning spell at the dead chanters body. With horrible sucking sound the skeletal form erupted from its fleshy confines to aid its dark master. Edyrnn realizing his impeding doom prayed for Marr to aid him and smite his new foe. In a burst of raw power and cracking thunder the newly risen skeleton blew apart, showering all the combatants with bone shards. Roaring in rage the ogre shadow knight charged Edyrnn with renewed vigor using every ounce of strength to crush his foe.

Reeling from the freezing blast, Krasilin looked up to see the large orc on his feet and non-to pleased with what he saw. In a voice that would haunt Krasilin for years to come the orc spoke, with a demonic tone "Foolish flesh, I am eternal. Nothing can stop me. Your soul and the soul of your kinsmen will be mine to feast upon." "Foolish flesh you dare try to assassinate me! I am Broxk Moonripper greatest of the orc priests, I am Kel’Rakein greatest of the demon princes and you will suffer for an eternity for such arrogance!" A swipe from his massive clawed hand, sent Krasilin into the far wall with a thud. "Demon, Orc, it matters not. I will send you back to whatever pit that spawned you." Krasilin wheezed.

Tsaathagua could not believe his luck, first he gets paid one hundred platinum to guard this orc priest what’s his name, now he actually got to fight someone worthy of his blade! Even though his opponent was only human, Tsaathagua knew he was special even before he cast his spells. This one was a true paladin, not like those others that claimed to be paladins; this one would be a great victory for him and his god Innoruuk! Allowing the human a moment of reprieve, Tsaathagua risked a glance at his employer. He was shocked to see the little man holding his own against the powerful priest. These two were truly great warriors to get this far and not have fallen!

Edyrnn knew the next few seconds would determine the outcome of this fight, he had spent too much energy healing his wounds. The ogre was barely injured and seemed to be fueled by some unnatural stamina he had never seen. With a war cry from ages past Edyrnn threw himself at his opponent, stabbing and slashing with righteous fury. The ogre seemed a little surprised that Edyrnn could be so energized. Good thought Edyrnn, it will give me the edge I seek and purge this evil from the land.

Tsaathagua was honestly surprised that the little man could fight with so much vigor, after the many cuts and bruises he had given the human. The surprise turned out to be to much as the little man got through his defenses and sliced Tsaathagua’s right thigh. "ARrrrrrGHGHH!" screamed Tsaathagua. Unbelievable that little humie hurt me, for that he will pay the ogre thought. Quickly speaking a dark prayer, Tsaathagua let out a sigh of relief partly from the paladins anguished scream and partly from the healing life force from the human. "Alright little squishy it’s time to die" grunted the ogre in its harsh language, the meaning of which still dawned on Edyrnn.

A blast of fire scorched the stone floor where Krasilin was a second before. Damn this orc is tough, Krasilin thought, I gotta try something else or Edyrnn and I are finished. Shifting his weight again to avoid another searing blast of fire, Krasilin bound his way around towards the engaged ogre, smiling all the way. Krasilin could hear the orc chanting another spell, running as fast as his little legs could carry him Krasilin leapt into the air between the space of Edyrnn and the ogre. A cry of anger from the ogre indicated his success and with brief mental pat on the back Krasilin sprinted towards a bewildered Moonripper. Moonripper unleashed another spell at Krasilin but this time Krasilin was prepared. Diving to the ground, Krasilin slid on his shield beneath the orcs startled form and hooked the orcs loincloth with the spike of his hammer. With his momentum going forward, Krasilin heaved his hammer upwards forcing Moonripper to land face first on the stone floor. Krasilin rolled his body to an upright position and bounded towards the fallen orc.

Damn that orc, he’s so preoccupied with killing that dwarf, he is forgetting about combat tactics. When this is over I better get a big bonus Tsaathagua thought to himself. Tsaathagua spoke up "Hey humie, you good fightur, me honur u and not eat ya."

"Comforting thought, but I don’t think I will be dying today." Replied Edyrnn hoarsely.

"Har har har, you humies so funny" Tsaathagua chuckled grimly

Feeling the battle had gone on long enough Tsaathagua intoned a prayer to his god to engulf his opponent in darkness. Sensing the evil magic building Edyrnn moved to disrupt the ogres casting and received a backhand for it. Picking himself up off the ground, Edyrnn was up and prepared for another round of fighting when the world went dark. This time ghost of all those he has slain in the course of his duty as paladin of Marr swarmed around him. Each ghost clawed and stabbed at their killer, inflicting phantom wounds that only injured the mind. Screaming in pain the young paladin attacked the darkness seeking to stop his tormentors but it availed him nothing. The attacks grew fierce, rending phantom wounds that would have slain a normal man in seconds. As each wound found a home on Edyrnn’s body a true wound was inflicted on his fragmenting mind. With a final gasp Edyrnn fell to the stone floor the last bit of life draining from his mouth and eyes in wisps of smoke.

Krasilin could hear the pains of his comrade but couldn’t aid him he had Moonripper to deal with, who was once again blasting him with spells. This fight is going to be over quick unless I try something different, all I need is to get one good hit on him, only if he would stand…Krasilin’s mind was sparked by a wonderful insight. Frantically Krasilin began reciting a spell he learned from a druid in the Faydark forest. Moonripper started to advance on the pathetic little dwarf that has troubled him for to long, he began a spell that would cost all his remaining power, but would be worth it to finish the stuntie. As Moonripper was finishing the last incantations to his spell he suddenly realized he couldn’t move. "ERE YA GO MATE" Krasilin yelled as he swung his heavy warhammer at Moonrippers' astonished face. With an impact that resounded through the temple complex, Moonripper fell backwards with a numerous cranial shards protruding from what was left of his skull.

Tsaathagua was startled from his victory by the bone breaking sound. Spinning around in hopes to see the dwarf splattered, he was shocked to see Moonripper lying on his back with a mangled face. Maybe the demon finally possessed Moonripper’s body, Tsaathagua thought. No wait, I don’t think skull bones are supposed to stick out of the head like that. Tsaathagua looked up and saw a triumphant look beaming across the dwarfs' face. "Not sure if I should be mad or amused at u ‘ittle guy." Tsaathagua stated.

"What do you mean you hairless ape"

"Well stuntie, me not happy u kill boss but me already git paid for job." Tsaathagua said with a toothy grin.

"Well the way I see it, you son of a motherless elf, we got buisness to discuss." Krasilin cackled.

"Wat dat, shortie" Tsaathagua asked happily feeling his wit was sharper then the dwarfs.

"Ya killed my friend, and now ye must pay dearly."

"Oh har har." Tsaathagua said dryly.

Before either one good charged the other, a roar of hundred footsteps echoed through the temple. "Hehe, gonna get it now ‘ittle guy, dem me bosses boys" Tsaathagua said as the first of a dozen orc warriors burst through the archway. The enraged orc warriors saw the carnage before them and decided to kill everyone in the room. To Tsaathaguas’ surprise they charged him as well. "Wait, I’m on…" Tsaathagua parried a cleaving attack "ur side…" Not understanding the circumstances that has befallen him, Tsaathagua bellowed his rage and tore into the mass of orcs swinging his massive sword. Seeing no way out, Krasilin let loose a war cry of courage and charged the mass of orcs.

A short while later Krasilin and Tsaathagua sat outside the temples main entrance out of breath and bleeding from dozens of cuts and bruises. Neither wanted to fight the other just now, they hurt too much for that. Long silence hung between the two survivors, until finally Tsaathagua rasped "Har har har, you fight good little guy, me respect u, me not eat ya." An exhausted Krasilin chuckled "We still got unfinished buisness there ya hairless ape, but we can finish it later." A toothy grin revealed the ogre’s amusement followed by the dwarfs beaming smile. Both cut short their dark mirth at the sounding of a dozen trumpets. "Uh oh, da boss is back" Tsaathagua moaned. "I thought Moonripper was your boss?" Krasilin asked. "Nah hez the boss’s bruder, boss won’t like me since I let you kill his bruder." Tsaathagua mumbled.

Both looked at each other for a few seconds, then scrambled to gather their belongings and sprinted for the nearest sand dune.

"Hey Kras, you day dremen again?" asked Tsaathagua, "I bet you thinken about hairy faced dorf women aren’t ya, har har har?" Snapped out of his memories Krasilin elbowed his comrade and said, "At least I can tell the difference between a dwarf male and a dwarven female. Have you looked lately at your kind ya hairless ape?"

"Er yeah, ogre wumen reallll pretty" Tsaathagua cooed.

"Sure thing friend, if ya like to hang from nose hairs as long as a human." Krasilin cracked.

Not realizing he had been insulted, Tsaathagua slid off the rock he’d been sitting on and said, "Where to Kras, me feel like breaking a few heads." Krasilin thought about it for a moment, and finally replied "Well we could take a boat down south to a place called Velious, I hear there are some dragons and giants down there with riches and magic." Tsaathagua licked his lips and stated "Mmmm dragon is a lizard and lizards taste like chicken, I like chicken." Krasilin smiled to himself, the ogre had a clever mind that was supported by his ravenous hunger for sentient flesh. "Alright lad, lets go crack some scaled skulls" replied Krasilin.

The two heroes, one for evil and one for good, set out to seek their fame and fortune in the lands of dragons. Fate will once again deal them a twisted hand…but that is another story.
