We are the guild, Deliverance. 
We are a collection of people from all walks of life, with common goals.
We are the teachers, the leaders and protectors of any who need it.
We promote unity through teamwork and acceptance.


To be a member of Deliverance, some basic requirements must be met.

  1. To be newly guilded, you must have made level 20 as a minimum. Existing members' alternate characters and real life friends can have this waved.

  2. You will play EQ in a manner that is within the rules. There will be no hacking, exploits, harassment or prejudice used within our ranks.

  3. You will be respectful to those around you. 

  4.  If you have an altercation with a person, keep it private. Do not use open chat channels as the means of your argument.

  5. When in groups, you will abide by the rules for looting set up by the group and agreed by the majority. If you do not like the rules that are set up, leave and remember them for future reference.

  6. Need before greed. When on guild functions, and a member is present that needs the item for use (not to sell or trade or twink), that member(s) will get priority on it. If you are playing one character and you need the item for a different one, that does not give you priority (unless others agree that you should have it).

  7. You will participate in the guild. Not every time, but when you can. If you want to solo to 60 and don't even participate in guildchat, then a guild is not for you.

  8. There will be no personal agendas in Deliverance. If you have a falling out with someone, make every effort to repair it. This is a guild for relatively mature people who can look beyond the situation to make amends. If you cannot repair things, keep it cordial. There will be no chips on shoulders in Deliverance.

  9. Help your guildmates. If you come across an item that a guildmate really needs, consider donating it to them. No, you are not required to do that, but karma smiles on those who help others.

  10. Members who are under 50 should concentrate on one character to get to at least 50. Naturally, you are not required to do this, but it is highly suggested, as we want your company in the upper levels.

  11. The most important rule. Real life MUST take precident over Everquest. Do NOT let this game take complete control over your life. Please balance work/friends/family/social life properly. Everquest should never come between you and the above.

Now the ugly. The above rules are fairly easy to abide by, most without trying. Should you be found in violation of the core rules, you will be unguilded. Not requested, not asked. Unguilded.


If you are unsure or have questions, any officer will be able to answer them.

The charter of our guild is fluid, meaning ever changing. Each member and officer considered an equal. Every opinion valid and valued.  When conflicts arise, the officers of the guild will use their best judgement to make things right.

As we grow, be patient and available to those in need.
