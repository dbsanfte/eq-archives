HERETICS OF THE APOCALYPSE

Who we are:

A group of adventurers, roaming Norrath seeking fame and fortune, we have discovered that there are many out there who wish to see us dead just because of our race, profession or religious preference. Well, those people have made a grave mistake, for the Heretics shall have the final word,
VENGEANCE SHALL BE OURS!!
 
Founded in the great city of Paineel by Scurge Darkterror, grandson of General Rellond Vorhest, highly decorated veteran of the Kerran Conflict.
 
MAJOR CHANGE
The majority of the Heretics have joined the guild Crusaders Immortal. We did this out of necessity...to adventure in the higher level places in Norrath a large group is a necessity. Being part of a larger guild will benefit both guilds by allowing them to have more members available to work together on various quests & raids.
The Heretics of the Apocalypse guild will still exist. A lot of the members are keeping some of their alternate characters in the guild. Scurge's mule character, Telley, now has the guild leader tag.
We are based on the Torvonnilous server. The guild accepts anybody who agrees with our philosophy. We require you to have reached your fifteenth season of adventure, and to have read our Code of Conduct page.
