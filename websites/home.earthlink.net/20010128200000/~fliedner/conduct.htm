HERETICS OF THE APOCALYPSE

Code of Conduct

We are accepting any adventurer who agrees with our philosophy/purpose, which is as follows: Take revenge on the narrow of mind who attack us because we are a race/class/religion that they don't approve of.

To become a member you must have the recommendation of a non-probationary guild member. You must be at least in your tenth season. You may have any secondary characters guilded only upon reaching Squire rank. Your secondary characters must also be level 10 or higher.

Unacceptable behavior: 1). Begging (asking repeatedly) other players (not just guild members) to get item(s) for you. Begging in guild chat is bad enough, but begging non guild players is even worse as it makes the whole guild look bad. This will not be tolerated. 2). Begging teleports from the guild wizards or druids. Remember, they are trying to level just like you. If they are in a group they aren't gonna leave it just to fly you around the world. To this point they have been very generous offering their TP services when they are ungrouped. Lets not piss them off and lose their services. 3). Excessive cussing in any chat channel. 4). Kill stealing / ninja looting. 5). Any action, not mentioned, that brings discredit towards the guild.

The guild has a rank structure which is primarily used to show length of time in the guild. The ranks, from lowest to highest, are Initiate, Squire, Knight, Sire/Madam, Lord/Lady, Liege, Commander, High Commander. The method for advancing in rank shall be at the discretion of the High Commander but so far consists of doing a simple quest and turning in the item(s) in conjunction with spendg time in the guild. The quest items depend on your level at the time the quest is given. Some examples of past quest items are: Split Paw Hide Tunic; 2 vials of Iksar blood; 2 pieces of barbed armor.
As of December 24, 2000 a new method of gaining rank will be implemented. Instead of quests we will promote members based on their attendance at guild functions.
To go from Initiate to Squire: attend 2 guild functions.
To go from Squire to Knight: attend 3 more guild functions.
To go from Knight to Lord: attend 3 more guild functions.
To go from Lord to Liege: attend 4 more guild functions.
Guild functions are planned dungeon raids / zone raids / meetings that have been posted on our message board.
Commanders are appointed on an as-needed basis so that we have 5 of them. Each commander has one vote to cast when voting on guild matters. The High Commander has 2 votes, for a total of 7 votes (and no ties).
