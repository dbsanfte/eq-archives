Update :: 27/08/04 :: breaking the mold...

Its un-orginal, simple... thats why we do it. I am not rough with my clothes what i buy usually lasts me a long time.. i've got a pair of shoes that after five years need to be put into the "camping only bin"...

The problem with this style of shopping that when the next millenium comes around, such as in this case, your look is well... umm... "old". I wanted to change that, I wanted to get attention, i wanted to be noticed more for just my bald head hehehe...

But being a guy, i was seriously lacking in the ability to pick out stylish clothes. I phone up a friend of mine and asked her if she'd like to go shopping with me. It wasnt long before we massed a large pile of clothes... and a serious bill too! I truly had a lot of fun. I spent several hundred dollars on clothes... ohh and a belt hehehe... Some may say that its a little too excessive.... who knows... the more important thing.. ive got a bold new look, and i had a total riot with Emily who i can't thank enough!!1

Update :: 10/08/04 :: those lazy days in the summer...

On Sunday 16 guys from the office went white river rafting in BC. If my memory serves me correctly, we travelled down 27km's of the Kicking Horse River. One of the guys, mike, is getting married in a few weeks. His best man, Tom, was trying to plan the weekend for us... the typical things were mentioned, strippers, getting hammered at a bar, golfing. After some time, i think Tom's parents mentioned the rafting idea. So sunday morning we all met at Esso on hi-way 1, headed off to canmore to get picked up. There are 5 classes of rapids, 1 being the easiest and 5 being extremely hard. The all day course we took went from Level 1 to level 4. Myself not being too fond of the water or being the best swimmer... i was a little hesitant to go. But after the total blast i had on sunday i'd do it again in a heartbeat!!! Next year we are gonna make a weekend outta it, first go rafting, camp over in Golden. The next day, go paragliding. I wasnt able to find a water proof camera for the trip, however someone was able to take 3 pics of us while we travelled down the river. I am hoping to get those in a few days, a week at the most.

Update :: 30/03/04 :: Szarin

Why is it some people have so much trouble with technology? I mean sure, some of it might be a little harder to grasp than others. However this just blows me away. I was at the liquor store yesterday (no surprise there :D ) and i was second in line of about 4 or 5 ppl total. The guy infront of the line went to pay for his flat of beer (24 cans for those need some clarification). He went to pay using his interact card, but for some reason only known to God he couldn't figure it out. We're not talking about the casier iether and how to ring the sale through. This guy had a problem with the concept of a piece of plastic that held a 4 or 5 digit number tied to several bank accounts to confirm one's actions with a keypad. This simple concept to everyone else was over this guy's head. After three attempts the guy flipped out because they were invalid PIN numbers. He threw his hands up in the air yelled something that no one around could understand, then proceed to run out the door. The cashier look puzzled and told the guy that it was the right PIN number finally but wrong account. The next two attempts were the wrong bank account again. On the 6th attempt he *only* got it right because the cashier had to walk him through it. It had been at least 15 mins by now and whether it was pure fustration of being a complete idiot or just imbarassed infront of others he figured... kicking the door with my foot will solve my problems... and with that... BANG he kicked the glass door with his work boots as the keypad finally said "APPROVED - Thank you."
