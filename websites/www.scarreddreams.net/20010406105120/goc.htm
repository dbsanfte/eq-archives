Guild Organization & Conduct

 

From the beginning, Tolkien's Revenge has been about friends grouping together in the spirit of fun, adventure and cooperation. To this day, this is still very true. Alas, times have changed and though we were once a young guild, we can now look around and see that we have grown and seasoned. As we look back to our humble beginnings and look forward to the dragons, planes and deities, we should never forget who we are or where we came from. Everyone was a new, bright and starry eyed adventurer once.

Since we are no longer a low level guild but one that is beginning to mature, some things have changed. For instance, there is a minimum level cap of 25 for new members *(plz read the section under the Guild conduct). Also, there are many officers with predefined positions. And now, there are guidelines for Guild Organization & Conduct. We have these guidelines to promote harmony and cooperation among members of the guild. As we have leveled up, the stakes have become higher as have the rewards, but still, some discipline is necessary.

The guild does have a hiearchy so to speak, based on the guild positions provided within the game. Our guild leader is Boraron. Since the position of guild leader is very demanding, Mallabik is second in command. There are also guild officers, although they are currently in two groups. The first group, is the High Council. This council has very specific roles within the guild and also arbitrate and make decisions concerning the welfare of the guild. The second group of officers have a more open role in the guild. There are many responsibilities that come with being an officer and it is not for everyone, even if it seems appealing at first glance. The majority of the guild has member status.

The following section provides guidelines for guild conduct. As individuals we all have different expectations and needs. We are all here to adventure, explore and have fun.

Guild Conduct
1) Be respectful of one another. Even if you just died, lost a level or whatever.
2) Conduct yourself in such a way that the rest of Norrath views the Tolkien's Revenge in a positive way.
3) Heed the advise of officers and leaders. They are only looking out for you and the rest of the guild.
4) When forming groups, be sure to define loot polices and share equally. Don't be a ninja-looter!
5) If you need help to do a quest or corpse retrieval, ask around but don't be annoying about it. Sometimes people are very busy at the moment and may not be able to help right away.
6) If you are having a dispute with another member, keep it in personal tells. Don't spam the guild. If you can't settle it among yourselves, contact a guild officer.
7) Members within the level range of the dungeon we are raiding are required to show.
8) All officers must attend meetings.

*We currently have invitations into the guild on hold, unless there is a special circumstance.

As we look to the future to plane, dragon and deity raids, it is important that we learn to work with one another now. The events and raids we do now help to train us for future high level encounters. As such, due to the high risks that are involved, some guidelines are necessary. Remember, your group and guild's safety depends on you. Not every raid is going to benefit every member. Sometimes raids are to help players with very tough quests, sometimes they are just to go out and try new things. Not everyone is going to get something every raid, but even so, we encourage you to come out and participate.

Raid Organization
1) Raids will try to accomodate as many members as is possible. This may mean that sometimes members may not always be able to participate.
2) Participating members will be split up into groups before entering the designated raid area. These groups will try to be as even and balanced as possible.
3) Each group will have a leader. The group leader is responsible for making sure everyone knows their role within the group and to call for help from another group if they need it.
4) Each group will have a designated puller. Only that puller pulls for that group. At times, there may even be a puller for two or more groups. Everyone in the group is to assist the puller. Make a hotkey. This is vital if an enchanter is present.
5) If a pull goes bad, and the group is about to be wiped out and no evac was able to be done, stay put. If you run around trying to escape, you may endanger another group besides your own.
6) Class specific loot will be randomed among the classes at the raid that can use it. Only if such a class is not present, will it be randomed among the rest of the participating members.
7) Generic loot will be ffa. Don't be a ninja looter! Share...
8) Raids will usually take place at some very interesting places. Curb your desire to sight-see. If you wander around, you may accidentally aggro mobs and jeopardize your group.
9) Listen to your raid officers and group leader. If you find the guidelines too difficult to abide by, you can choose to leave or may be asked to leave. If this is the case, arrangements will be made to leave in the safest way possible.
10) During raids, guild chat or ooc may be used to communicate between groups. If you are not participating in a raid, and if you find the raid spam annoying, please turn off guild chat. We will use ooc only if there are adventurers participating in the raid that are not members of our guild.

As the Tolkien's Revenge continues to adventure in the lands of Norrath, these guidelines may need to be revised. If you have any questions about this or just have a question in general, please feel free to contact a guild officer.
