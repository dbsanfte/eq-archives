Update :: 30/03/04 :: Szarin

Why is it some people have so much trouble with technology? I mean sure, some of it might be a little harder to grasp than others. However this just blows me away. I was at the liquor store yesterday (no surprise there :D ) and i was second in line of about 4 or 5 ppl total. The guy infront of the line went to pay for his flat of beer (24 cans for those need some clarification). He went to pay using his interact card, but for some reason only known to God he couldn't figure it out. We're not talking about the casier iether and how to ring the sale through. This guy had a problem with the concept of a piece of plastic that held a 4 or 5 digit number tied to several bank accounts to confirm one's actions with a keypad. This simple concept to everyone else was over this guy's head. After three attempts the guy flipped out because they were invalid PIN numbers. He threw his hands up in the air yelled something that no one around could understand, then proceed to run out the door. The cashier look puzzled and told the guy that it was the right PIN number finally but wrong account. The next two attempts were the wrong bank account again. On the 6th attempt he *only* got it right because the cashier had to walk him through it. It had been at least 15 mins by now and whether it was pure fustration of being a complete idiot or just imbarassed infront of others he figured... kicking the door with my foot will solve my problems... and with that... BANG he kicked the glass door with his work boots as the keypad finally said "APPROVED - Thank you."
