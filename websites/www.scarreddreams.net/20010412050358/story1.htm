This is a story of Tolkien's first journey to the Icelands Vellious. It began as a rumor of great treasures and mysteries. This is account through the eyes of Iksar Legionnaire. It may have not shared the same emotions or views from the other members, but I believe every one of us was so nervous that are words to each other were very limited. And so the journey begins....

We met at the new docks constructed at the Northern Dessert of Ro, Boraron our mighty leader requested for five of the officers to accompany him to do some re-con for good hunting grounds. After arriving we were confronted by a small gnome who asked of our intentions and how much we would pay for a ride that could only be provided by these. Well…unique gnomes to say the least. Kalanar gave him a small bag filled with platinum and a rather rudely walked passed him without a word, not knowing how to react the rest of us followed him without a word onto a small pirate running skipper. We reached a small island where a great boat awaited us. Along with a few others we know from other journeys. As the boat was making its final preparations to take off two the gnomes came for our crates and said they should be taken downstairs. Namtrow did not react kindly; "I know what will happen to my ales if they are left in the care of your kind". He bluntly stated this as he picked the two gnomes into the air and dropped them back down, Namtrow handed them a few bottles of ale and told them to be gone. The gnomes seemed to realize their theft was not going to work and quickly accepted the few bottles Namtrow offered them. Mallabik seemed to take an offence to the "your kind" comment but Namtrow quickly reassured him he was speaking of pirates not of gnomes as a race.

The boat finally set sail, I can only wonder why I agreed to this mission, as Namtrow was sitting their at home in the freezing weather, drinking ale and singing barbarian war songs. Boraron, Kalanar, Ziff and Mallabik where joining him with cheer...I simply sat their freezing my tail off. No one seemed to notice that my ale was slowly freezing to my hands, and as a warrior I had too much pride to ask for a blanket.

Then it happened, we made the docks of vellious, but there was nothing there but ice. I didn't like this at all, but of coarse as you can expect if you know those Barbarians and Dwarves, they were just setting up a fire and popping open a few more bottles. I was more than happy to help them with the fire. :+)

After passing gnomes, outposts and lands filled with polar cougars we reached our first obstacle. A bridge that was mile long, it was no normal bridge though. It was a series of icebergs that did not meet each other. It was necessary to swim a short distance from one to the next. By the time I reached the other side of this chain of icebergs I was chugging ale like the rest just to stay warm. We ventured slowly and carefully for 2 more days as Mallabik constantly wrote on empty pages of his spell book making maps of the land. Then the unimaginable happened, giants, intelligent giants, well organized like nothing we had ever seen before, and we were ambushed! What to do? We quickly made formation myself, Boraron, and Namtrow quickly made a triangle with the others in the middle. Mallabik had to cast levitate from a ring Sindarax gave him just before we left. He couldn't see over us it was funny, but at the time I didn't seem to be laughing. As giant after giant dropped we found ourselves becoming low on mana and health. We quickly rushed west hoping that there was somewhere to run closer than the regions to the east we had just left. We found what seemed to be an abandoned fort, Boraron shouted "There...We shall make our stand there"

It proved to be a big mistake. As we entered and the giants followed we set up death holes firing awesome rounds of arrows wiping a handful of giants out before we ran out of ammo, we rushed to the old thrown room of this fortress, when we discovered it was not abandoned! This was the giant's fort, they had ambushed us and lured us to their fort. This was the battle ever warrior dreams for, life or death, one way or the other it was a glorious battle. As Ziff shouted "Outta mana guys", the lord of these giants plunged from the top floor into the center of the group and knocked me across the room, at least a dozen giants busted through the door. "Mallbik shouted "EVAC!!!" and "Drexx get yer butt back over here now!!! EVAC!!!" the giant lord seemed to know what Mallabik meant, as he went straight for mall and picked him up, with Mallabik in one hand the giant was fighting Boraron with the other. I didn't know it at the time but Mallabik invised Ziff halfway though the battle so he could med; all these events would be our only savior.

Slamming against the wall with that much force, I remembered the new discipline taught to me by war baron Eatar. I quickly rose to the ground, everything was blurry, I recall little of the next few minutes, only that 3 giants stood between my party, and me. Several seconds later they did not. I screamed with all my might at the massive giant lord, as it turned to me Boraron leapt into the air and plunged his Soulfire into the back of the giant lord's heart. Ziff tumbled and leapt into the air as I saw Kalanar cracking the skull of a giant coming after Ziff. As Mallabik stood up sparks flew from his hands and a rainbow of colors shimmered everywhere. When I came to my senses I found ourselves on top of the wizard platform of North Ro. With only a few platinum gained after the ride from the boat, a few meager weapons from the giants, and the loss of one Soulfire. The blade was left in the giant lords back because we evacuated before anyone could grab it, we simply couldn't hold out to these giants any longer.... BEWARE the opponents of Vellious are nothing like you have ever seen....
