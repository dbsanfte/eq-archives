Many years ago in a land once known as East Commons there was a man named Tolkien who owned a supply store. Tolkien made many friends over the years of selling supplies to many of the adventures in the area. Tolkien made a promise to himelsef, not to be racist against any one that visited his little store. He sold to Dark elfs and Orge's as did he to humans. One day a few of his closest friends were on their way to visit Tolkien as they came aroudn the corner of the tunnel entrance they noticed his hut in ruins, burnt to the ground still smoldering. They ran quickly to give any assistance needed. However nothing could be done for Tolkien. He lay under his desk burnt. It was then the friends bonded together to seek the revenge of their friend Tolkien.
