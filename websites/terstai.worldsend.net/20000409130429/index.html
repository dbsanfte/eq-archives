Mourn the Passing of Gerard into the Shades 02/20/2000
We gathered at the ruins of Ters'Tai to hear the words that struck us all a staggering blow. Chey informed those gathered that the Shades of the Ters'Tai had called upon the High Lord to join them as it was his time to travel to lands beyond Norrath. The High Commander, Chey Elk'hrt, was passed the sacred talisman and the responsibility of the High Lord and Lord Midhir Gwylthcvinthil took on the responsibilities of the High Commander.

To offset such a tragic loss we happily welcomed three of our initiates into the Ters'Tai as full members. Welcome and congratulations to Baramek, Quasiir and Shadin.

Do to our growth and increasing responsibilities of the Council, it was decided to delegate areas of responsibility to each of the Council members. Lord Alyte was named Master Trainer, Lady Cahla was named Chancelloress of Membership, Lady Shontara was named Curator of Archives/Knowledge & Lord Toke was named Commissar of Events.

Another page of the prophecy is turned and as it was foretold in a time of prosperity and happiness the followers of the Ters'Tai would lose one held close to all their hearts. But with courage, honor and friendship they shall prevail over the darkness once again.

-High Commander Midhir Gwylthcvinthil of the Ters'Tai
