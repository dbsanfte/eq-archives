The Wedding To Remember!
[05/08/2000] Lady Cahla Riavu'Ald
We proudly announce that Livvy and Banyan Leucetios are now in wedded bliss!  After a confusing start, the wedding went off without a hitch.  Well, Banyan DID try to escape once, just as the ceremony began!  Blamed it on LinkDeath, sure Bannie!  Unfortunately, Lili seemed to be confused about the date and was strangely absent.  After many tries, Apprentice Guide Echelon appeared to perform a delightful ceremony.  He seemed to know Banyan and Livvy without ever meeting them!  "Do you promise to stand by Banyan, no matter how large or scary the trains may be?" And "Do you promise to stand by Livvy, no matter how many times the spells fizzle?"  It was a riot!  Not to mention the pre-wedding floor show provided by Graegar and Tedule of Winterblood.  Thanks for the "Fists of Death" showdown!  A wonderful time was had by all, and we appreciate the patience of all our distinguished guests.
