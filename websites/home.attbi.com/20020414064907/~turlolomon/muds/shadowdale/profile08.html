Turlina Lomon

 

Most people think that my first Shadowdale character was Turlo. He was my second. The first was his table-top mother, Turlina Lomon. I created her as a thief/sorcerer, and never had her pass first level. The sorcerer rules at the time were very buggy, and made no sense for anyone to use. I never created another sorcerer even after they completely revamped the system.
