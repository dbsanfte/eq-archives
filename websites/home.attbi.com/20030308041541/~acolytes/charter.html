What are the Acolytes?

The Acolytes are a family, are a group of friends who are united with a common goal.  A goal evil, neutral, and good players can all work towards together.  We exist in Norrath, the fantasy world of EverQuest, to escape the realm known as "Real Life."

What is our goal?

The Acolytes strive to command respect throughout the realms of Norrath.  All of Norrath shall know of the power the Acolytes have by the monsters we defeat and the bounty we receive in return.  We are here to be a mid-game to end-game guild.

What principles do the Acolytes embrace?

Respect, Excellence, Friendship.  If you need an explanation, this guild is not for you.
