Rogues In EverQuest

 

For the record, this was written way before the current implementation of rogues. Many of these issues have been resolved by Verant, and I look forward to what the future will hold.

I have spent quite a great deal of time reviewing website with people complaining about rogues. I have seen their petitions. I have read their surveys. And I have come to one conclusion. Power gamers, every single one. Playing a rogue is not about who can do the most damage, or who can solo what, it is about style. Style is everything for a rogue. Now, after reviewing a particularly power gamer influenced petition, I decided to create this section on my website. Please read my commentary below on several different subjects, and above all else, give me YOUR feedback. A simple petition with multiple choice answers does not give the proper results of a fully discussed forum.

POISON

Ah, the big controversy of poison. Reviewing recent questions I have seen asked, I have my own opinions. Let's start with the vials themselves. People argue that ruined pelts should be allowed to make vials. I disagree. They are ruined after all. However, regular poison vials should not require skins at all. Why do people only see one slide of an entire picture show? Go fig. I would like to see Sealed Vials implemented. I am not sure what would be put in them, but based on what I see now, it would be well worth the wait.

Now, regarding the poison itself. All poison components should stack. Right now, about...oh...half, maybe more, stack. In addition, and this is very important, poison itself should stack. This would allow the rogue to choose from a variety of poisons and actually stack up for an outing. Right now, you can fill your entire inventory and it will be gone after 1 hour of game play. Is this unbalancing? Some might think so, but I disagree. Think about the cost and time involved to prepare all of this. Most rogues I have seen don't use poison. I can't understand why. It really does seem to make a difference in my career.

All this business about supplying vendors with poison and increasing drop rates of components I am impartial to. I have no problem scrounging up the components I need, but it would be nice to have some practice poisons to train up apply poison with. That I leave in the hands of the implementors of the game.

BUGS & "FEATURES"

Ok, there are a few rogue specific bugs in the game, or so people claim. Some are just plain ignorance on the way the game works, but I have suggestions on how to fix it, and allow rogues to be placated.

Hotkeys locking up when a weapon procs, usually during a pickpocket attempt (timing is everything), needs to be addressed. This has been around since day one and has frequently affected both myself and others. I consider this bug to be the most serious.

Now, bugs related to poison. Poison can be interrupted or fizzle. I have had both happened. The reason for this is the way it was implemented, namely as a spell attached to your primary weapon slot, and not to the weapon itself. Although I am not too crazy about this particular implementation technique, it works, and prevents us from passing around our trade specific item. However, these fizzes and interrupts need to be fixed. We are already paying through the nose to use poison, yet I don't see a mage having to rebuy the spell ever time his fizzles. I would like this to be fixed as well.

Finally, and this is just a misinterpretation of the facts, is that hide and sneak fail randomly. I used to think that as well until I did some testing. If you right click a mob, if you loot a corpse, etc. it will remove the hide without giving you a message. Easy fix for this...give us the You are no longer hidden message when we perform such an action.

HIDE & SNEAK

The best fix for my rogue was allowing the hide/sneak combo in EverQuest. I truly enjoy this. Now, I have discussed a few issues with Mr. McQuaid via e-mail regarding why certain things we done a certain way, and the game has evolved quite a bit while continuing to strive toward game balance. (No, I am not an employee of Verant, I am a gamemaster in table top.)

I personally feel that hide/sneak should be allowed to cross zone lines. This is allowed in invisibility spells, and should carry over to us. Balance is the key feature, though. Invisibility has a random expire time. However, our limitation is speed. Although I would like to see a speed increase with level for sneak, it is not needed. Keep it as the balancing factor.

See invisibility should still see us. Why? Several reasons. Keep the game code simple. Allow people who are hidden together to see each other. Shall I go on? Undead should still see us. They don't have eyes, per say, but rather have an ability to sense the life force of a being. That is why there is a separate spell for invisibility toward undead. It masks your life force instead of your image.

Also, camping is an issue. Already if you camp while sneaking, you remain sneaking when you come back in. What about hiding? This would be useful to us with horrible ISPs (Curses to you US West!) who loose connection. This would keep me in hide mode and allow me to reconnect safely instead of in a city where I was bound.

Finally, all these spells and song effects that don't affect you...well...they do if you are a rogue. "JohnSmith tries to cast a spell on you but you are protected" can mean an instant death warrant to a rogue. I know. I had someone (more than once actually - different someones) do this to me intentionally in a hostile environment. Great way to PK without having the flag. Guides will not reimburse for that either. My point though...if they don't affect you, they shouldn't affect you! This should be fixed as well.

Now, a possible improvement to the skill, which would eliminate our need for an escape skill like so many other classes have, is to allow us to stop on a dime and hit hide instead of waiting 2-3 seconds. Hide should remove us from the aggression list of the mobs, similar to feign death. Something like this was abused during beta. My suggested fix, which looks like is already in the game, is to have backstab, instill doubt, any combat skill...to black out the hide and sneak buttons for 5 seconds. This would allow us to have to be in a defensive posture for us to hide instead of doing it immediately in combat.

THROWING WEAPONS

Throwing weapons definitely need to be improved. For the cost of, oh, a shuriken (1 sp), on a crit I can do a maximum of 15 damage. With a bow, I can hit for 20 or so on a regular basis. Considering the range on a throwing weapon, you can get one shot off for a pull. Makes it kinda useless other than pulling a mob.

I have seen several suggestions on sites, but most seem power-gamish. Increase crit totals, allow poisoning, increase amount thrown in a volley, etc. So far, the biggest one that attracts my attention is to allow the poisoning of thrown weapons. This would keep it separate from a bow, in that it requires a thrown weapon instead of an arrow. Why not poison arrows? Balance. And balance is something I can live with.

SKILL IDEAS

I can not believe the junk I have seen people post suggesting as skill ideas for Verant to implement. Some of them are just plain silly. I do have a suggestions for one new skill though. The only difference is, I concentrate on balance, and implementation code wise. Must be that good ol' MUD experience I have had over the years.

DISGUISE: (Suggested Level - 35) Allows the creation of one use, nodrop (custom made for the rogue), non-lore, masks used to cast the equivalent enchanter spell. These masks would be created in a makeup kit, four or six slot design, needing base, arabic glue, and two to four components to make the exact mask needed. Add the supplies to our local poison vendor. No need to create more shopkeepers. The makeup case itself could be a quest item requiring a mask of the deceiver or something. Pros: Fits in with background of master of stealth, definitely roguish, cost will be limiting factor. Cons: With this implementation, it seems hard to abuse. Please help me out here.

RANDOM THOUGHTS ON REMAINING SKILLS

Hit points of our class should be raised to equal the hybrid classes. We are a melee class after all. Forgot all that nonsense about bonus hps at certain levels and all that. I also was very disappointed when Ravenscale Armor was not implemented as a quest armor along with the rest of the classes. Instill doubt needs a bit of tweaking, but I believe Verant has already started to take a good look at it.
