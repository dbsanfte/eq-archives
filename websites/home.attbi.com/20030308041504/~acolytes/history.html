The Birth of the Acolytes

Many Moons ago, in the land of Antonica a tiny guild was born known as the “Crimson Acolytes”.  The guild was formed in the Area of East Commons.  Sacred Scrolls named the leader and founder as Kotara Thistlewind, known to others as Nastradamus.  At the time eight People joined Kotara on her quest to battle the evils of Norrath.  The scrolls mention these names as the ancient ones: Fallan, Panteradomain, Nazareth (aka Kirbee) Paulr, Drragorr, Mellyna, Sinat, Khallim, Dinnin, Dalrok, Scartlet, Ixben and Superhoss.

The Death Of the Crimson Acolytes

As time passed the small guild suffered a lack of new members.  Many left the guild to seek their own paths. With this our leader Kotara Thistlewind  began to leave the guild to seek out a realm know to many as Real Life.  She then passed the Sacred Fire of leader ship to Fallan the Cleric.

Being that most of the scrolls where Damaged we determined that an Evil Race known as the Varent destroyed what was left of the guild.  And many where scattered into the four winds.

Acolytes Reborn

Many of those that remained began to hold a secret meeting in the tunnels of East Commons.  There was word that the reminisce of the guild could join “The Celestial temple”.  But some protested and called for the rebirth of the Acolytes.  With this Fallan call upon the gods to grant the guild new life.  Within a few Moons the guild was reformed as “The Acolytes Reborn”.  Not wanting the same fate as the old guild, Fallan, Scartlet, Khallim, Ixben, Mellyna, and a few others began a massive recruiting quest to bring great strength to the guild.

Soon the guild had once again became strong and our quest for greatness continues
