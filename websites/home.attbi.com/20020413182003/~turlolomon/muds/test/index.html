Test MUD

 

With all the experience I have had playing MUDs and other online games, a lot of people forget how much experience I have being an administrator on them as well. Now, I have decided to become a full fledge implementor, and not co-implementor at that. Every decision with the code is mine, and mine alone. Do I expect this to be a popular mud? No. Do I care? No. My code will eventually be perfected, and at that point I might, but it is a never ending project, and one I am enjoying immensely.

The source code is derived from Circle MUD, a derivitive of Diku MUD. I have successfully got it to compile on the Windows platform, and I am currently working on it with a fine tooth comb. At this time, no files are public, but perhaps in time. At least I wanted to give a little background..
