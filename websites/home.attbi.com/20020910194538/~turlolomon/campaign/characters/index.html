Citizens of Lomon Manor

A path leads to a fortified castle, flags billowing in the wind. At the gate, an armoured soldier greets you. Meet the characters and non-player characters that form the table top Dungeons & Dragons campaign that has been going on since the early 1980s. A short description after each character only gives a taste at their true being. Welcome to the Lomon Chronicles. These pages were generated using the AD&D Core Rules Expansion 2.0 software.

Turlina Lomon: My dear, beloved mother. Her current whereabouts are unknown to all.

Turlo Lomon: The main character of course. Me at a very young age.

Zerai Githson Atreides: Dean of Assassin's Mountain, an elite school for the wealthy, and Regent for Lomon Manor, my home. He is one of the people that helped raise me, and thus, I call him Uncle Zerai

Z'hindsargh Githson Atreides: Younger brother of Zerai. He is an instructor at the school and twin to Zir'con. Lovingly called the Lightbulb by his friends.

Z'hinsson Atreides: Son of Z'hindsargh. He showed up in town one day with him and no one has had the guts to ask him where he came from.

Zir'con Githson Atreides: Younger brother of Zerai. He is the senior instructor for weapon mastery at the school and twin to Z'hindsargh. He longs to meet his match in battle to end is pitiful life (his words). I personally think he just needs a hug.
