Shadowdale MUD

 

Ah, the wonderful world of Toril, in yet another incarnation. To bad it went poof. Rumor is that they received a cease and desist order because they had too much copywrited material on it. Since I was an administrator there, I would have to agree. Some areas were plain copies of original works, and the code was not as original as the creator wanted us to believe. I was not active near the bitter end. That is why I have to rely on rumors that have been forwarded by players from the MUD.

There is a lot of informaton I collected over the years playing this MUD. However, one thing I have promised coming is why I left. So, without further ado...
