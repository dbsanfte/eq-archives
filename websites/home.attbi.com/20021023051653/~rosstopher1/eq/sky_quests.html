The Quest Hall - Index
To get to the quest hall, purchase the Key of Veeshan (looks like a jade and costs 1cp) from Key Master on island 1. Put it on your cursor and click on the center (north) teleport pad. The only way to exit the quest hall is to either jump down to EFP, or to make a body on the top of the island before you click on the teleport pad to enter the quest hall, and then have it revived when finished.

The Efreeti Chain - Index
No, not the ones on which you fall and die. Efreeti include the 3 efreeti in the Efreeti Chain: Noble Dojorn (Dojorn Island), Overseer of Air (Island 4), and Hand of Veeshan (Island 8). Note: The islands with the windmills are the ones on which the Efreeti spawn. In fact, the windmills are their spawn points. Efreeti also drop the Golden Efreeti armor (all/all but best for pure casters and druids due to the weight (too heavy for monks) and stats as all other classes have better available armor) and Wind Etched Armor of Ro (paladin-only armor).

    • Quest Drop Item Information - Index
    Normally, you can think of the quest rewards as being made from the items you return. With few exceptions, the hardest item to get (from the boss mob or from an efreeti) will be the same type of item as is the quest reward. For example, if the quest reward is a mask of some kind, chances are very high that the hardest item to get will also be a mask of some kind. Rarely, the reward item is the same type as the non-hardest piece to get.

    If the reward is a weapon, it will usually require an efreeti-dropped weapon, unless the quest item happens to be a non-efreeti weapon and is dropped from an island boss (less common).
