Garrett

 

Loosely based on the character Garrett from the Glen Cook series revolving around the same name, Garrett was yet another incarnation of the warrior/thief dual class I had yet to perfect. Trying to stay in character was a difficult task, and Shana, goddess of rangers, and wanna-be future designer of the Garrett zones, was pressurring me to work on the zone design with her. The character was retired without any serious game play.
