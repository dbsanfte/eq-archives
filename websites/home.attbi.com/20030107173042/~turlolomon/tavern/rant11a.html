The Charter and a tad bit more....

Guided by Voices

The Charter: I am not here to tell you about our skills, or how we rule the lands of Norrath, instead I think I will let our actions speak for themselves. We are different then most guilds, which my soul purpose in creating it. We are a hard group to classify, Call us role-player? That wouldn't be correct we don't role-play 24/7 and we don't require our members to have role-playing skills, we are who we choose to be when we choose to do so. Are we power gamers? No we certainly are not, power gamers are classified as people who wish to level as fast as they possibly can or get all the items in the game as fast as they can and we are far from that. So who are Guided by Voices exactly? We are the madmen in the dark corner of your bedroom; we have no reason, rhyme or purpose for why we are who we are. We don't wear a label nor classified in a category. The ONLY people we are loyal to are our members and friends in the guild or the game, We formed this guild to play together AS a guild doing the fun things that a lot of people forget about while waiting on lists or leveling. As far as paths, we don't have one....... we are only Guided by Voices. The future is in our hands.

History: The Guild was founded by Alacard, and Myself. We formed the guild to make a different in the world of Norrath, Friends since the beginning of time it seems; we seek only members worthy of the guild tag.

Rules: There are not many rules that make up the heart of this democracy, The Guild itself is ruled by the people and Guided by the Voices within, There is however an assembly made up of the 6 Officers in the guild, although regular members have just as much say as the assembly (in some cases) The assembly is there primarily to organize the guild and to mold it into what we wish. The Assembly members will be the leaders in our groups in raids, and since the guild is so new the rules are subject to change. But one thing that will not change will be the respect for each other. Respect isn't demanded it is EXPECTED. Also We aren't here to start trouble with others, So trouble makers wont be tolerated. HOWEVER If a guild or members have Beef, we will take care of it immediately. Like I said we aren't here to cause trouble but we also aren't here to put up with ignorance. And enemies that threaten our reputation will be disposed of =)

The Assembly so far...

Vermathrax-Leader and Co-founder
Alacard-Leader and Co-founder (not to be confused with Allacard) =)
Moldo First Officer
Turlo First Officer

I have the Other Officers picked out, they will be joining soon, just clearing red tape.... =P

Well all I hope that you all agree with what I have said, we have a long road ahead people that stick with it will be rewarded, If not by anything else they will be rewarded with friendship.

Well That's all folks!
