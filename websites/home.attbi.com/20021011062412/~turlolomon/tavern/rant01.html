Company Politics

 

You have heard of it. You more than likely have experienced it. But why does it exist? There is no reason behind it. Take this for an example. A department fudges some data to make their numbers look good, QA (me) catches it, and slams it back in their face. They start whining to the VP to change it back. VP makes decision. I am forced to go back on my original decision. Now, what are we dealing with here? Would you believe determining the Y2K status of a network of systems? What kind of bullshit is that? Aren't we trying to do a job right? Jesus. It makes me sick. We have to do a bunch of work to get ourselves in gear, and what do you know, people want to make their goals for that bonus in the fall, and start screwing around. What happens if the system fails you fucking morons?!?

I have encountered situations like this more than I could even care about. I do my job. I do it well. I am a results oriented type of person. I don't care how it gets done, but get it done. I meet all of my deadlines. I can back up every single one of my decisions. Is that enough? NO! Freakin' whiny punks who only care about money start getting involved and it screws up the whole system. How can a company run like this? Well, most do. No, scratch that. All companies do, although to different levels.

What can we do to prevent this kind of bullshit? Nada. Nothing. It sucks. This is the problem with companies. They care about the bottom line. The employees care about their bottom line. The quality of the service and product suffer because of it. I personally like hard-ass managers. One's who look at the finished product and see that I have done an amazing job. It's these freakin' Teflon managers that drive me absolutely nuts. Say one thing to someone, change it to someone else. Always playing the middle ground, aiming just to please, to kiss ass, to get ahead. What about the company?

Every single decision I make, every single day I work, is for the benefit of the company. I step on toes. I get manager's reamed for incompetence. I don't put up with shit. However, following corporate procedure, I have my hands tied most of the times. A manager made a mistake of stepping into my territory with a screw-up and now she is planning to leave the company. Good! I don't care if she was good looking, or had a great smile. She fucked up. She got chewed out by a director. And took it as a personal vendetta against me. Too bad I covered myself in backup to prove my point. Don't fuck with my job. Do your own. I have seen too many people who concentrate on making others look bad instead of actually doing their own work. I feel my work speaks for myself. It's accurate, well documented, and shows I care about a job. The funny thing is, I don't! I don't give a damn about any one job or another. I do the best job I can based on what I consider is my personal work ethics. I usually do contract work, and I give the customer value for every cent they pay. At least I try. Sometimes, when the rope gets tighter around my wrists, it gets frustrating.

Does this go on in just corporations? Nope. I have seen it in different spots. My second job. Barback - cleaner-upper type dude. It is a thankless job, but I find it relaxing. You have people screwing each other for tips. Customers being treated a lot better than they should because they are friends with the owner. I am sorry. If someone is drunk, cut them off! But, they look at profit. Politics comes into play when one customer, who is most likely drunk, makes a complaint about the bartender or waitress, and the owners have to step in to chew out the employee. Is the customer always right? I am sorry, but I don't think so. I know my SO doesn't agree, but I feel a bartender has every freakin' right in the world when they are in charge, and should feel no restraint about kicking someone out if they had too much. They are effectively the manager of the place when the owners are not there. Shoot, most owners are previous employees of the bar. Do they actually know how to run a business? NOT! Man, I see screw-ups all the time, mostly based on catering to their friends. The political influence in this case.

What can we, as patriotic Americans, do to help the problem with Company Politics? The truth is...nothing. Just bend over and hope they use a little Vaseline. It ain't going away, so you might as well get used to it.
