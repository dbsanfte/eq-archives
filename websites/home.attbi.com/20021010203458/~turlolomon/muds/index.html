Dungeons of MUD

 

The gate before you opens to the unknown. Before there was EverQuest, Ultima Online, or Asheron’s Call, there was the wonderful world of MUDs. MUD stands for Multi-User Dungeon. They are an on-line game that is in text form, similar to the old Zork adventures. You must use telnet to access them. A telnet client is useful to assist in play. Below, I have included some files on worlds I have ventured into.
