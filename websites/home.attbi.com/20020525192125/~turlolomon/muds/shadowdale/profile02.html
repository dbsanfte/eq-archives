Remigius

 

Studying the ways of the panther in the wild, Remigius took his analysis and perfected it into a form of martial arts. He traveled across the countryside, helping travelers in need, and defending those unable to take up arms. He took his personal quest very seriously. After a time, his travels took him deeper and deeper into the wilderness. Challenges were over come, and new enemies were vanquished. Before reaching the height of his power, the world was ripped asunder, and his soul was lost among thousands of others.

OOC: Remigius was loosely based on a character from Pillars of the Earth. This was my first monk character, and it seemed rather interesting. He was lost when SD2 crashed.
