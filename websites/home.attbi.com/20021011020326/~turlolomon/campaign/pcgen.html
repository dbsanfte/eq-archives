PC Gen

Have you visited your local gaming store lately? Take a look at just how many d20 games are now on the shelves. The open license agreement is pretty sweet, if you ask me. It is bringing back the creativity we used to see during the time of Judges Guild and Mayfair Games. What does this have to do with a computer program? Glad you asked...

PC Gen is a program designed for character creation and DM assistance using d20 games. Which game, you ask? All of them! Well, at least the ones that have been converted. PC Gen does not give you enough information to just use rules from any game book you have loaded (and there are lots). It gives you just enough information to make a choice if you are familiar with the rules. How is this different? Well, if you find something that catches your eye, it will tell you who published, and even includes links to their websites! What a fantastic marketing tool! I even heard some companies are supplying the conversion files themselves. JOY!

Now, the important stuff. Where can you get PC Gen? It's easy. Just follow this link: http://pcgen.sourceforge.net/
