Burning MUD

 

This is an interesting world, with a unique character leveling system. You start off single class, then at 50, you can become duel class. By choosing the same class twice, you gain access to special abilities. There are also many quest mobs throughout the game. Best of all, travel to different locales can be gained by various means, be it guide, coach, or teleport. Each, of course, has a corresponding expense. Best of all, there is a bank to store extra equipment at. The address is:
