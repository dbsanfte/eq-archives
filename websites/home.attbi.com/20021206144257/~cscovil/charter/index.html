The Golden Dawn Guild Charter

History speaks of a secret society that sought out forbidden knowledge. Free thinkers who walked the line between the light and the dark -- understanding the need for balance. They carried their torches bravely into the mysterious corners of Norrath. Many have searched for enlightenment and many have failed -- but it is carried upon soft breath that those now known as the Golden Dawn have reached a new chapter, gone further, and no longer require torches to breach the darkness.


The Golden Dawn encourages role playing and behaving in a manner becoming of a mature and responsible player. This does not mean you have to play your character as a mature person, simply that you should adhere to the rules of fair play and respect the established etiquette.

Rules of The Golden Dawn Guild

1. RESPECT. You will respect all of your fellow Golden Dawn members especially the guild officers. I can not stress the importance of this rule enough. Please re-read it a few times until it sticks.

2. LANGUAGE. Foul language will be frowned upon. Use it sparingly if at all. Read rule #1. (please also see guild chat protocol below)

3. BEGGING. No Golden Dawn member should be found begging for money or items.

4. SPAMMING. Do not spam a zone with repeated shouts or through any other available channel.

5. DISPUTES. All disputes should be taken to private tells and if it is internal to the guild should involve at least one officer to help mediate the issue. The officer's final decision shall end the dispute. Further disruption will lead to investigation and possible removal from the guild.

6. FAIR PLAY. We as a guild shall adhere to all rules of fair play. Do not kill steal. Honor camp sites.

7. EXPLOITS. There are certain exploits that Golden Dawn can not condone nor condemn. If you are taking advantage of such an exploit please be aware that you are a representative of our guild. Your actions reflect how others see us as a whole. You also run the risk of being banned by Verant in some situations.

8. TWINKING. We do not twink our recruits. Do not join us expecting to be outfitted with high level gear. We may, if we so choose, decide to award gifts to those we think deserve them.

9. PURCHASING ACCOUNTS (E-BAY). The Golden Dawn guild does not support the purchasing (or selling) of accounts on-line. If you are playing a high level toon along side your guild mates without the necessary skill you are endangering your friends.


Failure to adhere to the above rules will result in a meeting of the officeers. Upon review of the situation and with the casting of votes you may be removed from the guild or have a mark on your history (which can effect promotions, etc.)


Guild Chat Protocol

The Golden Dawn would like to foster an open channel for communication among all of our members. We ask that you use common courtesy when chatting. If someone informs you that they are uncomfortable with the current topic it's your duty to change the subject or discontinue the conversation.

Our ultimate goal is to give everyone a sense that it's okay to speak up and say when something is making them feel uneasy. We also want to give our mature members the freedom to speak in a more adult manner if it is, at the time, acceptable (this is very common during late evening hours.)

Also please think before bringing a controversial subject up in guild chat. Subjects that are specific about guild politics, or will require discussion at great length, are best left to the guild's private message board.

It is your responsibility to keep the atmosphere of the guild fun, relaxed, and open minded. Bad attitudes, egos, and immaturity can be checked at the door.
