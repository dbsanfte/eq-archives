Y2K Compliance

Why in God's name would I need a Year 2000 Compliance statement on my web site? Well, I used to research the Year 2000 Compliance of various pieces of equipment and software for a particular company I worked for on contract, and a friend of mine from there sent me an amusing email. Sorry, but I had to edit a portion of this email to protect his identity (and his job). Just so you know, it was done in exact protocol of the company we both worked for.

Dear Sir,

Thank you for your interest in Lomon Manor, a wholly owned subsidiary of TLC Enterprises. We at Lomon Manor believe in producing the finest products possible in the virtual community.
