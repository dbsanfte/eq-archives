Satan's Little Helpers

I was having lunch one day with my beloved Laura. Why is this unusual you say? Laura is a night person. Me dragging her out to lunch was quite an accomplishment. Tired, out of it, barely eating...yup, that’s Laura at lunchtime. After getting my burger and sitting down at a window table, Laura started going off on an interesting tangent. I attempt to reiterate the contents here over a Long Island Ice Tea and a few shots.

Barney is the Anti-Christ and Pokemon are his minions from hell. And let’s not forget the Tele-Tubbies! How can America sit by while its youth are enraptured by Satan himself? Barney this. Barney that. Can you really trust a six-foot tall dinosaur that likes to hold children? It’s evil I tell you! Fruits of the Dev-il himself.

"I love you. You love me." Wait a second you sick freak. Keep your hands off me. I don’t need a purple freak grabbing on me. Take a guess what happens when the camera is not broadcasting. If we could see that, the true nature of that perverted abomination would be revealed.

What is scarier is all this Pokemon crap. You got to a store, Pokemon. You buy groceries, Pokemon. You go to a bakery, Pokemon. What is the business with that? Ten different Pokemon cakes and only one Winnie-the-Pooh design! That tells you immediately something is very wrong with society. After all, Winnie is the only stuffed animal that should be nominated for Sainthood.

Now, back to Pokemon. Have any of you adults ever paid attention to what it is about? I think not. Cock fighting is frowned upon. This covers dog fights, mantis fights, and others. And what is Pokemon? Glorified cock fighting. Training your captured animals to fight others with down and dirty blood fests. Oh yeah, this is what we want our children to idolize. Think about it. Do you really know what the hell your children watch on TV?

Which brings us to Tele-Tubbies. What the hell is going on there? Now, please, don’t get me wrong. I have nothing against gays what so ever. But these things remind me of Pat from Saturday Night Live. Male? Female? Male? Who knows? But talk about confusing our youth. Sure, it might be politically correct to erase sexual stereo types, but add some damn content. Giggling sexless creatures that tell children how much they love them is disturbing to say the least.

So there we have it: Barney, Pokemon, and Tele-Tubbies all in one rant. Satan should be happy to have such a following. Hopefully, with this new insight, you can protect your children from walking down the path to hell.
