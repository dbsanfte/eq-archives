General Guild Rules

The general basis for these rules are common sense and maturity.

Member Conduct

  • Have FUN!
  • Respect Verant's policies.  Any violation of the PNP is cause for guild removal.
  • Guild chat is casual, but not everything goes.  If a member is offended by your language, please refrain from it.
  • If you are having an issue with another member, keep it in tells.  Contact an officer if necessary for help.
  • Kill Stealing is not tolerated and is cause for guild removal.
  • Ninja looting is not tolerated and is cause for guild removal.
  • Members must listen to officer's decisions. Remember, they are the ones that speak for us.
  • Help other guild members when you can.
  • Guild grouping is encouraged and expected.  Get to know your guild mates and their abilities.

Officer Conduct

  • Officers must follow all rules of conduct bound to regular members
  • Officers are to handle situations professionally.
  • Decisions are to be made aside from personal feelings.
  • Officers are to follow the set procedures when handling situations.
  • If an officer feels a guild removal is necessary until a situation is resolved, they may do so.
  • Officers should have logging on all the time.  In the eqclient.ini file, under the "[Defaults]" section make sure you see the line "Log=TRUE"

Joining Policy

The minimum level to join Acolytes Reborn is currently level 35 for all classes.  Exceptions CAN be made on a case by case basis if necessary.  The level restriction MIGHT be waived for certain classes if we are in need of them.

To join, somebody must sponsor you from our guild.  Upon having sponsorship, you must start a thread with your name in the topic under the recruitment forum on our message boards.  This is where you tell us about yourself, and also where officers may keep notes and dates about when you were invited in game, voted on, etc.  Once accepted in to the guild, you will go through the probationary period as described in these policies.

Other notes about joining:

  • Real life family and friends are an exception to the joining policy.  They will be allowed to join, but are exempt from the level limit.  They will still go through a probationary period, and follow the same rules.
  • We are not a "training" or "feeder" guild.  If you have plans to use the Acolytes as a stepping stone to a higher level guild, you should not apply.
  • We are not an alternate character guild.  It is expected that you guild your main character.  Exceptions to this rule have been and can be made, but are not made often.
  • It is preferred that you raid with us once or twice before becoming invited to the guild as well.  We realize, however, things may stand in the way of doing this.

Probationary Period

Probationary periods are given to all new recruits, and can also be used as a punishment if needed.  The probationary period can last for anywhere from 2 to 4 weeks, or until such time until they meet the minimum requirements joining requirements.  During your probationary period, you are allowed to attend all events.  No probationary member is awarded any loot we receive on raids unless they are the only person there that could use it and the item is NO DROP.  The probationary period is for the guild as a whole to evaluate the recruit on the following:

  1. Personality - Do you fit in with our guild?  Are you friendly and respectful?
  2. Attentiveness - Do you pay attention on raids?  Do you frequent our message boards and contribute to our guild?
  3. Do you follow the principles of the guild, as stated in the guild charter?  These are respect, excellence, and friendship.
  4. Do you adhere to the rules of conduct set forth in our policies?

Alternate Character Policy

Alternate characters ARE allowed in the Acolytes, however, the following stipulations are placed upon alternate characters:

  1. Alternate characters may only be guilded if and only if the main character is NOT on probation.
  2. Alternate characters will not be guilded unless they have reached level 10.  This is to prevent millions of alts getting created and having officers running all over Norrath trying to guild them.  Exceptions can be made on a case by case basis if an officer is nearby and willing to invite you.
  3. Alternate characters will not be allowed in the guild without a main character.  Exceptions to this rule can be made on a case by case basis upon consent of the guild leader and officers.

Officers are to follow a set procedure when handling a situation.  When a situation arises, they are to handle it as quick as possible.

If an officer is contacted from another player regarding a character, they should do the following:

  1. Ensure that logging is on.
  2. Go through the situation politely with the person.
  3. Ensure the person that the problem will be taken care of.
  4. Talk to the guild member in question.
  5. If the guild member is in obvious violation of guild policies, he should be warned and possibly guild removed.
  6. Post the situation, including the excerpt of the conversations from the log file, in the officers forum.
  7. Inform the original petitioner of how the situation was handled, and apologize for the inconvience.

If there is a problem within the guild:

  • Between one or two members over guild chat:
    1. The members should be told to take it to chat or /tells.
    2. The officer should attempt to contact both members and help mediate the problem.
    3. If the members do not discontinue the argument over guild chat, they should be guild removed until they calm down.
  • Between more than two members:
    1. The officer should attempt to calm the situation and ask that the members talk to him in tells or go to the gulid chat room.
    2. If a member becomes abusive, guild remove him and tell him why.
    3. Attempt to mediate the situation in tells, or in the guild chat room.
  • Between most or all of the people online:
    1. Attempt to calm the situation.
    2. Mediate the problem over guild chat.  Attempt to come to a solution to the problem.  If a solution cannot be found, the issue should be taken to the boards.

Raid Policy

Raids can be coordinated by anybody.  To coordinate a raid, simply figure out an objective for the raid, the strategy you will use, provide some links to information (drop information, mob information, etc., any good maps/strategy guides) and email your plan to the Raid Coordinator.  He will then email you back with an approval or disapproval of your plan.  Once your plan is approved, you may post your raid information to the Events board.  Be sure to include all information you sent to the Raid Coordinator.

Any loot awarded on a raid, with exceptions determined by the raid leader and/or an officer, will be the property of the person it is awarded to.  Loot will be awarded with the following rules:

  • If the raid leader has formed the raid for a specific piece of loot to go to a specific person and we obtain that piece of loot, it will be given to the predetermined person.
  • For major loot pieces that are obtained on a raid, the officer who is present will decide who is allowed to roll on it, or in some cases who it is awarded to without rolling.  If an officer isn't present, the raid leader will take this job.  Officers are expected to not play favorites, but to award loot on the basis that the person(s) who the loot is awarded to deserve the loot as well as will return something to the guild in having this loot.
    Example: Lets say we raid Kael and we get several melee armor drops.  It may seem unfair for all of them go to one person, however if this person is generally on each of our raids, and is generally the main tank, it will help the guild out a lot as a whole for this person to be well outfitted.
  • Rolls will generally be rolled with /random 100 unless otherwise specified.
  • Money obtained on raids will go to the guild treasury.
  • All Velious armor gems obtained on a raid will go to the guild treasury.
  • Items that are droppable may be chosen to go the guild treasury to be awarded at a later time.
  • If somebody is awarded an item on a raid, it is appreciated that if they aren't going to use what they had previously that the hand it down to another guild member that needs it.  It is EXPECTED that this will happen if they were using something awarded to them by the guild in the first place.  If something is borrowed from a friend, we understand that you can't give away property that isn't yours.

Guild Treasury

The Guild Treasury is to be used mainly for temporary purposes.  The things that are kept in the guild treasury are as follows:

  • Money: This money comes from raids and donations from members.  It is to be used to aid in paying for spell components used up during guild events
  • Spells: People, at times, will research spells they don't need, or obtain spell drops they don't need.  It is highly appreciated, especially on spells that are hard to find, if you donate it to the guild bank for another caster in the guild to claim.  Almost all 50+ spells are dropped off of high level mobs, and are generally hard to come by.
  • Quest Items: Not many quest items are droppable, but the ones that are that people don't need are welcome to be donated to the guild bank.
  • Items: The items in the guild bank are donated by members and items deemed on raids to be guild property.  If an item is given out, you will be told if it is yours to keep or whether it is still expected to be returned when you are finished with it.  It is expected that if you ask for something out of the guild bank, you will use it and not sell it.  If somebody donated a robe worth 2000pp to the bank, I'm sure the last thing they would want is for somebody to say they'll use it, then sell it.  They could have had that 2k for themselves.  If any member is found to have received an item for the guild for the purpose of selling it, or selling items that are deemed guild property and not awarded to that person, they will be removed from the guild.

Specific item rules and exceptions:

  • Velious quest gems that are in the treasury will not be given to somebody unless they have already obtained the armor drop.
