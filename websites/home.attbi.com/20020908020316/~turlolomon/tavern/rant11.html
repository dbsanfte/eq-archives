There was once a guild known as Guided by Voices. It was led by a necromancer named Vermathrax. We had the beginnings of strong organization, but then a big problem arose. Vermathrax decided to leave EverQuest. When doing so, he forgot to transfer leadership of the guild to a different member, which resulted in a guild without a guildmaster. This eliminated many crucial commands needed to bring a guild up to full strength. Soon afterwards, many guild members quite, leaving just one officer to try to keep it organized. Me. Membership dropped to an all time low of three. I raised it back up to ten, but then it started dropping again. Of note, the members we had at the end were excellent material. Unfortunately, many things brought upon my decision to resign.

The people who joined the guild claimed to love the name. However, with the exception of myself, everyone played the game with anonymous on. This prevented anyone from seeing the name. Lack of pride in a guild harms the officers in charge...namely me. Without pride, why do you belong to it?

When players do a /who all guide, attempting to see if a guide is on EverQuest to assist them in their problems, it pulls up members of Guided by Voices. Since I was the only one on who kept their guild title on, I was bombarded by questions, petition requests, and insults. I have walked the shoes of a guide, and I do not like it.

Numerous times, we had enough players to reform the guide (into Guided by the Voices). One time, we had six of our ten members on at the same time. The response? I am too busy. I am camping. I want to get an item first. Yada, yada, yada. The priority was for personal gain, not the general good of the guild.

The guild devolved into something I did not want to belong to. We rarely grouped together. The closest we had to a guild event was three of us joining up with others to obtain an item for our second most senior member. In the end, because he died, he refused to take the item we worked so hard to obtain.

Finally, during a GM Event, a member was given a special item. Immediately afterwards, he called it a night. How do you think that made your group members feel? Especially when they were the ones who obtained that item on their own before you arrived, and lost a life because of it. Call it a test of loyalty.

Enough of the rants, though. I posted this page because I did not want this guild to be forgotten. It deserves more than that. First off, here is the archive of the message board.

Guided by Voices Archive

I would like to address a few of the things mentioned on the board. Notice how organized we were at the beginning? Everything was going fine. Then...one day...Vermathrax was gone. Not a single good bye. Not fair well posting. Nothing. Thanks Vermathrax. As you can see, I worked as hard on the guild as you did. The only difference is, I did not give up. Not until now at least.

Anyway, the message forums will disappear after this uploads. Long live Guided by Voices.
