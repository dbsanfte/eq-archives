Why I Really Left Shadowdale

 

The reasons I left, giving up all of my mortal incarnations, and only kept my god character there, are simple. I was tired of the bullshit. It seems I must elaborate for those who were not present. When Turlo was a mortal there, before he even dreamed of becoming an administrator, bad things happened. They continued to a higher degree after the fateful day I took on the responsibility of a designer.

It started when two gods decided to take notice of me in a bad, bad way. You see, I am in the belief that rules (absolutes) should be coded, and policies (judgement calls) should be written down to prevent dramatic misinterpritation. This MUD had problems with both. None of the policies were in writing, and most of the rules were not coded, or written. And so my troubles began.

The first god that I somehow managed to piss off with Lillith. There was a policyin effect, mostly by heresay, and extremely dependant on who was on that the time, about equipment lying on the ground. I purposely set up an "incident" to get an official ruling. Lillith was a 59th level god, and second only to the implementor. She posted on the message board that anything left on the ground is fair game. Fine, I thought. An official policy in place. The next day, or there abouts, I was walking to the Halfling Village to purchase some bread. On the path directly leading to the village, a group was playing the reincarnate game with a fire elemental. Right when I walked into the room, completely unaware they were doing something this foolish on a major thoroughfare, a fireball went off. My equipment was shredded. Pieces of it flew off, and my bag was toast, littering the ground with its contents. I was pissed, grabbed everything, and left the area. That's when the chaos started. I was holding their items that were on the ground hostage until they replace my equipment. I thought that was fair. Lillith arrived, and was quite upset that I quoted her written policy back to her. I ended up being frozen, and had everything forceably taken away. She held that grudge for many years RL time...probably still does.

Euphrates was the second god I managed to get on the shit list of. One day, some friends and I were playing with random teleport. Several closed off zones were found, none of which should have been on the zone index file. One zone we went to was actually populated. We obtained several items there were not supposed to be reintroduced to the game, but were still in existance. Euphrates found out, froze me, and forceably took them away. That is when the expression "being mugged by the gods" came into play. It was the second time it happened.

On a seperate occassion, Euphrates found out that I was using a spider shaped dagger off hand. Now, rogues did not get dual wield, but he found out why. The damage bonuses from the weapon were added to attacks. This dagger was +5. However, because of my lack of dual wield, I would automatically drop the off hand dagger 75% of the time, or both daggers 25% of the time. It was only useful for backstabs. He froze me yet again, took the dagger away from me, and recoded it so I could not even wield it. This was not done for the global files, just mine. Forcing me to obtain another. They then made the change to global, but I convinced them to change it back but keep it for single class thieves only. After a long bit of hassle, it was done.

Accusations flew into the wind a great deal of times afterwards. Lillith accussed me of camping a shopkeeper waiting for his money to respawn so I can sell to him again. She never took it into account that respawns do not happen unless the zone resets entirely (which this one would not under the current game circumstances), or the shopkeeper was killed. The room was a saferoom, which meant I was safe from pickpockets. She didn't buy the excuse.

During my zone construction, Lillith ordered several rechecks on my work, since they did not find anything wrong with them. She stated "He has to be hiding something somewhere. He is too sneaky." No one ever gave me credit for having good work ethics. I never once added something inappropriate. I couldn't do Assassin's Mountain originally, as there were too many thief zones in the world. I had to explain the difference between Assassins and Thieves, as they are very different.

The beginning of the end started when Patch, my primary mortal at this time, was on while a god (Jandrea) shouted out to the world that clans would be added during the next upgrade. I quoted the conversation on a message board, and the next thing I know, a holy inquisition was going on. I was penalized a god level (a very serious offence), for sharing god only information to the world (when it was publicly announced). Jandrea never even got a slap on the wrist.

During the grand opening of Assassin's Mountain, which would have been the zone to promote me if I hadn't lost the level, I was heartbroken. It was designed to be for a group of 50s, the adepts of the game, and they walked through it with minimal problem. Patch, as I later found out, could solo the first two sections of it without difficulty. Gods are not allowed to send their own characters into a zone they created until two weeks after it was live. I kept to that faithfully. However, one person was selling the prize item from the zone in open auction, and, well let's face it, no one out bids me. The punishment was harsh. I was told that Nightfall, another god on the game, was penalized for the same thing and lost a level over it. Punishments of gods was never publicly discussed or posted, and I had no knowledge of this. Not only did I loose 53 again, but they froze Patch for a week, causing him to loose all of his equipment. There was never a written warning or policy on this.

Once I went through the trouble of reequipping Patch (a tedious task at best), I was approached by a mortal of Dalamar, a very kiss ass god that was rising quickly through the ranks without doing any well-written work. He wanted to purchase, well, practically all of my items. I refused, and when he persisted, I was quite rude about it. I log on the next day and found out that all of my items were missing. My money was not. I was informed by Unicus that I was force rented the night before at 3am. I informed him I camped at 10pm, and requested he check the logs. He stated that I was ghosted on for about 5 minutes before being force rented, but could tell me no more. Dalamar's alt was wearing my equipment the next day and laughed when he saw me.

The final straw came in with the implementation of Phlan. This was a zone from the Pool of Radiance book, and was very poorly done. Not just in interpreting the story, but in the code. Exploring it with my mortal Patch, once again requipped, I submitted a four page bug report. I was immediately accused of exploring with my god, as no mortal could figure out all these bugs. This once again showed that the higher ups did not actually earn their title. I know diku code well, and these bugs were all found with my mortal. Patch was deleted, I lost yet another level, after working so hard to get them back. I left.

Right before the MUD went poof, Uma, newly promoted to 60th, offered to reinstate my mortals and godhood. I declined.
