EverQuest UI Mods

Ya, ya. I need a background. This is my EverQuest User-Interface Mod page. There are some special things regarding these particuliar mods. First, the spell book pages can be turned by just clicking on the sides of the pages. Second, I added both memorization and casting bar to the spell gems bar. Yes, they both take the same spot, and both work perfectly. No one else has done that yet! Muhahaha. So, check it out, and let me know what you think.
