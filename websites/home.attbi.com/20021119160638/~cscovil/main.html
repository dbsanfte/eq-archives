Welcome!
If you are a guest or a member of our family please feel free to look around and participate in what our website has to offer.
Our community is hosted by ez-board. If you wish to post you will be required to create an account.
Only members of the guild may request to be given access to the private forums.

What is Golden Dawn?
We are a relaxed, fun-loving, guild who enjoy the challenges of the game EverQuest.
Our members enjoy raiding, helping each other out, casual hunting and socializing.

Where region do most of our players hail from?
Golden Dawn is an international guild but the majority are from the United States.
Guild events are planned using Eastern Standard Time (GMT -05:00).
Accomodations are always made for those on the west coast (PST)

Interested in joining Golden Dawn?
Please visit our community forums and submit an application.
All applications will be reviewed by the members of the guild. We advise seeking out current guild members and letting them get to know you both before and after submitting your application.
