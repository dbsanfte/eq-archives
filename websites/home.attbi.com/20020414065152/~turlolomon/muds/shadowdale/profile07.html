Legend

 

Unfortunately, Legend never made it to live play. She was created as a love interest for a world reknown player named Myth. A stat roller program was written and allowed to run all night. I logged what had happened, and, surprisingly enough, she actually managed to roll max (17-18) on all stats, and auto-logged into the game. At that point she was promptly deleted by the gods. Um...isn't that why they allowed us to roll stats? Sigh...once again, policy did not match action.
