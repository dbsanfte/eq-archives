Legal Definitions

 

With all the confusion I have seen regarding my Drunken Ramblings, I have created this page to explain some legal terms. Let's start off with the Webster's Dictionary definition.

slan·der (sln' dr) n.

1.Law. Oral communication of false statements injurious to a person's reputation.
2.A false and malicious statement or report about someone.

This is probably not good enough for most of you, so I delved into the Law Books and found the following.

WHAT IS "DEFAMATION" ?

Defamation, sometimes called "defamation of character", is spoken or written words that falsely and negatively reflect on a living person's reputation.

If a person or the news media says or writes something about you that is understood to lower your reputation, or that keeps people from associating with you, defamation has occurred. Slander and libel are two forms of defamation.

I had to include defamation because isn't that what slander is all about? Please note. It does specify living person. You can't slander the dead, and you can't slander a stupid cartoon.

WHAT IS "SLANDER" ?

Slander is a spoken defamation.

Amazing. How can I be guilty of slander if it is spoken? Oh...I think I know what you mean.

WHAT IS "LIBEL" ?

Libel is a written defamation. Generally, radio and television broadcasts that are defamatory are considered to be libel, rather than slander.

Ok. So, if Pikachu marches down to the courthouse, perhaps he can file some papers against me.

DON'T I HAVE A RIGHT TO EXPRESS MY OPINION?

Yes, so long as your statement of opinion is just an opinion, not containing specific facts that can be proved untrue.
