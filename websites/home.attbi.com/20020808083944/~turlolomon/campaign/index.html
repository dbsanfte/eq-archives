Citadel of the Red Dragon

Gaming, gaming, gaming. Different genres, different worlds. I love to game. If you are here, chances are, you have at least mild interest in it. Well, perhaps I should talk about what is on this page.

First, let me talk to you about my Dungeons and Dragons campaign. It has been going on since 1981. That's right. Over 20 years. It is amazing that it has been going on for so long. We are currently on the third generation of our characters. One will build up an empire, their decendants destroy it, and the next tries to rebuild. Go fig. Kinda like real life, eh?

Now, my records. More will be added as I get around to making them. (I have been especially excited over PC Gen, so expect more of that!)

Citizens of Lomon Manor: A short summary and thrown together character sheets for the major players using 2nd Edition rules for Dungeons and Dragons.

PC Gen: The best program for the d20 system I have found on the net. A good replacement for that vapor-ware Master Tools.

And speaking of Master Tools...I need to rant a bit about that. First, Wizards of the Coast came up with a great idea. Let's do a program right! They included a shareware version with every Player's Handbook. The program looked sharp. Very professionally put together. Looked clean. Easy to use interface. Everyone was looking forward to the full version being released.

That is when all hell broke loose. Hasbro decided to purchase Wizards of the Coast. The fate of the program was unsure, but if you check the latest interviews with the pseudo-designers (not the same people who started it), the program has been shredded. Only the two core books (Player's Handbook and Dungeon Master's Guide) will be included. No options for adding additional prestige classes will be made. They axed the map maker entirely. Sigh...and then they said...they MIGHT release that capability if the first one sells well. ETA on delivery? None. Welcome to the wonderful world of vapor-ware.
