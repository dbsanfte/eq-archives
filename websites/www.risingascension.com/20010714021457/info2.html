Relations with other Guilds and Individuals

Most guilds have a wide range of people in them, from saints to outright asses. Therefore, it is our policy as a guild to relate to individuals rather than to guilds as a whole. It is strictly prohibited for any RA member to partake in guild bashing in say, shout, or ooc. If you have a problem with an individual or individuals, leave their guild status out of it. Always remember what you say and do reflects not only on you character and personage, but the guild as a whole. This means that at times we expect you to walk away or bite your lip if there is a problem while you are hunting in an area. Tell an officer, the event leader, or Aeen; they will handle the problem. Always treat others how you wish to be treated. If all else fails, leave the area; it's not worth the trouble.  Top

Plane Farming

After initial break in, planes will not be farmed while Cazic, Inno, Vox, or Nagafen live. People permitting, all major encounters will be killed before any secondary farming occurs. For farming fear, after the break, we will decide as a guild whether we will farm the respawn or not. If so, we will in all respects treat it as a normal expedition, and will show up in groups when the respawn is due, positioning ourselves far away from any other farmers that have shown up, and pull spawns as they occur. There is nothing we can do to keep others out of the zone, and we will not try. Noone is to complain or make comments in say, shout, or ooc regarding the activities of anyone else in the zone. If other groups attempt to follow us around the zone to kill steal our pulls, we will simply /petition this harassment, end of story. In the event that we do not decide to continue farming fear as a guild, it will become an open zone. If another or group breaks fear, we will as a guild, respect their wishes regarding farming repops.  Top

Secondary Characters

Unless the expedition leader is informed otherwise in advance, it is assumed the character on the expedition is the character you are looting for. Secondary characters, while guilded for guildsay benefits, will not officially become part of the guild untill they meet current recruiting criteria. I.e., no rolling for loot for your twinked 20th level warrior - if there's overflow loot that noone needs, people can roll for qualified secondaries.  Top

Invites

Only the expedition leader may extend invites. If you would like to invite someone, ask in guildsay. If people agree, the leader will extend the invitation on your behalf. Only refer invitees to the expedition leader if you are sure they have read our looting policies as presented here. Lootwise, invitees will be treated exactly as if they were in the guild. Refer to our loot policies, and remember the golden rule. Non-invited tag alongs will not be considered for loot. If you are not in the guild, and would like to be invited on an expedition, ask someone who the expedition leader is, and request an invite. Depending on how people in the guild feel about you, and the amounts of your class along on the expedition already, you will be invited or not. If you are invited, familiarize yourself with our looting guidelines, as you will both benefit from and be held to them.  Top

Note from Aeen

After you have read these rules, think about them with an open mind, you will see that these rules are put in place for the good of the guild and to make however much you time you spend in game with us, your extended family a more enjoyable ride. Take having fun seriously, and it propagates itself onto the next person in your group and guild. While some may not agree with the rules of our guild, these rules were thought out and made for the benefit of you, the Rising Ascension member.  Top
