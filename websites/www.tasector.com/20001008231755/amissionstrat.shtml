Arm Missions

 

Mission Breakdowns

There is a small amount of randomness in the missions, and while these guidelines should always work, you might receive more or less opposition at any given time.

Mission 1
A Hero Returns

Start: 5 Peewees, 2 Flashes, 2 Stumpies, 2 Jeffys, 2 Rockos, 1 Hammer.

This one is simple: group all of your units and sweep the map. Kill everything you meet.

Mission 2
Core Kbot Base, Destroy It!

Start: Commander, 2 Rockos, 1 Hammer, 2 Peewees

There are three metal patches right around where you start. Put Metal Extractors on these and build three Solar Collectors. You might get jumped during this time, but your starting forces are adequate to destroy the little scumbags.

Lay down a Kbot lab and build a load of Hammers. Some people might like to mix up their forces, but we have found Hammers are the unit of choice for dishing out pain when you don't need the range of the Rocko. Once you have 20 or so Hammers, head to the southwest, where you will find the Core Kbot Lab you need to destroy. There will be some resistance, but nothing 20 Hammers can't handle.

Mission 3
Spider Technology

Start: Commander, 3 Hammers, 2 Rockos, 3 Spiders

Another easy mission. Nine out of 10 times, you can simply group the Spiders at the north and walk them to your base. They will take some damage, but at least one unit almost always makes it through. In most cases, all three will make it home.

Occasionally the game can be a bit tricky about admitting you have accomplished your mission. If you walk the Spiders to the middle of the small clearing beyond the Dragon's Teeth, it should be enough, but in some cases you are going to need to move them around a bit before the all clear gong sounds.

Mission 4
Core Contamination Spreads

Start: Commander, 2 Rockos, 1 Zeus

You will likely be attacked right out of the gate, and while your Rockos and Zeus can handle the first few attacks, you don't have much time to waste.

Build Metal Extractors on the three metal deposits in the vicinity, then build three Solar Collectors. After that put a Kbot lab into play and crank out a Construction Kbot and some Hammers. With about 10 Hammers in the field patrolling your base, the way is prepared.

Next, construct three Light Laser Towers, each guarded by a few Hammers, at the north, northeast and northwest corners of your base. That will likely stop the most fierce attack you will face for the moment.

Get an Advanced Kbot Lab into play and, with the help of the Construction Kbot, start cranking out Fidos. By this time, a few more Metal Extractors should be providing just enough material to keep pumping out Fidos at a good clip. When you've assembled a force of about 20 Fidos, you're ready to take the battle to the enemy. Call us un-elegant, but instead of constructing doing a fine mix of perfectly balanced forces, we just like to throw huge numbers of Fidos down folks' throats. It is the rare problem indeed that 20 Fidos won't cure.

At this point, you may overrun the enemy, making sure to destroy a radar unit in the mountains in the middle of the map.

Arm 5
The Gate to Thalassean

Start: Commander, 4 Tridents, 2 Lurkers, 2 Skeeters, 5 Fidos, 2 Bulldogs, 1 Jammer, 4 Jeffys, 3 Zippers

The land you are on runs straight down to bottom of screen with the same basic width. Near the bottom is an enemy base. The Stargate, however, is on an island to the southwest guarded by one torp launcher and a Core destroyer.

First send your Lurkers and Tridents to the southwest through the water to find and eliminate the defenses there. This force is big enough to do the job. Leave them there, or bring the Tridents back to help you in your land campaign, it doesn't really matter all that much.

The easiest way to kill the base below is to simply build your base defenses and start moving them south in a front. At first enemy opposition will be fairly strong, but will gradually thin out as the enemy just throws everything he has at your parameter. Keep building lasers, missile towers and finally plasma cannons further and further and further south. Support this building with Fidos and Hammers.

Finally you can just run your Fidos into the remains of the enemy base. After securing enemy base, walk commander across the water and capture the gate.

Mission 6
Beachhead on Thalassean

Start: Commander

Right off the bat capture as many of the attacking A.K's you can. If you must shoot them, don't use the D-gun. That way you can reclaim your metal, which is going to be your biggest need on this map. Then capture the metal production facility right in front of you.

Get some Solar Converters down and make sure you get all the metal spots on your island covered with Metal Extractors. Now put up a shipyard and make a Construction ship. Follow that with a couple of Skeeters and a couple of destroyers. This should keep the worst of the pain off of you for the moment. You are going to keep catching enemy pain from ships circling your island. But for a short period of time all you can do is deal with it.

Now put up a Kbot Lab and pop out a couple of Construction Kbots. Now start putting up Punisher plasma batteries. Put one near the center of your island to the rear and one on each of the "horns" pointing to the east. Along with a radar tower this will take care of most of your waterbound worries. You can just fire the Punishers indirectly and take out those pesky attacking ships.

Now in multiple playtests, we have found the site of the enemy shipyard you need to take out can be on either to the North or to the South of your island. When the incoming ships start to peter out that means the Core forces are pretty much out of ships and you can send out a small force to go and hunt at your leisure.

Mission 7
The Defense of Larab Harbor

Start: Commander, Various Shore Batteries, 3 Jethros, 5 Skeeters, 2 Crusaders, 1 Ranger, 1 Samson, 1 Zeus

Start building a construction ship and move your Skeeters and Crusaders immediately of the bay to defeat a small attack force of two destroyers coming in there. This will keep your Ranger intact if you do it quick enough. The second this attack force is defeated, move the remaining Skeeters over to the upper left side of the bay. About one out of every 10 tries this will keep your energy storage unit intact, as it is about to be attacked by a couple of enemy bombers.

At the start have your Commander build a metal extractor on the metal patch and then move to the Energy Storage unit and guard it. At the same time, move all of your Jethro's over to the left side of the screen. Again, all of this is to try to keep your Energy Storage unit alive during the bombing run.

Right after bombing, a couple of scout ships are going to come up the left side of your bay. Between your Ranger and Crusaders the scout ships should be short work.

After all of the abuse wears down, start your Construction ship to building a double line of Tidal Generators all around the coast of the bay. Start building more Construction Ships and set your Commander to making 12-20 Metal Makers, the first time you have seen this exciting new unit.

With plenty of metal production between the Metal Makers and the Tidal Generators it is time to start rocking. Build an Advanced Shipyard and another Shipyard. Crank out Crusaders like mad, a couple of more Construction ships with the Shipyards and 3 Conquerors and 3 Milleniums with the Advanced Shipyard.

Set your Construction ships to building a couple of vertical lines of 3-4 Torp Launchers down your bay with some sonar bouys in front of them. These torp launchers are going to really help out quite a bit, so get a good picture of where to put them. Look at the bay. By now the top half of it is pretty much full of shipyards and tidal generators. But the bottom half, near where the island and two outlets are should be mostly empty. At the left side you want to put 3 or 4 torp launchers in a line going north-south. At the middle and right side the same thing. Then right below them a couple of sonar bouys.

At the same time, or as soon as resources permit, have your Commander crank out a Robot Factory and make 4 Construction Kbots. Send one down the left side of the screen and have it start building plasma batteries. Send one down the right side to do the same thing. Have these units build a couple of radar towers as well.

Send one of your remaining Construction Kbots to build a load of Defenders around your Moho and have the last one guard the Moho. Remember, if the Moho goes down, the mission ends in failure. Keep building your Conquerors and group your Battleships and move them as far to the North as you can in your congested bay.

Now it is just a matter of waiting for the abuse to come to you. There are a huge number of enemy ships down to the south of that island blocking your bay, and going after them is definitely contra-indicated. However, if you run a couple of little groups of Destroyers down there and running them back up, sooner or later the enemy will come to you and get chewed up by your defenses. After you have eaten 3 or 4 enemy battleships that will pretty much be Core's last hurrah. You can safely send a few Conquerors down south to clear out the last couple of stragglers, normally amphibious tanks that have run from the action.

Mission 8
The Gate to Tergiverse IV

Start: Commander, 1 Ranger, 1 Zeus, 4 Jethros, 6 Skeeters, 1 Crusader

This one takes a while, but is a bunny after 7. Build a Construction Ship and set Mines on both metal patches. Use your Commander to clear the shrubs off the bottom of the screen. Use Construction Ship to build 12-20 tidal generators along the left side of the map where they will be out of the way. A double row running north to south seems to work the best.

Have your Commander build 12 or so metal makers on the bottom of the screen then pop out a couple of more Construction Ships. Have them build lots of Torp Launchers and bouys scattered pretty much randomly around.

Build a Hulk transport ship and run a Construction Kbot up to the nearest island. You can clear it first with Crusader fire if you want. If you work tight on that island, you can fit one Defender a pair of plasma batteries and a radar tower on that island. Go to the next island over and repeat.

From here on out it is just more of the same. Build an Advanced Shipyard or two and crank out a bunch of the bigger ships. Just keep on island hopping until you get where you are going.

Note: You might want to up quite a few Defenders on your home island, because air strikes can just ruin your whole day.

Mission 9
The Hydration Plant

Start: Commander

This mission is really easy. Just do what it tells you to do. Build a couple of Peepers to find the Hydration plant, whip out an Atlas and drop your Commander right on the Hydration Plant. Capture it and go to the next mission.

Mission 10
The Bromid Maze

Start: Commander, 1 Zeus, 2 Spiders, 1 Hammer

This mission is simply a lesson in applied use of the Brawler Gunship. Scout the bottom third of the map and close off the three entrances with Dragon's Teeth. On the one hill you can actually get on with a Construction Kbot build a plasma battery and a radar tower. Kill everything in your sites using indirect fire.

Build an Aircraft Plant then a Construction Aircraft and then an Advanced Aircraft Plant. Build six or so Brawlers and send them to the upper right portion of the map and wipe the enemy base. Target the enemy laser towers first then clean up the rest.

Mission 11
The Gate to Barathrum

Start: Commander, 2 Brawlers, 1 Zeus, 2 Zippers

This one is another sleeper. Build metal extractors on the patches then Kbot Plant. Use a Construction Kbot to build a Geothermal plant and then 6 metal makers. Put up 8 or so Defenders around your parameter. Get up to an Advanced Airfield and start cranking out Brawlers. Get up to an Advanced Kbot plant and crank out Fidos.

Eight Brawlers and some Fidos will cut through the enemy like a hot knife through butter.

Mission 12
Barathrum!

Start: Commander

This one isn't quick, but it's straight forward. Build a Kbot Plant and 2-3 Jethros as soon as you have some metal going.

Now, think Defenders. They are your friend. Build two to three in the little land bridge you start in. Now build a couple of Rockos and a Radar Tower and pick off the Light Laser the enemy has put at the mouth of the land bridge.

Once you get out in open, just build a base ringed with laser and rocket towers in the NW corner of the map. The Moho you need to get is lightly defended by a laser and rocket tower combo up in the NE corner of the map. You can take out these defenses with 6-8 Fidos and then capture everything up there.

At this point you will be so far ahead in resources you can take out the remainder of the Core forces however you like. Personally, we like 10 Hawks guarding 10 Brawlers. Your mileage may vary.

Mission 13
Landown's Interface

Start: Commander, Zeus, Fido, 4 Jethros, 6 Zippers

This one is a bunny, but it is time consuming. First, nothing is going to come at you on the ground due to a cliff that totally isolates the top 1/3 of the map. By the same logic, you aren't going to get any ground forces in there either.

Next point to note is that this is a high metal planet. This means that a mine on any patch of ground is going to produce .5 metal a tick. Put up some mines, solar collectors a Kbot Factory and then LOTS of Defenders to keep out those annoying air attacks. Then build some Advanced Aircraft Plants. Note that is plural.

Use the AA Plants to crank out Hawks and Brawlers. Each time you have about 5 of each, set them to patrol through the enemy base. Repeat this process until all of the nasties are gone.

They you can Atlas your Commander over to capture all of the production facilities or just destroy them with gunships.

There is zero chance of losing this one if you just build, build and build.

Mission 14
The Heat Increases

Start: Commander, 2 Jethros, 4 Fidos, 4 Zippers

Same drill as 13 with annoying walking bombs to throw you off. First, set your Zippers so that you can get the widest possible parameter that you can see all of with no gaps. This is very important, because having visual range is critical here. Next you station the Fidos behind the Zipper line. This takes care of the Roaches that are going to try to walk in and blow up your base.

Now suck metal out of the ground and build multiple Advance Air Plants and start patrols over enemy positions. That ends it.

If you want to, you can use ground units to fight things out instead. But this seems to be harder and longer than the air based approach. Don't forget to use Defenders for air defense.

Also, there is an unmentioned win condition in this one to destroy all Core targets.

Mission 15
Rougpelt

Start: Commander, One half dead Zipper

Right at the start you are going to get jumped by a load of Pyros. Kill them and don't try to capture them or they are going to eat you alive. Use D-gun liberally.

Repair the Zipper and run it out very slightly ahead of you, but make sure that it is set to hold fire. With the Zipper running ahead of you, it will start taking fire from enemy units. You can then use your Commander to slide in and capture them. After capture, repair all units. Once and a while you are going to lose a leading unit this way, but don't worry about it. After all, you need to break a few eggs to make a pizza. Make sure that any new units you capture are set to Hold Fire if you want to keep capturing things and not destroying them. There is one radar about 2/3 of the way down the left side of the map. This one is normally defended by a light laser tower. Another, without the pesky LLT is about 1/3 of the way down on the right side of the screen.

You can really pull this mission off with just the Commander and not worry about capturing anything if you are quick and dexterous, but we just enjoyed the fun of building up a force made up of enemy units.

Mission 16
Stockpile at the Abutment

Start: Commander, Zeus, 3 Zippers, 2 Fidos.

There is no ground attack required here. Just build up your planes and use them to spot for a new unit, the Big Bertha long distance pain device. Make sure you have a fusion plant up to provide the power that Bertha is going to suck down.

Mission 17
Fortress at Brooban's Isle

Start: Commander, Zeus, 3 Jethros, Skeeter, Crusader, Construction Ship

This is another straight forward mission. Build Tidal Generators and Metal Makers. Then go to airpower. You don't need multiple airstrips for this one, just a simple and an advanced.

Scout with your airpower and get a fusion reactor up and everything is downhill from there. Build multiple shipyards and start cranking out ships. At the same time, set some Brawlers and some Lvl 1 Fighters to patrolling the parameter of your base. Throw up a few missile towers to take care of enemy air.

At some point about 4 enemy battleships are going to stream in here. By the time they get there, you should have plenty of Conquerors around to eat them up. Having a few subs to throw at them will help also. Locate the Nuke you are looking for on the middle island using Air sorties. Important safety tip, make sure the planes you are using in this hunt are set to Return Fire only or they are likely to destroy the Nuke Launcher.

After you have used your planes and ships to neutralize the opposition, airlift your Commander to the silo to capture it.

Mission 18
Vengeance!

Start: Everything

This one is a real cakewalk. Simply sweep straight down the screen with all of your forces. There is no strategy here at all.

Mission 19
The Motien Ramp

Start: Commander, 1 Construction Kbot, 3 Zippers, 2 Fidos, 2 Samsons, 2 Spiders, 1 Invader, 1 Merl

There is a geothermal vent slightly to your east. Cap that. The Galactic Gate you want is up to the North East. At this point you have the tactics to execute this one pretty much any way you want to. Air or ground will easily pull this one off.

Note that you must destroy all enemy units in order to win this one.

Mission 20
Dump

Start: Commander, 1 Weasel, 1 Zeus, 6 Zippers, 2 Fidos

This one is another one where you just do what it says. Build lots of L2 Kbots, around 20 Fidos is our recommendation. Then walk your forces up the map and hammer on everything you see.

Avoid using vehicles on this map due to the confined spaces. Your opposition amounts to 15-20 bots and Goliath tanks mostly at the top of the map.

Mission 21
Welcome to Core Prime

Start: Commander

This one is nice because it has free metal and lots of geothermal energy for the taking.

Build 2 extractors, 3 Solar Energy units and a Kbot Lab. Use the Kbot Lab to produce one Construction Kbot. Crank out a couple of more Solars, 2 more extractors and a Geothermal Plant.

The reason we are outlining this after you know perfectly well how to build the simple things is that Core Prime tends to throw off anyone's building pattern. You see all that free metal and just go for it, screwing up your resource allocation.

Once you have the first Geothermal in place, it is downhill from there, pick your favorite way to take out the Core slime. The AI is going to throw tons of stuff up that ramp right in the center of your base, but a few missile towers, a couple of heavy laser towers and a few Fidos will take care of that right quick.

Our favorite way to deliver the real pain to the enemy on this one is a pack of Brawlers behind his lines, but you can get plenty of land units down the ramp to the East if you want to go that route.

Mission 22
Battle for Coordinate 6551:447

Start: Commander, Zeus, 4 Zippers

This is a bunny shoot. You are totally secure on your land position due to a strip of water between you and enemy emplacements. We used lots of planes on this one, but the best way to take care of things is just to airlift your Commander over to the enemy emplacements and D-gun to your heart's desire.

However, even after you have killed everything. You are going to find out there is still work to do. In each of the long canals you will find that a sub is prowling. You must kill these subs. The best way we have found to do this is to run your Commander into the water at one end of a canal and wait for the sub there to find him and start shooting. Then run him just up onto the shore, just a hair beyond the waterline. This will keep the sub interested in him. Run a Construction Kbot to the other end of the canal and build a shipyard. Use it to crank out a couple of destroyers and run them down to kill the sub. Repeat this with the other canal.

Mission 23
Crossing Aqueous Body 397

Start: Commander, Lurker, Crusader, 2 Peepers, Zeus, Trident, 2 Fidos

Build three Solar Collectors and three metal extractors then a Kbot Lab. There is not much to it from there. Don't be rash and engage the enemy directly. That line of islands in the center of the map is well fortified.

Instead build up to a couple of Berthas and use Hawks to spot for your guns. This will let you take out all of the defensive emplacements from a safe distance. Then you can send a few Conquerors in to mop up. This one takes a while, but you suffer almost no losses if you just take it easy.

Mission 24
Breakthrough to Central Conciousness

Start: Commander, 5 Zippers, 3 Fidos, 1 Zeus

This one is very simple. Core pretty much leaves you alone and that is a bad mistake on their part.

Things are slow going at first due to a lack of vents to get geothermal power, but once you get a Fusion Reactor up and running it takes care of the power problem. With your Fusion up, build 40 or so Brawlers and take the game. Be careful to make sure you got all the Core defensive emplacements and drop your Commander on top of the Dome with an Atlas.

Mission 25
Core Prime Apocalypse

Start: A few of everything.

This one looks a whole lot like Mission 18, and that is going to cause your first urge to be rounding up all of your units and running them upscreen. DO NOT DO THIS, you will be eaten alive. The strongest Core resistance of any mission is here.

There is no set strategy for how to deal with this mission. Instead, simply apply the principles you have learned to winning. This one is the hardest of all of the missions, but if you are careful you will win.

One thing that helps at the start is to build a line of Defenders to the north just out of the range of the Core fire. Then put up a Radar and use indirect fire from groups of about 5 Defenders at a time to blow out opposition on the ridge directly north. That gives you quite a bit of breathing room. The Defender line will also help take care of the occasional raid that Core sends down to harass your base. These raids aren't all that strong, but they do require a defensive parameter.

We took this one out using our signature massive air hordes, but be warned, there are quite a few Doomsday Machines to the North and you will need to pound quite a few of your Brawler hordes into them before they finally give up the ghost.

Once you have the Doomsday Machines out of commission, it all goes down from there and your planes or ground units can get pretty much a free hand killing the remaining forces.
