TA Cheats

 

 

 

In Total Annihilation, press Enter for the message box, and then type in the cheat codes.   Except for RADAR, they only work in multiplayer (if allowed) or skirmish games

  • +ATM - Gives you 1000 metal and energy.
  • +Bigbrother - Units will talk when you move them.
  • +bps - Shows the data transfer-rates in multi games.
  • +CDSTART - Starts CD Music.
  • +CDSTOP - Stops CD Music.
  • +COMBUSTION - Kills all enemy units.
  • +CONTOUR # - Displays a 3D contour, # = 1-15.
  • +CONTROL # - Use a different skirmish AI.
  • +CLOCK - Adds an in-game clock in the corner of the screen.
  • +DITHER - Dithering instead of line of sight.
  • +DOUBLESHOT - All weapons do twice the damage.
  • +HALFSHOT - All weapons do half the damage.
  • +HONK - Units honk when they stop.
  • +ILOSE - Causes you to lose.
  • +INFRARED - Toggles off the fog.
  • +IWIN - Causes you to win.
  • +KILL# - Kills off a player, where # is 0-4
  • +LIGHT - Hide the shaded objects, makes all buildings dark.
  • +LOGO# - Change your color, # is 0-10
  • +LUSHEE - Limited resource increase.
  • +MAKEPOSTER - Screen shot of the whole map (BMP format).
  • +MAPPING - Covers screen with darkness
  • +METEOR - Causes a random meteor shower.
  • +NANOLATHING - Doubles the Nanolathing AND metal usage !
  • +NOENERGY - Drops your energy to 0.
  • +NOMETAL - Drops your metal to 0.
  • +NOSHAKE - Stops explosion screen shakes.
  • +NOWISEE - Full map and disables line of sight.
  • +RADAR - 100% radar coverage.
  • +ReloadAIProfiles - AIProfile Reloads AI.
  • +SCREENCHAT - Toggles chat text in multiplayer games.
  • +SETI - Toggles radar minimap on/off.
  • +SHAREMAPPING x - Share map information to x, where x=ennemies or allies.
  • +SHARERADAR x - Share map information to x, where x=ennemies or allies.
  • +SHOOTALL - Your units target everything of the enemies it encounters.
  • +SHOWRANGES - Hold shift while mouse is on unit and it will show its range.
  • +SING - This command makes units "sing" when given orders.
  • +SWITCHALT - Allows you to switch between squads with alt+# keys.
  • +TILT - Max out men and material.
  • +VIEW# - Lets you see how much metal and energy a player has.
  • +ZIPPER - Speeds up the unit-building process.

Below is the first Campaign cheat code:  Click on Total Annihilation's Single Player icon. When the Single Player Game page appears type DRDEATH (it is not case sensitive). This will cause a Cavedog Entertainment bone to appear between the Load Game and Previous Menu icons on the right side of the screen. Clicking on the bone will take players to a special Play Any Game screen where they can play any Total Annihilation mission, even if they have not earned their way to that level yet.
