Bylaws of the Triune Alliance

Enacted by the Councils of the Alliance in accord with Article I, Section 2.



1. Any form of unwanted harassment is expressly forbidden, whether directed against a guild member or other person. Harassment generally constitutes unwanted ‘verbal’ attention, though in a particular case it may be difficult to adjudicate. A clear example of a particular form of harassment consists of persistent requests for money, items, powerleveling, etc. Persistent is taken to mean continuous requests of a person when said person has asked the requests to stop, but can also mean that the requests are being made systematically of a large number of guild members, albeit respecting requests to desist, to the full or partial exclusion of other communication. Those members found to be guilty of harassment are generally entitled to at least one warning. Subsequent infractions may be deemed isolated, indicating only a warning, but after a first infraction, punishment up to and including dismissal may be recommended.

2. Kill-stealing with the intent to do so is expressly forbidden. Depending on the severity of the infraction, anything from a warning to dismissal recommendation may be issued. In the special circumstances that a ‘named spawn’ is stolen, restitution of an appropriate nature may be asked, where possible.

3. Training with the intent to kill or hinder another is forbidden. Any other activity that aids an NPC in killing another, when intended maliciously, falls under this bylaw. Depending on severity, a warning may be given, along with the possibility of some form of restitution to the victim, up to a dismissal recommendation in the most dire circumstance.

4. Fraud of any kind is strictly forbidden. The defrauder may be absolved of all punishment if all fraudulently obtained items and/or monies are returned to their respectful owners. Otherwise, fraud is an action generally punishable by a dismissal recommendation.

5. Members of the Alliance should treat all those in Norrath deserving of it with respect, honor their word, and behave towards others with the highest regard, regardless their respective races. Past actions may of course earn one the ire of a member.

6. Members of the Alliance should be honest and fair in dividing the spoils with any adventuring group, and should respect established rules of any group or locale.

7. Recruitment is generally at the discretion of officers, but, at a minimum, those wishing to join the Triune Alliance must state in writing their agreement to these laws before they may be allowed to join.  Furthermore, it is suggested that an act of complete selflessness of some significance in another should be taken as a strong indication of a person’s worthiness of recruitment.

8. In order to establish harmony, there shall be a new office in our alliance, known as Assistant Moderator. Any officer, who may serve already as either a Judicator or be a member of the Council of Commons, may be recommended for this office. Upon recommendation, a simple majority vote in the Council of Commons will suffice to instate the new Assistant Moderator. The special duties of an Assistant Moderator shall consist of moderation of guild chat and assisting the Moderator in this capacity. Should the situation warrant, the Assistant Moderator may recommend that a particular case be presented before a Judicator for resolution. The Assistant Moderator may assist the Judicator in investigation and presentation of the case..
