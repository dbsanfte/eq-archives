The Triune Alliance is a guild that stands for dignity, honor, honesty, and camaraderie. Therefore before joining us you must satisfy a simple requirement. This requirement is that you must read our Bylaws and agree to them. They are our rules of conduct. They may sound harsh at first, but that is mainly to make sure people don’t loophole around the spirit of our rules. In reality they are administered with compassion and rigor, and it is mostly on your honor to abide by them. Your agreement to the Bylaws must be posted on our Membership Forum before we can invite you.
