It's good that now everyone can read this story here. Let me add a little from my own perspective. To start, by an amazing coincidence I asked Ciara about starting a guild just as she had been mulling over the same idea. At this time I said to her I would only join a guild of our friends if she would lead us. I felt she was the only one I could trust not to delete a guild on a whim, as had happened with Circle of Light (which I had not been a part of, but had many friends in). Shortly after our formation, I left Norrath for a month(IRL I was in Europe). When I came back, I, who had been Alnasat, was reborn as Lambik. Soon, Lambik was a member of Triune, and then an officer. As I'd been gone for a while, I was mostly unaware of some of the trials Triune had faced, but the night of the Triune raid on Unrest I was shocked to learn that things were worse than I'd known. Bodiciea, whom most of you know now as Rowwena, asked me out of the blue if I wanted to lead Triune. I had never desired to lead a guild in the past, in my mind I'd never felt worthy of such a thing, but this night I knew that if I did not say yes my guild that I'd helped found would die. That night those members that remained met in Butcherblock by the zone to Dagnor's Cauldron. Much was said that night, many pledges made. In a moment of inspiration (or perhaps rashness) I chose my surname (I'd just made my 20th season) to be vanTriune. However, despite the positive things said that night, I can only look back at it sadly, for in a span of a few weeks many more people left the guild, including those who'd said such inspiring words that night. Sadder still, now I think I am the only one who was there that night that still bears the Triune flag. To this day I have trouble walking through that area without an icy pall besetting my heart.
 

 Soon after these events transpired I started to think on what I should do as leader. I knew the pressures Ciara had borne would be too much for me as well, so I knew that the guild needed decentralized leadership. Out of this idea was born our constitution. However, I had a further motivation, that up till now I have revealed to no one. I hoped that if I could create a structured system of leadership that would take some burden away from the guildleader, that Ciara might return to us. Well, this dream may as well be dead now. After writing the constitution I hoped things would begin to move themselves more, but this could not always be so, but I must say there have been times where the harmony and strength of this guild have been amazing. Recently, I've completed writing our bylaws, a necessary companion to our constitution, but more still is needed. What we need is a community that will endure, not just by the stubbornness of a few, but by the strength of its unity. I know this can happen, I have seen glimpses of it in our past, and despite our tribulations, I foresee it will happen in the future. And who knows, if there is anything that I've learned in Norrath, things change with a rapidity that can shock any observer.

TRIUNE FOREVER!!!!

Lambik vanTriune
