Shocking Tales of Norrathian Travelers

North Karana

Calling into question the safety of teleportation, critics are once again voicing their concern over this mode of travel which has been becoming increasingly common among Norrathian citizens who no longer seem to consider older modes of travel acceptable. While spokespeople for both the Wizard and Druid guilds insist that there is no danger associated with teleportation, and that it is still safer than walking, many remain skeptical. Our photographer in the field, Iggwilv Sepulcrea, captured this image of a teleportation gone wrong, very wrong. As one anonymous witness on the scene was quoted as saying; “I think that I will take the boat from now on.” Both the Wizard and Druid Guilds refused to comment on the evidence presented in this photo.
