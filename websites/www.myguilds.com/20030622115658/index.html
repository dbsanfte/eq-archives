Welcome to MyGuilds!

MyGuilds is a website solution for guilds and groups of MMORPG players. While our current setup is best suited for Everquest players, we will be adding sites for other games in the future. Please continue to check our progress.

MyGuilds offers you a hosted website with features tailored to your needs. Your members can catch up on the latest guild accomplishments with the news page feature, which allows members you designate to create and publish articles. Our roster management system will allow members to add their characters and update them as needed. For more details on these and other exciting features, please browse our features page.
