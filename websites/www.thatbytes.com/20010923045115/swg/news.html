Welcome to the Intergalactic Coalition of Engineers

Description:

The Intergalactic Coalition of Engineers, I.C.E., is a player association (P.A.) dedicated in spreading its member's wealth of knowledge in technology across the galaxy. However, The I.C.E. is more than your typical trades or crafts P.A. The members of the I.C.E. will consist of many different species and professions. The goal of The I.C.E. will be establish a headquarters on an ideal planet in the galaxy. In order to accomplish this goal the I.C.E. will need the services of all available professions. The I.C.E. will need guards to protect the headquarters, bounty hunters to collect debt from unscrupulous vendors or customers, works, or adventurers as the I.C.E. refers to them, to collect rare and hard to get items for the engineers, and of course pilots to transport the I.C.E.'s highly technical goods. Since the headquarters of the I.C.E will be more than just a simple research facility, the I.C.E. will also need shopkeepers to sell our wares locally. The I.C.E. will also need cooks, tailors, barkeeps, and entertainers to enrich the lives of all the I.C.E. members.

F.A.Q.

Will the I.C.E. be affiliated with any factions?
Yes, but as of now the faction is undetermined. The I.C.E. will choose a faction that best supports its technological based ideals across the galaxy.

Will the I.C.E. participate in PvP?
As with joining any faction, and with the amount of turmoil in galaxy, there will be enemies of the I.C.E. and the faction we choose. That is why the I.C.E will need guards to defend our facilities and to sometimes attack competitors for natural resources our engineers need.

Will the I.C.E. have member dues?
Yes and No. To be a member of the I.C.E. you will not have to pay dues. However, in order to support the engineers in developing their high-tech items, members can contribute and be union members. Becoming a union member gives you discounts on items sold at the I.C.E. headquarters, protection from enemies, and voting rights.

How is I.C.E pronounced?
The proper way is say each letter as I - C - E. Not the word ice.

Is the I.C.E. a strict roleplaying P.A.?
No. There will be no roleplaying "rules". Roleplaying is encouraged, but not required. The only rule is to have fun.

Who are the I.C.E. members?
The members of I.C.E. consist of many friends, most whom have meet each other through other online games such as EverQuest and Ultima Online. However anyone is welcome to join at this time.
