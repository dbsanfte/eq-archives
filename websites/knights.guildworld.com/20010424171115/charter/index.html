Charter for the 'Norrathian Avengers' a Guild of the Torvinnolious server of EverQuest

 

PURPOSE:

We are about having fun, and achieving it any way we can. What does this mean? Well, whatever is fun for YOU in EverQuest, WE will try to give you more of. It's that simple. It's leaders are dedicated, hard-working folk, and you can count on us to help you whenever you need it, as long as the same invitation is granted back towards us once in a while. In order to enhance our ability to help one another we have developed a guild structure that while sounding complicate should allow for maximum fun with minimum effort. 

For the most part, acceptance in the guild is invitation only, but during these growing periods, we will be more lenient with inviting people into the guild.

  

CODE:

 All Members of the Guild will follow a code of ethics and honor:

1. Assist your fellow Guild Members wherever and whenever possible.

2. Be polite and courteous to all at all times. Think before you speak, most likely before harsh words. You are a reflection upon your guild and fellow members. Arguments in /guild, /ooc or /shout will not be tolerated.  If feel you have to argue with someone take it to /tell and keep it private.

3. Play fair. Kill stealing, ninja looting, public flaming, and other unscrupulous behavior will NOT be tolerated.

4. Have honor by behaving in a way that reflects well upon yourself and your guild. Recognize those that are honorable.  

5. Treat everyone you meet with the same respect you would like them to show you. Be proud of who you are, and the guild you belong to. 

6. Show pride in your class, race and membership in your Guild.

7.  Endeavor to refrain from strong language and profanity.  Continued use of such language even in role-playing will be frowned upon and can be brought before the council for disciplinary action up to and including dismissal form the Guild.  Profanity will include but not be limited to language, abbreviations and gestures.

8.  Defend the guild with your life if necessary.

9.  Be Open and Honest with other Guild Members at all times.  If you think someone can do a better job tell him or her privately. If it continues to be a problem notify the Council and we will try to help.  If the Council has done something you don't like post a message on the Guild Website and it will be addressed.  There will be NO deleting or editorializing of messages made on the Guild Web site.

10.  The Guild was designed with an element of role-play in mind.  Each member of the Guild has a title of some sort associated with his or her current rank.  It would be appreciated but not required that when addressing another Guild member of rank higher than you own in /guild, /say, /ooc and /shout that you use the appropriate title of the member you are addressing.

11. It is a requirement of the Guild that you visit the Guild Web Page at http://knights.guildworld.com at least once a week.  Any member that does not login in for 3 consecutive weeks without notifying a member of the Council will be removed from the guild at the first available opportunity.  It is hoped that you would visit the page as frequently as possible as it will be the hub for Guild information.

12.  We would prefer that Guild Members not use the /anon flag.  We hope that you are proud of your guild and would happily display it using the /role flag.  If you feel for some reason that you must use this flag it must be turned off before engaging in any guild Chat.  Repeated violation of this rule will be brought to the attention of the Council for review.

 

HEIRACHY :

 Lords:   These are the guild founders.  There are hereditary members of the Guild Council and the driving force behind the guild.  There are currently 6 members that hold this rank.   Lords have this rank for life or until they decide to step down.  Lords are not allowed to have assistants.   In their absence a Lord may give their vote by proxy to another Lord.  If at anytime in the future the Lords as a whole decide to elevate a member to Lord status a new council position will also be created to keep the hereditary and elected Council votes equal.

 Councilors:  Elected from the general Guild population.  They share an equal vote with the Lords and help to shape the destiny of the Guild.  There are an equal number of Councilors as there are Lords.  Four of the councilors will be elected one form each Class group or Melee, Healer, Hybrid and Caster.  The other two will be filled from the Guild as a whole and may be any class including one of those already represented by an elected Councilor.  Councilors will be required to run for only one type of seat either Class or at-large.  Should a Councilor be require to be removed from their post for any reason a 2/3 majority of the entire council is required.  At that time either the Councilors Corsair will assume the position or if there is no Corsair then Council will appoint someone.

 Corsairs:  These are the assistants to the Councilors and have officer power in the Guild.  Only elected Councilors can have Corsairs.  Appointments of Corsairs must be approved by a majority of the Lords.  Corsairs will assume the responsibilities of their Counselor if a Councilor steps down or is absent.  Assumption of responsibilities will only continue until an election can be held.  Corsairs for Class seats must be chosen from a class appropriate to their seat.  The all Guild seats may choose any member as their Corsair.

 Knights:  The hardworking backbone of the Guild.  These are the members who have in some way distinguished themselves but who are not currently, or do not wish, to hold a councilor seat or Corsair Position.  Eventually all Councilors and Corsairs will be required to be Knights to be nominated.  Until such time as the population of the Guild can support it this requirement is not in force.  Knights are nominated by the Lords.  Knights are approved by the full Council.  All nominations for Knights and above must be 20th level minimum.  Exceptions may be made, but will be rare.

 Squires:  This is the general guild population.  Members at this level can vote for and nominate councilors.  Additionally Members at this level and above can receive items from the Guild inventory and loans from the Guild Bank.

 Pages:  These are the new invites to the Guild.  Members at this level are on a Probationary Period of 1 month.  During that Month they must group at least twice with two separate guild members of at least squire level for a total of four groups with guild members.  The members grouped with will be required to make a report to the Council on the page for their review.  On successful completion of the probation period and getting favorable reviews from their grouping Guild mates the promotion to Squire will be automatically given.  If an unfavorable review is given the Page and the Reviewer will be asked to come before the Council for Review before promotion is given or membership is revoked.  If no member is available of the appropriate level and or Class to group with a Page an observer will be sent to watch the Page while he is grouped and the Observer will make the report on the Page to the council. Additionally a Page will be require to complete a Quest for the Guild. The Quest will result in an Item that will belong to the Guild. Quest will be appropriate to the Level of the Page and will be difficult for their level but not impossible. The quest may or may not be for an item for your particular class. You may very well be require to get a group together for all of or part of the Quest. Where appropriate the involvement of other Guild members is not only exceptable but can be used to fill the requirements for part one as well.  

 There is no minimum Limit for acceptance into the guild as a Page or becoming a Squire.  However, after our initial growing period, acceptance to the Page level of the Guild will be invitation only and must be approved by the Council.

 

GUILD BANK

 The Guild Bank is a member supported Bank for Loans and Savings.  Only members who make deposits in the Guild bank can make Loans.  At this time no interest will be given on fund deposited into or loaned from the bank.  Once a deposit has been made into the bank a member may make a loan from the bank for up to 2 times the amount they have deposited (Deposit 50pp get a loan for 100pp) provide funds are available.  Loans are for up to 30 days only.  A member may only have one loan from the bank at a time.  Any loan not repaid in 30 days will be brought to the attention of the Council.  The Council must approve any Loan of 1000pp or more.  Loans will be only made up to 50% of the available total in the bank.  Any depositor who wishes to withdraw their money from the guild bank while the funds are not available will be repaid from the Personal funds of the Council.  Restitution to the Councilors will be made as soon as Loans are returned.

  

LOOT

 The Guild will at times sponsor Guild Raids.  While on a Guild Raid any item of value that required more than one Party to obtain belongs to the Guild.  Depending on the items received several things may happen.  For No DROP Items all guild members present who can MOST USE the item will roll for it.  For example No Drop ALL ALL items with +WIS on them will be rolled for by WIS based casters only as they can use it the most.  If you are the only WIS based caster on the raid it is your item by default.  Items with other stats including those most useful to other classes will be handled the same.  No Drop Class specific items will be rolled on by all classes supported by the item that WILL get benefit from the item.  Others can always offer to pay for the item from the roll winner before looting.  On all other items, the item will go in the Guild Inventory to be allocated to the appropriate member of Squire level or above.  All items in the Guild Inventory belong to the Guild and will be returned when the member no longer needs the item.  Additionally at the discretion of the council items can be awarded permanently to members who have distinguished themselves through deeds and actions.  Should the council decide that an item Looted is more valuable to the guild as Cash then the item will be sold so that funds can be obtained to purchase items more useful to its members.   A Guild inventory list will be published and if you see an item you can use you should Petition a Council member for permission to use the item.  Items from guild inventory are only usable by those who are squire level and above.

 All members should feel free to post Item ‘wish’ lists.  Since it is the goal of the Guild to help the members have fun and fun would be obtained through filling a ‘wish’ list first consideration of raid locations will be given to areas where member wish lists can be obtained.

 

THE VOTING PROCESS

Council Member Votes will happen every 3 months real time.  The first official election of the guild will be on June 1, 2001.  Until that time the founding Lords will appoint Council members as they see fit.  

Nominations for positions will come from all Guild Members including Lords and Councilors.  You can nominate yourself, but you can only nominate one person and all nominations must be seconded. 

At this time there are 6 Seats to be nominated for.  Four seats require that you be the appropriate Class type, Healer (Cleric, Druid, Shaman), Melee (Warrior, Monk, Rogue), Hybrid (Ranger, Cleric, Shadow Night) or Caster (Necromancer, Magician, Wizard, Enchanter).  Any Class can fill the other two seats, but you must run for one seat type or the other.  The type of seat you are running for must be designated at the time of nomination.

Until such time as all nominations can be gathered from the Knights, the Lords must approve all nominations for Council seats.  Disapproval of a nomination WILL be discussed with the nominee.

 

Other major Guild decisions, at the discretion of the Council, will be presented to the entire membership to be put to a vote.  Major Guild decisions will include such things as Raids and Hunting Zones to concentrate on (Making it easier to group) as well as what to do with any donations of Money and Loot received from Members.

 

FINAL NOTES

This Guild is run by its founders a core group of 6.  We have endeavored to layout a Guild structure that will make it possible for all Guild members to have input into the destiny of the Guild.  Please if you EVER have a problem with the way the guild is being run or the way a Guild Member is treating you from the lowest of the low to the highest of the high don’t hesitate to contact any of the Lords or Councilors to voice your concerns.
