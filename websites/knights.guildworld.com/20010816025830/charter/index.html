Charter for the 'Norrathian Avengers' a Guild of the Torvinnolious server of EverQuest

 

PURPOSE:

We are about having fun, and achieving it any way we can. What does this mean? Well, whatever is fun for YOU in EverQuest, WE will try to give you more of. It's that simple. It's leaders are dedicated, hard-working folk, and you can count on us to help you whenever you need it, as long as the same invitation is granted back towards us once in a while. In order to enhance our ability to help one another we have developed a guild structure that while sounding complicate should allow for maximum fun with minimum effort. 

For the most part, acceptance in the guild is invitation only, but during these growing periods, we will be more lenient with inviting people into the guild.

  

CODE:

 All Members of the Guild will follow a code of ethics and honor:

1. Assist your fellow Guild Members wherever and whenever possible.

2. Be polite and courteous to all at all times. Think before you speak, most likely before harsh words. You are a reflection upon your guild and fellow members. Arguments in /guild, /ooc or /shout will not be tolerated.  If feel you have to argue with someone take it to /tell and keep it private.

3. Play fair. Kill stealing, ninja looting, public flaming, and other unscrupulous behavior will NOT be tolerated.

4. Have honor by behaving in a way that reflects well upon yourself and your guild. Recognize those that are honorable.  

5. Treat everyone you meet with the same respect you would like them to show you. Be proud of who you are, and the guild you belong to. 

6. Show pride in your class, race and membership in your Guild.

7.  Endeavor to refrain from strong language and profanity.  Continued use of such language even in role-playing will be frowned upon and can be brought before the council for disciplinary action up to and including dismissal form the Guild.  Profanity will include but not be limited to language, abbreviations and gestures.

8.  Defend the guild with your life if necessary.

9.  Be Open and Honest with other Guild Members at all times.  If you think someone can do a better job tell him or her privately. If it continues to be a problem notify the Council and we will try to help.  If the Council has done something you don't like post a message on the Guild Website and it will be addressed.  There will be NO deleting or editorializing of messages made on the Guild Web site.

10.  The Guild was designed with an element of role-play in mind.  Each member of the Guild has a title of some sort associated with his or her current rank.  It would be appreciated but not required that when addressing another Guild member of rank higher than you own in /guild, /say, /ooc and /shout that you use the appropriate title of the member you are addressing.
