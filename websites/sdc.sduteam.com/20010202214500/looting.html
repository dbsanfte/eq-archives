Perl has joined whole event.  He will get 25marks
Wiel has joined in the middle of event and leaves after finished. He will get 15 marks.
Madhk has joined at start of the event but leaves before finished. He will get 20 marks
Spinalzz has joined in the middle of event and leaves before finished. He will get 10 marks

RULES:
1) Priority
1st)Priority of event:
a)--the certain class(es) of that item and the members have taken part in that event.
b)--non-class but in-event / that class but not in-event
c)-- non-class and not in-event (means everyone)
2nd)Marks wins Cash
3rd)Higher mark wins lower marks
 If 2 members have the same marks and priority, GM / officers will make final decision on it.

2) For droppable items, GM / officers will collect them and will list the items on message board. Members who interested in those items plz post a reply on that thread within 3 days. The result will post at guild homepage to see who got the items.

3) If no one willing to pay marks to get the items (both no-drop or Droppable) will sell for guild fund. The buyer will have 3DAYS to collect the funds. The price of the item will be 1MARK=100PP. If he cant pay the funds, the bookkeepers will reduce the marks from him. And if no one willing to pay marks or money for the loot, no-drop items will let it proof or destroyed by officers, and droppable items will be kept by guild. To remind, the order will be like this: Marks > cash > destroy.

EXAMPLE 1
POH event drop a warrior gloves, Spinalzz(warrior) and Hanryman(warrior) need it. But they both not willing to pay the marks to loot it coz they think they should save the marks for BP. In this case, the gloves will be sold for 2000pp for guild fund. If no one willing to pay the cash either, officer will destroy it for fairness point.

4) The marks are based on ACCOUNT not CHAR. And the marks cannot be transferred under any reasons. If a member has 2 chars in the same account want share loot, he has to decide which ONE, and only one to share the loot during the event.
BUT if a member is forced to play another player's account in order to help the event, then the marks can be added to the member's own account after officer's confirmation. (In this case, u have to notify officers b4 the event has started.)

EXAMPLE 2
Spinal have 2 chars Spinalzz(warrior) and Bruces(monk). Spinalzz using warrior but a monk plane armor dropped and Gken(monk) need it also. He can use his marks to got the armor even he is using warrior but spinalzz have to choose what CHAR item he choose either warrior or monk before event start and pays the marks to get the monk armor.

EXAMPLE 3
Clerics are badly needed in chardok raid, Wavetide needs to play Terryc's cleric in order to help the event start. Then Wavetide can request to add the marks to his own account. after officers' confirmation.

5)When we GM/ Officers agree this event take marks, the marks system will take place.

6)GM/ Officers have the right to make final decision in special cases.

7)Members must send email to MicK. You WILL NOT be granted marks in events until you have registered.

Note:  The following members will be considered as special case. They have served the guild for long time but cannot join event much b'cos of the time difference. Their marks will be multiplied by 1.5 . These members are: Sogsog, Cozmo and Yonex.

If u think u should be considered as special case, plz email to MicK (mick@sduteam.com). GM and officers will consider your case.
