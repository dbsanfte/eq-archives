June 21, 1999

Ceador Sheds Light

I have been up in High Hold Pass for a day(Sunday) and have come to the conclusion that its awesome. The levels needed here go from 14-19 outside the Keep in the pass (Gnolls and Orcs) and 20+ inside the Keeps lower dungeon(goblins). You need to be in party of at least 4 and you should do great, 6 is better (found out you get bonus XP if there is a party of 6). The XP is great. Most everything will give you XP and the loot is great. In a rotation of 6 players I looted 2 pieces of chain mail(Boots and Tunic), bronze weapons, and other misc. stuff in one afternoon. The rare Named NPC the gives up the polished granite Tomahawk never showed but..... Just thought I would give out this info to the clan. Good money and Good XP. Check it Out. I should be up there for awhile ). As soon as I hit 20 I want to check out Lake Rathe. If you are going to a new area and need info/help /tell me or e-mail me, I might be able to help diminish the death factor with some good info.

Ceador, 2nd Officer

Clan Healer/Explorer
