Guide to 35 hell for Sol A
Zone:
Solusk's Eye		  
Solo or Group:
Solo
Player Level:
25 - 43
Mob Level:
22-40
MOBs:
Gnomes, Goblins, Clockworks
Experience:
13 %
Difficulty:
Easy for a monk
Loot:
Fantastic
Name:
Karm Hundredfists
Full Description:
I started this zone at 34 and halfway through 35 I have banked well over 1k. I am slightly twinked (by my level 27 pally who has since been nuked), but I imagine that you should aquire the equipment to solo here around 34/35. Note on the map the baroom. This is the key place to base yourself as there are only three mobs one of which you can solo and med after and two of which are the easiest feign split I have ever seen (with shruikens you will take no damage. The key to this room is that the mobs will not aggro on you if you are behind the bar unless they are behind the bar. Hunting here you will find inferno goblins, fire goblins, fire goblin wizards and fire goblin shamans, as well as the fire goblin bartender (drops drake hide sleeves <rare>, and drake hide legs <every time>, both good for monks due to light weight and stats). After clearing this room I usually go to the foremans room (all green and light blues at 34) you will have to feign flop your way there. There are three mobs in this room usually green ocaisionally light blue that are allmost unsplittable allthough they may be I have never had to killing one while taking damage feigning and binding or with mend you should be able to solo all three. Then I usually make a mad dash for the jail house. Here is a bit tougher as there are blue casters around and they can be kind of difficult to feign off. I have died once due to solusk priests aggroing and hitting me after i feigned ( bad timing in 35= :( unhappy monk) NOTE: luckily the cleric epic mob spawns in this zone so you will most likely be able to get a low cost rez from friendly and bored high levels who may even buff you. NOTE 2: The best part about this zone is the goblin merchant (besides from the gnome merchant whose items I will never afford) I have a rallic pack which comes in extremely handy as I can usually loot about 4 or 5 mobs without getting too heavy, also the goblin merchant sells food water and bandaids which comes in extremely handy when trying to keep weight low.<end of note> The goblin torturer drops a momento box weight 2 70% WR small items, which imo is better then the ee bag but worse then dusty ransackers or archeologists. He is an easy solo usually blue sometimes light blue and is in a room by himself allthough if you aggro mobs on the way in you may have to feign to get them to leave. Note3 Perfect feign flopping before coming here if youre an iksar (and not kos) try flopping through freeport its a bit of a fun run. If youre human just run around High keep for a while and youll get the hang of it. sub NOTE4 Feign flopping is when you run feign death when mobs aggro then wait for your feign death icon to de-grey, then get up and run till you agro rinse and repeat. I hope this was helpful if I left anything out I will email it and try to get it edited in. This document while being the musings of Karm Hundredfists is freely distributable and should be shared with anyone who is interested.
