The Rules
The idea behind EQlotto is simple, but there are still some rules that I will mention here. I really recommend that you read through the rules here before buying tickets, so that there are no misunderstandings.

1. First rule: all rules are subject to change, but changes will be posted here.

2. The only official representatives of EQlotto.com, meaning the only people you can currently trust to buy tickets from, are Rolis and Lottokeeper. If anyone else claims to be selling tickets from EQlotto, they are probably trying to rip you off. If I ever find another person I think I can trust and I decide to let him/her become an official ticket seller, I will make it known here on the website. So until then, only ROLIS and LOTTOKEEPER can be trusted.

3. The Lottery will be held on Sunday nights, probably by around 1AM. All drawings will be done by Lottokeeper in East Commons in the tunnel. (Lottokeeper is one of my characters made only to hold money and be there to sell tickets. We are the same person.) No tickets will be sold during the hour leading up to the drawing, as this time will be needed to finalize the list.

4. Drawings will be done using the /random feature in game. When you purchase a ticket, or tickets, you will be told your 3-digit number, starting with ticket #111, going up to the last ticket sold. Keep track of your number, but keep in mind that it will be listed on this website at the Current List page. It will be updated often. After I have calculated the total number of tickets sold, I will do a /random. If your number is the number that comes up, you're the winner. There are, however, some other things to know:
    -The first 10 tickets in each drawing (#101-110) will always be Wildcards. If one of these numbers is selected in the /random, the money rolls over to the next week's drawing, increasing the jackpot. These tickets, obviously, are not sold, but are reserved by the lottery.
    -If you purchase a ticket online, you MUST pay for your ticket before the drawing is held. If you buy a ticket and are unable to pay for it before the drawing, your number will be voided. if a voided number is picked when the drawing takes place, we will simply do another /random until a valid ticket is selected.
    -The winner of the lottery will receive 90% of the money gained from selling the tickets. The remaining 10%, yup, you guessed it, goes to me. Hey, I pay for the website so I should get something, shouldn't I?
    -If you have paid for your ticket, and you are the winner but you are not present at the time of the drawing, don't worry. You will have one full week to claim your prize. You will have until just before the following week's drawing to claim your prize. If a jackpot is not claimed by the time of the following week's drawing, that money will be added to the current jackpot.
    -The winner will be posted on the front page of the website, and I will try to track down the winner in game as well.
    -For obvious reasons, only the winner of the jackpot will be given the money, unless the winner sends me a tell giving me permission to give the money to another.

5. All sales are final. No refunds will be given, period.

6. Tickets are sold for money, and money only. Each ticket costs 10 (ten) platinum coins, and 10pp is the price of each ticket. A maximum of 10 tickets may be purchased by any one person (character).

7. All disputes will be settled by EQlotto.com. It is an honest establishment. I would not have spent the time making this site, and having it hosted, if I wanted to steal everyone's money. However, if a dispute does come up, such as more than one person claiming to have won a week's drawing, the decision will be made by me who gets the money. The decision will be made based on my records, which I take very seriously.

8. No members of the guild Random Acts are elligible to purchase tickets from EQlotto.com. Sorry, friends, but it just doesn't look good to sell to my guild mates. :)

9. All online purchases must be made 24 hours prior to the drawing. Online orders placed late will be assumed to be purchases for the drawing the next week.

10. I'm sure I'll have to create more rules in case players find a way around my Vision. Hey, this is Everquest, right?

That's a lot of rules for such a simple game...
