Welcome

I don't know if this site will amount to much of anything. Don't let the URL fools you (eqlotto was an idea I entertained a while ago, which bombed, so now I'm using my webspace to help the monk community in their hunting needs).

This site has two functions...1) To serve as a resource for all monks to view what others consider to be good hunting locations throughout the lands of Norrath...and 2) To allow monks to come here to post their own guides, using a simple form.

VERY IMPORTANT!! - When posting a guide, when you are typing up the description of the site, you can't go to a new paragraph just by pressing enter or return on your keyboard. You MUST type <br> whenever you want to break the line and go down to the next one. Please do this, or your descriptions all become one long continuous paragraph. If you have submitted a guide, and it's screwed up because of this, email me to let me know, and I can add some line breaks. Just tell me exactly where in your post.

So here we are. You can view the current guides, or post your own. Please try to verify that the info you post is correct.
