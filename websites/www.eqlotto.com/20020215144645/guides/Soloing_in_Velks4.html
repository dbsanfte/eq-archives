Full Description:
Gather up some bandaids, get your fungi tunic on and go hit Velks for some decent solo exp. Things to watch out for are Statues, jumping from extremely high places, and pulling trains. Easy zone, normally crowded, but if you're good you can nab a mob here and there and no one will ever notice. *Not sure on mob level
