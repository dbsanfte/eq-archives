Ch...ch...ch...changes are afoot. Sun, Dec 05, 2004
Recent events have spawned something in me. A renewal, you might call it. (If you want to call it insanity, that's fine, but I won't, I'm too busy deluding myself.)

Anyway, I've started keeping up better on guild events, happenings, and status. The roster is as up to date as i can get it with out either people claiming their characters or signing up. Over the next few weeks, I will also be updating the Game Lore section of the site with info from screenshots.

Today's main project, however, was updating the guild bank. It's there, folks, don't be afraid to ask for a loan. The primary purpose of the guild bank is to allow people to get all their spells when they level, a supplement to their own wallets and bank accounts, NOT a run-the-bank-dry without using your own money. Loans will also be given to supplement in the purchase of equipment, but be reasonable about it folks. If you are level 10 and dont have 100pp, please don't ask for 1.5kpp for a weapon. Kotah is currently the only person that has taken out a loan, hers was for 250pp. I will be keeping track of loans, though, and while they do not have to be paid back at any specific time, I ask that you pay it back when you can. Payments do not have to be in full, partial payments when you can afford it are ok.

There are also several items in the guild bank for loan or purchase. Spells there are on a first come first served basis, and do not have to be purchased or returned. I will be working with Nexus to update the acutal manager for the guild bank so that it will also display a "buy it now" purchase price. Until then, the price is listed as a comment.

If you want to make a donation of either money or items to the guild bank, send me or Lakotah a PM either on these boards or the SOE ones so that we can arrange a time to meet in game to receive your donation.

-- Coar


Fall of the Rivals of the Throne Tue, Nov 30, 2004
Sadly, it is my duty to announce the (possible temporary) fall of RoTT as a guild. With the introduction of EverQuest II and World of Warcraft, RoTT will not be a real tag. If you still want to be a pirate, and a Rival of the Throne, measures ARE being put in place to accomodate you.

The elaborate pirate spy network has infiltrated GoTT and unbeknownst (I always liked that word) to the Guardians, they have been hiding their TRUE identities. Enlisting the help of several of the most powerful wizards, enchanters, and magicians, they have hidden their true forms and devised a mystical means of secret and secure communications within their ranks.

Here they lie in wait, anticipating their chance to strike out at those who profess their loyalty to the throne.

-- Coar
