 Considering the Path of a Hack n' Slasher?
Team Comments was updated on the 23rd with these words from Lead Programmer Gaffer:

Life as a pure melee character in Origin
As many of you know, I'm Jeremy Gaffney, Tech Lead n' Main Combat Dood for ORIGIN. Welcome to the latest Comments from the Team.

One question that's arisen from time to time is: "What are you doing for folks who want to be pure melee types in Origin?"

In many games with skill-tree type systems, it's set up so that if you want to be able to go toe-to-toe with the bad guys, you do so at the expense of having interesting things to do in combat. On that same token, the price of having neat and unusual spells and skills is often that you are vulnerable, and can quickly become a monster's lunch.

To an extent, this arrangement is useful. After all, it promotes grouping and specialization, which in general are Good Things. And it's useful because it gives inexperienced players a very easy role to fill: "Go Stand There And Whack That Thing While I Do Something Interesting". However, not all people chose the role of a fighter/warrior/tank/meatshield because they were newbies, or really wanted to just stand there mashing a key or two...they wanted to be warriors involved in dramatic combat like Arnold in a Conan movie, or the warriors in the Seven Samurai. When you find out that the role you've dreamed of playing turns out to be very simple, it can be a tad disappointing...although, admittedly, Conan didn't seem to spend a lot of time strategizing.

Considering that combat has an important role in almost any adventuring game, we wanted to make sure it was fun for everyone. In ORIGIN, all combat schools start simple with only a few key choices, but rapidly different choices will emerge in the skill trees, which can lead to even more choices of actions in a given fight. Or, if you prefer, you can take your few choices you start with and just become better and better at them without confusing yourself with lots of options.

As you advance in ranks with a given weapon (advancing in most melee professions requires such, or advancing in unarmed combat), you will be able to gain new skills and new abilities. Skills tend to be passive (for example, +10 to hit with all swords, or +20% damage to unarmed attacks). Abilities tend to be active - a new attack or attack series is common, although there are also some skills which might modify others, such as a skill that improves your attack for the next minute, or adds to your attacks effects like bleeding or stunning damage for a few seconds.

The choices may seem simple initially, but as you go deeper and deeper, you can get more and more choices as you go. Adding to this, some of your earlier attacks can become more potent as you learn more skills and abilities. The simple attack you gained at level 2 may become quite deadly with 40 more levels of skill choices following - both because the various components of the attacks became more powerful as you attained more and more bonuses to hit, damage, and your special attacks, and also because certain boxes deeper in the tree can specifically enhance earlier gained attacks. Ultimately, Bob The Strategic Warrior may choose a large range of subtle abilities to enhance different attacks in various situations, as well as a wide variety of situation-specific attacks, while Fred the Mighty Crusher could still be pounding happily on his early attacks, watching them work with deadly efficiency and ignoring the possible strategies that might allow him to better interrupt spellcasting or other specialized trickery.

So... start simple, end complex. Or, if you prefer, start simple and stay simple. The most important thing is to not let the mechanics get in the way of a good time.

Jeremy Gaffney
aka Gaffer
Lead Programmer, ORIGIN

 Roleplayers unite!
From Ogodei:
Hello everyone,

I am currently planning a website with the purpose to bring the roleplayers in UWO:O together. What I mean with roleplayers are people who actually make use of the rich fiction this game offers and stay true to it (no Sith Lords please!). People who even add to the fiction and immersiveness by always staying in character while in public, and by telling their own stories, be they planned plotlines/quests, or just a result of roleplayed interaction with other people in the community.

I just found out - at least for myself - that it is the best way to enjoy a MMORPG for a very long time. MMORPGs usually do not have an ever-present storyline, like single player games. You need other goals than raising skills and hoarding loot. The best way to never get bored is to roleplay your character, make him a living, breathing being living in Sosaria, with his own mind, attitude and goals. In a roleplaying community there will be heroes and villains, romance, political intrigue, wars, etc... In short, your online life will get a meaning.

The larger such a community is, the easier it will be to make it a "living" world. So to avoid that the few roleplayers out there are scattered over all shards, I thought it would be a good idea to bring them together BEFORE they get to the character creation screen. For that I am looking for like-minded people to help me build up a website, that provides a forum of communication and information for roleplayers in UWO:Origin. Some of the planned features include, but are not limited, to the following:

- guild database/contacts/links
- in-game news (about events, quests, etc.)
- roleplaying tips (info about the fiction, like Jukan philosophy, etc.)
- player-written stories
- ...and of course messageboards, polls, etc, etc

I already did a similar site for the roleplayers on the Europa shard of Ultima Online, which is quite successful, so you might want to take a look at it (http://erpa.2y.net/). I learned a lot of valuable lessons there, that will hopefully be of some use for this new "project".

So if you already have plans for a rp-character, -guild or even -town, or if you are interested in helping with the site, please contact me at leopold@hastreiter.de.

Thanks for listening,
Ogodei
A noble endeavor!
Thanks Ogodei!

 Ultima 1 Remake Interview on GA-RPG
From Chris Johnson:
In the 80’s and early 90’s when you talked about computer role-playing games, the Ultima series of titles was always included in the discussion. Although the series has lost its luster in many folks eyes since Ultima VIII: Pagan first hit store shelves, it still maintains a huge fan base that includes a few people with enough interest to try and recreate a couple of these titles using newer engines. One of these groups is a team of young programmers and artists at Peroxide. The guys at Peroxide just wanted to get some experience making computer games in order to get jobs in the industry they love down the road a bit, and they just so happen to want to attempt a remake of Ultima 1 as one of their first projects.

With diehard fans of the Ultima Series literally begging Origin Systems to do just what Peroxide is attempting to do with Ultima 1- A Legend is Reborn, we thought it might be interesting to find out more about the project in our interview with Art Director Soren Seeberg below, Enjoy!

 Beta Spot Giveaway Promotion
From the official site:
We're happy to announce the end of our latest beta spot giveaway promotion! Wait - you didn't know there was a promotion? Read on to find out more...

In our search for beta testers for UWO:ORIGIN, it's crucial that we find testers who will test the game and submit bugs if they find them. So how do we find those players who we know will do this? Since we have a Test Center for UO, it made sense for us to look for those players who use the shard to test UO's new features and take the time to submit bugs!

 Official Site Updated-Comments from Starr Long and Contest
Two updates to the official site, the first being in the form of a new Comments from the Team by Starr Long:
Hello Everyone,

Right before the holidays, our latest video trailer was released. If you have not yet downloaded this latest video, I highly recommend it! We put the video together for the UO World Faire to show at the ORIGIN happy hour, and we're glad to finally get the opportunity to share it with the rest of the community, and hear what you think. We think it shows our game well, but it's hard to really express where your game is going through a video alone. As I've mentioned before, we have four main goals that we hope to achieve in the making of this game:

1. Amazing Combat
2. Cool, Unique World
3. Unbelievable Customizability
4. Great Accessibility

The first three goals listed have very strong visual elements, and you can see them well represented in the video -- with the amazing vistas, varieties of characters, and crazy kung fu. It is important to note as well that what you see in the video is pure gameplay! There are no rendered or cinematic sequences at all.

For those curious fans who have asked, The Grim Faeries once again provided the music for the video, and right now we have three different composers creating music for the game itself. We chose to have three so that each of our continents would have a very distinct sound to go along with their distinct looks. For the traditional medieval fantasy Ultima areas we have our very own George Oldziey back in the house. You may remember him as the composer and conductor of the amazingly beautiful orchestrated songs in Ultima Ascension. For the Meer areas we have Cenk Eroglu, a renowned producer and musician from Turkey, to give us a taste of world music. For the technological areas we have Curse Mackey of the Grim Faeries (and My Life with the Thrill Kill Cult) for that hard industrial sound. Each composer not only works within a unique musical style, but also uses different instrumentation, timing, and effects. This really helps the areas have very diverse atmospheres; especially when you add in the different architecture, skies, clothing, flora, fauna, and terrain.

It's pretty exciting work watching all these separate pieces coming together as we wrap up systems, fix bugs, and optimize. None of this is an easy task, though, so I ask that you all remain patient, and understand that we are more interested in delivering a quality product than meeting any specific date.

Finally, although it's a little late, I wanted to say how amazing the World Faire was. It makes me feel good about what we do here to see the passion stirred up by our fans. You are what makes these games such amazing and unique experiences. I only got to meet a few of you, but I hope that next year, when ORIGIN is a part of the Faire, that I will get to interact with more of you. Until next time...

Sincerely,
Starr Long
Producer, ORIGIN

<Gasper> <OrionStratics> With the magic system almost complete, can you finally give us some examples of spells and their effects?
<Ubiq> Certainly. The four primary schools each have their own personality to them, and thus have spells that suit them well.

 Sosarian Wallpaper Contest
From Joshua Rowan:
SOSARIAN WALLPAPER CONTEST

Origin Stratics is happy to welcome in the New Year with their own fun contest for the Origin community. Consider yourself a graphic artist? Then this one is right up your alley! So what is the "Sosarian Wallpaper" Contest? Basically, we are looking for Desktop Wallpapers that focus on the themes found in New Sosaria, whether it be in New Britannia, Avenosh, or Logosia. Themes can range from the abstract to specific themes surrounding the eight Virtues. To get some ideas, make sure to check out several pieces of art already done by players in our Player Art Gallery.
1. Guidelines:
  • Wallpapers need to be created in either of two sizes - 800x600 or 1024x768 (or both).
  • Entries need to be done in BMP format (for people to use as a wallpaper), then e-mailed to Joshua Rowan in a WinZip file.
  • All entries will be judged on a) Originality; b) Faithfulness to the atmosphere of New Sosaria; and c) Use of Origin Lore.
2. Entry: No purchase necessary. Limit one entry per person. The contest begins January 1, 2001 and ends January 8, 2001. All entries must be received by 11:59 PM EST on January 8, 2001.

3. Winner Selection: Winners will be selected by the Origin Stratics staff. Each entry will be evaluated by the staff and after careful review, the top selections will be awarded. Awards winners will be announced on January 12, 2001. Winners will be notified via email and an announcement will also be made on the Origin Stratics web site.

4. Prizes: Grand Prize will receive a Microsoft Sidewinder Game Voice. 1st Place will receive a free Stratics T-Shirt*. 2nd Place will receive a free 15oz Stratics Mug*. 3rd Place will receive a free Stratics Mouse Pad*. All other entries, as long as they meet requirements, will be displayed on an upcoming Wallpaper Section.

5. Eligibility: This contest is open to all worldwide 18 and older. All Stratics Team Members and OSI employees not eligible. Void where prohibited by law.

6. Conduct: By entering this Contest, you agree to be bound by these rules.

7. Winners List: The name of the prize winners will be posted on the Origin Stratics site.

* To view the Origin Stratics' Merchandise make sure to check out our Merchandise Section
Have fun! I look forward to seeing what you all come up with!

Joshua Rowan
ORIGIN Stratics Project Manager
http://origin.stratics.com
