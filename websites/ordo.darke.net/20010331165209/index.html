    Ph4t Gnomes! March 10th/6pm PST
    Submitted by:
    Sodiah Grym    		Submitted on:
    Fri Mar 02, 2001 07:33:56

    (forgive me for my love of events that require level 1 characters. It's an easy way for all of us to partake of the event for some fun and laughter and all be the correct level range)

    When: Saturday, March 10th at 6pm EST/3pm PST
    Where: Steamfont Mountains
    What:

    The gnomish wizards have had reoccuring visions of a great gnome warrior to arise from the ranks of the just born, and as such, they have begun extensive testing of all of the new children to find this warrior hero of all the gnomish people. Prophecy dictates that this hero will someday go on to be a great dragon slayer, and being a dragon's favorite snack, the gnomes are quite eager to find this child and help him along his way to growing into the proud warrior he or she is destined to be.

    So on Saturday, March 10th, the call will be put out for the great tests. All new children will be called forth to the fields in front of Ank'anon, and duel each other for the coveted title of Ph4t Gnome. The Duels will be overseen by the Mage Tynk and possibly his wizard companion Wync, as they hunt for the hero of prophecy.

    Requirements to attend: be on in guildchat before the start time of the event to find out the gnames of your gnome, and then create them and meet out in front of the city for being guilded and then the event. Duels will be done 1 on 1, elimination style, until we have a clear winner who will then be bestowed with the title of Ph4t Gnome and given the prize of 500pp (I'm trying to get a yak for the prize, but just in case I can't get my hands on one before then, this will be the default prize). But wait! There's more!

    The gnome that loses all his duels will also be given a title, henceforth to be known as DragonBait(tm), and given 100plat (also trying to get some armourish/weapon items, pp will be default prize) just to help him buy some stuff to keep him alive, because s/he'll need it!

    So come one, come all....help the gnomish people find their hero! Let the dragons beware, the Ph4t Gnome is coming!

    (this event is open to Ordo members and friends of theirs. If inviting a friend, be sure to let me know in advance, because they won't be privy to guildchat and I'll need to give them their instructions directly. ALSO: If anyone wants to donate pp to me to help defray/spread out the cost of the 600plat I am putting up as a prize, it would be most appreciated, but not required.)
