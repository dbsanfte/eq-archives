Who are the people of the Risen Phoenix guild? Well, what we are is a family. We help each other, we are social, and it's friendly over guild chat. We don't force people to do anything they don't want to do. We do not run the guild like a military operation (except on raids where it's a requirement) and we don't order members to be on raids or else.

We give members the freedom to be an individual, yet part of a family. We will eventually do planes, dragons, and the like. But we will do it at our own pace. We have grown much lately and are working to improve as a team. But we will reach these goals at our own pace, staying centered on our core value of family and friendship. It takes time to earn the experience to do Dragons, Planes, and other major events. We are working hard to do those things, and people who join us should understand that we are working hard towards those goals, but it takes time and more importantly, patience.

People stay in Risen Phoenix not because of the loot we can get and of the places we can go, but because they can remain an individual yet be part of a family. No one is ignored, and everyone has a name. It's like "Cheers"; everyone knows your name. Everyone says "Hi" when you come on, and everyone congratulates you when you do something that's a personal accomplishment.

We invite you to browse our boards and get a sense of whom we are. If you come here looking for loot, level or fame, you are probably in the wrong place. If you come here looking for friendship, exploration, and to learn a little of the world that is Norrath, come on in.
