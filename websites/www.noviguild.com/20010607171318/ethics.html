Novi's mission is as follows:

To build a community which promotes respect and support for one another and to promote goodwill to all that we encounter. We are focused on quality people, not numbers of people. We will work together as a team and help each other grow as individuals. We will maintain a level 45 or higher requirement and a vote for new players to join Novi.
We all know what is right and what is wrong at this stage of the game, issues that arise will be judged based on these merits.
Issues will be handled fair yet swiftly. Leadership of the guild will be responsible for settling any issues brought before them.
Leadership positions will be permanent unless someone steps down.
We will have 2 officers at the start, with 2 more once we reach our targeted membership numbers. Ideally one leader four officers and fifty to sixty members will be our goal in the future. The open leadership positions will be awarded to those who 1) want it 2) deserve it 3) earned it.
The subject of recruiting, we all have this power, I would never want to take this from anyone, however we will not let just anyone into the guild. One night of hunts and fun will not get someone in this guild. We are all great recruiters in my opinion, and it is easy to see how things can get out of control if we are not careful. It will take weeks to get in this guild, not one or two nights. We want to know who these people are, and if they deem us worthy of a guild then they will understand.
