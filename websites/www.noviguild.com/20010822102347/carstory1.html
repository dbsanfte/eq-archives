What or better yet where is Carillon Dynasty ...

In a remote land far from the reaches of The Nameless lies the dominion of Carillon.
While the lands of Norrath were intwined with turmoil and conflict from the racial onslaught of it's inhabitants, Carillon quietly grew in power and grace.
Untainted by the evils of Antonica, Carillon became a pure unity destined to one day rule the lands known as Norrath.
However, Carillon's epic journey to power was not a easy path, many tests of strength, courage and honor were to stand in it's path.
