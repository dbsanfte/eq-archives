The Tales of a feeble storyteller....
Scrooid Thedrooid
There was a reason... to help out a starving infantry of Coldains of Thurgadin. 
There was a plan... Gather foodstuffs and hone up on my "Home-Ec" skills. 

Hmm as a druid of the 40th, i had rarely looked to a marketable skill to be of any use, till now. if I was going to make a good "bear'n'bread" or "snow bunny stew", i'd better learn the basics of fine cookery.

First, I needed to be able to cook them... Standing in front of an oven was not my idea of fun, so I needed a portable cooking device... A Spit! I remember that when selling some of my spoils when I was a youth to Marton Sayor in the Qeynos Hills, that his young daughter sells Spits (no not sunflower seeds!). So I wispped my way to North Karana and headed west. I used to hate the run through the western Karanas, but now after travelling through many other zones, this doesn't seem as far as it once was... I waved "hello" to the two Cyclops, Froon and Choon (we have a fine history together :) and headed to the hills of Qeynos. Purchasing my spit, I then set my sights on the next needed item to start practising my culinary skill.... FISH!

After reading some recently purchased Tomes of the culinary arts, I found that if you take a piece of fish and wrap it ""Sushi-Maki" style in some Bat wing "Nori".. WALLAH! you have a delicious "Fish-Roll"! (pass the Wasabi!)...


So off to Freeport <civilization... shudder.. > to the docks to purchase some fresh fish. there are two merchants by the dock to the Ocean of Tears that flog fish, so I went there, and filled up a backpack with trout (took weeks to get rid of the smell!)

Next step was to get stacks of bat wings..... Now at first this sounded fun, I remember several places where the bats are plentifull, so with this in mind, I memorized my spell and cast "Ring of Butcherblock mountains" and the vapours surrounded me and swept me accross to the "Land of the Dorfs". Running to the entrance of Kaladim,I remember why I don't enjoy this area... many young Dwarven children shouting at the top of their lungs, asking for "Buffs", directions, and the everpopular "sow me!" (why is owning a female pig such a popular hobby with them?). When reaching the City Gates, I set upon my task and started trying to rid this land of flying mammals. Usually these poor creatures die with one hit or two, so I thought this would be a relaxing afternoon. And soon I was into quite a rythm of running around and doing some serious Bat-icide. After about fourty minutes of this, however, I was starting to lose patience with the local youth. There were two young Dwarves in particular who followed me around like "bum-flies in the desert", pestering me with "tells", asking for directions to the docks or requests for "Power-leveling"... and my Bat wing collection was not as large as I had hoped... grrrr!

Then. It struck me. I remember something from a past life, on another server.. there was a striking young Erudin woman in the East Commons area who perhaps had hundreds of wings of the bat!

After a quick (and probably rude to many a young dwarf.. hehe) portal to the West commons area I immediately turned east and ran towards the border!

LOADING... PLEASE WAIT! (yeah like there's any other choice!)

Ahhhh!... East Commons! What a marvel of ingenuity! An area where teen-agers try desperately to gain experience while at the same moment a host of "Hostess" bakers (Twinkie-makers) try to flog their recently won spoils of war at exhorbitant prices!

Upon reaching the first set of huts and opening the door of the most westward single hut, I find her!.. there she stands before me.. her seductive long slitted dress.. her broad shoulders.. her fine cleavage... <ahem>  (I digress..hehe). I enquire as to what wares she is willing to part with (after several tries at hailing her and asking this yummy NPC for a date). and LO and behold! yes!... she does sell Bat Wings! Purchasing many a stack, I set myself down (with a good view in front of this beauty!), and set up my spit (hoping the aroma of fresh barbecue-ing Trout might somehow sway her towards me..). Then i set out to practice my skill to a reasonable level of aptitude.

What a tedious and mind numming experience! and after depleting my stock (2 tailored backpacks full) of wings and fillets, I was only halfway to my intended level of expertise! So I purchased another pack full of Wings from this beautious Lady, bowed before her and made my farewell to her (I wish NPC's would interact more.. hehe). Then it was back to the docks for me... more fish acquired, and my spit set up, I combined and combined till I advanced my skill to a respectable level (about 130).

Back to the land of the Coldain for me... to see if I can help out the local "Meat Salesman", Mordin Frostcleaver, to feed the hungry Coldain.
